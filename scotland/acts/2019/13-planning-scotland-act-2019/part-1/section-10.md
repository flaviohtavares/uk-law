## 10. Key agencies[^key-8f0b85b5f5909b722d3be074d66605bf]

*Extent: S*

In section 23D of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997 (meaning of “key agency”)—

- (a) after “of” where it first occurs insert “ Part 1A or ”,
- (b) for “a body” substitute “ a person (other than an individual) or an officeholder ”.
