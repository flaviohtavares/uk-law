## 23. Notice by planning authority of certain applications made to them[^key-999ad5199eea3c0b1efc0eb9975afd7b]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997 is amended as follows.

(2) In section 34 (notice by planning authority of certain applications made to them), after subsection (2), insert—

> (2A) Where an application under subsection (2) is for a major development, a planning authority must give notice of the application to each—
>
> - (a) councillor of the local authority,
> - (b) member of the Scottish Parliament,
> - (c) member of the House of Commons,
>
> representing the district to which the application relates.

.
