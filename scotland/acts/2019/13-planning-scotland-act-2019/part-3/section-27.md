## 27. Delegation of development decisions[^key-60ec81b9efd96f7c834bdcaf9ab93ff4]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subsection (6A) of section 56 (arrangements for discharge of functions by local authorities) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 is repealed.

(2) Subsection (2) of section 14 (pre-determination hearings) of the Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006 is repealed.
