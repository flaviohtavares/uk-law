Planning (Scotland) Act 2019

2019 asp 13

An Act of the Scottish Parliament to make provision about how land is developed and used.

The Bill for this Act of the Scottish Parliament was passed by the Parliament on 20th June 2019 and received Royal Assent on 25th July 2019
