# Management of Offenders (Scotland) Act 2019, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2023-07-03. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2019/14
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 53. Re-release after revocation of licences generally

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-215fe8ccb2c583f8968ec03c1e8533fd] The 1993 Act is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-b127313f98696f3f1e49e76a78453cfd] In subsection (3)(b) of section 10A, the word “immediately” is repealed.

(3) In section 17—

- (a)[^key-215fe8ccb2c583f8968ec03c1e8533fd] in subsection (2), for the words “revocation of a person's licence” there is substituted “ return to prison of a person whose licence is revoked ”,
- (b)[^key-b127313f98696f3f1e49e76a78453cfd] in subsection (4)—
  - (i) the word “immediate” is repealed,
  - (ii) for the words from “the Secretary of State” to the end there is substituted “ the Scottish Ministers must give effect to the direction without undue delay ”.
