## 109. Status of the Scottish Road Works Commissioner[^key-615d1d7d3fef4983f2ba909a9a6556fb]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Transport (Scotland) Act 2005 is amended as follows.

(2) In section 16 (creation, appointment, status and funding of Scottish Road Works Commissioner), after subsection (4) insert—

> (4A) The Commissioner is, as such, to be regarded as a juristic person distinct from the individual for the time being holding the office.

.
