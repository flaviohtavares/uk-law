# Tenements (Scotland) Act 2004, section 15

> Text as at 2018-07-13.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 15. Prescriptive period for costs to which section 12 relates[^key-4bddcca10d2fea4fbb5cacaad3d3d447]

*Extent: S*

In Schedule 1 to the Prescription and Limitation (Scotland) Act 1973 (c. 52) (obligations affected by prescriptive periods of five years to which section 6 of that Act applies)—

- (a) after paragraph 1(ab) there shall be inserted—

  > - (ac) to any obligation to pay a sum of money by way of costs to which section 12 of the Tenements (Scotland) Act 2004 (asp 11) applies;

  ; and
- (b) in paragraph 2(e), for the words “or (ab)” there shall be substituted “ , (ab) or (ac) ”.
