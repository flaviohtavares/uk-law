# Criminal Procedure (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2004, section 21

> Text as at 2011-03-28.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 21. Increase in extended sentence which may be passed by sheriff court in certain cases[^key-df450a819cfaebc9aa20508e76c2bf72]

*Extent: S*

In section 210A(6) of the 1995 Act (which provides for the maximum extended sentence which may be imposed by the sheriff on sex and violent offenders), for “three years” substitute “ five years ”.
