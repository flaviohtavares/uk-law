# Criminal Procedure (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2004, section 23

> Text as at 2011-03-28.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 23. Admissibility of prior statements of witnesses[^key-0209bddcbea9b7a0f6c786015a43419c]

*Extent: S*

In section 260 (admissibility of prior statements of witnesses) of the 1995 Act, after subsection (4) insert—

> (5) A prior statement made by a witness shall not, in any proceedings on indictment, be inadmissible by reason only that it is not included in any list of productions lodged by the parties.

.
