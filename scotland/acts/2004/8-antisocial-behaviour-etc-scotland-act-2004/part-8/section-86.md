# Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004, section 86

> Extent: Scotland.

## 86. Notification of registration or refusal to register

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a local authority—

- (a) enters a person in its register under paragraph (a) of section 84(2); or
- (b) refuses to enter a person in its register under paragraph (b) of that section or subsection (7) or (8) of section 84[^c-b5d213793a000632a3ee62161e074e63],

the authority shall, as soon as practicable after doing so, give notice of the fact to the person.

(1A)[^c-a9b815a905188ee32f809e0e0ea07bbe] Where a local authority gives notice of the fact of registration under subsection (1)(a) it must, in doing so, give notice of the landlord registration number.

(2) Where a local authority refuses to enter a person in its register under section 84(2)(b), (7) or (8),[^c-f7e31f34a57f9a8c2c5c2ea1fd3e4792] the authority shall, as soon as practicable after doing so, send notice of the fact to—

- (a) each address specified by virtue of paragraph (b) of section 83(1) in the application for registration; and
- (b) if, by virtue of paragraph (c) of section 83(1), the application specified the name and address of a person, that person.
