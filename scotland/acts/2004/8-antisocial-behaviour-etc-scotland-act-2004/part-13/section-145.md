## 145. Short title and commencement

*Extent: S*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004.

(2)[^c21018181] This Act (other than this section and section 141) shall come into force on such day as the Scottish Ministers may by order appoint.
