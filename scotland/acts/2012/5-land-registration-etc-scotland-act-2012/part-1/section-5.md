## 5. Structure of title sheets[^c-1169f9773e21e08060e6dbd3d71c89b6]

*Extent: S*

(1) A title sheet is to comprise—

- (a) a property section,
- (b) a proprietorship section,
- (c) a securities section, and
- (d) a burdens section.

(2) A section of a title sheet may be sub-divided if and as the Keeper considers appropriate.
