## 2. The parts of the register[^c-dedb77588a411665ea7ec002d46ce4e2]

*Extent: S*

The Keeper must make up and maintain, as parts of the register—

- (a) the title sheet record,
- (b) the cadastral map,
- (c) the archive record, and
- (d) the application record.
