## 72. Discharge of caveat[^c-9f96e774ea26158d0750f18b6b813012]

*Extent: S*

A person—

- (a) in whose favour warrant to place a caveat has been granted, or
- (b) who has renewed a caveat under section 69(2),

may at any time discharge the caveat.
