## 64. Application of Part to specific deeds[^c-cebf2ab5fcffd3aaacd416525175558c]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may by order modify the application of this Part in relation to any deed of a kind specified in the order.

(2) Before making such an order, the Scottish Ministers must consult the Keeper.
