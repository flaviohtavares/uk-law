# Land Registration etc. (Scotland) Act 2012, section 96

## 96. Where requirement for writing satisfied by electronic document

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-3b399f8db8a398e49a5546bd7b6fc765][^c-5819f03c612baca1188f78a6796a0c99] The Requirements of Writing (Scotland) Act 1995 (c.7) (the “**1995 Act**”) is amended as follows.

(2) In section 1 (writing required for certain contracts, obligations, trusts, conveyances and wills)—

- (a) in subsection (2)—
  - (i)[^c-699beb3f389a540c0cbb8a8f58ecb0d9] for “subsections (2A) and” substitute “ subsection ”,
  - (ii)[^c-46ae49254f4c00bf04cd68d2e16d6ece] after “written document” insert “ which is a traditional document ”,
  - (iii)[^c-46ae49254f4c00bf04cd68d2e16d6ece] after “section 2” insert “ or an electronic document complying with section 9B ”,
  - (iv)[^c-699beb3f389a540c0cbb8a8f58ecb0d9] after paragraph (b) insert—

    > - (ba) the constitution of an agreement under section 66(1) of the Land Registration etc. (Scotland) Act 2012 (asp 5),

    ,
- (b) in subsection (3)—
  - (i)[^c-699beb3f389a540c0cbb8a8f58ecb0d9] for “subsections (2)(a) or (2A)” substitute “ subsection (2)(a) ”,
  - (ii)[^c-46ae49254f4c00bf04cd68d2e16d6ece] repeal “written”,
  - (iii)[^c-46ae49254f4c00bf04cd68d2e16d6ece] for “an electronic document complying with section 2A,” substitute “ section 9B ”,
- (c)[^c-699beb3f389a540c0cbb8a8f58ecb0d9] in subsection (5), for “subsections (2)(a) or (2A)” substitute “ subsection (2)(a) ”.

(3)[^c-46ae49254f4c00bf04cd68d2e16d6ece] The provisions of section 1 as amended by subsection (2) become Part 1 of the Act.

(4)[^c-46ae49254f4c00bf04cd68d2e16d6ece] The title of Part 1 is “When writing is required”.
