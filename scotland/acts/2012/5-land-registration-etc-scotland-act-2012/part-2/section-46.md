# Land Registration etc. (Scotland) Act 2012, section 46

## 46. Meaning of “disposition” in certain provisions[^key-57ad316c6250aec4ec8d5116e02ced6f][^key-6809a9386931b43968dc9402a6100fcc]

*Extent: S*

In the application of sections 21, 23, 30 and 48 and schedule 1A[^key-0289c177040cab5cb827638476ee7dab] to a case in which transfer of ownership is by virtue of compulsory acquisition, any reference in those sections or that schedule[^key-fd6e022540fddd9887c945a239fbe927] to a “**disposition**” includes a reference to—

- (a) a conveyance the form of which is provided for by an enactment,
- (b) a notarial instrument, or
- (c) a general vesting declaration.
