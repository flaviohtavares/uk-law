## 34. Withdrawal and amendments etc. of application[^key-4127d9a1e3220ea62d080554795ec07e]

*Extent: S*

(1) While an application for registration is pending, the applicant—

- (a) may withdraw it, but
- (b) except with the consent of the Keeper, may not substitute it or amend it.

(2) Land register rules may specify circumstances in which consent under subsection (1)(b) must be given.
