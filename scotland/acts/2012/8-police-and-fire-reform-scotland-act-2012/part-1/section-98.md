# Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012, section 98

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2025-12-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2012/8
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 98. Transitional and transitory provision[^c2141665]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-2af2aacc43dce276fcc62f3f783f7c91] A person mentioned in subsection (3) must provide the Authority with such information and assistance as the Authority may reasonably require.

(2)[^key-2af2aacc43dce276fcc62f3f783f7c91] A person mentioned in subsection (3) must provide the chief constable with such information and assistance as the chief constable may reasonably require.

(3)[^key-2af2aacc43dce276fcc62f3f783f7c91] Those persons are—

- (a) a police authority within the meaning of the Police (Scotland) Act 1967 (c.77) (“**the 1967 Act**”),
- (b) a joint police board constituted by an amalgamation scheme made under the 1967 Act,
- (c) a chief constable of a police force maintained under the 1967 Act,
- (d) the Scottish Police Services Authority.

(4) Schedule 4 makes provision about the period before the Police Service is established.

(5)[^key-2af2aacc43dce276fcc62f3f783f7c91] Schedule 5 contains provision about the transfer of constables, inspectors of constabulary, police cadets, staff, property, rights, liabilities and obligations.
