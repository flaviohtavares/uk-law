# Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012, section 74

## 74. Functions of inspectors

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-c2acbb652481a54fb5e921c47274e2b3] The Scottish Ministers may direct the inspectors of constabularyto make inquiries about any matter relating to the Authority or the Police Service as they consider appropriate.

(2) The inspectors of constabulary may make such other inquiries as they think fit about—

- (a)[^key-c2acbb652481a54fb5e921c47274e2b3] the state, efficiency and effectiveness of the Authority and the Police Service, and
- (b)[^key-0bae08c528f20f6b164357500dde3542] the arrangements made by the Authority and the chief constable under section 37(1) and (2).
