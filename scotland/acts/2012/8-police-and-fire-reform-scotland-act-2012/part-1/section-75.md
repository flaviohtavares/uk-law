# Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012, section 75

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2025-12-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2012/8
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 75. HMICS plan

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-0fa335de463a8c18d9f74e2ef02782e5] The inspectors of constabulary must prepare a plan setting out—

- (a) priorities for inquiries to be carried out by them, and
- (b) information on how inquiries will be carried out in a way which is proportionate, accountable and transparent.

(2)[^key-2d1766aaf51244f120d08719f042b169] The inspectors of constabulary—

- (a) must keep the plan under review, and
- (b) may from time to time revise the plan.

(3)[^key-0fa335de463a8c18d9f74e2ef02782e5] The inspectors of constabulary must, in preparing a plan (and any revised plan), consult such persons as they consider appropriate.

(4)[^key-0fa335de463a8c18d9f74e2ef02782e5] The inspectors of constabulary must publish the plan (and any revised plan) in such manner as they consider appropriate (having regard to the desirability of it being accessible to those whom the inspectors of constabulary consider likely to have an interest in it).
