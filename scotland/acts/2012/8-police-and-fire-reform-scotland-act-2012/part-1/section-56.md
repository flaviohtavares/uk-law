## 56. Right to appeal to the First-tier Tribunal[^c-2d0648151d5e599548eca533c3cc0854]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-07c1c16155464abd3d99234a89ccb273] A constable may appeal to the First-tier Tribunal[^c-9be0dba4917d0dbf7ffad8644b0db33a] against any decision to dismiss the constable, or to demote the constable in rank, taken in pursuance of regulations made under section 48.

(2)[^c-07c1c16155464abd3d99234a89ccb273] An appeal is competent only if the constable has exhausted any available process of review or appeal provided for in such regulations.
