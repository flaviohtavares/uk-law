# Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012, section 81

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2025-12-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2012/8

> Extent: Scotland.

## 81. Power to give directions after adverse HMICS report

*Extent: S*

(1) This subsection applies where a report given by the inspectors of constabulary under section 78 or 79states that the inspectors of constabulary are of the opinion—

- (a)[^c-632eda9b2c113c5a0cca3c882ddf1b0c] that the Authority or Police Service—
  - (i) is not efficient or effective, or
  - (ii) will, unless remedial measures are taken, cease to be efficient or effective, or
- (b)[^c-2daa16eb78ef2e9219bbe1d818e11d29] that best value for the Authority or the Police Service—
  - (i) has not been secured in pursuance of subsection (1) or, as the case may be, (2) of section 37, or
  - (ii) will not, unless remedial measures are taken, be so secured.

(2)[^c-2daa16eb78ef2e9219bbe1d818e11d29] Where subsection (1) applies, the Scottish Ministers may direct the Authority to take such measures as may be specified in the direction.

(3)[^c-2daa16eb78ef2e9219bbe1d818e11d29] The Authority must comply with any direction given under this section.
