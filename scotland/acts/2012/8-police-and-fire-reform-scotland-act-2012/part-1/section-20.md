## 20. Constables: general duties[^c-1db946d7f3f08a1f0e72cb14028106a4]

*Extent: S*

(1) It is the duty of a constable—

- (a) to prevent and detect crime,
- (b) to maintain order,
- (c) to protect life and property,
- (d) to take such lawful measures, and make such reports to the appropriate prosecutor, as may be needed to bring offenders with all due speed to justice,
- (e) where required, to serve and execute a warrant, citation or deliverance issued, or process duly endorsed, by a Lord Commissioner of Justiciary, sheriff, summary sheriff or justice of the peace[^c-6003daedd00ff15090a611316220084c] in relation to criminal proceedings, and
- (f) to attend court to give evidence.
