## 26. Police staff[^key-13b219786df6a7f17ff24aae367dcf29]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Authority may appoint police staffto assist in the carrying out of police functions.

(2) Police staffappointed under subsection (1) may be—

- (a) employed by the Authority, or
- (b) provided to the Authority under arrangements between the Authority and a third party.

(3) The chief constable has power to make appointments under subsection (1) on behalf of the Authority.
