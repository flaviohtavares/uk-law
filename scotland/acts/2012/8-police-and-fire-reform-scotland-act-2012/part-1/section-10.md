# Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2025-12-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2012/8
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 10. Constable's declaration[^key-481e6f5975b421ab2af9cdf92234b97f][^key-287d66a5fd6476639279f65106b45406][^key-39a77be68ba939d6026071363e5418af]

*Extent: S*

(1) An appointment of an individual as a constable has effect only where the individual has made a declaration in the following terms before a sheriff , summary sheriff[^key-6fbda9a6f46db1f8e3df90d0956f2199] or justice of the peace—

> I, do solemnly, sincerely and truly declare and affirm that I will faithfully discharge the duties of the office of constable with fairness, candour,[^key-14488fda6547a34ab1a4bf511cc0b019] integrity, diligence and impartiality, that I will follow the Code of Ethics for Policing in Scotland[^key-b31db4175c8de6f93272c2e432c295f4] and that I will uphold fundamental human rights and accord equal respect to all people, according to law.

.

(2) The Scottish Ministers may by order modify the declaration.
