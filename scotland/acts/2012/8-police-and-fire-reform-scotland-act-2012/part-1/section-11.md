# Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012, section 11

## 11. Ranks

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-7529759adb5c713df65ae61f73f1079b] The ranks which a constable may hold are—

- (a) chief constable,
- (b) deputy chief constable,
- (c) assistant chief constable,
- (d) chief superintendent,
- (e) superintendent,
- (f) chief inspector,
- (g) inspector,
- (h) sergeant,
- (i) constable.

(2)[^c-7529759adb5c713df65ae61f73f1079b] Constables appointed as senior officers under section 7 are to hold the rank corresponding to the office to which they are appointed.

(3)[^c-7529759adb5c713df65ae61f73f1079b] It is for the chief constable to assign, and to make promotions to, ranks below that of assistant chief constable.

(4)[^c-7529759adb5c713df65ae61f73f1079b] A constable may be demoted in rank only—

- (a) if the constable consents, or
- (b) in accordance with regulations made under section 48.

(5)[^c-196f656690dad87fc97e2c49a270ec6a] The Scottish Ministers may by regulations modify subsection (1) to add or remove any rank below that of chief constable.

(6)[^c-196f656690dad87fc97e2c49a270ec6a] Regulations made under subsection (5) may make such provision in consequence of the addition or removal of a rank as the Scottish Ministers consider appropriate including (in particular) provision—

- (a) creating or removing an office corresponding to the rank,
- (b) relating to the functions of senior officers,
- (c) modifying this or any other enactment.

(7)[^c-196f656690dad87fc97e2c49a270ec6a] Before making regulations, the Scottish Ministers must consult—

- (a) the chief constable,
- (b) the Authority,
- (c) the joint central committee of the Police Federation for Scotland,
- (d) such persons as appear to them to be representative of senior officers,
- (e) such persons as appear to them to be representative of superintendents (including chief superintendents), and
- (f) such other persons as they consider appropriate.
