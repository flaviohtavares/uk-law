## 108. Provision of other services[^key-ff89667a4c0938d2fdec3e8b05cb87f9]

*Extent: S*

In section 14 of the 2005 Act (provision of other services)—

- (a) in subsection (1)
  - (i) for “A relevant authority” substitute “ SFRS ”, and
  - (ii) for “the authority” substitute “ SFRS ”, and
- (b) in subsection (2), for “An authority” substitute “ SFRS ”.
