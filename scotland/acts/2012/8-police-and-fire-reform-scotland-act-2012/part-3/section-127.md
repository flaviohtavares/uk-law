## 127. Transitional provision etc.

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may by order make such transitional, transitory or saving provision as they consider appropriate for the purposes of, or in connection with, the coming into force of any provision of this Act.

(2) An order under this section may modify this or any other enactment.
