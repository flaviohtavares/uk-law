## 63. Meaning of officially accused[^key-9d203465f818065ed07d2e2473b97bb7][^key-1f2a5b7c424be9a3c5b0c0c282b9b1b6][^key-102a37e5984e6fdb44a7a04e7664c23e][^key-d9d94dc42b4a729e69e9b7806b14290c][^key-4f268c47387b4fe177f52d14c90b2d6b]

*Extent: S*

For the purposes of this Part, a person is officially accused of committing an offence if—

- (a) a constable charges the person with the offence, or
- (b) the prosecutor initiates proceedings against the person in respect of the offence.
