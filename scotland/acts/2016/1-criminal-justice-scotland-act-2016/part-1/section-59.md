# Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2016, section 59

## 59. Disapplication in relation to cases involving terrorism[^c-922d8eb8b7416910d7220c1e500dd207][^c-733b2bb2fbfee392c56d4bb12fa3d4f4]

*Extent: S*

(1) Nothing in this Part applies in relation to a person who is arrested under section 41(1) or 43B(1)[^c-85bfb79d29b5d39cb0fa8a38fe413933] of the Terrorism Act 2000.

(2) Subsection (1) is subject to paragraph 18 of Schedule 8 to the Terrorism Act 2000.
