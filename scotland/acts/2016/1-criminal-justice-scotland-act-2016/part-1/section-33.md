## 33. Consent to interview without solicitor[^key-5573a0a73aa028cf56dd08b5ab081dfe]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subsection (2) applies[^key-00264444665f64ee103e45d031a9075f] for the purpose of section 32(3)(a).

(2) A person may not consent to being interviewed without having a solicitor present if—

- (a) the person is under 18[^key-81033c393ff6a298f52e7969af350bc9] years of age
- (b)[^key-6a0f517ec695a095282be807394908ef] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) the person is 18[^key-76fdc58523edc60c26fa96abb141e380] years of age or over and, owing to mental disorder, appears to a constable to be unable to—
  - (i) understand sufficiently what is happening, or
  - (ii) communicate effectively with the police.

(3)[^key-932bd2e70f8df6700aa0844aeb06fd41] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-ad61a025760537887a48b2629dd56bcd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-5a0ca84601929a1b48abc18a7c2e174c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) In subsection (2)(c)—

- (a) “**mental disorder**” has the meaning given by section 328 of the Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003,
- (b) the reference to the police is to any—
  - (i) constable, or
  - (ii) person appointed as a member of police staff under section 26(1) of the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012.
