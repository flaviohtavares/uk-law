## 20. Information to be given in particular cases[^c-da3f3538f60b502a351bb994c7587925][^c-3f72a256a842823233ab43cdb6fb049e]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subsection (2) applies when—

- (a) a person is in police custody having been arrested under a warrant in respect of an offence listed in subsection (3)(b)[^c-2a3199c662d15f56653e4a4713ebce87], or
- (b) a person—
  - (i) is in police custody having been arrested without a warrant, and
  - (ii) since being arrested, the person has been charged by a constable with an offence listed in subsection (3)(b)[^c-2b974f3cd0e3a06ba6ee90be6836beb6].

(2) The person must be informed as soon as reasonably practicable—

- (a) that the person's case at, or for the purposes of, any relevant hearing [^c-9f0fae849ee0dd5b1679cbc76c1995a7]... in the course of the proceedings may be conducted only by a lawyer,
- (b) that it is, therefore, in the person's interests to get the professional assistance of a solicitor, and
- (c) that if the person does not engage a solicitor for the purposes of the conduct of the person's case at or for the purposes of the hearing, the court will do so.

(3)[^c-3482996491333531ba86ffd0155c79e6] For the purposes of subsections (1) and (2)—

- (a) “**relevant hearing**” is to be construed in accordance with section 288C(1A) or (as the case may be) 288DC(4) of the 1995 Act,
- (b) the list is—
  - (i) an offence to which section 288C of the 1995 Act applies (certain sexual offending),
  - (ii) an offence to which section 288DC of the 1995 Act applies (domestic abuse cases).
