## 55. Abolition of requirement for constable to charge[^c-58a0dd251bbe68cf65cf89f7e86b3e06][^c-84119cd14947dab8cd67cab113e00c7b]

*Extent: S*

Any rule of law that requires a constable to charge a person with an offence in particular circumstances is abolished.
