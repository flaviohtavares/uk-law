## 65. Limitation on what enables search[^key-54000fa274acce376f1e451140d8f234][^key-b909ce56d899569b1a5431c309ee66de][^key-68492e91f7332cca5a0ab1be407661fe][^key-bf5af943e75ae723cdd54d2d3597b53a][^key-164bab4a831ab31373d471549b4cf5c4][^key-3c0307362535af0769616f8e316b46b8][^key-8514eb46cb3105ea2d7776e0d566cad6][^key-8969e7701c9927035884d21beaeb7407][^key-b909ce56d899569b1a5431c309ee66de][^key-68492e91f7332cca5a0ab1be407661fe][^key-bf5af943e75ae723cdd54d2d3597b53a]

*Extent: S*

(1) This section applies in relation to a person who is not in police custody.

(2) It is unlawful for a constable to search the person otherwise than—

- (a) in accordance with a power of search conferred in express terms by an enactment, or
- (b) under the authority of a warrant expressly conferring a power of search.
