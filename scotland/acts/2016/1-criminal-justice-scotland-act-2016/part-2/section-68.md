## 68. Duty to consider child's wellbeing[^c-360998dde9e3b538da752800f60b8b34][^c-b909ce56d899569b1a5431c309ee66de][^c-68492e91f7332cca5a0ab1be407661fe][^c-bf5af943e75ae723cdd54d2d3597b53a][^c-3c9e46a00a814ac370f4e9b0004e6055][^c-10116f26da352de2b76438def161af5b][^c-9980d60cc3916cae8cb6c288b631c8db][^c-17a350f07dde1e6c002854523ef6f8cf][^c-b909ce56d899569b1a5431c309ee66de][^c-68492e91f7332cca5a0ab1be407661fe][^c-bf5af943e75ae723cdd54d2d3597b53a]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subsection (2) applies when a constable is deciding whether to search a child who is not in police custody.

(2) In taking the decision, the constable must treat the need to safeguard and promote the wellbeing of the child as a primary consideration.

(3) For the purposes of this section, a child is a person who is under 18 years of age.
