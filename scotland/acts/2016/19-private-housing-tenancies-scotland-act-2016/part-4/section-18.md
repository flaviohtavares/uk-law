## 18. Method by which rent may be increased[^c-eefe0ac7c1a2ddb3dc1767dd79ca96d5][^c-668a1e9f6900e6660f96ed7963fb97fb]

*Extent: S*

The rent payable under a private residential tenancy may be increased only in accordance with Chapter 2.
