## 24. Tenant's right to refer increase to rent officer[^key-86f0f16fc075c1686e02c87199223f1a]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-f0a4585b88e8adf95b165ea7d5606989] Having received a rent-increase notice, a tenant may make a referral to a rent officer for the area in which the let property is situated seeking an order under section 25.

(2)[^key-4894b658f5ab238ce0acb8fc7ac1b967] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-205a11d7f32a7485e0fdbf3b6f971118][^key-680047d95ff7a8c306dbbc20948d34f6] A referral to a rent officer under subsection (1) must be––

- (a) in the prescribed form,
- (b) accompanied by the prescribed fee (if any),
- (c) intimated by the tenant to the landlord in the prescribed manner, and
- (d) made before the end of the day falling 21 days after the tenant receives the notice.

(4)[^key-205a11d7f32a7485e0fdbf3b6f971118][^key-680047d95ff7a8c306dbbc20948d34f6] In subsection (3), “**prescribed**” means prescribed by the Scottish Ministers by regulations.
