## 28. Right of appeal to First-tier Tribunal[^c-870ed71b245fc544027c43644caf5ae8][^c-6de574a44980b0aa89efbb1ceb58c40d]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a rent officer has made an order under section 25(1) in relation to the rent payable under a private residential tenancy, the landlord or the tenant may appeal against the order to the First-tier Tribunal.

(2) An order under section 25(1) may not be appealed against—

- (a) more than 14 days after the order is made, or
- (b) where the order is made by virtue of section 33(2).

(3) Making an appeal under subsection (1) renders the order being appealed against of no effect.
