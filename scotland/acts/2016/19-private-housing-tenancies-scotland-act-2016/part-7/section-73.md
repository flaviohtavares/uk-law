# Private Housing (Tenancies) (Scotland) Act 2016, section 73

## 73. Minor errors in documents[^key-6b119593a54a796968033f44c6d5420a]

*Extent: S*

(1) An error in the completion of a document to which this section applies does not make the document invalid unless the error materially affects the effect of the document.

(2) This section applies to—

- (a) a notice under section 14(3), 16(3)(c), 22(1) , 43L(1),[^key-c144bb5e8547d714867f4ec8cbf19b5d] 61(1),
- (b) the document by which a referral is made to a rent officer under section 24(1) or 43N(2)[^key-d013ea91658a8a509735bc16673689e0],
- (c)[^key-a4fec980b4042007197f01abb0f34f7f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (ca)[^key-c28d8d500b718f803d7b6138f5e77332] the document by which a request for review is made to a rent officer under section 43Q(1),
- (cb) the document by which a request is made for the landlord’s consent under section 64B(1) or 64I(1), and
- (d) a notice to leave (as defined by section 62(1)).
