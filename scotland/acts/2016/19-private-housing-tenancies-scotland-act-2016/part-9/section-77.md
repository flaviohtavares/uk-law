## 77. Regulation-making powers

*Extent: S*

(1) A power to make regulations conferred by this Act includes the power to make different provision for different purposes or areas[^c-ddf59199fad03bf29d0b152ec9ed1025].

(2) Regulations under section 17(3), 22(2)(b) , 43L(3)(b), 48A(3)(c)[^c-ecc9f2f3688236dfe393ed0e21ce7a7c] , 61(3)(b) , 62(1)(d), 64B(1)(b) or (3)(d), 64C(3), 64I(1)(b) or (3)(d) or 64J(3)[^c-fe22c5abe032d82b851a33abc764a5c9] prescribing requirements which must be fulfilled by a notice , pre-notice or request (as the case may be)[^c-f88f492c8d168d938731d16ff37bfa00] may in particular require that a notice—

- (a) contain information specified in the regulations,
- (b) be in a form specified in the regulations,
- (c) be given in a manner specified in the regulations.

(3)[^c-0b47753d0f69a2dd797eb68925100516] Regulations under sections 6, 7, 11, 12 , 17A(6), 17D(1), 19(1)(a)[^c-953743fdb550a2f99296cb74573d8000], [^c-e8abe2fdc2aeefb0f0c6f016d09a5cd4]... 37 , 41, 43B(4), 43E(1) or (2), 43I(1)(b)(i), 43W(1), 48A(5) and (6), 51A(6), 59(5), 64E(1), 64F(1), 64L(1), 64M(1), 64N(1)[^c-5106cef0980de4e39bc401a3eb0baf4d] and paragraph 12(4)(b) of schedule 3[^M_F_d685489a-58a6-4b25-ab05-a3c304950129] are subject to the affirmative procedure.

(4) Regulations under sections 6A(3)(b), 6B(3)(b),[^c-5b7cefb8f18e6eb756409b336a1ab5a7] 17, 22, 24, 34, 43 , 43L(3)(b), 43N(3), 43Q(2)(b), 43S(4)(a), 48A(1)(b)(ii) and (c)(ii)(B), 48A(3)(c)[^c-4ac5e17e249c11da60cefed0a5db03a0] , 61 , 62, 64B(1)(b), 64B(3)(d), 64C(3), 64I(1)(b) and (3)(d) and 64J(3)[^c-4f18b5ff7963b25428444ebfbc85d32d] are subject to the negative procedure.
