## 85. Vesting of income received by debtor after sequestration[^key-062eb50364012eae06f8c61308ff5a44]

*Extent: S*

(1) Any income, of whatever nature, received by the debtor on a relevant date, other than income arising from the estate which is vested in the trustee in the sequestration, is to vest in the debtor.

(2) But subsection (1) is subject to sections 90 to 97.
