## 45. Procedure where no statutory meeting called[^key-5d74b3930162c8d38d4303acfb21df7b]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the trustee in the sequestration does not call the statutory meeting and the 7 days mentioned in section 44(5) expire, the trustee must forthwith make a report to AiB on the circumstance of the sequestration.

(2) But subsection (1) does not apply if AiB is the trustee.
