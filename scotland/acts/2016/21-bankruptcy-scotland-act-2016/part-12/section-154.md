## 154. Assets discovered after discharge of trustee: appeal[^c-d7206ea6ce25476a46e608f5050daa84]

*Extent: S*

Where AiB makes or refuses to make an appointment or reappointment under section 152, an interested person may, within 14 days after AiB's decision, appeal to the sheriff against that decision.
