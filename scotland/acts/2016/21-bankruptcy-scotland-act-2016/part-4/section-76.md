## 76. Commissioners[^c-610fbd8d4c583fedc133b03d791129cd]

*Extent: S*

In any sequestration where AiB is not the trustee[^c-dcf419ada714bed13ed1379092178b4b] there may be elected, in accordance with section 77, commissioners, whose general functions are—

- (a) to supervise the intromissions of the trustee in the sequestration with the sequestrated estate, and
- (b) to advise the trustee.
