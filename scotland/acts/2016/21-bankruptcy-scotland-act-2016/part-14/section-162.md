## 162. Voluntary trust deeds for creditors[^key-0584738965bffc04c49c0205a1e193b8]

*Extent: S*

Sections 163 to 193 and schedule 4 have effect in relation to voluntary trust deeds executed on or after the date on which this Part comes into force.
