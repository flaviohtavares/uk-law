# Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 2016, section 176

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2025-06-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2016/21
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 176. Dividend payments[^key-61d3c020b8ccede7b298dfcfed03babd]

*Extent: S*

(1) If the funds of the debtor's estate are sufficient, the trustee must pay a dividend out of it to the creditors no later than 6 weeks after the end of—

- (a) a first dividend period of 12[^key-681be301ca66e07714f7643e33eadbf9] months beginning with the date on which the trust deed is granted, and
- (b) any subsequent dividend period of 3[^key-d4402e9ec73ce5becfd630c0caf2abcc] months beginning with the end of the previous dividend period.

(2) The funds of the debtor's estate are “sufficient” if, after—

- (a) deduction of the trustee's fees and of any outlays payable under this Part, and
- (b) making allowance for future contingencies,

a dividend may be paid to the creditors amounting to at least 5 pence for each pound sterling of the debtor's debt, as at the date of protection, under the trust deed.
