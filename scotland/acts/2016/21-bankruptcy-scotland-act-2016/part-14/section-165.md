## 165. Protected status: the trustee[^key-f3d721185097958f7910dc170ca78ec9]

*Extent: S*

The trustee under the trust deed must be a person who would not be disqualified under section 49(3) to (5) from acting as the replacement trustee were the debtor's estate being sequestrated.
