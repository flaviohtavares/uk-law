## 169. Notice in register of insolvencies[^key-c94eeb893c0e211ef2f38a9bd9acee64]

*Extent: S*

After the trust deed has been delivered to the trustee, the trustee must without delay send a notice in such form as may be prescribed for the purposes of this section to AiB for publication by registration in the register of insolvencies.
