## 12. Petition for sequestration of estate: provision of information[^c-1681b9959c493d5f112e07c4776a1f98]

*Extent: S*

(1) A petitioner for sequestration of the estate of a debtor is, in so far as it is within the petitioner's knowledge, to state in the petition—

- (a) whether or not the debtor's centre of main interests is situated in the United Kingdom or in a member State (other than Denmark)[^c-8b8246a1e6345aaea33cc1f70f602220], and
- (b)[^c-ca518c23dd719411b1e82f799930f8c6] if the debtor's centre of main interests is situated in a member State (other than Denmark), whether or not the debtor possesses an establishment in the United Kingdom.
