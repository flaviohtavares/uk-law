# Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 2016, section 221

## 221. Outlays of insolvency practitioner in actings as interim trustee or trustee[^key-bdd5cf715f84cce95acf26562a8f9ef9]

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers may, by regulations, provide for the premium (or a proportionate part of the premium) of any bond of caution or other security required, for the time being, to be given by an insolvency practitioner to be taken into account as part of the outlays of the practitioner in the practitioner's actings as an interim trustee or as trustee in the sequestration.
