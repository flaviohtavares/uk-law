## 217. Meetings of creditors and commissioners[^key-01fa04385fa879bd5d9897fb9a2c498f]

*Extent: S*

Part 1 of schedule 6 has effect in relation to meetings of creditors other than the statutory meeting, Part 2 in relation to all meetings of creditors and Part 3 in relation to meetings of commissioners.
