## 214. Review of decision by Accountant in Bankruptcy: grounds of appeal[^key-b74df696963f8c9993de54423d9f0a6a]

*Extent: S*

(1) For the avoidance of doubt, an appeal under a provision mentioned in subsection (2) may be made on—

- (a) a matter of fact,
- (b) a point of law, or
- (c) the merits.

(2) The provisions are—

- (a) section 27(8),
- (b) section 37(5),
- (c) section 52(7),
- (d) section 57(8),
- (e) section 59(4),
- (f) section 61(8),
- (g) section 64(8),
- (h) section 65(7),
- (i) section 68(4),
- (j) section 71(4),
- (k) section 73(5),
- (l) section 92(5),
- (m) section 97(5),
- (n) section 110(7),
- (o) section 127(5),
- (p) section 139(6),
- (q) section 144(6),
- (qa)[^key-729daf32b427007e30760f9185b353f9] section 147B(5),
- (r) section 149(4),
- (s) section 151(7),
- (t) section 161(8),
- (u) section 213(4), and
- (v) paragraph 3(9) of schedule 2.
