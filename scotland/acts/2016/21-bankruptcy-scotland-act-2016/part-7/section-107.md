## 107. References in Part 7 to “the 1889 Act” and to “the 1999 Act”[^key-02ad40b24ce825e00f1dc21fbfe81386]

*Extent: S*

In this Part, references—

- to “the 1889 Act” are to the Judicial Factors (Scotland) Act 1889, and
- to “the 1999 Act” are to the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999.
