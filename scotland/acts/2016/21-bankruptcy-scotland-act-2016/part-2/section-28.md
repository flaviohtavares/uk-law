## 28. Benefit from another estate[^c-5ed2c0fbbe470d560e526c40fb577338]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a debtor learns, whether before or after the date of sequestration, that the debtor may derive benefit from another estate, the debtor must as soon as practicable after that date inform—

- (a) the trustee in the sequestration, of that fact, and
- (b) the person who is administering that other estate, of the sequestration.

(2) A debtor who fails to comply with subsection (1) commits an offence.

(3) A debtor who commits an offence under subsection (2) is liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
