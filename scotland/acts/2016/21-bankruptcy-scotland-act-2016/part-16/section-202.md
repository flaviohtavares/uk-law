## 202. Further duty of Accountant in Bankruptcy[^c-3a651ed3b51c42776eb0389c3dd12502]

*Extent: S*

AiB is, on receiving any notice under section 109(1) of the Insolvency Act 1986 in relation to a community interest company, to forward a copy of that notice to the Regulator of Community Interest Companies.
