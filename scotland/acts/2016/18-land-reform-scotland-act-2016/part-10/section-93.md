# Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2016, section 93

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 93. Repairing tenancies: subletting

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The 2003 Act is amended as follows.

(2) After section 7B (as inserted by section 105) insert—

> **7C. Subletting of repairing tenancies**
>
> (1) During the repairing period, a tenant may not sublet the land comprised in a lease constituting a repairing tenancy without the consent of the landlord.
>
> (2) After the expiry of the repairing period, a tenant may sublet the land comprised in a lease constituting a repairing tenancy only on such basis as the lease expressly permits.
