## 98. Repairing tenancies: compensation[^key-711b62d9d09531c80d707e357ff4965a]

*Extent: S*

(1) The 2003 Act is amended as follows.

(2) After section 59 insert—

> **59A. Compensation under repairing tenancies**
>
> The Scottish Ministers may by regulations provide that Part 4, in its application to repairing tenancies, has effect with such modifications as the regulations may specify.
