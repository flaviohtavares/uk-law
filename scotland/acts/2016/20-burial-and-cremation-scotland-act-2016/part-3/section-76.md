# Burial and Cremation (Scotland) Act 2016, section 76

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 76. Section 74: general power of appropriate health body[^c-f14a11c7e7e82541455c1c65f33b16e0]

*Extent: S*

(1) This section applies where it appears to the appropriate health body that no arrangements have been or are being made under section 74(2) or (4) for the remains of a still-born child to be buried , cremated or hydrolysed[^c-666d293f9c536d152481c476bf6b775c].

(2) The appropriate health body may make arrangements for the remains to be buried , cremated or hydrolysed[^c-dfa563bee0ea29f1d60f295f091c837d].

(3) In this section, “**appropriate health body**” has the meaning given by section 74(10).
