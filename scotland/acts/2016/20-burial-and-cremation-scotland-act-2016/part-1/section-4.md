# Burial and Cremation (Scotland) Act 2016, section 4

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 4. Provision of burial ground outwith local authority area[^c-1c88713d999653abe66772fb84e67bcc]

*Extent: S*

A local authority may provide a burial ground that is situated wholly or partly outwith the area of the authority.
