# Burial and Cremation (Scotland) Act 2016, section 18

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 18. Registers under section 17: offence[^key-d3019dbb33566a83bc34edc7f2c6f20c]

*Extent: S*

(1) A burial authority commits an offence if, without reasonable excuse, the authority contravenes section 17(2) by failing to prepare or maintain a register as required under that section.

(2) A burial authority which commits an offence under subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
