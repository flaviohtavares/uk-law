# Burial and Cremation (Scotland) Act 2016, section 25

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 25. Private burial register: offence

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A local authority commits an offence if, without reasonable excuse, the authority contravenes section 23(1) by failing to prepare or maintain a private burial register.

(2) A local authority which commits an offence under subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
