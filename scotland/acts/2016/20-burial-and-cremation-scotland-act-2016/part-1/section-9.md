# Burial and Cremation (Scotland) Act 2016, section 9

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 9. Unauthorised burial: offences[^key-a707a9bf2ecdc83b136b9e14b4c4b5f6]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person commits an offence if the person contravenes section 8(1) by knowingly carrying out a burial in respect of which no application has been granted.

(2) A person commits an offence if the person—

- (a) provides information in, or in connection with, an application mentioned in section 8(1) which the person knows to be false or misleading in a material way, or
- (b) recklessly provides information in, or in connection with, such an application which is false or misleading in a material way.

(3) A person who commits an offence under subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale or to both.

(4) A person who commits an offence under subsection (2) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
