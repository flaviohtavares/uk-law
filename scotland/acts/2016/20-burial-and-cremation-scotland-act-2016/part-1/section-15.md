## 15. Commonwealth War Graves Commission: right of burial[^c-e0abafcdcb571135844af7d9c15a9113]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subsection (2) applies where a burial authority sells a right of burial to the Commonwealth War Graves Commission.

(2) The right subsists in perpetuity.
