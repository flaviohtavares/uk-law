# Burial and Cremation (Scotland) Act 2016, section 26

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 26. Burial other than in burial ground: offence

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) It is an offence for a person knowingly to bury human remains unless—

- (a) the burial is carried out in a burial ground in accordance with section 8, or
- (b) the burial is a private burial carried out in accordance with section 22.

(2) A person who commits an offence under subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale or to both.
