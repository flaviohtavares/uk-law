# Burial and Cremation (Scotland) Act 2016, section 92

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 92. Section 91: offences[^c-6ac842231aa28348aeb1ec40fbb420cb]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person commits an offence if the person fails, without reasonable excuse, to comply with a requirement made by an inspector under section 91(1)(b) to produce a document, record or register.

(2) A person commits an offence if the person wilfully obstructs an inspector in the exercise of—

- (a) the power of entry conferred under section 91(1)(a),
- (b) the power to inspect or take copies of documents, records or registers conferred under section 91(1)(c), or
- (c) a power conferred by virtue of regulations under section 90.

(3) A person who commits an offence under subsection (1) or (2) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
