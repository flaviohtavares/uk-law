# Burial and Cremation (Scotland) Act 2016, section 104

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 104. Regulations: consultation requirements

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-15dc89dcf276fe3d7ae4bb2f4d12d051][^c-5cba07e2337c3cd8ce3b92987b32c8b5] Before making any regulations under section 6(1), 8(2), 10(1) or (2), 33(3)(b) or (c) or (4), 34(2) or (3), 35(3), 36(4)(b) or 42, the Scottish Ministers must consult—

- (a) burial authorities, and
- (b) any other persons the Scottish Ministers consider appropriate.

(2)[^c-5c5bc99ff5b566db3ed30a36111d8e88] Before making any regulations under section 22(1) or 23(2), the Scottish Ministers must consult—

- (a) local authorities, and
- (b) any other persons the Scottish Ministers consider appropriate.

(3)[^c-5c5bc99ff5b566db3ed30a36111d8e88] Before making any regulations under section 27(1), the Scottish Ministers must consult—

- (a) burial authorities,
- (b) cremation authorities,
- (ba)[^c-f9a23173c2d5760e1f7bf2e43be8a457] hydrolysis authorities,
- (c) inspectors, and
- (d) any other persons the Scottish Ministers consider appropriate.

(4)[^c-5c5bc99ff5b566db3ed30a36111d8e88] Before making any regulations under Part 2, the Scottish Ministers must consult—

- (a)[^c-b60b93f3dbb583be68051cf261c0a9b6] either (or both)—
  - (i) cremation authorities (if the regulations relate to cremation),
  - (ii) hydrolysis authorities (if the regulations relate to hydrolysis),
- (b) any other persons the Scottish Ministers consider appropriate.

(5)[^c-5c5bc99ff5b566db3ed30a36111d8e88] Before making any regulations under section 90, the Scottish Ministers must consult—

- (a) burial authorities,
- (b) cremation authorities,
- (ba)[^c-684b32dfafa0f70c9a640a4017338f22] hydrolysis authorities,
- (c) persons appearing to the Scottish Ministers to be representative of the interests of funeral directors in Scotland, and
- (d) any other persons the Scottish Ministers consider appropriate.

(6)[^c-5c5bc99ff5b566db3ed30a36111d8e88] Before making any regulations under section 99(1), the Scottish Ministers must consult any persons they consider appropriate.
