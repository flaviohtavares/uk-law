# Local Electoral Administration and Registration Services (Scotland) Act 2006, section 51

> Text as at 2022-09-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 51. Civil partnership procedure: registrations at sea

*Extent: S*

In section 93 of the 2004 Act (place of registration)—

- (a) in subsection (2), for the words from “outwith” to the end there is substituted

  > —
  >
  > - (a) in a registration district other than that of the authorised registrar carrying out the registration, or
  > - (b) in or on Scottish Waters.

  ; and
- (b) after subsection (4) there is inserted—

  > (5) “**Scottish Waters**” has the meaning given by section 26(2) of the Marriage (Scotland) Act 1977 (c. 15).

  .
