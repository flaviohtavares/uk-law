# Local Electoral Administration and Registration Services (Scotland) Act 2006, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2022-09-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Part 1: minor and consequential modifications

*Extent: S*

(introduced by section 35)

### Representation of the People Act 1983 (c. 2)

**1.**[^c20352561] The 1983 Act is amended as follows.

**2.**[^key-10b44016faae12ec6115958fc315d75f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**Not yet in force.**

**3.** In section 73(5)(d) (payment of expenses through election agent), for “section 90A(5)(b)” substitute “ section 90ZB(7) ”.

**Not yet in force.**

**4.** In section 74A (expenses which may be paid otherwise than by election agent)—

- (a) in subsection (1)(b), for “section 90A(1)” substitute “ section 90ZB(2) ”,
- (b) in subsection (2), the words “(determined in accordance with section 90B below)” are repealed,
- (c) in subsection (3), for “sections 90A to” substitute “ section 90ZB and ”.

**5.**[^c20352581] In section 76A(2) (application of power to vary provisions about election expenses)—

- (a) the word “or” immediately following paragraph (c) is repealed,
- (b) after paragraph (c) insert—

  > - (ca) section 75A(5) above; or

  .

**6.** In section 81 (return as to election expenses)—

- (a) in subsection (2)(c), after “section 75(2)” insert “ or 75A(6) ”,
- (b) in subsection (9)—
  - (i) for “subsection (3)” substitute “ subsection (4A) ”, and
  - (ii) the words “of each such matter” are repealed.

**7.**[^c20352591] In section 89 (inspection of returns and declarations)—

- (a) in subsection (1), after “section 75,” insert “ 75A, ”,
- (b) in subsection (3), after “section 75” insert “ or 75A ”.

**8.** In section 90C(2) (property, goods, services etc. provided free of charge or at a discount), for “section 90A(3)” substitute “ section 90ZB(4) ”.

**Not yet in force.**

**9.** In section 118 (interpretation of Part 2), in the definition of “election expenses” for “sections 90A” substitute “ sections 90ZB ”.

**10.**[^c20352601] In section 129(7)(b) (time for presentation or amendment of petition questioning local election), after “section 75” insert “ or 75A ”.

**11.** In section 168(1)(a)(i) (prosecution for corrupt practices), before “above” insert “ or 62B ”.

**12.**[^c20352611] In section 179 (offences by associations), after “section 110” insert “ or 110A ”.

**13.**[^c20352621] The amendments made by paragraphs 3, 4, 6(b), 8 and 9 apply to the 1983 Act only so far as it applies to a local government election in Scotland.

### Scottish Local Government (Elections) Act 2002 (asp 1)

**14.**[^c20352631] In section 5(4) of the Scottish Local Government (Elections) Act 2002, for “75(1)” substitute “ 75A(2) ”
