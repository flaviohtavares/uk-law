# Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006, section 49

> Text as at 2021-06-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 49. Interpretation of Part 9[^key-c724eef82c62542f2c7aaa476b6b3713][^key-2224dc30752de878bcea028aa0b6fa4d]

*Extent: S*

In this Part—

- “**BID arrangements**” has the meaning given by section 33;
- “**BID proposals**” has the meaning given by section 38;
- “**business improvement district**” has the meaning given by section 33;
- “**local authority**” means a council constituted under section 2 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994 (c. 39); and
- “**prescribed**” means prescribed by regulations made by statutory instrument by the Scottish Ministers.
