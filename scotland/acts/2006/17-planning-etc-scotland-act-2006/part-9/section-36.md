# Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006, section 36

> Text as at 2021-06-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 36. Duty to comply with arrangements[^c-41e4cd11e4b8f395c835a9817172d973][^c-d149be0ec87d659be73da250f4426085]

*Extent: S*

Where BID arrangements are in force, the local authority which made the arrangements must comply with them.
