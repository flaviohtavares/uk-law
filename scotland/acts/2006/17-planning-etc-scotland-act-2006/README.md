# Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006

> Text as at 2021-06-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006

2006 asp 17

An Act of the Scottish Parliament to make further provision relating to town and country planning; to make provision for business improvement districts; and for connected purposes.

The Bill for this Act of the Scottish Parliament was passed by the Parliament on 16th November 2006 and received Royal Assent on 20th December 2006
