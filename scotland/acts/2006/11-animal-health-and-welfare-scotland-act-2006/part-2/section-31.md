## 31. Offering animals as prizes[^key-99519ad4c337ca227de1cfa87da46d02]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person commits an offence if the person offers or gives an animal to another person as a prize.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply where the prize is offered or given in a family context.
