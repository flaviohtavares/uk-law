# Housing (Scotland) Act 2006, section 111

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2006/1

## 111. Penalty charge notices[^c1897831]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-9f12c92d39cbdd18cff39b282975f714] An authorised officer of an enforcement authority may, if the officer believes that a person has breached any duty under section 98, 99(1), 101(2) or 103(2), give a penalty charge notice to that person.

(2)[^c-9f12c92d39cbdd18cff39b282975f714] A penalty charge notice may not be given after the end of the period of 6 months beginning with the day on which it appeared to the officer that the duty was breached.

(3)[^c-ca69433c94564a8ec08e4d09f7e43fb0] Schedule 3 (which makes further provision about penalty charge notices) has effect.

(4)[^c-9f12c92d39cbdd18cff39b282975f714] The Scottish Ministers may by regulations make further provision about penalty charge notices or any other notice mentioned in schedule 3.

(5)[^c-9f12c92d39cbdd18cff39b282975f714] Such regulations may, in particular, include provision prescribing—

- (a) the form of penalty charge notices or any other notice mentioned in that schedule,
- (b) circumstances in which penalty charge notices may not be given,
- (c) the methods by which penalty charge notices or any other notice must be given,
- (d) the method or methods by which penalty charges may be paid.
