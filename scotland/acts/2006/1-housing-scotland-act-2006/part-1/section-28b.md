## 28B. Landlord application to the First-tier Tribunal: further provision[^c-90253b709408a3d4b72e05ae380e78cf][^M_F_08070170-1d78-4c89-aa15-cd3201071956]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may by regulations make further provision about the making or deciding of applications under section 28A.

(2) Those regulations may, in particular, make provision—

- (a) about the form and content of applications and notices,
- (b) prescribing a fee to accompany applications,
- (c) specifying circumstances when the First-tier Tribunal[^c-93341a3de97a2dd8de996ab27c8833aa] member must decide to reject an application or stop assisting a landlord,
- (d) about the procedure for—
  - (i) making decisions under section 28A(3) or (7),
  - (ii) giving notice under section 28A(5),
  - (iii) making representations under section 28A(6).

(3) In this section, “**the First-tier Tribunal[^c-7d3c821d3b186d948b2806a7a5052dd2] member**” means the member of the First-tier Tribunal[^c-c26eb5b96836bf8f6fc383ca45fb41cf] to whom the case has been allocated under section 28A(2).
