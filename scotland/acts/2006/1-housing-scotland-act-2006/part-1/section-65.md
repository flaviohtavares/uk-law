# Housing (Scotland) Act 2006, section 65

## 65. Part 1 appeals: determination[^c1897671]

*Extent: S*

(1) The sheriff, in determining an appeal under 64(1), may—

- (a) confirm the decision (and any work notice, demolition notice, demand for recovery of expenses or maintenance order served, or maintenance plan approved, devised or varied, in consequence of it),
- (b) quash the decision (and any such notice, demand, order or plan), or
- (c) make such other order as the sheriff thinks just.

(2) The Upper Tribunal[^key-1a04d5e231e799ea2b1a618f43a1f354] may determine an appeal under section 64(4) , (4A)[^key-225817377262d7e01cbe265925ecaa23] or (5) by—

- (a) confirming the decision (and any order or variation made, or certificate granted, in consequence of it),
- (b) remitting the decision (together with the Upper Tribunal’s[^key-01570616caaf890ab93368bc83edc521] reasons for doing so) to the Chamber President[^key-1fcb0ca6fc28c174e67d7577df677e43] or, as the case may be, the First-tier Tribunal for reconsideration, or
- (c) quashing the decision (and any order or variation made, or certificate granted, in consequence of it).

(3)[^key-e287ad021a4853b68559bf7909225824] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-3c8787be75030962faa09ca3827275c0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) The sheriff's determination or Upper Tribunal’s decision[^key-b68deaec3c810bf601854ba83680052f] on an appeal under section 64 is final (subject to subsection (6)).

(6)[^key-3014de6a82fa32a420b1e5a2f1f01b0e] The sheriff's determination on an appeal under paragraph (a), (b), (c)(i), (d)(i) or (g) of section 64(1) may be appealed to the sheriff principal within 21 days of the sheriff's determination; and the sheriff principal's decision on any such appeal is final.
