## 26. Effect of failure to comply with repairing standard enforcement order

*Extent: S*

(1) It is for the First-tier Tribunal[^key-a1d4828c7b1dfc03659bfddd461571f4] to decide whether a landlord has complied with a repairing standard enforcement order made by the First-tier Tribunal[^key-b4448a454a303bb44ef84dfea6b7ace2].

(2) Where the First-tier Tribunal decides[^key-538900b72a03bc8b0cfce12341670b9f] that a landlord has failed to comply with the repairing standard enforcement order, the First-tier Tribunal[^key-94846b73a110ef87e36f1946ef8daef5] must—

- (a) serve notice of the failure on the local authority, and
- (b) decide whether to make a rent relief order.

(3) The First-tier Tribunal[^key-b86031457ec78b58719356f413bffbb5] may not decide that a landlord has failed to comply with a repairing standard enforcement order—

- (a) unless the period within which the order requires the work to be completed has ended, or
- (b) if the First-tier Tribunal is[^key-c0a5e2df761804e61acefed3b0539309] satisfied, on the submission of the landlord or otherwise—
  - (i) that the landlord is unable to comply with the order because of a lack of necessary rights (of access or otherwise) despite having taken reasonable steps for the purposes of acquiring those rights, or
  - (ii) that the work required by the order is likely to endanger any person.

(4) Where the First-tier Tribunal is[^key-4b5900484837c4fa228dc4cae8975356] prevented by reason only of subsection (3)(b) from deciding that a landlord has failed to comply with a repairing standard enforcement order, the First-tier Tribunal[^key-a8d4f4a55609098fb969e22001b77854] must serve notice on the local authority stating that it considers[^key-e2f7f26e5dea3055442c4f1e5f1b02de] the landlord to be unable to comply with the repairing standard enforcement order.
