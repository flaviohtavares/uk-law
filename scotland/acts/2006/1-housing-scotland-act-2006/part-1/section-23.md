## 23. Consideration of applications[^c-511cd9a4eb4009ca375c28c10ebca7da]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Chamber President[^c-5aa7817b1e10e67963762fda1dba06ff] must decide whether to—

- (a) refer an application under section 22(1) or 22(1A)[^c-15877a11df51f7660710116a04b5c4d2] to the First-tier Tribunal[^c-9729dd1be6c476e4039c4337d173f7b9] , or
- (b) reject the application.

(2) The Chamber President[^c-7f097c1e30ca2c414a43d58b193df5e1] may reject an application only if the Chamber President considers—

- (a) that it is vexatious or frivolous,
- (b) where the tenant or third party applicant[^c-e6de1e66c8c85e7ed92ca7b9a2b4a5a2] has previously made an identical or substantially similar application in relation to the same house, that there has not been a reasonable period of time between the applications, or
- (c) that the dispute to which the application relates has been resolved.

(3) The Chamber President[^c-472b49b42a10f083b046f1fd65d9899e] must make a decision under subsection (1)—

- (a) within 14 days of the First-tier Tribunal’s[^c-b2292f94433f15bb9ac2c5e5b8726ee2] receipt of the application concerned, or
- (b) where the Chamber President[^c-d97a868d4ec53e385669f380fee53ba6] considers—
  - (i) that the decision cannot be made without further information, or
  - (ii) that there is a reasonable prospect of the dispute being resolved by the parties,

  by such later date as the Chamber President[^c-8a4adcaf86b08e9650e5c4f4b8b28f01] considers reasonable.

(4) The Chamber President[^c-1cef396e1e73e70cd74faa2359ef02df] must, as soon as practicable after rejecting an application under section 22(1)[^c-3a6d406b44ee550410adefb6428f7a72] give notice of the rejection—

- (a) to the tenant, and
- (b) where the Chamber President is aware of the name and address of a person who acts for the tenant in relation to the application, to that person.

(4A)[^c-ac366562093e58a38151310684e78b24] The Chamber President[^c-a5ba115c098cfc61da620093603bdbd6] must, as soon as practicable after rejecting an application under section 22(1A) give notice of the rejection to—

- (a) the third party applicant, and
- (b) the tenant.

(5) A notice under subsection (4) or (4A)[^c-c665fc4ced523fba4d74f3654a790d93] must—

- (a) set out the reasons for the rejection, and
- (b) explain the procedure for appealing against it.
