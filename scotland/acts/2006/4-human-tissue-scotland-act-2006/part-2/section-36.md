# Human Tissue (Scotland) Act 2006, section 36

> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 36. Organ or tissue sample removed before day on which section 27 comes into force[^c-32f2d76b1b897e39c3f32a30c82d68b8]

*Extent: S*

An organ or tissue sample—

- (a) removed from the body of a deceased person during an examination having the characteristics of a post-mortem examination carried out before the day on which section 27 comes into force; and
- (b) held immediately before that day for use for any purpose referred to in paragraphs (a) to (d) of section 23,

may be retained and used for any such purpose.
