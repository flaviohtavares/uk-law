# Human Tissue (Scotland) Act 2006, section 27

> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 27. Requirements for carrying out post-mortem examination[^key-159ed08ed659baf08918bb70a4e919e0]

*Extent: S*

A post-mortem examination may be carried out only if—

- (a) it is authorised in accordance with section 29, 30, 31, 32 or, as the case may be 33; and
- (b) the requirements of section 35 are satisfied.
