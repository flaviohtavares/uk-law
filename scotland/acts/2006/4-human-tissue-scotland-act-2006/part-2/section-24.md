> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 24. Disapplication of sections 23 and 27 to 37 as respects procurator fiscal[^c-d9e345f55f4837f14a7634eed91aa5e8]

*Extent: S*

Sections 23 and 27 to 37 do not apply to anything done for the purposes of the functions or under the authority of the procurator fiscal.
