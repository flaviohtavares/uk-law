# Human Tissue (Scotland) Act 2006, section 16D

> Text as at 2022-07-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 16D. Restrictions on carrying out of pre-death procedures[^key-e230affd49b4870a120a0a26ef441258]

*Extent: S*

(1) A pre-death procedure may be carried out only if it is a Type A procedure or Type B procedure.

(2) A Type A procedure may be carried out only in accordance with section 16E.

(3) A Type B procedure may be carried out only in accordance with—

- (a) section 16E, and
- (b) any provision made in regulations under section 16C(1) in relation to that procedure (or category of procedure).
