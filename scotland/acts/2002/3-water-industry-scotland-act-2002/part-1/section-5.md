## 5. Annual reports by, and information from, the Commissioner

*Extent: S*

(1) The Commission[^c-f22ebd7baea9c57f721ccfcc175e836d] must—

- (a) as soon as practicable after the end of each financial year, submit to the Scottish Ministers, and publish, a report on the exercise of the Commission's functions during that year, and
- (b) provide the Scottish Ministers with such information regarding the exercise of those functions as they may require.

(2) A report under subsection (1)(a) must include a summary of—

- (a) action taken by the Commission during the year to which the report relates in response to—[^c-ab40676e7b132e6f6cee2a000bf4279f]
  - (i) any advice, information or representation made to it by[^c-17908d0b8bded148045cb7cf971b17c8] Citizens Advice Scotland[^c-e2644efd8c1620574f676376eafdbb4d] under section 8(1)(a) or (c) of the Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007 (representative functions) , and
  - (ii) any proposals made to it under section 8(1)(b) of that Act[^c-fe246187e0fccf78d5504897c84739d7] , and
- (b) where the Commission has decided during that year not to take any action in response to any such advice, information, proposal or[^c-4402290da666032a21435404468bba7c] representation [^c-5e1548d3ca1d032cc4045fe02a1e08a9]... [^c-41c7b12c9fa2607bcdf895cec2363532]..., the reasons for that decision.

(3) A report under subsection (1)(a) must set out any directions under section 1 which have been given to the Commission during the period to which the report relates.

(4)[^c-7c42f39eadf7cb014cf5bdbf21f4c7df] The Scottish Ministers must lay before the Parliament a copy of each report submitted to them under subsection (1)(a).
