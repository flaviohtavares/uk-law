# Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002, section 2A

## 2A. Citizens Advice Scotland representations[^c-ebe873b80fc4f34229594d8b0246f6b1][^c-454781ec11958b253b1a9a63401c36f5]

*Extent: S*

(1) This section applies where Citizens Advice Scotland[^c-f46d626f49b82249819eba49d92e7bfc] has exercised a function under section 8(1) of the Consumers, Estate Agents and Redress Act 2007 (c. 17) (representative functions) in relation to the activities of Scottish Water.

(2) The persons listed in subsection (4) must have regard to any advice, information, proposal or representation made to them by Citizens Advice Scotland[^c-9b57fa5febcd53f37fbbca4bc4cf896a] under section 8(1) of that Act.

(3) Any persons listed in subsection (4) to whom a proposal is made under section 8(1)(b) of that Act must, within 6 months of receipt, publish a summary of their responses to the proposal.

(4) The persons are—

- (a) the Scottish Ministers,
- (b) Scottish Water,
- (c) the Water Industry Commission,
- (d) the Drinking Water Quality Regulator for Scotland,
- (e) the Scottish Environment Protection Agency.
