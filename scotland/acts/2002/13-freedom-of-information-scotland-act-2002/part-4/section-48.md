# Freedom of Information (Scotland) Act 2002, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2002/13

> Extent: Scotland.

## 48. When application excluded[^c20322291]

*Extent: S*

No application may be made to the Commissioner for a decision under section 47(1) as respects a request for review made to—

- (a) the Commissioner;
- (b) a procurator fiscal; or
- (c) the Lord Advocate, to the extent that the information requested is held by the Lord Advocate as head of the systems of criminal prosecution and investigation of deaths in Scotland.
