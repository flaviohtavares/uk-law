# Freedom of Information (Scotland) Act 2002, section 39

## 39. Health, safety and the environment

*Extent: S*

(1) Information is exempt information if its disclosure under this Act would, or would be likely to, endanger the physical or mental health or the safety of an individual.

(2)[^c20303461] Information is exempt information if a Scottish public authority—

- (a) is obliged by regulations under section 62 to make it available to the public in accordance with the regulations; or
- (b) would be so obliged but for any exemption contained in the regulations.

(3)[^c20303461] Subsection (2)(a) is without prejudice to the generality of section 25(1).
