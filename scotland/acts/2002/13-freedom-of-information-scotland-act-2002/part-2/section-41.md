# Freedom of Information (Scotland) Act 2002, section 41

## 41. Communications with Her Majesty etc. and honours

*Extent: S*

Information is exempt information if it relates to—

- (a) communications with Her Majesty, with other members of the Royal Family or with the Royal Household; or
- (b) the exercise by Her Majesty of Her prerogative of honour.
