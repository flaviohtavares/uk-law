# Debt Arrangement and Attachment (Scotland) Act 2002, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2024-01-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2002/17
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3. Money advice[^key-b26fa9961bdad1093829236cad5071f6][^key-c560863d82d474f4f3161475f6d09fd5][^key-c560863d82d474f4f3161475f6d09fd5]

*Extent: S*

(1) A debtor is not entitled to make an application for the approval, or the variation, of a debt payment programme unless the debtor has obtained the advice of a money adviser in relation to—

- (a) the debtor’s financial circumstances;
- (b) the effect of the proposed programme or, as the case may be, the proposed variation of the programme; and
- (c) the preparation of the application.

(2) Such an application shall—

- (a) contain a [^key-5ad9bcbf516643f814d8f44d11228446]... declaration by the money adviser who provided the advice referred to in subsection (1) above that such advice has been given; and
- (b) specify the name and address of the money adviser.

(3)[^key-5a530990c932beef9b9558e091b2076d] Subsections (1) and (2) above are subject to any contrary provision in regulations made under section 7(1) below.
