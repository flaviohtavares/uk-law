# Debt Arrangement and Attachment (Scotland) Act 2002, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2024-01-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2002/17
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1. Debt arrangement scheme[^c-baf787fed79666183ac85228c2de9c3b][^c-c560863d82d474f4f3161475f6d09fd5][^c-c560863d82d474f4f3161475f6d09fd5]

*Extent: S*

This Part of this Act constitutes a scheme (to be known as the “**debt arrangement scheme**”) under which persons[^c-c8b04251146530ca3052837a175c6844] may arrange for their debts to be paid under debt payment programmes.
