# Debt Arrangement and Attachment (Scotland) Act 2002, section 2

## 2. Debt payment programmes[^c-c560863d82d474f4f3161475f6d09fd5]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-68de51fb4817adf448690eaded9b4c43] A debt payment programme is a programme which provides for the payment of money owed by a debtor.

(1A)[^c-07e03f7a37bf3c7ab36f52234b16cedf] Subsection (1) above is subject to any provision in regulations made under section 7A(1) below.

(2)[^c-68de51fb4817adf448690eaded9b4c43] The Scottish Ministers may, on an application by a debtor, approve any debt payment programme set out in the application.

(3)[^c-2a28033effd221b7bb448b88e569b75f][^c-50c71ce59d5ba7d22eb95723d17fce61] Such an application [^c-eab05510cabd20f2cddd81a81acb217a]... shall—

- (a) specify, to the best of the debtor’s knowledge and belief, in relation to each debt which the debtor is proposing to be paid under the debt payment programme—
  - (i) the amount outstanding;
  - (ii) the creditor to whom the debt is due; and
  - (iii) the period for which the debt has been due;
- (b) set out the arrangements under which those debts are, in accordance with the provisions of the programme, to be paid, in particular specifying—
  - (i) the amounts which the debtor proposes to pay under the programme;
  - (ii) the proposed regularity of those payments;
  - (iii) the manner in which those payments are to be made; and
  - (iv) the manner in which, and period over which, each of the debts included in the programme is to be paid;
- (c) specify the name and address of the person (the “**payments distributor**”) who is to—
  - (i) receive payments from the debtor; and
  - (ii) pay, on behalf of the debtor, the debts included in the programme,

  in accordance with the provisions of the programme; and
- (d) contain such other information (including information relating to the debtor’s financial circumstances), and be in such form, as may be prescribed.

(4)[^c-2a28033effd221b7bb448b88e569b75f][^c-50c71ce59d5ba7d22eb95723d17fce61] Such an application shall, subject to any contrary provision in regulations made under section 7(1) or 7A(1)[^c-ef3f7a1e64325cd0fc1059c28f420a86] below, incorporate the consent, indicated in the prescribed form, of all the debtor’s creditors.

(5)[^c-68de51fb4817adf448690eaded9b4c43] A person’s name and address shall not be specified in an application for approval of a debt payment programme as a payments distributor unless that person has been approved by the Scottish Ministers as a person suitable to carry out the functions of a payments distributor.
