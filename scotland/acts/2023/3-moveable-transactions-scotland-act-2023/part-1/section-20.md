# Moveable Transactions (Scotland) Act 2023, section 20

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 20. The parts of the register[^key-3854d070b62ca967112c684225daaf93][^key-3a6c4bbd698dfcd1df3fd55555941dbd][^key-d70f3958abcd6e5960edc40090b4f363]

*Extent: S*

[^key-d70f3958abcd6e5960edc40090b4f363]

The Keeper must make up and maintain, as parts of the register—

- (a) the assignations record, and
- (b) the archive record.
