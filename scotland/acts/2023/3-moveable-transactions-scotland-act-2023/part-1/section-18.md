# Moveable Transactions (Scotland) Act 2023, section 18

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 18. Saving as respects International Interests in Aircraft Equipment (Cape Town Convention) Regulations 2015[^c-29bdcee7f178ce3e02378d5424779d33][^c-c69316d9fdb00ca9538c2ff9527f129e][^c-d70f3958abcd6e5960edc40090b4f363]

*Extent: S*

[^c-d70f3958abcd6e5960edc40090b4f363]

(1) This Part is without prejudice to the application, as respects the assignment and acquisition of associated rights, of the International Interests in Aircraft Equipment (Cape Town Convention) Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/912](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/912)).

(2) In subsection (1)—

- “**assignment**” has the meaning given by regulation 5, as read with regulation 35, of those regulations, and
- “**associated rights**” has the meaning given by regulation 5 of those regulations.
