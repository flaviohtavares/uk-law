# Moveable Transactions (Scotland) Act 2023, section 31

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 31. Proceedings involving the accuracy of the assignations record[^key-9aff4ce3e340fbf6bc866e8729de304b][^key-afad08d7516820978e5d499c34bb5976][^key-d70f3958abcd6e5960edc40090b4f363]

*Extent: S*

[^key-d70f3958abcd6e5960edc40090b4f363]

The Keeper is entitled to appear and be heard in any civil proceedings, whether before a court or tribunal, in which—

- (a) the accuracy of the assignations record, or
- (b) what is needed to correct an inaccuracy in the record,

is put in question.
