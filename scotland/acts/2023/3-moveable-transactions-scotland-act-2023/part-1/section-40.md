## 40. Repeal of Transmission of Moveable Property (Scotland) Act 1862[^key-7ca08a1cbc76488d7681baa6136fdc27][^key-542ec1c1f90ba9a2e525b49d22bd886c][^key-d70f3958abcd6e5960edc40090b4f363]
