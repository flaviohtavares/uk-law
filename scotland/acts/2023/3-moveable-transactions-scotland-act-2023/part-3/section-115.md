# Moveable Transactions (Scotland) Act 2023, section 115

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 115. Competence of registration of electronic documents[^key-d8269dbd92846a71444ce85457e2b7a6][^key-442e1da443bc07e51c096259bfd7e74a]

*Extent: S*

Section 9G(1)(d) of the Requirements of Writing (Scotland) Act 1995 (registration and recording of electronic documents) does not apply in relation to the registration of a document by the Keeper under this Act.
