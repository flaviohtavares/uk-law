# Moveable Transactions (Scotland) Act 2023, section 85

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 85. Order in which applications are to be dealt with[^key-8cd0bf5044faa04e846c282ef70ef51f][^key-067b1ce48df05e8070e3c811d4238d92][^key-7b42558dc607329ea231e134b27c67bb]

*Extent: S*

[^key-7b42558dc607329ea231e134b27c67bb]

The Keeper must deal with—

- (a) applications for registration of a statutory pledge under section 86, and
- (b) applications for registration of an amendment to a statutory pledge under section 88,

in the order in which they are received.
