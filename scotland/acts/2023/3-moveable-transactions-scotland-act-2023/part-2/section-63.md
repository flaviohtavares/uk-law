# Moveable Transactions (Scotland) Act 2023, section 63

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 63. The expression “pledge” in sections 64 to 77[^c-b60b6e6ff3d6d47da36f24f38ac60a10][^c-3509f890c430a882b3840b3d57b7dbf2][^c-7b42558dc607329ea231e134b27c67bb]

*Extent: S*

[^c-7b42558dc607329ea231e134b27c67bb]

In sections 64 to 77, the expression “**pledge**” does not include a pledge as defined in section 189(1) of the Consumer Credit Act 1974 (that is to say, does not include a pawnee’s rights over an article taken in pawn).
