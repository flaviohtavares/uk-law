# Moveable Transactions (Scotland) Act 2023, section 103

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 103. Proceedings involving the accuracy of the statutory pledges record[^c-90d82b79491b7c5f8ea8dce8b07bf276][^c-84dadc2f44c7dc3fbc2d7b37d856b7e8][^c-7b42558dc607329ea231e134b27c67bb]

*Extent: S*

[^c-7b42558dc607329ea231e134b27c67bb]

The Keeper is entitled to appear and be heard in any civil proceedings, whether before a court or tribunal, in which—

- (a) the accuracy of the statutory pledges record, or
- (b) what is needed to correct an inaccuracy in the record,

is put in question.
