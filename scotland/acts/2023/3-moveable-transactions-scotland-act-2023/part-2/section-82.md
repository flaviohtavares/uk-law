# Moveable Transactions (Scotland) Act 2023, section 82

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 82. The parts of the register[^key-cc03e3935ab5badda4516c847d7f18ab][^key-f50cf54a44df4379ea81577a47ea4136][^key-7b42558dc607329ea231e134b27c67bb]

*Extent: S*

[^key-7b42558dc607329ea231e134b27c67bb]

The Keeper must make up and maintain, as parts of the register—

- (a) the statutory pledges record, and
- (b) the archive record.
