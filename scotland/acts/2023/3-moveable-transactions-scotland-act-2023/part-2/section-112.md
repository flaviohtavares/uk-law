# Moveable Transactions (Scotland) Act 2023, section 112

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 112. Competence of creating an agricultural charge[^c-0672ef71ace0d1d04eafb86f61cab034][^c-f355b037ecc0ae732a4232557357cbac][^c-7b42558dc607329ea231e134b27c67bb]

*Extent: S*

[^c-7b42558dc607329ea231e134b27c67bb]

On the coming into force of this section, it ceases to be competent to create an agricultural charge (“agricultural charge” having the meaning given by section 5 of the Agricultural Credits (Scotland) Act 1929).
