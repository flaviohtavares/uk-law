## 19. Premises manager

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-47ab413a888d7d72d3c6ce340337be3a] In this Act, “**premises manager**”, in relation to any licensed premises in respect of which a premises licence has effect, means the individual for the time being specified as such in the premises licence.

(2)[^key-31c14f4469f49c1bbdc2fba9d2b30762][^key-46d3cb1891d96bad4ca7f51d13a34b24] An individual may not, at any one time, be the premises manager of more than one licensed premises; and, accordingly, if an individual who is the premises manager of licensed premises is subsequently specified in the premises licence of other licensed premises as the premises manager of those other premises, the subsequent specification is of no effect.
