## 48. Notification of change of name or address[^key-8eb0edc2d5f6d7ca6ee6890e0b249606]

*Extent: S*

(1) A premises licence holder must, not later than one month after the occurrence of any change in—

- (a) the licence holder's name or address, [^key-66b49d74c06d1240ea009d704130a414]...
- (b) the name or address of the premises manager specified in the licence , or[^key-d6e1f9762db2958e20a75dea548fbdd5]
- (c) the name or address of any person who is—
  - (i) a connected person in relation to the licence holder, [^M_F_6e523445-e000-4686-f92d-24a9f39906ea]...
  - (ii)[^M_F_a66acbd0-bb07-4d59-d665-b9f4f4fbd8e1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

give the appropriate Licensing Board notice of the change.

(2) A notice under subsection (1) must be accompanied by the premises licence or, if that is not practicable, by a statement of the reasons for the failure to produce the licence.

(2A)[^key-544864f9c7470a6705e2687d87749b0f] Where a Licensing Board receives a notice under subsection (1), the Board must give a copy of the notice to the chief constable[^key-26a7d779929e0d32870383f3c385a8b1].

(3) A premises licence holder who fails, without reasonable excuse, to comply with subsection (1), commits an offence.

(4) A person guilty of an offence under subsection (3) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 2 on the standard scale.
