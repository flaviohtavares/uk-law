# Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005, section 84A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2024-04-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2005/16
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 84A. Power of chief constable to report conduct inconsistent with the licensing objectives[^c-7e146082e2daf199b2ec85cfb60bbebd]

*Extent: S*

(1) If the chief constable[^c-cb139c6d43348f5f89d6a1e749caba8c] considers that any personal licence holder has acted in a manner which is inconsistent with any of the licensing objectives, the chief constable may report the matter to the relevant Licensing Board.

(2) Where a Licensing Board receives a report from the chief constable[^c-f80c604a8ddd577d47cc2c3b91e03798] under subsection (1), the Board must hold a hearing.

(3) Subsections (6), (6A), (7), (7A)[^c-75a1b7ec5b56e7a1893aa16b703644b8] and (8)[^c-6e08ab382ca0296513b5e7d76136679d] of section 84 and subsection (1)(b) of section 85 apply in relation to a hearing under subsection (2) of this section as they apply in relation to a hearing under subsection (3)(a) or (5) of section 84.

(4) In subsection (1), “ **relevant Licensing Board** ” has the meaning given in section 83(11).
