## 57. Notification of application to chief constable and Licensing Standards Officer[^c-cbffb13eb0e36a39f2cbab04681b692f]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a Licensing Board receives an occasional licence application, the Board must give notice of it, together with a copy of the application, to—

- (a) the chief constable[^c-4574283aec12ebc2fc0326cf5bcd2f6e], and
- (b) any Licensing Standards Officer for the area in which the subject premises are situated.

(2)[^c-b3f1bee4a7f761c380c123d9af023e06] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) A Licensing Standards Officer may, within 21 days of receipt of a notice under subsection (1)(b), prepare and submit to the Licensing Board a report setting out the Officer's comments on the application.

(3A)[^c-90a8ee1682b378ca5820b53fb6098a32] If a Licensing Standards Officer intends to prepare and submit a report but is, for a reason relating to coronavirus, unable to do so within the period mentioned in subsection (3)—

- (a) the Officer must, within that period, make the Licensing Board aware of that situation, and
- (b) if the Officer submits a report after the end of that period, the Board may take the report into account if the Board considers it reasonable to do so.

(4)[^c-461312f4e8285864bba50ccc25e24154] Subsection (5) applies where the Licensing Board is satisfied that the application requires to be dealt with quickly.

(5) Subsection (3) has[^c-dd1a106160fb8f8d608377a12bd5d501] effect in relation to the application as if the reference[^c-ba9eae2f7f934a55bc958d5dbd389fed] to the period of 21 days were a reference[^c-5dec9749e19c652b70f5c008828eeb9f] to such shorter period of not less than 24 hours as the Board may determine.
