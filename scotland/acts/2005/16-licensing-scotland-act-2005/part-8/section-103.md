# Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005, section 103

## 103. Allowing the sale of alcohol to a child or young person[^key-aa565ada07705d19766cab0fb7e740dd]

*Extent: S*

(1) Any responsible person who [^key-4112da77080357581e7dc9c46aab8fac]... allows alcohol to be sold to a child or a young person on any relevant premises commits an offence.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction to—

- (a) a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale,
- (b) imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months, or
- (c) both.
