## 120. Prohibition of late-night deliveries of alcohol[^c-0d82d197ce9571365f54c6aea2c646fd]

*Extent: S*

(1) This section applies where alcohol is sold on any relevant premises for consumption off the premises.

(2) A responsible person commits an offence if the person [^c-2d6e5706cb65a90f75987584e392d89a]... delivers the alcohol to any premises (other than licensed premises) between the hours of midnight and 6am.

(3) A responsible person who [^c-2d6e5706cb65a90f75987584e392d89a]... allows the alcohol to be so delivered commits an offence.

(4) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
