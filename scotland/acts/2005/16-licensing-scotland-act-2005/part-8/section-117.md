# Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005, section 117

## 117. Offences relating to sale of alcohol to trade[^c-e5da7ae5331067837f2fcc9310182802]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person who sells alcohol to trade otherwise than from licensed premises or[^c-6c600ca4758dfa2df512ccd812fc8d17] premises which are used exclusively for the purpose of the selling of goods (whether solely alcohol or not) to trade commits an offence.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
