# Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005, section 37

## 37. Expenses[^c-d0f9f047027b7e8e7819545e355da7c3]

*Extent: S*

In proceedings before it under sections 34 to 36 in relation to a charity, the Court of Session may, instead of awarding expenses against the charity, award expenses against a charity trustee of the charity or against any two or more of its charity trustees jointly and severally.
