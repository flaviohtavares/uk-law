# Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005, section 11

## 11. Change of name[^key-16e934a6dcf4cce6c4273fde67108272]

*Extent: S*

(1) A charity may change its name only with OSCR's consent.

(2) A charity which proposes to change its name must [^key-ae187482e37eca5d6d93b5c37c27e893]... give notice to OSCR specifying its proposed new name.

(3) Unless OSCR, within 28 days of the date on which a notice is given under subsection (2) —[^key-faaf8dd423406cc3a49b35020927c44b]

- (a) refuses its consent, or
- (b) directs the charity not to change its name to that proposed new name for a period of not more than 6 months specified in the direction,

OSCR is to be taken as having given its consent.[^key-b4b5dbac1f410198627abb6f5c3e81cf]

(3A)[^key-c86e464f2aea7b1986a9a050a6e9b871] A direction under subsection (3)(b)—

- (a) may be revoked at any time,
- (b) may be varied, but not so as to have effect for a period of more than 6 months from the date on which it is given.

(3B) Where OSCR gives such a direction it must, after making such inquiries as it thinks fit—

- (a) give its consent, whether or not subject to conditions, or
- (b) refuse its consent.

(4) OSCR may refuse to consent to a charity changing its name only where

- (a)[^key-f71bc6d390a557bd6cd64df143bfdf5f] it considers that the proposed new name falls within section 10 , or[^key-2a43fdddab7a1d9c75cd51c3f70fd605]
- (b) it is unable, after making reasonable inquiries, to satisfy itself that the proposed new name does not fall within section 10.

(5)[^key-69d136f00f76b63ce4a81eb7c62cb745] OSCR may, at the request of a charity, grant consent to a different proposed new name than that specified by the charity in the notice given by it under subsection (2).
