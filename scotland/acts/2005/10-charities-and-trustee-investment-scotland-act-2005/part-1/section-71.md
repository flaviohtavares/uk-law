# Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005, section 71

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-03-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2005/10
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 71. Decisions[^c-c8c5641bfb3ff4a1bc2ddc8e4b4cc63a]

*Extent: S*

This Chapter applies to any decision by OSCR (or by a person to whom OSCR's functions are delegated by virtue of section 38) to—

- (a) refuse an application for entry in the Register, including entry as a SCIO under section 55, 58 or 60,
- (b) refuse to disapply section 3(3)(aa) or (b)[^c-ce78a18e27c998fc6976184db778ec7a] in relation to a charity,
- (c)[^c-5ebb895848cae97429e11bdd89d4a2f3] refuse to consent to a charity changing name under section 11,
- (d) give a direction under section 12(2) , (2A)[^c-5cda69d6f9a997b6546b30a3397ae374] or (3),
- (e) refuse to give a direction under section 12(2) or (2A)[^c-eaedd8531b76a7be6699c652e95092c1],
- (f) refuse to consent to a charity taking any action set out in section 16(2),
- (g) give a direction under section 28(3),
- (h) make a requirement under section 29(1),
- (i) remove a charity from the Register under section 30(1) or (3),
- (ia)[^c-12f01198760b971db8a9711487e0a0e9] give a direction under section 30(1)(a),
- (ib)[^c-f6b2884b0d3d5b69fbd8199e134340ad] give a direction under section 30A(2),
- (ic) remove a charity from the Register under section 30A(4),
- (id)[^c-5698cccc82b64ab1bb7bed07ceaf8905] give a direction under section 30B(2),
- (j) suspend a person under section 31(4),
- (k) give a direction under section 31(5) or (8),
- (l) give a direction under section 31(6), (7) or (9),
- (m) refuse an application made for the purposes of section 39(1),
- (ma)[^c-f07472a11073321fca6ad8e4c00151b7] refuse an application made for the purposes of section 43A,
- (mb)[^c-69844e26cfcbb6d96c393d08733c364e] remove a charity from the Register under section 45A(3),
- (n) give a direction under section 53(2),
- (o) give a direction under section 67(7),
- (p) refuse to grant a waiver under section 69(4),
- (pa)[^c-43de70bd85677353ff6d7d627d8a3f96] refuse to grant a waiver under section 69B(3),
- (pb)[^c-f2f191e179ba399dd23042e4618ea7f9] appoint an interim charity trustee under section 70A(1) or (2)(c),
- (pc)[^c-f19cbf26c3795b102329bf06b2168c1a] refuse an application made for the purposes of section 70ZB(3),
- (q) refuse to designate a charity as a designated religious charity or designated national collector, or
- (r) withdraw the designation of a charity as a designated religious charity or designated national collector.
