# Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005, section 73

## 73. Effect of decisions[^key-86c11bf4e0ef9d0c46bb58625d88832d]

*Extent: S*

(1) Unless subsection (2) or (3) provides otherwise, a decision to which this Chapter applies (and any direction, requirement, suspension or other act in pursuance of such a decision) has effect from the day on which the notice required by section 72 is given.

(2) A decision referred to in section 71(d), (i), (ia),[^key-c3a45ed3ec9f6184f4aa686662e6a555] (ib), (ic),[^key-1d84c23f4adbe530b8f89cab5a31fd15] (id),[^key-7244fe8f44f9d5e81bc684f948e97377] (mb),[^key-ef0264e855564d23c8ca071c6cb21105] (o) , (pb)[^key-43a44a00589336f6dda7dc14d8c0d161] or (r) (and any direction, requirement, suspension or other act in pursuance of such a decision) is of no effect unless and until the notice required by section 72 is given and—

- (a) the period within which OSCR must, on request, review the decision expires without a request being made, or
- (b) where OSCR, on a request made within that period, confirms the decision (with or without variations)—
  - (i) the period within which that decision by OSCR may be appealed under section 76 to the First-tier Tribunal[^key-cc1f86e78e2d2c508cacd4ce1d8891c4] expires without an appeal being made, or
  - (ii) where such an appeal is made, it is abandoned or finally determined (by the First-tier Tribunal[^key-627d92e75140b761b54870f6730f32ff] or, as the case may be, the Upper Tribunal[^key-36a35f45005fcb4fa5731e475dde9017] ).

(3) A decision referred to in section 71(h) (and any corresponding requirement) is of no effect unless and until any notice[^key-d6d79bab2b89fcca43e7644dd8bbce42] required by section 72 is given and—

- (a) the period within which OSCR must, on request, review the decision expires without a request being made, or
- (b) where such a request is made, the date on which OSCR confirms the decision (with or without variations).
