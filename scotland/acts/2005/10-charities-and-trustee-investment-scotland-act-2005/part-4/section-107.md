## 107. Short title and commencement

*Extent: S*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005.

(2) This Act (except sections 102 and 103 and this section) comes into force on such day as the Scottish Ministers may by order appoint.
