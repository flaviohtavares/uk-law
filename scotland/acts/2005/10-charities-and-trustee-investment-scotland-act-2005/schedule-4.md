# Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005, SCHEDULE 4

## SCHEDULE 4 — Minor and consequential amendments and repeals

*Extent: S*

(introduced by section 104)

### Part 1 — Acts

#### 1. Recreational Charities Act 1958 (c. 17)[^key-8bf991ae4c5574d39868c4d9dee94db5]

In section 6(2) of the Recreational Charities Act 1958, the words from “or”, where second occurring, to “1962” are repealed.

#### 2. Local Government (Financial Provisions etc.) (Scotland) Act 1962 (c. 9)[^key-de86d3b46616f19d6b98c7d36c92b83c]

(1) In section 4 (reduction and remission of rates payable by charitable and other organisations) of the Local Government (Financial Provisions etc.) (Scotland) Act 1962, for paragraph (a) of subsection (10) substitute—

> - (a) “**charity**” means a body entered in the Scottish Charity Register,

.

(2) Paragraph 5 of Schedule 2 to that Act of 1962 is repealed.

#### 3. Sex Discrimination Act 1975 (c. 65)[^key-b8fe92c6a758fe36be233496787d900f][^key-bdaefe5855078e46350af10c0a37c64e]

#### 4. Education (Scotland) Act 1980 (c. 44)[^key-2cbcec8ff82ea0213e86b24a88763152]

In section 122(1) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980, for the definition of “charitable purposes” substitute—

> “**charitable purposes**” has the same meaning as in the Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005 (asp 10);

.

#### 5. Civic Government (Scotland) Act 1982 (c. 45)

In the Civic Government (Scotland) Act 1982—

- (a)[^key-a799c6034b506e0726bc4238bdbad297] in section 24(3), for paragraph (c) substitute—

  > - (c) the business of a charity (that is to say, a body which is entered in the Scottish Charity Register);

  ,
- (b) in section 39(3)(f), for the words from “charitable” to the end of that paragraph substitute “ benevolent collection (within the meaning of section 84 of the Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005 (asp 10)). ”,
- (c) section 119 (regulation of charitable collections) is repealed.

#### 6. Companies Act 1985 (c. 6)[^key-17a5fb725a4f066f9679663753da6b1e][^key-fb77645956f09dd8f06a7f5ca600ac2f]

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

#### 7. Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990 (c. 40)[^key-cddb7e83b38176859b0855aa14b4b418]

In the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990—

- (a) in section 1—
  - (i) subsections (4) to (6), and
  - (ii) the words which follow paragraph (b) in subsection (7),

  are repealed,
- (b) sections 2 to 8, 12 to 14 and 15(1) to (8) are repealed,
- (c) in section 9(1)(d)(ii), for “become a recognised body” substitute “ be entered in the Scottish Charity Register ”,
- (d) in section 10—
  - (i) in subsection (1)(d)(ii), for “become a recognised body” substitute “ be entered in the Scottish Charity Register ”,
  - (ii) subsections (6), (9)(b) and (11)(b) are repealed,
- (e) in section 15(9)—
  - (i) after “affect” insert

    > —
    >
    > - (a)

    ,
  - (ii) at the end insert

    > ; or
    >
    > - (b) any body entered in the Scottish Charity Register.

#### 8. Charities Act 1992 (c. 41)

**Not yet in force.**

In Schedule 6 to the Charities Act 1992, paragraph 10 is repealed.

#### 9. Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992 (c. 37)[^key-d8cb7b2b61867842da688cbbeb9c5ebf]

In section 19(3) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992, for “within the meaning of the Income Tax Acts” substitute “ (within the meaning of the Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005 (asp 10) ”.

#### 10. Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992 (c. 53)[^key-031d04fd3156edce1f4ed699ae6908c9]

In Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992, after paragraph 47 insert—

> **47A. Charities**
>
> Any Scottish Charity Appeals Panel constituted in accordance with section 75(1) of the Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005 (asp 10).

#### 11. Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994 (c. 39)

**Not yet in force.**

In Schedule 13 to the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994, paragraph 129(16) is repealed.

#### 12. Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000 (asp 7)[^key-54cb0637dd29253c695dbd9ca25dd274]

In schedule 3 to the Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000, before the entry relating to “Scottish Children's Reporter Administration” insert— “ The Scottish Charity Regulator ”.

#### 13. Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 2)[^key-494640087ca99683230b0da22c9fd5e7]

In the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2003—

- (a) in section 34(8), for the words from “which” to the end of that subsection substitute “ entered in the Scottish Charity Register ”,
- (b) in section 71(8), for the words from “which” to the end of that subsection substitute “ entered in the Scottish Charity Register ”.

#### 14. Public Appointments and Public Bodies etc. (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 4)[^key-2ccdc62b1c4756dbbb3f4203b981746d]

In schedule 2 to the Public Appointments and Public Bodies etc. (Scotland) Act 2003, before the entry relating to the “Scottish Children's Reporter Administration” insert— “ Scottish Charity Regulator ”.

### Part 2 — Subordinate legislation

#### 16. Arable Area Payments Regulations 1996 (S.I. 1996/3142)[^key-33994d88bd21eab4cf8166012361c172]

In regulation 9(3)(h) of the Arable Area Payments Regulations 1996, for the words from “a”, where it second occurs, to the end of the paragraph substitute “ , in relation to Scotland, a body entered in the Scottish Charity Register ”.

#### 17. Water and Sewerage Charges (Exemption) (Scotland) Regulations 2002 (S.S.I 2002/167)[^key-df3a14930fddd608edd7f8922dff5851]

In regulation 2(1) of the Water and Sewerage Charges (Exemption) (Scotland) Regulations 2002, in paragraph (i) of the definition of “net annual income”, for the words from “Scottish” to “1990” substitute “ body entered in the Scottish Charity Register ”.

#### 18. National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) (Scotland) Regulations 2004 (S.S.I. 2004/115)[^key-91f9c2e5b9f95664fdc7506ce9bb4c0e]

In the National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) (Scotland) Regulations 2004, for “section 7 of the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990”, where those words occur in—

- (a) regulation 5(2)(k)(i), and
- (b) paragraph 101(2)(m)(i) of schedule 5,

substitute “ section 34 of the Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005 (asp 10) ”.

#### 19. National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2004 (S.S.I. 2004/116)[^key-0bb8b49e200247c91723e66718793c36]

In the National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2004, for “section 7 of the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990”, where those words occur in—

- (a) regulation 3(2)(k)(i),
- (b) paragraph 66(3)(l)(i) of schedule 1,

substitute “ section 34 of the Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005 (asp 10) ”.
