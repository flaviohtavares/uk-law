# Fire (Scotland) Act 2005, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2016-12-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2005/5
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 51. Prohibition on employment of police

*Extent: S*

SFRS[^c-7ad8898d6ab0527b6630b2d0256e8b64] may not employ a constable (other than a special constable appointed under section 9 of the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 (asp 8))[^c-ced8049f789059f0d31bddd2465eccc5] for the purpose of carrying out any of the functions conferred on it[^c-a0a2c821ccc76e3640d48450afdacc0f] by virtue of this Act.
