## 16. Charging[^c2112910]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may by order (a “charging order”) authorise SFRS[^c-d17912f24035c2ae721799b309ad10ec] to charge a person of a description specified in the order for any action so specified taken by SFRS[^c-e8c92f6467f35610a529fa2f2ca4d96a].

(2) A charging order may authorise a charge to be imposed on, or recovered from, a person other than the person in respect of whom action is taken by SFRS[^c-124900d31926671de92b3ede51980978].

(3) A charging order may authorise charging for—

- (a) extinguishing fires;
- (b) protecting life; or
- (c) protecting property in the event of fires,

only in respect of action taken by SFRS at sea beyond the low water mark[^c-ba1cb9c3a34688ecce5ac471958e8abe].

(4) Where SFRS is[^c-37e61bc9299d35820343c4d883c221ad] authorised by a charging order to charge for taking action of a particular description and it[^c-d2e03da4b1ba2b3452d317ff6d0517f6] decides to do so—

- (a) the amount of the charge shall be set by SFRS[^c-915b8a1d8d05035da0c7a0060ed50913];
- (b) different amounts may be charged in different circumstances (and SFRS may charge nothing).

(5)[^c-850febef00e7cbce20cbca22d5d1ff75] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c-850febef00e7cbce20cbca22d5d1ff75] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^c2112956] The references in subsection (3) to “sea” include–

- (a) that part of the territorial sea of the United Kingdom which is not adjacent to Scotland; and
- (b) sea beyond the territorial sea of the United Kingdom.
