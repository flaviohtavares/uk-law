# Fire (Scotland) Act 2005, section 17

## 17. Duty to secure water supply

*Extent: S*

(1) SFRS[^key-8a806440b9878082d2bf731a7cacc91e] shall take all reasonable measures for securing that an adequate supply of water will be available for its[^key-931f86fdecf036bdd78effdaa80ec0a7] use for the purposes mentioned in subsection (2).

(2) Those purposes are—

- (a) extinguishing fires in the area of SFRS[^key-5adc116928c20afc4f13f775b4d3b6f9];
- (b) protecting life and property in the event of fires in its area;
- (c) rescuing people in the event of road traffic accidents in its area;
- (d) protecting people from serious harm in the event of road traffic accidents in its area;
- (e) carrying out any function conferred on SFRS[^key-9185ec343ec0dc933a86ca28b5962a97] by an order under section 11; and
- (f) fulfilling any requirement made of SFRS[^key-7dbd33eed030a60dcc2337ef644cfc28] by a direction given to it under section 12.
