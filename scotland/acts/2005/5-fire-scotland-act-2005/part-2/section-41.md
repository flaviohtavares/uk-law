# Fire (Scotland) Act 2005, section 41

## 41. Adherence

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-ccd8c7a6ea190ad3fd5edbdcfc6149c9] In carrying out its functions, SFRS must have regard to the framework document.

(2) Subsections (3) and (4) apply where the Scottish Ministers consider that SFRS[^key-96b831b7672b46571108893170e47e5b] is failing, or is likely to fail, to act in accordance with the framework document[^key-02db56b9cc672f2647199e2be8e953bd].

(3) The Scottish Ministers may cause an inquiry to be held into the matter.

(4) Subject to subsection (5), the Scottish Ministers may, for the purpose of securing that SFRS[^key-8659f809085401cb0ee039235d30ae60] acts in accordance with the document, by order require SFRS—

- (a) to take; or
- (b) to refrain from taking,

such action as is specified in the order.

(5) The Scottish Ministers may make an order under subsection (4) only where they consider that it would promote—

- (a) public safety;
- (b) the efficiency and effectiveness of SFRS[^key-b020d3cad5ce2da0fb3df24a2c57543e]; or
- (c) efficiency and effectiveness in connection with the matters in relation to which SFRS has[^key-788499d37d0cbeca564803fd51d86112] functions.

(6) Before making an order under subsection (4), the Scottish Ministers shall consult SFRS[^key-b1adea04d4b3c8f208709907440925e6].
