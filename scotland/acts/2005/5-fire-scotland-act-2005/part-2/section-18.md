# Fire (Scotland) Act 2005, section 18

## 18. Use of water

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to—

- (a) an agreement under section 19(1); and
- (b) section 9A of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980 (c. 45) (charging for emergency use of water),

SFRS[^key-b33c80a542a6285ef1ec4958e191dde4] may use any suitable supply of water for the purposes mentioned in section 17(2).

(2) SFRS[^key-8b30eb01208b541ca49bd9ad34df0be3] shall pay reasonable compensation for water used by virtue of subsection (1).
