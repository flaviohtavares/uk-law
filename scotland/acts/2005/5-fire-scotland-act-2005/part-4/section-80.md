# Fire (Scotland) Act 2005, section 80

## 80. Inquiries

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers may cause an inquiry to be held into—

- (a) the manner in which SFRS[^c-526043d1cc7542a094d28544f7869f9e] is carrying out any of its functions under this Act;
- (b) the circumstances of, or the steps taken to deal with—
  - (i) a fire;
  - (ii) a road traffic accident; or
  - (iii) an emergency of another kind in relation to which SFRS[^c-a57534aae3338db298416a96c36023b9] has functions under this Act.
