# Water Services etc. (Scotland) Act 2005, section 24

> Text as at 2022-11-10.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 24. Qualification of duty to provide services[^key-4a2e1a7811b6f45aa457ef6ceaaccb8f]

*Extent: S*

(1) In section 1 (duty of Scottish Water to provide for sewerage) of the 1968 Act, after subsection (6) there is added—

> (7) The duties imposed by subsections (1) and (2) above shall not require Scottish Water to do anything which is prejudicial to its compliance with—
>
> - (a) any directions given to it under section 56 of the Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002 (asp 3) so far as setting objectives of a type referred to in section 56A of that Act, or
> - (b) a statement of policy issued under section 29D of that Act.

.

(2) In section 6 (duty of Scottish Water to provide water supply) of the 1980 Act, after subsection (4) there is added—

> (5) The duties imposed by subsections (1), (2) and (4) shall not require Scottish Water to do anything which is prejudicial to its compliance with—
>
> - (a) any directions given to it under section 56 of the Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002 (asp 3) so far as setting objectives of a type referred to in section 56A of that Act, or
> - (b) a statement of policy issued under section 29D of that Act.

.
