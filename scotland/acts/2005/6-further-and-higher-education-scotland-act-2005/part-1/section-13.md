# Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005, section 13

## 13. Quality of fundable further and higher education[^key-580f9ac926e9479801ab5ad6ea89398a]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Council is to secure that provision is made for—

- (a) assessing; and
- (b) enhancing,

the quality of fundable further education and fundable higher education provided by post-16 education[^key-20761c5659b689381501e2e8430a4136] bodies.

(2) In exercising the function under subsection (1), the Council must, if it considers it appropriate to do so, consult such persons as appear to it to represent the interests of post-16 education[^key-671f4121c25772a345d6b90b67a1f76f] bodies or any class of them.
