## 22. Consultation and collaboration[^key-155e0404d0c5709eea9f5ed9652cb116]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Council must, in the exercise of its functions—

- (a) where it considers it appropriate to do so, consult any or all of the persons referred to in subsections (5) and (5A)[^key-6dbcb3dd94d73287a879970dd62e82d1]; and
- (b) so far as is consistent with the proper exercise of its functions, seek to secure the collaboration with the Council of those persons.

(2) The Council must, where it considers it appropriate to do so in the exercise of its functions, consult––

- (a) a trade union which appears to it to be representative of staff of post-16 education bodies and regional strategic bodies;[^key-1ee892aae352da74e1403a49e979818e]
- (aa) any body which appears to the Council to be representative of trade unions in Scotland; or
- (b) a body of persons which appears to it to be representative of students of the post-16 education bodies generally[^key-674322333a0d869d35745c8dd5bf18be].

(3) Any particular requirement for consultation imposed on the Council by virtue of this Act is without prejudice to subsections (1)(a) and (2).

(4) The persons referred to in subsection (5) must provide the Council with such information as it may reasonably require for the purposes of or in connection with the exercise of any of its functions.

(5) The persons are—

- (a) any local authority;
- (b) the governing body of any fundable body;
- (c) the governing body of any other body which provides fundable further education or fundable higher education;
- (d) Qualifications Scotland[^key-7a20d08e5b895d43604c222a0f9d003f];
- (e) Scottish Enterprise;
- (f) Highlands and Islands Enterprise; ;[^key-21c29a6e993560b8c9cc442111a5f2b9] [^key-131475c53246db04d23fe9dcab811018]...
- (fa) The Skills Development Scotland Co. Limited;
- (fb)[^key-e8b1ae6f741b9235891200aa1878bae2] South of Scotland Enterprise.
- (g)[^key-d81faab5dfc9acd54043e257c17fd3f4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (h)[^key-d81faab5dfc9acd54043e257c17fd3f4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (i)[^key-d81faab5dfc9acd54043e257c17fd3f4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5A)[^key-cc24a3ff417f03258ac4b6b7d9e6554b] The persons are—

- (a) persons who appear to the Council to be representative of apprentices,
- (b) employers of apprentices or persons who appear to the Council to be representative of such employers,
- (c) training providers (within the meaning given by section 12J(3)) or persons who appear to the Council to be representative of training providers.

(6)[^key-999ced5ec5c559d366d56d6d2d889ed9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) The Scottish Ministers may by order modify subsections (5) and (5A)[^key-c0c5dae246e91965e123782220aae023].

(8) The Council must, in relation to the provision of fundable further education and fundable higher education, —[^key-24b6d23f8c609f6a26165d465d52512e]

- (a) promote collaboration between post-16 education bodies; and
- (b) promote such collaboration between post-16 education bodies and regional strategic bodies as it considers appropriate.
