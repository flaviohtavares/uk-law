## 13A. Performance of regional strategic bodies[^c-1599c58cb86c4b2bbfc1df627439ac6f]

*Extent: S*

The Council is to secure that provision is made for—

- (a) assessing; and
- (b) enhancing,

the performance of regional strategic bodies.
