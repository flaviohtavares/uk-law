# Transport (Scotland) Act 2005, section 43

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 43. Guidance and direction to the Committee[^c-c8f33a85f2f2dc42e789d201a1a3bf92]

*Extent: S*

The Committee shall—

- (a) have regard to any written guidance issued to it by the Scottish Ministers;
- (b) comply with any written direction given to it by them,

as to how it is to discharge its functions.
