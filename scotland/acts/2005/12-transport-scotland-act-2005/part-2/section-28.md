# Transport (Scotland) Act 2005, section 28

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28. Duty of undertaker to notify completion of road works: form and procedure[^key-8a0b8c7e4ae515f6cda0d4f489c29726]

*Extent: S*

In section 129 of the 1991 Act (duty of undertaker to reinstate road after road works)—

- (a) in subsection (3), for “inform the road works authority” there is substituted “ give the required notice ”;
- (b) in subsection (4), for “notify the road works authority” there is substituted “ give the required notice ”;
- (c) after subsection (5) there is inserted—

  > (5A) For the purposes of subsection (3) or (4) an undertaker gives the required notice by—
  >
  > - (a) giving to the Commissioner a notice containing such information as may be prescribed, and
  > - (b) entering in the SRWR a copy of that notice.

  .
