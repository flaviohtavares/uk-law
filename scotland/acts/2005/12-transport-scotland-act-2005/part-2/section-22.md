# Transport (Scotland) Act 2005, section 22

> Text as at 2024-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 22. Restriction on works following substantial road works

*Extent: S*

(1) In section 117 of the 1991 Act (restriction on works following substantial road works)—

- (a)[^key-3465eaf6becb540023f844e17a494db7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^key-7cc7a1468f35c75f015793a1c872d578] in subsection (6), at the beginning of paragraph (b) there is inserted “ if convicted of an offence under this subsection, ”; and
- (c)[^key-7cc7a1468f35c75f015793a1c872d578] at end there is inserted—

  > (9) The Scottish Ministers may issue or approve for the purposes of this section a code of practice giving practical guidance as to the performance by road works authorities of their functions under this section; and in carrying out those functions a road works authority shall have regard to the code of practice.

  .

(2)[^key-7cc7a1468f35c75f015793a1c872d578] In section 114 of the 1991 Act (notice of starting date of works), in subsection (2), after “works” there is inserted “ or in cases where the undertaker has been given notice under section 117(1) ”.
