## 4. The Mental Welfare Commission for Scotland

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-252ed15f656b30c54943f8ef955e0fc7] There shall continue to be a body corporate known as the Mental Welfare Commission for Scotland (in this Act referred to as “**the Commission**”).

(2)[^c-252ed15f656b30c54943f8ef955e0fc7] The Commission shall discharge such functions as are conferred on it by virtue of—

- (a) this Act;
- (b) the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (asp 4); and
- (c) any other enactment.

(2A)[^c-2bd948414cbeec50bde6bea4137e2b3b] In so discharging its functions, the Commission shall act in a manner which seeks to protect the welfare of persons who have a mental disorder.

(3)[^c-51a6dc66456045be51e036e12471890c][^c-5c1b61c05c00e07e33eedaa215346857] Schedule 1 to this Act (which makes provision as respects the Commission) shall have effect.
