## 19. Statistical information[^c-ded1d47ef94b8e94bf9b597ad97da1d4]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-6b5debe98eccb88a661ecb340b111105] The Commission shall [^c-e655cf3db497635489dfd77c25ad6614]... provide the Ministers with, and publish, statistical or other information of such kind as may be prescribed in regulations[^c-3ff1ca1ac9ec80aaad800777f510474f] relating to the discharge of its functions.

(2)[^c-0d4f764bddcd7729610db27bb3e61030] Before making regulations under subsection (1) above, the Scottish Ministers shall consult such persons as they consider appropriate.
