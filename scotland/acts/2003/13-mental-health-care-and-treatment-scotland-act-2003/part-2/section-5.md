## 5. Duty to monitor operation of Act and promote best practice[^c-b3ce5954096bb712be3fb42b254a3fce]

*Extent: S*

The Commission shall—

- (a) monitor the practical application of the observance of Part 1[^c-1c95416b46926ecf48ffe6c8cf1a8e32] of this Act; and
- (b) promote best practice in relation to the practical application of the observance of Part 1 of this Act[^c-94850f135284e45259fb4324eb99f0d3].
