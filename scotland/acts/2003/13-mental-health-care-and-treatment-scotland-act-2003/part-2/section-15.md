## 15. Medical examination[^c-742385f2caf861d1010fdacd9d6987d7][^c-001f3b9a85436a4cccaefe11fcc5f2cf]

*Extent: S*

(1) A Commission Visitor[^c-28350135e1efacca198c2811f1144b05] may, in connection with the discharge by the Commission of any of its functions under—

- (a) this Act; or
- (b) the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (asp 4),

carry out in private a medical examination of a patient.

(1A)[^c-41327019b6e92d21a7cef0719088ea79] Only a Commission Visitor who has also been appointed as a Medical Visitor may exercise the functions under subsection (1).
