## 56. Effect of short-term detention certificate etc. on certain orders[^key-00e9682e44c6cd7974f1e0ce4a535a46][^key-5995fb5ae89d5393faf0c3e8f06c7c8a]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subsection (2) below applies where—

- (a) a patient is subject to a relevant order[^key-aee0b4cdb8bf386d4d99c233a6923c0e]; and
- (b) a short-term detention certificate is granted in respect of the patient.

(2)[^key-fbc1c9289e0575ea78448294b3dccbb7] A relevant order shall cease to authorise the measures specified in it for the period during which the patient is subject to—

- (a) the short-term detention certificate, or
- (b) an extension certificate.

(3)[^key-a7490792d9b4504af34ece88d107bd47] In this section, the references to a relevant order are to—

- (a) a compulsion order, or
- (b) a compulsory treatment order or an interim compulsory treatment order.
