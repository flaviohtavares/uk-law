## 21. The Mental Health Tribunal for Scotland

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-7cb47aa479d2e1adcb2be6179408a21f] There shall be a tribunal to be known as the Mental Health Tribunal for Scotland (in this Act referred to as “**the Tribunal**”).

(2)[^c-bcd8a4227924407617949179f287c3d0] The Tribunal shall discharge such functions as are conferred on it by virtue of this Act.

(3)[^c-7cb47aa479d2e1adcb2be6179408a21f] Regulations may make such provision in connection with the Tribunal as the Scottish Ministers consider appropriate.

(4)[^c-505f8c7796d0e8fd381af77b027f789a][^c-f9a61af994f039b910d237110eae1b55][^c-2192aad123d8f226c7f7ffe6b37644a5] Schedule 2 to this Act (which makes provision as respects the Tribunal and its proceedings) shall have effect.
