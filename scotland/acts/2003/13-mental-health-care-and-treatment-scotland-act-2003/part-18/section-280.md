## 280. Restriction of Scottish Ministers' power to delegate management of state hospitals[^c-c3d2fb0d676a5f8ac0e1a748d4d71c2b]

*Extent: S*

In section 102 of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 (c. 29) (provision and management of state hospitals)—

- (a) in subsection (4), paragraph (a) and the word “or” immediately following it; and
- (b) subsection (5),

shall cease to have effect.
