## 321. Appeal to Court of Session against decisions of sheriff principal[^c-c3a8ebab8aaa05d8e08f3143e9919c51][^c-52e64fee16b543eec36ea7b7188d1d2f][^c-22866aafc5a786354d2290854d1042f0]

*Extent: S*

(1) A relevant party to an appeal to the sheriff principal under section 320(2) of this Act may appeal to the Court of Session against the decision of the sheriff principal allowing or refusing the appeal.

(2) In subsection (1) above, “**relevant party**” has the same meaning as in section 320 of this Act.
