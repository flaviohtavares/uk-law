## 300. Meaning of “place of safety”[^key-0b927cf2e771e8172a20a840705e7f03]

*Extent: S*

In this Part of this Act, “**place of safety**” means—

- (a) a hospital;
- (b) premises which are used for the purpose of providing a care home service (as defined in paragraph 2 of schedule 12 to the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010[^key-dd4507bf074f509646df4788ac14b887] (asp 8)); or
- (c) any other suitable place (other than a police station) the occupier of which is willing temporarily to receive mentally disordered persons.
