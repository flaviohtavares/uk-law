## 246. Certificates under section 238[^key-4fa9fbb5f38ccce4c7f49039e7a1b109][^key-d4e8843b32f1ff85741eac460ea7877c]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-2bc1872f24a48ac3a2f5de297aa0124d] A certificate under section 238 of this Act shall contain such particulars as may be prescribed by regulations.

(2)[^key-db05f51cb9370e452fdc37fd7a1c8ab0] A person who gives a certificate under section 238 of this Act shall, before the expiry of the period of 7 days beginning with the day on which the certificate is given, send a copy of it to the Commission.
