## 161. Application to Tribunal by responsible medical officer[^key-6f4551517345d7d55f6670e3ec222d27][^key-841fb1f53a597b1f171eb6ab6778ae20]

*Extent: S*

An application under this section to the Tribunal by a patient’s responsible medical officer for an order varying a compulsion order—

- (a) shall state
  - (i) the matters mentioned in section 158(a)[^key-f07bf90f9c317fce7df33bfc6c00ca95](i) to (iv)[^key-f35960e1d5258570ae1670cb172ed9b2] of this Act; and
  - (ii)[^key-f91972a8ffbf886be1702842d5fd0458] whether the mental health officer agrees, or disagrees that the application should be made, or has failed to comply with the duty imposed by section 159(4C)(e)(i) of this Act; and
  - (iii) if the mental health officer disagrees, the reason for that disagreement.
- (b) shall be accompanied by such documents as may be prescribed by regulations.
