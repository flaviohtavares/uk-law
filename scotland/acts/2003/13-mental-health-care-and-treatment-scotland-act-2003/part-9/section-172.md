## 172. Tribunal’s order varying compulsion order[^key-2e5e300437842dc12d53785eaff774c1]

*Extent: S*

[^key-e779151f4bcb940588e356a44006a0e0]... where the Tribunal makes an order under section 166, 167 or 171 of this Act varying a compulsion order, the Tribunal—

- (a) shall specify in its order the modifications made by its order to the measures specified in the compulsion order; and
- (b) may specify in its order measures other than those set out in the application to which its order relates.
