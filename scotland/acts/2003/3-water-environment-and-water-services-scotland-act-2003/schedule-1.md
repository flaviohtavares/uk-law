# Water Environment and Water Services (Scotland) Act 2003, SCHEDULE 1

## SCHEDULE 1 — Matters to be included in river basin management plans

*Extent: S*

(introduced by sections 10 and 14)

### Part 1 — Matters to be included in every plan

**1.**[^c-1d24ffa194aab81ceb76081cfa9cd478] A summary of the characterisation of the river basin district carried out under section 5.

**2.**[^c-8583d90057cd6c9630d8a471b380436b] A summary of significant pressures, and the impact of human activity, on the status of the water environment within the district.

**3.**[^c-16ee8eb3bd9161971268fa696f2abd53] A summary of the register of protected areas maintained under section 7, including information sufficient to identify each protected area within the district.

**4.**[^c-669ecd79ae5040340d46f6ae797ba5a1] Information as to the arrangements for monitoring water status under section 8 and as to the results of such monitoring.

**5.**[^c-f3c9259e006b523814cd7ec2e8bc19b5] A list of the environmental objectives set under section 9 for every body of water in the district.

**6.**[^c-97db9ebc4e1857528ebf8609851bdde6] A summary of the programme of measures prepared under section 9 to be applied to achieve those objectives.

**7.**[^c-37aa4e13d8a260a525521ed79338839a] Information as to any sub-basin plan.

**8.**[^c-cf25c603e078ea7366d6f27f34e42970] A non-technical summary of the information provided under paragraphs 1 to 7.

**9.**[^c-7367ca68bd66afd56d7b70bb654526dd] A summary of—

- (a) the steps taken under subsections (3) to (6) of section 11 in relation to the plan, and
- (b) changes made to the plan in light of—
  - (i) the views and representations received on it, and
  - (ii) any advice given by a River Basin District Advisory Group under section 17(2).

**10.**[^c-39f3a84f8d02086fcaa2acb0d895cecc] Information as to—

- (a) SEPA, and
- (b) every responsible authority and regulator (within the meaning of schedule 2) having functions in relation to the river basin district.

**11.**[^c-f764af57e245eca6e9ce0a13cbe04662] The arrangements for making available to the public documents and information relevant to the preparation and implementation of the plan.

### Part 2 — Additional matters to be included in revised plans

**12.**[^c-e07c1a9fceae320f168c1c1a32d9e678] A summary of the changes which have been made as compared with the previous plan.

**13.**[^c-20f622c71c77d8d74fe2dc71e062edc7] An assessment of the progress made towards the achievement of the environmental objectives set under section 9, including the results of monitoring under section 8 and reasons for any failure to achieve any such objective.

**14.**[^c-a28675ecd76332cdb558019bb39260c2] A summary of any measures included in the programme of measures prepared under section 9 in relation to the previous plan which were not applied, with reasons for the non-application.

**15.**[^c-e1ea0bd021b570c07adde30b98f8ca02] A summary of any measures additional to those referred to in paragraph 14 which were applied to achieve the environmental objectives set under section 9.
