## 20. Completion certificates: offences[^key-8c05a7c893d189b9d17b79b0ccd30cef]

*Extent: S*

(1) Any person who, under this Act—

- (a) submits to a verifier a completion certificate containing a statement which that person knows to be false or misleading in a material particular, or
- (b) recklessly submits to a verifier a completion certificate containing a statement which is false or misleading in a material particular,

is guilty of an offence.

(2) Any verifier who accepts a completion certificate submitted to it under this Act knowing that the certificate contains a statement which is false in a material particular is guilty of an offence.
