## SCHEDULE 6 — Modification of enactments

*Extent: S*

(introduced by section 58)

**1.**[^key-4b618c32697c16d055273563c025210b] The Building (Scotland) Act 1959 is repealed.

**2.**[^key-6e1f1b6e5492cea82fa6eadeb8f16adb] In section 21(3) (definition of “building”) of the Sewerage (Scotland) Act 1968, for “29(1) of the Building (Scotland) Act 1959” substitute “ 55(1) and (2) of the Building (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 8) ”.

**3.**[^key-b7b685306a66f2063ef6c5ccaab034d0] In the Mines and Quarries (Tips) Act 1969, in section 34(3) (application of certain enactments)—

- (a) after “authority” in the first place where it occurs insert “ and, in the case of section 37(3) of the Building (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 8), the reference to “any such person” ”,
- (b) in paragraph (a), for the words from “25(2)” to “councils” substitute “ 37(3) and (4) of the Building (Scotland) Act 2003 (power ”.

**4.**[^key-85b66700d8b2691b68a0d2f17d12cee6] The Building (Scotland) Act 1970 is repealed.

**5.**[^key-27635bbeb6a51fb201caaa19a28f2300] In the Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons Act 1970, in section 6(2) (provision of sanitary conveniences at certain premises open to the public)—

- (a) for the words from “who” in the first place where it occurs to “conform to” substitute “ on whom a building regulations compliance notice has been served under section 25(3) of the Building (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 8) requiring the owner to secure that the building complies with ”,
- (b) the word “standards” is repealed,
- (c) for “section 3” substitute “ section 1 ”,
- (d) for “order” substitute “ notice ”.

**10.**[^key-23d246031e32ecfb55d5dc3af1c4dd03] In the Control of Pollution Act 1974, in section 61(2) (which requires applications for consent under section 61 to be made at the same time as or after any application for a building control approval)—
