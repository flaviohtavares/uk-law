# Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003, section 94

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2003/9
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 94. Content of notice[^c7968111]

*Extent: S*

The Lands Tribunal shall—

- (a) in any notice given by them under section 93 of this Act—
  - (i) summarise or reproduce the application;
  - (ii) set a date (being a date no earlier than twenty-one days after the notice is given) by which representations to them as respects the application may be made;
  - (iii) state the fee which must accompany any such representations; and
  - (iv) in the case of an application for the discharge, renewal or variation of a real burden, or for the preservation of a real burden or development management scheme, state that if the application is not opposed it may be granted without further inquiry; and
- (b) in any notice so given (other than by advertisement) in respect of an application under section 90(1)(a) or 91(1) of this Act, also set out the name and address of every person to whom the notice is being sent.
