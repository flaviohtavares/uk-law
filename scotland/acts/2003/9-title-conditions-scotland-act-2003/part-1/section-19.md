## 19. Confusio not to extinguish real burden[^c7955341]

*Extent: S*

A real burden is not extinguished by reason only that—

- (a) the same person is the owner of the benefited property and the burdened property; or
- (b) in a case in which there is no benefited property, the person in whose favour the real burden is constituted is the owner of the burdened property.
