# Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2003/9
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 15. Discharge[^c7955301]

*Extent: S*

(1) A real burden is discharged as respects a benefited property by registering against the burdened property a deed of discharge granted by or on behalf of the owner of the benefited property.

(2) In subsection (1) above, “**discharged**” means discharged—

- (a) wholly; or
- (b) to such extent as may be specified in the deed of discharge.
