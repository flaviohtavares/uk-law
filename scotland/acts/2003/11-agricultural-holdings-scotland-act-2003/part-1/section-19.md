# Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 2003, section 19

## 19. Resumption and irritancy: supplementary[^key-95e5e836035ef953596dd488e1887455]

*Extent: S*

Any provision of this Act as to the termination of a short limited duration tenancy , a limited duration tenancy, a modern limited duration tenancy or a repairing tenancy[^key-eac5f0c2dfa28b4d19a0f6567545662c] does not affect any right of the landlord to—

- (a) resume land under section 17 or 17A[^key-90ec3db422f74f27affa94b068ea95e2] ; or
- (b) remove a tenant—
  - (i) whose estate has been sequestrated; or
  - (ii) who for any reason has incurred irritancy of the lease or other liability to be removed.
