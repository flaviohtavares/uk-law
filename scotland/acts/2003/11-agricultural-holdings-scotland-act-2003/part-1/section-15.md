## 15. Permanent pasture[^c-8a887c6363d47aa95db0f61cf44e17b5]

*Extent: S*

Section 9 (arbitration as to permanent pasture) of the 1991 Act applies in relation to short limited duration tenancies , limited duration tenancies, modern limited duration tenancies and repairing tenancies[^c-23f8bece82e2d6b8e28a353a892fdd2b] as it does in relation to 1991 Act tenancies, but as if the references to the holding were references to the land.
