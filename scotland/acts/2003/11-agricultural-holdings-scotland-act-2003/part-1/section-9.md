# Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 2003, section 9

## 9. Review of rent under limited duration tenancies (affected by repeal, see note)Review of rent under limited duration tenancies, modern limited duration tenancies and repairing tenancies[^key-85a6dac72bc841190d27555b8d2d8af3][^key-5e222703f2048f4eadd79ab1034e5e38]

*Extent: S*

(A1)[^key-eec136515780050131102678ce6eebac][^key-2e6113fa8ee49b4718be5e637bfd9b9e] Where, by virtue of any provision, a review of rent due as payable under a lease constituting a limited duration tenancy , a modern limited duration tenancy or a repairing tenancy[^key-8032b1a50bdf43f814a47004677577f2] —

- (a) may be initiated only by the landlord; or
- (b) may only determine that the rent is to be increased,

the provision concerned is void and the rent due as payable under the lease is instead to be reviewed and determined in accordance with this section.

(1)[^key-eec136515780050131102678ce6eebac] Where a lease constituting a limited duration tenancy or a modern limited duration tenancy[^key-a6956a1519db07a1f31ced4d2da764fc] makes no provision for review of rent, the rent due as payable under the lease is to be reviewed and determined in accordance with this section.

(1A)[^key-d9c4cec7ea90b56f0aeb7e58237a8f42] The rent due as payable under a lease constituting a repairing tenancy is to be reviewed and determined in accordance with this section.

(2)[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f] A rent review is to take place on such date as the landlord or tenant may specify in a notice in writing to the other provided that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]

- (a) the notice is given not less than one year nor more than two years before the date so specified; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]
- (b) the date so specified is not less than three years— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]
  - (i) in the case of the first rent review, from the commencement of the tenancy; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f] in the case of any subsequent rent review, from the date of the review under this subsection which precedes it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]

(3) On review, subject to subsections (4) to (7), the rent payable is the rent which the tenancy would reasonably be expected to fetch in the open market where there is a willing landlord and a willing tenant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]

- (a) disregarding— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]
  - (i) any effect on rent due to the fact that the tenant is in occupation of the land; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f] any distortion in rent due to a scarcity of lets; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]
- (b) having regard to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]
  - (i) the terms of the tenancy (other than those relating to rent); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f] information about rents for other agricultural tenancies (including when fixed) and any factors affecting those rents (or any of them) except any distortion due to a scarcity of lets; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f] the current economic conditions in the relevant sector of agriculture. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]

(4) Account is to be taken of any increase in the rental value of the land resulting from the use of the land for a purpose that is not an agricultural purpose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]

(5) No account is to be taken of any increase in the rental value of the land resulting from improvements— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]

- (a) so far as— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]
  - (i) they have been carried out wholly or partly at the expense of the tenant (whether or not that expense has been or will be reimbursed by any grant) without equivalent allowance or benefit having been made or given by the landlord in consideration of their execution; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f] they have not been carried out under an obligation imposed on the tenant by the terms of the lease; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]
- (b) which have been carried out by the landlord, in so far as the landlord has received or will receive any grant in respect of them, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]

nor may the rent be determined to be a higher amount than would have been payable if those improvements had not been so carried out. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]

(6) For the purposes of subsection (5)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]

- (a) subject to paragraph (b), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]**improvements *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]**” is to be construed by reference to Schedule 5 to the 1991 Act; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]
- (b) the continuous adoption by the tenant of a standard of farming or a system of farming more beneficial to the land than the standard or system required by the terms of the lease or, in so far as no system of farming is so required, than the system of farming normally practised on comparable agricultural land in the district, is to be treated as an improvement executed at the tenant’s expense. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]

(7) No account is to be taken of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]

- (a) any reduction in the rental value of the land as a result of any dilapidation or deterioration of, or damage to, fixed equipment or land caused or permitted by the tenant; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]
- (b) any such reduction resulting from— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]
  - (i) the use of any of the land, or changes to the land, for a purpose that is not an agricultural purpose; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f] the carrying out of conservation activities on the land. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]

(8) The rent determined in accordance with this section is to take effect from the date of the rent review. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42d30eea556989cafd4fd78e9025bd5f]

(2) The landlord may initiate a review of the rent that is to be payable under the lease by serving a notice in writing on the tenant.

(3) The tenant may initiate such a review by serving a notice in writing on the landlord.

(4) A notice served under subsection (2) or (3) is a “rent review notice”.
