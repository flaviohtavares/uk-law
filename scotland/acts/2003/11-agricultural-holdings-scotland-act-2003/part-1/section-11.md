# Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 2003, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (98).** Text as at 2021-02-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2003/11

> Extent: Scotland.

## 11. Variation of rent by Land Court[^c-a7ea7b00f9674119f929523edc989cc8]

*Extent: S*

Where it appears to the Land Court, in determining any matter in relation to a limited duration tenancy , a modern limited duration tenancy or a repairing tenancy[^c-b210826fec43e23089280c89aa246cc5] by virtue of section 13 , 16, 16A or 16B[^c-d2acd8552cbf01097afb456c10262909] , that it is equitable that the rent payable under the lease should be varied, it may vary the rent accordingly.
