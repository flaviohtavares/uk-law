## 46. Payment of compensation by incoming tenant[^c-99ec722bfe493978ff8565bcf7ed401d]

*Extent: S*

Subsections (2) to (5) of section 35 (payment of compensation by incoming tenant) of the 1991 Act (as read with Schedule 5 to that Act) apply to compensation which is payable or has been paid to an outgoing tenant of a short limited duration tenancy , a limited duration tenancy or a modern limited duration tenancy[^c-3a94593442ae78f9b7be8a5067dbda68] by the landlord under section 45(1) of this Act as they do to compensation to which that section of that Act applies, but as if—

- (a) in subsection (4), paragraph (a) were omitted;
- (b) in subsections (4) and (5), the references to an agricultural holding and the holding were references to the land; and
- (c) in subsection (5), the words “a new” were read as “an”.
