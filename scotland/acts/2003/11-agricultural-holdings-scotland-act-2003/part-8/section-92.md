# Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 2003, section 92

## 92. Ancillary provision

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may by order make such incidental, supplemental, consequential, transitional, transitory or saving provision as they consider necessary or expedient for the purposes of or in consequence of this Act.

(2) An order under subsection (1) may, in particular, amend provisions of any enactment which apply to 1991 Act tenancies or land held under 1991 Act tenancies so as to apply them to short limited duration tenancies , limited duration tenancies, modern limited duration tenancies and repairing tenancies[^key-1f5d8c73ef803a1bdd24435b246f0fad] or land held under such tenancies.
