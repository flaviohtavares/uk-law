# Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 2003, section 93

## 93. Interpretation

*Extent: S*

In this Act (unless the context requires otherwise)—

- “**the 1991 Act**” means the Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 1991 (c. 55);
- “**1991 Act tenancy**” is to be construed in accordance section 1;
- “**agriculture**” is to be construed in accordance with section 85 (interpretation) of the 1991 Act; and “**agricultural**” and “**non-agricultural**” are to be construed accordingly;
- “**agricultural land**” means land used for agriculture for the purposes of a trade or business;
- “**fixed equipment**” is to be construed by reference to section 85 of the 1991 Act;
- “**the Land Court**” means the Scottish Land Court;
- “**landlord**” means any person for the time being entitled to receive the rents under a lease constituting a tenancy and includes the executor, assignee, legatee, disponee, guardian or[^c-0daa84529db601ac8bc4f5e2a6def4c8] legal representative (within the meaning of Part I of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995 (c. 36)) of a landlord or the trustee or interim trustee in the sequestration, under the Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 2016, of a landlord's estate;[^c-7d7dbb844e400e6d7d03269aeb3fa8ac]
- “[^c-b64165d71d97f2a621b3b86ed10855c3] **limited duration tenancy** ” means a tenancy—
  - ((a)) created by virtue of section 5(1), or
  - ((b)) converted by virtue of section 5(2), (3) or (4),

  before the repeal of that section by section 85(2) of the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2016,
- “**modern limited duration tenancy**” is to be construed in accordance with section 5A,
- “**the Parliament**” means the Scottish Parliament;
- “[^c-abaeed92f9206885086e164c05a740e4]**repairing tenancy**” is to be construed in accordance with section 5C,
- “**short limited duration tenancy**” is to be construed in accordance with section 4;
- “**tenant**” means the holder of land under a tenancy constituted by a lease and includes the executor, assignee, legatee, disponee, guardian or[^c-d78505504a27c92e04a2e0cc6a910a59] legal representative (within the meaning of Part I of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995) of a tenant or the trustee or interim trustee in the sequestration, under the Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 2016, of a tenant's estate;[^c-ed355b9d39b7646cf683200de37c5f6b]
- “**termination**”, in relation to a tenancy, means the termination of the lease constituting the tenancy.
