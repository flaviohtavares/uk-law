# Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 2003, section 39

## 39. Procedure for making orders and regulations under section 33 and this Part[^c-6ab74b611fc80402cf3b67fcf0af577f]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-97d78a55349e854cf21a4c03d044a511] Schedule 1 (which makes provision for the making of certain orders and regulations under section 33 and this Part of this Act) shall have effect.

(2)[^c-07a1f2a2c48c3c619530d37f0b303bbe] The Scottish Ministers may by order vary the provisions of schedule 1 to this Act.

(3) An order under subsection (2) above may make different provision for different purposes.

(4) Subsection (2) above is without prejudice to section 33(8)(b) of this Act.
