# Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 2003, section 3

## 3. Use of landing nets[^key-e1ce751557336a01e7e1608e45dcb81d][^key-2eb9e97e9520341524a0d5f2447a48f1]

*Extent: S*

Nothing in section 1 or 2 of this Act shall be construed as prohibiting the use of a knotless non-metallic[^key-02ac0f75d3c6af618a1f422ad5836427] landing net (that is, a net the mesh of which is knotless and made of non-metallic material)[^key-e618e8ee9a131f12af4295613c8a8fff] as auxiliary to the taking of fish by rod and line.
