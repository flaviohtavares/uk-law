# Scottish Elections (Representation and Reform) Act 2025, section 36

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 36. Nomination of candidate in local government elections: home address form[^key-1d1f4fe3f933da83a6b1519e46283b77][^key-278683bf87551d7b70c2fbbfb75590be]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Local Government Elections Order 2011 ([S.S.I. 2011/399](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2011/399)) is modified as follows.

(2) In Schedule 1—

- (a) in paragraph 4(11)(b)(i) for the words “the name of the relevant area” substitute “which of the relevant areas under rule 4(12) the candidate wants to be made public”,
- (b) in paragraph 4(12) in the definition of “relevant area” after “Scotland” insert—

  > - (i) the electoral ward, or
  > - (ii)

  ,
- (c) in paragraph 14(2)(ab) after “located” insert “as indicated on the home address form”.
