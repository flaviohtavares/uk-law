# Scottish Elections (Representation and Reform) Act 2025, section 14

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 14. Power to amend the schedule[^key-a904915ee1f7714bd0fbd15e4fc8fae9][^key-09a9d28cb6de40fbc3fc4518881ee095]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may by regulations modify the schedule so as to add or remove an offence.

(2) Regulations under this section are subject to the affirmative procedure.
