# Scottish Elections (Representation and Reform) Act 2025, section 61

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 61. Consequential revocations[^key-de7f2637a740d88926371dfe866a967c][^key-e299c13bd566a3917bee48a30a531f02]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Elections (Details to appear on Election Material) Regulations 2020 ([S.S.I. 2020/297](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2020/297)) are revoked.

(2) The Scottish Elections (Details to appear on Election Publications) Regulations 2020 ([S.S.I. 2020/298](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2020/298)) are revoked.
