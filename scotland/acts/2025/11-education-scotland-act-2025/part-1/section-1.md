# Education (Scotland) Act 2025, section 1

> Text as at 2026-08-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1. Qualifications Scotland[^key-9f997f635624da203322cfbc0f2cf1c0]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-9d9086e25c57e54f2a0c838e09977e52] A body corporate to be known as Qualifications Scotland (in Gaelic, Teisteanasan Alba) is established.

(2)[^key-5bc73f68af2bc62d4068b4470bcb3755][^key-160e6a2672dacd8fa6e0296bfb7af579] Qualifications Scotland has the functions conferred on it by or under this Act and any other enactment.

(3)[^key-5bc73f68af2bc62d4068b4470bcb3755][^key-160e6a2672dacd8fa6e0296bfb7af579] Schedule 1 makes further provision about the status, membership, staffing, procedures and powers of Qualifications Scotland.
