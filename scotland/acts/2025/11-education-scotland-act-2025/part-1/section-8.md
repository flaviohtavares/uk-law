# Education (Scotland) Act 2025, section 8

> Text as at 2026-08-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 8. Consultation with Strategic Advisory Council[^key-7985cd19761f361eae1037d993a1229a]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-13fcf0090fe58e99c54e4fe7a5395f97] Qualifications Scotland must, in the exercise of its functions, consult the Strategic Advisory Council in every case in which it appears to Qualifications Scotland appropriate to do so.

(2)[^key-13fcf0090fe58e99c54e4fe7a5395f97][^key-ea7658647e1cc5c5f49e481df92a7120] Qualifications Scotland must have regard to any guidance issued by the Scottish Ministers as to the fulfilment of the requirement imposed by subsection (1).

(3)[^key-4097d08a002782c0063cdebe6115de5f] The Scottish Ministers must publish any guidance issued as mentioned in subsection (2).

(4)[^key-4097d08a002782c0063cdebe6115de5f] Qualifications Scotland must publish its procedures for—

- (a) fulfilling the requirement imposed by subsection (1),
- (b) providing responses to any advice provided to it by the council.

(5)[^key-4097d08a002782c0063cdebe6115de5f] If Qualifications Scotland revises the procedures published under subsection (4), it must publish the procedures as revised.
