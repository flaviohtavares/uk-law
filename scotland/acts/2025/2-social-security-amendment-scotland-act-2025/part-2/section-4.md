# Social Security (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2025, section 4

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 4. Power to make provision about late applications for assistance[^c-dce8838b6ff377f8da54edc1a28bf854]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The 2018 Act is modified as follows.

(2) In schedule 2 (carer’s assistance regulations), after paragraph 9 insert—

> **9A.** If the regulations include provision of the kind mentioned in paragraph 9(a), they may make provision about the circumstances in which an application made after the deadline specified in the regulations may be treated as if it had been made by that deadline.

.

(3) In schedule 3 (cold-spell heating assistance regulations), after paragraph 7 insert—

> **7A.** If the regulations include provision of the kind mentioned in paragraph 7(a), they may make provision about the circumstances in which an application made after the deadline specified in the regulations may be treated as if it had been made by that deadline.

.

(4) In schedule 4 (winter heating assistance regulations), after paragraph 5 insert—

> **5A.** If the regulations include provision of the kind mentioned in paragraph 5(a), they may make provision about the circumstances in which an application made after the deadline specified in the regulations may be treated as if it had been made by that deadline.

.

> **8A.** If the regulations include provision of the kind mentioned in paragraph 8(a), they may make provision about the circumstances in which an application made after the deadline specified in the regulations may be treated as if it had been made by that deadline.
