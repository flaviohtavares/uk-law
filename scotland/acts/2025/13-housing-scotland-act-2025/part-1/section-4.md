## 4. Scottish Ministers to review local authority report[^key-a3c11436030d176fa99d3a6a1dfa3757][^key-489a7981e9e6c6c1b3ef1adeebd81beb]

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers must consider a report received from a local authority under—

- (a) section 1(1),
- (b) section 3(6), or
- (c) section 3(7),

as soon as reasonably practicable after receipt of the report.
