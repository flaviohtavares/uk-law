## 63. Form of jury affirmation[^key-0a598a25ede17638b240212d3a61c17f][^key-70053ba8d99fc1fc292fcc04ec5c8775]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Oaths Act 1978 is amended as follows.

(2) In section 6 (form of affirmation)—

- (a) in subsection (1), for “subsection (2)” substitute “subsections (2) and (3)”,
- (b) after subsection (2) insert—

  > (3) In the case of a jury in solemn proceedings, the affirmation is to be in the following form:
  >
  > <table>
  > <tbody>
  > <tr><td colspan="2"><em>On being asked by the clerk of court</em>: “Do you solemnly, sincerely and truly declare and affirm that you will well and truly try the accused and give a true verdict according to the evidence?”</td></tr>
  > <tr><td colspan="2"><em>The jurors who are affirming to reply</em>: “I do”.</td></tr>
  > </tbody>
  > </table>

  .
