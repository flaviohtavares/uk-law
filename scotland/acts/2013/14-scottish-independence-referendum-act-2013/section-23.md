# Scottish Independence Referendum Act 2013, section 23

> Text as at 2013-12-18.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 23. Information for voters

*Extent: S*

The Electoral Commission must take such steps as they consider appropriate to promote public awareness and understanding in Scotland about—

- (a) the referendum,
- (b) the referendum question, and
- (c) voting in the referendum.
