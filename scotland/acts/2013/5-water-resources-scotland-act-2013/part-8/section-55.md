## 55. Key definitions

*Extent: S*

In this Act—

- “**the 1968 Act**” means the Sewerage (Scotland) Act 1968,
- “**the 1980 Act**” means the Water (Scotland) Act 1980,
- “**the 2002 Act**” means the Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002,
- “**the 2003 Act**” means the Water Environment and Water Services (Scotland) Act 2003,
- “**the 2005 Act**” means the Water Services etc. (Scotland) Act 2005.
