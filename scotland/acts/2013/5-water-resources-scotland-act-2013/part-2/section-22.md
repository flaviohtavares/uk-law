## 22. Other definitions for Part[^c-20ffdf17107c19c2ec601eff41f8b29b]

*Extent: S*

In this Part—

- “**holder**” means person in whose favour the Scottish Ministers' approval under this Part has effect for the time being,
- “**premises**” includes any land, building or other place,
- “**SEPA**” means the Scottish Environment Protection Agency.
