## 21. 2018 Regulations[^c-6a317f7fbb57cc9503cf9c79608dfe7e][^c-f61fc04db950d26debe5ca82f90b9698]

*Extent: S*

(1) Nothing in this Part affects any requirement for an authorisation under the 2018[^c-fcb17f52609e5b52041c14b6f0936ee3] Regulations.

(2) An authorisation under the 2018 Regulations is subject to approval of a qualifying abstraction by the Scottish Ministers under this Part, so far as relevant in relation to the activities to which the authorisation relates (except where the authorisation exempts the abstraction under section 7(1)).

(3) In this Part—

- (a) “**the 2018 Regulations**” means the Environmental Authorisations (Scotland) Regulations 2018,[^c-12c1f5b992c34e289e4c78610da89e1c]
