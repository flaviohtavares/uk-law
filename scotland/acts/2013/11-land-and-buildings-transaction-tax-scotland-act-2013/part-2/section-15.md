# Land and Buildings Transaction Tax (Scotland) Act 2013, section 15

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 15. Chargeable transaction[^key-6f503d782510495cc71c4a4d139fa0e3]

*Extent: S*

A land transaction is a chargeable transaction unless it is—

- (a) an exempt transaction, or
- (b) otherwise exempt from charge.
