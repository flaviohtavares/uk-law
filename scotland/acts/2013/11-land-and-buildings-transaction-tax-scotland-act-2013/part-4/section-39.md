# Land and Buildings Transaction Tax (Scotland) Act 2013, section 39

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 39. Power to amend period in which returns must be made[^key-4380aa0067e78985089f63d74334931c]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may, by order, amend a provision listed in subsection (2) so as to substitute, for the period for the time being specified there, a different period.

(2) The provisions are—

- (a) section 29(3),
- (b) section 31(3),
- (c) section 33(3),
- (d) in schedule 19 (leases)—
  - (i) paragraph 10(3),
  - (ii) paragraph 11(3),
  - (iii) paragraph 20(3)(a),
  - (iv) paragraph 22(2)(a),
  - (v) paragraph 30(2)(a).
