## SCHEDULE 13B — (introduced by section 27)[^key-b84392918d9f57aee409a0c243731917]

*Extent: S*

### BUILDING SOCIETIES RELIEF

#### 1. The relief

A land transaction effected by or in consequence of any of the following is exempt from charge—

- (a) an amalgamation of two or more building societies under section 93 of the Building Societies Act 1986 (amalgamations),
- (b) a transfer of engagements between building societies under section 94 of that Act (transfer of engagements).

#### 2. Interpretation

In this schedule, “building society” has the meaning given by section 119(1) of the Building Societies Act 1986.
