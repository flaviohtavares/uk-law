# Land and Buildings Transaction Tax (Scotland) Act 2013, section 27

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 27. Reliefs

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-67c35c1dd8a5d61e1baed515f02e19c5][^c-32f9080068f4b85b343fa7c9ecfdf7a9] The following schedules provide for reliefs from the tax in relation to certain land transactions—

- schedule 3 (sale and leaseback relief),
- schedule 4 (relief for certain acquisitions of residential property),
- schedule 4A (first-time buyer relief),[^c-b73d2a2e4a86b330d615c98ad76d65c4]
- schedule 5 (multiple dwellings relief),
- schedule 6 (relief for certain acquisitions by registered social landlords),
- schedule 6A (relief for certain acquisitions by local authorities),[^c-8e60223e48ecd572b15331da00f35ef0]
- schedule 7 (alternative property finance relief),
- schedule 8 (relief for alternative finance investment bonds),
- schedule 9 (crofting community right to buy relief),
- schedule 10 (group relief),
- schedule 10A (sub-sale development relief),[^c-0b475c1531731f4ece7658a8b62a3cc3]
- schedule 11 (reconstruction relief and acquisition relief),
- schedule 12 (relief for incorporation of limited liability partnership),
- schedule 13 (charities relief),
- schedule 13A (friendly societies relief),[^c-60eff032090b9bdd3427b956dac3f001]
- schedule 13B (building societies relief),
- schedule 14 (relief for certain compulsory purchases),
- schedule 15 (relief for compliance with planning obligations),
- schedule 16 (public bodies relief).
- schedule 16A (visiting forces and international military headquarters reliefs),[^c-261a5786f2ba4fc4fbc2b49fcd4671cf]
- schedule 16B (relief for property accepted in satisfaction of tax),
- schedule 16C (lighthouses relief),
- schedule 16D (green freeports relief)[^c-609e6d0e7867b4edd02f365507ee4fb4],
- schedule 16E (investment zones relief).[^c-304d0e76cb73139b90664c117c20b6fd]

(2)[^c-2be9d2a7a981089043bfb2360f6343d7] Any relief under any of those schedules must be claimed in the first return made in relation to the transaction or in an amendment of that return.

(2A)[^c-4bc9d90a6543daa1548e5c4033a455c9] For the period allowed for amendment of returns, see section 83 of the Revenue Scotland and Tax Powers Act 2014 (asp 16).

(3)[^c-12f4e567c58b4e5202fe7d72d4a3ab75] The Scottish Ministers may, by order, modify this Act so as to—

- (a) add a relief,
- (b) modify an existing relief, or
- (c) remove a relief.

(4)[^c-12f4e567c58b4e5202fe7d72d4a3ab75] An order under subsection (3) may also modify any other enactment that the Scottish Ministers consider appropriate.
