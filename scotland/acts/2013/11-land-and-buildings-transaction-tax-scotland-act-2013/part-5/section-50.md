# Land and Buildings Transaction Tax (Scotland) Act 2013, section 50

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 50. Trusts[^key-d12bb8aee70dd06647eaabeaa123e491]

*Extent: S*

(1) Schedule 18 makes provision about the application of this Act in relation to trusts.

(2) The Scottish Ministers may, by regulations, modify schedule 18.

(3)[^key-7ba1fa36f3fe0cdfbd9f129f236c46e6] See also section 248 of the Revenue Scotland and Tax Powers Act 2014 (asp 16) (reviews, appeals etc.: trustees).
