# Land and Buildings Transaction Tax (Scotland) Act 2013, SCHEDULE 16B

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 16B — RELIEF FOR PROPERTY ACCEPTED IN SATISFACTION OF TAX[^key-7e5ebba50c8498d8259df1874f3969f9]

*Extent: S*

(introduced by section 27)

### The relief

**1.** A land transaction entered into under section 9(4) of the National Heritage Act 1980 (disposal of property accepted by the Commissioners for Revenue and Customs in satisfaction of tax) by a person mentioned in section 9(2) of that Act is exempt from charge.
