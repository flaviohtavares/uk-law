# Land and Buildings Transaction Tax (Scotland) Act 2013, section 58

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 58. Connected persons[^c-79302942ae6fabfcce16a5e21961e1ad]

*Extent: S*

Section 1122 of the Corporation Tax Act 2010 (c.4) (connected persons) has effect for the purposes of the following provisions—

- (a) section 14,
- (b) section 22,
- (c) section 23,
- (d) section 57,
- (e) paragraphs 1, 11 and 13 of schedule 2,
- (f) schedule 4,
- (g) Part 5 of schedule 5,
- (h) schedule 8,
- (ha)[^c-01653e42f4eb0dfa4b557e61dcfa2f1e] paragraph 17 of schedule 10A,
- (hb)[^c-1fd54c829bfea2252cfc4c3a999de13b] schedule 16D,
- (hc)[^c-45e6b06d9ff9c1ad6e432ac57234d9b7] schedule 16E,
- (i) schedule 17 (but see paragraph 49),
- (j) paragraph 17 of schedule 19.
