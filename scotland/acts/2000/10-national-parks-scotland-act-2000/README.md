National Parks (Scotland) Act 2000

2000 asp 10

An Act of the Scottish Parliament to make provision for National Parks.

The Bill for this Act of the Scottish Parliament was passed by the Parliament on 5th July 2000 and received Royal Assent on 9th August 2000
