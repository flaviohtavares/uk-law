# Abolition of Feudal Tenure etc. (Scotland) Act 2000, section 75

> Extent: Scotland.

## 75. Saving for contractual rights

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c21148721] As respects any land granted in feu before the appointed day, nothing in this Act shall affect any right (other than a right to feuduty) included in the grant in so far as that right is contractual as between the parties to the grant (or, as the case may be, as between one of them and a person to whom any such right is assigned).

(2)[^c21148691] In construing the expression “parties to the grant” in subsection (1) above, any enactment or rule of law whereby investiture is deemed renewed when the parties change shall be disregarded.
