## 87. Effect of order on existing parental right[^key-52a53ad4f7bba8a4d4c47df1f62cbafe]

*Extent: S*

The making of a permanence order extinguishes the parental right mentioned in subsection (1)(a) of section 2 of the 1995 Act of—

- (a) a parent of the child in respect of whom the order is made,
- (b) a guardian of such a child,

which, immediately before the making of the order, vested in the parent or, as the case may be, guardian.
