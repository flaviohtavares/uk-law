## 102. Effect of subsequent adoption order on permanence order[^key-ec812eddc0db0d0cdb25f993e6de58ae]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subsection (2) applies where—

- (a) a permanence order is in force in respect of a child, and
- (b) an adoption order is made in respect of the child.

(2) The permanence order ceases to have effect on the making of the adoption order.
