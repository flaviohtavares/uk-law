## 106. Child subject to compulsory supervision order: duty to refer to Principal Reporter[^c-e9199994f4a6cf770ce38fb824709a60]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-1ae16cc91d7c05e5c01f3a90d13a870c] Subsection (2) applies where—

- (a) a child is subject to a compulsory supervision order[^c-f0e7f35e7248f06b0111853f7c8b2a2b],
- (b) a registered adoption service is satisfied that the best interests of the child would be served by placing the child for adoption, and
- (c) it intends to place the child for adoption.

(2)[^c-1ae16cc91d7c05e5c01f3a90d13a870c] The registered adoption service must refer the child's case to the Principal Reporter.

(3)[^c-d2cfe5cc1ca3cabb2a9537e9e4d897af][^c-fa6abfdab475ff3e9f4afc35d5945e9b] The Scottish Ministers may make regulations specifying by reference to the occurrence of an event or events described in the regulations the period of time during which a referral under this section is to be made.
