## 109. Proceedings to be in private[^key-b5642c482bfd2e559f97d767356ac6f8]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-3f9639403ea1300e79edac2ffa1e9b86] Any proceedings before the court relating to applications under any of the provisions mentioned in subsection (2) must be heard and determined in private unless the court otherwise directs.

(2) Those provisions are—

- (a) section 24,
- (b) section 29,
- (c) section 30,
- (d) section 59,
- (e) section 80,
- (f) section 92,
- (g) section 93, [^key-e0cee54ad9aa940fbc6d0f36cd780cf0]...
- (ga) section[^key-3917380465bdc1fe53298485d4bb2d25]   98, and
- (h) section 99.
