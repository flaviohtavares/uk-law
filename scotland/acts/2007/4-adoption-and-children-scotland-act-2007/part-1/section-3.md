# Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007, section 3

## 3. Adoption service: regulations[^c-359396b1c0d6dd9def00bbfdb5aab7fe]

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers may by regulations—

- (a) amend subsection (4) or (5) of section 1 by—
  - (i) adding further services,
  - (ii) modifying the services mentioned in those subsections,
- (b)[^c-775512f05274899d7738effdde966f2b] make further provision about adoption services.
