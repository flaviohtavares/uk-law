# Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007, section 37

## 37. Information to be kept about adoptions[^key-4cf3954b9d7ca768bc4483933d031655][^key-6f08420b23753efb0da9dd9796de1585][^key-1aa9b1ca6bb367ab31400e0712afec2e][^key-1aa9b1ca6bb367ab31400e0712afec2e]

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers may make regulations for or in connection with specifying—

- (a) the information which an adoption agency must keep in relation to adoptions, and
- (b) the form and manner in which it must keep the information.
