## 34. Contravention of section 72 no bar to making order[^c-522260f3f73a62681421e7f24d67eef2][^c-1aa9b1ca6bb367ab31400e0712afec2e][^c-3f5a91cf248fdf7dcea8b2c998794643][^c-1aa9b1ca6bb367ab31400e0712afec2e]

*Extent: S*

The court may make an adoption order in relation to a child even where it is found that the applicants have, as respects the child, contravened section 72.
