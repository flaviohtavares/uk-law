# Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007, section 36

## 36. Revocation of compulsory supervision order on making of adoption order[^c-9b6d69d86f6705ad8a43375465be7fb5][^c-1aa9b1ca6bb367ab31400e0712afec2e][^c-1aa9b1ca6bb367ab31400e0712afec2e][^c-1dc1a71571ae177ac0914f8cb19e4209]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subsection (2) applies where—

- (a) the child to be adopted is subject to a compulsory supervision order[^c-dd244a023c87baf8272de8a7112000eb], and
- (b) the appropriate court is satisfied that, were it to make an adoption order in relation to the child, it would no longer be necessary that, for the protection, guidance, treatment or control of the child, the child be subject to the compulsory supervision order.[^c-0477f5112749b71521b3eae421f06cbc]

(2) The court must make an order providing that, on the making of the adoption order, the compulsory supervision order[^c-e7549e62775c0218d1f6e4c8f5e0deba] ceases to have effect.
