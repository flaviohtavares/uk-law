## 121. Short title and commencement

*Extent: S*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007.

(2) The provisions of this Act (except this section and sections 116 and 117) come into force on such day as the Scottish Ministers may by order appoint.

(3) An order under subsection (2) may appoint different days for different purposes.
