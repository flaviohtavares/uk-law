# Legal Profession and Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 2007, section 78

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (236).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2007/5

> Extent: Scotland.

## 78. Ancillary provision

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may by order make such incidental, supplemental, consequential, transitional, transitory or saving provision as they consider necessary or expedient for the purposes, or in consequence, of, or for giving full effect to, this Act or any provision of it.

(1A)[^key-f29f1267f20894620c565d666a75402d] The Scottish Ministers may by order[^key-ff33fc8f205a8637b0d27228e7666386] make such further provision as, having regard to the effect of the Legal Services Act 2007 so far as concerning the subject matter of Parts 1 and 2 of this Act (and applying in Scotland), they consider necessary or expedient in connection with this Act or any related provisions of the 1980 Act.

(2) An order under this section may—

- (a) make different provision for different purposes;
- (b) modify any enactment, instrument or document.
