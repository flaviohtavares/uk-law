## 80. Interpretation

*Extent: S*

In this Act—

- “**the 1980 Act**” means the Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1980 (c. 46);
- “**the 1986 Act**” means the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986 (c. 47);
- “**the 1990 Act**” means the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990 (c. 40).
