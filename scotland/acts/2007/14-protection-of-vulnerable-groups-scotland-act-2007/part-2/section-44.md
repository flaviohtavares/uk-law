# Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2007/14
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 44. The Scheme[^key-de61ad69d67b89978cec68992b456b73][^key-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^key-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b][^key-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^key-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b]

*Extent: S*

Ministers are to administer a scheme (“**the Scheme**”) under which information about individuals who carry out, or wish to carry out, regulated roles[^key-50c16dd4dd29cda3ee3f0904a686e626] with children or protected adults is—

- (a) collated, and
- (b) disclosed,

in accordance with this Part.
