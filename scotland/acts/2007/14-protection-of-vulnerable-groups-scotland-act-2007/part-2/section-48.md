## 48. Scheme record[^c-cdf97d2a37d8774f357417f9ee3e61f6][^c-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^c-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b][^c-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^c-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b]

*Extent: S*

A scheme record is a document comprising—

- (a)[^c-5009dc6ad030bf47b386c419eefe37a5] the information to be contained in a confirmation of scheme membership under section 46(2), and
- (b) vetting information about the scheme member which Ministers discover as a result of enquiries or arrangements made under section 47.
