# Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007, section 59

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2007/14
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 59. Withdrawal from scheme[^key-0ab1d8f23c155d3f63528575b3228bf6][^key-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^key-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b][^key-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^key-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b]

*Extent: S*

Ministers must remove a scheme member from the Scheme in relation to a type of regulated role[^key-b59c6dbce5e50eac6d300ea00bf51773] if—

- (a) the scheme member applies to be so removed, and
- (b) they are satisfied that the scheme member is not carrying out that type of regulated role[^key-6b476a68eefc7134cefc697f64078b21].
