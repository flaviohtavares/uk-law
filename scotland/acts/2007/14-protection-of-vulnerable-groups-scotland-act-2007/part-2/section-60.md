# Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007, section 60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2007/14
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 60. Notice of removal[^key-c5ded3a89f399df1847575cd576bf4cd][^key-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^key-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b][^key-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^key-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b]

*Extent: S*

(1) Ministers must give notice confirming that they have removed an individual from the Scheme under section 45B(3)(a) or (6),[^key-c91eba41ed3e5e4832a535d44a0158e0] 58 or 59 to—

- (a) the individual, and
- (b) any other person whom they think fit.

(2) A notice under subsection (1) must—

- (a) state the type of regulated role[^key-8931dc3c413e75c2599e88b57109fc3a] in relation to which the individual has been removed, and
- (b) say why the individual has been removed.

(2A)[^key-278f890cc92401e114440943c2ebb0a5] Where Ministers decide under section 59A(1) not to continue to consider whether to list the individual a notice under subsection (1) must also give notice of that fact.

(3) A notice under subsection (1)(b) need not comply with subsection (2)(b) if Ministers think that it would be inappropriate for the recipient to be informed of the reason for removal.
