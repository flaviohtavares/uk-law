# Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2007/14
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 76. Police access to scheme information[^c-c68e17c9277c3c72404b51381b25fa5d][^c-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^c-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b][^c-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^c-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b]

*Extent: S*

(1) Ministers must make available to the chief constable[^c-7218866a7472fb4ff1d40377ed04d828]—

- (a) the name of each individual participating in the Scheme,
- (b) confirmation of whether each such individual participates in the Scheme in relation to regulated roles[^c-87319a229ba00270382bd58988ce611d] with—
  - (i) children,
  - (ii) adults, or
  - (iii) both, and
- (c) any other information held by Ministers by virtue of their administration of the Scheme which Ministers consider would enable or assist constables of the Police Service of Scotland[^c-2d64f578f9fd11eac67079af88c5bf72] to satisfy themselves as to the identity of such individuals.

(2) Information disclosed under subsection (1) may be used by constables of the Police Service of Scotland[^c-cc94589edb09c7316c9545d78a74c897] only for —[^c-93a26a710796817f590eeadbd5ad4b0f]

- (a) the performance of the chief constable's functions under this Part, or
- (b) the law enforcement purposes within the meaning of section 31 of the Data Protection Act 2018.
