# Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007, section 64

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2007/14
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 64. Power to use personal data to check applicant's identity[^key-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^key-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-92d62c5c9410fea77fd2f553efdc0239] Ministers may use information given to them by personal data holders to check evidence of identity given to them for the purposes of section 62.

(2)[^key-d8a83f90fe093b2be17fe0546424d379][^key-ea642b89c413ebdb457d4af435c52446] Personal data holders are—

- The Identity and Passport Service
- The Driver and Vehicle Licensing Agency
- Ministers of the Crown in connection with keeping of records of national insurance numbers
- Such other persons holding data about individuals as may be prescribed
