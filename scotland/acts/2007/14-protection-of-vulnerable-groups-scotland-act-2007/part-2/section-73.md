## 73. Consideration of suitability[^key-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^key-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b]

*Extent: S*

References in this Part to a person (“A”) considering an individual's suitability to carry out, or to be offered or supplied for, any type of regulated role[^key-5107413e01a8fbff8910910cd187aa63] are references to A considering the individual's suitability—

- (a)[^M_I_d835ce3c-2408-445f-ac8c-806210e6b435] to carry out that type of regulated role[^key-5831e1aff0122e6a3fe7c597785d04ae] for A,
- (b)[^M_I_d835ce3c-2408-445f-ac8c-806210e6b435] to be supplied by A to carry out that type of regulated role[^key-5d7f4e619704f39d7647a2d2ae5486b4] for another person,
- (c)[^key-bebd847663d10e6ec575b1a0b344a60a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^key-bebd847663d10e6ec575b1a0b344a60a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^key-bebd847663d10e6ec575b1a0b344a60a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f)[^key-bebd847663d10e6ec575b1a0b344a60a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g)[^key-1aad01f23357e4843cf10d69a36d2abe][^key-8c02f91624d61efd8c06c4ffa47c0c1c] for any other prescribed purpose.
