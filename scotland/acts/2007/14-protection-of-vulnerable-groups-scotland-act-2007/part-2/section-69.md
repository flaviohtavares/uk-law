## 69. Penalties for offences relating to confirmation of scheme membership[^key-309994481846605924cd48c6fbdfac5b][^key-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^key-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b][^key-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^key-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b][^key-bedb86053b137de98127bc05b2b890f9]

*Extent: S*

A person guilty of an offence under section 65, 66 or 67 is liable on summary conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, or to both.
