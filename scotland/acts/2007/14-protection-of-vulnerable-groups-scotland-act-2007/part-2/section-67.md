## 67. Unlawful requests for and use of confirmation of scheme membership[^c-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^c-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b][^c-cebc9b00b0a8bbf9f5ac517cd3820803]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-632a79cf9320b874b9a84526ace0f4e3] It is an offence to request provision of, or to otherwise seek sight of, a confirmation of scheme membership under section 54[^c-3309e5cece0056171ef15f0d9f6839ff] for a purpose other than the permitted purpose.

(2)[^c-632a79cf9320b874b9a84526ace0f4e3] It is an offence to use a confirmation of scheme membership made available under section 54[^c-343a981c57679527ed927db22bbb1d3e] for a purpose other than the permitted purpose.

(3) The permitted purpose is to enable or assist a person (“Z”) to consider the suitability of the individual to whom the confirmation of scheme membership[^c-52840717e492f8b18a39d74fd7b07e97] relates—

- (a)[^c-632a79cf9320b874b9a84526ace0f4e3][^c-e86036f6f20b04dd53d68766bcb544aa] to carry out, or to be offered or supplied for, the type of regulated role to which the confirmation of scheme membership relates,
- (b)[^c-8ba0abda6ea21945bcb9e8874abbd1a0][^c-19195a4594e81ebbd37562b6797c6c3f] to carry out that type of regulated role[^c-ed54649979ae808de41d96f8f39da503] in prescribed circumstances for any person other than Z in pursuance of arrangements under which services are provided to Z.

(4)[^c-e1978b73ae4e1f93cce78472b8f41fc2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
