# Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007, section 58

>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 58. Removal from scheme[^c-d1bca8afd9b52df360ff3003620c46ca][^c-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^c-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b][^c-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^c-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b]

*Extent: S*

(1) Ministers must remove a scheme member from the Scheme in relation to a type of regulated role[^c-bbd3a50f0b342b7f1de2a06d5bb7a296] if they—

- (a) bar the member from carrying out that type of role[^c-870960b1e386f1d320230fd92203c032] by listing the member, or
- (b) become aware that the member has otherwise been barred from that type of regulated role[^c-98879b07b1d782f91dd37550492f9e01].

(2) Ministers may remove from the Scheme any scheme member who fails to pay any fee for participation in the Scheme which is prescribed under section 70.
