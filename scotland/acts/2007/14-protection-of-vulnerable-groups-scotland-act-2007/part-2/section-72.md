## 72. Procedure[^key-28d375420c435876f27812b6413821ea][^key-4f9b9624a37f42f7f53b8d79be6fb8f6][^key-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^key-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b][^key-d0188832d4d59bb40c21cf2b0aba075d][^key-2c573d169c1d48af0ff2422be30c9b2b]

*Extent: S*

(1) Ministers may, by regulations, make such further provision about the administration of the Scheme as they think fit.

(2) Regulations may, in particular—

- (a) prescribe further procedure relating to applications to join, or to be removed from, the Scheme or to renew membership of the Scheme[^key-f8258255689395e565f6d7d83179161e],
- (b) prescribe circumstances in which scheme members are to be removed from the Scheme,
- (c)[^key-53eb5fe1d45e738fb8a9fed60f0bd2d2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^key-88893b9ea006046b04f254f67c1e0400] prescribe further procedure for making requests for confirmations of scheme membership under section 54 and making such confirmations available in pursuance of such requests.
