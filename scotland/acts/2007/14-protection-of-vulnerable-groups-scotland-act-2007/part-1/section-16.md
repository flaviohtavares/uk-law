## 16. Inclusion in adults' list after consideration[^key-a458830565af7f756e2640f6125df3f2]

*Extent: S*

Ministers must list an individual in the adults' list if, after considering whether to do so, they are satisfied by information relating to the individual's conduct that the individual is unsuitable to carry out regulated roles with[^key-2dc608663974509c440b503adb43e1c6] adults.
