# Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007, section 19

## 19. Information held by public bodies etc.

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-d40941dac0852c94d7572387c1ff1825] Ministers may, for the purpose of enabling or assisting Ministers to decide whether to list an individual, require—

- (a) any person who holds vetting information of a type prescribed under section 49(1)(d) to provide them with any such vetting information relating to the individual,
- (b) any of the persons set out in subsection (3) to provide them with any information held by the person which Ministers think might be relevant.

(2)[^key-d40941dac0852c94d7572387c1ff1825] Information provided under subsection (1)(b) may, in particular, be information which relates to—

- (a) the regulated role[^key-f341702638d83d7982881c9e161acf0e] concerned, or
- (b) the protection of children or protected adults in general, or of any child or protected adult in particular.

(3)[^key-c9b970474fc6f3cd648c034b362f15e2] The persons who may be required to provide information under subsection (1)(b) are—

- Councils
- The General Teaching Council for Scotland
- Health Boards and Special Health Boards
- An integration joint board established by order under section 9 of the Public Bodies (Joint Working) (Scotland) Act 2014[^key-1e4e6111aa2276091d9df4fc1dccab30]
- Healthcare Improvement Scotland[^key-4a8997b3843bae35fed03da312dd3c69]
- His Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education in Scotland[^key-abfbb5813695cf89baf0f1e6aa72f20a]
- Her Majesty's Chief Inspector of Prisons for Scotland
- [^key-b1046558b647111c6a07381e8a83f422]...
- The Registrar of Chiropractors
- The registrar of dentists and dental care professionals
- The registrar of the General Medical Council
- The registrar of the General Optical Council
- The Registrar of health professionals
- The Registrar of Independent Schools in Scotland
- The Registrar of nurses and midwives
- The Registrar of Osteopaths
- The registrar of pharmacists[^key-a2ea76e3a624526b91a83671a5103306]
- [^key-2647f1ac2fbfcdc6d0c36279144f076c]...
- The Scottish Social Services Council
- Social Care and Social Work Improvement Scotland[^key-19f1a2e36a2026badbb63294f4135d57]
- [^key-b529c93bff1260c491f6ab5500e4ec05]...
- Any other person specified in an order made by Ministers
