## 25. Application for removal from list

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-0e77749aa8bdfcc426c962f612e5c94f] A listed individual may apply to Ministers for removal from—

- (a) the children's list, or
- (b) the adults' list.

(2)[^c-0e77749aa8bdfcc426c962f612e5c94f] References in this section to “the list” are to be read as references to the list from which the individual has applied for removal.

(3) An application for removal from the list is competent only if—

- (a)[^c-f340b09e18a8856c369051811c4e3824][^c-3e90a2b76ad60cadb3d184bd22deb827] it is made after the end of such period as may be prescribed (beginning on such date as may be prescribed), or
- (b)[^c-0e77749aa8bdfcc426c962f612e5c94f][^c-a667a5c33af9976426f2b9fc8e479068] Ministers are satisfied that there has been a relevant change in the applicant's circumstances since the applicant—
  - (i) was listed, or
  - (ii) last made an application for removal from the list under this section.

(4)[^c-0e77749aa8bdfcc426c962f612e5c94f] A period may not be prescribed under subsection (3)(a) in relation to a particular individual.

(4A)[^c-bba21e2e1692150016e17cd53d95c125] For the purposes of subsection (3)(b), a change in the applicant's circumstances is relevant if it is relevant to the question whether the applicant is no longer unsuitable to carry out regulated roles with children or, as the case may be, regulated roles with adults.
