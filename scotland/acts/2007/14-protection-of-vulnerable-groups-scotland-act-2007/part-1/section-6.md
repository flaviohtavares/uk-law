# Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007, section 6

## 6. Reference relating to matters occurring before provisions come into force

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-a70a88c15e4227ecded2dc34f5f2f117] The duties in sections 3 to 5 do not apply where the individual stopped carrying out the regulated role[^c-8d04cc8fc07ea1d642bf53bb67dfeac0] (or, as the case may be, the organisation's opinion was formed) before the date on which the provisions imposing the duties come into force.

(2)[^c-0305b6a93c98d4c9b51189d2bb830b76][^c-98dbb8b7a6c28c4671a08bab784f5be2] But an organisation may give Ministers any prescribed information which it holds in relation to such an individual if it wishes to do so.
