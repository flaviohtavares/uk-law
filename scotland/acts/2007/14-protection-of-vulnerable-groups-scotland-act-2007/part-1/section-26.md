## 26. Determination of application for removal from list[^key-67a4228590280dc236eab9d0deb11bf6]

*Extent: S*

(1) Ministers must determine a competent application for removal from the children's list—

- (a) where they are satisfied that the applicant is no longer unsuitable to carry out regulated roles[^key-fd3eb3d3e7c5b345d49631c932a91873] with children, by removing the individual from the list, or
- (b) where they are not so satisfied, by refusing the application.

(2) Ministers must determine a competent application for removal from the adults' list—

- (a) where they are satisfied that the applicant is no longer unsuitable to carry out regulated roles with[^key-13eb9813e3a54e77543a6560213652e2] adults, by removing the individual from the list, or
- (b) where they are not so satisfied, by refusing the application.
