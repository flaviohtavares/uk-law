# Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2007/14

> Extent: Scotland.

## 34. Barred individuals not to carry out regulated roles[^key-99e273b327a7abb1d0234859ec70c21b][^key-c9e81f941ad30a3187e69e7a20a4f224]

*Extent: S*

(1) It is an offence for an individual to carry out, or to seek or agree to carry out, any regulated role[^key-2a029bae41daf8fcd60da9af81374e47] from which the individual is barred.

(2) It is a defence for an individual charged with an offence under subsection (1) to prove that the individual did not know, and could not reasonably be expected to have known—

- (a) that the individual was barred from that regulated role[^key-18f7c2b15d8c5177b18a4059f3e2889e], or
- (b) that the role concerned was a regulated role[^key-a7e3630df4b61e4be13b9800323d5e3e].
