## 36. Personnel suppliers not to supply barred individuals for regulated roles[^key-cb4793377e5b966ce833e1351a9b1356][^key-8bb567fc320227a664fd0ff3676d72e6]

*Extent: S*

(1) A personnel supplier commits an offence if it—

- (a) offers or supplies an individual who is barred from regulated roles[^key-07b6cf6f9dff800c71d2c3d4818e6efd] to an organisation, and
- (b) knows or has reason to believe that the organisation will make arrangements for the individual to carry out a regulated role[^key-d498ed2051402afe8e9291f8332a7163] from which the individual is barred.

(2) It is a defence for a personnel supplier charged with an offence under subsection (1) to prove that it did not know, and could not reasonably be expected to have known, that the individual was barred from the regulated role[^key-ea90d0f1f4cc8122cc7d50f3fe86cb22].
