# Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2007/14
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 42. Power to regulate procedure etc.

*Extent: S*

(1) Ministers may, by regulations, make further provision about—

- (a)[^key-d9e24b5706b1c67628d25ff73d3f844b] the information about listed individuals which is to be included in the children's list and adults' list,
- (b)[^key-d9e24b5706b1c67628d25ff73d3f844b] the maintenance of those lists, and
- (c)[^key-cb8eb6cb8a7bf6b9a62035e037e526b8][^key-a70869ebf41ed7663ec60e924ee6f4b5] the procedure which is to be followed in relation to any decision which Ministers are authorised or required to take under this Part.

(2)[^key-cb8eb6cb8a7bf6b9a62035e037e526b8][^key-a70869ebf41ed7663ec60e924ee6f4b5] The regulations may, in particular, make provision in relation to the time within which anything has to be done.
