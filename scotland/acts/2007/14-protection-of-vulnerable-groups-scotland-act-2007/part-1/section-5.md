# Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007, section 5

## 5. Reference by employment business[^key-121c7dcc2e3fde60ce9c1714a5910adb][^key-bbc56de41b4ef92fa6172c864b4ad184]

*Extent: S*

A personnel supplier which carries on an employment business must give Ministers any prescribed information which it holds in relation to an individual whom it has offered or supplied for a regulated role[^key-7a0ee6135d0dbdf21408ea708e925507] if—

- (a) it has dismissed the individual on the referral ground,
- (b) the individual has otherwise stopped carrying out the regulated role[^key-604b04412eee79545ea51fa90ac79e84] in circumstances in which it would or might have dismissed the individual on the referral ground if the individual had not so stopped, or
- (c) it has, on the referral ground, decided not to offer or supply the individual for another regulated role[^key-d8c68172754fee312447b36807671647].
