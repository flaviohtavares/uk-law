## 4. Reference by employment agency[^key-abe03cc64f1fe1270de211ff84f0253f][^key-08aeb7b97161f4a5a9a9e1353eddd885]

*Extent: S*

A personnel supplier which carries on an employment agency must give Ministers any prescribed information which it holds in relation to an individual who is or has been carrying out a regulated role[^key-cc477ed1be25301c0e2bbec4a061bc50] if it has, on the referral ground, decided—

- (a) not to do any further business with the individual, or
- (b) not to—
  - (i) find the individual another regulated role[^key-af61610fd06990044443ddf34428d28a], or
  - (ii) offer or supply the individual for another regulated role[^key-5155f3888da7a34d228f2a0af6b3fb80].
