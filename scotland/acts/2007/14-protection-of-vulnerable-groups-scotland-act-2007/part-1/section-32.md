## 32. Individual not criminally responsible or unfit for trial: order treated as conviction[^c-d17fc99c0b1aff68e1a3d6a2c976d9bf]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-00a40a32219706910077ea2795158732][^c-aaaff4e78adb0b2def3785c09502603a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-00a40a32219706910077ea2795158732][^c-ceee26ad13dee1a3f653919e7c713e69][^c-96807f46386c8b31cafa18ed47dd2e49] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-aaaff4e78adb0b2def3785c09502603a] Subsection (4) applies to an individual if—

- (a) the individual is charged with an offence,
- (b) either—
  - (i) the individual is acquitted of the charge by reason of the special defence set out in section 51A of the 1995 Act[^c-66dee4bf9c29768ce9c23a7c87c5fb93] , or
  - (ii) the court makes a finding under section 55(2) of the 1995 Act in respect of the individual, and
- (c) the court makes any order mentioned in section 57(2)(a) to (d) of the 1995 Act in relation to the acquittal or finding.

(4)[^c-aaaff4e78adb0b2def3785c09502603a] An individual to whom this subsection applies is to be treated, for the purposes of sections [^c-592a0e65bf01f5819024b9e88d5d1854]... 17 and 24, as having been convicted of the offence.
