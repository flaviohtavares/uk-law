# Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007, section 92

## 92. Individuals barred from regulated roles[^key-1e23da554bfbac7769b29ab3e6e951fd][^key-04e1ad3466c60e3d1f2f8629630a03c6]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-cefc43c8314934cf3af735dfbbd5034c] An individual is barred from regulated roles[^key-c753846a1f626c7eb18a358da6a7022b] with children if the individual is—

- (a) listed in the children's list,
- (b) included in the children's barred list maintained under section 2 of the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 (c. 47),
- (c) included (otherwise than provisionally) in the list kept under article 3 of the Protection of Children and Vulnerable Adults (Northern Ireland) Order 2003, or
- (d) an individual falling within subsection (3).

(2)[^key-cefc43c8314934cf3af735dfbbd5034c] An individual is barred from regulated roles[^key-d791f13ef9974155c116a5480e1e161a] with adults if the individual is—

- (a) listed in the adults' list,
- (b) included in the adults' barred list maintained under section 2 of the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 (c. 47),
- (c) included (otherwise than provisionally) in the list kept under article 35 of the Protection of Children and Vulnerable Adults (Northern Ireland) Order 2003, or
- (d) an individual falling within subsection (4).

(3)[^key-41f4c1fe8981ff489cfe1af613c07cf6][^key-e979bc6516d710839e31ab96ebaa2f0f] An individual falls within this subsection if, under the law of the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man, any British overseas territory or any other territory or country outwith the United Kingdom, the individual is subject to a prohibition or disqualification which Ministers by order provide corresponds to being barred from regulated roles[^key-77266a19a21bb1225fa78fee9e196325] with children by virtue of any of paragraphs (a) to (c) of subsection (1).

(4)[^key-41f4c1fe8981ff489cfe1af613c07cf6][^key-e979bc6516d710839e31ab96ebaa2f0f] An individual falls within this subsection if, under the law of the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man, any British overseas territory or any other territory or country outwith the United Kingdom, the individual is subject to a prohibition or disqualification which Ministers by order provide corresponds to being barred from regulated roles[^key-fa57552149a63b5df68ef8265211f9f1] with adults by virtue of any of paragraphs (a) to (c) of subsection (2).

(5)[^key-41f4c1fe8981ff489cfe1af613c07cf6][^key-e979bc6516d710839e31ab96ebaa2f0f] Ministers may by order substitute such other list maintained under the law of England and Wales or, as the case may be, Northern Ireland, which in Ministers' opinion corresponds to the children's list for a list mentioned in subsection (1)(b) and (c).

(6)[^key-41f4c1fe8981ff489cfe1af613c07cf6][^key-e979bc6516d710839e31ab96ebaa2f0f] Ministers may by order substitute such other list maintained under the law of England and Wales or, as the case may be, Northern Ireland, which in Ministers' opinion corresponds to the adults' list for a list mentioned in subsection (2)(b) or (c).
