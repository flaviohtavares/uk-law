# Custodial Sentences and Weapons (Scotland) Act 2007, section 52

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 52. Prisoners serving more than one sentence: application of Part 2[^c-0379800ecd70b5af7761dc6bac34b985]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

Schedule 2 (which makes provision for the application of this Part to prisoners serving, or liable to serve, more than one sentence of imprisonment) has effect.
