# Custodial Sentences and Weapons (Scotland) Act 2007, section 39

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 39. Prisoners unlawfully at large[^key-0379800ecd70b5af7761dc6bac34b985]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

Where—

- (a) a prisoner's licence is revoked by virtue of section 37(1) or 38(2), and
- (b) the prisoner is at large,

the prisoner is unlawfully at large.
