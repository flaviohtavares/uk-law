# Adult Support and Protection (Scotland) Act 2007, section 73

> Text as at 2026-01-07.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 73. Applications to the Mental Health Tribunal for Scotland[^key-ad18731bd43d5fae1a1180f9fcc7a907]

*Extent: S*

After paragraph 13 of schedule 2 to the Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 13), insert—

> **13A. Withdrawn applications to be disregarded for certain purposes**
>
> For the purposes of sections 101(3)(c), 189(2)(a)(ii) and (b)(ii) and 213(2)(a)(ii) and (b)(ii) of this Act, an application to the Tribunal which is withdrawn by the applicant before it is determined is to be treated as not having been made.
