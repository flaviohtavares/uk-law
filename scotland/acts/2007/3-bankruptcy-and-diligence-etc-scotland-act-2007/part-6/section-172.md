# Bankruptcy and Diligence etc. (Scotland) Act 2007, section 172

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 172. Abolition of adjudication in security

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

Any enactment or rule of law enabling adjudication in security to be used ceases to have effect.
