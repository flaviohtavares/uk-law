# Bankruptcy and Diligence etc. (Scotland) Act 2007, section 65

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-08-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2007/3

## 65. Information from professional association[^c-4ee59f900144bdb9dc5adebc7db22b5a]

*Extent: S*

The Lord President of the Court of Session or any sheriff principal[^c-c622c1ea614255b64e236317a79a3279] may require the professional association to provide any information the Lord President or, as the case may be, sheriff principal[^c-e35090831b4920ef335ddcc358f6a52d] considers necessary or proper for the purposes of—

- (a) any inspection under section 66 of this Act;
- (b) any investigation under section 79(2) of the 1987 Act[^c-e7a6633caaa52c7705e3b1a40618ae91]; or
- (c)[^c-50bf341da7defca35fc913a57ac96a17] any disciplinary proceedings brought under section 79(3) of that Act.
