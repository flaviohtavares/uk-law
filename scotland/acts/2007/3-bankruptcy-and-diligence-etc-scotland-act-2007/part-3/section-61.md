## 61. Regulation of officers of court[^c2028290][^c-9520493bde70fa56f795a7e0a14d4210]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-5af9bd36388d1386047c012afc1b031a] The Scottish Ministers may, by regulations—

- (a) confer functions on;
- (b) remove functions from; or
- (c) otherwise modify the functions of,

officers of court[^c-5a3b62fe1eb3cb28991f5f4fc0a84023].

(2)[^c-5af9bd36388d1386047c012afc1b031a] The Scottish Ministers may, by regulations—

- (a) prescribe the types of business association which officers of court[^c-63cef534eb0ef08f717018a962257c33] may form in order to carry out their functions;
- (b) make provision about the ownership, membership, management and control of those business associations;
- (c) prescribe conditions which must be satisfied by those business associations;
- (d)[^c-47b6417054beca0f3430eb2971864113] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-5af9bd36388d1386047c012afc1b031a] Before making regulations under subsection (1) or (2) above, the Scottish Ministers must consult —[^c-de398ad512b4610a81334e6d8612455d]

- (a) the Lord President of the Court of Session; and
- (b) each sheriff principal.

(4)[^c-696fb753956d4673a8687e698108c104] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c-696fb753956d4673a8687e698108c104] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c-696fb753956d4673a8687e698108c104] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^c-696fb753956d4673a8687e698108c104] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
