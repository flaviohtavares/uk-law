## 78. Electronic publications and communications[^c-1e354183f87f77e038c3cb27c8ede7c7]

*Extent: S*

In this Part—

- (a) references to “publishing” include publishing by electronic means and cognate expressions are to be construed accordingly; and
- (b) any reference to a notification, [^c-6865dcb3ee086e8537ec897ddaf864eb]... being in writing includes a reference to that notification, [^c-6865dcb3ee086e8537ec897ddaf864eb]... being an electronic communication.
