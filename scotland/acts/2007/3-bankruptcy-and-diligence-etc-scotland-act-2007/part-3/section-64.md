# Bankruptcy and Diligence etc. (Scotland) Act 2007, section 64

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-08-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2007/3
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 64. Duty of professional association to forward complaints ...[^key-97f6ede97aa10391b0418ca6b60940b7][^key-a1f05a6f6df5ae07ce6be42423ef8cf6]

*Extent: S*

Where the professional association receives a complaint about an officer of court[^key-ee4e0be273c73a5705cf6ef95d5cbfea] or any services provided by the officer, the association must send details of the complaint and any material which accompanies it —

- (a)[^key-f55fbeaeb7e6cc181deeea2b184319d3] to the Lord President of the Court of Session;
- (b) in the case of a complaint about a messenger-at-arms, to the sheriff principal from whom the messenger-at-arms holds a commission as a sheriff officer; and
- (c) in the case of a complaint about a sheriff officer, to the sheriff principal from whom the sheriff officer holds a commission.
