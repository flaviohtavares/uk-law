## 62. Duty to notify Lord President and sheriff principal of bankruptcy etc.[^key-73595c41431a753a449d1dfbbe982f9f][^key-03f715cda9f78d68562b23c998f7de94]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where, in relation to an officer of court[^key-106afbe5ccc2d7b7ab2658c46aeaffa1], any of the events mentioned in subsection (2) below occurs, the officer must, before the expiry of the period of 28 days beginning with the occurrence of the event—

- (a)[^key-900623031f3f4e127733f51769f59f60] in the case of a messenger-at-arms, notify the Lord President of the Court of Session in writing of the event;
- (b) in the case of a sheriff officer, notify the sheriff principal from whom the officer holds a commission in writing of the event.

(2) The events referred to in subsection (1) above are—

- (a) the sequestration of the officer of court[^key-40027dc739fc44035db3b0be8725f357];
- (b) the granting by the officer of a trust deed for creditors;
- (c) the making of a bankruptcy restrictions order in respect of the officer;
- (d)[^key-a3ca7dd9fab41bfe0fb90cd11ff9978e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) the making, under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986 (c. 46), of a disqualification order against the officer;
- (f) where the officer is a partner in a partnership the sole or main business of which is the provision of officer of court[^key-569099930bc06da6ace9579781bf6f83] services—
  - (i) the granting by the partnership of a trust deed for creditors; or
  - (ii) the sequestration of the partnership;
- (g) where the officer is a member in a limited liability partnership the sole or main business of which is the provision of officer of court[^key-cb3a7d1a68776414c46c16af6279db16] services, the commencement of the winding up of that partnership on the ground of insolvency.

(3) In subsection (2) above, “**trust deed**” has the meaning given by section 5(4A) of the 1985 Act.
