## 53. Published information not to enable identification[^c-0f58f4d9666ad471ab77291146fd5f70]

*Extent: S*

Information—

- (a) contained in a report prepared under section 51(2); [^c-4b6e638df61d3a4ff01bd4e9fdbae366]...
- (b)[^c-4b6e638df61d3a4ff01bd4e9fdbae366] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

of this Act must not be in a form which identifies or enables the identification of officers of court[^c-2d24969a46fe97cfb8477d822bda77df] or persons against whom diligence has been executed.
