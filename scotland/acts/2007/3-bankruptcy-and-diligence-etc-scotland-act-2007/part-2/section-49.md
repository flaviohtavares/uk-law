# Bankruptcy and Diligence etc. (Scotland) Act 2007, section 49

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 49. Industrial and provident societies

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) For section 62 (floating charges created by Scottish societies) of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014[^c-f05da1ed23228346ee2d6fb6da2c23a9] (c. 48) substitute—

> **62. Floating charges: Scotland[^c-cbd4e3c6357228ba83858c5f26cceebd]**
>
> (1) Part 2 of the Bankruptcy and Diligence etc. (Scotland) Act 2007 (asp 3) (“**the 2007 Act**”) applies to a registered society as it applies to an incorporated company.
>
> (2) Subsection (3) applies where any assets of a registered society are subject to—
>
> - (a) a floating charge created under Part 2 of the 2007 Act (as applied by this section), and
> - (b) an agricultural charge created under Part 2 of the Agricultural Credits (Scotland) Act 1929.
>
> (3) For the purposes of determining the relative ranking of those charges, the following provisions of the 2007 Act apply as if the agricultural charge were a floating charge created under Part 2 of that Act on the date of creation of the agricultural charge—
>
> - section 40(1) to (3) (including as subject to section 41(1) to (4)),
> - section 45(3)(c).

.

(2) Section 63[^c-ad47710a9fb4a1f79d6dd44b1d29ecf1] (filing of information relating to charges) of that Act is repealed.

(3) In section 64 (notification of charges[^c-d4e8c07dad40f96e970af9da1cc7716f] etc: Scotland) of that Act—

- (a)[^c-8b41fa78654006b12954e52545bc01dc] for paragraph (a) of subsection (1) substitute—

  > - (a) the giving to the FCA of notice of any security, except a floating charge, granted by a registered society over any of its assets;
- (b) the references to section 63[^c-1365e342d0b9eea590022d792efcefab] of that Act are to be treated as references to that section as it had effect immediately before its repeal by subsection (2) above.
