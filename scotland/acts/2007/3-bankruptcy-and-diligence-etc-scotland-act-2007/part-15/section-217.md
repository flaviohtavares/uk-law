# Bankruptcy and Diligence etc. (Scotland) Act 2007, section 217

## 217. When removing not competent[^c2028944]

*Extent: S*

(1) It is not competent to execute a decree for removing from heritable property on—

- (a)[^key-05c95c07e830e59765b909139cf5509e] a Sunday;
- (b)[^key-05c95c07e830e59765b909139cf5509e] a day which is a public holiday in the area in which the decree is to be executed; or
- (c)[^key-49929db01af01cc3c933577e5bca5bff] such other day as may be prescribed by Act of Sederunt or by rules made under section 68 or paragraph 4 of schedule 9 of the Tribunals (Scotland) Act 2014[^key-d0386cd55a6826a782a9dd3feec1e85d].

(2)[^key-05c95c07e830e59765b909139cf5509e] The execution of such a decree must not—

- (a) begin before 8 a.m. or after 8 p.m.; or
- (b) be continued after 8 p.m.,

unless the officer of court[^key-5c51da9a381400d49b7292beb153393a] has obtained prior authority from the sheriff for the district in which the subjects or premises are situated , or from the First-tier Tribunal,[^key-aa968bff4c3419debdadefe0bab93c15] for such commencement or continuation.
