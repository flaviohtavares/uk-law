# Coronavirus (Recovery and Reform) (Scotland) Act 2022, section 17

> Text as at 2026-02-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 17. Report on public schools’ readiness for remote learning[^c-45c915495709343bab9caaf4d135eb5f][^c-c285c935e524aad9bf91de72ab78a49d]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers must report on—

- (a) how ready education authorities are for the provision of education in public schools by remote learning, and
- (b) any specific steps that have been taken by education authorities and the Scottish Ministers to improve readiness for the provision of education in public schools by remote learning.

(2) The Scottish Ministers must publish the report —

- (a) in the case of the first report, as soon as practicable after 31 July 2023,
- (b) in each other case, from time to time as the Scottish Ministers consider appropriate.

(3) For the purposes of subsection (1), the provision of education does not include the provision of early learning and childcare.
