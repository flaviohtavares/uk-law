> Extent: Scotland.

## 15. Review of regulations[^key-8f0a6957215fccf115c49a61ad25c2ca]

*Extent: S*

So long as regulations under this Chapter remain in effect, the Scottish Ministers must review the regulations—

- (a) before the end of the period of 21 days beginning with the day on which the regulations came into force, and
- (b) before the end of each subsequent period of 21 days.
