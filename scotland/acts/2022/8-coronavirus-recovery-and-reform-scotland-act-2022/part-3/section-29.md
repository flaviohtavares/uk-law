## 29. Power to make a register electronic[^c-be7d47592a8e6f527e4d77ed658afdc1]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Civil Partnership Act 2004 is modified as follows.

(2) In section 95 (further provision as to registration), after subsection (3) insert—

> (3ZA) A civil partnership register may, if the Registrar General so determines, be electronic rather than paper-based.
