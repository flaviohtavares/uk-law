## 24. Power to amend period of moratorium on diligence[^c-d357bff78dad847de76e0ca4db044e80]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 2016 is modified as follows.

(2) In section 198 (period of moratorium), after subsection (8), insert—

> (9) The Scottish Ministers may by regulations modify this section so as to vary any of the periods specified in subsections (1)(b)(i), (3), (5), (6)(b) or (7).

.

(3) In section 225 (regulations: general), in subsection (4)(a), after “194(1)” insert “, 198(9)”.
