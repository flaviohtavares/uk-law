## 22. Bank arrestments: protected minimum balance[^key-f3412f49e347edc124405dfd2829f896]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987 is modified by subsection (2).

(2) In section 73F (protection of minimum balance in certain bank accounts)—

- (a) in subsection (3), for “mentioned in subsection (4) below” substitute “of £1,000”,
- (b) subsection (4) is repealed,
- (c) in subsection (6)—
  - (i) the “and” at the end of paragraph (a)(ii) is repealed,
  - (ii) after paragraph (a), insert—

    > - (aa) vary the protected minimum sum mentioned in subsection (3)(a), and

    .

(3) The modifications made by subsection (2) have no effect in relation to an arrestment executed before 1 November 2022.
