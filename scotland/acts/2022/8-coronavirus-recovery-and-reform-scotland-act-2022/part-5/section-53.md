> Extent: Scotland.

## 53. Power to bring expiry forward[^key-b323424cfacfe257b13aaf852ef29c16]

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers may by regulations provide that any provision in the schedule—

- (a) does not expire at the time when it would otherwise expire (whether by virtue of section 52 or previous regulations under this section), and
- (b) expires instead at such earlier time as is specified in the regulations.
