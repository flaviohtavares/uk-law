# Fireworks and Pyrotechnic Articles (Scotland) Act 2022, section 19

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 19. Regulations: consultation[^key-a8f03805a09595aab17bc96919ee35e0]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Before making any regulations under this Part, the Scottish Ministers must consult such persons as they consider are likely to be interested in or affected by the licensing of fireworks.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to regulations under section 3.
