# Fireworks and Pyrotechnic Articles (Scotland) Act 2022, section 52

> Text as at 2023-06-22.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 52. Regulations[^c-6b3e8fed8d8f92be2e9e11be2510f07a]

*Extent: S*

Any power of the Scottish Ministers to make regulations under this Act, other than section 56, includes power to make—

- (a) different provision for different purposes,
- (b) incidental, supplementary, consequential, transitional, transitory or saving provision.
