# Fireworks and Pyrotechnic Articles (Scotland) Act 2022, section 56

> Text as at 2023-06-22.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 56. Commencement[^key-12b4036129d4742c1241563a7c541b2c]

*Extent: S*

(1) This section and sections 50, 51, 52, 53 and 57 come into force on the day after Royal Assent.

(2) The other provisions of this Act come into force on such day as the Scottish Ministers may by regulations appoint.

(3) Regulations under subsection (2) may—

- (a) include transitional, transitory or saving provision,
- (b) make different provision for different purposes.
