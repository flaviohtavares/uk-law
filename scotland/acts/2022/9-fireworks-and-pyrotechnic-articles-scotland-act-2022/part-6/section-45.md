> Extent: Scotland.

## 45. Time limit for prosecution of offences[^c-4eb7a0017f0ea956397512148d0dffff][^c-310208becc66e6fc75048684f16c0c7a]

*Extent: S*

Summary proceedings for an offence under this Act may be commenced at any time within the period of 12 months beginning with the day on which the offence was committed.
