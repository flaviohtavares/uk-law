# Fireworks and Pyrotechnic Articles (Scotland) Act 2022, section 38

> Text as at 2023-06-22.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 38. Exemptions from offences in Act[^c-aea3ef43894423c1eaa25025ee41d8d5]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-36379d01e167c224003d5bc16078ed68][^c-938f57fc42a9d37254c9fcacfd860a33][^c-fbafc9772768be1f942390aa477109c9] Schedule 1 contains exemptions to certain offences under Parts 2 to 5.

(2)[^c-c2881935e8c4e8eedff192613dea28f0] The Scottish Ministers may by regulations—

- (a) modify schedule 1 to add, amend or remove exemptions,
- (b) make further provision about the requirements that a person must meet to be treated as being employed by, or in business as, a professional organiser or operator of fireworks displays or pyrotechnics displays for the purposes of schedule 1.

(3)[^c-c2881935e8c4e8eedff192613dea28f0] Regulations under subsection (2) are subject to the affirmative procedure.
