## 39. Powers of local weights and measures authorities[^key-53a34dad7250cb37741a289288ed8403]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

Schedule 2 makes provision about the powers of a local weights and measures authority (and its officers) to enforce the offences under sections 5 and 22 in its area and related matters.
