## 90. Interim orders[^c-d9dd80855e2ad0b917bb455e554edfe8]

*Extent: S*

In section 47 of the Court of Session Act 1988 (interim interdict and other interim orders), after subsection (2) insert—

> (2A) The power under subsection (2) to make an order includes, in particular, power to make an order ad factum praestandum (including an interim order).
