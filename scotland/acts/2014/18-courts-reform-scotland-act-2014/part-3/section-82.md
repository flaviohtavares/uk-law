## 82. Appeals from simple procedure cases[^c-20cbc72048b27e736b0e97bfcabfde56]

*Extent: S*

(1) An appeal may be taken to the Sheriff Appeal Court under section 110 on a point of law only against a decision of the sheriff constituting final judgment in a simple procedure case.

(2) Any other decision of the sheriff in such a case is not subject to review.
