## 91. Warrants for ejection[^key-33c8b4e1ff4f67a56b4548552934feb4]

*Extent: S*

After section 47 of the Court of Session Act 1988, insert—

> **47A. Power to grant warrant for ejection**
>
> In any proceedings where the Court has competence to grant a decree of removing, it also has competence to grant a warrant for ejection.
