## 67. Trial to proceed despite objection to opinion and direction of the sheriff[^key-ccca76c8bc1e28d67d82a4c2420b12e4]

*Extent: S*

In jury proceedings, despite any objection being taken in the course of the trial to the opinion and direction of the sheriff—

- (a) the trial is to proceed, and
- (b) the jury are to return their verdict and, where necessary, assess damages.
