## 114. Appeal from the sheriff principal to the Court of Session[^c-f65d04c41af9f381e3af7ba54d5d3ca0]

*Extent: S*

(1) An appeal may be taken to the Court of Session against a decision of a sheriff principal constituting a final judgment in relevant civil proceedings.

(2) This section does not affect any other right of appeal against any decision of a sheriff principal to the Court of Session under any other enactment.

(3) This section is subject to any provision of any other enactment that restricts or excludes any right of appeal from a sheriff principal to the Court of Session.

(4) In subsection (1), “**relevant civil proceedings**” means civil proceedings (other than an appeal) under an enactment that provides for the proceedings to be brought before a sheriff principal rather than a sheriff.
