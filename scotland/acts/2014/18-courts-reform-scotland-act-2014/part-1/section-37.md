## 37. Saving for existing powers to provide for judicial specialisation[^c-47a1954ad851d98b5d60aac082f50002]

*Extent: S*

Sections 34 to 36 do not affect any power that the Lord President of the Court of Session has apart from those sections to provide for judicial specialisation in the sheriff courts.
