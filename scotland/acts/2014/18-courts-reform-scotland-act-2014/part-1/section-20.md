## 20. Cessation of appointment of judicial officers[^c-030947bf9836d82db5823797a07c9e3d]

*Extent: S*

(1) A judicial officer mentioned in subsection (3) may resign at any time by giving notice to that effect to the Scottish Ministers.

(2) An individual's appointment as such a judicial officer ends—

- (a) when the individual resigns in accordance with subsection (1),
- (b) when the individual retires from office,
- (c) if the individual is removed from office as such under section 25, or
- (d) if the individual is appointed as another such judicial officer.

(3) The judicial officers are—

- (a) a sheriff principal,
- (b) a sheriff,
- (c) a summary sheriff,
- (d) a part-time sheriff,
- (e) a part-time summary sheriff.
