## 46. The Sheriff Appeal Court[^c-f4804ad350be3008e1245651e44fce72][^c-fc3348d22ce03b05c02e8a4323da959a]

*Extent: S*

(1) There is established a court of law to be known as the Sheriff Appeal Court.

(2) The Court consists of judges each to be known as an Appeal Sheriff.
