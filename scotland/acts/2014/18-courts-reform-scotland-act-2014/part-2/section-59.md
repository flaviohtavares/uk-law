## 59. Clerk of the Sheriff Appeal Court[^c-ea83cf966ee8b4100b62240633037475]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service must appoint a person holding office as a sheriff clerk also to hold the office of Clerk of the Sheriff Appeal Court.

(2) A person's appointment as Clerk of the Sheriff Appeal Court does not affect the person's appointment as a sheriff clerk.

(3) A person holding office as Clerk of the Sheriff Appeal Court ceases to hold that office if the person ceases to hold office as a sheriff clerk.

(4) Otherwise, a person's appointment as Clerk of the Sheriff Appeal court—

- (a) lasts for such period, and
- (b) is on such other terms and conditions,

as the Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service may determine.

(5) In this section, “**sheriff clerk**” does not include sheriff clerk depute.
