# Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014, section 124

## 124. Payment of salaries and allowances of Court of Session judges[^key-d680b252859a9058a61bc0cc65673dbd][^key-5e24431f84ace31fc46d1d1d954a975d]

*Extent: S*

(1) The salaries and allowances[^key-77f7ea9afa52e64b4c2054be15c141de] of judges of the Court of Session determined under section 9 of the Administration of Justice Act 1973 (judicial salaries) are to be paid by the Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service.

(2) Sums required by the Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service for the payment of such salaries and such allowances[^key-13166ea5a2dd54fbed6eac5803e4e0d9] are charged on the Scottish Consolidated Fund.
