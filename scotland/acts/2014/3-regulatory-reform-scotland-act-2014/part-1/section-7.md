# Regulatory Reform (Scotland) Act 2014, section 7

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 7. Power to modify schedule 1[^c-732c45e75dbc4aaa3a7cb62aeb7c7f60]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may by order modify schedule 1 so as to—

- (a) add—
  - (i) a person, body or office-holder which has regulatory functions to the list of persons, bodies and office-holders for the time being listed there, or
  - (ii) a description of a person, body or office-holder having regulatory functions to that list,
- (b) remove—
  - (i) a person, body or office-holder from that list, or
  - (ii) a description of a person, body or office-holder from that list,
- (c) amend an entry on that list.

(2) An order under subsection (1) may, in relation to a person, body or office-holder or a description of a person, body or office-holder—

- (a) specify that a function is or is not to be a regulatory function for the purposes of section 1, 4 or 5,
- (b) specify the extent to which a function is or is not to be a regulatory function for such purposes.
