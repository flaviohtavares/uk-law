# Regulatory Reform (Scotland) Act 2014, section 53

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 53. Meaning of “relevant offence” and “SEPA” in Part 3[^c-52107f27ec19adae6e6d526af41d03ff]

*Extent: S*

In this Part—

- “**relevant offence**” means an offence specified in an order made by the Scottish Ministers for the purposes of this Part,
- “**SEPA**” means the Scottish Environment Protection Agency.
