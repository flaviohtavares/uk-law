# Procurement Reform (Scotland) Act 2014, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-03-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2014/12
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 8. General duties

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-77b6562861d5078cf9ef7de0c3bb1318] A contracting authority must, in carrying out a regulated procurement—

- (a) treat relevant economic operators equally and without discrimination,
- (b) act in a transparent and proportionate manner.

(2)[^c-d9b8771d0d0c1f68002e8ad36d962fe5] A contracting authority must also comply with the sustainable procurement duty.

(3)[^c-d9b8771d0d0c1f68002e8ad36d962fe5] However, a contracting authority must not do anything in pursuance of subsection (2) that would conflict with its duty under subsection (1).

(4)[^c-77b6562861d5078cf9ef7de0c3bb1318][^c-f0f2f82afc2c94b4524ce40ca579952a] A relevant economic operator is an economic operator who is a national of, or is established in, the United Kingdom or Gibraltar.

(5)[^c-77b6562861d5078cf9ef7de0c3bb1318] Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to a higher value regulated procurement[^c-78f45eff89e3e2574ad97cf20a2ea4e0].
