## 11. Supported businesses[^key-4c408e1d908784b34116fef8e082a417]

*Extent: S*

(1) Despite section 8, a contracting authority may restrict participation in a regulated procurement (other than a higher value regulated procurement[^key-1490fe303a68f39193d8eb4da2163466]) to supported businesses only.

(2) Where a contracting authority restricts participation under subsection (1), it must state that fact in the contract notice.

(3) For the purposes of this Act, a “supported business” is an economic operator who operates a supported business, or supported employment programme within the meaning of regulation 21[^key-8cfc1a339dba80604a9800d7bc87e8b8] of the Public Contracts Regulations.
