## 4. Assignment to office

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-62657d1fa12e929b83e143fc5a3630d7] There is established the office to be known as that of President of the Scottish Tribunals.

(2)[^key-62657d1fa12e929b83e143fc5a3630d7] It is for the Lord President to assign a person to that office.

(3)[^key-62657d1fa12e929b83e143fc5a3630d7] An assignment of a person to that office continues for as long as the Lord President considers appropriate.

(4)[^key-e4fd9df8413b514e80b45f9431f59999] The Lord President may nominate a Vice-President of the Upper Tribunal to act temporarily in that office—

- (a) if a person assigned to that office is for the time being unable to act in it, or
- (b) pending an assignment of a person to that office.

(5)[^key-41cccae77f4eb89a5b3d8b70c4bfb80e][^key-1c0ae1696a4166ee5719296a3d0a39b0] A person assigned to that office under subsection (2) or nominated to act in it under subsection (4) must be a judge of the Court of Session (but may not be a temporary judge).
