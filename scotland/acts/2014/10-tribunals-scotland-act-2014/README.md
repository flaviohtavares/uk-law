Tribunals (Scotland) Act 2014

2014 asp 10

An Act of the Scottish Parliament to establish the First-tier Tribunal for Scotland and the Upper Tribunal for Scotland; and for connected purposes.

The Bill for this Act of the Scottish Parliament was passed by the Parliament on 11th March 2014 and received Royal Assent on 15th April 2014[^key-6fc4702671173633592e65edd8755116][^key-646761f8f7560941f66e0caf8da87c07][^key-538ea9545f5aa2befcf61431f1284827]
