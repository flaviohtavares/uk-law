# Revenue Scotland and Tax Powers Act 2014, section 149

>

## 149. Restriction on inspection of documents[^key-427c6576d1f01f79bd06615d7e35e4d2][^key-8582aee1c20bc942bc848dcc0f3ead7c][^key-8582aee1c20bc942bc848dcc0f3ead7c]

*Extent: S*

A designated officer may not inspect a document under this Chapter if (or to the extent that), by virtue of Chapters 2 and 3, an information notice given at the time of the inspection to the occupier of the premises could not require the occupier to produce the document.
