# Revenue Scotland and Tax Powers Act 2014, section 234

## 234. Right to request review

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-c397f05141de37e0870e9eac7f0b7b19][^key-d70b173430ab63bb70b6df1a1c9d70e4] A person aggrieved by an appealable decision (the “appellant”) may request Revenue Scotland to review the decision.

(2)[^key-c397f05141de37e0870e9eac7f0b7b19][^key-d70b173430ab63bb70b6df1a1c9d70e4] An appellant may not request review if subsection (3), (4) or (5) applies.

(3)[^key-c397f05141de37e0870e9eac7f0b7b19][^key-d70b173430ab63bb70b6df1a1c9d70e4] This subsection applies where—

- (a) the decision which the appellant seeks to review is a decision of Revenue Scotland to amend a self-assessment under section 87 while an enquiry is in progress, and
- (b) the enquiry has not been completed.

(4) This subsection applies where—

- (a)[^key-c397f05141de37e0870e9eac7f0b7b19][^key-d70b173430ab63bb70b6df1a1c9d70e4] the appellant has given notice of appeal in relation to the same matter in question, or
- (b)[^key-6ad190880a233769111f80f52d36a6e5] the tribunal has determined the matter in question under section 244.

(5)[^key-c397f05141de37e0870e9eac7f0b7b19][^key-d70b173430ab63bb70b6df1a1c9d70e4] This subsection applies where the appellant has entered into a settlement agreement with Revenue Scotland in relation to the same matter in question and has not withdrawn from the agreement under section 246(4).

(6)[^key-c397f05141de37e0870e9eac7f0b7b19][^key-d70b173430ab63bb70b6df1a1c9d70e4] This section does not prevent the matter in question from being dealt with in accordance with section 246(1) and (2) (settling matters in question by agreement).
