## 249. References to the “tribunal”[^key-7d042a41ceecf1285b0cdd4763c324ae][^key-9bc0bb16d631c6587aa8ba794277d12e]

*Extent: S*

In this Part “**the tribunal**” means—

- (a) the First-tier Tribunal for Scotland[^key-ff080f2e58edd29afa63f05e18bc464b] ,
- (b) where determined by or under Scottish Tribunal Rules[^key-0556a024f4a8f326f7b5d245b125627f] , the Upper Tribunal for Scotland[^key-71e07c24d94976323b06050ba371b86f] .
