## 172. Scottish landfill tax and Scottish aggregates tax: 6 month penalty for failure to pay tax[^c-e1c1b5a5668d671b8a62c758884bbe28][^c-c1d555abc49e446e8be0895cbf7263e2]

*Extent: S*

If any amount of tax is unpaid after the end of the period of 6 months beginning with the penalty date, P is liable to a penalty of 5% of that amount.
