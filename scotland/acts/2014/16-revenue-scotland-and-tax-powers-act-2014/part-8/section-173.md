## 173. Scottish landfill tax and Scottish aggregates tax: 12 month penalty for failure to pay tax[^c-8c4875b93211d7655c2b281cd495459d][^c-40bb847fee678f34e282cfee71d9834b]

*Extent: S*

If any amount of tax is unpaid after the end of the period of 12 months beginning with the penalty date, P is liable to a penalty of 5% of that amount.
