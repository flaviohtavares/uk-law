# Revenue Scotland and Tax Powers Act 2014, section 216C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (74).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2014/16
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 216C. Failure to request approval of tax representative appointment: Scottish aggregates tax[^key-6e4eade77f8df44d23b4e8881cc2d5cd][^key-ef6f894bcf6aec3951e11854343a3c2d]

*Extent: S*

(1) This section applies to a person who—

- (a) becomes subject, in accordance with regulations under section 26(1) of the AT(S) Act 2024, to an obligation to request Revenue Scotland’s approval for the appointment of the person’s tax representative, but
- (b) fails (with or without making the appointment) to make the request as required by the regulations.

(2) The person is liable to a penalty of £10,000.
