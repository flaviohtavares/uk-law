## 216E. Failure to notify change to group treatment application or notification: Scottish aggregates tax[^c-96a709af28f18883e660aa57b35a5d02][^c-ef6f894bcf6aec3951e11854343a3c2d]

*Extent: S*

(1) This section applies to a person who fails to comply with section 31 of the AT(S) Act 2024.

(2) The person is liable to a penalty of £250.
