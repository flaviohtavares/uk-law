# Revenue Scotland and Tax Powers Act 2014, section 213

## 213. Special reduction in penalty under section 209

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-c28a57793641dfd5e5058b9e1dcae0b4] Revenue Scotland may reduce a penalty under section 209 if it thinks it right to do so because of special circumstances.

(2)[^key-c28a57793641dfd5e5058b9e1dcae0b4] In subsection (1) “**special circumstances**” does not include—

- (a) ability to pay, or
- (b) the fact that a potential loss of revenue from one taxpayer is balanced by a potential over-payment by another.

(3)[^key-c28a57793641dfd5e5058b9e1dcae0b4] In subsection (1) the reference to reducing a penalty includes a reference to—

- (a) remitting a penalty entirely,
- (b) suspending a penalty, and
- (c) agreeing a compromise in relation to proceedings for a penalty.

(4)[^key-c28a57793641dfd5e5058b9e1dcae0b4] In this section references to a penalty include references to any interest in relation to the penalty.

(5)[^key-ede33199845e391d5cc0b7e00d34ee8b] The powers in this section also apply after a decision of a tribunal or a court in relation to the penalty.
