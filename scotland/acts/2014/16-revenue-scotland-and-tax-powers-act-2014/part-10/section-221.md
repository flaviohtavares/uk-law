## 221. Issue of tax demands and receipts[^key-e518b3876549ff6cc6922b5c9fd6ea7c]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where tax is due and payable, Revenue Scotland may demand the sum charged from the person liable to pay it.

(2) On payment of the tax, Revenue Scotland must give a receipt.
