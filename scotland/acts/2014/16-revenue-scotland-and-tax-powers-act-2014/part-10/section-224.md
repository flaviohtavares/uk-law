## 224. Court proceedings[^c-1a8e977fec796a1ba51878cc84badc75]

*Extent: S*

Tax due and payable may be sued for and recovered from the person liable to pay it as a debt due to the Crown by proceedings—

- (a) in the sheriff court, or
- (b) in the Court of Session (sitting as the Court of Exchequer).
