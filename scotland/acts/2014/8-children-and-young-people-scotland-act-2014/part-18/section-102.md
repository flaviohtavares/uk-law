## 102. Commencement

*Extent: S*

(1) This Part (apart from sections 96, 97 and 98) comes into force on the day after Royal Assent.

(2) Subsections (2) to (5) of section 47 also come into force on the day after Royal Assent.

(3) The other provisions of this Act come into force on such day as the Scottish Ministers may by order appoint.

(4) An order under this section may include transitional, transitory or saving provision.
