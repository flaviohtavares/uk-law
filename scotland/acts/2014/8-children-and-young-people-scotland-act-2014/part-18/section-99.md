# Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014, section 99

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (56).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2014/8
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 99. Subordinate legislation

*Extent: S*

(1) Any power of the Scottish Ministers to make an order or regulations[^c-92ee2df35c41f5aa8e7ac4c63ed9d5ee] under this Act includes power to make—

- (a) different provision for different purposes,
- (b) such supplementary, incidental, consequential, transitional, transitory or saving provision as they consider appropriate.

(2) An order or (as the case may be) regulations[^c-7a1e46bbd5e6cef89523d598168c4b28] made under any of the following sections is subject to the affirmative procedure—

- [^c-88040b8342f0980d704e7ff5cd887194]...
- section 7(5)
- section 30(1)
- section 31(2)
- section 37(7)
- section 43(1)
- section 44(2)
- section 47(2)(c)(ii)
- section 47(4)
- section 48(2)
- section 51(2)
- section 56(2)
- section 57(2)(b)
- section 58(2)
- section 68(3)(b)
- section 71(5)(b)
- section 90A(7)[^c-1ad3fd6ec4e129d379c3cd76614ddb11]
- section 90B(4)
- section 90C(9)
- section 96(6).

(3) An order made under section 101 containing provisions which add to, replace or omit any part of the text of this or any other Act is subject to the affirmative procedure.

(4) All other orders or regulations[^c-1d1a32a5fbeac23a2ff1eeadc628b374] made under this Act are subject to the negative procedure.

(5) This section does not apply to an order made under section 102(3).
