# Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014, section 93

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (56).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2014/8
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 93. Provision of free school lunches

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-e87d5a857b349f7665f6fbee476f0eb6] Section 53 of the 1980 Act is amended as follows.

(2)[^c-e87d5a857b349f7665f6fbee476f0eb6] Subsection (2) is repealed.

(3)[^c-e87d5a857b349f7665f6fbee476f0eb6] In subsection (2A), after “lunches” insert “ which the authority are required to provide by virtue of subsection (3) ”.

(4)[^c-e87d5a857b349f7665f6fbee476f0eb6] In subsection (2C)(b), the words “(other than in the middle of the day)” are omitted.

(5)[^c-e87d5a857b349f7665f6fbee476f0eb6] In subsection (2D), the words “(2) or” are omitted.

(6)[^c-84917621d8535ee1d06c3a683df2100a][^c-221c6c5c3e10b29f235943a8330ade1b] In subsection (3), after paragraph (b) insert—

> - (c) who is in such yearly stage of primary or secondary education, or is of such other description, as the Scottish Ministers may by regulations prescribe.

.
