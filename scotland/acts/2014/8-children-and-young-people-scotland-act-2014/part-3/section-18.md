## 18. Interpretation of Part 3[^key-271b76ffa2c75c8344c06a4785c03372]

*Extent: S*

In this Part—

- “**children's services plan**” has the meaning given by section 8(2),
- “**service**” means any service or support—
  - (a) which must be provided by the person concerned, or
  - (b) which the person concerned has power to provide.
