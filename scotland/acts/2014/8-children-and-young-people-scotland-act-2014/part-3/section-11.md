## 11. Children's services plan: review[^c-022055f08c39c47c4c7f94791eab9990]

*Extent: S*

(1) A local authority and the relevant health board—

- (a) must keep the children's services plan for the area of the local authority under review, and
- (b) may in consequence prepare a revised children's services plan.

(2) The following provisions apply to a revised children's services plan as they apply to a children's services plan—

- section 9,
- section 10, and
- subsection (1) of this section.
