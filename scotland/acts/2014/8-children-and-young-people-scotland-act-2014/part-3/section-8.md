# Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014, section 8

## 8. Requirement to prepare children's services plan[^key-1e5cbaa9d39c99855dd91a59e2404266][^key-59161c82f3178de6d913c2a726b08f51]

*Extent: S*

(1) A local authority and the relevant health board must in respect of each 3 year period prepare a children's services plan for the area of the local authority.

(2)[^M_P_2263c0fb-994e-4dc8-8da5-b9bf8d823826] In subsection (1)—

- “**3 year period**” means—
  - (a) the period of 3 years beginning with such date after the coming into force of this section as the Scottish Ministers specify by order, and
  - (b) each subsequent period of 3 years,
- “**children's services plan**” means a document setting out their plans for the provision over that period of all—
  - (a) children's services, and
  - (b) related services.
