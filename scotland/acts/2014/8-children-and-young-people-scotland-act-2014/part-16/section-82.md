## 82. Safeguarders: exceptions to duty to prepare report on appointment[^c-0ef2dde147cc33ffff24913784017c6b]

*Extent: S*

In section 33 of the 2011 Act—

- (a) in subsection (1)(a), after “(2)” insert “ or (3) ”,
- (b) after subsection (2), insert—

  > (3) This subsection applies where the children's hearing was arranged under section 45, 46, 50, 96, 126 or 158.
