## 87. Limit on number of further interim compulsory supervision orders[^c-01cf94c73b10bfbc550b78b1ab7df1c2]

*Extent: S*

In section 96(4) of the 2011 Act, for the words from “the effect” to the end substitute “ it would be the third such order made under subsection (3) in consequence of the same interim compulsory supervision order made under section 93(5) ”.
