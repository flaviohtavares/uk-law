## 90B. Secure transportation: duty of providers to meet standards[^key-79b36aaffc0ece1a587921837ee842c3]

*Extent: S*

(1) The provider of a secure transportation service must meet the applicable standards.

(2) The persons mentioned in subsection (3) must, when making arrangements with another person for the provision of a secure transportation service, ensure that the service meets the applicable standards.

(3) The persons are—

- (a) a local authority,
- (b) the Scottish Ministers.

(4) The Scottish Ministers may by regulations modify subsection (3) by—

- (a) adding a person or description of persons,
- (b) removing a person or description of persons for the time being listed in it,
- (c) varying a description of a person for the time being listed in it.

(5) In this section and in section 90C—

- “**applicable standards**” means the standards, or (as the case may be) any revised standards, published under section 90A(1) which apply to the secure transportation service being provided,
- “**secure transportation service**” has the meaning given by section 90A(1).
