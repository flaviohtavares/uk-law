## 74. Interpretation of Part 13[^c-f5889dc41185dde69618387a3854be76]

*Extent: S*

In this Part––

- “**kinship care assistance**” has the meaning given by section 71(2),
- “**parent**” has the same meaning as it has in Part 1 of the 1995 Act.
