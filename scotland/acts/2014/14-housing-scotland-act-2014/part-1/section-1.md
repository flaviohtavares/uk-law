# Housing (Scotland) Act 2014, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2014/14
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1. Abolition of the right to buy

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-d48772d5da8b5adc9f8ce6e8357d4d53] Sections 61 to 81, 84 and 84A of the 1987 Act (right to buy provisions) are repealed.

(2)[^key-1826d1e368a490b3d2c00d7c2c85bd44] Section 52 of the 2001 Act (reports on right to buy) is repealed.

(3)[^M_I_925db2b1-ea45-448f-eee9-0c37c3083517][^key-8d4c27b5696adef112f2c0760049ecae] Sections 145 to 147 of the 2010 Act (duties to collect information in relation to right to buy) are repealed.
