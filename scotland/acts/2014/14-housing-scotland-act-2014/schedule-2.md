# Housing (Scotland) Act 2014, SCHEDULE 2

## SCHEDULE 2 — Minor and consequential amendments

*Extent: S*

(introduced by section 103)

### Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 (c.65)

**1.**[^key-d6648caeba7fe8a1ae041295a7679b4e] Section 156(4) of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 is repealed.

### Matrimonial Homes (Family Protection) (Scotland) Act 1981 (c.59)

**2.**[^key-cdfa7ded94f0f6b61d620490700164fe] Section 13(11) of the Matrimonial Homes (Family Protection) (Scotland) Act 1981 is repealed.

### Rent (Scotland) Act 1984 (c.58)

**3.**[^key-8b12c0034ad31806cd764886629306b4] In Case 7 of Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Rent (Scotland) Act 1984—

- (a) the word “either” is repealed,
- (b) paragraph (b) and the word “or” immediately preceding it are repealed.

### Housing (Scotland) Act 1987 (c.26)

**4.** (1)[^key-0c47591427f198cc0636bc645ff81b31] The 1987 Act is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-0c47591427f198cc0636bc645ff81b31] In section 19 of the 1987 Act—

- (a) in subsection (1), for “local authority or a registered social landlord” substitute “ social landlord ”,
- (b) in subsection (2)—
  - (i) for “housing provider” substitute “ social landlord ”,
  - (ii) for “housing providers” substitute “ social landlords ”,
- (c) for subsection (3) substitute—

  > (3) In this Part, “**social landlord**” means any local authority or any registered social landlord.

  .

(3)[^key-0c47591427f198cc0636bc645ff81b31] In section 20(2)—

- (a) for “local authority and a registered social landlord” substitute “ social landlord ”,
- (b) in paragraph (b), after sub-paragraph (ii) insert—

  > - (iia) that a dissolution of a civil partnership or a decree of separation of civil partners be obtained, or

  .

(4)[^key-0c47591427f198cc0636bc645ff81b31] In section 21(3), paragraph (ia) and the word “and” at the end of that paragraph are repealed.

(5) In section 24(5)(d), for “or 2” substitute “ , 2 or 2A ”.

(6) In section 31(5)(c), for “or 2” substitute “ , 2 or 2A ”.

(7)[^key-3b3c51019ec30ba50e877df2a6bb8a4f] In section 82—

- (a) the words “this Part and in” are repealed, and
- (b) the definitions of “application to purchase”, “heritable proprietor”, “housing co-operative”, “offer to sell”, “police authority” and “secure tenancy” are repealed.

(8)[^key-3b3c51019ec30ba50e877df2a6bb8a4f] The title to section 82 becomes “ **Interpretation of sections 14, 19 and 20** ”.

(9) In section 338(1)—

- (a) in the definition of “house”, the words “(except in relation to Part XIV)” are repealed,
- (b)[^key-0c47591427f198cc0636bc645ff81b31] the definition of “secure tenancy” is repealed.

### Housing (Scotland) Act 1988 (c.43)

**5.** (1)[^key-37303f39f10a8ab224bfe5e5b09aed98] The Housing (Scotland) Act 1988 is amended as follows.

(2) In section 42(1)(d), the words “or in pursuance of section 282(3)(b) of that Act (grant of a tenancy upon acquisition by public sector authority of defective dwelling)” are repealed.

(3)[^key-37303f39f10a8ab224bfe5e5b09aed98] Paragraph 7 of Schedule 2 is repealed.

(4) Paragraphs 19 to 26 of Schedule 7 are repealed.

### Local Government and Housing Act 1989 (c.42)

**Not yet in force.**

**6.** Section 166(1) to (5) of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 is repealed.

### Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993 (c.28)

**Not yet in force.**

**7.** Section 156 of the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993 is repealed.

### Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994 (c.39)

**Not yet in force.**

**8.** Paragraph 152(6) of Schedule 13 to the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994 is repealed.

### Abolition of Feudal Tenure etc. (Scotland) Act 2000 (asp 5)

**9.**[^key-8464fbfc9ca44742e61ae4edf2783740] Paragraph 48(3) of schedule 12 to the Abolition of Feudal Tenure etc. (Scotland) Act 2000 is repealed.

### Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (asp 10)

**10.** (1)[^key-63e5e9d20049cf494267311dc613e8d7] The 2001 Act is amended as follows.

(2) In section 5(4)(a), for “or 2” substitute “ , 2 or 2A ”.

(3)[^key-6222390e87520968faec1dc5da823565] Section 23(6)(d) is repealed.

(4)[^key-6222390e87520968faec1dc5da823565] Sections 42 to 51 are repealed.

(5) In schedule 10—

- (a)[^key-63e5e9d20049cf494267311dc613e8d7] paragraph 13(3)(c)(ii) is repealed,
- (b)[^key-6222390e87520968faec1dc5da823565] paragraph 13(6) to (20) is repealed,
- (c) paragraph 13(36) to (40) is repealed.

### Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002 (asp 3)

**Not yet in force.**

**11.** Paragraph 18(5) of schedule 7 to the Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002 is repealed.

### Scottish Public Services Ombudsman Act 2002 (asp 11)

**12.**[^key-4041941e740f81c97efd0c1ec1e77c17] Paragraph 44 of schedule 2 to the Scottish Public Services Ombudsman Act 2002 is repealed.

### Freedom of Information (Scotland) Act 2002 (asp 13)

**13.**[^key-ac46277f0fe9d844a64f43c3f0a198f2] (1) The Freedom of Information (Scotland) Act 2002 is amended as follows.

(2) In schedule 1, after paragraph 18A insert—

> **18B.** The Scottish Housing Regulator.

.

(3) Paragraph 85B of schedule 1 is repealed.

### Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 2)

**14.**[^key-f07e93b31daa888e5d86e7f2a9695502] (1) The Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2003 is amended as follows.

(2) Section 40(4)(g)(v) is repealed.

(3) Section 65(2)(d) is repealed.

(4) Section 84(2)(c) is repealed.

### Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 11)

**15.**[^key-d36bd1061dcf2ed7ef6d7fefc4fa2b67] Section 27(1)(g)(vi) of the Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 2003 is repealed.

### Fire (Scotland) Act 2005 (asp 5)

**16.**[^key-e01a3e6bb7f22b62834215d971ff3a90] Paragraph 13 of schedule 3 to the Fire (Scotland) Act 2005 is repealed.

### Housing (Scotland) Act 2006 (asp 1)

**17.**[^key-146a3e0ed542c2f0587401657200eb38] In section 22 of the 2006 Act—

- (a) subsection (4)(c) is repealed, and
- (b) subsection (6) is repealed.

### Housing (Scotland) Act 2010 (asp 17)

**18.** (1)[^key-cf3c146e11627423de6a0bdedc6c592b] The 2010 Act is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-cf3c146e11627423de6a0bdedc6c592b] In section 58(1), for “the” where it secondly occurs substitute “ a ”.

(3)[^key-d5ba35716120b1e62bdb1fd0a3c9e7a0] Section 108(1)(f) is repealed.

(4)[^key-cf3c146e11627423de6a0bdedc6c592b] In section 110(1), after paragraph (a) insert—

> - (aa) the proposed disposal is not by way of granting security over the land or any interest in it,

.

(5)[^key-cf3c146e11627423de6a0bdedc6c592b] In section 124, for “122” substitute “ 121 ”.

(6)[^key-d5ba35716120b1e62bdb1fd0a3c9e7a0] Sections 140 to 144 are repealed.

(7) In schedule 2—

- (a)[^key-d5ba35716120b1e62bdb1fd0a3c9e7a0] paragraph 3(4) is repealed,
- (b)[^key-cf3c146e11627423de6a0bdedc6c592b] paragraph 9 is repealed.

### Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 (asp 8)

**19.**[^key-e97194458a52a00ad3655994efbb90f1] Paragraph 56 of schedule 7 to the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 is repealed.
