## 66. Duration of a Part 1A site licence[^c-bffa1963b349550c299aee4b826396bf]

*Extent: S*

After section 32I of the 1960 Act (inserted by section 65), insert—

> **32J. Duration of a Part 1A site licence**
>
> (1) A Part 1A site licence—
>
> - (a) comes into operation at the time specified in or determined under the licence, and
> - (b) unless terminated by its revocation, continues in force until—
>   - (i) the licence holder is not entitled to the benefit of planning permission for the use of the land as a caravan site, or any planning permission for the use of the relevant permanent site as a caravan site expires, or
>   - (ii) if earlier, the day which is 5 years after the day on which the licence comes into operation.
>
> (2) The Scottish Ministers may, by order subject to the affirmative procedure, amend subsection (1)(b)(ii) so as to substitute for the figure for the time being specified there a different figure.
