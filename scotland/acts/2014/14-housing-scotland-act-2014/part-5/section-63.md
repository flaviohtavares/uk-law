# Housing (Scotland) Act 2014, section 63

>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 63. Licensing of sites for permanent residents[^key-14022ed110b7b3880a9a965f86ca4abd]

*Extent: S*

(1) In section 32(1) of the 1960 Act (application of Part 1 to Scotland), after paragraph (l) insert—

> - (m) the modifications in Part 1A.

.

(2) After section 32 of the 1960 Act, insert—

> **PART 1A — Licensing of relevant permanent sites in Scotland**
>
> **General application**
>
> **32A. Licences under Part 1A**
>
> (1) Subject to the modifications mentioned in subsection (2), Part 1 applies in relation to—
>
> - (a) a relevant permanent site as it applies to a caravan site within the meaning of section 1(4),
> - (b) a relevant permanent site application as it applies in relation to an application for a site licence under Part 1, and
> - (c) a site licence issued or renewed under this Part (a “Part 1A site licence”) as it applies to a site licence within the meaning of section 1(1).
>
> (2) The modifications are—
>
> - (a) the offence in section 1 does not apply to the holder of a Part 1A site licence in relation to that person's use of the relevant permanent site which is the subject of the licence,
> - (b) sections 3 and 6 do not apply in relation to a relevant permanent site application,
> - (c) sections 4 and 9 do not apply in relation to a Part 1A site licence, and
> - (d) the further modifications in this Part.

.
