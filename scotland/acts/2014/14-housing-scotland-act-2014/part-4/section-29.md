# Housing (Scotland) Act 2014, section 29

## 29. Register of letting agents

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-9bd0348774cfc11669255c1680ef97bc] The Scottish Ministers must establish and maintain a register of letting agents (the “register”).

(2) The register must contain an entry for each person entered in the register setting out—

- (a)[^key-9bd0348774cfc11669255c1680ef97bc] the name and address of the person entered in the register, and
- (b)[^key-cb342943baca7316f4e5755bfaed5b55][^key-113b2154ffb7c9e9da07b72a903588b4] such information relating to that person as the Scottish Ministers may by regulations prescribe.

(3)[^key-9bd0348774cfc11669255c1680ef97bc] The Scottish Ministers must make the information contained in the register publicly available by such means as they consider appropriate.
