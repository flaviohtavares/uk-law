## 30. Application for registration

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-32863fd768754b2970a360f5fc752319] A person may apply to the Scottish Ministers—

- (a) to be entered in the register, or
- (b) to renew that person's existing entry in the register.

(2) The application must—

- (a)[^key-32863fd768754b2970a360f5fc752319] state the name and address of the applicant,
- (b)[^key-32863fd768754b2970a360f5fc752319] state whether the applicant is—
  - (i) trading as a sole trader,
  - (ii) a partnership,
  - (iii) a company, or
  - (iv) a body with some other legal status,
- (c)[^key-32863fd768754b2970a360f5fc752319] in the case where the applicant is a company registered under the Companies Act 2006 (c.46), state the company's registered number,
- (d)[^key-32863fd768754b2970a360f5fc752319] in the case where the applicant is not a natural person, state the name and address of—
  - (i)[^key-49bb4d0f21e543c2114b617e4f471e60] the individual who holds the most senior position within the management structure of the relevant partnership, company or body , and[^key-e3eeb29e0eaf4f66072d77cb48771d26]
  - (ii) any other person who owns 25% or more of the relevant partnership, company or body,
- (e)[^key-32863fd768754b2970a360f5fc752319] state the name and address of any other person who—
  - (i)[^key-c01168c535823d331d7317293f27b922] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii) [^key-1b391e7fd77e18f1814ae50cd9e02ed0]... is (or is to be) directly concerned with the control or governance of the applicant's letting agency work (whether or not the applicant is a natural person), and
- (f)[^key-2cc9733301d8c70313f338a892d3e33d][^key-8fc8b849ce60d8fd82109043f6f07047] include such other information as the Scottish Ministers may by regulations prescribe.

(3)[^key-32863fd768754b2970a360f5fc752319] The application must be accompanied by a fee of such amount (if any) as the Scottish Ministers may determine.
