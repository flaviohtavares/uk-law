## 40. Removal from register on application[^c-78e3d0b8eba0cbf9fe4edb89baa478ba]

*Extent: S*

(1) A registered letting agent may apply to the Scottish Ministers to be removed from the register.

(2) The application must be accompanied by a fee of such amount (if any) as the Scottish Ministers may determine.

(3) The Scottish Ministers must remove a registered letting agent from the register if, having considered an application under this section, they are satisfied that—

- (a) the registered letting agent is no longer carrying out letting agency work, and
- (b) it is otherwise appropriate to remove that agent from the register.

(4) The Scottish Ministers must (if possible)[^c-7603c3acd1cd18e3d9bcafbb20d738ca], as soon as practicable after making their decision under this section, notify the agent who made the application of—

- (a) their decision,
- (b) in the case of a decision to remove the agent from the register, the date of removal from the register, and
- (c) in the case of a decision not to remove the agent from the register, their reasons for that decision.
