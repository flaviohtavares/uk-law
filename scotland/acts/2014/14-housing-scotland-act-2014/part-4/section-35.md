## 35. Fit and proper person: criminal record information[^key-a2d255b845b7499719b8e9adfa0c0b1f]

*Extent: S*

(1) This section applies where the Scottish Ministers have reasonable grounds to suspect that the information provided under this Part in relation to material falling within section 34(2) is, or has become, inaccurate.

(2) In deciding under this Part if a person is a fit and proper person, the Scottish Ministers may have regard to the information which would be included in a Level 1 disclosure (within the meaning of section 1 of the Disclosure (Scotland) Act 2020)[^key-225c085bf9ccf9bca8fafa33239c536a] in relation to that person.
