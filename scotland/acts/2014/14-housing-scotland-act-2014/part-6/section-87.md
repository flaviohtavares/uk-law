## 87. Work notices[^c-f4b82402da8b9a5b4d84c39fb2ab8243]

*Extent: S*

In section 30(1) of the 2006 Act (work which may be required under a work notice)—

- (a) the word “or” at the end of paragraph (a) is repealed, and
- (b) at the end of paragraph (b), insert

  > , or
  >
  > - (c) otherwise improving the security or safety of any house (whether or not situated in an HRA).

  .
