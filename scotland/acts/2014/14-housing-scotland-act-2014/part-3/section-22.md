## 22. Carbon monoxide alarms[^c-4b138f76536bef61f5676cb974e6735c]

*Extent: S*

In section 13 of the 2006 Act—

- (a) the word “and” after paragraph (e) of subsection (1) is repealed,
- (b) after paragraph (f) of subsection (1) insert

  > , and
  >
  > - (g) the house has satisfactory provision for giving warning if carbon monoxide is present in a concentration that is hazardous to health.

  ,
- (c) after subsection (5) insert—

  > (6) In determining whether a house meets the standard of repair mentioned in subsection (1)(g), regard is to be had to any building regulations and any guidance issued by the Scottish Ministers on provision for giving warning if carbon monoxide is present in a concentration that is hazardous to health.

  .
