# Historic Environment Scotland Act 2014, section 17

> Text as at 2017-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 17. Historic Environment Scotland's functions in relation to the marine environment[^c-d75e6deae3805a620d2fb33b7724d553]

*Extent: S*

Schedule 4 modifies the Marine (Scotland) Act 2010 (asp 5) to make provision for Historic Environment Scotland's functions in relation to the marine environment.
