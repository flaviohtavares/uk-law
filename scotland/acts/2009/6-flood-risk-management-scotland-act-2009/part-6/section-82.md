# Flood Risk Management (Scotland) Act 2009, section 82

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 82. Compensation

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-6f1aaa9b81648a7a0a5b21f9c246bb3c] SEPA must compensate any person who has sustained damage in consequence of—

- (a) any exercise of the power in section 76(1)(c) or (d), or
- (b) the exercise of a right of entry conferred by section 79(1) (including the ancillary rights mentioned in section 81(1) and (2)).

(2) A local authority must compensate any person who has sustained damage in consequence of—

- (a)[^c-0a6eeacf27f4078d6efe0daae3be6d3d] scheme operations carried out by or on behalf of the local authority,
- (b)[^c-0a6eeacf27f4078d6efe0daae3be6d3d] the subsequent maintenance of any such operations by or on behalf of the local authority,
- (c)[^c-0a6eeacf27f4078d6efe0daae3be6d3d] any other exercise of the power in section 56(1),
- (d)[^c-0a6eeacf27f4078d6efe0daae3be6d3d] the carrying out of works under section 59,
- (e)[^c-0a6eeacf27f4078d6efe0daae3be6d3d] the variation or revocation of an improvement order under section 61, or
- (f)[^c-6f1aaa9b81648a7a0a5b21f9c246bb3c] the exercise of a right of entry conferred by section 79(2) (including the ancillary rights mentioned in section 81(1) and (2)).
