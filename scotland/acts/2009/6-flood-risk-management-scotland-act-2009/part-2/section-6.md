> Text as at 2024-01-01.

## 6. “The Directive”

*Extent: S*

In this Act, subject to section 6A,[^c-396a29b9a43fa582bd9c24ce863fc4d7] “**the Directive**” means Directive [2007/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0060) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the assessment and management of flood risks.
