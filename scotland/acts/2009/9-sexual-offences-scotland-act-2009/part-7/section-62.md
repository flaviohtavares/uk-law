## 62. Short title and commencement

*Extent: S*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Sexual Offences (Scotland) Act 2009.

(2)[^c22556611] This Act (other than sections 1(4), 17(3), 58 to 60 and this section) comes into force in accordance with provision made by the Scottish Ministers by order.
