# Climate Change (Scotland) Act 2009, section 42

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 42. Reports: provision of further information to the Scottish Parliament[^c-657fd4a443808cbf02ab0074971fe79f]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the Scottish Ministers lay a report mentioned in subsection (2) before the Scottish Parliament, they must—

- (a) immediately send a copy of the report to the persons who convene and chair such committees of the Scottish Parliament as are for the time being appointed by virtue of standing orders; and
- (b) as soon as reasonably practicable after doing so, and in so far as reasonably practicable, make a statement to the Parliament relating to the report.

(1A)[^c-f21e42cecadcff71156e04a8298d73d3] If—

- (a) the year covered by the report is a target year, and
- (b) the emissions reduction target for that year has not been met,

the statement under subsection (1)(b) must explain why.

(2) Those reports are reports under—

- (a) section 33(1) (report on emissions reduction targets[^c-89e1e4ed2f11299d01b266cffb15ecc0]);
- (ab)[^c-b628f2bb8032e05cb81162dffb533df8] section 34A(1) (annual report on emissions reduction);
- (aa)[^c-2333d14841661ce2a9944d6e83dfaf4b] section 35B (climate change plan: annual progress reports);
- (b) section 36(2) (report on proposals and policies to compensate for excess emissions);
- (c)[^c-fbf2d9106839483acdaa4324879a4c0e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c-fbf2d9106839483acdaa4324879a4c0e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Where the Scottish Ministers lay a report on emissions reduction targets under section 33[^c-543c0df94383050468f8edef57decc90] or 34A[^c-5f9eb7979ac96eeca7a88b7121076acc], they must also, as soon as reasonably practicable after doing so, and in so far as reasonably practicable, attend, if invited, the proceedings of any such committees of the Scottish Parliament as are for the time being appointed by virtue of standing orders, for the purposes of giving evidence on the report.

(4) The Scottish Ministers must have regard to—

- (a) any resolution passed by the Scottish Parliament;
- (b) any report published by any committee of the Parliament for the time being appointed by virtue of standing orders,

relating to the content of any report referred to in subsection (2).
