# Climate Change (Scotland) Act 2009, section 36

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 36. Reports on proposals and policies where emissions reduction targets not met[^c-08cef488a3770ce24006c6bc634116d0][^c-166f26271f603a82a83042c1a4f956b3]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-69aadca615cd499b5a16cd360bbafee9] This section applies if the Scottish Ministers lay a report under section 33 which states that an emissions reduction target has not been met.

(2) Within the period of 6 months beginning with the day that the report referred to in subsection (1) is laid before the Parliament[^c-3616b98c507c8c144c82f32a79580ce7], the Scottish Ministers must lay a report before the Scottish Parliament setting out proposals and policies to compensate in future years for the excess emissions.
