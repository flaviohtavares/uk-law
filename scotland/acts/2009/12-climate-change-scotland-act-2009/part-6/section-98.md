# Climate Change (Scotland) Act 2009, section 98

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 98. Interpretation[^key-54848fa904c81d044c2d5ea09729532f]

*Extent: S*

In this Act—

- “**additional greenhouse gas**” has the meaning given by section 12(2);
- “**advisory body**” has the meaning given by section 24(2);
- “**advisory functions**” has the meaning given by section 24(3);
- [^key-4831e0a13aa78c69a6093e05a14e609f]...
- “**authorised person**” has the meaning given by section 80(5);
- “**baseline**” has the meaning given by section 11(1);
- “**baseline year**” means—
  - (a) in relation to a greenhouse gas mentioned in subsection (2) of section 11, the year mentioned in paragraph (a), (b), (c), (d) or, as the case may be, (e) of that subsection;
  - (b) in relation to an additional greenhouse gas, any year designated by virtue of section 12(3);
- “**carbon unit**” has the meaning given by section 20(4);
- “**climate change duties**” has the meaning given by section 44(4);
- “**Committee**” means the Scottish Committee on Climate Change;
- “[^key-7583985957edd101b84745c3cd8f8360]**current international carbon reporting practice**” has the meaning given by section 19(2),
- “**deposit and return scheme**” means a scheme established by virtue of section 84(1);
- [^key-3bb272c67d5fe31d678eaccbb91f014a]...
- “**emissions**” has the meaning given by section 17(1);
- “[^key-3fedc3ea650b330a5584ab2356a1dc43]**emissions reduction target**” means—
  - (ab) a Scottish carbon budget target (and references to meeting such a target are to be construed in accordance with section A2(4)), or[^key-895bf45bd64cc271cf5fd4c780c7b496]
  - (c) the net-zero emissions target,
- “**energy efficiency**” has the meaning given by section 60(9);
- “[^key-ec35a04dcf767309ab31161206855887]**fair and safe Scottish emissions budget**” has the meaning given by section 2B(2),
- “**greenhouse gas**” has the meaning given by section 10(1);
- [^key-12ad0f0020c902823d25d5be1eb8d068]...
- “**international carbon reporting practice**” has the meaning given by section 19(1)[^key-271702b3070ac44709545614dbfbc454];
- “[^key-1b23939d10439a40be08feed5745cba4]**just transition principles**” has the meaning given by section 35C(1),
- “**local authority**” means a council constituted under section 2 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994 (c. 39);
- “**monitoring body**” has the meaning given by section 47(2);
- “**net Scottish emissions**” is to be construed in accordance with[^key-1a55eaad1f642eede821a3eade207928] section 17(1);
- “**net Scottish emissions account**” has the meaning given by section 13(1);
- “[^key-6fe4f7cf3841e1c59036fc4786e50783]**net-zero emissions target**” has the meaning given by section A1(1),
- “**net-zero emissions target year**” has the meaning given by section A1(2),
- [^key-bb7d087db19975e701c65f7d2b268f51]...
- “public body” (except in Part 4 and section 93) has the meaning given by section 24(5);
- “**relevant body**” has the meaning given by section 20A(1)[^key-ce657cb272c4529142940f4bd9176cee];
- “**relevant public body**” has the meaning given by section 44(5);
- “**recycling**” has the meaning given by section 78(4);
- “**renewable sources**” has the meaning given by section 60(9);
- “[^key-62bc4aea5972beb0ee519eb2be12daf4]**Scottish carbon budget**” has the meaning given by section A2(1),
- “**Scottish carbon budget target**” has the meaning given by section A2(3),
- “**Scottish Committee on Climate Change**” has the meaning given by section 25(2);
- “**Scottish emissions**” has the meaning given by section 17(1);
- “**Scottish removals**” has the meaning given by section 17(1);
- “**SEPA**” has the meaning given by section 79(6);
- “[^key-915ac12ece1ef3b5bc3e072ad8554982]**target year**” means—
  - (a) the final year of a period for which a Scottish carbon budget is set,
  - (b) the net-zero emissions target year,
- “[^key-470d31f0e10f821889d63689cef032cd]**target-relevant international carbon reporting practice**” has the meaning given by section 19(2),
- “**target-setting criteria**” means the matters mentioned in section 2B(1)[^key-27bd2012ba0c9885fbb244d8fbabc584];
- “**the 1990 Act**” means the Environmental Protection Act 1990 (c. 43);
- “**the 2008 Act**” means the Climate Change Act 2008 (c. 27);
- [^key-2b15a37390efa3816f5291b5cfed21fc]...
- “**UK Committee on Climate Change**” has the meaning given by section 20A(2)[^key-339951efe39e1869915920b79cc93343];
- “[^key-ebc29ada05530c883b94f4679dd78753]**United Nations sustainable development goals**” means the goals set out in “Transforming our world: the 2030 Agenda for sustainable development” adopted by the General Assembly of the United Nations by resolution A/Res/70/1 of 25 September 2015,
- “**waste**” has the meaning given by section 78(4).
