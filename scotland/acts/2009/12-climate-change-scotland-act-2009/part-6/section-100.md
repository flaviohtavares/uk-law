# Climate Change (Scotland) Act 2009, section 100

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 100. Short title and commencement

*Extent: S*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Climate Change (Scotland) Act 2009.

(2) This Act (other than this section and sections 30[^key-cfe46714ab75a49a9dbe048f630c2072] to 32, 56, 70 and 96) comes into force on such day (in the case of sections 44 to 52, being no later than 18 months after the day on which the Bill for this Act receives Royal Assent) as the Scottish Ministers may, by order, appoint.

(3) Sections 30[^key-ef643a90d0d70d61380864e3b1964a77] to 32 and 56 come into force in accordance with section 26.

(4) Section 70 comes into force on the day after the Bill for this Act receives Royal Assent.

(5) Different days may, under subsection (2), be appointed for different purposes.
