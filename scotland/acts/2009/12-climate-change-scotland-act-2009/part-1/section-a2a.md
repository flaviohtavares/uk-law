# Climate Change (Scotland) Act 2009, section A2A

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## A2A. Scottish carbon budgets: 2026 to 2045[^c-dd1843d4cfdb6c308b8a38e42f91c569][^c-ed14c80abc272325ca29ed56cca23666]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish carbon budget for the period in column 1 of the following table is the corresponding proportion of the budget-relevant baseline in column 2—

<table>
<thead>
<tr><th>Column 1</th><th>Column 2</th></tr>
<tr><th>Scottish carbon budget period</th><th>Proportion of budget-relevant baseline</th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>2026 to 2030</td><td>43%</td></tr>
<tr><td>2031 to 2035</td><td>31%</td></tr>
<tr><td>2036 to 2040</td><td>20%</td></tr>
<tr><td>2041 to 2045</td><td>6%</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

(2) In subsection (1), “**budget-relevant baseline**” means the baseline multiplied by the number of years comprising the period in column 1.
