# Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015, section 103

> Text as at 2021-02-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 103. Guidance about common good registers[^key-ad272d82cf4a143aa840e90476b1a81c]

*Extent: S*

(1) In carrying out any of the duties imposed on it by section 102, a local authority must have regard to any guidance issued by the Scottish Ministers in relation to the duties.

(2) Before issuing any such guidance, the Scottish Ministers must consult—

- (a) local authorities,
- (b) community councils, and
- (c) such community bodies as the Scottish Ministers think fit.
