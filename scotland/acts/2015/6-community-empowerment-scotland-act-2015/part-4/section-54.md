# Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015, section 54

> Text as at 2021-02-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 54. Exercise of right to buy: date of entry and payment of price[^c-a75759800ebd09912bc11856e48cad14]

*Extent: S*

In section 56 of the 2003 Act (procedure for buying)—

- (a) in subsection (3)(a), for the word “6”, substitute “ 8 ”, and
- (b) after subsection (6), insert—

  > (7) Where a later date is agreed as mentioned in subsection (3)(c), the community body must, within 7 days of the agreement—
  >
  > - (a) notify Ministers in writing of the agreement,
  > - (b) inform Ministers—
  >   - (i) of the date on which the agreement was made, and
  >   - (ii) what the later date is, and
  > - (c) provide evidence to Ministers of the matters mentioned in paragraph (b).

  .
