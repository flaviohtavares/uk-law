# Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015, section 67

> Text as at 2021-02-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 67. Application by more than one crofting community body[^key-f8c17907b00cbb9376f35bdb2b199afb]

*Extent: S*

In section 76 of the 2003 Act (right to buy same croft land exercisable by only one crofting community body), for subsection (4)(b)(i), substitute—

> - (i) each person invited, under section 73(8)(a), to send them views on the application,

.
