# Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015, section 73

> Text as at 2021-02-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 73. Meaning of creditor in standard security with right to sell[^key-191341af485137d5f10752d6aec13ad0]

*Extent: S*

After section 97 of the 2003 Act, insert—

> **97ZA. Meaning of creditor in standard security with right to sell**
>
> Any reference in this Part to a creditor in a standard security with a right to sell land is a reference to a creditor who has such a right under—
>
> - (a) section 20(2) or 23(2) of the Conveyancing and Feudal Reform (Scotland) Act 1970, or
> - (b) a warrant granted under section 24(1) of that Act.
