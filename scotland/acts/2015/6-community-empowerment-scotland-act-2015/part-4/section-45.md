# Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015, section 45

> Text as at 2021-02-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 45. Notice of expiry of registration[^key-702fd8401ee641744e0e431372327f7c]

*Extent: S*

In section 44 of the 2003 Act (duration and renewal of registration), after subsection (5), insert—

> (6) The Scottish Ministers must send written notice to a community body which has a registered community interest of the date on which that interest will cease to have effect unless it is re-registered (“**the expiry date**”).
>
> (7) A notice under subsection (6) must be sent in the period beginning on the day which falls 12 months before the expiry date and ending 28 days after that day.
