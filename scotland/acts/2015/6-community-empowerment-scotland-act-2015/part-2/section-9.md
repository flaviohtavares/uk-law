# Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015, section 9

> Text as at 2021-02-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 9. Localities: comparison of outcomes

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-06bd5dc3ed2f51ccd10d508cc881289a] Each community planning partnership must, for the purposes of this Part, divide the area of the local authority into smaller areas.

(2)[^key-037c87f52f58ff8562b0cf189c2c7777][^key-1d110d7ffae4e580b87d42cc6b6d8ef9] The smaller areas mentioned in subsection (1) (“localities”) must be of such type or description as may be specified by the Scottish Ministers by regulations.

(3)[^key-06bd5dc3ed2f51ccd10d508cc881289a] Having carried out the duty under subsection (1), the community planning partnership must identify each locality in which persons residing there experience significantly poorer outcomes which result from socio-economic disadvantage than—

- (a) those experienced by persons residing in other localities within the area of the local authority, or
- (b) those experienced generally by persons residing in Scotland.

(4)[^key-06bd5dc3ed2f51ccd10d508cc881289a] In carrying out the duty under subsection (3), a community planning partnership must take account of the needs and circumstances of persons residing in the area of the local authority.

(5)[^key-037c87f52f58ff8562b0cf189c2c7777][^key-1d110d7ffae4e580b87d42cc6b6d8ef9] Regulations under subsection (2) may specify areas of a type or description subject to any conditions specified in the regulations.

(6)[^key-037c87f52f58ff8562b0cf189c2c7777][^key-1d110d7ffae4e580b87d42cc6b6d8ef9] The Scottish Ministers may by regulations specify that localities within the area of a local authority must each be of the same type or description as may be specified in regulations under subsection (2).

(7)[^key-06bd5dc3ed2f51ccd10d508cc881289a] In this section, references to the area of a local authority mean, in relation to a community planning partnership, the area of the local authority for which the partnership is carrying out community planning.
