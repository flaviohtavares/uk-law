> Text as at 2021-02-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 16. Duty to promote community planning[^key-1dbbabc83778b1a2b8ae16cec870ab16]

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers must promote community planning when carrying out any of their functions which might affect—

- (a) community planning,
- (b) a community planning partner.
