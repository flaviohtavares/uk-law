> Text as at 2021-02-24.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 141. Guidance under Parts 2, 3, 5, 8, 9 and 10: publication

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers must publish, in such manner as they think fit, any guidance issued by them relating to Part 2, Part 3, Part 5, Part 8, Part 9 or Part 10.
