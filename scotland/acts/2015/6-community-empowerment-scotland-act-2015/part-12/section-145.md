# Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015, section 145

> Text as at 2021-02-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 145. Commencement

*Extent: S*

(1) This section, sections 141 to 143 and section 146 come into force on the day after Royal Assent.

(2) The remaining provisions of this Act come into force on such day as the Scottish Ministers may by order appoint.

(3) An order under subsection (2) may include transitional or transitory provision or savings.
