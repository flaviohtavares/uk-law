# Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015, section 29

> Text as at 2021-02-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 29. Modification of outcome improvement process

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-015fdccede9a00112f68828778ad2b15] This section applies where a public service authority establishes an outcome improvement process under section 28(a) following a participation request by a community participation body.

(2)[^key-015fdccede9a00112f68828778ad2b15] Following consultation with the community participation body, the authority may modify the outcome improvement process.

(3)[^key-e4a03862e07855dd9c9f484b907fcac1][^key-50196bab3459bb0f6e25e39aa82c8010] Where the outcome improvement process is modified under subsection (2), the authority must publish such information about the modification as may be specified in regulations made by the Scottish Ministers.
