# Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015, section 26

> Text as at 2021-02-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 26. Proposed outcome improvement process

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-e6b40e857702505c09fff4e541b07e25] This section applies where a public service authority gives a community participation body a decision notice as mentioned in section 25(3).

(2)[^c-e6b40e857702505c09fff4e541b07e25] The community participation body may make written representations in relation to the proposed outcome improvement process.

(3)[^c-e6b40e857702505c09fff4e541b07e25] Any representations under subsection (2) must be made before the end of the period of 28 days beginning with the day on which the notice is given.

(4)[^c-e6b40e857702505c09fff4e541b07e25] Before giving notice under subsection (5), the authority must take into consideration any representations made under subsection (2).

(5)[^c-e6b40e857702505c09fff4e541b07e25] The authority must, before the end of the period of 28 days beginning with the day after the expiry of the period mentioned in subsection (3), give a notice to the community participation body containing details of the outcome improvement process that is to be established.

(6)[^c-9d80860d98e85f19779df12fc434503d][^c-7205d7cad5c6db32806ed2b0f1f31a4c] The authority must publish such information about the process as may be specified in regulations made by the Scottish Ministers.

(7)[^c-e6b40e857702505c09fff4e541b07e25] The authority must publish the information mentioned in subsection (6) on a website or by other electronic means.
