# Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015, section 77

> Text as at 2021-02-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 77. Meaning of “community transfer body”

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-5f9c097594428411ef2a25890af5398a] In this Part, “**community transfer body**” means—

- (a) a community-controlled body, or
- (b) a body mentioned in subsection (2).

(2)[^c-a4e6f79df00cea6fcbad0504f4c6e0b9][^c-e07551e0cc47840e348e6af0b677c7e5] The body is a body (whether corporate or unincorporated)—

- (a) that is designated as a community transfer body by an order made by the Scottish Ministers for the purposes of this Part, or
- (b) that falls within a class of bodies designated as community transfer bodies by such an order for the purposes of this Part.

(3)[^c-a4e6f79df00cea6fcbad0504f4c6e0b9][^c-e07551e0cc47840e348e6af0b677c7e5] Where the power to make an order under subsection (2)(a) is exercised in relation to a trust, the community transfer body is to be the trustees of the trust.
