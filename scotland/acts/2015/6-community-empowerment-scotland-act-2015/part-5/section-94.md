# Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015, section 94

> Text as at 2021-02-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 94. Duty to publish register of land

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-1b0d9523ce211421355bf2c8a836b9ee] Each relevant authority must establish and maintain a register of land mentioned in subsection (2).

(2)[^key-1b0d9523ce211421355bf2c8a836b9ee] The land is land which, to the best of the authority's knowledge and belief, is owned or leased by the authority.

(3)[^key-1b0d9523ce211421355bf2c8a836b9ee] Every relevant authority must—

- (a) make arrangements to enable members of the public to inspect, free of charge, its register of land at reasonable times and at such places as the authority may determine, and
- (b) make its register of land available on a website, or by other electronic means, to members of the public.

(4)[^key-d4b0f7a7fcb525a6225e930bce05aa86][^key-934c5217c74c937d83b230f26453d694] The Scottish Ministers may by regulations specify land, or descriptions of land, that a relevant authority need not include in its register of land.

(5)[^key-d4b0f7a7fcb525a6225e930bce05aa86][^key-934c5217c74c937d83b230f26453d694] Relevant authorities must have regard to any guidance issued by the Scottish Ministers in relation to the duties imposed on the authorities under this section.

(6)[^key-d4b0f7a7fcb525a6225e930bce05aa86][^key-934c5217c74c937d83b230f26453d694] Before issuing such guidance, the Scottish Ministers must consult the relevant authorities.

(7)[^key-1b0d9523ce211421355bf2c8a836b9ee] The omission of any land owned or leased by a relevant authority from the authority's register of land does not prevent an asset transfer request being made in respect of the land.
