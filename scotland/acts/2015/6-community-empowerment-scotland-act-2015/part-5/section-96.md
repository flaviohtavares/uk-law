# Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015, section 96

> Text as at 2021-02-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 96. Guidance[^key-59737b50b51293043f66177db5c3693a][^key-b1b8c454b5217c3a36478f114abcb887]

*Extent: S*

(1) A relevant authority must have regard to any guidance issued by the Scottish Ministers about the carrying out of functions by the authority under this Part.

(2) Before issuing such guidance, the Scottish Ministers must consult such persons as they think fit.
