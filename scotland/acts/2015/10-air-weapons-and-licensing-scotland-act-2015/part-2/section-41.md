# Air Weapons and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2015, section 41

> Text as at 2021-01-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 41. Licensing objectives: protecting young persons from harm[^key-862d1c2d1fdcfd951eff990bf006b49f]

*Extent: S*

In section 4 of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 (“**the 2005 Act**”) (the licensing objectives), in subsection (1)(e), after “children” insert “ and young persons ”.
