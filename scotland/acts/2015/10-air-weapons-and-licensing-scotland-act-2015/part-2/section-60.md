# Air Weapons and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2015, section 60

> Text as at 2021-01-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 60. Personal licences: grant, duration and renewal

*Extent: S*

(1) The 2005 Act is amended as follows.

(2) In section 74 (determination of personal licence application), in subsection (3)(c), after “revoked” insert “ under any provision of this Act other than section 87(3) ”.

(3)[^key-92ec1334d206c2f238e4f48a43c2fb89] In section 77 (period of effect of personal licence), in subsection (8), for “3” substitute “ 9 ”.

(4)[^key-92ec1334d206c2f238e4f48a43c2fb89] In section 78 (renewal of personal licence)––

- (a) in subsection (2)—
  - (i) for “2” substitute “ 9 ”,
  - (ii) for “3” substitute “ 12 ”,
- (b) in subsection (5), after “74” insert “ (other than subsection (3)(ba)) ”.

(5)[^key-92ec1334d206c2f238e4f48a43c2fb89] In section 84A (power of chief constable to report conduct inconsistent with the licensing objectives), in subsection (3), for “(8)(a)” substitute “ (8) ”.
