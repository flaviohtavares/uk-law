# Air Weapons and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2015, section 73

> Text as at 2021-01-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 73. Exemptions from requirements of sections 28 to 37 of 1982 Act[^c-6f3eb5079097cd1d3a784a23c0e172e1]

*Extent: S*

After section 37 of the 1982 Act insert—

> **37A. Exemptions**
>
> (1) The Scottish Ministers may by regulations make provision specifying circumstances in which the provisions of sections 28 to 37 are not to apply.
>
> (2) Regulations under subsection (1)—
>
> - (a) may make transitional, transitory or saving provision,
> - (b) are subject to the negative procedure.

.
