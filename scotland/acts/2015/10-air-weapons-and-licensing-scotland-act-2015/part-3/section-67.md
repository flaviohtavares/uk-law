> Text as at 2021-01-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 67. Removal of exemption warrants for certain metal dealers[^key-9e87695cedd46de06ac7980771706c62]

*Extent: S*

(1) The 1982 Act is amended as follows.

(2) In section 28 (metal dealers: licensing and regulation)—

- (a) in subsection (1), for the words “Subject to subsection (2) below, a” substitute “ A ”,
- (b) subsections (2) and (3) are repealed.

(3) Section 29 (metal dealers' exemption warrants) is repealed.
