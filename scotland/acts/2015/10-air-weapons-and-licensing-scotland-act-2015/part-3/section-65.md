# Air Weapons and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2015, section 65

> Text as at 2021-01-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 65. Exemptions from requirements of sections 10 to 21 of 1982 Act

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-d9e25b818a2fa55554d17c401302b10b] Section 22 of the 1982 Act (saving for certain vehicles etc.) is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-d9e25b818a2fa55554d17c401302b10b] The existing provision becomes subsection (1).

(3) Paragraph (c) of that subsection is repealed.

(4)[^key-d9e25b818a2fa55554d17c401302b10b] After that subsection, insert—

> (2) The Scottish Ministers may by regulations specify further circumstances in which sections 10 to 21 (with the exception of subsection (7) of section 21) are not to apply.
>
> (3) Regulations under subsection (2)—
>
> - (a) may make transitional, transitory and saving provision,
> - (b) are subject to the negative procedure.

.

(5)[^key-d9e25b818a2fa55554d17c401302b10b] The title to section 22 becomes “ **Exemptions** ”.
