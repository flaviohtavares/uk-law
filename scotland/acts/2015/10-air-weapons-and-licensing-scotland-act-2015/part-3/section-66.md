# Air Weapons and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2015, section 66

> Text as at 2021-01-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 66. Penalties for failure to have appropriate licence or comply with conditions[^c-c1b965fb0d5ad13c2f3b5a9a49df4e34]

*Extent: S*

In section 7 of the 1982 Act (offences etc.)—

- (a) in subsection (1)(a), after “is” insert “ a metal dealer's licence, an itinerant metal dealer's licence or ”,
- (b) in subsection (2)—
  - (i) the word “and” immediately following paragraph (aa) is repealed,
  - (ii) after paragraph (aa) insert—

    > - (ab) in a case where the licence is a metal dealer's licence or an itinerant metal dealer's licence, to such fine or imprisonment as is mentioned in subsection (1)(a) (or to both), and

    .
