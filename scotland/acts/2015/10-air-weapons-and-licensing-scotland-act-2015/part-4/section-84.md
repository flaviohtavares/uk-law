## 84. Interpretation

*Extent: S*

(1) In this Act—

- “**the 1982 Act**” means the Civic Government (Scotland) Act 1982,
- “**the 2005 Act**” means the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005.

(2) See section 40 for the interpretation of words and expressions used in Part 1.
