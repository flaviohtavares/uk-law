# Air Weapons and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2015, section 9

> Text as at 2021-01-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 9. Alignment of different types of certificate

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-0224b96706bb625652a68843b7dea21d][^key-c404cf1af75530db9ab165c5d48c537f] Subsection (2) applies where an individual—

- (a) holds a firearm or shot gun certificate, and
- (b) makes an application for the grant or renewal of an air weapon certificate under section 3.

(2)[^key-0224b96706bb625652a68843b7dea21d][^key-c404cf1af75530db9ab165c5d48c537f] Where this subsection applies, the applicant may request that the chief constable grant or renew an air weapon certificate for such shorter period than is provided for in section 8 as is appropriate to secure that it expires on the same day as the applicant's firearm or shot gun certificate (or, if the applicant holds both a firearm and shot gun certificate, either of them).

(3)[^key-0bc138390bdfb21ffc2079504eb69ca8] Subsection (4) applies where an individual—

- (a) holds an air weapon certificate, and
- (b) makes an application for the grant or renewal of a firearm or shot gun certificate under the 1968 Act.

(4)[^key-0bc138390bdfb21ffc2079504eb69ca8] Where this subsection applies, the applicant may make an application under section 3 of this Act for the air weapon certificate to be renewed as from the same day as that on which the firearm or shot gun certificate is granted or renewed.
