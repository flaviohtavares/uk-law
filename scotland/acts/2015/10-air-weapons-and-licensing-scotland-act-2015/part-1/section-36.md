# Air Weapons and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2015, section 36

> Text as at 2021-01-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 36. Power to make further provision[^key-5e738918006af1c841761d035971b789]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may by regulations make further provision for the purposes of this Part.

(2) Without limiting that generality (or the generality of section 85), regulations under subsection (1) may—

- (a) make provision about the application processes under this Part (for example, prescribing the form and content of applications, any required supporting documentation or making further provision about the verification of applications),
- (b) make provision in relation to air weapon certificates, police permits, visitor permits, event permits and approvals of air weapon clubs (for example, prescribing their form and content or the conditions which may or must be attached to them).
