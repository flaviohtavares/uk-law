# Air Weapons and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2015, section 2

> Text as at 2021-01-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 2. Requirement for air weapon certificate

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-07688589304998073c4f09311800f7d0] It is an offence for a person to use, possess, purchase or acquire an air weapon without holding an air weapon certificate.

(2)[^c-07688589304998073c4f09311800f7d0] A person who commits an offence under subsection (1) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both),
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or a fine (or both).

(3)[^c-07688589304998073c4f09311800f7d0] Schedule 1 contains exemptions from—

- (a) the offence under subsection (1), and
- (b) certain other offences under this Part.

(4)[^c-1867f1e59cf8304caaec4c5c524ef11a] The Scottish Ministers may by regulations amend schedule 1 so as to—

- (a) add further exemptions,
- (b) remove or modify exemptions.
