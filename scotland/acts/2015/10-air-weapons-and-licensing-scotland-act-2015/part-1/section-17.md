# Air Weapons and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2015, section 17

> Text as at 2021-01-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 17. Event permits

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-f662f6fa4bfa0b5bd2c75cd8dafa64d3][^key-37656faf058657ab0533c639dea8ee3e] The chief constable may, on the application of a person (“the organiser”) who is organising or otherwise responsible for an event, grant a permit authorising individuals at the event to borrow, hire, use and possess air weapons while engaging in an event activity without holding an air weapon certificate (“an event permit”).

(2)[^key-f662f6fa4bfa0b5bd2c75cd8dafa64d3][^key-37656faf058657ab0533c639dea8ee3e] The chief constable may, when granting an event permit, attach conditions to it.

(3)[^key-c115f1e68c5adc851ac2c9cc169466e4] The organiser must ensure that the event permit (or a copy of it) is prominently displayed at the event so as to be capable of being read by any person attending the event.

(4)[^key-c115f1e68c5adc851ac2c9cc169466e4] It is an offence for the organiser—

- (a) to fail to comply with a condition attached to the event permit, or
- (b) without reasonable excuse, to fail to comply with subsection (3).

(5)[^key-c115f1e68c5adc851ac2c9cc169466e4] A person who commits an offence under subsection (4) is liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.

(6)[^key-f662f6fa4bfa0b5bd2c75cd8dafa64d3][^key-37656faf058657ab0533c639dea8ee3e] An application for an event permit is valid only if it complies with the requirements of any regulations under section 36 which apply to the application.

(7)[^key-f662f6fa4bfa0b5bd2c75cd8dafa64d3][^key-37656faf058657ab0533c639dea8ee3e] For the purposes of this section, an “event activity” is an activity—

- (a) involving the use and possession of air weapons by individuals, and
- (b) which has been planned by (or on behalf of) the organiser as part of the event.
