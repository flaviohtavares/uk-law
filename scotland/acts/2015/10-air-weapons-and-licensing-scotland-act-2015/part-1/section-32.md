# Air Weapons and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2015, section 32

> Text as at 2021-01-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 32. Time limit for offences[^c-9ab56533b1b5e144ae8d000e3c108fa5]

*Extent: S*

Section 136 of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995 (time limit for certain offences) applies to an air weapon offence which is triable only summarily as if the references in subsection (1) of that section to 6 months were to 36 months (and subsection (2) of that section were omitted).
