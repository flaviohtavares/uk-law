# Air Weapons and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2015, section 34

> Text as at 2021-01-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 34. Appeals

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] A person aggrieved by a decision of the chief constable under a section listed in subsection (2) may appeal against the decision to the appropriate sheriff.

(2) The sections are—

- (a)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] section 5(1) (grant or renewal of air weapon certificate),
- (b)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] section 6(2) (air weapon certificate: conditions),
- (c)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] section 7(3)(b) (special requirements and conditions for young person's air weapon certificate),
- (d)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] section 10(1) (variation of air weapon certificate),
- (e)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] section 11(1)(a) or (2) (revocation of air weapon certificate),
- (f)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] section 12(1) (police permits),
- (g)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] section 13(1) or (6) (visitor permits),
- (h)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] section 14(3)(b) or (4)(b) (visitor permits: young persons),
- (i)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] section 15(2) (police and visitor permits: conditions),
- (j)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] section 16(1) (police and visitor permits: variation and revocation),
- (k)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] section 17(1) or (2) (event permits),
- (l)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] section 18(1), (3) or (5) (approval of air weapon clubs),
- (m)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] section 19(1) (variation of approval for air weapon clubs),
- (n)[^key-707a1954ed4bde7542de31c4de150755] section 29(9)(b) (forfeiture and disposal of air weapons).

(3)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] An appeal must be made within the period of 21 days beginning with the date on which the decision appealed against was made.

(4)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] An appeal under this section is to be determined on the merits (and not by way of review).

(5)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] The sheriff hearing the appeal may consider any evidence or other matter, whether or not it was available at the time the chief constable made the decision appealed against.

(6)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] On determining the appeal, the sheriff may—

- (a) dismiss the appeal,
- (b) give the chief constable such direction as the sheriff considers appropriate as respects the matter which is the subject of the appeal.

(7)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] The decision of the sheriff may be appealed against only on a point of law.

(8)[^key-58ba0001ba1f71ed959a1d00ec489a27] In this section, “**the appropriate sheriff**” means—

- (a) in a case where the appellant resides in Scotland, a sheriff of the sheriffdom in which the appellant resides, or
- (b) in a case where the appellant resides outwith Scotland, a sheriff of the sheriffdom of Lothian and Borders, sitting at Edinburgh.
