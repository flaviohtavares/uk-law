# Air Weapons and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2015, section 39

> Text as at 2021-01-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 39. Guidance

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-7b8896a6613aa5d07a833492f53f0171] The chief constable must, in exercising any function under this Part, have regard to any guidance issued by the Scottish Ministers.

(2)[^c-69739fd5b89bbb3d50054f84b8eb7af8] The Scottish Ministers must publish any guidance they issue for the purposes of this Part.

(3)[^c-69739fd5b89bbb3d50054f84b8eb7af8] The Scottish Ministers may revise and revoke such guidance.
