# Redress for Survivors (Historical Child Abuse in Care) (Scotland) Act 2021, section 48

> Text as at 2022-02-09.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 48. Report on effect of waiver on participation in redress scheme[^key-4ff40adb8ea28671eb36fd16f91c3fa2]

*Extent: S*

Before the end of the period of 18 months beginning with the day section 46 comes into force, the Scottish Ministers must lay before the Scottish Parliament a report setting out—

- (a) their assessment of—
  - (i) the impact (if any) of the waiver under section 46 on applications for a redress payment,
  - (ii) the effectiveness of the waiver in encouraging public authorities, voluntary organisations and other persons to become scheme contributors,
- (b) the steps (if any) they intend to take as a result of that assessment,
- (c) where they do not intend to take any such steps, their reasons for that.
