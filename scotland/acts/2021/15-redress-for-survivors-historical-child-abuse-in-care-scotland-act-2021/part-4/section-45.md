# Redress for Survivors (Historical Child Abuse in Care) (Scotland) Act 2021, section 45

>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 45. Information about previous payments: further provision[^key-6522228e925426e9dbff1435e831bd34]

*Extent: S*

(1) This section applies where—

- (a) an applicant provides information about a relevant payment to the Scottish Ministers in accordance with section 44,
- (b) the applicant has entered into a settlement or other agreement with any other person in relation to the relevant payment, and
- (c) the settlement or other agreement prohibits the applicant from disclosing that information.

(2) The disclosure of the information in accordance with section 44 is not to be treated as a breach of the settlement or other agreement.
