# Redress for Survivors (Historical Child Abuse in Care) (Scotland) Act 2021, section 35

> Text as at 2022-02-09.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 35. Decision-making panels[^key-596e3957859f98f57f12f924328ffced]

*Extent: S*

(1) An application for a redress payment is to be determined on behalf of Redress Scotland by a panel of members of Redress Scotland appointed by the chairing member in accordance with this section.

(2) A panel appointed to determine an application for a fixed rate payment or a next of kin payment is to consist of at least 2 members.

(3) A panel appointed to determine an application for an individually assessed payment is to consist of at least 3 members.
