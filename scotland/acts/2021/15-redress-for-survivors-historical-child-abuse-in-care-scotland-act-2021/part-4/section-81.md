# Redress for Survivors (Historical Child Abuse in Care) (Scotland) Act 2021, section 81

> Text as at 2022-02-09.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 81. Redress Scotland's power in relation to information and evidence[^key-b3e48eb4c88074a99ed683c077fd79cb]

*Extent: S*

(1) Redress Scotland may ask the Scottish Ministers to—

- (a) issue a notice under section 79,
- (b) issue a notice under section 80,
- (c) provide Redress Scotland with specified information, documents, objects, other items of evidence or evidence in the form of a written statement.

(2) The Scottish Ministers must comply with a request under subsection (1)(a) or (b) before the end of the period of 4 weeks beginning with the date on which the request was received by them.

(3) The Scottish Ministers must comply with a request under subsection (1)(c) before the end of such period as Redress Scotland specifies in relation to the request (which may not be less than 4 weeks beginning with the date on which the request was received by them).
