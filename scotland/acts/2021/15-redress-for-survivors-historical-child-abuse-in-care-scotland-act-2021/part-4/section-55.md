# Redress for Survivors (Historical Child Abuse in Care) (Scotland) Act 2021, section 55

> Text as at 2022-02-09.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 55. Review panels[^key-d471dd7281f7c861cab8f0134dced03f]

*Extent: S*

(1) A request for a review is to be determined on behalf of Redress Scotland by a panel of members of Redress Scotland appointed by the chairing member in accordance with this section (“a review panel”).

(2) A review panel—

- (a) is to consist of at least 3 members,
- (b) must not include any member of the panel whose determination is the subject of the request for a review.
