# Redress for Survivors (Historical Child Abuse in Care) (Scotland) Act 2021, section 23

> Text as at 2022-02-09.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 23. Power to create exceptions to eligibility[^c-288f63e50479076a3c09fe2794b91c26]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may by regulations provide that an application may not be made under section 18—

- (a) if the person who carried out the abuse was of a specified description,
- (b) if the abuse was carried out in circumstances of a specified description,
- (c) if the person who was abused came to be resident in the relevant care setting concerned in specified circumstances, or
- (d) if the person who was abused was resident in the relevant care setting concerned for a specified purpose.

(2) In subsection (1), “**specified**” means specified in the regulations.
