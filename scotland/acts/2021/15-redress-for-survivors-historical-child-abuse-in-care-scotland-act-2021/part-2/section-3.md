# Redress for Survivors (Historical Child Abuse in Care) (Scotland) Act 2021, section 3

> Text as at 2022-02-09.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3. Establishment[^key-8c8001db61dfa5a26c7281d13ed87fd5]

*Extent: S*

(1) Redress Scotland (in Gaelic, Ceartaich Alba) is established.

(2) Redress Scotland is a body corporate.

(3) Redress Scotland has the functions conferred on it under or by virtue of this Act and any other enactment.
