# Redress for Survivors (Historical Child Abuse in Care) (Scotland) Act 2021, section 11

> Text as at 2022-02-09.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 11. Duty on the Scottish Ministers and Redress Scotland to co-operate[^key-77e428ff1d141ce2bb45c004c60be1a7]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers and Redress Scotland must co-operate with one another in exercising the functions in relation to the redress scheme conferred on them or on it under or by virtue of this Act.

(2) Subsection (1) is subject to section 12.
