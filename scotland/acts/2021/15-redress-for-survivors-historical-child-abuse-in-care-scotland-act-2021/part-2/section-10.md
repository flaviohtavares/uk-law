# Redress for Survivors (Historical Child Abuse in Care) (Scotland) Act 2021, section 10

> Text as at 2022-02-09.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 10. Provision of administrative support to Redress Scotland[^key-d162c967f9886652c7dc18d42d0a737e]

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers must provide such administrative support to Redress Scotland as they consider necessary to enable Redress Scotland to perform the functions conferred on it under or by virtue of this Act.
