# Redress for Survivors (Historical Child Abuse in Care) (Scotland) Act 2021, section 6

> Text as at 2022-02-09.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 6. Independence[^key-6e9c01b54b856b10d5111e04a817960e]

*Extent: S*

(1) In performing the functions conferred on it under or by virtue of this Act, Redress Scotland is not subject to the direction or control of any member of the Scottish Government.

(2) Subsection (1) is subject to any contrary provision in this or any other enactment.
