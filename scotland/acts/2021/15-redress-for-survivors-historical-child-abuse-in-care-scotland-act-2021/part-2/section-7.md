## 7. Functions of Redress Scotland[^key-b3c3bd06e992f17a2f42406636c29d81]

*Extent: S*

Redress Scotland has the general function of making determinations in connection with applications by individuals under the redress scheme, including determining—

- (a) applications for redress payments,
- (b) requests for a review of a determination,
- (c) referrals for reconsideration of a determination where the determination may have been materially affected by an error.
