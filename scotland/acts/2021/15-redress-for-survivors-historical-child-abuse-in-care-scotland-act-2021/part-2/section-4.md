# Redress for Survivors (Historical Child Abuse in Care) (Scotland) Act 2021, section 4

> Text as at 2022-02-09.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 4. Status[^c-cfa8941819f51b3e92f44f3910084d0c]

*Extent: S*

(1) Redress Scotland—

- (a) is not a servant or agent of the Crown,
- (b) does not enjoy any status, immunity or privilege of the Crown.

(2) Redress Scotland's members and staff are not to be regarded as civil servants.
