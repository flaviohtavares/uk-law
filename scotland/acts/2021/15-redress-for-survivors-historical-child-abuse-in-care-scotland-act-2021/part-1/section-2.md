# Redress for Survivors (Historical Child Abuse in Care) (Scotland) Act 2021, section 2

> Text as at 2022-02-09.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 2. Meaning of “redress scheme”[^c-0bce3a1ef5189f84d8ca45880c5c937f]

*Extent: S*

In this Act, the “**redress scheme**” means the scheme established by this Act for financial redress and related support for and in respect of survivors of historical child abuse in relevant care settings in Scotland.
