# Heat Networks (Scotland) Act 2021, section 75

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 75. Registration of network wayleave rights

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Scottish Ministers may by regulations make provision for or about the registration of network wayleave rights.

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) may in particular include provision about—

- (a) how a network wayleave right is to be registered,
- (b) the information (including information in the form of a document) that must be included as part of any entry in the register,
- (c) who is required to establish and maintain a register of network wayleave rights,
- (d) fees payable in connection with the registration of a network wayleave right,
- (e) the form and manner of any register of network wayleave rights,
- (f) how the register is to be made available for inspection by members of the public,
- (g) what payment (if any) the licence holder is entitled to charge for supplying documents relating to network wayleave rights to members of the public,
- (h) the consequences of failure to comply with the requirements of the registration.

(3) Regulations under subsection (1) may modify any enactment (including this Act).
