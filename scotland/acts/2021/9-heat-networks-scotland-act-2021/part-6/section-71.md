# Heat Networks (Scotland) Act 2021, section 71

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 71. Assignation of network wayleave rights

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A network wayleave right may be assigned by a licence holder to another licence holder.

(2) But a network wayleave right in respect of land that is conferred by a necessary wayleave may be assigned to another licence holder only with the consent of the Scottish Ministers.

(3) A wayleave document is void to the extent that—

- (a) it prevents or limits the assignation of the network wayleave right to another licence holder,
- (b) it makes the assignation of the network wayleave right to another licence holder subject to a condition (including a condition requiring the payment of money).
