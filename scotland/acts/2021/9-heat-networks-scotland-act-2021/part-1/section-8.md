# Heat Networks (Scotland) Act 2021, section 8

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 8. Heat networks licence special conditions

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) In addition to the standard conditions, the licensing authority may include in a heat networks licence any special conditions (whether or not relating to the activities to be authorised by the licence) it considers appropriate.

(2) The licensing authority may provide in a heat networks licence that a special condition of the licence—

- (a) has effect or ceases to have effect, or
- (b) may be modified,

at such time, in such manner and in such circumstances as the licensing authority considers appropriate.
