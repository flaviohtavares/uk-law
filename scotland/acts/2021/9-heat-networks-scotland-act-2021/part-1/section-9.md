# Heat Networks (Scotland) Act 2021, section 9

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 9. Period of effect of heat networks licence

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

A heat networks licence—

- (a) has effect on the date specified in the licence, and
- (b) continues to have effect unless the licence is—
  - (i) revoked by the licensing authority in accordance with section 11, or
  - (ii) surrendered by the person holding the licence in accordance with the conditions of the licence.
