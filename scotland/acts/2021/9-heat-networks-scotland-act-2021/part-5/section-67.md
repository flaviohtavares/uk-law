> Text as at 2026-01-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 67. Interpretation of Part 5[^c-b2d172a3fb8a0f244f2172802a74282e][^c-f4f424195ab5bfafd1680145c1149e73]

*Extent: S*

In this Part—

- “**building assessment report**” has the meaning given in section 63(2),
- “**non-domestic building**” means a building other than a dwelling,
- “**relevant person**” means—
  - (a) a Scottish public authority within the meaning of section 3(1)(a) of the Freedom of Information (Scotland) Act 2002,
  - (b) such other person, or person of such description, as the Scottish Ministers may specify by regulations.
