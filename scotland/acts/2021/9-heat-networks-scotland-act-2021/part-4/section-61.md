# Heat Networks (Scotland) Act 2021, section 61

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 61. Compensation on revocation of heat network zone permit

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Scottish Ministers may by regulations make provision for or about the payment of compensation to the person holding a heat network zone permit in consequence of the revocation of a heat network zone permit as mentioned in section 59(1)(c).

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) may in particular make provision about—

- (a) the circumstances in which compensation is payable,
- (b) the calculation of compensation,
- (c) the procedure to be followed in connection with claiming compensation,
- (d) the review of decisions made under the regulations,
- (e) appeals against decisions made under the regulations.
