# Heat Networks (Scotland) Act 2021, section 55

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 55. Meaning of “permit authority”

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

In this Part, the “**permit authority**” means—

- (a) the Scottish Ministers, or
- (b) such other person as the Scottish Ministers may by regulations designate as the permit authority for the purposes of this Part.
