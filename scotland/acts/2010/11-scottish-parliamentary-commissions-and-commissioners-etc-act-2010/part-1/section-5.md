# Scottish Parliamentary Commissions and Commissioners etc. Act 2010, section 5

> Text as at 2018-03-12.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 5. Protection from actions for defamation[^key-573dc8cb8d0d906366d2492e28ab8c91]

*Extent: S*

(1) For the purposes of the law of defamation—

- (a) any statement made by the Commissioner or a member of the Commissioner’s staff in pursuance of the Commissioner’s functions under the Ethical Standards Act [^key-98e84dfbe0d68a742b9b7cd7b8c95ae9]... the Parliamentary Standards Act or the Lobbying (Scotland) Act 2016[^key-88155564548889907281303961047555] is absolutely privileged,
- (b) any statement made by the Commissioner or a member of the Commissioner’s staff in pursuance of the Commissioner’s other functions has qualified privilege, and
- (c) any statement made to the Commissioner or any member of the Commissioner’s staff in pursuance of the purposes of the Ethical Standards Act, the Parliamentary Standards Act [^key-3c69b2f75781704102c2c8eeb8797844]... the Public Appointments Act or the Lobbying (Scotland) Act 2016[^key-371734d0410c89efa5a33b880e153b57] has qualified privilege.

(2) In subsection (1), “statement” has the same meaning as in the Defamation Act [1996 (c.31)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/31) .
