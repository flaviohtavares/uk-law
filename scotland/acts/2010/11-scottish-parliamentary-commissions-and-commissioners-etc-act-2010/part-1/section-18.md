# Scottish Parliamentary Commissions and Commissioners etc. Act 2010, section 18

> Text as at 2018-03-12.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 18. Advisers, assessors and other services

*Extent: S*

(1) The Commissioner may obtain[^c-32a07a104f637ff01f539ca06fd5bae7] advice, assistance or any other service from any person who, in the opinion of the Commissioner[^c-6b6d1110de426d56063ded94688050d3], is qualified to give it.

(2) The Commissioner[^c-d1943c9e9a656188bf818137e497f1e9] may pay to that person such fees and allowances as the Commissioner[^c-77b0d7bdf1d9cbb2f0ab8d591c22b427] determines.

(3) Any payment under subsection (2) is subject to the approval of the Parliamentary corporation.
