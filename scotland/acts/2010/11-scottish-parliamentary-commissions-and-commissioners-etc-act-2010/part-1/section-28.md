# Scottish Parliamentary Commissions and Commissioners etc. Act 2010, section 28

> Text as at 2018-03-12.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28. Interpretation of Part 1

*Extent: S*

In this Part, except where the context otherwise requires—

- [^c-6da2babcdc74852c0a3c05370e87fc8f]...
- “Commissioner” means the Commissioner for Ethical Standards in Public Life in Scotland,[^c-a3201d720b43103bae4284e5bb6eb1be]
- “the Ethical Standards Act” means the Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act [2000 (asp 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2000/7),
- “**the Parliament**” means the Scottish Parliament,
- “**Parliamentary corporation**” means the Scottish Parliamentary Corporate Body.
- “the Parliamentary Standards Act” means the Scottish Parliamentary Standards Commissioner Act[^c-13d5728faf5edd3be917de59b6146610] [2002 (asp 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2002/16) , and
- “the Public Appointments Act” means the Public Appointments and Public Bodies etc. (Scotland) Act [2003 (asp 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2003/4) .
