# Scottish Parliamentary Commissions and Commissioners etc. Act 2010, section 4

> Text as at 2018-03-12.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 4. Independence of Commissioner[^c-3f10b981f5756a324d3ee6a25c2e08d9]

*Extent: S*

In the performance of the Commissioner’s functions, the Commissioner is[^c-3a15871cadf1cb736b82430f0d009960] not subject to the direction or control of—

- (a) any member of the Parliament,
- (b) any member of the Scottish Government[^c-2a8548247ab23419d2e130eedffbfccc], or
- (c) the Parliamentary corporation.
