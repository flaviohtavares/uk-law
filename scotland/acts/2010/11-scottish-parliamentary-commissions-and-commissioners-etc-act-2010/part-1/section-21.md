# Scottish Parliamentary Commissions and Commissioners etc. Act 2010, section 21

> Text as at 2018-03-12.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 21. Funding

*Extent: S*

(1) The Parliamentary corporation is to—

- (a) pay the remuneration and allowances of the[^key-b96b4dd61a95096a20ef91eb25a41a67] Commissioner,
- (b) pay any expenses properly incurred by the Commissioner[^key-f73f808dae73065ebaa016a10e54e55f] in the performance of the Commissioner’s[^key-2b7f538f04cc4435545698180f0ce3e6] functions, so far as not met out of sums received and applied by the Commissioner[^key-5c1b9086b97ea8bf8e20fa625579a0ff] under section 12(5),
- (c) indemnify the [^key-431c51a66cfc3469b3f44761e5069ddd]... Commissioner in respect of any liabilities incurred by the[^key-77485e188a402e27ec95bd772b8be097] Commissioner in the performance of the[^key-e0b7e446c468ab7509b614a35fd6716f] Commissioner's functions.

(2) Subsection (1)(b) does not require the Parliamentary corporation to pay any expenses incurred by the Commissioner[^key-39274670bcc51fe7d5aa76d58b2f1e75] which exceed or are otherwise not covered by a budget or, as the case may be, a revised budget approved under section 20.

(3) However, the Parliamentary corporation may pay those expenses.
