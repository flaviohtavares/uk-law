# Scottish Parliamentary Commissions and Commissioners etc. Act 2010, section 19

> Text as at 2018-03-12.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 19. Accountable officer

*Extent: S*

(1) The Parliamentary corporation must designate the Commissioner[^c-248aa749c5b35653b41653ff2e3e78fa] or a member of the Commissioner’s[^c-d4627463e423b12bbb254779684a21a8] staff as the accountable officer for the purposes of this section.

(2) The functions of the accountable officer are—

- (a) signing the accounts of the expenditure and receipts of the Commissioner[^c-1720a051d966f8e375390b72247599bd],
- (b) ensuring the propriety and regularity of the finances of the Commissioner,
- (c) ensuring that the resources of the Commissioner are used economically, efficiently and effectively, and
- (d) the duty in subsection (3).

(3) Where the accountable officer is not the Commissioner and[^c-fd34df339409ee661d4dad2fa43fb5c1] is required to act in some way but considers that to do so would be inconsistent with the proper performance of a function set out in subsection (2)(a) to (c), the accountable officer must—

- (a) obtain written authority from the Commissioner[^c-e2987cd7403a1d67aae317df56c159fd] before taking the action, and
- (b) as soon as possible, send a copy of the authority to the Auditor General for Scotland.

(4) The accountable officer is answerable to the Parliament for the performance of the functions in subsection (2).
