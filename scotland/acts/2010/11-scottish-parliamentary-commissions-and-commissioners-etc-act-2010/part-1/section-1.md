## 1. The Commissioner for Ethical Standards in Public Life in Scotland[^c-46debb3eb243a98e6d8c7bc2f0d996ff]

*Extent: S*

(1) There is to be a Commissioner for Ethical Standards in Public Life in Scotland (the “Commissioner”).

(2) The Commissioner is to be an individual appointed by the Parliamentary corporation with the agreement of the Parliament.

(3) The functions of the Commissioner are those conferred on the Commissioner by the following enactments—

- (a) the Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act [2000 (asp 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2000/7) ,
- (b) the Scottish Parliamentary Standards Commissioner Act [2002 (asp 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2002/16) , [^c-826caf8469bfc1da1ff3cd0185850d8d]...
- (c) the Public Appointments and Public Bodies etc. (Scotland) Act [2003 (asp 4) , and](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2003/4)[^c-c641c8f6516a261fe3e4939a53d4eb47]
- (d) the Lobbying (Scotland) Act 2016.
