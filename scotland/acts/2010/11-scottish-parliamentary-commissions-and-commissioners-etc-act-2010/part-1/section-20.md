# Scottish Parliamentary Commissions and Commissioners etc. Act 2010, section 20

> Text as at 2018-03-12.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 20. Budget

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-8e948d11e07b24fa7d63526d8aa954da] The Commissioner[^key-79df446ded84abe56edcd0e7e7cea867] must, before the start of each financial year, prepare proposals for the Commissioner’s[^key-1b22b2ebc3e16170fcc153c945896b7a] use of resources and expenditure during the year (a “budget”) and, by such date as the Parliamentary corporation determines, send the budget to the Parliamentary corporation for approval.

(2) The Commissioner[^key-03f080c54b20d4c24bebc00e42456e85] may, in the course of a financial year, prepare a revised budget for the remainder of the year and send it to the Parliamentary corporation for approval.

(3) In preparing a budget or a revised budget, the Commissioner[^key-eca1a7053ee4a65e275bd3caa51a6f69] must ensure that the resources of the Commissioner will be used economically, efficiently and effectively.

(4) A budget or revised budget must contain a statement that the Commissioner[^key-6a49439782aad734a3ec870f1d8b1aa1] has complied with the duty under subsection (3).
