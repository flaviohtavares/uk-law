## 18. Co-operation with other regulators

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-c2b7233faedbdec4aaca083fcdc1f0f0] The Regulator must, so far as consistent with the proper performance of its functions, seek to secure co-operation between it and other relevant regulators.

(2)[^key-c2b7233faedbdec4aaca083fcdc1f0f0] “Relevant regulators” are—

- (a) the Regulator of Social Housing[^key-bf3b0f331df475ca04a0cb6c848047dc],
- (b) the Office of the Scottish Charity Regulator,
- (c) Healthcare Improvement Scotland,
- (d) Social Care and Social Work Improvement Scotland,
- (e) the Scottish Public Services Ombudsman,
- (f)[^key-2469b4bc78d587c21bea24ed19cf24ac] the Financial Conduct Authority,
- (fa) the Prudential Regulation Authority,
- (g) the registrar of companies, and
- (h) the Accounts Commission for Scotland.

(3)[^key-51c9177efdf52b0ecdf489e15ae3d20e] The Regulator may disclose any information to any relevant regulator—

- (a) for any purpose connected with the performance of the Regulator's functions, or
- (b) for the purpose of enabling or assisting the relevant regulator to perform any functions.
