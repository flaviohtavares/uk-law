## 113. Disposals resulting in change of landlord[^c-ed97bf0a40e411c064d9f362c333f101]

*Extent: S*

(1) This Chapter applies to a disposal of land by a registered social landlord to which section 107(4) applies.[^c-ba15a2d55202880c451c220f19a5f852]

(2)[^c-7c83c406869b1d1f6ce1ca65f789685d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Where this Chapter applies to only a part of a disposal of land, it applies to that part as to a separate disposal.
