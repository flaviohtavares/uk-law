# Housing (Scotland) Act 2010, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2010/17
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 70. Delivery of accounts and audit[^key-379c2ccf7961d6adef59194b6b05b86a]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-8a49d567b24faa9c1c88f7a06e3d7c7b] A registered social landlord must provide the Regulator with a copy of its accounts and auditor's report within 6 months of the end of the period to which they relate.

(2)[^key-0f996e9985c26b623dfc759a22157f48] In respect of the financial year ending with 31 March 2020—

- (a) subsection (1) does not apply,
- (b) a registered social landlord must provide the Regulator with a copy of its accounts and auditor’s report within 9 months of the end of the period to which they relate.
