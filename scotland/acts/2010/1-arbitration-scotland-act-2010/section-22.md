## 22. Saving for other bases of recognition or enforcement[^c-93fdd6a5985a0b8e5bb27b3beec66558]

*Extent: S*

Nothing in sections 19 to 21 affects any other right to rely on or enforce a Convention award in pursuance of any other enactment or rule of law.
