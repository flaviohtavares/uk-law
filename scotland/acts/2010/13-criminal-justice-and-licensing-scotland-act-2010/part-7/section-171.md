# Criminal Justice and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2010, section 171

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2023-03-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2010/13
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 171. Abolition of common law rules[^key-4527701d9668509f740ef3a1c1ebb549]

*Extent: S*

Any rule of law providing for—

- (a) the special defence of insanity,
- (b) the plea of diminished responsibility, or
- (c) insanity in bar of trial,

ceases to have effect.
