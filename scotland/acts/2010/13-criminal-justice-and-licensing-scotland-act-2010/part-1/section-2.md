## 2. The Council's objectives[^key-e4121fef24229b99768ad993847007f8]

*Extent: S*

The Council must, in carrying out its functions, seek to—

- (a) promote consistency in sentencing practice,
- (b) assist the development of policy in relation to sentencing,
- (c) promote greater awareness and understanding of sentencing policy and practice.
