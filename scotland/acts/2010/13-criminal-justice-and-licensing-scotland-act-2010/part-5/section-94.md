## 94. Upper age limit for jurors[^key-7c1e0a17c1fd99c378a67eb7d49d27c2]

*Extent: S*

(1) Section 1 of the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1980 (c.55) (qualification of jurors) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a) in paragraph (b), at beginning insert “ subject to subsection (1A), ”, and
- (b) the words “, civil or criminal” are repealed.

(3) After subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) In relation to criminal proceedings, a person is qualified and liable to serve as a juror despite being over 65 years of age.

.
