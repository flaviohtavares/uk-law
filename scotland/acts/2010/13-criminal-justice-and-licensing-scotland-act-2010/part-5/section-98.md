## 98. Sharing information with anti-fraud organisations[^key-e67f31c0c22f0845f4512b7e042eb66f]

*Extent: S*

In the Serious Crime Act 2007 (c.27), the following provisions are repealed—

- (a) in section 68 (disclosure of information to prevent fraud), subsections (5) and (6),
- (b) in section 69 (offence for certain further disclosures of information), subsection (3), and
- (c) in section 71 (code of practice for disclosure of information to prevent fraud)—
  - (i) subsection (4), and
  - (ii) in subsection (6), the definition of “relevant public authority”.
