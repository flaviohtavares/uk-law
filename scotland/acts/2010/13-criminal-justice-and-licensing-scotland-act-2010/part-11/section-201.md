## 201. Orders and regulations

*Extent: S*

(1) Any power of the Scottish Ministers to make regulations or an order under this Act is exercisable by statutory instrument.

(2) Any such power includes power to make—

- (a) such incidental, supplementary, consequential, transitional, transitory or saving provision as the Scottish Ministers think necessary or expedient,
- (b) different provision for different purposes or different areas.

(3) Subject to subsection (4), a statutory instrument containing regulations or an order under this Act (except an order under section 206(1)) is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

(4) A statutory instrument containing—

- (a) an order under section 27(1),
- (b)[^key-a179922b443ba51013a5938e1a5f47c5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) an order under section 92(1),
- (d) an order under section 204(1) containing provisions which modify any enactment (including this Act), or
- (e) an order under section 205(1) containing provisions which add to, replace or omit any part of the text of an Act,

is not to be made unless a draft of the instrument containing the order has been laid before, and approved by resolution of, the Parliament.
