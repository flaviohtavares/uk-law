## 159. Exemptions from disclosure[^key-7853ac16171ee37b13579debe62a9432]

*Extent: S*

Information must not be disclosed by virtue of this Part to the extent that it is material the disclosure of which is prohibited by section 56 of the Investigatory Powers Act 2016[^key-a5f81cfc558c7458e2f522b0f590d3ea] .
