# Criminal Justice and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2010, section 51

## 51. Abolition of offences of sedition and leasing-making[^key-4a2bf1a18885edeccbfc5c6c3a266bff]

*Extent: S*

The following offences under the common law of Scotland are abolished—

- (a) the offence of sedition,
- (b) the offence of leasing-making.
