## 181. Premises licence applications: modification of layout plans[^c-651d162b2199e1cdf7cd62974eccd1ae]

*Extent: S*

In section 23 of the 2005 Act (determination of premises licence application), in subsection (7)(b), after “plan” insert “ or layout plan (or both) ”.
