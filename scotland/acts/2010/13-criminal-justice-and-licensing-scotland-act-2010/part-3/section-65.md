## 65. Meaning of “organisation”[^key-1cbf60b331339f3f7c8cf863ed7efc39]

*Extent: S*

In section 307(1) of the 1995 Act (interpretation), after the definition of “order for lifelong restriction”, insert—

> “**organisation**” means—
>
> - (a) a body corporate;
> - (b) an unincorporated association;
> - (c) a partnership;
> - (d) a body of trustees;
> - (e) a government department;
> - (f) a part of the Scottish Administration;
> - (g) any other entity which is not an individual;

.
