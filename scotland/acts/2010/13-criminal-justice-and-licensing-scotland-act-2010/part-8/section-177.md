# Criminal Justice and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2010, section 177

## 177. Licensing of late night catering[^c-7012ba143346ae158a0f7987a1beff0e]

*Extent: S*

(1) Section 42 of the 1982 Act (late hours catering) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsections (1) and (2), for “meals or refreshment” in each place where those words occur substitute “ food ”.

(3) In subsection (2), for “they are” substitute “ it is ”.

(4) In subsection (3), for “meals or refreshments” in both places where those words occur substitute “ food ”.

(5) After subsection (6), add—

> (7) In this section “**food**” has the meaning given in section 1 of the Food Safety Act 1990 (c.16).

.
