# Crofting Reform (Scotland) Act 2010, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (82).** Text as at 2013-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2010/14
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1. The Crofting Commission

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-dcd59533a3e7aa273055d40014176c32] The Crofters Commission is renamed and is to be known as the Crofting Commission.

(2)[^key-dcd59533a3e7aa273055d40014176c32] The “Crofters Commission” is the Commission—

- (a) established by section 1 of the Crofters (Scotland) Act 1955 (c.21); and
- (b) continued in being by section 1 of the Crofters (Scotland) Act 1993 (c.44) (the “**1993 Act**”).

(3)[^key-69f6315d7607d827f89fb4c36dea3285][^key-148e43db3585430df413fb1794a81a47] For schedule 1 to the 1993 Act, substitute the schedule contained in schedule 1.

(4)[^key-dcd59533a3e7aa273055d40014176c32] Any reference in any enactment to the Crofters Commission is, unless the contrary intention appears, to be construed as a reference to the Crofting Commission.
