# Marine (Scotland) Act 2010, section 24

> Text as at 2026-10-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 24. Pre-application consultation report[^c-ef99528af2aba60aa6b1cf0bf0dad3ab]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person who, before submitting an application for a marine licence, was required to comply with section 23 and who proceeds to submit that application must prepare a report as to what has been done to effect such compliance.

(2) A report under subsection (1) is to be in such form as the Scottish Ministers may by regulations prescribe.
