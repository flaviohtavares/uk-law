## 41. Defences: electronic communications: emergency works[^key-ec1e4889caa5bf5f59e1e9f7e87a1f34][^M_C_ccd80625-7652-4e9b-e138-b93bc090e693]

*Extent: S*

(1) It is a defence for a person charged with an offence under section 39(1) in relation to any activity to prove that—

- (a) for the purposes of Part 10 of Schedule 3A to the Communications Act 2003 (the electronic communications code)[^key-c2304957ff452f24c7aa837e2a7905f4] (undertaker's works), the person is the operator or a relevant undertaker, and
- (b) the activity was carried out for the purpose of executing emergency works, within the meaning of that code.
