## 168. Commencement and short title

*Extent: S*

(1) The provisions of this Act, except this section and sections 1, 2, 19, 64, 65, 66, 106, 133, 157, 165 and 166, come into force on such day as the Scottish Ministers may by order appoint.

(2) This Act may be cited as the Marine (Scotland) Act 2010.
