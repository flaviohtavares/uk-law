## 72. Demonstration and Research MPAs: further provision[^c-8ad072997820c3587ed62e0cb6aa81eb]

*Extent: S*

(1) A designation order designating an area as a Demonstration and Research MPA—

- (a) must identify the area's boundaries,
- (b) may provide for a boundary to be determined by, or by reference to, mean high water spring tide.

(2) A Demonstration and Research MPA may include (in addition to an area of sea referred to in section 67(1)) an area of seashore lying above mean high water spring tide if—

- (a) the area of seashore adjoins the area of sea, and
- (b) the inclusion of the area of seashore is necessary to further or support the purpose for which the area of sea is designated.
