## 124. Obstructing an authorised person[^c-24009b4b22de2514b4b87eb598180cd5]

*Extent: S*

Preventing or obstructing a person from doing anything which the person is authorised to do under section 120 [^c-34d8bf6c58a6b3748df5646ffa97b97c]... (intentionally or recklessly) is an offence.
