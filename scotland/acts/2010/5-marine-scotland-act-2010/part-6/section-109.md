# Marine (Scotland) Act 2010, section 109

> Text as at 2026-10-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 109. Exceptions: licensed activity[^c-2f96a6611f8c1a55c66313a986ee7d1d]

*Extent: S*

It is not an offence under section 107—

- (a) to kill or take a seal in accordance with a seal licence,
- (b) to take a seal in order to, or to injure a seal when attempting to, kill it in accordance with a seal licence,
- (c) to kill or injure a seal when attempting to take it in accordance with a seal licence, or
- (d) to do anything in accordance with a licence granted under regulation 44 of the Conservation (Natural Habitats, &c.) Regulations.
