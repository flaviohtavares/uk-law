## 117. Offence: harassment at haul-out sites[^c-bf6cc23198a7eed83de6e1ea3c7d2809]

*Extent: S*

Harassing a seal (intentionally or recklessly) at a haul-out site is an offence.

“**haul-out site**” means any place which the Scottish Ministers, after consulting United Kingdom Research and Innovation[^c-24ddbfa1aa2cc2cdad7e164e1e4e0b16], by order designate as such for the purposes of this section.
