> Text as at 2026-10-26.

## 107. Offence: killing, injuring or taking seals[^c-7fdd4a3a39f97fe243bac5d2da8049d2]

*Extent: S*

Killing, injuring or taking a live seal (intentionally or recklessly) is an offence.
