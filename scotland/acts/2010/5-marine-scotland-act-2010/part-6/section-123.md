## 123. Duty to produce authority[^c-5ebbb2fb1d88d35293d626665291e697]

*Extent: S*

A person doing anything authorised under section 120 [^c-07a75bb32b0c9edcac9aa7cfce0e9050]... must produce evidence of the person's authority if asked to do so.
