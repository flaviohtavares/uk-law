# Marine (Scotland) Act 2010, section 131

> Text as at 2026-10-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 131. Enforcement of marine licensing regime[^c-3f398ec90e386e527b841e56bc67c30e]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-b2e15ea0f97e88721a959a88f0feea21] For the purposes of enforcing Part 4 a marine enforcement officer has—

- (a) the common enforcement powers conferred by this Act,
- (b) the power conferred by section 150 (to require information relating to certain substances and objects).

(2)[^c-b2e15ea0f97e88721a959a88f0feea21] Subject to subsection (3), the powers which a marine enforcement officer has for the purposes of enforcing Part 4 may be exercised in the Scottish marine area and in any other part of Scotland.

(3)[^c-b2e15ea0f97e88721a959a88f0feea21] Those powers may not be exercised in relation to any British warship.

(4)[^c-ddf09f89a05a0f0256cc548925737517] In this section and section 132, “**British warship**” means a ship belonging to Her Majesty and forming part of Her Majesty's armed forces.
