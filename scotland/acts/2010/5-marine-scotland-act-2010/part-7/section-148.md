## 148. Assistance, etc.[^c-014e9c7b1254dd4ab97d50f5cc5e6d4c]

*Extent: S*

(1) To assist in carrying out any relevant functions, a marine enforcement officer may bring—

- (a) any other person,
- (b) any equipment or materials.

(2) A person who is brought by a marine enforcement officer to provide assistance may exercise any powers conferred by this Act which the officer may exercise, but only under the supervision or direction of the officer.
