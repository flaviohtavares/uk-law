# Legal Services (Scotland) Act 2010, section 101

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 101. Will writers and services

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) For the purposes of this Part, will writing services are services that are—

- (a) described in subsection (2), and
- (b) provided (or offered)—
  - (i) to members of the public, and
  - (ii) for a fee, gain or reward.

(2) The services are those of drawing or preparing wills or other testamentary writings.

(3) For the purposes of this Part, a will writer is a person on whom, in accordance with an approving body's regulatory scheme, the right to provide will writing services is conferred.
