## 74. Pretending to be licensed[^key-a80489aed30082e68eb86cc129be267d]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person commits an offence if the person—

- (a) pretends to be a licensed provider, or
- (b) takes or uses any name, title, addition or description implying falsely that the person is a licensed provider.

(2) A person who commits an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
