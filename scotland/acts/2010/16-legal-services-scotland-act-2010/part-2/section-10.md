# Legal Services (Scotland) Act 2010, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (68).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2010/16
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 10. Authorisation to act

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-cb48f71fc0e5861ebba4c604ec5e2457] An approved regulator may not exercise any of its regulatory functions unless it is authorised to do so by the Scottish Ministers under this section.

(2)[^key-9bbba3e2eaf8b7be942cd0c156e05048][^key-ddc30c5a7ad5b9c6e39e12c61149d8d7] The Scottish Ministers may give their authorisation if they are satisfied (or continue to be satisfied)—

- (a) as mentioned in subsection (1) of section 7,
- (b) as regards any criteria provided for under subsection (5)(b) of that section.

(3)[^key-9bbba3e2eaf8b7be942cd0c156e05048][^key-ddc30c5a7ad5b9c6e39e12c61149d8d7] Their authorisation may be given with restrictions imposed by reference to particular categories of—

- (a) licensed provider,
- (b) legal services.

(4)[^key-9bbba3e2eaf8b7be942cd0c156e05048][^key-ddc30c5a7ad5b9c6e39e12c61149d8d7] Their authorisation may be given—

- (a) either—
  - (i) without limit of time, or
  - (ii) for a fixed period of at least 3 years,
- (b) subject to conditions.

(5)[^key-9bbba3e2eaf8b7be942cd0c156e05048][^key-ddc30c5a7ad5b9c6e39e12c61149d8d7] The Scottish Ministers may, after consulting the approved regulator, vary (including by addition or deletion) any restrictions or conditions imposed under subsection (3) or (4)(b).

(6)[^key-9bbba3e2eaf8b7be942cd0c156e05048][^key-ddc30c5a7ad5b9c6e39e12c61149d8d7] The Scottish Ministers may by regulations make further provision about authorisation under this section including (in particular) the process for requests for their authorisation.
