## 17. Licensing appeals[^c-6725d9715ea814694b212b09b7f82d70]

*Extent: S*

(1) An applicant for a licence or (as the case may be) a licensed provider may appeal against a relevant licensing decision taken by virtue of this Part—

- (a) to the sheriff,
- (b) within the period of 3 months beginning with the date on which that decision is intimated to it.

(2) A relevant licensing decision is a decision to—

- (a) refuse the licensed provider's application for—
  - (i) a licence, or
  - (ii) renewal of its licence,
- (b) attach conditions or restrictions to its licence, or
- (c) revoke or suspend its licence.
