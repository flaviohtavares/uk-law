## 85. Privileged material[^key-edf5b4068df88e0fde2b0c236b70be92]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subsection (2) applies to the publication under this Part of any—

- (a) advice, report or notice, or
- (b) other material.

(2) For the purposes of the law on defamation, the publication is privileged.

(3) But subsection (2) is ineffective if it is proved that the publication was made with malice.
