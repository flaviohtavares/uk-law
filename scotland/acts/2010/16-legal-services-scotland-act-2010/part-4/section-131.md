## 131. Acting as approved regulator[^c-d50d6b49545252e6592c4bcc4e7805e3]

*Extent: S*

After section 1 of the 1980 Act insert—

> **1A. Power to act as statutory regulator**
>
> The Society may—
>
> - (a) act as an approved regulator within the meaning of Part 2 of the 2010 Act,
> - (b) do anything that is necessary or expedient for the purposes of doing so.
