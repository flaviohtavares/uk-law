# Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2010/8

> Extent: Scotland.

## 27. Explanatory document laid before the Scottish Parliament[^key-50b99ed5f4700fd210fe9c9fe3d738f9][^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

*Extent: S*

(1) The explanatory document referred to in section 25(2)(b)(ii) must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) explain under which power (or powers) in this Part the provision contained in the draft order is made, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) introduce and give reasons for the provision, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (c) in the case of an order under section 14— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (i) explain why the Scottish Ministers consider that the conditions in section 16(2) (where relevant) are satisfied or the condition in section 16(10) is satisfied, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] explain how the provision made by the order would improve the exercise of public functions, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] if the order relates to the functions of the Scottish Ministers, or confers functions on or transfers or delegates functions to, the Scottish Ministers, describe the functions and identify the part of the Scottish Administration through which the functions are, or are to be, exercised, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (d) in the case of an order under section 17(1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (i) explain why the Scottish Ministers consider that the conditions in section 18(2) (where relevant) are satisfied or the condition in section 18(8) is satisfied, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] include, so far as appropriate, an assessment of the extent to which the provision made by the order would remove or reduce any burden or burdens (within the meaning of that section), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (e) identify and give reasons for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (i) any functions of legislating conferred by the order, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] the procedural requirements attaching to the exercise of those functions, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (f) give details of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (i) any consultation undertaken under section 26, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] any representations received as a result of the consultation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] the changes (if any) made to the proposed draft order as a result of those representations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(2) Where a person making representations in response to consultation under section 26 has requested the Scottish Ministers not to disclose them, the Scottish Ministers must not disclose them under subsection (1)(f)(ii) if or to the extent that to do so would (disregarding any connection with proceedings in the Scottish Parliament) constitute a breach of confidence actionable by any person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(3) If information in representations made by a person in response to consultation under section 26 relates to another person, the Scottish Ministers need not disclose the information under subsection (1)(f)(ii) if or to the extent that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) it appears to the Scottish Ministers that the disclosure of that information could adversely affect the interests of that other person, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) the Scottish Ministers have been unable to obtain the consent of that other person to the disclosure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(4) Subsections (2) and (3) do not affect any disclosure that is requested by, and made to, a committee of the Parliament charged with reporting on the draft order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
