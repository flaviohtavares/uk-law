# Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010, section 20

## 20. Subordinate legislation and powers of direction, appointment and consent[^c-3c7dcfe53d8e9830e81eab86cd02dfad][^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

*Extent: S*

(1) An order under section 14 or 17(1) may confer or transfer a function of legislating only on or to the Scottish Ministers, the First Minister or the Lord Advocate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(2) An order under section 14 or 17(1) may not make provision for the delegation of any function of legislating. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(3) An order under section 14 or 17(1) may not make provision to confer a function of legislating on the Scottish Ministers, the First Minister or the Lord Advocate unless the conditions in subsections (4) and (5) are satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(4) The condition in this subsection is that the function is exercisable by statutory instrument. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(5) The condition in this subsection is that such a statutory instrument— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of the Scottish Parliament, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) is not to be made unless a draft of the statutory instrument has been laid before and approved by a resolution of the Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(6) Subsections (1) to (3) do not apply to provision which merely restates an enactment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(7) An order under section 14 or 17(1) may not make provision which has the effect of transferring to a person other than the Scottish Ministers, the First Minister or the Lord Advocate any function to which subsection (8) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(8) This subsection applies to any function of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) giving directions, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) appointing a person to any office or position, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (c) consenting to any thing, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

conferred by any enactment on the Scottish Ministers, the First Minister or the Lord Advocate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
