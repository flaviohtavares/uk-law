# Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010, section 26

## 26. Consultation[^c-1426853df53844bc3a034f8de707cbe6][^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

*Extent: S*

(1) If the Scottish Ministers propose to make an order under section 14 or 17(1) they must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) consult such organisations as appear to them to be representative of interests substantially affected by the proposals, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) where the proposals relate to the functions of one or more persons, bodies or office-holders, consult those persons, bodies or office-holders, or persons appearing to them to be representative of those persons, bodies or office-holders, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (c) in such cases as they consider appropriate, consult the Scottish Law Commission, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (d) consult such other persons as they consider appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(2) For the purposes of any consultation required by subsection (1), the Scottish Ministers must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) lay before the Parliament— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (i) a copy of the proposed draft order, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] a copy of the proposed explanatory document referred to in section 25(2)(b)(ii) (excepting the details required by section 27(1)(f)), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) send a copy of the proposed draft order and proposed explanatory document to any person to be consulted under subsection (1), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (c) have regard to any representations about the proposed draft order that are made to them within 60 days of the date on which the copy of the proposed draft order is laid before the Parliament under paragraph (a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(3) In calculating any period of 60 days for the purposes of subsection (2)(c), no account is to be taken of any time during which the Parliament is dissolved or is in recess for more than 4 days. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(4) If, as a result of any consultation required by subsection (1), it appears to the Scottish Ministers that it is appropriate to change the whole or any part of their proposals, they must undertake such further consultation with respect to the changes as they consider appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(5) If, before the day on which this section comes into force, any consultation was undertaken which, had it been undertaken after that day, would to any extent have satisfied the requirements of this section, those requirements are to that extent to be taken to have been satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
