# Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2010/8

## 14. Public functions: efficiency, effectiveness and economy[^key-69f9400efbf0d80bbc4ddaecaebbc12a][^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may by order make any provision which they consider would improve the exercise of public functions, having regard to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) efficiency, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) effectiveness, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (c) economy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(2) In subsection (1), “public functions” are functions of the persons, bodies and office-holders listed in schedule 5, subject to any limitations specified in that schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(3) The provision that may be made under subsection (1) includes provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) modifying, conferring, abolishing, transferring, or providing for the delegation of, any function, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) amending the constitution of a person, body or office-holder listed in schedule 5 other than— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (i) the Scottish Ministers, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] the Scottish Court Service, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] a cross-border public authority, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] a person listed by virtue of section 15(5)(e), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (v) a company (within the meaning of the Companies Act 2006 (c. 46)), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (c) creating— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (i) a person, body or office-holder on which functions are conferred, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] a person, body or office-holder to which functions (modified or otherwise) are transferred or may be delegated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(4) For the purposes of subsection (3)(b)(ii), the constitution of the Scottish Court Service is as set out in schedule 3 to the Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008 (asp 6). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(5) The transfer or delegation referred to in subsection (3)(a) must be a transfer or delegation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) a person, body or office-holder listed in schedule 5, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) a person, body or office-holder created in pursuance of subsection (3)(c), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (c) a local authority (meaning a council constituted by section 2 of the Local Government *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] etc. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] (Scotland) Act 1994 (c. 39)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(6) An order under this section containing provision creating a person, body or office-holder in pursuance of subsection (3)(c)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) must include provision adding that person, body or office-holder to schedule 5, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) may include provision adding that person, body or office-holder to schedule 6, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (c) may specify the extent to which any functions are to be public functions for the purposes of subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(7) An order under this section may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) modify any enactment, instrument or other document, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) contain such consequential, incidental, transitional, transitory or saving provision as the Scottish Ministers consider appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(8) An order under this section may include provision dissolving any person, body or office-holder listed in schedule 5, other than those listed in subsection (3)(b)(i) to (v), but only if the person, body or office-holder has, or will have by virtue of the order, no exercisable functions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(9) An order under this section may bind the Crown. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(10) An order under this section must be made in accordance with this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
