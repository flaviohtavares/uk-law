# Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010, section 16

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2010/8
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 16. Preconditions[^key-9f4dea24b7baa12f33f3174d1877ab22][^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may not make provision under section 14, other than provision which merely restates an enactment, unless they consider that the conditions in subsection (2), where relevant, are satisfied in relation to that provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(2) Those conditions are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) the effect of the provision is proportionate to the policy objective, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) the provision does not remove any necessary protection, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (c) any public function which is to be modified will, as modified, be broadly consistent with the general objects or purpose of the person, body or office-holder concerned, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (d) any function which is conferred on a person, body or office-holder listed in schedule 5 (other than a function being transferred without substantial modification from another such person, body or office-holder) is broadly consistent with the general objects or purpose of the person, body or office-holder concerned, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (e) any function which is conferred on a person, body or office-holder created by virtue of section 14 is broadly consistent with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (i) the general objects or purpose of a person, body or office-holder listed in schedule 5 which is abolished, or whose functions are modified, by virtue of section 14 or otherwise, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] public functions abolished or modified by virtue of that section or otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(3) Examples of protections for the purposes of subsection (2)(b) are (without prejudice to the generality of that provision) protections in relation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) the independence of judicial decision-making, or decision-making of a judicial nature, by a person occupying a judicial office, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) civil liberties, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (c) health and safety of persons, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (d) the environment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (e) cultural heritage (including access, through display, exhibition or otherwise, to cultural heritage). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(4) For the purposes of subsection (2)(b), the continued independence of the judiciary as mentioned in section 1 of the Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008 (asp 6) is a necessary protection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(5) For the purposes of subsection (2)(b), the holding, care or preservation of property which is cultural heritage by persons separate from the Scottish Ministers and any statutory restrictions on the disposal of such property are necessary protections where the property is vested in such persons as trustees for the public under statute. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(6) For the purposes of subsection (2)(b), the provision in paragraph 7(4) of schedule 2 to the Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 13) as to who the convener shall be in relation to certain proceedings before the Mental Health Tribunal for Scotland is a necessary protection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(7) For the purposes of subsection (2)(b), a provision is not to be treated as removing a necessary protection if provision is made that delivers the same or similar protection in an alternative manner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(8) For the purposes of the application of subsection (2)(c), (d) and (e) to functions exercised, or to be exercised, by the Scottish Ministers, references to their general objects or purpose are to the broad remit of the part of the Scottish Administration through which the functions are, or are to be, exercised. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(9) For the purposes of subsection (2)(d), a modification of a function being transferred is not to be treated as substantial if it is necessary to enable the effective exercise of the function by the person, body or office-holder to which it is transferred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(10) The Scottish Ministers may not make provision under section 14 which merely restates an enactment unless they consider that the provision made would make the law more accessible or more easily understood. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(11) In subsection (3)(a) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]**judicial office *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) the office of judge of any court, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) the office of member of any tribunal, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (c) any other office, or appointment, consisting of functions of a judicial nature. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
