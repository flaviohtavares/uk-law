# Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010, section 30

## 30. Interpretation of Part 2[^key-a3fd6581e6791f222b50c58ffd747ed6][^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

*Extent: S*

In this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- “**cultural heritage *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]**” includes objects, structures and other things (in whatever form) resulting from human activity of all periods, traditions, ways of life and the historic, scientific, artistic and literary associations of people, places and landscapes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- “**restate an enactment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]**” means to replace it with alterations only of form or arrangement (and for these purposes to remove an ambiguity is to make an alteration other than one of form or arrangement); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- “function of legislating” is a function of legislating by order, rules, regulations or other subordinate instrument. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
