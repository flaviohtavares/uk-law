# Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2010/8

## 15. Public functions: further provision[^c-53c207ea2420d225a3d37a48531e2f5c][^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

*Extent: S*

(1) Schedule 5, which lists persons, bodies and office-holders for the purposes of section 14, has effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(2) The Scottish Ministers may by order modify schedule 5 by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) adding an entry for any person, body or office-holder falling within subsection (5), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) removing any entry. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(3) An order under subsection (2)(a) containing provision adding an entry to schedule 5 may include provision adding a corresponding entry to schedule 6. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(4) An order under subsection (2)(b) containing provision removing an entry from schedule 5 must include provision removing any corresponding entry from schedule 6. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(5) Those persons, bodies and office-holders are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) an office-holder in the Scottish Administration, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) a Scottish public authority with mixed functions or no reserved functions, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (c) a cross-border public authority, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (d) a publicly-owned company, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (e) any other person, not being a public body or the holder of a public office, who either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (i) appears to the Scottish Ministers to exercise functions of a public nature, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] is providing, under a contract made with a person, body or office-holder listed in schedule 5, any service the provision of which is the function of that person, body or office-holder. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(6) A company is publicly-owned for the purposes of subsection (5)(d) if it is wholly owned— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) by the Scottish Ministers, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) by any other person, body or office-holder listed in schedule 5 except a person, body or office-holder listed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (i) by virtue of subsection (5)(e)(i) in relation only to some of its functions, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] by virtue of subsection (5)(e)(ii). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(7) For the purposes of subsection (6) a company is wholly owned— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) by the Scottish Ministers if it has no members except— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (i) the Scottish Ministers or companies wholly owned by the Scottish Ministers, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] persons acting on behalf of the Scottish Ministers or of such companies, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) by any other person, body or office-holder if it has no members except— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (i) the person, body or office-holder or companies wholly owned by the person, body or office-holder, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] persons acting on behalf of the person, body or office-holder or of such companies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(8) An entry added to schedule 5 by an order under subsection (2)(a) made by virtue of subsection (5)(e) must specify the functions of a public nature or, as the case may be, the service being provided; and only those functions or that service are public functions of the person in question for the purposes of section 14(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(9) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- “**company *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]**” includes any body corporate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- “**local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]**” means a council constituted by section 2 of the Local Government *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] etc. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] (Scotland) Act 1994 (c. 39); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- “**Scottish public authority with mixed functions or no reserved functions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]**” is to be construed in accordance with paragraphs 1(4) and 2 of Part 3 of Schedule 5 to the Scotland Act 1998 (c. 46); but does not include a local authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
