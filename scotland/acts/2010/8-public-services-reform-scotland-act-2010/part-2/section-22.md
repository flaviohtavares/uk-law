## 22. Criminal penalties[^c-93f62bf4b562c190c2d1a07a2b1997af][^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

*Extent: S*

(1) An order under section 14 or 17(1) may not make provision to create a new offence that is punishable, or increase the penalty for an existing offence so that it is punishable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) on indictment, with imprisonment for a term exceeding two years, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) on summary conviction, with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (i) imprisonment for a term exceeding 12 months, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a] a fine exceeding level 5 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(2) In the case of an offence which is triable either on indictment or summarily and is not an offence triable on indictment only by virtue of section 292(6) and (7) of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995 (c. 46), the reference in subsection (1)(b)(ii) to a fine exceeding level 5 on the standard scale is to be construed as a reference to the statutory maximum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(3) Subsection (1) does not apply to provision which merely restates an enactment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
