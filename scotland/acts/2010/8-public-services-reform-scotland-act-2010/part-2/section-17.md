# Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010, section 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2010/8
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 17. Power to remove or reduce burdens[^c-c14babaf7387e0fe0f1fe23994d64827][^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may by order make any provision which they consider would remove or reduce any burden, or the overall burdens, resulting directly or indirectly for any person from any legislation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(2) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]**burden *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]**” means any of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) a financial cost, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) an administrative inconvenience, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (c) an obstacle to best regulatory practice, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (d) an obstacle to efficiency, productivity or profitability, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (e) a sanction, criminal or otherwise, which affects the carrying on of any lawful activity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(3) For the purposes of subsection (1), a financial cost or administrative inconvenience may result from the form of any legislation (for example, where the legislation is hard to understand). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(4) For the purposes of subsection (2)(c), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]**best regulatory practice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]**” means practice under which (in particular) regulatory activities should be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) carried out in a way that is transparent, accountable, proportionate and consistent, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) targeted only at such cases as require action. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(5) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]**legislation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]**” means any of the following or a provision of any of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) a public general or local Act of Parliament (whenever passed) or an Act of the Scottish Parliament (whenever passed), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) any Order in Council, order, rules, regulations, scheme, warrant, byelaw or other subordinate instrument made at any time under an Act referred to in paragraph (a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(6) The provision that may be made under subsection (1) includes provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) abolishing, conferring or transferring, or providing for the delegation of, functions of any description, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) creating a body or office. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(7) An order under this section may not amend the constitution of the Scottish Court Service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(8) For the purposes of subsection (7), the constitution of the Scottish Court Service is as set out in schedule 3 to the Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(9) An order under this section may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

- (a) modify any enactment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
- (b) contain such consequential, incidental, transitional, transitory or saving provision as the Scottish Ministers consider appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(10) An order under this section may include provision dissolving any body or office, other than those listed in section 14(3)(b)(i) to (v), but only if the body or office has, or will have by virtue of the order, no exercisable functions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(11) An order under this section may bind the Crown. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]

(12) An order under this section must be made in accordance with this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4e0bdd7f3adfeaa1ef22a305a56bcb9a]
