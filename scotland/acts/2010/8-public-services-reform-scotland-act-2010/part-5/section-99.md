# Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010, section 99

## 99. Arrangements entered into by local authority or health body: services to be registered[^key-49e92482d3737f458a4978ebbf3b409a][^key-f7fa9c6ba148aaad9ca12ba5d2c7b83e]

*Extent: S*

Where, in the performance of its functions—

- (a) a local authority, or
- (b) a health body,

makes arrangements with any person to provide a care service, it must ensure that the service, when provided, is registered under Chapter 3.
