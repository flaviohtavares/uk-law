## SCHEDULE 7 (affected by repeal, see note) — Order-making powers: modifications of enactments (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb][^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]

### Deregulation and Contracting Out Act 1994 (c. 40) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]

**1.**[^c-a104ada0fabdc06cdd71f453415714b2] (1) The Deregulation and Contracting Out Act 1994 is amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]

(2) Sections 1 to 5 and Schedule 1 are repealed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]

(3) In section 6 (model provisions with respect to appeals), in subsection (7)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]

- (a) in the definition of “enactment”— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]
  - (i) the word “and” is omitted, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb] at the end insert *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb] “ , an enactment contained in an Act of the Scottish Parliament (whenever passed) and an enactment contained in an instrument made under an Act of the Scottish Parliament (whenever made) ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb],
- (b) for the definition of “enforcement action” substitute— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]

  > “**enforcement action *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]
  >
  > - (a) in relation to any restriction, requirement or condition, any action taken with a view to or in connection with imposing any sanction (whether criminal or otherwise) for failure to observe or comply with it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]
  > - (b) in relation to a restriction, requirement or condition relating to the grant or renewal of licences, includes any refusal to grant, renew or vary a licence, the imposition of any condition on the grant or renewal of a licence and any variation or revocation of a licence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]

  ,
- (c) for the definition of “interested person” substitute— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]

  > “**interested person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]
  >
  > - (a) the person against whom enforcement action may be or has been taken; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]
  > - (b) any other person who will or may be required to meet, or to make a significant contribution towards, the cost of observing the restriction or complying with the requirement or condition; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]
  > - (c) where the enforcement action which may be or has been taken relates specifically to goods or services which are to be or have been supplied by a person other than the one against whom enforcement action may be or has been taken, that person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]

  .

(4) Sub-paragraph (2) does not affect the continuation in force of any order under section 1 of that Act which was made on or before the day on which that sub-paragraph comes into force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]

### Criminal Procedure (Consequential Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1995 (c. 40) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]

**2.**[^c-a637472b2c1f627a7dfaae233679bb43] In Schedule 4 to the Criminal Procedure (Consequential Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1995 (minor and consequential amendments), paragraph 96 is repealed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3b49e85359ae0785802498e7a148f5bb]
