# Housing (Scotland) Act 2001, section 31

## 31. Effect of work on rent[^c484768]

*Extent: S*

In assessing the rent to be payable under a Scottish secure tenancy by—

- (a) a tenant who has carried out work on the house,
- (b) a person who has succeeded that tenant in the tenancy, or
- (c) the spouse or civil partner[^key-a16195943e3e47668faa0df98e9cd4a7] of a person mentioned in paragraph (b) or a person living with that person as husband and wife or in a relationship which has the characteristics of the relationship between civil partners[^key-f085b2303601e0b46ceca70403771b3c] ,

no account is to be taken at any time of any improvement in the value or amenities of the house resulting from the work.
