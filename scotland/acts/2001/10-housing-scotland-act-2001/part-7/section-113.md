## 113. Commencement and short title

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c286678] Except for sections 109 and 110 and this section, the provisions of this Act come into force on such day as the Scottish Ministers may by order appoint.

(2)[^c286678] Different days may be appointed under this section for different purposes or different areas.

(3) This Act may be cited as the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001.
