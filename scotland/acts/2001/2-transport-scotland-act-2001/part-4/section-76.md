## 76. Amendment of Road Traffic Act 1988

*Extent: S*

In section 40 of the Road Traffic Act 1988 (c.52) (power to subsidise certain authorities and bodies for giving road safety information and training)—

- (a) the words “authorities or bodies other than” are repealed; and
- (b) at the end there shall be inserted “ or by other authorities or bodies ”.
