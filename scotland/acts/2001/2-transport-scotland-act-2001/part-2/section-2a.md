## 2A. Provision of bus services etc. by local transport authorities[^key-69f956bc16989fd5b2d4800e482b7b4b][^key-94740ad0c4ccd3877b3580ddf47ca7eb]

*Extent: S*

(1) A local transport authority may provide a service for the carriage of passengers by road using vehicles that require a PSV operator's licence to do so.

(2) The local transport authority must be satisfied that the provision of such a service will contribute to the implementation of their relevant general policies.
