## 3E. Partnership schemes: Scottish Ministers' traffic regulation orders[^c-7fab356574d73010f23c1ed4fcc76d0a][^c-9c57e144c310e4e8000f0a0f750e3a2d]

*Extent: S*

(1) This section applies if, in relation to a proposed partnership scheme or the proposed variation of an existing scheme, the provision of a facility or the taking of a measure requires the making of a traffic regulation order in respect of a road for which the Scottish Ministers are the traffic authority (within the meaning of section 121A of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984).

(2) The partnership scheme may not be made, postponed, varied or revoked unless it is made, postponed, varied or revoked by the local transport authority and the Scottish Ministers acting jointly.
