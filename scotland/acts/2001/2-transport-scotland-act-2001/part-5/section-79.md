# Transport (Scotland) Act 2001, section 79

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (29).** Text as at 2025-10-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2001/2

## 79. Guidance

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may issue guidance to—

- (a) local traffic authorities in relation to charging schemes;
- (b) local traffic authorities in relation to the designation of roads as home zones by virtue of section 74(1) of this Act;
- (ba)[^c-378aaa8391e796a5f63b9fbb1ecd34fa] local transport authorities in relation to the exercise of their functions under section 2A of this Act,
- (c) local transport authorities in relation to partnership schemes[^c-d6a07a3ab983a0888b1516cac20ec325], franchising frameworks[^c-5aa4b071b049871948908afe3230a350], ticketing schemes and the provision of information under section 34 of this Act; and
- (d) local transport authorities and local traffic authorities (or any one or more local traffic authorities)[^c-20c132c33728ff3cc3ce31ec6495ec2a] in relation to the preparation of local transport strategies;

and such authorities shall have regard to any such guidance.

(2) Guidance issued under this section or any other section[^c-b65c1e42f260174997439478ed8ce313] shall be published in such manner as the Scottish Ministers consider appropriate; and the Scottish Ministers may at any time vary or revoke guidance issued by them under this section.
