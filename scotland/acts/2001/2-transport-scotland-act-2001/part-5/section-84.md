## 84. Short title and commencement

*Extent: S*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Transport (Scotland) Act 2001.

(2)[^c1033166] The provisions of this Act, other than this section, shall come into force on such day as the Scottish Ministers may by order appoint.

(3)[^c1033166] Different days may be so appointed for different purposes.
