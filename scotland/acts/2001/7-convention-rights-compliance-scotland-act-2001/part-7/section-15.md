# Convention Rights (Compliance) (Scotland) Act 2001, section 15

> Text as at 2010-12-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 15. Short title and commencement

*Extent: S*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Convention Rights (Compliance) (Scotland) Act 2001.

(2)[^c887660] Parts 1, 2 and 5 of this Act come into force on such day as the Scottish Ministers, by order made by statutory instrument, appoint, and different days may be so appointed for different purposes.

(3) The other provisions (except this section) of this Act come into force on the day after it receives Royal Assent.
