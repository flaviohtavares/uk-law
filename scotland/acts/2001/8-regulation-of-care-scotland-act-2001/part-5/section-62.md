## 62. Duty of Commission and Council to consult each other

*Extent: S*

The [^key-06de8702e3618021d7c0b1b82a4d6bf8]... Council shall [^key-062fed35b70e8d86ee4af0622647ca9f]..., in the exercise of its functions, consult Social Care and Social Work Improvement Scotland[^key-09b0ecbd9855e8bc33a243155a0c002c] in every case in which it appears to the Council[^key-6dab01ca338a4ba7683a0f8454a36329] appropriate that there should be such consultation.
