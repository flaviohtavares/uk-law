## 63. Guidance as to consultation

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers shall issue guidelines to [^key-06dff0cb92a6441c46e5f94d616bd43e]... the Council as to the fulfilment, by the Council[^key-e5d539180f0c92e4c77847ae61c41e67], of any requirement under this Act to consult any person or group of persons.
