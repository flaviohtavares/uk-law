# Regulation of Care (Scotland) Act 2001, section 60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2001/8

## 60. Grants

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may make grants to [^c-653eaf9252834aff497f9a2e6ac67a09]... the Council towards expenses incurred, or to be incurred, by it in connection with—

- (a) the initial establishment of the Council[^c-22105d3fe8d15027ca77771a91df59e1]; and
- (b) the discharge by the Council[^c-32e23691878842ef7083063bfc1bbb8e] of its functions.

(2) Any grant made under subsection (1) above may be made on such terms and subject to such conditions (including conditions as to repayment) as the Scottish Ministers think fit; and the Scottish Ministers may from time to time after the grant is made vary such terms and conditions.
