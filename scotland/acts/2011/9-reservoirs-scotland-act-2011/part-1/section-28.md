## 28. Appointment and removal of panel members

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-5f441ebe511c717e785f65fa4a83d6ac] A civil engineer who wishes to be appointed to a panel under section 27 must make an appropriate application.

(2)[^key-5f441ebe511c717e785f65fa4a83d6ac] An appointment is for such period as the Scottish Ministers determine.

(3)[^key-5f441ebe511c717e785f65fa4a83d6ac] A civil engineer appointed to a panel is eligible for re-appointment.

(4)[^key-5f441ebe511c717e785f65fa4a83d6ac] The Scottish Ministers may remove an engineer from a panel where they are satisfied that the engineer is not fit or qualified to remain on it.

(5)[^key-5f441ebe511c717e785f65fa4a83d6ac] The Scottish Ministers must give an engineer removed from a panel under subsection (4) notice of the removal.

(6)[^key-5f441ebe511c717e785f65fa4a83d6ac] Notice under subsection (5) must specify the grounds on which the engineer has been removed from the panel.

(7)[^key-8100ee9865dbe204446499f6e823cd44] In this section, “an appropriate application” is an application made in accordance with regulations made by the Scottish Ministers.

(8)[^key-8100ee9865dbe204446499f6e823cd44] The Scottish Ministers may by regulations make provision for the determining and charging of fees in connection with applications for membership of panels established by them under section 27.
