# Reservoirs (Scotland) Act 2011, section 27

> Text as at 2017-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 27. Panels of reservoir engineers

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers must—

- (a)[^c-cda853a61c33087ae79603a183c7d146][^c-fba9debef4d8bcc45b8b1f2c6e73f985] establish one or more panels of reservoir engineers for the purposes of this Part and specify by order the sections of this Part under which the members of any such panel may be appointed,
- (b)[^c-4f5ba91d0d5e9d1aedf394cde6caeadb] appoint civil engineers who they consider to be fit and qualified for appointment to such panels to be members of such of the panels as they consider appropriate.
