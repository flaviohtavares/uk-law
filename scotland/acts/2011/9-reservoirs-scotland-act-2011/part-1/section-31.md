# Reservoirs (Scotland) Act 2011, section 31

> Text as at 2017-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 31. Consultation with Institution of Civil Engineers

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers must consult the President of the Institution of Civil Engineers (or, if that Institution appoints a committee for the purpose, that committee), before—

- (a)[^key-b54d7775c2d70f22d3bc9ed43ca51823] establishing a panel under section 27,
- (b)[^key-a8a46b0c47408cfb96e9b6abb7b2d7ee] making an order under that section,
- (c)[^key-1701e3244b457a50a37b4ea2112993ae] making an appointment to a panel under that section,
- (d)[^key-1701e3244b457a50a37b4ea2112993ae] removing an engineer from a panel under section 28(4),
- (e)[^key-1701e3244b457a50a37b4ea2112993ae] dissolving a panel under section 29(1),
- (f)[^key-1701e3244b457a50a37b4ea2112993ae] directing that an engineer is no longer entitled to act under section 29(4),
- (g)[^key-1701e3244b457a50a37b4ea2112993ae] making a decision in a review under section 30.

(2)[^key-a8a46b0c47408cfb96e9b6abb7b2d7ee] The Scottish Ministers may reimburse the Institution of Civil Engineers for any expenses incurred by it by virtue of this section.
