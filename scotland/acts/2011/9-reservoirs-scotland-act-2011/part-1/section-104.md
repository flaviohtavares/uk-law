> Text as at 2017-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 104. SEPA: Ministerial directions[^c-d5e1ea577592253e3c78537377bb15e2]

*Extent: S*

SEPA must, in carrying out its functions under this Part, act subject to and in accordance with such directions as may be given by the Scottish Ministers.
