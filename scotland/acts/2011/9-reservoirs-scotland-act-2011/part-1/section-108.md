> Text as at 2017-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 108. Civil liability[^c-33095b05ffe5bbda8d05a5e60e6e4cf1]

*Extent: S*

This Part does not confer a right to claim damages in respect of a breach of an obligation imposed by the Part.
