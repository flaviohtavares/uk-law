## 89. Guidance: appeals[^key-9ba853b6b5553c00f15b9b7e124166a2][^key-c2493f04e7f7b73938a245344ecb579d]

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers must publish guidance on the process of making appeals in pursuance of sections 69, 74, 75[^key-1fffccdafcf6db19a4fdf2b87fd6813b]... 83, 85 and 88.
