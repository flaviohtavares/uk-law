# Reservoirs (Scotland) Act 2011, section 93

> Text as at 2017-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 93. Powers of entry: supplementary

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-f507dd27a9d6f987f787a709c38689e3] Where entry under section 91 is for a purpose in subsection (2)(k) of that section the right to enter extends to any neighbouring land.

(2)[^c-8c4d8cd1091221d5b847507a6c88d4e1] A right to enter land conferred by section 91 includes a right, subject in the case of a right exercisable in accordance with a warrant to the terms of the warrant, to—

- (a) take on to the land such other persons and such materials and equipment (including vehicles) as may be reasonably required for the purposes of assisting the person,
- (b) do anything else which is reasonably required in order to fulfil the purpose for which entry is taken.

(3)[^c-8c4d8cd1091221d5b847507a6c88d4e1] Except for a purpose referred to in section 91(2)(k), a person may not demand entry to land which is occupied unless either—

- (a) at least 7 days' notice has been given to the occupier, or
- (b) the entry is authorised by a warrant under section 92.

(4)[^c-8c4d8cd1091221d5b847507a6c88d4e1] Notice under subsection (3)(a) must—

- (a) specify the purpose for which entry is required,
- (b) specify so far as practicable the nature of the proposed works on the land.

(5)[^c-8c4d8cd1091221d5b847507a6c88d4e1] A person authorised by virtue of section 91 to enter land must on request produce written evidence of the authorisation.
