# Reservoirs (Scotland) Act 2011, section 51

> Text as at 2017-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 51. Recording of water levels etc. and record keeping

*Extent: S*

(1) The reservoir manager of a high-risk reservoir or medium-risk reservoir must maintain a record of the following matters in respect of the reservoir (“the recorded matters”) in accordance with this section—

- (a)[^c-529e3f4bc24bf74be8dfefc26fdf9774] water levels and depth of water in the reservoir, including the flow of water over any waste weir or overflow,
- (b)[^c-529e3f4bc24bf74be8dfefc26fdf9774] leakages,
- (c)[^c-529e3f4bc24bf74be8dfefc26fdf9774] repairs,
- (d)[^c-529e3f4bc24bf74be8dfefc26fdf9774] settlements of walls or other works,
- (e)[^c-9ba767b9d0e8d5ab2419956bbac5016a][^c-3463e39db428c3896cb37ec8b76c90bc] such other matters as the Scottish Ministers by regulations specify.

(2)[^c-da91a913a1ca9f0d4e59b7f85bf9d403] The Scottish Ministers may by regulations make provision as to—

- (a) the form of the record to be maintained,
- (b) the information to be included in relation to the recorded matters.

(3)[^c-529e3f4bc24bf74be8dfefc26fdf9774] A construction engineer, an inspecting engineer or a supervising engineer appointed in relation to the reservoir may give directions to the reservoir manager as to—

- (a) the manner in which the information referred to in subsection (2)(b) is to be recorded,
- (b) the intervals at which the record is to be updated.

(4)[^c-529e3f4bc24bf74be8dfefc26fdf9774] The reservoir manager must comply with any directions under subsection (3).

(5)[^c-529e3f4bc24bf74be8dfefc26fdf9774] The reservoir manager must install and maintain such instruments as may be necessary to provide the information to be recorded in relation to the recorded matters.

(6)[^c-529e3f4bc24bf74be8dfefc26fdf9774] Sections 56 and 58 make further provision about records to be maintained by the reservoir managers of controlled reservoirs.
