> Text as at 2017-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 107. Change to the Institution of Civil Engineers[^c-9dba76d86cf226c047d8ed90a451724c]

*Extent: S*

If the Institution of Civil Engineers ceases to exist, the Scottish Ministers may by order amend references in this Part to the Institution and to its President.
