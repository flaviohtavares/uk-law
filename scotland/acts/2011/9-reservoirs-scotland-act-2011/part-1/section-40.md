> Text as at 2017-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 40. Preliminary and final certificates: compliance[^c-18f27b51087467c58fc23847db3459ec]

*Extent: S*

The reservoir manager of a controlled reservoir must (subject to section 60(3)) comply with the requirements of any preliminary certificate or final certificate for the time being applicable to the reservoir.
