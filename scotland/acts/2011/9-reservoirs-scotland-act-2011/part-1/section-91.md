# Reservoirs (Scotland) Act 2011, section 91

> Text as at 2017-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 91. Powers of entry

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-b08e5aa9ca05c75503fd9530caae6c24] A person authorised by SEPA is entitled, at any reasonable time for any of the purposes in subsection (2), to enter—

- (a) land on which a controlled reservoir is situated,
- (b) land on which SEPA considers there is situated a structure or area (or combination) referred to in section 1(4) in relation to which SEPA is considering requesting the making an order under that subsection,
- (c) land on which a structure or area which previously at any time has been a controlled reservoir is situated.

(2) The purposes are to carry out an inspection, survey or other operation—

- (a)[^c-b08e5aa9ca05c75503fd9530caae6c24] to determine whether any provision of this Part applies,
- (b)[^c-a5f6ac1a4f891fb9600e1b934731986d] for the purpose of assisting SEPA in giving the reservoir a provisional risk designation or risk designation, or reviewing its risk designation,
- (c)[^c-784a94ce88d0cc1c066f9ef9ef303cc4] to determine whether a measure directed in a safety report has been taken,
- (d)[^c-784a94ce88d0cc1c066f9ef9ef303cc4] to determine whether the reservoir manager of a controlled reservoir is complying with the requirements of a preliminary certificate or final certificate,
- (e)[^c-784a94ce88d0cc1c066f9ef9ef303cc4] to determine—
  - (i) whether a measure directed in an inspection report has been taken (whether before or after the giving of a notice under section 69),
  - (ii) the period to be specified in a notice under that section,
- (f)[^c-784a94ce88d0cc1c066f9ef9ef303cc4] to determine whether a direction by a supervising engineer under section 50(4) has been complied with,
- (g)[^c-784a94ce88d0cc1c066f9ef9ef303cc4] to determine whether the records required by sections 51 and 56 are being maintained,
- (h)[^c-784a94ce88d0cc1c066f9ef9ef303cc4] to determine whether incidents are being reported in accordance with regulations under section 54(1),
- (i)[^c-784a94ce88d0cc1c066f9ef9ef303cc4] to determine whether a flood plan is being prepared in accordance with regulations under section 55(1),
- (j)[^c-784a94ce88d0cc1c066f9ef9ef303cc4] for the purposes of section 71,
- (k)[^c-784a94ce88d0cc1c066f9ef9ef303cc4] to determine what (if any) emergency measures should be taken under section 77, or for any purpose connected with taking such measures,
- (l)[^c-b08e5aa9ca05c75503fd9530caae6c24] to assess whether any offence under this Part may be being committed.
