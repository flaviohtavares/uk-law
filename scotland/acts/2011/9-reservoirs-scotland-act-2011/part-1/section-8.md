## 8. The 1975 Act and its repeal[^c-6c6a9633ee4b5a55d6a4976080f2d784]

*Extent: S*

(1) “**The 1975 Act**” means the Reservoirs Act 1975 (c.23).

(2) The 1975 Act is repealed.
