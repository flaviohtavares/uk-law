# Reservoirs (Scotland) Act 2011, section 112

> Text as at 2017-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 112. Consequential amendment and repeals

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-a4f895e2d4ea649e88cc535b7277e6e4] In section 14(2)(a) of the Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982 (c.43), for “(within the meaning of the Reservoirs Act 1975)” substitute “ (within the meaning of the Reservoirs (Scotland) Act 2011 (asp 9) but irrespective of the volume of water held in the reservoir) ”.

(2)[^key-a4f895e2d4ea649e88cc535b7277e6e4] Paragraph 98 of Schedule 13 to the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994 (c.39) is repealed.

(3)[^key-a4f895e2d4ea649e88cc535b7277e6e4] Paragraph 7 of schedule 7 to the Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002 (asp 3) is repealed.

(4)[^key-a4f895e2d4ea649e88cc535b7277e6e4] In section 55(2)(f) of the Building (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 8), for “large raised reservoir within the meaning of the Reservoirs Act 1975 (c.23)” substitute “ controlled reservoir within the meaning of the Reservoirs (Scotland) Act 2011 (asp 9) ”.

(5)[^key-ee0e74da980ebbdd3c9d32bc39f1cc9b] Part 7 of the Flood Risk Management (Scotland) Act 2009 (asp 6) is repealed.
