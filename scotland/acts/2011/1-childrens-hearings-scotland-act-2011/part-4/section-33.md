# Children's Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011, section 33

## 33. Functions of safeguarder[^c-d91daec57f65083a086b0e6f481380bb]

*Extent: S*

(1) A safeguarder appointed in relation to a child by virtue of section 30 must—

- (a) except where subsection (2) or (3)[^c-b3b35318a67e1d5dc75ac3d014c5bddd] applies, on being so appointed, prepare a report setting out anything that, in the opinion of the safeguarder, is relevant to the consideration of the matter before the children's hearing,
- (b) so far as reasonably practicable, attend the children's hearing, and
- (c) prepare any report that the safeguarder is required to prepare by a children's hearing.

(2) This subsection applies where the children's hearing directs the Principal Reporter under section 93(2)(a) or 94(2)(a) to make an application to the sheriff.

(3)[^c-4279c5d16162ac7ae8b161b7be897616] This subsection applies where the children's hearing was arranged under section 45, 46, 50, 96, 126 or 158.
