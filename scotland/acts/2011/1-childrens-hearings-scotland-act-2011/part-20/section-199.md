# Children's Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011, section 199

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (266).** Text as at 2026-03-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2011/1
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 199. Meaning of “child”[^c-2afb6ed8ba8d86c2d1d271dc9e7da34e]

*Extent: S*

(1) In this Act, “**child**” means a person who is under 16[^c-11dfdfc00036b4f99219d94efa7179df] 18 years of age (but subject to subsections (2) to (9) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc2bb4bc6b3550232148dfde08193474] subsection (2)).

(2) In paragraph (o) of section 67(2) and the other provisions of this Act in their application in relation to that paragraph, “**child**” means a person who is of school age.

(3)[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160] Subsection (4) applies where a person becomes 16 years of age— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]

- (a) after section 66 applies in relation to the person, but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]
- (b) before a relevant event. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]

(4) For the purposes of the application of this Act to the person, references in this Act to a child include references to the person until a relevant event occurs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]

(5) A relevant event is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]

- (a) the making of a compulsory supervision order in relation to the person, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]
- (b) the notification of the person under section 68(3) that the question of whether a compulsory supervision order should be made in respect of the person will not be referred to a children's hearing, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]
- (c) the discharge of the referral. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]

(6) Subsection (7) applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]

- (a) a compulsory supervision order is in force in respect of a person on the person's becoming 16 years of age, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]
- (b) a compulsory supervision order is made in respect of a person on or after the person becomes 16 years of age. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]

(7) For the purposes of the application of the provisions of this Act relating to that order, references in this Act to a child include references to the person until whichever of the following first occurs— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]

- (a) the order is terminated, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]
- (b) the person becomes 18 years of age. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]

(8) Subsection (9) applies where a case is remitted to the Principal Reporter under section 49(7)(b) of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]

(9) For the purposes of the application of this Act to the person whose case is remitted, references in this Act to a child include references to the person until whichever of the following first occurs— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]

- (a) a children's hearing or the sheriff discharges the referral, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]
- (b) a compulsory supervision order made in respect of the person is terminated, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]
- (c) the person becomes 18 years of age. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1e03d2829b96eb494fa344769cc5160]
