## 193. Formal communications[^c-bd27847122328d5d7cb937978ad9be1f]

*Extent: S*

(1) The following are formal communications—

- (a) a notice,
- (b) a determination,
- (c) a direction,
- (d) a report,
- (e) a statement,
- (f) a referral under section 127.

(2)[^c-f5282d1bcf13e53f7a0084fdadba09a1][^c-95205885443238afbe51e4a9b811fa10][^c-586263d2919478b6834cc3e5ba06e9ec] A formal communication must be in writing.

(3)[^c-f5282d1bcf13e53f7a0084fdadba09a1][^c-95205885443238afbe51e4a9b811fa10][^c-586263d2919478b6834cc3e5ba06e9ec] That requirement is satisfied by a formal communication in electronic form which is—

- (a) sent by electronic means, and
- (b) capable of being reproduced in legible form.

(4)[^c-f5282d1bcf13e53f7a0084fdadba09a1][^c-95205885443238afbe51e4a9b811fa10][^c-586263d2919478b6834cc3e5ba06e9ec] A formal communication sent in accordance with subsection (3) is to be taken to be received on the day it is sent.
