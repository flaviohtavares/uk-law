## 19. Effect of the charter[^M_I_54864dc1-b3ab-405c-a40d-3ee434dea4bb]

*Extent: S*

(1) A court or tribunal in civil or criminal proceedings may take the Scottish social security charter into account when determining any question arising in the proceedings to which the charter is relevant.

(2) Breach of the charter does not of itself give rise to grounds for any legal action.
