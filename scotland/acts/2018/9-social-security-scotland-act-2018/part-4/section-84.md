## 84. Retention of right to assistance on bankruptcy etc.[^c-2ccdaa6625e6018837b2732047b53d65]

*Extent: S*

An individual's entitlement to assistance under or by virtue of this Act does not transfer to another person—

- (a) on the individual's sequestration, or
- (b) on the appointment of a judicial factor on the individual's estate under section 41 of the Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1980.
