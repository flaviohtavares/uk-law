# Islands (Scotland) Act 2018, section 20

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 20. Review of wards in certain local government areas[^c-26ebe6031f15d38c6c556b5b1a7f5846]

*Extent: S*

(1) As soon as practicable after the commencement of this section, Boundaries Scotland[^c-8328922259ed21c7d5fbbac377faa66b] must—

- (a) review the electoral arrangements for each local government area mentioned in subsection (2), for the purpose of considering future electoral arrangements for those areas, and
- (b) formulate proposals for those arrangements.

(2) The local government areas are the areas of—

- (a) Argyll and Bute Council,
- (b) Comhairle nan Eilean Siar,
- (c) Highland Council,
- (d) North Ayrshire Council,
- (e) Orkney Islands Council,
- (f) Shetland Islands Council.

(3) Part 2 of the 1973 Act applies to a review under subsection (1) as it applies to a review under section 16 of that Act except that section 17 of that Act has effect as if it required Boundaries Scotland[^c-7bb331e4c1264132cc7ca8fa0162ca44] to submit a report on the review before such date as the Scottish Ministers may direct.

(5) In this section, “**1973 Act**” means the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973.
