## 11. Guidance about section 7 duty

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-ce44246888c8af7fec472cea098f701a] A relevant authority must have regard to any guidance issued by the Scottish Ministers about the duty imposed by section 7.

(2)[^c-47b4c84968ba6c09229e85b6c2d5e77a] Before issuing guidance under subsection (1), the Scottish Ministers must consult—

- (a) each local authority listed in the schedule,
- (b) such other persons as they consider represent the interests of island communities, and
- (c) such persons as they consider likely to be affected by the guidance.
