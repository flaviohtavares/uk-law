## 33. Information about persons aged under 16

*Extent: S*

(1) Section 14 of the Scottish Elections (Reduction of Voting Age) Act 2015 (exceptions from prohibition on disclosure of information about persons aged under 16) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a) omit the “or” following paragraph (a),
- (b) after paragraph (b) insert

  > , or
  >
  > - (c) the conduct of a referendum in accordance with the Referendums (Scotland) Act 2020.
