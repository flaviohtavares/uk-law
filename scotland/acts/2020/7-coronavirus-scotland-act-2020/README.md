# Coronavirus (Scotland) Act 2020

> Text as at 2023-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

Coronavirus (Scotland) Act 2020

2020 asp 7

An Act of the Scottish Parliament to make provision in connection with coronavirus; and for connected purposes.

The Bill for this Act of the Scottish Parliament was passed by the Parliament on 1st April 2020 and received Royal Assent on 6th April 2020
