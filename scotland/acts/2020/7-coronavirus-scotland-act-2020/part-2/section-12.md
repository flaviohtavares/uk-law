# Coronavirus (Scotland) Act 2020, section 12

> Text as at 2023-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 12. Expiry

*Extent: S*

(1) Part 1 expires at the end of[^key-d9a953fabc1907ca8360acb169cc192d] 30 September 2022[^key-b410d0189276bc09961d6c3701ebab70].

(2) Subsection (1) is subject to section 13.

(3)[^key-6b34e8be2b027eef7dd1e1b928546636] The Scottish Ministers may by regulations amend subsection (1) so as to replace “31 March 2022” with “30 September 2022”.

(5) Regulations under subsection (3) are subject to the affirmative procedure.

(6) At the same time as[^key-46ed64418685e9d0c9fa2fb9866e01f0] laying a draft Scottish statutory instrument containing regulations under subsection (3) before the Scottish Parliament in accordance with section 29(2) of the Interpretation and Legislative Reform (Scotland) Act 2010, the Scottish Ministers must lay before the Parliament a statement of their reasons why the regulations should be made.

(7)[^key-3864b35a880639ac621e4846e321a283] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8)[^key-3864b35a880639ac621e4846e321a283] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9) The Scottish Ministers may by regulations make transitional, transitory or saving provision in connection with the expiry under subsection (1) of any provision of this Act.

(10) Regulations under subsection (9)—

- (a) may make different provision for different purposes or areas,
- (b) are subject to the negative procedure.
