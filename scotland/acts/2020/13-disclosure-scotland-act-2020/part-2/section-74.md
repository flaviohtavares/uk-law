# Disclosure (Scotland) Act 2020, section 74

>

## 74. Regulated roles[^key-c0057ed6c775a00dd4ddcb4cd7ee3d7e]

*Extent: S*

(1) The PVG Act is amended as follows.

(2) For section 91 (regulated work) substitute—

> **91. Regulated roles**
>
> (1) A regulated role means a regulated role with children or adults.
>
> (2) A regulated role with children has the meaning given in schedule 2.
>
> (3) A regulated role with adults has the meaning given in schedule 3.
>
> (4) In this Act, there are two types of regulated roles—
>
> - (a) regulated roles with children, and
> - (b) regulated roles with adults.
>
> (5) References in this Act to types of regulated roles are to be construed accordingly.

.

(3)[^key-e4268e4b42ade55d82db0a0c9be8d633] For schedule 2 (regulated work with children) substitute the schedule contained in schedule 3.

(4)[^key-a1c7565e70322641799e8b02f3d0116b] For schedule 3 (regulated work with adults) substitute the schedule contained in schedule 4.
