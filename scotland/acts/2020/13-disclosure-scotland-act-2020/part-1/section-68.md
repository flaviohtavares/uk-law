## 68. Definition of consideration of suitability[^c-0569a459a5bba585ecdbe489ff50293b][^c-525e9d81a8d5bc493303d1baf51c5211]

*Extent: S*

In sections 17(1)(b) and 44(2), the references to a person (“A”) considering an individual's suitability to carry out, or to be offered or supplied for, a type of regulated role are references to A considering the individual's suitability—

- (a) to carry out that type of regulated role for A,
- (b) to be supplied by A to carry out that type of regulated role for another person,
- (c) for any other prescribed purpose.
