## 62. Fees for provision of information by the chief constable[^key-66a5f3cff7b82a1d962bc20fc278682f]

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers must pay to the Scottish Police Authority such fee as they think appropriate for the provision of information to them by the chief constable in pursuance of any provision of this Part.
