# Disclosure (Scotland) Act 2020, section 1

## 1. Level 1 disclosure[^c-79387f74ada2f65ef3c9277951e9c7af]

*Extent: S*

In this Part, a “**Level 1 disclosure**”, in relation to an individual, is a certificate—

- (a)[^c-6e3630d7fe192ca4f8e2d46a4a7d76aa] containing the prescribed details of every unspent conviction (including an unspent childhood conviction) of the individual that is recorded in central records or, if there are no such convictions, stating that fact, and
- (b) if the individual is subject to the notification requirements of Part 2 of the Sexual Offences Act 2003, stating that fact.
