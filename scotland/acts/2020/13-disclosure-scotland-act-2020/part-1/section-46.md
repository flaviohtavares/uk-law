## 46. Register of accredited bodies[^c-0a341dcab48763dd7506b55874bf0bf5]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers must maintain a register for the purposes of this Part (referred to in this Part as the “**register of accredited bodies**”).

(2) In this Part—

- (a) an “accredited body” is a person who is registered in the register of accredited bodies, and
- (b) references to “registration” are to registration in the register of accredited bodies.
