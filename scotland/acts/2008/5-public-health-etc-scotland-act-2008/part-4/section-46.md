# Public Health etc. (Scotland) Act 2008, section 46

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 46. Authorised steps[^c-ff7879abf14449c101a30b4deeb7f7c5]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where—

- (a) a quarantine order;
- (b) a short term detention order; or
- (c) an exceptional detention order,

is made, the steps which the order may authorise are the steps mentioned in subsection (2).

(2) Those steps are—

- (a) disinfection;
- (b) disinfestation; and
- (c) decontamination.
