# Public Health etc. (Scotland) Act 2008, section 4

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 4. Duty of local authorities to protect public health[^c-986d024c4bc92c9debea46b20d9f6c4b]

*Extent: S*

Each local authority is to continue to make provision, or secure that provision is made, for the purpose of protecting public health in its area.
