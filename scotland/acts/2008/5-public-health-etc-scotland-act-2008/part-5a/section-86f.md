# Public Health etc. (Scotland) Act 2008, section 86F

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 86F. Exclusion of requirement to undergo medical treatment[^key-1fbe3b64e74b1d617988b2f4af4d44cc]

*Extent: S*

(1) Regulations under section 86A(1) may not include provision requiring a person to undergo medical treatment.

(2) “Medical treatment” includes vaccination and other prophylactic treatment.
