# Public Health etc. (Scotland) Act 2008, section 123

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 123. Meaning of “premises”[^c-0b596c9c5be45f892d829d6d11ab229d]

*Extent: S*

In this Act—

- “**premises**” includes—
  - (a) any land or building; or
  - (b) any other place, including—
    - (zi) a tent or other moveable structure,[^c-34ebaaa8828f8a6b55e0b21d66b4390c]
    - (i) a mobile home; [^c-904ea0c21b093c943ad6d10891dce50c]...
    - (ii) a vehicle , and[^c-bf6dc15807d004abc3287d0b52533115]
    - (iii) an offshore installation (within the meaning given by regulation 3 of the Offshore Installations and Pipeline Works (Management and Administration) Regulations 1995 ([S.I. 1995/738](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/738))).
- “**mobile home**” includes[^c-61262fd36cf859fc4552c94ba4b994c1] a caravan or houseboat[^c-f7a2766ce8dec8ea8c43738e08ade199] used as a dwelling; and
- “**vehicle**” includes any vessel, aircraft or hovercraft.
