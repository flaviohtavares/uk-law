# Public Health etc. (Scotland) Act 2008, section 126

> Text as at 2026-03-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 126. Repeals, revocations and saving[^c-61d15f22a7212ea9c9da19a3cf63fe24][^c-429af6a009fe47eeb78e2549f96ae02f]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-0c00acda383c34d00c635d6a749a95f8] Schedule 3 (which contains repeals and revocations) has effect.

(2) Despite the repeal by schedule 3 of the Public Health (Scotland) Act 1897 (c. 38), section 166 of that Act continues to have effect for the purposes of section 101 of the 1978 Act (protection of health boards and common services agency).
