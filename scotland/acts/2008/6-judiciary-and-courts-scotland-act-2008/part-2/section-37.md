# Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2008/6
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Further provision about tribunals

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-df138561a97c0b1ee2a26816a3551e1a] A tribunal constituted under section 35 may require any person—

- (a) to attend its proceedings for the purpose of giving evidence,
- (b) to produce documents in the person's custody or under the person's control.

(2)[^key-df138561a97c0b1ee2a26816a3551e1a] A person on whom such a requirement is imposed is not obliged to answer any question or produce any document which the person would be entitled to refuse to answer or produce in a court in Scotland.

(3)[^key-df138561a97c0b1ee2a26816a3551e1a] Subsection (4) applies where a person on whom a requirement has been imposed under subsection (1)—

- (a) refuses or fails, without reasonable excuse, to comply with the requirement,
- (b) refuses or fails, without reasonable excuse, while attending the tribunal proceedings to give evidence, to answer any question, or
- (c) deliberately alters, conceals or destroys any document which the person is required to produce.

(4)[^key-df138561a97c0b1ee2a26816a3551e1a] The Court of Session may, on an application made to it by the tribunal—

- (a) make such order for enforcing compliance as it sees fit, or
- (b) deal with the matter as if it were a contempt of the Court.

(5)[^key-6157c00ccef196f22fdedd908f4886b1] The Court of Session may by act of sederunt make provision as to the procedure to be followed by and before tribunals constituted under section 35.

(6)[^key-df138561a97c0b1ee2a26816a3551e1a] The Scottish Ministers may pay such remuneration to, and expenses of, members of tribunals constituted under section 35 as they think fit.

(7)[^key-df138561a97c0b1ee2a26816a3551e1a] The Scottish Ministers must pay such expenses as they consider are reasonably required to be incurred to enable a tribunal constituted under section 35 to carry out its functions.
