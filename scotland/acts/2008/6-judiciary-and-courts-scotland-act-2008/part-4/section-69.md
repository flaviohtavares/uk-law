## 69. Guidance[^c-b2f72f20e62e141cf10373885acc798f]

*Extent: S*

The SCTS[^c-96dd6eeaca12e04981eaa23b0b5c378e] must, in carrying out its functions, have regard to any guidance issued by the Scottish Ministers.
