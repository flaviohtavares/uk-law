# Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008, section 60

## 60. The Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service[^c2077126][^c-d755b3abc8f5cbacd9b5a18cdade8227]

*Extent: S*

(1) There is established a body corporate to be known as the Scottish Courts and Tribunals[^c-75729b39ac5c2136a596d3624e9a640f] Service (referred to in this Part as “**the SCTS[^c-96dd6eeaca12e04981eaa23b0b5c378e]**”).

(2)[^c-85617d7b95314418b5f54a55c7e8118e] Schedule 3 makes further provision about the SCTS.
