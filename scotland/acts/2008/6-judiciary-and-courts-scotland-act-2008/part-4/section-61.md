## 61. Administrative support for the Scottish courts and judiciary[^c-3ec37681f3fa8285e51a6ae2e00e347f]

*Extent: S*

(1) The SCTS[^c-96dd6eeaca12e04981eaa23b0b5c378e] has the function of providing, or ensuring the provision of, the property, services, officers and other staff required for the purposes of—

- (a) the Scottish courts, and
- (b) the judiciary of those courts.

(2) In carrying out that function, the SCTS must—

- (a) take account, in particular, of the needs of members of the public and those involved in proceedings in the Scottish courts, and
- (b) so far as practicable and appropriate, co-operate and co-ordinate activity with any other person having functions in relation to the administration of justice.

(3) In this Part, “**the Scottish courts**” has the meaning given by section 2(6).
