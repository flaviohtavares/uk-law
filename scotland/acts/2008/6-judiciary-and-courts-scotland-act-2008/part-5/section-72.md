# Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008, section 72

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2008/6

## 72. Interpretation

*Extent: S*

In this Act—

- [^c-93911e391325249f081f7fbaaf19c865]...
- “**the Inner House**” means the Inner House of the Court of Session,
- “**the Lord President**” means the Lord President of the Court of Session,
- “**office of part-time sheriff**” means an appointment under section 8(1), or a reappointment under section 9(1) or (3), of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014[^c-04ab0f5cafd4aa761d5f0143547ca241], and references to suspension from that office are to be construed accordingly,
- “[^c-34e87889aaef2b8833f3b5763aab2cba] **office of part-time summary sheriff** ” means an appointment under section 10(1), or a reappointment under section 11(1) or (3), of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014, and references to suspension from that office are to be construed accordingly,
- “**office of temporary judge**” means an appointment under section 20B(1) (temporary judges)[^c-877519cb2ed1da5f28055658485efbf0], and references to removal or suspension from that office are to be construed accordingly,
- “**office of temporary sheriff principal**” means an appointment under section 6(2) of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014[^c-7fbe2c354b6c625749754c0276308735], and references to suspension from that office are to be construed accordingly.
