# Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asp/2008/6

## 76. Commencement and short title

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c2077301] The provisions of this Act, other than this section and sections 71, 72, 74 and 75, come into force in accordance with provision made by order by the Scottish Ministers.

(2) This Act may be cited as the Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008.
