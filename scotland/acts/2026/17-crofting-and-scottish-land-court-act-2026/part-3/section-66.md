# Crofting and Scottish Land Court Act 2026, section 66

> Text as at 2026-10-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 66. Commencement[^c-31d101c7ee3c5441e3e24cce82bdc948]

*Extent: S*

(1) This section and sections 64, 65 and 67 come into force the day after Royal Assent.

(2) The other provisions of this Act come into force on such day as the Scottish Ministers may by regulations appoint.

(3) Regulations under this section may—

- (a) make different provision for different purposes or areas,
- (b) include transitional, transitory or saving provision.
