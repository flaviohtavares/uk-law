# Crofting and Scottish Land Court Act 2026, section 58

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 58. Savings etc.[^c-780fc54c5d07da38adc4f95634b05e8d]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The transfer of jurisdiction from the Tribunal to the Court does not affect—

- (a) the principles on which any question is to be determined,
- (b) the persons on whom the determination is binding,
- (c) any provision which requires particular matters to be expressly dealt with or embodied in the determination, or which relates to evidence.

(2) The transfer of proceedings before the Tribunal to the Court does not affect the validity or effect of anything done in, for or by the Tribunal in respect of the exercise of its jurisdiction or functions.
