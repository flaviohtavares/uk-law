# Crofting and Scottish Land Court Act 2026, section 26

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 26. Consultation on common grazings[^key-980914e86c026385e279a559b756b20d]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Scottish Ministers must, within 5 years of Royal Assent, undertake a consultation on measures to improve the regulation of common grazings.

(2) The consultation under subsection (1) must, in particular, include consideration of—

- (a) measures to increase the number of common grazings for which a grazings committee is appointed,
- (b) measures to improve transparency in relation to the persons holding shares in common grazings, and
- (c) action that may be taken where such persons referred to in paragraph (b) cannot be identified.
