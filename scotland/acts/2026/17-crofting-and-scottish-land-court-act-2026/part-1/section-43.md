# Crofting and Scottish Land Court Act 2026, section 43

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 43. Reversion of resumed land[^c-a8fd1cbbd298821641403bf70c68b0cf]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The 1993 Act is modified by subsection (2).

(2) In section 21A (reversion of resumed land)—

- (a) subsection (1A) is repealed,
- (b) in subsection (1B)—
  - (i) in the opening words, for “land which, before being resumed as mentioned in subsection (1), was a registered croft (or part of such a croft)” substitute “an order under subsection (1) that land be resumed”,
  - (ii) in paragraph (a), for “an order under that subsection” substitute “the order”.

(3) The 2010 Act is modified by subsections (4) to (6).

(4) In section 4(4) (first registration), paragraph (h) is repealed.

(5) In section 10(3)(a) (completion of registration), “(h),” is repealed.

(6) In schedule 2, in table 1, the entry relating to the making of an application as mentioned in section 4(4)(h) is repealed.
