# Crofting and Scottish Land Court Act 2026, section 17

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 17. Guidance relating to absences from crofts[^c-7ae3f8af0ad127b373a002c5532c21ca]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

After section 21D of the 1993 Act insert—

> **21E. Guidance relating to absences from crofts**
>
> (1) The Commission must publish guidance in relation to how it exercises its functions under sections 21B to 21D.
>
> (2) Guidance published under this section is to include information about—
>
> - (a) what may (or may not) be considered a good reason for a person not to be ordinarily resident on, or within 32 kilometers of a croft or, as the case may be, owner-occupied croft,
> - (b) how the duties under this Act that apply in relation to a croft, or as the case may be, an owner-occupied croft, are to be complied with during any period that consent under section 21B(1) has been granted, and
> - (c) the conditions that may be imposed when granting consent under section 21B including, in particular, conditions as to the duration of any absence and when that may be extended.
>
> (3) Guidance under this section may include such other information as the Commission consider appropriate.
