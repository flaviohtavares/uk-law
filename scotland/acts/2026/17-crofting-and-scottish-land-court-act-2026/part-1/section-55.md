# Crofting and Scottish Land Court Act 2026, section 55

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 55. Interpretation of Part 1[^key-b0026216bda8279686239b0a7a1bb15b]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

In this Part—

- “**the 1993 Act**” means the Crofters (Scotland) Act 1993,
- “**the 2010 Act**” means the Crofting Reform (Scotland) Act 2010.
