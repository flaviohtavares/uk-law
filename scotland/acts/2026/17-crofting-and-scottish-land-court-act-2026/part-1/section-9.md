# Crofting and Scottish Land Court Act 2026, section 9

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 9. Statutory conditions: entitlement to fixed equipment[^key-4e3acdda6dd9c0ed5e1dc8a477bf804c]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The 1993 Act is modified as follows.

(2) In schedule 2 (the statutory conditions)—

- (a) for paragraph 4 substitute—

  > **4.** The crofter is not entitled to any fixed equipment from the landlord in respect of the croft.

  ,
- (b) paragraph 7 is repealed.
