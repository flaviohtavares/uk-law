# Crofting and Scottish Land Court Act 2026, section 28

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28. Tenancies which cannot be private residential tenancies[^key-b23ab80800a229545d3721d455e8887f]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Private Housing (Tenancies) (Scotland) Act 2016 is modified as follows.

(2) In schedule 1, after paragraph 4, insert—

> **4A.** (1) A tenancy cannot be a private residential tenancy if the let property includes a croft.
>
> (2) For the purposes of this paragraph, “**croft**” means—
>
> - (a) a holding which is a croft within the meaning of section 3 of the Crofters (Scotland) Act 1993,
> - (b) land or a holding in relation to which the Crofting Commission have made a determination under section 3A(1) of that Act or, as the case may be, section 3A(2) of that Act, to constitute the land or holding as a croft,
> - (c) a holding which is a croft within the meaning of section 3ZA of that Act.
