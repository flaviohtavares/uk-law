# Crofting and Scottish Land Court Act 2026, section 50

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 50. Crofting census notices[^key-b77cf60b956bd03e3667cfd65021663d]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The 1993 Act is modified as follows.

(2) In section 40A (annual notices)—

- (a) in subsection (5), for “as soon as reasonably practicable after the end of each successive 1 year period” substitute “at least once in every subsequent period of 3 years”,
- (b) for subsection (7), substitute—

  > (7) Section 55 applies in relation to a notice given under subsection (1) of this section as if for subsection (1)(b) of that section there were substituted—
  >
  > > - (b) by being sent by post to the proper address of the person,
  >
  > .

  ,
- (c) the section heading becomes “**Duties notice**”.
