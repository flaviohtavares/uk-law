# Children (Care, Care Experience and Services Planning) (Scotland) Act 2026, section 24

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 24. Return to continuing care[^c-76c914f67b4b3c4126cfd76b1ab1721f]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Children (Scotland) Act 1995 is amended as follows.

(2) In section 26A (provision of continuing care: looked after children), after subsection (7) insert—

> (7A) For the purposes of subsection (7)(a) above, the duty to provide continuing care only ceases until such time (if any) as the person requests to return to the accommodation.

.
