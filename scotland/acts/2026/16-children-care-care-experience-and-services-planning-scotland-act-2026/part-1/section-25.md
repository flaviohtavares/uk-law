# Children (Care, Care Experience and Services Planning) (Scotland) Act 2026, section 25

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 25. Corporate parenting: application to persons looked after before age 16 and planning[^key-3a1ece1ea6d418a4de5beb0993aa0f70]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 is amended as follows.

(2) In section 57 (children and young people in relation to whom corporate parenting responsibilities apply)—

- (a) in subsection (1), for paragraph (b) substitute—

  > - (b) every child and young person under the age of 26 who—
  >   - (i) is not looked after by a local authority, but
  >   - (ii) has, at any point in the child or young person’s life, been so looked after.

  ,
- (b) subsection (2) is repealed.

(3) In section 59 (planning by corporate parents), after subsection (1), insert—

> (1A) A plan or revised plan must include information on how the corporate parent proposes to exercise its corporate parenting responsibilities in a way that delivers the recommendations set out in the report published on 5 February 2020 on behalf of the Independent Care Review established by the Scottish Ministers in October 2016 (“the Promise”).

.

(4) In section 99(2) (powers subject to affirmative procedure), the words “section 57(2)(b)” are repealed.
