# Children (Care, Care Experience and Services Planning) (Scotland) Act 2026, section 52

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 52. Children’s advocacy services: non-instructed advocacy[^key-dbfeafc4f5a7150794b3f1afebd507db]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 is amended as follows.

(2) In section 122 (children’s advocacy services), in subsection (7)—

- (a) the words from “means” to the end become paragraph (a),
- (b) after that paragraph insert “, and

  > - (b) includes, where a child is unable due to the child’s age or capacity to instruct that such services be provided to the child, services provided other than on the child’s own instruction.

  .
