# Children (Care, Care Experience and Services Planning) (Scotland) Act 2026, section 63

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 63. Reporting on children’s services plans[^c-da3371bd308addab5ff68c0a59ad2b05][^c-b79e1d8365df698c36b4f6270175dc82]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 is amended as follows.

(2) In section 13 (reporting on children’s services plan)—

- (a) after subsection (1) insert—

  > (1A) The Scottish Ministers may by regulations prescribe other matters to be included in the report.

  ,
- (b) after subsection (2) insert—

  > (3) Before making regulations under subsection (1A), the Scottish Ministers must consult—
  >
  > - (a) lead children’s services planning bodies,
  > - (b) such other persons as they consider appropriate.

  .
