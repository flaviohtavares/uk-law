## 12. Scottish Natural Heritage representation on advisory panels[^c-930293e2339b52522eb668c5efff5bc0][^c-b31314419a28be0044568a3894a30ad7]

*Extent: S*

(1) Section 4 of the 1996 Act (appointment of panels) is modified as follows.

(2) For subsections (4) and (5) substitute—

> (4) SNH may appoint a member of SNH or a member of SNH’s staff as a member of a panel under subsection (1).
