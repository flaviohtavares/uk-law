## 47. Licensing: protection of mountain hares[^c-f19c234425ef274023444ba898c95db2][^c-ba0fbd456048ab0f1077d6205b8c051b]

*Extent: S*

(1) Section 16 of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (power to grant licenses) is modified as follows.

(2) In subsection (3), after paragraph (e) insert—

> - (ea) for the purpose of falconry to kill or take mountain hares (*Lepus Timidus*) during the period beginning with 1 October and ending with the last day of February,

.
