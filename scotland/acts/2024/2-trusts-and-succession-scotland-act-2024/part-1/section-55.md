# Trusts and Succession (Scotland) Act 2024, section 55

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 55. Application to protectors of certain provisions relating to removal from office and to decision making[^c-1c7d2cc79066744f54a250a47540705d]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Sections 7, 13 and 14 apply in relation to a protector and the duties of a protector as they apply in relation to a trustee and the duties of a trustee.

(2) This section applies irrespective of when the trust was created.
