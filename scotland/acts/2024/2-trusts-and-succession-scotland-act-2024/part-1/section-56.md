# Trusts and Succession (Scotland) Act 2024, section 56

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 56. Resignation of protector[^c-855065b6b5eba194ea5301ed7a624969]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A protector has power to resign office by notice in writing sent to the trustees.

(2) The resignation takes effect on receipt of that notice.

(3) But any resignation sent under this section in order to facilitate a breach of trust is of no effect.

(4) This section applies irrespective of when the trust was created.
