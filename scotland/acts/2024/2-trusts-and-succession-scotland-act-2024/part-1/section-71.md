# Trusts and Succession (Scotland) Act 2024, section 71

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 71. Expenses of application[^c-68c166bec2869992eb3d4fbcba10c3b7]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Any question of expenses in relation to an application under this Act is to be determined by the court.

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1), the court may, if it considers it reasonable to do so, direct that any such expenses be paid out of the trust property.
