# Trusts and Succession (Scotland) Act 2024, section 51

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 51. Application to supervisors of certain provisions relating to removal from office and to decision making[^key-e51590d4e7d210789b4e4d6821155d7f]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Sections 7, 13 and 14 apply in relation to a supervisor and the duties of a supervisor as they apply in relation to a trustee and the duties of a trustee.

(2) This section applies irrespective of when the trust was created.
