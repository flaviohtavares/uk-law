## 76. Effect of divorce, dissolution or annulment on special destination[^key-c011471e1adf4fb20581e73fbbf2ea00]

*Extent: S*

In section 2(2) of the Succession (Scotland) Act 2016 (effect of divorce, dissolution or annulment on special destination), after “succession to” insert “A’s interest in”.
