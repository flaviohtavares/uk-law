## 36. Direct references to Supreme Court: compatibility question arising in proceedings[^key-b37e35bc3651b06582e51fad81f2d4cb]

*Extent: S*

The Lord Advocate may require any court or tribunal to refer to the Supreme Court any compatibility question which has arisen in proceedings before it to which the Lord Advocate is a party.
