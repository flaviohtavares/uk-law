## 12. Power for Scottish Commission for Human Rights to bring or intervene in proceedings[^c-5446f455ff044ab214245315ab13bead]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Commission for Human Rights Act 2006 is amended as follows.

(2) After section 4 insert—

> **4A. Proceedings under the United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child (Incorporation) (Scotland) Act 2024**
>
> For the purposes of its general duty, the Commission may—
>
> - (a) bring proceedings under section 7(1)(a) of the United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child (Incorporation) (Scotland) Act 2024,
> - (b) intervene in proceedings in which a person claims that a public authority has acted (or proposes to act) in a way which is made unlawful by section 6(1) of that Act.

.

(3) In section 6 (no power to assist in claims or legal proceedings), after subsection (2) insert—

> (2A) Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply to proceedings brought or intervened in by the Commission under section 4A.

.
