# The Management of Offenders etc. (Scotland) Act 2005 (Specification of Persons) Amendment Order 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 365

Criminal Law

The Management of Offenders etc. (Scotland) Act 2005 (Specification of Persons) Amendment Order 2020

Made

10th November 2020

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

12th November 2020

Coming into force

8th January 2021

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10(3) of the Management of Offenders etc. (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Management of Offenders etc. (Scotland) Act 2005 (Specification of Persons) Amendment Order 2020 and comes into force on 8 January 2021.

## 2. Amendment of the Management of Offenders etc. (Scotland) Act 2005 (Specification of Persons) Order 2007

In the schedule of the Management of Offenders etc. (Scotland) Act 2005 (Specification of Persons) Order 2007 (persons specified for the purposes of section 10(3) of the Management of Offenders etc. (Scotland) Act 2005)[^f00002], after the penultimate paragraph insert—

> The Scottish Ministers in exercise of their functions under the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018[^f00003].

.

HUMZA YOUSAF

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

10th November 2020

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Management of Offenders etc. (Scotland) Act 2005 (Specification of Persons) Order 2007 (“the 2007 Order”). Article 2 amends the schedule of the 2007 Order to include the Scottish Ministers, in exercising their functions under the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018, as a ‘person’ required to mutually co-operate with the responsible authorities specified in section 10(7) of the Management of Offenders etc. (Scotland) Act 2005.
