## 25. Voluntary return point operators[^c-147b6487e81b3ae219da17860d94bdeb]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person may operate a return point at a place other than a premises on which a return point must be operated under regulation 19(2) only[^c-6ee42bd749f654033aaaa52bb8cd750a] if they are registered with the scheme administrator[^c-564d05fdc18a8110cd5fd5ac0743cd7c] as a voluntary return point operator under article 31 (registration of voluntary return point operators) of the Designation Order 2025[^c-fc01efe425460ff639cc80b5661c48e9].

(2) An application for registration must—

- (a) be made in writing,
- (b) contain the information set out in schedule 4, and
- (c) include any information as may be reasonably requested by the scheme administrator[^c-334d03379bb75d73b036c443818a658f].

(3)[^c-1e47683135bd24dbecb565878c4cc856] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-1e47683135bd24dbecb565878c4cc856] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c-1e47683135bd24dbecb565878c4cc856] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c-1e47683135bd24dbecb565878c4cc856] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^c-1e47683135bd24dbecb565878c4cc856] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8) A registered voluntary return point operator must—

- (a) comply with the obligations in regulation 20, and
- (b) notify the scheme administrator[^c-8577ae7b0f38b6eb2cece73ab5e6f28d] of any material change in the information provided in accordance with paragraph (2) within 28 days of the date of the change[^c-cf281d896951d451751edc307dd9db8c].

(9) A registered voluntary return point operator must comply with paragraph (8) from the date on which the registration takes effect[^c-5d97b89878c22b0721f047324172d51b].
