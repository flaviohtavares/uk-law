## 20A. Hospitality retailers[^c-e6ef7aef825e9d9c0928c4866cbd5734]

*Extent: S*

A hospitality retailer must retain for collection by the[^M_F_6a9013fe-5232-40b8-e1c5-bd6af9a5a776] scheme administrator the scheme packaging in which scheme articles were sold by that hospitality retailer for consumption on the premises of sale.
