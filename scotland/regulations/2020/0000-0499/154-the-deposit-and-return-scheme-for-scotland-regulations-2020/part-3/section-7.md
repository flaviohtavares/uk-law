## 7. Application for registration of a producer[^key-5ac9e8953b7f55c7d270b2d7d2725c97]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-3151003be2b04d903055d0f05422692c] An application for registration of a producer must be made to the scheme administrator by the producer.

(2)[^key-4e55a9d1419b92d30b977f6daa545c49] An application for registration of a producer must be made—

- (a) before 1 October 2027, or
- (b) where a person is not a producer on that date, within 28 days of that person becoming a producer.

(3) An application must—

- (a) be made in writing,
- (b) [^key-19de1d2441b311a16f70a13c26b13b75]... contain the information set out in schedule 1,
- (c) contain any other information as may be reasonably requested by the scheme administrator[^key-1665acac0080428df51f870739e5cba7], [^key-01a6bbab907493ff54cc11d2572e5826]...
- (d)[^key-01a6bbab907493ff54cc11d2572e5826] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-348e4c9a30d32244ccdd9195bd715aef] Where the application is granted, the producer will be treated as registered from the date specified by the scheme administrator under article 23(2)(b) (producer registration) of the Designation Order 2025.
