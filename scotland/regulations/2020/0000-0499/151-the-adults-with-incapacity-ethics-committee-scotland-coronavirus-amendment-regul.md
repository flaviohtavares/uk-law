# The Adults with Incapacity (Ethics Committee) (Scotland) (Coronavirus) Amendment Regulations 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 151

Adults With Incapacity

The Adults with Incapacity (Ethics Committee) (Scotland) (Coronavirus) Amendment Regulations 2020

Made

14th May 2020

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

15th May 2020

Coming into force

21st May 2020

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 51(6) of the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Adults with Incapacity (Ethics Committee) (Scotland) (Coronavirus) Amendment Regulations 2020 and come into force on 21 May 2020.

## 2. Amendment of the Adults with Incapacity (Ethics Committee) (Scotland) Regulations 2002

(1) The Adults with Incapacity (Ethics Committee) (Scotland) Regulations 2002[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3 (membership of the committee), in paragraphs (4) and (5), for “10” substitute “12” in both places it occurs.

(3) In regulation 4 (chair and vice-chair)—

- (a) for “and” in the second place it occurs substitute “,”, and
- (b) after “Vice-Chair” insert “and up to two Alternate Vice-Chairs”.

(4) In regulation 7(2) (procedures)—

- (a) for “7” substitute “3”,
- (b) in sub-paragraph (a) after “Vice-Chair” insert “or an Alternate Vice-Chair”, and
- (c) in sub-paragraph (b) for “two members” substitute “a member”,
- (d) in sub-paragraph (c) for “two members” substitute “a member”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Adults with Incapacity (Ethics Committee) (Scotland) Regulations 2002 (“the 2002 Regulations”).

Regulation 2(2) amends regulation 3(4) and (5) to extend the consecutive period a person may be appointed to the Ethics Committee (“the Committee”) from 10 to 12 years.

Regulation 2(3) amends the 2002 Regulations to allow Ministers to appoint up to two Alternate Vice Chairs in addition to a Chair and Vice Chair.

Regulation 2(4) amends the 2002 Regulations to reduce the number of Committee members required to approve any research from 7 to 3 and adjust the description of persons to be included in that number. The amendment provides that one of the 3 persons must be the Chair or in the absence of the Chair the Vice-Chair or an Alternate Vice Chair.
