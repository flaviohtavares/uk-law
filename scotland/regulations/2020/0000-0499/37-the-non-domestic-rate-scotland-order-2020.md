# The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2020

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2020 No. 37

Rating And Valuation

The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2020

Made

19th February 2020

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

21st February 2020

Coming into force

1st April 2020

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7B(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2020 and comes into force on 1 April 2020.

## 2. Non-domestic rate for 2020-2021

For the purposes of section 7B of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, the Scottish Ministers prescribe a rate of 49.8 pence per pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of financial year 2020-2021.

BEN MACPHERSON

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

19th February 2020

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes a rate of 49.8 pence in the pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of the financial year 2020-2021. A rate of 49 pence in the pound was prescribed by the Scottish Ministers as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland for the financial year 2019-2020 ([S.S.I. 2019/35](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2019/35)).
