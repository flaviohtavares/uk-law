# The Registration of Fish Farming and Shellfish Farming Businesses Amendment (No. 2) (Scotland) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Scottish Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a drafting defect in [S.S.I. 2002/193](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/193) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that instrument.

2002 No. 220

RIVER

SALMON AND FRESHWATER FISHERIES

The Registration of Fish Farming and Shellfish Farming Businesses Amendment (No. 2) (Scotland) Order 2002

Made

8th May 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

8th May 2002

Coming into force

10th May 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7(1), (2) and (3) of the Diseases of Fish Act 1983[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Registration of Fish Farming and Shellfish Farming Businesses Amendment (No. 2) (Scotland) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 10th May 2002.

## 2. Amendment of the Registration of Fish Farming and Shellfish Farming Businesses Amendment (Scotland) Order 2002

For article 2(4) of the Registration of Fish Farming and Shellfish Farming Businesses Amendment (Scotland) Order 2002[^f00002] there is substituted–

> (4) After paragraph 3 of Part I of Schedule 3 (particulars to be included in records) there is inserted–
>
> > **4.** A copy of all information notified to the Scottish Ministers in accordance with articles 4A and 4B of this Order.

.

ALLAN WILSON

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

8th May 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Registration of Fish Farming and Shellfish Farming Businesses Amendment (Scotland) Order 2002 to correct a drafting defect (article 2).
