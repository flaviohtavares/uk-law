# The Protection of Wild Mammals (Scotland) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 181 (C. 12)

ANIMALS

COUNTRYSIDE

The Protection of Wild Mammals (Scotland) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2002

Made

9th April 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 12(2) of the Protection of Wild Mammals (Scotland) Act 2002[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Protection of Wild Mammals (Scotland) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2002.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of the Protection of Wild Mammals (Scotland) Act 2002 is 1st August 2002.

ROSS FINNIE

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

9th April 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 1st August 2002 as the date for coming into force of sections 1 to 11 of the Protection of Wild Mammals (Scotland) Act 2002.
