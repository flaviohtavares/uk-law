# The National Health Service (Clinical Negligence and Other Risks Indemnity Scheme) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 239

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE

The National Health Service (Clinical Negligence and Other Risks Indemnity Scheme) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002

Made

20th May 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

22nd May 2002

Coming into force

14th June 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(5), 85B, 105(7) and 108(1) of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (Clinical Negligence and Other Risks Indemnity Scheme) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 14th June 2002.

## 2. Amendment of regulation 3 of the National Health Service (Clinical Negligence and Other Risks Indemnity Scheme) (Scotland) Regulations 2000

(1) Regulation 3 of the National Health Service (Clinical Negligence and Other Risks Indemnity Scheme) (Scotland) Regulations 2000[^f00002] is amended in accordance with the following paragraphs.

(2) At the end of paragraph (c) “and” is omitted.

(3) At the end of paragraph (d) there is added–

> and
>
> - (e) the Mental Welfare Commission for Scotland.

.

MALCOLM CHISHOLM

A member of the Scottish Executive

St. Andrew’s House Edinburgh

20th May 2002

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the National Health Service (Clinical Negligence and Other Risks Indemnity Scheme) (Scotland) Regulations 2000 ([S.S.I. 2000/54](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2000/54)) (“the 2000 Regulations”) which established a scheme under which members of the scheme could make provision for meeting liabilities arising out of negligence in the carrying out of functions and for indemnity for other financial loss.

Regulation 2 amends regulation 3 of the 2000 Regulations in order to add the Mental Welfare Commission for Scotland as a member of the scheme.
