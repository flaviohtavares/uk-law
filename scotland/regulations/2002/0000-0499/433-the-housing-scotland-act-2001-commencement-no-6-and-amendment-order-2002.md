# The Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Commencement No. 6 and Amendment) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 433 (C. 22)

HOUSING

The Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Commencement No. 6 and Amendment) Order 2002

Made

19th September 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 113(1) and (2) of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Commencement No. 6 and Amendment) Order 2002.

(2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001.

## 2. Commencement of provisions

The provisions of the Act which are specified in column 1 of the Schedule to this Order shall, insofar as not already in force, come into force on 30th September 2002, but, where a particular purpose in relation to any such provision is specified in column 2 of that Schedule, that provision shall come into force on that day only for that purpose.

## 3. Amendment

(1) The Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Commencement No. 5, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2002[^f00002] is amended in accordance with paragraph (2) below.

(2) In the Table in the Schedule, the second paragraph in the third column of the entry for the Housing (Scotland) Act 1988[^f00003] is omitted.

MARGARET CURRAN

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

19th September 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force various provisions of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (“the Act”).

Article 2 and the Schedule bring into force on 30th September 2002 provisions, listed in that Schedule, which relate to repeal of management agreements between housing co operatives and local authorities.

Article 3 amends the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Commencement No. 5, Transitional Provisions and Savings) Order 2002.
