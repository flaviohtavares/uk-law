# The Scottish Secure Tenancies (Exceptions) Amendment Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Scottish Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.S.I. 2002/314](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/314) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Instrument.

2002 No. 434

HOUSING

The Scottish Secure Tenancies (Exceptions) Amendment Regulations 2002

Made

19th September 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

23rd September 2002

Coming into force

30th September 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 109(2) of, and paragraph 3(2) of schedule 1 to, the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Scottish Secure Tenancies (Exceptions) Amendment Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 30th September 2002.

## 2. Amendment of Regulations

(1) The Scottish Secure Tenancies (Exceptions) Regulations 2002[^f00002] are amended in accordance with these Regulations.

(2) For paragraphs (b) and (c) of regulation 2, substitute–

> - (b) any institution within the higher education sector within the meaning of section 56(2) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992[^f00003];
> - (c) any institution which provides further education within the meaning of section 135(1) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00004];

.

MARGARET CURRAN

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

19th September 2002

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Scottish Secure Tenancies (Exceptions) Regulations 2002 by replacing the descriptions of certain institutions for the provision of higher education and further education.
