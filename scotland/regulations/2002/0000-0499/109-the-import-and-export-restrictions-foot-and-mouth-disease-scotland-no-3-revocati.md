# The Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Scotland) (No. 3) Revocation Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 109

ANIMALS

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Scotland) (No. 3) Revocation Regulations 2002

Made

6th March 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

13th March 2002

Coming into force on

7th March 2002

The Scottish Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Scotland) (No. 3) Revocation Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 7th March 2002.

## 2. Revocations

The Regulations listed in column 1 of the Schedule to these Regulations are revoked to the extent specified in column 3 of that Schedule.

DAVID R DICKSON

A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers

Pentland House,

Edinburgh

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations implement in Scotland article 3 of Commission Decision [2002/153/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0153) (O.J. No. L 50, 21.2.02, p.98) concerning certain protection measures with regard to foot-and-mouth disease in the United Kingdom, repealing Decision [2001/740/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0740) and amending for the eighth time Decision [2001/327/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0327).

They revoke the restrictions on imports and exports of certain animals and animal products contained in the Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (No. 3) Regulations 2001 as amended, as set out in the Schedule.
