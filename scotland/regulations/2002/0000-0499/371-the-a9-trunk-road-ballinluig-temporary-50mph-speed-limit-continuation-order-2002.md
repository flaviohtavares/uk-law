# The A9 Trunk Road (Ballinluig) (Temporary 50mph Speed Limit) (Continuation) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 371

ROAD TRAFFIC

The A9 Trunk Road (Ballinluig) (Temporary 50mph Speed Limit) (Continuation) Order 2002

Made

14th August 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

16th August 2002

Coming into force

2nd September 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 88(4) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

**1.** This Order may be cited as the A9 Trunk Road (Ballinluig) (Temporary 50mph Speed Limit) (Continuation) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 2nd September 2002.

**2.** The provisions of the A9 Trunk Road (Ballinluig) (Temporary 50mph Speed Limit) Order 2001[^f00002] shall continue until midnight on 30th November 2002.

J G BARTON

A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers

Victoria Quay,

Edinburgh

14th August 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order continues in force the provisions of the A9 Trunk Road (Ballinluig) (Temporary 50mph Speed Limit) Order 2001. That Order, which would otherwise cease to have effect at midnight on 1st September 2002, imposes a temporary 50mph speed limit on two lengths of the A9 Trunk Road at Ballinluig.
