# The Disabled Persons (Badges for Motor Vehicles) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 451

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Disabled Persons (Badges for Motor Vehicles) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002

Made

3rd October 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

4th October 2002

Coming into force

8th November 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 21(7F) of the Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons Act 1970[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Disabled Persons (Badges for Motor Vehicles) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 8th November 2002.

## 2. Amendment of the 2000 Regulations

In regulation 2(1A) (interpretation) of the Disabled Persons (Badges for Motor Vehicles) (Scotland) Regulations 2000[^f00002], at the end there shall be inserted–

> - , a badge issued under a provision of the law of Northern Ireland corresponding to that section or a badge issued by any member State other than the United Kingdom for purposes corresponding to the purposes for which badges under that section are issued

.

LEWIS MACDONALD

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

3rd October 2002

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Disabled Persons (Badges for Motor Vehicles) (Scotland) Regulations 2000.

Regulation 2 has the effect of enabling disabled person’s badges issued in Northern Ireland or any member State to be displayed as if they were badges issued by a local authority in Scotland.
