# The Children (Scotland) Act 1995 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 12 (C. 1)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The Children (Scotland) Act 1995 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2002

Made

21st January 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 105(1) of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Children (Scotland) Act 1995 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2002.

## 2. Appointed day

22nd January 2002 is the appointed day for the coming into force, so far as not already in force, of Schedule 4, paragraph 26(8), and Schedule 5 to the Children (Scotland) Act 1995 insofar as they relate to section 103 of the Children Act 1975[^f00002].

CATHY JAMIESON

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

21st January 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 22nd January the repeal of section 103 of the Children Act 1975 which provided for the establishment of a panel of persons from whom curators ad litem, reporting officers and safeguarders were to be appointed.

(This note is not part of the Order)
