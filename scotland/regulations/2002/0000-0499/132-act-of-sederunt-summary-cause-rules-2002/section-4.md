# Act of Sederunt (Summary Cause Rules) 2002, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Revocation

The Acts of Sederunt mentioned in column (1) of Schedule 2 to this Act of Sederunt are revoked to the extent specified in column (3) of that Schedule except–

- (a) in relation to any summary cause commenced before 10th June 2002; and
- (b) for the purposes of the Act of Sederunt (Small Claim Rules) 1988[^f00002].
