# The Scottish Legal Services Ombudsman (Compensation) (Prescribed Amount) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 32

LEGAL SERVICES

The Scottish Legal Services Ombudsman (Compensation) (Prescribed Amount) Order 2002

Made

29th January 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

31st January 2002

Coming into force

28th February 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 34A(10) of the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Legal Services Ombudsman (Compensation) (Prescribed Amount) Order 2002 and shall come into force on 28th February 2002.

## 2. Prescribed amount

For the purposes of section 34A of the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990 the prescribed amount is £1,200.

JAMES R WALLACE

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

29th January 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes the amount which the stated amount, in terms of section 34A of the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990, cannot exceed.

The stated amount is such compensation payment as may be recommended by the Scottish legal services ombudsman.

The previous prescribed amount was £1,000 and this Order raises that amount to £1,200.
