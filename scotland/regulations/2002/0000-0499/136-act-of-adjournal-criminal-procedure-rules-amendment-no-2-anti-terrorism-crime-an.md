# Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules Amendment No.2) (Anti-Terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001) 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 136

SHERIFF COURT

Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules Amendment No.2) (Anti-Terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001) 2002

Made

1st March 2002

Coming into force

4th March 2002

The Lord Justice General, the Lord Justice Clerk and the Lords Commissioners of Justiciary in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 305 of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Act of Adjournal—

- (a) may be cited as the Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules Amendment No.2) (Anti-Terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001) 2002;
- (b) shall come into force on 4 March 2002; and
- (c) shall be inserted in the Books of Adjournal.

## 2. Amendment of the Criminal Procedure Rules 1996

In rule 43.2(1) (applications under the Terrorism Act 2000 or Anti-Terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001) of the Criminal Procedure Rules 1996[^f00002], omit sub-paragraphs (f) to (j).

W Douglas Cullen

Lord Justice General, I.P.D.

Edinburgh

1 March 2002

(This note does not part of the Act of Adjournal)

This Act of Adjournal amends the Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules) 1996 (“the principal Rules”).

Paragraph 2 omits from rule 43.2(1) of the principal Rules provision in relation to applications to the High Court of Justiciary under Schedule 1 to the Anti-Terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001 in connection with terrorist cash.
