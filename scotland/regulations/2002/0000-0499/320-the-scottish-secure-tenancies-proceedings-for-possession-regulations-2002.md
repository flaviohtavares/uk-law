# The Scottish Secure Tenancies (Proceedings for Possession) Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 320

HOUSING

The Scottish Secure Tenancies (Proceedings for Possession) Regulations 2002

Made

26th June 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

27th June 2002

Coming into force

30th September 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 14(4) and 109(2) of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Scottish Secure Tenancies (Proceedings for Possession) Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 30th September 2002.

## 2. Form of notice

The form of the notice contained in the Schedule to these Regulations is hereby prescribed for the purposes of section 14(4) of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001.

## SCHEDULE — NOTICE OF PROCEEDINGS FOR RECOVERY OF POSSESSION

Regulation 2

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/320/images/ssi_20020320_en_001) [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/320/images/ssi_20020320_en_002) [image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/320/images/ssi_20020320_en_003)

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the form of notice to be used by a landlord when notifying its tenant under a Scottish secure tenancy, within the meaning of Part 2 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001, that the landlord may commence possession proceedings in respect of the dwellinghouse which is the subject of the Scottish secure tenancy.

Such notices must be served by a landlord on a tenant and any qualifying occupier, in terms of section 14(2) of that Act.

The notice sets out the time limits within which the landlord is permitted to commence possession proceedings and the grounds on which the landlord is seeking possession.
