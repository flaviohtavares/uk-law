# The Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) (Scotland) Regulations 2002, section 29A

> Text as at 2024-01-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 29A. Restrictions on cultivation[^key-dbe15154c473dbe70c406dd404da5b4b]

*Extent: S*

(1) This regulation applies to—

- (a) a consent granted[^key-4cb21c6bf3419e397cfd55c02c8f4a49] by the Scottish Ministers under section 111(1) of the Act (including a renewed consent granted under that section in accordance with[^key-6ba470af9a922800075ce0a661cfe773] regulation 26),
- (b) a pre-exit[^key-b90dfedd1c2a005c7678edba8b2f3fd2] approved product, or
- (c) an authorisation under the Food and Feed Regulation .[^key-26d1f08537c4df6dcc356cdf426409c6]

[^key-67b6ca917642f4a7f00fad502dd5d089]...

(2)[^key-b833c799257a4962de9d5b46fd42d2c6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The Scottish Ministers may, in the circumstances prescribed in paragraph (7), do any of the following—

- (a) include such limitations and conditions in the consent or renewed consent as they consider appropriate to restrict or prohibit the cultivation of genetically modified organisms in all or any part of Scotland[^key-c3ecd5d27ca2431ae7b17d24354148d7],
- (b) issue a suspension notice in respect of the[^key-3725786c49f71fe9210723dad273d6d0] consent or renewed consent,
- (c) issue a suspension notice in respect of the pre-exit[^key-48c49a61b4f9cafa4bd5df8e2562aa57] approved product, or
- (d) issue a suspension notice in respect of the authorisation under the Food and Feed Regulation.

(4) The effect of a suspension notice issued under paragraph (3)(b) is to suspend the operation of the consent in respect of the cultivation of a genetically modified organism for all or a specified geographical area of Scotland.

(5) The effect of a suspension notice issued under paragraph (3)(c) is to suspend, so far as it applies to the requirement in section 111(1)(a) of the Act for all or a specified geographical area of Scotland, the operation of the exemption in regulation 15(aa)[^key-8a9615e4af21be5376fb448a55fd7f52] (exempt activities), in respect of the cultivation of a specified pre-exit[^key-1f9dcdc3a746a8158829f114e70e2b79] approved product.

(6) The effect of a suspension notice issued under paragraph (3)(d) is to suspend, so far as it applies to the requirement in section 111(1)(a) of the Act for all or a specified geographical area of Scotland, the operation of the exemption in regulation 15(a)[^key-96c0d995367180321c095a16876e091c], in respect of the cultivation of genetically modified food or genetically modified[^key-39cab597629f1f8a5247b421eb5aff47] feed authorised under the Food and Feed Regulation.

(7) The prescribed circumstances are that the Scottish Ministers are of the view that it is necessary to restrict or, as the case may be, prohibit[^key-a69b86ca79ebbc54dd553299b698559e] the cultivation of a genetically modified organism, or a group of genetically modified organisms, on the basis of compelling grounds that—

- (a) may include one or more of the following—
  - (i) environmental policy objectives,
  - (ii) town and country planning,
  - (iii) land use,
  - (iv) socio-economic impacts,
  - (v) the avoidance of the presence of genetically modified organisms in other products [^key-70f0c0a8e07111c8f66c3f1daa876c33]...,
  - (vi) agricultural policy objectives,
  - (vii) subject to paragraph (8), public policy,
- (b) are in conformity with assimilated[^key-8ece0a9d7c3bc959fca455a51d937605] law,
- (c) are proportional,
- (d) are non-discriminatory, and
- (e) do not conflict with the environmental risk assessment carried out pursuant to assimilated[^key-0bdc3f7c5663accbcaecf2c105805989][^key-206ef63bca3be2d69e73ddb61750bda1] law on the deliberate release into the environment of genetically modified organisms (including the Food and Feed Regulation).

(8) Public policy can only be relied upon in combination with at least one other ground included in sub-paragraph (a)(i) to (vi) of paragraph (7).

(9) Before taking any of the actions in paragraph (3), the Scottish Ministers must—

- (a)[^key-7aaf4e6eddc87213cfa8b227fc84e513] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) where they consider appropriate, in the case of a notice served under regulation 29A(3)(d), notify Food Standards Scotland, and
- (c) make the reasons for the decision to take those actions available to the public.

(10) A condition or suspension notice under paragraph (3) must not take effect until the expiry of the period of 75 days starting on the date of the notification under paragraph (9).

(11) The Scottish Ministers may amend a limitation or condition referred to in paragraph (3)(a), or a suspension notice issued under paragraph (3)(b), (c) or (d),[^key-4b095085b862487f4ba2101cb7e8c498] to take account of any comments they receive[^key-d823922697f8c952738ec3c19e4b85b9] prior to its coming into force.

(12) The Scottish Ministers must—

- (a) communicate the limitation,[^key-c5e63a77dc4fee255d69b5752a62299f] condition or suspension notice , as the case may be,[^key-3e1e64cbcd7c79db62f2e8c3d0786c7d] to [^key-97a5981e5f7d45766abb17b7573510d1]... the consent or authorisation holder without delay, and
- (b) make details of the condition or suspension notice available to the public.
