# The Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) (Scotland) Regulations 2002, section 29B

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 29B. Removal of restrictions under regulation 29A[^key-dbe15154c473dbe70c406dd404da5b4b]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may, at any time, do any of the following in respect of measures adopted under regulation 29A—

- (a) vary a limitation or[^key-c326d060746722b0f9c577d7dfd759a9] condition in a consent or renewed consent[^key-723e684916edf9a23c12e8520ec19e89] to remove or modify a restriction or prohibition on the cultivation of genetically modified organisms in all or any part of Scotland[^key-2898f894ecbafc344ecc490fca657cbc],
- (b) withdraw a suspension notice in respect of a consent or renewed consent[^key-1e5258c22611bc1197e0d9031867c39d],
- (c) withdraw a suspension notice in respect of a pre-exit[^key-61623b46c3f1c5d34b4965e9fa4ae6e6] approved product,
- (d) withdraw a suspension notice in respect of an authorisation under the Food and Feed Regulation.

(2) The Scottish Ministers must notify the consent or authorisation holder[^key-dfbc4eb43da6efaaa1d98fb0858ea676] of any action taken under paragraph (1) without delay.
