# The Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) (Scotland) Regulations 2002, section 9

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 9. Exempt activities

*Extent: S*

The cases and circumstances prescribed for the purposes of section 111(7) of the Act in which persons are exempt from the requirements of section 111(1)(a) of the Act, insofar as those requirements apply to the release of genetically modified organisms, are all cases and circumstances in which an approved product , which is permitted to be marketed for a use in pursuance of its consent[^key-3daf91922de29b2f6628b28d0ce297a3] or authorisation[^key-6afccc72c90812eee362de026ba960da], is released in accordance with the limitations, conditions and restrictions[^key-58eb2444c0085bb378ff7665210ea0bf] to which that use[^key-e3bc96ce6afd1895f2bb527f3808bd47] of the product is subject.
