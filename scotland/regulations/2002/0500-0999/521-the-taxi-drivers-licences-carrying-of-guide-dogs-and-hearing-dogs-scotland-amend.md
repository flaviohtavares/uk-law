# The Taxi Drivers' Licences (Carrying of Guide Dogs and Hearing Dogs) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Scottish Statutory Instrument has been printed to correct errors in [S.S.I. 2002/500](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/500) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Instrument.

2002 No. 521

LICENCES AND LICENSING

The Taxi Drivers' Licences (Carrying of Guide Dogs and Hearing Dogs) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002

Made

27th November 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

27th November 2002

Coming into force

2nd December 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 20(1) and (2A) of the Civic Government (Scotland) Act 1982[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Taxi Drivers' Licences (Carrying of Guide Dogs and Hearing Dogs) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 2nd December 2002.

## 2. Amendment of the Taxi Drivers' Licences (Carrying of Guide Dogs and Hearing Dogs) (Scotland) Regulations 2002

(1) The Taxi Drivers' Licences (Carrying of Guide Dogs and Hearing Dogs) (Scotland) Regulations 2002[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 1 (Citation, commencement, extent and interpretation), omit paragraph (2).

(3) In regulation 2 (Prescribed condition), for “1st” substitute “3rd”.

PETER PEACOCK

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

27th November 2002

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Taxi Drivers' Licences (Carrying of Guide Dogs and Hearing Dogs) (Scotland) Regulations 2002.

Regulation 2 corrects a technical inconsistency in relation to the application of regulations 1(2) and 2 of those Regulations.
