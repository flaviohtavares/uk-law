# The Fur Farming (Prohibition) (Scotland) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 519 (C. 27)

ANIMALS

COUNTRYSIDE

The Fur Farming (Prohibition) (Scotland) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2002

Made

25th November 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(1) of the Fur Farming Prohibition (Scotland) Act 2002[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Fur Farming (Prohibition) (Scotland) Act 2002 (Commencement) Order 2002.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of the Fur Farming (Prohibition) (Scotland) Act 2002 is 1st January 2003.

ROSS FINNIE

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

25th November 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 1st January 2003 as the date for coming into force of sections 1 to 5 of the Fur Farming (Prohibition) (Scotland) Act 2002.
