# The Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 548

PUBLIC PASSENGER TRANSPORT

The Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002

Made

12th December 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

13th December 2002

Coming into force

15th January 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 60(1)(e) of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 61(2) of that Act hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 15th January 2003.

## 2. Amendment of the 2001 Regulations

In regulation 13 of the Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2001[^f00002] for “£38” wherever that sum appears there shall be substituted “£40”.

LEWIS MACDONALD

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

12th December 2002

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 13 of the Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2001 by increasing the fee for an application to register or vary particulars of a bus service (other than a community bus service) from £38 to £40.
