# The Potatoes Originating in Egypt (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 518

PLANT HEALTH

The Potatoes Originating in Egypt (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002

Made

21st November 2002

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

22nd November 2002

Coming into force

13th December 2002

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Potatoes Originating in Egypt (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 13th December 2002.

## 2. Amendment of the Potatoes Originating in Egypt (Scotland) Regulations 2001

(1) The Potatoes Originating in Egypt (Scotland) Regulations 2001[^f00002] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In the Schedule–

- (a) in the column headed “*Instrument*”, after the entry “Commission Decision [2001/664/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0664)”, there is added the entry “Commission Decision [2002/903/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0903)”[^f00003]; and
- (b) in the column headed “*Reference*”, after the entry “O.J. No. L 233, 31.08.01, p.49”, there is added the entry “ O.J. No. L 312, 15.11.02, p.28”.

ROSS FINNIE

A member of the Scottish Executive

Pentland House,

Edinburgh

21st November 2002

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations implement in Scotland Commission Decision [2002/903/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0903) (O.J. No. L 312, 15.11.02, p.28) amending Commission Decision [96/301/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1996/0301) (“the principal Decision”) (O.J. No. L 115, 9.5.96, p.47) authorising Member States temporarily to take emergency measures against the dissemination of *Pseudomonas solanacearum* (Smith) Smith (now referred to as *Ralstonia solanacearum* (Smith) Yabuuchi et al.) as regards Egypt. The Regulations add Commission Decision [2002/903/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0903) to the list of instruments amending the principal Decision contained in the Schedule to the Potatoes Originating in Egypt (Scotland) Regulations 2001 ([S.S.I. 2001/421](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2001/421)) (regulation 2). Decision [2002/903/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0903) renews the framework within which potatoes may be imported from Egypt into the territory of the European Community during the 2002/2003 season.

No Regulatory Impact Assessment has been carried out in respect of the extension of these controls to the 2002/2003 season.
