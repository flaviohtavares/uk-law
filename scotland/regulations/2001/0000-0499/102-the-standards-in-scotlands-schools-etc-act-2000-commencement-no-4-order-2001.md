# The Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 102 (C. 4)

EDUCATION

The Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2001

Made

13th March 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 61(2) of the Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2001.

## 2. Appointed day

23rd March 2001 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 55 (abolition of Scottish Joint Negotiating Committee for School Education) of the Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000.

J McCONNELL

A member of the Scottish Executive

Victoria Quay,

Edinburgh

13th March 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 23rd March 2001 section 55 of the Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000 which abolishes the Scottish Joint Negotiating Committee for School Education (“the SJNC”), the statutory collective bargaining forum for Scottish teachers' pay and conditions.

(This note is not part of the Order)
