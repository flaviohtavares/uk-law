>
> . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
>
> **Areas in England[^key-439da6915d3818a68783907a03422330]**
>
> . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
>
> **Areas in Wales[^key-439da6915d3818a68783907a03422330]**
>
> . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
>
> In this Schedule–
>
> B = bovine meat;
>
> S/G = sheep and goat meat;
>
> P = pig meat;
>
> FG = farmed game of species susceptible to food-and-mouth disease;
>
> WG = wild game of species susceptible to food-and-mouth disease;
>
> \+ means that the area is one where the animals in the column may be produced and the meat in question may be produced and dispatched;
>
> \* the three areas listed which consist of Parts of the Highland Council area together comprise the whole of the Highland Council area;
>
> \# the two areas listed which consist of Parts of the Dumfries and Galloway Council area together comprise the whole of the Dumfries and Galloway Council area.
