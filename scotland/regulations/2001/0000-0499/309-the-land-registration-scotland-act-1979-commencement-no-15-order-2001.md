# The Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979 (Commencement No. 15) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 309 (C. 14)

LAND REGISTRATION

The Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979 (Commencement No. 15) Order 2001

Made

11th September 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 30(2) of the Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979 (Commencement No. 15) Order 2001.

## 2. Commencement of provisions

Sections 2(1) and (2) and 3(3) of the Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979 shall come into force on 1st April 2002 in the areas, for the purpose of registration of writs, of the County of Inverness and the County of Nairn.

JAMES WALLACE

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

11th September 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 2002 in the areas of the Counties of Inverness and Nairn, sections 2(1) and (2) and 3(3) of the Land Registration (Scotland) Act 1979. Section 2(1) and (2) provides for the circumstances in which an interest in land shall be registerable and section 3(3) provides that certain persons are to obtain a real right only by registration.

(This note is not part of the Order)
