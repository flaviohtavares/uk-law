PLANT HEALTH

The Plant Health (Great Britain) Amendment (Scotland) Order 2001

Made

26th June 2001

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

28th June 2001

Coming into force

18th September 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^c15929371] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Plant Health (Great Britain) Amendment (Scotland) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 18th September 2001.

(2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

## 2. Amendment of the Plant Health (Great Britain) Order 1993

The Plant Health (Great Britain) Order 1993 [^c15929381] is amended in accordance with the following articles.

## 3.

In article 2(1) (interpretation)–

- (a) for the definition of “Directive [77/93/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0093)” there shall be substituted–

  > “Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029)” means Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) [^c15929391] of 8th May 2000 on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community, as amended by Commission Directive 2001/32 [^c15929401] and Commission Directive [2001/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0033) [^c15929411];

  ;

  and
- (b) in the definition of “protected zone”–
  - (i) for “Directive [77/93/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0093)” in each place where those words occur, there shall be substituted “ Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) ”; and
  - (ii) for “Article 16a” there shall be substituted “ Article 18 ”.

*(This note is not part of the Order)*

This Order amends the Plant Health (Great Britain) Order 1993 (“the principal Order”), in relation to Scotland, so as to implement–

**a)** Commission Directive [2001/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0032), which recognises protected zones exposed to particular plant health risks in the Community, and

**b)** Commission Directive [2001/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0033), which amends certain annexes to Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) on protective measures against the introduction into the Community of organisms harmful to plants or plant products and against their spread within the Community.

The Order amends the geographical definitions of various protected zones (articles 5 to 9 and 11 to 14) in the principal Order. It also updates the references in the principal Order to Council Directive [77/93/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0093) (O.J. No. L 26, 31.1.97, p.20) with Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) (articles 3, 4, 10(a) and 15). Council Directive [79/93/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1979/0093) has now been repealed and is consolidated in Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029).

Articles 12 to 14 of the Order implement Commission Directive [2001/32/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0032) and articles 5 to 9 and 11 of the Order implement Commission Directive [2001/33/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0033).
