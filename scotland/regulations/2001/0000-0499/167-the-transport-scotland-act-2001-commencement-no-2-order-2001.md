# The Transport (Scotland) Act 2001 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Scottish Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of an omission in [S.S.I. 2001/132](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2001/132) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Scottish Statutory Instrument.

2001 No. 167 (C. 9)

TRANSPORT

The Transport (Scotland) Act 2001 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2001

Made

25th April 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 84(2) and (3) of the Transport (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Transport (Scotland) Act 2001 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2001.

## 2. Commencement of Provision

Section 80 of the Transport (Scotland) Act 2001 shall come into force on 1st May 2001.

SARAH BOYACK

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

25th April 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 80 of the Transport (Scotland) Act 2001.

Section 80 provides that a director, manager, secretary or similar officer of a body corporate or a partner of a Scottish partnership may be required to pay a civil penalty under the Transport (Scotland) Act 2001 where an act or omission has been done with their consent or connivance or where the act or omission is attributable to them.

(This note is not part of the Order)
