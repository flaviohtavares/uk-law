# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Declaratory (No. 5) Amendment Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 180

ANIMALS

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Declaratory (No. 5) Amendment Order 2001

Made

16th May 2001

Coming into force at 2.00 p.m. on

16th May 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by article 17(1) of the Foot and Mouth Disease Order 1983[^f00001] and of all powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Declaratory (No. 5) Amendment Order 2001 and shall come into force at 2.00 p.m. on 16th May 2001.

## 2. Extension of infected area

For Schedule 1 of the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Declaratory (No. 5) Order 2001[^f00002], there is substituted the Schedule 1 set out in the Schedule to this Order.

DAVID R DICKSON

A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers

Pentland House,

Edinburgh

16th May 2001
