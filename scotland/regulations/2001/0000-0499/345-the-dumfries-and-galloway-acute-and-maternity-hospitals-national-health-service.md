# The Dumfries and Galloway Acute and Maternity Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 345

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE

The Dumfries and Galloway Acute and Maternity Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001

Made

28th September 2001

Coming into force

30th September 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 12A(1) and 105(6) and (7) of, and paragraphs 1 and 3(1)(c) of Schedule 7A to, the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Dumfries and Galloway Acute and Maternity Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2001 and shall come into force on 30th September 2001.

(2) In this Order “the principal Order” means the Dumfries and Galloway Acute and Maternity Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of the principal Order

For article 4 of the principal Order there is substituted–

> **4. Directors and trustees of the trust**
>
> The trust shall have, in addition to the chairman, 2 trustees and 5 executive directors.

.

SUSAN C DEACON

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

28th September 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Dumfries and Galloway Acute and Maternity Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1993 (“the 1993 Order”). Article 2 amends, by substitution, article 4 of the 1993 Order to reduce the number of trustees from 5 to 2. The number of executive directors remains unchanged.
