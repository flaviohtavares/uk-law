# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2001, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Withdrawal from an agreement

Any provider may withdraw from an agreement by giving the Health Board not less than 3 months notice in writing of their intention to withdraw.
