# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2001, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Variation of an agreement

A Health Board and provider who have entered into an agreement may, after following such procedure as may be set out in the agreement, vary it by a further agreement in writing.
