# The National Health Service (Personal Medical Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2001, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Enforcement of directions as to payments

Any directions, as to payments, made under section 17A(8) of the 1978 Act shall be enforceable in like manner as extract registered decrees arbitral bearing warrant for execution issued by the sheriff court of any sheriffdom in Scotland.
