# The Designation of UHI Millennium Institute (Scotland) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 39

EDUCATION

The Designation of UHI Millennium Institute (Scotland) Order 2001

Made

12th February 2001

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

16th February 2001

Coming into force

1st April 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 44(1) and 60(3) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Designation of UHI Millennium Institute (Scotland) Order 2001 and shall come into force on 1st April 2001.

## 2. Designation of UHI Millennium Institute

(1) UHI Millennium Institute is hereby designated as an institution eligible to receive support from funds administered by the Scottish Higher Education Funding Council.

(2) The designation of UHI Millennium Institute by paragraph (1) of this article shall remain in effect notwithstanding any change of name of that institution.

WENDY ALEXANDER

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

12th February 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates UHI Millennium Institute as an institution which may be funded by the Scottish Higher Education Funding Council. It also makes provision for the designation to continue in the event of a change of name of UHI Millennium Institute.
