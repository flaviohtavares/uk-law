# The Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Scottish Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of an error in [S.S.I. 2001/219](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2001/219) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Scottish Statutory Instrument.

2001 No. 251

PUBLIC PASSENGER TRANSPORT

The Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2001

Made

29th June 2001

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

29th June 2001

Coming into force

1st July 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 6(2)(a), (3)(a), (8)(a), (9), and 8(6) of the Transport Act 1985[^f00001] and by section 60(1)(e) and (f), (1A) and (2) of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 61(2) of the said Act of 1981[^f00003], hereby make the following Regulations:

**1.** These Regulations may be cited as the Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2001 and shall come into force on 1st July 2001.

**2.** (1) Regulation 14 of the Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2001[^f00004] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph (1)(a)(ii), for “9 of the Schedule”, substitute “10 of Schedule 1”.

SARAH BOYACK

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

29th June 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations correct a reference in the Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2001 to “paragraph 9 of the Schedule” which should have read “paragraph 10 of Schedule 1”.
