# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Shorthand Writers in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 136

SHERIFF COURT

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Shorthand Writers in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 2001

Made

30th March 2001

Laid before Parliament

11th April 2001

Coming into force

1st May 2001

The Lords of Council and Session, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Shorthand Writers in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 2001 and shall come into force on 1st May 2001.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Increase in fees

(1) The table of fees set out in Schedule 2 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Witnesses and Shorthand Writers in the Sheriff Court) 1992[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 1 (attendance), for the amounts of £27.83 and £111.30 there are substituted the amounts of £28.82 and £115.25 respectively.

(3) In paragraph 4 (transcripts), for the amounts of £4.56 and £5.57 there are substituted the amounts of £4.72 and £5.77 respectively.

(4) In paragraph 5 (copies), for the amount of £0.38 there is substituted the amount of £0.39.

## 3. Saving

Paragraph 2 does not affect such fees as are chargeable for work done before 1st May 2001.

Rodger of Earlsferry

Lord President, I.P.D.

Edinburgh

30th March 2001

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt increases the fees payable to shorthand writers in the Sheriff Court by about 3.55%. The last increases were in the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Shorthand Writers in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 2000 ([S.S.I. 2000/145](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2000/145)).
