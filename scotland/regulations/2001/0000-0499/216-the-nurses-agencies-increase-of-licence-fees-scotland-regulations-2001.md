# The Nurses Agencies (Increase of Licence Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 216

NURSES AND MIDWIVES

The Nurses Agencies (Increase of Licence Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2001

Made

28th June 2001

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

29th June 2001

Coming into force

1st August 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 28 and 33(4) of the Nurses (Scotland) Act 1951[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Nurses Agencies (Increase of Licence Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2001, and shall come into force on 1st August 2001.

## 2. Level of fees

In regulation 3(4) of the Nurses Agencies (Scotland) Regulations 1961[^f00002] (which prescribes the fees payable upon making a first and subsequent application for a licence to carry on an agency for the supply of nurses) for “£4 4s. 0d.” there shall be substituted “£175” and for “£2 2s. 0d.” there shall be substituted “£125”.

## 3. Revocation

The Nurse Agencies (Increase of Licence Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 1968[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

MALCOLM CHISHOLM

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

28th June 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase from £4.20 to £175 the fee payable on making a first application to a local authority for an annual licence to carry on an agency for the supply of nurses and increase from £2.10 to £125 the fee payable on making a subsequent application in respect of that agency.
