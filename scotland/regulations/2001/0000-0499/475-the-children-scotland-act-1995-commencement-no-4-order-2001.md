# The Children (Scotland) Act 1995 (Commencement No.4) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 475 (C. 23)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The Children (Scotland) Act 1995 (Commencement No.4) Order 2001

Made

20th December 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 105(1) of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Children (Scotland) Act 1995 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2001.

## 2. Appointed Day

22nd January 2002 is the day appointed for the coming into force, so far as not already in force, of section 101 (panel for curators ad litem, reporting officers and safeguarders) of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995.

CATHY JAMIESON

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

20th December 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 22nd January 2002 section 101 of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995, which provides for the establishment of a panel of persons from whom curators ad litem, reporting officers and safeguarders may be appointed.
