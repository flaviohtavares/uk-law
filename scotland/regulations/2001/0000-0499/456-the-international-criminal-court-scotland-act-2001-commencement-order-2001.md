# The International Criminal Court (Scotland) Act 2001 (Commencement) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 456 (C. 20)

INTERNATIONAL CRIMINAL COURT

The International Criminal Court (Scotland) Act 2001 (Commencement) Order 2001

Made

10th December 2001

Coming into force

17th December 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 30(2) of the International Criminal Court (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the International Criminal Court (Scotland) Act 2001 (Commencement) Order 2001.

## 2. Appointed Day

The provisions of the International Criminal Court (Scotland) Act 2001, in so far as not already in force, shall come into force on 17th December 2001.

JAMES R WALLACE

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

10th December 2001

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the provisions of the International Criminal Court (Scotland) Act 2001, in so far as not already in force, into force on 17th December 2001.

Section 30 of the 2001 Act came into force on Royal Assent on 24th September 2001.
