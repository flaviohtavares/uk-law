## 1. Citation, commencement and extent (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d1d34336c0f19e566f4d93ae1c9a9102][^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]

## 2. Amendment of the Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-007123d8481793f8fd02669ea7acb626][^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]

The Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d][^f00002] are amended in accordance with regulations 3 to 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]

**3.**[^key-e6744fa9bc5e3ccf21792b3ee295cae7] After regulation 9 (procedure relating to the withdrawal of approvals), insert– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]

> **9A. Fees (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]**
>
> (1) Subject to paragraphs (5) and (6), an eligible person who applies to the competent body under regulations 5 or 7 to approve an establishment as an establishment on which an establishment activity or new establishment activity may be exercised shall– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
>
> - (a) pay the relevant fee to the competent body; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
> - (b) reimburse the competent body the cost of any laboratory analysis incurred by that body in connection with the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
>
> (2) Any fee payable under paragraph (1)(a) shall be payable at the time the application is submitted to the competent body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
>
> (3) Any reimbursement due under paragraph (1)(b) shall be payable to the competent body on demand. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
>
> (4) In relation to any application under regulations 5 or 7, the competent body need not– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
>
> - (a) take any step specified in regulation 6(1) until the relevant fee has been paid to it; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
> - (b) take any step specified in regulation 6(2) or regulation 7(3)(b) or (4) until, in accordance with paragraph (1)(b) above, it has been reimbursed the cost of any laboratory analysis incurred by it in connection with the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
>
> (5) Where an eligible person makes an application under regulation 5 seeking approval of an establishment as one on which more than one establishment activity may be exercised, that person shall be liable to pay a single relevant fee, which fee shall be the highest fee payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
>
> (6) Where an eligible person makes an application under regulation 7 seeking approval of an establishment as one on which more than one new establishment activity may be exercised, that person shall be liable to pay a single relevant fee, which fee shall be the highest fee payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
>
> (7) In this regulation, “relevant fee” means the fee specified in Column 2 of Part I of Schedule 1A which is opposite to the reference in Column 1 of that Part to an application of the kind being made by the applicant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]

.

**4.**[^key-350f9318c03bacceba94e577b7bcd78a] After regulation 16 (procedure relating to the withdrawal of approvals), insert– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]

> **16A. Fees (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]**

>
> - (a) pay the relevant fee to the competent body; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]

>
> (2) Any fee payable under paragraph (1)(a) shall be payable at the time the application is submitted to the competent body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
>
> (3) Any reimbursement due under paragraph (1)(b) shall be payable to the competent body on demand. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
>
> (4) In relation to any application under regulations 12 or 14, the competent body need not– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
>
> - (a) take any step specified in regulation 13(1) until the relevant fee has been paid to it; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
> - (b) take any step specified in regulation 13(3) or regulation 14(3)(b) or (4) until, in accordance with paragraph (1)(b) above, it has been reimbursed the cost of any laboratory analysis incurred by it in connection with the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
>
> (5) Where an eligible person makes an application under regulation 12 seeking approval as a person who may exercise more than one intermediary activity, that person shall be liable to pay a single relevant fee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
>
> (6) Where an eligible person makes an application under regulation 14 seeking approval as a person who may exercise more than one new intermediary activity, that person shall be liable to pay a single relevant fee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
>
> (7) In this regulation, “relevant fee” means the fee specified in Column 2 of Part II of Schedule 1A which is opposite to the reference in Column 1 of that Part to an application of the kind being made by the applicant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]

.

These Regulations, which extend to Scotland only, amend the Feeding Stuffs (Establishments and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999 ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]S.I. [1999/1872](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1872)). These Regulations implement Council Decision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d] [98/728/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1998/0728)[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d] concerning a Community system of fees in the animal feed sector (O.J. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d] No. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d] L 346, 22.12.98, p.51).

These Regulations amend *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d] S.I. [1999/1872](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1872) by– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]

- (a) inserting new regulation 9A providing for the payment of fees and the reimbursement of the costs of laboratory analyses to the specified approval body (“the competent body”) by a person applying to that body for approval of an establishment under regulation 5 or 7 of that instrument, and making supplementary provisions relating thereto (regulation 3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
- (b) inserting new regulation 16A providing for the payment of fees and the reimbursement of the costs of laboratory analyses to the competent body by a person applying to it for approval as an “intermediary” under regulation 12 or 14 of that instrument, and making supplementary provisions relating thereto (regulation 4); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
- (c) inserting new Schedule 1A specifying the fees payable by virtue of new regulations 9A and 16A (regulation 5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in respect of these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-af1c341815f91ac275644d794a29637d]
