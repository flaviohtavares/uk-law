# The Cereal Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section 1

> Text as at 2023-10-06.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^c-92d97dac6bb0331adcc4a74b3462d6c7]

*Extent: S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Cereal Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2005, and shall come into force on 1st July 2005.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.
