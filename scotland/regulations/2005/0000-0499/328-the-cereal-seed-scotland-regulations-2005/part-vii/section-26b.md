# The Cereal Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section 26B

> Text as at 2023-10-06.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 26B. Transitional provision for official labels on IP completion day[^c-ec08b65fddc6720c9e3bf6f33a5d8806]

*Extent: S*

A label pre-printed before IP completion day which at the date on which it was printed was an official label for the purposes of these Regulations is to be treated as an official label for a package of Basic Seed, Seed of a Certified Generation or seed of a Conservation Variety or, as the case may be, a small package of Pre basic Seed, Basic Seed or Seed of a Certified Generation for the purposes of any use of that label before the end of the period of twelve months beginning with the day after the day on which IP completion day falls.
