> Text as at 2023-10-06.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28. Revocations and supplementary provisions[^c-d021e14be0e92cecf94e9a555696bc98]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the Regulations set out in column 1 of Schedule 8 are revoked to the extent specified in the corresponding entry in relation to those Regulations in column 3 of Schedule 8.

(2) Section 17 (repeal and re enactment) of the Interpretation Act 1978 shall not apply in relation to general licences granted under the Cereal Seeds Regulations 1993.
