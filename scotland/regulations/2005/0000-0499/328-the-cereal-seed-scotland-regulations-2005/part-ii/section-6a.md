# The Cereal Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section 6A

> Text as at 2023-10-06.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 6A. Importation from a country granted equivalence[^key-6ca83d9e434b8272f21e1c5da905a595][^key-391278b6bb8fadb6e1aed93e312042e2]

*Extent: S*

Seed imported from a country granted equivalence[^key-b286e8b26205c79d787522d90da7ba45] must be labelled with a label approved by the Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development for the varietal certification on the control of seed moving in international trade.
