# The Cereal Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section 13

> Text as at 2023-10-06.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 13. Requirement for homogeneity[^c-7ca45dce1d493adbe22d813a12bf35ad]

*Extent: S*

No person shall market seed in accordance with regulation 6(1)(b)(i), 11 or 12 unless it is marketed in a homogeneous seed lot or in part of a homogeneous seed lot.
