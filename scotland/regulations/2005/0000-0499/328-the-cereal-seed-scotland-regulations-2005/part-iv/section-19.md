# The Cereal Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section 19

> Text as at 2023-10-06.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 19. Marketing of Excepted Seed Marketed in Bulk[^c-fb81bcc5f2e125a5028fcdd2c35ff4c2]

*Extent: S*

(1) Regulations 16 and 17 shall not apply to marketing of Excepted Seed Marketed in Bulk where the person marketing the seed delivers a note to the final consumer, not later than the time of delivery of the seed, containing the information given on the official label on the container from which the seed was taken.

(2) A supplier of seed shall prior to 10th January 2006 and prior to 10th January in each year thereafter notify the Scottish Ministers in such manner as the Scottish Ministers may from time to time specify of the quantities of Excepted Seed Marketed in Bulk by that supplier in the period from 1st January to 31st December in the immediately preceding year.
