# The Cereal Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section 18

> Text as at 2023-10-06.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 18. Marketing of unpacketed seed[^c-8887bfce51d34fee18ba698735c25953]

*Extent: S*

A person may sell any seed otherwise than in a package which is sealed, labelled, inscribed or otherwise dealt with in accordance with regulation 16 and regulation 17(1), (2), (4) to (12), (14) and (15) where–

- (a) the seed is sold in a quantity not exceeding 5 kilograms to the final consumer; and
- (b) the seed is taken, in the presence of the final consumer, from a container on which there is clearly and visibly marked, or near which there is clearly and visibly displayed, a statement containing particulars of the matters specified in the relevant paragraph of Part IV of Schedule 6.
