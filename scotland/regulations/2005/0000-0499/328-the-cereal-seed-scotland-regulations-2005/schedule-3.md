# The Cereal Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2005, SCHEDULE 3

> Text as at 2023-10-06.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 3 — PARTICULARS TO BE SPECIFIED IN AN OFFICIAL CERTIFICATE OR A BREEDER'S CONFIRMATION

*Extent: S*

Regulation 2(1), paragraphs 1 and 4 of Part I of Schedule 2 and paragraph 5 of Part II of Schedule 2

### 1. Particulars to be specified in an official certificate[^c-edb18f11b80e3539b1f1f21cf6e46092]

The following particulars shall be specified in an official certificate:–

- (a) name and address of certifying authority;
- (b) applicant’s name, address and, if appropriate, registered or licensed number[^c-6e0ff16487c564af52cf7de3aaa1a5a5];
- (c) seed lot reference number;
- (d) species/variety/category/level, as appropriate;
- (e) net weight of seed lot and number of containers;
- (f) seed treatment, if any; and
- (g) if the seed is Pre basic Seed or Basic Seed and the seed attains a lower percentage of germination than that specified in paragraph 16(1) of Part II of Schedule 4, a statement to that effect.

### 2. Particulars to be specified in a breeder’s confirmation[^c-03711c5b50dbff626a17d76afff3fe5f]

The following particulars shall be specified in a breeder’s confirmation:–

- (a) name and address of issuing authority;
- (b) applicant’s name, address and, if appropriate, registered or licensed number[^c-6a865dc65c5859ae20cd5acd2518c921];
- (c) seed lot reference number;
- (d) species/variety, or AFP number and breeder’s designation if appropriate, category/level, as appropriate;
- (e) net weight of seed lot and number of containers;
- (f) seed treatment, if any;
- (g) a statement that the breeder’s confirmation does not constitute an official certificate and does not permit marketing of the seed; and
- (h) if the seed is Pre basic Seed or Basic Seed and the seed attains a lower percentage of germination than that specified in paragraph 16(1) of Part II of Schedule 4, a statement to that effect.
