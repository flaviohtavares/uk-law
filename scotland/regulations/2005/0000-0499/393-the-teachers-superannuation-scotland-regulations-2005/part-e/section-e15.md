# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section E15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## E15. Duration of retirement pension

Subject to regulations E16 to E19 and E22, a retirement pension under regulation E7 continues to be payable until the death of the person entitled to it.
