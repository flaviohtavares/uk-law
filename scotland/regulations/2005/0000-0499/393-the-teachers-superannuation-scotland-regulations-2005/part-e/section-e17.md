# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section E17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## E17. Withdrawal of incapacity pensions

Where a teacher who becomes entitled on or after 1st April 1997 to payment of a pension under regulation E6(1)(c) re enters pensionable employment, or employment which would be pensionable but for an election under regulation B7 or the lack of an election under regulation B2, the teacher shall cease to be entitled to that pension; and if he or she subsequently becomes entitled to benefits under these Regulations any reckonable service which was taken into account in calculating the pension under regulation E6(1)(c) shall be taken into account in calculating those benefits.
