# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section E12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## E12. Modification for national insurance

Part I of Schedule 9 has effect for modifying the retirement pensions of teachers who were in pensionable employment before 1st April 1980 including retirement pensions that have been actuarially reduced under regulation E7(4).
