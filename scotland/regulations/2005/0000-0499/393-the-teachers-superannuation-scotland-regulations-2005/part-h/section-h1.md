# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section H1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## H1. Teachers' superannuation account

(1) The Scottish Ministers shall keep accounts of the scheme in a form approved by the Treasury.

(2) The accounts shall be open to examination by the Auditor General.
