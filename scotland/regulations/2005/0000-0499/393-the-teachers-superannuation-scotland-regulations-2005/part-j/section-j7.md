# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section J7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## J7. Extension of time

The Scottish Ministers may in any particular case extend, or treat as having been extended, the time within which anything is required or authorised to be done under these Regulations.
