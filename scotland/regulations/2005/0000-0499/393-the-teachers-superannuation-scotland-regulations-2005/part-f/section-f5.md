# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section F5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## F5. Effect of pension sharing on protected rights and guaranteed minimum pension

Where a member has protected rights or a guaranteed minimum in relation to a pension provided by the Scheme, these shall in the case of a pension debit in relation to the member’s rights under the Scheme be reduced in terms of the provisions in sections 10(4) and (5) and 15A of the 1993 Act[^f00055].
