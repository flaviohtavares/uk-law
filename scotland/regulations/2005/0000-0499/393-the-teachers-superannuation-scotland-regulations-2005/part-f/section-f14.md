# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section F14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## F14. Transfer values

The Scottish Ministers shall not pay or accept a transfer value within the meaning of regulations G1 and G2 in respect of any pension credit rights or pension credit benefits.
