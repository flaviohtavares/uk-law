# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section B1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## B1. Full-time service

Subject to regulations B6 to B9, a teacher is in pensionable employment where he or she is in full-time service in an employment specified in Schedule 2.
