# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section B9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## B9. Further employment

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) and regulations B6 to B8, a teacher who is already in receipt of a teacher’s pension, including cases where the annual rate of pension has been reduced to zero in accordance with regulation E18, is in pensionable employment where on or after 1st October 2001 he or she enters into full time or part time service in an employment specified in Schedule 2.

(2) A teacher who is excluded from pensionable employment by virtue of regulation B6(5) of the 1992 Regulations shall not be in pensionable employment unless he or she makes an election under regulation B8.
