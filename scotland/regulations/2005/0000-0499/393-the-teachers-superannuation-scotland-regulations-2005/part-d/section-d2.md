# The Teachers' Superannuation (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section D2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## D2. Exclusion from reckonable service

The following periods do not count as reckonable service–

- (a) any period in respect of which contributions made by the teacher have been returned and not repaid to the Scottish Ministers in pursuance of regulation C14;
- (b) any period in respect of which contributions have not been paid by the teacher unless the Scottish Ministers direct otherwise; and
- (c) any period in respect of which a transfer value has been paid under regulation G1.
