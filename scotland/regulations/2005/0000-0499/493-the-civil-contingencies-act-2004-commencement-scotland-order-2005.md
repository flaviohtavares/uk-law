# The Civil Contingencies Act 2004 (Commencement) (Scotland) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 493 (C.26)

CIVIL CONTINGENCIES

The Civil Contingencies Act 2004 (Commencement) (Scotland) Order 2005

Made

5th October 2005

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 34(2) and (3) of the Civil Contingencies Act 2004[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Civil Contingencies Act 2004 (Commencement) (Scotland) Order 2005.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order “the Act” means the Civil Contingencies Act 2004.

## 3. Provisions coming into force on 6th October 2005

The following provisions of the Act shall come into force on 6th October 2005:–

- (a) section 2(4) and (6);
- (b) section 2(5) in so far as this provision relates to section 2(4) or (6);
- (c) section 3(2);
- (d) section 3(4) and (5) in so far as these provisions relate to section 3(2);
- (e) section 4(3) and (7);
- (f) section 4(4) and (5) in so far as these provisions relate to section 4(3) or (7);
- (g) section 6(2) and (5);
- (h) section 6(3) in so far as this provision relates to section 6(2) or (5);
- (i) section 13(2);
- (j) section 13(3) in so far as this provision relates to section 13(2);
- (k) section 14(2);
- (l) section 17(3) and (5); and
- (m) section 17(1) and (6) in so far as these provisions relate to section 17(3) or (5).

## 4. Provisions coming into force on 14th November 2005

The following provisions of the Act shall come into force on 14th November 2005:–

- (a) section 1(4) in so far as it relates to the Scottish Ministers;
- (b) section 2(1) and (2) in so far as these provisions relate to section 2(4) or (6);
- (c) section 3(3) in so far as this provision relates to sections 2(4) or 3(2);
- (d) section 4(1) in so far as this provision relates to section 4(3) or (7) and only for the purpose of permitting a body specified in paragraph 13 of Schedule 1 to the Act to provide advice and assistance of the kind specified in section 4(1);
- (e) section 4(8) in so far as this provision relates to section 4(3) or (7);
- (f) section 5(2) and (5);
- (g) section 5(3) and (4) in so far as these provisions relate to section 5(2) or (5);
- (h) section 6(6) in so far as this provision relates to section 6(2) or (5);
- (i) section 8;
- (j) section 9(2);
- (k) section 9(3) and (4) in so far as these provisions relate to section 9(2); and
- (l) section 11.

## 5. Provision coming into force on 15th May 2006

Section 4(1) of the Act in so far as this provision relates to section 4(3) or (7) shall come into force on 15th May 2006 to the extent not already in force.

HUGH HENRY

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

5th October 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force the remaining provisions of Part 1 of the Civil Contingencies Act 2004 (“the Act”).

Article 3 brings various provisions into force on 6th October 2005 to enable the Scottish Ministers to make and lay regulations to come into force on the 14th November 2005.

Article 4 commences various provisions of Part 1 of the Act on 14th November 2005 to coincide with the regulations which will come into force on that date. Section 4(1) of the Act is commenced on this date only for the purpose of permitting local authorities to provide advice and assistance to the public in relation to business continuity.

Article 5 commences in full the duty imposed by section 4(1) of the Act on the 15th May 2006.

(This note is not part of the Order)
