# The Fodder Plant Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section 28

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28. Revocations and supplementary provisions[^key-b29c30dbd82fc70ec43a4ab35c4f8035]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the Regulations set out in column 1 of Schedule 8 are revoked to the extent specified in the corresponding entry in relation to those Regulations in column 3 of Schedule 8.

(2) Section 17 (repeal and re-enactment) of the Interpretation Act 1978 shall not apply in relation to general licences granted under the Fodder Plant Seeds Regulations 1993.
