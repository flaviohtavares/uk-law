# The Fodder Plant Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section 1

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^c-192f30c72141725a6929331d71b679e9]

*Extent: S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Fodder Plant Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 1st July 2005.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.
