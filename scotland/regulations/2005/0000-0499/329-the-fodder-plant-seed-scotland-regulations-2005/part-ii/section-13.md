# The Fodder Plant Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section 13

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 13. Requirement for homogeneity[^key-df87445b1fe31a9c0a228349b30f57c0]

*Extent: S*

No person shall market seed in accordance with regulation 6(1)(b)(i) or (ii), 11 or 12 unless it is marketed in a homogeneous seed lot or in part of a homogeneous seed lot.
