# The Fodder Plant Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2005, section 6A

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 6A. Importation from a country granted equivalence[^key-c0953ef13bb91d29351099b617a3bffb][^key-e223ae9d3ab8386366c0d16c58a27671]

*Extent: S*

Seed imported from a country granted equivalence[^key-41da8c77066b6335c09b290e5fdb7021] must be labelled with a label approved by the Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development for the varietal certification on the control of seed moving in international trade.
