# Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) (No. 2) 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 382

COURT OF SESSION

Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) (No. 2) 2005

Made

15th July 2005

Coming into force

1st August 2005

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 57(2) of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) (No.2) 2005 and shall come into force on 1st August 2005.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Registration Appeal Court

(1) The court for hearing appeals under section 57(1)(b) of the Representation of the People Act 1983 shall consist of the Right Honourable Lord Abernethy, the Right Honourable Lord Nimmo Smith and the Honourable Lord Emslie.

(2) The Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) 2005[^f00002] is revoked.

Cullen of Whitekirk

Lord President I.P.D.

Edinburgh

15th July 2005

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt appoints three judges to hear registration appeals under section 57 of the Registration of the People Act 1983 and revokes the Act of Sederunt (Registration Appeal Court) 2005.
