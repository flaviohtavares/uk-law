# The Regulation of Care (Excepted Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2002 Partial Revocation Regulations 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No.96

SOCIAL CARE

The Regulation of Care (Excepted Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2002 Partial Revocation Regulations 2005

Made

1st March 2005

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

2nd March 2005

Coming into force

1st April 2005

The Scottish Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 2(4) of the Regulation of Care (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Regulation of Care (Excepted Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2002 Partial Revocation Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 1st April 2005.

## 2. Amendment to principal Regulations

Regulation 3 (school care accommodation services) of the Regulation of Care (Excepted Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2002[^f00002] is revoked.

RHONA BRANKIN

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

1st March 2005

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke regulation 3 of the Regulation of Care (Excepted Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2002 ([S.S.I. 2002/120](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/120)), which came into force on 1st April 2002. The effect of this is that the school care accommodation services referred to in that regulation now fall within the definition of “school care accommodation service” in section 2(4) of the [Regulation of Care (Scotland) Act 2001 (asp 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2001/8) (“the Act”). Section 2(4) of the Act was brought into force on 1st April 2002 by article 2(a) of the Regulation of Care (Scotland) Act 2001 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional Provisions) Order 2002 ([S.S.I. 2002/162](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/162)).
