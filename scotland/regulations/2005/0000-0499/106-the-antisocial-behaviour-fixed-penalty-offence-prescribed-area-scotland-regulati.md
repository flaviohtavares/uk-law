# The Antisocial Behaviour (Fixed Penalty Offence) (Prescribed Area) (Scotland) Regulations 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 106

ANTISOCIAL BEHAVIOUR

The Antisocial Behaviour (Fixed Penalty Offence) (Prescribed Area) (Scotland) Regulations 2005

Made

1st March 2005

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

3rd March 2005

Coming into force

1st April 2005

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 129(2) of the Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Antisocial Behaviour (Fixed Penalty Offence) (Prescribed Area) (Scotland) Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 1st April 2005.

## 2. Prescribed area

For the purposes of section 129(1) of the Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004, the Tayside combined police area[^f00002] which comprises the police areas for the local government areas of Perthshire and Kinross, Angus and Dundee City[^f00003] is a prescribed area.

HUGH HENRY

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

1st March 2005

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe the Tayside combined police area as an area in which a person may be given a fixed penalty notice if a constable has reason to believe that the person has committed a fixed penalty offence as defined in section 128(1) of the Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004.

The Tayside combined police area comprises the police areas for the local government areas of Perthshire and Kinross, Angus and Dundee City.
