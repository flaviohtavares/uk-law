# The Invergarry-Kyle of Lochalsh Trunk Road (A87) Extension (Skye Bridge Crossing) Toll Order (Revocation) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No.167

ROADS AND BRIDGES

SPECIAL ROADS

The Invergarry-Kyle of Lochalsh Trunk Road (A87) Extension (Skye Bridge Crossing) Toll Order (Revocation) Order 2005

Made

21st March 2005

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

22nd March 2005

Coming into force

2nd May 2005

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 34(1) of the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, with the consent of the concessionaire in terms of section 34(5) of that Act, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Invergarry-Kyle of Lochalsh Trunk Road (A87) Extension (Skye Bridge Crossing) Toll Order (Revocation) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 2nd May 2005.

## 2. Revocations

The following orders are hereby revoked–

- (i) The Invergarry-Kyle of Lochalsh Trunk Road (A87) Extension (Skye Bridge Crossing) Toll Order 1992[^f00002]; and
- (ii) The Invergarry-Kyle of Lochalsh Trunk Road (A87) Extension (Skye Bridge Crossing) Toll Order (Variation) Order 1999[^f00003].

NICOL STEPHEN

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

21st March 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Invergarry-Kyle of Lochalsh Trunk Road (A87) Extension (Skye Bridge Crossing) Toll Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/1501](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1501)) and the Invergarry-Kyle of Lochalsh Trunk Road (A87) Extension (Skye Bridge Crossing) Toll Order (Variation) Order 1999 ([S.S.I. 1999/196](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/1999/196)).
