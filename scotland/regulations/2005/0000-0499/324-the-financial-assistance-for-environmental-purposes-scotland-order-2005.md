# The Financial Assistance for Environmental Purposes (Scotland) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 324

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION

The Financial Assistance for Environmental Purposes (Scotland) Order 2005

Made

8th June 2005

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

9th June 2005

Coming into force

1st July 2005

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 153(4) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Financial Assistance for Environmental Purposes (Scotland) Order 2005 and shall come into force on 1st July 2005.

(2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

## 2. Variation of section 153(1) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990

Section 153(1) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00002] (purposes for which financial assistance may be given) is varied by adding after paragraph (tt)–

> - (uu) the scheme known as the Bio-energy Infrastructure Scheme for Scotland.

.

LEWIS MACDONALD

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

8th June 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies section 153(1) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 so as to enable the Scottish Ministers to give financial assistance to, or for the purposes of, the Bio-energy Infrastructure Scheme for Scotland.
