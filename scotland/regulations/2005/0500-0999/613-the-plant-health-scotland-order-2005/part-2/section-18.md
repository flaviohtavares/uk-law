# The Plant Health (Scotland) Order 2005, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Requirement for plant health movement document

Relevant material which is subject to an agreement in article 12(5) or (6) and which has not been discharged by an inspector pursuant to article 10(1) shall not be moved within Scotland or, where applicable, from Scotland to any other place within the European Community unless it is accompanied by a plant health movement document.
