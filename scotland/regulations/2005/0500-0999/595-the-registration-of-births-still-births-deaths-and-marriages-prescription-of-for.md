# The Registration of Births, Still-births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 595

REGISTRATION OF BIRTHS, DEATHS, MARRIAGES, ETC.

The Registration of Births, Still-births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2005

Made

23rd November 2005

Coming into force

5th December 2005

The Registrar General, in exercise of the powers conferred on him by sections 21(4), 22(1), 32(1) and 54(1)(b) of the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling him in that behalf and with the approval of the Scottish Ministers under section 54 of that Act[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Births, Still-births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2005 and shall come into force on 5th December 2005.

(2) In these Regulations, “the principal Regulations” means the Registration of Births, Still births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Regulations 1997[^f00003].

## 2. Amendment of the principal Regulations

For the form set out in Schedule 8 to the principal Regulations substitute the form set out in Schedule 1 to these Regulations.

**3.** For the form set out in Schedule 10 to the principal Regulations substitute the form set out in Schedule 2 to these Regulations.

**4.** For the form set out in Schedule 17 to the principal Regulations substitute the form set out in Schedule 3 to these Regulations.

These Regulations amend the Registration of Births, Still-Births, Deaths and Marriages (Prescription of Forms) (Scotland) Regulations 1997.

A revised form of Certificate of Registration of Still-birth is substituted to enable more detail to be provided.

Revised Death and Marriage register pages are substituted to reflect the changes brought about by the Civil Partnership Act [2004 (c. 33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/33).
