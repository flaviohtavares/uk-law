# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 10) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 628

PUBLIC HEALTH

CONTAMINATION OF FOOD

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 10) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2005

Made

7th December 2005

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

8th December 2005

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(1) and (2) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 10) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2005 and shall come into force at 1430 hours on 7th December 2005.

## 2. Revocation

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 10) (Scotland) Order 2005[^f00002] and the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 10) (Scotland) Partial Revocation Order 2005[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

S DAVIDSON

A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

7th December 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 10) (Scotland) Order 2005 (“the principal Order”) which prohibited–

- (a) fishing for or taking scallops within the areas designated in the Schedule to that Order (article 4 of the principal Order);
- (b) movement of such scallops out of those areas (article 5 of the principal Order); and
- (c) landing, using in the preparation or processing for supply of food of, supplying and other specified activities in relation to, such scallops from the designated areas (article 6 of the principal Order).

The effect of this Order is to remove the prohibitions in respect of the areas of sea designated as Areas 2 8 in the Schedule to the principal Order.

The prohibitions in respect of the area of sea designated as Area 1 in that Schedule were removed by the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 10) (Scotland) Partial Revocation Order 2005, which is also revoked by this Order.
