# The Firefighters' Pension Scheme Amendment (Scotland) Order 2005, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Amendment of the Firefighters' Pension Scheme (Scotland only)

The Firefighters' Pension Scheme set out in Schedule 2 to the Firemen’s Pension Scheme Order 1992[^f00003], as it has effect in Scotland, shall be amended in accordance with the Schedule to this Order.
