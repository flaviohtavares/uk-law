# The Non-Domestic Rates (Levying) (Scotland) (No. 3) Regulations 2010 Revocation Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 76

Rating And Valuation

The Non-Domestic Rates (Levying) (Scotland) (No. 3) Regulations 2010 Revocation Order 2011

Made

10th February 2011

Coming into force

31st March 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by article 11(4)(b) of the Scotland Act 1998 (Transitory and Transitional Provisions) (Statutory Instruments) Order 1999[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rates (Levying) (Scotland) (No. 3) Regulations 2010 Revocation Order 2011 and comes into force on 31st March 2011.

## 2. Revocation

The Non-Domestic Rates (Levying) (Scotland) (No. 3) Regulations 2010[^f00002] are revoked.

JOHN SWINNEY

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

10th February 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Non-Domestic Rates (Levying) (Scotland) (No. 3) Regulations 2010 which made provision for the amount payable in certain circumstances as non-domestic rates in respect of non-domestic subjects in Scotland.

The Scottish Parliament approved a motion to annul those Regulations on 2nd February 2011.
