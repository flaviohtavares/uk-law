## 50. Unclaimed property

*Extent: S*

Any items of a prisoner's personal property may be disposed of by the Governor in any manner the Governor deems appropriate where the items—

- (a) are held in the prison storage facilities; and
- (b) remain unclaimed by the prisoner for a period of more than 12 months after the prisoner is released from prison other than on temporary release.
