## 13. Recording data about a prisoner’s religion, belief or non-belief[^key-a095e13249479a0a7e26fbffa7d23b9b]

*Extent: S*

The Governor must maintain a record of any declaration made by a prisoner in accordance with rule 44A(2) or (3).
