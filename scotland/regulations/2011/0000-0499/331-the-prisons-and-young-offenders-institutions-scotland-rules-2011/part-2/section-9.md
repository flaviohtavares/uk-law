## 9. Procedure on reception of prisoners

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies to every prisoner on reception.

(2) Every prisoner must be searched in accordance with rule 92.

(3) The Governor may in the course of, or following, a search under paragraph (2)—

- (a) deal with any prohibited article in the possession of the prisoner in accordance with rule 104; and
- (b) deliver any medicines and medical appliances in the possession of the prisoner to a healthcare professional.

(4) An officer may—

- (a) order that a prisoner must take a shower or a bath; or
- (b) on the advice of a healthcare professional, order that a prisoner must not take a shower or bath.
