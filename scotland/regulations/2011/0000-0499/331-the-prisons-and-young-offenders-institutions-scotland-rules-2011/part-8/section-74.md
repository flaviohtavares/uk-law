## 74. Visits by members of the Parole Board for Scotland

*Extent: S*

(1) A prisoner may receive a visit from members of the Parole Board for Scotland where the purpose of that visit is to interview the prisoner in terms of rule 15(3) of the Parole Board (Scotland) Rules 2001 [^c21071751].

(2) Where a prisoner receives a visit in terms of this rule the visit—

- (a) may take place within the sight of an officer; and
- (b) must take place outwith the hearing of any officer unless the prisoner or the visitor otherwise requests.
