# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 68

> Text as at 2026-10-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 68. Visits by ... constables[^c-43c4a7367cb6ad06cf485cbad858469b]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a [^c-43c4a7367cb6ad06cf485cbad858469b]... constable [^c-73b97aa2afc0ca85d521c4f59d3a84ef]... may visit or see a prisoner for one or more of the following purposes—

- (a) to interview the prisoner, provided the prisoner is willing to be interviewed;
- (b) to identify the prisoner;
- (c) to charge the prisoner with an offence.

(2)[^c-fb00018283faf3d10dbe5e5296317022] A [^c-43c4a7367cb6ad06cf485cbad858469b]... constable may only visit or see a prisoner under paragraph (1) on production of the written authority of a procurator fiscal or a chief inspector[^c-3250dde4c7379a1e5d3a09ddc6d77e10] of the Police Service of Scotland.

(3) A visit to a prisoner in terms of paragraph (1)(a) may take place in such area of the prison and under such conditions as the Governor may specify except that such a visit must take place—

- (a) within the sight of an officer; and
- (b) outwith the hearing of an officer unless the prisoner or the visitor requests otherwise.
