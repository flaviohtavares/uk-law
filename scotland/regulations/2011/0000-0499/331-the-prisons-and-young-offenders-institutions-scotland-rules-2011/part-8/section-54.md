## 54. Prisoners' correspondence

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to rules 55 to 57 and 60[^key-3391e2867b154ecc3d69d6a16c51fdb4], a prisoner may send and receive letters and packages by means of the postal service, or otherwise.

(2) Nothing in these Rules gives a prisoner any right to send or receive electronic communications.
