# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 141

## 141. Fees and gratuities

*Extent: S*

Officers and employees must not—

- (a) receive any unauthorised fee, gratuity or other consideration in connection with his or her duties;
- (b) directly or indirectly, have any interest in any contract in connection with the prison or any other prison;
- (c) receive any fee, gratuity or other consideration from or on behalf of any contractor at, or any person tendering for a contract in connection with, a prison.
