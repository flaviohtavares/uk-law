# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 140

> Extent: Scotland.

## 140. Transactions with prisoners or in connection with the prison

*Extent: S*

(1) Officers and employees must not take part in any business or pecuniary transaction with, or on behalf of, a prisoner, except with the authority of the Scottish Ministers.

(2) Officers or employees must not—

- (a) bring in or take out, or attempt to bring in or take out, or knowingly allow to be brought in or taken out, to or for any prisoner; or
- (b) deposit in any place with intent that it shall come into the possession of any prisoner,

any item whatsoever, except with the authority of the Governor.
