## 139. General duty of officers and employees

*Extent: S*

Every officer and employee must—

- (a) act in conformity with these Rules;
- (b) obey any lawful instructions of the Governor and of the Scottish Ministers; and
- (c) inform the Governor promptly of any breach of these Rules and any abuse or impropriety.
