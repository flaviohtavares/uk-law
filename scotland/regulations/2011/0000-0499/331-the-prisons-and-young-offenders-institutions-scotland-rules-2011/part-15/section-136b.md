## 136B. Extension of certain periods of temporary release – coronavirus[^key-bd20d7fde153cd0705e6a40f383e66aa]

*Extent: S*

(1) For the duration of a coronavirus outbreak, rule 136A has effect subject to the following modifications.

(2) In paragraph (2), for “seven” substitute “14”.

(3) In paragraph (4), after “means” insert “home leave or”.
