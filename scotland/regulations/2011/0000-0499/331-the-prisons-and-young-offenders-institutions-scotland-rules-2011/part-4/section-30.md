## 30. Provision of bedding

*Extent: S*

(1) The Governor must—

- (a) provide every prisoner with a separate bed or sleeping plinth;
- (b) provide sufficient bedding as necessary for the prisoner's warmth and health; and
- (c) make provision for the bedding to be changed or cleaned as frequently as required to ensure good hygiene.

(2) A prisoner may request to have only a mattress instead of a bed and the Governor may agree to this.

(3) A prisoner may only have bedding which the Governor has provided under this rule or which the Governor has consented to the prisoner having.
