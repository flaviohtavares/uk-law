## 119. Direction with respect to the disciplinary appeals procedure

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may provide in a direction such conditions as they consider appropriate with respect to the form and manner in which—

- (a) a disciplinary appeal may be made; and
- (b) any decision in relation to a disciplinary appeal may be given.

(2) The Governor must ensure that the following are provided for prisoners—

- (a) supplies of any form specified in a direction made under paragraph (1);
- (b) assistance in the completion of any such form; and
- (c) assistance in writing a disciplinary appeal under rule 118.
