## 111. Reporting breaches of discipline

*Extent: S*

An officer must inform the Governor in writing immediately where he or she—

- (a) becomes aware, or suspects, that a prisoner has committed a breach of discipline; and
- (b) decides to charge the prisoner under rule 112.
