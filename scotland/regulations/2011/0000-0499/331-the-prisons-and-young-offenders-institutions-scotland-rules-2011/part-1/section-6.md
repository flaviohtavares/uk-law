## 6. Elimination of discrimination, harassment and victimisation[^key-bc2620e5d0b1fb0b6c1a0178f2588e53]

*Extent: S*

Subject to the provisions of these Rules or of any direction made for any purpose specified in these Rules, the Governor must seek to eliminate within the prison discrimination, harassment and victimisation[^key-a07accf588c27c3edc560ef7026bede3] against prisoners on the grounds of—

- (a) age;
- (b) disability;
- (c) gender reassignment;
- (d) marriage and civil partnership;
- (e) pregnancy and maternity;
- (f) race;
- (g) religion or belief;
- (h) sex;
- (i) sexual orientation; or
- (j) other status.
