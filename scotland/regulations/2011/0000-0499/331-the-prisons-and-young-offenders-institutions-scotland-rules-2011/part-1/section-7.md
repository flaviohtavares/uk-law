## 7. Availability of Rules, directions etc.[^c-fb3d78cc249e99b94b76140c0cc265c2]

*Extent: S*

The Governor must ensure that a copy of each of the following items is made available for inspection by officers and prisoners in each accommodation block and in the prison library—

- (a) these Rules;
- (b) any direction made under these Rules; and
- (c) any authorisation made under section 41ZB(8) of the Act.
