## 5A. Delegation of Governor functions[^c-d6249b3bd5ce6c7f3445f4cc782dd996]

*Extent: S*

The Governor may authorise the Deputy Governor to exercise, on their behalf, the Governor’s function in section 3C(5)(b) of the 1993 Act.
