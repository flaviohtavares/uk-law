## 125. Direction with respect to complaints procedures

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may provide in a direction such conditions as they consider appropriate with respect to the form and manner in which—

- (a) any complaint as mentioned in rules 122 to 124 may be made or referred; and
- (b) any reply or decision in relation to such a complaint may be given.

(2) The Governor must ensure that the following are provided for prisoners—

- (a) supplies of any form specified in a direction made under paragraph (1);
- (b) assistance in the completion of any such form;
- (c) assistance in making a written complaint under rules 122 to 124; and
- (d) assistance in referring a complaint to the Scottish Public Services Ombudsman[^c-8a6d440e75d5a0ab5d0f2a0028c26c42] under rule 123(10) or 124(6).
