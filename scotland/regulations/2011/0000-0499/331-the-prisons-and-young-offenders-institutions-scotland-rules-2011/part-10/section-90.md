> Text as at 2026-10-29.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 90. Supervision of the prison and control of prisoners

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of these Rules, the Governor is responsible for—

- (a) the supervision of the whole prison; and
- (b) the control of prisoners confined in the prison.

(2) The Governor must, as far as practicable, visit and inspect on a daily basis those parts of the prison where prisoners are employed or accommodated.
