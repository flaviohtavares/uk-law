## 43. Prisoners' welfare

*Extent: S*

The Governor must ensure that every prisoner is given reasonable assistance and facilities to maintain and develop relationships with family and friends and with such other persons and agencies outwith the prison as may best offer the prisoner assistance—

- (a) during the sentence or period of committal;
- (b) in preparation for release from prison other than temporary release; and
- (c) after release from prison.
