## 37. Provision of accommodation for healthcare services

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers must make such arrangements as they consider necessary to provide accommodation within every prison for the provision of healthcare services by healthcare professionals.

(2) For the purposes of this rule “**accommodation**” does not include machinery or equipment used in the provision of healthcare services.
