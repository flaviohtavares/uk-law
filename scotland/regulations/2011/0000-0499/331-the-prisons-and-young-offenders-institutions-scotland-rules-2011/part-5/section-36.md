# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 2011, section 36

> Text as at 2026-10-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 36. Use of vaping products[^c-fa870f9a2d5869eef9a2093a69116a3d][^c-887f4a78eba3489694bd35ec74533fe1]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (3), prisoners may only use a vaping[^c-3ca410c91dbbaf88cf9f1a6ca0502d2e] product in the following areas of a prison—

- (a) in a cell or room in which a single prisoner is accommodated;
- (b) subject to paragraph (2), in a cell or room in which two or more prisoners are accommodated; or
- (c) in the open air, in any place specified in a direction by the Scottish Ministers.

(2) Prisoners must not use a vaping[^c-c417a50aff4fa761b9f35caf52e76363] product in a cell or room which—

- (a) accommodates two or more prisoners; and
- (b) has been designated by the Governor, in accordance with any direction given by the Scottish Ministers, as a cell in which vaping products are not to be used.

(3) Before commencing a check of a cell or a room the Governor may instruct a prisoner that, for a period starting 30 minutes before the check is to proceed and ending when the check is complete, the prisoner must—

- (a) not use a vaping[^c-ee25e4f0640e569ac1090833e69f2ca6] product in the cell or room; and
- (b) open any windows or vents in the cell or room.

(4) No person may use a vaping[^c-cd122739ced171a8431b502d85ef9db6] product in the facilities provided in a prison for mothers and babies.

(5) In this rule, and in paragraph 26 of schedule 1, to “use a vaping[^c-b340d49dafb39a7e689d8d53181d3407] product” means to inhale the vapour or aerosol[^c-9a0237b4a2a268f66736bf31a17f7ea9] from a vaping product.
