## 41A. Accommodation in specified conditions – coronavirus[^key-56bd0138ba33cfca34896600fb80170d]

*Extent: S*

(1) For the duration of a coronavirus outbreak, rule 41 has effect subject to the following modifications.

(2) In sub-paragraph (3)(c), for “72 hours” substitute “14 days”.

(3) In sub-paragraph (12)(a), for “72 hours” substitute “14 days”.
