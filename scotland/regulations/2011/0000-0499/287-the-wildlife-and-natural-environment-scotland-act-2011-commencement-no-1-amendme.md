# The Wildlife and Natural Environment (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Amendment Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.S.I. 2011/279](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2011/279) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

The Scottish Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 43(1) and (2)(a) of the Wildlife and Natural Environment (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Wildlife and Natural Environment (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Amendment Order 2011 and comes into force on 29th June 2011.

## 2. Amendment of Commencement No. 1 Order

(1) The Wildlife and Natural Environment (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2011[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) For article 2(1)(l)(ii) substitute—

> - (ii) the first to the thirteenth, the fifteenth to the eighteenth, and the last entry of Part 2 of the schedule;

.

STEWART STEVENSON

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

Edinburgh

28th June 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects an error in the Wildlife and Natural Environment (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2011 ([S.S.I. 2011/279](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2011/279)).

The effect is that the Finance Act 1924 and the Game Act 1970 are repealed, and the Destructive Imported Animals Act 1932 is not repealed.
