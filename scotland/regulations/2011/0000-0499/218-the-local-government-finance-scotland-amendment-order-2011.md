# The Local Government Finance (Scotland) Amendment Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 218

Local Government

The Local Government Finance (Scotland) Amendment Order 2011

Approved by the Scottish Parliament

Made

2nd March 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

2nd March 2011

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 1(4) of Schedule 12 to the Local Government Finance Act 1992[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with paragraph 2(2) of that Schedule they have consulted with such associations of local authorities as appear to them to be appropriate.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Finance (Scotland) Amendment Order 2011 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is approved by a resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 2. Amendment of the Local Government Finance (Scotland) Order 2011

For Schedule 1 (revenue support grant and distribution of non-domestic rate income 2011-2012) to the Local Government Finance (Scotland) Order 2011[^f00002], substitute the Schedule 1 set out in the Schedule to this Order.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Local Government Finance (Scotland) Order 2011 by replacing Schedule 1 to that Order with the Schedule 1 set out in the Schedule to this Order. Column 2 of the Schedule lists the amount of revenue support grant which is payable to each local authority in Scotland in respect of the financial year 2011-2012. Column 3 of the Schedule lists the amount of non-domestic rate income to be distributed to each local authority in Scotland in respect of the financial year 2011-2012.
