## 2. Sanction for the purpose of general regulation 12 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a910371a5a7e1b4a45e753126b0a14fe][^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670]

(1) This rule applies in relation to any civil proceedings in the sheriff court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670]

(2) For the purposes of this rule, proceedings at first instance are to be regarded as separate from proceedings on appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670]

(3) The sheriff, or as the case may be, the sheriff principal, may, on the motion or incidental application of a party or of his or her own accord, grant sanction for the purpose of general regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670] 12 (employment of counsel) of Schedule *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670] 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment and Further Provisions) 1993 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670][^f00002] in relation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670]

- (a) appearance at any hearing in the proceedings; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670]
- (b) preparation of any document to be lodged in relation to the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670]

(4) Sanction may be granted under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670] (3)—

- (a) before, at, or after the hearing or, before or after the preparation of the document concerned; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670]
- (b) at the time of, or at any time prior to, the disposal of the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670]

(5) Refusal to grant sanction before the hearing has taken place or before the document has been prepared does not prevent such sanction being granted at or after the hearing, or after the preparation of the document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670]

(6) In granting sanction under paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670] (3), the sheriff or, as the case may be, the sheriff principal may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670]

- (a) sanction the employment of one or more pleader; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670]
- (b) grant sanction in relation to one or more hearings in the proceedings or in relation to the preparation of one or more documents to be lodged in the proceedings; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670]
- (c) impose such restrictions in respect of the extent of such sanction as he or she thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9af0f2df554923fbf6dd5541f112a670]

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

General regulation 12 in the Schedule to the Act of Sederunt that makes provision about the fees of solicitors in the sheriff court ([S.I. 1993/3080](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/3080)), provides that the fees of counsel or a solicitor advocate are allowable only where the court has granted sanction for the employment of counsel. This Act of Sederunt contains rules relating to the procedure for obtaining such sanction.

In particular, it provides that the sheriff or, as the case may be, the sheriff principal, may sanction the employment of counsel to appear at any hearing in proceedings before them, or to prepare any document to be lodged in relation to those proceedings. It also provides that such sanction may be provided at the time of, or at any time prior to, the disposal of the proceedings.
