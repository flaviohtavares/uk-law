# The Licensing (Local Licensing Forum) (Scotland) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 130

Licensing (liquor)

The Licensing (Local Licensing Forum) (Scotland) Order 2011

Made

17th February 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

21st February 2011

Coming into force

1st September 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 2(2) of schedule 2 to the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Licensing (Local Licensing Forum) (Scotland) Order 2011 and comes into force on 1st September 2011.

## 2. Amendment of maximum number of members of a local licensing forum

In paragraph 2(1) of schedule 2 to the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005, for “20” substitute “21”.

KENNY MACASKILL

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

17th February 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order increases the maximum number of members in a local licensing forum from 20 to 21.
