# Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules Amendment No.6) (Sexual Offences Prevention Order) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 355  

High Court Of Justiciary

Sheriff Court

Justice Of The Peace Court

Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules Amendment No.6) (Sexual Offences Prevention Order) 2011

Made

7th October 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

11th October 2011

Coming into force

1st November 2011

The Lord Justice General, the Lord Justice Clerk and the Lords Commissioners of Justiciary, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 305 of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation, commencement etc.

(1) This Act of Adjournal may be cited as the Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules Amendment No.6) (Sexual Offences Prevention Order) 2011.

(2) It comes into force on 1st November 2011.

(3) A certified copy of this Act of Adjournal is to be inserted in the Books of Adjournal.

## 2. Form of sexual offences prevention order

(1) The Criminal Procedure Rules 1996[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) In the appendix, for Form 48.2[^f00003] substitute Form 48.2 set out in the Schedule to this Act of Adjournal.

A.C. HAMILTON

Lord Justice General

I.P.D.

Edinburgh

7th October 2011

## SCHEDULE

Paragraph 2(2)

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Act of Adjournal)

This Act of Adjournal amends Form 48.2 contained in the appendix of the Criminal Procedure Rules 1996; in consequence of the commencement of section 100 of the Criminal Justice and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2010, which amended the Sexual Offences Act 2003.
