# The Public Appointments and Public Bodies etc. (Scotland) Act 2003 (Amendment of Specified Authorities) (No. 2) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 236

Public Bodies

The Public Appointments and Public Bodies etc. (Scotland) Act 2003 (Amendment of Specified Authorities) (No. 2) Order 2011

Made

21st March 2011

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(2)(a) of the Public Appointments and Public Bodies etc. (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 18(4) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Public Appointments and Public Bodies etc. (Scotland) Act 2003 (Amendment of Specified Authorities) (No. 2) Order 2011 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Amendment of the list of specified authorities

(1) Schedule 2 to the Public Appointments and Public Bodies etc. (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00002] (the specified authorities) is amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In the listing of the specified authorities—

- (a) under the heading “*Executive bodies*” insert, at the appropriate place, “Architecture and Design Scotland”;
- (b) under the heading “*Advisory bodies*” omit “Architecture and Design Scotland”.

FIONA HYSLOP

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

21st March 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends schedule 2 to the Public Appointments and Public Bodies etc. (Scotland) Act 2003 to the effect that “Architecture and Design Scotland” is listed under the heading of Executive bodies rather than Advisory bodies.
