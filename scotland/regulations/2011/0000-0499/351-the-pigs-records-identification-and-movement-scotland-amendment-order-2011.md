# The Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Scottish Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.S.I. 2011/327](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2011/327) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that instrument.

2011 No. 351

Animals

Animal Health

The Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2011

Made

5th October 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

7th October 2011

Coming into force

4th November 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1 and 8(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2011 and comes into force on 4th November 2011.

## 2. Amendment of the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Scotland) Order 2011

For article 3 (notifications etc.) of the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Scotland) Order 2011[^f00002] substitute—

> **3. Notifications etc.**
>
> Any notice, walking licence, notification, authorisation or approval under this Order must be in writing, may be made subject to conditions and may be amended, suspended or revoked by notice in writing at any time.

.

RICHARD LOCHHEAD

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

5th October 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Scotland) Order 2011.

Article 3 of that Order made provision to the effect that a notice could be made otherwise than in writing. This was a defect. Article 2 of this Order substitutes a new article 3 that omits that provision.
