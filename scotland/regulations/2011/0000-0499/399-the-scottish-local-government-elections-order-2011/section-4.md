# The Scottish Local Government Elections Order 2011, section 4

> Text as at 2025-10-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 4. Application of Part III of the Representation of the People Act 1983[^key-ec50ad5c4f71a150649e647c3869013c]

*Extent: S*

Part III[^f00013] of the 1983 Act applies to elections of councillors for local government areas with exception of the following provisions—

- (a) sections 120 to 126;
- (b) section 128(3)(a) and (4);
- (c) sections 130 to 133;
- (d) sections 135 and 135A;
- (e) section 144; and
- (f) section 145A.
