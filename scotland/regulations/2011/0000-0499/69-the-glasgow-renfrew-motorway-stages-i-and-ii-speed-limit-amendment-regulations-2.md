# The Glasgow Renfrew Motorway (Stages I and II) (Speed Limit) Amendment Regulations 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 69

Road Traffic

The Glasgow Renfrew Motorway (Stages I and II) (Speed Limit) Amendment Regulations 2011

Made

8th February 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

10th February 2011

Coming into force

2nd May 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 17(2) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 134(2)[^f00002] of that Act they have consulted with such representative organisations as they think fit.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Glasgow Renfrew Motorway (Stages I and II) (Speed Limit) Amendment Regulations 2011 and come into force on 2nd May 2011.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Glasgow Renfrew Motorway (Stages I and II) (Speed Limit) Regulations 1976 (“the 1976 Regulations”) and partially revoke the Glasgow Renfrew Motorway (Stages I and II) (Speed Limit) Amendment Regulations 1980. The 1976 Regulations regulate the speed on the Glasgow Renfrew Motorway (M8). The amendments extend an existing 50mph zone on the main carriageway and apply 50mph speed limits to certain connecting roads (regulation 2).
