# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2011, section V3

> Text as at 2025-10-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## V3. Pension sharing order activates pension sharing creating pension debits and credits

*Extent: S*

On the coming into effect of a pension sharing order—

- (a) the transferor's shareable rights under the Scheme become subject to a debit of the appropriate amount (“pension debit”[^c21042361]) as defined in regulation V4, and
- (b) the transferee becomes entitled to a credit of that amount (“pension credit”[^c21042371]) as against the Scottish Ministers.

## Annotations

[^c21042361]: Marginal citation: This definition is that in section 29(1)(a) of the 1999 Act and under corresponding Northern Ireland legislation.

[^c21042371]: Marginal citation: This definition is that in section 29(1)(b) of the 1999 Act and under corresponding Northern Ireland legislation.
