> Text as at 2025-10-31.

> Extent: Scotland.

## V18. Pension Transfers

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers must not accept any transfer value into the Scheme or pay any transfer value out of the Scheme in respect of any pension credit rights or pension credit benefits.
