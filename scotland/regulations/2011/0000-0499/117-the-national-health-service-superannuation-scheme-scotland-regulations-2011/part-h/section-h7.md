# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2011, section H7

> Text as at 2025-10-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## H7. Increase of child allowance when child not dependent on surviving parent or spouse, civil partner or scheme partner of a parent[^key-c6f09024682a28254ba027a5b0af4d5d]

*Extent: S*

(1) If a member dies leaving a dependent child and there is a surviving parent (or spouse, civil partner or scheme partner[^key-2203cd90cab75946a0ade063311a5c54] of the parent) and at least one dependent child who is not being maintained by the surviving parent (or spouse, civil partner or scheme partner of the parent), the Scottish Ministers may increase the amount of the child allowance that would otherwise be payable under these Regulations.

(2) The allowance may, at the Scottish Minister's discretion, be increased up to an amount equal to the amount that would have been payable if there were no surviving parent (or spouse, civil partner or scheme partner[^key-0bb1ba94563f4f274d6ca370accc041d] of the parent).
