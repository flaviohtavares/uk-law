# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2011, section B5

> Text as at 2025-10-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## B5. Joining or rejoining this Section of the scheme after opting out[^key-90c9eaba7a6906dca9ee267270890a07]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (3), a person[^key-25ec4365a1db9c3a45bbc0bc1b87cc7f] who has opted out of this Section of the scheme in accordance with regulation B4(1) may, if in NHS employment, join or[^key-386c1b061b400089b5c884541ac2ef57] rejoin this Section of the scheme by giving notice in writing to the employing authority.

(2) The person[^key-f654158d409138136c8ef2a3ee3f1412] referred to in paragraph (1) is to be included in this Section of the scheme on the first day of the pay period immediately after the notice referred to in paragraph (1) is received or such later date (which must be the first day of a pay period) as is specified in the notice.

(3)[^key-7286043f7bd512f70206c920936b7d3d] A person who has opted out may not rejoin this Section of the scheme during any period of absence from work for any reason.
