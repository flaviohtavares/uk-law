# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2011

> Text as at 2025-10-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

2011 No. 117

National Health Service

The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2011

Made

15th February 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

18th February 2011

Coming into force

1st April 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of, and Schedule 3 to, the Superannuation Act 1972 [^c21041511], and of all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 10(1) of that Act, these Regulations are made with the consent of the Treasury [^c21041521].

In accordance with section 10(4) of that Act, the Scottish Ministers have consulted with such representatives of persons likely to be affected by these Regulations as appear to them to be appropriate.[^key-c1f9f87515ec72075da3e765cb4bac05][^key-7b8856d77a1157f4598bf2a73cfeada4][^key-511f1946451324e707617106f58d13d0][^key-8db355cf2712d9f1c9b9c6486055e19a]

These Regulations consolidate, with minor drafting amendments, the provisions of the National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 1995 which provide for the superannuation of persons engaged in the National Health Service in Scotland (other than those subject to the National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2008).
