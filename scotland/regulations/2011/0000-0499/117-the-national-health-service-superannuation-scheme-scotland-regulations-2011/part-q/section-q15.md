# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2011, section Q15

> Text as at 2025-10-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## Q15. Effect of death or early payment of pension after option exercised under regulation Q8, Q10 or Q11[^key-9b20b94a7a52bbabe391504e1765cc68]

*Extent: S*

(1) If a member in respect of whom an option under regulation Q8, Q10 or Q11 has been exercised dies before the end of the period of 12 months beginning with the date on which the option was exercised—

- (a) an amount equal to the contributions paid under the option must be paid—
  - (i) in the case of an option under regulation Q8 or Q10, to the member's personal representatives; and
  - (ii) in the case of an option under regulation Q11, to the employing authority which made the contribution; and
- (b) regulation Q14(4) does not apply.

(2) Subject to regulation Q13(5) and (6),[^key-a1975a9c7eaea279f9df93df5e12beff] if a member in respect of whom an option under regulation Q8 has been exercised dies after the end of the period of 12 months beginning with the date on which the option was exercised and before the end of the contribution option period, regulation Q14(4) applies as if all contributions due after the date of death had been made.

(3) If a member in respect of whom an option under regulation Q8, Q10 or Q11 has been exercised becomes entitled to a pension under regulation E3 as a result of a claim made before the end of the period of 12 months beginning with the date on which the option was exercised—

- (a) regulation Q14(2) and (4) does not apply; and
- (b) an amount equal to the contributions paid under the option must be paid—
  - (i) in the case of an option under regulation Q8 or Q10, to the member; and
  - (ii) in the case of an option under regulation Q11, to the employing authority which made the contribution.

(4) Subject to regulation Q13(5) and (6),[^key-c0351bcb2b8530139c138c7ebc7fb99e] if a member in respect of whom an option under regulation Q8 has been exercised becomes entitled to a pension under regulation E3 before the end of the contribution period as a result of a claim made after the end of the period of 12 months beginning with the date on which the option was exercised, regulation Q14(2) and (4) applies as if all contributions under the option had been made.

(5) If a member in respect of whom an option under regulation Q8, Q10 or Q11 has been exercised—

- (a) becomes entitled to a pension under regulation E6, E7, or E11;
- (b) becomes entitled to a pension under regulation E1 or E12 before reaching the age of 60; or
- (c) becomes entitled to a pension under regulation E1 after age 60 but before the member's chosen birthday ; or[^key-f2f427d2b3b50b59ad7e12e27fe866cf]
- (d)[^key-fafeb8aa8bb0eed85cadfcceaf305f32] becomes entitled to a pension under regulation E11A before the member’s chosen birthday.

the increase in the member's pension which would otherwise be due under regulation Q14(2) or regulation Q16 is reduced.

(6) The amount of the reduction is such amount as the Scottish Ministers determine, after consulting the scheme actuary, to be appropriate by reason of the payment of the increase before the member reaches the member's chosen birthday.
