# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2011, section N2

> Text as at 2025-10-31.

> Extent: Scotland.

## N2. Transfers in from health service schemes

*Extent: S*

In the case of a member transferring in service from another health service scheme the time limit referred to in regulation N1 will not apply in respect of service from such a scheme.
