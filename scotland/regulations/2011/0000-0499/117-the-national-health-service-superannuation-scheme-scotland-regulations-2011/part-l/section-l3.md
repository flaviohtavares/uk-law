# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2011, section L3

> Text as at 2025-10-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## L3. Preserved pension to count as qualifying service

*Extent: S*

If a member leaves pensionable employment with a preserved pension and, after returning, again leaves pensionable employment without becoming entitled to an immediate pension under regulation E1 to E11 (pensions for members), the member will be entitled to a preserved pension under regulation E12 in respect of the period after the break whether or not the member has 2 years' qualifying service in respect of that period.
