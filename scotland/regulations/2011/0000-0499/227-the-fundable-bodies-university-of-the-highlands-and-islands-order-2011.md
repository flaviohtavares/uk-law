# The Fundable Bodies (University of the Highlands and Islands) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 227

Education

The Fundable Bodies (University of the Highlands and Islands) Order 2011

Made

17th March 2011

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7(1) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 7(1) of that Act, the Scottish Further and Higher Education Funding Council has approved the modification made in this Order.

In accordance with section 34(4) of that Act, a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Fundable Bodies (University of the Highlands and Islands) Order 2011 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Modification of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005

In schedule 2 to the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005 (fundable bodies)[^f00002]—

- (a) omit “UHI Millennium Institute”; and
- (b) after “University of Glasgow” insert “University of the Highlands and Islands”.

MICHAEL W RUSSELL

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

17th March 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005 (“the Act”) established the Scottish Further and Higher Education Funding Council and made provision relating to the funding of bodies which provide further and higher education. Those bodies are described in the Act as “fundable bodies” and are listed in schedule 2 to the Act.

This Order updates the list of fundable bodies by amending schedule 2 to the Act to reflect the change of name of UHI Millennium Institute to University of the Highlands and Islands.
