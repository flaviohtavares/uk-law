# The Water Environment (Relevant Enactments and Designation of Responsible Authorities and Functions) (Scotland) Order 2011

Made

25th October 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

27th October 2011

Coming into force

1st December 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(8) of the Water Environment and Water Services (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^c-992e458e994d839f38d7f12776a90556]

(1) This Order may be cited as the Water Environment (Relevant Enactments and Designation of Responsible Authorities and Functions) (Scotland) Order 2011 and comes into force on 1st December 2011.

(2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Water Environment and Water Services (Scotland) Act 2003.

## 2. Specification of relevant enactments[^c-0a39c49618830fcc31d8a81ac18e0062]

The enactments referred to in Schedule 1 are specified as relevant enactments for the purposes of section 2 of the Act[^f00002].

## 3. Designation of responsible authorities and functions[^c-9728f55e50a84184222d1f9100bc8b03]

(1) The public bodies and office holders referred to in column 1 of Schedule 2 are designated as responsible authorities for the purposes of Part 1 of the Act.

(2) The functions conferred upon each responsible authority by the enactments shown in column 2 of Schedule 2 are, insofar as they relate to the water environment, specified as designated functions of that authority for the purposes of Part 1 of the Act.

## 4. Revocation[^c-c95e48ce1fce65c19ab5f121ef6a34ca]

The Water Environment (Relevant Enactments and Designation of Responsible Authorities and Functions) Order 2008[^f00003] is revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the enactments set out in Schedule 1 as relevant enactments for the purposes of section 2 of the Water Environment and Water Services (Scotland) Act 2003 (“the Act”).

The effect of specification is to require the Scottish Ministers and SEPA to exercise their functions under those enactments so as to secure compliance with the requirements of Directive [2000/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0060) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23rd October 2000 establishing a framework for Community action in the field of water policy[^f00022] and to act in accordance with the requirements of section 2(4) of the Act.

This Order also designates certain public bodies and office-holders as responsible authorities for the purposes of Part 1 of the Act. Article 3(1) of the Order designates the bodies and office-holders set out in column 1 of Schedule 2 for this purpose.

The functions of those bodies and office-holders which are conferred by the enactments set out in column 2 of Schedule 2, and which relate to the water environment, are specified by Article 3(2) of the Order as designated functions for the purposes of Part 1 of the Act.
