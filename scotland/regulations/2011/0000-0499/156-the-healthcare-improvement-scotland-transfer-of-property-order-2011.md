# The Healthcare Improvement Scotland (Transfer of Property) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 156

National Health Service

The Healthcare Improvement Scotland (Transfer of Property) Order 2011

Made

22nd February 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

24th February 2011

Coming into force

31st March 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 132(1) of the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Healthcare Improvement Scotland (Transfer of Property) Order 2011 and comes into force on 31st March 2011.

## 2. Transfer of Property held by NHS Quality Improvement Scotland to Healthcare Improvement Scotland

On 1st April 2011 all property, rights and liabilities to which NHS Quality Improvement Scotland[^f00002] was entitled or subject immediately before that date transfer to and vest in Healthcare Improvement Scotland[^f00003].

S ROBISON

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

22nd February 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the transfer, on 1st April 2011, of the property and liabilities held by the Special Health Board NHS Quality Improvement Scotland as at 31st March 2011, to Healthcare Improvement Scotland.

## Annotations

[^f00001]: Footnote: [2010 asp 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2010/8).

[^f00002]: Footnote: NHS Quality Improvement Scotland is a Special Health Board constituted by article 3 of the NHS Quality Improvement Scotland Order 2002 ([S.S.I. 2002/534](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/534)). NHS Quality Improvement Scotland will be dissolved on 1st April 2011 by [S.S.I. 2011/36](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2011/36). “Special Health Board” is defined in section 108 of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act [1978 (c.29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/29) (“the 1978 Act”).

[^f00003]: Footnote: Healthcare Improvement Scotland is established by section 10A of the 1978 Act, section 10A was inserted into that Act by section 108 of the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010.
