# The NHS Quality Improvement Scotland (Dissolution) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 36

National Health Service

The NHS Quality Improvement Scotland (Dissolution) Order 2011

Made

20th January 2011

Coming into force

1st April 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(1)(b) and 105(6) and (7) of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the NHS Quality Improvement Scotland (Dissolution) Order 2011 and comes into force on 1st April 2011.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS Quality Improvement Scotland and consequent revocations

(1) NHS Quality Improvement Scotland[^f00002] is dissolved.

(2) The enactments in the Schedule to this Order are revoked.

SHONA ROBISON

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

20th January 2011

## SCHEDULE — REVOCATIONS

Article 2(2)

**1.** The NHS Quality Improvement Scotland Order 2002[^f00003].

**2.** The NHS Quality Improvement Scotland (Transfer of Officers) Regulations 2002[^f00004].

**3.** The NHS Quality Improvement Scotland (Amendment) Order 2005[^f00005].

**4.** The NHS Quality Improvement Scotland (Establishment of the Scottish Health Council) Regulations 2005[^f00006].

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order dissolves the Special Health Board known as NHS Quality Improvement Scotland and revokes the enactments relating to its constitution.
