# The Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 8 (C. 2)

Children And Young Persons

The Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2011

Made

18th January 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 206(2) of the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2011.

## 2. Appointed day

19th January 2011 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011—

- (a) section 1 (the National Convener);
- (b) paragraph 8(7) of schedule 1 (the National Convener).

ADAM INGRAM

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

18th January 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 19th January 2011 section 1 of, and paragraph 8(7) of schedule 1 to, the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (“the Act”).

The Act received Royal Assent on 6th January 2011. Part 20 (other than section 203) came into force on Royal Assent.
