# The Police (Retention and Disposal of Motor Vehicles) (Scotland) Amendment Revocation Regulations 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 429

Police

The Police (Retention and Disposal of Motor Vehicles) (Scotland) Amendment Revocation Regulations 2011

Made

5th December 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

6th December 2011

Coming into force

21st December 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 127 of the Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Police (Retention and Disposal of Motor Vehicles) (Scotland) Amendment Revocation Regulations 2011 and come into force on 21st December 2011.

## 2. Revocation

The Police (Retention and Disposal of Motor Vehicles) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2011[^f00002] are revoked.

KENNY MACASKILL

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

5th December 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Police (Retention and Disposal of Motor Vehicles) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2011, which would but for the revocation have come into force on 22nd December 2011.
