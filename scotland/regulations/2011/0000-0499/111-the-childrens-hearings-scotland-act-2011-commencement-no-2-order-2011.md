# The Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 111 (C. 10)

Children And Young Persons

The Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2011

Made

16th February 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 206(2) of the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2011.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 and “the Schedule” means the Schedule to this Order.

## 2. Appointed day

(1) The day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Act specified in column 1 of the Schedule is 18th April 2011.

(2) The subject matter of each provision of the Act specified in column 1 is listed in column 2 of the Schedule.

(3) Where a particular purpose is specified in column 3 of the Schedule, in relation to a provision, that provision only comes into force for the purpose specified in that column.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force certain provisions of the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (“the Act”). Article 2 brings into force on 18th April 2011 all the provisions of the Act listed in the Schedule to the Order. These provisions relate to the establishment, functions and powers of the new body Children’s Hearings Scotland, and the powers of the Scottish Ministers relating to the National Convener and the new body. This is the second Commencement Order in respect of the Act.

NOTE AS TO EARLIER COMMENCEMENT ORDERS

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Act received Royal Assent on 6th January 2011 and Part 20 (other than section 203) came into force on that day.
