# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme etc. (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2011, section 127

> Text as at 2013-07-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 127. Amendment of regulation 7[^c-69f7091d80eb218cad951ac00b7ab505]

*Extent: S*

In regulation 7 (partial surrender of annual allowance)—

- (a) for “Superannuation Scheme” substitute “2011”;
- (b) for “(allocation of part of pension)” substitute “(allocation of pension by member for benefit of dependant)”; and
- (c) for “Superannuation Regulations” substitute “2011 Regulations”.
