> Text as at 2013-07-01.

## 123. Amendment of regulation 3[^key-a6049c7221f99009152080376d22c781]

*Extent: S*

In regulation 3 (persons to whom the Regulations apply)—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “E3 of the Superannuation Scheme” substitute “E6 of the 2011”; and
- (b) in paragraph (b)—
  - (i) for “E3B of the Superannuation Scheme” substitute “E8 of the 2011”; and
  - (ii) for “E3” substitute “E6”.
