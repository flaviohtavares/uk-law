# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme etc. (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2011, section 128

> Text as at 2013-07-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 128. Amendment of regulation 8[^key-c79b4d9c2abf07f449464f30a4632956]

*Extent: S*

In regulation 8 (compensation payable to widow, widower, surviving civil partner or dependants)—

- (a) in paragraph (2)—
  - (i) for “Superannuation Scheme” substitute “2011”; and
  - (ii) for “Superannuation Regulations” substitute “2011 Regulations”;
- (b) in paragraph (3), for “Superannuation Scheme” substitute “2011”; and
- (c) in paragraph (4)—
  - (i) for “Superannuation Scheme” in both places where it occurs substitute “2011”; and
  - (ii) in the full out, for “Superannuation Regulations” substitute “2011 Regulations”.
