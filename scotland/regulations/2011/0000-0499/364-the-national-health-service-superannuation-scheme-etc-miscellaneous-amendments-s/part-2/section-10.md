# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme etc. (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2011, section 10

> Text as at 2013-07-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 10. Amendment of regulation L1[^key-bfb3e6a807d8660e155371a106937306]

*Extent: S*

In regulation L1 (treatment of pensionable service of early leavers returning to pensionable employment), in paragraph (3)(b)—

- (a) for “(early retirement pension (ill health))” substitute “(early retirement pension on ill health grounds (pre 1st April 2008))”;
- (b) for “(ill health pension on early retirement)” substitute “(early retirement pension on ill health grounds (post 1st April 2008))”; and
- (c) for the words “but” to the end of the paragraph substitute “but there will be no such increase to any of the member’s pensionable service if the member’s pensionable service before and after the break is treated separately”.
