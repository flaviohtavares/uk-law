## 7. Amendment of regulation F2[^c-4aee6c8e8e1a98aa15c931bd248829f9]

*Extent: S*

In regulation F2 (lump sum when member dies after pension becomes payable), in paragraph (7), for “has” substitute “died on or before 5th April 2011 and had”.
