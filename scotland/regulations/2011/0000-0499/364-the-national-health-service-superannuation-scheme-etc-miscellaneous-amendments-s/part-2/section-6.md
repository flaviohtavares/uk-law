# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme etc. (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2011, section 6

> Text as at 2013-07-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 6. Amendment of regulation E13[^c-5a0c0ca97e763e8e2d05fc2dbe1dbba5]

*Extent: S*

In regulation E13 (lump sum on retirement)—

- (a) in paragraph (2), after “pension” insert “to which the member is entitled under regulations E1 to E11 (and in relation to which any additional pension purchased under regulations Q8, Q10 or Q11 is disregarded)”;
- (b) in paragraph (4)—
  - (i) after “member” insert “on becoming”; and
  - (ii) after “regulation” insert “on or before 5th April 2011”.
