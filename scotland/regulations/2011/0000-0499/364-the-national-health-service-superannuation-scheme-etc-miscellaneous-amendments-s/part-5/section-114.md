# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme etc. (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2011, section 114

> Text as at 2013-07-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 114. Amendment of regulation 10[^key-391e7c2a3433be6010518dd136230421]

*Extent: S*

In regulation 10 (outward transfers)—

- (a) in paragraph (4)(a), for “1995” substitute “2011”;
- (b) in paragraph (4A)(a), for “(early leaver without preserved pension) of the 1995” substitute “(applications for transfer value payments: time limits) of the 2011”; and
- (c) in paragraph (8)—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (b)(i), for “60” substitute “75”; and
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (b)(ii), for “1995” substitute “2011”.
