## 112. Amendment of regulation 6[^key-6939f97a247c31f821d926322589b615]

*Extent: S*

In regulation 6 (circumstances in which elections cease to have effect)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a), for “E6 of the 1995” substitute “E12 of the 2011”;
- (b) in paragraph (1)(c), for “1995” substitute “2011”; and
- (c) in paragraph (1)(d), for “1995” substitute “2011”.
