# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme etc. (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2011, section 117

> Text as at 2013-07-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 117. Amendment of regulation 15[^c-fc6e789113ad3a116334c6b78fb06d6f]

*Extent: S*

In regulation 15 (payments by Secretary of State)—

- (a) in paragraph (3)(b)(ii)(aa), for “1995” substitute “2011”;
- (b) in paragraph (9), for “E6 of the 1995” substitute “E12 of the 2011”; and
- (c) in paragraph (10)(a), for the words from “E2(7)” to “1995” substitute “E2(8) (early retirement pension on ill health grounds (pre 1st April 2008)), E3(7) (early retirement on ill health grounds (post 1st April 2008)) or E12(7) (preserved pensions) of the 2011”.
