# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme etc. (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2011, section 111

> Text as at 2013-07-01.

## 111. Amendment of regulation 4[^key-8b571710abffaa2bc722f0ab207c7ff3]

*Extent: S*

In regulation 4 (payment and amount of additional contributions)—

- (a) in paragraph (3)(b), for “1995” substitute “2011”; and
- (b) in paragraph (3A)—
  - (i) in the definition of “employer”, for “1995” substitute “2011”; and
  - (ii) in the definition of “salary”, for “1995” substitute “2011”.
