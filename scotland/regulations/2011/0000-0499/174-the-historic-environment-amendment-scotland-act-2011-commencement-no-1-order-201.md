# The Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 174 (C. 14)

Ancient Monuments

Town And Country Planning

The Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2011

Made

3rd March 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 33(2) and (3) of the Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2011.

## 2. Appointed Day

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011 specified in column 1 of the Schedule (the subject matter of which is described in column 2 of the Schedule) is 30th June 2011.

(2) Where a purpose is specified in column 3 of the Schedule in relation to any provision, that provision comes into force on 30th June 2011 for that purpose only.

FIONA HYSLOP

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

3rd March 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 30th June 2011 sections 1, 5, 7 to 9, 11 to 13, 27, 29(1) and 30 of the Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011 (“the Act”) for all purposes and sections 6, 15, 20, 23, 24 and 26 for the limited purposes of enabling the Scottish Ministers to make, or to make provision by means of, subordinate legislation.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 23rd February 2011. Sections 31 to 33 of the Act came into force the following day.
