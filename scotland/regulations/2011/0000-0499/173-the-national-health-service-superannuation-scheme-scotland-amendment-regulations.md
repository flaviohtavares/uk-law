# The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

These Regulations have been made to correct errors in [S.S.I. 2011/117](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2011/117) and are being issued free of charge to all known recipients of these Regulations.

2011 No. 173

National Health Service

The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2011

Made

3rd March 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

7th March 2011

Coming into force

1st April 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 10 and 12 of, and Schedule 3 to, the Superannuation Act 1972[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 10(1) of that Act, these Regulations are made with the consent of the Treasury[^f00002].

In accordance with section 10(4) of that Act, the Scottish Ministers have consulted with such representatives of persons likely to be affected by these Regulations as appear to them to be appropriate.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2011.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1st April 2011.

## 2. Amendment of the National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2011

The National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2011[^f00003] are amended as follows—

- (a) in regulation A2(4) in the definition of “the 2008 Section” omit “Part 1 or 2 (whichever is applicable) of”;
- (b) in regulation B1(3) for “Secretary of State” substitute “Scottish Ministers”;
- (c) in regulation E6(1)(a) at the end of sub-paragraph (iii) insert “or”;
- (d) in regulation G16(3)(a) for “these Regulations” substitute “the National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2008[^f00004]”; and
- (e) in paragraph 1 of schedule 1, in the definition of “Common Services Agency” for “the 1972 Act” substitute “the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00005]”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make minor drafting amendments to the National Health Service Superannuation Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2011, which provide for the superannuation of persons engaged in the National Health Service in Scotland (other than those subject to the National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2008).
