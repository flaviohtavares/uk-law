# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Shorthand Writers in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 166  

Sheriff Court

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Shorthand Writers in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 2011

Made

24th February 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

28th February 2011

Coming into force

2nd May 2011

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Shorthand Writers in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 2011 and comes into force on 2nd May 2011.

(2) A certified copy of this Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Increase in fees of shorthand writers

(1) The Table of Fees set out in the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Witnesses and Shorthand Writers in the Sheriff Court) 1992[^f00002] is amended in accordance with the following sub-paragraph.

(2) In Schedule 2 (shorthand writers’ fees)—

- (a) in paragraph 1 (attendance), for the amounts of £37.87 and £151.39 substitute the amounts of £39.25 and £156.85 respectively;
- (b) in paragraph 4 (transcripts), for the amounts of £6.20 and £7.59 substitute the amounts of £6.40 and £7.85 respectively; and
- (c) in paragraph 5 (copies), for the amount of £0.50 substitute the amount of £0.52.

## 3. Saving

Paragraph 2 of this Act of Sederunt shall not affect fees chargeable for work done, or outlays incurred, before 2nd May 2011.

A. C. HAMILTON

Lord President

I.P.D.

Edinburgh

24th February 2011

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt increases the fees payable to shorthand writers in the Sheriff Court by 3.6%. The last increase was effected by the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Shorthand Writers in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 2009 ([S.S.I. 2009/103](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2009/103)).

This Act of Sederunt does not apply to fees chargeable for work done before it comes into force.
