# The Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 372 (C. 35)

Ancient Monuments

Town And Country Planning

The Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2011

Made

27th October 2011

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

31st October 2011

Coming into force

1st December 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 33(2) and (3) of the Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2011 and comes into force on 1st December 2011.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions (to the extent not already in force) of the Historic Environment (Amendment) (Scotland) Act 2011 specified in column 1 of the Schedule (the subject matter of which is described in column 2 of the Schedule) is 1st December 2011.

FIONA HYSLOP

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

27th October 2011

(This note is not part of the Order)
