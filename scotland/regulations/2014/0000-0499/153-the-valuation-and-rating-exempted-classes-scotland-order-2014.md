# The Valuation and Rating (Exempted Classes) (Scotland) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 153

Rating And Valuation

The Valuation and Rating (Exempted Classes) (Scotland) Order 2014

Made

28th May 2014

Coming into force

1st June 2014

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Valuation and Rating (Exempted Classes) (Scotland) Act 1976[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 1(6) of that Act, the Scottish Ministers have consulted with such associations of local authorities and associations of persons as appeared to them to be concerned and with such local authorities and persons with whom consultation appeared to them to be desirable.

In accordance with section 1(7) of that Act, a draft of this Order has been laid before, and approved by resolution of, the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Valuation and Rating (Exempted Classes) (Scotland) Order 2014 and comes into force on 1st June 2014.

## 2. Exempted lands and heritages

(1) No lands and heritages consisting of the pipeline first described in paragraph (3) shall be entered in the valuation roll.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1) “the pipeline” means the pipe or system of pipes, together with any associated apparatus and works, so far as lying seaward of the low water mark of ordinary spring tides.

(3) The description is the pipeline from the Shetland Gas Plant Terminal, Sullom Voe, Shetland Islands, used for the export of gas via Firths Voe, Shetland to the North Sea pipeline known as the FRIGG UK Association (FUKA) Pipeline.

JOHN SWINNEY

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

28th May 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the exemption from rating of a pipeline for transportation of gas from the Shetland Gas Plant Terminal at Sullom Voe, Shetland. The pipeline is known as the Shetland Island Regional Gas Export (SIRGE) System pipeline.
