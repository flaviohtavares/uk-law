# The Lands Tribunal for Scotland Amendment (Fees) Rules 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 24

Lands Tribunal

The Lands Tribunal for Scotland Amendment (Fees) Rules 2014

Made

23rd January 2014

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

27th January 2014

Coming into force

6th March 2014

The Scottish Ministers make the following Rules, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3(6) and (12)(e) of the Lands Tribunal Act 1949[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Lands Tribunal for Scotland Amendment (Fees) Rules 2014 and come into force on 6th March 2014.

## 2. Table of Fees

At the end of the Table of Fees contained in Schedule 2 to the Lands Tribunal for Scotland Rules 1971[^f00002] insert the fees set out in the table in the Schedule to these Rules.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules amend the Lands Tribunal for Scotland Rules 1971 from 6th March 2014.

They add new fees in respect of applications, referrals and other procedure under the Long Leases (Scotland) Act 2012 and the Land Registration etc. (Scotland) Act 2012.
