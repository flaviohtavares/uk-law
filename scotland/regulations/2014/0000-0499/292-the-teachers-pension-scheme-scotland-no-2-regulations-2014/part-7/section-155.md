## 155. Death grant: death of pension credit member before pension becomes payable[^key-b37cd4a2806e1986591c103de4fd5e6c]

*Extent: S*

(1) A death grant is payable under this regulation on the death of a pension credit member (D) who dies before a pension credit retirement pension becomes payable to D under regulation 152.

(2) The amount of the death grant is found by—

- (a) taking the amount of credited pension which would have become payable to D at the date of D’s death if D had reached normal pension age; and
- (b) multiplying that amount by 2.25.
