## 211. Provision of benefit information statements to members[^key-34f0438dba1ec4e77cbd570cb960565a]

*Extent: S*

The scheme manager must provide an annual benefit information statement to each active member of this scheme in accordance with section 14 of the Act and Treasury directions made under that section.
