# The Teachers’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) ( No. 2) Regulations 2014, section 88

> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 88. The new employment condition[^key-bf00bce35f9eabc00db7e56c5a43384f]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person (P) meets the new employment condition if—

- (a) P leaves all eligible employment;
- (b) within 6 months after leaving all eligible employment, P enters a new employment; and
- (c) on the day after the entitlement day referred to in regulation 89 the reduced annual rate of P’s pensionable earnings in the new employment does not exceed 80% of the average annual rate of P’s pensionable earnings for the last 6 months of the previous employment.

(2) In this regulation—

- “a new employment” means—
  - (a) one or more new eligible employments;
  - (b) one or more employments with an employer mentioned in any paragraph in Part 2 of Schedule 1 that is not an eligible employment where P’s normal duties in the employment include providing education or services ancillary to education (other than administrative services); or
  - (c) one or more new eligible employments and one or more employments mentioned in paragraph (b); and
- “previous employment” means one or more previous eligible employments.
