> Text as at 2025-08-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 92. Phased retirement proportion[^c-1873c21b1f5884a78efa64e254fc7419]

*Extent: S*

(1) A phased retirement application must specify the proportion of accrued earned pension for which payment is claimed (“phased retirement proportion”).

(2) The phased retirement proportion must not exceed 75%.
