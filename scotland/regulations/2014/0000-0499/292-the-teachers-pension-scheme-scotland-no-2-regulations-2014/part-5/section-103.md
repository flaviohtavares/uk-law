> Extent: Scotland.

## 103. Interpretation[^key-07978ec83db9b6f5f9c598fbe76f2b45]

*Extent: S*

In this Chapter—

- “ill-health application” means an application under regulation 159 for payment of—
  - (a) an ill-health pension; and
  - (b) if applicable, a total incapacity pension;
- “medical report” means a medical report accompanying an ill-health application.
