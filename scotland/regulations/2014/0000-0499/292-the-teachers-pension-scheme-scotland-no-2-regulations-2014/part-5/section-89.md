# The Teachers’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) ( No. 2) Regulations 2014, section 89

> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 89. Meaning of “entitlement day” (phased retirement pension)[^key-4529460dd8518d96ee3b185d909c6a85]

*Extent: S*

The entitlement day for a phased retirement pension is—

- (a) if a person (P) meets the reduced annual rate condition, the day on which the reduced annual rate takes effect; or
- (b) if P meets the new employment condition, the day on which P enters a new employment.
