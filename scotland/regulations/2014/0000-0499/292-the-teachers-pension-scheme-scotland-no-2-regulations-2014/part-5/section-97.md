> Text as at 2025-08-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 97. Meaning of “entitlement day” (premature retirement pension)[^c-b7c7dbe031e419b7a4f373c0c88bc1bd]

*Extent: S*

The entitlement day for a premature retirement pension is the day after a person leaves all eligible employment.
