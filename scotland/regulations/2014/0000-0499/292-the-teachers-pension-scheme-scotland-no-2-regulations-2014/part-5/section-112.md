# The Teachers’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) ( No. 2) Regulations 2014, section 112

> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 112. Meaning of “entitlement day” (total incapacity pension)[^c-69ffe18e6baa8cce453cd050ff038f41]

*Extent: S*

The entitlement day for a total incapacity pension payable with an ill-health pension is the same as the entitlement day for the ill-health pension.
