# The Teachers’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) ( No. 2) Regulations 2014, section 120

> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 120. Meaning of “entitlement day” (short-service annuity)[^key-ee65777e70435c955980206c714bed13]

*Extent: S*

The entitlement day for a short-service annuity is the date specified in an application under regulation 159 for payment of the annuity, which must be no earlier than 6 weeks after the day on which the application is made.
