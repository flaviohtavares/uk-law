# The Teachers’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) ( No. 2) Regulations 2014, section 37

> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 37. Pensionable earnings for the purpose of accruing benefits[^key-c439d857b60f5b6f3085daa3800d42d9]

*Extent: S*

For the purpose of calculating a member’s (P’s) amount of earned pension for a pay period—

- (a) if P’s pensionable earnings are reduced during sick leave, carer’s leave,[^key-7253deaac42864cd365f32dec34a9565] adoption leave, maternity leave, parental leave, shared parental leave[^key-3c094a74ac796663cea163b48e7457d1] , parental bereavement leave,[^key-df506cb9d46f8e659e75d1a4725cf423] or paternity leave, P’s pensionable earnings for that period include P’s notional pensionable earnings; and
- (b) if an election under regulation 25 has effect or is taken to have effect for that period, P’s pensionable earnings for that period are an amount equal to P’s notional salary.
