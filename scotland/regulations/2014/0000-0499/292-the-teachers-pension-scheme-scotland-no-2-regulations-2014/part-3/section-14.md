## 14. Meaning of “P’s automatic enrolment date” and “P’s automatic re-enrolment date”[^c-2a9417c42196f4da4b41aa8a91090d56]

*Extent: S*

For the purpose of this Part—

- (a) “P’s automatic enrolment date” is the date on which section 3 of PA 2008 applies to a person (P) in relation to P’s eligible employment; and
- (b) “P’s automatic re-enrolment date” is a date determined under regulation 12 of the Occupational and Personal Pension Schemes (Automatic Enrolment) Regulations 2010[^f00019] in relation to P’s eligible employment.
