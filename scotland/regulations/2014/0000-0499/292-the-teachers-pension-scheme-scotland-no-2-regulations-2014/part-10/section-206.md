## 206. Application for acceptance of a club transfer value[^c-078693c6ebb803f4230e7ca6cdcfaec1]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person (P) may apply for payment of a club transfer value to be accepted from another club scheme.

(2) The application must—

- (a)[^c-18da196834f1cdcef62c6207dea866e4] be made within 12 months after the day on which P enters pensionable service under this scheme;
- (b) be by written notice to the scheme manager;
- (c) specify the pension scheme from which the club transfer value is to be received; and
- (d) meet any other conditions the scheme manager requires.
