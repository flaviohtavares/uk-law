# The Teachers’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) ( No. 2) Regulations 2014, section 123

> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 123. General[^key-d51a1c9a55eaaf2f70038a3460cee2ac]

*Extent: S*

In this Part—

- “member” means a member other than a pension credit member;
- “survivor’s benefits” means any of the following—
  - (a) a death grant;
  - (b) a survivor’s pension;
- “survivor’s pension” means a surviving adult pension, additional (surviving adult) pension or child pension.
