# The Teachers’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) ( No. 2) Regulations 2014, section 126

> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 126. Meaning of “dies out of service”[^key-4108c270143e9775ad613632c14a1db7]

*Extent: S*

A member (D) dies out of service for the purpose of survivor’s benefits if—

- (a) D does not die in service; or
- (b) D does not die as a pensioner member.
