# The Teachers’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) ( No. 2) Regulations 2014, section 138

>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 138. Supplementary death grant payable on death of pensioner member[^key-f80d2ab1966645c82f18b92de1898f3e]

*Extent: S*

(1) A death grant may be payable[^key-0b1bf16536bcac5d100f3ff13210ddb2] under this regulation (“supplementary death grant”) if—

- (a) a member (D) dies as a pensioner member; and
- (b) AR is greater than AP.

(2) The amount of the death grant is AR-AP,

where—

- AR is 5 x D’s annual rate of retirement pension payable as at the date of D’s death; and
- AP is the total amount of pension which was payable to D up until D’s death.
