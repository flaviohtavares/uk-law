> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 127. Meaning of “dies as a pensioner member”[^c-cb06ff0e33d4906864c523e050e53e66]

*Extent: S*

In this Part, a member (D) dies as a pensioner member if a retirement pension becomes payable before D’s death.
