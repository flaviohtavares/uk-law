## 2. Establishment of Scottish Teachers’ Pension Scheme 2015[^c-e87b209055e9665f7e07cef189857bef]

*Extent: S*

(1) These Regulations establish a scheme for the payment of pensions and other benefits to or in respect of teachers in Scotland.

(2) For the purpose of these Regulations, a person in an eligible employment is a teacher.

(3) This scheme is to be known as the Scottish Teachers’ Pension Scheme 2015.
