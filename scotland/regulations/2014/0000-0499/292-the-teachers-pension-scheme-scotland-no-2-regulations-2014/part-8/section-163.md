## 163. Amount of lump sum payable under regulation 162[^key-25181121ec7e4bfca015bd5f1b1ff94a]

*Extent: S*

The amount of a lump sum payable under regulation 162 must—

- (a) be a multiple of £12; and
- (b) not exceed P’s permitted maximum.
