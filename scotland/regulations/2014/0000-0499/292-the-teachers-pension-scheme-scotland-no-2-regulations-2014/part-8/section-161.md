# The Teachers’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) ( No. 2) Regulations 2014, section 161

> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 161. Member declaration[^c-9944724b4a6cd4c14e94ebae3e948f33]

*Extent: S*

(1) The scheme manager may not pay a member a lump sum under this Chapter unless the member declares in writing that, on payment of the lump sum, paragraph 3A of Schedule 29 to FA 2004[^f00025] would not apply.

(2) The declaration must be—

- (a) signed by the member;
- (b) in a form specified by the scheme manager; and
- (c) provided by a date determined by the scheme manager.
