# The Teachers’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) ( No. 2) Regulations 2014, section 78

> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 78. Separate account for each capacity of membership[^key-64e73632ce3c2e08f41a297c500eacf4]

*Extent: S*

(1) If a pension credit member is also an active member, deferred member or pensioner member, the pension credit member’s account is in addition to the other account or accounts to be established under this Part in respect of the member in the other capacity.

(2) If a pension credit member has rights under this scheme which are attributable, directly or indirectly, to pension credit derived from the rights of more than one pension debit member of this scheme, a separate account is to be established under this regulation in respect of the pension credit attributable to the rights of each such pension debit member.
