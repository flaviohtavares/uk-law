## 72. Meaning of “commutation amount”[^key-8fc308c9beca66359996eaa0bfa419e6]

*Extent: S*

In these Regulations, “commutation amount”, in relation to any pension, means the amount of pension exchanged for a lump sum under regulation 162, 165 or 168.
