> Text as at 2025-08-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 68. Meaning of “phased retirement earned pension”[^c-824134046bde062fd2da5de5db12d7e0]

*Extent: S*

The amount of phased retirement earned pension is the sum of—

- (a) the amount of accrued earned pension calculated under regulation 45(2); and
- (b) the leaver index adjustment for that amount.
