# The Teachers’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) ( No. 2) Regulations 2014, section 53

> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 53. Ill-health pension ceases to be payable[^c-66b5386b27aac6a25a2da6a1e473e886]

*Extent: S*

If an ill-health pension ceases to be payable to a person (P) under regulation 111 or 115 and P re-enters pensionable service—

- (a) the pensioner member’s account must be closed;
- (b) the active member’s account must be re-established and credited with an amount equal to the annual rate of pension payable immediately before the ill-health pension ceased to be payable.
