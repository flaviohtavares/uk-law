> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 61. Account to specify amount of accrued earned pension and leaver index adjustment[^key-62a8c277245f4ce345a5f12115180563]

*Extent: S*

The deferred member’s account must specify—

- (a) the amount of accrued earned pension; and
- (b) the leaver index adjustment for that amount.
