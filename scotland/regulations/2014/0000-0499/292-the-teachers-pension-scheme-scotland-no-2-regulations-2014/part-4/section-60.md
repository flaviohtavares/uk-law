# The Teachers’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) ( No. 2) Regulations 2014, section 60

> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 60. Establishment of deferred member’s account[^c-399cd511fef52b9393d684abe72bcfda]

*Extent: S*

When an active member leaves all pensionable service and becomes a deferred member—

- (a) the active member’s account must be closed; and
- (b) a pension account for the deferred member must be established (“deferred member’s account”).
