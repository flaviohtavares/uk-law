## 47. Closure of pension accounts on transfer out or repayment of balance of contributions[^c-9ebf2eef4c4216d98580dd8bee182daf]

*Extent: S*

All pension accounts relating to a member (P) (other than a pension credit member’s account) must be closed if—

- (a) a transfer payment is made in respect of P’s accrued rights under this scheme; or
- (b) P is repaid the balance of contributions under regulation 183.
