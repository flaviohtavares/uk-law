# The Teachers’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) ( No. 2) Regulations 2014, section 71

>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 71. Meaning of “actuarial adjustment”[^key-f99be7be39e6f2c5d643e3aff3b6334b]

*Extent: S*

In these Regulations, “actuarial adjustment” means the actuarial adjustment that is applied when calculating the annual rate of pension payable—

- (a) to a member who, on the entitlement day for a pension other than an age retirement pension or an ill-health pension, has not reached normal pension age; or
- (b) to an active member who becomes a pensioner member after reaching normal pension age.
