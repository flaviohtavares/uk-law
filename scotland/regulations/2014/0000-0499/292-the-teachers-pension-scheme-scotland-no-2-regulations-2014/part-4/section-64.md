# The Teachers’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) ( No. 2) Regulations 2014, section 64

> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 64. Account established after ill-health pension ceases to be payable[^c-5a27316a546552df41ae75e5614b3e74]

*Extent: S*

If an ill-health pension ceases to be payable to a person (P) under regulation 111 or 115 and P does not re-enter pensionable service—

- (a) a deferred member’s account must be established; and
- (b) that account must be credited with an amount equal to the annual rate of pension payable immediately before the ill-health pension ceased to be payable.
