>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 65. Account established after phased retirement pension ceases to be payable[^c-ba12c3c5686cfebc402e02f9dc332e92]

*Extent: S*

If a phased retirement pension ceases to be payable under regulation 94 and P does not re-enter pensionable service—

- (a) a deferred member’s account must be established; and
- (b) the scheme manager must increase the amount of accrued earned pension by the phased retirement proportion.
