> Extent: Scotland.

## 69. Meaning of “phased retirement additional pension”[^c-66e3868378b9f89a3873b981b9193b9a]

*Extent: S*

The amount of phased retirement additional pension is the sum of—

- (a) the amount of accrued additional pension calculated under regulation 45(3); and
- (b) the leaver PIA index adjustment for that amount.
