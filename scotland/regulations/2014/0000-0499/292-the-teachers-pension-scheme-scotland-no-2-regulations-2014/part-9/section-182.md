> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 182. Meaning of “entitlement day” (repayment of balance of contributions)[^key-a6279772cff44761bfcf914cfd77b5a9]

*Extent: S*

The entitlement day for repayment of the balance of contributions is one month after the last day of pensionable service.
