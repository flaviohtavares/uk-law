# The Public Water Supplies (Scotland) Regulations 2014, section 42

> Text as at 2023-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 42. Approved persons

*Extent: S*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies to the authority conferred on Scottish Water by—

- (a) regulation 40(1); or
- (b) a warrant under regulation 41(1).

(2) The authority is exercisable on behalf of Scottish Water by any person approved (in writing) by Scottish Water for the purposes mentioned in regulation 40(1).
