# The Public Water Supplies (Scotland) Regulations 2014, section 44

> Text as at 2023-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 44. Obstruction offence

*Extent: S*

(1) A person commits an offence if the person intentionally obstructs an approved person in the exercise—

- (a) in an emergency, of the authority mentioned in regulation 42(1)(a); or
- (b) of the authority mentioned in regulation 42(1)(b).

(2) In paragraph (1)—

- “**an emergency**” has the same meaning as it has in regulation 40(6); and
- “**approved person**” means person approved as mentioned in regulation 42(2).

(3) A person who commits an offence under paragraph (1) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
