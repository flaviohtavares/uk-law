# The Public Water Supplies (Scotland) Regulations 2014, section 52

> Text as at 2023-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 52. Authorised departures

*Extent: S*

Regulation 24(4)(c) does not apply in relation to an application for authorisation made under regulation 20 of the 2001 Regulations [^c22302851] which was—

- (a) made before 1st January 2015; and
- (b) not determined by the Scottish Ministers before that date.

## Annotations

[^c22302851]: Marginal citation: By virtue of section 17(2)(b) of the [Interpretation Act 1978 (c.30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/30), an application made under regulation 20 of the Water Supply (Water Quality) (Scotland) Regulations 2001 has effect as if made under regulation 24 of these Regulations.
