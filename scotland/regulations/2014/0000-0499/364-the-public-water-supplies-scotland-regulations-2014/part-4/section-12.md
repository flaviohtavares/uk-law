# The Public Water Supplies (Scotland) Regulations 2014, section 12

> Extent: Scotland.

## 12. Sampling at service reservoirs

*Extent: S*

Scottish Water must take, or cause to be taken, from each of its service reservoirs in each week in which the service reservoir is in use, one sample for analysis—

- (a) for determining the concentration of residual disinfectant;
- (b) for testing for compliance with the prescribed concentration and value for—
  - (i) coliform bacteria (item 3) in Table A; and
  - (ii) *Escherichia coli* (item 4) in Table A; and
- (c) for determining whether the prescribed concentration or value for colony count (item 5) in Table C (in so far as the point of monitoring is a service reservoir) is complied with.
