## 49. Amendment of the Building (Scotland) Act 2003

*Extent: S*

In the Building (Scotland) Act 2003 [^c22302761], in paragraph 5(2)(n) of schedule 1, for the words from “Table B” to the end, substitute “ Part 1 of Table B in Schedule 1 to the Public Water Supplies (Scotland) Regulations 2014 (SSI 2014/364) ”.
