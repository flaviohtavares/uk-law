# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Amendment (No. 3) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of errors in [S.S.I. 2014/110](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2014/110) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2014 No. 149

Pensions

Made

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

30th May 2014

Coming into force

28th June 2014

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 34(1) to (4) and 60(2) of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 34(5) of that Act, they have consulted with such persons as they considered appropriate.

## 1. Citation, commencement and effect

(1) This Order may be cited as the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Amendment (No. 3) Order 2014.

(2) This Order comes into force on 28th June 2014, but—

- (a) articles 3(b) and 7(a) have effect from 22nd May 2014; and
- (b) articles 3(a), 5(a), 7(b) and 8 have effect from 23rd May 2014.

## 2. Amendment of the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Order 2007

Schedule 1 to the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Order 2007[^f00002] is amended in accordance with articles 3 to 5.

**3.** In Part 1 (citation and interpretation), in paragraph (1) of rule 2 (interpretation)—

- (a) after the definition of “independent qualified medical practitioner”, insert—

  > - “initial date” means 23rd May 2014;

  ; and
- (b) omit the definition of “the Scheme Actuary”.

**4.** In Part 11 (pensionable pay, pension contributions and purchase of additional service)—

- (a) in rule 6A (election to purchase service during the limited period)—
  - (i) in paragraph (2)(a), omit “his”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (4), omit “his”;
  - (iii) in paragraph (5), for “his” substitute “the member’s”; and
  - (iv) in paragraph (6), omit “his”; and
- (b) in paragraph (7) of rule 6B (election to purchase service during the limited period: supplemental provision), for “he” substitute “the member”.

**5.** In Part 12 (transfers into and out of the Scheme)—

- (a) in paragraph (3) of rule 11A (transfer of accrued rights under the 1992 Scheme to special membership of this scheme), for “rule 5(13)” substitute “rule 5A(13) of Part 11”; and
- (b) in rule 18 (converting membership from standard membership to special membership – special pensioner members)—
  - (i) in paragraph (5), omit “his”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (6)(b), after “his” (where it first occurs) insert “or her”; and
  - (iii) in paragraph (6)(b), omit “his” (where it second occurs).

## 6. Amendment of the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2014

The Schedule to the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2014[^f00003] is amended in accordance with articles 7 and 8.

**7.** In paragraph 1—

- (a) omit sub-paragraph (a)(iv) and (vii); and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (a)(viii), for “opt in” substitute “normal retirement age”.

**8.** In paragraph 12—

- (a) in the heading, for “Part 14” substitute “Part 13”; and
- (b) for “Part 14”, substitute “Part 13”.
