# The Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (Argyll and Bute Council) Designation Order 2014

> Text as at 2020-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

2014 No. 84

Road Traffic

The Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (Argyll and Bute Council) Designation Order 2014

Made

26th March 2014

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

28th March 2014

Coming into force

12th May 2014

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 1(1), 2(1) and 3(3) of Schedule 3 to the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with paragraphs 1(1)(d) and 2(1)(c) of that Schedule they have received an application for this Order from Argyll and Bute Council.

In accordance with paragraphs 1(3) and 2(3) of that Schedule they have consulted the chief constable of the Police Service of Scotland.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-10cb77161bcc324fe6be92ee5e99b353]

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (Argyll and Bute Council) Designation Order 2014 and comes into force on 12th May 2014.

## 2. Interpretation[^key-0e02cadd17f9214d00924b6a7d7f6b30]

In this Order—

- “the 1984 Act” means the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00002];
- “the 1991 Act” means the Road Traffic Act 1991; and
- “the parking area” means the area designated as a permitted parking area and a special parking area by article 3.

## 3. Designation as permitted parking area and special parking area[^key-9bad8eef85ec15b031ddee159dec9e56]

That part of the Argyll and Bute local government area[^f00003] specified in Schedule 1 is designated as—

- (a) a permitted parking area; and
- (b) a special parking area.

## 4. Modifications and application of Part II of the 1991 Act[^key-ff473128e9b56296c124c5f4f9e9e2ef]

Sections 66, 69 to 74, 79 and 82 of, and Schedule 6 to, the 1991 Act apply in relation to the parking area subject to the modifications specified in Schedule 2.

## 5. Modifications of the 1984 Act[^key-84ff90c7be56dabdcdd530943806fff9]

The 1984 Act is modified in relation to the parking area as specified in Schedule 3.

## 6. Disapplication of schedule 1 of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992[^key-2c4cf7a2a098cfaec97bd20ceafdbdd7]

The reference in paragraph 40(b) of schedule 1 of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992 to a parking adjudicator appointed under section 73(3) of the 1991 Act does not apply in relation to the parking area.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends to Argyll and Bute Council, arrangements for enforcing parking controls already available in London and certain other areas in England and in Edinburgh, Glasgow, Perth and Kinross, Aberdeen, Dundee, South Lanarkshire, Renfrewshire, East Ayrshire, South Ayrshire, East Renfrewshire, Fife and East Dunbartonshire.

The Order designates Argyll and Bute local government area (with certain specified exceptions) as a permitted parking area and as a special parking area in accordance with Schedule 3 to the Road Traffic Act 1991 (“the 1991 Act”). The Order applies, to the designated area, various provisions of the 1991 Act and modifies them where necessary. It also makes consequential modifications to certain provisions of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 (“the 1984 Act”) dealing with parking and related matters.

While the Order is in force, certain specified offences will be decriminalised: for example, in the case of the permitted parking area, breaches of orders relating to free on‑street parking places and in the case of orders prohibiting or restricting waiting, loading and unloading.

Enforcement will be carried out by parking attendants provided under section 63A of the 1984 Act. A system of parking adjudicators will deal with disputes.
