# The Adults with Incapacity (Supervision of Welfare Guardians etc. by Local Authorities) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 157

Adults With Incapacity

The Adults with Incapacity (Supervision of Welfare Guardians etc. by Local Authorities) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2014

Made

3rd June 2014

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

5th June 2014

Coming into force

12th August 2014

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 10(3)(a) and 86(2) of the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Adults with Incapacity (Supervision of Welfare Guardians etc. by Local Authorities) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2014 and come into force on 12th August 2014.

## 2. Amendment of the Adults with Incapacity (Supervision of Welfare Guardians etc. by Local Authorities) (Scotland) Regulations 2002

In regulation 2A(5)(a) of the Adults with Incapacity (Supervision of Welfare Guardians etc. by Local Authorities) (Scotland) Regulations 2002[^f00002] (variation or cessation of local authorities duties), for “regulation 2(1)(b)” substitute “regulation 2(1)”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Adults with Incapacity (Supervision of Welfare Guardians etc. by Local Authorities) (Scotland) Regulations 2002 (“the principal Regulations”) to correct an erroneous cross-reference inserted into the principal Regulations by the Adults with Incapacity (Supervision of Welfare Guardians etc. by Local Authorities) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2014.
