# The Post-16 Education (Scotland) Act 2013 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 144 (C. 10)

Education

The Post-16 Education (Scotland) Act 2013 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2014

Made

28th May 2014

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

30th May 2014

Coming into force

1st August 2014

The Scottish Ministers make the following order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 23(2) of the Post-16 Education (Scotland) Act 2013[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Post-16 Education (Scotland) Act 2013 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2014 and comes into force on 1st August 2014.

## 2. Appointed day

1st August 2014 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 8(1) and (2) of the Post-16 Education (Scotland) Act 2013 (regional strategic bodies) for the purpose of inserting the University of the Highlands and Islands into Part 2 of schedule 2A to the 2005 Act[^f00002].

MICHAEL RUSSELL

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

28th May 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force section 8(1) and (2) of the Post-16 Education (Scotland) Act 2013 (regional strategic bodies) on 1st August 2014 for the purpose of inserting the University of the Highlands and Islands into Part 2 of schedule 2A to the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005 as a regional strategic body.
