# The Lanarkshire Colleges Order 2014

> Text as at 2025-07-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

2014 No. 250

Education

The Lanarkshire Colleges Order 2014

Made

23rd September 2014

Coming into force

1st October 2014

The Scottish Ministers make the following order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3(5) and 60(3) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992[^f00001] and by sections 7A(1), 7B(2), 7C(1) and 34(2) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00002] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 7A(2) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005, they have consulted with the board of management of New College Lanarkshire, to which this order relates, the representatives of any trade union which the college recognises or which otherwise appears to the Scottish Ministers to be representative of the college’s staff, the college’s students’ association, the local authorities for the areas in which the college is situated, the Scottish Further and Higher Education Funding Council and other persons appearing to them as likely to be affected by article 2 of the Order.

In accordance with section 7B(3) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005, they have consulted with the Scottish Further and Higher Education Funding Council, the local authorities for the areas in which South Lanarkshire College provides fundable further education or fundable higher education which is to be funded by the regional strategic body and other persons appearing to them as likely to be affected by article 3 of the Order.

In accordance with section 7C(6) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005, they have consulted with the boards of management of New College Lanarkshire and South Lanarkshire College, to which this Order relates, the representatives of any trade union which is recognised by the colleges or which otherwise appears to the Scottish Ministers to be representative of the colleges’ staff, the colleges’ students’ associations, the Scottish Further and Higher Education Funding Council, the local authorities for the areas in which the colleges are situated, and other persons appearing to them as likely to be affected by article 4 of the Order.

In accordance with section 60(2A)(a) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992 and 34(4)(bc) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^key-19d7996795210c51b00f796e20361ae3]

(1) This Order may be cited as the Lanarkshire Colleges Order 2014 and comes into force on 1st October 2014.

(2) In this Order—

- “the 1992 Act” means the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992; and
- “the 2005 Act” means the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005.

## 2. Designation of Regional College[^key-bcee7173c25c4acd6d11011bad122fcd]

(1) The board of management of New College Lanarkshire[^f00003] whose board of management is established in pursuance of Part 1 of the 1992 Act is designated as a regional college.

(2) The designation of the board of management of New College Lanarkshire as a regional college by paragraph (1) is to remain in effect notwithstanding any change of its name.

## 3. Regional Strategic Bodies[^key-1ce0feb66651e02d23e7a11db78385ac]

In schedule 2A to the 2005 Act[^f00004]—

- (a) in Part 1, omit “Regional Board for Lanarkshire Colleges”; and
- (b) in Part 2, before the entry for the University of the Highlands and Islands, insert “New College Lanarkshire”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provision in relation to New College Lanarkshire and South Lanarkshire College.

Article 2(1) designates the board of management of New College Lanarkshire as a regional college. Article 2(2) provides that the designation as a regional college remains in force notwithstanding any change of name of New College Lanarkshire.

Article 3 amends schedule 2A to the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005 by removing the entry for the Regional Board for Lanarkshire Colleges from Part 1 of that schedule and by inserting New College Lanarkshire as an other regional strategic body into Part 2 of that schedule.

Article 4 assigns the board of management of South Lanarkshire College to the regional strategic body, New College Lanarkshire, which is a regional strategic body by virtue of article 3. Article 4(2) provides that the board of management of South Lanarkshire College remains assigned to New College Lanarkshire notwithstanding any change of name of either college.

Article 5 makes provision under the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992 (“the 1992 Act”) to amend Schedule 2 to the 1992 Act to alter the constitution of the board of management of New College Lanarkshire to reflect its new role as a regional strategic body. Article 5(1), (3) and (5) amend Schedule 2 to the 1992 Act to make it clear that paragraphs 3, 5 and 5C of that Schedule apply to the board of management of New College Lanarkshire as modified by this Order.

Article 5(2)(a) increases the size of the board of management of New College Lanarkshire so that it must have at least 19 and not more than 22 members. Article 5(2)(b) reduces from two to one the number of students from New College Lanarkshire on the board. Article 5(2)(c) provides that chairing member of the board of South Lanarkshire College, as well as the principal of South Lanarkshire College and the two elected members of staff on the board of South Lanarkshire College will also be members of the board of management of New College Lanarkshire. It also makes provision that one student from South Lanarkshire College is a member of the board of management of New College Lanarkshire. Article 5(2)(d) provides that, if the principal of South Lanarkshire College has been removed from the board of management of New College Lanarkshire under section 24 of the 1992 Act, that person would be disqualified from being part of the board of management of New College Lanarkshire.

Article 5(4) and (6) makes consequential amendments to the provisions in Schedule 2 to the 1992 Act regarding qualifications, tenure of office and resignation of the additional members of the board of management of New College Lanarkshire.
