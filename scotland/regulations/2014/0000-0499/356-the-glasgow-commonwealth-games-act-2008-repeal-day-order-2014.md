# The Glasgow Commonwealth Games Act 2008 (Repeal Day) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 356

Sports Grounds And Sporting Events

The Glasgow Commonwealth Games Act 2008 (Repeal Day) Order 2014

Made

10th December 2014

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

12th December 2014

Coming into force

1st January 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 50(2) of the Glasgow Commonwealth Games Act 2008[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Glasgow Commonwealth Games Act 2008 (Repeal Day) Order 2014 and comes into force on 1st January 2015.

## 2. Repeal day

1st January 2015 is specified as the repeal day on which the Glasgow Commonwealth Games Act 2008 ceases to have effect.

JAMIE HEPBURN

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

10th December 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 50 of the Glasgow Commonwealth Games Act 2008 provides for that Act to cease to have effect on the “repeal day”. This Order specifies that the repeal day is 1st January 2015.
