# The Legal Profession and Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 2007 (Membership of the Scottish Legal Complaints Commission) Amendment Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 272

Legal Profession

The Legal Profession and Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 2007 (Membership of the Scottish Legal Complaints Commission) Amendment Order 2014

Made

21st October 2014

Coming into force

22nd October 2014

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(2) of and paragraph 2(7) of schedule 1 to the Legal Profession and Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 2007[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 79(3) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before, and approved by resolution of, the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Legal Profession and Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 2007 (Membership of the Scottish Legal Complaints Commission) Amendment Order 2014 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Membership of the Scottish Legal Complaints Commission

Paragraph 2 of schedule 1 to the Legal Profession and Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 2007 is amended as follows—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (1)(b), for “11” substitute “8”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (3), for “6” substitute “5”;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (4) for “5” substitute “3”.

KENNY MACASKILL

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

21st October 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes changes to the number and composition of the membership of the Scottish Legal Complaints Commission.
