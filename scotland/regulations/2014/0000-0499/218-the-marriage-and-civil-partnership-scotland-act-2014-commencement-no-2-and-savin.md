# The Marriage and Civil Partnership (Scotland) Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2 and Saving Provisions) Amendment Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This S.S.I. has been made in consequence of defects in [S.S.I. 2014/212](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2014/212) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that instrument.

2014 No. 218 (C. 19)

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

11th August 2014

Coming into force

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 36(2) and (3) of the Marriage and Civil Partnership (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Marriage and Civil Partnership (Scotland) Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2 and Saving Provisions) Amendment Order 2014 and comes into force on 1st September 2014.

## 2. Amendment of the Commencement No. 2 and Saving Provisions Order

(1) The Marriage and Civil Partnership (Scotland) Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2 and Saving Provisions) Order 2014[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 3(2)(a) (saving), after “commence” insert “on”.

(3) For the Schedule substitute the Schedule in the Schedule to this Order.
