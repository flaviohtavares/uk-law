# The Civil Partnership Between Persons of Different Sexes (Prescribed Bodies) (Scotland) Regulations 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 24

Civil Partnership

The Civil Partnership Between Persons of Different Sexes (Prescribed Bodies) (Scotland) Regulations 2021

Made

18th January 2021

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

20th January 2021

Coming into force

1st June 2021

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 94A(2A)(a)(i) of the Civil Partnership Act 2004[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 94A(2B)(a) of the Civil Partnership Act 2004 the bodies prescribed in regulation 2 have requested the Scottish Ministers to prescribe them.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Partnership Between Persons of Different Sexes (Prescribed Bodies) (Scotland) Regulations 2021 and come into force on 1 June 2021.

## 2. Prescribed bodies for the registration of different sex civil partnership

The bodies prescribed for the purpose of section 94A(2A)(a)(i) of the Civil Partnership Act 2004 (persons who may register different sex civil partnerships) are listed in the schedule of these Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations prescribe bodies for the purpose of section 94A(2A)(a)(i) of the Civil Partnership Act 2004. Celebrants of religious or belief bodies that are so prescribed may register civil partnerships between couples of different sexes without being individually registered or authorised for the purpose by the Registrar General of Births, Deaths and Marriages for Scotland.
