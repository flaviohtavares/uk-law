# The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 63

Rating And Valuation

The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2021

Made

4th February 2021

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

8th February 2021

Coming into force

1st April 2021

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7B(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2021 and comes into force on 1 April 2021.

## 2. Non-domestic rate for 2021-2022

For the purposes of section 7B of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, the Scottish Ministers prescribe a rate of 49 pence per pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of financial year 2021-2022.

KATE FORBES

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

4th February 2021

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes a rate of 49 pence in the pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of the financial year 2021-2022. A rate of 49.8 pence in the pound was prescribed by the Scottish Ministers as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland for the financial year 2020-2021 ([S.S.I. 2020/37](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2020/37)).
