# The Animal Welfare (Licensing of Activities Involving Animals) (Scotland) Regulations 2021, section 9

> Text as at 2021-10-10.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 9. Power to take samples from animals[^c-3f1ffa3e61aa53b08e714d84ad9d3a8f]

*Extent: S*

For the purposes of checking compliance with these Regulations or the licence conditions, an inspector may take samples, or arrange for samples to be taken by a veterinary surgeon, from any animals on premises occupied by an operator for laboratory testing.
