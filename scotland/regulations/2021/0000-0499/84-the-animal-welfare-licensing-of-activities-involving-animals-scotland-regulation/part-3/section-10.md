# The Animal Welfare (Licensing of Activities Involving Animals) (Scotland) Regulations 2021, section 10

> Text as at 2021-10-10.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 10. Duty to assist in the taking of samples from animals[^c-f2b7f15860ff96b90ae4bb3587d0a3f4]

*Extent: S*

An operator must comply with any reasonable request of an inspector to facilitate the identification and examination of an animal and the taking of samples in accordance with regulation 9 and, in particular, must arrange the suitable restraint of an animal if so requested by an inspector.
