# The Animal Welfare (Licensing of Activities Involving Animals) (Scotland) Regulations 2021, section 16

> Text as at 2021-10-10.
>

## 16. Guidance[^key-95f6e86745114e7405355cbedd0df310]

*Extent: S*

A local authority must have regard in the carrying out of its functions under these Regulations to such guidance as may be issued by the Scottish Ministers.
