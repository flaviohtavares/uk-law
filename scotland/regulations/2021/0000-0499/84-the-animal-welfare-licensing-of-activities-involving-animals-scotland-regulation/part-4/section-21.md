# The Animal Welfare (Licensing of Activities Involving Animals) (Scotland) Regulations 2021, section 21

>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 21. Notices of revocation and variation upon reinstatement[^c-c20f4e0ca4df17e58971d7641c2b5893]

*Extent: S*

(1) A notice of a decision to—

- (a) revoke a licence regardless of whether under regulation 18(10)(a)(iii) or 20,
- (b) vary and reinstate a licence as varied under regulation 18(10)(a)(ii).

must comply with paragraph (2).

(2) The notice must—

- (a) state the licensing authority’s reasons for the decision, and
- (b) give notice of the licence holder’s right of appeal to the sheriff and the period under regulation 27 within which such an appeal may be brought.
