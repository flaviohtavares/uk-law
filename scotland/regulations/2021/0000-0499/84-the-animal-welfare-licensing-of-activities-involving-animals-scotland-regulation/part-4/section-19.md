> Extent: Scotland.

## 19. Reinstatement of a suspended licence by a licensing authority[^c-80f238a391f1707d07ad46c8fd819b7a]

*Extent: S*

(1) A licensing authority must reinstate a suspended licence by way of notice once it is satisfied that the grounds specified in the notice of suspension have been or will be remedied.

(2) Where a licensing authority reinstates a licence under paragraph (1), it may reduce the period for which it is reinstated.
