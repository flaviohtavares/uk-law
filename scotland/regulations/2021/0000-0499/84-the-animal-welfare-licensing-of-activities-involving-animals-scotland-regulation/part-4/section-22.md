> Text as at 2021-10-10.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 22. Obstruction of inspectors[^key-c8f15970db58d586357fd1ca18b62b8a]

*Extent: S*

A person must not intentionally obstruct an inspector appointed for the purposes of these Regulations in the exercise of any powers conferred by or under these Regulations.
