# The Animal Welfare (Licensing of Activities Involving Animals) (Scotland) Regulations 2021, section 20

> Text as at 2021-10-10.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 20. Revocation[^key-3c4404db0ae6603e325e0e4c460bebf3]

*Extent: S*

(1) A licensing authority may revoke a licence at any time on any of the grounds in regulation 17 by service of a notice on the licence holder.

(2) The revocation takes effect on service of the notice if served in accordance with regulation 21.
