# The Domestic Abuse Act 2021 (Commencement) (Scotland) Regulations 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 239 (C. 14)

Criminal Law

The Domestic Abuse Act 2021 (Commencement) (Scotland) Regulations 2021

Made

11th June 2021

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

15th June 2021

Coming into force

29th June 2021

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 90(3) of the Domestic Abuse Act 2021[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Domestic Abuse Act 2021 (Commencement) (Scotland) Regulations 2021 and come into force on 29 June 2021.

## 2. Appointed day

29 June 2021 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 74(2) and part 2 of schedule 3 of the Domestic Abuse Act 2021 (amendments relating to offences committed outside the UK).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force, on 29 June 2021, section 74(2) and part 2 of schedule 3 of the Domestic Abuse Act 2021 (“the Act”). These provisions extend extraterritorial jurisdiction to certain sexual offences under the Sexual Offences (Scotland) Act [2009 (asp 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2009/9), the offence of stalking under section 39 of the Criminal Justice and Licensing (Scotland) Act [2010 (asp 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2010/13) and the common law offence of assault. The provisions also make amendments to existing extraterritorial offences under section 11(1) and (2) of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act [1995 (c. 46)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/46).

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 29 April 2021. Of those provisions extending to Scotland, section 81 and sections 85 to 91 came into force on the day the Act was passed.
