## 2. Years to which these Regulations apply[^c-62aa4f5be5cab01a55b25cbd1515b2eb]

*Extent: S*

Subject to regulation 97 (transitional provision) and regulation 101 (savings provision), the financial year commencing 1 April 2022 and each subsequent financial year are prescribed as the years for which these Regulations apply.
