## 58. Meaning of “retirement pension income”[^c-dc617be68af987490e0f9eff29cba035]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), in regulation 57(1)(a) “retirement pension income” has the same meaning as in section 16 of the State Pension Credit Act 2002[^f00177] as extended by regulation 16 of the State Pension Credit Regulations 2002.

(2) Retirement pension income includes any increase in a Category A or Category B retirement pension mentioned in section 16(1)(a) of the State Pension Credit Act 2002 which is payable under Part 4 of the 1992 Act in respect of an applicant’s partner.
