# The Council Tax Reduction (Scotland) Regulations 2021, section 45

> Text as at 2026-11-06.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 45. Meaning of “earned income”[^c-39ffdb4cd9ff51700d48ac088e770324]

*Extent: S*

“Earned income” means the remuneration or profits derived from—

- (a) employment under a contract of service or in an office, including elective office,
- (b) a trade, profession or vocation, or
- (c) any other paid work, or
- (d) any income treated as earned income in accordance with this Chapter.
