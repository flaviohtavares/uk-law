## 36. Calculation of income and capital of members of applicant’s family[^c-5628b4fb8c652fa1292465200911d01c]

*Extent: S*

(1) The income and capital of an applicant’s partner is to be treated as income and capital of the applicant and is to be calculated or estimated in accordance with the provisions of this Part in the same way the applicant’s income and capital is calculated or estimated and any reference to the “applicant” in this Part and in schedule 4 is, except where the context otherwise requires, to be construed for the purposes of this Part as if it included a reference to the applicant’s partner.

(2) The income and capital of a child or young person is not to be treated as the income and capital of the applicant.
