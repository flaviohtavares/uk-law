# The Homeless Persons (Unsuitable Accommodation) (Scotland) (Modification and Revocation) (Coronavirus) Amendment Order 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Housing

The Homeless Persons (Unsuitable Accommodation) (Scotland) (Modification and Revocation) (Coronavirus) Amendment Order 2021

Made

26th May 2021

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

27th May 2021

Coming into force

29th June 2021

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 29(3) and (4) of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Homeless Persons (Unsuitable Accommodation) (Scotland) (Modification and Revocation) (Coronavirus) Amendment Order 2021 and comes into force on 29 June 2021.

## 2. Amendment of the Homeless Persons (Unsuitable Accommodation) (Scotland) (Modification and Revocation) (Coronavirus) Order 2021

(1) The Homeless Persons (Unsuitable Accommodation) (Scotland) (Modification and Revocation) (Coronavirus) Order 2021[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 1(2), for “30 June 2021” substitute “30 September 2021”.

BEN MACPHERSON

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

26th May 2021

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Homeless Persons (Unsuitable Accommodation) (Scotland) (Modification and Revocation) (Coronavirus) Order 2021 (“the previous Order”).

Article 2 of this Order substitutes a new expiry date into Article 1 of the previous Order so that Article 2 of the previous Order expires on 30 September 2021 rather than 30 June 2021.

This means that the modifications of the Homeless Persons (Unsuitable Accommodation) (Scotland) Order 2014 made by Article 2 of the previous Order continue in effect until 30 September 2021.
