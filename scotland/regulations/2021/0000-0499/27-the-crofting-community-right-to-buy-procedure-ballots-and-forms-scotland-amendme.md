# The Crofting Community Right to Buy (Procedure, Ballots and Forms) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 27

Land Reform

The Crofting Community Right to Buy (Procedure, Ballots and Forms) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2021

Made

19th January 2021

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

20th January 2021

Coming into force

24th February 2021

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 73(5) and (11), 75(2), (4), (6), and (7), 82(1) and (2), 90(6) and 98(3) of the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Crofting Community Right to Buy (Procedure, Ballots and Forms) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2021 and come into force on 24 February 2021.

## 2. Amendments to the Crofting Community Right to Buy (Procedure, Ballots and Forms) (Scotland) Regulations 2020

(1) The Crofting Community Right to Buy (Procedure, Ballots and Forms) (Scotland) Regulations 2020[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulations 19 and 20, for “1 December 2020”, in each place that it occurs, substitute “24 February 2021”.

A member of the Scottish Government

Edinburgh

19th January 2021

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made in exercise of the powers in sections 73(5) and (11), 75(2), (4), (6), and (7), 82(1) and (2), 90(6) and 98(3) of the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2003.

Regulation 2 amends dates contained in saving and transitional provisions in the Crofting Community Right to Buy (Procedure, Ballots and Forms) (Scotland) Regulations 2020, to align those dates with the coming into force date for those Regulations.
