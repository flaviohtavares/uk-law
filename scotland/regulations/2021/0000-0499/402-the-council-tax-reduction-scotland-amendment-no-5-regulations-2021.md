# The Council Tax Reduction (Scotland) Amendment (No. 5) Regulations 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 402

Council Tax

The Council Tax Reduction (Scotland) Amendment (No. 5) Regulations 2021

Made

5th November 2021

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

9th November 2021

Coming into force

1st January 2022

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 80, 113(1) and paragraph 1 of schedule 2 of the Local Government Finance Act 1992[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Council Tax Reduction (Scotland) Amendment (No. 5) Regulations 2021 and come into force on 1 January 2022.

## 2. Amendment of the Council Tax Reduction (Scotland) Regulations 2012

(1) The Council Tax Reduction (Scotland) Regulations 2012[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) In schedule 5 (capital to be disregarded) after paragraph 74[^f00003] insert—

> **75.** Any redress payment made under Part 4 of the Redress for Survivors (Historical Child Abuse in Care) (Scotland) Act 2021[^f00004].

.

TOM ARTHUR

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Council Tax Reduction (Scotland) Regulations 2012 (“**the Working Age Regulations**”).

The Redress for Survivors (Historical Child Abuse in Care) (Scotland) Act 2021 created a scheme whereby survivors of historical child abuse in care may apply for and receive redress payments. Regulation 2 amends schedule 5 of the Working Age Regulations so that such payments are disregarded in the calculation of capital for the purposes of council tax reduction under those Regulations.
