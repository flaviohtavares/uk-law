## 1.4. Computation of periods of time[^c-d1339bcfdb510f41846568bd7d7b4a75]

*Extent: S*

If any period of time specified in these Rules expires on a Saturday, Sunday or public or court holiday, it is extended to expire on the next day that the office of the Clerk is open for civil business.
