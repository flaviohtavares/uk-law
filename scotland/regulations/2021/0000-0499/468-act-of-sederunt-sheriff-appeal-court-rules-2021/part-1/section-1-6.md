# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2021, section 1.6

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1.6. Forms[^key-2d1255144ffc6c4582eb73d9a1523b47]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where there is a reference in these Rules to a form, it is a reference to that form in schedule 2.

(2) Where these Rules require a form to be used, that form may be varied where the circumstances require it.
