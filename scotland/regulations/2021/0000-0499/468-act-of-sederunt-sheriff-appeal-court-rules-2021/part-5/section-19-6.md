## 19.6. Form of bond of caution[^key-8c75b644406c20d9112e4589f1cb9f0e]

*Extent: S*

(1) A bond of caution must oblige the cautioner to make payment of the sums as validly and in the same manner as the party is obliged.

(2) In this rule—

- “**cautioner**” includes the cautioner’s heirs and executors;
- “**party**” means the person to whom the cautioner is bound, and that person’s heirs and successors;
- “the sums” are the sums for which the cautioner is bound to the party.
