# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2021, section 15.1

> Text as at 2025-01-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 15.1. Intimation of motions by other means[^c-666492092b28c96ffdbec577aeac9346]

*Extent: S*

(1) The lodging party must give intimation of that party’s intention to lodge the motion, and of the terms of the motion, to every other party in Form 15.1.

(2) That intimation must be accompanied by a copy of any document referred to in the motion.
