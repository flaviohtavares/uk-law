# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2021, section 16.1

> Text as at 2025-01-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 16.1. Application of this Chapter[^key-bd544f2472c9a5a51ee6e5acc5d198ea]

*Extent: S*

This Chapter applies to any application to the Court that is made by minute, other than a joint minute, a minute of abandonment or a minute of amendment.
