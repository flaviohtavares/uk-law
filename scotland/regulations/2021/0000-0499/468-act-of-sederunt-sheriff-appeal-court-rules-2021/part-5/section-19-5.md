# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2021, section 19.5

> Extent: Scotland.

## 19.5. Cautioners and other guarantors[^c-27f295bb4e0208d35a324feca2431089]

*Extent: S*

A bond of caution or other security may only be given by a person who is an authorised person within the meaning of section 31 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (authorised persons)[^f00014].
