> Extent: Scotland.

## 13.8. Issuing of orders by email[^c-7da36a2112bc601ae52f65e1ef053033]

*Extent: S*

Where the Court makes an order determining a motion which was lodged in accordance with Chapter 14, the Clerk must email a copy of the order to the addresses of the lodging party and every receiving party.
