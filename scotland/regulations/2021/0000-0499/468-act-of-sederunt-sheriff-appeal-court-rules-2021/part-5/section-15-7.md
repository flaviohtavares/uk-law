## 15.7. Modification of Chapter 5[^c-8aa18ed2cc69ba54e2092bc4cb231afd]

*Extent: S*

For the purposes of this Chapter, the following provisions in Chapter 5 do not apply—

- (a) rule 5.6(1)(e);
- (b) rule 5.7(2)(e).
