> Text as at 2025-01-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 20.6. Decree for expenses in name of solicitor[^key-1be580c7e447797838b6894f17b56d87]

*Extent: S*

The Court may allow a decree for expenses to be extracted in the name of the solicitor who conducted the appeal.
