# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2021, section 21.2

> Extent: Scotland.

## 21.2. Application for an award of expenses[^key-d448e443e1f91498de04ec56a274df5b]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where civil proceedings have been brought by a pursuer, another party to the action (“**the applicant**”) may make an application to the Court for an award of expenses to be made against the pursuer, on one or more of the grounds specified in either or both—

- (a) section 8(4)(a) to (c) of the 2018 Act;
- (b) paragraph (2) of this rule.

(2) The grounds specified in this paragraph, which are exceptions to section 8(2) of the 2018 Act, are as follows—

- (a) failure by the pursuer to obtain an award of damages greater than the sum offered by way of a tender lodged in process;
- (b) unreasonable delay on the part of the pursuer in accepting a sum offered by way of a tender lodged in process;
- (c) abandonment of the appeal by the pursuer in terms of rule 10.1(1) to (4), or at common law.
