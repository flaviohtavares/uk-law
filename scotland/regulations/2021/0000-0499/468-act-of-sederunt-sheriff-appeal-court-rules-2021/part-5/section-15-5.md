> Text as at 2025-01-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 15.5. Joint motions[^key-acd4d80027875269924e1ad331e1447b]

*Extent: S*

(1) A joint motion by all parties need not be intimated.

(2) Such a motion is to be lodged by any of the parties.
