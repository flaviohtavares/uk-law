> Extent: Scotland.

## 15.2. Opposition to motions[^c-911823b982f67596841263bb4ac0e22f]

*Extent: S*

(1) A receiving party may oppose a motion by lodging a notice of opposition in Form 15.2.

(2) Any notice of opposition must be lodged within 7 days after the date of intimation of the motion.

(3) The procedural Appeal Sheriff may, on the application of the lodging party, either—

- (a) vary the period of 7 days mentioned in paragraph (2); or
- (b) dispense with intimation on any party.

(4) An application mentioned in paragraph (3) must—

- (a) be included in the motion;
- (b) give reasons for varying the period or dispensing with intimation, as the case may be.

(5) The procedural Appeal Sheriff may allow a notice of opposition to be lodged late, on cause shown.
