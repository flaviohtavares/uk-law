> Extent: Scotland.

## 15.6. Hearing of opposed motions[^c-9d197a00a642b4b9ea336e144d4c82fd]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a notice of opposition in Form 15.2 is lodged, the motion is to be heard by the procedural Appeal Sheriff on the first suitable court day after the lodging of the notice of opposition.

(2) The Clerk must intimate the date and time of the hearing to the parties.
