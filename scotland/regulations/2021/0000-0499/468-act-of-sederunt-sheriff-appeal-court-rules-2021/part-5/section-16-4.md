# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2021, section 16.4

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 16.4. Consent to minute[^key-727c270e415b5a06f11236228dbe50b3]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a person to whom a minute is intimated seeks to consent to the minute, that person may do so by lodging a notice to that effect.

(2) Where every person to whom a minute is intimated consents to the minute, the procedural Appeal Sheriff is to determine the minute in chambers without the appearance of those persons, unless the procedural Appeal Sheriff otherwise determines.
