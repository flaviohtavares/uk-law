# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2021, section 19.2

> Text as at 2025-01-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 19.2. Form of application to find caution or give security[^key-309419cb2ea932373937566da028c86c]

*Extent: S*

An application—

- (a) for an order for caution or other security;
- (b) to vary or recall such an order,

is to be made by motion.
