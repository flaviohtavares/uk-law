## 19.7. Caution or other security: sufficiency and objections[^c-7e58bca5c0a46d7695308f01bff14b38]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Clerk must be satisfied that any document lodged in process under rule 19.4(4) is in proper form.

(2) A party who is dissatisfied with the sufficiency or form of any document lodged in process under rule 19.4(4) may apply to the Court by motion for an order under rule 19.9(1).
