> Extent: Scotland.

## 19.9. Failure to find caution or give security[^key-e84e57aaa82429b63b92d3d124d2df48]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a person who has been ordered to find caution or give security fails to do so, any other party may apply to the Court by motion for a finding that the person is in default.

(2) Despite rule 3.1, a person who fails to find caution or give security is only in default if the Court grants a motion under paragraph (1) and makes a finding that the person is in default.
