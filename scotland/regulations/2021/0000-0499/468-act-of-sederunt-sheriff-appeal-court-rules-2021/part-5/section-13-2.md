## 13.2. Making of motions[^c-e7498fbb1aaefc81b619c79f3ed2f85d]

*Extent: S*

A motion may be made either—

- (a) orally, in accordance with rule 13.3; or
- (b) in writing, in accordance with rule 13.4.
