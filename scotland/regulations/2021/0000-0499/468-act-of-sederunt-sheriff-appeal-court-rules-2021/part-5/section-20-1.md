> Extent: Scotland.

## 20.1. Taxation of expenses[^key-ed3476e1e27f8190b792ffb57a04089b]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the Court makes an award of expenses in any appeal, those expenses must be taxed before decree is granted for them.

(2) This rule does not apply where the Court modifies those expenses to a fixed sum.
