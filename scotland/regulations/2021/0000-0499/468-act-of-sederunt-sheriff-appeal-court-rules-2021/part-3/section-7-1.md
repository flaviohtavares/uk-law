> Text as at 2025-01-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 7.1. Application of this Chapter[^key-74916b27bc89c45749818dd17b90a13f]

*Extent: S*

This Chapter applies to an appeal which has been appointed to proceed under procedure before three Appeal Sheriffs, to be known as Chapter 7 procedure.
