# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2021, section 7.13

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 7.13. Referral to family mediation[^key-714da0b0054fc4e83865d4e844526c15]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the decision appealed against concerns an order made by the sheriff under section 11(1) of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995 (court orders relating to parental responsibilities etc.)[^f00011], the procedural Appeal Sheriff may refer that matter to a family mediator.

(2) In this rule, “**family mediator**” means a person accredited as a mediator in family mediation to an organisation which is concerned with such mediation and which is approved for the purposes of the Civil Evidence (Family Mediation) (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00012] by the Lord President of the Court of Session.
