> Extent: Scotland.

## 7.12. Extension of notes of evidence[^key-6f19625a4dba213398769216e6d4aaf8]

*Extent: S*

It is not necessary to produce notes of evidence in relation to any issue in respect of which the parties are agreed that the decision appealed against is not to be submitted to review.
