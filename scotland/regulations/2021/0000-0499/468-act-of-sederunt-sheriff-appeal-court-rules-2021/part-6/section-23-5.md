## 23.5. Sist of appeal[^key-b17ee6688f51c57df484764ffa06d3ad]

*Extent: S*

(1) When a reference is made, the Court is to sist the appeal until the European Court determines the reference, unless the Court orders otherwise.

(2) Where an appeal is sisted under paragraph (1), the Court may recall the sist for the purposes of making an interim order.
