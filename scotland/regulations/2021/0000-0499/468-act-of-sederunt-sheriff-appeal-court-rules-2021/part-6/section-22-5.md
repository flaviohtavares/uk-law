# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2021, section 22.5

> Text as at 2025-01-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 22.5. Participation by the relevant authority[^key-a62c50d2024c179c163077ce85cfe349]

*Extent: S*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where a relevant authority has given notice to the Clerk that it intends to take part in the appeal as a party.

(2) Within 7 days after permission to proceed is given, the relevant authority must lodge a minute containing the relevant authority’s written submissions in respect of the compatibility question or devolution issue.
