# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2021, section 28.1A

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28.1A. Application for an order[^key-150967e7ebb474be40bdfbfc6a197f6d]

*Extent: S*

(1) A party to the proceedings may apply to the court for an order under this Chapter to restrict the reporting of the proceedings.

(2) An application for an order under this Chapter must be made by motion and be accompanied by Form 28.1A.
