# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2021, section 26.8

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 26.8. Taking of evidence by commissioner: conduct of commission[^key-34e71d52eab193de37dfcd2bbbda75c5]

*Extent: S*

(1) The commissioner must administer the oath *de fideli administratione* to the clerk.

(2) The commissioner is to administer the oath to the vulnerable witness in Form 25.4-A unless the witness elects to affirm.

(3) Where the witness elects to affirm, the commissioner must administer the affirmation in Form 25.4-B.
