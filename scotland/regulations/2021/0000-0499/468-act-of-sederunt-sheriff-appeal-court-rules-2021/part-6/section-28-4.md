# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2021, section 28.4

> Extent: Scotland.

## 28.4. Notification of reporting restrictions[^key-8057cad4db4d1c279f3a4decf57f6f30]

*Extent: S*

Where the Court makes an order, the Clerk must immediately—

- (a) send a copy of the order to any interested person;
- (b) arrange for the publication of the making of the order on the Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service website.
