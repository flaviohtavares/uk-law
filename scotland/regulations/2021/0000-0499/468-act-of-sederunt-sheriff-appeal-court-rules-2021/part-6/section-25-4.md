# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2021, section 25.4

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 25.4. Administration of oath or affirmation to witnesses[^key-b7e832e5f1694a381e91bd4a42d1890b]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Appeal Sheriff is to administer the oath to a witness in Form 25.4-A unless the witness elects to affirm.

(2) Where a witness elects to affirm, the Appeal Sheriff must administer the affirmation in Form 25.4-B.
