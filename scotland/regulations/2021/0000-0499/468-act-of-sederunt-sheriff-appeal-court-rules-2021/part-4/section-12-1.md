# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2021, section 12.1

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 12.1. Application of this Chapter[^key-b1db962468e2f77ad628c64b9424b902]

*Extent: S*

This Chapter applies where a party seeks the permission of the Court to appeal to the Court of Session against a decision of the Court constituting final judgment in civil proceedings under section 113 of the 2014 Act (appeal from the Sheriff Appeal Court to the Court of Session).
