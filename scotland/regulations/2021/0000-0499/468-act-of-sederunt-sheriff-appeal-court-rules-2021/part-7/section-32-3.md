> Extent: Scotland.

## 32.3. Hearing of appeal[^c-a9121c2e7ce270ea4288c52a4fbbd82a]

*Extent: S*

(1) At the hearing, a party may only raise questions of law or procedural irregularities of which notice has not been given if the Court permits the party to do so.

(2) Where the Court grants permission, it may do so on such conditions as to expenses or otherwise as the Court thinks fit.
