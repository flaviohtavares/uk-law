> Extent: Scotland.

## 33.2. Form of appeal[^c-4c734ccb88677dd0eb58e09468a713f4]

*Extent: S*

(1) An appeal is made by lodging a note of appeal in Form 33.2.

(2) Rule 6.2(2)(a) to (c) and (g) to (h) applies for the purpose of making an appeal under this rule.
