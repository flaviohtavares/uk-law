> Extent: Scotland.

## 29.12. Estimates of duration of hearing of application for new trial[^key-52df76811dcbe0ee08080712e1e1f5a6]

*Extent: S*

The parties must lodge estimates of the duration of any hearing required to dispose of the application for a new trial in Form 29.12 no later than 7 days before the procedural hearing.
