## 29.14. Application to enter jury verdict[^c-4287a99d51c6d041c7c0421c660c0fd6]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies to an application under section 71(2) of the 2014 Act (verdict subject to opinion of the Court).

(2) Such an application is to be made in Form 29.14.

(3) When an application is lodged, the party lodging it must also lodge—

- (a) a print containing—
  - (i) the pleadings in the sheriff court process;
  - (ii) the interlocutors in the sheriff court process;
  - (iii) the issues and counter-issues;
- (b) the verdict of the jury;
- (c) any exception and the determination on it of the sheriff presiding at the trial.

(4) Unless the procedural Appeal Sheriff otherwise directs—

- (a) it is not necessary for the purposes of such a motion to print the notes of evidence;
- (b) but the notes of the sheriff presiding at the trial may be produced at any time if required.

(5) The procedural Appeal Sheriff may refer an application referred to in paragraph (1) to the Court in cases of complexity or difficulty.
