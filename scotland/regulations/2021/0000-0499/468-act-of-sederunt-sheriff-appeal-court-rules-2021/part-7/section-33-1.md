# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2021, section 33.1

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 33.1. Application of this Chapter[^c-c8a85a02d79c03a6c362cb79be2cfe55]

*Extent: S*

This Chapter applies to an appeal against the decision of a sheriff under section 38(3), 46(3)[^c-b9961803c659ee95aaf3ce98a04e2b7a] and 67(3) of the Age of Criminal Responsibility (Scotland) Act 2019[^f00040].
