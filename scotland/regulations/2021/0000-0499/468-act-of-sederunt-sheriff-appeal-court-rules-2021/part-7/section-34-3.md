# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2021, section 34.3

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 34.3. Saving provision[^key-c8847167452b3d585c6ee02e4f3027a1]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) to (4), Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015 (“**the 2015 Rules**”) is saved in so far as it applies to any proceedings commenced before the coming into force of this Act of Sederunt.

(2) Rules 21.1 and 21.5 of the 2015 Rules are saved in respect of appeals in which a reference to the European Court is made before IP completion day.

(3) In paragraph (2), “**reference**” has the meaning given in rule 21.1 of the 2015 Rules.

(4) Paragraph (1) does not apply to rules 21.2 to 21.4 of the 2015 Rules.
