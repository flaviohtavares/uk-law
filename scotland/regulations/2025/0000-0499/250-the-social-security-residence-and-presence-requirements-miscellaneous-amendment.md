# The Social Security (Residence and Presence Requirements) (Miscellaneous Amendment) (Scotland) Regulations 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 250

Social Security

The Social Security (Residence and Presence Requirements) (Miscellaneous Amendment) (Scotland) Regulations 2025

Made

10th September 2025

Coming into force

11th September 2025

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13 of the Social Security Act 1988[^f00001], section 175(3) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00002] and sections 28(2), 31(2) and 32(2) of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018[^f00003] (“**the 2018 Act**”) and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 13(2) of the Social Security Act 1988, the Scottish Ministers have consulted the Welsh Ministers[^f00004].

In accordance with section 96(2) of the 2018 Act[^f00005], a draft of these Regulations has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

In accordance with section 97(2) of the 2018 Act[^f00006], the Scottish Ministers have informed the Scottish Commission on Social Security of their proposals, notified the Scottish Parliament that they have done so and made their proposals publicly available by such means as they consider appropriate.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Residence and Presence Requirements) (Miscellaneous Amendment) (Scotland) Regulations 2025.

(2) These Regulations come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

(3) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make provision in respect of the social security assistance entitlement of certain persons arriving in Scotland from Israel, the Occupied Palestinian Territories (the West Bank, including East Jerusalem, and Gaza) or the occupied Golan Heights following the escalation of violence between Israel and Iran that began on 13 June 2025. These Regulations also make provision for persons who have been granted, or are deemed to have been granted, leave outside the immigration rules made under section 3(2) of the Immigration Act 1971.

Regulations 2 to 4 amend the Early Years Assistance (Best Start Grants) (Scotland) Regulations 2018, the Welfare Foods (Best Start Foods) (Scotland) Regulations 2019, the Carer’s Assistance (Young Carer Grants) (Scotland) Regulations 2019 respectively to insert a new category of person into the list of persons who are exempt from having to satisfy the habitual residence test. The category being inserted is a person with leave to enter or remain in the United Kingdom granted under or outside the immigration rules, has a right of abode in the UK or an Irish citizen who does not require leave to enter or remain in the UK, where that person was residing in Israel, the Occupied Palestinian Territories (the West Bank, including East Jerusalem, and Gaza) or the occupied Golan Heights immediately before 13 June 2025, left those territories in connection with the violence which escalated on 13 June 2025 and arrived in the United Kingdom on or before 13 December 2025.

Regulations 5 to 9 amend the Disability Assistance for Children and Young People (Scotland) Regulations 2021, the Disability Assistance for Working Age People (Scotland) Regulations 2022, the Carer’s Assistance (Carer Support Payment) (Scotland) Regulations 2023, the Disability for Older People (Scotland) Regulations 2024 and the Disability Assistance (Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance) Regulations 2025 to insert the same category of person as inserted by regulations 2 to 4 into the list of persons who are exempt from having to satisfy the past presence test and habitual residence test. Additionally, they add provision for persons who have been granted, or who are deemed to have been granted, leave outside the immigration rules to this list.
