# The Children (Care and Justice) (Scotland) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 38(3) of the Children (Care and Justice) (Scotland) Act 2024[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Children (Care and Justice) (Scotland) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2025 and come into force on 15 December 2025.

(2) In these Regulations—

- (a) “**the Act**” means the Children (Care and Justice) (Scotland) Act 2024,
- (b) “**the 1995 Act**” means the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00002].

## 2. Appointed day

(1) 15 December 2025 is the appointed day for the coming into force of section 9 of the Act (support for victims in children’s hearings).

(2) 30 March 2026 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Act—

- (a) section 1 (age of referral to children’s hearing), for the purpose of its application to sections 50, 70B, 142 and 142A of the 1995 Act, as amended or, as the case may be, inserted by section 16 of the Act,
- (b) section 12 (meaning of “**child**”), for the purpose of its application to sections 50, 70B, 142 and 142A of the 1995 Act, as amended or, as the case may be, inserted by section 16 of the Act,
- (c) section 15 (custody of children before commencement of proceedings),
- (d) section 16 (steps to safeguard welfare and safety of children in criminal proceedings).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force various provisions of the Children (Care and Justice) (Scotland) Act 2024 (“**the Act**”).

Regulation 2(1) appoints 15 December 2025 for the coming into force of section 9 of the Act, which inserts a new section 179D into the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 requiring the Scottish Ministers, by regulations, to make provision for or in connection with the provision of support services to victims in the children’s hearings system.

Regulation 2(2) appoints 30 March 2026 for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Act:

- sections 1 and 12 of the Act, for the purpose of their application to sections 50, 70B, 142 and 142A of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995 (“**the 1995 Act**”), as amended or, as the case may be, inserted by section 16 of the Act,
- section 15, which amends the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2016 in relation to the custody of children involved in criminal proceedings, and
- section 16, which amends the 1995 Act, to make provision for steps to safeguard the welfare and safety of children involved in criminal proceedings (solemn and summary).

For the purposes of the above provisions of the 1995 Act, as so amended, a child means a person under the age of 18 years.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 4 June 2024. Sections 35, 36, 38 and 39 came into force the following day. Section 33 came into force on 16 July 2024.
