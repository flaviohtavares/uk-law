# The Registers of Scotland (Fees and Plain Copies) Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 103

Registers And Records

Moveable Transactions

The Registers of Scotland (Fees and Plain Copies) Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2025

Made

27th March 2025

Coming into force

1st April 2025

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 107(1) and 110(1) and (2), of the Land Registration etc. (Scotland) Act 2012[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 110(3) of that Act, the Scottish Ministers have consulted the Keeper of the Registers of Scotland about (among other things), the expenses incurred by the Keeper in relation to the matters specified in section 110(3)(a) of that Act, and the desirability of encouraging registering, recording and entering in any register under the management and control of the Keeper.

In accordance with section 116(3)(k) and (l) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Registers of Scotland (Fees and Plain Copies) Miscellaneous Amendments Order 2025 and comes into force on 1<sup></sup>April 2025.

(6) After Part 7, insert—

> **PART 8 — Register of Assignations**
>
> Article 3(g)
>
> **11. Registration Fees**
>
> (1) Where an application for registration of an assignation document is made under section 23(1) (application for registration) of the 2023 Act, the fee is £30.
>
> (2) Where an assignation document mentioned in sub-paragraph (1) assigns different claims to different assignees, the fee is £30 for each claim for which registration is applied.
>
> **PART 9 — Register of Statutory Pledges**
>
> Article 3(h) and (i)
>
> **12. Registration Fees**
>
> (1) Where an application for registration of a constitutive document is made under section 86(1) (application for registration of statutory pledge) of the 2023 Act, the fee is £30.
>
> (2) Where a constitutive document mentioned in sub-paragraph (1) creates more than one statutory pledge, the fee is £5 for each additional statutory pledge for which registration is applied.
>
> (3) Where an application for registration of an amendment document is made under section 88(1) (application for registration of amendment) of the 2023 Act, the fee is £30 for each statutory pledge record entry amended.
>
> **13. Correction Fees**
>
> (1) Where an application for correction of an entry in the statutory pledges record is made under section 96(1) (application by secured creditor for correction of statutory pledges record) of the 2023 Act, the fee is £10 for each entry corrected.
>
> (2) Where an application for correction of an entry in the statutory pledges record is made under section 98(6) (demand that application for correction be made under section 96), the fee is £10 for each entry corrected.
>
> (3) Where an application for registration of an amendment document as mentioned in paragraph 12(3) is made that also corrects the statutory pledges record in terms of section 96(1) of the 2023 Act, no additional correction fee is payable.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends both the Registers of Scotland (Fees) Order 2014 (“**the Fees Order**”) and the Registers of Scotland (Information and Access) Order 2014 (“**the Information Order**”) to provide for:

- the fees payable in relation to registration, correction, providing access to and information from the Register of Assignations and the Register of Statutory Pledges, both created by the Moveable Transactions (Scotland) Act 2023; and
- information to be made available by the Keeper of the Registers of Scotland (“the Keeper”) in relation to the Register of Assignations and the Register of Statutory Pledges.

Article 2 amends the Fees Order by:

- extending the list in article 3 to provide for the fees payable in relation to registration in the Register of Assignations & registration and correction in the Register of Statutory Pledges;
- extending the table of fees in Part 7 of schedule 1 to provide for the fees payable for plain copies and extracts of information in either the Register of Assignations or the Register of Statutory Pledges and also to correct two minor typographical errors in [S.S.I. 2021/139](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2021/139);
- amending schedule 1 by inserting two new Parts. Part 8 details the fees payable in relation to registration in the Register of Assignations. Part 9 details the fees payable in relation to registration and correction in the Register of Statutory Pledges.

Article 3 amends the Information Order to provide for the application and issue by the Keeper of plain copies of information in the Register of Assignations or the Register of Statutory Pledges.
