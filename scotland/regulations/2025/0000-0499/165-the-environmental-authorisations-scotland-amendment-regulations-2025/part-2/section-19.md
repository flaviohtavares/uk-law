## 19. Amendment of regulation 37 (power to require the provision of information)[^c-1e21b6edb6dd5363cdcc146d8b28ddd5]

*Extent: S*

In regulation 37(2)(b)(iii) (power to require the provision of information), after “waste” both times it appears, insert, “, including radioactive waste,”.
