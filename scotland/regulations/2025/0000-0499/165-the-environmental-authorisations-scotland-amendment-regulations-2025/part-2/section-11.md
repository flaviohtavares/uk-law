# The Environmental Authorisations (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2025, section 11

> Text as at 2026-03-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 11. Amendment of regulation 17 (form and content of a registration)[^key-af56ebb36d6d54e09de8ec400b367067]

*Extent: S*

In regulation 17 (form and content of a registration)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(c), for “the” where it first appears, substitute “any”,
- (b) in paragraph (2), for “the”, substitute “any”,
- (c) in paragraph (5)—
  - (i) omit “either or both”,
  - (ii) after sub-paragraph (b), at the end, insert—

    > ,
    >
    > - (c) any standard conditions, in circumstances where no standard conditions were specified at the time the registration was granted

    .
