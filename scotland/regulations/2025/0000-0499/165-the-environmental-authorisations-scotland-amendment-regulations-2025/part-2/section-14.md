# The Environmental Authorisations (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2025, section 14

> Text as at 2026-03-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 14. Amendment of regulation 24 (review of permits)[^key-5815563ee339d91ba48f3d08084e6959]

*Extent: S*

In regulation 24 (review of permits)—

- (a) re-number the existing provision as paragraph (1),
- (b) after paragraph (1) as re-numbered, insert—

  > (2) Where SEPA carries out a review under paragraph (1), any fee payable under a charging scheme for that review is payable by the authorised person.

  .
