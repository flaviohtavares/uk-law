## 31. Amendment of regulation 63 (accelerated applications)[^c-36d923f7eb9759e25210f924334fbe6e]

*Extent: S*

In regulation 63 (accelerated applications)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(b), after “initiated by SEPA”, insert “in relation to a permit”,
- (b) in paragraph (6), in the opening words, delete “in so far as it is practicable to do so”.
