# The Environmental Authorisations (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2025, section 8

> Text as at 2026-03-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 8. Amendment of regulation 5 (interpretation: authorised person)[^key-f7e1983708b383d10a2cd59c360f295a]

*Extent: S*

In regulation 5 (interpretation: authorised person)—

- (a) in paragraph (2)(c), in the opening words, for “the” substitute “an”,
- (b) after paragraph (3), insert—

  > (4) Where a person becomes an authorised person—
  >
  > - (a) as a result of the operation of paragraph (2),
  > - (b) in respect of an activity which is carried on in accordance with a permit or a registration, and
  > - (c) there is a regulatory notice, surrender notice or costs recovery notice in effect in respect of that permit or registration,
  >
  > the duty to comply with the notice is transferred to that person.

  .
