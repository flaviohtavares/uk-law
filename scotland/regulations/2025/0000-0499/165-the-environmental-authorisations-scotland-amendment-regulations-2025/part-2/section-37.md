# The Environmental Authorisations (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2025, section 37

> Text as at 2026-03-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 37. Amendment of schedule 3 (register)[^key-111da5ea48827ee0531d50447dbc9f64]

*Extent: S*

In schedule 3 (register)—

- (a) in Table 1—
  - (i) after entry 7, insert—

    > |  |  |
    > | --- | --- |
    > | 7A. | Any notice given by SEPA to an applicant under paragraph 6 of schedule 1, and where applicable, any information provided in response to such a notice. |

    ,
  - (ii) after entry 13, insert—

    > |  |  |
    > | --- | --- |
    > | 13A. | Any licence, permit, registration or authorisation granted by SEPA which is a deemed permit, registration or authorisation under these Regulations. |

    ,
  - (iii) in entry 37, for “Any” substitute “An”,
  - (iv) in entry 38, for “inspection report” substitute “programme of inspections (as defined in schedule 8)”,
  - (v) after entry 38, insert—

    > <table>
    > <tbody>
    > <tr><td>39.</td><td colspan="2">Any exemptions applied by SEPA in accordance with regulation 24(3) of the 2013 Regulations.</td></tr>
    > <tr><td>40.</td><td colspan="2">Notification of a refusal by SEPA under paragraph 9(1) of schedule 1 for an application in relation to a waste incineration plant or waste co-incineration plant.</td></tr>
    > <tr><td>41.</td><td colspan="2">A list of all waste incineration plant and co-incineration plant with a nominal capacity of less than 2 tonnes per hour in operation.</td></tr>
    > <tr><td rowspan="9">42.</td><td colspan="2">In relation to any decision on an application or SEPA initiated variation for a schedule 20 emissions activity—</td></tr>
    > <tr><td>(a)</td><td>notification of a refusal by SEPA under paragraph 9(1) of schedule 1,</td></tr>
    > <tr><td>(b)</td><td>the reasons for any decision under paragraph 9(1) of schedule 1,</td></tr>
    > <tr><td>(c)</td><td>how any representations made in response to a consultation by SEPA were taken into account by SEPA in reaching their decision,</td></tr>
    > <tr><td>(d)</td><td>the title of the BAT reference documents as defined in paragraph 3(1) of schedule 19, relevant to the activity,</td></tr>
    > <tr><td>(e)</td><td>how the permit conditions, including emission limit values have been determined in relation to the best available techniques and emission levels associated with the best available techniques,</td></tr>
    > <tr><td>(f)</td><td>where a derogation is granted under paragraph 9(6) of schedule 20, the reasons for that derogation with reference to the criteria in paragraph 9(6) of schedule 20,</td></tr>
    > <tr><td>(g)</td><td>that the decision is subject to consultations with a neighbouring state(s) as a result of paragraph 18 of schedule 20.</td></tr>
    > <tr><td colspan="2">The information in sub-paragraphs (a), (b) (f) must be made available electronically.</td></tr>
    > <tr><td>43.</td><td colspan="2">In relation to a decision to grant a surrender application for a schedule 20 emissions activity, a copy of any notice issued by SEPA under paragraph 15(5)(b) of schedule 1 confirming that steps specified in a surrender notice have been satisfied.<br>This information must be made available electronically.</td></tr>
    > <tr><td>44.</td><td colspan="2">The site visit report in relation to an inspection conducted under paragraph 14 of schedule 20.</td></tr>
    > </tbody>
    > </table>

    ,
  - (vi) in entries 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 13, 14, 15 and 32, insert, at the end—

    > Where related to an industrial emissions activity or operating a medium combustion plant, this information must be made available electronically.

    ,
- (b) in paragraph 1—
  - (i) after “non-compliance penalty”, insert “and”,
  - (ii) omit “and “notice of intent””.
