# The Environmental Authorisations (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2025, section 38

> Text as at 2026-03-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 38. Amendment of schedule 4 (provisions relating to appeals)[^key-857a1304ff00a9a740c32a21298434f8]

*Extent: S*

In schedule 4 (provisions relating to appeals)—

- (a) in paragraph 4—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (b) before “withdrawal” insert “deemed”,
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (c) delete “of determination”,
  - (iii) in sub-paragraph (d)—
    - (aa) for “the authorised person”, substitute “another person”,
    - (bb) for “an authorisation”, substitute “a notice or permit”,
    - (cc) for “of the notice”, substitute “that a copy of the notice or permit is”,
    - (dd) for “3” substitute “4”,
- (b) in paragraph 24, for “inquiry” substitute “hearing”.
