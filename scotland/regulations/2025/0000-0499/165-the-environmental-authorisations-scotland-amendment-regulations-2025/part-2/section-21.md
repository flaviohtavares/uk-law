> Text as at 2026-03-26.

## 21. Amendment of regulation 41 (application for commercial confidentiality)[^c-2c46fc5dcfa64db0b2289a773886e5be]

*Extent: S*

In regulation 41 (application for commercial confidentiality), after paragraph (1), insert—

> - (1A) Where an application for commercial confidentiality is made to SEPA, any fee payable under a charging scheme for the application is payable by the applicant or the authorised person, as appropriate.
