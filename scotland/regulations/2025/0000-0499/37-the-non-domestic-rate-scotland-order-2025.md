# The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 37

RATING AND VALUATION

The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2025

Made

18th February 2025

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

20th February 2025

Coming into force

1st April 2025

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7B(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2025 and comes into force on 1 April 2025.

## 2. Non-domestic rate for 2025-2026

For the purposes of section 7B of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, the Scottish Ministers specify a rate of 49.8 pence per pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of the financial year 2025-2026.

IVAN MCKEE

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew's House

Edinburgh

18th February 2025

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies a rate of 49.8 pence in the pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of the financial year 2025-2026. A rate of 49.8 pence in the pound was also prescribed by the Scottish Ministers as the non-domestic rate for the financial years 2022-2023 ([S.S.I. 2022/36](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2022/36)), 2023-2024 ([S.S.I. 2023/29](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2023/29)), and 2024-2025 ([S.S.I. 2024/3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2024/3)).
