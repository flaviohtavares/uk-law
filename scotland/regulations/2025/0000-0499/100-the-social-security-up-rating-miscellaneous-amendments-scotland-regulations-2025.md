# The Social Security (Up-rating) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 100

Social Security

The Social Security (Up-rating) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2025

Made

27th March 2025

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(1) of the Social Security Act 1988[^f00001], section 70(8) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^f00002], sections 28(2), 30(2), 31(2), 32(2), 34(2), 79(1) and 95 of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018[^f00003] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 13(2) of the Social Security Act 1988, they have consulted with the Welsh Ministers[^f00004].

In accordance with section 97(2) of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018, the Scottish Ministers have informed the Scottish Commission on Social Security of their proposals, notified the Scottish Parliament that they have done so and made their proposals publicly available by such means as they consider appropriate.

In accordance with section 96(2) of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018[^f00005], a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Up-rating) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2025.

(2) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4) these Regulations come into force on 1 April 2025.

(3) Regulation 13 comes into force on 6 April 2025.

(4) Regulations 2, 9, 10, 11, 14 and 16(3) come into force on 7 April 2025.

(5) In these Regulations—

- “**the Adult Disability Payment Regulations 2022**” means the Disability Assistance for Working Age People (Scotland) Regulations 2022[^f00006],

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations provide for an increase in various values and amounts of forms of assistance payable by virtue of Regulations made under the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 and make amendments to the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) Regulations 1976, and the Welfare Foods (Best Start Foods) (Scotland) Regulations 2019.

Regulation 2 amends regulation 8(1) of the Social Security (Invalid Care Allowance) Regulations 1976 so that the value of earnings at which a person shall be treated as gainfully employed is increased.

Regulation 13(2) amends regulation 14(1) of the Carer’s Assistance (Carer Support Payment) (Scotland) Regulations 2023 so that the value of the earnings limit (in any award week) for entitlement to carer support payment is increased.

Regulation 13(3) amends schedule 2 (calculation of earnings) of the Carer’s Assistance (Carer Support Payment) (Scotland) Regulations 2023 so that any remuneration paid to the individual in respect of neonatal care leave is treated as earnings for assessing entitlement to Carer Support Payment.

Regulation 16(2) makes supplementary provision by way of amendment to regulation 8(2) (mobility requirements: higher rate mobility component) of the Disability Assistance (Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance) Regulations 2025 to specify that it is the individual’s physical condition as a whole that is taken into account when determining whether the individual meets the eligibility condition at regulation 8(2)(a).

The schedule contains saving and transitional provisions so that the previous values of the various forms of assistance are still payable in certain circumstances.

The Regulations come into force on 1 April 2025 with the exception of regulation 13 which comes into force on 6 April 2025, and regulations 2, 9, 10, 11, 14 and 16(3) which come into force on 7 April 2025.
