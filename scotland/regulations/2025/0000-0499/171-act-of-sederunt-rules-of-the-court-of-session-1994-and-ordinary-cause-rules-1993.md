# Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session 1994 and Ordinary Cause Rules 1993 Amendment) (Miscellaneous) (Amendment) 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 171

Court Of Session

Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session 1994 and Ordinary Cause Rules 1993 Amendment) (Miscellaneous) (Amendment) 2025

Made

5th June 2025

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

6th June 2025

Coming into force

1st July 2025

In accordance with section 4 of the Scottish Civil Justice Council and Criminal Legal Assistance Act 2013[^f00001], the Court of Session has approved draft rules submitted to it by the Scottish Civil Justice Council.

The Court of Session therefore makes this Act of Sederunt under the powers conferred by section 103(1) of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00002], section 48 of the Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982[^f00003] and all other powers enabling it to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement, etc.

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session 1994 and Ordinary Cause Rules 1993 Amendment) (Miscellaneous) (Amendment) 2025.

(2) It comes into force on 1st July 2025.

(3) A certified copy is to be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session 1994 and Ordinary Cause Rules 1993 Amendment) (Miscellaneous) 2025

(1) Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session 1994 and Ordinary Cause Rules 1993 Amendment) (Miscellaneous) 2025[^f00004] is amended in accordance with this paragraph.

(2) In paragraph 2(3)(f) (amendment of rule 62.46 of the Rules of the Court of Session 1994), for “rule 64.46(2)(c)” substitute “rule 62.46(2)(c)[^f00005]”.

(3) In paragraph 3(c)(v) (amendment of rule 62.28 of the Rules of the Court of Session 1994), for the new paragraph (3AAA) to be inserted substitute—

> (3AAA) For applications to which paragraph (1)(bb) applies, in the case referred to in Article 11 (judicial settlements) of the 2019 Hague Convention, in addition to the information specified in paragraph (2) there must be produced with the petition a certificate of a court of the State of origin that the judicial settlement or a part of it is enforceable in the same manner as a judgment in the State of origin.

.

PAUL CULLEN

Lord President

I.P.D.

Edinburgh

5th June 2025

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session 1994 and Ordinary Cause Rules 1993 Amendment) (Miscellaneous) 2025 (“2025 instrument”), which is not yet in force. Paragraph 2(2) corrects a typographical error in paragraph 2(3)(f) of the 2025 instrument. Paragraph 2(3) corrects an error in the new rule 62.28(3AAA) of the Rules of the Court of Session 1994 ([S.I. 1994/1443](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1994/1443)) which is to be inserted by the 2025 instrument. This instrument substitutes a new rule 62.28(3AAA) for the one to be inserted by the 2025 instrument to correct the error.
