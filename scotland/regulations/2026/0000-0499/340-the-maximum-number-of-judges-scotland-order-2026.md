# The Maximum Number of Judges (Scotland) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 340

COURT OF SESSION

The Maximum Number of Judges (Scotland) Order 2026

Made

11th November 2026

Coming into force

12th November 2026

At the Court at Buckingham Palace, the 11th day of November 2026

Present,

The King's Most Excellent Majesty in Council

This Order is made in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(2) of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00001] and all other powers enabling His Majesty to do so.

His Majesty is satisfied that the Lord President of the Court of Session has been consulted in accordance with section 1(3A) of that Act[^f00002].

In accordance with section 1(3) of that Act[^f00003], a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

Accordingly, His Majesty is pleased, by and with the advice of His Privy Council, to order as follows:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Maximum Number of Judges (Scotland) Order 2026 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Amendment of the Court of Session Act 1988

In section 1(1) of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00004] (maximum number of judges), for “36” substitute “40”.

## 3. Revocation

The Maximum Number of Judges (Scotland) Order 2022[^f00005] is revoked.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order amends section 1(1) of the Court of Session Act 1988 so as to increase the maximum number of persons who may be appointed as judges of the Court of Session from 36 to 40. Article 3 revokes the Maximum Number of Judges (Scotland) Order 2022 which had previously increased the maximum number of judges of the Court of Session to 36.
