The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 255(7)(a) of the Crime and Policing Act 2026[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Crime and Policing Act 2026 (Commencement No. 1) (Scotland) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 9 December 2026.

(2) In these Regulations, “**the Act**” means the Crime and Policing Act 2026.

## 2. Appointed day

(1) 9 December 2026 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Act which are specified in paragraph (2).

(2) The specified provisions are—

- (a) section 121 (police stations at which notifications may be given: Scotland and Northern Ireland),
- (b) section 126(1) and (2) (power of entry and search).

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force on 9 December 2026 sections 121 and 126(1) and (2) of the Crime and Policing Act 2026 (“**the Act**”). Under section 255(7) of the Act, the Scottish Ministers are able to commence certain of its provisions by regulations in relation to Scotland.

Section 121 of the Act replaces the current requirement in section 87(1)(a) of the Sexual Offences Act [2003 (c. 42)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/42) (“**the 2003 Act**”) for police stations at which sex offender notifications may be given to be prescribed in regulations with a requirement for the chief constable of Police Scotland to publish a list of police stations at which such notifications may be made. (For the meaning of “police area” and “chief officer of police” see schedule 1 of the Interpretation Act [1978 (c. 30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/30)).

Section 126(1) and (2) of the Act removes the current requirement for an application for a search warrant under section 96A of the 2003 Act (police powers of entry to and examination of relevant offender’s home address) to be made by a Police Scotland officer of at least superintendent rank. It provides instead for such applications to be made by any Police Scotland officer authorised to make the application by an officer of at least inspector rank.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 29 April 2026. Sections 69, 70, 84, 193 to 196, 240, 241, 243 to 245, 248, 249 and 251 to 257 and schedule 24 came into force on that day and sections 68, 181, 183 and 204 came partially into force on that day. In accordance with section 255(3) of the Act, sections 83, 96, 108 to 112, 164, 167, 168, 190 to 192, 200 to 203, 232, 235 to 237 and 250 and schedule 27 came into force on 29 June 2026.
