# The National Health Service (Common Staffing Method) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 54

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE

The National Health Service (Common Staffing Method) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2026

Made

4th February 2026

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

6th February 2026

Coming into force

1st April 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 12IJ(3) and 105(7) of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (Common Staffing Method) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2026 and come into force on 1 April 2026.

## 2. Amendment of the National Health Service (Common Staffing Method) (Scotland) Regulations 2024

(1) The National Health Service (Common Staffing Method) (Scotland) Regulations 2024[^f00002] are amended in accordance with this regulation.

(2) In regulation 4 (Professional judgement tool), for “Version 4” substitute “Version 5[^f00003]”.

(3) In the second column of the table in the schedule (Staffing level tools)—

- (a) in each place it appears, for “Version 4” substitute “Version 5[^f00004]”,
- (b) for “Maternity Staffing Level Tool Version 3” substitute “Maternity Services Staffing Level Tool Version 1[^f00005]”,
- (c) for “SCAMPS - Scottish Children’s Acuity Measurement in Paediatric Settings Version 3” substitute “SCAMPS - Scottish Children’s Acuity Measurement in Paediatric Settings Version 4[^f00006]”,
- (d) other than provided for in paragraphs (b) and (c), in each place it appears, for “Version 3” substitute “Version 4[^f00007]”, and
- (e) for “Version 1” substitute “Version 2[^f00008]”.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the National Health Service (Common Staffing Method) (Scotland) Regulations 2024 (“**2024 Regulations**”) which make provision in relation to the common staffing method. The common staffing method was introduced into the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 (“**1978 Act**”) by section 4 of the Health and Care (Staffing) (Scotland) Act 2019 and includes, among other things, the use of a “staffing level tool” and a “professional judgement tool” in determining the appropriate staffing levels for particular kinds of health care provision.

Regulation 2 makes amendments to provisions in the 2024 Regulations which prescribe, for the purposes of section 12IJ(3) of the 1978 Act, the professional judgement tool and the staffing level tools.

The professional judgement tool and the staffing level tools are publicly available online on Healthcare Improvement Scotland’s website at https://www.healthcareimprovementscotland.scot/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/HSP-SLT-Methodologies-and-Multipliers-January-2026.pdf.
