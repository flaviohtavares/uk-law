# The Wildlife Management and Muirburn (Scotland) Act 2024 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 240

NATURE CONSERVATION

The Wildlife Management and Muirburn (Scotland) Act 2024 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2026

Made

20th August 2026

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

24th August 2026

Coming into force

10th October 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 34(1) of the Wildlife Management and Muirburn (Scotland) Act 2024[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Wildlife Management and Muirburn (Scotland) Act 2024 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 10 October 2026.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Muirburn was previously regulated under the Hill Farming Act 1946 (“**the 1946 Act**”). Part 2 of the Wildlife Management and Muirburn (Scotland) Act 2024 (“**the 2024 Act**”) establishes a licensing scheme for the carrying out of muirburn. This scheme replaces the 1946 Act regime on 15 September 2026 when Part 2 of the 2024 Act comes fully into force and the majority of the muirburn provisions in the 1946 Act are repealed.

These Regulations make consequential amendments to secondary legislation relating to the environment in Scotland as a result of the commencement of Part 2 of the 2024 Act.
