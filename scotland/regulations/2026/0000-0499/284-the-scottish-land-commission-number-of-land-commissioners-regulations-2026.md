# The Scottish Land Commission (Number of Land Commissioners) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 284

LAND REFORM

PUBLIC BODIES

The Scottish Land Commission (Number of Land Commissioners) Regulations 2026

Made

6th October 2026

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

8th October 2026

Coming into force

3rd December 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by [section 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2016/18/section/4) [(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2016/18/section/4) of the [Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2016](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2016/18/contents)[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Scottish Land Commission (Number of Land Commissioners) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 3 December 2026.

## 2. Amendment of the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2016

In [section 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2016/18/section/4) [(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2016/18/section/4) [(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2016/18/section/4) (the Scottish Land Commission) of the [Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2016](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2016/18/contents), for “five” substitute “four”.

GILLIAN MARTIN

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

6th October 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2016 to change the composition of the Scottish Land Commission. Regulation 2 amends section 4(4)(a) to reduce the number of Land Commissioners from five to four.
