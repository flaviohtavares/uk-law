# The Provision of Early Learning and Childcare (Specified Children) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 329

Education

The Provision of Early Learning and Childcare (Specified Children) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2026

Made

4th November 2026

Coming into force

5th November 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 47(2)(c)(ii) of the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00001], and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 99(2) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Provision of Early Learning and Childcare (Specified Children) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2026 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Amendment of the Provision of Early Learning and Childcare (Specified Children) (Scotland) Order 2014

In article 1(2) of the Provision of Early Learning and Childcare (Specified Children) (Scotland) Order 2014[^f00002] (citation, commencement, interpretation and application), in the definition of “qualifying benefit”, on each occasion where it appears, for “£850” substitute “£885”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Provision of Early Learning and Childcare (Specified Children) (Scotland) Order 2014 (“**the 2014 Order**”). The 2014 Order specifies those children who are eligible pre-school children for the purposes of section 47(2)(c)(ii) of the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 and who are therefore entitled to the mandatory amount of early learning and childcare (as defined in section 48 of that Act).

The 2014 Order specifies that certain children whose parents are in receipt of “qualifying benefits” are eligible pre-school children. This Order amends the definition of “qualifying benefit” in article 1(2) of the 2014 Order. This has the effect that a child is an eligible pre-school child where the child’s parent is in receipt of universal credit under the Welfare Reform Act [2012 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/5) provided that the person’s (or, where the parent is a member of a couple, the couple’s) income does not exceed £885 in the assessment period preceding the application for early learning and childcare being made.
