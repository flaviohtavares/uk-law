# The Renewables Obligation (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 338

ELECTRICITY

The Renewables Obligation (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2026

Made

11th November 2026

Coming into force

12th November 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 32, 32A, 32J and 32K of the Electricity Act 1989[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 32L(1)[^f00002] of that Act the Scottish Ministers have consulted the Gas and Electricity Markets Authority, the National Association of Citizens Advice Bureaux, Consumer Scotland, electricity suppliers to whom this Order applies and such generators of electricity from renewable sources and other persons as the Scottish Ministers considered appropriate.

In accordance with section 32L(3)[^f00003] of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Renewables Obligation (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2026 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Amendment of the Renewables Obligation (Scotland) Order 2009

The Renewables Obligation (Scotland) Order 2009[^f00004] is amended in accordance with articles 3 to 8.

## 3. Amendment of article 2 (interpretation)

(1) In article 2(1)—

- (a) after “animal excreta” insert—

  > - “**BICS excluded electricity**” has the meaning given in regulation 3(1) of the Renewables Obligation and Electricity Capacity (Amendment) (British Industrial Competitiveness Scheme Excluded Electricity) Regulations 2026[^f00005];

  ,
- (b) in the definition of “relevant electricity”[^f00006]—
  - (i) after sub-paragraph (a) omit “and”,
  - (ii) after sub-paragraph (a) insert—

    > - (aa) in respect of—
    >   - (i) any obligation period in respect of which article 12D applies; or
    >   - (ii) any month of an obligation period in respect of which a revised obligation level applies in accordance with article 12E(2),
    >
    >   any electricity supplied to customers in Scotland other than EII excluded electricity and BICS excluded electricity; and

    .

## 4. Amendment of article 5 (the renewables obligation)

In article 5(2)(a)[^f00007] for “or 12C” substitute “, 12C, 12D or 12E”.

## 5. Amendment of article 12 (determining the number of renewables obligation certificates to be produced by a designated electricity supplier)

In article 12(A1)[^f00008] for “in respect of which article 12A applies” substitute “beginning on or after 1 April 2019”.

## 6. Amendment of article 12A (obligation periods beginning on or after 1st April 2019)

In article 12A[^f00009]—

- (a) at the end of the heading insert “and ending on or before 31st March 2027”,
- (b) in paragraph (1), after “1st April 2019” insert “and ending on or before 31st March 2027”.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Renewables Obligation (Scotland) Order 2009 (“**the 2009 Order**”).

The 2009 Order imposes an obligation (the “renewables obligation”) on all electricity suppliers which supply electricity in Scotland to produce a certain number of Scottish renewables obligation certificates in respect of electricity they supply to customers in Scotland during an “obligation period”. Each obligation period runs from 1 April to 31 March.

Article 3 amends article 2 of the 2009 Order to include a definition of “BICS excluded electricity” so that this proportion of electricity can then be excluded from a supplier’s liability under the 2009 Order. The definition of “relevant electricity” is accordingly updated. BICS excluded electricity is electricity used in respect of a manufacturing site which is used to carry out specified manufacturing activities.

Articles 4, 5, and 6 make amendments to the 2009 Order which are consequential on the amendment made by article 7. Article 7 inserts new articles 12D and 12E into the 2009 Order. New article 12D makes provision for the determination of suppliers’ renewables obligations for periods beginning on or after 1 April 2027. The formulas used to calculate suppliers’ liabilities take account of the exclusion of BICS excluded electricity. New article 12E enables the Scottish Ministers to revise the obligation level published on or before 1 October 2026 in respect of the 2027/28 obligation period. A revision may only be made once, and only before 1 April 2027.

Article 8 makes amendments to article 53(5) of the 2009 Order which have the effect that for obligation periods beginning on or after 1 April 2027, suppliers must, in addition to other information provided for in article 53, provide information to the Gas and Electricity Markets Authority in relation to EII excluded electricity (within the meaning of regulation 6(1) of the Electricity Supplier Obligations (Amendment & Excluded Electricity) Regulations 2015), and BICS excluded electricity.
