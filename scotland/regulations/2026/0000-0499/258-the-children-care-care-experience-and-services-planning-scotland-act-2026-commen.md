# The Children (Care, Care Experience and Services Planning) (Scotland) Act 2026 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

The Children (Care, Care Experience and Services Planning) (Scotland) Act 2026 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2026

Made

8th September 2026

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

10th September 2026

Coming into force

30th September 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 70(2) of the Children (Care, Care Experience and Services Planning) (Scotland) Act 2026[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Children (Care, Care Experience and Services Planning) (Scotland) Act 2026 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 30 September 2026.

(2) In these Regulations—

- “**the 2014 Act**” means the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00002],
- “**the Act**” means the Children (Care, Care Experience and Services Planning) (Scotland) Act 2026.

## 2. Appointed day

(1) 30 September 2026 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Act specified in column 1 of the table in the schedule (the subject matter of which is described in the corresponding entry in column 2 of that table).

(2) Where a purpose is specified in column 3 of that table, the corresponding provision specified in column 1 comes into force in accordance with paragraph (1) only for that purpose.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring provisions of the Children (Care, Care Experience and Services Planning) (Scotland) Act 2026 (“**the Act**”) into force. The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 15 May 2026. By virtue of section 70 of the Act, that section together with sections 67, 68 and 71 came into force on 16 May 2026.

Regulation 2(1) and the schedule bring the provisions of the Act specified in column 1 of the table in the schedule into force on 30 September 2026. Where relevant, these provisions are brought into force for the purposes stated in column 3 of that table only.
