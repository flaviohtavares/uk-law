# The Employment Tribunals Extension of Jurisdiction (Amendment) (Scotland) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 327

EMPLOYMENT TRIBUNALS

The Employment Tribunals Extension of Jurisdiction (Amendment) (Scotland) Order 2026

Made

3rd November 2026

Coming into force

9th November 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3, 8(3) and 41(4) of the Employment Tribunals Act 1996[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with the requirements of article 2 of the Scotland Act 1998 (Transfer of Functions to the Scottish Ministers etc.) Order 1999[^f00002], as read with schedule 1 of the Order, the Scottish Ministers have obtained the agreement of the Secretary of State to the making of this Order.

In accordance with section 41(2)(a) of the Employment Tribunals Act 1996, a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Employment Tribunals Extension of Jurisdiction (Amendment) (Scotland) Order 2026 and comes into force on 9 November 2026.

## 2. Amendment of the Employment Tribunals (Extension of Jurisdiction) (Scotland) Order 1994

(1) The Employment Tribunals Extension of Jurisdiction (Scotland) Order 1994[^f00003] is amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In article 7(a) and (b) (time within which proceedings may be brought) for “three” substitute “six”.

## 3. Transitional provision

(1) The amendments made by article 2 apply where—

- (a) the effective date of termination of the contract giving rise to the claim occurs on or after 9 November 2026, or
- (b) there is no effective date of termination and the last day on which the employee worked in the employment which has been terminated occurs on or after 9 November 2026.

(2) In this article, “the effective date of termination”—

- (a) in relation to an employee whose contract of employment is terminated by notice, whether given by their employer or by the employee, means the date on which the notice expires,
- (b) in relation to an employee whose contract of employment is terminated without notice, means the date on which the termination takes effect, and
- (c) in relation to an employee who is employed under a limited-term contract which terminates by virtue of the limiting event without being renewed under the same contract, means the date on which the termination takes effect.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Employment Tribunals Extension of Jurisdiction (Scotland) Order 1994 (“**the 1994 Order**”). Article 2 amends article 7 of the 1994 Order to extend the time limit for bringing proceedings before an employment tribunal in Scotland, in respect of employees’ contract of employment claims, from three months to six months. Employees’ contract of employment claims include claims for breach of a contract of employment, or for a sum due under such a contract, where the claim arises or is outstanding on the termination of the employment.

Article 3 defines “the effective date of termination” and makes transitional provision so that the amendments apply only where the effective date of termination of the contract of employment giving rise to the claim – or, where there is no effective date of termination, the last day on which the employee worked in the employment which has terminated – occurs on or after 9 November 2026.
