# The Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (Dumfries and Galloway Council) Designation Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 296

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (Dumfries and Galloway Council) Designation Order 2026

Made

27th October 2026

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

29th October 2026

Coming into force

7th December 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 1(1), 2(1) and 3(3) of schedule 3 of the Road Traffic Act 1991[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with paragraphs 1(1)(d) and 2(1)(c) of that schedule the Scottish Ministers have received an application for this Order from Dumfries and Galloway Council[^f00002].

In accordance with paragraphs 1(3) and 2(3) of that schedule the Scottish Ministers have consulted the chief constable of the Police Service of Scotland.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic (Permitted Parking Area and Special Parking Area) (Dumfries and Galloway Council) Designation Order 2026 and comes into force on 7 December 2026.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “**the 1991 Act**” means the Road Traffic Act 1991,
- “**the parking area**” means the area designated as a permitted parking area and a special parking area by article 3.

## 3. Designation as permitted parking area and special parking area

That part of the Dumfries and Galloway local government area[^f00003] specified in schedule 1 is designated as—

- (a) a permitted parking area, and
- (b) a special parking area.

## 4. Modifications and application of Part II of the 1991 Act

Sections 66, 69 to 74, 79 and 82 and schedule 6 of the 1991 Act apply in relation to the parking area subject to the modifications specified in schedule 2.

## 5. Modifications of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984

The Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00004] is modified in relation to the parking area as specified in schedule 3.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order applies, to the Dumfries and Galloway local government area, arrangements for enforcing parking controls already available in London and certain other areas in England and in Edinburgh, Glasgow, Perth and Kinross, Aberdeen, Dundee, South Lanarkshire, Renfrewshire, East Ayrshire, South Ayrshire, East Renfrewshire, Fife, East Dunbartonshire, Argyll and Bute, Inverclyde, Highland, East Lothian, Angus, Stirling, North Lanarkshire, Midlothian, Falkirk, North Ayrshire and West Dunbartonshire local government areas.

The Order designates Dumfries and Galloway local government area (with specified exceptions) as a permitted parking area and as a special parking area in accordance with schedule 3 of the Road Traffic Act 1991 (“**the 1991 Act**”).

The Order applies, to the designated area, various provisions of the 1991 Act and modifies them where necessary. It also makes consequential modifications to certain provisions of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 (“**the 1984 Act**”) dealing with parking and related matters.

While the Order is in force, certain specified offences will be decriminalised: for example, in the case of the permitted parking area, breaches of orders relating to on‑street parking places and, in the case of the special parking area, orders prohibiting or restricting waiting, loading and unloading.

Enforcement will be carried out by parking attendants provided under section 63A of the 1984 Act. The First-tier Tribunal will hear appeals against decisions by the parking authority.
