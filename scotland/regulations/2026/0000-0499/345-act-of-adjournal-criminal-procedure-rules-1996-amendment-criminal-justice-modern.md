# Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules 1996 Amendment) (Criminal Justice Modernisation and Abusive Domestic Behaviour Reviews (Scotland) Act 2025) 2026

The High Court of Justiciary makes this Act of Adjournal under the powers conferred on it by section 305 of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00001] and all other powers enabling it to do so.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation, etc.

(1) This Act of Adjournal may be cited as the Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules 1996 Amendment) (Criminal Justice Modernisation and Abusive Domestic Behaviour Reviews (Scotland) Act 2025) 2026.

(2) Paragraph 2(2) and schedule 1 come into force on the date that section 11 of the 2025 Act comes into force.

(3) Paragraph 2(3) and schedule 2 come into force on the date that section 5 of the 2025 Act comes into force.

(4) Paragraph 2(4) and schedule 3 come into force on the date that section 7 of the 2025 Act comes into force.

(5) In this Act of Adjournal, “**the 2025 Act**” means the Criminal Justice Modernisation and Abusive Domestic Behaviour Reviews (Scotland) Act 2025[^f00002].

(6) A certified copy is to be inserted in the Books of Adjournal.

## 2. Amendment of the Criminal Procedure Rules 1996

(1) The Criminal Procedure Rules 1996[^f00003] are amended in accordance with this paragraph.

(2) In Chapter 8 (the indictment)[^f00004], after rule 8.3 (notice of previous convictions), insert—

> **8.4. Addition of charge to indictment**
>
> An application under section 70AA(1) (addition of charge to indictment) of the Act of 1995 is to be in Form 8.4.

.

(3) After Chapter 26 (documentary evidence)[^f00005], insert—

> **CHAPTER 26A — DIGITAL PRODUCTIONS**
>
> **26A.1. Application for a direction relating to an image of physical evidence**
>
> (1) A written application under section 279B(2) (images of physical evidence) of the Act of 1995 is to be in Form 26A.1.
>
> (2) A copy of an application under section 279B(2) of the Act of 1995 must be served on all other parties in the proceedings by any of the methods of service in rule 2.3 (general provisions for service).

.

(4) In Chapter 27 (routine evidence, sufficient evidence and proof of previous convictions)[^f00006], after rule 27.3 (form of notice in relation to certain evidential certificates) insert—

> **27.3A. Form of notice in relation to body-worn video evidence**
>
> A notice under section 283A(3) (evidence as to time and place of body-worn video recordings) of the Act of 1995 is to be in Form 27.3-A.

.

(5) In the appendix—

- (a) after Form 8.3 (form of notice of previous convictions in solemn proceedings)[^f00007] insert Form 8.4 (form of application for the addition of a charge to the indictment under section 70AA(1) of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995) as set out in schedule 1 of this Act of Adjournal;
- (b) after Form 26.1-C (form of certificate that statement not contained in business document)[^f00008] insert Form 26A.1 (form of application for a direction relating to an image of physical evidence under section 279B(2) of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995) as set out in schedule 2 of this Act of Adjournal;
- (c) after Form 27.3 (form of notice in relation to certain evidential certificates)[^f00009] insert Form 27.3-A (form of notice disputing the accuracy of the time, date or place of the events recorded) as set out in schedule 3 of this Act of Adjournal.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Act of Adjournal)

This Act of Adjournal makes various amendments to the Criminal Procedure Rules 1996 (“**the 1996 Rules**”) in connection with the Criminal Justice Modernisation and Abusive Domestic Behaviour Reviews (Scotland) Act 2025 (“**the 2025 Act**”).

Paragraph 2(2) and schedule 1 insert a new Rule 8.4 and associated Form 8.4 into the 1996 Rules. These changes make provision for a procedure by which the Crown can seek to add a charge to the indictment, further to section 11 of the 2025 Act.

Paragraph 2(3) and schedule 2 insert a new Chapter 26A and associated Form 26A.1 into the 1996 Rules. These changes make provision for an application to be made to the court seeking a direction in relation to an image of physical evidence, further to section 5 of the 2025 Act.

Paragraph 2(4) and schedule 3 insert new Rule 27.3A and associated Form 27.3-A into the 1996 Rules. These changes make provision for notice to be given to the prosecutor that an accused does not accept the evidence as to the time, date and/or place of body-worn video evidence, further to section 7 of the 2025 Act.
