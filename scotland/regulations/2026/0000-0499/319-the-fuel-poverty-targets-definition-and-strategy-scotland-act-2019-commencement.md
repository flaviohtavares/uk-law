# The Fuel Poverty (Targets, Definition and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 319 (C. 35)

HOUSING

The Fuel Poverty (Targets, Definition and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2026

Made

28th October 2026

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

30th October 2026

Coming into force

9th November 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 20(2) of the Fuel Poverty (Targets, Definition and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These regulations may be cited as the Fuel Poverty (Targets, Definition and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 9 November 2026.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 18(3) and (4) of the Fuel Poverty (Targets, Definition and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019 is 9 November 2026.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations commence section 18(3) and (4) of the Fuel Poverty (Targets, Definition and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019. Section 18(3) and 18(4) makes consequential amendments to section 89 and 95 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 to amend the definition of fuel poverty.
