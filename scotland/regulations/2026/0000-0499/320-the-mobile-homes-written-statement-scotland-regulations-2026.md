# The Mobile Homes (Written Statement) (Scotland) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

30th October 2026

Coming into force

9th December 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(2)(e) of the Mobile Homes Act 1983[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Mobile Homes (Written Statement) (Scotland) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 9 December 2026.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to any written statement given on or after 9 December 2026 with respect to an agreement to which the 1983 Act applies in Scotland.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations—

- “**the 1983 Act**” means the Mobile Homes Act 1983,
- “**written statement**” means the written statement that the owner of a protected site is required by section 1(2) of the 1983 Act (particulars of agreements) to give to the proposed occupier.

## 3. Written statement: prescribed requirements

The requirement with which a written statement must comply for the purposes of section 1(2) of the 1983 Act (in addition to the requirements of section 1(2)(a) to (d) of the 1983 Act) is that it must be in the form set out in the schedule of these Regulations or a form substantially to the same effect.

## 4. Revocation

The Mobile Homes (Written Statement) (Scotland) Regulations 2013[^f00002] are revoked.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Mobile Homes Act 1983 (“**the 1983 Act**”) applies to all agreements under which persons are entitled to station a mobile home on a protected site and occupy it as their only or main residence. Section 1(2) of the 1983 Act provides that before such an agreement is entered into, the site owner must give to the proposed occupier of the mobile home a written statement. These regulations set out the form that such a written statement must be in, where that statement is given on or after 9 December 2026 (regulation 1(2)).

Regulation 3 provides that the written statement must be in the form set out in the schedule.

Regulation 4 revokes the Mobile Homes (Written Statement) Regulations 2013.

The schedule sets out the form that a written statement must be in.

Part 1 of the written statement contains information about the occupier’s rights under the agreement.

Part 2 of the written statement sets out the main provisions of the agreement. These are name and address of the parties, particulars of the land, pitch fee, its review and additional charges.

Part 3 of the written statement sets out the terms to be implied by section 2(1) of the 1983 Act.

Part 4 of the written statement contains other express terms of the agreement.

A Business and Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as it has no significant impact on the private or voluntary sectors.
