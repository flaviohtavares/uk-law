# The Representation of the People (Postal Voting for Local Government Elections) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 317

REPRESENTATION OF THE PEOPLE

The Representation of the People (Postal Voting for Local Government Elections) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2026

Made

29th October 2026

Coming into force

30th October 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by [sections 53](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2/section/53) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2/section/53) [(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2/section/53) and [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2/section/53) and 201(3) and paragraph 12 of schedule 2 of the [Representation of the People Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2/contents)[^f00001] (“**the 1983 Act**”) and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 7(1) and (2)(e) of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000[^f00002], the Scottish Ministers have consulted with the Electoral Commission.

In accordance with section 201(2) of the 1983 Act[^f00003], a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation, commencement and transitional provision

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Representation of the People (Postal Voting for Local Government Elections) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2026 and come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

(2) These Regulations have no effect for the purposes of any election for which the date of the poll is on or before 5 May 2027.

## 2. Amendment of the Representation of the People (Postal Voting for Local Government Elections) (Scotland) Regulations 2007

(1) The Representation of the People (Postal Voting for Local Government Elections) (Scotland) Regulations 2007[^f00004] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 15(3) (spoilt postal ballot papers)[^f00005], for “10 p.m.” substitute “5 p.m.”.

(3) In regulation 16 (lost ballot papers)[^f00006]—

- (a) in paragraph (2), for “10 p.m.” substitute “5 p.m.”,
- (b) in paragraph (6), for “10 p.m.” substitute “5 p.m.”.

JAMIE HEPBURN

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House

Edinburgh

29th October 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Representation of the People (Absent Voting at Local Government Elections) (Scotland) Regulations 2007.

Regulation 2 changes the time limit on the day of the poll by which voters utilising a postal vote for local government elections may request a replacement of a spoilt or lost ballot paper. The time limits are changed from 10 p.m. to 5 p.m., reversing the changes made by [S.S.I. 2021/436](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2021/436).
