# The Community Wealth Building (Scotland) Act 2026 (Commencement) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 265 (C. 28)

ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT

The Community Wealth Building (Scotland) Act 2026 (Commencement) Regulations 2026

Made

16th September 2026

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

18th September 2026

Coming into force

1st October 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 14(2) of the Community Wealth Building (Scotland) Act 2026[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Community Wealth Building (Scotland) Act 2026 (Commencement) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 1 October 2026.

## 2. Appointed day

1 October 2026 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the Community Wealth Building (Scotland) Act 2026, so far as not already in force.

IVAN MCKEE

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House

Edinburgh

16th September 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations appoint 1 October 2026 as the day on which the remaining provisions of the Community Wealth Building (Scotland) Act 2026 (“**the Act**”) come into force. The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 25 March 2026. Sections 12, 13, 14 and 15 came into force the day after Royal Assent.
