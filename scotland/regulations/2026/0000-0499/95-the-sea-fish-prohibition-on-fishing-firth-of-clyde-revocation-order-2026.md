# The Sea Fish (Prohibition on Fishing) (Firth of Clyde) Revocation Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 95

Sea Fisheries

Conservation Of Sea Fish

The Sea Fish (Prohibition on Fishing) (Firth of Clyde) Revocation Order 2026

Made

at 9.15 a.m. on 17th February 2026

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

at 1.15 p.m. on 17th February 2026

Coming into force

18th February 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 5(1)(a), 5(2) and 20(1) of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Sea Fish (Prohibition on Fishing) (Firth of Clyde) Revocation Order 2026 and comes into force on 18 February 2026.

## 2. Revocation

The Sea Fish (Prohibition on Fishing) (Firth of Clyde) Order 2026[^f00002] is revoked.

MAIRI GOUGEON

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

At 9.15 a.m. on 17th February 2026

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Sea Fish (Prohibition on Fishing) (Firth of Clyde) Order 2026. That Order prohibited all methods of fishing within specified areas of the Firth of Clyde and also provided exemptions within specified areas.
