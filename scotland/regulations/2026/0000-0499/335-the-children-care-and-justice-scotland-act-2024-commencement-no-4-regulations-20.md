# The Children (Care and Justice) (Scotland) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 335 (C. 40)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The Children (Care and Justice) (Scotland) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2026

Made

10th November 2026

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

12th November 2026

Coming into force

1st December 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 38(3) of the Children (Care and Justice) (Scotland) Act 2024[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Children (Care and Justice) (Scotland) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 4) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 1 December 2026.

(2) In these Regulations—

- “**the Act**” means the Children (Care and Justice) (Scotland) Act 2024,
- “**the 2010 Act**” means the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010[^f00002],
- “**the 2011 Act**” means the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00003].

## 2. Appointed day

1 December 2026 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Act—

- (a) section 2(3) (children’s hearing: duty to have regard to the effects of trauma on the child),
- (b) section 3(1) (child assessment and child protection measures:secure accommodation), for the purpose of its application to the amendment to the 2011 Act in section 3(7) of the Act,
- (c) section 3(7) (emergency placement of child in secure accommodation), for the purpose of enabling the Scottish Ministers to make regulations under section 57B of the 2011 Act (as inserted by section 3(7) of the Act) about the emergency placement of a child in secure accommodation,
- (d) section 6(3) (compulsory supervision orders: movement restriction conditions),
- (e) section 27(1) (secure accommodation services), for the purpose of its application to the amendments to the 2010 Act in section 27(2), (3) and (4) of the Act,
- (f) section 27(2), (3) and (4) (secure accommodation services), for the purpose of enabling the Scottish Ministers to make regulations under section 78A of the 2010 Act (as inserted by section 27(2) of the Act) about the approval of secure accommodation services.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force various provisions of the Children (Care and Justice) (Scotland) Act 2024 (“**the Act**”).

Regulation 2 appoints 1 December 2026 for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Act—

- section 2(3) (children’s hearing: duty to have regard to the effects of trauma on the child),
- section 3(1) (child assessment and child protection measures:secure accommodation), but only for the purpose of its application to the amendment to the 2011 Act in section 3(7) of the Act,
- section 3(7) (emergency placement of child in secure accommodation), but only for the purpose of enabling the Scottish Ministers to make regulations under section 57B of the 2011 Act (as inserted by section 3(7) of the Act) about the emergency placement of a child in secure accommodation,
- section 6(3) (compulsory supervision orders: movement restriction conditions),
- section 27(1) (secure accommodation services), but only for the purpose of its application to the amendments to the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 (“**the 2010 Act**”) in sections 27(2), (3) and (4) of the Act,
- section 27(2), (3) and (4) (secure accommodation services), but only for the purpose of enabling the Scottish Ministers to make regulations under section 78A of the 2010 Act (as inserted by section 27(2) of the Act) about the approval of secure accommodation services.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 4 June 2024. Sections 35, 36, 38 and 39 came into force the following day. Section 33 came into force on 16 July 2024.
