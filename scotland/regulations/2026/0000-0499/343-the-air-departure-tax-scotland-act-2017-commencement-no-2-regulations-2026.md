Made

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

Coming into force

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 48(2) of the Air Departure Tax (Scotland) Act 2017[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Air Departure Tax (Scotland) Act 2017 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 1 December 2026.

(2) In these Regulations, “**the Act**” means the Air Departure Tax (Scotland) Act 2017.

## 2. Appointed day

1 December 2026 is the day appointed for the coming into force, so far as not already in force, of the provisions of the Act specified in column 1 of the table in the schedule of these Regulations (the subject matter of which is described in the corresponding entry in column 2 of that table).

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force a number of provisions in Part 2 (key concepts), Part 3 (tax rates) (to the extent that the relevant provisions are not already in force), and Part 4 (payment, collection and management of tax) of the Air Departure Tax (Scotland) Act 2017 (“**the Act**”) on 1 December 2026. These provisions are required in connection with facilitating the voluntary registration of taxpayers in advance of Air Departure Tax (“**ADT**”) coming into operation on 1 April 2027 and to enable the Scottish Ministers to make regulations concerning tax bands and tax rate amounts for ADT.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 25 July 2017. Sections 42, 43, 44 and 46 to 49, schedule 1 and schedule 3 came into force the following day.
