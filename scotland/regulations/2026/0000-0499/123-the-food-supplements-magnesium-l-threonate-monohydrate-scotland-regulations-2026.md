# The Food Supplements (Magnesium L-threonate monohydrate) (Scotland) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 123

FOOD

Made

4th March 2026

Coming into force

5th March 2026

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by regulations 2(2) and 3 of the Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with regulation 7(3) of those Regulations, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

There has been consultation as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Food Supplements (Magnesium L-threonate monohydrate) (Scotland) Regulations 2026 and come into force on 5 March 2026.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

## 2. Amendment of the Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019

(1) The Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In schedule 2 (vitamin and mineral substances which may be used in the manufacture of food supplements), in Part B of the table (minerals), after the entry for “magnesium citrate malate”[^f00003] insert “magnesium L-threonate monohydrate”.

## 3. Setting of purity criteria for magnesium L-threonate monohydrate

The purity criteria for magnesium L-threonate monohydrate are set in the schedule.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations are made under powers set out in regulations 2(2) and 3 of the Nutrition (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/651](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/651)) (“**the 2019 Regulations**”).

These Regulations permit the use of the mineral substance, magnesium L-threonate monohydrate, in the manufacture of food supplements. They do so by amending the list of mineral substances which is set out in schedule 2 of the 2019 Regulations, by the addition of an entry for this mineral substance (regulation 2).

These Regulations set the purity criteria for the mineral substance, as provided in the schedule of these Regulations (regulation 3).

No business and regulatory impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations as no impact upon business, charities or voluntary bodies is foreseen.

A Policy Note has been published alongside this instrument at Legislation.gov.uk.
