# The Inquiries into Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths etc. (Scotland) Act 2016 (Consequential Provisions) Regulations 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 156

Sheriff Court

The Inquiries into Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths etc. (Scotland) Act 2016 (Consequential Provisions) Regulations 2017

Made

16th May 2017

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

17th May 2017

Coming into force

15th June 2017

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 41(1) and (2) of the Inquiries into Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths etc. (Scotland) Act 2016[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Inquiries into Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths etc. (Scotland) Act 2016 (Consequential Provisions) Regulations 2017 and come into force on 15th June 2017.

## 2. The Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths Inquiry Procedure (Scotland) Rules 1977

The Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths Inquiry Procedure (Scotland) Rules 1977[^f00002] are revoked.

## 3. The Merchant Shipping (Returns of Births and Deaths) Regulations 1979

In regulation 7(1) of the Merchant Shipping (Returns of Births and Deaths) Regulations 1979 (records of deaths where master unable to act)[^f00003], for “Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths Inquiry (Scotland) Act 1976” substitute “Inquiries into Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths etc. (Scotland) Act 2016”.

## 4. The Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths Inquiry Procedure (Scotland) Amendment Rules 1992

The Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths Inquiry Procedure (Scotland) Amendment Rules 1992[^f00004] are revoked.

## 5. The Transfer of Functions (Lord Advocate and Secretary of State) Order 1999

In the schedule of the Transfer of Functions (Lord Advocate and Secretary of State) Order 1999 (functions transferred from the Lord Advocate to the Secretary of State)[^f00005], omit the entry relating to section 7 of the Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths Inquiry (Scotland) Act 1976[^f00006].

## 6. The National Health Service (Discipline Committees) (Scotland) Regulations 2006

In regulation 6(3) of the National Health Service (Discipline Committees) (Scotland) Regulations 2006 (time limits)[^f00007], for “Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths Inquiry (Scotland) Act 1976” substitute “Inquiries into Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths etc. (Scotland) Act 2016”.

## 7. The Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths Inquiry Procedure (Scotland) Amendment Rules 2007

The Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths Inquiry Procedure (Scotland) Amendment Rules 2007[^f00008] are revoked.

## 8. Saving provision

Regulations 2 to 7 do not apply to inquiries to which the Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths Inquiry (Scotland) Act 1976 continues to apply by virtue of regulation 4(1) of the Inquiries into Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths etc. (Scotland) Act 2016 (Commencement No. 3, Transitional and Saving Provisions) Regulations 2017[^f00009].

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make provision in consequence of the coming into force of the Inquiries into Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths etc. (Scotland) Act 2016 (the “2016 Act”) on 15th June 2017. The 2016 Act replaces the Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths Inquiry (Scotland) Act 1976 (the “1976 Act”).

Regulation 2 revokes the Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths Inquiry Procedure (Scotland) Rules 1977. Regulations 4 and 7 revoke amending instruments.

Regulation 3 amends the Merchant Shipping (Returns of Births and Deaths) Regulations 1979 to update a reference to the 1976 Act. This is related to the amendment to section 108(6) of the Merchant Shipping Act 1995 made in paragraph 5 of schedule 2 of the 2016 Act.

Regulation 5 amends the Transfer of Functions (Lord Advocate and Secretary of State) Order 1999 to remove a redundant reference to the 1976 Act.

For England and Wales, parallel amendments to those contained in regulations 3 and 5 are made in paragraphs 10 and 11 of the schedule of the Inquiries into Fatal Accidents and Sudden Deaths etc. (Scotland) Act 2016 (Consequential Provisions and Modifications) Order 2016 ([S.I. 2016/1142](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/1142)).

Regulation 6 amends the National Health Service (Discipline Committees) (Scotland) Regulations 2006 to update a reference to the 1976 Act.

Regulation 8 is a saving provision with effect that the amendments and revocations referred to do not apply in relation to an inquiry under the 1976 Act. This is to reflect that for a transitional period there will be inquiries taking place under both the 1976 and 2016 Acts.
