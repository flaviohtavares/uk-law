# The First-tier Tribunal for Scotland Housing and Property Chamber (Procedure) Regulations 2017, section 1

## 1. Citation and commencement[^key-6982a49faeca5bdcbf8bf5adcd75219b]

*Extent: S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the First-tier Tribunal for Scotland Housing and Property Chamber (Procedure) Regulations 2017 and the Rules set out in the schedule may be cited as the First-tier Tribunal for Scotland Housing and Property Chamber Rules of Procedure 2017.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1st December 2017.
