> Extent: Scotland.

## 2.5. Judicial continuity[^c-c80a9f507a07b4df01ca563fc198cdbe]

*Extent: S*

Where possible, the same sheriff is to—

- (a) consider the first notice and make the first order;
- (b) preside at all preliminary hearings; and
- (c) preside at the inquiry.
