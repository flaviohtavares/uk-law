# Act of Sederunt (Fatal Accident Inquiry Rules) 2017, section 7.1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 7.1. Form of notice[^c-9cc0d7c83f0e5c39e0d0d4223a4fe9bf]

*Extent: S*

(1) Notice under section 32 that further inquiry proceedings are to be held is to be given in Form 7.1.

(2) The notice must—

- (a) set out the identity of the deceased;
- (b) have appended to it a copy of the determination in the inquiry into the death of the deceased; and
- (c) contain the information required by section 32(2)(a) and (b).
