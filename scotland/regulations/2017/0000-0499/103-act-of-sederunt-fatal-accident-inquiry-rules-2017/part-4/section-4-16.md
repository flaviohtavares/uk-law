## 4.16. Information presented by expert witnesses[^key-00ab934e2182cb649f0413acdbab0c0b]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies where the sheriff orders that an expert witness is to present information at the inquiry by—

- (a) witness statement (see rule 4.13); or
- (b) video recording (see rule 4.14).

(2) The witness statement of an expert witness—

- (a) may consist of that witness’s report; or
- (b) must incorporate that witness’s report.
