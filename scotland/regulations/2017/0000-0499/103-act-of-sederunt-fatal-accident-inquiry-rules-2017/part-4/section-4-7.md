> Extent: Scotland.

## 4.7. Witness lists[^key-3c9683cd827893f712ec4721acd43126]

*Extent: S*

(1) A list of witnesses must be lodged by the date ordered by the sheriff.

(2) A participant may only present a witness who is not listed with the leave of the sheriff.
