# Act of Sederunt (Fatal Accident Inquiry Rules) 2017, section 3.5

> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.5. Other participation[^c-07807f32cf278c0e3db094b493a3800e]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person who is not given notice under section 17(1) but who wishes to participate in an inquiry may apply to the sheriff to participate.

(2) That application must—

- (a) set out why that person’s participation would further the purpose of the inquiry; and
- (b) be made at least 14 days before the start of the inquiry.

(3)[^c-7b601d675d595c9c23bc09f0b735e625] Schedule 1 does not apply to applications made under this rule.
