# Act of Sederunt (Fatal Accident Inquiry Rules) 2017, section 1.4

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 1.4. Forms[^key-9133e8f2a1d26ad2112830cac3c25bac]

*Extent: S*

(1) A reference in these Rules to a form is a reference to that form in schedule 3.

(2) Those forms may be varied where the circumstances require it.
