# Act of Sederunt (Fatal Accident Inquiry Rules) 2017, section 1.1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 1.1. Citation and commencement, etc.[^c-e2f12153303d4b82c481001531475cf3]

*Extent: S*

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fatal Accident Inquiry Rules) 2017.

(2) It comes into force on 15th June 2017.

(3) A certified copy is to be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.
