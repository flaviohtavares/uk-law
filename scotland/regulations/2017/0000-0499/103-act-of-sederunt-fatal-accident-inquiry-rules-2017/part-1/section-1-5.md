## 1.5. Miscellaneous and general matters[^key-c105c1cd5d1e63c3b543a42453146f5f]

*Extent: S*

Schedule 4 makes provision about—

- (a) lodging;
- (b) live links;
- (c) reporting restrictions;
- (d) the style of oath and affirmation;
- (e) interventions; and
- (f) lay representation and support.
