## 34. Powers of entry, etc.: further provision[^c-a7cebcfbb9b341a1bf19b5a9acf493e7]

*Extent: S*

(1) Schedule 8 makes further provision about powers of entry etc. conferred by—

- (a) regulation 29(1);
- (b) paragraph 3(2)(a) of schedule 6;
- (c) paragraph 3(2)(a) of schedule 7; and
- (d) regulation 32(3)(a).

(2) References in this Part to a power conferred by any of those provisions include references to such a power exercisable by virtue of a warrant under schedule 8.
