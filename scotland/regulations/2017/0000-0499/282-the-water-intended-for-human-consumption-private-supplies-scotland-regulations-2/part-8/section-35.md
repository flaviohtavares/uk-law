## 35. Recovery of expenses[^c-df911a599562f308550abba1be6ee5b9]

*Extent: S*

Schedule 9 makes provision for the recovery by an enforcing authority of certain expenses reasonably incurred by it in the exercise of its functions under these Regulations.
