> Extent: Scotland.

## 25. Information for certain public authorities[^c-d005a6ddebec486a4d8ceea98c0e7377]

*Extent: S*

By 31st March in each year, an enforcing authority must provide to the following persons adequate and up-to-date information on the quality of the water available to the consumers of the water in its area for the preceding year:—

- (a) the Drinking Water Quality Regulator for Scotland;
- (b) any Health Board;
- (c) the Scottish Environment Protection Agency; and
- (d) the Scottish Ministers.
