## 16. Duty of care: supplies of water[^c-51b5e8254691138eaad0d92ae3349c9f]

*Extent: S*

A person, in relation to a supply of water, must not take any action which has the effect of allowing any deterioration of the quality of the water (in so far as that is relevant for the protection of human health).
