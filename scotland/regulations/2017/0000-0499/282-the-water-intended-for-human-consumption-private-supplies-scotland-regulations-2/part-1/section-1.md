# The Water Intended for Human Consumption (Private Supplies) (Scotland) Regulations 2017, section 1

> Text as at 2017-10-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^key-a58f841c93b58a4c03f81b258d06d828]

*Extent: S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Water Intended for Human Consumption (Private Supplies) (Scotland) Regulations 2017 and come into force on 27th October 2017.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.
