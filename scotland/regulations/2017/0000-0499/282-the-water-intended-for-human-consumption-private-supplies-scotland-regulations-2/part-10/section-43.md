# The Water Intended for Human Consumption (Private Supplies) (Scotland) Regulations 2017, section 43

> Text as at 2017-10-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 43. Water (Scotland) Act 1980: meaning of “wholesome”[^c-10a7112680de90e89253a3539b5fd9be]

*Extent: S*

(1) For the purposes of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00020], water (to which these Regulations apply) which is supplied to any premises is—

- (a) to be regarded as wholesome if it satisfies the water quality standards; and
- (b) not to be regarded as wholesome if it fails to satisfy the water quality standards.

(2) In paragraph (1) “premises” has the meaning it has in section 109(1) (interpretation) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980.
