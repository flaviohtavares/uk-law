# The Water Intended for Human Consumption (Private Supplies) (Scotland) Regulations 2017, section 5

> Text as at 2017-10-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 5. Register of supply systems, etc.[^c-7c6bca3509d3434270b53f14f123416d]

*Extent: S*

An enforcing authority must prepare and maintain a register of information in relation to—

- (a) each private water supply system (including any modifications of, or extensions to, the supply system) used, or intended to be used, to supply water to premises in its area; and
- (b) the quality of water introduced into, and supplied through and from, the supply system.
