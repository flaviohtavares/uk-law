# The Water Intended for Human Consumption (Private Supplies) (Scotland) Regulations 2017, section 6

> Text as at 2017-10-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 6. Contents of the register[^c-f0798bf3fbbd3d1296b85aa079a474da]

*Extent: S*

(1) The enforcing authority must ensure that the register includes a record of the information specified in schedule 1, by the corresponding dates specified in that schedule.

(2) Information relating to the same supply system, including information about—

- (a) the relevant supply zone;
- (b) the quality of water introduced into, and supplied through and from, the system; and
- (c) premises served by the system,

must be kept together in the same part of the register.
