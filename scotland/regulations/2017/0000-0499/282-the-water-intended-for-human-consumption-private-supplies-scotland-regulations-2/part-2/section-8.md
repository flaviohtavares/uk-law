# The Water Intended for Human Consumption (Private Supplies) (Scotland) Regulations 2017, section 8

> Text as at 2017-10-27.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 8. Duty to provide information[^key-cb372392bc403ea88c5ca6e217bbaee5]

*Extent: S*

Where water is being, or is intended to be, supplied to premises through any part of a supply system which is not recorded in the register (“the unrecorded part”), each person who owns the premises must ensure that the enforcing authority (“the registering authority”) for the area in which the premises is situated is, or has been, provided with all the information relating to the unrecorded part which the registering authority needs in order to comply with its duties under this Part.
