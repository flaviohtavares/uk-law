# The Limitation (Childhood Abuse) (Scotland) Act 2017 (Commencement) Regulations 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 279 (C. 20)

Limitation Of Actions

The Limitation (Childhood Abuse) (Scotland) Act 2017 (Commencement) Regulations 2017

Made

4th September 2017

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

6th September 2017

Coming into force

4th October 2017

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the Limitation (Childhood Abuse) (Scotland) Act 2017[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Limitation (Childhood Abuse) (Scotland) Act 2017 (Commencement) Regulations 2017 and come into force on 4th October 2017.

## 2. Appointed day

4th October 2017 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 1 of the Limitation (Childhood Abuse) (Scotland) Act 2017 (removal of 3 year limitation period in certain actions).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Limitation (Childhood Abuse) (Scotland) Act 2017 received Royal Assent on 28th July 2017. Sections 2 and 3 came into force the following day.

Regulation 2 of these Regulations brings into force the remaining provision of that Act (section 1) on 4th October 2017. Section 1 makes amendments to the Prescription and Limitation (Scotland) Act [1973 (c.52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/52) to remove the limitation period for actions of damages in respect of personal injuries resulting from childhood abuse.
