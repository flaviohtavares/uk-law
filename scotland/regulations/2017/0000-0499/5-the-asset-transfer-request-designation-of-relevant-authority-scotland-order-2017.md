# The Asset Transfer Request (Designation of Relevant Authority) (Scotland) Order 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 5

Community Empowerment

The Asset Transfer Request (Designation of Relevant Authority) (Scotland) Order 2017

Made

11th January 2017

Coming into force

23rd January 2017

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 78(3) of the Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 142(2) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before, and approved by resolution of, the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Asset Transfer Request (Designation of Relevant Authority) (Scotland) Order 2017 and comes into force on 23rd January 2017.

## 2. Designation of relevant authority

Historic Environment Scotland[^f00002] is designated as a relevant authority for the purposes of Part 5 (asset transfer requests) of the Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates Historic Environment Scotland as a relevant authority for the purposes of Part 5 of the Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015.
