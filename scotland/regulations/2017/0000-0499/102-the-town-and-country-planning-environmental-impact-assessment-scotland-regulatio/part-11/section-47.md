# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Scotland) Regulations 2017, section 47

> Text as at 2024-12-05.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 47. Application to Court of Session

*Extent: S*

For the purposes of Part XI of the Act (validity), the references in section 239(1)(b) and (2) [^c23241481] to action of the Scottish Ministers or a planning authority which is not within the powers of the Act are to be taken to extend, as the case may be, to—

- (a) a grant of planning permission by the Scottish Ministers or the planning authority in contravention of regulation 3; and
- (b) a grant of multi-stage consent by the Scottish Ministers or the planning authority in contravention of regulation 32.
