# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Scotland) Regulations 2017, section 51

> Text as at 2024-12-05.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 51. Access to review procedure before a court

*Extent: S*

Any non‑governmental organisation promoting environmental protection and meeting any requirements under the law is deemed to have an interest for the purposes of Article 11(1)(a) of the Directive and rights capable of being impaired for the purposes of Article 11(1)(b) of the Directive. (and for such purpose Article 11(1)(b) is to be read as if the reference to[^c-c3c7e6fb3e428ecd11d7236169e7a842] “ a Member State ” were a reference to “Scotland”).
