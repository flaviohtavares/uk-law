# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Scotland) Regulations 2017, section 27

> Text as at 2024-12-05.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 27. Publication of additional information

*Extent: S*

(1) Where additional information is provided to the planning authority or the Scottish Ministers, regulations 20 to 22, 24 and 25 apply to the provision of such additional information as they apply to the submission of an EIA report as if references to the EIA report were references to that additional information.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply in relation to additional information to the extent that—

- (a) the information is provided for the purposes of an inquiry held under the Act;
- (b) the written requirement for the information states that it is to be provided for such purposes; and
- (c) the information is required to be publicised as part of that inquiry.
