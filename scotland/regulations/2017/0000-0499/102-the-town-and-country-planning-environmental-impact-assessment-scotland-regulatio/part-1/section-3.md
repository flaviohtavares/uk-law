# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Scotland) Regulations 2017, section 3

> Extent: Scotland.

## 3. Prohibition on granting planning permission without an environmental impact assessment

*Extent: S*

The planning authority or the Scottish Ministers, as the case may be, must not grant planning permission for EIA development unless an environmental impact assessment has been carried out in respect of that development and in carrying out such assessment the planning authority or the Scottish Ministers, as the case may be, must take the environmental information into account.
