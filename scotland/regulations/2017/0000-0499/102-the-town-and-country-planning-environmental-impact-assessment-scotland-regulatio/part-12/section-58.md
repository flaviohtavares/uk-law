# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Scotland) Regulations 2017, section 58

> Text as at 2024-12-05.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 58. Amendment of the Town and Country Planning (Appeals) (Scotland) Regulations 2013

*Extent: S*

(1) The Town and Country Planning (Appeals) (Scotland) Regulations 2013 [^c23241521] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In regulation 2 (interpretation)—

- (a) in the definition of “application for multi-stage consent” for “2011” substitute “ 2017 ”;
- (b) for the definition of “EIA development” and “environmental statement” substitute—

  > “**EIA development**” and “**EIA report**” have the same meaning as in the Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Scotland) Regulations 2017;
  >
  > “**environmental statement**” has the same meaning as in the Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Scotland) Regulations 2011 [^c23241531] as those Regulations had effect on 15th May 2017;

  ; and
- (c) in the definition of “validation date”, in paragraph (a)(ii) for “environmental statement” substitute “ EIA report or environmental statement, as the case may be, ”.
