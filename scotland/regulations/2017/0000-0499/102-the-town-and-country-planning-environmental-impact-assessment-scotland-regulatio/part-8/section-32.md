> Extent: Scotland.

## 32. Prohibition on granting an application for multi-stage consent without an environmental impact assessment

*Extent: S*

The planning authority or the Scottish Ministers, as the case may be, must not grant an application for multi-stage consent in respect of EIA development unless an environmental impact assessment has been carried out in respect of that development and in carrying out such assessment the planning authority or the Scottish Ministers, as the case may be, must take the environmental information into account.
