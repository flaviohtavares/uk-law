The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 36(2) and (3) of the Health (Tobacco, Nicotine etc. and Care) (Scotland) Act 2016[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Health (Tobacco, Nicotine etc. and Care) (Scotland) Act 2016 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2017 and come into force on 1st October 2017.

## 2. Appointed day

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Health (Tobacco, Nicotine etc. and Care) (Scotland) Act 2016 specified in column 1 of the schedule (the subject matter of which is described in column 2 of the schedule) is 1st October 2017.

(2) Where a purpose is specified in column 3 of that schedule, a provision mentioned in column 1 comes into force in accordance with paragraph (1) only for that purpose.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force provisions in Part 2 (duty of candour) and Part 3 (ill-treatment and wilful neglect) of the Health (Tobacco, Nicotine etc. and Care) (Scotland) Act 2016 (“the Act”) on 1st October 2017.

The Part 2 provisions are brought into force for the purpose of making regulations setting out the duty of candour procedure.

The whole of Part 3 is commenced.

Sections 34, 35, 36 and 37 of the Act came into force on 7th April 2016, which was the day after Royal Assent.
