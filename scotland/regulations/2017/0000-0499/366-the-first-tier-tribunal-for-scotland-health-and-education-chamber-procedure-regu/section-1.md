# The First-tier Tribunal for Scotland Health and Education Chamber (Procedure) Regulations 2017, section 1

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-793c6509574022a64d93bada68de9215]

*Extent: S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the First-tier Tribunal for Scotland Health and Education Chamber (Procedure) Regulations 2017, and the Rules set out in the schedule may be cited as The First-tier Tribunal for Scotland Health and Education Chamber Rules of Procedure 2018.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 12th January 2018.
