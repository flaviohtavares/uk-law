# The Road Works (Reinstatement) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Scottish Statutory Instrument has been printed to correct errors in [S.S.I. 2003/417](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/417) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that instrument.

ROADS AND BRIDGES

The Road Works (Reinstatement) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2003

Made

27th October 2003

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

28th October 2003

Coming into force

18th November 2003

The Scottish Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 130 and 163(1) and (3) of the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Works (Reinstatement) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 18th November 2003.

## 2. Amendment of Regulations

In regulation 2 of the Road Works (Reinstatement) (Scotland) Regulations 1992[^f00002] omit “per annum” at the end of the definition of “a road carrying more than 125 million standard axles”.

NICOL STEPHEN

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

27th October 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Road Works (Reinstatement) (Scotland) Regulations 1992 (the “1992 Regulations”). The 1992 Regulations prescribed requirements for the specification of materials to be used and the standard of workmanship to be observed by undertakers in reinstating streets by reference to a Code of Practice issued in 1992. The Scottish Ministers have approved the issue of a new Code of Practice entitled “Specification for the Reinstatement of Openings in Roads” (the “new Code”). These Regulations make a minor amendment to the 1992 Regulations by removing a reference to “per annum” which appears in the definition of “immediate works”.

The new Code is published by Astron on behalf of the Scottish Executive. Copies are available from the Stationery Office Bookshop, 71 Lothian Road, Edinburgh, EH3 9AZ (tel 0870 606 5566).
