# The Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 519

CRIMINAL LAW

The Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2003

Made

30th October 2003

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

31st October 2003

Coming into force

24th November 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 14(2) of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2003 and shall come into force on 24th November 2003.

## 2. Amendment of the Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Order 2003

After paragraph 4 in the Schedule to the Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Order 2003[^f00002] insert “4A. Robbery.”.

HUGH HENRY

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

30th October 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds the crime of robbery to the list of prescribed offences for which a victim will be afforded an opportunity to make a victim statement for the purposes of section 14(2) of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2003.

The effect of the Order is that, in addition to the offences already prescribed in the Victim Statement (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Order 2003 ([S.S.I. 2003/44](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/44)) (“the principal order”), a victim statement may be made on or after commencement of the principal order on 25th November 2003 where proceedings are to be taken or are likely to be taken in respect of robbery.
