# The Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Transfer of Scottish Homes Property and Liabilities) Order 2003, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order–

- “the 1988 Act” means the Housing (Scotland) Act 1988[^f00002]; and
- “Scottish Homes” means the body corporate established by section 1 of the 1988 Act[^f00003].
