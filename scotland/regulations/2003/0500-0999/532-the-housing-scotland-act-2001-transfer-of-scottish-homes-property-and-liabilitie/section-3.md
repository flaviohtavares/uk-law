# The Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Transfer of Scottish Homes Property and Liabilities) Order 2003, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Transfer of property and liabilities

All property and liabilities of Scottish Homes in relation to–

- (a) the heritable property and other rights and interests specified in Part I of Schedule 1 to this Order under exception of the property specified in Part 2 of that Schedule; and
- (b) the Minutes of Agreement specified in Schedule 2 to this Order,

are hereby transferred to and vest in the Scottish Ministers.
