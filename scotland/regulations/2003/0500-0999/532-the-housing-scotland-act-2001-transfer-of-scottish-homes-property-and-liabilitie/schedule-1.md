# The Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Transfer of Scottish Homes Property and Liabilities) Order 2003, SCHEDULE 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 1

Article 3

### PART I — HERITABLE PROPERTY, RIGHTS AND INTERESTS TRANSFERRING TO SCOTTISH MINISTERS

**1.** All heritable property to which Scottish Homes is entitled except the property described in Part 2.

**2.** All rights and interests of Scottish Homes in relation to any grant, loan or security provided under section 2(2) of the 1988 Act.

### PART II — EXCEPTED PROPERTY

#### 1. Certain Securities

Any property rights and interests in relation to a loan or security by an individual who was formally a secure tenant, created in consequence of the exercise of a function conferred on Scottish Homes under section 1(3)b of the 1988 Act.
