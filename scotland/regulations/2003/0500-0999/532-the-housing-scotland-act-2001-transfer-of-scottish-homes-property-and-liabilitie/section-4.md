# The Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Transfer of Scottish Homes Property and Liabilities) Order 2003, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Consequential and supplementary provision

(1) Nothing in this Order shall have the effect of completing the title of the Scottish Ministers to any land transferred by this Order, or to any other right or interest transferred by this Order, title to which requires to be completed by registration.

(2) Any property, in respect of which but for this Order–

- (a) delivery or possession; or
- (b) intimation of its assignation,

would be required in order to complete title to it, shall transfer to the Scottish Ministers by virtue of this Order as if at the date on which the transfer of the property by this Order takes effect the Scottish Ministers had taken delivery or possession of the property or, as the case may be, had made intimation of its assignation to them.
