# The Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Amendment Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Scottish Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.S.I. 2003/453](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/453) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that instrument.

2003 No. 621

TITLE CONDITIONS

The Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Amendment Order 2003

Made

29th December 2003

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

5th January 2004

Coming into force

30th January 2004

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 38(4) of the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Amendment Order 2003 and shall come into force on 30th January 2004.

## 2. Amendment

In Part II of the Schedule to the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Order 2003[^f00002], for “The National Trust for Places of Historic Interest and Natural Beauty” substitute “The National Trust for Scotland for Places of Historic Interest or Natural Beauty”.

HUGH HENRY

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

29th December 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order corrects the name of the National Trust for Scotland, which was incorrectly stated in the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Order 2003.
