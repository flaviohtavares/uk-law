# The Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 516 (C. 28)

ADULTS WITH INCAPACITY

The Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2003

Made

30th October 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 89(2) of the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2003.

## 2. Commencement

Section 85 of and schedule 3 to the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (so far as not already in force) shall come into force on 4th November 2003.

CATHY JAMIESON

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

30th October 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 4th November 2003 as the date for the coming into force (so far as not already in force) of section 85 of and schedule 3 to the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000.

The provisions of the Act remaining to be commenced after this Order are certain provisions from schedules 4 to 6.

(This note is not part of the Order)

The following provisions of the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 have been brought into force by the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2001 ([S.S.I. 2001/81](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2001/81)), as amended by the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 1) (Amendment) Order 2002 ([S.S.I. 2002/172](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/172)), the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Order 2000 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2002 ([S.S.I. 2002/189](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/189)), the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2003 ([S.S.I. 2003/136](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/136)), the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 3) Partial Revocation Order 2003 ([S.S.I. 2003/227](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/227)) and the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2003 ([S.S.I. 2003/267](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/267)).
