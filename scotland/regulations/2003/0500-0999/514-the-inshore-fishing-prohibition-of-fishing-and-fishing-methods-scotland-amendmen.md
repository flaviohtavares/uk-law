# The Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing and Fishing Methods) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Scottish Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.S.I. 2003/404](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/404) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that instrument.

2003 No. 514

SEA FISHERIES

The Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing and Fishing Methods) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2003

Made

29th October 2003

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

30th October 2003

Coming into force

31st October 2003

The Scottish Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Inshore Fishing (Scotland) Act 1984[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and having consulted such bodies as they consider appropriate, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing and Fishing Methods) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 31st October 2003.

## 2. Amendment of the Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing and Fishing Methods) (Scotland) Order 1989

For item 4 of Schedule 2 to the Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing and Fishing Methods) (Scotland) Order 1989[^f00002] substitute item 4 as set out in the Schedule to this Order.

## 3. Revocation

Article 2(5) of and the Schedule to the Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing and Fishing Methods) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2003[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

ROSS FINNIE

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

29th October 2003

This Order amends the Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing and Fishing Methods) (Scotland) Order 1989 by substituting item 4 of Schedule 2, which was itself inserted by article 2(5) of the Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing and Fishing Methods) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2003 ([S.S.I. 2003/404](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/404)) (article 2). The amendment substituting item 4 is necessary to correct an error in one of the co ordinates in [S.S.I. 2003/404](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/404).
