# The River Ewe Salmon Fishery District (Baits and Lures) Revocation Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 614

RIVER

SALMON AND FRESHWATER FISHERIES

The River Ewe Salmon Fishery District (Baits and Lures) Revocation Regulations 2003

Made

23rd December 2003

Coming into force

24th December 2003

The Scottish Ministers, on application to them by the River Ewe District Salmon Fishery Board, having consulted and directed that notice be given in accordance with paragraphs 3 and 4 of Schedule 1 to the Salmon Act 1986[^f00001], as applied by section 8(5) of that Act and there being no objections or representations made to the application, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 8 of that Act and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the River Ewe Salmon Fishery District (Baits and Lures) Revocation Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 24th December 2003.

## 2. Revocation

The River Ewe Salmon Fishery District (Baits and Lures) Regulations 1998 are hereby revoked[^f00002].

GORDON BROWN

A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers

Pentland House, Edinburgh

23rd December 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the River Ewe Salmon Fishery District (Baits and Lures) Regulations 1998.
