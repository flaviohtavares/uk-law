# The Argyll and Clyde National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 325

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE

The Argyll and Clyde National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2003

Made

24th June 2003

Coming into force

1st July 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 12A(1) and 105(6) of and paragraph 25(1) of Schedule 7A to the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, having received an application[^f00002] from each of the National Health Service trusts specified in Schedule 1 to this Order, and after completion of the consultation prescribed[^f00003] under paragraph 25(3) of the said Schedule 7A, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Argyll and Clyde National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 1st July 2003.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS Trusts

The National Health Service trusts specified in Schedule 1 to this Order are hereby dissolved.

## 3. Revocations

The Orders specified in Schedule 2 to this Order are hereby revoked.

MALCOLM CHISHOLM

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

24th June 2003

## SCHEDULE 1 — DISSOLUTION OF NHS TRUSTS

Article 2

- Argyll and Clyde Acute Hospitals National Health Service Trust
- Lomond and Argyll Primary Care National Health Service Trust
- Renfrewshire and Inverclyde Primary Care National Health Service Trust.

This Order provides by article 2 for the dissolution on 1st July 2003 of the three National Health Service trusts detailed in Schedule 1. As a consequence of those dissolutions this Order also revokes the Orders establishing those trusts and Orders amending those establishment Orders, which are all detailed in Schedule 2.
