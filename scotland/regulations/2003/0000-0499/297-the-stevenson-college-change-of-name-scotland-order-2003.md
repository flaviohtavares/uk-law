# The Stevenson College (Change of Name) (Scotland) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 297

EDUCATION

The Stevenson College (Change of Name) (Scotland) Order 2003

Made

30th May 2003

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

6th June 2003

Coming into force

1st August 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(4) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, having undertaken such consultation as appeared to them to be appropriate, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Stevenson College (Change of Name) (Scotland) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 1st August 2003.

## 2. Change of name of Stevenson College

The name of Stevenson College is changed to Stevenson College Edinburgh, and the name of the board of management of that college is changed to the Board of Management of Stevenson College Edinburgh.

JAMES R WALLACE

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

30th May 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order changes the name of Stevenson College to Stevenson College Edinburgh. That college is under the control of a board of management in terms of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992 and the Order therefore also changes the name of the board of management to reflect the new name of the College.
