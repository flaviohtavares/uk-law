# The Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 316 (C. 16)

MENTAL HEALTH

The Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2003

Made

17th June 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 333(3) and (4) of the Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2003.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of paragraph 10 of schedule 1 and, for that purpose, section 4(3) of the Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003 is 1st July 2003.

TOM McCABE

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

17th June 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force paragraph 10 in Part 2 of schedule 1 to the Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003 (“the Act”). The Act received Royal Assent on 25th April 2003.

Part 2 of schedule 1 to the Act contains transitional provisions which amend sections 2 and 6 of the Mental Health (Scotland) Act [1984 (c. 36)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/36). These amendments relate to the Mental Welfare Commission with new provision for the appointment of a chief officer to the Commission.
