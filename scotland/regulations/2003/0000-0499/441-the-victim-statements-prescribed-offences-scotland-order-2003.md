# The Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 441

CRIMINAL LAW

The Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Order 2003

Made

17th September 2003

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

18th September 2003

Coming into force

25th November 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 14(2) of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Victim Statements (Prescribed Offences) (Scotland) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 25th November 2003.

## 2. Prescribed offences

An offence specified in the Schedule to this Order is prescribed for the purposes of section 14(2) of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00002].

HUGH HENRY

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

17th September 2003

## SCHEDULE — Offence prescribed for the purposes of section 14(2) of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2003

Article 2

### 1. Non-sexual crimes of violence

Murder.

**2.** Culpable homicide.

**3.** Abduction.

**4.** Assault.

**5.** Cruel and unnatural treatment.

**6.** An offence under section 12 of the Children and Young Person’s (Scotland) Act [1937 (c. 37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1937/37) (cruelty to persons under 16).

**7.** An offence under section 41(1)(a) of the Police (Scotland) Act [1967 (c. 77)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1967/77) (assault on constables, etc) but only in respect of an assault on a constable.

### 8. Sexual Crimes of violence and indecent crimes

Rape.

**9.** Sodomy.

**10.** Abduction of a woman or girl with intent to rape.

**11.** Assault with intent to rape or ravish.

**12.** Indecent assault.

**13.** Lewd, indecent or libidinous behaviour or practices.

**14.** An offence under section 106(1)(a) or 107 of the Mental Health (Scotland) Act [1984 (c. 36)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/36) (unlawful sexual intercourse with mentally handicapped female or with patient).

**15.** An offence under any of the following provisions of the Criminal Law (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act [1995 (c. 39)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/39):–

- (a) sections 1 to 3 (incest and related offences);
- (b) section 5 (unlawful sexual intercourse with girl under 16);
- (c) section 6 (indecent behaviour towards girl between 12 and 16);
- (d) section 7(2) and (3) (procuring);
- (e) section 8 (abduction and unlawful detention);
- (f) section 10 (seduction, prostitution, etc. of girl under 16);
- (g) section 13(5)(b) or (c) (homosexual offences).

**16.** An offence under section 3 of the Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act [2000 (c. 44)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/44) (abuse of position of trust).

**17.** Any offence where there was a significant sexual aspect to the offender’s behaviour in committing the offence.

### 18. Housebreaking etc

Theft by housebreaking.

### 19. Racially motivated crimes

An offence under section 50A of the Criminal Law (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 1995 (racially-aggravated harassment).

**20.** An offence under section 96 of the Crime and Disorder Act [1998 (c. 37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/37) (offences racially aggravated).

### 21. Road Traffic

An offence under section 1 of the Road Traffic Act [1998 (c. 52)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/52) (causing death by dangerous driving).

**22.** An offence under section 3A of that Act (causing death by careless driving when under influence of drink or drugs).

### 23. Other

Fireraising.

### 24. Inchoate offences

An offence of conspiring or inciting the commission of an offence specified in this Schedule.

**25.** An offence of aiding, abetting, counselling or procuring the commission of such an offence.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This order prescribes the offences for which a victim will be afforded an opportunity to make a victim statement for the purposes of section 14(2) of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2003.

The effect of the Order is that a victim statement may be made where proceedings are to be taken or are likely to be taken in respect of one or more of the prescribed offences.
