# The Protection of Animals (Anaesthetics) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 476

ANIMALS

The Protection of Animals (Anaesthetics) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2003

Made

1st October 2003

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

2nd October 2003

Coming into force

3rd October 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Protection of Animals (Anaesthetics) Act 1954[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, after consulting the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons and such other persons representing agricultural interests as appeared to them to be appropriate, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Protection of Animals (Anaesthetics) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2003 and shall come into force on 3rd October 2003.

(2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

## 2. Amendment of the Protection of Animals (Anaesthetics) Act 1954

In paragraph 6 of the First Schedule (excepted operations) to the Protection of Animals (Anaesthetics) Act 1954[^f00002] for “pig 4 weeks” substitute “pig 8 days”.

ROSS FINNIE

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

1st October 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends paragraph 6 of the First Schedule to the Protection of Animals (Anaesthetics) Act 1954. It reduces the age beyond which castration of pigs may not be performed without anaesthetic. Under this Order, castration may only be performed before the animal has reached the age of 8 days, as opposed to 4 weeks. This amendment is necessitated by the changes made by the Welfare of Farmed Animals (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2003, implementing Council Directive [2001/88/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0088) (O.J. No. L 316, 1.12.2001, p.1) and Commission Directive [2001/93/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2001/0093) (O.J. No. L 316 1.12.2001 p.36), which amend the Welfare of Farmed Animals (Scotland) Regulations 2000 ([S.S.I. 2000/442](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2000/442) as amended by [S.S.I. 2002/334](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/334)), setting out requirements applying to the keeping of pigs. The amending Regulations include a similar provision relating to the castration of pigs.
