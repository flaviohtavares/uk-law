# The Education (Disability Strategies) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Scottish Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.S.I. 2002/391](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/391) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that instrument.

2003 No. 10

EDUCATION

The Education (Disability Strategies) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2003

Made

9th January 2003

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

10th January 2003

Coming into force

1st February 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3(6) and 5 of the Education (Disability Strategies and Pupils' Educational Records) (Scotland) Act 2002[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, after consulting such representatives of responsible bodies and with such other persons (or their representatives) likely to be affected by the Regulations as appear to them to be appropriate, in accordance with section 5(3) of the said Act, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Disability Strategies) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 1st February 2003.

## 2. Amendment of the Education (Disability Strategies) (Scotland) Regulations 2002

Regulation 3(h) of the Education (Disability Strategies) (Scotland) Regulations 2002[^f00002] is omitted.

CATHY JAMIESON

A member of the Scottish Executive

Victoria Quay, Edinburgh

9th January 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend provisions in the Education (Disability Strategies) (Scotland) Regulations 2002 which set out the alternative forms in which responsible bodies should make their accessibility strategies available on request.
