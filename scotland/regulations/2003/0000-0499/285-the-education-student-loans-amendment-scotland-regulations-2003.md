# The Education (Student Loans) Amendment (Scotland) Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

4th June 2003

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

6th June 2003

Coming into force

1st August 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(2)(b) and (7) of and paragraph 1(1) of Schedule 2 to the Education (Student Loans) Act 1990[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Education (Student Loans) Amendment (Scotland) Regulations 2003 and shall come into force on 1st August 2003.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland and, in so far as they extend beyond Scotland, they do so only as a matter of Scots Law[^f00002].

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which come into force on 1st August 2003, amend the Education (Student Loans) Regulations 1998 governing loans made under the Education (Student Loans) Act 1990. These are mortgage style repayment loans made, for the most part, to students who began their courses before 1st August 1998.

These Regulations increase the maximum amounts which may be lent to students in relation to an academic year in line with inflation (regulation 3).
