# The Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000 (Register of Interests) Amendment Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Scottish Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.S.I. 2003/135](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/135) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that instrument.

2003 No. 203

LOCAL GOVERNMENT

ETHICAL STANDARDS

The Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000 (Register of Interests) Amendment Regulations 2003

Made

19th March 2003

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

21st March 2003

Coming into force

1st May 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7(2) of the Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000[^f00001], and all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000 (Register of Interests) Amendment Regulations 2003, and shall come into force on 1st May 2003.

## 2. Amendments to the Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000 (Register of Interests) Regulations 2003

(1) The Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000 (Register of Interests) Regulations 2003[^f00002] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) In Regulation 2 (interpretation), before the definition of “declaration of acceptance” insert–

> - “the 2000 Act” means the Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000;

.

(3) In Regulation 4(2)(c) (first notice) for “Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000” substitute “2000 Act”.

PETER J PEACOCK

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

19th March 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000 (Register of Interests) Regulations 2003 (“the principal regulations”).

Regulation 2 inserts a definition of the term “the 2000 Act” that had been omitted from Regulation 2 of the principal regulations, and makes the necessary consequential amendment to Regulation 4 of the principal regulations.
