# The Collagen and Gelatine (Intra Community Trade) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 466

FOOD

The Collagen and Gelatine (Intra Community Trade) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2003

Made

30th September 2003

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

1st October 2003

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(1)

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Collagen and Gelatine (Intra Community Trade) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2003 and shall come into force at 11.00 hours on 30th September 2003.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

## 2. Revocations

The Collagen and Gelatine (Intra Community Trade) (Scotland) Regulations 2003[^f00002], the Collagen and Gelatine (Intra Community Trade) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2003[^f00003] and the Collagen and Gelatine (Intra Community Trade) (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2003[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

## 3. Amendments to the Products of Animal Origin (Import and Export) Regulations 1996

In paragraphs 9 and 12 of Schedule 3 to the Products of Animal Origin (Import and Export) Regulations 1996[^f00005] omit “Commission Decision [2003/42/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0042) (O.J. No. L 13, 18.1.03, p.24) as amended by Commission Decision [2003/503/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0503) (O.J. No. L 170, 9.7.03, p.30)”.

TOM McCABE

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

30th September 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which extend to Scotland only, revoke the Collagen and Gelatine (Intra-Community Trade) (Scotland) regulations 2003 (“the principal Regulations”) as amended.

The principal Regulations gave effect to Commission Decision [2003/42/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0042) (O.J. No. L 13, 18.1.03, p.24) as amended. That Decision is repealed by a new Commission Decision made on 29th September 2003.

The principal Regulations amend the Products of Animal Origin (Import and Export) Regulations by giving effect to Commission Decision [2003/42/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0042) as amended. These Regulations amend the 1996 Regulations by deleting reference to Commission Decision [2003/42/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0042) as amended.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations.
