# The Police Grant (Scotland) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 172

POLICE

The Police Grant (Scotland) Order 2003

Made

11th March 2003

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

11th March 2003

Coming into force

1st April 2003

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 32(3) and (5) of the Police (Scotland) Act 1967[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Police Grant (Scotland) Order 2003 and shall come into force on 1st April 2003.

(2) In this Order–

- “authority” means a police authority (not being a constituent authority) or a joint police board;
- “financial year 2003 2004” means the period of 12 months commencing on 1st April 2003; and
- “police grant” means the grant made under section 32(1) of the Police (Scotland) Act 1967.

## 2. Aggregate amount of grants for financial year 2003-2004

The aggregate amount of police grant to be made for the financial year 2003 2004 shall be £459,944,000.

The amount of police grant to be made for the financial year 2003 2004 to each authority specified in column 1 of the Table below shall be the amount determined in relation to that authority which is set out in column 2 of that Table opposite to the name of that authority.

## 4. Payment of grant

The police grant payable to an authority by virtue of this Order for the financial year 2003 2004 shall be paid to that authority in monthly instalments on the 15th day of each month during that year.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made under section 32(3) and (5) of the Police (Scotland) Act 1967, as substituted by section 45(1) of the Crime and Punishment (Scotland) Act 1997, which makes provision for the Scottish Ministers to make grants out of money from the Scottish Consolidated Fund for police purposes to police authorities and joint police boards.

This Order determines–

- (a) the aggregate amount of grants to be made under section 32(1) to all police authorities and joint police boards for the financial year 2003 2004 (article 2); and
- (b) the amount of such grants to be made to each police authority or joint police board (article 3).

The Order also sets out how and when the grant is payable (article 4).
