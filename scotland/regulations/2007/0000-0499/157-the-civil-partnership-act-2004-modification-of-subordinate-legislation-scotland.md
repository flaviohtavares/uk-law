# The Civil Partnership Act 2004 (Modification of Subordinate Legislation) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 157

CIVIL PARTNERSHIP

The Civil Partnership Act 2004 (Modification of Subordinate Legislation) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2007

Made

6th March 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

6th March 2007

Coming into force

1st August 2007

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 259(1), (2) and (4) of the Civil Partnership Act 2004[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

This Order may be cited as the Civil Partnership Act 2004 (Modification of Subordinate Legislation) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2007 and shall come into force on 1st August 2007.

**2.** This Order shall extend to Scotland only.

## 3. Revocation

The Order specified in column 1 of the Schedule is revoked to the extent specified in column 3 thereof.

NICOL STEPHEN

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

6th March 2007

This Order revokes certain articles of the Civil Partnership Act 2004 (Modification of Subordinate Legislation) Order 2005 as a result of the consolidation of the Nursing and Midwifery Student Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 1992 (S.I. [1992/580](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/580)), the Education Authority Bursaries (Scotland) Regulations 1995 (S.I. [1997/93](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/93)), the Students' Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 1999 (S.I. [1999/1131](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1131)), the Education (Student Loans) (Scotland) Regulations 2000 ([S.S.I. 2000/200](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2000/200)), the Graduate Endowment (Scotland) Regulations 2001 ([S.S.I. 2001/280](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2001/280)) and the Education Maintenance Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 2004 ([S.S.I. 2004/273](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2004/273)).
