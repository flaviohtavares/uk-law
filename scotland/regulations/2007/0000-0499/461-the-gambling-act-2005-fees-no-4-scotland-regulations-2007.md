# The Gambling Act 2005 (Fees No. 4) (Scotland) Regulations 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 461

BETTING, GAMING AND LOTTERIES

The Gambling Act 2005 (Fees No. 4) (Scotland) Regulations 2007

Made

15th October 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

17th October 2007

Coming into force

19th November 2007

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 219(3)(b) of the Gambling Act 2005[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Gambling Act 2005 (Fees No. 4) (Scotland) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 19th November 2007.

## 2. Fee in connection with temporary use notices

The fee payable under section 219(3)(b) of the Gambling Act 2005 on giving a temporary use notice in relation to premises in Scotland is £350.

KENNY MACASKILL

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

15th October 2007

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

Part 9 of the Gambling Act 2005 (“the Act”) enables a person holding an operating licence to give a temporary use notice in respect of premises. The effect of the notice is to authorise the premises to be used during the period specified in the notice for carrying on gambling activities without an offence being committed under section 37 of the Act (which makes it unlawful to use premises for providing facilities for gambling without the appropriate premises licence). These Regulations provide that a fee of £350 will be payable on giving a temporary use notice in relation to premises in Scotland.
