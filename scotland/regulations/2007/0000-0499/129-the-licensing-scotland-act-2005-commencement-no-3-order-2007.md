# The Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 129 (C.15)

LICENSING (LIQUOR)

The Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2007

Made

27th February 2007

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 146(2) and 150(2) of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 3) Order 2007.

## 2. Appointed days

19th March 2007 is appointed as the day for the coming into force of section 142 of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005.

**3.** 1st May 2007 is appointed as the day for the coming into force of the provisions of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 specified in the Schedule to this Order.

GEORGE LYON

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

27th February 2007

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 142 (guidance) of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 into force on 19th March 2007 and also commences on 1st May 2007 the various provisions of that Act specified in the Schedule to the Order.

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional Provisions) Order 2006 ([S.S.I. 2006/286](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2006/286)) brought into force on 1st June 2006–

- (a) subsections (1) to (3) and (6) of section 105 of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005; and
- (b) section 149 of, and Schedule 7 to, that Act so far as relating to a minor repeal in the Licensing (Scotland) Act 1976.
