# The Occasional Licence (Scotland) Regulations 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 96

LICENSING (LIQUOR)

The Occasional Licence (Scotland) Regulations 2007

Made

20th February 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

21st February 2007

Coming into force

1st February 2008

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 56(7) and (8)(j), 134, 146(2) and 147(1) of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Occasional Licence (Scotland) Regulations 2007 and shall come into force on 1st February 2008.

## 2. Form of occasional licence

An occasional licence is to be in the form set out in Schedule 1.

## 3. Application for an occasional licence

An occasional licence application is to be in the form set out in Schedule 2.

GEORGE LYON

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers
