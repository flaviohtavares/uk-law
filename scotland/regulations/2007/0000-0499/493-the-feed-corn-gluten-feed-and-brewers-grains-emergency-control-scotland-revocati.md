# The Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 493

AGRICULTURE

The Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2007

Made

1st November 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

2nd November 2007

Coming into force

30th November 2007

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

There has been consultation as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2007 and shall come into force on 30th November 2007.

## 2. Revocation

The Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) (Scotland) Regulations 2005[^f00003] are revoked.

## 3. Consequential amendments to the Genetically Modified Animal Feed (Scotland) Regulations 2004

In regulation 8 (inspection, detention and seizure of suspect animal feed) of the Genetically Modified Animal Feed (Scotland) Regulations 2004[^f00004] paragraphs (7A) and (7B) are omitted.

## 4. Consequential amendment of the Official Feed and Food Controls (Scotland) Regulations 2005

In Schedule 2 (definition of relevant feed law) of the Official Feed and Food Controls (Scotland) Regulations 2005[^f00005], paragraph (f) is omitted.

SHONA ROBISON

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

1st November 2007

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Feed (Corn Gluten Feed and Brewers Grains) (Emergency Control) (Scotland) Regulations 2005 and make consequential amendments to The Genetically Modified Animal Feed (Scotland) Regulations and the Official Feed and Food Controls (Scotland) Regulations 2005 ([S.S.I. 2005/616](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2005/616)).

The Regulations implement Commission Decision [2007/157/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0157) repealing Decision [2005/317/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0317) on emergency measures regarding the non-authorised genetically modified organism Bt10 in maize products (O.J. No. L 68, 8.3.2007, p.8).

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in relation to these Regulations.
