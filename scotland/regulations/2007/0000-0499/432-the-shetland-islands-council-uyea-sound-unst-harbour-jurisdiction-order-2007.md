# The Shetland Islands Council (Uyea Sound, Unst) Harbour Jurisdiction Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 432

HARBOURS, DOCKS, PIERS AND FERRIES

The Shetland Islands Council (Uyea Sound, Unst) Harbour Jurisdiction Order 2007

Made

18th September 2007

Coming into force

19th September 2007

The Shetland Islands Council have applied for an order under section 33 of the Zetland County Council Act 1974[^f00001]:

No objections to the application have been made:

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 33 of the Act[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Shetland Islands Council (Uyea Sound, Unst) Harbour Jurisdiction Order 2007 and shall come into force on 19th September 2007.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order “the Uyea Sound area” means so much of the tidal waters of Uyea Sound as is bounded seawards by an imaginary straight line drawn in an easterly direction from a point at latitude 60°41.25'N, longitude 00°55.16'W to a point at latitude 60°41.14'N, longitude 00°54.38'W and the reference to co-ordinates are references to WGS 84 (ETRS89) datum.

## 3. Designation of the Uyea Sound area

The Uyea Sound area is designated as an area within which the Council shall exercise jurisdiction as the harbour authority.

## 4. Uyea Sound Area to be within area of Council

The Uyea Sound area shall be deemed for all purposes to be situated within the administrative area of the Council.

ALASTAIR WILSON

A member of the staff of the Scottish Executive

Victoria Quay Edinburgh

18th September 2007

(This note is not part of the Order)

Shetland Islands Council exercise jurisdiction as harbour authority under the Zetland County Council Act 1974 in respect of the area specified in Schedule 1 to that Act.

The Order establishes a new harbour area at Uyea Sound, Unst within which the Council shall exercise harbour jurisdiction pursuant to section 6 of the 1974 Act and that area shall be deemed for all purposes to be within the administrative area of the Council.

The applicant for this Order is Shetland Islands Council, Town Hall, Lerwick, Shetland.
