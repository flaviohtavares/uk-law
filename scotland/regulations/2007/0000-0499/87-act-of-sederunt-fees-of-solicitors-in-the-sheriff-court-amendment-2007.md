# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 87

SHERIFF COURT

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 2007

Made

16th February 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

20th February 2007

Coming into force

1st April 2007

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 2007 and shall come into force on 1st April 2007.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of Table of Fees

(1) Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment and Further Provisions) 1993[^f00002] shall be amended in accordance with the following sub paragraphs.

(2) In the Table of Fees, in the provisions mentioned in column 1 of the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt, for the amounts specified in column 2 of that Schedule, there shall be substituted the amounts specified in column 3 of that Schedule.

(3) In Part II of Chapter II (defended ordinary actions, commercial actions and family actions commenced on or after 1st January 1994)–

- (a) in paragraph 4(b), for “Options Hearing” there shall be substituted “expiry of adjustment period”; and
- (b) in paragraph 20(b), at the end there shall be inserted “(not to include drawing, intimating and lodging any written motion)”.

## 3. Saving

Paragraph 2 shall not affect fees chargeable for work done, or outlays incurred, before 1st April 2007.

A C HAMILTON

Lord President, I.P.D.

Edinburgh

16th February 2007

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt makes amendments to the Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment and Further Provisions) 1993 (S.I. [1993/3080](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1993/3080)) (“the Table of Fees”).

Paragraph 2(2) increases the fees recoverable under the Table of Fees by about 4%.

Paragraph 2(3) amends Part II of Chapter II (defended actions commenced on or after 1st January 1994) to provide for recovery of the restricted adjustment fee where an action settles prior to the end of the adjustment period and to clarify that the extra-judicial settlement fee does not include the drawing, intimating and lodging of a written motion.

This Act of Sederunt does not apply as respects fees chargeable for work done, or outlays incurred, before it comes into force.
