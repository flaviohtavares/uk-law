# The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 241 (C. 21)

SECURITY INDUSTRY

The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2007

Made

13th March 2007

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 178(6)(b) and (9) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00001], and having consulted with the Secretary of State in accordance with section 178(6) of that Act, make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2007.

## 2. Appointed day and extent

6th April 2007 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005–

- (a) paragraphs 8 and 9 of Schedule 15 (Private Security Industry Act 2001[^f00002]: Scottish extent), so far as those paragraphs extend to England and Wales, and in so far as they are not already in force; and
- (b) section 171(1), so far as relating to those paragraphs in their extent to England and Wales.

CATHY JAMIESON

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

13th March 2007

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force provisions of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 which amend the Private Security Industry Act 2001 (“the 2001 Act”) in its application to Scotland. This Order commences the amendments in Schedule 15 for England and Wales on 6th April 2007.

Sections 15 and 18 of the 2001 Act are not yet in force in Scotland. Those sections of the 2001 Act are to be brought into force for Scotland on 6th April 2007 by a commencement order made under the 2001 Act. Sections 15 and 18 of the 2001 Act are already in force in England and Wales. The amendments being made to sections 15 and 18 by this Order have already been made to those sections in so far as they apply to Scotland by the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2006. The amendments in respect of Scotland made to those sections by virtue of this Order will take effect for England and Wales on 6th April 2007, the same date on which those sections are brought into force in Scotland.
