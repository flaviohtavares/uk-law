# The Provision of School Education for Children under School Age (Prescribed Children) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 396

EDUCATION

The Provision of School Education for Children under School Age (Prescribed Children) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2007

Made

30th August 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

3rd September 2007

Coming into force

25th September 2007

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 1(1B) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001]and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Provision of School Education for Children under School Age (Prescribed Children) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2007 and comes into force on 25th September 2007.

## 2. Amendment of the prescribed amount of school education

(1) The Provision of School Education for Children under School Age (Prescribed Children) (Scotland) Order 2002[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In article 3(1) (amount of school education to be provided for children of a prescribed description) for “412.5 hours of school education” substitute “475 hours of school education”.

ADAM H INGRAM

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

30th August 2007

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 32 of the Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000 amended section 1 of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980 (“the 1980 Act”) by imposing a duty on an education authority to secure the provision of school education for categories of children as may be prescribed by the Scottish Ministers. Section 1(1B) of the 1980 Act provides the power to prescribe the amount of school education that children within those categories are to receive.

The Provision of School Education for Children under School Age (Prescribed Children) (Scotland) Order 2002 ([S.S.I. 2002/90](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2002/90)

) prescribes the category of children eligible to receive pre-school education and article 3(1) of that Order provides that such children are to receive 412.5 hours of school education during the school year.

Article 2(2) of this Order amends the prescribed amount of education so that it is increased to 475 hours of school education during the school year.
