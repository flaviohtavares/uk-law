# The Housing (Scotland) Act 2006 (Consequential Amendments) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 475

HOUSING

The Housing (Scotland) Act 2006 (Consequential Amendments) Order 2007

Made

19th October 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

25th October 2007

Coming into force

20th November 2007

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 190 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2006[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Housing (Scotland) Act 2006 (Consequential Amendments) Order 2007 and comes into force on 20th November 2007.

## 2. Amendments

(1) In each statutory instrument referred to in column 1 of the Schedule, where “rent assessment” appears in each of the provisions listed in column 2, substitute “private rented housing”.

(2) In paragraph 6 of the form of notice in the Schedule to the Assured Tenancies (Rent Book) (Scotland) Regulations 1988[^f00002]–

- (a) for the heading substitute “Determination by a private rented housing committee”; and
- (b) in subparagraphs (1) and (2), for “RAC” substitute “private rented housing committee”.

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes amendments to 5 statutory instruments to replace references to “rent assessment panel” and “rent assessment committee” with “private rented housing panel” and “private rented housing committee” respectively. The amendments are consequential upon section 21 of the [Housing (Scotland) Act 2006 (asp 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2006/1), which makes provision for the names of the panel and committee.

Paragraphs 4, 5, 7, 16, 17 and 19 of Schedule 6 to that Act made similar consequential amendments to the Rent (Scotland) Act [1984 (c. 58)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/58), the Housing (Scotland) Act [1988 (c. 43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/43), the Tribunals and Inquiries Act [1992 (c. 53)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/53) and the [Scottish Public Services Ombudsman Act 2002 (asp 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2002/11).
