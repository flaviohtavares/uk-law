# The Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 79

PUBLIC PASSENGER TRANSPORT

The Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2007

Made

13th February 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

16th February 2007

Coming into force

1st April 2007

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 52(1) and 60(1)(e) and (1A) of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and after consultation with representative organisations in accordance with section 61(2) of that Act, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2007 and shall come into force on 1st April 2007.

## 2. Amendment of the principal Regulations

(1) The Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2001[^f00002] are amended in accordance with this regulation.

(2) In regulation 13(1)(a) and (2)(a) for “£11” substitute “£12”.

(3) In regulation 13(1)(b) and (2)(b) for “£51” substitute “£54”.

## 3. Revocations

The following Regulations–

- (a) the Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2002[^f00003];
- (b) the Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2004[^f00004]; and
- (c) the Public Service Vehicles (Registration of Local Services) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2005[^f00005],

are revoked.

TAVISH SCOTT

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

13th February 2007

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations increase the fees for the registration of, or the variation of the registration of, a community bus service, other than a service supplied under an agreement providing for service subsidies, from £11 to £12 (an increase of 9.1%). The fees were last increased on 1st November 2004.

These Regulations also increase the fees for the registration of, or the variation of the registration of, a local bus service, other than a community bus service. The fee is increased from £51 to £54 (an increase of 5.9%). The fees were last increased on 29th August 2005.

Regulation 3 revokes previous instruments which increased fees.

A Regulatory Impact Assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business is available from Bus and Taxi Policy, the Scottish Executive, Bus, Freight and Roads Division, Area 2 F(North), Victoria Quay, Edinburgh EH6 6QQ.
