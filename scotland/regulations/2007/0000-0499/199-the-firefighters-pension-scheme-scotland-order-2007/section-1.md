# The Firefighters' Pension Scheme (Scotland) Order 2007, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement, effect and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Firefighters' Pension Scheme (Scotland) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 1st April 2007, but shall have effect from 6th April 2006.

(2) This Order extends to Scotland only.
