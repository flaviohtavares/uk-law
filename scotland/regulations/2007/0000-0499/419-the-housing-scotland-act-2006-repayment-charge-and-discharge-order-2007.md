# The Housing (Scotland) Act 2006 (Repayment Charge and Discharge) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 419

HOUSING

The Housing (Scotland) Act 2006 (Repayment Charge and Discharge) Order 2007

Made

6th September 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

7th September 2007

Coming into force

8th October 2007

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 174 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2006[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Housing (Scotland) Act 2006 (Repayment Charge and Discharge) Order 2007 and comes into force on 8th October 2007.

## 2. Form of repayment charge and discharge

A repayment charge must be in the form set out in Schedule 1 or as close to it as circumstances permit.

**3.** A discharge of a repayment charge must be in the form set out in Schedule 2 or as close to it as circumstances permit.

The Schedules to this Order set out the form of a repayment charge and the form of a discharge of a repayment charge which can be made by a local authority under Part 7 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2006.

A local authority is entitled to make a repayment charge to recover a sum under section 59(1) or (2) of, or paragraph 6(1) of schedule 5 to that Act.
