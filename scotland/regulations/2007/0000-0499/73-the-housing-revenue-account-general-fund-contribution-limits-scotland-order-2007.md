# The Housing Revenue Account General Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 73

HOUSING

The Housing Revenue Account General Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 2007

Made

8th February 2007

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

15th February 2007

Coming into force

15th March 2007

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 204 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Housing Revenue Account General Fund Contribution Limits (Scotland) Order 2007 and shall come into force on 15th March 2007.

## 2. General fund contribution limits

The amount of contribution out of its general fund which a local authority may estimate that it will carry to the credit of its housing revenue account for the year 2007–2008 shall be limited to nil.

RHONA BRANKIN

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

8th February 2007

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that local authorities may not include in their estimates for the year 2007–2008 any contribution from their general fund to their housing revenue account.
