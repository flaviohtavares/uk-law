# The Police (Injury Benefit) (Scotland) Regulations 2007, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement, effect and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Police (Injury Benefit) (Scotland) Regulations 2007 and come into force on 7th March 2007 but shall have effect from 6th April 2006[^f00003].

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

## Annotations

[^f00003]: Footnote: Retrospective effect is permitted by section 1(5) of the Police Pensions Act [1976 (c. 35)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1976/35).
