# The Police (Injury Benefit) (Scotland) Regulations 2007, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. Authorities responsible for payment of awards

(1) An award which is payable to or in respect of a person by reason of their having served as a regular police officer shall be payable by the police authority of the force in which the officer last served as such.

(2) An award which is payable to or in respect of a person by reason of their having been injured while serving as a member of a police force other than a regular police officer shall be payable by the police authority of the force in which the officer was serving when the officer received the injury.
