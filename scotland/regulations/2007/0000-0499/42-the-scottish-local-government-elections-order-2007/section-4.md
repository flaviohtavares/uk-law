# The Scottish Local Government Elections Order 2007, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Application of Part III of the Representation of the People Act 1983

Part III of the 1983 Act applies to elections of councillors for local government areas with exception of the following provisions–

- (a) sections 120 to 126;
- (b) section 128(3)(a) and (4);
- (c) sections 130 to 133;
- (d) sections 135 and 135A;
- (e) section 144; and
- (f) section 145A
