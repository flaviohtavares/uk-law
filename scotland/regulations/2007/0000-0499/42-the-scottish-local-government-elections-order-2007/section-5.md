> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Consequential amendment

In section 45 of the 1983 Act, for “this Act and the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994” substitute “this Act, the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994 and the Local Governance (Scotland) Act 2004”[^f00009].
