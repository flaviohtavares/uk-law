# The Police Pensions (Scotland) Regulations 2007, section 81

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 81. Authorities responsible for payment of awards

(1) An award which is payable to or in respect of a person by reason of that officer having served as a regular police officer shall be payable by the police authority.

(2) An award which is payable to or in respect of a pension credit member under Part 6, and any sum paid in commutation of such an award, is payable by the relevant police authority.
