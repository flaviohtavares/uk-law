# The Police Pensions (Scotland) Regulations 2007, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. Deductions of tax from awards under regulation 9(4) or 34

The police authority may deduct from any payment by way of an award under regulation 9(4) or 34–

- (a) that part of any contributions equivalent premium paid in respect of the regular police officer as is permitted under section 61 of the 1993 Act[^f00009]; and
- (b) the tax for the time being chargeable on that award under section 205 of the Finance Act 2004[^f00010].
