# The Police Pensions (Scotland) Regulations 2007, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Reckoning of part-time service

(1) In the case of a regular police officer who has spent one or more periods in part-time service, the number of days of pensionable service in any such period shall be calculated by the application of the following fraction–

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2007/201)

where

- A is the total number of determined hours of part-time service specified under the part-time appointment of that officer in the period in question and B is the number of hours per week if the service during that period were on a full-time basis.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1) a period of part-time service is to be taken to have ended and another such period to have begun on the occurrence of any change in the result achieved after applying that fraction.
