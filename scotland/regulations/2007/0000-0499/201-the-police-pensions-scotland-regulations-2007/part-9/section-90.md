# The Police Pensions (Scotland) Regulations 2007, section 90

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 90. Awards to servicemen

(1) Subject to regulation 95, this regulation applies to a serviceman who at the end of that serviceman’s period of relevant service in the reserve forces is or was permanently disabled for the performance of the ordinary duties of a member of the police force.

(2) A serviceman to whom this regulation applies shall be entitled–

- (a) to an ill-health pension under regulation 29 on the same conditions in all respects as if the serviceman were such a regular police officer as is mentioned in paragraph (1) of that regulation; or
- (b) where the serviceman does not meet the condition specified in regulation 29(3)(a)(i), to an award by way of repayment of that serviceman’s aggregate pension contributions under regulation 34.
