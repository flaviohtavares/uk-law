# The Police Pensions (Scotland) Regulations 2007, section 92

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 92. Prevention of duplication

Regulation 49 shall apply in relation to a pension in pursuance of regulation 91 as if, after the words “under regulation 39” in regulation 49, there were inserted the words “or regulation 91”.
