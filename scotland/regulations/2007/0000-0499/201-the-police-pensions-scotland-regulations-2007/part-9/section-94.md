# The Police Pensions (Scotland) Regulations 2007, section 94

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 94. Servicemen who do not resume service in their former force

Where a serviceman who ceased to serve as a regular police officer in order to undertake a period of relevant service in the reserve forces does not or did not resume service in that serviceman’s former force within a month of the end of that period, the serviceman shall be treated for the purposes of these Regulations as having ceased to serve as a regular police officer in that force as from the end of that serviceman’s period of relevant service in the reserve forces.
