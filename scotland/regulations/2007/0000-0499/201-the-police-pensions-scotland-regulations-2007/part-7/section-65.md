# The Police Pensions (Scotland) Regulations 2007, section 65

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 65. Eligibility for awards – general

Subject to the following provisions of this Part, the question whether a person is entitled to any, and if so what, awards under these Regulations shall be determined in the first instance by the police authority.
