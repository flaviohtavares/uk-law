# The Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 472 (C. 39)

LICENSING (LIQUOR)

The Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2007

Made

18th October 2007

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 146(2) and 150(2) of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 4) Order 2007.

## 2. Commencement

The provisions of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 specified in Schedule 1 come into force on 1st February 2008.

**3.** So far as not already in force, the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 comes into force at 5.00 a.m on 1st September 2009.

KENNY MacASKILL

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

18th October 2007

This Order brings certain specified provisions of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 into force on 1st February 2008 (article 2 and Schedules 1 and 2). So far as not already in force, that Act is brought into force at 5.00 a.m. on 1st September 2009 (article 3).

Provisions of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 have previously been brought into force by [S.S.I. 2006/286](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2006/286) and

[2007/129](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2007/129).
