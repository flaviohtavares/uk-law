# The Transport and Works (Scotland) Act 2007 (Applications and Objections Procedure) Rules 2007, section 25

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 25. Power to set later time limits

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers may at any time in any particular case allow further time for the taking of any step (including a step which they are required or enabled to take themselves) which may or must be taken pursuant to these Rules and any reference to a date by which or a period within which any step is required to be taken shall be construed accordingly.
