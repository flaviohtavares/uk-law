# The Transport and Works (Scotland) Act 2007 (Applications and Objections Procedure) Rules 2007, section 23

## 23. Submission of objections to inquiry or hearing

*Extent: S*

Where the Scottish Ministers cause a public local inquiry or hearing to be held under section 9 of the Act, the Scottish Ministers shall submit every objection made pursuant to rule 19 to the person appointed to hold the inquiry or hearing, as soon as it is reasonably practicable to do so.
