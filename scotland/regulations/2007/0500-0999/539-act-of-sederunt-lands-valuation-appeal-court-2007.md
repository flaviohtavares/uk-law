# Act of Sederunt (Lands Valuation Appeal Court) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 539

COURT OF SESSION

Act of Sederunt (Lands Valuation Appeal Court) 2007

Made

30th November 2007

Coming into force

10th December 2007

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 7 of the Valuation of Lands (Scotland) Amendment Act 1879[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Lands Valuation Appeal Court) 2007 and shall come into force on 10th December 2007.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Lands Valuation Appeal Court

(1) The judges named for the purposes of appeal under section 7 of the Valuation of Lands (Scotland) Amendment Act 1879 are–

- The Right Honourable Lord Gill;
- The Right Honourable Lord Nimmo Smith;
- The Right Honourable Lord Kingarth;
- The Honourable Lord Clarke;
- The Honourable Lord Hodge.

(2) The Act of Sederunt (Lands Valuation Appeal Court) 2002[^f00002] is revoked.

A. C. HAMILTON

Lord President I.P.D.

Edinburgh

30th November 2007

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt appoints the judges who may hear appeals under section 7 of the Valuation of Lands (Scotland) Amendment Act 1879. It revokes the Act of Sederunt (Lands Valuation Appeal Court) 2002, which previously made such provision.
