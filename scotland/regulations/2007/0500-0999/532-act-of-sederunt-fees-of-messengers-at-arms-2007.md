# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 532

COURT OF SESSION

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 2007

Made

27th November 2007

Coming into force

1st January 2008

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 6 of the Execution of Diligence (Scotland) Act 1926[^f00001], section 5 of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, with the concurrence of the Lord Lyon King of Arms, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers-at-Arms) 2007 and shall come into force on 1st January 2008.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of fees

(1) Subject to subparagraph (2) below, the Table of Fees in the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt shall be substituted for the Table in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Messengers at Arms) (No. 2) 2002[^f00003].

(2) Subparagraph (1) shall apply to work carried out by a messenger-at-arms on or after 1st January 2008 but shall not affect the fees payable as respects any work so carried out before that date.

A C HAMILTON

Lord President I.P.D.

Edinburgh

27th November 2007
