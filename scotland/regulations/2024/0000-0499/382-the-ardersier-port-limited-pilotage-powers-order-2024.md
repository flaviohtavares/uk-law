# The Ardersier Port Limited (Pilotage Powers) Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 382

Pilotage

The Ardersier Port Limited (Pilotage Powers) Order 2024

Made

17th December 2024

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

19th December 2024

Coming into force

20th January 2025

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 1(4) of the Pilotage Act 1987[^f00001] and all other powers enabling Ministers to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Ardersier Port Limited (Pilotage Powers) Order 2024 and comes into force on 20 January 2025.

## 2. Application of the Pilotage Act 1987

Ardersier Port Limited[^f00002] is a competent harbour authority[^f00003] for the purposes of the Pilotage Act 1987.

FIONA HYSLOP

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

17th December 2024

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that Ardersier Port Limited is a competent harbour authority for the purposes of the Pilotage Act 1987.

The applicant for the Order is Ardersier Port Limited having their registered office at Level 4, Dashwood House, 69 Old Broad Street, London EC2M 1QS.
