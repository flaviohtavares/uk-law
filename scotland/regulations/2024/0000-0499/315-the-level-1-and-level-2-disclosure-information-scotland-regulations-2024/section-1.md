# The Level 1 and Level 2 Disclosure Information (Scotland) Regulations 2024, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Level 1 and Level 2 Disclosure Information (Scotland) Regulations 2024 and come into force on the same day as section 8 of the Disclosure Act.
