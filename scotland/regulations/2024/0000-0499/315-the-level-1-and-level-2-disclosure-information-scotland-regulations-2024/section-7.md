# The Level 1 and Level 2 Disclosure Information (Scotland) Regulations 2024, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Level 2 disclosures (non-PVG scheme members): prescribed details of circumstances in which an applicant is barred

(1) For the purposes of section 16(4)(a)(ii) of the Disclosure Act, the detail of the list under which the applicant is barred from regulated roles with children is prescribed.

(2) For the purposes of section 16(4)(b)(ii) of the Disclosure Act, the detail of the list under which the applicant is barred from regulated roles with adults is prescribed.
