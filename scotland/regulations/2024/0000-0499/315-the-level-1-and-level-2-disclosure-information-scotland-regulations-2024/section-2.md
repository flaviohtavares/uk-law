# The Level 1 and Level 2 Disclosure Information (Scotland) Regulations 2024, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations—

- “**the 1995 Act**” means the Children (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00002],
- “**the 2000 Act**” means the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000[^f00003],
- “**the 2003 Act**” means the Sexual Offences Act 2003[^f00004],
- “**the 2005 Act**” means the Protection of Children and Prevention of Sexual Offences (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00005],
- “**the 2016 Act**” means the Abusive Behaviour and Sexual Harm (Scotland) Act 2016[^f00006],
- “**the Disclosure Act**” means the Disclosure (Scotland) Act 2020,
- “**the PVG Act**” means the Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007[^f00007],
- “**the Sentencing Code**” means the code contained in the Sentencing Act 2020 (see section 1 of that Act)[^f00008],
- “**school**” has the meaning given in section 135(1) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00009],
- “**school care accommodation service**” has the meaning given in paragraph 3 of schedule 12 of the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010[^f00010].
