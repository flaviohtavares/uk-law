# The Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000 (Codes of Conduct for Members of certain Scottish Public Authorities) Amendment Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

The Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000 (Codes of Conduct for Members of certain Scottish Public Authorities) Amendment Order 2024

Made

6th February 2024

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

8th February 2024

Coming into force

1st April 2024

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 32(1) of the Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000 (Codes of Conduct for Members of certain Scottish Public Authorities) Amendment Order 2024 and comes into force on 1 April 2024.

## 2. Amendment of the Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000 (Codes of Conduct for Members of certain Scottish Public Authorities) Order 2006

In the schedule of the Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000 (Codes of Conduct for Members of certain Scottish Public Authorities) Order 2006[^f00002], after the entry relating to Mobility and Access Committee for Scotland insert—

> - National Smart Ticketing Advisory Board

.

FIONA HYSLOP

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

6th February 2024

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order inserts the National Smart Ticketing Advisory Board into the list of public authorities in the schedule of the Ethical Standards in Public Life etc. (Scotland) Act 2000 (Codes of Conduct for Members of certain Scottish Public Authorities) Order 2006. The effect of this amendment is to require the Scottish Ministers to issue a code or codes of conduct in respect of the conduct of members of the National Smart Ticketing Advisory Board.
