# The Academic Awards and Distinctions (SRUC) (Scotland) Order of Council 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 219

Education

The Academic Awards and Distinctions (SRUC) (Scotland) Order of Council 2024

Made

29th August 2024

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

2nd September 2024

Coming into force

14th October 2024

At the Council Chamber, Whitehall, the 29th day of August 2024

By the Lords of His Majesty’s Most Honourable Council

Their Lordships make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 48(1) and 60(3) of the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Academic Awards and Distinctions (SRUC) (Scotland) Order of Council 2024 and comes into force on 14 October 2024.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “**the Act**” means the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992,
- “**the Institution**” means SRUC[^f00002].

## 3. Competence to grant academic awards and distinctions

(1) The Institution is specified as competent to grant such degrees, diplomas, certificates or other academic awards or distinctions as may be granted in pursuance of section 48(2) and (3) of the Act other than in respect of completion of a programme of supervised research.

(2) The date specified for the purpose of section 48(2)(a) of the Act is 14 October 2024.

(3) Following any change of name of the Institution the competence to grant degrees, diplomas, certificates or other academic awards or distinctions, arising from the specification in paragraph (1), continues to apply to the Institution as renamed.

Richard Tilbrook

Clerk of the Privy Council

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies SRUC, a higher education institution, as competent to grant degrees, diplomas, certificates and other academic awards or distinctions except where the award or distinction is made on completion of a programme of supervised research.

Article 3(2) provides that the institution may grant such awards and distinctions in respect of courses and programmes of study completed on or after 14 October 2024.

Article 3(3) ensures that the competence to grant academic awards and distinctions continues to apply should the institution be renamed.
