# The Water Supply (Water Fittings) (Scotland) Byelaws Extension Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 139

Water Supply

The Water Supply (Water Fittings) (Scotland) Byelaws Extension Order 2024

Made

7th May 2024

Coming into force

10th May 2024

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 72(4) of the Water (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Water Supply (Water Fittings) (Scotland) Byelaws Extension Order 2024 and comes into force on 10 May 2024.

## 2. Extension

The period during which the Water Supply (Water Fittings) (Scotland) Byelaws 2014[^f00002] are to remain in force is extended to 12 May 2029.

JON RATHJEN

A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers

Victoria Quay

Edinburgh

7th May 2024

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends the period during which the Water Supply (Water Fittings) (Scotland) Byelaws 2014 are to remain in force to 12 May 2029. A copy of the Byelaws may be obtained from Scottish Water, The Bridge, Buchanan Gate Business Park, Cumbernauld Road, Stepps, G33 6FB, and online at www.scottishwater.co.uk.

[^f00002]: Footnote: The Water Supply (Water Fittings) (Scotland) Byelaws 2014 were made by Scottish Water on 13 May 2014 and came into operation on 12 July 2014. Sections 70 to 72 of the 1980 Act were relevantly amended by paragraphs 37 to 39 of schedule 13 of the 1994 Act and by paragraphs 44 to 46 of schedule 6 of the Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002.
