# The Animal Welfare (Livestock Exports) Act 2024 (Commencement) (Scotland) Regulations 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 170 (C. 16)

Animals

The Animal Welfare (Livestock Exports) Act 2024 (Commencement) (Scotland) Regulations 2024

Made

13th June 2024

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

17th June 2024

Coming into force

22nd July 2024

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred by section 7(2)(b) of the Animal Welfare (Livestock Exports) Act 2024[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Animal Welfare (Livestock Exports) Act 2024 (Commencement) (Scotland) Regulations 2024 and come into force on 22 July 2024.

## 2. Appointed day

22 July 2024 is the day appointed for the coming into force of sections 1 (prohibition of export of livestock for slaughter) and 5 (connected repeals in relation to horses) of the Animal Welfare (Livestock Exports) Act 2024.

JIM FAIRLIE

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

13th June 2024

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force in relation to Scotland the remaining provisions of the Animal Welfare (Livestock Exports) Act [2024 (c. 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/11) (“**the Act**”) which did not come into force on the day on which the Act was passed. Those provisions are sections 1 (prohibition of export of livestock for slaughter) and 5 (connected repeals in relation to horses) of the Act. They come into force on 22 July 2024. The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 20 May 2024.
