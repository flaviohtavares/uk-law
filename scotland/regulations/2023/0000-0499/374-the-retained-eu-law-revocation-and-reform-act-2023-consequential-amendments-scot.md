# The Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Consequential Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2023

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

2023 No. 374

Retained Eu Law Reform

The Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Consequential Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2023

Made

7th December 2023

Coming into force

1st January 2024

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 19(1) of the Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with paragraph 7(1) of schedule 5 of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^key-bc32522997cfcf131e6763668d507483]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023 (Consequential Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2023 and come into force on 1 January 2024.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland and the Scottish zone, and insofar as they extend beyond Scotland and the Scottish zone they do so only as a matter of Scots law.

(3) In paragraph (2), “**Scotland**” and “**the Scottish zone**” have the same meaning as in section 126(1) and (2) of the Scotland Act 1998[^f00002].

## 2. Amendments of primary legislation[^key-fd8bc8c11ba00520d28890f4cf9c791d]

Schedule 1 of these Regulations (amendments of primary legislation) has effect.

## 3. Amendments of secondary legislation[^key-3d6424086c62a74fa952e44e81822c4c]

Schedule 2 of these regulations (amendments of secondary legislation) has effect.
