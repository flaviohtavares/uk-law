# The Public Procurement (Agreement on Government Procurement) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2023

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2023 No. 287

Public Procurement

The Public Procurement (Agreement on Government Procurement) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2023

Made

at 10.38 a.m. on 18th October 2023

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

at 2.30 p.m. on 18th October 2023

Coming into force

30th October 2023

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(1)(b)(i) of the Trade Act 2021[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Public Procurement (Agreement on Government Procurement) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2023.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 30 October 2023.

## 2. Amendment of the Public Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2015

(1) The Public Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2015[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) in the definition of “GPA”, for “16th May 2021” substitute “30 October 2023”.

(3) In the list in schedule 1A (parties to the GPA), after “New Zealand” insert —

> North Macedonia, Republic of

.

## 3. Amendment of the Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2016

(1) The Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2016[^f00003] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) in the definition of “GPA”, for “16th May 2021” substitute “30 October 2023”.

(3) In the list in schedule A1 (parties to the GPA), after “New Zealand” insert–

> North Macedonia, Republic of

.

## 4. Amendment of the Concession Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2016

(1) The Concession Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2016[^f00004] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation) in the definition of “GPA”, for “16th May 2021” substitute “30 October 2023”.

(3) In the list in schedule 1A (parties to the GPA), after “New Zealand” insert–

> North Macedonia, Republic of

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations insert the Republic of North Macedonia into the list of parties to the Agreement on Government Procurement (“the GPA”) specified in the Public Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2015, the Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2016 and the Concession Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2016 in accordance with the obligations of the United Kingdom under the GPA.

These Regulations also amend the definition of the GPA set out in each of the three sets of Regulations being amended.
