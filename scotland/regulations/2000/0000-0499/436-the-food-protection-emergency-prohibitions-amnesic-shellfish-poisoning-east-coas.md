# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (East Coast) (No. 2) (Scotland) Partial Revocation Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 436

PUBLIC HEALTH

CONTAMINATION OF FOOD

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic ShellfishPoisoning) (East Coast) (No. 2) (Scotland) Partial Revocation Order 2000

Made

29th November 2000

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

4th December 2000

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section1(1) and (2) and section 24(3) of the Food and Environment Protection Act1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf,hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions)(Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (East Coast) (No. 2) (Scotland) PartialRevocation Order 2000 and shall come into force at 1730 hours on 29thNovember 2000.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order “the principal Order” means the Food Protection(Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (East Coast) (No. 2)(Scotland) Order 2000[^f00002].

## 3. Partial Revocation of the principal Order

The principal Order is revoked insofar as it designates any part ofArea 2 described in the Schedule to the principal Order.

DAVID R DICKSON

A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

29th November 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the first partial revocation of the Food Protection(Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (East Coast) (No. 2)(Scotland) Order 2000 ([S.S.I. 2000/370](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2000/370)) (“the principal Order”) whichprohibited–

- (a) fishing for or taking scallops within the areas designated in theSchedule to that Order (article 4 of the principal Order);
- (b) movement of such scallops out of these areas (article 5 of theprincipal Order); and
- (c) landing, using in the preparation or processing for supply of food of,supplying and other specified activities in relation to, such scallops fromthe designated areas (article 6 of the principal Order).

The effect of this Order is to remove the prohibitions in respect ofthe area of sea designated as Area 2 in the Schedule to the principal Order.
