# The Potatoes Originating in Egypt (Amendment) (Scotland) Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 8

PLANT HEALTH

The Potatoes Originating in Egypt (Amendment) (Scotland) Regulations 2000

Made

13th January 2000

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

17th January 2000

Coming into force

7th February 2000

The Scottish Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them in that behalf hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Potatoes Originating in Egypt (Amendment) (Scotland) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 7th February 2000.

(2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order “the principal Order” means the Potatoes Originating in Egypt Regulations 1998[^f00002].

## 3. Amendment of the principal Order

In regulation 2 (interpretation) of the principal Order for the definition of “the Decision” there shall be substituted–

> - “the Decision” means Commission Decision [96/301/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1996/0301) authorising Member States temporarily to take additional measures against the dissemination of*Pseudomonas solanacearum*(Smith) Smith as regards Egypt[^f00003] as amended by Commission Decision [98/105/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1998/0105) authorising Member States temporarily to take emergency measures against the dissemination of the*Pseudomonas solanacearum*(Smith) Smith as regards Egypt[^f00004], Commission Decision [98/503/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1998/0503) authorising Member States temporarily to take emergency measures against the dissemination*of Pseudomonas solanacearum*(Smith) Smith as regards Egypt[^f00005] and Commission Decision [99/842/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1999/0842) authorising Member States temporarily to take emergency measures against the dissemination of*Pseudomonas solanacearum* as regards Egypt[^f00006];

.

**4.** In regulation 3(2) (imports of potatoes originating in Egypt) and regulation 5A(1) (charges in respect of the sampling of imports of potatoes), for the words “*Pseudomonas solanacearum*(Smith) Smith” there shall be substituted “*Ralstonia solanacearum*(Smith) Yabuuchi et al”[^f00007].

ROSS FINNIE

13th January 2000

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations implement Commission Decision [99/842/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1999/0842) amending Decision [96/301/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1996/0301) and authorising Member States temporarily to take emergency measures against the dissemination of*Pseudomonas solanacearum*(Smith) Smith as regards Egypt (O.J. No. L326, 18.12.99, p.68) by amending the definition of “the Decision” in the Potatoes Originating in Egypt Regulations 1998 (the 1998 Regulations) (regulation 3). Decision [99/842/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1999/0842) makes minor modifications to the requirements applying to potatoes imported into Member States from Egypt.

The Regulations also substitute the references to “*Pseudomonas solanacearum*(Smith) Smith” in the 1998 Regulations with the term “*Ralstonia solacearum*(Smith) Yabuuchi et al.” (regulation 4).
