# The Colours in Food (Amendment) (Scotland) Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 131

FOOD

The Colours in Food (Amendment) (Scotland) Regulations 2000

Made

28th April 2000

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

9th May 2000

Coming into force

30th June 2000

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 6(4), 16(1)(a) and (e), 17(1), 26(1) and (3) and 48(1) of, and paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to, the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and after consultation in accordance with section 48(4) of that Act with such organisations as appear to them to be representative of interests likely to be substantially affected by the following Regulations, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Colours in Food (Amendment) (Scotland) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 30th June 2000.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

## 2. Amendment of the Colours in Food Regulations 1995

The Colours in Food Regulations 1995[^f00002] shall be amended by the addition, at the end of the definition of “Directive [95/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045)” in regulation 2(1), of the words “as amended by Commission Directive [1999/75/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0075)[^f00003]”.

SUSAN C DEACON

A member of the Scottish Executive

St. Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

28th April 2000

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which extend to Scotland only, amend the Colours in Food Regulations 1995 by bringing up to date a reference to Directive [1995/45/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0045) laying down specific criteria concerning colours for use in foodstuffs so as to cover its amendment by Directive [1999/75/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0075), which changed the specification for “E160a(i) Mixed Carotenes”.
