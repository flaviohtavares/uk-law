# The Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Miscellaneous enactments: consequential provisions

(1) The Sludge (Use in Agriculture) Regulations 1989[^f00033] are modified in accordance with Part I of Schedule 8.

(2) The Environmental Protection (Applications, Appeals and Registers) Regulations 1991[^f00034] are modified in accordance with Part II of Schedule 8.

(3) The Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995[^f00035] are modified in accordance with Part III of Schedule 8.

(4) The Bovines and Bovine Products (Trade) Regulations 1999[^f00036] are modified in accordance with Part IV of Schedule 8.

(5) The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water Regulations 1999[^f00037] are modified in accordance with Part IV Schedule 8.
