# The Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Regulations relating to general food hygiene, temperature control and fish and shellfish hygiene: consequential provisions

(1) The Food Safety (General Food Hygiene) Regulations 1995[^f00010] are modified in accordance with Part I of Schedule 3.

(2) The Food Safety (Temperature Control) Regulations 1995[^f00011] and modified in accordance with Part II of Schedule 3.

(3) The Food Safety (Fishery Products and Live Shellfish) (Hygiene) Regulations 1998[^f00012] are modified in accordance with Part III Schedule 3.
