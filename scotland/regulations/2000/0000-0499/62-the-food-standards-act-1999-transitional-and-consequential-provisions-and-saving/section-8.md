# The Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Regulations relating to novel foods and food irradiation: consequential provisions

(1) The Novel Foods and Novel Food Ingredients Regulations 1997[^f00027] are modified in accordance with Part I of Schedule 6.

(2) The Novel Foods and Novel Food Ingredients (Fees) Regulations 1997[^f00028] are modified in accordance with Part II of Schedule 6.

(3) The Food (Control of Irradiation) Regulations 1990[^f00029] are modified in accordance with Part III of Schedule 6.
