# The Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Regulations relating to meat hygiene, etc.: consequential provisions

(1) The Meat Hygiene Appeals Tribunal (Procedure) Regulations 1992[^f00013] are modified in accordance with Part I of Schedule 4.

(2) The Meat Products (Hygiene) Regulations 1994[^f00014] are modified in accordance with Part II of Schedule 4.

(3) The Fresh Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1995[^f00015] are modified in accordance with Part III of Schedule 4.

(4) The Poultry Meat, Farmed Game Bird Meat and Rabbit Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1995[^f00016] are modified in accordance with Part IV of Schedule 4.

(5) The Animal By-Products (Identification) Regulations 1995[^f00017] are modified in accordance with Part V of Schedule 4.

(6) The Wild Game Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) Regulations 1995[^f00018] are modified in accordance with Part VI of Schedule 4.

(7) The Minced Meat and Meat Preparations (Hygiene) Regulations 1995[^f00019] are modified in accordance with Part VII of Schedule 4.

(8) The Fresh Meat (Beef Controls) (No. 2) Regulations 1996[^f00020] are modified in accordance with Part VIII of Schedule 4.

(9) The Beef Bones (Scotland) Regulations 1999[^f00021] are modified in accordance with Part IX of Schedule 4.

(10) The Specified Risk Material Regulations 1997[^f00022] are modified in accordance with Part X of Schedule 4.

(11) The Meat (Hygiene and Inspection) (Charges) Regulations 1998[^f00023] are modified in accordance with Part XI of Schedule 4.

(12) The Specified Risk Material (Inspection Charges) Regulations 1999[^f00024] are modified in accordance with Part XII of Schedule 4.
