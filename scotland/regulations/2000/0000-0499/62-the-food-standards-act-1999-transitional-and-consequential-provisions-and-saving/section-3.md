# The Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Regulations relating to dairy and egg hygiene: consequential provisions

(1) The Ice Cream (Scotland) Regulations 1948[^f00002] are modified in accordance with Part I of Schedule 1.

(2) The Dairy Products (Hygiene) (Scotland) Regulations 1995[^f00003] are modified in accordance with Part II of Schedule 1.

(3) The Egg Products Regulations 1993[^f00004] are modified in accordance with Part III of Schedule 1.

(4) The Eggs (Marketing Standards) Regulations 1995[^f00005] are modified in accordance with Part IV of Schedule 1.
