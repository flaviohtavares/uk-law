# The Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (Scotland) Regulations 2000, and come into force on 1st April 2000.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.
