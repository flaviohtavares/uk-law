# The Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Regulations relating to imports and exports: consequential provisions

(1) The Imported Food (Scotland) Regulations 1985[^f00025] are modified in accordance with Part I of Schedule 5.

(2) The Products of Animal Origin (Import and Export) Regulations 1996[^f00026] are modified in accordance with the provisions of Part II of Schedule 5.
