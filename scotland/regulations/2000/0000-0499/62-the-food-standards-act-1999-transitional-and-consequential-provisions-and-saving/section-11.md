# The Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Monitoring of enforcement action, etc.: consequential provisions

The definition of “relevant legislation” in section 15(1) of the 1999 Act has effect as if the references in sub-paragraph (a) to the provisions of regulations or orders made under the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00038] Act included references to the provisions of the Regulations made under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00039] specified in Schedule 9 to these Regulations.
