# The Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Regulations relating to feedingstuffs: consequential provisions

(1) The Feeding Stuffs Regulations 1995[^f00006] are modified in accordance with Part I of Schedule 2.

(2) The Feeding Stuffs (Establishment and Intermediaries) Regulations 1999[^f00007] are modified in accordance with Part II of Schedule 2.

(3) Except in relation to zootechnical products (as referred to in section 29(2)(d) of the 1999 Act), the Feeding Stuffs (Enforcement) Regulations 1999[^f00008] are modified in accordance with Part III of Schedule 2.

(4) The Animal Feeding Stuffs from Belgium (Control) (Scotland) Regulations 2000[^f00009] are modified in accordance with Part IV of Schedule 2.
