# The Adoption (Intercountry Aspects) Act 1999 (Commencement No. 4) (Scotland) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Scottish Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.S.I. 2000/223](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2000/223) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Scottish Statutory Instrument

2000 No. 390 (C. 15)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS

The Adoption (Intercountry Aspects) Act 1999 (Commencement No. 4) (Scotland) Order 2000

Made

6th November 2000

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 18(3) of the Adoption (Intercountry Aspects) Act 1999[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation, interpretation and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Adoption (Intercountry Aspects) Act 1999 (Commencement No. 4) (Scotland) Order 2000.

(2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

## 2. Appointed day

10th November 2000 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 13 of the Adoption (Intercountry Aspects) Act 1999 insofar as it inserts a new subsection (3A) after subsection (3) of section 65 of the Adoption (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00002].

JACK McCONNELL

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

6th November 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 10th November 2000 as regards Scotland, that part of section 13 of the Adoption (Intercountry Aspects) Act [1999 (c. 18)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/18), which inserts a new subsection (3A) into section 65 of the Adoption (Scotland) Act 1978. The new subsection (3A) provides that in relation to the proposed adoption of a child resident outside the British Islands, references to arrangements for the adoption of a child include references to arrangements for an assessment for the purpose of indicating whether a person is suitable or not to adopt a child.

This Order replaces the Adoption (Intercountry Aspects) Act 1999 (Commencement No. 2) (Scotland) Order 2000 ([S.S.I. 2000/223](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2000/223)) as that Order was a nullity.
