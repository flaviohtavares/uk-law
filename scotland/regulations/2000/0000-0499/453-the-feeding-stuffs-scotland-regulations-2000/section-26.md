# The Feeding Stuffs (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Revocations

Except insofar as they made modifications to the Act, the Feeding Stuffs Regulations 1995[^f00027], the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1996[^f00028], the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1998[^f00029], the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 1998[^f00030] and the Feeding Stuffs (Amendment) Regulations 1999[^f00031] are hereby revoked.
