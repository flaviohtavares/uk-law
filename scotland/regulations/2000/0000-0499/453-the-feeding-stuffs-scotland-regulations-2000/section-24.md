# The Feeding Stuffs (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Enforcement of provisions made under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972

Insofar as any provision of these Regulations is made under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972, that provision shall be enforced as if it was made under those provisions of Part IV of the Act under which the other provisions of these Regulations are made, and the provisions of that Part of the Act shall apply accordingly.
