# The Feeding Stuffs (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Modification of the Agriculture Act 1970 in relation to imported feeding stuffs

In the application of section 69(1) with respect to the prescribed material, the words “and in either case before it is removed from the premises,” shall be disregarded in relation to feeding stuffs which have been imported.
