# The Feeding Stuffs (Scotland) Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 453

AGRICULTURE

The Feeding Stuffs (Scotland) Regulations 2000

Made

28th December 2000

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

3rd January 2001

Coming into force

31st January 2001

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 66(1), 68(1) and (1A), 69(1), (3), (6) and (7), 70(1), 74(1), 74A, 77(4), 78(6) and 84 of the Agriculture Act 1970[^f00001] and, after consultation as required by section 84(1) of the said Act with the persons or organisations appearing to represent the interests concerned, and in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf hereby make the following Regulations:
