# The Advice and Assistance (Assistance by Way of Representation) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 109

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE

The Advice and Assistance (Assistance by Way of Representation) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2000

Made

7th April 2000

Coming into force

10th April 2000

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 9(1), (2)(a), (dd) and (de) and 37(1) of the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations, a draft of which has, in accordance with section 37(2) of that Act, been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Advice and Assistance (Assistance by Way of Representation) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 10th April 2000.

(2) In these Regulations–

- “the principal Regulations” means the Advice and Assistance (Assistance by Way of Representation) (Scotland) Regulations 1997[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of principal Regulations

In regulation 1(2) of the principal Regulations (interpretation)–

- (a) after the definition of “the Act”, there shall be inserted the following:–

  > - “adjudicator” means an adjudicator appointed by virtue of Schedule 3 to the 1999 Act[^f00003] and includes–
  >   - (a) an adjudicator who acts as a special adjudicator for the purposes of section 8 of the Asylum and Immigration Appeals Act 1993[^f00004]; and
  >   - (b) an adjudicator who acts as a special adjudicator for the purposes of section 3 of the Asylum and Immigration Act 1996[^f00005];

  ;
- (b) after the definition of “the 1995 Act”, there shall be inserted the following:–

  > - “the 1999 Act” means the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999[^f00006];

  ; and
- (c) after the definition of “governor”[^f00007], there shall be inserted the following:–

  > - “Immigration Appeal Tribunal” means the Tribunal continued in force by virtue of section 56(1) of the 1999 Act

  .

**3.** In regulation 3 of the principal Regulations (application of Part II of the Act to assistance by way of representation: miscellaneous proceedings), after paragraph (j) there is inserted the following:–

> ;
>
> - (k) proceedings before an adjudicator or the Immigration Appeal Tribunal.

.

**4.** After regulation 8 of the principal Regulations (petitions for the appointment of an executor), there is inserted the following:–

> **8A. Prescribed proceedings**
>
> Assistance by way of representation shall be available in relation to all proceedings under Part V of the Mental Health (Scotland) Act 1984[^f00008] without reference to the financial limits under section 8 of the Act (availability of advice and assistance),and section 11(2) of the Act (client’s contributions) shall not apply as respects assistance by way of representation received in relation to such proceedings.

.

These Regulations make assistance by way of representation available in relation to proceedings before an adjudicator (including a special adjudicator) or the Immigration Appeal Tribunal (regulation 3). the purposes of section 9(2)(dd) and (de) of the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act [1986 (c. 47)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/47) (“the 1986 Act”) (regulation 4). Subsection 9(2)(dd) and (de) were inserted into the 1986 Act by section 32 of the Access

The Regulations also prescribe proceedings for to Justice Act [1999 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/22). As such, where assistance by way of representation is made available in relation to proceedings under Part V of the Mental Health (Scotland) Act [1984 (c. 36)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/36) no account is taken of the financial eligibility test under section 8 of the 1986 Act or the client contribution test under section 11 of that Act.
