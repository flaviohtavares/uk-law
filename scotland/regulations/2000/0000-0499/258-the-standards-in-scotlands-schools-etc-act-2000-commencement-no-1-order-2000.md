# The Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 258 (C. 8)

EDUCATION

The Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2000

Made

25th July 2000

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 61(2) of the Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2000.

## 2. Appointed day

28th July 2000 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 22 (interruption of process of transition to self-governing status) of the Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000.

SAM GALBRAITH

A member of the Scottish Executive

Victoria Quay,

Edinburgh

25th July 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 28th July 2000 section 22 of the Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000 which relates to schools in the process of transition to self-governing status. Section 22 provides that if a school’s proposals for self-governing status have been approved by the Secretary of State or the Scottish Ministers, but that the school has not yet assumed full self governing status, the school will remain under the local authority’s management. In practice, this provision affects only Fort William Primary School, which is the only school in this transitional state.
