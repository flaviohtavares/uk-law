The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2, 28B(1)(a)(ii) and (3) and 135(1) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Education (School and Placing Information) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on 12th December 2000.

## 3. Transitional provision

For the school year 2000/2001 the information required to be published by and the method of publication required by paragraphs 2(r) and (s) of Schedule 1 to the Education (School and Placing Information) (Scotland) Regulations 1982 shall be as follows:–

- (a) the qualification results for years 1996/1997-1998/1999 shall be included in the handbooks (using the analyses which existed prior to the coming into force of these Regulations); and
- (b) the authority shall ensure that the qualifications results for school year 1999/2000 shall be published and made available free of charge to all parents within 6 weeks of receipt by the school of the required information.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the provisions of the Education (School and Placing Information) (Scotland) Regulations 1982.

Regulation 2 updates the information to be contained in the school handbook in regard to exam results. It takes account of the formation of the Scottish Qualification Authority and the implementation of Higher Still to provide more relevant comparison information to parents and children. Further for the school year 2000/2001 it allows for the school handbooks to be printed without the results of the 2000 exam diet.

Regulation 3 makes transitional provision.
