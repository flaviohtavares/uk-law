# The Environment Act 1995 (Commencement No. 19) (Scotland) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 433 (C. 16)

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION

The Environment Act 1995 (Commencement No. 19) (Scotland) Order 2000

Made

29th November 2000

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 125(3) of the Environment Act 1995[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and extent

(1) This Order shall be cited as the Environment Act 1995 (Commencement No. 19) (Scotland) Order 2000.

(2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 1st January 2001

(1) The provision of the Environment Act 1995 mentioned in paragraph (2) below shall (insofar as not already in force) come into force on 1st January 2001.

(2) Section 120(1) of the Environment Act 1995 insofar as it relates to paragraph 29(26) of Schedule 22.

SAM GALBRAITH

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

29th November 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which extends to Scotland only, brings into force on 1st January 2001 section 120(1) of the Environment Act [1995 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/25) insofar as it relates to paragraph 29(26) of Schedule 22, which inserts sections 49A (enforcement notices as respects discharge consents) and 49B (appeals against enforcement notices) into the Control of Pollution Act [1974 (c. 40)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1974/40).

(This note is not part of the Order)
