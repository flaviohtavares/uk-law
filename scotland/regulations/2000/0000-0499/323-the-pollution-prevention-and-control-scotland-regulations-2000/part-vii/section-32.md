# The Pollution Prevention and Control (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 32

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 32. Admissability of evidence

Where–

- (a) by virtue of a condition of a permit, an entry is required to be made in any record as to the observance of any condition of the permit; and
- (b) the entry has not been made,

that fact shall be admissible as evidence that that condition has not been observed.
