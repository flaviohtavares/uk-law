# The Pollution Prevention and Control (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Enforcement by the Courts

If SEPA is of the opinion that proceedings for an offence under regulation 30(1)(d) would afford an ineffectual remedy against a person who has failed to comply with the requirements of an enforcement notice or a suspension notice, it may take proceedings in any court of competent jurisdiction for the purpose of securing compliance with the notice.
