# The Pollution Prevention and Control (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Requirement for permit to operate installation and mobile plant

(1) No person shall operate an installation or mobile plant after the prescribed date for that installation or mobile plant, except under and to the extent authorised by a permit granted by SEPA.

(2) In paragraph (1), the “prescribed date” means the appropriate date set out in or determined in accordance with Schedule 3.
