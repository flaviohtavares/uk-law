# The Pollution Prevention and Control (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 36. Consequential Amendments

Schedule 10 shall have effect and the enactments mentioned in that Schedule, so far as they extend to Scotland, shall have effect with the amendments there specified (being minor amendments consequential on provisions of these Regulations).
