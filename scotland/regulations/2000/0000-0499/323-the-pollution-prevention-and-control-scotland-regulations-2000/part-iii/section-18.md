# The Pollution Prevention and Control (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Duty of SEPA to ensure compliance with conditions

While a permit is in force it shall be the duty of SEPA to take such action under these Regulations as may be necessary for the purpose of ensuring that the conditions of the permit are complied with.
