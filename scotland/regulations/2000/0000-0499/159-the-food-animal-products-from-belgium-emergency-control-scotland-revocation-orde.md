# The Food (Animal Products from Belgium) (Emergency Control) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 159

FOOD

The Food (Animal Products from Belgium) (Emergency Control) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2000

Made

25th May 2000

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

31st May 2000

Coming into force

21st June 2000

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 6(4), 13(1) and 48(1) of the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, having regard in accordance with section 48(4A) of that Act to relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food (Animal Products from Belgium) (Emergency Control) (Scotland) Revocation Order 2000 and shall come into force on 21st June 2000.

## 2. Revocation

The Food (Animal Products from Belgium) (Emergency Control) (Scotland) Order 2000[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

SUSAN C DEACON

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

25th May 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which applies to Scotland, revokes the Food (Animal Products from Belgium) (Emergency Control) (Scotland) Order 2000 ([S.S.I. 2000/15](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2000/15)), and implements in Scotland, in relation to food, Commission Decision [2000/301/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0301) repealing the protective measures with regard to contamination by dioxins of certain products of porcine and poultry origin intended for human or animal consumption (O.J. No. L97, 19.4.2000, p.16).
