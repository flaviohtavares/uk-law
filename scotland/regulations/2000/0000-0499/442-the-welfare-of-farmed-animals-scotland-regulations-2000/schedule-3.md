# The Welfare of Farmed Animals (Scotland) Regulations 2000, SCHEDULE 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE 3 — ADDITIONAL CONDITIONS UNDER WHICH POULTRY OTHER THAN LAYING HENS KEPT IN BATTERY CAGES MUST BE KEPT

Regulation 5

Where any poultry other than laying hens kept in battery cages are kept in a building, they must be kept on, or have access at all times to, well maintained litter or to a well-drained area for resting.
