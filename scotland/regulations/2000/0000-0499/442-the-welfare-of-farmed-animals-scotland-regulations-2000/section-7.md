# The Welfare of Farmed Animals (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Additional duties on owners and keepers of cattle

Owners and keepers of cattle must ensure that, in addition to the conditions specified in Schedule 1, the conditions under which the animals are kept comply with the requirements set out in Schedule 5.
