# The Welfare of Farmed Animals (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Additional duties on owners and keepers of pigs

Owners and keepers of pigs must ensure that, in addition to the conditions specified in Schedule 1, the conditions under which the animals are kept comply with–

- (a) the general additional requirements set out in Part II of Schedule 6; and
- (b) the specific additional requirements set out in Parts III, IV, V or VI (as the case may be) applicable to the particular category of pig being kept.
