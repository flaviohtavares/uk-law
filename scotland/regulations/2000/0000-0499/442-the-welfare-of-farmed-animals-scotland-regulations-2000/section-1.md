# The Welfare of Farmed Animals (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Welfare of Farmed Animals (Scotland) Regulations 2000 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which they are made.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.
