# The Welfare of Farmed Animals (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Additional duties on owners and keepers of poultry other than laying hens kept in battery cages

Owners and keepers of poultry other than laying hens kept in battery cages must ensure that, in addition to the conditions specified in Schedule 1, the conditions under which the birds are kept comply with the requirements set out in Schedule 3.
