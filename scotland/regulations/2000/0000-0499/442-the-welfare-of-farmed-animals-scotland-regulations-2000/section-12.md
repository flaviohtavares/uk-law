# The Welfare of Farmed Animals (Scotland) Regulations 2000, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Powers of entry

An authorised person exercising powers of entry under the Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1968 for the purposes of these Regulations may take with him a representative of the European Commission acting for the purposes of–

- (a) Council Directive [88/166/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1988/0166)[^f00005] laying down minimum standards for the protection of laying hens kept in battery cages;
- (b) Council Directive [91/629/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0629)[^f00006] laying down minimum standards for the protection of calves as amended by Council Directive [97/2/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1997/0002)[^f00007] and Commission Decision [97/182/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1997/0182)[^f00008];
- (c) Council Directive [91/630/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0630)[^f00009] laying down minimum standards for the protection of pigs; or
- (d) Council Directive [98/58/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0058)[^f00010] concerning the protection of animals kept for farming purposes,

as well as any person he takes under powers provided under section 6(3) of that Act.
