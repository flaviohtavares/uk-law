# The Road Traffic Reduction Act 1997 (Commencement) (Scotland) Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 101 (C. 3)

ROAD TRAFFIC

The Road Traffic Reduction Act 1997 (Commencement) (Scotland) Order 2000

Made

27th March 2000

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 4(3) of the Road Traffic Reduction Act 1997[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and after consultation with such associations of local authorities as appear to them to be concerned in accordance with section 4(4), of that Act hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Road Traffic Reduction Act 1997 (Commencement) (Scotland) Order 2000.

(2) This order shall extend to Scotland only.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of the Road Traffic Reduction Act 1997 is 21st April 2000.

Sarah Boyack

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

27th March 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, in relation to Scotland, the Road Traffic Reduction Act [1997 (c. 54)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/54) (“the Act”).

Article 2 provides that all the provisions of the Act shall come into force on 21st April 2000.
