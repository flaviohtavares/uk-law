# The Yorkhill National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 355

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE

The Yorkhill National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000

Made

28th September 2000

Coming into force

29th September 2000

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 12A(1) and (4) and 105(6) of, and paragraphs 1 and 3(1)(b) of Schedule 7A to, the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Yorkhill National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 2000 and shall come into force on 29th September 2000.

(2) In this Order “the principal Order” means the Yorkhill National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of the principal Order

For article 3 of the principal Order there is substituted–

> **3. Nature and functions of the trust**
>
> (1) The trust is established for the purpose specified in section 12A(1) of the Act.
>
> (2) The trust’s functions shall be to provide goods and services for the purpose of the Health Service at, from or through hospital accommodation and facilities at the Royal Hospital for Sick Children, Yorkhill, the Queen Mother’s Hospital Yorkhill, and at establishments associated with those hospitals, and also including the management of any teaching and research facilities associated with those establishments and the support services related thereto

.

**3.** In article 4 of the principal Order (directors of the trust) for “non-executive directors” there is substituted “trustees”.

SUSAN C DEACON

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

28th September 2000

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Yorkhill National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992 (S.I. [1992/3321](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/3321)) to substitute a new article 3 relating to the nature and functions of the trust and to amend article 4 to substitute a reference to “trustees” for the reference to “non-executive directors”.
