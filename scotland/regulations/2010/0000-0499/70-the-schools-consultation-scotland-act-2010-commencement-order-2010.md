# The Schools (Consultation) (Scotland) Act 2010 (Commencement) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 70 (C. 2)

Education

The Schools (Consultation) (Scotland) Act 2010 (Commencement) Order 2010

Made

24th February 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 22(2) of the Schools (Consultation) (Scotland) Act 2010[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Schools (Consultation) (Scotland) Act 2010 (Commencement) Order 2010.

## 2. Appointed day

The provisions of the Schools (Consultation) (Scotland) Act 2010, in so far as they are not already in force, come into force on 5th April 2010.

MICHAEL RUSSELL

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

24th February 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 5th April 2010 those provisions of the Schools (Consultation) (Scotland) Act 2010 which are not already in force.
