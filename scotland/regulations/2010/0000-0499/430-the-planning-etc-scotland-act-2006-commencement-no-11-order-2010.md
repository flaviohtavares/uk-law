# The Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006 (Commencement No. 11) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 430 (C. 29)

Town And Country Planning

The Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006 (Commencement No. 11) Order 2010

Made

2nd December 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 59(2) of the Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006 (Commencement No. 11) Order 2010.

## 2. Commencement

Section 54(18)(a) (further amendment of the principal Act) of the Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006 comes into force on 16th December 2010.

## 3. Amendment of the Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2010

(1) The Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006 (Commencement No. 10) Order 2010[^f00002] is amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In column 1 of the Schedule for “(18)(a) and (b)(i)” substitute “(18)(b)(i)”.

STEWART STEVENSON

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

2nd December 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 54(18)(a) of the Planning etc. (Scotland) Act 2006 into force on 16th December 2010. Article 2 amends the Planning etc. (Scotland) Act (Commencement No. 10) Order 2010.
