# The National Health Service (Superannuation Scheme, Pension Scheme, Injury Benefits and Additional Voluntary Contributions) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010, section 104

> Text as at 2013-07-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 104. Amendment of the National Health Service (Scotland) (Injury Benefits) Regulations 1998[^c-3e1522bb6d67c66e16fcb9b3fe8c6733]

*Extent: S*

(1) The National Health Service (Scotland) (Injury Benefits) Regulations 1998[^f00010] shall be amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 (interpretation) in the definition of “NHS employment” after “1995” insert “and in the National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2008”.
