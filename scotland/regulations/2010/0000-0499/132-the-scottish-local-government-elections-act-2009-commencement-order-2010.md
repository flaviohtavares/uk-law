# The Scottish Local Government (Elections) Act 2009 (Commencement) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 132 (C. 6)

Representation Of The People

Local Government

The Scottish Local Government (Elections) Act 2009 (Commencement) Order 2010

Made

24th March 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(3) of the Scottish Local Government (Elections) Act 2009[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Local Government (Elections) Act 2009 (Commencement) Order 2010.

## 2. Commencement

The day appointed for the coming into force of the Scottish Local Government (Elections) Act 2009, in so far as it is not already in force, is 30th April 2010.

BRUCE CRAWFORD

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

24th March 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides that the Scottish Local Government (Elections) Act 2009, except section 3, comes into force on 30th April 2010.

Section 3 commenced on Royal Assent, which was signified on 21st July 2009.

This Act will change the date of the Local Government elections in Scotland so that they are no longer held in the same year as elections to the Scottish Parliament. It also gives the Scottish Ministers powers to make secondary legislation about publication of information about votes cast at local government elections.
