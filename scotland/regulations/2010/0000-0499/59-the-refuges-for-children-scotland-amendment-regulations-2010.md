# The Refuges for Children (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 59

Children And Young Persons

The Refuges for Children (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010

Made

19th February 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

22nd February 2010

Coming into force

16th March 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 38(2) of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Refuges for Children (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010 and come into force on 16th March 2010.

## 2. Amendment of the Refuges for Children (Scotland) Regulations 1996

(1) The Refuges for Children (Scotland) Regulations 1996[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 3 (designation of refuges: local authority establishments and foster households) for paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) the household of a person who is approved as a foster carer under the Looked After Children (Scotland) Regulations 2009[^f00003],

.

(3) In regulation 10(2) (withdrawal of designation of approval) for paragraph (c) substitute—

> - (c) where a person approved as a foster carer under the Looked After Children (Scotland) Regulations 2009 has had that approval terminated under those Regulations;

.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make amendments to the Refuges for Children (Scotland) Regulations 1996 consequential upon the coming into force of the Looked After Children (Scotland) Regulations 2009.
