# The Scottish Social Services Council (Appointments, Procedure and Access to the Register) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 379

Social Care

The Scottish Social Services Council (Appointments, Procedure and Access to the Register) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2010

Made

2nd November 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

4th November 2010

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 7 of schedule 2 to the Regulation of Care (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with paragraph 7 of schedule 2 to that Act they have consulted with such persons or groups of persons as they consider appropriate.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Scottish Social Services Council (Appointments, Procedure and Access to the Register) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2010 and come into force on the same day as section 44 (the scheme) of the Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment to the Scottish Social Services Council (Appointments, Procedure and Access to the Register) Regulations 2001

In regulation 4(1) (disqualification for appointment) of the Scottish Social Services Council (Appointments, Procedure and Access to the Register) Regulations 2001[^f00003]—

- (a) omit “and” from the end of sub-paragraph (f);
- (b) after “suspended” in sub-paragraph (g) insert—

  > ; and
  >
  > - (h) any person barred from regulated work with children or adults within the meaning of section 92(1) or (2) of the Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act  2007

  .

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations come into force on the same day as section 44 (which establishes the new vetting and disclosure scheme) of the Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007 (“the 2007 Act”); that day will be appointed by the Scottish Ministers by an order under that Act.

Regulation 2 amends regulation 4(1) of the Scottish Social Services Council (Appointments, Procedure and Access to the Register) Regulations 2001 to provide that any person barred from regulated work with children or adults within the meaning of section 92(1) and (2) of the 2007 Act is disqualified from appointment as convener or member of the Scottish Social Services Council.
