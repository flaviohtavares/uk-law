# The Local Government Finance (Scotland) Amendment Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 154

Local Government

The Local Government Finance (Scotland) Amendment Order 2010

Approved by Scottish Parliament

Made

3rd March 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

3rd March 2010

Coming into force

15th April 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 1(4) of Schedule 12 to the Local Government Finance Act 1992[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with paragraph 2(2) of that Schedule they have consulted with such associations of local authorities as appear to them to be appropriate.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Finance (Scotland) Amendment Order 2010 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is approved by a resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 2. Amendment of the Local Government Finance (Scotland) Order 2010

For Schedule 1 (revenue support grant and distribution of non-domestic rate income 2010-2011) to the Local Government Finance (Scotland) Order 2010[^f00002], substitute the Schedule 1 set out in the Schedule to this Order.

A member of the Scottish Executive

Edinburgh

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Local Government Finance (Scotland) Order 2010 by replacing Schedule 1 to that Order with the Schedule 1 set out in the Schedule to this Order. Only the figures contained in column 2 of the Schedule are affected. Column 2 lists the amount of revenue support grant which is payable to each local authority in Scotland in respect of the financial year 2010-2011.
