# The Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007 (Modification of Enactments) Order 2010

> Text as at 2013-06-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

2010 No. 21

Children And Young Persons

The Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007 (Modification of Enactments) Order 2010

Made

26th January 2010

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 116 of the Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 117(5) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-6327e7765b6a3371d6a58cb234556034]

This Order may be cited as the Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007 (Modification of Enactments) Order 2010 and shall come into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Modifications of enactments[^c-1d50dbd395cfe7c4f3cde31d095532d1]

The Schedule to this Order, which makes modification of certain enactments, has effect.

ADAM INGRAM

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

## SCHEDULE — MODIFICATION OF ENACTMENTS

Article 2

### Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965

**1.**[^c-d7081ce85ad0b985c48eb2b9c891ad87] In subsection (5) of section 39E of the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965[^f00002] (abbreviated extracts) for “section 45(1) of the Adoption Act [1978 (c.28)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/28)” substitute “section 53(1) of the Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act [2007 (asp 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2007/4)”.

### Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968

**2.**[^c-cb98e7bdac8b42584c18058a3ec5bb0e] In subsection (3A) of section 10 of the Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968[^f00003] (financial and other assistance to voluntary organisations etc. for social work) the word “support” is repealed.

### Foster Children (Scotland) Act 1984

**3.**[^c-b081cc57145c3ee98c364c91eac7e415] In subsection (1) of section 7 of the Foster Children (Scotland) Act 1984[^f00004] (persons disqualified from keeping foster children)—

- (a) the word “or” immediately following paragraph (f) is repealed;
- (b) after paragraph (g) add—

  > ; or
  >
  > - (h) all his parental responsibilities and parental rights have been extinguished by a permanence order under section 80 of the Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act [2007 (asp 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2007/4)

  .

### Civil Evidence (Family Mediation) (Scotland) Act 1995

**4.**[^c-9a8f07ed18da2d5a238a15992be41ca4] In sub-paragraph (iii) of section 2(1)(d) of the Civil Evidence (Family Mediation) (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00005] (exceptions to general rule of inadmissibility) for “section 29(1)” substitute “section 28(1)”.

### Children (Scotland) Act 1995

**5.**[^c-0d1bf7fe6553a174125b81455b5b8444] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

### Regulation of Care (Scotland) Act 2001

**6.**[^c-dfe8f8c7b043bf34509787c997995e38] (1) The Regulation of Care (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00007] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 2 (care services) subsection (13) is repealed.

(3) In paragraph (b) of subsection (6) of section 25[^f00008] (inspections)—

- (a) after “(c.18)” insert “or section 3, 8, 13 or 37 of the [Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007 (asp 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2007/4))”; and
- (b) the words “or section 8 or 37 of the [Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007 (asp 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2007/4))” are repealed.

### Local Electoral Administration and Registration Services (Scotland) Act 2006

**7.**[^c-9a7744604d82fba5ff041ea220c1cbd1] In paragraph (k) of subsection (2) of section 58 of the Local Electoral Administration and Registration Services (Scotland) Act 2006[^f00009] (issuing of other material kept or held by the Registrar General), for the words from “or”, where it first occurs, to the end of the paragraph, substitute “maintained under section 53(1) of the [Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007 (asp 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2007/4)) or any register or book maintained in pursuance of section 55(1) of that Act”.

### Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007

**8.**[^c-fcca328adcfe82c085b1639efd734def] (1) The Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007[^f00010] is amended as follows.

(2) In section 10 (provision of services) the word “adoption”, where it occurs in each of subsections (1) and (2)(a), is repealed.

(3) In section 26 (looked after children: adoption not proceeding) subsection (10) is repealed.

(4) In subsection (2) of section 76 (adoption societies which are not registered adoption services) for “that Act” substitute “the 2002 Act”.

(5) In subsection (2) of section 109 (proceedings to be in private)—

- (a) in paragraph (g) the word “and” is repealed; and
- (b) after paragraph (g) insert—

  > - (ga) section 98, and

  .

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes modifications to primary legislation. These modifications are consequential and supplemental to the [Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007 (asp 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2007/4)).

The modifications are set out in the Schedule to the Order, which Schedule has effect in accordance with article 2 of the Order.
