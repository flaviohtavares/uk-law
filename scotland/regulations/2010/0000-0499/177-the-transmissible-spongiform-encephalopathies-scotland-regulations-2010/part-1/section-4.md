# The Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies (Scotland) Regulations 2010, section 4

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 4. Exception for research

*Extent: S*

(1) The provisions of Schedules 2 to 8 do not apply in relation to animals kept for the purposes of research in approved research premises.

(2) If a bovine animal, sheep or goat kept in approved research premises or its progeny dies or is killed, the occupier must dispose of it as a Category 1 animal by-product in accordance with Regulation (EC) No.  1069/2009[^c-6c19284e9c4f43a30b9351b1276819ba] and failure to do so is an offence.
