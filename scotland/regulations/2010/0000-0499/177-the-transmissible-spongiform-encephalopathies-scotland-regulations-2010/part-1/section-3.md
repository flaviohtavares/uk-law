# The Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies (Scotland) Regulations 2010, section 3

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3. Appointment of competent authority

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers are the competent authority for the purposes of the EU TSE Regulation except in relation to paragraphs 1 to 18 of[^key-967837e494e321b56c632ade8677c41d] Schedule 7 and paragraphs 1, 3 and 4 of Schedule 8 where the Food Standards Scotland[^key-34dd3ceee22ca2f2718d1ad03d118305] are the competent authority.
