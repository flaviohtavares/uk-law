# The Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies (Scotland) Regulations 2010, section 22

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 22. Saving provision

*Extent: S*

Notwithstanding the revocation of the Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies (Scotland) Regulations 2006 [^c20301781], regulation 21 (miscellaneous amendments) of and paragraphs 1 and 2 of Schedule 8 to those Regulations shall remain in full force and effect.
