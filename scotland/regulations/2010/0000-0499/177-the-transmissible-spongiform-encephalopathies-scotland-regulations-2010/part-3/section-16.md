# The Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies (Scotland) Regulations 2010, section 16

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 16. Notices restricting movement

*Extent: S*

(1) If a notice is served restricting movement, an inspector may subsequently permit movement under the authority of a licence.

(2) The person transporting under the authority of a licence must carry the licence during movement and produce it on demand to an inspector.

(3) Failure to comply with paragraph (2) is an offence.
