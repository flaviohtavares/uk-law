# The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (Scotland) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 428

Registration Of Births, Deaths, Marriages, Etc.

The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (Scotland) Order 2010

Made

1st December 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

2nd December 2010

Coming into force

1st January 2011

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5(1) and (2) of, and paragraph 1(b) of Schedule 3 to, the Public Expenditure and Receipts Act 1968[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (Scotland) Order 2010 and comes into force on 1st January 2011.

(2) In this Order a reference to a numbered column is to the column in the Schedule to this Order bearing that number.

## 2. Fees

The enactments in column 1 are amended by increasing the sum specified by that enactment as the amount or maximum amount payable in respect of the matter described in column 2 from the sum shown in column 3 to the sum shown in column 4.

## 3. Revocation

The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (Scotland) Order 2009[^f00002] is revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends fees payable, under certain provisions of the Savings Bank Act 1887, the Factories Act 1961, the Education (Scotland) Act 1980 and the Social Security Administration Act 1992 for certificates of birth, death or marriage for the purposes of those Acts (article 2). The new fees are set out in the Schedule.

The Order also revokes the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Fees) (Scotland) Order 2009 (article 3).
