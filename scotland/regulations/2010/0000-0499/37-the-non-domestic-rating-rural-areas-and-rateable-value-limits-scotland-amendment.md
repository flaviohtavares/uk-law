# The Non-Domestic Rating (Rural Areas and Rateable Value Limits) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 37

Rating And Valuation

The Non-Domestic Rating (Rural Areas and Rateable Value Limits) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2010

Made

9th February 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

10th February 2010

Coming into force

1st April 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5 and paragraphs 3(2)(b) and 4(2)(b) of Schedule 2 to the Local Government and Rating Act 1997[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Non‑Domestic Rating (Rural Areas and Rateable Value Limits) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2010 and shall come into force on 1st April 2010.

## 2. Amendment of the Non-Domestic Rating (Rural Areas and Rateable Value Limits) (Scotland) Order 2005

(1) The Non‑Domestic Rating (Rural Areas and Rateable Value Limits) (Scotland) Order 2005[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 3(1) (rateable value limits for mandatory relief)—

- (a) In subparagraph (a) for “£7,000” substitute “£8,500”;
- (b) In subparagraph (b) for “£10,500” substitute “£12,750”; and

(3) In article 3(2) (rateable value limits for discretionary rate relief) for “£14,000” substitute “£17,000”.

JOHN SWINNEY

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

9th February 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Non‑Domestic Rating (Rural Areas and Rateable Value Limits) (Scotland) Order 2005 (“the 2005 Order”) to increase the rateable value limits prescribed by Ministers in article 3 of that Order and comes into force on 1st April 2010.
