# The Guar Gum (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No.153  

Agriculture

Food

The Guar Gum (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2010

Made

15th April 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

16th April 2010

Coming into force

17th April 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Guar Gum (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2010 and come into force on 17th April 2010.

## 2. Revocation

The Guar Gum (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) (Scotland) Regulations 2008[^f00002] are revoked.

SHONA ROBISON

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

15th April 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Guar Gum (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) (Scotland) Regulations 2008, which implemented in Scotland Commission Decision [2008/352/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2008/0352). That Decision was repealed with effect from 15th April 2010 by Commission Regulation (EU) 258/2010 (O.J. No. L 80, 26.3.2010, p.28).
