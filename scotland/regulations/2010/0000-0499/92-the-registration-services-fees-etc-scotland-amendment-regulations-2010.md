# The Registration Services (Fees, etc.) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 92

Registration Of Births, Deaths, Marriages,etc.

The Registration Services (Fees, etc.) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010

Made

4th March 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

5th March 2010

Coming into force

1st April 2010

The Registrar General makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 3(1) and 19(2) of the Marriage (Scotland) Act 1977[^f00001] and sections 88(2) and 95(4) of the Civil Partnership Act 2004[^f00002] and all other powers enabling him to do so.

The Scottish Ministers[^f00003] have approved the making of these Regulations in accordance with section 25(1) of the 1977 Act and section 126(3) of the 2004 Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Registration Services (Fees, etc.) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010 and come into force on 1st April 2010.

## 2. Amendments to the Registration Services (Fees, etc.) (Scotland) Regulations 2006

(1) Schedule 3 to the Registration Services (Fees, etc.) (Scotland) Regulations 2006[^f00004] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) In Part I (fees payable under the Marriage (Scotland) Act 1977), in column 3—

- (a) for “£28.00” substitute “£30.00”; and
- (b) for £50.00” substitute “£55.00”.

(3) In Part II (fees payable under the Civil Partnership Act 2004 – registration). in column 3—

- (a) for “£28.00” substitute “£30.00”; and
- (b) for “£50.00” substitute “£55.00”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend fees payable under the Registration Services (Fees, etc.) (Scotland) Regulations 2006 in connection with services provided under the Marriage (Scotland) Act 1977 and the Civil Partnership Act 2004.

They increase the fees payable for submission of a marriage notice (regulation 2(2)(a)) and for a civil marriage ceremony (regulation 2(2)(b)). They also increase the fees for submission of a notice of a proposed civil partnership (regulation 2(3)(a) and for civil partnership registration (regulation 2(3)(b)).
