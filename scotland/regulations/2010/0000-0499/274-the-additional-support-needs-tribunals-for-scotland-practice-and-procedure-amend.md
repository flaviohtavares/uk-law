# The Additional Support Needs Tribunals for Scotland (Practice and Procedure) Amendment (No. 2) Rules 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 274

Education

The Additional Support Needs Tribunals for Scotland (Practice and Procedure) Amendment (No. 2) Rules 2010

Made

1st July 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

2nd July 2010

Coming into force

15th August 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 17(4) of, and paragraph 11 of Schedule 1 to, the Education (Additional Support for Learning) (Scotland) Act 2004[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Additional Support Needs Tribunals for Scotland (Practice and Procedure) Amendment (No. 2) Rules 2010 and come into force on 15th August 2010.

## 2. Amendment of the Additional Support Needs Tribunals for Scotland (Practice and Procedure) Amendment Rules 2010

In rule 1(1) (citation, commencement and interpretation) of the Additional Support Needs Tribunals for Scotland (Practice and Procedure) Amendment Rules 2010[^f00002] for “16th August” substitute “14th November”.

ADAM INGRAM

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

1st July 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Rules)

These Rules change the date on which the Additional Support Needs Tribunals for Scotland (Practice and Procedure) Amendment Rules 2010 come into force from 16th August 2010 to 14th November 2010.
