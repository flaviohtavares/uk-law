# The Climate Change (International Aviation and Shipping) (Scotland) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

27th May 2010

Coming into force

28th May 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 16(1) to (3) of the Climate Change (Scotland) Act 2009[^f00001].

In accordance with section 96(4) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

Before this draft was so laid, the Scottish Ministers requested the advice of the relevant body[^f00002] in accordance with section 16(6) of that Act.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Climate Change (International Aviation and Shipping) (Scotland) Order 2010 and comes into force on the day after the day it is made.

(2) In this Order—

- “radiative force factor” is the multiplier which reflects the direct and indirect non-carbon dioxide climate change impacts of emissions at altitude from international aviation; and
- “UK National Inventory Report” means the UK National Inventory Report to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change[^f00003].

## 2. Emissions from international aviation attributable to Scotland

For the purposes of determining Scottish emissions of a greenhouse gas for—

- (a) the year that is the baseline year for that gas; and
- (b) every other year in the period starting on 1st January 2010 and ending on 31st December 2050,

emissions from international aviation of that gas are to be taken into account as calculated in accordance with Schedule 1.

## 3. Radiative force factor

The radiative force factor for each greenhouse gas is 1.

## 4. Emissions from international shipping attributable to Scotland

For the purposes of determining Scottish emissions of a greenhouse gas for—

- (a) the year that is the baseline year for that gas; and
- (b) every other year in the period starting on 1st January 2010 and ending on 31st December 2050,

emissions from international shipping of that gas are to be taken into account as calculated in accordance with Schedule 2.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provision regarding the emissions of greenhouse gases from international aviation and shipping that are to be attributable to Scotland.

The Scottish Ministers requested advice from the relevant body (the UK Committee on Climate Change (UKCCC)) as required by section 16(6) of the Climate Change (Scotland) Act 2009 (“the 2009 Act”). The UKCCC advice is set out in “Scotland’s path to a low carbon economy” dated February 2010, and available at http://www.theccc.org.uk/reports/scottish-report.

Section 10(1) of the 2009 Act defines “greenhouse gases”.

Articles 2 and 4 provide for a share of the annual United Kingdom emissions of each greenhouse gas from international aviation and shipping to be attributed to Scotland in the manner set out in Schedules 1 and 2.

The emissions so attributed will be included in net Scottish emissions for the purposes of the emissions targets in sections 1 (2050 target), 2 (interim target) and 3 (annual targets) of the 2009 Act.

The emissions attributed to international aviation are subject to a radiative force factor as required by section 16(3) of the 2009 Act. Article 3 provides that the radiative force factor for each greenhouse gas is 1.

A Regulatory Impact Assessment has not been produced in relation to this Order as it has no direct impact on businesses, charities or the voluntary sector.
