# The National Health Service (Discipline Committees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010

> Text as at 2013-08-19.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

2010 No. 226

National Health Service

The National Health Service (Discipline Committees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010

Made

9th June 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

10th June 2010

Coming into force

2nd July 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 17P, 25(2), 26(2), 27(2), 105(7), 106(a) and 108(1) of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and section 17 of the Health and Medicines Act 1988[^f00002] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with paragraph 24(1) of Schedule 7 to the Tribunals Courts and Enforcement Act 2007[^f00003], they have consulted the Administrative Justice and Tribunals Council and its Scottish Committee.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^key-0cb4887ed1c8c6f7b29a24747b4d11e7]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (Discipline Committees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010 and come into force on 2nd July 2010.

(2) In these Regulations, “the principal Regulations” means the National Health Service (Discipline Committees) (Scotland) Regulations 2006[^f00004].

## 2. Amendment of the principal Regulations[^key-7d5362fcce423caa3a309a44f89692ee]

(1) The principal Regulations are amended in accordance with the following paragraphs.

(2) In regulation 2(1) (interpretation)—

- (a) insert after the definition of “complaint”—

  > - “dental body corporate” means a body corporate entitled by virtue of section 43 of the Dentists Act 1984[^f00005] to carry on the business of dentistry;

  ;
- (b) insert after the definition of “doctor’s panel”—

  > - “him or her” includes “it” when referring to a practitioner who is a dental body corporate;
  > - “his or her” includes “its” when referring to a practitioner who is a dental body corporate;

  ;
- (c) for the definition of “General Dental Services Regulations” substitute ““General Dental Services Regulations” means, in respect of the period to 1st July 2010, the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1996[^f00006] and, in respect of the period from 2nd July 2010, the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2010[^f00007];”;

  - (i) for “Schedules 1 and 2” substitute “Schedule 1”;

- (f) in the definition of “treatment” after “dentist” insert “or dental body corporate”.

(4) In regulation 4(6)(a)(ii) (provisions relating to the start of disciplinary proceedings) after “dentist” insert “or a dental body corporate”.

(5) In regulation 5(3)(b) (referral to discipline committee) after “dentist” insert “or a dental body corporate”.

(6) In regulation 6(7)(c) (time limits)—

- (a) after “dentist” where it first occurs insert “or dental body corporate”;
- (b) omit “by the dentist” in both places, where it appears.

(7) In regulation 10(7)(b) (procedure on appeal to Scottish Ministers) after “dentist” insert “or a dental body corporate”.

(8) In regulation 11 (recovery of amounts from practitioners following appeal) after “dentist” in each place where it appears insert “or dental body corporate”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the National Health Service (Discipline Committees) (Scotland) Regulations 2006 (“the principal Regulations”) which make provision relating to the NHS discipline committees.

These Regulations are consequential upon the coming into force of certain amendments made to the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 (“the 1978 Act”) by the Smoking, Health and Social Care (Scotland) Act 2005 (“the 2005 Act”) as regards dental bodies corporate. They also contain an amendment to the principal Regulations correcting a cross reference to “practitioner” which erroneously included a member of the joint discipline committee for the purposes of that definition in Schedule 2.

Regulation 2(2)(a) inserts a new definition of “dental body corporate”. The definition of “dental body corporate” is by reference to section 43 of the Dentists Act 1984 as amended by the Dentists Act 1984 (Amendment) Order 2005 ([S.I. 2005/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/2011)).

Regulation 2(2)(b) inserts new definitions for the phrases “him or her” and “his or her” which modify those phrases to include “it” and “its” respectively when they refer to a dental body corporate.

Regulation 2(2)(c) amends the definition of “General Dental Services Regulations” to take account of the revocation of the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1996 ([S.I. 1996/177](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/177)) and their replacement by the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2010 ([S.S.I. 2010/208](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2010/208)), (the “GDS regulations 2010”).

Regulation 2(2)(c) amends the definition of “practitioner” by inserting “dental body corporate”.

Regulation 2(2)(d) limits the exception of the application of the definition of “practitioner” to Schedule 1 and consequently applies that definition to Schedule 2. This, together with the amendment in Regulation 2(11)(d)(i) corrects a cross reference to practitioner in Schedule 2 which erroneously included a member of the joint discipline committee for the purposes of that Schedule.

Regulation 2(2)(e) amends the definition of “terms of service” by inserting “or dental body corporate”.

Regulation 2(2)(f) amends the definition of “treatment” by inserting “or dental body corporate”.

Regulation 2(3) amends regulation 2(2)(a) of the principal Regulations to take account of the revocation of the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 1996 and their replacement by the GDS Regulations 2010 on 2nd July 2010.

Regulations 2(4), (5), (6), (7), (8), (10) and (11)(d)(ii) amend regulations 4(6)(a), 5(3)(b), 6(7)(c), 10(7)(b) and 11, Schedule 1, paragraph 2(4)(b) and Schedule 2, paragraph 9(b)(ii) respectively of the principal Regulations by inserting references to “dental body corporate”.

Regulation 2(9) amends regulation 16 of the principal Regulations by substituting reference to the Administrative Justice and Tribunals Council for reference to the Council on Tribunals following the replacement of the latter by the former in terms of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007.

Regulation 2(11)(a) and (b) amends paragraph 2 of Schedule 2 of the principal Regulations in consequence of the GDS Regulations 2010.

Regulation 2(11)(c) amends paragraph 5(2)(a) of Schedule 2 to provide for circumstances where a dental body corporate may be required to attend at discipline committee hearing.
