# The Food Enzymes (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Scottish Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.S.I. 2009/435](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2009/435) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that instrument.

2010 No. 26

Food

The Food Enzymes (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010

Made

3rd February 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

4th February 2010

Coming into force

12th February 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 16(1)(a) and (e), and 48(1) of the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001], and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 48(4A) of that Act, they have had regard to relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency[^f00002].

There has been consultation as required by Article 9 of Regulation (EC) No. 178/2002 of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00003].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Food Enzymes (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2010 and come into force on 12th February 2010.

## 2. Amendment of the Food Enzymes (Scotland) Regulations 2009

(1) The Food Enzymes (Scotland) Regulations 2009[^f00004] are amended as follows.

(2) For regulation 8 substitute—

> **8. Amendment of the Caseins and Caseinates (Scotland) Regulations 1986**
>
> In the Schedule to the Caseins and Caseinates (Scotland) Regulations 1986[^f00005], in column 2 of Part II (technological adjuvants and bacterial cultures) after “rennet” and “other milk-coagulating enzymes” in each case add “meeting the requirements of Regulation [(EC) No. 1332/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/1332)[^f00006] of the European Parliament and of the Council on food enzymes”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations correct an error in the Food Enzymes (Scotland) Regulations 2009. They amend the Caseins and Caseinates (Scotland) Regulations 1986 and delete from the 2009 Regulations the provision which purported to amend the Caseins and Caseinates Regulations 1985 ([S.I. 1985/2026](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1985/2026)). Those 1985 Regulations do not in fact have any application in Scotland.

No Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
