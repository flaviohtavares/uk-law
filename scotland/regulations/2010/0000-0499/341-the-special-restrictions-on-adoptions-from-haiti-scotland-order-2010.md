# The Special Restrictions on Adoptions from Haiti (Scotland) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 341

Children And Young Persons

The Special Restrictions on Adoptions from Haiti (Scotland) Order 2010

Made

23rd September 2010

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

27th September 2010

Coming into force

15th October 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 62(3) of the Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Special Restrictions on Adoptions from Haiti (Scotland) Order 2010 and comes into force on 15th October 2010.

## 2. Declaration of special restrictions

Special restrictions[^f00002] apply in relation to the bringing of children into the United Kingdom from the Republic of Haiti in the cases mentioned in section 62(2) of the Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order applies the special restrictions which are set out in section 64(1) of the Adoption and Children (Scotland) Act 2007 (“the Act”) in relation to the adoption of children from Haiti. As a result the Scottish Ministers will not be permitted to take any steps to further the bringing of children into the United Kingdom from Haiti for the purposes of adoption, unless they are satisfied that they should do so under section 64(2) of the Act.
