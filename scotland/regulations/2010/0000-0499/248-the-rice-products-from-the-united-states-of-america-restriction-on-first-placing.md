# The Rice Products from the United States of America (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

17th June 2010

Coming into force

18th June 2010

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Rice Products from the United States of America (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2010 and come into force on 18th June 2010.

## 2. Revocation

The Rice Products from the United States of America (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) (Scotland) Regulations 2008[^f00002] are revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Rice Products from the United States of America (Restriction on First Placing on the Market) (Scotland) Regulations 2008 ([S.S.I. 2008/87](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2008/87)).

Those Regulations implemented Commission Decision [2006/601/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0601) on emergency measures regarding the non-authorised genetically modified organism “LL RICE 601” in rice products (O.J. No. L 244, 7.9.2006, p.27), as last amended by Commission Decision [2008/162/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2008/0162). Commission Decision [2006/601/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0601), as amended, was repealed by Commission Decision 2010/315/EU (O.J. No. L 141, 9.6.2010, p.10).

A full business and regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
