# The National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2010, section 32

> Text as at 2026-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 32. Signatures[^c-cff3f214b4a7468ec4d701fab80b24ed]

*Extent: S*

(1) Any[^c-c0b68de8d37b4f75d240ed7c7a20ad62] signature required by these Regulations (including the terms of service) shall be in handwriting and not by means of a stamp and the name and position of the signatory shall be clearly stated.

(2) Any signature required by these Regulations shall be the signature of the relevant dentist or, in the case of a body corporate, a director.
