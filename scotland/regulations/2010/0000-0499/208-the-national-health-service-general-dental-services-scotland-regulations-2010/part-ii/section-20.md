## 20. Arrangements for emergency cover[^c-d53a83c5cbef166b7f58b0e599d1c811]

*Extent: S*

It shall be the responsibility of a Health Board to make any arrangements that may be necessary to enable a salaried dentist whose name is included in sub-part A of the first part of its dental list to comply with the dentist’s obligations under paragraph 6 of Schedule 1.
