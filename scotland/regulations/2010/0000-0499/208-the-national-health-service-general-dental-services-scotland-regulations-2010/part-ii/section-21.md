## 21. Health Board patient information leaflets[^c-24c6c9e5b224730ab74b6ab565c05e63]

*Extent: S*

A Health Board shall compile and make available a patient information leaflet which shall include the information specified in Schedule 6 and details of the provision of general dental services by salaried dentists in the Health Board’s area.
