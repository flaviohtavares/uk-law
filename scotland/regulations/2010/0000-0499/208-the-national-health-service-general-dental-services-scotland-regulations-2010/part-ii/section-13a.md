# The National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2010, section 13A

> Text as at 2026-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 13A. Re-instatement of designation[^c-04088ad19ec8ae7ac577291c6dcf2920]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a dentist has had their designation as a domiciliary care dentist removed under regulation 12A, and the occurrence that led to that removal is overturned, either on appeal, or for any other reason, the Health Board may designate that dentist as a domiciliary care dentist without a request being made in accordance with paragraph 1 of schedule B1 if the Health Board is satisfied that there is no further information which it should consider, and provided that the Health Board receives undertakings from that dentist to comply with these Regulations.

(2) Where the Health Board considers that further information is required, a dentist who wishes to be designated again as a domiciliary care dentist must provide that further information and undertakings or, at the option of the Health Board, submit a full request in accordance with paragraph 1 of schedule B1.
