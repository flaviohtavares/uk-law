## 16. Information about assistants[^c-0653b40d1acfe3ef1cc905307ce39e8c]

*Extent: S*

A Health Board shall send to the SDPB any information it receives under paragraph 44 of Schedule 1 (deputies and assistants) within 7 days of the date of its receipt of the information.
