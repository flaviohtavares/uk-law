# The Number of Inner House Judges (Variation) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 449

Court Of Session

The Number of Inner House Judges (Variation) Order 2010

Made

15th December 2010

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2A) and (2B) of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 2(2A) of that Act, they have consulted the Lord President.

In accordance with section 2(2D) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Number of Inner House Judges (Variation) Order 2010 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Alteration of the number of senior judges in the First Division of the Inner House

In section 2(2) (composition of court) of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00002] for “four” substitute “five”.

KENNY MACASKILL

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

15th December 2010

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends section 2(2) of the Court of Session Act 1988 to increase the number of senior judges in the First Division of the Inner House of the Court of Session from four to five. The total number of judges in the Inner House, including the Lord President and the Lord Justice Clerk, will therefore increase from eleven to twelve.
