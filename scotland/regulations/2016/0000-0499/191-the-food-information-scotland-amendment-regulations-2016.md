# The Food Information (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 191

Food

The Food Information (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2016

Made

2nd June 2016

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

3rd June 2016

Coming into force

20th July 2016

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 6(4), 16(1), 17, 18, 26, and 48(1) of, and paragraph 1 of schedule 1 of, the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001], section 2(2) of, and paragraph 1A of schedule 2 of, the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

These Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972, and it appears to the Scottish Ministers that it is expedient for any reference to specified provisions of—

- (a) Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011[^f00003] of the European Parliament and of the Council on the provision of food information to consumers, amending Regulations [(EC) No 1924/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1924) and [(EC) No 1925/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1925) of the European Parliament and of the Council, and repealing Commission [Directive 87/250/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1987/0250), Council [Directive 90/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1990/0496), Commission [Directive 1999/10/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0010), [Directive 2000/13/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0013) of the European Parliament and of the Council, Commission Directives [2002/67/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0067) and [2008/5/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0005) and [Commission Regulation (EC) No 608/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0608); and
- (b) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 828/2014 on the requirements for the provision of information to consumers on the absence or reduced presence of gluten in food[^f00004],

to be construed as a reference to those provisions of those Regulation as amended from time to time.

So far as the following Regulations are made in exercise of powers under the Food Safety Act 1990, the Scottish Ministers have had regard to relevant advice given by Food Standards Scotland in accordance with section 48(4A)[^f00005] and (6) of that Act.

There has been consultation, as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety, during the preparation and evaluation of the following Regulations[^f00006].

## 1. Citation commencement interpretation and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Food Information (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2016.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 20th July 2016.

(3) In these Regulations, “the 2014 Regulations” means the Food Information (Scotland) Regulations 2014[^f00007].

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations make provision to enforce in Scotland the requirements of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 828/2014 on the requirements for the provision of information to consumers on the absence or reduced presence of gluten in food (“Regulation 828/2014”).

Regulation 2 makes amendments to the Food Information (Scotland) Regulations 2014 to ensure that Articles 3(1) and 4 of Regulation 828/2014 can be enforced. In particular, they have the effect of making it an offence to fail to comply with those Articles and they ensure that powers of entry are available under section 32 of the Food Safety Act 1990 for the purposes of enforcing those Articles.

Regulation 3 revokes the Foodstuffs Suitable for People Intolerant to Gluten (Scotland) Regulations 2010.

A full business and regulatory impact assessment of the effect these Regulations will have on the costs of business, the voluntary sector and the public sector has been prepared and placed in the Scottish Parliament Information Centre. Copies may be obtained from Food Standards Scotland, Pilgrim House, Old Ford Road, Aberdeen, AB11 5RL.
