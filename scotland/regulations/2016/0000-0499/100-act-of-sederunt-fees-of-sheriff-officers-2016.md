# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 100

Sheriff Court

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 2016

Made

12th February 2016

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

16th February 2016

Coming into force

1st April 2016

In accordance with section 4 of the Scottish Civil Justice Council and Criminal Legal Assistance Act 2013[^f00001], the Court of Session has approved draft rules submitted to it by the Scottish Civil Justice Council.

The Court of Session therefore makes this Act of Sederunt under the powers conferred by section 106(1) of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00002] and all other powers enabling it to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement, etc.

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 2016.

(2) It comes into force on 1st April 2016.

(3) A certified copy is to be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of fees

For the Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) (No. 2) 2002[^f00003] substitute the Table of Fees in the Schedule.

## 3. Savings

Paragraph 2 does not affect the fees payable as respects any work carried out before 1st April 2016.

Lord President

Edinburgh

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt substitutes a new Table of Fees for the Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) (No. 2) 2002.

The fee levels in the new Table of Fees represent an increase of 1.3% on the existing fees. This increase takes effect from 1st April 2016.
