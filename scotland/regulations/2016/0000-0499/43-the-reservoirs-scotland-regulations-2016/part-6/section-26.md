# The Reservoirs (Scotland) Regulations 2016, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Notice of relevant works

A notice of proposed relevant works by a reservoir manager under section 33(1) (notice to SEPA and appointment of construction engineer) of the Act must contain the information specified in Schedule 5.
