# The Reservoirs (Scotland) Regulations 2016, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Applications for review of decisions to appoint or remove civil engineers from panels etc.

(1) An application for a review of a decision under section 30(1) of the Act must be made in writing to the Scottish Ministers.

(2) The application must contain the information specified in Schedule 4.
