# The Reservoirs (Scotland) Regulations 2016, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. Notice of appointment of supervising engineer

A notice by a reservoir manager under section 49(1) (appointment of supervising engineers etc.) of the Act must—

- (a) include the content specified in the form in Schedule 14; and
- (b) be in the form set out in Schedule 14.
