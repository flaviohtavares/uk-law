> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 36. Statement by supervising engineer

A statement by a supervising engineer under section 50(8) (supervising engineer and monitoring of reservoir) of the Act must be in the form set out in Schedule 15.
