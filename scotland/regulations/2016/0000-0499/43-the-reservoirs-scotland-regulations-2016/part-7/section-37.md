# The Reservoirs (Scotland) Regulations 2016, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Recording of water levels etc. and record keeping

(1) The record of matters (“the recorded matters”) to be maintained under section 51(1) (recording of water levels etc. and record keeping) of the Act must, in addition to the matters required by subsection (1)(a) to (d) of that section, include the other matters specified in Schedule 16.

(2) The information specified in the form (as read with the Notes to the form) in Schedule 17 is to be included in relation to the recorded matters.

(3) The record to be maintained under section 51(1) of the Act must be in the form set out in Schedule 17.
