# The Reservoirs (Scotland) Regulations 2016, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Annual subsistence fee: general

(1) Subject to regulation 16, SEPA may charge the reservoir manager of each controlled reservoir which has been given a risk designation under the Act an annual fee in relation to the performance by SEPA of its functions under Part 1 of the Act.

(2) The reservoir manager must pay the annual subsistence fee to SEPA.

(3) The annual subsistence fee is recoverable as a civil debt.
