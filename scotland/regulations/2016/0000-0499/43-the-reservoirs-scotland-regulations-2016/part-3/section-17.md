# The Reservoirs (Scotland) Regulations 2016, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Transfer fees: general

(1) Subject to regulation 18, SEPA may charge the reservoir manager of a controlled reservoir a fee in relation to a notice given to SEPA by that manager under section 15(2) of the Act.

(2) The reservoir manager must pay the new manager fee to SEPA.

(3) The new manager fee is recoverable as a civil debt.
