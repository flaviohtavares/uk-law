# The Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This S.S.I. has been made in consequence of drafting errors in [S.S.I. 2016/290](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2016/290) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that instrument.

2016 No. 317

Legal Aid And Advice

The Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2016

Made

17th October 2016

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

18th October 2016

Coming into force

28th November 2016

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 33(2)(a) and (3) of the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2016 and come into force on 28th November 2016.

## 2. Amendment of the Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Amendment Regulations 2016

(1) The Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Amendment Regulations 2016[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(2)(c), in inserted sub-paragraph (ba), for “72(3)” substitute “72(9)”.

(3) In the schedule, in the table of fees—

- (a) in paragraph 2, in each of sub-paragraphs (a) and (b), for “6” substitute “7”; and
- (b) in paragraph 5(b), after “sheets” insert “(or part thereof)”.
