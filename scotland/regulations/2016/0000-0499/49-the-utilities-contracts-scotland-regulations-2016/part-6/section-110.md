> Text as at 2026-03-24.

## 110. Revocations and consequential amendments[^key-f1367863d1793d6efc18857212928688]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to regulation 111 (transitional provision and saving where procurement commenced before 18th April 2016), the 2012 Regulations are revoked.

(2) The consequential and miscellaneous amendments set out in Schedule 3 (consequential and miscellaneous amendments to subordinate legislation) have effect.
