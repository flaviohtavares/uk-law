# The Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2016, section 70

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 70. Publication otherwise than on the UK e-notification service[^key-6a93f74a4f088f303913103f26386a83][^key-5e28ba8145cb02108747f2da65a80589]

*Extent: S*

(1) In addition to the publication of the notices referred to in regulations 50 and[^key-ed12c58624c9d43c9724c7c3a826b91a] 65 to 68 on the UK e-notification service[^key-febdb3a1a90f85b3180d455c9dcf8987], a utility may publish the information contained in such notices on the internet on a buyer profile.

(2) A buyer profile may also include—

- (a) periodic indicative notices which are published on it pursuant to regulation 65(3)(b) (periodic indicative notices);
- (b) information on on-going invitations to tender, scheduled purchases, contracts concluded, procedures cancelled; and
- (c) any useful general information, such as a contact point, telephone and fax number, postal address and e-mail address.

(3) The notices referred to in regulations 50 and[^key-790dcbd9f3f79efb12260425ac383571] 65 to 68, and the information contained in them, must not be published otherwise than on the UK e-notification service before they are published on that service.[^key-b94131737e4934a4dd7c514c29608aea]

(4) Notwithstanding paragraph (3), publication may take place otherwise than on the UK e-notification service[^key-41e979183e56a4e150c31d47d639f54e] where a utility has not been notified of the publication on that service within 48 hours after the notice was submitted to that service[^key-9e717c5dc00464019b10514472fe270e].

(5) A notice published otherwise than on the UK e-notification service[^key-97dd1dd0d2e7458713d57f374ebbd3ce] must not contain information other than that contained in the notice submitted to that service[^key-15eea23992b08129e4e9cbe5ea7c56f1] or published on a buyer profile, but must indicate the date of submission of the notice to that service[^key-09f18cd8b2afe694529ebce66d3906db] or its publication on the buyer profile.

(6) Where a periodic indicative notice is to be published on a buyer profile—

- (a) the periodic indicative notice may not be so published before the utility submits to the UK e-notification service[^key-a64262109fad01ac044c4bb6f0ee01e0] the notice referred to in regulation 65(4) (periodic indicative notices); and
- (b) the periodic indicative notice must indicate the date of that submission[^key-247bdca63d47b61213507b3e67657d9f].
