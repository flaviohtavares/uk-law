# The Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2016, section 41A

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 41A. Conditions relating to the GPA[^c-7635130eed893598419d7e0a68e76924]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a utility within the meaning of regulation 4(1)(a) (utilities) is covered by Annex 3 to the United Kingdom’s Appendix 1 to the GPA in respect of a procurement that is covered by Annexes 4 to 7 to that Appendix, the utility must accord to the works, supplies, services and economic operators of any GPA party treatment no less favourable than the treatment accorded to the works, supplies, services and economic operators of the United Kingdom.
