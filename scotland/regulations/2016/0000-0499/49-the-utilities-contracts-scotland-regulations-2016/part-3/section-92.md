# The Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2016, section 92

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 92. Notices[^c-4c7a608b1d5ceb979b0665b0784ff571][^c-1b1d9e17a6874a671389d4beda09643a][^c-1b1d9e17a6874a671389d4beda09643a]

*Extent: S*

(1) A utility that intends to carry out a design contest must call for competition by means of a contest notice which includes the information set out in Annex XIX to the Utilities Contracts Directive , but as if, in paragraph 15, “date of dispatch” were a reference to the date on which the notice is submitted to the UK e-notification service for the purposes of these Regulations[^c-11d3c1f3f248cd343ae015c396a03373].

(2) Where a utility intends to award a subsequent service contract under regulation 48(4)(f) (use of the negotiated procedure without prior call for competition) this must be indicated in the contest notice.

(3) A utility that has held a design contest must—

- (a) make the results known by means of a notice which must include the information set out in Annex XX to the Utilities Contracts Directive , but as if, in paragraph 11, “date of dispatch” were a reference to the date on which the notice is submitted to the UK e-notification service for the purposes of these Regulations[^c-fc6b5e796b35fd6018ae15f53590bcf4]; and
- (b) submit[^c-5ae45465074f24f647bc227ecc82646f] such notice to the UK e-notification service[^c-34bffacc0c55c9cbbb0b458a3e725c6a] within 30 days of the closure of the design contest.

(4) A utility may withhold from publication information on the result of the contest where the release of the information—

- (a) would impede law enforcement or otherwise be contrary to the public interest;
- (b) would prejudice the commercial interests of any person; or
- (c) might prejudice fair competition between economic operators.

(5) The utility must submit[^c-9a04ad1a40d7b9829da8a152d47c8329] the notices referred to in this regulation for publication in accordance with regulation 69 (publication on the UK e-notification service[^c-57b457bd5586fc46a0d392019c57d8bb]).
