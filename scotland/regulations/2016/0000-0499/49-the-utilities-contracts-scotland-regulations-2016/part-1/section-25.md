# The Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2016, section 25

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 25. Procurement covering several activities and involving defence or security aspects[^c-c7dd8e1e138ca9f97658143e58621b55]

*Extent: S*

(1) This regulation applies where a procurement has as its subject a contract covering more than one activity, where one or more of those activities are covered by regulation 3(2)[^c-6826b573a184ee36817083be8d410ad5] or are the supply of anything within section 7(1) of the Procurement Act 2023[^c-daa6eb69cee8f87c85a310a5dc920ee0].

(2) A utility may decide to undertake procurement for the award of—

- (a) separate contracts for the separate activities; or
- (b) a single contract.

(3) Any decision to undertake a procurement for the award of a single contract must not be made for the purpose of excluding the procurement from the application of these Regulations or the Procurement Act 2023[^c-5989f8c7ef56ef70668b73fb916a543a].

(4) Where a utility decides to undertake procurement for the award of separate contracts for separate activities the applicable law for the procurement of each separate activity will be determined by the characteristics of such activity.

(5) Where a utility decides, in accordance with paragraph (6), to undertake a procurement for the award of a single contract and—

- (a) part of the contract is intended to cover an activity which is covered by regulation 3(2)[^c-6c0efa0501c1bce65802f33097a02983], the contract may be awarded without applying these Regulations; or
- (b) part of the contract would, if it were awarded as a separate contract, be considered a defence and security contract, the procurement may be undertaken in accordance with the Procurement Act 2023[^c-06817ae08a19ad34a17bad3f1aa0bdeb], in which event these Regulations do not apply to such procurement.

(6) A utility may only decide to undertake a procurement for the award of a single contract of a kind referred to in paragraph (5) where such decision is justified by objective reasons.

(7) Paragraph (5)(b) is without prejudice to the thresholds and exclusions provided for by the Procurement Act 2023[^c-38b883ffbfaf22ba1d26b8fceca92816].

(8) Paragraph (5)(a) applies to a mixed contract to which both paragraph (5)(a) and (5)(b) could otherwise apply.
