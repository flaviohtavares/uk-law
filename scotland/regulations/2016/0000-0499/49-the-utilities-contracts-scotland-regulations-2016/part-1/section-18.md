# The Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2016, section 18

> Text as at 2026-03-24.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 18. Exclusion: Contracts and design contests awarded or organised for purposes other than the pursuit of a covered activity or for the pursuit of such an activity outside the United Kingdom[^key-f57e2bbf7de34d644740e11b6b39756d][^key-76d5e003821693a75581614cc5b2e509]

*Extent: S*

(1) These Regulations do not apply to procurement for the award of a contract or the organisation of a design contest by a utility—

- (a) for purposes other than the pursuit of its activities as described in regulations 8 to 14; or
- (b) for the pursuit of such activities outside the United Kingdom[^key-56a0cbb9e72cd04c68394376fd7b0604], in conditions not involving the physical use of a network or geographical area within the United Kingdom[^key-9671277d1e77c4a46eacb41c436f53ef].
