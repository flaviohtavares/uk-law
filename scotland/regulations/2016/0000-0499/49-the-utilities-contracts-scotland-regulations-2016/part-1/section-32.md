# The Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2016, section 32

> Extent: Scotland.

## 32. Activities directly exposed to competition[^c-2258d0c550bfd31b7a505eb790d6f605]

*Extent: S*

(1) These Regulations do not apply to procurement for the award of a contract or a design contest intended to enable an activity mentioned in regulations 8 to 14 to be carried out where that activity is directly exposed to competition on markets to which access is not restricted.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1), the activity is directly exposed to competition on markets to which access is not restricted only if—

- (a) the activity is covered by the following Commission decisions—
  - (i) Commission [Decision 2006/211/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0211) establishing that Article 30(1) of [Directive 2004/17/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0017) of the European Parliament and of the Council coordinating the procurement procedures of entities operating in the water, energy, transport and postal services sectors applies to electricity generation in England, Scotland and Wales (notified under document number C(2006) 690)[^f00033];
  - (ii) Commission [Decision 2007/141/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2007/0141) establishing that Article 30(1) of [Directive 2004/17/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0017) of the European Parliament and of the Council coordinating the procurement procedures of entities operating in the water, energy, transport and postal services sectors applies to the supply of electricity and gas in England, Scotland and Wales (notified under document number C(2007) 559)[^f00034]; and
  - (iii) Commission [Decision 2010/192/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2010/0192) exempting exploration for and exploitation of oil and gas in England, Scotland and Wales from the application of [Directive 2004/17/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0017) of the European Parliament and of the Council coordinating the procurement procedures of entities operating in the water, energy, transport and postal services sectors (notified under document C(2010) 1920)[^f00035]; [^c-e6b9e77c9eea30365a3109f5e8596ff2]...
- (b)[^c-e6b9e77c9eea30365a3109f5e8596ff2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The activity referred to in paragraph (1) may form a part of a larger sector or be exercised only in certain parts of the United Kingdom.
