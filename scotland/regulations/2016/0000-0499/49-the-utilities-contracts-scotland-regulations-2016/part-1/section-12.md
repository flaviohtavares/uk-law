## 12. Ports and airports[^key-9dfa8235ac978eff71770d5cf431ce35]

*Extent: S*

These Regulations apply to activities relating to the exploitation of a geographical area for the purpose of the provision of any of the following—

- (a) airports;
- (b) maritime ports;
- (c) inland ports;
- (d) other terminal facilities to carriers by air, sea or inland waterway.
