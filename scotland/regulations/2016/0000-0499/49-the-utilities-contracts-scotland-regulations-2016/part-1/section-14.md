## 14. Extraction of oil and gas and exploration for, or extraction of, coal or other solid fuels[^key-83af28d7a39a2932cebb18dc1048a975]

*Extent: S*

These Regulations apply to activities relating to the exploitation of a geographical area for the purpose of—

- (a) extracting oil or gas;
- (b) extracting coal or other solid fuels;
- (c) exploring for coal or other solid fuels.
