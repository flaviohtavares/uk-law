# The Utilities Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2016, section 17

> Extent: Scotland.

## 17. Exclusion: Contracts awarded for the purpose of resale or lease to third parties[^c-ed54434fd948b4e1a5da64627b6f5c0a]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (3), these Regulations do not apply to procurement for the award of a contract for the purpose of resale or lease to third parties, provided that the utility enjoys no special or exclusive right to sell or lease the subject of such contracts and other entities are free to sell or lease the subject of such contracts under the same conditions as the utility.

(2)[^c-7e598cafb89637764077d8bddd0d333f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Paragraph (1) does not apply to procurement carried out by a central purchasing body in order to perform a centralised purchasing activity.
