# The Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 (Exclusions and Exceptions) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2016, section 4

> Text as at 2016-02-08.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 4. Substitution of Schedule A1 to the 2013 Order[^key-d8a9e594ebde4fa497f222522a8ad406]

*Extent: S*

For Schedule A1 to the 2013 Order (offences which must always be disclosed) substitute—

> **SCHEDULE A1 — OFFENCES WHICH MUST ALWAYS BE DISCLOSED**
>
> Article 2A
>
> **Common law offences**
>
> **1.** Abduction.
>
> **2.** Abortion.
>
> **3.** Assault to the danger of life.
>
> **4.** Assault to severe injury.
>
> **5.** Assault with intent to rape or ravish.
>
> **6.** Assault with intent to commit the statutory offence of rape.
>
> **7.** Bestiality.
>
> **8.** Cruel and unnatural treatment of persons.
>
> **9.** Culpable homicide.
>
> **10.** Drugging.
>
> **11.** Extortion.
>
> **12.** Hamesucken.
>
> **13.** Hijacking.
>
> **14.** Piracy.
>
> **15.** Plagium.
>
> **16.** Reset of plagium.
>
> **17.** Treason.
>
> **18.** Uttering threats.
>
> **Statutory offences**
>
> **19. Armed forces**
>
> An offence under section 42 of the Armed Forces Act 2006[^f00004] (criminal conduct) where the corresponding offence under the law of England and Wales is, or corresponds to, an offence listed in this Schedule.
>
> **20. Aviation and maritime**
>
> An offence under the Piracy Act 1837[^f00005].
>
> **21.** An offence under any of the following provisions of the Aviation Security Act 1982[^f00006]—
>
> - (a) section 1 (hijacking);
> - (b) section 2 (destroying, damaging or endangering safety of aircraft);
> - (c) section 3 (other acts endangering or likely to endanger safety of aircraft); and
> - (d) section 4 (offences in relation to certain dangerous articles).
>
> **22.** An offence under section 9 of the Aviation and Maritime Security Act 1990[^f00007] (hijacking of ships).
>
> **23.** An offence under article 241 of the Air Navigation Order 2009[^f00008] in respect of a contravention of article 137 of that Order (endangering safety of aircraft).
>
> **24. Children**
>
> An offence under section 12 of the Children and Young Persons (Scotland) Act 1937[^f00009] (cruelty to persons under 16).
>
> **25. Explosives**
>
> An offence under the Explosive Substances Act 1883[^f00010].
>
> **26. Firearms and other weapons**
>
> An offence under any of the following provisions of the Firearms Act 1968[^f00011]—
>
> - (a) section 4 (conversion of weapons)[^f00012];
> - (b) section 5 (weapons subject to a general prohibition)[^f00013];
> - (c) section 16 (possession of firearm with intent to injure);
> - (d) section 16A[^f00014] (possession of firearm with intent to cause fear or violence);
> - (e) section 17[^f00015] (use of firearm to resist arrest);
> - (f) section 18 (carrying firearm with criminal intent);
> - (g) section 19[^f00016] (carrying firearm in a public place);
> - (h) section 20[^f00017] (trespassing with firearm);
> - (i) section 21[^f00018] (possession of firearm by persons previously convicted of crime);
> - (j) section 24[^f00019] (supplying firearms to minors);
> - (k) section 25 (supplying firearm to person drunk or insane);
> - (l) section 28A(7)[^f00020] (certificates: supplementary);
> - (m) section 29[^f00021] (variation of firearm certificates);
> - (n) section 30D(3) (revocation of certificates: supplementary)[^f00022];
> - (o) section 39[^f00023] (offences in connection with registration);
> - (p) section 40[^f00024] (compulsory register of transactions in firearms);
> - (q) section 46(5) (power of search with warrant);
> - (r) section 47 (powers of constables to stop and search); and
> - (s) section 48 (production of certificates).
>
> **27.** An offence under the Chemical Weapons Act 1996[^f00025].
>
> **28. Forced marriage**
>
> An offence under section 122 of the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 (offence of forced marriage: Scotland)[^f00026].
>
> **29. Human trafficking and exploitation**
>
> An offence under section 22 of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00027] (traffic in prostitution etc.).
>
> **30.** An offence under section 4 of the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc.) Act 2004[^f00028] (trafficking people for exploitation).
>
> **31.** An offence under section 47 of the Criminal Justice and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2010[^f00029] (slavery, servitude and forced or compulsory labour).
>
> **32.** An offence under the Prohibition of Female Genital Mutilation (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00030].
>
> **33. Medical professions etc.**
>
> An offence under section 10Z9 of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00031] (offences in relation to registration).
>
> **34.** An offence under any of the following provisions of the Medical Act 1983[^f00032]—
>
> - (a) section 49 (penalty for pretending to be registered)[^f00033]; and
> - (b) section 49A (penalty for pretending to hold a licence to practise)[^f00034] .
>
> **35.** An offence under article 38 (offences relating to the Register) of the Pharmacy Order 2010[^f00035].
>
> **36. Medicines**
>
> An offence under any of the following provisions of the Medicines Act 1968[^f00036]—
>
> - (a) section 67(1A) and (1B) (offences under Part III)[^f00037]; and
> - (b) section 78 (restrictions on use of titles, descriptions and emblems)[^f00038].
>
> **37. Official Secrets**
>
> An offence under the Official Secrets Act 1911[^f00039].
>
> **38. Prostitution**
>
> An offence under any of the following provisions of the Criminal Law (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00040]—
>
> - (a) section 7[^f00041] (procuring);
> - (b) section 11[^f00042] (trading in prostitution and brothel-keeping); and
> - (c) section 12 (allowing child to be in brothel).
>
> **39. Road traffic**
>
> An offence under any of the following provisions of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00043]—
>
> - (a) section 1[^f00044] (causing death by dangerous driving);
> - (b) section 3ZC[^f00045] (causing death by driving: disqualified drivers); and
> - (c) section 3A[^f00046] (causing death by careless driving when under influence of drink or drugs).
>
> **40. Serious organised crime**
>
> An offence under any of the following provisions of the Criminal Justice and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2010[^f00047]—
>
> - (a) section 28 (involvement in serious organised crime);
> - (b) section 30 (directing serious organised crime); and
> - (c) section 31 (failure to report serious organised crime).
>
> **41. Sexual offences**
>
> An offence under section 50(3) of the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979[^f00048] (penalty for improper importation of goods) in relation to goods prohibited to be imported under section 42 of the Customs Consolidation Act 1876[^f00049], but only where the prohibited goods include indecent photographs of persons.
>
> **42.** A sexual offence within the meaning given by section 210A(10) of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00050] other than an offence mentioned in paragraph (xxvii)(ZF) or (ZG) of that section (engaging while an older child in sexual conduct with or towards another older child).
>
> **43.** An offence under section 113 of the Sexual Offences Act 2003[^f00051] (breach of sexual offences prevention order or interim sexual offences prevention order, etc.).
>
> **44.** An offence under section 7 of the Protection of Children and Prevention of Sexual Offences (Scotland) Act 2005 (offence: breach of RSHO or interim RSHO etc.)[^f00052].
>
> **45. Stalking and harassment**
>
> An offence under section 50A of the Criminal Law (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00053] (racially aggravated harassment).
>
> **46.** An offence under any of the following provisions of the Criminal Justice and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2010[^f00054]—
>
> - (a) section 38 (threatening or abusive behaviour); and
> - (b) section 39 (offence of stalking).
>
> **47. Terrorism**
>
> An offence under any of the following provisions of the Terrorism Act 2000[^f00055]—
>
> - (a) section 11 (membership);
> - (b) section 12 (support);
> - (c) section 15 (fund-raising);
> - (d) section 16 (use and possession);
> - (e) section 17 (funding arrangements);
> - (f) section 17A(2) or (4) (insurance payments made in response to terrorist demands)[^f00056];
> - (g) section 18 (money laundering);
> - (h) section 19[^f00057] (disclosure of information: duty);
> - (i) section 21A[^f00058] (failure to disclose: regulated sector);
> - (j) section 21D[^f00059] (tipping off: regulated sector);
> - (k) section 38B[^f00060] (information about acts of terrorism);
> - (l) section 39[^f00061] (disclosure of information, &c.);
> - (m) section 54 (weapons training)[^f00062];
> - (n) section 56 (directing terrorist organisation);
> - (o) section 57 (possession for terrorist purposes)[^f00063];
> - (p) section 58 (collection of information)[^f00064];
> - (q) section 58A (eliciting, publishing or communicating)[^f00065]; and
> - (r) section 61 (inciting terrorism overseas).
>
> **48.** An offence under any of the following provisions of the Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001[^f00066]—
>
> - (a) section 47 (use etc. of nuclear weapons);
> - (b) section 50 (assisting or inducing certain weapons-related acts overseas);
> - (c) section 52 (powers of entry);
> - (d) section 54 (offences);
> - (e) section 67 (offences);
> - (f) section 79 (prohibition of disclosures relating to nuclear security)[^f00067];
> - (g) section 80 (prohibition of disclosures of uranium enrichment technology)[^f00068];
> - (h) section 113 (use of noxious substances or things to cause harm and intimidate)[^f00069];
> - (i) section 114 (hoaxes involving noxious substances or things); and
> - (j) paragraph 7 of Schedule 3 (offences).
>
> **49.** An offence under the Terrorism Act 2006[^f00070].
>
> **50.** An offence under any of the following provisions of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008[^f00071]—
>
> - (a) section 2 (offence of obstruction);
> - (b) section 54 (offences relating to notification);
> - (c) paragraph 15 of Schedule 5 (breach of foreign travel restriction order and offence);
> - (d) paragraph 30 of Schedule 7 (offences: failure to comply with requirement imposed by direction);
> - (e) paragraph 30A of Schedule 7[^f00072] (offences: relevant person circumventing requirements); and
> - (f) paragraph 31 of Schedule 7 (offences in connection with licences).
>
> **51. Violent offender orders**
>
> An offence under section 113(1) of the Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008[^f00073] (breach of violent offender order or interim violent offender order).
>
> **52. Vulnerable persons**
>
> An offence under section 83 of the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000[^f00074] (offence of ill-treatment and wilful neglect).
>
> **53.** An offence under section 315 of the Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00075] (ill-treatment and wilful neglect of mentally disordered person).
>
> **54.** An offence under any of the following provisions of the Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007[^f00076]—
>
> - (a) section 34 (barred individuals not to do regulated work);
> - (b) section 35 (organisations not to use barred individuals for regulated work); and
> - (c) section 36 (personnel suppliers not to supply barred individuals for regulated work).
>
> **55.** Any offence where the conduct in respect of which the person was convicted also constituted a breach of a banning order granted under section 19 of the Adult Support and Protection (Scotland) Act 2007[^f00077] (banning orders).
>
> **56. Witness protection**
>
> An offence under any of the following provisions of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00078]—
>
> - (a) section 86 (offence of disclosing information about protection arrangements); and
> - (b) section 88 (offences of disclosing information relating to persons assuming new identity).
>
> **57. Statutory aggravations**
>
> An offence in relation to which either of the following provisions applies—
>
> - (a) section 29(1) of the Criminal Justice and Licensing (Scotland) Act 2010[^f00079] (offences aggravated by connection with serious organised crime); or
> - (b) section 31 of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (offences aggravated by terrorism)[^f00080].
>
> **Other**
>
> **58. Common law aggravations**
>
> An offence the conviction for which indicates that it was committed against a child.
>
> **59.** An offence the conviction for which indicates that it included a sexual element.
>
> **60. Inchoate offences**
>
> An offence committed by aiding, abetting, counselling, procuring or inciting the commission of the offence of murder or any offence listed in paragraphs 1 to 59 of this Schedule.
>
> **61.** An offence committed by attempting or conspiring to commit the offence of murder or any offence listed in paragraphs 1 to 59 of this Schedule.
>
> **62. Superseded offences**
>
> An offence superseded (whether directly or indirectly) by any offence listed in paragraphs 1 to 61 of this Schedule (and any qualification in relation to a listed offence applies to the superseded offence as it applies to the listed offence).
>
> **63. Combined offences**
>
> An offence which was charged, and the conviction for which was received, in conjunction with any offence listed in paragraphs 1 to 62 of this Schedule.
>
> **64. Corresponding offences elsewhere in the UK or abroad**
>
> An offence under the law of England and Wales or Northern Ireland, or any country or territory outside the United Kingdom, which corresponds to any offence listed in paragraphs 1 to 63 of this Schedule.
