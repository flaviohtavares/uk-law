# The Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (Commencement No. 11) Partial Revocation Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 233 (C. 20)

Children And Young Persons

The Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (Commencement No. 11) Partial Revocation Order 2016

Made

24th August 2016

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

24th August 2016

Coming into force

30th August 2016

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 102(3) of the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (Commencement No. 11) Partial Revocation Order 2016 and comes into force on 30th August 2016.

## 2. Amendment of the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (Commencement No. 11) Order 2016

Article 2(a)(i) and (iii) of the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (Commencement No. 11) Order 2016[^f00002] is revoked.

JOHN SWINNEY

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

24th August 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 (Commencement No. 11) Order 2016 (“Commencement No. 11 Order”) by revoking article 2(a)(i) and (iii) of that Order.

Commencement No. 11 Order now only provides for the commencement on 31st August 2016 of section 68 of the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014, so far as not already in force, and section 95 of that Act, for all purposes.
