# The Named Persons (Training, Qualifications, Experience and Position) and the Child’s Plan (Scotland) Revocation Order 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2016 No. 234

Children And Young Persons

The Named Persons (Training, Qualifications, Experience and Position) and the Child’s Plan (Scotland) Revocation Order 2016

Made

24th August 2016

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

24th August 2016

Coming into force

30th August 2016

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 19(3)(b), 34(4), 35(8), 35(9), 39(6), 99(1)(a) and 99(1)(b) of the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Named Persons (Training, Qualifications, Experience and Position) and the Child’s Plan (Scotland) Revocation Order 2016 and comes into force on 30th August 2016.

## 2. Revocations

The following Orders are revoked—

- (a) the Named Persons (Training, Qualifications, Experience and Position) (Scotland) Order 2016[^f00002]; and
- (b) the Child’s Plan (Scotland) Order 2016[^f00003].

JOHN SWINNEY

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

24th August 2016

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Named Persons (Training, Qualifications, Experience and Position) (Scotland) Order 2016 and the Child’s Plan (Scotland) Order 2016.
