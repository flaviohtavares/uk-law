## 5. Descriptions of building and work, including the provision of services, fittings and equipment, not requiring a warrant

*Extent: S*

For the purposes of section 8(8) of the Act, any work which consists solely of a building or conversion, including the provision of services, fittings or equipment, of a kind specified in Schedule 3, shall meet the standards required by regulations 8 to 12 but shall not, subject to the exceptions and conditions, require a building[^key-575f85de89fa209c7c1a5dd654649e45] warrant.
