## 9. Building standards – construction[^c-8bc3e33a18f97fe0b0489b5401f76088]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), construction must be carried out so that the work complies with the applicable requirements of schedule 5 [^M_F_0dd445e4-0f8c-4a17-813a-24e8529bb7a9].

(2) Only the requirements of the standards set out in paragraphs 1.1, 3.17, 3.18, 3.19, 3.20, 3.21, 3.22 and 4.4 of schedule 5 apply in the case of the construction of a building of the type described in paragraph 23A of schedule 3.
