# The Advice and Assistance (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2004 Amendment Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 305

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE

The Advice and Assistance (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2004 Amendment Regulations 2004

Made

25th June 2004

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

25th June 2004

Coming into force

27th June 2004

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 33(2)(b) and (3) of the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Advice and Assistance (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2004 Amendment Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 27th June 2004.

## 2. Amendment of the Advice and Assistance (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2004

In the Advice and Assistance (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2004[^f00002] for regulation 2 substitute–

> - These Regulations shall apply only in relation to fees for work done or outlays incurred on or after 28th June 2004.

.

COLIN D BOYD

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

25th June 2004

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 2 of the Advice and Assistance (Scotland) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2004. In respect of existing cases where advice and assistance was granted prior to 28th June 2004 the fee increases will apply to work done or outlays incurred on or after that date. In respect of new cases where advice and assistance is granted on or after 28th June 2004 the new fees will also apply.
