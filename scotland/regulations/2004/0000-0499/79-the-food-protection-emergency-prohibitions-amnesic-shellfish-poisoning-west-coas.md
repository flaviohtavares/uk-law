# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 3) (Scotland) Order 2003 Revocation Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 79

PUBLIC HEALTH

CONTAMINATION OF FOOD

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 3) (Scotland) Order 2003 Revocation Order 2004

Made

27th February 2004

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

1st March 2004

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Scottish Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1(1) and (2) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 3) (Scotland) Order 2003 Revocation Order 2004 and shall come into force at 1500 hours on 27th February 2004.

## 2. Revocation

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 3) (Scotland) Order 2003[^f00002] is hereby revoked.

IAN GORDON

A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

27th February 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 3) (Scotland) Order 2003 (“the principal Order”) which prohibited–

- (a) fishing for or taking scallops within the areas designated in the Schedule to the principal Order (article 4 of the principal Order);
- (b) movement of such scallops out of those areas (article 5 of the principal Order); and
- (c) landing, using in the preparation or processing for supply of food of, supplying and other specified activities in relation to, such scallops from the designated areas (article 6 of the principal Order).

The effect of this Order is to remove the prohibitions in respect of the areas designated in the Schedule to the principal Order.
