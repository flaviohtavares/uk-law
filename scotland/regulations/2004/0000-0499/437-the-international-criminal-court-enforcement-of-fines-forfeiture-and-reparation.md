# The International Criminal Court (Enforcement of Fines, Forfeiture and Reparation Orders) (Scotland) (Revocation) Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Scottish Statutory Instrument revokes [S.S.I. 2004/360](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2004/360) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that instrument.

2004 No. 437

INTERNATIONAL CRIMINAL COURT

The International Criminal Court (Enforcement of Fines, Forfeiture and Reparation Orders) (Scotland) (Revocation) Regulations 2004

Made

6th October 2004

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

7th October 2004

Coming into force

9th October 2004

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 26 of the International Criminal Court (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the International Criminal Court (Enforcement of Fines, Forfeiture and Reparation Orders) (Scotland) (Revocation) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 9th October 2004.

## 2. Revocation

The International Criminal Court (Enforcement of Fines, Forfeiture and Reparation Orders) (Scotland) Regulations 2004[^f00002] are hereby revoked.

CATHY JAMIESON

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

6th October 2004

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the International Criminal Court (Enforcement of Fines, Forfeiture and Reparation Orders) (Scotland) Regulations 2004 ([S.S.I. 2004/360](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2004/360)) which came into force on 1st October 2004. Those Regulations are being revoked in order to allow further consideration to be given to issues raised by the Subordinate Legislation and Justice 1 Committees.
