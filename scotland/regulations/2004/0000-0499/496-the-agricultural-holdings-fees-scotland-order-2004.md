# The Agricultural Holdings (Fees) (Scotland) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 496

LANDLORD AND TENANT

The Agricultural Holdings (Fees) (Scotland) Order 2004

Made

18th November 2004

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

22nd November 2004

Coming into force

15th December 2004

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 25(7) of the Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

The Order may be cited as the Agricultural Holdings (Fees) (Scotland) Order 2004 and shall come into force on 15th December 2004.

## 2. Fees for registration and extracts

The fees which may be charged by the Keeper[^f00002] for the registration of tenants' interests in acquiring land, and the provision of extracts and copy extracts of registration relating to the Register of Community Interests in Land[^f00003] for the purposes of the Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 2003 are specified in the Schedule to this Order.

A member of the Scottish Executive

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order sets out the fees which are chargeable by the Keeper of the Register of Community Interests in Land for the provision of services relating to the registration of tenants' interests in buying land under Part 2 of the [Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 11)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2003/11).

The fee for the registration of a tenant’s interest in land is £40. A subsequent registration of a registered interest (in terms of section 25(14) of that Act) will incur a fee of £25. Extracts and copy extracts will be charged according to the number of pages, the number and type of any maps and whether authentication is required. A handling fee of £6 may be charged for subsequent extracts in accordance with similar services provided by the Keeper of the Registers of Scotland.
