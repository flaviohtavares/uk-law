# The Oil and Fibre Plant Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2004, section 25B

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 25B. Transitional provision for official labels on IP completion day[^c-ff965adcaf0b8f0bd2715da680276774]

*Extent: S*

A label pre-printed before IP completion day which at the date on which it was printed was an official label for the purposes of these Regulations is to be treated as an official label for a package of Basic Seed, Seed of a Certified Generation, Commercial Seed, seed of a Conservation Variety or a varietal association of seed, for the purposes of any use of that label before the end of the period of twelve months beginning with the day after the day on which IP completion day falls.
