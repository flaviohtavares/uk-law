# The Oil and Fibre Plant Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2004, section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^c-d67167d06726f8f9299e3e9306ece2dc]

*Extent: S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Oil and Fibre Plant Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2004, and shall come into force on 26th September 2004.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.
