# The Oil and Fibre Plant Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2004, section 13

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 13. Requirement for homogeneity[^c-a29c9b80826ae937c6d6388a2d27672b]

*Extent: S*

No person shall market seed in accordance with regulation 6(1)(b)(i) or (ii), 11 or 12 unless it is marketed in a homogeneous seed lot or in part of a homogeneous seed lot.
