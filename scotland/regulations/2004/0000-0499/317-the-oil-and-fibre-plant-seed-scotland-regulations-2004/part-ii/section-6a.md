# The Oil and Fibre Plant Seed (Scotland) Regulations 2004, section 6A

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 6A. Importation from a country granted equivalence[^c-02d2e7644d8006cb9057284cbb1b89df][^c-9da3b65db273a0a7b87926648910ec67]

*Extent: S*

Seed imported from a country granted equivalence[^c-ce36a860da75c3095136f5a4bb28ee27] must be labelled with a label approved by the Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development for the varietal certification on the control of seed moving in international trade.
