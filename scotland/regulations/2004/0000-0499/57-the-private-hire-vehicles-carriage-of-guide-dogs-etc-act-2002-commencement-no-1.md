# The Private Hire Vehicles (Carriage of Guide Dogs etc.) Act 2002 (Commencement No. 1) (Scotland) Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 57 (C.5)

LICENCES AND LICENSING

The Private Hire Vehicles (Carriage of Guide Dogs etc.) Act 2002 (Commencement No. 1) (Scotland) Order 2004

Made

12th February 2004

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(2) of the Private Hire Vehicles (Carriage of Guide Dogs etc.) Act 2002[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Private Hire Vehicles (Carriage of Guide Dogs etc.) Act 2002 (Commencement No. 1) (Scotland) Order 2004.

## 2. Commencement of section 2 of the Private Hire Vehicles (Carriage of Guide Dogs etc.) Act 2002

Section 2 of the Private Hire Vehicles (Carriage of Guide Dogs etc.) Act 2002 shall come into force on 31st March 2004.

TAVISH SCOTT

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

12th February 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force section 2 of the Private Hire Vehicles (Carriage of Guide Dogs etc.) Act 2002. The Act received Royal Assent on 7th November 2002. Section 6 came into force on Royal Assent.

(This note is not part of the Order)

The following provisions of the Private Hire Vehicles (Carriage of Guide Dogs etc.) Act 2002 have been brought into force by commencement order made before the date of this Order.

| Provision | Date of Commencement | Extent | S.I. No. |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Section 1(1) partially | 31st December 2003 | England and Wales | [2003/3123 (C. 123)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/3123) |
| Section 1(1) (for the purposes for which it was not already in force) | 31st March 2004 | England and Wales | [2003/3123 (C. 123)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/3123) |

The remaining provisions to be commenced do not extend to Scotland.
