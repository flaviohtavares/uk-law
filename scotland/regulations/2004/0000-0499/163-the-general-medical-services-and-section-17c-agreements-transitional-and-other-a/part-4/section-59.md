# The General Medical Services and Section 17C Agreements (Transitional and other Ancillary Provisions) (Scotland) Order 2004, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Dispute resolution

Any dispute relating to a pilot scheme that has been referred to the Scottish Ministers under section 17A of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00053] (NHS contracts) and which has not been determined on or before 31st March 2004 shall continue to be dealt with in accordance with the provisions of section 17A.

## Annotations

[^f00053]: Footnote: Section 17A was inserted by the National Health Service and Community Care Act [1990 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/19), section 30 and amended by the Health Authorities Act [1995 (c. 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/17), Schedule 1, paragraph 102(2), the National Health Service (Primary Care) Act [1997 (c. 46)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/46), Schedule 2, paragraph 36, the Health Act [1999 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/8), Schedule 4, paragraph 46 and [S.I. 1991/195](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1991/195).
