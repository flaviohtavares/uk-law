# The General Medical Services and Section 17C Agreements (Transitional and other Ancillary Provisions) (Scotland) Order 2004, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. Existing pilot schemes

Any pilot scheme under which personal medical services were being provided on 31st March 2004 shall, on the coming into force of paragraph 2 of the Schedule to the 2004 Act, be deemed to have been made under section 17C of the 1978 Act[^f00045] and accordingly shall not cease to have effect merely because of the repeals in paragraph 2 of the Schedule to the 2004 Act.

## Annotations

[^f00045]: Footnote: Section 17C was inserted by the 1997 Act sections 21(2) and 41(3) and is amended by section 2 of the 2004 Act.
