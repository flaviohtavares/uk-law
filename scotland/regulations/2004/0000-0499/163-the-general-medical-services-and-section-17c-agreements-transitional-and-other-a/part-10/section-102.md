# The General Medical Services and Section 17C Agreements (Transitional and other Ancillary Provisions) (Scotland) Order 2004, section 102

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 102. Revocation of the Choice Regulations

The National Health Service (Choice of Medical Practitioner) (Scotland) Regulations 1998[^f00099], the National Health Service (Choice of Medical Practitioner) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2000[^f00100] and the National Health Service (Choice of Medical Practitioner) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2001[^f00101] are hereby revoked.
