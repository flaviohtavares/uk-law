# The General Medical Services and Section 17C Agreements (Transitional and other Ancillary Provisions) (Scotland) Order 2004, section 63

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 63. Terms of general medical services contracts and default contracts

Unless the contract is of a type or nature to which a particular article does not apply–

- (a) a general medical services contract shall include or be deemed to include terms which have the effect specified in articles 64 to 74; and
- (b) a default contract shall include or be deemed to include terms which have the effect specified in articles 64 to 67 and 69, 70 and 74.
