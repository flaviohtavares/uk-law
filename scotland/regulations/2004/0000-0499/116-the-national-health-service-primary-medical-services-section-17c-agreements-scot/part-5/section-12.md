# The National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2004, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Additional services and content of agreements

An agreement which includes the provision of additional services must, in addition to any other terms required by these Regulation–

- (a) in relation to all such services included in the agreement, contain a provision which has the same effect as that specified in paragraph 1 of Schedule 3;
- (b) in relation to each such service included in the agreement, contain provisions which have the same effect as those specified in Schedule 3, which are relevant to that service.
