# The National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2004, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Provision of primary medical services

(1) Whether or not the agreement includes arrangements for the provision of services which are not primary medical services, an agreement must include arrangements for the provision of one or more of the following primary medical services:–

- (a) essential services;
- (b) additional services;
- (c) enhanced services;
- (d) out of hours services, in respect of, subject to regulation 26 and paragraph 3 of Schedule 4, part or all of the out of hours period and some or all services.
