# The National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2004, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Out of hours services and content of agreements

An agreement which includes the provision of out of hours services by virtue of this regulation or regulation 26 must, in addition to any other terms required by these Regulations, unless it is of a type or nature to which a particular provision does not apply, contain terms with the same effect as those specified in Schedule 4.
