# The National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2004, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. NHS contracts

If the provider is to be treated as a health service body the agreement must state that it is an NHS contract.
