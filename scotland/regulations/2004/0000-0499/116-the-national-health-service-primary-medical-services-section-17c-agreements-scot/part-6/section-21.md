# The National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2004, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Finance

An agreement must contain a term to the effect that where, pursuant to directions of the Scottish Ministers under sections 2(5)[^f00047] or section 17E(3A) of the Act, a Health Board is required to make a payment to a provider under an agreement but subject to conditions, those conditions shall be a term of the agreement.

## Annotations

[^f00047]: Footnote: Section 2(5) was amended by the National Health Service and Community Care Act [1990 (c. 19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/19), Schedule 9, paragraph 19(1).
