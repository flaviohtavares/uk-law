# The National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2004, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Prescribed period under section 17D(3) of the Act

The period prescribed for the purposes of section 17D(3) of the Act (persons with whom agreements may be made)[^f00043] is 6 months.

## Annotations

[^f00043]: Footnote: Section 17D(3) was inserted by the [Primary Medical Services (Scotland) Act 2004 (asp 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2004/1), section 2(3)(e).
