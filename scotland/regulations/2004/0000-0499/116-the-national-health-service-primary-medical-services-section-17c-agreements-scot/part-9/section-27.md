# The National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2004, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Revocation

Subject to the provisions of any order made by the Scottish Ministers section 7 of the 2004 Act (ancillary provisions), the National Health Service (Personal Medical Services) (Scotland) Regulations 2001[^f00049] are hereby revoked.
