# The National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2004, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Commencement

An agreement shall provide for services to be provided under it from any date on or after 1st April 2004.
