## 8. Prescribed period under section 17L(6) of the Act[^key-00e396e97061bbe3e2da5e66c71bc694]

*Extent: S*

The period prescribed for the purposes of section 17L(6) of the Act (eligibility to be contractor under general medical services contract)[^f00048] is six months.
