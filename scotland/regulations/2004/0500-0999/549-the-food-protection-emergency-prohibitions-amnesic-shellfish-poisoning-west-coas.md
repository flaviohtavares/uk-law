# The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 10) (Scotland) Partial Revocation Order 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 549

PUBLIC HEALTH

CONTAMINATION OF FOOD

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 10) (Scotland) Partial Revocation Order 2004

Made

21st December 2004

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

22nd December 2004

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1(1) and (2) and 24(3) of the Food and Environment Protection Act 1985[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 10) (Scotland) Partial Revocation Order 2004 and shall come into force at 1645 hours on 21st December 2004.

## 2. Revocation

The Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 10) (Scotland) Order 2004[^f00002] is hereby revoked in so far as it designates the areas described as Areas 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 in the Schedule to that Order.

Pam Whittle

A member of the staff of the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House, Edinburgh

21st December 2004

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is a partial revocation of the Food Protection (Emergency Prohibitions) (Amnesic Shellfish Poisoning) (West Coast) (No. 10) (Scotland) Order 2004 (“the principal Order”) which prohibited–

- (a) fishing for or taking scallops within the areas designated in the Schedule to the principal Order (article 4 of the principal Order);
- (b) movement of such scallops out of the designated areas (article 5 of the principal Order); and
- (c) landing, using in the preparation or processing for supply of food of, supplying and other specified activities in relation to, such scallops from the designated areas (article 6 of the principal Order).

The effect of this Order is to remove the prohibitions in respect of the areas of sea designated as Areas 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 in the Schedule to the principal Order.
