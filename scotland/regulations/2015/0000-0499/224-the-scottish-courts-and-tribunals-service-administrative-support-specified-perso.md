# The Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service (Administrative Support) (Specified Persons) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 224

Scottish Courts And Tribunals Service

The Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service (Administrative Support) (Specified Persons) Order 2015

Made

27th May 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

29th May 2015

Coming into force

27th June 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 62(1)(h) of the Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

The Scottish Ministers have consulted the Lord President in accordance with section 62(2) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service (Administrative Support) (Specified Persons) Order 2015 and comes into force on 27th June 2015.

## 2. Specified persons

The Scottish Sentencing Council[^f00002] is specified for the purposes of section 62 of the Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008.

MICHAEL MATHESON

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

27th May 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order adds the Scottish Sentencing Council to the list of persons that the Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service provides, or must ensure the provision of, administrative support for under section 62 of the Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008 (the 2008 Act).

By virtue of section 62(1) of the 2008 Act this includes providing, or ensuring the provision of, property, services and any staff required for the purposes set out in that section.
