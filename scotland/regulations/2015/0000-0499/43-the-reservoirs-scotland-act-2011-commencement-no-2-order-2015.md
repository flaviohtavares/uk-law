# The Reservoirs (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 43 (C. 7)

Flood Risk Management

Water Supply

The Reservoirs (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2015

Made

3rd February 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

5th February 2015

Coming into force

20th February 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 116(1) of the Reservoirs (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Reservoirs (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2015 and comes into force on 20th February 2015.

## 2. Appointed day

(1) 20th February 2015 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Reservoirs (Scotland) Act 2011 specified in paragraph (2).

(2) The provisions are—

- (a) section 27(a) (panels of reservoir engineers: establishment), in so far as not already in force; and
- (b) section 31(1)(a) (consultation with Institution of Civil Engineers).

AILEEN McLEOD

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

3rd February 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 20th February 2015 the provisions of the Reservoirs (Scotland) Act 2011 (“the Act”) listed in article 2. In particular, these provisions require the Scottish Ministers to establish one or more panels of reservoir engineers for the purposes of the Act.
