# The Scottish Tribunals (Administrative Support for Listed Tribunals) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 405

Tribunals And Inquiries

The Scottish Tribunals (Administrative Support for Listed Tribunals) Order 2015

Made

26th November 2015

Coming into force

1st December 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 3(5) of schedule 4 to the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 133(2)(a) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Scottish Tribunals (Administrative Support for Listed Tribunals) Order 2015 and comes into force on 1st December 2015.

## 2. Amendment of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014

In paragraph 3 of schedule 4 to the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014, after sub-paragraph (2)(c) insert—

> - (ca) the private rented housing panel constituted in accordance with that Schedule,
> - (cb) the homeowner housing panel constituted in accordance with that Schedule,

.

PAUL WHEELHOUSE

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

26th November 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014 to allow the Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service to provide administrative support to the homeowner housing panel and private rented housing panel until such time as their functions are transferred to the Scottish Tribunals.
