# The Police Act 1997 and the Protection of Vulnerable Groups (Scotland) Act 2007 Remedial (No. 2) Order 2015, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Current disclosure requests under sections 52 and 53 of the 2007 Act

(1) Paragraph (2) of this article applies where the Scottish Ministers—

- (a) have, before the relevant date, received a request for—
  - (i) disclosure of a scheme member’s scheme record under section 52 (disclosure of scheme records) of the 2007 Act; or
  - (ii) disclosure of a scheme member’s short scheme record under section 53 (disclosure of short scheme records) of the 2007 Act; and
- (b) have not by that date disclosed the scheme record or, as the case may be, short scheme record.

(2) Any request for a disclosure referred to in paragraph (1)(a) is to be treated for all purposes as having been received after the relevant date.
