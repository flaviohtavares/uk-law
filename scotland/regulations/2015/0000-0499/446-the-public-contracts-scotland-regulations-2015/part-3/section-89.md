# The Public Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 89

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-03-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ssi/2015/446
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 89. Automatic Suspension of authority power to proceed with contract award

*Extent: S*

(1) Without prejudice to the application of any relevant standstill period, where proceedings under this Part are served on a contracting authority in relation to a contract that has not been entered into, a framework agreement that has not been concluded or a dynamic purchasing system that has not been established, the contracting authority must not enter into the contract, conclude the framework agreement or establish the dynamic purchasing system unless—

- (a) the proceedings are determined, discontinued or disposed of; or
- (b) the court, by interim order, brings to an end the prohibition.
