# The Public Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 94

## 94. Other orders

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the court declines to make an ineffectiveness order under regulation 93, the Court must, without prejudice to the other powers of the court, order—

- (a) the payment by the contracting authority of a financial penalty; or
- (b) the shortening of the duration of the contract or framework agreement.

(2) In determining what order to make under paragraph (1)(a), regulation 90(4) (powers and duties of the court) applies.

(3) Regulation 90(5) and (6) (powers and duties of the court) applies to an order made under paragraph (1)(b) as it applies to an order made under regulation 90(3)(b) (powers and duties of the court).
