# The Public Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 9

## 9. Exclusions: Electronic Communications

*Extent: S*

(1) These Regulations do not apply to procurement for the award of a public contract or to the organisation of a design contest for the principal purpose of permitting the contracting authority to provide or exploit public communications networks or to provide an electronic communications service to the public.

(2) In this regulation, “**public communications networks**” and “**electronic communication service**” have the same meanings as in Directive [2002/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0021) of the European Parliament and of the Council on a common regulatory framework for electronic communications networks and services [^c22606341] as amended from time to time.
