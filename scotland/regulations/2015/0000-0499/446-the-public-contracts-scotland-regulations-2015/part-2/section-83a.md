## 83A. General provisions about regulations under this Part[^key-be697e5d26276e13aa267b1a1d3cb347]

*Extent: S*

(1) Regulations under this Part are to be made by Scottish statutory instrument subject to the negative procedure.

(2) Regulations under this Part may make supplementary, incidental, transitional or saving provision.
