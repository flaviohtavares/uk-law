# The Public Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 82

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-03-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ssi/2015/446
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 82. Retention of contract copies

*Extent: S*

(1) A contracting authority must, at least for the duration of the contract, keep copies of all concluded contracts with a value equal to or greater than—

- (a) £884,720[^c-b470d8582e96d08f33903175783dce53] in the case of public supply contracts or public service contracts;
- (b) £8,847,200[^c-41c7a39a3b4b6618aa2f5f39ba3fdbe1] in the case of public works contracts.

(2) A contracting authority must grant access to those contracts, but access to specific documents or items of information may be denied to the extent and on the conditions provided for in the rules on access to documents and data protection applicable in the relevant part of the United Kingdom[^c-adf3cd5cce6783e422292ed0c51c9231].
