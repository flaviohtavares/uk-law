# The Public Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 80

## 80. Appointment and composition of the jury

*Extent: S*

(1) A contracting authority must appoint a jury which must be composed exclusively of natural persons who are independent of participants in the contest.

(2) Where a particular professional qualification is required from participants in a contest, at least a third of the members of the jury must have that qualification or an equivalent qualification.
