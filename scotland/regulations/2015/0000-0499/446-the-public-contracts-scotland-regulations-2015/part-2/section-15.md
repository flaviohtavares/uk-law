# The Public Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 15

## 15. Specific situations: Research and development services

*Extent: S*

These Regulations only apply to procurement for the award of public service contracts for research and development service which are covered by CPV Codes 73000000-2 to 73120000-9, 73300000-5, 73420000-2[^c-f57e7d6dbab30dcf9898c511b3307c01] and 73430000-5 provided that—

- (a) the benefits accrue exclusively to the contracting authority for use in the conduct of its own affairs; and
- (b) the service provided is wholly remunerated by the contracting authority.
