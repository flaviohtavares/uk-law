## 49. Prior information notices

*Extent: S*

(1) A contracting authority may make known its intentions of planned procurements through the publication of a prior information notice.

(2) A prior information notice must contain the information set out in section I of Part B of Annex V to the Directive , but as if—[^key-aa25c46b94508ff20ea45103090f0eec]

- (a) in paragraph 2, “the second and third sub-paragraph of Article 53(1)” were a reference to regulation 54(3) and (4) of these Regulations, and
- (b) in paragraph 9, “date of dispatch” were a reference to the date on which the notice is submitted to the UK e-notification service for the purposes of these Regulations.

(3) A contracting authority wishing to publish a prior information notice must—

- (a) submit[^key-a21f918652c7f4470ec9cf05b7b1de68] it for publication in accordance with regulation 52 (publication on the UK e-notification service[^key-81be055c8ff0803d126f1e01972de97d]); or
- (b) publish it on the contracting authority's buyer profile in accordance with regulation 53 (publication otherwise than on the UK e-notification service[^key-0bd719ea6b21725d3d78a8f2c417ff36]).

(4) Where the prior information notice is published by the contracting authority on its buyer profile, the contracting authority must submit[^key-52ffbcd441b0d26a7191b2856fb14b61] for publication, in accordance with regulation 52 (publication on the UK e-notification service[^key-c53302d13fa101e7b4ff496e796ffb08]), a notice of the publication on its buyer profile.

(5) The notice of publication referred to in paragraph (4) must contain the information set out in Part A of Annex V to the Directive , but as if, in paragraph 6, “date of dispatch” were a reference to the date on which the notice is submitted to the UK e-notification service for the purposes of these Regulations[^key-975e2fd668b02da074e4e65da19b3730].

(6)[^key-9c915299e8142bd6d8e9c7d233c588d7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^key-b9ca07c7dc058b4bf648a21c2e665ead] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8) The[^key-4bb1b259d54551b2fb5e6d4d17e3a98b] period covered by a prior information notice must be a maximum of 12 months from the date on which the notice is sent for publication.
