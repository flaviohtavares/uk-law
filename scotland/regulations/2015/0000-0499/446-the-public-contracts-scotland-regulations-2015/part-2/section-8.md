# The Public Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 8

## 8. Exclusions: Concessions

*Extent: S*

(1) These Regulations do not apply to procurement for the award of a works concession contract or a services concession contract[^c-ff5cba2e6dd4dc8a3931667739cb3051] which—

- (a)[^c-50748b25b8c0075d7bd146598c805d24] is subject to the application of the Concession Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2016,
- (b) would be subject to the application of those Regulations[^c-2d099daa0d6329908e0cb6ad4935a09a] but for any provision contained therein.

(2)[^c-76f3662066a31bec29b1aff31fdba261] In this regulation “works concession contract” and “services concession contract” have the meaning given in regulation 3 (meaning of “concession contract”) of the Concession Contracts (Scotland) Regulations 2016.
