# The Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965 (Prohibition on Disposal of a Body without Authorisation) Regulations 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 27A(2) of the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation[^c-a9cbd8b12dd4020cbdcacea4f28eb1c7]

These Regulations may be cited as the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965 (Prohibition on Disposal of a Body without Authorisation) Regulations 2015 and come into force on 13th May 2015.

**2.**[^c-78b91a45b00446d9e38373a02376eef1] In these Regulations—

- “the 1965 Act” means the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965;
- “the 1984 Act” means the Anatomy Act 1984[^f00002];
- “the 2011 Act” means the Certification of Death (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00003].

## 3. Requirements for disposal of a body in Scotland where death occurs (or is deemed to have occurred) in Scotland[^c-a8121fcd1a3635695ba9f4e58625aa1b]

In relation to a person who dies in Scotland (including a person who is deemed by virtue of section 22(4) of the 1965 Act to have died in Scotland), a certificate given by a district registrar for a registration district under section 21(4) (still-births) or section 27(1) (free certificate of registration of death) of the 1965 Act is specified for the purpose of section 27A(2)(a) of that Act.

## 4. Requirements for disposal of a body in Scotland where death occurs in England or Wales[^c-970a5f7b405ea584b530c577cc8d6fc9]

In relation to a person who dies in England or Wales, the following certificates are specified for the purpose of section 27A(2)(a) of the 1965 Act—

- (a)
  - (i)[^c-8c6c29b8a5b1333e0cbc8b9f7e37547a] a certificate equivalent to that given by a district registrar for a registration district under section 21(4) or 27(1) of the 1965 Act; and
  - (ii)[^c-e35a51a8d2da782483d4b698b331fd00] an acknowledgement by a Coroner that notice of the intention to remove the body out of England or Wales has been received by the Coroner together with intimation that the Coroner does not intend to hold an inquest; or
- (b)[^c-8bdce2e8432513a032404b78344963e0] a certificate authorising the burial of the body given by a Coroner in accordance with regulations made in pursuance of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009; or
- (c)[^c-f26809f3ccdb723ecfb4ea10df00dc0b] a certificate given by a Coroner in accordance with regulations made in pursuance of section 7 of the Cremation Act 1902[^f00004].

## 5. Requirements for disposal of a body in Scotland where death occurs in Northern Ireland[^c-15be687d381501ebbf21a5ea705bf490]

In relation to a person who dies in Northern Ireland, the following certificates are specified for the purpose of section 27A(2)(a) of the 1965 Act—

- (a)
  - (i)[^c-641956a82f1ed042d643ab67a922febf] an equivalent certificate to that given by a district registrar for a registration district under section 21(4) or 27(1) of the 1965 Act; or[^c-ec2b38e433a4f256603de1d060027bd9]
  - (ii)[^c-60833f99523599c3436d3be4168ad0a8] a certificate of authorisation[^c-d75cb9284142c34eb0e06e1c58319f99] given by the coroner in accordance with section 9 of the Coroners Act (Northern Ireland) 1959; and.
- (b) a Coroner’s certificate for removal of the body out of Northern Ireland.

## 6. Requirements for disposal of a body in Scotland where death occurs outwith the United Kingdom[^c-7819c99734a4db5a32c13efbb4bcb4e2]

In relation to a person who dies outwith the United Kingdom, the following certificates and documents are specified for the purpose of section 27A(2)(a) of the 1965 Act—

- (a) a certificate given by a medical reviewer under section 17(4) of the 2011 Act; and
- (b) a certificate given by a medical reviewer under section 18(3) of the 2011 Act (medical reviewers to authorise cremation or hydrolysis[^c-f92acebb19674bf621f35d323b2b097c]) if the person’s body is to be cremated or hydrolysed (within the meaning given by section 107 of the Burial and Cremation (Scotland) Act 2016)[^c-b001d7cdbd44ae8baca9568fa11b4b2b] in Scotland.

## 7. Requirements for disposal of a body in Scotland where anatomical examination has taken place[^c-fde11aae90901ba6f7882ad673b6aa3b]

(1) Where a body has undergone anatomical examination pursuant to the provisions of the 1984 Act—

- (a) a certificate in the form of Form M set out in the Schedule, and containing the information specified in that form, is specified for the purpose of section 27A(2)(a) of the 1965 Act for the disposal of the body;
- (b) a certificate in the form of Form N set out in the Schedule, and containing the information specified in that form in relation to “Anatomical Examination”, is specified for the purpose of section 27A(2)(a) of the 1965 Act for the disposal of any part of the body.

(2) A certificate specified by paragraph (1) may be given by a person licensed under section 3(2) of the 1984 Act.

## 8. Requirements for disposal of part of a body in Scotland where post-mortem examination has taken place[^c-eebc11756e6bbf3f258ad4bd368f3346]

Where a body has undergone post-mortem examination, a certificate in the form of Form N set out in the Schedule, and containing the information specified in that form in relation to “Post-Mortem[^c-ede22739df4e5d41bd54e9cfbc4af9c4] Examination”, is specified for the purpose of section 27A(2)(a) of the 1965 Act for the disposal of any part of the body.

## 9. Requirements for disposal of a body in Scotland where death investigated by procurator fiscal[^c-a7f9118e1dbb7539f67b20d62704c571]

Where the death of a person has been investigated by the procurator fiscal, a certificate in the form of Form E1 set out in schedule 2, confirming that the remains of the deceased may be destroyed, is specified for the purpose of section 27A(2)(a) of the 1965 Act for the disposal of the remains of that person by cremation or hydrolysis.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations specify the certificates required for the disposal of deceased persons and stillborn children in Scotland under section 27A of the Registration of Births, Deaths and Marriages (Scotland) Act 1965. Regulation 3 provides the certificates required where a person has died in Scotland. Regulations 4 and 5 provide for the certificates required where a person has died in England, Wales and Northern Ireland. Regulation 6 provides for the certificates required where a person has died outwith the United Kingdom. Regulation 7 provides for the additional certificates required where a person has undergone an anatomical examination. Regulation 8 provides for the additional certificates required where a person has undergone a post-mortem examination.
