# The Certification of Death (Scotland) Act 2011 (Post-Mortem Examinations – Death Outwith United Kingdom) Regulations 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Public Health

The Certification of Death (Scotland) Act 2011 (Post-Mortem Examinations – Death Outwith United Kingdom) Regulations 2015

Made

2nd April 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

2nd April 2015

Coming into force

13th May 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 19(4) of the Certification of Death (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Certification of Death (Scotland) Act 2011 (Post-Mortem Examinations – Death Outwith United Kingdom) Regulations 2015 and come into force on 13th May 2015.

## 2. Form and content of an application under section 19(2) of the Certification of Death (Scotland) Act 2011

An application under section 19(2) of the Certification of Death (Scotland) Act 2011 must be in the Form set out in the Schedule and contain the information and documents specified in that form.

## SCHEDULE — Form of application under Section 19 of the Certification of Death (Scotland) Act 2011

Regulation 2

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations specify in regulation 2 the form and content of an application for assistance in the making of arrangements for post-mortem examinations and for the meeting of the cost of such examination under section 19 of the Certification of Death (Scotland) 2011.
