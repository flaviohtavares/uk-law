# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 5.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 5.5. Methods of intimation: by sheriff officer (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-72e07f334b09f91f9baeaaa16a256746][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A sheriff officer may give intimation on behalf of an intimating party by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) delivering it personally to the receiving party; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) leaving it in the hands of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) a resident at the receiving party’s dwelling place; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] an employee at the receiving party’s place of business. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where a sheriff officer has been unsuccessful in giving intimation in accordance with paragraph (1), the sheriff officer may give intimation by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) depositing it in the receiving party’s dwelling place or place of business; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) leaving it at the receiving party’s dwelling place or place of business in such a way that it is likely to come to the attention of that party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
