# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 3.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.2. Sanctions where a party is in default (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b017ef6aef7a80200f019ba38ca7e61d][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) applies where a party is in default; but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) does not apply where a party is in default because the party has failed to comply with rule 17.4(1) (peremptory hearing). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The procedural Appeal Sheriff may make any order to secure the expeditious disposal of the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) In particular, the procedural Appeal Sheriff may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) refuse the appeal, where the party in default is the appellant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) allow the appeal, if the condition in paragraph (4) is satisfied, where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) the party in default is the sole respondent; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] every respondent is in default. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The condition is that the appellant must show cause why the appeal should be allowed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
