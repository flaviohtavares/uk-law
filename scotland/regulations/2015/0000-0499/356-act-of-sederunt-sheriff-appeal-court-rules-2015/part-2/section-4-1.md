# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 4.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 4.1. Representation and support (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a45bc9b5f415c235a9cedd90eefe40a5][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A natural person who is a party to proceedings may appear and act on that party’s behalf. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) That person is to be known as a party litigant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) A party may be represented in any proceedings by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) a legal representative (see rule 4.2); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) a lay representative (see rule 4.3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) A lay supporter (see rule 4.5) may assist a party litigant with the conduct of any proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
