# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 4.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 4.3. Lay representation: applications (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8abd1280c7b43679de8b132ee702f5c6][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule does not apply where any other enactment makes provision for a party to a particular type of case to be represented by a lay representative. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) A party is represented by a lay representative if that party is represented by a person who is not a legal representative. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) A party litigant may apply to the Court for permission to be represented by a lay representative. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) An application is to be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) made by motion; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) accompanied by a document in Form 4.3 signed by the prospective lay representative. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) The Court may grant an application only if it considers that it would *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] be in the interests of justice[^key-2553c9034f8fd56248a975698ff93d85] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) Where the Court grants permission, it may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) do so in respect of one or more specified hearings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) withdraw permission of its own accord or on the motion of any party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
