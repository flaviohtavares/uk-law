# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 4.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 4.5. Lay support: applications (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-356f717a848b0b65ab124008c6fc58b7][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A party litigant may apply to the Court for permission for a named person to assist the party litigant in the conduct of proceedings, and such a person is to be known as a lay supporter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) An application is to be made by motion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The Court may refuse an application only if it is of the opinion that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the named person is an unsuitable person to act as a lay supporter (whether generally or in the proceedings concerned); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) it would be contrary to the efficient administration of justice to grant it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The Court, if satisfied that it would be contrary to the efficient administration of justice for permission to continue, may withdraw permission— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) of its own accord; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) on the motion of any party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
