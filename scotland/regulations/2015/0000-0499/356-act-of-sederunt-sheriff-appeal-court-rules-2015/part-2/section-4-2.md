# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 4.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 4.2. Legal representation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a9fd4e283c98ad3d358573cfc8527250][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

A party is represented by a legal representative if that party is represented by an advocate or a solicitor. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
