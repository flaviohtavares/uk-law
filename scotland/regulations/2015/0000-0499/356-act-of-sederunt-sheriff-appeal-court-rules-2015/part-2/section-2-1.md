# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 2.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 2.1. Relief for failure to comply with rules (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e5c9fd9fa5f32adce106e396e1f751af][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Court may relieve a party from the consequences of a failure to comply with a provision in these Rules. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The Court may do so only where the party shows that the failure is due to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) mistake; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) oversight; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) any other excusable cause. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Where relief is granted, the Court may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) impose conditions that must be satisfied before relief is granted; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) make an order to enable the appeal to proceed as if the failure had not occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
