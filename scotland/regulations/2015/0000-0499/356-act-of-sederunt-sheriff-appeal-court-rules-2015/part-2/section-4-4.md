# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 4.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 4.4. Lay representation: functions, conditions and duties (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-413990bade395eef6955f91ad48167fd][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A lay representative may represent a party at a specified hearing *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] [^key-ad01430b2272b6162346ce15647b535e]....

(2) The party must appear along with the lay representative at any hearing where the lay representative is to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] represent the party[^key-94f940885b989e95b1f14fb3d061c68f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950].

(2A)[^key-c9a8b2d21a78c3c0f4a226877847f22f] Where permission is granted under rule 4.3, the lay representative may do anything in the preparation or conduct of the hearing that the party may do. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) A party may show any document (including a court document) or communicate any information about the proceedings to that party’s lay representative without contravening any prohibition or restriction on disclosure of the document or information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Where a document or information is disclosed under paragraph (3), the lay representative is subject to any prohibition or restriction on disclosure in the same way that the party is. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) A lay representative must not receive directly or indirectly from the party any remuneration or other reward for assisting the party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) Any expenses incurred by a party in connection with a lay representative are not recoverable expenses in the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
