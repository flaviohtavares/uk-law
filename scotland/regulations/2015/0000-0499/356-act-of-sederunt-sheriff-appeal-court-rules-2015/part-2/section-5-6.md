# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 5.6

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 5.6. Additional methods of intimation where receiving party represented by solicitor (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-061fb4936410a97214cee4ba84c255a3][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) An intimating party may give intimation to the solicitor for the receiving party by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) delivering it personally to the solicitor; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) delivering it to a document exchange of which the solicitor is a member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) first class post; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) fax; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (e) electronic means. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where intimation is given by the method in paragraph (1)(a), (d) or (e) not later than 1700 hours on any day, the date of intimation is that day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Where intimation is given by the method in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) paragraph (1)(b) or (c); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) paragraph 1(a), (d) or (e) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] [^c-b12b3955be9b8b058ab4876024a09246]... after 1700 hours on any day, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

the date of intimation is the next day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
