# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 5.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 5.1. Interpretation of this Chapter (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c94bc22558df5a3fc45ca10b42751478][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) In this Chapter— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- “first class post” means a postal service which seeks to deliver documents or other things by post no later than the next working day in all or the majority of cases; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “intimating party” means any party who has to give intimation in accordance with rule 5.2(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “receiving party” means any party to whom intimation is to be given in accordance with rule 5.2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “recorded delivery” means a postal service which provides for the delivery of documents or other things by post to be recorded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where this Chapter authorises intimation to be given by electronic means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) intimation may only be given by this method if the intimating party and the solicitor for the receiving party have notified the Court that they will accept intimation by electronic means at a specified email address; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the intimation is to be sent to the specified email address of the solicitor for the receiving party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Where this Chapter authorises a document to be lodged by electronic means, it is to be sent to the email address of the Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
