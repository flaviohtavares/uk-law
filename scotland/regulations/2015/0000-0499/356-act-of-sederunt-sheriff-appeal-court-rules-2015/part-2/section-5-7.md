# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 5.7

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 5.7. Lodging (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9523f16355568395612ef4389537a869][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where any provision in these Rules requires a party to lodge a document, it is to be lodged with the Clerk. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) A document may be lodged by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) delivering it personally to the office of the Clerk; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) delivering it to a document exchange of which the Clerk is a member; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) first class post; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) fax; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (e) electronic means. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
