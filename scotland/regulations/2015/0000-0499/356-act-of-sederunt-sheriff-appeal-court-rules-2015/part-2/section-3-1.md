# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 3.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3.1. Circumstances where a party is in default (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-24067c37b395340a1f5f03adad5edab6][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

A party is in default if that party fails— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) to comply with the timetable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) to implement an order of the Court within the period specified in the order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) to appear or be represented at any hearing; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) otherwise to comply with any requirement imposed on that party by these Rules. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
