# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 5.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 5.2. Intimation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0157197a7e1e338e48e7a4aa23decbec][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Unless the Court orders otherwise, where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) any provision in these Rules requires a party to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) lodge any document; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] intimate any other matter; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the Court orders a party to intimate something, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

intimation is to be given to every other party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where the Court makes an order, the Clerk is to intimate the order to every party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
