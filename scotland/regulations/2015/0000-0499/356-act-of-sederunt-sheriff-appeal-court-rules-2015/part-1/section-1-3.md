# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 1.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1.3. Computation of periods of time (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7d361c780bfed34c119c3101551323c7][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

If any period of time specified in these Rules expires on a Saturday, Sunday or public or court holiday, it is extended to expire on the next day that the office of the Clerk is open for civil business. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
