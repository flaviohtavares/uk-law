# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 1.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 1.1. Citation and commencement, etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-61df099d0bdc955b008ee80cbfeefa2b][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) It comes into force on 1st January 2016. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) A certified copy is to be inserted in the Books of Sederunt. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
