# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 1.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1.5. Forms (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2b44e0f7b603b454016fcbd70cadff70][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where there is a reference in these Rules to a form, it is a reference to that form in Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where these Rules require a form to be used, that form may be varied where the circumstances require it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
