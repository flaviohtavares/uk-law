# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 1.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1.2. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d8c68308e172dd769703a86507f5c3bd][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) In this Act of Sederunt— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- “the 2014 Act” means the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “the Clerk” means the Clerk of the Sheriff Appeal Court; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “advocate” means a practising member of the Faculty of Advocates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “the Court” means the Sheriff Appeal Court; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “grounds of appeal” has the meaning given by rule 6.2(2)(b); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “party litigant” has the meaning given by rule 4.1(2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “procedural Appeal Sheriff” has the meaning given by paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “procedural hearing” means a hearing under rule 7.14 or rule 28.13; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “provisional procedural order” means an order under rule 6.6(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “sheriff court process” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (a) the sheriff court process for the cause that is appealed to the Court; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (b) where the cause is recorded in an official book of the sheriff court, a copy of the record in that book certified by the sheriff clerk; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “sheriff’s note” means a note setting out the reasons for the decision appealed against; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “solicitor” means a person qualified to practise as a solicitor under section 4 of the Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1980 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00004];
- “timetable” means a timetable in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (a) Form 7.2 issued under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
    - (i) rule 7.2(1) (timetable in appeal); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
    - (ii) rule 7.6(5)(a) (recall of sist: issuing revised timetable); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
    - (iii) rule 7.6(6)(b) (variation of timetable: issuing revised timetable); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (b) Form 28.5 issued under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
    - (i) rule 28.5(1) (timetable in application for new trial); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
    - (ii) rule 28.6(6)(a) (recall of sist: issuing revised timetable); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
    - (iii) rule 28.6(7)(b) (variation of timetable: issuing revised timetable). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) In relation to an application under section 69(1) or 71(2) of the 2014 Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- “appeal” includes that application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “appellant” includes the applicant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “note of appeal” includes an application in Form 28.2 or Form 28.14. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
