# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 7.7

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 7.7. Questions about competency (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e4c29fafaf8aafde9a7e0e37e448ce2f][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A question about the competency of an appeal may be referred to the procedural Appeal Sheriff by any respondent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) A question is referred by lodging a reference in Form 7.7. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) A question may be referred within 14 days after the timetable is issued under rule 7.2(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) When a reference is lodged, the Clerk is to fix a hearing and intimate the date and time of that hearing to the parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Within 14 days after the date on which the reference is lodged, each party must lodge a note of argument. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) That note of argument must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) give fair notice of the submissions the party intends to make on the question of competency; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) comply with the requirements in rule 7.12(3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(7) Paragraphs (4) and (5) of rule 7.12 apply to that note of argument. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
