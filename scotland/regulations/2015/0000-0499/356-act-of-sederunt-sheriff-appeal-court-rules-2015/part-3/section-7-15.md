# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 7.15

> Text as at 2023-10-02.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 7.15. Transmission of sheriff court process (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-2540727decd78ca3d8e59420ef3e7e45][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] procedural Appeal Sheriff[^key-7409ce344a019d4bfb2da85e975e3132] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] may order that the sheriff court process, or any part of it, is to be transmitted to the Clerk— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) of its own accord; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) on cause shown, where any party to the appeal applies for such an order by motion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] procedural Appeal Sheriff[^key-ae5908cef7feabc3871ce5def149f764] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] makes such an order, the Clerk must send a copy of the order to the sheriff clerk. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Within 4 days after receipt of the order, the sheriff clerk must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) send written notice to each party to the cause; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) certify on the interlocutor sheet that subparagraph (a) has been complied with; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) transmit the sheriff court process, or the specified part of it, to the Clerk. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) On receipt of the sheriff court process, the Clerk must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) mark the date of receipt on— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) the interlocutor sheet or the copy record from the sheriff court books, where the entire process is transmitted; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] the part of process that has been transmitted, where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] procedural Appeal Sheriff[^key-07eee3a146b70df2defe79817afbcf65] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] has specified that only part of the process is to be transmitted; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) send written notice of that date to the appellant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Where the Clerk or a sheriff clerk fails to comply with this rule— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) that does not affect the validity of the appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] procedural Appeal Sheriff[^key-6a86134f08eb9732a198de021cca66a1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] may, as it thinks fit, make an order to enable the appeal to proceed as if the failure had not occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
