# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 6.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 6.4. Applications to appeal out of time (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b52120375fdd0ca4d19a71cfb2c905bc][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies where the enactment under which the appeal is made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) specifies a period within which the appeal must be made; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) provides that a party may apply to the Court to allow an appeal to be made outwith that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) An application to allow an appeal to be received out of time is to be made by motion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) That motion is to be made when the note of appeal is lodged. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Where a motion to allow an appeal to be received out of time is refused— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the Clerk is to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) notify the sheriff clerk that leave to appeal out of time has been refused; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] transmit the note of appeal to the sheriff clerk; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the sheriff clerk is to place the note of appeal in the process. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
