# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 6.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 6.1. Application of this Chapter (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d257f16260e41f8a539432f9a48f2579][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

This Chapter applies to an appeal against a decision of a sheriff in civil proceedings except— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) an application for a new trial under section 69(1) of the 2014 Act (see Chapter 28); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) an application to enter a jury verdict under section 71(2) of the 2014 Act (see Chapter 28); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) an appeal under section 38 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1971 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00005] (see Chapter 29); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) an appeal by stated case under section 163(1), 164(1), 165(1) or 167(1) of the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00006] (see Chapter 30); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (da (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b151c68b9643775670d6beb6071507bb][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] an appeal under section 38(3), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] 46(3)[^key-dec90569304713cb1aa5f3c1eb3cb240] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] or 67(3) of the Age of Criminal Responsibility (Scotland) Act 2019 (see Chapter 31); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (e)[^key-274da909a41e5d8125961711a7ad9c24] an appeal against an interlocutor granting decree of divorce in a simplified divorce application (see rule 33.81 of the Ordinary Cause Rules 1993); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (f) an appeal against an interlocutor granting decree of dissolution of civil partnership in a simplified dissolution of civil partnership application (see rule 33A.74 of the Ordinary Cause Rules 1993. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
