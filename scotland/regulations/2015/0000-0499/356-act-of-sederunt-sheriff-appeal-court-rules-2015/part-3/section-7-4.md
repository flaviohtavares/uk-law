# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 7.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 7.4. Urgent disposal (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d6bfd04c3b3aff26d5befad6207d20eb][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The procedural Appeal Sheriff may order urgent disposal of an appeal— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) of the procedural Appeal Sheriff’s own accord; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) on the application of the appellant or a respondent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where the appellant or a respondent seeks urgent disposal, an application for urgent disposal is to be made by motion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) An application may be made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) by the appellant, when the note of appeal is lodged; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) by the respondent, not later than the expiry of the period for lodging answers specified in rule 6.5(1)(b) (order for intimation and answers). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Where the decision appealed against concerns an order made by the sheriff under section 11(1) of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995 (court orders relating to parental responsibilities etc.) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00007], the appellant must seek urgent disposal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Where the procedural Appeal Sheriff proposes to order urgent disposal of the procedural Appeal Sheriff’s own accord— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the Clerk must notify every party to the appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) any party who objects to urgent disposal may make representations within such time and in such manner as the procedural Appeal Sheriff orders. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
