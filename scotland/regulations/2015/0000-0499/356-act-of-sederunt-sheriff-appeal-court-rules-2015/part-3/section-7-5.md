# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 7.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 7.5. Urgent disposal: determination (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8db11cb58f2bdd21f2973fb24da5207c][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where an application for urgent disposal is opposed, it may only be disposed of after the procedural Appeal Sheriff has heard parties on it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where a party makes representations objecting to urgent disposal in accordance with rule 7.4(5), the procedural Appeal Sheriff must hear parties before ordering urgent disposal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) At a hearing under paragraph (1) or (2), the parties must provide the procedural Appeal Sheriff with an assessment of the likely duration of the hearing to determine the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) When ordering urgent disposal of an appeal, the procedural Appeal Sheriff must make an order specifying— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the procedure to be followed in the appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the periods for complying with each procedural step. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Accordingly, the following rules apply only to the extent that the procedural Appeal Sheriff specifies in the order made under paragraph (3)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) rule 7.2 (timetable in appeal); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) rule 7.7 (questions about competency of appeal); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) rule 7.8 (questions about competency: determination); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) rule 7.9 (appeal print); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (e) rule 7.10 (appendices to the appeal print: contents); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (f) rule 7.11 (appendices to the appeal print considered unnecessary); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (g) rule 7.12 (notes of argument); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (h) rule 7.13 (estimates of duration of appeal hearing); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (i) rule 7.14 (procedural hearing). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
