# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 7.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 7.2. Timetable in appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f469e450bcf1d9e96f5821f540e049f8][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Clerk must issue a timetable in Form 7.2 when— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) a provisional procedural order appointing the appeal to the standard appeal procedure becomes final or is confirmed; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the Court makes an order appointing the appeal to the standard appeal procedure under rule 6.7(5)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) When the Clerk issues a timetable, the Clerk must also fix a procedural hearing to take place after completion of the procedural steps specified in paragraph (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The timetable specifies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the dates by which parties must comply with those procedural steps; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the date and time of the procedural hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The procedural steps are the steps mentioned in the first column of the following table, provision in respect of which is found in the rule mentioned in the second column— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

| Procedural step *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] | Rule *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] |
| --- | --- |
| Cross appeals: lodging of grounds of appeal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] | 7.3(1) |
| Cross appeals: lodging of answers *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] | 7.3(2) |
| Referral of question about competency of appeal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] | 7.7(3) |
| Lodging of appeal print *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] | 7.9(1) and (2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] |
| Lodging of appendices to appeal print *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] | 7.10(1) |
| Giving notice that the appellant considers appendix unnecessary *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] | 7.11(1) |
