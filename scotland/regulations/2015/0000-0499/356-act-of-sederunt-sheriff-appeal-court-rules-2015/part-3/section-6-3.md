# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 6.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 6.3. Time for appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a0be2bb1fb75c9def97360795b502ad3][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) An appeal must be made within 28 days after the date on which the decision appealed against was given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) This rule does not apply where the enactment under which the appeal is made specifies a period within which the appeal must be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
