# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 7.16

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 7.16. Extension of notes of evidence (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-531ec1858139be84c3d2fa81892840ae][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The parties may agree that, in relation to any particular issue, the decision appealed against is not to be submitted to review. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) It is not necessary to reproduce the notes of evidence or documents relating to that issue. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
