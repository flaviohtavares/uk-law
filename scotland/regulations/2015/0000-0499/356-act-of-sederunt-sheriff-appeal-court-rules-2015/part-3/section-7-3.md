# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 7.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 7.3. Cross-appeals (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9b9929e00784caee459aa4e8ccb8f1b7][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A respondent who seeks to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) appeal against any decision of the sheriff; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) challenge the grounds on which the sheriff made the decision appealed against, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

may lodge grounds of appeal in Form 7.3 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] within 28 days after the timetable is issued under rule 7.2(1)[^key-cb6a1c00fbe1cbe737106ba0d279b220] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950].

(2) The appellant may lodge answers to the respondent’s grounds of appeal within 28 days after the grounds are intimated to the appellant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
