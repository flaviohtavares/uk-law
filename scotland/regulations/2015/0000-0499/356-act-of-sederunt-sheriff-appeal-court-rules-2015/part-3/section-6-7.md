# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 6.7

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 6.7. Provisional orders: representations (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-6ee4d3fc63f724d1f716a106333dd4cc][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Any person to whom a provisional procedural order under rule 6.6 has been intimated may make representations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] [^key-136565f80c7cd8c67e69da23206bee4e]... before that order becomes final. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Representations are to be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) made in Form 6.7; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) lodged within 14 days after the date of intimation of the provisional order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2A)[^key-a005b6dad975a816f20550709c8caece] Paragraph (2B) applies where the procedural Appeal Sheriff varies the period of 14 days mentioned in rule 6.5(1)(b) in accordance with rule 6.5(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2B) The procedural Appeal Sheriff may also vary the period of 14 days mentioned in paragraph (2)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Representations must specify why, taking into account the matters in rule 6.6(3), it is not appropriate for the appeal to proceed in accordance with the provisional procedural order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) If representations are made, the Clerk is to fix a hearing and intimate the time and date of that hearing to every person to whom the provisional order was intimated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) At that hearing, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] the procedural Appeal Sheriff[^key-6a698c4871d957965869e02e1ec209ab] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) confirm the provisional procedural order; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) recall the provisional procedural order and make an order appointing the appeal to the standard appeal procedure or the accelerated appeal procedure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) If no representations are made in accordance with paragraph (2), the provisional procedural order becomes final. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
