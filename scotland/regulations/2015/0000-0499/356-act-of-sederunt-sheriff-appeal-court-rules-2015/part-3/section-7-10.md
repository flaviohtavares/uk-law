# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 7.10

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 7.10. Appendix to appeal print: contents (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a5a71049822cb0d60a4220f2633fe3c9][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The appellant must lodge an appendix to the appeal print no later than 7 days before the procedural hearing, unless rule 7.11(1) (giving notice that appellant considers appendix unnecessary) is complied with. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The appendix is to contain— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) any document lodged in the sheriff court process that is founded upon in the grounds of appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the notes of evidence from any proof, if it is sought to submit them for consideration by the Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Where the sheriff’s note has not been included in the appeal print and it subsequently becomes available, the appellant must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) include it in the appendix where the appendix has not yet been lodged; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) lodge a supplementary appendix containing the sheriff’s note. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The parties must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) discuss the contents of the appendix; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) so far as possible, co-operate in making up the appendix. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
