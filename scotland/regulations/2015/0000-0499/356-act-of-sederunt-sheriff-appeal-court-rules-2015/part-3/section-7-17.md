# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 7.17

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 7.17. Referral to family mediation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-176d965477e696cdd64887d6495969cf][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the decision appealed against concerns an order made by the sheriff under section 11(1) of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995 (court orders relating to parental responsibilities etc.) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00008], the procedural Appeal Sheriff may refer that matter to a family mediator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) In this rule, “family mediator” means a person accredited as a mediator in family mediation to an organisation which is concerned with such mediation and which is approved for the purposes of the Civil Evidence (Family Mediation) (Scotland) Act 1995 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00009] by the Lord President of the Court of Session. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
