# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 6.6

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 6.6. Initial case management of appeals (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-985484385de177855e09da5b0cbcd1e8][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) When the procedural Appeal Sheriff makes an order for intimation and answers in accordance with rule 6.5(1), the procedural Appeal Sheriff must also make a provisional procedural order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The provisional procedural order must provisionally appoint the appeal to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the standard appeal procedure (see Chapter 7); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the accelerated appeal procedure (see Chapter 27). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) When considering which procedure is appropriate for the appeal, the procedural Appeal Sheriff must take into account— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the importance of the appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the complexity of the appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) the novelty of the points of law raised by the appeal; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) the presumption in paragraph (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The following categories of appeal are presumed to be appropriate for the accelerated appeal procedure— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) appeals against a decision of the sheriff to grant decree by default; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) appeals against a decision of the sheriff to refuse a reponing note. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) A provisional procedural order under this rule is to be intimated at the same time and in the same manner as the order for intimation and answers made in accordance with rule 6.5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
