# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 6.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 6.2. Form of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-fb28de88c287f0ba7af8e58cdbc41157][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) An appeal is made by lodging a note of appeal in Form 6.2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The note of appeal must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) the decision complained of; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] the date on which the decision was made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] the date on which it was intimated to the appellant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] any other relevant information; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) state the grounds of appeal in brief specific numbered paragraphs setting out concisely the grounds on which it is proposed that the appeal should be allowed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (ba (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-01b181f5569e62046df456641cee7305][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] have appended to it a copy of the interlocutor containing the decision appealed against; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) where the sheriff’s note is available, have appended to it a copy of the note; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) where the sheriff’s note is not available, indicate whether the appellant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) has requested that the sheriff writes a note and is awaiting its production; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] requests that the sheriff write a note; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] considers that the appeal is sufficiently urgent that the Court should hear and determine the appeal without the sheriff’s note; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (e) state whether, taking into account the matters in rule 6.6(3), the appellant considers that the appeal should be appointed to the standard appeal procedure or to the accelerated appeal procedure; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (f) be signed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] [^key-ede8a5088d4784dfd2b425f8648154ec]...;
- (g) where the appellant is represented by a solicitor, specify the name and business address of the solicitor. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) When a note of appeal is lodged, the appellant must lodge a process made up in accordance with paragraph 4 of Schedule 1 (form of process). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
