# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 20.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 20.3. Raising a devolution issue: intimation and service (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4a5885d648cef564f0806ee22ce4c09d][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies to the intimation of a devolution issue on a relevant authority under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) paragraph 5 of Schedule 6; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) paragraph 23 of Schedule 10; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) paragraph 14(1) of Schedule 9. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) When a devolution issue is raised, the party raising it must intimate the devolution issue to the relevant authority unless the relevant authority is a party to the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Within 14 days after intimation, the relevant authority may give notice to the Clerk that it intends to take part in the appeal as a party under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) paragraph 6 of Schedule 6; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) paragraph 24 of Schedule 10; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) paragraph 14(2) of Schedule 9. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
