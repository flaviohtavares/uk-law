# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 24.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 24.3. Determination of notices and applications (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-969fb5486a5aa7b04f30b7f2ed476397][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) When a notice or application under this Chapter is lodged, the Court may require any of the parties to provide further information before determining the notice or application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The Court may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) determine the notice or application by making an order under section 12(1) or (6) or 13(2) of the 2004 Act without holding a hearing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) fix a hearing at which parties are to be heard on the notice or application before determining it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The Court may make an order altering the date of the proof in order that the notice or application may be determined. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
