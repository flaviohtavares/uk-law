# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 24.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 24.1. Interpretation and application of this Chapter (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8f9946b3a6271684fd4b8131ebc469dc][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This Chapter applies where proof or additional proof is ordered to be taken under rule 23.1(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) In this Chapter— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- “2004 Act” means the Vulnerable Witnesses (Scotland) Act 2004 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00024];
- “child witness notice” has the meaning given by section 12(2) of the 2004 Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “review application” means an application under section 13(1)(a) of the 2004 Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “vulnerable witness application” has the meaning given by section 12(6) of the 2004 Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
