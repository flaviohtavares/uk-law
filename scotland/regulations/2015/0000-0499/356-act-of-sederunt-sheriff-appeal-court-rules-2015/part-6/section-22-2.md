# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 22.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 22.2. Applications to intervene (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-25e81725d0cb40d29d74952ae0639f72][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) An application for leave to intervene is to be made in Form 22.2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Such an application is to be lodged in the process of the appeal to which it relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) When an intervener lodges an application, rule 5.2(1) applies as if the intervener were a party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The parties may request a hearing on the application to intervene within 14 days after the application is lodged. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Where a hearing is requested— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the Court is to appoint a date and time for a hearing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the Clerk must notify the date and time of the hearing to the parties and the applicant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) Where no hearing is requested, the Court may appoint a date and time for a hearing of its own accord and the Clerk must notify the date and time of the hearing to the parties and the applicant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
