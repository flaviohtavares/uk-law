# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 20.8

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 20.8. Reference to the Inner House or Supreme Court: procedure following determination (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6058565588bbe5f084abb87b51a14255][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies where either the Inner House of the Court of Session or the Supreme Court has determined a devolution issue. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Upon receipt of the determination, the Clerk must place a copy of the determination before the Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The Court may, on the motion of any party or otherwise, order such further procedure as may be required. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Where the Court makes an order other than on the motion of a party, the Clerk must intimate a copy of the order on all parties to the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
