# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 21.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 21.1. Interpretation of this Chapter (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4902f2ffc91f03f845a440085c9fe183][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

In this Chapter— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- “European Court” means the Court of Justice of the European Union; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “reference” means a reference to the European Court for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (a) a preliminary ruling under Article 267 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00018];
  - (b) a ruling on the interpretation of the Conventions mentioned in Article 1 of Schedule 2 to the Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00019] under Article 3 of that Schedule; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (c) a preliminary ruling on the interpretation of the instruments mentioned in Article 1 of Schedule 3 to the Contracts (Applicable Law) Act 1990 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00020] under Article 2 of that Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
