# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 20.6

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 20.6. Reference to the Inner House or Supreme Court (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f1cf909934cf77e52dcaf920d198881d][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies to the reference of a devolution issue to the Inner House of the Court of Session for determination under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) paragraph 7 of Schedule 6; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) paragraph 25 of Schedule 10; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) paragraph 15 of Schedule 9. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) This rule also applies where the Court has been required by a relevant authority to refer a devolution issue to the Supreme Court under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) paragraph 33 of Schedule 6; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) paragraph 33 of Schedule 10; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) paragraph 29 of Schedule 9. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The Court is to make an order concerning the drafting and adjustment of the reference. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The reference must specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the questions for the Inner House or the Supreme Court; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the addresses of the parties; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) a concise statement of the background to the matter, including— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) the facts of the case, including any relevant findings of fact; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] the main issues in the case and contentions of the parties with regard to them; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) the relevant law including the relevant provisions of the Scotland Act 1998, the Government of Wales Act 2006 or the Northern Ireland Act 1998; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (e) the reasons why an answer to the questions is considered necessary for the purpose of disposing of the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) The reference must have annexed to it— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) a copy of all orders made in the appeal; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) a copy of any judgments in the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) When the reference has been drafted and adjusted, the Court is to make and sign the reference. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(7) The Clerk must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) send a copy of the reference to the parties to the proceedings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) certify on the back of the principal reference that subparagraph (a) has been complied with. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
