# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 26.1A

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 26.1A. Application for an order (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-caf8e271879ea9aa78dcd3197c93364e][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A party to the proceedings may apply to the court for an order under this Chapter to restrict the reporting of the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) An application for an order under this Chapter must be made by motion and be accompanied by Form 26.1A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
