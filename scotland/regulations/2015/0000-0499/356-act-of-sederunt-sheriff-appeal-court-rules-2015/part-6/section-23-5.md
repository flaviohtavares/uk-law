# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 23.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 23.5. Recording of evidence (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-16dac26e8ea56c6082d89f7a5fb7ff0e][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The evidence given at a hearing is to be recorded, unless the parties agree to dispense with the recording of evidence and the Appeal Sheriff considers that it is appropriate to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The evidence is to be recorded by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) a shorthand writer to whom the oath *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] *de fideli administratione *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]* has been administered in connection with the Court; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) by tape recording or other mechanical means approved by the Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) In the first instance, the solicitors for the parties are personally liable to pay, in equal shares— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the fees of a shorthand writer; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the fee payable for recording evidence by tape recording or other mechanical means. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The record of evidence is to include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) any objection taken to a question or to the line of evidence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) any submission made in relation to such an objection; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) the ruling of the Appeal Sheriff in relation to the objection and submission. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
