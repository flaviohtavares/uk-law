# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 24.9

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 24.9. Taking of evidence by commissioner: lodging and custody of video record and documents (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e3affb3bbfc37ba2b37ee4bf8627f795][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The commissioner is to lodge the video record of the commission and any relevant documents with the Clerk. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) When the video record and any relevant document are lodged, the Clerk is to notify every party— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) that the video record has been lodged; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) whether any relevant documents have been lodged; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) of the date on which they were lodged. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The video record and any relevant documents are to be kept by the Clerk. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Where the video record has been lodged— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the name and address of the vulnerable witness and the record of the witness’s evidence are to be treated as being in the knowledge of the parties; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the parties need not include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) the name of the witness in any list of witnesses; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] the record of evidence in any list of productions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
