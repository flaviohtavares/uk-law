# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 24.7

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 24.7. Taking of evidence by commissioner: interrogatories (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-cf6dc06d6ce753a8af26aeec8b92bf26][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies where the Court— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) authorises the special measure of taking evidence by a commissioner under section 19(1) of the 2004 Act; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) orders that interrogatories are to be prepared. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The party who cited the vulnerable witness must lodge draft interrogatories in process. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Any other party may lodge cross-interrogatories. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The parties may adjust their interrogatories and cross-interrogatories. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) At the expiry of the adjustment period, the parties must lodge the interrogatories and cross-interrogatories as adjusted in process. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) The Court is to resolve any dispute as to the content of the interrogatories and cross-interrogatories, and approve them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(7) When the Court makes an order for interrogatories to be prepared, it is to specify the periods within which parties must comply with the steps in this rule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
