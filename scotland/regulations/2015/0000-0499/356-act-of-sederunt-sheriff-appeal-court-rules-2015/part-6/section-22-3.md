# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 22.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 22.3. Applications to intervene: determination (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c9d60d35934c15c54c4f9a1265b257f6][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Court may determine an application for leave to intervene without a hearing, unless a hearing is fixed under rule 22.2(5) or (6). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) In an application for leave to intervene under section 30(1) of the Equality Act 2006, the Court may grant leave only if it is satisfied that the proposed submissions are likely to assist the Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Where the Court grants leave to intervene, it may impose any conditions that it considers desirable in the interests of justice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) In particular, the Court may make provision about any additional expenses incurred by the parties as a result of the intervention. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) When an application is determined, the Clerk must notify the parties and the applicant of the outcome. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
