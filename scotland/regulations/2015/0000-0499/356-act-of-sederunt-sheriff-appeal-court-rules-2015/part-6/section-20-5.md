# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 20.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 20.5. Participation by the relevant authority (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3ef3a0aef2a3bc94b25b1c2c42a490aa][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Paragraph (2) applies where a relevant authority has given notice to the Clerk that it intends to take part in the appeal as a party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Within 7 days after permission to proceed is given, the relevant authority must lodge a minute containing the relevant authority’s written submissions in respect of the devolution issue. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
