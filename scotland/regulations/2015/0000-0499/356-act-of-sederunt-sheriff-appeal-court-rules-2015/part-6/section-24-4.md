# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 24.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 24.4. Determination of notices and applications: supplementary orders (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1d13e4472a817310d2f0e219070c5f70][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

Where the Court determines a notice or application under this Chapter and makes an order under section 12(1) or (6) or 13(2) of the 2004 Act, the Court may make further orders to secure the expeditious disposal of the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
