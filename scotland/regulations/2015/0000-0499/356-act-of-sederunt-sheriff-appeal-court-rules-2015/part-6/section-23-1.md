# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 23.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 23.1. Taking proof in the course of an appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-41deaa984bc0e6ad76b4d075fdfe41f6][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) If it is considered necessary, proof or additional proof may be ordered— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) by the procedural Appeal Sheriff at a procedural hearing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) by the Court in the course of an appeal hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where the procedural Appeal Sheriff orders that proof or additional proof is to be taken— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the procedural Appeal Sheriff is to appoint a date and time for a hearing for that to be done; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) so far as reasonably practicable, the hearing is to be before the procedural Appeal Sheriff who made the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Where the Court orders that proof or additional proof is to be taken, the Court is to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) remit the proof to be taken before any Appeal Sheriff; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) appoint a date and time for a hearing for that to be done; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) continue the appeal hearing until the Appeal Sheriff reports the proof to the Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Where a hearing is fixed under this rule, the Clerk must notify the date and time of the hearing to the parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
