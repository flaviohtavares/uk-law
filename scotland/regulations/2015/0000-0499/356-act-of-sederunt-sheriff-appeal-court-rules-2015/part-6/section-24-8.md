# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 24.8

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 24.8. Taking of evidence by commissioner: conduct of commission (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-41b7fbb4490b07014cea4ea75e7ba1cd][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The commissioner is to administer the oath *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] *de fideli administratione *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]* to the clerk. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The commissioner is to administer the oath to the vulnerable witness in Form 23.4-A unless the witness elects to affirm. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Where the witness elects to affirm, the commissioner is to administer the affirmation in Form 23.4-B. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
