# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 20.7

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 20.7. Reference to the Inner House or Supreme Court: further procedure (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a30c48fc705f7d738301af13c9aafd67][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) On a reference being made, the appeal must, unless the Court otherwise orders, be sisted until the devolution issue has been determined. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Despite a reference being made, the Court continues to have the power to make any interim order required in the interests of the parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The Court may recall a sist for the purpose of making such interim orders. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) On a reference being made the Clerk must send the principal copy of the reference to (as the case may be)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the Deputy Principal Clerk of the Court of Session; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the Registrar of the Supreme Court (together with 7 copies). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Unless the Court orders otherwise, the Clerk must not send the principal copy of the reference where an appeal against the making of the reference is pending. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) An appeal is to be treated as pending— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) until the expiry of the time for making that appeal; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) where an appeal has been made, until that appeal has been determined. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
