# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 22.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 22.5. Form of intervention (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-88a9b0ba5c704ba8d1228f38a1669291][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) An intervention is to be by way of written submission. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) A written submission (including any appendices) must not exceed 5,000 words. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The intervener must lodge the written submission within such time as the Court may direct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) In exceptional circumstances, the Court may allow— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) a written submission exceeding 5,000 words to be made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) an oral submission to be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Where the Court allows an oral submission to be made, it is to appoint a date and time for the submission to be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) The Clerk must notify that date and time to the parties and the intervener. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
