# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 26.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 26.1. Interpretation and application of this Chapter (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0aa1a59d83e7ccc329662c9d53ef7521][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This Chapter applies to orders which restrict the reporting of proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) In this Chapter, “interested person” means a person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) who has asked to see any order made by the Court which restricts the reporting of proceedings, including an interim order; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) whose name is included on a list kept by the Lord President for the purposes of this Chapter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
