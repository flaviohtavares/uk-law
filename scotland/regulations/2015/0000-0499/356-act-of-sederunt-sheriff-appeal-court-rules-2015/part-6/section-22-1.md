# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 22.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 22.1. Application and interpretation of this Chapter (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-fd548771d31f63fa41dadfb7a436a678][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This Chapter applies to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) interventions in legal proceedings by the CEHR under section 30(1) of the Equality Act 2006 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00022];
- (b) interventions in civil proceedings (other than children’s hearing proceedings) by the SCHR under section 14(2) of the Scottish Commission for Human Rights Act 2006 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00023].

(2) In this Chapter— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- “the CEHR” means the Commission for Equality and Human Rights; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “the SCHR” means the Scottish Commission for Human Rights. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
