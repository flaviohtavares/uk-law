# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 24.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 24.2. Form of notices and applications (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e8f1a908c890688d4f72cd6f3e703a8e][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A child witness notice is to be made in Form 24.2–A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) A vulnerable witness application is to be made in Form 24.2–B. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) A review application is to be made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) in Form 24.2–C; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) orally, if the Court grants leave. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
