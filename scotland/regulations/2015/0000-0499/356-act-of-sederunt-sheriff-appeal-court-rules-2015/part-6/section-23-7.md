# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 23.7

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 23.7. Recording objections where recording of evidence dispensed with (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-75ff77266c55c1fce3072b3c7f450e0c][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

Where the recording of evidence has been dispensed with under rule 23.5(1), a party may request that the Appeal Sheriff record in the report of the proof— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) any objection taken to a question or to the line of evidence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) any submission made in relation to such an objection; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) the ruling of the Appeal Sheriff in relation to the objection and submission. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
