# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 23.6

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 23.6. Transcripts of evidence (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-42e702624d6d2d6034efb7d874869b5e][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A transcript of the record of the evidence is to be made only where the Appeal Sheriff orders it to be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) In the first instance, the solicitors for the parties are personally liable, in equal shares, for the cost of making the transcript. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The transcript provided for the use of the Court is to be certified as a faithful record of the evidence by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the shorthand writer who recorded the evidence; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) where the evidence was recorded by tape recording or other mechanical means, by the person who transcribed the record. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The Appeal Sheriff may alter the transcript where the Appeal Sheriff considers it necessary to do so, but only after hearing parties on the proposed alterations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Where the Appeal Sheriff alters the transcript, the Appeal Sheriff is to authenticate the alterations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) The transcript may only be borrowed from process on cause shown. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(7) Where a transcript is required for the purpose of an appeal but the Appeal Sheriff has not directed that it be made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the appellant may request a transcript from the shorthand writer or the person in whose possession the recording of the evidence is; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) in the first instance, the solicitor for the appellant is liable for the cost of the transcript; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) the appellant must lodge the transcript in process; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) any party may obtain a copy by paying the fee of the person who made the transcript. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
