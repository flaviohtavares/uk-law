# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 21.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 21.3. Preparation of reference (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-689d99b3f3ea94be29fe56848c85f3f3][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the Court decides that a reference is to be made, it is to make an order specifying— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) by whom the reference is to be drafted and adjusted; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the periods within which the reference is to be drafted and adjusted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) A reference is to be drafted in Form 21.3 unless the Court directs otherwise when it makes an order under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) In drafting and adjusting the reference, parties are to have regard to the Recommendations to national courts and tribunals in relation to the initiation of preliminary ruling proceedings issued by the European Court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00021].

(4) When the reference has been drafted and any adjustments required by the Court have been made, the Court is to make and sign the reference. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) When the reference is made, the Clerk must notify the parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
