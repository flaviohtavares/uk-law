# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 24.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 24.5. Intimation of orders (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ea354b2e84465f5939efc1c3d96c2c87][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the Court makes an order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) fixing a hearing under rule 24.3(2)(b); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) altering the date of a proof or other hearing under rule 24.3(3); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) under section 12(1) or (6) or 13(2) of the 2004 Act, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

the Clerk is to intimate the order in accordance with this rule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Intimation is to be given to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) every party to the proceedings; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) any other person named in the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Intimation is to be made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) on the day that the hearing is fixed or the order is made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) in the manner ordered by the Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
