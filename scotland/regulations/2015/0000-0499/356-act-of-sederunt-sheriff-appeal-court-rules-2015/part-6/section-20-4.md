# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 20.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 20.4. Raising a devolution issue: permission to proceed (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d5985480e59cab68243130ccf03b0aba][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) When a devolution issue is raised, the Clerk is to fix a hearing and intimate the date and time of that hearing to the parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Within 14 days after the Clerk intimates the date and time of the hearing, each party must lodge a note of argument. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) That note of argument must summarise the submissions the party intends to make on the question of whether a devolution issue arises in the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) At the hearing, the procedural Appeal Sheriff is to determine whether a devolution issue arises in the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Where the procedural Appeal Sheriff determines that a devolution issue arises, the procedural Appeal Sheriff is to grant permission for the devolution issue to proceed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) Where the procedural Appeal Sheriff determines that no devolution issue arises, the procedural Appeal Sheriff is to refuse permission for the devolution issue to proceed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(7) At the hearing the procedural Appeal Sheriff may make any order, including an order concerning expenses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(8) In this rule, “party” includes a relevant authority that has given notice to the Clerk that it intends to take part in the appeal as a party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
