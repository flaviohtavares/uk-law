# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 26.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 26.2. Interim orders (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a9f6851aecef499faa950f786deb9ac5][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-3b09f8b02b1fff51fa7dd9079fe9529d][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the Court is considering making an order, the Court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] must first[^key-9c28c0f7fd473778bc93df23f486d1d8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] make an interim order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) [^key-905a3590282c823a52af6b459fd9efd1]... The Clerk must immediately send a copy of the interim order to any interested person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The Court is to specify in the interim order why the Court is considering making an order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
