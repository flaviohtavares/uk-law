# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 25.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 25.2. Application for use of live link (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b7ed96a9c0fffd37f8f447211707bf58][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A party may apply to the Court to use a live link to make a submission or to give evidence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) An application to use a live link is to be made by motion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Where a party seeks to use a live link other than a live television link, the motion must specify the proposed arrangement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The Court must not grant a motion to use a live link other than a live television link unless the proposed arrangement meets the requirements in paragraph (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) The requirements are that the person using the live link is able to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) be seen and heard, or heard, in the courtroom; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) see and hear, or hear, the proceedings in the courtroom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
