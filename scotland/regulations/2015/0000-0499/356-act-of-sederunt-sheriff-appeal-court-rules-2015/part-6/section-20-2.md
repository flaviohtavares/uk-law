# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 20.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 20.2. Raising a devolution issue (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-94585bd47326a27283143fbc91b065aa][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A devolution issue is raised by specifying a devolution issue in Form 20.2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) A devolution issue in Form 20.2 is to be lodged— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) by an appellant, when the note of appeal is lodged; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) by a respondent, when answers to the note of appeal are lodged, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

unless the Court allows an appellant or a respondent to raise a devolution issue at a later stage in proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) An application to allow a devolution issue to be raised after the note of appeal has been lodged or answers to the note of appeal have been lodged, as the case may be, is to be made by motion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The party raising a devolution issue must specify, in sufficient detail to enable the Court to determine whether a devolution issue arises— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the facts and circumstances; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the contentions of law, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

on the basis of which it is alleged that the devolution issue arises in the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) The Court may not determine a devolution issue unless permission has been given for the devolution issue to proceed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
