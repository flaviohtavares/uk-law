# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 25.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 25.1. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-37027400b810358e25062ac52e01aecd][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

In this Chapter— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- “evidence” means the evidence of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (a) the party; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (b) a person who has been or may be cited to appear before the court as a witness; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “live link” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (a) a live television link; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (b) where the Court gives permission in accordance with rule 25.2(4), an alternative arrangement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “submission” means any oral submission which would otherwise be made to the Court by a party or that party’s representative, including an oral submission in support of a motion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
