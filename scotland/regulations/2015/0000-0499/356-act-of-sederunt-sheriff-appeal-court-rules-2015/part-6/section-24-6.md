# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 24.6

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 24.6. Taking of evidence by commissioner: preparatory steps (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f5938cc5ea60aa36c91bab0f5c7b48b9][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies where the Court authorises the special measure of taking evidence by a commissioner under section 19(1) of the 2004 Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The commission is *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] to[^key-4866021f0fa8360faeb3bbb014eadb69] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] proceed without interrogatories unless the Court otherwise orders. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The order of the Court authorising the special measure is sufficient authority for citing the vulnerable witness to appear before the commissioner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The party who cited the vulnerable witness— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) must give the commissioner— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) a certified copy of the order of the Court appointing the commissioner; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] a copy of the appeal documents; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] where rule 24.7 applies, the approved interrogatories and cross-interrogatories; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) must instruct the clerk to the commission; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) is responsible in the first instance for the fee of the commissioner and the clerk. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) The commissioner is to fix a hearing at which the commission will be carried out. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) The commissioner must consult the parties before fixing the hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(7) An application by a party for leave to be present in the room where the commission is carried out is to be made by motion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(8) In this rule, “appeal documents” means any of the following documents that have been lodged in process by the time the use of the special measure is authorised— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the note of appeal and answers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) where there is a cross appeal, the grounds of appeal and answers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) the appeal print and appendices; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) the notes of argument. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
