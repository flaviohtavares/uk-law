# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 22.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 22.4. Invitations to intervene (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-6a4c1c7443f73fc2c98e4f6765ad3ad2][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) An invitation to intervene under section 14(2)(b) of the Scottish Commission for Human Rights Act 2006 is to be in Form 22.4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The Clerk must send a copy of Form 22.4 to the parties to the proceedings and to the SCHR. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) When the Clerk sends a copy of Form 22.4 to the SCHR, the Clerk must also send— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) a copy of the note of appeal and any answers to it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the appeal print, if it is available; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) any other documents relating to the appeal that the Court thinks are relevant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Where the Court invites the SCHR to intervene, it may impose any conditions that it considers desirable in the interests of justice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) In particular, the Court may make provision about any additional expenses incurred by the parties as a result of the intervention. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
