## 21.5. Sist of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0fbfdeb9096a20fb28d1edba5f49850d][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) When a reference is made, the Court is to sist the appeal until the European Court determines the reference, unless the Court orders otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where an appeal is sisted under paragraph (1), the Court may recall the sist for the purposes of making an interim order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
