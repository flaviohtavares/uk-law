# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 26.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 26.3. Representations (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1e3ef5504550b57c6ee894740fba7710][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-d9a22f9eb0e87df182d9e9fff03381d0][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-99ada2a295487cd9a8546da79e5d7fcb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) An interested person who would be directly affected by the making of an order is to be given an opportunity to make representations to the Court before the order is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Representations are to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) be made in Form 26.3; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) include reasons why an urgent hearing is necessary, if an urgent hearing is sought; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) be lodged no later than 2 days after the interim order is sent to interested persons in accordance with rule 26.2(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) If representations are made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the Court is to appoint a date and time for a hearing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) on the first suitable court day; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] where the Court considers that an urgent hearing is necessary, at an earlier date and time; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the Clerk must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) notify the date and time of the hearing to the parties to the proceedings and any person who has made representations; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] send a copy of the representations to the parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Where no interested person makes representations in accordance with paragraph (3), the Clerk is to put the interim order before the Court in chambers in order that the Court may resume consideration of whether to make an order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) Where the Court, having resumed consideration, makes no order, it must recall the interim order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(7) Where the Court recalls an interim order, the Clerk must immediately notify any interested person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
