# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 19.4A

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 19.4A. Auditor’s statement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4f030326d646404578833f13ed5f2027][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The auditor of court must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) prepare a statement of the amount of expenses as taxed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) transmit the process, the taxed account and the statement to the clerk; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) on the day on which the documents referred to in sub-paragraph (b) are transmitted, intimate that fact and the date of the report to each party to whom the auditor intimated the diet of taxation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The party found entitled to expenses must, within 7 days after the date of receipt of intimation under paragraph (1)(c), send a copy of the taxed account to the party found liable in expenses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Where no objections are lodged under rule 19.5 (objections to taxed account), the court may grant decree for the expenses as taxed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
