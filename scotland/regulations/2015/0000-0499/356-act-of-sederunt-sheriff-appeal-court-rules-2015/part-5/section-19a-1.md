# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 19A.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 19A.1. Application and interpretation of this Chapter (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4b7265070c6c7d5b76bf816a0af410c4][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This Chapter applies in civil proceedings, where either or both— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) an application for an award of expenses is made to the Court; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) such an award is made by the Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where this Chapter applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) rules 9.1(4) and (5) (application to abandon appeal); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) any common law rule entitling a pursuer to abandon an appeal, to the extent that it concerns expenses, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

are disapplied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) In this Chapter— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- “the Act” means the Civil Litigation (Expenses and Group Proceedings) (Scotland) Act 2018; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “the applicant” has the meaning given in rule 19A.2(1), and “applicants” is construed accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “civil proceedings” means civil proceedings to which section 8 of the Act (restriction on pursuer’s liability for expenses in personal injury claims) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
