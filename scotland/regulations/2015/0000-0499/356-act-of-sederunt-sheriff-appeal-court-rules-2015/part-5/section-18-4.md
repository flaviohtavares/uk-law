# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 18.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 18.4. Methods of finding caution or giving security (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e88402e5cb04b8c136271a67319b1020][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person who is ordered to find caution must do so by obtaining a bond of caution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) A person who is ordered to consign a sum of money into court must do so by consignation under the Sheriff Courts Consignations (Scotland) Act 1893 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00010] in the name of the Clerk. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The Court may order a person to give security by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) a method other than those mentioned in paragraphs (1) and (2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) a combination of two or more methods of security. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Any document by which an order to find caution or give security is satisfied must be lodged in process. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) A document lodged under paragraph (4) may not be borrowed from process. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
