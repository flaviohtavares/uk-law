# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 12.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 12.4. Written motions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-8e7396918dd87946c9b29439b1d3c202][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A motion in writing is made by lodging it with the Clerk in accordance with Chapter 13 or Chapter 14. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Chapter 13 (motions lodged by email) applies where each party to an appeal has provided to the Clerk an email address for the purpose of transacting motion business. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Chapter 14 (motions lodged by other means) applies where a party to an appeal has not provided to the Clerk an email address for the purpose of transacting motion business. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
