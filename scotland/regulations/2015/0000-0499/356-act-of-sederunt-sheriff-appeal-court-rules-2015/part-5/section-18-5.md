# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 18.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 18.5. Cautioners and other guarantors (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b8d39ee06827be11e8fea63d541c2a28][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

A bond of caution or other security may only be given by a person who is an authorised person within the meaning of section 31 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00011].
