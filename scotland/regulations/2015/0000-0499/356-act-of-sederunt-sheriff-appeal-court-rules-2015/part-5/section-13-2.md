# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 13.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 13.2. Opposition to motions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e671c944b3c22d426172942d764e6a92][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A receiving party must intimate any opposition to a motion by sending an email in Form 13.2 (form of opposition to motion by email) to the address of the lodging party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Any opposition to a motion must be intimated to the lodging party not later than 1700 hours on court day 3. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Late opposition to a motion must be sent to the address of the Court and may only be allowed with the leave of the procedural Appeal Sheriff, on cause shown. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
