# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 19A.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 19A.2. Application for an award of expenses (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4b7265070c6c7d5b76bf816a0af410c4][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where civil proceedings have been brought by a pursuer, another party to the action (“the applicant”) may make an application to the Court for an award of expenses to be made against the pursuer, on one or more of the grounds specified in either or both— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) section 8(4)(a) to (c) of the Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) paragraph (2) of this rule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The grounds specified in this paragraph, which are exceptions to section 8(2) of the Act, are as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) failure by the pursuer to obtain an award of damages greater than the sum offered by way of a tender lodged in process; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) unreasonable delay on the part of the pursuer in accepting a sum offered by way of a tender lodged in process; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) abandonment of the appeal by the pursuer in terms of rule 9.1(1) to (3), or at common law. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
