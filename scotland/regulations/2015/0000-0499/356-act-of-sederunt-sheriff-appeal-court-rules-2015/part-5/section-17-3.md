# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 17.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 17.3. Arrangements for peremptory hearing (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a763b94e15ad3363c3343845ae3950f7][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) On the first available court day after notice is given under rule 17.2(1), the procedural Appeal Sheriff is to make an order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) ordaining the party whose solicitor has withdrawn from acting to appear or be represented at a peremptory hearing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) fixing a date and time for the peremptory hearing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) appointing any other party to the appeal to intimate the order and a notice in Form 17.3 to that party within 7 days after the date of the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) A peremptory diet is to be fixed no sooner than 14 days after the date on which an order is made under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The procedural Appeal Sheriff may vary the period of 7 days mentioned in paragraph (1) or the period of 14 days mentioned in paragraph (2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) of the procedural Appeal Sheriff’s own accord; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) on cause shown, on the application of any other party to the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Where any previously fixed hearing is to occur within 14 days after the date on which the procedural Appeal Sheriff is to make an order under paragraph (1), the procedural Appeal Sheriff may continue consideration of the matter to the previously fixed hearing instead of making an order under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Where an order and a notice in Form 17.3 are intimated under this rule, the party appointed to intimate them must lodge a certificate of intimation in Form 6.5— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) within 14 days after the date of intimation; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) before the peremptory hearing, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

whichever is sooner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
