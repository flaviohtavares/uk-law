# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 12.7

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 12.7. Determination of unopposed motions in writing (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0a46c45ef93ec99e442c9a82378b3f47][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Clerk may determine any unopposed motion in writing other than a motion which seeks a final interlocutor. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where the Clerk considers that such a motion should not be granted, the Clerk must refer the motion to the procedural Appeal Sheriff. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The procedural Appeal Sheriff is to determine— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) a motion referred under paragraph (2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) an unopposed motion which seeks a final interlocutor, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

in chambers without the appearance of parties, unless the procedural Appeal Sheriff otherwise determines. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
