## 12.2. Making of motions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d6263a6414a99a52a3394fa3126c5dbb][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

A motion may be made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) orally, in accordance with rule 12.3; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
