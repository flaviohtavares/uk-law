# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 17.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 17.2. Giving notice of withdrawal to the Court (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-75ab8600e5fa6497c9df2066b85123e6][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a solicitor withdraws from acting on behalf of a party, the solicitor must give notice in writing to the Clerk and to every other party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply if the solicitor withdraws from acting at a hearing in the presence of the other parties or their representatives. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Paragraph (4) applies if a solicitor who withdraws from acting is aware that the address of the party for whom the solicitor acted has changed from that specified in the instance of the note of appeal or answers to the note of appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The solicitor must disclose to the Clerk and every other party the last known address of the party for whom the solicitor acted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
