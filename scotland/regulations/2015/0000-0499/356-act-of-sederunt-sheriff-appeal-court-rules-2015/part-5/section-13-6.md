# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 13.6

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 13.6. Variation of periods of intimation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-cc25518883ad40f9dae80c771d9b6917][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) every receiving party in an appeal consents to a shorter period of intimation; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the Court shortens the period of intimation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

the motion may be lodged by the lodging party, or heard or otherwise determined by the Court at an earlier time and date than that which is specified in this Chapter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
