# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 12.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 12.5. Provision of email addresses to the Clerk (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-25c3c6f6c48b182412d68f0bf37ddbb0][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A solicitor representing a party in an appeal must provide to the Clerk an email address for the purpose of transacting motion business. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) A solicitor who does not have suitable facilities for transacting motion business by email may make a declaration in writing to that effect, which must be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) sent to the Clerk; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) intimated to each of the other parties to the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) A party who is not represented by a solicitor may provide to the Clerk an email address for the purpose of transacting motion business. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The Clerk must maintain a list of the email addresses provided for the purpose of transacting motion business, which must be published in up to date form on the website of the Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) The Clerk must also include on that list an email address of the Court for the purpose of lodging motions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
