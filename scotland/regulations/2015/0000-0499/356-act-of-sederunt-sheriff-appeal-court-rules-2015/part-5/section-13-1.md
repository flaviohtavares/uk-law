# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 13.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 13.1. Intimation of motions by email (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6a3daea56cb5424e4c0b640cb32349f4][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The lodging party must give intimation of that party’s intention to lodge the motion, and of the terms of the motion, to every other party by sending an email in Form 13.1 (form of motion by email) to the addresses of every party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The requirement under paragraph (1) to give intimation of a motion to a party by email does not apply where that party— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) has not lodged answers within the period of notice for lodging those answers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) has withdrawn or is deemed to have withdrawn those answers; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) became a party to the appeal by minute, but has withdrawn or is deemed to have withdrawn that minute. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) A motion intimated under this rule must be intimated not later than 1700 hours on a court day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
