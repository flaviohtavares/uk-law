# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 19A.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 19A.3. Award of expenses (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4b7265070c6c7d5b76bf816a0af410c4][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the determination of an application made under rule 19A.2(1) is at the discretion of the court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where, having determined an application made under rule 19A.2(1), the Court makes an award of expenses against the pursuer on the ground specified in rule *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] 19A.2(2)(a) or (b)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the pursuer’s liability is not to exceed the amount of expenses the applicant has incurred after the date of the tender; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the liability of the pursuer to the applicant, or applicants, who lodged the tender is to be limited to an aggregate sum, payable to all applicants (if more than one) of 75% of the amount of damages awarded to the pursuer and that sum is to be calculated without offsetting against those expenses any expenses due to the pursuer by the applicant, or applicants, before the date of the tender; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) the Court must order that the pursuer’s liability is not to exceed the sum referred to in sub-paragraph (b), notwithstanding that any sum assessed by the Auditor of Court as payable under the tender procedure may be greater or, if modifying those expenses to a fixed sum in terms of rule 19.1(2) (taxation of expenses), that such sum does not exceed that referred to in sub-paragraph (b); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) where the award of expenses is in favour of more than one applicant the Court, failing agreement between the applicants, is to apportion the award of expenses recoverable under the tender procedure between them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) In the event that the Court makes an award of expenses against the pursuer on the ground specified in rule 19A.2(2)(c), the Court may make such orders in respect of expenses, subject to such conditions if any, as it considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
