# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 18.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.

## 18.1. Application of this Chapter (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4964e20f4573b781efb2ca840f25639f][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

This Chapter applies to any appeal in which the Court has power to order a person to find caution or give other security. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
