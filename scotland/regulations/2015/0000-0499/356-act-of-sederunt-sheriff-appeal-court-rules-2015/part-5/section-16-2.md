# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 16.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 16.2. Amendment of note of appeal and answers etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4a747578d33853d527883af806c82c0c][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A party who has lodged a document specified in paragraph (2) may apply by motion to amend that document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The documents are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) a note of appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) answers to a note of appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) grounds of appeal in a cross-appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) answers to grounds of appeal in a cross-appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Such a motion must include the text of the proposed amendment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) An application under paragraph (1) is to be accompanied by an application to vary the timetable under rule 7.6(1)(c) or rule 28.6(1)(c) (sist of proceedings and variation of timetable) if such an application is necessary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
