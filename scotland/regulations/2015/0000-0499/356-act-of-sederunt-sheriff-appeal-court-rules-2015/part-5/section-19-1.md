# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 19.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 19.1. Taxation of expenses (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-fe0b282df9fb8bb216abb3a5d53327b9][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the Court makes an order allowing expenses in any appeal, those expenses must be taxed before decree is granted for them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) This rule does not apply where the Court modifies those expenses to a fixed sum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
