# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 19.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 19.5. Objections to taxed account (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4f030326d646404578833f13ed5f2027][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A party to an appeal who has appeared or been represented at a diet of taxation may object to the auditor of court’s statement by lodging in process a note of objection within 14 days after the date of the statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The party lodging a note of objection is referred to in this rule as “the objecting party”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) On lodging the note of objection the objecting party must apply by motion for an order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) allowing the note to be received; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) allowing a hearing on the note of objection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) On the granting of the order mentioned in paragraph (3), the objecting party must intimate to the auditor of court— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the note of objection; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the interlocutor containing the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Within 14 days after receipt of intimation of the items mentioned in paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] (4) the auditor of court must lodge in process a statement of reasons in the form of a minute stating the reasons for the auditor’s decision in relation to the items to which objection is taken in the note. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) On the lodging of the statement of reasons the clerk must fix a hearing on the note of objection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(7) At the hearing, the court may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) sustain or repel any objection in the note of objection or remit the account of expenses to the auditor of court for further consideration; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) find any party liable in the expenses of the procedure on the note of objection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
