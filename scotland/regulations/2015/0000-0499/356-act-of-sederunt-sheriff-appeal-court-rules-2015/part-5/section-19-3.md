# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 19.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 19.3. Time for lodging account of expenses (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f6cec501f17cb170594810f31515d242][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A party found entitled to expenses must lodge an account of expenses in process— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) not later than 4 months after the final judgment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) at any time with permission of the court, but subject to such conditions, if any, as the court thinks fit to impose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where an account of expenses is lodged by the Scottish Legal Aid Board in reliance on regulation 39(2)(a) of the Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) Regulations 2002, paragraph *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] (1)(a) applies as if the period specified there is 8 months. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) In this rule, “final judgment” has the meaning assigned by section 136(1) of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
