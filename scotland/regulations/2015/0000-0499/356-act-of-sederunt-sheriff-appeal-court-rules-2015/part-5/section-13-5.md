# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 13.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 13.5. Lodging opposed motions by email (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ad2c6d758f8a8106ef44b2c49ca85e38][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies where opposition to a motion has been intimated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The motion must be lodged by the lodging party not later than 1230 hours on court day 4 by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) sending an email in Form 13.1 headed “Opposed motion”, to the address of the court; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) attaching to that email the opposition in Form 13.2 intimated by the receiving party to the lodging party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) That motion is to be heard by the procedural Appeal Sheriff on the first suitable court day after court day 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The Clerk must intimate the date and time of the hearing to the parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
