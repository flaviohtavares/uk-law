# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 14.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 14.5. Joint motions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9c4525bd88bf03e4203907f8f6544d3d][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A joint motion by all parties need not be intimated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Such a motion is to be lodged by any of the parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
