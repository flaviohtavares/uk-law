# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 14.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 14.1. Intimation of motions by other means (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d56ef3c61558bf902fd3ce37a723bc01][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The lodging party must give intimation of that party’s intention to lodge the motion, and of the terms of the motion, to every other party in Form 14.1 (form of motion). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) That intimation must be accompanied by a copy of any document referred to in the motion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
