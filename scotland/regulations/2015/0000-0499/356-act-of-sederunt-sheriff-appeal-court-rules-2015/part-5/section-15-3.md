# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 15.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 15.3. Orders for intimation and answers (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9e4127653aa5794e6a9c712d0bd371b3][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) On the first available court day after being lodged, a minute is to be brought before the procedural Appeal Sheriff for an order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) for intimation, within 7 days after the date of the order, to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) every other party to the appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] any other person who appears to have an interest in the minute; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) for any person on whom the minute is intimated to lodge answers, if so advised, within 14 days after the date of intimation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) fixing a hearing on the minute and any answers no sooner than 28 days after the date of the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The procedural Appeal Sheriff may vary the periods of 7 days, 14 days and 28 days mentioned in paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) of the procedural Appeal Sheriff’s own accord; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) on cause shown, on the application of the applicant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) An application mentioned in paragraph (2)(b) must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) be included in the minute; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) give reasons for varying the period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Where a minute is intimated in accordance with an order under this rule, the applicant must lodge a certificate of intimation in Form 6.5 within 14 days after the date of intimation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
