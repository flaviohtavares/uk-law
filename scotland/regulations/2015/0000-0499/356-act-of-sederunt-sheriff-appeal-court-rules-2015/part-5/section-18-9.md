# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 18.9

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 18.9. Failure to find caution or give security (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-29a470cf8f5595da00ff31d5aed39706][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a person who has been ordered to find caution or give security fails to do so, any other party may apply to the Court by motion for a finding that the person is in default. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Despite rule 3.1 (circumstances where a party is in default), a person who fails to find caution or give security is only in default if the Court grants a motion under paragraph (1) and makes a finding that the person is in default. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
