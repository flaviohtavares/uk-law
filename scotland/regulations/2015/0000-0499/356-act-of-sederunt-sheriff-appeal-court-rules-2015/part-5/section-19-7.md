# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 19.7

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 19.7. Expenses of curator (affected by repeal, see note) ad litem (affected by repeal, see note) appointed to a respondent (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-bc7d74d633b44850f0e2865b219d6862][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies where a curator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] *ad litem *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]* is appointed to any respondent to an appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The appellant is responsible in the first instance for the payment of the expenses of a curator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] *ad litem *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]* mentioned in paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Those expenses are any fees of the curator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] *ad litem *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]* and any outlays incurred by the curator from the date of appointment until any of the following steps occur— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the lodging of a minute stating that the curator does not intend to lodge answers to the note of appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the lodging of answers by the curator, or the adoption of answers that have already been lodged; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) the discharge of the curator before either of the steps in subparagraphs (a) or (b) occurs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
