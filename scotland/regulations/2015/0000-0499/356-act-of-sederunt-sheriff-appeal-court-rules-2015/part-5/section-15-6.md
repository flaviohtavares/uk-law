# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 15.6

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 15.6. Applications to enter process as respondent (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c4842f9bdc34973769a33c67e1e645c6][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person on whom the appeal has not been intimated may apply by minute for leave to enter the process as a party minuter and lodge answers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) A minute under paragraph (1) must specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the applicant’s title and interest to enter the process; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the basis for the answers that the applicant proposes to lodge. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) At the hearing fixed under rule 15.3(1)(c), the procedural Appeal Sheriff is to determine whether the applicant has shown title and interest to enter the process. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) If the procedural Appeal Sheriff is satisfied, the procedural Appeal Sheriff may grant the applicant leave to enter the process and lodge answers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Where leave is granted, the procedural Appeal Sheriff is to make such further order as the procedural Appeal Sheriff thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) In particular, such an order may include an order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) varying any timetable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) as to the expenses of the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
