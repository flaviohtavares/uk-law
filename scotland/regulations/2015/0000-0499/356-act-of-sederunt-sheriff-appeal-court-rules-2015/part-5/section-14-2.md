# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 14.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 14.2. Opposition to motions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e94ff4a3f9e4894897f42cfd899df84f][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A receiving party may oppose a motion by lodging a notice of opposition in Form 14.2 (form of opposition to motion). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Any notice of opposition must be lodged within 7 days after the date of intimation of the motion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The procedural Appeal Sheriff may, on the application of the lodging party— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) vary the period of 7 days mentioned in paragraph (2); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) dispense with intimation on any party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) An application mentioned in paragraph (3) must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) be included in the motion; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) give reasons for varying the period or dispensing with intimation, as the case may be. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) The procedural Appeal Sheriff may allow a notice of opposition to be lodged late, on cause shown. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
