# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 17.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 17.1. Interpretation of this Chapter (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-fca8ed8164f4489a9428afc4aeef84d2][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

In this Chapter, “peremptory hearing” means a hearing at which a party whose solicitor has withdrawn from acting must appear or be represented in order to state whether or not the party intends to proceed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
