# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 19A.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 19A.5. Award against legal representatives (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4b7265070c6c7d5b76bf816a0af410c4][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

Section 8(2) of the Act does not prevent the Court from making an award of expenses against a pursuer’s legal representative in terms of section 11 (awards of expenses against legal representatives) of the Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
