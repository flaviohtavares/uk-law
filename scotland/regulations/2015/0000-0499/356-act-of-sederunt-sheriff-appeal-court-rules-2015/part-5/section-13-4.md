# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 13.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 13.4. Lodging unopposed motions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e088fcea0f2db198535221df0e508d1f][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies where no opposition to a motion has been intimated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The motion must be lodged by the lodging party not later than 1230 hours on court day 4 by sending an email in Form 13.1 headed “Unopposed motion” to the address of the court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) That motion is to be determined by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] 1700 hours[^c-9bb3ab2784c4351ae577c02b96fab3b1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] on court day 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Where for any reason it is not possible for that motion to be determined in accordance with paragraph (3), the Clerk must advise the parties of that fact and give reasons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
