# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 19.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 19.4. Diet of taxation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4f030326d646404578833f13ed5f2027][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where an account of expenses is lodged for taxation, the clerk must transmit the account and the process to the auditor of court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), the auditor of court must fix a diet of taxation on receipt of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the account of expenses; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the process; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) vouchers in respect of all outlays claimed in the account, including counsel’s fees; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) a letter addressed to the auditor of court— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) confirming that the items referred to in sub-paragraph (c) have been intimated to the party found liable in expenses; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] providing such information as is required to enable the auditor of court to give intimation to the party found liable in expenses in accordance with paragraph (4)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The auditor of court may fix a diet of taxation where paragraph (2)(c) or (d), or both, have not been complied with. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The auditor of court must intimate the diet of taxation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the party found entitled to expenses; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the party found liable in expenses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) The party found liable in expenses must, not later than 4.00 pm on the fourth business day before the diet of taxation, intimate to the auditor of court and to the party found entitled to expenses particular points of objection, specifying each item objected to and stating concisely the nature and ground of objection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) Subject to paragraph (7), if the party found liable in expenses fails to intimate points of objection under paragraph (5) within the time limit set out there, the auditor of court must not take account of them at the diet of taxation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(7) Where a failure to comply with the requirement contained in paragraph (5) was due to mistake, oversight or other excusable cause, the auditor of court may relieve a party of the consequences of such failure on such conditions, if any, as the auditor thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(8) At the diet of taxation, or within such reasonable period of time thereafter as the auditor of court may allow, the party found entitled to expenses must make available to the auditor of court all documents, drafts or copies of documents sought by the auditor and relevant to the taxation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(9) In this rule, a “business day” means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public or court holiday. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
