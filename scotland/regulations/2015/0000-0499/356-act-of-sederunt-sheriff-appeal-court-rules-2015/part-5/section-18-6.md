# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 18.6

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 18.6. Form of bond of caution (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-94a8f082ef1ced44fe20dae67260486e][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A bond of caution must oblige the cautioner to make payment of the sums as validly and in the same manner as the party is obliged. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) In this rule— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- “cautioner” includes the cautioner’s heirs and executors; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “party” means the person to whom the cautioner is bound, and that person’s heirs and successors; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “the sums” are the sums for which the cautioner is bound to the party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
