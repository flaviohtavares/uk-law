# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 18.8

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 18.8. Insolvency or death of cautioner or guarantor (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9ccb0c48dc8232f2dbf8928928b8b9c1][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies where caution has been found by bond of caution or security has been given by guarantee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where one of the events specified in paragraph (3) occurs, the party entitled to benefit from the caution or guarantee may apply to the Court by motion for further caution to be found or further security to be given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The events are that the cautioner or guarantor— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) becomes apparently insolvent within the meaning of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] section 16 of the Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 2016[^c-64b441675fe3122bc111a74470afe6f2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950];
- (b) calls a meeting of the cautioner or guarantor’s creditors to consider the state of that person’s affairs; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) dies unrepresented; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) is a company and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) an administration, bank administration or building society special administration order has been made in respect of it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] a winding up, bank insolvency or building society insolvency order has been made in respect of it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] a resolution for its voluntary winding up has been passed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] a receiver of all or any part of its undertaking has been appointed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (v) a voluntary arrangement within the meaning of section 1(1) of the Insolvency Act 1986 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00013] has been approved under Part I of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
