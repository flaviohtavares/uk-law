> Text as at 2023-10-02.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 14.3. Consent to motions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-fd22688cdb9e800dd8891e6e151ece29][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

Where a receiving party seeks to consent to a motion, that party may do so by lodging a notice to that effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
