# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 14.6

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 14.6. Hearing of opposed motions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a380e014863545fb707187df07484e22][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a notice of opposition in Form 14.2 (form of opposition to motion) is lodged, the motion is to be heard by the procedural Appeal Sheriff on the first suitable court day after the lodging of the notice of opposition. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The Clerk must intimate the date and time of the hearing to the parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
