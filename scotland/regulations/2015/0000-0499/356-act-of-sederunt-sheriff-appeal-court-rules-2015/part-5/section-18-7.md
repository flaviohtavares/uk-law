# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 18.7

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 18.7. Caution or other security: sufficiency and objections (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-743827efa3ecabbcee53a0d576f8ac79][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Clerk must be satisfied that any document lodged in process under rule 18.4(4) is in proper form. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) A party who is dissatisfied with the sufficiency or form of any document lodged in process under rule 18.4(4) may apply to the Court by motion for an order under rule 18.9 (failure to find caution or give security). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
