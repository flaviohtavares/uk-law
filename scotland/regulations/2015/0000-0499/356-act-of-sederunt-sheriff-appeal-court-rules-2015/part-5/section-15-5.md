# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 15.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 15.5. Minutes of sist and transference (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-356636c6e2e6b71dfc110ab150bfcdf5][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies where a party to an appeal (“P”) dies or comes under legal incapacity while the appeal is depending before the Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Any person who claims to represent P or P’s estate may apply to the Court by minute to be sisted as a party to the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) If no person makes an application under paragraph (2), any other party may apply to the Court by minute to transfer the appeal in favour of or against (as the case may be) the person who represents P or P’s estate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) An application under paragraph (3) must be intimated to the person specified in the minute as representing P or P’s estate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
