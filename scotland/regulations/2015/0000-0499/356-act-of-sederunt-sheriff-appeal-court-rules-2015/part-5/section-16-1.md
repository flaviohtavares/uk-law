# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 16.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 16.1. Amendment of sheriff court pleadings (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-eeabf94c2ef3055137bd35a7e8678076][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Any party to an appeal may apply by motion to amend the pleadings in the sheriff court process. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where the procedural Appeal Sheriff— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) allows an amendment to the pleadings in the sheriff court process; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) considers that the amendment makes a material change to the pleadings, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

the procedural Appeal Sheriff may set aside the decision appealed against and remit the matter to the sheriff for a further hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
