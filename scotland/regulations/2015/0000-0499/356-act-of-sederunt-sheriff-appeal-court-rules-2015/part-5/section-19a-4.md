# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 19A.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 19A.4. Procedure (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4b7265070c6c7d5b76bf816a0af410c4][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) An application under rule 19A.2(1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) must be made by motion, in writing, and Chapters 12 (motions: general), 13 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] (motions lodged by email) and 14 (motions lodged by other means) otherwise apply to motions made under this Chapter; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) may be made at any stage in the case prior to the granting of an order disposing of the expenses of the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where an application under rule 19A.2(1) is made, the Court may make such orders as it thinks fit for dealing with the application, including an order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) requiring the applicant to intimate the application to any other person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) requiring any party to lodge a written response; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) requiring the lodging of any document; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) fixing a hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
