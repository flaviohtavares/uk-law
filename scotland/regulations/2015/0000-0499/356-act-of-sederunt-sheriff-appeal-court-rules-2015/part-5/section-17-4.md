# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 17.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 17.4. Peremptory hearing (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bf4b60955e88c0e5f7cbb03b9c4dab65][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) At a peremptory hearing, the party whose solicitor has withdrawn from acting must appear or be represented in order to state whether the party intends to proceed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where the party fails to comply with paragraph (1), the Court may make an order mentioned in paragraph (3) only if it is satisfied that the order and notice in Form 17.3 have been intimated to that party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The orders are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) if the party is the appellant, an order refusing the appeal; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) if the party is the respondent and the condition in paragraph (4) is satisfied, an order allowing the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The condition is that the appellant must show cause why the appeal should be allowed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) If the Court is not satisfied that the order and notice in Form 17.3 have been intimated to that party, it may make— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) an order fixing a further peremptory hearing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) any other order that the Court considers appropriate to secure the expeditious disposal of the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
