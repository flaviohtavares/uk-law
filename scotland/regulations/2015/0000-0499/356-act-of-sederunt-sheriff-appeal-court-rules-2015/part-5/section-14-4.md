# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 14.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 14.4. Lodging of motions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-afaccaef7b5771a437006759601e67f6][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The motion must be lodged by the lodging party within 5 days after the date of intimation of the motion, unless paragraph (3) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The lodging party must also lodge— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) a certificate of intimation in Form 6.5 (certificate of intimation); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) so far as practicable, any document referred to in the motion that has not already been lodged. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Where the procedural Appeal Sheriff varies the period for lodging a notice of opposition to a period of 5 days or less, the motion must be lodged no later than the day on which that period expires. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
