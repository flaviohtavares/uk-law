# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 12.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 12.1. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d251a79e739fe41042e0580e5e09247b][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) In this Chapter, Chapter 13 and Chapter 14— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- “court day” means a day on which the office of the Clerk is open; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “court day 1” means the court day on which a motion is treated as being intimated under rule 13.1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “court day 3” means the second court day after court day 1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “court day 4” means the third court day after court day 1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “lodging party” means the party lodging the motion; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “receiving party” means a party receiving the intimation of the motion from the lodging party; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- “transacting motion business” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (a) intimating and lodging motions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (b) receiving intimation of motions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (c) intimating consent or opposition to motions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (d) receiving intimation of opposition to motions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) In this Chapter and Chapter 13, a reference to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the address of a party is a reference to the email address included in the list maintained under rule 12.5(4) of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) that party’s solicitor; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] that party; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the address of the court is a reference to the email address of the court included in that list under rule 12.5(5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
