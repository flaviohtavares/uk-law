# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 12.8

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 12.8. Issuing of orders by email (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-73725ff608a1347d534444876d0eee29][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

Where the Court makes an order determining a motion which was lodged in accordance with Chapter 13, the Clerk must email a copy of the order to the addresses of the lodging party and every receiving party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
