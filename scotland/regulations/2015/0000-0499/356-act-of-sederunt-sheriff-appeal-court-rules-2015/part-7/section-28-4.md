# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 28.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28.4. Applications out of time (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6d357d5e0af534875ddece75005b4d08][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) An application to allow an application for a new trial to be lodged outwith the period specified in rule 28.2(2) is to be included in the application made under rule 28.2(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where the procedural Appeal Sheriff allows such an application, the application for a new trial is to be received on such conditions as to expenses or otherwise as the procedural Appeal Sheriff thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
