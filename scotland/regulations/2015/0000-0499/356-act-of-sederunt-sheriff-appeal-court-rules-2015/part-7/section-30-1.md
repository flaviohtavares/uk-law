# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 30.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 30.1. Application and interpretation of this Chapter (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e6655380263e87921c1eec92bb906390][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This Chapter applies to an appeal by stated case under section 163(1), 164(1), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] [^c-cadfb91c99c7d3575e7fe81d94530431]... 165(1) and 167(1) of the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00029].

(2) In this Chapter, “parties” means the parties specified in rule 3.59(2) of the Act of Sederunt (Child Care and Maintenance Rules) 1997 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00030].
