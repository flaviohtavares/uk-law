# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 28.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28.2. Form of application for new trial (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9a70ab4b557ef1f354d079235736c387][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) An application for a new trial is to be made in Form 28.2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Such an application must be made within 7 days after the date on which the jury have returned their verdict. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] application[^key-671f5a1ead18104c9ebea5ec876597ec] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] must specify the grounds on which the application is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) When an application for a new trial is lodged, the party lodging it must also lodge— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) a print containing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) the pleadings in the sheriff court process; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] the interlocutors in the sheriff court process; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] the issues and counter-issues; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the verdict of the jury; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) any exception and the determination on it of the sheriff presiding at the trial; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) a process made up in accordance with paragraph 4 of Schedule 1 (form of process). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
