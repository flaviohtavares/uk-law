# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 30.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 30.4. Determination of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9b4bd12dd495a0b6783e78b154748579][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) At the conclusion of the hearing, the Court may either give its decision orally or reserve judgment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where the Court reserves judgment, it must give its decision in writing within 28 days. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
