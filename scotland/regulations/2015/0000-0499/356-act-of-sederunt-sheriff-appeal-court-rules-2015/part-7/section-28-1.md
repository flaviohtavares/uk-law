# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 28.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28.1. Application of this Chapter (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-fd77693e9fe19759d70fde4b03f9b862][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

This Chapter applies to an application— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) for a new trial under section 69(1) of the 2014 Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) to enter a verdict under section 71(2) of the 2014 Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
