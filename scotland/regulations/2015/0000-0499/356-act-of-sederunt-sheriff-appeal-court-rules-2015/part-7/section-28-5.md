# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 28.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28.5. Timetable in application for new trial (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1f65223006ede8db1547b7043dc85127][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Clerk must issue a timetable in Form 28.5 when an application is lodged under rule 28.2(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) When the Clerk issues a timetable, the Clerk must also fix a procedural hearing to take place after completion of the procedural steps specified in paragraph (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The timetable specifies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the dates by which parties must comply with those procedural steps; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the date and time of the procedural hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The procedural steps are the steps mentioned in the first column of the following table, provision in respect of which is found in the rule mentioned in the second column— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

| Procedural step *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] | Rule *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] |
| --- | --- |
| Referral of question about competency of application *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] | 28.7(3) |
| Lodging of appendices to print *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] | 28.9(1) |
| Giving notice that the applicant considers appendix unnecessary *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] | 28.10(1) |
| Lodging of notes of argument *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] | 28.11(1) |
| Lodging of estimates of duration of hearing of application for new trial *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] | 28.12 |
