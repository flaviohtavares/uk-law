# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 31.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 31.3. Hearing of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-728ff1477da80e4e04823f1cd43efcc6][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) On receipt of the appeal, the Clerk must fix forthwith a hearing to take place within 3 working days (within the meaning of section 76 of the Age of Criminal Responsibility (Scotland) Act 2019) and intimate the date, time and place of that hearing to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the constable who applied for the order to which the decision relates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the child or person acting on behalf of the child to whom the decision relates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) any other person the Court considers has an interest in the proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
