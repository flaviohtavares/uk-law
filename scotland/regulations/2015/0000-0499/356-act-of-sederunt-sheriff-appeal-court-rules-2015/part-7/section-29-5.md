# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 29.5

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 29.5. Determination of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e0fea4ef25feaf8c21167b8ce9b4d8a3][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) At the conclusion of the hearing, the Court may either give its decision orally or reserve judgment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where the Court reserves judgment, it must give its decision in writing within 28 days. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The Court may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) adhere to or vary the decision appealed against; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) recall the decision and substitute another decision for it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) remit the matter to the sheriff for further procedure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The Court may not remit the matter to the sheriff in order that further evidence may be led. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
