# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 28.11

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28.11. Notes of argument (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-5a800053e7dc07a16e3eb24e3f90f876][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The parties must lodge notes of argument no later than 7 days before the procedural hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) A note of argument must summarise briefly the submissions the party intends to develop at the hearing of the application for a new trial. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) A note of argument must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) state, in brief numbered paragraphs, the points that the party intends to make; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) after each point, identify by means of a page or paragraph reference the relevant passage in any notes of evidence or other document on which the party relies in support of the point; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) for every authority that is cited— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) state the proposition of law that the authority demonstrates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] identify the page or paragraph references for the parts of the authority that support the proposition; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) cite only one authority for each proposition of law, unless additional citation is necessary for a proper presentation of the argument. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Where a note of argument has been lodged and the party lodging it subsequently becomes aware that an argument in the note is not to be insisted upon, that party must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) give written notice of that fact to the Clerk; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) intimate that notice to every other party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Where a party wishes to advance an argument at a hearing that is not contained in that party’s note of argument, the party must apply by motion for leave to advance the argument. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
