# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 28.12

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28.12. Estimates of duration of hearing of application for new trial (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-96decc9a95653153fe81020b34e3ad45][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

The parties must lodge estimates of the duration of any hearing required to dispose of the application for a new trial in Form 28.12 not later than 7 days before the procedural hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
