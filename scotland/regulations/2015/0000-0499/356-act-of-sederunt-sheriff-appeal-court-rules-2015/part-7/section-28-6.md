# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 28.6

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28.6. Sist of application for new trial and variation of timetable (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-06497c710c68cfd71bd9a2fa7d6a2788][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Any party may apply by motion to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) sist the application for a new trial for a specified period; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) recall a sist; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) vary the timetable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) An application is to be determined by the procedural Appeal Sheriff. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) An application to sist the application for a new trial or to vary the timetable may only be granted on special cause shown. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The procedural Appeal Sheriff may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) grant the application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) refuse the application; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) make an order not sought in the application, where the procedural Appeal Sheriff considers that doing so would secure the expeditious disposal of the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Where the procedural Appeal Sheriff makes an order sisting the application for a new trial, the Clerk is to discharge the procedural hearing fixed under rule 28.5(2) (timetable: fixing procedural hearing). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) When a sist is recalled or expires, the Clerk is to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) issue a revised timetable in Form 28.5; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) fix a procedural hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(7) Where the procedural Appeal Sheriff makes an order varying the timetable, the Clerk is to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) discharge the procedural hearing fixed under rule 28.5(2) (timetable: fixing procedural hearing); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) issue a revised timetable in Form 28.5; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) fix a procedural hearing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
