# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 28.9

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28.9. Appendices to print: contents (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0b349ff40ad537f85e4988de3a45f1d1][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The applicant must lodge an appendix to the print mentioned in rule 28.2(4)(a) no later than 7 days before the procedural hearing, unless rule 28.10(1) (giving notice that applicant considers appendix unnecessary) is complied with. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The appendix is to contain— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) any document lodged in the sheriff court process that is founded upon in the application for a new trial; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the notes of evidence from the trial, if it is sought to submit them for consideration by the Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Where the sheriff’s note has not been included in the print and it subsequently becomes available, the applicant must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) include it in the appendix where the appendix has not yet been lodged; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) lodge a supplementary appendix containing the sheriff’s note. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The parties must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) discuss the contents of the appendix; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) so far as possible, co-operate in making up the appendix. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
