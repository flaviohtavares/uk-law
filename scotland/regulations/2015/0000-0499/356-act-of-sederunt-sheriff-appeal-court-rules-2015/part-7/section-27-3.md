# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 27.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 27.3. Application to remove appeal from accelerated appeal procedure (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1b35e61d41efb2bcf47c1d920e2207ee][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The procedural Appeal Sheriff may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) of the procedural Appeal Sheriff’s own accord; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) on the application of any party, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

order that an appeal is to proceed under the standard appeal procedure instead of the accelerated appeal procedure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(1A)[^key-7aaf400dce746a903487088d60a21440] An application is to be made by motion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The procedural Appeal Sheriff may only make such an order if the procedural Appeal Sheriff is satisfied that, taking into account the matters in rule 6.6(3), it is no longer appropriate for the appeal to proceed under the accelerated appeal procedure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) That order must appoint the appeal to proceed under the standard appeal procedure and specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the procedure to be followed in the appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the periods for complying with each procedural step. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
