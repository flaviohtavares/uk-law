# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 28.10

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28.10. Appendices to print considered unnecessary (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a9b9da9a1137b6d9132063280fea5608][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the applicant considers that it is not necessary to lodge an appendix, the applicant must, no later than 7 days before the procedural hearing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) give written notice of that fact to the Clerk; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) intimate that notice to every respondent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where the applicant complies with paragraph (1), the respondent may apply by motion for an order requiring the applicant to lodge an appendix. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) An application under paragraph (2) must specify the documents or notes of evidence that the respondent considers should be included in the appendix. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) In disposing of an application under paragraph (2), the procedural Appeal Sheriff may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) grant the application and make an order requiring the applicant to lodge an appendix; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) refuse the application and make an order requiring the respondent to lodge an appendix; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) refuse the application and make no order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Where the procedural Appeal Sheriff makes an order requiring the applicant or the respondent to lodge an appendix, that order must specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the documents or notes or evidence to be included in the appendix; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the time within which the appendix must be lodged. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
