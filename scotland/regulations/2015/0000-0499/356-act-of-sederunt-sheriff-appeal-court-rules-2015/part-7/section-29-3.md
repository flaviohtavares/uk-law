# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 29.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 29.3. Transmission of appeal: time to pay direction (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1eba4c6e2169fa11d528bc4f7b8cb62c][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Within 4 days after the sheriff states in writing the reasons for the sheriff’s original decision in accordance with rule 25.4(4) of the Summary Cause Rules 2002 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] [^key-f3332ddf1cb90912aee37ac294d3a9c0]..., the sheriff clerk must transmit to the Clerk— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the appeal in Form 33 of the Summary Cause Rules 2002 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] [^key-08d2c1bf5f3b1dd61e206eec6848f116]...;
- (b) the sheriff’s written reasons for the sheriff’s original decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) On receipt of those documents, the Clerk is to fix a hearing and intimate the date, time and place of that hearing to the parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
