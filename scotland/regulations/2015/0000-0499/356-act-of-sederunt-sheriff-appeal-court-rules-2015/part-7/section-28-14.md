# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 28.14

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28.14. Application to enter jury verdict (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a3660c17ab2cb4bb40eeafb3807b9d44][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies to an application under section 71(2) of the 2014 Act (verdict subject to opinion of the Court). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Such an application is to be made in Form 28.14. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) When *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] an application[^c-441da39546cd42bb822b77c94c5d13ba] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] is lodged, the party lodging it must also lodge— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) a print containing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) the pleadings in the sheriff court process; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] the interlocutors in the sheriff court process; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] the issues and counter-issues; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the verdict of the jury; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) any exception and the determination on it of the sheriff presiding at the trial; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) a process made up in accordance with paragraph 4 of Schedule 1 (form of process). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Unless the procedural Appeal Sheriff otherwise directs— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) it is not necessary for the purposes of such a motion to print the notes of evidence; but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the notes of the sheriff presiding at the trial may be produced at any time if required. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) The procedural Appeal Sheriff may refer an application referred to in paragraph (1) to the Court in cases of complexity or difficulty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
