# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 27.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 27.2. Hearing of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bade4269a67889895df28daaecdf813c][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

The Clerk must fix a hearing and intimate the date and time of that hearing to parties when— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) a provisional procedural order appointing the appeal to the accelerated appeal procedure becomes final or is confirmed; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the Court makes an order appointing the appeal to the accelerated appeal procedure under rule 6.7(5)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
