# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 28.7

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28.7. Questions about competency of application (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1b857563ce4f2e33824006e87cae6572][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) A question about the competency of an application for a new trial may be referred to the procedural Appeal Sheriff by a party, other than the applicant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) A question is referred by lodging a reference in Form 28.7. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) A question may be referred within 7 days after the date on which the application for a new trial was lodged. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Where a reference is lodged, the Clerk is to fix a hearing and intimate the time and date of that hearing to the parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Within 7 days after the date on which the reference is lodged, each party must lodge a note of argument. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) That note of argument must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) give fair notice of the submissions the party intends to make on the question of competency; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) comply with the requirements in rule 28.11(3) (notes of argument). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(7) Paragraphs (4) and (5) of rule 28.11 apply to a note of argument lodged under paragraph (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
