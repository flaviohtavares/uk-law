# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 28.3

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28.3. Application for new trial: restrictions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b2db8a06a8d3a487ef610134459a49d4][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) An application for a new trial which specifies the ground in section 69(2)(a) of the 2014 Act (misdirection by sheriff) may not be made unless the procedure in rule 36B.8 of the Ordinary Cause Rules 1993 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^f00025] (exceptions to sheriff’s charge) has been complied with. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) An application for a new trial which specifies the ground in section 69(2)(b) of the 2014 Act (undue admission or rejection of evidence) may not be made unless objection was taken to the admission or rejection of evidence at the trial and recorded in the notes of evidence under the direction of the sheriff presiding at the trial. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) An application for a new trial which specifies the ground in section 69(2)(c) of the 2014 Act (verdict contrary to evidence) may not be made unless it sets out in brief specific numbered propositions the reasons the verdict is said to be contrary to the evidence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
