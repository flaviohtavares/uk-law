# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 29.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 29.4. Hearing of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d390befb5c24feedef9626336d492451][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Court is to hear parties orally on all matters connected with the appeal, including liability for expenses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Any party may apply by motion for the question of liability for expenses to be heard after the Court gives its decision on the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) At the hearing, a party may only raise questions of law of which notice has not been given if the Court permits the party to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The Court may permit a party to amend any question of law or to add any new question of law. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Where the Court grants permission under paragraph (3) or (4), it may do so on such conditions as to expenses or otherwise as the Court thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
