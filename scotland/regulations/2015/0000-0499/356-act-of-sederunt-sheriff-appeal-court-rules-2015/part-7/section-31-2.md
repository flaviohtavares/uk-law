# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 31.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 31.2. Form of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-728ff1477da80e4e04823f1cd43efcc6][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) An appeal is made by lodging a note of appeal in Form 31.2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Rule 6.2(2)(a) to (ba) and (f) to (g) applies for the purpose of making an appeal under this rule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
