# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 28.8

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28.8. Questions about competency: determination (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-adeea4ecf20109563e3977190ae04915][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) At a hearing on the competency of an application for a new trial, the procedural Appeal Sheriff may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) refuse the application as incompetent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) find the application to be competent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) reserve the question of competency until the hearing of the application; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) refer the question of competency to the Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The procedural Appeal Sheriff may make an order as to the expenses of the reference. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Where the question of competency is referred to the Court, it may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) refuse the application as incompetent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) find the application to be competent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) reserve the question of competency until the hearing of the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The Court may make an order as to the expenses of the reference. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
