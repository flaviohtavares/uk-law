## 31.1. Application of this Chapter (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-728ff1477da80e4e04823f1cd43efcc6][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

This Chapter applies to an appeal against the decision of a sheriff under section 38(3), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] 46(3)[^key-298c524caf04d21d54df4fb1e6a72d7a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] and 67(3) of the Age of Criminal Responsibility (Scotland) Act 2019. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
