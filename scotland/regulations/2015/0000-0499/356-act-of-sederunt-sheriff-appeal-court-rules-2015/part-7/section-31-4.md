# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 31.4

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 31.4. Determination of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-728ff1477da80e4e04823f1cd43efcc6][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) At the conclusion of the hearing, the Court may either give its decision orally or reserve judgment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where the Court reserves judgment, it must give its decision in writing within 28 days. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The President of the Sheriff Appeal Court may vary the period in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
