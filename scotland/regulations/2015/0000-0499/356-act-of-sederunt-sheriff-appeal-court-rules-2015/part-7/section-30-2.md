# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 30.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 30.2. Transmission of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0a073bd9f49f7a1ae17edb1f33565d65][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-1d8887ab6188654b1353780190c917bd] Within 4 days after the sheriff has signed the stated case, the sheriff clerk must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) send the parties a copy of the stated case; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) transmit to the Clerk— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) the stated case; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] all documents and productions in the case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) On receipt of the stated case, the Clerk is to fix a hearing and intimate the date, time and place of that hearing to the parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
