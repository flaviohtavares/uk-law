# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 29.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 29.2. Transmission of appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-23156a7c44016c1818fab7c620ebd750][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-53ae4d4e6798150df862e475d2fe63c5] Within 4 days after the sheriff has signed the stated case, the sheriff clerk must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) send the parties a copy of the stated case; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) transmit to the Clerk— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (i) the stated case; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] all documents and productions in the case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) On receipt of the stated case, the Clerk is to fix a hearing and intimate the date, time and place of that hearing to the parties. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
