# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 8.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 8.2. Determination of application to refuse appeal due to delay (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-4b0c0a8ba94a1b34da838391252db5d2][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) The procedural Appeal Sheriff may refuse the appeal if the procedural Appeal Sheriff considers that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) there has been an inordinate and inexcusable delay on the part of any party or any party’s solicitor; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) such delay results in unfairness specific to the factual circumstances, including the procedural circumstances, of the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The procedural Appeal Sheriff must take into account the procedural consequences of allowing the appeal to proceed for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the parties to the appeal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the efficient disposal of business in the Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
