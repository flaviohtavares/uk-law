# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 10.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 10.1. Application to remit appeal to the Court of Session (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1f9336526b77e3b81fad0c871a23494f][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) An application under section 112 of the 2014 Act (remit of appeal from the Sheriff Appeal Court to the Court of Session) is to be made by motion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Within 4 days after the Court has made an order remitting an appeal to the Court of Session, the Clerk must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) give notice of the remit to each party; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) certify on the interlocutor sheet that subparagraph (a) has been complied with; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) transmit the process to the Deputy Principal Clerk of Session. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Failure by the Clerk to comply with paragraph (2)(a) or (b) does not affect the validity of a remit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
