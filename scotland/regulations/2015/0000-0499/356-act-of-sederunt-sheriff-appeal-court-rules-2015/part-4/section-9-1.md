# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 9.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 9.1. Application to abandon appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-aa4d7ca5e300a92c4181211500140582][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) An appellant may apply to the Court to abandon an appeal by lodging a minute of abandonment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where all of the parties consent to the abandonment of the appeal, the Court must refuse the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Where the other parties do not consent to the abandonment of the appeal, the Court may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) refuse the application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) grant the application and refuse the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) If the Court refuses an appeal under this rule, it may make an order as to the expenses of the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) If the Court refuses an application, it may make an order as to the expenses of the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
