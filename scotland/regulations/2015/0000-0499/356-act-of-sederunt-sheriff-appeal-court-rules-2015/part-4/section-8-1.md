# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 8.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 8.1. Application to refuse appeal due to delay (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ec595b04c0b276087cfb70d8493314b5][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) Any party may apply to the procedural Appeal Sheriff to refuse the appeal if the conditions in paragraph (2) are met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The conditions are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) there has been an inordinate and inexcusable delay by another party or another party’s solicitor; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) unfairness has resulted from that delay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) An application is to be made by motion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) That motion must specify the grounds on which refusal of the appeal is sought. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
