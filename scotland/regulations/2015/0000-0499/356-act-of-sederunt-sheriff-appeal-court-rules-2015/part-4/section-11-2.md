# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 11.2

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 11.2. Applications for permission to appeal (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9f1a7ce46e85f4ca9a834bc70a858d0d][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

(1) An application to the Court for permission to appeal to the Court of Session is to be made in Form 11.2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Such an application must be lodged within 14 days after the date on which the Court gave its decision on the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) When an application is made, the Clerk is to fix a hearing and intimate the time and date of that hearing to the parties to the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) The hearing is, so far as reasonably practicable, to be before the Appeal Sheriff or Appeal Sheriffs who made the decision in respect of which permission to appeal is sought. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
