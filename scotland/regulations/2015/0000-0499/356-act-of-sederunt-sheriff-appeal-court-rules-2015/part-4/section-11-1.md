# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, section 11.1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 11.1. Application of this Chapter (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8b9786fe4506824bb2b95f06aa0734ed][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

This Chapter applies where a party seeks the permission of the Court to appeal to the Court of Session against a decision of the Court constituting final judgment in civil proceedings under section 113 of the 2014 Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
