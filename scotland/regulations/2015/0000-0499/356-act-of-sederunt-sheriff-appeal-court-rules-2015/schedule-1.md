# Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2023-10-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 1 (affected by repeal, see note) — ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

*Extent: S*

Rule 1.4 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

### 1. Quorum of the Court (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f5766a32afff84dff4f0dec2a0c211fd][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(1) The quorum of the Court for the types of business specified in subparagraph (3) is one Appeal Sheriff. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) The quorum of the Court for any other business is three Appeal Sheriffs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) The types of business are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (za (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-99d5953c91956613f82e54c54508ebec][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] relieving a party from the consequences of a failure to comply with a provision in these Rules under rule 2.1(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (a) disposing of an application for leave to receive an appeal out of time under rule 6.4(2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) disposing of an application to abandon an appeal under rule 9.1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) disposing of an application for permission to appeal to the Court of Session under rule 11.2(1), where the decision in respect of which permission to appeal is sought was made by one Appeal Sheriff; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) a peremptory hearing under rule 17.4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (da (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-244bd750cea20afe3c49a4b142aea1a7][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] disposing of an application for an additional fee under rule 19.2(1), where the order allowing expenses was made by one Appeal Sheriff; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (e) disposing of an application to ordain a party to lodge an account of expenses under rule 19.3(2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (ea (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6db65d906bc99ef37edf13e524d386dd][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] disposing of a note of objections under rule 19.5(3), where the order allowing expenses was made by one Appeal Sheriff; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (f) disposing of an application to allow a devolution issue to be raised after the note of appeal has been lodged or answers to the note of appeal have been lodged under rule 20.2(3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (g) a hearing fixed under Chapter 27 (accelerated appeal procedure); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (h) a hearing fixed under Chapter 29 (appeals from summary causes *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] [^c-8bf6b946b3744416bc4e584ba13082bf]...);
- (ha (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d035e716a3f9bf1c6820a43217bc843f][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] a hearing fixed under Chapter 31 (appeals under Part 4 of the Age of Criminal Responsibility (Scotland) Act 2019); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (i) disposing of an application for authority to address the Court in Gaelic or to give oral evidence in Gaelic under paragraph 6 of this Schedule; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (j) any business where the Rules provide for that business to be disposed of by the procedural Appeal Sheriff. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

### 2. Procedural Appeal Sheriff (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2fd539696310db2a28cca6407ea32b5a][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(1) Every Appeal Sheriff is a procedural Appeal Sheriff. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Where the Court considers it appropriate to do so, the Court may dispose of any business where the Rules provide for that business to be disposed of by the procedural Appeal Sheriff. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

### 3. Signature of interlocutors etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-02696594a2967a6d1cf4996e98846611][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(1) Any order made by the Court under these Rules is to be contained in an interlocutor. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) An interlocutor is to be signed in accordance with subparagraphs (3) to (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Where the Court is constituted by more than one Appeal Sheriff when an order is made, the interlocutor is to be signed by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the Appeal Sheriff who presided over the Court when the order was made; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) in the event of the death, disability or absence of that Appeal Sheriff, the next senior Appeal Sheriff who sat on that occasion, after such consultation with the other Appeal Sheriffs who sat as may be necessary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Where the Court is constituted by one Appeal Sheriff, the interlocutor is to be signed by that Appeal Sheriff. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Where the Clerk determines an unopposed motion in writing in accordance with rule 12.7(1), the interlocutor is to be signed by the Clerk unless the procedural Appeal Sheriff directs otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5A)[^c-56c60d03f691ff2b02a4bf455475e6df] The Clerk may sign any other interlocutor if directed to do so by the procedural Appeal Sheriff. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5B) A direction under subparagraph (5A) need not be in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) An interlocutor signed in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] subparagraphs (5) and (5A)[^c-9c9db27a27f65ef4492bb9913355638b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] is to be treated for all purposes as if it had been signed by an Appeal Sheriff. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(7) An extract of an interlocutor which is not signed in accordance with the provisions of this rule is void and has no effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(8) An interlocutor may, on cause shown, be corrected or altered at any time before extract by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the Appeal Sheriff who signed it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) in the event of the death, disability or absence of that Appeal Sheriff, by any other Appeal Sheriff; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) where the interlocutor was signed in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950] subparagraphs (5) and (5A)[^c-eaf7835a2e1ef0aa4227a2f9d2ddf0e7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950], by any Appeal Sheriff. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

### 4. Form of process (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0deff313a47b76b1514078a6316cc875][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(1)[^c-947d4b0ed61ea9ae66a64fa3a967d2dc] A process must include the following steps of process— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) a minute of proceedings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) an inventory of process. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) Any document lodged with the Clerk is to be placed in the process. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

### 5. Decrees, extracts and execution (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c6f656ec3c0fbd7680117dd2d4c69b30][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(1) In this paragraph, “decree” includes any order or interlocutor which may be extracted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) A decree may be extracted at any time after whichever is the later of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) the expiry of the period within which an application for leave to appeal may be made, if no such application is made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) the date on which leave to appeal is refused, if there is no right to appeal from that decision; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (c) the expiry of the period within which an appeal may be made, if no such appeal is made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (d) the date on which an appeal is finally disposed of. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) A party may apply by motion to the procedural Appeal Sheriff to allow an extract to be issued earlier than a date referred to in subparagraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Nothing in this paragraph affects the power of the Court to supersede extract. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(5) Where execution may follow on an extract decree, the decree is to include the warrant for execution specified in subparagraph (6). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(6) That warrant is “This extract is warrant for all lawful execution hereon”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(7)[^c-c4ddb8949182d57186f8ca1f578730ba] Where interest is included in or payable under a decree, the rate of interest is 8 per cent a year unless otherwise stated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

### 6. Use of Gaelic (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-24b2bb817e0cd8ea299c49a18d6c3f4c][^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(1) This paragraph applies where the use of Gaelic by a party has been authorised by the sheriff in the proceedings out of which an appeal arises. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(2) That party may apply by motion for authority to address the Court in Gaelic at— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

- (a) an appeal hearing fixed under rule 7.14(3)(a); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
- (b) a hearing under rule 29.4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(3) Where proof or additional proof is ordered in accordance with rule 23.1 (taking proof in the course of an appeal) and that party wishes to give oral evidence in Gaelic, the party may apply by motion for authority to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]

(4) Where the Court grants authority under paragraph (2) or (3), an interpreter is to be provided by the Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f0b547b9fda9054e3bcfc93085b1a950]
