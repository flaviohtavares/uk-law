# The Post-16 Education (Scotland) Act 2013 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 82 (C. 18)

Education

The Post-16 Education (Scotland) Act 2013 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2015

Made

25th February 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

27th February 2015

Coming into force

31st March 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 23(2) of the Post-16 Education (Scotland) Act 2013[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Post-16 Education (Scotland) Act 2013 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2015 and comes into force on 31st March 2015.

## 2. Appointed day

31st March 2015 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the Post-16 Education (Scotland) Act 2013, so far as not already in force.

ANGELA CONSTANCE

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

25th February 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force on 31st March 2015 the provisions of the Post-16 Education (Scotland) Act 2013 which are not yet fully in force. The main effect is to fully commence paragraph 8(6) of the schedule to that Act, which inserts section 7D(7) to (9) into the Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 2005.
