# The Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 2015, section 92

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (33).** Text as at 2026-02-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ssi/2015/425
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 92. Consequential amendment[^c-805cf5f0bca3f816c95c851feea00bd8]

*Extent: S*

In the definition of “election petition” in rule 69.1 of the Rules of the Court of Session 1994[^f00053] (interpretation of Chapter 69: election petitions), for “article 84 of and Schedule 6 to the Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 2010” substitute “article 82 of and Schedule 6 to the Scottish Parliament (Elections etc.) Order 2015”.
