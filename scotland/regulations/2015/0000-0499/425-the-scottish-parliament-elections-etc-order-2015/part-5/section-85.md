> Text as at 2026-11-03.

## 85. Advertisements[^c-5ef76a0359a0d4f56dd5da2ce5ee5b93]

*Extent: S*

The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) (Scotland) Regulations 1984[^f00050] shall have effect in relation to the display on any site in Scotland of an advertisement relating specifically to a Scottish parliamentary election as they have effect in relation to the display of an advertisement relating specifically to a parliamentary election.
