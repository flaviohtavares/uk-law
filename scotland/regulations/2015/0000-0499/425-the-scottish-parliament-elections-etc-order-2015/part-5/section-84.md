## 84. Dissolution of Parliament: minimum period[^key-70c4363db6981f93def6ca95de786a0a]

*Extent: S*

The “minimum period” for the purpose of section 2(3) of the 1998 Act (ordinary general elections) is a period of 20 days[^key-19e754d38b7259ceabc140857bcf964e], computed in accordance with rule 2 of the Scottish Parliamentary Election Rules (computation of time).
