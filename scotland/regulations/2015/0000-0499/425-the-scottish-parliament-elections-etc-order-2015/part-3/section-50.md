## 50. Where no return and declaration is needed[^c-012f09aa391c25e48a1f8a327c45bead]

*Extent: S*

Notwithstanding anything in articles 47 and 48, no return or declaration as to election expenses shall be required in the case of a person—

- (a) who is a candidate, but is so only because that person has been declared by others to be a candidate; and
- (b) who has not consented to the declaration or taken any part as a candidate at the election.
