## 151. Amount of lump sum death grant payable on death of active member[^key-ab84aba68dcb1da093fc17ccb7529756]

*Extent: S*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to a continuous period of pensionable service under this scheme (“period of service”).

(2) If the member is a transition member with continuity of service, the period of service includes the member’s period of pensionable service under the existing police pension scheme before the transition date for that member.
