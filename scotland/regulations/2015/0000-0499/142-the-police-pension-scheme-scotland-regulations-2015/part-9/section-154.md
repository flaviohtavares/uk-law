# The Police Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 154

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 154. Application of Chapter[^key-dfa9dad2e5a8d3ebb7fb7798cbfe48dc]

*Extent: S*

(1) This Chapter applies in relation to a member’s continuous period of pensionable service under this scheme (“the period of service”).

(2) If a member has more than one period of service, this Chapter applies in relation to each period of service.
