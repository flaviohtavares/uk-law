## 126. Meaning of “surviving adult’s pension”[^key-24878748257aa2cff7b50235d98c7b04]

*Extent: S*

In these Regulations, “surviving adult’s pension” means any of the following pensions payable to a surviving adult under this Chapter—

- (a) a surviving adult’s earned pension;
- (b) a surviving adult’s lower tier ill-health pension;
- (c) a surviving adult’s enhanced upper tier ill-health pension;
- (d) a surviving adult’s added pension.
