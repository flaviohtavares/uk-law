> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 161. Payment of member contributions[^key-ed68869345d35dfc3f6dff82296fe682]

*Extent: S*

(1) Member contributions for each pay period are to be deducted by the member’s employer from the member’s pensionable earnings for that pay period.

(2) A member’s employer may make contributions on the member’s behalf in circumstances determined by the scheme manager.
