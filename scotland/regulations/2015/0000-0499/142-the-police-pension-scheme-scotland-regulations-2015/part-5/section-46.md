> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 46. Establishment of active member’s account[^c-607e29e766cd416496b023b8042633e7]

*Extent: S*

(1) The scheme manager must establish a pension account for a member who is in pensionable service under this scheme from the day on which the member begins that pensionable service.

(2) For the purpose of these Regulations, an account established under paragraph (1) is called an active member’s account.
