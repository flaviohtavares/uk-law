# The Police Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 55A

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 55A. Account established after ill-health pension ceases to be payable[^key-bddf5489fdb278fb0c4dd9074e941eba]

*Extent: S*

If a lower tier ill-health pension ceases to be payable to a person (P) under Part 7 (retirement benefits) and P does not re-enter pensionable service—

- (a) a deferred member’s account must be established; and
- (b) that account must be credited with an amount equal to the annual rate of the lower tier ill-health pension payable immediately before the lower tier ill-health pension ceased to be payable.
