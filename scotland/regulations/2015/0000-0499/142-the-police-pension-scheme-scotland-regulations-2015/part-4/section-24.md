# The Police Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 24

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 24. Pensionable earnings[^c-3d7af99d9cce5a1ddd753d085c7cb886]

*Extent: S*

(1) This regulation applies for the purpose of these Regulations, other than for the purpose of calculating the rate of member contributions under Part 10 (contributions).

(2) A member’s pensionable earnings for any period—

- (a)[^c-274102b1bf689055d50206e6aa508da1] for a member in full-time service, means—
  - (i) pay at the rate to which the person is entitled in that period, account being taken of any retrospective increase in that rate, and
  - (ii) earnings in respect of on-call allowance,
- (b)[^c-4f08afb458bc9f3fa48ea9a8c095eaa0] for a member in part-time service, means—
  - (i) so much of the member’s pay at that rate as is attributable to the member’s determined hours specified under the member’s part-time appointment, account being taken of any retrospective increase in that rate, and
  - (ii) earnings in respect of on-call allowance.
