## 20. Automatic re-enrolment[^c-8cc80c335369453115deea037f0978f6]

*Extent: S*

(1) This regulation applies if, on the automatic re-enrolment date, a person (P) in eligible service is not in pensionable service under this scheme.

(2) On the automatic re-enrolment date, the scheme manager must enrol P in this scheme in relation to that eligible service if the employer is required under section 5 (automatic re-enrolment) of the Pensions Act 2008[^f00035] to make arrangements for P to be an active member of a pension scheme and the employer has not made the choice referred to in regulation 17A(1)[^c-16417182cacfbc25fa986465b7bc85b3].

(3) For the purpose of paragraph (2), the employer is required to make arrangements under that section even if—

- (a) P has not reached the age of 22;
- (b) P has reached normal pension age under this scheme; or
- (c) the earnings requirements under that section are not met.
