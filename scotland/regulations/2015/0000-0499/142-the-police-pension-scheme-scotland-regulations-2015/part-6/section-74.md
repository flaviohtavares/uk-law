## 74. Compulsory retirement void if appeal successful[^c-da2954a01bab596664d3f59303a87da4]

*Extent: S*

The compulsory retirement of a member under this Part is void if—

- (a) the selected medical practitioner decides that the member is permanently medically unfit for performing the ordinary duties of a member of the police force;
- (b) the member appeals under Schedule 1 against the decision of the selected medical practitioner; and
- (c) the appeal board decides that the member is not permanently medically unfit for performing the ordinary duties of a member of the police force.
