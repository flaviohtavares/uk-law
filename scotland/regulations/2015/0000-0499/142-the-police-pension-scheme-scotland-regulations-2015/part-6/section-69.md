# The Police Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 69

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 69. Application of Chapter[^key-9539e27f6328ac87ecca81698e56995f]

*Extent: S*

This Chapter applies in relation to a member of the police force (“the member”)—

- (a) who is an active member of this scheme; or
- (b) who, for the purpose of determining entitlement to payment of benefits to or in respect of the member, is taken to be an active member of this scheme[^f00043].
