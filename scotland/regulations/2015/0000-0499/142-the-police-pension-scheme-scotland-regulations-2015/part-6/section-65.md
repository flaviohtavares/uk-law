# The Police Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 65

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 65. Permanent medical unfitness[^key-fd4e9aa0f141bd6de2430a8b4cb39fac]

*Extent: S*

(1) In these Regulations, a reference to a member of the police force or a former member of the police force (“the member”) being permanently medically unfit is taken to be a reference to—

- (a) the member being medically unfit at the time the selected medical practitioner decides the question; and
- (b) that medical unfitness being at that time likely to be permanent.

(2)[^key-e5d66cd8821b77b41180e51d5e16be40] For the purpose of deciding whether or not the member’s medical unfitness is likely to be permanent, the member is taken to receive normal appropriate medical treatment.

(3)[^key-e5d66cd8821b77b41180e51d5e16be40] In this regulation, “appropriate medical treatment” does not include medical treatment that the employer decides is reasonable for the member to refuse.

(4)[^key-e5d66cd8821b77b41180e51d5e16be40] The member may appeal under regulation 197 (appeals to sheriff) or 198 (appeals to tribunal) against a decision of the employer as to whether a refusal to accept medical treatment is reasonable.
