# The Police Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 75

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 75. Application of Chapter[^key-e0abb9697a693463b3d521fae4df11f9]

*Extent: S*

(1) This Chapter applies in relation to a member of the police force who is a deferred member of this scheme.

(2) For the purpose of this Chapter, a former member of the police force is taken to be a member of the police force.
