# The Police Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 168

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 168. Application of Part[^key-78dffbfc7ed8849ff4f70055068a1caf]

*Extent: S*

This Part—

- (a) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 1 of Part 4ZA of PSA 1993 (transfer rights)[^key-72b5002c345fc8247282d3f95c9f82af] and is without prejudice to that Chapter; and
- (b) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 2 of that Part (revaluation of accrued benefits)[^key-e80c9d07595c4fc2ca61a7e7d9a74280] and is without prejudice to that Chapter.
