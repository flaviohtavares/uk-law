# The Police Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 178

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 178. Interpretation of Chapter[^key-9878808dc50765ea0941692267b5de07]

*Extent: S*

In this Chapter—

- “club transfer statement” means a statement under regulation 182 of the amount of club transfer earned pension;
- “transfer date” means the later[^key-2b6d1e347bdf650a419fb94c133414f5] of—
  - (a) if the scheme manager has provided a transfer statement or a club transfer statement, the last day of the period of 2 months beginning with the date of the statement; and
  - (b) the day on which the transfer payment is received by the scheme manager;
- “transfer payment request” means a request to the scheme manager under this Chapter that a transfer payment be accepted from another pension scheme;
- “transfer statement” means a statement under regulation 180(2).
