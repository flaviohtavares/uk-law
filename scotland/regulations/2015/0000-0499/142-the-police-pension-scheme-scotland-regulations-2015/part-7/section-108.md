## 108. Refusal to be medically examined[^c-6a02db6692fb8cd64a7e2f192076ab2a]

*Extent: S*

The employer may make a decision under this Chapter on such evidence and medical advice as the employer in its discretion thinks necessary if—

- (a) a question as to the medical unfitness of a member of the police force is referred to a selected medical practitioner for decision; and
- (b) the member wilfully or negligently fails to submit to any medical examination or to attend any interviews that the selected medical practitioner considers necessary in order to make a decision.
