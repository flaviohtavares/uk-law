## 82. Claim for payment of full retirement pension (active members)[^c-66a5202e69a5ffc667d7db6e95da258e]

*Extent: S*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to an active member of this scheme.

(2) A claim for payment of a full retirement pension—

- (a) may only be made by written notice to the scheme manager; and
- (b) must comply with the notice period specified in regulation 83.

(3) If the member claims payment of the pension before reaching normal pension age under this scheme, the notice must state if the member has opted to buy out the early payment reduction.

(4) A full retirement pension calculated under regulation 88 (active members) is payable to the member in respect of each month as from the member’s last day of eligible service.
