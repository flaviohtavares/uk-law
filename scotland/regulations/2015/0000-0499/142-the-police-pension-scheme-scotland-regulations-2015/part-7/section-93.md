## 93. Claim for payment of ill-health pension[^c-2f461a49bddeb72f85d4ef398ec3c0c8]

*Extent: S*

(1) For the purpose of an appeal to the sheriff under regulation 197 or an appeal to a tribunal under regulation 198, an active member of this scheme is taken to claim payment of an ill-health pension under this Chapter on the date on which the member is required to retire under Part 6.

(2) An ill-health pension under this Chapter is payable to the member in respect of each month as from the date on which the member was required to retire under Part 6.
