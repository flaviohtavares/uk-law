## 80. Entitlement to full retirement pension (active members)[^c-23fc2d81fb4169855e5865718fd0d180]

*Extent: S*

(1) An active member of this scheme who has reached normal minimum pension age (P) is entitled to payment for life of a retirement earned pension if—

- (a) P has ceased to be in pensionable service under this scheme;
- (b) P has at least 2 years’ qualifying service or a transfer payment otherwise than from another occupational pension scheme has been received by this scheme in relation to the member;
- (c) P has left eligible service; and
- (d) P claims payment of a full retirement pension under regulation 82 (claim - active members).

(2) On becoming entitled to payment for life of a retirement earned pension, P is entitled to payment for life of a retirement added pension of any description if the retirement account specifies an amount of retirement added pension of that description.
