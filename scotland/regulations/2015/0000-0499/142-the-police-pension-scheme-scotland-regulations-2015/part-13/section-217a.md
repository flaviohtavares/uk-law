> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 217A. Prohibition on assignation or charging of benefits[^key-64bcf22dd86a79371f0b85572ee6d453]

*Extent: S*

Any assignation of, or charge on, or any agreement to assign or charge, any right to a benefit under this scheme is void.
