## 207. Forfeiture: appeals to tribunal[^key-5e66eb31dbaca0fefb4981dc4fa4c65f]

*Extent: S*

(1) If the pension supervising authority proposes to require the scheme manager to withhold any benefits payable to an inspector of constabulary (“the member”), the member or a person claiming payment of a benefit in respect of the member may, subject to regulation 199 (limitations on appeals), lodge an appeal against the proposal with the scheme manager.

(2) Regulation 215 (procedure on appeals to tribunal) sets out the procedure for appeals under this regulation to the tribunal.
