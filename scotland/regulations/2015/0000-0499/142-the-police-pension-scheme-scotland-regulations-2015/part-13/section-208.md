## 208. Scheme manager to be scheme administrator for purposes of Part 4 of Finance Act 2004[^key-c448eaebb2adfce56221d62dc619594e]

*Extent: S*

The scheme manager is appointed to be responsible for all functions that are functions conferred or imposed on the scheme administrator by or under Part 4 of FA 2004 (pension schemes etc).
