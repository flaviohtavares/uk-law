## 217. Provision of benefit information statements to members[^c-1aaef8defe6761c208baa9eeab5acf86]

*Extent: S*

The scheme manager must provide benefit information statements to each active member of this scheme in accordance with—

- (a) section 14 of the Act (information about benefits); and
- (b) Treasury directions given under that section.
