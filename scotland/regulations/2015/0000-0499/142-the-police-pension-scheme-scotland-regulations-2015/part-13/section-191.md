> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 191. Reduction in pension debit member’s benefits[^key-1ec4888aa31108caef1efe1170c17d2c]

*Extent: S*

(1) The benefits to which a pension debit member is entitled under Part 7 are subject to the reduction to be made under section 31 of WRPA 1999.

(2) Member contributions and payments for added pension refunded under Part 10 to a pension debit member are subject to the reduction to be made under section 31 of WRPA 1999.
