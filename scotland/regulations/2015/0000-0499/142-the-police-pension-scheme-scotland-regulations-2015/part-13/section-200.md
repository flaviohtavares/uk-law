> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 200. Pension supervising authority[^c-a3690753bd1011b026a438fabab4fc13]

*Extent: S*

(1) For the purpose of this Chapter, the pension supervising authority is the Scottish Police Authority.

(2) The pension supervising authority may require the scheme manager to withhold benefits payable to a member under this Chapter.
