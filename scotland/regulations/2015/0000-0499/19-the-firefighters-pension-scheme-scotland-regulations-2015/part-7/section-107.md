## 107. Reduction in pension debit member’s benefits[^key-eb3f95ca68c2ec8b367bb458125deed2]

*Extent: S*

The benefits to which a pension debit member is entitled under these Regulations are subject to the reduction of the relevant amount calculated in accordance with regulation 54 (establishment of pension credit member’s account).
