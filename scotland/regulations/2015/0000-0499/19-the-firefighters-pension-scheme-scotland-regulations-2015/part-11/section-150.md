# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 150

> Extent: Scotland.

## 150. Fees and allowances payable to the board[^c-32eb14b9f4a4b0440fc20aff534180a9]

*Extent: S*

(1) There must be paid to the board and the reviewing member—

- (a) such fees and allowances (including those payable to the reviewing member for work undertaken on the review of documents under regulation 146(7) (reference of appeal to the board)) as are determined in accordance with arrangements made by the scheme manager; or
- (b) where no such arrangements have been made, such fees and allowances as the scheme manager may from time to time determine.

(2) The fees and allowances payable under paragraph (1) must—

- (a) be paid by the scheme manager; and
- (b) be treated for the purposes of regulation 151 (expenses of each party) as part of the scheme manager’s expenses.
