## 77. Meaning of “initial period”[^key-9474ee947da3367539fd6f4bd1eec026]

*Extent: S*

For the purposes of these Regulations[^key-9a14b3dc29142e78a15c77463a472dff], the “initial period” is the period of 13 weeks commencing on the day after the death of the member during which a bereavement pension may be payable to any surviving partner or eligible child.
