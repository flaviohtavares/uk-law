# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 88

> Extent: Scotland.

## 88. Eligible child’s pension payable on death of pensioner member[^key-3976e0b0e4dac50fde3cbe0ba879394f]

*Extent: S*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to an eligible child of a member who dies if, at the date of death, the member is a pensioner member (P).

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), the annual rate of an eligible child’s pension is an amount equal to the specified proportion of the amount of retirement pension payable to P immediately before P’s death.

(3) Where an early payment reduction had been made on P’s retirement, the amount in paragraph (2) is the specified proportion of the amount of retirement pension that would have been payable if that reduction had not been made.
