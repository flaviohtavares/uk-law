> Extent: Scotland.

## 13. Automatic re-enrolment[^key-dcfae602c923a08b1718199e417977c9]

*Extent: S*

(1) This regulation applies if, on the automatic re-enrolment date, an eligible person (P) in relation to service in a scheme employment is not in pensionable service under this scheme in relation to service in that employment.

(2) On the automatic re-enrolment date, the scheme manager must enrol P in this scheme in relation to service in that employment if the employer is required under section 5 of the Pensions Act 2008[^f00038] (automatic re-enrolment) to make arrangements for P to be an active member of a pension scheme.
