# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 11

> Text as at 2025-07-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 11. Automatic enrolment[^c-b995521b064aaa3db9892b77ea9370a3]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person (P) who is not in pensionable service[^f00037] under the scheme immediately before P’s first eligible day of service in a scheme employment begins pensionable service under this scheme on P’s first eligible day of service in that employment unless—

- (a) regulation 15 (opting out before the end of the first three months) applies; or
- (b) P is a transition member with continuity of service and paragraph (2) applies.

(2) This paragraph applies if—

- (a) P’s first eligible day of service in the scheme employment is the day after P’s closing date; and
- (b) on P’s closing date—
  - (i) P was in service in the same employment, and
  - (ii) P had opted out of the 1992 Scheme or the NFPS, as the case may be, in relation to that service.
