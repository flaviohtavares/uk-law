# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 137

> Text as at 2025-07-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 137. Request to confirm details on certificate[^key-d32ace80cd248c3364bfa5ac0656836e]

*Extent: S*

Where a member has been provided with a certificate under regulation 136 (requirement for scheme manager to provide a certificate) and is dissatisfied with the information stated on the certificate, that member may within the period of three months commencing on the date on which the certificate was received, request the scheme manager to confirm the accuracy of the information contained in it or to provide an amended certificate.
