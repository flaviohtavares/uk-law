## 139. Certificate to be given to new employer[^c-06856619ed93cfa8eeb8415f4af9670c]

*Extent: S*

A member who has been provided with a certificate under regulation 136 (requirement for scheme manager to provide a certificate) must give the certificate to that member’s new employer.
