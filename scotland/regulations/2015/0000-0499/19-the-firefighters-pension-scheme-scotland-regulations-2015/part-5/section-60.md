## 60. Annual rate of retirement pension (deferred members)[^key-91e074b4584e1267856a7948913a484e]

*Extent: S*

(1) This regulation applies when a deferred member of this scheme becomes entitled to the immediate payment of a retirement pension.

(2) The annual rate of a retirement pension payable to a member to whom this regulation applies is calculated by subtracting from the retirement amount of deferred pension specified in the deferred member’s account—

- (a) the early payment reduction (if any), specified in that account in relation to that amount;
- (b) the commutation amount (if any) specified in that account in relation to that amount;
- (c) the total allocation amount (if any) specified in that account in relation to that amount.
