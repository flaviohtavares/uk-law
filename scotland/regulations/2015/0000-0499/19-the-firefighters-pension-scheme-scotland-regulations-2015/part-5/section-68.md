# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 68

> Text as at 2025-07-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 68. Review of ill-health award or early payment of retirement pension[^key-4736cffee8dbbfb2ef0050dee9f97c5c][^key-abb108e3658ab90e5850dcd99d78d58f]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a member (P) has been in receipt of an ill-health award under regulation 65 (entitlement to lower tier ill-health pension and higher tier ill-health pension) for less than 10 years, and is under deferred pension age, the scheme manager must consider, at such intervals as it considers appropriate, whether P has become capable—

- (a) of performing any duty appropriate to the role from which P retired on grounds of ill-health; and
- (b) of undertaking regular employment.

(2) The scheme manager must consider, at such intervals as it considers appropriate before the member reaches deferred pension age, in relation to a pensioner member who was a deferred member and whose pension is being paid early by virtue of regulation 67 (early payment of retirement pension to a deferred member), whether the member has become capable of undertaking regular employment.
