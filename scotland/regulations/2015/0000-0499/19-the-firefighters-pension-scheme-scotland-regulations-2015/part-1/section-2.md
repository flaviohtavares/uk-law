# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 2

> Text as at 2025-07-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 2. Establishment of Firefighters’ Pension Scheme 2015[^key-48003bd847c7923d7508fd6ded393023]

*Extent: S*

(1) These Regulations establish a scheme for the payment of pensions and other benefits to or in respect of fire and rescue workers[^f00002] who are firefighters in Scotland.

(2) This scheme is to be known as the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) 2015.
