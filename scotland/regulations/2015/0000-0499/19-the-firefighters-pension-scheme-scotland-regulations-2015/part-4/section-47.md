> Extent: Scotland.

## 47. Adjustment of account after early payment of deferred pension ceases[^c-9183936d76958eb4f868b62de041e44f]

*Extent: S*

When a deferred member who has received the early payment of a deferred pension under regulation 67 (early payment of retirement pension to deferred member) ceases to be entitled to the early payment of the deferred pension under regulation 69(7) (consequences of review), the scheme manager must make the necessary adjustments to the deferred member’s account.
