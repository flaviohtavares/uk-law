# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 52

> Text as at 2025-07-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 52. Establishment of survivor member’s account[^key-91846168ed89b68ef2692c274c1a2efc]

*Extent: S*

(1) The scheme manager must establish a pension account for each person who is entitled to a pension under this scheme as a surviving partner or eligible child following the death of an active member, deferred member or pensioner member.

(2) The scheme manager must—

- (a) close the active member’s account or the deferred member’s account or the retirement account (as the case may be) and the added pension account (if any); and
- (b) establish a pension account for each survivor member.

(3) For the purpose of these Regulations—

- (a) a person entitled to a pension in the circumstances mentioned in paragraph (1) is called a survivor member; and
- (b) an account established under paragraph (2)(b) is called a survivor member’s account.
