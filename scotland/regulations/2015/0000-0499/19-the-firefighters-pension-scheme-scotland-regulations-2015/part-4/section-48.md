# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 48

> Text as at 2025-07-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 48. Account established after ill-health award ceases to be payable[^c-bb3a8b551bcdd598885489d84f3cabf9]

*Extent: S*

If a lower tier ill-health pension ceases to be payable to a person (P) under regulation 69 (consequences of review) and P does not re-enter pensionable service—

- (a) a deferred member’s account must be established; and
- (b) that account must be credited with an amount equal to the annual rate of the lower tier ill-health pension payable immediately before the lower tier ill-health pension ceased to be payable.
