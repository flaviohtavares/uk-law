## 167. Scheme administrator for the purposes of FA 2004[^key-f2116546d7a12cbb0fe2e8c20d1ab638]

*Extent: S*

The scheme manager is appointed to be responsible for all liabilities and responsibilities connected with the functions conferred or imposed on the scheme administrator by or under Part 4 of FA 2004.
