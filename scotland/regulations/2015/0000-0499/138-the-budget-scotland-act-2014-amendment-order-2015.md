# The Budget (Scotland) Act 2014 Amendment Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 138

Public Finance And Accountability

The Budget (Scotland) Act 2014 Amendment Order 2015

Made

19th March 2015

Coming into force

20th March 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7(1) of the Budget (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 7(2) of that Act, a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Budget (Scotland) Act 2014 Amendment Order 2015 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

(2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Budget (Scotland) Act 2014.

## 2. Amendment of amounts specified in section 4 (overall cash authorisations)

In section 4 of the Act, for the amount specified—

- (a) in subsection (2) (Scottish Administration), substitute “£32,644,274,000”;
- (b) in subsection (3)(a) (Forestry Commissioners), substitute “£58,935,000”; and
- (c) in subsection (3)(c) (Scottish Parliamentary Corporate Body), substitute “£77,396,000”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Budget (Scotland) Act 2014 (“the Act”).

Article 2 amends the overall cash authorisations specified in section 4(2) and (3)(a) and (c) of the Act in relation to the Scottish Administration, the Forestry Commissioners and the Scottish Parliamentary Corporate Body.

Article 3(2) amends the purposes in purposes 1 to 3, 6 to 8, and 17 and omits purpose 16.

Article 3(3) amends the amount of resources (other than accruing resources) that may be used for purposes 1 to 13, 15 and 17 in schedule 1 to the Act. It also amends the total amount of those resources for all the purposes in that schedule.

Article 3(4) amends the amount of resources (accruing resources) that may be used for purposes 1, 8 10 and 13 in schedule 1 to the Act. It also amends the total amount of those resources for all the purposes in that schedule.

Article 4 amends the amount of resources other than accruing resources that may be used by the direct-funded bodies for certain purposes in schedule 2 to the Act.

The Order reflects the reallocation of resources between Scottish Government portfolios following the First Minister’s announcement of the new portfolio structure on 21st November 2014.
