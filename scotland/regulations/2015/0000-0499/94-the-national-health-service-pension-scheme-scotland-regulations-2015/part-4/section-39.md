# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 39

> Text as at 2025-10-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 39. Eligibility for refund

*Extent: S*

(1) Contributions made by a member (M) may be repaid to M only if—

- (a) the 1993 Act condition applies; or
- (b) the short service condition applies.

(2) The “1993 Act condition” is that—

- (a) Chapter 2 of Part 4ZA[^c-cf45a17489af668d732f0855b5907284] of the 1993 Act applies to M; and
- (b) the repayment is made in accordance with that Chapter.

(3) The short service condition applies if—

- (a) M has not attained normal pension age;
- (b) M does not qualify for benefits under regulation 71;
- (c) M is not a pensioner member;
- (d) M ceases to be an active member;
- (e) the 1993 Act condition does not apply to M; and
- (f) M makes a claim in writing to the scheme manager for the repayment.
