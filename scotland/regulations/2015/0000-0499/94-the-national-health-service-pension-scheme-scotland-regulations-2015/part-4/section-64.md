# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 64

> Text as at 2025-10-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 64. Retirement pension (other than ill-health pension) becomes payable before the end of the contributions payment period

*Extent: S*

(1) This regulation applies if any of the following retirement pensions becomes payable to M before the end of the contributions payment period—

- (a) a partial retirement (if M has elected to receive additional pension with it);
- (b) a premature retirement pension;
- (c) an early retirement pension.

(2) If this regulation applies—

- (a) periodic payments for additional pension cease to be payable on the entitlement day for that pension; and
- (b) the amount of accrued additional pension as at the day M leaves the pensionable service concerned is an amount determined by the scheme manager, having regard to—
  - (i) the period over which the contributions were paid; and
  - (ii) the length of the contribution payment period.
