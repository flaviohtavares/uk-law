# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 56

> Text as at 2025-10-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 56. Lump sum or periodic payments

*Extent: S*

(1) An employing authority's additional pension contributions must be made by lump sum.

(2) A member's additional pension contributions may be paid either by lump sum or by periodic payments.
