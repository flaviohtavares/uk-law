# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2025-10-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Pension Board

*Extent: S*

Regulation 4

### 1. Composition of the pension board

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the scheme manager is to determine—

- (a) the membership of the Scottish NHS Pension Board (“**the Board**”);
- (b) the manner in which the members of the Board may be appointed and removed; and
- (c) the terms of appointment of the members of the Board.

(2) The Board is to include equal numbers of members representing employers and scheme members.

(3)[^key-d99dc5173b2584416839d7db51ace496] The scheme manager must be satisfied that a person to be appointed as a member of the Board does not have a conflict of interest.

### 2. Terms of office of members

The duration of the term of office of each member of the Board is to be determined by the scheme manager on appointment.

### 3. Disqualification from appointment as a member

A person is disqualified from appointment as a member of the Board if that person has a conflict of interest.

### 4. Appointment, term of office and cessation of office of the chair

(1) The scheme manager is to appoint the chair of the Board.

(2) A member serving as chair shall cease to be chair—

- (a) on ceasing to be a member;
- (b) if the member resigns as chair, which the member may do at any time by giving a minimum of three months' notice (or such shorter period as the scheme manager is prepared to accept) in writing to the scheme manager;
- (c) if the member's membership of the board is suspended by the scheme manager.

### 5. Conflicts of interest

(1) The scheme manager must, from time to time, be satisfied that none of the members of the Board has a conflict of interest.

(2) For the purpose of enabling the scheme manager to be satisfied that a person referred to in sub-paragraph (3) does not have a conflict of interest, that person must provide the scheme manager with such information as the scheme manager reasonably requires.

(3) Those persons are—

- (a) a member of the Board;
- (b) a person proposed to be appointed by the scheme manager as a member of the Board.

### 6. Information

(1) The information about the pension board required to be published by section 6 (pension board: information) of the 2013 Act must additionally include a statement in relation to each member of the board, setting out any financial or other interest not being a conflict of interest but which has the potential to constitute a conflict of interest in the future.

(2) The obligation to include a statement mentioned in sub-paragraph (1) may be satisfied in whole or in part by the publication of any information provided to the scheme manager for the purposes of paragraph 5(2).
