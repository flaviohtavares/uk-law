## 2. Establishment of the NHS Pension Scheme (Scotland) 2015

*Extent: S*

(1) A scheme is established for the payment of pensions and other benefits to or in respect of—

- (a) health service workers mentioned in regulation 18(1);
- (b) such other persons as are mentioned in regulation 18(2).

(2) This scheme is to be known as the NHS Pension Scheme (Scotland) 2015.
