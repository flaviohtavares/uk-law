# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 1

> Text as at 2025-10-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1. Citation, extent and commencement

*Extent: S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015 and extend to Scotland.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1st April 2015.
