# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 4

> Text as at 2025-10-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 4. Pension board

*Extent: S*

(1) There is to be a pension board (“The Scottish NHS Pension Board”) which is constituted in accordance with Schedule 1 and has responsibility for assisting the scheme manager in relation to—

- (a) securing compliance with these Regulations and other legislation relating to the governance and administration of—
  - (i) this scheme; and
  - (ii) any statutory pension scheme that is connected with it;
- (b) securing compliance with any requirements imposed by the Pensions Regulator in relation to—
  - (i) this scheme; and
  - (ii) any statutory pension scheme that is connected with it.
