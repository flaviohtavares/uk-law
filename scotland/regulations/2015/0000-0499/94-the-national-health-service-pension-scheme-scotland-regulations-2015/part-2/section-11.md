> Text as at 2025-10-31.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 11. No agreement reached

*Extent: S*

(1) This regulation applies if no agreement is reached as mentioned in regulation 10.

(2) If the costs of the scheme are outside the margins mentioned in regulation 9, the Scottish Ministers must adjust the fraction specified in paragraph 13(3) of Schedule 7 so as to achieve the target cost.
