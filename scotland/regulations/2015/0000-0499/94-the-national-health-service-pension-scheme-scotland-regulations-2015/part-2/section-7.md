# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2015, section 7

> Text as at 2025-10-31.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 7. Actuarial valuations

*Extent: S*

(1) The scheme actuary must carry out an actuarial valuation of this scheme and connected schemes as at the effective date.

(2) The scheme actuary must provide a valuation report to the scheme manager not later than such a date as may be agreed by the scheme manager.

(3) The effective date is—

- (a) in respect of the first valuation under paragraph (1), 31st March 2016;
- (b) in respect of subsequent valuations, such dates as enable the scheme to comply with the Treasury directions as to valuations.
