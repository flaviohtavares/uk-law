# The Waste (Meaning of Hazardous Waste and European Waste Catalogue) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2015

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

2015 No. 188

Environmental Protection

The Waste (Meaning of Hazardous Waste and European Waste Catalogue) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2015

Made

5th May 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

7th May 2015

Coming into force

8th June 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of, and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to, the European Communities Act 1972 [^c22524501].

These Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972, and it appears to the Scottish Ministers that it is expedient for the references in regulations 4, 5, 8 and 11 to Commission Decision [2000/532/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0532) replacing Decision [94/3/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1994/0003) establishing a list of wastes pursuant to Article 1(a) of Council Directive [75/442/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1975/0442) on waste and Council Decision [94/904/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1994/0904) establishing a list of hazardous waste pursuant to Article 1(4) of Council Directive [91/689/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/1991/0689) on hazardous waste [^c22524511] to be references to that instrument as amended from time to time.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Waste (Meaning of Hazardous Waste and European Waste Catalogue) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Scotland) Regulations 2015 and come into force on 8th June 2015.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

## 2. Amendment of the Environmental Protection Act 1990

In section 75 of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 [^c22524521] (meaning of “waste”) for subsection (12) substitute—

> (12) In this Part, “**the Waste Directive**” means Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste and repealing certain Directives [^c22524531], as amended by Commission Regulation (EU) No 1357/2014 replacing Annex III to Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) [^c22524541].

*(This note is not part of the Regulations)*

These Regulations amend various enactments to effect changes as a consequence of (1) the amendment of Directive [2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0098) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste (“**the Directive**”) (OJ L 312. 22.11.2008, p.89) by Commission Regulation 1357/2014/EU (“**the Regulation**”) (OJ L 365, 19.12.2004, p.89) and (2) the amendment of Commission Decision [2000/532/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2000/0532) establishing a list of wastes (“**the 2000 Decision**”) (OJ L 226, 6.9.2000, p.3) by Commission Decision 2014/955/EU (“**the 2014 Decision**”) (OJ L 370, 30.12.2014).

The Regulation amends the Directive by replacing Annex III (properties of waste which render it hazardous) to the Directive.

The 2000 Decision sets out a list of wastes known as the European Waste Catalogue. The 2014 Decision amends that list.

No business and regulatory impact assessment has been prepared for these Regulations as no significant change is foreseen to the existing impacts upon business, charities or voluntary bodies.
