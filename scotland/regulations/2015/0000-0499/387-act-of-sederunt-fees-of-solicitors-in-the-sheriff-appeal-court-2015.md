# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Appeal Court) 2015

> Text as at 2023-06-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

2015 No. 387

Sheriff Appeal Court

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Appeal Court) 2015 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

Made *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

17th November 2015 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

Laid before the Scottish Parliament *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

18th November 2015 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

Coming into force *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

1st January 2016 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

In accordance with section 4 of the Scottish Civil Justice Council and Criminal Legal Assistance Act 2013 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0][^f00001], the Court of Session has approved draft rules submitted to it by the Scottish Civil Justice Council with such modifications as it thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

The Court of Session therefore makes this Act of Sederunt under the powers conferred by section 106(1) of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0][^f00002] and all other powers enabling it to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

## 1. Citation and commencement, (affected by repeal, see note) etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-28eec34334b2dd30ee2a02fa653c89d9][^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0][^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Appeal Court) 2015. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(2) It comes into force on 1st January 2016. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(3) A certified copy is to be inserted in the Books of Sederunt. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

## 2. Application (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-830c6482f4beb0522c056f90aee4b8c8][^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(1) This Act of Sederunt regulates the taxation of accounts of expenses between party and party in the Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(2) Nothing in this Act of Sederunt affects the inherent powers of the Court in relation to expenses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

## 3. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b3f1619ad55655a33e15fe21adb0b649][^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(1) In this Act of Sederunt— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

- “advocate” means a practising member of the Faculty of Advocates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]
- “the Court” means the Sheriff Appeal Court; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]
- “solicitor” means a person qualified to practise as a solicitor under section 4 of the Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1980 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0][^f00003];
- “solicitor advocate” means a solicitor having a right of audience before the Court of Session by virtue of section 25A of the Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1980 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0][^f00004].

(2) In this Act of Sederunt— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

- (a) a sheet consists of 250 words or numbers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]
- (b) a page consists of 125 words. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(3) Where there is a reference in this Act of Sederunt to a rule, it is a reference to that rule in the Act of Sederunt (Sheriff Appeal Court Rules) 2015 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0][^f00005].

## 4. Basis of charging (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-68eff0610f62ab97ffc10603c259ff15][^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(1) A solicitor may prepare an account of expenses on the basis of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

- (a) Schedule 1 (detailed fees); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]
- (b) Schedule 2 (inclusive fees). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(2) A solicitor may not prepare an account of expenses partly on the basis of Schedule 1 and partly on the basis of Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(3) Only the proper expenses of process may be included in an account of expenses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

## 5. Outlays (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7ffd78fcb46c0ecfca0c2364a6f1e825][^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

Any outlays reasonably incurred by a solicitor in relation to an appeal may be included in an account of expenses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

## 6. Value added tax (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-26aee923b5eb10d238d0ca46cef4d76d][^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(1) This paragraph applies where work done by a solicitor constitutes a supply of services in respect of which value added tax is chargeable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(2) The amount of value added tax chargeable may be included in an account of expenses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

## 7. Copying of documents (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7ad22177916586abce6e39328965c615][^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(1) This paragraph applies where a solicitor proposes to include a charge for copying of documents in an account of expenses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(2) Where the copying was done other than in the place of business of the solicitor, it is to be shown as an outlay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(3) The account of expenses must specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

- (a) the purpose of the copying; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]
- (b) the number of copies made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]
- (c) the amount of the charge or outlay that the solicitor proposes should be allowed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(4) The auditor of court is only to allow a charge or outlay if the auditor determines that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

- (a) the copying had to be done in circumstances which were in some way exceptional; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]
- (b) the documents which required to be copied were unusually numerous having regard to the nature of the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(5) Where the auditor allows a charge or outlay, the auditor is to allow an amount that the auditor considers reasonable in the circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

## 8. Travel time (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0ac1dd027f882aa9dc0132da5031a8c3][^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

Where a charge for travel time is included in an account of expenses, the auditor is only to allow it on cause shown. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

## 9. Employment of advocate or solicitor advocate (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-52c5a9018777df99a81bef2b163076ee][^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(1) This paragraph applies where the Court has sanctioned work in an appeal as suitable for the employment of counsel. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(2) Where an advocate or a solicitor advocate is instructed, the auditor is to allow— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

- (a) the reasonable fees of an advocate or a solicitor advocate for doing that work; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]
- (b) the applicable fees for instructing an advocate or a solicitor advocate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(3) Where a consultation is reasonably required in relation to that work, the auditor may allow— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

- (a) the reasonable fees of an advocate or a solicitor advocate for the consultation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]
- (b) the applicable fees for attending a consultation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(4) Except on cause shown, the auditor may only allow fees under paragraph (3) in respect of one consultation in the course of the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(5) Where a solicitor advocate is not instructed by another solicitor, the auditor is not to allow the fees mentioned in subparagraphs (2)(b) and (3)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

## 10. Disallowance of expenses (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-5bd64b2583d92efb7e518aa1d368558a][^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(1) At taxation, the auditor may only allow expenses if they are reasonable for conducting the appeal in a proper manner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

(2) The auditor is to disallow any expenses— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

- (a) that the auditor considers to be unnecessary; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]
- (b) in relation to any part of the appeal where the party entitled to expenses was unsuccessful; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]
- (c) where any part of the expenses have been incurred through the fault of the party entitled to them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

## 11. Inclusive fees: modification by auditor (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-02ab6120105fbbe2f2e59b1ff1d7bac2][^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

Where an account of expenses is prepared on the basis of Schedule 2 (inclusive fees), the auditor may increase or reduce any fee if the auditor thinks it appropriate to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5440f2908aab2fd8f7fec3b6de1e9ef0]

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt regulates the taxation of accounts of expenses between parties in relation to proceedings in the Sheriff Appeal Court.

Paragraph 4 provides that an account of expenses may be prepared either on the basis of the detailed fees set out in Schedule 1, or on the basis of the inclusive fees set out in Schedule 2.

Paragraphs 5 and 6 respectively provide that reasonably incurred outlays and VAT may be included in an account.

Paragraph 7 sets out the procedure to be followed where a solicitor wishes to include a charge for the copying of documents in an account.

Paragraph 8 provides that a charge for travel time is only to be allowed by the auditor on cause shown.

Paragraph 9 regulates the allowance of fees of advocates and solicitor advocates as outlays.

Paragraph 10 sets out the principles to be applied by the auditor in taxing an account.

Paragraph 11 enables the auditor to modify fees where an account has been prepared on the basis of the inclusive fees set out in Schedule 2.

Schedule 1 specifies the charges that may be included in an account if it is prepared on a detailed fee basis.

Schedule 2 specifies the charges that may be included in an account if it is prepared on an inclusive fee basis. The fees in Part 1 apply to appeals under the standard appeal procedure or the accelerated appeal procedure. Special provision is made in Parts 2 and 3 for certain special appeal proceedings: Part 2 applies to applications for a new trial or to enter a jury verdict, and Part 3 applies to appeals from summary causes and small claims.
