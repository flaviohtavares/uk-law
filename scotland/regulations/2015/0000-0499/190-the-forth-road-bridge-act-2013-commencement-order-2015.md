# The Forth Road Bridge Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 190 (C. 30)

Roads And Bridges

The Forth Road Bridge Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2015

Made

6th May 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

8th May 2015

Coming into force

1st June 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7(2) of the Forth Road Bridge Act 2013[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Forth Road Bridge Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2015 and comes into force on 1st June 2015.

## 2. Appointed day

1st June 2015 is appointed as the day for the coming into force of the Forth Road Bridge Act 2013, so far as not then already in force.

DEREK MACKAY

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House

Edinburgh

6th May 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Sections 6 to 8 of the Forth Road Bridge Act 2013 came into force on 29th June 2013 (the day after that Act received Royal Assent). This Order appoints 1st June 2015 as the day on which the remaining provisions of that Act will come into force.
