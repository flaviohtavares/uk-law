# The Town and Country Planning (Hazardous Substances Inquiry Session Procedure) (Scotland) Amendment Rules 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This SSI has been printed to correct errors in [S.S.I. 2015/182](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2015/182) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that instrument.

2015 No. 250

Tribunals And Inquiries

The Town and Country Planning (Hazardous Substances Inquiry Session Procedure) (Scotland) Amendment Rules 2015

Made

4th June 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

8th June 2015

Coming into force

14th September 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following Rules in exercise of the powers conferred by section 9 of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Town and Country Planning (Hazardous Substances Inquiry Session Procedure) (Scotland) Amendment Rules 2015 and come into force on 14th September 2015.

## 2. Amendment of the Town and Country Planning (Hazardous Substances Inquiry Session Procedure) (Scotland) Rules 2015

(1) The Town and Country Planning (Hazardous Substances Inquiry Session Procedure) (Scotland) Rules 2015[^f00002] are amended in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (3).

(2) In rule 4(1) (notice of inquiry session and specified matters) omit “must be given”.

(3) In the Schedule (closed evidence)—

- (a) in paragraph 3, for “is restricted” substitute “are restricted”; and
- (b) in paragraph 10(1), in the definition of “appointed representative” after “(who is also a specified person)” insert “appointed”.
