# The Non-Domestic Rating (Valuation of Utilities) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 50

Rating And Valuation

The Non-Domestic Rating (Valuation of Utilities) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2015

Made

3rd February 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

5th February 2015

Coming into force

1st April 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6A(1)(aa) and (1B) of the Valuation and Rating (Scotland) Act 1956[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 6A(1D) of that Act[^f00002] they have consulted with such associations of local authorities and such other persons as they think appropriate.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rating (Valuation of Utilities) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2015 and comes into force on 1st April 2015.

## 2. Amendment of the Non-Domestic Rating (Valuation of Utilities) (Scotland) Order 2005

(1) The Non-Domestic Rating (Valuation of Utilities) (Scotland) Order 2005[^f00003] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 3(2) (Electricity Transmission and Distribution) for “SP Distribution Limited” substitute “SP Distribution plc”.

(3) In the table forming part of article 7A(2) (Fixed Line Telecommunications)[^f00004] in the first column (Company name), for “Vtesse Networks Limited” substitute “Interoute Vtesse Limited”.

JOHN SWINNEY

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

3rd February 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Non-Domestic Rating (Valuation of Utilities) (Scotland) Order 2005 to reflect the change of name of a company listed in article 3(2) and of a company listed in article 7A(2).
