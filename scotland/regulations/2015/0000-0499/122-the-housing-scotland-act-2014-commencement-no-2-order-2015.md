# The Housing (Scotland) Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 122 (C. 26)

Housing

The Housing (Scotland) Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2015

Made

18th March 2015

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

20th March 2015

Coming into force

1st April 2015

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 104(3) of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Housing (Scotland) Act 2014 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2015 and comes into force on 1st April 2015.

## 2. Appointed day

(1) 1st April 2015 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2014 specified in paragraph (2).

(2) The provisions are—

- (a) section 85 (Tenement management scheme), insofar as not already in force;
- (b) section 87 (Work notices);
- (c) section 88 (Maintenance plans);
- (d) section 90 (Non-residential premises: repayment charges);
- (e) section 91 (Repayment charges: recovery of repayable amount); and
- (f) section 92 (Charging orders).

M J BURGESS

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

18th March 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 1st April 2015 the provisions of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2014 (“the 2014 Act”) listed in article 2.
