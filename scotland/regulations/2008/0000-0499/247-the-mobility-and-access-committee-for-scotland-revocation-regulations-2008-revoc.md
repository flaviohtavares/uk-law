# The Mobility and Access Committee for Scotland Revocation Regulations 2008 Revocation Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 247

TRANSPORT

The Mobility and Access Committee for Scotland Revocation Regulations 2008 Revocation Regulations 2008

Made

12th June 2008

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

13th June 2008

Coming into force

14th June 2008

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 72 and 81(2) of the Transport (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Mobility and Access Committee for Scotland Revocation Regulations 2008 Revocation Regulations 2008 and come into force on 14th June 2008.

## 2. Revocation

The Mobility and Access Committee for Scotland Revocation Regulations 2008[^f00002] are revoked.

STEWART STEVENSON

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

12th June 2008

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Mobility and Access Committee for Scotland Revocation Regulations 2008.

These Regulations mean that the Mobility and Access Committee for Scotland will continue in existence beyond 1st July 2008.
