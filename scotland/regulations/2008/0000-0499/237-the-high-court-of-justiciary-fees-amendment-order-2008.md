# The High Court of Justiciary Fees Amendment Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 237

HIGH COURT OF JUSTICIARY

The High Court of Justiciary Fees Amendment Order 2008

Made

6th June 2008

Laid before the Parliament

6th June 2008

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2 of the Courts of Law Fees (Scotland) Act 1895[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the High Court of Justiciary Fees Amendment Order 2008 and, subject to paragraph (2), shall come into force on 1st August 2008.

(2) Article 2(b) and Schedule 2 shall come into force, and article 2(a) and Schedule 1 shall cease to have effect, on 1st April 2010.

## 2. Amendment of the High Court of Justiciary Fees Order 1984

The Table of Fees in the Schedule to the High Court of Justiciary Fees Order 1984[^f00002] shall be substituted by–

- (a) the Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to this Order on 1st August 2008; and
- (b) the Table of Fees in Schedule 2 to this Order on 1st April 2010.

KENNY MACASKILL

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

6th June 2008

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies new fee levels payable to the Principal Clerk of Justiciary or any officer acting for the Principal Clerk in relation to proceedings in the High Court of Justiciary. Fee levels were last amended on 16th July 2007 by the High Court of Justiciary Fees Amendment Order 2007 ([S.S.I. 2007/321](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2007/321)). These new fees will be applicable from 1st August 2008 and are found in the Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to this Order.

This Order also specifies new fee levels which will be applicable from 1st April 2010, replacing those fees applicable from 1st August 2008. These fees are found in the Table of Fees in Schedule 2 to this Order.
