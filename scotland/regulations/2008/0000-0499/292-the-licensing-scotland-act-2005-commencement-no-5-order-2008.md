# The Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 292 (C. 24)

LICENSING (LIQUOR)

The Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2008

Made

1st September 2008

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 146(2) and 150(2) of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 5) Order 2008.

## 2. Commencement

5th September 2008 is appointed as the day for the coming into force of section 123 of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005.

KENNY MACASKILL

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

1st September 2008

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 123 (excluded premises) of the Licensing (Scotland) Act 2005 into force on 5th September 2008.
