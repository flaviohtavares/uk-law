# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) (Scotland) Regulations 2008, section 103

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 103. Appropriate fund and appropriate administering authority[^c-63d198bdb80002970f6abc1938c3d85e]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

For a pension credit member–

- (a) the appropriate fund is the fund which is the appropriate fund for the transferor on the transfer date; and
- (b) the appropriate administering authority is the administering authority of the fund.
