# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Administration) (Scotland) Regulations 2008, section 44

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 44. Pension increases under the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^c-446144f72034ce4dd8ccd4bcf72b05b9]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

Any increase in a pension required by reason of Chapter 3 of Part 4 of the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^f00035] (protection of increases in guaranteed minimum pensions: anti franking) must be paid from the appropriate fund.
