## 82. Changes of fund and variable time employees[^c-bc1cf1e59b6122a3a45224278f0e5433]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) An inter fund transfer under regulation 81(2) in respect of a member who is a variable time employee prior to the transfer and who remains a variable time employee after the transfer shall be on the basis that his period of membership transfers on a day for day basis.
