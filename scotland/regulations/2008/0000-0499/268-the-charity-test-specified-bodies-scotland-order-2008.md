2008 No. 268

CHARITIES

The Charity Test (Specified Bodies) (Scotland) Order 2008

Made

26th June 2008

Coming into force

27th June 2008

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7(5) of the Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 103(5) of that Act, a draft of the Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement[^c-1de0e251fbc9675edf6322143835ea18]

This Order may be cited as the Charity Test (Specified Bodies) (Scotland) Order 2008 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Specified Bodies[^c-a1fac97df03ee04cdcc45eda1258f761]

Section 7(4)(b) of the Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005 is disapplied in relation to the bodies specified in the Schedule to this Order.

(This note is not part of the Order)

By this Order Scottish Ministers have disapplied paragraph (b) of section 7(4) of the Charities and Trustee Investment (Scotland) Act 2005 (“the 2005 Act”) in relation to the bodies specified in the Schedule to the Order.

Section 7(4)(b) of the 2005 Act provides that if a body’s constitution expressly permits the Scottish Ministers or a Minister of the Crown to direct or otherwise control its activities, it will not meet the charity test in the 2005 Act.
