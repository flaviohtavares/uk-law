# The Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 43

ROADS AND BRIDGES

The Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2008

Made

19th February 2008

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

20th February 2008

Coming into force

1st April 2008

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 134 of the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 163A of that Act[^f00002] they have consulted with such–

- (a) persons considered by them to be representative of the interests of undertakers;
- (b) road works authorities; and
- (c) other persons,

as they think appropriate.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2008 and shall come into force on 1st April 2008.

## 2. Amendment of the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2003

In regulation 3(1) of the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2003[^f00003] for “£24.00” substitute “£25.00”.

## 3. Revocation

The Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2007[^f00004] are revoked.

STEWART STEVENSON

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

19th February 2008

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations provide for an increase to £25.00 (previously £24.00) in the fee payable by undertakers for inspections of their work by road works authorities in Scotland under regulation 3(1) of the Road Works (Inspection Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2003 and revokes a previous instrument which increased the fees in 2007.
