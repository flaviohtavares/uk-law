# The Shrimp Fishing Nets (Scotland) Amendment Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 10

SEA FISHERIES

The Shrimp Fishing Nets (Scotland) Amendment Order 2008

Made

17th January 2008

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

18th January 2008

Coming into force

7th February 2008

The Scottish Ministers, make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(1) of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them to do so:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Shrimp Fishing Nets (Scotland) Amendment Order 2008 and shall come into force on 7th February 2008.

## 2. Amendment of the Shrimp Fishing Nets (Scotland) Order 2004

In article 3 (prohibition on fishing for shrimps without a separator trawl or sorting grid) of the Shrimp Fishing Nets (Scotland) Order 2004[^f00002] for paragraph (2)(d) substitute–

> - (d) where sea fish have been caught, the quantity of either common shrimp (*Crangon* spp.) or Aesop shrimp (*Pandalus montagui*) or a combination of the two retained on board does not exceed 5% of the total live weight of catch retained on board in accordance with Article 25 of Council Regulation [(EC) No. 850/98](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1998/0850)[^f00003].

.

RICHARD LOCHHEAD

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

17th January 2008

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends one of the specified exceptions to the prohibition against carrying or deploying a net with a mesh size of between 16 and 31 millimetres provided for in article 3 of the Shrimp Fishing Nets (Scotland) Order 2004.

The exception is that 5% or less by the live weight of the catch retained on board comprises common shrimp, Aesop shrimp or a combination of the two.

No Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared in respect of this Order.
