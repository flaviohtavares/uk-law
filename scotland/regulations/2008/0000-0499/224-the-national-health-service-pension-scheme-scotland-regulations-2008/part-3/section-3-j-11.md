# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2008, section 3.J.11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.J.11. General prohibition on unauthorised payments

Nothing in these rules requires or authorises the making of any payment, which, if made, would be an unauthorised payment for the purposes of Part 4 of the 2004 Act (see section 160(5)[^f00151] (payments by registered pension schemes) of that Act).
