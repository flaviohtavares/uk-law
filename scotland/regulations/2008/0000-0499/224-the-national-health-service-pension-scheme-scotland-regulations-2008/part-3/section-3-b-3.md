# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2008, section 3.B.3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.B.3. Concurrent employments

A practitioner may participate in the scheme in respect of employment as a practitioner even if he or she also participates in the scheme in Part 2 in respect of concurrent whole-time or part time employment as an officer (within the meaning of Part 2).
