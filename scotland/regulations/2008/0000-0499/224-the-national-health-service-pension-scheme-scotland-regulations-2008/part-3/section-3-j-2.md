# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2008, section 3.J.2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.J.2. Claims for benefits

A person claiming to be entitled to benefits under this Part must make a claim in writing to the Scottish Ministers and provide such evidence of entitlement as the Scottish Ministers may require.
