# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2008, section 3.A.14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3.A.14. Limit on pensionable earnings: dental practitioners carrying on deceased person’s business

In the case of a dental practitioner employed by persons carrying on a deceased practitioner’s dentistry business, pensionable earnings cannot exceed the total of the amount paid to him or her by those persons, plus any amounts paid to him or her by a Health Board that those persons allow him or her to retain.
