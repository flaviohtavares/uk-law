# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2008, section 3.A.13

## 3.A.13. Exclusions and deductions from pensionable earnings: all practitioners[^c-e9a052d7dd1a29487485d2222be1388e]

*Extent: S*

Any sum that is withheld or otherwise recovered from a practitioner under the National Health Service (Service Committees and Tribunal) (Scotland) Regulations 1992[^f00125] will be excluded or deducted from the practitioner’s pensionable earnings in such manner and to such extent as the Scottish Ministers may approve.
