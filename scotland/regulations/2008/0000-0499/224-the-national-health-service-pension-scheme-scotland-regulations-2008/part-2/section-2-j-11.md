## 2.J.11. General prohibition on unauthorised payments[^c-41208e8bfcb5edc3cd110a881fff8033]

*Extent: S*

(1) Nothing in these rules requires or authorises the making of any payment, which, if made, would be an unauthorised payment for the purposes of Part 4 of the 2004 Act (see section 160(5) of that Act (payments by registered pensions schemes)[^f00079]).
