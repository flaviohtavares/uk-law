# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2008, section 2.J.1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.J.1. Appointment of scheme administrator

For the purposes of this Part and of Part 4 of the 2004 Act, the scheme administrator is the Scottish Public Pensions Agency.
