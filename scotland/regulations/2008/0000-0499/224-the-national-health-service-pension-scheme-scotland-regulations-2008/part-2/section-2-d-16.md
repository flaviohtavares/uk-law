# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2008, section 2.D.16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.D.16. Reduction in pension debit member’s benefits

(1) The benefits to which a pension debit member is entitled under this Chapter are subject to the reduction to be made under section 31 (reduction of benefit) of the 1999 Act.
