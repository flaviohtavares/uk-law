# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2008, section 2.H.7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.H.7. Application of this Chapter to part-time employment

(1) This regulation provides for the application of this Chapter where the old employment or the new employment is a part-time employment.

(2) The reference in regulation 2.H.5(1)(a) to the employed pensioner’s reckonable pay for the purposes of the old service pension must be taken as a reference to such amount as in the opinion of the Scottish Ministers would have been the reckonable pay for those purposes if–

- (a) the old employment had been a comparable whole-time employment; and
- (b) accordingly the member’s reckonable pay had been determined without reference to regulation 2.A.13 (meaning of “reckonable pay”: non-concurrent part-time employment) or 2.A.14 (meaning of “reckonable pay”: concurrent part-time employments).
