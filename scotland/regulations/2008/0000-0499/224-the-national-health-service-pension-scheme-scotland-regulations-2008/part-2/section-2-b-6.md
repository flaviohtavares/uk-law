# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2008, section 2.B.6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2.B.6. Restriction on further participation in the scheme

(1) A person who ceases to meet any of the conditions in regulation 2.B.1 in an employment or is prevented by regulation 2.B.2 or 2.B.3 from continuing to be an active member in an employment must cease to be an active member of the scheme in that employment.

(2) Accordingly–

- (a) a person falling within paragraph (1) may not make any further contributions to the scheme under Chapter 2.C; and
- (b) any further service of the person is not pensionable service for the purposes of the scheme.
