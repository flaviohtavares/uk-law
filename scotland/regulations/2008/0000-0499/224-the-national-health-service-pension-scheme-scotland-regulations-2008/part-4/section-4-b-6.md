## 4.B.6. Cases with less than one year of concurrent officer service[^c-4544d9adbe11993318c4414fd066b316]

*Extent: S*

Where a member has less than one year of pensionable service as an officer concurrently with pensionable service as a practitioner, the reference amount is the amount determined by calculation method A.
