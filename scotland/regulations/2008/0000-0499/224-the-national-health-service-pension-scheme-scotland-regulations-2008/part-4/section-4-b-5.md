# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2008, section 4.B.5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4.B.5. Cases with more than one year of officer service

Where a member has been employed as an officer for one year or more after ceasing to be a practitioner, the reference amount is the better of–

- (a) the amount determined by calculation method C; and
- (b) the base amount.
