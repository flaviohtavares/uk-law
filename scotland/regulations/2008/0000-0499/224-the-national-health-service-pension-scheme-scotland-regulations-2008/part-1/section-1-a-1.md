# The National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2008, section 1.A.1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1.A.1. Citation, commencement and effect

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Health Service Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2008.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 28th June 2008, but have effect from 1st April 2008[^f00004].

## Annotations

[^f00004]: Footnote: Section 12(1) of the Superannuation Act 1972 provides that Regulations may be framed so as to have effect from a date earlier than that of their making.
