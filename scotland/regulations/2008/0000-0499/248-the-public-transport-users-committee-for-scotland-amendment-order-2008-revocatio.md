# The Public Transport Users' Committee for Scotland Amendment Order 2008 Revocation Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 248

TRANSPORT

The Public Transport Users' Committee for Scotland Amendment Order 2008 Revocation Order 2008

Made

12th June 2008

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

13th June 2008

Coming into force

14th June 2008

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 41(1) and (2)(a) of the Transport (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Public Transport Users' Committee for Scotland Amendment Order 2008 Revocation Order 2008 and comes into force on 14th June 2008.

## 2. Revocation

The Public Transport Users' Committee for Scotland Amendment Order 2008[^f00002] is revoked.

STEWART STEVENSON

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

12th June 2008

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Public Transport Users' Committee for Scotland Amendment Order 2008.
