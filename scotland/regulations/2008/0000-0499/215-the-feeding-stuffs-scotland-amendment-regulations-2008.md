# The Feeding Stuffs (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Coming into force

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 66(1), 68(1), 74A and 84 of the Agriculture Act 1970[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

There has been consultation as required by section 84(1) of the Agriculture Act 1970 with such persons or organisations appearing to represent the interests concerned and as required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00002].

## 1. Citation and Commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Feeding Stuffs (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2008 and come into force on 30th July 2008.

## 2. Amendment of the Feeding Stuffs (Scotland) Regulations 2005

In Chapter A of Schedule 7 (permitted feeding stuffs intended for particular nutritional purposes and provisions relating to their use) to the Feeding Stuffs (Scotland) Regulations 2005[^f00003], for the entries relating to reduction of the risk of milk fever, substitute the entries set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

These Regulations make further amendments to the Feeding Stuffs (Scotland) Regulations 2005 (“the Feeding Stuffs Regulations”).

These Regulations provide for the implementation of Commission Directive [2008/4/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0004) (“the Directive”) amending Directive [94/39/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1994/0039) as regards feedingstuffs intended for the reduction of the risk of milk fever (O.J. No. L6, 10.1.2008, p.4) (as corrected by a Corrigendum (O.J. No. L 22, 25.1.2008, p.21). The Directive permits, subject to specified labelling requirements, two additional types of dietetic feed to be marketed for the reduction of milk fever in dairy cows.

These Regulations amend the table in Schedule 7 (permitted feeding stuffs intended for particular nutritional purposes and provisions relating to their use) to the Feeding Stuffs Regulations, by replacing the existing entries relating to the reduction of the risk of milk fever with the revised entries contained in the Directive (regulation 2).

A regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument, as it has no impact on the costs of business.
