# The Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) (Scotland) (No. 2) Regulations 2008

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2008-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ssi/2008/295
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

2008 No. 295

CLEAN AIR

The Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) (Scotland) (No. 2) Regulations 2008

Made

3rd September 2008

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

4th September 2008

Coming into force

1st October 2008

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 20(6) and 63(1) of the Clean Air Act 1993[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^c-f8659f87b7f92d772ea4670a5ca382aa]

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) (Scotland) (No. 2) Regulations 2008 and come into force on 1st October 2008.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

## 2. Authorised fuels for the purposes of Part III of the Clean Air Act 1993[^c-2caba3fed38601ae417acfcffec028a8]

Anthracite, semi anthracite, electricity, gas, low volatile steam coals and the fuels described in the Schedule are declared to be authorised fuels for the purposes of Part III of the Clean Air Act 1993.

## 3. Revocation and savings[^c-168d6d15ab612ef3d3c62028f3530251]

(1) The Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) (Scotland) Regulations 2008[^f00002] are revoked.

(2) Any fuel manufactured before 1st October 2008 which, immediately before that date, was an authorised fuel for the purposes of Part III of the Clean Air Act 1993 continues to be an authorised fuel for those purposes notwithstanding the revocation of the Regulations referred to in paragraph (1).

These Regulations, which extend to Scotland only, declare the fuels which are authorised fuels for the purposes of Part III of the Clean Air Act 1993. The authorised fuels are described in regulation 2 and the Schedule.

The Regulations revoke (regulation 3(1)) and re-enact the Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) (Scotland) Regulations 2008 with the addition of a further authorised fuel, Therma briquettes (paragraph 43 of the Schedule).

A savings provision ensures that authorised fuels manufactured before 1st October 2008 may still be used in a smoke control area (regulation 3(2)).

No Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared for this instrument as it has no impact on the costs of business.
