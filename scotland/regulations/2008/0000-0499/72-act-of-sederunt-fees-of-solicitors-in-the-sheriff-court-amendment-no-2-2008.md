# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment No. 2) 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Scottish Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.S.I. 2008/40](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2008/40) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that instrument.

2008 No. 72

SHERIFF COURT

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment No. 2) 2008

Made

29th February 2008

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

4th March 2008

Coming into force

31st March 2008

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment No. 2) 2008 and shall come into force on 31st March 2008.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 2008

In paragraph 2(1) of the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 2008[^f00002] (amendment of general regulations) after “claims” insert “falling within paragraph 1 (actions of a value from £1,000 to £2,500) of the following table”.

A C HAMILTON

Lord President I.P.D.

Edinburgh

29th February 2008

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt corrects an error in the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 2008.
