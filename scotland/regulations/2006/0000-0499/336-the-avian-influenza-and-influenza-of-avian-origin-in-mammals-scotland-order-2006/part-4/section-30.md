# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, section 30

> Text as at 2025-05-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 30. Restrictions on trade in things from areas where measures have been disapplied

*Extent: S*

No person shall allow any poultry, other captive bird, hatching egg, used litter, manure or slurry from premises in respect of which any of the measures in Schedules 4 or 5 have been disapplied under articles 28 or 29 to be supplied for export outside the United Kingdom[^c-1012159222765f6a01330580a672e21a], unless licensed to do so by the Scottish Ministers.
