# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, section 23

> Text as at 2025-05-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 23. Veterinary inquiry at infected premises

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers must ensure that veterinary inquiries continue at all infected premises for such period as they consider necessary.
