# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, section 32

> Text as at 2025-05-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 32. Measures in restricted zones

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers must, in a declaration of a restricted zone under article 26(5), apply such measures as they consider necessary to reduce the risk of the spread of avian influenza.

(2) These measures may include some or all of the measures set out in Schedules 4 and 5 and those referred to in article 33.
