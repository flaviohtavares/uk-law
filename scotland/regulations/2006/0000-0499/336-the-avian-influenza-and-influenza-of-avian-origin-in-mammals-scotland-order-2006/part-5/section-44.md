# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, section 44

> Text as at 2025-05-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 44. Measures at other premises

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers must, if they suspect that highly pathogenic avian influenza or highly pathogenic avian influenza virus exists at any slaughterhouse or in any vehicle or have confirmed that it exists there–

- (a) apply the measures set out in Schedule 1 to the premises of origin;
- (b) apply the measures set out in Schedule 1 to premises which they consider to be suspect premises (because of their epidemiological link with the premises of origin);
- (c) apply the measures set out in Schedule 2 to the premises of origin, unless they are satisfied (having considered the results of veterinary inquiries) that this is not necessary.

(2) The Scottish Ministers must, if they suspect that highly pathogenic avian influenza exists at any border control[^key-a3182beb602e3d0757ce371b02a2baa1] post or have confirmed that it exists there, apply the measures set out in Schedule 1 to premises which they consider to be suspect premises (because of their epidemiological link with the border control post).

(3) The Scottish Ministers must apply measures under this article by notice to the occupier of the relevant premises.

(4) In this article, “**premises of origin**” means any premises from which poultry or other captive birds which may be infected were moved to the slaughterhouse or onto the vehicle.
