# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, section 39

> Text as at 2025-05-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 39. Measures at border control posts[^c-8ada9ade6e214b3891267ac6b74af10b]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers must if they consider it necessary to reduce the risk of the spread of disease–

- (a) require a veterinary inquiry to be carried out at a border control[^c-aa2eed19ce8bfe06737918f015ee25bf] post where avian influenza is suspected or has been confirmed; and
- (b) impose (by notice to the occupier of the border control post) some or all of the measures set out in Schedule 1.

(2) The Scottish Ministers must, by notice to the occupier of the border control post direct where birds specified in the notice are to be killed, slaughtered or isolated.

(3) The Scottish Ministers must ensure that poultry and other captive birds at border control posts which are to be killed under paragraph 5 of Schedule 3 to the Act or the Regulations are killed or slaughtered without delay.

(4) The Scottish Ministers must ensure that poultry and other captive birds which are not to be so killed or slaughtered are kept isolated in accordance with a veterinary inspector's instructions.
