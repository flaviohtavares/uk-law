# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, section 35

> Text as at 2025-05-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 35. Veterinary measures at slaughterhouses

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers must, if they suspect that highly pathogenic avian influenza exists at any slaughterhouse or have confirmed that it exists there–

- (a) serve a notice on the occupier of the slaughterhouse informing the occupier; and
- (b) ensure that a veterinary inquiry is carried out at the slaughterhouse.

(2) No person shall move poultry from any slaughterhouse where highly pathogenic avian influenza is suspected or has been confirmed.
