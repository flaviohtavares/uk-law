# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, section 71

> Text as at 2025-05-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 71. Duty to provide reasonable assistance

*Extent: S*

(1) Any person required to give reasonable assistance or information to a person executing this Order must, unless the person has reasonable cause, do so without delay.

(2) The occupier of any premises from, or to, which the occupier wishes any thing to be moved under licence under this Order must allow any person authorised by the Scottish Ministers to enter those premises for the purposes of deciding whether or not such a licence should be granted or maintained.
