# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, section 75

> Text as at 2025-05-29.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 75. Duty to comply with declarations, licences, notices and designations

*Extent: S*

Any person to whom any requirement in a declaration, licence, notice or designation under this Order applies must–

- (a) comply with the requirement (unless authorised otherwise by licence); and
- (b) comply with any reasonable requests which an inspector may make to that person to ensure that the requirement is met.
