# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, section 86

> Text as at 2025-05-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 86. Enforcement

*Extent: S*

(1) The local authority must, subject to paragraph (2), enforce this Order.

(2) The Scottish Ministers may, in relation to particular cases or cases of a particular description, direct that they will enforce this Order.
