# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, section 79

> Text as at 2025-05-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 79. Redirection of mammals, birds and eggs in transit

*Extent: S*

A veterinary inspector may, by notice to the owner of driver or any vehicle moving any mammal, bird or egg or any other thing to any premises (including regulated places) where avian influenza or influenza virus of avian origin is suspected or confirmed, require any mammal, bird, egg or any other things specified in the notice to be moved to other premises specified in the notice instead.
