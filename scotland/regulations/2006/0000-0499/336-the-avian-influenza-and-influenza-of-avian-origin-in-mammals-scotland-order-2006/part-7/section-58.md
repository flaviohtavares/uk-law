# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, section 58

> Text as at 2025-05-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 58. Movements off the premises[^key-ff9f37d8e00a6d0e135c0cab4f94ab3e]

*Extent: S*

No person may move a mammal tested under article 56 off the premises until a veterinary inspector has confirmed that the mammal is not infective and licenses the movement.
