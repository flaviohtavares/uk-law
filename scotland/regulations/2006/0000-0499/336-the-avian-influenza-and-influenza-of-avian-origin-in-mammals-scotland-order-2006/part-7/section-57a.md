# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, section 57A

> Text as at 2025-05-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 57A. Compensation[^c-ac08a50ff2ba6e8d239a3b3053319188]

*Extent: S*

Where the Scottish Ministers cause a mammal to be slaughtered under section 32 of the Act in its application to influenza virus of avian origin, they must pay compensation as follows—

- (a) where the mammal slaughtered was infected with influenza virus of avian origin the compensation payable is the value of the mammal immediately before it became so infected,
- (b) in every other case the compensation payable is the value of the mammal immediately before it was slaughtered.
