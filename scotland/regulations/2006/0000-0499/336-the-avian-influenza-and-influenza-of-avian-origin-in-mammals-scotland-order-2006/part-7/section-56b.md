# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, section 56B

> Text as at 2025-05-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 56B. Restrictions on mammals on confirmation of influenza virus of avian origin[^key-1bf66f1fa8c01671f00f21ee2d741055]

*Extent: S*

If the Scottish Ministers confirm that influenza virus of avian origin exists on any premises, they may (by notice served on the occupier of the premises) impose, in addition to the restrictions and requirements set out in schedule 8, the restrictions and requirements set out in schedule 9.
