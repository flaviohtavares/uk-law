# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, Schedule 8

> Text as at 2025-05-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## Schedule 8 — Measures where influenza virus of avian origin is suspected in kept mammals[^key-29610b2d03caf2294b91cdc0ab154c0e]

*Extent: S*

Articles 56A, 56B, 59

### 1. Record of kept mammals and birds

The occupier must make and update daily a record of mammals, poultry or other captive birds kept on the premises showing for each species the number or approximate number—

- (a) alive,
- (b) which have died,
- (c) which show clinical signs of avian influenza or influenza virus of avian origin,
- (d) which have been born on the premises.

### 2. Separation or isolation of mammals

The occupier must, in accordance with the instructions of a veterinary inspector—

- (a) ensure that all mammals are kept separately to or isolated from other mammals, poultry and other captive birds on the premises, and
- (b) take all reasonable steps to minimise any contact on the premises with wild mammals or wild birds.

### 3. Prohibition on the movement of mammals and birds to and from the premises

No person may move any mammal, poultry or other captive bird to or from the premises, unless licensed by a veterinary inspector.

### 4. Prohibition on the removal of other things liable to transmit avian influenza or influenza virus of avian origin

No person may remove from or bring onto the premises any carcase, mammal carcase, meat (including offal), milk and any other animal products, animal feed, material, waste, droppings, slurry, manure, germinal products or anything else liable to transmit avian influenza or influenza virus of avian origin unless—

- (a) they are licensed by a veterinary inspector to do so, and
- (b) they observe such biosecurity measures as are directed by a veterinary inspector in order to prevent the risk of spread of disease.

### 5. Prohibition on the movement of people, equipment and vehicles to and from the premises

No person may enter or leave the premises and no person may move any vehicle or equipment from the premises unless the movement is licensed by a veterinary inspector or an inspector under the direction of a veterinary inspector.

### 6. Disinfection at entrances and exits

The occupier must, in accordance with an inspector’s instructions, provide and maintain means of disinfection at the entrances and exits of the premises and of buildings housing mammals, poultry or other captive birds.
