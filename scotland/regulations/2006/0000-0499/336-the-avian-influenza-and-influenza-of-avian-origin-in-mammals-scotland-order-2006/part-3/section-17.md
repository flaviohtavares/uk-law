# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, section 17

> Text as at 2025-05-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 17. Declaration of a temporary control zone around the suspect premises

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers must in a declaration of a temporary control zone (in relation to premises in such part or parts of the zone as they think fit), impose such measures including those listed in Schedule 1 as they consider necessary to reduce the risk of the spread of avian influenza.
