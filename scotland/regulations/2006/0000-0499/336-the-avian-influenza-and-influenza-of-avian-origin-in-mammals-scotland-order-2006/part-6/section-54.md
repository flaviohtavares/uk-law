# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, section 54

> Text as at 2025-05-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 54. Size of zones

*Extent: S*

A low pathogenic avian influenza restricted zone must be centred on the part of the infected premises which the Scottish Ministers consider most appropriate and have a radius of at least 1 kilometre.
