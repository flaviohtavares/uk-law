# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Scotland) Order 2006, section 49

> Text as at 2025-05-29.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 49. Veterinary surveillance of birds

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Ministers shall make arrangements for birds which are on special category premises and which the Scottish Ministers decide are not to be killed under paragraph 5 of Schedule 3 to the Act or under regulation 4 of the Regulations, to be monitored for avian influenza.
