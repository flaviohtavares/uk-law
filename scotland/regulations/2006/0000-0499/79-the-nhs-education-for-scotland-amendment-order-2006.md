# The NHS Education for Scotland Amendment Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 79

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE

The NHS Education for Scotland Amendment Order 2006

Made

27th February 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

28th February 2006

Coming into force

23rd March 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2(1)(b) and (1C) and 105(6) of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the NHS Education for Scotland Amendment Order 2006 and shall come into force on 23rd March 2006.

## 2. Amendment of the NHS Education for Scotland Order 2002

In article 4(1) of the NHS Education for Scotland Order 2002[^f00002] (functions of the Board), for paragraph (a) substitute–

> - (a) functions in relation to education and training relating to the health service including, without prejudice to the foregoing generality, functions specified in section 47 of the Act (making available facilities for education, research and training);

.

ANDREW P KERR

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

27th February 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the NHS Education for Scotland Order 2002 (“the Order”) which constituted a Special Health Board to be known as NHS Education for Scotland (“the Board”).

Article 2 amends article 4(1)(a) of the Order to confer on the Board all the functions of the Scottish Ministers in relation to education and training relating to the health service.
