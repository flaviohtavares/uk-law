# The Regulation of Care (Applications and Provision of Advice) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 272

SOCIAL CARE

The Regulation of Care (Applications and Provision of Advice) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2006

Made

19th May 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

22nd May 2006

Coming into force

20th June 2006

The Scottish Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7(2)(a) and 14(3) of the Regulation of Care (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Regulation of Care (Applications and Provision of Advice) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2006 and shall come into force on 20th June 2006.

## 2. Amendment of the principal Order

(1) The Regulation of Care (Applications and Provision of Advice) (Scotland) Order 2002[^f00002] shall be amended as follows.

(2) After article 2(6) (disapplication of provisions)[^f00003], insert–

> (7) Where an application is for registration of an adult placement service, paragraph 12 of the Schedule shall not apply.

.

LEWIS MACDONALD

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

19th May 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Regulation of Care (Applications and Provision of Advice) (Scotland) Order 2002 so that it specifies the information which is to be provided by a person who makes an application for registration of an adult placement service under Part 1 of the Regulation of Care (Scotland) Act 2001.
