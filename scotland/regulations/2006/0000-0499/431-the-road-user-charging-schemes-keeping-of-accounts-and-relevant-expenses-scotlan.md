# The Road User Charging Schemes (Keeping of Accounts and Relevant Expenses) (Scotland) Regulations 2005 Revocation Regulations 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Scottish Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.S.I. 2005/654](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2005/654) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that instrument.

2006 No. 431

TRANSPORT

The Road User Charging Schemes (Keeping of Accounts and Relevant Expenses) (Scotland) Regulations 2005 Revocation Regulations 2006

Made

17th August 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

18th August 2006

Coming into force

30th September 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraphs 1 and 4 of schedule 1 to the Transport (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Road User Charging Schemes (Keeping of Accounts and Relevant Expenses) (Scotland) Regulations 2005 Revocation Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 30th September 2006.

## 2. Revocation

The Road User Charging Schemes (Keeping of Accounts and Relevant Expenses) (Scotland) Regulations 2005[^f00002] are revoked.

TAVISH SCOTT

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

17th August 2006

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Road User Charging Schemes (Keeping of Accounts and Relevant Expenses) (Scotland) Regulations 2005.
