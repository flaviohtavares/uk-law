# The Housing (Scotland) Act 2006 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 14 (C. 1 )

HOUSING

The Housing (Scotland) Act 2006 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2006

Made

16th January 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 195(3) of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2006[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Housing (Scotland) Act 2006 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2006.

## 2. Commencement of Provisions

The day appointed for the coming into force of sections 176 and 177 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2006 is 29th January 2006.

MALCOLM CHISHOLM

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

16th January 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 29th January 2006 sections 176 and 177 of the [Housing (Scotland) Act 2006 (asp 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2006/1). Section 176 amends the [Antisocial Behaviour etc. (Scotland) Act 2004 (asp 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2004/8), and section 177 amends the [Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (asp 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2001/10).
