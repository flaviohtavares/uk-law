# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Order 2006, section 46

## 46. Cleansing and disinfection of vehicles: provision of facilities, equipment and materials

*Extent: S*

Where cleansing and disinfection of vehicles is required at any premises by this Order or by virtue of a licence granted or directions given under it, the occupier of those premises shall, at that person's own expense, provide adequate facilities and proper equipment and materials for that cleansing and disinfection.
