# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Order 2006, section 47

> Text as at 2021-12-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 47. Marks applied under this Order

*Extent: S*

No person shall remove, obscure or erase a mark applied to any animal, carcase, animal product, vehicle or other thing under this Order unless an inspector has given written authority.
