## 3. Extension of definitions of “animals” and “poultry”

*Extent: S*

For the purposes of the Act in its application to the disease and to this Order–

- (a) the definition of “animals” in section 87(1) of the Act is extended to include all four footed beasts;
- (b) the definition of “poultry” in section 87(4) of the Act is extended to include all species of birds.
