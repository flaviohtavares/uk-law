# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Order 2006, section 1

> Text as at 2021-12-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

*Extent: S*

(1) This Order may be cited as the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 23rd February 2006.

(2) This Order extends to Scotland only.
