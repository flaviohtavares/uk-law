## 60. Amendment to the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Ascertainment of Value) (Scotland) (No. 4) Order 2001

*Extent: S*

In the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Ascertainment of Value) (Scotland) (No. 4) Order 2001 [^c15344161] in article 2(6), after the words “Animal Health Act 1981”, insert “ or regulation 8(2) or 9(1) of the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Slaughter and Vaccination) (Scotland) Regulations 2006 ”.
