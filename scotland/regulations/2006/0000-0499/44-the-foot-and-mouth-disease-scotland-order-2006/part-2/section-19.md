# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Scotland) Order 2006, section 19

> Text as at 2021-12-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 19. Measures applicable in respect of a supplementary movement control zone

*Extent: S*

(1) No person shall move any animal in respect of which a supplementary movement control zone applies on to or off any premises in such a zone or into or out of such a zone except where the movement is–

- (a) through the zone without stopping; or
- (b) necessary to complete a journey started before the creation of the zone; or
- (c) the movement of a horse for veterinary treatment under the authority of a licence granted by an inspector.

(2) Paragraph (1) applies–

- (a) without prejudice to article 16(1);
- (b) in a temporary control zone in place of article 16(2) or, where the temporary control zone was declared under article 16(3), paragraph 4 of Schedule 2; and
- (c) in addition to any requirement or restriction which applies in any part of a supplementary movement control zone because of–
  - (i) a vaccination zone or vaccination surveillance zone; or
  - (ii) a wild animal infected zone.

(3) An inspector granting a licence under paragraph (1)(c) shall take account of the recommended measures in points 2.1.1 to 2.1.7[^key-a4b1077ae0c2dedcee995bc10e2d7b18] of Annex VI to the Directive, reading point 2.1.4 as if for the words from “Decisions” to the end there were substituted[^key-222c27c69bb205090fb9b8494ba84c7d] “ Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/262 ”.
