# The Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing for Cockles) (Scotland) (No. 3) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 487

SEA FISHERIES

The Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing for Cockles) (Scotland) (No. 3) Order 2006

Made

5th October 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

5th October 2006

Coming into force

12th November 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Inshore Fishing (Scotland) Act 1984[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and having consulted such bodies as they consider appropriate, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing for Cockles) (Scotland) (No. 3) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 12th November 2006.

## 2. Prohibition of fishing for cockles

Subject to articles 3 and 4, fishing for cockles (Cerastoderma edule) from or by means of any vehicle in Scottish inshore waters is prohibited.

## 3. Excepted area

The prohibition in article 2 shall not apply to fishing for cockles within that part of Scottish inshore waters which lies within the area bounded by the mean high-water mark of ordinary spring tides, by a straight line extending due east from the Isle of Whithorn at a point at 54°41'46" North latitude and 04°22'00" West longitude and by the line referred to in article 3(b)(i) of the Scottish Adjacent Waters Boundaries Order 1999[^f00002].

## 4. Exception for scientific investigation

The prohibition in article 2 shall not apply to fishing for cockles by, or authorised by, the Scottish Ministers for the purposes of scientific investigation.

## 5. Revocation

The Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing for Cockles) (Scotland) (No. 2) Order 2006[^f00003] is revoked.

ROSS FINNIE

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

5th October 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prohibits, subject to two exceptions, fishing for cockles (Cerastoderma edule) from or by means of any vehicle in Scottish inshore waters (article 2). “Scottish inshore waters” is defined in section 9(1) of the Inshore Fishing (Scotland) Act 1984.

This prohibition does not apply in the area of the Solway Firth specified in article 3 and shown in the illustrative map which accompanies this Note, in which area such fishing may be permitted only in terms of a license issued under the Solway Firth Regulated Fishery (Scotland) Order 2006 ([S.S.I. 2006/57](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2006/57)).

Fishing for cockles by, or authorised by, the Scottish Ministers for the purpose of scientific investigation is excepted from the prohibition (article 4).

The Order revokes the Inshore Fishing (Prohibition of Fishing for Cockles) (Scotland) (No) Order 2006 (article 5).
