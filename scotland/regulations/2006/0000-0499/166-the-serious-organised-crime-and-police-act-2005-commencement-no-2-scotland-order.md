# The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No. 2) (Scotland) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 166 (C. 15 )

CRIMINAL LAW

POLICE

The Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No. 2) (Scotland) Order 2006

Made

22nd March 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 178(3), (4), (5) and (9) of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005 (Commencement No. 2) (Scotland) Order 2006.

## 2. Appointed days and extent

(1) 1st April 2006 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005–

- (a) sections 60 to 68 and 70 (investigatory powers of DPP etc.), so far as they extend to Scotland;
- (b) Chapter 4 of Part 2 (including Schedule 5) (protection of witnesses and other persons), so far as it extends to Scotland;
- (c) sections 163 (criminal record certificates) except for the purpose of inserting section 113E into the Police Act 1997[^f00002], 165 (certain references to police forces) and 166 (further amendments to Police Act 1997 as it applies to Scotland) in so far as that section is not already in force, all as they extend to Scotland;
- (d) section 174(2) (minor and consequential amendments, repeals and revocations) and Schedule 17 (repeals and revocations) so far as they relate–
  - (i) to sections 113 and 115 of the Police Act 1997 as those sections apply to Scotland; and
  - (ii) to section 125 of that Act, to the Regulation of Care (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00003], to the Protection of Children (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00004] and to the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00005]; and
- (e) Schedule 14 (except paragraph 13) (amendments of Part 5 of Police Act 1997) as it extends to Scotland.

(2) 1st May 2006 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005–

- (a) section 77 (financial reporting orders: making in Scotland); and
- (b) sections 79, 80(1) and (2) and 81 (financial reporting orders: effect, variation and revocation, verification and disclosure) so far as they extend to Scotland.

CATHY JAMIESON

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

22nd March 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force provisions of the Serious Organised Crime and Police Act 2005. The provisions specified in article 2(1) are brought into force on 1st April 2006, and those specified in article 2(2) are brought into force on 1st May 2006.
