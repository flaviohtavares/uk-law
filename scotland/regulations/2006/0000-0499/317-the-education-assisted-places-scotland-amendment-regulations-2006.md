Coming into force

1st August 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 75A(9) and (10) and 75B of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and after having consulted such bodies as appear to the Scottish Ministers to be appropriate and to be representative of participating schools in accordance with section 75A(11) of that Act, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the E ducation (Assisted Places) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 1st August 2006.

## 2. Amendment of Education (Assisted Places) (Scotland) Regulations 2001

The Education (Assisted Places) (Scotland) Regulations 2001[^f00002]are amended as follows:–

- (a) in regulation 9(3) and (5) (references to income) for the sum of “£1,573” in the three places where it occurs substitute “£1,615”;
- (b) in regulation 13(2) (scales of remission) for the sum of “£12,193” substitute “£12,522”;
- (c) in regulation 15(6) (school travel grants for day pupils and amounts thereof) for the sums of “£12,212” and “£12,016” substitute “£12,542” and “£12,340” respectively;
- (d) in regulation 16(5) (school travel grants for boarding pupils and amounts thereof) for the sums of “£12,212”and “£12,016” substitute “£12,542” and “£12,340” respectively;
- (e) for regulation 17(2) substitute–

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (Assisted Places) (Scotland) Regulations 2001 to uprate the qualifying income levels for the remission of fees and charges and the making of grants under the assisted places scheme.

The deduction made from relevant income for dependent children and relatives has been increased from £1,573 to £1,615 (regulation 2(a)).

The level of income at or below which fees are to be wholly remitted is increased from £12,193 to £12,522 with corresponding increases in the extent of remission where the relevant income exceeds that figure (regulation 2(b) and (f)).

The qualifying income levels for school travel grants and clothing grants are also uprated, with clothing grants being increased by either £2 or £1 (regulation 2(c), (d) and (e)).
