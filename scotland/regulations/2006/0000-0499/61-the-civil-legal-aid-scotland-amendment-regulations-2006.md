# The Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 61

LEGAL AID AND ADVICE

The Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2006

Made

14th February 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

16th February 2006

Coming into force

10th April 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 17(2B) and 36(1) of the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 10th April 2006.

## 2. Application

These Regulations shall apply only in relation to any case where an application for civil legal aid is made on or after 10th April 2006.

## 3. Amendment of the Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) Regulations 2002

In regulation 33(b) of the Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) Regulations 2002[^f00002], for “£4,531” substitute “£4,653”.

HUGH HENRY

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

14th February 2006

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) Regulations 2002 to provide that the requirement on a person in receipt of civil legal aid to pay the amount of any net liability of the Scottish Legal Aid Fund shall not apply to the first £4,653 recovered or preserved by virtue of certain family proceedings (this sum is increased from £4,531).
