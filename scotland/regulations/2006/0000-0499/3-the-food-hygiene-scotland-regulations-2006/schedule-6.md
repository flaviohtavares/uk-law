# The Food Hygiene (Scotland) Regulations 2006, SCHEDULE 6

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 6 — Restrictions on the placing on the market of raw milk and raw cream intended for direct human consumption

*Extent: S*

Regulation 32

**1.** No person shall place on the market raw milk, or raw cream, intended for direct human consumption.

**2.** Any person who contravenes paragraph 1 is guilty of an offence.

**3.** In any proceedings for an offence in respect of a contravention of paragraph 1, it shall be a defence for the accused to prove that the raw milk or raw cream in respect of which the offence is alleged to have been committed was intended for export–

- (a) to England, Wales or Northern Ireland [^key-8da073449a3743c76de391fae560f8ba]... and that the milk or cream complies with Regulation 853/2004 and with any national rules applicable to that part of the United Kingdom [^key-a8ce40d27fcf0b48d08c0cbeed67b805]..., made pursuant to Article 10.8(a) of Regulation 853/2004;
- (b) to a third country in accordance with Article 12 of Regulation 178/2002 as read with Article 11 of Regulation 852/2004.
