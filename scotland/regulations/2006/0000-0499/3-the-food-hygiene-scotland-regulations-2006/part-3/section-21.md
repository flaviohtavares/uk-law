## 21. Appeal to Court of Session

*Extent: S*

A person who is aggrieved by–

- (a) the dismissal of an appeal under regulation 20(1), or
- (b) a decision to make a hygiene prohibition order or a hygiene emergency prohibition order,

may appeal to the Court of Session.
