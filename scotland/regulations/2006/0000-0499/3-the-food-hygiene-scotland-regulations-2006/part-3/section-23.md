# The Food Hygiene (Scotland) Regulations 2006, section 23

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 23. Application of section 9 of the Food Safety Act 1990

*Extent: S*

Section 9 of the Act (inspection and seizure of suspected food) [^c15347981] shall apply for the purposes of these Regulations with the modification that it shall apply in relation to an authorised officer of an enforcement authority as it applies in relation to an authorised officer of a food authority.

## Annotations

[^c15347981]: Marginal citation: Section 9 was amended by the [General Food Regulations 2004 (S.I. 2004/3279)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/3279) and section 1(1) and (2) (definition of “food”) was substituted by the [Food Safety Act 1990 (Amendment) Regulations 2004 (S.I. 2004/2990)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/2990).
