# The Food Hygiene (Scotland) Regulations 2006, section 1

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

*Extent: S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Food Hygiene (Scotland) Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 11th January 2006.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.
