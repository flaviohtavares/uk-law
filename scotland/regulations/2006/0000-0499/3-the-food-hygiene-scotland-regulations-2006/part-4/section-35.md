> Text as at 2024-01-01.

## 35. Savings

*Extent: S*

For the purposes of these Regulations, any notice served or sample procured under the Food Hygiene (Scotland) Regulations 2005 [^c15348001] is deemed to have been served or procured under these Regulations.
