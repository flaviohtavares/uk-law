# The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 92

RATING AND VALUATION

The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2006

Made

1st March 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

2nd March 2006

Coming into force

1st April 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 7B(1) and 37(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 1st April 2006.

## 2. Non-domestic rate for 2006 2007

For the purposes of section 7B of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, the Scottish Ministers prescribe a rate of 44.9 pence per pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of financial year 2006 2007.

TOM McCABE

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

1st March 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes a rate of 44.9 pence in the pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of the financial year 2006–2007. A rate of 46.1 pence in the pound was the figure prescribed as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland by the Scottish Ministers for the financial year 2005 2006.
