# The Erskine Bridge (Temporary Suspension of Tolls) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 157

ROADS AND BRIDGES

The Erskine Bridge (Temporary Suspension of Tolls) Order 2006

Made

16th March 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

16th March 2006

Coming into force on 1st April 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6 of the Erskine Bridge Tolls Act 1968[^f00001] and of all others enabling them in that behalf, and having given not less than fourteen days' public notice in accordance with section 6(3) of that Act, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Erskine Bridge (Temporary Suspension of Tolls) Order 2006 and shall come into force on 1st April 2006.

## 2. Provision

The tolls leviable in accordance with the Erskine Bridge Tolls Order 1992[^f00002] shall be suspended during the period beginning on 1st April 2006 and ending on 1st July 2006 at 2400 hours.

TAVISH SCOTT

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

16th March 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order suspends tolls on the Erskine Bridge from 1st April 2006 until 2400 hours on 1st July 2006.
