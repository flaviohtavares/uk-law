# The Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 264

SEEDS

The Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2006

Made

17th May 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

19th May 2006

Coming into force

12th June 2006

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 16(1) and (1A)(e) of the Plant Varieties and Seeds Act 1964[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and after consultation in accordance with section 16(1) of that Act with representatives of such interests as appear to them to be concerned, hereby make the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2006 and shall come into force on 12th June 2006.

## 2. Amendment to the Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2005

In the Schedule to the Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2005[^f00002], for the entries in paragraph 6 (fees for nuclear stock), columns 1 and 2, substitute the entries in the following table–

17th May 2006

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which come into force on 12th June 2006, amend the Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2005 (“the 2005 Regulations”), which set fees payable to the Scottish Ministers for certain matters arising under the Seed Potatoes (Scotland) Regulations 2000. These Regulations substitute new fees for nuclear stock in paragraph 6 of the Schedule to the 2005 Regulations as follows (regulation 2)–

| Matter | Old Fee | New Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fees for nuclear stock– |  |  |
| - (a) initiation of new nuclear stock; | £152.00 (6 microplants) | £159.00 (6 microplants) |
| - (b) provision of in vitro maintained nuclear stock; and | £44.00 (6 microplants) | £46.00 (6 microplants) |
| - (c) in vitro maintenance of nuclear stock. | £62.00 annual fee (per variety) | £65.00 annual fee (per variety) |

No Regulatory Impact Assessment has been prepared in respect of these Regulations.
