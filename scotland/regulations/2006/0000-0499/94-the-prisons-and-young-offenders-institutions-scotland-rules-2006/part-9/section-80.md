# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 2006, section 80

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 80. Application of Part 9

(1) Rules 81 to 84, 86(3) and 87(2) shall not apply to untried and civil prisoners.

(2) Rules 81 to 84, 86(3),87(2) and 88 shall not apply to young prisoners.
