# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 2006, section 48

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 48. Possession of tobacco by untried and civil prisoners

An untried prisoner or a civil prisoner shall be entitled to keep tobacco in his or her possession provided that he or she is at least 16 years old.
