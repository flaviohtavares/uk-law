# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 2006, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Smoking

No prisoner shall smoke in a prison except–

- (a) where rule 22(1) applies, in a cell or room in which that prisoner is accommodated by him or herself;
- (b) where rule 22(2) applies, in a cell or room in which that prisoner is accommodated and which the Governor designates as a smoking area in accordance with a direction given by the Scottish Ministers; or
- (c) in the open air, in any place as the Scottish Ministers may specify in a direction.
