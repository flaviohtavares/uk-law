# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 2006, section 150

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 150. Fees and gratuities

No officer or employee shall–

- (a) receive any unauthorised fee, gratuity or other consideration in connection with his or her duties as an officer or employee;
- (b) directly or indirectly, have any interest in any contract in connection with the prison or any other prison; or
- (c) receive any fee, gratuity or other consideration from or on behalf of any contractor at, or any person tendering for a contract in connection with, the prison or any other prison.
