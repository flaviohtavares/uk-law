# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 2006, section 148

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 148. General duty of officers and employees

It shall be the duty of every officer and employee–

- (a) to conform to these Rules;
- (b) to obey any lawful instructions of the Governor or of the Scottish Ministers; and
- (c) to inform the Governor promptly of any breach of these Rules or any abuse or impropriety which comes to his or her knowledge.
