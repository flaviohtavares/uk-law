# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 2006, section 154

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 154. Application of Part 17

(1) Except as mentioned in paragraphs (2) and (3), this Part shall apply to visiting committees for prisons and young offenders institutions.

(2) Rule 156 does not apply to visiting committees for prisons.

(3) In the application of this part to young offenders institutions and visiting committees for such institutions–

- (a) in rule 157–
  - (i) paragraph (2) shall not apply; and
  - (ii) in paragraph (5), the words “required to be” shall be omitted; and
- (b) rules 155 and 164 and Schedule 2 and 3 shall not apply.
