# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 2006, section 8

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 8. Production of warrant, order, direction or certificate

No person shall be received as a prisoner into prison unless there exists and is produced in respect of that person a valid warrant, order, direction or certificate authorising detention in custody.
