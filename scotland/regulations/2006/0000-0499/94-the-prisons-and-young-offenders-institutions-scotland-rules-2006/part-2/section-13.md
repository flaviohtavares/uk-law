# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 2006, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Classification of prisoners

Every prisoner may be classified by the Governor according to–

- (a) age;
- (b) sex;
- (c) offence or matter in respect of which the prisoner is committed to prison;
- (d) period of sentence or committal; and
- (e) previous record.
