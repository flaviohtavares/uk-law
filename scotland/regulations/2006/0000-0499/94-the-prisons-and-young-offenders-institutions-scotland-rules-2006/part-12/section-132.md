# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 2006, section 132

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 132. Direction with respect to complaints procedures

(1) The Scottish Ministers may provide in a direction such conditions as they consider appropriate with respect to the form and manner in which–

- (a) any complaint as mentioned in rules 123 to 128 may be made or referred; and
- (b) any reply or decision in relation to such a complaint may be given.

(2) The Governor shall ensure that–

- (a) supplies of any form specified in a direction made for the purposes of paragraph (1); and
- (b) information as to where prisoners may obtain assistance in the completion of any such form,

are readily available to prisoners.
