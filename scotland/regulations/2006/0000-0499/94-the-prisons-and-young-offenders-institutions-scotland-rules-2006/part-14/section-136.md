# The Prisons and Young Offenders Institutions (Scotland) Rules 2006, section 136

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 136. Pre-release preparation

The Governor shall at the appropriate time discuss with every prisoner his or her immediate needs on release and may also arrange for any other person to see the prisoner to discuss such needs.
