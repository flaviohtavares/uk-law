# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 539

SHERIFF COURT

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 2006

Made

7th November 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

9th November 2006

Coming into force

1st January 2007

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00001], section 6 of the Execution of Diligence (Scotland) Act 1926[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 2006 and shall come into force on 1st January 2007.

(2) This Act of Sederunt shall be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of fees

(1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2) below, the Table of Fees in the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt shall be substituted for the Table in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) (No. 2) 2002[^f00003].

(2) Sub-paragraph (1) shall apply to work carried out by a sheriff officer on or after 1st January 2007 but shall not affect the fees payable as respects any work so carried out before that date.

A C HAMILTON

Lord President I.P.D.

Edinburgh

7th November 2006
