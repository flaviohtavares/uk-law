# The Register of Sasines (Application Procedure) Amendment Rules 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 568

REGISTERS AND RECORDS

The Register of Sasines (Application Procedure) Amendment Rules 2006

Made

28th November 2006

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

29th November 2006

Coming into force

22nd January 2007

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 5(2) of the Abolition of Feudal Tenure etc. (Scotland) Act 2000[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, and after consultation with the Lord President of the Court of Session, hereby make the following Rules:

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Rules may be cited as the Register of Sasines (Application Procedure) Amendment Rules 2006 and shall come into force on 22nd January 2007.

## 2. Amendment to the Schedule to the Register of Sasines (Application Procedure) Rules 2004

For the Schedule to the Register of Sasines (Application Procedure) Rules 2004[^f00002] substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to these Rules.

D McNULTY

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh
