# The Wildlife and Natural Environment (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 281 (C. 29)

Wildlife

The Wildlife and Natural Environment (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2012

Made

22nd October 2012

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

24th October 2012

Coming into force

22nd November 2012

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 43(1) and (2)(a) of the Wildlife and Natural Environment (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Wildlife and Natural Environment (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Amendment (No. 2) Order 2012 and comes into force on 22nd November 2012.

## 2. Amendment of Commencement No. 2 Order

(1) Article 2 (appointed days) of the Wildlife and Natural Environment (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2011[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) After paragraph (3) insert—

> (3A) 22nd November 2012 is the day appointed for the coming into force of—
>
> - (a) section 13(3) of the 2011 Act so far as it inserts section 11A(4) and (9) into the 1981 Act; and
> - (b) section 13(5) of the 2011 Act.

.

(3) In paragraph (4), for “1st January 2013” substitute “1st April 2013”.

PAUL WHEELHOUSE

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

22nd October 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Wildlife and Natural Environment (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2011 (“the Order”) with regard to the commencement of section 13 (snares) of the Wildlife and Natural Environment (Scotland) Act 2011 (“the 2011 Act”).

It inserts a new article 2(3A) into the Order with the effect that section 13(3) (insofar as it inserts section 11A(4) and (9) into the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981) and section 13(5) of the 2011 Act come into force on 22nd November 2012.

It also amends article 2(4) of the Order so that the provisions of section 13 of the 2011 Act not already in force will come into force on 1st April 2013.
