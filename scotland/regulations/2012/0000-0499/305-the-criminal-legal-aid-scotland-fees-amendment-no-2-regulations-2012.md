# The Criminal Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This S.S.I. has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.S.I.  2012/276](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2012/276) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that instrument.

2012 No. 305

Legal Aid And Advice

The Criminal Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Amendment (No. 2) Regulations 2012

Made

13th November 2012

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

15th November 2012

Coming into force

18th December 2012

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 33(2)(a) and (3)(a) and 36(1) of the Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Criminal Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Amendment (No.  2) Regulations 2012 and come into force on 18th December 2012.

(2) These Regulations apply only to fees for work done and outlays incurred on or after 18th December 2012.

## 2. Amendment to the Criminal Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Regulations 1989

In regulation 3A(g) of the Criminal Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Regulations 1989[^f00002], for “1998” substitute “1988”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Criminal Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Regulations 1989 to correct an erroneous reference to an Act in regulation 3A. Regulation 3A was inserted by the Criminal Legal Aid (Scotland) (Fees) Amendment Regulations 2012.
