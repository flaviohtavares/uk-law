# The Population (Statistics) Act 1938 Modifications (Scotland) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 287

Family Law

The Population (Statistics) Act 1938 Modifications (Scotland) Order 2012

Made

24th October 2012

Coming into force

1st January 2013

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 44(1) and (2) of the Family Law (Scotland) Act 2006[^f00001], section 259(1), (2) and (3) of the Civil Partnership Act 2004[^f00002] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 44(5) of the Family Law (Scotland) Act 2006[^f00003] and section 259(8)(b)[^f00004] of the Civil Partnership Act 2004, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Population (Statistics) Act 1938 Modifications (Scotland) Order 2012 and comes into force on 1st January 2013.

## 2. Modification of the Population (Statistics) Act 1938

In the Schedule (particulars which may be required) to the Population (Statistics) Act 1938[^f00005], for paragraph 1(c) substitute—

> - (c) the number of previous children born to the mother and how many of them were—
>   - (i) born alive; and
>   - (ii) still-born;
> - (d) where the parents are married to, or in a civil partnership with, each other—
>   - (i) the date of their marriage or formation of civil partnership; and
>   - (ii) whether the mother had been married or in a civil partnership before that  date.

.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order modifies the Population (Statistics) Act [1938 (c.12)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1938/12) in consequence of the abolition of the status of illegitimacy. It also makes modifications in consequence of the Civil Partnership Act [2004 (c.33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/33).
