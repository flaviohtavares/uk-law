# The Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 252 (C. 23)

Children And Young Persons

The Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2012

Made

12th September 2012

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

14th September 2012

Coming into force

19th September 2012

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 206(2) of the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2012 and comes into force on 19th September 2012.

## 2. Appointed day

19th September 2012 is the appointed day for the coming into force of paragraph 10(2)(a) of schedule 1 to the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011.

AILEEN CAMPBELL

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

12th September 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 19th September 2012 paragraph 10(2)(a) of schedule 1 to the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 providing that the function of the National Convener under paragraph 24 of schedule 1 to that Act (National Convener’s annual report) may not be delegated.
