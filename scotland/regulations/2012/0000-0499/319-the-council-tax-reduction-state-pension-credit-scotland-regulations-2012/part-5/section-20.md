## 20. Applicable amount

*Extent: S*

An applicant's applicable amount is the aggregate of each of the following amounts which apply in the applicant's case—

- (a) an amount in respect of the applicant's personal allowance, determined in accordance with paragraph 2 of Schedule 1;
- (b) an amount in respect of any child or young person who is a member of the applicant's family, determined in accordance with paragraph 3 of Schedule 1 (personal allowances);
- (c)[^key-9833007d73bb11a52472872c597bcba8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^key-41f7071f9f0c9f9f8283e9b0263f3c6b]...
- (d) the amount of any premium which may be applicable to the applicant, determined in accordance with Parts 3 and 4 of Schedule 1 (disability premiums) , and[^key-a647a8f7ef6cc72f165b8297c4b31194]
- (e) the amount of £19.48[^key-863f77245008150f697b8b4aeada7de2] if the applicant is entitled to an amount (“a transitional family premium”) by virtue of regulation 2 (transitional provision) of the Council Tax Reduction (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2016.
