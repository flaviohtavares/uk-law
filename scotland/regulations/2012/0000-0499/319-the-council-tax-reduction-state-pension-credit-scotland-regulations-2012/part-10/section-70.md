## 70. Alternative means of notifying changes of circumstances

*Extent: S*

Where a change of circumstances described in regulation 69 is a birth or death the relevant authority may determine for a particular class of case that the duty in that regulation to notify a change in circumstances may be discharged by personal attendance at an office specified by that authority.
