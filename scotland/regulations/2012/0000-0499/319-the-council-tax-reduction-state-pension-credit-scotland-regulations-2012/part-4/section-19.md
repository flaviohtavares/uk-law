## 19. Persons not entitled to council tax reduction: persons subject to immigration control

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-a3c5281ec92598b3c22f49f3c6bbcac5] A person is not entitled to council tax reduction in respect of a day and a dwelling of which the person is a resident if the person is throughout that day a person subject to immigration control [^c-d3115ef447fbe94853be3cbd4719bb64]....

(2)[^c-890f748e1fcff949bfe95a2598f5c15b] In paragraph (1) “a person subject to immigration control” has the meaning given in section 115(9) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, but does not include a person who—

- (a) is a national of a state which has ratified the European Convention on Social and Medical Assistance (done in Paris on 11th December 1953) or a state which has ratified the Council of Europe Social Charter (signed in Turin on 18th October 1961); and
- (b) is lawfully present in the United Kingdom.
