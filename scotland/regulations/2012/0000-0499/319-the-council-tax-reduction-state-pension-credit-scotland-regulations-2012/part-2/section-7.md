# The Council Tax Reduction (State Pension Credit) (Scotland) Regulations 2012, section 7

## 7. Entitlement of only one member of a family

*Extent: S*

The entitlement of one member of a family to council tax reduction in respect of a dwelling excludes entitlement to that reduction in respect of that dwelling for any other member of the family for the same period.
