## 30. Calculation of average weekly income from ... tax credits[^M_F_28ec9877-1128-487a-d81e-1c9cdba5fb14]

*Extent: S*

(1) This regulation applies where an applicant receives a working tax credit or child tax credit[^M_F_b5e990cc-e7f2-4b84-8003-fcdf123c1d37].

(2) [^M_F_52518384-97be-44b4-961b-dd3351e36652]... The period over which that[^M_F_ff147dab-7b2f-4b98-cbdb-4945ea8c9767] tax credit is to be taken into account is the period set out in paragraph (3).

(3) Where the instalment in respect of which payment of that tax credit is made is—

- (a) a daily instalment, the period is one day, being the day in respect of which the instalment is paid;
- (b) a weekly instalment, the period is 7 days, ending on the day on which the instalment is due to be paid;
- (c) a two weekly instalment, the period is 14 days, commencing 6 days before the day on which the instalment is due to be paid; and
- (d) a 4 weekly instalment, the period is 28 days, ending on the day on which the instalment is due to be paid.
