# The Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Amendment Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 42 (C. 9)

Registers And Records

The Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Amendment Order 2012

Made

13th February 2012

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

15th February 2012

Coming into force

23rd February 2012

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 16(1) of the Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Amendment Order 2012 and comes into force on 23rd February 2012.

## 2. Amendment of Commencement No. 1 Order

(1) The Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2012[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2, after “(interpretation) of” insert “, and the schedule (authorities to which Part 1 applies) to,”.

FIONA HYSLOP

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

13th February 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Public Records (Scotland) Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2012 so that the schedule to the Act also comes into force on 24th February 2012.
