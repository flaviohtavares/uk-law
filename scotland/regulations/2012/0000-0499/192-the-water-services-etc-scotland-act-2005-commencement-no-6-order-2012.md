# The Water Services etc. (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 192 (C. 18)

Water Industry

The Water Services etc. (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2012

Made

11th June 2012

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

13th June 2012

Coming into force

22nd July 2012

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 37(2) of the Water Services etc. (Scotland) Act 2005[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Water Services etc. (Scotland) Act 2005 (Commencement No. 6) Order 2012 and comes into force on 22nd July 2012.

## 2. Appointed Day

22nd July 2012 is the day appointed for the coming into force of paragraph 7(5) of schedule 5 to the Water Services etc. (Scotland) Act 2005.

ALEX NEIL

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

11th June 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order appoints 22nd July 2012 for the coming into force of paragraph 7(5) of schedule 5 to the Water Services etc. (Scotland) Act 2005 (“the 2005 Act”).

That provision repeals section 40 of the [Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002 (asp 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2002/3) (“the 2002 Act”), which empowered the Scottish Ministers to make regulations reducing the charges payable by certain persons under section 37 of that Act. Provision for reduced charges is now made through the Ministers’ statement of policy regarding charges that is issued to Scottish Water and the Water Industry Commission for Scotland under section 29D of the 2002 Act.
