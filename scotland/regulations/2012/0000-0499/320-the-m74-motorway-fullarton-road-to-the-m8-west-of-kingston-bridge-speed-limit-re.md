# The M74 Motorway (Fullarton Road to the M8 West of Kingston Bridge) (Speed Limit) Regulations 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 320

Road Traffic

The M74 Motorway (Fullarton Road to the M8 West of Kingston Bridge) (Speed Limit) Regulations 2012

Made

22nd November 2012

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

23rd November 2012

Coming into force

28th December 2012

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 17(2), (3) and (3ZA) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 17(3A)[^f00002] of that Act they have consulted with such representative organisations as they think fit.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the M74 Motorway (Fullarton Road to the M8 West of Kingston Bridge) (Speed Limit) Regulations 2012 and come into force on 28th December 2012.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations—

- “the M8” means the M8/A8 Edinburgh - Greenock Trunk Road;
- “the M74” means the M74/A74(M)/A74 Glasgow - Carlisle Trunk Road;
- “point 1” means a point 32 metres or thereby east of the point of intersection of the centre lines of the M74 and Carmyle Avenue;
- “point 2” means a point 710 metres or thereby southeast of the point of intersection of the centre lines of the M74 and Polmadie Road;
- “point 3” means a point 60 metres or thereby northeast of the point of intersection of the centre lines of Scotland Street and West Street,

and references to “the Schedule” are to the Schedule to these Regulations.

## 3. Speed limits

No person may drive a motor vehicle at a speed exceeding—

- (a) 60 miles per hour on those lengths of road described in Part 1 of the Schedule;
- (b) 50 miles per hour on those lengths of road described in Part 2 of the Schedule;
- (c) 30 miles per hour on those lengths of road described in Part 3 of the Schedule.

## 4. Exemptions

No speed limit imposed by these Regulations applies to vehicles falling within regulation 3(4) of the Road Traffic Exemptions (Special Forces) (Variation and Amendment) Regulations 2011[^f00003] when used in accordance with regulation 3(5) of those Regulations.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations apply permanent speed limits to the M74 motorway between Fullarton Road, Glasgow and its connection to the M8 motorway west of the Kingston Bridge. Speed limits are applied to the main carriageway and slip roads.

Regulation 3 and Part 1 of the Schedule set the maximum speed limit for the driving of motor vehicles at 60 miles per hour on certain lengths of road.

Regulation 3 and Part 2 of the Schedule set the maximum speed limit for the driving of motor vehicles at 50 miles per hour on certain lengths of road.

Regulation 3 and Part 3 of the Schedule set the maximum speed limit for the driving of motor vehicles at 30 miles per hour on certain lengths of road.

Regulation 4 provides that the speed limits do not apply to vehicles used for naval, military or air force purposes while being driven in response, or for practice in responding, to a national security emergency by a member of the special forces who has been trained in driving at high speeds or for the purpose of such training.

The Regulations come into force on 28th December 2012. Prior to that date, temporary speed limits applied to the roads covered by these Regulations, by virtue of the M74 Motorway (Fullarton Road to the M8 Motorway West of Kingston Bridge) (Temporary Speed Restriction) Order 2011 ([S.S.I. 2011/275](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2011/275)).
