# The Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Assistance to Registered Social Landlords and Other Persons) (Grants) Amendment Revocation Regulations 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 306

Housing

The Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Assistance to Registered Social Landlords and Other Persons) (Grants) Amendment Revocation Regulations 2012

Made

13th November 2012

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

15th November 2012

Coming into force

18th November 2012

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 93(2) and 109(1) and (2) of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 93(4) of that Act, the Scottish Ministers have consulted with such bodies representing local authorities and registered social landlords and such other persons as they think fit.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Assistance to Registered Social Landlords and Other Persons) (Grants) Amendment Revocation Regulations 2012 and come into force on 18th November 2012.

## 2. Revocation

The Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Assistance to Registered Social Landlords and Other Persons) (Grants) Amendment Regulations 2012[^f00002] are revoked.

M J BURGESS

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

13th November 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke the Housing (Scotland) Act 2001 (Assistance to Registered Social Landlords and Other Persons) (Grants) Amendment Regulations 2012, which are due to come into force on 19th November 2012.
