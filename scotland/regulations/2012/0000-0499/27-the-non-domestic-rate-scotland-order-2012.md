# The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 27

Rating And Valuation

The Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2012

Made

7th February 2012

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

9th February 2012

Coming into force

1st April 2012

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 7B(1) and 37(1) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Non-Domestic Rate (Scotland) Order 2012 and comes into force on 1st April 2012.

## 2. Non-domestic rate for 2012-2013

For the purposes of section 7B of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975, the Scottish Ministers prescribe a rate of 45 pence per pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of financial year 2012-2013.

JOHN SWINNEY

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

7th February 2012

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order prescribes a rate of 45 pence in the pound as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland in respect of the financial year 2012-2013. A rate of 42.6 pence in the pound was the figure prescribed by the Scottish Ministers as the non-domestic rate to be levied throughout Scotland for the financial year 2011-2012 ([S.S.I. 2010/457](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2010/457)).
