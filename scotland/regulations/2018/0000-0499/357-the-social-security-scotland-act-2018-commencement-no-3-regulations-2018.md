# The Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 357 (C. 22)

Social Security

The Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2018

Made

20th November 2018

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

22nd November 2018

Coming into force

10th December 2018

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 99(2) of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2018 and come into force on 10 December 2018.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 32 and schedule 6 (early years assistance) of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 is 10 December 2018.

SHIRLEY-ANNE SOMERVILLE

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

20th November 2018

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring section 32 and schedule 6 of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (“the 2018 Act”) into force on the day they appoint.

The Bill for the 2018 Act received Royal Assent on 1 June 2018. Part 7 of the 2018 Act (sections 95 to 100: final provisions) came into force the following day.
