# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Court of Session, Sheriff Appeal Court and Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Made

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

8th June 2018

Coming into force

24th September 2018

In accordance with section 4 of the Scottish Civil Justice Council and Criminal Legal Assistance Act 2013[^f00001], the Court of Session has approved draft rules submitted to it by the Scottish Civil Justice Council.

The Court of Session therefore makes this Act of Sederunt under the powers conferred by sections 103(1), 105(1) and 106(1) of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00002] and all other powers enabling it to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement, etc.

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Court of Session, Sheriff Appeal Court and Sheriff Court) (Amendment) 2018.

(2) It comes into force on 24th September 2018.

(3) A certified copy is to be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment and Further Provisions) 1993

(1) Schedule 1 of the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment and Further Provisions) 1993[^f00003] is amended in accordance with this paragraph.

(2) In paragraph 15 of the General Regulations[^f00004], for “£35” substitute “£37”.

(3) In the provisions of the Table of Fees which are mentioned in column 1 of schedule 1 of this Act of Sederunt, for the amounts specified in column 2, substitute the amounts specified in column 3.

## 3. Amendment of the Rules of the Court of Session 1994

(1) Rule 42.16 (table of fees) of the Rules of the Court of Session 1994[^f00005] is amended in accordance with this paragraph.

(2) In paragraph (1A)[^f00006], for “£35” substitute “£37”.

(3) In the provisions of the Table of Fees in paragraph (3) which are mentioned in column 1 of schedule 2 of this Act of Sederunt, for the amounts specified in column 2, substitute the amounts specified in column 3.

## 4. Amendment of the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Appeal Court) 2015

(1) The Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Appeal Court) 2015[^f00007] is amended in accordance with this paragraph.

(2) In the provisions of schedule 1 (detailed fees) mentioned in column 1 of Part 1 of schedule 3 of this Act of Sederunt, for the amounts specified in column 2, substitute the amounts specified in column 3.

(3) In the provisions of schedule 2 (inclusive fees) mentioned in column 1 of Part 2 of schedule 3 of this Act of Sederunt, for the amounts specified in column 2, substitute the amounts specified in column 3.

## 5. Saving

Paragraphs 2, 3 and 4 have no effect as regards fees payable in respect of work carried out before 24th September 2018.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends the fees that are prescribed in the tables of solicitors’ fees in Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Court) (Amendment and Further Provisions) 1993, in the Rules of the Court of Session 1994, and in Act of Sederunt (Fees of Solicitors in the Sheriff Appeal Court) 2015. The tables in question regulate the fees that can be recovered under an award of expenses in respect of work carried out by a party’s solicitor in civil proceedings in the Court of Session, Sheriff Appeal Court and sheriff court. The amended fees have effect in respect of work carried out on or after 24th September 2018.
