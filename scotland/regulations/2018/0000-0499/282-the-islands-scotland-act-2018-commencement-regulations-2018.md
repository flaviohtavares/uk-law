# The Islands (Scotland) Act 2018 (Commencement) Regulations 2018

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2018 No. 282 (C. 19)

Local Government

The Islands (Scotland) Act 2018 (Commencement) Regulations 2018

Made

18th September 2018

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

20th September 2018

Coming into force

4th October 2018

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 31 of the Islands (Scotland) Act 2018[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Islands (Scotland) Act 2018 (Commencement) Regulations 2018 and come into force on 4 October 2018.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of sections 3 to 6, 15 to 21 and 27 to 29 of the Islands (Scotland) Act 2018 is 4 October 2018.

PAUL WHEELHOUSE

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Bill for the Islands (Scotland) Act 2018 (“the Act”) received Royal Assent on 6 July 2018. Sections 1, 2, 30, 31 and 32 came into force on the following day. These Regulations bring sections 3 to 6, 15 to 21 and 27 to 29 into force on 4 October 2018. The Act makes provision for a national islands plan, duties in relation to island communities imposed on certain public authorities, the electoral representation of island communities and to establish a licensing scheme in respect of marine developments adjacent to islands.
