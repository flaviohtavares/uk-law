# The National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 16

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 16. NHS contracts

If the provider is to be treated as a health service body the agreement must state that it is an NHS contract.
