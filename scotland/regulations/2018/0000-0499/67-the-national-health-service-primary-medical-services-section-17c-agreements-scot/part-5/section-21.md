# The National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 21

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 21. Additional obligations in relation to practice premises

(1) A provider who receives financial assistance must comply with the obligations set out in schedule 4 throughout the period that the provider receives that assistance and a term to this effect must be included in the agreement.

(2) In this regulation, “financial assistance” means financial assistance from a Health Board or the Scottish Ministers in the form of—

- (a) a recurring payment to the provider of—
  - (i) the provider’s owner-occupier borrowing costs;
  - (ii) notional rent payments as an owner-occupier; or
  - (iii) reimbursement of the provider’s rent payments;

  in accordance with directions under sections 2(5) and 17E(3A) of the Act[^f00066]; or
- (b) a loan secured over the practice premises**.**

## Annotations

[^f00066]: Footnote: Section 2(5) was amended by paragraph 19 of schedule 9 of the National Health Service and Community Care Act [1990 (c.19)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/19). Section 17E(3A) was inserted by section 2(4) of the Primary Medical Services (Scotland) Act 2004.
