# The National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 2

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 2. Application

These Regulations apply to an agreement—

- (a) to which the National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2004[^key-a4e113549f73358d19fcb1064f9184b1] applied immediately before 1st April 2018; or
- (b) which is entered into between a provider and the Health Board on or after 1st April 2018.
