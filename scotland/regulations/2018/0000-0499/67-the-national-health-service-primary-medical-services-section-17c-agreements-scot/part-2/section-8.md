# The National Health Service (Primary Medical Services Section 17C Agreements) (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 8

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 8. Prescribed period under section 17D(3) of the Act

The period prescribed for the purposes of section 17D(3) of the Act (persons with whom agreements may be made)[^f00060] is 6 months.

## Annotations

[^f00060]: Footnote: Section 17D was inserted by section 21(2) of the National Health Service (Primary Care) Act [1997 (c.46)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1997/46) and amended by section 2(3) of the Primary Medical Services (Scotland) Act [2004 (asp 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2004/1).
