# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 15

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 15. NHS contracts

If the contractor is to be regarded as a health service body pursuant to regulation 13, the contract must state that it is an NHS contract.
