# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 18A

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 18A. Health Board support for contractors[^key-e17675aa2b87b5e8b26b640ef86e038a]

(1)[^key-58e63ec43f61ba33159065b99eb7674e] A contract must include terms which have the same effect as schedules 2B (community treatment and care services) and 2C (pharmacotherapy services).

(2)[^key-b95ffdda4d3756a52a297a755de8df6f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Any support which the contractor receives from a pharmacist independent prescriber supplied by the Health Board in connection with dispensing services which the contractor provides (under paragraph 44 of schedule 6) is not to be treated as support for the provision of pharmacotherapy services.
