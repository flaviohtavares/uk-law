# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 29

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 29. Arrangements on termination

A contract must make suitable provision for arrangements on termination of the contract, including the consequences (whether financial or otherwise) of the contract ending.
