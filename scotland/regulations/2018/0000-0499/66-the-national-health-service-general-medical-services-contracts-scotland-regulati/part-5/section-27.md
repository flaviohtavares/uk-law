# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 27

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 27. Finance

The contract must contain a term to the effect that where, pursuant to any directions of the Scottish Ministers under sections 2(5) and 17M(3) of the Act, a Health Board is required to make a payment to a contractor under a contract but subject to conditions, those conditions are to be a term of the contract.
