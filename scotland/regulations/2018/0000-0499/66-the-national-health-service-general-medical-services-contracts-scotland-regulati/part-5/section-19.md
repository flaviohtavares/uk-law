# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 19

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 19. Additional services

A contract which includes the provision of any additional services must—

- (a) in relation to all such services, contain a term which has the same effect as that specified in paragraph 1 of schedule 1; and
- (b) in relation to each such service, contain terms which have the same effect as those specified in schedule 1, which are relevant to that service.
