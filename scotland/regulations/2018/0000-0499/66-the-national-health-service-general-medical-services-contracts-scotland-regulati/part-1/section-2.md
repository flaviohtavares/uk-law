# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 2

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 2. Application

These Regulations apply to a contract—

- (a) to which the National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) (Scotland) Regulations 2004[^f00002] applied immediately before 1st April 2018; or
- (b) which is entered into between a contractor and the Health Board on or after 1st April 2018.
