# The Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Commencement No. 4 and Saving Provision) Regulations 2018

Made

20th December 2018

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

21st December 2018

Coming into force

21st January 2019

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 99(2) and (3) of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 [^c23490751].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Commencement No. 4 and Saving Provision) Regulations 2018 and come into force on 21 January 2019.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of sections 21 and 22 and schedule 1 (Scottish Commission on Social Security) of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 is 21 January 2019.

## 3. Saving provision[^key-82b33d9afc61f7c82be4d51581e8cb2a]

Nothing in regulation 2 brings a provision into force for any purpose within exception 1(a) insofar as it relates to industrial injuries benefits or 1(c) in section F1 (benefits for disability and industrial injury) in Part 2 of schedule 5 of the Scotland Act 1998 [^c23490761].

*(This note is not part of the Regulations)*

These Regulations bring sections 21 and 22 and schedule 1 of the Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (“**the 2018 Act**”) into force on the day they appoint. These provisions relate to the Scottish Commission on Social Security.

Regulation 3 makes saving provision. This is to ensure that the commencement of these provisions of the 2018 Act has no effect on the arrangements made by regulation 4(2)(a) of the Scotland Act 2016 (Transitional) Regulations 2017 (S.I. 2017/444). Those arrangements delay, for a transitional period, the transfer to the Scottish Ministers of the Secretary of State's functions in relation to certain disability and industrial injury benefits.

The Bill for the 2018 Act received Royal Assent on 1 June 2018. Part 7 of the 2018 Act (sections 95 to 100: final provisions) came into force the following day.
