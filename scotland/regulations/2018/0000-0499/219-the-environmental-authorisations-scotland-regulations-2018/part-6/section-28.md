# The Environmental Authorisations (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 28

> Text as at 2026-03-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28. Effect of transfer

*Extent: S*

(1) With effect from the date on which SEPA grants an application for transfer of a registration or permit, the person who was the authorised person immediately before the transfer ceases to be an authorised person (in respect of the part of the registration or permit being transferred[^key-b92625570db929389ce05859fa90f160]).

(2) Where—

- (a) a regulatory notice or a surrender notice is in force in respect of a permit or registration; and
- (b) the permit or registration is transferred to the proposed transferee, either in whole or in part,

the duty to comply with the notice is also transferred to the proposed transferee to the extent that it relates to the permit or registration (or part of the permit or registration) transferred.
