# The Environmental Authorisations (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 17

> Text as at 2026-03-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 17. Form and content of a registration

*Extent: S*

(1) A registration granted by SEPA must be in writing and must specify—

- (a) the activity authorised;
- (b) the authorised person;
- (c) any[^key-38349133cc98cb75f3e764bbceda47a9] standard conditions to which the registration is subject;
- (d) the date on which the registration takes effect; and
- (e) the authorised place.

(2) A registration has effect subject to any[^key-22efb1c1b72be998e7ca31dc9a931e21] standard conditions specified in accordance with paragraph (1)(c) and (5) and regulation 19(4).

(3) A reference in a registration to standard conditions is to the conditions as revised from time to time.

(4) SEPA may specify in a registration that the registration ceases to have effect on a specified date.

(5) SEPA may, after a registration is granted, from time to time specify in writing that a registration is to be subject to [^key-24cd9253131d2f53ebf6ffd9f649b8a9]...—

- (a) different standard conditions;
- (b) additional standard conditions ,[^key-84af97bf7d3ced81d8b32c6910315485]
- (c) any standard conditions, in circumstances where no standard conditions were specified at the time the registration was granted.
