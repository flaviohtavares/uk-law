> Text as at 2026-03-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 33. Determining and revising standard conditions

*Extent: S*

(1) SEPA may determine standard conditions in accordance with this Part.

(2) SEPA may revise standard conditions at any time.

(3) In this Part, a reference to revising standard conditions means—

- (a) replacing conditions;
- (b) amending conditions;
- (c) removing conditions; and
- (d) adding new conditions.

(4)[^key-756cd471e6bb5667a4b77293f6af0e42] SEPA may include such conditions as it thinks fit in any standard conditions.
