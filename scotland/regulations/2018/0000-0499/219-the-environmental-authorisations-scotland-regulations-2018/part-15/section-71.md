> Text as at 2026-03-26.

## 71. Offences — acts or default of third person

*Extent: S*

Where the commission by any person of an offence under these Regulations is due to the act or default of some other person, that other person may be charged with and convicted of the offence by virtue of this regulation whether or not proceedings for the offence are taken against the first-mentioned person.
