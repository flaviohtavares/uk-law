# The Environmental Authorisations (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 9

> Text as at 2026-03-26.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 9. General aims

*Extent: S*

(1) SEPA must take the general aims into account when carrying out a relevant function.

(2) The general aims are that all appropriate measures are taken—

- (a) to prevent or, where that is not practicable, to minimise environmental harm;
- (b) to prevent and to limit the consequences of accidents which could have an impact on the environment; and
- (c) to use resources in a sustainable way,

in the carrying on, and decommissioning, of regulated activities and following cessation of the carrying on of the regulated activity.
