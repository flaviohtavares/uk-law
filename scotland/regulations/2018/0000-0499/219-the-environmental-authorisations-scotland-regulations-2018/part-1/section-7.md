> Text as at 2026-03-26.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 7. Prohibition

*Extent: S*

A person must not carry on a regulated activity except in so far as it is—

- (a) authorised under these Regulations; and
- (b) carried on in accordance with, and to the extent authorised by, that authorisation.
