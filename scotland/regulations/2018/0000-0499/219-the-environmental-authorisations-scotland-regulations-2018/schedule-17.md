## SCHEDULE 17 — MANAGEMENT OF WASTE BATTERIES[^key-44065645c833dade3e90c5aa623ddc22]

*Extent: S*

Regulation 6(1)

### PART 1 — Scope and interpretation

#### 1. Scope

This schedule applies to the activity of treating and the storage on a treatment facility, of waste batteries.

#### 2. Interpretation

In this schedule—

- “**battery**” means any source of electrical energy generated by direct conversion of chemical energy and consisting of one or more primary battery cells (non-rechargeable) or one or more secondary battery cells (rechargeable, an accumulator), but does not include—
  - (a) equipment connected with the protection of essential security interests, arms, munitions and war material, with the exclusion of products that are not intended for specifically military purposes, or
  - (b) equipment designed to be sent into space,
- “**treatment**” means any activity carried out on waste batteries and accumulators after they have been handed over to a treatment facility for sorting, preparation for recycling or preparation for disposal.

### PART 2 — Amendments to common framework provisions

#### 3. Authorisations: conditions

SEPA must ensure that an authorisation for the treatment and storage at a treatment facility of waste batteries must include such conditions as it considers appropriate to ensure that—

- (a) treatment, at a minimum, includes the removal of all fluids and acids,
- (b) treatment, and any storage, including temporary storage, takes place on impermeable surfaces with suitable weatherproof covering or in suitable containers.
