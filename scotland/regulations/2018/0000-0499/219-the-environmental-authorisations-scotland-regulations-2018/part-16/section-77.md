> Text as at 2026-03-26.

## 77. Application of the Regulations to SEPA

*Extent: S*

If SEPA is in control, or intends to be in control, of the carrying on of a regulated activity, SEPA must comply with guidance issued by the Scottish Ministers in that regard.
