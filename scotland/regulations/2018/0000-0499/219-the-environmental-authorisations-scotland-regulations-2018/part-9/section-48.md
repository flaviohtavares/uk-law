> Text as at 2026-03-26.

## 48. Withdrawing regulatory notices

*Extent: S*

(1) SEPA must withdraw a regulatory notice if it is satisfied that the steps required by the notice have been taken.

(2) A notice withdrawing a regulatory notice must specify—

- (a) the date on which the withdrawal takes effect; and
- (b) the reasons for the withdrawal of the notice.
