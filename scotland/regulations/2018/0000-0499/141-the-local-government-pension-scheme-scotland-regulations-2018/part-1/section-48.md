# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 48

> Text as at 2025-04-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 48. Limit on total amount of benefits

*Extent: S*

(1) No person is entitled under any provision of these Regulations to receive benefits the capital value of which exceed that person's lifetime allowance, except in accordance with actuarial guidance issued by the Scottish Ministers, and any benefits to which a person is entitled are restricted accordingly.

(2) In this regulation “**lifetime allowance**” is to be construed in accordance with section 218 of and schedule 36 of the Finance Act 2004 [^c22970851] and Schedule 6 to the Finance Act 2014 and, where applicable, is to include primary protection, enhanced protection, individual protection or fixed protection within the meaning of those provisions [^c22970861].

(3) The capital value of a person's benefits shall be calculated in accordance with actuarial guidance issued by the Scottish Ministers.
