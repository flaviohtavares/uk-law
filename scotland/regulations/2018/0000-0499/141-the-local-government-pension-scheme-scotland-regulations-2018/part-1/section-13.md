> Text as at 2025-04-02.

## 13. Contributions during reserve forces service leave

*Extent: S*

(1) An active member on reserve forces service leave must pay contributions in accordance with regulations 9 or 10.

(2) The contributions under paragraph (1) are payable on the member's assumed pensionable pay calculated under regulation 21 (assumed pensionable pay).
