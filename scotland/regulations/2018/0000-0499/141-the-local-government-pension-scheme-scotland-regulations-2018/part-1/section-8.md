# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 8

> Text as at 2025-04-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 8. Pension credit and survivor members

*Extent: S*

(1) A person is a pension credit member of the Scheme if that person has been given a pension credit in the Scheme as a consequence of a pension debit created under section 29 of the Welfare Reform and Pensions[^key-ae66a65ab7f1de571aa0dde40db0fb6a] Act 1999 [^c22970631] in relation to a member of the Scheme.

(2) A person is a survivor member of the Scheme if that person is entitled to a benefit under regulations 39, 40, 42, 43, 45 or 46 (survivor pensions).
