# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 2

> Text as at 2025-04-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 2. Introductory

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-8c98c8d03588122c0e6c72ed0abba244] These Regulations consolidate with certain amendments the provisions of the Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2014, which established a scheme for the payment of pensions and other benefits to or in respect of persons employed by Scheme employers, and constitute “the Scheme”.

(2) The Scheme[^key-23c24acd1bb419256aac0848aebc4c15] manager responsible for the local administration of pensions and other benefits payable under these Regulations is referred to in these Regulations as the “**administering authority**”.

(3) The Scottish Ministers may, after consultation with the Scheme actuary, issue actuarial guidance to administering authorities.

(4) Schedule 1 (interpretation) contains definitions of expressions used in these Regulations which apply for their interpretation unless the context indicates that they have a different meaning.
