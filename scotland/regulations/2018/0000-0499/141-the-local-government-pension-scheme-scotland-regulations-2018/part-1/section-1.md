# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 1

> Text as at 2025-04-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and effect

*Extent: S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2018, and come into force on 1st June 2018.

(2) Regulations 18(7), 26(8), 29(11), 29(13),[^c-8703273c0603d29d09a8a15cb91c1430] 30(2), 40(13), 58(2), the definitions of “Club Memorandum”, “Club Transfer[^c-6b169a867d966f93cd7ffd61615c5abd]” and “revaluation adjustment” in schedule 1 and paragraph 6 of schedule 5 have effect from 1st April 2015.

(3) Regulation 54(2) has effect from 5th April 2016.

(4) Regulation 56(2) and (4) has effect from 31st March 2016.
