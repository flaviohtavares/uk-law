# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 50

> Text as at 2025-04-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 50. Pension debits

*Extent: S*

(1) Administering authorities shall have regard to actuarial guidance issued by the Scottish Ministers as to reduction of benefits payable under these Regulations in consequence of a pension debit.

(2) An administering authority shall make such adjustments to a member's pension accounts as are required to give effect to a pension debit.
