# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 54

> Text as at 2025-04-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 54. Accounts and audit

*Extent: S*

(1) After any of its pension funds has been audited, an administering authority must immediately send copies of the following to each body whose employees are active members—

- (a) a summary of the revenue account and balance sheet of the fund; and
- (b) any report by the auditor.

(2) The pension input period for the purposes of section 238 of the Finance Act 2004 [^c22970881] is the year ending on 5th April 2016 and each year ending on 5th April after that year.
