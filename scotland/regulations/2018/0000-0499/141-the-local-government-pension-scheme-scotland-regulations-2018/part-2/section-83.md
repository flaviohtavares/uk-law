# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 83

> Text as at 2025-04-02.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 83. Pension increase under the Pension Schemes Act 1993

*Extent: S*

Any increase of pension required by reason of Chapter 3 of Part 4 of the Pension Schemes Act 1993 (protection of increases in guaranteed minimum pensions: anti-franking) [^c22971001] must be paid from the appropriate fund held by the administering authority.
