# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 82

> Text as at 2025-04-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 82. Tax

*Extent: S*

The appropriate administering authority may deduct from any payment of benefits under the Scheme any tax to which they may become chargeable under the Finance Act 2004.
