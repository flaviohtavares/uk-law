# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 102

> Text as at 2025-04-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 102. Revocations and transitional provisions

*Extent: S*

(1) The Regulations specified in Schedule 6 are revoked.

(2) Anything done under or by virtue of any regulation revoked by these Regulations if it could have been done under or for the purpose of these Regulations, is deemed to have been done under or by virtue of the corresponding provision of these Regulations and anything begun under or by virtue of any such regulation may be continued under these Regulations as if begun under these Regulations.
