# The Local Government Pension Scheme (Scotland) Regulations 2018, section 99

> Text as at 2025-04-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 99. Separate employments etc.

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a person holds separate employments under one Scheme employer, these Regulations apply as if each of them were under a different employer.

(2) This regulation also applies where an employee of a Scheme employer is also employed to carry out one or more of the additional duties.

(3) The additional duties are duties as—

- (a) a returning officer at—
  - (i) local government elections; or
  - (ii) elections for the Scottish Parliament; or
  - (iii)[^key-964db8db58bfce61473f7a0287791822] UK Parliamentary elections;
- (b) an acting returning officer (including as a regional or local returning officer at a European Parliamentary election).
