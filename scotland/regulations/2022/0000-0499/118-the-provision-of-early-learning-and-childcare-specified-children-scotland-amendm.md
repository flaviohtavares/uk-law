# The Provision of Early Learning and Childcare (Specified Children) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 118

Education

The Provision of Early Learning and Childcare (Specified Children) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2022

Made

28th March 2022

Coming into force

1st April 2022

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 47(2)(c)(ii) of the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00001], and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 99(2) of that Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Provision of Early Learning and Childcare (Specified Children) (Scotland) Amendment Order 2022 and comes into force on 1 April 2022.

## 2. Amendment of the Provision of Early Learning and Childcare (Specified Children) (Scotland) Order 2014

In article 1(2) of the Provision of Early Learning and Childcare (Specified Children) (Scotland) Order 2014[^f00002] (citation, commencement, interpretation and application), in the definition of “qualifying benefit”—

- (a) for “£7,500” substitute “£7,920”,
- (b) on each occasion where it appears, for “£625” substitute “£660”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Provision of Early Learning and Childcare (Specified Children) (Scotland) Order 2014 ([S.S.I. 2014/196](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2014/196)) (“**the 2014 Order**”). The 2014 Order specifies those children who are eligible pre-school children for the purposes of section 47(2)(c)(ii) of the Children and Young People (Scotland) Act [2014 (asp 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2014/8) and who are therefore entitled to the mandatory amount of early learning and childcare (as defined in section 48 of that Act).

The 2014 Order specifies that certain children whose parents are in receipt of “qualifying benefits” are eligible pre-school children. This Order amends the definition of qualifying benefits in article 1(2) of the 2014 Order to increase the income thresholds for those in receipt of certain benefits. The income threshold for those in receipt of child tax credit and working tax credit under Part 1 of the Tax Credits Act [2002 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/21) is increased to £7,920. The income threshold for those in receipt of universal credit under the Welfare Reform Act [2012 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/5) is increased to £660.
