# The Seed (Equivalence of Countries) (Amendment) (Scotland) Regulations 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 328

Seeds

The Seed (Equivalence of Countries) (Amendment) (Scotland) Regulations 2022

Made

8th November 2022

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

10th November 2022

Coming into force

30th December 2022

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 16(1) and (1A) of the Plant Varieties and Seeds Act 1964[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 16(1) of that Act they have consulted with representatives of such interests as appear to them to be concerned.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Seed (Equivalence of Countries) (Amendment) (Scotland) Regulations 2022 and come into force on 30<sup></sup>December 2022.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

## 2. Amendment of Council Decision [2003/17/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0017)

In Article 6 of Council [Decision 2003/17/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0017) on the equivalence of field inspections carried out in third countries on seed-producing crops and on the equivalence of seed produced in third countries[^f00002], for “31 December 2022” substitute “31 December 2029”.

MAIRI MCALLAN

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Article 6 of Council [Decision 2003/17/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0017) of 16 December 2002 on the equivalence of field inspections carried out in third countries on seed-producing crops and on the equivalence of seed produced in third countries to extend the expiry date of this Decision.

A full impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no, or no significant, impact on the private, voluntary or public sector is foreseen.
