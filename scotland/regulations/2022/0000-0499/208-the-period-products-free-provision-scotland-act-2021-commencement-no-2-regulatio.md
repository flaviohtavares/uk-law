The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the power conferred on them by section 11(2) of the Period Products (Free Provision) (Scotland) Act 2021[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Period Products (Free Provision) (Scotland) Act 2021 (Commencement No. 2) Regulations 2022 and come into force on 15 August 2022.

## 2. Appointed day

15 August 2022 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Period Products (Free Provision) (Scotland) Act 2021—

- (a) section 1 (local authorities to ensure period products generally obtainable free of charge),
- (b) section 2 (education providers to ensure period products obtainable free of charge by pupils and students),
- (c) section 3 (specified public service bodies to ensure period products obtainable free of charge by persons in their premises),
- (d) section 4 (arrangements under sections 1, 2 and 3: particular requirements), and
- (e) section 8 (information to be provided to public).

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring into force sections 1 to 4 and section 8 of the Period Products (Free Provision) (Scotland) Act 2021 (“**the Act**”) on 15 August 2022.

The Period Products in Schools (Scotland) Regulations 2020 ([S.S.I. 2020/183](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2020/183)) will be revoked when section 2(8) of the Act is brought into force.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 12 January 2021. Sections 9 (key definitions), 10 (interpretation), 11 (commencement) and 12 (short title) came into force on the following day. Sections 5 (guidance), 6 (statement on exercise of functions), and 7 (consultation) were brought into force by the Period Products (Free Provision) (Scotland) Act 2021 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2021 ([S.S.I. 2021/104](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2021/104)) on 18 March 2021.
