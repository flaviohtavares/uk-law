# The Financial Assistance for Environmental Purposes (Scotland) (No. 2) Order 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 278

Environmental Protection

The Financial Assistance for Environmental Purposes (Scotland) (No. 2) Order 2022

Made

20th September 2022

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

22nd September 2022

Coming into force

17th November 2022

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 153(4) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Financial Assistance for Environmental Purposes (Scotland) (No. 2) Order 2022 and comes into force on 17 November 2022.

(2) This Order extends to Scotland only.

## 2. Variation of section 153(1) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990

After paragraph (z7) of section 153(1) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 (financial assistance for environmental purposes)[^f00002] insert—

> - (z8) any scheme, programme or organisation for the purposes of eliminating or reducing greenhouse gas emissions from the heating of buildings;
> - (z9) any scheme, programme or organisation for the purposes of improving the energy efficiency of buildings.

.

PATRICK HARVIE

20th September 2022

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order varies section 153(1) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 to add any scheme, programme or organisation for the purposes of eliminating or reducing greenhouse gas emissions from the heating of buildings, and any scheme, programme or organisation for the purposes of improving the energy efficiency of buildings. This will enable the Scottish Ministers to give financial assistance to, or for the purposes of, those schemes, programmes or organisations.
