# Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules Amendment) (Miscellaneous) 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 144

HIGH COURT OF JUSTICIARY

SHERIFF COURT

JUSTICE OF THE PEACE COURTS

Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules Amendment) (Miscellaneous) 2009

Made

3rd April 2009

Coming into force

30th April 2009

The Lord Justice General, the Lord Justice Clerk and the Lords Commissioners of Justiciary, under and by virtue of the powers conferred on them by section 305 of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Act of Adjournal may be cited as the Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules Amendment) (Miscellaneous) 2009 and comes into force on 30<sup>th</sup> April 2009.

(2) This Act of Adjournal is to be inserted in the Books of Adjournal.

(3) In this Act of Adjournal, “the Rules” means the Criminal Procedure Rules 1996[^f00002].

## 2. Transfer of solemn proceedings (sheriff court)

(1) After Chapter 8B of the Rules (failure of accused to appear)[^f00003] insert—

> **CHAPTER 8C — TRANSFER OF PROCEEDINGS (SHERIFF COURT)**
>
> **8C.1. Transfer of solemn proceedings outwith sheriffdom**
>
> A written application by the prosecutor under section 34A of the Act of 1995 (initiating proceedings outwith sheriffdom: exceptional circumstances)[^f00004] shall be in Form 8C.1.

.

(2) After Form 8B.1[^f00005] in the appendix to the Rules insert the form set out in Part 1 of the Schedule to this Act of Adjournal.

## 3. Transfer of summary proceedings

(1) After Chapter 17 of the Rules (summary pre-trial procedure) insert—

> **CHAPTER 17A — TRANSFER OF SUMMARY PROCEEDINGS**
>
> **17A.1. Transfer of summary proceedings**
>
> (1) A written application by the procurator fiscal under—
>
> - (a) section 137A(1) of the Act of 1995 (transfer within sheriffdom)[^f00006] shall be in Form 17A.1-A;
> - (b) section 137B(1) of the Act of 1995 (transfer outwith sheriffdom due to exceptional circumstances)[^f00007] shall be in Form 17A.1-B;
> - (c) section 137B(1A) of the Act of 1995 (transfer outwith sheriffdom where accused cited to attend diet in other summary proceedings or other summary proceedings commenced) shall be in Form 17A.1-C;
> - (d) section 137B(1C) of the Act of 1995 (transfer outwith sheriffdom: intention to take summary proceedings where other summary proceedings commenced) shall be in Form 17A.1-D;
> - (e) section 137B(3) of the Act of 1995 (transfer outwith sheriffdom due to exceptional circumstances: revocation or variation of order) shall be in Form 17A.1-E;
> - (f) section 137B(4) of the Act of 1995 (transfer outwith sheriffdom where other summary proceedings commenced or intended: revocation or variation of order) shall be in Form 17A.1-F;
> - (g) section 137C(1) of the Act of 1995 (initiating custody cases outwith sheriffdom: exceptional circumstances)[^f00008] shall be in Form 17A.1-G;
> - (h) section 137D(1) of the Act of 1995 (transfer of justice of the peace court proceedings where accused person to be sentenced in sheriff court)[^f00009] shall be in Form 17A.1-H.
>
> (2) Where the sheriff principal consents to an application under paragraph (1)(b), (e) or (g), he shall docquet his consent to the application.
>
> (3) Where a sheriff consents to an application under paragraph (1)(c), (d) or (f), he shall docquet his consent to the application.

.

(2) After Form 17.2[^f00010] in the appendix to the Rules insert the forms set out in Part 2 of the Schedule to this Act of Adjournal.

## 4. Identification procedures

(1) In the heading of Chapter 28 of the Rules (identification parades) for “parades” substitute “procedures”.

(2) For “parade” substitute “procedure” wherever it appears in each of the following places in:

- (a) rule 28.2 of the Rules (order requiring accused to participate in identification parade)[^f00011], in the heading and in paragraph (1); and
- (b) the heading of Form 28.2 in the appendix to the Rules (form of application for order requiring accused to participate in identification parade)[^f00012].

## 5. Service of bills and petitions

After Chapter 29 of the Rules (precognition on oath of defence witnesses) insert–

> **CHAPTER 29A — SERVICE OF BILLS OF ADVOCATION AND SUSPENSION AND PETITIONS TO THE NOBILE OFFICIUM**
>
> **29A.1. Service of bill or petition**
>
> (1) Where a first order for service is sought in relation to—
>
> - (a) a bill of advocation;
> - (b) a bill of suspension; or
> - (c) a petition to the nobile officium,
>
> the complainer or petitioner shall provide two copies of the bill or petition when presenting it to the Clerk of Justiciary for registration.
>
> (2) Where a first order for service is granted, the Clerk of Justiciary shall provide the complainer or petitioner with a certified copy of—
>
> - (a) the bill or petition; and
> - (b) the interlocutor granting first order for service.
>
> (3) The complainer or petitioner shall serve the certified copy of the bill or petition and interlocutor on the respondent.
>
> (4) Where a bill or petition arises from proceedings in a lower court, the Clerk of Justiciary shall intimate a copy to that court.

.

## 6. Animal dealing licences: disqualification

(1) In rule 51.1 of the Rules (interpretation: animal health and welfare)[^f00013] in the definition of “disqualification order” for “or section 40 of the 2006 Act” substitute—

> - , section 40 of the 2006 Act or regulation 21(2) of the Licensing of Animal Dealers (Young Cats and Young Dogs) (Scotland) Regulations 2009[^f00014]

.

(2) In Form 51.5 in the appendix to the Rules[^f00015] after the words “Animal Health and Welfare (Scotland) Act 2006\]” insert—

> - \[*or* regulation 21(1) of the Licensing of Animal Dealers (Young Cats and Young Dogs) (Scotland) Regulations 2009\]

.

(This note is not part of the Act of Adjournal)

This Act of Adjournal amends the Criminal Procedure Rules 1996.

Paragraph 2 inserts a new Chapter 8C providing a form for an application by the prosecutor for the transfer of solemn proceedings in the sheriff court under section 34A of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995.

Paragraph 3 inserts a new Chapter 17A, providing forms for applications by the prosecutor for the transfer of summary proceedings in the sheriff court under sections 137A, 137B, 137C and 137D of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995.

Paragraph 4 makes minor amendments to Chapter 28 and Form 28.2 in relation to identification procedures.

Paragraph 5 inserts a new Chapter 29A, providing rules in relation to the service of bills of advocation and suspension and petitions to the nobile officium.

Paragraph 6 amends Chapter 51 in consequence of the Licensing of Animal Dealers (Young Cats and Young Dogs) (Scotland) Regulations 2009.
