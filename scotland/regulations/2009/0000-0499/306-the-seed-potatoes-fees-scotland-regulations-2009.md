# The Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 306

Seeds

The Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2009

Made

2nd September 2009

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

3rd September 2009

Coming into force

25th September 2009

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 16(1), (1A)(e) and (5)(a) of the Plant Varieties and Seeds Act 1964[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 16(1) of that Act, they have consulted with representatives of such interests as appear to them to be concerned.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2009 and come into force on 25th September 2009.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations “Approved Stock” means seed potatoes that are to be subjected to crop inspection or official examination for the purposes of regulation 3(5) of the Seed Potatoes (Scotland) Regulations 2000[^f00002].

## 3. Payment of Fees

Fees must be paid to the Scottish Ministers in respect of the matters arising under the Seed Potatoes (Scotland) Regulations 2000 and specified in relation to an entry in column 1 of the Schedule to these Regulations, calculated on the basis corresponding to that entry specified in column 2 of that Schedule.

## 4. Withdrawal of application

(1) If an application for crop inspection referred to in paragraph 2 of the Schedule is withdrawn before the arrival of an inspector for the purpose of inspecting the crop for the first time the fee paid will be refunded to an applicant, subject to the charging of an administration fee of £1.50 per half‑hectare.

(2) If the labels and seals referred to in paragraph 4 of the Schedule are returned unused within 2 months of issue, the fee paid will be refunded to an applicant, subject to the charging of an administration fee of £1.00 per tonne.

## 5. Cancellation of an official examination

If an arrangement has been made with an applicant for the carrying out of an official examination (referred to in paragraph 5 of the Schedule) on a particular day, but, on that day, the applicant cancels the arrangement, the applicant will be subject to the charging of an administration fee of £25.00.

## 6. Exemptions

The Scottish Ministers may exempt any person, class of person, or persons generally, from compliance in whole or in part, with the provisions of these Regulations in relation to the payment of fees and any exemption so made may be made subject to conditions.

## 7. Revocation

The Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2005[^f00003] and the Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2007[^f00004] are hereby revoked.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations which extend to Scotland only prescribe the fees payable in respect of certain matters arising under the Seed Potatoes (Scotland) Regulations 2000 (regulation 3 and the Schedule). They consolidate, with amendments, provisions relating to fees previously contained in the Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Regulations 2005 (“the 2005 Regulations”) as amended by the Seed Potatoes (Fees) (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2007 (“the 2007 Regulations”). Amendments to the 2005 Regulations in this consolidation are largely of a minor or consequential drafting nature, or provide for an increase in the rate of fees payable.

These Regulations prescribe the fees payable—

- (a) for the carrying out of crop inspections, tuber inspections and the provision of official labels and seals as regards the classes of seed potatoes known as the Pre‑basic TC, Pre‑basic 1 to 4, Super Elite, Elite and test and trial seed potatoes and as regards Approved Stock (regulation 3 and the Schedule);
- (b) for the initiation, provision and maintenance of nuclear stock (regulation 3 and the Schedule);
- (c) for administration where an application for inspection as Pre-basic class generations 1 to 4, Super Elite or Elite is withdrawn before the arrival of an inspector due to inspect the crop for the first time (regulation 4 and the Schedule);
- (d) for administration where labels and seals are returned unused in respect of Pre‑basic 1 to 4, Super Elite, Elite or A (regulation 4 and the Schedule); and
- (e) for administration where an arrangement has been made by an applicant for an official examination of tubers to take place on a particular day but the applicant cancels the arrangement on that day (regulation 5 and the Schedule).

The Scottish Ministers may exempt any person, class of persons, or persons generally from compliance, in whole or in part, with the provisions of these Regulations (regulation 6).
