# The Criminal Proceedings etc. (Reform) (Scotland) Act 2007 (Commencement No. 7) Revocation Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 238 (C. 19)

CRIMINAL LAW

The Criminal Proceedings etc. (Reform) (Scotland) Act 2007 (Commencement No. 7) Revocation Order 2009

Made

10th June 2009

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 84(1) and (2) of the Criminal Proceedings etc. (Reform) (Scotland) Act 2007[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and revocation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Criminal Proceedings etc. (Reform) (Scotland) Act 2007 (Commencement No. 7) Revocation Order 2009.

(2) The Criminal Proceedings etc. (Reform) (Scotland) Act 2007 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2009[^f00002] is revoked.

KENNY MACASKILL

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

10th June 2009

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Criminal Proceedings etc. (Reform) (Scotland) Act 2007 (Commencement No. 7) Order 2009 which would otherwise have brought into force provisions in the Criminal Proceedings etc. (Reform) (Scotland) Act 2007 in their application to the Sheriffdom of South Strathclyde, Dumfries and Galloway on 29th June 2009.
