# The Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 190 (C. 14)

TITLE CONDITIONS

The Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2009

Made

14th May 2009

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 129(4) of the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2009.

## 2. Commencement of provisions

The day appointed for the coming into force of Part 6 of the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 is 1st June 2009.

FERGUS EWING

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

14th May 2009

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the provisions of Part 6 of the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (“the Act”) relating to the development management scheme into force on 1st June 2009. On the coming into force of Part 6 the Act is fully commenced.

The Act received Royal Assent on 3rd April 2003. Section 129(2) of the Act brought various provisions into force on the appointed day, 28th November 2004 (see section 122(1) of the Act and S.S.I.

[2003/456](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2003/456)). Section 129(3) and (5) of the Act specify provisions which came into force on the day after Royal Assent.

(This note is not part of the Order)

The following provisions of the Act have been brought into force by commencement order made before the date of this Order.
