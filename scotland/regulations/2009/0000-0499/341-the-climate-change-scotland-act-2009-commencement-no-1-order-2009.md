# The Climate Change (Scotland) Act 2009 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 341 (C. 27)

Climate Change

The Climate Change (Scotland) Act 2009 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2009

Made

6th October 2009

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 100(2) and (5) of the Climate Change (Scotland) Act 2009[^f00001].

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Climate Change (Scotland) Act 2009 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2009.

## 2. Commencement

(1) Provisions of the Climate Change (Scotland) Act 2009 come into force as follows.

(2) The following provisions come into force on 31st October 2009—

- (a) sections 1 to 24, 26, 33 to 43, 53 to 55, 57 to 62, 74 to 95, 97 and 98;
- (b) section 99, but only insofar as it relates to the provisions of schedule 2 mentioned in sub‑paragraph (c); and
- (c) paragraphs 1, 2 and 7 of schedule 2.

(3) Sections 63 to 69 and 71 to 73 come into force on 1st April 2010.

(4) Sections 44 to 52 come into force on 1st January 2011.

STEWART STEVENSON

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

6th October 2009
