# The Renewables Obligation (Scotland) Order 2009, section 12E

> Text as at 2026-11-12.

## 12E. Revision of the obligation level published on or before 1 October 2026 in respect of the 2027/28 obligation period[^c-512931b10ce7bca8ff714bfb0e331eff]

(1) This article applies in respect of the 2027/28 obligation period.

(2) The Scottish Ministers may revise the obligation level published on or before 1 October 2026 in respect of the 2027/28 obligation period in accordance with paragraph (5).

(3) If the Scottish Ministers revise the obligation level under paragraph (2), they may provide that the obligation level published on or before 1 October 2026 applies for part of the 2027/28 obligation period and the revised obligation level applies for the remainder of the 2027/28 obligation period.

(4) The power conferred by paragraph (2) may only be exercised once, and may not be exercised after 31 March 2027.

(5) Any revision of the obligation level under paragraph (2) must be calculated in accordance with—

- (a) article 12D(2) where the total obligation for the 2027/28 period is calculation A, or
- (b) article 12D(3) where the total obligation for the 2027/28 period is calculation B.

(6) If the Scottish Ministers revise the obligation level under paragraph (2), they must publish the revised obligation level within 7 days beginning on the day on which the obligation level is revised.

(7) In this article—

- “**2027/28 obligation period**” means the obligation period beginning on 1 April 2027,
- “**obligation level**” means the number of renewables obligation certificates that a designated electricity supplier is required to produce in respect of each megawatt hour of electricity that it supplies to customers in Scotland during the obligation period in order to discharge its renewables obligation for that period,
- “**revised obligation level**” means the obligation level as revised under paragraph (2).
