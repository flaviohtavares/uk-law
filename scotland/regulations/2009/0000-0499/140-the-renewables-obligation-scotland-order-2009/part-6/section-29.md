# The Renewables Obligation (Scotland) Order 2009, section 29

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 29. Microgenerators

(1) This article applies to a generating station which–

- (a) is a microgenerator; and
- (b) has not had a declared net capacity in excess of 50 kilowatts at any time after 31st March 2009.

(2)[^key-7ae4dda7d844d9e5ab7577e209be600b] The amount of electricity to be stated in each SROC issued in respect of electricity generated—

- (a) by a generating station to which this article applies; and
- (b) using—
  - (i) pre-2013 capacity;
  - (ii) 2013/14 capacity; or
  - (iii) 2014/15 capacity,

is [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2009/140) of a megawatt hour.

(3) The amount of electricity to be stated in each SROC issued in respect of electricity generated—

- (a) by a generating station to which this article applies; and
- (b) using 2015/16 capacity,

is [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2009/140) of a megawatt hour.

(4) The amount of electricity to be stated in each SROC issued in respect of electricity generated—

- (a) by a generating station to which this article applies; and
- (b) using post-2016 capacity,

is [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2009/140) of a megawatt hour.

(5) This article is subject to article 32.
