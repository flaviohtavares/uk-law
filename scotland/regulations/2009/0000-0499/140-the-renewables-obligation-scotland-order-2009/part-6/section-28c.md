# The Renewables Obligation (Scotland) Order 2009, section 28C

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 28C. Low-range co-firing in the 2013/14 and 2014/15 obligation periods[^key-680ccff0063d6dac6e5b9b0977b74802]

(1) This article applies to electricity which is generated—

- (a) before 1st April 2015;
- (b) in the way described as “low-range co-firing” in Schedule 2; and
- (c) by a generating station to which article 29 does not apply.

(2) Subject to paragraph (4), the amount of electricity to be stated in each SROC issued in respect of electricity to which this article applies is [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2009/140) of a megawatt hour.

(3) Paragraph (4) applies to electricity to which this article applies which is generated—

- (a) in the way described as “low-range co-firing with CHP” in Schedule 2; and
- (b) using—
  - (i) pre-2013 capacity; or
  - (ii) 2013/15 capacity in respect of which a declaration has been made in accordance with article 28(7).

(4) Where this paragraph applies, the amount of electricity to be stated in each SROC is—

- (a) in respect of the qualifying proportion of the electricity to which this paragraph applies, [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2009/140) of a megawatt hour; and
- (b) in respect of the remainder of the electricity to which this paragraph applies, [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2009/140) of a megawatt hour.

(5) This article is subject to articles 28D, 28E and 32.
