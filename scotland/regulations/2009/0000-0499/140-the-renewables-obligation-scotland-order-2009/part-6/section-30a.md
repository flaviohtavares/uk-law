# The Renewables Obligation (Scotland) Order 2009, section 30A

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 30A. Offshore wind generating stations using 2006/10 wind turbines[^c-1a621b42aef67e064b178757ca50ac31][^c-b67881bd39a1931c5b80204edfedcfef]

(1)[^c-704fb260b44366a28dc205266948e6e6] This article applies to electricity which is generated—

- (a) from wind;
- (b) by a generating station which is offshore; and
- (c) using 2006/10 wind turbines.

(2)[^c-6bf553c8663044b9ef76a1fb79240e0e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-6bf553c8663044b9ef76a1fb79240e0e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-f813c1a695aea5388bfcb48362cacb31] The amount of electricity to be stated in each SROC issued in respect of electricity to which this article applies is [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2009/140) of a megawatt hour.

(5) Where the electricity [^c-6f295f782e8b6de8a26538e0a0641e1f]... to which this article applies is generated in part using 2006/10 wind turbines[^c-faf59afdc9178505e3e9b296024e4cfb], but the amount of electricity so generated is not measured separately from electricity generated otherwise than by using those turbines, the percentage of the electricity which is to be treated (for the purposes of paragraph (4)) as having been generated using those turbines is the total installed capacity of those turbines at the date of generation of the electricity expressed as a percentage of the total installed capacity of the station at that date.

(6)[^c-94d64f73a6ff90279d2fa92031038aa3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^c-ddc2b4f1954eb0b5587ada0059197bfc] In this article, “2006/10 wind turbine”, in relation to a generating station, means a wind turbine which, in the Authority’s view, forms part of the station from a date no earlier than 12th July 2006 and no later than 31st March 2010.

(8) This article is subject to article 32.
