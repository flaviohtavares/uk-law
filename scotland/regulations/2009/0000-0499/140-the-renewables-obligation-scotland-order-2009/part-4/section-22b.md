# The Renewables Obligation (Scotland) Order 2009, section 22B

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 22B. Common agricultural policy requirements[^c-b6c3c24e27c3a6a354d44f95ef716117]

(1)[^c-043b9405f5c1775738c12d50e730c3aa] No SROCs are to be issued in respect of any electricity generated by a generating station from bioliquid if—

- (a) the bioliquid is derived from biomaterial which—
  - (i) is of agricultural origin;
  - (ii) was cultivated in the United Kingdom or the EU[^c-bcede1a8263d7203bf803d7ae7de9e7a]; and
  - (iii) is not waste; and
- (b) the Authority is satisfied that the biomaterial referred to in sub-paragraph (a) was—
  - (i)[^c-3a3168f51828eae32b74e3b9fc6e4b3e] cultivated in a manner that breached a requirement or standard listed in the third column of the table in Annex 2 to Regulation (EU) No 1306/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the financing, management and monitoring of the common agricultural policy (“**the 2013 Regulation**”) and corresponding to the entry in the first column of that table for “environment, climate change, good agricultural condition of land”;
  - (ii) cultivated in a manner that breached statutory management requirement number 10 in Annex 2 to the 2013 Regulation; or
  - (iii) obtained from land which does not meet the minimum requirements for good agricultural and environmental condition defined pursuant to Article 94 of the 2013 Regulation.

(2)[^c-7fc4d2745ec2dd946ed7d8f4807cb860] In paragraph (1), a reference to the 2013 Regulation is a reference to—

- (a) in relation to biomaterial cultivated in the United Kingdom on or after IP completion day, the 2013 Regulation as it forms part of domestic law;
- (b) in any other case, the 2013 Regulation as it has effect in EU law.
