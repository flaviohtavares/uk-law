# The Renewables Obligation (Scotland) Order 2009, section 19

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 19. Generating stations generating under arrangements referred to in the Electricity (Northern Ireland) Order 1992

No SROCs are to be issued in respect of any electricity generated by a generating station which generates electricity under the arrangements or additional arrangements referred to in Article 35(1) of the Electricity (Northern Ireland) Order 1992 [^c1964626].

## Annotations

[^c1964626]: Marginal citation: [S.I. 1992/231 (N.I. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1992/231), [Article 35](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1992/231/article/35) is prospectively repealed by [S.I. 2003/419 (N.I. 6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2003/419), but the relevant provision has not yet been commenced.
