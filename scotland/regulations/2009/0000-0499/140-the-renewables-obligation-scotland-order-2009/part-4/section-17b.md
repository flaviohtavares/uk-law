# The Renewables Obligation (Scotland) Order 2009, section 17B

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 17B. Microgenerators in respect of which feed-in tariffs may be available[^key-b813e37f4b899e100dd0cd2ffc107a0c]

SROCs are not to be issued in respect of any electricity generated on or after 1st April 2010 by a microgenerator—

- (a) if that microgenerator is a hydro generating station; or
- (b) if that electricity is generated—
  - (i) from gas formed by the anaerobic digestion of material which is neither sewage nor material in a landfill;
  - (ii) from the direct conversion of sunlight into electricity; or
  - (iii) from wind.
