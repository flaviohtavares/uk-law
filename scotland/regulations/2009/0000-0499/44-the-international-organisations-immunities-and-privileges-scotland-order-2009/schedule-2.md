## SCHEDULE 2 — INTERNATIONAL TELECOMMUNICATIONS SATELLITE ORGANISATION

*Extent: S*

Article 2

### General

**1.**[^key-408441befd21d44a1157d1a294e84896] In this Schedule–

- “the Organisation” means the International Telecommunications Satellite Organisation to which the 1979 Order applies;
- “the 1979 Order” means the INTELSAT (Immunities and Privileges) Order 1979[^f00012];
- “the 1961 Convention Articles” means the Articles (being certain Articles of the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations signed in 1961) which are set out in Schedule 1 to the Diplomatic Privileges Act 1964.

### The Organisation

**2.**[^key-76909145a1465b83c62708e38e8c1254] The Organisation shall have the like relief from non-domestic rates on its official premises as, in accordance with Article 23 of the 1961 Convention Articles, is accorded in respect of the premises of a diplomatic mission.
