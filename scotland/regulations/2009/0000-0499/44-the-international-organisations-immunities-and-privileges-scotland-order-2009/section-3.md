# The International Organisations (Immunities and Privileges) (Scotland) Order 2009, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2009-02-12. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ssi/2009/44
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 3. Revocations[^c-c696899e271f4e050cfe728d475600d6]

*Extent: S*

The following Orders are revoked (so far as they apply in or as regards Scotland)–

- (a) in consequence of Schedule 1, the INMARSAT (Immunities and Privileges) Order 1980[^f00003];
- (b) in consequence of Schedule 4, the International Sea-Bed Authority (Immunities and Privileges) Order 1996[^f00004];
- (c) in consequence of Schedule 8–
  - (i) the Inter-Governmental Maritime Consultative Organisation (Immunities and Privileges) Order 1968[^f00005];
  - (ii) the Inter-Governmental Maritime Consultative Organization (Immunities and Privileges) (Amendment) Order 1972[^f00006];
  - (iii) the Inter-Governmental Maritime Consultative Organisation (Immunities and Privileges) (Amendment) Order 1982[^f00007];
- (d) in consequence of Schedule 11, the International Tribunal for the Law of the Sea (Immunities and Privileges) Order 1996[^f00008].
