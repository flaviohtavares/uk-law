# The Looked After Children (Scotland) Regulations 2009, section 18

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 18. Meetings of the fostering panel

*Extent: S*

(1) No business shall be conducted by the fostering panel unless at least 3 of its members meet as the panel.

(2) The fostering panel must make a written record of its proceedings and the reasons for its recommendations.

(3) In paragraph (1) “**member**” does not include any medical adviser or legal adviser.
