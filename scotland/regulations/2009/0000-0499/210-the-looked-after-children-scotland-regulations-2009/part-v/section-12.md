# The Looked After Children (Scotland) Regulations 2009, section 12

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 12. Agreement with kinship carers

*Extent: S*

Where a local authority make a decision to approve a kinship carer under regulation 11 that authority must enter into a written agreement with the kinship carer regarding the matters and obligations in Schedule 5 and any other matters and obligations as the authority consider appropriate.
