# The Looked After Children (Scotland) Regulations 2009, section 51

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 51. Fostering allowances

*Extent: S*

Where a person was receiving, before their revocation, an allowance under regulation 9 of the Fostering Regulations that person may continue to receive payments so made which, had there been no revocation, would have fallen to that person, or that person may agree to receive, instead of the continued payments, payments in accordance with regulation 33 of these Regulations.
