# The Looked After Children (Scotland) Regulations 2009, section 2

> Text as at 2021-09-30.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: S*

In these Regulations–

- “**the 1995 Act**” means the Children (Scotland) Act 1995;
- “the[^c-747b813f41b34ffb474e1ca1344dedcf] 2011 Act” means the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011;
- “**the Arrangements to Look After Children Regulations**” means the Arrangements to Look After Children (Scotland) Regulations 1996 [^c19720791];
- “child protection order” means an order mentioned in section 37 of the 2011 Act;
- “compulsory supervision order” has the meaning given by section 83 of the 2011 Act;
- “contact direction”—
  - (a) in relation to a child protection order, has the meaning given by section 41(3) of the 2011 Act;
  - (b) in relation to a compulsory supervision order, interim compulsory supervision order or medical examination order, means a measure contained within such an order of the type mentioned in section 83(2)(g) or 87(2)(e) of that Act;
- “exclusion order” has the meaning given by section 76(12) of the 1995 Act;
- “**foster carer**” means a person approved as a foster carer in accordance with a decision made under regulation 22(3), 23 or 26(8);
- “**the Fostering Regulations**” means the Fostering of Children (Scotland) Regulations 1996 [^c19720801];
- “**Health Board**” means a Health Board constituted under section 2 of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 [^c19720811];
- “interim compulsory supervision order” has the meaning given by section 86 of the 2011 Act;
- “**kinship carer**” means a person approved as a kinship carer in accordance with a decision made under regulation 10;
- “medical examination order” has the meaning given by section 87 of the 2011 Act;
- “**parent**” means either the mother or father of the child or both;
- “**registered fostering service**” means a person other than a local authority whose functions consist of or include the making of arrangements for or in connection with the performance of functions assigned to a local authority–
  - (a) under section 26(1)(a) of the 1995 Act; or
  - (b) by virtue of section 5(2) to (4) of the Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968 [^c19720821],

  and who is registered under Part 5 of the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010[^c-b64edadfeee312e25fac9029f60a5ac8]; and
- “relevant person” has the meaning given by section 200 of the[^c-06a92b043e6f6a0cc76b873773fe9abf] 2011 Act and includes a person deemed to be a relevant person by virtue of section 81(3), 160(4)(b) or 164(6) of that Act; [^c-c00f60a8ba01260c17c8c255002f014e]...
- “sibling of the child” means—[^c-0664dddfa9437c0a812728ff63707468]
  - (a) a person who has at least one parent in common with the child, and
  - (b) any other person with whom the child has lived or is living, and with whom the child has an ongoing relationship with the character of a relationship between siblings;
- “warrant to secure attendance” has the meaning given by section 88 of the 2011 Act;
