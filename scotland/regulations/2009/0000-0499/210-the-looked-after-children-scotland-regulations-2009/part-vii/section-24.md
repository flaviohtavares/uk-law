> Text as at 2021-09-30.

## 24. Agreements with foster carers

*Extent: S*

Where a local authority make a decision to approve a person as a foster carer that authority must enter into a written agreement with the foster carer regarding the matters and obligations in Schedule 6 and any other matters or obligations as the authority consider appropriate.
