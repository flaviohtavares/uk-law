## 21. Foster carers

*Extent: S*

(1) A local authority may make a decision to approve a person as a suitable carer for a child [^key-434ad27b7b722bc4f57d6706f030d78b]... which carer shall be known as a “foster carer”.

(2) A decision to approve a person as a foster carer must be made in accordance with regulation 22.
