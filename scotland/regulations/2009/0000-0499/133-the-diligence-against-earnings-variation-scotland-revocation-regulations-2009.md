# The Diligence against Earnings (Variation) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 133

ENFORCEMENT

DILIGENCE

The Diligence against Earnings (Variation) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2009

Made

26th March 2009

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

27th March 2009

Coming into force

28th March 2009

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 49(7)(a), 53(3) and 63(6) of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Diligence against Earnings (Variation) (Scotland) Revocation Regulations 2009.

(2) They come into force on 28th March 2009.

## 2. Revocation of the Diligence against Earnings (Variation) (Scotland) Regulations 2009

The Diligence against Earnings (Variation) (Scotland) Regulations 2009[^f00002] are revoked.

FERGUS EWING

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

26th March 2009

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Diligence against Earnings (Variation) (Scotland) Regulations 2009 changed certain figures relating to diligence against earnings in Part III of the Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987 from 6th April 2009.

These Regulations revoke those Regulations before they come into force.
