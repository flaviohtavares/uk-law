# The Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 192 (C. 15)

COURT OF SESSION

HIGH COURT OF JUSTICIARY

JUDICIAL APPOINTMENTS AND DISCIPLINE

JUDICIAL INDEPENDENCE

SCOTTISH COURT SERVICE

SHERIFF COURT

The Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2009

Made

18th May 2009

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 76(1) of the Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2009.

(2) References to “the Act” mean the Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008.

## 2. Appointed days

The day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Act specified in column (1) of the Schedule (the subject matter of each provision being mentioned in column (2)) is 1st June 2009.

FERGUS EWING

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

18th May 2009
