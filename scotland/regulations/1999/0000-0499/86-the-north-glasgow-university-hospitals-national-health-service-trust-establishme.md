# The North Glasgow University Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No.86

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE

The North Glasgow University Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999

Made

29th September 1999

Coming into force

1st October 1999

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 12A(1) and (4) of, and paragraphs 1 and 3(1)(b) and (c) of Schedule 7A to, the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the North Glasgow University Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Amendment Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st October 1999.

(2) In this Order “the principal Order” means the North Glasgow University Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1998[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment of the principal Order

(1) For article 3 of the principal Order there is substituted–

> **3. Nature and functions of the trust**
>
> (1) The trust is established for the purpose specified in section 12A(1) of the Act.
>
> (2) The trust’s functions shall be to provide goods and services for the purpose of the Health Service at, from or through hospital accommodation and facilities at Glasgow Royal Infirmary, Stobhill General Hospital, Western Infirmary, Gartnavel General Hospital and Glasgow Dental Hospital, all Glasgow, and at establishments associated with those hospitals, and also including the management of any teaching and research facilities associated with those establishments and the support services related thereto.

.

(3) In article 4 of the principal Order (directors of the trust) for the words “non-executive directors” there is substituted “trustees”.

IAIN GRAY

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

29th September1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the North Glasgow University Hospitals National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1998 (S.I. [1998/2729](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/2729)) to substitute a new article 3 relating to the nature and functions of the trust and to amend article 4 to substitute a reference to “trustees” for the reference to “non-executive directors”.
