# The Erskine Bridge (Temporary Suspension of Tolls) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 116

ROADS AND BRIDGES

The Erskine Bridge (Temporary Suspension of Tolls) Order 1999

Made

15th October 1999

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

15th October 1999

Coming into force

16th October 1999

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon themby section 6 of the Erskine Bridge Tolls Act 1968[^f00001] and of allother powers enabling them in that behalf and having given not less thanfourteen days public notice in accordance with section 6(3) of that Act,hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Erskine Bridge (Temporary Suspension ofTolls) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 16th October 1999.

(2) In this Order, “the Principal Order” means the Erskine BridgeTolls Order 1992[^f00002].

## 2. Provision

The tolls leviable in accordance with the Principal Order shall besuspended, during the period beginning on 16th October 1999 and ending on15th April 2001, at any time, between 1800 hours on a Saturday and 0700hours on the next following Monday–

- (a) where the use by any person of a vehicle on all of the lengths of roadspecified in the Schedule to this Order is prohibited for a period of nofewer than 12 hours by virtue of an Order made under section 14(1)(a) ofthe Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00003]; and
- (b) within one hour before, or one hour after, any such period of suchprohibition.

Due to the need to carry out essential repairs at the Kingston Bridge,a series of closures of that bridge are necessary.

In consequence of those closures and in order to alleviate trafficcongestion, tolls on the Erskine Bridge are to be suspended during therelevant closure periods when the Kingston Bridge is closed to traffic inboth directions for a period of 12 hours or more. The suspension is tocommence one hour before and after such closures and will apply only duringthe times stipulated in article 2. This Order gives effect to thatsuspension of tolls
