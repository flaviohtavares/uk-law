# Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules Amendment No. 4) (Drug Treatment and Testing Orders) 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 191

HIGH COURT OF JUSTICIARY

SHERIFF COURT

SUMMARY JURISDICTION

Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules Amendment No. 4) (Drug Treatment and Testing Orders) 1999

Made

16th December 1999

Coming into force

20th December 1999

The Lord Justice General, Lord Justice Clerk and Lords Commissioners of Justiciary under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 305 of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00001], and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

–

(1) This Act of Adjournal may be cited as the Act of Adjournal (Criminal Procedure Rules Amendment No. 4) (Drug Treatment and Testing Orders) 1999 and shall come into force on 20th December 1999

(2) This Act of Adjournal shall be inserted in the Books of Adjournal.

## 2. Form of drug treatment and testing order.

–

(1) The Criminal Procedure Rules 1996[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) After rule 20.12A insert–

> **20.12B. Form and notification of drug treatment and testing orders**
>
> A drug treatment and testing order made under section 234B of the Act of 1995 shall be in Form 20.12B.

.

(3) In the appendix, after Form 20.12A-D, insert Form 20.12B as set out in the Schedule to this Act of Adjournal.

Rodger of Earlsferry

Lord Justice General I.P.D.

Edinburgh

16th December 1999

## SCHEDULE I

FORM 20.12B

**Form of drug treatment and testing order made under section 234B of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995**

*DRUG TREATMENT AND TESTING ORDER*

under section 234B of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995

Rule 20.12B

[image: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/1999/191/images/ssi_19990191_en_001)

(This note is not part of the Act of Adjournal)

Section 89 of the Crime and Disorder Act [1998 (c. 37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/37) inserted section 234B into the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995 to empower a court to make drug treatment and testing orders in certain circumstances.

This Act of Adjournal amends the Criminal Procedure Rules 1996 by prescribing the form in which such drug treatment and testing orders are to be made.
