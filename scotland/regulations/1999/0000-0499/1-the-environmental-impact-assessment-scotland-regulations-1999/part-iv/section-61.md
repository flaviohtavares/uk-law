# The Environmental Impact Assessment (Scotland) Regulations 1999, section 61

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 61. Confidentiality

Any person required to provide information under this Part shall not be required to provide information which that person is entitled or bound to hold in confidence.
