# The Health Act 1999 (Commencement No. 6) (Scotland) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 115 (C. 73)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE

The Health Act 1999 (Commencement No. 6) (Scotland) Order 1999

Made

13th October 1999

The Scottish Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 67(1) of the Health Act 1999[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, hereby make the following Order–

## 1. Citation, interpretation and extent

(1) This Order may be cited as the Health Act 1999 (Commencement No. 6) (Scotland) Order 1999.

(2) In this Order “the Act” means the Health Act 1999.

(3) This order extends to Scotland only.

## 2. Appointed day

14th October 1999 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 59 of the Act.

SUSAN C DEACON

A member of the Scottish Executive

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

13th October 1999

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force, in relation to Scotland, further provisions of the Health Act [1999 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/8) (“the Health Act”).

The provisions which are brought into force relate to the recovery of charges and payments where a person fails to pay an NHS charge or receives a payment or benefit towards the cost of an NHS charge or service to which he is not entitled.
