# The Looked After Children (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 14

Children And Young Persons

The Looked After Children (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2013

Made

24th January 2013

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

28th January 2013

Coming into force

15th March 2013

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 17(1) and (7) of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Looked After Children (Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2013 and come into force on 15th March 2013.

## 2. Amendment of the Looked After Children (Scotland) Regulations 2009

At the end of regulation 6(1)(a) of the Looked After Children (Scotland) Regulations 2009[^f00002] (death of a looked after child) omit “and” and insert—

> - (aa) notify Social Care and Social Work Improvement Scotland[^f00003]; and

.

AILEEN CAMPBELL

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

24th January 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 6(1) of the Looked After Children (Scotland) Regulations 2009 which make provision in relation to the death of looked after children with the effect that Social Care and Social Work Improvement Scotland must also be notified immediately by a local authority when a child who is looked after by that local authority dies.
