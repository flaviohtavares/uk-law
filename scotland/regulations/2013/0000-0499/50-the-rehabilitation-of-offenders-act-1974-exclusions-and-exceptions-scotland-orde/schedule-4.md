## SCHEDULE 4 — EXCEPTED PROFESSIONS, OFFICES, EMPLOYMENTS AND OCCUPATIONS

*Extent: S*

Article 5(a)

### PART 1 — Professions

**1.**[^key-eb344e2996949f35ac5c839c6cede3dc] Medical practitioner.

**2.**[^key-6880c8b871e693c551ae8d161bbaede6] Advocate, solicitor.

**3.**[^key-8e3943f6ce6a5c0d080190fd2b559c26] Accountant.

**4.**[^key-0df9c2cfa0332d67e0c975702cfa4ed8] Dentist or any profession complementary to dentistry for which a title is specified in regulations under section 36A(2) of the Dentists Act 1984[^f00093] (professions complementary to dentistry) by virtue of section 36A(3) of that Act.

**5.**[^key-69ee37c93e266c8e642c01856e1fded1] Veterinary surgeon.

**6.**[^key-9ff93ec6bdde9f0042eb9e21922b6389] Nurse or midwife.

**7.**[^key-1e1bb59c8b9854c258035a4971dc1daf] Ophthalmic optician, dispensing optician.

**8.**[^key-2c25eb181ed485242effeb9cd5ecf6ff] Registered pharmacist.

**9.**[^key-093cda80214b4f58fccfe11a046ba6db] Registered pharmacy technician.

**10.**[^key-6a152346a57746d3a11eb7afb07b3253] Registered teacher.

**11.**[^key-7ce7822ef6d8835b4e0394e680ca28d3] Any profession to which the Health Professions Order 2001[^f00094] applies and which is undertaken following registration under that Order.

**12.**[^key-4055397ea7ebbb62511922c201730de9] Registered osteopath.

**13.**[^key-441be83590d5b7d97669d09c48835b35] Registered chiropractor.

**14.**[^key-bce5c0f8c4848db3891cdd5a0c33bcda] Actuary.

**15.**[^key-28fb4021236f5e06b220b1eaf3535ba5] Registered European lawyer, registered foreign lawyer.

**16.**[^key-ee297a58132febda1f9286aed76098a0] Social worker.

**17.**[^key-77bc5c6468f9be7683bf5263ccdbb0cc] Social service worker.

**18.**[^key-551cf8c2db63fb0c58190b23f57d7ddc] Registered associate.

### PART 2 — Offices and employments

**1.**[^key-481548fdcbc21d27da0204f8513b5c56] Judicial appointments.

**2.**[^key-7ba7e3f029d84f0c0eaf2b8ad0ee90be] Prosecutors, officers assisting prosecutors, and officers assisting in the work of the Crown Office.

**3.**[^key-b5c43e744aa82b69fdc57114c36ef1bd] Justices of the Peace and members of local authorities with signing functions under section 76 of the Criminal Proceedings etc. (Reform) (Scotland) Act 2007[^f00095].

**4.**[^key-1a030e94bee163d2229e896bfec0398b] Clerks (including depute and assistant clerks) and officers of the High Court of Justiciary, the Court of Session and the justice of the peace court, sheriff clerks (including sheriff clerks depute) and their clerks and assistants and other support officers assisting in the work of the Scottish Court Service.

**5.**[^key-b578e7a1f5efd2900c8b0155f78c568d] Precognition agents.

**6.**[^M_F_b209712a-7832-4259-fb1c-b0203c50df38] Constables, police custody and security officers, persons appointed as police cadets to undergo training with a view to becoming constables and naval, military and air force police.

**6A.** Persons employed for the purposes of a police force established under any enactment and persons appointed to assist in the carrying out of police functions.

**7.**[^key-aa7320ce3e3bd4b94cf914d02d826568] Any office, employment or work which is concerned with the administration of, or is otherwise normally carried out wholly or partly within the precincts of, a prison, remand centre, young offenders institution, detention centre or removal centre, prison monitoring co-ordinators appointed under section 7A(2) of the Prisons (Scotland) Act 1989 and independent prison monitors appointed under section 7B(2)(a) of that Act.

**8.**[^key-fddaadbbed79bf719fd2edaa7647c08a] Traffic wardens appointed under or in accordance with section 95 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984[^f00098].

**9.**[^key-7ea7a1f21b1accaf25f99912f89ec5c8] Any employment or work which is concerned with the provision of a care service.

**10.**[^key-5b053a7f24d4bc864cbf18533955e01c] Any employment or work which is concerned with the provision of health services and which is of such a kind as to enable the holder to have access to persons in receipt of such services in the course of that person’s normal duties.

**11.**[^key-f35303b7c06e0ca16e197f42b8200e29] A regulated role[^key-af94d276017ebc786b3b24bf693a711b] with children.

**12.**[^key-5682f051d1b55394e133b8a580ea3aeb] Any employment or work in the Scottish Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals where the person employed or working, as part of his or her duties, may carry out the killing of animals.

**13.**[^key-570502d5a93e651f0830f6346876ba32] Any office, employment or work in the Serious Fraud Office.

**14.**[^key-e3349b43b6a9f65d6b8c2a182890d1a5] Any office, employment or work in the Serious Organised Crime Agency.

**15.**[^key-31d9da7e9e3dc93b7c334bf8a0d0123d] Any office, employment or work in Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs.

**16.**[^key-7ff3aefd7dc4dcface42be50df73de8b] Any employment which is concerned with the monitoring, for the purposes of child protection, of communications by means of the internet.

**17.**[^key-60e1da17d0b0da2bcc03e28bf82bf1f3] Any office or employment in the Scottish Social Services Council.

**18.**[^key-c9a035171204bf1146abe272554d61d7] Her Majesty’s Inspectors, or any person appointed by the Scottish Ministers for the purposes of section 66 of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980[^f00099] or section 9 of the Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000[^f00100], or members of any Management Board established to assist either Her Majesty’s Inspectors or any such person, or any individual undertaking employment or work for Her Majesty’s Inspectors or any such person in relation to the carrying out of inspections under section 66 of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980, section 9 of the Standards in Scotland’s Schools etc. Act 2000 or section 115 of the 2010 Act[^f00101], or otherwise in regard to matters associated with such inspections.

**19.**[^key-0d47f16beb15616898a8bd69e9627a0c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**20.**[^key-1ee0083f4b1d0c4d0c582103a660e2c4] Members of a panel established by virtue of section 101(1) of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995[^f00103] (panels for curators *ad litem*, reporting officers and safeguarders).

**21.**[^key-93491227cc92fdaed2977c8f87dc548c] Any office or employment in the Risk Management Authority.

**22.**[^key-bfcc7408c727ef5597577feb341fc154] Any office or employment in the Scottish Criminal Cases Review Commission.

**23.**[^key-d67b227ab2a8cd24e15ea03755b1ff65] Any office or employment in the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service[^key-e608872528a2aaf70c4f483bec4ae1cc].

**24.**[^key-c7dbf7d69ac607ce55e4f493f0904293] Any employment or work in a body concerned primarily with the provision of counselling or other support to individuals who are or appear to be victims of, or witnesses to, offences, and which involves having access to personal information about such individuals.

**25.**[^key-6582e3c255976674e7f231944ebeb790] A regulated role[^key-0e7febca6e25a3c1db8e8011127d7346] with adults.

**26.**[^key-21321402db76b99ef061c0366f8996b3] Members mentioned in section 35(4)(c) of the Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008[^f00105] of a tribunal constituted under section 35(1) of that Act to consider the fitness for judicial office of a person holding a judicial office mentioned in section 35(2) of that Act.

**27.**[^key-3dcd186c0bdbc7f022df556d1aa64f0a] Members mentioned in section 21(4)(d) of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014 of a tribunal constituted under section 21(1) or (2) of that Act to consider the fitness for office of a person holding a judicial office mentioned in section 21(3) of that Act.

**28.**[^key-d1bf3385e01f0ef715888ea255acb292] Lay members of the Judicial Appointments Board for Scotland appointed by the Scottish Ministers under paragraph 2 of schedule 1 to the Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008.

**29.**[^key-d0804f2d3a14130bf4120389e5879787] Non-judicial members of the Scottish Court Service mentioned in paragraph 2(3)(d) of schedule 3 to the Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008.

**30.**[^key-d74003675afe72515c5f7c75bdb19933] Any office or employment in Social Care and Social Work Improvement Scotland.

**31.**[^key-7d2c7b82ea5769cbf86fb4ce3f60c559] Any office or employment in the General Teaching Council for Scotland.

**32.**[^key-5376373ae518444dcf1195dc25544db0] A Head of Practice or a member of a Practice Committee of a licensed legal services provider.

### PART 3 — Occupations

**1.**[^key-c1937647235400cdaf158c25a799b880] Firearms dealer.

**2.**[^key-e38a455366b8e47f6eeed1ceb6e798c1] Any occupation in respect of which an application to the Gambling Commission for a licence, certificate or registration is required by or under any enactment.

**3.**[^key-7c4cf11882bcd504e555419625f4602d] Any occupation which is concerned with the management of a place in respect of which the approval of the Scottish Ministers is required by section 1 of the Abortion Act 1967[^f00107].

**4.**[^key-682bb4b10959ddf506add873b7ea72a6] Any occupation in respect of which the holder is required pursuant to regulations 4, 5 and 11 of the Explosives Regulations 2014[^key-313bf094240415c23beeccdf169f5304] to obtain from the chief officer of police an explosives certificate certifying that person to be a fit person to acquire or acquire and keep explosives.

**5.**[^key-08c3452752f6fc499da3fcdd4bf3ed4e] Taxi driver or private hire driver.

**6.**[^key-726e3bfe66d448b4120bf4c915212382] Any occupation in respect of which an application to the Security Industry Authority for a licence is required by the Private Security Industry Act 2001[^f00109].

**7.**[^key-cf13e1015efa1ae337196b564ab49eda] Any occupation which is concerned with visiting persons detained in police stations, for the purposes of examining and reporting on the conditions under which they are held.

**8.**[^key-13f5ad07a49d7259ca85ba2bab128638] Any occupation in respect of which a licence or registration is required by or under Part V (driving instruction) of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^f00110].
