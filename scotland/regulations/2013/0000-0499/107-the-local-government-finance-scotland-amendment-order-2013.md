# The Local Government Finance (Scotland) Amendment Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 107

Local Government

The Local Government Finance (Scotland) Amendment Order 2013

Made

19th March 2013

Coming into force

20th March 2013

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 1 of Schedule 12 to the Local Government Finance Act 1992[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with paragraph 2(2) of that Schedule, they have consulted with such associations of local authorities as appear to them to be appropriate.

In accordance with paragraph 2(3) of that Schedule[^f00002], a draft of this Order has been laid before, and approved by resolution of, the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Local Government Finance (Scotland) Amendment Order 2013 and comes into force on the day after the day on which it is made.

## 2. Amendment of the Local Government Finance (Scotland) Order 2013

For Schedule 1 (revenue support grant and distribution of non-domestic rate income 2013-2014) to the Local Government Finance (Scotland) Order 2013[^f00003], substitute the Schedule 1 set out in Schedule 1 to this Order.

**3.** For Schedule 2 (revenue support grant 2012-13) to the Local Government Finance (Scotland) Order 2013, substitute the Schedule 2 set out in Schedule 2 to this Order.

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Local Government Finance (Scotland) Order 2013 by replacing Schedule 1 to that Order with the Schedule 1 set out in the Schedule 1 to this Order and by replacing Schedule 2 to that Order with the Schedule 2 set out in the Schedule 2 to this Order. Column 2 of Schedule 1 lists the amount of revenue support grant, which is payable to each local authority in Scotland in respect of the financial year 2013-2014. Column 3 of Schedule 1 lists the amount of non-domestic rate income to be distributed to each local authority in Scotland in respect of the financial year 2013-2014. Column 2 of Schedule 2 redetermines the amount of revenue support grant, which is payable to each local authority in Scotland in respect of the financial year 2012-13.
