# The Children's Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Rules of Procedure in Children's Hearings) Rules 2013, section 8

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 8. Requirement to include child's views in documents

*Extent: S*

Where any document is to be given to members of the children's hearing or pre-hearing panel under, or by virtue of, the Act, or these Rules, the document must contain any views expressed by the child which have been given to the person who has prepared that document.
