# The Children's Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Rules of Procedure in Children's Hearings) Rules 2013, section 85

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 85. Determination of a non-disclosure request

*Extent: S*

(1) The Reporter must refer any non-disclosure request received from any person to a children's hearing for determination.

(2) The Reporter may submit a non-disclosure request to a children's hearing for determination at the Reporter's own initiative.
