# The Children's Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Rules of Procedure in Children's Hearings) Rules 2013, section 54

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 54. Children's hearing determining a matter referred under section 79 (referral of certain matters for pre-hearing determination) of the Act

*Extent: S*

Where by virtue of section 80(3) (determination of matter referred under section 79) of the Act the children's hearing is to determine a matter referred under section 79 of the Act—

- (a) rules 47A (other than paragraph (1)) to 48A[^c-897f8672c80cb381580d99bcec10836f] apply; and
- (b) references in those rules to the pre-hearing panel are to be read as references to the children's hearing.
