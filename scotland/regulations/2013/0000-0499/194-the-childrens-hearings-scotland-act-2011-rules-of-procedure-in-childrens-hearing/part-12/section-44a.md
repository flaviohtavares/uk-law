# The Children's Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Rules of Procedure in Children's Hearings) Rules 2013, section 44A

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 44A. Specification of matters that may be determined by pre-hearing panel[^c-1a2929b9854d7d5291dd342a67f74361]

*Extent: S*

Whether a person should be allowed to attend a children’s hearing only by electronic means (see rule 20C) is specified for the purpose of these Rules as a matter that may be determined by pre-hearing panels.
