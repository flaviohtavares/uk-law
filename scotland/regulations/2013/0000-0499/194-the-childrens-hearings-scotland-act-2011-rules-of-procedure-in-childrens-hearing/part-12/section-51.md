# The Children's Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Rules of Procedure in Children's Hearings) Rules 2013, section 51

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 51. Provision of information to persons deemed to be relevant persons

*Extent: S*

Where the pre-hearing panel deems a person to be a relevant person under section 81(3) (determination of claim that person be deemed a relevant person) of the Act, as soon as practicable after that determination, the Reporter must give to that person all information given under these Rules which is to be given to each relevant person.
