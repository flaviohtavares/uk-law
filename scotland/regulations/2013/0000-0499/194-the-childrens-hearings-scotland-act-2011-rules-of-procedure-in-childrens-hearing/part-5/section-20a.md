# The Children's Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Rules of Procedure in Children's Hearings) Rules 2013, section 20A

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 20A. Attendance by custody officer, etc. at pre-hearing panel or children’s hearing[^c-e378c338209ae09bbf022ca88f603100]

*Extent: S*

An individual is authorised to attend a pre-hearing panel or children’s hearing if—

- (a) another person is in the individual’s lawful custody, and
- (b) that other person is attending the panel or hearing.
