# The Children's Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Rules of Procedure in Children's Hearings) Rules 2013, section 100

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

> Extent: Scotland.

## 100. Service of notification and documents

*Extent: S*

Any notice or other document authorised or required under these Rules to be given by the Reporter to any person may be given by the Reporter or by any police constable.
