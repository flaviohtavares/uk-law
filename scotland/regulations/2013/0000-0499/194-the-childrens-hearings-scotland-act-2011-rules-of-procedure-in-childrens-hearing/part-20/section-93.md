# The Children's Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Rules of Procedure in Children's Hearings) Rules 2013, section 93

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 93. Notifying decision of section 158 (compulsory supervision order: suspension pending appeal) hearing

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a children's hearing has been held by virtue of section 158 of the Act the Reporter must give to the persons mentioned in paragraph (2) the information mentioned in paragraph (3) within 5 days of the children's hearing.

(2) Those persons are—

- (a) the child;
- (b) each relevant person;
- (c) any appointed safeguarder.

(3) That information is—

- (a) the decision of the children's hearing;
- (b) the reasons for that decision.
