# The Children's Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Rules of Procedure in Children's Hearings) Rules 2013, section 88A

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 88A. Notifying decision of a children’s hearing to an individual afforded an opportunity to participate[^c-dbdfde73a11be88545a1d23b93b5fe97]

*Extent: S*

Where a children’s hearing makes a direction which regulates contact between the child and an individual afforded an opportunity to participate in relation to the hearing by virtue of rule 2A, the Reporter must give to the individual—

- (a) a copy of the direction, and
- (b) the reasons for the direction.
