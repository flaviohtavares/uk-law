# The Children's Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Rules of Procedure in Children's Hearings) Rules 2013, section 43

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 43. Arranging a children's hearing under section 142 (review of determination that person be deemed a relevant person)

*Extent: S*

(1) This rule applies where a children's hearing under section 142(3) of the Act deferred determining the review under section 142(2) of the Act until a subsequent children's hearing.

(2) Wherever practicable when issuing notice under rule 22 and in all cases as soon as practicable before the beginning of the children's hearing to be held by virtue of section 142 of the Act, the Reporter must give to the persons mentioned in paragraph (3) any relevant document or other information for the consideration of the children's hearing.

(3) Those persons are—

- (a) the child;
- (b) any relevant person;
- (c) any appointed safeguarder;
- (d) the three members of the children's hearing.
