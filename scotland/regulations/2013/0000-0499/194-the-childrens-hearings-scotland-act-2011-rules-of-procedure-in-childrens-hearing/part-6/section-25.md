# The Children's Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (Rules of Procedure in Children's Hearings) Rules 2013, section 25

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 25. Information to be sent to the members of the children's hearing

*Extent: S*

Wherever practicable 7 days before, and no later than 3 days before, the intended date of the children's hearing the Reporter must give to the three members of the children's hearing notification of the date, time and place of the hearing.
