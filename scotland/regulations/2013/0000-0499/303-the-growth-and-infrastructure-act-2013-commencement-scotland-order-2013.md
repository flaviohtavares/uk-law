# The Growth and Infrastructure Act 2013 (Commencement) (Scotland) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 303 (C. 24)

Infrastructure Planning

Town And Country Planning

The Growth and Infrastructure Act 2013 (Commencement) (Scotland) Order 2013

Made

30th October 2013

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

1st November 2013

Coming into force

1st December 2013

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 35(4) of the Growth and Infrastructure Act 2013[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Growth and Infrastructure Act 2013 (Commencement) (Scotland) Order 2013 and comes into force on 1st December 2013.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for section 21(4) to (6) of the Growth and Infrastructure Act 2013 to come into force is 1st December 2013.

DEREK MACKAY

Authorised to sign by the Scottish Ministers

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

30th October 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings section 21(4) to (6) of the Growth and Infrastructure Act 2013 into force on 1st December 2013.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 25th April 2013. Sections 1(1) (partially) and sections 4, 7, 9, 16, 19, 26, 32, 33, 35 and 36 and Schedules 2 and 4 came into force on that date. Sections 11, 12, 17, 18, 29 and 30 came into force on 25th June 2013.
