# The Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Amendment Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 289

Title Conditions

The Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Amendment Order 2013

Made

9th October 2013

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

11th October 2013

Coming into force

9th December 2013

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 38(4) of the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

They are satisfied as to the matters mentioned in section 38(5) of that Act.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Amendment Order 2013 and comes into force on 9th December 2013.

## 2. Amendment of the list of prescribed conservation bodies

Part II of the Schedule to the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Order 2003[^f00002] (conservation bodies prescribed under section 38(4) of the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003) is amended by inserting, in the appropriate place according to alphabetical order, “Perth and Kinross Heritage Trust”.

KENNY MACASKILL

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

9th October 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003 (Conservation Bodies) Order 2003 by adding Perth and Kinross Heritage Trust to the list of prescribed conservation bodies. Conservation bodies are bodies in favour of whom it is competent to create a conservation burden under section 38(1) of the Title Conditions (Scotland) Act 2003.
