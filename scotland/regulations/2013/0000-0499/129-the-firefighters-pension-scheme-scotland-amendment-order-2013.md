# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Amendment Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 129

Fire Services

Pensions

The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Amendment Order 2013

Made

18th April 2013

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

22nd April 2013

Coming into force

1st May 2013

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 34 and 60 of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 34(5) of that Act, they have consulted with such persons as they considered appropriate.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Amendment Order 2013.

(2) This Order comes into force on 1st May 2013.

## 2. Amendment of the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Order 2007

Schedule 1 to the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) Order 2007[^f00002] (in which the New Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland) is set out) is amended in accordance with article 3.

**3.** For the Table in Annex A1 (pensions contributions), substitute—

> | *Pensionable pay* | *Contribution rate from 1st May 2013* |
> | --- | --- |
> | Up to and including £15,000 | 8.5% of pensionable pay |
> | More than £15,000 and up to and including £21,000 | 9.1% of pensionable pay |
> | More than £21,000 and up to and including £30,000 | 9.6% of pensionable pay |
> | More than £30,000 and up to and including £40,000 | 9.9% of pensionable pay |
> | More than £40,000 and up to and including £50,000 | 10.1% of pensionable pay |
> | More than £50,000 and up to and including £60,000 | 10.2% of pensionable pay |
> | More than £60,000 and up to and including £100,000 | 10.5% of pensionable pay |
> | More than £100,000 and up to and including £120,000 | 10.8% of pensionable pay |
> | More than £120,000 | 11.1% of pensionable pay |

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order changes as from 1st May 2013 the pension contribution rates of firefighters in Scotland who are members of the New Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Scotland).

In addition, a new band of pensionable pay has been created so that those firefighters who earn more than £15,000 and up to and including £21,000 will pay a lower rate than those earning more than £21,000 and up to and including £30,000.
