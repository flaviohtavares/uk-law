# Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No. 7) (Miscellaneous) 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 317

Court Of Session

Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No. 7) (Miscellaneous) 2013

Made

6th November 2013

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

8th November 2013

Coming into force

2nd December 2013

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred by section 5 of the Court of Session Act 1988[^f00001], section 446 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002[^f00002], sections 6(9), 6(10) and 11 of the Justice and Security Act 2013[^f00003] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf, do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation, commencement etc.

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Rules of the Court of Session Amendment No. 7) (Miscellaneous) 2013.

(2) It comes into force on 2nd December 2013.

(3) A certified copy of this Act of Sederunt is to be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

(4) The Rules of the Court of Session[^f00004] are amended in accordance with the following paragraphs.

## 2. Applications to amend the name of a party in more than one cause

In rule 24.6(4)[^f00005], for “(3)” substitute “(5)”.

## 3. Civil Recovery Investigations

In rule 76.37(3) (disclosure orders)[^f00006], after “Proceeds of Crime Act 2002” insert “(External Investigations) Order 2013”.

## 4. Justice and Security Act 2013

In rule 104.5(1) (hearing on applications)[^f00007], for “(4)” substitute “(6)”.

BRIAN GILL

Lord President

I.P.D.

Edinburgh

6th November 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends the Rules of the Court of Session. Paragraph 2 amends rule 24.6(4) (applications to amend the name of a party in more than one cause). Paragraph 3 amends rule 76.37(3) (civil recovery investigations: disclosure orders). Paragraph 4 amends rule 104.5(1) (Justice and Security Act 2013: hearing on applications).
