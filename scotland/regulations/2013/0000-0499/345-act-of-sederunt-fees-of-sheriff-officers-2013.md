# Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 345

Sheriff Court

Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 2013

Made

10th December 2013

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

12th December 2013

Coming into force

27th January 2014

The Lords of Council and Session, under and by virtue of the powers conferred upon them by section 40 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907[^f00001], section 6 of the Execution of Diligence (Scotland) Act 1926[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling them in that behalf[^f00003], do hereby enact and declare:

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) 2013 and comes into force on 27th January 2014.

(2) A certified copy of this Act of Sederunt is to be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of fees

For the Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) (No. 2) 2002[^f00004] substitute the Table of Fees in the Schedule to this Act of Sederunt.

## 3. Savings

Paragraph 2 does not affect the fees payable as respects any work carried out before 27<sup>th</sup> January 2014.

BRIAN GILL

Lord President

I.P.D.

Edinburgh

10th December 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt substitutes a new Table of Fees for the Table of Fees in Schedule 1 to the Act of Sederunt (Fees of Sheriff Officers) (No. 2) 2002, by substituting a new Table of Fees.

The fee levels in the new Table of Fees represent an increase of 2.15% on the existing fees. This increase takes effect from 27th January 2014.
