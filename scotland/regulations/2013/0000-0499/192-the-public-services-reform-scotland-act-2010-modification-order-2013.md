# The Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 Modification Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 192

Children And Young Persons

The Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 Modification Order 2013

Made

30th May 2013

Coming into force in accordance with article 1

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 15(2) of the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 25(3)(a) of that Act, they have consulted Children’s Hearings Scotland.

In accordance with section 25(3)(b)(ii) of that Act, they have laid before the Scottish Parliament an explanatory document.

In accordance with section 25(3)(b)(i) and (c) of that Act[^f00002], a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation and commencement

(1) This Order may be cited as the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 Modification Order 2013.

(2) This Order comes into force on the same day as section 7 (holding of children’s hearing) of the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011[^f00003].

## 2. Modification of schedule 5 to the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010

In schedule 5 (improvement of public functions: listed bodies) to the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010—

- (a) omit “any Children’s Panel” and “any Children’s Panel Advisory Committee”; and
- (b) after “Caledonian Maritime Assets Ltd” insert “Children’s Hearings Scotland”.

AILEEN CAMPBELL

Authorised to sign the Scottish Ministers

30th May 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes modifications to schedule 5 of the Public Services Reform (Scotland) Act 2010 (“the 2010 Act”) in consequence of the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011.

Schedule 5 of the 2010 Act is introduced by section 15 (public functions: further provision) of that Act and lists the persons, bodies and office-holders in respect of which the Scottish Ministers may, by order under section 14 of that Act, make any provision which they consider would improve the exercise of public functions having regard to efficiency, effectiveness and economy.

This Order adds Children’s Hearings Scotland (a new body created under the Children’s Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011) to the list of bodies in schedule 5 and removes the entries for Children’s Panels and Children’s Panel Advisory Committees.
