# The Budget (Scotland) Act 2012 Amendment Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2013 No. 117

Public Finance And Accountability

The Budget (Scotland) Act 2012 Amendment Order 2013

Made

26th March 2013

Coming into force

31st March 2013

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 7(1) of the Budget (Scotland) Act 2012[^f00001] and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 7(2) of that Act, a draft of this Order has been laid before and approved by resolution of the Scottish Parliament.

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) This Order may be cited as the Budget (Scotland) Act 2012 Amendment Order 2013 and comes into force on 31st March 2013.

(2) In this Order, “the Act” means the Budget (Scotland) Act 2012.

## 2. Amendment of amounts specified in section 4 (overall cash authorisations)

In section 4 of the Act, for the amount specified—

- (a) in subsection (2) (Scottish Administration), substitute “£31,178,566,000”;
- (b) in subsection (3)(a) (Forestry Commissioners), substitute “£66,750,000”; and
- (c) in subsection (3)(b) (Food Standards Agency), substitute “£9,830,000”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Budget (Scotland) Act 2012 (“the Act”).

Article 2 amends the overall cash authorisations specified in section 4(2) and (3) of the Act in relation to the Scottish Administration, the Forestry Commissioners and Food Standards Agency.

Article 3 amends the amount of resources (other than accruing resources) that may be used for certain purposes in schedule 1 to the Act. It also amends the total amount of those resources.

Article 4 amends the amount of resources (other than accruing resources) that may be used by the Forestry Commissioners, the Food Standards Agency and the Scottish Parliamentary Corporate Body for certain purposes in schedule 2 to the Act.
