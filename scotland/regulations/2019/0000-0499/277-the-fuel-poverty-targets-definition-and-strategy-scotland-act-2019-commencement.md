# The Fuel Poverty (Targets, Definition and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 277 (C. 9)

Energy

Housing

The Fuel Poverty (Targets, Definition and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2019

Made

3rd September 2019

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

5th September 2019

Coming into force

19th September 2019

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 20(2) of the Fuel Poverty (Targets, Definition and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Fuel Poverty (Targets, Definition and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2019 and come into force on 19 September 2019.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of sections 1 to 18(2) (excluding section 7(2)(g)) of the Fuel Poverty (Targets, Definition and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019 is 19 September 2019.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations commence provisions in the Fuel Poverty (Definition, Targets and Strategy) (Scotland) Act 2019 (“the Act”). Sections 19, 20 and 21 of the Act came into force on 19 July 2019 (the day after Royal Assent).
