# The Plant Health (Official Controls and Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Regulations 2019, section 1

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1. Citation, commencement and extent[^key-cf28167e1b4e552c1b325413024c6f79]

*Extent: S*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Plant Health (Official Controls and Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Regulations 2019 and come into force on 14 December 2019.

(2) These Regulations extend to Scotland only.
