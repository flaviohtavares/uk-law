# The Plant Health (Official Controls and Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Regulations 2019, section 7

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 7. Derogation to the requirement to give prior notification in accordance with Article 1(1) of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1013[^c-98a4d38571ebd2cac81232e7e580c103]

*Extent: S*

(A1)[^c-b07e09d1001aba8fb52a83961d674991] The operator who is responsible for a controlled consignment of forestry material which is to be brought into Scotland by air or a controlled consignment which is to be brought into a RoRo, port in Scotland[^c-afb57d54ddc6134ec9e081159877926f] must have notified the Scottish Ministers of the consignment’s expected arrival at least four working hours before its expected arrival in Scotland.

(1) In the case of any controlled consignment which consists, in whole or in part, of unprocessed logs or sawn or chipped wood and is to be brought into Scotland at a point of entry which only has a temporary border control post, the operator who is responsible for the controlled consignment must notify the Scottish Ministers of the consignment's arrival at least three working days before its expected arrival in Scotland.

(2) Article 1(1) of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1013 does not apply to any operator who is responsible for a controlled consignment referred to in paragraph (1).

(3) In this regulation—

- “**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1013**” means Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1013 on prior notification of consignments of certain categories of animals and goods entering the Union [^c23946081],
- “RoRo” means a RoRo listed location within the meaning of regulation 130 of the Customs (Import Duty) (EU Exit) Regulations 2018,[^c-d878b19b5048889bec4110674733eb75]
- “**temporary border control post**” means a border control post in Scotland which has been exempted from the obligations in Article 64(3)(a), (c) and (f) of the Official Controls Regulation pursuant to Article 4 of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/1012 supplementing Regulation (EU) 2017/625 of the European Parliament and of the Council by derogating from the rules on the designation of control points and from the minimum requirements for border control posts [^c23946091].
