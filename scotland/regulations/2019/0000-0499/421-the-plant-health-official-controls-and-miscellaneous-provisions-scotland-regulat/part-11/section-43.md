## 43. Defence: reasonable excuse for obstruction etc.[^key-c2afdbaa4730070f714c106b4d71d3a8]

*Extent: S*

It is a defence for a person charged with an offence under regulation 42 to show that the person had a reasonable excuse for the obstruction or failure in question.
