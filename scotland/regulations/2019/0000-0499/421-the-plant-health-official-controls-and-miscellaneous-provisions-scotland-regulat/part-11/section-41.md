# The Plant Health (Official Controls and Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Regulations 2019, section 41

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 41. Improper use of ... plant passports , Northern Ireland plant health labels or certificates[^c-6c940e4e17947f41a8347e992dba9b81][^c-48d18b20e079c16fcb4b2e99d907c6f4][^c-133156453c55472c96cef2945877c2ec]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person commits an offence if the person—

- (a) dishonestly issues a UK[^c-53bedd27573a45836cfd1f6ecc48af06] plant passport , a Northern Ireland plant health label[^c-0f10eb896f6815e087d72588e1339c67] or a certificate,
- (b) dishonestly alters a [^c-53920857a3bd261fe40448f6fa864060]... plant passport , a Northern Ireland plant health label or a certificate,
- (c) dishonestly re-uses a [^c-b4c5fbc794d2b9dd6e9c79eadf2bf2d4]... plant passport , a Northern Ireland plant health label or a certificate.

(2) In this regulation, “**certificate**” has the same meaning as in regulation 40.
