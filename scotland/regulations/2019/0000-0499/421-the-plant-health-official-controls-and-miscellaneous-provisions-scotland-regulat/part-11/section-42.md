# The Plant Health (Official Controls and Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Regulations 2019, section 42

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 42. Obstruction etc.[^key-51bfc2c4b690d0689075614ce4a25036]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person commits an offence if the person—

- (a) intentionally obstructs a plant health inspector or an authorised person acting in the execution or enforcement of the [^key-a8777658f1e9cb6ae6b3ee6bcf20b56d]... Plant Health Regulation, the Official Controls Regulation or these Regulations,
- (b) fails to give to a plant health inspector or an authorised person acting in the execution or enforcement of the [^key-a8777658f1e9cb6ae6b3ee6bcf20b56d]... Plant Health Regulation, the Official Controls Regulation or these Regulations any assistance or information which the inspector or authorised person may reasonably require for those purposes,
- (c) fails to produce a document or record when required to do so by a plant health inspector or authorised person acting in the execution or enforcement of the [^key-a8777658f1e9cb6ae6b3ee6bcf20b56d]... Plant Health Regulation, the Official Controls Regulation or these Regulations.

(2)[^key-284233ab197281f55bb5f38e9b36a2f6] In paragraph (1)—

- (a) “authorised person” means a person authorised by the Scottish Ministers,
- (b) a reference to the Plant Health Regulation or the Official Controls Regulation includes assimilated[^key-516c3aac460fd160d2b4171c71f324d5] law adopted, or regulations made, under the Plant Health Regulation or the Official Controls Regulation and any other assimilated law relating to plant health.
