## 39. Defence: reasonable excuse for failure to comply with requirements of notices etc.[^c-b2bc765b670ada74d267f488ecadc73d]

*Extent: S*

It is a defence for a person charged with an offence under regulation 37 or 38 to show that the person had a reasonable excuse for contravening or failing to comply with the prohibition or requirement in question.
