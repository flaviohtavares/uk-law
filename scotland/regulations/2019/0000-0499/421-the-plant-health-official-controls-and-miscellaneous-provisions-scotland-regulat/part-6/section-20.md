# The Plant Health (Official Controls and Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Regulations 2019, section 20

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 20. Other applications[^key-0ec509c7333cfb091599df7e6b89139e]

*Extent: S*

(1) The following applications must be made to the Scottish Ministers in the manner and form required by the Scottish Ministers—

- (a) an application for a temporary authorisation to permit a relevant activity for official testing, scientific or educational purposes, trials, varietal selection or breeding,
- (b) an application for an authorisation referred to in Article 64(2) of the [^key-65df1b64d275f8c4cf50ba250dfacfcd]... Plant Health Regulation,
- (c) an application for an authorisation referred to in Article 89(1) of the [^key-acb5a90d7c986950f61ab9de0a2ff272]... Plant Health Regulation,
- (d) an application for an authorisation referred to in Article 98(1) of the [^key-265359bd9c171596b3353a77bab848ea]... Plant Health Regulation,
- (e) an application for the issue of a phytosanitary certificate for export or a phytosanitary certificate for re-export[^key-e68cbacdf98849692010816a3c11dea3].

(2) In this regulation—

“**relevant activity**” means an activity which would otherwise be prohibited under the [^key-a53edb1ab8588367ecfa035b2c6c3536]... Plant Health Regulation or any other [^key-a53edb1ab8588367ecfa035b2c6c3536]... plant health rule involving—

- (a) the introduction of a plant pest or a plant, plant product or other object into Scotland,
- (b) the movement of a plant pest or a plant, plant product or other object within Scotland,
- (c) the holding of a controlled plant pest or a plant, plant product or other object at premises in Scotland,
- (d) the multiplication of a plant pest at premises in Scotland.
