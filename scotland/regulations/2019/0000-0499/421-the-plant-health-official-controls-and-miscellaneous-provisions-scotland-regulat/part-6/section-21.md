# The Plant Health (Official Controls and Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Regulations 2019, section 21

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 21. Authorisations for other purposes[^c-db9e9cd93829b8a15ab7128b1a0a96ce]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may grant an authorisation to permit—[^c-07acfb4fc0da79c57770f1e8e2d0b171]

- (a) the carrying out of any activity specified in a plant health derogation,
- (b) the introduction into Scotland, the movement within Scotland or the holding or multiplication in Scotland of a potential quarantine plant pest for official testing, scientific or educational purposes, trials, varietal selection or breeding, or
- (c) the carrying out of any other activity which requires the approval of the Scottish Ministers under , or by virtue of,[^c-0bd15d3042435f20c8915f37fa439ce7] the [^c-0b9c1562a6fafa782a10563e96f722b0]... Plant Health Regulation, the Official Controls Regulation , any regulations made under the Plant Health Regulation or the Official Controls Regulation[^c-1b0d7af5454b7007a2debfe8bf9e6a46] or these Regulations.

(2) An application for any such authorisation must be made to the Scottish Ministers in the manner and form required by the Scottish Ministers.

(3) In paragraph (1)—[^c-0b2ae4191b2a0e33457ce3d401a64657]

- “plant health derogation” means a derogation from provisions of the Plant Health Regulation which is set out in[^c-03b5c6bfd88e26f34448f2f254c4fc98] assimilated[^c-add5eeec72d84e8340b23e4410c658da] law or regulations made under the Plant Health Regulation or the Official Controls Regulation,
- “potential quarantine plant pest” means a plant pest which is not a GB quarantine pest or a provisional GB quarantine pest[^c-d5d2b265c53b61a6f70556a059ef8903], but which, in the opinion of the Scottish Ministers, fulfils the criteria set out in Subsection 1 of Section 3 of Annex 1 to that Regulation or may fulfil the criteria in Subsection 2 of that Section.
