## 19. Applications for registration[^c-bef7d185dea524a3ad44b4890897757a]

*Extent: S*

An application for registration pursuant to Article 66(1) of the [^c-2f8208d8905f196432ac3a8507ae3296]... Plant Health Regulation which is to be submitted to the Scottish Ministers must be submitted in the manner and form required by the Scottish Ministers.
