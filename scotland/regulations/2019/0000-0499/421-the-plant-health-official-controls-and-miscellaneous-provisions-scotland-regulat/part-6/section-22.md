# The Plant Health (Official Controls and Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Regulations 2019, section 22

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 22. Authorisations granted by the Scottish Ministers[^key-918263f95e186497f055def9fb51c78c]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-f4e1963647cde2126d51f47de4af51ad] An authorisation granted by the Scottish Ministers for the purposes of the [^key-d381437d4a949d86a62ed6d8153e25a0]... Plant Health Regulation , the Official Controls Regulation or for the purposes of, or under, these Regulations[^key-a7c69b7974f9cc5d87b21aab761e1bbc] must be in writing and may be granted—

- (a) subject to conditions,
- (b) for an indefinite period or a specified period.

(2) An authorisation granted by the Scottish Ministers may be modified, suspended or revoked at any time by the Scottish Ministers by notice in writing.
