## 34. Interpretation[^key-74a45b74aa93aed530acdbe6dcb0532d]

*Extent: S*

In this Part, “**premises**” includes any place, including land, building, vehicle, vessel, aircraft, hovercraft, freight container, railway wagon, trailer or movable building or structure.
