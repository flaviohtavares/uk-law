## 24. Notification requirements in relation to seed potatoes[^key-f5f8ef5ec941a773378cc728892c01b0]

*Extent: S*

(1) A professional operator who is bringing any of the following potatoes into Scotland must, at least two days before the expected date of their arrival in Scotland, provide written notification to a plant health inspector of the matters referred to in paragraph (2)—

- (a) seed potatoes grown outside Scotland, [^key-42b4d3a7d9c51c9a7cd8a0ddc1036ded]...
- (b)[^key-42b4d3a7d9c51c9a7cd8a0ddc1036ded] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The matters are—

- (a) the proposed time, date and means of landing,
- (b) the proposed place of landing,
- (c) their proposed destination and use,
- (d) their variety and quantity, and
- (e) the identification number of the producer of the potatoes or the reference number of the lot.
