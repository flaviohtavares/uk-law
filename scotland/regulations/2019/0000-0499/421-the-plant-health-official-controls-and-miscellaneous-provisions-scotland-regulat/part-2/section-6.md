# The Plant Health (Official Controls and Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Regulations 2019, section 6

> Text as at 2025-11-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 6. Disclosure of information[^key-bb522b719d0e0f2073d24332b0015cca]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Scottish Ministers may disclose information to any other competent authority in another part of the United Kingdom or to a CD authority[^key-918fa8d129c9e4cf6f68c2c7c441e0a8] for the purposes of the [^key-dcead9dc42e6b51f9576a42a632b1dc6]... Plant Health Regulation , the Windsor Framework (Plant Health) Regulations 2023[^key-4080822b00ea968778efdf97d26e7f5f] or the Official Controls Regulation.

(2) Nothing in paragraph (1) affects any other power or requirement of the Scottish Ministers to disclose information.
