# The Planning (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 2, Saving and Transitional Provisions) Amendment Regulations 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This S.S.I. has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.S.I. 2019/377](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2019/377) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of those instruments.

2019 No. 411

Town And Country Planning

The Planning (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 2, Saving and Transitional Provisions) Amendment Regulations 2019

Made

2nd December 2019

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

4th December 2019

Coming into force

19th December 2019

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 63(2) and (3) of the Planning (Scotland) Act 2019[^f00001].

## 1. Citation and commencement

These Regulations may be cited as the Planning (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 2, Saving and Transitional Provisions) Amendment Regulations 2019 and come into force on 19 December 2019.

## 2. Amendment of the Planning (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 2, Saving and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2019

(1) The Planning (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 2, Saving and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2019[^f00002] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In regulation 9 (enforcement of conditions – saving provision) for “planning contravention” substitute “breach of condition”.

KEVIN STEWART

St Andrew’s House,

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend regulation 9 of the Planning (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 2, Saving and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2019 so that it correctly refers to a breach of condition notice.
