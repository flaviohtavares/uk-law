# Act of Sederunt (Simple Procedure Amendment) (Civil Online) 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 122

Sheriff Court

Act of Sederunt (Simple Procedure Amendment) (Civil Online) 2019

Made

28th March 2019

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

29th March 2019

Coming into force

25th April 2019

In accordance with section 4 of the Scottish Civil Justice Council and Criminal Legal Assistance Act 2013[^f00001], the Court of Session has approved draft rules submitted to it by the Scottish Civil Justice Council with such modifications as it thinks appropriate.

The Court of Session therefore makes this Act of Sederunt under the powers conferred by section 104(1) of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014[^f00002] and all other powers enabling it to do so.

## 1. Citation and commencement, etc.

(1) This Act of Sederunt may be cited as the Act of Sederunt (Simple Procedure Amendment) (Civil Online) 2019.

(2) It comes into force on 25th April 2019.

(3) A certified copy is to be inserted in the Books of Sederunt.

## 2. Amendment of the Act of Sederunt (Simple Procedure) 2016

(1) The Act of Sederunt (Simple Procedure) 2016[^f00003] is amended in accordance with this paragraph.

(2) In schedule 2 (forms)—

- (a) in Form 4A (response form)[^f00004], in the introduction, after “There are guidance notes for each part of the form.”, insert—

  ;
- (b) in Form 6A (notice of claim)[^f00005], in Part B, after “(b) if you want to admit the claim and ask for time to pay, send a Time to Pay Application to the court.”, insert—

  .

CJM SUTHERLAND

Edinburgh

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Act of Sederunt)

This Act of Sederunt amends the Act of Sederunt (Simple Procedure) 2016 to include references to the Civil Online portal on the Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service website. From 25th April 2019, respondents will be able to complete a Response Form through Civil Online.
