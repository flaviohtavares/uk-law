# The Representation of the People Act 1983 Remedial (Scotland) Revocation Order 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 408

Representation Of The People

The Representation of the People Act 1983 Remedial (Scotland) Revocation Order 2019

Made

27th November 2019

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

2nd December 2019

Coming into force

27th January 2020

The Scottish Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 14(5) of the Convention Rights (Compliance) (Scotland) Act 2001[^f00001] (“the 2001 Act”) and all other powers enabling them to do so.

In accordance with section 14(4) of the 2001 Act, the Scottish Ministers have laid before the Scottish Parliament a statement summarising all the observations to which they had to have regard under section 14(3) of the 2001 Act in relation to the Representation of the People Act 1983 Remedial (Scotland) Order 2019[^f00002], and have specified that they consider it appropriate to revoke that Order.

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Representation of the People Act 1983 Remedial (Scotland) Revocation Order 2019 and comes into force on 27 January 2020.

## 2. Revocation

The Representation of the People Act 1983 Remedial (Scotland) Order 2019 is revoked.

MICHAEL RUSSELL

A member of the Scottish Government

St Andrew’s House,

Edinburgh

27th November 2019

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order revokes the Representation of the People Act 1983 Remedial (Scotland) Order 2019.
