# The Planning (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 314 (C. 15)

Town And Country Planning

The Planning (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2019

Made

3rd October 2019

Laid before the Scottish Parliament

7th October 2019

Coming into force

8th November 2019

The Scottish Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 63(2) and (3) of the Planning (Scotland) Act 2019[^f00001].

## 1. Citation, commencement and interpretation

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Planning (Scotland) Act 2019 (Commencement No. 1) Regulations 2019 and come into force on 8 November 2019.

(2) In these Regulations—

- “the Act” means the Planning (Scotland) Act 2019, and
- “the 1997 Act” means the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997[^f00002].

## 2. Appointed day

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the day appointed for the coming into force of the provisions of the Act specified in column 1 of the table in the schedule (the subject matter of which is described in column 2 of that table) is 8 November 2019.

(2) Where a purpose is specified in column 3 of that table in relation to any provision specified in column 1, that provision comes into force on 8 November 2019 only for that purpose.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations bring sections 1, 2 and 10 of the Planning (Scotland) Act 2019 (“the Act”) into force on 8 November 2019 for all purposes and sections 3, 5, 7, 11, 14 and 62 and paragraph 9 of schedule 2 of the Act into force on that date for the limited purposes of enabling regulations to be made and guidance to be issued, varied, published and revoked.

The Bill for the Act received Royal Assent on 25 July 2019. Sections 58 to 61, 63 and 64 came into force on the following day.
