# Removings Act 1693

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

Removings Act 1693

1693 c.40

Act anent the Term of Whitsunday[^c920064][^c920065][^c920066]

Our Soveraigne Lord and Lady The King and Queens Majesties with advice and consent of the Estates of Parliament . . . [^c920067] Statute and Declare that the Fifteenth day of May . . . [^c920067] shall be in all time comeing in place of the former Terme of Whitsunday to all effects whatsoever as well as to Removeings
