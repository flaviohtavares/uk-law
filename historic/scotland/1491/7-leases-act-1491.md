# Leases Act 1491

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

Leases Act 1491

1491 c.7

Of the tennentis of landis that . . .[^c919721] ar uthir wayis alterit[^c919722]

Item it is statut and ordinit that . . . [^c919723] quhen ony laidy haffand terce or coniunctfeftment happinis to decess or that landis be Redemit and lowsit be Reuersioune gift or selling . . . [^c919723] or ony vtheris wayis ony landis happinis to be alterit the tennentis lauboraris and Inhabitantis ony of the said landis sall Remane vnput furth or Removit quhill the nixt terme of witsonday folowand payand to the lord that sall entir to the saidis landis malez and dewiteis aucht and wont of the saidis landis quhilk broikin sall Induce na possessioune langare na the said witsonday
