# Common Good Act 1491

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

Common Good Act 1491

1491 c.19

Of the commoun gud of all burrowis.[^c919714][^c919715]

Item it is statut and ordinit that the commoune gud of all our souerane lordis burrowis within the realme be obseruit and kepit to the commoune gude of the toune and to be spendit in commoune And necessare thingis of the burght be the avise of the consale of the toune for the tyme and dekkynnis of craftis quhare thai ar . . . [^c919716]
