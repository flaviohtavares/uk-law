# Theft Act 1607

> Text as at 2006-11-15.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

Theft Act 1607

1607 c.6

Act Anent woddis parkis planting dowcattis et cetera[^c919858][^c919859]

Oure Souerane Lord and Estaittis of this present parliament . . . [^c919860] Statutis and Ordinis that quha-soeuir . . . [^c919860] Steillis Beis [^c-cff0d5520cee0ac889a8355d5c8bb5b0]... Shall be callit and convenit thairfoir as a braker of the Law . . . [^c919860] befoir . . . [^c919860] ony . . . [^c919860] ordinar magistrat within this realme . . . [^c919860] And the penaltie to be Imposit and takin of the Controvenaris befoir the saidis ordinar Inferiour Judges Auchtnocht to exceid the sowme of fourtie pundis of this Realme . . . [^c919860]
