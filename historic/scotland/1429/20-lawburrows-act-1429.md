# Lawburrows Act 1429

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

Lawburrows Act 1429

1429 c.20

Of soverte askit be ony of the kingis liegis that hes doute of his life[^c919692][^c919693]

Item It is statute ande ordanit that gif ony of the kingis liegis haf ony doute of his life outhir be dede or manance or violent presumpcioun ande he ask souerte of thaim that he doutis the schiref sal tak souerte of the party that the complante is maid apoun sa that the party playntife mak prufe of the dede or of manance or of the violent presumpcioun maid or done till hym . . . [^c919694]
