# Precedence Act 1661

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

Precedence Act 1661

1661 c. 211

Act anent the precedencie of the President of the Session the Lord Register the Lord Advocat . . .[^c919902][^c919903][^c919904]

The Kings Maiestie and Estates of Parliament . . . [^c919905] ffinds and Declares That the persons and Officers of State . . . [^c919905] shall in all tyme comeing Have their Precedencies in maner following To wit The President of the Session the first place The Clerk of Register the Second place The Advocat the thrid place . . . [^c919905] Lykas his Maiestie with advice of his Estates Doe heirby Decerne and Ordaine the persons and officers of State aforesaid and their Successours in these Offices To conforme themselffs in their Precedencies To this present Act As they will be ansuerable
