# Lawburrows Act 1597

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

Lawburrows Act 1597

1597 c.40

The panis of Law burroues Suld be payit . . .[^c9198521] The cautioner may be Perseuit or the Principall[^c9198531][^c9198541]

Oure Souerane Lord and estaitis of Parliament statutis and ordanis that the panis of contrauentioun salbe payit be the Principall pairtie That is chairgeit to find Lawborrouis albeit he . . . [^c9198551] find not cautioun And gif he find cautioun That baithe he as Principall and als his cautioner salbe subiect to the payment thairof at the optioun of the Perseuar as in all vthair pecuniall obligationis
