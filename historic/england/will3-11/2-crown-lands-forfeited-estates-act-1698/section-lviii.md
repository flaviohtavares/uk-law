# Crown Lands (Forfeited Estates) Act 1698, section LVIII

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## LVIII. Proviso for Grant for Children of Sir Charles Porter.

Provided, alsoe, That nothing in this Act shall make void a Grant to or in Trust for the Children of[^c000011] Sir Charles Porter, late Lord Chancellor of Ireland but that the same shall be and remaine as if this Act had never beene made.
