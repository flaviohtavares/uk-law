# Crown Lands (Forfeited Estates) Act 1698, section XCII

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## XCII. Proviso for the Hundred of West Barnfield, Kent, Tything of Northmore in Oxon, &c.

*Forest of Chute*.

Provided neverthelesse, That the Hundred of West Barnfield formerly rated and assessed in the Lath of Alesford, in the County of Kent, may for the future, if the Commissioners think fitt, be rated and assessed in the Lath of Scray[^c000016] in the County aforesaid; as likewise may the Tithing of Northemore in the County of Oxon, be assessed in the Hundred of Bampton, in the said County; and the Tythings of Charlbury, Faller, and Finstock, in the Hundred of Chadlington, in the said County; and alsoe the whole Towne and Parish of Leeds, in the County of Yorke, in the Hundred of Skyrack in the said County; and the Forest of Chute shall be assessed and pay where the same was assessed for the First Aid of Four Shillings in the Pound granted to His Majesty.
