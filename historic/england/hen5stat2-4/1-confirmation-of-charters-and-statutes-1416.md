Confirmation of Charters and Statutes (1416)

1416 c. 1

STATUTES OF THE SAME FOURTH YEAR. *A.D.* 1416.[^c919056]

## I. Charters and Statutes confirmed.

FIRST, That the Great Charter, . . . [^c919057], and all other Statutes and Ordinances made in his Time, and in the Times of his noble Progenitors Kings of England, and not repealed, shall be firmly holden and kept in all Points.
