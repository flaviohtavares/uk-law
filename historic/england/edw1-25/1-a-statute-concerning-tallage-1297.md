A Statute Concerning Tallage (1297)

1297 c. 1

[^c918897][^c918896]A STATUTE concerning TALLAGE.

## I. No Tallage or Aid without Assent of Parliament.

No Tallage or Aid shall be taken[^c918898] or levied by Us or our Heirs in our Realm, without the good will and Assent of ([^c918899]) Archbishops, Bishops, Earls, Barons, Knights, Burgesses, and other Freemen of the Land.[^c918900]
