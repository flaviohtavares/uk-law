A Statute for those who are born in Parts beyond Sea (1350)

1350 c. 0

[^c950552][^c950551]A STATUTE for those who are born in Parts beyond Sea. Of the Twenty-fifth Year of K. EDWARD, The Third.

Statute the First.

## The King’s Children declared inheritable in England, wheresoever born.

. . . [^c950553] the Law of the Crown of England is, and always hath been such, that the Children of the Kings of England, in whatsoever Parts they be born, in England or elsewhere, be able and ought to bear the Inheritance after the death of their Ancestors; which Law our . . . [^c950553] Lord the King, the . . . [^c950553] Prelates Earls, Barons, and other great Men, and all the Commons assembled in this Parliament, do approve and affirm for ever . . . [^c950556]
