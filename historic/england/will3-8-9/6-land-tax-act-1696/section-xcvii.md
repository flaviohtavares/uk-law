# Land Tax Act 1696, section XCVII

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## XCVII. Tallies to be delivered at the Exchequer for Monies paid in by Receivers.

And be it further enacted That as any of the said Bills shall be paid[^c000014] into the Exchequer by any of His Majesties Receivers aforesaid the Officers of the said Excheq\[uer\] shall cause Tallies to be levied & delivered to the said Receivers for their Discharge as amply & effectually to all Intents and Purposes as if they had paid Money in Specie And that such of the said Bills as shall be paid into the Receipt of the Excheq\[uer\] shall be immediately cancelled.
