# Land Tax Act 1696, section XCIV

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## XCIV.

*Treasury may make forth Bills of Credit for £1,500,000*.

And for the better and more speedy answering of the Supplies hereby granted or that shall or may be granted for the Service of the War for[^c000013] Yeare of our Lord One thousand six hundred ninety & seven Be it enacted by the Authority aforesaid That it shall and may be lawfull to and for the Co\[m\]missioners of His Majesties Treasury or any Three of them now being or for the High Treasurer or any Three or more of the Co\[m\]missioners of His Majesties Treasury for the time being to cause Bills to be made forth att the Receipt of the Exchequer in such Manner and Forme as they shall appoint for any Su\[m\]m or Su\[m\]ms of Money not exceeding One Million five hundred thousand Pounds by virtue of this Act and to issue the same from time to time to such Uses of the Warr and in such Proportions as they shall thinke fitt.
