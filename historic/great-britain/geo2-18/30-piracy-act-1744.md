# Piracy Act 1744

> This law has been repealed or revoked. Ended on 1993-11-05.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

Piracy Act 1744 [^c918406](repealed 5.11.1993)

1744 c.30

**No longer in force (ended 1993-11-05).**

## 1.] Subjects or denizens committing hostilities at sea, &c. against his Majesty’s subjects or giving aid, etc. to enemies at sea, may be tried as pirates;

All persons being the . . . [^c918414] subjects . . . [^c918414] of his Majesty, who, during the present or any future wars, have committed or shall commit any hostilities upon the sea, or in any haven, river, creek, or place where the admiral or admirals have power, authority, or jurisdiction against his Majesty’s subjects, by virtue or under colour of any commission from any of his Majesty’s enemies, or have been or shall be any other ways adherent or giving aid or comfort to his Majesty’s enemies upon the sea, or in any haven, river, creek, or place where the admiral or admirals have power, authority, or jurisdiction may be tried as pirates, felons, and robbers in the said Court of Admiralty, on ship-board or upon the land, in the same manner as persons guilty of piracy, felony, and robbery are by the said Act directed to be tried: . . . [^c918416]

## 2. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c918417]

## 3. Criminals not tried by this Act, may be tried for high treason, by 28 Hen. 8. c. 15.

Provided, that nothing in this Act contained shall be construed to extend to prevent any persons guilty of any of the said crimes, who shall not be tried according to this Act, from being tried for high treason within this realm, according to the Offences at Sea Act 1536.
