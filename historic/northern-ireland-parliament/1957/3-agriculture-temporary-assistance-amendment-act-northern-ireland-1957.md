## 4. Short title and citation.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Agriculture (Temporary Assistance ) (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland), 1957.

(2) This Act and the Agriculture (Temporary Assistance) Act (Northern Ireland), 1954, may be cited together as the Agriculture (Temporary Assistance) Acts (Northern Ireland), 1954 and 1957.
