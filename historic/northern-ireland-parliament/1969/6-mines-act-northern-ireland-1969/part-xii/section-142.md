> Text as at 2018-06-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 142. Restriction on institution of certain proceedings.

*Extent: N.I.*

No proceedings for an offence under \[[^c5453121] section 129(1) of\] this Act shall be instituted against any such person as is mentioned in section 133(1)( *a*) except by an inspector or by or with the consent of the Ministry or the Attorney General.
