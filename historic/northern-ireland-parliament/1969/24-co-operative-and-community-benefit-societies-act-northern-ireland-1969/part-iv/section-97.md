## 97. Regulations.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department[^key-ae5d01985c4b9290df7aa8c115036446] may make regulations—

- (a)[^key-2f867fed594f6b53add123cb39431dc5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^key-2f867fed594f6b53add123cb39431dc5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^key-2f867fed594f6b53add123cb39431dc5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^key-2f867fed594f6b53add123cb39431dc5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) prescribing anything which may be or is to be prescribed by the Department under this Act;

and generally for carrying this Act into effect.

(2) Any such regulations may impose reasonable fines on persons who contravene or fail to comply with any of those regulations; and any such fine shall be recoverable on the summary conviction of the offender.
