# Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act (Northern Ireland) 1969, section 21

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 21. Remedy for debts from members.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) All moneys payable to a registered society by a member thereof shall be a debt due from that member to the society and shall be recoverable as such in the county court for the district within which the society's registered office is situate or within which the member resides, at the option of the society.

(2) A registered society shall have a lien on the shares of any member for any debt due to the society by that member, and may set off any sum credited to the member on those shares in or towards the payment of that debt.
