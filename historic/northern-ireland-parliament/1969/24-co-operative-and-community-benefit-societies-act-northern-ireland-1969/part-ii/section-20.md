# Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act (Northern Ireland) 1969, section 20

## 20. Advances to members.

*Extent: N.I.*

Without prejudice to any provision included by virtue of section 11, the rules of a registered society may provide for advances of money to members—

- (a) on the security of real or personal property; or
- (b) if the society is registered to carry on banking business, in any manner customary in the conduct of such business.
