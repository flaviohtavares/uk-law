## 44. Remuneration of qualified auditors.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department may[^key-ca2ee02a23aa7eff7d62012c6e87ca9e] prescribe the maximum rates of remuneration to be paid by all registered societies or such registered societies as may be specified for the audit of their accounts and balance sheets by qualified auditors or for the making of a report for the purposes of section 37A(4)(a) or (5) or 43A(2)(a) or (b).[^c16934711].

(2) where a maximum rate of remuneration has been prescribed under subsection (1), no auditor or reporting accountant shall, in respect of his services to that society, ask for, receive or be entitled to receive remuneration in excess of the rate so prescribed.

(3) In this section, “reporting accountant” means a person appointed to make a report for the purposes of section 37A(4)(a) or (5) or 43A(2)(a) or (b).
