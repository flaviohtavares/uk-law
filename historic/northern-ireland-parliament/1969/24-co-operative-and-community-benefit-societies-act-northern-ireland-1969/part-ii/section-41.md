## 41. Meaning of qualified auditor[^key-b6e8bacf9ef24e8d70a9456edc32bc19]

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Act qualified auditor means a person eligible for appointment as a statutory auditor under Part 42 of the Companies Act 2006
