# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 1972, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-05-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/apni/1972/9

## 42. Councillors not to be appointed officers.[^key-7c457045359b996923e0ddf445662298][^key-050a4dd372948fcd9ff964617811bf4a][^key-069f0467d68c91ce0a7db0adb5bcfffc]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c16963111] Subject to subsection (2), a person shall, so long as he is and for twelve months after he has ceased to be, a councillor, be disqualified for being appointed by the council to any paid office which is prescribed under section 4(1)(a)[^key-a744a182834ccccc2ed6782a7a3e481d] .

(2)[^key-1d9440d0f33653b3f8442a79faac8d85] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In this section—

- “council” includes a committee or sub-committee of a council;
- “councillor” includes a member of such a committee or sub-committee, whether he is a member of the council or not.
