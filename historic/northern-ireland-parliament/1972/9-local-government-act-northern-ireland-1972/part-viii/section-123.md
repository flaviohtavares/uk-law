## 123. Penalty for destroying notices.[^c19071991]

*Extent: N.I.*

[^c16964581] A person who wilfully destroys, tampers with, pulls down, injures or defaces—

- (a) any board on or to which any bye-law, notice or other matter put up by the authority of the Ministry or of a council is inscribed or affixed; or
- (b) any advertisement, placard, bill or notice put up by or under the direction of a council;

shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale[^c16964591].
