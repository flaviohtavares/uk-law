# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 1972, section 126

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-05-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/apni/1972/9
>

## 126. Compensation for damage by council.[^c19072001]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A council shall make compensation to any person who has sustained damage, for which compensation is not payable under any other statutory provision, by reason of the exercise by the council of any of its functions in relation to a matter as to which he has not himself been in default.

(2) Subsections (2) to (9) of section 38 of the Mineral Development Act (Northern Ireland) 1969 [^m00017] shall have effect for the purposes of any claim for compensation under this section as if, in those subsections, any reference to that section, that Act or the Ministry of Commerce were a reference to, respectively, this section, this Act and the council.
