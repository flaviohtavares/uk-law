## SCHEDULE 1 — DECLARATIONS ON ACCEPTANCE OF OFFICE[^c-e5cbc61e7dccfdb92cffa65d7196d9e7]

*Extent: N.I.*

Section 7.

### PART I — DECLARATION OF COUNCILLOR

I  having been chosen Councillor for the District of  hereby declare that I take the said office upon myself and will duly and faithfully fulfil the duties thereof according to the best of my judgment and ability …

[^c16965121]I affirm that I have read and will[^c16965131] observe the Northern Ireland Local Government Code of Conduct for Councillors (as revised from time to time)[^c-b9183b30aa5d0c44b132f93eb68bee9c] in the performance of my functions as a councillor.

Dated this day of  Signed

*Part II rep. by 1973 NI 10*
