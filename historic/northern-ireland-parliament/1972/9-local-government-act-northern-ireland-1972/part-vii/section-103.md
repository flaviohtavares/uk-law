## 103. Taxation of costs.

*Extent: N.I.*

No costs incurred by a council in the promotion of, or opposition to, a Bill, being costs which are liable to be taxed under any statutory provision[^c16964031], shall be charged to the funds of the council unless they have been so taxed and allowed
