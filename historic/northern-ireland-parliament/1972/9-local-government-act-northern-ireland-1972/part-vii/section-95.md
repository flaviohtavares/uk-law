# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 1972, section 95

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-05-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/apni/1972/9
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 95. Provisions of offices, halls, etc.[^c20367551][^c20720531][^c-77950f26d1b8abbd38472c340162633b][^c-30994d9fce20941f8ef94c719d54d37b]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A council may provide and maintain offices, halls or other buildings to be used for the purpose of transacting the business of the council or for public meetings, assemblies or entertainments.

(2) A council may acquire land otherwise than by agreement for the purposes of this section.
