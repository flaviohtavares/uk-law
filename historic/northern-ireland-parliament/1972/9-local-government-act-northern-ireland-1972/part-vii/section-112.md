# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 1972, section 112

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-05-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/apni/1972/9

## 112. Insurance.[^c20367791][^c20720691][^key-442b385be19e16768c7419060707c51b][^key-ef88b18c3b41747288a7c0f8248b3b71]

*Extent: N.I.*

A council may—

- (a) insure any property in which it has an insurable interest;
- (b) insure against any contingency which may result in the imposition of any liability on or loss to the council.
