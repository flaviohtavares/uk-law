# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 1972, section 108

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 108. Contributions to other voluntary bodies.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c16964171] A council may contribute to the funds of any voluntary body which provides any public service in Northern Ireland.

(2) A council may contribute under subsection (1) towards the funds of a voluntary body in respect of publicity only where the publicity is incidental to the main purpose for which the contribution is given.
