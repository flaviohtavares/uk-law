## 115D. Sections 115A to 115C: supplementary provisions.[^c16963821]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Sections 115A to 115C apply to any publicity expressly or impliedly authorised by any statutory provision, including sections 107 to 110 and section 37 of the Local Government Finance Act (Northern Ireland) 2011[^c22293361].

(2) Nothing in sections 115A to 115C shall be construed as applying to anything done by a council in the discharge of its duties under sections 23 to 27.
