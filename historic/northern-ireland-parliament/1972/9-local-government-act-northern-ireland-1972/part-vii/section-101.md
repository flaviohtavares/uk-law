# Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 1972, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-05-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/apni/1972/9
>

## 101. Power to promote or oppose local or personal Bills.

*Extent: N.I.*

Subject to the provisions of this Act, where a council is satisfied that it is expedient to promote or oppose any local or personal Bill in Parliament, the council may promote or oppose the Bill and may defray the expenses incurred in so doing.
