*Extent: N.I.*

Indecent Advertisements (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1971

1971 Chapter 18

An Act to amend the Indecent Advertisements Act 1889.

\[8th June 1971\]

*S.1 amends s.5 of 1889 c.18*

## 2. Short title and citation.

This Act may be cited as the Indecent Advertisements (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1971, and this Act and the Indecent Advertisements Act 1889 [^m00001] may be cited together as the Indecent Advertisements Acts (Northern Ireland) 1889 and 1971.
