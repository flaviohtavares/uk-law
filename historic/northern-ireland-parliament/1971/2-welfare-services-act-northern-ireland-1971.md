# Welfare Services Act (Northern Ireland) 1971

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

Welfare Services Act (Northern Ireland) 1971

1971 Chapter 2

An Act to consolidate certain enactments relating to welfare services.

\[16th February 1971\]

*Part I (ss.1,2), Part II (ss.3‐12), Part III (ss.13‐21) rep. by 1972 NI 14*

## PART IV — GENERAL WELFARE MATTERS

*Ss.22‐24 rep. by 1972 NI 14*

### 25. Burial or cremation of the dead.

(1) It shall be the duty of every \[[^c5455111] district council\] ….[^c5455121] to cause to be buried or cremated the body of any person who has died or been found dead in its \[[^c5455111] district\], where it appears to the \[[^c5455111] district council\] that suitable arrangements for the disposal of the body have not been, or are not being, made otherwise than by the \[[^c5455111] district council\].

*Subs.(2) rep. by SRO (NI) 1973/211; subs.(3) rep. by 1972 NI 14*

(4) A \[[^c5455111] district council\] …. …[^c5455171] may recover from the estate of the deceased person, or from any person who, for the purposes of \[[^c5455111] the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 [^m00001]\], was liable to maintain the deceased person immediately before his death, expenses incurred under subsection (1)…[^c5455191][^c5455201]. . . .

(5)[^key-4e12351d38dcb4d93da5858953d05bc9][^key-6768eb5df4105dc84d43ef96efb7dc3d] Nothing in the foregoing provisions of this section shall affect any enactment regulating or authorising the burial, cremation or anatomical examination of the body of a deceased person; and a body shall not be cremated under this section where the \[[^c5455111] district council\] has reason to believe that cremation would be contrary to the wishes of the deceased.

*Ss.26‐29 rep. by 1972 NI 14*

*Part V (ss.30 — 35), Part VI (ss.36 — 44) rep. by 1972 NI 14*

## PART VII — SUPPLEMENTARY

*Ss.45‐47 rep. by 1972 NI 14*

### 48. Short title.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Welfare Services Act (Northern Ireland) 1971.
