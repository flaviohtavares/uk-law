New Towns (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1968

1968 Chapter 33

An Act to amend the New Towns Act (Northern Ireland) 1965 and for purposes connected with that matter.

\[12th December 1968\]

*S. 1(1) rep. in pt. by SRO (NI) 1973/285, residue with Schedule, effects amendments; subs. (2) rep. by SRO (NI) 1973/285*

*S. 2 inserts s. 32A in 1965 c. 13 (NI)*

*S. 3 rep. by SLR 1976*

## 4. Short title, construction and citation.

(1) This Act may be cited as the New Towns (Amendment) Act (Northern Ireland) 1968.

(2) This Act shall be construed as one with the New Towns Acts (Northern Ireland) 1965 and 1966, and this Act and those Acts may be cited together as the New Towns Acts (Northern Ireland) 1965 to 1968.
