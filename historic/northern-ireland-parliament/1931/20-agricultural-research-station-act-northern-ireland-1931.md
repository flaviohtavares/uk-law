# Agricultural Research Station Act (Northern Ireland) 1931

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

Agricultural Research Station Act (Northern Ireland) 1931(repealed)

1931 Chapter 20

[^c1903237]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## 1. Confirmation of agreement and exemption of agreement from stamp duty.

**No longer in force (ended 2006-04-01).**

(1) The scheduled agreement is hereby confirmed and shall have effect as if enacted in this Act.

(2) No stamp duty shall be charged on the scheduled agreement.

## 2. Short title, construction and citation.

**No longer in force (ended 2006-04-01).**

This Act may be cited as the Agricultural Research Station Act (Northern Ireland), 1931, and shall be construed as one with the Agricultural Research Station Act (Northern Ireland), 1927 [^c1648765], and that Act and this Act may be cited together as the Agricultural Research Station Acts (Northern Ireland), 1927 and 1931.
