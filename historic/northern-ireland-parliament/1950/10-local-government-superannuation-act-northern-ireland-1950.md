# Local Government (Superannuation) Act (Northern Ireland) 1950

> Text as at 2006-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

Local Government (Superannuation) Act (Northern Ireland) 1950

1950 Chapter 10

An Act to provide for the establishment of the Northern Ireland Local Government Officers' Superannuation Committee; to empower the Ministry of Health and Local Government to make provision, by regulations to be administered by the said Committee, for the grant of superannuation benefits to or in respect of officers of local authorities and certain other bodies; to repeal, with certain savings, the existing law relating to the superannuation of officers of local authorities; and for purposes connected with the matters aforesaid.

\[4th May 1950\]

*Ss. 1‐5A rep. by 1951 c. 9 (NI); 1960 c. 20 (NI); (with saving for existing rights) 1972 NI 10; SR 1981/96*

## 5B. Special provisions respecting certain servants of Belfast City Council.[^c543650]

For the purpose of calculating a superannuation allowance, whether granted before or after the passing of this Act, under section five of the Belfast Corporation Act (Northern Ireland), 1943 [^c1650798], as amended by section seven of the Belfast Corporation (General Powers) Act (Northern Ireland), 1948 [^c1650803], the \[[^c543651] council of the City of Belfast\] may at their discretion disregard wholly or partly any amount by which any such sum as is referred to in sub-paragraph (ii) of paragraph ( *b*) of sub-section (1) of the said section seven exceeds the sum of \[[^c543652] £1.30\].\]

*Ss. 6‐9 rep. by 1954 c. 33 (NI); (with saving for existing rights) 1972 NI 10; SR 1981/96*

## 10. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Local Government (Superannuation) Act (Northern Ireland), 1950.

*First, Second and Third Schedules rep. by 1972 NI 10; SR 1981/96*
