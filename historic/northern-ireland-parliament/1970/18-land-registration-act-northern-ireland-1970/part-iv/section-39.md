# Land Registration Act (Northern Ireland) 1970, section 39

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 39. Registration of certain burdens.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any of the matters specified in Part I of Schedule 6 may, subject to Part X of this Act, be entered as burdens on the title register[^c16955891].

(2) \[[^c16955911] Without prejudice to Article 8(a) of the Property (Northern Ireland) Order 1978\] the provisions of Part II of Schedule 6 shall apply in relation to the registration of Schedule 6 burdens.
