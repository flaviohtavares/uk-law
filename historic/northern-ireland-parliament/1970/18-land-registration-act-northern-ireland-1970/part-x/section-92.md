# Land Registration Act (Northern Ireland) 1970, section 92

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 92. Application of certain other provisions of the Act to statutory charges.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Land Registry Rules may, in relation to the Statutory Charges Register and the registration of any statutory charge, apply, with such modifications as may be specified therein, any of the following provisions of this Act, namely—

- (a) Part I;
- (b) sections 8, 62, 63, and 71[^c16957901];
- (c) Schedules 1 and 9.

(2) The provisions of sections 6 and 7 shall apply to any decision of the Registrar made under section 91.

(3) Except as provided by this section, the provisions of this Act, other than of sections 82, 83, 84, 85, 85A and 86 to 99 and of Schedules 12 to 14, shall not apply in relation to the Statutory Charges Register or to the registration of any statutory charge.

(4) References in this Act, and in any statutory provision amended by this Act, to this Part include references to Schedule 11.
