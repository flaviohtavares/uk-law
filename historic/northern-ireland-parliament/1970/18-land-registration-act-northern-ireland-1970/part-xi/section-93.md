# Land Registration Act (Northern Ireland) 1970, section 93

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 93. Financial provisions.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c16957941] Subject to subsection (2), such salaries and remuneration of officials and other staff appointed to the Land Registry, and such other expenses incurred by, through or on behalf of the Land Registry in the operation of this Act, as the Ministry may, approve may be defrayed out of moneys provided by Parliament.

*Subs.(2) rep. by 1992 NI 7*
