# Electoral Law Act (Northern Ireland) 1962, section 101

> Text as at 2024-05-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 101. Service of notices, etc.

*Extent: N.I.*

Any summons, notice or other document required by any provision of this Part to be served on any person for the purposes of any proceeding in the High Court or an election court, may be served—

- (a) in any such manner as is referred to in section twenty-four of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland), 1954 [^m00006]; or
- (b) in such other manner as the court having seizin of the proceeding may direct.
