> Text as at 2024-05-07.

## 128. Financial provisions.

*Extent: N.I.*

All expenses incurred by the Ministry and by the Ministry of Finance[^c18659591] for the purposes of this Act and not otherwise provided for under this Act shall be defrayed out of moneys provided by \[[^c18659601] the Parliament of the United Kingdom\].
