# Electoral Law Act (Northern Ireland) 1962, section 122

> Text as at 2024-05-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 122. Effect of incapacities with respect to the Parliament of the United Kingdom.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) So long as any person is subject, by virtue of the Representation of the People Acts, to any incapacity with respect to the Parliament of the United Kingdom, he shall be subject to the like incapacity with respect to the \[[^c18659571] Assembly.\]

(2) Where by reason of anything done in reference to an election a person is subject to an incapacity with respect to the Parliament of the United Kingdom and the incapacity is limited to a particular constituency or constituencies then the like incapacities imposed by this section with respect to the \[[^c18659571] Assembly\] shall be limited to any constituency which is included in whole or in part in the area for which the said election was held.
