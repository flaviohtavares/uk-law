## 14B. Travelling and subsistence allowances.[^c18656931]

*Extent: N.I.*

There may be paid,[^c18656941]. . . , to the Chief Electoral Officer and to persons appointed, or to whom functions are delegated, under section 14A travelling and subsistence allowances at such rates as the Secretary of State, with the approval of the Treasury, may determine.\]
