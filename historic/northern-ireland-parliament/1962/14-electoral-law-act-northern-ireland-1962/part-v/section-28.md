# Electoral Law Act (Northern Ireland) 1962, section 28

> Text as at 2024-05-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 28. Preparation and publication of registers.

*Extent: N.I.*

*Subs. (1)‐(6) rep. by 1989 c. 3*

(7) …[^c18657211] for the purpose only of determining whether a member of a local authority is qualified to continue to be such member for the remainder of his term of office by virtue of his being a local elector, the register in force \[[^c18657221] at the time of his becoming such a member\] shall continue in force until the election day for that local authority in the next local election year.
