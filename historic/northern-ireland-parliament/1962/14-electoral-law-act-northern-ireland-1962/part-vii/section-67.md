# Electoral Law Act (Northern Ireland) 1962, section 67

> Text as at 2024-05-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 67. Voting by proxy.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) [^c18658551] \[[^c18658561] An\] elector to whom this section applies may, subject to sub-section (2), appoint a person to vote on his behalf at any election initiated during the period for which under the Seventh Schedule the appointment continues in force.

(2) [^c18658551] This section applies to—

- (a) any elector registered by virtue of a service qualification;
- (b) any other \[[^c18658581] parliamentary\] elector who satisfies the electoral officer,…[^c18658591], that in any six consecutive months he will or is likely to be at sea or out of the United Kingdom for a period or two or more periods together exceeding four weeks.

(3) An elector registered by virtue of a service qualification may not vote otherwise than in person or by proxy.

(4) [^c18658551] The provisions of the Seventh Schedule shall have effect in relation to the appointment and voting of proxies.

(5)[^c18658611] This section and Schedule 7 shall not apply at a local election.
