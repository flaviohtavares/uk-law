# Electoral Law Act (Northern Ireland) 1962, section 116

> Text as at 2024-05-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 116. Evidence by certificate of holding of elections.

*Extent: N.I.*

On any prosecution for an electoral misdemeanour and on any proceedings for a penalty under section forty-eight the certificate of the returning officer at an election that the election mentioned in the certificate was duly held and that the person named in the certificate was a candidate at the election shall be sufficient evidence of the facts therein stated.
