# Electoral Law Act (Northern Ireland) 1962, section 109

> Text as at 2024-05-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 109. Penalty for illegal practices.

*Extent: N.I.*

A person guilty of an illegal practice shall be liable to a fine not exceeding one hundred pounds. In respect of an illegal practice at a local election this section shall have effect as if for the words one hundred pounds there were substituted level 5 on the standard scale.[^c18659251]
