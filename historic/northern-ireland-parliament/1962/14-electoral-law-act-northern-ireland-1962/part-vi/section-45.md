# Electoral Law Act (Northern Ireland) 1962, section 45

> Text as at 2024-05-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 45. Claim by election agent at an election.

*Extent: N.I.*

So far as circumstances admit, this Act shall apply to a claim for his remuneration by an election agent at \[[^c18657801] an election\] and to the payment thereof in like manner as if he were any other creditor, and if any difference arises respecting the amount of the claim, the claim shall be a disputed claim within the meaning of this Act and be dealt with accordingly.
