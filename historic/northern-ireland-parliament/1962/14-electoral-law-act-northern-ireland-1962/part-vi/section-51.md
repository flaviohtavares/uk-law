# Electoral Law Act (Northern Ireland) 1962, section 51

> Text as at 2024-05-07.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 51. Publication of summary of return as to election expenses at elections.

*Extent: N.I.*

At \[[^c18657951] an election\] the returning officer shall, within ten days after he receives from the election agent of a candidate a return as to election expenses, publish in two or more newspapers circulating in the constituency for which the election was held, a summary of the return accompanied by a notice of the time and place at which the return and declarations (including the accompanying documents) can be inspected.
