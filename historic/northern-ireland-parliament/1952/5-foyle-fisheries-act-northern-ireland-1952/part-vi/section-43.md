## 43. Fishing for salmon or trout in a river by cross lines.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c20334741] If any person takes, catches or fishes for any salmon or trout by means of cross lines in any river, he shall be guilty of an offence against this Act.

(2)[^c20334871] In subsection (1) “**cross lines**” means fishing lines—

- (a) reaching from one position to another across water and fixed or held at each of their ends; and
- (b) having attached to them one or more baited hooks or lures, artificial or otherwise.
