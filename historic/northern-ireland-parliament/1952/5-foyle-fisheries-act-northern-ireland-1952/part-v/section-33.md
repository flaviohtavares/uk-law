# Foyle Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1952, section 33

## 33. Use of nets during close season or close time.

*Extent: N.I.*

If any person does any of the following things during the annual close season for salmon or trout or weekly close time for salmon or trout[^c20329861]—

- (a) places, affixes or attaches any net to any stake, bridge, sluice or lock gates of a canal or other such fixed erections, or
- (b) lays, draws or fishes with any net whatsoever,

he shall be guilty of an offence against this Act.
