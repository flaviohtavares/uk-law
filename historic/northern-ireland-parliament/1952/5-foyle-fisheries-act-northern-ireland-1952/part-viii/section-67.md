## 67. Application of penal provisions of Act.

*Extent: N.I.*

The provisions of this Act relating to offences apply to—

- (a) acts and omissions in the Londonderry Area or the Newry Area[^c11537151] by any person, and
- (b) acts and omissions in any part of the Foyle Area or the Carlingford Area by a person resident in Northern Ireland.
