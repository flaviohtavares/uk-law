# Foyle Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1952

Foyle Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1952

1952 Chapter 5

[^c11536081][^M_C_01d5e15b-9128-40dd-a391-08f994374f14]An Act to authorise the making of a certain agreement with respect to fishing rights in the tidal waters of the Lough and River Foyle and its tributaries, to provide for the management, conservation, protection and improvement of the fisheries in the Foyle Area, to establish a Foyle Fisheries Commission and to define its functions, to dissolve the board of conservators for the Londonderry district, and to provide for other matters (including the charging of fees on certain fishing licences) connected with the matters aforesaid.

\[25th March 1952\]
