## 6. Deposit of copies of orders and regulations.

*Extent: N.I.*

A copy of every order or regulation made under this Act shall be deposited—

- (a)[^c-598c9233c8fa8c429b320cffbc83e254] with the chief clerk and clerk of petty sessions; and
- (c) in every Royal Ulster Constabulary Barracks situate in the Londonderry Area or the Newry Area[^c11536251].
