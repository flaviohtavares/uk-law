### (not yet in force: Notice of appeal)

**(not yet in force: 1).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: A notice of appeal shall—)*

- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: state—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: the name and address of the appellant;)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: the name and address of the appellant's legal representative, if appropriate; and)*
  - ((not yet in force: iii)) *(not yet in force: an address for service;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: contain—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: a brief statement of the facts;)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: a summary of the principal grounds for contesting the decision of the Commission and the arguments supporting those grounds;)*

  - ((not yet in force: iv)) *(not yet in force: a schedule listing all the documents annexed to the notice of appeal; and)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: be accompanied by such fee (if any) as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: If the appellant wishes to request confidential treatment for any part of his appeal, he shall indicate in the notice of appeal, or within 14 days of sending it to the Appeals Board, the relevant passages or documents, together with the reasons for the request, and, if so directed by the Appeals Board, supply a non-confidential version of the notice of appeal.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: There shall be annexed to the notice of appeal a copy of every document on which the appellant intends to rely.)*

**(not yet in force: 2).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: If the Appeals Board considers that the notice of appeal does not comply with paragraph 1, or is incomplete, or is lacking in clarity, the Appeals Board may give such directions as may be necessary to ensure that the notice of appeal is put in order and dealt with justly.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The Appeals Board may, if satisfied that the efficient conduct of the proceedings so require, defer service of the notice of appeal on the Commission until after the directions referred to in sub-paragraph (1) have been complied with.)*

**(not yet in force: 3).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The Appeals Board may strike out a notice of appeal at any stage in the proceedings if—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: having considered the grounds of appeal set out in the notice of appeal—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: it considers that the notice of appeal discloses no valid ground of appeal; or)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: it is of the opinion that the appeal is vexatious, frivolous or without substance or foundation; or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the notice of appeal does not comply with paragraph 1 in a substantial respect, and the appellant has not remedied the defect pursuant to a direction under paragraph 2(1); or)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: the appellant fails to comply with a direction of the Appeals Board.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Where the Appeals Board strikes out a notice of appeal it may make any consequential order it considers appropriate.)*

**(not yet in force: 4).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The appellant may amend the notice of appeal only with the permission of the Appeals Board.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Where the Appeals Board grants permission under sub-paragraph (1) it may do so on such terms as it thinks fit, and shall give such further or consequential directions as may be necessary.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The Appeals Board shall not grant permission to amend the notice of appeal in order to add a new ground for contesting the decision unless—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: such ground is based on matters which have come to light since the notice of appeal was sent to the Appeals Board; or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: it was not practicable to include that ground in the notice of appeal; or)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: the circumstances are, in the opinion of the Appeals Board, exceptional.)*

### (not yet in force: Response to notice of appeal)

**(not yet in force: 7).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The Commission shall send a response to the Appeals Board in the form required by this paragraph so that the response is received within six weeks, or such further time as the Appeals Board may allow, of the date on which the Commission received a copy of the notice of appeal sent in accordance with paragraph 6(b).)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The response shall contain—)*

- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: the relief sought by the Commission and any directions sought pursuant to paragraph 14; and)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: a copy of the documents on which the Commission intends to rely and a schedule listing those documents.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: On receiving the response, the Appeals Board shall send a copy of the response to the appellant.)*

**(not yet in force: 8).** *(not yet in force: If the Commission wishes to request confidential treatment for any part of its response, it shall indicate in the response, or within 14 days of sending it to the Appeals Board, the relevant passages or documents, together with the reasons for the request, and, if so directed by the Appeals Board, supply a non-confidential version of the response.)*

**(not yet in force: 10).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The Appeals Board may, after hearing the parties, strike out the response at any stage in the proceedings if—)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: When the Appeals Board strikes out a response it may make any consequential order it considers appropriate.)*

**(not yet in force: 11).** ((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The Commission may amend the response only with the permission of the Appeals Board.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Where the Appeals Board grants permission under sub-paragraph (1) it may do so on such terms as it thinks fit, and shall give such further or consequential directions as may be necessary.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The Appeals Board shall not grant permission to amend the response in order to add a new ground for contesting the decision unless—)*
