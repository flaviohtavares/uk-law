## 56. Limitations on exercise of powers.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The powers conferred by this Part on a private river watcher shall be exercised only for the protection of the fisheries specified in his instrument of appointment.

(2) The powers conferred by this Part on a member or officer of the Commission shall be exercised only for the protection of the fisheries of the Foyle Area or the Carlingford Area[^c11537001].
