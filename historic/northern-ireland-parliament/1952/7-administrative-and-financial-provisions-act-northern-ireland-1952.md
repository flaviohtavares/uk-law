## 10. Interpretation.

(1) In this Act the expression “enactment” includes any provision in any Act (whether public general, local or private) of the Parliament of Northern Ireland or of the Parliament of the United Kingdom and a provision in any Order in Council, order, regulation, rule, bye-law, scheme or other instrument made under any such Act.

*Subs. (2)(3) rep. by 1954 c. 33 (NI)*

*S. 11, with Schedule, effects repeals*

## 12. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Administrative and Financial Provisons Act (Northern Ireland), 1952.
