# Roads Act (Northern Ireland) 1948

> Text as at 2006-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

Roads Act (Northern Ireland) 1948 [^c543645]

1948 Chapter 28

An Act to provide that the Ministry of Commerce shall be the road authority for such of the principal roads in Northern Ireland as may for the time being constitute the main system of routes for through traffic therein; to facilitate road traffic and prevent danger to persons using roads; to make certain amendments in the law relating to roads; and for purposes connected with the matters aforesaid.

\[23rd December 1948\]

*The Act has been repealed subject to savings for s.17 (abandonment, etc., of roads), s.34 (acquisition of land, etc.), [^c543646]s.41 (Crown rights), [^c543647]sch.5 (provisions as to compulsory acquisition) and sch.6 (provisions as to inquiries) as applied by any other statutory provision.*
