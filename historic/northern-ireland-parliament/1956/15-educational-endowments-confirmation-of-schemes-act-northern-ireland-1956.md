# Educational Endowments (Confirmation of Schemes) Act (Northern Ireland) 1956

> Text as at 2015-06-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

Educational Endowments (Confirmation of Schemes) Act (Northern Ireland) 1956 [^c5441711]

1956 Chapter 15

An Act to confirm, with modifications, certain educational endowments schemes; and for purposes connected therewith.

\[4th December 1956\][^key-3c8b25035fea4ec0e3626868e55ed13f]

## 1. Retrospective confirmation and enactment of certain educational endowment schemes.

The schemes named in the first column of the Schedule and approved by the Court Orders referred to in the second column of the Schedule shall, with the modifications thereof specified in the third column of the Schedule, respectively have effect and be deemed always to have had effect as if enacted by Parliament on the date of the respective Court Orders.

## 2. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Educational Endowments (Confirmation of Schemes) Act (Northern Ireland), 1956.
