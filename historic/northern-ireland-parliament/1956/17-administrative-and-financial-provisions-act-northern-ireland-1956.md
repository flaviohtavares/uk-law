# Administrative and Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 1956

> Text as at 2006-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

Administrative and Financial Provisions Act (Northern Ireland) 1956

1956 Chapter 17

An Act to make certain amendments in the law relating to holdings subject to land purchase annuity; to amend the law with respect to the duration and cost of firearm certificates and permits; to make further provision with respect to the salary of the Comptroller and Auditor-General; to make further provision with respect to public accounts and the receipt, issue, borrowing and funding of public moneys; to provide for the variation of the rate of interest payable on certain compensation moneys; and otherwise to amend the law with respect to certain administrative and financial matters and with respect to certain functions of the Ministry of Finance.

\[4th December 1956\]

## 1. Amendment of certain provisions of the Land Purchase Acts as to the creation of certain tenancies.[^c544172]

Notwithstanding anything contained in the following enactments—

*Paras. (a)‐(d) rep. by 1997 NI 8*

- (e) sub-section (1) of section fifty-four of the Irish Land Act, 1903 [^c1652278]; and
- (f) sub-section (1) of section thirty of the Northern Ireland Land Act, 1925 [^c1652281];

it shall not be necessary to obtain the consent of the Ministry of Finance to the creation of any tenancy for a term of thirty-one years or less of a holding or part of a holding to which those enactments apply and, accordingly, those enactments shall not apply to any such tenancy created after the passing of this Act.

## 14. Amendment as to rate of interest on compensation moneys for land compulsorily acquired under certain enactments.

(1) [^c544173] The rate of interest payable on compensation money in respect of land compulsorily acquired under any of the following enactments, that is to say:—

- …
- [^c544174]section thirty-four of the Roads Act (Northern Ireland), 1948 [^c1652269];
- …
- [^c544175]shall be such rate as may from time to time be determined by the Ministry of Finance, and accordingly—

  *Para.(a) with First Schedule, effects amendments; para.(b) rep. by 1966 c.36 (NI)*
- *Subs. (2) rep. by SLR 1980*

(3) A determination of the rate of interest by the Ministry of Finance under sub-section (1) or under section twelve of the Public Health and Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act (Northern Ireland), 1955 [^c1652272], shall be made by order which shall be subject to negative resolution.
