# Statute Law Revision Act (Northern Ireland) 1953

> Text as at 2006-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

Statute Law Revision Act (Northern Ireland) 1953

1953 Chapter 1

An Act to repeal certain enactments which have ceased to be in force or have become unnecessary, to amend the short title of 2 Anne c.5 (Ir.), and for purposes connected with the matters aforesaid.

\[17th February 1953\]

*S. 1, with Schedule, effects repeals with savings*

*S. 2 amends sch. to 1951 c. 1 (NI)*

## 3. Short title and citation.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Statute Law Revision Act (Northern Ireland), 1953.

(2) The Statute Law Revision Act (Northern Ireland), 1952 [^m00001], and this Act may be cited together as the Statute Law Revision Acts (Northern Ireland), 1952 and 1953.
