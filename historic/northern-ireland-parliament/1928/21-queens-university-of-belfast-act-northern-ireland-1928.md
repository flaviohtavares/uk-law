# Queen's University of Belfast Act (Northern Ireland) 1928

> Text as at 2006-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

Queen's University of Belfast Act (Northern Ireland) 1928

1928 Chapter 21

An Act to make provision for the payment of additional grants to the Queen's University of Belfast, and for defraying certain expenses of the Agricultural Faculty of the said University, and to empower the governing body of the said University to borrow money.

\[18th December 1928\]

*This Act has been repealed subject to a saving for section 2 (Expenses of Agricultural Faculty in teaching and research) (see 2004 NI 23)*
