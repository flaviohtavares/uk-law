## 189. Service of documents.

*Extent: N.I.*

In addition to the methods of service permitted by section 24 of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 [^m00014], any document which is required or permitted under this Act to be served on any person, being the \[[^c16998071] master, charterer or owner\] of or a person employed on a boat, may be served on that person by being left at or on board that boat.
