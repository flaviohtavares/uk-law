## 207. Extension to hand lines of provisions relating to angling.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to subsection (2), the provisions of this Act relating to angling shall extend to fishing by hand line, and accordingly any reference to a rod and line shall be construed as including a reference to a hand line.

(2) The provisions to which subsection (1) applies shall not include any provision of Part III …[^c16998871].
