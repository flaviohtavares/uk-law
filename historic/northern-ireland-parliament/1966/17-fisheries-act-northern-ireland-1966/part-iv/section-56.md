# Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966, section 56

## 56. Restrictions on the use of fishing engines and rods and lines at or in the vicinity of dams.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) No person, other than a person to whom subsection (2) applies, shall—

- [(a)][^c16994651] use any fishing engine (other than a rod and line) from any position on a dam or[^c16994661] at any place within 200 metres[^c16994681] above or below any dam; \[[^c16994651] or
- (b) use a rod and line from any position on or within 5 metres or such other distance as may be prescribed by regulations[^c21337121] above or below any dam\].

(2) Subsection (1) shall not apply to any person who is for the time being the owner of a several fishery in any place referred to in that subsection, and who is a successor in title of any other person who had exercised in that place the right of taking fish during the period of twenty years immediately preceding 14th August 1850 \[[^c16994651]; and paragraph ( *b*) of that subsection shall not apply in any case or class of case in which it is excluded (either absolutely or upon the observance of prescribed conditions) by regulations.\]

(3) If any person contravenes subsection (1) he shall be guilty of an offence, …[^c16994761].
