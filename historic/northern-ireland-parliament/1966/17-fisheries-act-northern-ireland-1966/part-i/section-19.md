## 19. Regulations.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Ministry may make regulations prescribing anything which, under this Act, may be prescribed by regulations, and providing for any matter in respect of which regulations may be made under this Act.

(2) Regulations made under this Act shall be subject to negative resolution.
