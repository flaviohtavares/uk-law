## 11B. Discharge of effluent.[^c19202811][^c16993501]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where—

- (a) an application is made to the Department [^c-c90eaaddc14d5dafeabe2c2589dccb1b]... under paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the Water (Northern Ireland) Order 1999 ( “the 1999 Order”)[^c16993511], and
- (b) in the opinion of the Department[^c-4f878d7c782d692a5016c0f589b06741] , the purpose of the application is to obtain its consent to the discharge into a waterway of effluent or matter in connection with the operation of a fish farm,

the Department may[^c-093e288fec52913fbb23f7c0d477a2cf] make a direction—

- (i) that a local public inquiry to be held under section 11A(3) shall also consider any objection (other than one which is withdrawn or is, in the opinion of the Department [^c-ecf252d51018ea8f442d878b85bf1577]..., vexatious, frivolous or insubstantial) received in response to the advertisment of the application under paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to 1999 Order, [^c-7e0c08a0b46d7ff28fc9ffd952b0cee2]...
- (ii)[^c-ab33a57ea0f2f5c2532dff966f9e0117] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The Department [^c-b83242196b49bd96e03b4f1180d86873]... shall give notice of any direction made under subsection (1) to—

- (a) the person making the application;
- (b)[^c16993551] the Foyle, Carlingford and Irish Lights Commission, where the discharge is to be made in the Londonderry Area or the Newry area;
- (c) [^c21314681]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

and (without prejudice to section 23 of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954) that person and the Foyle Fisheries Commission [^c21314691]. . . shall be entitled to appear and be heard at the inquiry.

(3) Article 13(1) of the 1999 Order shall not apply to such an application where the Department [^c-4246c82f80e51fe6628cf0da5259fd54]... makes a direction under subsection (1) of this section.

(4) Paragraph 2(2) of Schedule 1 to the 1999 Order shall not apply to any application under paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to that Order for the purpose mentioned in paragraph (b) of subsection (1) of this section.

*Subs. (5) rep. by 1999 NI 6*
