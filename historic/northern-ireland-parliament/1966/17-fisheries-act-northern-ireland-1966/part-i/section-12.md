## 12. Power of Ministry to conduct or assist research.

*Extent: N.I.*

The Ministry may, with the approval of the Ministry of Finance, conduct or promote, or assist (by grants or otherwise) any person in conducting, research or investigations into any matter relating to fish or any fishery.
