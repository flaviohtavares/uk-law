## 7A. Permits.[^c22559861]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where the Department owns the fishing rights in any waters—

- (a) the Department may issue permits, or make arrangements for their issue through agents, for periods not exceeding one year, authorising the holder of such a permit to use a fishing engine in the waters to which the permit relates; and
- (b) may, subject to subsection (2), charge for the issue of such permits such reasonable sums as the Department may determine[^c16993421].

(2) The Department may under subsection (1)(a) issue complimentary permits to persons not ordinarily resident in Northern Ireland and nothing in this Act shall require such persons to make any payment in respect of such permits.

(3) Where permits are issued under subsection (1) in relation to any waters, any person who, without obtaining such a permit, uses a fishing engine in those waters, shall be guilty of an offence.
