## 97. Weekly close time.

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Act the expression “the weekly close time” means, in relation to any locality, the period prescribed by regulations[^c21337261] as such for that locality, or if no period is prescribed, the period commencing at 6 o'clock a.m. on each Saturday and ending at 6 o'clock a.m. on the then next-following Monday.
