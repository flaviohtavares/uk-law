> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/apni/1966/17

## 44. Complimentary licences.

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department[^c21335461] may [^c21309391]. . . authorise the issue to a person not ordinarily resident in Northern Ireland of a complimentary licence to use a rod and line and, where it[^c21335491] do so, nothing in this Act or in any regulations[^c21335481] made by the Department thereunder shall require that person to pay any licence duty in respect of that licence.
