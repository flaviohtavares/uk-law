# Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966, section 166

## 166. Definitions for purposes of Part XI.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In this Part—

- “authorised person” means (except where otherwise provided)[^c16996751] a person being—
  - (a) a member of the Royal Ulster Constabulary, or
  - (b) an officer appointed by the Ministry, or
  - (c) [^c21309821]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (d) a private water bailiff;
- [^c21309831]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- *Definition rep. by 1981 NI 7*

(2) In sections 174 to 177, 179, 184 and 185 the expression “authorised person” includes—

- (a) any British sea-fishery officer, [^c16996791]…

*Para.(b) rep. by 1981 NI 7*

(3) In subsection (2) “British sea-fishery officer” means \[[^c16996801] any person who is such an officer for the purposes of the Sea Fisheries Acts (as defined by section 19(1) of the Sea Fisheries Act 1968 [^m00012]).\]
