# Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966, section 168

## 168. Restriction on powers of officers of the Department .[^c21337611]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c20327221] Subject to subsection (2), an officer of the Department[^c21337641] shall not enter upon any lands or waters for the purpose of enforcing the fishing rights of any owner or occupier thereof, or of any person in whom fishing rights are vested.

(2) Subsection (1) shall not apply in any case where the fishing rights in question are rights of the Ministry or rights of an owner or occupier of land or waters with whom[^c16996851] the Department has entered into an agreement under section 25(2)(c)[^c21309841].
