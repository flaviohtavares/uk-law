## 119. Temporary continuance of licence on death.

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the holder of a dealer's licence dies, the licence shall, unless forfeited under section 200, enure for the benefit of his personal representative or his surviving spouse or civil partner[^c16996161] or any other member of his family until the expiration of two months from his death or such longer period as the Department[^c21337521] may allow.
