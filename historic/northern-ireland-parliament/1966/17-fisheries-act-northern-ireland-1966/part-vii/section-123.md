## 123. Penalties for offences under Part VII.

*Extent: N.I.*

Any person guilty of an offence under any provision of this Part (other than \[[^c16996291] section 110(6)\]) shall be liable—

- [(a)[^c16996301] on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding £500 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to both such fine and such imprisonment;
- (b) (except for an offence under section 120 or 122) on conviction on indictment to an unlimited fine[^c16996311] or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or to both such fine and such imprisonment.\]
