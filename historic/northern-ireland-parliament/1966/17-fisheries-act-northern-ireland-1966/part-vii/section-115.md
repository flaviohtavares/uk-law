# Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966, section 115

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/apni/1966/17
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 115. Renewal of licence.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where the holder of a dealer's licence applies within the time before its expiration prescribed by the Department[^c21337441] , or where a person who was the holder of a dealer's licence and is not for the time being disqualified under section 200 for holding such a licence applies within the time so prescribed after its expiration, and the application—

- (a) is made in writing in the prescribed form; and
- (b) is accompanied by a fee of such amount[^c16996141] as may be prescribed by regulations[^c21337461];

the Department may renew that licence.

(2) Every dealer's licence renewed under this section shall (unless previously terminated) continue in force until the expiration of the calendar year for which the licence is renewed, and shall then expire.
