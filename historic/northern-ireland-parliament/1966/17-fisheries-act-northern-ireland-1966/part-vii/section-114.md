# Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966, section 114

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/apni/1966/17
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 114. Grant of licence.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a person who is not for the time being disqualified under section 200 for holding a dealer's licence makes application in writing to the Department[^c21337401] in the form prescribed by the Department and forwards therewith—

- (a) a certificate signed by a justice of the peace that that person is a fit and proper person to receive a licence; and
- (b) a fee of such amount[^c16996121] as may be prescribed by regulations[^c21337431];

the Department may grant a dealer's licence to that person.

(2) A dealer's licence shall (unless previously terminated) continue in force until 31st December of the year in which it is granted and shall then expire.
