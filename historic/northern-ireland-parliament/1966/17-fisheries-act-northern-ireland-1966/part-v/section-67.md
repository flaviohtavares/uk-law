# Fisheries Act (Northern Ireland) 1966, section 67

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/apni/1966/17
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 67. Mesh of nets made of non-rigid material for the taking of salmon in the sea or tideways.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) If, in the sea or in the tidal portion of a river, any person uses any net to which this section applies with a mesh of less size than 4.4 centimetres[^c16995261] from knot to knot, to be measured along the side of the square, or 17.6 centimetres to be measured all round each such mesh, such measurements being taken in the clear when the net is wet, then, subject to subsection (2), he shall be guilty of an offence.

(2) The Department[^c21339221] may by regulations[^c21339231] permit the use of nets to which this section applies having meshes of a less size than that specified in subsection (1), and this section shall have application with respect to any net to which such regulations apply as if for the dimensions specified in subsection (1) there were substituted the dimensions specified in those regulations.

(3) In this section the expression “net to which this section applies” means any net or other engine which is not formed of a rigid material and which is used for the taking of salmon.
