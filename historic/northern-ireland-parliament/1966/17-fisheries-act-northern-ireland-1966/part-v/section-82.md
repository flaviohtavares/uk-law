## 82. Offence of affecting flow of water through free gap by alteration of bed of river.

*Extent: N.I.*

If any alteration is made in the bed of a river in such manner as to reduce the flow of water through the free gap in a fishing weir, the person making the alteration shall be guilty of an offence …[^c16995661].
