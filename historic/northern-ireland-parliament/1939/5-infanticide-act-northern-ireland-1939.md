# Infanticide Act (Northern Ireland) 1939

> Text as at 2011-06-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

Infanticide Act (Northern Ireland) 1939

1939 Chapter 5

An Act to repeal and re-enact with modifications the provisions of the Infanticide Act, 1922, as applied to Northern Ireland by the Uniformity of Laws Act (Northern Ireland), 1922.

\[2nd May 1939\]

## 1. Offence of infanticide.

(1) Where a woman by any wilful act or omission causes the death of her child, being a child under the age of twelve months, but at the time of the act or omission the balance of her mind was disturbed by reason of her not having fully recovered from the effect of giving birth to the child or by reason of the effect of lactation consequent upon the birth of the child, then, if[^c2128879] the circumstances were such that but for this Act the offence would have amounted to murder or manslaughter[^c2128881], she shall be guilty of felony, to wit of infanticide, and may for such offence be dealt with and punished as if she had been guilty of the offence of manslaughter of the child.

(2) Where upon the trial of a woman for the murder or manslaughter[^c2128885] of her child, being a child under the age of twelve months, the jury are of opinion that she by any wilful act or omission caused its death, but that at the time of the act or omission the balance of her mind was disturbed by reason of her not having fully recovered from the effect of giving birth to the child or by reason of the effect of lactation consequent upon the birth of the child, then the jury may, if[^c2128883] the circumstances were such that but for the provisions of this Act they might have returned a verdict of murder or manslaughter[^c2251360] , return in lieu thereof a verdict of infanticide.

(3) Nothing in this Act shall affect the power of the jury upon an indictment for the murder of a child to return a verdict of manslaughter or a verdict of not guilty on the ground of insanity[^c543488].

*Subs.(4) rep. by 1967 c. 18 (NI)*

## 2. Short title.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Infanticide Act (Northern Ireland), 1939.

*Subs. (2) rep. by SLR (NI) 1952; subs. (3) rep. by 1954 c. 33 (NI)*
