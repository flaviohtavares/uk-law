# Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967, section 32

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 32. Copies of licences.[^c17018181]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where the Ministry is satisfied that a licence granted under Part II [^c-8ca5e6627a76e254d7506ec2302afa7f]... has been lost, destroyed or so defaced by accident as to be incapable of use, the Ministry may, on payment of a fee of \[[^c17018191] 25p\] issue a copy thereof to the person to whom the licence was granted or, as the case may be, to the person to whom the licence was transferred by virtue of regulations made under section 33(2).
