# Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967, section 34

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 34. Forgery of licences.[^c17018251]

*Extent: N.I.*

[^c17018261] If, with intent to deceive, any person—

- (a) [^c17018271]. . . alters or uses or lends to, or allows to be used by, any other person, a licence granted under Part II [^key-91669452e32fa6c7bd47751dd2af2bdf]... \[[^c17018281] or any certificate or diploma referred to in section 46D(1)[^c17018291][^key-91669452e32fa6c7bd47751dd2af2bdf] ...\][^key-91669452e32fa6c7bd47751dd2af2bdf] ... or[^c17018321] an authorisation or a control document issued under Interbus, the RSR Protocol or the RPT Chapter[^key-1ccbe41e4eea2beca0a1f2c0e3627b16] ;
- (b) makes or has in his possession any document [^M_F_dab5d712-ecb6-44c6-f62b-dc1bef4926d1]... so closely resembling any such licence \[[^c17018281], certificate, diploma\] , authorisation or control document[^key-478b26bb1db41f59c27a0fe6acfec05b] as to be calculated to deceive;

he shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable —

- (i) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale[^c17018361] or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or to both such fine and such imprisonment;
- (ii) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, with or without a fine.
