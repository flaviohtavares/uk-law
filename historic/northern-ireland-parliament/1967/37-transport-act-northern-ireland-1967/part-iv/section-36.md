# Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967, section 36

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 36. Disclosure of information.[^c17018511]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any information which is obtained under Part II, [^key-09d9367871aaaf51ad3e7a92bb1f8546]... or this Part shall not, without the previous consent in writing of the person from whom the information was obtained, be disclosed except for the purposes of—

- (a) the execution of those Parts, and of general statistics and returns;
- (b) any legal proceedings arising out of those Parts, or any criminal proceedings, whether so arising or not;
- (c) any reports of any proceedings of the kind referred to in paragraph ( *b*).

(2) Any person who contravenes subsection (1) shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale[^c17018521], or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or to both such fine and such imprisonment.
