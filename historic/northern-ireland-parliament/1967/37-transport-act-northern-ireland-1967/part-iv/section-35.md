# Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967, section 35

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 35. False statements.[^c17018381]

*Extent: N.I.*

[^c17018391] A person who for the purpose of—

- (a) obtaining the grant of a licence under Part II [^key-667cc7ff19100c79fb4421409c94a1db]... to himself or any other person;
- (b) preventing the grant of any such licence; or
- (c) procuring the attachment of conditions to, or the amendment of conditions attached to, any such licence; \[[^c17018401] or
- (d)[^key-26808b336ab704beb8a870f8810c3a9e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^c17018421] obtaining from the Department any certificate of professional competence recognised for the purposes of section 46D[^key-8c82fb660296f43fb8ea282754ba074e] . or[^c17018411]
- (f) obtaining the issue of an authorisation or a control document under Interbus, the RSR Protocol or the RPT Chapter[^key-34b84d049fc4df0a609c6e92d5e0f66f] ;

knowingly makes a statement which is false in a material particular shall (without prejudice to \[[^c17018481] Article 10 of the Perjury (Northern Ireland) Order 1979 [^m00006]\]) be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale[^c17018491] or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or to both such fine and such imprisonment.
