# Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967, section 33

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 33. Transfer of licences.[^c17018201]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to subsection (2), a licence granted under Part II [^key-828a8f189618c39e9d71b65bf436d946]... shall not be transferable by the holder of the licence or by operation of law to any other person.

(2) The Ministry may by regulations provide for the holding of a licence granted under Part II [^key-d3762162d60e1824e5684d16f81d0c60]... by a person other than the person to whom the licence was granted where—

- (a) the person to whom the licence was granted dies or becomes incapacitated, or is adjudged bankrupt or makes a voluntary arrangement proposed for the purposes of, and approved under, Part VIII of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^c17018211] or becomes subject to a bankruptcy restrictions order[^key-6bfa857280a02d0dc0429a3e76c5c425] or has had a debt relief order made in respect of him, or becomes subject to a debt relief restrictions order[^key-2d8e652c21985414c0a2bc77eb01b6ef] ;
- (b) an order has been made by a court, or a resolution has been passed for the winding up of the undertaking of the person to whom the licence was granted, except for the purpose of reconstruction;
- (c) a receiver or liquidator of the undertaking of the person to whom the licence was granted has been appointed or the undertaking enters administration[^c19796931]; or
- (d) the person to whom the licence was granted is a government department (including a department of the Government of the United Kingdom) or a body established by or under any statutory provision and the functions of that department or body are transferred by or under a statutory provision to any other person; \[[^c17018241] or
- (e) [^key-f8b9b6f9f89ea3d05bb3f1d366a81dae]..., the licence has been revoked under section 10(3A) [^key-f8b9b6f9f89ea3d05bb3f1d366a81dae]... as from some future date.\]
