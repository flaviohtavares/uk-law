> Text as at 2025-04-01.

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 41. Authorisation of inspectors.[^c17018621]

*Extent: N.I.*

An inspector appointed under this Part shall, when exercising his functions under this Part produce, if so required a duly authenticated document showing his authority to act as such inspector.
