> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 54. Pension schemes.

*Extent: N.I.*

The Holding Company may, but only with the written consent of the Ministry, provide by means of pension schemes for the payment of pensions and other superannuation benefits to or in respect of persons who are or have been in the employment of the Holding Company.
