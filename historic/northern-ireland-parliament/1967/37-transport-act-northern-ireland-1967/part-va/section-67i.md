# Transport Act (Northern Ireland) 1967, section 67I

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 67I. Orders under sections 67A(2), 67G(1) and 67H.[^c17016891]

*Extent: N.I.*

Article 133(2) and (4) of, and Schedule 8 to, the Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993[^c17019201] shall, subject to the modifications thereof specified in Schedule 1A, apply in relation to the making of orders under section 67A(2), 67G(1) or 67H in like manner as they apply in relation to the making of orders under any of the Articles mentioned in Article 133(1) of that Order.
