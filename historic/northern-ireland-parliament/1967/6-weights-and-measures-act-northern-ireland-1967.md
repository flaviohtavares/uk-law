> Text as at 2006-01-01.

*Extent: N.I.*

Weights and Measures Act (Northern Ireland) 1967

1967 Chapter 6

An Act to make amended provision with respect to weights and measures and for connected purposes.

\[25th April 1967\]

*This Act has been repealed except for sections 38(1) and 45(1) which are reproduced below (see 1981 NI 10 art.54(2) sch.11)*

## 38. Fees under section 20 of the Petroleum (Consolidation) Act (Northern Ireland) 1929.

(1) Subject to subsection (2), such fees shall be payable under section 20 of the Petroleum (Consolidation) Act (Northern Ireland) 1929 [^c1668149] in respect of tests of apparatus under that section as the Ministry[^c544665], with the approval of the Ministry of Finance, may determine; and accordingly … *residue amends s.20 of, and sch.2 to, 1929 c.13 (NI)*

## 45. Short title.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Weights and Measures Act (Northern Ireland) 1967.
