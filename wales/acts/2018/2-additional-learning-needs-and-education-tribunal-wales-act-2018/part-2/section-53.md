## 53. Additional learning provision otherwise than in schools[^c-0efd7a536a425941851e20710f736474][^c-b9dcf9b2c6727c5ae45b43a03e6debda][^c-177d636efeb4a3ae16867ceaec62f0a0][^c-0970ae213fc890b271732beaf6600db3][^c-bbba451f6faabaf8d1a858de82532809][^c-618e525a3ab8b42374418ef9aa7b0199][^c-203b4a74be126382d924d92980f215af][^c-f62c9932e5eb95274df84667185313e2][^c-f0b76ac27271f1c687b8d5bcee5e1b6b][^c-da5764774135843ce840545a2e8de26c][^c-c9a2bb7ca0484adefec9f0d9c3130d2d][^c-d1081f040389d2bf3eeea1ebf3ac4bf2][^c-a34f0df72bc42baeac2facc27704aae4][^c-8c503955c06d6c584911bb6e4263d837]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority may arrange for the additional learning provision described in an individual development plan it maintains for a child, or any part of that additional learning provision, to be made otherwise than in a school.

(2) But a local authority may only do so if it is satisfied that it would be inappropriate for the additional learning provision to be made in a school.
