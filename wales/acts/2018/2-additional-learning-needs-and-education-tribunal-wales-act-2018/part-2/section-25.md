## 25. Relationship of individual development plans to other similar documents[^key-3aa7b5022604bd37deab35d43b47734f][^key-a78b854257b766e397ae60f410c440cc][^key-5a8c24a64e6548df968fb92dcc65db42][^key-5a43a3216ab5391a10a19a40f35f03f4][^key-b33c3b14d08cd164961609824ce14486][^key-b8a3d4a5b847f657921e8d07d0919797][^key-20ef70a3427daf52309b591eb864d531][^key-7b65d1591b75177dcc1fa93813c2bea0][^key-0b12b7f4aded29742d53d75341d532e0][^key-34f068ff1b5301fb7a288c0945f7f0d4][^key-3cd3aa028604eb6d3ba20632265d3fed][^key-77d88a7d1eef1ae8cec80e2e730244a1][^key-380fe8ed401a17ebf3575d03f1eb44b5][^key-257af560d166258c0a67c3b5a1aa445f]

*Extent: E+W*

A governing body or local authority may—

- (a) prepare, review or revise a plan under this Part at the same time as it or another body is preparing, reviewing or revising another document in the case of the person concerned, and
- (b) include the other document in the plan or include the plan in the other document.
