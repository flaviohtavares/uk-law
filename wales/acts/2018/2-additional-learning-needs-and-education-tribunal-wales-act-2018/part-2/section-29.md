# Additional Learning Needs and Education Tribunal (Wales) Act 2018, section 29

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29. Circumstances in which the duties in sections 26(2), 27(2) and 28(3) do not apply[^c-4d8f32276a8b55b454d1bd15fe1f1dc3][^c-ffe88ec16f46ddf226ce73dea7080410][^c-d148afcac76d67a45ce61c4367b59fd0][^c-61eacb6000073cb596dd6b6efc7c8510][^c-3a72650f8fb7689b629d72bc62903c3a][^c-59a4eebf80dc4b4b7e701a88655ca152][^c-04c22475c0492542f5dd4014e884f111][^c-2d42d36e2e2f9523e48061715e6b8ca5][^c-4db213a6cf4eeb78b10744e86fd7494e][^c-9da29c5009a92183603ef1d735187751][^c-a0bcecfe2ae40acd02ae6645c718c805][^c-9ae24c463682e282789e642ba25792bf][^c-ac2818b0a4e84ed3fcff20ff3ebe2549][^c-a99810d6fa2bf083a713694ac7a1b08e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Following a request under section 26(1)(b), 27(1)(b) or 28(1)(b), the duty in section 26(2), 27(2) or 28(3) (as the case may be) does not apply in relation to a child or young person if any of the circumstances in subsection (2) apply.

(2) The circumstances are—

- (a) the local authority has previously made a decision under the same section in relation to the same child or young person and it is satisfied that—
  - (i) the child's or young person's needs have not changed materially since the previous decision, and
  - (ii) there is no new information that would materially affect that decision.
- (b) the request relates to a child who has become looked after by a local authority.
