## 38. Local authority power to direct governing bodies of maintained schools[^key-b29fafb013bb435dcc1f30e3c63a14dc][^key-5ed039d4c39de74fa3576160cda57bef][^key-72481c3970931c15b238ddcdd9e0d74f][^key-75e246224ba8e123dfff86f5e8cdc8f7][^key-a3c2d5cb8cb54778ee7748b70ef2bf4b][^key-a84247404e74cf92173a275519fe083e][^key-735712438cbf975be341bb4b8f23d92e][^key-9ce3ed1e6e6d79b0d87aaff8ad036091][^key-c224d2ec40f3229b80636178f39edc73][^key-2c67c1ebf12814a390b539a68b8e6642][^key-9a287afe898d3618fa086555649efeb2][^key-d414921f68319f5a6c9803fc9249a233][^key-3396a18ab59900f057970f749441c5b8][^key-39815458bd698f05b153b7f9a1728ca9]

*Extent: E+W*

Any power of a local authority under this Chapter to direct the governing body of a maintained school is not exercisable in respect of a school that the authority does not maintain unless the authority has consulted the local authority that maintains the school about its intention to exercise the power.
