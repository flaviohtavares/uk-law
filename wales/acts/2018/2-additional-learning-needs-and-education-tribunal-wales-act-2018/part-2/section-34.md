## 34. Individual development plan after a young person's 25th birthday[^key-9d25f9d83a6d156c35a2837d79fa73a3][^key-1dd14df9159d58c7e646cea25b874cda][^key-c2ea9aa6a06926efe447a4f8826282e4][^key-cdd09049bb042894c0fa41d7c9dc8c39][^key-656a526c4f7766e68b7226cd4fa3e507][^key-f0773d7a58b322be6926a6476169fb56][^key-5203ff8c0a4113c0276f9ad2d38c00bf][^key-31f7d2b44bbfd90d667a3db90ec852b4][^key-96dff011482d89f9c6f7155e28866419][^key-9bcf0eb29cdc49891c832bede3298560][^key-c455d7e1afd8eed6a730968031db9e34][^key-f2a7b264ce9385ab8b4d396c4f4ce974][^key-80e47c33f8f305f1268946ff307f255d][^key-66d35069fbed3242ec402a48e8d2b36d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The duty of the governing body of an institution in the further education sector under section 12, or of a local authority under section 14, to prepare or maintain a plan for a young person ceases to apply at the end of the academic year during which the young person attains the age of 25.

(2) In this section, “**academic year**” means—

- (a) in relation to a young person who attends an institution in the further education sector, a period of 12 months ending on 31 July, and
- (b) in relation to any other young person, a period of 12 months ending on the day the young person's course of education or training ends or the day before the young person attains the age of 26 (whichever is earlier).
