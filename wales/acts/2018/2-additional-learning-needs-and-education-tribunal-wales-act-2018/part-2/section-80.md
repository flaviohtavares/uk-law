# Additional Learning Needs and Education Tribunal (Wales) Act 2018, section 80

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 80. Allowances for attendance at the Education Tribunal for Wales[^key-15431ad836cb2e62c51d4190aff1b6cf]

*Extent: E+W*

The Welsh Ministers may pay allowances for the purpose of or in connection with the attendance of persons at the Education Tribunal for Wales.
