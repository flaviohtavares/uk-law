# Legislation (Wales) Act 2019, section 12

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Measurement of distance[^c23844251][^c-8baa7c3022bc718f79986b9f5b1a12f2]

*Extent: E+W*

A reference to a distance in an Act of Senedd Cymru[^c-c70ef026524547ed9192318715d88a08] or a Welsh subordinate instrument is a reference to that distance measured in a straight line on a horizontal plane.
