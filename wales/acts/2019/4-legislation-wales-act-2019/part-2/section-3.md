# Legislation (Wales) Act 2019, section 3

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Legislation to which this Part applies[^c23844161][^key-567cc88f2a8185db5e389dea8d134f8d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Part applies to—

- (a) this Act;
- (b) Acts of Senedd Cymru[^key-e2b216466e134994c6cba01281525211] that receive Royal Assent on or after 1 January 2020[^key-43d88aab42bdf693dda7cd9ee7992d71];
- (c) Welsh subordinate instruments that are made on or after 1 January 2020[^key-f3cfa6d08e012b7fef1fbf63f0622ae7].

(2) “**Welsh subordinate instrument**” means an instrument (whether or not that instrument is a Welsh statutory instrument or[^key-245a59280c1677d000dea3f2ce3cff14] statutory instrument) containing only one or both of the following—

- (a) subordinate legislation that is made under an Act of Senedd Cymru[^key-c70ef026524547ed9192318715d88a08] or an Assembly Measure, whether by the Welsh Ministers or by any other person;
- (b) subordinate legislation that—
  - (i) is made under an Act of the Parliament of the United Kingdom or assimilated direct[^key-31c8a46961244c441f97e2ec1aa4ae1e] legislation,
  - (ii) is made only by the Welsh Ministers or any other devolved Welsh authority (within the meaning given by section 157A of the Government of Wales Act 2006 (c. 32)), and
  - (iii) applies only in relation to Wales.

(3)[^key-834995effad396f7653397edbac66fca] References in this Part to an Act of Senedd Cymru are (unless otherwise provided) references to an Act of Senedd Cymru to which this Part applies by virtue of subsection (1).

(3A) References in this Part to a Welsh subordinate instrument are (unless otherwise provided) references to the subordinate legislation contained in a Welsh subordinate instrument to which this Part applies by virtue of subsection (1).

(4)[^key-2f481ab665049ff0937faaaef39bfd60] In relation to subordinate legislation that relates to fishing, fisheries or fish health and is made after section 45 of the Fisheries Act [2020 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2020/22) comes into force, the reference in subsection (2)(b)(iii) to Wales includes the area of the Welsh zone beyond the seaward limits of the territorial sea.
