> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. References to certain EU instruments[^c23844391][^c-5c91c6767cd0c5de1c66262ca670bb27][^c-3d2109ca37154178b08872325a366d93]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where—

- (a) an Act of Senedd Cymru[^c-c70ef026524547ed9192318715d88a08] or a Welsh subordinate instrument refers to an EU instrument (“A”), and
- (b) before the day on which the [^c-fe09bab4484bdf0f2daad64e3a5bcfd3]... Act receives Royal Assent or the [^c-cd294b6a47650554b05f297fefcc9f91]... instrument is made, A has been amended, extended or applied by another EU instrument (“B”).

(2) The reference to A is a reference to A as amended, extended or applied by B.

(3) See also paragraph 2A of Schedule 8 to the European Union (Withdrawal) Act[^c-2698e7a6112df1e44d69a39c17d03b3a] [2018 (c.16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/16) [^c-584d46f9e525f2f9fca9581c345d3ea1]... .
