# Legislation (Wales) Act 2019, section 31

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31. Orders and regulations bringing Acts of Senedd Cymru into force[^c23844441][^c-f832edd7a0494b5fcc8ab5b639cc9c1c][^c-e2b216466e134994c6cba01281525211]

*Extent: E+W*

Where an Act of Senedd Cymru[^c-c70ef026524547ed9192318715d88a08] provides for an order or regulations to appoint—

- (a) the day on which the Act comes into force, or
- (b) the day on which a provision in the Act comes into force,

the order or regulations may appoint different days for different purposes.
