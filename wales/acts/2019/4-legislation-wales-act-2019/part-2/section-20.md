# Legislation (Wales) Act 2019, section 20

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. Varying and withdrawing directions[^c23844331][^c-87a9d60d58ef646710dbdbf0febca238]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A power to give directions conferred by an Act of Senedd Cymru[^c-c70ef026524547ed9192318715d88a08] or a Welsh subordinate instrument may be exercised to vary or withdraw any directions given under the power.

(2) A duty to give directions imposed by an Act of Senedd Cymru or a Welsh subordinate instrument includes a power (exercisable in the same way and subject to the same conditions or limitations as the duty) to vary, or withdraw and replace, any directions given under the duty.
