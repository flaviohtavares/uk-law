# Legislation (Wales) Act 2019, section 37

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Interpretation of provisions about amendment, repeal and revocation in this Part[^c23844501][^c-6be65c15d8c758fd29446d0af4fc8825][^c-9139d64f3f1bbfe9514656bacb3fdff0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part, references to repealing or revoking an enactment or abolishing a rule of law include—

- (a) substituting anything for the enactment or rule (or for any part of it);
- (b) limiting the application or effect of the enactment or rule;
- (c) providing for the enactment or rule to cease to have effect.

(2) For the purposes of sections 34 to 36 (but not section 33)—

- (a) the expiry of a temporary Act of Senedd Cymru[^c-c70ef026524547ed9192318715d88a08] is to be treated as a repeal of the Act by an Act of Senedd Cymru or a Welsh subordinate instrument;
- (b) the expiry of a temporary Welsh subordinate instrument is to be treated as a revocation of the instrument by an Act of Senedd Cymru or a Welsh subordinate instrument.

(3)[^c-4cde3bed73e5e9f8104cfff588e59215] In sections 32 to 35, and in subsection (1) as it applies for the purposes of those sections, “enactment includes an enactment that is, or is contained in, a Measure of the Church Assembly or of the General Synod of the Church of England or an instrument made under such a Measure.
