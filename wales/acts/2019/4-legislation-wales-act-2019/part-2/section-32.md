# Legislation (Wales) Act 2019, section 32

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 32. Amendments made to or by Welsh legislation[^c23844451][^c-8391d8152441a96309074a91d7f9a0c7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where an enactment amends an Act of Senedd Cymru[^c-c70ef026524547ed9192318715d88a08] or a Welsh subordinate instrument by inserting or substituting words or other material, the words or material have effect as part of that Act or instrument.

(2) Where an Act of Senedd Cymru or a Welsh subordinate instrument amends an enactment by inserting or substituting words or other material, the words or material have effect as part of that enactment.

(3) See also section 23ZA of the Interpretation Act 1978 (c. 30) for provision about the application of that Act to assimilated direct[^c-e017fcd8cfff61953419f9306cc486e1] legislation that is amended by an Act of Senedd Cymru or a Welsh subordinate instrument (or by certain other legislation).
