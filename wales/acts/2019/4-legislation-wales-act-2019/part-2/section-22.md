# Legislation (Wales) Act 2019, section 22

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22. Edition of Act of Senedd Cymru or Assembly Measure referred to[^c23844351][^key-16d7f511874a2dcd22686de6de086724][^key-c70ef026524547ed9192318715d88a08]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where an Act of Senedd Cymru or a Welsh subordinate instrument refers to an Act of Senedd Cymru (including an Act of Senedd Cymru to which this Part does not apply) or an Assembly Measure.

(2) The reference is a reference to the certified copy of the official print[^key-2755a03212b13bfbea36aa22ec2742cf] of the Act, or to the Measure as approved, which is published—

- (a) by the King’s Printer for Wales[^key-908a4dc47dcb3d3d822d736dd163b87a], or
- (b) under the superintendence or authority of His Majesty’s[^key-29372fb4ba47da5c2546fc19e0e27dfe] Stationery Office.
