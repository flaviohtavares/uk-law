# Legislation (Wales) Act 2019, section 33

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Repeals and revocations do not revive law previously repealed, revoked or abolished[^c23844461][^c-4c0cc53d03cfff502b27bc49c073bebf]

*Extent: E+W*

Where—

- (a) an Act of Senedd Cymru[^c-c70ef026524547ed9192318715d88a08] or a Welsh subordinate instrument repeals or revokes an enactment (“A”), and
- (b) A previously repealed or revoked any other enactment (“B”) or abolished any other rule of law (“C”),

the repeal or revocation of A does not revive B or C.
