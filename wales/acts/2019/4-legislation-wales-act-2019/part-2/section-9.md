# Legislation (Wales) Act 2019, section 9

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Variations of a word or expression due to grammar etc.[^c23844221][^key-ce71200266981ce259abd403af5decb7]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a word or expression in an Act of Senedd Cymru[^key-c70ef026524547ed9192318715d88a08] or a Welsh subordinate instrument is given a meaning by an enactment, other parts of speech and grammatical forms or modifications of the word or expression are to be interpreted in accordance with that meaning.
