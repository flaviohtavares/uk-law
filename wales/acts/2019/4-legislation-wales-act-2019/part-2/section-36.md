# Legislation (Wales) Act 2019, section 36

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. Referring to an Act of Senedd Cymru or Welsh subordinate instrument after repeal or revocation[^c23844491][^key-b5ffb4a7d0380c19e85db8453a899e26][^key-63cfe669158a842c993a34fd75512d92]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-41028e90d212f989f8c5011e04fcb944] An Act of Senedd Cymru[^key-c70ef026524547ed9192318715d88a08] may continue to be referred to by the short title conferred on it by an enactment despite the repeal of that enactment.

(2)[^key-7aa680357485640bd8d5879c2e72027c] A Welsh subordinate instrument may continue be referred to by its title despite the revocation of the instrument.
