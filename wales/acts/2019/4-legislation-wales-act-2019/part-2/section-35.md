# Legislation (Wales) Act 2019, section 35

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Effect of re-enactment[^c23844481][^key-49999b4cd215a167f7ac86707a7d5d3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where an enactment (“A”) is—

- (a) repealed or revoked by an Act of Senedd Cymru[^key-c70ef026524547ed9192318715d88a08] or a Welsh subordinate instrument, and
- (b) re-enacted (with or without modification) by an enactment (“B”) which is, or is contained in, an Act of Senedd Cymru or a Welsh subordinate instrument.

(2) A reference to A in any enactment, instrument or document is to be read as (or as including) a reference to B.

(3) So far as any subordinate legislation made under A or having effect as if it were made under A could have been made under B, it is to have effect as if made under B.

(4) So far as anything done or having effect as if it were done under A could have been done under B, it is to have effect as if done under B.

(5)[^key-a992ca78308511d3a03e60331de2bd69] Subsection (3) does not cause section 28 to have effect in relation to any subordinate legislation where it did not previously have effect.
