# Legislation (Wales) Act 2019, section 5

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Equal status of Welsh and English language texts[^c23844181][^c-e164e5ddbd14d117b570c0c1913a7308]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where an Act of Senedd Cymru[^c-c70ef026524547ed9192318715d88a08] is enacted, or a Welsh subordinate instrument is made, in Welsh and English.

(2) The Welsh language text and the English language text have equal status for all purposes.
