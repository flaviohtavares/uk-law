# Legislation (Wales) Act 2019, section 29

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29. Time when Welsh legislation comes into force[^c23844421][^c-abe401f1a1d507f089843e24b656a6a3]

*Extent: E+W*

Where—

- (a) an Act of Senedd Cymru[^c-c70ef026524547ed9192318715d88a08] or a Welsh subordinate instrument, or
- (b) a provision in an Act of Senedd Cymru or a Welsh subordinate instrument,

comes into force on a day provided for in an enactment, the Act, instrument or provision comes into force at the beginning of that day.
