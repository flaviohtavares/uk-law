# Legislation (Wales) Act 2019, section 28

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Application of Welsh legislation to the Crown[^c23844411][^c-d92e097b122e07055cc4efabf451994a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An Act of Senedd Cymru[^c-c70ef026524547ed9192318715d88a08] binds the Crown.

(2) A Welsh subordinate instrument binds the Crown so far as it is made under an enactment which binds the Crown or confers a power to make provision binding the Crown.

(3) An Act of Senedd Cymru or a Welsh subordinate instrument does not make the Crown criminally liable, but it applies to persons in the service of the Crown as it applies to other persons.
