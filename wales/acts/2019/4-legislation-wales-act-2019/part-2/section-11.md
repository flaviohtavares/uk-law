# Legislation (Wales) Act 2019, section 11

> Text as at 2026-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. References to the Sovereign[^c23844241][^key-f33aa381df2e699f8dcb1b5dce3d270a]

*Extent: E+W*

A reference to the Sovereign in an Act of Senedd Cymru[^key-c70ef026524547ed9192318715d88a08] or a Welsh subordinate instrument is to be read as a reference to the Sovereign for the time being.
