# Public Services Ombudsman (Wales) Act 2019, section 33

## 33. Provisions in regulations adding persons to Schedule 3[^key-884126a856f4853fa5f0d11b2541e355]

*Extent: E+W*

If the Welsh Ministers propose to make regulations under section 31(2) adding a person to Schedule 3, they must also specify in the regulations—

- (a) whether all or only some of the person's functions are to fall within the remit of the Ombudsman under this Part;
- (b) if only some of the person's functions are to fall within the remit of the Ombudsman under this Part, which those functions are.
