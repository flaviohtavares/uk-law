# Disused Mine and Quarry Tips (Wales) Act 2025, section 73

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 73. Provision of administrative, technical or professional services[^key-19e43d85c7333c8815ed23574f493260]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Authority may provide administrative, professional or technical services to any devolved Welsh authority (within the meaning of section 157A of the Government of Wales Act [2006 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/32)).

(2) The Authority may charge a fee for providing a service under subsection (1).
