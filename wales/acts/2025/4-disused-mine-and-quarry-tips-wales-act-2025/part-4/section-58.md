# Disused Mine and Quarry Tips (Wales) Act 2025, section 58

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Meaning of “relevant public authority”[^c-efb8724cf7487211bd19a1f2340c9fb0]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) In this Part, “**relevant public authority**” means—

- (a) the Welsh Ministers;
- (b) Natural Resources Wales;
- (c) a council for a county or county borough in Wales;
- (d) a National Park Authority for a National Park in Wales;
- (e) the Coal Authority;
- (f) a Fire and Rescue Authority for an area in Wales.

(2) In subsection (1)(f), “**Fire and Rescue Authority**” means a fire and rescue authority constituted under section 2 of the Fire and Rescue Services Act [2004 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/21), or a scheme to which section 4 of that Act applies.

(3) The Welsh Ministers may by regulations amend this section to change the definition of “relevant public authority”.

(4) Regulations under subsection (3) may not include a person within the scope of the definition unless the person is a devolved Welsh authority (within the meaning of section 157A(1)(a) of the Government of Wales Act [2006 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/32)).
