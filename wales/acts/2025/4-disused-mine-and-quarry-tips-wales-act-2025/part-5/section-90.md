# Disused Mine and Quarry Tips (Wales) Act 2025, section 90

> Text as at 2025-09-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 90. General interpretation[^key-075c9817696f30a0bec63f4a7a24ed90]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Act—

- “**the court**” (“***y llys***”) means the High Court or the county court.
- “**operations**” (“***gweithrediadau***”) includes, but is not limited to, building and engineering operations.
