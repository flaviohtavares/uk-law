# Disused Mine and Quarry Tips (Wales) Act 2025, section 85

> Text as at 2025-09-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 85. Power to modify application of Act to Authority land[^key-469392d623ebfda9197c92debf613248]

*Extent: E+W*

The Welsh Ministers may, by regulations, modify the application of this Act in relation to land in which the Authority has an estate or interest.
