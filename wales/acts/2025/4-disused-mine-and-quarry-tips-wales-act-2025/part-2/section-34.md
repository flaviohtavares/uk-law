# Disused Mine and Quarry Tips (Wales) Act 2025, section 34

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 34. Penalty for obstructing monitoring activities or assessments[^c-64e25bfb192be0b3600713e59c7ddf45]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A person who intentionally obstructs or interferes with—

- (a) an inspection or other monitoring activity under Chapter 1, or
- (b) a preliminary assessment or full assessment under Chapter 2,

commits an offence.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction to a fine.

(3) A contravention of subsection (1) by the Crown does not make the Crown criminally liable (but see section 28(3) of the [Legislation (Wales) Act 2019 (anaw 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2019/4) regarding the liability of persons in the service of the Crown).
