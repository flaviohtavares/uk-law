# Disused Mine and Quarry Tips (Wales) Act 2025, section 12

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Meaning of “preliminary assessment”[^c-2bd813a08ad8d9abe3b255d2de22fdb0]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In this Chapter, “**preliminary assessment**” means an assessment of whether it appears that the criteria for registering a disused tip may be met.
