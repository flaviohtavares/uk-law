# Visitor Accommodation (Register and Levy) Etc. (Wales) Act 2025, section 21

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21. Double jeopardy[^c-08412cefbbea8e44d42e9524510d0e0d]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

A person is not liable to a penalty under this Part in respect of anything if the person has been convicted of an offence in relation to it.
