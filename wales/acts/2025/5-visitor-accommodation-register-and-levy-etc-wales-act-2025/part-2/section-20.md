# Visitor Accommodation (Register and Levy) Etc. (Wales) Act 2025, section 20

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. Payment of penalties[^key-b90050c9a4a3dea50599811f1bb2091f]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

A penalty under this Part must be paid before the end of the period of 30 days beginning with the day on which the penalty notice is issued (but see section 182 of the [Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016 (anaw 6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/6) (payment of penalties in the event of a review or appeal)).
