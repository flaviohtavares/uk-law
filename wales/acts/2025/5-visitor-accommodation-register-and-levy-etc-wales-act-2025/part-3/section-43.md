## 43. Principal council account for proceeds of the levy[^c-d0d86f1ae7887e93fbf184e5ba3ea303]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A principal council that has introduced the levy must maintain a separate account for the proceeds of the levy.

(2) In this Chapter, “**proceeds of the levy**” means the amount paid to a principal council by WRA under section 24A of the Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016 (payments of net proceeds of visitor levy to principal councils).
