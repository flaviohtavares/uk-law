# Visitor Accommodation (Register and Levy) Etc. (Wales) Act 2025, section 62

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 62. Power to extend Act to berths and moorings[^c-ee82de90e4f92e9b447ed604f56b24cc]

*Extent: E+W*

The Welsh Ministers may by regulations amend this Act, and the [Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016 (anaw 6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/6), so that either or both of Parts 2 and 3 of this Act apply or are replicated, with or without modifications, in respect of berths and moorings provided for vessels.
