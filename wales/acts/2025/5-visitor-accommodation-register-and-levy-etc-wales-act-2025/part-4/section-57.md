# Visitor Accommodation (Register and Levy) Etc. (Wales) Act 2025, section 57

## 57. Power to make further provision about partnerships and unincorporated bodies[^c-dd40520e2fb7953774207fffa63a7a64][^c-2db1187fc2290ab6be10d4eb25210ae4]

*Extent: E+W*

The Welsh Ministers may by regulations add to, repeal or revoke or otherwise amend any provision made by an enactment relating to the register under Part 2 or the levy (including this Act) about cases where persons carry on business in partnership or as an unincorporated body.
