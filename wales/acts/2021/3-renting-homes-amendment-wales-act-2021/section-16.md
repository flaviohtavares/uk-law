# Renting Homes (Amendment) (Wales) Act 2021, section 16

> Text as at 2023-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 16. Fee for further copy of written statement to be a permitted payment[^c-20de962f464ee574bc315d98ed74eac4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Renting Homes (Fees etc.) (Wales) Act 2019 is amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 1 (permitted payments), after paragraph 10A insert—

> **10B. Payment for further copy of written statement**
>
> A payment of a reasonable fee for a further written statement of a standard occupation contract is a permitted payment.

(3) In section 4, after subsection (2)(i) insert—

> - (j) payments in respect of further copies of a written statement.
