# Renting Homes (Amendment) (Wales) Act 2021, section 17

> Text as at 2023-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Interpretation[^c-7689d167b0bf9cc2cac4999c06da8ddd]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Act—

- (a) “**standard contract**” means—
  - (i) a periodic standard contract under the 2016 Act;
  - (ii) a fixed term standard contract under that Act,

  as to which, see section 8 of the 2016 Act;
- (b) “**the 2016 Act**” means the Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016 (anaw 1).
