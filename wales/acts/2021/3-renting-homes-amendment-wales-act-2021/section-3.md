# Renting Homes (Amendment) (Wales) Act 2021, section 3

> Text as at 2023-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Standard contracts with minimum notice period of two months[^c-709923974aae7a35a17b990dc503a080]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 1 inserts a new Schedule 8A into the 2016 Act, setting out standard contracts which can be terminated by the landlord on giving two months' notice.
