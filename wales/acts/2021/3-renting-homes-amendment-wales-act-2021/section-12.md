# Renting Homes (Amendment) (Wales) Act 2021, section 12

> Text as at 2023-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Landlord's request to vary periodic standard contract terms: removal of additional notice procedure[^key-8dd398ee9dde5e98af88afe48e20e028]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The 2016 Act is amended as follows.

(2) In section 125 (variation of contract)—

- (a) in subsection (1)—
  - (i) for “127)—” substitute “ 127) by agreement between the landlord and the contract-holder. ”;
  - (ii) omit paragraphs (a) and (b);
- (b) in subsection (2) omit the words from “; but subsection (1)(b)” to the end.

(3) Omit section 126 (variation by landlord: notice procedure).

(4) In section 173 (landlord's notice), omit subsection (3).
