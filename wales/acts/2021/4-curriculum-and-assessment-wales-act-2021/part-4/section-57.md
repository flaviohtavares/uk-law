# Curriculum and Assessment (Wales) Act 2021, section 57

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2021/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 57. Promoting and maintaining understanding of progression[^key-e255c7e7a925d1092aeeca194fc3cb42]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-4792af6383d4a3b6ae2dbfe151dc9802][^key-e3e785902d16a74c65a1c59f9976b571] The Welsh Ministers may direct a relevant person (or relevant persons of a specified description) to take specified steps with a view to promoting and maintaining understanding of progression in the context of a relevant curriculum.

(2)[^key-a06cf43c0ec60e786d7731098d41fcce] A relevant person to whom a direction is given under this section must comply with the direction.

(3)[^key-a06cf43c0ec60e786d7731098d41fcce] In this section—

- (a) “relevant curriculum” and “relevant person” have the meaning given in section 56, and
- (b) “**specified**” means specified in a direction under this section.
