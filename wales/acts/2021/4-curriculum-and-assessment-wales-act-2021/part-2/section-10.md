# Curriculum and Assessment (Wales) Act 2021, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2021/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. Curriculum design[^c-d5eeaf4a114158a643c0f90790805b08][^c-702e88a01e9004f6a72fa225d0d2d617][^c-ac528e1a152a3bc5cb71f1632238f40e][^c-9bfafd6d5c2d18acab955a5dd4548f05][^c-818baaa1f076bfe577ec768e718be11c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The head teacher of a school must design a curriculum for the school's pupils.

(2) That curriculum must comply with the requirements in sections 20 to 24, and any requirement imposed under section 25.
