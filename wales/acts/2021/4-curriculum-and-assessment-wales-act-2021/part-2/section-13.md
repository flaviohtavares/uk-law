## 13. Welsh Ministers' duty to publish a curriculum[^key-68d2e4b7c998e9954c74c1e66ad44c3f][^key-81b63383c5c8717fa7a6713e728161ae][^key-8193d63298fce5cbb070e9517200fc4c][^key-198e1cd091aef067550d5484f89c8c90][^key-448f546dc4f669fd7544bc09f871f581]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers must publish a curriculum (the “section 13 curriculum”) that they consider suitable for children for whom funded non-maintained nursery education is provided.

(2) The section 13 curriculum must comply with the requirements in sections 20 to 24 [^key-d4791a18c7e30505877c46ae50840970]....
