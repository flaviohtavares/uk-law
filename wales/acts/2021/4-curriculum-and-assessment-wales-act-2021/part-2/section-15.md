# Curriculum and Assessment (Wales) Act 2021, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2021/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. Curriculum adoption[^c-c66585b61a1f3637df8efb90755dc668][^c-0a8aa8a8acc50a3c68de631bf1910930][^c-13b6be112d557618f4c6a60ef729d837][^c-59d6312f6746896aafcbb55dc1e1b22c][^c-246cddeb0336ae50ff6e255983b46f40]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A provider of funded non-maintained nursery education must—

- (a) adopt a curriculum for children for whom that education is provided (whether the section 13 curriculum or another curriculum the provider considers suitable), and
- (b) publish a summary of the adopted curriculum.

(2) But a curriculum may not be adopted under this section unless it complies with the requirements in sections 20 to 24 [^c-01cd9547079dee82394bd2b000ecdea1]....
