# Curriculum and Assessment (Wales) Act 2021, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2021/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18. Power to make supplementary provision about curriculum summaries[^key-6e849a69c2511975ccedb1c6f10f1860][^key-e2a62b23368c5ce4361aee96a16994ff][^key-0b71824fe5e5dc0c61ba1c30251c3eac][^key-ec8a05a03e75c0962ee1da48f71dc53b][^key-64e30d0e379358c9495a364bf918021f]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations may make provision—

- (a) about information that is to be included in a summary of an adopted curriculum published under this Part;
- (b) about the publication of a summary of an adopted curriculum (including provision about how publication must take place, and the date by which it must take place).
