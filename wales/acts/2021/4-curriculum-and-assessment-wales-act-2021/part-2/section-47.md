## 47. Exception for pupils for whom arrangements are made under section 19A of the Education Act 1996[^c-2dc9c5b0588ca687510ade1133d645a4][^c-32e83572888b1e8a50d0a3abf220d245][^c-eca4687acadf04b77cb08addca0d36b8][^c-f90608d28809e0283eb6d7c259aee4c5][^c-e0ae4a7d2b010274b40dc02761326529]

*Extent: E+W*

Sections 27, 28, 29 and 30 do not apply in relation to pupils for whom arrangements are made under section 19A of the Education Act 1996 (c. 56) (see, instead, Part 3).
