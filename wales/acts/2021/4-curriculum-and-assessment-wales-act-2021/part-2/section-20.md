## 20. The four purposes[^key-2e35125317b79ae5fecb8f42236f7411][^key-5adce7153b32f83ad70c38821d1f90af][^key-661862fe1aeb08bc0bc783fe5fe50d3f][^key-f0b7ae80bfd13425aa14aae5b8d63832][^key-d4155326bbac4da56b5d8cb0f302e7ce]

*Extent: E+W*

The curriculum must enable pupils, or children, to develop in the ways described in the four purposes.
