## 70. Power to apply Act to detained children and detained young persons[^c-0252bb705ccdb47c9a15a6afb6424586]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Regulations may apply provisions of this Act, with or without modifications, to—

- (a) detained children in Wales of a description specified in the regulations, and
- (b) detained young persons in Wales of a description specified in the regulations.

(2) In this section, a detained child or detained young person means a child or young person who is detained in pursuance of—

- (a) an order made by a court, or
- (b) an order of recall made by the Secretary of State.
