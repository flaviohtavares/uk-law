# Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2021/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Notices etc. of local authority meetings[^c24511301][^c-49cba8b738472aeae884f8c6fc8acbb6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-11a60a371ddb4307e11aacdd7bd3bdba] Part 1 of Schedule 4 makes amendments to the 1972 Act and other Acts, concerning notices and other documents relating to meetings of local authorities.

(2)[^c-8f69cad89a4cc648e9c41f10b84e732d] Any notice or other document relating to a local authority meeting which is required under any enactment to be published electronically is, for the purposes of paragraph 5 of Schedule 1 to the Defamation Act [1996 (c. 31)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/31), to be treated as a document required by law to be open to public inspection.

(3) In subsection (2) “local authority meeting” has the same meaning as in section 50(5).
