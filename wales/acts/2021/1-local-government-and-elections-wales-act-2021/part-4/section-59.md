## 59. Content of, and duty to have regard to, guidance under section 38 of the 2000 Act[^key-1f9075c202e72fa548e7e02574b6fef5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 38 of the 2000 Act (guidance) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1), after “local authority” insert “ , an elected mayor or an executive leader ”.

(3) After subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) The guidance may, among other things, include provision designed to encourage good practice in relation to equality and diversity (within the meaning of section 8(2) of the Equality Act 2006).
