# Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021, section 66

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2021/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 66. Power to require authorities to appoint joint overview and scrutiny committees[^c-c528c7ace814287758756e878fefdca4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 58 of the 2011 Measure (joint overview and scrutiny committees) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1), after “authorities may” insert “ or must ”.

(3) In subsection (3), for paragraph (a) substitute—

> - (a) provision about the circumstances in which arrangements may be made;
> - (aa) provision prescribing circumstances in which arrangements must be made;
> - (ab) provision for arrangements to be made subject to prescribed conditions or limitations;

.

(4) In subsection (4)—

- (a) omit “, in exercising or deciding whether to exercise any function conferred on it by or under this section,”;
- (b) after “Welsh Ministers” insert “ in relation to the exercise of any function it has under or by virtue of this section ”.
