## SCHEDULE 3 — Amendments relating to Part 2: general power of competence

*Extent: E+W*

(introduced by sections 29 and 37)

### PART 1 — Amendments relating to Chapter 1 of Part 2: the general power

#### Local Government Act 2000 (c. 22)

**1.**[^key-971112f6a02087191c15ad31dab879d1] (1) Part 1 of the 2000 Act (promotion of economic, social or environmental well-being etc.) is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-3a7782def26a0f725bb7c8b5cecd4f27] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-3a7782def26a0f725bb7c8b5cecd4f27] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Omit section 5 (power to amend or repeal enactments).

(5) In section 7 (power to modify enactments concerning plans etc.), in subsection (1) for “subsections (4) and (6)” substitute “ subsection (4) ”.

(6) In the cross-heading preceding section 9, for the words after “under” substitute “ this Part ”.

(7) In section 9A (procedure for orders under sections 5 and 7)—

- (a) in subsection (1), omit “5 or”;
- (b) in subsection (2), omit “5 or”;
- (c) in subsection (3)—
  - (i) omit “5 or”;
  - (ii) omit “5(3A) or”;
- (d) in subsections (5) to (7) and the heading, omit “5 or” in each place where it occurs.

#### Local Government Act 2003 (c. 26)

**2.**[^key-e8b50e476ba393044495380d4484d449] In the Local Government Act 2003, in section 116 (local polls)—

- (a) in paragraph (a)(ii), for “services, or” substitute “ services. ”;
- (b) omit paragraph (b).

#### Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007 (c. 28)

**3.**[^key-fe1d5e373953282a421216b69faf919e] In section 115 of the Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007 (orders under Part 1 of the 2000 Act), omit subsections (3) and (4).

#### Local Government (Wales) Measure 2009 (nawm 2)

**4.**[^key-b04d8474a243fad1f3bead1c4586611e] In the 2009 Measure, in Schedule 2 (minor and consequential amendments) omit paragraphs 1 and 2 and the cross-heading preceding them.

#### Local Government (Wales) Measure 2011 (nawm 4)

**5.**[^key-a0a876bf356b1cdf51cbaafa8c4dc27a] In the 2011 Measure, omit section 126(2) and (3) (which amend sections 2 and 5 of the 2000 Act).

#### Localism Act 2011 (c. 20)

**6.**[^key-0978290dd7814331835fb406e51d13c2] In the Localism Act 2011, in Schedule 1 (consequential amendments) omit paragraph 3.

#### Well-being of Future Generations (Wales) Act 2015 (anaw 2)

**7.**[^key-f02c8191cb3d82a2f7b67ba729dd9c26] In the Well-being of Future Generations (Wales) Act 2015, in Schedule 4 (public services boards: consequential amendments and repeals) omit paragraphs 3 and 4.

#### This Act

**8.**[^key-b9d675c511a7da8f7a1e1ef525514fa8] In Schedule 14 to this Act, omit paragraph 2.

### PART 2 — Amendments relating to Chapter 2 of Part 2: eligible community councils

#### Local Government Act 1972 (c. 70)

**9.**[^key-9cc2b7893c389ee98cb754fb8504774c] In the 1972 Act, in section 137(9) (power of local authorities to incur expenditure for certain purposes not otherwise authorised), in paragraph (b) after “community council” insert “ which is not an eligible community council for the purposes of Part 2 of the Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021 (general power of competence) ”.

#### Local Government Act 2000 (c. 22)

**10.**[^key-847f0234653d830fdeae616019740002] In the 2000 Act, omit sections 2 and 3 (community councils' power to promote well-being).

#### Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 (c. 41)

**11.**[^key-b02c3d8b368b21dd24af41d154a041e6] (1) The Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 is amended as follows.

(2) In section 55 (late claims: refusal of support), in subsection (4) omit paragraph (c) (but not the “and” which follows it).

(3) In Schedule 3 (withholding and withdrawal of support), in paragraph 1(1) omit paragraph (k).

#### Local Government Act 2003 (c. 26)

**12.**[^key-2d5a9e230e7cbbfe12c0e014ff745257] (1) The Local Government Act 2003 is amended as follows.

(2) In section 93 (power to charge for discretionary services), in subsection (7) omit paragraph (c).

(3) In Schedule 3 (amendment of certain powers), omit paragraph 12.

#### Local Government (Wales) Measure 2011 (nawm 4)

**13.**[^key-ea1c50851d4886de7c921a1f01694f5c] (1) The 2011 Measure is amended as follows.

(2) Omit section 127 (enactments preventing a community council from exercising its well-being power).

(3) Omit Chapter 9 of Part 7 (quality accreditation in community government).

(4) In section 172 (orders and regulations)—

- (a) in subsection (2)(a), omit “or 140”;
- (b) in subsection (2)(b) omit “127 or”.

(5) Omit section 173 (procedure applicable to certain orders under section 127).

#### Localism Act 2011 (c. 20)

**14.**[^key-8ff73c4dda9a14891d2953b9db75ac0a] In the Localism Act 2011, in Schedule 1 (consequential amendments) omit paragraphs 2 and 4 and the cross-heading preceding paragraph 2.

#### This Act

**15.**[^key-d911b574ea369cbcc1d7fb73ab5bf86d] In Part 1 of this Schedule, in paragraph 1 omit sub-paragraphs (2) and (3).
