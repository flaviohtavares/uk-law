# Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021, section 161

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2021/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 161. Head of democratic services

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-233bfb8f9041de2d01315af61435ed8c] In section 8 of the 2011 Measure, after subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) A local authority must have regard to any guidance issued by the Welsh Ministers about the local authority's function under subsection (1)(b).

(2)[^key-bc0d83cc667a216a0cf3438019411b7f] In subsection (4) of that section—

- (a) omit paragraph (b);
- (b) in paragraph (c), for “that section” substitute “ section 5 of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 ”.

(3)[^key-bc0d83cc667a216a0cf3438019411b7f] In section 43(2) of the Localism Act 2011 (c. 20) (meaning of “**chief officer**” for the purposes of pay policy statements), at the end of paragraph (e) insert

> ;
>
> - (f) its head of democratic services designated under section 8(1) of the Local Government (Wales) Measure 2011 (designation by council of a county or county borough in Wales).
