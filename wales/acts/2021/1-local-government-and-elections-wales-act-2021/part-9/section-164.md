# Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021, section 164

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2021/1

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 164. Directions under section 48 of the 2013 Act[^c-8989c0d6541f8f285ecc02f7fffeee7e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 48 of the 2013 Act (directions and guidance relating to reviews under Part 3 of that Act) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (2)—

- (a) in paragraph (a) for “(including, where the Commission has made recommendations or proposals to them, further reviews)” substitute “ (regardless of whether in the circumstances the Commission would have the power, or would be subject to a duty, to conduct the review) ”;
- (b) after paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (aa) where the Commission has made recommendations or proposals to the Welsh Ministers, to conduct a further review under this Part,
  > - (ab) to stop conducting a review under this Part,

  ;
- (c) in paragraph (b) for “section 28” substitute “ this Part ”.

(3) In subsection (5)—

- (a) omit “to” in the first place it occurs;
- (b) in paragraph (a), at the beginning insert “ to ”;
- (c) after paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (aa) to stop conducting a review under section 25 or 31,
  > - (ab) not to conduct a review under section 25 or 31 during a period specified in the direction,

  ;
- (d) in paragraph (b), at the beginning insert “ to ”.
