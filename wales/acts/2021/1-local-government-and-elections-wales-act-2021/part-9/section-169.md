# Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021, section 169

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 169. National Park authorities: disapplication of the 2009 Measure[^c-460b16ec2d07cebd7ae4d07bf2cbb623]

*Extent: E+W*

In the 2009 Measure omit—

- (a) section 1(b) (meaning of “Welsh improvement authority”);
- (b) section 4(3)(b) and (4)(c) (aspects of improvement);
- (c) section 11(1)(e) (meaning of “powers of collaboration”).
