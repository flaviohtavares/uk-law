# Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021, section 118

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2021/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 118. Proceedings etc.[^c-3ade9fba4194e8b12b3e1deb745221c5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 83 of the 2011 Measure (proceedings) is amended as follows.

(2) For subsections (1) and (2) substitute—

> (1) A meeting of a governance and audit committee is to be chaired—
>
> - (a) by the committee chair, or
> - (b) if the committee chair is absent, by the deputy chair.
>
> (2) If both the committee chair and the deputy chair are absent the committee may appoint a member of the committee who is not a member of the local authority's executive, or an assistant to its executive, to chair the meeting.

(3) Omit subsection (8).

(4) In Schedule 6 to this Act (assistants to executives), omit paragraph 6(5).

(5) In Schedule 10 to this Act (consequential amendments relating to governance and audit committees), omit paragraph 5(a) and (b).
