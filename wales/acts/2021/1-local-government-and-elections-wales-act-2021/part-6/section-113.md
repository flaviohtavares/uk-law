# Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021, section 113

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2021/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 113. Disapplication of the 2009 Measure in relation to principal councils and repeal of provisions about coordination of audit[^key-4bb482aab746349ac80fb18bb265b916]

*Extent: E+W*

In the 2009 Measure omit—

- (a) section 1(a) (meaning of “Welsh improvement authority”);
- (b) section 4(3)(a) (aspects of improvement);
- (c) section 11(1)(b) and (2) (meaning of “powers of collaboration”);
- (d) section 16(2)(a) and (b) (meaning of “**relevant regulators**” and “**relevant functions**”);
- (e) section 22(5) (special inspection reports relating to housing benefit);
- (f) section 23 (coordination of audit);
- (g) section 25(4)(d) (statement of practice by the Auditor General for Wales);
- (h) section 33 (information sharing); and in consequence, in section 159 of this Act omit subsection (10).
