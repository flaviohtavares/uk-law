# Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2021/1

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. Duty to notify when resolution passed[^c-f8b287533e2edcc71e69d43862fb41b2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a principal council exercises its power to change the voting system under section 8, the council must notify the Welsh Ministers and the Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru[^c-7151ff299a20b49d8ce982b8ae3ea996] of the change.

(2) The notification must—

- (a) be made within a period of 14 days beginning on the day on which the resolution under section 9 was passed,
- (b) confirm that the council has passed a resolution in accordance with section 9,
- (c) specify the voting system that is to apply, and
- (d) specify the date on which the resolution was passed.
