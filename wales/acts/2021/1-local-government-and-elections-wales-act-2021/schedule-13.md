# Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021, SCHEDULE 13

## SCHEDULE 13 — Abolition of polls consequent on community meetings under the 1972 Act

*Extent: E+W*

(introduced by section 162)

### Local Government Act 1972 (c. 70)

**1.**[^key-55bbf65522f049d0892eb52be8f9e78c] The 1972 Act is amended as follows.

**2.**[^key-7d9dc9645547ad84a148af5b1bdf16bf] Omit sections 33B and 33C (principal council's response to a poll demanded at a community meeting).

**3.**[^key-aa6cddd20dac3c98bd7e9ace552c580f] In section 150(7) (expenses of polls)—

- (a) omit, in the second place it occurs, “or community”;
- (b) after “meeting”, in the second place it occurs, insert “ or of a community governance poll (as to which, see paragraph 34(8) of Schedule 12) ”.

**4.**[^key-03e9790412005ced6c8f7707c5f09c19] In section 243(3) (computation of time)—

- (a) omit “or community”;
- (b) after “meeting” insert “ or a community governance poll (as to which, see paragraph 34(8) of Schedule 12) ”.

**5.**[^key-2ab9c25aed5b18f4e50e3ac184ec444f] In Schedule 12, omit paragraphs 26A and 29A (response by community council to community poll).

**6.**[^key-22c89c2083ed1899c134e51afb87059c] (1) In Schedule 12, paragraph 34 (the making of decisions by community meetings) is amended as follows.

(2) In sub-paragraph (1), for “poll consequent thereon” substitute “ community governance poll ”.

(3) In sub-paragraph (2)—

- (a) omit “, in the first instance,”;
- (b) omit “unless a poll is demanded”.

(4) Omit sub-paragraph (4).

(5) For sub-paragraphs (5) and (6) substitute—

> (5) The Welsh Ministers may by regulations make provision about the conduct of community governance polls.
>
> (6) Regulations under sub-paragraph (5) may apply any enactment relating to elections or referendums (with or without modifications) to community governance polls.
>
> (7) A statutory instrument containing regulations under sub-paragraph (5) is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of Senedd Cymru.

(6) At the end of paragraph 34 insert—

> (8) In this Part of this Schedule, “**community governance poll**” means a poll held on a proposal of a kind mentioned in section 27A, 27C, 27E, 27G, 27I or 27K.

**7.**[^key-25397b99063615d1abcde40bd130d696] In paragraph 37 of Schedule 12 (lending of ballot boxes etc.), in sub-paragraph (1) for “poll consequent on a community meeting” substitute “ community governance poll ”.

**8.**[^key-12db9c5b5e93830982613450ac66c796] In paragraph 38 of Schedule 12 (offences) for “poll consequent on a community meeting” substitute “ community governance poll ”.

**9.**[^key-2b9e5481e00122268cb7926473f1cad6] In Schedule 12, omit paragraphs 38A and 38B (notification of principal council of result of poll consequent on a community meeting).

### Local Government and Housing Act 1989 (c. 42)

**10.**[^key-168cb9c9d37936687414d69f21d37054] In the Local Government and Housing Act 1989, in section 5 omit subsection (8B) (functions of monitoring officers in relation to polls consequent on a community meeting).

### Local Government (Wales) Measure 2011 (nawm 4)

**11.**[^key-056c0a26616b82bfd8de9895df1dbd4d] In the 2011 Measure, omit sections 93 to 99.

### This Act

**12.**[^key-0531119c57142cb454fdfd043c70fabe] In this Act, omit paragraph 1(8) of Schedule 2 (amendment of paragraph 34 of Schedule 12 to the 1972 Act).
