# Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021, section 173

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2021/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 173. Power to make consequential and transitional provision etc.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the Welsh Ministers consider it necessary or expedient for the purposes of, in consequence of, or for giving full effect to any provision of this Act, they may by regulations make—

- (a) supplementary, incidental or consequential provision;
- (b) transitional, transitory or saving provision.

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) may amend, modify, repeal or revoke any enactment.
