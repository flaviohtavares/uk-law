# Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021, section 141

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2021/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 141. Requirement on principal councils to provide information etc. to other bodies[^c24522271]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-a4003d660596952c2bca873160419c4c] The Welsh Ministers may direct a principal council (“council A”) to provide a relevant body with any information or documents which the Welsh Ministers consider appropriate—

- (a) for the purposes of considering whether to transfer the functions of council A to another principal council (“council B”) or to a new principal council,
- (b) for the purposes of giving effect to such a transfer, or
- (c) otherwise in connection with such a transfer.

(2)[^key-a4003d660596952c2bca873160419c4c] The following are relevant bodies—

- (a) any other principal council (including council B) whose area will be, or is likely to be, affected by any merger regulations or restructuring regulations made in respect of council A;
- (b) any transition committee established by council A (see Schedule 11);
- (c) if a new principal area containing all or part of council A's area is to be constituted, the shadow council for the new principal area.

(3)[^key-22e0106b8d6d9d3d9e537de3acd03cd8] The Welsh Ministers may also direct council B to provide another relevant body or council A with any information or documents which the Welsh Ministers consider appropriate as mentioned in subsection (1)(a), (b) or (c).
