# Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021, section 140

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2021/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 140. Requirement on principal councils to provide information etc. to the Welsh Ministers[^c24522231][^c24522241]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may direct a principal council (“council A”) to provide them with any information or documents they consider appropriate—

- (a)[^c-3133603008ac53126ec0d544fe0f3a06] for the purposes of considering whether to transfer the functions of council A to another principal council (“council B”) or to a new principal council,
- (b) for the purposes of giving effect to such a transfer, or
- (c) otherwise in connection with such a transfer.

(2)[^c-6a8ef26e0be6f55622dcabe2c9da9656] The Welsh Ministers may also direct council B to provide the Welsh Ministers with any information or documents which they consider appropriate as mentioned in subsection (1)(a), (b) or (c).
