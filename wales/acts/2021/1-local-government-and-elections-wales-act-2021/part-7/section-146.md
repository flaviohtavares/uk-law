# Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021, section 146

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2021/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 146. Guidance

*Extent: E+W*

The following must have regard to any guidance issued by the Welsh Ministers for the purposes of this Part (and see section 123 in relation to guidance on merger applications)—

- (a) a principal council (including a shadow council and a shadow executive);
- (b) a transition committee (as to which, see Schedule 11);
- (c) a committee or body established under section 134(2)(a) or (b);
- (d) a public body—
  - (i) established by provision included in regulations under section 147(6)(a) or (b);
  - (ii) to which provision included in regulations under section 147(6)(a) or (b) relates.
