## 16. Relationship with the Auditor General[^key-0a4540c27ee2bfe582b799f646e360f2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Auditor General is to be the chief executive (but not an employee) of the WAO.

(2) Schedule 2 contains further provision about the relationship between the WAO and the Auditor General.
