# Public Audit (Wales) Act 2013, section 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2013/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. WAO to monitor and provide advice

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-5783fff288f7186dee1442951979ec5d] The WAO must, in such manner as it considers appropriate, monitor the exercise of the Auditor General's functions.

(2)[^c-f7d87c640391d03aaaac0d47761b23f5][^c-826a1b9c6e2e453fd8dd5c3f4a74ff30] The WAO may provide advice to the Auditor General about the Auditor General's functions.

(3)[^c-f7d87c640391d03aaaac0d47761b23f5][^c-826a1b9c6e2e453fd8dd5c3f4a74ff30] The Auditor General must have regard to any advice given.
