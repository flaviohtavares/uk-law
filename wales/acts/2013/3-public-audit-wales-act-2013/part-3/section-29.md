# Public Audit (Wales) Act 2013, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2013/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29. Indemnification

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-fdc15b465ac4f1b12fbb23cc6dabc9fb] There is to be charged on and paid out of the Welsh Consolidated Fund any amount payable by an indemnified person in consequence of any liability for a breach of duty.

(2)[^key-fdc15b465ac4f1b12fbb23cc6dabc9fb] The liability must not be to another indemnified person.

(3) The following are indemnified persons—

- (a)[^key-c4d18fe2231ee3f8eb1a31fb12df0b6a] a former or current Auditor General appointed under this Act;
- (b)[^key-fdc15b465ac4f1b12fbb23cc6dabc9fb] the WAO;
- (c)[^key-fdc15b465ac4f1b12fbb23cc6dabc9fb] a former or current member of the WAO;
- (d)[^key-c4d18fe2231ee3f8eb1a31fb12df0b6a] a former or current employee of the WAO;
- (e)[^key-c4d18fe2231ee3f8eb1a31fb12df0b6a] a person who is providing, or has provided, services to the Auditor General or to the WAO under arrangements made by the WAO.

(4)[^key-fdc15b465ac4f1b12fbb23cc6dabc9fb] A breach of duty for the purposes of subsection (1) means a breach of duty (whether under a contract or agreement or otherwise, and whether by reason of an act or omission) incurred by an indemnified person in exercising functions that fall to be exercised by that person in accordance with the provision of an enactment.
