Public Audit (Wales) Act 2013

2013 anaw 3

An Act of the National Assembly for Wales to make provision reforming audit arrangements in Wales; continuing the office of Auditor General for Wales and creating a new body to be known as the Wales Audit Office; providing for the Auditor General for Wales to audit local government bodies in Wales; and for connected purposes.

\[29 April 2013\]

**Having been passed by the National Assembly for Wales and having received the assent of Her Majesty, it is enacted as follows:**
