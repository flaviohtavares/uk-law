# Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru etc. Act 2013, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2025-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2013/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. Funding

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may pay grants to the Commission of such amounts as they may determine.

(2) A grant is made subject to any conditions specified by the Welsh Ministers (including conditions about repayment).
