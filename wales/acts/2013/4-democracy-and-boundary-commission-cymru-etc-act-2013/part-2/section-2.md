## 2. Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru[^c-7219a4575732b23eb75591b8e618aa62]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The body corporate called the Local Government Boundary Commission for Wales (established under section 53 of the 1972 Act) is to continue in existence.

(2)[^c-2415eeece6a05773dcfa3a9dfc366bba] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-09ca4ee69007650bc4ce33a34122e7f8] That body corporate (which was first renamed by subsection (2)) is renamed the Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru (referred to in this Act as “the Commission).
