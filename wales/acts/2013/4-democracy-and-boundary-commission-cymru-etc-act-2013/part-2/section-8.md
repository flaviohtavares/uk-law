# Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru etc. Act 2013, section 8

## 8. Chief executive

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Commission must employ a chief executive.

(2) The chief executive is to be appointed by the Commission[^c-963bed75e3d129870c945143a57452f2] on such terms and conditions as it[^c-5e3ce3311b0020f4a0d1199237d3678c] may determine (including conditions as to remuneration, pension, allowances and expenses).

(2A)[^c-03a185e8ef1e02f90169070c1bafea07] But if the office of chief executive has been vacant for more than six months, the Welsh Ministers may appoint a chief executive on such terms and conditions as they may determine (including conditions as to remuneration, pension, allowances and expenses).

(3) Before appointing a chief executive under subsection (2A),[^c-64ccb7296c16afec513f55b1e25ce67e] the Welsh Ministers must consult the Commission.

(4)[^c-166cb77d2fc442d19dae54a9dc5e0d08] The chief executive may not be—

- (a)[^c-0646cba866d0add15c5e51fcb2ac70d6] a member of a UK legislature;
- (ba)[^c-51ae4f23ad208dad8936d8688e8a75c4] a‍ person engaged by a member of‍ a UK legislature, under a contract of service or a contract for services, in connection with the carrying out of the member’s functions;
- (bb) a person engaged by a registered political party under a contract of service or a contract for services;
- (bc) a special advise‍r;
- (c) a member or member of staff[^c-ba701a34e72ea00252626a5dc20243a7] of a local authority;
- (d)[^c-91a2cfcd7cd4725a0582a1d5d9603f1d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) a member or member of staff[^c-cf0d97ba7e12d50e689958d8a82c01c8] of a National Park authority for a National Park in Wales;
- (ea)[^c-3998a958ed9ebf23cf2662ceca0f89c5] a member or member of staff of a corporate joint committee established by regulations made under Part 5 of the [Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021 (asc 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2021/1);
- (eb) a member or member of staff of a fire and rescue authority constituted by a scheme under section 2 of the Fire and Rescue Services Act [2004 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/21), or a scheme to which section 4 of that Act applies;
- (f) a police and crime commissioner for a police area in Wales.

(5) The Commission must, in exercising its functions under this section, have regard to any guidance issued by the Welsh Ministers.
