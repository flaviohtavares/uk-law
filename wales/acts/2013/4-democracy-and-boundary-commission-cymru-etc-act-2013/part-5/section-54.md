# Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru etc. Act 2013, section 54

## 54. Restriction on payments in relation to promoting or opposing Bills

*Extent: E+W*

A local authority may not make a payment to any of its members for acting as counsel or agent in promoting or opposing a Bill under section 52 or 53.
