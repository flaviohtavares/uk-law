# Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru etc. Act 2013, section 61

## 61. Audit committees

*Extent: E+W*

In section 82 of the 2011 Measure (membership), after subsection (6) insert—

> (7) An audit committee is to be treated as a body to which section 15 of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 (duty to allocate seats to political groups) applies.
