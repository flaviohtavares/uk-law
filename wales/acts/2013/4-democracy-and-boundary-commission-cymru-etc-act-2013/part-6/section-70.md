# Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru etc. Act 2013, section 70

## 70. Ancillary provision

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may by order make such incidental, consequential, supplemental, transitional, transitory or savings provision as they consider appropriate for the purposes of, or in connection with, giving full effect to, any provision made by or under this Act.

(2) An order under this section may modify this or any other enactment.
