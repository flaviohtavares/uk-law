# Mobile Homes (Wales) Act 2013, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2013/6
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Minimum length of notice[^key-cbe85ab008a8d98eb7ce9192b27575ed]

*Extent: E+W*

In any case where a residential contract is determinable by notice given by either party to the other, a notice is of no effect unless it is given not less than 4 weeks before the date on which it is to take effect.
