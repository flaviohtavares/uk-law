## 35. Guidance by Welsh Ministers[^key-a973f1aea92b88a6b99e962ed0df6997]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may issue guidance to local authorities as to the performance of their functions under this Part.

(2) Local authorities must have regard to any guidance issued under subsection (1).
