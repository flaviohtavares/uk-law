## 26. Revocation on death, change of ownership or cessation of use[^c-ff54a37894568d6cf2711915faf91853]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) When the holder of a site licence in respect of any land dies or ceases to be the owner of the land, the site licence is revoked.

(2) Where land in respect of which a site licence is in force ceases to be used as a regulated site, the site licence is revoked.
