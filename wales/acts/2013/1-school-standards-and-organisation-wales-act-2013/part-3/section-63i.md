## 63I. Interpretation of Chapter 3A[^c-63280d7b9111ad815ea5808eb8e76682]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Chapter—

- “**the Code**” (“***y Cod***”) means the code on school organisation issued under section 38(1);
- “**the Commission**” (“*y Comisiwn*) means the Commission for Tertiary Education and Research.
