# School Standards and Organisation (Wales) Act 2013, section 65

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2013/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 65. Direction to consider making regional provision[^c-591f42c1eee489366e509d5f66da8dc6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may direct local authorities to consider whether they (or any of them) would be able to carry out their special education functions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-43b30c13151e7788b2c31b58184b9596] additional learning needs functions, in respect of children with the special educational needs *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-69f7d38100bf004603ab99af3dfee459] additional learning needs specified in the direction, more efficiently or effectively if regional provision were made.

(2) The authorities to whom a direction is given must report their conclusions to the Welsh Ministers no later than the time specified in the direction.

(3) A direction under this section may be given to local authorities generally or to one or more authorities specified in the direction.
