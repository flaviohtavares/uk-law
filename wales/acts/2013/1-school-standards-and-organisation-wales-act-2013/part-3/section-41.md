## 41. Proposals to establish mainstream schools[^key-861fcf25ca103da07f20031d2c41ffd1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority may make proposals to establish—

- (a) a new community school, or
- (b) a new maintained nursery school.

(2) Any person may make proposals to establish a new voluntary school.

(3)[^key-8b0fece98609a650b14167685ba11288] See section 22(1) of the Welsh Language and Education Act (Wales) 2025 (asc 2) for provision about a register of the proposed language category of schools for which proposals have been made under this section.
