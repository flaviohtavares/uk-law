## 101. Short title and inclusion as one of the Education Acts

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The short title of this Act is the School Standards and Organisation (Wales) Act 2013.

(2) This Act is to be included in the list of Education Acts set out in section 578 of the Education Act 1996.
