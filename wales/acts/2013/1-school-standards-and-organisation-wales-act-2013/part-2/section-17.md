## 17. General power to give directions and take steps[^c-d3d6370a8ea1afed54da5dfe5ef83c88][^c-baf168d4d0b1587a89f7a3cbcb582a7a][^c-b5783297d72e1599d14b4fc9f1f75afa][^c-c462eed24bcd467131ae427d125075a7][^c-7cbc49217c3e6df1112fb195ceddb694][^c-be660f062e248bf1d5f3d45a458f12c5][^c-d24085b28597cbcb90d58feedb4a7513][^c-baf168d4d0b1587a89f7a3cbcb582a7a][^c-b5783297d72e1599d14b4fc9f1f75afa][^c-c462eed24bcd467131ae427d125075a7][^c-7cbc49217c3e6df1112fb195ceddb694][^c-be660f062e248bf1d5f3d45a458f12c5][^c-d24085b28597cbcb90d58feedb4a7513]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if the Welsh Ministers have the power to intervene in the conduct of a maintained school.

(2) If the Welsh Ministers think it is appropriate for the purposes of dealing with the grounds for intervention, the Welsh Ministers may—

- (a) give directions to the governing body or head teacher, or
- (b) take any other steps.
