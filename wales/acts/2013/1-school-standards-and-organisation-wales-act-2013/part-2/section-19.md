## 19. Directions[^key-7b06b7e107e972222e1b75ce97dec5a3][^key-baf168d4d0b1587a89f7a3cbcb582a7a][^key-b5783297d72e1599d14b4fc9f1f75afa][^key-c462eed24bcd467131ae427d125075a7][^key-7cbc49217c3e6df1112fb195ceddb694][^key-be660f062e248bf1d5f3d45a458f12c5][^key-d24085b28597cbcb90d58feedb4a7513][^key-baf168d4d0b1587a89f7a3cbcb582a7a][^key-b5783297d72e1599d14b4fc9f1f75afa][^key-c462eed24bcd467131ae427d125075a7][^key-7cbc49217c3e6df1112fb195ceddb694][^key-be660f062e248bf1d5f3d45a458f12c5][^key-d24085b28597cbcb90d58feedb4a7513]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A governing body of a maintained school or a head teacher subject to a direction under this Chapter must comply with it.

(2) This includes a direction to exercise a power or duty that is contingent upon the opinion of the governing body or head teacher.

(3) A direction under this Chapter—

- (a) must be in writing;
- (b) may be varied or revoked by a later direction;
- (c) is enforceable by mandatory order on application by, or on behalf of, the person who gave the direction.
