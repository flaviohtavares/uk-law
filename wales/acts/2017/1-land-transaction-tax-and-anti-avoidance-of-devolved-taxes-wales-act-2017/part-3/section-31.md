# Land Transaction Tax and Anti-avoidance of Devolved Taxes (Wales) Act 2017, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-02-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2017/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31. Reliefs: anti-avoidance[^c-8e7f35756acd2056aa64a9ff92f1dbbc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Relief is not available under any of the provisions mentioned in subsection (2) or (3) of section 30 in respect of a land transaction—

- (a) which is a tax avoidance arrangement, or
- (b) which forms part of arrangements which are tax avoidance arrangements.

(2) An arrangement is a ““tax avoidance arrangement”” if—

- (a) the obtaining of a tax advantage for any person is the main purpose, or one of the main purposes, of the buyer in the land transaction entering into the arrangement, and
- (b) the arrangement lacks genuine economic or commercial substance other than the obtaining of a tax advantage.

(3) In this section—

- “**"arrangement”**” (“**"*trefniant***”") includes any transaction, scheme, agreement, grant, understanding, promise, undertaking or series of any of those things (whether or not legally enforceable);
- “**"tax”**” (“**"*treth***”") means land transaction tax, income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty land tax, stamp duty reserve tax or stamp duty;
- “**"tax advantage”**” (“**"*mantais drethiannol***”") means—
  - (a) relief or increased relief from tax,
  - (b) repayment or increased repayment of tax,
  - (c) avoidance or reduction of a charge to tax, or
  - (d) deferral of a payment of tax or advancement of a repayment of tax.
