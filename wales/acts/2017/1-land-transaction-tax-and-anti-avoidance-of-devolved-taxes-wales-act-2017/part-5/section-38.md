## 38. Joint buyers: returns and declarations[^c-e21f8bedc7b7838049fbdbde5a8f6895]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the transaction is a notifiable transaction, a single return is required.

(2) The declaration required by section 53 (declaration that return is complete and correct) must be made by all the buyers.
