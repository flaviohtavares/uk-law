# Land Transaction Tax and Anti-avoidance of Devolved Taxes (Wales) Act 2017, section 65

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-02-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2017/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 65. Registration of land transactions

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-549c20fb9204387fb41aef4dea0987cf] The Chief Land Registrar (““the Registrar””) may not register, record or otherwise reflect in an entry made in the register of title maintained by the Registrar a notifiable land transaction or a document effecting or evidencing such a transaction unless a WRA certificate is produced with the application to register, record or otherwise reflect the transaction.

(2)[^key-549c20fb9204387fb41aef4dea0987cf] A ““WRA certificate”” is a certificate issued by WRA stating that a return has been made in respect of the transaction.

(3)[^key-549c20fb9204387fb41aef4dea0987cf] But subsection (1) does not apply in so far as—

- (a) the notifiable land transaction or a document effecting or evidencing that transaction is required to be registered, recorded or otherwise reflected in an entry made in the register of title without any application to register;
- (b) the entry registers, records or otherwise reflects an interest or right other than the chargeable interest acquired by the buyer in the land transaction.

(4)[^key-549c20fb9204387fb41aef4dea0987cf] This section does not apply to—

- (a) a contract which is to be treated as a land transaction by virtue of—
  - (i) section 10(4) (contract and transfer), or
  - (ii) section 11(3) (contract providing for transfer to third party);
- (b) a notional or additional notional transaction within the meaning given in Schedule 2;
- (c) an agreement for a lease which is to be treated as a land transaction by virtue of paragraph 20(1) of Schedule 6;
- (d) a variation of a lease which is to be treated as a land transaction by virtue of paragraph 24(1) or 25(1) of that Schedule.

(5)[^key-856a582f6af324d4c98e0070ca1acd5a] The Welsh Ministers may by regulations make provision about WRA certificates.

(6)[^key-856a582f6af324d4c98e0070ca1acd5a] Regulations made under subsection (5) may in particular—

- (a) make provision as to the conditions that must be met before a certificate is issued;
- (b) make provision about the issue of duplicate certificates;
- (c) provide for the issue of multiple certificates where a return is made relating to more than one transaction.

(7) The Registrar—

- (a)[^key-549c20fb9204387fb41aef4dea0987cf] must allow WRA to inspect any certificates produced under this section, and
- (b)[^key-856a582f6af324d4c98e0070ca1acd5a] may enter into arrangements for affording WRA other information and facilities for verifying that the requirements of this Act have been complied with.
