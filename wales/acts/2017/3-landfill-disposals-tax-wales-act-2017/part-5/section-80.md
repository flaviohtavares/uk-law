# Landfill Disposals Tax (Wales) Act 2017, section 80

> Text as at 2022-09-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 80. Reviews and appeals relating to designation of groups of companies[^c-f37cd9bfeca95cae8370372aee9f0c69]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 172 of TCMA (appealable decisions), in subsection (2), after paragraph (j) (inserted by section 58 of this Act) insert—

> - (k) a decision relating to the designation of a group of bodies corporate for the purposes of landfill disposals tax.
