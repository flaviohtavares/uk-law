# Landfill Disposals Tax (Wales) Act 2017, section 58

> Text as at 2022-09-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Reviews and appeals relating to designation of non-disposal areas[^c-4f240bd3874fd699e75509fd227e661d]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 172 of TCMA (appealable decisions), in subsection (2), after paragraph (i) (inserted by section 38 of this Act) insert—

> - (j) a decision relating to the designation of a non-disposal area for the purposes of landfill disposals tax;

.
