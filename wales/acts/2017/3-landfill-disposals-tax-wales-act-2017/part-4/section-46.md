# Landfill Disposals Tax (Wales) Act 2017, section 46

> Text as at 2022-09-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. Calculation of tax chargeable on taxable disposal

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-c4e97e813a04c1ee1a76c21f715efd7a] This section applies to a taxable disposal of material made at a place that is not, and does not form part of, an authorised landfill site.

(2)[^c-c4e97e813a04c1ee1a76c21f715efd7a] The amount of tax chargeable on the disposal is to be calculated by multiplying the taxable weight of the material in tonnes by the unauthorised disposals rate.

(3)[^c-c4e97e813a04c1ee1a76c21f715efd7a] The taxable weight of the material is the weight of the material determined by WRA using any method it thinks appropriate.

(4)[^c-236820bdcfdbf87c47846e9aa35a227d][^c-0a07b3c5f1180327d7bfde1d3f10c093] The unauthorised disposals rate is the rate per tonne prescribed for the purposes of subsection (2) in regulations.

(5)[^c-c4e97e813a04c1ee1a76c21f715efd7a] Regulations under subsection (4) may prescribe different rates for different descriptions of material.
