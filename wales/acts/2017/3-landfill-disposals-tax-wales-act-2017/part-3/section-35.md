# Landfill Disposals Tax (Wales) Act 2017, section 35

> Text as at 2022-09-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Duty to be registered

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-71fa1d8d8d2ef4a697116eb5a2f199f7] A person who carries out taxable operations must be registered with WRA.

(2)[^key-306c20af5bd37cf36fd56a9de4662e0b] A person who intends to carry out taxable operations but is not registered—

- (a) must apply in writing to WRA to be registered, and
- (b) must do so at least 14 days before the day on which the person begins to carry out taxable operations.

(3)[^key-306c20af5bd37cf36fd56a9de4662e0b] WRA must register the person if satisfied that the application—

- (a) contains the information WRA requires to register the person, and
- (b) is in the form (if any) specified by WRA.

(4)[^key-306c20af5bd37cf36fd56a9de4662e0b] WRA must issue a notice to the person of its decision on the application.

(5)[^key-306c20af5bd37cf36fd56a9de4662e0b] If WRA registers the person, the notice must set out the person's entry in the register.
