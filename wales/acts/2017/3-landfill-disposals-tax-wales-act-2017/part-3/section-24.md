# Landfill Disposals Tax (Wales) Act 2017, section 24

> Text as at 2022-09-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Reviews and appeals relating to method for determining weight of material[^key-c68e12bf906051110eadf190a879c920]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 172 of TCMA (appealable decisions), in subsection (2), after paragraph (g) (inserted by paragraph 62 of Schedule 23 to LTTA) insert—

> - (h) a decision relating to the method to be used by the operator of an authorised landfill site to determine the weight of material for the purposes of landfill disposals tax;

.
