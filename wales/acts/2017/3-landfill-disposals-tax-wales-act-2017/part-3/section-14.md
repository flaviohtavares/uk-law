# Landfill Disposals Tax (Wales) Act 2017, section 14

> Text as at 2022-09-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Calculation of tax chargeable on taxable disposal

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-30d482fe4ef887bf12e799ad2af113fe] This section applies to a taxable disposal of material made at an authorised landfill site.

(2)[^c-30d482fe4ef887bf12e799ad2af113fe] The amount of tax chargeable on the disposal is to be calculated by multiplying the taxable weight of the material in tonnes by the standard rate.

(3)[^c-f1636074e0c01a91c9820872cf761452][^c-9e8e93f357089bd0c329cfb662235089] The standard rate is the rate per tonne prescribed for the purposes of subsection (2) in regulations.

(4)[^c-30d482fe4ef887bf12e799ad2af113fe] Subsection (2) does not apply to the disposal if the material disposed of—

- (a) consists entirely of one or more qualifying materials (see section 15), or
- (b) is a qualifying mixture of materials (see section 16).

(5)[^c-30d482fe4ef887bf12e799ad2af113fe] The amount of tax chargeable on a disposal of that description is instead to be calculated by multiplying the taxable weight of the material in tonnes by the lower rate.

(6)[^c-f1636074e0c01a91c9820872cf761452][^c-9e8e93f357089bd0c329cfb662235089] The lower rate is the rate per tonne prescribed for the purposes of subsection (5) in regulations.

(7)[^c-30d482fe4ef887bf12e799ad2af113fe] Regulations under subsection (3) or (6) may prescribe different rates for different descriptions of material.

(8)[^c-30d482fe4ef887bf12e799ad2af113fe] See section 18 for provision about how the taxable weight of the material in a taxable disposal is to be calculated.
