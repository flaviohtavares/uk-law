# Landfill Disposals Tax (Wales) Act 2017, section 37

> Text as at 2022-09-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Cancellation of registration

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-0f9aac6a60917a650b35012be9b9d7e0] A registered person who ceases to carry out taxable operations must apply in writing to WRA for the cancellation of the person's registration.

(2)[^c-0f9aac6a60917a650b35012be9b9d7e0] The application must be made before the end of the period of 30 days beginning with the day on which the person ceases to carry out taxable operations.

(3)[^c-0f9aac6a60917a650b35012be9b9d7e0] WRA may cancel a person's registration if satisfied that the person has ceased to carry out taxable operations (whether or not the person has applied for the cancellation).

(4)[^c-0f9aac6a60917a650b35012be9b9d7e0] But WRA may not cancel the person's registration unless satisfied that

- (a)[^c-6072c05901ed2bdb6ef89888aed977d1] all of the tax that the person is required to pay has been paid, and[^c-f37c77c1a483789cbcd61f2f005b32d4]
- (b) all of the tax credit to which the person is entitled and which the person has claimed—
  - (i) has been set off against an amount of tax that the person would otherwise be required to pay, or
  - (ii) has been paid to the person.

(5)[^c-5a4ec0997371b061b1cc142b3974b213] WRA may also cancel a person's registration if satisfied that the person has not carried out taxable operations and does not intend to do so.

(6)[^c-5a4ec0997371b061b1cc142b3974b213] If WRA cancels a person's registration, it must issue a notice of the cancellation to the person.
