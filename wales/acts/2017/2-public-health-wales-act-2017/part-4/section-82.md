# Public Health (Wales) Act 2017, section 82

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (47).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2017/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 82. Offences

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-a14314cab2f0dfed86401bea58565983] A person who contravenes section 58 (licensing requirement) commits an offence.

(2)[^c-a14314cab2f0dfed86401bea58565983] A person who contravenes a prohibition specified, under section 61(3)(c), in notice given under section 61(1) (designation of person for purposes of section 58(3)) commits an offence.

(3)[^c-a14314cab2f0dfed86401bea58565983] A person who, without reasonable cause, contravenes the requirement in section 69(2) (approval requirement) commits an offence.

(4)[^c-a14314cab2f0dfed86401bea58565983] A person who, without reasonable cause, contravenes a notice under section 77 (stop notices) commits an offence.

(5)[^c-2717f5386ad56895e7e223940c62a92e] A person who, without reasonable cause, contravenes a notice under section 78 (licence holder remedial action notices) commits an offence.

(6)[^c-2717f5386ad56895e7e223940c62a92e] A person who, without reasonable cause, contravenes a notice under section 79 (premises remedial action notice) commits an offence.

(7)[^c-2717f5386ad56895e7e223940c62a92e] A person who, in an application for the issue, variation or renewal of a special procedure licence or for approval of premises or a vehicle under section 70—

- (a) makes a statement that is false or misleading, and
- (b) either knows, or is reckless as to whether, it is false or misleading,

commits an offence.

(8)[^c-2717f5386ad56895e7e223940c62a92e] In subsection (7), “**false or misleading**” means false or misleading in a material particular.

(9)[^c-2717f5386ad56895e7e223940c62a92e] A person who commits an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine.
