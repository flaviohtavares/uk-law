## 57. What is a special procedure?[^c-fa220295131b61050a162756ec19e954]

*Extent: E+W*

Each of the following is a special procedure for the purposes of this Part—

- (a) acupuncture;
- (b) body piercing;
- (c) electrolysis;
- (d) tattooing.
