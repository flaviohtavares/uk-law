## 67. Grant or refusal of application for renewal[^key-2935984e83a17fede9ffefff6b03c0ef]

*Extent: E+W*

Sections 65, 66 and 68 apply for the purposes of an application to renew a special procedure licence as if that application were an application for the issue of a licence.
