# Public Health (Wales) Act 2017, section 39

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 39. Authorised officers

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

References in this Chapter to an authorised officer are to any person (whether or not an officer of the local authority) authorised by a local authority for the purposes of this Chapter.
