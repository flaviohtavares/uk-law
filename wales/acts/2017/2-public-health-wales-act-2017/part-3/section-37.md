# Public Health (Wales) Act 2017, section 37

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Moveable structures etc.

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Regulations may provide for the application of this Chapter in relation to premises consisting of a moveable structure, a stall, a tent or a vehicle to be subject to such modifications as the Welsh Ministers consider necessary or expedient.
