# Public Health (Wales) Act 2017, section 36

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. Excepted premises

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

The provisions of this Chapter do not apply in relation to a tobacco or nicotine business so far as carried on at premises of a description specified in regulations.
