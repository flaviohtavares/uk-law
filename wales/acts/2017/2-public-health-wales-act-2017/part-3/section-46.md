# Public Health (Wales) Act 2017, section 46

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. Power to make test purchases

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

An authorised officer may make such purchases and arrangements, and secure the provision of such services, as the officer considers necessary for the purposes of this Chapter.
