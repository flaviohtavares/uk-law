## SCHEDULE 2 — SMOKING: CONSEQUENTIAL AMENDMENTS

*Extent: E+W*

(introduced by section 29)

### Health Act 2006 (c.28)

**1.**[^c-7871dd018722a87cfecc2aafbb8619ef] The Health Act 2006 is amended as follows.

**2.**[^c-8fd5f27762ea232752838a37cd2d43f8] In the heading to Chapter 1 of Part 1 after “vehicles” insert “ in England ”.

**3.**[^c-c80d1e8d67e624ec1deb47bf0e459f13] In section 1 (introduction), in subsection (1) after “vehicles” insert “ in England ”.

**4.**[^c-96236f695710638ee0b7b3fa74b3cf12] In section 2 (smoke-free premises)—

- (a) in subsection (1) after “Premises” insert “ in England ”;
- (b) in subsection (2) after “Premises” insert “ in England ”;
- (c) in subsection (5) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “ Secretary of State ”.

**5.**[^c-8567155a5e217f91c1adf6fb70a1462f] In section 3 (smoke-free premises (exemptions)), in subsection (1)—

- (a) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “ Secretary of State ”;
- (b) after “premises” in both places insert “ in England ”.

**6.**[^c-b415741fc77555898f6999f0968a00f7] In section 4 (additional smoke-free places)—

- (a) in subsection (1)—
  - (i) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “ Secretary of State ”;
  - (ii) after “place” in both places insert “ in England ”;
- (b) in subsection (3)—
  - (i) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “ Secretary of State ”;
  - (ii) for “authority's” substitute “Secretary of State's”.

**7.**[^c-0888436d5eeaff9504a8c8f0b7dc6a1c] In section 5 (vehicles), in subsection (1)—

- (a) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “ Secretary of State ”;
- (b) after “vehicles” insert “ in England ”.

**8.**[^c-aa1b3e8d0f1573f36c04ebfc9400a42d] In section 6 (no-smoking signs), in subsections (2), (3) and (4) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “ Secretary of State ”.

**9.**[^c-cbbe6d4c28f196194b87b2358a4b8f19] In section 8 (offence of failing to prevent smoking in smoke-free place), in subsection (3) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “ Secretary of State ”.

**10.**[^c-5bef85bd743565cfde87729d92f3440d] In section 9 (fixed penalties), in subsection (1A) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “ Secretary of State ”.

**11.**[^c-f4cb69a5249a9f09d9e89b3caeef57c6] In section 10 (enforcement)—

- (a) in subsection (1) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “ Secretary of State ”;
- (b) in subsection (4) for “appropriate national authority” in both places substitute “ Secretary of State ”.

**12.**[^c-69dd629f68f2fdac30046004ad048b81] In section 11 (obstruction etc of officers), in subsection (5) for “appropriate national authority” in both places substitute “ Secretary of State ”.

**13.**[^c-74b9771c71e43fcd0f992403ac8f6140] In section 12 (interpretation and territorial sea)—

- (a) in subsection (2) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “ Secretary of State ”;
- (b) omit subsection (3)(b).

**14.**[^c-fdf88f0f36eaed6d507694e1ab946666] In section 82 (interpretation), in subsection (1) omit the definition of “the appropriate national authority”.

**15.**[^c-61746492bba6b3f2d770c4b98036ce1e] In Schedule 1 (fixed penalties), in paragraphs 4 and 17 for “appropriate national authority” substitute “ Secretary of State ”.

**16.**[^c-546373f84f76bfc264c3d6f1aa5350ba] In Schedule 2 (powers of entry, etc), in paragraph 10 for “appropriate national authority” in both places substitute “ Secretary of State ”.
