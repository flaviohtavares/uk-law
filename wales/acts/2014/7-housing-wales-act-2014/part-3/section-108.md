# Housing (Wales) Act 2014, section 108

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (144).** Text as at 2026-06-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2014/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 108. Interpretation[^key-a9a873807e551d5bd6708b673c814d8e]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “**accommodation needs**” (“***anghenion llety***”) includes, but is not limited to, needs with respect to the provision of sites on which mobile homes may be stationed;
- “**Gypsies and Travellers**” (“***Sipsiwn a Theithwyr***”) means—
  - (a) persons of a nomadic habit of life, whatever their race or origin, including—
    - (i) persons who, on grounds only of their own or their family's or dependant's educational or health needs or old age, have ceased to travel temporarily or permanently, and
    - (ii) members of an organised group of travelling show people or circus people (whether or not travelling together as such), and
  - (b) all other persons with a cultural tradition of nomadism or of living in a mobile home;
- “**mobile home**” (“***cartref symudol***”) has the meaning given by section 60 of the Mobile Homes (Wales) Act 2013.
