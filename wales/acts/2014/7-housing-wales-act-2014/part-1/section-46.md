## 46. Regulations about fees[^c-8809d1649b0b72eb53c85e5be60ac022][^c-4c4b7aa6f5bcd583bad1c9dbb03736e1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Regulations made under this Part which prescribe the amount of a fee payable by a person in connection with applications to be registered or licensed may provide that the fee is to be—

- (a) an amount stated in the regulations;
- (b) determined by a person or means specified in the regulations.

(2) Such regulations may prescribe a different fee for different persons.
