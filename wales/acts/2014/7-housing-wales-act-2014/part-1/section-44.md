## 44. Restriction on terminating tenancies[^c-f55c7786ff1e3230c026b1efe5692795]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A notice within subsection (1A)[^c-a179ef783019e045eb828cafb2aaf22c] may not be given in relation to a dwelling subject to a domestic tenancy which is a standard occupation contract[^c-59b9a0931de5ab07633e4e5c05f9fe98] if—

- (a) the landlord is not registered in respect of the dwelling, or
- (b) the landlord is not licensed under this Part for the area in which the dwelling is located and the landlord has not appointed a person who is licensed under this Part to carry out all property management work in respect of the dwelling on the landlord's behalf.

(1A)[^c-8ef7a1d78b09f484ef3beea364f63668] A notice is within this subsection, if it is a notice—

- (a) given under section 173(1) of the Renting Homes (Wales) Act [2016 (anaw 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/1) (landlord’s notice);
- (b) given under section 186(1) of that Act (landlord’s notice in connection with end of term);
- (c) as described in section 194(1) of that Act (landlord’s break clause);
- (d) given under paragraph 25B of Schedule 12 to that Act (landlord’s notice to terminate a converted fixed term standard contract).

(2) Subsection[^c-587b5124c430a8df16155bf0be12544d] (1) does not apply for the period of 28 days beginning with the day on which the landlord's interest in the dwelling is assigned to the landlord.
