## 45. Landlords who are trustees[^key-125d1a128477296fa9260d4fe1a1f08e]

*Extent: E+W*

If trustees constitute a landlord, the landlord may be registered or licensed for the purposes of this Part under a name which is a collective description of the trustees as the trustees of the trust in question.
