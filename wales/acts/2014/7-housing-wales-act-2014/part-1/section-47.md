## 47. Information about applications[^key-d3b8f205dc7d75c1073853dc67a31432]

*Extent: E+W*

A licensing authority must publish information about its requirements relating to—

- (a) the form and content of applications to be registered and licensed;
- (b) information to be provided when making applications.
