## 22. Licence conditions[^key-c3a8ff1102e611cb27df06d072c7e3d1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A licence must be granted subject to a condition that the licence holder complies with any code of practice issued by the Welsh Ministers under section 40.

(2) A licensing authority may grant a licence subject to such further conditions as it considers appropriate.
