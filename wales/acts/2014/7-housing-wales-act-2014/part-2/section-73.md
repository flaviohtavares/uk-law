## 73. Duty to help to secure accommodation for homeless applicants[^c-f136fd9e20b78406f7876af9c59294e0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local housing authority must help to secure that suitable accommodation is available for occupation by an applicant, if the authority is satisfied that the applicant is—

- (a) homeless, and
- (b) eligible for help.

(2) But the duty in subsection (1) does not apply if the authority refers the application to another local housing authority (see section 80).
