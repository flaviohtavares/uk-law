# Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2014/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 48. Exception for provision of housing etc[^key-15e1eeac99678d7d9f5796e4bf86e6c1][^key-875892a3eaf37d1ab536a32411d3abdf]

*Extent: E+W*

A local authority may not meet an adult's needs for care and support (including a carer's needs for support) under sections 35 to 45 or discharge its duty under section 15 by doing anything which that authority or another local authority is required to do under—

- (a) the Housing (Wales) Act 2014[^key-fe5816ec3a65bc41e6a67a8a6a21b8d6], or
- (b) any other enactment specified in regulations.
