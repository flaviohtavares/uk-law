# Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014, section 86

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2014/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 86. ... Homes provided, equipped and maintained by Welsh Ministers or Secretary of State[^c-67bf8103a2fb21987a0dcb357bfc4b1b][^c-aa2cdb1abd5e9a76405a8b0ab39f4886][^M_F_373bbccc-1662-45d7-aaf3-5ca1cea54a8e]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a local authority places a child it is looking after in a [^M_F_373bbccc-1662-45d7-aaf3-5ca1cea54a8e]... home provided, equipped and maintained by the Welsh Ministers or the Secretary of State under section 82(5) of the Children Act 1989, it must do so on such terms and conditions as the Welsh Ministers or the Secretary of State (as the case may be) may from time to time determine.
