# Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014, section 156

## 156. Power to direct exercise of other social services functions[^key-77f48ced82b9c1b00742ca519aaa26f4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the Welsh Ministers think it is expedient, a direction under section 154 or 155 may relate to the performance of social services functions in addition to the functions to which the grounds for intervention relate.

(2) The Welsh Ministers may have regard (among other things) to financial considerations in deciding whether it is expedient that a direction should relate to social services functions other than the functions relating to the grounds for intervention.
