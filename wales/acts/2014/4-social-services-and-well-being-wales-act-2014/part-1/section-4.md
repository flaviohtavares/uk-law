# Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2014/4

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Meaning of “care and support”

*Extent: E+W*

Any reference to care and support in this Act is to be construed as a reference to—

- (a) care;
- (b) support;
- (c) both care and support.
