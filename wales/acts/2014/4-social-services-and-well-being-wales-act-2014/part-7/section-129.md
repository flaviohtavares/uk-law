# Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014, section 129

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (21).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2014/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 129. Abolition of local authority's power to remove persons in need of care and attention[^key-11aa89c87bb72933b72ab9b5ed683cde]

*Extent: E+W*

Section 47 of the National Assistance Act 1948 (which enables local authorities to apply for a court order to remove persons in need of care and attention from home to hospitals or other places) ceases to apply to persons in Wales.
