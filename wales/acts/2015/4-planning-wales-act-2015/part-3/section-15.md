# Planning (Wales) Act 2015, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (36).** Text as at 2025-12-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2015/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. Joint planning boards: functions relating to surveys and local development plans[^c22542711]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-b322877fca511e01bc439679e7a7353b] PCPA 2004 is amended as follows.

(2)[^c-b322877fca511e01bc439679e7a7353b] In section 78 (interpretation of Part 6), for subsection (3) substitute—

> (3) But—
>
> - (a) a National Park authority is the local planning authority for the whole of its area;
> - (b) a joint planning board is the local planning authority for the whole of its united district (and references to the area of a local planning authority are, in relation to such a board, to be construed as references to its united district).

(3)[^c-4f2f64830ea59947c7d07d8acd1f5628] In section 62 (local development plan), in subsection (7) (relevant local well-being plan), after paragraph (b) insert—

> - (c) in the case of an authority which is a joint planning board, the public services board for an area that includes any part of that authority's united district.
