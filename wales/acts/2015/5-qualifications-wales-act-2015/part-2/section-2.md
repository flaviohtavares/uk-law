# Qualifications Wales Act 2015, section 2

> Text as at 2024-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Establishment of Qualifications Wales[^c22547571]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-c3d660e5470dd47821c76697778b1a79] Qualifications Wales is established as a body corporate.

(2)[^key-49776df41e945fc653599e62e901f749][^key-c615677d5765812e5be5756a8fbeb789] Schedule 1 contains further provision about Qualifications Wales.

(3) Schedule 2 makes provision about transfers of staff and property to Qualifications Wales.
