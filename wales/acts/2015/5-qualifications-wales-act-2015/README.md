# Qualifications Wales Act 2015

> Text as at 2024-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

Qualifications Wales Act 2015

2015 anaw 5

An Act of the National Assembly for Wales to establish a new body to be known as Qualifications Wales; providing for Qualifications Wales to be able to recognise bodies responsible for awarding certain qualifications in Wales and to approve certain qualifications awarded in Wales and to perform certain other functions; and for connected purposes.

\[5 August 2015\]

**Having been passed by the National Assembly for Wales and having received the assent of Her Majesty, it is enacted as follows:**
