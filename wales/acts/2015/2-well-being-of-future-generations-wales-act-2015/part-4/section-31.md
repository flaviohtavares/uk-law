## 31. Invitations to participate: issue, response and duration of participation[^key-cae4c4e05c7b19e6628d3e1739f975ed]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An invitation under section 30(1) must be issued as soon as is reasonably practicable following—

- (a) the first meeting of a public services board (see paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 3), and
- (b) each meeting held under paragraph 3(1) of that Schedule.

(2) An invitation under section 30(1) or (2)—

- (a) may be in such form as the board determines; but
- (b) must specify the person to whom a response is to be sent.

(3) An invited participant may participate in the activity of a board in the period—

- (a) beginning with the date on which the response accepting the invitation is received by the person to whom it is to be sent, and
- (b) ending on the date on which the next ordinary election is held under section 26 of the Local Government Act 1972 (c.70) (election of councillors).
