# Well-being of Future Generations (Wales) Act 2015, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-08-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2015/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Directions to merge , demerge or collaborate[^c-0a8875a446c754990104d6aaff940b63][^c-4ac2feb0f0b348e044b956112d13e687]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Before giving a direction under section 47(2) or (8) or section[^c-0699f4e953637aa450e016cbe002a718] 48(2) the Welsh Ministers must consult each member of the public services board or[^c-6f2d560d81ce43a552658ad6b1cdafaa] boards they intend to direct.

(2) When giving such a direction the Welsh Ministers must publish a statement containing their reasons for giving it.

(3)[^c-9e32b510949111c824be9ead9aad0dd7] The Welsh Ministers may vary or revoke such a direction.
