# Well-being of Future Generations (Wales) Act 2015, section 14

## 14. Guidance[^key-2068fa828b976cec6141c5cfbe39ef81]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers must issue guidance to other public bodies about the exercise of functions under this Part.

(2) In exercising a function under this Part, a public body must take such guidance into account.
