# Environment (Wales) Act 2016, section 79

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 79. Appeal against variation etc. of marine licence for non-payment of fee or deposit[^c-c441b0789bf324ea08d217580a3dfd15][^c-ab2742ef4987504acb4085389b7a68ad][^c-02180d6ec00e5438ede43135c5496fd6]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 108 of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 (appeals against notices), after subsection (2) insert—

> (2A) The Welsh Ministers must by regulations make provision for any person to whom a notice is issued under section 72A(7) or 107A(4) to appeal against that notice.
