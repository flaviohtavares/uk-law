# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 30

## 30. Statement of amount of money collected by WRA[^c-fa099a3c10aa01e255b66510205440d1][^c-c82743af780a484ac8eac63999bfbbae]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) WRA must prepare in respect of each financial year, in accordance with directions given by the Welsh Ministers, a statement of the amount of money collected by it during the financial year in the exercise of its functions [^c-e5e7e1095ecbb305a91212b0f63f7041]....

(1A)[^c-08f8631cc5e711ffe86bc84c5bb5ba86] Before giving a direction under subsection (1) that relates only to the visitor levy, the Welsh Ministers must consult each principal council that has introduced, or decided to introduce, the levy in its area.

(2) Directions under this section may be varied or revoked at any time.
