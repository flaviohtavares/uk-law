## 81D. Meaning of ““tax”” and “tax advantage[^c-96bda5550910e9fad55651c5bf86620d]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of this Part—

- “**"tax”**” (“**"*treth***”") means any devolved tax;
- “**"tax advantage”**” (“**"*mantais drethiannol***”") means—
  - (a) relief or increased relief from tax,
  - (b) repayment or increased repayment of tax,
  - (c) avoidance or reduction of a charge to tax,
  - (d) deferral of a payment of tax or advancement of a repayment of tax, [^c-f9721b1e4740a89ea0e0103e2ef1330e]...
  - (e) avoidance of an obligation to deduct or account for tax , and[^c-04cf82a5dc69aaaa48b90a908f261b7a]
  - (f) tax credit or increased tax credit.
