# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 64

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 64. Disallowing claims for relief due to unjustified enrichment[^c-1ecede14dc56979a4cfd82b273791398][^c-d3ae055264108127a476cd922ff95477][^c-76f17d1ed7f39f31f2964b334baefc3e]

*Extent: E+W*

WRA need not give effect to a claim for relief made under section 63 or 63A[^c-6b77709d148b2486f325c24ad5d6457d] if, or to the extent that, repayment or discharge of the amount would unjustly enrich the claimant.
