# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 37

## 37. Overview of Part

*Extent: E+W*

This Part is about the assessment of WRA-collected[^key-fff3af6ed431e746ec4d55fe1114ea14] taxes and includes provision about—

- (a) keeping records;
- (b) tax returns;
- (c) enquiries by WRA into tax returns;
- (d) determinations by WRA of WRA-collected tax due where no tax return is made;
- (e) assessments by WRA of WRA-collected tax due and of amounts payable in respect of tax credits[^key-79fd020a7a8141d5a66cc805d765fc97];
- (f) claims for relief from double assessment and for repayment of WRA-collected tax;
- (g) the making of claims.
