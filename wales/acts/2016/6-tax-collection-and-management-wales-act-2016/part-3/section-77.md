# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 77

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/6
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 77. Giving effect to amendments under section 75[^c-72eb4c1bacdd2a181dbd2b8e4618884f][^c-01bbbdf3cb698058845380c94b1e49fb]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Within 30 days after the day on which a notice under section 75(2)(b) is issued WRA must give effect to the amendment by making such adjustment as may be necessary, whether⁠—

- (a) by way of assessment on the claimant, or
- (b) by discharge or repayment of WRA-collected[^c-fff3af6ed431e746ec4d55fe1114ea14] tax.

(2) An assessment made under subsection (1) is not out of time if it is made within the time mentioned in that subsection.
