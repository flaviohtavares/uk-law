# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/6
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 39. Preservation of information etc.[^key-b0ff4f724c7ec3d8c258510d3fb70e79]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-b6aa4eb142cf230c27e9e71c3175de4a] The duty under section 38 or 38A[^key-340bc37d72c2732a7d6d4eb34710c3f6] to preserve records may be satisfied—

- (a) by preserving them in any form and by any means, or
- (b) by preserving the information contained in them in any form and by any means, subject to any conditions or exceptions prescribed by regulations made by the Welsh Ministers.

(2)[^key-254dfb88d3abda679d8c4588f12dfc06] But this is subject to any requirement specified under section 21(7) (water discount record) or 43(2) (landfill disposals tax summary) of LDTA.
