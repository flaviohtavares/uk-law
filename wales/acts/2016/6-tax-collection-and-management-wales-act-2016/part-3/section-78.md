# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 78

## 78. Time limit for making claims[^key-3c907edd4a3cee76df0edb1a42466913][^key-9b65f9557611ad87faa7469dd1f7d727]

*Extent: E+W*

A claim under section 62 or 63 must be made within the period of 4 years beginning with the day after the filing date for the tax return to which the payment by way of WRA-collected[^key-fff3af6ed431e746ec4d55fe1114ea14] tax, or the assessment or determination, relates.
