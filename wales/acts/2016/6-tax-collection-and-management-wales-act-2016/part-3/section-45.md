# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/6
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45. Amendment of tax return during enquiry to prevent loss of tax[^c-da4b0dfcb5234d23da81b1540138c781]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If, during the period when an enquiry into a tax return is in progress, WRA forms the opinion—

- (a) that the amount stated in the tax return as the amount of WRA-collected[^c-fff3af6ed431e746ec4d55fe1114ea14] tax chargeable[^c-e70f5cbfa2599724f6b2f608a3d00e3a] is insufficient, and
- (b) that, unless the return is immediately amended, there is likely to be a loss of WRA-collected tax,

WRA may by notice issued to the person who made the return amend it to make good the insufficiency.

(1A)[^c-cf2ecf13bfcd0ba9fcea40f718514e48] If, during the period when an enquiry into a tax return is in progress, WRA forms the opinion—

- (a) that the amount of tax credit claimed in the tax return is excessive, and
- (b) that, unless the return is immediately amended, there is likely to be a loss of WRA-collected tax,

WRA may by notice issued to the person who made the return amend it so that the amount claimed is no longer excessive.

(2) If the enquiry is one that is limited by section 44(2) to matters arising from an amendment of the tax return,

- (a)[^c-d7c4a258ed176c6dd9a126cb9eb4ae9e] subsection (1) applies only so far as the insufficiency is attributable to the amendment , and[^c-36f9e91a01460ea61331bc4205818fbe]
- (b) subsection (1A) applies only so far as the excessive amount is attributable to the amendment.

(3) Where a notice is issued under subsection (1) or (1A)[^c-a8341b06756506137d5b4cbe659a8f04], the person who made the tax return may no longer amend it under section 41.

(4) The person who made the tax return must pay any amount, or additional amount, of WRA-collected tax payable as a result of the amendment before the end of the period of 30 days beginning with the day on which notice of the amendment is issued.

(5) For the purposes of this section and sections 45A and 46[^c-f239bad065fcbdeae3f9ad794656f705] the period during which an enquiry into a tax return is in progress is the whole of the period—

- (a) beginning with the day on which notice of enquiry into the tax return is issued, and
- (b) ending with the day on which the enquiry is completed (see section 50).
