## 62. Claim for relief in case of double assessment[^c-5b842b8e9f974d6655c5277cc7775297][^c-bca4088959d6973276c7c6bdefd66332]

*Extent: E+W*

A person who believes that a WRA-collected[^c-fff3af6ed431e746ec4d55fe1114ea14] tax has been assessed on that person more than once in respect of the same matter may make a claim to WRA for relief against any double charge.
