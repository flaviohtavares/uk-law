# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 40

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Meaning of “filing date”[^key-cbf9b1d2446c57707e264cbc80d60791]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-2fe5fd98eed25f4b61c2f07dfb61c543] In the Welsh Tax Acts[^key-d304fd757be16c888e532d0adba39c0b], the “**filing date**”—[^key-a97fce9977aebb54600558a1d1f19105]

- (a) in relation to a tax return for land transaction tax, is the day by which the return is required to be made under LTTA;
- (b) in relation to a tax return for landfill disposals tax, has the meaning given by section 39(4) of LDTA.

(2)[^key-3f07e7fc324645c315bcf0cb7a04a644] In this Act the “filing date, in relation to a visitor levy return, has the meaning given by section 36 of the [Visitor Accommodation (Register and Levy) Etc. (Wales) Act 2025 (asc 00)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2025/5).
