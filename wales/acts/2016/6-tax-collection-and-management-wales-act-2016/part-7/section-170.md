# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 170

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/6

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 170. Enforcement by taking control of goods[^key-73ad1c13be354b57e3d8e69dd63cf5bd]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a person does not pay WRA a relevant amount which is payable by the person, WRA may use the procedure in Schedule 12 to TCEA[^key-73457a00a98f49a0860f44f55ed602de] (taking control of goods) to recover that amount.

(2) In section 63(3) of that Act (enforcement agents), after paragraph (b) insert—

> - (ba) a person authorised to use the procedure in Schedule 12 by the Welsh Revenue Authority (or by a person to whom the Welsh Revenue Authority has delegated the function of authorising the use of the procedure);

.
