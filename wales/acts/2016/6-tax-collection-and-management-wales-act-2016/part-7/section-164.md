# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 164

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 164. Meaning of “relevant amount”[^key-d72e8b4624f4bab784c6b4ad5f01055e]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part, “**relevant amount**” means—

- (a) WRA-collected[^key-fff3af6ed431e746ec4d55fe1114ea14] tax;
- (b) interest on WRA-collected tax;
- (c) a penalty relating to WRA-collected tax or the register under Part 2 of the[^key-c77026e5bb78e692212e23dc22088258] [Visitor Accommodation (Register and Levy) Etc. (Wales) Act 2025 (asc 00)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2025/5);
- (d) interest on a penalty relating to WRA-collected tax;
- (e)[^key-248f1122a0ca06041cf1272b692bc092] an amount payable in respect of a tax credit;
- (f) interest on an amount payable in respect of a tax credit.
