# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 181J

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/6
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 181J. Application of this Chapter to amounts payable in respect of tax credit[^c-fc58ca74ecb146febb5c958304a18e0d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Chapter applies to the payment and recovery of amounts relating to tax credits—

- (a) as if references to an amount of WRA-collected[^c-fff3af6ed431e746ec4d55fe1114ea14] tax (including amounts of landfill disposals tax) were references to an amount payable in respect of a tax credit,
- (b) as if references to interest on an amount of WRA-collected tax were references to interest on an amount payable in respect of a tax credit, and
- (c) as if references to WRA-collected tax being charged or chargeable on a person were references to an amount being payable by a person in respect of a tax credit.
