## 181A. Review or appeal not to affect requirement to pay[^key-07590d4a8d46069e9d15d065011b324f]

*Extent: E+W*

The fact that a person to whom an appealable decision applies has—

- (a) requested a review of the decision, or
- (b) appealed against it,

does not affect any requirement on the person to pay an amount of WRA-collected[^key-fff3af6ed431e746ec4d55fe1114ea14] tax (and interest on that amount).
