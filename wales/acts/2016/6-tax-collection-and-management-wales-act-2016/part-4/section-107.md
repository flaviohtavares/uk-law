# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 107

## 107. Producing authorisation to carry out inspections[^key-c974f9cf27574f6ad701414d2bc7edc5]

*Extent: E+W*

If a person carrying out an inspection under section 103 , 103A, 103B[^key-7be483546f6fca1b7b82d28b963a5398] or 106 is unable to produce evidence of authority to carry out the inspection when asked to do so by—

- (a) the occupier of the premises, or
- (b) any other person who appears to be in charge of, or to control, the premises,

the inspection must stop and may not be continued until such evidence is produced.
