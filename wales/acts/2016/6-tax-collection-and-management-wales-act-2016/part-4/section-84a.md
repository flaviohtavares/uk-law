# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 84A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/6
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 84A. Meaning of “prejudice to the assessment or collection of WRA-collected tax”[^c-e748f117db69ee7d8986ce645350d0ce][^c-fff3af6ed431e746ec4d55fe1114ea14]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part, references to prejudice to the assessment or collection of WRA-collected tax include prejudice to the assessment or collection of any amount payable in respect of a tax credit.
