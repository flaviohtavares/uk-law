# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 118

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/6

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 118. Penalty for failure to make devolved tax return on or before filing date[^key-0edf019204a1bd21a588393ee3b6b5fb][^key-e918d2ff370fcb4a08e879035e775f76]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-49381d0baa6073357d29dc2f79c458c6] A person who is required to make a[^key-0c36e89a0b3d1bb9cce23402641c78c9] devolved[^key-428ab777db8f5e2288574ace14ef6206] tax return is liable to a penalty of £100 if the person fails to make a devolved tax return on or before the filing date.

(2)[^key-c433931743c37752289b9ae7596e5bb9] But see section 118A for an exception to the rule above.
