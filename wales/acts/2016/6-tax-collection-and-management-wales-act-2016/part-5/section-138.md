# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 138

## 138. Potential lost revenue: delayed tax[^c-9b18767c82aff10e26560626591e1c40]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where an inaccuracy resulted in an amount of WRA-collected[^c-fff3af6ed431e746ec4d55fe1114ea14] tax being declared later than it should have been (“the delayed tax”), the potential lost revenue is—

- (a) 5% of the delayed tax for each year of the delay;
- (b) a percentage of the delayed tax, for each period of delay of less than a year, equating to 5% per year.

(2) This section does not apply to a case to which section 137 applies.
