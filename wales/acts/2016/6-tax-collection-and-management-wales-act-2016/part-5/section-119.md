# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 119

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/6

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 119. Penalty for failure to make tax devolved return within 6 months from filing date[^c-a0e7418163264c6f3a8945b7c7101cdd][^c-293859b8fffa26d28febed7afc03186b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person who is required to make a[^c-7ea68f05afc293d65a67a53e96435eb5] devolved[^c-6f011852417d37553be12609f3a5efa3] tax return is liable to a penalty if the person's failure to make a devolved tax return continues after the end of the period of 6 months beginning with the day after the filing date.

(2) The penalty is the greater of—

- (a) 5% of the amount of the devolved tax to which the person would have been liable if the tax return had been made, and
- (b) £300.
