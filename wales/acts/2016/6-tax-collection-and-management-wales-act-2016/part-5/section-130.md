# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 130

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/6

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 130. Amount of penalty for inaccuracy in document given to WRA[^c-15e5e89fa0397156ed099a9062da7b79]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The penalty for a deliberate inaccuracy is an amount not exceeding[^c-8eac47ba31a4d420cf823081d58509a0] 100% of the potential lost revenue.

(2) The penalty for a careless inaccuracy is an amount not exceeding[^c-808abd02f2192ee5f2fbb8cf9b5badd3] 30% of the potential lost revenue.
