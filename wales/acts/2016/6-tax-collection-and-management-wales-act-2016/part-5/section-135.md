# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 135

## 135. Potential lost revenue: normal rule[^key-613093e9898cda391ee7d48581f2ac5a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The “potential lost revenue” in respect of—

- (a) an inaccuracy in a document (including an inaccuracy attributable to a supply of false information or withholding of information), or
- (b) a failure to notify an under-assessment,

is the additional amount payable in respect of a WRA-collected[^key-fff3af6ed431e746ec4d55fe1114ea14] tax or tax credit[^key-f39ceb3802bb5da615c890c17047c9f5] as a result of correcting the inaccuracy or under-assessment.

(2) The reference in subsection (1) to the additional amount payable includes a reference to—

- (a) an amount payable to WRA having been erroneously paid by way of repayment of WRA-collected tax, [^key-a59ac181c0f00413068d8c345cc94c0d]...
- (b) an amount which would have been repayable by WRA had the inaccuracy or under-assessment not been corrected, and[^key-34bf5b9ba8f1ce14ee193d3fae2c9f7d]
- (c) an amount which WRA would have been required to set off against a person’s liability to tax, or to pay to a person, had the inaccuracy or under-assessment not been corrected.
