# Tax Collection and Management (Wales) Act 2016, section 191

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 191. Giving notices and other documents to WRA

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where a provision of the Visitor Accommodation (Register and Levy) Etc. (Wales) Act 2025,[^key-5f9baa5251a68fc27d7b0257ae40f64c] the Welsh Tax Acts, or of regulations made under them,[^key-9135b5b7d99d73256491417fc57874f2] requires or permits a person to make a tax return or[^key-bb917f0d4617c9f9b3c7115caf1701f0] to give a notice or other document to WRA (whether the expression “give” or any other expression is used) (but see subsection (4)).

(2)[^key-bf42f5630928355e25afbe2dce8a31b2] The tax return, notice or other document must—

- (a) be in such form,
- (b) contain such information,
- (c) be accompanied by such other documents, and
- (d) be given in such manner,

as may be specified by WRA.

(3) But subsection (2) is subject to any different provision made in or under the Welsh Tax Acts[^key-c99edffbbf31b10e15c0e96dc1a1fc11] or the Visitor Accommodation (Register and Levy) Etc. (Wales) Act 2025[^key-9add8021290e1d8aa04cfa1b9380ecb6].

(4) This section does not apply to any document given to WRA by the Welsh Ministers or the tribunal.
