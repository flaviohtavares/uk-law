## 112. Withdrawal: power to prescribe time limits[^c-9b5e93fa10fa01905f49fd3988d7d9eb][^c-7fba3fd8b5803caf37284996a96f03ac]

*Extent: E+W*

The Welsh Ministers must prescribe supplementary provisions specifying a minimum time period between the date on which a notice under section 111 is given to the landlord, and the date specified in the notice.
