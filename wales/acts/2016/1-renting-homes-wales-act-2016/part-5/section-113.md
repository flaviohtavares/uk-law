## 113. Lodgers[^c-57111757705cac2a3e845ce7a8dc9045]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The contract-holder under a secure contract may allow persons to live in the dwelling as lodgers.

(2) This section is a fundamental provision which is incorporated as a term of all secure contracts.
