## 106. Variation of fundamental terms[^key-112d2cce2fc00c13c10e5e715167cc4d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A fundamental term of a secure contract may be varied by agreement between the landlord and the contract-holder (subject to section 108).

(2) This section is a fundamental provision which is incorporated as a term of all secure contracts.
