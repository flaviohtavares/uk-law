# Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016, section 194

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-06-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 194. Landlord's break clause[^c-4ca200aca8245db8f4ff709d1c87f156][^c-e671333116f5f6dec709d4d64d9fbf61][^c-b6d383a30ce08064689ce895046fd098][^c-b6d383a30ce08064689ce895046fd098]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A fixed term standard contract which is within subsection (1A)[^c-4a6fb30f8fbb4178b18dfbdd98862243] may contain a term enabling the landlord to end the contract before the end of the fixed term by giving the contract-holder notice that he or she must give up possession of the dwelling on a date specified in the notice.

(1A)[^c-09edb42981e79c03f3db3c871ba9b3a3] A fixed term standard contract is within this subsection if—

- (a) it is made for a term of two years or more, or
- (b) it is within Schedule 9C (whether or not it is made for a term of two years or more).

(2) References in this Act to a landlord's break clause, in relation to a fixed term standard contract, are to the term mentioned in subsection (1).
