# Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016, section 203

## 203. Landlord's review of decision to give a notice[^key-b6d383a30ce08064689ce895046fd098]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-c55ee74fbfc4a1aeed7d513d1fe9392e] If the contract-holder, in accordance with section 202, requests a review of the landlord's decision to give a notice, the landlord must carry out the review.

(2)[^key-c55ee74fbfc4a1aeed7d513d1fe9392e] Following a review, the landlord may—

- (a) confirm the decision to give the notice, or
- (b) reverse the decision.

(3)[^key-c55ee74fbfc4a1aeed7d513d1fe9392e] The landlord must notify the contract-holder of the outcome of the review before the date after which the landlord is able to make a possession claim.

(4)[^key-c55ee74fbfc4a1aeed7d513d1fe9392e] If the landlord confirms the decision, the notice must set out the reasons for the confirmation.

(5)[^key-780d6bbbf506e3aef10f140e2b943998][^key-3a59e702c09bd69341cc9bb6018c2d08] The Welsh Ministers may prescribe the procedure to be followed in connection with a review under this section.

(6)[^key-780d6bbbf506e3aef10f140e2b943998][^key-3a59e702c09bd69341cc9bb6018c2d08] Regulations under subsection (5) may, amongst other things—

- (a) require the review to be carried out by a person of appropriate seniority who has not been involved in the decision, and
- (b) set out circumstances in which a contract-holder is entitled to an oral hearing, and whether and by whom he or she may be represented at such a hearing.
