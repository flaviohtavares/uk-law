# Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016, section 147

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-06-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 147. Overview of Part[^key-09707474fd67a5850a0128a32651bf22][^key-b6d383a30ce08064689ce895046fd098][^key-b6d383a30ce08064689ce895046fd098]

*Extent: E+W*

The following table provides an overview of this Part—

TABLE 1

| **CHAPTER** | **OCCUPATION CONTRACTS TO WHICH IT APPLIES** | **CONTENT OF CHAPTER** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | All occupation contracts (except section 151, which applies only to introductory standard contracts and prohibited conduct standard contracts) | - (a) ways in which occupation contracts may be ended,<br>- (b) circumstances in which landlords may make a claim to the court for recovery of possession of a dwelling, and<br>- (c) “possession notices”, which are notices landlords must give to contract-holders before making a possession claim under section 157 (breach of contract), section 160[^key-0d3559e03b5161c8d1607db6b1c2a569] (in relation to estate management grounds), sections 165 or 170 (recovery of possession after contract-holder's notice), section 181 or 187 (serious rent arrears) or section 191 (recovery of possession after use of contract-holder's break clause). |
| 2 | All occupation contracts | Certain circumstances in which occupation contracts can end without a possession claim. |
| 3 | All occupation contracts | Landlords' possession claims on—<br>- (a) ground of contract-holder's breach of contract, and<br>- (b) estate management grounds. |
| 4 | Secure contracts | Contract-holder's right to end the contract. |
| 5 | Periodic standard contracts | - (a) contract-holder's right to end the contract, and<br>- (b) rights of landlord to end the contract and make a possession claim. |
| 6 and 7 | Fixed term standard contracts | - (a) what happens at the end of the term,<br>- (b) contract-holder's right to end the contract, and<br>- (c) rights of landlord to end the contract and make a possession claim. |
| 8 | Introductory standard contracts and prohibited conduct standard contracts | Review by landlord, when required by contract-holder, of landlord's decision to give a notice requiring possession on certain grounds. |
| 9 and 10 | All occupation contracts | - (a) powers of court in relation to all possession claims, and<br>- (b) powers of court in relation to possession claims concerning discretionary grounds for possession. |
| 11 | Secure contracts | Powers and duties of court in relation to possession claims concerning a contract-holder's notice. |
| 12 | Standard contracts | Powers and duties of court in relation to possession claims concerning absolute grounds for possession. |
| 13 to 15 | All occupation contracts | - (a) rights of landlord in relation to abandonment of dwelling by contract-holder,<br>- (b) termination and exclusion where there are joint contract-holders, and<br>- (c) forfeiture and notices to quit not available in relation to occupation contracts. |
