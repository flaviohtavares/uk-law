# Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016, section 190

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-06-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 190. Minimum notice period[^c-be4490cd9641a72a9acc821a88907117][^c-b6d383a30ce08064689ce895046fd098][^c-b6d383a30ce08064689ce895046fd098]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The date specified in a notice under a contract-holder's break clause may not be less than four weeks after the day on which the notice is given to the landlord.

(2) This section is a fundamental provision which is incorporated as a term of all fixed term standard contracts with a contract-holder's break clause.
