# Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016, section 195

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-06-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 195. Minimum notice period[^c-70b0207618c3eeec65ac274bd1dc84da][^c-b6d383a30ce08064689ce895046fd098][^c-b6d383a30ce08064689ce895046fd098]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The date specified in a notice under a landlord's break clause may not be less than six months[^c-e17249e9fb916f67e75f8fabef73cf00] after the day on which the notice is given to the contract-holder.

(2)[^c-480dfedb96ec12e67d1352cc0f4f4b94] This section is a fundamental provision which is incorporated as a term of all fixed term standard contracts, except fixed term standard contracts which—

- (a) do not have a landlord's break clause, or
- (b) are within Schedule 8A (whether or not they have a landlord's break clause).
