# Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016, section 195A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-06-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 195A. Minimum notice period: fixed term standard contracts within Schedule 8A[^key-9de92687a0553386971ac3c1b1d697a9][^key-b6d383a30ce08064689ce895046fd098][^key-18afc2f892229fb01421ea832bc3010a][^key-b6d383a30ce08064689ce895046fd098]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a fixed term standard contract is within Schedule 8A, the date specified in a notice under a landlord's break clause may not be less than two months after the day on which the notice is given to the contract-holder.

(2) This section is a fundamental provision which is incorporated as a term of all fixed term standard contracts which—

- (a) have a landlord's break clause, and
- (b) are within Schedule 8A.
