## 223. Power to vary periods of time relating to abandonment[^key-b46ea50761435d150b632ff44d5be1fa][^key-b6d383a30ce08064689ce895046fd098][^key-b6d383a30ce08064689ce895046fd098]

*Extent: E+W*

The Welsh Ministers may by regulations—

- (a) amend section 220(8) by substituting a different period of time for the period for the time being referred to;
- (b) amend section 222(1) by substituting a different period of time for the period for the time being referred to.
