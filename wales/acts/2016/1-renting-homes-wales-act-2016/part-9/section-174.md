# Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016, section 174

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-06-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 174. Minimum notice period[^key-ed1d8a17c3674e2da54394292b09e73f][^key-b6d383a30ce08064689ce895046fd098][^key-b6d383a30ce08064689ce895046fd098]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The date specified in a notice under section 173 may not be less than six months[^key-fb2602ed72e54ac80700884b0a6d6c1f] after the day on which the notice is given to the contract-holder.

(2)[^key-1f52b51d17be59fe5c440dd32e8ca686] This section is a fundamental provision which is incorporated as a term of all periodic standard contracts, except periodic standard contracts which—

- (a) do not incorporate section 173 as a term of the contract, or
- (b) are within Schedule 8A (whether or not they incorporate section 173 as a term of the contract).
