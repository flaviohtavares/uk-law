# Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016, section 236

## 236. Form of notices, statements and other documents

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-4b7dfaa3ec84615e68a03a059451178b] This section applies to any notice or other document (including a copy of a document) required or authorised to be given or made by or because of this Act.

(2)[^c-4b7dfaa3ec84615e68a03a059451178b] The notice or document must be in writing.

(3)[^c-80673361b15c2637f7b7a55a883e983f][^c-b32acaa75d17415bd3733863d0232cd4] The Welsh Ministers may prescribe the form of the notice or document and, unless the regulations provide otherwise, a notice or document not in the prescribed form is of no effect.

(4)[^c-80673361b15c2637f7b7a55a883e983f][^c-b32acaa75d17415bd3733863d0232cd4] The notice or document may be in electronic form (subject to section 237(4)) provided it—

- (a) has the certified electronic signature of each person by whom it is required to be signed or executed, and
- (b) complies with such other conditions as may be prescribed.

(5)[^c-4b7dfaa3ec84615e68a03a059451178b] A notice or document within subsection (4) is to be treated as signed or executed by each person whose certified electronic signature it has.

(6)[^c-4b7dfaa3ec84615e68a03a059451178b] If a notice or document in electronic form is authenticated by a person as agent, it is to be regarded for the purposes of any enactment as authenticated by that person under the written authority of that person's principal.

(7)[^c-4b7dfaa3ec84615e68a03a059451178b] References to an electronic signature and to the certification of such a signature are to be read in accordance with section 7(2) and (3) of the Electronic Communications Act 2000 (c. 7).
