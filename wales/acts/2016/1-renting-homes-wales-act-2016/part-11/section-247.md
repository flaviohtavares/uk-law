## 247. Meaning of “variation” of occupation contract

*Extent: E+W*

In this Act “**variation**”, in relation to an occupation contract—

- (a) includes the addition or removal of a term of the contract;
- (b) does not include any change in the identity of the landlord or contract-holder under the contract.
