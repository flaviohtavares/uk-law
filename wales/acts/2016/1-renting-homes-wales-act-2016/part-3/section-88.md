## 88. Right of set off[^c-eeb70c9b9415c0046220b21da90c09a9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the landlord under an occupation contract is liable to pay the contract-holder compensation under section 87, the contract-holder may set off that liability against rent.

(2) This section is a fundamental provision which is incorporated as a term of all occupation contracts.
