## 43. Form of security[^key-858e6b6978b4ffab8e583a8e034c977b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The landlord under an occupation contract may not require security to be given in any form other than—

- (a) money, or
- (b) a guarantee.

(2) This section is a fundamental provision which is incorporated as a term of all occupation contracts.
