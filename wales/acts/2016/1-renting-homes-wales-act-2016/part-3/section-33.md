## 33. Editorial changes[^key-87550f90c8f66e0c5f69ecaba573d0a7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The written statement may set out the fundamental and supplementary terms of the occupation contract with editorial changes.

(2) Editorial changes are changes to the wording of a fundamental or supplementary term which do not change the substance of that term in any way.[^key-8e191b066f1b0a2b445e39974e8f021d]...
