## 146. Temporary exclusion: guidance

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-ebb53680f856eb6225f1b6c41258e7d1][^key-e8374a5c2f6ee4b8de867eb3cabd59bd] The Welsh Ministers must issue guidance about the exercise by landlords of their functions under section 145.

(2)[^key-4987bf08ee669b6a056926a2ddb6ff74] In the exercise of those functions, a landlord must have regard to guidance issued under subsection (1).
