## 26. Key matters of all occupation contracts[^c-c4b6acdcf99a72f601e40fd7e0871bb0]

*Extent: E+W*

The following are key matters in relation to all occupation contracts—

- (a) the dwelling,
- (b) the occupation date,
- (c) the amount of rent or other consideration, and
- (d) the rental periods.
