## 89. Application of Part[^key-e76d67a16c5d56c9e80782654d041f35]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Chapter 2 applies to all secure contracts, all periodic standard contracts, and all fixed term standard contracts made for a term of less than seven years (see section 90).

(2) Chapter 3 applies to all occupation contracts.
