# Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016, section 48

## 48. Failure to submit or publish annual return[^c-767fb1600a72b7c7642067547277e82a][^c-2ee2083919738e8c37fe7e6dd592b754]

*Extent: E+W*

It is an offence for a service provider to —[^c-12f8b00e3d23283e7301c519329e71da]

- (a) fail to submit an annual return to the Welsh Ministers within the time limit prescribed under section 10(4), or
- (b) fail to publish an annual return on its website within the time limit prescribed under section 10(4A).
