# Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Meaning of “regulated service”

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Act, “**regulated service**” means—

- (a)[^c-b37702ae1d2eeeb8f4a331d0ca14951e] a care home service,
- (b)[^c-b37702ae1d2eeeb8f4a331d0ca14951e] a secure accommodation service,
- (c)[^c-b37702ae1d2eeeb8f4a331d0ca14951e] a residential family centre service,
- (d)[^c-4989515fbbd4e0be34b0a5c7e49cb7dd] an adoption service,
- (e)[^c-4989515fbbd4e0be34b0a5c7e49cb7dd] a fostering service,
- (f)[^c-4989515fbbd4e0be34b0a5c7e49cb7dd] an adult placement service,
- (g)[^c-4989515fbbd4e0be34b0a5c7e49cb7dd] an advocacy service,
- (h) a domiciliary support service, and
- (i) any other service comprising the provision of care and support in Wales as may be prescribed.

(2)[^c-3b9304bd83a6070cff4d6a225c756483] Schedule 1 makes further provision about the meaning of terms used in subsection (1).

(3)[^c-b37702ae1d2eeeb8f4a331d0ca14951e] The Welsh Ministers may by regulations prescribe things which, despite Schedule 1, are not to be treated as regulated services for the purposes of this Act.

(4)[^c-b37702ae1d2eeeb8f4a331d0ca14951e] Before making regulations under this section the Welsh Ministers must consult such persons as they think appropriate.
