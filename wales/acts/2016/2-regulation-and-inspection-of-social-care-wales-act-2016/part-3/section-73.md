# Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016, section 73

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 73. Rules: general

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-01cc5c92d446357ecba3ff44cef10442][^c-bf402d4267b58ddc256b9478673dedb8] Any power conferred on SCW by or under this Act to make rules must be exercised by an instrument in writing.

(2)[^c-01cc5c92d446357ecba3ff44cef10442][^c-bf402d4267b58ddc256b9478673dedb8] An instrument containing rules must specify the provision under which the rules are made.

(3)[^c-8686ff6c2e9770a05bf74cbb04be00dd] To the extent that an instrument containing rules does not comply with subsection (2) it is void.

(4)[^c-8686ff6c2e9770a05bf74cbb04be00dd] Any power of SCW to make rules conferred by or under this Act may be exercised—

- (a) so as to make different provision for different purposes;
- (b) so as to make incidental, supplemental, consequential and transitional provision.

(5)[^c-8686ff6c2e9770a05bf74cbb04be00dd] SCW must—

- (a) publish rules made by it, and
- (b) ensure the rules are publicly available until such time as they cease to have effect.

(6)[^c-8686ff6c2e9770a05bf74cbb04be00dd] SCW may charge a fee for providing a person with a copy of rules made by it.
