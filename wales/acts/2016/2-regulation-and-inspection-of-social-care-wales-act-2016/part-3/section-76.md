# Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Guidance[^key-97fa7f476fc6ef918c13775d5ff883bf]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In exercising its functions SCW must have regard to any guidance given to it by the Welsh Ministers.

(2) The Welsh Ministers must publish any guidance they give to SCW.
