# Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016, section 77

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/2

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 77. Directions[^c-d866a25fd21ff731faa1d0e3a9dc8513]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In exercising its functions SCW must comply with any directions given to it by the Welsh Ministers.

(2) A direction—

- (a) must be in writing;
- (b) may be varied or revoked by a subsequent direction.
