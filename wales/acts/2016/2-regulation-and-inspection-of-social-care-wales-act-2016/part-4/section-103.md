# Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016, section 103

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 103. Decisions on appeal to the registration appeals panel[^c-bd09890b4280b91bf4ef6280e67876c0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-bd72213a83bcdef1e529fc0f55bad094] Subject to subsection (2),[^c-b3293c4618ba22af6d755cc18bbffbb4] on an appeal under section 101 a registration appeals panel may—

- (a) confirm the registrar's decision,
- (b) substitute for the decision appealed against another decision of a kind that the registrar could have made, or
- (c) remit the case to the registrar to dispose of in accordance with the directions of the panel.

(2)[^c-8ad04b6df12066122e93f556b14f3992] Where an appeal is made under section 101(1A), a registration appeals panel must remit the case to the registrar to dispose of in accordance with the directions of the panel.
