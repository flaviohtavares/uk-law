## 80. The register[^c-d5568a02bcb84a43a45b952ca87ee881]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) SCW must keep a register of—

- (a) social workers,
- (b) social care workers of any other description specified by the Welsh Ministers by regulations, [^M_F_c43d79ac-9986-4667-dd19-c4964a4425d3]...
- (c)[^c-718e53da34de9a79b59fa7a8576e816c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c-1aaf5d23d712ce06d0cbef9cdff9b220] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) There must be a separate part of the register—

- (a) for social workers;
- (b) for each description of social care worker specified in regulations made under subsection (1)(b);
- (c)[^c-5a36cc1b27c679c5ac4d271468eef04e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c-4c0e5782240e02eecb7d00b8e4989922] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) For the purposes of Parts 3 to 8 of this Act—

- (a) the part mentioned in subsection (2)(a) is the “social worker part” of the register;
- (b) a part mentioned in subsection (2)(b) is an “added part” of the register;
- (c)[^c-8ea661b96c68ad52179b5d3fd857ae1e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c-b195fa98125ccb98dc9e5e8e08b4b904] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
