# Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016, section 95

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/anaw/2016/2

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Duty to restore a register entry[^key-dd8daa94f1d2784b75a0e1a82709ac53]

*Extent: E+W*

If the registrar is satisfied that an entry, or an annotation to an entry, has been removed from the register in error, the registrar must restore that entry or annotation to the register.
