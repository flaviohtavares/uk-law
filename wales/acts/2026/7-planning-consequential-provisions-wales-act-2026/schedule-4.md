# Planning (Consequential Provisions) (Wales) Act 2026, SCHEDULE 4

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 4 — AMENDMENTS TO OTHER LEGISLATION

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(introduced by section 2)

### PART 1 — ORDERS IN COUNCIL

#### 1. National Assembly for Wales (Transfer of Functions) Order 1999 ([S.I. 1999/672](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/672))[^c-4a1ebdde81ea7937328959be0f33a62a]

In Schedule 1 to the National Assembly for Wales (Transfer of Functions) Order 1999, omit the entries for—

- (a) the Town and Country Planning Act 1990;
- (b) the Town and Country Planning (Costs of Inquiries etc.) Act 1995.

#### 2. National Assembly for Wales (Transfer of Functions) Order 2000 ([S.I. 2000/253](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2000/253))[^c-aee2642aea998cd49ae44d73dbb9f1f4]

In Schedule 3 to the National Assembly for Wales (Transfer of Functions) Order 2000, omit paragraph (d).

#### 3. Welsh Ministers (Transfer of Functions) Order 2018 ([S.I. 2018/644](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/644))[^c-47f2898aefff839131f09b3be04b0a77]

The Welsh Ministers (Transfer of Functions) Order 2018 is amended as follows.

#### 4.[^c-10b699558dbe29e0d0f244cacc202959]

Omit article 25.

#### 5.[^c-81885614afb4c83230eb2c73f74eb670]

Omit article 44.

### PART 2 — CHURCH OF ENGLAND MEASURES

#### 6. Repair of Benefice Buildings Measure [1972 (No. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/1972/2)[^c-1bea45b7db0c408a6db67077818167b6]

In section 2(3) of the Repair of Benefice Buildings Measure 1972, after “section 1 of the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990” insert “or section 76 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”.

#### 7. Mission and Pastoral Measure [2011 (No. 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2011/3)[^c-e4ccca86606efea6380b8421906190ba]

In section 106(1) of the Mission and Pastoral Measure 2011—

- (a) in the definition of “listed building” and “conservation area”—
  - (i) after ““conservation area”” insert

    > —
    >
    > - (a) in relation to England,

    ;
  - (ii) after the paragraph (a) created by that amendment insert—

    > - (b) in relation to Wales, have the meanings given by section 210 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023;

    ;
- (b) in the definition of “local planning authority”—
  - (i) after ““local planning authority”” insert

    > —
    >
    > - (a) in relation to England,

    ;
  - (ii) after the paragraph (a) created by that amendment insert—

    > - (b) in relation to Wales, means a planning authority within the meaning given by section 7 of the Planning (Wales) Act 2026;

    .

#### 8. Ecclesiastical Jurisdiction and Care of Churches Measure [2018 (No. 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukcm/2018/3)[^c-38baf06a277f3fc47523b7d6417de083]

The Ecclesiastical Jurisdiction and Care of Churches Measure 2018 is amended as follows.

#### 9.[^c-2a876e7fe58aa2a694cff0d1c702d01c]

In section 44(8)—

- (a) after “means” insert

  > —
  >
  > - (a) in the case of an area in England,

  ;
- (b) after the paragraph (a) created by that amendment insert—

  > - (b) in the case of an area in Wales, the planning authority for the purposes of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (see section 210 of that Act).

#### 10.[^c-39d3afb9108d73e0083e715d720d9eca]

In section 46(1)(d), after “Town and Country Planning Act 1990” insert “or a tree preservation order or woodland preservation order under the Planning (Wales) Act 2026”.

#### 11.[^c-3a3ffcc72f4a66013bc010d4a5379a2c]

In section 55(8)—

- (a) after paragraph (c) (but before the “or” that follows it) insert—

  > - (ca) the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023,
  > - (cb) the Planning (Wales) Act 2026,

  ;
- (b) in paragraph (d), for “(b) or (c)” substitute “(b), (c), (ca) or (cb)”.

#### 12.[^c-61ae2c9cb371e094ed76f7f91cb88f67]

In section 63—

- (a) in subsection (5), at the beginning insert “In relation to England,”;
- (b) after subsection (5) insert—

  > (5A) In relation to Wales, “listed building and “conservation area each have the same meaning as in the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023, and “local planning authority means the planning authority for the purposes of that Act (see section 210 of that Act).

#### 13.[^c-8468d69f42200d4568e5ff81d7ff1324]

In section 77—

- (a) in subsection (7)(c), at the end insert “or the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”;
- (b) in subsection (8)—
  - (i) after “has the same meaning” insert

    > —
    >
    > - (a) in relation to England,

    ;
  - (ii) after the paragraph (a) created by that amendment insert—

    > - (b) in relation to Wales, as in the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (see section 76(5) of that Act).

#### 14.[^c-50a010614b0e53b26e0d661a9d5e82a7]

In section 80(3)—

- (a) after paragraph (c) (but before the “or” that follows it) insert—

  > - (ca) the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023,
  > - (cb) the Planning (Wales) Act 2026,

  ;
- (b) in paragraph (d), for “(b) or (c)” substitute “(b), (c), (ca) or (cb)”.

#### 15.[^c-81648a2ba9635f93fec9a86bd6510691]

In section 93—

- (a) in subsection (4), at the beginning insert “In relation to land in England,”;
- (b) after subsection (4) insert—

  > (4A) In relation to land in Wales, “Crown land has the same meaning as in the Planning (Wales) Act 2026, and “appropriate authority means the appropriate Crown authority for the purposes of that Act (see section 401 of that Act).
