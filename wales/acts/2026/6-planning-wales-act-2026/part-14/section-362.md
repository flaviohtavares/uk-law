# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 362

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 362. Size and composition of committees to exercise functions[^key-a7f314938bcaebe32853afcee005a51e]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Regulations may impose requirements relating to the size and composition of a committee or sub-committee by which a relevant function of a planning authority is to be exercised.

(2) A planning authority may not arrange for a relevant function of the authority to be exercised by a committee or sub-committee of the authority that fails to satisfy a requirement of regulations under this section.

(3) If a committee or sub-committee exercising a relevant function fails to satisfy such a requirement, paragraph 43 of Schedule 12 to the Local Government Act (validity of proceedings not affected by vacancies etc.) does not apply in relation to the failure.
