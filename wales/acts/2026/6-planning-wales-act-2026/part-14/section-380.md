# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 380

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 380. Meaning of “decision document” and “correctable error”[^c-f3dcdc3a20fbfcfef207d7f55d2406f9]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) This section applies for the purposes of this Chapter.

(2) “**Decision document**” means a document that records—

- (a) a decision to which section 375 applies (see subsection (2) of that section),
- (b) a decision in proceedings on an appeal under section 131, 215 or 254 (appeal against enforcement notice, maintenance of land notice or tree replacement notice), or
- (c) any other decision made under or by virtue of this Act that is of a description specified in regulations.

(3) “**Correctable error**” means an error that—

- (a) is contained in any part of the decision document that records the decision, but
- (b) is not part of any reasons given for the decision,

and “**error**” includes omission.
