# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 274

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 274. Meaning of “relevant acquisition” and “relevant appropriation”[^c-276b240edcbb0d67675bb7e7ddafd322]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) For the purposes of sections 275 to 277, a “relevant acquisition” takes place where—

- (a) a local authority or the Welsh Ministers—
  - (i) acquire land under this Part or Part 11 or under Chapter 5 of Part 3 of the Historic Environment Act, or
  - (ii) acquire land in Wales compulsorily under any other enactment;
- (b) an English local authority or a government department other than the Welsh Ministers acquires land in Wales compulsorily under any enactment other than Part 9 or 10 of the Town and Country Planning Act [1990 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8) or Chapter 5 of Part 1 of the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act [1990 (c. 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/9);
- (c) a statutory undertaker acquires land in Wales compulsorily under any enactment.

(2) For the purposes of sections 275 to 277, a “relevant appropriation” takes place where land is appropriated by a local authority for planning purposes.

(3) For the application of sections 275 to 277 to certain other land, see—

- (a) section 54 of the Civil Aviation Act [1982 (c. 16)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1982/16) (land acquired by agreement by the Secretary of State, the Civil Aviation Authority or an air traffic licensee);
- (b) section 59(6) of the Airports Act [1986 (c. 31)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/31) (land acquired by agreement by a relevant airport operator);
- (c) paragraph 3 of Schedule 4 to the Communications Act [2003 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/21) (land acquired compulsorily by an electronic communications code operator);
- (d) paragraph 86A of Schedule 4 to the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act [2003 (c. 43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/43) (land held by an NHS foundation trust);
- (e) section 159(7) of the National Health Service (Wales) Act [2006 (c. 42)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/42) (land held by the Welsh Ministers for the purposes of the health service) and paragraph 32 of Schedule 3 to that Act (land held by an NHS trust);
- (f) section 211(8) of the National Health Service Act [2006 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/41) (land in Wales held by the Secretary of State for the purposes of the health service in England) and paragraph 32 of Schedule 4 to that Act (land in Wales held by an NHS trust in England).

(4) In subsection (1)—

- (a) “**English local authority**” means—
  - (i) a local authority, within the meaning given by section 336(1) of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990, for an area in England, or
  - (ii) a National Park authority for a National Park in England;
- (b) “**statutory undertaker**” includes an electricity licensee only if the licensee is entitled to exercise a power conferred by Schedule 3 to the Electricity Act [1989 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/29) (compulsory acquisition of land by licensees).
