# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 269

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 269. Power of local authority to obtain possession of dwellings let under certain tenancies[^c-70a94e27678218ceb7feac515e65192d]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) This section applies to a dwelling that a local authority—

- (a) has acquired or appropriated for planning purposes, and
- (b) holds for the purposes for which it was acquired or appropriated.

(2) If the Welsh Ministers certify that possession of the dwelling is immediately required for those purposes, nothing in the Rent Act [1977 (c. 42)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/42) or Part 1 of the Housing Act [1988 (c. 50)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/50) prevents the authority from obtaining possession of the dwelling.
