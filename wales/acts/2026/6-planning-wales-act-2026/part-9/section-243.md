# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 243

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 243. Registers of information relating to tree preservation orders and woodland preservation orders[^c-3efda3a4e2fb85a21be79f5a65173f7f]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Tree preservation regulations may make provision for the keeping of, and public access to, registers containing information relating to tree preservation orders and woodland preservation orders.
