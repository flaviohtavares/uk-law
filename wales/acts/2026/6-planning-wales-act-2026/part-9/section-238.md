# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 238

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 238. Interests of amenity: factors to be taken into account[^c-4f2fcfb66495d10f8839345ac5d556ab]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Tree preservation regulations may specify factors that must be taken into account in determining whether it is appropriate in the interests of amenity—

- (a) to make provision for the preservation of trees or woodlands under section 234(1), 235(1) or 236(1) or (2), or
- (b) to make an order under section 236(3).
