# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 235

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 235. Power of planning authority to make woodland preservation order[^key-1dd517edb1610e38130a058cc0d4c85c]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A planning authority may make a woodland preservation order if it considers that it is appropriate in the interests of amenity to make provision for the preservation of woodlands in its area.

(2) A woodland preservation order is an order for the preservation of the woodlands specified in the order.

(3) A woodland preservation order applies to all the trees in the woodlands to which the order relates, whether or not they were in existence when the order was made.

(4) See also—

- (a) section 241(5), which provides that if regulations provide for a prohibition not to apply to things done with consent, the regulations may also provide that a woodland preservation order applies to trees planted in accordance with a condition of a consent,
- (b) section 246(6), which provides that a woodland preservation order applies to replacement trees planted in accordance with section 246.
