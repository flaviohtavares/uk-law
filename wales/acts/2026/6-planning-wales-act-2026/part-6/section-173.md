# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 173

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 173. Use of CIL: funding infrastructure[^key-7b0e2820e0798956bbd2aca71a0e8425]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A charging authority that receives CIL must apply it, or cause it to be applied, to supporting development by funding the provision, improvement, replacement, operation or maintenance of infrastructure; but this is subject to sections 195 to 199.

(2) In this Chapter “**infrastructure**” includes—

- (a) roads and other transport facilities;
- (b) flood defences;
- (c) schools and other educational facilities;
- (d) medical facilities;
- (e) sporting and recreational facilities;
- (f) open spaces.

(3) CIL regulations may amend subsection (2) to—

- (a) add, modify or remove an entry in the list of matters included within the meaning of “infrastructure”;
- (b) list matters excluded from the meaning of “infrastructure”.
