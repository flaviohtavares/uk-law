# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 170

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 170. Interpretation of this Chapter[^c-752c03ff363637e93797cccc73b1ea0d]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In this Chapter—

- “**development consent obligation**” (“***rhwymedigaeth cydsyniad datblygiad***”) means a planning obligation entered into in connection with an application (or a proposed application) for an order granting development consent;
- “**enforcing authority**” (“***awdurdod gorfodi***”) has the meaning given by section 165(6)(e);
- “**infrastructure consent obligation**” (“***rhwymedigaeth cydsyniad seilwaith***”) means a planning obligation entered into in connection with an application (or a proposed application) for an infrastructure consent order.
