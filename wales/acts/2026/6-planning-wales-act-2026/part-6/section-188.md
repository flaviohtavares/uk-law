# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 188

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 188. Consequences of failure to comply[^c-390bb1f3a030c4a906b4e4658e5cdcd7]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Provision about enforcement in CIL regulations must include provision about the consequences of late payment and failure to pay.

(2) Provision about enforcement in CIL regulations may include provision about the consequences of failure to—

- (a) assume liability for CIL;
- (b) give notice under CIL regulations;
- (c) comply with another procedure under CIL regulations.
