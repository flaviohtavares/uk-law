# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 190

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 190. Power to stop development[^key-57cce248f2724504c7a2c1cbbc3d20a1]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Provision about enforcement in CIL regulations may include provision—

- (a) for a decision relating to CIL planning permission to be suspended or cancelled;
- (b) enabling a charging authority or other public authority to prohibit development until a person—
  - (i) assumes liability for CIL, or
  - (ii) makes a payment of CIL.
