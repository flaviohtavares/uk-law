# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 200

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 200. Reviews and appeals[^key-f0cea4eadcaeed26a1a68638a0241517]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) CIL regulations may include provision about reviews and appeals in connection with CIL.

(2) In particular, the regulations may make provision about—

- (a) the period within which a right of review or right of appeal may be exercised,
- (b) the procedure for reviews and appeals, and
- (c) the payment of fees and award of costs.
