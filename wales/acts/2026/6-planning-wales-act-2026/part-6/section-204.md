# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 204

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 204. CIL regulations: general[^key-15034944e823d4502dc45c6b9ff2d57a]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

CIL regulations may—

- (a) make different provision for different areas;
- (b) apply an enactment, with or without modifications;
- (c) provide (or enable a charging schedule to provide) for exceptions;
- (d) confer (or enable a charging schedule to confer) a discretionary power on the Welsh Ministers, a local authority or another person specified in the regulations.
