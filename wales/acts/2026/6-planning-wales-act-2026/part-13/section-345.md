# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 345

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 345. Powers of personal representatives in respect of blight notice served before death[^c-9d55bad2d8062a232c04d23b50c8caa1]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In relation to any time after the death of a person who has served a blight notice, sections 325(1), 328(2), 329(1), 332(2), 339(1), 340(1) and 341 apply as if the references in them to the claimant were to the claimant’s personal representatives.
