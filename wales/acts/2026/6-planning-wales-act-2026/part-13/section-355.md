# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 355

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 355. Power of the Welsh Ministers to acquire land identified in infrastructure policy statement[^key-52bce237ad19281f137abb4ecfc2f1c3]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Where a blight notice has been served in respect of land falling within paragraph 28 of Schedule 19, the Welsh Ministers have power to acquire compulsorily any interest in the land in pursuance of the blight notice.
