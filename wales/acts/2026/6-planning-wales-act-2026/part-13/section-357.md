# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 357

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 357. No withdrawal of notice to treat deemed to have been served under this Part[^c-fada233a8356eff16fee3397a5333e8a]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Where a notice to treat is treated as having been served by virtue of section 331, 332, 339 or 340, an appropriate authority may not withdraw it under section 31 of the Land Compensation Act [1961 (c. 33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1961/33) (but see section 341(3) for deemed withdrawal of a notice to treat where a blight notice is withdrawn).
