# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 349

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 349. Grounds of objection to blight notice served by mortgagee[^c-bb5913c784001a1a78baa373d0a01cfd]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The grounds on which objection may be made in a counter-notice under section 325 to a blight notice served under section 348 are—

- (a) Ground 1;
- (b) Ground 2;
- (c) Ground 3;
- (d) Ground 4 (in the case of land falling within paragraph 1, 2 or 12 but not also within paragraph 13, 14 or 15 of Schedule 19);
- (e) that, on the day the blight notice was served, the claimant had no interest as mortgagee in any part of the hereditament or agricultural unit to which the notice relates;
- (f) that (for reasons specified in the counter-notice), on the day the blight notice was served, the claimant did not have the power referred to in section 348(3)(b);
- (g) that none of the conditions specified in section 348(3)(c) is met;
- (h) that neither of the conditions specified in section 348(3)(d) is met;
- (i) Ground 8.

(2) See sections 327 and 336(4) for further provision about grounds of objection.
