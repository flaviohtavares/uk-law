# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 352

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 352. Power of the Welsh Ministers to acquire land identified by National Development Framework for Wales[^c-4b49f1ab02c7791949c16bebc66a5c57]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Where a blight notice has been served in respect of land falling within paragraph 2 of Schedule 19, the Welsh Ministers have power to acquire compulsorily any interest in the land in pursuance of the blight notice.
