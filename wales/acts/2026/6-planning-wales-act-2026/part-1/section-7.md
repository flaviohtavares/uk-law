# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 7

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. The planning authority for an area[^c-eb6b2501fd5fb2c2dd6bc75af4a8adbd]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The planning authority for an area in Wales is the county council or county borough council for the area.

(2) But—

- (a) a National Park authority for a National Park in Wales is the planning authority for the area of the Park;
- (b) a joint planning board established under section 8 is the planning authority for its joint planning area.

(3) Subsection (2)(a) applies to a National Park authority from the time specified in the order under section 63 of the Environment Act [1995 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/25) establishing the authority.

(4) Subsection (2)(b) applies to a joint planning board from the time specified in the regulations under section 8 establishing the board.
