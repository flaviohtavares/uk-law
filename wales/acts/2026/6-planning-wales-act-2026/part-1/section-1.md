## 1. Introduction to this Act[^key-36b511554fb6ce7dd3dcb3af799dd14e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Act forms part of a code of Welsh law relating to planning.

(2) It consolidates enactments contained in or made under various Acts, including—

- (a) the Town and Country Planning Act [1990 (c. 8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8);
- (b) section 22 of, and Schedule 2 to, the Planning and Compensation Act [1991 (c. 34)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/34), and Schedules 13 and 14 to the Environment Act [1995 (c. 25)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/25), so far as they continue to have effect immediately before this Act comes into force;
- (c) the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act [2004 (c. 5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/5);
- (d) Part 11 of the Planning Act [2008 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/29);
- (e) the Planning (Wales) Act [2015 (anaw 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2015/4).

(3) The Planning (Consequential Provisions) (Wales) Act 2026 amends and repeals enactments, and makes transitional and saving provision, in connection with this Act.
