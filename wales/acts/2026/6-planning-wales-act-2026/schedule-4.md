# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, SCHEDULE 4

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 4 — APPLICATIONS TO THE WELSH MINISTERS: EXERCISE OF FUNCTIONS BY INSPECTORS

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(introduced by section 83(6))

### PART 1 — PROVISION OF PRE-APPLICATION SERVICES BY INSPECTOR

#### 1. Replacement of inspector[^c-fff0204a9d85bac285c384fd5a4f3e56]

Where an inspector has been appointed to exercise functions under regulations made under section 53 (pre-application services) in relation to a proposed application under section 78 or 80, the Welsh Ministers may at any time—

- (a) revoke the inspector’s appointment, and
- (b) appoint another inspector to exercise the functions.

#### 2. Service of direction that functions are to be exercised by the Welsh Ministers[^c-89edec8338566bac673c3e58284a2c0a]

(1) This paragraph applies where the Welsh Ministers give a direction under section 83(3)(a) that functions are to be exercised in respect of a proposed application by them instead of by an inspector.

(2) A copy of the direction must be served on—

- (a) the inspector (if any) appointed in relation to the proposed application,
- (b) the person who proposes to make the application, and
- (c) the planning authority to which the application would otherwise be made.

#### 3. Revocation of direction[^c-47d04f5a66eb89b998823a51531cb559]

(1) The Welsh Ministers may by a further direction revoke a direction under section 83(3)(a).

(2) The Welsh Ministers must serve a copy of a further direction under this paragraph on the persons on whom paragraph 2(2) required the direction under section 83(3)(a) to be served.

(3) Where the Welsh Ministers give a further direction under this paragraph, they must appoint an inspector to exercise functions under regulations made under section 53 in relation to the proposed application.

### PART 2 — DETERMINATION OF APPLICATION BY INSPECTOR

#### 4. Powers and duties of inspector[^c-9eb05fb330c11b58c826a002db43f67e]

(1) An inspector appointed to determine an application made under section 78 or 80 has, so far as the context permits, the same powers and duties that the Welsh Ministers have by virtue of regulations under section 81(3).

(2) Sub-paragraph (3) applies where an enactment (other than section 83 or this Schedule)—

- (a) refers (or is to be read as referring) to the Welsh Ministers in a context relating to or capable of relating to an application made under section 78 or 80, or
- (b) refers (or is to be read as referring) to anything (other than the making of an application) done or authorised or required to be done by, to or before the Welsh Ministers in connection with any such application.

(3) So far as the context permits, the enactment is to be read, in relation to an application determined or to be determined by an inspector, as if the reference to the Welsh Ministers were or included a reference to an inspector.

#### 5. Local inquiry, hearing or written representations[^c-8c1541f89818a52f7280950f049d35f2]

(1) This paragraph applies where an application made under section 78 or 80 is to be determined by an inspector.

(2) The inspector may hold a local inquiry or a hearing in connection with the application if a determination under section 366 provides for the application to be considered in that way.

(3) The costs of the local inquiry or hearing are to be met by the Welsh Ministers; but this is subject to section 372.

(4) The Welsh Ministers or the inspector may appoint an assessor to advise the inspector on any matters that arise—

- (a) at a local inquiry or hearing held by the inspector in connection with the application or in consequence of such an inquiry or hearing, or
- (b) in written representations made to the inspector in connection with the application or in consequence of such representations.

#### 6. Replacement of inspector[^c-cdd5358ecc57f5c6ab7576728ae2b483]

At any time before an inspector has determined an application made under section 78 or 80, the Welsh Ministers may—

- (a) revoke the inspector’s appointment, and
- (b) appoint another inspector to determine the application.

#### 7. Direction that application is to be determined by the Welsh Ministers[^c-ff6b0b7f22554fcc0650c705ce945a7a]

(1) This paragraph applies where the Welsh Ministers give a direction under section 83(3)(b) that an application is to be determined by them instead of by an inspector.

(2) A copy of the direction must be served on—

- (a) the inspector (if any) appointed to determine the application,
- (b) the applicant, and
- (c) the planning authority to which the application would otherwise have been made.

(3) In determining the application, the Welsh Ministers may take account of any report made to them by an inspector previously appointed to determine it.

(4) Subject to that, for the purpose of the determination of the application by the Welsh Ministers, the application is to be treated as if no inspector had ever been appointed to determine it.

#### 8. Revocation of direction[^c-eb9c2ef4fb00d40e52866306cf6592b5]

(1) The Welsh Ministers may by a further direction revoke a direction under section 83(3)(b) at any time before the determination of the application.

(2) The Welsh Ministers must serve a copy of a further direction under this paragraph on the persons on whom paragraph 7(2) required the direction under section 83(3)(b) to be served.

(3) Where the Welsh Ministers give a further direction under this paragraph—

- (a) they must appoint an inspector (“the new inspector”) to determine the application;
- (b) anything done by or on behalf of the Welsh Ministers in connection with the application that might have been done by an inspector is, unless the new inspector directs otherwise, to be treated as having been done by the new inspector;
- (c) subject to that, section 83 and this Schedule apply to the application as if no direction had been given under section 83(3)(b).

#### 9. Supplementary[^c-dab206fed4c0099fcea86176d1748c0d]

It is not a ground of application to the High Court for statutory review under section 376 that an application made under section 78 or 80 ought to have been determined by the Welsh Ministers instead of an inspector, unless the applicant challenges the inspector’s power to determine the application before the decision on the application is given.
