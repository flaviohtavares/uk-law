# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 40

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Guidance[^c-282e8ac6dd83ef827a5e45868762c585]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

In exercising functions conferred by or under Chapters 3 and 4 and by regulations under section 41, a corporate joint committee or planning authority must have regard to any relevant guidance issued by the Welsh Ministers.
