# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 33

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Revision of plan[^key-bc4d38deab90350b5a208f647a324c21]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A plan-making authority may prepare a revision of its plan at any time.

(2) The authority must prepare a revision of its plan—

- (a) if, after a review under section 32, it considers that the plan should be revised;
- (b) at any other time if the Welsh Ministers direct it to do so.

(3) This Part applies to the revision of a plan as it applies to the preparation of the plan.
