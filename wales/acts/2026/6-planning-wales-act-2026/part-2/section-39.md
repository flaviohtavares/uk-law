## 39. Urban development corporations[^c-56fe1f2c2a5e9d194de516a677b73b62]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

The Welsh Ministers may direct that Chapters 3 and 4 do not apply to the area of an urban development corporation.
