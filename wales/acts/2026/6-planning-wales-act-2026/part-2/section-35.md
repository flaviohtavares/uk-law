# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 35

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Annual monitoring report[^c-1efdf89f2dea04d913dcf813ed5995b3]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

A plan-making authority must make an annual report to the Welsh Ministers about the extent to which the objectives set out in its plan are being achieved.
