## 409. Coming into force[^key-c98b60482caa82d02a0448a024826b56]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The following provisions come into force on the day after the day this Act receives Royal Assent—

- (a) Chapter 1 of Part 1 (introduction and overview);
- (b) section 42 (overview of Part 3);
- (c) section 407 (regulations);
- (d) section 408 (interpretation);
- (e) this section;
- (f) section 410 (short title).

(2) The other provisions of this Act come into force on a day appointed by the Welsh Ministers in an order made by Welsh statutory instrument.

(3) An order under subsection (2) may make transitional or saving provision in connection with the coming into force of a provision of this Act.
