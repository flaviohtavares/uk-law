# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 91

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 91. Benefit of planning permission[^key-589ca96dc06804fc3c4e2032018ceef1]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Planning permission to develop land has effect for the benefit of the land and of all persons for the time being interested in it; but this is subject to the terms of the permission.
