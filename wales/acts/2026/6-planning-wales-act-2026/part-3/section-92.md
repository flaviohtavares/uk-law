# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 92

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 92. Permission to erect a building: purposes for which building may be used[^key-3d8a6f1504068904ef15794939cc6a5a]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Planning permission for the erection of a building may specify the purposes for which the building may be used.

(2) If no purposes are specified, the planning permission is to be read as including permission to use the building for the purpose for which it is designed.
