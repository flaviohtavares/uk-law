> This provision is not yet in force.

## 108. Apportionment and recovery of compensation for depreciation[^c-888c36495f823a1884b71c2d87f67e76]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Schedule 10 provides for the apportionment and recovery of compensation for depreciation of the value of an interest in land that becomes payable under section 105 or 106.
