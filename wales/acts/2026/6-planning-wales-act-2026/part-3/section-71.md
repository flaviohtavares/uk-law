# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 71

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 71. Power to refuse to consider application made after issue of enforcement notice[^key-41244d9859f1c8e883c0745c72ab96a7]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

A planning authority may refuse to consider an application for planning permission if—

- (a) granting the application would involve granting planning permission for matters specified in an enforcement notice as constituting a breach of planning control, and
- (b) the enforcement notice was issued before the authority received the application.
