# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 310

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 310. Conditional grant of planning permission[^c-1e372f0e443f2146207664eb1f5c788b]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Operational planning permission must not be granted subject to a condition mentioned in section 67(3)(a) (planning permission granted for a limited period) without the agreement of the statutory undertaker to whose land the permission relates.
