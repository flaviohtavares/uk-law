# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 312

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 312. Discontinuance orders, prohibition orders and protection orders[^key-c8f40c9b75ae51384abada9bfb203cda]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Sections 206 and 207 and Schedules 14 and 15 (discontinuance orders, prohibition orders and protection orders) have effect in relation to operational land of a statutory undertaker as if—

- (a) the reference to the Welsh Ministers in paragraph 4(3) of Schedule 15 were a reference to the Welsh Ministers or the Welsh Ministers and the appropriate Minister, and
- (b) all other references to the Welsh Ministers were references to the Welsh Ministers and the appropriate Minister.
