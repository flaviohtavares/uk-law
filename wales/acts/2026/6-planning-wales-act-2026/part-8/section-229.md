# Planning (Wales) Act 2026, section 229

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 229. Deemed planning permission for advertisements displayed in accordance with regulations[^key-ab5b2f7437a48426ff6402315195d410]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

Where the display of an advertisement—

- (a) involves development of land, and
- (b) complies with control of advertisements regulations,

planning permission is to be treated as having been granted for the development.
