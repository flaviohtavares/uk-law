# Building Safety (Wales) Act 2026, section 93

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 93. Interpretation[^key-53c1b68381c5692590d887998fdc2298]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) In this Part—

- “**person having control**” (“***person â rheolaeth***”) has the meaning given by section 263(1) of the Housing Act [2004 (c. 34)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/34) (but that definition does not apply for the purposes of section 79(6));
- “**person managing**” (“***person sy’n rheoli***”) has the meaning given by section 263(3) of that Act.

(2) Except as provided in section 78(7), other expressions used in this Part that are also used in the Housing Act 2004 have the same meanings in this Part as they have in that Act.
