# Senedd Cymru (Members and Elections) Act 2024, section 15

> Text as at 2026-05-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. Quorum for meetings of the Commission[^c-7e8046840e0c31bf65afd68b65918d77]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 6 of the 2013 Act (proceedings of the Commission), after subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) The Welsh Ministers may by regulations amend subsection (1) to change the quorum, but may not change the quorum to a number which is lower than 3.
