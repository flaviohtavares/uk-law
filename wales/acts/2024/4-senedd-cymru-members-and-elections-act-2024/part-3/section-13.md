> Text as at 2026-05-08.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Number of members of the Commission[^key-810ba66787ac2d07e0e294c02ff4b098]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 4(1) of the 2013 Act (members of the Commission), for paragraph (c) substitute—

> - (c) at least 1 but no more than 7 other members.
