# Senedd Cymru (Members and Elections) Act 2024, section 17

> Text as at 2026-05-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Senedd constituencies for a general election held after 6 April 2026 and before regulations under section 49J of the 2013 Act take effect[^c-b0fe1d80999a57b7f8474d1d69adc029]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 2 makes provision about the Senedd constituencies for which Members of the Senedd will be elected at‍ a general election the poll for which is held after 6 April 2026 and before the first set of regulations made under section 49J of the 2013 Act takes effect; and in particular, about the functions of the Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru in determining what those constituencies are to be.
