# Senedd Cymru (Members and Elections) Act 2024, section 23

> Text as at 2026-05-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Interpretation[^c-4c004139cd9569b4126eb539a0521211]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Act—

- “**the 2006 Act**” (“***Deddf 2006***”) is to be interpreted in accordance with section 1;
- “**the 2013 Act**” (“***Deddf 2013***”) is to be interpreted in accordance with section 11(2);
- “**general election**” (“***etholiad cyffredinol***”) means an ordinary general election or an extraordinary general election held under Part 1 of the 2006 Act;
- “**the Senedd**” (“***y Senedd***”) means Senedd Cymru.
