Senedd Cymru (Members and Elections) Act 2024

2024 asc 4

An Act of Senedd Cymru to make provision about Members of the Senedd and offices held by those Members; Senedd Cymru constituencies; returning and maintaining Senedd Cymru; the Local Democracy and Boundary Commission for Wales; and for connected purposes.

\[24 June 2024\]

**Having been passed by Senedd Cymru and having received the assent of His Majesty, it is enacted as follows:**
