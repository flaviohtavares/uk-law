## 3. Frequency of ordinary general elections[^key-da6e38f32d45ee4c8425e6678bd0462a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In section 3(1) (ordinary general elections) of the 2006 Act, for “fifth” substitute “fourth”.

(2) In the Wales Act [2014 (c. 29)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/29), omit section 1 (frequency of ordinary general elections).
