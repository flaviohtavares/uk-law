# Senedd Cymru (Members and Elections) Act 2024, section 6

> Text as at 2026-05-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Disqualification from being a Member of the Senedd or a candidate[^c-bd8bf1e1eb7193e7dfa82e2a276a74f5]

*Extent: E+W*

In Part 1 of Schedule 1A (disqualification) to the 2006 Act, after paragraph 7 insert—

> **8. Persons not registered in electoral register at an address in Wales**
>
> A person who is not registered in the register of local government electors at an address within a Senedd constituency.
