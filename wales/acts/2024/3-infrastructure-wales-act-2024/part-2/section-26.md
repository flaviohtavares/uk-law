## 26. Directions under section 22: regulations about procedure[^key-0624c4a1639e07e6859908603153e2c2][^key-792406168335a89c2f5de60a2b4bca4d]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations may make provision about the following procedural matters in connection with directions under section 22, 23 or 24—

- (a) time limits for making decisions following requests for directions;
- (b) the form of requests for directions;
- (c) information to be provided in connection with requests for directions;
- (d) the persons or persons of a description to be notified in connection with requests for directions.
