## 19. Requirement for infrastructure consent[^key-01a2e8163ef24823790708972d8130a6][^key-c66d5e146ca961eabf1e11b0cea3ac71]

*Extent: E+W*

The consent of the Welsh Ministers (“infrastructure consent”) is required for development to the extent that the development is or forms part of a significant infrastructure project.
