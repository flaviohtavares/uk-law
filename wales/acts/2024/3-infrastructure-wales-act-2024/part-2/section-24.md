# Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024, section 24

## 24. Directions specifying that development is not a significant infrastructure project[^c-bb7c157f9a5917ec91080f460d851240][^c-e37eece2d9c7b3b382ad0711accc3221]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may give a direction specifying development that would otherwise be a significant infrastructure project as not being a significant infrastructure project.

(2) Development specified under this section is not to be treated as a significant infrastructure project for the purposes of this Act.

(3) The Welsh Ministers may only give a direction under subsection (1) if the development will (when completed) be partly in Wales or the Welsh marine area.

(4) As soon as reasonably practicable after giving a direction under subsection (1), the Welsh Ministers must—

- (a) publish the direction, and
- (b) lay a statement about the direction before Senedd Cymru explaining its effect and why it was made.
