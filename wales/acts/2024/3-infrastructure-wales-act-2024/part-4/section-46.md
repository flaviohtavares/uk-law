## 46. Power to enter Crown land in connection with examination[^key-d74975554e1723e88d598f950a748c47][^key-2746f91e080e07f925faf8354573ddde]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 45 applies to Crown land subject to subsections (2) and (3).

(2) A person must not enter Crown land unless the person (“**P**”) has the permission of—

- (a) a person appearing to P to be entitled to give it, or
- (b) the appropriate Crown authority.

(3) Subsections (2)(b), (3) and (4) of section 45 do not apply in relation to anything done by virtue of this section.
