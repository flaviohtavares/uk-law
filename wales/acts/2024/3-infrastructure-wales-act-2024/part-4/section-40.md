# Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-03-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2024/3

## 40. Appointing an examining authority[^key-a878896947dcb4d54da6e0a5a4ea8e45]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-d5e4db021205680062fd5b44d0d9bcf8] The Welsh Ministers must appoint a person or a panel of persons to examine each valid application for infrastructure consent.

(2)[^key-22a03e509861a0122b48ef27a7b41407] The Welsh Ministers may appoint a person or a panel of persons to examine an application to revoke or change an infrastructure consent order.

(3)[^key-d5e4db021205680062fd5b44d0d9bcf8] The Welsh Ministers must publish a document setting out the criteria to be applied in deciding whether to appoint a person or panel of persons under subsection (2).

(4)[^key-d5e4db021205680062fd5b44d0d9bcf8] The Welsh Ministers may at any time in relation to an application—

- (a) revoke an appointment of a person or a person on a panel under this section, or
- (b) make an appointment of a person or person to a panel under this section.

(5)[^key-d5e4db021205680062fd5b44d0d9bcf8] Regulations may make further provision for or in connection with the appointment of an examining authority under subsection (1) or (2).

(6)[^key-d5e4db021205680062fd5b44d0d9bcf8] The regulations may include (among other things) provision for or in connection with—

- (a) appointing members to a panel (including provision about changing membership of a panel and the consequences of any such changes);
- (b) allocating functions to persons on a panel and decision-making by a panel;
- (c) replacing a panel with a person or a new panel or replacing a person with a panel or a new person (and the consequences of such changes);
- (d) conditions of appointment.

(7)[^key-d5e4db021205680062fd5b44d0d9bcf8] In this Act, “**examining authority**” means a person or panel of persons appointed under this section.
