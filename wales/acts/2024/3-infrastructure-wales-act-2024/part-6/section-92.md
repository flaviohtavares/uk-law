# Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024, section 92

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-03-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2024/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 92. Changing and revoking infrastructure consent orders: formalities[^key-e81dcaffd2cca05db0842d4b0028c20d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies to—

- (a)[^key-73b85ccf8b9de7ddda7d12b2f9c2c8bd] an order made under section 87;
- (b)[^key-73b85ccf8b9de7ddda7d12b2f9c2c8bd] a notice issued under section 87;
- (c)[^key-16908fa17d4c0891dae3a2251478f9aa] an order made under section 90.

(2)[^key-73b85ccf8b9de7ddda7d12b2f9c2c8bd] The Welsh Ministers must publish the order or the notice (as the case may be) in such manner as they think appropriate.

(3)[^key-46c3a58b49af232c5639f20070034961][^key-59f6070250e9119eff5c2aa9d5619395] But if the order is required to be contained in a statutory instrument (by virtue of section 87(4) or section 91(7)), as soon as practicable after the instrument containing the order is made, the Welsh Ministers must lay before Senedd Cymru a copy of the instrument.
