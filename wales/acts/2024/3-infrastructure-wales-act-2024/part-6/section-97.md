# Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024, section 97

## 97. Benefit of infrastructure consent order[^c-6fe1aebf6612adcef320a85a6574030a][^c-6fda213f75fa3d1423a4ab6414c8da93]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If an infrastructure consent order is made in respect of any land, the order has effect for the benefit of the land and all persons for the time being interested in the land.

(2) Subsection (1) is subject to any contrary provision made in the order.
