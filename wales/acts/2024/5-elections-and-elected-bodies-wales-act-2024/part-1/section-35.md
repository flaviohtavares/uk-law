# Elections and Elected Bodies (Wales) Act 2024, section 35

> Text as at 2025-07-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Authorised persons not required to pay through election agent[^c-0cfd295e399343ada1f39ad63953116b][^c-f5ca5f57db70298feea61f2529b0c7d9]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 73(5) of the 1983 Act (payment of expenses through election agent), in paragraph (ca), omit “, otherwise than in relation to an election in Wales under the local government Act,”.
