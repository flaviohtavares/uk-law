# Elections and Elected Bodies (Wales) Act 2024, section 31

> Text as at 2025-07-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31. Interpretation of this Chapter[^c-304a9e671a9c0e47f3572aa8205d406c][^c-007f01fa8bac0ebbb89a9f07d500b5f9]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Chapter—

- “**financial assistance**” (“***cymorth ariannol***”) means grants, loans or guarantees;
- “**registered political party**” (”*plaid wleidyddol gofrestredig*”) means a party registered under Part 2 of the 2000 Act.
