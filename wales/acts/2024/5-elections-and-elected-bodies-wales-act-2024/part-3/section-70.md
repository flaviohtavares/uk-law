# Elections and Elected Bodies (Wales) Act 2024, section 70

> Text as at 2025-07-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 70. General interpretation[^key-e0910815840fb1cb9c82317befb3747b]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Act—

- “1983 Act”(“***Deddf 1983***”) means the Representation of the People Act [1983 (c. 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/2);
- ”2000 Act”(“***Deddf 2000***”) means the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act [2000 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/41);
- “2013 Act”(“***Deddf 2013***”) means the [Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru etc. Act 2013 (anaw 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2013/4);
- “principal council”(“***prif gyngor***”) means the council of a county or county borough in Wales.
