# Elections and Elected Bodies (Wales) Act 2024, section 62

> Text as at 2025-07-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 62. Disqualification for corrupt or illegal practice: local government elections[^key-58aa6333369ff7e8a258298a38ca38d2]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 80A(1) of the Local Government Act [1972 (c. 70)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/70) (disqualification for election or being a member of a local authority in Wales), after paragraph (b) insert—

> - (ba) the person is incapable of being elected to or holding elective office in a district council in Northern Ireland under Part 10 of the Electoral Law Act (Northern Ireland) [1962 (c. 14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1962/14) (corrupt or illegal practices);

.
