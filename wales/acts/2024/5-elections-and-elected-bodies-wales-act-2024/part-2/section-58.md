# Elections and Elected Bodies (Wales) Act 2024, section 58

> Text as at 2025-07-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Transfer of property, rights and liabilities[^key-c1aaa1f985c697d6f1aef943d4e9f5a9][^key-97ef44c63284565a0134f2cf1486645e]

*Extent: E+W*

All property, rights and liabilities vested in the Independent Remuneration Panel for Wales immediately before its abolition by section 56 are transferred to the Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru.
