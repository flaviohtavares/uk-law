> Text as at 2025-07-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 56. Abolition of Independent Remuneration Panel for Wales[^c-25f0c9e8585576adf6d8c767e93b3941][^c-eb17cfb041ec67d33317337fca64f65b]

*Extent: E+W*

In the [Local Government (Wales) Measure 2011 (nawm 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/mwa/2011/4) (Independent Remuneration Panel for Wales: functions relating to remuneration of members), omit sections 141 to 159 and Schedule 2.
