# Elections and Elected Bodies (Wales) Act 2024, section 46

> Text as at 2025-07-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. Meaning of “mandatory consultees” in Part 3 of the 2013 Act[^c-c29b021681be627f5f2b44456278852a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The 2013 Act is amended as follows.

(2) In section 34(3) (pre-review procedure), after paragraph (c) insert—

> - (ca) the National Park authority for a National Park in an area affected by the review,
> - (cb) the Port Health authority constituted under section 2 of the Public Health (Control of Disease) Act [1984 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/22) for a port health district in an area affected by the review,
> - (cc) the Welsh Language Commissioner,

.
