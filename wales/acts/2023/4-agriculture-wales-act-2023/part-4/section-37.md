# Agriculture (Wales) Act 2023, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2023/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Conditions of tree felling licences[^key-2ede1ab1663fa8ad0f42c933c7dc7264][^key-ccd571f2cd1d5f89e38f60859a27b518]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 10 of the [Forestry Act 1967 (c. 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1967/10) (application for felling licence and decision of appropriate authority), in subsection (2), at the end of paragraph (b), insert

> ; or
>
> - (c) in relation to land in Wales, after consultation with the applicant for the licence, for the purpose of—
>   - (i) conserving or enhancing natural beauty;
>   - (ii) conserving flora, fauna, geological or physiographical features, or natural habitats.
