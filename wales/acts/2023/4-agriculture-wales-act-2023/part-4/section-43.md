# Agriculture (Wales) Act 2023, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2023/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43. Service of documents[^key-0df3bfcce723bcd7e345d18bf2b03230][^key-7f225a9b2322f65c530e1bb1fe178a92]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 30 (service of documents) of the [Forestry Act 1967 (c.10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1967/10) is amended as follows.

(2) After subsection (5), insert—

> (6) For the purposes of this section, any reference in this Part to the giving of a notice or document by the Natural Resources Body for Wales is to be treated as if it were a reference to the serving of a document.
