## 28. Meaning of “relevant activity”[^c-0628beff33688326f9015d390f7f3fa1][^c-c804b3fa52f42983b6d37240609c322d]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Chapter, “**relevant activity**” means—

- (a) an activity listed in section 51(1) (meaning of “**agriculture**”);
- (b) an ancillary activity.
