## SCHEDULE 1 — AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS RELEVANT TO MARKETING STANDARDS PROVISIONS

*Extent: E+W*

(introduced by section 34)

### 1. Milk and milk products[^key-50c8774e7c87d22c5fec33b1b5c43c55][^key-ffe9f10b1313db482062990a9a090157]

Products falling within any of entries (a) to (f) in the table in Part XVI of Annex 1 of the CMO Regulation.

### 2. Spreadable fats[^key-c5b382e1983279745c54f3fdb42986db][^key-c46f5efdaa24b9e814f95a318b7a4d68]

Products that—

- (a) fall within any of paragraphs (a) to (c) in Point 1 of Part VII of Annex VII of the CMO Regulation, and
- (b) have a fat content of at least 10% but not more than 90% by weight.

### 3. Beef and veal[^key-3d04f7676f2d7baeec945f9ce66f5895][^key-747505862c6dce4b3581a32c92229c22]

Products falling within the table in Part XV of Annex 1 of the CMO Regulation, but excluding any entry in the table for live animals.

### 4. Poultry and poultrymeat[^key-45b874d0ef894b68b3a4878deee29c17][^key-298679e5499e22cd05414d4088c29b9f]

Products falling within the table in Part XX of Annex 1 of the CMO Regulation, including any entry in the table for live poultry.

### 5. Eggs and egg products[^key-f4a2fd16793832d4d1db3ddd95b5b1c6][^key-44d57dcd8038be1ab31aa60cd2cd26a7]

Products falling within the table in Part XIX of Annex 1 of the CMO Regulation.

### 6. Fruit and vegetables, other than olives[^key-16ac301aab0da794fda2a2e099a611db][^key-8777273b627718a8dc1b12faad2ce923]

Products falling within the table in any of Parts IX to XI of Annex 1 of the CMO Regulation.

### 7. Olive oil and table olives[^key-a25b2fc111c1f6bf4cbe43bed3ae8743][^key-60e11fddad9cdcc23d9994b8835feac6]

Products falling within the table in Part VII of Annex 1 of the CMO Regulation.

### 8. Hops[^key-ead2533c65ea68cf9cb7e93ac2dacf43][^key-e488477713e87974a7645807a0d27dad]

Products falling within the table in Part VI of Annex 1 of the CMO Regulation.

### 9. Wine[^key-7296105f416bfcf9708bdb563593affb][^key-c651e947cc5d46eea81cdc19d8aff04a]

Products falling within the table in Part XII of Annex 1 of the CMO Regulation.

### 10. Aromatised wine[^key-6da1ad5cab744b24b3d6801ada0830d0][^key-0f52c5d3ac1d3bfbb97e84421bd99dc1]

Products falling within the definition of “aromatised wine products” in Article 3 of the Aromatised Wine Regulation.

### 11. Interpretation[^key-5a48f8373dc302288372c81411945a12][^key-e538ca57597053b3aec3aaaab7947b50]

In this Schedule “**the Aromatised Wine Regulation**” means [Regulation (EU) No 251/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/0251) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 February 2014 on the definition, description, presentation, labelling and the protection of geographical indications of aromatised wine products.
