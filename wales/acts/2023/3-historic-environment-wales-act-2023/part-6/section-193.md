## 193. Duty to maintain and publish list of historic place names[^c-c0ff4e73afd1aa2ce080178b247233ac][^c-81d7363681f50088e8073d2dfe7fe871]

*Extent: E+W*

The Welsh Ministers must maintain a list of historic place names in Wales, and must publish the up-to-date list.
