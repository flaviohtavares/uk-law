## 170. Form of documents[^c-b7bf5a5ebd5f7351b394b531473809ea][^c-519a65e86736f972f30a8b0726d937ef]

*Extent: E+W*

The Welsh Ministers may by regulations specify the form and content of any notice, order or other document that a local authority is authorised or required to serve, make or issue under or by virtue of Part 3, Part 4, this Part or Part 7 as it applies for the purposes of any of those Parts.
