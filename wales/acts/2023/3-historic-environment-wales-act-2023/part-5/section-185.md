## 185. Meaning of “decision document” and “correctable error”[^key-553f49d4d9730847094256921b3c038b][^key-8f35256e60073ccc1c12c89527a86207]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies for the purposes of sections 186 and 187.

(2) “**Decision document**” means a document which records—

- (a) a decision to which section 182 applies (see subsection (2) of that section),
- (b) a decision on an appeal under section 127 (appeal against enforcement notice), or
- (c) any other decision made under or by virtue of Part 3, Part 4 or this Part that is of a description specified in regulations made by the Welsh Ministers.

(3) “**Correctable error**” means an error which—

- (a) is contained in any part of the decision document which records the decision, but
- (b) is not part of any reasons given for the decision,

and “**error**” includes omission.
