## 202. Making claims for compensation[^c-eb421d4a4e17f00b958ae58135ed05dd][^c-9941f20ba1fd043fccf1dc1651d87997]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may by regulations—

- (a) make provision about how a claim for compensation under this Act must be made;
- (b) amend any provision of this Act which specifies the period within which a claim for compensation must be made.

(2) The Welsh Ministers may extend the period for making a claim for compensation under this Act in a particular case, if they are satisfied that there are good reasons for doing so.

(3) The period for making a claim may be extended—

- (a) at any time, whether before or after the period ends, and
- (b) more than once.
