# Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023, section 198

## 198. Offences in connection with section 197[^c-86d2de696ce6782884ef1c63f3fdd91a][^c-af7542e1896d715ea2b5b7313c34e77a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person required to provide information by a notice under section 197 commits an offence if the person fails, without reasonable excuse, to provide the information.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.

(3) A person required to provide information by a notice under section 197 commits an offence if the person, in purported compliance with the notice, knowingly provides information which is false or misleading in a material respect.

(4) A person guilty of an offence under subsection (3) is liable on summary conviction, or on conviction on indictment, to a fine.
