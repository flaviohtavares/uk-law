# Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023, section 211

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 211. Consequential and transitional provision etc.[^key-8f983b4621362a8c9f5c97c16989a8b7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-5eb0ddf8500d68f8743d08d3435800c6] Schedule 13 contains minor and consequential amendments and repeals.

(2)[^key-5eb0ddf8500d68f8743d08d3435800c6] Schedule 14 contains transitional and saving provisions.

(3)[^key-8f983b4621362a8c9f5c97c16989a8b7] The Welsh Ministers may by regulations—

- (a) make provision that is incidental or supplementary to, or consequential on, any provision of this Act;
- (b) make transitional, transitory or saving provision in connection with any provision of this Act.

(4)[^key-8f983b4621362a8c9f5c97c16989a8b7] Regulations under subsection (3) may amend, repeal or revoke any enactment (including any provision of this Act).
