## 203. Determination of compensation claims by Upper Tribunal[^c-14889208e8f2647011dfa79b3778611a][^c-aae1f42bc4d786091d67156ef6c1b7d4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any dispute about compensation under this Act is to be referred to and determined by the Upper Tribunal.

(2) Section 4 of the [Land Compensation Act 1961 (c. 33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1961/33) (costs) applies to the determination of a question referred under this section as it applies to the determination of a question referred under section 1 of that Act, but as if references to the acquiring authority were references to the person from whom compensation is claimed.
