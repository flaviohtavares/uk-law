## 129. Grounds for appeal not to be raised in other proceedings[^key-be5eaeb777a232ff6b921cc2a490ba55][^key-1dc08a56c8433b204b9657fdc4ac8fd4]

*Extent: E+W*

The validity of an enforcement notice may not be questioned, on any of the grounds on which an appeal may be made under section 127, in any proceedings except an appeal under that section.
