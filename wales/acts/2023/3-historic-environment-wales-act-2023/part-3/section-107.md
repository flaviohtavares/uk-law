# Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023, section 107

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (33).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2023/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 107. Modification and revocation of consent[^key-c6b53d063c6d4bdb09110ca5f47255e9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-95c27f7ddc0afdfbde0357a26c0cad2e] Where listed building consent has been granted on an application or appeal under this Part, the planning authority in whose area the listed building is situated or the Welsh Ministers may by order modify or revoke the consent to any extent.

(2)[^key-95c27f7ddc0afdfbde0357a26c0cad2e] An order modifying or revoking listed building consent for any works may be made at any time before the works are completed, but does not affect consent for works carried out before the order takes effect.

(3) In Schedule 8—

- (a)[^key-5fcf4476bc0b1d3e1b5178113af69e50][^key-160e794efd4e7952015ed5d63e6d4b58] Part 1 makes provision about the procedures that must be followed before an order made by a planning authority under this section takes effect (either with or without confirmation by the Welsh Ministers);
- (b)[^key-95c27f7ddc0afdfbde0357a26c0cad2e] Part 2 makes provision about the procedure that must be followed before the Welsh Ministers make an order under this section.
