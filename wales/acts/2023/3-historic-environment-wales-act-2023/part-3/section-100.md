## 100. Right to appeal against planning authority decision or failure to make decision[^c-9e9c5862072f875e67da4a8b1bc94987][^c-c008a835e570334cb5b6f147dfa01186]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-59ef8d00254404781d7bf9166f232de4] This section applies where an application has been made to a planning authority for—

- (a) listed building consent,
- (b) the variation or removal of conditions of listed building consent, or
- (c) approval of details of works under a condition of listed building consent.

(2)[^c-59ef8d00254404781d7bf9166f232de4] The applicant may appeal to the Welsh Ministers if the planning authority—

- (a) refuses the application, or
- (b) grants the application subject to conditions or, in the case of an application for the variation or removal of conditions, grants it and imposes new conditions.

(3)[^c-59ef8d00254404781d7bf9166f232de4] The applicant may also appeal to the Welsh Ministers if the planning authority has done none of the following within the determination period—

- (a) given notice to the applicant of its decision on the application, or
- (b) in the case of an application for listed building consent or for the variation or removal of conditions, given notice to the applicant that it has—
  - (i) exercised its power under section 93 to refuse to consider the application, or
  - (ii) referred the application to the Welsh Ministers under section 94.

(4) In subsection (3) “**the determination period**” means—

- (a)[^c-e3745bf9e1eedc8cc57355d1ed199b79] the period specified in regulations made by the Welsh Ministers, or
- (b)[^c-59ef8d00254404781d7bf9166f232de4] a longer period agreed in writing between the applicant and the planning authority.
