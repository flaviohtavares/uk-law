# Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023, section 147

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 147. Steps for preservation of listed buildings in disrepair[^c-99d0f35a2a44512cfae9358a07f63148]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Welsh Ministers may by regulations make provision for and in connection with conferring powers on local authorities or the Welsh Ministers to take steps to secure the proper preservation of listed buildings which have fallen into disrepair.

(2) The regulations may, in particular, provide for—

- (a) notices requiring owners of listed buildings which have fallen into disrepair to carry out works to secure their proper preservation (“preservation notices”);
- (b) appeals against preservation notices;
- (c) offences for failure to comply with preservation notices.

(3) Regulations under this section—

- (a) may disapply, or apply or reproduce with or without modifications, any provision of this Part or Part 5 or 7;
- (b) may amend this Part or those Parts.

(4) Regulations under this section may not make any provision that binds the Crown.
