# Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023, section 161

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (33).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2023/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 161. Requirement for demolition to be authorised[^key-d3906ee542e5900d8f69526a94f8e9d2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-486d2ad4b01e6dd308e624b40beb181c][^key-c4cb13daf2deb442a9b66cb94b498255] A person must not carry out works for the demolition of a building to which this section applies, or cause such works to be carried out, unless the works are authorised under section 162.

(2) This section applies to any building in a conservation area, except—

- (a)[^key-486d2ad4b01e6dd308e624b40beb181c] a building which is a scheduled monument (but see section 11);
- (b)[^key-486d2ad4b01e6dd308e624b40beb181c] a listed building (but see section 88);
- (c)[^key-2ec06d8ac0515994e3bc015a92d6f39c] a building of a description specified in regulations made by the Welsh Ministers;
- (d)[^key-486d2ad4b01e6dd308e624b40beb181c] a building of a description specified in a direction given to an individual planning authority by the Welsh Ministers.

(3)[^key-486d2ad4b01e6dd308e624b40beb181c] The Welsh Ministers may direct a planning authority that, despite any provision made by regulations under subsection (2)(c), this section is to apply to a building of a description specified in the direction.

(4)[^key-486d2ad4b01e6dd308e624b40beb181c] Subsection (1) does not prohibit works carried out by or on behalf of the Crown in the circumstances set out in paragraphs (a) to (d) of section 117(4) (emergency works).

(5)[^key-486d2ad4b01e6dd308e624b40beb181c] Schedule 11 makes provision about the effect of this section ceasing to apply to a building.

(6)[^key-d2ab070bd04e9e30b0fa066ab0eb497a] This section is subject to section 20(1)(c)(iii) of the Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024 (authorisation by conservation area consent not required for development to the extent that infrastructure consent required).”.
