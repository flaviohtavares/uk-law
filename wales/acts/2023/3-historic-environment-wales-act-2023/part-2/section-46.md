# Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023, section 46

## 46. Supplementary provision about guardianship deeds[^key-b07f32edd2bd0994c65c0cb49a9f54c2][^key-9e503e5206101f6beefda6c4bbc3ac5e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A guardianship deed is a local land charge.

(2) Every person deriving title to a monument of special historic interest from, through or under any person who has executed a guardianship deed is bound by the deed unless the person derives title by virtue of any disposal made by the person who executed the deed before the date of the deed.

(3) The Welsh Ministers or a local authority may not become guardians of a building or structure occupied as a dwelling by any person other than the caretaker of the building or structure or a member of the caretaker’s family.

(4) Any person who has any estate or interest in a monument under guardianship has the same right and title to, and estate or interest in, the monument in all respects as if the monument were not under guardianship; but this is subject to any provision to the contrary in this Part.
