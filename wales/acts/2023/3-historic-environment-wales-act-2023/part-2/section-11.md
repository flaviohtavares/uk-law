# Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (33).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2023/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. Requirement for works to be authorised[^c-75a6c6219b726efa96ec613a50d167f8][^c-fcd3c619b0c867e72b530447fb8d128a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person must not carry out works to which this section applies, or cause or permit such works to be carried out, unless the works are authorised under this Chapter.

(2) This section applies to—

- (a) works resulting in the demolition or destruction of, or any damage to, a scheduled monument;
- (b) works for the purpose of removing or repairing a scheduled monument or any part of it, or of making any alterations or additions to the monument or any part of it;
- (c) flooding or tipping operations on land in, on or under which a scheduled monument is situated.

(3)[^c-c3930f6d81647b1ef35ef8114710c939] This section is subject to section 20(1)(c)(i) of the Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024 (class authorisations and authorisation by scheduled monument consent not required for development to the extent that infrastructure consent required).
