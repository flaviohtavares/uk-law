# Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023, section 70

## 70. Compensation for damage caused by exercise of certain powers under this Part[^c-b1130afa870d8441efe59d27a97ab651][^c-f556d474c1ba01c6054669f2bed0b439]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies to any power to enter, or to do anything on, any land under section 40 or sections 65 to 68.

(2) Any person interested in land is entitled, on making a claim to the Welsh Ministers, to be paid compensation by them for any damage caused to the land or to property on it in the exercise of a power to which this section applies.

(3) A claim for compensation under this section must be made in writing within 6 months beginning with the day the damage was caused (or if the damage was caused over more than one day, the last day it was caused).
