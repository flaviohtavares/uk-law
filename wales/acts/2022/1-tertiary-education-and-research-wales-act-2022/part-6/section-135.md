# Tertiary Education and Research (Wales) Act 2022, section 135

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-08-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2022/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 135. Other information, advice and guidance[^c-a655f4ba01bc398b49b8aefa726f1a3c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-cc7f32be63c997946c346b8f1aff0253] The Commission may give advice and issue guidance (whether general or specific) to any person about the provision of tertiary education or any matter connected with the Commission’s functions.

(2)[^c-cc7f32be63c997946c346b8f1aff0253] The Commission must—

- (a) identify good practice in relation to the sharing of information by the persons specified in subsection (3), and
- (b) give advice and issue guidance about such practice to those persons.

(3)[^c-724ba247e448553a5f5867590de9bcd6] The persons specified in this subsection are—

- (a) a registered provider,
- (b) a person other than a registered provider providing tertiary education funded or otherwise secured by the Commission.

(4)[^c-cc7f32be63c997946c346b8f1aff0253] The Commission must publish any guidance it issues under subsections (1) and (2).

(5)[^c-cc7f32be63c997946c346b8f1aff0253] The Commission must establish systems for collecting information which is designed to secure that its decisions with regard to tertiary education are made on a sound basis.
