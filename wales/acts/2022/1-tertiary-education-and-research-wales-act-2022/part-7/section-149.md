## 149. Short title[^key-3b30806823bfa7833a423f49bbd6fceb]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The short title of this Act is the Tertiary Education and Research (Wales) Act 2022.

(2) This Act is to be included in the list of Education Acts set out in section 578 of the [Education Act 1996 (c. 56)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56).
