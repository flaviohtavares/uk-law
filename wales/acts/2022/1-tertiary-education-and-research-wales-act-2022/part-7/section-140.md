## 140. Duty to consult the Commission on careers services[^key-5f1b199cf03d0827628ea902f23df2f3][^key-340f821cc902475a443e15b6db161d08]

*Extent: E+W*

In the [Employment and Training Act 1973 (c.50)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1973/50), after section 9 insert—

> **9A. Duty of Welsh Ministers to consult the Commission for Tertiary Education and Research**
>
> (1) In each financial year the Welsh Ministers must consult the Commission for Tertiary Education and Research on strategic priorities in the next financial year for the performance of their duty in section 8 and the exercise of their power in section 9.
>
> (2) In this section, “financial year means a period of 12 months ending on 31 March.
