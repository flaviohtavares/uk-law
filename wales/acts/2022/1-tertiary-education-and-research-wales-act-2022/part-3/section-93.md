# Tertiary Education and Research (Wales) Act 2022, section 93

## 93. Education and training for persons aged 16 to 19[^c-9ac6f0d1f50abec64fba273b1365aea5][^c-00807f840e8a9fe3465aecce6603fce0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Commission must secure the provision of proper facilities‍ for Wales for—

- (a) further education suitable to the requirements of persons who are over compulsory school age but have not attained the age of 19, and
- (b) training suitable to the requirements of such persons.

(2) Facilities are proper if they are—

- (a) of a quantity sufficient to meet the reasonable needs of individuals,
- (b) of a quality adequate to meet those needs, and
- (c) sufficient to satisfy the entitlements conferred under section 33F of the [Learning and Skills Act 2000 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/21).
