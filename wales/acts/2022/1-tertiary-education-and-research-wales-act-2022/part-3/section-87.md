## 87. Policy on funding powers[^c-0adbe861aabb0950d8c3e917555a4653]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-c352e02ff19d0b1235a3fdbc8053e0ae][^c-b81c5ac847a417036aaef08cf6d5fe8f] The Commission must publish a statement of its policy on how it intends to exercise its funding powers.

(2)[^c-121c3cd1fb1f9997d5ca49332da03fc9] In preparing the statement, the Commission must have regard to the principle that decisions about the provision or securing of financial resources should be made in a way that is transparent.

(3)[^c-ae7f3e6fabc752761b06a5fe8ec84d6f] The Commission must keep the statement under review and may revise it.

(4)[^c-ae7f3e6fabc752761b06a5fe8ec84d6f] Before publishing the statement or a revised statement, the Commission must consult such persons as it considers appropriate.

(5)[^c-c352e02ff19d0b1235a3fdbc8053e0ae][^c-a890817407d69b9550e0155f806c2929][^c-5cbcc5765970b6fe166e2101792fa16a] The Commission’s funding powers are its powers to provide or secure financial resources under sections 88, 89, 97, 101, 103, 104 and 105.
