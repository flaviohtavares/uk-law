## 127. Complaints procedures[^key-bd06cf0cb31d1a926defefdfa1f5db21][^key-cca96fc4947ab0963c8aca21e4a4d202]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Commission must take such steps as appear to it appropriate to ensure that a relevant tertiary education provider—

- (a) has in place a procedure for investigating complaints about an act or omission of the provider which are made by persons who are undertaking or have undertaken relevant courses, and
- (b) takes reasonable steps to make the procedure known to persons undertaking relevant courses.

(2) For the meaning of “relevant course” and “relevant tertiary education provider”, see section 126(10).
