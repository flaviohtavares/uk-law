# Tertiary Education and Research (Wales) Act 2022, section 67

## 67. Annual plan of the Chief Inspector[^c-9e7b9db9a64927773727db6e18939877][^c-5a36bd58a1e54f67d3ec5b3213eb5924]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Chief Inspector must, for each financial year, prepare a plan setting out estimates of⁠—

- (a) the expenditure necessary, in the financial year to which the plan relates, in order to secure that the functions of the Chief Inspector are exercised effectively, and
- (b) the income which the Chief Inspector will receive in that financial year and which may be applied towards meeting the expenses of the Chief Inspector.

(2) The plan must contain proposals for the management of any funds which may be provided to the Chief Inspector in that financial year under—

- (a) section 68 of this Act (funding by the Commission), and
- (b) section 104(4) of the [Government of Wales Act 1998 (c. 38)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/38) (funding by the Welsh Ministers).

(3) The plan must be submitted, for the purposes of consultation, to—

- (a) the Commission, and
- (b) the Welsh Ministers,

by such time before the beginning of the financial year to which it relates as they may jointly direct.

(4)[^c-d202774587f1d5befa0e2dc97b7614b9] The Chief Inspector may publish the plan after it has been approved by—

- (a) the Commission, and
- (b) the Welsh Ministers.
