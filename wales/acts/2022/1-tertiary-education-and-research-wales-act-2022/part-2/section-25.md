# Tertiary Education and Research (Wales) Act 2022, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-08-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2022/1

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. The register[^c-cb34ad184a61f3db9ee1c24c144d900d][^c-f0a37e443ac946ab4f99a7cc620c8675]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-d5e4000bcd672bdefab3b200b740b345][^c-c5deefcd3e10a93ba1e0318aceae079b] The Commission must establish and maintain a register of tertiary education providers in Wales (referred to in this Act as “**the register**”).

(2)[^c-ed7c7eb0d98d370949b706c94eefa9ff] The Welsh Ministers must, by regulations, specify one or more categories of registration for which the Commission must make provision in the register.

(3)[^c-ed7c7eb0d98d370949b706c94eefa9ff] A category of registration specified in the regulations must relate to the provision of one or more kinds of tertiary education.

(4)[^c-d5e4000bcd672bdefab3b200b740b345][^c-c5deefcd3e10a93ba1e0318aceae079b] The Commission must register a tertiary education provider in a category of the register if—

- (a) its governing body applies for it to be registered in the category,
- (b) it is a tertiary education provider in Wales,
- (c) it provides, or has provided on its behalf, the kind of tertiary education that relates to the category,
- (d)[^c-79341da255fcf4a0bee812f40c9e2a7b] it satisfies the initial registration conditions applicable to it in respect of the registration sought (see section 27),
- (e) registration is not prohibited by provision made in regulations under subsection (5), and
- (f) the application complies with any requirements imposed under subsection (7).

(5)[^c-ed7c7eb0d98d370949b706c94eefa9ff] The Welsh Ministers may, by regulations, prohibit the registration of a tertiary education provider in one category of the register at the same time that it is registered in one or more of the other categories.

(6)[^c-ed7c7eb0d98d370949b706c94eefa9ff] The Commission must not register a tertiary education provider in the register otherwise than—

- (a)[^c-d5e4000bcd672bdefab3b200b740b345] in a category of registration specified in regulations under subsection (2);
- (b)[^c-d5e4000bcd672bdefab3b200b740b345] in accordance with subsection (4), section 44 (change in registration category without application) and any regulations made under subsection (5).

(7)[^c-8d47032e9d53f3e73ad663815b977b91] The Commission may determine—

- (a) the form of an application for registration,
- (b) the information to be contained in it or provided with it, and
- (c) the way in which an application is to be submitted.

(8)[^c-ed7c7eb0d98d370949b706c94eefa9ff] The Welsh Ministers may, by regulations, make provision about the information which must be contained in a tertiary education provider’s entry in the register.

(9)[^c-ed7c7eb0d98d370949b706c94eefa9ff] Once registered, a tertiary education provider’s ongoing registration in a category of the register is subject to the provider satisfying—

- (a)[^c-c8f20a301cd7d03de158b0bd8c8cab25] the general ongoing registration conditions applicable to the provider’s registration in the category and as they may be later revised (see section 28), and
- (b) the specific ongoing registration conditions (if any) imposed on it in that category of registration and as they may be later varied (see section 29).

(10)[^c-b58cfce4fc1d99ae0a1cf68eb6d22555][^c-ed7c7eb0d98d370949b706c94eefa9ff] References in this Part to the ongoing registration conditions of a tertiary education provider are to the conditions mentioned in subsection (9)(a) and (b).

(11)[^c-ed7c7eb0d98d370949b706c94eefa9ff] The Commission must make the information contained in the register, and the information previously contained in it, publicly available by such means as it considers appropriate.
