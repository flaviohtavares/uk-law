## 30. Proportionate conditions etc.[^key-719429e1d9e4fdaf110ffb8c8fa174f7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-e03ab65b3bc500011a9d8423fafa294e] The Commission must ensure that—

- (a) the requirements specified under section 27(2), and
- (b) all ongoing registration conditions,

are proportionate to the Commission’s assessment of the risks posed.

(2)[^key-1f131b7d432efaa2de2ff2bf725be379] In light of its duty under subsection (1), the Commission must keep all ongoing registration conditions under review.
