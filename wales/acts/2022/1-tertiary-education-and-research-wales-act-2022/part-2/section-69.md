## 69. Grounds for intervention[^key-2e6885b0131bdf0f11a63af35543c550][^key-8607113e1232a59427bcd5b193b7eb47]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of sections 70 and 71, the grounds for intervention in the conduct of a tertiary education provider in Wales that is an institution within the further education sector are as follows—

- (a) the provider’s affairs have been or are being mismanaged by its governing body;
- (b) the provider’s governing body has failed to comply with a duty under any enactment;
- (c) the provider’s governing body has acted or is proposing to act unreasonably in the exercise of its functions under any enactment;
- (d) the provider is performing significantly less well than it might in all the circumstances reasonably be expected to perform, or is failing or likely to fail to give an acceptable standard of education or training.
