# Tertiary Education and Research (Wales) Act 2022, section 61

## 61. Additional functions of the Chief Inspector[^c-5e00d7fd454a387b326eb47a2d6d3b40][^c-75ebfaf79e46e69f5d62c4afa8bcce59]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may, by regulations, confer other functions on the Chief Inspector in connection with the education or training described in section 57(1).

(2) The functions conferred by the regulations may (among other things) include functions with respect to training of or for teachers, lecturers, trainers or other persons engaged in the provision of such education or training.
