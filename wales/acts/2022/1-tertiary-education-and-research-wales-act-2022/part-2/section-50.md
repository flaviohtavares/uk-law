# Tertiary Education and Research (Wales) Act 2022, section 50

## 50. Quality assurance frameworks[^key-49a0f2ea9d319a2d0b72ffbbbab7b628]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-ada2e239ebe21b970366358583f7e0ef] The Commission may publish quality assurance frameworks.

(2)[^key-ada2e239ebe21b970366358583f7e0ef] A quality assurance framework is a document setting out guidance and information on matters of policy and practice concerning—

- (a) criteria for assessing the quality of tertiary education;
- (b) processes for assessing the quality of tertiary education;
- (c) the roles and responsibilities of—
  - (i) persons assessing the quality of tertiary education,
  - (ii) providers of tertiary education as regards the quality of tertiary education, and
  - (iii) any other persons as regards the quality of tertiary education as the Commission considers appropriate;
- (d) consideration of the views of learners about the quality of the tertiary education they receive;
- (e) the professional development of members of the tertiary education workforce;
- (f) any other matter the Commission considers relevant to assurance of the quality of tertiary education.

(3)[^key-ada2e239ebe21b970366358583f7e0ef] The Commission may revise, replace or withdraw any quality assurance framework published under this section.

(4) Before publishing a framework (or any revised framework) or withdrawing a framework under this section, the Commission must consult—

- (a)[^key-ea24cbb775b95a753081721c2bf02919] each registered provider,
- (b)[^key-ada2e239ebe21b970366358583f7e0ef] Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales (“the Chief Inspector”), and
- (c)[^key-ada2e239ebe21b970366358583f7e0ef] any other persons the Commission considers appropriate.

(5)[^key-6e405c7527f6b1d7500e99ddf9f53b82] The persons mentioned in subsection (6) must, so far as relevant to the exercise of their functions, have regard to a quality assurance framework published under this section.

(6)[^key-6e405c7527f6b1d7500e99ddf9f53b82] The persons are—

- (a) the Commission;
- (b) the Chief Inspector;
- (c) a designated body (see section 56).

(7)[^key-ada2e239ebe21b970366358583f7e0ef] Nothing in this section affects the Commission’s other powers to issue guidance.

(8)[^key-ada2e239ebe21b970366358583f7e0ef] “**Members of the tertiary education workforce**” has the same meaning as in section 5.
