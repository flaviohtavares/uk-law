# Tertiary Education and Research (Wales) Act 2022, section 9

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Promoting tertiary education through the medium of Welsh[^key-3ee50260d1c2b55fc418e947f3ff270a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-d247c417dd0121ab891200ccf89ac035][^key-fb1a3dac4a276999b5c65f41c0af411d] The Commission must—

- (a) encourage‍ demand for, and participation in,‍ Welsh tertiary education provided through the medium of Welsh;
- (b) take all reasonable steps to ensure that there is sufficient‍ Welsh tertiary education provided through the medium of Welsh to meet‍ demand;
- (c) encourage the provision of tertiary education through the medium of Welsh by—
  - (i) registered providers in Wales, and
  - (ii) other persons providing tertiary education funded or otherwise secured by the Commission.

(2)[^key-63d12dcf285c9ea165e84759a8378734] The Welsh Ministers must designate a person to give relevant advice to the Commission.

(3)[^key-63d12dcf285c9ea165e84759a8378734] In this section, “**relevant advice**” means advice given for the purpose of assisting the Commission in the discharge of its duties under subsection (1).

(4)[^key-63d12dcf285c9ea165e84759a8378734] A person may be designated under subsection (2) only if the Welsh Ministers consider the person is suitable to give advice on the following—

- (a) the promotion, maintenance, development and planning of tertiary education provided in Wales through the medium of Welsh,
- (b) the promotion of the acquisition and improvement of Welsh language skills,
- (c) the maintenance, development, planning and provision of activities to support the acquisition and improvement of Welsh language skills, and
- (d) collaboration between tertiary education providers in Wales in relation to the matters mentioned in paragraphs (a) to (c).

(5)[^key-63d12dcf285c9ea165e84759a8378734] The duty in subsection (2) does not apply if—

- (a) the Welsh Ministers consider there is no person suitable to give advice on the matters mentioned in subsection (4), or
- (b) there is no person who consents to be designated.

(6)[^key-e621edded270456f7dc046a508ad6398] The Commission must have regard to any relevant advice given to it by a person designated under subsection (2).

(7)[^key-63d12dcf285c9ea165e84759a8378734] The Commission must publish a designation made by the Welsh Ministers under subsection (2).

(8)[^key-63d12dcf285c9ea165e84759a8378734] A designation under subsection (2) may be removed.
