# Tertiary Education and Research (Wales) Act 2022, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-08-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/asc/2022/1

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Encouraging participation in tertiary education[^c-c3e89487913887cac9c0dc54c8ca7027][^c-ea04f1fac8eeaa3af3ad7353a4e18b15][^c-049bda9eedf1ce9fb8cddcc77819ec0d]

*Extent: E+W*

The Commission must—

- (a) encourage individuals who are ordinarily resident in Wales, in particular those who have additional learning needs, to participate in tertiary education, and
- (b) encourage employers in Wales to participate in the provision of tertiary education.
