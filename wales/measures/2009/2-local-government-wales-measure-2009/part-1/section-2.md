# Local Government (Wales) Measure 2009, section 2

## 2. General duty in relation to improvement

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-bf317a720a7e05adf40235dc44f6583d] A Welsh improvement authority must make arrangements to secure continuous improvement in the exercise of its functions.

(2)[^c-510e48a908767a052c21a86b8ae5bdd2] In discharging its duty under subsection (1), an authority must have regard in particular to the need to improve the exercise of its functions in terms of—

- (a) strategic effectiveness;
- (b) service quality;
- (c) service availability;
- (d) fairness;
- (e) sustainability;
- (f) efficiency; and
- (g) innovation.

(3)[^c-510e48a908767a052c21a86b8ae5bdd2] For the meanings of paragraphs (a) to (g) of subsection (2), see section 4.
