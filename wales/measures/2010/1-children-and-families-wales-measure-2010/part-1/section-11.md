# Children and Families (Wales) Measure 2010, section 11

> Text as at 2025-11-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. Local authority duties in respect of play opportunities for children

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-4cbd679e4c06d67f456944478cabc3e5] A local authority must assess the sufficiency of play opportunities in its area for children in accordance with regulations.

(2)[^key-4cbd679e4c06d67f456944478cabc3e5] Regulations may include provision about—

- (a) the matters to be taken into account in assessing sufficiency;
- (b) the date by which a first assessment is to be carried out;
- (c) frequency of assessments;
- (d) review of assessments;
- (e) publication of assessments.

(3)[^key-134d8d90276634ea397ceeb6ef36b2c4] A local authority must secure sufficient play opportunities in its area for children, so far as reasonably practicable, having regard to its assessment under subsection (1).

(4)[^key-134d8d90276634ea397ceeb6ef36b2c4] A local authority must—

- (a) publish information about play opportunities in the authority's area for children, and
- (b) keep the information published up to date.

(5)[^key-4cbd679e4c06d67f456944478cabc3e5] In performing its duties under this section, a local authority must have regard (among other things)—

- (a) to the needs of children who are disabled persons (within the meaning of section 1 of the Disability Discrimination Act 1995 (c. 50);
- (b) to the needs of children of different ages.

(6)[^key-4cbd679e4c06d67f456944478cabc3e5] In this section—

- “**play**” includes any recreational activity;
- “**sufficient**”, in relation to play opportunities, means sufficient having regard to quantity and quality.
