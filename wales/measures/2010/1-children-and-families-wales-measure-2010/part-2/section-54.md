# Children and Families (Wales) Measure 2010, section 54

> Text as at 2025-11-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 54. Co-operation between authorities[^key-6597bb42b6fd8797d96200e34640cbd9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If it appears to the Welsh Ministers that any local authority could, by taking any specified action, help in the exercise of any of their functions under this Part, they may request the help of the authority, specifying the action in question.

(2) An authority whose help is requested must comply with the request if it is compatible with its own statutory and other duties and does not unduly prejudice the discharge of any of its functions.
