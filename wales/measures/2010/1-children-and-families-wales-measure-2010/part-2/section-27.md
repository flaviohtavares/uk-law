> Text as at 2025-11-19.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. Prescribed requirements for registration of providers of day care for children[^c-d6e3252bb77ea93f889bf88561759520]

*Extent: E+W*

The prescribed requirements for registration of providers of day care for children may include requirements relating to—

- (a) the applicant;
- (b) the premises on which the day care is to be provided;
- (c) the arrangements for day care on those premises;
- (d) any person who may be caring for children on those premises;
- (e) any other person who may be on those premises.
