# Children and Families (Wales) Measure 2010, section 52

> Text as at 2025-11-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52. Functions of local authorities[^key-43e00b8ffa5e0ad36606fc13f23bd039]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations may require a local authority to secure the provision—

- (a) of information or advice about child minding and day care;
- (b) of training about the provision of child minding and day care.
