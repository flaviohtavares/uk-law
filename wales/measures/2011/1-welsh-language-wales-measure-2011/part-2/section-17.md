## 17. Consultation[^key-d42c7380e7f7251bd8eedf71f788d420]

*Extent: E+W*

If, in connection with the exercise of a function, the Commissioner consults—

- (a) the Advisory Panel, or
- (b) any other person in accordance with this Measure,

the Commissioner must have regard to the consultation in exercising the function.
