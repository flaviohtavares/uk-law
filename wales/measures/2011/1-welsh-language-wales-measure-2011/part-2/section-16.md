# Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011, section 16

## 16. Welsh Ministers' power of direction

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-66402b0f1e5e8320b3cdc8e7077d6510] The Welsh Ministers may give directions to the Commissioner.

(2) But the Welsh Ministers may not direct the Commissioner in relation to the following matters—

- (a)[^key-36b282e364d529935cb814cb992f892c] giving a compliance notice to a person under Chapter 6 of Part 4 (including the content of a compliance notice to be given to a person);
- (b)[^key-36b282e364d529935cb814cb992f892c] Part 5 (enforcement of standards);
- (c)[^key-66402b0f1e5e8320b3cdc8e7077d6510] Part 6 (the freedom to use Welsh).

(3)[^key-66402b0f1e5e8320b3cdc8e7077d6510] The Commissioner must comply with directions given by the Welsh Ministers.
