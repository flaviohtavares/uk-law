# Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/mwa/2011/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Inquiries

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-531dffb4a21c3aeb035bee4eb021c7bc] The Commissioner may conduct an inquiry into any matter relating to any of the Commissioner's functions.

(2)[^c-d3b8e9a4906ddf3dfa123f30677ad724][^c-c59811fd30e427e0362c158200d0c07f] But that is subject to subsections (3) to (5).

(3) Subsection (1) does not authorise the Commissioner to conduct an inquiry in a case where he or she—

- (a)[^c-531dffb4a21c3aeb035bee4eb021c7bc] may or must carry out a standards investigation under Chapter 8 of Part 4, or
- (b)[^c-202864131e1d8a7eed0077090908a1c7] undertakes (and does not discontinue) an investigation under Part 5.

(4)[^c-202864131e1d8a7eed0077090908a1c7] Subsection (1) does not authorise the Commissioner to conduct an inquiry into the failure, by one or more particular persons, to comply with one or more relevant requirements.

(5)[^c-531dffb4a21c3aeb035bee4eb021c7bc] Subsection (1) does not authorise the Commissioner to conduct an inquiry into the interference, by one or more particular persons, with the freedom to communicate in Welsh (but see Part 6 for power to investigate certain interference with that freedom).

(6)[^c-d3b8e9a4906ddf3dfa123f30677ad724][^c-c59811fd30e427e0362c158200d0c07f] Subsection (4) or (5) does not prevent the Commissioner from taking the conduct of one or more particular persons into account when conducting an inquiry into—

- (a) failure to comply with relevant requirements, or
- (b) interference with the freedom to communicate in Welsh.

(7)[^c-531dffb4a21c3aeb035bee4eb021c7bc] The Commissioner may—

- (a) discontinue, or
- (b) suspend,

the conduct of an inquiry or any aspect of an inquiry.

(8)[^c-202864131e1d8a7eed0077090908a1c7] If, in the course of an inquiry, the Commissioner begins to suspect that a person may have failed to comply with one or more relevant requirements—

- (a) in continuing the inquiry the Commissioner must, so far as possible, avoid further consideration of whether or not the person has failed to comply with the requirements,
- (b) the Commissioner may commence an investigation into that question under Part 5, and
- (c) the Commissioner may use information or evidence acquired in the course of the inquiry for the purpose of the investigation.

(9)[^c-531dffb4a21c3aeb035bee4eb021c7bc] Schedule 2 makes supplemental provision about inquiries.

(10)[^c-202864131e1d8a7eed0077090908a1c7] In this section, a reference to failure to comply with one or more relevant requirements has the same meaning as in Part 5.
