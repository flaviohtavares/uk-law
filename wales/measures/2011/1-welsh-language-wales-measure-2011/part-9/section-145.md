## 145. Replacement of Welsh language schemes with standards

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The functions conferred on the Board by Part 2 of the 1993 Act (and transferred as mentioned in section 143) are abolished.

(2)[^c-d69e8a687c6fa47ef03c7bdd336429de] The following provisions of the 1993 Act are repealed—

- (a) Part 2;
- (b) section 34(1) and (3).
