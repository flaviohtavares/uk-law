# Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011, section 144

## 144. Abolition of Board's general functions and replacement of schemes with standards

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-c1cbb46bcc9c49e41da1cdc54aa75b9c][^key-59021aded912978d280b586ed5537edb] The functions conferred by section 3 of the 1993 Act (and transferred as mentioned in section 143) are abolished.

(2)[^key-d28a1776f76364c6bfa515c97bca42fa] Part 2 of the 1993 Act ceases to apply to a person if and when that person first becomes subject to the duty under section 25(1) of this Measure to comply with a standard.

(3) The following provisions of the 1993 Act are repealed—

- (a)[^key-0a15cab1b4237282864a319f9e337c14][^key-22091bcf17d72f37afd3d4acb63bc0d6][^key-59021aded912978d280b586ed5537edb] section 3;
- (b)[^key-f52c77052d44d563f769ec4ed6ebb61e] section 4(1).
