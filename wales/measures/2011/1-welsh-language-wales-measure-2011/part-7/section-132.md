## 132. President's annual report[^c-859242ad0eb5e2fd8f10fca9ee39df68]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) As soon as practicable after the end of each financial year, the President must—

- (a) produce a report on the Tribunal's exercise of its functions in that financial year, and
- (b) lay a copy of the report before the National Assembly for Wales.
- (c) The President must comply with any requirements of the National Assembly for Wales in relation to the form of the report and the laying of it.
