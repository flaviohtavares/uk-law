## 133. Training etc for members of Tribunal[^c-94900647cfa0e00ef714b11242fe8c1e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The President must maintain appropriate arrangements for the training, guidance and welfare of members of the Tribunal.

(2) It is for the President to decide what arrangements are appropriate for that purpose.
