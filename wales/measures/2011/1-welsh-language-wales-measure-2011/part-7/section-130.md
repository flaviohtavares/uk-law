## 130. Financial year[^key-15b5d514d982b53185f227c01ef75b8f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Tribunal's first financial year is the period that begins with the commencement day and ends with—

- (a) the following 31 March (if the commencement day is 1 April), or
- (b) the second following 31 March (if the commencement day is not 1 April).

(2) Subject to that, the Tribunal's financial year is the period of 12 months ending with 31 March.

(3) In this section “**commencement day**” means the day when section 120 comes into force.
