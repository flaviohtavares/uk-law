# Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011, section 124

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/mwa/2011/1

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 124. Practice directions[^c-7cf5bacc05239094e803b3f5db990ccf]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-c7a4e36346028f8865df23266caf712e] The President may give directions as to the practice and procedure of the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7a4e36346028f8865df23266caf712e]

(2) But the President may not give practice directions unless they have been approved by the Welsh Ministers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7a4e36346028f8865df23266caf712e]

(3) Subsection (2) does not apply to practice directions to the extent that they relate to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7a4e36346028f8865df23266caf712e]

- (a) the application or interpretation of the law, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7a4e36346028f8865df23266caf712e]
- (b) the making of decisions by members of the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7a4e36346028f8865df23266caf712e]

(4) The power under this section to give practice directions includes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7a4e36346028f8865df23266caf712e]

- (a) power to vary or revoke practice directions given in exercise of the power, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7a4e36346028f8865df23266caf712e]
- (b) power to make different provision for different purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c7a4e36346028f8865df23266caf712e]
