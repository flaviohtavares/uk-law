## 120. The Welsh Language Tribunal

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-82f7972e413c0be5dfe6e97f2de8b4ed] There is to be a Welsh Language Tribunal (referred to in this Measure as the “Tribunal”).

(2)[^c-895deaa1285fc4f0486a0ab715907348] The Tribunal is to consist of the following members—

- (a) the President of the Welsh Language Tribunal (referred to in this Measure as the “President”);
- (b) legally-qualified members; and
- (c) lay members.

(3)[^c-895deaa1285fc4f0486a0ab715907348] The Welsh Ministers must appoint the members of the Tribunal.

(4)[^c-655c4057377bbf2b9cdc76c8b4de5a38][^c-d441f5bf108213126734b2f78e368c98] Schedule 11 makes further provision about the Tribunal.
