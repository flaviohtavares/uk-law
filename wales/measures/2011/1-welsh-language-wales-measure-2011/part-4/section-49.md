## 49. Varying compliance notices[^c-65050df3d370a1f26dfbb8c42644e904]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Commissioner may vary any compliance notice.

(2) Sections 45 to 47 apply to the variation of a compliance notice as they apply to the giving of a compliance notice, but only insofar as the notice is varied.

(3) Section 48 applies to the variation of a compliance notice as it applies to the giving of a compliance notice.
