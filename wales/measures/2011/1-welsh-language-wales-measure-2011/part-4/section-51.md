## 51. When compliance notice in force[^c-2021d83b52b84572e0d4b39b466b4980]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A compliance notice given to a person (P) is in force from the day when the Commissioner gives the notice to P.

(2) A compliance notice remains in force unless — and until — it is revoked.

(3) This section applies for the purposes of this Measure.
