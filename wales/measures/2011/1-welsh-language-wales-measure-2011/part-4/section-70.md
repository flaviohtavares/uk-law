# Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/mwa/2011/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 70. Interpretation[^c-62dc761487bd4e58c7c5bc4f0d6386fd]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part—

- (a) references to a person being liable to be required to comply with standards are to be read in accordance with section 33;
- (b) references to a person's entry in the Schedule 6 table or the Schedule 8 table are to be read in accordance with section 34;
- (c) references to a standard being potentially applicable to a person are to be read in accordance with sections 36 and 37;
- (d) references to a standard being specifically applicable to a person are to be read in accordance with section 39.

(2) In this Part—

- “**Schedule 5 table**” means the table in Schedule 5;
- “**Schedule 6 table**” means the table in Schedule 6;
- “**Schedule 7 table**” means the table in Schedule 7;
- “**Schedule 8 table**” means the table in Schedule 8.
