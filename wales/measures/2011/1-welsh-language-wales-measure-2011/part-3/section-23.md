# Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/mwa/2011/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Advisory Panel

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-b4d70d45437e3483c634b170a4b56237][^key-d08ea15a7b53bc7b48532155b0cc974e] The Welsh Ministers must appoint persons to be members of a panel of advisers to the Commissioner.

(2)[^key-724526f4e9722e9df0dafbc622e480db] The panel is to be known as the Advisory Panel to the Welsh Language Commissioner (referred to in this Measure as the “Advisory Panel”).

(3)[^key-724526f4e9722e9df0dafbc622e480db] The Welsh Ministers must secure that, as far as it is practicable, there are at least 3, but not more than 5, members of the Advisory Panel at any time.

(4)[^key-cfbc8ec080c283f1714cf9659d07a248][^key-d08ea15a7b53bc7b48532155b0cc974e] Schedule 4 makes further provision about the members of the Advisory Panel.
