## 76. No failure to comply with a relevant requirement[^key-95b7a4613066024d63489a04a6a06eb5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if the Commissioner determines that D has not failed to comply with a relevant requirement.

(2) The Commissioner may—

- (a) take no further action, or
- (b) act under subsection (3).

(3) The Commissioner may do one or more of the following things—

- (a) give D or any other person recommendations;
- (b) give D or any other person advice.

(4) If the investigation that led to the determination follows a complaint under section 93, the relevant decision notice must inform the person who made the complaint of the right to appeal under section 99.

(5) This section is subject to section 85.

(6) In this section, “**relevant decision notice**” means the decision notice which the Commissioner is required by section 73 to give to the person who made the complaint under section 93.
