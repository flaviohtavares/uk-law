## 78. No imposed enforcement action[^c-3b3664310308ed601205ab3c746f6dc4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if the Commissioner—

- (a) determines that D has failed to comply with a relevant requirement, but
- (b) decides—
  - (i) to take no further action, or
  - (ii) to act under section 77(4).

(2) The relevant decision notice must give the Commissioner's reasons for deciding—

- (a) to take no further action, or
- (b) to act under section 77(4) and not under section 77(3).

(3) This section is subject to section 85.

(4) In this section “**relevant decision notice**” means the notice which the Commissioner is required by section 73 to give to D.
