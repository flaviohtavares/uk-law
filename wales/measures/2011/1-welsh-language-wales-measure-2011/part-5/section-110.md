# Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011, section 110

## 110. Interpretation[^c-d81c86fc29a7c8a862c08733e36e1f2a]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “**enforcement action**” (“***camau gorfodi***”), in relation to an investigation under section 71, means one or more of the following—
  - (a) requiring D to prepare an action plan for the purpose of preventing the continuation or repetition of D's failure;
  - (b) requiring D to take steps for the purpose of preventing the continuation or repetition of D's failure;
  - (c) publicising D's failure;
  - (d) requiring D to publicise the failure;
  - (e) imposing a civil penalty on D;
- “**interested person**” (“***person a chanddo fuddiant***”), in relation to an investigation under section 71, means—
  - (a) D, and
  - (b) if the investigation follows a complaint under section 93, the person who made the complaint.
