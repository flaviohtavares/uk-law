# Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011, section 81

## 81. Publicising the failure to comply[^c-6bd0ff631187a14647ddb85f01efac29]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Measure, references to the Commissioner publicising D's failure to comply with the relevant requirement are to the Commissioner doing either or both of the following—

- (a) publishing a statement that D has failed to comply with the relevant requirement;
- (b) publishing the investigation report produced in relation to the investigation of D.

(2) In this Measure, references to D being required to publicise the failure to comply with the relevant requirement are to D being required to publicise any or all of the following—

- (a) a statement that D has failed to comply with the relevant requirement;
- (b) the investigation report produced in relation to the investigation of D;
- (c) other information relating to D's failure to comply with the relevant requirement.
