# Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011, section 140

## 140. Absolute privilege

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of the law of defamation, the following are absolutely privileged—

- (a)[^key-b2b70067c5220068ab72256075edd363] the publication of a matter by the Commissioner in the exercise of any of his or her functions;
- (b)[^key-b2b70067c5220068ab72256075edd363] the publication of a matter by a member of the Advisory Panel in the exercise of any of his or her functions;
- (c)[^key-3ae2565416827114383157aae5fc97d2] the publication of a matter by a person in compliance with a requirement in a decision notice;
- (d)[^key-573027e4e7298a1fe46bc488ec556f73][^key-2644304b8216e6ae5cc30cbb3bdd5408] the publication, in a communication between—
  - (i) the Commissioner, and
  - (ii) a protected person,

  of a matter in connection with an inquiry or investigation;
- (e)[^key-573027e4e7298a1fe46bc488ec556f73][^key-2644304b8216e6ae5cc30cbb3bdd5408] the publication, in a communication between—
  - (i) the complainant or a person acting on behalf of the complainant, and
  - (ii) a representative,

  of a matter in connection with an investigation under Part 5 or Part 6.

(2)[^key-b2b70067c5220068ab72256075edd363] In this section a reference to the Commissioner includes the following persons—

- (a) the members of the Commissioner's staff;
- (b) any person acting on the Commissioner's behalf or assisting in the exercise of the Commissioner's functions.
