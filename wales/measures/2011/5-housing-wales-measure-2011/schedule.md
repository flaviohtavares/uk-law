> Text as at 2019-01-26.

## SCHEDULE — MINOR AND CONSEQUENTIAL AMENDMENTS

*Extent: E+W*

*(introduced by section 88)*

### Housing Act 1996 (c. 52)

**1.**[^key-d396b297fdfaa4fa0eed28105f6e0683] Amend the Housing Act 1996 as follows.

**2.**[^key-a26d98b937311571e1d77cedbcc7f572] In the title of Part 1, for “in Wales” substitute “ regulated by the Welsh Ministers ”.

**3.**[^key-6bb6ba67cee56a5437ffc4a607bc9781] In section 7 of the Housing Act 1996 (regulation of registered social landlords), after “dissolution of a registered social landlord.” insert— “ Part 3A relates to inspection. ”

**4.**[^key-ab3302c4f52a89e4ff03aa0ed1dd0542] In the heading to section 34, after “performance” insert “ – housing in England ”.

**5.**[^key-62a84151d925a33492cb3fa79ef7f61e] In section 34 (standards of performance – housing in England), in paragraph (a), after “provision of housing” insert “ in England ”.

**6.**[^key-ce69218a31b6c26c3f24cab95b10f46b] In the heading to section 36, after “Ministers” insert “ – housing in England ”.

**7.**[^key-a0e67d9f96c5b844315ee2ca94f858bd] In section 36 (issue of guidance by the Welsh Ministers – housing in England)—

- (a) in subsection (1), after “housing accommodation” insert “ in England ”;
- (b) omit subsection (2A).

**8.**[^key-3fa549e55bc76f8c0dd65c97b0b78fdd] In section 37 (powers of entry), in subsection (1), after “in accordance with” insert “ standards set under section 33A or ”.

**9.**[^key-5aca88573f7f31cbfcfd907d809f5b4d] In section 39 (insolvency, etc of registered social landlord: scheme of provisions), after sub-paragraph (1)(b) insert—

> - (ba) for the appointment of an interim manager during a moratorium (section 43A),

.

**10.**[^key-47171fe769554e904fbde209ccd62ad7] In section 46 (appointment of manager to implement agreed proposals), after subsection (4) insert—

> (4A) The Welsh Ministers may amend or revoke any directions given by them.

**11.**[^key-355924cf145e05012b8046df08799b72] In section 52 (general provisions as to orders), in subsection (1), after “39,” insert “ 50J, ”.

**12.**[^key-3db12697dfed273b99a1251471801754] In section 64 of the Housing Act 1996 (index of defined expressions: Part 1), at the appropriate places in the Table insert—

> |  |  |

> | local housing authority | section 230 |

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | misconduct | section 63 |

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | received public assistance | section 58(1A) |

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | representations | section 63 |

.

**13.**[^key-f75ec2c5bce734390b9450b156409707] In paragraph 5 of Schedule 1 (restriction on power of removal in case of registered charity)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (1)—
  - (i) after “has” insert “ received public assistance. ”;
  - (ii) omit the words from “, at any time” to the end of the sub-paragraph;
- (b) omit sub-paragraph (2).

**14.**[^key-ffa13699ee753d3a4eb5d07d3d0768b8] In paragraph 6 of Schedule 1 (registered charity: power to appoint a new officer), in sub-paragraph (2)(a), for the words from “financial assistance” to “paragraph 5” substitute “ public assistance ”.

**15.**[^key-d75378d119a05c5b680f9cc3b69e54ac] In paragraph 12 of Schedule 1 (amalgamation and dissolution etc of industrial and provident society), in sub-paragraph (6), for “Secretary of State” substitute “ Welsh Ministers ”.

**16.**[^key-7d8b5fce876e9d863e5f259add87b000] In paragraph 13 of Schedule 1 (arrangement, reconstruction, etc of company), in sub-paragraph (7), for “Secretary of State” substitute “ Welsh Ministers ”.

**17.**[^key-60c0ba24e7923adc271f8c3c50c1543a] In paragraph 18 of Schedule 1 (accounting and audit or reporting requirements for charities), in sub-paragraph (8), for “Corporation” substitute “ Welsh Ministers ”.

**18.**[^key-ea835c6e509fc5ce03bba125ce0ba8f7] In paragraph 20 of Schedule 1 (inquiry), in sub-paragraph (1) omit the words from “For this purpose” to the end of the paragraph.

**19.**[^key-19ec3ef8abe72f1a1f2b746288cc480f] In paragraph 25 of Schedule 1 (disqualification as officer of registered social landlord), in sub-paragraph (4), for “Corporation” substitute “ Welsh Ministers ”.

**20.**[^key-9a14465cfa011c9755305bedec1a016f] In paragraph 28 of Schedule 1 (availability of powers in relation to registered charities)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (1)—
  - (i) after “has” insert “ received public assistance. ”;
  - (ii) omit the words from “, at any time” to the end of the sub-paragraph;
- (b) omit sub-paragraph (2).
