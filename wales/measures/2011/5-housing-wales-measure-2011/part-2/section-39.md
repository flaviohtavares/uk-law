# Housing (Wales) Measure 2011, section 39

> Text as at 2019-01-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 39. Guidance about complaints about performance[^key-6895023617c52a620cb8f9fdeffc0489]

*Extent: E+W*

After section 35 of the Housing Act 1996 insert—

> **Complaints about performance**
>
> **35A. Guidance about complaints about performance**
>
> (1) The Welsh Ministers may publish guidance about complaints to the Welsh Ministers about the performance of registered social landlords.
>
> (2) The guidance may specify (among other things)—
>
> - (a) the procedure to be followed in making a complaint;
> - (b) the criteria used by the Welsh Ministers in deciding whether to investigate a complaint;
> - (c) periods within which the Welsh Ministers aim to inform complainants of the result of complaints.
>
> (3) The Welsh Ministers may revise or withdraw the guidance.
>
> (4) This section does not apply in relation to complaints about a registered social landlord's provision of housing in England.
