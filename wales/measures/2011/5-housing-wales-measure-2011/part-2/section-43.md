# Housing (Wales) Measure 2011, section 43

> Text as at 2019-01-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43. Inspection: overview and application[^key-26fe549f3db50617a1720465c346c974]

*Extent: E+W*

After Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the Housing Act 1996 insert—

> **PART 3A — INSPECTION**
>
> **19B. Overview and application**
>
> (1) This Part provides for the inspection of a registered social landlord's affairs.
>
> (2) But this Part does not apply in relation to affairs relating only to the provision of housing in England.
