# Housing (Wales) Measure 2011, section 63

> Text as at 2019-01-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 63. Appeal[^key-fec30054bf45cc7d893950ddcac8ac87]

*Extent: E+W*

After section 50M of the Housing Act 1996 insert—

> **50N. Appeal**
>
> A registered social landlord who is given a penalty notice may appeal to the High Court against—
>
> - (a) the imposition of the penalty,
> - (b) its amount, or
> - (c) both.
