# Housing (Wales) Measure 2011, section 50

> Text as at 2019-01-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 50. Welsh Ministers' enforcement powers: general[^c-3bc225d64dd2b85e0f8695c492caff25]

*Extent: E+W*

After section 50 of the Housing Act 1996 insert—

> **CHAPTER 4A — ENFORCEMENT POWERS**
>
> **General**
>
> **50A. Application of Chapter 4A**
>
> This Chapter does not apply in relation to a registered social landlord's provision of housing in England.
