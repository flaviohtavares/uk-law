# Rights of Children and Young Persons (Wales) Measure 2011, section 9

> Text as at 2022-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Other interpretive provisions

*Extent: E+W*

In this Measure—

- “the Assembly” (*y Cynulliad*) means the National Assembly for Wales;
- “child” (*plentyn*) means a person who has not attained the age of 18;
- “enactment” (*deddfiad*) means—
  - (i) Act of Parliament,
  - (ii) Measure or Act of the Assembly,
  - (iii) subordinate legislation within the meaning of section 21(1) of the Interpretation Act 1978 (c. 30), or
  - (iv) subordinate legislation made under any Measure or Act of the Assembly;
- “young person” (*pobl ifanc*) means a person who has attained the age of 18 but not the age of 25.
