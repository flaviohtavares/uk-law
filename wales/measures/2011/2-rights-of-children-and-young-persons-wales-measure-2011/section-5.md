# Rights of Children and Young Persons (Wales) Measure 2011, section 5

> Text as at 2022-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Duty to promote knowledge of the Convention

*Extent: E+W*

The Welsh Ministers must take such steps as are appropriate to promote knowledge and understanding amongst the public (including children) of the Convention and the Protocols.
