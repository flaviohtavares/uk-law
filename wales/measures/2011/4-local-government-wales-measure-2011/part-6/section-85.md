# Local Government (Wales) Measure 2011, section 85

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/mwa/2011/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 85. Guidance[^c-b3a74d494b9e8585de5d5fd62db7e3b5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may give guidance to local authorities—

- (a) about the functions of governance and[^c-372f71200e84de7f1408f6c18eb30684] audit committees and the exercise of those functions, or
- (b) about the membership of such[^c-39f4a7a171aec330be567764bf97ed06] committees.

(2) A local authority and its governance and[^c-f264fcc10d802367f54711b1f75378e3] audit committee must have regard to guidance given by the Welsh Ministers under subsection (1).
