## 86. Termination of membership on ceasing to be member of authority[^key-510f35522c261c9a147c374c99b661e7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies to a person (P) who is—

- (a) appointed to be a member of a governance and[^key-df919b978833818a26684b865f8bb31d] audit committee of a local authority, and
- (b) is a member of the authority at the time of that appointment.

(2) If P ceases to be a member of the authority, P also ceases to be a member of the governance and[^key-0de8c83bf64b35499eeb104774ec7198] audit committee.

(3) But subsection (2) does not apply if P—

- (a) ceases to be a member of the authority by reason of retirement, and
- (b) is re-elected a member of the authority not later than the day of retirement.

(4) Subsection (3) is subject to the standing orders of the authority or the governance and[^key-aabf3831655f9d3bd6ab4ee406f45b23] audit committee.
