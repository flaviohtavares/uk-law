# Local Government (Wales) Measure 2011, section 81

## 81. Local authorities to appoint governance and audit committees[^key-6a1d09ed421115e237367ea9a942dc55][^key-ac612798c9847c6b1264cd08e8e22a2b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority must appoint a committee (a “governance and audit committee”)[^key-e5160e3f002ecfd113e66c882f894673] to—

- (a) review and scrutinise the authority's financial affairs,
- (b) make reports and recommendations in relation to the authority's financial affairs,
- (c) review and assess the risk management, internal control , performance assessment[^key-3dc221cd89658321bf9c59cfbcbef4e6] and corporate governance arrangements of the authority,
- (d) make reports and recommendations to the authority on the adequacy and effectiveness of those arrangements,
- (da)[^key-ef17bad80b7dc7907a954883a8c4f08e] review and assess the authority's ability to handle complaints effectively,
- (db) make reports and recommendations in relation to the authority's ability to handle complaints effectively,
- (e) oversee the authority's internal and external audit arrangements, and
- (f) review the financial statements prepared by the authority.

(1A)[^key-97257059821fd909b9a6139fa76f3f98] See Chapter 1 of Part 6 of the Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021 (performance and governance of principal councils) for further functions of governance and audit committees.

(2) A local authority may confer on its governance and[^key-69bbe8cb8abfb552f4d8548eba50ca69] audit committee such other functions as the authority considers suitable to be exercised by such a committee.

(3) It is for a governance and[^key-3a7d94713de96fac8c66fa847bed2769] audit committee to determine how to exercise its functions.
