# Local Government (Wales) Measure 2011, section 82

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 82. Membership[^key-22345ffb186ac9329ed7b64fb62d9456]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority is to appoint the members of its governance and[^key-52174b88c07967b465365278e6ec8995] audit committee.

(2) A local authority must secure that—

- (a) [^key-c010f0749dd19a8557d49b29ea66ff4b]... two-thirds of the members of that committee[^key-498c68d2ae15bc9b0176336c15b56bb5] are members of the authority;
- (b)[^key-fac9fda7bdd1dce3038a51af2cd45b42] one-third of the members of that committee are lay persons;
- (c) no more than one of the members of that committee[^key-5bc63f66dd8db7de2759ab99fd344e2f] is a member of the authority's executive (but see subsection (8))[^key-a4a816b27f5f30e4916380c574dcda4e] or an assistant to its executive[^key-a66d124d3406adf35c114a01cacc75ab];
- (d) the senior member of its executive is not a member of that committee[^key-10fc576ac775731e43cffa783c1415de].

(3) Subsection (2)(c) does not require the membership of a local authority's governance and[^key-cbd1d569911e18bb9a2c295f59537a93] audit committee to include a member of the authority's executive or an assistant to its executive[^key-8cf1dd4db36579abfdb342489664818f].

(4) The appointment of a person as a member of a governance and[^key-8feb6574fd6529f392ca4babda901f66] audit committee has no effect if the membership of the committee breaches subsection (2) immediately after the appointment (whether or not by virtue of the appointment).

(5) In a case where one or more persons are to become, or to cease to be, members of a governance and[^key-8e15f80d9996281251b50fadf252cf7a] audit committee at a particular time, all those changes of membership are to be taken into account in determining whether the membership of the committee breaches subsection (2).

(5A)[^key-8289dfe3e93bb10988365ebfc87be076] A governance and audit committee is to appoint—

- (a) a member of the committee as its chair (“the committee chair”), and
- (b) a member of the committee as the deputy to the committee chair (“the deputy chair”).

(5B) The member appointed as the committee chair must be a lay person.

(5C) The member appointed as the deputy chair must not be a member of the local authority's executive or an assistant to its executive.

(6)[^key-c0fb920bdfd4cdec7983bfb7fade6a0a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^key-4a0072560f47c60a5c0dd0ef7c9f8ff7] A governance and[^key-453a0d9e2cfe04e77c6fceb376c5a21a] audit committee is to be treated as a body to which section 15 of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 (duty to allocate seats to political groups) applies.

(8)[^key-88393a1795a0081063efea676d8c9751] If two or more members of a local authority are appointed to the executive to share office, more than one of those members may be appointed to its governance and audit committee (and if they are so appointed, together they count as one executive member for the purposes of subsection (2)(c)).
