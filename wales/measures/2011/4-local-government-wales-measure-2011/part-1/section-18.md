## 18. Reports and recommendations by head of democratic services[^c-460206474f88ec7e3bbfd1c83f104209]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The head of democratic services for a local authority must, as soon as practicable after preparing a report or making a recommendation under section 9(1)(h), send to each member of the authority's democratic services committee a copy of the report or recommendation.

(2) A democratic services committee must consider any report or recommendation sent to the members of the committee under this section at a meeting held not more than three months after copies of the report are first sent to members of the committee.
