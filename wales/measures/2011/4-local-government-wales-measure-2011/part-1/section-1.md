# Local Government (Wales) Measure 2011, section 1

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Duty to conduct a survey[^key-597d2c5f4520b767cbf76e29cfecbd44]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority must, in accordance with a direction[^key-8dd5bfe16d3a34d4a56f0ea1bdd79306] under this section, conduct a survey of—

- (a) councillors in its area, and
- (b) unsuccessful candidates for election to the office of councillor in its area.

(2) A local authority must conduct a survey , or arrange for the conduct of a survey, in relation to[^key-1264922f0514aacb93ba445535f2570a] each ordinary election to—

- (a) the council of the county or county borough, and
- (b) a community council in the local authority's area.

(3)[^key-9984b2c9ce9d3a5002dfa1001ca42321] The Welsh Ministers may give a direction to local authorities that—

- (a) specifies the questions that must be asked in a survey;
- (b) specifies requirements about—
  - (i) the form of the survey;
  - (ii) how the survey is to be conducted;
  - (iii) the collation of information from the survey.

(3A)[^key-6af604e4b859c6508260dea138a5eb10] A survey in the case of an ordinary election may be conducted—

- (a) entirely after the ordinary election, or
- (b) by asking the candidates for election to the office of councillor to respond to the survey[^key-ef4239d2ada20b6aa2ae68f7b66ade50] before the ordinary election and collating the information provided afterwards.

(3B)[^key-7a5bfb9a5be090cc1c46b75e65a1c6b0] A local authority may include questions in a survey, or arrange for the inclusion of questions in a survey, in addition to the questions required by a direction of the Welsh Ministers under this section.

(4) The questions that may be specified in a direction[^key-16b5b433a1f1d562c6968faaf9be801d] under subsection (3) include (but are not limited to) questions about the individual to whom they are addressed which relate to—

- (a) gender;
- (b) sexual orientation;
- (c) language;
- (d) race;
- (e) age;
- (f) disability;
- (g) religion or belief;
- (h) health;
- (i) education and qualifications;
- (j) employment;
- (k) work as a councillor.

(5) Nothing in this section places a duty on any individual[^key-592dde6eaf378b4763f5a9863aab6216] to provide any information.

(6)[^key-a095d3906dd6175be716a6aa09446325] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) section—

- “**belief**” (“***cred***”) means any religious or philosophical belief and a reference to belief includes a reference to a lack of belief;
- “**councillor**” (“***cynghorydd***”) includes community councillor;
- “**disability**” (“***anabledd***”) means a physical or mental impairment that has a substantial and long-term adverse effect on a person's ability to carry out normal day to day activities;
- “**race**” (“***hil***”) means colour, nationality or ethnic or national origins;
- “**religion**” (“***crefydd***”) means any religion and a reference to religion includes a reference to a lack of religion;
- “**sexual orientation**” (“***cyfeiriadedd rhywiol***”) means a person's sexual orientation towards—
  - (a) persons of the same sex,
  - (b) persons of the opposite sex, or
  - (c) persons of either sex.
