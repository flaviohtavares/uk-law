## 17. Termination of membership on ceasing to be member of authority[^key-f5f074b200bb52163cd7112f186ed689]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies to a person (P) who is—

- (a) appointed to be a member of a democratic services committee of a local authority, or of a sub-committee of such a committee, and
- (b) is a member of the authority at the time of that appointment.

(2) If P ceases to be a member of the authority, P also ceases to be a member of the democratic services committee or sub-committee.

(3) But subsection (2) does not apply if P—

- (a) ceases to be a member of the authority by reason of retirement, and
- (b) is re-elected a member of the authority not later than the day of retirement.

(4) Subsection (3) is subject to the standing orders of the authority or the democratic services committee or sub-committee.
