# Local Government (Wales) Measure 2011, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/mwa/2011/4

## 30. Guidance[^key-16b2b34982c58f42d41e2abaca4a4fd8]

*Extent: E+W*

When exercising its functions, a local authority must have regard to guidance given by the Welsh Ministers in relation to the rights of members of the authority under this Part.
