# Local Government (Wales) Measure 2011, section 117

## 117. Guidance about giving public notice of co-option

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-54dbf6b36c4a6531cec8939a98ec526e] In exercising functions under subsections (2) to (5) of section 116, the members of a community council and a community council must have regard to guidance given by the Welsh Ministers.

(2) The reference to functions in subsection (1) includes a reference to functions under section 232 of the Local Government Act 1972 in relation to a notice required to be given under section 116(2).
