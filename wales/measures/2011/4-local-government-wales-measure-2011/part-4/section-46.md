## 46. Changes of executive arrangements requiring approval in a referendum

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of this Chapter, a change of executive arrangements requires approval in a referendum if—

- (a) the existing form of executive, or
- (b) the proposed form of executive,

is a mayor and cabinet executive.
