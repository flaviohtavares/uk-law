# Local Government (Wales) Measure 2011, section 52

## 52. Powers under which executive arrangements may be varied

*Extent: E+W*

A local authority which is operating executive arrangements may not vary or replace those arrangements except as provided for in—

- (a) Chapter 1 or 2 of this Part, or
- (b) regulations under section 34, 35 or 36 of the Local Government Act 2000.
