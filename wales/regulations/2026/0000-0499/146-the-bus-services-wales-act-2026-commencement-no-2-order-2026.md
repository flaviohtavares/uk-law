# The Bus Services (Wales) Act 2026 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 146 (C. 15)

PUBLIC PASSENGER TRANSPORT, WALES

The Bus Services (Wales) Act 2026 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2026

Made

26 November 2026

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred on them by section 48(3) of the Bus Services (Wales) Act 2026[^f00001].

## 1. Title

The title of this Order is the Bus Services (Wales) Act 2026 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2026.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 19 January 2027

The following provisions of the Bus Services (Wales) Act 2026 come into force on 19 January 2027—

- (a) section 27(1), (2), (3)(a), (6) and (7) (provision of information to the Welsh Ministers by current and former operators for the purposes of functions under Part 2 or 3);
- (b) section 28(1), (2) and (3)(a) (provision of information about infrastructure and certain other matters to the Welsh Ministers by local authorities and community councils for the purposes of functions under Part 2);
- (c) section 31 (provision of information: exclusion on grounds of legal privilege);
- (d) section 32(1), (2)(a), (3), (4), (5), (6), (7), (9)(a)(ii), (9)(b)(ii) and (10) (enforcement of information requirements);
- (e) section 33 (enforcement of information requirements: appeals);
- (f) section 37 (application of TUPE).

Mark Hooper

Deputy Minister for Transport under authority of the Cabinet Minister for Enterprise, Connectivity and Energy, one of the Welsh Ministers

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the second commencement order made under the Bus Services (Wales) Act 2026 (“**the Act**”).

This Order brings the following sections of the Act into force on 19 January 2027—

- (a) section 27(1), (2), (3)(a), (6) and (7);
- (b) section 28(1), (2) and (3)(a);
- (c) section 31;
- (d) section 32(1), (2)(a), (3) to (7), (9)(a)(ii), (9)(b)(ii) and (10);
- (e) section 33;
- (f) section 37.

Sections 27(1), (2), (3)(a), (6) and (7) and 28(1), (2) and (3)(a) make provision in relation to the Welsh Ministers giving notice to obtain information from operators and former operators of certain bus services and from local authorities and community councils. The remaining provisions of sections 27 and 28 are already in force in accordance with section 48(1)(f) and (g) of the Act. Therefore, this Order brings sections 27 and 28 fully into force on 19 January 2027.

Section 31 excludes from the information sharing requirements in sections 27, 28 and 30 of the Act any information that would be subject to legal privilege in legal proceedings.

Section 32(1), (2)(a), (3) to (7), (9)(a)(ii), (9)(b)(ii) and (10) makes provision in relation to enforcement action for failures to provide information in accordance with section 27 or 30. The remaining provisions of section 32 are already in force in accordance with section 48(1)(j) of the Act. Therefore, this Order brings section 32 fully into force on 19 January 2027.

Section 33 makes provision for appeals against enforcement action taken under section 32.

Section 37 places a duty on the Welsh Ministers to make regulations specifying circumstances in which certain specific arrangements made under the Act will be treated as giving rise to a transfer that is within the Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) Regulations 2006, where this would not otherwise have been the case. The regulations must also apply certain provisions of, and made under, the Pensions Act 2004 in relation to a transfer of this type.
