# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 43

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 43. Marloes and St Brides — change of community name

The existing community of Marloes and St Brides retains the name of Marloes and St Brides in the English language and is to be known as Marloes a Sain Ffraid in the Welsh language.
