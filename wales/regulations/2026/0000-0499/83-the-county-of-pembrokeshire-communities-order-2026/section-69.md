# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 69

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Cosheston, Hundleton, and Pembroke — changes in community boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the community of Cosheston shown shaded on Map 31—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Pembroke;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of St Mary North;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Pembroke: St Mary North.
