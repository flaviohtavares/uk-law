# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 90

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 90. Abolition of the community wards in the community of Lampeter Velfrey

The following existing community wards of the community of Lampeter Velfrey are abolished—

- (a) Lampeter Velfrey;
- (b) Ludchurch.
