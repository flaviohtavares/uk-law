# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. Spittal, and Rudbaxton — change in community boundaries and consequential change to electoral arrangements

The part of the community of Spittal shown shaded on Map 19—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Rudbaxton;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Rudbaxton and Spittal.
