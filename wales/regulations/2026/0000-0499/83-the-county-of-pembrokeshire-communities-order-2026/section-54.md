# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 54

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 54. Penally, and Manorbier — change in community boundaries and consequential change to electoral arrangements

The part of the community of Penally shown shaded on Map 23—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Manorbier;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Manorbier and Penally.
