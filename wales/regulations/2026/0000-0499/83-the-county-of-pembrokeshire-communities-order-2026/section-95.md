# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 95

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 95. Abolition of community wards in the community of Kilgetty/Begelly

The following existing community wards of the community of Kilgetty/Begelly are abolished—

- (a) Begelly;
- (b) Kilgetty.
