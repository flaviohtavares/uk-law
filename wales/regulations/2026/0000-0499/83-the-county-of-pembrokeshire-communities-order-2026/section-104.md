# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 104

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 104. Abolition of the community wards in the community of Nevern

The following existing community wards of the community of Nevern are abolished—

- (a) Moylegrove;
- (b) Nevern.
