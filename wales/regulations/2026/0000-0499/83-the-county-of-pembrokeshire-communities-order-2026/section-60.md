# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 60

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 60. Letterston, Puncheston, Scleddau, Fishguard, and Cwm Gwaun — changes in community boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the community of Letterston shown shaded on Map 25—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Puncheston;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Bro Gwaun.
