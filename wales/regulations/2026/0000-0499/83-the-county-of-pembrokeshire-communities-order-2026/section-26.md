# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. The Havens — change of community name

The existing community of The Havens retains the name of The Havens in the English language and is to be known as Yr Aberoedd in the Welsh language.
