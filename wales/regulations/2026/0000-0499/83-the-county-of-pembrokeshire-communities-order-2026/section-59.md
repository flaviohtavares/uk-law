# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Abolition of the community wards in the community of Scleddau

The following existing community wards of the community of Scleddau are abolished—

- (a) Scleddau North;
- (b) Scleddau South;
- (c) Trecwn.
