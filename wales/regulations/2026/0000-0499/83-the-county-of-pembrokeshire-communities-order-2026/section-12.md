# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Abolition of the community wards in the community of Tiers Cross

The following existing community wards of the community of Tiers Cross are abolished—

- (a) Tiers Cross;
- (b) Thornton.
