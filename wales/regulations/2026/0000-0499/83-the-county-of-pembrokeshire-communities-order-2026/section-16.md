# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Johnston, Tiers Cross, Walwyn’s Castle, Camros, and Haverfordwest — changes in community and community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the community of Johnston shown shaded on Map 9 and numbered 1—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Tiers Cross;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of St Ishmael’s.
