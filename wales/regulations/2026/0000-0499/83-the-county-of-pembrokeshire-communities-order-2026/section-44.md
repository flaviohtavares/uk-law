# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 44

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 44. Marloes and St Brides, and Dale — change in community boundaries and consequential change to electoral arrangements

The part of the community of Marloes and St Brides shown shaded on Map 20—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Dale;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of St Ishmael’s.
