# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 82

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 82. St Davids, and Solva — change in community boundaries and consequential change to electoral arrangements

The part of the community of St Davids shown shaded on Map 36—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Solva;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Solva.
