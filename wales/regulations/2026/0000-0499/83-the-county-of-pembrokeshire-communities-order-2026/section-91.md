# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 91

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 91. Lampeter Velfrey, and Templeton — change in community boundaries and consequential change to electoral arrangements

The part of the community of Lampeter Velfrey shown shaded on Map 42—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Templeton;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Narberth: Rural.
