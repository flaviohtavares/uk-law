# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Llawhaden, and Clunderwen — changes in community boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The parts of the community of Llawhaden shown shaded on Map 1 and Map 2—

- (a) are transferred to the community of Clunderwen;
- (b) form part of the electoral ward of Maenclochog.
