# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 111

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 111. Crymych, Boncath, Cilgerran, Maenordeifi, Clydau, and Eglwyswrw — changes in community boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the community of Crymych shown shaded on Map 53—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Boncath;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Boncath and Clydau.
