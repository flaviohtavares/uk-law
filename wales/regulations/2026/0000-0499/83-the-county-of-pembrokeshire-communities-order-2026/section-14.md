# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Walwyns Castle — change of community name

The existing community of Walwyns Castle is to be known as Walwyn’s Castle in the English language and retains the name of Castell Gwalchmai in the Welsh language.
