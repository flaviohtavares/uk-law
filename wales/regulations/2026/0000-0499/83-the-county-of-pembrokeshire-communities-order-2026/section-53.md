# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 53

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 53. Abolition of the community wards in the community of Manorbier

The following existing community wards of the community of Manorbier are abolished—

- (a) Jameston and Lydstep;
- (b) Manorbier.
