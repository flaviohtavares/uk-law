# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 87

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 87. Narberth — community ward electoral arrangements

In the community of Narberth, the number of councillors to be elected for—

- (a) the Narberth Rural ward is 3;
- (b) the Narberth Urban ward is 10.
