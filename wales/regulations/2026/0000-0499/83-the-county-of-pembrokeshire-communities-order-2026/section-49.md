# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. Abolition of the community wards in the community of Uzmaston, Boulston and Slebech

The following existing community wards of the community of Uzmaston, Boulston and Slebech are abolished—

- (a) Uzmaston and Boulston;
- (b) Slebech.
