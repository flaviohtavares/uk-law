# The County of Pembrokeshire (Communities) Order 2026, section 96

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 96. Amroth, Kilgetty/Begelly, Saundersfoot, St Mary Out Liberty, and Tenby — changes in community and community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The parts of the community of Amroth shown shaded on Map 43 and numbered 1 and 2—

- (a) are transferred to the community of Kilgetty/Begelly;
- (b) form part of the electoral ward of Kilgetty and Begelly.
