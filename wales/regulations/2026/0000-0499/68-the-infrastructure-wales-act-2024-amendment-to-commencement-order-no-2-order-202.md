# The Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024 (Amendment to Commencement Order No. 2) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Welsh Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of defects in [S.I. 2025/1306 (W. 212)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2025/1306) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2026 No. 68 (C. 6)

INFRASTRUCTURE PLANNING, WALES

The Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024 (Amendment to Commencement Order No. 2) Order 2026

Made

4 March 2026

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 147(2) of the Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024[^f00001].

## 1. Title and coming into force

(1) The title of this Order is the Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024 (Amendment to Commencement Order No. 2) Order 2026.

(2) This Order comes into force on the 11 March 2026.

## 2. Amendments to the Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2025

In article 2 of the Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2025[^f00002]—

- (a) in paragraph (b), after “90” insert “to the extent not already in force”;
- (b) in paragraph (c), after “91” insert “to the extent not already in force”.

Rebecca Evans

Cabinet Secretary for Economy, Energy and Planning, one of the Welsh Ministers

4 March 2026

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes amendments to the Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2025 (“**the 2025 Order**”) made by the Welsh Ministers under the Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024.

Article 3 of this Order amends article 2(b) and (c) of the 2025 Order, clarifying that those sections come into force on the day specified to the extent that they are not already in force.
