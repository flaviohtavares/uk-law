# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 63

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 63. Beulah — combination of community wards

The community wards of Bettws Evan and Brongwyn of the community of Beulah, as changed by this Order, are combined to form a new community ward shown shaded on Map 31 which—

- (a) is to be known as Betws Ifan and Brongwyn in the English language and Betws Ifan a Brongwyn in the Welsh language;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Beulah and Llangoedmor.
