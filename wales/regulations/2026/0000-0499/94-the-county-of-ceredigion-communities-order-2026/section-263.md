# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 263

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 263. Tirymynach — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Tirymynach comprises the new Tirymynach community ward of the new community of Tirymynach.

**264.** In the electoral ward of Tirymynach, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.

**265.** The electoral ward of Borth comprises the new Geneu’r Glyn community ward of the new community of Tirymynach and the community of Borth.

**266.** In the electoral ward of Borth, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
