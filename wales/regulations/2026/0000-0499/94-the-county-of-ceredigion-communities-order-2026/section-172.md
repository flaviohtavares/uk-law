# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 172

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 172. Creation of the new community ward of Llangwyryfon

A new community ward is created in the new community of Lledrod which is to be known as Llangwyryfon in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 2 on Map 66;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanrhystyd.
