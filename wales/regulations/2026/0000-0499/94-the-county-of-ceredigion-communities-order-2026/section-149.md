# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 149

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 149. Llangeitho — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Llangeitho comprises the area of the new community of Llangeitho.

**150.** In the electoral ward of Llangeitho, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
