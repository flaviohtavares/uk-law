# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 235

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 235. Trawsgoed — changes in community boundaries

The part of the existing Gwnnws Isaf community ward of the existing community of Ystrad Meurig shown shaded on Map 75—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Trawsgoed;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Llanafan;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Ystwyth.
