# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 249

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 249. Melindwr — abolition of community wards

The following existing community wards of the community of Melindwr are abolished—

- (a) Goginan;
- (b) Llanbadarn y Creuddyn Uchaf;
- (c) Penllwyn.
