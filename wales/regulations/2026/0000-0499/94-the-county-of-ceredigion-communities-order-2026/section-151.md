# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 151

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 151. Llangybi — changes in community boundaries

The part of the existing Llanfihangel Ystrad community ward of the existing community of Llanfihangel Ystrad shown shaded on Map 59—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Llangybi;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Silian;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llangybi.

**152.** The part of the existing Llanfihangel Ystrad community ward of the existing community of Llanfihangel Ystrad shown shaded on Map 60—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Llangybi;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Llangybi;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llangybi.

**153.** The part of the existing Llangybi community ward of the existing community of Llangybi shown shaded on Map 61—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Llanfihangel Ystrad;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Llanfihangel Ystrad;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanfihangel Ystrad.
