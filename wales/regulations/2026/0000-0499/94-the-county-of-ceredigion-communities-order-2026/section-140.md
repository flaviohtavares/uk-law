# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 140

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 140. Llanddewi Brefi and Llangeitho — abolition of the communities of Llanddewi Brefi and Llangeitho, and consequential changes to community councils

The community of Llanddewi Brefi as changed by this Order is abolished.

**141.** The existing community council of Llanddewi Brefi is dissolved.

**142.** The community of Llangeitho as changed by this Order is abolished.

**143.** The existing community council of Llangeitho is dissolved.
