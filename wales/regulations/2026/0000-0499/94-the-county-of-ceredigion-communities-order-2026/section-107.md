# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 107

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 107. Creation of the new community of Ciliau Aeron and Nantcwnlle

A new community is created which is to be known as Ciliau Aeron and Nantcwnlle in the English language and Ciliau Aeron a Nantcwnlle in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 48;
- (b) forms part of the electoral wards of Ciliau Aeron, and Llangybi.
