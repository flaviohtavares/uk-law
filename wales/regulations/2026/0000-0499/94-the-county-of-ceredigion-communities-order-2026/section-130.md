# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 130

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 130. Creation of the new community of Henfynyw and Llannarth

A new community is created which is to be known as Henfynyw and Llannarth in the English language and Henfynyw a Llannarth in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 54;
- (b) forms the electoral ward of Llannarth and part of the electoral ward of Ciliau Aeron.
