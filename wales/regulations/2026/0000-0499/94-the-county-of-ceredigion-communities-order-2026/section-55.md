# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 55

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 55. Aberporth — change of community name

The community of Aberporth as changed by this Order is to retain the name of Aberporth in the English language and is to be known as Aber-porth in the Welsh language.
