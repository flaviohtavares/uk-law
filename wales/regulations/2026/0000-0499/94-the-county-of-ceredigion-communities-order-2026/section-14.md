# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Aberystwyth — change of community ward names

The Canol/Central community ward of the community of Aberystwyth as changed by this Order is to retain the name of Canol/Central in the English language and is to be known as Canol Aberystwyth in the Welsh language.

**15.** The Gogledd/North community ward of the community of Aberystwyth as changed by this Order is to retain the name of Gogledd/North in the English language and is to be known as Gogledd Aberystwyth in the Welsh language.
