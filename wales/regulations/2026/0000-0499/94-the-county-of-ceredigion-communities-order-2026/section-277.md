# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 277

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 277. Creation of the new community of Ystrad Fflur

A new community is created which is to be known as Ystrad Fflur in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 81;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Tregaron and Ystrad Fflur and part of the electoral ward of Lledrod.
