# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 163

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 163. Lledrod and Llangwyryfon — changes in community and community ward boundaries

The part of the existing Blaenpennal community ward of the existing community of Lledrod shown shaded on Map 63—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Llangwyryfon;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanrhystyd.

**164.** The part of the existing community of Llanilar shown shaded on Map 64—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Llangwyryfon;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanrhystyd.

**165.** The part of the existing Haminiog community ward of the existing community of Llanrhystyd shown shaded on Map 65—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Llangwyryfon;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanrhystyd.
