# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 94

Local GOVERNMENT, WALES

The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026

Made

17 March 2026

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2) and (3)

The Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru[^f00001] (“**the Commission**”) has, under section 36(5)(a) of the Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru etc. Act 2013[^f00002] (“**the 2013 Act**”), submitted to the Welsh Ministers a report dated October 2024 containing its recommendations for change and the details of a review of the community arrangements within the County of Ceredigion.

The Welsh Ministers, after consideration of the matters referred to and set out in section 37(2)(b) and (c) of the 2013 Act, have decided to give effect to the Commission’s recommendations with modification. The Welsh Ministers also make supplemental provision as considered necessary or expedient.

Under section 37(3A) and (3B) of the 2013 Act[^f00003], more than 6 weeks have elapsed since those recommendations were submitted to the Welsh Ministers.

The Welsh Ministers have published a statement setting out their decision in respect of each recommendation under section 37(6) of the 2013 Act[^f00004].

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 37(1) and 40(1) and (2)(a) and (b) of the 2013 Act.

Under section 37(2A) of the 2013 Act[^f00005], the Welsh Ministers make this Order more than 6 months preceding the day of an ordinary council election under section 26 of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00006].

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order implements the recommendations of the Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru (“**the Commission**”), which reported in October 2024 on its review of the community electoral arrangements in the County of Ceredigion. The Commission’s report recommended changes to the existing boundaries of communities within the area of the County of Ceredigion and consequential changes to electoral arrangements. This Order implements the Commission’s recommendations with modification. This Order also makes supplemental provision as considered necessary or expedient by the Welsh Ministers in relation to some community names.

The effect of this Order is to make changes to a number of communities and consequential changes to electoral arrangements. This Order also abolishes a number of existing communities and community wards, dissolves their community councils and creates new communities, new community wards and new community councils. This Order also makes administrative provision for the operation of, and setting of precepts relating to, those new community councils.

The Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976 referred to in article 2 of this Order contain incidental, consequential, transitional and supplementary provisions about the effect and implementation of orders such as this.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to this Order. As a result, it was not considered necessary to carry out a regulatory impact assessment as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with this Order.

Prints of the maps labelled “1” to “81” to which this Order relates are deposited in the office of the Welsh Government at Cathays Park, Cardiff, CF10 3NQ (Elections Division), and with Ceredigion County Council. Prints deposited with Ceredigion County Council are open to inspection by anyone affected by the provisions of this Order.
