# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 76

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 76. Blaenrheidol and Pontarfynach — establishment of new community council and community electoral arrangements

A council for the new community of Blaenrheidol and Pontarfynach is established.

**77.** In the new community of Blaenrheidol and Pontarfynach, the number of councillors to be elected for the new community ward of—

- (a) Blaenrheidol is 4;
- (b) Pontarfynach is 4.
