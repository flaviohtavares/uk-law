# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 111

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 111. Ciliau Aeron and Nantcwnlle — establishment of new community council and community ward electoral arrangements

A council for the new community of Ciliau Aeron and Nantcwnlle is established.

**112.** In the new community of Ciliau Aeron and Nantcwnlle, the number of councillors to be elected for the new community ward of—

- (a) Cilcennin is 3;
- (b) Ciliau Aeron is 4;
- (c) Nantcwnlle is 6.
