# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 228

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 228. Creation of the new community of Llangrannog and Penbryn

A new community is created which is to be known as Llangrannog and Penbryn in the English language and Llangrannog a Phenbryn in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 74;
- (b) forms the electoral ward of Penbryn and part of the electoral ward of Llandysilio and Llangrannog.
