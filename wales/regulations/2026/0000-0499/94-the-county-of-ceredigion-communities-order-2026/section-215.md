# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 215

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 215. Llanfihangel Ystrad — establishment of new community council and community electoral arrangements

A council for the new community of Llanfihangel Ystrad is established.

**216.** In the new community of Llanfihangel Ystrad, the number of councillors to be elected for the new community ward of—

- (a) Dihewyd is 3;
- (b) Llanfihangel Ystrad is 6;
- (c) Llanwnnen is 3.
