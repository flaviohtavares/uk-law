# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Lampeter — changes in community boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the existing Silian community ward of the existing community of Llangybi shown shaded on Map 14—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Lampeter;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Lampeter.
