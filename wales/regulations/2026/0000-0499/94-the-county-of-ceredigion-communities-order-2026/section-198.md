# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 198

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 198. Creation of the new community of Llanfarian and Llanrhystud

A new community is created which is to be known as Llanfarian and Llanrhystud in the English language and Llanfarian a Llanrhystud in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 69;
- (b) forms the electoral ward of Llanfarian and part of the electoral ward of Llanrhystyd.
