# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 243

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 243. Ystwyth — establishment of new community council and community ward electoral arrangements

A council for the new community of Ystwyth is established.

**244.** In the new community of Ystwyth, the number of councillors to be elected for the new community ward of—

- (a) Llanilar is 7;
- (b) Trawsgoed is 6.
