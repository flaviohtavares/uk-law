# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 271

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 271. Ystrad Fflur, Ysbyty Ystwyth and Ystrad Meurig — abolition of the communities of Ystrad Fflur, Ysbyty Ystwyth and Ystrad Meurig, and consequential changes to community councils

The existing community of Ystrad Fflur is abolished.

**272.** The existing community council of Ystrad Fflur is dissolved.

**273.** The community of Ysbyty Ystwyth as changed by this Order is abolished.

**274.** The existing community council of Ysbyty Ystwyth is dissolved.

**275.** The community of Ystrad Meurig as changed by this Order is abolished.

**276.** The existing community council of Ystrad Meurig is dissolved.
