# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Llandysul — change of community ward names

The community ward of Pontshaen in the community of Llandysul as changed by this Order is to retain the name of Pontshaen in the English language and is to be known as Pontsiân in the Welsh language.

**29.** The community ward of Tregroes in the community of Llandysul as changed by this Order is to retain the name of Tregroes in the English language and is to be known as Tre-groes in the Welsh language.
