# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 223

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 223. Llangrannog and Penbryn — changes in community ward boundaries

The part of the existing community of Llangrannog shown shaded on Map 73—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Penbryn;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Penbryn.
