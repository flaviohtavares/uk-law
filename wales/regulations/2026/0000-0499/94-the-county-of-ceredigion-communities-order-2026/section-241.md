# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 241

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 241. Creation of the new community ward of Llanilar

A new community ward is created in the new community of Ystwyth which is to be known as Llanilar in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 1 on Map 76;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Ystwyth.
