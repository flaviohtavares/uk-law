# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 211

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 211. Creation of the new community of Llanfihangel Ystrad

A new community is created which is to be known as Llanfihangel Ystrad in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 71;
- (b) forms part of the electoral wards of Llanfihangel Ystrad and Llanwenog.
