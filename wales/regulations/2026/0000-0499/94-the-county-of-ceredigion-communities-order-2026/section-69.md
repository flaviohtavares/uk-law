# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 69

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Blaenrheidol and Pontarfynach — abolition of the communities of Blaenrheidol and Pontarfynach, and consequential changes to community councils

The community of Blaenrheidol as changed by this Order is abolished.

**70.** The existing community council of Blaenrheidol is dissolved.

**71.** The community of Pontarfynach as changed by this Order is abolished.

**72.** The existing community council of Pontarfynach is dissolved.
