# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 254

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 254. Tirymynach and Geneu’r Glyn — abolition of the communities of Tirymynach and Geneu’r Glyn, and consequential changes to community councils

The community of Tirymynach as changed by this Order is abolished.

**255.** The existing community council of Tirymynach is dissolved.

**256.** The community of Geneu’r Glyn as changed by this order is abolished.

**257.** The existing community council of Geneu’r Glyn is dissolved.
