# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 145

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 145. Creation of the new community ward of Llanddewi Brefi

A new community ward is created in the new community of Llangeitho which is to be known as Llanddewi Brefi in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 1 on Map 58;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llangeitho.
