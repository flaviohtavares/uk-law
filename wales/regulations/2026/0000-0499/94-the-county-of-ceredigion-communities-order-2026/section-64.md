# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 64

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 64. Beulah — community electoral ward arrangements

In the community of Beulah, the number of councillors to be elected for the community ward of—

- (a) Betws Ifan and Brongwyn is 4;
- (b) Beulah is 3;
- (c) Llandygwydd is 4.
