# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 260

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 260. Creation of the new community ward of Geneu’r Glyn

A new community ward is created in the new community of Tirymynach which is to be known as Geneu’r Glyn in the English language and Genau’r-glyn in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 2 on Map 80;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Borth.
