# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 236

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 236. Llanilar and Trawsgoed — abolition of the communities of Llanilar and Trawsgoed, and consequential changes to community councils

The community of Llanilar as changed by this Order is abolished.

**237.** The existing community council of Llanilar is dissolved.

**238.** The existing community of Trawsgoed as changed by this Order is abolished.

**239.** The existing community council of Trawsgoed is dissolved.
