# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Llandysul, and Llandyfriog — changes in community and community ward boundaries, and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the Trefol community ward of the community of Llandysul shown shaded and numbered 1 on Map 15—

- (a) is transferred to the Tregroes community ward of the community of Llandysul;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llandysul North and Troedyraur.

**27.** The part of the Orllwyn Teifi community ward of the community of Llandyfriog shown shaded and numbered 2 on Map 15—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Llandysul;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Tregroes;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llandysul North and Troedyraur.
