# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 206

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 206. Llanfihangel Ystrad and Llanwnnen — changes in community ward boundaries

The part of the existing community of Llanwnnen shown shaded on Map 70—

- (a) is transferred to existing community of Llanfihangel Ystrad;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Dihewyd;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanfihangel Ystrad.
