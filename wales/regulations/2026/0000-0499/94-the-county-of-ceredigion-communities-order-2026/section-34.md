# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. New Quay and Llanllwchaiarn — changes in community and community ward boundaries

The part of the existing community of Llanllwchaiarn shown shaded on Map 16—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of New Quay;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of New Quay and Llanllwchaearn.

**35.** The part of the existing community of Llanllwchaiarn shown shaded and numbered 1 on Map 17—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Llanarth;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Llanarth;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llannarth.

**36.** The part of the existing community of Llanllwchaiarn shown shaded and numbered 2 on Map 17—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Llanarth;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Llanarth;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llannarth.
