# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 170

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 170. Creation of the new community of Lledrod

A new community is created which is to be known as Lledrod in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 66;
- (b) forms part of the electoral wards of Lledrod, and Llanrhystyd.
