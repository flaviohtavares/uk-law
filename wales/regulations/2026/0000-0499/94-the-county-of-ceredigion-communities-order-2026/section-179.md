# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 179

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 179. Llandysiliogogo and Troedyraur — abolition of the communities of Llandysiliogogo and Troedyraur, and consequential changes to community councils.

The existing community of Llandysiliogogo is abolished.

**180.** The existing community council of Llandysiliogogo is dissolved.

**181.** The existing community of Troedyraur is abolished.

**182.** The existing community council of Troedyraur is dissolved.
