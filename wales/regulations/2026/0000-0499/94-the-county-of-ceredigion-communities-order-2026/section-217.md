# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 217

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 217. Llanfihangel Ystrad — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Llanfihangel Ystrad comprises the new Dihewyd and Llanfihangel Ystrad community wards of the new community of Llanfihangel Ystrad.

**218.** In the electoral ward of Llanfihangel Ystrad, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.

**219.** The electoral ward of Llanwenog comprises the new Llanwnnen community ward of the new community of Llanfihangel Ystrad, and the community of Llanwenog.

**220.** In the electoral ward of Llanwenog, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
