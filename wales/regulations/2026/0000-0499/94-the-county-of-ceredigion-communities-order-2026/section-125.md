# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 125

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 125. Llanbadarn Fawr — community electoral arrangements

In the community of Llanbadarn Fawr, the number of councillors to be elected for the community ward of—

- (a) Padarn is 5;
- (b) Sulien is 6.
