# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 189

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 189. Llandysiliogogo and Troed-yr-aur — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Llandysilio and Llangrannog comprises the new Llandysiliogogo community ward of the new community of Llandysiliogogo and Troed-yr-aur, and the new Llangrannog community ward of the new community of Llangrannog and Penbryn.

**190.** In the electoral ward of Llandysilio and Llangrannog, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.

**191.** The electoral ward of Llandysul North and Troedyraur comprises the Pontshaen and Tregroes community wards of the community of Llandysul, and the new Llangynllo and Troed-yr-aur community wards of the new community of Llandysiliogogo and Troed-yr-aur.

**192.** In the electoral ward of Llandysul North and Troedyraur, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
