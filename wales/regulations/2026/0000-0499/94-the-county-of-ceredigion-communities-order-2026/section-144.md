# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 144

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 144. Creation of the new community of Llangeitho

A new community is created which is to be known as Llangeitho in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 58;
- (b) forms the electoral ward of Llangeitho.
