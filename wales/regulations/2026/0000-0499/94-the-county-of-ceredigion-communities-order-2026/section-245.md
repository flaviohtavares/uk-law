# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 245

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 245. Ystwyth — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Ystwyth comprises the area of the new community of Ystwyth.

**246.** In the electoral ward of Ystwyth, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
