# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 44

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 44. New Quay — establishment of new community council and community ward electoral arrangements

A council for the new community of New Quay is established.

**45.** In the new community of New Quay, the number of councillors to be elected for the new community ward of—

- (a) New Quay is 6;
- (b) Llanllwchaearn is 5.
