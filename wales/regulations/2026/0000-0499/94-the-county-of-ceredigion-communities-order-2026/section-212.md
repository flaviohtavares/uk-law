# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 212

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 212. Creation of the new community ward of Dihewyd

A new community ward is created in the new community of Llanfihangel Ystrad which is to be known as Dihewyd in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 1 on Map 71;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanfihangel Ystrad.
