# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 147

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 147. Llangeitho — establishment of new community council and community ward electoral arrangements

A council for the new community of Llangeitho is established.

**148.** In the new community of Llangeitho, the number of councillors to be elected for the new community ward of—

- (a) Llanddewi Brefi is 6;
- (b) Llangeitho is 6.
