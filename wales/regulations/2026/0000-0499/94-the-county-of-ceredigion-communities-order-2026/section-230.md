# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 230

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 230. Creation of the new community ward of Penbryn

A new community ward is created in the new community of Llangrannog and Penbryn which is to be known as Penbryn in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 2 on Map 74;
- (b) forms the electoral ward of Penbryn.
