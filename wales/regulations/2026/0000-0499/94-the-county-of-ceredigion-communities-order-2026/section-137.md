# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 137

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 137. Llangeitho — changes in community and community ward boundaries

The part of the existing Gwynfil community ward of the existing community of Llangeitho shown shaded on Map 55—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Llanddewi Brefi;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llangeitho.

**138.** The part of the existing Penuwch community ward of the existing community of Llangeitho shown shaded on Map 56—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Lledrod;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Blaenpennal;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Lledrod.

**139.** The part of the existing Gwynfil community ward of the existing community of Llangeitho shown shaded on Map 57—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Llangybi;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Llangybi;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llangybi.
