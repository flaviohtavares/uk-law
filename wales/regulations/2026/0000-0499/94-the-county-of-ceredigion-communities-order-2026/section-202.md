# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 202

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 202. Llanfarian and Llanrhystud — establishment of new community council and community electoral arrangements

A council for the new community of Llanfarian and Llanrhystud is established.

**203.** In the new community of Llanfarian and Llanrhystud, the number of councillors to be elected for the new community ward of—

- (a) Llanbadarn y Creuddyn Isaf is 6;
- (b) Llanychaearn is 3;
- (c) Llanrhystud is 6.
