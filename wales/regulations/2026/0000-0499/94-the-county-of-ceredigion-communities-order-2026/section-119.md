# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 119

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 119. Faenor — changes in community and community ward boundaries, and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the Faenor community ward of the community of Faenor shown shaded on Map 49—

- (a) is transferred to the Waunfawr community ward of the community of Faenor;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Faenor.

**120.** The part of the Waunfawr community ward of the community of Faenor shown shaded on Map 50—

- (a) is transferred to the Faenor community ward of the community of Faenor;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Faenor.

**121.** The part of the existing Llangorwen community ward of the existing community of Tirymynach shown shaded on Map 51—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Faenor;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Faenor;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Faenor.

**122.** The part of the community ward of Faenor of the community of Faenor shown shaded on Map 52—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Llanbadarn Fawr;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Padarn;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanbadarn Fawr.
