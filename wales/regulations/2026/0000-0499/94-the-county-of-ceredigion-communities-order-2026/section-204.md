# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 204

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 204. Llanfarian and Llanrhystud — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Llanfarian comprises the new Llanbadarn y Creuddyn Isaf and Llanychaearn community wards of the new community of Llanfarian and Llanrhystud.

**205.** In the electoral ward of Llanfarian, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
