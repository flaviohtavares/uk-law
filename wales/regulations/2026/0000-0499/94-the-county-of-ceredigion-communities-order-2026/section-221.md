# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 221

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 221. Llangoedmor — changes in community boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the community of Llangoedmor shown shaded on Map 72—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Y Ferwig;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Aberporth and Y Ferwig.
