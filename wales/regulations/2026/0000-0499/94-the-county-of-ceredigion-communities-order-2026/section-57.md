# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 57

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 57. Beulah — changes in community and community ward boundaries, and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the existing Brongwyn community ward of the community of Beulah shown shaded on Map 25—

- (a) is transferred to the Beulah community ward of the community of Beulah;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Beulah and Llangoedmor.

**58.** The part of the Beulah community ward of the community of Beulah shown shaded on Map 26—

- (a) is transferred to the existing Bettws Evan community ward of the community of Beulah;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Beulah and Llangoedmor.

**59.** The part of the Beulah community ward of the community of Beulah shown shaded on Map 27—

- (a) is transferred to the Llandygwydd community ward of the community of Beulah;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Beulah and Llangoedmor.

**60.** The part of the community of Llangoedmor shown shaded on Map 28—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Beulah;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Beulah;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Beulah and Llangoedmor.

**61.** The part of the Beulah community ward of the community of Beulah shown shaded on Map 29—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Llangoedmor;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Beulah and Llangoedmor.

**62.** The part of the Bettws Evan community ward of the community of Beulah shown shaded on Map 30—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Penbryn;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Penbryn.
