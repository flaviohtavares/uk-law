# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 96

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 96. Ciliau Aeron and Nantcwnlle — changes in community boundaries

The part of the Llanbadarn Trefeglwys community ward of the community of Dyffryn Arth shown shaded on Map 41—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Ciliau Aeron;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Cilcennin;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Ciliau Aeron.

**97.** The part of the Llanbadarn Trefeglwys community ward of the community of Dyffryn Arth shown shaded on Map 42—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Nantcwnlle;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Nantcwnlle;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llangybi.

**98.** The part of the existing Cilcennin community ward of the existing community of Ciliau Aeron shown shaded on Map 43—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Dyffryn Arth;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Llanddewi Aberarth;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Aberaeron and Aberarth.

**99.** The part of the existing Ciliau Aeron community ward of the existing community of Ciliau Aeron shown shaded on Map 44—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Llanfihangel Ystrad;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Dihewyd;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanfihangel Ystrad.

**100.** The part of the existing Cilcennin community ward of the existing community of Ciliau Aeron shown shaded on Map 45—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Llanfihangel Ystrad;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Llanfihangel Ystrad;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanfihangel Ystrad.

**101.** The part of the existing Gartheli community ward of the existing community of Nantcwnlle shown shaded on Map 46—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Llangybi;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Llangybi;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llangybi.

**102.** The part of the existing Nantcwnlle community ward of the existing community of Nantcwnlle shown shaded on Map 47—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Llangeitho;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Penuwch;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llangeitho.
