# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 224

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 224. Llangrannog and Penbryn — abolition of the communities of Llangrannog and Penbryn, and consequential changes to community councils

The community of Llangrannog as changed by this Order is abolished.

**225.** The existing community council of Llangrannog is dissolved.

**226.** The community of Penbryn as changed by this Order is abolished.

**227.** The existing community council of Penbryn is dissolved.
