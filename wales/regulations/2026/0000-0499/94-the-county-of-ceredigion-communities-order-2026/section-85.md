# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 85

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 85. Llangynfelyn and Ysgubor-y-coed — abolition of the communities of Llangynfelyn and Ysgubor-y-coed, and consequential changes to community councils

The community of Llangynfelyn as changed by this Order is abolished.

**86.** The existing community council of Llangynfelyn is dissolved.

**87.** The existing community of Ysgubor-y-coed is abolished.

**88.** The existing community council of Ysgubor-y-coed is dissolved.
