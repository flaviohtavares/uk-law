# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 124

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 124. Llanbadarn Fawr — changes in community boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the community ward of Padarn of the community of Llanbadarn Fawr shown shaded on Map 53—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Llanfarian;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Llanbadarn y Creuddyn;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanfarian.
