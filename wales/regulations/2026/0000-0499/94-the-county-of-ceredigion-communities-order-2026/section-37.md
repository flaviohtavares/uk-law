# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. New Quay and Llanllwchaiarn — abolition of the communities of New Quay and Llanllwchaiarn, and consequential changes to community councils

The community of New Quay as changed by this Order is abolished.

**38.** The existing community council of New Quay is dissolved.

**39.** The community of Llanllwchaiarn as changed by this Order is abolished.

**40.** The existing community council of Llanllwchaiarn is dissolved.
