# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 154

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 154. Llanfair Clydogau and Llangybi — abolition of the communities of Llanfair Clydogau and Llangybi, and consequential changes to community councils

The existing community of Llanfair Clydogau is abolished.

**155.** The existing community council of Llanfair Clydogau is dissolved.

**156.** The community of Llangybi as changed by this Order is abolished.

**157.** The existing community council of Llangybi is dissolved.
