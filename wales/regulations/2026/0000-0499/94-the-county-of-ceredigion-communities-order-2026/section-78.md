# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 78

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 78. Blaenrheidol and Pontarfynach — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Melindwr comprises the area of the new community of Blaenrheidol and Pontarfynach, and the community of Melindwr.

**79.** In the electoral ward of Melindwr, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
