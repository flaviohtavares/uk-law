# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 65

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 65. Blaenrheidol and Pontarfynach — changes in community and community ward boundaries

The part of the existing community of Blaenrheidol shown shaded on Map 32—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Pontarfynach;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Melindwr.

**66.** The part of the existing community of Ysbyty Ystwyth shown shaded on Map 33—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Pontarfynach;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Melindwr.

**67.** The part of the community of Melindwr shown shaded on Map 34—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Blaenrheidol;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Melindwr.

**68.** The part of the existing community of Pontarfynach shown shaded on Map 35—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Melindwr;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Melindwr.
