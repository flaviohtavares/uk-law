# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 187

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 187. Llandysiliogogo and Troed-yr-aur — establishment of new community council and community electoral arrangements

A council for the new community of Llandysiliogogo and Troed-yr-aur is established.

**188.** In the new community of Llandysiliogogo and Troed-yr-aur, the number of councillors to be elected for the new community ward of—

- (a) Llandysiliogogo is 7;
- (b) Llangynllo is 3;
- (c) Troed-yr-aur is 5.
