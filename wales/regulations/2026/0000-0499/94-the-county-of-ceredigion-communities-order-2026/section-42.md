# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 42

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 42. Creation of the new community ward of New Quay

A new community ward is created in the new community of New Quay which is to be known as New Quay in the English language and Ceinewydd in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 1 on Map 18;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of New Quay and Llanllwchaearn.
