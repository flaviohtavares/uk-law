# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. Creation of the new community of New Quay

A new community is created which is to be known as New Quay in the English language and Ceinewydd in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 18;
- (b) forms the electoral ward of New Quay and Llanllwchaearn.
