# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 166

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 166. Lledrod and Llangwyryfon — abolition of the communities of Lledrod and Llangwyryfon, and consequential changes to community councils

The community of Lledrod as changed by this Order is abolished.

**167.** The existing community council of Lledrod is dissolved.

**168.** The community of Llangwyryfon as changed by this Order is abolished.

**169.** The existing community council of Llangwyryfon is dissolved.
