# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 46

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 46. New Quay and Llanllwchaearn — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of New Quay and Llanllwchaearn comprises the area of the new community of New Quay.

**47.** In the electoral ward of New Quay and Llanllwchaearn, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
