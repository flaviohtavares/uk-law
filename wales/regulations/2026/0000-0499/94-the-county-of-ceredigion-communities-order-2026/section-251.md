# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 251

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 251. Tirymynach and Geneu’r Glyn — changes in community ward boundaries and community boundaries

The part of the existing community of Geneu’r Glyn shown shaded on Map 77—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Tirymynach;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Tirymynach;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Tirymynach.

**252.** The part of the community of Borth shown shaded on Map 78—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Geneu’r Glyn;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Borth.

**253.** The part of the existing Tirymynach community ward of the existing community of Tirymynach shown shaded on Map 79—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Trefeurig;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Trefeurig.
