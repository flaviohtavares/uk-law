# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 126

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 126. Henfynyw and Llanarth — abolition of the communities of Henfynyw and Llanarth, and consequential changes to community councils

The community of Henfynyw as changed by this Order is abolished.

**127.** The existing community council of Henfynyw is dissolved.

**128.** The existing community of Llanarth is abolished.

**129.** The existing community council of Llanarth is dissolved.
