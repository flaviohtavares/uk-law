# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 207

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 207. Llanfihangel Ystrad and Llanwnnen — abolition of the communities of Llanfihangel Ystrad and Llanwnnen, and consequential changes to community councils

The community of Llanfihangel Ystrad as changed by this Order is abolished.

**208.** The existing community council of Llanfihangel Ystrad is dissolved.

**209.** The existing community of Llanwnnen is abolished.

**210.** The existing community council of Llanwnnen is dissolved.
