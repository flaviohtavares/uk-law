# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Aberystwyth — community ward electoral arrangements

In the community of Aberystwyth, the number of councillors to be elected for the community ward of—

- (a) Bronglais is 3;
- (b) Canol/Central is 3;
- (c) Gogledd/North is 3;
- (d) Penparcau is 5;
- (e) Rheidol is 4.
