# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 175

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 175. Lledrod — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Lledrod comprises the new Lledrod community ward of the new community of Lledrod, and the new community wards of Ystrad Meurig and Ysbyty Ystwyth of the new community of Ystrad Fflur.

**176.** In the electoral ward of Lledrod, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.

**177.** The electoral ward of Llanrhystyd comprises the new Llangwyryfon community ward of the new community of Lledrod, and the new Llanrhystud community ward of the new community of Llanfarian and Llanrhystud.

**178.** In the electoral ward of Llanrhystyd, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
