# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 193

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 193. Llanrhystyd — changes in community boundaries

The part of the existing Haminiog community ward of the existing community of Llanrhystyd shown shaded on Map 68—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Llansantffraid;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llansanffraid.
