# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 52

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 52. Aberporth — changes in community boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the Beulah community ward of the community of Beulah shown shaded on Map 22—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Aberporth;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Aberporth and Y Ferwig.

**53.** The part of the existing community of Penbryn shown shaded on Map 23—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Aberporth;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Aberporth and Y Ferwig.

**54.** The part of the community of Aberporth shown shaded on Map 24—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Penbryn;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Penbryn.
