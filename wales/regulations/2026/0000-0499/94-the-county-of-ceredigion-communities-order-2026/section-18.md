# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Cardigan — changes in community and community ward boundaries, and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the Mwldan community ward of the community of Cardigan shown shaded on Map 10—

- (a) is transferred to the Teifi community ward of the community of Cardigan;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Teifi.

**19.** The part of the Mwldan community ward of the community of Cardigan shown shaded on Map 11—

- (a) is transferred to the Rhyd-y-Fuwch community ward of the community of Cardigan;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Teifi.

**20.** The part of the Mwldan community ward of the community of Cardigan shown shaded on Map 12—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Y Ferwig;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Aberporth and Y Ferwig.

**21.** The part of the Mwldan community ward of the community of Cardigan shown shaded on Map 13—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Y Ferwig;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Aberporth and Y Ferwig.
