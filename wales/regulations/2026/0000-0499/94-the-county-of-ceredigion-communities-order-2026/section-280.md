# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 280

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 280. Creation of the new community ward of Ystrad Meurig

A new community ward is created in the new community of Ystrad Fflur which is to be known as Ystrad Meurig in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 3 on Map 81;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Lledrod.
