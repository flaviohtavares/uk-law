# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 92

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 92. Llangynfelyn and Ysgubor-y-coed — establishment of new community council and community electoral arrangements

A council for the new community of Llangynfelyn and Ysgubor-y-coed is established.

**93.** In the new community of Llangynfelyn and Ysgubor-y-coed, the number of councillors to be elected for the new community ward of—

- (a) Llangynfelyn is 5;
- (b) Ysgubor-y-coed is 3.
