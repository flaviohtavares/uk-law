# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 173

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 173. Lledrod — establishment of new community council and community electoral arrangements

A council for the new community of Lledrod is established.

**174.** In the new community of Lledrod, the number of councillors to be elected for the new community ward of—

- (a) Lledrod is 6;
- (b) Llangwyryfon is 4.
