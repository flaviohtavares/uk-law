# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 110

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 110. Creation of the new community ward of Nantcwnlle

A new community ward is created in the new community of Ciliau Aeron and Nantcwnlle which is to be known as Nantcwnlle in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 3 on Map 48;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llangybi.
