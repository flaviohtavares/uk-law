# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Aberaeron — changes in community boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the existing community of Henfynyw shown shaded on Map 1—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Aberaeron;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Aberaeron and Aberarth.

**4.** The part of the Llanddewi Aberarth community ward of the community of Dyffryn Arth shown shaded on Map 2—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Aberaeron;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Aberaeron and Aberarth.
