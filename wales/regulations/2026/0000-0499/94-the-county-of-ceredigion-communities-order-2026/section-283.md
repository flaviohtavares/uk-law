# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 283

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 283. Ystrad Fflur — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Tregaron and Ystrad Fflur comprises the new Ystrad Fflur community ward of the new community of Ystrad Fflur and the community of Tregaron.

**284.** In the electoral ward of Tregaron and Ystrad Fflur, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
