# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 286

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 286. New community councils

The proper officer of Ceredigion County Council must convene the first meeting of each of the new community councils established by articles 44, 76, 92, 111, 133, 147, 161, 173, 187, 202, 215, 231, 243, 261 and 281.
