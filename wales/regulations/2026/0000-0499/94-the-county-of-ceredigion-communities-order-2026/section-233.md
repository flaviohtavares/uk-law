# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 233

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 233. Llangrannog and Penbryn — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Penbryn comprises the new Penbryn community ward of the new community of Llangrannog and Penbryn.

**234.** In the electoral ward of Penbryn, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
