# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 267

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 267. Borth — change of community name

The community of Borth as changed by this Order is to retain the name of Borth in the English language and is to be known as Y Borth in the Welsh language.
