# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 73

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 73. Creation of the new community of Blaenrheidol and Pontarfynach

A new community is created which is to be known as Blaenrheidol and Pontarfynach in the English language and Blaenrheidol a Phontarfynach in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 36;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Melindwr.
