# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Llandysul — community ward electoral arrangements

In the community of Llandysul, the number of councillors to be elected for the community ward of—

- (a) Capel Dewi is 2;
- (b) Pontshaen is 2;
- (c) Trefol is 5;
- (d) Tregroes is 2.
