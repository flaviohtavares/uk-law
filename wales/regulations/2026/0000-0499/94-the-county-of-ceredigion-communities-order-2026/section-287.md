# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 287

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 287. New community councils — residuary successors

For the purposes of any provision of the 1976 Regulations which refers to the residuary successor of a dissolved authority—

- (a) for the existing community councils of New Quay and Llanllwchaiarn, the new community council of the new community of New Quay is specified as the residuary successor;
- (b) for the existing community councils of Blaenrheidol and Pontarfynach, the new community council of the new community of Blaenrheidol and Pontarfynach is specified as the residuary successor;
- (c) for the existing community councils of Llangynfelyn and Ysgubor-y-coed, the new community council of the new community of Llangynfelyn and Ysgubor-y-coed is specified as the residuary successor;
- (d) for the existing community councils of Ciliau Aeron and Nantcwnlle, the new community council of the new community of Ciliau Aeron and Nantcwnlle is specified as the residuary successor;
- (e) for the existing community councils of Henfynyw and Llannarth, the new community council of the new community of Henfynyw and Llannarth is specified as the residuary successor;
- (f) for the existing community councils of Llanddewi Brefi and Llangeitho, the new community council of the new community of Llangeitho is specified as the residuary successor;
- (g) for the existing community councils of Llanfair Clydogau and Llangybi, the new community council of the new community of Llangybi is specified as the residuary successor;
- (h) for the existing community councils of Lledrod and Llangwyryfon, the new community council of the new community of Lledrod is specified as the residuary successor;
- (i) for the existing community councils of Llandysiliogogo and Troedyraur, the new community council of the new community of Llandysiliogogo and Troed-yr-aur is specified as the residuary successor;
- (j) for the existing community councils of Llanfarian and Llanrhystyd, the new community council of the new community of Llanfarian and Llanrhystud is specified as the residuary successor;
- (k) for the existing community councils of Llanfihangel Ystrad and Llanwnnen, the new community council of the new community of Llanfihangel Ystrad is specified as the residuary successor;
- (l) for the existing community councils of Llangrannog and Penbryn, the new community council of the new community of Llangrannog and Penbryn is specified as the residuary successor;
- (m) for the existing community councils of Llanilar and Trawsgoed, the new community council of the new community of Ystwyth is specified as the residuary successor;
- (n) for the existing community councils of Tirymynach and Geneu’r Glyn, the new community council of the new community of Tirymynach is specified as the residuary successor;
- (o) for the existing community councils of Ystrad Fflur, Ysbyty Ystwyth and Ystrad Meurig, the new community council of the new community of Ystrad Fflur is specified as the residuary successor.
