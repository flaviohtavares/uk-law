# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 113

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 113. Ciliau Aeron and Nantcwnlle — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Ciliau Aeron comprises the new Cilcennin and Ciliau Aeron community wards of the new community of Ciliau Aeron and Nantcwnlle, and the new Henfynyw community ward of the new community of Henfynyw and Llannarth.

**114.** In the electoral ward of Ciliau Aeron, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.

**115.** The electoral ward of Llangybi comprises the new community of Llangybi and the new Nantcwnlle community ward of the new community of Ciliau Aeron and Nantcwnlle.

**116.** In the electoral ward of Llangybi, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
