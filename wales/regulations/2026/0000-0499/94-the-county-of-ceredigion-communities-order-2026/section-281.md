# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 281

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 281. Ystrad Fflur — establishment of new community council and community ward electoral arrangements

A council for the new community of Ystrad Fflur is established.

**282.** In the new community of Ystrad Fflur, the number of councillors to be elected for the new community ward of—

- (a) Ysbyty Ystwyth is 4;
- (b) Ystrad Fflur is 6;
- (c) Ystrad Meurig is 4.
