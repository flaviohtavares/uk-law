# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Cardigan — community ward electoral arrangements

In the community of Cardigan, the number of councillors to be elected for the community ward of—

- (a) Mwldan is 4;
- (b) Rhyd-y-Fuwch is 3;
- (c) Teifi is 3.
