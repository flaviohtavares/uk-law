# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 261

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 261. Tirymynach — establishment of new community council and community electoral arrangements

A council for the new community of Tirymynach is established.

**262.** In the new community of Tirymynach, the number of councillors to be elected for the new community ward of—

- (a) Geneu’r Glyn is 4;
- (b) Tirymynach is 10.
