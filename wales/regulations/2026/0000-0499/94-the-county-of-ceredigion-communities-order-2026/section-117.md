# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 117

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 117. Dyffryn Arth — change of community ward name

The community ward of Llanddewi Aberarth as changed by this Order is to be known as Llanddewi Aberarth in the English language and Llanddewi Aber-arth in the Welsh language.
