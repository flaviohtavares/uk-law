# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 194

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 194. Llanfarian and Llanrhystyd — abolition of the communities of Llanfarian and Llanrhystyd, and consequential changes to community councils

The community of Llanfarian as changed by this Order is abolished.

**195.** The existing community council of Llanfarian is dissolved.

**196.** The community of Llanrhystyd as changed by this Order is abolished.

**197.** The existing community council of Llanrhystyd is dissolved.
