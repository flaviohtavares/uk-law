# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 118

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 118. Dyffryn Arth — community ward electoral arrangements

In the community of Dyffryn Arth, the number of councillors to be elected for the community ward of—

- (a) Llanbadarn Trefeglwys is 4;
- (b) Llanddewi Aberarth is 3.
