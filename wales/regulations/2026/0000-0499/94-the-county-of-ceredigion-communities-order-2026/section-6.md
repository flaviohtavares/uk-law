# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Aberystwyth — changes in community and community ward boundaries, and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the Gogledd/North community ward of the community of Aberystwyth shown shaded and numbered 1 on Map 3—

- (a) is transferred to the Canol/Central community ward of the community of Aberystwyth;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Aberystwyth Morfa a Glais.

**7.** The part of the Canol/Central community ward of the community of Aberystwyth shown shaded and numbered 2 on Map 3—

- (a) is transferred to the Bronglais community ward of the community of Aberystwyth;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Aberystwyth Morfa a Glais.

**8.** The part of the Penparcau community ward of the community of Aberystwyth shown shaded on Map 4—

- (a) is transferred to the Rheidol community ward of the community of Aberystwyth;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Aberystwyth Rheidol.

**9.** The part of the Sulien community ward of the community of Llanbadarn Fawr shown shaded on Map 5—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Aberystwyth;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Bronglais;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Aberystwyth Morfa a Glais.

**10.** The part of the Sulien community ward of the community of Llanbadarn Fawr shown shaded on Map 6—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Aberystwyth;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Bronglais;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Aberystwyth Morfa a Glais.

**11.** The part of the existing Llanbadarn y Creuddyn community ward of the existing community of Llanfarian shown shaded on Map 7—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Aberystwyth;
- (b) forms part of the Penparcau community ward;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward Aberystwyth Penparcau.

**12.** The part of the existing Llanbadarn y Creuddyn community ward of the existing community of Llanfarian shown shaded on Map 8—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Aberystwyth;
- (b) forms part of the Penparcau community ward;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Aberystwyth Penparcau.

**13.** The part of the Gogledd/North community ward of the community of Aberystwyth shown shaded on Map 9—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Faenor;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Waunfawr;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Faenor.
