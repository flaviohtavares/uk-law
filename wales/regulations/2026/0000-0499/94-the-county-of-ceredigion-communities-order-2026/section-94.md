# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 94

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 94. Llangynfelyn and Ysgubor-y-coed — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Ceulan a Maesmawr comprises the areas of the community of Ceulan a Maesmawr, and the new community of Llangynfelyn and Ysgubor-y-coed.

**95.** In the electoral ward of Ceulan a Maesmawr, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
