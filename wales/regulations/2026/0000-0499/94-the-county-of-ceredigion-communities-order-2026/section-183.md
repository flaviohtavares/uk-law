# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 183

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 183. Creation of the new community of Llandysiliogogo and Troed-yr-aur

A new community is created which is to be known as Llandysiliogogo and Troed-yr-aur in the English language and Llandysiliogogo a Throed-yr-aur in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 67;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llandysilio and Llangrannog and part of the electoral ward of Llandysul North and Troedyraur.
