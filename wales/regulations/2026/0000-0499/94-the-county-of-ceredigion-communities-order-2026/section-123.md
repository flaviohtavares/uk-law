# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 123

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 123. Faenor — community electoral arrangements

In the community of Faenor, the number of councillors to be elected for the community ward of—

- (a) Faenor is 3;
- (b) Waunfawr is 5.
