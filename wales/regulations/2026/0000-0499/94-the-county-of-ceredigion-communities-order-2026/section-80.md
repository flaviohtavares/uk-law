# The County of Ceredigion (Communities) Order 2026, section 80

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 80. Ceulanamaesmawr — changes in community boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the existing community of Llangynfelyn shown shaded on Map 37—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Ceulanamaesmawr;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Ceulan a Maesmawr.

**81.** The part of the community of Trefeurig shown shaded on Map 38—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Ceulanamaesmawr;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Ceulan a Maesmawr.

**82.** The part of the community of Ceulanamaesmawr shown shaded on Map 39—

- (a) is transferred to the existing community of Tirymynach;
- (b) forms part of the existing community ward of Tirymynach;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Tirymynach.
