# The Animal Health Act 1981 (Extension of Definition of Disease) (Wales) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 114

ANIMALS, WALES

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Animal Health Act 1981 (Extension of Definition of Disease) (Wales) Order 2026

Made

16 July 2026

Coming into force

24 July 2026

The Welsh Ministers[^f00001] make this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 88(2) of the Animal Health Act 1981.

## 1. Title, application and coming into force

(1) The title of this Order is the Animal Health Act 1981 (Extension of Definition of Disease) (Wales) Order 2026.

(2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

(3) This Order comes into force on 24 July 2026

## 2. Extension of the definition of “disease”

For the purposes of the Animal Health Act 1981, the definition of “disease” in section 88(1) of that Act is extended so as to include swine vesicular disease.

Llyr Gruffydd

Cabinet Minister for Rural Resilience and Sustainability, one of the Welsh Ministers

16 July 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order extends, for the purposes of the Animal Health Act [1981 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/22), the definition of “disease” in section 88(1) of that Act so as to include swine vesicular disease.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to this Order. As a result, it was not considered necessary to carry out a regulatory impact assessment as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with this instrument.
