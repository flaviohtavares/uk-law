# The Deposit Scheme for Drinks Containers (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 97

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 97. Spent convictions of individuals

NRW must remove details of any conviction from the public register once the rehabilitation period for a sentence has ended under section 5 of the Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974[^f00014].

## Annotations

[^f00014]: Footnote: [1974 c. 53](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1974/53). Section 5 of the Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 was amended by section 139(4) of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act [2012 (c. 10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/10). There are other amendments to section 5 but they are not relevant for the purposes of these Regulations.
