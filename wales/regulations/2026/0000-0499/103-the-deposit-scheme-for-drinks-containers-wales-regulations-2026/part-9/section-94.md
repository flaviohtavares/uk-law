# The Deposit Scheme for Drinks Containers (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 94

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 94. Penalties and costs received under this Part

(1) NRW must pay any penalty it receives under this Part into the Welsh Consolidated Fund established under section 117 of the Government of Wales Act 2006[^f00013].

(2) A local weights and measures authority may retain any penalty and any costs that it receives under this Part.
