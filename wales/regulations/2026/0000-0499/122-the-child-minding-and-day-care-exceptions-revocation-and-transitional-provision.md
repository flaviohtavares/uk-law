# The Child Minding and Day Care Exceptions (Revocation and Transitional Provision) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 122

SOCIAL CARE, WALES

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS, WALES

The Child Minding and Day Care Exceptions (Revocation and Transitional Provision) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2026

Made

14 October 2026

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2)

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 19(4) and (5) and 74(2)(c) of the Children and Families (Wales) Measure 2010[^f00001] (“the Measure”).

In accordance with the Senedd approval procedure applied by section 74(5) of the Measure, a draft of this Welsh statutory instrument was laid before, and approved by resolution of, Senedd Cymru [^f00002].

## 1. Title and coming into force

(1) The title of this Order is the Child Minding and Day Care Exceptions (Revocation and Transitional Provision) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2026.

(2) This Order comes into force on 1 April 2027, immediately after the Child Minding and Day Care Exceptions (Revocation and Transitional Provision) (Wales) Order 2026[^f00003] comes into force.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order, “**the Order**” means the Child Minding and Day Care Exceptions (Revocation and Transitional Provision) (Wales) Order 2026.

## 3. Amendments to the Order

The Order is amended in accordance with article 4.

## 4. Insertion of Part 5 and the Schedule

(1) After Part 4 insert—

> **Part 5 — Consequential Amendments**
>
> **23. Consequential amendments**
>
> The Schedule contains amendments consequential upon the coming into force of this Order.

(2) At the end of the Order insert the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

Sioned Williams

Deputy First Minister and Cabinet Minister for Social Justice and Equality, one of the Welsh Ministers

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made under sections 19(4) and (5) and 74(2)(c) of the Children and Families (Wales) Measure 2010 (“the Measure”).

The Child Minding and Day Care Exceptions (Revocation and Transitional Provision) (Wales) Order 2026 (“**the 2026 Order**”) revokes and replaces the Child Minding and Day Care Exceptions (Wales) Order 2010 (“**the 2010 Order**”). The 2026 Order comes into force on 1 April 2027 and sets out exceptions to what constitutes “child minding” and “**day care for children**” for the purposes of Part 2 of the Measure.

This Order amends the 2026 Order by inserting a new article 23 and a Schedule of consequential amendments that update references to the 2010 Order in relevant secondary legislation.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to this Order. As a result, it was not considered necessary to carry out a regulatory impact assessment as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with this Order.
