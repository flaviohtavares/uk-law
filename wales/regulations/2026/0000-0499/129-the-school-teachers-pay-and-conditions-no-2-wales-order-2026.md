# The School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (No. 2) (Wales) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 129

EDUCATION, WALES

The School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (No. 2) (Wales) Order 2026

Made

22 October 2026

Coming into force

20 November 2026

The Welsh Ministers make this Order in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by [sections 122](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/122) [(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/122), [123](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/123) and [124](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/124) of the [Education Act 2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents)[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002].

In accordance with [section 126](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/126)[^f00003] of that Act, the Welsh Ministers have consulted such of the persons and bodies referred to in that section as appeared to the Welsh Ministers to be appropriate.

## 1. Title, coming into force, application and interpretation

(1) The title of this Order is the School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (No. 2) (Wales) Order 2026.

(2) This Order comes into force on 20 November 2026.

(3) This Order applies to school teachers in Wales.

(4) In this Order, “**school teacher**” has the meaning given by [section 122](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/122) [(3) to (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/section/122) of the [Education Act 2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/32/contents).

## 2. The Document

(1) The provisions set out in section 2 of the Document have effect on and after 1 September 2026 for the purposes of determining—

- (a) the remuneration of school teachers, and
- (b) other conditions of employment of school teachers which relate to their professional duties and working time.

(2) In this article, “**the Document**” means the document entitled “School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (Wales) Document 2026 and guidance on school teachers’ pay and conditions” and published on the gov.wales website[^f00004].

## 3. Revocation

The [School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (Wales) Order 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2026/29/contents)[^f00005] is revoked.

Anna Brychan

Cabinet Minister for Education and the Welsh Language, one of the Welsh Ministers

22 October 2026

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes provision for the determination of the remuneration of school teachers in Wales and other conditions of their employment which relate to their professional duties or working time.

The Order makes this provision by reference to section 2 of a document entitled “**School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (Wales) Document 2026 and guidance on school teachers’ pay and conditions**” (“**the Document**”). The Document can be found on the Welsh Government website: https://www.gov.wales/teaching-and-leadership.

Article 2 makes retrospective provision, under section 123(3) of the Education Act 2002, to provide that the provisions set out in section 2 of the Document have effect on and after 1 September 2026 notwithstanding that the Order comes into force after that date.

Article 3 revokes the School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions (Wales) Order 2026.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to this Order. As a result, a regulatory impact assessment has been prepared as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with this Order. A copy can be obtained from the School Practitioner Division, Welsh Government, Cathays Park, Cardiff, CF10 3NQ.
