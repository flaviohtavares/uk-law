# The Local Elections (Wales) (Amendment) Rules 2026, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**4.** In rule 6 (nomination papers: descriptions), for paragraph (4)(b) substitute—

> - (b) the description—
>   - (i) consists of the registered name of each of the parties shown in either or both of the versions described in paragraph (5), or
>   - (ii) is a description registered for use by each of the parties under section 28B of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000[^f00005] (joint descriptions), and

.

## Annotations

[^f00005]: Footnote: [2000 c. 41](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/41). Section 28B was inserted by section 49(1) of the Electoral Administration Act [2006 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/22).
