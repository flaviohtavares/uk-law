# The Local Elections (Wales) (Amendment) Rules 2026, section 57

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**57.**  In Appendix 6A[^f00022] (guidance for voters at combined polls), at the end of the form, after the final paragraph insert—

> **Voting is secret. Do not let anyone see how you have voted.**
>
> **If you make a mistake or need some help, just ask the staff.**

## Annotations

[^f00022]: Footnote: Appendix 6A was inserted by rule 10(5) of [S.I. 2023/1064 (W. 180)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2023/1064).
