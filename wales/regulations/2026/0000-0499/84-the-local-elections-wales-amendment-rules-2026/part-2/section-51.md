# The Local Elections (Wales) (Amendment) Rules 2026, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**51.** In Appendix 1 (form of nomination paper), for the table in section 4 (candidate’s statement of party membership) substitute—

> <table>
> <tbody>
> <tr><td><strong>Registered name or names of each registered political party</strong></td><td colspan="2"><strong>The dates during the 12 months period when you were a member (using the format dd/mm/yyyy to dd/mm/yyyy)</strong><br> <br><em>You do not need to complete the table if you have included a permitted party description in section 3 and have not been a member of any registered political party during the 12 months period other than the party or parties to which the description relates.</em><br><em>If you were a member of a party at the start of the period, the date provided in the <strong>From</strong> column will be the start of the period.</em><br><em>If you joined a party during the period, the date provided in the <strong>From</strong> column will be the date you joined the party.</em><br><em>If you ceased to be a member of a party during the period, the date provided in the <strong>To</strong> column will be the date on which you left the party.</em><br><em>If you remain a member of a party when you sign the form, you can insert “now” in the <strong>To</strong> column instead of a date.</em></td></tr>
