# The Local Elections (Wales) (Amendment) Rules 2026, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Rules—

- “**the 1983 Act**” (“***Deddf 1983***”) means the Representation of the People Act 1983;
- “**the Communities Rules**” (“y *Rheolau Cymunedau*”) means the Local Elections (Communities) (Wales) Rules 2021[^f00003];
- “**the Principal Areas Rules**” (“y *Rheolau Prif Ardaloedd*”) means the Local Elections (Principal Areas) (Wales) Rules 2021[^f00004].
