# The Local Elections (Wales) (Amendment) Rules 2026, section 85

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 85. Schedule 2 to the Communities Rules

Schedule 2 to the Communities Rules (rules for conduct of an election of councillors for a community where poll is taken together with poll at a relevant election) is amended in accordance with rules 86 to 109.
