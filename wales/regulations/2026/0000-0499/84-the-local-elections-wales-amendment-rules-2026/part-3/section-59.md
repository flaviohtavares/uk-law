# The Local Elections (Wales) (Amendment) Rules 2026, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Rule 5 of the Communities Rules

 In rule 5(12) of the Communities Rules (filling of casual vacancies), for “the expected day of” substitute “the fourth day after”.
