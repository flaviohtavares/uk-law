# The County of Carmarthenshire (Electoral Arrangements) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 125

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, WALES

The County of Carmarthenshire (Electoral Arrangements) Order 2026

Made

16 October 2026

Coming into force in accordance with article 1(2) and (3)

The Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru[^f00001] (“**the Commission**”) has, under section 36(5) of the Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru etc. Act 2013[^f00002] (“**the 2013 Act**”), submitted to the Welsh Ministers a report dated January 2026 containing its recommendations for change and the details of its review of the electoral arrangements for the County of Carmarthenshire.

The Welsh Ministers have decided to give effect to the Commission’s recommendations without modification.

Under section 37(3A) and (3B) of the 2013 Act[^f00003], more than 6 weeks have elapsed since those recommendations were submitted to the Welsh Ministers and the Welsh Ministers have had regard to any representations made during that period.

The Welsh Ministers have published a statement setting out their decision in respect of each recommendation under section 37(6) of the 2013 Act[^f00004].

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 37(1)(a) and 40(1) and (2)(a) and (b) of the 2013 Act.

Under section 37(2A) of the 2013 Act[^f00005], the Welsh Ministers make this Order more than 6 months preceding the day of an ordinary council election under section 26 of the Local Government Act 1972[^f00006].

## 1. Title and coming into force

(1) The title of this Order is the County of Carmarthenshire (Electoral Arrangements) Order 2026.

(2) For any purpose set out in regulation 4(1) of the 1976 Regulations[^f00007], this Order comes into force on 6 November 2026.

(3) For all other purposes, this Order comes into force at 23:59 hours on the day before the ordinary day of election of councillors in 2027.

## 2. Interpretation

(1) In this Order—

- “**the 1972 Act**” (“***Deddf 1972***”) means the Local Government Act 1972;
- “**the 1976 Regulations**” (“***Rheoliadau 1976***”) means the Local Government Area Changes Regulations 1976;
- “**the 2013 Act**” (“***Deddf 2013***”) means the Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru etc. Act 2013;
- “**electoral ward**” (“***ward etholiadol***”) means any area for which councillors are elected to Carmarthenshire County Council;

any reference to a map means one of the 4 maps marked “Map of the County of Carmarthenshire (Electoral Arrangements) Order 2026” deposited in accordance with regulation 5 of the 1976 Regulations, labelled “1” to “4”, and a reference to the numbered map is a reference to the map that bears that number;

where a boundary is shown on a map as running along a road, railway line, footway, watercourse or similar geographical feature, it is to be treated as running along the centre line of the feature.

(2) Words and expressions used in this Order have the same meaning as they have in Part 3 of the 2013 Act, except so far as a contrary intention appears.

(3) The provisions in this Order take precedence over any conflicting provision in any previous statutory instrument made under section 58(2) of the 1972 Act[^f00008] or order made under section 37(1) of the 2013 Act[^f00009].

## 3. Electoral arrangements for the County of Carmarthenshire

(1) The electoral wards of the County of Carmarthenshire, as they exist immediately before the ordinary day of election of councillors in 2027, are abolished.

(2) The County of Carmarthenshire is divided into 47 electoral wards each bearing the English language name listed in column 1 and the Welsh language name listed in column 2 of the Table in the Schedule to this Order.

(3) Each electoral ward comprises the areas specified in relation to that electoral ward in column 3 of the Table in the Schedule to this Order.

(4) The number of members of Carmarthenshire County Council to be elected for each electoral ward is the number specified in column 4 of the Table in the Schedule to this Order.

## 4. The communities of Llangyndeyrn and Llanelli Rural - consequential changes to electoral ward arrangements

The part of the community of Llanelli Rural shown shaded on Map 1 forms part of the community of Llangyndeyrn in the electoral ward of Llangyndeyrn[^f00010].

## 5. The communities of Llangyndeyrn and Trimsaran - consequential changes to electoral ward arrangements

The part of the community of Trimsaran shown shaded on Map 2 forms part of the community of Llangyndeyrn in the electoral ward of Llangyndeyrn[^f00011].

## 6. The communities of Llangyndeyrn and Pontyberem - consequential changes to electoral ward arrangements

The part of the community of Pontyberem shown shaded on Map 3 forms part of the community of Llangyndeyrn in the electoral ward of Llangyndeyrn[^f00012].

## 7. The communities of Llanboidy and Whitland - consequential changes to electoral ward arrangements

The part of the community of Llanboidy shown shaded on Map 4 forms part of the community of Whitland in the electoral ward of Whitland[^f00013].

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order implements the recommendations of the Democracy and Boundary Commission Cymru (“**the Commission**”), which reported in January 2026 on its review of the electoral arrangements for the County of Carmarthenshire. The Commission’s report proposed a reduction in the number of electoral wards from 51 to 47 and a decrease in the number of councillors from 75 to 74. The Commission’s report also recommended consequential changes to electoral arrangements for the County of Carmarthenshire as a result of changes to community boundaries that have already been implemented.

This Order implements the Commission’s recommendations without modification.

Article 3 of this Order abolishes the existing electoral arrangements for the County of Carmarthenshire and introduces the Schedule, which sets out the new electoral arrangements for the County of Carmarthenshire.

This Order also implements recommendations for consequential changes to electoral arrangements for some communities within the County of Carmarthenshire. Following changes made to community and community ward boundaries, the consequential changes were not implemented for the electoral wards at the same time.

Article 4 provides for the transfer of part of the community of Llanelli Rural to the community of Llangyndeyrn in the electoral ward of Llangyndeyrn.

Article 5 provides for the transfer of part of the community of Trimsaran to the community of Llangyndeyrn in the electoral ward of Llangyndeyrn.

Article 6 provides for the transfer of part of the community of Pontyberem to the community of Llangyndeyrn in the electoral ward of Llangyndeyrn.

Article 7 provides for the transfer of part of the community of Llanboidy to Whitland electoral ward.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to this Order. As a result, it was not considered necessary to carry out a regulatory impact assessment as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with this Order.

Prints of the maps labelled “1” to “4” to which this Order relates are deposited in the office of the Welsh Government at Cathays Park, Cardiff, CF10 3NQ (Elections Division), and with Carmarthenshire County Council. Prints deposited with Carmarthenshire County Council are open to inspection by anyone affected by the provisions of this Order.
