# The National Health Service (General Dental Services Contracts and Patient Charges) (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 7

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Appeal[^key-97a5dac7e8e3ef7c9cf48440c999af40]

*Extent: E+W*

A person who has been served with a notice under regulation 6(1) may appeal to the First-tier Tribunal against the decision of the Local Health Board that the conditions in regulation 4 or 5 are not met.
