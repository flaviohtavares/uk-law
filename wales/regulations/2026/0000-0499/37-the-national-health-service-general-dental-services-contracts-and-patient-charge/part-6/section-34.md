# The National Health Service (General Dental Services Contracts and Patient Charges) (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 34

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 34. Interpretation of Part 6[^c-184049cbe37fd93ef45d48e25a688e02]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “**existing contract**” (“***contract presennol***”) means a contract entered into under the 2006 Regulations which is in force immediately before 11 March 2026;
- “**existing contractor**” (“***contractwr presennol***”) means a contractor who is a party to an existing contract.
