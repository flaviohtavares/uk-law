# The National Health Service (General Dental Services Contracts and Patient Charges) (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 40

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Disputes and appeals[^c-29c0071443acb163c4fa49d05fc85757]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where, before 11 March 2026—

- (a) a dispute has been referred for resolution under the 2006 Regulations, or
- (b) an appeal has been made under the 2006 Regulations,

the dispute or appeal continues to be dealt with in accordance with the 2006 Regulations as if they had not been revoked.

(2) Where a dispute or appeal arises on or after 11 March 2026 in relation to a matter which occurred before that date, the dispute or appeal must be dealt with in accordance with these Regulations.
