# The National Health Service (General Dental Services Contracts and Patient Charges) (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 41

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Charges[^key-f239bf0ae6cc36e449462abf5286caf6]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a course of treatment is started before 1 April 2026 and continues after that date—

- (a) the charge applicable is the charge set out in the treatment plan provided to the patient under paragraph 7 of Schedule 3 to the 2006 Regulations calculated in accordance with the 2006 (Charges) Regulations as they applied at the time the treatment plan was accepted by the patient;
- (b) any guarantee period is calculated in accordance with the 2006 (Charges) Regulations as they were in force when the restoration was provided.
