# The National Health Service (General Dental Services Contracts and Patient Charges) (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 35

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Continuity of existing contracts[^c-fec8696dd2b36886985d215aa1443e99]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An existing contract has effect on and after 1 April 2026 as if it were a contract entered into under these Regulations.

(2) Paragraph (1) is subject to regulations 36 and 39 to 41.

(3) The Local Health Board and the contractor may agree to vary an existing contract to bring it into conformity with these Regulations.
