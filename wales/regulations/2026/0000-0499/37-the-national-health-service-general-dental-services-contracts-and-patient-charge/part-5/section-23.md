## 23. Appeals against mid-year financial adjustments[^c-92280c8651964741eb80649e4a1a5f33]

*Extent: E+W*

A contractor who is subject to a mid-year financial adjustment in accordance with regulation 22 may appeal that decision to the Welsh Ministers in accordance with the procedure provided for in paragraph 53 or 54, as the case may be, of Schedule 3.
