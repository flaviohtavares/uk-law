# The National Health Service (General Dental Services Contracts and Patient Charges) (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 11

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. NHS contracts[^key-e76a301a78d94bf38b078f39b4e7fd7d]

*Extent: E+W*

In the case of a contractor who must be regarded as a health service body pursuant to regulation 9, the contract must state that it is an NHS contract.
