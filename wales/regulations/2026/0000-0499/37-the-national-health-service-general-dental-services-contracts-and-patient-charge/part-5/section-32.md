# The National Health Service (General Dental Services Contracts and Patient Charges) (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 32

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 32. Arrangements on termination of a contract[^key-bfe510b46b6691e6027929dd6d141af7]

*Extent: E+W*

A contract must make provision for the arrangements which are to apply on the termination of the contract, including—

- (a) the transfer of patient records,
- (b) the transfer of responsibility for ongoing courses of treatment,
- (c) the return of any equipment or materials supplied by the Local Health Board,
- (d) financial consequences of termination of a contract, including—
  - (i) payment for services provided up to the date of termination of the contract;
  - (ii) recovery of any overpayments;
  - (iii) any financial penalties arising from the termination of the contract,
- (e) the contractor’s continuing obligations in respect of confidentiality and data protection, and
- (f) dispute resolution in relation to any matter arising from the termination of the contract.
