# The National Health Service (General Dental Services Contracts and Patient Charges) (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 26

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Appeal against application of regulation 25[^c-218ea30bbdfa75f46dabe30c6bec8a29]

*Extent: E+W*

A contractor who is subject to a financial adjustment or to whom financial recovery has been applied in accordance with regulation 25, may appeal the Local Health Board’s decision to the Welsh Ministers in accordance with the procedure provided for in paragraph 53 or 54, as the case may be, of Schedule 3.
