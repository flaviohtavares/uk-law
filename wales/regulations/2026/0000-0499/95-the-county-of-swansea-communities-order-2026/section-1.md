# The County of Swansea (Communities) Order 2026, section 1

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Title and coming into force[^c-4d8422f58eb8abbf6e2012ada80ed498]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The title of this Order is the County of Swansea (Communities) Order 2026.

(2) For any purpose set out in regulation 4(1) of the 1976 Regulations[^f00007], this Order comes into force on 6 May 2026.

(3) For all other purposes, this Order comes into force at 23:59 hours on the day before the ordinary day of election of councillors in 2027.
