## 51. Community of Bishopston — changes to community electoral arrangements[^c-8ad2998c32c7cd32d31d58a923471bd7]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Bishopston, the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Bishopston ward is 5;
- (b) Murton ward is 4.
