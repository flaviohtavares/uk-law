## 84. Cwmbwrla, Penderry, and Penllergaer — changes in community and community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^key-77395460f13af9e8c666d787c57a43dd]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the community of Cwmbwrla shown shaded on Map 38—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Penderry;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Penderry.
