## 73. Dunvant, Killay, and Upper Killay — changes in community and community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^key-c4297ee89c4f253ab62118af76fc3e4d]

*Extent: E+W*

The parts of the community of Three Crosses shown shaded on Map 29—

- (a) are transferred to the community of Dunvant;
- (b) form part of the electoral ward of Dunvant and Killay.
