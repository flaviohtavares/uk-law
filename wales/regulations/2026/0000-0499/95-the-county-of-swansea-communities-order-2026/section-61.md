> Text as at 2026-07-31.

**61.**[^key-86cea1b1c44ff6c1239cc5ad1e4819fe] In the community of Pennard, the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Kittle ward is 2;
- (b) Southgate ward is 5;
- (c) Ilston ward is 2.
