# The County of Swansea (Communities) Order 2026, section 101

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**101.**[^key-eb9c00883dfe2862568797631d8a4f63] The part of the Mayals ward of the community of Mumbles shown shaded on Map 48—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Mumbles;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of West Cross;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Mumbles.
