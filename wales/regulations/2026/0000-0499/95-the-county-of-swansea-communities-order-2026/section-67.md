# The County of Swansea (Communities) Order 2026, section 67

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**67.**[^key-9a3eadd760cba2daa873c6d52d1ddfd6] The part of the Kingsbridge ward of the community of Llwchwr shown shaded on Map 27—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Gowerton;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Gowerton West;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Gowerton.
