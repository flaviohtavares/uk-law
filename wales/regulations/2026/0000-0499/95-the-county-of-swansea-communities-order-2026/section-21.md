## 21. Llanrhidian Lower, and Llanrhidian Higher — changes in community and community ward boundaries[^key-7b64fb740febfafb14fdb9dd626a9eec]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the community of Llanrhidian Lower shaded on Map 14—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Llanrhidian Higher;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Llanmorlais;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Gower.
