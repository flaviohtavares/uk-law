# The County of Swansea (Communities) Order 2026, section 66

> Text as at 2026-07-31.

> Extent: England, Wales.

**66.**[^c-76ac1d4865f039642b925d4bbebafb43] The part of the Garden Village ward of the community of Llwchwr shown shaded on Map 26—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Cockett;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Cockett.
