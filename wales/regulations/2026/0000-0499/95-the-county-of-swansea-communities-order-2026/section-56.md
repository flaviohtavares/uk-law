> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 56. Creation of the new community of Pennard[^key-d64222aea9d2f12c5b3456b3a294a5c1]

*Extent: E+W*

A new community is created which is to be known as Pennard in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) is shown shaded on Map 20;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Pennard.
