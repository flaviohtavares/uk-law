> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 63. Llwchwr, Gorseinon, Grovesend and Waungron, Gowerton, and Cockett — changes in community and community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^key-99012794d7c051e251c80195635192e9]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Upper Loughor ward of the community of Llwchwr shown shaded on Map 23—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Gorseinon;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Gorseinon West;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Gorseinon.
