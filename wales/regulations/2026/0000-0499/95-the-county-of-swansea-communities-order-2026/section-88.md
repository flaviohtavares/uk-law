> Text as at 2026-07-31.

## 88. Gowerton, Llanrhidian Higher, and Three Crosses — changes in community and community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^key-2e4ee494bca74c0f203e0d7ec5305810]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Penclawdd ward of the community of Llanrhidian Higher shown shaded on Map 40—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Three Crosses;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Fairwood.
