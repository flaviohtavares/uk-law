# The County of Swansea (Communities) Order 2026, section 68

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**68.**[^c-f9ef5058a7a846da4d2e5f514ede26d0] The part of Upper Loughor[^c-330955adb2d20864f69eb01e9d2d9bd2] ward of the community of Llwchwr shown shaded on Map 28—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Llwchwr;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Kingsbridge;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llwchwr.
