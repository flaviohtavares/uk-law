# The County of Swansea (Communities) Order 2026, section 64

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**64.**[^c-e82c4175bae88f02aa19e1099105bd27] The part of the Garden Village ward of the community of Llwchwr shown shaded on Map 24—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Gorseinon;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Gorseinon East;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Gorseinon.
