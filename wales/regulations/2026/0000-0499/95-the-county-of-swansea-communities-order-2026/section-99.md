## 99. Mumbles — changes in community and community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^c-390c3a1c409e7de9cb933ba9c7e34490]

*Extent: E+W*

The parts of the West Cross ward of the community of Mumbles shown shaded on Map 46—

- (a) are transferred to community of Mumbles;
- (b) form part of the community ward of Newton;
- (c) form part of the electoral ward of Mumbles.
