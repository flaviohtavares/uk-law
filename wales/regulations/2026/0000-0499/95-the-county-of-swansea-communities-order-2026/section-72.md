## 72. Community of Llwchwr — changes to electoral arrangements[^key-14ccefc90cd1ea2f9afb7f18590d9bc6]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Llwchwr, the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Garden Village ward is 2;
- (b) Kingsbridge ward is 5;
- (c) Lower Loughor ward is 3;
- (d) Upper Loughor ward is 4.
