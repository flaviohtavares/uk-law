## 102. Townhill and Uplands — changes in community and community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^c-b2673080563385324c438f3d2efb6f32]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the community of Uplands shown shaded on Map 49—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Townhill;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Townhill.
