# The County of Swansea (Communities) Order 2026, section 28

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Creation of the new community ward of Llangennith[^c-0f56a883584aef14bff4839c1a94d3ae][^c-b812662cf5536f345a4c20bd11b73191]

*Extent: E+W*

A new community ward is created in the community of Gower North which is to be known as Llangennith[^c-1a160df53076c5527498972970fc65c6] in the English language and Llangynydd in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 2 on Map 16;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Gower.
