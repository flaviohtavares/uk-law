## 93. Community of Llanrhidian Higher — changes to electoral arrangements[^key-5ba76248295aedd2934914359f50b993]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Llanrhidian Higher, the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Llanmorlais ward is 3;
- (b) Pen-clawdd ward is 6.
