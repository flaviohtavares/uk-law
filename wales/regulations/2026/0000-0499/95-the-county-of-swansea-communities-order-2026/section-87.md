## 87. Community of Penlle’r-gaer — changes to electoral arrangements[^c-7567a95bae28f592205d4c10d221e66b]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Penlle’r-gaer, the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Penlle’r-gaer East ward is 6;
- (b) Penlle’r-gaer West ward is 3.
