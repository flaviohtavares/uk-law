## 41. Creation of the new community of Gower South[^key-8d23eda5f5bc62b5563e30fc55cea167]

*Extent: E+W*

A new community is created which is to be known as Gower South in the English language and De Gŵyr in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 17;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Gower.
