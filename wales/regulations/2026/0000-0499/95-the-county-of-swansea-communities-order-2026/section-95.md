## 95. Landore, Morriston, and Mynyddbach — changes in community and community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^c-92d5ba3d5fca9e258022cd7152df96d0]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the community of Landore shown shaded on Map 43—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Morriston;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Morriston.
