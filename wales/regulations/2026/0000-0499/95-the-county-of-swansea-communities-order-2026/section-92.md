## 92. Community of Gowerton — changes to electoral arrangements[^c-fb575dd427752b94e303fca6be80bac8]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Gowerton, the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Gowerton East ward is 5;
- (b) Gowerton West ward is 5.
