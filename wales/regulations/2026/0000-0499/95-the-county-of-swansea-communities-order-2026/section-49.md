# The County of Swansea (Communities) Order 2026, section 49

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**49.**[^c-1a140605be0dd6fe1f6fa2bc7595843d] In the community of Gower South, the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Horton ward is 1;
- (b) Penrice ward is 2;
- (c) Port Eynon ward is 2;
- (d) Knelston ward is 2;
- (e) Reynoldston ward is 2;
- (f) Rhosili ward is 2.
