## 79. Bonymaen, Llansamlet, and St Thomas — changes in community and community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^key-e58cf0953b3c775aa3e5d71b6591ec36]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the community of Bonymaen shown shaded on Map 33—

- (a) is transferred to the community of St Thomas;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of St Thomas.
