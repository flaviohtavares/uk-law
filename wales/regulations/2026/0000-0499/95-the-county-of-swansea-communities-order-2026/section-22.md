> Text as at 2026-07-31.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22. Llangennith, Llanmadoc and Cheriton, and Llanrhidian Lower — abolition of community and community councils[^key-dfa245cce6250fa77896f38bbd5acf02]

*Extent: E+W*

The community of Llangennith[^key-bc7f8c8230cef95ad915c0aa17d71b6a], Llanmadoc[^key-20073a84bc449d686baf52811eaa94e2] and Cheriton is abolished.
