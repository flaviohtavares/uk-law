## 29. Creation of the new community ward of Llanmadog[^c-af8a38449f6c541747e47770aed59d1f]

*Extent: E+W*

A new community ward is created in the community of Gower North which is to be known as Llanmadog in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 3 on Map 16;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Gower.
