# The County of Swansea (Communities) Order 2026, section 100

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**100.**[^c-45c517a67998e2f789213229292b9e39] The part of the Newton ward of the community of Mumbles shown shaded on Map 47—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Mumbles;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Oystermouth;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Mumbles.
