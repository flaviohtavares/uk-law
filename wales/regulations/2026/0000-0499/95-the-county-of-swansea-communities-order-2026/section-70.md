## 70. Community of Gorseinon — changes to electoral arrangements[^key-55b992522d311a8d9084e881adc27071]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Gorseinon, the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Gorseinon Central ward is 2;
- (b) Gorseinon East ward is 4;
- (c) Gorseinon West ward is 3;
- (d) Penyrheol ward is 4.
