## 62. Three Crosses, and Pennard — changes in community and community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^key-a399d3854dc5e3847ed838ed6b0ca8a6]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the community of Three Crosses shown shaded on Map 22—

- (a) is transferred to the new community of Pennard;
- (b) forms part of the new community ward of Ilston;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Pennard.
