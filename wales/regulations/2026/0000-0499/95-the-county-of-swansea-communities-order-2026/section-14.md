## 14. Pontarddulais — consequential changes to community electoral arrangements[^c-3ba5774ed66e50108b0e42b50fff2fd7]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Pontarddulais, the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Pentre-bach and Garn-swllt ward is 2;
- (b) Dulais East ward is 2;
- (c) Dulais West ward is 3;
- (d) Cwm Dulais ward is 5.
