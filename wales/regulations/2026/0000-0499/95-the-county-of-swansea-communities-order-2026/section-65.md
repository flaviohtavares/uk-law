# The County of Swansea (Communities) Order 2026, section 65

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**65.**[^c-c7a811810160e91f0b0c35ba79bb35fb] The part of the Kingsbridge ward of the community of Llwchwr shown shaded on Map 25—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Gorseinon;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Gorseinon East;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Gorseinon.
