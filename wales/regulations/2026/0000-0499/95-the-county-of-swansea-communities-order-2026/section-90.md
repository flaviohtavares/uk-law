# The County of Swansea (Communities) Order 2026, section 90

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**90.**[^key-c69abf6ef7208e991a88391ad050f8c2] The part of the community of Three Crosses shown shaded on Map 42—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Llanrhidian Higher;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Penclawdd;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Pen-clawdd.
