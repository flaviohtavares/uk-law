## 57. Creation of the new community ward of Kittle[^c-d5e97def372e3110aaa7adf4cf73f04a]

*Extent: E+W*

A new community ward is created in the community of Pennard which is to be known as Kittle in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 1 on Map 21;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Pennard.
