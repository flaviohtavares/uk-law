# The County of Swansea (Communities) Order 2026, section 89

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**89.**[^c-3f94cdf86817b031e2c7f51337e6f0b8] The part of the Gowerton West ward of the community of Gowerton shown shaded on Map 41—

- (a) is transferred to community of Llanrhidian Higher;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Penclawdd;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Pen-clawdd.
