## 26. Creation of the new community of Gower North[^key-bfa52d5768a55019a967919c1f6f2cce]

*Extent: E+W*

A new community is created which is to be known as Gower North in the English language and Gogledd Gŵyr in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 15;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Gower.
