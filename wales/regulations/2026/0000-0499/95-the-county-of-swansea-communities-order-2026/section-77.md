## 77. Killay – change to electoral arrangements[^c-6787ff30c9d9175d83ea743f3e96554d]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Killay, the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Killay North ward is 5;
- (b) Killay South ward is 5.
