## 58. Creation of the new community ward of Southgate[^key-57883810a62f9f1ad57b9f7cb528e790]

*Extent: E+W*

A new community ward is created in the community of Pennard which is to be known as Southgate in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 2 on Map 21;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Pennard.
