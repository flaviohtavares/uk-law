## 81. Cockett, Sketty, and Waunarlwydd — changes in community and community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^c-859cfdcd33daf524d759e1929a151c1e]

*Extent: E+W*

The parts of the community of Sketty shown shaded on Map 35—

- (a) are transferred to the community of Cockett;
- (b) form part of the electoral ward of Cockett.
