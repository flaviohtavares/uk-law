## 27. Creation of the new community ward of Cheriton[^c-3aad598fc7e35e35ddaa3b4a56414502]

*Extent: E+W*

A new community ward is created in the community of Gower North which is to be known as Cheriton in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 1 on Map 16;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Gower.
