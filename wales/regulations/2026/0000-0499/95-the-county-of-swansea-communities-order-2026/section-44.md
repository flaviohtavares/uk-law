## 44. Creation of the new community ward of Port Eynon[^key-52d6dd385cb3514d491f807f3422cee8]

*Extent: E+W*

A new community ward is created in the community of Gower South which is to be known as Port Eynon in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 3 on Map 18;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Gower.
