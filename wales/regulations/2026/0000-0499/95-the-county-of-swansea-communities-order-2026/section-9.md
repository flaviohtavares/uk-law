## 9. Consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^key-3f217ea280d72d7d2d90f4449359252f]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Clydach, the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Craig-cefn-parc ward is 2;
- (b) Clydach ward is 4;
- (c) Graigfelen ward is 3;
- (d) Vardre ward is 4.
