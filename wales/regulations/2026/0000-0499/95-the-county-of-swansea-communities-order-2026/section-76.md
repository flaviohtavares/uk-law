# The County of Swansea (Communities) Order 2026, section 76

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**76.**[^key-27475e92a90682585138d424cc626992] The part of the Killay South ward of the community of Killay shown shaded on Map 32—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Dunvant;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Dunvant and Killay.
