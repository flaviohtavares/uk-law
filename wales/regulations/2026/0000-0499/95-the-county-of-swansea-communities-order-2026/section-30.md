## 30. Creation of the new community ward of Llanrhidian Lower[^c-ed1accff05335dbe39cc835250872aea]

*Extent: E+W*

A new community ward is created in the community of Gower North which is to be known as Llanrhidian Lower in the English language and Llanrhidian Isaf in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 4 on Map 16;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Gower.
