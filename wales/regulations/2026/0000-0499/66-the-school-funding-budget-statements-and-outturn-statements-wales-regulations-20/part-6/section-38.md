# The School Funding, Budget Statements and Outturn Statements (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 38

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 38. Timing of publication of outturn statements[^c-1ee31aa0694a78cd3467ed06fe7011ca]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

An outturn statement must be published before 31 July following the end of the funding period to which it relates.
