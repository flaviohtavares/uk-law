# The School Funding, Budget Statements and Outturn Statements (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 35

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Form of outturn statements[^key-ffa5a93adf8d47e39ec1e7dacce6586b]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) An outturn statement must be prepared in both English and Welsh.

(2) An outturn statement must include—

- (a) a heading at the top of the first page indicating that it is an outturn statement,
- (b) the name of the local authority that prepared the statement,
- (c) the funding period to which it relates, and
- (d) the local authority’s unitary reference number.
