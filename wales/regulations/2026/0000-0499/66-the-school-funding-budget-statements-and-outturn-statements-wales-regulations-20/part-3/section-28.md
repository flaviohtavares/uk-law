# The School Funding, Budget Statements and Outturn Statements (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 28

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Arrangements approved by the Welsh Ministers[^key-a83e3d699117d83fd8e76f4517bdb0db]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

The Welsh Ministers may, where it appears to them to be expedient to do so, authorise a local authority to determine or redetermine a school’s budget share—

- (a) at any time,
- (b) to such extent as they may specify, and
- (c) in accordance with arrangements approved by them in place of the arrangements provided for elsewhere in these Regulations.
