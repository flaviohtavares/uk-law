# The School Funding, Budget Statements and Outturn Statements (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 7

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Individual schools budget[^c-5e3fc7d04ed5c9a462a1562c0bfb918d]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) A local authority must on or before 14 February immediately preceding the beginning of a funding period, deduct from its schools budget any or all of the classes or descriptions of planned expenditure set out in Schedule 3, in whole or in part, as it proposes to deduct in order to arrive at its individual schools budget for that funding period.

(2) The expenditure referred to in paragraph (1) includes expenditure on associated administrative costs and associated overheads.
