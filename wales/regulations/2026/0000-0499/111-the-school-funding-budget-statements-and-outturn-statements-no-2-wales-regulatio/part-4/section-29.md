# The School Funding, Budget Statements and Outturn Statements (No. 2) (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Required content of schemes

A financial scheme must deal with the matters connected with the financing of schools maintained by it set out in Schedule 4.
