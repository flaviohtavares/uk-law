# The School Funding, Budget Statements and Outturn Statements (No. 2) (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Title, coming into force and application

(1) The title of these Regulations is the School Funding, Budget Statements and Outturn Statements (No. 2) (Wales) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 31 January 2027.

(3) These Regulations apply in relation to financial years beginning on or after 1 April 2027.

(4) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.
