# The School Funding, Budget Statements and Outturn Statements (No. 2) (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Timing of publication of outturn statements

An outturn statement must be published before 31 July following the end of the funding period to which it relates.
