# The School Funding, Budget Statements and Outturn Statements (No. 2) (Wales) Regulations 2026, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Pupil numbers: dual registration

(1) Where a pupil is a dual registered pupil then that pupil must be treated as being a full-time pupil at each maintained school unless the local authority expressly provides otherwise in its formula.

(2) In this regulation “**a dual registered pupil**” means a pupil registered at more than one maintained school in accordance with regulations made under section 434 of the 1996 Act[^f00044].
