# The Environment (Principles, Governance and Biodiversity Targets) (Wales) Act 2026 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 119 (C. 11)

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION, WALES

The Environment (Principles, Governance and Biodiversity Targets) (Wales) Act 2026 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2026

Made

30 September 2026

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred on them by section 46(4) of the Environment (Principles, Governance and Biodiversity Targets) (Wales) Act 2026[^f00001].

## 1. Title

The title of this Order is the Environment (Principles, Governance and Biodiversity Targets) (Wales) Act 2026 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2026.

## 2. Provision coming into force on 5 October 2026

Section 9 (independence of the OEGW) of the Environment (Principles, Governance and Biodiversity Targets) (Wales) Act 2026 comes into force on 5 October 2026.

Llyr Gruffydd

Cabinet Minister for Rural Resilience and Sustainability, one of the Welsh Ministers

30 September 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the first commencement order made by the Welsh Ministers under the Environment (Principles, Governance and Biodiversity Targets) (Wales) Act 2026 (“**the Act**”).

This Order brings section 9 of the Act into force on 5 October 2026. Section 9 requires the Welsh Ministers to have regard to the need to protect the independence of the Office of Environmental Governance Wales (“the OEGW”) when exercising their functions in respect of the OEGW.
