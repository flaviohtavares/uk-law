# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 108

EDUCATION, WALES

The Education (Student Support) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2026

Made

23 June 2026

Coming into force

31 July 2026

The Welsh Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by sections 22(1)(a) and (2)(c) and 42(6) of the Teaching and Higher Education Act 1998[^f00001], and now exercisable by them[^f00002].

## 1. Title and coming into force

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Education (Student Support) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2026.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 31 July 2026.

## 2. Application

These Regulations apply in relation to the provision of support to a student in relation to an academic year which begins on or after 1 August 2026.

## 3. Amendment of the Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018

The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018[^f00003] are amended in accordance with regulations 4 to 6.

## 4. Amendment to regulation 8

After regulation 8(1) (designation of other courses) insert—

> (1A) When specifying a course under paragraph (1), the Welsh Ministers may specify that the course is to be treated as being provided by an ordinary provider for the purposes of regulation 40(2)(c)(i).

## 5. Amendments to regulation 40

In regulation 40 (amount of tuition fee loan)—

- (a) in paragraph (2), at the beginning insert “Subject to paragraph (4),”;
- (b) for paragraph (2)(c)(i) substitute—

  > - (i) “**ordinary provider**” means a provider—
  >   - (aa) falling within Condition 4 of regulation 6(1),
  >   - (bb) specified in column 2 of Table 14A in Schedule 1A, in so far as they provide the course described in the corresponding entry in column 3, or
  >   - (cc) treated as such by virtue of a designation made by the Welsh Ministers under regulation 8;

  ;
- (c) in paragraph (2)(c)(ii), at the end insert “, where the institution is not treated as an ordinary provider within the meaning of paragraph (i) under the terms of that designation”;
- (d) after paragraph (3) insert—

  > (4) The maximum loan amount to which a protected student is entitled is calculated as if their course were provided by an ordinary provider within the meaning of paragraph (2)(c)(i).
  >
  > (5) In paragraph (4), “**protected student**” means a student undertaking a course that satisfies the requirements set out in Schedule 2A.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018 (“**the 2018 Regulations**”).

The 2018 Regulations provide for financial support for eligible students taking designated courses of higher education which begin on or after 1 August 2018.

These Regulations amend—

- (a) the power to designate courses under regulation 8 of the 2018 Regulations, so that the Welsh Ministers may specify that such a course is to be treated as being provided by an ordinary provider for the purposes of calculating the maximum amount of tuition fee loan under regulation 40 of the 2018 Regulations, and
- (b) regulation 40 of, and insert a new Schedule 2A into, the 2018 Regulations to ensure students on continuing courses listed in that Schedule are entitled to the fee loan applicable to an ordinary provider.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to these Regulations. As a result, a regulatory impact assessment has been prepared as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with these Regulations. A copy can be obtained from the Strategy and Funding Division, Welsh Government, Cathays Park, Cardiff, CF10 3NQ.
