# The Bovine Viral Diarrhoea (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 110

ANIMALS, WALES

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Bovine Viral Diarrhoea (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2026

Made

24 June 2026

Coming into force

1 July 2026

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1 and 8(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001].

## 1. Title and coming into force

(1) The title of this Order is the Bovine Viral Diarrhoea (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2026.

(2) This Order comes into force on 1 July 2026.

## 2. Amendment of the Bovine Viral Diarrhoea (Wales) Order 2024

(1) The Bovine Viral Diarrhoea (Wales) Order 2024[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 40(2) (Pre-movement testing), before sub-paragraph (a) insert—

> - (za) for animals that (at the point a sample is taken) are aged 20 days or less, take an ear tag sample of tissue in accordance with article 13, or

.

(3) In article 41(2) (Post-movement testing of animals without a known individual BVD status from herds outside of Wales), before sub-paragraph (a) insert—

> - (za) take an ear tag sample of tissue in accordance with article 13, or

.

Llyr Gruffydd

Cabinet Minister for Rural Resilience and Sustainability, one of the Welsh Ministers

24 June 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Bovine Viral Diarrhoea (Wales) Order 2024 ([S.I. 2024/753 (W. 105)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2024/753)) (“**the 2024 Order**”).

Article 2(2) amends article 40(2) of the 2024 Order allowing keepers themselves to take ear tag samples of tissue (in accordance with article 13 of the 2024 Order) from calves that are aged 20 days or less at the point the sample is taken, for the purpose of pre-movement testing.

Article 2(3) amends article 41(2) of the 2024 Order allowing keepers themselves to take ear tag samples of tissue (in accordance with article 13 of the 2024 Order) from animals for the purpose of post-movement testing.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to this Order. As a result, it was not considered necessary to carry out a regulatory impact assessment as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with this Order.
