# The Legislation (Wales) Act 2019 (Amendment of Schedule 1) Regulations 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 143

INTERPRETATION OF LEGISLATION, WALES

The Legislation (Wales) Act 2019 (Amendment of Schedule 1) Regulations 2026

Made

25 November 2026

Coming into force

1 December 2026

The Welsh Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by [section 6](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2019/4/section/6)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2019/4/section/6) and [(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2019/4/section/6) of the [Legislation (Wales) Act 2019](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2019/4/contents)[^f00001].

In accordance with the Senedd approval procedure applied by section 43(2) of that Act, a draft of this Welsh statutory instrument was laid before, and approved by resolution of, Senedd Cymru.

## 1. Title and coming into force

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Legislation (Wales) Act 2019 (Amendment of Schedule 1) Regulations 2026.

(2) The Regulations come into force on 1 December 2026.

## 2. Amendments to [Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2019/4/schedule/1) to the [Legislation (Wales) Act 2019](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2019/4/contents)

(1) The Table in [Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2019/4/schedule/1) to the [Legislation (Wales) Act 2019](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2019/4/contents) (definitions of words and expressions appearing in Acts of Senedd Cymru and Welsh subordinate instruments) is amended as follows.

(2) Insert the following entry in the appropriate place in alphabetical order—

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| “general limit in a magistrates’ court (*terfyn cyffredinol mewn llys ynadon*) | “**general limit in a magistrates’ court**”, in relation to a term of imprisonment in respect of an offence triable either way under the law of England and Wales, means the limit laid down in respect of the offence by section 224(1) of the Sentencing Code (as it has effect from time to time)”. |

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend [Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2019/4/schedule/1) to the [Legislation (Wales) Act 2019](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2019/4/contents), which makes provision about the meaning of certain words and expressions when they appear in Acts of Senedd Cymru and Welsh subordinate instruments. The words and expressions are to be interpreted in accordance with Schedule 1 except so far as express provision is made to the contrary or the context requires otherwise.

The Regulations insert provisions into Schedule 1 about the meaning of “general limit in a magistrates’ court”.

They also amend the [Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2023/3/contents), the [Building Safety (Wales) Act 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2026/5/contents), and the [Planning (Wales) Act 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2026/6/contents) as a consequence of the insertion of the defined term “general limit in a magistrates’ court” in [Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2019/4/schedule/1) to the [Legislation (Wales) Act 2019](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2019/4/contents).

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to these Regulations and it was not considered necessary to carry out a regulatory impact assessment as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with these Regulations.
