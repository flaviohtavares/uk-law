# The Health and Social Care (Wales) Act 2025 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2026

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2026 No. 130 (C. 13)

SOCIAL CARE, WALES

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS, WALES

The Health and Social Care (Wales) Act 2025 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2026

Made

22 October 2026

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power conferred by section 29(2) of the Health and Social Care (Wales) Act 2025[^f00001].

## 1. Title

The title of this Order is the Health and Social Care (Wales) Act 2025 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2026.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 30 October 2026

Sub-paragraphs (2), (3), (4) and (5) of paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 to the Health and Social Care (Wales) Act 2025, and section 22 of that Act insofar as it relates to those sub-paragraphs, come into force on 30 October 2026.

Delyth Jewell

Deputy Minister for Social Care, Mental Health and Women’s Health, under the authority of the Cabinet Minister for Health and Care, one of the Welsh Ministers

22 October 2026

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This is the second commencement order made by the Welsh Ministers under the Health and Social Care (Wales) Act [2025 (asc 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2025/1) (“**the 2025 Act**”).

Article 2 brings into force, on 30 October 2026, the provisions specified in that article. Those provisions make minor and consequential amendments to the Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act [2016 (anaw 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/2) in respect of provisions in the 2025 Act which relate to the restrictions on profit in the provision of social care services to children.
