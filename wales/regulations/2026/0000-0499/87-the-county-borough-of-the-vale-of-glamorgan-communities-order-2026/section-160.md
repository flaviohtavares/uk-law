# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 160

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 160. Creation of the new community ward of Monknash

A new community ward is created in the community of St Donats and Wick which is to be known as Monknash in the English language and Yr As Fawr in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 2 on Map 34;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of St Bride’s Major.
