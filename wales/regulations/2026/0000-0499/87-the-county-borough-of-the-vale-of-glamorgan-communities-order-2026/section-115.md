# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 115

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 115. Llandow, Llanfair, and Llanmaes — abolition of the communities of Llandow, Llanfair, and Llanmaes, and consequential changes to community councils

The community of Llandow is abolished.

**116.** The community council of Llandow is dissolved.

**117.** The community of Llanfair is abolished.

**118.** The community council of Llanfair is dissolved.

**119.** The community of Llanmaes as changed by this Order is abolished.

**120.** The community council of Llanmaes is dissolved.
