# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 50

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 50. Dinas Powys — Cross Common, Eastbrook, Murch, and Twyn, changes in community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the Cross Common ward of the community of Dinas Powys shown shaded and numbered 1 on Map 16—

- (a) is transferred to the community ward of Murch of the community of Dinas Powys;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Dinas Powys.

**51.** The part of the Cross Common ward of the community of Dinas Powys shown shaded on Map 17—

- (a) is transferred to the community ward of Twyn of the community of Dinas Powys;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Dinas Powys.

**52.** The part of the Twyn ward of the community of Dinas Powys shown shaded and numbered 2 on Map 16—

- (a) is transferred to the community ward of Cross Common of the community of Dinas Powys;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Dinas Powys.

**53.** The part of the Michaelston ward of the community of Michaelston-le-Pit and Leckwith shown shaded on Map 18—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Dinas Powys;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Eastbrook;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Dinas Powys.
