# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 94

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 94. Peterston-super-Ely, and St Georges-super-Ely —abolition of the communities of Peterston-super-Ely and St Georges-super-Ely and consequential changes to community councils

The community of Peterson-super-Ely as changed by this Order is abolished.

**95.** The community council of Peterston-super-Ely is dissolved.

**96.** The community of St Georges-super-Ely as changed by this Order is abolished.

**97.** The community council of St Georges-super-Ely is dissolved.
