# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 139

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 139. Creation of the new community ward of Llangan

A new community ward is created in the community of Colwinston and Llangan which is to be known as Llangan in the English language and Llanganna in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 3 on Map 32;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llandow.
