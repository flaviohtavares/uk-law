# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 67

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 67. Llandough — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Llandough comprises the community of Llandough and Cwrtyrala.

**68.** In the electoral ward of Llandough, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
