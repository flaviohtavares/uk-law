# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 126

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 126. Creation of the new community ward of Llanfair

A new community ward is created in the community of Llandow, Llanfair and Llanmaes which is to be known as Llanfair in the English language and Llan-fair in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 5 on Map 31;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llandow.
