# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. Penllyn — community ward electoral arrangements

In the community of Penllyn, the number of councillors to be elected for—

- (a) the Llansannor ward is 2;
- (b) the Penllyn ward is 3;
- (c) the Ystradowen ward is 4.
