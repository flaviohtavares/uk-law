# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 100

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 100. Creation of the new community ward of St Brides-super-Ely

A new community ward is created in the community of Peterston and St Georges which is to be known as St Brides-super-Ely in the English language and Llansanffraid-ar-Elái in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 2 on Map 29;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Peterston-super-Ely.
