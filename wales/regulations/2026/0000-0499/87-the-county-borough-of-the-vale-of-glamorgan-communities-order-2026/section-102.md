# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 102

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 102. Peterston and St Georges — establishment of community council and community ward electoral arrangements

A council for the community of Peterson and St Georges is established.

**103.** In the community of Peterston and St Georges, the number of councillors to be elected for—

- (a) the Peterston-super-Ely ward is 4;
- (b) the St Brides-super-Ely ward is 1;
- (c) the St Georges-super-Ely ward is 2.
