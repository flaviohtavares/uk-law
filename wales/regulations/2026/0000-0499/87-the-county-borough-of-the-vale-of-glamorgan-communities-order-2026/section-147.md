# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 147

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 147. Creation of the new community ward of Ewenny

A new community ward is created in the community of Ewenny and St Bride’s Major which is to be known as Ewenny in the English language and Ewenni in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 1 on Map 33;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of St Bride’s Major.
