# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 41

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 41. Creation of the new community ward of Llanblethian

A new community ward is created in the community of Cowbridge with Llanblethian which is to be known as Llanblethian in the English language and Llanfleiddan in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 12;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Cowbridge.

**42.** In the community of Cowbridge with Llanblethian, the number of councillors to be elected for—

- (a) the Aberthin ward is 2;
- (b) the Cowbridge Central ward is 6;
- (c) the Cowbridge North ward is 4;
- (d) the Llanblethian ward is 3.
