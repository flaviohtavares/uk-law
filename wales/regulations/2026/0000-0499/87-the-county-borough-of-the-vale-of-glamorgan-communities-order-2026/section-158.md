# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 158

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 158. Creation of the new community of St Donats and Wick

A new community is created which is to be known as St Donats and Wick in the English language and Sain Dunwyd a’r Wig in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 34;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of St Bride’s Major.
