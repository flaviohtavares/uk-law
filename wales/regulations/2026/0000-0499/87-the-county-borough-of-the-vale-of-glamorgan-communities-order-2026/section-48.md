# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 48

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 48. Llansannor — change of community ward name

The Llansannor ward of the community of Penllyn as changed by this Order retains the English language name of Llansannor and is to be known as Llansanwyr in the Welsh language.
