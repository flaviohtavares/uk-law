# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 23

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 23. Creation of the new community ward of Cosmeston

A new community ward is created in the community of Penarth which is to be known as Cosmeston in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the areas shown shaded and numbered 1 and 2 on Map 6;
- (b) forms the new electoral ward of Cosmeston.
