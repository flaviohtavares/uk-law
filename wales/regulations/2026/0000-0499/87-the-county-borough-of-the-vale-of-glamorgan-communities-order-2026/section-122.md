# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 122

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 122. Creation of the new community ward of Llandow

A new community ward is created in the community of Llandow, Llanfair and Llanmaes which is to be known as Llandow in the English language and Llandŵ in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 1 on Map 31;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llandow.
