# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 85

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 85. Creation of the new community ward of Dyffryn

A new community ward is created in the community of St Nicholas and Bonvilston which is to be known as Dyffryn in the English language and the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 28;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of St Nicholas and Llancarfan.
