# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 43

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 43. Cowbridge — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Cowbridge comprises the community of Cowbridge with Llanblethian and the community of Penllyn.

**44.** In the electoral ward of Cowbridge, the number of councillors to be elected is 3.
