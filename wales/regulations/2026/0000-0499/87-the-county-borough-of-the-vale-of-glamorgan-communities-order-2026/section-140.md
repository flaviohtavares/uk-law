# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 140

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 140. Colwinston and Llangan — establishment of community council and community ward electoral arrangements

A council for the community of Colwinston and Llangan is established.

**141.** In the community of Colwinston and Llangan, the number of councillors to be elected for—

- (a) the Colwinston ward is 3;
- (b) the Llangan ward is 3;
- (c) the St Mary Hill ward is 1.
