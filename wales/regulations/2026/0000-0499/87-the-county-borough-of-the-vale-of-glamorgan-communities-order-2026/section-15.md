# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Waterfront — electoral ward arrangements

A new electoral ward is created which is to be known as Waterfront in the English language and Glannau’r Barri in the Welsh language and which comprises the area of the community ward of Waterfront.

**16.** In the electoral ward of Waterfront, the number of councillors to be elected is 2.
