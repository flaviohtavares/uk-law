# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 88

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 88. St Nicholas and Bonvilston — community ward electoral arrangements

In the community of St Nicholas and Bonvilston, the number of councillors to be elected for—

- (a) the Bonvilston ward is 4;
- (b) the St Nicholas ward is 6;
- (c) the Dyffryn ward is 1.
