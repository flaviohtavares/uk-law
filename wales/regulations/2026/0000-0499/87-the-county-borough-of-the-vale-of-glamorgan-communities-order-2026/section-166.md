# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 166

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 166. New community councils

The proper officer of the Vale of Glamorgan County Borough Council must convene the first meeting of each of the new community councils established by articles 65, 102, 113, 128, 140, 150 and 163.
