# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 113

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 113. Pendoylan and Welsh St Donats — establishment of community council and community ward electoral arrangements

A council for the community of Pendoylan and Welsh St Donats is established.

**114.** In the community of Pendoylan and Welsh St Donats, the number of councillors to be elected for—

- (a) the Pendoylan ward is 4;
- (b) the Welsh St Donats ward is 4.
