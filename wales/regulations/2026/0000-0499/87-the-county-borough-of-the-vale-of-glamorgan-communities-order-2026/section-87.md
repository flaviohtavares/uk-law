# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 87

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 87. Llancarfan — community ward electoral arrangements

In the community of Llancarfan, the number of councillors to be elected for—

- (a) the Llancarfan ward is 5;
- (b) the Llantrithyd ward is 2.
