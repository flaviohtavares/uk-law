# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 148

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 148. Creation of the new community ward of Ogmore-by-Sea

A new community ward is created in the community of Ewenny and St Bride’s Major which is to be known as Ogmore-by-Sea in the English language and Aberogwr in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 2 on Map 33;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of St Bride’s Major.
