# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 154

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 154. St Donats and Wick — abolition of the communities of St Donats and Wick and consequential changes to community councils

The community of St Donats is abolished.

**155.** The community council of St Donats is dissolved.

**156.** The community of Wick is abolished.

**157.** The community council of Wick is dissolved.
