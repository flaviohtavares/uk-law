# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Llantwit Major — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Llantwit Major comprises the community of Llantwit Major.

**32.** In the electoral ward of Llantwit Major, the number of councillors to be elected is 4.
