# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 90

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 90. St Nicholas and Llancarfan — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of St Nicholas and Llancarfan comprises the communities of Llancarfan, and St Nicholas and Bonvilston.

**91.** In the electoral ward of St Nicholas and Llancarfan, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
