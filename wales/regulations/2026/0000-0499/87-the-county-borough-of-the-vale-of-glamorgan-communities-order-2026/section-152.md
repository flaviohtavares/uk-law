# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 152

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 152. St Bride’s Major — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of St Bride’s Major comprises the new community of Ewenny and St Bride’s Major and the new community of St Donats and Wick.

**153.** In the electoral ward of St Bride’s Major, the number of councillors to be elected is 2.
