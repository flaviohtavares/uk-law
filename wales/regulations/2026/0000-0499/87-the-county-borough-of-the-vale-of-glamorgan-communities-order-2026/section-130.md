# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 130

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 130. Llandow — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Llandow comprises the new community of Colwinston and Llangan and the new community of Llandow, Llanfair and Llanmaes.

**131.** In the electoral ward of Llandow, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
