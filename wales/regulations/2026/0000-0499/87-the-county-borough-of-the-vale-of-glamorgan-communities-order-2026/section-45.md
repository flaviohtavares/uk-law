# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Penllyn — Llansannor, and Ystradowen, changes in community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements

The part of the Penllyn ward of the community of Penllyn shown shaded on Map 13—

- (a) is transferred to the community ward of Llansannor of the community of Penllyn;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Cowbridge.

**46.** The part of the Ystradowen ward of the community of Penllyn shown shaded on Map 14—

- (a) is transferred to the community ward of Llansannor of the community of Penllyn;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Cowbridge.

**47.** The part of the Welsh St Donats ward of the community of Welsh St Donats shown shaded on Map 15—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Penllyn;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Ystradowen;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Cowbridge.
