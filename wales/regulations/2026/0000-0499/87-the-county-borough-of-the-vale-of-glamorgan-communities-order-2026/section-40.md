# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Creation of the new community ward of Cowbridge Central

A new community ward is created in the community of Cowbridge with Llanblethian which is to be known as Cowbridge Central in the English language and Canol y Bont-faen in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the areas shown shaded and numbered 1 and 2 on Map 10;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Cowbridge.
