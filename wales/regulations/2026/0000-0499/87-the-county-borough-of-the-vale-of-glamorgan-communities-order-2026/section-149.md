# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 149

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 149. Creation of the new community ward of St Bride’s Major

A new community ward is created in the community of Ewenny and St Bride’s Major which is to be known as St Bride’s Major in the English language and Saint-y-brid in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 3 on Map 33;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of St Bride’s Major.
