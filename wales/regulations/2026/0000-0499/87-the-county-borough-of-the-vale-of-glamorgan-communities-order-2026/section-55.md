# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 55

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 55. Dinas Powys — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Dinas Powys comprises the community of Dinas Powys.

**56.** In the electoral ward of Dinas Powys, the number of councillors to be elected is 4.
