# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 106

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 106. Pendoylan and Welsh St Donats — abolition of the communities of Pendoylan and Welsh St Donats and consequential changes to community councils

The community of Pendoylan as changed by this Order is abolished.

**107.** The community council of Pendoylan is dissolved.

**108.** The community of Welsh St Donats as changed by this Order is abolished.

**109.** The community council of Welsh St Donats is dissolved.
