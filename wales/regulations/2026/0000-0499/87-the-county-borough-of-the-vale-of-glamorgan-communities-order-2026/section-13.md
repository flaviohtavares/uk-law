# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Creation of the new community ward of Waterfront

A new community ward is created in the community of Barry which is to be known as Waterfront in the English language and Glannau’r Barri in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the areas shown shaded and numbered 2, 3 and 4 on Map 1;
- (b) forms the new electoral ward of Waterfront.
