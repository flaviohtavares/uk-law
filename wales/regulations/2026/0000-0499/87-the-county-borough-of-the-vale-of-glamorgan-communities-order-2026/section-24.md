# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Penarth — community ward electoral arrangements

In the community of Penarth, the number of councillors to be elected for—

- (a) the Cornerswell ward is 4;
- (b) the Cosmeston ward is 1;
- (c) the Plymouth ward is 4;
- (d) the St Augustine’s ward is 5;
- (e) the Stanwell ward is 3.
