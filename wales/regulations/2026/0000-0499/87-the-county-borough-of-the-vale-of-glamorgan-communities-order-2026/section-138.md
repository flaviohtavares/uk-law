# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 138

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 138. Creation of the new community ward of St Mary Hill

A new community ward is created in the community of Colwinston and Llangan which is to be known as St Mary Hill in the English language and Eglwys Fair y Mynydd in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 2 on Map 32;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llandow.
