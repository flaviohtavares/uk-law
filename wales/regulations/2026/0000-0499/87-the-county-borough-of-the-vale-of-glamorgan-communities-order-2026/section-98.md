# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 98

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 98. Creation of the new community of Peterston and St Georges

A new community is created which is to be known as Peterston and St Georges in the English language and Llanbedr-y-fro a Sain Siorys in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 29;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Peterston-super-Ely.
