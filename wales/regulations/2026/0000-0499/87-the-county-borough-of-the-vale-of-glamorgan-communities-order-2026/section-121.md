# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 121

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 121. Creation of the new community of Llandow, Llanfair and Llanmaes

A new community is created which is to be known as Llandow, Llanfair and Llanmaes in the English language and Llandŵ, Llan-fair a Llan-faes in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 31;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llandow.
