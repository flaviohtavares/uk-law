# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 142

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 142. Ewenny and St Bride’s Major — abolition of the communities of Ewenny and St Bride’s Major and consequential changes to community councils

The community of Ewenny is abolished.

**143.** The community council of Ewenny is dissolved.

**144.** The community of St Bride’s Major is abolished.

**145.** The community council of St Bride’s Major is dissolved.
