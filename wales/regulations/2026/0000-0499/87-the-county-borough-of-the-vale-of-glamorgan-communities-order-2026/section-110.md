# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 110

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 110. Creation of the new community of Pendoylan and Welsh St Donats

A new community is created which is to be known as Pendoylan and Welsh St Donats in the English language and Pendeulwyn a Llanddunwyd in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 30;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Peterston-super-Ely.
