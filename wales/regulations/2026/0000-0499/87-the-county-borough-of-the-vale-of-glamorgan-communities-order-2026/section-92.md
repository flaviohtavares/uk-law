# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 92

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 92. Wenvoe — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Wenvoe comprises the community of Wenvoe.

**93.** In the electoral ward of Wenvoe, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
