# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Cosmeston — electoral ward arrangements

A new electoral ward is created which is to be known as Cosmeston in the English language and the Welsh language and which comprises the area of the community ward of Cosmeston.

**26.** In the electoral ward of Cosmeston, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
