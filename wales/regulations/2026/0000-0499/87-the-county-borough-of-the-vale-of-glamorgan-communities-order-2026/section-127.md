# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 127

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 127. Creation of the new community ward of St Hilary

A new community ward is created in the community of Llandow, Llanfair and Llanmaes which is to be known as St Hilary in the English language and Saint Hilari in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 6 on Map 31;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llandow.
