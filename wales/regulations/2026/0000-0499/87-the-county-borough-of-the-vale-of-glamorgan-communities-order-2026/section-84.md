# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 84

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 84. Abolition of the community ward of St Lythans

The St Lythans ward of the community of Wenvoe as changed by this Order is abolished.
