# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 62

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 62. Creation of the new community ward of Llandough

A new community ward is created in the community of Llandough and Cwrtyrala which is to be known as Llandough in the English language and Llandochau in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 1 on Map 19;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llandough.
