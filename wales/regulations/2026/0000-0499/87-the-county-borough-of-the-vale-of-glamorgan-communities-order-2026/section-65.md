# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 65

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 65. Llandough and Cwrtyrala — establishment of community council and community electoral arrangements

A council for the community of Llandough and Cwrtyrala is established.

**66.** In the community of Llandough and Cwrtyrala, the number of councillors to be elected for—

- (a) the Llandough ward is 6;
- (b) the Leckwith ward is 1;
- (c) the Michaelston ward is 1.
