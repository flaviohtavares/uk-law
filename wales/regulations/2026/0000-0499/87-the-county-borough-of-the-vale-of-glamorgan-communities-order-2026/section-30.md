# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Llantwit Major — community ward electoral arrangements

In the community of Llantwit Major, the number of councillors to be elected for—

- (a) the Boverton ward is 5;
- (b) the North ward is 5;
- (c) the South East ward is 3;
- (d) the West ward is 2.
