# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 57

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 57. Llandough, and Michaelston-le-Pit and Leckwith — abolition of the communities of Llandough, and Michaelston-le-Pit and Leckwith, changes to community councils and electoral arrangements

The community of Llandough is abolished.

**58.** The community council of Llandough is dissolved.

**59.** The community of Michaelston-le-Pit and Leckwith as changed by this Order is abolished.

**60.** The community council of Michaelston-le-Pit and Leckwith is dissolved.
