# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 54

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 54. Dinas Powys — community ward electoral arrangements

In the community of Dinas Powys, the number of councillors to be elected for—

- (a) the Cross Common ward is 4;
- (b) the Eastbrook ward is 4;
- (c) the Murch ward is 4;
- (d) the Twyn ward is 4.
