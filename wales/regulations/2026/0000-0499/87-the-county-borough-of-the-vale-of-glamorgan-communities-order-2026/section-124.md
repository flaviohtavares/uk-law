# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 124

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 124. Creation of the new community ward of Llysworney

A new community ward is created in the community of Llandow, Llanfair and Llanmaes which is to be known as Llysworney in the English language and Llyswyrny in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 3 on Map 31;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llandow.
