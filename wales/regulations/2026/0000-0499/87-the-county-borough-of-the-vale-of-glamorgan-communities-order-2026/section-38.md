# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 38

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 38. Abolition of the community wards of Cowbridge, Llanblethian West, and Llanblethian East

The following wards of the community of Cowbridge with Llanblethian as changed by this Order are abolished—

- (a) Cowbridge;
- (b) Llanblethian West;
- (c) Llanblethian East.
