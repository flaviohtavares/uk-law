# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 86

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 86. Llantrithyd — change of community ward name

The Llantrithyd ward of the community of Llancarfan as changed by this Order retains the English language name of Llantrithyd and is to be known as Llantriddyd in the Welsh language.
