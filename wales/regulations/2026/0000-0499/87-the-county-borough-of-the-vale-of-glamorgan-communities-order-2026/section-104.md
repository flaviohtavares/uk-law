# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 104

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 104. Peterston-super-Ely — electoral ward arrangements

The electoral ward of Peterston-super-Ely comprises the new community of Peterston and St Georges and the new community of Pendoylan and Welsh St Donats.

**105.** In the electoral ward of Peterston-super-Ely, the number of councillors to be elected is 1.
