# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 150

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 150. Ewenny and St Bride’s Major — establishment of community council and community ward electoral arrangements

A council for the community of Ewenny and St Bride’s Major is established.

**151.** In the community of Ewenny and St Bride’s Major, the number of councillors to be elected for—

- (a) the Ewenny ward is 3;
- (b) the Ogmore-by-Sea ward is 6;
- (c) the St Bride’s Major ward is 4.
