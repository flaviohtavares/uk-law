# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 61

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 61. Creation of the new community of Llandough and Cwrtyrala

A new community is created which is to be known as Llandough and Cwrtyrala in the English language and Llandochau a Chwrtyrala in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded on Map 19;
- (b) forms the electoral ward of Llandough.
