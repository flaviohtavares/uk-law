# The County Borough of the Vale of Glamorgan (Communities) Order 2026, section 112

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 112. Creation of the new community ward of Welsh St Donats

A new community ward is created in the community of Pendoylan and Welsh St Donats which is to be known as Welsh St Donats in the English language and Llanddunwyd in the Welsh language and which—

- (a) comprises the area shown shaded and numbered 2 on Map 30;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Peterston-super-Ely.
