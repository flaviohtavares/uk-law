# The Products of Animal Origin (Disease Control) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2026

Made

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/22/section/1) of the [Animal Health Act 1981](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/22/contents)[^f00001].

## 1. Title and coming into force

(1) The title of this Order is the Products of Animal Origin (Disease Control) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2026.

(2) This Order comes into force on 20 November 2026.

## 2. Amendments to the Products of Animal Origin (Disease Control) (Wales) Regulations 2008

(1) The [Products of Animal Origin (Disease Control) (Wales) Regulations 2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2008/1275/contents)[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 (interpretation: general), in the definition of “disease”, for “sheep and goat plague,” substitute “peste des petits ruminants;”.

(3) In regulation 3 (restricted animal, restricted poultry and restricted meat: definitions), for paragraph (8) substitute—

> (8) In these Regulations, “**restricted meat**” (“***cig dan gyfyngiadau***”) is meat—
>
> - (a) produced from a restricted animal or restricted poultry,
> - (b) produced from an animal held on premises on a date specified by the Welsh Ministers for the purposes of disease control that is subsequently declared to be an infected area, a protection zone or a surveillance zone,
> - (c) produced from pigs that have been vaccinated against classical swine fever or African swine fever within a period of time specified by the Welsh Ministers, except where a marker vaccine (within the meaning given in regulation 2(1) of the Diseases of Swine Regulations 2014[^f00003]) has been used, or
> - (d) that has come into contact with meat produced in accordance with sub-paragraphs (a), (b) or (c).

(4) For Schedule 2 (approved meat treatments) substitute the Schedule set out in the Schedule to this Order.

Explanatory Note

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Products of Animal Origin (Disease Control) (Wales) Regulations 2008 ([S.I. 2008/1275 (W. 132)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2008/1275)) (“**the 2008 Regulations**”). The definitions of “disease” and “restricted meat” in the 2008 Regulations are updated, and a new Schedule 2 is substituted into the 2008 Regulations to reflect the current approved risk mitigating treatments for meat and casings.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to this Order. As a result, it was not considered necessary to carry out a regulatory impact assessment as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with this Order.
