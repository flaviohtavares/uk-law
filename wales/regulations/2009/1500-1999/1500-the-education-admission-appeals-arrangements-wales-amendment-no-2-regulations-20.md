# The Education (Admission Appeals Arrangements) (Wales) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 1500 (W.145)

EDUCATION, WALES

The Education (Admission Appeals Arrangements) (Wales) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2009

Made

16 June 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

18 June 2009

Coming into force

15 July 2009

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 94(5A), 95(3A) and 138(7) and (8) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002], and after consultation with the Administrative Justice and Tribunals Council in accordance with Schedule 7 to the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007[^f00003], make the following Regulations.

## 1. Title, Commencement and Application

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Education (Admission Appeals Arrangements) (Wales) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2009, and they come into force on 15 July 2009.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

## 2. Amendment of Regulations

(1) The Education (Admission Appeals Arrangements) (Wales) Regulations 2005[^f00004] are amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 2 to the Regulations, in each place it occurs, replace—

- “local authority” with “local education authority”.

Jane Hutt

Minister for Children, Education, Lifelong Learning and Skills, one of the Welsh Ministers

16 June 2009

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Education (Admission Appeals Arrangements) (Wales) Regulations 2005 [S.I. 2005/1398 (W.112)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2005/1398) (“the 2005 Regulations”) and come into force on 15 July 2009. The amendment in regulation 2 makes a minor technical change to the 2005 Regulations and replaces the words “local authority” in Schedule 2 with “local education authority”.
