# The Zoonoses and Animal By-Products (Fees) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 2427 (W.197)

ANIMALS, WALES

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Zoonoses and Animal By-Products (Fees) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made

6 September 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

7 September 2009

Coming into force

28 September 2009

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 56(1) and (2) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00001] and now vested in them by section 59(5) of the Government of Wales Act 2006, and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to the European Communities Act 1972 [^f00002] with the consent of the Treasury, make the following Regulations.

These Regulations make provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and it appears to the Welsh Ministers that it is expedient for certain references to provisions of Community instruments to be construed as a reference to those provisions as amended from time to time.

## 1. Title, application and commencement

The title of these Regulations is the Zoonoses and Animal By-Products (Fees) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2009. They apply in relation to Wales and come into force on 28 September 2009.

## 2. Amendment

In regulation 2 (Interpretation) of the Zoonoses and Animal By-Products (Fees) (Wales) Regulations 2008 for the definition of “official control samples” substitute the following definition—

> - “official control samples” (“*samplau rheoli swyddogol*”) means samples taken by the Welsh Ministers under any of the following—
>   - (a) point 2.1.2.2 of the Annex to Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1003/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1003) implementing Regulation [(EC) No 2160/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2160) as regards a Community target for the reduction of the prevalence of certain salmonella serotypes in breeding flocks of *Gallus gallus* and amending Regulation [(EC) No 2160/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2160)[^f00003], as amended from time to time;
>   - (b) point 2.1 of the Annex to Commission Regulation [(EC) No 1168/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1168) implementing Regulation [(EC) No 2160/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/2160) as regards a Community target for the reduction of the prevalence of certain salmonella serotypes in laying flocks of *Gallus gallus* and amending Regulation [(EC) No 1003/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1003)[^f00004], as amended from time to time; or
>   - (c) point 1(b) and (c) of the Annex to Commission Regulation [(EC) No 646/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0646) implementing Regulation 2160/2003 as regards a Community target for the reduction of the prevalence of certain salmonella serotypes in broiler flocks of *Gallus gallus* and repealing Regulation [(EC) No 1091/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1091)[^f00005], as amended from time to time.

.

Elin Jones

Minister for Rural Affairs, one of the Welsh Ministers

6 September 2009

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which apply in relation to Wales, amend the Zoonoses and Animal By-Products (Fees) (Wales) Regulations 2008 ([S.I. 2008/2716) (W. 245)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2008/2716). They make provision for the Welsh Ministers to charge fees for taking or supervising the taking of official samples required under Commission Regulation [(EC) No 646/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0646).

A regulatory impact assessment of the effect of these Regulations has been prepared and copies may be obtained from the Welsh Assembly Government, Cathays Park, Cardiff, CF10 3NQ.
