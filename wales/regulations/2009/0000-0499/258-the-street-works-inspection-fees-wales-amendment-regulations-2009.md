# The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 258 (W.28)

HIGHWAYS, WALES

The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2009

Made

10 February 2009

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

11 February 2009

Coming into force

1 April 2009

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 75 and 104(1) of the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991[^f00001], and now vested in them[^f00002], make the following regulations:

## 1. Title, commencement and application

The title of these Regulations is the Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2009. They come into force on 1 April 2009 and apply in relation to Wales.

## 2. Amendment to the Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) Regulations 2006

The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) Regulations 2006, as amended[^f00003] are further amended in accordance with regulation 3.

**3.** In regulation 3, in paragraphs (1) and (8), for “£25.00” substitute “£50.00”.

Ieuan Wyn Jones

Minister for the Economy and Transport, one of the Welsh Ministers.

10 February 2009

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008 (“the 2008 Regulations”). The 2008 Regulations further amended the Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) Regulations 2006 (“the 2006 Regulations”) so as to increase the fee payable by undertakers for inspections of their work by street authorities in Wales, under regulation 3 of the 2006 Regulations.

The fee originally prescribed in the 2006 Regulations was £21. The fee was altered to £24 in July 2007 by the Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2007 ([S.I. 2007 No.1713 (W.150)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2007/1713)), and was increased to £25 by the 2008 Regulations. The fee is now further increased to £50.
