# The Welsh Ambulance Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 201 (W.26)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, WALES

The Welsh Ambulance Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 2009

Made

2 February 2009

Coming into force

3 February 2009

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of powers conferred by sections 18, 203 and 204(1) of and paragraph 4 of Schedule 3 to the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^f00001], hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

The title of this Order is the Welsh Ambulance Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) (Amendment) Order 2009 and it comes into force on 3 February 2009.

## 2. Amendment to the Welsh Ambulance Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order

In Article 4 of The Welsh Ambulance Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1998[^f00002], after the word “chairman”, replace the word “five” with the word “seven”.

Edwina Hart

Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers

2 February 2009

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Welsh Ambulance Services National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1998, to increase the number of non-executive directors from 5 to 7 (article 2).
