# The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 1382 (W.138)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERICE, WALES

The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2009

Made

8 June 2009

Coming into force

9 June 2009

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of powers conferred by sections 21 and 203(10) of and paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^f00001] and with the consent of the Treasury in accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to that Act, hereby make the following Order.

## 1. Title and Commencement

(1) The title of this Order is the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2009 and it comes into force on 9 June 2009.

## 2. Amendment to the Originating Capital of NHS Trusts

(1) The National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) (Wales) Order 2009[^f00002] is amended as follows;

(2) For the Schedule to the National Health Service Trusts (Originating Capital) (Wales) Order 2009, substitute the Schedule to this Order.

Edwina Hart

Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers.

8 June 2009
