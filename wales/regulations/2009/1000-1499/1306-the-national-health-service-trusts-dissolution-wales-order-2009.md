# The National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) (Wales) Order 2009

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2009 No. 1306 (W.117)

THE NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICES, WALES

The National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) (Wales) Order 2009

Made

21 May 2009

Coming into force

1 October 2009

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of powers conferred on them by sections 18 and 204(1) of and paragraph 28(1) of Schedule 3 to the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^f00001] and after completion of consultation prescribed under paragraph 28(3) of Schedule 3 to that Act[^f00002] make the following Order:

## 1. Title and Commencement

The Title of this Order is the National Health Service Trusts (Dissolution) (Wales) Order 2009 and it comes into force on 1 October 2009.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS Trusts

The National Health Service Trusts listed in column 1 of the Schedule are dissolved and the corresponding Orders listed in column 2 of the Schedule are revoked.

Edwina Hart

Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers

21 May 2009
