# The Elections and Elected Bodies (Wales) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 846 (W. 148) (C. 36)

Representation Of The People, Wales

Public Bodies, Wales

The Elections and Elected Bodies (Wales) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2025

Made

11 July 2025

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 72(4) and (6) of the Elections and Elected Bodies (Wales) Act 2024[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Title and interpretation

(1) The title of this Order is the Elections and Elected Bodies (Wales) Act 2024 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2025.

(2) In this Order, “**the Act**” means the Elections and Elected Bodies (Wales) Act 2024.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 18 July 2025

Section 24 (reports by the Electoral Commission) and section 64 (undue influence) of the Act come into force on 18 July 2025.

Jayne Bryant

Cabinet Secretary for Housing and Local Government, one of the Welsh Ministers

11 July 2025

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the second commencement order made by the Welsh Ministers under the Elections and Elected Bodies (Wales) Act 2024 (“**the Act**”).

Article 2 of this Order brings into force section 24 (reports by the Electoral Commission) and section 64 (undue influence) of the Act on 18 July 2025.
