# The Senedd Cymru (Representation of the People) Order 2025, section 144

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 144. Premises used for election purposes[^c-905f30f568f3d49b4cb721130d38ccb6]

*Extent: E+W*

In relation to premises in Wales, section 65(6) of the Local Government Finance Act 1988[^f00070] has effect as if—

- (a) the reference to public meetings in furtherance of a person’s candidature at a parliamentary or local government election included a reference to public meetings promoting a particular result at a Senedd election, and
- (b) the reference to use of a room by a returning officer for the purpose of taking the poll in a parliamentary or local government election included a reference to use of a room by a returning officer for the purpose of taking the poll in a Senedd election in accordance with Schedule 5 to this Order.
