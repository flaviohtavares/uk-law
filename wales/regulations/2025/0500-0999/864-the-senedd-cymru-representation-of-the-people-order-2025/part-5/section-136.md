# The Senedd Cymru (Representation of the People) Order 2025, section 136

## 136. Advertisements[^c-beb729aa53d1382e3c79f21fe2171816]

*Extent: E+W*

The Town and Country Planning (Control of Advertisements) Regulations 1992[^f00069] have effect in relation to the display, on any site in Wales, of an advertisement relating specifically to a Senedd election as those Regulations have effect in relation to the display of an advertisement relating specifically to a parliamentary election.
