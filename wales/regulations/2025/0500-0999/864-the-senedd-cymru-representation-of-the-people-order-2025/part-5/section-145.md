## 145. Dissolution of the Senedd[^c-fb0bbf9ca840bb43098cb5163f858ec3]

*Extent: E+W*

The “minimum period” for the purpose of section 3(2)(a) of the 2006 Act is a period of 21 days, calculated in accordance with rule 2 of Schedule 5.
