## 130. Evidence by certificate of returning officer[^c-37ceb769aaa460473a6b5e07bf277507]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A certificate of the returning officer at a Senedd election specified in paragraph (2), will be sufficient evidence of the facts stated in it for the following purposes—

- (a) any prosecution for a corrupt or illegal practice, or for any illegal payment or employment, and
- (b) any proceedings for a penalty under article 59 (penalty for sitting or voting where no return and declarations are delivered etc.).

(2) The certificates are those that certify—

- (a) that the election mentioned in the certificate was duly held, and
- (b) that the person named in the certificate was a candidate at the election.
