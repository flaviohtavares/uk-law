## 92. Judges’ expenses and reception[^c-afeae5e331c38f8e25ef8fb11121871c]

*Extent: E+W*

In relation to the trial of a Senedd election petition the following expenses are to be paid out of the Welsh Consolidated Fund—

- (a) the travelling and other expenses of the judges, and
- (b) all expenses properly incurred in providing them with necessary accommodation and with a proper court.
