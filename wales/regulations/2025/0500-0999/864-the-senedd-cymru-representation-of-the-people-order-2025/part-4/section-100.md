## 100. Expenses of witnesses[^c-eee0649ffa0542018e28dba9fc13a9b0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The reasonable expenses incurred by a person in connection with their attendance to give evidence at the trial may be allowed by a certificate of the election court or prescribed officer.

(2) The amount of expenses allowed under paragraph (1) must be in accordance with allowances given to a person who attends a trial of a civil action.

(3) If the witness was called and examined by virtue of article 98(2), the expenses referred to in paragraph (1) must be deemed part of the expenses of providing a court, but otherwise they are deemed costs of the petition.
