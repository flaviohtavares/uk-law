# The Senedd Cymru (Representation of the People) Order 2025, section 112

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 112. Consequence of a candidate being reported guilty of corrupt or illegal practice[^c-d68eb24184d0146fac8a83fd3b87dc1e]

*Extent: E+W*

When an elected candidate is reported by an election court to be personally guilty or guilty by their agents of any corrupt or illegal practice, that candidate’s election will be void.
