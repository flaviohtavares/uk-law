## 95. Petition at issue[^key-4bb93d195f83d4dad81a3c09814bc5ab]

*Extent: E+W*

The Senedd election petition will be at issue—

- (a) when the petitioner gives the security for costs by a deposit of money equal to the amount of the security required, the time when the security is given, and
- (b) in any other case, whichever of the following happens later—
  - (i) when the time prescribed for the making of objections under article 94(5) expires, or
  - (ii) when an objection is made, the time that objection is disallowed or removed.
