## 131. Evidence by certificate of electoral registration[^c-9a54c2bb16bdf76ea95d5af7e2e0c320]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The certificate of a registration officer that a person is or is not, or was or was not, duly registered in the officer’s register in respect of any address, will be sufficient evidence of the facts stated in it.

(2) A document purporting to be such a certificate will be received in evidence and presumed to be such a certificate unless the contrary is proved.
