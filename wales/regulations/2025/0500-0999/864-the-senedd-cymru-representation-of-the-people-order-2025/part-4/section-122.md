## 122. Prosecutions for illegal practices[^c-c1a41ef380d2f0c043739745ef3a5f20]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person guilty of an illegal practice is liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.

(2) On a prosecution for an illegal practice, it will be sufficient to allege that the person charged is guilty of an illegal practice.
