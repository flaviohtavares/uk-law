## 79. Prohibition of paid canvassers[^key-ced16f98c88510df6558505cb20f73bd]

*Extent: E+W*

If, either before, during or after a Senedd election, a person (“**P**”) is engaged or employed for payment or promise of payment as a canvasser for the purpose of promoting or procuring a particular result at the election, both P and the person engaging or employing P are guilty of illegal employment.
