## 80. Providing money for illegal purposes[^c-a2743f634d6a91d7b56ad79c8c07cc3b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a person is guilty of an illegal payment where that person knowingly provides money—

- (a) for any payment which is contrary to the provisions of this Order,
- (b) for any expenses incurred in excess of the maximum amount allowed by this Order, or
- (c) for replacing any money expended in any such payment or expenses.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply where the payment or the incurring of the expenses may have been previously allowed in pursuance of article 120 (application for relief).
