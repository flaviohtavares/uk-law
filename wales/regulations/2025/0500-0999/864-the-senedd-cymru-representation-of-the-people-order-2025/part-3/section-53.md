## 53. Election agent’s claim[^key-f7954156963e348cf9ce2ca05497c866]

*Extent: E+W*

So far as circumstances permit, this Order applies to any claim made by the election agent of an individual candidate for that agent’s remuneration and to its payment as if the agent were any other creditor, and if any difference arises about the amount of the claim, the claim will be a disputed claim within the meaning of this Order and will be dealt with in accordance with article 52.
