## 76. Corrupt withdrawal from candidature[^c-4180ae81b67aba150be87b801455f0b3]

*Extent: E+W*

Any person who corruptly induces or procures any other person to withdraw from being a candidate at a Senedd election, in consideration of any payment or promise of payment, and any person withdrawing in pursuance of the inducement or procurement, is guilty of an illegal payment.
