## 57. Circumstances in which no return or declaration is required[^key-ea4c9aab9ea6a229db5921c1b9b26fb9]

*Extent: E+W*

Notwithstanding anything in article 54, 55 or 56, no return or declaration as to election expenses is required in the case of a person—

- (a) who is a candidate at a Senedd election, but is so only because that person has been declared by others to be a candidate, and
- (b) who has not consented to the declaration or taken any part as a candidate at the election.
