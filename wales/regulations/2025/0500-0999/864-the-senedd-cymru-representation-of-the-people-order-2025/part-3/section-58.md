## 58. Penalty for failure as respects return or declarations[^key-254a06edd095a7a4969a08d3d821f395]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to the provisions of article 60, if any candidate or election agent fails to comply with the requirements of article 54, 55 or 56 the candidate or election agent is guilty of an illegal practice.
