## SCHEDULE 9 — Modification of Election Petition Rules 1960

*Extent: E+W*

Articles 86 and 88(1)

### 1. Senedd election petition: modification of the Election Petition Rules 1960[^M_I_6ecdf55d-d521-4e87-a4cb-6b41ea39403b]

The Election Petition Rules 1960[^f00132] (“**the 1960 Rules**”) apply to a Senedd election petition subject to the modifications set out in this Schedule.

### 2. Modification of rule 2(2) (definitions)[^M_M_d5dbb229-41bd-4360-8aaa-b257d0756c55]

(1) Rule 2(2) is to be read as if it contained the following additional defined terms—

- “**candidate**” means an individual candidate or a party list candidate for return as a Senedd member;
- “**the Clerk of the Senedd**” is to be construed in accordance with section 26 of the Government of Wales Act 2006;
- “**party list candidate**” means a candidate included on a list of a registered political party;
- “**Presiding Officer of the Senedd**” is to be construed in accordance with section 25 of the Government of Wales Act 2006;
- “**registered political party**” means a party registered under Part 2 of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000;
- “**Senedd Cymru election**” means the holding of elections for the return of all Senedd members;
- “**Senedd constituency**” is to be construed in accordance with section 2 of the Government of Wales Act 2006;
- “**Senedd election petition**” means a petition presented in pursuance of Part 4 of the Senedd Cymru (Representation of the People) Order 2025.

(2) In rule 2(2), the definitions are modified as follows—

- (a) except in rule 2(3), “the Act” is to be read as meaning this Order, and any reference to a section of the 1983 Act is to be read as a reference to the corresponding provision in this Order, as set out in paragraph 8 of this Schedule;
- (b) “local election petition”, is to be read as if it were omitted as is rule 18 and any reference in the 1960 Rules to a provision of the 1983 Act which applies only to a local election petition;
- (c) “petition” is to be read as meaning a Senedd election petition;
- (d) “constituency” is to be read as meaning the Senedd constituency to which the petition relates.

### 3. Modification of rule 2(3) (prescribed officer)[^M_I_55a34ddb-c3ae-4e9e-d2c0-78f6c6970b4b]

In rule 2(3), “the prescribed officer in relation to elections under the local government Act” is to be read as “the prescribed officer in relation to elections under Part 1 of the Government of Wales Act 2006”.

### 4. Modification of rule 4(1) (form of petition)[^M_M_08a84930-97cd-4abb-dd94-81305ac0b2d1]

In rule 4(1), sub-paragraph (b)—

- (a) “in the case of a parliamentary election” is to be read as if it were omitted;
- (b) “the Clerk of the Crown” is to be read as “the Clerk of the Senedd”.

### 5. Modification of rule 9(3) (display etc. of notice of time and place of trial)[^M_I_d15c69cd-8b9e-43c7-9963-a128c3419b94]

In rule 9(3)—

- (a) “a parliamentary election petition” is to be read as “a Senedd election petition”;
- (b) “and in any other case not less than seven days,” is to be read as if it were omitted.

### 6. Modification of rule 10(1) (identifying votes at issue)[^M_I_297a7384-c6fe-4ae1-ee8c-0a1cb95f8cc4]

Rule 10(1) is to be read as follows—

> (1) Where the petition claims the seat for an unsuccessful candidate on the ground that the individual candidate, or the registered political party on whose list the candidate was a party list candidate, had a higher electoral figure than that of another individual candidate or of another registered political party to whom a seat in the Senedd constituency was allocated, every party must, not less than 7 days before the day fixed for the trial—
>
> - (a) file a list of the votes the candidate contends were wrongly admitted or rejected stating, in respect of each such vote, the grounds for the candidate’s contention, and
> - (b) serve a copy of the list on every other party and the Director of Public Prosecutions.
