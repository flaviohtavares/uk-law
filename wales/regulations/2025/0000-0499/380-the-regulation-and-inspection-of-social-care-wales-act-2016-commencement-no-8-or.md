# The Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 380 (W. 75) (C. 14)

Social Care, Wales

The Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2025

Made

24 March 2025

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 188(1) and (3) of the Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016[^f00001], make the following Order.

## 1. Title and interpretation

(1) The title of this Order is the Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016 (Commencement No. 8) Order 2025.

(2) In this Order, “**the Act**” means the Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016.

## 2. Appointed day

31 March 2025 is the appointed day for the coming into force of section 160(5) of the Act.

Dawn Bowden

Minister for Children and Social Care, under the authority of the Cabinet Secretary for Health and Social Care, one of the Welsh Ministers.

24 March 2025

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This is the eighth Commencement Order made by the Welsh Ministers under the Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016 (“**the Act**”). It brings into force section 160(5) of the Act on 31 March 2025.

Section 160(5) provides Social Care Wales with the power to apply to the First-tier Tribunal for an order requiring the supply of information or production of documents, requested in accordance with section 160, where either of these is being withheld.
