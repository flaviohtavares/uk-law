The Welsh Ministers make this Order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001].

## 1. Title and coming into force

(1) The title of this Order is the Trade in Animals and Related Products (Amendment and Legislative Functions) and Animal Health (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) (EU Exit) (Amendment) Order 2025.

(2) This Order comes into force on 31 March 2025.

## 2. Amendment of the Trade in Animals and Related Products (Amendment and Legislative Functions) and Animal Health (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) (EU Exit) Regulations 2022

(1) The Trade in Animals and Related Products (Amendment and Legislative Functions) and Animal Health (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) (EU Exit) Regulations 2022[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 18 (modifications to directive 2009/156), for paragraph (6)(e)(ii) substitute—

> - (ii) in point (a), for “in accordance with the procedure referred to in Article 21(3)” there were substituted “by an Order made by the Welsh Ministers under section 10 of the Animal Health Act 1981

.

Huw Irranca-Davies

Deputy First Minister and Cabinet Secretary for Climate Change and Rural Affairs, one of the Welsh Ministers

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

The Trade in Animals and Related Products (Amendment and Legislative Functions) and Animal Health (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) (EU Exit) Regulations 2022 ([S.I. 2022/1348 (W. 271)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2022/1348)) (“**the 2022 Regulations**”) addressed failures of assimilated law to operate effectively and corrected other deficiencies which arose from the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union and amended the Trade in Animals and Related Products (Wales) Regulations 2011 ([S.I. 2011/2379 (W. 252)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2011/2379)).

This Order corrects an error in the 2022 Regulations by amending the modification made to Directive 2009/156 to enable Orders made under section 10 of the Animal Health Act 1981 to determine the period in which Equidae may be dispatched from territories considered to be infected with African horse sickness.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to this Order. As a result, it was not considered necessary to carry out a regulatory impact assessment as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with this Order.
