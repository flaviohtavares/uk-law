# The Health Services (Provider Selection Regime) (Wales) Regulations 2025, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Adding additional providers to a framework agreement

(1) A relevant authority must, during the term of a framework agreement, follow the Competitive Process to allow additional providers to be selected to be party to the framework agreement.

(2) The Competitive Process referred to in paragraph (1) must be commenced at least once—

- (a) during the period of 4 years beginning on the day the framework agreement was concluded, and
- (b) during the period of 4 years beginning the day after the Competitive Process to select additional providers to be party to the framework agreement under paragraph (2)(a) has concluded.
