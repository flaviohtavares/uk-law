# The Health Services (Provider Selection Regime) (Wales) Regulations 2025, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Award of a contract based on a single-provider framework agreement

(1) This regulation applies where a framework agreement is concluded between a relevant authority and single provider, and the relevant authority awards a contract without a competition.

(2) The relevant authority must—

- (a) award any contract without a competition, and
- (b) submit for publication a notice of the award of the contract within 30 days of the contract being awarded.

(3) The notice referred to in paragraph (2)(b) must include the information set out in Schedule 3.
