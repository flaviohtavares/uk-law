# The Health Services (Provider Selection Regime) (Wales) Regulations 2025, section 5

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 5. Procurement principles

When procuring relevant health services, a relevant authority must act—

- (a) with a view to—
  - (i) securing the needs of the people who use the services,
  - (ii) improving the quality of the services, and
  - (iii) improving efficiency in the provision of the services;
- (b) transparently, fairly and proportionately;
- (c) having regard to the Wales procurement policy statement published under section 14 of the 2023 Act.
