# The Social Care Wales (Specification of Social Care Workers) (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 340 (W. 69)

Social Care, Wales

The Social Care Wales (Specification of Social Care Workers) (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations 2025

Made

12 March 2025

Coming into force

1 April 2025

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 80(1)(b) and 187(1)(b) of the Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016[^f00001] (“**the 2016 Act**”), make the following Regulations.

In accordance with section 187(2)(n) of the 2016 Act, a draft of this instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, Senedd Cymru[^f00002].

## 1. Title and commencement

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Social Care Wales (Specification of Social Care Workers) (Registration) (Amendment) Regulations 2025.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1 April 2025.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations “**the 2016 Regulations**” means the Social Care Wales (Specification of Social Care Workers) (Registration) Regulations 2016[^f00003].

## 3. Amendment to regulation 2 of the 2016 Regulations

In regulation 2 (interpretation) of the 2016 Regulations, insert the following in the appropriate place—

> - “**special school residential service**” (“***gwasanaeth preswyl ysgol arbennig***”) has the same meaning as that given in regulation 2 of the Regulated Services (Special School Residential Services) (Wales) Regulations 2023[^f00004];

.

## 4. Amendments to regulation 3 of the 2016 Regulations

Regulation 3 (specification of social care workers) of the 2016 Regulations is amended as follows—

- (a) in paragraph (2)(b)—
  - (i) at the end of paragraph (iv) omit “or”;
  - (ii) at the end of paragraph (v) insert “or”;
  - (iii) after paragraph (v) insert—

    > - (vi) a special school residential service,

    ;
- (b) in paragraph (2)(bb)—
  - (i) at the end of paragraph (iv) omit “or”;
  - (ii) at the end of paragraph (v) insert “or”;
  - (iii) after paragraph (v) insert—

    > - (vi) a special school residential service,

    ;

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Social Care Wales (Specification of Social Care Workers) (Registration) Regulations 2016.

Section 80(1)(b) of the Regulation and Inspection of Social Care (Wales) Act 2016 requires Social Care Wales to keep a register of social care workers of such description as may be specified by the Welsh Ministers in regulations.

These Regulations specify an additional description of social care workers in respect of which Social Care Wales must keep a register, namely those who are employed (whether as an employee or a worker) or engaged under a contract for services to provide care and support to any person in Wales in connection with a special school residential service.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to these Regulations. As a result, a regulatory impact assessment has been prepared as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with these Regulations. A copy can be obtained from the Department of Health and Social Services, Welsh Government, Cathays Park, Cardiff, CF10 3NQ and is published on www.gov.wales.
