# The Basic Payment Scheme (Tapering, Payment Entitlements and Closure) (Wales) Regulations 2025

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2025 No. 1282 (W. 209)

Agriculture, Wales

The Basic Payment Scheme (Tapering, Payment Entitlements and Closure) (Wales) Regulations 2025

Made

3 December 2025

Coming into force in accordance with regulation 1(2) and (3).

The Welsh Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 16(1) of the Agriculture (Wales) Act 2023[^f00001].

In accordance with section 50(6) of that Act, a draft of these Regulations has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, Senedd Cymru.

## 1. Title, coming into force and application

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Basic Payment Scheme (Tapering, Payment Entitlements and Closure) (Wales) Regulations 2025.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1 January 2026, subject to paragraph (3).

(3) Regulation 3(2) of, and the Schedule to, the Regulations come into force on 1 January 2029.

(4) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations—

- “**basic payment scheme**” (“***cynllun y taliad sylfaenol***”) means the Basic Payment Scheme under the Direct Payments Regulation;
- “the Direct Payments Regulation”[^f00002] (“***y Rheoliad Taliadau Uniongyrchol***”) means [Regulation (EU) No1307/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1307) of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing rules for direct payments to farmers under support schemes within the framework of the common agricultural policy.

## 3. Closure of the basic payment scheme

(1) The basic payment scheme will cease to exist at the end of 31 December 2028.

(2) The Schedule makes provision revoking legislation governing the basic payment scheme.

## 4. Tapering of payments under the basic payment scheme

(1) The Direct Payments Regulation is amended as follows.

(2) After Article 5A (the total ceiling for Wales), insert—

> **Article 5B — Tapering of payments under the basic payment scheme in Wales**
>
> **1.** The Welsh Ministers may annually reduce payments under the basic payment scheme by such amounts as they consider appropriate.

## 5. Transfer and use of entitlements

(1) The Direct Payments Regulation is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 34 (transfer of payment entitlements), after paragraph (1) insert—

> (1A) Transfer of payment entitlements, by any method, can only take place if it includes the transfer of an eligible hectare in the same transaction from the same transferor.

## 6. Young farmer payment

(1) Commission Delegated [Regulation (EU) No639/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/0639) of 11 March 2014 supplementing [Regulation (EU) No1307/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1307) of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing rules for direct payments to farmers under support schemes within the framework of the common agricultural policy and amending Annex X to that Regulation[^f00003], is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 25 (transfer of entitlements)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), after “at any time of the year” insert “providing that each entitlement is transferred with an eligible hectare”;
- (b) after paragraph (2), insert—

  > (3) For the purpose of this Article, “**eligible hectare**” has the meaning given in Article 32(2) of [Regulation (EU) No1307/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1307).

(3) In Article 49 (access of legal persons to the payment for young farmers), after paragraph (4) insert—

> (5) The annual payment for young farmers in Wales referred to in Article 50(1) of [Regulation (EU) No1307/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1307) is only available to those young farmers who are already receiving such payment before 1 January 2026.

## 7. Closure of the national and regional reserves in the Direct Payments Regulation

(1) The Direct Payments Regulation is amended as follows.

(2) Omit Article 30 (establishment and use of the national reserve or regional reserves).

(3) Omit Article 31 (replenishment of the national reserve or regional reserves).

## 8. Closure of the national and regional reserves in Commission Delegated [Regulation (EU) No639/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/0639)

(1) Commission Delegated [Regulation (EU) No639/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/0639) is amended as follows.

(2) Omit Article 26 (reversion to the national or regional reserve due to retention on transfer of payment entitlements).

(3) Omit Article 28 (establishment of payment entitlements from the national or regional reserve under Article 30(6) of [Regulation (EU) No1307/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1307)).

(4) Omit Article 29 (establishment of payment entitlements from the national or regional reserve under Article 30(7) of [Regulation (EU) No1307/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1307)).

(5) Omit Article 30 (further rules on the establishment of payment entitlements from the national or regional reserve).

## 9. Closure of the national and regional reserves in the Common Agricultural Policy Basic Payment and Support Schemes (Wales) Regulations 2015

(1) The Common Agricultural Policy Basic Payment and Support Schemes (Wales) Regulations 2015[^f00004] are amended as follows.

(2) Omit regulation 10 (basic payment scheme entitlements).

(3) Omit regulation 11 (transfer of entitlements).

(4) Omit regulation 18 (national reserve).

## 10. Additional amendments in the Direct Payments Regulation

(1) The Direct Payments Regulation is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 5A (the total ceiling for Wales)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), for “must” substitute “may”;
- (b) in paragraph (2)—
  - (i) for “must” substitute “may”, and
  - (ii) for “before the start of the relevant year” substitute “at any time before payments are made”;
- (c) in paragraph (3), for “must” substitute “may”;
- (d) in paragraph (4), for “, for any relevant year, must” substitute “may”;
- (e) omit paragraph (5).

(3) In Article 22 (basic payment scheme ceiling)—

- (a) in paragraph (2)—
  - (i) at the beginning insert “The Welsh Ministers may determine”, and
  - (ii) omit the words from “is the amount which” to the end;
- (b) omit paragraph (4);
- (c) omit paragraph (5).

## 11. Additional amendments in the Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) No809/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/0809)

(1) Commission Implementing [Regulation (EU) No809/2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2014/0809) laying down rules for the application of [Regulation (EU) No1306/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1306) of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to the integrated administration and control system, rural development measures and cross compliance[^f00005] is amended as follows.

(2) Omit Article 22 (allocation or increase of the value of payment entitlements).

(3) In Article 23 (recovery of undue payment entitlements), for “revert to the national reserve or regional reserves referred to in Article 30 of [Regulation (EU) No1307/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1307)”, in both places it occurs, substitute “be adjusted accordingly”.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend assimilated law and other legislation in relation to the Basic Payment Scheme (“**BPS**”) established under [Regulation (EU) No1307/2013](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2013/1307) (“**the Direct Payments Regulation**”). These Regulations give the Welsh Ministers the power to taper payments under the BPS from 1 January 2026 until the end of 2028 when the BPS will close. These Regulations are made by the Welsh Ministers under powers conferred by section 16(1) of the Agriculture (Wales) Act 2023.

Regulation 3 provides for the closure of the BPS on 31 December 2028 and lists legislation to be revoked upon closure of the scheme.

Regulation 4 amends the Direct Payments Regulation to allow the Welsh Ministers to taper payments under the BPS until its closure.

Regulation 5 amends the Direct Payments Regulation to change the way in which BPS payment entitlements can be transferred. Entitlements cannot be transferred unless each entitlement is transferred with an eligible hectare in the same transaction from the same transferor.

Regulation 6 amends assimilated law to prevent young farmers claiming Young Farmer Payment under the Direct Payments Regulation for the first time.

Regulations 7, 8 and 9 make provision to close the national and regional reserves, the effect of which being that farmers are unable to obtain entitlements to use for claims under the BPS from those reserves.

Regulation 10 and 11 make additional amendments to assimilated law.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to these Regulations. As a result, a regulatory impact assessment has been prepared as to the likely costs and benefits of complying with these Regulations. A copy can be obtained from Welsh Government, Cathays Park, Cardiff, CF10 3NQ and is published on www.gov.wales.
