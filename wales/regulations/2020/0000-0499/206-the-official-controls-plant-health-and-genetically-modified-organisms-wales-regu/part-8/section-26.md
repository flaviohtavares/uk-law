# The Official Controls (Plant Health and Genetically Modified Organisms) (Wales) Regulations 2020, section 26

> Text as at 2025-02-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Notification requirements in relation to other plants and plant products[^c-1bf1675cc9acf6b010b3fb3263b1a0f0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A professional operator who is bringing [^c-4e97e287639afa1d6a1e9343712356eb]... into Wales any solid fuel wood from a third country to which Article 47(1) of the Official Controls Regulation does not apply[^c-283373e1283f485b3ad6d3324a4a666c] must, before or no later than four days after the date of their arrival in Wales, provide written notification to a plant health inspector of the matters referred to in paragraph (2)—

- (a)[^c-6b6ecb9aca61d51e952035b6778d3d33] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-6b6ecb9aca61d51e952035b6778d3d33] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-6b6ecb9aca61d51e952035b6778d3d33] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The matters are—

- (a) the expected date of the arrival of the consignment or, if the consignment has arrived in Wales, the date on which it first arrived in Wales;
- (b) the intended destination of the consignment, or if the consignment has arrived at its intended destination in Wales, its current location;
- (c) the genus, species and quantity of the [^c-6b6ecb9aca61d51e952035b6778d3d33]... wood in the consignment;
- (d) the country from which the [^c-8998ac00e914f2be8cecaebb232b9690]... wood has been, or is[^c-f1df37f08d3c4a021cc9095ef45ad0de] to be, consigned;
- (e)[^c-0347b59f35b0f1e0bca57a6557952890] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f)[^c-d6ed84e1bae41fcbe8ab673aeaf3dac9] the address of the consignor, and
- (g) details of any phytosanitary treatments applied to the wood.

(3) In this regulation “**solid fuel wood**” means fuel wood in the form of logs, billets, twigs, faggots or other similar forms.
