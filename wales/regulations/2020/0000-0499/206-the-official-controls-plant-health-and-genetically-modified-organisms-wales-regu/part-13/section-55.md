## 55. Amendment to secondary legislation on official controls in relation to genetically modified organisms[^c-0991d411ad418ec81534c67948f67539]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Official Controls (Animals, Feed and Food, Plant Health Fees etc.) (Wales) Regulations 2020 [^c23987491] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2(1), in the definition of “relevant legislation”, for “sub-paragraphs (a), (c), (d), (e) and (f)” substitute “ sub-paragraphs (a) to (f) ”.

(3) In regulation 13(5)(b), in the Welsh language text, after “bodlonir” insert “ un ”.
