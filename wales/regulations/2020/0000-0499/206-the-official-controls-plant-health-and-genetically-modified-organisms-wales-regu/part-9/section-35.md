# The Official Controls (Plant Health and Genetically Modified Organisms) (Wales) Regulations 2020, section 35

> Text as at 2025-02-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Disclosure of information to other competent authorities[^c-4e2a151f2136d5d1fb01a2e242436fdc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may disclose information to any other competent authority in the United Kingdom or to a CD authority[^c-89fd6871c93d36cf01fd366ffea2265e] for the purposes of the [^c-29e0adbb7815a6be1ec14befb3dc0af1]... Plant Health Regulation , the Windsor Framework (Plant Health) Regulations 2023[^c-5fc360ebe11dd2b69c9652361695f4e0] or the Official Controls Regulation.

(2) Nothing in paragraph (1) affects any other power or requirement of the Welsh Ministers to disclose information.
