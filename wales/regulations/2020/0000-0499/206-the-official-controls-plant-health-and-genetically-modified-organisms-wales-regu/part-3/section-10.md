# The Official Controls (Plant Health and Genetically Modified Organisms) (Wales) Regulations 2020, section 10

> Text as at 2025-02-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. Official measures in relation to non-compliant consignments or consignments which pose a risk to plant health[^key-c36b96e945b29aaa08f98afd195e2b6e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies to—

- (a) any controlled consignment or regulated item which, in the opinion of a plant health inspector, has been brought into Wales from a third country in contravention of a[^key-bda2f11f427cdf3af132983502fcdc43] plant health rule,
- (b) any controlled consignment or regulated item which has been brought into Wales from a third country and which does not otherwise comply with a[^key-e5b06b258120b0675ee7750dc50584ff] plant health rule, or
- (c) any consignment which has been brought into Wales from a third country and which, in the opinion of a plant health inspector, poses a risk to plant health in Wales or to any other part of Great Britain[^key-074eabfaff53292a1e753e387c681b44].

(2) A plant health inspector must serve a notice on the operator who is responsible for the consignment or item—

- (a) placing the consignment or item under official detention, and
- (b) setting out the measures which the operator must take in relation to the consignment or item.
