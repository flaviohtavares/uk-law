# The Official Controls (Plant Health and Genetically Modified Organisms) (Wales) Regulations 2020, section 38

> Text as at 2025-02-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 38. General[^key-b020e34854e7c1d6fbd6103ac792716a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person commits an offence if the person contravenes or fails to comply with—

- (a) regulation [^key-da942ab20834a57e1cd28a7b7ed7ffa8]... 26(1);
- (b)[^key-8f05ed59369a9192c70de287cedb1c26] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) paragraphs 2(1) or (2), 5(1) or (2), 11(1), 12(1), 13(1), 14(1), 15, 20(1), 21(7), 22(2), 27(1), 28(7), 29(2) or 31(2) of Schedule 2;
- (d) a provision of the [^key-8e433eb79cde94a0124592cfa7150e9a]... Plant Health Regulation specified in Part 1 of Schedule 3;
- (e) a provision of the Official Controls Regulation specified in Part 2 of Schedule 3 (insofar as it applies to plants, plant products or other objects which are subject to a[^key-30f115f99f60e72f3c513e1253f07782] plant health rule);
- (f) a provision of any other assimilated direct[^key-16c209cecbea66004a2c28d3ee89f3e1] legislation specified in Part 3 of Schedule 3;
- (fa)[^key-cc3faa8e1f3f67b0595ae7ffd2e1d14a] a provision of the Windsor Framework (Plant Health) Regulations 2023 specified in Schedule 3A
- (g)[^key-7ebf1710a0936c0fab4e6019afa76c8a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) But paragraph (1) does not apply to anything done under, or in accordance with—

- (a) an authorisation or permit which is granted under these Regulations or has effect under or by virtue of these Regulations;
- (b) an approval granted under regulation 13(6) or an approval referred to in regulation 54(1);
- (c) a notice which is given by a plant health inspector or an appropriate authority under these Regulations, or has effect under or by virtue of these Regulations.
