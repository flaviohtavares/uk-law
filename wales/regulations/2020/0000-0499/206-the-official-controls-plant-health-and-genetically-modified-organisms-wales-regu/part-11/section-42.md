# The Official Controls (Plant Health and Genetically Modified Organisms) (Wales) Regulations 2020, section 42

> Text as at 2025-02-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 42. Improper use of ... plant passports , Northern Ireland plant health labels or certificates[^c-6b0c9ae19ebe9c4df439c2857cac2f00][^c-075e4d3ec9d8b6d9a54054b2da502ca8][^c-de874c8a5759157fd3a4a933e667c01f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person commits an offence if the person—

- (a) dishonestly issues a UK plant passport[^c-fc5aa2b71aa29b79cef5df725bafb94a] , a Northern Ireland plant health label[^c-89af1dff64a150cd3a6f62f184535b28] or a certificate,
- (b) dishonestly alters a [^c-8bc4563646516b0ba7d64004d883e069]... plant passport , a Northern Ireland plant health label or a certificate, or
- (c) dishonestly re-uses a [^c-99202bab1c4c9a7f04db9b485e96212c]... plant passport , a Northern Ireland plant health label or a certificate.

(2) In paragraph (1), “**certificate**” means a phytosanitary certificate for export, a phytosanitary certificate for re-export or a pre-export certificate.
