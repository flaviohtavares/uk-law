# The Adoption Agencies (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2020, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Title, commencement and interpretation

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Adoption Agencies (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2020.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1 April 2020.

(3) In these Regulations, “the Principal Regulations” means the Adoption Agencies (Wales) Regulations 2005[^f00004].
