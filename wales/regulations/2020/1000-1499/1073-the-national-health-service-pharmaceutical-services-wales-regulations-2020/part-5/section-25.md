# The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) (Wales) Regulations 2020, section 25

> Text as at 2026-06-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. Appeals[^key-3c7f57cdab6c8522fe1137d1eea2aa73]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Schedule 4 makes provision for appeals to the Welsh Ministers in respect of decisions of Local Health Boards made under this Part, save for those regulations listed in paragraph (2).

(2) There is no right of appeal in respect of a decision of a Local Health Board—

- (a) to make or not to make, or to extend, a temporary amendment to a pharmaceutical list under regulation 21 (applications involving temporary relocation), or
- (b) to extend or not to extend the relevant period under regulation 24 (application to extend the relevant period).
