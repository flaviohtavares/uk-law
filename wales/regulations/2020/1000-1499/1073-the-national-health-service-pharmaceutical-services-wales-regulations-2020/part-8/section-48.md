# The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) (Wales) Regulations 2020, section 48

> Text as at 2026-06-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 48. Conditions relating to local resolution of disputes over terms of service[^key-d148e3ec7f8639f16956f73dff8a7d1e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) It is a condition of the inclusion of each NHS pharmacist or NHS appliance contractor in a pharmaceutical list by the Local Health Board that the NHS pharmacist or NHS appliance contractor will make every reasonable effort to communicate with the Local Health Board with a view to resolving any dispute between either the NHS pharmacist or NHS appliance contractor and the Local Health Board relating to compliance with the terms of service under which pharmaceutical services are provided.

(2) The Local Health Board may not vary or remove the condition imposed by virtue of paragraph (1).
