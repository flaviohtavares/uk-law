# The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) (Wales) Regulations 2020, section 28

> Text as at 2026-06-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Provision of pharmaceutical services for immediate treatment or personal administration[^key-7bb9e9deb9807ee1788d1c3070b1b24d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a doctor whose name is included in a medical performers list may—

- (a) provide to a patient any appliance or drug, not being a Scheduled drug, where such provision is needed for the immediate treatment of that patient before a provision can otherwise be obtained, and
- (b) provide to a patient any appliance or drug, not being a Scheduled drug, which the doctor personally administers or applies to the patient.

(2) A doctor may only provide a restricted availability appliance if it is for a person or a purpose specified in the Drug Tariff.
