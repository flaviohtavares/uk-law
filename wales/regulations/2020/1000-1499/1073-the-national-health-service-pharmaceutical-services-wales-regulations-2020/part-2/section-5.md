> Text as at 2026-06-02.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Date by which the first pharmaceutical needs assessment is to be published[^key-886692f4370e90b503273cc566757967]

*Extent: E+W*

Each Local Health Board must publish its first pharmaceutical needs assessment within 12 months of the date on which these Regulations come into force.
