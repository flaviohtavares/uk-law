# The National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) (Wales) Regulations 2020, section 1

> Text as at 2026-06-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Title, commencement and application[^key-3300d7bd66cedff17eed9c0cca6913c5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The title of these Regulations is the National Health Service (Pharmaceutical Services) (Wales) Regulations 2020.

(2) The following provisions come into force on 1 October 2020—

- (a) this Part;
- (b) Parts 2 to 4;
- (c) Parts 9 to 11.

(3) Parts 5 to 8 come into force on 1 October 2021.

(4) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.
