## 14. Appeals against decisions under Part 4[^key-c497ed87ab28dfc22cbf7e6c24f46e85]

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 1 and 2 of Schedule 4 make provision for appeals to the Welsh Ministers in respect of decisions made under this Part.
