# The Health Protection (Coronavirus Restrictions) (No. 5) (Wales) Regulations 2020, section 45

> Text as at 2022-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45. Arrest without warrant[^key-8d20a03967e4ba3b4c49be96537b269c]

*Extent: E+W*

Section 24 of the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 [^c24382741] applies in relation to an offence under these Regulations as if the reasons in subsection (5) included—

- (a) to maintain public health;
- (b) to maintain public order.
