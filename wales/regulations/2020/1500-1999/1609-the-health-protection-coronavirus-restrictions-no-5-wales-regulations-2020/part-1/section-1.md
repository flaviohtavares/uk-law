# The Health Protection (Coronavirus Restrictions) (No. 5) (Wales) Regulations 2020, section 1

> Text as at 2022-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Title, application and coming into force[^key-1c5cff11af98039bb688d6c4e22c28ac]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Health Protection (Coronavirus Restrictions) (No. 5) (Wales) Regulations 2020.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

(3) These Regulations, other than Part 6, come into force on 20 December 2020[^key-31e14c93da2f2be140caac1f7d0e5805].

(4) Part 6 comes into force on 23 December 2020.
