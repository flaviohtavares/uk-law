# The Health Protection (Coronavirus Restrictions) (No. 5) (Wales) Regulations 2020, section 58

> Text as at 2022-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Revocation[^c-6d92c154615738907e807ffa28741c0f]

*Extent: E+W*

The following Regulations are revoked—

- (a) the Health Protection (Coronavirus Restrictions) (No. 4) (Wales) Regulations 2020, other than regulation 48;
- (b) regulation 4 of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel and Restrictions) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2020 [^c24382931];
- (c) regulation 6 of the Health Protection (Coronavirus, International Travel and Restrictions) (Amendment) (No. 2) (Wales) Regulations 2020 [^c24382941].
