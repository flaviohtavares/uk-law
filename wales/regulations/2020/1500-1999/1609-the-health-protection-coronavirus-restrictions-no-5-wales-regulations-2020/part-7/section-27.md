# The Health Protection (Coronavirus Restrictions) (No. 5) (Wales) Regulations 2020, section 27

> Text as at 2022-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. Compliance notices[^c-840a497dcd045f646de35de14be9d57d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An enforcement officer may give a compliance notice to a person if the officer has reasonable grounds for suspecting that the person is contravening a requirement in—

- (za)[^c-8624948e14d5851422c081148b294372] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (a)[^c-6788a0e973be50c573a325bb646afa1e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) paragraph 7(1) [^c-b518067ef7413de87ec0da50cf06832e]... of Schedule 1,
- (c) paragraph 7(1) [^c-d3e06454acb6c0e4f645dec7104a2637]... of Schedule 2,
- (d) paragraph 7(1), 8(1) or 9(1)[^c-6def6f63a648cfdd7df0e0eccc7cc782] of Schedule 3, or
- (e) paragraph 7(1), 8(1), 9(1) , 10(1) or 11(3)[^M_F_8a76a52b-0120-44e0-f510-7083b18395f0] of Schedule 4.

(2) A compliance notice may specify measures that the person to whom it is given must take as soon as is reasonably practicable so as to prevent that person from continuing to contravene the requirement.
