## SCHEDULE 7 — Regulated premises

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 15

[^key-564d469848ef90f7f3a740c981be3013]*Food and drink businesses*

**1.**[^key-9201ad11ad1871ab674ca8fa561112a2] Bars (including bars in members' clubs).

**2.**[^key-3831a2dcc12e92c756167dfbcbc47261] Public houses.

**3.**[^key-6aa3cf21d23e41c08394df692f89c969] Cafes, canteens and restaurants (including workplace canteens and dining rooms in members' clubs).

*Holiday and travel accommodation*

**4.**[^key-b4c8ca1acd1ba6fce354c3d4bed6c8b6] Camping sites.

**5.**[^key-69057f2b1130c506e827932e8ac521fb] Holiday sites.

**6.**[^key-5890ed424b86e3fa913f05eacdc7bd4b] Hotels and bed and breakfast accommodation.

**7.**[^key-952de563220edabce6e5a408c267b611] Other holiday accommodation (including holiday apartments, hostels and boarding houses).

*Public services etc.*

**8.**[^key-912cf2b463e87fceaf067f4e03197524] Medical or health services.

**9.**[^key-ebc3496c5f6a6449fff780c7e8e06329] Recycling and waste centres.

**10.**[^key-84ac691ff0e82b3ac93ab7a1241cf357] Community centres.

**11.**[^key-fa30c7f508e7bd0e0cd4f9f4a52995b4] Libraries and archive services.

**12.**[^key-9c674c0a112d69a52441d29d2c3c8295] Places of worship.

**13.**[^key-637ac6239044d435aa8e2fc5aaf5700d] Funeral directors.

**14.**[^key-c3c4c0e85e63919d61f5711404c4da7b] Crematoriums.

**15.**[^key-c107b80d72a583cd9702c8de178c3f9b] Veterinary surgeons.

*Personal services etc.*

**16.**[^key-ab4f7fabb261ddc8c5e88b88c538f5cc] Hair salons and barbers.

**17.**[^key-c4bf948c5131910d0189937435a5e3f2] Nail and beauty salons including tanning and electrolysis services.

**18.**[^key-e77d00b42c44e924d558d15e21177b38] Body piercings and tattooing services.

*Leisure and social etc.*

**19.**[^key-af3073b9fb9e5b1ebf12614f840c985c] Cinemas.

**20.**[^key-d50668e7abedd2a25d1df95690a08fe2] Concert halls and theatres.

**21.**[^key-c0cdd76f096256fe039f5ad4735068d6] Casinos.

**22.**[^key-3d3961754b1ecca57901219eb61301dc] Bingo halls.

**23.**[^key-92c8ea0b203d7ceef1ecd8a45125ca72] Amusement arcades.

**24.**[^key-230dd07b3977b883c2d7053a7f797af1] Bowling alleys.

**25.**[^key-8c71e1c7e36003ee257df262dc913cce] Indoor play centres or areas.

**26.**[^key-9dd0b22fd1c985695bc8269b964bfd01] Playgrounds.

**27.**[^key-8eb73cc8fa09fdc525d6f2c2860dcaf2] Funfairs, amusement parks and theme parks.

**28.**[^key-bbdb247d316eaf5fc80280132069f829] Holiday, leisure activity or events businesses.

**29.**[^key-930db230ddfaf70c8fbba6dd77750c2c] Museums and galleries.

**30.**[^key-e904d9434d8d0e90faaa6695188d26ad] Ice skating[^key-f2cce0071979920ccbd4e7a2382ec7ba] rinks.

**31.**[^key-4fe6515bb54a1690ba1f85dbb34aec6b] Trampoline parks and centres.

**32.**[^key-e1582314a92f2732f217e25b34ea0fb3] Indoor skate parks and centres.

**33.**[^key-9a0cd893c3c589569974831c4b85dff7] Spas.

**34.**[^key-68fa000587d4818b8c52c1a772dd7b2a] Venues for events or conferences (including venues for weddings).

**35.**[^key-02892df5dc406ed34e728cd38e4863d0] Visitor attractions.

*Sports and exercise*

**36.**[^key-3fb42dd86bfb38ff14d1a0f1d3fd19ef] Sports or exercise facilities, including indoor fitness studios and gyms.

**37.**[^key-14da69749430b5b325758fa8baa10112] Swimming pools.

**38.**[^key-e11bf613ac9061562e12ad522e45eefc] Sports courts, bowling greens, golf courses and enclosed sports grounds or pitches (whether outdoors or indoors).

*Retail etc.*

**39.**[^key-c721509d637ecac39e6ce0359ea34a35] Any business offering goods or services for sale or hire in retail premises, including—

- (a) auction houses;
- (b) car dealerships;
- (c) markets;
- (d) betting shops;
- (e) garden centres and plant nurseries;
- (f) pharmacies (including non-dispensing pharmacies) and chemists;
- (g) banks, building societies and other financial services providers;
- (h) post offices;
- (i) car repair and MOT services;
- (j) livestock markets or auctions;
- (k) laundrettes and dry cleaners;
- (l) petrol stations;
- (m) taxi or vehicle hire businesses.

**40.**[^key-04475478c80ed76f29b832b4a7a67cca] Shopping centres and shopping arcades.

**41.**[^key-ad5fbd62a7c3c9559a51a37070efc2eb] Storage and distribution facilities, including delivery drop off points.

**42.**[^key-ff7f3fa943669e2476cf998e37e0a8aa] Estate or letting agents, developer sales offices and show homes.
