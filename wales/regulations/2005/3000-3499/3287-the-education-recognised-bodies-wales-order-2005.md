# The Education (Recognised Bodies) (Wales) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 3287 (W.250)

EDUCATION, WALES

The Education (Recognised Bodies) (Wales) Order 2005

Made

29 November 2005

Coming into force

1 December 2005

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 216(1) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001] and now vested in the National Assembly for Wales, the National Assembly for Wales hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title, commencement and application

(1) The title of this Order is the Education (Recognised Bodies) (Wales) Order 2005 and it comes into force on 1 December 2005.

(2) This Order applies to Wales.

## 2. Recognised Bodies

The bodies that are specified in the Schedule to this Order are designated as bodies that appear to the National Assembly for Wales to be recognised bodies.

## 3. Revocation

The Education (Recognised Bodies) (Wales) Order 2003[^f00002] is revoked.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00003].

D. Elis-Thomas

The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

29 November 2005

This Order lists all those bodies that appear to the National Assembly for Wales to be recognised bodies within section 214(2)(a) or (b) of the Education Reform Act 1988. These are universities, colleges or other bodies which are authorised by Royal Charter or by or under Act of Parliament to grant degrees and other bodies for the time being permitted by those bodies to act on their behalf in the granting of degrees. An award granted by such a body is not an award of a kind referred to in section 214(1) which makes it an offence to grant, offer to grant, or issue any invitation relating to certain unrecognised degrees and awards.

This Order updates and replaces the list of bodies contained in the Education (Recognised Bodies) (Wales) Order 2003. It includes a number of institutions which have been authorised to award degrees under section 76 of the Further and Higher Education Act [1992 (c. 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/13), and section 48 of the [Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act (c.37)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/37). It also includes a number of schools, colleges and institutes of London University which have been permitted by the University to award University of London degrees.
