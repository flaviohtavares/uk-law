# The National Council for Education and Training for Wales (Transfer of Functions to the National Assembly for Wales and Abolition) Order 2005, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2005/3238
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Provision for continuity in the exercise of functions[^key-647227678ee4ffb2c046aa009428ef37]

*Extent: E+W*

Anything done by or in relation to the Council which has effect immediately before the transfer date shall so far as it is required for continuing its effect on and after the transfer date have effect as if done by or in relation to the National Assembly.
