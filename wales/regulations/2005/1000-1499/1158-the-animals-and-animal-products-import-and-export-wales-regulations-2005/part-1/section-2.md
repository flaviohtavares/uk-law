# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Exception

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), these Regulations do not apply to veterinary checks on movements of pets (other than equidae) accompanied by and under the responsibility of a natural person, where such movements are not the subject of a commercial transaction.

(2) Where any person is accompanying and has under his or her responsibility more than five pets travelling together which —

- (a) are each of a species listed in Annex I to Regulation [(EC) No. 998/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0998)[^f00009]; and
- (b) come from a third country other than one listed in section 2 of part B of Annex II to Regulation [(EC) No. 998/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2003/0998),

these Regulations apply to the veterinary checks on the movements of those animals, notwithstanding that their movement is not the subject of a commercial transaction.
