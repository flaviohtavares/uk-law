# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 35

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 35. Revocations

The following Regulations are revoked so far as they apply in relation to Wales—

- (a) The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000[^f00024];
- (b) Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2000[^f00025];
- (c) Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) (Amendment) (No. 1) Regulations 2000[^f00026];
- (d) Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2000[^f00027];
- (e) Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2000[^f00028];
- (f) Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2002[^f00029];and
- (g) Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (England and Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2002[^f00030].
