# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 28

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 28. Notification of decisions

If the consignor or the consignor’s representative, or the importer or the importer’s representative, so requests, any decision taken refusing entry or varying the conditions of entry must be forwarded to him or her in writing by the person taking the decision, giving the reasons for the decision and the details of any right of appeal against the decision, including the relevant time limits.
