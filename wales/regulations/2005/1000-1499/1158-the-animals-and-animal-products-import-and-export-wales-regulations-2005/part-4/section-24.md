# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 24

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 24. Application of Part 4

This Part applies in respect of imported animals which originate outside the European Community but in respect of which all the checks required under Council Directive [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0496) have been carried out in another member State.
