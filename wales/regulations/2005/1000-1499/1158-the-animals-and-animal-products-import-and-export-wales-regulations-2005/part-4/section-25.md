# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 25

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 25. Imports

A person must not import any animal to which this Part applies unless it is accompanied by the common veterinary entry document and the authenticated copy of the original health certificate issued at the point of importation into the European Community under Article 7(1) of Council Directive [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0496) and Article 3 of Regulation [(EC) No. 282/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0282).
