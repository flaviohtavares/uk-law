# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Application of Part 3

(1) This Part applies in respect of animals imported into —

- (a) from a country other than a member State, and
- (b) from another member State if the animal originated in a country which is not a member State and all the checks provided for in Council Directive [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0496) have not been carried out.
