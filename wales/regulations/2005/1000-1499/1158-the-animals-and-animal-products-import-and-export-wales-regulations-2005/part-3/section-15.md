# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 15

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 15. Official veterinarians

The National Assembly must periodically designate such veterinary inspectors to act as official veterinarians as are necessary for the purposes of this Part and may revoke such designation at any time.
