# The Animals and Animal Products (Import and Export) (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Payment of fees

The official veterinarian must not authorise the release of animals from a quarantine centre or border inspection post unless he or she is satisfied that all veterinary checks for which a charge is made have been paid for, and that, where relevant, a deposit covering any cost provided for in Article 9(1)(a), 9(2), the second and third indents of Article 10(1), Article 10(6) and Article 12(2) of Council Directive [91/496/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0496) has been lodged.
