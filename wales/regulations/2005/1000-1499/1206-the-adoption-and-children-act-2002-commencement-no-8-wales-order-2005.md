# The Adoption and Children Act 2002 (Commencement No. 8) (Wales) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 1206 (W.78) (C.54)

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS, WALES

The Adoption and Children Act 2002 (Commencement No. 8) (Wales) Order 2005

Made

19 April 2005

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by section 148(6) of the Adoption and Children Act 2002[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order —

## 1. Name, interpretation and application

(1) This Order is called the Adoption and Children Act 2002 (Commencement No. 8) (Wales) Order 2005.

(2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

## 2. Appointed Day

6 June 2005 is the appointed day for the coming into force of sections 3(3) and (4) of the Adoption and Children Act 2002.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00002].

D. Elis-Thomas

The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

19 April 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the eighth Commencement Order made under the Adoption and Children Act 2002 (“the Act”) and applies to Wales only.

The Order brings into force on 6 June 2005 sections 3(3) and 3(4) of the Act.

(This Note is not part of the Order)
