# The Adoption Agencies (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 7

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Adoption agency arrangements for adoption work[^c-5887cb54b03fbd74d66f463982ca4e94]

*Extent: E+W*

An adoption agency must, in consultation with such persons in the central list as the agency considers appropriate and, to the extent specified in regulation 8(4) with the agency’s medical adviser, prepare and implement a written policy and procedural instructions governing the exercise of functions of the agency and an adoption panel in relation to adoption and such instructions must be kept under review and, where appropriate, revised by the agency.
