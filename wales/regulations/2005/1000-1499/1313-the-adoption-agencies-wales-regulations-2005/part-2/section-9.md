# The Adoption Agencies (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 9

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Advice to adoption panels in adoptions with a foreign element[^key-5887cb54b03fbd74d66f463982ca4e94]

*Extent: E+W*

The adoption agency must ensure that adoption panels are properly advised by an appropriately qualified person in relation to adoptions with a foreign element if such a case is being considered.
