# The Adoption Agencies (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 1

> Text as at 2023-11-06.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application[^key-53ae0659a894766f52197cafaeb5dd20]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Adoption Agencies (Wales) Regulations 2005 and come into force on 30 December 2005.

(2) These Regulations apply to Wales.
