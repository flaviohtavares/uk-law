# The Adoption Agencies (Wales) Regulations 2005, SCHEDULE 5

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 5 — INFORMATION ABOUT THE CHILD TO BE GIVEN TO THE PROSPECTIVE ADOPTER

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 32(1)

**1.**[^c-ff2fb586483a2ab20f7c887b6a1012a8] Details of the child.

**2.**[^c-563dc4702471edcb723b157b85b209ee] Photograph and physical description.

**3.**[^c-f3ac23a8b104dfc4749e789dceff084d] Details of the child’s family circumstances and home environment, including details of the child’s family (parents, siblings and significant others).

**4.**[^c-302a1b8852d4fc5dd37b73f72a734f92] Chronology of the child’s care.

**5.**[^c-7b7a745b083174dfd988238d6bbe972a] The child’s behaviour, how the child interacts with other children and relates to adults.

**6.**[^c-ccec980b4087977c914b20cbaf9e3893] Whether the child is looked after [^c-9feccec69fc930da6033ff88cc4dc747]... and, if so, the reasons and why the child is to be placed for adoption.

**7.**[^c-4a697590f78f765f23c5126592d77c53] Details of the child’s placement history including reasons for any placement breakdowns.

**8.**[^c-a1313b90eea7e4eee7596a85bdaa0397] Details of the child’s state of health, health history and any need for health care which might arise in the future.

**9.**[^c-6ff551dfc328b4711704fb815364a7c8] Details of the child’s educational history, a summary of the child’s progress to date and whether assessed or likely to be assessed for special educational needs under the Education Act 1996.

**10.**[^c-478c46cdd1c89af2b02de9888549e440] The child’s ascertainable wishes and feelings in relation to adoption, and contact with the child’s parent, guardian, relative or other significant person.

**11.**[^c-4216441260664f5f3356d309962aa5f6] The wishes and feelings of the child’s parent, guardian, relative or other significant person in relation to adoption and contact.

**12.**[^c-c02c0452ec6330fe703cd2a68cad14c4] The views of the person with whom the child is living about adoption.

**13.**[^c-0accc467b467c11d565eaaf0eb2c3ffe] The assessment of the child’s needs for adoption support services and the agency’s proposals for meeting those needs.

**14.**[^c-79375c117013fc690ab78d36e1a1746d] The agency’s proposals for allowing any person contact with the child.

**15.**[^c-aea2135f61facac6b032612bb082c760] The proposed time-scale for placement.

**16.**[^c-94ea8b4a0a8363e3cde931d59b561b48] Any other information which the agency considers relevant.
