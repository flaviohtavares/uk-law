# The Adoption Agencies (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 16

> Text as at 2023-11-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 16. Requirement to obtain information (including health information) about the child’s family[^c-a3c4c57ccc5c22dde31486b85faa04e9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The adoption agency must, so far as is reasonably practicable, obtain the information about the child’s family which is specified in Parts 3 and 4 of Schedule 1.

(2) The adoption agency must, so far as is reasonably practicable, obtain the information about the health of each of the child’s birth[^c-f18a3b33197c4801e94ebc69b1a97247] parents and brothers and sisters which is specified in Part 5 of Schedule 1.
