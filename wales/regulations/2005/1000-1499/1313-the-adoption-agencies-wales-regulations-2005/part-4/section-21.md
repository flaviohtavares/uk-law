## 21. Registration of interest in adoption[^c-7f90cd67511442ae5b44192f37dfdffe]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations 22 to 27 apply when a person has notified an adoption agency that they want to adopt a child and the agency has notified that person that it has decided to proceed with the pre-assessment process in respect of that person.
