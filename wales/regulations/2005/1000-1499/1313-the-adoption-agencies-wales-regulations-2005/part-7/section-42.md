> Text as at 2023-11-06.

## 42. Confidentiality of case records[^c-5dd1e5b760b77ab2e64a4db5a2b89838]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to regulation 43, any information obtained or reports, recommendations or decisions made by virtue of these Regulations must be treated by the adoption agency as confidential.
