> Text as at 2023-11-06.

## 45. Application of regulations 41 to 43[^key-4623bdfa0c29decda9e10d124ba235dc]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations 41 to 43 do not apply to case records which are subject to the regulations made under sections 56 to 68 of the Act.
