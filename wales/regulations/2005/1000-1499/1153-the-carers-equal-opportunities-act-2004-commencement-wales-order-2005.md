# The Carers (Equal Opportunities) Act 2004 (Commencement) (Wales) Order 2005

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2005 No. 1153 (W.70) (C.53)

SOCIAL SERVICES, WALES

The Carers (Equal Opportunities) Act 2004 (Commencement) (Wales) Order 2005

Made

12 April 2005

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by section 6(3) of the Carers (Equal Opportunities) Act 2004[^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation

This Order may be cited as the Carers (Equal Opportunities) Act 2004 (Commencement) (Wales) Order 2005.

## 2. Appointed day

18 April 2005 is the day appointed for sections 1 to 5 of the Carers (Equal Opportunities) Act 2004 to come into force in relation to Wales.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00002]

D. Elis-Thomas

The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

12 April 2005

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings the Carers (Equal Opportunities) Act 2004 into force in relation to Wales on 18 April 2005.
