# The Government of Maintained Schools (Wales) Regulations 2005, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2020-12-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Election of teacher and staff governors

*Extent: E+W*

(Regulations 5 and 6)

**1.**[^key-15657c1926246c49de43b2aab30e00d3] In this Schedule “appropriate authority” (“*awdurdod priodol*”) has the same meaning as in Schedule 1.

**2.**[^key-c6552a730aa2a9d385edbd0f28b91b9a] Where a local authority[^key-7392db45f0ca293cc83c43ddea16e50c] is the appropriate authority in relation to a school, that authority may delegate to the head teacher of the school any of its functions under this Schedule.

**3.**[^key-9c1ee1f48f6089a849f28e55b54f4060] Subject to paragraphs 4 to 6, the appropriate authority must make all the necessary arrangements for the election of teacher and staff governors.

**4.**[^key-a1297c9403c1cf659c5905c12b584dd1] The appropriate authority is to determine for the purposes of an election of teacher or staff governors whether a person is a school teacher or otherwise employed to work at the school.

**5.**[^key-0f9419c9bc362185ea01b4db4ddb7f9f] The power conferred by paragraph 3—

- (a) does not include power to impose any requirements as to the minimum number of votes required to be cast for a candidate to be elected, but
- (b) does include the power to make provision as to qualifying dates.

**6.**[^key-e48a89823517c3647569b70ff810a8dd] Any election which is contested must be held by secret ballot.
