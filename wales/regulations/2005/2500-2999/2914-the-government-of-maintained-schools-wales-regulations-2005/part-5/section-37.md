# The Government of Maintained Schools (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 37

> Text as at 2020-12-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Duty to secure making of instruments of government[^key-c6092f79d2d81b2a4267d9e0fc3a7bad]

*Extent: E+W*

A local authority[^key-a944f274d91baf78f1ce6acb913f655b] must make an instrument of government in accordance with these Regulations—

- (a) for each nursery school which is maintained by them no later than 31 March 2006, and
- (b) for all other schools maintained by them, no later than 31 August 2008.
