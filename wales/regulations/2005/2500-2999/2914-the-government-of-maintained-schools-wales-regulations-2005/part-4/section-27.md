# The Government of Maintained Schools (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 27

> Text as at 2020-12-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. Removal of authority, foundation, representative, additional community and sponsor governors[^key-d0c211d66df7025f3a27c302d67e5f06][^key-eab23fc155e5f50b1b66ea6804bb35b9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any authority governor[^key-a35742c49761905a1b45914a2465403b], foundation governor, or representative governor may be removed from office by the person who appointed him or her, who must give written notice thereof to the clerk to the governing body and to the governor so removed.

(2) An *ex officio* foundation governor may be removed from office by the person named in the instrument of government as the person entitled to remove him or her, and that person must give written notice thereof to the clerk to the governing body and to the governor so removed.

(3) Any additional community governor or sponsor governor may be removed by the person who nominated him or her, who must give written notice thereof to the clerk to the governing body and to the governor so removed.
