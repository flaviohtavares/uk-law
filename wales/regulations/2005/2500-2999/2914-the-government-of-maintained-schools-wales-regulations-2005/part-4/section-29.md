# The Government of Maintained Schools (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 29

> Text as at 2020-12-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29. Removal of appointed parent governors, partnership governors and associate pupil governors:[^key-be810cf0acf071407581c26e7d3cec2f][^key-fb561615684f3d8d80b2606bd2e42183]

*Extent: E+W*

Any parent governor appointed by the governing body under paragraphs 10 to 12 of Schedule 1, any partnership governor and any associate pupil governor may be removed by the governing body in accordance with the procedure set out in regulation 30.
