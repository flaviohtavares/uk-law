# The Government of Maintained Schools (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 12A

> Text as at 2020-12-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12A. Associate pupil governors[^key-2169953d10989adbe69a8b03c5fe9f5c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In these Regulations “**associate pupil governor**” means a registered pupil nominated by the school council to be a member of the governing body and appointed as such by the governing body in accordance with regulation 7 of the School Councils (Wales) Regulations 2005.

(2) The maximum number of associate pupil governors on any governing body is two.
