# The Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004 (Consequential Amendments) (Wales) Order 2005

> Text as at 2007-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

2005 No. 2929 (W.214)

FIRE AND RESCUE SERVICES, WALES

The Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004 (Consequential Amendments) (Wales) Order 2005

Made

19 October 2005

Coming into force

25 October 2005

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 53(2), 60 and 62 of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004[^f00001], hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Title, commencement and application[^c-adfb86e3aeea1aa64c5aa045f4fa5a66]

(1) The title of this Order is the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004 (Consequential Amendments) (Wales) Order 2005 and comes into force on 25 October 2005.

(2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

## 2. Local Government Act 2003[^c-3aa8e76483e6ca7ba19f90357b34c597]

In section 83(2) of the Local Government Act 2003[^f00002], for the words “fire authorities” substitute “fire and rescue authorities”.
