# The Hazardous Waste (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 9

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Specific waste to be treated as non-hazardous

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Assembly may decide, in exceptional cases, on the basis of documentary evidence provided by the holder, and having regard to Annex[^key-d189fadb4a05e663006f9b819253c893] III and the limit values of concentration in the List of Wastes, that a specific batch of waste in Wales which—

- (a) is listed as hazardous waste in the List of Wastes;
- (b)[^key-23c02f36aad7d976c188aad4506947d2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) through of a type not listed as a hazardous waste in the List of Wastes, is treated as hazardous pursuant to regulation 8(2),

does not display any of the properties listed in Annex III [^key-2a855a4a0ef86f45ea81d939d6462cd4]...and accordingly that it is to be treated for all purposes as non-hazardous in Wales.

(1A)[^key-6f3dfda8f9da2481623a9c1c7748c217] The power at paragraph (1) to decide that waste be treated as non-hazardous does not apply to waste which has been diluted or mixed with the aim of lowering the initial concentrations of hazardous substances to a level below the thresholds for defining waste as hazardous.

(2) A specific batch of waste produced in England, Scotland or Northern Ireland and listed as a hazardous waste in the List of Wastes and which is for the time being determined [^key-53862dea14561abcc162f5d52ff5163b]... to be non-hazardous pursuant to paragraph (3)[^key-3728213a05505e3776750629b7cceb1d], is, subject to any determination made under regulation 8, to be treated for all purposes as non-hazardous in Wales.

(3)[^key-4b6d9395b353763129a6078c1a43e1cd] For the purposes of paragraph (2), a specific batch of waste is determined to be non-hazardous if it is the subject of a decision—

- (a) in relation to England, by the Secretary of State under regulation 9 of the Hazardous Waste (England and Wales) Regulations 2005;
- (b) in relation to Northern Ireland, by the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs under regulation 10 of the Hazardous Waste Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005;
- (c) in relation to Scotland, by the Scottish Ministers that the Scottish Ministers consider that the waste displays none of the hazardous properties listed in Annex III.
