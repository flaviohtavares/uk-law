# The Hazardous Waste (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 58

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. NRBW to retain registers etc[^c-b8dc88f030cff73662d4ada32d5fd0fa]

*Extent: E+W*

The NRBW must retain registers and any accompanying records sent to it pursuant to regulation 51(3) for a period of not less than three years commencing with the receipt thereof by the NRBW.
