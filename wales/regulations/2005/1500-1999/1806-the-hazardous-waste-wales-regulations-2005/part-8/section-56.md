# The Hazardous Waste (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 56

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 56. Inspections of hazardous waste producers

*Extent: E+W*

It is the duty of the NRBW[^c-5c7483f4d012be10285b681ecc8cd37d] to carry out appropriate periodic inspections of hazardous waste producers.
