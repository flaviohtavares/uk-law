## 60. Provision of information to the National Assembly

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The NRBW[^c-857643f70dfafc5325b7fa35473b3ab9] must inform the Assembly each year of any changes in the following information for every establishment or undertaking which carries out disposal or recovery of hazardous waste principally on behalf of third parties [^c-995b379ed09c323b6e7c5ad80aea6615]...

- (a) name and address;
- (b) the method used to treat waste; and
- (c) the types and quantities of waste which can be treated.
