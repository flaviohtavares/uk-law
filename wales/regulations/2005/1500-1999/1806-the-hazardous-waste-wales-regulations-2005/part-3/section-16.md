# The Hazardous Waste (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 16

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 16. Agricultural waste

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations do not apply to agricultural waste before 15 May 2007[^c-845832758339b9c5c921f26fc4d09c00], but apply on and after that date to agricultural waste whenever it became waste.

(2) For the purpose of this Regulation, “**agricultural waste**” means waste from premises used for agriculture within the meaning of the Agriculture Act 1947 [^c15550791].
