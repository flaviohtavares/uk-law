# The Hazardous Waste (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 25

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. Notification by consignor

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A consignor who proposes to remove, or cause to be removed, any hazardous waste from any premises must notify those premises to the NRBW[^key-770b01db0d0b557943370fcb7bebfce0] if—

- (a) they are the site premises and the producer is not known or cannot be located without unreasonable inconvenience or expense; or
- (b) the waste was not produced at those premises.
