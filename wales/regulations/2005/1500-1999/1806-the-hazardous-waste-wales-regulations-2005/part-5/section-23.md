# The Hazardous Waste (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 23

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Exemption from the requirement to notify[^key-56174c00a24e84aa3787c9457099b8c7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Exempt premises need not be notified to the NRBW[^key-770b01db0d0b557943370fcb7bebfce0] pursuant to this Part.

(2) In these Regulations, premises are exempt premises if they comply with the condition in paragraph (3) and with either of the conditions in paragraph (4) or (5).

(3) The condition in this paragraph is that no hazardous waste is removed from the premises by any person other than—

- (a) a carrier registered under the Control of Pollution (Amendment) Act 1989; or
- (b) a carrier exempt from the requirement to register under that Act.

(4) The condition in this paragraph is that the premises are a ship.

(5) The condition in this paragraph is that the qualifying limitation applies.
