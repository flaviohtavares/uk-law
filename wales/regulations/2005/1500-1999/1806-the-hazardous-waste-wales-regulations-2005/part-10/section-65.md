# The Hazardous Waste (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 65

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 65. Offences

*Extent: E+W*

It is an offence for a person to fail to comply with any requirement imposed on that person by or under the following provisions of these Regulations—

- (a) Part 4;
- (b) regulations 21, 22 , 24, 25 and 26;
- (c) regulations 34 to 43[^c-9a309a8c4670412f01b71aea39ac035e];
- (d) regulation 46 and Schedule 7;
- (e) Part 7 (with the exception of regulation 52); and
- (f) regulation 62.
