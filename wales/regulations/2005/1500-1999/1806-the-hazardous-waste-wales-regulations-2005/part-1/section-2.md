# The Hazardous Waste (Wales) Regulations 2005, section 2

> Text as at 2024-01-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. The Waste Directive and the meaning of Waste[^key-ad44d5d0ed4fdf78ce25b9f452c91307]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of these Regulations—

- (a)[^key-57856da8b8bd022d20350edffbc7714e] “the Waste Directive” (“*y Gyfarwyddeb Wastraff*”) means [Directive 2008/98/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2008/0098) of the European Parliament and of the Council on waste and repealing certain Directives, as last amended by Directive (EU) 2018/851[^key-a11826b01f0242797b2220795c2c528a] , and read in accordance with regulation 2A[^key-a37601eb548bee403edef4ea067bf0c8];
- (b) “waste” (“*gwastraff*”) means anything that—
  - (i) is waste within the meaning of Article 3(1) of the Waste Directive , as read with Articles 5 and 6 of that Directive[^key-7abe4fbbf32252f116c879dffa13cc43]; and
  - (ii) subject to regulation 15, is not excluded from the scope of that Directive by Article 2(1), (2) or (3).

(2) In these Regulations, a reference to the Waste Directive conditions is a reference to the conditions set out in Article 13 of that Directive, that is to say, to ensure that waste management is carried out without endangering human health, without harming the environment and, in particular—

- (a) without risk to water, air, soil, plants or animals;
- (b) without causing a nuisance through noise or odours; and
- (c) without adversely affecting the countryside or places of special interest.
