# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) Order 2006, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. Veterinary surveillance of birds

The National Assembly must ensure that birds on special category premises which are not killed under paragraph 5 of Schedule 3 of the Act are monitored for avian influenza.
