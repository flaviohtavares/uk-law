# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) Order 2006, section 88

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 88. Revocations and amendments

(1) The Diseases of Poultry (Wales) Order 2003[^f00012] is amended by inserting after article 1(2)—

> (3) This Order does not apply in relation to avian influenza.

(2) In article 2 of the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978[^f00013], the definition of “Diseases of Poultry Order” is substituted by—

> - “Diseases of Poultry Order” means the Diseases of Poultry (Wales) Order 2003 and the Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) Order 2006;

.
