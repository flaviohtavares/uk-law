# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) Order 2006, section 87

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 87. Enforcement

(1) The local authority must, subject to paragraph (2), enforce this Order.

(2) The National Assembly may, in relation to particular cases or to other cases of a particular description, direct that it will enforce this Order.
