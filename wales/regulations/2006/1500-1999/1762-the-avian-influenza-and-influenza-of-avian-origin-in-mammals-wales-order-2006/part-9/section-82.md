# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) Order 2006, section 82

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 82. Inspection of vehicles

For the purposes of section 65A of the Act (inspection of vehicles), any controlled zone is a designated area until it is ended or becomes part of another zone.
