# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) Order 2006, section 73

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 73. Provision of information

No person is to furnish information which he or she knows to be false or misleading to a person executing this Order.
