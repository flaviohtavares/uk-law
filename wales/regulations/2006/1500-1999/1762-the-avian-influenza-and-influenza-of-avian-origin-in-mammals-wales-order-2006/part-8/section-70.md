# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) Order 2006, section 70

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 70. Measures at other restocked premises

The National Assembly may carry out the measures set out in articles 68 and 69 at other restocked premises where poultry or other captive birds are kept.
