# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) Order 2006, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Restrictions where avian influenza is suspected in transit

A person exercising powers under articles 10 to 13 must take into account any measures taken in respect of vehicles under articles 38, 42, 43 and 45.
