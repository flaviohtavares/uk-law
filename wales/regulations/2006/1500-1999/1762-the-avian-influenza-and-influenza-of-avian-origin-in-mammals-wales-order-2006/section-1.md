# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) Order 2006, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

**1.** This Order transposes Council Directive [2005/94/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0094) (OJ No L10, 14.1.2006, p16) (“the Directive”) on Community measures for the control of avian influenza, except for Chapter IX of the Directive. Chapter IX of the Directive deals with vaccination and will be transposed by the Avian Influenza (Vaccination) (Wales) Regulations 2006. This Order also amends the Diseases of Poultry (Wales) Order 2003 (S.I. [2003/1079](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/1079)) and the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Wales) Order 1978 (S.I. [1978/32](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1978/32)). The Directive repeals Council Directive [92/40/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0040).
