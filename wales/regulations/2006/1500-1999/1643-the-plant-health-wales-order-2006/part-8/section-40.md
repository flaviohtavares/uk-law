# The Plant Health (Wales) Order 2006, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Licences to carry out activities prohibited by this Order

Notwithstanding any of the provisions of this Order, any plant pest or relevant material may be landed, kept, stored, sold, planted, moved or otherwise disposed of in Wales and any other thing prohibited by this Order may be done under the authority of a licence, whether general or specific, granted by the National Assembly in exercise of any derogation permitted by Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029).
