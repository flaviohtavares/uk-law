# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 71

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 71. Designation of premises to which things may be moved

*Extent: E+W*

(1) On receipt of an application in writing by the occupier of the premises, the National Assembly may designate the following premises for the following purposes—

- (a) a slaughterhouse for the purpose of receiving poultry moved under a licence granted under this Order;
- (b) a hatchery for the purpose of receiving eggs moved under a licence granted under this Order;
- (c) an egg packing centre for the purpose of receiving eggs moved under a licence granted under this Order;
- (d) any premises for the purpose of receiving eggs moved under a licence granted under this Order for use for scientific, diagnostic or pharmaceutical purposes.

(2) The National Assembly must not make a designation unless it is satisfied that the risk of the transmission of avian influenza from the premises is minimal.

(3) The following premises are deemed to be designated under paragraph (1)(d), unless the National Assembly directs otherwise by notice to the occupier of the premises—

- (a) premises licensed to manufacture or assemble human vaccines under regulation 17 of the Human Medicines Regulations 2012[^key-e61213b13b925960a8df650ee56e9056];
- (b) premises authorised to manufacture animal vaccines under regulation 5 of the Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2013[^key-f3183c1ce978ab2cf0d4af69d88da339];
- (c) premises specified in a licence granted under section 2C[^key-e0b053084547accced8aac5955e45bfe] of the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986 [^c17275711];
- (d) premises licensed under article 4 of the Specified Animals Pathogens (Wales) Order 2008[^key-f22f13b3209f121a8d4431396182072c].
