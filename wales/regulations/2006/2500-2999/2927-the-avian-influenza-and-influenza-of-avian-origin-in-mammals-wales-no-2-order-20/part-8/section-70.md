# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 70

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 70. Measures at other restocked premises

*Extent: E+W*

The National Assembly may, in relation to other restocked premises where poultry or other captive birds are kept—

- (a) apply the measures in article 68; and
- (b) by notice to the occupier of the premises, require him or her to carry out the measures in article 69.
