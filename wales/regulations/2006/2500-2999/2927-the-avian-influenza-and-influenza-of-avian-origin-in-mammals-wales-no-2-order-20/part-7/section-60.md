# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 60

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 60. Movements off the premises

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), no person is to move any mammal tested in the course of an inquiry under article 12[^c-3408115e451967ca9ab2387976394f35] off the premises until a veterinary inspector has confirmed that the mammal is not infective and licenses the movement.

(2) A veterinary inspector may license the movement of pigs and other mammals in which infection with avian influenza virus[^c-29782667ac11c472f79f2e7affad8259] is confirmed to other premises (including slaughterhouses) if he or she is satisfied (as a result of further tests on the animals) that the movement will not significantly increase the risk of the spread of avian influenza virus[^c-da2355d6c5c7d624d66cce9473f46021].
