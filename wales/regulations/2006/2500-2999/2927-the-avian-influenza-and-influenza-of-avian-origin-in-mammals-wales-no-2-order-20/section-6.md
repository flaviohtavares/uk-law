# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 6

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**6.** Part 3 of the Order and Schedule 1 set out measures to deal with any suspected outbreak of avian influenza at premises other than slaughterhouses and border inspection posts. Measures when highly pathogenic avian influenza is confirmed on such premises are set out in Part 4 and Schedule 2. These measures may, under article 13, also be applied to premises where disease is suspected. Part 4 also provides for the declaration of protection zones, surveillance zones and restricted zones around infected premises. Measures in protection and surveillance zones are set out in Schedules 4 and 5.
