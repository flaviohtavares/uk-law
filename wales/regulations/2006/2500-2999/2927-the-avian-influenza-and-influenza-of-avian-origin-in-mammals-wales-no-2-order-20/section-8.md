# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 8

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**8.** Measures where low pathogenic avian influenza is confirmed at premises other than slaughterhouses and border inspection posts are set out in Part 6 and in Schedule 6. These include the declaration of low pathogenic avian influenza zones. Measures in such zones are set out in Schedule 7.
