# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 1

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**1.** This Order revokes and replaces the Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) Order 2006 (S.I. 2006/1762 (W. 184)) (“**the original Order**”). It continues to transpose Council Directive [2005/94/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2005/0094) on Community measures for the control of avian influenza and repealing Directive [92/40/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0040) (OJ No L10, 14.1.2006, p16), (“**the Directive**”), other than Chapter IX. Chapter IX of the Directive deals with vaccination and will be transposed by the Avian Influenza (Vaccination) (Wales) (No. 2) Regulations 2006. This Order also implements, in part—

- (a) Commission Decision [2005/734/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0734) laying down biosecurity measures to reduce the risk of transmission of highly pathogenic avian influenza caused by influenza A virus of subtype H5N1 from birds living in the wild to poultry and other captive birds and providing for an early detection system in areas at particular risk (OJ No L 274, 20.10.2005, p. 105, as amended by Commission Decisions [2005/745/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0745), [2005/855/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0855) and [2006/574/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0574)); and
- (b) Commission Decision [2006/474/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2006/0474) concerning measures to prevent the spread of highly pathogenic avian influenza caused by influenza A virus of subtype H5N1 to birds kept in zoos and approved bodies, institutes and centres in the Member States and repealing Decision [2005/744/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2005/0744) (OJ No L 187, 8.7.2006, p. 37).
