# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 25

> Text as at 2025-12-31.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. Veterinary inquiry at infected premises

*Extent: E+W*

The National Assembly must ensure that veterinary inquiries continue at all infected premises for such period as it considers necessary.
