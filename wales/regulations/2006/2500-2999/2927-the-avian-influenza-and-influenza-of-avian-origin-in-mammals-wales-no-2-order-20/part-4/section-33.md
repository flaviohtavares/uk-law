# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 33

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Alternative measures where avian influenza is confirmed at a hatchery or in other captive birds on special category premises

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If, following confirmation of highly pathogenic avian influenza at a hatchery or in any[^c-3cfe6a9b9264d2234d0d155a91fe01d4] other captive birds on special category premises, or any birds on special category premises listed in article 22(3)(a) to (e) or (h),[^c-59d7c6e90c35e1166e15321ffaa9eefa] the National Assembly is satisfied that applying less stringent measures than those in articles 28 to 31 would not endanger disease control, it may decide—

- (a) not to make a declaration of any controlled zone around the premises;
- (b) to declare fewer zones than required under article 28;
- (c) to declare a controlled zone smaller in size than provided for in article 29;
- (d) to specify in his declaration of a controlled zone that one or more of the measures in Schedules 4 and 5 do not apply in that zone.

(2) The National Assembly must carry out a risk assessment before reaching a decision under paragraph (1).
