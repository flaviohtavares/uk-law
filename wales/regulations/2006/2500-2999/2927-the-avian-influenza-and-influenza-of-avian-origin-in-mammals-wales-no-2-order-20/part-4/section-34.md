# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 34

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 34. Measures in restricted zones

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The National Assembly must, in a declaration of a restricted zone under article 28(5), apply, in each zone so declared, such measures as he considers necessary to reduce the risk of the spread of avian influenza virus[^key-9a9ff082912172a1a7c1be601827bdf3].

(2) These measures may include some or all of the measures in Schedules 4, 5 and 8 and articles 31A, 35 and 64[^key-e6308cf619000f13bc73935a7493df86].
