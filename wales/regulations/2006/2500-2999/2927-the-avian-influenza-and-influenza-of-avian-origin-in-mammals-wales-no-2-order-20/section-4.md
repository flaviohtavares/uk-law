# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 4

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**4.** Part 1 of the Order contains introductory provisions. Article 3 extends the definitions of “poultry”, “animals” and “disease” in the Animal Health Act 1981 (c. 22). It also applies the slaughter power in section 32 of that Act to any infection in mammals caused by influenza virus of avian origin.
