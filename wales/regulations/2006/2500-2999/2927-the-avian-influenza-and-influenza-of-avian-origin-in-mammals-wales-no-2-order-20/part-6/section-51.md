# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 51

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 51. Veterinary surveillance of poultry or other captive birds[^c-0dff04dcba73aa1c34b38fe03f403b02]

*Extent: E+W*

The National Assembly must ensure that poultry or other captive[^c-58f1c735a5783de7a0f4c1a0965484c1] birds on infected premises which are special category premises not killed under paragraph 5 of Schedule 3 of the Act are monitored for avian influenza.
