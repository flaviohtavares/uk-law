# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 10

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**10.** General measures applicable on suspicion or confirmation of avian influenza are set out in Part 8 and measures on inspection and enforcement are set out in Part 9. Article 86(2) provides for notices, licences and designations under the original Order to remain in force as if they were notices, licences or designations under this Order.
