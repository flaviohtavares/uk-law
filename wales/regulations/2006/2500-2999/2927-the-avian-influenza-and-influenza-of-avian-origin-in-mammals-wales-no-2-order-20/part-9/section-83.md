# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 83

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 83. Powers of inspectors in case of default

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If any person fails to comply with a requirement of this Order or of a declaration, licence, notice or designation under it, an inspector may take the steps he or she considers necessary to ensure the requirement is met at the expense of that person .

(2) An inspector's powers under paragraph (1) include powers to—

- (a) direct any person to take or refrain from specified action in respect of any place, animal, bird, vehicle, or other thing; and
- (b) seize and detain any thing.
