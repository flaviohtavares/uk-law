# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 85

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 85. Enforcement

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The local authority must enforce this Order.

(1A)[^key-7b84f843dc4a129988ced235d2363241] But in relation to designated slaughterhouses, this Order is enforced by the Food Standards Agency.

(2) The National Assembly may direct, in relation to cases of a particular description or to particular cases, that it will enforce this Order instead.
