# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 10

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. Notice of restrictions where avian influenza is suspected on premises

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to article 11 and whether or not notification has been made under article 9, an inspector must serve a notice of restrictions on the occupier of any premises where he or she suspects that avian influenza or influenza of avian origin[^c-d2489c2a1fd61b618e14eb810cc8e10c] exists or may have existed within the preceding 56 days.

(2)[^c-2ef325657be79b0e63a5ac20c4181253] Subject to article 11, the notice of restrictions must require the occupier of the premises to comply with—

- (a) in the case of premises where poultry or other captive birds are present, the measures in Schedule 1, and
- (b) in the case of premises where mammals are present, the measures in Schedules 1 and 9.

(3) A veterinary inspector[^c-2da96ed38e57648ca9a666b978180fb2] must not withdraw the notice of restrictions unless instructed to do so by the Chief Veterinary Officer or the presence of disease has been negated[^c-b95d550b0784b465a6b960dc1d71792a].
