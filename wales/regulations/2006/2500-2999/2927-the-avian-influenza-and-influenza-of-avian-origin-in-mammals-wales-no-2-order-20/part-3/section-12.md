# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 12

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Veterinary inquiries and sampling

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-de05858629f57bc3a325dacbe3149a71]  The Welsh Ministers must ensure that a veterinary inquiry is started at a suspect premises as soon as reasonably practicable if they suspect—

- (a) that poultry, other captive birds or kept mammals on the premises—
  - (i) are infected with avian influenza virus, or
  - (ii) have been moved to those premises from premises where avian influenza or influenza of avian origin is suspected or has been confirmed, or
- (b) that infection may have been present on the premises in the preceding 56 days.

(2) If the National Assembly has authorised a veterinary inspector or another veterinary surgeon to take samples from poultry , other captive birds or kept mammals[^c-0cedf86718c20a770054435d6d119583] on premises other than suspect premises or other than for the purposes of the veterinary inquiry referred to in paragraph (1), it must direct by notice to the occupier of the premises which[^c-b1eefabcbc092e413dc3a98916bf33dd] of the measures in Schedule 1 or Schedule 9[^c-1a08d8d93718e58cdae665ae42b2428b] apply to the premises.

(3)[^c-b5924bfa1ab92ad70457edf818a4b688] The Welsh Ministers must ensure that any veterinary inquiry conducted under paragraph (1) or (2) is conducted in accordance with the procedures set out in the diagnostic manual.

(4)[^c-d3030dc5332d3106e19ac1b76a59b777] A veterinary inquiry under paragraph (1) conducted at premises where mammals are kept may be carried out whether or not—

- (a) poultry or other captive birds are kept on the premises, or
- (b) avian influenza virus is present or has been present on the premises in the preceding 56 days.
