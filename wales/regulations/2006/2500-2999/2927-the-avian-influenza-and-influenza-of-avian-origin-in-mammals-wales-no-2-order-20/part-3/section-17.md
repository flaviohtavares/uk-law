# The Avian Influenza and Influenza of Avian Origin in Mammals (Wales) (No 2) Order 2006, section 17

> Text as at 2025-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Declaration of a temporary control zone

*Extent: E+W*

In a declaration of a temporary control zone, the National Assembly must impose, in relation to premises in all or such part of the zone as it thinks fit, such measures as it considers necessary to reduce the risk of the spread of avian influenza virus[^key-6388d75ccb8c31298b8e2cd9275640c8].
