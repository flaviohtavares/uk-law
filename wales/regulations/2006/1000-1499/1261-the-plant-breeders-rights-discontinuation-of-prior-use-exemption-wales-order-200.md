# The Plant Breeders' Rights (Discontinuation of Prior Use Exemption) (Wales) Order 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 1261 (W.118)

PLANT BREEDERS' RIGHTS, WALES

The Plant Breeders' Rights (Discontinuation of Prior Use Exemption) (Wales) Order 2006

Made

3 May 2006

Coming into force

12 May 2006

The National Assembly for Wales in exercise of the powers conferred by section 9(6) of the Plant Varieties Act 1997[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002] after consultation with such organisations as appear to it to be representative of persons likely to be substantially affected, makes the following Order:

## 1. Title, commencement and application

(1) The title of this Order is the Plant Breeders' Rights (Discontinuation of Prior Use Exemption) (Wales) Order 2006 and comes into force on 12 May 2006.

(2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

## 2. Discontinuation of Prior Use Exemption

Section 9(5) of the Plant Varieties Act 1997 ceases to have effect.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00003]

D. Elis-Thoma

The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

3 May 2006

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order applies in relation to Wales and comes into force on 12 May 2006.

The Order discontinues the prior use exemption in section 9(5) of the Plant Varieties Act 1997.

A Regulatory Appraisal has been prepared for these Regulations. Copies can be obtained from the Department for Environment, Planning and Countryside, Welsh Assembly Government, Cathays Park, Cardiff CF10 3NQ.
