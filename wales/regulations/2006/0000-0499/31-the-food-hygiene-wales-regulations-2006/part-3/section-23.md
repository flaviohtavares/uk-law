# The Food Hygiene (Wales) Regulations 2006, section 23

> Text as at 2022-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Application of section 9 of the Food Safety Act 1990

*Extent: E+W*

Section 9 of the Act (inspection and seizure of suspected food) [^c15473661] applies for the purposes of these Regulations with the modification that it applies in relation to an authorised officer of an enforcement authority as it applies in relation to an authorised officer of a food authority.
