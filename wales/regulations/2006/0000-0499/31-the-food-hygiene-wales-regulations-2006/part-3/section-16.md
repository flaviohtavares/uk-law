> Text as at 2022-09-30.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 16. Time limit for prosecutions

*Extent: E+W*

No prosecution for an offence under these Regulations which is punishable under paragraph (2) of regulation 17 may be begun after the expiry of—

- (a) three years from the commission of the offence; or
- (b) one year from its discovery by the prosecutor,

whichever is the earlier.
