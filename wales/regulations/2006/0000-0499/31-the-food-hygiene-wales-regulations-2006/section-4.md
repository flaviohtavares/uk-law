# The Food Hygiene (Wales) Regulations 2006, section 4

> Text as at 2022-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**4.** The Community Instruments are—

- (a) Regulation [(EC) No. 852/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0852) of the European Parliament and of the Council on the hygiene of foodstuffs (OJ No. L139, 30.4.2004, p.1; the revised text of this Regulation is now set out in a Corrigendum, OJ No. L226, 25.6.2004, p.3);
- (b) Regulation [(EC) No. 853/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0853) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down specific hygiene rules for food of animal origin (OJ No. L139, 30.4.2004, p.55; the revised text of this Regulation is now set out in a Corrigendum, OJ No. L226, 25.6.2004, p.22);
- (c) Regulation [(EC) No. 854/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2004/0854) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down specific rules for the organisation of official controls on products of animal origin intended for human consumption (OJ No. L155, 30.4.2004, p.206; the revised text of this Regulation is now set out in a Corrigendum, OJ No. L226, 25.6.2004, p.83);
- (d) Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2073/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2073) on microbiological criteria for foodstuffs (OJ No. L338, 22.12.2005, p.1); and
- (e) Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 2075/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/2075) laying down specific rules on official controls for Trichinella in meat (OJ No. L338, 22.12.2005, p.60).
