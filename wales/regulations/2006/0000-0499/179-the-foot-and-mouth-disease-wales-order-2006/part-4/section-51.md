# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Wales) Order 2006, section 51

> Text as at 2025-04-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 51. Compliance with notices and directions

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any notice served under this Order must be complied with at the expense of the person on whom it is served, except where otherwise provided in that notice.

(2) Any direction given under this Order must be complied with at the expense of the person to whom it is given, except where otherwise provided in a written direction of the National Assembly.
