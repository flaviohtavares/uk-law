# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Wales) Order 2006, section 49

> Text as at 2025-04-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Reasonable assistance

*Extent: E+W*

Any person required to give reasonable assistance or information to a person acting in the execution of this Order for the performance of his or her functions under it must, unless he or she has reasonable cause, do so without delay.
