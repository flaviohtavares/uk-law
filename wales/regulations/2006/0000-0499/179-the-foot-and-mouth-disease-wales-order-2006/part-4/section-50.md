# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Wales) Order 2006, section 50

> Text as at 2025-04-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 50. False information

*Extent: E+W*

No person is to furnish information which he or she knows to be false or misleading to a person acting in the execution of this Order.
