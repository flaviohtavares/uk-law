# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Wales) Order 2006, section 62

> Text as at 2025-04-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 62. Amendment to the Disease Control (Wales) Order 2003

*Extent: E+W*

In the Disease Control (Wales) Order 2003 [^c15478871], for article 3 substitute—

> **3. Exception**
>
> This order does not apply in relation to a movement to or from a zoo licensed under the Zoo Licensing Act 1981.
