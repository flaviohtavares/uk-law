## 61. Amendment to the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Ascertainment of Value) (Wales) (No. 5) Order 2001

*Extent: E+W*

In the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Ascertainment of Value) (Wales) (No.5) Order 2001 [^c15478861]—

- (a) in article 2(1), for “paragraph 3(2)” substitute “ paragraphs 2A(10) and 3(2) ”;
- (b) in article 2(6), for “paragraph 3(1)” substitute “ paragraph 2A(2) or paragraph 3(1) ”.
