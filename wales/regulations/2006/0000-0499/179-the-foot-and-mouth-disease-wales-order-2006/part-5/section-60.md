# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Wales) Order 2006, section 60

> Text as at 2025-04-03.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 60. Amendment to the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978

*Extent: E+W*

In article 2 of the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978 [^c15478851], for the definition “ “Foot and Mouth Disease Orders”, substitute “**Foot and Mouth Disease Orders**” means the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Wales) Order 2006 and the Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Control of Vaccination) (Wales) Regulations 2006;”.
