> Text as at 2025-04-03.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Extension of definitions of “animals” and “poultry”

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of the Act in its application to the disease and to this Order—

- (a) the definition of “animals” in section 87(1) of the Act is extended to include all four-footed beasts;
- (b) the definition of “poultry” in section 87(4) of the Act is extended to include all birds.
