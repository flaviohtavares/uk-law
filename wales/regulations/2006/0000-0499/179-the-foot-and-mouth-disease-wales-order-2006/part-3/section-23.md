# The Foot-and-Mouth Disease (Wales) Order 2006, section 23

> Text as at 2025-04-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Sampling and clinical examination of susceptible animals before slaughter

*Extent: E+W*

Where the National Assembly serves notice of its intention to cause slaughter of animals under paragraph 2A or 3 of Schedule 3 to the Act, it must ensure that any sampling and clinical examination it considers necessary to carry out the veterinary inquiry in article 12 is undertaken before, or immediately following, slaughter.
