# The National Health Service (Personal Dental Services Agreements) (Wales) Regulations 2006, section 3

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Conditions: introductory[^key-ba70ded5fe79b88f794f1770fcefdb1b]

*Extent: E+W*

A Relevant Body may only enter into an agreement if the conditions set out in—

- (a) regulation 4; and
- (b) in the case of an agreement to be entered into with a qualifying body[^f00031], on or after the coming into force for all purposes of article 39 of the Dentists Act Order (substitution of sections 43 and 44), regulation 5,

are met.

[^f00031]: Footnote: A “qualifying body” is defined in section 28D(2) of the Act.
