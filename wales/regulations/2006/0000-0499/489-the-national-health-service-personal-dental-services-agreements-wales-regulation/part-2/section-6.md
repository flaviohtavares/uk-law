# The National Health Service (Personal Dental Services Agreements) (Wales) Regulations 2006, section 6

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Reasons[^c-bf1a896d71d96ed2e3aec4b638dc3145]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a Relevant Body is of the view that the conditions in regulation 4 or 5 for entering into an agreement are not met it will notify in writing the person or persons intending to enter into the agreement of its view and its reasons for that view and of his, her, its, or their right of appeal under regulation 7.

(2) The Relevant Body will also notify in writing of its view and its reasons for that view, any director, chief executive or secretary of a qualifying body that is notified under paragraph (1) where its reasons for the decision relates to that person or those persons.
