> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 19. Arrangements on termination[^c-d35aa26e4fbc73b7a2f97337eb4b5f2c]

*Extent: E+W*

An agreement will make suitable provision for arrangements on termination of an agreement including the consequences (whether financial or otherwise) of the agreement ending.
