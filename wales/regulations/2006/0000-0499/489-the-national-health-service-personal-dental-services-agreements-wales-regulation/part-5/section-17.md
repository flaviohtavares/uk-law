# The National Health Service (Personal Dental Services Agreements) (Wales) Regulations 2006, section 17

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Finance[^key-fd0c11a0fd26635f929f61260d67a014]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The agreement must contain a term which has the effect of requiring—

- (a) the Relevant Body to make payments to the contractor under the agreement promptly and in accordance with both the terms of the agreement and any other conditions relating to the payment contained in directions given by the Welsh Ministers under section 66(4) of the 2006 Act (regulations about section 64 arrangements)[^key-44805944c8fa8a93d67e271e8911c59c];
- (b) the contractor to make payments promptly to the Relevant Body and in accordance with both the terms of the agreement and any other conditions relating to the payment contained in directions given by the Welsh Ministers under section 12 (functions of Local Health Boards) or 66(4) of the 2006 Act[^key-dd0d456059fc31bdca1c1accebe93ec2].

(2) The obligation referred to in paragraph (1) is subject to any right the Relevant Body has to set off against an amount payable to the contractor an amount that—

- (a) is owed by the contractor to the Relevant Body under the agreement;
- (b) has been paid to the contractor owing to an error or in circumstances when it was not due; or
- (c) the Relevant Body may withhold from the contractor in accordance with the terms of the agreement or any other applicable provisions contained in directions given by the Welsh Ministers under section 66(4) of the 2006 Act[^key-b4a2994256b1a37857b4dcc03c09f810].

(3) The agreement must contain a term to the effect that where, pursuant to directions under section 12 or 66(4) of the 2006 Act[^key-39c04e7e9daced190e228e6fe84a4e72], a Relevant Body is required to make a payment to a contractor under an agreement but subject to conditions, those conditions are to be a term of the agreement.
