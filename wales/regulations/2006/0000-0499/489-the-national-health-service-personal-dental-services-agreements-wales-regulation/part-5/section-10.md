# The National Health Service (Personal Dental Services Agreements) (Wales) Regulations 2006, section 10

> Text as at 2026-04-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. NHS contracts[^c-70f325c3e3d969c7f26f060491a32df4]

*Extent: E+W*

If the contractor is to be regarded as a health service body, the agreement must state that it is an NHS contract.
