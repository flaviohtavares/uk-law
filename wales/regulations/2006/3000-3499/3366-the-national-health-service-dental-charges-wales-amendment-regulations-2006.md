# The National Health Service (Dental Charges) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2006 No. 3366 (W.308)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, WALES

The National Health Service (Dental Charges) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2006

Made

13 December 2006

Coming into force

15 December 2006

The National Assembly for Wales makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 79 and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001].

## 1. Title, commencement and application

(1) The title of these Regulations is the National Health Service (Dental Charges) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2006 and they come into force on 15 December 2006.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales[^f00002].

## 2. Amendment to the National Health Service (Dental Charges) (Wales) Regulations 2006

In Schedule 3 to the National Health Service (Dental Charges) (Wales) Regulations 2006[^f00003] (Band 3 Charges — Provision of Appliances) paragraph (i) is omitted.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00004]

D. Elis-Thomas

The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

13 December 2006

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations correct an error in the National Health Service (Dental Charges) (Wales) Regulations 2006 ([S.I. 2006/491 (W.60)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2006/491)) (“the 2006 Regulations”).

Regulation 2 removes the reference to “crowns in other materials” in the list of appliances for which a Band 3 charge may be made and recovered in Schedule 3 to the 2006 Regulations.

A full Regulatory Appraisal of the effect has been prepared in respect of the 2006 Regulations. A copy of the Regulatory Appraisal can be obtained from Community Primary Care and Health Services Policy Directorate, National Assembly for Wales, Cathays Park, Cardiff CF10 3NQ.
