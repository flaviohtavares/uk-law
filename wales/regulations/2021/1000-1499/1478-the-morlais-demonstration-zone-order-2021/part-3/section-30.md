# The Morlais Demonstration Zone Order 2021, section 30

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 30. Incorporation of the mineral code

Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 2 to the Acquisition of Land Act 1981 (minerals) are incorporated in this Order subject to the modifications that—

- (a) for “the acquiring authority” substitute “the undertaker”;
- (b) for “undertaking” substitute “authorised works”; and
- (c) for “compulsory purchase order” substitute “this Order”.
