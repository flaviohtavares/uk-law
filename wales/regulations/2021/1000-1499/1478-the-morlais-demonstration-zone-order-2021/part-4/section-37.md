# The Morlais Demonstration Zone Order 2021, section 37

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 37. Disapplication of legislative provisions

(1) The provisions of section 36 of the Electricity Act 1989[^f00038] do not apply in relation to the authorised works.

(2) The provisions of section 23 of the Land Drainage Act 1991[^f00039] do not apply in relation to the authorised works.
