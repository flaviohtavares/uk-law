# The Morlais Demonstration Zone Order 2021, section 42

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 42. Obstruction of construction of authorised works

Any person who, without reasonable excuse—

- (a) obstructs any person acting under the authority of the undertaker in constructing, maintaining, operating, repowering or decommissioning of any authorised work; or
- (b) interferes with, moves or removes any apparatus belonging to any person acting under the authority of the undertaker,

is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
