# The Morlais Demonstration Zone Order 2021, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Power to lop trees overhanging the authorised works and removal of hedgerows

(1) The undertaker may fell or lop any tree or shrub near any part of the authorised works, or cut back its roots, if it reasonably believes it to be necessary to do so to prevent the tree or shrub from obstructing or interfering with the construction, maintenance, operation or decommissioning of the authorised works or any apparatus used on the authorised works.

(2) In exercising the powers of paragraph (1), the undertaker must not do any unnecessary damage to any tree or shrub and must pay compensation to any person for any loss or damage arising from the exercise of those powers.

(3) Any dispute as to a person’s entitlement to compensation under paragraph (2), or as to the amount of compensation, must be determined under Part 1 of the 1961 Act.

(4) The undertaker may remove any hedgerows within the onshore Order limits that may be required to be removed for the purposes of carrying out the authorised works.
