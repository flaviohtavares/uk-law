# The Morlais Demonstration Zone Order 2021, section 46

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 46. Certification of plans etc

The undertaker must, as soon as practicable after the making of this Order, submit copies of the environmental statement, book of reference, the offshore works plans, the onshore plans, the outline marine biodiversity enhancement strategy, the sections and the restricted area plan to the Welsh Ministers for certification that they are, respectively, true copies of the environmental statement, book of reference, the offshore works plans, the onshore plans, the outline marine biodiversity enhancement strategy, the sections and the restricted area plan referred to in this Order; and a document so certified is admissible in any proceedings as evidence of the contents of the document of which it is a copy.
