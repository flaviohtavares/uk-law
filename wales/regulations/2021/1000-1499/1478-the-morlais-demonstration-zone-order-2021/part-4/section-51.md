# The Morlais Demonstration Zone Order 2021, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. The Neighbourhood Planning Act 2017

The provisions of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Neighbourhood Planning Act 2017[^f00049] do not apply as regards this Order.
