# The Commons Act 2006 (Commencement No. 6) (Wales) Order 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 1015 (W. 240) (C. 55)

Commons, Wales

The Commons Act 2006 (Commencement No. 6) (Wales) Order 2021

Made

8 September 2021

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred upon the appropriate national authority by section 56(1) of the Commons Act 2006[^f00001].

## 1. Title and application

(1) The title of this Order is the Commons Act 2006 (Commencement No. 6) (Wales) Order 2021.

(2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

## 2. Provisions coming into force

The following provisions of the Commons Act 2006 come into force on 30 September 2021—

- (a) section 52 (minor and consequential amendments), insofar as necessary for the purposes of paragraph (b) of this article;
- (b) paragraph 5(c) of Schedule 5 (Criminal Justice and Public Order Act [1994 (c. 33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/33)).

Lesley Griffiths

Minister for Rural Affairs and North Wales, and Trefnydd, one of the Welsh Ministers

8 September 2021

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order commences certain provisions of the Commons Act 2006 (“the 2006 Act”), in relation to Wales. Article 2 brings into force section 52 of the 2006 Act, insofar as necessary for the purpose of paragraph 5(c) of Schedule 5 to the 2006 Act. Article 2 also brings that paragraph into force so as to amend the definition of “the local authority” in the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994.
