# The Local Elections (Principal Areas) (Wales) Rules 2021, section 4

> Text as at 2026-03-13.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Section 22(3)(a) of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000: use of “Annibynnol”[^c-d98f16b7b1b95e567046e5b85b1cf988][^M_C_54cce426-3dd1-4b1c-de6f-1d5d43eebd9c]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of section 22(3)(a) of the Political Parties, Elections and Referendums Act 2000[^f00009], the description “Annibynnol” may be given in a candidate’s nomination paper at an election of councillors to a county council or county borough council in Wales, instead of, or in addition to, the description “Independent”.
