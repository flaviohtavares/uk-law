> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 90. Creation of the new community ward of Pandy[^c-2889ed0cbda74e91d4ef47cb3134debe][^c-9049340f21474b48fd1da047f5a00633]

*Extent: E+W*

The new community ward of Pandy is created in the community of Crucorney which—

- (a) comprises the area shown hatched on Map 68;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Crucorney.
