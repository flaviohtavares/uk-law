# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 70

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**70.**[^key-7463f8332b6dd62f1f37cdfee8c5a6ec][^key-a2a488a6d7f572d2374f0ac80e155112] The part of the St Mary’s ward of the community of Chepstow shown hatched on Map 59—

- (a) is transferred to the new Mount Pleasant ward of the community of Chepstow;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Larkfield and St Kingsmark.
