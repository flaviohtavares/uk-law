> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 10. Castle and Priory — change in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^key-2d686b03a00c7d8455ca48007417a9cc][^key-ad6124927acf4d57520aa7eba76cdef6]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Castle ward of the community of Abergavenny shown hatched on Map 9—

- (a) is transferred to the Priory ward of the community of Abergavenny;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Priory.
