> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 203. Portskewett, Leechpool and Portskewett Village — change in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^c-4c859f295ab673d6ae4a1c8f0ee54980][^c-2254107bd340a696b38845f7e8882c70]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Leechpool ward of the community of Portskewett shown hatched on Map 135—

- (a) is transferred to the Portskewett Village ward of the community of Portskewett;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Portskewett.
