## 125. Llanbadoc — community electoral arrangements[^c-7906b9d8b684b85ce3b8101d1974d898][^c-ad185e794ac605cd0112f30493fa481b]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Llanbadoc the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Glascoed ward is 2;
- (b) Little Mill ward is 3;
- (c) Llanbadoc ward is 2;
- (d) Monkswood ward is 2.
