> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. Castle and Grofield — change in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^c-a9776e7940e6de25ce7cc4ecdc247f53][^c-5bdf0d4d9c6a80721176fcbc3bd062cd]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Castle ward of the community of Abergavenny shown hatched on Map 10—

- (a) is transferred to the Grofield ward of the community of Abergavenny;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Grofield.
