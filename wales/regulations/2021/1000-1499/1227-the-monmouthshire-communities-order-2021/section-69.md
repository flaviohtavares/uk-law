# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 69

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**69.**[^c-880a22fa7b2711faf9d8269182e98cf2][^c-d90f1fbd733ff0f3f2d2d1e8080e129f] The part of the St Kingsmark ward of the community of Chepstow shown hatched on Map 58—

- (a) is transferred to the new Mount Pleasant ward of the community of Chepstow;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Larkfield and St Kingsmark.
