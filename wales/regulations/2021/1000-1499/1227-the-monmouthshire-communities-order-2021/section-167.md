# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 167

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**167.**[^key-58dabae8ab44ff98755f96c0ca8dd136][^key-79e0626559bf52c74804bd5888ff4ea8] The part of the Salisbury ward of the community of Magor with Undy shown hatched on Map 119—

- (a) is transferred to the new Magor West ward of the community of Magor with Undy;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Magor West.
