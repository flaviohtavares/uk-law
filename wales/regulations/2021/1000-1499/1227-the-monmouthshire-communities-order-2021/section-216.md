> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 216. Tintern and Trellech United — change in community boundary and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^c-f58a829cb04fe44bf341e727de454b8b][^c-94bdac9ce23e27bb47502b4d8981620b]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the community of Trellech United shown hatched on Map 143—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Tintern;
- (b) forms the community ward of Llandogo;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of St Arvans.
