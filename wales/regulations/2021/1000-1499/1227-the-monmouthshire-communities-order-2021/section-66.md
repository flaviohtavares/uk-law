# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 66

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**66.**[^key-c925d5018ef38842d60dd930918a7e09][^key-49264dddc34e96bf329de521693999ff] The part of the St Christophers ward of the community of Chepstow shown hatched on Map 55—

- (a) is transferred to the new Maple Avenue ward of the community of Chepstow;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Bulwark and Thornwell.
