# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 188

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**188.**[^c-2e20ebd5f7a3fdf752c0dc416f4e2010][^c-57be4aec00e841948f35e759d59e28ca] The part of the community of Raglan shown hatched on Map 124—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Mitchel Troy;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Tregare;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Mitchel Troy.
