> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 127. Clydach and Gilwern — change in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^c-e63be6b5b5e5c78eecb77e2b8060518b][^c-298f322d3c5f94dbb6e38d392d28705f]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Gilwern ward of the community of Llanelly shown hatched on Map 96—

- (a) is transferred to the Clydach ward of the community of Llanelly;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanelly Hill.
