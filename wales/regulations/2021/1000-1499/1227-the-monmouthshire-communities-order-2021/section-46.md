# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 46

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**46.**[^key-016a72dac0c32fb24f688f219e9f8511][^key-368ee9b96d1c359a2aa2487d0d14a65c] The part of the of West End ward of the community of Caldicot shown hatched on Map 42—

- (a) is transferred to the new The Village ward of the community of Caldicot;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Severn.
