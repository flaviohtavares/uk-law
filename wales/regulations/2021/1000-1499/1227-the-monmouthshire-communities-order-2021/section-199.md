> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 199. Dixton with Osbaston — change of community ward name and consequential change to electoral ward[^c-2d17a9152943cc589b30f70b6c2d254b][^c-cc0a8e3f87a00a816adad60bc4c38e58]

*Extent: E+W*

The existing Dixton with Osbaston ward of the community of Monmouth as changed by this Order and shown hatched on Map 134—

- (a) is re-named Osbaston;
- (b) forms the electoral ward of Dixton with Osbaston.
