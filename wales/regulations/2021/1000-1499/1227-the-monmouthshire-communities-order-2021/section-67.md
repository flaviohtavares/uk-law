# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 67

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**67.**[^key-89abe2fd0cb1ce965e04aa8a4dc5c3ad][^key-80bde06e81c270841461e3b15a670e3d] The part of the St Christophers ward of the community of Chepstow shown hatched on Map 56—

- (a) is transferred to the new Bulwark ward of the community of Chepstow;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Bulwark and Thornwell.
