> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 204. Raglan, Llandenny and Kingcoed — change in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^c-ffb978f9c839eaa0df2dd38390055923][^c-5668229ed5b33131701cffe52de651fa]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Llandenny ward of the community of Raglan shown hatched on Map 136—

- (a) is transferred to the new Kingcoed ward of the community of Raglan;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Raglan.
