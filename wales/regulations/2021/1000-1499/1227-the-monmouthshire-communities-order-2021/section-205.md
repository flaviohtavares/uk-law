> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 205. Creation of the new community ward of Kingcoed[^c-025d0256e9bf6c87e7f2c5a786f93a7b][^c-8eb17262c7615c20cbd323a9ec1219b2]

*Extent: E+W*

The new ward of Kingcoed of the community of Raglan is created which—

- (a) comprises the area shown hatched on Map 136;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Raglan.
