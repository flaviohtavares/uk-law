## 176. Creation of the new community ward of Undy[^key-f3ba172756ea331e94668d154fb6467a][^key-0488c65252eb5e699e2f0981c605e1ec]

*Extent: E+W*

The new community ward of Undy is created in the community of Magor with Undy which—

- (a) comprises the areas shown hatched on Map 120;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Magor East.
