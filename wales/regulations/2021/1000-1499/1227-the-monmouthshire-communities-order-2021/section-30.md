# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 30

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**30.**[^c-866572a7b0a8aa766b42f75c51f0e37c][^c-17986e1c7c8755db3920abc13abc5dfb] The part of the Llanvair Discoed ward of the community of Caerwent shown hatched on Map 26—

- (a) is transferred to the St Brides Netherwent ward of the community of Caerwent;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Caerwent.
