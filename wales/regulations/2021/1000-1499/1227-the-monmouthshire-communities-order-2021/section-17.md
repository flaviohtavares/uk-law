> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Grofield and Llanwenarth Citra — change in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^c-fedbfb58febb303afb8cee5fa072bbfe][^c-b1969efbdf81c78e3f84f520a19f2cd4]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Grofield ward of the community of Abergavenny shown hatched on Map 15—

- (a) is transferred to the Llanwenarth Citra ward of the community of Abergavenny;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Cantref.
