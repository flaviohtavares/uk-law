# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 147

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**147.**[^c-8e0636640525a03b642a5ae3805e13d5][^c-559bc920c2e12d03dcbf8018741a6a31] The part of the Sgyrrid West ward of the community of Llantilio Pertholey shown hatched on Map 106—

- (a) is transferred to the Croesonen East ward of the community of Llantilio Pertholey;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Croesonen.
