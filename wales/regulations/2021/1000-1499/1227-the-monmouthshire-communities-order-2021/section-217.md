# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 217

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**217.**[^c-8edace217cdaf9f95d5de4eb83e4e5fd][^c-df411daafe2dafba0bcd469c5bc6e3e3] The part of the community of Tintern shown hatched on Map 144—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Trellech United;
- (b) forms the community ward of Trellech Grange;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Trellech United.
