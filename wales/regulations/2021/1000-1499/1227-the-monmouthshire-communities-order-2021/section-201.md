## 201. Monmouth — community ward electoral arrangements[^key-48717ae130b0d56275e97fc6e773a228][^key-6b05201ebb85ee83115894353ff6339e]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Monmouth the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Drybridge ward is 4;
- (b) Osbaston ward is 4;
- (c) Overmonnow ward is 3;
- (d) Town ward is 4.
