# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 38

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

**38.**[^key-04618484102b3458ba91d69ebf2a693f][^key-2dc8371297a70238aad184e55b2d774d] The part of the Dewstow ward of the community of Caldicot shown hatched on Map 33—

- (a) is transferred to the West End ward of the community of Caldicot;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of West End.
