# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 139

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**139.**[^c-4f1dc478ed487dabc3ccf7a4f0b65dd9][^c-f77fdec263165cae6b9dfff4ceebe142] In the community of Llangybi the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Coed-Y-Paen ward is 1;
- (b) Llandegfedd ward is 1;
- (c) Llangattock Nigh Caerleon ward is 1;
- (c) Llangybi ward is 3;
- (d) Llanhennock ward is 1;
- (e) Tredunnock ward is 1.
