## 51. Caldicot — community ward electoral arrangements[^c-70562f1096490044130438073d6899ed][^c-75982c01f42186698d5335ebb5f8eef2]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Caldicot the number of councillors to be elected for—

- (a) the Caldicot Cross ward is 3;
- (b) the Dewstow ward is 3;
- (c) the Severn ward is 2;
- (d) The Village ward is 2.
