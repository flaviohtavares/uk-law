## 105. Goetre — change of community ward name[^key-60f399d21f7dde2dc299fb79302924cc][^key-588420c0920cea49d04c4172129b3c56]

*Extent: E+W*

The existing Goetre ward of the community of Goetre Fawr as changed by this Order and shown hatched, on Map 82—

- (a) is re-named Goytre;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Goetre Fawr.
