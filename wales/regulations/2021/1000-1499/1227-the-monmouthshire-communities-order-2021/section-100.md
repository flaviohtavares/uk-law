# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 100

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**100.**[^key-52ce413c433fa8d8bf8ef5a383d76918][^key-4a7b146634b3638a31725d8abc25717b] The part of the community of Llanover shown hatched on Map 77—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Goetre Fawr;
- (b) forms part of the new community ward of Nant-Y-Derry;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Goetre Fawr.
