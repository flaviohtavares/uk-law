> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 160. Magor with Undy — change in community boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^c-a80c513a2e9c405425b822dd2fd57f29][^c-9cdd9dfb2d136928e2c9e782f2cf00c4]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Salisbury ward of the community of Magor with Undy shown hatched on Map 112—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Rogiet;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Rogiet.
