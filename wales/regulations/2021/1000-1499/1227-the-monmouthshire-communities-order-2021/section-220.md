> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 220. Chapel Hill and Tintern Parva — combination of community wards[^key-9ffce7c4c70915030ce1091e38d17486][^key-1d83ba4fad152cc8655b3f59b7368d83]

*Extent: E+W*

The existing Chapel Hill ward of the community of Wye Valley as changed by this Order is combined with the existing Tintern Parva ward of the community of Wye Valley, to form the new community ward of Tintern of the community of Wye Valley as shown hatched on Map 147.
