> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 50. Creation of the new community ward of Caldicot Cross[^key-a8c21ca307fdfd26dfa282ae49fa4bc4][^key-ecd59d7c3fadb6cec65ea276ef723bce]

*Extent: E+W*

The new community ward of Caldicot Cross of the community of Caldicot is created which—

- (a) comprises the area shown hatched on Map 45;
- (b) forms the electoral ward of Caldicot Cross.
