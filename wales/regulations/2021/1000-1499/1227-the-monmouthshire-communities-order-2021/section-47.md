# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 47

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**47.**[^c-870bbdbf553f152cc6a16ca67b1780e7][^c-a394f6ff760c0084c6cb44f86e9e2220] The part of the West End ward of the community of Caldicot shown hatched on Map 43—

- (a) is transferred to the new The Village ward of the community of Caldicot;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Severn.
