# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 162

> Text as at 2024-06-25.

**162.**[^key-335553a6948f7017438fca03b7bb448f][^key-126bf1b8339bf9c89fb9e851b194ff80] The part of the Mill ward of the community of Magor with Undy shown hatched on Map 114—

- (a) is transferred into the community of Rogiet;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Rogiet.
