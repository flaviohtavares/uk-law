> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 131. Llanfoist Fawr and Llanover — change in community boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^key-50f68a97375c3e30cfe352da4d24aca1][^key-ed2543f3c1748efa41062b6a937802da]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the community of Llanover shown hatched on Map 98—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Llanfoist Fawr;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Llanellen;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanfoist Fawr.
