## 245. Creation of the new community ward of Whitecastle[^key-00d0f203386a34d4d55c7a4ebefad95b][^key-5852c86537416e4bcf4ec456177581c6]

*Extent: E+W*

The new community ward of Whitecastle is created in the community of Whitecastle which—

- (a) comprises the area shown hatched on Map 166;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llantilio Crossenny.
