# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 197

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

**197.**[^c-a1aed9be8fcd86f1a109ffe7c9e70932][^c-877d19e10a2cd4cd30acf9a451750fb3] The part of the Drybridge ward of the community of Monmouth shown hatched on Map 132—

- (a) is transferred to the Town ward of the community of Monmouth;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Drybridge.
