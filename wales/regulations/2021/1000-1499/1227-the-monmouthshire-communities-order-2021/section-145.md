> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 145. Croesonen East, Croesonen West, and Sgyrrid West — change in community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral wards[^key-be077fd8076f3b0aa1f2953a24172f15][^key-cfc063b9642afe05e8b3b0d261657e69]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Croesonen East ward of the community of Llantilio Pertholey shown hatched on Map 104—

- (a) is transferred to the Sgyrrid West ward of the community of Llantilio Pertholey;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Mardy.
