> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 141. Llanover — change of community name[^c-8068218be4b35b3f22b75f76c24c8d5a][^c-00fc69fb4fc2b9d29cf37e8e3932afef]

*Extent: E+W*

The existing community of Llanover as changed by this Order and shown hatched on Map 103—

- (a) is re-named Gobion Fawr;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanover.
