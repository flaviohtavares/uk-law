> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 32. Caldicot, Portskewett and Rogiet, — changes in community boundaries and consequential change to electoral arrangements[^c-af1059a663d3179b65143638b94e937c][^c-9385de41ab96977085632364b7680f8d]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the community of Portskewett shown hatched on Map 27—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Caldicot;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Severn;
- (c) forms part of the new electoral ward Severn.
