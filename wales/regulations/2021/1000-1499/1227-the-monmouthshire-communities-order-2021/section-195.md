# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 195

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**195.**[^key-e646f482e6c12d9ff90b0af57049896e][^key-247d41dcd64d82c4af056580c702743e] The part of the Town ward of the community of Monmouth shown hatched on Map 130—

- (a) is transferred to the Drybridge ward of the community of Monmouth;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Drybridge.
