## 98. Devauden — community electoral arrangements[^c-6f5f90b9910cbdfa910eee6c47d92d7a][^c-afaeb16bce62050ca1eb5dda87af7ce8]

*Extent: E+W*

- (a) Kilgrwwg ward is 1;
- (b) Llanvihangel Tor-Y-Mynydd ward is 1.
