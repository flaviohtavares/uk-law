# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 164

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

**164.**[^key-d5363e1b8d5f2fb161264d101a26b796][^key-28950d1d464f3a079661b489bb6cff7e] The part of the Denny ward of the community of Magor with Undy shown hatched on Map 116—

- (a) is transferred to the new Magor East ward of the community of Magor with Undy;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Magor East.
