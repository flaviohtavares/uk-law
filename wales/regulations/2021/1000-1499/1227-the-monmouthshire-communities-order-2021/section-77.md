# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 77

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 77. St Mary’s — change of community ward name[^c-8a1c8f60b5a54b942d0bf74eb87cbcd7][^c-13c3489f41469332066068752b1b9e7a]

*Extent: E+W*

The existing St Mary’s ward of the community of Chepstow as changed by this Order and shown hatched on Map 65—

- (a) is re-named Chepstow Castle;
- (b) forms the new electoral ward of Chepstow Castle.
