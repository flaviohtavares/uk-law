> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 96. Devauden and Llanvihangel Wolvesnewton — changes in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^key-07be30ef707b80167ea7c4a516b04009][^key-e2c760ebfa01eaffbb1b1a5252430e88]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Llanvihangel Wolvesnewton ward of the community of Devauden shown hatched on Map 74—

- (a) is transferred to the Devauden ward of the community of Devauden;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Devauden.
