# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 109

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 109. Grosmont and Skenfrith — change in community boundary and consequential change to electoral arrangements[^c-61c44c09908b764d593ed124869d3dfc][^c-630b61849f0f1c39f677f0b8cffa8d27]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the community of Grosmont shown hatched on Map 83—

- (a) is transferred to the new community of Skenfrith;
- (b) forms the new community ward of Llanvetherine;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llantilio Crossenny.
