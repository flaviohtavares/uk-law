> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 134. Llanfoist Fawr — community electoral arrangements[^key-a9ed570be593f803dbc023e8ab332dbf][^key-af941a079e14b6f2f1ac06017ab8425c]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Llanfoist Fawr the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Llanfoist ward is 5;
- (b) Govilon ward is 6.
