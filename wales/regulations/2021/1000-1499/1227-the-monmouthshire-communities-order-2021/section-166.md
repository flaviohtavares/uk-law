# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 166

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**166.**[^c-c108381e0880a3bd417f36b520de7c13][^c-83acccfa4af43ca998631ee34f826824] The part of the Mill ward of the community of Magor with Undy shown hatched on Map 118—

- (a) is transferred to the new Magor East of the community of Magor with Undy;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Magor East.
