# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 62

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**62.**[^c-16c3d5c6a1c0150af90d168dbd3ae5a0][^c-9630950c2b416379afb55eed9129c640] The part of the Larkfield ward of the community of Chepstow shown hatched on Map 51—

- (a) is transferred to the new Mount Pleasant ward of the community of Chepstow;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Larkfield and St Kingsmark.
