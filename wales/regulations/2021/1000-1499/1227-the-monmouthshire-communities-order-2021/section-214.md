> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 214. St Arvans and Tintern — change in community boundary and consequential changes to electoral wards[^key-c1044e148a9d045187ebeb8f377e7017][^key-1809a5f33b6057aac53f2662e061d0f8]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Penterry ward of the community of Tintern shown hatched on Map 142—

- (a) is transferred to the community of St Arvans;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of St Arvans.
