# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 237

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**237.**[^c-61524c938430548b9e43e2121c56ae37][^c-be0a8325f943ea1e88a43493606d6176] The parts of the community of Llangattock Vibon Avel shown hatched on Map 158—

- (a) are transferred to the community of Whitecastle;
- (b) form part of the new community ward of Newcastle;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llantilio Crossenny.
