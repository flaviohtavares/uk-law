## 23. Caerwent and Rogiet — change in community boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^key-7e1c5fc8d59b23419b2bdb44de173727][^key-a9e39ad0cbbacbca09f8adc2b658fd0f]

*Extent: E+W*

The parts of the community of Caerwent shown hatched on Map 19—

- (a) are transferred to the community of Rogiet;
- (b) form part of the electoral ward of Rogiet.
