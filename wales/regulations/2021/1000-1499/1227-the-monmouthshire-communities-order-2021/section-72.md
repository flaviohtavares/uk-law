# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 72

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**72.**[^key-ec29e55b46d1cdc2c1c3b5a329b75b05][^key-1f9a754ca2d03c6552acdb53d67533e4] The part of the Thornwell ward of the community of Chepstow shown hatched on Map 61—

- (a) is transferred to the new Bulwark ward of the community of Chepstow;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Bulwark and Thornwell.
