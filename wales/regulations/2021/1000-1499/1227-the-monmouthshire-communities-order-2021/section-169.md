# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 169

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**169.**[^key-f154c5b9c3bc813631967af031a071bd][^key-e9770f8ad92ae4eb372fb96b2575e0c8] The part of The Elms ward of the community of Magor with Undy shown hatched on Map 121—

- (a) is transferred to the new Magor East ward of the community of Magor with Undy;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Magor East.
