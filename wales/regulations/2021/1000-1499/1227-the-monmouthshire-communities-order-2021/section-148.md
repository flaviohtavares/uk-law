> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 148. Croesonen East and Croesonen West — combination of community wards and consequential change to electoral ward[^c-61df7a68d55b1833c3dbe4218e5f02f1][^c-28fd3565e7ee06e95f0456a4a46f1e4d]

*Extent: E+W*

The existing Croesonen East and Croesonen West wards of the community of Llantilio Pertholey as changed by this Order and shown hatched on Map 107—

- (a) are combined and form the community ward of Croesonen;
- (b) form the electoral ward of Croesonen.
