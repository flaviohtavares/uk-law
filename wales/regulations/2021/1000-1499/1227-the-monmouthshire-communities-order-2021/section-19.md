## 19. Priory — change of community ward name[^key-b9069d57748908bfd90ed2e0d02c8c22][^key-73334f433cc3a278b1d3972477c619f9]

*Extent: E+W*

The Priory ward of the community of Abergavenny as changed by this Order and shown hatched on Map 17—

- (a) is re-named Park;
- (b) forms the electoral ward of Priory.
