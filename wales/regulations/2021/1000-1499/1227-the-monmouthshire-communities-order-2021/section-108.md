## 108. Goetre Fawr — community electoral arrangements[^c-095ed635913620666563e486ccbbc220][^c-502845097705cb3c7b0125a43e222ab4]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Goetre Fawr the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Goetre Wharf ward is 2;
- (b) Goytre ward is 4;
- (c) Llanover ward is 1;
- (d) Nant-Y-Derry ward is 1.
