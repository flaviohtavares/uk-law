# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 45

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**45.**[^key-2a44a7d83c5a2948d7dab9c962e12455][^key-63b09229979e4f404a0940ee2a42b383] The part of the West End ward of the community of Caldicot shown hatched on Map 41—

- (a) is transferred to the Severn ward of the community of Caldicot;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Severn.
