## 222. Wye Valley — community electoral arrangements[^c-f65d0e573138646c2075fc96bc988aec][^c-4ecb8b617f4f090e6d352eea3a618398]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Wye Valley the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Llandogo ward is 3;
- (b) Tintern ward is 4.
