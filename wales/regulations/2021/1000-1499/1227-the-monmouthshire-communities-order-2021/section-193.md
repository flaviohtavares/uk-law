# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 193

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 193. Dixton with Osbaston, Drybridge, Overmonnow and Town — changes in community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral wards[^c-0a8ccdeeec6b52a34bff21b46adbcc9a][^c-f6d89570f883feefd0b96906d01a160a]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Town ward of the community of Monmouth shown hatched on Map 128—

- (a) is transferred to the Dixton with Osbaston ward of the community of Monmouth;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Dixton with Osbaston.
