# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 249

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 249. Llangattock Vibon Avel — dissolution of community and consequential change to community council[^key-be743646501adc775b56d82ffec0154a][^key-8b337237dff51261a9ee51a3a4b61752]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The existing community of Llangattock Vibon Avel as changed by this Order is dissolved.

(2) The community council of Llangattock Vibon Avel is dissolved.
