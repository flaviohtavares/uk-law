# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 93

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**93.**[^key-7be4bb3fcdd5d0103c6be9c6f225210e][^key-467dcb97346ce6052fe4d94534a7c65e] The part of the community of St Arvans shown hatched on Map 71—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Devauden;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Itton;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Devauden.
