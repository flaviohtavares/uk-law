> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18. Lansdown and Priory — change in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^key-1681b935974fc42d408203cfd642b35f][^key-f3f928c373722b3edd09caaa2298d21c]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Lansdown ward of the community of Abergavenny shown hatched on Map 16—

- (a) is transferred to the Priory ward of the community of Abergavenny;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Priory.
