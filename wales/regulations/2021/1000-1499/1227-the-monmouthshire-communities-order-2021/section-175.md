> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 175. Creation of the new community ward of Magor East[^c-3b9e044abc670dd0472383904a834287][^c-0d281dc2dc505781294efe18b55c7a85]

*Extent: E+W*

The new community ward of Magor East is created in the community of Magor with Undy which—

- (a) comprises the areas shown hatched on Maps 116, 118 and 121;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Magor East.
