# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 33

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**33.**[^key-e4d613e6944ccfd931933e44a093e899][^key-0f2648154d79a5339da50d5679f36364] The part of the community of Rogiet shown hatched on Map 28—

- (a) is transferred to the Community of Caldicot;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of West End;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of West End.
