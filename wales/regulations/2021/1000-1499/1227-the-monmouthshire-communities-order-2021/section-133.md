> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 133. Llanwenarth Ultra — change of community ward name and consequential change to electoral ward[^c-9686ea98b40d34e1f15cb43475f7862b][^c-1ec14f044775f1beec8d2c7727bbf6b6]

*Extent: E+W*

The existing Llanwenarth Ultra ward of the community of Llanfoist Fawr as changed by this Order and shown hatched on Map 100—

- (a) is re-named the community ward of Govilon;
- (b) forms the electoral ward of Llanwenarth Ultra.
