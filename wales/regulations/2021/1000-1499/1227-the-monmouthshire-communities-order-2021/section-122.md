**122.**[^c-45bf30f3fda4c50734753ac9b3e282c3][^c-cdf5d121545fd0c5c3f82ff5f4102f44] The part of the community of Llanarth shown hatched on Map 93—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Raglan;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Raglan;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Raglan.
