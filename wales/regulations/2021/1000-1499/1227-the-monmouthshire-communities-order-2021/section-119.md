# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 119

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**119.**[^key-90e5866bd51e831b66d0579cfb0195da][^key-62bd784de07facf57ad36e5b58e5ceda] The part of the community of Llanover shown hatched on Map 90—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Llanarth;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Llanarth;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Gobion Fawr.
