> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 191. Mitchel Troy — community electoral arrangements[^key-196b6635ff5c37dca070d7f7d8288ac5][^key-db1fe4f9944585084477ceea6d6010c5]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Mitchel Troy the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Cwmcarvan ward is 1;
- (b) Mitchel Troy ward is 2;
- (c) Pen-y-Clawdd ward is 1.
