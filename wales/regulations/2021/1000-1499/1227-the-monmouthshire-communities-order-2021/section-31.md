## 31. Caerwent — community electoral arrangements[^key-2191c6c4bf4dd6bfc4cb8f8259da51ff][^key-4150e5e4836db487765498bea706f01f]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Caerwent the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Caerwent ward is 3;
- (b) Llanvair Discoed ward is 1;
- (c) St. Brides Netherwent ward is 1.
