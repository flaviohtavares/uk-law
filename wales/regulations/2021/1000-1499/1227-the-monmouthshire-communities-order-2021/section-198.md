> Text as at 2024-06-25.

**198.**[^c-40f9a4cd9af5f690b4fb3f98da01784a][^c-03397eccfa6cc7e294dd71bcd6f9c66e] The part of the Overmonnow ward of the community of Monmouth shown hatched on Map 133—

- (a) is transferred to the Town ward of the community of Monmouth;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Drybridge.
