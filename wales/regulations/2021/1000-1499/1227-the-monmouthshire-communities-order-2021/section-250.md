> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 250. Llantilio Crossenny — dissolution of community of and consequential change to community council[^c-c9232fa3189000de6bab38400d392160][^c-006167163cc549ba04b5ed72175e9e5f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The existing community of Llantilio Crossenny as changed by this Order is dissolved.

(2) The community council of Llantilio Crossenny is dissolved.
