> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 142. Gobion Fawr — community electoral arrangements[^key-7914b3c62d5b08237088bd8a0f82492a][^key-d1f393b4e0b7c83265c94bb1c67d43d2]

*Extent: E+W*

The community of Gobion Fawr consists of four wards—

- (a) Llanddewi Rhydderch;
- (b) Llanfair Cilgydyn;
- (c) Llangattock-Nigh-Usk;
- (d) Llanvapley.
