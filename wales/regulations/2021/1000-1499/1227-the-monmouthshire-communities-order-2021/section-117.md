## 117. Llanarth, Llanover, Raglan and Whitecastle — change in community boundaries and consequential change to electoral arrangements[^c-3429830802848da84a514ee19bb8c88d][^c-42adccff54fdc36b0dc9313d6fec3051]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the community of Llanarth shown hatched on Map 88—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Llanover;
- (b) forms the community ward of Llanvapley;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanover.
