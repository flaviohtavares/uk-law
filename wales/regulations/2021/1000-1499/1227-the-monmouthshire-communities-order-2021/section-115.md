> Text as at 2024-06-25.

**115.**[^c-2413ef2f53202b731253a3b9872bb1d6][^c-cac90ae37b7f2b144887f72b34d5f0e1] The part of the community of Gwehelog Fawr shown crosshatched on Map 87—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Raglan;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Llandenny;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Raglan.
