> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 48. Creation of the new community ward of The Village and dissolution of the community ward of Green Lane[^c-4b6fc9e709b0c1a46359009cfa870b9b][^c-1358689ec92d189a9465ae98b19eb5a8]

*Extent: E+W*

The new community ward of The Village of the community of Caldicot is created which—

- (a) comprises the area shown hatched on Map 44;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Severn.
