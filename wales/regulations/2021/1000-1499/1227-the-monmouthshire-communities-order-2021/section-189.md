# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 189

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**189.**[^key-51ddc4f6dc3966d0f241f6c33ef88906][^key-60d3c6c0de6189d9663b3e358a4da8ab] The part of the community of Mitchel Troy shown hatched on Map 125—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Monmouth;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Overmonnow;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Overmonnow.
