> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 86. Crucorney, Grosmont and Skenfrith — change in community boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^key-aacd47acb59c15086c80ee8eaa3d6f22][^key-7d6978858ec1baefdcf8ebc596a580c1]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the community of Crucorney shown hatched on Map 66—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Grosmont;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Llangattock Lingoed;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Crucorney.
