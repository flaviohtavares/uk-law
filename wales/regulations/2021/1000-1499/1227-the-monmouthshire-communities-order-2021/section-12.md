> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Priory and Castle — change in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^c-09ab3a6828c20a6fa1eca452c8604537][^c-c8dc104f1ced62af82587e3c6cf688c3]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Priory ward of the community of Abergavenny shown hatched on Map 11—

- (a) is transferred to the Castle ward of the community of Abergavenny;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Castle.
