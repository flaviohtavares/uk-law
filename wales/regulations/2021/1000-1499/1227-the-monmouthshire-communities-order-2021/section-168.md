# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 168

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**168.**[^c-4e436c050aad2480314dc9050f3f6538][^c-127c2fe119ec643c01e20f799a34d05a] The part of The Elms ward of the community of Magor with Undy shown hatched on Map 120—

- (a) is transferred to the new Undy ward of the community of Magor with Undy;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Magor East.
