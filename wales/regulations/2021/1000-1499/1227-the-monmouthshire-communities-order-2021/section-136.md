> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 136. Llangybi and Llanhennock — combination of communities and consequential change to community councils and electoral arrangements[^c-57e2c4705b6f0ea00b49c01d7515c043][^c-22f986afc642c2d18ff3c59652fe7ba6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The areas of the communities of Llangybi and Llanhennock as shown hatched on Map 101 are combined to form the community of Llangybi, which forms part of the electoral ward of Llangybi Fawr.

(2) The community councils of Llangybi and Llanhennock are dissolved.
