> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 97. Llanvihangel Wolvesnewton — change of community ward name[^c-bf4db23c8fa394afa05dfe92866384b5][^c-e945a0d254e41b0f8c08fa69952ebbd9]

*Extent: E+W*

The existing Llanvihangel Wolvesnewton ward of the community of Devauden as changed by this Order and shown hatched on Map 75—

- (a) is re-named Llanvihangel Tor-Y-Mynydd;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Devauden.
