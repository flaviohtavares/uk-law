> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 91. Crucorney — community ward electoral arrangements[^c-0e78cd87355513e46d36e81b00e7e121][^c-e422e83ee5d4e1ff8af89c1ce33bce39]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Crucorney the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Cwmyoy ward is 1;
- (b) Forest & Ffwddog ward is 1;
- (c) Llanvihangel Crucorney ward is 2;
- (d) Pandy ward is 2.
