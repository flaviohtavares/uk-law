> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Caerwent, Crick, Dinham, Llanvair Discoed and St Brides Netherwent — changes in community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral wards[^key-1cab40532868856387459b233a2fcf91][^key-b928c4990194ae0daea3d3c4d3afe265]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the St Brides Netherwent ward of the community of Caerwent shown hatched on Map 22—

- (a) is transferred to the Caerwent ward of the community of Caerwent;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Caerwent.
