# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 159

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**159.**[^c-4986cda42589d2d05daf98cec3d619d3][^c-ba5c5969d180740556d38fff19a48e95] In the community of Llantrisant Fawr the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Gwernesney ward is 1;
- (b) Llangwm ward is 3;
- (c) Llansoy ward is 1;
- (d) Llantrisant ward is 3.
