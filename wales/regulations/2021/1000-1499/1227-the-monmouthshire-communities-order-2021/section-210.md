**210.**[^key-d87e36b74f3ef8164df3338f49b974c8][^key-527b574110cdfdd9c7dfe83a8eb4f47b] The part of the Shirenewton ward of the community of Shirenewton shown hatched on Map 139—

- (a) is transferred to the new Earlswood and Newchurch ward of the community of Shirenewton;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Shirenewton.
