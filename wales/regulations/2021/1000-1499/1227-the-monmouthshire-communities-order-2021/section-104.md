# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 104

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

**104.**[^key-f654271de21db91b0c5aaf927498ed69][^key-44240b823edb79462581ddb6f7442b93] The part of the Goetre ward of the community of Goetre Fawr shown hatched and numbered 1 on Map 81—

- (a) is transferred to the Mamhilad ward of the community of Goetre Fawr;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Goetre Fawr.
