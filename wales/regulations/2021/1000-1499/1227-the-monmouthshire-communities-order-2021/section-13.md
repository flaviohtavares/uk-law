> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 13. Castle and Priory — change in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^c-a759cc3aca37887f963fc6370fc9467f][^c-7300d9523258b9e922634a0f6ba66fcb]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Castle ward of the community of Abergavenny shown hatched on Map 12—

- (a) is transferred to the Priory ward of the community of Abergavenny;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Priory.
