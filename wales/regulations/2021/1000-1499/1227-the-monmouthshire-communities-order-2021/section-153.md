## 153. Llantrisant Fawr and Llangwm — combination of communities and consequential changes to community councils and electoral arrangements[^key-34da3d4091b69c77170d2d69b5612098][^key-b6e1a4267703be922bb5718b810f4976]

*Extent: E+W*

The areas of the existing communities of Llantrisant Fawr and Llangwm as shown hatched on Map 111 are combined to form the community of Llantrisant Fawr.
