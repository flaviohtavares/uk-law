# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 71

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**71.**[^c-06246b7b0554fb053bf7ce22b0d77956][^c-8f14f4b388047506505944ba73ae5c98] The part of the Thornwell ward of the community of Chepstow shown hatched on Map 60—

- (a) is transferred to the new Maple Avenue ward of the community of Chepstow;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Bulwark and Thornwell.
