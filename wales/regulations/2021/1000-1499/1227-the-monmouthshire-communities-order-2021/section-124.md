## 124. Llanarth — community electoral arrangements[^key-cd4c610d3d4217f4de07010f0043db6b][^key-4966cf3adef5c8e214ef2325dbcf66f2]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Llanarth the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Bryngwyn ward is 2;
- (b) Clytha ward is 1;
- (c) Kemeys Commander and Llancayo ward is 2;
- (d) Llanarth ward is 3.
