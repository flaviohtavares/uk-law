## 213. Shirenewton — community electoral arrangements[^c-b9be6d37f14ec74a0878551afcb89fd3][^c-0f9e2158276d7c599410577803e1a0f7]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Shirenewton the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Earlswood and Newchurch ward is 2;
- (b) Shirenewton and Mynyddbach ward is 5.
