# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 150

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 150. Mardy and Sgyrrid West — change in community ward boundary and consequential change to electoral ward[^c-b101cc4c7bdc877bda35984cd46887a5][^c-ce28bae8e987165b88378be7e17c6cda]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Sgyrrid West ward of the community of Llantilio Pertholey shown hatched on Map 109—

- (a) is transferred to the Mardy ward of the community of Llantilio Pertholey;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Mardy.
