> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Portskewett and Caerwent — change in community boundaries and consequential change to electoral arrangements[^c-6f00a8844762d057b5ac9a63f1f08936][^c-6203688e92496ee0b2af8f469236194e]

*Extent: E+W*

The parts of the community of Portskewett shown hatched on Map 20—

- (a) are transferred to the community of Caerwent;
- (b) form part of the community ward of Crick;
- (c) form part of the electoral ward of Caerwent.
