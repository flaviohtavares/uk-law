> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 218. Tintern — change of community name[^key-01573be30d4e0693b99b50a0c98fd6ee][^key-dc7cefcd3a6ee69391c14059b6ab57a6]

*Extent: E+W*

The existing community of Tintern as changed by this Order and shown hatched on Map 145—

- (a) is re-named Wye Valley;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of St Arvans.
