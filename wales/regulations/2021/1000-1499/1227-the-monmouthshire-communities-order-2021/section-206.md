## 206. Raglan — community electoral arrangements[^key-fa6409764d9a1d7a92172a3ff4334a3d][^key-c44737c4ae411366696935d460812357]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Raglan the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Gwehelog ward is 1;
- (b) Kingcoed ward is 1;
- (c) Llandenny ward is 1;
- (d) Raglan ward is 6.
