> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 110. Grosmont and Llangua — change in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^key-09ef7d3745fac0c4ff98519924c0c52f][^key-3664781a316b27c2efa86dcd24a9d34b]

*Extent: E+W*

The Llangua ward of the community of Grosmont shown hatched on Map 84—

- (a) is transferred to the Grosmont ward of the community of Grosmont;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Crucorney.
