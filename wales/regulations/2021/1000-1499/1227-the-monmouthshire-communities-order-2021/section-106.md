> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 106. Mamhilad — change of community ward name[^c-55bbab50f8e00946dfa2feb5baf8e53b][^c-188091a3f7024f728e4de0bb85876401]

*Extent: E+W*

The existing Mamhilad ward of the community of Goetre Fawr as changed by this Order and shown hatched, and numbered 1 and 2 on Map 81—

- (a) is re-named Goetre Wharf;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Goetre Fawr.
