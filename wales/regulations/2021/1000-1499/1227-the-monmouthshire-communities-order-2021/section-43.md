# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 43

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**43.**[^key-598b7e541e97d67ca469752247d39718][^key-301c02e3a26c0a8260bea137faa8a5c4] The part of the Severn ward of the community of Caldicot shown hatched on Map 39—

- (a) is transferred to the new Caldicot Cross ward of the community of Caldicot;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Caldicot Cross.
