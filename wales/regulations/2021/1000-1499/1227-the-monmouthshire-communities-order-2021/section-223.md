> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 223. Trellech United, Llanishen and Trellech Town — changes in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^key-9b68b7a176dfd8b3f6062e05d9228f1f][^key-ae56d7e03507643a6e226fd1298dd338]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Llanishen ward of the community of Trellech United shown hatched on Map 148—

- (a) is transferred to the Trellech Town ward of the community of Trellech United;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Trellech United.
