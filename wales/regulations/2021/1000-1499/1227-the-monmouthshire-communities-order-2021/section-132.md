# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 132

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 132. Llanfoist and Llanwenarth Ultra — change in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral ward[^key-907fba78460ddcf6afcbb08fff6e6b84][^key-1c209a57eef255c0e20fc1af855db0a6]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Llanfoist ward of the community of Llanfoist Fawr shown hatched on Map 99—

- (a) is transferred to the Llanwenarth Ultra ward of the community of Llanfoist Fawr;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanwenarth Ultra.
