# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 4

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**4.**[^key-ebeb65e190f2b1afe312b2d15dec7930][^key-aede930ff816770fb02c52f63f91b07e] The part of the community of Llantilio Pertholey shown hatched on Map 2—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Abergavenny;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Lansdown;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Lansdown.
