## 8. Cantref and Llanwenarth Citra — change in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^c-18937dd6e72a73ffe06c868f361632df][^c-9da08d09b3223f237085388e8eeece4b]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Cantref ward of the community of Abergavenny shown hatched on Map 7—

- (a) is transferred to the Llanwenarth Citra ward of the community of Abergavenny;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Cantref.
