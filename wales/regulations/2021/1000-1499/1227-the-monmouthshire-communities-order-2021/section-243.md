> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 243. Creation of the new community ward of Penrhos[^c-ae37807023d2c280e1fb8477445e051a][^c-6574f064c267c7edf2f6eefb5e0276aa]

*Extent: E+W*

The new community ward of Penrhos is created in the community of Whitecastle which—

- (a) comprises the area shown hatched on Map 164;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llantilio Crossenny.
