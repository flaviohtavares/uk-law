> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 75. Creation of the new community ward of Maple Avenue[^key-aad5e94f990152dc6f4092f4e03daae0][^key-41af65838158cc42892e2f0f9859dfa1]

*Extent: E+W*

The new community ward of Maple Avenue of the community of Chepstow is created which—

- (a) comprises the area shown hatched on Map 63;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Bulwark and Thornwell.
