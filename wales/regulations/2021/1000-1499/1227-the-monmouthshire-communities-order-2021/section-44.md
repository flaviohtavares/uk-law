# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 44

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**44.**[^c-a81a9eb32152f6a79d614c72cdc238ee][^c-45b263944eeadd824d6069f0160e2aa4] The part of the Severn ward of the community of Caldicot shown hatched on Map 40—

- (a) is transferred to the new The Village ward of the community of Caldicot;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Severn.
