# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 35

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Caldicot Castle, Caldicot Cross, Dewstow, Green Lane, Severn, West End, and The Village — changes in community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral wards[^key-f8ae8e2f4c608c73d8815350c5a82bb1][^key-e7e4a862b0a8e00e4ef29da1d30041b3]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Caldicot Castle ward of the community of Caldicot shown hatched on Map 30—

- (a) is transferred to the new Caldicot Cross ward of the community of Caldicot;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Caldicot Cross.

**36.**[^key-f9469a153807205ade67486c53dbd847][^key-0cb1780ec19bf28aea4ce72353adf7b5] The part of the Caldicot Castle ward of the community of Caldicot shown hatched on Map 31—

- (a) is transferred to the new ward of The Village of the community of Caldicot;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Severn.

**37.**[^key-a86b36c7e9baaf99250a1ad22af05f92][^key-93e10c4d8d49f317c749dcdaf71b4830] The part of the Caldicot Castle ward of the community of Caldicot shown hatched on Map 32—

- (a) is transferred to the Severn ward of the community of Caldicot;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Severn.
