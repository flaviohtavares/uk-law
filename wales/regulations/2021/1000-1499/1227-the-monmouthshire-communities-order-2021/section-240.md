> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 240. Creation of the new community ward of Llangattock Vibon Avel[^key-011e364e97e2037a98eb57b6586566d8][^key-ca76046348628bcab07cb75e35f89643]

*Extent: E+W*

The new community ward of Llangattock Vibon Avel is created in the community of Whitecastle which—

- (a) comprises the area shown hatched on Map 161;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llantilio Crossenny.
