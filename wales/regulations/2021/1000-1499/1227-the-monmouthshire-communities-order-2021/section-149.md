> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 149. Croesonen and Mardy — change in community ward boundary and consequential change to electoral ward[^key-3819ae3fb5f261e74ea1ee112a02289d][^key-712f71e88aedf657bad9d6ab1b30aa46]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Mardy ward of the community of Llantilio Pertholey shown hatched on Map 108—

- (a) is transferred to the Croesonen ward of the community of Llantilio Pertholey;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Croesonen.
