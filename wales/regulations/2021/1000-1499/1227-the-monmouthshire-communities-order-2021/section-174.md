> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 174. Creation of the new community ward of Magor West[^key-fe9699fd261cb24fd42710c30bfeafb9][^key-ac275c7c25bbe165d2940aefbfdfbde3]

*Extent: E+W*

The new community ward of Magor West is created in the community of Magor with Undy which—

- (a) comprises the areas shown hatched on Maps 115, 117 and 119;
- (b) forms the electoral ward of Magor West.
