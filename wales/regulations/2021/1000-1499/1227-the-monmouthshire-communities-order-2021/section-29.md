# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 29

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**29.**[^c-670d5da8a3aa293c15089857b56ad5fc][^c-040de104f030b7c6fbc27ab615c19378] The part of the Llanvair Discoed ward of the community of Caerwent shown hatched on Map 25—

- (a) is transferred to the Dinham ward of the community of Caerwent;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Caerwent.
