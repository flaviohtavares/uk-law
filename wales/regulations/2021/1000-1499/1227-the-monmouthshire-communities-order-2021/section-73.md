# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 73

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 73. Dissolution of the community ward of St Christophers and the creation of the new community ward of Bulwark[^c-60b159b8c4de07af9c3c279051510008][^c-cf2570937942ed14a35d7574ca1dc0d2]

*Extent: E+W*

The existing St Christophers ward of the community of Chepstow as changed by this Order is dissolved and the new community ward of Bulwark is created.
