> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 76. Creation of the new community ward of Mount Pleasant[^key-302e4c948ff574d79773f4b088882639][^key-be3357af55cebf583dc89dd1ee8c2796]

*Extent: E+W*

The new community ward of Mount Pleasant of the community of Chepstow is created which—

- (a) comprises the area shown hatched on Map 64;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Larkfield and St Kingsmark.
