# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 41

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**41.**[^c-255796dd5109533f2d3b7ed9b023d92b][^c-6819106f52e198066ee2e4c44a89c591] The part of the Green Lane ward of the community of Caldicot shown hatched on Map 36—

- (a) is transferred to the new Caldicot Cross ward of the community of Caldicot;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Caldicot Cross.
