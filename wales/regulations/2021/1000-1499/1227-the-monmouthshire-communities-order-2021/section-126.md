> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 126. Llanelly and Llanfoist Fawr — change in community boundaries and consequential changes in electoral arrangements[^key-1fed6dc2b416ccddabbfff1038064b5a][^key-f0e40bb7021880a9da4cb2b6709bd76a]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the community of Llanfoist Fawr shown hatched on Map 95—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Llanelly;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Gilwern;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanelly Hill.
