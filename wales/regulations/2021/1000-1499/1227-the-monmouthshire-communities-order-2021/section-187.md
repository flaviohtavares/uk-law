> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 187. Mitchel Troy, Monmouth, Raglan and Whitecastle — changes in community boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^c-986cd28f30e1acf8c74f616387fdb6e4][^c-b7d65cefc6dc79767a5f61ad0bafc585]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the community of Raglan shown hatched on Map 123—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Mitchel Troy;
- (b) forms the community ward of Pen-y-Clawdd;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Mitchel Troy.
