# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 194

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**194.**[^key-53bc743a5f03b071556942a5d861c1dd][^key-35edf3176d298265f3e39a276d3dc89b] The part of the Dixton with Osbaston ward of the community of Monmouth shown hatched on Map 129—

- (a) is transferred to the Town ward of the community of Monmouth;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Drybridge.
