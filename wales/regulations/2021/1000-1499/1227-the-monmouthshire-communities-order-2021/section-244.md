> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 244. Creation of the new community ward of Rockfield and St Maughans[^key-b2abf5e79b407c07224d45ef06230aee][^key-e9344992fdc9ce2879a96016e1ed18c4]

*Extent: E+W*

The new community ward of Rockfield and St Maughans is created in the community of Whitecastle which—

- (a) comprises the area shown hatched on Map 165;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llantilio Crossenny.
