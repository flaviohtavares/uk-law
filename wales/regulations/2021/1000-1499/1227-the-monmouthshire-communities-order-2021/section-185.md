## 185. Mathern, Mounton and Pwllmeyric — change in community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral wards[^c-5799bc73f36eb4811fb66f43d30d2d71][^c-a7ddb6cd71608d48ab35eef41328abda]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Mounton ward of the community of Mathern shown hatched on Map 122—

- (a) is transferred to the Pwllmeyric ward of the community of Mathern;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Shirenewton.
