# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 68

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**68.**[^c-167237a2a2ce9b9a6f775251fce2be67][^c-910dbf36e6f74f22d442b80d55e7a0d3] The part of the St Mary’s ward of the community of Chepstow shown hatched on Map 57—

- (a) is transferred to the St Kingsmark ward of the community of Chepstow;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Larkfield and St Kingsmark.
