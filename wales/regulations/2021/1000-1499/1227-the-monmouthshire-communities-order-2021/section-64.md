# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 64

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**64.**[^c-4ce404ec112043e9f6c55fa114891a54][^c-7e15fca5280e064d4cc1a21397c6fcb0] The part of the St Christophers ward of the community of Chepstow shown hatched on Map 53—

- (a) is transferred to the Larkfield ward of the community of Chepstow;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Larkfield and St Kingsmark.
