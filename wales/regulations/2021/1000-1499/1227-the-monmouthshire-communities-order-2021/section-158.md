# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 158

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**158.**[^key-13210ac4b8dee80dd6a5085716b5519e][^key-055641419406816d10f6764436eab2ae] The community of Llantrisant Fawr consists of four wards—

- (a) Gwernesney;
- (b) Llangwm;
- (c) Llansoy;
- (d) Llantrisant.
