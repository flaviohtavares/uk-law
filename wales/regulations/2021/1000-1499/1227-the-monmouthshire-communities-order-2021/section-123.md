> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 123. Llanarth and Clytha -- change in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^c-5869510620a92a84b71b3baabaaacab8][^c-ef9f47780906f171782470f7721c53e2]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Clytha ward of the community of Llanarth shown hatched on Map 94—

- (a) is transferred to the Llanarth ward of the community of Llanarth;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanover.
