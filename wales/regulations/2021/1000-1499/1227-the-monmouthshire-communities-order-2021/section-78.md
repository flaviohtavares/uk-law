> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 78. Chepstow — community ward electoral arrangements[^key-4e51ec27415a14c83c7e6a7b16584871][^key-35b53635fd5730f185b27a7813aa53a9]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Chepstow the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Larkfield ward is 2;
- (b) Bulwark ward is 4;
- (c) Chepstow Castle ward is 3;
- (d) Maple Avenue ward is 1;
- (e) Mount Pleasant ward is 3.
