# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 102

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**102.**[^key-f1890894969c56b9b7e4dea42776828a][^key-48bdc8117ae2def66f56d40719f22301] The part of the community ward of Mamhilad of the community of Goetre Fawr shown hatched on Map 79—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Llanbadoc;
- (b) forms the community ward of Little Mill;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llanbadoc.
