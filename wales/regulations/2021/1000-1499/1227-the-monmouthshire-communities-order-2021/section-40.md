# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 40

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**40.**[^key-2439527b08ae1bd8d731ae1c669b07f7][^key-1f69c9d93eb5a4c097c472c5233442fe] The part of the Green Lane ward of the community of Caldicot shown hatched on Map 35—

- (a) is transferred to the Dewstow ward of the community of Caldicot;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Dewstow.
