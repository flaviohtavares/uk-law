# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 227

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**227.**[^c-590070afb29c690e5fb77a5836c61629][^c-0c9bc5f62ab8043407d51d1b1ef9073a] The part of the community of Llangattock Vibon Avel shown hatched on Map 151—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Skenfrith;
- (b) forms part of the new community ward of Skenfrith;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Llantilio Crossenny.
