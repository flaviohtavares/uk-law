## 242. Creation of the new community ward of Newcastle[^key-f6775fae1fce209bbc705343638d4985][^key-a90c7908a2e0ed2ded0d9a85e25e7537]

*Extent: E+W*

The new community ward of Newcastle is created in the community of Whitecastle which—

- (a) comprises the area shown hatched on Map 163;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llantilio Crossenny.
