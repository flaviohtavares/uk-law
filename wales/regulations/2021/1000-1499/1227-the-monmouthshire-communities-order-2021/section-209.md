> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 209. Earlswood, Mynyddbach, Newchurch, and Shirenewton — changes in community ward boundaries and consequential changes to electoral wards[^c-82a8ee7df632bc9f5241db2bc26b082e][^c-0c0eba7815d15ba1bcbf054ac4696dcb]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Earlswood ward of the community of Shirenewton shown hatched on Map 138—

- (a) is transferred to the new Shirenewton and Mynyddbach ward of the community of Shirenewton;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Shirenewton.
