## 235. Whitecastle — creation of new community[^c-15e8505c4103c1dbe00375b7f42a543e][^c-9f4a37eb44912a3d6cebc0e0c3147202]

*Extent: E+W*

The new community of Whitecastle is created which—

- (a) comprises the area shown hatched on Map 156;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llantilio Crossenny.
