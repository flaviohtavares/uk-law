> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 16. Priory and Grofield — change in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^c-55cc4b44860702f75949073471d4c65a][^c-498c2dea9e21351e10b7190eda7625d4]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the Priory ward of the community of Abergavenny shown hatched on Map 14—

- (a) is transferred to the Grofield ward of the community of Abergavenny;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Grofield.
