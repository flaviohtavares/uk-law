## 163. Magor East, Magor West, Undy, Denny, Mill, Salisbury and The Elms — change in community ward boundaries and consequential change to electoral wards[^c-ab10ed569dc63a0a216c7ecf64c6810b][^c-7245e074842c764536250d614a71e7d3]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the of Denny ward of the community of Magor with Undy shown hatched on Map 115—

- (a) is transferred to the new Magor West ward of the community of Magor with Undy;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Magor West.
