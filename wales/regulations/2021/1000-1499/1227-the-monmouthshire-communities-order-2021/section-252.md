> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 252. New community councils[^c-0eb5ac2dcc13f48b988225fe9bb1e7e0][^c-71dd8089757e68f55ab21f6b590b0068]

*Extent: E+W*

The proper officer of Monmouthshire County Council must convene the first meeting of each of the new community councils established by articles 137, 157, 232 and 246.
