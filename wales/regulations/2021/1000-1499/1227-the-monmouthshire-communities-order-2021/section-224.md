## 224. Trellech United — community electoral arrangements[^key-1b845073c8d97373a1133327409348f7][^key-3275bd81a7c4e088a6c2e6bee5c669e4]

*Extent: E+W*

In the community of Trellech United the number of councillors to be elected for the—

- (a) Penallt ward is 3;
- (b) Trellech Town ward is 3;
- (c) Trellech Grange ward is 1.
