# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 3

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Abergavenny, Llanfoist Fawr, Llanover and Llantilio Pertholey — change in community boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^c-6ba16692f3e38061d11ea6f62bb1632f][^c-b69a44b194bca027bfff56a1028ca9ef]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the community of Llanfoist Fawr shown hatched on Map 1—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Abergavenny;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Llanwenarth Citra;
- (c) forms part of the electoral ward of Cantref.
