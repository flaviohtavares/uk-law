> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 113. Gwehelog Fawr, Llanarth and Raglan — change in community boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^key-48146997f7eeb01f169939cd3551e91f][^key-29761fa98cd3344173e6a0704c40347a]

*Extent: E+W*

The parts of the community of Gwehelog Fawr shown hatched on Map 85 and Map 86—

- (a) are transferred to the community of Llanarth;
- (b) form the community ward of Kemeys Commander and Llancayo;
- (c) form part of the electoral ward of Llanover.
