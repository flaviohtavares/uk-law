> Extent: England, Wales.

## 57. Chepstow, Mathern, and St Arvans— change in community boundaries and consequential changes to electoral arrangements[^c-48416aacfe124fe83f65be32bb3e02d7][^c-9cc041a45b18a4efb325a69ebb23d2b3]

*Extent: E+W*

The part of the community of Mathern shown hatched on Map 46—

- (a) is transferred to the community of Chepstow;
- (b) forms part of the community ward of Larkfield;
- (c) forms part of the new electoral ward of Larkfield and St Kingsmark.
