# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 95

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**95.**[^key-5d89ba2c10b6ac408164e13e3f2fe020][^key-bade8734db4114a83800185eeaf09cb4] The part of the community of Devauden shown hatched on Map 73—

- (a) is transferred to the community of St Arvans;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of St Arvans.
