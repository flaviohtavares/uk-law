# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 146

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**146.**[^c-b2a4bf2b1894772835d3f75a25630ff2][^c-cb94d106f299def740952f58ea6c8020] The part of the Sgyrrid West ward of the community of Llantilio Pertholey shown hatched on Map 105—

- (a) is transferred to the Croesonen East ward of the community of Llantilio Pertholey;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Croesonen.
