# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 89

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 89. Creation of the new community ward of Cwmyoy[^c-2de641354b64354f88c9a6d98398fb04][^c-015bb05c79f0aafa199a3a70dcf1ec38]

*Extent: E+W*

The new ward of Cwmyoy is created in the community of Crucorney which—

- (a) comprises the combined areas of the Upper Cwmyoy and Lower Cwmyoy wards of the community of Crucorney shown hatched on Map 69;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Crucorney.
