# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 165

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**165.**[^c-057a1b52cbb4a8163695dc33fb6b54bb][^c-5cfdbe9a743a16837ede4009a505b14b] The part of the Mill ward of the community of Magor with Undy shown hatched on Map 117—

- (a) is transferred to the new Magor West ward of the community of Magor with Undy;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Magor West.
