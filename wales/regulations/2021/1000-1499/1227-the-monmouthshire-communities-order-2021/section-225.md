## 225. Skenfrith — creation of new community[^key-c0962cbbec60622621e616636df71951][^key-169a21141145d539f2917c80c11898e8]

*Extent: E+W*

The new community of Skenfrith is created which—

- (a) comprises the area shown hatched on Map 149;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llantilio Crossenny.
