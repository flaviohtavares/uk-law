# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 65

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**65.**[^c-ec574100584b0123522aaaf351c7f37f][^c-3b203b789f4fab942a160c5ab2d3cc86] The part of the St Christophers ward of the community of Chepstow shown hatched on Map 54—

- (a) is transferred to the Thornwell ward of the community of Chepstow;
- (b) forms part of the new electoral ward of Bulwark and Thornwell.
