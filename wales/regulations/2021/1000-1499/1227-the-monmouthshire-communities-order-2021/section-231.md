> Text as at 2024-06-25.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 231. Creation of the new community ward of Skenfrith[^key-b007dc4883c4eca05c0c29cdce2ccfd7][^key-37ab549eb3db465c0577ef8dfa53f315]

*Extent: E+W*

The new community ward of Skenfrith is created in the community of Skenfrith which—

- (a) comprises the area shown hatched on Map 155;
- (b) forms part of the electoral ward of Llantilio Crossenny.
