# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 116

> Text as at 2024-06-25.

## 116. Gwehelog Fawr — dissolution of community and consequential change to community council[^key-1d5cfab9633301b9617e362e02a599f1][^key-af123585a9210a6838976fb7fc4bae4b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The existing community of Gwehelog Fawr as changed by this Order is dissolved.

(2) The community council of Gwehelog Fawr is dissolved.
