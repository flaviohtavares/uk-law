# The Monmouthshire (Communities) Order 2021, section 151

> Text as at 2024-06-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 151. Sgyrrid East and Sgyridd West — combination of wards and consequential change to electoral ward[^c-1a1ec63bc7634514cb9fde0ca230b27c][^c-8c9fa13a93fe264b5f1ec7eb45594ebf]

*Extent: E+W*

The existing Sgyrrid East and Sgyrrid West wards of the community of Llantilio Pertholey as changed by this Order and shown hatched on Map 110—

- (a) are combined and form the community ward of Sgyrrid of the community of Llantilio Pertholey;
- (b) form part of the electoral ward of Mardy.
