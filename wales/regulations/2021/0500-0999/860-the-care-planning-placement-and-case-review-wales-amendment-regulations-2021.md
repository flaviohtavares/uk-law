# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2021 No. 860 (W. 199)

Children And Young Persons, Wales

Education, Wales

The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2021

Made

15 July 2021

Coming into force

1 September 2021

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 83(2B) and (5), 84(b) and 196(2) of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014[^f00001], make the following Regulations.

In accordance with section 196(6) of that Act, a draft of these Regulations was laid before, and approved by a resolution of, Senedd Cymru[^f00002].

## 1. Title and commencement

The title of these Regulations is the Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2021 and they come into force on 1 September 2021.

## 2. Amendment of the Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015

The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015[^f00003] are amended as provided for in regulations 3 to 6.

## 3. Amendment to regulation 2

In the definition of “personal education plan” in regulation 2(1)[^f00004], for “in regulation 5(b)(ii)” substitute “by section 83(2A) of the 2014 Act[^f00005]”.

## 4. Amendment to regulation 3

In regulation 3, for “These Regulations” substitute “Parts 2 to 10”.

## 5. Insertion of Part 1A

After regulation 3 insert—

> **PART 1A — CHILDREN FOR WHOM NO PERSONAL EDUCATION PLAN IS TO BE PREPARED**
>
> **3A.** The following categories of looked after child are prescribed for the purposes of section 83(2B) of the 2014 Act—
>
> - (a) any child who has been placed for adoption under the Adoption and Children Act 2002[^f00006] unless the child falls within regulation 56;
> - (b) any child in relation to whom the circumstances set out in regulation 62(2) apply;
> - (c) any child who is looked after only by reason of being remanded to youth detention accommodation.

## 6. Amendments to regulation 5

In regulation 5—

- (a) omit paragraph (1)(b)(ii);
- (b) after paragraph (1) insert—

  > (1A) The personal education plan (see section 83(2A) to (2D) of the 2014 Act) must include the information set out in paragraph 2 of Schedule 2.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/1818 (W. 261)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2015/1818)) (“the 2015 Regulations”) in light of amendments to section 83 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act [2014 (anaw 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2014/4) (“the 2014 Act”) made by section 16 of the Additional Learning Needs and Education Tribunal (Wales) Act [2018 (anaw 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2018/2). Regulation 6 removes the requirement in the 2015 Regulations that a care and support plan include a personal education plan, because that requirement has been inserted into section 83 of the 2014 Act. Regulation 5 prescribes the categories of looked after children for whom no personal education plan is to be prepared as part of their care and support plan. The categories prescribed are those for whom a personal education plan was not required by the 2015 Regulations. The matters that must be included in a personal education plan are unchanged.

The Welsh Ministers’ Code of Practice on the carrying out of Regulatory Impact Assessments was considered in relation to these Regulations. As a result, a regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared as to the likely cost and benefit of complying with these Regulations.
