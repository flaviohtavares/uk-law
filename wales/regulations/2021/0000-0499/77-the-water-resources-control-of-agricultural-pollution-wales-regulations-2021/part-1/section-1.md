> Text as at 2026-10-15.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Title, application and commencement[^c-7a6fb7d0af866de0812c949086e87a8a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Water Resources (Control of Agricultural Pollution) (Wales) Regulations 2021.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

(3) These Regulations come into force on 1 April 2021.
