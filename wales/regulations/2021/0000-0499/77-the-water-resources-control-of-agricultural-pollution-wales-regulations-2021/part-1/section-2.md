# The Water Resources (Control of Agricultural Pollution) (Wales) Regulations 2021, section 2

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Transitional measures for holdings not previously in a nitrate vulnerable zone[^c-b1d0356d7b1cdfb31cc3ddabdbbd167a]

*Extent: E+W*

In a holding or part of a holding that was not previously situated within a nitrate vulnerable zone as shown on the relevant map marked “Nitrate Vulnerable Zones Index Map 2013”[^f00002] (the “NVZ index map”)[^c-3ab35ed38960abf0d56599d184e16715]—

- (a)[^c-4774cc5e6b44e1b6994aa8cfa0ba4310] regulations 5 to 11, 15, 23, 27, 33 to 35, and 37 to 43 do not apply until 1 January 2023,
- (b) regulations 4 and 36 do not apply until 1 January 2024[^c-a0136e885091a2936796d99871cd339d] however, regulation 4 does not apply to qualifying grassland holdings until 1 January 2025[^c-7fa48da0c3740e47afee9e47399ac6cb], and
- (c) regulations 17 to 21, 25, 26, and 28 to 31 do not apply until 1 August 2024.
