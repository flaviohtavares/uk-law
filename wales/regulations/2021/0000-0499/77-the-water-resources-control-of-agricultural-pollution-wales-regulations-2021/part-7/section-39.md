# The Water Resources (Control of Agricultural Pollution) (Wales) Regulations 2021, section 39

> Text as at 2026-10-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 39. Records of crops sown[^c-72e6ccb0fdf8a2a3c97bc54f27c6d42d]

*Extent: E+W*

An occupier who intends to spread nitrogen fertiliser must record within one week of sowing a crop—

- (a) the crop sown, and
- (b) the date of sowing.
