# The Water Resources (Control of Agricultural Pollution) (Wales) Regulations 2021, section 33

> Text as at 2026-10-15.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Recording the size of the holding[^c-040ebb3626a8120443de392723d63a12]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The occupier of a holding must maintain a record of the total size of the holding calculated in accordance with regulation 4(3).

(2) If the size of the holding changes this record must be updated within one month.
