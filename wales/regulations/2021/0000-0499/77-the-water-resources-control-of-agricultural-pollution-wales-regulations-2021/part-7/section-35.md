## 35. Annual records relating to storage[^key-dd36c5f0fe8fa037fd390fa1defe1d2c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Before 30 April each year the occupier of a holding with livestock must record, for the previous storage period referred to in regulation 29 the number and category of animals in a building or on a hardstanding during the storage period.

(2) The occupier must also record the sites used for field heaps and the dates of use.
