> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Subsequent records[^c-fd4ce231985489801d643a6a2c595e11]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An occupier who has used nitrogen fertiliser must record the yield achieved by an arable crop within one week of ascertaining it.

(2) Before 30 April each year an occupier must record how any grassland was managed in the previous calendar year.
