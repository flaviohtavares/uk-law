## 14. Spreading organic manure near surface water, boreholes, springs or wells[^key-d16edead0a3e65426b8210ff8c1c6fb4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Except where paragraph 26 of Schedule 1A applies,[^key-eecaca4f73500b3d625134fd573d9df2] No person may spread organic manure within 10 metres of surface water unless using precision spreading equipment in which case no person may spread organic manure within 6 metres of surface water.

(2) But livestock manure (other than slurry and poultry manure) may be spread there if—

- (a) it is spread on land managed for breeding wader birds or as a species-rich semi-natural grassland and the land is—
  - (i) notified as a Site of Special Scientific Interest under the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981[^f00005], or
  - (ii) subject to an agri-environment commitment entered into under Council Regulation [(EC) 1698/2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2005/1698)[^f00006], or Regulation (EU) 1305/2013[^f00007],
- (b) it is spread between 1 June and 31 October inclusive,
- (c) it is not spread directly on to surface water, and
- (d) the total annual amount does not exceed 12.5 tonnes per hectare.

(3) No person may spread organic manure within 50 metres of a borehole, spring or well.
