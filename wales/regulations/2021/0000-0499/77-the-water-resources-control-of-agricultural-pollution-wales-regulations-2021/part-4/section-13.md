# The Water Resources (Control of Agricultural Pollution) (Wales) Regulations 2021, section 13

> Text as at 2026-10-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Spreading manufactured nitrogen fertiliser near surface water[^c-f9bbb5a7cc2cae1be69c956a1786f503]

*Extent: E+W*

No person may spread manufactured nitrogen fertiliser within 2 metres of surface water.
