# The Education (Student Finance) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) (EU Exit) Regulations 2021, section 191

> Text as at 2021-04-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 191.[^c-5911e38941715fbb8774d81b09dcc0cc]

*Extent: E+W*

In Schedule 2, in paragraph 8 (category 8 – EU nationals)—

- (a) in the heading, at the end insert “— courses beginning before 1 August 2021”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (1A), in the English language text, for “implementation period”, in each place it occurs, substitute “IP”;
- (c) omit sub-paragraph (5).
