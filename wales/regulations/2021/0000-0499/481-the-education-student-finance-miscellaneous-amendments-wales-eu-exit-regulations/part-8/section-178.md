## 178.[^c-c751382f4d7b3abee4dbf3d4031e6fda]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 10 (eligible students – exceptions) at the end insert—

> Exception 12
>
> The designated course begins on or after 1 January 2028 and the only paragraph or paragraphs of Schedule 2 into which P falls is one or more of paragraphs 7A, 8B or 8D.
