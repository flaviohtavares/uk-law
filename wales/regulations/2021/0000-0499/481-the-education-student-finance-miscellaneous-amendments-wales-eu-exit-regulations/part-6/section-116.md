## 116.[^c-30392b484bdcfac02cecd23979397f15]

In regulation 10(1) (eligible students – exceptions), at the end insert—

> Exception 8
>
> The designated course begins on or after 1 January 2028 and the only paragraph or paragraphs in Schedule 2 into which P falls is one or more of paragraphs 5A, 6B, or 6D.
