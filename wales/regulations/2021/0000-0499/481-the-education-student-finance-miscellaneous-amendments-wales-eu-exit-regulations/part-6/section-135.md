# The Education (Student Finance) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) (EU Exit) Regulations 2021, section 135

> Text as at 2021-04-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 135.[^c-be3ae297d8833bc694427a5932465bb0]

*Extent: E+W*

In Schedule 2, in paragraph 5 (category 5 – persons who are settled in the United Kingdom and have exercised a right of residence elsewhere)—

- (a) in the heading, at the end insert “— courses beginning before 1 August 2021”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (1)(b), in the English language text, for “implementation period” substitute “IP”;
- (c) omit sub-paragraph (5).
