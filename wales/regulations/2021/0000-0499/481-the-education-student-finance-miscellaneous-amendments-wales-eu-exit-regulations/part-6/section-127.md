# The Education (Student Finance) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) (EU Exit) Regulations 2021, section 127

> Text as at 2021-04-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 127.[^c-c074671d1ca8ab52046e57ca16c0cab6]

*Extent: E+W*

In Schedule 1, in paragraph 4 (meaning of Erasmus year), for sub-paragraph (3) substitute—

> (3) In sub-paragraph (1), “ERASMUS scheme” means—
>
> - (a) the European Union’s action scheme for the mobility of university students, or
> - (b) the scheme established by the Secretary of State for Education known as the Turing Scheme.
