# The Education (Student Finance) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) (EU Exit) Regulations 2021, section 118

> Text as at 2021-04-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 118.[^c-57f5df44af49fb075c22c115df36f55d]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 22A(1) (persons who cease to have stateless leave)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “a Category 2A eligible student” substitute “an eligible student as a result of being a person granted stateless leave”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b)(ii), for “a Category 2A eligible student” substitute “an eligible student”.
