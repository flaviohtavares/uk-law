# The Education (Student Finance) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) (EU Exit) Regulations 2021, section 119

> Text as at 2021-04-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 119.[^c-c9591eaf08a53524939ffef6424bffe6]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 23A(1) (persons who cease to have section 67 leave to remain)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (a), for “a Category 3A eligible student” substitute “an eligible student as a result of being a person with section 67 leave to remain”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b)(ii), for “a Category 3A eligible student” substitute “an eligible student”.
