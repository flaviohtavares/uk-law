# The Education (Student Finance) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) (EU Exit) Regulations 2021, section 123

> Text as at 2021-04-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 123.[^c-3c21b5e70fab5e0b011ac5700f157568]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 62(2) (qualifying conditions for disabled student’s grant - exceptions), for Exception 2 substitute—

> Exception 2
>
> The only paragraph or paragraphs of Schedule 2 into which the eligible student falls is one or more of paragraphs 1(3), 6(1), 6A(1), 6C or 6D.
