# The Education (Student Finance) (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) (EU Exit) Regulations 2021, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Application

These Regulations apply in relation to the provision of support to a student, and to fees and awards applicable, in relation to an academic year which begins on or after 1 August 2021, whether or not anything done under these Regulations is done before, on, or after that date.
