# The Local Authorities (Alternative Arrangements) (Wales) Regulations 2001, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Directions by the Board to planning and licensing committees

The Board may direct that any decision to be made by a planning committee, a licensing committee or area committee in respect of a function of any of the descriptions specified in column (1) of part A of Schedule 1 (functions relating to town and country planning and development control) shall be determined by the local authority.
