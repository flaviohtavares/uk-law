# The Local Authorities (Alternative Arrangements) (Wales) Regulations 2001, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Functions which may be the responsibility of an authority’s Board

The functions specified in Schedule 2, may, but need not be, the responsibility of the Board of the authority.
