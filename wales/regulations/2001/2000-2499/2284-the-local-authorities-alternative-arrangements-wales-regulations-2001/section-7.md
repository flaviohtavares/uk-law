# The Local Authorities (Alternative Arrangements) (Wales) Regulations 2001, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Functions which are to be the responsibility of the Board

(1) A local authority operating alternative arrangements shall delegate its functions to a Board subject to the limitations in regulations 8, 9, 10 and 11.

(2) Nothing in these Regulations shall prevent a local authority from exercising those functions delegated to a Board in accordance with paragraph (1).
