# The Local Authorities (Alternative Arrangements) (Wales) Regulations 2001, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Power to make arrangements: the local authority and the Board

In the case of a local authority operating alternative arrangements—

- (a) a relevant body has power to make arrangements under section 101(5) of the 1972 Act in accordance with regulation 19; and
- (b) subject to paragraph (a) the Board may arrange for the discharge of any of its functions—
  - (i) by the Board,
  - (ii) by a member of the Board;
  - (iii) by a committee of the Board;
  - (iv) by an officer of the authority.
