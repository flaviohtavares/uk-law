# The Local Authorities (Alternative Arrangements) (Wales) Regulations 2001, section 16

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 16. Arrangements for functions of a local authority to be discharged by the Board of another local authority

(1) A local authority may make arrangements with another local authority for the discharge by the Board of that local authority of any functions of the first mentioned authority which are not the responsibility of the Board of that authority if—

- (a) the functions are not functions of that other local authority; or
- (b) the functions are functions of that other local authority and are the responsibility of the Board of that other authority.

(2) Any arrangements made under this regulation are not to prevent the authority that made the arrangements from exercising the functions to which they relate.
