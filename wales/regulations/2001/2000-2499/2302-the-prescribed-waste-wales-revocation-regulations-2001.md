# The Prescribed Waste (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2302 (W. 190)

ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION, WALES

The Prescribed Waste (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2001

Made

26th June 2001

Coming into force

27 June 2001

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary for State by section 57(6) and 63(1) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Prescribed Waste (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2001.

(2) These Regulations shall come into force on 27 June 2001.

(3) These Regulations apply to Wales.

**2.** (1) The Prescribed Waste (Wales) Regulations 2001[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00004]

Dafydd Elis-Thomas

The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

Dated 26th June 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which apply to Wales, revoke the Prescribed Waste (Wales) Regulations 2001 ([SI 2001/1506 (W. 104)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2001/1506)), which enabled the National Assembly for Wales to direct the holder of a waste management licence to accept and keep, or accept and treat or dispose of the caracasses (or part of caracasses) of certain types of animal which had been slaughtered as a result of measures taken in connection with foot-and-mouth disease.
