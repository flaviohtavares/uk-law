> Text as at 2007-03-28.

## 14. Revocations

*Extent: E+W*

The Welfare of Livestock Regulations 1994 [^c15964651] and the Welfare of Livestock (Amendment) Regulations 1998 [^c15964661] are hereby revoked as regards Wales only.
