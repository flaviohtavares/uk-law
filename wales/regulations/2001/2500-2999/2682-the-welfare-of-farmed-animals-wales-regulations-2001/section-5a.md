# The Welfare of Farmed Animals (Wales) Regulations 2001, section 5A

> Text as at 2007-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5A. Additional duties on owners and keepers of laying hens[^key-e0b846cc5e6f34921818a79faf933f1a]

*Extent: E+W*

Owners and keepers of establishments with more than 350 laying hens shall ensure that in addition to the requirements set out in Schedule 1, the conditions under which the birds are kept comply with the requirements set out in Schedules 3A, 3B, 3C and 3D.
