# The Welfare of Farmed Animals (Wales) Regulations 2001, section 1

> Text as at 2007-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Title, commencement and application

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Welfare of Farmed Animals (Wales) Regulations 2001 and, shall come into force on 31st July 2001.

(2) These Regulations shall apply to Wales only.
