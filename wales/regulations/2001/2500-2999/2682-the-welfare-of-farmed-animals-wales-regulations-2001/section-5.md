# The Welfare of Farmed Animals (Wales) Regulations 2001, section 5

> Text as at 2007-03-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Additional duties on owners and keepers of poultry (other than those kept in the systems referred to in Schedules 3A, 3B and 3C)[^key-e4ca1ce604ad83eea122062c25a68265]

*Extent: E+W*

Owners and keepers of poultry (other than those kept in the systems referred to in Schedules 3A, 3B and 3C) shall ensure that, in addition to the requirements set out in Schedule 1, the conditions under which the birds are kept comply with the requirements set out in Schedule 3.
