# The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 2681 (W.222)

HIGHWAYS, WALES

The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2001

Made

17th July 2001

Coming into force

1 August 2001

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by sections 75 and 104(1) of the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991[^f00001] and which are now exercisable by it in relation to Wales[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2001 and they come into force on the 1 August 2001.

(2) These Regulations apply to Wales.

## 2. Increase in the inspection fee

In regulation 3(1) of the Street Works (Inspection Fees) Regulations 1992[^f00003] there shall be substituted for the figure of “£14.50” there the figure of “£15.50”.

## 3. Revocation

The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1998[^f00004] are revoked so far as they apply to Wales.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00005].

D. Elis Thomas

Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

17th July 2001

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations provide for an increase from £14.50 to £15.50 in the fee payable by undertakers for inspections of their work by street authorities in Wales under regulation 3(1) of the Street Works (Inspection Fees) Regulations 1992 (S.I. [1992/1688](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1688)). The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Amendment) Regulations 1998 (S.I. [1998/978](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/978)) (which provided for an earlier increase in the fee) are revoked so far as they apply to Wales.
