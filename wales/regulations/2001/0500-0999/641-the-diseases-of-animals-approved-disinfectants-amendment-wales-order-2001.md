# The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (Wales) Order 2001

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2001 No. 641 ( W.31 )

ANIMALS, WALES

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (Wales) Order 2001

Made at: —

4:55pm on 1st March 2001

Coming into force at: —

5:00 pm on 1st March 2001

The National Assembly for Wales and the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, acting jointly, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 1, 7(1)(a), (b), (c) and (d), 7(2) and 23(f) and (g) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001], and now vested in them[^f00002], make the following Order—

## 1. Title, commencement and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) (Amendment) (Wales) Order 2001 and shall come into force at 5.00 p.m. on 1st March 2001.

(2) This Order shall apply to Wales only.

## 2. Amendments to the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978

The Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978[^f00003] (“the 1978 Order”) shall be amended by adding the entries listed in the Schedule to this Order to Schedule 1 to the 1978 Order.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00004]

D. Elis Thomas

The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

Signed at 4:00pm on 1st March 2001

Helene Hayman

Minister of State, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food

Signed at 4:55pm on 1st March 2001

This Order, which applies in Wales only (article 1(2)), amends the Diseases of Animals (Approved Disinfectants) Order 1978 (S.I. [1978/32](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1978/32)), referred to in this Order as “the 1978 Order”.

This Order (article 2 and the Schedule) amends Schedule 1 to the 1978 Order by adding to that schedule several entries for other approved disinfectants.

This Order has been notified under the urgent reasons provisions of Article 9C(7) of Directive [98/34/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0034) of the European Parliment and Council of 22 June 1998 laying down a procedure for the provision of information in the field of technical standards and regulations (OJ No. L204, 21.7.98, p.37).

A Regulatory Appraisal (made under section 65(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998 and the Standing Orders of the National Assembly for Wales) has not been prepared in respect of this Order.
