# The Marketing of Vegetable Plant Material (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2190 (W.174)

PLANT HEALTH, WALES

The Marketing of Vegetable Plant Material (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2007

Made

25 July 2007

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

27 July 2007

Coming into force

28 August 2007

The Welsh Ministers are designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community.

They make the following Regulations under the powers conferred by that section:

## 1. Title, commencement and application

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Marketing of Vegetable Plant Material (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2007.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 28 August 2007 and apply in relation to Wales.

## 2. Amendments to the Marketing of Vegetable Plant Material Regulations 1995

(1) The Marketing of Vegetable Plant Material Regulations 1995[^f00003] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 2 (interpretation), in paragraph (1), for the definition of “Directive [92/33/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0033)” substitute—

> - “Directive [92/33/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0033)” means Council Directive [92/33/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0033) on the marketing of vegetable propagating and planting material other than seed;

[^f00004].

(3) In regulation 3 (plant material to which these Regulations apply)—

- (a) in paragraph (1)(a), for “Schedule 1” substitute “Annex II to Directive [92/33/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0033)”;
- (b) in paragraph (1)(b), for “Schedule 1” substitute “that Annex”.

(4) In regulation 8 (information to accompany plant material)—

- (a) in paragraph (2), for “Council Directive [77/93/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0093)” substitute “Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029)”[^f00005];
- (b) in paragraph (4), for “Schedule 1” substitute “Schedule 2”.

(5) Omit Schedule 1 (genera and species to which Regulations apply).

I. W. Jones

Deputy First Minister and Minister for Economy and Transport, one of the Welsh Ministers

25 July 2007

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations, which apply in relation to Wales, come into force on 28 August 2007. They amend the Marketing of Vegetable Plant Material Regulations 1995 (S.I. [1995/2652](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/2652)) (“the principal Regulations”), to update the list of genera and species of plant material to which those Regulations apply. This gives effect to Article 1 of Commission Directive [2006/124/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0124) (OJ No. L 339, 6.12.2006, p.12) which amends Annex II to Council Directive [92/33/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0033) (OJ No. L 157, 10.6.1992, p.1) on the marketing of vegetable propagating and planting material other than seed, by extending its scope to Zea Mays L. (popcorn and sweetcorn).

In addition to minor and drafting amendments, regulation 2 provides that the principal Regulations apply to the list of genera and species in Annex II to Council Directive [92/33/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0033).

A regulatory impact assessment has been carried out in relation to these Regulations. Copies of that assessment can be obtained from the Welsh Assembly Government, Cathays Park, Cardiff CF10 3NQ.
