# The Trinity College, Carmarthen (Designated Institutions in Higher Education) (Wales) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2112 (W.170)

EDUCATION, WALES

The Trinity College, Carmarthen (Designated Institutions in Higher Education) (Wales) Order 2007

Made

23 July 2007

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

24 July 2007

Coming into force

1 September 2007

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 129(1) of the Education Reform Act 1988[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002], make the following Order:

## 1. Title, commencement and application

(1) The title of this Order is the Trinity College, Carmarthen (Designated Institutions in Higher Education) (Wales) Order 2007 and it comes into force on 1 September 2007.

(2) This Order applies to Wales.

## 2. Designation

Trinity College, Carmarthen, being an educational institution of the kind described in sub-section (2) of section 129 of the Education Reform Act 1988, is designated as an institution for the purpose of that section.

Jane E. Hutt

Minister for Education, Children and Young People, one of the Welsh Ministers

23 July 2007

(This note is not part of the Order)

Trinity College, Carmarthen is designated by this Order for the purposes of section 129 of the Education Reform Act 1988. This means that it is eligible to receive support from funds administered by a higher education funding council and is to be treated as an institution within the higher education sector for the purposes of the Education Acts.
