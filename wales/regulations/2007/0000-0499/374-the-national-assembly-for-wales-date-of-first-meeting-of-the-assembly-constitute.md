# The National Assembly for Wales (Date of First Meeting of the Assembly Constituted by the Government of Wales Act 2006) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 374 (W.34)

CONSTITUTIONAL LAW

DEVOLUTION, WALES

The National Assembly for Wales (Date of First Meeting of the Assembly Constituted by the Government of Wales Act 2006) Order 2007

Made

13 February 2007

Coming into force

14 February 2007

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the power conferred on it by paragraph 3 of Schedule 11 to the Government of Wales Act 2006[^f00001], makes the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

The title of this Order is the National Assembly for Wales (Date of First Meeting of the Assembly Constituted by the Government of Wales Act 2006) Order 2007 and it comes into force on 14 February 2007.

## 2. Date of First Meeting of the Assembly Constituted by the Government of Wales Act 2006

The date for the first meeting of the Assembly constituted by the Government of Wales Act 2006 after the 2007 election is 9 May 2007.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00002].

D. Elis-Thomas

The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

13 February 2007

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which is made under paragraph 3 of Schedule 11 to the Government of Wales Act [2006 (c. 32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/32), provides that the date specified for the first meeting of the Assembly constituted by that Act is 9 May 2007.
