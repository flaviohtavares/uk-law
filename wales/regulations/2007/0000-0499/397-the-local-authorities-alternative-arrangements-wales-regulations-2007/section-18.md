# The Local Authorities (Alternative Arrangements) (Wales) Regulations 2007, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Revocation

The following are revoked—

- (a) the Local Authorities (Alternative Arrangements) (Wales) Regulations 2001 [^f00011]
- (b) the Local Authorities (Alternative Arrangements) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2002[^f00012];
- (c) the Local Authorities (Alternative Arrangements) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2003[^f00013]; and
- (d) the Local Authorities (Alternative Arrangements) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2004[^f00014]).
