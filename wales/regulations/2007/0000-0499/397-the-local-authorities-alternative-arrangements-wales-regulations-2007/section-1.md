# The Local Authorities (Alternative Arrangements) (Wales) Regulations 2007, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Title, commencement and application

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Local Authorities (Alternative Arrangements) (Wales) Regulations 2007 and they come into force on 16 February 2007.

(2) These Regulations apply to Wales.
