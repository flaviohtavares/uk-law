# The Local Authorities (Executive Arrangements) (Functions and Responsibilities) (Wales) Regulations 2007, section 4

> Text as at 2025-09-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Functions which may be the responsibility of an authority’s executive[^key-6f4cb8973ae82ab028bbcff456a98c0f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The functions specified in Schedule 2, may, but need not be, the responsibility of an executive of the authority.

(2) Nothing in these Regulations prevents a local authority from exercising those functions delegated to an executive of the authority
