# The Local Authorities (Executive Arrangements) (Functions and Responsibilities) (Wales) Regulations 2007, section 1

> Text as at 2025-09-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Title, commencement and application[^c-b72d91624fad6b11ff9543b97eb6b242]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Local Authorities (Executive Arrangements) (Functions and Responsibilities) (Wales) Regulations 2007 and they come into force on 16 February 2007.

(2) These Regulations apply to Wales.
