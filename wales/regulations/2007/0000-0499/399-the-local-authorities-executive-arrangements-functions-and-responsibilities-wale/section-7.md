# The Local Authorities (Executive Arrangements) (Functions and Responsibilities) (Wales) Regulations 2007, section 7

> Text as at 2025-09-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Revocation[^c-d88b7bbd13151a4b7846b786ee76e24b]

*Extent: E+W*

The following are revoked—

- (a) the Local Authorities Executive Arrangements (Functions and Responsibilities) (Wales) Regulations 2001[^f00007];
- (b) the Local Authorities Executive Arrangements (Functions and Responsibilities) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2002[^f00008];
- (c) the Local Authorities Executive Arrangements (Functions and Responsibilities) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2003[^f00009]; and
- (d) the Local Authorities Executive Arrangements (Functions and Responsibilities) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2004[^f00010].
