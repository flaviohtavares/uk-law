The Welsh Ministers, with the consent of the Treasury, make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by section 56(1) of the Finance Act 1973[^f00001] and now vested in them:

## 1. Title, commencement and application

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2007.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 13 July 2007 and apply in relation to Wales.

## 2. Amendment of the Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) (Wales) (No.2) Regulations 2006

(1) The Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) (Wales) (No. 2) Regulations 2006[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Plant Health (Import Inspection Fees) (Wales) (No.2) Regulations 2006 (S.I. [2006/2832 (W. 253)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2006/2832)) (“the principal Regulations”). The principal Regulations implement Article 13d of Council Directive [2000/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0029) (OJ No. L 169, 10.7.00, p. 1), which requires member States to charge fees to cover the costs occasioned by the documentary checks, identity checks and plant health checks of certain imports of plants, plant products and other objects from third countries.

Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations includes provision for reduced rates for certain plants and plant products agreed under the procedure provided for in Articles 13d(2) and 18(2) of the Directive. These Regulations substitute a new Schedule 2 to the principal Regulations, to give effect to the latest agreement in respect of reduced rate fees.
