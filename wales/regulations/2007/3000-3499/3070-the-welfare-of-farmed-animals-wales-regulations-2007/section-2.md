# The Welfare of Farmed Animals (Wales) Regulations 2007, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Definitions and interpretation[^key-4a0e99423ebed08b77e0a615167f9a0f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In these Regulations—

- “calf” (“*llo*”) means a bovine animal up to six months old;
- “conventionally reared meat chicken” (“[^key-84e4292412fa8bbaa18b514231f3ea95]*iâr fwyta a fegir yn gonfensiynol*”) means an animal of the species *Gallus gallus* that is kept for meat production, other than one—
  - (a) that is on a holding with fewer than 500 such animals or with only breeding stocks of such animals;
  - (b) that is on a hatchery;
  - (c) in relation to which the term “Extensive indoor (barn-reared)” (“*Megir yn helaeth dan do (mewn cytiau*)”), “Free range” (“*Iâr fuarth*”), “Traditional free range” (“*Iâr fuarth-dull traddodiadol*”) or “Free range – total freedom” (“Iâr fuarth– dull hollol rydd”) can be used within the meaning of point (b), (c), (d) or (e) of Annex V to Commission Regulation [(EC) No 543/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0543) laying down detailed rules for the application of Council Regulation [(EC) No 1234/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/1234) as regards the marketing standards for poultrymeat[^f00005]; or
  - (d) that is organically reared in accordance with Council Regulation [(EC) No 834/2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2007/0834) on organic production and labelling of organic products and repealing Regulation [(EEC) No 2092/91](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1991/2092)[^f00006];
- “food business operator” (“*gweithredydd busnes bwyd*”) has the meaning given by Article 3 of Regulation [(EC) No 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00007];
- “keeper” (“*ceidwad*”) means any person responsible for or in charge of animals whether on a permanent or temporary basis;
- “laying hen” (“*iâr ddodwy*”) means a hen of the species Gallus gallus which has reached laying maturity and is kept for the production of eggs not intended for hatching;
- “litter” (“*llaesodr*”) means, in relation to laying hens and conventionally reared meat chickens[^key-70804f26c0a8c52e0b8610866e3a939d], any friable material enabling those birds[^key-f12659e4fde830af9f89a49bf0f53620] to satisfy their ethological needs;
- “nest” (“*nyth*”) means a separate space for egg laying, the floor component of which may not include wire mesh that can come into contact with the birds, for an individual hen or for a group of hens;
- “person responsible” (“*person sy'n gyfrifol*”) for an animal has the same meaning as in section 3 of the Animal Welfare Act 2006;
- “pig” (“*mochyn*”) means an animal of the porcine species of any age, kept for breeding or fattening;
- “stocking density” (“[^key-090868edb12cf134b5d08fba32113781]*dwysedd stocio*”) means, in relation to any house in which conventionally reared meat chickens are kept, the total live weight of chickens which are present in the house at the same time per m<sup>2</sup> of usable area;
- “usable area” (“*lle y gellir ei ddefnyddio*”) means , in relation to laying hens,[^key-48ef0a5570935c8bcd214a26142fe75f] an area, other than that taken up by a nest, used by laying hens which is at least 30cm wide with a floor slope not exceeding 14% and with headroom of at least 45cm , or, in relation to conventionally reared meat chickens, a littered area accessible to the chickens at any time[^key-c74249adf365f091e90f46ad7951abba].

(2) Expressions used in these Regulations that are not defined in these Regulations and are used in the following Directives, have the meaning they bear in those Directives—

- (a) in relation to pigs, [Directive 2008/120/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0120)[^key-5ce60f2c5d3e86252138bef74e7133d4] laying down minimum standards for the protection of pigs;
- (aa)[^key-975b0abec9efeb2c4c5b87e06a48ec1f] in relation to conventionally reared meat chickens, Council Directive [2007/43/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0043) laying down minimum rules for the protection of chickens kept for meat production[^f00008].
- (b) in relation to laying hens, Directive [99/74/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0074)[^f00005]; [^key-0fd2c4526a66bc7e0834cf5dc2dbbb7f]...
- (c) in relation to calves, [Directive 2008/119/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2008/0119)[^key-ec62996e908ec83edcfa7c7220455fa2] laying down minimum standards for the protection of calves.

(3) An expression used in regulation 4 or Schedule 1, which is not defined in these Regulations and which appears in Directive [98/58/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1998/0058)[^f00009], has the same meaning as it has for the purposes of that Directive.
