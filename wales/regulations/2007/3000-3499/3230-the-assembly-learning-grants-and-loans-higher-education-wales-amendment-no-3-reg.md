# The Assembly Learning Grants and Loans (Higher Education) (Wales) (Amendment) (No.3) Regulations 2007

Made

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

13 November 2007

Coming into force

4 December 2007

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by sections 22, 42(6) and 43(1) of the Teaching and Higher Education Act 1998[^f00001], and now exercisable by them[^f00002] make the following Regulations:

## 1. Title, commencement and application

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Assembly Learning Grants and Loans (Higher Education) (Wales) (Amendment) (No. 3) Regulations 2007.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 4 December 2007 and apply in relation to Wales.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations “the 2007 Regulations” (“*Rheoliadau 2007*”) means the Assembly Learning Grants and Loans (Higher Education) (Wales) Regulations 2007[^f00003].

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Assembly Learning Grants and Loans (Higher Education) (Wales) Regulations 2007 (“the 2007 Regulations”).

## Amendments to the 2007 Regulations

The 2007 Regulations provide for support for students taking designated higher education courses in respect of an academic year beginning on or after 1 September 2007.

These Regulations amend the 2007 Regulations in relation to the provision of support to students who are undertaking designated distance learning courses set out in the new regulations 61E (support for distance learning courses), 61F (amount of support) and 61H (disabled distance learning students' allowance).

Regulations 4 to 16 amend the figures in regulations 61E, 61F and 61 H of the 2007 Regulations.
