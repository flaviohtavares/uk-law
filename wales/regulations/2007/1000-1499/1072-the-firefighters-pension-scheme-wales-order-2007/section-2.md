# The Firefighters' Pension Scheme (Wales) Order 2007, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. New pension scheme for firefighters in Wales

The New Firefighters' Pension Scheme (Wales), which is set out in Schedule 1 to this Order, and which makes provision for the payment of pensions and lump sums to and in respect of persons who are or have been employed by Welsh fire and rescue authorities as firefighters (including persons who die while so employed), has effect.
