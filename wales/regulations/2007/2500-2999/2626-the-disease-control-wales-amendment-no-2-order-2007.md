# The Disease Control (Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2626 (W.223)

ANIMALS, WALES

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Disease Control (Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2007

Made

7 September 2007

Coming into force

12.00 noon on 8 September 2007

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 8(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

(1) The title of this Order is the Disease Control (Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Order 2007.

(2) This Order applies in relation to Wales and comes into force at 12.00 noon on 8 September 2007.

## 2. Amendment

The Disease Control (Wales) Order 2003[^f00002] is amended by omitting article 3A.

Rhodri Morgan AC/AM

First Minister

7 September 2007

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Disease Control (Wales) Order 2003 (S.I. [2003/1966 (W.211)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2003/1966)) to remove the emergency measures that increase the standstill period from 6 to 20 days and disapply paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 and paragraph 13 of Schedule 2 in respect of certain movements in a sole occupancy group.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared for this Order.
