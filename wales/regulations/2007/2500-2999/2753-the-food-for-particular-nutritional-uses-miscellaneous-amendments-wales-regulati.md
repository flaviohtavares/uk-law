# The Food for Particular Nutritional Uses (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) Regulations 2007

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2007 No. 2753 (W.232)

FOOD, WALES

The Food for Particular Nutritional Uses (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) Regulations 2007

Made

18 September 2007

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

19 September 2007

Coming into force

15 October 2007

The Welsh Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 17(1) and (2), 26(1)(a) and 48(1) of the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001].

In accordance with section 48(4A) of the Food Safety Act 1990, the Welsh Ministers have had regard to relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency.

As required by Article 9 of Regulation [(EC) No. 178/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0178) of the European Parliament and of the Council laying down the general principles and requirements of food law, establishing the European Food Safety Authority and laying down procedures in matters of food safety[^f00002], there has been open and transparent public consultation during the preparation and evaluation of these Regulations.

## 1. Title, application and commencement

The title of these Regulations is the Food for Particular Nutritional Uses (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) Regulations 2007; they apply in relation to Wales and come into force on 15 October 2007.

## 2. Amendment of the Infant Formula and Follow-on Formula Regulations 1995

(1) The Infant Formula and Follow-on Formula Regulations 1995[^f00003] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2) in so far as they apply in relation to Wales.

(2) In regulation 22 (offences and enforcement)—

- (a) in paragraph (1), insert at the beginning the words “Subject to paragraph (2B),”; and
- (b) immediately after paragraph (2A), add the following paragraph—

  > (2B) A person shall not be considered to have contravened or failed to comply with the provisions contained in regulation 2 where the sale which would otherwise have constituted a contravention of or a failure to comply with those provisions is the sale of an infant formula falling within the derogation set out in Article 1 of Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 1609/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/1609) authorising the placing on the market of infant formulae based on hydrolysates of whey protein derived from cows' milk protein for a two-year period[^f00004].

  .

## 3. Amendment of the Foods Intended for Use in Energy Restricted Diets for Weight Reduction Regulations 1997

(1) The Foods Intended for Use in Energy Restricted Diets for Weight Reduction Regulations 1997[^f00005] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2) in so far as they apply in relation to Wales.

(2) In regulation 4 (labelling, advertising and presentation) omit the words “or to a reduction in the sense of hunger or an increase in the sense of satiety”.

## 4. Amendment of the Medical Food (Wales) Regulations 2000

(1) The Medical Food (Wales) Regulations 2000[^f00006] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In regulation 2 (interpretation), for the definition of “the Directive” (“*y Gyfarwyddeb*”) substitute the following definition—

> - “the Directive” (“*y Gyfarwyddeb*”) means Commission Directive [1999/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0021) on dietary foods for special medical purposes[^f00007] as amended by the Act concerning the conditions of accession of the Czech Republic, the Republic of Estonia, the Republic of Cyprus, the Republic of Latvia, the Republic of Lithuania, the Republic of Hungary, the Republic of Malta, the Republic of Poland, the Republic of Slovenia and the Slovak Republic and the adjustments to the Treaties on which the European Union is founded[^f00008] and Commission Directive [2006/82/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0082) adapting Directive 91/321 on infant formulae and follow-on formulae and Directive [1999/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0021) on dietary foods for special medical purposes, by reason of the accession of Bulgaria and Romania[^f00009];

.

## 5. Amendment of the Food for Particular Nutritional Uses (Addition of Substances for Specific Nutritional Purposes) (Wales) Regulations 2002

(1) The Food for Particular Nutritional Uses (Addition of Substances for Specific Nutritional Purposes) (Wales) Regulations 2002[^f00010] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In paragraph (5) of regulation 3 (restrictions on sale) for the words “1st January 2007” substitute the words “1st January 2010”.

## 6. Amendment of the Processed Cereal-based Foods and Baby Foods for Infants and Young Children (Wales) Regulations 2004

(1) The Processed Cereal-based Foods and Baby Foods for Infants and Young Children (Wales) Regulations 2004[^f00011] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation), for the definition of “the Directive” (“*y Gyfarwyddeb*”) substitute the following definition —

> - “the Directive” (“*y Gyfarwyddeb*”) means Commission Directive [2006/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0125) on processed cereal-based foods and baby foods for infants and young children[^f00012];

.

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

**1.** These Regulations, which apply in relation to Wales, amend the Regulations identified in the following paragraphs in the manner described in those paragraphs.

**2.** These Regulations amend the Infant Formula and Follow-on Formula Regulations 1995 (S.I. [1995/77](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1995/77), as amended, “the 1995 Regulations”) in so far as they apply in relation to Wales to provide for the execution and enforcement of Commission Regulation (EC) No. [1609/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1609/2006) authorising the placing on the market of infant formula based on hydrolysates of whey protein derived from cows' milk protein for a two-year period (OJ No. L299, 28.10.2006, p.9). They amend the 1995 Regulations to provide that a person will not be considered to have contravened or failed to comply with the provisions contained in regulation 2 of the 1995 Regulations where the sale which would otherwise have constituted a contravention of or a failure to comply with those provisions and thus an offence is the sale of an infant formula falling within the derogation set out in Commission Regulation (EC) No. [1609/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1609/2006) (regulation 2(2)).

**3.** These Regulations amend the Foods Intended for Use in Energy Restricted Diets for Weight Reduction Regulations 1997 (S.I. [1997/2182](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/2182), as amended, “the 1997 Regulations”) in so far as they apply in relation to Wales to implement Commission Directive [2007/29/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0029) amending Directive [96/8/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0008) as regards labelling, advertising or presenting foods intended for use in energy-restricted diets for weight reduction (OJ No. L139, 31.5.2007, p.22). They amend the 1997 Regulations to provide for the removal of the prohibition on selling certain foods where the labelling, advertising or presentation of that food refers to a reduction in the sense of hunger or an increase in the sense of satiety (regulation 3(2)).

**4.** These Regulations amend the Medical Food (Wales) Regulations 2000 (S.I. [2000/1866 (W.125)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2000/1866), as amended, “the 2000 Regulations”) to implement Commission Directive [2006/82/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0082) adapting Directive [91/321/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1991/0321) on infant formulae and follow-on formulae and Directive [1999/21/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1999/0021) on dietary food for special medical purposes, by reason of the accession of Bulgaria and Romania (OJ No. L362, 20.12.2006, p.94). They amend the 2000 Regulations by substituting a revised definition of “the Directive” (to take account of Commission Directive [2006/82/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0082)) for the existing definition of “the Directive” in regulation 2 (interpretation) (regulation 4(2)).

**5.** These Regulations amend the Food for Particular Nutritional Uses (Addition of Substances for Specific Nutritional Purposes) (Wales) Regulations 2002 (S.I. [2002/2939 (W.280)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2002/2939), as amended, “the 2002 Regulations”) to implement Commission Directive [2007/26/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2007/0026) amending Directive [2004/6/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2004/0006) to extend its period of application (OJ No. L118, 8.5.2007, p.5). They amend the 2002 Regulations to provide that certain restrictions contained in those Regulations will not apply to certain substances until 1st January 2010 (regulation 5(2)).

**6.** These Regulations amend the Processed Cereal-based Foods and Baby Foods for Infants and Young Children (Wales) Regulations 2004 (S.I. [2004/314 (W.32)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2004/314), as amended, “the 2004 Regulations”) to implement Commission Directive [2006/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0125) on processed cereal-based foods and baby foods for infants and young children (OJ No. L339, 6.12.2006, p.16). They amend the 2004 Regulations by substituting a revised definition of the “the Directive” (so that it now refers to Commission Directive [2006/125/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0125)) for the existing definition of “the Directive” in paragraph (1) of regulation 2 (interpretation) (regulation 6(2)).

**7.** A full regulatory impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business and the voluntary sector is available from the Food Standards Agency, 11th Floor, Southgate House, Cardiff, CF10 1EW.
