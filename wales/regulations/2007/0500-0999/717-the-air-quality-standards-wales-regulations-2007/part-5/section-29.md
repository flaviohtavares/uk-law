# The Air Quality Standards (Wales) Regulations 2007, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Collation of information etc.

(1) The National Assembly must ensure that the information specified in Part 1 of Schedule 12 is obtained and collated.

(2) The criteria for aggregating data and calculating statistical parameters specified in Part 2 of Schedule 12 applies as respects ozone.
