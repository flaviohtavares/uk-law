# The Air Quality Standards (Wales) Regulations 2007, section 17

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 17. Measurement of PM<sub>2·5</sub>

(1) The National Assembly must, in accordance with paragraph (2), install and operate measuring stations to supply representative data on concentrations of PM<sub>2·5</sub>.

(2) For the purpose of paragraph (1), the National Assembly must—

- (a) choose the number of measuring stations it considers necessary;
- (b) insofar as possible, co-locate the measuring stations with any sampling points established for PM<sub>10</sub> in accordance with regulation 15(1), or
  - (ii) in any other case, locate the measuring stations in accordance with the principles set out in Parts 1, 4 and 5 of Schedule 5 as they apply to PM<sub>10</sub>;
- (c) use reference methods for sampling and measurement that it considers suitable; and
- (d) have regard to the data quality objectives set out in Part 1 of Schedule 8.
