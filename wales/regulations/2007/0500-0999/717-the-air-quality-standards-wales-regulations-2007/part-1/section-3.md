# The Air Quality Standards (Wales) Regulations 2007, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Designation of National Assembly for Wales

The National Assembly is designated as the competent authority for the purposes of Article 3 (implementation and responsibilities) of Council Directive [96/62/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1996/0062).
