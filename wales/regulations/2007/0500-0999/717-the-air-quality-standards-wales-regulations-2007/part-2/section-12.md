# The Air Quality Standards (Wales) Regulations 2007, section 12

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 12. Duty to assess air quality

The National Assembly must assess the concentration of each pollutant within each zone in accordance with regulations 13 to 16.
