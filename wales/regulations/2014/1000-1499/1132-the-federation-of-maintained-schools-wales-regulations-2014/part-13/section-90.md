> Text as at 2022-02-04.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 90. Incorporation of governing body of a school leaving a federation[^key-16b7025c70579da7680a5958460a69fe]

*Extent: E+W*

On the date of dissolution the temporary governing body of each federated school is incorporated as the governing body of each de-federated school under the name given in the school’s instrument of government.
