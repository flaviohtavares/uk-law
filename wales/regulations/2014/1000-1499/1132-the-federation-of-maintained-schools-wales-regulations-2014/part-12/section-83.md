# The Federation of Maintained Schools (Wales) Regulations 2014, section 83

> Text as at 2022-02-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 83. Incorporation of governing body of a school leaving a federation[^key-85ae20f9bf1ae7aa4f85cf76e8af69ff]

*Extent: E+W*

On the de-federation date the temporary governing body of the de-federated school is incorporated as the governing body of that school under the name given in the school’s instrument of government.
