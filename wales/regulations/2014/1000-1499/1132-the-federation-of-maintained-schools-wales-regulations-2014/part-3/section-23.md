## 23. Representative governors[^c-72b9f6eea3fbc8652d97f62c61a7591e]

*Extent: E+W*

In these Regulations “representative governor” (“*llywodraethwr cynrychiadol*”) means a person who is appointed as such in accordance with Schedule 6.
