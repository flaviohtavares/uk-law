## 24. Associate pupil governors[^key-8fca7f060cc4a65bbfd10d2d8a85e553]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In these Regulations “associate pupil governor” (“*disgybl-lywodraethwr cyswllt*”) means a registered pupil nominated by the school council to be a member of the federated governing body and appointed as such by the federated governing body in accordance with regulation 7 of the School Council Regulations.

(2) The maximum number of associate pupil governors on any federated governing body is two.
