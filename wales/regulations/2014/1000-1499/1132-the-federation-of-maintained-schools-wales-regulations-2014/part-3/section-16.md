> Extent: England, Wales.

## 16. Staff governors[^key-838dc7d7ef7ad451428941e269d1fb81]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In these Regulations “staff governor” (“*staff-lywodraethwr*”) means a person —

- (a) who is elected in accordance with Schedule 3 as a member of the governing body of a federation by persons who are employed to work at the federation or at a federated school otherwise than as a school teacher; and
- (b) who is a person so working at the time when that person is elected.

(2) Upon ceasing to work at a school within the federation, a staff governor of a school is disqualified from continuing to hold office as such a governor.

(3) Subject to paragraph (4) a person is disqualified from election as a staff governor to a governing body if that person—

- (a) has previously been elected as a staff governor to the same governing body within the last two years; or
- (b) is employed to work at the same federated school as any person elected as a staff governor to that governing body within the last two years.

(4) Paragraph (3)(b) does not apply to—

- (a)[^key-ba993e399fdcdaed1f4ff70422167f74] any person employed to work at two or more schools in the federation ; or[^key-2815da70a706f1a9ba6510e179449bf2]
- (b) a federation that consists of only two schools.
