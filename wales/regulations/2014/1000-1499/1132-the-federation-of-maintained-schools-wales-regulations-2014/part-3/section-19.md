## 19. Additional community governors[^key-966fd0f100a59a0f314691567e32811e]

*Extent: E+W*

In these Regulations “additional community governor” (“*llywodraethwr cymunedol ychwanegol*”) means a governor appointed in accordance with regulation 31.
