## 15. Teacher governors[^c-05a7805112492d92ad8fb373a9bb27e9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In these Regulations “teacher governor” (“*athro-lywodraethwr*”) means a person—

- (a) who is elected in accordance with Schedule 3 as a governor by school teachers at any school within the federation; and
- (b) who is such a school teacher at a time when that person is elected.

(2) Upon ceasing to work at the school a teacher governor is disqualified from continuing to hold office as such a governor.

(3) Subject to paragraph (4) a person is disqualified from election as a teacher governor to a governing body if that person—

- (a) has previously been elected as a teacher governor to the same governing body within the last two years; or
- (b) is employed to work at the same federated school as any person elected as a teacher governor to that governing body within the last two years.

(4) Paragraph (3)(b) does not apply to—

- (a)[^c-dc3da6ee577596f78ae948870e5ace13] any person employed to work at two or more federated schools in the federation ; or[^c-dcd34d795a43ae2557881069ae787300]
- (b) a federation that consists of only two schools.
