# The Federation of Maintained Schools (Wales) Regulations 2014, section 41

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Procedure for removal of governors by the governing body[^key-9215ad5802e4d223b5a15aec3d90ab68]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to the removal of a governor from office in accordance with regulation 39 or 40.

(2) A resolution to remove a governor from office which is passed at a meeting of the governing body does not have effect unless—

- (a) before the governing body resolve to remove the governor from office, the governor or governors proposing that person’s removal at that meeting state their reasons for doing so and the governor whom it is proposed to remove is given an opportunity to make a statement in response; and
- (b) the matter of the governor’s removal from office is specified as an item of business on the agenda for the meeting.

(3) After a resolution to remove a governor from office has been passed the governing body must inform the person removed from office of the reasons for the removal in writing.
