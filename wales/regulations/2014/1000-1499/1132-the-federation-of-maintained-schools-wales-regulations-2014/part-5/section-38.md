## 38. Removal of local authority, foundation, representative, additional community and sponsor governors[^c-8e9c7fd9d245f172413c30952fefdd37]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any local authority governor, foundation governor, or representative governor may be removed from office by the person who appointed that governor, who must give written notice thereof to the clerk to the governing body and to the governor so removed.

(2) An ex officio foundation governor may be removed from office by the person named in the instrument of government as the person entitled to remove that governor and that person must give written notice thereof to the clerk to the governing body and to the governor so removed.

(3) Any additional community governor or sponsor governor may be removed by the person who nominated that governor who must give written notice thereof to the clerk to the governing body and to the governor so removed.
