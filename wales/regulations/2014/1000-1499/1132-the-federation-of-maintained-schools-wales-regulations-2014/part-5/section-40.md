## 40. Removal of appointed parent governors, partnership governors and associate pupil governors[^key-ac198041b356241ffac88ee9a867631f]

*Extent: E+W*

Any parent governor appointed by the governing body under paragraphs 9 to 11 of Schedule 2, any partnership governor and any associate pupil governor may be removed by the governing body in accordance with the procedure set out in regulation 41.
