# The Federation of Maintained Schools (Wales) Regulations 2014, section 76

> Text as at 2022-02-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 76. Information for the governing body of a federation[^c-01f4f046410f11c50e0edcd36d51afb4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Immediately before the federation date, the governing body of a school which is to become a federated school must prepare, for the purpose of assisting the governing body of the federation, a written report on the action which it has taken in the discharge of its functions relating to the school.

(2) All minutes and papers of a governing body of a school which is to become a federated school on the federation date, including the report prepared under paragraph (1), is to be made available to the governing body of the federation.
