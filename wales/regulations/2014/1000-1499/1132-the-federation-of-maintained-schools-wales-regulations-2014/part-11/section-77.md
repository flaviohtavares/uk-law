## 77. Financing of federations[^key-ba536a4609afea7910acf65fa94a5cb9]

*Extent: E+W*

Except as provided by regulation 78, Chapter 4 of Part 2 of the 1998 Act (*financing of maintained schools*) applies to federated schools and their governing bodies as it applies to other maintained schools and their governing bodies.
