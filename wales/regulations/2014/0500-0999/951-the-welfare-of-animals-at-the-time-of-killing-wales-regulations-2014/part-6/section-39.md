> Extent: England, Wales.

## 39. Appeals against enforcement notices[^c-d9d516a7ab6e791d6ecce415815d7008]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person who is aggrieved by—

- (a) a decision of an inspector to serve an enforcement notice; or
- (b) a decision of an inspector to refuse to issue a completion notice,

may appeal against it.

(2) The right of appeal is to the First-tier Tribunal.

(3) An enforcement notice is not suspended pending an appeal unless the First-tier Tribunal orders otherwise.

(4) On appeal the First-tier Tribunal may either cancel or confirm the enforcement notice, with or without modification, or make such order as it thinks fit as regards refusal to serve a completion notice.
