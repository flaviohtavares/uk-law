# The Welfare of Animals at the Time of Killing (Wales) Regulations 2014, section 34

> Text as at 2024-01-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 34. Inspectors[^key-a068562b43cae404a9312210516404ff]

*Extent: E+W*

The competent authority or a local authority may appoint inspectors for the purpose of enforcing the EU Regulation and these Regulations.
