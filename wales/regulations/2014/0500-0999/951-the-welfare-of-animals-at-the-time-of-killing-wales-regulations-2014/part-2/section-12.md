> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Requirement for a licence[^c-15a67c02759d5edfe6d5a332cfaa4ded]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to regulations 14 and 23(2), no person may carry out an operation specified in regulation 13 except under and to the extent authorised by a licence registered with the competent authority.
