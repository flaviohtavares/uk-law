# The Welfare of Animals at the Time of Killing (Wales) Regulations 2014, section 18

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18. Refusal to grant a certificate, temporary certificate or licence[^key-78af8b21ed189906b7390a254c56ce65]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The competent authority may, by notice in writing, refuse to grant a certificate, temporary certificate or licence if satisfied that the applicant—

- (a) has failed to meet any of the conditions in regulations 8, 10 or 16 (as the case may be); or
- (b) is not a fit and proper person to hold a certificate, temporary certificate or licence.

(2) The notice must—

- (a) give reasons for the refusal; and
- (b) give details of the right of appeal against the decision.
