# The Welfare of Animals at the Time of Killing (Wales) Regulations 2014, section 23

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. WASK licences[^c-da47c9d8c8932faa8821dffd115238e3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) On the coming into force of these Regulations, a WASK licence which is still in force immediately before 20 May 2014 continues in existence as a qualification equivalent to a certificate in accordance with Article 21(7).

(2) A WASK licence holder may carry out an operation specified in regulation 6 or 13 without holding a certificate or licence registered with the competent authority provided that, by 8 December 2015, the WASK licence holder—

- (a) registers the WASK licence as a certificate with the competent authority; and
- (b) pays a fee in accordance with regulation 24.
