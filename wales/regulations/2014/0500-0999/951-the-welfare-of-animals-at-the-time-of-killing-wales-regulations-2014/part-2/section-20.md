# The Welfare of Animals at the Time of Killing (Wales) Regulations 2014, section 20

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. Modification of a certificate or licence[^key-2edcadcdfd0c3f591b26aefc10713ce2]

*Extent: E+W*

The competent authority must modify a certificate or licence in respect of an operation, category of animal or (where appropriate) type of equipment as requested by an applicant if—

- (a) the applicant meets the conditions in regulation 8 or 16 (as the case may be) in respect of the modification; and
- (b) the competent authority is satisfied the applicant is a fit and proper person to hold a certificate or licence, as modified.
