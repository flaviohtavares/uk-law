# The Welfare of Animals at the Time of Killing (Wales) Regulations 2014, section 1

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Title, application and commencement[^key-d070d08851411c551fd0ad6fae1f8b89]

*Extent: E+W*

These Regulations—

- (a) are entitled the Welfare of Animals at the Time of Killing (Wales) Regulations 2014;
- (b) apply in relation to Wales; and
- (c) come into force on 20 May 2014.
