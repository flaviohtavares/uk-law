## 2. Expiry[^key-a8e98d0e18c564865d848ec23531ef4a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The following provisions cease to have effect on 8 December 2015—

- (a) regulation 44; and
- (b) Schedule 7.

(2) The following provisions cease to have effect on 8 December 2019—

- (a) regulation 30(1)(h);
- (b) regulation 45; and
- (c) Schedule 8.
