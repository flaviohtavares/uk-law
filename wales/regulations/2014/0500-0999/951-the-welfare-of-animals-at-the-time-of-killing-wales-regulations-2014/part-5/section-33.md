# The Welfare of Animals at the Time of Killing (Wales) Regulations 2014, section 33

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Penalties[^c-e7ac2aa7ad8c1f1a22b8c9de6a184e94]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person guilty of an offence under regulation 30 or 31 is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, except where specified in paragraph (2).

(2) A person guilty of an offence under regulation 30(1)(g) in relation to a contravention of Article 3 is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months.
