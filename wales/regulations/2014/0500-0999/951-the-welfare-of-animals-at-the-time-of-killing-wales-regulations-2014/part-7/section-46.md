# The Welfare of Animals at the Time of Killing (Wales) Regulations 2014, section 46

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. Revocations[^c-c55b3cc253d3a8849bf86ed6b84bdd10]

*Extent: E+W*

The following instruments and enactments are revoked in relation to Wales—

- (a) the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) Regulations 1995[^f00014];
- (b) the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) (Amendment) Regulations 1999[^f00015];
- (c) regulation 10(3) of, and Part 3 of Schedule 8 to, the Food Standards Act 1999 (Transitional and Consequential Provisions and Savings) (England and Wales) Regulations 2000[^f00016]; and
- (d) the Welfare of Animals (Slaughter or Killing) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2007[^f00017].
