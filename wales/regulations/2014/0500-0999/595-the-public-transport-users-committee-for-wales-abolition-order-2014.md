# The Public Transport Users’ Committee for Wales (Abolition) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 595 (W. 71)

Transport, Wales

The Public Transport Users’ Committee for Wales (Abolition) Order 2014

Made

12 March 2014

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

13 March 2014

Coming into force

15 April 2014

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon the National Assembly for Wales by sections 8 and 9 of the Transport (Wales) Act 2006[^f00001], and now vested in them[^f00002] make the following Order:

## 1. Title, commencement and application

(1) The title of this Order is the Public Transport Users’ Committee for Wales (Abolition) Order 2014 and it comes into force on 15 April 2014.

(2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order—

- “the 2009 Order” (“*Gorchymyn 2009*”) means the Public Transport Users’ Committee for Wales (Establishment) Order 2009[^f00003]

## 3. Abolition of the Public Transport Users’ Committee for Wales

(1) The Public Transport Users’ Committee for Wales established under article 3 of the 2009 Order is abolished; and

(2) the 2009 Order is hereby revoked.

Edwina Hart

one of the Welsh Ministers

Minister for the Economy, Science and Transport,

12 March 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order abolishes the Public Transport Users’ Committee for Wales, established under article 3 of the Public Transport Users’ Committee for Wales (Establishment) Order 2009 by virtue of sections 8 and 9 of the Transport (Wales) Act 2006.

No regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in respect of this instrument as no cost to the business or voluntary sector is foreseen.
