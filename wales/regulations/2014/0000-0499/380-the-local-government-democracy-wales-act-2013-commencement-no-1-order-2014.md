# The Local Government (Democracy) (Wales) Act 2013 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 380 (W. 45) (C. 15)

Local Government, Wales

The Local Government (Democracy) (Wales) Act 2013 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2014

Made

20 February 2014

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 75(3) of the Local Government (Democracy) (Wales) Act 2013[^f00001].

## 1. Title and interpretation

The title of this Order is the Local Government (Democracy) (Wales) Act 2013 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2014.

## 2. Appointed Day

The appointed day for the coming into force of section 63 of the Local Government (Democracy) (Wales) Act 2013 is 1 April 2014.

Lesley Griffiths

Minister for Local Government and Government Business, one of the Welsh Ministers

20 February 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is made under section 75(3) of the Local Government (Democracy) (Wales) Act 2013 (“the Act”). It is the first commencement order to be made under the Act.

Article 2 of this Order provides that 1 April 2014 is the appointed day for the coming into force of section 63 of the Act.

Section 63 amends the Local Government (Wales) Measure 2011 (“the Measure”) by inserting a new section 143A. Section 143A provides the Independent Remuneration Panel for Wales (“the Panel”) with powers in relation to the salaries of the heads of paid service in county, county borough councils and fire and rescue authorities (defined in section 143A as “qualifying relevant authorities”). Under section 143A, the Panel is able to make recommendations to a qualifying relevant authority about any policy in the authority’s pay policy statement which relates to the salary of the authority’s head of paid service, and any change in salary of the authority’s head of paid service. Where a qualifying relevant authority is proposing a change of salary of its head of paid service that is not commensurate with a change to the salaries of its other staff, that authority must consult the Panel about the proposals before making the change. The Panel is required to have regard to any guidance issued by the Welsh Ministers when exercising its functions under section 143A.
