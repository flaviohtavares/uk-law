# The School Standards and Organisation (Wales) Act 2013 (Commencement No. 4 and Savings Provisions) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 178 (W. 26) (C. 5)

Education, Wales

The School Standards and Organisation (Wales) Act 2013 (Commencement No. 4 and Savings Provisions) Order 2014

Made

29 January 2014

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 97 and 100 of the School Standards and Organisation (Wales) Act 2013[^f00001] make the following Order:

## 1. Title and interpretation

(1) The title of this Order is the School Standards and Organisation (Wales) Act 2013 (Commencement No.4 and Savings Provisions) Order 2014.

(2) In this Order, “the 2013 Act” (“*Deddf 2013*”) means the School Standards and Organisation (Wales) Act 2013.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 20 February 2014

Subject to article 3, the appointed day for the following provisions of the 2013 Act coming into force is 20 February 2014—

- (a) Chapter 1 of Part 2 (intervention in conduct of maintained schools);
- (b) Chapter 2 of Part 2 (intervention in local authorities);
- (c) section 96 (repeal of provision for code of practice for local authority school relations);
- (d) section 99 (minor and consequential amendments) in so far as it relates to the amendments made by Part 1 of Schedule 5;
- (e) Schedule 1 (governing bodies consisting of interim executive members);
- (f) Part 1 of Schedule 5 (minor and consequential amendments relating to Part 2 of the 2013 Act); and
- (g) paragraphs 32 and 34(2) of Part 3 of Schedule 5 (repeal of section 58 of the Education and Inspections Act 2006).

## 3. Savings in relation to sections 496 to 497A of the Education Act 1996

(1) The coming into force of any of the provisions set out in article 2 does not affect any act done, any declaration or any direction under, or in relation to the exercise of the Welsh Ministers’ powers in sections 496 to 497A of the Education Act 1996[^f00002] and those sections continue to apply as if the provisions set out in article 2 had not been made.

(2) Where the Welsh Ministers have issued a direction in exercise of their powers in sections 496 to 497A of the Education Act 1996 (“the direction powers”) before any of the provisions set out in article 2 come into force and they are satisfied that a further direction should be issued in relation to the same matters in exercise of the direction powers those sections continue to apply as if the provisions set out in article 2 had not been made.

Huw Lewis

Minister for Education and Skills, one of the Welsh Ministers

29 January 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This is the fourth commencement order made by the Welsh Ministers under the School Standards and Organisation (Wales) Act 2013 (“the 2013 Act”). This Order brings into force on 20 February 2014 the following provisions of the 2013 Act:

- (a) Chapter 1 of Part 2 (intervention in conduct of maintained schools);
- (b) Chapter 2 of Part 2 (intervention in local authorities);
- (c) section 96 (repeal of provision for code of practice for local authority school relations);
- (d) section 99 (minor and consequential amendments) in so far as it relates to the amendments made by Part 1 of Schedule 5;
- (e) Schedule 1 (governing bodies consisting of interim executive members);
- (f) Part 1 of Schedule 5 (minor and consequential amendments relating to Part 2 of the 2013 Act); and
- (g) paragraphs 32 and 34(2) of Part 3 of Schedule 5 (repeal of section 58 of the Education and Inspections Act 2006).

Article 3 of this Order provides that any act, declaration or direction that the Welsh Ministers have made under or relating to their powers in sections 496 to 497A of the Education Act 1996 (“the 1996 Act”) continue to apply. It also provides that a further direction, in relation to the same matters, may also be made under sections 496 to 497A of the 1996 Act.
