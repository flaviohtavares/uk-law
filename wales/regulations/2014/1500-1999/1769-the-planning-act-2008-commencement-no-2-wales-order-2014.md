# The Planning Act 2008 (Commencement No. 2) (Wales) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2014 No. 1769 (W. 181) (C. 76)

Town And Country Planning, Wales

The Planning Act 2008 (Commencement No. 2) (Wales) Order 2014

Made

2 July 2014

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the power conferred by section 241(3) of the Planning Act 2008[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Title

The title of this Order is the Planning Act 2008 (Commencement No. 2) (Wales) Order 2014.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 8 August 2014 in relation to Wales

The following provisions of the Planning Act 2008 (so far as they are not yet in force) come into force in relation to Wales on 8 August 2014—

- (a) section 185; and
- (b) section 199.

Carl Sargeant

Minister for Housing and Regeneration, one of the Welsh Ministers

2 July 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force, on 8 August 2014, the following provisions of the Planning Act 2008, in relation to Wales, so far as they are not already in force—

- section 185 (power of High Court to remit strategies, plans and documents); and
- section 199 (fees for planning applications).
