# The Active Travel (Wales) Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed in substitution of the SI of the same number and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2014 No. 2589 (W. 256) (C. 112)

Highways, Wales

The Active Travel (Wales) Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2014

Made

24 September 2014

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the power contained in section 14(1) of the Active Travel (Wales) Act 2013[^f00001].

## 1. Title and interpretation

(1) The title of this Order is the Active Travel (Wales) Act 2013 (Commencement) Order 2014.

(2) In this Order, *“*the Act” (“*y Ddeddf*”) means the Active Travel (Wales) Act 2013.

## 2. Commencement

Sections 3 to 11 of the Act are to come into force on the day after the day on which this Order is made.

Edwina Hart

Minister for Economy, Science and Transport, one of the Welsh Ministers

24 September 2014

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force all the provisions of the Active Travel (Wales) Act 2013, save for those that commenced on Royal Assent.
