# The Street Works (Fixed Penalty) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 466 (W.41)

HIGHWAYS, WALES

The Street Works (Fixed Penalty) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

Made

22 February 2008

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

25 February 2008

Coming into force

12th May 2008

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 48(2), 95A(5), 97 and 104(1) of, and paragraphs 2, 4(1), 5(2), 8(a) and 9(b) of Schedule 4B to, the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991[^f00001], and now vested in them[^f00002], make the following regulations.

## 1. Title, commencement and application

The title of these Regulations is the Street Works (Fixed Penalty) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008. They come into force on 12 May 2008 and apply in relation to Wales.

## 2. Amendments to the Street Works (Fixed Penalty) (Wales) Regulations 2008

(1) The Street Works (Fixed Penalty) (Wales) Regulations 2008[^f00003] are amended in accordance with paragraph (2).

(2) In the Welsh text, in Regulation 5(3), there is substituted the word “brintiedig” for the word “brintiadwy”.

Ieuan Wyn Jones

Minister for the Economy and Transport, one of the Welsh Ministers

22 February 2008

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend The Street Works (Fixed Penalty) (Wales) Regulations 2008.

Specifically these Regulations amend Regulation 5(3) in respect of the Welsh text only, replacing the word “brintiadwy” (meaning “printable”) with the word “brintiedig” (meaning “printed”).
