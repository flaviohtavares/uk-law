# The Fire and Rescue Authorities (Improvement Plans) (Wales) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 199 (W.25)

FIRE AND RESCUE SERVICES, WALES

The Fire and Rescue Authorities (Improvement Plans) (Wales) Order 2008

Made

30 January 2008

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

1 February 2008

Coming into force

29 February 2008

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on the National Assembly for Wales by sections 6(3), 7(6) and 29(1) of the Local Government Act 1999[^f00001], and now vested in them[^f00002] hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title, coming into force, interpretation and application

(1) The title of this Order is The Fire and Rescue Authorities (Improvement Plans) (Wales) Order 2008 and it comes into force on 29 February 2008.

(2) In this Order—

- “the Act” (“*y Ddeddf*”) means the Local Government Act 1999; and
- “improvement plan” (“*cynllun gwella*”) means a best value performance plan within the meaning of section 6(1) of the Act.

(3) This Order applies only to best value authorities in Wales which are a fire and rescue authority constituted by a scheme to which section 4 of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004[^f00003] applies and has effect in relation to the year 2008-2009 and subsequent financial years.

## 2. Date for publication of improvement plan

Best value authorities must publish their improvement plan for a financial year by 31 October of the year to which the plan relates.

## 3. Date for sending copies of auditors' reports

Copies of an auditor’s report relating to an improvement plan must be sent in accordance with section 7(5) of the Act by 30 November of the year to which the plan relates.

Brian Gibbons

Minister for Social Justice and Local Government, one of the Welsh Ministers

30 January 2008

(This note is not part of the Order)

Part I of the Local Government Act 1999 imposes duties on local and other authorities to prepare a best value performance plan (in this Order called an “improvement plan”) for each financial year.

This Order specifies the date by which a fire and rescue authority’s improvement plan for a financial year must be published and the date by which copies of an auditor’s report relating to the plan are to be sent to the authority, the Auditor General for Wales, and, if required, the Welsh Ministers.

A regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in connection with this Order. A copy can be obtained from Fire and Rescue Services Branch, Community Safety Division, Welsh Assembly Government, Rhydycar, Merthyr Tydfil CF481UZ (telephone 01685 729221).
