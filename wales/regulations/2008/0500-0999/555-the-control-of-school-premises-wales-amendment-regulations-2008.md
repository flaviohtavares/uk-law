# The Control of School Premises (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 555 (W.55)

EDUCATION, WALES

The Control of School Premises (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

Made

29 February 2008

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

3 March 2008

Coming into force

31 March 2008

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on the National Assembly for Wales by sections 31 and 210(7) of the Education Act 2002 and now vested in them[^f00001] make the following Regulations.

**1.** (1) The title of these Regulations is The Control of School Premises (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 and they come into force on 31 March 2008.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

**2.** In the Welsh language text of regulation 10(a) of the Control of School Premises (Wales) Regulations 2008[^f00002] delete the words “neu benderfyniad a wneir yn unol â pharagraff 62 neu 63 o Atodlen 8 i Ddeddf Addysg Bellach ac Uwch 1992”.

Jane Hutt

Minister for Children, Education, Lifelong Learning and Skills, one of the Welsh Ministers

29 February 2008

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Control of School Premises (Wales) Regulations 2008 (“the 2008 Regulations”) make provision about the control by governing bodies of the occupation and use of school premises. These Regulations amend the 2008 Regulations so as to correct a mistake in them. Regulation 10(a) in the Welsh language text of the 2008 Regulations contains additional words not included in the English language text of regulation 10(a). Accordingly, regulation 2 of these Regulations deletes those additional words.
