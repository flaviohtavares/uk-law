# The National Health Service (Optical Charges and Payments) and (General Ophthalmic Services) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 577 (W.56)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, WALES

The National Health Service (Optical Charges and Payments) and (General Ophthalmic Services) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2008

Made

3 March 2008

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

4 March 2008

Coming into force

28 March 2008

Welsh Ministers make the following regulations in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 71, 76, 77, 128, 129, 203(9) and (10) of the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^f00001].

## 1. Title, commencement and application

The title of these Regulations is the National Health Service (Optical Charges and Payments) and (General Ophthalmic Services) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2008 which come into force on 28 March 2008.

## 2. Amendment of regulation 1 of the National Health Service (Optical Charges and Payments) Regulations 1997

In the definition of “NHS sight test fee” in regulation 1(2) of the National Health Service (Optical Charges and Payments) Regulations 1997[^f00002] —

- (a) for “£52.04” substitute “£53.34” and
- (b) for “£18.85” substitute “£19.32”.

## 3. Amendment of regulation 10 of the National Health Service (General Ophthalmic Services) Regulations 1986

In regulation 10(1) (the statement) of the National Health Service (General Ophthalmic Services) Regulations 1986[^f00003] at the end of regulation 10(1)(b) for the words from “A determination” to “relates.” substitute —

> - A determination may provide that such fees and allowances may be paid in respect of a period beginning on a date earlier than the date of the determination if, taking the determination as a whole, it is not detrimental to the persons to whose remuneration it relates.

.

Edwina Hart

Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers

3 March 2008

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the National Health Service (Optical Charges and Payments) Regulations 1997 so as to make reference to an increase in the NHS sight test fees of 2.5%. The Regulations also amend regulation 10 of the National Health Service (General Ophthalmic Services) Regulations 1986 to provide that where Welsh Ministers make provision in a determination as to the fees to be paid by a Local Health Board for the testing of sight by ophthalmic medical practitioners and opticians, the determination may provide that the fees may be paid in respect of a period beginning on a date earlier than the date of the determination, if taking the determination as a whole, it is not detrimental to the persons to whose remuneration it relates.
