# The Ceredigion and Mid Wales National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 934 (W.94)

THE NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, WALES

The Ceredigion and Mid Wales National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2008

Made

28 March 2008

Coming into force

1 April 2008

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of powers conferred by section 204(1) and paragraph 28(1) of Schedule 3 to the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^f00001] and after completion of consultation prescribed under paragraph 28(3) of Schedule 3 to that Act[^f00002] hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title and Commencement

The Title of this Order is the Ceredigion and Mid Wales National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2008 and it comes into force on 1 April 2008.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS Trust

The Ceredigion and Mid Wales National Health Service Trust established by the Ceredigion and Mid Wales National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1992[^f00003] is hereby dissolved, and accordingly that Order is hereby revoked.

Edwina Hart

Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers

28 March 2008

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution of Ceredigion and Mid Wales National Health Service Trust on 1 April 2008.
