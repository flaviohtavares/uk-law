# The Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (Wales) Order 2008, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Classes of Fireplaces exempted from Section 20 of the Clean Air Act 1993

The classes of fireplace described in the Schedule are, upon the conditions therein specified, exempted from the provisions of Section 20 of the Clean Air Act 1993 (which prohibits smoke emissions in smoke control areas).
