# The Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (Wales) Order 2008, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Title, commencement, and application

(1) The title of this Order is the Smoke Control Areas (Exempted Fireplaces) (Wales) Order 2008 and it comes into force on 31 December 2008.

(2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.
