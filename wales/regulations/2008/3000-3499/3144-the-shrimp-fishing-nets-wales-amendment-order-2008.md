# The Shrimp Fishing Nets (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 3144 (W.279)

SEA FISHERIES, WALES

CONSERVATION OF SEA FISH

The Shrimp Fishing Nets (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2008

Made

8 December 2008

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

9 December 2008

Coming into force

2 January 2009

The Welsh Ministers are designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002], as read with paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to that Act[^f00003], in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community.

This Order amends the 2008 Order that makes provision for a purpose mentioned in section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 and it appears to the Welsh Ministers that it is expedient for the references to European Community instruments in the 2008 Order to be construed as references to those instruments as amended from time to time.

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(1) of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967[^f00004], and in exercise of the powers conferred by section 2(2) of, and paragraph 1A of Schedule 2 to, the European Communities Act 1972, make the following Order:

## 1. Title, application and commencement

The title of this Order is the Shrimp Fishing Nets (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2008. It applies in relation to Wales and comes into force on 2 January 2009.

## 2. Amendments

(1) The Shrimp Fishing Nets (Wales) Order 2008[^f00005] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 3, paragraph 1(d), of the English text for “or” substitute “of”, and in article 3, paragraph 1(ch) of the Welsh text delete the word “ac”.

Elin Jones

Minister for Rural Affairs, one of the Welsh Ministers

8 December 2008

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends article 3(1)(d) of the Shrimp Fishing Nets (Wales) Order 2008 (“the 2008 Order”).
