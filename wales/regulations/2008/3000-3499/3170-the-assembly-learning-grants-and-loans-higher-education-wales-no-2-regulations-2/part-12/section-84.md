# The Assembly Learning Grants and Loans (Higher Education) (Wales) (No.2) Regulations 2008, section 84

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 84. Part-time grants for dependants — general

(1) An eligible part-time student qualifies for part-time grants for dependants provided that—

- (a) the part-time student is not excluded from qualification by any of the following paragraphs, regulation 79 or regulation 81; and
- (b) the part-time student satisfies the qualifying conditions for the particular grant for which he or she is applying.

(2) An eligible part-time student does not qualify for part-time grants for dependants if the student is a prisoner.

**85.** (1) The part-time grants for dependants consist of the following elements—

- (a) part-time adult dependants' grant;
- (b) part-time childcare grant;
- (c) part-time parents' learning allowance.

(2) The qualifying conditions for each element are set out in regulations 86 to 93 and the amounts payable in respect of each element are determined in accordance with those regulations..

(3) A deduction may be made from any element of the part-time grants for dependants in accordance with regulations 91 and 92.
