# The North Wales National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Property, Rights and Liabilities) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 2443 (W.215)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, WALES

The North Wales National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Property, Rights and Liabilities) Order 2008

Made

15 September 2008

Coming into force

16 September 2008

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of powers conferred by section 203(9) and (10) and paragraph 9(1) of Schedule 3 to the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^f00001], hereby make this Order.

## 1. Title, Commencement and Interpretation

(1) The title of this Order is the North Wales National Health Service Trust (Transfer of Property, Rights and Liabilities) Order 2008 and it comes into force on 16 September 2008.

(2) In this Order, unless the context otherwise requires;

- “the transfer date” (“*y dyddiad trosglwyddo*”) means the 16 September 2008;

## 2. Transfer of property, rights and liabilities

On the transfer date the property known as Abergele Hospital (Land Registry Number CYM 324611), and all the rights and liabilities attaching to that property, shall be transferred from the Welsh Ministers to the North Wales National Health Service Trust.[^f00002]

Edwina Hart

Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers

15 September 2008

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the transfer of property known as Abergele Hospital from the Welsh Ministers to the North Wales National Health Service Trust from the 16 September 2008.
