# The Fire and Rescue Services (National Framework) (Wales) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 2298 (W.197)

FIRE AND RESCUE SERVICES, WALES

The Fire and Rescue Services (National Framework) (Wales) Order 2008

Made

28 August 2008

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

1 September 2008

Coming into force

22 September 2008

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred on the National Assembly for Wales by sections 21(6), 60 and 62 of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002] make the following Order:

## 1. Title, commencement and application

(1) The title of this Order is the Fire and Rescue Services (National Framework) (Wales) Order 2008 and it comes into force on 22 September 2008.

(2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

## 2. The National Framework

The Fire and Rescue National Framework prepared by the Welsh Ministers and entitled “The Fire and Rescue National Framework for Wales 2008—2011” published by the Welsh Ministers on 28 August 2008 on the Welsh Assembly Government’s website[^f00003] has effect as a revision, which appears to the Welsh Minsters to be significant, of the “The Fire and Rescue National Framework (Wales) 2005”[^f00004].

Brian Gibbons

Minister for Social Justice and Local Government, one of the Welsh Ministers

28 August 2008

(This note is not part of the Order)

Section 21 of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004 (“the Act”) requires the Welsh Ministers to prepare a Fire and Rescue National Framework (“Framework”), which must set out priorities and objectives for Fire and Rescue Authorities and may provide guidance. Fire and Rescue Authorities must have regard to the Framework in carrying out their functions.

Article 2 of this Order, which has effect in relation to Fire and Rescue Authorities in Wales, substitutes the Fire and Rescue National Framework for Wales 2008 — 2011 for the Fire and Rescue National Framework (Wales) 2005.

The Fire and Rescue National Framework for Wales 2008 — 2011 will have effect from 22 September 2008. The Fire and Rescue National Framework (Wales) 2005 was given effect by [SI 2005/760 (W.64)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2005/760). Copies of the Fire and Rescue National Framework for Wales 2008 — 2011 may be obtained from Fire and Rescue Services Branch, Welsh Assembly Government, Rhyd y car, Merthyr Tydfil, CF48 1UZ. It was published on the Welsh Assembly Government website (http://new.wales.gov.uk/topics/housingandcommunity/safety/fire/?lang=en) on 28 August 2008.

A regulatory impact assessment has been prepared in connection with this Order. A copy can be obtained from the Fire and Rescue Services Branch, Welsh Assembly Government, Rhyd y car, Merthyr Tydfil, CF48 1UZ (tel: 01685 729253).
