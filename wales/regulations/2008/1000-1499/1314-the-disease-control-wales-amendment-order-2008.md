# The Disease Control (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1314 (W.136)

ANIMALS, WALES

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Disease Control (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2008

Made

14 May 2008

Coming into force

6 June 2008

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1, 7, and 8(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001], make the following Order.

## 1. Title and commencement

The title of this Order is the Disease Control (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2008. It applies in relation to Wales and comes into force on 6 June 2008.

## 2. Amendment of the Disease Control (Wales) Order 2003

(1) The Disease Control (Wales) Order 2003[^f00002] is amended as follows.

(2) In article 2, omit the definition of “veterinary surgeon”.

(3) For paragraph (3) of article 14, substitute—

> (3) Every animal which is moved under a specific licence must be kept separate throughout such movement from any animal which is not being moved under that licence.

.

Elin Jones

Minister for Rural Affairs, one of the Welsh Ministers

14 May 2008

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the Disease Control (Wales) Order 2003 (S.I. [2003/1966 (W.211)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2003/1966)). It removes an unnecessary definition and clarifies the type of licence referred to in article 14(3) of that Order.

A regulatory impact assessment has not been produced for this instrument as no impact on the private or voluntary sectors is foreseen.
