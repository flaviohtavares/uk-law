# The Assembly Learning Grants and Loans (Higher Education) (Wales) Regulations 2008, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Interpretation

For the purposes of this Part—

- (a) any reference to expenditure incurred for the purpose of attending an institution or period of study—
  - (i) includes expenditure both before and after so attending; and
  - (ii) does not include any expenditure in respect of which a grant is payable under regulation 24.
- (b) “qualifying quarter” (“*chwarter cymhwysol*”) means a quarter during which the eligible student attends as part of his or her course an overseas institution or the Institute for at least half the period covered by that quarter.
