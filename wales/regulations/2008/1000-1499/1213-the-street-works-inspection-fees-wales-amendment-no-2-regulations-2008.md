# The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2008 No. 1213 (W.121)

HIGHWAYS, WALES

The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008

Made

29 April 2008

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

30 April 2008

Coming into force

22 May 2008

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by sections 75 and 104(1) of the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991[^f00001], and now vested in them[^f00002], make the following regulations:

## 1. Title, commencement and application

The title of these Regulations is the Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) (Amendment) (No. 2) Regulations 2008. They come into force on 22 May 2008 and apply in relation to Wales.

## 2. Amendment to the Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) Regulations 2006

The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) Regulations 2006, as amended[^f00003] are further amended in accordance with regulation 3.

**3.** In regulation 3, in paragraphs (1) and (8), for “£24.00” substitute “£25.00”.

## 4. Revocation of the Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008

The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 [^f00004] are hereby revoked.

Ieuan Wyn Jones

Minister for the Economy and Transport, one of the Welsh Ministers

29 April 2008

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations revoke and replace the Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2008 (“the 2008 Regulations”). The 2008 Regulations further amend the Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) Regulations 2006 (“the 2006 Regulations”) so as to increase the fee payable by undertakers for inspections of their work by street authorities in Wales, under regulation 3 of the 2006 Regulations.

The fee originally prescribed in the 2006 Regulations was £21. The fee was altered to £24 in July 2007 by the [Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2007 (S.I. 2007 No.1713 (W.150))](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2007/1713), and is now increased to £25.
