# The Integrated Family Support Teams (Family Support Functions) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

> Text as at 2011-01-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

SOCIAL CARE, WALES

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, WALES

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS, WALES

The Integrated Family Support Teams (Family Support Functions) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

Made

29 January 2011

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

1 February 2011

Coming into force

1 March 2011

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 58(2) of the Children and Families (Wales) Measure 2010[^f00001], make the following regulations.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application[^key-6acbc23d296e9252655eb2bde2288c4a]

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Integrated Family Support Teams (Family Support Functions) (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 and they come into force on 1 March 2011.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

**2.**[^key-3250418952ba88f64975b180e3c117b1] In Table 1 of Schedule 1 to the Integrated Family Support Teams (Family Support Functions) (Wales) Regulations 2010[^f00002] delete the words –

> - s.82 National Health Service Act 2006 (*Cooperation between NHS bodies and local authorities*)

.

Gwenda Thomas

Deputy Minister for Social Services under authority of the Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers

29 January 2011

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Integrated Family Support Teams (Family Support Functions) (Wales) Regulations 2010 by removing reference to section 82 of the National Health Service Act 2006 in the list of local authority functions which apply in relation to children (Table 1 of Schedule 1) and are prescribed as “family support functions” for the purposes of section 58 of the Children and Families (Wales) Measure 2010.
