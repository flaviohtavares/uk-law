# The Public Health Wales National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) (Wales) Order 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 3047 (W.322)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, WALES

The Public Health Wales National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) (Wales) Order 2011

Made

19 December 2011

Coming into force

9 January 2012

The Welsh Ministers in exercise of powers conferred by section 21 of and paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^f00001] and with the consent of the Treasury in accordance with paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 4 to that Act, hereby make the following Order.

## 1. Title and commencement

The title of this Order is the Public Health Wales National Health Service Trust (Originating Capital) (Wales) Order 2011 and it comes into force on 9 January 2012.

## 2. Originating Capital of NHS Trusts

The originating capital of Public Health Wales National Health Service Trust[^f00002] is £7,489,000.

Lesley Griffiths

The Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers

19 December 2011

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order specifies the amount of originating capital for Public Health Wales National Health Service Trust.
