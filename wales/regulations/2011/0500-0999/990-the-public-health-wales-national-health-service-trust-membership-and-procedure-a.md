# The Public Health Wales National Health Service Trust (Membership and Procedure) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2011 No. 990 (W.144)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, WALES

The Public Health Wales National Health Service Trust (Membership and Procedure) (Amendment) Regulations 2011

Made

29 March 2011

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

30 March 2011

Coming into force

23 June 2011

The Welsh Ministers make the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 18(4), 203(9) and (10) of and paragraph 4 of Schedule 3 to the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^f00001].

## 1. Title and commencement

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Public Health Wales National Health Service Trust (Membership and Procedure) (Amendment) Regulations 2011.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 23 June 2011.

## 2. Amendment of the Public Health Wales National Health Service Trust (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 2009

(1) The Public Health Wales National Health Service Trust (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 2009[^f00002] are amended as follows.

(2) In regulation 1(2) (interpretation), omit the definition of “trade union”.

(3) In regulation 5(1) (non-executive directors), omit sub-paragraph (d).

(4) In regulation 10 (termination of tenure of office of chair and non-executive directors), for regulation 10(8), substitute—

> Where the non-executive director referred to in sub-paragraph (c) of regulation 5(1) ceases to be a member or employee of a voluntary sector organisation in Wales, the Welsh Ministers will terminate his or her appointment as non-executive director.

.

(5) In paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 (procedures for appointment of the chair and non-executive directors), for “members” substitute “directors”.

(6) In paragraph 2(2) of Schedule 2 (rules as to meetings and proceedings of the Trust), for “members” substitute “directors”.

Edwina Hart

The Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers

29 March 2011

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the Public Health Wales National Health Service Trust (Membership and Procedure) Regulations 2009 (S.I. [2009/1385 (W.141)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2009/1385) by removing the requirement for the non-executive directors to include a person who is an officer of a trade union or other employee representative body representing staff of the Public Health Wales National Health Service Trust.

There are also minor amendments to regulation 1(2) (interpretation), paragraph 2 of Schedule 1,(procedures for appointment of the chair and non-executive directors), and paragraph 2(2) of Schedule 2 (rules as to meetings and proceedings of the Trust).
