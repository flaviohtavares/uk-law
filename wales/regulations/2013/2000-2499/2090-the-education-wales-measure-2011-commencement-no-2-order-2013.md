# The Education (Wales) Measure 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been printed in substitution of the SI of the same number and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2013 No. 2090 (W. 205) (C. 87)

Education, Wales

The Education (Wales) Measure 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2013

Made

22 August 2013

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 32 of the Education (Wales) Measure 2011[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Title and application

(1) The title of this Order is the Education (Wales) Measure 2011 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2013.

(2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

## 2. Commencement

Sections 22 to 25 of the Education (Wales) Measure 2011 come into force on 22 August 2013.

Huw Lewis

Minister for Education and Skills, one of the Welsh Ministers

22 August 2013

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This is the second commencement order made by the Welsh Ministers under the Education (Wales) Measure 2011 (“the 2011 Measure”). The Order brings Chapter 2 of Part 2 of the 2011 Measure into force on 19 September 2013. Chapter 2 of Part 2 of the 2011 Measure makes provision about training for governors and clerks and the provision of a clerk to a school governing body.
