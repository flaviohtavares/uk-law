# The Council Tax Reduction Schemes and Prescribed Requirements (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 40

> Text as at 2026-01-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Notification relating to change of circumstances[^key-69ed27fdd004fa9d776a4e89ae7ce73e]

*Extent: E+W*

Where by virtue of regulation 37(1) or 38 a person is treated as having made an application under a scheme, the following provisions in a relevant authority’s scheme do not apply in relation to that person’s application—

- (a) a provision contained in a scheme by virtue of regulation 33(3) of, and paragraph 1(7) of Schedule 13 to these Regulations (who may make an application); or
- (b) paragraph 107(7) of the scheme prescribed in the Schedule to the Council Tax Reduction Schemes (Default Scheme) (Wales) Regulations 2013 (who may make an application).
