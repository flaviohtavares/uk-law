# The Council Tax Reduction Schemes and Prescribed Requirements (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 38

> Text as at 2026-01-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 38. Applications received between 15 February 2014 and 31 March 2014[^key-25dfb38600256805ed9b5f778a97c898]

*Extent: E+W*

A person who makes a claim for a reduction under a 2013 scheme on any day during the period beginning on 15 February 2014 and ending on 31 March 2014 is to be treated as having made an application for a reduction under a scheme on the same day.
