# The Council Tax Reduction Schemes and Prescribed Requirements (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 21

> Text as at 2026-01-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21. Classes of person who must be included in a scheme[^c-8775504e04417c326fc79ab8fcf9ba49]

*Extent: E+W*

The classes of persons described in regulations 22 to 25 are classes of person prescribed for the purposes of paragraph 3(1)(a) of Schedule 1B to the 1992 Act[^f00094] and who must be included in and entitled to a reduction under an authority’s scheme.
