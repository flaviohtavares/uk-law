# The Council Tax Reduction Schemes and Prescribed Requirements (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 1

> Text as at 2026-01-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Title, commencement and application[^c-b0540f2eba92c968be2e224636ddadc6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Council Tax Reduction Schemes and Prescribed Requirements (Wales) Regulations 2013.

(2) These Regulations come into force on the day after the day on which they are made and apply in relation to Wales.

(3) These Regulations apply in relation to council tax reduction schemes made by authorities for financial years beginning on or after 1 April 2014.
