# The Council Tax Reduction Schemes and Prescribed Requirements (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 27

> Text as at 2026-01-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. Classes of person who must not be included in a scheme[^key-066e22c80f372532f844470298eb3f70]

*Extent: E+W*

The classes of persons described in regulations 28 to 31 are classes of person prescribed for the purposes of paragraph 3(1)(b) of Schedule 1B of the 1992 Act[^f00102] and who must not be included in nor entitled to a reduction under an authority’s scheme.
