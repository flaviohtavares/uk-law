> Text as at 2026-01-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29. Persons subject to immigration control[^c-dfc8495fe02c737f21b7111129b1d2cc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The[^c-202a22f9b2ff81ad3ba37fc8af82f0ea] class of person described in this regulation consists of any person who is subject to immigration control.

(2)[^c-50ac30975f7d93af73355203fbc27496] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) “Person subject to immigration control” (“*person sy’n ddarostyngedig i reolaeth fewnfudo*”) has the meaning given in section 115(9) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999.
