# The Council Tax Reduction Schemes and Prescribed Requirements (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 31

> Text as at 2026-01-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31. Students[^c-bd8aefde122bbf51a0cfe7e40553692d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The class of person described in this regulation consists of any person who is a student.

(2) In this regulation “student” (“*myfyriwr*”) has the meaning given by paragraph 1 of Part 1 of Schedule 11.

(3) Schedule 11, which contains matters that must be included in a scheme in respect of students has effect.
