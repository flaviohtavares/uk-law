# The Council Tax Reduction Schemes and Prescribed Requirements (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 34

> Text as at 2026-01-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 34. Provision for pensioners and persons who are not pensioners[^c-e30276c2d7902158f1da4da8535fcea3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A scheme must include the provisions set out within Schedules 12 to 14.

(2) The provisions mentioned in paragraph (1) must apply to all applications for a council tax reduction unless otherwise provided.

(3) Schedules 12 to 14, which contain matters that must be included in a scheme in respect of all applicants for a council tax reduction, unless otherwise provided, have effect.

(4) For the purposes of this regulation, the provisions in paragraphs 3 and 4 of Schedule 13 (backdating) are minimum requirements.

(5) For the purposes of this regulation, the amount to be disregarded in accordance with paragraph 1(a) and (b) of Schedule 4 (amounts to be disregarded for war disablement pension, war widow’s pension and war widower’s pension: pensioners) and paragraph 20(a) and (b) of Schedule 9 (amounts to be disregarded for war disablement pension, war widow’s pension and war widower’s pension; persons who are not pensioners) is a minimum amount.
