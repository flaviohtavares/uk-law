# The Council Tax Reduction Schemes and Prescribed Requirements (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 17

> Text as at 2026-01-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Preparation of a scheme[^c-0d1fa77ba007ae2df3586d9ea3e3f20c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Before making a scheme an authority must—

- (a) publish a draft scheme in such manner as it thinks fit, and
- (b) consult any persons it considers are likely to have an interest in the operation of its scheme.

(2) Having made a scheme, the authority must publish that scheme in such manner as it thinks fit.
