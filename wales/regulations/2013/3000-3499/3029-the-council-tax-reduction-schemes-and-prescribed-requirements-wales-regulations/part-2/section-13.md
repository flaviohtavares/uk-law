# The Council Tax Reduction Schemes and Prescribed Requirements (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 13

> Text as at 2026-01-26.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. First financial year of schemes[^c-986f7217f667f8820f3dc6520a10d014]

*Extent: E+W*

Each authority in Wales must make a scheme no later than the 31 January 2014, and the first financial year to which that scheme relates must be the year beginning 1 April 2014.
