# The Council Tax Reduction Schemes and Prescribed Requirements (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 16

> Text as at 2026-01-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 16. Scheme procedural requirements[^c-179177232857ba14989418311f90a937]

*Extent: E+W*

A scheme must state—

- (a) the procedure by which a person may apply for a reduction under a scheme;
- (b) the procedure by which a person may appeal against a decision of an authority with respect to—
  - (i) a person’s entitlement to a reduction under a scheme; or
  - (ii) the amount of any reduction to which the person is entitled.
- (c) the procedure by which a person may apply to an authority for a reduction under section 13A(1)(c) of the 1992 Act.
