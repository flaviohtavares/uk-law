# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 76

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 76. Eligible distance learning students (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-232914b0f0c654918ad96a853a375997][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible distance learning student qualifies for support in connection with the eligible distance learning student undertaking a designated distance learning course subject to and in accordance with this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (8), a person is an eligible distance learning student in connection with a designated distance learning course if in assessing the person’s application for support under regulation 84 the Welsh Ministers determine that the person falls within one of the categories set out in Part 2 of Schedule 1. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) Subject to paragraph (8), a person (“A” in this paragraph) is not an eligible distance learning student if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) subject to paragraph (4), there has been bestowed on A or paid to A in connection with the distance learning course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) a healthcare bursary whether or not the amount of such bursary is calculated by reference to A’s income; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] any allowance under the Nursing and Midwifery Student Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 2007 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00069]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] a Scottish healthcare allowance whether or not the amount of such allowance is calculated by reference to A’s income; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) A is in breach of any obligation to repay any loan; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) A has reached the age of 18 and has not ratified any agreement for a loan made with A when A was under the age of 18; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) A has, in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers, shown by A’s conduct that A is unfitted to receive support under this Part; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (e) subject to paragraph (5), A is a prisoner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) Paragraph (3)(a) does not apply if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the person applying for support under this Part is a disabled student; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) there has been bestowed on or paid to the person in connection with the distance learning course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) a healthcare bursary the amount of which is calculated by reference to the person’s income; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] a Scottish Healthcare Allowance whether or not the amount of such allowance is calculated by reference to the person’s income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) Paragraph (3)(e) does not apply in respect of an academic year during which the eligible distance learning student enters prison to serve a custodial sentence or is released from prison having served such a sentence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) For the purposes of paragraphs (3)(b) and (3)(c), “loan” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*benthyciad *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means a loan made under the student loans legislation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(7) In a case where the agreement for a loan is subject to the law of Scotland, paragraph (3)(c) only applies if the agreement was made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) before 25 September 1991; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) with the concurrence of the borrower’s curator or at a time when the borrower had no curator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(8) Subject to paragraphs (10) to (12), a person is an eligible distance learning student for the purposes of this Part if the person satisfies the conditions in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] paragraph (9)(a), (b) or (c)[^c-7fd9d013830559884a713c8486b4c590] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a].

(9) The conditions referred to in paragraph (8) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) person qualified as an eligible distance learning student in connection with an earlier academic year of the present distance learning course pursuant to regulations made by the Welsh Ministers under section 22 of the 1998 Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] person was ordinarily resident in Wales on the first day of the present distance learning course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] person’s status as an eligible distance learning student has not terminated; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) Welsh Ministers have previously determined that the person is an eligible— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] student in connection with a designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] distance learning student in connection with a designated distance learning course other than the present distance learning course; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
    - (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] part-time student in connection with a designated part-time course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] person’s status as an eligible student, eligible distance learning student or as an eligible part-time student in connection with the course referred to in sub-paragraph (b)(i) has been converted or transferred from that course to the present distance learning course as a result of one or more conversions or transfers in accordance with regulations made by the Welsh Ministers under section 22 of the 1998 Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] person was ordinarily resident in Wales on the first day of the first academic year of the course referred to in sub-paragraph (b)(i); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] person’s status as an eligible distance learning student has not terminated *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] ;[^c-c40f7734c527f13c9adaba6d4054580c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c)[^c-2e0065f388f7cce605b5aa777b6e4d99] the— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) present distance learning course is an end-on course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] person qualified as an eligible distance learning student in connection with the course in relation to which the present course is an end-on course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] period of eligibility in respect of the course in sub-paragraph (c)(ii) only ceased on the grounds that the student had completed the course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] person was ordinarily resident in Wales on the first day of the first academic year of the course in sub-paragraph (c)(ii). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(10) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the Welsh Ministers determined that, by virtue of being a refugee or the spouse, civil partner, child or step-child of a refugee, a person (“A” in this paragraph) was an eligible distance learning student in connection with an application for support for an earlier year of the present distance learning course or an application for support in connection with a designated course, designated part-time course or other designated distance learning course from which A’s status as an eligible student, eligible part-time student or eligible distance learning student has been transferred to the present distance learning course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) as at the day before the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support begins, the refugee status of A or of A’s spouse, civil partner, parent (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) or step-parent, as the case may be, has expired and no further leave to remain has been granted and no appeal is pending (within the meaning of section 104 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00070]),

A’s status as an eligible distance learning student terminates immediately before the first day of the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(11) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the Welsh Ministers determined that, by virtue of being a person with leave to enter or remain or the spouse, civil partner, child or step-child of such a person, a person (“A” in this paragraph) was an eligible distance learning student in connection with an application for support for an earlier year of the present distance learning course or an application for support in connection with a designated course, designated part-time course or other designated distance learning course from which A’s status as an eligible student, eligible part-time student or eligible distance learning student has been transferred to the present distance learning course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) as at the day before the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support begins, the period for which the person with leave to enter or remain is allowed to stay in the United Kingdom has expired and no further leave to remain has been granted and no appeal is pending (within the meaning of section 104 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

A’s status as an eligible distance learning student terminates immediately before the first day of the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(12) Paragraphs (10) and (11) do not apply where the student began the course in connection with which the Welsh Ministers determined that the student was an eligible student or eligible part-time student, as the case may be, before 1 September 2007. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(13) An eligible distance learning student may not, at any one time, qualify for support under these Regulations for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) more than one designated distance learning course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) a designated distance learning course and a designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) a designated distance learning course and a designated part-time course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) a designated distance learning course and a designated postgraduate course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
