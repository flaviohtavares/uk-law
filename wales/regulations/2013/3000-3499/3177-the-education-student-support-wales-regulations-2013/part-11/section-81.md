# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 81

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 81. Amount of support (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d3e0400dd28dbb25711ac9c1b6ea1ca7][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) and regulation 87(6), the amount of support payable under regulation 80 in respect of an academic year is as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) if at the date of the eligible distance learner student’s application the eligible distance learning student or the eligible distance learning student’s partner is entitled— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) under Part VII of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00073] to income support, housing benefit or council tax benefit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] under Part 1 of the Jobseekers Act 1995 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00074] to income–based jobseeker’s allowance or under section 2 of the Employment and Training Act 1973 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00075] to an allowance under the arrangements known as the New Deal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] under Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2007 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00076] to an income-related employment and support allowance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] to universal credit; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (v) to a reduction under a council tax reduction scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

  the maximum amount of support available under regulation 80(1) is payable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) where the relevant income is less than £16,865, the maximum amount of support available under regulation 80(1) is payable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) where the relevant income is £16,865, the maximum amount of support available under regulation 80(1)(b) is payable together with £50 less than the maximum amount of support available under regulation 80(1)(a); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) where the relevant income exceeds £16,865 but is less than £25,435, the maximum amount of support available under regulation 80(1)(b) is payable and the amount of support payable under regulation 80(1)(a) is the amount determined in accordance with paragraph (2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (e) where the relevant income is £25,435, the maximum amount of support available under regulation 80(1)(b) is payable and the amount of support payable under regulation 80(1)(a) is £50; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (f) where the relevant income exceeds £25,435 but is less than £26,095, the maximum amount of support available under regulation 80(1)(b) is payable and no support is payable under regulation 80(1)(a); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (g) where the relevant income is £26,095 or more but less than £28,180 no support is available under regulation 80(1)(a) and the amount of support payable under regulation 80(1)(b) is the amount left after deducting from the maximum amount of support available under regulation 80(1)(b) £1 for every £1.886 by which the relevant income exceeds £26,095; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (h) where the relevant income is £28,180, no support is payable under regulation 80(1)(a) and the amount of support payable under regulation 80(1)(b) is £50; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (i) where the relevant income exceeds £28,180 no support is payable under regulation 80(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) Where paragraph (1)(d) applies, the amount of support payable under regulation 80(1)(a) is determined by deducting from the maximum amount of support available under regulation 80(1)(a) one of the following amounts— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £50 plus a further £1 for each complete £9.26 by which the relevant income exceeds £16,865; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) where the actual fees are less than £1,025, an amount equal to that left after deducting from the amount calculated under sub-paragraph (a) the difference between £1,025 and the actual fees (unless the amount is a negative number in which case the maximum amount of support available under regulation 80(1)(a) is payable). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
