# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 2 (affected by repeal, see note) — DESIGNATED COURSES (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations 5, 78 and 95 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

**1.**[^c-d894d6f48636ff894fe04769ef5ff46c] A first degree course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

**2.**[^c-17f61236bd434a2571a98ab6e00badbb] A course for the Diploma of Higher Education. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

**3.**[^c-0cf033362a120d37248410d5304d0119] A course for the Higher National Diploma or Higher National Certificate of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the Business & Technician Education Council; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the Scottish Qualification Authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

**4.**[^c-e4fe1bf2fdde1f7ba73301b60dba62d5] A course for the certificate of Higher Education. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

**5.**[^c-2ffd8e6a17054a6c4a373a1d3f39266a] A course for the initial training of teachers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

**6.**[^c-e10690862413a384b3dd3e24034d07fd] A course for the further training of youth and community workers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

**7.**[^c-d92a972b565e1339d8b099b2c5315cbb] A course in preparation for a professional examination of a standard higher than that of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) examination at advanced level for the General Certificate of Education or the examination at higher level for the Scottish Certificate of Education; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the examination for the National Certificate or the National Diploma of either of the bodies mentioned in paragraph 3, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

not being a course for entry to which a first degree (or equivalent qualification) is normally required. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

**8.**[^c-e6fd4027990fa10bef722a7e1badf78f] A course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) providing education (whether or not in preparation for an examination) the standard of which is higher than that of courses providing education in preparation for any of the examinations mentioned in paragraph 7(a) or (b) but not higher than that of a first degree course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) for entry to which a first degree (or equivalent qualification) is not normally required. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
