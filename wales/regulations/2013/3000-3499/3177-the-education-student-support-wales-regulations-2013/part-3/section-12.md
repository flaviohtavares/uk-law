# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 12

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Requirement to enter into a contract for a loan (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1ce8364ff0177b955a54fa081b39bad7][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

To receive a loan under these Regulations an eligible student must enter into a contract with the Welsh Ministers on terms to be decided by the Welsh Ministers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
