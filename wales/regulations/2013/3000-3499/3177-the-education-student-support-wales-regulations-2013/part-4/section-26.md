# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 26

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. New private institution fee loan (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ebd7c974f53337462097bab51310d7d8][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A new system eligible student who is a 2012 cohort student qualifies in accordance with this regulation for a new private institution fee loan in respect of, or otherwise in connection with, the 2012 cohort student’s attendance on a designated course provided by a private institution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) A new private institution fee loan is not available in respect of an academic year if that year is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) a bursary year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) an Erasmus year of a course provided by an institution in Scotland or Northern Ireland; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) an Erasmus year of a course provided by an institution in England or Wales where the course began before 1 September 2012. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) The maximum amount of new private institution fee loan available under this regulation to a 2012 cohort student in respect of an academic year of a designated course provided by a private institution where none of the circumstances in regulations 17(4), 20(7), 20(8) or 20(9) applies is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £6,000; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the fees payable by the student in respect of, or otherwise in connection with, that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) The maximum amount of new private institution fee loan available under this regulation to a 2012 cohort student in respect of an academic year of a designated course provided by a private institution where one of the circumstances in regulation 17(4) or regulation 20(7) applies is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £3,000; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the fees payable by the student in respect of, or otherwise in connection with, that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) The maximum amount of new private institution fee loan available under this regulation to a 2012 cohort student in respect of an academic year of a designated course provided by a private institution where regulation 20(8) applies is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £1,200; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the fees payable by the student in respect of, or otherwise in connection with, that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) The maximum amount of new private institution fee loan available under this regulation to a 2012 cohort student in respect of an academic year of a designated course provided by a private institution where regulation 20(9) applies is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £900; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the fees payable by the student in respect of, or otherwise in connection with, that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(7) Where a new system eligible student has applied for a new private institution fee loan of less than the maximum amount available in relation to an academic year, the new system eligible student may apply to borrow an additional amount which, when added to the amount already applied for, does not exceed that maximum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
