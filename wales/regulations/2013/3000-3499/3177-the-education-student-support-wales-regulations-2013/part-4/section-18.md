# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 18

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18. Amount of the grant for fees at a private institution (not on behalf of a publicly funded institution): old system eligible students (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bdd11b9a24a8fc61241346232b6895a0][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year of a designated course at a private institution is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £1,285; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the fees payable by the student in connection with that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) In the case of a designated course at the University of Buckingham, the amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year is £3,275. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
