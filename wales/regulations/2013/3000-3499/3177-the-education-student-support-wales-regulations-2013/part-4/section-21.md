# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 21

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21. General qualifying conditions for fee contribution loans and fee loans (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ba5b3324f2b815ef51a4533f1d140437][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible student qualifies for a fee contribution loan or a fee loan in connection with the student’s attendance on a designated course in accordance with this Part provided that the student is not excluded from qualification by the following paragraph, regulation 6 or regulation 7. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) An eligible student does not qualify for a fee contribution loan or a fee loan in respect of an academic year if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) that year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) is a bursary year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] an Erasmus year of a course provided by an institution in Scotland or Northern Ireland; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] an Erasmus year of a course provided by an institution in England or Wales where the course began before 1 September 2012; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the designated course is an old flexible postgraduate course for the initial training of teachers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) Paragraph (2) does not apply in relation to an accelerated graduate entry course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
