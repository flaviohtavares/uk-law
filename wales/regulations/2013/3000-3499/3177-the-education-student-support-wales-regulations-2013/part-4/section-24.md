# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 24

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Fee loans in respect of courses beginning before 1 September 2012: new system eligible students qualifying for a fee grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-008b302ad59546703883e26c0e33eb0c][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A new system eligible student who qualifies for a fee grant may apply under this regulation for a fee loan in respect of the new system eligible student’s attendance on the qualifying designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The maximum amount of fee loan available under this regulation is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £1,380 or, where any of the circumstances in regulation 17(4) apply, £680; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the remainder of the fees payable by the new system eligible student less an amount equal to the fee grant in respect of or otherwise in connection with that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) Where the new system eligible student has applied for a fee loan of less than the maximum amount available in relation to an academic year, the new system eligible student may apply to borrow an additional amount which, when added to the amount already applied for, does not exceed that maximum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
