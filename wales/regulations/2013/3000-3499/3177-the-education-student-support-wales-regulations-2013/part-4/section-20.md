# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 20

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. New fee grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-6c144c7844077a1d8c2b5f08af681656][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a new system eligible student who is a 2012 cohort student qualifies in accordance with this regulation for a new fee grant in respect of, or otherwise in connection with, the 2012 cohort student’s attendance on a designated course provided by a publicly funded institution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) A new fee grant is not available in respect of an academic year if that year is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) a bursary year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) an Erasmus year of a course provided by an institution in Scotland or Northern Ireland; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) an Erasmus year of a course provided by an institution in England or Wales where the course began before 1 September 2012. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) The maximum amount of new fee grant available under this regulation to a 2012 cohort student in respect of an academic year of a designated course where none of the circumstances in regulation 17(4) or paragraphs (7), (8) or (9) applies is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £5,315; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the amount by which the fees payable by the applicant exceed £3,685. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) The maximum amount of new fee grant available under this regulation to a 2012 cohort student in respect of an academic year of a designated course where one of the circumstances in regulation 17(4) or paragraph (7) applies is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £2,665; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the amount by which the fees payable by the applicant exceed £1,835. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) The maximum amount of new fee grant available under this regulation to a 2012 cohort student in respect of an academic year of a designated course where paragraph (8) applies is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £900; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the amount by which the fees payable by the applicant exceed £900. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) The maximum amount of new fee grant available under this regulation to a 2012 cohort student in respect of an academic year of a designated course where paragraph (9) applies is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £675; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the amount by which the fees payable by the applicant exceed £675. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(7) This paragraph applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) in respect of an academic year of a sandwich course provided by an institution in Scotland or Northern Ireland which began on or after 1 September 2012 and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) during which any periods of full-time study are in aggregate less than 10 weeks; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] if in respect of that academic year and any previous academic years of the course the aggregate of any one or more periods of attendance which are not periods of full-time study at the institution (disregarding intervening vacations) exceeds 30 weeks; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) in respect of an academic year of a course provided by an institution in Scotland or Northern Ireland in conjunction with an overseas institution which began on or after 1 September 2012 and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) during which the periods of full-time study at the institution in the United Kingdom are in aggregate less than 10 weeks; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] if in respect of that academic year and any previous academic years of the course the aggregate of any one or more periods of attendance which are not periods of full-time study at the institution in the United Kingdom (disregarding intervening vacations) exceeds 30 weeks. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(8) This paragraph applies in respect of an academic year of a sandwich course provided by an institution in England or Wales which began on or after 1 September 2012 and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) during which any periods of full-time study are in aggregate less than 10 weeks; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) if in respect of that academic year and any previous academic years of the course the aggregate of any one or more periods of attendance which are not periods of full-time study at the institution (disregarding intervening vacations) exceeds 30 weeks. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(9) This paragraph applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) in respect of an academic year of a course provided by an institution in England or Wales in conjunction with an overseas institution which began on or after 1 September 2012 and; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) during which the periods of full-time study at the institution in the United Kingdom are in aggregate less than 10 weeks; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] if in respect of that academic year and any previous academic years of the course the aggregate of any one or more periods of attendance which are not periods of full-time study at the institution in the United Kingdom (disregarding intervening vacations) exceeds 30 weeks; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) in respect of an Erasmus year of a course provided by an institution in England or Wales which began on or after 1 September 2012. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
