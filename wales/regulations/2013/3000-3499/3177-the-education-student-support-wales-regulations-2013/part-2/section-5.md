# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 5

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Designated courses (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b5f1fb1fbace54bd99388159301b79b1][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2), (3) and (4), a course is a designated course for the purposes of section 22(1) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] and regulation 4 if it is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) listed in Schedule 2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) one of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) a full-time course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] a sandwich course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] a course for the initial training of teachers which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] began before 1 September 2010; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] begins on or after 1 September 2010 where the student transfers to the present course pursuant to regulation 8 from a course for the initial training of teachers which began before 1 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] September 2010; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
    - (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] began on or after 1 September 2010 but before 1 September 2011 and in relation to which the student was a 2010 gap year student; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] a distance learning course other than a course to which regulation 78(5) applies; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) not a designated distance learning course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) of at least— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) one academic year’s duration; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] six weeks’ duration in the case of a flexible postgraduate ITT course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (e) wholly provided *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] in the United Kingdom[^c-cc246304becb96430b1165cdaf3e0df4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] by a publicly funded educational institution in the United Kingdom or provided by such an institution in conjunction with an institution outside the United Kingdom; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (f) for a course beginning on or after 1 September 2012 which falls within paragraphs 1, 2, 4, 6, 7 or 8 of Schedule 2, a course leading to an award granted or to be granted by a body falling within section 214(2)(a) or (b) of the Education Reform Act 1988 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00046].

(2) A course falling within paragraph 7 or 8 of Schedule 2 is not a designated course where the governing body of a maintained school has arranged for the provision of such a course to a pupil of the school. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) A course that is taken as part of an employment-based teacher training scheme is not a designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) Paragraph (1)(c) does not apply where the person applying for support under regulation 9 in connection with the course is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) a disabled eligible student; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) undertaking that course in the United Kingdom but not in attendance because the person is unable to attend for a reason which relates to the person’s disability. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) For the purposes of paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) a course is provided by an institution if it provides the teaching and supervision which comprise the course, whether or not the institution has entered into an agreement with the student to provide the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) a university and any constituent college or institution in the nature of a college of a university is regarded as publicly funded if either the university or the constituent college or institution is publicly funded; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) an institution is not regarded as publicly funded by reason only that it receives public funds from the governing body of a higher education institution in accordance with section 65(3A) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00047].

(6) A course to which this paragraph applies is considered to be a single course for a first degree or for an equivalent qualification even if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the course leads to another degree or qualification being conferred before the degree or equivalent qualification; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) part of the course is optional. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(7) Paragraph (6) applies to a course the standard of which is not higher than a first degree which leads to a qualification as a medical doctor, dentist, veterinary surgeon, architect, landscape architect, landscape designer, landscape manager, town planner or town and country planner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(8) For the purposes of section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] and regulation 4(1) the Welsh Ministers may designate courses of higher education which are not designated under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
