# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 4

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Eligible students (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2941a0ed7ad05693ef185d74910c6e24][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible student qualifies for support in connection with a designated course subject to and in accordance with these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) Subject to paragraph (7), a person is an eligible student in connection with a designated course if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) in assessing the person’s application for support under regulation 9 the Welsh Ministers determine that the person falls within one of the categories set out in Part 2 of Schedule 1; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the person is not excluded by paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) Subject to paragraph (7), a person (“A” in this paragraph) is not an eligible student if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) an old award has been bestowed on A in respect of A’s attendance on the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) A is eligible for a loan in relation to an academic year of the course under the Education (Student Loans) Act 1990 or the Education (Student Loans) (Northern Ireland) Order 1990; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) there has been bestowed on, or paid to, A in relation to A’s attendance on the course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) a healthcare bursary, other than a universal healthcare bursary, the amount of which is not calculated by reference to A’s income; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] any allowance under the Nursing and Midwifery Student Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 2007 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00045];
- (d) A is in breach of any obligation to repay any loan; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (e) A has reached the age of 18 and has not ratified any agreement for a loan made with A when A was under the age of 18; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (f) A has, in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers, shown by A’s conduct that A is unfitted to receive support under these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) For the purposes of paragraphs (3)(d) and (3)(e), “loan” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*benthyciad *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means a loan made under the student loans legislation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) In a case where the agreement for a loan is subject to the law of Scotland, paragraph (3)(e) only applies if the agreement was made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) before 25 September 1991, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) with the concurrence of the borrower’s curator or at a time when the borrower had no curator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) An eligible student in respect of whom the first academic year of the specified designated course begins on or after 1 September 2000 does not, at any one time, qualify for support under these Regulations for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) more than one designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) a designated course and a designated part-time course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) a designated course and a designated postgraduate course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) a designated course and a designated distance learning course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(7) Subject to paragraphs (9) to (11), if a person satisfies the conditions in paragraph (8)(a),(b) or (c)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) paragraphs (2) and (3) do not apply to the person; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the person is an eligible student for the purposes of these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(8) The conditions referred to in paragraph (7) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) person qualified as an eligible student in connection with an earlier academic year of the present course pursuant to regulations made by the Welsh Ministers under section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a];
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] person was ordinarily resident in Wales on the first day of the first academic year of the present course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] person’s status as an eligible student has not terminated; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) present course is an end-on course (other than one of the kind referred to in paragraph (c) of the definition of “end-on course” in regulation 2) which the person is starting on or after 1 September 2006; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] person qualified as an eligible student in connection with the course in relation to which the present course is an end-on course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] period of eligibility in respect of the course in sub-paragraph (b)(ii) only ceased on the grounds that the student had completed the course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] person was ordinarily resident in Wales on the first day of the first academic year of the course in sub-paragraph (b)(ii); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) the— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) Welsh Ministers have previously determined that the person is an eligible— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] part-time student in connection with a designated part-time course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] student in connection with a designated course other than the present course; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
    - (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] distance learning student in connection with a designated distance learning course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] person’s status as an eligible part-time student, an eligible distance learning student or as an eligible student in connection with the course in sub-paragraph (c)(i) has been converted or transferred from that course to the present course as a result of one or more conversions or transfers in accordance with regulations made by the Welsh Ministers under section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a];
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] person was ordinarily resident in Wales on the first day of the first academic year of the course referred to in sub-paragraph (c)(i); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] person’s status as an eligible student has not terminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(9) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the Welsh Ministers have determined that, by virtue of being a refugee or the spouse, civil partner, child or step-child of a refugee, a person (“A” in this paragraph) was— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) an eligible student in connection with an application for support for an earlier year of the present course, an application for support for a course in relation to which the present course is an end-on course or an application for support in connection with a designated part-time course, a designated distance learning course or other designated course from which A’s status as an eligible part-time student, an eligible distance learning student or an eligible student has been transferred to the present course; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] a qualifying student in connection with an application for support for an earlier year of the qualifying course or other qualifying course from which A’s status as a qualifying student has been transferred to the qualifying course in respect of which the student is applying for support; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) as at the day before the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support starts, the refugee status of A or of A’s spouse, civil partner, parent (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) or step-parent, as the case may be, has expired and no further leave to remain has been granted and no appeal is pending (within the meaning of section 104 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

A’s status as an eligible or qualifying student terminates immediately before the first day of the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(10) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the Welsh Ministers have determined that, by virtue of being a person with leave to enter or remain or the spouse, civil partner, child or step-child of such a person, a person (“A” in this paragraph) was— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) an eligible student in connection with an application for support for an earlier year of the present course, an application for support for a course in relation to which the present course is an end-on course or an application for support in connection with a designated part-time course, designated distance learning course or other designated course from which A’s status as an eligible part-time student, eligible distance learning student or eligible student has been transferred to the present course; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] a qualifying student in connection with an application for support for an earlier year of the qualifying course or other qualifying course from which A’s status as a qualifying student has been transferred to the qualifying course in respect of which the student is applying for support; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) as at the day before the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support starts, the period for which the person with leave to enter or remain is allowed to stay in the United Kingdom has expired and no further leave to remain has been granted and no appeal is pending (within the meaning of section 104 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

A’s status as an eligible or qualifying student terminates immediately before the first day of the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(11) Paragraphs (9) and (10) do not apply where the student began the course in connection with which the Welsh Ministers determined that A was an eligible part-time student, an eligible student or a qualifying student, as the case may be, before 1 September 2007. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(12) Subject to paragraph (13), a prisoner who begins the present course on or after 1 September 2012 will not be an eligible student unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) they are an eligible prisoner; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) they are an eligible student who has transferred to the present course on or after 1 September 2012 under regulation 8 from a course beginning before 1 September 2012; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) the present course is an end-on course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(13) Paragraph (12) does not apply in respect of an academic year during which the student enters or is released from prison. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
