> Text as at 2017-02-17.

## 1. Title, commencement and application (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-23272dfdac9dad99fb9aca95c83b292e][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) These Regulations come into force on 10 January 2014 and apply in relation to Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
