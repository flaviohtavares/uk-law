# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 98

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 98. New part-time fee loan (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-cc786bb3669bf708332a44f6338075f2][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible part-time student who is a new eligible part-time student qualifies in accordance with this regulation for a new part-time fee loan in connection with the student’s attendance on, or undertaking of a designated part-time course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) A new-part time fee loan is not available in respect of an academic year of a designated part-time course which is a bursary year or an Erasmus year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) The maximum amount of new part-time fee loan available under this regulation to a new eligible part-time student in respect of an academic year of a designated part-time course provided by an institution in Wales is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £2,625; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the fee payable by the student in respect of that academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) The maximum amount of new part-time fee loan available under this regulation to a new eligible part-time student in respect of an academic year of a designated part-time course provided by a publicly funded institution in England, Scotland or Northern Ireland is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £6,750; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the fee payable by the student in respect of that academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) The maximum amount of new part-time fee loan available under this regulation to a new eligible part-time student in respect of an academic year of a designated part-time course provided by a private institution in England, Scotland or Northern Ireland is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £4,500; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the fee payable by the student in respect of that academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) An eligible part-time student who is not a new eligible part-time student will not qualify for any support under this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
