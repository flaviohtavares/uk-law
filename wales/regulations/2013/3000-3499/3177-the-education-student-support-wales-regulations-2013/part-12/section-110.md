# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 110

> Text as at 2017-02-17.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 110. Part-time grants for dependants – final calculation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1e71992b64ff41b76d18172c229456ad][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The amount payable in respect of a particular element of the part-time grants for dependants is determined in accordance with this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The amount payable varies according to the intensity of study. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- The intensity of study is calculated as follows and expressed as a percentage *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

  [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2013/3177) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

  where PT and FT have the meanings given by regulation 97(2) and (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) In the case of part-time adult dependants’ grant, where the intensity of study is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) 50 per cent or more but less than 60 per cent, the amount payable is equal to 50 per cent of the resulting amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) 60 per cent or more but less than 75 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] per cent, the amount payable is equal to 60 per cent of the resulting amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) 75 per cent or more, the amount payable is equal to 75 per cent of the resulting amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) For the purposes of paragraph (3), “the resulting amount” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*y swm sy’n deillio o hyn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means the amount of part-time adult dependants’ grant determined in accordance with regulation 106 with deductions (if any) having been applied in accordance with regulation 109. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) In the case of part-time childcare grant, where the intensity of study is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) 50 per cent or more but less than 60 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] per cent, the amount payable is equal to 50 per cent of the resulting amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) 60 per cent or more but less than 75 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] per cent, the amount payable is equal to 60 per cent of the resulting amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) 75 per cent or more, the amount payable is equal to 75 per cent of the resulting amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) For the purposes of paragraph (5), “the resulting amount” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*y swm sy’n deillio o hyn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means the amount of part-time childcare grant determined in accordance with regulation 106 with deductions (if any) having been applied in accordance with regulation 109. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(7) In the case of part-time parents’ learning allowance, where the intensity of study is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) 50 per cent or more, but less than 60 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] per cent, the amount payable is equal to 50 per cent of the resulting amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) 60 per cent or more but less than 75 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] per cent, the amount payable is equal to 60 per cent of the resulting amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) 75 per cent or more, the amount payable is equal to 75 per cent of the resulting amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(8) For the purposes of paragraph (7), “the resulting amount” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*y swm sy’n deillio o hyn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means the amount of part-time parents’ learning allowance determined in accordance with regulation 106 with deductions (if any) having been applied in accordance with regulation 109. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(9) No element of part-time grants for dependants is payable where the intensity of study is less than 50 per cent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
