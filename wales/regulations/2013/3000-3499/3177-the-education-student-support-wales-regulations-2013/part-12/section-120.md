# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 120

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 120. Payment of new part-time fee loan (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d0bd662cb4d017d275b15ef53e535a99][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers must pay the new part-time fee loan for which a new eligible part-time student qualifies in respect of an academic year of a designated part-time course to an academic authority to which the new eligible part-time student is liable to make payment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The Welsh Ministers may pay the new part-time fee loan in such instalments (if any) and at such times as they consider appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) The Welsh Ministers must not pay the new part-time fee loan or any instalment of that loan unless they have received from the relevant academic authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) a request for payment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) a declaration under regulation 113(2) to (6). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) The Welsh Ministers may make provisional payments under this regulation in such cases as they deem appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
