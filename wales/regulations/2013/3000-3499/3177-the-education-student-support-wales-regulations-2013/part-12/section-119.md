# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 119

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 119. Payment of grants for fees in respect of designated part-time courses beginning before 1 September 2014 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-996905414995bb43bf9919c00b8cf4ae][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), the Welsh Ministers must pay the grant in respect of fees for which the eligible part-time student qualifies in relation to an academic year of a designated part-time course beginning before 1 September 2014 to the appropriate academic authority after a written request for payment has been received which the Welsh Ministers consider to be a valid request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The Welsh Ministers may make payments under paragraph (1) at such times and in such instalments (if any) as they see fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) The Welsh Ministers may make provisional payments under paragraph (1) in such cases as they deem appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
