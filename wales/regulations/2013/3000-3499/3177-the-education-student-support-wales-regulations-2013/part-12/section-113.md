# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 113

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 113. Information and other matters (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-740e82d11b428abd20b055d19a067ebd][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Schedule 3 applies in respect of the provision of information by an applicant and an eligible part-time student. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), the appropriate academic authority must, on the request of the applicant, complete a declaration in such form as may be required by the Welsh Ministers to accompany the application for support under regulation 111. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) An academic authority is not required to complete a declaration if it is unable to give the confirmation required by paragraph (4)(a)(ii) or (4)(b)(ii). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) In this Part, “declaration” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*datganiad *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) where the applicant is applying for support in connection with the designated part-time course beginning before 1 September 2014 for the first time, a statement that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) provides the course information; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] confirms that the applicant has undertaken at least two weeks of the designated part-time course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) in any other case where the applicant is applying for support in connection with a designated part-time course beginning before 1 September 2014, a statement that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) provides the course information; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] confirms that the applicant has enrolled to undertake the academic year of the designated part-time course in respect of which the applicant is applying for support under this Part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) where the applicant is applying for support in connection with a designated part-time course beginning on or after 1 September 2014, a statement that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) provides the course information; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] confirms that the applicant has undertaken at least two weeks of the designated part-time course in the academic year of that course in respect of which the applicant is applying for support under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) In this regulation, “course information” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*gwbodaeth am y cwrs *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the amount of fees being charged in respect of the academic year in respect of which the applicant is applying for support under this Part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the intensity of study; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) certification by the academic authority that it considers— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) the course to be a designated part-time course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] that it will be possible for the applicant to complete the course within the period specified in regulation 95(1)(c). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) For the purposes of paragraph (5)(c)(ii) the academic authority must have regard to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) any increase in intensity of study that would be required for the applicant to complete the course within the period specified in regulation 95(1)(c); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) any parts of the course which the applicant has been required to repeat. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(7) An academic authority must notify the Welsh Ministers as soon as is reasonably practicable when any one of the following events occurs— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) an eligible part-time student ceases to attend or undertake a designated part-time course during the academic year in respect of which the student is claiming support under this Part and the academic authority has determined or agreed that the student will not return during that academic year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) changes are made or occur to any of the course information submitted as part of a declaration under paragraphs (2) to (6). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(8) Where an academic authority gives notice to the Welsh Ministers under paragraph (7), the academic authority must also provide the Welsh Ministers with such further information as the Welsh Ministers may require in relation to the relevant event in paragraph (7). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(9) For the purposes of paragraph (8) “relevant event” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*digwyddiad perthnasol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means the event or events under paragraph (7) which form the subject of the notice given under paragraph (7). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
