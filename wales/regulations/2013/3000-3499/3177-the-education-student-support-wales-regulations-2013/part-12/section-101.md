# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 101

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 101. Part-time grants for dependants – general (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b66fcf10607f3a7f54cba0a24c7815f8][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible part-time student qualifies for part-time grants for dependants provided that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the part-time student is not excluded from qualification by any of the following paragraphs, regulation 93 or regulation 96; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the part-time student satisfies the qualifying conditions for the particular grant for which the student is applying. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2)[^key-4c6be151196b437b937b9b2e2315434c] Subject to paragraph (5), an eligible part-time student does not qualify for part-time grants for dependants if the eligible part-time student is a prisoner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) An eligible part-time student who is a new eligible part-time student does not qualify for part-time grants for dependants in relation to an academic year of a designated part-time course if the intensity of study during that year is less than 50 per cent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) For the purposes of paragraph (3) the intensity of study during an academic year of a designated part-time course is to be calculated in accordance with regulation 97(2) and (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5)[^key-25e65e8dcae614b3a9daac1f7e610b14] Paragraph (2) does not apply in respect of an academic year during which the eligible part-time student enters prison or is released from prison. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

**102.**[^key-b9f8cce6b8947783447e5ffc00caa07e] (1) The part-time grants for dependants consist of the following elements— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) part-time adult dependants’ grant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) part-time childcare grant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) part-time parents’ learning allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The qualifying conditions for each element are set out in regulations 103 to 110 and the amounts payable in respect of each element are determined in accordance with those regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) A deduction may be made from any element of the part-time grants for dependants in accordance with regulations 108 and 109. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
