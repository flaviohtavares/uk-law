# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 103

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 103. Part-time adult dependants’ grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bd28d79e9b006c75070e8ec796b2e671][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible part-time student qualifies for a part-time adult dependants’ grant in connection with the eligible part-time student’s attendance on a designated part-time course in accordance with this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The part-time adult dependants’ grant is available in respect of one dependant of an eligible part-time student who is either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the eligible part-time student’s partner; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) an adult dependant of the eligible part-time student whose net income for the relevant year does not exceed £3,923. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) The amount of part-time adult dependants’ grant payable in respect of an academic year is calculated in accordance with regulations 106 and 108 to 110, the basic amount being— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £2,732; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) where the person in respect of whom the eligible part-time student is applying for part-time adult dependants’ grant is ordinarily resident outside the United Kingdom, such amount not exceeding £2,732 as the Welsh Ministers consider reasonable in the circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
