# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 94

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 94. Students becoming eligible during the course of the academic year (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-bb497e8d38c8db863029b56f68bf8d76][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where one of the events listed in paragraph (4) occurs in the course of an academic year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) a student may qualify for a grant in respect of fees or a new part-time fee loan in respect of that academic year in accordance with this Part provided that the relevant event occurred within the first three months of the academic year; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) a grant in respect of fees or a new part-time fee loan are not available in respect of any academic year beginning before the academic year in which the relevant event occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) Where one of the events listed in sub-paragraphs (a), (b), (e), (f), (g), (h) or (i) of paragraph (4) occurs in the course of an academic year, a student may qualify for part-time grants for dependants in accordance with this Part in respect of all or part of that academic year but the student does not qualify for a grant in respect of any academic year beginning before the academic year in which the relevant event occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) Where one of the events listed in sub-paragraphs (a), (b), (e), (f), (g) (h) or (i) of paragraph (4) occurs in the course of an academic year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) a student may qualify for a grant for books, travel and other expenditure, a new part-time course grant or a grant for disabled part-time students’ living costs in respect of that academic year in accordance with this Part; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) neither a grant for books, travel and other expenditure, a new part-time course grant or a grant for disabled part-time students’ living costs is available in respect of any academic year beginning before the academic year in which the relevant event occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) The events are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the student’s course becomes a designated part-time course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the student or the student’s spouse, civil partner or parent (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) is recognised as a refugee or becomes a person with leave to enter or remain; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) a state accedes to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] where the student is a national of that state or a family member (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) of a national of that state; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) the student becomes a family member (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) of an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] EU national *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a];
- (e) the state of which the student is a national accedes to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] where the student has been ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] Islands *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] throughout the three year-period immediately preceding the first day of the first academic year of the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (f) the student acquires the right of permanent residence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (g) the student becomes the child of a Turkish worker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (h) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 6(1)(a) of Schedule 1; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (i) the student becomes the child of a Swiss national. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
