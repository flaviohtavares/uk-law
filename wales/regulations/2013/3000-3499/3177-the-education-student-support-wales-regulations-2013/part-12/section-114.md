# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 114

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 114. Transfer of status (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-07ecd927f79eafa2b393672c429aa6d7][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where an eligible part-time student transfers from a designated part-time course to another designated part-time course *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]**,** the Welsh Ministers must transfer the student’s status as an eligible part-time student to that other course where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) they receive a request from the eligible part-time student to do so; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) they are satisfied that one or more of the grounds for transfer in paragraph (2) applies; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) the period of eligibility has not terminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The grounds for transfer are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the eligible part-time student starts to undertake another designated part-time course at the same institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the eligible part-time student starts to undertake a designated part-time course at another institution; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) after commencing a designated part-time course for a first degree (other than an honours degree) the eligible part-time student is, before the completion of that course, admitted to a designated part-time course for an honours degree in the same subject at the same institution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), an eligible part-time student who transfers under paragraph (1) is entitled, for the remainder of the academic year in which the eligible part-time student transfers, to continue to receive in connection with the course to which the eligible part-time student transfers the support under this Part for which the Welsh Ministers have determined the eligible part-time student qualifies in respect of the course from which the eligible part-time student transfers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) The Welsh Ministers may re-assess the amount of support payable after the transfer in accordance with this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) An eligible part-time student who transfers under paragraph (1) after the Welsh Ministers have determined the eligible part-time student’s support under this Part in connection with the academic year of the course from which the eligible part-time student is transferring but before the eligible part-time student completes that year may not apply for another grant under regulation 97(1)(b), regulation 99, regulation 100 or regulations 101 to 110 in connection with the academic year of the course to which the eligible part-time student transfers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) Where an eligible part-time student transfers under paragraph (1) from a designated part-time course beginning before 1 September 2014, the maximum amount of support under regulation 97(1)(a) in respect of the academic year of the course to and from which the eligible part-time student transfers is the amount of support with fees available in connection with the course which has the highest intensity of study as defined in regulation 97. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(7) Where a new eligible part-time student transfers under paragraph (1) from a designated part-time course beginning on or after 1 September 2014, the maximum amount of new part-time fee loan available under regulation 98 in respect of the academic year of the course to and from which the new eligible part-time student transfers is the amount available in connection with the course which attracts the highest amount of new part-time fee loan in accordance with regulation 98(3) to (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
