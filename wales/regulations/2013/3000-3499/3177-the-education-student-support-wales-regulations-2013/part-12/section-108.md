# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 108

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 108. Part-time grants for dependants - calculation of contribution (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-80e2d54a07ec7e8352701c6d63437bb1][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible part-time student’s contribution in respect of an academic year and part-time dependants’ grants payable in respect of that year is the amount, if any, calculated under Schedule 6. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The Welsh Ministers may require an eligible part-time student to provide from time to time such information as they consider necessary as to the income of any person whose means are relevant to the assessment of the student’s contribution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
