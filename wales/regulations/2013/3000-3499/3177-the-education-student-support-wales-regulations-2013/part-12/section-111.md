# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 111

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 111. Applications for support (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c6e021ebe6c1f4b82f70518a7b98905b][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person must apply for support in connection with each academic year of a designated part-time course by completing and submitting to the Welsh Ministers an application in such form as the Welsh Ministers may require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The application must be accompanied by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) a declaration under regulation 113(2) to (6) completed by the academic authority; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) such additional documentation as the Welsh Ministers may require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), the application must reach the Welsh Ministers within a period of nine months beginning with the first day of the academic year of the course in respect of which it is submitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) Paragraph (3) does not apply where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) one of the events listed in regulation 94(4) occurs after the first day of the academic year in respect of which the applicant is applying for support under this Part, in which case the application must reach the Welsh Ministers within a period of nine months beginning with the day on which the event occurred; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the applicant is applying for a grant for disabled part-time students’ living costs, in which case the application must reach the Welsh Ministers as soon as is reasonably practicable; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) the Welsh Ministers consider that having regard to the circumstances of the particular case the time limit should be relaxed, in which case the application must reach the Welsh Ministers not later than such date as they specify. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) The Welsh Ministers may take such steps and make such inquiries as they consider necessary to determine whether the applicant is an eligible part-time student, whether the applicant qualifies for support under this Part and the amount of support payable, if any. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) The Welsh Ministers must notify the applicant in writing of whether or not the applicant qualifies for support under this Part and, if the applicant does qualify, the amount of support payable in respect of the academic year, if any. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
