# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 112

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 112. Assistance with fees in respect of attendance on part-time courses in England, Northern Ireland or Scotland beginning before 1 September 2014 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-47f911f6c8707a9af98954fd1d65dd93][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may pay support under this Part to assist with fees to an eligible part-time student in connection with the eligible part-time student’s attendance on a designated part-time course beginning before 1 September 2014 in England, Northern Ireland or Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The support paid under paragraph (1) must not exceed the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the maximum amount of support that would have been payable to the eligible part-time student under regulation 97(1)(a) had the eligible part-time student been undertaking the course in Wales; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the maximum amount of support to assist with fees that in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers would have been payable to the eligible part-time student according to whether the eligible part-time student attends the designated part-time course in England, Northern Ireland or Scotland— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) pursuant to regulations made by the Secretary of State under section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] had the eligible part-time student been ordinarily resident in England and undertaking the part-time course in England; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] pursuant to regulations made under Articles 3 and 8(4) of the Education (Student Support) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 had the eligible part-time student been ordinarily resident in Northern Ireland and undertaking the part-time course in Northern Ireland; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] from funds of the Scottish Further and Higher Education Funding Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00086] had the eligible part-time student been ordinarily resident in Scotland and undertaking the part-time course in Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
