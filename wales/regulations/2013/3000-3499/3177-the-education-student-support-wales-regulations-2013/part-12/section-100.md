# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 100

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 100. Grants for disabled part-time students’ living costs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1c082240019f8f51c7d377e3b36fd189][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible part-time student qualifies in accordance with this regulation for a grant for disabled part-time students’ living costs to assist with the additional expenditure which the Welsh Ministers are satisfied the student is obliged to incur in connection with the eligible part-time student undertaking a designated part-time course by reason of a disability to which the eligible part-time student is subject. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), the amount of grant for which an eligible part-time student qualifies under this regulation is the amount that the Welsh Ministers consider appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) The amount of the grant must not exceed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £15,885 in respect of an academic year for expenditure on a non-medical personal helper; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) £5,332 in respect of all the academic years during the period of eligibility for expenditure on major items of specialist equipment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) the additional expenditure incurred— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) within the United Kingdom for the purpose of attending the institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] within or outside the United Kingdom for the purpose of attending, as a part of the eligible part-time student’s course, any period of study at an overseas institution or for the purpose of attending the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] Institute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a];
- (d) £1,338 in respect of an academic year for any other expenditure including expenditure incurred for the purposes referred to in sub-paragraph (a) or (b) which exceeds the specified maxima. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) Subject to paragraphs (5) and (6), a grant for disabled part-time students’ living costs is payable to an eligible part-time student in respect of the four quarters of the academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) Where a grant for disabled part-time students’ living costs is used for expenditure on major items of specialist equipment (within the meaning of paragraph (3)(b)) it may be payable in respect of the whole academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) Where regulation 94(3) applies, an eligible part-time student may only qualify for a grant for disabled part-time students’ living costs for the purposes specified in paragraphs (3)(a), (c) and (d) in respect of such quarters as begin after the relevant event in regulation 94(4) occurs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(7)[^c-569c6f717836bcb63768963cf4cced1d] Subject to paragraph (8), a new eligible part-time student does not qualify for a grant for disabled part-time students’ living costs if the new eligible part-time student is a prisoner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(8) Paragraph (7) does not apply in respect of an academic year during which the new eligible part-time student enters prison or is released from prison. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
