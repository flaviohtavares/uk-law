# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 109

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 109. Part-time grants for dependants - application of contribution (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f978679222e82f249649900b126f789d][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An amount equal to the contribution or the remainder of the contribution, as the case may be, calculated under Schedule 6, is to be applied until it is extinguished against the amount of the particular element of part-time grants for dependants for which the eligible part-time student qualifies as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) first, to reduce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **PTADG *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]**;
- (b) second, to reduce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **PTCCG *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]**;
- (c) third, to reduce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **PTPLA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]**.

(2) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) **PTADG *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]** is the amount, if any, of the part-time adult dependants’ grant calculated in accordance with regulation 106; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) **PTCCG *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]** is the amount, if any, of the part-time childcare grant calculated in accordance with regulation 106; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) **PTPLA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]** is the amount, if any, of the part-time parents’ learning allowance calculated in accordance with regulation 106 (except the first £50 of the allowance). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
