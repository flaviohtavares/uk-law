# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 99

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 99. New part-time course grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2f74e7ac07dc508e3116c2f44dd34733][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible part-time student who is a new eligible part-time student qualifies in accordance with this regulation for a new part-time course grant for books, travel and other expenditure in connection with the student’s attendance on, or undertaking of a designated part-time course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) A new eligible part-time student does not qualify for a new part-time course grant in relation to an academic year of a designated part-time course if the intensity of study during that year is less than 50 per cent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (2) the intensity of study during an academic year of a designated part-time course is to be calculated in accordance with regulation 97(2) and (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) The maximum amount of new part-time course grant for the purposes of paragraph (5) is £1,155. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) The amount of new part-time course grant payable to a new eligible part-time student in relation to an academic year of a designated part-time course is calculated as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the maximum amount of new part-time course grant is payable where at the date of the application for the grant, a new eligible part-time student or the new eligible part-time student’s partner is entitled— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) under Part VII of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 to income support, housing benefit or council tax benefit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] under Part 1 of the Jobseekers Act 1995 to income-based jobseekers allowance or under section 2 of the Employment and Training Act 1973 to an allowance under the arrangements known as the New Deal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] under Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2007 to an income-related employment and support allowance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] to universal credit; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (v) to a reduction under a council tax reduction scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the maximum amount of new part-time course grant is payable where the relevant income is less than £26,095; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) where the relevant income is £26,095 or more but less than £28,180 the amount of new part-time course grant payable is the amount left after deducting from the maximum amount of new part-time course grant £1 for every £1.886 by which the relevant income exceeds £26,095; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) a new part-time course grant of £50 is payable where the relevant income is £28,180; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (e) a new part-time course grant is not available where the relevant income exceeds £28,180. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) For the purposes of this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) “child” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*plentyn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) in relation to a new eligible part-time student includes any child of the new eligible part-time student’s partner and any child for whom the new eligible part-time student has parental responsibility; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) “current financial year” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*y flwyddyn ariannol gyfredol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means the financial year which includes the first day of the academic year in respect of which a person is being assessed for a new part-time course grant under this regulation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) “dependent” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*dibynnol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means wholly or mainly financially dependent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) “financial year” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*blwyddyn ariannol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means the period of twelve months for which the income of the new eligible part-time student is computed for the purposes of the income tax legislation which applies to it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (e) “income” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*incwm *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means gross income from all sources excluding— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) any payment made under section 23C(5A) of the Children Act 1989; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] any tax credits awarded pursuant to any claims under section 3 of the Tax Credits Act 2002; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (f) subject to sub-paragraph (g), “partner” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*partner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means any of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) the spouse of a new eligible part-time student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] the civil partner of a new eligible part-time student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] a person ordinarily living with a new eligible part-time student as if the person were the new eligible part-time student’s spouse or civil partner; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (g) a person who would otherwise be a partner under sub-paragraph (f) is not treated as a partner if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers, that person and the new eligible part-time student are separated; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] the person is ordinarily living outside the United Kingdom and is not maintained by the new eligible part-time student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (h) “preceding financial year” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*blwyddyn ariannol flaenorol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means the financial year immediately preceding the current financial year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (i) “relevant income” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*incwm perthnasol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) has the meaning given in paragraph (7). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(7) Subject to paragraph (8), a new eligible part-time student’s relevant income is equal to the new eligible part-time student’s financial resources in the preceding financial year less— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) £2,000 in respect of the new eligible part-time student’s partner; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) £2,000 in respect of the only or eldest child who is dependent on the new eligible part-time student or the new eligible part-time student’s partner; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) £1,000 in respect of each other child who is dependent on the new eligible part-time student or the new eligible part-time student’s partner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(8) Where the Welsh Ministers are satisfied that a new eligible part-time student’s financial resources in the preceding financial year are greater than the new eligible part-time student’s financial resources in the current financial year and that the difference between the two amounts is £1,000 or more, they must assess that student’s resources by reference to those resources in the current financial year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(9) In this regulation a new eligible part-time student’s financial resources in a financial year means the aggregate of the new eligible part-time student’s income for that year together with the aggregate of the income for that year of any person who at the date of the application for a new part-time course grant is the new eligible part-time student’s partner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(10)[^c-eea48b83a10d5f95189b0672b8e89ec9] Subject to paragraph (11), a new eligible part-time student does not qualify for a new part-time course grant if the new eligible part-time student is a prisoner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(11) Paragraph (10) does not apply in respect of an academic year during which the new eligible part-time student enters prison or is released from prison. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
