# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 36

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. Interpretation of regulations 37 to 39 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0cc6f56e1d15a44fad6e09345ff6dfef][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of regulations 37 to 39— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) any reference to expenditure incurred for the purpose of attending an institution or period of study or period of overseas work placement in an Erasmus year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) includes expenditure both before and after so attending; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] does not include any expenditure in respect of which a grant is payable under regulation 29; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) “qualifying quarter” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*chwarter cymhwysol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means a quarter during which the eligible student attends as part of the eligible student’s course an overseas institution, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] Institute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] or overseas work placement in an Erasmus year for at least half the period covered by that quarter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
