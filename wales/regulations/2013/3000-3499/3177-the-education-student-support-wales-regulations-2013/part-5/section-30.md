# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 30

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30. Grants for dependants - general (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4bc4011aa836c50bc8ec21caa907731f][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The grants for dependants consist of the following elements— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) adult dependants’ grant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) childcare grant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) parents’ learning allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The qualifying conditions for each element and the amounts payable are set out in regulations 31 to 34. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) A deduction may be made from any element of the grants for dependants in accordance with regulation 67. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
