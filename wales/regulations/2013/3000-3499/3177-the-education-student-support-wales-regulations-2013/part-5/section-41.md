# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 41

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Maintenance grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7b885c228d7599f0a12c5109e9b3f59b][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A new system eligible student who is not a new cohort student qualifies in accordance with regulation 42 for a maintenance grant for living costs in connection with that eligible student’s attendance on a designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) A new system eligible student who is a 2010 cohort student or a 2012 cohort student qualifies in accordance with regulation 43 for a maintenance grant for living costs in connection with that eligible student’s attendance on a designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) A new system eligible student who is a 2011 cohort student qualifies in accordance with regulation 44 for a maintenance grant for living costs in connection with that eligible student’s attendance on a designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) A new system eligible student does not qualify for a maintenance grant if that eligible student qualifies for a special support grant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) Subject to paragraph (6), a maintenance grant for living costs is payable to a new system eligible student in respect of the four quarters of the academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) Where one of the events listed in regulation 28(13) occurs in the course of an academic year, a new system eligible student may only qualify for a maintenance grant for living costs in respect of such quarters as begin after the relevant event occurs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
