# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 38

> Text as at 2017-02-17.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 38. Amount of the grant for travel (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-39d13021419a49b17572713196dde6c9][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The amount of grant payable under regulation 37(1) in respect of an academic year is equal to the reasonable expenditure that the Welsh Ministers determine the eligible student is obliged to incur for the purposes set out in that regulation less £303. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The amount of grant payable under regulation 37(2) in respect of an academic year is calculated as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2013/3177) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- *X* is the aggregate of the reasonable travel costs that the eligible student is obliged to incur in each qualifying quarter for the purposes set out in regulation 37. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- *Y* is the aggregate of the expenditure incurred in each qualifying quarter specified in paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) The expenditure specified in paragraph (2) is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) expenditure that the eligible student reasonably incurs in insuring against liability for the cost of medical treatment provided outside the United Kingdom for any illness or personal injury contracted or suffered during the period the eligible student is attending the overseas institution, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] Institute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] or overseas work placement in an Erasmus year (“the placement” in this paragraph); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the cost of a visa or visas that the eligible student is obliged to obtain in order to attend the overseas institution, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] Institute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] or placement; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) medical costs that the eligible student reasonably incurs in order to fulfil a mandatory condition of entry into the territory, country or state in which the overseas institution, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] Institute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] or placement is situated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
