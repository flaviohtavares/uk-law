# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 40

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Higher education grants (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-bdfd7b6301c33f7a7d0b91ab7c302f19][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An old system eligible student qualifies in accordance with this regulation for a higher education grant in connection with the old system eligible student’s attendance on a designated course to defray the cost of books, equipment, travel or childcare incurred for the purpose of attending that course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) An old system eligible student does not qualify for a higher education grant unless the old system eligible student began the specified designated course on or after 1 September 2004. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) The maximum amount of higher education grant available in respect of an academic year is £1,000. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) An old system eligible student who qualifies for a higher education grant is entitled to receive an amount as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) in any case where the household income is £16,765 or less, the old system eligible student is entitled to receive the maximum amount of grant available; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) in any case where the household income exceeds £16,765 and does not exceed £22,750, the old system eligible student receives an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **M – A**, where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **M** is £1,000 and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **A** is £1 for every £6.30 by which the household income exceeds £16,765; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) in any case where the household income exceeds £22,750, no grant is payable under this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
