# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 46

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. Special support grant – new system eligible students who are not new cohort students (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-461717310771c18507f84cb1bbf0ef92][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The maximum amount of special support grant available to a new system eligible student who is not a new cohort student in respect of an academic year is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) in the case of a type 1 teacher training student, £1,500; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) in the case of a type 2 teacher training student, £3,000; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) in the case of a type 3 teacher training student, £1,500; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) in the case of a new system eligible student other than a type 1, type 2 or type 3 teacher training student, £3,000. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) A type 1 teacher training student who qualifies for special support grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) where the household income is £18,370 or less, the eligible student receives £1,500; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) where the household income exceeds £18,370 but does not exceed £27,852, the eligible student receives an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **M - (A/2)** where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **M** is £1,500 and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **A** is £1 for every £5.674 by which the household income exceeds £18,370; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) where the household income exceeds £27,852, or the student opts when applying for grant not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income, the eligible student receives £664. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) A type 2 teacher training student who qualifies for special support grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) where the household income is £18,370 or less, the eligible student receives £3,000; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) where the household income exceeds £18,370 but does not exceed £27,852, the eligible student receives an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **M - A** where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **M** is £3,000 and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **A** is £1 for every £5.674 by which the household income exceeds £18,370; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) where the household income exceeds £27,852, or the student opts when applying for the grant not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income the eligible student receives £1,329. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) A type 3 teacher training student who qualifies for special support grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) where the household income is £18,370 or less, the eligible student receives £1,500; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) where the household income exceeds £18,370 but does not exceed £27,852, the eligible student receives an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **M - (A/2)** where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **M** is £1,500 and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **A** is £1 for every £5.674 by which the household income exceeds £18,370; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) where the household income exceeds £27,852 but does not exceed £39,329, the eligible student receives an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **RM - (A/2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]**, where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **RM *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]** is £664 and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **A** is £1 for every £8.97 by which the household income exceeds £27,852; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) where the household income exceeds £39,329, no special support grant is payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) A new system eligible student other than a type 1, type 2 or type 3 teacher training student who qualifies for a special support grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) where the household income is £18,370 or less, the eligible student receives £3,000; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) where the household income exceeds £18,370 but does not exceed £27,852, the eligible student receives an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **M - A** where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **M** is £3,000 and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **A** is £1 for every £5.674 by which the household income exceeds £18,370; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) where the household income exceeds £27,852 but does not exceed £39,329, the eligible student receives an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **RM - A *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]**, where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **RM *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]** is £1,329 and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] **A** is £1 for every £8.97 by which the household income exceeds £27,852; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) where the household income exceeds £39,329, no special support grant is payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
