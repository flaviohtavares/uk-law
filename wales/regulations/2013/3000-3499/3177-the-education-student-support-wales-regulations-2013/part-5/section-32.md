# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 32

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 32. Grants for dependants - childcare grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d47195ed11c0fd60848c9d57f663cabf][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible student qualifies, in connection with the eligible student’s attendance on a designated course, for a childcare grant in accordance with this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) Subject to paragraphs (3) and (4), the childcare grant is available in respect of an academic year in which the eligible student incurs prescribed childcare charges for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) a dependent child who is under the age of 15 immediately before the beginning of the academic year, including a dependent child who is born after the beginning of the academic year; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) a dependent child who has special educational needs within the meaning of section 312 of the Education Act 1996 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00056] and is under the age of 17 immediately before the beginning of the academic year, including a dependent child who is born after the beginning of the academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) An eligible student does not qualify for a grant under this regulation if the eligible student or the eligible student’s partner has elected to receive the childcare element of the working tax credit under Part I of the Tax Credits Act 2002 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00057] or is entitled to an award of universal credit which includes an amount under regulation 31 of the Universal Credit Regulations 2013 (childcare costs element) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00058].

(4) An eligible student does not qualify for a grant under this regulation if the prescribed childcare charges that the eligible student incurs are paid or to be paid by the student to the eligible student’s partner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) Subject to paragraphs (6), (7) and (8) the basic amount of childcare grant for each week is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) for one dependent child, 85 per cent of the prescribed childcare charges, subject to a maximum amount of £161.50 per week; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) for two or more dependent children, 85 per cent of the prescribed childcare charges, subject to a maximum amount of £ 274.55 per week, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

except that the eligible student does not qualify for any such grant in respect of each week falling within the period between the end of the course and the end of the academic year in which the course ends. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) For the purposes of calculating the basic amount of childcare grant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) a week runs from Monday to Sunday; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) where a week in respect of which prescribed childcare charges are incurred falls partly within and partly outside the academic year in respect of which childcare grant is payable under this regulation, the maximum weekly amount of grant is calculated by multiplying the relevant maximum weekly amount in paragraph (5) by the number of days of that week falling within the academic year and dividing the product by seven. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(7) Where an eligible student’s application for a childcare grant does not identify a childcare provider, the Welsh Ministers may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) limit the amount of childcare grant paid to the student to 85 per cent of the prescribed childcare charges up to a maximum amount of £115 per week; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) limit the payment of the childcare grant to one quarter of the academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(8) Subject to paragraph (9), a childcare grant is payable to an eligible student in respect of the four quarters of the academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(9) Where one of the events listed in regulation 28(13) occurs in the course of an academic year, an eligible student may only qualify for a childcare grant in respect of such quarters as begin after the relevant event occurs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
