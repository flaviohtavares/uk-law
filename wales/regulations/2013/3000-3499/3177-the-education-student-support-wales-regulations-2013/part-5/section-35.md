(1) In regulations 31 to 34— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) subject to paragraph (5), “adult dependant” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*dibynnydd mewn oed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means, in relation to an eligible student, an adult person dependent on the eligible student other than the eligible student’s child, the eligible student’s partner (including a spouse or civil partner from whom the Welsh Ministers consider the eligible student is separated) or the eligible student’s former partner; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) “child” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*plentyn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) in relation to an eligible student includes any child of the eligible student’s partner who is dependent on the eligible student and any child for whom the eligible student has parental responsibility who is dependent on the eligible student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) “dependant” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*dibynnydd *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means, in relation to an eligible student, the eligible student’s partner, the eligible student’s dependent child or an adult dependant, who in each case is not an eligible student and does not hold a statutory award; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) “dependent” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*dibynnol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means wholly or mainly financially dependent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (e) “dependent child” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*plentyn dibynnol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means, in relation to an eligible student, a child dependent on the eligible student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (f) “financial year” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*blwyddyn ariannol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means the period of twelve months in respect of which the income of a dependant (whose income is calculated under regulations 31 to 34) is computed for the purposes of the income tax legislation which applies to it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (g) “lone parent” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*rhiant unigol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means an eligible student who does not have a partner and who has a dependent child; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (h) “Member State” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*Aelod-wladwriaeth *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means a Member State of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a];
- (i) “net income” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*incwm net *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) has the meaning given in paragraph (7); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (j) subject to sub-paragraphs (p), (q), (r) and paragraphs (3) and (4), “partner” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*partner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means any of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) the spouse of an eligible student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] the civil partner of an eligible student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] a person ordinarily living with an eligible student as if the person were the eligible student’s spouse where an eligible student falls within paragraph 2(1)(a) of Schedule 5 and began the designated course on or after 1 September 2000; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] a person ordinarily living with an eligible student as if the person were the eligible student’s civil partner where an eligible student falls within paragraph 2(1)(a) of Schedule 5 and began the designated course on or after 1 September 2005; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (k) “preceding financial year” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*blwyddyn ariannol flaenorol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means the financial year immediately preceding the relevant year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (l) “prior financial year” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*blwyddyn ariannol gynharach *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means the financial year immediately preceding the preceding financial year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (m) “relevant year” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*blwyddyn berthnasol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means the academic year of the present course in respect of which the eligible student’s dependant’s income falls to be assessed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (n) “residual income” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*incwm gweddilliol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means taxable income after the application of paragraph (10) (in the case of an eligible student’s partner) or paragraph (11) (in the case of an eligible student’s adult dependant); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (o) “taxable income” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*incwm trethadwy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means, in respect of the prior financial year, a person’s taxable income from all sources computed for the purposes of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) the Income Tax Acts; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] the income tax legislation of another Member State which applies to the person’s income; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] where the legislation of more than one Member State applies to the period, the legislation under which the Welsh Ministers consider the person will pay the largest amount of tax in that period, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

  except that no account is taken of the income referred to in paragraph (2) which is paid to another party; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (p) unless otherwise indicated, a person who would otherwise be a partner under sub-paragraph (j) is not treated as a partner if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers, that person and the eligible student are separated; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] the person is ordinarily living outside the United Kingdom and is not maintained by the eligible student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (q) for the purposes of the definition of “adult dependant” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*dibynnydd mewn oed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”), a person is to be treated as a partner if the person would be a partner under sub-paragraph (j) but for the fact that the eligible student with whom the person is ordinarily living does not fall within paragraph 2(1)(a) of Schedule 5; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (r) for the purposes of the definitions of “child” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*plentyn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) and “lone parent” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*rhiant unigol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”), a person is to be treated as a partner if the person would be a partner under sub-paragraph (j) but for the date on which the eligible student began the specified designated course or the fact that the eligible student with whom the person is ordinarily living does not fall within paragraph 2(1)(a) of Schedule 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The income referred to in this paragraph is any benefits under a pension arrangement pursuant to an order made under section 23 of the Matrimonial Causes Act 1973 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00059] which includes provision made by virtue of sections 25B(4) and 25E(3) of that Act or pension benefits under Part 1 of Schedule 5 to the Civil Partnership Act 2004 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00060] which includes provision made by virtue of Parts 6 and 7 of that Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) For the purposes of regulation 33— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) paragraph (1)(p) does not apply; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) a person is to be treated as a partner if the person would be a partner under paragraph (1)(j) but for the fact that the eligible student with whom the person is ordinarily living does not fall within paragraph 2(1)(a) of Schedule 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) For the purposes of determining whether a person is the former partner of an eligible student’s partner, “partner” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*partner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) in relation to an eligible student’s partner means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the spouse of an eligible student’s partner; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the civil partner of an eligible student’s partner: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) where the eligible student began the specified designated course on or after 1 September 2000, a person (“A”) ordinarily living with an eligible student’s partner (“B”) as if A were B’s spouse; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) where the eligible student began the specified designated course on or after 1 September 2005, a person (“A”) ordinarily living with an eligible student’s partner (“B”) as if A were B’s civil partner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) Subject to paragraph (6), for the purposes of the definitions of “adult dependant” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*dibynnydd mewn oed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) and “dependent child” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*plentyn dibynnol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”), the Welsh Ministers may treat an adult person or child as dependent on an eligible student if they are satisfied that the adult person or child— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) is not dependent on only— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) the eligible student; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] the eligible student’s partner; but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) is dependent on the eligible student and the eligible student’s partner together. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) The Welsh Ministers must not treat an adult person (“A”) as dependent on an eligible student in accordance with paragraph (5), if A is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the spouse or civil partner of the eligible student’s partner (including a spouse or civil partner from whom the Welsh Ministers consider the eligible student’s partner is separated); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the former partner of the eligible student’s partner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(7) A dependant’s net income is the dependant’s income from all sources (for the relevant year for the purposes of regulation 31(2)(b) and for the prior financial year for the purposes of regulation 34(2)) reduced by the amount of income tax and social security contributions payable in respect of it but disregarding— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) any pension, allowance or other benefit paid by reason of a disability or incapacity to which the dependant is subject; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) child benefit payable under Part IX of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00061];
- (c) any financial support payable to the dependant by a local authority in accordance with regulations made under sections 2, 3 and 4 of the Adoption and Children Act 2002 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00062];
- (d) any guardian’s allowance to which the dependant is entitled under section 77 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (e) in the case of a dependant with whom a child being looked after by a local authority is boarded out, any payment made to that dependant in pursuance of section 23 of the Children Act 1989 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00063];
- (f) any payment made to the dependant under section 23C(5A) of the Children Act 1989 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00064];
- (g) any payments made to the dependant under section 15 of and Schedule 1 to the Children Act 1989 in respect of a person who is not the dependant’s child or any assistance given by a local authority pursuant to section 24 of that Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00065];
- (h) any child tax credit to which the dependant is entitled under Part I of the Tax Credits Act 2002 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00066]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (i) in the case of a dependant who is entitled to an award of universal credit— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) any amount that is included in the calculation of the award under regulation 27(1) of the Universal Credit Regulations 2013, in respect of the fact that the dependant has limited capability for work or limited capability for work and work related activity; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] any amount or additional amount that is included in the calculation of the award under regulation 24 of those Regulations (the child element). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(8) Where an eligible student or the eligible student’s partner makes any recurrent payments which were previously made by the eligible student in pursuance of an obligation incurred before the first academic year of the eligible student’s course, the eligible student’s partner’s residual income is reduced by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) an amount equal to the payments in question for the academic year, if in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers, the obligation had been reasonably incurred; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(9) For the purposes of paragraph (7), where the dependant is a dependent child and payments are made to the eligible student towards the dependent child’s maintenance, those payments are to be treated as the dependent child’s income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(10) An eligible student’s partner’s residual income is determined in accordance with paragraph 6 of Schedule 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(11) An eligible student’s adult dependant’s residual income is determined in accordance with paragraph 5 of Schedule 5 (other than sub-paragraphs (9), (10), or (11) of paragraph 5) with references to the parent being construed as references to the eligible student’s adult dependant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
