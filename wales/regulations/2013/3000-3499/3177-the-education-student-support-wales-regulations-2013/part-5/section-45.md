# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 45

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45. Special support grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-af22dd74412490dba71ea7e903556914][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A new system eligible student who is not a new cohort student qualifies in accordance with regulation 46 for a special support grant in connection with that eligible student’s attendance on a designated course to defray the cost of books, equipment, travel or childcare incurred for the purpose of attending that course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) A new system eligible student who is a 2010 cohort student or a 2012 cohort student qualifies in accordance with regulation 47 for a special support grant in connection with that eligible student’s attendance on a designated course to defray the cost of books, equipment, travel or childcare incurred for the purpose of attending that course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) A new system eligible student who is a 2011 cohort student qualifies in accordance with regulation 48 for a special support grant in connection with that student’s attendance on a designated course to defray the cost of books, equipment, travel or childcare incurred for the purpose of attending that course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) A new system eligible student qualifies for a special support grant if that eligible student— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) falls within a prescribed category of person for the purposes of section 124(1)(e) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00067];
- (b) is treated as being liable to make payments in respect of a dwelling prescribed by regulations made under section 130(2) of that Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00068]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) is liable, or treated as being liable to make payments in respect of the accommodation they occupy as their home under regulation 25(3) of the Universal Credit Regulations 2013. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) Subject to paragraph (6), a special support grant is payable to a new system eligible student in respect of the four quarters of the academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) Where one of the events listed in regulation 28(13) occurs in the course of an academic year, a new system eligible student may only qualify for a special support grant in respect of such quarters as begin after the relevant event occurs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
