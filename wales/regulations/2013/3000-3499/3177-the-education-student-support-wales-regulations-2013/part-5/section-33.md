# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 33

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Grants for dependants - parents’ learning allowance (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-276df7d94471c45305f21a5706914cd1][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible student qualifies in connection with the student’s attendance on a designated course for the parents’ learning allowance if the student has one or more dependants who are dependent children. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The amount of parents’ learning allowance payable in respect of an academic year is calculated in accordance with regulation 34, the basic amount being £1,557. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
