# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 124

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 124. Designated postgraduate courses (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c61dfcb25914f3969e1682e5c541f79c][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A postgraduate course is designated for the purposes of section 22(1) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] and regulation 122 if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) it is a course entry for which a first degree (or equivalent qualification) or higher is normally required; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) it is a course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) of at least one academic year’s duration; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] in the case of a part-time course which began before 1 September 2014, it is ordinarily possible to complete the course in not more than twice the period ordinarily required to complete the full time equivalent; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] in the case of a part-time course which begins on or after 1 September 2014, it is ordinarily possible to complete the course in not more than four times the period ordinarily required to complete the full time equivalent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) it is wholly provided by a publicly funded educational institution in the United Kingdom or is provided by such an institution conjunction with an institution outside the United Kingdom; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) it is not a course for the initial training of teachers or a course taken as part of an employment based teacher training scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) a course is provided by an institution if it provides the teaching and supervision which comprise the course, whether or not it has entered an agreement with the student to provide the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) a university and any constituent college or institution in the nature of a college of a university is regarded as publicly funded if either the university or the constituent college or institution is publicly funded; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) an institution is not regarded as publicly funded by reason only that it receives public funds from the governing body of a higher education institution in accordance with section 65(3A) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^f00088].

(3) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b)(ii)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) “full-time equivalent” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*cwrs llawnamser cyfatebol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means a full-time course leading to the same qualification as the part-time course in question; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) “period ordinarily required to complete the full-time equivalent” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*cyfnod y mae ei angen fel arfer i gwblhau’r cwrs llawnamser cyfatebol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means the period in which a standard full-time student would complete the full-time equivalent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) “standard full-time student” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*myfyriwr llawnamser safonol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means a student who is to be taken— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) to have started the full-time equivalent on the same date as the eligible part-time student started the part-time course in question; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] not to have been excused any part of the full-time equivalent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] not to have repeated any part of the full-time equivalent; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] not to have been absent from the full-time equivalent other than during vacations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) For the purposes of section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] and regulation 122, the Welsh Ministers may designate courses of higher education which are not designated under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
