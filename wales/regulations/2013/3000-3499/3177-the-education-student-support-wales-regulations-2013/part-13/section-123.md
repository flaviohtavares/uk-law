# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 123

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 123. Students becoming eligible during the course of the academic year (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-f791d98a9f89355af07ac86169eb86ff][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where one of the events listed in paragraph (2) occurs in the course of an academic year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) a student may qualify for a grant under this Part in respect of that academic year in accordance with this Part; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) a grant of the kind available under this Part is not available in respect of any academic year beginning before the academic year in which the relevant event occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The events are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the student’s course becomes a designated postgraduate course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the student, or the student’s spouse, civil partner or parent (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) is recognised as a refugee or becomes a person with leave to enter or remain; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) the state of which the student is a national accedes to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] where the student has been ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] Islands *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] throughout the three-year period immediately preceding the first day of the first academic year of the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) the student acquires the right of permanent residence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (e) the student becomes a child of a Turkish worker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (f) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 6(1)(a) of Part 2 of Schedule 1; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (g) the student becomes the child of a Swiss national. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
