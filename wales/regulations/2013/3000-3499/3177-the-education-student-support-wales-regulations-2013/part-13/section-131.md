# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 131

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 131. Overpayments (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1c0e2cb3c524540d895a25351d131e21][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible postgraduate student must, if so required by the Welsh Ministers, repay any amount paid to the eligible postgraduate student under this Part which for whatever reason exceeds the amount of grant to which the eligible postgraduate student is entitled under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The Welsh Ministers must recover an overpayment of grant under this Part unless they consider it is not appropriate to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) The methods of recovery are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) subtracting the overpayment from any kind of grant payable to the eligible postgraduate student from time to time pursuant to regulations made by the Welsh Ministers under section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a];
- (b) taking such other action for the recovery of an overpayment as is available to them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) A payment of grant under this Part made before the relevant date is an overpayment if the eligible postgraduate student withdraws from the course before the relevant date unless the Welsh Ministers decide otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) In this regulation, the “relevant date” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*dyddiad perthnasol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) is the date on which the first term of the academic year in question actually begins. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) In either of the circumstances in paragraphs (7) and (8), there is an overpayment of grant under this Part unless the Welsh Ministers decide otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(7) The circumstances referred to in paragraph (6) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the Welsh Ministers apply all or part of the grant under this Part to the purchase of specialist equipment on behalf of the eligible postgraduate student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the student’s period of eligibility terminates after the relevant date; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) the equipment has not been delivered to the student before the student’s period of eligibility terminates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(8) The circumstances referred to in paragraph (6) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the eligible postgraduate student’s period of eligibility terminates; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) a payment of grant under this Part in respect of specialist equipment is made to the student after the student’s period of eligibility terminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(9) Where there is an overpayment of the grant under this Part, the Welsh Ministers may accept the return of specialist equipment purchased with the grant by way of recovery of all or part of the overpayment if they consider it is appropriate to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
