# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 130

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 130. Payment of grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-721cd8b5e7d6899fccdaa08fe4c0b0de][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may pay a grant for which an eligible postgraduate student qualifies under this Part in such instalments (if any) and at such times as they consider appropriate and in the exercise of their functions under this Part they may make provisional payments pending the final calculation of the amount of grant for which the student qualifies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) Payments may be made in such manner as the Welsh Ministers consider appropriate and they may make it a condition of entitlement to payment that the eligible postgraduate student must provide them with particulars of a bank or building society account in the United Kingdom into which payments may be made by electronic transfer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
