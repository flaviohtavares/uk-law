# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 129

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 129. Amount of grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-696dd420bb3d270a269de87041fcab82][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the grant payable to an eligible postgraduate student under this Part is such amount as the Welsh Ministers consider appropriate to assist with one or more types of eligible expenditure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The grant must not exceed £10,590 in respect of an academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) For the purposes of this Part, the “types of eligible expenditure” are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) expenditure on a non-medical helper; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) expenditure on major items of specialist equipment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) additional expenditure incurred— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) within the United Kingdom for the purpose of attending the institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] within or outside the United Kingdom for the purpose of attending, as part of the course, any period of study at an overseas institution or for the purposes of attending the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] Institute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a].

(4) Subject to paragraphs (5) and (6), a grant under this Part is payable to an eligible postgraduate student in respect of the four quarters of the academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) Where a grant under this Part is used for expenditure on major items of specialist equipment (within the meaning of paragraph (3)(b)) it may be payable in respect of the whole academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) Where one of the events listed in regulation 123(2) occurs in the course of an academic year, an eligible postgraduate student may only qualify for a grant under this Part for the purposes specified in paragraph (3)(a) and (c) in respect of such quarters as begin after the relevant event occurs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
