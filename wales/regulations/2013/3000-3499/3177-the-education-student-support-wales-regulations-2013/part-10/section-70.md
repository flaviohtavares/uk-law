# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 70

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 70. Payment of grants for living costs (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b5c58764d2c7f02d1da66aac24becd1e][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to the following paragraphs, the Welsh Ministers may pay support under Part 5 in such instalments (if any) and at such times as they consider appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) An academic authority is required to send an attendance confirmation to the Welsh Ministers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) The Welsh Ministers must not pay the first instalment or, where it has been determined not to pay support under Part 5 by instalments, make any payment of support under that Part to an eligible student before they have received an attendance confirmation unless an exception referred to in paragraph (4) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) For the purposes of paragraph (3), an exception applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) a grant for disabled students’ living costs is payable in which case that particular grant may be paid before the Welsh Ministers have received an attendance confirmation; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the Welsh Ministers have determined that owing to exceptional circumstances it would be appropriate to make a payment without receiving an attendance confirmation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) Where a final assessment cannot be made on the basis of the information provided by the eligible student, the Welsh Ministers may make a provisional assessment and payment of support under Part 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) Payments of support under Part 5 are to be made in such manner as the Welsh Ministers consider appropriate and they may make it a condition of entitlement to payment that the eligible student must provide them with particulars of a bank or building society account in the United Kingdom into which payments may be made by electronic transfer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(7) Subject to paragraph (8), no support under Part 5 is payable in respect of any day of an academic year on which the eligible student is a prisoner, unless in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers it would be appropriate in all the circumstances for support to be paid in respect of that day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(8) Paragraph (7) does not apply in respect of grants for disabled students’ living costs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(9) In deciding whether support is payable under paragraph (7) the circumstances to which the Welsh Ministers must have regard include the financial hardship not paying the support would cause and whether not paying the support would affect the eligible student’s ability to continue the course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(10) No support under Part 5 is payable in respect of any payment period beginning after an eligible student’s period of eligibility terminates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(11) Where an eligible student’s period of eligibility terminates on or after the relevant date, the Welsh Ministers must determine— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the amount of each grant for living costs for which that student qualifies that would be payable in respect of the relevant payment period if that student’s period of eligibility had not terminated (the “full amount”); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) how much of the full amount is payable in respect of the period which runs from the first day of the relevant payment period up to and including the day on which the eligible student’s period of eligibility terminated (the “partial amount”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(12) In this regulation, the “relevant date” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*y dyddiad perthnasol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) is the date on which the first term of the academic year in question actually begins. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(13) If the Welsh Ministers have made a payment of grant for living costs in respect of the relevant payment period before the point in that period at which the eligible student’s period of eligibility terminated and that payment exceeds the partial amount of that grant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) they may treat the excess as an overpayment of that grant; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) if they consider that it is appropriate to do so they may extend that student’s period of eligibility in respect of that grant until the end of the relevant payment period and determine that the full amount of the grant is payable in respect of that payment period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(14) Subject to paragraph (15), if a payment of a grant for living costs in respect of the relevant payment period is due to be made or is made after the eligible student’s period of eligibility has terminated, the amount of that grant payable is the partial amount unless the Welsh Ministers consider it appropriate to extend the period of eligibility in respect of that grant until the end of the relevant payment period and to determine that the full amount of that grant is payable in respect of that payment period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(15) Paragraph (14) does not apply to a payment of grant for disabled students’ living costs in respect of specialist equipment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(16) No support under Part 5 is payable in respect of a payment period during any part of which an eligible student is absent from the eligible student’s course, unless in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers it would be appropriate in all the circumstances for support to be paid in respect of the period of absence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(17) In deciding whether it would be appropriate for support to be payable under paragraph (16) the circumstances to which the Welsh Ministers must have regard include the reason for the student’s absence, the length of the absence and the financial hardship which not paying the support would cause. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(18) An eligible student is not to be considered absent from the eligible student’s course if the eligible student is unable to attend due to illness and the eligible student’s absence has not exceeded 60 days. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(19) Where, after the Welsh Ministers have made any payment of support under Part 5 or Part 6, they make a determination of the amount of a grant for living costs for which the eligible student qualifies either for the first time or by way of revision of a provisional or other determination of that amount— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) if the determination increases the amount of that grant for which the eligible student qualifies they must pay the additional amount and may do so in such instalments (if any) and at such times as they consider appropriate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) if the determination decreases the amount of that grant for which the eligible student qualifies they must subtract the amount of the decrease from the amount of that grant which remains to be paid; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) if the amount of the decrease is greater than the amount of that grant remaining to be paid the latter amount is reduced to nil and the balance subtracted from any other grant for living costs for which the student qualifies in respect of the academic year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) any remaining overpayment is recoverable in accordance with regulation 74. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
