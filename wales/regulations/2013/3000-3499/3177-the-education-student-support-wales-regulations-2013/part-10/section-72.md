# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 72

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 72. Information requirements (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-584bac6b0acaedb914083febd7dca7b2][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may at any time request from an applicant or an eligible student information that they consider is required to recover a loan. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The Welsh Ministers may at any time request from an applicant or an eligible student sight of their valid national identity card, valid passport issued by the state of which they are a national or their birth certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) Where the Welsh Ministers have requested information under this regulation, they may withhold any payment of a loan until the applicant or eligible student provides what has been requested or provides a satisfactory explanation for not complying with the request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) The Welsh Ministers may at any time require an applicant or an eligible student to enter into an agreement to repay a loan by a particular method. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) Where the Welsh Ministers have requested an agreement as to the method of repayment under this regulation, the Welsh Ministers may withhold any payment of a loan until the applicant or eligible student provides what has been requested. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
