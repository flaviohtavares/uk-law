# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 49

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Qualifying conditions for loans for living costs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ef5100d54acf1302fd16943a4df2ee5b][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible student qualifies for a loan for living costs in connection with the eligible student’s attendance on a designated course if the eligible student satisfies the condition in paragraph (2) and is not excluded by paragraph (3) or regulation 7. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The condition is that the eligible student is under the age of 60 on the relevant date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) An eligible student does not qualify for a loan for living costs if the only paragraph in Part 2 of Schedule 1 into which the student falls is paragraph 9. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) An eligible student does not qualify for a loan for living costs in connection with the eligible student’s attendance on a designated course if that course is a flexible postgraduate ITT course which is of less than one academic year’s duration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(5) An eligible student does not qualify for a loan for living costs in connection with the eligible student’s attendance on a designated course if that course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) begins on or after 1 September 2009; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) leads to qualification as a landscape architect, landscape designer, landscape manager, town planner or town and country planner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(6) An old system eligible student who falls within paragraph (a) or (d)(i) of the definition of “old system eligible student” in regulation 2 qualifies for a loan for living costs in connection with the old system eligible student’s attendance on a designated course if the old system eligible student satisfies the condition in paragraph (2) and is not excluded by paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(7) An eligible student to whom this paragraph applies is treated as being in attendance on the designated course for the purpose of qualifying for a loan for living costs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(8) Paragraph (7) applies to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) a compressed degree student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) a disabled eligible student who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) is not a compressed degree student; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] is undertaking a designated course in the United Kingdom but is not in attendance because the eligible student is unable to attend for a reason which relates to the eligible student’s disability; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) an eligible student on a period of study or a period of work placement in an Erasmus year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(9) An eligible student does not qualify for a loan for living costs under this Part if the eligible student is a prisoner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(10) An eligible student does not qualify for a loan for living costs under this Part if the eligible student is undertaking a distance learning course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
