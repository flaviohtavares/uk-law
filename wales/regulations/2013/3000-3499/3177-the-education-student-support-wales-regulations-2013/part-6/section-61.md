# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 61

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 61. Increases in maximum amount (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-5ef1f5da099a1949f8f4ab8f1700ff7e][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where an eligible student is required to attend the eligible student’s course for a period exceeding 30 weeks and 3 days in an academic year, the maximum amount of loan for living costs specified in regulations 51 to 55 must be increased for each week or part week of attendance in that academic year beyond 30 weeks and 3 days by, for such a student in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) category 1, £61; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) category 2, £116; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) category 3, £126; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) category 4, £126; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (e) category 5, £91. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) Where an eligible student attends the eligible student’s course for a period of not less than 45 weeks in any continuous period of 52 weeks the amount of loan for living costs specified in regulations 51 to 55 is increased for each week in the 52 week period during which the eligible student did not attend by the amounts referred to in paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) This regulation does not apply in the case of a student with reduced entitlement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
