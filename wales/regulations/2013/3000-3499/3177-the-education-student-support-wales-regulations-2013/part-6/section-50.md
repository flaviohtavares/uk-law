# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 50

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 50. General (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-49e21b59ca0714c06f11467ed6490043][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

The maximum amount of loan for living costs in respect of an academic year is calculated as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) where the eligible student is an old system eligible student with full entitlement, in accordance with regulation 51; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) where the eligible student is a new system eligible student with full entitlement who is not a new cohort student, in accordance with regulations 52 and 53; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) where the eligible student is a new system eligible student with full entitlement who is a 2010 cohort student, a 2012 cohort student or a 2012 accelerated graduate entry student who is undertaking their first year of study, in accordance with regulation 54; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) where the eligible student is a new system eligible student with full entitlement who is a 2011 cohort student, in accordance with regulation 55; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (e) where the eligible student is a student with reduced entitlement, in accordance with regulation 56. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
