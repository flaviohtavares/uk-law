# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 62

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 62. Deductions from loans for living costs (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-623f322307ca3393320789b102042a12][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A deduction from the amount of loan for living costs calculated under this Part in respect of an old system eligible student with full entitlement or a new system eligible student with full entitlement may be made in accordance with regulation 67. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) A deduction from the amount of loan for living costs calculated under this Part in respect of a student with reduced entitlement may not be made under regulation 67. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
