# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 52

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52. Maximum amount of loans for new system eligible students with full entitlement who are not new cohort students (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a13475afd606f11e43b806608df0a26a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies to a new system eligible student with full entitlement who is not a new cohort student (other than a type 1 or type 2 teacher training student whose contribution exceeds nil). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) Subject to regulations 57 to 62, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year other than a final year of a course that is not an intensive course is equal to ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]**X-Y**) where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- **X** is, for such a student in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) category 1, £4,027; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii) category 2, £7,288; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii) category 3, £6,202; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iv) category 4, £6,202; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (v) category 5, £5,202; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- **Y** is the maintenance grant amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) Subject to regulations 57 to 62, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is equal to ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]**X-Y**) where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- **X** is, for such a student in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) category 1, £3,645; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii) category 2, £6,636; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iii) category 3, £5,394; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (iv) category 4, £5,394; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (v) category 5, £4,819; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- **Y** is the maintenance grant amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(4) In this regulation, “the maintenance grant amount” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*swm y grant cynhaliaeth *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) where the new system eligible student referred to in paragraph (1) qualifies under regulation 42 for an amount of maintenance grant not exceeding £1,329, the amount of maintenance grant payable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) where the new system eligible student referred to in paragraph (1) qualifies under regulation 42 for an amount of maintenance grant exceeding £1,329, £1,329; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) where no maintenance grant is payable, nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

**53.**[^key-017f7cacf7bcc59521e2bacbcc983bc8] (1) This regulation applies to a type 1 or type 2 teacher training student whose contribution exceeds nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) Subject to regulations 57 to 62, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year other than the final year of a course that is not an accelerated course is, for such student in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) category 1, £4,027; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) category 2, £7,288; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) category 3, £6,202; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) category 4, £6,202; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (e) category 5, £5,202. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) Subject to regulations 57 to 62, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year of a course that is the final year of a course that is not an accelerated course is, for such a student in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) category 1, £3,645; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) category 2, £6,636; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) category 3, £5,394; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) category 4, £5,394; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (e) category 5, £4,819. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
