# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 58

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Loans for living costs payable in respect of three quarters of the academic year (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-975e5f5ec54d5050b37dda7394a1ba58][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to regulation 60, the loan for living costs is payable in respect of three quarters of the academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The loan for living costs is not payable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) in the case of a compressed degree student, in respect of the quarter nominated by the Welsh Ministers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) in any other case, in respect of the quarter in which, in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers, the longest of any vacation occurs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
