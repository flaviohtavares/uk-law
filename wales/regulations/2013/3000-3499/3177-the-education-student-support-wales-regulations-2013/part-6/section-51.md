# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 51

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 51. Maximum amount of loans for old system eligible students with full entitlement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-fb504e2b340de15566ea392a3ee38b66][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to regulations 57 to 62, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which an old system eligible student with full entitlement qualifies in respect of an academic year other than the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is, for such a student in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) category 1, £3,987; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) category 2, £7,215; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) category 3, £6,140; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) category 4, £6,140; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (e) category 5, £5,150. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) Subject to regulations 57 to 62, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which an old system eligible student with full entitlement qualifies in respect of an academic year which is the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is, for such a student in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) category 1, £3,608; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) category 2, £6,570; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (c) category 3, £5,340; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (d) category 4, £5,340; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (e) category 5, £4,771. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
