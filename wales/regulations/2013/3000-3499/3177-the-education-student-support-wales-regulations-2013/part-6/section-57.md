# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 57

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 57. Students residing with parents (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-69a699a028d3cffe4a0d4d1c9bb9a97b][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), where an eligible student (“A” in this paragraph) resides at A’s parents’ home and the Welsh Ministers are satisfied that in all the circumstances A’s parents by reason of age, incapacity or otherwise cannot reasonably be expected to support A and that it would be appropriate for the amount of loan payable to a student in a category other than category 1 to apply in A’s case, A must be treated as if A were not residing at A’s parents’ home. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to an eligible student who begins a course on or after 1 September 2004. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
