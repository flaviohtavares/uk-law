# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 64

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 64. Additional amount of loans (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-6748726622c997fd1bcca0d48c88e6c4][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible student may apply to borrow an additional amount of loan for living costs where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

- (a) the Welsh Ministers determine that the maximum amount of loan for living costs which has been notified to the eligible student in relation to an academic year should be increased (including an increase from nil) as a result of a reassessment of the eligible student’s contribution or otherwise; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
- (b) the Welsh Ministers consider that the increase in the maximum amount does not result from the eligible student— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (i) failing to provide information promptly which might affect the eligible student’s ability to qualify for a loan or the amount of loan for which the eligible student qualifies; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] providing information which is inaccurate in any material particular. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) The additional amount under paragraph (1) is an amount which when added to the amount already applied for does not exceed the increased maximum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) Where an eligible student has applied for a loan of less than the maximum amount to which the eligible student is entitled in relation to the academic year, the eligible student may apply to borrow an additional amount which, when added to the amount already applied for, does not exceed the relevant maximum applicable in the eligible student’s case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
