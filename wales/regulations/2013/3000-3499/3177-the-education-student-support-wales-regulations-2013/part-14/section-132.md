# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2013, section 132

> Text as at 2017-02-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 132. Amendment of the (affected by repeal, see note) 2012 Regulations (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-5b0c366e9a721637111e5b9baaf1350c][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a][^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] 2012 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a] are amended in accordance with the following paragraphs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(2) In regulation 2(1), in the appropriate place, insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

> - “compressed first year course” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*cwrs blwyddyn gyntaf gywasgedig *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means a designated course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
>   - (a) which begins on or after 1 September 2013; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
>   - (b) where the first year of the course is undertaken on a compressed basis and can be completed in a period of not more than seven months; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
>   - (c) where no other years of the course are undertaken on such a compressed basis. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

(3) In regulation 2(1), for the definition of “academic year”, substitute— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

> - “academic year” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*blwyddyn academaidd *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]*”) means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
>   - (a) in respect of an academic year of a course other than the first year of a compressed first year course, the period of twelve months beginning on 1 January, 1 April, 1 July or 1 September of the calendar year in which the academic year of the course in question begins according to whether that academic year begins on or after 1 January and before 1 April, on or after 1 April and before 1 July, on or after 1 July and before 1 August or on or after 1 August and on or before 31 December respectively; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]
>   - (b) in respect of an academic year which is the first year of a compressed first year course, the period of eight months beginning on 1 January, 1 April, 1 July or 1 September of the calendar year in which the academic year of the course in question begins according to whether that academic year begins on or after 1 January and before 1 April, on or after 1 April and before 1 July, on or after 1 July and before 1 August or on or after 1 August and on or before 31 December respectively; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fb65aa289c1f5f6c86282db81d96956a]

.
