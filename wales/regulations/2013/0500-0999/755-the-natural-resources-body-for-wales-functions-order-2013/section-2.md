# The Natural Resources Body for Wales (Functions) Order 2013, section 2

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W*

In this Order—

- “**the 1990 Act**” (“***Deddf 1990***”) means the Environmental Protection Act 1990 [^c21769891];
- “**the 1995 Act**” (“***Deddf 1995***”) means the Environment Act 1995 [^c21769901];
- “**the Body**” (“***y Corff***”) means the Natural Resources Body for Wales;
- “**the Establishment Order**” (“***y Gorchymyn Sefydlu***”) means the Natural Resources Body for Wales (Establishment) Order 2012 [^c21769911];
- “**local enactment**” (“***deddfiad lleol***”) means any local Act or any subordinate legislation made under or by virtue of a local Act.
