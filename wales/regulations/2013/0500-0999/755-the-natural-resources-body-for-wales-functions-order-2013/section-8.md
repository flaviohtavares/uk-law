# The Natural Resources Body for Wales (Functions) Order 2013, section 8

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 8. Abolition of the Countryside Council for Wales

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The CCW is abolished.

(2) Accordingly, the following are repealed—

- (a) sections 128 to 134 of the 1990 Act [^c21769921];
- (b) Schedules 6, 8 and 9 to the 1990 Act [^c21769931];
- (c) Part 1 of the National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949 [^c21769941].
