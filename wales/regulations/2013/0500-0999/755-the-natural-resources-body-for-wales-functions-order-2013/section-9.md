# The Natural Resources Body for Wales (Functions) Order 2013, section 9

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Abolition of advisory committees

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The following are abolished—

- (a) the Environment Protection Advisory Committee established pursuant to section 12(6) of the 1995 Act;
- (b) the regional and local fisheries advisory committee established pursuant to section 13(5) of the 1995 Act.

(2) Accordingly, the following provisions of the 1995 Act are repealed—

- (a) section 12 [^c21769951];
- (b) section 13 [^c21769961];
- (c) Schedule 3;
- (d) paragraph 3 of Schedule 23.

## Annotations

[^c21769951]: Marginal citation: The other committees established under section 12 of the 1995 Act were abolished by the [Public Bodies (Abolition of Environment Protection Advisory Committees) Order 2012 (S.I. 2012/2407)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/2407).

[^c21769961]: Marginal citation: The other committees established under section 13 were abolished by the [Public Bodies (Abolition of Regional and Local Fisheries Advisory Committees) Order 2012 (S.I. 2012/2406)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/2406).
