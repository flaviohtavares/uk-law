# The Natural Resources Body for Wales (Functions) Order 2013, section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Title, commencement and extent

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The title of this Order is the Natural Resources Body for Wales (Functions) Order 2013.

(2) This Order comes into force on 1 April 2013.

(3) An amendment, repeal or revocation made by this Order has the same extent as the provision to which it relates.
