# The National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements)(Wales) Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1024 (W.121)

NATIONAL ASSISTANCE SERVICES, WALES

The National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements)(Wales) Regulations 2004

Made

30th March 2004

Coming into force

12th April 2004

The National Assembly for Wales makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 22(4) of the National Assistance Act 1948[^f00001] and now vested in the National Assembly for Wales[^f00002].

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These regulations may be cited as The National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements) (Wales) Regulations 2004 and come into force on 12th April 2004.

(2) These Regulations apply to Wales only.

## 2. Sum needed for personal requirements

The sum which a local authority assumes a person to need for his personal requirements under section 22(4) of the National Assistance Act 1948 is £18.40 per week.

## 3. Revocation

The National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements)(Wales) Regulations 2003[^f00003] are hereby revoked.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00004].

D. Elis-Thomas

The Presiding Office of the National Assembly

30th March 2004

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations set out the weekly sum which local authorities in Wales are to assume, in the absence of special circumstances, that residents in accommodation arranged under Part III of the National Assistance Act 1948, will need for their personal requirements.

From 12th April 2004 all such residents will be assumed to need £18.40 per week for their personal requirements.

These regulations supersede the National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements)(Wales) Regulations 2003 which are revoked and which prescribed a weekly amount of £17.80.
