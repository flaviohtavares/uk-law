# The Care Homes (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2004 No. 1314 (W.139)

SOCIAL CARE, WALES

The Care Homes (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2004

Made

11th May 2004

Coming into force

31st May 2004

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by section 22(1) and (2)(d) and section 118(7) of the Care Standards Act 2000[^f00001], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Care Homes (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2004 and shall come into force on 31st May 2004.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

## 2. Amendment of the Care Homes (Wales) Regulations 2002

In Regulation 2(1) of the Care Homes (Wales) Regulations 2002[^f00002] (interpretation), in the definition of “health care professional”, for “, child psychotherapist or speech therapist” there is substituted “or child psychotherapist”.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00003].

D. Elis-Thomas

The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

11th May 2004

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These amending Regulations remove the reference to speech therapists from the definition of “health care professional” in the Care Homes (Wales) Regulations 2002. This reflects the fact that speech therapists are now part of the profession of speech and language therapists, which is covered by section 60(2) of the Health Act 1999 as a profession regulated by the [Health Professions Order 2001 (S.I. 2002 No.254)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/254).
