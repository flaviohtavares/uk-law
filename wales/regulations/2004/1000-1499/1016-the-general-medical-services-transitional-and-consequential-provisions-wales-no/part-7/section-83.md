# The General Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions (Wales) (No. 2) Order 2004, section 83

> Text as at 2023-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 83. Continuing validity of forms[^key-8f2114e969a487fa0393a6bacaac9cda]

*Extent: E+W*

Notwithstanding the amendments made by paragraphs 1 and 5 of Schedule 1 to the form of certificates set out in Part 2 of Schedule 2 to the Social Security (Medical Evidence) Regulations 1976[^f00053] and in Part 2 of the Schedule to the Statutory Maternity Pay (Medical Evidence) Regulations 1987[^f00054], a form which complies with those regulations as in force on 31st March 2004 shall continue to be valid.
