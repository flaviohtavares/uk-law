# The General Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions (Wales) (No. 2) Order 2004, section 80

> Text as at 2023-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 80. Functions of Local Medical Committees[^key-5ade9ea196e18b4a5c262f1bf7256762]

*Extent: E+W*

Where—

- (a) a Local Medical Committee has, before the relevant date had any matter referred to it for its consideration that it had not finally determined before the relevant date; and
- (b) pursuant to this Part, that matter is to be determined by the Local Medical Committee on or after the relevant date,

the Local Medical Committee that had had the matter referred to it shall be deemed to be a Local Medical Committee that is recognised by a Local Health Board pursuant to section 45A of the 1977 Act for the purpose of exercising the continuing functions conferred on it in relation to the matter by this Part.
