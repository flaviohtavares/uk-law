# The General Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions (Wales) (No. 2) Order 2004, section 35

> Text as at 2023-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Entry on to medical performers list of persons approved under regulations 18A or 18B of the 1992 Regulations[^key-c7c6de927ca9e4164e18e1795f3a3cfc]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a Local Health Board—

- (a) had, on 31st March 2004, approved a medical practitioner under regulation 18A or 18B of the 1992 Regulations[^f00042] but had not yet entered his or her name on its medical list in accordance with regulation 18F(1) of those Regulations[^f00043]; and
- (b) intends to enter into a default contract or general medical services contract with two or more individuals practising in partnership one of whom is the person so approved,

it shall add that person’s name to its medical performers list unless he or she is already on the medical performers list of another Local Health Board and is not withdrawing from that list.
