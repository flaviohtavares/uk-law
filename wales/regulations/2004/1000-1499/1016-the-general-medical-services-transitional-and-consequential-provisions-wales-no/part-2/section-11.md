# The General Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions (Wales) (No. 2) Order 2004, section 11

> Text as at 2023-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. Acceptance of temporary residents[^key-4e8541929b15d62d49d83d18bdf9fd36]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This article applies where—

- (a) on or before 31st March 2004, a relevant medical practitioner had accepted a person as a temporary resident under paragraph 7(b) of Schedule 2 to the 1992 Regulations[^f00022]; and
- (b) on 31st March 2004, his or her responsibility for that patient had not yet been terminated under paragraphs 9 and 10 of that Schedule[^f00023] or the period for which the person was accepted as a temporary resident had not yet come to an end.

(2) In a case to which this article applies, the person shall, on 1st April 2004, be treated as if he or she had been accepted as a temporary resident by the succeeding contractor under—

- (a) the term of its general medical services contract which gives effect to paragraph 16 of Schedule 6 to the 2004 Regulations, subject to the modification that reference to a period of three months in that term shall be read as a reference to a period of three months starting with the date on which the person was accepted as a temporary resident by the relevant medical practitioner under paragraph 7 of Schedule 2 to the 1992 Regulations; or
- (b) the equivalent term of its default contract subject to a modification to the same effect.
