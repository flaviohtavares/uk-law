# The General Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions (Wales) (No. 2) Order 2004, section 25

> Text as at 2023-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. Inquiries about prescriptions and referrals[^key-edc402510fc56589af62a38abad3822b]

*Extent: E+W*

Where, on or before 31st March 2004, a relevant medical practitioner had received an inquiry about prescriptions or referrals from a Local Health Board under paragraph 49 of Schedule 2 to the 1992 Regulations[^f00034] but had not yet responded to that inquiry, the succeeding contractor shall respond as if the inquiry had been made under—

- (a) the term of its general medical services contract which gives effect to paragraph 77 of Schedule 6 to the 2004 Regulations; or
- (b) the equivalent term of its default contract.
