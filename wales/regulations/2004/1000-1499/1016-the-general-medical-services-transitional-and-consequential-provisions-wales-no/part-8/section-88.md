# The General Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions (Wales) (No. 2) Order 2004, section 88

> Text as at 2023-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 88. Saving of section 279 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992[^c-9f69cf5fba6c8461f876a1cbc96e6ff5]

*Extent: E+W*

Notwithstanding the coming into force of the amendments to the definition of worker in section 279 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992[^f00073] (health service practitioners) made by paragraph 59 of Schedule 11 to the 2003 Act[^f00074], in relation to any complaint arising in respect of a matter which occurred before 1st April 2004, section 279 shall have effect as if those amendments had not been brought into force.
