# The General Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions (Wales) (No. 2) Order 2004, section 89

> Text as at 2023-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 89. Saving of sections 2A and 6(5) of the Health Service Commissioners Act 1993[^c-94fbd922870f5e967341678df8137973]

*Extent: E+W*

Notwithstanding the coming into force of the amendments to sections 2A and 6(5) of the Health Service Commissioners Act 1993[^f00075] made by paragraphs 62 and 63 of Schedule 11 to the 2003 Act[^f00076], in relation to any complaint arising in respect of a matter which occurred before 1st April 2004, sections 2A and 6(5) shall have effect as if those amendments had not been brought into force.
