# The General Medical Services Transitional and Consequential Provisions (Wales) (No. 2) Order 2004, section 57

> Text as at 2023-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 57. Terms of general medical services contracts and default contracts[^key-b1dfbd3e85c7e4474be537352a8adc97]

*Extent: E+W*

Unless the contract or agreement is of a type or nature to which a particular article does not apply—

- (a) a general medical services contract shall include or be deemed to include terms which have the effect specified in articles 58,59, 60, 61, 62, 63, 64, 65, 66, 67 and 68;
- (b) a default contract shall include or be deemed to include terms which have the effect specified in articles 58, 59, 60, 61, 63, 64 and 68.
