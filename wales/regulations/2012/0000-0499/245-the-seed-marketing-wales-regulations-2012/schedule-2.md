# The Seed Marketing (Wales) Regulations 2012, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2024-10-07.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Certification requirements

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations 10, 11, 13 and 23

### PART A1 — Introduction[^c-e12e9656621072ef2c1252d93e09cc51]

#### A1. Interpretation

In this Schedule—

- “EU Plant Health Regulation” (“*Rheoliad Iechyd Planhigion yr UE*”) means [Regulation (EU) 2016/2031](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/2031) of the European Parliament and of the Council on protective measures against pests of plants;
- “[^c-ea9caaa8936c39aeebb8e6edb9bc2dc4]**PFA quarantine pest**” (“***pla cwarantin ardal sy’n rhydd rhag plâu***”) means a pest within the meaning given by Article 32(1) of the EU Plant Health Regulation;
- “RNQP” (“*PRHG*”) means a GB[^c-1d826db8d92211698ae36c857e897823] regulated non-quarantine pest within the meaning given by Article 36 of the EU Plant Health Regulation;
- “[^c-3c2917f585207907103a1f73bbe35f6f]**GB quarantine pest**” (“***pla cwarantin Prydain Fawr***”) means a pest within the meaning given by Article 4 of the EU Plant Health Regulation.

### PART 1 — Beet

#### 1. Scope of Part 1

This Part regulates the type of beet in Schedule 1.

#### 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations—

- (a) “**monogerm seed**” (“***hadau uneginol***”) means seed that is genetically monogerm; and
- (b) “**precision seed**” (“***hadau trachywir***”) means seed designed for use in precision drills to produce single seedlings.

#### 3. Permitted types of beet seed

Beet seed must be—

- (a) pre-basic seed;
- (b) basic seed; or
- (c) certified seed.

#### 4. Meaning of “pre-basic seed”

Pre-basic seed is seed—

- (a) produced from a generation prior to pre-basic seed by or under the responsibility of the breeder according to well-defined practices for the maintenance of the variety; and
- (b) intended for the production of—
  - (i) more pre-basic seed;
  - (ii) basic seed; or
  - (iii) with the breeder's written authority, certified seed.

#### 5. Meaning of “basic seed”

Basic seed is seed—

- (a) produced under the responsibility of the breeder according to well-defined practices for the maintenance of the variety; and
- (b) intended for the production of certified seed.

#### 6. Meaning of “certified seed”

Certified seed is seed that is of direct descent from basic seed and is intended for the production of beet.

#### 7. Crop and seed requirements

(1) Crop inspections by official or licensed crop inspectors must be carried out in accordance with Article 2(3)(A) (a) to (d)[^c-c7dff6fc07ccb3fcdbfb6b35f460d6d9] of, and Annex I(A) to, Council Directive [2002/54/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/54) on the marketing of beet seed [^c21191371], and the crop must satisfy the conditions in that Annex.

(2) The seed produced by the crop must be sampled in accordance with Article 9(2) and Annex II to that Directive, and must satisfy the conditions in Annex I(B) to that Directive.

(3) The seed must be labelled in accordance with Schedule 3[^c-c756bc86fbf02acd629c244f76d102fb].

(4) The maximum moisture content must not exceed 15% by weight.

(5) Diseases and harmful organisms that reduce the usefulness of the seed must be at the lowest possible level.

(6)[^c-807dfcf355aee2491fcccb5264e18061] For the purposes of this regulation, Council Directive [2002/54/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/54) is to be read as if—

- (a) in Article 2(3)(A)(a)(iii), for “officially licensed by the seed certification authority of the Member State concerned” there were substituted “ licensed by the Welsh Ministers ”;
- (b) in Annex 1—
  - (i) in point A—
    - (aa) in paragraph 3, for “certification authority” there were substituted “ Welsh Ministers ”;
    - (bb) in the last paragraph, for the words from “common” to “that Directive” there were substituted “GB Variety List”;
  - (ii) in point B, in paragraph 3(c)—
    - (aa) the reference to “Member States” were a reference to “The Welsh Ministers”;
    - (bb) the reference to “Community” were omitted.

### PART 2 — Cereals

#### CHAPTER 1 — Types of seed

##### 8. Scope of Part 2

This Part regulates the types of cereal in Schedule 1.

##### 9. Permitted types of cereal seed

(1) Cereal seed must be—

- (a) pre-basic seed;
- (b) basic seed;
- (c) certified seed;
- (d) certified seed, first generation;
- (e) certified seed, second generation; or
- (f) seed of a higher voluntary standard.

(2) The seed may be a mixture of cereal species of different varieties provided that each variety in the mixture is certified and is effective against the propagation of harmful organisms.

##### 10. Meaning of “pre-basic seed” (including hybrids)

Pre-basic seed (including hybrids) is seed—

- (a) produced from a generation prior to pre-basic seed or from pre-basic seed by or under the responsibility of the breeder according to well-defined practices for the maintenance of the variety; and
- (b) intended for the production of—
  - (i) more pre-basic seed;
  - (ii) basic seed; or
  - (iii) with the breeder's written authority, certified seed of categories CS, C1 or C2 (hybrid varieties may not be produced to C1 or C2).

##### 11. Meaning of “basic seed”

Basic seed is seed complying with the following conditions.

| *Type of cereal* | *Condition* |
| --- | --- |
| Self-pollinating varieties of oats, naked oats, barley, rye, triticale, wheat, durum wheat and spelt wheat | The seed must be—<br>- (a) produced under the responsibility of the breeder according to well-defined practices for the maintenance of the variety; and<br>- (b) intended for the production of certified seed of categories CS C1 or C2 |
| Hybrid varieties of oats, barley, rye, wheat, durum wheat, spelt wheat, triticale and maize | The seed must be intended for the production of hybrids |
| Maize spp open-pollinated varieties | The seed must be—<br>- (a) produced under the responsibility of the breeder according to well-defined practices for the maintenance of the variety;<br>- (b) intended for the production of seed of this variety of the category 'certified seed' triple cross or of top cross hybrid variety or of intervarietal hybrids |
| Maize, inbred lines | Any seed |

##### 12. Meaning of “certified seed”

Certified seed is seed—

- (a) of rye, maize, or triticale and hybrids of barley, oats, wheat, durum wheat, spelt wheat;
- (b) that has been produced directly from basic seed or, if the breeder so requests, from seed of a generation prior to basic seed that satisfies the conditions for basic seed; and
- (c) that is intended for purposes other than the production of cereal seed.

##### 13. Meaning of “certified seed, first generation”

Certified seed, first generation is seed—

- (a) of oats, barley, triticale, wheat, durum wheat or spelt wheat, other than hybrids in each case;
- (b) produced directly from basic seed or, if the breeder so requests, from pre-basic seed that satisfies the conditions for basic seed; and
- (c) intended either for the production of seed of the category 'certified seed, second generation' or for purposes other than the production of cereal seed.

##### 14. Meaning of “certified seed, second generation”

Certified seed, second generation is seed—

- (a) of oats, barley, triticale, wheat, durum wheat or spelt wheat, other than hybrids in each case;
- (b) produced directly from basic seed, from certified seed, first generation or, if the breeder so requests, from pre-basic seed that satisfies the conditions for basic seed; and
- (c) intended for purposes other than the production of cereal seed.

##### 15. Crop and seed requirements

(1) Crop inspections by official or licensed crop inspectors must be carried out in accordance with Article 2(3)(A) (a) to (d)[^c-ba83b13a163dd777d0c63ff7af35a557] of, and Annex I to, Council Directive [66/402/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1966/402) on the marketing of cereal seed [^c21191381], and the crop must satisfy the conditions in that Annex.

(2) The seed produced by the crop must be sampled in accordance with Article 7 of, and Annex III to, that Directive (except paragraphs 1a(f) and 1b of Article 7)[^c-04c0c198a77f7ba74abd14e9a185a694] and must satisfy the conditions in Annex II to that Directive.

(2A)[^c-e9f5cb9586df9ba47425acdf01758e97] For the purposes of sub-paragraphs (1) and (2), Council Directive [66/402/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1966/402) is to be read as if—

- (a) in Article 2(3)(A)(a)(iii), for “officially licensed by the seed certification authority of the Member State concerned” there were substituted “ licensed by the Welsh Ministers ”;
- (b) in Article 7—
  - (i) references to “Member States” were references to “Welsh Ministers”;
  - (ii) in paragraph 1a(a), for “seed certification authority of the Member State concerned” there were substituted “ Welsh Ministers ”.

(3) The maximum moisture content must not exceed 17% by weight.

(4)[^c-0cb5dfb016541e0df73e6a72752a31c4] The crop and the seed produced by the crop must be practically free from any pests which reduce the usefulness and quality of the seed.

(5) The crop and the seed produced by the crop must comply with the requirements concerning GB[^c-b76920b364183360a2cb7c7cb595ef79] quarantine pests, PFA[^c-98c4467738319d5468612d4d001e7c04] quarantine pests and RNQPs set out in implementing acts adopted pursuant to the EU Plant Health Regulation, and measures adopted pursuant to Article 30(1) of that Regulation.

#### CHAPTER 2 — Higher voluntary standards

##### 16. Higher voluntary standards for cereals

(1) Barley, oats, wheat (including durum wheat and spelt wheat) or naked oats classified as basic seed, or certified seed of categories C1 or C2, may be marketed as being of a higher voluntary standard, which is a standard higher than that regulated under Chapter 1, if it meets the higher standards in this Chapter.

(2) The crop must have at least 99.99% (by number) species purity.

(3) Other than a hybrid, the minimum varietal purity of seed is—

- (a) 99.95% for basic seed;
- (b) 99.9% for C1 seed;
- (c) 99.7% for C2 seed.

(4) In the case of C1 and C2 the seed must have 99% by weight analytical purity.

##### 17. Additional requirements for barley, wheat, durum wheat and spelt wheat

In the case of barley, wheat, durum wheat and spelt wheat—

- (a) the maximum number of wild oats in a crop is 7 per hectare.
- (b) the maximum percentage by number of loose smut infection in seed is—
  - (i) 0.1% for basic seed;
  - (ii) 0.2% for C1 and C2 seed.

##### 19. Ergot and sclerotia

In a sample of 1000g—

- (a) in the case of basic seed there must be no ergot or sclerotia;
- (b) in the case of certified seed (C1 or C2) there must be no more than one piece of ergot or sclerotia.

### PART 3 — Fodder plants

#### CHAPTER 1 — Basic standards

##### 20. Scope of Part 3

This Part regulates the types of fodder plants in Schedule 1.

##### 21. Permitted types of fodder seed

(1) Fodder seed must be—

- (a) pre-basic seed;
- (b) basic seed;
- (c) certified seed;
- (d) certified seed, first generation;
- (e) certified seed, second generation;
- (f) commercial seed; or
- (g) seed of a higher voluntary standard.

(2) The seed may be a mixture of fodder species of different varieties provided that each variety in the mixture is certified.

##### 22. Meaning of “pre-basic seed”

Pre-basic seed is seed—

- (a) produced from a generation prior to pre-basic seed by or under the responsibility of the breeder according to well-defined practices for the maintenance of the variety; and
- (b) intended for the production of—
  - (i) more pre-basic seed;
  - (ii) basic seed; or
  - (iii) with the breeder's written authority, certified seed CS, C1 or C2.

##### 23. Meaning of “basic seed”

(1) Basic seed is seed of—

- (a) bred varieties; or
- (b) local varieties.

(2) Seed of bred varieties is seed—

- (a) produced under the responsibility of the breeder according to well-defined practices for the maintenance of the variety;
- (b) intended for the production of certified seed.

(3) Seed of local varieties is seed—

- (a) produced under official control from material officially accepted as being of the local variety on one or more holdings situated within a clearly demarcated region of origin;
- (b) intended for the production of certified seed.

##### 24. Meaning of “certified seed”

Certified seed is seed (other than of field beans, field peas, lucerne (*Medicago sativa*), lupins and vetches)—

- (a) produced directly from basic seed or, if the breeder so requests, from pre-basic seed that satisfies the conditions for basic seed; and
- (b) intended for purposes other than the production of seed.

##### 25. Meaning of “certified seed, first generation”

Certified seed, first generation in relation to field beans, field peas, lucerne (*Medicago sativa*), lupins and vetches is seed—

- (a) produced directly from basic seed or, if the breeder so requests, from pre-basic seed that satisfies the conditions for basic seed; and
- (b) intended for—
  - (i) the production of certified seed, second generation (in the case of field beans and field peas only); or
  - (ii) purposes other than the production of seed (in all cases).

##### 26. Meaning of “certified seed, second generation”

Certified seed, second generation in relation to field beans, field peas, lucerne (*Medicago sativa*), lupins and vetches is seed—

- (a) produced directly from basic seed, from certified seed, first generation (C1) or, if the breeder so requests, from pre-basic seed that satisfies the conditions for basic seed; and
- (b) intended for purposes other than the production of seed of fodder plants.

##### 27. Meaning of “commercial seed”

Commercial seed is seed of annual meadowgrass, Hungarian vetch or sainfoin that is identifiable as belonging to a species.

##### 28. Crop and seed requirements

(1) Crop inspections by official or licensed crop inspectors must be carried out in accordance with Article 2(3)(A) (a) to (d)[^c-93c35d8011f8aff2f9d21a9c8aeaeb5f] of, and Annex I to, Council Directive [66/401/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1966/401) on the marketing of fodder plant seed [^c21191391], and the crop must satisfy the conditions in that Annex.

(1A)[^c-f3b1c583f41ce092e63f37e8905c543a] For the purposes of sub-paragraph (1), Council Directive [66/401/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1966/401) is to be read as if—

- (a) in Article 2(3)(A)(a)(iii), for “officially licensed by the seed certification authority of the Member State concerned” there were substituted “ licensed by the Welsh Ministers ”;
- (b) in Annex 1, in paragraph 4, in the second sub-paragraph, the words from “Upon” to the end were omitted.

(2) The seed produced by the crop must be sampled in accordance with Annex III to that Directive, and must satisfy the conditions in Annex II to that Directive.

(3)[^c-dad9d6fcd409a48fa3c85a04ca45c47b] The crop and the seed produced by the crop must be practically free from any pests which reduce the usefulness and quality of the seed.

(4) The crop and seed produced by the crop must comply with the requirements concerning GB[^c-dc5b16da5c73a81ea5a56e78be481355] quarantine pests, PFA[^c-4d7bd67c742c8d4c7d2f5c2291d0713b] quarantine pests and RNQPs set out in implementing acts adopted pursuant to the EU Plant Health Regulation, and measures adopted pursuant to Article 30(1) of that Regulation.

#### CHAPTER 2 — Higher voluntary standards

##### 29. Higher voluntary standards for fodder seed[^c-62c7fb99822245cccfacf75a8f068376]

- (a) cocksfoot, festulolium, hybrid ryegrass, Italian ryegrass, meadow fescue, perennial ryegrass, red clover, red fescue, sainfoin, small timothy, timothy, smooth stalked meadow grass, tall fescue and white clover, where any of these are classified as certified seed (CS);

### PART 4 — Oil and fibre

#### 31. Scope of Part 4

This Part regulates the types of oil and fibre plants in Schedule 1.

#### 32. Permitted types of oil and fibre seed

Oil and fibre seed must be—

- (a) pre-basic seed;
- (b) basic seed;
- (c) certified seed;
- (d) certified seed, first generation;
- (e) certified seed, second generation;
- (f) certified seed, third generation; or
- (g) commercial seed.

#### 33. Meaning of “pre-basic seed”

(1) “Pre-basic seed” is seed of a generation prior to basic seed—

- (a) that has been produced by or under the responsibility of the breeder according to well-defined practices for the maintenance of the variety, and
- (b) intended to be used for the production of more pre-basic seed, basic seed, or with the breeder's written authority—
  - (i) in the case of black mustard, brown mustard, dioecious hemp, sunflower, swede rape, turnip rape or white mustard, CS seed;
  - (ii) in the case of monoecious hemp or soya bean, C1 or C2 seed;
  - (iii) in the case of flax or linseed, C1, C2 or C3 seed.

(2) But in relation to a component of a hybrid variety, “**pre-basic seed**” means seed of a generation prior to basic seed that is intended to be used for the production of—

- (a) more pre-basic seed;
- (b) basic seed; or
- (c) with the breeder's written authority, CS seed.

#### 34. Meaning of “basic seed” for non-hybrid varieties

In the case of a non-hybrid variety, basic seed is seed—

- (a) produced under the responsibility of the breeder according to well-defined practices for the maintenance of the variety;
- (b) intended for the production of—
  - (i) certified seed,
  - (ii) certified seed, first generation,
  - (iii) certified seed, second generation; or
  - (iv) certified seed, third generation.

#### 35. Meaning of “basic seed” for inbred lines

In the case of an inbred line, basic seed is seed of an inbred line of a hybrid that satisfies the conditions for basic seed.

#### 36. Meaning of “basic seed” for simple hybrids

In the case of a simple hybrid, basic seed is seed intended for the production of hybrids.

#### 37. Meaning of “certified seed”

Certified seed is seed of black, brown and white mustard, dioecious hemp, sunflower, swede rape or turnip rape—

- (a) produced directly from basic seed or, if the breeder so requests, from seed of a generation prior to basic seed that satisfies the conditions for basic seed;
- (b) intended for purposes other than the production of seed of oil or fibre plants.

#### 38. Meaning of “certified seed, first generation”

Certified seed, first generation is seed of monoecious hemp, flax, linseed or soya—

- (a) produced directly from basic seed or, if the breeder so requests, from pre-basic seed that satisfies the conditions for basic seed;
- (b) intended either for the production of—
  - (i) certified seed, second generation;
  - (ii) where appropriate, certified seed, third generation; or
  - (iii) for purposes other than the production of seed of oil or fibre plants.

#### 39. Meaning of “certified seed, second generation”

(1) Certified seed, second generation is seed of flax, linseed, soya or monoecious hemp.

(2) In the case of flax, linseed, soya, it is seed—

- (a) produced directly from basic seed, from certified seed of the first generation or, if the breeder so requests, from pre-basic seed that satisfies the conditions for basic seed; and
- (b) intended for—
  - (i) purposes other than the production of seed or,
  - (ii) where appropriate, the production of certified seed, third generation seed.

(3) In the case of monoecious hemp it is seed—

- (a) produced directly from certified seed of the first generation established and officially controlled with a view to the production of certified seed of the second generation; and
- (b) intended for the production of hemp to be harvested in flower.

#### 40. Meaning of “certified seed, third generation”

Certified seed, third generation is seed from flax or linseed—

- (a) of direct descent from basic seed, from certified seed of the first or second generation or, if the breeder so requests, from seed of a generation prior to basic seed; and
- (b) intended for purposes other than the production of seed.

#### 41. Meaning of “commercial seed”

Commercial seed (black mustard seed only) is seed that is identifiable as belonging to a species.

#### 42. Crop and seed requirements

(1) Crop inspections by official or licensed crop inspectors must be carried out in accordance with Article 2(5)(A) (a) to (d)[^c-85888323cd9aa360016b4d24c59ff65e] of, and Annex I to, Council Directive [2002/57/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/57) on the marketing of seed of oil and fibre plants [^c21191401], and the crop must satisfy the conditions in that Annex.

(1A)[^c-f29945f58faf3819fb0d805be1959859] For the purposes of sub-paragraph (1), Council Directive [2002/57/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/57) is to be read as if—

- (a) in Article 2(5)(A)(a)(iii), for “officially licensed by the seed certification authority of the Member State concerned” there were substituted “ licensed by the Welsh Ministers ”;
- (b) in Annex 2, in Part 1, in paragraph 5C, the last sub-paragraph were omitted.

(2) The seed produced by the crop must be sampled in accordance with Annex III to that Directive and must satisfy the conditions in Annex II to that Directive.

(3)[^c-66efdaed4ea134b5759d4e66ec9e1307] The crop and the seed produced by the crop must be practically free from any pests which reduce the usefulness and quality of the seed.

(4) The crop and seed produced by the crop must comply with the requirements concerning GB[^c-b2864e11ab6c69e60e2e9e51bcbae0f2] quarantine pests, PFA[^c-3c45cd337a0a13366125f1a8ad137e52] quarantine pests and RNQPs set out in implementing acts adopted pursuant to the EU Plant Health Regulation, and measures adopted pursuant to Article 30(1) of that Regulation.

#### 43. Requirements for a varietal association

(1) Seed marketed as a varietal association must comply with this paragraph.

(2) The varietal association must be an association of certified CS seed of a specified pollinator-dependant hybrid variety with certified CS seed of one or more specified pollinator varieties both of which have been accepted on to the GB Variety List[^c-3058cecbe18cd0142433665140cf3c4c].

(3) The seed of the female and male components of a varietal association must have been dressed using seed dressings of different colours.

(4) The seed must be mechanically combined in the proportions jointly determined by the persons responsible for the maintenance of these components.

(5) The proportions must be notified to the Welsh Ministers by the person responsible for the maintenance of the pollinator-dependent hybrid and pollinators within the varietal association.

(6) In this paragraph—

- (a) “**pollinator-dependent hybrid**” (“***hybrid peillydd-ddibynnol***”) means the male-sterile component within the varietal association (female component);
- (b) “**pollinator**” (“***peillydd***”) means a component shedding pollen within a varietal association.

### PART 5 — Vegetables

#### 44. Scope of Part 5

This Part regulates the types of vegetables in Schedule 1.

#### 45. Permitted types of vegetable seed

(1) Vegetable seed must be—

- (a) pre-basic seed;
- (b) basic seed;
- (c) certified seed; or
- (d) standard seed.

(2) The seed may be a mixture of different varieties of the same vegetable species provided that each variety in the mixture is standard seed.

#### 46. Meaning of “pre-basic seed”

Pre-basic seed is seed—

- (a) produced from a generation prior to pre-basic seed by or under the responsibility of the breeder according to well-defined practices for the maintenance of the variety; and
- (b) intended for the production of—
  - (i) more pre-basic seed;
  - (ii) basic seed; or
  - (iii) with the breeder's written authority, certified seed.

#### 47. Meaning of “basic seed”

(1) Basic seed is seed—

- (a) produced under the responsibility of the breeder according to well-defined practices for the maintenance of the variety;
- (b) intended for the production of certified seed.

(2) For the avoidance of doubt this includes seeds intended as a component of a hybrid variety of vegetable.

#### 48. Meaning of “certified seed”

Certified seed is seed—

- (a) produced directly from basic seed or, if the breeder so requests, from seed of a generation prior to basic seed that satisfies the conditions for basic seed;
- (b) intended mainly for the production of vegetables.

#### 49. Meaning of “standard seed”

Standard seed is seed that is intended mainly for the production of vegetables and has been approved by the Welsh Ministers as having sufficient varietal purity and varietal identity.

#### 50. Crop and seed requirements

(1) Crop inspections by official or licensed crop inspectors must be carried out in accordance with Article 2(4)(A) (a) to (d)[^c-12d1ec31cb59f9d34611a416c24724dc] of, and Annex I to, Council Directive [2002/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/55) on the marketing of vegetable seed [^c21191411], and the crop must satisfy the conditions in that Annex.

(2) The seed produced by the crop must be sampled in accordance with Article 25 (except paragraphs 1a(f) and 1b)[^c-031254e5a5f521e03800c7d24763a8b2] of, and Annex III to, that Directive and must satisfy the conditions in Annex II to that Directive.

(2A)[^c-9de08edc9dff6dd64f6beaac05a3dde2] For the purposes of sub-paragraphs (1) and (2), Council Directive [2002/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2002/55) is to be read as if—

- (a) in Article 2(4)(A)(a)(iii), for “officially licensed by the seed certification authority of the Member State concerned” there were substituted “ licensed by the Welsh Ministers ”;
- (b) in Article 25—
  - (i) references to “Member States” were references to “Welsh Ministers”;
  - (ii) in paragraph 1a(a), for “seed certification authority of the Member State concerned” there were substituted “ Welsh Ministers ”.

(3) Sub-paragraph (1) does not apply in the case of standard seed.

(4)[^c-35519d3a32684218986d212f83435cee] The crop and seed produced by the crop must be practically free from any pests which reduce the usefulness and quality of the seed.

(4A) The crop and seed produced by the crop must comply with the requirements concerning GB[^c-2e35db68b55b5602f0ad2456df293d55] quarantine pests, PFA[^c-4d5b4d2cd2d056aa49f8d505c58db977] quarantine pests and RNQPs set out in implementing acts adopted pursuant to the EU Plant Health Regulation, and measures adopted pursuant to Article 30(1) of that Regulation.

(5) After marketing, vegetable seed is subject to control by the Welsh Ministers for varietal identity and varietal purity.
