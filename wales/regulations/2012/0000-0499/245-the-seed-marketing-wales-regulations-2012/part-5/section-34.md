# The Seed Marketing (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 34

> Text as at 2024-10-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 34. Revocation

*Extent: E+W*

The following Regulations are revoked—

- (a) the Oil and Fibre Plant Seed (Wales) Regulations 2004 [^c21191261];
- (b) the Vegetable Seed (Wales) Regulations 2005 [^c21191271];
- (c) the Cereal Seed (Wales) Regulations 2005 [^c21191281];
- (d) the Beet Seed (Wales) Regulations 2005 [^c21191291];
- (e) the Fodder Plant Seed (Wales) Regulations 2005 [^c21191301];
- (f) the Seed (Registration, Licensing and Enforcement) (Wales) Regulations 2005 [^c21191311];
- (g) the Cereal Seed (Wales) and Fodder Plant Seed (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2006 [^c21191321];
- (h) the Seed (Wales) (Amendments for Tests and Trials etc) Regulations 2007 [^c21191331];
- (i) the Seed (Conservation Varieties Amendments) (Wales) Regulations 2009 [^c21191341];
- (j) the Seed (Miscellaneous Amendments) (Wales) Regulations 2010 [^c21191351];
- (k) the Vegetable Seed (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 [^c21191361].
