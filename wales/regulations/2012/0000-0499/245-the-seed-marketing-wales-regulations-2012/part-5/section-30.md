# The Seed Marketing (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 30

> Text as at 2024-10-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30. Marketing seed under a specific derogation

*Extent: E+W*

The Welsh Ministers may temporarily permit the marketing of seed not satisfying the requirements of minimum germination under conditions determined in accordance with[^key-d93b164c89650c4eec3f2fb55511c2d0] Commission Regulation [(EC) No 217/2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2006/0217) laying down rules for the application of Council Directives [66/401/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1966/0401), [66/402/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1966/0402), [2002/54/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0054), [2002/55/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0055) and [2002/57/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0057) as regards the authorisation of Member States to permit temporarily the marketing of seed not satisfying the requirements in respect of the minimum germination [^c21191241].
