# The Seed Marketing (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 7

> Text as at 2024-10-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Maintainers for pre-basic and basic seed

*Extent: E+W*

Pre-basic seed and basic seed may only be produced with the written authorisation of the person listed as the maintainer of that seed in the GB Variety List, NI Variety List or an equivalent list in a country granted equivalence[^key-e55f92a4166d98e23b96191074e94705].
