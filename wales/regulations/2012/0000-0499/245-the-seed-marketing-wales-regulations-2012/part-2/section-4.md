# The Seed Marketing (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 4

> Text as at 2024-10-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Seed to which these Regulations apply

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations apply in relation to seed of the plants in the first column of the table in Schedule 1 intended to be used for agricultural or horticultural production, but do not apply in relation to seed intended to be used for ornamental plants.

(2) These Regulations do not apply in relation to seed intended for export outside Great Britain[^key-61694daeb4818d59c97bebcf10bf60e6] (other than regulation 26 which relates to certification for export).
