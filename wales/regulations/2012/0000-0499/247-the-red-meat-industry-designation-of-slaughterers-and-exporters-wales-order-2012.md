# The Red Meat Industry (Designation of Slaughterers and Exporters) (Wales) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 247 (W.40)

AGRICULTURE,WALES

The Red Meat Industry (Designation of Slaughterers and Exporters) (Wales) Order 2012

Made

1 February 2012

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

2 February 2012

Coming into force

1 April 2012

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of powers conferred on them by sections 4(3) and 17 of the Red Meat Industry (Wales) Measure 2010[^f00001].

## 1. Title, commencement and application

The title of this Order is the Red Meat Industry (Designation of Slaughterers and Exporters) (Wales) Order 2012. It applies in relation to Wales and comes into force on 1 April 2012 .

## 2. Designation of slaughterers and exporters as liable to pay the levy

The Welsh Ministers designate slaughterers and exporters as liable to levy.

Alun Davies

Deputy Minister for Agriculture, Food, Fisheries and European Programmes, under authority of the Minister for Business, Enterprise, Technology and Science, one of the Welsh Ministers

1 February 2012

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order implements provisions contained in section 4(3) of the Red Meat Industry (Wales) Measure 2010.

Article 2 designates slaughterers and exporters as being liable to pay the levy.

A regulatory impact assessment of the effect that this instrument will have on the costs of business and the voluntary sector has not been prepared.
