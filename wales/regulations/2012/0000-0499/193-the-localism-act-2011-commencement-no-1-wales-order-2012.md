# The Localism Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2012 No. 193 (W.31) (C.6)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, WALES

The Localism Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2012

Made

25 January 2012

Coming into force

31 January 2012

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by section 240(3) of the Localism Act 2011[^f00001].

## 1. Title and application

The title of this Order is the Localism Act 2011 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2012 and it applies in relation to Wales.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 31 January 2012

The following provisions of the Localism Act 2011 come into force on 31 January 2012—

- (a) sections 38 to 43 (pay accountability);
- (b) section 69 (non-domestic rates; discretionary relief).

Carl Sargeant

Minister for Local Government and Communities, one of the Welsh Ministers.

25 January 2012

(This note is not part of the Order)

This is the first Commencement Order made by the Welsh Ministers under the Localism Act 2011 (“the Act”).

Article 2 of the Order brings into force, on 31 January 2012, sections 38 to 43 of the Act that relate to pay accountability, and section 69 that relates to discretionary relief from non-domestic rating.
