# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 78

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 78. Designated distance learning courses (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3e65b82e3e12b742fb3c8917faf66ed2][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A course is designated for the purposes of section 22(1) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] and regulation 76 if it is designated by the Welsh Ministers under this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) Subject to paragraph (4), the Welsh Ministers may designate a course under this regulation if in their opinion— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the course is listed in Schedule 2 other than a course for the initial training of teachers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the course is a full-time course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) the course is of at least one academic year’s duration; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) students undertaking the course in the United Kingdom are not required to be in attendance on it by the institution providing the course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (e) subject to paragraph (5), the course began before 1 September 2012. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) For the purposes of determining whether the requirement in paragraph (2)(d) is satisfied the Welsh Ministers may disregard— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) any requirement imposed by the institution providing the course to attend any institution for the purposes of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) registration or enrolment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] an examination; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) any requirement imposed by the institution providing the course to attend any institution on a weekend or during any vacation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) any period of attendance at the institution providing the course which a student may but is not required to complete by that institution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) The Welsh Ministers may not designate a course as a designated distance learning course if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) it falls within paragraph 7 or 8 of Schedule 2; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the governing body of a maintained school has arranged for the provision of the course to a pupil of the school. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) A course which begins on or after 1 September 2012 is a designated distance learning course where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) a student transfers to that course pursuant to regulation 87 from a previous designated distance learning course which began before 1 September 2012; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) that course would otherwise be a designated course for the purposes of regulation 5. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
