# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 82

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 82. Interpretation of regulation 81 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0ed8a9a5b5fff9c4805b68b403a2ef1c][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of regulation 81 and this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) subject to sub-paragraph (b), “partner” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*partner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means any of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) the spouse of an eligible distance learning student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] the civil partner of an eligible distance learning student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] a person ordinarily living with an eligible distance learning student as if the person were the eligible distance learning student’s spouse where an eligible distance learning student is aged 25 or over on the first day of the academic year in respect of which the eligible distance learning student is being assessed for support and where the eligible distance learning student began the specified designated distance learning course before 1 September 2005; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] a person ordinarily living with an eligible distance learning student as if the person were the eligible distance learning student’s spouse or civil partner where an eligible distance learning student began the specified designated distance learning course on or after 1 September 2005; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) a person who would otherwise be a partner under sub-paragraph (a) is not to be treated as a partner if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers, that person and the eligible distance learning student are separated; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] the person is ordinarily living outside the United Kingdom and is not maintained by the eligible distance learning student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) “relevant income” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*incwm perthnasol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) has the meaning given in paragraph (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), an eligible distance learning student’s relevant income is equal to the eligible distance learning student’s financial resources in the preceding financial year less— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) £2,000 in respect of the eligible distance learning student’s partner; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) £2,000 in respect of the only or eldest child who is dependent on the eligible distance learning student or the eligible distance learning student’s partner; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) £1,000 in respect of each other child who is dependent on the eligible distance learning student or the eligible distance learning student’s partner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) Where the Welsh Ministers are satisfied that an eligible distance learning student’s financial resources in the preceding financial year are greater than the eligible distance learning student’s financial resources in the current financial year and that the difference between the two amounts is £1,000 or more, they must assess that student’s financial resources by reference to those resources in the current financial year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) In this regulation, an eligible distance learning student’s financial resources in a financial year means the aggregate of the eligible distance learning student’s income for that year together with the aggregate of the income for that year of any person who at the date of the application for support under this Part is the eligible distance learning student’s partner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) “child” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*plentyn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) in relation to an eligible distance learning student includes any child of the student’s partner and any child for whom the student has parental responsibility; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) “current financial year” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*blwyddyn ariannol gyfredol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means the financial year which includes the first day of the academic year in respect of which a person is being assessed for support under this Part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) “dependent” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*dibynnol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means wholly or mainly financially dependent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) “financial year” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*blwyddyn ariannol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means the period of twelve months for which the income of the eligible distance learning student is computed for the purposes of the income tax legislation which applies to it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (e) “income” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*incwm *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means gross income from all sources excluding— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) any payment made under section 23C(5A) of the Children Act 1989; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] any tax credits awarded pursuant to any claims under section 3 of the Tax Credits Act 2002 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00069];
- (f) “preceding financial year” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*blwyddyn ariannol flaenorol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means the financial year immediately preceding the current financial year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (g) “specified designated distance learning course” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*cwrs dysgu o bell dynodedig a bennir *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means the course in respect of which the person is applying for support under this Part or, where the student’s status as an eligible distance learning student has been transferred to the present distance learning course as a result of one or more transfers of that status by the Welsh Ministers from a distance learning course (the “initial course”) (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*cwrs cychwynnol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) in connection with which the Welsh Ministers determined the student to be an eligible distance learning student pursuant to regulations made under section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51], the specified designated distance learning course is the initial course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
