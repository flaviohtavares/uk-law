# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 85

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 85. Declarations provided by academic authorities (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4d5c2005ff869a1305283438bc5c69e2][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the academic authority must, on the request of the applicant, complete a declaration in such form as may be required by the Welsh Ministers to accompany the application for support under regulation 84. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) An academic authority is not required to complete a declaration if it is unable to give the confirmation required by sub-paragraph 3(a)(ii) or 3(b)(ii). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) In this Part, “declaration” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*datganiad *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) where the applicant is applying for support in connection with the designated distance learning course for the first time, a statement that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) provides the course information; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] confirms that the applicant has undertaken at least two weeks of the designated distance learning course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) in any other case, a statement that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) provides the course information; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] confirms that the applicant has enrolled to undertake the academic year of the designated distance learning course in respect of which the applicant is applying for support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) In this regulation, “course information” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*gwybodaeth am y cwrs *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the amount of fees being charged in respect of the academic year in respect of which the applicant is applying for support under this Part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) certification by the academic authority that it considers the applicant is undertaking the designated distance learning course in Wales; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) in any case where the applicant is a disabled student, certification by the academic authority that it considers the applicant has chosen to undertake the designated distance learning course for a reason other than that the applicant is unable to attend a designated course for a reason which relates to the applicant’s disability. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
