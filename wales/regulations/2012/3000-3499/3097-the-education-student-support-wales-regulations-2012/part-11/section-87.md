# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 87

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 87. Transfer of status (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-aa3d034405b78e31be8292e01baac5e6][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where an eligible distance learning student transfers from a designated distance learning course to another designated distance learning course, the Welsh Ministers must transfer the student’s status as an eligible distance learning student to that other course where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) they receive a request from the eligible distance learning student to do so; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) they are satisfied that one or more of the grounds for transfer in paragraph (2) applies; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) the period of eligibility has not terminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The grounds for transfer are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the eligible distance learning student starts to undertake another designated distance learning course at the same institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the eligible distance learning student starts to undertake a designated distance learning course at another institution; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) after commencing a designated distance learning course for a first degree (other than an honours degree) the eligible distance learning student is, before the completion of that course, admitted to a designated distance learning course for an honours degree in the same subject at the same institution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) Subject to paragraph (4), an eligible distance learning student who transfers under paragraph (1) is to receive in connection with the academic year of the course to which the eligible distance learning student transfers the remainder of the support under this Part for which the Welsh Ministers have determined the eligible distance learning student qualifies in respect of the academic year of the course from which the eligible distance learning student transfers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) The Welsh Ministers may re-assess the amount of support payable after the transfer in accordance with this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) An eligible student who transfers under paragraph (1) after the Welsh Ministers have determined the eligible distance learning student’s support under this Part in connection with the academic year of the course from which the eligible distance learning student is transferring but before the eligible distance learning student completes that year, may not apply for another grant under regulation 80(1)(b) or regulation 83 in connection with the academic year of the course to which the eligible distance learning student transfers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) Where a student transfers under paragraph (1), the maximum amount of support under regulation 80(1)(a) in respect of the academic years to and from which the eligible distance learning student transfers is the amount of support available in connection with the course with the highest actual fees as defined in regulation 80. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
