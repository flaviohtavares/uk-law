# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 80

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 80. Support for distance learning courses (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-e5b264f6f1ff7cbcf072cbb92c7bf5f2][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of this regulation, the support available is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) a grant in respect of fees not exceeding the lesser of the following amounts— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) £1,025; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] the actual fees, being the amount of fees charged to the eligible distance learning student in respect of an academic year of the designated distance learning course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) a grant not exceeding £1,155 for books, travel and other expenditure in connection with the designated distance learning course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) An eligible distance learning student does not qualify for support under paragraph (1)(b) if the only paragraph in Part 2 of Schedule 1 into which the eligible distance learning student falls is paragraph 9. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) An eligible distance learning student does not qualify for support under this regulation if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the eligible distance learning student is a disabled student; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) there has been bestowed on or paid to the eligible distance learning student in connection with the designated distance learning course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) a healthcare bursary the amount of which is calculated by reference to the eligible distance learning student’s income; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] a Scottish healthcare allowance whether or not the amount of such allowance is calculated by reference to the eligible distance learning student’s income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) An eligible distance learning student does not qualify for support under this regulation unless the Welsh Ministers consider that the eligible distance learning student is undertaking the designated distance learning course in Wales on the first day of the first academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) An eligible distance learning student will no longer qualify for support under this regulation if the Welsh Ministers consider that the eligible distance learning student is undertaking the designated distance learning course outside the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) An eligible distance learning student does not qualify for support under this regulation if the eligible distance learning student has undertaken one or more distance learning courses for eight academic years in aggregate and the eligible distance learning student has received in respect of each of those academic years a loan or a grant of the kind described in paragraph (7). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(7) The loans and grants are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) a loan, a grant in respect of fees or a grant for books, travel and other expenditure each made in respect of an academic year of a distance learning course pursuant to regulations made under section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51];
- (b) a loan, a grant in respect of fees or a grant for books, travel and other expenditure each made in respect of an academic year of a distance learning course by the Department for Employment and Learning (Northern Ireland) pursuant to regulations made under Articles 3 and 8(4) of the Education (Student Support) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00063]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) a loan in respect of an academic year of a distance learning course made pursuant to regulations made under sections 73(f), 73B and 74(1) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00064].

(8) An eligible distance learning student does not qualify for support under this regulation if the eligible distance learning student holds a first degree from an educational institution in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(9) For the purposes of paragraph (7), a degree is not to be treated as a first degree where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) it is a degree (other than an honours degree) that has been awarded to the eligible distance learning student who has completed the required modules, examinations or other forms of assessment for the eligible distance learning student’s first degree course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) that student is undertaking the present distance learning course so as to obtain an honours degree on completion of the required modules, examinations or other forms of assessment (whether or not that student continues the course at the same educational institution after the award of the degree referred to in sub-paragraph (a)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(10) No support is payable to an eligible distance learning student under these Regulations in connection with that student undertaking a distance learning course that is not a designated distance learning course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
