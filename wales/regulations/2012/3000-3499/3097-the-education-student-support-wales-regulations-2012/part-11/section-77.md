# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 77

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 77. Students becoming eligible during the course of the academic year (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-79d7e66c74de4144a74415c9e44bb8c5][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where one of the events listed in paragraph (4) occurs in the course of an academic year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) a student may qualify for a grant in respect of fees in respect of that academic year in accordance with this Part provided that the relevant event occurred within the first three months of the academic year; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) a grant in respect of fees is not available in respect of any academic year beginning before the academic year in which the relevant event occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) Where one of the events listed in sub-paragraphs (a), (b), (e), (f), (g), (h) or (i) of paragraph (4) occurs in the course of an academic year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) a student may qualify for a grant for books, travel and other expenditure in respect of that academic year in accordance with this Part; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) a grant for books, travel and other expenditure is not available in respect of any academic year beginning before the academic year in which the relevant event occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) Where one of the events listed in sub-paragraphs (a), (b), (e), (f), (g), (h) or (i) of paragraph (4) occurs in the course of an academic year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) a student may qualify for a grant for disabled distance learning students' living costs in respect of that academic year in accordance with this Part; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) a grant for disabled distance learning students' living costs is not available in respect of any academic year beginning before the academic year in which the relevant event occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) The events are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the student’s course becomes a designated distance learning course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the student, the student’s spouse, civil partner or parent (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) is recognised as a refugee or becomes a person with leave to enter or remain; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) a state accedes to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] and the student is a national of that state or a family member (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) of a national of that state; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) the student becomes a family member (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) of an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] EU national *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51];
- (e) the state of which the student is a national accedes to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] where the student has been ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] Islands *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] throughout the three-year period immediately preceding the first day of the first academic year of the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (f) the student acquires the right of permanent residence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (g) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 6(1)(a) of Schedule 1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (h) the student becomes the child of a Swiss national; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (i) the student becomes the child of a Turkish worker. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
