### 2. Household income (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3b1bd4bce9056eddc4afc18067293dc8][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(1) The amount of an eligible part-time student’s contribution depends on the household income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The household income is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) in the case of an eligible part-time student who has a partner, the residual income of the eligible part-time student aggregated with the residual income of the eligible part-time student’s partner (subject to sub-paragraph (4)); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) in the case of an eligible part-time student who does not have a partner, the residual income of the eligible part-time student. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) In determining the household income under sub-paragraph (2), the sum of £1,130 is deducted for each child wholly or mainly financially dependent on the eligible part-time student or that student’s partner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) For the purpose of calculating the contribution payable in respect of a parent student, the residual income of the parent student’s partner must not be aggregated under paragraph (a) of sub-paragraph (2) in the case of a parent student whose child or whose partner’s child who is an eligible student holds an award in respect of which the household income is calculated with reference to the residual income of the parent student or of the parent student’s partner or of both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

### 3. Calculation of eligible part-time student’s residual income (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f773ebdb7c363ffb0d3b3acb424e72c1][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(1) For the purpose of determining the residual income of an eligible part-time student, there is deducted from the eligible part-time student’s taxable income (unless already deducted in determining taxable income) the gross amount of any premium or other sum paid by the eligible part-time student in relation to a pension (not being a pension payable under a policy of life insurance) in respect of which relief is given under section 188 of the Finance Act 2004 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00094], or where the eligible part-time student’s income is computed for the purposes of the income tax legislation of another Member State, the gross amount of any such premium or sum in respect of which relief would be given if that legislation made provision equivalent to the Income Tax Acts. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) Where the only paragraph in Part 2 of Schedule 1 into which an eligible part-time student falls is paragraph 9 and the eligible part-time student’s income arises from sources or under legislation different from sources or legislation normally relevant to a person referred to in paragraph 9 of Part 2 of Schedule 1, the eligible part-time student’s income is not disregarded in accordance with sub-paragraph (1) but is instead disregarded to the extent necessary to ensure that the eligible part-time student is treated no less favourably than a person who is referred to in any paragraph of Part 2 of Schedule 1 would be treated if in similar circumstances and in receipt of similar income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) Where the eligible part-time student receives income in a currency other than sterling, the value of that income for the purpose of this paragraph is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) if the eligible part-time student purchases sterling with the income, the amount of sterling the eligible part-time student so receives; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) otherwise, the value of the sterling which the income would purchase using the rate for the month in which it is received published by the Office for National Statistics *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00095].

### 5. Calculation of contribution (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-390f7a1507bb5f1e8a4bd3cb658c06e1][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(1) The contribution payable in relation to an eligible part-time student is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) in any case where the household income exceeds £39,793, £1 for every £9.27 by which the household income exceeds £39,793; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) in any case where the household income is £39,793 or less, nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The contribution must not in any case exceed £6,208. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) The contribution may be adjusted in accordance with paragraph 6. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) Where sub-paragraph (5) applies, the aggregate contributions must not exceed £6,208. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) This sub-paragraph applies where the household income consists of the residual income of an eligible part-time student and the eligible part-time student’s partner where both hold a statutory award. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

### 6. Split contributions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9a9c719ca655c93ed125688def6ee953][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

Where the same household income is used to assess the amount of a statutory award for which two or more persons qualify the contribution payable in respect of the eligible part-time student is divided by the number of such persons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
