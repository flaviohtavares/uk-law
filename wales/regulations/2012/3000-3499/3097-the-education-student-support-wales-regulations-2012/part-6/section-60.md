# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 60

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 60. Students becoming eligible during the course of an academic year (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b0908cfff734f6a3e29bffa911341a6f][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a student becomes an eligible student during the course of an academic year as a result of one of the events listed in paragraph (2), the student may qualify for a loan for living costs in respect of such quarters of that academic year in respect of which a loan for living costs is payable as begin after the relevant event in paragraph (2) occurs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The events are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the student’s course becomes a designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the student, the student’s spouse, civil partner or parent (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) is recognised as a refugee or becomes a person with leave to enter or remain; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) the state of which the student is a national accedes to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] where the student has been ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] Islands *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] throughout the three-year period immediately preceding the first day of the first academic year of the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) the student acquires the right of permanent residence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (e) the student becomes the child of a Turkish worker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (f) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 6(1)(a) of Schedule 1; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (g) the student becomes the child of a Swiss national. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) An eligible student to whom paragraph (1) applies does not qualify for a loan for living costs in respect of any academic year beginning before the academic year in which the relevant event occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) The maximum amount of loan for living costs payable is the aggregate of the maximum amount of loan for each quarter in respect of which the student qualifies for support under this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) The maximum amount of loan for living costs for each such quarter is one third of the maximum amount of loan for living costs which would apply for the academic year if the student fell into the category which applies to the relevant quarter for the duration of the academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
