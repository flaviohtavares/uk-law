# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 62

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 62. Deductions from loans for living costs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0f0cff6b3f9f5a346fae2bc4a9578dbe][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A deduction from the amount of loan for living costs calculated under this Part in respect of an old system eligible student with full entitlement or a new system eligible student with full entitlement may be made in accordance with regulation 67. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) A deduction from the amount of loan for living costs calculated under this Part in respect of a student with reduced entitlement may not be made under regulation 67. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
