# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 56

## 56. Students with reduced entitlement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-764a4b992a1f06825446276fa1c16ba4][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

  - (i) category 1, £1,893; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

  - (v) category 5, £2,522. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) where the student applies for a loan for living costs and opts not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income, an amount equal to ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**X-Y**) where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - **X** is, for such a student in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
