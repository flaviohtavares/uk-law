# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 52

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52. Maximum amount of loans for new system eligible students with full entitlement who are not new cohort students (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-68425186fcb578a5b35f956aea37d912][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies to a new system eligible student with full entitlement who is not a new cohort student (other than a type 1 or type 2 teacher training student whose contribution exceeds nil). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) Subject to regulations 57 to 62, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year other than a final year of a course that is not an intensive course is equal to ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**X-Y**) where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- **X** is, for such a student in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) category 1, £3,987; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii) category 2, £7,215; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii) category 3, £6.140; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iv) category 4, £6,140; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (v) category 5, £5,150; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- **Y** is the maintenance grant amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) Subject to regulations 57 to 62, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is equal to ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**X-Y**) where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- **X** is, for such a student in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) category 1, £3,608; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii) category 2, £6,570; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii) category 3, £5,340; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iv) category 4, £5,340; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (v) category 5, £4,771; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- **Y** is the maintenance grant amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) In this regulation, “the maintenance grant amount” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*swm y grant cynhaliaeth *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) where the new system eligible student referred to in paragraph (1) qualifies under regulation 42 for an amount of maintenance grant not exceeding £1,329, the amount of maintenance grant payable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) where the new system eligible student referred to in paragraph (1) qualifies under regulation 42 for an amount of maintenance grant exceeding £1,329, £1,329; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) where no maintenance grant is payable, nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

**53.**[^key-45cf853682f981c5ad9ea4a872eb50d3] (1) This regulation applies to a type 1 or type 2 teacher training student whose contribution exceeds nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) Subject to regulations 57 to 62, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year other than the final year of a course that is not an accelerated course is, for such student in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) category 1, £3,987; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) category 2, £7,215; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) category 3, £6,140; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) Subject to regulations 57 to 62, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year of a course that is the final year of a course that is not an accelerated course is, for such a student in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
