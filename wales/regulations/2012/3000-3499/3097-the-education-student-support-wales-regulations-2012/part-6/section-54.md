# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 54

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 54. Maximum amount of loans for new system eligible students with full entitlement who are 2010 cohort students, 2012 cohort students or 2012 accelerated graduate entry students undertaking their first year of study (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-2b175f69f3e641cf34637859d798a235][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies to a new system eligible student with full entitlement who is a 2010 cohort student, a 2012 cohort student or a 2012 accelerated graduate entry student who is undertaking their first year of study. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) Subject to regulations 57 to 62, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year other than a final year of a course that is not an intensive course is equal to ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**X-Y**) where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- **X** is, for such a student in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) category 1, £3,987; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii) category 2, £7,215; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii) category 3, £6,140; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iv) category 4, £6,140; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (v) category 5, £5,150; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- **Y** is the maintenance grant amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) Subject to regulations 57 to 62, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is equal to ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**X-Y**) where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- **X** is, for such a student in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) category 1, £3,608; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii) category 2, £6,570; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii) category 3, £5,340; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iv) category 4, £5,340; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (v) category 5, £4,771; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- **Y** is the maintenance grant amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) In this regulation, “the maintenance grant amount” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*swm y grant cynhaliaeth *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) where the new system eligible student referred to in paragraph (1) qualifies under regulation 43 for an amount of maintenance grant, the amount that is equal to £0.50 for every £1 of maintenance grant for which that student qualifies, up to a maximum value for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **Y** of £2,575; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) where no maintenance grant is payable under regulation 43, nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
