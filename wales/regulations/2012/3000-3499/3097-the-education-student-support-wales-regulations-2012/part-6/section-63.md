# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 63

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 63. Interpretation of Part 6 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b7625c5a4fe1a0a67b756624844c8cd1][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) a student is in category 1 if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) subject to regulation 57, the student resides at the student’s parents' home while attending the course; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] the student began the present course before 1 September 2009 and is a member of a religious order who resides in a house of that order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) a student is in category 2 if the student is not in category 1 and the student attends one or more of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) a course at the University of London; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] a course at an institution which requires attendance for at least half the time in aggregate of any quarter of the course in the academic year at a site wholly or partly within the area comprising the City of London and the former Metropolitan Police District; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] a sandwich course at an institution which requires the student to undertake work experience or a combination of work experience and study provided that the student undertakes such work experience or combination of work experience and study for at least half the time in aggregate of any quarter of the course in the academic year at a site or sites wholly or partly within the area comprising the City of London and the former Metropolitan Police District; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) a student is in category 3 if the student is not in category 1 and the student attends an overseas institution as part of the student’s course or attends an overseas work placement in an Erasmus year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) a student is in category 4 if the student is not in category 1 and attends the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] Institute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51];
- (e) a student is in category 5 if the student is not in categories 1 to 4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (f) a “new system eligible student with full entitlement” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*myfyriwr cymwys o dan y drefn newydd sydd â hawlogaeth lawn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) is a new system eligible student other than a student with reduced entitlement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (g) an “old system eligible student with full entitlement” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*myfyriwr cymwys o dan yr hen drefn sydd â hawlogaeth lawn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) is an old system eligible student other than a student with reduced entitlement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (h) “parent” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*rhiant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”), except where otherwise indicated, is to be construed in accordance with paragraph 1(1)(f) of Schedule 5; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (i) the “relevant date” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*dyddiad perthnasol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means the first day of the first academic year of the specified designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (j) a “student with reduced entitlement” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*myfyriwr sydd â hawlogaeth ostyngol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) is an eligible student who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) is not eligible for a grant for living costs in respect of the academic year by virtue of regulation 28(3)(a) or (b) or regulation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 28(7)[^key-0ef2ff4371568d8330c338ccb8e4395e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] opts when applying for a loan for living costs not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (k) where the duration of a graduate-entry or postgraduate-level course for the initial training of teachers is only one academic year, that year is not to be treated as the final year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
