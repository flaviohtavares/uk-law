# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 3

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Revocation, savings and transitional provisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-671d1b0725f90710689185b28e02ec1f][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (11), the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 2011 (No. 2) Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] are revoked in relation to Wales on 1 September 2013. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 2003 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] continue to apply to the provision of support to students in relation to an academic year which began on or after 1 September 2003 but before 1 September 2004. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 2004 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] continue to apply to the provision of support to students in relation to an academic year which began on or after 1 September 2004 but before 1 September 2005. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 2005 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] continue to apply to the provision of support to students in relation to an academic year which began on or after 1 September 2005 but before 1 September 2006. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 2006 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] continue to apply to the provision of support to students in relation to an academic year which began on or after 1 September 2006 but before 1 September 2007. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 2007 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] continue to apply to the provision of support to students in relation to an academic year which began on or after 1 September 2007 but before 1 September 2008. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(7) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 2008 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] continue to apply to the provision of support to students in relation to an academic year which began on or after 1 September 2008 but before 1 September 2009. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(8) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 2008 (No. 2) Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] continue to apply to the provision of support to students in relation to an academic year which began on or after 1 September 2009 but before 1 September 2010. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(9) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 2009 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] continue to apply to the provision of support to students in relation to an academic year which began on or after 1 September 2010 but before 1 September 2011. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(10) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 2011 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] continue to apply to the provision of support to students in relation to an academic year which begins on or after 1 September 2011 but before 1 September 2012. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(11) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 2011 (No. 2) Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] continue to apply to the provision of support to students in relation to an academic year which begins on or after 1 September 2012 but before 1 September 2013. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(12) For the purposes of paragraphs (2) to (4), any reference to the Secretary of State in relation to any function conferred on the Secretary of State by the Regulations referred to in those paragraphs, is to be read in relation to Wales as a reference to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the Welsh Ministers, in the case of a function referred to in section 44(1) of the Higher Education Act 2004 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00039]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the Welsh Ministers or the Secretary of State, in the case of a function referred to in section 44(2) of the Higher Education Act 2004. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(13) These Regulations apply in relation to the provision of support to students in respect of an academic year which begins on or after 1 September 2013 whether anything done under these Regulations is done before, on or after 1 September 2013. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(14) Where a person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) attends a course in respect of which a transitional award was made; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) received no award under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 1962 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] in respect of that person’s attendance on a course but a transitional award would have been made had the person applied for an award under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 1962 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] and the person’s resources had not exceeded the person’s requirements, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

that person is to be treated as an old system eligible student for the purposes of Parts 4 and 5 of these Regulations in connection with that course or in connection with any subsequent course to which the award (either made or which would have been made) would have transferred if transitional awards provided for payments after the first year of the course, despite any other provision in these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(15) Unless paragraph (16) applies to the person referred to in paragraph (14), that person qualifies for support by way of loan for living costs under Part 6 of these Regulations if that person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) is an eligible student under these Regulations; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) satisfies the qualifying conditions for support under that Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(16) Where a person received or was eligible to receive a loan in relation to an academic year of a course under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 1998 Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51], that person is to be treated as an old system eligible student for the purposes of Part 6 of these Regulations in connection with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) that course; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) any subsequent designated course which (disregarding any intervening vacation) the person begins immediately after ceasing that course, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

despite any other provisions in these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(17) Unless paragraph (14) applies to the person referred to in paragraph (16), that person qualifies for support under Parts 4 and 5 of these Regulations if that person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) is an eligible student under these Regulations; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) satisfies the relevant qualifying conditions under those Parts. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
