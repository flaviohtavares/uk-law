# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 9

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Applications for support (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c49fdaa4b2182e7a01ded3bbbeec7255][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person must apply for support in connection with each academic year of a designated course by completing and submitting to the Welsh Ministers an application in such form and accompanied by such documentation as the Welsh Ministers may require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The Welsh Ministers may take such steps and make such inquiries as they consider necessary to determine whether the applicant is an eligible student, whether the applicant qualifies for support under these Regulations and the amount of support payable, if any. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) The Welsh Ministers must notify the applicant of whether or not the applicant qualifies for support under these Regulations and, if the applicant does qualify, the amount of support payable in respect of the academic year, if any. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
