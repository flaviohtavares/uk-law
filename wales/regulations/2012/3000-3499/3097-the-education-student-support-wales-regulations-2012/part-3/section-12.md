# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 12

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Requirement to enter into a contract for a loan (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-87c8550e2efdba328ed87770f738a355][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

To receive a loan under these Regulations an eligible student must enter into a contract with the Welsh Ministers on terms to be decided by the Welsh Ministers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
