# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 93

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 93. Eligible part-time students (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b09d027ac172abd8f7f637a0678e7c61][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible part-time student qualifies for support in connection with the student undertaking a designated part-time course subject to and in accordance with this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) A person is an eligible part-time student in connection with a designated part-time course if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) in assessing the person’s application for support under regulation 109 the Welsh Ministers determine that the person falls within one of the categories set out in Part 2 of Schedule 1; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the person is not excluded by paragraph (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) Subject to paragraph (7), a person (“A” in this paragraph) is not an eligible part-time student if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) there has been bestowed on or paid to A in relation to A undertaking the part-time course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) a healthcare bursary whether or not the amount of such bursary is calculated by reference to A’s income; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] any allowance under the Nursing and Midwifery Student Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 2007; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] a Scottish healthcare allowance whether or not the amount of such allowance is calculated by reference to A’s income; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) A is in breach of any obligation to repay any loan; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) A has reached the age of 18 and has not ratified any agreement for a loan made with A when A was under the age of 18; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) A has, in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers, shown by A’s conduct that A is unfitted to receive support under this Part; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (e) subject to paragraph (4), A is a prisoner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) Paragraph (3)(e) does not apply in respect of an academic year during which the student enters prison or is released from prison. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) For the purposes of paragraphs (3)(b) and (3)(c), “loan” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*benthyciad *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means a loan made under the student loans legislation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) In a case where the agreement for a loan is subject to the law of Scotland, paragraph (3)(c) only applies if the agreement was made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) before 25 September 1991; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) with the concurrence of the borrower’s curator or at a time when the borrower had no curator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(7) Subject to paragraphs (9) to (11), a person is an eligible part-time student for the purposes of this Part if the person satisfies the conditions in paragraphs (8)(a) or (b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(8) The conditions referred to in paragraph (7) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) person qualified as an eligible part-time student in connection with an earlier academic year of the present part-time course pursuant to regulations made by the Welsh Ministers under section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51];
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] person was ordinarily resident in Wales on the first day of the present part-time course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] person’s status as an eligible part-time student has not terminated; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) Welsh Ministers have previously determined that the person is an eligible— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] student in connection with a designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] part-time student in connection with a designated part-time course other than the present part-time course; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] distance learning student in connection with a designated distance learning course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] person’s status as an eligible student, an eligible distance learning student or as an eligible part-time student in connection with the course referred to in sub-paragraph (b)(i) has been converted or transferred from that course to the present part-time course as a result of one or more conversions or transfers in accordance with regulations made by the Welsh Ministers under section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51];
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] person was ordinarily resident in Wales on the first day of the first academic year of the course referred to in sub-paragraph (b)(i); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] person’s status as an eligible part-time student has not terminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(9) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the Welsh Ministers determined that, by virtue of being a refugee or the spouse, civil partner, child or step-child of a refugee, a person (“A” in this paragraph) was an eligible part-time student in connection with an application for support for an earlier year of the present part-time course or an application for support in connection with a designated course, designated distance learning course or other designated part-time course from which A’s status as an eligible part-time student, eligible student or eligible distance learning student has been transferred to the present part-time course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) as at the day before the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support begins, the refugee status of A or of A’s spouse, civil partner, parent (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) or step-parent, as the case may be, has expired and no further leave to remain has been granted and no appeal is pending (within the meaning of section 104 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

A’s status as an eligible part-time student terminates immediately before the first day of the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(10) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the Welsh Ministers determined that, by virtue of being a person with leave to enter or remain or the spouse, civil partner, child or step-child of such a person, a person (“A” in this paragraph) was an eligible part-time student in connection with an application for support for an earlier year of the present part-time course or an application for support in connection with a designated course, designated distance learning course or other designated part-time course from which A’s status as an eligible part-time student, eligible student or eligible distance learning student has been transferred to the present part-time course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) as at the day before the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support begins, the period for which the person with leave to enter or remain is allowed to stay in the United Kingdom has expired and no further leave to remain has been granted and no appeal is pending (within the meaning of section 104 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

A’s status as an eligible part-time student terminates immediately before the first day of the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(11) Paragraphs (9) and (10) do not apply where the student started the course in connection with which the Welsh Ministers determined that the student was an eligible part-time student or eligible student, as the case may be, before 1 September 2007. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(12) An eligible part-time student does not qualify for support under regulation 97(1)(b), regulation 98 or regulations 99 to 108 if the only paragraph in Part 2 of Schedule 1 into which the eligible part-time student falls is paragraph 9. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(13) Subject to paragraphs (14) and (15), an eligible part-time student qualifies for support— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) under regulation 97(1)(a) if the Welsh Ministers consider that the eligible part-time student is undertaking the designated part-time course in Wales; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) under regulations 97(1)(b), 98 or 99 to 108 if the Welsh Ministers consider that the eligible part-time student is undertaking the designated part-time course in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(14) An eligible part-time student does not qualify for support under regulations 97, 98 or 99 to 108 in respect of a part-time distance learning course, unless the Welsh Ministers consider that the student is undertaking the course in Wales on the first day of the first academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(15) An eligible part-time student will no longer qualify for support under regulations 97, 98 or 99 to 108 in respect of a part-time distance learning course if the Welsh Ministers consider that the student is undertaking the course outside the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(16) An eligible part-time student does not qualify for support under regulation 97 or regulations 99 to 108 if the eligible part-time student has undertaken one or more part-time courses for eight academic years in aggregate and the eligible part-time student has received in respect of each of those academic years a loan or a grant of the kind described in paragraph (17). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(17) The loans and grants referred to in paragraph (16) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) a loan, a grant in respect of fees or a grant for books, travel and other expenditure each made in respect of an academic year of a part-time course pursuant to regulations made under section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51];
- (b) a loan, a grant in respect of fees or a grant for books, travel and other expenditure each made in respect of an academic year of a part-time course by the Department for Employment and Learning (Northern Ireland) pursuant to regulations made under Articles 3 and 8(4) of the Education (Student Support) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) a loan in respect of an academic year of a part-time course made pursuant to regulations made under sections 73(f), 73B and 74(1) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(18) Subject to paragraphs (19) and (20), an eligible part-time student does not qualify for support under regulation 97 or regulations 99 to 108 if the student holds a first degree from an educational institution in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(19) For the purposes of paragraph (18), a degree is not to be treated as a first degree where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) it is a degree (other than an honours degree) that has been awarded to the eligible part-time student who has completed the required modules, examinations or other forms of assessment for the eligible part-time student’s first degree course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) that student is undertaking the present part-time course so as to obtain an honours degree on completion of the required modules, examinations or other forms of assessment (whether or not that student continues the course at the same institution after the award of the degree referred to in sub-paragraph (a)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(20) Paragraph (18) does not prevent an eligible part-time student from qualifying for support under regulation 97 or regulations 99 to 108 if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the present part-time course is a course for the initial training of teachers which started on or after 1 September 2010; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the duration of that course does not exceed four years; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) the eligible part-time student is not a qualified teacher. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(21) An eligible part-time student may not, at any one time, qualify for support for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) more than one designated part-time course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) a designated part-time course and a designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) a designated part-time course and a designated distance learning course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) a designated part-time course and a designated postgraduate course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
