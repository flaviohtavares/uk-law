# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 117

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 117. Payment of grants for fees (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-252c803849b50f35bf39ab5f5f7bd42d][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), the Welsh Ministers must pay the grant in respect of fees for which the eligible part-time student qualifies to the appropriate academic authority after a written request for payment has been received which the Welsh Ministers consider to be a valid request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The Welsh Ministers may make payments under paragraph (1) at such times and in such instalments (if any) as they see fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) The Welsh Ministers may make provisional payments under paragraph (1) in such cases as they deem appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
