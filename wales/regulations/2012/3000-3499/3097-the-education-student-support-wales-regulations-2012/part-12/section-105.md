# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 105

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 105. Part-time grants for dependants — interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-710c58581509ca4f3a99fe4f7c5bc0be][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In regulations 99 to 104— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) subject to paragraph (4), “adult dependant” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*dibynnydd mewn oed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means, in relation to an eligible part-time student, an adult person dependent on the eligible part-time student other than the eligible part-time student’s child, the eligible part-time student’s partner (including a spouse or civil partner from whom the Welsh Ministers consider the eligible part-time student is separated) or the eligible part-time student’s former partner; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) “child” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*plentyn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) in relation to an eligible part-time student includes any child of the eligible part-time student’s partner who is dependent on the eligible part-time student and any child for whom the eligible part-time student has parental responsibility who is dependent on the eligible part-time student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) “dependant” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*dibynnydd *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means, in relation to an eligible part-time student, the eligible part-time student’s partner, the eligible part-time student’s dependent child or an adult dependant, who in each case is not an eligible student and does not hold a statutory award; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) “dependent” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*dibynnol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means wholly or mainly financially dependent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (e) “dependent child” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*plentyn dibynnol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means, in relation to an eligible part-time student, a child dependent on the eligible part-time student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (f) “lone parent” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*rhiant unigol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means an eligible part-time student who does not have a partner and who has a dependent child; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (g) “net income” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*incwm net *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) has the meaning given in paragraph (6); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (h) subject to sub-paragraphs (i), (j), (k) and paragraphs (2) and (3) “partner” (“partner”) means any of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) the spouse of an eligible part-time student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] the civil partner of an eligible part-time student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] a person ordinarily living with an eligible part-time student as if the person were the eligible part-time student’s spouse where an eligible part-time student is aged 25 or over on the first day of the academic year in respect of which household income falls to be assessed for the purposes of Schedule 6 and began the designated part-time course on or after 1 September 2000; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] a person ordinarily living with an eligible part-time student as if the person were the eligible part-time student’s civil partner where an eligible part-time student is aged 25 or over on the first day of the academic year in respect of which household income falls to be assessed for the purposes of Schedule 6 and began the designated part-time course on or after 1 September 2005; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (i) unless otherwise indicated, a person who would otherwise be a partner under sub-paragraph (h) is not treated as a partner if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers, that person and the eligible part-time student are separated; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] the person is ordinarily living outside the United Kingdom and is not maintained by the eligible part-time student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (j) for the purposes of the definition of “adult dependant” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*dibynnydd mewn oed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”), a person is to be treated as a partner if the person would be a partner under sub-paragraph (h) but for the fact that the eligible part-time student with whom the person is ordinarily living is not aged 25 or over on the first day of the academic year in respect of which household income falls to be assessed for the purposes of Schedule 6; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (k) for the purposes of the definitions of “child” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*plentyn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) and “lone parent” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*rhiant unigol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”), a person is to be treated as a partner if the person would be a partner under sub-paragraph (h) but for the date on which the eligible part-time student began the specified designated part-time course or the fact that the eligible part-time student with whom the person is ordinarily living is not aged 25 or over on the first day of the academic year in respect of which household income falls to be assessed for the purposes of Schedule 6. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) For the purposes of regulation 102— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) paragraph (1)(i) does not apply; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) a person is to be treated as a partner if the person would be a partner under paragraph (1)(h) but for the fact that the eligible part-time student with whom the person is ordinarily living is not aged 25 or over on the first day of the academic year in respect of which household income falls to be assessed for the purposes of Schedule 6. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) For the purposes of determining whether a person is the former partner of an eligible part-time student’s partner, “partner” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*partner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) in relation to an eligible part-time student’s partner means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the spouse of an eligible part-time student’s partner; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the civil partner of an eligible part-time student’s partner; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) where the eligible part-time student began the specified designated part-time course on or after 1 September 2000, a person (“A”) ordinarily living with an eligible part-time student’s partner (“B”) as if A were B’s spouse; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) where the eligible part-time student began the specified designated part-time course on or after 1 September 2005, a person (“A”) ordinarily living with an eligible part-time student’s partner (“B”) as if A were B’s civil partner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) Subject to paragraph (5), for the purposes of the definitions of “adult dependant” (“dibynnydd mewn oed”) and “dependent child” (“plentyn dibynnol”), the Welsh Ministers may treat an adult person or child as dependent on an eligible part-time student if they are satisfied that the adult person or child— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) is not dependent on only— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) the eligible part-time student; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] the eligible part-time student’s partner; but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) is dependent on the eligible part-time student and the eligible part-time student’s partner together. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) The Welsh Ministers must not treat an adult person (“A”) as dependent on an eligible part-time student in accordance with paragraph (4), if A is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the spouse or civil partner of the eligible part-time student’s partner (including a spouse or civil partner from whom the Welsh Ministers consider the eligible part-time student’s partner is separated); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the former partner of the eligible part-time student’s partner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) Subject to paragraph (7), a dependant’s net income is the dependant’s income from all sources for the academic year in question reduced by the amount of income tax and social security contributions payable in respect of it but disregarding— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) any pension, allowance or other benefit paid by reason of a disability or incapacity to which the dependant is subject; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) child benefit payable under Part IX of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00073];
- (c) any financial support payable to the dependant by a local authority in accordance with regulations made under sections 2, 3 and 4 of the Adoption and Children Act 2002 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00074];
- (d) any guardian’s allowance to which the dependant is entitled under section 77 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (e) in the case of a dependant with whom a child being looked after by a local authority is boarded out, any payment made to that dependant in pursuance of section 23 of the Children Act 1989 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00075];
- (f) any payment made to the dependant under section 23C(5A) of the Children Act 1989; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (g) any payments made to the dependant under section 15 of and Schedule 1 to the Children Act 1989 in respect of a person who is not the dependant’s child or any assistance given by a local authority pursuant to section 24 of that Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00076]; [^c-d73962fd844cda6280e285b0a9ea790b]...
- (h) any child tax credit to which the dependant is entitled under Part I of the Tax Credits Act 2002 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00077] ;[^c-3642bbd51a5f016563528b7f415c1a12] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (i)[^c-8a03507ebffb96b65118f8173d006b3f] in the case of a dependant who is entitled to an award of universal credit— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) any amount that is included in the calculation of the award under regulation 27(1) of the Universal Credit Regulations 2013, in respect of the fact that the dependant has limited capability for work or limited capability for work and work-related activity; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] any amount or additional amount that is included in the calculation of the award under regulation 24 of those Regulations (the child element). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(7) Where an eligible part-time student or the eligible part-time student’s partner makes any recurrent payments which were previously made by the eligible part-time student in pursuance of an obligation incurred before the first academic year of the eligible part-time student’s course, the eligible part-time student’s partner’s net income is the net income calculated in accordance with paragraph (2) reduced by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) an amount equal to the payments in question for the academic year, if in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers, the obligation had been reasonably incurred; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) such lesser amount, if any, as the Welsh Ministers consider appropriate if, in their opinion, a lesser obligation could reasonably have been incurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(8) For the purposes of paragraph (6), where the dependant is a dependent child and payments are made to the eligible part-time student towards the dependent child’s maintenance, those payments are to be treated as the dependent child’s income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
