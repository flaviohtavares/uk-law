# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 116

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 116. Interpretation of regulation 115 (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-246efd19b00facffbda68fc12244e697][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 115— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) “attendance confirmation” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*cadarnhad o bresenoldeb *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means confirmation in writing from the academic authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) that the eligible part-time student has enrolled for the academic year where the eligible part-time student— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] is applying for one or more elements of the part-time grants for dependants (“part-time support” in this regulation) in connection with a designated part-time course for the first time; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] has a disability; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] is undertaking the course but not attending (regardless of whether the reason for not attending relates to the student’s disability); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] that the eligible part-time student has been present at the institution and begun to attend the course where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] the student is applying for part-time support in connection with a designated part-time course for the first time; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] the student’s status as an eligible part-time student has not been transferred to the designated part-time course from another course at the same institution; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] sub-paragraph (i)(cc) does not apply; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] that the eligible part-time student has enrolled for the academic year where the eligible part-time student is applying for part-time support in connection with a designated part-time course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] other than for the first time; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] for the first time after the student’s status as an eligible part-time student has been transferred to that course from another course at the same institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) “payment period” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*cyfnod talu *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means a period in respect of which the Welsh Ministers pay the support under regulations 99 to 108 or would have paid such support if the eligible part-time student’s period of eligibility had not terminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
