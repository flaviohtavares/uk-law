# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 101

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 101. Part-time adult dependants' grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-88808dfbd50dd9978fd0b78fa8c15555][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible part-time student qualifies for a part-time adult dependants' grant in connection with the eligible part-time student’s attendance on a designated part-time course in accordance with this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The part-time adult dependants' grant is available in respect of one dependant of an eligible part-time student who is either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the eligible part-time student’s partner; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) an adult dependant of the eligible part-time student whose net income does not exceed £3,923. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) The amount of part-time adult dependants' grant payable in respect of an academic year is calculated in accordance with regulations 104 and 106 to 108, the basic amount being— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) £2,732; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) where the person in respect of whom the eligible part-time student is applying for part-time adult dependants' grant is ordinarily resident outside the United Kingdom, such amount not exceeding £2,732 as the Welsh Ministers consider reasonable in the circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
