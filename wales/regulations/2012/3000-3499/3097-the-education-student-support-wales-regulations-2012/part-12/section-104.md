# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 104

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 104. Part-time grants for dependants — initial calculations (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-b74668b9e6e92743371fbef7a5177744][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to the following paragraphs and regulations 106 to 108, the amount payable in respect of a particular element of the part-time grants for dependants for which the eligible part-time student qualifies is the amount of that element remaining after applying, until it is extinguished, an amount equal to ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**A − B**) as follows and in the following order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) to reduce the basic amount of the part-time adult dependants' grant where the eligible part-time student qualifies for that element under regulation 101; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) to reduce the basic amount of the part-time childcare grant for the academic year where the eligible part-time student qualifies for that element under regulation 102; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) to reduce the basic amount of the part-time parents' learning allowance where the eligible part-time student qualifies for that element under regulation 103. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) In this regulation and subject to paragraph (8)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- **A** is the aggregate of the net income of each of the eligible part-time student’s dependants; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- **B** is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (a) £1,159 where the eligible part-time student has no dependent child; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (b) £3,473 where the eligible part-time student is not a lone parent and has one dependent child; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (c) £4,632 where the eligible part-time student— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (i) is not a lone parent and has more than one dependent child; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (ii) is a lone parent and has one dependent child; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (d) £5,797 where the eligible part-time student is a lone parent and has more than one dependent child. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) Subject to paragraphs (5), (6) and (13), where B is greater than or equal to A, the basic amount of each element of the part-time grants for dependants for which the eligible part-time student qualifies is payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) Where ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**A − B**) is equal to or exceeds the aggregate of the basic amounts of the elements of the part-time grants for dependants for which the eligible part-time student qualifies, the amount payable in respect of each element is nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) The amount of the part-time adult dependants' grant calculated under paragraph (1) in respect of an adult dependant is reduced by one half where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the eligible part-time student’s partner— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) is an eligible part-time student; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] holds a statutory award; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) account is taken of that partner’s dependants in calculating the amount of support for which that partner qualifies or the payment to which that partner is entitled under the statutory award. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) The amount of the part-time childcare grant calculated under paragraph (1) is reduced by one half where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the eligible part-time student’s partner— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) is an eligible part-time student; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] holds a statutory award; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) account is taken of that partner’s dependants in calculating the amount of support for which that partner qualifies or the payment to which that partner is entitled under the statutory award. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(7) Where the amount of the part-time parents' learning allowance calculated under paragraph (1) is £0.01 or more but less than £50, the amount of part-time parents' learning allowance payable is £50. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(8) Paragraphs (9) to (12) apply where, in the course of the academic year, any of the following occurs— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) there is a change in the number of the eligible part-time student’s dependants; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) a person becomes or ceases to be a dependant of the eligible part-time student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) the eligible part-time student becomes or ceases to be a lone parent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) a student becomes an eligible part-time student as a result of an event referred to in regulation 94(4)(a), (b), (e), (f), (g), (h) or (i). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(9) For the purposes of determining the respective values of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **A** and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **B** and whether part-time adult dependants' grant or part-time parents' learning allowance is payable, the Welsh Ministers must determine the following in relation to each relevant quarter by reference to the eligible part-time student’s circumstances in the relevant quarter— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) how many dependants the eligible part-time student is to be treated as having; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) who those dependants are; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) whether the eligible part-time student is to be treated as a lone parent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(10) The amount of part-time grants for dependants for the academic year is the aggregate of the amounts of part-time adult dependants' grant and part-time parents' learning allowance calculated in respect of each relevant quarter under paragraph (11) and the amount of any part-time childcare grant for the academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(11) The amount of part-time adult dependants' grant and part-time parents' learning allowance in respect of a relevant quarter is one third of what that grant or allowance would be for the academic year if the eligible part-time student’s circumstances in the relevant quarter as determined under paragraph (9) applied for the duration of the academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(12) In this regulation, a “relevant quarter” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*chwarter perthnasol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) in the case of a person referred to in paragraph (8)(d), a quarter which begins after the relevant event occurs other than a quarter during which, in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers, the longest of any vacation occurs; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) otherwise, a quarter other than the one quarter during which, in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers, the longest of any vacation occurs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(13) A deduction may be made in accordance with regulations 106 and 107 from the amount payable in respect of a particular element of the part-time grants for dependants calculated under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
