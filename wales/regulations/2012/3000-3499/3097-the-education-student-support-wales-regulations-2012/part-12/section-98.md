# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 98

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 98. Grants for disabled part-time students' living costs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-13f481363026430f9f7ac982cb96c209][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible part-time student qualifies in accordance with this regulation for a grant for disabled part-time students' living costs to assist with the additional expenditure which the Welsh Ministers are satisfied the student is obliged to incur in connection with the eligible part-time student undertaking a designated part-time course by reason of a disability to which the eligible part-time student is subject. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) Subject to paragraph (3), the amount of grant for which an eligible part-time student qualifies under this regulation is the amount that the Welsh Ministers consider appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) The amount of the grant must not exceed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) £15,885 in respect of an academic year for expenditure on a non-medical personal helper; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) £5,332 in respect of all the academic years during the period of eligibility for expenditure on major items of specialist equipment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) the additional expenditure incurred— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) within the United Kingdom for the purpose of attending the institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] within or outside the United Kingdom for the purpose of attending, as a part of the eligible part-time student’s course, any period of study at an overseas institution or for the purpose of attending the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] Institute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51];
- (d) £1,338 in respect of an academic year for any other expenditure including expenditure incurred for the purposes referred to in sub-paragraph (a) or (b) which exceeds the specified maxima. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
