# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 96

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 96. Period of eligibility (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-defcee3c451abd57f529ffe0d414588c][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A student’s status as an eligible part-time student is retained in connection with a designated part-time course until that status terminates in accordance with this regulation or regulation 93. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The period for which an eligible part-time student retains the status referred to in paragraph (1) is the “period of eligibility” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*cyfnod cymhwystra *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”).

(3) Subject to the following paragraphs and regulation 93, the period of eligibility terminates at the end of the academic year in which the eligible part-time student completes the eligible part-time student’s designated part-time course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) The period of eligibility terminates when the eligible part-time student (“A” in this paragraph)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) withdraws from A’s designated part-time course in circumstances where the Welsh Ministers have not transferred or converted or will not transfer or convert A’s status under regulation 112 or 113; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) abandons or is expelled from A’s designated part-time course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) The period of eligibility terminates at the end of the academic year during or at the end of which it becomes impossible for the eligible part-time student to complete the designated part-time course within the period specified in regulation 95(1)(c) even if the eligible part-time student increases the eligible part-time student’s intensity of study. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) The Welsh Ministers may terminate the period of eligibility where the eligible part-time student (“A” in this paragraph) has in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers shown by A’s conduct that A is unfitted to receive support under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(7) If the Welsh Ministers are satisfied that an eligible part-time student has failed to comply with any requirement to provide information under this Part or has provided information which is inaccurate in a material particular, the Welsh Ministers may take such of the following actions as they consider appropriate in the circumstances— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) terminate the period of eligibility; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) determine that the student no longer qualifies for any particular support or particular amount of support under this Part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) treat any support paid to the student as an overpayment which may be recovered under regulation 118. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(8) Where the period of eligibility terminates— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) before the end of the academic year in which the eligible part-time student completes the designated part-time course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) otherwise than under paragraph (5), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

the Welsh Ministers may, at any time, renew, or extend the period of eligibility for such period as they determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
