# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 95

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 95. Designated part-time courses (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1c7666504afe8761e46cca4328e5c54e][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), a part-time course is designated for the purposes of section 22(1) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] and regulation 93 if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) it is a course listed in Schedule 2 other than a course for the initial training of teachers which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) began before 1 September 2010; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] begins on or after 1 September 2010 where the student transfers to the present course pursuant to regulation 8 from a course for the initial training of teachers which began before 1 September 2010; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] began on or after 1 September 2010 but before 1 September 2011 and in relation to which the student is a 2010 gap year student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) it is of at least one academic year’s duration; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) it is ordinarily possible to complete the course in not more than twice the period ordinarily required to complete the full-time equivalent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) it is wholly provided by a publicly funded educational institution in the United Kingdom or is provided by such institution in conjunction with an institution outside the United Kingdom; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (e) it is not designated by or under regulation 5; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (f) it is not designated by or under regulation 78. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) A course falling within paragraph 7 or 8 of Schedule 2 is not a designated part-time course where the governing body of a maintained school has arranged for the provision of such a course to a pupil of the school. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) A course that is taken as part of an employment -based teacher training scheme is not a designated part-time course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) For the purposes of paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) a course is provided by an institution if it provides the teaching and supervision which comprise the course, whether or not the institution has entered into an agreement with the student to provide the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) a university and any constituent college or institution in the nature of a college of a university is regarded as publicly funded if either the university or the constituent college or institution is publicly funded; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) an institution is not regarded as publicly funded by reason only that it receives public funds from the governing body of a higher education institution in accordance with section 65(3A) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00070].

(5) For the purposes of paragraph (1)(c)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) “full-time equivalent” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*cwrs llawnamser cyfatebol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means a full-time course leading to the same qualification as the part-time course in question; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the “period ordinarily required to complete the full-time equivalent” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*cyfnod y mae ei angen fel arfer i gwblhau'r cwrs llawnamser cyfatebol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) where the course is provided by or on behalf of the Open University, the period that a standard full-time student would require to complete the full-time equivalent if that student were awarded 120 credit points in each academic year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] where the course is provided by or on behalf of any other institution, the period in which a standard full-time student would complete the full-time equivalent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) “standard full-time student” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*myfyriwr llawnamser safonol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) is a student who is to be taken— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) to have started the full-time equivalent on the same date as the eligible part-time student started the part-time course in question; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] not to have been excused any part of the full-time equivalent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] not to have repeated any part of the full-time equivalent; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] not to have been absent from the full-time equivalent other than during vacations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) For the purposes of section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] and regulation 93(1) the Welsh Ministers may designate courses of higher education which are not designated under paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
