# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 110

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 110. Assistance with fees in respect of attendance on a course in England, Northern Ireland or Scotland (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7a2b0206554067f6b016be43b0e3b5f5][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may pay support under this Part to assist with fees to an eligible part-time student in connection with the eligible part-time student’s attendance on a designated part-time course in England, Northern Ireland or Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The support paid under paragraph (1) must not exceed the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the maximum amount of support that would have been payable to the eligible part-time student under regulation 97(1)(a) had the eligible part-time student been undertaking the course in Wales; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the maximum amount of support to assist with fees that in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers would have been payable to the eligible part-time student according to whether the eligible part-time student attends the designated part-time course in England, Northern Ireland or Scotland— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) pursuant to regulations made by the Secretary of State under section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] had the eligible part-time student been ordinarily resident in England and undertaking the part-time course in England; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] pursuant to regulations made under Articles 3 and 8(4) of the Education (Student Support) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 had the eligible part-time student been ordinarily resident in Northern Ireland and undertaking the part-time course in Northern Ireland; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] from funds of the Scottish Further and Higher Education Funding Council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00078] had the eligible part-time student been ordinarily resident in Scotland and undertaking the part-time course in Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
