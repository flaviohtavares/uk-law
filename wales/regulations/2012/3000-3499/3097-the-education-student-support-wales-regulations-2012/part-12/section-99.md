# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 99

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 99. Part-time grants for dependants — general (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-71a34a8161091d737379c81818c756a7][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible part-time student qualifies for part-time grants for dependants provided that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the part-time student is not excluded from qualification by any of the following paragraphs, regulation 93 or regulation 96; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the part-time student satisfies the qualifying conditions for the particular grant for which the student is applying. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) An eligible part-time student does not qualify for part-time grants for dependants if the eligible part-time student is a prisoner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

**100.**[^key-1d03e9a32f72bb2637aa2ac3b718214d] (1) The part-time grants for dependants consist of the following elements— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) part-time adult dependants' grant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) part-time childcare grant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) part-time parents' learning allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The qualifying conditions for each element are set out in regulations 101 to 108 and the amounts payable in respect of each element are determined in accordance with those regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) A deduction may be made from any element of the part-time grants for dependants in accordance with regulations 106 and 107. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
