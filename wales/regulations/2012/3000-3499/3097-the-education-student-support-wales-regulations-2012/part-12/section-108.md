# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 108

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 108. Part-time grants for dependants — final calculation (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6500ae3054c85707824aee8287750e9f][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The amount payable in respect of a particular element of the part-time grants for dependants is determined in accordance with this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The amount payable varies according to the intensity of study. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- The intensity of study is calculated as follows and expressed as a percentage *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

  [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2012/3097) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

  where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

  - PT and FT have the meanings given by regulation 97(2) and (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) In the case of part-time adult dependants' grant, where the intensity of study is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) 50 per cent or more but less than 60 per cent, the amount payable is equal to 50 per cent of the resulting amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) 60 per cent or more but less than 75 per cent, the amount payable is equal to 60 per cent of the resulting amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) 75 per cent or more, the amount payable is equal to 75 per cent of the resulting amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) For the purposes of paragraph (3), “the resulting amount” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*y swm sy'n deillio o hyn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means the amount of part-time adult dependants' grant determined in accordance with regulation 104 with deductions (if any) having been applied in accordance with regulation 107. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) In the case of part-time childcare grant, where the intensity of study is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) 50 per cent or more but less than 60 per cent, the amount payable is equal to 50 per cent of the resulting amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) 60 per cent or more but less than 75 per cent, the amount payable is equal to 60 per cent of the resulting amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) 75 per cent or more, the amount payable is equal to 75 per cent of the resulting amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) For the purposes of paragraph (5), “the resulting amount” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*y swm sy'n deillio o hyn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means the amount of part-time childcare grant determined in accordance with regulation 104 with deductions (if any) having been applied in accordance with regulation 107. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(7) In the case of part-time parents' learning allowance, where the intensity of study is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) 50 per cent or more, but less than 60 per cent, the amount payable is equal to 50 per cent of the resulting amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) 60 per cent or more but less than 75 per cent, the amount payable is equal to 60 per cent of the resulting amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) 75 per cent or more, the amount payable is equal to 75 per cent of the resulting amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(8) For the purposes of paragraph (7), “the resulting amount” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*y swm sy'n deillio o hyn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means the amount of part-time parents' learning allowance determined in accordance with regulation 104 with deductions (if any) having been applied in accordance with regulation 107. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(9) No element of part-time grants for dependants is payable where the intensity of study is less than 50 per cent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
