# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 6

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Period of eligibility (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7f726610a50bbacce811460c2967422b][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A student’s status as an eligible student is retained in connection with a designated course until that status terminates in accordance with this regulation or regulation 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The period for which an eligible student retains the status referred to in paragraph (1) is the “period of eligibility” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*cyfnod cymhwystra *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”).

(3) Subject to the following paragraphs and regulation 4, the “period of eligibility” terminates at the end of the academic year in which the student completes the designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) The period of eligibility terminates when the eligible student— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) withdraws from the eligible student’s designated course in circumstances where the Welsh Ministers have not transferred or converted or will not transfer or convert the eligible student’s status as an eligible student under regulation 8, regulation 88 or regulation 113; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) abandons or is expelled from the eligible student’s designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) The Welsh Ministers may terminate the period of eligibility where the eligible student has shown by the eligible student’s conduct that the eligible student is unfitted to receive support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) If the Welsh Ministers are satisfied that an eligible student has failed to comply with any requirement to provide information under these Regulations or has provided information which is inaccurate in a material particular, the Welsh Ministers may take such of the following actions as they consider appropriate in the circumstances— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) terminate the period of eligibility; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) determine that the student no longer qualifies for any particular support or particular amount of support under these Regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) treat any support paid to the student under these Regulations as an overpayment which may be recovered under regulations 74, 92, 118, 128 and paragraph 15 of Schedule 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(7) Where the period of eligibility terminates before the end of the academic year in which the student completes the designated course, the Welsh Ministers may, at any time, renew the period of eligibility for such period as they determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(8) Despite paragraph (1), a new system eligible student or 2006 gap year student who has not attended a previous course is only eligible for a grant for fees, fee grant, new fee grant, fee contribution loan, fee loan or a grant for living costs in respect of the present course for the number of academic years equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **OD+R+1 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**.

(9) Despite paragraph (1) and subject to paragraph (11), a new system eligible student or 2006 gap year student who has attended a previous course is only eligible for a grant for fees, fee grant, new fee grant, fee contribution loan, fee loan or a grant for living costs in respect of the present course for the number of academic years equal to ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**OD+R+1 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**) − **PC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**, except that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) no deduction equivalent to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **PC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** applies in the case of a teacher training student or a 2012 accelerated graduate entry student; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) in the case of an eligible student who did not successfully complete the latest previous course because of compelling personal reasons— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) one additional year is added; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] a further additional year may be added if the Welsh Ministers consider it appropriate to do so having regard to those reasons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(10) Paragraph (11) applies to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) a new system eligible student who is on an end-on course of the kind described in paragraph (a) or (b) of the definition of “end-on course” in regulation 2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) a new system eligible student who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) has completed a full-time course listed in paragraph 2 or 3 of Schedule 2; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] is on a full-time first degree course (other than a first degree course for the initial training of teachers) that the student did not begin immediately after the course referred to in paragraph (i); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] has not taken a full-time first degree course after the course referred to in paragraph (i) and before the present course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) a new system eligible student who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) has completed a full-time foundation degree course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] is on a full-time honours degree course that the student did not begin immediately after the course referred to in paragraph (i) and before the present course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] has not taken a full-time first degree course after the course referred to in paragraph (i) and before the present course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) an old system eligible student who is a student on an end-on course of the kind described in paragraphs (a) and (b) of the definition of “end-on course” in regulation 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(11) Despite paragraph (1), an eligible student to whom this paragraph applies is only eligible for a grant for fees, fee grant, new fee grant, fee contribution loan, fee loan or a grant for living costs in respect of the present course for the number of academic years equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **(D + X) − PrC. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**

(12) Despite paragraph (1), a continuing student is only eligible for a grant for fees, a fee contribution loan or a grant for living costs in respect of the present course for the number of academic years equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **(A+R+1) − Y**.

(13) Despite paragraph (1) and subject to paragraph (14), a transferring student is only eligible for a grant for fees, fee grant, new fee grant, fee contribution loan, fee loan or a grant for living costs in respect of the present course for the number of academic years equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **(A+R+1) − Y**.

(14) A transferring student starting the first full academic year of a further course to which the student transfers under regulation 8 on or after 1 September 2011 is only eligible for a grant for fees, fee grant, new fee grant, fee contribution loan, fee loan or a grant for living costs in respect of the further course for the number of years equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **(A+R+1) − Y − Z**.

(15) In any case where the number of academic years for which a grant for fees, fee grant, new fee grant, fee contribution loan, fee loan or a grant for living costs is available in accordance with this regulation is less than the number of academic years that make up the period ordinarily required for the completion of the present course, the academic years in which the student is eligible for such a grant or loan for fees or a grant for living costs are the latest years of the present course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(16) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) **A** is the number of academic years from 31 August 2006 that make up the period ordinarily required for the completion of the present course or, in the case of a transferring student, the previous course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) **D** is the greater of 3 and the number of academic years that make up the ordinary duration of the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) **OD *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** is the number of academic years that make up the period ordinarily required for the completion of the present course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) **PC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** is the number of years of attendance by the eligible student on a previous course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (e) **X** is 1 where the ordinary duration of the preliminary course was less than three years and 2 where the ordinary duration of the preliminary course was three years; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (f) **R** is the number of repeated academic years on the present course starting on or after 1 September 2006 that are repeats of preceding academic years that the eligible student was unable to complete successfully because of compelling personal reasons; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (g) **PrC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** is the number of academic years that the student spent on the preliminary course excluding any years of repeat study for compelling personal reasons; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (h) **Y** is the number of years of the present course, or the previous course in the case of a transferring student, in respect of which it has been determined before 1 September 2006 under regulations made under section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] that support was not available; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (i) **Z** is the number of academic years spent on a previous course beginning on or after 1 September 2006; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (j) “continuing student” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*myfyriwr sy'n parhau *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) is an old system eligible student who started the present course before 1 September 2006 and is continuing on that course after 31 August 2012; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (k) “teacher training student” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*myfyriwr ar gwrs hyfforddi athrawon *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means a student who is not a qualified teacher attending a course for the initial training of teachers where the duration of the course does not exceed 2 years and where the course is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) a full-time course; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] a part-time course (the duration of which being expressed as its full-time equivalent) and either the course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] began before 1 September 2010; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] begins on or after 1 September 2010 where the student transfers to the course pursuant to regulation 8 from a course for the initial training of teachers beginning before 1 September 2010; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] began on or after 1 September 2010 but before 1 September 2011 and in relation to which the student is a 2010 gap year student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (l) “transferring student” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*myfyriwr sy'n trosglwyddo *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means an old system eligible student who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) began a designated course on or after 1 September 2006 and is continuing on that course after 31 August 2012; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] begins a designated course on or after 1 September 2012 and whose status as an eligible student transferred to the course as a result of one or more transfers of that status by the Welsh Ministers pursuant to regulations made under section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] from a designated course that the eligible student began before 1 September 2006. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(17) In calculating the number of years for the purpose of this regulation, attendance for part of an academic year is treated as a whole academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(18) The Welsh Ministers may, at any time, renew or extend the period of eligibility for such further period as they determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(19) The Welsh Ministers may confer eligibility to a grant for fees, fee grant, new fee grant, fee contribution loan, fee loan or a grant for living costs otherwise than in accordance with paragraphs (8) to (16). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(20) For the purposes of this regulation and subject to the exceptions in paragraphs (22), (23) and (24) a “previous course” is any full-time higher education course or any part-time course for the initial training of teachers which the student began to attend or, in the case of a compressed degree course or a designated distance learning course, undertake before the present course and which meets one or both of the conditions in paragraph (21). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(21) The conditions referred to in paragraph (20) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the course is provided by an institution in the United Kingdom which was publicly funded for some or all of the academic years during which the student took the course; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) any scholarship, exhibition, bursary, grant, allowance or award of any description which was paid in respect of the student’s attending or, in the case of a compressed degree course or a designated distance learning course, undertaking the course to defray fees was from public funds or funds attributable to public funds. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(22) A course which would otherwise be a previous course will not be treated as such if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the present course is a course for the initial training of teachers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the duration of the present course does not exceed two years where the present course is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) a full-time course; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] a part-time course (the duration of which being expressed as its full-time equivalent) and either the present course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] began before 1 September 2010; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] begins on or after 1 September 2010 where the student transfers to the present course pursuant to regulation 8 from a course for the initial training of teachers beginning before 1 September 2010; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] began on or after 1 September 2010 but before 1 September 2011 and in relation to which the student is a 2010 gap year student; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) the student is not a qualified teacher. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(23) A course for the Certificate in Education which would otherwise be a previous course will not be treated as such if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the present course is a course for the degree (including an honours degree) of Bachelor of Education; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the student transferred to the present course from the course for the Certificate in Education before the completion of that course or began the present course on completion of the course for the Certificate in Education. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(24) A course for the degree (other than an honours degree) of Bachelor of Education will not be treated as a previous course if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the present course is a course for the honours degree of Bachelor of Education; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the student transferred to the present course from the course for the degree (other than an honours degree) of Bachelor of Education before the completion of that course or began the present course on completion of the course for the degree (other than an honours degree) of Bachelor of Education. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
