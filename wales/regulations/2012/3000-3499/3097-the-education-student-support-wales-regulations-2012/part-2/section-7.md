# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 7

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Previous study (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9184a191d4fb1060fb97a04ccb70ff24][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (3), (4) and (6), an eligible student who has attained an honours degree from an institution in the United Kingdom does not qualify for a grant for fees, fee grant, new fee grant, fee contribution loan or a fee loan. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) Subject to paragraphs (4) and (5), an eligible student (“A” in this paragraph) who starts A’s designated course on or after 1 September 2006 does not qualify for a loan for living costs if A has attained an honours degree from an institution in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) Paragraph (1) does not apply to an eligible student attending a designated course where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the course is a course for the initial training of teachers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the duration of the course does not exceed two years where the course is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) a full-time course; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] a part-time course (the duration of which being expressed as its full-time equivalent) and either the course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] began before 1 September 2010; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] begins on or after 1 September 2010 where the student transfers to the course pursuant to regulation 8 from a course for the initial training of teachers beginning before 1 September 2010; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] began on or after 1 September 2010 but before 1 September 2011 and in relation to which the student is a 2010 gap year student; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) the eligible student is not a qualified teacher. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) Where the present course is considered to be a single course because of regulations 5(6) and 5(7) and it leads to an honours degree from an institution in the United Kingdom being conferred on the eligible student before the final degree or equivalent qualifications, the eligible student is not prevented from qualifying for support under these Regulations by virtue of paragraph (1) or (2) in respect of any part of the single course by virtue of having that honours degree. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) Paragraph (2) does not apply where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the present course leads to qualification as a social worker, medical doctor, dentist, veterinary surgeon or architect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the eligible student is to receive any payment under a— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) healthcare bursary the amount of which is calculated by reference to the student’s income; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] Scottish healthcare allowance the amount of which is calculated by reference to the student’s income in respect of any academic year of the present course; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) the present course is a course for the initial training of teachers which is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) a full-time course; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] a part-time course which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] began before 1 September 2010; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] begins on or after 1 September 2010 where the student transfers to the present course pursuant to regulation 8 from a course for the initial training of teachers beginning before 1 September 2010; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] began on or after 1 September 2010 but before 1 September 2011 and in relation to which the student is a 2010 gap year student. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) Paragraph (1) does not apply to an eligible student attending a designated course which is an accelerated graduate entry course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
