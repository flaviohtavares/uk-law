# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 18

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18. Amount of the grant for fees at a private institution (not on behalf of a publicly funded institution): old system eligible students (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-4eeb2de8c30062eb950563eb5cb808c9][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year of a designated course at a private institution is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) £1,285; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the fees payable by the student in connection with that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) In the case of a designated course at the University of Buckingham, the amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year is £3,275. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
