# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 16

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 16. Grants for fees: qualifying conditions for old system eligible students (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-335cfa4736ada3eb0a58ce97d0542f0e][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to regulations 6 and 7, an old system eligible student qualifies in accordance with this regulation for a grant in respect of the fees for an academic year payable by the student in respect of, or otherwise in connection with, the student’s attendance on a designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year is determined in accordance with regulation 17 or 18. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) An old system eligible student does not qualify for a grant for fees in respect of an academic year of a designated course if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) that year is a bursary year or an Erasmus year; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the designated course is a flexible postgraduate ITT course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
