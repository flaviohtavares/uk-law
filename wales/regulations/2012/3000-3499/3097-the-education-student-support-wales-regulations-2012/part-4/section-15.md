# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 15

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. Events (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d83062cbf96f779f48ee2d87a79fbd1f][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

The events are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the student’s course becomes a designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the student or the student’s spouse, civil partner or parent (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) is recognised as a refugee or becomes a person with leave to enter or remain; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) a state accedes to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] where the student is a national of that state or a family member (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) of a national of that state; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) the student becomes a family member (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) of an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] EU national *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51];
- (e) the student acquires the right of permanent residence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (f) the student becomes the child of a Turkish worker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (g) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 6(1)(a) of Schedule 1; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (h) the student becomes the child of a Swiss national. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
