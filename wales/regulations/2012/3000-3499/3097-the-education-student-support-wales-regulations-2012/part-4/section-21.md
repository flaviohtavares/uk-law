# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 21

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21. General qualifying conditions for fee contribution loans and fee loans (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-1492073de22d51c9143942651df2b1c7][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible student qualifies for a fee contribution loan or a fee loan in connection with the student’s attendance on a designated course in accordance with this Part provided that the student is not excluded from qualification by the following paragraph, regulation 6 or regulation 7. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) An eligible student does not qualify for a fee contribution loan or a fee loan in respect of an academic year if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) that year is a bursary year or an Erasmus year; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the designated course is an old flexible postgraduate course for the initial training of teachers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) Paragraph (2) does not apply in relation to an accelerated graduate entry course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
