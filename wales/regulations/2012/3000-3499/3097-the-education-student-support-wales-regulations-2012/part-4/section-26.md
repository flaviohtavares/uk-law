# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 26

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. New private institution fee loan (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-125d974dcbf9e795aa1ef5d46a33ae25][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A new system eligible student who is a 2012 cohort student qualifies in accordance with this regulation for a new private institution fee loan in respect of, or otherwise in connection with, the 2012 cohort student’s attendance on a designated course provided by a private institution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) A new private institution fee loan is not available in respect of an academic year if that year is a bursary year or an Erasmus year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) The amount of a new private institution fee loan in respect of an academic year of a designated course provided by a private institution must not exceed the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) £6,000 or, where any of the circumstances in regulation 17(4) apply, £3000; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the fees payable by the student in respect of, or otherwise in connection with, that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) Where a new system eligible student has applied for a new private institution fee loan of less than the maximum amount available in relation to an academic year, the new system eligible student may apply to borrow an additional amount which, when added to the amount already applied for, does not exceed that maximum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
