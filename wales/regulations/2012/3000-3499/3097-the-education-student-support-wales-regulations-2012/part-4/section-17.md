# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 17

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Amount of grants for fees at a publicly funded institution and at a private institution on behalf of a publicly funded institution: old system eligible students (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-6d9b9ca8c8e8c0f431a4a29a58f92988][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Unless one of the circumstances set out in paragraph (4) applies, the basic amount of the grant for fees for an old system eligible student in respect of an academic year of a designated course at a publicly funded institution is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) £1,380 where the course is provided by an institution in Wales, England or Scotland; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) £1,425 where the course is provided by an institution in Northern Ireland; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) the fees payable by the old system eligible student in connection with that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The basic amount of the grant for fees for an old system eligible student in respect of an academic year of a designated course at a publicly funded institution where one of the circumstances in paragraph (4) applies is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) £680 where the course is provided by an institution in Wales, England or Scotland; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) £700 where the course is provided by an institution in Northern Ireland; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) the fees payable by the old system eligible student in connection with that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) Where a contribution exceeding nil is calculated under Schedule 5, a deduction will be made from the grant for fees determined under paragraph (1) or (2) in accordance with regulation 67. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) The circumstances are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the final academic year of the designated course where that year is ordinarily required to be completed after less than 15 weeks' attendance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) in respect of a sandwich course, an academic year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) during which any periods of full-time study are in aggregate less than 10 weeks; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] if in respect of that academic year and any previous academic years of the course the aggregate of any one or more periods of attendance which are not periods of full-time study at the institution (disregarding intervening vacations) exceeds 30 weeks; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) in respect of a course for the initial training of teachers (including a course leading to a first degree) which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) began before 1 September 2010; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] begins on or after 1 September 2010 where the student transfers to the present course pursuant to regulation 8 from a course for the initial training of teachers beginning before 1 September 2010; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] began on or after 1 September 2010 but before 1 September 2011 and in relation to which the student is a 2010 gap year student, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

  an academic year during which any periods of full-time study are in aggregate less than 10 weeks; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) in respect of a course provided in conjunction with an overseas institution, an academic year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) during which the periods of full-time study at the institution in the United Kingdom are in aggregate less than 10 weeks; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] if in respect of that academic year and any previous academic years of the course the aggregate of any one or more periods of attendance which are not periods of full-time study at the institution in the United Kingdom (disregarding intervening vacations) exceeds 30 weeks. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) In the case of a designated course at Heythrop College, the amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year is £2,465. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) In the case of a designated course at Guildhall School of Music and Drama, the amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year is £5,030. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(7) The basic amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year at a private institution providing a designated course on behalf of a publicly funded institution is the lesser of £1,285 and the fees payable by the student in connection with that year if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the designated course began on or after 1 September 2001; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the designated course is provided on behalf of a publicly funded institution; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) none of the circumstances in paragraph (4) apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(8) The basic amount of the grant for fees in respect of an academic year at a private institution providing a designated course on behalf of a publicly funded institution is the lesser of £680 and the fees payable by the old system eligible student in connection with that year if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the designated course began on or after 1 September 2001; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the designated course is provided on behalf of a publicly funded institution; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) one or more of the circumstances in paragraph (4) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(9) Where a contribution exceeding nil is calculated under Schedule 5, a deduction will be made from the amount of the grant for fees determined under paragraph (7) or (8) in accordance with regulation 67. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
