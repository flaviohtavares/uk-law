# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 20

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. New fee grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-776158e4ea571789ce9fb763d66a3921][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), a new system eligible student who is a 2012 cohort student qualifies in accordance with this regulation for a new fee grant in respect of, or otherwise in connection with, the 2012 cohort student’s attendance on a designated course provided by a publicly funded institution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) A new fee grant is not available in respect of an academic year if that year is a bursary year or an Erasmus year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) The maximum amount of new fee grant available under this regulation to a 2012 cohort student in respect of an academic year of a designated course where none of the circumstances in regulation 17(4) applies is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) £5,425; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the amount by which the fees payable by the applicant exceed £3,575. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) The maximum amount of new fee grant available in respect of such an academic year under this regulation to a 2012 cohort student where one of the circumstances in regulation 17(4) applies is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) £2,720; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the amount by which the fees payable by the application exceed £1,780. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
