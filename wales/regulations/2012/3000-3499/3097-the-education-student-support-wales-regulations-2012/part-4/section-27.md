# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 27

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. Accelerated graduate entry fee loan (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6d30a5d0f3e93fb8c4d1586e0e6442b4][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A 2012 accelerated graduate entry student qualifies in accordance with this regulation for an accelerated graduate entry fee loan in respect of, or otherwise in connection with, the 2012 accelerated graduate entry student’s attendance on a designated course which is an accelerated graduate entry course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) An accelerated graduate entry fee loan is not available in respect of an academic year which is an Erasmus year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) The amount of an accelerated graduate entry fee loan in respect of an academic year of a designated course which is an accelerated graduate entry course must not exceed the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) £5,535; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the amount by which the fees payable by the 2012 accelerated graduate entry student exceed £3,465. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) Where a 2012 accelerated graduate entry student has applied for an accelerated graduate entry fee loan of less than the maximum amount available in relation to an academic year, the 2012 accelerated graduate entry student may apply to borrow an additional amount which, when added to the amount already applied for, does not exceed that maximum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
