# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 13

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Fee Support Generally (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bccbcd93bf7514df4100222cdcc640a3][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Fee support under this Part in respect of an academic year may not exceed the fees payable by the eligible student in respect of that academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) For the purposes of calculating the amount of fee support under this Part, an institution that provides courses designated by regulation 4 of the Education (Student Support) (Dance and Drama) Regulations 1999 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00043] is not to be regarded as publicly funded by reason only that it receives public funds from the governing body of a higher education institution in accordance with section 65(3A) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00044].

(3) An eligible student to whom paragraph (4) applies is treated as if the eligible student were in attendance on the designated course for the purpose of qualifying for fee support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) This paragraph applies to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) a compressed degree student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) a disabled eligible student who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) is not a compressed degree student; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] is undertaking a designated course in the United Kingdom but is not in attendance because the eligible student is unable to attend for a reason which relates to the eligible student’s disability. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) An eligible student who is undertaking a distance learning course does not qualify for any fee support under this Part in respect of that course unless the Welsh Ministers consider that the student is undertaking the course in Wales on the first day of the first academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) An eligible student who is undertaking a distance learning course will no longer qualify for any fee support under this Part in respect of that course if the Welsh Ministers consider that the student is undertaking the course outside the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
