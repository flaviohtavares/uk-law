# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 67

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 67. Application of contribution (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a7dc158b3835940be2c4559d59495e23][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) to (7), an amount equal to the contribution or the remainder of the contribution, as the case may be, calculated under Schedule 5, is to be applied until it is extinguished against the amount of the particular grants and loans for which the eligible student qualifies as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) first, to reduce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **GFF *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**;
- (b) second, to reduce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **ADG *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**;
- (c) third, to reduce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **CCG *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**;
- (d) fourth, to reduce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **PLA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**;
- (e) fifth, to reduce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **LLC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** to no less than the minimum level for the academic year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (f) sixth, to reduce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **GFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**.

(2) In the case of an old system eligible student, subject to paragraph (4), where the basic amount of the grant for fees has been calculated in accordance with regulation 17(1) or 17(7), to determine the actual amount of grant for fees that is payable, the Welsh Ministers must apply the contribution in accordance with paragraph (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) In the case of an old system eligible student where the basic amount of the grant for fees has been calculated in accordance with regulation 17(2) or 17(8) and one of the circumstances set out in regulation 17(4)(b) or (d) applies, to determine the actual amount of grant for fees payable the Welsh Ministers must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) first, apply the contribution to reduce the basic amount of the grant for fees; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) second, if the contribution is not extinguished, deduct an amount equal to the basic amount of the grant for fees from what is left of the contribution reducing the remainder of the contribution to no less than nil; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) third, if the contribution is still not extinguished, apply the remainder first to reduce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **ADG *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** and paragraph (1) is modified accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) Where the course is a course for the initial training of teachers (other than a course for a first degree), there is no contribution applied against the basic amount of the grant for fees and the contribution is first applied to reduce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **ADG *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** and paragraph (1) is modified accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) In the case of an Erasmus year, the Welsh Ministers must apply the amount by which the contribution exceeds £1,380 first to reduce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **ADG *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** and paragraph (1) is modified accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) Where the student does not qualify for a grant for fees for any other reason, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **GFF *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** is nil and the contribution is applied first to reduce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **ADG *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** and paragraph (1) is modified accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(7) In the case of a new system eligible student, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **GFF *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** is to be treated as nil and the contribution is applied first to reduce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **ADG *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** and paragraph (1) is modified accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(8) In this regulation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) **ADG *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** is the amount, if any, of the adult dependants' grant calculated in accordance with regulation 34; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) **CCG *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** is the amount, if any, of the childcare grant calculated in accordance with regulation 34; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) **GFF *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** is the amount, if any, of grant for fees for which the eligible student qualifies under Part 4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) **GFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** is the amount of the grant for travel for which the eligible student qualifies under regulation 37, if any; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (e) **LLC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** is the amount of loan for living costs, if any, for which the eligible student (other than a student with reduced entitlement) qualifies under Part 6 to no less than the minimum level for the academic year specified in paragraph (9); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (f) **PLA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** is the amount, if any, of the parents' learning allowance calculated under regulation 34 (except the first £50 of the allowance). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(9) Subject to paragraphs (10) and (11), the “minimum level for the academic year” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*lefel isaf am y flwyddyn academaidd *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) in regulation 67(1)(e) is, in the case of a student in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) category 1, £2,990; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) category 2, £5,412; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) category 3, £4,606; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) category 4, £4,606; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (e) category 5, £3,863. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(10) Subject to paragraph (11), where the academic year in question is the final year of a course other than an intensive course, the “minimum level for the academic year” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*lefel isaf am y flwyddyn academaidd *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) is, in the case of a student in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) category 1, £2,706; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) category 2, £4,928; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) category 3, £4,005; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) category 4, £4,005; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (e) category 5, £3,578. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(11) Where different categories apply to an eligible student for different quarters of the academic year, the minimum levels in paragraphs (9) and (10) are the aggregate of the amounts determined under paragraph (11) for each of the three quarters in respect of which a loan is payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(12) The amount determined for each quarter is one third of the amount in paragraph (9) or (10) which corresponds to the rate applicable for the quarter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(13) This paragraph applies to type 1 and type 2 teacher training students who qualify for a maintenance grant and whose contribution exceeds nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(14) The loan for living costs payable in respect of an academic year to a student to whom paragraph (13) applies is calculated as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- *A − B*
- where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- *A* is the amount of loan for living costs left after applying the contribution in accordance with this Part; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- *B* is the amount of maintenance grant payable to the eligible student. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(15) Categories 1 to 5 have the meaning given in regulation 63. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
