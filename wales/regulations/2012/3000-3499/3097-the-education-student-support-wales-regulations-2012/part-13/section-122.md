# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 122

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 122. Period of eligibility (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3522611689fc8b317c0b855bab15b71c][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A student’s status as an eligible postgraduate student is retained in connection with a designated postgraduate course until that status is terminated in accordance with this regulation or regulation 119. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The period for which an eligible postgraduate student retains the status referred to in paragraph (1) is the “period of eligibility” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*cyfnod cymhwystra *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”).

(3) Subject to the following paragraphs and regulation 119, the period of eligibility terminates at the end of the period ordinarily required for completion of the designated postgraduate course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) The period of eligibility terminates when the eligible postgraduate student (“A” in this paragraph and paragraph (5))— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) withdraws from A’s designated postgraduate course in circumstances where the Welsh Ministers have not transferred or will not transfer A’s status as an eligible postgraduate student to another course under regulation 123; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) abandons or is expelled from A’s designated postgraduate course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) The Welsh Ministers may terminate the period of eligibility where A has shown by A’s conduct that A is unfitted to receive support under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) Where the eligible postgraduate student is undertaking a designated postgraduate course that is a part-time course, the period of eligibility terminates at the end of the academic year during or at the end of which it becomes impossible for the eligible postgraduate student to complete the course within the period specified in regulation 121(1)(b)(ii). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(7) If the Welsh Ministers are satisfied that an eligible postgraduate student has failed to comply with any requirement to provide information under this Part or has provided information which is inaccurate in a material particular, the Welsh Ministers may take such of the following actions as they consider appropriate in the circumstances— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) terminate the period of eligibility; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) determine that the student no longer qualifies for a grant or any particular amount of grant under this Part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) treat any support paid to the student as an overpayment which may be recovered under regulation 128. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(8) Where the period of eligibility terminates on or before the expiry of the period ordinarily required for the completion of the designated postgraduate course, the Welsh Ministers may, at any time, renew the period of eligibility for such periods as they determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
