# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 124

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 124. Applications for support (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-357eb772d07bd58d65f058d6364e2959][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person must apply for a grant under this Part in connection with each academic year of a designated postgraduate course by completing and submitting to the Welsh Ministers an application in such form and accompanied by such documentation as the Welsh Ministers may require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The application must reach the Welsh Ministers as soon as is reasonably practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) The Welsh Ministers may take such steps and make such inquiries as they consider necessary to determine whether the applicant is an eligible postgraduate student, whether the applicant qualifies for a grant and the amount of grant payable, if any. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) The Welsh Ministers must notify the applicant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) whether the applicant qualifies for a grant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) if the applicant does qualify, the amount payable in respect of the academic year, if any; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) how that amount is allocated between the types of eligible expenditure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
