# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 126

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 126. Amount of grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-3fd7903951158d685e5693afb1568a3c][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the grant payable to an eligible postgraduate student under this Part is such amount as the Welsh Ministers consider appropriate to assist with one or more types of eligible expenditure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The grant must not exceed £10,590 in respect of an academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) For the purposes of this Part, the “types of eligible expenditure” are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) expenditure on a non-medical helper; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) expenditure on major items of specialist equipment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) additional expenditure incurred— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) within the United Kingdom for the purpose of attending the institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] within or outside the United Kingdom for the purpose of attending, as part of the course, any period of study at an overseas institution or for the purposes of attending the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] Institute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51].
