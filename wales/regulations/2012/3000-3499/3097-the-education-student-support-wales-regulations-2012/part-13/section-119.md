# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 119

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 119. Eligible postgraduate students (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f9248cea975b34b0e91a167abd922f8e][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible postgraduate student qualifies, subject to and in accordance with this Part, for a grant to assist with the additional expenditure which the Welsh Ministers are satisfied the eligible postgraduate student is obliged to incur by reason of a disability to which the eligible postgraduate student is subject in respect of the eligible postgraduate student undertaking a designated postgraduate course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) A person is an eligible postgraduate student in connection with a designated postgraduate course if that person satisfies the conditions in paragraph (3) and is not excluded by paragraph (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) The conditions referred to in paragraph (2) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the Welsh Ministers, in assessing a person’s application for support under regulation 124, have determined in connection with the designated postgraduate course that the person falls within one of the categories set out in Part 2 of Schedule 1; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the Welsh Ministers are satisfied that, by reason of a disability to which the person is subject, the person will be obliged to incur additional expenditure in respect of undertaking the course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) Subject to paragraph (9), a person (“A” in this paragraph) is not an eligible postgraduate student if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) there has been bestowed on or paid to A in relation to A undertaking the course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) a healthcare bursary; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] any allowance under the Nursing and Midwifery Student Allowances (Scotland) Regulations 2007; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] any allowance, bursary or award of similar description made by a Research Council; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] any allowance, bursary or award of similar description made by A’s institution which includes any payment for the purpose of meeting additional expenditure incurred by A by reason of A’s disability; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (v) any allowance, bursary or award of similar description made under section 67(4)(a) of the Care Standards Act 2000 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00079] which includes payment for meeting additional expenditure incurred by A by reason of A’s disability; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) A is in breach of an obligation to repay any loan; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) A has reached the age of 18 and has not ratified any agreement for a loan made with A when A was under the age of 18; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) A has, in the opinion of the Welsh Ministers, shown by A’s conduct that A is unfitted to receive support under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) For the purposes of paragraphs (4)(b) and (4)(c), “loan” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*benthyciad *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means a loan made under the student loans legislation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) In a case where the agreement for a loan is subject to the law of Scotland, paragraph (4)(c) only applies if the agreement was made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) before the 25 September 1991; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) with the concurrence of the borrower’s curator or at a time when the borrower had no curator. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(7) An eligible postgraduate student does not qualify for a grant under this Part if the only paragraph in Part 2 of Schedule 1 into which the eligible postgraduate student falls is paragraph 9. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(8) Save where the circumstances described in regulation 126(3)(c)(ii) apply such that an eligible postgraduate student undertakes part of that student’s course overseas, an eligible postgraduate student does not qualify for a grant under this Part unless the eligible postgraduate student is undertaking the course in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(9) Subject to paragraphs (11) to (13) and despite paragraphs (3)(a) and (4), a person is an eligible postgraduate student for the purposes of this Part if the person satisfies the conditions in paragraph (3)(b) and paragraph (10)(a) or (b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(10) The conditions referred to in paragraph (9) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) person qualified as an eligible postgraduate student in connection with an earlier academic year of the present postgraduate course pursuant to regulations made by the Welsh Ministers under section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51];
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] person was ordinarily resident in Wales on the first day of the first academic year of the present postgraduate course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] person’s status as an eligible postgraduate student has not terminated; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) Welsh Ministers have previously determined that the person is an eligible postgraduate student in connection with a designated postgraduate course other than the present postgraduate course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] person’s status as an eligible postgraduate student in connection with the course in sub-paragraph (b)(i) has been transferred from that course to the present course as a result of one or more transfers in accordance with regulations made by the Welsh Ministers under section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 1998 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51];
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] person was ordinarily resident in Wales on the first day of the first academic year of the course referred to in sub-paragraph (b)(i); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] person’s status as an eligible postgraduate student has not terminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(11) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the Welsh Ministers have determined that, by virtue of being a refugee or the spouse, civil partner, child or step-child of a refugee, a person (“A” in this paragraph) was an eligible postgraduate student in connection with an application for support for an earlier year of the present postgraduate course or an application in connection with another designated postgraduate course from which A’s status as an eligible postgraduate student has been transferred to the present postgraduate course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) as at the day before the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support starts, the refugee status of A or of A’s spouse, civil partner, parent (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) or step-parent has expired and no further leave to remain has been granted and no appeal is pending (within the meaning of section 104 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

A’s status as an eligible postgraduate student terminates immediately before the first day of the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(12) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the Welsh Ministers have determined that, by virtue of being a refugee or the spouse, civil partner, child or step-child of a refugee, a person (“A” in this paragraph) was an eligible postgraduate student in connection with an application for support for an earlier year of the present postgraduate course or an application in connection with another designated postgraduate course from which A’s status as an eligible postgraduate student has been transferred to the present postgraduate course; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) as at the day before the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support, the period for which the person with leave to enter or remain is allowed to stay in the United Kingdom has expired and no further leave to remain has been granted and no appeal is pending (within the meaning of section 104 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

A’s status as an eligible postgraduate student terminates immediately before the first day of the academic year in respect of which A is applying for support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(13) Paragraphs (11) and (12) do not apply where the student began the course in connection with which the Welsh Ministers determined that the student was an eligible postgraduate student before 1 September 2007. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(14) An eligible postgraduate student does not, at any one time, qualify for support for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) more than one designated postgraduate course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) a designated postgraduate course and a designated distance learning course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) a designated postgraduate course and a designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) a designated postgraduate course and a designated part-time course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
