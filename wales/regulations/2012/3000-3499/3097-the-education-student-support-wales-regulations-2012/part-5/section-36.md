# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 36

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. Interpretation of regulations 37 to 39 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c31db505cd1d68fffad29d6620c708aa][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of regulations 37 to 39— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) any reference to expenditure incurred for the purpose of attending an institution or period of study or period of overseas work placement in an Erasmus year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) includes expenditure both before and after so attending; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] does not include any expenditure in respect of which a grant is payable under regulation 29, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) “qualifying quarter” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*chwarter cymhwysol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means a quarter during which the eligible student attends as part of the eligible student’s course an overseas institution, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] Institute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] or overseas work placement in an Erasmus year for at least half the period covered by that quarter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
