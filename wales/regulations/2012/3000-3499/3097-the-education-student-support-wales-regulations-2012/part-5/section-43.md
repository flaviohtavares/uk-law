# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 43

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43. Maintenance grant — new system eligible students who are 2010 cohort students or 2012 cohort students (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0ab18838d981012dca525604e1606d2d][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The maximum amount of maintenance grant available to a new system eligible student who is a 2010 cohort student or a 2012 cohort student in respect of an academic year is £5,161. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) A new system eligible student who is a 2010 cohort student or a 2012 cohort student and who qualifies for a maintenance grant in respect of an academic year receives an amount as follows in respect of that year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) where the household income is £18,370 or less, the eligible student receives £5,161; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) where the household income exceeds £18,370 but does not exceed £26,500, the eligible student receives an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **M − A**, where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **M** is £5,161 and A is £1 for every £3.653 by which the household income exceeds £18,370; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) where the household income exceeds £26,500 but does not exceed £34,000, the eligible student receives an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **RM − A *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**, where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **RM *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** is £2,936 and A is £1 for every £4.18 by which the household income exceeds £26,500; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) where the household income exceeds £34,000 but does not exceed £50,020, the eligible student receives an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **SM − A *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]**, where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] **SM *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]** is £1,142 and A is £1 for every £14.67 by which the household income exceeds £34,000; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (e) where the household income is £50,020, the eligible student receives £50; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (f) where the household income exceeds £50,020, no maintenance grant is payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
