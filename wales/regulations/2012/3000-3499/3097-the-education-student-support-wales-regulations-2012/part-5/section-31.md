# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 31

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31. Grants for dependants — adult dependants' grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-07f42ed34a3be0286d41f8ade6478df1][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible student qualifies for an adult dependants' grant in connection with the eligible student’s attendance on a designated course in accordance with this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The adult dependants' grant is available in respect of one dependant of an eligible student who is either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the eligible student’s partner; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) an adult dependant of the eligible student whose net income does not exceed £3,923. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) The amount of adult dependants' grant payable in respect of an academic year is calculated in accordance with regulation 34, the basic amount being— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) £2,732; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) where the person in respect of whom the eligible student is applying for adult dependants' grant is ordinarily resident outside the United Kingdom, such amount not exceeding £2,732 as the Welsh Ministers consider reasonable in the circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
