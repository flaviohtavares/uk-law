# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 28

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. General qualifying conditions for grants for living costs (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bd10e05f78f415b03c5090576628f831][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible student qualifies for a grant under this Part provided that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the eligible student is not excluded from qualification by any of the following paragraphs, regulation 6 or regulation 7; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the eligible student satisfies the qualifying conditions for the particular grant for which the eligible student is applying. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) An eligible student does not qualify for a grant under this Part if the only paragraph in Part 2 of Schedule 1 into which the eligible student falls is paragraph 9. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) An eligible student does not qualify for a grant under this Part in respect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) an academic year which is a bursary year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) an academic year of a course for the initial training of teachers which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) began before 1 September 2010; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] begins on or after 1 September 2010 where the eligible student transfers to the present course pursuant to regulation 8 from a course for the initial training of teachers beginning before 1 September 2010; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] began on or after 1 September 2010 but before 1 September 2011 and in relation to which the student was a 2010 gap year student, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

  during which the periods of full-time attendance, including attendance for the purpose of teaching practice, are in aggregate less than 6 weeks; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) a flexible postgraduate ITT course which is of less than one academic year’s duration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) Paragraph (3)(b) does not apply for the purposes of qualification for a grant for disabled students' living costs under regulation 29. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) Subject to paragraph (6), a 2012 accelerated graduate entry student does not qualify for a grant under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) Paragraph (5) does not apply for the purposes of regulations 29 to 35, to a 2012 accelerated graduate entry student who is undertaking their first year of study on an accelerated graduate entry course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(7) An eligible student does not qualify for a grant under this Part in respect of any academic year of a sandwich course where the periods of full-time study are in aggregate less than 10 weeks unless the periods of work experience constitute unpaid service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(8) For the purposes of paragraph (7), “unpaid service” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*gwasanaeth di-dâl *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) unpaid service in a hospital or in a public health service laboratory or with a primary care trust in the United Kingdom; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) unpaid service with a local authority in the United Kingdom acting in the exercise of their functions relating to the care of children and young persons, health or welfare or with a voluntary organisation providing facilities or carrying out activities of a like nature in the United Kingdom; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) unpaid service in the prison or probation and aftercare service in the United Kingdom; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) unpaid research in an institution in the United Kingdom or, in the case of an eligible student attending an overseas institution as part of the eligible student’s course, in an overseas institution; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (e) unpaid service with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) a Strategic Health Authority established pursuant to section 13 of the National Health Service Act 2006 or a Special Health Authority established pursuant to section 28 of that Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00045];
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] a Local Health Board established pursuant to section 11 of the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006 or a Special Health Authority established pursuant to section 22 of that Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00046];
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] a Health Board or a Special Health Board constituted under section 2 of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00047];
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] the Regional Health and Social Care Board or the Regional Agency for Public Health and Social Well-being established under sections 7 and 12 of the Health and Social Care (Reform) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00048];
  - (v) the National Health Service Commissioning Board established under section 1H of the National Health Service Act 2006 or a Clinical Commissioning Group established under section 1I of that Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00049]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (vi (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] the National Institute for Health and Care Excellence established under section 232 of the Health and Social Care Act 2012 or the Health and Social Care Information Centre established under section 252 of that Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^f00050].

(9) Subject to paragraph (10), an eligible student does not qualify for a grant under regulations 40 to 48 in respect of an academic year of a designated course if the student does not qualify for relevant support in respect of that academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(10) Paragraph (9) does not apply if the reason that the student does not qualify for relevant support in respect of an academic year of a designated course is because— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) that academic year is an Erasmus year; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the designated course is an old flexible postgraduate course for the initial training of teachers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(11) In paragraph (9) “relevant support” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*cymorth perthnasol *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means, in the case of a grant under regulation 40, a grant for fees, or, in the case of a grant under regulations 41 to 48, a fee loan. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(12) Where one of the events listed in paragraph (13) occurs in the course of an academic year, a student may qualify for a particular grant in accordance with this Part in respect of all or part of that academic year but that student does not qualify for a grant in respect of any academic year beginning before the academic year in which the relevant event occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(13) The events are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the student’s course becomes a designated course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the student, the student’s spouse, civil partner or parent (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) is recognised as a refugee or becomes a person with leave to enter or remain; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) the state of which the student is a national accedes to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] European Union *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] where the student has been ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] Islands *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] throughout the three-year period immediately preceding the first day of the first academic year of the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) the student acquires the right of permanent residence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (e) the student becomes the child of a Turkish worker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (f) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 6(1)(a) of Schedule 1; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (g) the student becomes the child of a Swiss national. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(14) Subject to paragraph (15), an eligible student does not qualify for a grant under this Part if the eligible student is a prisoner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(15) Paragraph (14) does not apply in respect of a grant for disabled students' living costs which is payable in connection with a designated course beginning before 1 September 2012. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(16) A student to whom this paragraph applies is treated as being in attendance on the designated course for the purpose of qualifying for the following grants— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) grants for dependants; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) grant for disabled students' living costs; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) maintenance grant or special support grant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) higher education grant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(17) Paragraph (16) applies to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) a compressed degree student; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) a disabled eligible student who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) is not a compressed degree student; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] is undertaking a designated course in the United Kingdom but is not in attendance because the eligible student is unable to attend for a reason which relates to the eligible student’s disability; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) an eligible student on a period of study or a period of work placement in an Erasmus year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(18) An eligible student who is undertaking a distance learning course does not qualify for any grant under this Part other than (where appropriate) a grant for disabled students' living costs pursuant to regulation 29. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
