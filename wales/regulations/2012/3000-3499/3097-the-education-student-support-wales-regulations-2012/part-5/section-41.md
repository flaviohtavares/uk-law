# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 41

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Maintenance grant (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e3b90164af9ab974cfa49b8dddb7aeaf][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A new system eligible student who is not a new cohort student qualifies in accordance with regulation 42 for a maintenance grant for living costs in connection with that eligible student’s attendance on a designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) A new system eligible student who is a 2010 cohort student or a 2012 cohort student qualifies in accordance with regulation 43 for a maintenance grant for living costs in connection with that eligible student’s attendance on a designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) A new system eligible student who is a 2011 cohort student qualifies in accordance with regulation 44 for a maintenance grant for living costs in connection with that eligible student’s attendance on a designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) A new system eligible student does not qualify for a maintenance grant if that eligible student qualifies for a special support grant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
