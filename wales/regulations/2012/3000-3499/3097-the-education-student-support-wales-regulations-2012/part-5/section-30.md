# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 30

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30. Grants for dependants — general (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-2c97c767bbbf4a791af6aabc51755908][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The grants for dependants consist of the following elements— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) adult dependants' grant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) childcare grant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) parents' learning allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) The qualifying conditions for each element and the amounts payable are set out in regulations 31 to 34. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) A deduction may be made from any element of the grants for dependants in accordance with regulation 67. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
