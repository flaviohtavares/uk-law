# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 29

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29. Grants for disabled students' living costs (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-54760d6531dd9bc71c97f6644958df21][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible student qualifies in accordance with this regulation for a grant for disabled students' living costs to assist with the additional expenditure which the Welsh Ministers are satisfied the eligible student is obliged to incur by reason of a disability to which the student is subject in connection with the eligible student’s attendance on, or undertaking of a designated course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) Subject to the following paragraphs, the amount of grant for disabled students' living costs under this regulation is the amount that the Welsh Ministers consider appropriate in accordance with the eligible student’s circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(3) Except where paragraph (5) applies, the amount of the grant for disabled students' living costs must not exceed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) £21,181 in respect of an academic year for expenditure on a non-medical personal helper; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) £5,332 in respect of all the academic years during the period of eligibility for expenditure on major items of specialist equipment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) the additional expenditure incurred— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) within the United Kingdom for the purpose of attending the institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] within or outside the United Kingdom for the purpose of attending, as a part of the eligible student’s course, any period of study at an overseas institution or for the purpose of attending the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] Institute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51];
- (d) £1,785 in respect of an academic year for any other expenditure including expenditure incurred for the purposes referred to in sub-paragraph (a) or (b) which exceeds the maxima specified in those paragraphs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(4) Where the eligible student has received payments to assist with expenditure on major items of specialist equipment in connection with the course by virtue of holding a transitional award, the maximum amount of grant under paragraph (3)(b) is reduced by the amount of those payments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(5) The maximum amount of grant under paragraphs (3)(a) and (3)(d) is £15,885 and £1,338, respectively where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) an eligible student attends a course for the initial training of teachers which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) began before 1 September 2010; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] begins on or after 1 September 2010 where the student transfers to the present course pursuant to regulation 8 from a course for the initial training of teachers beginning before 1 September 2010; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] began on or after 1 September 2010 but before 1 September 2011 and in relation to which the student is a 2010 gap year student; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) in any academic year of that course, the periods of full-time study and full-time teaching practice are in aggregate less than 6 weeks. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(6) An eligible student does not qualify for a grant for disabled students' living costs under this regulation in respect of a distance learning course unless the Welsh Ministers consider that the student is undertaking the course in Wales on the first day of the first academic year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(7) An eligible student who is undertaking a distance learning course will no longer qualify for a grant for disabled students' living costs under this regulation in respect of that course if the Welsh Ministers consider that the student is undertaking the course outside the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
