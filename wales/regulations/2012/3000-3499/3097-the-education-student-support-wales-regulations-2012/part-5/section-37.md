# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 37

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Qualifying conditions for the grant for travel (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-186a7288dd95487a1286f17a19b5d9e9][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A grant is available to an eligible student attending a course in medicine or dentistry (a necessary part of which is a period of study by way of clinical training) in respect of the reasonable expenditure which the eligible student is obliged to incur in an academic year for the purpose of attending in connection with the eligible student’s course any hospital or other premises in the United Kingdom (not comprised in the institution) at which facilities for clinical training are provided other than expenditure incurred for the purpose of residential study away from the institution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) A grant is available to an eligible student in respect of the reasonable expenditure which the eligible student is obliged to incur in each qualifying quarter within or outside the United Kingdom for the purpose of attending as part of the eligible student’s course an overseas institution, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] Institute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] or overseas work placement in an Erasmus year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
