# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 129

(1) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] 2011 (No. 2) Regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] are amended in accordance with the following paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

(2) In regulation 25(6)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) at the end of sub-paragraph (e)(iii) omit “or”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) at the end of sub-paragraph (e)(iv) for “.” substitute “;”; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
