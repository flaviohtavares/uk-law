# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, section 75

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 75. Payments — interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-2266c971818c906b4a8012396144c379][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) “attendance confirmation” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*cadarnhad o bresenoldeb *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means confirmation in writing from the academic authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (i) that the eligible student has enrolled for the academic year where the eligible student— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] is applying for support under these Regulations in connection with a designated course for the first time; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] has a disability; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] is undertaking the course but not attending (regardless of whether the reason for not attending relates to the eligible student’s disability); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] that the eligible student has been present at the institution and begun to attend the course where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] the student is applying for support under these Regulations in connection with a designated course for the first time; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] the student’s status as an eligible student has not been transferred to the course from another designated course at the same institution; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (cc (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] sub-paragraph (i)(cc) does not apply; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] that the eligible student has enrolled for the academic year where the eligible student is applying for support in connection with a designated course— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] other than for the first time; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
    - (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51] for the first time after the student’s status as an eligible student has been transferred to that course from another course at the same institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) “payment period” (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*cyfnod talu *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]*”) means a period in respect of which the Welsh Ministers pay the relevant support under Part 5 or Part 6 or would have paid such support if the eligible student’s period of eligibility had not terminated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
