# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2012, SCHEDULE 3

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 3 (affected by repeal, see note) — INFORMATION (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51][^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations 11, 86, 111 and 125 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

**1.**[^c-eaa37337c5f812f1d86f3db7498b0240] Every applicant, eligible student, eligible distance learning student, eligible part-time student and eligible postgraduate student must, as soon as reasonably practicable after being requested to do so, provide the Welsh Ministers with such information as the Welsh Ministers consider they require for the purposes of these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

**2.**[^c-7bd7977aa8a9e7bb67eddc3df5db0597] Every applicant, eligible student, eligible distance learning student, eligible part-time student and eligible postgraduate student must forthwith inform the Welsh Ministers and provide them with particulars if any of the following occurs— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

- (a) the applicant or student withdraws from, abandons or is expelled from their course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (b) the applicant or student transfers to any other course at the same or at a different institution; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (c) the applicant or student ceases to undertake their course and does not intend to or is not permitted to continue it for the remainder of the academic year; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (d) the applicant or student is absent from their course for more than 60 days due to illness or for any period for any other reason; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (e) the month for the start or completion of the course changes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
- (f) the applicant’s or student’s home or term-time address or telephone number changes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]

**3.**[^c-9d8659202f4338e7a6df8b3bdf6dd4b5] Information provided to the Welsh Ministers under these Regulations must be in the format that the Welsh Ministers require and, if they require the information to be signed by the person providing it, an electronic signature in such form as the Welsh Ministers may specify satisfies such a requirement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-640cffab865051583171c3072f59bd51]
