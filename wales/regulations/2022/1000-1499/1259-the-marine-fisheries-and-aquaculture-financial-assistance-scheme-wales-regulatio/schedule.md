# The Marine, Fisheries and Aquaculture (Financial Assistance) Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2022, SCHEDULE

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## SCHEDULE — Activities for which the Welsh Ministers may pay grants and make loans under this Scheme

Regulation 4(1)

### PART 1

#### 1. For the purpose of the conservation, enhancement or restoration of the marine and aquatic environment

Activities which promote innovation.

**2.** Activities relating to professional advisory services.

**3.** Activities which promote human capital and networking.

**4.** Activities which mitigate the effects of adverse extenuating circumstances.

**5.** Activities which establish or improve infrastructure for marine and freshwater users.

**6.** Activities which contribute to the sustainable development of aquaculture sites.

**7.** Activities which reduce the impact of seafood production on the marine environment.

**8.** Activities which contribute towards conservation, restoration or enhancement of aquatic ecosystems and biodiversity.

**9.** Activities which contribute to the design and implementation of conservation measures.

**10.** Activities which support marine spatial planning for the sustainable use of marine and coastal resources.

**11.** Activities which contribute towards the mitigation of climate change or its effects.

**12.** Activities which maintain or improve animal health and welfare.
