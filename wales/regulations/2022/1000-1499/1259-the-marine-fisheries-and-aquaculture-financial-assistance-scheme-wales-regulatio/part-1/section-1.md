# The Marine, Fisheries and Aquaculture (Financial Assistance) Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2022, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Title, commencement, extent and application

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Marine, Fisheries and Aquaculture (Financial Assistance) Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2022.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1 December 2022.

(3) These Regulations—

- (a) extend to England and Wales;
- (b) apply in relation to—
  - (i) Wales;
  - (ii) the Welsh zone;
  - (iii) Welsh fishing boats.
