# The Marine, Fisheries and Aquaculture (Financial Assistance) Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2022, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. Variation, suspension and revocation of approval

(1) The Welsh Ministers may, at any time before the grant or loan has been paid in full, suspend or revoke the approval, in whole or in part, or vary a condition of the approval (including adding or removing conditions).

(2) The Welsh Ministers must notify the applicant of a decision made under paragraph (1).
