# The Marine, Fisheries and Aquaculture (Financial Assistance) Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2022, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Decision and notification

(1) The Welsh Ministers must—

- (a) reject or refuse to approve an application, or
- (b) approve an application in whole or in part, unconditionally or subject to such conditions as they may determine.

(2) The Welsh Ministers must notify the applicant of the decision made under paragraph (1).
