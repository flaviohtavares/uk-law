# The Marine, Fisheries and Aquaculture (Financial Assistance) Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2022, section 4

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 4. Payments of grants and making of loans

(1) The Welsh Ministers may, in accordance with this Scheme, pay grants or make loans, in respect of the activities listed in the Schedule to these Regulations.

(2) The Welsh Ministers may pay a grant or make a loan under paragraph (1) to any person—

- (a) who has applied for a grant or a loan, and
- (b) whose application the Welsh Ministers have approved.
