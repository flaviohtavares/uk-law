# The Marine, Fisheries and Aquaculture (Financial Assistance) Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2022, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Method of payment

The Welsh Ministers may pay a grant or make a loan under this Scheme by such instalments and at such time as they may determine.
