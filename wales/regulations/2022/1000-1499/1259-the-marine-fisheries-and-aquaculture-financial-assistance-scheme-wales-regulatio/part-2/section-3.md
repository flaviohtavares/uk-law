# The Marine, Fisheries and Aquaculture (Financial Assistance) Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2022, section 3

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 3. Establishment of the Scheme

(1) These Regulations establish a Scheme for the giving of financial assistance by the Welsh Ministers under paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 6 to the Fisheries Act 2020.

(2) Part 3 of these Regulations constitutes the Scheme.
