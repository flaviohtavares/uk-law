# The Seed (Equivalence) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 1276 (W. 259)

Seeds, Wales

The Seed (Equivalence) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2022

Made

5 December 2022

Laid before Senedd Cymru

7 December 2022

Coming into force

30 December 2022

The Welsh Ministers in whom the powers conferred by section 16(1) to (4) of the Plant Varieties and Seeds Act 1964[^f00001] are now vested[^f00002], make the following Regulations.

The Welsh Ministers have consulted with representatives of such interests as appear to the Welsh Ministers to be concerned with these Regulations in accordance with section 16(1) of that Act.

## 1. Title, application and commencement

The title of these Regulations is the Seed (Equivalence) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2022, they apply in relation to Wales and they come into force on 30 December 2022.

## 2. Amendment of Council Decision [2003/17/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2003/0017)

(1) Council Decision 2003/17 on the equivalence of field inspections carried out in third countries on seed-producing crops and on the equivalence of seed produced in third countries[^f00003]is amended as follows.

(2) In Article 6, for “31 December 2022” substitute “31 December 2029”.

Lesley Griffiths

Minister for Rural Affairs and North Wales, and Trefnydd, one of the Welsh Ministers

5 December 2022

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend Article 6 of Council Decision 2003/17 of 16 December 2002 on the equivalence of field inspections carried out in third countries on seed-producing crops and on the equivalence of seed produced in third countries to extend the expiry date of this Decision.
