# The Curriculum and Assessment (Wales) Act 2021 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2022

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2022 No. 12 (W. 6) (C. 1)

Education, Wales

The Curriculum and Assessment (Wales) Act 2021 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2022

Made

6 January 2022

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 84(2), (3) and (4) of the Curriculum and Assessment (Wales) Act 2021[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Title and interpretation

(1) The title of this Order is the Curriculum and Assessment (Wales) Act 2021 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2022.

(2) In this Order, “**the Act**” means the Curriculum and Assessment (Wales) Act 2021.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 11 January 2022

The following provisions of the Act come into force on 11 January 2022—

- (a) section 56(1) of the Act for the purposes of making regulations under that section, and
- (b) section 57(1) of the Act for the purposes of issuing a direction under that section.

## 3. Provisions coming into force on 1 September 2022

The following provisions of the Act come into force on 1 September 2022—

- (a) section 56 to the extent it is not already in force, and
- (b) section 57 to the extent it is not already in force.

Jeremy Miles

Minister for Education and Welsh Language, one of the Welsh Ministers

6 January 2022

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force provisions of the Curriculum and Assessment (Wales) Act 2021 (“**the Act**”).

This Order provides that the provisions listed in article 2 come into force on 11 January 2022 but only for the purposes of making regulations under section 56(1) of the Act and for the purposes of issuing a direction under section 57(1) of the Act.

Article 3 of this Order provides that sections 56 and 57 come into force to the extent they are not already in force on 1 September 2022.
