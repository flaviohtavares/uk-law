# The Child Minding and Day Care (Wales) Regulations 2010, section 13

> Text as at 2022-12-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Compliance with regulations – more than one registered person

*Extent: E+W*

Where there is more than one registered person in respect of the provision of day care for relevant children on the same premises anything which is required under these Regulations to be done by the registered person will, if done by one of the registered persons, not be required to be done by any of the other registered persons.
