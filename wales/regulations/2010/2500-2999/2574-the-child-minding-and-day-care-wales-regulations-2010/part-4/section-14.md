# The Child Minding and Day Care (Wales) Regulations 2010, section 14

> Text as at 2022-12-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. National minimum standards

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The registered person must have regard to the national minimum standards which relate to the type of care provided by the registered person.

(2) Any allegation that the registered person has failed to comply with paragraph (1) may taken into account in the exercise by the Welsh Ministers of their functions under Part 2 of the Measure and in any proceedings under that Part of the Measure.
