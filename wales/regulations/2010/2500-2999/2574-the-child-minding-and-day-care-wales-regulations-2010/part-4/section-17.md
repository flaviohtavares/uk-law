# The Child Minding and Day Care (Wales) Regulations 2010, section 17

> Text as at 2022-12-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Assessment of service

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may at any time request the registered person to undertake an assessment of the service provided by that person to relevant children.

(2) Within 28 days of receiving a request under paragraph (1), the registered person must supply to the appropriate office the assessment in the form required by the Welsh Ministers.

(3) The registered person must take reasonable steps to ensure that the assessment is not misleading nor inaccurate.
