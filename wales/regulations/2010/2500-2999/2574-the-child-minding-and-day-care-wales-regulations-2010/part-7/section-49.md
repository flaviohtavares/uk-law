# The Child Minding and Day Care (Wales) Regulations 2010, section 49

> Text as at 2022-12-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Saving

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the revocation of the 2004 Regulations will not affect—

- (a) any decision made;
- (b) any notification of suspension;
- (c) any period of suspension or limitation or time; or
- (d) any decision made by the First-tier Tribunal,
- (e) under or in accordance with the 2004 Regulations.

(2) In relation to the revocation of the 2004 Regulations, legal proceedings may be brought, or continued and remedies or penalties may be imposed and enforced in relation to acts done under, in relation to, or in contravention of any provision made by the 2004 Regulations before the relevant date, as if the revocation in regulation 48 had not been made.
