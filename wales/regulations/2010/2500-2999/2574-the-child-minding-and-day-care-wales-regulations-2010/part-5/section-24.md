# The Child Minding and Day Care (Wales) Regulations 2010, section 24

> Text as at 2022-12-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Health needs of children

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The registered person must promote and protect the health of relevant children.

(2) In particular the registered person must ensure that—

- (a) each child is provided with such individual support as may be required in the light of any particular health needs or disability of the child; and
- (b) at all times, at least one person caring for relevant children has a suitable first aid qualification.
