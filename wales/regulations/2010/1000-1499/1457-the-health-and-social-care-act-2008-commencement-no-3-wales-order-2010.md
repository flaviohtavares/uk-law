# The Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Commencement No. 3) (Wales) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 1457 (W.130) (C.83)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, WALES

The Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Commencement No. 3) (Wales) Order 2010

Made

19 May 2010

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 161(3) and (4) and 170(3) of the Health and Social Care Act 2008[^f00001] make the following Order:

## 1. Title and application

(1) The title of this Order is the Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Commencement No 3) (Wales) Order 2010.

(2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

## 2. Appointed day

21 May 2010 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Health and Social Care Act 2008—

- (a) section 140, in so far as it relates to Part 2 of Schedule 12;
- (b) section 166, in so far as it relates to the repeals mentioned in paragraph (d);
- (c) Part 2 of Schedule 12; and
- (d) the repeals included in Part 4 of Schedule 15 that relate to sections 174 to 176 of, and Schedule 8 to, the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^f00002].

Edwina Hart

Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers

19 May 2010

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 21 May 2010 provisions of the Health and Social Care Act 2008 (“the Act”) relating to the funding of expenditure on community pharmaceutical services provided as part of the National Health Service in Wales. The principal changes are: firstly, that funding for community pharmaceutical services that previously had been held centrally will become part of the allocations given to Local Health Boards; and secondly, the Welsh Ministers are given powers to designate any element of the remuneration paid by Local Health Boards for community pharmaceutical services which is not referable to the cost of drugs. Designation is a mechanism which allows the Welsh Ministers to control the way the funding of a designated element is apportioned between Local Health Boards.
