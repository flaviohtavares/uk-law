# The Policing and Crime Act 2009 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 1375 (W.120) (C.82)

LOCAL GOVERNMENT, WALES

LICENCES AND LICENSING, WALES

The Policing and Crime Act 2009 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2010

Made

27 April 2010

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 116(4)(b) and (7) of the Policing and Crime Act 2009[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Title, application and interpretation

(1) The title of this Order is the Policing and Crime Act 2009 (Commencement No. 1) (Wales) Order 2010 and it applies in relation to Wales.

(2) In this Order—

- “the 2009 Act” (“*Deddf 2009*”) means the Policing and Crime Act 2009,
- “the 2003 Act” (“*Deddf 2003*”) means the Licensing Act 2003[^f00002],
- “the 1982 Act” (“*Ddeddf 1982*”) means the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1982[^f00003].

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1 May 2010

1 May 2010 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the following provisions of the 2009 Act for the purposes of making orders under paragraph 3 of Schedule 3 to that Act:

- (a) section 27(11) (provision for Schedule 3 to the 2009 Act to have effect);
- (b) paragraphs 3 and 5 of Schedule 3 (power to make transitional provision on adoption of Schedule 3 to the 1982 Act as amended).

## 3. Provisions coming into force on 8 May 2010

8 May 2010 is the appointed day for the coming into force of the following provisions of the 2009 Act:

- (a) section 27 (regulation of lap dancing and other sexual entertainment venues etc) so far as is not already in force;
- (b) Schedule 3 (lap dancing and other sexual entertainment venues etc: transitional provision) so far as not already in force;
- (c) paragraph 23 of Schedule 7 (amendment to the 2003 Act).

Carl Sargeant

Minister for Social Justice and Local Government, one of the Welsh Ministers

27 April 2010

(This note is not part of the Order)

This is the first commencement order made under the Policing and Crime Act 2009 by the Welsh Ministers. It applies in relation to Wales.

Article 2 lists the provisions of the Act which come into force on 1 May 2010 for the purposes of making orders relating to lap dancing and other sexual entertainment venues. Article 3 brings the remainder of the provisions of the 2009 Act relating to sexual entertainment venues into force on 8 May 2010.
