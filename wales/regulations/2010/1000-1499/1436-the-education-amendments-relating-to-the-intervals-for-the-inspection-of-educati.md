The Welsh Ministers in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 122(1) and section 138(7) and (8) of, and paragraph 6B(1)(a) of Schedule 26, to the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^f00001], and in exercise of the powers conferred on the National Assembly for Wales by section 77(2) and (9), section 150 and section 152 of the Learning and Skills Act 2000[^f00002], and in exercise of the powers conferred on the National Assembly of Wales by section 28(1), section 50(4) and (8) and section 120(2) of, and paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 6, to the Education Act 2005[^f00003], and now vested in them make the following Regulations:

## 1. Title, commencement and application[^c-d485c3c69c015ec0b98d0f7cecdc7395]

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Education (Amendments Relating to the Intervals for the Inspection of Education and Training) (Wales) Regulations 2010 and they come into force on 1 September 2010.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

## 4. Amendment to the Education (School Inspection) (Wales) Regulations 2006[^c-df64891b01424fc667ae607c26b6c3eb]

(1) The Education (School Inspection) (Wales) Regulations 2006[^f00006] are amended as follows.

(2) For regulation 6, substitute—

> **6.** (1) The Chief Inspector must secure that every school to which section 28 of the 2005 Act applies is inspected—
>
> - (a) in the case of a school which has not been previously inspected, within six years of the date on which pupils were first admitted to the school, and
> - (b) in all other cases, within six years of the date on which the last inspection was completed.
>
> (2) For the purpose of this regulation the date on which the last inspection was completed is the date of the last inspection under section 28 of the 2005 Act.

.

## 5. Amendments to the Inspection of the Careers and Related Services (Wales) Regulations 2006[^c-fe09519bbe27fe60bd04f818e09050c3]

(1) The Inspection of the Careers and Related Services (Wales) Regulations 2006[^f00007] are amended as follows.

(2) For regulation 3, substitute—

> **3.** (1) This regulation applies in the case of a service provider or relevant provider who has not previously been inspected under sections 55 or 56 of the 2005 Act.
>
> (2) The Chief Inspector must inspect the service provider or relevant provider under sections 55 or 56 of the 2005 Act (as the case may be), within six years of the date of which the service provider or relevant provider first became such a provider.

.

(3) For regulation 4, substitute—

> **4.** (1) Save where regulation 3 applies, the Chief Inspector must inspect each service provider under section 55 of the 2005 Act and each relevant provider under section 56 of the 2005 Act within six years of the date on which the last inspection was completed.
>
> (2) For the purposes of this regulation the date on which the last inspection was completed is the date of the last inspection report.

.

Carwyn Jones

First Minister, one of the Welsh Ministers

17 May 2010

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations amend the—

- (a) Education (Inspection of Nursery Education) (Wales) Regulations 1999,
- (b) Inspection of Education and Training (Wales) Regulations 2001,
- (c) Education (School Inspection) (Wales) Regulations 2006, and
- (d) Inspection of Careers and Related Services (Wales) Regulations 2006,

so as to require Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales or Prif Arolygydd Ei Mawrhydi dros Addysg a Hyfforddiant yng Nghymru to secure that inspections take place within six years of the date of the last inspection.
