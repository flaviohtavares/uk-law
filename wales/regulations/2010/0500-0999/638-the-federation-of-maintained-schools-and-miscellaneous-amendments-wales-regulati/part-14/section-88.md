# The Federation of Maintained Schools and Miscellaneous Amendments (Wales) Regulations 2010, section 88

> Text as at 2014-05-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 88. Amendment to the Education (Foundation Body) (Wales) Regulations 2001[^key-7e4fe7efee96687985878bdf99aa839b]

*Extent: E+W*

In sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph 2, of Schedule 2 to the Education (Foundation Body) (Wales) Regulations 2001[^f00048], for “rescinded;” substitute “rescinded or a moratorium period under a debt relief order applies in relation to him or her; or”.
