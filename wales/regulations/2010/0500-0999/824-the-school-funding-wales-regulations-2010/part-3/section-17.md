## 17. Differential funding[^key-0b7b8d5bcc9921cad3a096b771a6bc35]

*Extent: E+W*

A local authority must not use in their formula any factors or criteria which discriminate between schools by reference to their category under the 1998 Act except where differences in the functions of the governing bodies of schools of different categories justify such discrimination.
