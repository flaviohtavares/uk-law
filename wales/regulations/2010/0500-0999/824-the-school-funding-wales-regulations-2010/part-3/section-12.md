# The School Funding (Wales) Regulations 2010, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2010/824
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. New Schools[^key-4d43273e263d89b9b8fb760292d91993]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A new school must have a delegated budget from the date when the school first admits pupils.

(2) But a local authority’s schools forum may on the application of the authority—

- (a) vary that date to one proposed by the authority,
- (b) vary it to a different date, or
- (c) refuse to vary the date.

(3) Where the schools forum vary the date to a different date or refuse to vary it, the local authority may apply to the Welsh Ministers to vary the date.

(4) The Welsh Ministers may—

- (a) vary the date to one proposed by the authority,
- (b) vary it to a different date, or
- (c) refuse to vary the date.
