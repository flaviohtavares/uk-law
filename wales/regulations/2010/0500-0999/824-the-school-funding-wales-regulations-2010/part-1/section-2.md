> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Revocation[^c-40b8ff91e6d55cbc63b74b0c761ca95c]

*Extent: E+W*

The Non-schools Education Budget, Schools Budget and Individual Schools Budget (Wales) Regulations 2003[^c-e4fd6e1b27ed8a1c6b5366a6d7c91d52], the Schools Budget Shares (Wales) Regulations 2004[^f00006], and The Education (Local Authority Financial Schemes) (Wales) Regulations 2004 are revoked in relation to financial years beginning on or after 1 April 2011.
