## 1. Title, commencement and application[^key-862950d0dadd518939717617b230c501]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The title of these Regulations is the School Funding (Wales) Regulations 2010, and they come into force on 1 September 2010.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to financial years beginning on or after 1 April 2011.

(3) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.
