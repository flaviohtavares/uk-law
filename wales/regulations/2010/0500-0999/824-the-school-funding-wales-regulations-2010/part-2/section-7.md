## 7. Individual schools budget[^key-3dde442e555ff26304446226995eba61]

*Extent: E+W*

Before 14 February immediately preceding the beginning of a funding period, a local authority must deduct from their schools budget any or all of the classes or descriptions of planned expenditure set out in Schedule 2, in whole or in part, in order to arrive at their individual schools budget for that funding period.
