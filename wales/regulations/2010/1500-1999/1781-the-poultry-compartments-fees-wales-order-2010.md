# The Poultry Compartments (Fees) (Wales) Order 2010

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

ANIMALS, WALES

The Poultry Compartments (Fees) (Wales) Order 2010

Made

8 July 2010

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

9 July 2010

Coming into force

30 July 2010

The Welsh Ministers, make this order in exercise of the powers conferred by section 84 of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] .

## 1. Title, application and commencement[^c-0dd0106651a1fe599f54ebd703dce153]

The title of this Order is the Poultry Compartments (Fees) (Wales) Order 2010. It applies in relation to Wales and comes into force on 30 July 2010.

## 2. Interpretation[^c-feec6a0aa7eda12e4a1a8f6ed05504eb]

In this Order—

- “administrative site” means premises in which documents or records relating to a production site are kept; and
- “production site” means premises where poultry are kept for the purposes of breeding, rearing, fattening or egg production and which are the subject of an application under the Poultry Compartments (Wales) Order 2010.

## 3. Fees[^c-76f954180f8e2110324c29511b61f63e]

The fees for inspections pursuant to the Poultry Compartments (Wales) Order 2010[^f00002] set out in the Schedule to this Order are payable to the Welsh Ministers by the applicant within 30 days of the inspection.

Elin Jones

Minister for Rural Affairs, one of the Welsh Ministers.

8 July 2010

## SCHEDULE[^c-0deeff121c3678fdb5a2ab4c4daf539f]

Article 2

[^c-0deeff121c3678fdb5a2ab4c4daf539f]

| Activity | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| First day or part day of an inspection at an administrative site | £1,400 [^c-404a380af8056f2c1465562d0151baff]... |
| Each additional day or part day of an inspection at an administrative site | £1,000 [^c-404a380af8056f2c1465562d0151baff]... |
| A production site inspection | £1,000 [^c-404a380af8056f2c1465562d0151baff]... |

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the payment of fees for inspections in relation to applications for recognition as a compartment under the Poultry Compartments (Wales) Order 2010. That Order implements Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 616/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0616) which makes provision for the recognition of poultry compartments which meet high standards of biosecurity (OJ No L 181, 14.7.2009, p. 16).

A regulatory impact assessment has not been prepared for this instrument.
