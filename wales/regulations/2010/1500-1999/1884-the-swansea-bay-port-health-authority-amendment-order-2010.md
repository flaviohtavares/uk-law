# The Swansea Bay Port Health Authority (Amendment) Order 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 1884 (W.182)

PORT HEALTH AUTHORITIES, WALES

The Swansea Bay Port Health Authority (Amendment) Order 2010

Made

22 July 2010

Coming into force

26 July 2010

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 2, 3 and 4 of the Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984[^f00001], having given the notices required by sections 2 and 4 of that Act and not having received any notice of objection thereto, make the following Order:

## 1. Title and commencement

The title of this Order is the Swansea Bay Port Health Authority (Amendment) Order 2010 and it comes into force on 26 July 2010.

## 2. Amendments to the 1991 Order

The Swansea Bay Port Health Authority Order 1991[^f00002] is amended as follows:

- (a) in Schedule 2, for the heading “*The Public Health (Control of Disease) Act [1984 (c. 77)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/77)* ”, substitute “*The Public Health (Control of Disease) Act [1984 (c. 22)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/22)* ”;
- (b) in Schedule 2, under the heading “*The Public Health (Control of Disease) Act [1984 (c. 77)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/77)* ”, for—

  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | Part I | Administrative and financial provisions |
  > | Part II (except sections 11 and 12, 21 — 23 and 39 — 42) | Control of disease |
  > | Part III |  |
  > | Section 48 | Removal of body to mortuary or for immediate burial |
  > | Section 56 | Application of Part II and III |
  > | Section 61 | Power of entry |

- substitute the following—

  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | Section 45M and all functions conferred under the Health Protection (Part 2A Orders) (Wales) Regulations 2010[^f00003] | Local authority power to apply to a Justice of the Peace for an order to protect human health from risk of infection or contamination |
  > | Section 46 | Burial and cremation |
  > | Section 48 | Removal of body to mortuary or for immediate burial |
- and
  - (a) at the end of Schedule 2, add—

    > **Health Protection (Local Authority Powers) (Wales) Regulations 2010[^f00004]**
    >
    > |  |  |
    > | --- | --- |
    > | Regulation 2 | Requirement to keep a child away from school |
    > | Regulation 3 | Requirement to provide details of children attending school |
    > | Regulation 4 | Disinfection or decontamination of things on request of owner |
    > | Regulation 5 | Disinfection or decontamination of things on request of person with custody or control |
    > | Regulation 6 | Disinfection or decontamination of premises on request of the owner |
    > | Regulation 7 | Disinfection or decontamination of premises on request of tenant |
    > | Regulation 8 | Requests for co-operation for health protection purposes |
    > | Regulation 9 | Restriction of contact with dead bodies |
    > | Regulation 10 | Restriction of access to dead bodies |
    > | Regulation 11 | Relocation of dead bodies |

Signed by the authority of the Minister for Health and Social Services, one of the Welsh Ministers

David Worthington

Head of Health Protection Division

22 July 2010

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order makes amendments to enactments set out in Schedule 2 to the Swansea Bay Port Health Authority Order 1991.
