# The Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 (Prescribed Period and Appropriate Officer) (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2010 No. 1724 (W.165)

EDUCATION, WALES

The Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 (Prescribed Period and Appropriate Officer) (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2010

Made

30 June 2010

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

2 July 2010

Coming into force

25 July 2010

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by sections 13, 56(1), 60 and 61(5) of the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006[^f00001] make the following Regulations:

## 1. Title, commencement and application

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 (Prescribed Period and Appropriate Officer) (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2010 and they come into force on 25 July 2010.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

## 2. Revocation

The Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 (Prescribed Period and Appropriate Officer) (Wales) Regulations 2010[^f00002] are revoked.

Leighton Andrews

Minister for Children, Education and Lifelong Learning, one of the Welsh Ministers

30 June 2010

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 (“the 2006 Act”) provides the legislative framework for a new vetting and barring scheme for people who work with children and vulnerable adults. Section 13 of the 2006 Act requires that a check be made in relation to any person appointed to the governing body of an educational establishment. The Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 (Prescribed Period and Appropriate Officer) (Wales) Regulations 2010 (“the 2010 regulations”) prescribe, for the purposes of section 13, the period within which that check must be carried out and the appropriate officer responsible for carrying it out.

The Secretary of State is conducting a review of the vetting and barring scheme. As a consequence of that review section 13 of the 2006 Act will not be fully commenced on 26 July 2010. Consequently, these Regulations revoke the 2010 regulations before they come into force on 26 July 2010.
