# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 93

> Text as at 2026-02-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 93. Part-time parents’ learning allowance[^c-29dd752dcfef244201374cf820218810]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible part-time student qualifies in connection with the student’s attendance on or undertaking of[^c-081cf934e04dd3f1ed7a5fe149d43756] a designated part-time course for the part-time parents’ learning allowance if the student has one or more dependants who are dependent children.

(2) The amount of part-time parents’ learning allowance payable in respect of an academic year is calculated in accordance with regulations 94 and 96 to 98, the basic amount being £1,983[^c-88324943f554e23d67ce19c5eff6edca].
