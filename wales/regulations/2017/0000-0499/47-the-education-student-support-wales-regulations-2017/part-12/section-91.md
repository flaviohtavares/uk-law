# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 91

> Text as at 2026-02-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 91. Part-time adult dependants’ grant[^c-adf2dd4d49ad879dec99b60849bf647c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible part-time student qualifies for a part-time adult dependants’ grant in connection with the eligible part-time student’s attendance on or undertaking of[^c-f16831e3cdd38d4bb610bbf934a9494a] a designated part-time course in accordance with this regulation.

(2) The part-time adult dependants’ grant is available in respect of one dependant of an eligible part-time student who is either—

- (a) the eligible part-time student’s partner; or
- (b) an adult dependant of the eligible part-time student whose net income for the relevant year does not exceed £3,923.

(3) The amount of part-time adult dependants’ grant payable in respect of an academic year is calculated in accordance with regulations 94 and 96 to 98, the basic amount being—

- (a) £3,474[^c-d475f29d84e02cc0aef5fc207e6cb92d]; or
- (b) where the person in respect of whom the eligible part-time student is applying for part-time adult dependants’ grant is ordinarily resident outside the United Kingdom, such amount not exceeding £3,474[^c-8f30bbcd084cdaa9d340b89ac21de599] as the Welsh Ministers consider reasonable in the circumstances.
