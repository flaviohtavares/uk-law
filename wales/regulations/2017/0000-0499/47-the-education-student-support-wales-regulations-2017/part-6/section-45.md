# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 45

> Text as at 2026-02-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45. Students with reduced entitlement[^key-ba3efcb1cfbf5ba713fe43ac65a55c05]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to regulations 46 to 51, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student with reduced entitlement qualifies in respect of an academic year of a course other than the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is—

- (a)[^key-1ce162f683942aa2cff557657e7be137] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) where the student falls within regulation 23(6), for such a student in—
  - (i) category 1, £3,117[^key-7df3e839552cd21926cc1cf37f574048];
  - (ii) category 2, £5,842[^key-4215b35dd0ed22a8999f8f46b111b631];
  - (iii) category 3, £4,971[^key-9bbe53fccfe9c747a094574e086a10b6];
  - (iv) category 4, £4,971[^key-7d56e5a9d70fb5e442d7508e7d126ca2];
  - (v) category 5, £4,156[^key-f104bdce8105b35e6b7214326ac41d18].
- (c) where the student applies for a loan for living costs and opts not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income, an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2017/47) where—

  **X** is, for such a student in—

  - (i) category 1, £4,924[^key-4a32793d4b0dfb3609886f234d872845];
  - (ii) category 2, £8,910[^key-ce6e77939aa91f05dd4929443457e3e5];
  - (iii) category 3, £7,585[^key-50104c60efb572370e6b736034d1add7];
  - (iv) category 4, £7,585;
  - (v) category 5, £6,361[^key-f11f7cbbaa9c2598779e83eec190f5bb];

  **Y** is nil.

(2) Subject to regulations 46 to 51, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student with reduced entitlement qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is—

- (a)[^key-ca2c0e5fa06da51771d2f4bf49f72a2a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) where the student falls within regulation 23(6), for such a student in—
  - (i) category 1, £2,369[^key-a2617d7ecf7df10b57a7b61fdf9468de];
  - (ii) category 2, £4,467[^key-a8942cc97733ce7032d80373486f0eda];
  - (iii) category 3, £3,634[^key-95317b3dbda95edbe4820baf7aace933];
  - (iv) category 4, £3,634[^key-30f9c44aff1f5ce4241f3fc59a122cf1];
  - (v) category 5, £3,239[^key-a0e808194cbe265ce7473da6c115db02].
- (c) where the student applies for a loan for living costs and opts not to provide the information needed to calculate the household income an amount equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2017/47) where—

  **X** is, for such a student in—

  - (i) category 1, £4,459[^key-4fd07fadff6464d046624da1b51a6f55];
  - (ii) category 2, £8,114[^key-c9456951633f99157ec9e6ffc6a88be6];
  - (iii) category 3, £6,596[^key-6b563af2b752d43deb631b3761e908fe];
  - (iv) category 4, £6,596;
  - (v) category 5, £5,893[^key-100343bc37a547c0d8df689c5b70c917];

  **Y** is nil.
