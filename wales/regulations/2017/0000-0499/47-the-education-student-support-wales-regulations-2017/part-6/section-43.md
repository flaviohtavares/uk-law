# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 43

> Text as at 2026-02-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43. Maximum amount of loans for eligible students with full entitlement who are 2010 cohort students, 2012 cohort students or 2012 accelerated graduate entry students ...[^key-b633123ab3026526778f41111ef0a02c][^key-e519b534354202baf37a99cdd1820824]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies to an eligible student with full entitlement who is a 2010 cohort student, a 2012 cohort student or a 2012 accelerated graduate entry student [^key-40d3d02aa91ea54a90847372a4135353]....

(2) Subject to regulations 46 to 51, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year other than a final year of a course that is not an intensive course is equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2017/47) where—

- **X** is, for such a student in—
  - (i) category 1, £5,358 for a 2010 cohort student, otherwise[^key-8e3bde9cf0ada8def9473f59fb5caaff] £6,565[^key-f71952ba62e03e8dda44548bf8a49fe2];
  - (ii) category 2, £9,697 for a 2010 cohort student, otherwise[^key-f4b874842590a5a40ffc50dfa593d232] £11,880[^key-961de8df3a3619755e5522ec9cb2522f];
  - (iii) category 3, £8,253 for a 2010 cohort student, otherwise[^key-a5c5c02b2e9fbb5247f8e6cd5adedb0c] £10,113[^key-d373af61f6dd70008f01e6a3c2f75258];
  - (iv) category 4, £8,253 for a 2010 cohort student, otherwise[^key-bf43fafed9325a295d1e42bbcef9e60e] £10,113;
  - (v) category 5, £6,922 for a 2010 cohort student, otherwise[^key-f91e9338d926a666d03b65026e42c00c] £8,481[^key-eddd8eb528ece8f1dbca7b7e37931d3d];
- **Y** is the maintenance grant amount.

(3) Subject to regulations 46 to 51, the maximum amount of loan for living costs for which a student to whom this regulation applies qualifies in respect of an academic year that is the final year of a course that is not an intensive course is equal to [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2017/47) where—

- **X** is, for such a student in—
  - (i) category 1, £4,851 for a 2010 cohort student, otherwise[^key-2eeede984b7142e67ed5aeadb74ca2ab] £5,945[^key-071e4ee767a0e1e499e6c0a5ac7ef6d6];
  - (ii) category 2, £8,830 for a 2010 cohort student, otherwise[^key-1d4386edf7c1bd7fd151e90bb39911bd] £10,818[^key-8bb59c535e3ec6dcbdc4e93d44e94ce0];
  - (iii) category 3, £7,179 for a 2010 cohort student, otherwise[^key-e15da997b959c6d6db8ed223ea9db954] £8,795[^key-cc58af5db49a2c4f57551037187e190a];
  - (iv) category 4, £7,179 for a 2010 cohort student, otherwise[^key-66fbec177cbd78829d582c8df1df832a] £8,795;
  - (v) category 5, £6,412 for a 2010 cohort student, otherwise[^key-e4b172c0ed28c94068afb512baeda28b] £7,857[^key-9b95f8177c5accfa8f88c9e64aa7f9d3];
- **Y** is the maintenance grant amount.

(4) In this regulation, “the maintenance grant amount” (“*swm y grant cynhaliaeth*”) is—

- (a) where the eligible student referred to in paragraph (1) qualifies under regulation 36 for an amount of maintenance grant, the amount that is equal to £0.50 for every £1 of maintenance grant for which that student qualifies, up to a maximum value for Y of £2,580;
- (b) where no maintenance grant is payable under regulation 36, nil.
