> Text as at 2026-02-12.

## 51. Deductions from loans for living costs[^key-854cab90a93f387f8e6807e996553c68]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A deduction from the amount of loan for living costs calculated under this Part in respect of an eligible student with full entitlement may be made in accordance with regulation 56.

(2) A deduction from the amount of loan for living costs calculated under this Part in respect of a student with reduced entitlement may not be made under regulation 56.
