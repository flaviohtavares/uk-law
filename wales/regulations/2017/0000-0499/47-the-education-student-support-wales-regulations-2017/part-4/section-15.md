# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 15

> Text as at 2026-02-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. Events[^key-3b34dd3e3e055042d80b7751cf2aefc9]

*Extent: E+W*

The events are—

- (a) the student’s course becomes a designated course;
- (b)[^key-3400808d0c222185dcbc877d5b8befee] the student or the student’s spouse, civil partner or parent (as defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1) is recognised as a refugee, becomes a person granted stateless leave, becomes a person with leave to enter or remain or becomes a person granted humanitarian protection under paragraph 339C of the immigration rules;
- (ba)[^key-76728c0ba904ad8a7cefa0adff2c165c] the student or the student’s parent becomes a person with section 67 leave to remain or a person[^key-8e66c1b0d15e8658da20c0d74b333576] granted leave to enter or remain[^key-4df8b1dab8acc8f8892c29d9cb0533b2] as a protected partner;
- (bb)[^key-d2477902f5113dc73b8e9e02e9a0fb5f] the student becomes a person with Calais leave;
- (bc)[^key-cbb57c3a2308d6834f1cd84243add7dd] the student becomes a protected Ukrainian national or the spouse, civil partner, child or step-child of such a person[^key-d91751c4651c0e576b53db6b3d56a7a2];
- (c)[^key-52b718d115e6f5e990b92317f29ef822] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^key-7a17e74158fd46c655f4d04ad738a2e4] the student becomes a family member described in paragraph 9A(1)(a), 9C(1)(a) or 9D(1)(a) of Schedule 1;
- (e)[^key-77488f20ddac0c527442874fd0615131] the student becomes a person described in paragraph 2(1)(a), 2A(1)(a) or 3(1)(a)[^key-ce69b29cad5758288c3254017ad9cc7c] of Schedule 1;
- (f)[^key-8a22c7a37f4d87eff8bc19412900be89] where regulation 4(2A)(a) applies, the student becomes a person described in paragraph 12(a) of Schedule 1;
- (g) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 6A(1)(a) of Schedule 1 or, where regulation 4(2A)(a) applies, in[^key-a292d644e7d34c5e033bcfa014b1a387] paragraph 6(1)(a) of Schedule 1; [^key-bd7e3b6f68805fc33afa5a7b244f0735]...
- (h)[^key-85e16d5aee7debae0d0a60ffd8ff80ec] the student becomes a person described in paragraph 11A(a) of Schedule 1 or, where regulation 4(2A)(a) applies, in paragraph 11(1)(a) of Schedule 1; or
- (i)[^key-6d7596703c1c7665ea907c20b929f01f] the student becomes a person described in paragraph 9B(1)(a)(ii) of Schedule 1.
