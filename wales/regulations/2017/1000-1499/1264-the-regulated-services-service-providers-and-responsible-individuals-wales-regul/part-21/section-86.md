# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 86

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 86. Offences – responsible individuals

*Extent: E+W*

(1) It is an offence for the responsible individual to fail to comply with a requirement of any of the provisions specified in paragraph (2) [^c23886941].

(2) The provisions specified for the purposes of paragraph (1) are the provisions of regulations 67(1), 68(1), 71(1), 71(2), 73(1), 73(2), 73(3), 74(1), 74(2), 75(1), 80(4), 81(1), 84(1) and 84(3).

## Annotations

[^c23886941]: Marginal citation: For penalties upon conviction for an offence under this regulation, see section 51(1) of the Act.
