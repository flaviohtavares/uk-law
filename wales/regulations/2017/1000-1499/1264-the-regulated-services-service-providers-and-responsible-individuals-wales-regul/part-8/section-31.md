# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 31

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31. Deprivation of liberty

*Extent: E+W*

An individual must not be deprived of their liberty for the purpose of receiving care and support without lawful authority.
