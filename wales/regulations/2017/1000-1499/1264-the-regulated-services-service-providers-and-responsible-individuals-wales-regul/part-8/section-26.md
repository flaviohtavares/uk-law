# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 26

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Safeguarding - overarching requirement

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must provide the service in a way which ensures that individuals are safe and are protected from abuse, neglect and improper treatment.
