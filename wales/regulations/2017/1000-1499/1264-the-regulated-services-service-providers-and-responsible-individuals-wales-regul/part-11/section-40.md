# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 40

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Application of regulations in this Part

*Extent: E+W*

The regulations in this Part only apply to a service provider in respect of the provision of a domiciliary support service which the provider is registered to provide.
