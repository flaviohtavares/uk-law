# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 57

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 57. Health and safety

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must ensure that any risks to the health and safety of individuals are identified and reduced so far as reasonably practicable.
