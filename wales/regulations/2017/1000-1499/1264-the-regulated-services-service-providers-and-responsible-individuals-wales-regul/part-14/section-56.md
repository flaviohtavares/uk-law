# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 56

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 56. Hygiene and infection control

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The service provider must have arrangements in place to ensure—

- (a) satisfactory standards of hygiene in the delivery of the service;
- (b) the appropriate disposal of general and clinical waste.

(2) The service provider must have policies and procedures in place for the control of infection and to minimise the spread of infection and must ensure that the service is provided in accordance with these policies and procedures.
