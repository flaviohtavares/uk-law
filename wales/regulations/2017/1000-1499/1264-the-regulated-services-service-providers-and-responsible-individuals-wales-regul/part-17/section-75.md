# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 75

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 75. Other reports to the service provider

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The responsible individual must, without delay, report to the service provider—

- (a) any concerns about the management or provision of the service;
- (b) any significant changes to the way the service is managed or provided;
- (c) any concerns that the service is not being provided in accordance with the statement of purpose for the service.

(2) But this requirement does not apply where the service provider is an individual.
