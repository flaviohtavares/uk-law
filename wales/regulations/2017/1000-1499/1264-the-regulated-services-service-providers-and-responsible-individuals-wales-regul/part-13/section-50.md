# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 50

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 50. Additional requirements – en-suite bathrooms

*Extent: E+W*

All bedrooms used for the provision of the service must have an en suite bathroom which includes an accessible wash hand basin, lavatory and shower.
