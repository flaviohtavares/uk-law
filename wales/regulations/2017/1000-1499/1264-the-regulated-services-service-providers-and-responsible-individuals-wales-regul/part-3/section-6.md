# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 6

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Requirements in relation to the provision of the service

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must ensure that the service is provided with sufficient care, competence and skill, having regard to the statement of purpose.
