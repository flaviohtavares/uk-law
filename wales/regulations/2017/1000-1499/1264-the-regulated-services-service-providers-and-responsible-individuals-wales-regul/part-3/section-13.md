# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 13

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Duty of candour

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must act in an open and transparent way with—

- (a) individuals who are receiving care and support,
- (b) any representatives of those individuals, and
- (c) in the case of a child who is provided with accommodation, the placing authority.
