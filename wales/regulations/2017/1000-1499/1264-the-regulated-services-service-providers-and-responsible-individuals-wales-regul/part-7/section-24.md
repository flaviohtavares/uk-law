# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 24

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Language and communication

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The service provider must take reasonable steps to meet the language needs of individuals.

(2) The service provider must ensure that individuals are provided with access to such aids and equipment as may be necessary to facilitate the individual's communication with others.
