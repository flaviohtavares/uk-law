# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 22

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22. Continuity of care

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must put arrangements in place to ensure that individuals receive such continuity of care as is reasonable to meet their needs for care and support.
