# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 17

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Records of personal plans

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must—

- (a) keep a record of—
  - (i) the personal plan and any revised plan, and
  - (ii) the outcome of any review, and
- (b) give a copy of the personal plan and any revised plan to—
  - (i) the individual,
  - (ii) any representative, unless this is not appropriate or would be inconsistent with the individual's well-being, and
  - (iii) where a child is provided with accommodation as part of a care home service, the placing authority.
