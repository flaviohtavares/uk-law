# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 38

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 38. Information for staff

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The service provider must ensure that all persons working at the service (including any person allowed to work as a volunteer) are provided with information about the service and the way it is provided.

(2) The service provider must ensure that there are arrangements in place to make staff aware of any codes of practice about the standards of conduct expected of social care workers, which is required to be published by Social Care Wales under section 112(1)(a) of the Act.
