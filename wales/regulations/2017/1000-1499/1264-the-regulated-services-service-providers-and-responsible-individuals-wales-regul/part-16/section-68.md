# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 68

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 68. Fitness requirements for appointment of manager

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The responsible individual must not appoint a person to manage the service unless that person is fit to do so.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1), a person is not fit to manage the service unless the requirements of regulation 35(2) (fitness of staff) are met in respect of that person.
