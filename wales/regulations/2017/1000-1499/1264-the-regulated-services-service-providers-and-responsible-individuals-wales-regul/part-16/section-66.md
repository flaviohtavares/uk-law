# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 66

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 66. Supervision of management of the service

*Extent: E+W*

The responsible individual must supervise the management of the service, which includes taking the steps described in regulations 64, 72 and 73.
