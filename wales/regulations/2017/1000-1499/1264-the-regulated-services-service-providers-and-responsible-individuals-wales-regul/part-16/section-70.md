# The Regulated Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 70

> Text as at 2024-03-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 70. Duty to report the appointment of manager to service provider

*Extent: E+W*

On the appointment of a manager in accordance with regulation 67(1), the responsible individual must give notice to the service provider of—

- (a) the name of the person appointed, and
- (b) the date on which the appointment is to take effect.
