# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 44

> Text as at 2026-06-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 44. Prohibition on the grant of planning permission for unauthorised EIA development

*Extent: E+W*

The Welsh Ministers or an inspector must not grant planning permission or subsequent consent under section 177(1) of the 1990 Act (grant or modification of planning permission on appeals against enforcement notices) [^c23311481] in respect of unauthorised EIA development unless an environmental impact assessment has been carried out in respect of that development.
