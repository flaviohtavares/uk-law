# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 43

> Text as at 2026-06-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43. Duty to ensure objectives of the Directive are met

*Extent: E+W*

Relevant planning authorities, in the exercise of their enforcement functions, must have regard to the need to secure compliance with any law which implemented the Directive and with the objectives of the Directive[^c-5b55adf741be875663bf6dfd080c412a] .
