# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 3

> Text as at 2026-06-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Prohibition on granting planning permission , infrastructure consent or subsequent consent without environmental impact assessment[^c-fa216b500618ca4aedbf79fd6c949057]

*Extent: E+W*

A relevant planning authority or the Welsh Ministers or examining authority[^c-81f448f66d862559bdb64dcf6b87a78b] or an inspector must not grant planning permission , infrastructure consent[^c-5a5b49d89bcf6606cca3756b00b93ee1] or subsequent consent for EIA development unless an environmental impact assessment has been carried out in respect of that development.
