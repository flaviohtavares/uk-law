# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 30

> Text as at 2026-06-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30. Application of Parts 2 to 7

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Part applies where an application for planning permission or infrastructure consent[^key-706f645ff1612265021907bb9a1f4224] is made to the Welsh Ministers and so that “**application**” (“***cais***”) in this Part means an application for planning permission or infrastructure consent so made.

(2) Parts 2 to 7 apply subject to the exceptions in the following paragraph and the modifications and supplementary provisions in this Part.

(3) Regulations 6, 7(1), 7(2), 8 to 15, 20, and 22 do not apply.
