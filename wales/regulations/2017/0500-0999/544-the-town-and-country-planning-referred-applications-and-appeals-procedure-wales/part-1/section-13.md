## 13. Prescribed period[^c-96d8364d2cd0f3a92f8dac5053b0c585]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of section 319B of the Planning Act [^c-144d3f4dc2df8986b18e2a1c5a2d1fa5]... and section 21B of the Hazardous Substances Act the prescribed period is 6 weeks from the starting date.
