> Text as at 2024-12-31.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Representations to be taken into account[^key-0d65dbd90a83e5a2a307bacee27dddcc]

*Extent: E+W*

In deciding an appeal the Welsh Ministers or the appointed person as the case may be, may disregard any representations, documents, evidence or information received after the relevant time limits.
