# The Town and Country Planning (Referred Applications and Appeals Procedure) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 5

> Text as at 2024-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Transmission of documents[^key-cdeaa39e73c69f154d02881d433b2f31]

*Extent: E+W*

Notices or documents required to be sent or supplied under these Regulations may be sent or supplied by—

- (a) post;
- (b) using electronic communications to transmit the notice or document to a person at such address as may for the time being be specified by that person for such purpose; or
- (c) providing a direct link to the notice or document to a person at such address as may for the time being be specified by that person for such purpose.
