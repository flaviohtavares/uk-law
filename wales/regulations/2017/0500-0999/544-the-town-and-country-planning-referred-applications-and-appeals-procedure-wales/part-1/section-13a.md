## 13A. Period specified for choice of inquiry, hearing or written procedure[^key-78240f17a1c8c6d7cb5058012a253d89]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of section 174(3) of the 2023 Act, the period specified is 6 weeks from the starting date.
