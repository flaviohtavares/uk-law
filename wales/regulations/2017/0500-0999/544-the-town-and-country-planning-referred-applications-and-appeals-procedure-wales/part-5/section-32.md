# The Town and Country Planning (Referred Applications and Appeals Procedure) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 32

> Text as at 2024-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 32. Absence, adjournment etc.[^key-4f4c3df04285d524bad723e02a136f4d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may proceed with a hearing in the absence of the appellant, the local planning authority and any person invited to take part.

(2) The Welsh Ministers may from time to time adjourn a hearing and, if the date, time and place of the adjourned hearing are announced at the hearing before the adjournment, no further notice is required.
