# The Town and Country Planning (Referred Applications and Appeals Procedure) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 28

> Text as at 2024-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Appointment of assessor[^c-ef5bc7121c6db00167bec375099cef8c]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the Welsh Ministers appoint an assessor under paragraph 6 of Schedule 6 to the Planning Act, paragraph 3(2) of Schedule 12 to the 2023 Act[^c-0bf0a649f3c75ac345e1ab9c9fdbb8ce] or paragraph 6 of the Schedule to the Hazardous Substances Act, they must notify the appellant, the local planning authority and any person invited to take part in the hearing of the name of the assessor and of the matters on which the assessor is to advise the appointed person.
