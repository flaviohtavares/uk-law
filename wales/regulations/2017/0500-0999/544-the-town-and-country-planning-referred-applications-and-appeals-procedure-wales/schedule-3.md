# The Town and Country Planning (Referred Applications and Appeals Procedure) (Wales) Regulations 2017, SCHEDULE 3

> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 3 — Modifications where enforcement notices issued by Welsh Ministers

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 52

### 1. Interpretation[^c-c5b34dfee67d171e7e6d2c2fa83e8505]

Regulation 3 is read as if in the definition of “full statement of case”, paragraph (d) provided—

> - (d) means and is comprised of in relation to enforcement appeals—
>   - (i) a written statement by the Welsh Ministers containing—
>     - (aa) a response to each ground of appeal pleaded by the appellant; and
>     - (bb) full particulars of the case the Welsh Ministers propose to put forward in relation to the appeal; and
>   - (ii) copies of any supporting documents the Welsh Ministers propose to refer to or put in evidence.

### 2. Further information[^c-472e71e14b5c8dd6fb26b47a63520912]

Regulation 9 is read as if paragraph (1)(b) were omitted.

### 3. Inspection of documents[^c-17e33f5c11c79bb8efc36b9e3eed3d49]

### 4. Determination of procedure[^c-8f0f790d4af28f16631ed94d30ffdeb0]

  ;
- (b) paragraphs (6) and (7) were omitted.
