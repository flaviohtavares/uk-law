# The Town and Country Planning (Referred Applications and Appeals Procedure) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 18

> Text as at 2024-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18. Application of Part 3[^key-34ba399f2e3e4ff2a5b3f80e64d45aa2]

*Extent: E+W*

This Part only applies where—

- (a) a householder, advertisement consent or minor commercial appeal is made in relation to an application; and
- (b) the Welsh Ministers determine that it is a matter which is to be considered on the basis of written representations only.
