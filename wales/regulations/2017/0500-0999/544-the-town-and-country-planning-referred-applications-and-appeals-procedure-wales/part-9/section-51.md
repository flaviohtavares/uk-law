# The Town and Country Planning (Referred Applications and Appeals Procedure) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 51

> Text as at 2024-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 51. Modifications where national security direction given[^c-c40945f63b5d2a3e471f09c39869d109]

*Extent: E+W*

The modifications set out in Schedule 2 have effect where a direction is given by the Welsh Ministers under—

- (a) section 321(3)[^f00016] of the Planning Act (planning inquiries to be held in public subject to certain exceptions);
- (b)[^c-923bf573b45b3af63f9bb9e3b088f471] section 178(2) of the 2023 Act (local inquiries); or
- (c) paragraph 6(6) of the Schedule[^f00018] to the Hazardous Substances Act (local inquiries and hearings).
