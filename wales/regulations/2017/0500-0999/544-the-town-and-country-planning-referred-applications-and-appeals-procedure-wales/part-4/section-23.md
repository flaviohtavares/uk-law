# The Town and Country Planning (Referred Applications and Appeals Procedure) (Wales) Regulations 2017, section 23

> Text as at 2024-12-31.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Interested persons’ representations[^c-36d004aa9d74ea09656033890b262bfb]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person who has been notified under regulation 17 may send representations to the Welsh Ministers so as to be received by the Welsh Ministers within 4 weeks of the starting date.

(2) Interested persons must send three copies of any representations they make to the Welsh Ministers so as to be received within 4 weeks of the starting date.

(3) The Welsh Ministers must send a copy of any representations received from interested persons to the appellant and the local planning authority.
