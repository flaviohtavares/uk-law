# The Disease Control (Wales) (Amendment) (Amendment) Order 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2017 No. 677 (W. 159)

Animals, Wales

Animal Health

The Disease Control (Wales) (Amendment) (Amendment) Order 2017

Made

7 June 2017

Coming into force

10 June 2017

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 1, 8(1), and 83(2) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001].

## 1. Title, application and commencement

(1) The title of this Order is the Disease Control (Wales) (Amendment) (Amendment) Order 2017.

(2) This Order applies in relation to Wales.

(3) This Order comes into force on 10 June 2017.

## 2. Amendments to the Disease Control (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2017

The Disease Control (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2017[^f00002] is amended as follows.

**3.** In article 1 (title, application and commencement) for paragraph (3) substitute—

> (3) This Order comes into force on 11 September 2017.

Lesley Griffiths

Cabinet Secretary for Environment and Rural Affairs, one of the Welsh Ministers

7 June 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the date in article 1 of the Disease Control (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2017 ([S.I. 2017/674 (W. 156)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2017/674)) so as that Order comes into force on 11 September 2017.
