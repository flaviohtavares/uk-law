# The Public Health (Wales) Act 2017 (Commencement) (Amendment) Order 2017

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

This Statutory Instrument has been made in consequence of a defect in [S.I. 2017/949 (W. 237) (C. 85)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2017/949) and is being issued free of charge to all known recipients of that Statutory Instrument.

2017 No. 967 (W. 249) (C. 90)

Public Health, Wales

The Public Health (Wales) Act 2017 (Commencement) (Amendment) Order 2017

Made

3 October 2017

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 126(2) and (3) of the Public Health (Wales) Act 2017[^f00001], make the following Order.

## 1. Title

The title of this Order is the Public Health (Wales) Act 2017 (Commencement) (Amendment) Order 2017.

## 2. Amendment of Public Health (Wales) Act 2017 (Commencement) Order 2017

Article 3 of the Public Health (Wales) Act 2017 (Commencement) Order 2017[^f00002] is revoked.

Rebecca Evans

Minister for Social Services and Public Health, under authority of the Cabinet Secretary for Health, Well-being and Sport, one of the Welsh Ministers

3 October 2017

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order made by the Welsh Ministers amends the Public Health (Wales) Act 2017 (Commencement) Order 2017 (“the original Commencement Order”).

Article 3 of the original Commencement Order brings into force on 1 January 2018 provisions of the Public Health (Wales) Act 2017 relating to the intimate piercing of children (Part 5 and section 94 for the purposes of Part 5).

This Order revokes article 3 of the original Commencement Order, which was included in error. Those provisions will be brought into force at a later date by a separate commencement order.
