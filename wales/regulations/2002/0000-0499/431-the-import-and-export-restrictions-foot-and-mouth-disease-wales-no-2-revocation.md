# The Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Wales) (No.2) (Revocation) Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 431 (W.53)

ANIMALS, WALES

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Wales) (No.2) (Revocation) Regulations 2002

Made

26th February 2002

Coming into force

27th February 2002

The National Assembly for Wales, being designated[^f00001] for the purposes of section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972[^f00002] in relation to the common agricultural policy of the European Community, in exercise of the powers conferred on it by that section, and of all other powers enabling it in that behalf, makes the following Regulations—

## 1. Title, application and commencement

(1) These Regulations are called the Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Wales) (No.2) (Revocation) Regulations 2002 and apply to Wales.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 27th February 2002.

## 2. Revocation of the Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Wales) (No.2) Regulations 2002

The Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Wales) (No.2) Regulations 2002[^f00003] are revoked.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00004].

D. Elis-Thomas

The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

Date 26th February 2002

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations give effect in relation to Wales to article 3 of Commission Decision [2002/153/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2002/0153) (OJ No. L50, 21.2.2002, p. 98). That Decision repealed Commission Decision [2001/740/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0740) as amended concerning certain protection measures with regard to foot-and-mouth disease in the United Kingdom (OJ No. L277, 20.10.2001, p. 30). The Regulations revoke the Import and Export Restrictions (Foot-and-Mouth Disease) (Wales) (No.2) Regulations 2002 which implemented the last amendment to Commission Decision [2001/740/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/decision/2001/0740).

A Regulatory Appraisal has not been prepared for these Regulations.
