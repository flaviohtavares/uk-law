# The Transport Act 2000 (Commencement No. 2) (Wales) Order 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 2024 (W.208) (C.65)

TRANSPORT, WALES

The Transport Act 2000 (Commencement No. 2) (Wales) Order 2002

Made

31 July 2002

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by section 275(2) of the Transport Act 2000 [^f00001] hereby makes the following Order:

## 1. Citation and application

(1) This Order may be cited as the Transport Act 2000 (Commencement No. 2) (Wales) Order 2002.

(2) This Order applies to Wales.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on the 14 August 2002

The following provisions of the Transport Act 2000 come into force on 14 August 2002 in relation to Wales, namely—

- (a) section 154(6)
- (b) section 274 insofar as it relates to the entries in Part II to Schedule 31 (Repeals and revocations).

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00002]

D. Elis-Thomas

The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

31st July 2002

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on the 14th August 2002, in relation to Wales, certain provisions of Part II of the Transport Act 2000 (“the Act”), namely:

- (a) Section 154(6), which provides that section 92 of the Finance Act 1965 (grants towards duty charged on bus fuel) and section 111 of the Transport Act 1985 (unregistered and unreliable local services: reduction of fuel duty grant) are to cease to have effect; and
- (b) section 274 insofar as it relates to Part II of Schedule 31 (Repeals and revocations).
