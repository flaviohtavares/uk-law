# The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 3181 (W.297)

HIGHWAYS, WALES

The Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2002

Made

17th December 2002

Coming into force

1st January 2003

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon the Secretary of State by sections 75 and 104(1) of the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991[^f00001] and which are now exercisable by the National Assembly in relation to Wales[^f00002], hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Street Works (Inspection Fees) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2002 and come into force on 1st January 2003.

(2) These Regulations apply to Wales.

## 2. Increase in the inspection fee

In regulation 3(1) of the Street Works (Inspection Fees) Regulations 1992[^f00003] there is to be substituted for the figure of “£15.50” there the figure of “£20”.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00004]

D. Elis-Thomas

The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

17th December 2002

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations provide for an increase from £15.50 to £20 in the fee payable by undertakers for inspections of their work by street authorities in Wales under regulation 3(1) of the Street Works (Inspection Fees) Regulations 1992 (S.I. [1992/1688](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1992/1688)).
