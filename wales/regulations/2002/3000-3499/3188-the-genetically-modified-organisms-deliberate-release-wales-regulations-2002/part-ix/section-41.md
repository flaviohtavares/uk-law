# The Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) (Wales) Regulations 2002, section 41

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Application of Part VI of the Act to Wales

*Extent: E+W*

In consequence of the provision made by regulations 4, 5, 20(1), 30, 31, 34(2) and 40, section 163A of the Act ceases to have effect.
