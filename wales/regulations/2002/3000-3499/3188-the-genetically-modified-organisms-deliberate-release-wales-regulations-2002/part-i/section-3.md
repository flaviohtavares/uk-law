# The Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) (Wales) Regulations 2002, section 3

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Designation of competent authority

*Extent: E+W*

The National Assembly for Wales shall be the competent authority in relation to Wales for the purposes of the Deliberate Release Directive.
