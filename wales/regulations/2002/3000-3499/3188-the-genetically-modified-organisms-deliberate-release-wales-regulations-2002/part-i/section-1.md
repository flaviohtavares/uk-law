# The Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) (Wales) Regulations 2002, section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) (Wales) Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 31st December 2002.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.
