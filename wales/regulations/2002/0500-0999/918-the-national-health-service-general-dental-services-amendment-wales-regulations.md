# The National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2002

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2002 No. 918 (W. 106)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, WALES

The National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2002

Made

28 March 2002

Coming into force

1st April 2002

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred upon it by sections 15(1), 35(1), 36(1) and (3) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement, interpretation and application

(1) These regulations may be cited as the National Health Service (General Dental Services) (Amendment) (Wales) Regulations 2002 and shall come into force on 1st April 2002.

(2) In these regulations—

- “the 1992 Regulations” (“*Rheoliadau 1992*”) means the National Health Service (General Dental Services) Regulations 1992[^f00002];

(3) These Regulations shall apply to Wales only.

## 2. Amendment of Schedule 4 to the 1992 Regulations

In Part II of Schedule 4 to the 1992 Regulations (prior approval treatment), in each of paragraphs 1 and 2, for “£260” substitute “£375”.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00003]

Rhodri Morgan

The First Secretary

Date 28 March 2002

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These Regulations further amend the National Health Service (General Dental Services) Regulations 1992 (“the 1992 Regulations”).

The 1992 Regulations regulate the terms on which general dental services are provided under the National Health Service Act 1977.

**Regulation 2** amends Schedule 4 to the 1992 Regulations to increase (from £260 to £375) the amount specified therein as the maximum cost, or likely cost, of care and treatment which a dentist may undertake without seeking the prior approval of the Dental Practice Board.
