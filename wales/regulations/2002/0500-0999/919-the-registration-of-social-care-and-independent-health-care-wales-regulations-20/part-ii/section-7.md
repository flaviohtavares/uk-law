# The Registration of Social Care and Independent Health Care (Wales) Regulations 2002, section 7

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 7. Notice of changes

The applicant shall give notice in writing to the National Assembly of any change specified below which occurs after the application for registration is made and before it is determined—

- (a) any change of the name or address of the applicant or any responsible person;
- (b) where the applicant is an organisation, any change of director, manager, secretary or other person responsible for supervising the management of the organisation.
