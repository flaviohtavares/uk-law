# The Registration of Social Care and Independent Health Care (Wales) Regulations 2002, section 6

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 6. Interview

The responsible person shall attend an interview for the purpose of enabling the National Assembly to determine whether the applicant is fit to carry on or manage the establishment in respect of which the applicant seeks to be registered.
