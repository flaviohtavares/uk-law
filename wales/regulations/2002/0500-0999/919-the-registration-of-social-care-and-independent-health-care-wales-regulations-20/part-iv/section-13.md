# The Registration of Social Care and Independent Health Care (Wales) Regulations 2002, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Report as to financial viability

If it appears to the registered person that the establishment is likely to cease to be financially viable at any time within the following six months, the registered person shall give a report to the appropriate office of the National Assembly of the relevant circumstances.
