# The Residential Property Tribunal Procedures and Fees (Wales) Regulations 2016, section 36

> Text as at 2019-12-02.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. Enforcement[^key-f8c080e8cbaa35fe25ba971b41e0476f]

*Extent: E+W*

Any decision of the tribunal may, with the permission of the county court, be enforced in the same way as orders of such a court.
