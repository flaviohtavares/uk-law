# The Residential Property Tribunal Procedures and Fees (Wales) Regulations 2016, section 52

> Text as at 2019-12-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52. Revocation[^key-73fcd545d8f71073a19581aba67a16c4]

*Extent: E+W*

The following Regulations and Orders are revoked—

- (a) The Residential Property Tribunal Procedures and Fees (Wales) Regulations 2012[^f00010];
- (b) The Residential Property Tribunal Procedures and Fees (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2014[^f00011];
- (c) The Residential Property Tribunal Procedures and Fees (Wales) (Amendment No. 2) Regulations 2014[^f00012];
- (d) The Residential Property Tribunal Procedures and Fees (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2015[^f00013];
- (e) article 5(1), (2) and (6)(c)(i) of the Mobile Homes Act 1983 (Amendment of Schedule 1 and Consequential Amendments) (Wales) Order 2013[^f00014];
- (f) paragraph 9 of Schedule 2 to the Marriage (Same Sex Couples) Act 2013 (Consequential Provisions) Order 2014[^f00015];
- (g) paragraph 98 of the Schedule to the Public Bodies (Abolition of Administrative Justice and Tribunals Council) Order 2013[^f00016].
