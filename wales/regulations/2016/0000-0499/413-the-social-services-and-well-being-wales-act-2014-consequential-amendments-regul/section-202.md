# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 202

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**202.**[^key-a621f35002d947df06fe1d51ff930dae] In section 21[^f00186] (positions of trust)—

- (a) in subsection (3)(a), after “Children Act [1989 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41)” insert “or section 81(6) of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”;
- (b) in subsection (3)(b) for “that Act” substitute “the Children Act 1989”;
- (c) in subsection (8)(a) after “Children Act [1989 (c. 41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41)” insert “or section 76 or 77 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”;
- (d) at the end of subsection (10)(a), insert “or”;
- (e) after subsection (10)(a), insert—

  > - (aa) section 106(1) of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 in respect of category 1 or 2 young persons within the meaning of that Act,

  .
