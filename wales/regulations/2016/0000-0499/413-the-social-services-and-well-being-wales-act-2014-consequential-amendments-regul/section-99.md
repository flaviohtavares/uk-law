# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 99

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**99.**[^c-7327cb9c5420c32ff7fa957d6996c65a] In section 42[^f00121] (right of officer of the Service to have access to local authority records) in paragraph (b) of subsection (1), after “1970” insert “or for the purposes of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”.
