# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 24

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**24.**[^key-d52022aa4baa6a60326136d677e00dc0] In Schedule 1[^f00018]—

- (a) omit the entries relating to the National Assistance Act 1948, except the entry relating to section 49 of that Act;
- (b) in the entry relating to the Health Visiting and Social Work (Training) Act 1962[^f00019] omit “, and as extended by section 45(9) of the Health Services and Public Health Act [1968 (c. 46)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/46)”;
- (c) in the entry relating to the Health Services and Public Health Act 1968 omit the reference to section 45;
- (d) omit the entry relating to the Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968[^f00020];
- (e) omit the entry relating to the Children Act 1975[^f00021];
- (f) omit the entry relating to the Supplementary Benefits Act 1976[^f00022];
- (g) omit the entry relating to the Health and Social Services and Social Security Adjudications Act 1983[^f00023];
- (h) in the entry relating to the Disabled Persons (Services, Consultation and Representation) Act 1986[^f00024]—
  - (i) for “1 to 4” substitute “1, 2 and 4”;
  - (ii) for “, 7 and 8” substitute “and 7”;
- (i) in the entry relating to the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00025], omit—

  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | Section 46 | Preparation of plans for community care services |

  ;
- (j) omit the reference to the Carers and Disabled Children Act 2000[^f00026];
- (k) in the entry relating to the Community Care (Delayed Discharges etc.) Act 2003[^f00027], omit—

  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | Part 2 | Functions imposed by regulations relating to eligibility for free provision of personal care to persons living at home. |

  ;
- (l) omit the entry relating to Carers (Equal Opportunities) Act 2004[^f00028];
- (m) in Column 1 of the entry relating to the Children Act 2004[^f00029] for “, 13 to 16 and 31 to 34” substitute “and 13 to 16”;
- (n) omit the entry relating to the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^f00030];
- (o) omit the entry relating to the Children and Families (Wales) Measure 2010[^f00031];
- (p) omit the entry relating to the Social Care Charges (Wales) Measure 2010[^f00032];
- (q) omit the entry relating to the Carers Strategies (Wales) Measure 2010[^f00033];
- (r) omit the entry relating to the Mental Health (Wales) Measure 2010[^f00034].
