# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 92

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**92.**[^key-524a9ee9123f93ea5691b94544d7d93e] In section 26A[^f00110] (advocacy services)—

- (a) omit subsection (2A)[^f00111];
- (b) in subsections (3)(a) and (4)[^f00112], for “appropriate national authority” substitute “Secretary of State”.

## Annotations

[^key-524a9ee9123f93ea5691b94544d7d93e]: Commencement: Reg. 92 in force at 6.4.2016, see [reg. 2(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2016/413/regulation/2/1)

[^f00110]: Footnote: Section 26A was inserted by the Adoption and Children Act [2002 (c. 38)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/38), section 119.

[^f00111]: Footnote: Subsection (2A) was inserted by the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act [2003 (c. 43)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/43), section 116(3), and was amended by the Education and Inspections Act [2006 (c. 40)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/40), Schedule 14, paragraphs 9 and 11 and Part 5 of Schedule 18.

[^f00112]: Footnote: Subsections (3)(a) and (4) of section 26A were amended by the Children and Young Persons Act [2008 (c. 23)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/23), Schedule 3, paragraphs 1 and 18.
