# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 93

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**93.**[^c-28919029e8e20173f8e8bf0949637e1e] In section 27 (co-operation between authorities)—

- (a) after subsection (3)(d) insert—

  > - (da) any local authority in Wales,

  ;
- (b) in subsection (3)(e)[^f00113] for “appropriate national authority” substitute “Secretary of State”;
- (c) after subsection (3) insert—

  > (3A) The Secretary of State must not authorise the Welsh Ministers under subsection (3)(e) without their consent.
