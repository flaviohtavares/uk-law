# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 98

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**98.**[^c-4cba9ed445796418106a46851009d9c9] In section 34[^f00119] (parental contact etc. with children in care)—

- (a) in subsection (1) after “section 22(3)(a)” insert “or, where the local authority is in Wales, under section 78(1)(a) of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”;
- (b) after subsection (6A)[^f00120] insert—

  > (6B) Where (by virtue of an order under this section, or because subsection (6) applies) a local authority in Wales is authorised to refuse contact between the child and a person mentioned in any of paragraphs (a) to (c) of section 95(1) of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014, section 95(1) of that Act does not require the authority to promote contact between the child and that person.
