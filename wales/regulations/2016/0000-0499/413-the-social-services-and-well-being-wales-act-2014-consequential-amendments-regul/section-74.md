# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 74

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**74.**[^c-302f72d0bd11f18785e381d918185329] In section 23A[^f00086] (the responsible authority and relevant children)—

- (a) in subsection (2)(a) for “by any local authority” substitute “by any local authority in England or by any local authority in Wales”;
- (b) in subsection (3) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “Secretary of State”;
- (c) in subsection (5) for “appropriate national authority”, in both places that it appears, substitute “Secretary of State”.
