# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 117

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**117.**[^c-19d9fc529081c4a00188dfd19b95e6a2] In Schedule 2, for paragraph 20(1)(a)[^f00140] (death of children being looked after by local authorities) substitute—

> - (a) shall notify the Secretary of State and Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education, Children’s Services and Skills;
