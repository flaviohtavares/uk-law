# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 79

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**79.**[^c-855d7611b6f289b6d3708c635dee5666] In section 23D[^f00091] (personal advisers)—

- (a) in subsection (1) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “Secretary of State”;
- (b) in subsection (2) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “Secretary of State”.
