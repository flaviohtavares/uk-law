# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**4.**[^key-a2aac7d8be49ee856fc14083ab1911b2] In section 34(7A)[^f00008] (attendance at court of parent of child or young person charged with an offence etc.) after “Children Act 1989” insert “or section 76 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”.
