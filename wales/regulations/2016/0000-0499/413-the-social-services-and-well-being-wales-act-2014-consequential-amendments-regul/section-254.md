# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 254

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**254.**[^key-c9e091bfe4288d3602d6cae19d73c445] In section 30(8)[^f00203] (provision of vetting information) (until it ceases to have effect by virtue of section 72(1) of the Protection of Freedoms Act 2012)—

- (i) after “Health and Social Care Act [2001 (c 15)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/15),” omit “or”;
- (ii) after “Care Act 2014” insert “or in accordance with regulations made under sections 50 to 53 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”.
