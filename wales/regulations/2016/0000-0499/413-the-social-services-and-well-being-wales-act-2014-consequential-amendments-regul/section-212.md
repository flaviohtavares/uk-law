# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 212

**212.**[^c-104f73778d835ddbbc3f09bdc52a4e96] In section 325 (arrangements for assessing risks posed by certain offenders), in subsection (9), in the definition of “social services functions”[^f00190]—

- (a) after “given” insert

  > - —
  >   - (a) in relation to England,

  ;
- (b) at the end insert—

  > - (b) in relation to Wales, has the meaning given by section 143 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014

  .
