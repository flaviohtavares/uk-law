# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 80

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**80.**[^c-8a32324cc3d09cd2dc3efe51699b3281] In section 23E[^f00092] (pathway plans)—

- (a) in subsection (1) after “prescribed” insert “in regulations made by the Secretary of State”;
- (b) in subsection (1B) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “Secretary of State”;
- (c) in subsection (2) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “Secretary of State”.
