# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 214

**214.**[^key-bccaa1b0c3b03ac58557a039e16d94a3] In section 54 (restriction on disclosure of information) of the Public Audit (Wales) Act 2004, in subsection (5) for “the Local Authority Social Services Act [1970 (c. 42)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1970/42)” substitute “section 143 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”.
