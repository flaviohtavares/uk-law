# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**32.**[^key-c12a50879a2bb3a868dd44b85135cda7] Schedule 2 to the Reserve Forces Act 1980 (army and air-force pensioners and other former soldiers not liable to be recalled for service) is amended as follows—

- (a) in paragraph 2A[^f00037] after “2014” insert “or section 18(1) of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”;
- (b) omit paragraph 3(a).
