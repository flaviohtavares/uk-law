# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 127

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**127.**[^key-5cd65c1c686abc18efcbf569a9fac3a6] In section 48 (inspection of premises used for provision of community care services)—

- (a) in subsection (1)[^f00145] for “community care services” substitute “services under Part 4 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”;
- (b) in subsection (8) for “and “community care services”” to the end substitute “has the same meaning as in section 47”.
