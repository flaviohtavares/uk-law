# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**31.**[^c-46fd3cd2c39175686da958821d8270e7] In section 38 of the Land Compensation Act 1973 (amount of disturbance payment for persons who do not have compensatable interests), in subsection (3), in paragraph (a)[^f00036], for “section 29 of the National Assistance Act 1948” substitute “Part 4 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”.
