# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 108

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**108.**[^c-3a2bb1d7efd2ca131f516d7b37d3f521] In Schedule 2, in paragraph 4 (prevention of neglect and abuse)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (2)(b) after “another local authority” insert “or in the area of a local authority in Wales”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (2) after “that other local authority” insert “or the local authority in Wales, as the case may be”;
- (c) in sub-paragraph (3) after “that other local authority” insert “or the local authority in Wales”.
