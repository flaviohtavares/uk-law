# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**21.**[^c-6799abc9ad89f3fbf11e52b9f17d21a6] In section 6 (director of social services)—

- (a) in subsection (A1)[^f00016] omit “in England”;
- (b) omit subsection (1);
- (c) in subsection (2)[^f00017], in both places where it occurs, omit “or (as the case may be) social services”.
