# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 123

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**123.**[^key-76da6e8e42c975612f22283b382d0967] In section 27 of the Opticians Act 1989 (sale and supply of optical appliances), in subsection (3)(e)(i)[^f00142], for the words from “registered as blind” to the end, substitute “section 18(1) of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014.”
