# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 137

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**137.**[^c-9895c562a5e337c8413858d05b444273] In section 171ZS(1)[^f00154] (Part 12ZB: supplementary) in the definition of “local authority foster parent”, for “section 22C(12)” substitute “section 105(1)”.
