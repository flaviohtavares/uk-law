# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 157

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**157.**[^c-ff9ed3963631c134c6f916addbf5eff7] In section 562(3)[^f00170] (Act not to apply to persons detained under order of a court) after “Children Act 1989” insert “or section 119(4) of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (use of accommodation for restricting liberty)”.
