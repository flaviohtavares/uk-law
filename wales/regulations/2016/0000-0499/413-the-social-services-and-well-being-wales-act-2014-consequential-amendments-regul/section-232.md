# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 232

> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**232.**[^key-5939e867e7d7a8d80c54174ac185c7ab] For section 25(2) (charges where local authority provide childcare) substitute—

> (2) Subsection (1) does not apply to childcare provided under sections 37 to 39 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (meeting care and support needs of children), provision as to charges for such care being made by Part 5 of that Act.
