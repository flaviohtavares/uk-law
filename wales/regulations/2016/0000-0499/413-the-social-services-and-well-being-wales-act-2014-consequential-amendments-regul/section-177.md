# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 177

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**177.**[^c-c10981c72996472451646d3764b5e7d0] (1) The Carers and Disabled Children Act 2000 is repealed.

(2) The repeal of that Act does not affect the validity of anything done before these Regulations come into force under section 17B of the Children Act 1989, or under regulations made under that section, as it applies by virtue of section 7(2) of the Carers and Disabled Children Act 2000 (reference to Secretary of State in section 17B to be treated as reference to the National Assembly for Wales in so far as the section applies in relation to Wales).
