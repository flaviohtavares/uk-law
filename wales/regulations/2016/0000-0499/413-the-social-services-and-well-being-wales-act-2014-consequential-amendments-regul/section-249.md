# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 249

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**249.**[^key-b06a67e6dd04b4b3b5e71e38cf18cacc] In section 194 (power to make payments towards expenditure on community services)—

- (a) for subsection (1)(a), substitute—

  > - (a) a local social services authority in Wales towards expenditure incurred or to be incurred by it in connection with any of its functions which are social services functions for the purposes of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act [2014 (anaw 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2014/4),

  ;
- (b) after subsection (1)(a), insert—

  > - (aa) a local social services authority in England towards expenditure incurred or to be incurred by it in connection with any social services functions (within the meaning of the Local Authority Social Services Act [1970 (c. 42)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1970/42)), other than functions under section 3 of the Disabled Persons (Employment) Act [1958 (c. 33)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1958/33),

  .
