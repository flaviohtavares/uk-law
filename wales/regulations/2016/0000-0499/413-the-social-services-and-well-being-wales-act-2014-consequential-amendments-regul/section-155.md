# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 155

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**155.**[^c-f37c6ebd4cd6268099fb6e62ed4bb5dd] In section 515(4)[^f00168] (provision of teaching services for day nurseries), in the definition of “day nursery” after “and other children)” insert “or sections 37 to 39 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (meeting care and support needs of children)”.

[^f00168]: Footnote: Section 515(4) was substituted by the Childcare Act [2006 (c. 21)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/21), Schedule 2, paragraph 25(1), (4).
