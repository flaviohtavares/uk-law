# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 156

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**156.**[^key-bcbca1bb006b26428c5defb3381e2502] In section 535(4)[^f00169] (provision of teaching services for day nurseries), in the definition of “day nursery” after “and other children)” insert “or sections 37 to 39 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (meeting care and support needs of children)”.
