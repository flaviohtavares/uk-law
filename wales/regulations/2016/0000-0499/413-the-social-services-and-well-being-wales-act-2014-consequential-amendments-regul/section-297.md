# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 297

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**297.**[^c-3fb1a3847f18ddafc73305b0aceee82e] In section 53 (direct payments: further provision)—

- (a) omit subsection (11) from the English text[^f00207];
- (b) after subsection (10) insert the following—

  > (11) The ways in which a local authority may discharge its duty under section 117 of the Mental Health Act 1983 include by making direct payments; and for that purpose Schedule A1 (which includes modifications of sections 50 and 51 and this section) has effect.
