# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**20.**[^key-7622505b42e6eff3d75075802541d98f] In section 1[^f00015] (local authorities)—

- (a) for “the councils of non-metropolitan counties, metropolitan districts and London boroughs” substitute “the councils of non-metropolitan counties and metropolitan districts in England, the councils of London boroughs”;
- (b) omit from “but, in relation to Wales” to the end.
