# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 71

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**71.**[^c-c5b32a94155c37bee6af8c99a0b1d84c] For section 22E[^f00083] (children’s homes provided by appropriate national authority) substitute—

> **22E. Children’s homes provided by Secretary of State or Welsh Ministers**
>
> Where a local authority place a child they are looking after in a children’s home provided, equipped and maintained by the Secretary of State or the Welsh Ministers under section 82(5), they must do so on such terms as the Secretary of State or the Welsh Ministers (as the case may be) may from time to time determine.
