# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 128

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**128.**[^c-424742ba721ea0560c9e441a1c97cf33] In section 60(3)[^f00146] of the Criminal Justice Act 1991 (remands and committals to local authority accommodation)—

- (a) after “Children Act 1989” insert “or section 119 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”, and
- (b) for “of that Act” substitute “of the Children Act 1989”.
