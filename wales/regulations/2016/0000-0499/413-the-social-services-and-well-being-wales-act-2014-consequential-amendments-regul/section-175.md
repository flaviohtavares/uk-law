# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 175

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**175.**[^c-76d09f490c88a0489f7048fa682f03c6] In section 121 (general interpretation)—

- (a) in subsection (1)—
  - (i) in the definition of “local authority foster parent” after “has the same meaning as in” insert “section 105(1) of”;
  - (ii) in the definition of “social services functions” after “1970” insert “or the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”;
- (b) for subsection (10) substitute—

  > (10) References in this Act to a child being looked after by a local authority shall be construed—
  >
  > - (a) in relation to a local authority in England, in accordance with section 22 of the 1989 Act,
  > - (b) in relation to a local authority in Wales, in accordance with section 74 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014.
