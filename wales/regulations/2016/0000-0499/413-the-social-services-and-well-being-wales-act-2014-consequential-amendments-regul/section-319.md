# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 319

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**319.**[^key-d9360253950ab971aaca33e20792256f] In section 197 (general interpretation and index of defined expressions)—

- (a) in subsection (1) for the definition of “local authority foster parent” substitute—

  > - “local authority foster parent” (“*rhiant maeth awdurdod lleol*”) means a person authorised as such in accordance with regulations made by virtue of—
  >   - (a) sections 87 and 93;
  >   - (b) paragraph 12F of Schedule 2 to the Children Act 1989 (regulations providing for approval of local authority foster parents);

  ;
- (b) for subsection (2)(b) substitute—

  > - (b) a reference to a child looked after by a local authority in England has the same meaning as a reference in section 22 of the Children Act 1989 to a child who is looked after by a local authority in England;

  .
