# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 205

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**205.**[^key-13a150c79efd61513fb0657dda2c1670] In section 114 (complaints about social services)—

- (a) in subsection (2) for “under subsection (1)” substitute “under this section”;
- (b) omit subsections (3) and (4);
- (c) in subsection (5)—
  - (i) in paragraph (a) omit “in the case of regulations under subsection (1),”;
  - (ii) omit paragraph (b)[^f00188];
- (d) in the heading at the end insert “: England”.
