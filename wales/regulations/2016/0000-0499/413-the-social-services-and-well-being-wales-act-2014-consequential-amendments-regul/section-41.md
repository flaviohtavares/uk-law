# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**41.**[^key-47aa96897c30775597fd2e52b30938e4] In section 46 of the Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984 (burial and cremation), for subsection (2)[^f00046] substitute—

> (2) Any council which is the local authority for the purposes of the Local Authority Social Services Act 1970 may cause to be buried or cremated the body of any deceased person who immediately before his death was being provided with accommodation under Part 1 of the Care Act 2014.
>
> (2A) A local authority within the meaning of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 may cause to be buried or cremated the body of any deceased person who immediately before his death was being provided with accommodation under Part 4 of that Act.
