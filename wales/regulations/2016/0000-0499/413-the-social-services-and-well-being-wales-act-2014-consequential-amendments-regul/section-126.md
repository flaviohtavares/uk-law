# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 126

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**126.**[^key-894126a4750354cb2b1d2e310588ea83] In section 47 (assessment of needs for community care services)—

- (a) in subsection (1)[^f00143] omit “(in the case of a local authority in England) or of community care services (in the case of a local authority in Wales)”;
- (b) in subsection (2)[^f00144]—
  - (i) after “local authority” insert “in England”;
  - (ii) omit “, in the case of a local authority in England”;
- (c) omit subsection (7);
- (d) in subsection (8) for “and “community care services” have the same meanings as in section 46 above” substitute “means a county council in England, a district council for an area in England for which there is no county council, the council of a county or county borough in Wales, a London borough council or the Common Council of the City of London”.
