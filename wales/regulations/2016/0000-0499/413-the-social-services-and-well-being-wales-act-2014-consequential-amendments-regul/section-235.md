# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 235

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**235.**[^key-fcd1a84008dad786ef8591f76882036f] In section 93A(7)[^f00198] (recording and reporting the use of force by members of staff: England) in the definition of “parent”, after “Children Act 1989” insert “or section 76 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”.
