# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 245

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**245.**[^c-187ea0ef688494d5198a2179de17896f] In section 32 (supply of goods and services by local authorities), for subsection (4) substitute—

> (4) “services” means—
>
> - (a) in relation to a local authority in England, the services of persons employed by the authority for the purposes of its functions under the Local Authority Social Services Act [1970 (c. 42)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1970/42);
> - (b) in relation to a local authority in Wales, the services of persons employed by the authority for the purposes of functions which are social services functions for the purposes of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act [2014 (anaw 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2014/4).
