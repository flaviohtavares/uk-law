# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 293

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**293.**[^key-493fd69280922e6249f23a82ba5f80df] In section 62 of the Care Act 2014 (power to meet child’s carer’s needs for support) in subsection (3) for “under section 17 of the Children Act 1989.” substitute—

> under—
>
> - (a) section 17 of the Children Act 1989, or
> - (b) sections 37 to 39 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014.
