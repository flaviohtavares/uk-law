# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**70.**[^c-d9bbf9bb989ddcd2f8729936d7c2cbf4] In section 22C[^f00081] (ways in which looked after children are to be accommodated and maintained)—

- (a) in subsection (9A)[^f00082] omit “are a local authority in England and”;
- (b) in subsection (11) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “Secretary of State”;
- (c) for subsection (12) substitute—

  > (12) For the meaning of “local authority foster parent” see section 105(1).
