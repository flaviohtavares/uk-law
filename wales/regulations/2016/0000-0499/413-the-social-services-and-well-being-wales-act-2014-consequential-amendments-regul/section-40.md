# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**40.**[^key-bd3a530d85f267da5ea497cc254f7d4e] In section 22 (arrears of contributions charged on interest in land in England and Wales)—

- (a) after subsection (2B)[^f00043] insert—

  > (2C) A local authority in Wales may not create, or be required by directions under subsection (2A) to create, a charge under this section on or after the day on which section 2 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 came into force.

  ;
- (b) after subsection (3A)[^f00044] insert—

  > (3B) Subject to subsection (5) below, a charge under this section created by a local authority in Wales shall be in respect of any amount which is outstanding from time to time and is—
  >
  > - (a) assessed as due to be paid by the person to the authority for the Part III accommodation, or
  > - (b) due by the person to the authority under Part 5 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 in respect of meeting needs for care and support by the provision of accommodation for the person, including anything provided in connection with that accommodation.

  ;
- (c) in subsection (4)[^f00045] omit “Wales or”.
