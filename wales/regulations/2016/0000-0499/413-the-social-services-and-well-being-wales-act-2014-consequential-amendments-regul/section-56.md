# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 56

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**56.**[^key-cc0c85f89c99ffbe8a86a1b7f177f7fc] In subsection (11) of section 14F[^f00068] (special guardianship support services)—

- (a) after “local authority” in the first place it occurs insert “in England”;
- (b) at the end of the subsection insert “and see sections 164 and 164A of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 for provision about co-operation between local authorities in Wales and other bodies.”
