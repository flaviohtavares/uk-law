# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**15.**[^c-bd494febb47730abda367c58622e75f5] In section 63(8) (provision of instruction in certain activities connected with health or welfare), in the definition of “the relevant enactments”—

- (a) in paragraph (a)[^f00013] after “2006” insert “or for the purposes of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”;
- (b) in paragraph (b)[^f00014]—
  - (i) omit “Part III of the National Assistance Act 1948”;
  - (ii) omit “section 45 of this Act and”;
  - (iii) after “2006 Acts” insert “and Part 4 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”.
