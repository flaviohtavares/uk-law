# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 304

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**304.**[^key-a6512ebe18d80c35ca99fd069bd38049] In section 119 (use of accommodation for restricting liberty)—

- (a) in subsection (2)(c) after “local authority” insert “or a local authority in England”;
- (b) in subsection (6) for the words from “representation funded by” to “Criminal Defence Service” substitute “the provision of representation under Part 1 of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012”;
- (c) after subsection (10) insert—

  > (11) An order made under this section in relation to a child, if it would otherwise still be in force, ceases to have effect when the child reaches the age of 18.
