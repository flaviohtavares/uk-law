# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 268

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**268.**[^key-39b21e989afb54e62a6206bf2f84049a] (1) Section 24 (general interpretation) of the Learner Travel (Wales) Measure 2008 is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1) in the definition of “local authority” for “the Local Authority Social Services Act [1970 (c.42)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1970/42)” substitute “the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014.”

(3) In subsection (2) for “section 22(1) of the Children Act [1989 (c.41)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41)” substitute “section 74 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”.
