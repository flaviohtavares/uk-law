# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Commencement[^key-ebb31bff6a5ecd6f7027ac405ba6bb89]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations come into force on 6 April 2016 subject to paragraphs (2) to (6).

(2) Regulation 140 comes into force on the day that section 85D of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00002] comes into force.

(3) Regulation 158 comes into force on the day that section 562J of the Education Act 1996[^f00003] comes into force.

(4) Regulation 235 comes into force on the day that section 93A of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^f00004]comes into force.

(5) Regulation 253 comes into force on the day that the amendment to section 6 of the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006[^f00005] made by paragraph 8 of Schedule 14 to the Health and Social Care Act 2008[^f00006] comes into force.

(6) Regulations 322 and 323 come into force immediately after the amendment to the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014, which inserts into that Act, Schedule A1 (Direct Payments: After-care Under the Mental Health Act 1983), made by paragraph 1 of Part 2 of Schedule 4 to the Care Act 2014[^f00007], comes into force.
