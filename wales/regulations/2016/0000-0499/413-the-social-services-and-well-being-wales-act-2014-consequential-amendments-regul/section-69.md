# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 69

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**69.**[^key-185944217b8bfc4f50f249b7484afad9] In section 22 (general duty of local authority in relation to children looked after by them)—

- (a) in subsection (1)[^f00078] for “In this Act” substitute “In this section”;
- (b) in subsection (3B)[^f00079] omit “in England”;
- (c) in subsection (3C) omit “in England”;
- (d) in subsection (7)[^f00080]—
  - (i) for “appropriate national authority”, in both places that it appears, substitute “Secretary of State”, and
  - (ii) for “the local authority” substitute “the authority”.
