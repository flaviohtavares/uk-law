# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 131

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**131.**[^c-1b8da2e0b145e209cf456f3c20915822] In section 143 (meaning of “person responsible for child or qualifying young person”), in subsection (3)(c)[^f00148]—

- (a) omit sub-paragraph (i);
- (b) after sub-paragraph (vii) omit “or”;
- (c) after sub-paragraph (viii) insert—

  > or
  >
  > - (ix) Part 4 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014.
