# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**39.**[^c-eb4353c2bc28a64098d1714950789be1] In section 17 (charges for local authority services in England and Wales)—

- (a) omit subsections (2A)[^f00041] and (5);
- (b) in paragraph (a) of subsection (3) omit “or a service within subsection (2A)[^f00042]”;
- (c) in the heading to the section, omit the words “and Wales”.
