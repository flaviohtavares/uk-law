# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 120

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**120.**[^key-eb6d9b1f9873ed1344c9c687a3bcb897] In Schedule 2, in paragraph 25[^f00141] (regulations)—

- (a) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “Secretary of State”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (b) for “a local authority” substitute “they”;
- (c) for sub-paragraph (b)(ii) substitute—

  > - (ii) any other local authority under paragraph 24(2).
