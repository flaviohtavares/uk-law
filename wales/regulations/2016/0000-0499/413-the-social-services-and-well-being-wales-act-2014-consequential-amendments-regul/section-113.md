# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 113

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**113.**[^key-761c925c59c72c13dc64491a382d9ba3] In Schedule 2, in paragraph 12F[^f00137] (approval of local authority foster parents)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (1)(a) after “prescribed” insert “in regulations made by the Secretary of State”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (1)(b) for “that national authority” substitute “the Secretary of State”;
- (c) in the following provisions for “appropriate national authority” in each place that it occurs, substitute “Secretary of State”—
  - (i) sub-paragraph (1)(b);
  - (ii) sub-paragraph (4);
  - (iii) sub-paragraph (5);
  - (iv) sub-paragraph (6);
  - (v) sub-paragraph (7);
  - (vi) sub-paragraph (8);
  - (vii) sub-paragraph (9);
- (d) omit sub-paragraph (10);
- (e) in sub-paragraph (11), in the definition of “organisation” after “includes” insert “the Welsh Ministers,”.
