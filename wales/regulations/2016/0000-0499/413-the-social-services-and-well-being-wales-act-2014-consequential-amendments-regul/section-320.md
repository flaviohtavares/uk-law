# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 320

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**320.**[^key-953a25652ff284294b7a3114467d6efe] In Schedule 1, in paragraph 3 (contribution orders) at the end insert—

> (12) A contribution order in relation to a child, if it would otherwise still be in force, ceases to have effect when the child reaches the age of 18.
