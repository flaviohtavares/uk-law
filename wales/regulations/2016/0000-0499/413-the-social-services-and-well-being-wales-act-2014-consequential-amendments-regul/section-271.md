# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 271

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**271.**[^c-a63605991949ac9374af8ebe28f795cb] In section 50[^f00206] (interpretation of Part 2), in the definition of “community care services” for paragraph (aa) substitute—

> - (aa) in relation to Wales, services under Part 4 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 or section 117 of the Mental Health Act 1983;

.
