# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**44.**[^c-bbf643be3fd479946310a80af3fa8f5b] In section 104 (travel concessions) in subsection (2)(b)[^f00048], for paragraph (ii) substitute—

> - (ii) by any local authority within the meaning of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 in the discharge of their functions under Part 4 of that Act (meeting needs).
