# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**37.**[^key-793d624af260b9f22bb5cb45ad1333af] In section 135[^f00040] (warrant to search for and remove patients to place of safety), in subsection (6) for “Part III of the National Assistance Act 1948” substitute “Part 4 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”.
