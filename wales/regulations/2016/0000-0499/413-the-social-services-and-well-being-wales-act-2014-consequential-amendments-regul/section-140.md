# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 140

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**140.**[^key-48f87cec553311a96d11c51216b32faf] In section 85D(7) of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992[^f00157] (recording and reporting the use of force by members of staff: England) after “Children Act 1989” insert “or section 76 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”.
