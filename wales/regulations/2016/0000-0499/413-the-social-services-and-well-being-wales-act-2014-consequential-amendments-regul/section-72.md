# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 72

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413

> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**72.**[^c-48d560c31d5f256691ad93d08b3acfa7] In section 23ZA(3)(a)[^f00084] (duty of local authority to ensure visits to, and contact with, looked after children and others) for “appropriate national authority” substitute “Secretary of State”.
