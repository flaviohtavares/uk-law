# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**58.**[^c-f9882f8682066f897c34107013937ab9] In section 17 (provision of services for children in need, their families and others)—

- (a) in subsection (4)[^f00069] for “appropriate national authority” substitute “Secretary of State”;
- (b) at the end of the section insert—

  > (13) The duties imposed on a local authority by virtue of this section do not apply in relation to a child in the authority’s area who is being looked after by a local authority in Wales in accordance with Part 6 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014.
