# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 105

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**105.**[^key-388c17ea5c4dffaf3886dac4d8901b77] For section 104A[^f00132] (regulations and orders made by the Welsh Ministers under Part 3 etc.) substitute—

> **104A. Regulations made by the Welsh Ministers under section 31A or Part 7**
>
> (1) Any power of the Welsh Ministers under section 31A or Part 7 to make regulations shall be exercisable by statutory instrument.
>
> (2) Any such statutory instrument shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of the National Assembly for Wales.
