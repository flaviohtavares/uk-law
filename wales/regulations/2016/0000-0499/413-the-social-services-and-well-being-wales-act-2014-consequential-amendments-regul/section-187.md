# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 187

*Extent: E+W*

**187.**[^c-541a09a354a66e459ec37ca35832fa3e] In section 4 of the Homelessness Act 2002 (interpretation for sections 1 to 3), in the definition of “social services authority”, after “[1970 (c. 42)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1970/42)” insert “or Part 8 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”.
