# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 82

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**82.**[^key-270e4b3279ed09ceb3c88d6d7d8c4060] In section 24A[^f00097] (advice and assistance)—

- (a) in subsection (2)(b) after “local authority,” insert “or local authority in Wales”;
- (b) in subsection (3)(a) after “local authority” insert “(without subsequently being looked after by a local authority in Wales),”.
