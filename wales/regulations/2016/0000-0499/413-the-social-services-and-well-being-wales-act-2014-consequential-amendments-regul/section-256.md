# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 256

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**256.**[^key-37240c536833f73857caa5889c533985] (1) Section 38 of the Income Tax Act 2007 is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (2), for paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) registered as a severely sight-impaired adult in a register kept under section 18(1) of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (registers kept by local authorities in Wales).

(3) In subsection (4)[^f00204]—

- (a) in paragraph (a)—
  - (i) omit “as a blind person in a register kept under section 29 of the National Assistance Act 1948 or”;
  - (ii) after “Care Act 2014” insert “or section 18(1) of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”;
- (b) in paragraph (b), omit “of blindness or”.
