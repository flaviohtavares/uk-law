# The Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (Consequential Amendments) Regulations 2016, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/wsi/2016/413
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**47.**[^c-1c22056737d1fd0a4835a9164a0a1324] In section 2(5) (rights of authorised representatives of disabled persons)—

- (a) in paragraph (b)[^f00049], for “Part III of the 1948 Act or Part 1 of the Care Act 2014” substitute “Part 1 of the Care Act 2014 or Part 4 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”;
- (b) in paragraph (bb)[^f00050] after “Part III of the Children Act 1989” insert “, or under Part 6 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”;
- (c) in paragraph (c)[^f00051], for “section 26 of the 1948 Act or Part 1 of the Care Act 2014” substitute “Part 1 of the Care Act 2014 or, in Wales, in compliance with a local authority’s duty to meet the needs of the disabled person pursuant to Part 4 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”; and
- (d) in paragraph (cc)[^f00052] after “under section 17 of the Children Act 1989” insert “, or under Part 4 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014”.
