## 2. Revocations and disapplication[^c-4d3b794f6b7b3c613b5eee55eff64625]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Regulations specified in the first column of Schedule 1 are revoked to the extent specified in relation to each in the third column.

(2) The Regulations specified in the first column of Schedule 2 are disapplied in relation to Wales to the extent specified in relation to each in the third column.
