# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2016, section 53

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 53. Development in Wales likely to have significant effects in (affected by repeal, see note) an (affected by repeal, see note) EEA State (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d][^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d][^c-62704c6c5cd4b1c9f4d8f8e2a2648fb8][^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d][^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (a) it comes to the attention of the Welsh Ministers that development proposed to be carried out in Wales is the subject of an EIA application and is likely to have significant effects on the environment in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] an[^c-fc9d1ce083cfffb09bd9822c57dbe9a7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] EEA State; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) an[^c-0ed72ea78b5c14d5291bd31700c257d6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] EEA State likely to be significantly affected by such development so requests, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

the Welsh Ministers must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (i) send to the EEA State as soon as possible and no later than their date of publication in The London Gazette referred to in paragraph (ii) below, the particulars mentioned in paragraph (2) and, if relevant, the information referred to in paragraph (3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] publish the information in paragraph (i) above in a notice placed in The London Gazette indicating the address where additional information is available; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] give the EEA State a reasonable time in which to indicate whether it wishes to participate in the procedure for which these Regulations provide. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

(2) The particulars referred to in paragraph (1)(b)(i) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (a) a description of the development, together with any available information on its possible significant effect on the environment in another Member State; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) information on the nature of the decision which may be taken. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

(3) Where an EEA State indicates, in accordance with paragraph (1)(b)(iii), that it wishes to participate in the procedure for which these Regulations provide, the Welsh Ministers must as soon as possible send to that EEA State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (a) a copy of the application concerned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) a copy of any planning permission relating to the development; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (c) a copy of any environmental statement in respect of the development; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (d) relevant information regarding the procedure under these Regulations, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

but only to the extent that such information has not been provided to the EEA State earlier in accordance with paragraph (1)(b)(i). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

(4) The Welsh Ministers must also— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (a) arrange for the particulars and information referred to in paragraphs (2) and (3) and any further information and any other information to be made available, within a reasonable time, to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] authorities in the relevant EEA State which that state has designated as responsible for performing the duties arising from the Directive[^c-6a2f20a57808489df2c7d1c184feedc5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] and the public concerned in the territory of the EEA State likely to be significantly affected; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) ensure that those authorities and the public concerned are given an opportunity, before planning permission for the development is granted, to forward to the Welsh Ministers, within a reasonable time, their opinion on the information supplied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

(5) The Welsh Ministers must *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] [^c-7401e3351c1f56b224835912468e5a15]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]—

- (a) enter into consultations with the EEA State concerned regarding, inter alia, the potential significant effects of the development on the environment of that EEA State and the measures envisaged to reduce or eliminate such effects; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) determine in agreement with the other EEA State a reasonable period of time for the duration of the consultation period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

(6) Where an EEA State has been consulted in accordance with paragraph (5), on the determination of the application concerned the Welsh Ministers must inform the EEA State of the decision and must forward to it a statement of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (a) the content of the decision and any conditions attached to it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) the main reasons and considerations on which the decision is based including, if relevant, information about the participation of the public; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (c) a description, where necessary, of the main measures to avoid, reduce and, if possible, offset the major adverse effects of the development. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
