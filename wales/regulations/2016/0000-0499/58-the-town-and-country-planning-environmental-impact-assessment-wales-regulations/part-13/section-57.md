# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2016, section 57

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 57. Extension of the period for an authority's decision on a planning application (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d][^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of section 78 of the 1990 Act (right to appeal against planning decisions and failure to take such decisions), in determining the time which has elapsed without the relevant planning authority giving notice to the applicant of their decision, where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (a) the authority have notified an applicant in accordance with regulation 10(1) that the submission of an environmental statement is required; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) the Welsh Ministers have given a screening direction in relation to the development in question, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (c) no account is to be taken of any period before the issue of the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

(2)[^c-e0ea8303906b40e3c8e6e707d2d975d9] Where it falls to an authority to determine an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] EIA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] application, articles 22 (time periods for decisions) and 23 (applications made under planning condition) of the 2012 Order have effect as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (a) each of the references in articles 22(2)(a) and 23 to a period of 8 weeks is a reference to a period of 16 weeks; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) the reference in article 22(2)(aa) to the period of 12 weeks is a reference to the period of 20 weeks. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
