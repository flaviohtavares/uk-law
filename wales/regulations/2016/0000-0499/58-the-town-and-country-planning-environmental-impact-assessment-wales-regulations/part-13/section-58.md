# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2016, section 58

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Extension of the power to provide in a development order for the giving of directions as respects the manner in which planning applications are dealt with (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d][^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

*Extent: E+W*

Provisions included in a development order by virtue of section 60 of the 1990 Act (permission granted by development order) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] [^c23296961] which enable the Welsh Ministers to give directions, must enable them to direct that development which is both of a description mentioned in Column 1 of the table in Schedule 2, and of a class described in the direction is EIA development for the purposes of these Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
