# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2016, section 60

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 60. Revocation of statutory instruments and transitional provisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d][^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The statutory instruments in Schedule 8 are revoked, to the extent shown in that Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

(2) Nothing in paragraph (1) affects the continued application of the instruments revoked by that paragraph, and these Regulations do not apply, in relation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (a) any application lodged or received by an authority before the commencement date, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) any undetermined ROMP application to which those instruments apply in accordance with the Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Undetermined Reviews of Old Mineral Permissions) (Wales) Regulations 2009, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (c) any appeal in relation to an application under sub-paragraph (a) or (b), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (d) any matter in relation to which a local planning authority have, before that date, issued an enforcement notice under section 172 of the 1990 Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

(3) In this regulation, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]**ROMP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]**” (“***ROMP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]***”) and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]**ROMP application *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]**” (“***cais ROMP *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]***”) have the same meaning as in regulation 52(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
