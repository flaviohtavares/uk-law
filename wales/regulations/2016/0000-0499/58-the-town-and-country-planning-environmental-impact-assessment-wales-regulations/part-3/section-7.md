# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2016, section 7

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Applications which appear to require screening opinion (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d][^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

*Extent: E+W*

Where it appears to the relevant planning authority that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (a) an application which is before them for determination is a Schedule 1 application or a Schedule 2 application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) the development in question has not been the subject of a screening opinion or screening direction; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (c) the application is not accompanied by a statement referred to by the applicant as an environmental statement for the purposes of these Regulations, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

paragraphs (4) and (5) of regulation 5 apply as if the receipt or lodging of the application were a request made under regulation 5(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
