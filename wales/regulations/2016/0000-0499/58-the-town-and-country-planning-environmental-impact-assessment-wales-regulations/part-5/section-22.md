# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2016, section 22

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22. Further information and evidence in respect of environmental statements (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d][^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a relevant planning authority, the Welsh Ministers or inspector dealing with an application or appeal in relation to which the applicant or appellant has submitted an environmental statement, are of the opinion that the statement should contain additional information in order to be an environmental statement, the relevant planning authority, the Welsh Ministers or inspector must notify the applicant or appellant accordingly and the applicant or appellant must provide that additional information; and such additional information is referred to in these Regulations as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]**further information *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]**” (“***gwybodaeth bellach *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]***”).

(2) Paragraphs (3) to (9) apply in relation to further information and any other information except in so far as— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (a) the further information and any other information is provided for the purposes of an inquiry or hearing held under the 1990 Act; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) the request for the further information made pursuant to paragraph (1) stated that it was to be provided for such purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

(3) The recipient of further information or any other information must publish in a local newspaper circulating in the locality in which the land is situated a notice stating— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (a) the name of the applicant for planning permission or subsequent consent, or the appellant (as the case may be), and the name and address of the relevant planning authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) the date on which the application was made and, if it is the case, that it has been referred to the Welsh Ministers for determination or is the subject of an appeal to the Welsh Ministers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (c) in the case of a subsequent application, sufficient information to enable the planning permission for the development to be identified; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (d) the address or location and the nature of the proposed development; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (e) that further information or any other information is available in relation to an environmental statement which has already been provided; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (f) that a copy of the further information or any other information and of any environmental statement which relates to any planning permission or subsequent application may be inspected by members of the public at all reasonable hours; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (g) an address in the locality in which the land is situated at which the further information or any other information may be inspected and the latest date on which it will be available for inspection (being a date not less than 21 days later than the date on which the notice is published); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (h) an address in the locality in which the land is situated (whether or not the same as that given pursuant to sub-paragraph (g)) at which copies of the further information or any other information may be obtained; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (i) that copies may be obtained there so long as stocks last; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (j) if a charge is to be made for a copy, the amount of the charge; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (k) that any person wishing to make representations about the further information or any other information should make them, before the date stated in accordance with sub-paragraph (g), to the relevant planning authority, the Welsh Ministers or the inspector (as the case may be); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (l) the address to which representations should be sent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

(4) The recipient of the further information or any other information must send a copy of it to each person to whom, in accordance with these Regulations, the statement to which it relates was sent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

(5) Where the recipient of the further information or any other information is the relevant planning authority they must send to the Welsh Ministers one copy of the further information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

(6) The recipient of the further information may by notice require the applicant or appellant to provide such number of copies of the further information or any other information as is specified in the notice (being the number required for the purposes of paragraph (4) or (5)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

(7) Where information is requested under paragraph (1) or any other information is provided, the relevant planning authority, the Welsh Ministers or the inspector, as the case may be,— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (a) must suspend determination of the application or appeal; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) must not determine it before— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
  - (i) the expiry of 21 days after the date on which the further information or any other information was sent to all persons to whom the statement to which it relates was sent, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] the expiry of 21 days after the date that notice of it was published in a local newspaper, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

  whichever is the later. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

(8) The applicant or appellant who provides further information or any other information, in accordance with paragraph (1) must ensure that a reasonable number of copies of the information are available at the address named in the notice published pursuant to paragraph (3)(h) as the address at which such copies may be obtained. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

(9) A reasonable charge reflecting printing and distribution costs may be made to a member of the public for a copy of the further information or any other information, made available in accordance with paragraph (8). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

(10) The relevant planning authority or the Welsh Ministers or an inspector may require an applicant or appellant to produce such evidence as they may reasonably call for to verify any information in the environmental statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
