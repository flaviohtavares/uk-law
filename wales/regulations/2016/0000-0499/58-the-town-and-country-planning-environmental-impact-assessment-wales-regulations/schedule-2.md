# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2016, SCHEDULE 2

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 2 (affected by repeal, see note) — Descriptions of development and applicable thresholds and criteria for the purposes of the definition of “Schedule 2 development” (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d][^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 2(1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

**1.** In the table below— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- “**area of the works *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]**” (“***arwynebedd gwaith *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]***”) includes any area occupied by apparatus, equipment, machinery, materials, plant, spoil heaps or other facilities or stores required for construction or installation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- “**controlled waters *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]**” (“***dyfroedd a reolir *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]***”) has the same meaning as in the Water Resources Act 1991 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] [^c23297041];
- “**floorspace *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]**” (“***arwynebedd llawr *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]***”) means the floorspace in a building or buildings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
