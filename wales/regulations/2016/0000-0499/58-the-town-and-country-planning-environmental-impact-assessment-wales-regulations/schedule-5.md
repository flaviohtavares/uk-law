# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2016, SCHEDULE 5

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 5 (affected by repeal, see note) — Local Development Orders (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d][^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 38(3) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

**1.** In a case to which this Schedule has effect, these Regulations apply, subject to the following modifications. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

**2.** Regulations 3, 5 to 12, 18 and 19 do not apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

**3.** In regulation 4— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (a) paragraph (2)(a) does not apply; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) in paragraph (2)(b), (5) and (10), for “relevant” read “local”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (c) read as if paragraph (7)(b) were omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

**4.** Regulation 13 is to be read as if it provided— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

> (1) Where a proposed local development order is EIA development, the local planning authority must state its opinion as to the information to be provided in the environmental statement (“a scoping opinion”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
>
> (2) A scoping opinion under paragraph (1) must include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
>
> - (a) a plan sufficient to identify the land; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
> - (b) a brief description of the nature and purpose of the development and of its possible effects on the environment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
> - (c) such other information or representations as the local planning authority may wish to provide or make. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
>
> (3) An authority must not adopt a scoping opinion until they have consulted the consultees. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
>
> (4) Before adopting a screening opinion the authority must take into account— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
>
> - (a) the specific characteristics of the particular development; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
> - (b) the specific characteristics of development of the type concerned; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
> - (c) the environmental features likely to be affected by the development. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

**5.** Regulation 15 is to be read as if it provided— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

> **15.** (1) A local planning authority which intends to prepare an environmental statement may enquire of a consultee whether the consultee has any information which the consultee or the local planning authority considers relevant to the preparation of the environmental statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
>
> (2) If the consultee has such information they must treat the enquiry by the local planning authority as a request for information by the local planning authority under regulation 5(1) of the Environmental Information Regulations 2004 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] [^c23297081].

**6.** Regulation 16 is to be read as if it provided— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

> **16.** (1) Where a statement, referred to as an “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]**environmental statement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]**”, has been prepared in relation to EIA development for which a local planning authority proposes to grant planning permission by a local development order, the local planning authority must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
>
> - (a) send a copy of the statement to the consultees and inform them that they may make representations; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
> - (b) notify any particular person of whom the authority are aware, who is likely to be affected by, or has an interest in, the application, who is unlikely to become aware of it by means of electronic publication, a site notice or by local advertisement, of an address in the locality in which the land is situated where a copy of the statement may be obtained and the address to which representations may be sent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
>
> (2) The local planning authority must not make the local development order until the expiry of 21 days from the last date on which a copy of the statement was served in accordance with this regulation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
