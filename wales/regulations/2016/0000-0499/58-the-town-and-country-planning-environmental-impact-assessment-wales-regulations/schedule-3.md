# The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2016, SCHEDULE 3

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 3 (affected by repeal, see note) — Selection criteria for screening Schedule 2 development (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d][^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 4(6) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

### 1. Characteristics of development (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

The characteristics of development must be considered having regard, in particular, to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (a) the size of the development; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) the cumulation with other development; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (c) the use of natural resources; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (d) the production of waste; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (e) pollution and nuisances; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (f) the risk of accidents, having regard in particular to substances or technologies used. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

### 2. Location of development (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

The environmental sensitivity of geographical areas likely to be affected by development must be considered, having regard, in particular, to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (a) the existing land use; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) the relative abundance, quality and regenerative capacity of natural resources in the area; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (c) the absorption capacity of the natural environment, paying particular attention to the following areas— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
  - (i) wetlands; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] coastal zones; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] mountain and forest areas; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] nature reserves and parks; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
  - (v) areas classified or protected under Member States' legislation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] or under the legislation of any part of the UK,[^key-ac38bdc70d7fe10a83d66dd781544b79] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] areas designated *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] [^key-f37c1a37cb761a5ae31ef50943474547]... under EU-derived domestic legislation which transposed[^key-bd7fff730746c086c431ab6c2ab42f2c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] Council Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] [2009/147/EC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/147)[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] on the conservation of wild birds *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] [^c23297061] and Council Directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] [92/43/EEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1992/43)[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] on the conservation of natural habitats and of wild fauna and flora *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] [^c23297071];
  - (vi (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] areas in which the environmental quality standards laid down in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] retained EU law[^key-fc25118f2529d820257a34fd9097ed34] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] have already been exceeded; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
  - (vii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] densely populated areas; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
  - (viii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d] landscapes of historical, cultural or archaeological significance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

### 3. Characteristics of the potential impact (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

The potential significant effects of development must be considered in relation to criteria set out under paragraphs 1 and 2 above, and having regard in particular to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]

- (a) the extent of the impact (geographical area and size of the affected population); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (b) the transfrontier nature of the impact; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (c) the magnitude and complexity of the impact; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (d) the probability of the impact; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
- (e) the duration, frequency and reversibility of the impact. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_209d1942-cb0d-4153-dd8a-58d3bb91c88d]
