# The National Health Service (Ophthalmic Services) (Wales) Regulations 2023, section 10

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. Duty to prepare a combined list[^key-41d5844bda1af3de6f61131f39293a4a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Each Local Health Board must prepare, for its area, a combined list.

(2) The combined list must consist of—

- (a) a list of the qualified practitioners who undertake to provide primary ophthalmic services and have been approved by the Local Health Board under regulation 13 (“the ophthalmic list”), and
- (b) in a separate part, a list of the qualified practitioners approved by the Local Health Board for the purpose of assisting in the provision of primary ophthalmic services under regulation 13 (“the supplementary list”).

(3) The combined list must contain the information set out in Part 1 of Schedule 3.
