# The National Health Service (Ophthalmic Services) (Wales) Regulations 2023, section 32

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 32. Arrangements for the provision of primary ophthalmic services[^key-36449dc4ff3dad3ad2fb497a58869657]

*Extent: E+W*

A Local Health Board’s arrangements for the provision of primary ophthalmic services must incorporate—

- (a) the terms of service,
- (b) the Statement (in accordance with which the Local Health Board is required to make payments to contractors for the provision of general ophthalmic services), and
- (c) any directions given by the Welsh Ministers under section 12(3) of the Act (functions of Local Health Boards) relating to remuneration for the provision of eye examination services or compliance with other provisions of the terms of service (in accordance with which the Local Health Board is required to make payments to contractors) (collectively, the “fee directions”).
