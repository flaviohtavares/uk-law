# The National Health Service (Ophthalmic Services) (Wales) Regulations 2023, section 4A

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4A. Issue of orders signed by a qualifying optometrist[^c-c7d9518d344e8d5f5af7bc13232151b5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A qualifying optometrist must order ophthalmic listed medicines or ophthalmic listed appliances as are required for the treatment of any patient to whom it is providing services under the contract by issuing to the patient an order signed by a qualifying optometrist.

(2) Every order must—

- (a) be signed by the qualifying optometrist,
- (b) be issued separately to each patient to whom the contractor is providing services under the contract, and
- (c) be requested on a form that is supplied for the purposes of this paragraph by the Local Health Board.
