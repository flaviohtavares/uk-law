## 4. General ophthalmic services and primary ophthalmic services[^c-275dcbc638845114a114df5fc0cba956]

*Extent: E+W*

In these Regulations—

- (a) “**general ophthalmic services**” means the services that a contractor must provide under paragraph 23 (testing of sight) of the terms of service, and
- (b) “**primary ophthalmic services**” means, collectively—
  - (i) general ophthalmic services, and
  - (ii) eye examination services.
