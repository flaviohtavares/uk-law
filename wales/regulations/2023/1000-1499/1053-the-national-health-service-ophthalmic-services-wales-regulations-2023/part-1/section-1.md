# The National Health Service (Ophthalmic Services) (Wales) Regulations 2023, section 1

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Title, coming into force and application[^c-96367a1e87e0d18af8b5b53104305021]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The title of these Regulations is the National Health Service (Ophthalmic Services) (Wales) Regulations 2023.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 20 October 2023.

(3) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.
