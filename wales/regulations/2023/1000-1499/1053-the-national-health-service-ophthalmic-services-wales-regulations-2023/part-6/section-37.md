# The National Health Service (Ophthalmic Services) (Wales) Regulations 2023, section 37

> Text as at 2025-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Publication of information[^key-b84d1229479e86d58fe702cfa852b731]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Local Health Board must make available for inspection at its offices, and at such other places in its area as appear to the Local Health Board to be convenient for informing any person interested, copies of—

- (a) these Regulations, and
- (b) the Statement.

(2) The Local Health Board is not required to make available copies of both documents referred to in paragraph (1) at every place at which it makes available copies of either of them.
