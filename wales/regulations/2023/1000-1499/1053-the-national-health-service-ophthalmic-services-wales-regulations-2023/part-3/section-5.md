## 5. Eligibility for a sight test[^c-66360f861798ade6dc7eb7282eb167bf]

*Extent: E+W*

A person who meets any of the criteria in paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 is eligible for a sight test under general ophthalmic services (and is referred to in these Regulations as an “**eligible person**”).
