# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) (Wales) Regulations 2023, section 26

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Sub-contracting[^key-70a8aed09ea31063e06e4f1580c939b2]

*Extent: E+W*

A contract must contain terms which prevent a contractor from sub-contracting any of its obligations under the contract in relation to clinical matters, or non-clinical matters directly affecting patients, except in the circumstances provided for by paragraph 76 of Schedule 3.
