# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) (Wales) Regulations 2023, section 24

> Text as at 2026-09-30.

## 24. Dataset and business rules[^c-22a7b7120b5cb2c3c85a98da31b158f0][^c-378a6f3e9f0bbab47deb66e2108dfbf1]

*Extent: E+W*

A contract must contain a term requiring contractors to provide data, where applicable, in accordance with the business rules utilised within the Assurance Framework.
