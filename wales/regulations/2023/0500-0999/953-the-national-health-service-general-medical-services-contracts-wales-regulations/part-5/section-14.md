# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) (Wales) Regulations 2023, section 14

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Health service contract[^c-ef36211935132732aea03ee18d0fc228]

*Extent: E+W*

If, by virtue of regulation 10 or 11, a contractor is to be regarded as a health service body, the contract must state that it is an NHS contract.
