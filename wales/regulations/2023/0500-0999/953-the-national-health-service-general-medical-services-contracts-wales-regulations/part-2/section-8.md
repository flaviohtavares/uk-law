# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) (Wales) Regulations 2023, section 8

> Text as at 2026-09-30.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 8. Right of appeal[^key-edbb34b6655f832cd66fdaaf557b6e48]

*Extent: E+W*

A person who has been given a notice by the Local Health Board under regulation 7(1) may appeal to the First-tier Tribunal against the decision of the Local Health Board that the conditions in regulation 5 or 6 are not met.
