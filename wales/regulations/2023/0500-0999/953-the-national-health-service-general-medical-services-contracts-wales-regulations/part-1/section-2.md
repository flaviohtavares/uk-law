# The National Health Service (General Medical Services Contracts) (Wales) Regulations 2023, section 2

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Application[^key-0851e9b63478886bf55864dd61acdc17]

*Extent: E+W*

These Regulations apply in relation to Wales and to a contract—

- (a) to which the 2004 Regulations[^key-dc147043e8367d76f3bb6727278f8c04] applied immediately before the date on which these Regulations come into force, or
- (b) which is entered into between a contractor and a Local Health Board on or after that date.
