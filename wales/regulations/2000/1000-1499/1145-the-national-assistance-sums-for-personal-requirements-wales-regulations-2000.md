# The National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements) (Wales) Regulations 2000

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2000 No. 1145 (W.81)

NATIONAL ASSISTANCE SERVICES, WALES

The National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements) (Wales) Regulations 2000

Made

6th April 2000

Coming into force

10th April 2000

The National Assembly for Wales makes the following Regulations in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State by section 22(4) of the National Assistance Act 1948[^f00001] and of all other powers enabling the Secretary of State in that behalf, and now vested in the National Assembly for Wales[^f00002]:–

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements) (Wales) Regulations 2000, and shall come into force on 10th April 2000.

(2) These Regulations shall apply to Wales only.

## 2. Sum needed for personal requirements

The sum which under section 22(4) of the National Assistance Act 1948 a local authority shall assume that a person will need for that person’s personal requirements shall be £15.45 per week.

## 3. Revocation

The National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements) Regulations 1999[^f00003], in so far as they apply to Wales, are hereby revoked.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00004].

D. Elis Thomas

The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

6th April 2000

(This note does not form part of the Regulations.)

These Regulations set out the weekly sum which local authorities in Wales are to assume, in the absence of special circumstances, that residents in accommodation arranged under Part III of the National Assistance Act 1948 will need for their personal requirements.

From 10th April 2000 all such residents will be assumed to need £15.45 per week for their personal requirements.

These Regulations supersede in Wales the National Assistance (Sums for Personal Requirements) Regulations 1999 (which provided for the sum residents were assumed to need from 12th April 1999) which are revoked in so far as they apply to Wales.
