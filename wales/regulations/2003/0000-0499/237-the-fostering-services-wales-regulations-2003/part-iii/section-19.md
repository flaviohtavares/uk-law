# The Fostering Services (Wales) Regulations 2003, section 19

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 19. Staffing of fostering service

The fostering service provider must ensure that there is, having regard to—

- (a) the size of the fostering service, its statement of purpose, and the numbers and needs of the children placed by it; and
- (b) the need to safeguard and promote the health and welfare of children placed with foster parents,

a sufficient number of suitably qualified, competent and experienced persons working for purposes of the fostering service.
