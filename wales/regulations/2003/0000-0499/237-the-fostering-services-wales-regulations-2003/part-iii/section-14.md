# The Fostering Services (Wales) Regulations 2003, section 14

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 14. Duty to promote contact

The fostering service provider must, subject to the provisions of the foster placement agreement and any court order relating to contact, promote contact between a child placed with a foster parent and the child’s parents, relatives and friends unless such contact is not reasonably practicable or consistent with the child’s welfare.
