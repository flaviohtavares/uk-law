# The Fostering Services (Wales) Regulations 2003, section 36

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 36. Termination of placements

(1) A responsible authority must not allow the placement of a child with a particular person to continue if it appears to them that the placement is no longer the most suitable way of performing their duty under (as the case may be) section 22(3) or 61(1)(a) and (b) of the 1989 Act.

(2) Where it appears to an area authority that continuation of a placement would be detrimental to the welfare of the child concerned, the area authority must remove the child forthwith.

(3) An area authority which removes a child under paragraph (2) must forthwith notify the responsible authority.
