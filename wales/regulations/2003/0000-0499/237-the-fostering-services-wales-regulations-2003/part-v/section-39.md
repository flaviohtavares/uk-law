# The Fostering Services (Wales) Regulations 2003, section 39

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 39. Placements outside Wales

(1) A voluntary organisation must not place a child outside the British Islands[^f00016].

(2) Where a responsible authority makes arrangements to place a child outside Wales it must ensure, so far as reasonably practicable, that the requirements which would have applied under these Regulations had the child been placed in Wales, are complied with.

## Annotations

[^f00016]: Footnote: British Islands are defined in the Interpretation Act [1978 (c. 30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/30) as meaning the United Kingdom, the Channel Islands and the Isle of Man.
