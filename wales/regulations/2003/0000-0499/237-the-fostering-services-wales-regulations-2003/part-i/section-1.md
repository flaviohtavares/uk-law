# The Fostering Services (Wales) Regulations 2003, section 1

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Fostering Services (Wales) Regulations 2003 and are to come into force on 1st April 2003.

(2) These Regulations apply to Wales only.
