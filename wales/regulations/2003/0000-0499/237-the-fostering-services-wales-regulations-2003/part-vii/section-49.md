# The Fostering Services (Wales) Regulations 2003, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. Compliance with regulations

Where there is more than one registered person in respect of a fostering agency, anything which is required under these regulations to be done by the registered person, if done by one of the registered persons, will not be required to be done by any of the other registered persons.
