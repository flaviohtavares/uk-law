# The Animal Gatherings (Interim Measures) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 169 (W.29)

ANIMALS, WALES

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Animal Gatherings (Interim Measures) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2003

Made

30th January 2003

Coming into force

31st January 2003

The National Assembly for Wales and the Secretary of State, acting jointly in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 1, 7, 8 and 83 of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] make the following Order:

## 1. Title, application and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Animal Gatherings (Interim Measures) (Wales) (Amendment) Order 2003; it applies in relation to Wales only and comes into force on 31st January 2003.

## 2. Amendment to the Animal Gatherings (Interim Measures) (Wales) Order 2002

Article 1(2) of the Animal Gatherings (Interim Measures) (Wales) Order 2002[^f00002] is amended by substituting “1st April 2003” for “1st February 2003”.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales

D.Elis-Thomas

The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

29th January 2003

Whitty

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

30th January 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order amends the [Animal Gatherings (Interim Measures) (Wales) Order 2002 (S. I. 2002/283 (W. 34)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2002/283) as amended) so that that Order continues in effect until 1st April 2003.
