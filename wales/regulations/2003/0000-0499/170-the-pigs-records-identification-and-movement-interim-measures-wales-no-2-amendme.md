# The Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 170 (W.30)

ANIMALS, WALES

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2003

Made

30th January 2003

Coming into force

31st January 2003

The National Assembly for Wales and the Secretary of State, acting jointly in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 1 and 8(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Title, application and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No. 2) (Amendment) Order 2003; it applies in relation to Wales only and comes into force on 31st January 2003.

## 2. Amendment of Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No. 2) Order 2002

(1) The Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No.2) Order 2002[^f00002] is amended in accordance with this article.

(2) In article 1(2) “1st November 2003” is substituted for “1st February 2003”.

(3) In article 3(1) of the Welsh language text only “Rhaid i berchennog neu geidwad unrhyw fochyn ar ddaliad hysbysu'n” is substituted for “Rhaid i berchennog unrhyw fochyn ar ddaliad neu berson sydd â gofal drosto i hysbysu'n”.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales

John Marek

The Deputy Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

30th January 2003

Whitty

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State,

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

30th January 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which applies to Wales only, amends the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No. 2) Order 2002 (S.I. [2002/2303 (W.228)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2002/2303)), (“the principal Order”).

The only substantive change made to the principal Order by this Order is to amend the date that the principal Order ceases to have effect (from 1st February 2003 to 1st November 2003) (article 2). A defined term (ceidwad) is substituted for an equivalent phrase in the Welsh text of article 3(1).

A regulatory appraisal has not been prepared for this Order.
