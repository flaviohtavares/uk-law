# The Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No.2) (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 2763 (W.268)

ANIMALS, WALES

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No.2) (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2003

Made

30th October 2003

Coming into force

31st October 2003

The National Assembly for Wales and the Secretary of State, in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 1, 8(1) and 83(2) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] hereby make the following Order:

## 1. Title, application and commencement

This Order is called the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No.2) (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2003; it applies to Wales and comes into force on 31st October 2003.

## 2. Amendment of Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No.2) Order 2002

(1) The Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No.2) Order 2002[^f00002] is amended in accordance with this article.

(2) In article 1(2) for “1st November 2003” there is substituted “31st March 2004”.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00003]

D.Elis-Thomas

The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

30th October 2003

Ben Bradshaw

Parliamentary Under-Secretary of State,

Department of Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

30th October 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

Were it not for this Order, the Pigs (Records, Identification and Movement) (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No.2) Order 2002 would cease to have effect on 1st November 2003. In order to consult on permanent provisions to replace the interim provisions contained in that Order, this Order permits that Order to remain in force until 31st March 2004.
