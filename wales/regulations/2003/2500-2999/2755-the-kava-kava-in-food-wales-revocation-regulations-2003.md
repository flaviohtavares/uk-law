# The Kava-kava in Food (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 2755 (W.266)

FOOD, WALES

The Kava-kava in Food (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2003

Made

29th October 2003

Coming into force

30th October 2003

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 16(1)(a), (e) and (f), 18(1)(c), 26(1) and (3) and 48(1) of the Food Safety Act 1990[^f00001] and now vested in it[^f00002], and having had regard in accordance with section 48(4A) of that Act to relevant advice given by the Food Standards Agency, hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Title, commencement and application

These Regulations —

- (a) may be cited as the Kava-kava in Food (Wales) (Revocation) Regulations 2003;
- (b) shall come into force on 30th October 2003;
- (c) shall apply to Wales only.

## 2. Revocation

The Kava-kava in Food (Wales) Regulations 2002 [^f00003] are hereby revoked.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00004].

D.Elis-Thomas

The Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

29th October 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

These regulations revoke the Kava-kava in Food (Wales) Regulations 2002.
