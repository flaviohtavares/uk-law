# The Powys Local Health Board (Additional Functions) (Regulations) 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 815 (W.100)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, WALES

The Powys Local Health Board (Additional Functions) (Regulations) 2003

Made

20th March 2003

Coming into force

1st April 2003

The National Assembly for Wales, in exercise of powers conferred upon it by sections 16BB(2), (3) and (5) and 126(4) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] hereby makes the following Regulations:

## 1. Citation, commencement and application

These Regulations may be cited as the Powys Local Health Board (Additional Functions) Regulations 2003 and come into force on 1st April 2003 and apply to Wales only.

## 2. Interpretation

In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires —

- “the Board” (“*y Bwrdd*”) means the Powys Local Health Board[^f00002]
- “the relevant date” (“*y dyddiad perthnasol*”)means the 1st April 2003
- “the Additional Functions” (“*y Swyddogaethau Ychwanegol*”) means —
  - (a) the ownership and management of hospital accommodation and services at Bronllys hospital, Bronllys, Powys LD3 0LU and associated hospitals[^f00003]; and
  - (b) the management of community health services provided from Bronllys Hospital, Bronllys, Powys, LD3 0LU and the ownership of associated premises; and
  - (c) functions of managers under the Mental Health Act 1983[^f00004].

## 3. Functions to be exercised by Powys Local Health Board

The Additional Functions are to be exercised by Board as from the relevant date.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998[^f00005].

John Marek

The Deputy Presiding Officer of the National Assembly

20th March 2003

(This note is not part of the Regulations)

The Local Health boards (Functions) Regulations 2003 ([S.I. 2003/150 (W.20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2003/150)) conferred upon Local Health Boards established for all areas of Wales pursuant to section 16BA of the National Health Service Act 1977 certain specified functions. These Regulations confer upon Powys Local Health Board certain additional functions being functions previously undertaken by the Powys Health Care National Health Service Trust (“the trust”). The trust was dissolved by the Powys Health Care National Health Service Trust (Dissolution) Order 2003 ([S.I. 2003/817 (W.20)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2003/817)).
