# The Sheep and Goats Identification and Movement (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No.2) (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2003

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2003 No. 946 (W.127)

ANIMALS, WALES

ANIMAL HEALTH

The Sheep and Goats Identification and Movement (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No.2) (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2003

Made

26th March 2003

Coming into force

31st March 2003

The National Assembly for Wales and the Secretary of State, acting jointly in exercise of the powers conferred upon them by sections 1 and 8(1) of the Animal Health Act 1981[^f00001] make the following Order:

## 1. Title, application and commencement

This Order may be cited as the Sheep and Goats Identification and Movement (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No. 2) (Amendment) (No.2) Order 2003; it applies in relation to Wales and comes into force on 31st March 2003.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Order “the Principal Order” means the Sheep and Goats Identification and Movement (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No. 2) Order 2002[^f00002].

## 3. Amendment of article 1

In article 1(2) of the Principal Order the words “1st August 2003” are substituted for “1st April 2003”.

## 4. Amendment of article 7

For sub-paragraph (2)(a) of article 7 of the Principal Order the following sub-paragraph is substituted —

> - (a) before the relevant date, was marked in accordance with regulation 7(4) or (5) or regulation 14 of the 2000 Regulations; or

.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales

D. Elis-Thomas

Presiding Officer

26th March 2003

E. A. Morley

Parliamentary Under Secretary of State

Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

27th March 2003

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order, which applies in relation to Wales, amends the Sheep and Goats Identification and Movement (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No. 2) Order 2002 (S.I. [2002/2302 (W.227)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2002/2302)) (“the Principal Order”).

The Order amends the date that the Principal Order ceases to have effect (from 1st April 2003 to 1st August 2003) (article 3) and corrects a textual mistake introduced by the Sheep and Goats Identification and Movement (Interim Measures) (Wales) (No. 2) (Amendment) 2003 (S.I. [2003/167 (W.27)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2003/167)) (article 4).

A regulatory appraisal has not been prepared for this Order.
