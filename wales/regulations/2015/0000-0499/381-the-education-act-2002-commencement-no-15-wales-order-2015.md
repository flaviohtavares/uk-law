# The Education Act 2002 (Commencement No. 15) (Wales) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 381 (W. 40) (C. 22)

Education, Wales

The Education Act 2002 (Commencement No. 15) (Wales) Order 2015

Made

24 February 2015

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred upon the National Assembly for Wales by section 216(4) and (5) of the Education Act 2002[^f00001] and now vested in them[^f00002], make the following Order:

## 1. Title

The title of this Order is the Education Act 2002 (Commencement No. 15) (Wales) Order 2015.

## 2. Appointed Day

(1) The appointed day is 27 February 2015.

(2) Chapter 1 of Part 1 of the Education Act 2002[^f00003] (which comprises sections 1 to 5 and relates to powers to facilitate innovation) comes into force on the appointed day.

Huw Lewis

Minister for Education and Skills, one of the Welsh Ministers

24 February 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 27 February 2015 Chapter 1 of Part 1 (which is sections 1 to 5) of the Education Act 2002. That Chapter gives powers, which are now vested in Welsh Ministers, to make orders to facilitate the implementation of innovative projects and makes provision about matters related to those powers.
