# The Education Workforce Council (Main Functions) (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 19A

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19A. Requirement to be registered: further education learning support workers[^c-7d84bfa28492e6767ea227f17ec37ac6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^M_F_d8855ae4-ea09-4f79-8d12-13bacbfec27b] A person must not provide the services set out in section 16(2) of the 2014 Act unless they are registered under section 9 of the 2014 Act (register maintained by the Council) in the category of further education learning support worker.

(2)[^c-1d02ed33ed1fb1a0c0bf19507384b1aa] Paragraph (1) does not apply to a person who is registered under section 9 of the 2014 Act (register maintained by the Council) in the category of further education teacher in so far as that person is supporting the provision of the services as part of the person’s role as a further education teacher.
