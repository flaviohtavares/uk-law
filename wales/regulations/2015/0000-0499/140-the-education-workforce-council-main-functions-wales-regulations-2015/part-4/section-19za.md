# The Education Workforce Council (Main Functions) (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 19ZA

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19ZA. Council to review list of specified qualifications[^c-9cfe513a45c6a6ae12e619e270a82e63]

*Extent: E+W*

The Council must review the list of specified qualifications and report to the Welsh Ministers on an annual basis any amendments which it considers may be required.
