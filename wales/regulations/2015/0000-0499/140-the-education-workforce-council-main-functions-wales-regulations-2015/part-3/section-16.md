# The Education Workforce Council (Main Functions) (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 16

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 16. Extension of specified period[^c-1f48c9b42594d3e8f4d46f2d2aa4fbca]

*Extent: E+W*

Where any person is permitted to carry out work specified in regulation 17 for a specified period by virtue of regulation 18A or[^c-8ed50395e0820a1d7de47b92e0b03b63] any of the provisions of Schedule 3, that period will be extended by a period equal to the aggregate of any period or periods during which the person concerned is absent from work—

- (a) in exercise of that person’s—
  - (i) right to maternity leave conferred by section 71 or 73 of the 1996 Act[^f00019] or a contract of employment and has the right to return to work by virtue of either of these sections or a contract of employment;
  - (ii) right to parental leave conferred by section 76 of the 1996 Act;
  - (iii) right to paternity leave conferred by section 80A or 80B of the 1996 Act[^f00020]; [^c-653acd496ca4fbfde48bedea514eb371]...
  - (iv) right to adoption leave conferred by section 75A or 75B of the 1996 Act[^f00021]; [^c-7570777c32ceaffb3c8124c1763650eb]...
  - (v)[^c-8c879146a909d4ab0199a3355a740b2f] right to shared parental leave conferred by section 75E or 75G of the 1996 Act; [^c-4d6fc033b15d0cdab7c6356feb5d23a0]...
  - (vi) right to parental bereavement leave conferred by section 80EA of the 1996 Act; or
  - (vii)[^c-b7bec4298f36e7beb6b851dd7973898c] right to neonatal care leave conferred by section 80EF of the 1996 Act;
- (b) because of pregnancy; or[^c-a6a778a994fe0579a2177af966f04cbf];
- (c)[^c-fb68bfb688bcdc2bea630f1efc585097] because of sickness or injury.
