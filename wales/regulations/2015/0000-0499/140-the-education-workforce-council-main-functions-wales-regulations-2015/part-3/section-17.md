# The Education Workforce Council (Main Functions) (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 17

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Specified work[^c-d100f78400305f60da97e30a1c2dc17b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Each of the following activities is specified work for the purposes of these Regulations—

- (a) planning and preparing lessons and courses for pupils;
- (b) delivering lessons to pupils;
- (c) assessing the development, progress and attainment of pupils; [^c-69395328b8d7095e90d7bb3d08c8ed30]...
- (d) reporting on the development, progress and attainment of pupils.
- (e)[^c-1757a635aa377069f3c7062c1a6c4d79] undertaking a senior leadership role in managing teaching and learning at the school.

(2) In paragraph (1)(b) “delivering” includes delivery via distance learning or computer aided techniques.
