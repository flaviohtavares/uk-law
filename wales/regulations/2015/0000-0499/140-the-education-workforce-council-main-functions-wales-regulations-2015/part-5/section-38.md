# The Education Workforce Council (Main Functions) (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 38

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 38. Consequences of failure to comply with a conditional registration order[^key-3944371225deb4fdb0a945b1e7895043]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a Fitness to Practise[^key-a14784f8595f70c24685506dea86ea92] Committee is satisfied that a registered person against whom a conditional registration order has been made has failed to comply with any condition of it, it may make a suspension or prohibition order in relation to the registered person.
