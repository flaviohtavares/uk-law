# The Education Workforce Council (Main Functions) (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 24

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Use of the code of conduct and practice in disciplinary matters[^c-97f7d6cece618a9ccb4ff4d782d9cb3c]

*Extent: E+W*

An Investigating Committee or a Fitness to Practise[^c-a14784f8595f70c24685506dea86ea92] Committee must take into account any failure by a registered person to comply with the code of conduct and practice in any disciplinary proceedings against that person.
