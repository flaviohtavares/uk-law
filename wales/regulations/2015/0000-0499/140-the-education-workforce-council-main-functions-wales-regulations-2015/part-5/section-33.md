> Text as at 2026-04-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Administration of oaths and affirmations[^c-4b7f4f341d2ce77e32ebf198eed1fe34]

*Extent: E+W*

A Fitness to Practise[^c-a14784f8595f70c24685506dea86ea92] Committee may require any witness at a hearing to give evidence on oath or affirmation and for that purpose there may be administered an oath or affirmation in due course.
