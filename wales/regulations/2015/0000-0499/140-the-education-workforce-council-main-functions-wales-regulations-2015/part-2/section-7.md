## 7. Provisional registration[^c-774c7791edb7a1d45c0873c85f4f2199]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person is eligible for provisional registration if for the time being that person meets the following condition[^c-f764077690c6ba981ae8f307e963e49d] in this regulation.

(2) The [^c-42cf920d202b7cbb8b7e578b11bbf769]... condition is that the person is—

- (a) a qualified teacher; and
- (b) yet to satisfactorily complete an induction period.
