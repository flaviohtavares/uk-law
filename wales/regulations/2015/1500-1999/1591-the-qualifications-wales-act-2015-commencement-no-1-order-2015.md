# The Qualifications Wales Act 2015 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 1591 (W. 191) (C. 92)

Education, Wales

The Qualifications Wales Act 2015 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2015

Made

16:55 on 5 August 2015

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the power conferred by section 60(2) and (3) of the Qualifications Wales Act 2015[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Title and interpretation

The title of this Order is the Qualifications Wales Act 2015 (Commencement No. 1) Order 2015.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 6 August 2015

The following provisions of the Qualifications Wales Act 2015 come into force on 6 August 2015—

- (a) section 2(1) (establishment of Qualifications Wales);
- (b) section 2(2) so far as it relates to Part 1 and paragraph 37 of Schedule 1;
- (c) Part 1 of Schedule 1 (establishment of Qualifications Wales); and
- (d) paragraph 37 (in Part 2) of Schedule 1 (consequential amendment to the Freedom of Information Act 2000[^f00002]).

Huw Lewis

Minister for Education and Skills, one of the Welsh Ministers

5 August 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order brings into force on 6 August 2015 the provisions of the Qualifications Wales Act 2015 (“the Act”) listed in article 2 as provided for in that article.

Section 2(1) of the Act establishes Qualifications Wales as a body corporate and section 2(2) introduces Schedule 1. Part 1 of Schedule 1 makes further provision related to the establishment of Qualifications Wales, including on its membership. Paragraph 37 of Schedule 1 amends the Freedom of Information Act 2000 to add Qualifications Wales to the list of public bodies and offices in Part 6 of Schedule 1 to that Act.
