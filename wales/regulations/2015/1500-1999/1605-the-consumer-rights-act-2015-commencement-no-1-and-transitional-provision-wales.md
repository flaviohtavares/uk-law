# The Consumer Rights Act 2015 (Commencement No. 1 and Transitional Provision) (Wales) Order 2015

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2015 No. 1605 (W. 203) (C. 93)

Education, Wales

The Consumer Rights Act 2015 (Commencement No. 1 and Transitional Provision) (Wales) Order 2015

Made

10 August 2015

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 97(2) and 100(3)(b) of the Consumer Rights Act 2015[^f00001] make the following Order.

## 1. Title, application and interpretation

(1) The title of this Order is the Consumer Rights Act 2015 (Commencement No. 1 and Transitional Provision) (Wales) Order 2015.

(2) This Order applies in relation to Wales only.

(3) In this Order, “the Act” (“*y Ddeddf*”) means the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1 September 2015

Section 89 of the Act comes into force on the 1 September 2015.

## 3. Transitional arrangements

(1) A qualifying complaint about an act or omission of a qualifying institution falling within section 11(e) or (f) of the Higher Education Act 2004[^f00002] can only be reviewed by the designated operator where the act or omission complained of—

- (a) occurs on or after 1<sup> </sup>September 2015; or
- (b) occurs before that date but continues on or after it.

(2) In this article the terms “qualifying complaint” (“*cwyn gymhwysol*”), “qualifying institution” (“*sefydliad cymhwysol*”) and “designated operator” (“*gweithredwr dynodedig*”) bear the same meanings as they bear in Part 2 of the Higher Education Act 2004.

Huw Lewis

Minister for Education and Skills, one of the Welsh Ministers

10 August 2015

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

Article 2 of this Order brings into force, in relation to Wales, section 89 of the Consumer Rights Act 2015 (“the Act”) which amends sections 11 and 12 of the Higher Education Act 2004[^f00003] by expanding the list of higher education providers which are required to join the higher education complaints handling scheme.

Article 3 of the Order makes transitional provision in respect of those new institutions being brought into the higher education complaints scheme, namely that complaints in relation to those new institutions can only be brought about acts or omissions occurring on or after 1 September 2015, or where an act or omission occurs prior to 1 September 2015 and continues after that date.
