# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 41

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Considerations to which the responsible authority must have regard

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The considerations to which the responsible authority must have regard in reviewing every case are set out in paragraphs 1 to 17[^key-60268b96ca20399bed77915d7eb35258] of Schedule 8.

(2) The additional considerations to which the responsible authority must have regard in reviewing C's case where C is part of a family which is being supported by an IFS team are set out in paragraphs 18 to 26[^key-838885aff2a213b8669546d33750c729] of Schedule 8.

(3)[^key-e73299cc3eda63c9911d7bb99b2285c3] In paragraph (2) and in Schedule 8—

- “family” (“*teulu*”) has the meaning given in regulation 18(3) of the Partnership Regulations.
