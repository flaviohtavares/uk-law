# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 46

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. Arrangements for ceasing to look after a child who is not a category 1 young person

*Extent: E+W*

In any case where C is not in the care of the responsible authority and is not likely to be a category 1 young person [^c22637671] when the local authority cease to look after him or her, the care and support plan (or where regulation 58 applies, the detention placement plan) must include details of the advice and other support that the responsible authority intends to provide for C when C ceases to be looked after by it.

## Annotations

[^c22637671]: Marginal citation: “Category 1 young person” is defined in section 104(2) of the 2014 Act.
