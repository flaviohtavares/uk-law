# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 60

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 60. Modification of Part 5

*Extent: E+W*

Part 5 (visits by the responsible authority's representative etc.) applies with the modification that in regulation 31(7)(a), the responsible authority must ensure that R visits C, where C is remanded to YDA or C is detained, whenever reasonably requested to do so by the director, governor or registered manager (as the case may be) of the prison, YDA or the premises in which C is required to reside.
