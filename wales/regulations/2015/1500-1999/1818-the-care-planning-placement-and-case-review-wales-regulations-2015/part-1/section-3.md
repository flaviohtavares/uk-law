# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 3

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3.

*Extent: E+W*

Parts 2 to 10[^c-4550c50677db7de7f1ac9060f86e5c1e] do not apply in relation to any child who is looked after by a local authority and who has been placed for adoption under the Adoption and Children Act 2002 [^c22637451] unless the child falls within regulation 56.
