# The Care Planning, Placement and Case Review (Wales) Regulations 2015, SCHEDULE 6

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 6 — Agreement with an independent fostering service relating to the discharge of the responsible authority's functions[^key-b59309a864421b00a3b04f15bd794c75]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 29

**1.** The agreement must contain the following information—

- (a) the services to be provided to the responsible authority by the registered provider[^key-54f4707eec88ebd4771cffa7c012577a],
- (b) the arrangements for the selection by the responsible authority of F from those approved by the registered provider[^key-1e3dc0d2983440aa0745d877d653648e],
- (c) a requirement for the registered provider[^key-1f93a3a5af1286c80ccb7674f5eb9199] to submit reports to the responsible authority on any placements as may be required by the responsible authority, and
- (d) the arrangements for the termination of the agreement.

**2.** Where the agreement relates to a particular child, it must also contain the following information—

- (a) F's details,
- (b) details of any services that C is to receive and whether the services are to be provided by the responsible authority or by the registered provider[^key-91238ac4886245afb4092b9292241a58],
- (c) the terms (including as to payment) of the proposed placement agreement,
- (d) the arrangements for record keeping about C and for the return of records at the end of the placement,
- (e) a requirement for the registered provider[^key-368637d90be3117f2830ad934af54fd1] to notify the responsible authority immediately in the event of concerns about the placement, and
- (f) whether and on what basis other children may be placed with F.
