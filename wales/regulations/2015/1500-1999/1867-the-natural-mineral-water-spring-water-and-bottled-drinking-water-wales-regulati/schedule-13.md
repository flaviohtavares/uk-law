# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Wales) Regulations 2015, SCHEDULE 13

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 13 — Amendments to other legislation

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 38

### 1. The Private Water Supplies (Wales) Regulations 2010

The Private Water Supplies (Wales) Regulations 2010 [^c22639441] are amended as follows.

**2.** In regulation 3(a), for “the Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Wales) Regulations 2007” substitute “ the Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Wales) Regulations 2015 ”.

### 3. The Food Safety (Sampling and Qualifications) (Wales) Regulations 2013

The Food Safety (Sampling and Qualifications) (Wales) Regulations 2013 [^c22639451] are amended as follows.

**4.** In Schedule 1, omit the reference to “The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Wales) Regulations 2007”.

### 5. The Food Information (Wales) Regulations 2014

The Food Information (Wales) Regulations 2014 [^c22639461] are amended as follows.

**6.** In Schedule 7, omit paragraphs 49 and 50.
