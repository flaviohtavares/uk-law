# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 22

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22. Sale of bottled drinking water

*Extent: E+W*

No person may sell bottled drinking water which is—

- (a) bottled in contravention of regulation 19;
- (b) labelled in contravention of regulation 20; or
- (c) advertised in contravention of regulation 21.
