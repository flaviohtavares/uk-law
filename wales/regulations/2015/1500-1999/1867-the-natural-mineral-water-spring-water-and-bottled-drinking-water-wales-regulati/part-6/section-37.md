# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 37

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Revocations

*Extent: E+W*

The following Regulations are revoked—

- (a) the Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Wales) Regulations 2007 [^c22639351];
- (b) the Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2009 [^c22639361];
- (c) the Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 [^c22639371];
- (d) the Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Wales) (Amendment) Regulations 2011 [^c22639381].
