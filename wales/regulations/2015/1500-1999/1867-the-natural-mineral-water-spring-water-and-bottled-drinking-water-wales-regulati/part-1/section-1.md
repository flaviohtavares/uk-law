# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 1

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Title, commencement and application

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Wales) Regulations 2015.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 28 November 2015 and apply in relation to Wales.
