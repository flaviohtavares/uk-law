# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 3

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Exemptions

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations do not apply to any water which—

- (a) is a medicinal product within the meaning of regulation 2(1) of the Human Medicines Regulations 2012[^c-408e3d21a46107380a9541a353398d41];
- (b) is a natural mineral water which is used at source for curative purposes in thermal or hydromineral establishments;
- (c) is not intended for sale for drinking by humans; or
- (d)[^c-22412a9ef7f67313016a785f95828f3e] is a natural mineral water intended for—
  - (i) movement into Northern Ireland; or
  - (ii) export to a third country.

(2) These Regulations do not apply to packaged ice portions intended for use in cooling food.
