# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 28

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. General

*Extent: E+W*

The food authority must ensure that each sample is representative of the quality of the water concerned consumed throughout the year in which the sample is taken.
