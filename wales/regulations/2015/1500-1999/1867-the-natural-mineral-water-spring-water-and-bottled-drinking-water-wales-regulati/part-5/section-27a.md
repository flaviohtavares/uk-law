# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 27A

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27A. Fluoride removal treatment[^key-3d895ad4a02a1d0059da58bfb04a5275]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of these Regulations, a fluoride removal treatment means—

- (a) a treatment of natural mineral water or water intended to be bottled and labelled as “spring water”, “dŵr ffynnon”, or its equivalent in any other language, with activated alumina in order to remove fluoride which is authorised in accordance with regulations 9(1)(a)(iii) or 15(a)(iii) and Schedule 2;
- (b) in the case of natural mineral water or water intended to be bottled and labelled as “spring water”, “dŵr ffynnon”, or its equivalent in any other language, brought into Wales from another part of the United Kingdom [^key-5f39a2f440f681aeacad2494ad94ebea]..., a treatment which has been authorised by the responsible authority in the area in which the water is extracted as complying with the requirements of the relevant bottled water legislation that applies in that part of the United Kingdom[^key-4e59e850fec45b488bfee73318798f8f] and which does not have a disinfectant action; or
- (c) in the case of natural mineral water or water intended to be bottled and labelled as “spring water”, “dŵr ffynnon”, or its equivalent in any other language, brought into Wales from a third country[^key-e90aa231e304188280c29f4e19e14b8c], a treatment that complies with Articles 1 and 2[^key-5b78546677cc0a081d21a17e08250602] of Regulation 115/2010 and which does not have a disinfectant action.
