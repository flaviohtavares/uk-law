# The Natural Mineral Water, Spring Water and Bottled Drinking Water (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 27B

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27B. Ozone-enriched air treatment[^c-810733c372174f7ef8146507708713db]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of these Regulations, an ozone-enriched air treatment means—

- (a) a treatment of natural mineral water or water intended to be bottled and labelled as “spring water”, “dŵr ffynnon”, or its equivalent in any other language, with ozone-enriched air which is authorised in accordance with regulations 9(1)(a)(iv) or 15(a)(iv) and Schedule 3;
- (b) in the case of natural mineral water or water intended to be bottled and labelled as “spring water”, “dŵr ffynnon”, or its equivalent in any other language, brought into Wales from another part of the United Kingdom [^c-9c28c191de439873aa53abce7d5d7aa4]..., a treatment which has been authorised by the responsible authority in the area in which the water is extracted under the relevant bottled water legislation applying[^c-bdb89bc845ab8cd305644cf36d90eeb6] in that part of the United Kingdom [^c-690452198269a9f574d5cb145fd2aaf1]... and which does not have a disinfectant action; or
- (c) in the case of natural mineral water or water intended to be bottled and labelled as “spring water”, “dŵr ffynnon”, or its equivalent in any other language, brought into Wales from a third country[^c-41ab4ac1d760bf3d0b1fefcd7bd28f84], a treatment that has been authorised by the responsible authority in that country in accordance with procedures for authorising ozone-enriched air treatment in that country which have been determined by the Agency to be equivalent to the requirements of these Regulations, or by the[^c-a395fcbdf6257d68d8ac4fc7cb0bd252] responsible authority in another part of the United Kingdom [^c-cd56ee915dc0e107879c9b99e80519da]... to be equivalent to the requirements of the relevant bottled water legislation that applies in that part of the United Kingdom[^c-050431c3f1b7482089541a738fd859dc], and which does not have a disinfectant action.
