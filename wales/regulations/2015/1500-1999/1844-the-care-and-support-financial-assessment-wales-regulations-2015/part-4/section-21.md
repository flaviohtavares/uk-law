## 21. Calculation of capital outside the United Kingdom[^c-43b4c1dc30611d94556a8fb03b166d3b]

*Extent: E+W*

Capital which A possesses outside the United Kingdom is to be calculated in accordance with the method set out in regulation 50 of the Income Support Regulations (calculation of capital outside the United Kingdom).
