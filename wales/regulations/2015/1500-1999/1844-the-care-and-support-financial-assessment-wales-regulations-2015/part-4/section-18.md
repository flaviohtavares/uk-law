## 18. Calculation of capital[^c-9c3a15367357b01a321a90b13b776a5d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The capital of A to be taken into account in a financial assessment is, subject to paragraph (2), to be the whole of A’s capital calculated in accordance with this Part and any income treated as capital under regulation 19.

(2) Any capital, where applicable, specified in Schedule 2 is to be disregarded in the calculation of the person’s capital under paragraph (1).
