## 20. Calculation of capital in the United Kingdom[^key-0064694669f667b874af39bdb1128c9d]

*Extent: E+W*

Capital which A possesses in the United Kingdom is to be calculated at its current market or surrender value (whichever is the higher), less—

- (a) where there would be expenses attributable to sale, 10%; and
- (b) the amount of any encumbrance secured on it.
