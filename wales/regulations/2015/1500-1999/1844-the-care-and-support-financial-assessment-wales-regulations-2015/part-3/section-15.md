## 15. Other sums to be disregarded[^c-c28885d9ff840f0a3a42ae8ef8883aea]

*Extent: E+W*

There is to be disregarded in the calculation of A’s total income for the purposes of the financial assessment any sum, where applicable, specified in Part 1 of Schedule 1, in accordance with Part 2 of that Schedule.
