# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 2

> Text as at 2025-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Establishment of Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) 2015[^key-50beeb0005b6b94c9157b5c1648fb1bc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) These Regulations establish a scheme for the payment of pensions and other benefits to or in respect of fire and rescue workers[^f00006] who are firefighters in Wales.

(2) This scheme is to be known as the Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) 2015.
