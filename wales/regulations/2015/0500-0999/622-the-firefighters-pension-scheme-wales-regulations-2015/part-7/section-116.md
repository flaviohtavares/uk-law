> Text as at 2025-09-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 116. Reduction in pension debit member’s benefits[^c-c581fd4ad01aa0e1bda578567fc6b72f]

*Extent: E+W*

The benefits to which a pension debit member is entitled under these Regulations are subject to the reduction of the relevant amount calculated in accordance with regulation 63 (establishment of pension credit member’s account).
