# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 14

> Text as at 2025-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Delegation[^c-e332b9dc4ce045d093467928bf15dfc0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may delegate any functions under these Regulations, including this power to delegate.

(2) The scheme manager may delegate any functions under these Regulations, including this power to delegate, to such persons or employees of such person as may be authorised in that behalf by the scheme manager.
