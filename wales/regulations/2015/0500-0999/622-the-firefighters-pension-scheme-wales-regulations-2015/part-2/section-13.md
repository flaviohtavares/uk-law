## 13. The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme Advisory Board for Wales: guidance[^c-c1051a419570d6ad4d77af1ae1629809]

*Extent: E+W*

The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme Advisory Board for Wales must have regard to guidance issued by the Welsh Ministers in relation to the exercise by the Board of its functions under these Regulations.
