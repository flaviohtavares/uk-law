# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 30

> Text as at 2025-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30. Pension credit member[^key-fb8725b3b07eccad380079071917b2d8]

*Extent: E+W*

A person is a pension credit member of this scheme if that person has been given a pension credit in the scheme as a consequence of a pension debit created under section 29 of the WRPA[^key-da630bf7616b3500cc94f13f9f76903f] 1999 in relation to a member of this scheme.
