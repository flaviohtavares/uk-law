# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 17

> Text as at 2025-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Service in two or more scheme employments[^key-54ab7000818c0890a4d9b99bce0c15dd]

*Extent: E+W*

If a person is in service in two or more scheme employments, regulation 16 (eligible persons) applies separately in relation to each employment (whether or not the employment is with the same authority).
