# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 147

> Text as at 2025-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 147. Effect of transfers-out[^key-cbf03236b26bf8ede57bc0351adc84df]

*Extent: E+W*

If a transfer payment is made under this Chapter in respect of a member’s accrued rights to benefits under this scheme, those rights are extinguished.
