> Extent: England, Wales.

## 154. Amount of club transfer earned pension[^c-e31f3f35e2ba551275cb682f2111dec6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies in relation to a club transfer value payment received from another club scheme.

(2) For the scheme year in which the transfer date falls, the amount of club transfer earned pension which P is entitled to count is—

- (a) the amount specified in the club transfer value statement; or
- (b) if such a statement is not provided or is not calculated in accordance with actuarial guidance, an amount calculated by the scheme manager in accordance with actuarial guidance.
