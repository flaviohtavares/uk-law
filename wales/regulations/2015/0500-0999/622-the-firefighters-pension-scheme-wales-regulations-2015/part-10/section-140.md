# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 140

> Text as at 2025-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 140. Application of this Part[^c-c6ef5d96a45bfc5caba789eb471b1c93]

*Extent: E+W*

This Part—

- (a) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 4 of Part 4 of PSA 1993 (transfer values) and is without prejudice to that Chapter; and
- (b) supplements the rights conferred by or under Chapter 5 of that Part (early leavers: cash transfer sums and contribution refunds)[^f00062] and is without prejudice to that Chapter.
