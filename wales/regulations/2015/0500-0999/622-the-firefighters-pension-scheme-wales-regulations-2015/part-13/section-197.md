# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 197

> Text as at 2025-09-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 197. Duty to have regard to guidance[^c-49f4c491aeaf1baf8db3ed770bbb41fa]

*Extent: E+W*

A scheme manager must have regard to any guidance issued by the Welsh Ministers for the purposes of this Part.
