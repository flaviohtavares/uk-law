# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 195

> Text as at 2025-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 195. Information to be provided to a member before reserve forces service leave[^c-c2746d7defddebcd88452488237d3089]

*Extent: E+W*

The scheme manager must give a member who is about to start on a period of reserve forces service leave a statement stating—

- (a) the assumed pensionable pay for that member whilst on reserve forces service leave;
- (b) the member contribution rate to apply during that period;
- (c) details of any payments to be paid by the employer to the member whilst on reserve forces service leave; and
- (d) the employer contribution which applies during that period.
