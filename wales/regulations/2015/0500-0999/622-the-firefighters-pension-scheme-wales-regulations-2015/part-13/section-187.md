# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 187

> Text as at 2025-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 187. Scheme administrator for the purposes of the Finance Act 2004[^c-6cff22ec9ef5c671fcbafa0f9264b155]

*Extent: E+W*

The scheme manager is appointed to be responsible for all liabilities and responsibilities connected with the functions conferred or imposed on the scheme administrator by or under Part 4 of FA 2004 which the scheme manager assumes as sub-scheme administrator under regulation 3 of, and Schedule 3 to, the Registered Pension Schemes (Splitting of Schemes) Regulations 2006[^f00085].
