# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 168

> Text as at 2025-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 168. The board’s report[^c-96009524b65881b22c5569293d404987]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The board must supply the Welsh Ministers with—

- (a) a written report of its decision on the relevant medical issues; and
- (b) if the board is of the opinion that the appeal was frivolous, vexatious or manifestly ill-founded, a statement to that effect (which may form part of the report).

(2) The Welsh Ministers must supply the parties with a copy of the report and of any separate statement under paragraph (1)(b).
