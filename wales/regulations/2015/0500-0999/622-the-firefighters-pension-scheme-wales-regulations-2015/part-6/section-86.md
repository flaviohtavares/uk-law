# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 86

> Text as at 2025-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 86. Meaning of “initial period”[^c-11266d47e5185a64d4ff98417a273c05]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of these Regulations[^c-7e6ce569b0320433c001f3464f1eed01], the “initial period” (“*cyfnod dechreuol*”) is the period of 13 weeks commencing on the day after the death of the member during which a bereavement pension may be payable to any surviving partner or eligible child.
