# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 100

> Text as at 2025-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 100. Increase in eligible child’s pension where member was pension debit member[^c-0132664b3c51722ed0283fd363d41bfa]

*Extent: E+W*

If the member’s benefits were at the date of death subject to a reduction under section 31 of WRPA 1999, any eligible child’s pension must be calculated as though that reduction had not been made.
