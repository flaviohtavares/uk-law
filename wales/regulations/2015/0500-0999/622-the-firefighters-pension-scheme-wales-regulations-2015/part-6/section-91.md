# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 91

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 91. Reduction in pensions in cases of wide age disparity[^c-adeafcc87c3f526a45ca0139a5606ecd]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies if, on the death of a member of this scheme, a surviving partner’s pension is payable to a person who is more than 12 years younger than the member.

(2) The annual rate of that pension is reduced by the lower of—

- (a) 50% of the amount of the annual rate of the pension so calculated; or
- (b) [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2015/622) of that amount,

  where N is the number of whole years by which the surviving partner is younger than the member.
