# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 104

> Text as at 2025-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 104. Person to whom lump sum death benefit payable[^c-db580f407d193249fd20d9b9d3e467bd]

*Extent: E+W*

The scheme manager may, at its absolute discretion, pay any lump sum death benefit payable under this Chapter to or for the benefit of the member’s nominee, personal representatives or any person appearing to the scheme manager to have been a relative or dependant of the member.
