# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 80A

> Text as at 2025-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 80A. Option to commute part of an equivalent amount[^c-d4d809dc572084c7b2995b176bec3e64]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A member who becomes entitled to the immediate payment of an equivalent amount to the NFPS lower tier ill-health pension or to an equivalent amount to the 1992 Scheme lower tier ill-health pension may opt under this regulation to exchange part of the pension for a lump sum.

(2) The option may only be exercised—

- (a) by written notice to the scheme manager which sets out the amount to be commuted; and
- (b) before the first payment of the equivalent amount to the NFPS lower tier ill-health pension or to an equivalent amount to the 1992 Scheme lower tier ill-health pension is made.

(3) Where the person is entitled to the immediate payment of an equivalent amount to the NFPS lower tier ill-health pension and exercises the option to commute under this regulation, the lump sum is calculated—

- (a) in the case of a person who is a connected member of the NFPS, in accordance with paragraphs (2) and (4) of rule 9 (commutation: general) of Part 3 of the NFPS, and
- (b) in the case of a person who is a connected special member of the NFPS, in accordance with paragraphs (2A), (4) and (4A) of rule 9 of Part 3 of that scheme.

(4)[^c-d10d761e98b618509c84bf27f2b5211f] Where the person is entitled to the immediate payment of an equivalent amount to the 1992 Scheme lower tier ill-health pension and exercises the option to commute under this regulation, the lump sum is calculated in accordance with rule B7 (commutation – general provision) of the 1992 Scheme.
