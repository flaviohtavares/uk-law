# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 57

> Text as at 2025-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 57. Account established after ill-health award ceases to be payable[^c-daa60e78bca64cdec37f2b72d88bf161]

*Extent: E+W*

If a lower tier ill-health pension ceases to be payable to a person (P) under regulation 78 (consequences of review) and P does not re-enter pensionable service—

- (a) a deferred member’s account must be established; and
- (b) that account must be credited with an amount equal to the annual rate of the lower tier ill-health pension payable immediately before the lower tier ill-health pension ceased to be payable.
