# The Firefighters’ Pension Scheme (Wales) Regulations 2015, section 64

> Text as at 2025-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 64. Other pension accounts[^key-68034eddafc64d1bb9ca6cfb5e8d00cd]

*Extent: E+W*

If a pension credit member of this scheme is also an active member, deferred member, pensioner member or survivor member of this scheme, the scheme manager must establish a pension credit member’s account in addition to any other account established for the member under this Part.
