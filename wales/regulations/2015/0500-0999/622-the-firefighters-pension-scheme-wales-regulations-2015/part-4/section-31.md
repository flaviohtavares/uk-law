> Text as at 2025-09-01.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31. Description of pension[^key-f623ffbdfbce54243e30a0e47344c746]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purpose of this Part, “description of pension” (“*disgrifiad o bensiwn*”) means any of the following—

- (a) earned pension;
- (b) transferred pension;
- (c) club transfer earned pension;
- (d) added pension.
