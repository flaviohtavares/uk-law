# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018, section 67

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 67. Grant for travel not payable for expenditure covered by disabled student's grant

*Extent: E+W*

Where a disabled student's grant is payable to assist an eligible student with expenditure that the student is obliged to incur in connection with the present course by reason of the student's disability, no grant for travel is payable under regulation 65 or 66 in respect of the same expenditure.
