# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018, section 23H

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**23H.**[^key-335abc892719a3e1b9e9059129caddb8] Regulations 22, 22A, 23, 23A, 23B, 23C, 23D, 23E, 23F or 23G do not apply where as at the end of the day before the first day of the academic year in respect of which P is applying for support—

- (a) P, or
- (b) the person who, as a result of their immigration status, caused P to be an eligible student,

is a British or Irish citizen.
