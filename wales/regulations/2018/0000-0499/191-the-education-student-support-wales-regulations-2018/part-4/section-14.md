# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018, section 14

> Text as at 2026-07-31.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Maximum period of eligibility – tuition fee loans and grants for new students

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies to an eligible student who—

- (a) is undertaking a full-time or sandwich course, and
- (b) has not undertaken a previous course.

(2) Where this regulation applies, the maximum period of eligibility for a tuition fee loan, a base grant, a maintenance grant or[^c-d98009c4ee54de1914b8c221eb393db5] a grant for travel [^c-a8367eeeaa38a12a4b57e1d1cbd8011a]... is the period calculated as follows—

- Ordinary duration of the present course.
- *Plus*
- Number of academic years repeated by the eligible student for compelling personal reasons.
- *Plus*
- One year.
