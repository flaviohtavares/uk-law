# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018, section 23E

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**23E.**[^key-62b1d895d9d15d48c460f69fbf32702e] Where—

- (a) the Welsh Ministers have determined that, by virtue of—
  - (i) falling within paragraph (1)(a)[^key-4385cb5f7b2b153cda41c8919d59de31] [^key-a1532118e6388f9733885d3e19991e63]... (iv) or (v) of the definition of “person with protected rights”, or
  - (ii) meeting the conditions in paragraph 1(2)(a) [^key-a67810325fce9f44906459d3503d012c]... (iv) of Schedule 2,

  a person (“P”) is an eligible student in connection with an application for support for an academic year of a designated course, and
- (b) as at the day before the first day of the academic year, P is not a person with protected rights,

  P’s status as an eligible student terminates immediately before the first day of the academic year in respect of which P is applying for support.
