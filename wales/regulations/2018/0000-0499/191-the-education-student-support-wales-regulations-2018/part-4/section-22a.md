# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018, section 22A

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22A. Persons who cease to have stateless leave[^c-b72cefa02f104f0ac17f47301e6e05da]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This regulation applies where—

- (a) a person (“P”) was an eligible student as a result of being a person granted stateless leave[^c-aefa04ee17a1eaf44695e3a7d0e372ad] (see Schedule 2) in connection with an application for support—
  - (i) for an earlier year of the present course,
  - (ii) for a full time-course in relation to which the present course is a full-time end-on course, or
  - (iii) for a course from which P’s status as an eligible student has been transferred to the present course under regulation 28 [^c-be49752929649cd895acd244a504219b]..., and
- (b) as at the end of the day before the first day of the academic year in respect of which P is applying for support, the period for which—
  - (i) P, or
  - (ii) the person, who, as a result of being a person granted stateless leave, caused P to be an eligible student[^c-f7e7bfa2b8d0eb0636c3e8b5f13e3ded],

    is allowed to stay in the United Kingdom has expired and no further leave to remain has been granted and no application for administrative review in accordance with the immigration rules is pending.

(2) Where this regulation applies, P’s status as an eligible student terminates immediately before the first day of the academic year in respect of which P is applying for support.
