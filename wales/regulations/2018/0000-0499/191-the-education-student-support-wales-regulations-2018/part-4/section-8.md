# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018, section 8

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 8. Designation of other courses

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may specify that a course is to be treated as a designated course despite the fact that, but for the specification, it would not otherwise be a designated course [^c23554441].

(1A)[^key-c0d533c498cf1375e633c659c10cb15d] When specifying a course under paragraph (1), the Welsh Ministers may specify that the course is to be treated as being provided by an ordinary provider for the purposes of regulation 40(2)(c)(i).

(2) The Welsh Ministers may suspend or revoke the specification of a course made under paragraph (1).
