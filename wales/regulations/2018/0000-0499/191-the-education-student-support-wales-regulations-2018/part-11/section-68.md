# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018, section 68

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 68. Grants for dependants

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The following are grants made available by the Welsh Ministers to an eligible student in respect of costs associated with certain dependants of the student for an academic year—

- (a) an adult dependants grant (see Chapter 2);
- (b) a parents' learning grant (see Chapter 3);
- (c) a childcare grant (see Chapter 4).

(2) In these Regulations, those grants are collectively referred to as “**grants for dependants**” (“**GfDs**”).
