# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018, section 72

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 72. Maximum amount of adult dependants grant

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In Table 11, Column 2 sets out the maximum amount of adult dependants grant payable in respect of an academic year in the corresponding entry in Column 1.

(2) But where the person in respect of whom the eligible student is applying is ordinarily resident outside the United Kingdom, the amount of adult dependants grant payable is an amount, not exceeding the maximum amount, which the Welsh Ministers consider reasonable in the circumstances.

Table 11[^key-cd477855ca5f3d911a406ea134374e29]

| *Column 1**Academic year* | *Column 2**Maximum amount of adult dependants grant* |
| --- | --- |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2018 but before 1 September 2020 | £2,732 |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2020 but before 1 September 2021 | £3,094 |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2021 but before 1 September 2022[^key-14764335239bf2d42ea0834871b0a7c2] | £3,190 |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2022[^key-954affc399964e70f8ae59a27d32ebd7] but before 1 September 2023[^key-39cf5300940d178894a8b71c27b16613] | £3,262 |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2023[^key-7dbfde43411044b9765925a76f119c88] but before 1 September 2024[^key-7c63f2f1c3936c04e88f12169560bdf1] | £3,322 |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2024[^key-cef37a4a6534a7f65bc70ce43e76e401] but before 1 September 2025[^key-6de876eaa4eaade2029048cb34cb25e8] | £3,353 |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2025[^key-0e30f603563b420a051e91c716136dde] but before 1 September 2026[^key-6260d79e90c78c5c5127640be0d8ddea] | £3,407 |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2026[^key-9842ee1bbc4e83c8d2edeabc5d47b4dc] | £3,474 |
