> Text as at 2026-07-31.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 82. Payment based on provisional assessment

*Extent: E+W*

Where the Welsh Ministers make a provisional decision on an application under regulation 32, the Welsh Ministers may make a payment based on that decision.
