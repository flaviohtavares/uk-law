# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018, section 83

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 83. Payment of tuition fee loan

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a tuition fee loan is payable to an eligible student, the Welsh Ministers must pay that amount to the academic authority to which the student is liable to make payment.

(2) The Welsh Ministers may pay that amount in instalments or in a single lump sum.
