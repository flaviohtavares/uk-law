# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018, section 81

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 81. Qualifying for grants or maintenance loan during the academic year

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where one of the events in paragraph (3) occurs, the eligible student may qualify for a grant or maintenance loan.

(2) But the amount of grant or loan payable to the eligible student—

- (a)[^c-98cdc900c744d35528ed65f836f992d3] will only, in the case of a grant, be in respect of the remaining days of the academic year, and, in the case of a maintenance loan, only be in respect of the remaining term days of the academic year, in either case beginning with the day the relevant event occurs, and
- (b) in relation to a maintenance loan, will only be payable if it is a quarter in respect of which the loan would otherwise be payable under regulation 85(6) and (7).

(3) The events are—

- (a) the student's course becomes a designated course;
- (b) the student becomes an eligible student on the grounds that–
  - (i)[^c-7dcd5f9447d3cfc1b0b3c1537fb25303] the student or the student’s spouse, civil partner or parent is recognised as a refugee, becomes a person granted stateless leave, becomes a person with leave to enter or remain or becomes a person granted humanitarian protection under paragraph 339C of the immigration rules;
  - (ia)[^c-984f93b42b493e497786831d35cb4926] the student or the student’s parent becomes a person with section 67 leave to remain or a person[^c-1ffa15a82033c22f16925904cc41ae0c] with leave to enter or remain[^c-fca75dc80b92cf9e6b653d861b628dcb] as a protected partner;
  - (ib)[^c-e43a1b7c59967a2290d59f233a37e6e1] the student becomes a person with Calais leave;
  - (ic)[^c-e545a0bc32483bcc3b4b3d3c98236e00] the student becomes a person with leave to enter or remain as a relevant Afghan citizen or the spouse, civil partner, child or stepchild of such a person[^c-50cc067b5624c8e7a66440d48b48caa2];
  - (id)[^c-66a9662871458665ffdf95a124f1c326] the student becomes a protected Ukrainian national or the spouse, civil partner, child or stepchild of such a person[^c-edf15965b5ed4cd30ccaf37ab010f171];
  - (ii)[^c-9d06ebc8be531b4f66e2376052482c0e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii)[^c-de39c368973bef2bd0ec2c9e52354692] the student becomes a person described in paragraph 1(1)(a) or (2)(a)[^c-10daea79e98b360908e9c1241e1e36a2] of Schedule 2;
  - (iv)[^c-06770415aea7f7aaa57ee4038fdc5546] where regulation 9(1A)(a) applies, the student becomes a person[^c-0a40af6e4a9b9b5d4f8dfa7c958abfb1] described in paragraph 8(1)(a) of Schedule 2;
  - (v) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 4A(1)(a) of Schedule 2 or, where regulation 9(1A)(a) applies, in[^c-53a85982e35ac7ebb1c89f6e46918a70] paragraph 4(1)(a) of Schedule 2;
  - (vi)[^c-23fa1eee1e35ffe2f9f4a833ecf36cf2] the student becomes a person described in paragraph 7A(a) of Schedule 2 or, where regulation 9(1A)(a) applies, in paragraph 7(1)(a) of Schedule 2;
  - (vii)[^c-b0e53b8adcdaadf6d833a5d4b724bc9a] the student becomes a person described in paragraph 6B(1)(a)(ii) of Schedule 2.
