# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018, section 80

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 80. Qualifying for a tuition fee loan during the academic year

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where one of the events listed in paragraph (2) occurs within 3 months of the first day of the academic year, the student may qualify for a tuition fee loan in respect of that academic year.

(2) The events are—

- (a) the present course becomes a designated course;
- (b) the student becomes an eligible student on the grounds that—
  - (i)[^key-8f9ab92045648c72e0b13ac75052b4b7] the student or the student’s spouse, civil partner or parent is recognised as a refugee, becomes a person granted stateless leave, becomes a person with leave to enter or remain or becomes a person granted humanitarian protection under paragraph 339C of the immigration rules;
  - (ia)[^key-095ccaa209e0ca01163d5af8d491dffa] the student or the student’s parent becomes a person with section 67 leave to remain or a person[^key-abb524ccb05316f4bcea8af3f105cf3b] granted leave to enter or remain[^key-6df536d638fdefa32f6a33e7a187fcd4] as a protected partner;
  - (ib)[^key-1e47cf79eb1a4ba52518095a1bbbf648] the student becomes a person with Calais leave;
  - (ic)[^key-f92bf8e2581dbd6e55160b342f59765f] the student becomes a person with leave to enter or remain as a relevant Afghan citizen or the spouse, civil partner, child or stepchild of such a person[^key-7e18d5a7dcd5f91af5f000fe857421fa];
  - (id)[^key-d92cefe2353c62f88a65a26cfc7e60ba] the student becomes a protected Ukrainian national or the spouse, civil partner, child or stepchild of such a person[^key-92ed6e60a7da32deef04b8ca710457ad];
  - (ii)[^key-1c54a63215b3ad110fa7ea90faa65bb9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii)[^key-e40d06fd0ba6f1ac3f1c986c714a326f] the student becomes a family member described in paragraph 6A(1)(a), 6C(1)(a), 6D(1)(a) or 6D(2)(a)[^key-7fe6852f8f62e62f8408884b4f0adbff] of Schedule 2;
  - (iv)[^key-00cf6129c171d2b53ac050a7ae8936a7] the student becomes a person described in paragraph 1(1)(a), (2)(a) or (3)(a), or 6BB[^key-57d26ee897590ba014bdf8b8bda0277e] of Schedule 2;
  - (v)[^key-3308ee42f571140570d83ab71fbe0508] where regulation 9(1A)(a) applies, the student becomes a person[^key-74347e82cedd9088ae7cfd90d53624d0] described in paragraph 8(1)(a) of Schedule 2;
  - (vi) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 4A(1)(a) of Schedule 2 or, where regulation 9(1A)(a) applies, in[^key-c76ebb99d7e2c2fb762ac1d50e49ae0a] paragraph 4(1)(a) of Schedule 2;
  - (vii)[^key-a71b853f0aa4f7a7da5e79b5adac6809] the student becomes a person described in paragraph 7A(a) of Schedule 2 or, where regulation 9(1A)(a) applies, in paragraph 7(1)(a) of Schedule 2; or
  - (viii)[^key-289a2b24ecb791e87d05746adbc5f23b] the student becomes a person described in paragraph 6B(1)(a)(ii) of Schedule 2.

(3) In this regulation and regulation 81, the following terms have the same meaning as in Schedule 2—

- “person granted stateless leave” (“person y rhoddwyd caniatâd iddo aros fel person diwladwriaeth”)[^key-8079507e5eacc4ef6bb98ab5dc20bca1];
