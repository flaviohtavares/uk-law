# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018, section 51

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 51. Special support payment: qualifying conditions

*Extent: E+W*

An eligible student qualifies for a special support payment in respect of an academic year of the present course if the eligible student satisfies one of the following conditions— *Condition A* The eligible student, for the purposes of assessing entitlement to income support, falls within a prescribed category of person for the purposes of section 124(1)(e) [^c23554681] of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992. *Condition B* The eligible student, for the purposes of assessing entitlement to housing benefit, is treated as being liable to make payments in respect of a dwelling prescribed by regulations made under section 130(2) of that Act [^c23554691]. *Condition C* The eligible student, for the purposes of assessing entitlement to universal credit, is liable or is treated as being liable under regulation 25(3) of the Universal Credit Regulations 2013 [^c23554701] to make payments in respect of accommodation the student occupies as his or her home.
