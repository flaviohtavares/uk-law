## 52. Maximum amount of maintenance grant treated as special support payment

*Extent: E+W*

In Table 6, Column 2 sets out the maximum amount of maintenance grant payable as special support payment in respect of the academic year set out in the corresponding entry in Column 1.

Table 6

| *Column 1**Academic year* | *Column 2**Maximum amount of maintenance grant payable as part of a special support payment* |
| --- | --- |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2018 | £4,161 for a full-time course<br>£5,000 multiplied by the intensity of study for a part-time course |
