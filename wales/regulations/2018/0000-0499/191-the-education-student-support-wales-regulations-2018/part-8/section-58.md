# The Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018, section 58

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Amount of maintenance loan: part-time students

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where an eligible student's present course is a part-time course (a “part-time student”), the amount of maintenance loan payable to the student is calculated as follows— Maximum amount of maintenance loan available to the student (see Table 10). *Minus* Amount of maintenance grant payable to the student under regulation 47.

(2) In Table 10, Column 1 specifies the academic year in relation to which the maximum amount of maintenance loan in Column 2 is available.

Table 10[^c-eea4ea8984d2745a0d0d3e6c769778f9]

| *Column 1**Academic year* | *Column 2**Maximum amount of maintenance loan available to part-time student* |
| --- | --- |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2018 but before 1 September 2019 | £5,650 multiplied by the intensity of study |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2019 but before 1 September 2020 | £5,815 multiplied by the intensity of study |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2020 but before 1 September 2021 | £6,245 multiplied by the intensity of study |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2021 but before 1 September 2022[^c-199887c6b1e8c415d581872882b335b6] | £6,640 multiplied by the intensity of study |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2022[^c-d62fc9a13c3b35697aae186f10a60f26] but before 1 September 2023[^c-ff55b8d13fd1944fe764b259e62717e4] | £6,905 multiplied by the intensity of study |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2023[^c-46ab27240e7e170e164841f321e57df3] but before 1 September 2024[^c-5dd1828d8363fb3fc5c7a7692249b49b] | £7,650 multiplied by the intensity of study |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2024[^c-839e5c7924d03ad1461291c87ee39a2c] but before 1 September 2025[^c-fc2f13a7981089d2b61d9e407c48ef43] | £7,965 multiplied by the intensity of study |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2025[^c-172417f2befca39ab9ca0f46caf7b194] but before 1 September 2026[^c-a1024e27edadd3048aea151f8f73b735] | £8,105, multiplied by the intensity of study |
| Beginning on or after 1 September 2026[^c-2d49d099cbde78ca9efa14c329daba2d] | £8,265 multiplied by the intensity of study |

(3)[^c-0b6a3606222f4c40b0a3132865a7f772] This regulation is subject to regulation 58A.
