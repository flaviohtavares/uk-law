# The Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies (Wales) Regulations 2018, section 1

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Title, application and commencement

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies (Wales) Regulations 2018.

(2) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.

(3) These Regulations come into force on 1 October 2018.
