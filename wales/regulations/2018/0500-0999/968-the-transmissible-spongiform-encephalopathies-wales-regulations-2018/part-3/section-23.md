# The Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies (Wales) Regulations 2018, section 23

> Text as at 2020-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In Schedule 3 to the Official Feed and Food Controls (Wales) Regulations 2009 [^c23383461], for “Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies (Wales) Regulations 2008” substitute “ Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies (Wales) Regulations 2018 ”.

(2) The Animal By-Products (Enforcement) (Wales) Regulations 2014 [^c23383471] are amended as follows—

- (a) in regulation 20(a) (penalties), omit “not exceeding the statutory maximum”;
- (b) in regulation 29(1) (transitional provision), omit “for the period ending on 31 December 2014,”;
- (c) in Schedule 2, omit paragraph 7.
