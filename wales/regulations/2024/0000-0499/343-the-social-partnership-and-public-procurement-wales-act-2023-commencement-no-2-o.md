# The Social Partnership and Public Procurement (Wales) Act 2023 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 343 (W. 64) (C. 16)

Social Partnership, Wales

The Social Partnership and Public Procurement (Wales) Act 2023 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2024

Made

7 March 2024

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 48(1) of the Social Partnership and Public Procurement (Wales) Act 2023[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Title

The title of this Order is the Social Partnership and Public Procurement (Wales) Act 2023 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2024.

## 2. Provisions coming into force on 1 April 2024

The following provisions of the Social Partnership and Public Procurement (Wales) Act 2023 come into force on 1 April 2024—

- (a) section 17;
- (b) section 19.

Hannah Blythyn

Deputy Minister for Social Partnership, under the authority of the Minister for Social Justice, one of the Welsh Ministers

7 March 2024

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order made by the Welsh Ministers brings into force certain provisions of the Social Partnership and Public Procurement (Wales) Act [2023 (asc 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2023/1) (“**the Act**”).

Article 2 of this Order brings section 17 (social partnership duty: Welsh Ministers) and section 19 (social partnership reports: Welsh Ministers) of the Act info force on the 1 April 2024.
