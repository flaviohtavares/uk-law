# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 65

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 65. Oversight of adequacy of resources

(1) The responsible individual must report to the service provider on the adequacy of the resources available to provide the service in accordance with the requirements on service providers in Parts 2 to 12 of these Regulations.

(2) Such reports must be made on a quarterly basis.

(3) But the requirement in paragraph (1) does not apply where the service provider is an individual.
