# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 56

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 56. Whistleblowing

(1) The service provider must have arrangements in place to ensure that all persons working at the service are able to raise concerns about matters that may adversely affect the health, safety or well-being of individuals for whom the service is provided.

(2) These arrangements must include—

- (a) having a whistleblowing policy in place and acting in accordance with that policy, and
- (b) establishing arrangements to enable and support people working at the service to raise such concerns.

(3) The provider must ensure that the arrangements required under this regulation are operated effectively.

(4) When a concern is raised, the service provider must ensure that—

- (a) the concern is investigated;
- (b) appropriate steps are taken following an investigation;
- (c) a record is kept of both the above.
