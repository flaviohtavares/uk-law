# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 27

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 27. Prohibition on the use of corporal punishment

The service provider must ensure that persons working at the service use no form of corporal punishment at any time against any individual to whom accommodation is provided.
