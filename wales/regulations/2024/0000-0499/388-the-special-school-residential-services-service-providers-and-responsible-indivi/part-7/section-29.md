# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 29

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 29. Interpretation of Part 7

In this Part—

- “**improper treatment**” (“***triniaeth amhriodol***”) includes discrimination or unlawful restraint, including a deprivation of liberty that is not authorised in accordance with the terms of the Mental Capacity Act 2005[^f00017];
- “**neglect**” (“***esgeulustod***”) has the same meaning as in section 197(1) of the 2014 Act.
