# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 18

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 18. Continuity of care

The service provider must put arrangements in place to ensure that individuals receive such continuity of care as is reasonable to meet their needs for care and support.
