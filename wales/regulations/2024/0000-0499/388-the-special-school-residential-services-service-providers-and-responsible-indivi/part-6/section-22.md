# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 22

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 22. Access to health and other services

(1) The service provider must put in place arrangements for individuals—

- (a) to be able to access treatment, advice and other services from any health care professional as necessary, and
- (b) to be supported to access such services.

(2) In this regulation, “**health care professional**” means a person who is registered as a member of any profession to which section 60(2) of the Health Act 1999[^f00016] applies.
