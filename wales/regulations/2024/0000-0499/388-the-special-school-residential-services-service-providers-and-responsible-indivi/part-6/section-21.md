# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Respect and sensitivity

(1) The service provider must ensure that individuals are treated with respect and sensitivity.

(2) This includes, but is not limited to—

- (a) respecting the individual’s privacy and dignity;
- (b) respecting the individual’s rights to confidentiality;
- (c) promoting the individual’s autonomy and independence;
- (d) having regard to any relevant protected characteristics (as defined in section 4 of the Equality Act 2010[^f00015]) of the individual.
