# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 20

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 20. Language and communication

(1) The service provider must take reasonable steps to meet the language needs of individuals.

(2) The service provider must ensure that the individual is provided with access to such aids and equipment as may be necessary to facilitate the individual’s communication with others.
