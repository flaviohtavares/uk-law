# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. Health and safety

The service provider must ensure that any risks to the health and safety of individuals are identified and reduced so far as reasonably practicable.
