# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 9

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 9. Duty of candour

The service provider must act in an open and transparent way with—

- (a) individuals who are receiving care and support,
- (b) any parent or carer of those individuals, and
- (c) any placing authority.
