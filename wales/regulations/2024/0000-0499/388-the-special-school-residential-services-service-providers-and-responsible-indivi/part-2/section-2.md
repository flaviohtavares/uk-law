# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 2

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 2. Requirements in relation to the provision of the service

The service provider must ensure that the service is provided with sufficient care, competence and skill, having regard to the statement of purpose.
