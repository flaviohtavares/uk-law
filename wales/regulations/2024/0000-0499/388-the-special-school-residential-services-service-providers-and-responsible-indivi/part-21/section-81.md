# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 81

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 81. Amendment of the Regulated Services (Annual Returns) (Wales) Regulations 2017

In regulation 5 of the Regulated Services (Annual Returns) (Wales) Regulations 2017[^f00024], after “secure accommodation service” insert “, special school residential service”.
