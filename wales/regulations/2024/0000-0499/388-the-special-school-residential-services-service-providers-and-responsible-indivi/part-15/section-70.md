# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 70

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 70. Duty to ensure policies and procedures are up to date

The responsible individual must put suitable arrangements in place to ensure that the service provider’s policies and procedures as required by regulation 8(1) to (3) are kept up to date, having regard to the statement of purpose.
