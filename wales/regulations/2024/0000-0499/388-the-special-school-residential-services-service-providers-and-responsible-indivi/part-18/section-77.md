# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 77

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 77. Offences - responsible individuals

(1) It is an offence for the responsible individual to fail to comply with a requirement of any of the provisions specified in paragraph (2).

(2) The provisions specified for the purposes of paragraph (1) are the provisions of regulations 58(1), 59(1), 62(1) and (2), 64(1) and (2), 65(1) and (2), 66(1), 71(4), 72(1) and 75(1) and (3).
