# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Additional requirements – outdoor space

The external grounds (or, in the case of Category B premises, any part of the external grounds developed in conjunction with the building of the extension) which are used for the provision of the service in accordance with regulation 37(9) must—

- (a) be accessible to individuals in wheelchairs or with other mobility problems,
- (b) have sufficient and suitable seating, and
- (c) be designed to meet the needs of all individuals including those with physical, sensory and cognitive impairments.
