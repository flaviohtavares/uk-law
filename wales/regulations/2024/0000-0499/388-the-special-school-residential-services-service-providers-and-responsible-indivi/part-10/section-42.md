# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 42

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 42. Additional requirements – en-suite bathrooms

All bedrooms used for the provision of the service must have an en-suite bathroom which includes an accessible wash hand basin, lavatory and shower.
