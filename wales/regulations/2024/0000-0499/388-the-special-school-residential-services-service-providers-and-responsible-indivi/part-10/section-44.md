# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 44

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 44. Additional requirements – communal space

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), the amount of sitting, recreational and dining space which is used for the provision of the service in accordance with regulation 37(5) must be at least—

- (a) 4.1 square metres for each individual;
- (b) 5.1 square metres for wheelchair users.

(2) For Category B premises, this regulation applies so that the space requirement must be met in relation to any additional rooms for individuals.
