# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 34

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 34. Information for staff

(1) The service provider must ensure that all persons working at the service are provided with information about the service and the way it is provided.

(2) The service provider must ensure that there are arrangements in place to make staff aware of any codes of practice about the standards of conduct expected of social care workers, which are required to be published by the workforce regulator under section 112(1)(a) of the Act.
