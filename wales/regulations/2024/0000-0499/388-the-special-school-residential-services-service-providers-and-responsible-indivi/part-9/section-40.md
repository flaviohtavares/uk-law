# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 40

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 40. Facilities and equipment

The service provider must ensure that the facilities and equipment used for the provision of the service are—

- (a) suitable and safe for the purpose for which they are intended to be used;
- (b) used in a safe way;
- (c) properly maintained;
- (d) kept clean to a standard which is appropriate for the purpose for which they are being used;
- (e) stored appropriately.
