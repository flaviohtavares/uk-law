# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 59

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 59. Fitness requirements for appointment of manager

(1) The responsible individual must not appoint a person to manage the service unless that person is fit to do so.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1), a person is not fit to manage the service unless the requirements of regulation 31(2) (fitness of staff) are met in respect of that person.
