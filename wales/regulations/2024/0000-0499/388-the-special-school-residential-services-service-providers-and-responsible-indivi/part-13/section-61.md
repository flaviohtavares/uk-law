# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 61

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 61. Duty to report the appointment of manager to service provider

On the appointment of a manager in accordance with regulation 58(1), the responsible individual must give notice to the service provider of—

- (a) the name of the person appointed, and
- (b) the date on which the appointment is to take effect.
