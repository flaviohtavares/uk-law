# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 57

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 57. Supervision of management of the service

The responsible individual must supervise the management of the service, which includes taking the steps described in regulations 58, 63 and 64.
