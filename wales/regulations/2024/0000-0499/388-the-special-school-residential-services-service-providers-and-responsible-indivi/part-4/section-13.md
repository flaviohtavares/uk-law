# The Special School Residential Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 13

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 13. Records of personal plans

The service provider must—

- (a) keep a record of—
  - (i) the personal plan and any revised plan, and
  - (ii) the outcome of any review, and
- (b) give a copy of the personal plan and any revised plan to—
  - (i) the individual,
  - (ii) the parent or carer of the individual, unless this is not appropriate or would be inconsistent with the individual’s well-being, and
  - (iii) any placing authority.
