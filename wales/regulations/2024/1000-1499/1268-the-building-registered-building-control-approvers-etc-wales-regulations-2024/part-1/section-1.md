# The Building (Registered Building Control Approvers etc.) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 1

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Title, coming into force and application[^c-424f2ac4aba2c3353ffad3c9ef2084ac]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The title of these Regulations is the Building (Registered Building Control Approvers etc.) (Wales) Regulations 2024.

(2) These Regulations come into force on 1 January 2025.

(3) These Regulations apply in relation to Wales.
