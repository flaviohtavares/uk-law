## 7. Form, grounds and period for rejecting amendment notice[^key-18756e63a84ee48051f01370f88f1e83]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The prescribed form of an amendment notice is Form 2(W) in Schedule 1.

(2) An amendment notice must be accompanied by the plans and documents described in the form prescribed by paragraph (1).

(3) The grounds on which a local authority must reject an amendment notice are those prescribed in paragraphs 1 to 11 of Schedule 2 and the form to be used for rejecting an amendment notice is Form 7(W) in Schedule 1.

(4) The period within which a local authority may give notice of rejection of an amendment notice is 5 working days beginning with the day on which the notice is given.
