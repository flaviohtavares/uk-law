> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Form, grounds and period for rejecting final certificate[^c-7d51fcf8bc960e4a36e9450d34b34f01]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The prescribed form of a final certificate is Form 5(W) in Schedule 1.

(2) The grounds on which a local authority must reject a final certificate are those prescribed in Schedule 4 and the form to be used for rejecting a final certificate is Form 7(W) in Schedule 1.

(3) The period within which a local authority may give notice of rejection of a final certificate is 10 working days beginning with the day on which the certificate is given.

(4) A final certificate given by an approver to a local authority in accordance with these Regulations is evidence (but not conclusive evidence) that the requirements specified in the certificate have been complied with.
