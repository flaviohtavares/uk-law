# The Building (Registered Building Control Approvers etc.) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 13

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Form of notice under section 51C of the 1984 Act[^c-833c85f3a36c866a7d4a74c01a0b6fbb]

*Extent: E+W*

When giving a notice under section 51C(2) of the 1984 Act (change of person intending to carry out work) the form set out as Form 8(W) in Schedule 1 must be used.
