# The Building (Registered Building Control Approvers etc.) (Wales) Regulations 2024, SCHEDULE 2

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 2 — Grounds for rejecting an initial notice, an amendment notice, or a plans certificate combined with an initial notice

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations 6, 7 and 10

### 1. Form[^c-8673a6044aea3a3625909f5f2b35bafa]

The notice is not in the prescribed form.

### 2. Wrong local authority[^c-6263b4310d36cfcd53aadfbe08f21e42]

No part of the work described in the notice is to be carried out in the area of the local authority to whom the notice has been given.

### 3. Capacity of approver[^c-c7bd48a74e2847f4fcd00d15963aa29e]

The person who signed the notice as an approver is not an approver on the date the notice is rejected or if they are an approver on that date, their registration does not include all the work described in the notice.

**4.**[^c-7a408a1f499017bf780c4c3d892ad875] Additionally, in the case of an amendment notice, the person who signed the amendment notice as an approver is not the approver who gave the initial notice.

### 5. Breach of section 54B[^c-9cb25ace1c9d3ec7dc5bf4277e2c3303]

The person specified in the notice as the registered building inspector who gave advice in relation to that notice—

- (a) was not a registered building inspector on the date the notice was submitted, or
- (b) was a registered building inspector on that date but their registration did not include all the work described in the notice.

### 6. Information about the proposed work[^c-826dccd637633d62518c97529155e18e]

Neither the notice nor the accompanying plans and documents include—

- (a) the location and a description of the work, including the use of any building to which the work relates;
- (b) information needed by the local authority to decide whether the notice is to be rejected on ground 10 or 11;
- (c) information as to whether or not the work concerns a new dwelling.
- (d)[^c-e6793e957ae947f4ed80a16b915c4af2] a statement setting out the date when work will reach the point when it is to be regarded as commenced and, if applicable, the client’s assessment of when building control input[^c-d4e6f175ee2c8e090d4bf700d8aef111] is required for the proposed work.

### 7. Fire and rescue authority[^c-73e1777a0fb191a5558d31f91a184afb]

The approver is obliged by regulation 8 to consult the fire and rescue authority before giving a plans certificate or final certificate, and the notice does not contain an undertaking to do so.

### 8. Sewerage undertaker[^c-e6a68902c646594ac061010ba2df7135]

The approver is obliged by regulation 9 to consult the sewerage undertaker before giving a plans certificate or final certificate, and the notice does not contain an undertaking to do so.

### 9. Independence[^c-0aa69622e2a6e230009461e8735a10b7]

The notice does not contain a declaration by the approver that the approver does not, and while the initial notice is in force will not, have any professional or financial interest in that work.

### 10. Drain connection[^c-e86be41f250c8a77cbea925ab8f3429e]

In the case of the erection or extension of a building, the local authority considers that, in order to comply with the requirements of Part H of Schedule 1 to the 2010 Regulations, a proposed drain or private sewer must discharge to an existing sewer, but the statement accompanying the notice does not describe such an arrangement.

### 11. Local enactments[^c-f76e750c2db4df9b9d72af38352093da]

The local authority is not satisfied that the work described will comply with any local enactment which requires or authorises them to reject an application for building control approval[^c-65e0d7c01ba08f3cb0e2724c019d758f] submitted in accordance with building regulations.

### 12. Earlier notice[^c-a3f655d115283d844bcf79b0905c4d99]

(1) Except where sub-paragraph (2) applies, an initial notice (“an earlier notice”) has already been given in respect of any part of the work described in the notice.

(2) The ground in sub-paragraph (1) does not apply if—

- (a) subject to sub-paragraph 3,[^c-93702131fb23ac885bca0e066be704c6] an earlier notice has ceased to be in force and the local authority has taken no positive step to supervise the work described in it, or
- (b) the notice is accompanied by an undertaking by the approver who gave an earlier notice to the effect that the approver will cancel that notice as soon as the initial notice under consideration is accepted.

(3)[^c-7f5bff5793ddc8b2b98554349f899248] Sub-paragraph (2)(a) does not apply if—

- (a) the earlier notice has been cancelled by the client or the approver,
- (b) the client has received a notice of contravention in accordance with regulation 16(1), and
- (c) the client has failed to remedy the contravention in the time specified in regulation 16(3).

### 13. Commencement of work[^c-c26a7bc590d8b963398ab30a6c69a316]

Where a statement has been included detailing when the client considers building control input is required, the local authority may[^c-cf320666ff668d59d4f3ed6a7099271d] reject it if it disagrees with the initial notice, amendment notice, or a plans certificate combined with an initial notice.
