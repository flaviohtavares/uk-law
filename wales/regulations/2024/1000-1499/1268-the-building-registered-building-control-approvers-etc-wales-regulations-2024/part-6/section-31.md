## 31. Events causing public body’s notice to cease to be in force[^key-6b9b85a5bc9bdd4fd4f80f7ebf3fe8f9]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 14 applies where a public body’s notice is given as it applies where an initial notice is given but as if—

- (a) any reference to an initial notice were to a public body’s notice, and
- (b) any reference to a final certificate were to a public body’s final certificate.
