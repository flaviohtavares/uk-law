## 30. Form, grounds and period for rejecting public body’s final certificate[^c-09df8b4b15ca92a0135c40ce83b2d277]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The prescribed form of a public body’s final certificate is Form PB4(W) in Schedule 1 and the grounds on which a local authority must reject a final certificate are those prescribed in Schedule 8.

(2) The period within which a local authority may give notice of rejection of a public body’s final certificate is 10 working days beginning with the day on which the certificate is given.
