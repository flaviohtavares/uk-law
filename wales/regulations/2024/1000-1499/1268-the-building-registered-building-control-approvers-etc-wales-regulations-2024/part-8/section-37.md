# The Building (Registered Building Control Approvers etc.) (Wales) Regulations 2024, section 37

> Text as at 2026-07-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Contravention of certain regulations not to be an offence or be subject to a compliance notice[^c-0cf2d680b96afd7925b02f13607ed580][^c-707e95b878262489be84e89729052d26]

*Extent: E+W*

Each of these Regulations, other than regulation 17 (local authority powers in relation to partly completed work) , regulation 13C (Notifying a Registered Building Control Approver of a change in dutyholders) and regulation 13D (Compliance declaration to be given to Registered Building Control Approvers[^c-3dc155e4f9dfd9168dd293806d9a8395], is designated as a provision to which sections 35 (penalty for contravening building regulations) and 35B (compliance notices) do not apply[^c-5ffbbb8ac0b03617ec809abc18361e0c].
