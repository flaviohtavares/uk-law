# The Agriculture (Wales) Act 2023 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2024

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2024 No. 789 (W. 123) (C. 50)

Agriculture, Wales

The Agriculture (Wales) Act 2023 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2024

Made

9 July 2024

The Welsh Ministers, in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 56(4) of the Agriculture (Wales) Act 2023[^f00001], make the following Order:

## 1. Title and interpretation

(1) The title of this Order is the Agriculture (Wales) Act 2023 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2024.

(2) In this Order, “**the Act**” means the Agriculture (Wales) Act 2023.

## PART 1 — Provisions coming into force

### 2. Provisions coming into force on 15 July 2024

Section 24 of the Act comes into force on 15 July 2024.

Huw Irranca-Davies

Cabinet Secretary for Climate Change and Rural Affairs, one of the Welsh Ministers

9 July 2024

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order is the second commencement order made by the Welsh Ministers under the Agriculture (Wales) Act 2023[^f00002] (“**the Act**”).

This Order brings section 24 (Agricultural Holdings: dispute resolution relating to financial support) of the Act into force on 15 July 2024.
