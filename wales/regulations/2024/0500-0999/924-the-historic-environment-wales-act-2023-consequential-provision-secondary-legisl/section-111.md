# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 111

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 111. The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2017[^key-cb32a945350b4e111f850f563bdfe601]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 2(1) of the Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2017[^f00052], for sub-paragraph (d) of the definition of “sensitive area” substitute—

> - (d) a scheduled monument within the meaning given by section 3 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023;

.
