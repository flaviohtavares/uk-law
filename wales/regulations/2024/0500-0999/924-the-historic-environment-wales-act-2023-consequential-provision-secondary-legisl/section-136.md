# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 136

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 136. The Electricity and Gas (Energy Company Obligation) Order 2022[^c-b029fe34ac0ccae09152c33388f9080f]

*Extent: E+W*

In article 79(3) of the Electricity and Gas (Energy Company Obligation) Order 2022[^f00057]—

- (a) in the definition of “conservation area”—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (a), omit “and Wales”;
  - (ii) after sub-paragraph (a) insert—

    > - (aa) in the case of premises in Wales, section 210 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023;

    ;
- (b) in the definition of “listed building”—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (a), omit “and Wales”;
  - (ii) after sub-paragraph (a) insert—

    > - (aa) in the case of premises in Wales, section 76 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023;

    .
