# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 54

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 54. The Marine Licensing (Exempted Activities) Order 2011[^c-22448b0b012b3ecc8ed143977328672f]

*Extent: E+W*

In article 31(b) of the Marine Licencing (Exempted Activities) Order 2011[^f00034], after “1979” insert “or a monument entered in the schedule maintained under section 3 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”.
