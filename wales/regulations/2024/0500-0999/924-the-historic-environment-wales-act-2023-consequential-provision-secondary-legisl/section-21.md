# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 21

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21. The Hedgerows Regulations 1997[^c-d8e7190e009f54f40e4192240602a434]

*Extent: E+W*

In Schedule 1 to the Hedgerows Regulations 1997[^f00016], in Part 2, paragraph 2(a), after “1979” insert “or in the schedule of monuments maintained by the Welsh Ministers under section 3(1) of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”.
