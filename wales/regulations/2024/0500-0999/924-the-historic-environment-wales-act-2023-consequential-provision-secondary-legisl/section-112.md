# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 112

> Text as at 2025-12-15.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 112. The Electricity Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (England and Wales) Regulations 2017[^c-0173ac61d772f53b20c96c2737c13830]

*Extent: E+W*

In Schedule 2 to the Electricity Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) (England and Wales) Regulations 2017[^f00053], in paragraph 4(e)—

- (a) after “monument” insert “in England”;
- (b) after “1979” insert “or a scheduled monument in Wales within the meaning given by section 3 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”.
