# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 85

> Text as at 2025-12-15.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 85. The Local Inquiries and Qualifying Procedures (Standard Daily Amount) (Wales) Regulations 2017[^c-35e344842ec393967e8f65878232d329]

*Extent: E+W*

In the Local Inquiries and Qualifying Procedures (Standard Daily Amount) (Wales) Regulations 2017[^f00048], in regulation 1(4), in the definition of “local inquiry”, after “(costs of an inquiry under that Act)” insert “or paragraph 3 of Schedule 6 to the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”.
