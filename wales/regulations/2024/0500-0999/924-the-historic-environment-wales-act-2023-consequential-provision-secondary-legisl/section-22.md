# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 22

> Text as at 2025-12-15.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22. The Public Gas Transporter Pipe-line Works (Environmental Impact Assessment) Regulations 1999[^key-3f47122aaf2c824989e996aca8c7f09f]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 2 of the Public Gas Transporter Pipe-line Works (Environmental Impact Assessment Regulations 1999[^f00017], in paragraph (1), in the definition of “sensitive area”, sub-paragraph (g), after “1979” insert “or the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”.
