# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 24

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. The Environmental Impact Assessment (Forestry) (England and Wales) Regulations 1999[^key-40cb3d6218948c8c9a94d7a442a66a6a]

*Extent: E+W*

In Schedule 2 to the Environmental Impact Assessment (Forestry) (England and Wales) Regulations 1999[^f00019], in paragraph 1, in the definition of “sensitive area”, sub-paragraph (f), after “1979” insert “or the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”.
