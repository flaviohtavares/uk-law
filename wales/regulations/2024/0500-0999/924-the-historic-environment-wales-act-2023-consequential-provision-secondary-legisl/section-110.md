# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 110

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 110. The Environmental Impact Assessment (Agriculture) (Wales) Regulations 2017[^key-5d93b547e93c05b1541d443d642444f3]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 5(7) of the Environmental Impact Assessment (Agriculture) (Wales) Regulations 2017[^f00051], for sub-paragraph (f) substitute—

> - (f) a scheduled monument within the meaning given by section 3 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023.
