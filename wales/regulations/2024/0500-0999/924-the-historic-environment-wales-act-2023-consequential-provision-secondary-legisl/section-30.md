## 30. The Town and Country Planning (Electronic Communications) (Wales) (No. 1) Order 2004[^key-6b0ba97c1233930d117936ef8fbdcd83]

*Extent: E+W*

In the Town and Country Planning (Electronic Communications) (Wales) (No. 1) Order 2004[^f00022]—

- (a) in article 2, omit the definition of “the Listed Buildings Act”;
- (b) omit articles 7 to 9.
