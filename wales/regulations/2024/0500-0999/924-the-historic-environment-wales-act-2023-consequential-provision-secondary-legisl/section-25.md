# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 25

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. The Electronic Communications Code (Conditions and Restrictions) Regulations 2003[^c-86c9d539dd1fa9494b38c597e05cd0a7]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 2(2) of the Electronic Communications Code (Conditions and Restrictions) Regulations 2003[^f00020]—

- (a) in the definition of “conservation area”—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (a), omit “and Wales”;
  - (ii) after sub-paragraph (a) insert—

    > - (aa) in relation to Wales, any area designated as a conservation area under section 158 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023;

    ;
- (b) in the definition of “statutory list of buildings”—
  - (i) in sub-paragraph (a), omit “and Wales”;
  - (ii) after sub-paragraph (a) insert—

    > - (aa) in relation to Wales, the list of buildings of special architectural or historic interest maintained under section 76(1) of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023;

    .
