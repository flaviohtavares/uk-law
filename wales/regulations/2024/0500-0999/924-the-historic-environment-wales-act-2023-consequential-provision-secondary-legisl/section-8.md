# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 8

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 8. The Housing (Prescribed Forms) (No. 2) Regulations 1990[^key-2b8a9d6cd933c157360e983bf4be1c62]

*Extent: E+W*

In the Schedule to the Housing (Prescribed Forms) (No. 2) Regulations 1990[^f00008], in Form 18, in the second sub-paragraph of paragraph 2, after “1990” insert “or under section 76 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”.
