# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 135

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 135. The Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2017[^c-e274787316cd82ce87bddba065f2068f]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 70(1)(h) of the Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2017[^f00056]—

- (a) omit the “or” after sub-paragraph (i);
- (b) at the end of sub-paragraph (ii), before the full-stop insert—

  > ; or
  >
  > - (iii) paragraph 2(6) of Schedule 9 to the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (action by Welsh Ministers on rejection of purchase notice by planning authority)

  .
