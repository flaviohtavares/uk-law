# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 20

> Text as at 2025-12-15.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. The National Park Authorities (Wales) (Amendment) Order 1996[^c-873d8c5500adaaee2c689a98102b6388]

*Extent: E+W*

In the Schedule to the National Park Authorities (Wales) (Amendment) Order 1996[^f00015], in Part 2, paragraph 2, omit the entry for the inserted paragraph 12 relating to the Operations in Areas of Archaeological Importance (Forms of Notice etc.) Regulations 1984 and the heading before it.
