# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 45

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45. The Overhead Lines (Exemption) (England and Wales) Regulations 2009[^key-8b046243f095b77b814737a3ce8a79ae]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 3(2) of the Overhead Lines (Exemption) (England and Wales) Regulations 2009[^f00029]—

- (a) in the definition of “conservation area” and “listed building”—
  - (i) after “meanings” insert “in relation to areas and buildings in England”;
  - (ii) after “1990” insert “and in relation to areas and buildings in Wales, have the meanings in section 210 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”;
- (b) in the definition of “scheduled monument”—
  - (i) after “meaning” insert “in relation to monuments in England”;
  - (ii) after “1979” insert “and in relation to monuments in Wales, has the meaning in section 3(7) of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”.
