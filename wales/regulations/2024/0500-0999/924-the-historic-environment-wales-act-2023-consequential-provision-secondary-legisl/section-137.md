# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 137

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 137. Revocation and saving[^c-babc2f50025055ca92ced4a51c19e054]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The following instruments are revoked—

- (a) the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2016 (Commencement No. 1 and Transitional Provisions) Order 2017[^f00058];
- (b) the Ancient Monuments (Claims for Compensation) (Wales) Regulations 2017[^f00059];
- (c) the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2016 (Commencement No. 2) Order 2021[^f00060].

(2) The continuing operation of the saving made by section 105(4) of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023[^f00061] is not affected by paragraph (1)(a) or (c).

(3) The following instruments are revoked to the extent that they remain in force—

- (a) the Ancient Monuments (Applications for Scheduled Monument Consent) (Welsh Forms and Particulars) Regulations 2001[^f00062];
- (b) the Ancient Monuments (Claims for Compensation) (Wales) Regulations 1991[^f00063].
