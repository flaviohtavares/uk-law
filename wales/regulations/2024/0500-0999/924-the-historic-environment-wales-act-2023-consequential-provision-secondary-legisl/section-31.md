> Text as at 2025-12-15.

## 31. The Planning (National Security Directions and Appointed Representatives) (Wales) Regulations 2006[^key-431fb37705b61179775b6a3b74abe4ed]

*Extent: E+W*

The Planning (National Security Directions and Appointed Representatives) (Wales) Regulations 2006[^f00023] are amended as follows.

**32.**[^key-5628f4a532e87b689ddeaea460d6963c] In regulation 1, after paragraph (2) insert—

> (3) These Regulations form part of a code of law relating to the historic environment of Wales.
