> Text as at 2025-12-15.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. The Operations in Areas of Archaeological Importance (Forms of Notice etc.) Regulations 1984[^key-3a40dadf37689290935e36987d738e72]

*Extent: E+W*

The Operations in Areas of Archaeological Importance (Forms of Notice etc.) Regulations 1984[^f00005] are amended as follows—

- (a) in regulation 1(2), omit “and Wales”;
- (b) in regulation 4(a)(ii), omit “, or on the Secretary of State” to “in Wales”.
