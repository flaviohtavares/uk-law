# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 84

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 84. The Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2016[^c-86ae50945acb89c5b26849fb2ba1cc98]

*Extent: E+W*

In the Town and Country Planning (Environmental Impact Assessment) (Wales) Regulations 2016[^f00047], in regulation 2(1), in the definition of “sensitive area”, for sub-paragraph (d) substitute “(d) a scheduled monument within the meaning given by section 3 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023;”.
