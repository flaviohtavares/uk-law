# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 38

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**38.**[^c-4a859ead36e023c2976547effa3547c9] In Schedule 5—

- (a) in the first column of paragraph 14 of the table—
  - (i) in paragraph (i), after “1990” insert “or Part 3 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (ii), after “1979” insert “or a monument scheduled under Part 2 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”;
- (b) in the first column of paragraph 15 of the table, in paragraph (i), after “1990” insert “or Part 4 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”;
- (c) in the second column of paragraph 15 of the table—
  - (i) omit “and (ii)”;
  - (ii) after sub-paragraph (b) insert—

    > 15 (ii) The Historic Buildings and Monuments Commission for England.
