# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 39

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 39. The Transport and Works (Model Clauses for Railways and Tramways) Order 2006[^c-b64ac8c414a3dc610c6329867b22e1ca]

*Extent: E+W*

The Transport and Works (Model Clauses for Railways and Tramways) Order 2006[^f00025] is amended as follows.

**40.**[^c-142f2116f73b8725c1bb77ef0c008838] In paragraph 16(6) of Schedule 1, after “1979” insert “or the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”.

**41.**[^c-adddab8935707d34fdc0dbd533bcf911] In paragraph 21(6) of Schedule 2, after “1979” insert “or the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”.
