# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 48

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 48. The Infrastructure Planning (Decisions) Regulations 2010[^key-c799fc3c9921e0798ef0cb07844624af]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 2 of the Infrastructure Planning (Decisions) Regulations 2010[^f00032]—

- (a) in the definition of “conservation area”—
  - (i) for “and in Scotland” substitute “, in Scotland”;
  - (ii) after “1997” insert “and in Wales under section 158 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”;
- (b) in the definition of “listed building”—
  - (i) for “and in Scotland” substitute “, in Scotland”;
  - (ii) after “1997” insert “and in Wales under section 76 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”;
- (c) in the definition of “scheduled monument”, after “1979” insert “and in Wales under section 3 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”.
