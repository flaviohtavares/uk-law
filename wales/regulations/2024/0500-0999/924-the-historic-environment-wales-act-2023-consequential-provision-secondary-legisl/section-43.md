# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 43

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43. The Non-Domestic Rating (Unoccupied Property) (Wales) Regulations 2008[^key-cff856acd35b18fb92833e1750b8ca32]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 4 of the Non-Domestic Rating (Unoccupied Property) (Wales) Regulations 2008[^f00027]—

- (a) for paragraph (e) substitute—

  > - (e) which is the subject of a temporary listing notice under section 83 or 84 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 or is included in the list maintained under section 76(1) of that Act;

  ;
- (b) in paragraph (f), for “section 1 of the Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979” substitute “section 3 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”.
