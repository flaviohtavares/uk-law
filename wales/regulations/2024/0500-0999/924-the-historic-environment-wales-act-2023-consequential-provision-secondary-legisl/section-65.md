# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 65

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 65. The Energy Efficiency (Private Rented Property) (England and Wales) Regulations 2015[^key-93248ffddd9e74c667d03f3663f7476e]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 2(1) of the Energy Efficiency (Private Rented Property) (England and Wales) Regulations 2015[^f00040], in sub-paragraph (e) of the definition of “third party consent”—

- (a) omit “or”;
- (b) after “that Act” insert “, listed in accordance with section 76 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023, or in a conservation area designated in accordance with section 158 of that Act”.
