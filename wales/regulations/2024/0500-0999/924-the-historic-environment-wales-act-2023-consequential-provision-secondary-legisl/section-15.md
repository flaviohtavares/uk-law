# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 15

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**15.**[^key-3c1557f08af6482be95e48ee25aaacac] In article 1(2)—

- (a) for the definition of “listed building” substitute—

  > - “**listed building**” has the meaning given by section 76 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023;

  ;
- (b) for the definition of “scheduled monument” substitute—

  > - “**scheduled monument**” has the meaning given by section 3(7) of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023;

  ;
- (c) omit the definition of “site of archaeological interest”.
