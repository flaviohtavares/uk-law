# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 19

> Text as at 2025-12-15.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19. The Noise Insulation (Railways and Other Guided Transport Systems) Regulations 1996[^c-bc8731b1efb694d19a55539b444c34a5]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 12(3) of the Noise Insulation (Railways and Other Guided Transport Systems) Regulations 1996[^f00014], after “the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990” insert “or section 89 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023”.
