# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 131

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

**131.**[^key-a6b15d8e83ab5686cbaa4e1f4ad94c46] In regulation 9—

- (a) in paragraph (1)—
  - (i) in the words before sub-paragraph (a), for “section 2D(5)(c) of the 1990 Act” substitute “section 82(2)(c) of the 2023 Act”;
  - (ii) in sub-paragraph (c), for “section 2A of the 1990 Act” substitute “section 78 of the 2023 Act”;
- (b) in paragraph (2), for “section 2D(5)(c) of the 1990 Act” substitute “section 82(2)(c) of the 2023 Act”.
