# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 46

> Text as at 2025-12-15.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. The Corporation Tax (Land Remediation Relief) Order 2009[^c-146d6deabf2aabd420b01f680c4f5ed2]

*Extent: E+W*

In the table in article 5 of the Corporation Tax (Land Remediation Relief) Order 2009[^f00030], after the entry for the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990, insert—

> |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 | section 138 | requirement to serve repairs notice before starting compulsory acquisition |

.
