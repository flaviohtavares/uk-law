# The Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023 (Consequential Provision) (Secondary Legislation) Regulations 2024, section 64

> Text as at 2025-12-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 64. The Infrastructure Planning (Interested Parties and Miscellaneous Prescribed Provisions) Regulations 2015[^c-8ad0adf1bf374c473dc0deee97287e67]

*Extent: E+W*

In Part 2 of Schedule 2 to the Infrastructure Planning (Interested Parties and Miscellaneous Prescribed Provisions) Regulations 2015[^f00039], for the entry for the Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979 and the text in the corresponding column substitute—

> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | *Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023* | A consent under section 12 and Schedule 3 (authorisation of classes of works) |
> |  | A consent under section 13 (authorisation of works by scheduled monument consent) |

.
