# The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (County of Monmouthshire) Designation Order 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 568 (W. 124)

Road Traffic, Wales

The Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (County of Monmouthshire) Designation Order 2019

Made

13 March 2019

Laid before the National Assembly for Wales

14 March 2019

Coming into force

8 April 2019

This Order is made by the Welsh Ministers[^f00001] in exercise of the powers conferred on the National Assembly for Wales by paragraph 8(1) of Schedule 8 and paragraph 3(1) of Schedule 10 to the Traffic Management Act 2004[^f00002].

Monmouthshire County Council has applied to the Welsh Ministers for an Order to be made under these powers in respect of part of that Council’s area.

The Welsh Ministers have consulted the chief officer of Gwent Police in accordance with the requirements of paragraph 8(3) of Schedule 8 and paragraph 3(4) of Schedule 10 to the Traffic Management Act 2004.

## 1. Title and commencement

The title of this Order is the Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (County of Monmouthshire) Designation Order 2019 and it comes into force on 8 April 2019.

## 2. Designation of civil enforcement area for parking contraventions and special enforcement area

The Welsh Ministers designate the area described in the Schedule to this Order as—

- (a) a civil enforcement area for parking contraventions; and
- (b) a special enforcement area.

Ken Skates

Minister for Economy and Transport, one of the Welsh Ministers

13 March 2019

## SCHEDULE

Article 2

### Area designated as a civil enforcement area for parking contraventions and a special enforcement area

The area of the County of Monmouthshire (“the County”) with the exception of—

- (a) the length of the M4 Motorway within the County, from the boundary with the City of Newport to the boundary at the Prince of Wales Bridge, including its exit and entry slip roads;
- (b) the length of the M48 Motorway within the County, from its junction with the M4 Motorway to the boundary at the Wye Bridge, including its exit and entry slip roads;
- (c) the length of the A40 Trunk Road within the County, from its junction with the A465 Trunk Road to the boundary with the County of Powys and from its junction with the A465 Trunk Road to the boundary with the County of Herefordshire, including its exit and entry slip roads;
- (d) the length of the A465 Trunk Road within the County, from its junction with the A40 Trunk Road to the boundary with the County Borough of Blaenau Gwent and from its junction with the A40 Trunk Road to the boundary with the County of Herefordshire, including its exit and entry slip roads;
- (e) the length of the A449 Trunk Road within the County, from the boundary with the City of Newport to its junction with the A40 Trunk Road, including its exit and entry slip roads;
- (f) the length of the A4042 Trunk Road within the County, from the boundary with the County Borough of Torfaen to its junction with the A465 Trunk Road;
- (g) the length of the A466 Trunk Road within the County, from its junction with the M48 motorway to its junction with A48 Trunk Road; and
- (h) the length of the A48 Trunk Road within the County, from its junction with the A466 Trunk Road to the boundary with the County of Gloucestershire.

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order designates the area described in the Schedule to this Order as a civil enforcement area for parking contraventions and a special enforcement area for the purposes of Part 6 of the Traffic Management Act 2004. This Order enables Monmouthshire County Council to enforce parking contraventions within the area described in the Schedule to this Order through a civil law regime, as opposed to enforcement by police or traffic wardens in a criminal law context.

A full Regulatory Impact Assessment and Explanatory Memorandum can be obtained from the Orders Branch, Transport, Economic Infrastructure, Welsh Government, Cathays Park, Cardiff CF10 3NQ or on the National Assembly for Wales website at http://senedd.assembly.wales/mgIssueHistoryHome.aspx?IId=14828
