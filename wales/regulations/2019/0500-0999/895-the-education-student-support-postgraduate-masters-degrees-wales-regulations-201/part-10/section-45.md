# The Education (Student Support) (Postgraduate Master's Degrees) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 45

> Text as at 2026-03-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45.[^key-14d75c8f2afa457c6e1ab7364756a3eb]

*Extent: E+W*

In regulation 2 (interpretation), in paragraph (1)—

- (a) for the definition of “fees” substitute—

  > “**fees**” (“***ffioedd***”) has the meaning given in section 57(1) of the Higher Education (Wales) Act 2015 [^c23728171];

  ;
- (b) in the appropriate place insert—

  > “**person with section 67 leave to remain**” (“***person sydd â chaniatâd i aros o dan adran 67***”) means a person who—
  >
  > - (a) has extant leave to remain in the United Kingdom under section 67 of the Immigration Act 2016 [^c23728181] and in accordance with the immigration rules [^c23728191]; and
  > - (b) has been ordinarily resident in the United Kingdom and Islands throughout the period since the person was granted such leave;

  .
