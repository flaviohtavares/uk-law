# The Education (Student Support) (Postgraduate Master's Degrees) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 16

> Text as at 2026-03-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 16.[^c-efa2baa5856d68ab0116bea705fc361e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The events are—

- (a) the student's course becomes a designated course;
- (b) the student becomes an eligible student on the grounds that—
  - (i)[^c-4280468e734202ed98274cfccc483004] the student or the student’s spouse, civil partner or parent is recognised as a refugee, becomes a person granted stateless leave, becomes a person with leave to enter or remain or becomes a person granted humanitarian protection under paragraph 339C of the immigration rules;
  - (ia)[^c-47b945cd9f0e81e9dba99909bbb4ec79] the student becomes a person with leave to enter or remain as a relevant Afghan citizen or the spouse, civil partner, child or stepchild of such a person[^c-e8941a0d8cc8681b337c4e5c49065461];
  - (ib)[^c-aff931b93fc7e8846b4cc89eb8ac1cd6] the student becomes a protected Ukrainian national or the spouse, civil partner, child or stepchild of such a person[^c-9ae47b577fbb8de7492629a43acf1003];
  - (ii)[^c-db51f4bb85b527cd107f7be426c6949f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii)[^c-af90941742dfadff0e523dc7293f9df0] the student becomes a family member described in paragraph 8A(1)(a), 8B(1)(a), 8C(a) , 8D(1)(a) or 8D(2)(a)[^c-46f7f752d34fb2894e49836ed94958d8] of Schedule 2;
  - (iv)[^c-7e2b4edabd4bae6d077c760cbb87c62b] the student becomes a person described in paragraph 1(1)(a), (2)(a) or (3)(a), or 8BB[^c-fcccee5c82a6e9e096edea6816b081de] of Schedule 2;
  - (v)[^c-a99dd967e0a8da9e66c4f7a5ddb34ff8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (vi) the student becomes a person described in paragraph 6A(1)(a)[^c-9062c25d62027356f0826fd0a8a81c1e] of Schedule 2;
  - (vii)[^c-fca482690b6916c1d63aef8bca1f7bb0] the student becomes a person described in paragraph 9A(a) of Schedule 2;
  - (viii) the student or the student's parent becomes a person with section 67 leave to remain or a person[^c-d89dea10f9fad07a29c7996b1d856b18] granted leave to enter or remain[^c-9ec497b1905bdee87ca71564392317d6] as a protected partner;
  - (ix)[^c-873630ba73ba59852e95d0795ebc04d8] the student becomes a person with Calais leave.
- (c) the student commences a designated course after the start date of the designated course as the relevant academic authority has permitted the student to commence the course at this later start date.

(2) In this regulation, the following terms have the same meaning as in Schedule 2—
