> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Designated courses – exceptions[^c-321e18f71ae903f2e6ac2555f1d4c7be]

*Extent: E+W*

A course is not a designated course if it is recognised as a designated course for the purposes of—

- (a) regulation 5 or 83 of the Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2017 [^c23728091] (“**the 2017 Student Support Regulations**”);
- (b) regulation 5 or 8 of the Education (Student Support) (Wales) Regulations 2018 [^c23728101] (“**the 2018 Student Support Regulations**”);
- (c) regulation 4 of the Education (Postgraduate Doctoral Degree Loans) (Wales) Regulations 2018 [^c23728111] (“**the 2018 Doctoral Degree Loans Regulations**”).
