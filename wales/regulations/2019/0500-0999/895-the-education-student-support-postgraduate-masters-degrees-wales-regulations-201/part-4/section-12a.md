# The Education (Student Support) (Postgraduate Master's Degrees) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 12A

> Text as at 2026-03-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12A. Persons who cease to have leave to remain under residence scheme immigration rules[^c-e1f0771ed59246c09b44d95c8843475d][^c-54ecbb64a3eaffeb64d0e147a7aaa014]

*Extent: E+W*

Where—

- (a) the Welsh Ministers have determined that, by virtue of—
  - (i) falling within paragraph (1)(a)[^c-76dc4c2e0ce5a8f68aaff81e10b14db3][^c-a05e289018399a2c5e93bd92502cd5a8]... (iv) or (v) of the definition of “person with protected rights”, or
  - (ii) meeting the conditions in paragraph 1(2)(a)[^c-c1d1d00411d56241a0787a8f8ac42ad1]... (iv) of Schedule 2,

  a person (“A”) is an eligible student in connection with an application for support for a designated course, and
- (b) as at the day before the relevant day[^c-cfb39413b4a9e11eded8afcb92ce3274], A is not a person with protected rights,

A’s status as an eligible student terminates immediately before the relevant day[^c-859f691c06cabe875b8dfd6971fabcc6].
