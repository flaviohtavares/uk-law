> Text as at 2026-03-04.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. Students becoming eligible during a course[^c-284d8789ce6c0a1b8fab6664858c838d]

*Extent: E+W*

Where one of the events listed in regulation 16(1) occurs during the currency of a student's course, a student may qualify for support under these Regulations, provided the student complies with the application provisions set out in Part 5.
