# The Education (Student Support) (Postgraduate Master's Degrees) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 12H

> Text as at 2026-03-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12H. Persons who cease to be protected Ukrainian nationals[^key-6a93b71c9900857b058d870807fd503b]

*Extent: E+W*

Where—

- (a) the Welsh Ministers have determined that, by virtue of being a protected Ukrainian national or the spouse, civil partner, child or stepchild of such a person, a person (“**A**”) was an eligible student in connection with an application for support for a designated course, and
- (b) as at the day before the relevant day, the period for which A or[^key-18830c9e4e038415650321b5254f3017] A’s spouse, civil partner, parent or step-parent, as the case may be, is allowed to stay in the United Kingdom[^key-65bf61f45968ef6c00a0960a96e4649d] has expired and no further leave to enter or remain has been granted,

A’s status as an eligible student terminates immediately before the relevant day.
