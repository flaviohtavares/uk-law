# The Education (Student Support) (Postgraduate Master's Degrees) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 11

> Text as at 2026-03-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. Period of eligibility – general rule[^key-a84597f6ec23ffe58dd5e8fae451054b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A student's status as an eligible student in connection with a designated course is retained until the end of the student's period of eligibility unless terminated in accordance with regulation 12 , 12A[^key-4492811e3d2fa64b9d0a9d5340b71329] or 13.

(2) A student's period of eligibility ends at the end of the academic year in which the student completes the designated course.
