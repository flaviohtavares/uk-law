# The Education (Student Support) (Postgraduate Master's Degrees) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 14

> Text as at 2026-03-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Reinstatement of eligibility after termination[^c-d84c0483465e22c704ea5b97f4d3cb6f]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a student's period of eligibility terminates under regulation 12 or 13 during the academic year in which the student completes the designated course, the Welsh Ministers may reinstate the student's period of eligibility for such period as they think appropriate.
