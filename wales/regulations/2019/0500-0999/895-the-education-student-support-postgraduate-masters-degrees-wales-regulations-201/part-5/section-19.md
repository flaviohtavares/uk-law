# The Education (Student Support) (Postgraduate Master's Degrees) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 19

> Text as at 2026-03-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19. Time limits[^key-96b24e9c10b896b96400ac49692c71bd]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to paragraph (2), an application under regulation 18(1) or an application to amend the amount of the loan under regulation 31(4) or 31ZA(4)[^key-30cd531373bcde315dcbd2ae961d4428] must reach the Welsh Ministers no later than the end of the ninth month of the final academic year of the course.

(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply where the Welsh Ministers consider that having regard to the circumstances of the particular case the time limit should not apply, in which case the application to amend the amount must reach the Welsh Ministers no later than such date as they specify in writing.
