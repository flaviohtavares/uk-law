# The Education (Student Support) (Postgraduate Master's Degrees) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 23

> Text as at 2026-03-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Requirement to enter into a contract for a loan[^c-fed66fa6de7ed471f16645cd9db60632]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An eligible student may not receive a contribution to costs loan under these Regulations unless the student enters into a contract for the loan with the Welsh Ministers.

(2) The contract—

- (a) must be in such form and on such terms, and
- (b) may be required to be signed in such manner (including electronically),

as the Welsh Ministers may specify.
