# The Education (Student Support) (Postgraduate Master's Degrees) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 18

> Text as at 2026-03-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18. Requirement to apply for support[^c-316f146f1856975769d7f368f6ab205d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person does not qualify for support as an eligible student in relation to a designated course unless the person makes an application for support in relation to that course.

(2) An application under paragraph (1) must—

- (a) be in such form and contain such information as the Welsh Ministers may require,
- (b) be accompanied by such documentation as the Welsh Ministers may require,
- (c) reach the Welsh Ministers within the time limit specified in regulation 19, and
- (d) in relation to a designated course beginning before 1 August 2024,[^c-442fdeffe6b6a86208176daba8a2e2ab] state whether the person is applying for—
  - (i) a base grant,
  - (ii) a contribution to costs grant,
  - (iii) a contribution to costs loan, or
  - (iv) any combination of the above.
