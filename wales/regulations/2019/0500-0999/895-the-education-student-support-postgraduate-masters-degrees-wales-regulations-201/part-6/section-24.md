> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Base grant and contribution to costs grant[^key-db3e73c11b3975d9c6bbace6e8319f07]

*Extent: E+W*

A base grant and contribution to costs grant are grants made available by the Welsh Ministers to an eligible student in relation to a designated course beginning before 1 August 2024[^key-a17570e74f2ee91b1b925256ce113664].
