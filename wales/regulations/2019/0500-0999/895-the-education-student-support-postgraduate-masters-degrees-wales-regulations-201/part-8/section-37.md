# The Education (Student Support) (Postgraduate Master's Degrees) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 37

> Text as at 2026-03-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Overpayments - general[^c-bf29a7452c0684f4b6b5c459d80ba260]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where an eligible student has been paid an amount of any grant or contribution to costs loan which exceeds the amount to which the student is entitled under these Regulations, the student must repay the excess amount if required to do so by the Welsh Ministers.

(2) In this Chapter, references to an eligible student are to be treated as including a person who has received support but is not, or is no longer, an eligible student.
