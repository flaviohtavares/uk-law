# The Regulated Adoption Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 29

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29. Adequacy of premises[^key-4b34cf2514748625e50d51f122c799a0]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must ensure that the premises used for the operation of the service have adequate facilities for—

- (a) the supervision of staff;
- (b) the secure storage of records.
