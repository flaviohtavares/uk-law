# The Regulated Adoption Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 14

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Service agreement[^key-c8d78582f92e53e8a6384154fb102c31]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The service provider must ensure that every individual who receives support is given a signed copy of any agreement relating to—

- (a) the support provided to the individual;
- (b) any other services provided to the individual.

(2) The service provider must ensure that the individual receives such assistance as is necessary to enable the individual to understand the information contained in any such agreement.
