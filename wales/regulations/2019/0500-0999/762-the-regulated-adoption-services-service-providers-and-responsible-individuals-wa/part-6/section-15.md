# The Regulated Adoption Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 15

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. Standards of support – overarching requirements[^c-35e205a88dc2929db05cbe8adf220de7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The service provider must ensure that support is provided in a way which protects, promotes and maintains the safety and well-being of individuals.

(2) The service provider must ensure that support is provided in a way which—

- (a) maintains good personal and professional relationships with individuals and staff, and
- (b) encourages and assists staff to maintain good personal and professional relationships with individuals.
