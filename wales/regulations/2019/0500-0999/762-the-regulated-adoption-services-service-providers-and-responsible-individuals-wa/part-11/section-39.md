# The Regulated Adoption Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 39

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 39. Duty to report the appointment of manager to service provider[^c-84c6956e59cdae97867c7ddb7ea278f9]

*Extent: E+W*

On the appointment of a manager in accordance with regulation 36(1), the responsible individual must give notice to the service provider of—

- (a) the name of the person appointed, and
- (b) the date on which the appointment is to take effect.
