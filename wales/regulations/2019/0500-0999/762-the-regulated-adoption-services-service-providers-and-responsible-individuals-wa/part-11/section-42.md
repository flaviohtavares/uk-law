# The Regulated Adoption Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 42

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 42. Visits[^c-d73ebdbbf786ec286c2804cb35654ff8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The responsible individual must—

- (a) visit the premises from which the service is provided,
- (b) meet with members of staff who are employed to provide a service from each place in respect of which the responsible individual is designated, and
- (c) meet with individuals or any representatives of individuals for whom a service is being provided from each such place.

(2) The frequency of such visits and meetings is to be determined by the responsible individual having regard to the statement of purpose, but must be at least every 3 months.
