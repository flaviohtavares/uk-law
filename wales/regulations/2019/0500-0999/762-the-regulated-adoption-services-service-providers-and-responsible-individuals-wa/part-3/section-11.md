# The Regulated Adoption Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 11

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. Duty of candour[^key-9a174eb8507c8e62bd39bd64a0ce83be]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must act in an open and transparent way with—

- (a) individuals;
- (b) any representatives of those individuals.
