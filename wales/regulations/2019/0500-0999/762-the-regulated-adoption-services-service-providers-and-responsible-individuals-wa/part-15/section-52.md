# The Regulated Adoption Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 52

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52. Duty of candour[^key-9f5933a09c557c6f9172a2bcf6ce3f91]

*Extent: E+W*

The responsible individual must act in an open and transparent way with—

- (a) individuals, and
- (b) any representatives of those individuals.
