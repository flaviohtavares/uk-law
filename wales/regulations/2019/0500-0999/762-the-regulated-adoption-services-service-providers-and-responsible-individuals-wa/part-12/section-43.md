# The Regulated Adoption Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 43

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43. Oversight of adequate resources[^c-530e7945f7e166fe9644f088abea86c1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The responsible individual must report to the service provider on the adequacy of the resources available to provide the service in accordance with the requirements of these Regulations.

(2) Such reports must be made on a quarterly basis.

(3) But this requirement does not apply where the service provider is an individual.
