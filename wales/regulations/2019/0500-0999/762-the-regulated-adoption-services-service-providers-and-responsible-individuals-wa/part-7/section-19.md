# The Regulated Adoption Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 19

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19. Safeguarding - overarching requirement[^key-85592a9b79614ae89a8eb6baeaa7aef3]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must provide the service in a way which ensures that individuals are safe and are protected from abuse, neglect and improper treatment.
