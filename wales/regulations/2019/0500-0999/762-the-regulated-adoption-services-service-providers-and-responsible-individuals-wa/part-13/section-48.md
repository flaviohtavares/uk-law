# The Regulated Adoption Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 48

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 48. Duty to ensure policies and procedures are up to date[^key-0830ccd22b48ff813a89e1b22bc3fdf9]

*Extent: E+W*

The responsible individual must put suitable arrangements in place to ensure that the service provider's policies and procedures are kept up to date, having regard to the statement of purpose.
