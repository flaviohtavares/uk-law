# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 51

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 51. Fitness requirements for appointment of manager[^key-ce13cb60eb42b530e28dff6be2e786a5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The responsible individual must not appoint a person to manage the service unless that person is fit to do so.

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (1), a person is not fit to manage the service unless the requirements of regulation 30(2) (fitness of staff) are met in respect of that person.
