# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 55

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 55. Arrangements when manager is absent[^key-5c1d6652060a06b1c3ad470117636230]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The responsible individual must put suitable arrangements in place to ensure that the service is managed effectively at any time when there is no manager or when the manager is absent from the service.

(2) If there is no manager or the manager is absent for a period of more than 28 days, the responsible individual must—

- (a) notify the service provider and the Welsh Ministers, and
- (b) inform them of the arrangements which have been put in place for the effective management of the service.
