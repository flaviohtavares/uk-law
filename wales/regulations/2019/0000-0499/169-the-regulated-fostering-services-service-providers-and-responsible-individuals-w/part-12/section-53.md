# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 53

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 53. Duty to report the appointment of manager to service provider[^key-edade6c95bf909662cc8faa01895d974]

*Extent: E+W*

On the appointment of a manager in accordance with regulation 50(1), the responsible individual must give notice to the service provider of—

- (a) the name of the person appointed, and
- (b) the date on which the appointment is to take effect.
