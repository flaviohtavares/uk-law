# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 37

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Adequacy of facilities[^key-2f4d3af6e32de5e2bd31b3f482bea62a]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must ensure that the premises used for the operation of the service have adequate facilities for—

- (a) the supervision of staff, and
- (b) the secure storage of records.
