# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 36

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. Premises, facilities and equipment - service providers[^key-719c00a8ec3d47f3828c8d5792be8b39]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must ensure that the premises, facilities and equipment used for the operation of the service are suitable, having regard to the statement of purpose for the service.
