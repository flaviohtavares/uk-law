# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 64

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 64. Statement of compliance with the requirements as to standards of care and support[^key-b2eb11c80e84b4c7dd20a865eab67761]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The responsible individual must prepare the statement required to be included in the annual return under section 10(2)(b) of the 2016 Act.

(2) When preparing the statement, the responsible individual must have regard to the assessment of the standard of care and support which is included in the report prepared in accordance with regulation 63(4).
