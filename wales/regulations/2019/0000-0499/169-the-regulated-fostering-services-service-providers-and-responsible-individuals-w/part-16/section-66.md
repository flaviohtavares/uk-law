# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 66

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 66. Duty of candour[^key-44693a75893aebdbb9e99638473e801e]

*Extent: E+W*

The responsible individual must act in an open and transparent way with—

- (a) any child who is placed by the service provider,
- (b) the parents of any such child,
- (c) foster parents and prospective foster parents,
- (d) the placing authority.
