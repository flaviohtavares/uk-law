# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 45

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45. Policies and procedures to foster parents[^key-aa0674a46388e230b97978ac951abed1]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must ensure that foster parents are familiar with and act in accordance with the policies and procedures established under regulations 11, 21, 22, 25, 26, 27, 42 and 48.
