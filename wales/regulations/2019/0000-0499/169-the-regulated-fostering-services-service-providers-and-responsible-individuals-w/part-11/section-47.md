# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 47

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 47. Effective working relationships[^key-79a3854468a62b25493bd6e6dc762a84]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must—

- (a) maintain good professional relationships with foster parents, and
- (b) encourage and assist foster parents to maintain good personal relationships with children placed with them.
