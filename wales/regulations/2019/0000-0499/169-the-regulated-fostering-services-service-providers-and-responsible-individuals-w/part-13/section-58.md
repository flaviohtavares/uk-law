# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 58

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Other reports to the service provider[^key-c7f6b0b058c870056c2f3fa8d48e34b2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The responsible individual must, without delay, report to the service provider—

- (a) any concerns about the management or provision of the service,
- (b) any significant changes to the way in which the service is managed or provided, and
- (c) any concerns that the service is not being provided in accordance with the statement of purpose for the service.

(2) The requirement in paragraph (1) does not apply where the service provider is an individual.
