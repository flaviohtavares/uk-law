# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 62

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 62. Duty to ensure policies and procedures are up to date[^c-fe28cf26bb4ab8f240213b2fc7a88bb4]

*Extent: E+W*

The responsible individual must put suitable arrangements in place to ensure that the service provider's policies and procedures are kept up to date, having regard to the statement of purpose.
