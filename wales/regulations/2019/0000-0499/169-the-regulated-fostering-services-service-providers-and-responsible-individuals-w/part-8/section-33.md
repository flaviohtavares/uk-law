# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 33

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Information for staff[^key-19c5e8b80db540c11052277d4aad4e3b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The service provider must ensure that all persons working at the service (including any person allowed to work as a volunteer) are provided with information about the service and the way it is provided.

(2) The service provider must ensure that there are suitable arrangements in place to make staff aware of any codes of practice about the standards of conduct expected of social care workers which are required to be published by SCW under section 112(1)(a) of the 2016 Act.
