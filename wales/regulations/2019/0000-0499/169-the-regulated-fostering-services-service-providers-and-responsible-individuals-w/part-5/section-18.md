# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 18

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18. Compliance with foster care agreement[^key-158c67a32f9aecfafcfe96a95349934a]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must monitor the foster parents' compliance with the requirements of the foster care agreement.
