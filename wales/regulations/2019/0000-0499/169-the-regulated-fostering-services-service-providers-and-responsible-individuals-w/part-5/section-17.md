# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 17

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Respect and sensitivity[^key-9359da049658772635b0403036a8e694]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The service provider must put arrangements in place to ensure that children placed by the provider are treated with respect and sensitivity.

(2) This includes, but is not limited to—

- (a) respecting the child's privacy and dignity,
- (b) respecting the child's right to confidentiality,
- (c) promoting the child's autonomy and independence, and
- (d) having regard to any relevant protected characteristics (as defined in section 4 of the Equality Act 2010) of the child.
