# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 19

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19. Duty to promote contact[^c-3c1ada5516f64a61cc5361cc24788872]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must ensure foster parents promote contact between a child placed with them and the child's parents, relatives and friends, in accordance with the child's care and support plan and any court order relating to contact.
