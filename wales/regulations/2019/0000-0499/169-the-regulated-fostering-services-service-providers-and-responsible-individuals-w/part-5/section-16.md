# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 16

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 16. Language and communication[^c-6948e61e3eef70867a0bbdde37732bc8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The service provider must take reasonable steps to meet the language needs of children.

(2) The service provider must ensure that children are provided with access to such aids and equipment as may be necessary to facilitate their communication with others.
