# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 13

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Standards of care and support – overarching requirements[^c-1172c3ac3492304180b4a875e403402a]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must ensure that foster parents give care and support to a child placed with them—

- (a) in accordance with the child's care and support plan, and
- (b) in a way which maintains, protects and promotes the safety and well-being of the child.
