# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 14

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Continuing suitability of the placement[^c-b450c8909dfe6dc0f789c5dd158eda4d]

*Extent: E+W*

If, as a result of a change in the child's assessed needs, the placement made by the service provider is no longer suitable to the meet the child's needs for care and support, even after any reasonable adjustments have been made, the service provider must immediately give written notice of this to the placing authority.
