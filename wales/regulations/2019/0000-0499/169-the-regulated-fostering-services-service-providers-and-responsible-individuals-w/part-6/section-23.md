# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 23

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Prohibition on the use of corporal punishment[^c-7d40e47adcd18abfdeab3bd98551a103]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must ensure that foster parents do not use any form of corporal punishment at any time against any child.
