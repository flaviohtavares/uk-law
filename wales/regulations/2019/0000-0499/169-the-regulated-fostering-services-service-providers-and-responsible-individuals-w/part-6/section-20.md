# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 20

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. Safeguarding – overarching requirements[^key-3203ad0dd0a3103449aea5fbcc5236dc]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must put arrangements in place to ensure that children placed by it are safe and are protected from abuse, neglect and improper treatment.
