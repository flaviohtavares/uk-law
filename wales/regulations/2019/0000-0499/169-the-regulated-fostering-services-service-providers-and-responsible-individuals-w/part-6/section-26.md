# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 26

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Procedure when child is absent without permission[^key-bb9b0f5d5f35d8afd7174c78089e3c9f]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must ensure that there is a procedure to be followed when any child who has been placed with foster parents by the provider is absent without permission.
