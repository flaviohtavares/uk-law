# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 24

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Deprivation of liberty[^key-77f764b6f92de4a47b3b1fa9b4d6aefd]

*Extent: E+W*

A child must not be deprived of their liberty for the purpose of receiving care and support without lawful authority.
