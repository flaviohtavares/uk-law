# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 25

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. Policy and procedures on bullying[^key-4b4eaafd4b8b6f2d498b5242144467e0]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must have in place a policy on the prevention of bullying and procedures for dealing with an allegation of bullying.
