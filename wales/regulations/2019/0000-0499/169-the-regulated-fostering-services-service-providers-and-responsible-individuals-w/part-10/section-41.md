# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 41

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Conflicts of interest[^key-5e1b8cef80dbb6954d8ad6aff9230877]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must have effective arrangements in place to identify, record and manage potential conflicts of interest.
