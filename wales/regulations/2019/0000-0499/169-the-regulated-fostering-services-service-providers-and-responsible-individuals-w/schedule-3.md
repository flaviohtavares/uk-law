# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, SCHEDULE 3

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## SCHEDULE 3 — Notifications by the service provider

*Extent: E+W*

Regulation 40

### PART 1 — Notifications to the Welsh Ministers

**1.**[^c-861e45e062ec21653c7828aaf4584540] Any revision to the statement of purpose, 28 days prior to the revised statement of purpose coming into effect.

**2.**[^c-6c772c026530db13aaa69e40456d32b4] Service provider (individual or organisation) changes their name.

**3.**[^c-0c99c4d5a2b19dd8148b363d8d2e5ab7] Where, on or after 1 April 2020, the service provider is a body corporate, any change in the—

- (a) directors,
- (b) trustees, or
- (c) members of the managing committee,

  of the body corporate.

**3A.** Where, on or after 1 April 2020, the service provider is an unincorporated body, any change in the persons who are concerned in the management and control of the body.

**4.**[^c-b7895c4c9f66c755ee5b65738cd3ac81] Where the service provider is an individual, the appointment of a trustee in bankruptcy in relation to that individual.

**5.**[^c-9329db8ff2fc475f758a5b501e080a6a] Where the service provider is a body corporate or partnership, the appointment of a receiver, receiver manager, liquidator or provisional liquidator in relation to that body corporate[^c-3b1d0edbe7428762a420c7d2d455eac0] or partnership.

**6.**[^c-ee5a1d06af64522c218860632033f141] Where the service provider is a partnership, death of one of the partners.

**7.**[^c-f660fd645b4b52c7909c3a7ccffd685c] Where the service provider is a partnership, any change in the partners.

**8.**[^c-677ba5eacb86765665eb9d198866e238] Expected absence of the responsible individual for 28 days or more, 7 days prior to the commencement of the absence.

**9.**[^c-d49f2bf320c765df68b0deab8c9cef0a] Unexpected absence of the responsible individual, no later than 7 days after the commencement of the absence.

**10.**[^c-c74747e916f872fa33d30c242a918c25] Unexpected absence of the responsible individual for 28 days or more, where no prior notification has been given, immediately on the expiry of 28 days following the commencement of the absence.

**11.**[^c-684510ab3a420d593e3caab40cb97146] Return from absence of the responsible individual.

**12.**[^c-5725fb127d05204d920644c45b9b67eb] The responsible individual ceases, or proposes to cease, being the responsible individual for the service.

**13.**[^c-cc47d92e1e90ac78510159cf54b5d3cb] Any abuse or allegation of abuse in relation to a child that involves the service provider, a member of staff, a volunteer and/or a foster parent.

**14.**[^c-8fc9ab5706a5793f1e4f0c08d24ba1e8] Service provider, responsible individual or appointed manager convicted of criminal offence.

**15.**[^c-25f4b90ac765eef404ffac97bc4c945e] Any allegation of misconduct by a member of staff.

**16.**[^c-52938350a4651400d3d1609485680cee] Serious accident or injury of a child placed with foster parents.

**17.**[^c-a951aa4d1f89a8896fe4e0148f895c54] Any incident which is reported to the police relating to a child placed with foster parents.

**18.**[^c-fe42cc57512b435260f629d219e9e547] Death of a child placed with foster parents.

**19.**[^c-6b2b6be0e5cdbfc7cf736490b4dd4368] Any referral to the Disclosure and Barring Service pursuant to the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006.

**20.**[^c-a6a28558cdab74f4e9a25a6da90201df] Instigation and outcome of any child protection enquiry involving a child placed with foster parents.

**21.**[^c-2f92f19890718aa7ea8e2d7be9ea5154] Any incident of child sexual or criminal exploitation or any suspected child sexual or criminal exploitation.

**22.**[^c-0bc020afc33be9e5afbe11bba9b0b510] Any proposed change to the address of the principal office, 28 days prior to the change taking place.

**22A.**[^c-42e191976cb6d10976c4b301640d1838] Any change of telephone number or electronic mail address for the service.

### PART 2 — Notifications to the placing authority

**23.**[^c-dff0c7919cca61a548c03b956ceca33f] Death of a child placed with foster parents.

**24.**[^c-f40a353d82ed2ee3620301b1f7fab392] Any referral to the Disclosure and Barring Service pursuant to the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006.

**25.**[^c-6a3fe8594efa536538916f4bede6a280] Serious accident or injury of a child placed with foster parents.

**26.**[^c-84a99b8dcb26132def3a28021f641ce0] Outbreak at the home of foster parents of any infectious disease which in the opinion of a general practitioner attending the home is sufficiently serious to be so notified.

**27.**[^c-0229f6b276c633158749b61a91fabe75] Allegation that a child placed with foster parents has committed a serious offence.

**28.**[^c-18b570ab05e16ee530684f9e0cf89fae] Any incident which is reported to the police relating to a child placed with foster parents.

**29.**[^c-2224a3abc9435b3210e73102c4e17afb] Any incident of a child placed with foster parents being absent without permission.

**30.**[^c-5d1f8731ff53df4b18c5aa7f25ba0363] Any serious complaint or allegation about any foster parents approved by the service provider.

**31.**[^c-b129ada52c69fccf330065dba03b8dae] Instigation and outcome of any child protection enquiry involving a child placed with foster parents.

**32.**[^c-7c4beee8db0abbd6164c9cf36c0ff72a] Any incident of child sexual or criminal exploitation or any suspected child sexual or criminal exploitation.

### PART 3 — Notifications to the area authority in whose area the foster home is situated

**33.**[^c-7c27b78572094e87751ba92c41ca15b5] Death of a child placed with foster parents.

**34.**[^c-7e328e71071acc7cc391e0813385a6c6] Outbreak at the home of foster parents of any infectious disease which in the opinion of a general practitioner attending the home is sufficiently serious to be so notified.

**35.**[^c-eddb6db4ea87106bc8f11916e3db38ba] Any incident of child sexual or criminal exploitation or any suspected child sexual or criminal exploitation.

**36.**[^c-7b5c1781e54da7da512ffd0702ca7768] Instigation and outcome of any child protection enquiry involving a child placed with foster parents.

**37.**[^c-0e30bd7c8a3d75192a158bd6a27eb523] Any incident which is reported to the police relating to a child placed with foster parents.

### PART 4 — Notifications to the Local Health Board in whose area the child is placed

**38.**[^c-ddcf81d7d110acd98ff6cb340e3d80c8] The death of a child placed with foster parents.

**39.**[^c-ec3cb5fdb5973cc4708d3187919be17b] Outbreak at the home of foster parents of any infectious disease which in the opinion of a general practitioner attending the home is sufficiently serious to be so notified.

### PART 5 — Notifications to the police

**40.**[^c-3e5d5e3b61851df32f4d23c9f0afe06f] Allegation that a child placed with foster parents has committed a serious offence.

**41.**[^c-0bac1810c2e08c0ddd2e3ca11c32e803] Any incident of child sexual or criminal exploitation or any suspected child sexual or criminal exploitation.
