# The Regulated Fostering Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 70

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 70. Appointment of liquidators etc.[^c-c05f1c61dc6cf597e7c3e7aa5e49fc05]

*Extent: E+W*

An appointed person [^c23552831] must—

- (a) without delay, give written notice to the Welsh Ministers of their appointment and the reasons for their appointment;
- (b) within 28 days of their appointment, inform the Welsh Ministers of their intentions regarding the future operation of the service.
