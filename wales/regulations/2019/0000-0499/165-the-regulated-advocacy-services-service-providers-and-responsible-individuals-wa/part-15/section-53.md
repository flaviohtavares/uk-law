# The Regulated Advocacy Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 53

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 53. Duty of candour[^key-99a9f003424ec5de05a14dd3d4941953]

*Extent: E+W*

The responsible individual must act in an open and transparent way with—

- (a) individuals,
- (b) any representatives of those individuals.
