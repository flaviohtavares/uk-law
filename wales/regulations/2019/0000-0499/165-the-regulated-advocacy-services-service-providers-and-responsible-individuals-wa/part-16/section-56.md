# The Regulated Advocacy Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 56

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 56. Offences – responsible individuals[^key-44c30cde7346fb0b9c3949371a8c1b52]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) It is an offence for the responsible individual to fail to comply with a requirement of any of the provisions specified in paragraph (2) [^c23552591].

(2) The provisions specified for the purposes of paragraph (1) are the provisions of regulations 37(1), 38(1), 41(1), 41(2), 43(1), 43(2), 44(1), 44(2), 45(1), 50(4), 51(1), 54(1) and 54(3).
