# The Regulated Advocacy Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 10

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. Duty of candour[^c-176297cce5cb480baa518424327be21a]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must act in an open and transparent way with—

- (a) individuals,
- (b) any representatives of those individuals.
