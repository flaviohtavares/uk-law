# The Regulated Advocacy Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 3

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Requirements in relation to the provision of the service[^c-a072cede67bb0ff0d30ef85ab5c5f0bf]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must ensure that the service is provided with sufficient care, competence and skill, having regard to the statement of purpose.
