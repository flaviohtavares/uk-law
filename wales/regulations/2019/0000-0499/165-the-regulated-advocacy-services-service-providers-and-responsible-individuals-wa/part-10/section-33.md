> Text as at 2023-12-31.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Conflicts of interest[^key-aa416044d8688fa124ce7cdb91a25b90]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must have effective arrangements in place to identify, record and manage conflicts of interest.
