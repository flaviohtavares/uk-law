# The Regulated Advocacy Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 30

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30. Premises[^key-8cba4dae437daecedb1770569d7452f1]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must ensure that the premises used for the operation of the service have adequate facilities for the secure storage of records.
