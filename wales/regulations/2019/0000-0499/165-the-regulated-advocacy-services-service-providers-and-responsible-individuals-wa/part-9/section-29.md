# The Regulated Advocacy Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 29

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29. Overarching requirement[^key-3da33575184f507b77b6f656e3354d2e]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must ensure that the premises, facilities and equipment are suitable for the service, having regard to the statement of purpose for the service.
