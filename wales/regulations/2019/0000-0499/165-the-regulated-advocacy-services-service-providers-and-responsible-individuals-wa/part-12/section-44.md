# The Regulated Advocacy Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 44

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 44. Oversight of adequacy of resources[^key-66a99d3df227362e0f2ee724214debc2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The responsible individual must report to the service provider on the adequacy of the resources available to provide the service in accordance with the requirements on service providers in Parts 3 to 15.

(2) Such reports must be made on a quarterly basis.

(3) But this requirement does not apply where the service provider is an individual.
