# The Regulated Advocacy Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 46

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. Engagement with individuals and others[^key-40f7efd03c699df02ac777ca013235e9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The responsible individual must put suitable arrangements in place for obtaining the views of—

- (a) individuals,
- (b) any representatives of those individuals,
- (c) service commissioners, and
- (d) staff employed at the service,

on the quality of advocacy provided and how this can be improved.

(2) The responsible individual must report the views obtained to the service provider so that these views can be taken into account by the service provider when making any decisions on plans for improvement of the quality of advocacy provided.
