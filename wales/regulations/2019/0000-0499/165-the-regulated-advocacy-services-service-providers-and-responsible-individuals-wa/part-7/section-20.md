# The Regulated Advocacy Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 20

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. Safeguarding - overarching requirement[^key-5f8acf62ba37f2c210f8242f650f6568]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must provide the service in a way which ensures that individuals are safe and are protected from abuse, neglect and improper treatment.
