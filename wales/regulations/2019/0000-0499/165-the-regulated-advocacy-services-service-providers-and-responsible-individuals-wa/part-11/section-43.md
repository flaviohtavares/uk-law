# The Regulated Advocacy Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 43

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43. Visits[^key-d6a842fbd3255e3c3a19743c0f6435eb]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The responsible individual must—

- (a) visit the premises from which the service is provided;
- (b) meet with members of staff who are employed to provide the service in relation to each place in respect of which the responsible individual is designated; and
- (c) meet with individuals for whom advocacy is being provided in relation to each such place.

(2) The frequency of such visits and meetings is to be determined by the responsible individual having regard to the statement of purpose but must be at least every three months.
