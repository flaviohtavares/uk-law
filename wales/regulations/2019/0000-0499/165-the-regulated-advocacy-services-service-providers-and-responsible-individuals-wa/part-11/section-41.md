# The Regulated Advocacy Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 41

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Duty to report appointment of manager to the workforce and service regulators[^c-a82aa5441d65e3d149c57b5fd8451bb2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) On the appointment of a manager in accordance with regulation 37(1), the responsible individual must give notice to SCW and to the service regulator of—

- (a) the name, date of birth and SCW registration number of the person appointed, and
- (b) the date on which the appointment is to take effect.

(2) In a case where the service provider is an individual and the service regulator has agreed to the service provider managing the service, the service provider must give notice to SCW of—

- (a) the name, date of birth and SCW registration number of the service provider, and
- (b) the date from which the service provider is to manage the service.
