# The Regulated Advocacy Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 19

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19. Confidentiality[^key-97c2da8a1f4880ad98a9cbeeb133b0f7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The service provider must respect the individual's rights to confidentiality.

(2) The service provider must have in place a policy about the right of individuals to confidentiality, which also includes the circumstances when an individual's right to confidentiality may be over-ridden, and must operate the service in accordance with the policy.
