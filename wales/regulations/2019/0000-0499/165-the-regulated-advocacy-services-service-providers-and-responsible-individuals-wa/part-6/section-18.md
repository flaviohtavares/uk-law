# The Regulated Advocacy Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 18

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18. Respect and sensitivity[^c-15e9515f59dcc8de7d0b258b97f39b66]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The service provider must ensure that individuals are treated with respect and sensitivity.

(2) This includes, but is not limited to—

- (a) respecting the individual's privacy and dignity;
- (b) promoting the individual's autonomy and independence;
- (c) having regard to any relevant protected characteristics (as defined in section 4 of the Equality Act 2010 [^c23552551]) of the individual.
