# The Smoke Control Areas (Authorised Fuels) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 2

> Text as at 2019-06-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Authorised fuels for the purposes of Part III of the Clean Air Act 1993[^c-1dfd4663260ceedc964cceedd3a0e318]

*Extent: E+W*

Anthracite, semi-anthracite, electricity, gas, low volatile steam coals and the fuels described in the Schedule are declared to be authorised fuels for the purposes of Part III of the Clean Air Act 1993.
