# The Adult Placement Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 65

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 65. Offences – responsible individuals[^c-3589cdb1366f71e59fec6efdfacc789f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) It is an offence for the responsible individual to fail to comply with a requirement of any of the provisions specified in paragraph (2) [^c23552381].

(2) The provisions specified for the purposes of paragraph (1) are the provisions of regulations 46(1), 47(1), 50(1), 50(2), 52(1), 52(2), 53(1), 53(2), 54(1), 59(4), 60(1), 63(1) and 63(3).
