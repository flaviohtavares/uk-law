# The Adult Placement Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 9

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 9. Duty of candour[^c-cf2c3ae3646975ec0738eb2c06771afb]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must act in an open and transparent way with—

- (a) the individual,
- (b) any representative of the individual,
- (c) the adult placement carer, and
- (d) the service commissioner.
