# The Adult Placement Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 30

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30. Compliance with employer's code of practice[^key-55589f7f13ad383f3bd1d0f783d94002]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must adhere to the code of practice on the standards of conduct and practice expected of persons employing or seeking to employ social care workers [^c23552361], which is published by SCW under section 112(1)(b) of the Act.
