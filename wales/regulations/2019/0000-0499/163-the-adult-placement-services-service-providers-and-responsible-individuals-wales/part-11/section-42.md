# The Adult Placement Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 42

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 42. Conflicts of interest[^key-5274635673102354a5bbd85d9d292ba1]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must have effective arrangements in place to identify, record and manage potential conflicts of interest.
