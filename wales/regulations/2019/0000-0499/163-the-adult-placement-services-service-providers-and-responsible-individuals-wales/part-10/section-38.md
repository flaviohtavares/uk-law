# The Adult Placement Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 38

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 38. Adequacy of premises[^key-b8f25d28242f3bfc98f5744d70c71fe4]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must ensure that the premises used for the operation of the service have adequate facilities for—

- (a) the supervision of staff;
- (b) the secure storage of records.
