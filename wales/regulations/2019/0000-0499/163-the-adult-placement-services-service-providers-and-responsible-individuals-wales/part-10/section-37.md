# The Adult Placement Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 37

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Overarching requirement[^key-8cfce6393c37147ee211175348c728ae]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must ensure that the premises, facilities and equipment are suitable for the service, having regard to the statement of purpose for the service.
