# The Adult Placement Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 21

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21. Safeguarding – overarching requirement[^c-1c3093f515a0fe42ee6bf790a890e938]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must put arrangements in place to ensure that individuals are safe and are protected from abuse, neglect and improper treatment.
