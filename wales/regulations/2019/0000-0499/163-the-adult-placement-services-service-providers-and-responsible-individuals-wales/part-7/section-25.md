# The Adult Placement Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 25

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. Deprivation of liberty[^key-a7ea88bf328853fd6cc1035804775faa]

*Extent: E+W*

An individual must not be deprived of his or her liberty for the purpose of receiving care and support without lawful authority.
