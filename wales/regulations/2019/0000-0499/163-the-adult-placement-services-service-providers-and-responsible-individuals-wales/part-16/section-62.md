# The Adult Placement Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 62

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 62. Duty of candour[^key-9a3e6949cbb0d428ba0340a515cf7f26]

*Extent: E+W*

The responsible individual must act in an open and transparent way with—

- (a) the individual;
- (b) any representative of the individual;
- (c) the adult placement carer;
- (d) the service commissioner.
