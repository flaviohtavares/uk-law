# The Adult Placement Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 61

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 61. Support for staff raising concerns[^c-7db217a7d7fe9ae0b7080344317d6a17]

*Extent: E+W*

The responsible individual must ensure that the service provider's whistleblowing policy is being complied with and that the arrangements to enable and support people working at the service to raise such concerns are being operated effectively.
