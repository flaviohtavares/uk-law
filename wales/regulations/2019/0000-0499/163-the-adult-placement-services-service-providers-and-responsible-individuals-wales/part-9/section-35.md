# The Adult Placement Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 35

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 35. Support and information for adult placement carers[^c-42aefd56f3de7f87225c9e7f56a412e3]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must put arrangements in place to ensure that an adult placement carer has the information they need to provide care and support to individuals to achieve their personal outcomes.
