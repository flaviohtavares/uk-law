# The Adult Placement Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 34

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 34. Effective relationships[^key-be47ac769a091cd65a25a4f070f64600]

*Extent: E+W*

The service provider must—

- (a) maintain good professional relationships with an adult placement carer; and
- (b) encourage and assist an adult placement carer to maintain good personal relationships with individuals.
