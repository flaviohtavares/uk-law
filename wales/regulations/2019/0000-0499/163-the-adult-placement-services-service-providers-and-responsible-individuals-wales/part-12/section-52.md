# The Adult Placement Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 52

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52. Visits[^key-7ef86254de3b9315c8e435bd49aa1e89]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The responsible individual must—

- (a) visit the premises from which the service is provided;
- (b) meet with members of staff who are employed to provide the service in relation to each place in respect of which the responsible individual is designated, and
- (c) meet with individuals for whom the service is being provided and their adult placement carer.

(2) The frequency of such visits and meetings is to be determined by the responsible individual having regard to the statement of purpose but must take place at least every three months.
