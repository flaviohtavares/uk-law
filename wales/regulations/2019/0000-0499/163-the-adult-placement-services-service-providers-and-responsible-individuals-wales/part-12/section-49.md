# The Adult Placement Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 49

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Duty to report the appointment of manager to the service provider[^c-82834c1161f0c0867818fb31441ab74c]

*Extent: E+W*

On the appointment of a manager in accordance with regulation 46(1), the responsible individual must give notice to the service provider of—

- (a) the name of the person appointed, and
- (b) the date on which the appointment is to take effect.
