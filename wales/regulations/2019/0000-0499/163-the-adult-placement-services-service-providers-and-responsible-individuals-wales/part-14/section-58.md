# The Adult Placement Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 58

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Duty to ensure policies and procedures are up to date[^c-8a192470a6408e885ce866ef58f47e9e]

*Extent: E+W*

The responsible individual must put suitable arrangements in place to ensure that the service provider's policies and procedures are kept up to date, having regard to the statement of purpose.
