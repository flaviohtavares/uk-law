# The Adult Placement Services (Service Providers and Responsible Individuals) (Wales) Regulations 2019, section 57

> Text as at 2023-12-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 57. Duty to ensure there are systems in place for keeping of records[^key-f909773a33f100001d017fd49c33053b]

*Extent: E+W*

The responsible individual must ensure that there are effective systems in place in relation to the keeping of records, which include systems for ensuring the accuracy and completeness of records which are required to be kept by regulation 40.
