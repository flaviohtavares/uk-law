# The Equality Act 2010 (Commencement No. 14) (Wales) Order 2019

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

2019 No. 1469 (W. 259) (C. 52)

Equality, Wales

The Equality Act 2010 (Commencement No. 14) (Wales) Order 2019

Made

19 November 2019

The Welsh Ministers make the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on them by section 216(6)(b) of the Equality Act 2010[^f00001].

## 1. Title

The title of this Order is the Equality Act 2010 (Commencement No. 14) (Wales) Order 2019.

## 2. Provision coming into force

Section 2 (power to amend section 1) of the Equality Act 2010, in so far as it confers a power on the Welsh Ministers, comes into force on 22 November 2019.

Jane Hutt

Deputy Minister and Chief Whip, under authority of the Welsh Ministers

19 November 2019

EXPLANATORY NOTE

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order commences section 2 (power to amend section 1) of the Equality Act 2010 (“the Act”), in so far as it confers a power on the Welsh Ministers.
