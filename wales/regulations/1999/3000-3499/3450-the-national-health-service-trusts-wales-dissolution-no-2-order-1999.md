# The National Health Service Trusts (Wales) (Dissolution No. 2) Order 1999

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

1999 No. 3450 (W. 48)

NATIONAL HEALTH SERVICE, WALES

The National Health Service Trusts (Wales) (Dissolution No. 2) Order 1999

Made

15th December 1999

Coming into force

1st April 2000

The National Assembly for Wales makes the following Order in exercise of the powers conferred on the Secretary of State for Wales by Section 126(3) of the National Health Service Act 1977[^f00001] and section 5(1), and paragraph 29(1) of Schedule 2 to the National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990[^f00002] and of all other powers enabling the Secretary of State in that behalf and now vested in the National Assembly for Wales[^f00003], considering it appropriate in the interests of the health service to make this Order[^f00004], and after completion of the consultation prescribed under paragraph 29(3) of Schedule 2 to the latter Act[^f00005]:

## 1. Citation and commencement

This Order may be cited as the National Health Service Trusts (Wales) (Dissolution No. 2) Order 1999 and shall come into force on 1st April 2000.

## 2. Dissolution of NHS Trusts

(1) The University Hospital of Wales and Llandough Hospital National Health Service Trust and the Cardiff and District Community National Health Service Trust are hereby dissolved.

(2) The Orders specified in column (1) of the Schedule to this Order are hereby revoked.

Signed on behalf of the National Assembly for Wales under section 66(1) of the Government of Wales Act 1998.[^f00006]

Dafydd Elis Thomas

Presiding Officer

15th December1999

## SCHEDULE — ORDERS REVOKED

Article2(2)

- The University Hospital of Wales and Llandough Hospital National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999 ([S.I. 1999/1119](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1119))
- The Cardiff and District Community National Health Service Trust (Establishment) Order 1999 ([S.I. 1999/1118](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/1118))

(This note is not part of the Order)

This Order provides for the dissolution of the University Hospital of Wales and Llandough Hospital NHS Trust and the Cardiff and District Community NHS Trust and for the revocation of their respective establishment orders.
