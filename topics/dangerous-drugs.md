# Dangerous Drugs

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **DANGEROUS DRUGS**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Misuse of Drugs Act 1971](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1971/38-misuse-of-drugs-act-1971.md) — 183 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Drugs Act 2005](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2005/17-drugs-act-2005.md) *(changes pending)* — 5 regulations on this topic made under it
