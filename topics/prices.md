# Prices

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **PRICES**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Prices Act 1974](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1974/24-prices-act-1974.md) — 15 regulations on this topic made under it
