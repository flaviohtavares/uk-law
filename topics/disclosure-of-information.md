# Disclosure of Information

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **DISCLOSURE OF INFORMATION**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Digital Economy Act 2017](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2017/30-digital-economy-act-2017/README.md) — 12 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Serious Crime Act 2007](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2007/27-serious-crime-act-2007/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 5 regulations on this topic made under it
