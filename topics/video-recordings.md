# Video Recordings

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **VIDEO RECORDINGS**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Video Recordings Act 1984](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1984/39-video-recordings-act-1984.md) — 17 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Digital Economy Act 2010](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2010/24-digital-economy-act-2010/README.md) — 3 regulations on this topic made under it
