# Antarctica

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **ANTARCTICA**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Antarctic Act 1994](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1994/15-antarctic-act-1994.md) *(changes pending)* — 35 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Antarctic Treaty Act 1967](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1967/65-antarctic-treaty-act-1967.md) — 12 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Antarctic Act 2013](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2013/15-antarctic-act-2013.md) — 5 regulations on this topic made under it
