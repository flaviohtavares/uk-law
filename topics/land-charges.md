# Land Charges

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **LAND CHARGES**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Local Land Charges Act 1975](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1975/76-local-land-charges-act-1975.md) *(changes pending)* — 14 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Land Charges Act 1972](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1972/61-land-charges-act-1972.md) — 11 regulations on this topic made under it
