# Land and Buildings Transaction Tax

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **LAND AND BUILDINGS TRANSACTION TAX**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Land and Buildings Transaction Tax (Scotland) Act 2013](../scotland/acts/2013/11-land-and-buildings-transaction-tax-scotland-act-2013/README.md) — 26 regulations on this topic made under it
