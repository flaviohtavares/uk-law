# Overseas Territories

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **OVERSEAS TERRITORIES**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Saint Helena Act 1833](../uk-parliament/acts/public/will4-3-4/85-saint-helena-act-1833.md) — 33 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Administration of Justice Act 1956](../uk-parliament/acts/public/eliz2-4-5/46-administration-of-justice-act-1956.md) — 6 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Colonial Courts of Admiralty Act 1890](../uk-parliament/acts/public/vict-53-54/27-colonial-courts-of-admiralty-act-1890.md) — 3 regulations on this topic made under it
