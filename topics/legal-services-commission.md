# Legal Services Commission

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **LEGAL SERVICES COMMISSION**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Access to Justice Act 1999](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1999/22-access-to-justice-act-1999/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 124 regulations on this topic made under it
