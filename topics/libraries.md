# Libraries

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **LIBRARIES**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Public Lending Right Act 1979](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1979/10-public-lending-right-act-1979.md) — 51 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Public Libraries and Museums Act 1964](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1964/75-public-libraries-and-museums-act-1964.md) — 3 regulations on this topic made under it
