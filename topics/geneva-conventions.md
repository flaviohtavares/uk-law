# Geneva Conventions

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **GENEVA CONVENTIONS**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Geneva Conventions Act 1957](../uk-parliament/acts/public/eliz2-5-6/52-geneva-conventions-act-1957/README.md) — 12 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Geneva Conventions (Amendment) Act 1995](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1995/27-geneva-conventions-amendment-act-1995/README.md) — 6 regulations on this topic made under it
