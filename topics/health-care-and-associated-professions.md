# Health Care and Associated Professions

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **HEALTH CARE AND ASSOCIATED PROFESSIONS**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Medical Act 1983](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1983/54-medical-act-1983/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 63 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Health Act 1999](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1999/8-health-act-1999/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 38 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Dentists Act 1984](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1984/24-dentists-act-1984/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 23 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Opticians Act 1989](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1989/44-opticians-act-1989/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 17 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Chiropractors Act 1994](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1994/17-chiropractors-act-1994/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 9 regulations on this topic made under it
- [National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2002/17-national-health-service-reform-and-health-care-professions-act-2002/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 8 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Health (Wales) Act 2003](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2003/4-health-wales-act-2003.md) — 3 regulations on this topic made under it
