# Professions Supplementary to Medicine

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **PROFESSIONS SUPPLEMENTARY TO MEDICINE**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Professions Supplementary to Medicine Act 1960](../uk-parliament/acts/public/eliz2-8-9/66-professions-supplementary-to-medicine-act-1960.md) — 12 regulations on this topic made under it
