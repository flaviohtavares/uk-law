# National Debt

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **NATIONAL DEBT**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [National Debt Act 1972](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1972/65-national-debt-act-1972.md) — 60 regulations on this topic made under it
- [National Loans Act 1968](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1968/13-national-loans-act-1968.md) — 7 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Finance Act 1942](../uk-parliament/acts/public/geo6-5-6/21-finance-act-1942.md) — 6 regulations on this topic made under it
