# Retained EU Law Reform

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **RETAINED EU LAW REFORM**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Retained EU Law (Revocation and Reform) Act 2023](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2023/28-retained-eu-law-revocation-and-reform-act-2023.md) — 75 regulations on this topic made under it
