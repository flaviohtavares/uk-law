# Control of Fuel and Electricity

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **CONTROL OF FUEL AND ELECTRICITY**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Energy Act 1976](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1976/76-energy-act-1976.md) — 21 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Fuel and Electricity (Control) Act 1973](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1973/67-fuel-and-electricity-control-act-1973.md) — 7 regulations on this topic made under it
