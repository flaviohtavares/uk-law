# Countryside

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **COUNTRYSIDE**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2000/37-countryside-and-rights-of-way-act-2000/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 120 regulations on this topic made under it
- [National Parks (Scotland) Act 2000](../scotland/acts/2000/10-national-parks-scotland-act-2000/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 8 regulations on this topic made under it
- [National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949](../uk-parliament/acts/public/geo6-12-13-14/97-national-parks-and-access-to-the-countryside-act-1949/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 4 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Nature Conservancy Council Act 1973](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1973/54-nature-conservancy-council-act-1973.md) — 3 regulations on this topic made under it
