# Freedom of Information

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **FREEDOM OF INFORMATION**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Freedom of Information Act 2000](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2000/36-freedom-of-information-act-2000/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 35 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Freedom of Information (Scotland) Act 2002](../scotland/acts/2002/13-freedom-of-information-scotland-act-2002/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 16 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Data Protection Act 1998](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1998/29-data-protection-act-1998/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 5 regulations on this topic made under it
