# International Immunities and Privileges

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **INTERNATIONAL IMMUNITIES AND PRIVILEGES**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [International Organisations Act 1968](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1968/48-international-organisations-act-1968.md) — 90 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Arms Control and Disarmament (Privileges and Immunities) Act 1988](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1988/2-arms-control-and-disarmament-privileges-and-immunities-act-1988.md) — 9 regulations on this topic made under it
