# Overseas Development and Co-Operation

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **OVERSEAS DEVELOPMENT AND CO-OPERATION**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Commonwealth Development Corporation Act 1978](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1978/2-commonwealth-development-corporation-act-1978.md) — 4 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency Act 1988](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1988/8-multilateral-investment-guarantee-agency-act-1988.md) — 3 regulations on this topic made under it
