# Nuclear Safeguards

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **NUCLEAR SAFEGUARDS**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Nuclear Safeguards Act 2000](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2000/5-nuclear-safeguards-act-2000.md) — 5 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Nuclear Safeguards Act 2018](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2018/15-nuclear-safeguards-act-2018.md) — 3 regulations on this topic made under it
