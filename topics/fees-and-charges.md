# Fees and Charges

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **FEES AND CHARGES**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Finance Act 1973](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1973/51-finance-act-1973.md) — 79 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Medicines Act 1971](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1971/69-medicines-act-1971.md) — 12 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Finance (No. 2) Act 1987](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1987/51-finance-no-2-act-1987.md) — 7 regulations on this topic made under it
