# Trustees

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **TRUSTEES**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Public Trustee Act 1906](../uk-parliament/acts/public/edw7-6/55-public-trustee-act-1906.md) — 24 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Trustee Investments Act 1961](../uk-parliament/acts/public/eliz2-9-10/62-trustee-investments-act-1961.md) *(changes pending)* — 21 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Public Trustee (Fees) Act 1957](../uk-parliament/acts/public/eliz2-5-6/12-public-trustee-fees-act-1957.md) — 8 regulations on this topic made under it
