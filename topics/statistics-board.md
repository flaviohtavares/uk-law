# Statistics Board

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **STATISTICS BOARD**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Statistics and Registration Service Act 2007](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2007/18-statistics-and-registration-service-act-2007/README.md) — 9 regulations on this topic made under it
