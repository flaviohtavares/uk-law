# Public Service Pensions

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **PUBLIC SERVICE PENSIONS**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Public Service Pensions Act 2013](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2013/25-public-service-pensions-act-2013/README.md) — 191 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Public Service Pensions and Judicial Offices Act 2022](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2022/7-public-service-pensions-and-judicial-offices-act-2022/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 41 regulations on this topic made under it
