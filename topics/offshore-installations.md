# Offshore Installations

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **OFFSHORE INSTALLATIONS**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Petroleum Act 1987](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1987/12-petroleum-act-1987.md) — 132 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Oil and Gas (Enterprise) Act 1982](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1982/23-oil-and-gas-enterprise-act-1982.md) *(changes pending)* — 75 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Mineral Workings (Offshore Installations) Act 1971](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1971/61-mineral-workings-offshore-installations-act-1971.md) — 11 regulations on this topic made under it
