# Public Order

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **PUBLIC ORDER**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Public Order Act 2023](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2023/15-public-order-act-2023.md) *(changes pending)* — 7 regulations on this topic made under it
