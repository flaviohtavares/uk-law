# Medicines

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **MEDICINES**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Medicines Act 1968](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1968/67-medicines-act-1968/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 313 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Medicines Act 1971](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1971/69-medicines-act-1971.md) — 32 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Medicines and Medical Devices Act 2021](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2021/3-medicines-and-medical-devices-act-2021.md) — 16 regulations on this topic made under it
