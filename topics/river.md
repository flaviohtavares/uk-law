# River

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **RIVER**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Salmon Act 1986](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1986/62-salmon-act-1986.md) — 55 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries (Consolidation) (Scotland) Act 2003](../scotland/acts/2003/15-salmon-and-freshwater-fisheries-consolidation-scotland-act-2003/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 25 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Import of Live Fish (England and Wales) Act 1980](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1980/27-import-of-live-fish-england-and-wales-act-1980.md) — 7 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries Act 1975](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1975/51-salmon-and-freshwater-fisheries-act-1975/README.md) — 6 regulations on this topic made under it
