# Senior Courts of

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **SENIOR COURTS OF**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Civil Procedure Act 1997](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1997/12-civil-procedure-act-1997.md) *(changes pending)* — 59 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Senior Courts Act 1981](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1981/54-senior-courts-act-1981/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 28 regulations on this topic made under it
