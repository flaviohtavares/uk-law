# Patents

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **PATENTS**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Patents Act 1977](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1977/37-patents-act-1977/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 67 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Patents Act 1949](../uk-parliament/acts/public/geo6-12-13-14/87-patents-act-1949.md) — 6 regulations on this topic made under it
