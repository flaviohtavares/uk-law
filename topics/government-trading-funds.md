# Government Trading Funds

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **GOVERNMENT TRADING FUNDS**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Government Trading Funds Act 1973](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1973/63-government-trading-funds-act-1973.md) — 74 regulations on this topic made under it
