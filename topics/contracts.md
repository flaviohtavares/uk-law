# Contracts

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **CONTRACTS**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1998/20-late-payment-of-commercial-debts-interest-act-1998.md) — 12 regulations on this topic made under it
