# Land Drainage

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **LAND DRAINAGE**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Land Drainage Act 1991](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1991/59-land-drainage-act-1991/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 107 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Land Drainage Act 1976](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1976/70-land-drainage-act-1976.md) — 21 regulations on this topic made under it
