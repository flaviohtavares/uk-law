# Open Spaces

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **OPEN SPACES**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Parks Regulation (Amendment) Act 1926](../uk-parliament/acts/public/geo5-16-17/36-parks-regulation-amendment-act-1926.md) — 14 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Crown Estate Act 1961](../uk-parliament/acts/public/eliz2-9-10/55-crown-estate-act-1961.md) — 4 regulations on this topic made under it
