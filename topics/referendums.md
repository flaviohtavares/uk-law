# Referendums

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **REFERENDUMS**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [European Union Referendum Act 2015](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2015/36-european-union-referendum-act-2015/README.md) — 6 regulations on this topic made under it
