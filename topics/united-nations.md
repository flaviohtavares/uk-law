# United Nations

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **UNITED NATIONS**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [United Nations Act 1946](../uk-parliament/acts/public/geo6-9-10/45-united-nations-act-1946.md) — 208 regulations on this topic made under it
