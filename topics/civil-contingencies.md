# Civil Contingencies

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **CIVIL CONTINGENCIES**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Civil Contingencies Act 2004](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2004/36-civil-contingencies-act-2004/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 13 regulations on this topic made under it
