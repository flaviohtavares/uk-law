# Securities

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **SECURITIES**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Stock Transfer Act 1982](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1982/41-stock-transfer-act-1982.md) *(changes pending)* — 14 regulations on this topic made under it
