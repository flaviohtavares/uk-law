# New Towns

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **NEW TOWNS**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [New Towns (Scotland) Act 1968](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1968/16-new-towns-scotland-act-1968/README.md) — 19 regulations on this topic made under it
- [New Towns Act 1981](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1981/64-new-towns-act-1981/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 11 regulations on this topic made under it
