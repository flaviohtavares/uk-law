# Registration of Births, Deaths, Marriages, etc

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **REGISTRATION OF BIRTHS, DEATHS, MARRIAGES, ETC**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)
