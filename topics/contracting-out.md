# Contracting Out

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **CONTRACTING OUT**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Deregulation and Contracting Out Act 1994](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1994/40-deregulation-and-contracting-out-act-1994/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 46 regulations on this topic made under it
