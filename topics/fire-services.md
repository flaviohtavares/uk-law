# Fire Services

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **FIRE SERVICES**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Fire Services Act 1947](../uk-parliament/acts/public/geo6-10-11/41-fire-services-act-1947.md) — 81 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Fire Services Act 1959](../uk-parliament/acts/public/eliz2-7-8/44-fire-services-act-1959.md) — 27 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Statutory Instruments Act 1946](../uk-parliament/acts/public/geo6-9-10/36-statutory-instruments-act-1946.md) *(changes pending)* — 25 regulations on this topic made under it
