# Maintenance of Dependants

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **MAINTENANCE OF DEPENDANTS**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1972](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1972/18-maintenance-orders-reciprocal-enforcement-act-1972.md) — 28 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Maintenance Orders Act 1958](../uk-parliament/acts/public/eliz2-6-7/39-maintenance-orders-act-1958.md) *(changes pending)* — 5 regulations on this topic made under it
