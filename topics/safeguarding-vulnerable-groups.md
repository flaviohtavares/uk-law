# Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **SAFEGUARDING VULNERABLE GROUPS**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts
