# Cinemas and Films

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **CINEMAS AND FILMS**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Films Act 1985](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1985/21-films-act-1985.md) *(changes pending)* — 35 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Cinemas Act 1985](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1985/13-cinemas-act-1985.md) — 3 regulations on this topic made under it
