# Museums and Galleries

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **MUSEUMS AND GALLERIES**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [National Heritage Act 1983](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1983/47-national-heritage-act-1983/README.md) — 8 regulations on this topic made under it
- [British Museum Act 1963](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1963/24-british-museum-act-1963.md) — 7 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Museums and Galleries Act 1992](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1992/44-museums-and-galleries-act-1992.md) — 4 regulations on this topic made under it
