# Ministers of the Crown

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **MINISTERS OF THE CROWN**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Ministers of the Crown Act 1975](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1975/26-ministers-of-the-crown-act-1975.md) — 125 regulations on this topic made under it
