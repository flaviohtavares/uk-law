# Data Protection

Laws in force under the legislation.gov.uk subject heading **DATA PROTECTION**. Repealed laws are left out. *Changes pending* means changes not yet applied to the text; *as originally made* means the text may have changed since. [All topics](README.md)

## Acts

Acts and Orders in Council with this heading, or under which most of the regulations made carry it.

- [Data Protection Act 2018](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2018/12-data-protection-act-2018/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 17 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Data Protection Act 1998](../uk-parliament/acts/public/1998/29-data-protection-act-1998/README.md) *(changes pending)* — 12 regulations on this topic made under it
- [Data (Use and Access) Act 2025](../uk-parliament/acts/public/2025/18-data-use-and-access-act-2025/README.md) — 7 regulations on this topic made under it
