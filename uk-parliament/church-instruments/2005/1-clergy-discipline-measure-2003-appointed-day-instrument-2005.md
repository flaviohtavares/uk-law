# Clergy Discipline Measure 2003 (Appointed Day Instrument 2005)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2005 No. 1

Clergy Discipline Measure 2003 (Appointed Day Instrument 2005)

**Whereas** the Clergy Discipline Measure received the Royal Assent on the tenth day of July 2003

**And Whereas** by section 48(2) of the said Measure it is provided that the said Measure shall come into force on such date as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York may jointly appoint and that different dates may be appointed for different provisions

**And Whereas** by an instrument dated the eighteenth day of September 2003 under my hand and that of the Most Reverend David Michael Hope formerly Archbishop of York we did jointly appoint that certain provisions of the said Measure should come into force on the first day of October 2003 but no other provisions of the said Measure have yet been brought into force

**And Whereas** the See of York has been vacant since the twenty–eighth day of February 2005 by reason of the resignation of the said David Michael Hope

**Now therefore** **I ROWAN DOUGLAS** by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury do hereby appoint that the following provisions of the said Measure shall come into force on the 1st day of June 2005:

Subsections (3) and (4) of Section 449
