# Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 2005 (Appointed Day Instrument 2005)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2005 No. 2

Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 2005 (Appointed Day Instrument 2005)

**Whereas** the Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure received the Royal Assent on the twenty–fourth day of March 2005

**And Whereas** by section 11(2) of the said Measure it is provided that the said Measure shall come into force on such date as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York may jointly appoint and that different dates may be appointed for different provisions

**And Whereas** the See of York has been vacant since the twenty–eighth day of February 2005 by reason of the resignation of the said David Michael Hope

**Now therefore** **I ROWAN DOUGLAS** by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury do hereby appoint that:

- all the provisions of the said Measure other than section 9 shall come into force on the first day of June 2005; and
- the said section 9 shall come into force on the first day of September 2005.
