# National Institutions Measure 1998 (Appointed Day Instrument 1998)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

1998 No.1

National Institutions Measure 1998 (Appointed Day Instrument 1998)

**Whereas** the National Institutions Measure 1998 received the Royal Assent on the second day July 1998

**And Whereas** by section 15 of the said Measure it is provided that the Measure shall come into force on such date as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York may jointly appoint and that different dates may be appointed for different provisions

**Now therefore we GEORGE LEONARD** by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury and **DAVID MICHAEL** by Divine Providence Archbishop of York do hereby jointly appoint that all the provisions of the said Measure shall come into force on the first day of January 1999.

Dated this fourteenth day of October One thousand nine hundred and ninety-eight
