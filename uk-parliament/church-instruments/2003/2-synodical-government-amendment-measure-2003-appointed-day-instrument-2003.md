# Synodical Government (Amendment) Measure 2003 (Appointed Day Instrument 2003)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2003 No. 2

Synodical Government (Amendment) Measure 2003 (Appointed Day Instrument 2003)

**Whereas** the Synodical Government (Amendment) Measure received the Royal Assent on the sixth day of March 2003

**And Whereas** by section 4(2) of the said Measure it is provided that the said Measure shall come into force on such day as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York may jointly appoint

**Now therefore** we **ROWAN DOUGLAS** by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury and **DAVID MICHAEL** by Divine Providence Archbishop of York do hereby jointly appoint that the whole of the said Measure shall come into force on the first day of January 2004
