# Amending Canon No. 34 (Appointed Day No. 1) Instrument 2016

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2016 No. 1

Amending Canon No. 34 (Appointed Day No. 1) Instrument 2016

*Made*

*14th April 2016*

*Coming into force*

*16th May 2016*

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by paragraph 6 of Amending Canon No. 34, make the following instrument:

## 1. Citation

This instrument may be cited as Amending Canon No. 34 (Appointed Day No. 1) Instrument 2016.

## 2. Appointed day

Paragraph 3 of Amending Canon No. 34, and the Canon C 30 inserted by it, shall come into force on 16th May 2016 so far as necessary for enabling the House of Bishops to make, amend or revoke Regulations under that Canon.

*+ Justin Cantuar*

Archbishop of Canterbury

*+ Sentamu Ebor*

Archbishop of York

14th April 2016

*(This note is not part of the Order)*

This instrument brings into force provisions in paragraph 3 of Amending Canon No. 34 that will enable the House of Bishops with effect from 16th May 2016 to make, amend or revoke regulations about diocesan safeguarding advisors and the carrying out of risk assessments.
