# The Mission and Pastoral Measure 2011(Appointed Day) Instrument 2012

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2012 No. 1

The Mission and Pastoral Measure 2011(Appointed Day) Instrument 2012

*Made*

*2nd May 2012*

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 112(3) of the Mission and Pastoral Measure 2011 [^c2128007], make the following Instrument:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

1 This instrument may be cited as the Mission and Pastoral Measure 2011 (Appointed Day) Instrument 2012.

This Instrument may be cited as the Ecclesiastical Fees (Amendment) Measure 2011 (Appointed Day No.1) Instrument 2011.

2 In this Instrument "the Measure" means the Mission and Pastoral Measure 2011.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of the Measure is 1st July 2012.

*+ Rowan Cantuar*

Archbishop of Canterbury

*+ Sentamu Ebor*

Archbishop of York

2nd May 2012

*(This note is not part of the Instrument)*

This Instrument brings into force, on 1st July 2012, the provisions of the Mission and Pastoral Measure 2011.
