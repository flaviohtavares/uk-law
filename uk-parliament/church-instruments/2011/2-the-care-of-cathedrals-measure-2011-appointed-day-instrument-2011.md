# The Care of Cathedrals Measure 2011 (Appointed Day) Instrument 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2011 No. 2

The Care of Cathedrals Measure 2011 (Appointed Day) Instrument 2011

*Made*

*12th July 2011*

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 34(2) of the Care of Cathedrals Measure 2011 [^c2094736], make the following Instrument:

## 1. Citation

This Instrument may be cited as the Care of Cathedrals Measure 2011(Appointed Day ) Instrument 2011.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Care of Cathedrals Measure 2011 is 1st September 2011.

*+ Rowan Cantuar:*

Archbishop of Canterbury

*+ Sentamu Ebor:*

Archbishop of York

12th July 2011

*(This note is not part of the Instrument)*

This Instrument brings into force, on 1st September 2011, the whole of the Care of Cathedrals Measure 2011. The Measure consolidates, with corrections and minor improvements, the Care of Cathedrals Measure 1990, the Care of Cathedrals (Supplementary Provisions) Measure 1994 and the Care of Cathedrals (Amendment ) Measure 2005, and related enactments.
