# The Ecclesiastical Fees (Amendment) Measure 2011 (Appointed Day No.1) Instrument 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2011 No. 1

The Ecclesiastical Fees (Amendment) Measure 2011 (Appointed Day No.1) Instrument 2011

*Made*

*7th June 2011*

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(2) of the Ecclesiastical Fees (Amendment) Measure 2011 [^c2088943], make the following Instrument:

## 1. Citation

This Instrument may be cited as the Ecclesiastical Fees (Amendment) Measure 2011 (Appointed Day No.1) Instrument 2011.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Ecclesiastical Fees (Amendment) Measure 2011 is 1st July 2011—

- Part 1
- Section 4
- Section 5(2) and (3)
- Section 6
- Schedule 1
- Schedule 2.

*+ Rowan Cantuar*

Archbishop of Canterbury

*+ Sentamu Ebor*

Archbishop of York

7th June 2011

*(This note is not part of the Instrument)*

This Instrument brings into force Part 1 and secton 4, 5(2) and (3), and 6 of, and Schedules 1 and 2 to, the Ecclesiastical Fees (Amendment) Measure 2011 on 1st July 2011.
