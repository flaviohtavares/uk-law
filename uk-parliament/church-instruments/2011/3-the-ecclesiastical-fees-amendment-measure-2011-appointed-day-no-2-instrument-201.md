# The Ecclesiastical Fees (Amendment) Measure 2011 (Appointed Day No. 2) Instrument 2011

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2011 No. 3

The Ecclesiastical Fees (Amendment) Measure 2011 (Appointed Day No. 2) Instrument 2011

*Made*

*29th September 2011*

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 6(2) of the Ecclesiastical Fees (Amendment) Measure 2011 [^c2106807], make the following Instrument:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This Instrument may be cited as the Ecclesiastical Fees (Amendment) Measure 2011 (Appointed Day No. 2) Instrument 2011.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of those provisions of the Ecclesiastical Fees (Amendment) Measure 2011 which have not previously been brought into force is 1st October 2011.

*+ Rowan Cantuar*

Archbishop of Canterbury

*+ Sentamu Ebor*

Archbishop of York

29th September 2011

*(This note is not part of the Instrument)*

This Instrument brings into force, on 1st October 2011, the remaining provisions of the Ecclesiastical Fees (Amendment) Measure 2011.
