# Pensions Measure 1997 (Appointed Day Instrument 1997)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

1997 No.1

Pensions Measure 1997 (Appointed Day Instrument 1997)

**Whereas** the Pensions Measure 1997 received the Royal Assent on the twenty first day of March 1997

**And Whereas** by section 11(2) of the said Measure it is provided that the said Measure shall come into force on such date as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York may jointly appoint

**Now therefore we GEORGE LEONARD** by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury and **DAVID MICHAEL** by Divine Providence Archbishop of York do hereby jointly appoint that the said Measure shall come into force on the first day of January 1998.
