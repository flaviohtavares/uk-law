# Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 1992 (Appointed Day Instrument 1992)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

1992 No.2

Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 1992 (Appointed Day Instrument 1992)

**Whereas** the Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure received the Royal Assent on the sixth day of March 1992

**And Whereas** by section19(2) of the said Measure it is provided that the Measure shall come into force on such date as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York may jointly appoint and that different dates may be appointed for different provisions

**And Whereas** by an Instrument under Our hands dated the twenty-seventh day of May 1992 we did appoint that certain provisions of the said Measure should come into force on the first day of June 1992 but no other provisions of the said Measure have been brought into force by Us

**Now therefore** we GEORGE LEONARD by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury and JOHN STAPYLTON by Divine Providence Archbishop of York do hereby jointly appoint that the following provisions of the said Measure shall come into force on the eleventh day of July 1992:-

Sections 2, 3 and 15

Schedule 3 paragraphs 1, 5 and 12

Schedule 4 Part II - the item relating to section 11 of the Cremation Act 1902

Dated this eleventh day of July One thousand nine hundred and ninety two
