# Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 1992 (Appointed Day Instrument 1992)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

1992 No.3

Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 1992 (Appointed Day Instrument 1992)

**Whereas** the Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure received the Royal Assent on the sixth day of March 1992

**And Whereas** by section 19(2) of the said Measure it is provided that the Measure shall come into force on such date as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York may jointly appoint and that different dates may be appointed for different provisions

**And Whereas** by an Instrument under Our hands dated the twenty-seventh day of May 1992 we did appoint that certain provisions of the said Measure should come into force on the first day of June 1992 and by a further Instrument under Our hands dated the eleventh day of July 1992 we did appoint that further provisions of the said Measure should come into force on the said eleventh day of July 1992 but the remaining provisions of the said Measure have not been brought into force by Us

**Now therefore** we GEORGE LEONARD by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury and JOHN STAPYLTON by Divine Providence Archbishop of York do hereby jointly appoint that the remaining provisions of the said Measure, that is to say:-

Section 4 and Schedule 1

shall come into force on the first day of January 1993

Dated this seventh day of September One thousand nine hundred and ninety two
