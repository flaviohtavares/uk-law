# Churchwardens Measure 2001 (Appointed Day Instrument 2001)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2001 No. 2

Churchwardens Measure 2001 (Appointed Day Instrument 2001)

**Whereas** the Churchwardens Measure 2001 received the Royal Assent on the Tenth day of April 2001

**And whereas** section 14(2) of the said Measure provides that the said Measure shall come into force on such day as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York may jointly appoint

**Now therefore** we **GEORGE LEONARD** by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury and **DAVID MICHAEL** by Divine Providence Archbishop of York do hereby jointly appoint that the said Measure shall come into force on the First day of January 2002
