# Care of Places of Worship Measure 1999 (Appointed Day Instrument 2001)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2001 No.1

Care of Places of Worship Measure 1999 (Appointed Day Instrument 2001)

**Whereas** the Care of Places of Worship Measure recieved the Royal Assent on the thirtieth day of June 1999

**And Whereas** by section 7(2) of the said Measure it is provided that the said Measure shall come into force on such date as the Archhbishops of Cantebury and York may jointly appoint

**Now therefore we GEORGE LEONARD** by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury and **DAVID MICHAEL** by Divine Providence Archbishop of York do hereby jointly appoint that the whole of the said Measure shall come into force on the first day of July 2001.
