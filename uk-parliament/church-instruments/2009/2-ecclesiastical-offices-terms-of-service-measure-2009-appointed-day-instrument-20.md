# Ecclesiastical Offices (Terms of Service) Measure 2009 (Appointed Day Instrument 2009)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2009 No. 2

Ecclesiastical Offices (Terms of Service) Measure 2009 (Appointed Day Instrument 2009)

**Whereas** the Ecclesiastical Offices (Terms of Service) Measure 2009 received the Royal Assent on the second day of April 2009

**And whereas** by section 13(2) of the said Measure it is provided that the said Measure shall come into force on such day as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York shall jointly appoint and that different days may be appointed for different provisions

**And whereas** an Instrument dated the eleventh day of June 2009 appointed that certain provisions of the said Measure should come into force on the first day of July 2009

**And whereas** the remaining provisions of the said Measure have not hitherto been brought into force

**Now therefore WE ROWAN DOUGLAS** by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury and **JOHN TUCKER MUGABI SENTAMU** by Divine Providence Archbishop of York **do hereby jointly appoint** that section 8 of the said Measure shall come into force on the twenty-fourth day of November 2009

Dated this twenty-fourth day of November in the Year of Our Lord two thousand and nine
