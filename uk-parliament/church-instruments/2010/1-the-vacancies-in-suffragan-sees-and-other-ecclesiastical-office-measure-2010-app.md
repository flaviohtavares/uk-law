# The Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Office Measure 2010 (Appointed Day No. 1) Instrument 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2010 No. 1

The Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Office Measure 2010 (Appointed Day No. 1) Instrument 2010

*Made*

*17th May 2010*

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4(2) of the Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Offices Measure 2010 [^c2032372], make the following Instrument: Measure 2009 received the Royal Assent on the second day of April 2009

## 1. Citation

This Instrument may be cited as the Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Offices Measure 2010 ( Appointed Day No. 1) Instrument 2010.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of sections 1 and 3 of the Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Offices Measure 2010 is 1st June 2010.

*+ Rowan Cantuar*

Archbishop of Canterbury

*+ Sentamu Ebor*

Archbishop of York

17th May 2010

*(This note is not part of the Instrument)*

This Instrument brings into force, on 1st June 2010, sections 1 and 3 of the Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Offices Measure 2010. The provisions commenced reduce the number of persons to be presented to Her Majesty for appointment to a suffragan see from two to one and abolish rights of the Crown relating to cession.
