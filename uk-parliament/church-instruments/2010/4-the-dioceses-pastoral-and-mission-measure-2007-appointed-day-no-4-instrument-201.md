# The Dioceses, Pastoral and Mission Measure 2007 (Appointed Day No. 4) Instrument 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2010 No. 4

The Dioceses, Pastoral and Mission Measure 2007 (Appointed Day No. 4) Instrument 2010

*Made*

*10th June 2010*

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 66(2) of the Dioceses, Pastoral and Mission Measure 2007 [^c2036239], make the following Instrument:

## 1. Citation

This Instrument may be cited as the Dioceses, Pastoral and Mission Measure 2007 (Appointed Day No. 4) Instrument 2010.

## 2. Appointed day

1st January 2011 is the day appointed for the coming into force of the following provisions of the Dioceses, Pastoral and Mission Measure 2007 —

- (a) section 12;
- (b) section 17;
- (c) section 65 so far as it relates to the provisions of Schedule 7 specified in paragraph (d);
- (d) Schedule 7 so far as it relates to subsections (1)(b) and (6) of section 18 of the Dioceses Measure 1978 [^c2036240] and subsections (5) and (7) of that section so far as they have not already been repealed.

*+ Rowan Cantuar*

Archbishop of Canterbury

*+ Sentamu Ebor*

Archbishop of York

10th June 2010

*(This note is not part of the Instrument)*

This Instrument brings into force, on 1st January 2011, those provisions of the Dioceses, Pastoral and Mission Measure 2007 that are not already in force.
