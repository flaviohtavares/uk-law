# The Ecclesiastical Offices (Terms of Service) Measure 2009 (Appointed Day No. 4) Instrument 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2010 No. 8

The Ecclesiastical Offices (Terms of Service) Measure 2009 (Appointed Day No. 4) Instrument 2010

*Made*

*14th December 2010*

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(2) of the Ecclesiastical Offices (Terms of Service ) Measure 2009 [^c20675581], make the following Instrument:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1 )This Instrument may be cited as the Ecclesisatical Offices (Terms of Service ) Measure 2009 (Appointed Day No. 4) Instrument 2010.

(2) In this Instrument "the Measure" means the Ecclesiastical Offices (Terms of Service ) Measure 2009.

## 2. Appointed Day

The day appointed for the coming into force of those provisions of the Measure which have not previously been brought into force (except paragraphs 6, 10, 12 and 13 of Schedule 2 ) is 31st January 2011.

\+ Rowan Cantuar:

Archbishop of Canterbury

\+ Sentamu Ebor:

Archbishop of York

14th December 2010

*(This note is not part of the Instrument)*

This Instrument brings into force, on 31st January 2011, the remaining provisions of the Ecclesiastical Offices (Terms of Service)Measure 2009, with the exception of paragraphs 6, 10, 12 and 13 of Schedule 2.
