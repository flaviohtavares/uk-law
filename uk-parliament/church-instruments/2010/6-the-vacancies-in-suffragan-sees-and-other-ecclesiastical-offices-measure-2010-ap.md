# The Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Offices Measure 2010 (Appointed Day No. 3) Instrument 2010

*Extent: E*

2010 No. 6

The Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Offices Measure 2010 (Appointed Day No. 3) Instrument 2010

*Made*

23rd November 2010

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4(2) of the Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Offices Measure 2010 [^c20607701], make the following Instrument:

## 1. Citation

This Instrument may be cited as the Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Offices Measure 2010 (Appointed Day No. 3) Instrument 2010.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of sections 2 of the Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Offices Measure 2010 is 1st January 2011.

\+ Rowan Cantuar:

Archbishop of Canterbury

\+ Sentamu Ebor:

Archbishop of York

23rd November 2010

*(This note is not part of the Instrument)*

This Instrument brings into force, on 1st January 2011, section 2 of the Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Offices Measure 2010. The provisions commenced provide for the exercise by 'the relevant bishop' of the Crown's right of presentation to an ecclesiastical office which is vacant during a vacancy in a diocesan see and to which the right of presentataion would, but for the vacancy in see, have been exercisable by the diocesan bishop.
