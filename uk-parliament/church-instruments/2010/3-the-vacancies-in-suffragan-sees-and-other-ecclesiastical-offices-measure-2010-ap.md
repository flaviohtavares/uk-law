# The Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Offices Measure 2010 (Appointed Day No. 2) Instrument 2010

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2010 No. 3

The Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Offices Measure 2010 (Appointed Day No. 2) Instrument 2010

*Made*

*10th June 2010*

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4(2) of the Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Offices Measure 2010 [^c2036238], make the following Instrument:

## 1. Citation

This Instrument may be cited as the Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Offices Measure 2010 (Appointed Day No. 2) Instrument 2010.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 4 of the Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Offices Measure 2010, so far as not already in force, is 11th June 2010.

*+ Rowan Cantuar*

Archbishop of Canterbury

*+ Sentamu Ebor*

Archbishop of York

10th June 2010

*(This note is not part of the Instrument)*

This Instrument brings into force, on 10th June 2010, section 4 (Citation, commencement and extent) of the Vacancies in Suffragan Sees and Other Ecclesiastical Offices Measure 2010 so far as that section is not already in force.
