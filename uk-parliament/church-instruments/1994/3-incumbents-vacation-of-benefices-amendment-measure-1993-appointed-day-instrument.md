# Incumbents (Vacation of Benefices) (Amendment) Measure 1993 (Appointed Day Instrument 1994)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

1994 No.3

Incumbents (Vacation of Benefices) (Amendment) Measure 1993 (Appointed Day Instrument 1994)

**Whereas** the Incumbents (Vacation of Benefices) (Amendment) Measure 1993 received the Royal Assent on the twenty-seventh day of July 1993

**And Whereas** by section 16(2) of the said Measure it is provided that the said Measure shall come into operation on such date as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York may jointly appoint

**Now therefore** we GEORGE LEONARD by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury and JOHN STAPYLTON by Divine Providence Archbishop of York do hereby jointly appoint that the said Measure shall come into operation on the first day of September 1994

Dated this twenty fifth day of July One thousand nine hundred and ninety four
