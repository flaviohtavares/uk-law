# Pastoral (Amendment) Measure 1994 (Appointed Day Instrument 1994)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

1994 No.2

Pastoral (Amendment) Measure 1994 (Appointed Day Instrument 1994)

**Whereas** the Pastoral (Amendment) Measure received the Royal Assent on the twenty fourth day of March 1994

**And Whereas** by section 15(2) of the said Measure it is provided that the Measure shall come into force on such date as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York may jointly appoint

**Now therefore** we GEORGE LEONARD by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury and JOHN STAPYLTON by Divine Providence Archbishop of York do hereby jointly appoint that the said Measure shall come into force on the first day of April 1994

Dated this twenty fifth day of March One thousand nine hundred and ninety four
