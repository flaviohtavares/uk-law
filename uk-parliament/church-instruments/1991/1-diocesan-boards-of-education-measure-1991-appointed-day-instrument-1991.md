# Diocesan Boards of Education Measure 1991 (Appointed Day Instrument 1991)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

1991 No. 1

Diocesan Boards of Education Measure 1991 (Appointed Day Instrument 1991)

**Whereas** the Diocesan Boards of Education Measure received the Royal Assent on the twenty fifth day of July 1991

**And Whereas** by section 13 of the said Measure it is provided that the Measure shall come into force on such date as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York may jointly appoint

**Now We** **GEORGE LEONARD** by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury and **JOHN STAPYLTON** by Divine Providence Archbishop of York do hereby jointly appoint that the said Measure shall come into force on the first day of August 1991

Dated this twenty sixth day of July One thousand nine hundred and ninety one
