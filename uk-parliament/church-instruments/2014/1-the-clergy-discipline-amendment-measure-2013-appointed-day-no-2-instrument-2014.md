# The Clergy Discipline (Amendment) Measure 2013 (Appointed Day No. 2) Instrument 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2014 No. 1

The Clergy Discipline (Amendment) Measure 2013 (Appointed Day No. 2) Instrument 2014

*Made*

*27th January 2014*

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10(2) of the Clergy Discipline (Amendment) Measure 2013 [^c2200748], make the following instrument:

## 1. Citation

This Instrument may be cited as the Clergy Discipline (Amendment) Measure 2013 (Appointed Day No. 2) Instrument 2014.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of those provisions of the Clergy Discipline (Amendment) Measure 2013 which have not previously been brought into force is 1st February 2014.

*+ Justin Cantuar*

Archbishop of Canterbury

*+ Sentamu Ebor*

Archbishop of York

27th January 2014

*(This note is not part of the Order)*

This Instrument brings into force, on 1st February 2014, all those provisions of the Clergy Discipline (Amendment) Measure 2013 which have not previously been brought into force. The Clergy Discipline Measure 2003, sections 3 and 7 of the Ecclesiastical Jurisdiction Measure 1963, and section 10 of the Ecclesiastical Jurisdiction Measure 1986.
