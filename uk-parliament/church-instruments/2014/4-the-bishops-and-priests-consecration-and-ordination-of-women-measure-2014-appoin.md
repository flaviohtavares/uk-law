# The Bishops and Priests (Consecration and Ordination of Women) Measure 2014 (Appointed Day No. 1) Instrument 2014

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2014 No. 4

The Bishops and Priests (Consecration and Ordination of Women) Measure 2014 (Appointed Day No. 1) Instrument 2014

*Made*

*14th November 2014*

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the power conferred by section 4(2) of the Bishops and Priests (Consecration and Ordination of Women) Measure 2014 [^c2230193], make the following instrument:

## 1. Citation

(1) This instrument may be cited as the Bishops and Priests (Consecration and Ordination of Women) Measure 2014 (Appointed Day No. 1) Instrument 2014.

(2) In this Instrument “**the Measure**” means the Bishops and Priests (Consecration and Ordination of Women) Measure 2014.

## 2. Appointed day

Section 1(1) and (so far as not already in force) section 4 of the Measure come into force on the day on which this Instrument is made.

*+ Justin Cantuar*

Archbishop of Canterbury

*+ Sentamu Ebor*

Archbishop of York

14th November 2014

*(This note is not part of the Order)*

This instrument brings into force, on 14th November 2014, section 1(1) and (in so far as not already in force) section 4 of the Bishops and Priests (Consecration and Ordination of Women) Measure 2014. The remaining provisions will be brought into force at a later date.
