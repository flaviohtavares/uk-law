# The Clergy Discipline (Amendment) Measure 2013 (Appointed Day No. 1) Instrument 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2013 No. 2

The Clergy Discipline (Amendment) Measure 2013 (Appointed Day No. 1) Instrument 2013

*Made*

*31st May 2013*

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 10(2) of the Clergy Discipline (Amendment) Measure 2013 [^c2186730], make the following instrument:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

This instrument may be cited as the Clergy Discipline (Amendment) Measure 2013 (Appointed Day No. 1) Instrument 2013.

## 2. Appointed day

1st July 2013 is the day appointed for the coming into force of section 9(1) of the Clergy Discipline (Amendment) Measure so far as it relates to the provisions in paragraph 10 of the Schedule.

*+ Justin Cantuar*

Archbishop of Canterbury

*+ Sentamu Ebor*

Archbishop of York

31st May 2013

*(This note is not part of the Instrument)*

This Instrument brings into force, on 1st July 2013, provisions in the Clergy Discipline (Amendment) Measure 2013 in respect of the issuing of a Code of Practice by the Clergy Discipline Commission pursuant to section 39 of the Clergy Discipline Measure 2003 [^c2186731].
