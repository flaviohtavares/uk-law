# The Diocese in Europe Measure 2013 (Appointed Day) Instrument 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2013 No. 3

The Diocese in Europe Measure 2013 (Appointed Day) Instrument 2013

*Made*

*10th June 2013*

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(2) of the Diocese in Europe Measure 2013 [^c2186728], make the following Instrument:

## 1. Citation and interpretation

(1) This Instrument may be cited as the Diocese in Europe Measure 2013 (Appointed Day) Instrument 2013.

(2) In this Instrument “the Measure” means the Diocese in Europe Measure 2013.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 1 of the Measure is 1st January 2014.

## 3.

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 2 of the Measure is 1st July 2013.

*+ Justin Cantuar*

Archbishop of Canterbury

*+ Sentamu Ebor*

Archbishop of York

10th June 2013

*(This note is not part of the Instrument)*

This Instrument brings into force the provisions of the Diocese in Europe Measure 2013. In accordance with the power conferred by the Measure, it makes different provision for different sections.
