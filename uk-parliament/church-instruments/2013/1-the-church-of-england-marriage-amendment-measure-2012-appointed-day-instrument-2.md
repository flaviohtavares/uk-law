# The Church of England Marriage(Amendment) Measure 2012 (Appointed Day) Instrument 2013

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2013 No. 1

The Church of England Marriage(Amendment) Measure 2012 (Appointed Day) Instrument 2013

*Made*

*21st May 2013*

The Archbishops of Canterbury and York, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 3(2) of the Church of England Marriage (Amendment) Measure 2012 [^c2180529] , make the following Instrument:

## 1. Citation

This instrument may be cited as the Church of England Marriage (Amendment) Measure 2012 (Appointed Day) Instrument 2013.

## 2. Appointed day

The day appointed for the coming into force of section 1 of the Church of England Marriage (Amendment) Measure 2012 is 1st June 2013.

*+ Justin Cantuar*

Archbishop of Canterbury

*+ Sentamu Ebor*

Archbishop of York

21st May 2013

*(This note is not part of the Instrument)*

This Instrument brings into force, on 1st June 2013, section 1 of the Church of England Marriage (Amendment) Measure 2012.

Section 2 and 3 of the Measure are already in force, having come into force, in accordance with section 3(2) of the Measure, on the day on which the Measure was passed.
