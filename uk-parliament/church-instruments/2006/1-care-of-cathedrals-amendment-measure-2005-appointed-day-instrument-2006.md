# Care of Cathedrals (Amendment) Measure 2005 (Appointed Day Instrument 2006)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2006 No. 1

Care of Cathedrals (Amendment) Measure 2005 (Appointed Day Instrument 2006)

**Whereas** the Care of Cathedrals (Amendment) Measure received the Royal Assent on the twenty–fourth day of March 2005

**And Whereas** by section 20(3) of the said Measure it is provided that the said Measure shall come into force on such day as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York may jointly appoint and that different days may be appointed for different provisions

**And Whereas** an Instrument dated the second day of June 2005 appointed that certain provisions of the said Measure should come into force on the sixth day of June 2005

**And Whereas** no other provisions of the said Measure have yet been brought into force

**Now therefore** **WE ROWAN DOUGLAS** by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury and **JOHN TUCKER MUGABI SENTAMU** by Divine Providence Archbishop of York do hereby jointly appoint that the following provisions of the said Measure shall come into force on the seventh day of February 2006:

- Subsection (4)(a) of section 8
- Subsection (1) of section 12
- Section 17 so far as it relates to paragraphs 1–8 and 12 of Schedule 1
- Section 18
- Section 19 so far as it relates to the provisions of Schedule 3 specified below
- Paragraphs 1–8 and 12 of Schedule 1
- Schedule 2
- Sub–paragraphs (b), (e), (g) and (i) of paragraph 4 of Schedule 3
- Paragraph 7 of Schedule 3
