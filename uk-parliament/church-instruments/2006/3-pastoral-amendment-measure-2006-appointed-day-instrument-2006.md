# Pastoral (Amendment) Measure 2006 (Appointed Day Instrument 2006)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2006 No. 3

Pastoral (Amendment) Measure 2006 (Appointed Day Instrument 2006)

**Whereas** the Pastoral (Amendment) Measure 2006 received the Royal Assent on the eleventh day of July 2006.

**And Whereas** by section 2(2) of the said Measure it is provided that the Measure shall come into force on such day as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York may jointly appoint and that different days may be appointed for different provisions.

**Now therefore** **WE ROWAN DOUGLAS** by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury and **JOHN TUCKER MUGABI SENTAMU** by Divine Providence Archbishop of York do hereby jointly appoint that all the provisions of the said Measure shall come into force on the first day of January 2007:
