# Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 2006 (Appointed Day Instrument 2006)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

2006 No. 2

Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 2006 (Appointed Day Instrument 2006)

**Whereas** the Church of England (Miscellaneous Provisions) Measure 2006 received the Royal Assent on the eleventh day of July 2006.

**And Whereas** by section 16(2) of the said Measure it is provided that the Measure shall come into force on such day as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York may jointly appoint and that different days may be appointed for different provisions.

**Now therefore** **WE ROWAN DOUGLAS** by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury and **JOHN TUCKER MUGABI SENTAMU** by Divine Providence Archbishop of York do hereby jointly appoint that all the provisions of the said Measure, other than section 1 and Schedule 1, shall come into force on the first day of October Two thousand and six.
