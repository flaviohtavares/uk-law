# Care of Churches and Ecclesiastical Jurisdiction Measure 1991 (Appointed Day Instrument 1993)

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.
>
> Extent: England.

*Extent: E*

1993 No.1

Care of Churches and Ecclesiastical Jurisdiction Measure 1991 (Appointed Day Instrument 1993)

**Whereas** the Care of Churches and Ecclesiastical Jurisdiction Measure received the Royal Assent on the twenty-fifth day of July 1991

**And Whereas** by section 33(2) of the said Measure it is provided that the Measure shall come into force on such date as the Archbishops of Canterbury and York may jointly appoint and that different dates may be appointed for different provisions

**Now We** GEORGE LEONARD by Divine Providence Archbishop of Canterbury and JOHN STAPYLTON by Divine Providence Archbishop of York do hereby jointly appoint that all the provisions of the said Measure shall come into force on the first day of March 1993

Dated this sixteenth day of February One thousand nine hundred and ninety three
