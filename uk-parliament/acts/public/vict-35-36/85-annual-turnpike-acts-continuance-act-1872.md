Annual Turnpike Acts Continuance Act 1872

1872 c.85

An Act . . .[^c568186] to make further provisions concerning Turnpike Roads.

\[10th August 1872\]

[^c568188]Whereas it is expedient to continue for limited times some of the Acts herein-after specified, and to repeal others, and[^c568187] to make further provisions concerning turnpike roads:

## 1—11. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c568189]

## 12. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c568190]

## 13. Provisions in Railways Clauses Consolidation Act, 1845, relating to turnpike roads to apply to such roads on their becoming ordinary highways.

Such of the provisions of the [^c568191]Railways Clauses Consolidation Act 1845 with respect to the crossing of roads and other interference therewith as relate to turnpike roads shall continue in force in relation to any road which, having been a turnpike road, may at any time after the passing of this Act become an ordinary highway, in the same manner as if such road had continued to be a turnpike road; and in the construction of the said provisions, when applied to any such road as aforesaid, the highway authority for the road[^c568192] shall be deemed to be the trustees or commissioners thereof; . . . [^c568193]

## 16. Short title.

This Act may be cited for all purposes as “**The Annual Turnpike Acts Continuance Act 1872.**”
