# Parks Regulation Act 1872

> Text as at 2005-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Parks Regulation Act 1872

1872 c.15

An Act for the Regulation of the Royal Parks and Gardens.

\[27th June 1872\][^c5680611][^c5680621][^c5680631][^c-e98922195e1fa232fa6d0fd1f5076359]

## 1. Short title.

This Act may be cited for all purposes as “**The Parks Regulation Act 1872.**”

## 2. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c5680641]

## 3. Definition of “park-keeper.”

Park constable[^c5680651] shall mean any person who, previously to the passing of this Act, has been or may hereafter be appointed park constable of a park as defined by this Act.

## 3A. Attestation of park constables.[^c5680671]

Every park constable shall on appointment be attested as a constable by making a declaration before a justice of the peace that he will duly execute the office of constable.

## 4. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c5680681]

## 5.[^c5680801][^c5680791]

*Extent: E+W*

Any person who—

(a) within the view of a park constable acts in contravention of any of the said regulations in the park where the park constable has jurisdiction; and

(b) when required by any park constable or by any police constable to give his name and address gives a false name or false address,

shall be liable on summary conviction to a penalty of an amount not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale, [^c5680811]. . ..

## 5. Park-keeper may apprehend any offender whose name or residence is not known.[^c5680701][^c5680691]

*Extent: S*

Any park constable[^c5680711] in uniform, and any persons whom he may call to his assistance, may take into custody, without a warrant, any offender who in the park where such park constable has jurisdiction, and within the view of such park constable acts in contravention of any of the said regulations, provided that the name or residence of such offender is unknown to and cannot be ascertained by such park constable.

If any such offender, when required by any park constable or by any police constable to give his name and address, gives a false name or false address, he shall, on conviction by a court of summary jurisdiction, by liable to a penalty not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale[^c5680761].

## 6. Penalty on assaults on park-keeper.

Where any person is convicted of an assault on any park constable[^c5680871] when in the execution of his duty, such person shall, on conviction by a court of summary jurisdiction, in the discretion of the court, be liable either to pay a penalty not exceeding £50[^c5680891][^c5680881]level 2 on the standard scale, [^c5680911]. . ., or to be imprisoned for any term not exceeding six months . . . [^c5680921]

## 7. Powers, duties, and privileges of park-keeper.

Every park constable[^c5680971] in addition to any powers and immunities specially conferred on him by this Act, shall, within the limits of the park of which he is park constable, have all such powers, privileges, and immunities, and be liable to all such duties and responsibilities, as any police constable has within the police area[^c5680991] in which such park is situated; and any person so appointed a park constable as aforesaid shall obey such lawful commands as he may from time to time receive from the Secretary of State[^c5681011] in respect of his conduct in the execution of his office.

## 8. Police constables to have the same powers, &c. as park-keepers.

Every police constable belonging to the police force for the police area[^c5681051] in which any park, . . . [^c5681061] to which this act applies is situate shall have the powers, privileges, and immunities of a park constable[^c5681071] within such park, . . . [^c5681061]

## 9. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c5681091]

## 10. Publication of regulations.

Copies of regulations to be observed in pursuance of this Act by persons using a . . . [^c5681101] park to which this Act applies shall be put up in such park in such conspicuous manner as the Secretary of State[^c5681111] may deem best calculated to give information to the persons using the park.

## 11. Saving of certain rights.

Nothing in this Act shall authorise any interference with any rights of way or any right whatever to which any person or persons may be by law entitled.

## 12. Act to be cumulative.

All powers conferred by this Act shall be deemed to be in addition to and not in derogation of any powers conferred by any other Act of Parliament, and any such powers may be exercised as if this Act had not been passed.

## 13. Saving of the rights of the Crown.

Nothing in this Act contained shall be deemed to prejudice or affect any prerogative or right of Her Majesty, or any power, right, or duty of the Secretary of State[^c5681121], or any powers or duties of any officers, clerks, or servants, appointed by Her Majesty or by the Secretary of State.

## 14. Saving of metropolitan Streets Act.

Nothing in this Act contained shall affect the Metropolitan Streets Act 1867, or the application thereof to any park to which it is by law applicable.
