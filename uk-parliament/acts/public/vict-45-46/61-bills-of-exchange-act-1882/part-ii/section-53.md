# Bills of Exchange Act 1882, section 53

> Text as at 2023-09-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Funds in hands of drawee.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A bill, of itself, does not operate as an assignment of funds in the hands of the drawee available for the payment thereof, and the drawee of a bill who does not accept as required by this Act is not liable on the instrument. This sub-section shall not extend to Scotland.

(2) Subject to section 75A of this Act,[^c580834] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4db7848bff1d1948aa4a03c0d27ee4f]in Scotland, where the drawee of a bill other than a cheque[^key-c4d209df64e722b5f0c0d047e84bb8fd] has in his hands funds available for the payment thereof, the bill operates as an assignment of the sum for which it is drawn in favour of the holder, from the time when the bill is presented to the drawee.
