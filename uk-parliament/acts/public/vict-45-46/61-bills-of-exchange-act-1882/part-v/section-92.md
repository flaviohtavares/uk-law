# Bills of Exchange Act 1882, section 92

> Text as at 2023-09-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Computation of time.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where, by this Act, the time limited for doing any act or thing is less than three days, in reckoning time, non-business days are excluded.

- “**Non-business days**” for the purposes of this Act mean—
- (a) Saturday[^c580899] Sunday, Good Friday, Christmas Day:
- (b) A bank holiday under the[^c580900] [^c580901]Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971:
- (c) A day appointed by Royal proclamation as a public fast or thanksgiving day.
- (d)[^c580902] A day declared by an order under section 2 of the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 to be a non-business day.

Any other day is a business day.
