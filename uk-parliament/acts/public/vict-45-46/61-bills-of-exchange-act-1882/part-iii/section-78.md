# Bills of Exchange Act 1882, section 78

> Text as at 2023-09-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Crossing a material part of cheque.[^c580875][^c-f0dfb51f70893b390d0ad64a6af504f9][^c-08c94f06b01ac01a534b5f3b8c0f9527][^c580874]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A crossing authorised by this Act is a material part of the cheque; it shall not be lawful for any person to obliterate or, except as authorised by this Act, to add to or alter the crossing.
