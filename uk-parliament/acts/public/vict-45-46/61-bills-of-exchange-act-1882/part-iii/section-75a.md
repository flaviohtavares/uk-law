# Bills of Exchange Act 1882, section 75A

> Text as at 2023-09-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75A.[^c580845]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-73ceec0ecfa763f726fd9538b49a4b59] On the countermand of payment of a cheque, the banker shall be treated as having no funds available for the payment of the cheque. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-73ceec0ecfa763f726fd9538b49a4b59]

(2) This section applies to Scotland only. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-73ceec0ecfa763f726fd9538b49a4b59]
