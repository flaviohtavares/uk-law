# Bills of Exchange Act 1882, section 77

> Text as at 2023-09-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Crossing by drawer or after issue.[^c580865][^c580864]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c580866] A cheque may be crossed generally or specially by the drawer.

(2) Where a cheque is uncrossed, the holder may cross it generally or specially.

(3)[^c580866] Where a cheque is crossed generally the holder may cross it specially.

(4)[^c580866] Where a cheque is crossed generally or specially, the holder may add the words “not negotiable.”

(5)[^c580866] Where a cheque is crossed specially, the banker to whom it is crossed may again cross it specially to another banker for collection.

(6)[^key-dd849d3681ec3c53c7707c9e7d0716ba][^key-f248c103b3837b64b2321c976fa60055] Where an uncrossed cheque, or a cheque crossed generally, is sent to a banker for collection, he may cross it specially to himself.
