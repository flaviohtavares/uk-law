# Factories Act 1937, section 125

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 125. Certificate of appointment of inspector.

Every inspector shall be furnished with the prescribed certificate of his appointment, and when visiting a factory or place to which any of the provisions of this Act apply shall, if so required, produce the said certificate to the occupier or other person holding a responsible position of management at the factory.
