# Factories Act 1937, section 69

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 69. Duty of examining surgeon to investigate and report in certain cases.

(1) It shall be the duty of the examining surgeon to investigate and report—

- (a) upon cases of death or injury caused by exposure in a factory to fumes or other noxious substances, or due to any other special cause specified in instructions of the Secretary of State as requiring investigation; and
- (b) upon any case of death or injury which the inspector for the district in pursuance of any general or special instructions of the Secretary of State may refer to him for that purpose; and
- (c) upon any case of disease of which he receives notice under this Act.

(2) The examining surgeon, for the purpose of an investigation under this section, shall have the like powers as an inspector, including power to enter any room in a building to which the person killed, injured, or affected has been removed.
