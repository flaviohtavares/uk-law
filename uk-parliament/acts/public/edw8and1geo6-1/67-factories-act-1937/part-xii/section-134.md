# Factories Act 1937, section 134

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 134. Fine for offence by parent.

If a young person is employed in any factory in contravention of the provisions of this Act, the parent of the young person shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding five pounds, unless it appears to the court that the contravention occurred without the consent, connivance, or wilful default of the parent.
