# Factories Act 1937, section 148

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 148. Application of Arbitration Acts.

The Arbitration Acts, 1889 to 1934, shall not apply to proceedings under this Act except in so far as they may be applied by regulations made under this Act.
