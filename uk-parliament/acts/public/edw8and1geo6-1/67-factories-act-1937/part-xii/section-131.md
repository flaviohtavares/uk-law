# Factories Act 1937, section 131

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 131. Fines for offences for which no express penalty provided.

Subject as hereinafter in this Act provided, any person guilty of an offence under this Act for which no express penalty is provided by this Act shall be liable to a fine not exceeding twenty pounds, and, if the contravention in respect of which he was so convicted is continued after the conviction he shall (subject to the provisions of section one hundred and thirty-two of this Act) be guilty of a further offence and liable in respect thereof to a fine not exceeding five pounds for each day on which the contravention was so continued.
