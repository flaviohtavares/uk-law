# Factories Act 1937, section 77

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 77. Prohibition of Sunday employment.

Subject to the special exceptions allowed under this Part of this Act, a woman or young person shall not be employed on Sunday in a factory nor shall a woman or young person employed in any factory on any other day of the week, be employed on Sunday about the business of the factory or in any other business carried on by the occupier.
