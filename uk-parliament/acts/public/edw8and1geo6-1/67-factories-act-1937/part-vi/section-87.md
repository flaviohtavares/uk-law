# Factories Act 1937, section 87

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 87. Exception as to continuous employment of male young persons employed with men.

The provisions of this Part of this Act forbidding the continuous employment of a young person for a spell of more than four-and-a-half hours without an interval of at least half-an-hour shall, in the case of male young persons who have attained the age of sixteen and are employed with men and whose continuous employment is necessary to enable the men to carry on their work, have effect, as respects any spell commencing in the morning, as if five hours were substituted for four-and-a-half hours as the length of the spell for which they may be employed continuously.
