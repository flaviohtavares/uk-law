# Factories Act 1937, section 55

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 55. Laundries.

In every laundry—

- (a) effective steps shall he taken by means of a fan or otherwise to regulate the temperature in every ironing room, and to carry away the steam in every washhouse;
- (b) all stoves for heating irons shall be so separated from any ironing room or ironing table as to protect the workers from the heat thereof;
- (c) no gas iron emitting any noxious fumes shall be used.
