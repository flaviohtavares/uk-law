# Factories Act 1937, section 51

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 51. Prohibition of use of white phosphorus in manufacture of matches.

(1) No person shall use white phosphorus in the manufacture of matches.

(2) For the purposes of this Part of this Act the expression “**white phosphorus**” means the substance usually known as white or yellow phosphorus.
