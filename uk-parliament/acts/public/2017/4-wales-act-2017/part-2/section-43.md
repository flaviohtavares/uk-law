## 43. Alignment of associated development consent

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 115 of the Planning Act 2008 (development for which development consent may be granted) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (2)(c), for ““or (4)”” substitute “ , (4) or (4A) ”.

(3) After subsection (4) insert—

> (4A) Development is within this subsection if the development within subsection (1)(a) with which it is associated is—
>
> - (a) the construction or extension of a generating station that is or (when constructed or extended) is expected to be within section 15(3A) or (3B), or
> - (b) the installation of an electric line that is or (when installed) is expected to be within section 14(1)(b).
