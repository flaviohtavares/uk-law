# High Speed Rail (London - West Midlands) Act 2017, section 60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Royal parks

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may grant a lease of royal park land within the Act limits if it appears to the Secretary of State that the land is required for Phase One purposes.

(2) A lease may be granted under subsection (1) for such period, for such consideration (if any) and otherwise on such terms as the Secretary of State thinks fit.

(3) The Secretary of State may grant an easement or other right over royal park land within the Act limits if it appears to the Secretary of State that the easement or right is required for Phase One purposes.

(4) An easement or other right may be granted under subsection (3)—

- (a) in fee simple or for such period as the Secretary of State thinks fit, and
- (b) for such consideration (if any) and otherwise on such terms as the Secretary of State thinks fit.

(5) In this section “**"royal park land”**” means land subject to management under section 22 of the Crown Lands Act 1851 (royal parks etc).
