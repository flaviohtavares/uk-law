# Neighbourhood Planning Act 2017, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Financial provisions[^c22730181]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following are to be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenditure incurred under or by virtue of this Act by a Minister of the Crown, a person holding office under Her Majesty or a government department, and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable under any other Act out of money so provided.
