# Neighbourhood Planning Act 2017, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30. Interpretation[^c-9eedcf597bfb5e7f43f5f7eaf6fe13f7]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Chapter—

- “**acquiring authority**” has the meaning given in section 18(1);
- “**the notice period**” has the meaning given in section 20(2);
- “[^c-710cfcfa5202a411650ba09f42425d0f]**obligation under a conservation covenant**” has the same meaning as in Part 7 of the Environment Act 2021;
- “**possession**” means exclusive occupation;
- “**relevant right or interest**” has the meaning given by section 23(10).
