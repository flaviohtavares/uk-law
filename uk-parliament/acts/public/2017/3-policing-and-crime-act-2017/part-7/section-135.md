## 135. Meaning of “alcohol”: inclusion of alcohol in any state[^c22719741][^key-51eb0b128c9d270cf5f80b3d87b145e4]

*Extent: E+W*

In the Licensing Act 2003, in section 191(1) (meaning of “alcohol”) after “liquor” insert “ (in any state) ”.
