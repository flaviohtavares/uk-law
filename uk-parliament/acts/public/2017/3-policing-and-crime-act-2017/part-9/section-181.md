# Policing and Crime Act 2017, section 181

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 181. Financial provision

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following are to be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenditure incurred under or by virtue of this Act by the Secretary of State;
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable under any other Act out of money so provided.
