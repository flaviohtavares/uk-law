## 167. Sections 164 to 166: supplementary

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A pardon under section 164 or 165[^key-1be3638cee98b4e42a421c59fa9361fc]... does not—

- (a) affect any conviction, caution or sentence, or
- (b) give rise to any right, entitlement or liability.

(2) Nothing in this section or in section 164 or 165[^key-5a61ba47534b712ee1b488c64e93a683] [^key-01efd74b7ef220c3095e5e429e3843ed]... affects the prerogative of mercy.
