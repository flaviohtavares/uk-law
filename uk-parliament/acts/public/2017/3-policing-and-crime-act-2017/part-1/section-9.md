# Policing and Crime Act 2017, section 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. The London Fire Commissioner[^c22718391]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-360186e70ab0b6af8365230cfb8f475e] The London Fire and Emergency Planning Authority is abolished.

(2)[^key-360186e70ab0b6af8365230cfb8f475e] The functions of the London Fire and Emergency Planning Authority are (subject to this Act) transferred to the London Fire Commissioner.

(3)[^key-3d260c0a972de92deed50ac6c8155ec8][^key-22a94bda98d67c588177398fcb777e48][^key-5a8f4fd34d3a4f9332f4b870ab7e3a8b] Schedule 2—

- (a) amends the Greater London Authority Act 1999 in consequence of the abolition of the London Fire and Emergency Planning Authority,
- (b) amends that Act so as to make provision about the London Fire Commissioner, and
- (c) makes consequential amendments to other Acts.
