# Policing and Crime Act 2017, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Removal of references to ACPO[^c22718861]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 14 removes references in legislation to the Association of Chief Police Officers and replaces most of them with references to the National Police Chiefs' Council.
