# Policing and Crime Act 2017, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/3

## 50. Freedom of Information Act etc: Police Federation for England and Wales[^c22718851]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-d25b89a20804d25c8c1c9ceaf145b36a] The Police Federation for England and Wales is to be treated for the purposes of—

- (a) the Freedom of Information Act 2000,
- (b) the data protection legislation[^key-46b820cdf0e1342c0da6d10e1ca7c994], and
- (c) section 18 of the Inquiries Act 2005,

as if it were a body listed in Part 5 of Schedule 1 to the 2000 Act (public authorities).

(2)[^key-8a4e852b55483a77184b5a5f53262f73] In this section, “**the data protection legislation**” has the same meaning as in the Data Protection Act 2018 (see section 3 of that Act).
