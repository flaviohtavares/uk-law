# Children and Social Work Act 2017, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Fees

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-e7ed4f95726462b7b11e9992e4c53882] The Secretary of State may by regulations confer power on the regulator to charge fees in connection with—

- (a) registration or continued registration in a register mentioned in section 39;
- (b) assessing whether a person meets a professional standard relating to proficiency as mentioned in section 41(4);
- (c) approval or continued approval in accordance with a scheme mentioned in section 43.

(2)[^c-33330c28699e4405e78a8a2c02838449][^c-1e2ff9dc2f36a0915b0121d6cb369d56] The regulator is responsible for setting the level of fees in accordance with any provision made by the regulations.

(3)[^c-e7ed4f95726462b7b11e9992e4c53882][^c-33330c28699e4405e78a8a2c02838449] Before determining the level of any fee the regulator must—

- (a) consult any persons they consider appropriate, and
- (b) obtain the approval of the Secretary of State.

(4)[^c-e7ed4f95726462b7b11e9992e4c53882][^c-33330c28699e4405e78a8a2c02838449] The regulations may authorise fees to be set at a level that exceeds the cost of the things in respect of which they are charged.

(5)[^c-e7ed4f95726462b7b11e9992e4c53882][^c-33330c28699e4405e78a8a2c02838449] But the regulations must require the level of any fees to be set with a view to ensuring that, so far as possible, the regulator's fee income does not exceed its expenses (taking one year with another).

(6)[^c-e7ed4f95726462b7b11e9992e4c53882][^c-33330c28699e4405e78a8a2c02838449] Regulations under this section may include provision about the collection and recovery of fees.

(7)[^c-e7ed4f95726462b7b11e9992e4c53882][^c-33330c28699e4405e78a8a2c02838449] The regulations must require the regulator to pay any fee income to the Secretary of State unless the Secretary of State, with the consent of the Treasury, directs otherwise.
