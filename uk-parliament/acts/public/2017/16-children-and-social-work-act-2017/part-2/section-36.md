# Children and Social Work Act 2017, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. Social Work England

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-78a334446edeb0c2c4d7a6e33a90822d] A body corporate called Social Work England is established.

(2)[^c-78a334446edeb0c2c4d7a6e33a90822d] Social Work England is referred to in this Part as “**the regulator**”.

(3)[^c-97915eaccbc281199baeaa7c6b7a7380][^c-3bfe7b89f0a4ae532c96f99fe16d2317] Schedule 3 makes further provision about the regulator.

(4)[^c-78a334446edeb0c2c4d7a6e33a90822d] The Secretary of State may by regulations rename Social Work England.

(5)[^c-78a334446edeb0c2c4d7a6e33a90822d] Regulations under subsection (4) may include consequential amendments to any provision contained in or made under this or any other Act.
