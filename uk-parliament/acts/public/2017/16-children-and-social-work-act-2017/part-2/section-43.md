# Children and Social Work Act 2017, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43. Education and training

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-aacf5f786e9f3767f5de93f64dac95a2] The regulator must, in relation to people who are or who wish to become social workers in England, determine and publish standards of education or training.

(2)[^c-22f2e7e5a43aa1697ac2da2ae9d1fd1e] Before determining a standard under this section the regulator must—

- (a) consult such persons as the regulator considers appropriate, and
- (b) obtain the Secretary of State's approval of the standard.

(3)[^c-22f2e7e5a43aa1697ac2da2ae9d1fd1e] The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision for the regulator to operate a scheme for the approval of—

- (a) courses of education or training for people who are or who wish to become social workers in England;
- (b) qualifications for people who are or who wish to become social workers in England.

(4)[^c-22f2e7e5a43aa1697ac2da2ae9d1fd1e] The regulations may make provision in connection with the approval scheme.

(5)[^c-22f2e7e5a43aa1697ac2da2ae9d1fd1e] For example, the regulations may make provision about—

- (a) the criteria for approval or continued approval;
- (b) the procedure for approval or renewal of approval;
- (c) duties to provide information to the regulator;
- (d) inspections in connection with the approval or continued approval of courses or qualifications (including provision for the appointment of people to carry out inspections);
- (e) appeals against decisions in connection with approval;
- (f) the publication of the scheme.

(6)[^c-22f2e7e5a43aa1697ac2da2ae9d1fd1e] The provision that may be made under the regulations about the appointment of people to carry out inspections includes provision about—

- (a) payments to be made to those appointed;
- (b) staff, facilities or other assistance.
