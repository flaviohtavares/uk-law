# Children and Social Work Act 2017, section 52

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Information and advice

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-3a82e095467b94a1e69baf37c65c67d7] The regulator may publish or disclose information about any matter relating to its functions or give advice about any matter relating to its functions.

(2)[^c-a85f9c4fab13814849cc9be3f07aa530] The Secretary of State may by regulations —

- (a) make provision requiring the regulator to publish or disclose information, or give advice, under subsection (1);
- (b) make other provision supplementing subsection (1).
