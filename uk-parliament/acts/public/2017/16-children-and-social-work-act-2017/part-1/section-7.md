## 7. Maintained schools: guidance for staff member for looked after pupils[^c-81b34a3e65e0b2677417407d96481a6c]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 20 of the Children and Young Persons Act 2008 (designated staff member for looked after pupils) after subsection (2) insert—

> (2A) If the school is in England, the governing body must ensure that the designated person has regard to any guidance issued by the Secretary of State.
