# Technical and Further Education Act 2017, section 1

> Text as at 2025-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. The Institute for Apprenticeships and Technical Education

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-4f9d428ecc51d12deb3beca794d0a70b] The Institute for Apprenticeships is renamed the Institute for Apprenticeships and Technical Education (referred to in this Act as “**the Institute**”).

(2)[^c-fb5d0adc8870bec96f3bd57977f03072] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-fa20176422e2d86e207a0b6bd1f2d089] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-2ccc10f0a383bb0284cf01f37019d8b9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c-c8b7f0e7f5ab2965000df2e6b208d4d9][^c-5f0cefd5ad1ad7ce1f6e8bcc2907a189][^c-ae65cded3c5f57f9d1bec880c8ce9294][^c-b330d0ac02cee2635d46b677a9adcd74][^c-581c7b29ba8e336e4f67d479b9d34d00] Schedule 1—

- (a) confers functions on the Institute in relation to technical education qualifications,
- (b) makes provision about the sharing of information,
- (c) makes provision about the charging of fees by the Institute in connection with evaluations of the quality of apprenticeship assessments, and
- (d) makes transitional provision.
