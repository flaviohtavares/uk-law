# Cultural Property (Armed Conflicts) Act 2017, section 19

> Text as at 2023-02-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Property liable to forfeiture[^c-3c13a364b9e9bc082f049031de7de3c4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Unlawfully exported cultural property is liable to forfeiture if it is imported into the United Kingdom after this section comes into force.
