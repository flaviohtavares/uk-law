# Finance (No. 2) Act 2017, section 54

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Forfeiture[^c22795991][^c-f7cbc775aa124e80e270249882054640][^c-f097a664fcf58121fe712b3168a09a3b][^c-a722102f4bef674d5b867b25d34173f7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a person—

- (a) carries on an imported goods[^c-f60f36eb99df26125761e8729c229f4c] fulfilment business, and
- (b) is not an approved person,

any goods within subsection (2) are liable to forfeiture under CEMA 1979.

(2) Goods are within this subsection if—

- (a) they are stored by the person, and
- (b) their storage by the person constitutes, or has constituted, the carrying on of an imported goods[^c-40becf3abecf19095a408ff4002895d4] fulfilment business by the person.
