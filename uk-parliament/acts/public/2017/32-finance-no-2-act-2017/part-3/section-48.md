# Finance (No. 2) Act 2017, section 48

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Carrying on an imported goods fulfilment business[^c22795931][^key-7631aec6307d2e99fcef93972e13c93a][^key-469815e809f7e6f758780bfe66e25942]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Part a person carries on an imported goods[^key-a029a5981db55229e588741e204a7e18] fulfilment business if the person, by way of business—

- (a) stores imported goods[^key-d0f95038f6509ef241763b9296022973] which are owned by a person who is not UK-established[^key-123f0ab4da9cda9efc90d8bb085afd29], or
- (b) stores imported goods[^key-f827b25387a75fd9c1fcc476e10cadc9] on behalf of a person who is not UK-established[^key-e608fd805dc9448bbb6880544c9c2742],

at a time when the conditions in subsection (2) are met in relation to the goods.

(2) The conditions are that—

- (a) there has been no supply of the goods in the United Kingdom for the purposes of VATA 1994, and
- (b) the goods are being offered for sale in the United Kingdom or elsewhere.

(3) But a person does not carry on an imported goods[^key-3b3ba03e53c649bb6c10d08ba8ca0fc1] fulfilment business if the person's activities within subsection (1) are incidental to the carriage of the goods.

(4)[^key-0e6df128a80a526983e4172590bc4978] Goods are “imported goods” if they have been imported into the United Kingdom for the purposes of VATA 1994 (as to which, see section 15 and paragraph 1 of Schedule 9ZB[^key-180b2e1ecf2428dabc8e4c519927f75a]).

(4A)[^key-79643617aa2d3b93fd1e32a074522978] But goods that are treated as imported for the purposes of VATA 1994 as a result of paragraph 3 of Schedule 9ZB are not imported goods for the purposes of this Part.

(5) A person is “UK-established” if the person's business establishment is in the United Kingdom as determined for the purposes of section 9 of VATA 1994.
