> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Digital reporting and record-keeping for income tax etc: further amendments[^c22793351]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-aa711a5c98d64f612d17253010fe6f65] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-24c4cf40bba89d2178bfab7a336316c3] The Commissioners for Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs may by regulations amend or modify any provision of the Taxes Acts in consequence of the provision made by section 60 [^c-1e28c389d46f7a715aef74d98c9ca443]....

(3)[^c-24c4cf40bba89d2178bfab7a336316c3] Regulations under subsection (2) may make transitional, transitory or saving provision.

(4)[^c-24c4cf40bba89d2178bfab7a336316c3] Regulations under subsection (2) must be made by statutory instrument.

(5)[^c-24c4cf40bba89d2178bfab7a336316c3] A statutory instrument containing regulations under subsection (2) may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the House of Commons.

(6)[^c-7be2549316d5bac3880cb31fcfd93aba][^c-15071e5b986696011b3ee6ca9dfe3297] Subsections (2)[^c-9c8485beb926ef9df4c8c06269356f01] to (5) [^c-fcbc80c9bf37a4e65dee9ae158768360]... come into force on such day as the Treasury may by regulations made by statutory instrument appoint.

(7) Regulations under subsection (6) may appoint different days for different purposes.
