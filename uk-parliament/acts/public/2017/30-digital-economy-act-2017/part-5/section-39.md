# Digital Economy Act 2017, section 39

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Disclosure of information by water and sewerage undertakers etc[^c-08550f8a8a945f75e47ec6d677884c75][^c-5107bf168716d4db622834ba50cc7bb6][^c-714d05f3bcd825736e772d7cfdd22463]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the condition in subsection (2) is met, a person to whom information may be disclosed under section 38 may disclose information held by that person to a specified person.

(2) That condition is that the disclosure is for the purpose of assisting eligible people (see section 38(9A))[^c-27ed45a9596f95292c214f03e0476255] in England and Wales by—

- (a) reducing their water or sewerage costs,
- (b) improving efficiency in their use of water, or
- (c) improving their health or financial well-being.
