# Finance Act 2017, section 36

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Levy rates[^c-0365fb06352b0340a91cce55443560cd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Soft drinks industry levy is charged—

- (a) in the case of chargeable soft drinks that meet the higher sugar threshold, at the rate of[^c-b46bab8f65add090422f81491bdca33f] £2.78[^c-891c5852f0639aac3010182889986d33] per 10 litres of prepared drink;
- (b) in the case of chargeable soft drinks that do not meet the higher sugar threshold, at the rate of[^c-4122442902ff89a9c33db14c2d826fd0] £2.08[^c-cab6d7091bd29f96a80bf4f85173db66] per 10 litres of prepared drink.

(2) A chargeable soft drink meets the higher sugar threshold if it contains at least 8 grams of sugars (whether or not as a result of containing added sugar ingredients) per 100 millilitres of prepared drink.
