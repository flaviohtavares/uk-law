# Higher Education and Research Act 2017, section A3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## A3. Duty to promote the importance of freedom of speech and academic freedom[^key-533bb55b30788a9212ac7dbb0b6c79ab]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The governing body of a registered higher education provider must promote the importance of—

- (a) freedom of speech within the law, and
- (b) academic freedom for academic staff of registered higher education providers and their constituent institutions,

in the provision of higher education.
