# Higher Education and Research Act 2017, section 109

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 109. Predecessor bodies and preservation of symbolic property

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following bodies corporate (each a “research council”) cease to exist—

- (a)[^key-8c94a580412a6847278f0eee8286dda4] the Arts and Humanities Research Council,
- (b)[^key-8c94a580412a6847278f0eee8286dda4] the Biotechnology and Biological Sciences Research Council,
- (c)[^key-8c94a580412a6847278f0eee8286dda4] the Economic and Social Research Council,
- (d)[^key-8c94a580412a6847278f0eee8286dda4] the Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council,
- (e) the Medical Research Council,
- (f)[^key-8c94a580412a6847278f0eee8286dda4] the Natural Environment Research Council,
- (g)[^key-3a5764e02ad7ff1a73c3fe43186c7894] the Science and Technology Facilities Council, and
- (h)[^key-8c94a580412a6847278f0eee8286dda4] the Technology Strategy Board.

(2)[^key-91afb8b9d739e4fd1edbaab39698a457][^key-608ba612eddabe088e67604d53d416d9] The Royal Charters establishing the research councils, and any supplemental Royal Charter granted to a council, are revoked.

(3)[^key-f4d54a08055312bfdf1512b38b2dca09] A property transfer scheme made by the Secretary of State under Schedule 10 in connection with a research council must, in particular, make provision for the transfer of the symbolic property of the council to UKRI.

(4)[^key-f4d54a08055312bfdf1512b38b2dca09] The symbolic property of a research council is—

- (a) the name of, and any other name used by, the council,
- (b) any goodwill in a name falling within paragraph (a),
- (c) any logo or insignia of the council, and
- (d) any seal of the council.
