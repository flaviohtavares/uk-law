# Higher Education and Research Act 2017, section 91

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. United Kingdom Research and Innovation[^c-a8f2991e47188fba658de2e7fded8af0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A body corporate called United Kingdom Research and Innovation or, in Welsh, Ymchwil ac Arloesedd y Deyrnas Unedig, is established.

(2) In this Act that body is referred to as “**UKRI**”.

(3) Section 92 and Schedule 9 contain further provision about UKRI.
