## 69C. Free speech complaints scheme[^c-3fe137a0764e69a92879a95a963cbb20]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 6A contains provision for a scheme for the making and investigation of complaints relating to the duties of registered higher education providers, their constituent institutions and students’ unions in respect of freedom of speech and academic freedom.
