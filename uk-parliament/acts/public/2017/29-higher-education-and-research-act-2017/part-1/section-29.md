# Higher Education and Research Act 2017, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29. Power to approve an access and participation plan

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-7abe0794217fc3f1831bc0a2f1796f91] The governing body of an institution may apply to the OfS for the OfS's approval of a proposed access and participation plan relating to the institution for the purposes of satisfying an access and participation plan condition.

(2)[^c-1098992b66021cfa154b704ad7498977][^c-a65113d776b0a93f9172893174ea92f6] An access and participation plan is a plan that complies with sections 30 to 32.

(3)[^c-7abe0794217fc3f1831bc0a2f1796f91] The OfS may, if it thinks fit, approve the plan.

(4)[^c-6e525bed0d9b4dd71a6cca49c07f7990] The OfS may issue guidance as to the matters to which the OfS will have regard in deciding whether to approve plans.

(5)[^c-7abe0794217fc3f1831bc0a2f1796f91] The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision about the procedure to be followed in connection with the giving of approval under this section.

(6)[^c-7abe0794217fc3f1831bc0a2f1796f91] The regulations may, in particular, specify matters to which the OfS is, or is not, to have regard in making any determination relating to approval.

(7)[^c-7abe0794217fc3f1831bc0a2f1796f91] The regulations may require the institution to which any plan approved under this section relates to publish the plan in the manner prescribed by the regulations.
