# Higher Education and Research Act 2017, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. The register

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-68caec53756375af4e5478eaa052087f][^c-9565039bc8eca9a0930a30fb17af4863] The OfS must establish and maintain a register of English higher education providers (referred to in this Part as “**the register**”).

(2)[^c-5a0bcfc8d333eadfb2c631460c49d603] The register may be divided by the OfS into different parts representing such different categories of registration as the OfS may determine.

(3)[^c-5a0bcfc8d333eadfb2c631460c49d603] The OfS must register an institution in the register (or, where it has been divided into parts, in a particular part of the register) if—

- (a) its governing body applies for it to be registered in the register (or in that part),
- (b) it is, or intends to become, an English higher education provider,
- (c) it satisfies the initial registration conditions applicable to it in respect of the registration sought (see section 5), and
- (d) the application complies with any requirements imposed under subsection (5).

(4)[^c-5a0bcfc8d333eadfb2c631460c49d603] The OfS may not otherwise register an institution in the register.

(5)[^c-5a0bcfc8d333eadfb2c631460c49d603] The OfS may determine—

- (a) the form of an application for registration in the register (or in a particular part of the register),
- (b) the information to be contained in it or provided with it, and
- (c) the manner in which an application is to be submitted.

(6)[^c-5a0bcfc8d333eadfb2c631460c49d603] The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision about the information which must be contained in an institution's entry in the register.

(7)[^c-ce36604e759bc3b0937372be4ca4db53][^c-9565039bc8eca9a0930a30fb17af4863] Once registered, an institution's ongoing registration is subject to satisfying—

- (a) the general ongoing registration conditions applicable to it at the time of its registration and as they may be later revised (see section 5), and
- (b) the specific ongoing registration conditions (if any) imposed on it at the time of its registration and as they may be later varied (see section 6).

(8)[^c-f73a5b7783be892e6617aa52a01a0d5d] References in this Part to the ongoing registration conditions of an institution are to the conditions mentioned in subsection (7)(a) and (b).

(9)[^c-5a0bcfc8d333eadfb2c631460c49d603] The OfS must make the information contained in the register, and the information previously contained in it, publicly available by such means as it considers appropriate.

(10)[^c-f73a5b7783be892e6617aa52a01a0d5d] In this Part—

- (a) a “**registered higher education provider**” means an institution which is registered in the register, and
- (b) references to “registration” are to be read accordingly.
