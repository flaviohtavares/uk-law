> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/29

## 1. The Office for Students[^c-13082e086398242abab8084a2ad9648f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A body corporate called the Office for Students is established.

(2) In this Act that body is referred to as “**the OfS**”.

(3) Schedule 1 contains further provision about the OfS.
