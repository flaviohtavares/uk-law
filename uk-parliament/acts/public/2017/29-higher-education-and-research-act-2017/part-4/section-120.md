# Higher Education and Research Act 2017, section 120

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/29

## 120. Directions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any power conferred by this Act to give directions includes power, exercisable in the same manner and subject to the same conditions or limitations, to vary or revoke directions previously given.
