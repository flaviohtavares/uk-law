# Higher Education and Research Act 2017, section 121

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/29

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 121. General interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act—

- “**notice**” means written notice (and to “**notify**” means to give written notice);
- “**the OfS**” has the meaning given by section 1;
- “**primary legislation**” means—
  - (a) an Act of Parliament;
  - (b) an Act of the Scottish Parliament;
  - (c) a Measure or Act of the National Assembly for Wales;
  - (d) Northern Ireland legislation;
- “**secondary legislation**” means an instrument made under primary legislation;
- “**UKRI**” has the meaning given by section 91.
