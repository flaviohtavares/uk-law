# Criminal Finances Act 2017, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Forfeiture of money held in bank and building society accounts[^c-a25c632bef386d557a1b828b5fcfddd2][^c-6a4b03e046c19ecfc923a07d96237824][^c22725431]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 4 contains amendments to the Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001 which enable the forfeiture of money held in a bank or building society account which—

- (a) is intended to be used for the purposes of terrorism,
- (b) consists of resources of a proscribed organisation, or
- (c) is, or represents, property obtained through terrorism.
