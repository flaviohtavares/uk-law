# Criminal Finances Act 2017, section 21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/22
>

## 21. Immigration officers[^key-988746baa69a863c2851e2e6dd3dfd4e][^c22725241]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-d5968b145c1941ec6d13f2205a1a5c40] Section 24 of the UK Borders Act 2007 (seizure of cash) is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-d5968b145c1941ec6d13f2205a1a5c40] For the heading substitute “ Exercise of civil recovery powers by immigration officers ”.

(3)[^key-d5968b145c1941ec6d13f2205a1a5c40] For subsection (1) substitute—

> (1) Chapters 3 to 3B of Part 5 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (civil recovery) apply in relation to an immigration officer as they apply in relation to a constable.

(4)[^key-d5968b145c1941ec6d13f2205a1a5c40] In subsection (2)(a), for “section 289” substitute “ sections 289 and 303C and Chapter 3B ”.

(5)[^key-6c38686dcdd444755ff273860081c4de] In subsection (2)(c), for “and 297A” substitute “ , 297A and 303E and in Chapter 3B (see section 303Z2(4)) ”.

(6)[^key-d5968b145c1941ec6d13f2205a1a5c40] In subsection (2)(d), for “section 292” substitute “ sections 292 and 303G ”.

(7)[^key-d5968b145c1941ec6d13f2205a1a5c40] In subsection (2)(e), for “and 293A” substitute “ , 293A, 303H and 303I ”.

(8)[^key-d5968b145c1941ec6d13f2205a1a5c40] In subsection (2)(f), in the words before sub-paragraph (i), after “295(2)” insert “ or 303L(1) ”.

(9)[^key-d5968b145c1941ec6d13f2205a1a5c40] In subsection (2)(f)(ii), after “298” insert “ or (as the case may be) 303O ”.

(10)[^key-d5968b145c1941ec6d13f2205a1a5c40] In subsection (2)(g), after “298” insert “ , 303O or 303Z14 ”.

(11)[^key-d5968b145c1941ec6d13f2205a1a5c40] In subsection (2)(h), after “302” insert “ , 303W or 303Z18 ”.
