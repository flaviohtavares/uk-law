# Criminal Finances Act 2017, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2017/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Accredited financial investigators[^c22725341]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-3bfbaa3aa6c942995dceca8c2473164d][^c-a7fb0d315323741d1c5fa7f6ee7264ed] The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 is amended as follows.

(2)[^c-854ff4df3fb61a60267bc83173458277] In section 47G (appropriate approval for exercise of search and seizure powers in England and Wales), in subsection (3), after paragraph (b) insert—

> - (ba) in relation to the exercise of a power by an accredited financial investigator who is—
>   - (i) a member of the civilian staff of a police force in England and Wales (including the metropolitan police force), within the meaning of Part 1 of the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011, or
>   - (ii) a member of staff of the City of London police force,
>
>   a senior police officer,

.

(3)[^c-e4260550bd7529bcf17bff334f38dbaa] In section 195G (appropriate approval for exercise of search and seizure powers in Northern Ireland), in subsection (3), after paragraph (b) insert—

> - (ba) in relation to the exercise of a power by an accredited financial investigator who is a member of staff of the Police Service of Northern Ireland, a senior police officer,

.

(4)[^c-854ff4df3fb61a60267bc83173458277][^c-136b9aaf43d3221d2a8d5199dcab620b] In section 290 (prior approval for exercise of search powers in relation to cash), in subsection (4), after paragraph (b) insert—

> - (ba) in relation to the exercise of a power by an accredited financial investigator who is—
>   - (i) a member of the civilian staff of a police force in England and Wales (including the metropolitan police force), within the meaning of Part 1 of the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011,
>   - (ii) a member of staff of the City of London police force, or
>   - (iii) a member of staff of the Police Service of Northern Ireland,
>
>   a senior police officer,

.
