## 120. Appointment etc. of rent officers.

*Extent: E+W*

Section 63 of the [^c12873041] Rent Act 1977 (schemes for the appointment of rent officers) shall have effect subject to the amendments in Part I of Schedule 14 to this Act and after section 64 of that Act there shall be inserted the sections set out in Part II of that Schedule.
