# Housing Act 1988, section 140

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 140. Amendments and repeals.[^c12874001]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Schedule 17 to this Act, which contains minor amendments and amendments consequential on the provisions of this Act and the [^c12874011]Housing (Scotland) Act 1988, shall have effect [^c12874021]. . ..

(2) The enactments specified in Schedule 18 to this Act, which include some that are spent, are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule, but subject to any provision at the end of that Schedule and to any saving in Chapter V of Part I of or Schedule 17 to this Act.
