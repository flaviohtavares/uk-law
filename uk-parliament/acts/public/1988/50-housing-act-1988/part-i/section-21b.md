# Housing Act 1988, section 21B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 21B. Requirement for landlord to provide prescribed information (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0e50142a2db1b497254b11ce8bb89d96][^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f][^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations require information about the rights and responsibilities of a landlord and a tenant under an assured shorthold tenancy of a dwelling-house in England (or any related matters) to be given by a landlord under such a tenancy, or a person acting on behalf of such a landlord, to the tenant under such a tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) require the information to be given in the form of a document produced by the Secretary of State or another person, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) provide that the document to be given is the version that has effect at the time the requirement applies, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (c) specify cases where the requirement does not apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(3) A notice under subsection (1) or (4) of section 21 may not be given in relation to an assured shorthold tenancy of a dwelling-house in England at a time when the landlord is in breach of a requirement imposed by regulations under subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(4) A statutory instrument containing regulations made under subsection (1) is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
