## 22. Reference of excessive rents to (affected by repeal, see note) appropriate tribunal (affected by repeal, see note).[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f][^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f][^c-dcec4c314cd7ee03c8e2f3e96701e522][^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to section 23 and subsection (2) below, the tenant under an assured shorthold tenancy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] [^c12866271]. . . may make an application in the prescribed form to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] the appropriate tribunal[^c-cf8ebab24ef3cd2b10518fd097da680b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] for a determination of the rent which, in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] the appropriate tribunal’s[^c-8658282926598bac81f723749fd4d574] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] opinion, the landlord might reasonably be expected to obtain under the assured shorthold tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(2) No application may be made under this section if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) the rent payable under the tenancy is a rent previously determined under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] [^c12866281]. . .
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c12866291][^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] the tenancy is one to which section 19A above applies and more than six months have elapsed since the beginning of the tenancy or, in the case of a replacement tenancy, since the beginning of the original tenancy; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) the tenancy is an assured shorthold tenancy falling within subsection (4) of section 20 above (and, accordingly, is one in respect of which notice need not have been served as mentioned in subsection (2) of that section). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(3) Where an application is made to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] the appropriate tribunal[^c-1d55c6dd4e703e8e94e5dcb7e270d575] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] under subsection (1) above with respect to the rent under an assured shorthold tenancy, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] the appropriate tribunal[^c-b0fcfefdc5b3c677f01c89c388144ca1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] shall not make such a determination as is referred to in that subsection unless they consider— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) that there is a sufficient number of similar dwelling-houses in the locality let on assured tenancies (whether shorthold or not); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) that the rent payable under the assured shorthold tenancy in question is significantly higher than the rent which the landlord might reasonably be expected to be able to obtain under the tenancy, having regard to the level of rents payable under the tenancies referred to in paragraph (a) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(4) Where, on an application under this section, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] the appropriate tribunal[^c-d75e4c0a07672d9ab19c15a173378ccf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] make a determination of a rent for an assured shorthold tenancy— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) the determination shall have effect from such date as *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] the appropriate tribunal[^c-6473249e6d10151e8f89f72cea8ba99a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] may direct, not being earlier than the date of the application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) if, at any time on or after the determination takes effect, the rent which, apart from this paragraph, would be payable under the tenancy exceeds the rent so determined, the excess shall be irrecoverable from the tenant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (c) no notice may be served under section 13(2) above with respect to a tenancy of the dwelling-house in question until after the first anniversary of the date on which the determination takes effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(5) Subsections (4), (5) and (8) of section 14 above apply in relation to a determination of rent under this section as they apply in relation to a determination under that section and, accordingly, where subsection (5) of that section applies, any reference in subsection (4)(b) above to rent is a reference to rent exclusive of the amount attributable to rates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(5A)[^c12866301] Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) an assured tenancy ceases to be an assured shorthold tenancy by virtue of falling within paragraph 2 of Schedule 2A to this Act, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) at the time when it so ceases to be an assured shorthold tenancy there is pending before *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] the appropriate tribunal[^c-58fe0eaf5acd89bab4d37868f41bcc50] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] an application in relation to it under this section, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

the fact that it so ceases to be an assured shorthold tenancy shall, in relation to that application, be disregarded for the purposes of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(6)[^c12866311] In subsection (2)(aa) above, the references to the original tenancy and to a replacement tenancy shall be construed in accordance with subsections (6) and (7) respectively of section 21 above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
