# Housing Act 1988, section 6A

## 6A. Demotion because of anti-social behaviour (affected by repeal, see note)[^c18692291][^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f][^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies to an assured tenancy if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]—[^c-f1e107e3ec8f1cd85ba923cb7ef99b83] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

- (a) the landlord is a non-profit registered provider of social housing, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
- (b) the landlord is a profit-making registered provider of social housing and the dwelling-house let on the tenancy is social housing within the meaning of Part 2 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
- (c)

the landlord is a registered social landlord. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

(2) The landlord may apply to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f] the county court[^c-b82ba771b2230dd84f2fea2771f2ed84] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f] for a demotion order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

(3) A demotion order has the following effect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

- (a) the assured tenancy is terminated with effect from the date specified in the order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
- (b) if the tenant remains in occupation of the dwelling-house after that date a demoted tenancy is created with effect from that date; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
- (c) it is a term of the demoted tenancy that any arrears of rent payable at the termination of the assured tenancy become payable under the demoted tenancy; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
- (d) it is also a term of the demoted tenancy that any rent paid in advance or overpaid at the termination of the assured tenancy is credited to the tenant’s liability to pay rent under the demoted tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

(4) The court must not make a demotion order unless it is satisfied— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

- (a)[^c-a6938bc63199160cce03f828cbb88e9a] that the tenant or a person residing in or visiting the dwelling-house has engaged or has threatened to engage in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
  - (i) conduct that is capable of causing nuisance or annoyance to some person (who need not be a particular identified person) and that directly or indirectly relates to or affects the landlord's housing management functions, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f] conduct that consists of or involves using housing accommodation owned or managed by the landlord for an unlawful purpose, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
- (b) that it is reasonable to make the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

(5) The court must not entertain proceedings for a demotion order unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

- (a) the landlord has served on the tenant a notice under subsection (6), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
- (b) the court thinks it is just and equitable to dispense with the requirement of the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

(6) The notice must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

- (a) give particulars of the conduct in respect of which the order is sought; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
- (b) state that the proceedings will not begin before the date specified in the notice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
- (c) state that the proceedings will not begin after the end of the period of twelve months beginning with the date of service of the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

(7) The date specified for the purposes of subsection (6)(b) must not be before the end of the period of two weeks beginning with the date of service of the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

(8) Each of the following has effect in respect of a demoted tenancy at the time it is created by virtue of an order under this section as it has effect in relation to the assured tenancy at the time it is terminated by virtue of the order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

- (a) the parties to the tenancy; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
- (b) the period of the tenancy; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
- (c) the amount of the rent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
- (d) the dates on which the rent is payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

(9) Subsection (8)(b) does not apply if the assured tenancy was for a fixed term and in such a case the demoted tenancy is a weekly periodic tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

(10) If the landlord of the demoted tenancy serves on the tenant a statement of any other express terms of the assured tenancy which are to apply to the demoted tenancy such terms are also terms of the demoted tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

(10A)[^c-24e59fdb084388b321042f0877577c92] In subsection (4)(a)(ii) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]**housing accommodation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]**” includes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

- (a) flats, lodging-houses and hostels; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
- (b) any yard, garden, outhouses and appurtenances belonging to the accommodation or usually enjoyed with it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
- (c) any common areas used in connection with the accommodation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]

(11) For the purposes of this section a demoted tenancy is a tenancy to which section 20B of the Housing Act 1988 applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3615db5537050fa8c2492b63001a725f]
