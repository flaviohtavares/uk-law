# Housing Act 1988, section 21

## 21. Recovery of possession on expiry or termination of assured shorthold tenancy. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f][^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Without prejudice to any right of the landlord under an assured shorthold tenancy to recover possession of the dwelling-house let on the tenancy in accordance with Chapter I above, on or after the coming to an end of an assured shorthold tenancy which was a fixed term tenancy, a court shall make an order for possession of the dwelling-house if it is satisfied— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) that the assured shorthold tenancy has come to an end and no further assured tenancy (whether shorthold or not) is for the time being in existence, other than *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] an assured shorthold periodic tenancy (whether statutory or not)[^c12866231] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) the landlord or, in the case of joint landlords, at least one of them has given to the tenant not less than two months’ notice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] in writing[^c12866241] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] stating that he requires possession of the dwelling-house. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(1A)[^c-47c19043463f115b55f42292f8867558] Subsection (1B) applies to an assured shorthold tenancy of a dwelling-house in England if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) it is a fixed term tenancy for a term certain of not less than two years, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) the landlord is a private registered provider of social housing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(1B) The court may not make an order for possession of the dwelling-house let on the tenancy unless the landlord has given to the tenant not less than six months' notice in writing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) stating that the landlord does not propose to grant another tenancy on the expiry of the fixed term tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) informing the tenant of how to obtain help or advice about the notice and, in particular, of any obligation of the landlord to provide help or advice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(2) A notice under paragraph (b) of subsection (1) above may be given before or on the day on which the tenancy comes to an end; and that subsection shall have effect notwithstanding that on the coming to an end of the fixed term tenancy a statutory periodic tenancy arises. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(3) Where a court makes an order for possession of a dwelling-house by virtue of subsection (1) above, any statutory periodic tenancy which has arisen on the coming to an end of the assured shorthold tenancy shall end (without further notice and regardless of the period) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] in accordance with section 5(1A)[^c20515131] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f].

(4) Without prejudice to any such right as is referred to in subsection (1) above, a court shall make an order for possession of a dwelling-house let on an assured shorthold tenancy which is a periodic tenancy if the court is satisfied— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) that the landlord or, in the case of joint landlords, at least one of them has given to the tenant a notice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] in writing[^c12866251] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] stating that, after a date specified in the notice, being the last day of a period of the tenancy and not earlier than two months after the date the notice was given, possession of the dwelling-house is required by virtue of this section; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) that the date specified in the notice under paragraph (a) above is not earlier than the earliest day on which, apart from section 5(1) above, the tenancy could be brought to an end by a notice to quit given by the landlord on the same date as the notice under paragraph (a) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(4ZA (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-13c02eb6e9210ebc5efec0cb8c135466][^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] In the case of a dwelling-house in England, subsection (4)(a) above has effect with the omission of the requirement for the date specified in the notice to be the last day of a period of the tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(4A)[^c20515181] Where a court makes an order for possession of a dwelling-house by virtue of subsection (4) above, the assured shorthold tenancy shall end in accordance with section 5(1A). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(4B)[^c-cb602a007a177dc8e42244dc12696644] A notice under subsection (1) or (4) may not be given in relation to an assured shorthold tenancy of a dwelling-house in England— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) in the case of a tenancy which is not a replacement tenancy, within the period of four months beginning with the day on which the tenancy began, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) in the case of a replacement tenancy, within the period of four months beginning with the day on which the original tenancy began. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(4C) Subsection (4B) does not apply where the tenancy has arisen due to section 5(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(4D) Subject to subsection (4E), proceedings for an order for possession under this section in relation to a dwelling-house in England may not be begun after the end of the period of six months beginning with the date on which the notice was given under subsection (1) or (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(4E) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) a notice under subsection (4) has been given in relation to a dwelling-house in England, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) paragraph (b) of that subsection requires the date specified in the notice to be more than two months after the date the notice was given, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

proceedings for an order for possession under this section may not be begun after the end of the period of four months beginning with the date specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(5)[^c12866261] Where an order for possession under subsection (1) or (4) above is made in relation to a dwelling-house let on a tenancy to which section 19A above applies, the order may not be made so as to take effect earlier than— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) in the case of a tenancy which is not a replacement tenancy, six months after the beginning of the tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) in the case of a replacement tenancy, six months after the beginning of the original tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(5A)[^c18719311] Subsection (5) above does not apply to an assured shorthold tenancy to which section 20B (demoted assured shorthold tenancies) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(6) In *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] subsections (4B)(b) and[^c-2a91cbea9a63d7ee4071628b271e880b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] (5)(b) above, the reference to the original tenancy is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) where the replacement tenancy came into being on the coming to an end of a tenancy which was not a replacement tenancy, to the immediately preceding tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) where there have been successive replacement tenancies, to the tenancy immediately preceding the first in the succession of replacement tenancies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(7) For the purposes of this section, a replacement tenancy is a tenancy— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) which comes into being on the coming to an end of an assured shorthold tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) under which, on its coming into being— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
  - (i) the landlord and tenant are the same as under the earlier tenancy as at its coming to an end, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] the premises let are the same or substantially the same as those let under the earlier tenancy as at that time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(8)[^c-89f932b1dbc5b0e6f38f6dd690240249] The Secretary of State may by regulations made by statutory instrument prescribe the form of a notice under subsection (1) or (4) given in relation to an assured shorthold tenancy of a dwelling-house in England. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(9) A statutory instrument containing regulations made under subsection (8) is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
