# Housing Act 1988, section 20B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 20B. Demoted assured shorthold tenancies (affected by repeal, see note)[^c18718081][^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f][^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An assured tenancy is an assured shorthold tenancy to which this section applies (a demoted assured shorthold tenancy) if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) the tenancy is created by virtue of an order of the court under section 82A of the Housing Act 1985 or section 6A of this Act (a demotion order), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) the landlord is *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] a private registered provider of social housing or[^key-919bdb487650ff59de310648266238b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] a registered social landlord. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(2) At the end of the period of one year starting with the day when the demotion order takes effect a demoted assured shorthold tenancy ceases to be an assured shorthold tenancy unless subsection (3) applies *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f], but see section 20C[^key-3a11190e5daf8ada024713ba9707b522] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f].

(3) This subsection applies if before the end of the period mentioned in subsection (2) the landlord gives notice of proceedings for possession of the dwelling house. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(4) If subsection (3) applies the tenancy continues to be a demoted assured shorthold tenancy until the end of the period mentioned in subsection (2) or (if later) until one of the following occurs— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) the notice of proceedings for possession is withdrawn; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) the proceedings are determined in favour of the tenant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (c) the period of six months beginning with the date on which the notice is given ends and no proceedings for possession have been brought. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(5) Registered social landlord has the same meaning as in Part 1 of the Housing Act 1996. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
