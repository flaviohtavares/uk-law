# Housing Act 1988, section 14A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/50

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 14A. Interim increase before 1st April 1994 of rent under assured periodic tenancies in certain cases where landlord liable for council tax (affected by repeal, see note)[^c12866101][^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400][^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In any case where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400]

- (a) under Part I of the Local Government Finance Act 1992 the landlord of a dwelling-house let under an assured tenancy to which section 13 above applies or a superior landlord is liable to pay council tax in respect of a dwelling (within the meaning of that Part of that Act) which includes that dwelling-house, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400]
- (b) under the terms of the tenancy (or an agreement collateral to the tenancy) the tenant is liable to make payments to the landlord in respect of council tax, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400]
- (c) the case falls within subsection (2) or subsection (3) below, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400]
- (d) no previous notice under this subsection has been served in relation to the dwelling-house, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400]

the landlord may serve on the tenant a notice in the prescribed form proposing an increased rent to take account of the tenant’s liability to make payments to the landlord in respect of council tax, such increased rent to take effect at the beginning of a new period of the tenancy specified in the notice being a period beginning not earlier than one month after the date on which the notice was served. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400]

(2) The case falls within this subsection if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400]

- (a) the rent under the tenancy has previously been increased by virtue of a notice under section 13(2) above or a determination under section 14 above, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400]
- (b) the first anniversary of the date on which the increased rent took effect has not yet occurred. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400]

(3) The case falls within this subsection if a notice has been served under section 13(2) above before 1st April 1993 but no increased rent has taken effect before that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400]

(4) No notice may be served under subsection (1) above after 31st March 1994. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400]

(5) Where a notice is served under subsection (1) above, the new rent specified in the notice shall take effect as mentioned in the notice unless, before the beginning of the new period specified in the notice— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400]

- (a) the tenant by an application in the prescribed form refers the notice to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400] the appropriate tribunal[^c-253548416ee8c4d86670cd47a03cf994] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400], or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400]
- (b) the landlord and the tenant agree on a variation of the rent which is different from that proposed in the notice or agree that the rent should not be varied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400]

(6) Nothing in this section (or in section 14B below) affects the right of the landlord and the tenant under an assured tenancy to vary by agreement any term of the tenancy (including a term relating to rent). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-946bd0d57a50ad12d6a3c2a87738f400]
