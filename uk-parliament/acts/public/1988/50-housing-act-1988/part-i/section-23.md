> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Termination of (affected by repeal, see note) tribunal’s (affected by repeal, see note) functions. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f][^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f][^c-022c3478a2b7738f94988403c7e34c09][^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f][^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the Secretary of State by order made by statutory instrument so provides, section 22 above shall not apply in such cases or to tenancies of dwelling-houses in such areas or in such other circumstances as may be specified in the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(2) An order under this section may contain such transitional, incidental and supplementary provisions as appear to the Secretary of State to be desirable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(3) No order shall be made under this section unless a draft of the order has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
