# Housing Act 1988, section 21A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 21A. Compliance with prescribed legal requirements (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d244525997e5004539b5b99bca1a3a0f][^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f][^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A notice under subsection (1) or (4) of section 21 may not be given in relation to an assured shorthold tenancy of a dwelling-house in England at a time when the landlord is in breach of a prescribed requirement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(2) The requirements that may be prescribed are requirements imposed on landlords by any enactment and which relate to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) the condition of dwelling-houses or their common parts, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) the health and safety of occupiers of dwelling-houses, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (c) the energy performance of dwelling-houses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(3) In subsection (2) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] **enactment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]** ” includes an enactment contained in subordinate legislation within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(4) For the purposes of subsection (2)(a) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f] **common parts *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]** ” has the same meaning as in Ground 13 in Part 2 of Schedule 2. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(5) A statutory instrument containing regulations made under this section is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
