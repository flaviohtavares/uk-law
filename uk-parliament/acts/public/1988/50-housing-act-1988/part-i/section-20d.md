# Housing Act 1988, section 20D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20D. Assured shorthold tenancies following family intervention tenancies (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-38a9e653b660b088674e3c0cdeafd492][^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f][^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An assured tenancy that arises by virtue of a notice under paragraph 12ZA(2) of Schedule 1 in respect of a family intervention tenancy is an assured shorthold tenancy if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) the landlord under the assured tenancy is a private registered provider of social housing, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) the dwelling-house is in England, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (c) the family intervention tenancy was granted to a person on the coming to an end of an assured shorthold tenancy under which the person was a tenant, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (d) the notice states that the family intervention tenancy is to be regarded as an assured shorthold tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(2) This section does not apply if the family intervention tenancy was granted before the coming into force of section 163(3) of the Localism Act 2011. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
