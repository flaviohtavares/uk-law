## 20. Assured shorthold tenancies: pre-Housing Act 1996 tenancies. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c12866181][^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f][^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to subsection (3) below, an assured tenancy which is not one to which section 19A above applies is an assured shorthold tenancy if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) it is a fixed term tenancy granted for a term certain of not less than six months, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) there is no power for the landlord to determine the tenancy at any time earlier than six months from the beginning of the tenancy; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (c) a notice in respect of it is served as mentioned in subsection (2) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(2) The notice referred to in subsection (1)(c) above is one which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) is in such form as may be prescribed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) is served before the assured tenancy is entered into; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (c) is served by the person who is to be the landlord under the assured tenancy on the person who is to be the tenant under that tenancy; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (d) states that the assured tenancy to which it relates is to be a shorthold tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(3) Notwithstanding anything in subsection (1) above, where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) immediately before a tenancy (in this subsection referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]**the new tenancy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]**”) is granted, the person to whom it is granted or, as the case may be, at least one of the persons to whom it is granted was a tenant under an assured tenancy which was not a shorthold tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) the new tenancy is granted by the person who, immediately before the beginning of the tenancy, was the landlord under the assured tenancy referred to in paragraph (a) above, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

the new tenancy cannot be an assured shorthold tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(4) Subject to subsection (5) below, if, on the coming to an end of an assured shorthold tenancy (including a tenancy which was an assured shorthold but ceased to be assured before it came to an end), a new tenancy of the same or substantially the same premises comes into being under which the landlord and the tenant are the same as at the coming to an end of the earlier tenancy, then, if and so long as the new tenancy is an assured tenancy, it shall be an assured shorthold tenancy, whether or not it fulfils the conditions in paragraphs (a) to (c) of subsection (1) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(5) Subsection (4) above does not apply if, before the new tenancy is entered into (or, in the case of a statutory periodic tenancy, takes effect in possession), the landlord serves notice on the tenant that the new tenancy is not to be a shorthold tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(5A)[^c12866191] Subsections (3) and (4) above do not apply where the new tenancy is one to which section 19A above applies *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

(6) In the case of joint landlords— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) the reference in subsection (2)(c) above to the person who is to be the landlord is a reference to at least one of the persons who are to be joint landlords; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) the reference in subsection (5) above to the landlord is a reference to at least one of the joint landlords. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
