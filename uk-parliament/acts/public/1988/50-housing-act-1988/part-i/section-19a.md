# Housing Act 1988, section 19A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 19A. Assured shorthold tenancies: post-Housing Act 1996 tenancies. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c12866171][^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f][^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

*Extent: E+W*

An assured tenancy which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

- (a) is entered into on or after the day on which section 96 of the Housing Act 1996 comes into force (otherwise than pursuant to a contract made before that day), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
- (b) comes into being by virtue of section 5 above on the coming to an end of an assured tenancy within paragraph (a) above, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]

is an assured shorthold tenancy unless it falls within any paragraph in Schedule 2A to this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fc20bf788fddf36d09dbf266cb7b193f]
