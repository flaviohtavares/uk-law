> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/50

## 16H. Landlords acting through others[^key-ccd11368d8177d7a3387995799ae416b]

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in section 16D or 16E prevents a landlord from fulfilling or contravening an obligation through another person acting on their behalf.
