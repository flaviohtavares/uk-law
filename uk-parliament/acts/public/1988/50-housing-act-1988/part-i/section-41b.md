# Housing Act 1988, section 41B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/50
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 41B. Provision of information as to exemption from council tax[^c12867501]

*Extent: E+W*

A billing authority within the meaning of Part I of the Local Government Finance Act 1992 shall, if so requested in writing by a rent officer or the appropriate tribunal[^c-582abcadf198648a606fd77da2bd654c] in connection with his or their functions under any enactment, inform the rent officer or the appropriate tribunal in writing whether or not a particular dwelling (within the meaning of Part I of the Local Government Finance Act 1992) is, or was at any time specified in the request, an exempt dwelling for the purposes of that Part of that Act.
