## 6. Fixing of terms of statutory periodic tenancy. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b][^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this section, in relation to a statutory periodic tenancy,— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]

- (a) “**the former tenancy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]**” means the fixed term tenancy on the coming to an end of which the statutory periodic tenancy arises; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]
- (b) “**the implied terms *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]**” means the terms of the tenancy which have effect by virtue of section 5(3)(e) above, other than terms as to the amount of the rent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]

but nothing in the following provisions of this section applies to a statutory periodic tenancy at a time when, by virtue of paragraph 11 or paragraph 12 in Part 1 of Schedule 1 to this Act, it cannot be an assured tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]

(2) Not later than the first anniversary of the day on which the former tenancy came to an end, the landlord may serve on the tenant, or the tenant may serve on the landlord, a notice in the prescribed form proposing terms of the statutory periodic tenancy different from the implied terms and, if the landlord or the tenant considers it appropriate, proposing an adjustment of the amount of the rent to take account of the proposed terms. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]

(3) Where a notice has been served under subsection (2) above,— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]

- (a) within the period of three months beginning on the date on which the notice was served on him, the landlord or the tenant, as the case may be, may, by an application in the prescribed form, refer the notice to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] the appropriate tribunal[^key-d244d181d391f73fdaf2905ed3b5454a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] under subsection (4) below; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]
- (b) if the notice is not so referred, then, with effect from such date, not falling within the period referred to in paragraph (a) above, as may be specified in the notice, the terms proposed in the notice shall become terms of the tenancy in substitution for any of the implied terms dealing with the same subject matter and the amount of the rent shall be varied in accordance with any adjustment so proposed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]

(4) Where a notice under subsection (2) above is referred to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] the appropriate tribunal[^key-89eb43986f4f75d92d1f3fef321b002a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b], the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] appropriate tribunal[^key-20904a0b9cc5d7f579aefe20a6b36cc4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] shall consider the terms proposed in the notice and shall determine whether those terms, or some other terms (dealing with the same subject matter as the proposed terms), are such as, in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] the appropriate tribunal’s[^key-7be0ce77fb7282e08424cb2ab201857c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] opinion, might reasonably be expected to be found in an assured periodic tenancy of the dwelling-house concerned, being a tenancy— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]

- (a) which begins on the coming to an end of the former tenancy; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]
- (b) which is granted by a willing landlord on terms which, except in so far as they relate to the subject matter of the proposed terms, are those of the statutory periodic tenancy at the time of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] the appropriate tribunal’s *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] consideration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]

(5) Whether or not a notice under subsection (2) above proposes an adjustment of the amount of the rent under the statutory periodic tenancy, where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] the appropriate tribunal[^key-92248fd9e40e4c029e0b2ac11e75fdac] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] determine any terms under subsection (4) above, they shall, if they consider it appropriate, specify such an adjustment to take account of the terms so determined. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]

(6) In making a determination under subsection (4) above, or specifying an adjustment of an amount of rent under subsection (5) above, there shall be disregarded any effect on the terms or the amount of the rent attributable to the granting of a tenancy to a sitting tenant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]

(7) Where a notice under subsection (2) above is referred to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] the appropriate tribunal[^key-6fe6fdb2d5c7a9ab23162e930712c63d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b], then, unless the landlord and the tenant otherwise agree, with effect from such date as the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] appropriate tribunal[^key-953edb10a43f5a7c020be6346dd2dbee] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] may direct— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]

- (a) the terms determined by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] appropriate tribunal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] shall become terms of the statutory periodic tenancy in substitution for any of the implied terms dealing with the same subject matter; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]
- (b) the amount of the rent under the statutory periodic tenancy shall be altered to accord with any adjustment specified by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] appropriate tribunal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b];

but for the purposes of paragraph (b) above the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] appropriate tribunal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] shall not direct a date earlier than the date specified, in accordance with subsection (3)(b) above, in the notice referred to them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]

(8) Nothing in this section requires *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] the appropriate tribunal[^key-0a244205a8963891e945387047e2e901] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b] to continue with a determination under subsection (4) above if the landlord and tenant give notice in writing that they no longer require such a determination or if the tenancy has come to an end. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a760ef349982638d0f5378b0191ba04b]
