## 14B. Interim determination of rent by (affected by repeal, see note) the appropriate tribunal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c12866111][^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df][^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df][^c-8d312c77d915b86bf602b4c5de63717c][^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where, under subsection (5)(a) of section 14A above, a tenant refers to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df] the appropriate tribunal[^c-8e5e2ae166980c05faced08381e6b953] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df] a notice under subsection (1) of that section, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df] appropriate tribunal[^c-83824905498e30f8ec116ed27f95f8bf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df] shall determine the amount by which, having regard to the provisions of section 14(3A) above, the existing rent might reasonably be increased to take account of the tenant’s liability to make payments to the landlord in respect of council tax. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df]

(2) Where a notice under section 14A(1) above has been referred to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df] the appropriate tribunal[^c-d8bae3384ca3b08ae7989f15d0ca48bc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df], then, unless the landlord and the tenant otherwise agree, the existing rent shall be increased by the amount determined by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df] appropriate tribunal[^c-8fc89a5d3f869726a6c6e2762ef883a7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df] with effect from the beginning of the new period specified in the notice or, if it appears to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df] appropriate tribunal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df] that that would cause undue hardship to the tenant, with effect from such later date (not being later than the date the increase is determined) as the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df] appropriate tribunal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df] may direct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df]

(3) In any case where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df]

- (a) the appropriate tribunal[^c-26c8cac63bdc35df01fd6e01780af54c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df] have before them at the same time the reference of a notice under section 13(2) above relating to a tenancy (in this subsection referred to as “the section 13 reference”) and the reference of a notice under section 14A(1) above relating to the same tenancy (in this subsection referred to as “the section 14A reference”); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df]
- (b) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df] appropriate tribunal[^c-faf2796111cc06df5291df98bed088df] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df] propose to hear the two references together, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df]

the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df] appropriate tribunal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df] shall make a determination in relation to the section 13 reference before making their determination in relation to the section 14A reference, and if in such a case the date specified in the notice under section 13(2) above is later than the date specified in the notice under section 14A(1) above, the rent determined under the section 14A reference shall not take effect until the date specified in the notice under section 13(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df]

(4) In this section “rent” has the same meaning as in section 14 above; and section 14(4) above applies to a determination under this section as it applies to a determination under that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8af3a7a8d2ba1d4b12c7318e1c007df]
