# Dartford-Thurrock Crossing Act 1988, section 21

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21. List of tolls to be exhibited.

*Extent: E+W*

A list of the tolls for the time being leviable shall at all times be exhibited in a conspicuous position within a reasonable distance of any place at which tolls are payable.
