# Dartford-Thurrock Crossing Act 1988, section 30

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30. Planning permission.

*Extent: E+W*

If any works for or in connection with the exercise of any functions under this Part of this Act or the maintenance or improvement of any part of the crossing are carried out on any land otherwise than by or on behalf of the Secretary of State, they shall not be taken for the purposes of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^c8137891] to involve development of the land.
