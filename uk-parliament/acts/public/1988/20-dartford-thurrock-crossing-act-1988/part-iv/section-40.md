# Dartford-Thurrock Crossing Act 1988, section 40

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Rating.

*Extent: E+W*

No part of the crossing shall be liable to be rated or to be included in any valuation list or in any rate.
