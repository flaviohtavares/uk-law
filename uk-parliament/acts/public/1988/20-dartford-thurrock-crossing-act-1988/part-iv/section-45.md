# Dartford-Thurrock Crossing Act 1988, section 45

> Text as at 2026-06-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45. Financial provision.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament any expenses of the Secretary of State under this Act and any increase attributable to this Act in the sums so payable under any other enactment.

(2) Any sums received by or on behalf of the Secretary of State under this Act shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund.

(3) Any sums paid to the person appointed during any toll extension period under paragraph 4 of Schedule 6 to this Act—

- (a) in respect of tolls or charges payable by virtue of this Act; or
- (b) under any composition agreement under section 20 of this Act;

shall be taken to be received by that person on behalf of the Secretary of State.
