## 187. Interpretation of sections 185 and 186 and Schedules 9 and 10.[^c22180881][^c22181531][^c23450551]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) [^c21579891]In sections 185 and 186, this section and Schedules 9 and 10 “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**the relevant provisions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” means those sections (including this section) and Schedules. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]

(2) For the purposes of the relevant provisions, except where the context otherwise requires— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]

- “**appropriate percentage *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” shall be construed in accordance with paragraph 3 of Schedule 10; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**approved *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**”, in relation to a scheme, means approved under Schedule 9; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**associated company *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” has the same meaning as in section 416, except that, for the purposes of paragraph 23 of Schedule 9, subsection (1) of that section shall have effect with the omission of the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491] “ or at any time within one year previously ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491];
- “**bonus date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” has the meaning given by paragraph 17 of Schedule 9; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**capital receipt *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” means money or money’s worth to which the trustees of or a participant in a profit sharing scheme become or becomes entitled as mentioned in section 186(3), but subject to paragraph 4 of Schedule 10; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**certified contractual savings scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” has the meaning given by section 326; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**control *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” has the same meaning as in section 840; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**grantor *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**”, in relation to any scheme, means the company which has established the scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**group scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” and, in relation to such a scheme, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**participating company *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” have the meanings given by paragraph 1(3) and (4) of Schedule 9; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**initial market value *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**”, in relation to shares in a profit sharing scheme, has the meaning given by paragraph 30(4) of Schedule 9; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**locked-in value *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**”, in relation to any shares, shall be construed in accordance with section 186(5); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**market value *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” has the same meaning as in Part VIII of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491] 1992 Act[^c21579901] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491];
- “**new holding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” has the meaning given by section *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491] 126(1)(b) of the 1992 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491];
- “**participant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**”, in relation to a profit sharing scheme, means an individual to whom the trustees of the scheme have appropriated shares; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**participant’s shares *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**”, in relation to a participant in a profit sharing scheme, means, subject to paragraph 5(4) of Schedule 10, shares which have been appropriated to the participant by the trustees; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- [^c22162681]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**period of retention *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” has the meaning given by paragraph 2 of Schedule 10; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**release date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**”, in relation to any of the shares of a participant in a profit sharing scheme, means the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491] third[^c22180981] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491] anniversary of the date on which they were appropriated to him; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**relevant amount *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**”, in relation to a participant in a profit sharing scheme, means an amount which is *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491] not less than £3,000 and not more than £8,000[^c21579931] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491] but which, subject to that, is 10 per cent. of his salary (determined under subsection (5) below) for the year of assessment in question or the preceding year of assessment, whichever is the greater; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**relevant requirements *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” has the meaning given by paragraph 1 of Schedule 9; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**savings-related share option scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” has the meaning given by paragraph 1 of Schedule 9; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” means a savings-related share option scheme, a share option scheme which is not a savings-related share option scheme or a profit sharing scheme, as the context may require; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**shares *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” includes stock; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- [^c21579941]“**specified age *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**”, in relation to a scheme, means the age specified in pursuance of paragraph 8A of Schedule 9 as the specified age for the purposes of the scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**the trustees *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**”, in relation to an approved profit sharing scheme or the shares of a participant in such a scheme, means the body of persons for the establishment of which the scheme must provide as mentioned in paragraph 30 of Schedule 9; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- “**just instrument *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**”, in relation to an approved profit sharing scheme, means the instrument referred to in paragraph 30(1)(c) of Schedule 9. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]

(3) [^c21579951]For the purposes of the application of the relevant provisions in relation to any share option scheme or profit sharing scheme, a person has a material interest *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491] in a company if he, either on his own or with one or more associates, or if any associate of his with or without such other associates,—[^c21579961] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]

- (a) is the beneficial owner of, or able, directly or through the medium of other companies, or by any other indirect means to control, more than 25 per cent., or in the case of a share option scheme which is not a savings-related share option scheme more than 10 per cent., of the ordinary share capital of the company, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]
- (b) where the company is a close company, possesses, or is entitled to acquire, such rights as would, in the event of the winding-up of the company or in any other circumstances, give an entitlement to receive more than 25 per cent., or in the case of a share option scheme which is not a savings-related share option scheme more than 10 per cent., of the assets which would then be available for distribution among the participators. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]

- In this subsection “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**associate *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” has the meaning given by section 417(3) and (4) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491] and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**participator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]**” has the meaning given by section 417(1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491].

(4) [^c21579981]Subsection (3) above shall have effect subject to the provisions of Part VI of Schedule 9. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]

(5) [^c21579991]For the purposes of subsection (2) above, a participant’s salary for a year of assessment means such of the emoluments of the office or employment by virtue of which he is entitled to participate in a profit sharing scheme as are liable to be paid in that year under deduction of tax pursuant to section 203 after deducting therefrom amounts included by virtue of Chapter II of this Part.

(6) [^c21580001]Section 839 shall apply for the purposes of the relevant provisions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]

(7) [^c21580011]For the purposes of the relevant provisions a company is a member of a consortium owning another company if it is one of a number of companies which between them beneficially own not less than three-quarters of the other company’s ordinary share capital and each of which beneficially owns not less than one-twentieth of that capital. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c23450491]

(8) [^c21580021]Where the disposal referred to in section 186(4) is made from a holding of shares which were appropriated to the participant at different times, then, in determining for the purposes of the relevant provisions—

- (a) the initial market value and the locked-in value of each of those shares, [^c22181261]. . .
- (b) [^c22181261]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9) [^c21580031]Any of the relevant provisions with respect to—

- (a) the order in which any of a participant’s shares are to be treated as disposed of for the purposes of those provisions, or
- (b) the shares in relation to which an event is to be treated as occurring for any such purpose,

shall have effect in relation to a profit sharing scheme notwithstanding any direction given to the trustees with respect to shares of a particular description or to shares appropriated to the participant at a particular time.

(10) [^c21580041]In the relevant provisions “**workers’ cooperative**” means a registered society[^c23451051], as defined in[^c23451011] section 1119 of CTA 2010[^c23451041], which is a cooperative society and the rules of which include provisions which secure—

- (a) that the only persons who may be members of it are those who are employed by, or by a subsidiary of, the society and those who are the trustees of its profit sharing scheme; and
- (b) that, subject to any provision about qualifications for membership which is from time to time made by the members of the society by reference to age, length of service or other factors of any description, all such persons may be members of the society;

and in this subsection “**cooperative society**” has the same meaning as in section 2 of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014[^c23451061] or, as the case may be, section 1 of[^c23451071] the [^c21580061]Industrial and Provident Societies Act (Northern Ireland) 1969.
