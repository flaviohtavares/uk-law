# Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988, section 274

## 274. Limits on relief under section 266.[^c23417781]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) [^c21585951]The aggregate of the premiums [^c23417661]. . . in respect of which relief is given to any person under section 266 shall not exceed £1,500 in any year of assessment or one-sixth of that person’s total income, whichever is the greater.

(2) [^c21585961]The aggregate of the relief given under section 266[^c23417681] in respect of premiums [^c23417701]. . . payable for securing any benefits other than capital sums on death shall not exceed the amount of the income tax calculated at 12.5%[^c23417721] on £100.

(3) [^c23417741]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) [^c21585991]War insurance premiums shall not be taken into account in calculating the limits of one-sixth of total income or of £100 mentioned in this section.

In this subsection “**war insurance premiums**” means any additional premium [^c23417761]. . . paid in order to extend an existing life insurance policy to risks arising from war or war service abroad, and any part of any premium [^c23417761]. . . paid in respect of a life insurance policy covering those risks, or either of them, which is[^c22749951] attributable to those risks, or either of them.
