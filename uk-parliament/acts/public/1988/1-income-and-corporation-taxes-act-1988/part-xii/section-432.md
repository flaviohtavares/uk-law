# Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988, section 432

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 432. Separation of different categories of business.[^c22794391][^c23602111][^c22786001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2007-07-19).**

(1) [^c22771521]Where an insurance company carries on life assurance business in conjunction with insurance business of any other category[^c22857411], the life assurance business shall, for the purposes of the Corporation Tax Acts, be treated as a separate business from any other category of business carried on by the company.
