# Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Stock and dividends in name of Treasury etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c23353441] No tax shall be chargeable in respect of the stock or dividends—

- (a) transferred, in pursuance of any Act of Parliament, to accounts in the books of the Bank of England in the name of the Treasury or the National Debt Commissioners, or
- (b) transferred, in pursuance of any Act of Parliament, to the Treasury or the National Debt Commissioners and in respect of which the Treasury or those Commissioners are entered as holder in the registers kept by the Registrar of Government Stock,

but the Bank of England and the Registrar of Government Stock shall each transmit to the Board an account of the total amount thereof in those books or registers as the case may be.

(2) No tax shall be chargeable in respect of the stock or dividends belonging to the Crown, in whatever name they may stand in the books of the Bank of England or in the registers kept by the Registrar of Government Stock[^c23353461].

(3)[^c21825931] In this section “dividends” means any interest, public annuities, dividends or shares of annuities.

(4)[^c23353481] In this section “**Registrar of Government Stock**” means the person or persons appointed in accordance with regulations under section 47(1)(b) of the Finance Act 1942 (see regulation 3 of the Government Stock Regulations 2004).
