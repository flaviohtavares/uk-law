## 376. Qualifying borrowers and qualifying lenders.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) [^c21595831]Subject to subsection (2) below, an individual is a qualifying borrower with respect to the interest on any loan.

(2) In relation to interest paid at a time when the borrower or the borrower’s husband or wife holds an office or employment which would, but for some special exemption or immunity from tax, be a taxable employment under Part 2 of ITEPA 2003 (as defined by section 66(3) of that Act)[^c23427621], the borrower is not a qualifying borrower.

(3) In subsection (2) above references to the borrower’s husband or wife do not include references to a separated husband or wife [^c22741351]. . . .

(4) [^c21595841]The following bodies are qualifying lenders:—

- (a) a building society;
- (b) a local authority;
- (c) the Bank of England;
- (d) [^c22741981]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^c22742071] a person who has permission under Part 4A[^c23438251] of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 to effect or carry out contracts of long-term insurance;
- (f) any company to which property and rights belonging to a trustee savings bank were transferred by section 3 of the [^c21595861]Trustee Savings Bank Act 1985;
- (g) [^c22742091]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (h) a development corporation within the meaning of the [^c21595881]New Towns Act 1981 or the [^c21595891]New Towns (Scotland) Act 1968;
- (j)[^c23438071] the Homes and Communities Agency;
- (ja)[^c23438231] the Greater London Authority so far as exercising its housing or regeneration functions or its new towns and urban development functions;
- (k)[^c23438211] the Regulator of Social Housing,
- (ka)[^c22740371] the Secretary of State if the loan is made by him under section 79 of the [^c22740411]Housing Associations Act 1985;
- (l) the Northern Ireland Housing Executive;
- (m) the Scottish Special Housing Association;
- (n) [^c22740391]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (o) the Church of England Pensions Board;
- (p)[^c22742111] any body which is for the time being registered under section 376A.
