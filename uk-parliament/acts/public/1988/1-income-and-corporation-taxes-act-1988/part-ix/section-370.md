## 370. Relevant loan interest.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) [^c21595181]Subject to this section and sections 373[^c22741051] to 376, in this Part “**relevant loan interest**” means interest which is paid and payable in the United Kingdom to a qualifying lender and to which subsection (2) [^c22740921]. . . below applies.

(2) Subject to subsection (4) below, this subsection applies to interest if, disregarding section 353(2) and any other provision applying to interest falling to be treated as relevant loan interest[^c22738311]—

- (a) it is interest falling within section [^c22740941]. . . 365; and
- (b) apart (where applicable) from[^c22766391] section [^c22740941]. . . 365(3), the whole of the interest would be eligible for relief under section 353[^c23428491];[^c22740991]. . .
- (c) [^c22740991]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) [^c22741001]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) [^c21595261]In determining whether subsection (2) above applies to any interest, section 365 shall[^c22741071] have effect as if the words “or the Republic of Ireland” were omitted.
