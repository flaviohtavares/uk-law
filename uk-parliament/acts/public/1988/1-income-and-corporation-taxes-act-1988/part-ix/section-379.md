# Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988, section 379

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 379.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In sections 369 to 378—

- “contracts of general insurance” means contracts which fall within Part I of Schedule 1 to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001 and “contracts of long-term insurance” means contracts which fall within Part II of that Schedule;[^c22742171]
- “**prescribed**” [^c22738471]. . . means prescribed by the Board;
- “**qualifying borrower**” has the meaning given by section 376(1) to (3);
- “**qualifying lender**” has the meaning given by section 376(4) [^c22741461]. . . ;
- “**regulations**” [^c22766421] . . . means regulations made by the Board under section 378;
- “**relevant loan interest**” has the meaning given by section 370(1);
- “[^c22741491]**separated**” means separated under an order of a court of competent jurisdiction or by deed of separation or in such circumstances that the separation is likely to be permanent.
