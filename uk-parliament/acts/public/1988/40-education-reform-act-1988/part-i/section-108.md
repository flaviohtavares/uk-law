## 108. Recovery of wasted examination fees.[^c12672861][^c12672871]

*Extent: E+W*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

(1) Notwithstanding section 106(5) of this Act, where—

- (a) the governing body of any maintained school or the local education authority have paid or are liable to pay any fee in respect of the entry of a registered pupil at the school for a public examination in any syllabus for that examination; and
- (b) the pupil fails without good reason to meet any examination requirement for that syllabus;

that body or authority may recover the amount of the fee from the pupil’s parent.

(2) It shall be for the body or authority who have paid or are liable to pay the fee in question to determine for the purposes of this section any question whether a pupil who has failed to meet any such examination requirement had good reason for the failure.
