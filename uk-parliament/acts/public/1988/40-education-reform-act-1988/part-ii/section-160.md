## 160. Adjustments of block grant in respect of expenditure on advanced further education.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-f1342966b3c1fd99ba7a369081c9444f] The block grant payable to a local authority in England for any year beginning on or after the date appointed for the purposes of section 126(1) of this Act shall not be subject to adjustment in accordance with paragraph 6 of Schedule 10 to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f1342966b3c1fd99ba7a369081c9444f] [^c12675181]Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 (which relates to adjustments of block grant in respect of expenditure on advanced further education as between local authorities). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f1342966b3c1fd99ba7a369081c9444f]

(2) In relation to any expenditure incurred by such an authority on or after that date in the exercise of the authority’s functions as a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f1342966b3c1fd99ba7a369081c9444f] local authority[^c-dd8a5cd1b7a66756b606dd2897e7636c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f1342966b3c1fd99ba7a369081c9444f] , sub-paragraphs (3)(a) and (5)(b) of paragraph 5 of that Schedule (which define certain expenditure for the purposes of that paragraph) shall each have effect as if the words “other than that to which paragraph 6 below applies" were omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f1342966b3c1fd99ba7a369081c9444f]

(3) On that date Part I of that Schedule (which relates to adjustments of block grant in respect of expenditure on advanced further education as between England and Wales) shall cease to have effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f1342966b3c1fd99ba7a369081c9444f]

(4) Anything done by the Secretary of State before the passing of this Act for the purpose of making in the block grant payable to a local authority in England adjustments under paragraph 5 or 6 of that Schedule in respect of expenditure incurred in the years beginning in 1985, 1986 and 1987 by local authorities in England in connection with further education of an advanced character (including the training of teachers) shall be deemed to have been done in accordance with that paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f1342966b3c1fd99ba7a369081c9444f]

(5) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f1342966b3c1fd99ba7a369081c9444f]

- “**local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f1342966b3c1fd99ba7a369081c9444f]**” means a body which is a local authority for the purposes of Part VI of that Act; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f1342966b3c1fd99ba7a369081c9444f]
- “**year *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f1342966b3c1fd99ba7a369081c9444f]**” has the same meaning as in that Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f1342966b3c1fd99ba7a369081c9444f]
