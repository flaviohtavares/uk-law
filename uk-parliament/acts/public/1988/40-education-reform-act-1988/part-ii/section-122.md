## 122. Orders incorporating higher education institutions maintained by local authorities in Wales.[^c12673541][^c-dd8a5cd1b7a66756b606dd2897e7636c][^c-1d6a967e105e9da352abe2cd44285dd9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to subsection (2) below, if at any time it appears to the Secretary of State, in the case of any institution maintained by a local authority in Wales[^c-5b919e911a8776baffb3f4dd6ba1b24a], that its full-time equivalent enrolment number for courses of higher education exceeds 55 per cent. of its total full-time equivalent enrolment number he may make an order under this section with respect to that institution.

(2)[^c12673551] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c12673551] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c12673551] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c12673551] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) An order under this section with respect to any institution shall make provision for the establishment of a body corporate for the purpose of conducting that institution as from the transfer date applicable in relation to that body corporate.
