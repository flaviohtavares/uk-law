# Education Reform Act 1988, section 123

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123. Provisions supplementary to sections 121 to 122A.[^c-dab2ffd1339325a2001bfb15415a1452]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) References in this Act to a higher education corporation are references to a body corporate established under section 121 or 122 of this Act or which has become a higher education corporation by virtue of section[^c12673621] 122ZA or[^c-5af9c1ae7630076d737ee1279113cf98] 122A of this Act.

(2) In this Act “**transfer date**” means, in relation to a higher education corporation, the date appointed under section 126 of this Act in relation to the transfer under that section of property, rights and liabilities to that corporation.

(3)[^c12673631] Schedule 7 to this Act has effect with respect to each higher education corporation in Wales[^c-ed82520d9dfbe3e289fe8874f4fdd204] established before the appointed day (within the meaning of section 124A of this Act) unless an instrument of government for the corporation made under that section has effect.

(4) A higher education corporation in Wales[^c-c80681640b2b64f214c0c831117e3967] established under section 122 of this Act on or after that day for the purpose of conducting any institution shall be established initially under the name given in the order under that section establishing the corporation.
