# Education Reform Act 1988, section 125A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 125A. Charitable status of a higher education corporation.[^c-e842bffc95e87f14bf7ab77243005cdd]

*Extent: E+W*

A higher education corporation shall be a charity and—

- (a) a higher education corporation in England[^c-6abd8df2f9cda003f454077e00667d89] is, in accordance with Schedule 3 to the Charities Act 2011, an exempt charity for the purposes of that Act, and
- (b) a higher education corporation in Wales[^c-90d52b8a288d78a6e0809c9143d7f82b] is, in accordance with regulations made in compliance with section 31(3) of that Act, excepted from registration under that Act.
