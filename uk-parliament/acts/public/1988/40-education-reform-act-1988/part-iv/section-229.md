## 229. Power to make different provision for Wales in regulations under the 1944 and 1967 Acts.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c12681381] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) In section 4 of the [^c12681391]Education Act 1967 (loans for capital expenditure for purposes of colleges of education), after subsection (3) there shall be inserted the following subsection—

> (3A) Regulations under this section may make in relation to Wales provision different from that made in relation to England.
