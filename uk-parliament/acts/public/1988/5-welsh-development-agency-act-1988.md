# Welsh Development Agency Act 1988

> This law has been repealed or revoked. Ended on 1991-12-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Welsh Development Agency Act 1988 [^c804928](repealed 19.12.1991)

1988 c. 5

**No longer in force (ended 1991-12-19).**

## 1. Increase of financial limit.

In section 18(3) of the [^c804930]Welsh Development Agency Act 1975 (financial limit for the Agency), for the words “£450 million” there shall be substituted the words “ £700 million ”.

## 2. Short title and extent.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Welsh Development Agency Act 1988.

(2) This Act does not extend to Northern Ireland.
