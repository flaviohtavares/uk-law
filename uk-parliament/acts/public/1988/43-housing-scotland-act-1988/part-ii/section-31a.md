# Housing (Scotland) Act 1988, section 31A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 31A. Succession after the Private Housing (Tenancies) (Scotland) Act 2016 comes into force[^key-0af68e0ba2767180df344c536af25d74]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subsection (2) applies where—

- (a) a sole tenant under an assured tenancy dies on or after 1 December 2017[^key-d5e9290498598cfb457a561db4edb22f] , and
- (b) an individual succeeds to the tenancy.

(2) As soon as the individual becomes the tenant, the tenancy—

- (a) ceases to be an assured tenancy (if, but for this section, it would have been one), and
- (b) becomes a private residential tenancy as defined in the Private Housing (Tenancies) (Scotland) Act 2016.
