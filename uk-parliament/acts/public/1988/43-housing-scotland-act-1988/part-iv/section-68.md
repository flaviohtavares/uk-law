# Housing (Scotland) Act 1988, section 68

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 68. Abolition of local authority’s power to refer Part VII contracts to the First-tier Tribunal[^c-c85035cf0ea6b6765ec1992c22bc7aa3]

*Extent: S*

In section 65(1) of the [^c10516571]Rent (Scotland) Act 1984 (which empowers the lessor, the lessee and the local authority to refer a Part VII contract to the First-tier Tribunal[^c-6fc11e2c3dbdaea891f8484546c9a287]), the words “or the local authority” and in sections 66(1), 68 and 71 (1) of that Act the words “or the local authority” in each place where they occur shall cease to have effect.
