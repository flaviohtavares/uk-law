# Housing (Scotland) Act 1988, section 69

## 69. Exclusion of SSHA, development corporations and co-operative housing associations from rent limitation under Part VI of Rent (Scotland) Act 1984.

*Extent: S*

In section 61(1) of the Rent (Scotland) Act 1984, the definition of “housing association” shall have effect, and shall be deemed always to have had effect, with the addition at the end of the words

> except that it does not include—
>
> - (a) the Scottish Special Housing Association;
> - (b) a development corporation (within the meaning of the New Towns (Scotland) Act 1968); or
> - (c) a co-operative housing association within the meaning of the Housing Associations Act 1985.
