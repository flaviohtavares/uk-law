# Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/53
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Court may take particulars endorsed ... into consideration.[^c18160881][^c-4066e805a57b8084928c5a0d887c3649]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-b148666c8c843301e982e29f7ecf39b5] Where a person is convicted of an offence involving obligatory or discretionary disqualification—

- (a) any existing endorsement on [^c-6f04e67d3e7e0e5b9694fe7d6219f670]... his driving record is prima facie evidence of the matters endorsed, and
- (b) the court may, in determining what order to make in pursuance of the conviction, take those matters into consideration.

(2) This section has effect notwithstanding anything in section 166(1) to (6) of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995[^c18160921] (requirements as to notices of penalties and previous convictions).
