## 27. Production of licence.[^c18160371]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c18160381][^c-b077bacd7d0a3c739c925773a8d1b202] Where—

- (a) a person who is the holder of a licence is convicted of an offence involving obligatory or discretionary disqualification, and
- (b) a court proposes to make, or makes, an order disqualifying the person,

the court may require the licence to be produced to it.

(2)[^c18160411] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c18160421][^c18160431] If the holder of the licence [^c-6ceb5a33d56570061d4058ee4ccdb099]... does not produce it [^c-63404a8288e6ace9864ae45fe001d8e4]... as required under this section or[^c18160471] section 40 of the Crime (Sentences) Act 1997, section 146 or 147 of the Powers of Criminal Courts (Sentencing) Act 2000[^c18160481] or section 223A or 436A of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1975 , or if the holder of the license does not produce it[^c18160491] [^c-63404a8288e6ace9864ae45fe001d8e4]... as required by section 40B of the [^c18160501]Child Support Act 1991 or paragraph 5 of Schedule 3ZB to the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^c-6b597157b4de367b321447c1294583bd], then, unless he satisfies the court that he has applied for a new licence and has not received it—

- (a) he is guilty of an offence, and
- (b) unless the licence is already treated as being revoked under section 37(1),[^c-03347c1941d4ffdfe24055f4b37619bc] the licence shall be suspended from the time when its production was required until it[^c18160511] [^c-63404a8288e6ace9864ae45fe001d8e4]... is produced[^c-0982a9eb84ed85eb0bb79d56e23a5ba7] to the court and shall, while suspended, be of no effect.

(4)[^c-1a98d9b5c1c320afb068c035a280f621] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4A)[^c-5a7a40d0f00a6518df3d16e16d65caa9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c-71a85d4e72e0b8a686d9086549e1759f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
