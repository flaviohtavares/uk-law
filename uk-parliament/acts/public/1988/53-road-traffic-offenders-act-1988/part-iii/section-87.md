# Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988, section 87

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/53

## 87. Guidance on application of Part III.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Secretary of State must issue guidance to chief officers of police for police areas and to the chief constable of the British Transport Police[^c18165531] in respect of the operation of this Part of this Act with the objective so far as possible of working towards uniformity.
