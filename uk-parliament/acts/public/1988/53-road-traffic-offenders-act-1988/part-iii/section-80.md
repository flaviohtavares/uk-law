# Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988, section 80

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/53

## 80. Certificates about payment etc.[^c-ae7181868d7da3c234bf189c7654e27b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In any proceedings a certificate—

- (a) that payment of a fixed penalty was or was not received, by a date specified in the certificate, by the person to whom it was required to be paid[^c-b7f43e73f9fd3b6397536e47655a84e8], or
- (b) that a letter containing an amount sent by post in payment of a fixed penalty was marked as posted on a date so specified , or[^c-8998ba44c1b813510e6999709f8e91a2]
- (c) that the identification requirements specified in section 69(3C) or 75(8B) have been fulfilled,

shall, if the certificate purports to be signed by the person to whom the penalty[^c-33dd6397e67cf19445d15c6e8b339536] was required to be paid, be evidence (and, in Scotland, sufficient evidence) of the facts stated.
