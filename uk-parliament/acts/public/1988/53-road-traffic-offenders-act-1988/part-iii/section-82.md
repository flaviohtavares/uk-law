## 82. Accounting for fixed penalties: England and Wales.[^c18165261]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c18165271] In England and Wales, sums paid to the fixed penalty clerk[^c-04f5834bca84f8896e3320d0bc57f49b] by way of fixed penalty for an offence shall be treated for the purposes of section 38 of the Courts Act 2003 (application of receipts of designated officers)[^c-e385b7280871e4dee1a4128f46045622] as if they were fines imposed on summary conviction for that offence.
