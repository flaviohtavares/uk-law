## 84. Regulations.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision as to any matter incidental to the operation of this Part of this Act, and in particular—

- (a) for prescribing any information or further information to be provided in any notice, notification, certificate or receipt under section 52(1), [^c-9062230e78402f7f5dc97c963d108ea6]... 59(1), [^c-3b767bcdfbc5bdea239f1b6cb0251aa0]... 63(2), 70(2) and (3)(b), 73(4)(b), 75(1), (1A), (2), (3) or (3B) or 76(3)(c) or (4)(b)[^c-b8281aacf3e1c26245c19a55f26340e6] of this Act or in any official form for a statutory statement mentioned in Schedule 4 to, or a statement under section 66(2) of, this Act, and[^c-32e1a95d033eaa0cf21959bffd766d6f]
- (b) the amount of the penalty stated in the offer is less than the fixed penalty applicable in the circumstances,
