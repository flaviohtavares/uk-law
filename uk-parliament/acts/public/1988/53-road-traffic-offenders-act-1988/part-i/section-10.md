## 10. Jurisdiction of district court in Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Notwithstanding anything in any enactment or rule of law to the contrary, a justice of the peace court[^c-340f568622e470c2469d671fa24598ca] in Scotland may try—

- (a) any fixed penalty offence (within the meaning of Part III of this Act), and
- (b) any other offence in respect of which a conditional offer (within the meaning of sections 75 to 77A[^c-fe47683c835bb49bfb06ccddecc4882d] of this Act) may be sent.
