# Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/53
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Exemption from Licensing Act offence.

*Extent: E+W+S*

A person liable to be charged with an offence under section 3A, 4[^c18157551], 5, 7 or 30 of the [^c18157561]Road Traffic Act 1988 (drink and drugs) is not liable to be charged under section 12 of the [^c18157571]Licensing Act 1872 with the offence of being drunk while in charge, on a highway or other public place, of a carriage.
