# Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/53

## 8. Duty to include date of birth and sex in written plea of guilty.[^c18157911]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A person who—

- (a) gives a notification to[^c18157921] the designated officer for a magistrates' court[^key-68c935a9aef32354247e61a6a87ce582] in pursuance of section 12(4) of the [^c18157931]Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 (written pleas of guilty),
- (aa)[^key-5d1d6d3b03207719fd211004dd4a661c] serves a written notification on the designated officer for a magistrates' court stating a desire to plead guilty and to be tried in accordance with section 16A of the Magistrates' Courts Act 1980 (trial by single justice on the papers),
- (ab)[^key-7542eafb7d6c2a7cab9488d65cb7d3d1] gives a notification amounting to acceptance of the automatic online conviction option (within the meaning of section 16G of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980), or
- (b) gives a written intimation of a plea of guilty in pursuance of section 334(3) of the [^c18157941]Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1975,

in respect of an offence involving obligatory or discretionary disqualification or of such other offence as may be prescribed by regulations under section 105 of the [^c18157951]Road Traffic Act 1988, must include in the notification or intimation a statement of the date of birth and sex of the accused.
