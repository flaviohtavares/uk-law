# Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (91).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/53
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Use of records kept by operators of goods vehicles.

*Extent: E+W+S*

In any proceedings for an offence under section 40A of the Road Traffic Act 1988 or[^c18158401] for a contravention of or failure to comply with construction and use requirements (within the meaning of Part II of the [^c18158411]Road Traffic Act 1988) or regulations under section 74 of that Act, any record purporting to be made and authenticated in accordance with regulations under that section shall be evidence (and in Scotland sufficient evidence) of the matters stated in the record and of its due authentication.
