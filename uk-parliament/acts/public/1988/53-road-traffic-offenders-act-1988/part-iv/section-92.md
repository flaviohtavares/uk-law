## 92. Application to Crown.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The following provisions of this Act apply to vehicles and persons in the public service of the Crown: sections 1, 2, 3, 15, 16 20[^c18165901] and 49 and the provisions connected with the licensing of drivers.
