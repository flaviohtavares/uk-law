## 91ZA. Application to Northern Ireland licence holders[^key-58c425b17a371b684d98807e73ea375f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The references to a licence in the following provisions of this Act include references to a Northern Ireland licence—

- (a) section 7,
- (b) section 26(7) and (8) [^key-15de36bfbd8ffcdd1eb49fb471a30283]...,
- (c) section 27,
- (ca)[^key-2076d6d73e8249abd45c6dbb80a232df] section 37A,
- (d)[^key-dc584fbce8a0942328bd3db063a10300] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^key-dc584fbce8a0942328bd3db063a10300] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f)[^key-dc584fbce8a0942328bd3db063a10300] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g)[^key-be154a8fdef699cbc000b8ecda634058] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (h)[^key-4c3591ae51f4b49826f592f6e8eb2624] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (i)[^key-4c3591ae51f4b49826f592f6e8eb2624] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (j)[^key-4c3591ae51f4b49826f592f6e8eb2624] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (k) section 47(2) [^key-55e65fc23eb8cf6613fbc4db5292277f]...,
- (l)[^key-8bdc3a50f0aaa87493800ed87ed27d76] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Accordingly, the reference in section 27(3)(b) of this Act to the suspension of a licence is to be construed in relation to a Northern Ireland licence holder as a reference to his ceasing to be authorised by virtue of section 109(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1988 to drive in Great Britain a motor vehicle of any class.

(3)[^key-d8dfb69699e964dc0447072a278c20c3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-d8dfb69699e964dc0447072a278c20c3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-d8dfb69699e964dc0447072a278c20c3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^key-d8dfb69699e964dc0447072a278c20c3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) Where a court orders the holder of a Northern Ireland licence to be disqualified, it must send the Northern Ireland licence [^key-3c3bfb7f8233d82b383b2fffc308abf6]..., on its[^key-a69bfd447b195cd89f390a3b93b06e82] being produced to the court, to the Secretary of State.

(8) The licence [^key-283f199fab88a9bd2f8c6db656dc1ac0]... must be sent to such address as the Secretary of State may determine.

(9) Where—

- (a) a notice is sent to the Secretary of State under section 44A above of an order for the endorsement of a person's driving record[^key-e8308ba103e293be1d3a23a1aea65d28], and
- (b) the particulars contained in the notice include—
  - (i) particulars of an offence in respect of which the holder of a Northern Ireland licence , or a person normally resident in Northern Ireland who does not hold a licence,[^key-3f403816a30a658a9b9b754263b1f415] is disqualified by an order of a court, and
  - (ii) particulars of the disqualification,

the Secretary of State must send a notice containing the particulars mentioned in paragraph (b)(i) and (ii) to the licensing authority in Northern Ireland.
