## 89. Consequential amendments.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In relation to acquisitions of shares or interests in shares on or after 26th October 1987—

- (a) for the words from “section 138(1)(a)” to “**value of the shares**” in [^c-f6b20133fbf5e7993871808a2e71fa6f]... section 186(2)(b) (approved profit sharing schemes) of the Taxes Act 1988, and
- (b)[^c-887c1612fe9e21a826f7cbbc56e38814] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

there shall be substituted the words “ section 78 or 79 of the Finance Act 1988 in respect of the shares ”.
