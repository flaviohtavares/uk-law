# Finance Act 1988, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-02-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/39

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Charge and rate of corporation tax for financial year 1988.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Corporation tax shall be charged for the financial year 1988 at the rate of 35 per cent.
