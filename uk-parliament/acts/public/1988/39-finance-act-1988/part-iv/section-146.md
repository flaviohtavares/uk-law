## 146. Post-consolidation amendments.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The enactments specified in Schedule 13 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in that Schedule (being amendments to correct errors in the Taxes Act 1988 and in the amendments made by the [^c13529671]Finance Act 1987 for the purposes of the consolidation effected by the Taxes Act 1988).
