## 7. Meaning of “sparkling” in relation to wine and made-wine.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 1 to the [^c13526241] Alcoholic Liquor Duties Act 1979 (wine and made-wine), in paragraph 1(1) under the heading “Interpretation” (meaning of “sparkling”), for the words “1 bar in excess of atmospheric pressure” there shall be substituted the words “ 1.5 bars in excess of atmospheric pressure ”.
