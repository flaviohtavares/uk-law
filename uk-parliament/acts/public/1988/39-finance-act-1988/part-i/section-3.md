# Finance Act 1988, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-02-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Hydrocarbon oil.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 6(1) of the [^c13525961]Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act 1979, for “£0.1938” (light oil) and “£0.1639” (heavy oil) there shall be substituted “ £0.2044 ” and “ £0.1729 ” respectively.

(2) In section 13A of that Act (rebate on unleaded petrol), for “£0.0096” there shall be substituted “ £0.0202 ”.

(3) This section shall be deemed to have come into force at 6 o’clock in the evening of 15th March 1988.
