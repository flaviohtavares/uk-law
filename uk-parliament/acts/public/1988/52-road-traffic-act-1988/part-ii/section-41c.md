# Road Traffic Act 1988, section 41C

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## (not yet in force: 41C). (not yet in force: Breach of requirement as to speed assessment equipment detection devices)[^c-700dc55eb20fcbac8de34348ad2bfa98][^c19271991]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

*(not yet in force: A person who—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: contravenes or fails to comply with a construction or use requirement as to speed assessment equipment detection devices, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: uses on a road a motor vehicle or trailer which does not comply with such a requirement, or causes or permits a motor vehicle or trailer to be so used,)*

*(not yet in force: is guilty of an offence.)*
