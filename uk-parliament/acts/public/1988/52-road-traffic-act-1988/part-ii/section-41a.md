# Road Traffic Act 1988, section 41A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (261).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41A. Breach of requirement as to brakes, steering-gear or tyres.[^c-846e98114ae16057c8e8cb7aa8eed682][^c13972981]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A person who—

- (a) contravenes or fails to comply with a construction and use requirement as to brakes, steering-gear or tyres, or
- (b) uses on a road a motor vehicle or trailer which does not comply with such a requirement, or causes or permits a motor vehicle or trailer to be so used,

is guilty of an offence.
