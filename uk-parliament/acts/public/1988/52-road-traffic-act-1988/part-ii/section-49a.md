# Road Traffic Act 1988, section 49A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## (not yet in force: 49A). (not yet in force: Use of records of goods vehicle examinations,) (not yet in force: etc.)[^c19272101]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: This section applies to—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the records maintained by the Secretary of State (or caused by him to be maintained) under section 49(3A) of this Act, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the records maintained by the Secretary of State in connection with any functions exercisable by him under or by virtue of the Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The Secretary of State may use the information contained in records falling within either paragraph of subsection (1) above—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: to check the accuracy of the records falling within the other paragraph of that subsection, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: where appropriate, to amend or supplement information contained in those records.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The Secretary of State may also use the information contained in records falling within paragraph (b) of that subsection for the purpose of promoting compliance with section 53 of this Act.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: This section does not limit any powers of the Secretary of State apart from this section.)*
