# Road Traffic Act 1988, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (261).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Directions to pedestrians.[^c13972461][^c13972471][^c19469871]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where a constable in uniform or traffic officer[^c19217581] is for the time being engaged in the regulation of vehicular traffic in a road, a person on foot who proceeds across or along the carriageway in contravention of a direction to stop given by the constable in the execution of his duty or the traffic officer (as the case may be)[^c19217621], either to persons on foot or to persons on foot and other traffic, is guilty of an offence.
