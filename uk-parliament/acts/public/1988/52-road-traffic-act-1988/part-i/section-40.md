# Road Traffic Act 1988, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (261).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Road safety grants[^c19480991][^c13972731][^c-0873264abff48a81c08852d78d7166cd][^c19269921]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A national transport authority may make payments to any local authority or any other authority or body for meeting the whole or part of the capital or running costs of any measures for promoting road safety.

(2) A “**national transport authority**” means—

- (a) the Secretary of State acting with the approval of the Treasury, or
- (b) the National Assembly for Wales.

## 40. Powers of Secretary of State to subsidise bodies other then local authorities for giving road safety information and training.[^c19481001][^c13972731][^c-0873264abff48a81c08852d78d7166cd]

*Extent: S*

The Secretary of State may, with the approval of the Treasury, make out of monies provided by Parliament contributions towards the cost of measures for promoting road safety, being measures taken by [^c13972741]. . . local authorities (within the meaning of section 39 of this Act) or by other authorities or bodies[^c13972751].
