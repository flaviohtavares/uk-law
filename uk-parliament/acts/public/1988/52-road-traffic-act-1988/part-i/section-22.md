# Road Traffic Act 1988, section 22

## 22. Leaving vehicles in dangerous positions.[^c13972091][^c-d50e0f115590d61c1ad6c87a5be22f59]

*Extent: E+W+S*

If a person in charge of a vehicle causes or permits the vehicle or a trailer drawn by it to remain at rest on a road in such a position or in such condition or in such circumstances as to involve a danger of injury[^c13972101] to other persons using the road, he is guilty of an offence.
