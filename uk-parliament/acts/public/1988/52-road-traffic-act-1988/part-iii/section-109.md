## 109. Provisions as to Northern Ireland drivers’ licences.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-f9367bb9d7e34d5b40fae154b96a61e7] The holder of a Northern Ireland driving licence[^c13987241] may drive, and a person may cause or permit the holder of such a licence to drive, in Great Britain, in accordance with that licence,[^c19169241] a motor vehicle of any class which he is authorised by that licence to drive, and which he is not disqualified from driving under this Part or Part IV[^c13987251] of this Act, notwithstanding that he is not the holder of a licence under this Part of this Act.

(2)[^c-c1fcd30208bebc38e328682007745832] For the purposes of this Act, any driver holding a Northern Ireland licence shall be under the same obligation to produce such a licence as if it had been a licence granted under this Part of this Act, and the provisions of this Act as to the production of licences granted under this Part of this Act shall apply accordingly.
