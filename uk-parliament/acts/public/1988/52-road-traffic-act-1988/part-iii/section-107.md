# Road Traffic Act 1988, section 107

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (261).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 107. Service of notices.[^c13985651]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A notice authorised to be served on any person under this Part or Part IV of this Act or a Community licence required to be returned to its holder by section[^c13985661] 99B, 99E or 109A[^c19163571] of this Act may be served on, or returned to him by delivering it to him or by leaving it at his proper address or by sending it to him by post; and for the purposes of this section and section 7 of the [^c13985671]Interpretation Act 1978 in its application to this section the proper address of any person shall be his latest address as known to the person serving the notice.
