## 87. Drivers of motor vehicles to have driving licences.[^c13978791][^c13978801][^c13978811][^c19130701][^c-070c115c5370cbc47f436eed31c22ce2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) It is an offence for a person to drive on a road a motor vehicle of any class otherwise than in accordance with[^c13978821] a licence authorising him to drive a motor vehicle of that class.

(2) It is an offence for a person to cause or permit another person to drive on a road a motor vehicle of any class otherwise than in accordance with a licence authorising that other person[^c13978831] to drive a motor vehicle of that class.
