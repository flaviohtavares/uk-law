# Road Traffic Act 1988, section 98A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 98A). (not yet in force: Compulsory surrender of old-form licences)[^c19276101]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The Secretary of State may by order require the holders of licences of a specified description, or any specified description of the holders of such licences, to surrender the licences)* *(not yet in force: *(not yet in force: and their counterparts)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19276431])* *(not yet in force: to the Secretary of State.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: An order under this section may specify as the description of licences to be surrendered—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: licences which are not in the form of a photocard, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: licences in the form of a photocard of a description no longer specified by the Secretary of State as a form in which licences are granted.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: An order under this section must specify the date by which the licences to which it relates)* *(not yet in force: *(not yet in force: (and their counterparts))* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19276441])* *(not yet in force: are to be surrendered; and may specify different dates in relation to different descriptions of licence holders.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: An order under this section must include provision for the grant of a new licence to every holder of a licence surrendered)* *(not yet in force: *(not yet in force: (with its counterpart))* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19276451])* *(not yet in force: in pursuance of the order who—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: pays such fee (if any) as is specified by the order,)* [^key-b34b87eb6f61a7ada44237bed07447a6]*(not yet in force: ...)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: provides the Secretary of State with such evidence or further evidence as the Secretary of State may require (which may include a photograph which is a current likeness of him).)* *(not yet in force: and[^key-462c455242ac94bac4942f15a44a05b1])*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: provides such evidence or further evidence as the Secretary of State may require for the purpose of satisfying the Secretary of State that the holder is not suffering from a relevant or prospective disability.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: A replacement licence granted pursuant to provision made by virtue of subsection (4) above expires on the date on which the surrendered licence would have expired had it not been surrendered (but subject to subsection (6) below).)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: Where the period for which the surrendered licence was granted was based on an error with respect to the licence holder's date of birth such that (if the error had not been made) that licence would have been expressed to expire on a different date, the replacement licence expires on that different date.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: A person who, without reasonable excuse, fails to comply with any requirement to surrender a licence)* *(not yet in force: *(not yet in force: and its counterpart)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19276461])* *(not yet in force: imposed by an order under this section is guilty of an offence.)*

((not yet in force: 8)) *(not yet in force: An order under this section may—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: make different provision for different cases, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: contain such incidental and supplementary provisions as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.)*

((not yet in force: 9)) *(not yet in force: The power to make an order under this section is exercisable by statutory instrument.)*

((not yet in force: 10)) *(not yet in force: Before making an order under this section the Secretary of State must consult with such representative organisations as he thinks fit.)*

((not yet in force: 11)) *(not yet in force: A statutory instrument containing an order under this section is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.)*
