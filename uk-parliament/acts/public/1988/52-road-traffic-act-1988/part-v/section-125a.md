# Road Traffic Act 1988, section 125A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (261).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 125A. Registration of disabled persons.[^c13988571][^c19252421][^c13988561]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5] A person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]

- (a) suffers from a relevant disability or a prospective disability, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]
- (b) holds a current disabled person’s limited driving licence, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]
- (c) holds a current emergency control certificate, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]

may apply to the Registrar for his name to be entered in the register as that of a disabled instructor. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]

(2) An application under subsection (1) above shall be made in such manner, and shall be accompanied by such particulars, as the Secretary of State may determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]

(3) If, at the time when he makes an application under subsection (1) above, a person is aware— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]

- (a) that he is suffering from a relevant or prospective disability which he has not previously disclosed to the Secretary of State in making an application for his current emergency control certificate, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]
- (b) that a relevant or prospective disability from which he has at any time suffered (and which has been previously so disclosed) has become more acute since the granting of his current emergency control certificate, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]

his application under subsection (1) above must be accompanied by written notification of the nature and extent of his disability. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]

(4) Any person who fails without reasonable excuse to comply with the requirement imposed by subsection (3) above is guilty of an offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]

(5) Where a person duly applies for the entry of his name in the register as that of a disabled instructor and satisfies the Registrar that the conditions in subsection (6) below are fulfilled in his case, the Registrar must, subject to section 125B(4) of this Act, on payment of such fee, if any, as may be prescribed by regulations, enter the applicant’s name in the register with an indication that he is a disabled person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]

(6) The conditions referred to in subsection (5) above are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]

- (a) the applicant has passed such examination of ability to give instruction in the driving of motor cars (consisting of a written examination, a practical test of ability and fitness to drive and a practical test of ability and fitness to instruct) as may be prescribed by regulations, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]
- (b) the conditions set out in paragraphs (c) and (d) of section 125(3) of this Act are fulfilled, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]
- (c) apart from the fulfilment of the preceding conditions, he is a fit and proper person to have his name entered in the register as that of a disabled instructor; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]

and for the purposes of paragraph (b) above, references in paragraphs (c) and (d) of subsection (3) of section 125 of this Act to the making of an application under subsection (2) of that section shall be read as references to the making of an application under subsection (1) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]

(7) Regulations may provide that persons of such class as may be specified in the regulations shall be exempt from the condition mentioned in paragraph (a) of subsection (6) above as regards such part of the examination mentioned in that paragraph as may be so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]

(8) In this Part of this Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]

- “**appropriate motor car *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]**” means, subject to section 125B(2) of this Act, a motor car equipped with automatic transmission; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]
- “**disability *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]**” means a want of physical ability affecting the driving of motor cars; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]
  - (i) “**relevant disability *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]**”, in relation to a person, means any disability which is prescribed in regulations or any other disability likely to cause the driving of a motor car by him to be a source of danger to the public; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]
  - (ii) “**prospective disability *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]**”, in relation to a person, means any other disability which, at the material time, is not of such a kind that it is a relevant disability but, by virtue of the intermittent or progressive nature of the disability or otherwise, may become a relevant disability in course of time; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]
- “**disabled person’s limited driving licence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]**” means a licence of one of the following kinds, that is to say— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]
  - (a) a licence to drive a motor car granted under Part III of this Act (not being a provisional licence) which is limited, by virtue of a notice served under section 92(5)(b) of this Act, to an appropriate motor car; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5] [^c13988581]...
  - (b) a licence to drive a motor car granted under the law in force in Northern Ireland (not being a licence corresponding to a provisional licence) which is similarly limited by virtue of any corresponding provision of that law; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5] and[^c13988591] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]
  - (c) a Community licence authorising the driving of a motor car which is similarly limited by virtue of any corresponding provision of the law under which it was issued *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5] [^M_F_42fab9ce-8a31-4fa0-ee66-de333109cd80]...;

- “**emergency control assessment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]**” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]**emergency control certificate *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]**” mean an assessment and a certificate under section 133A of this Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]
- “**modifications *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]**”, in relation to a motor car, includes equipment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]
- “**registered disabled instructor *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]**” means a person whose name is in the register with an indication that he is disabled; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]

and any reference, in relation to a person, to the class of motor car covered by his disabled person’s limited driving licence is a reference to the class of motor car specified in the notice served on him under section 92(5)(b) of this Act but disregarding any modifications specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_be0044f6-ef5b-4145-fc3d-b0f03ada7cf5]
