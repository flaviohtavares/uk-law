# Road Traffic Act 1988, section 111

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (261).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/52

## 111. Functions of traffic commissioners.[^c13987441]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A traffic commissioner[^c-f8debba6d1271fe21df1d15247579466] shall exercise the functions conferred by the following provisions of this Part of this Act relating to the conduct of

- (a)[^c13987461] applicants for and holders of large goods vehicle and passenger-carrying vehicle drivers’ licences, and
- (b)[^c13987461] holders of LGV Community licences and PCV Community licences.

(2) Traffic commissioners shall, in the exercise of those functions, act in accordance with directions given by the Secretary of State; but such directions shall be general directions not relating to the exercise of functions in a particular case.
