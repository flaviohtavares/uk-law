## 163. Power of police to stop vehicles.[^c13992941]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person driving a mechanically propelled vehicle[^c13992951] on a road must stop the vehicle on being required to do so by a constable in uniform or a traffic officer[^c19217752].

(2) A person riding a cycle on a road must stop the cycle on being required to do so by a constable in uniform or a traffic officer[^c19217772].

(3) If a person fails to comply with this section he is guilty of an offence.
