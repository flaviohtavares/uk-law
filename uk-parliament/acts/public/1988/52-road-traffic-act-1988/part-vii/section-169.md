# Road Traffic Act 1988, section 169

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (261).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 169. Pedestrian contravening constable’s direction to stop to give name and address.[^c13997331][^c13997341]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A constable may require a person committing an offence under section 37 of this Act to give his name and address, and if that person fails to do so he is guilty of an offence.
