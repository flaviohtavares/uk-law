# Road Traffic Act 1988, section 166

## 166. Powers of certain officers as respects goods vehicles and passenger-carrying vehicles.[^c13997241]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any of the following officers, namely—

- (a) an examiner appointed under section 68 of this Act,
- (b) a certifying officer appointed under section 7(1) of the Public Passenger Vehicles Act [^c13997251]1981,
- (c) an examiner appointed under section 7(2) of that Act, and
- (d) A person authorised for the purpose by a traffic commissioner appointed under the Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981,[^c13997261]

may, on production if so required of his authority, exercise in the case of goods vehicles or passenger-carrying vehicles of any prescribed class all such powers as are, under section 164(1), (3) or (5A)[^key-42e91c2a13078a05244356bbfc3cd087] or 165 of this Act, exercisable by a constable.
