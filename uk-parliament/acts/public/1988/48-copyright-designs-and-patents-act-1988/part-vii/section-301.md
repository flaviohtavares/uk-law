# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 301

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 301. Provisions for the benefit of Great Ormond Street Hospital for Children.[^c-420877e4ea786219767a57e4c1f653ff]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provisions of Schedule 6 have effect for conferring on GOSH Children's Charity for the benefit of Great Ormond Street Hospital for Children[^c-0cd57b1f09130f3a3e2b3ff2a343bb18] a right to a royalty in respect of the public performance, commercial publication or communication to the public[^c19408781] of the play “Peter Pan” by Sir James Matthew Barrie, or of any adaptation of that work, notwithstanding that copyright in the work expired on 31st December 1987.
