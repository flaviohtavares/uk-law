# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 211

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 211. Expressions having same meaning as in copyright provisions.[^c20314291][^c20314881]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following expressions have the same meaning in this Part as in Part I (copyright)—

- assignment (in Scotland),[^c20327071]
- broadcast,
- business,
- [^c20312421]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^c20312431]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- communication to the public,[^c20312441]
- country,
- defendant (in Scotland),
- delivery up (in Scotland),
- the[^c20327161] EEA,
- EEA state,
- film,
- injunction (in Scotland)[^c20312461]
- literary work,
- published, [^c20312471]. . .
- signed,[^c20327101]
- sound recording, and[^c20312481]
- wireless broadcast.[^c20312491]

(2) The provisions of—[^c20327121]

- (a) section 5B(2) and (3) (supplementary provisions relating to films), and
- (b) section 6(3) to (5A) and section 19(4) (supplementary provisions relating to broadcasting),

apply for the purposes of this Part, and in relation to an infringement of the rights conferred by this Part, as they apply for the purposes of Part I and in relation to an infringement of copyright.
