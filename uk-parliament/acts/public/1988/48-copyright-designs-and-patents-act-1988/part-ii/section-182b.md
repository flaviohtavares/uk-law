# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 182B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 182B. Consent required for issue of copies to public.[^c20313641][^c20311571][^c20314851]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A performer’s rights are infringed by a person who, without his consent, issues to the public copies of a recording of the whole or any substantial part of a qualifying performance.

(2) References in this Part to the issue to the public of copies of a recording are to—

- (a) the act of putting into circulation in the UK-EEA area[^c-c21da42297d7a0706cd80fd5642d13df] copies not previously put into circulation in the UK-EEA area by or with the consent of the performer, or
- (b) the act of putting into circulation outside the UK-EEA area copies not previously put into circulation in the UK-EEA area or elsewhere.

(3) References in this Part to the issue to the public of copies of a recording do not include—

- (a) any subsequent distribution, sale, hiring or loan of copies previously put into circulation (but see section 182C: consent required for rental or lending), or
- (b) any subsequent importation of such copies into the United Kingdom or an[^c-a2a7ddcaa6b5b6e185404de822db242e] EEA state,

except so far as paragraph (a) of subsection (2) applies to putting into circulation in the UK-EEA area[^c-7163cd91afb61df109fb65802ed3e3d0] copies previously put into circulation outside the UK-EEA area.

(3A)[^c-55af5534d52684ca67f6a98297c80d8d] In this section “**UK-EEA area**” means the United Kingdom and the EEA, taken together.

(4) References in this Part to the issue of copies of a recording of a performance include the issue of the original recording of the live performance.

(5) The right of a performer under this section to authorise or prohibit the issue of copies to the public is referred to in this Chapter[^c20323881] as “distribution right”.
