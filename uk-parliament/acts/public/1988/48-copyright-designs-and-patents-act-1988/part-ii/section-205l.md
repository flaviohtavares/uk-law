# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 205L

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48

## 205L. Moral rights not assignable[^c20314971]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The rights conferred by this Chapter are not assignable.
