# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 189

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 189. Acts permitted notwithstanding rights conferred by this Chapter.[^c20313761][^c20314851][^c20323971]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provisions of Schedule 2 specify acts which may be done notwithstanding the rights conferred by this Chapter[^c20323961], being acts which correspond broadly to certain of those specified in Chapter III of Part I (acts permitted notwithstanding copyright).
