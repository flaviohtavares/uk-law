# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 194

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 194. Infringement actionable as breach of statutory duty.[^c20314001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An infringement of —[^c20312011]

- (a) a performer’s non-property rights, or
- (b) any right conferred by this Chapter[^c20325281] on a person having recording rights,

is actionable by the person entitled to the right as a breach of statutory duty.
