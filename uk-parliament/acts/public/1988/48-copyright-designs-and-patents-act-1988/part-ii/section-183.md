# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 183

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 183. Infringement of performer’s rights by use of recording made without consent.[^c20313681][^c20314851]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A performer’s rights are infringed by a person who, without his consent—

- (a) shows or plays in public the whole or any substantial part of a qualifying performance, or
- (b) communicates to the public[^c20311631] the whole or any substantial part of a qualifying performance,

by means of a recording which was, and which that person knows or has reason to believe was, made without the performer’s consent.
