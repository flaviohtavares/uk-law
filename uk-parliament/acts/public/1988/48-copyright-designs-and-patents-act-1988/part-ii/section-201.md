# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 201

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 201. False representation of authority to give consent.[^c20314101]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It is an offence for a person to represent falsely that he is authorised by any person to give consent for the purposes of this Chapter[^c20325371] in relation to a performance, unless he believes on reasonable grounds that he is so authorised.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale or both.
