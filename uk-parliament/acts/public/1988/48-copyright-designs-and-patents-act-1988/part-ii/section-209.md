# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 209

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 209. Territorial waters and the continental shelf.[^c20314261][^c20314881][^key-955c41072a9ee12c4178dd2fce6841d3][^key-b07deb2dbe91e6b7eda0afe3df506ad0][^key-52ded3fa73c9ba424066677bc167b3ac]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Part the territorial waters of the United Kingdom shall be treated as part of the United Kingdom.

(2) This Part applies to things done in the United Kingdom sector of the continental shelf on a structure or vessel which is present there for purposes directly connected with the exploration of the sea bed or subsoil or the exploitation of their natural resources as it applies to things done in the United Kingdom.

(3) The United Kingdom sector of the continental shelf means the areas designated by order under section 1(7) of the [^c20312401]Continental Shelf Act 1964.
