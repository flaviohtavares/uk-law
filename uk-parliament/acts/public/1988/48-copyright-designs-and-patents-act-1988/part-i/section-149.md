## 149. Jurisdiction of the Tribunal.[^c13757061]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Copyright Tribunal has jurisdiction under this Part[^c13757071] to hear and determine proceedings under—

- (za)[^c-a62d3c8cd8abbf586f39c86b061af29d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (zb)[^c13757091] section 93C (application to determine amount of equitable remuneration under section 93B);
- (a) section 118, 119, or 120 (reference of licensing scheme);
- (b) section 121 or 122 (application with respect to entitlement to licence under licensing scheme);
- (c) section 125, 126 or 127 (reference or application with respect to licensing by licensing body);
- (ca)[^c19373511] section 128B (reference by the Secretary of State under section 128A);
- (cc)[^c13757101] section 135D or 135E (application or reference with respect to use as of right of sound recordings in broadcasts [^c19373531] . . . );
- (d) section 139 (appeal against order as to coverage of licensing scheme or licence);
- (e) section 142 (application to settle royalty or other sum payable for lending of certain works[^c13757111];
- (f) section 144(4) (application to settle terms of copyright licence available as of right);
- (fa)[^c-40815a3b8f2c27d60ed0c4d574dd4116] paragraph 7 of Schedule ZA1 (application to determine compensation for use of orphan works).
- (g)[^c13757121] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (h)[^c13757121] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
