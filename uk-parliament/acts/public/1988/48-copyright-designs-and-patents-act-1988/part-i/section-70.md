# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Recording for purposes of time-shifting.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19323731] The making in domestic premises[^c19323791] for private and domestic use of a recording of a broadcast [^c19323811]. . . solely for the purpose of enabling it to be viewed or listened to at a more convenient time does not infringe any copyright in the broadcast [^c19323811]. . . or in any work included in it.

(2)[^c19323771] Where a copy which would otherwise be an infringing copy is made in accordance with this section but is subsequently dealt with—

- (a) it shall be treated as an infringing copy for the purposes of that dealing; and
- (b) if that dealing infringes copyright, it shall be treated as an infringing copy for all subsequent purposes.

(3) In subsection (2), “ **dealt with** ” means sold or let for hire, offered or exposed for sale or hire or communicated to the public.
