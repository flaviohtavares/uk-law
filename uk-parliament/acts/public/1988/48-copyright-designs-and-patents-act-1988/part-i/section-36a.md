# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 36A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36A. Lending of copies by educational establishments[^c13755211]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Copyright in a work is not infringed by the lending of copies of the work by an educational establishment.
