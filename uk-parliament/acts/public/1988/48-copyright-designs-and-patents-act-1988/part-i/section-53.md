# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Things done in reliance on registration of design.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The copyright in an artistic work is not infringed by anything done—

- (a) in pursuance of an assignment or licence made or granted by a person registered —[^key-efe8271c77a41135ab15e4d96bd4522e]
- (i) under the [^c13755561]Registered Designs Act 1949 as the proprietor of a corresponding design, and , or[^key-ca4afdaab0543453bff884cf2f7babd4]
- (ii) under the Community Design Regulation as the right holder of a corresponding registered Community design
- (b) in good faith in reliance on the registration and without notice of any proceedings for the cancellation or invalidation[^c13755571] of the registration or , in a case of registration under the 1949 Act,[^key-b8276492bb4da5cdfc333aadec6d9ba6] for rectifying the relevant entry in the register of designs;

and this is so notwithstanding that the person registered as the proprietor was not the proprietor of the design for the purposes of the 1949 Act or, in a case of registration under the Community Design Regulation, that the person registered as the right holder was not the right holder of the design for the purposes of the Regulation[^key-0f3f25e4c2560cdf2e8ff767c97a0203].

(2) In subsection (1) a “**corresponding design**”, in relation to an artistic work, means a design within the meaning of the 1949 Act which if applied to an article would produce something which would be treated for the purposes of this Part as a copy of the artistic work.

(3)[^key-71e21b5dfadb40241ae79c3506b9af40] In subsection (1), a “**corresponding registered Community design**”, in relation to an artistic work, means a design within the meaning of the Community Design Regulation which if applied to an article would produce something which would be treated for the purposes of this Part as a copy of the artistic work.

(4)[^key-27ff6aad40d652ff871ec002125f6936] In this section, “**the Community Design Regulation**” means Council Regulation [(EC) No 6/2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2002/0006) of 12 December 2001 on Community designs.
