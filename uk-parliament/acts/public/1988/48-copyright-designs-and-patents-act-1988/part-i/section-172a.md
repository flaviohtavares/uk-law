## 172A. Meaning of EEA and related expressions.[^c13757711][^c13757691][^c13757701]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19454401] In this Part—

- “ **the EEA** ” means the European Economic Area; and
- “ **EEA state** ” means a member State, Iceland, Liechtenstein or Norway.
