# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 145

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 145. The Copyright Tribunal.[^c13756961]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Tribunal established under section 23 of the [^c13756971]Copyright Act 1956 is renamed the Copyright Tribunal.

(2)[^c13756981] The Tribunal shall consist of a chairman and two deputy chairmen appointed by the Lord Chancellor, after consultation with the Lord Advocate, and not less than two or more than eight ordinary members appointed by the Secretary of State.

(3) A person is not eligible for appointment as chairman or deputy chairman unless—[^c13756991]

- (a)[^c19458561] he satisfies the judicial-appointment eligibility condition on a 5-year basis;
- (b) he is an advocate or solicitor in Scotland of at least 5[^c19458581] years’ standing;
- (c) he is a member of the Bar of Northern Ireland or solicitor of the Court of Judicature of Northern Ireland[^c20334341] of at least 5 years’ standing; or
- (d) he has held judicial office.
