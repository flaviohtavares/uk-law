# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 219

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 219. Qualification by reference to employer.[^M_F_d00d31f3-423e-4867-ce87-a5f09e812edb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A design qualifies for design right protection if it is created in the course of employment with[^c-a4a52f69ffd2a10364022a95738afbbc] a qualifying person.

(2) In the case of [^c-5f96eb433c2b1353a943e1ccf5ab9e7c]... joint employment a design qualifies for design right protection if any of the [^c-5f96eb433c2b1353a943e1ccf5ab9e7c]... employers is a qualifying person.

(3) Where a design which is [^c-c827c111a916d9fc631f54b5ee514d95]... created in the course of joint employment qualifies for design right protection under this section, only those [^c-c827c111a916d9fc631f54b5ee514d95]... employers who are qualifying persons are entitled to design right under section 215(3)[^c-0ec3790c7e840dfdb6610d20f074070c] (first ownership of design right: entitlement of [^c-c827c111a916d9fc631f54b5ee514d95]... employer).
