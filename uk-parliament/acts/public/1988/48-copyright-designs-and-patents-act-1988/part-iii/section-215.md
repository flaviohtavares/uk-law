# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 215

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 215. Ownership of design right.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The designer is the first owner of any design right in a design which is not created [^key-6b225df539423d748adfd28a56570da9]... in the course of employment.

(2)[^key-5ad719f6526928c636c6a2292ad7763b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Where [^key-a9d2ade14a4b4eca1f8efb219d3d9720]... a design is created by an employee in the course of his employment, his employer is the first owner of any design right in the design.

(4) If a design qualifies for design right protection by virtue of section 220 (qualification by reference to first marketing of articles made to the design), the above rules do not apply and the person by whom the articles in question are marketed is the first owner of the design right.
