# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 224

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 224. Assignment of right in registered design presumed to carry with it design right.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a design consisting of a design in which design right subsists is registered under the [^c13760931]Registered Designs Act 1949 and the proprietor of the registered design is also the design right owner, an assignment of the right in the registered design shall be taken to be also an assignment of the design right, unless a contrary intention appears.
