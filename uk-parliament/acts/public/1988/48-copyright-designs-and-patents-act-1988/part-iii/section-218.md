# Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 218

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 218. Qualification by reference to designer.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to a design which is not created [^key-983637f2f84b69027c61a38f5228e539]...in the course of employment.

(2) A design to which this section applies qualifies for design right protection if the designer is [^key-64e62581c74d6578a97b7bbee581da53]... a qualifying person.

(3) A joint design to which this section applies qualifies for design right protection if any of the designers is [^key-98e4b940c2c122b8e6632b706aa80874]... a qualifying person.

(4) Where a joint design qualifies for design right protection under this section, only those designers who are [^key-6b432ec9bb194338f0d7444f685ae2ea]... qualifying persons are entitled to design right under section 215(1) (first ownership of design right: entitlement of designer).
