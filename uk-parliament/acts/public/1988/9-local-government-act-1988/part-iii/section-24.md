## 24. Power to provide financial assistance for privately let housing accommodation.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] Subject to section 25 below, a local housing authority shall have power to provide any person with financial assistance for the purposes of, or in connection with, the acquisition, construction, conversion, rehabilitation, improvement, maintenance or management (whether by that person or by another) of any property which is or is intended to be privately let as housing accommodation. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] For the purposes of this section and section 25 below a local authority provide a person with financial assistance if they do or agree to do any of the following, that is to say— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] make a grant or loan to that person; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] guarantee or join in guaranteeing the performance of any obligation owed to or by that person; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] indemnify or join in indemnifying that person in respect of any liabilities, loss or damage; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]
- (d (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] if that person is a body corporate, acquire share or loan capital in that person. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] For the purposes of this section property is privately let as housing accommodation at any time when— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] it is occupied as housing accommodation in pursuance of a lease or licence of any description or under a statutory tenancy; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] the immediate landlord of the occupier of the property is a person other than a local authority in England and Wales or a public-sector landlord in Scotland. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] Neither section 438 of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] [^c13394841]Housing Act 1985 (local authority mortgage interest rates) nor section 219 of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] [^c13394851]Housing (Scotland) Act 1987 (local authority home-loan interest rates) shall apply in relation to anything done under this section. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]
