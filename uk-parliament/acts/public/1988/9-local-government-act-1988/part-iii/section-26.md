- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] may be given in relation to a particular case or in relation to such description of cases (including cases described by reference to a particular local authority or a particular manner of providing assistance or benefits) as may be specified in the consent; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] except in relation to anything already done or agreed to be done on the authority of the consent, may be varied or revoked by a notice given or published by the Secretary of State in such manner as he may consider appropriate. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] In determining whether to give a consent for the purposes of section 25 above in relation to any assistance or benefit or whether to vary or revoke a consent so given, and in determining to what (if any) conditions such a consent should be subject, the Secretary of State— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]

- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] may take into account any other matter whatever which he considers relevant. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]

(2A (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7ae357497360bd017833d04a5dd7aff3][^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] Before determining whether to consent under section 25 to the exercise of a power, the Secretary of State shall consult the Regulator of Social Housing if— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]

- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] exercise of the power would involve the disposal by the authority of social housing (within the meaning of Part 2 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008). *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] Where before the passing of this Act any statement has been made by or on behalf of the Secretary of State— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]

- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] that, if any such enactment is passed, his statement is to be treated as a consent for the purposes of that enactment, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]

that statement shall have effect, both for the purposes of subsection (4) of section 25 above and after the passing of this Act, as if it were a consent given for the purposes of that section on the conditions (if any) specified in the statement. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]

- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63] in the case of a consent relating to any assistance or benefits provided before the passing of this Act, as if the consent had been such a statement; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cef1bc3d2ec8fb3cb2b08fa2c9a99c63]
