# Local Government Act 1988, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-02-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 34. Arrangements under Employment and Training Act 1973.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In section 45(2) of the [^c13395431]Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1982 (local authorities having power to enter into arrangements under [^c13395441]Employment and Training Act 1973) after paragraph (a) there shall be inserted—

> - (aa) an authority established by Part III or IV of the Local Government Act 1985 or by an order under section 10 of that Act;
> - (ab) a body corporate established by an order under section 67 of that Act.

(2) This section shall be deemed to have come into force, as regards any authority or body concerned, on the day the authority or body was established.

(3) This section extends to England and Wales only.
