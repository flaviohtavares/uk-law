# Local Government Act 1988, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-02-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 40. Finance.

*Extent: E+W+S*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenses of the Secretary of State incurred in consequence of this Act;
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable out of money so provided under any other enactment.
