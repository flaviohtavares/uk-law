## Inquests: special cases

### 13. Order to hold investigation.[^key-d781cb48eda58b30337beb600f73ac98]

(1) This section applies where, on an application by or under the authority of the Attorney-General, the High Court is satisfied as respects a coroner (“the coroner concerned”) either—

- (a) that he refuses or neglects to hold an inquest or an investigation[^key-21f5e999c95dc54b87bc623e95949eaf] which ought to be held; or
- (b) where an inquest or an investigation[^key-c698b7d79ea07fd0b964b58fa747fe68] has been held by him, that (whether by reason of fraud, rejection of evidence, irregularity of proceedings, insufficiency of inquiry, the discovery of new facts or evidence or otherwise) it is necessary or desirable in the interests of justice that an investigation (or as the case may by, another investigation)[^key-aa15c01ad09c43d308da9342648449cc] should be held.

(2) The High Court may—

- (a) order an investigation under Part 1 of the Coroners and Justice Act 2009[^key-1aebcf628a7a5cd13500a235684b68fb] to be held into the death either—
  - (i) by the coroner concerned; or
  - (ii) by a senior coroner, area coroner or assistant coroner in the same coroner area[^key-a0646142a0721f46ef04d7ad4c7eef24];
- (b) order the coroner concerned to pay such costs of and incidental to the application as to the court may appear just; and
- (c) where an inquest has been held, quash any inquisition on, or determination or finding made at[^key-dad89c0516e6215caf46a5ca7024c0fa] that inquest.

(3)[^M_F_dc13462d-e003-4b2d-8839-f7fef7fef870] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-7e6a07949c78d6fe5518176e4ce022e1] For the purposes of this section, “coroner” means a coroner appointed under section 1 of this Act, or a senior coroner, area coroner or assistant coroner appointed under the Coroners and Justice Act 2009.

(5)[^key-b6f72dc23e4c3084954a28d403d18da9] This section does not apply in relation to a death that resulted directly from the Troubles (which has the same meaning as in Schedule 1A to the Coroners and Justice Act 2009 — see paragraph 4 of that Schedule).
