## 121. Continuation of trials for murder on death or discharge of juror.

*Extent: E+W*

In section 16(2) of the [^c12795001]Juries Act 1974 (cases where trial not to proceed on death or discharge of juror without assent of prosecution and accused) the words “for murder or shall cease to have effect.
