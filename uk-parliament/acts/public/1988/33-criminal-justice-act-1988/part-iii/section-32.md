## 32. Evidence through television links when witness abroad: service courts.[^c19022041][^c-e6749b7a0e7a60e8022f025ddcb2cf1b]

*Extent: E+W*

(A1)[^c-27058cb8e33d4f2ccc42e4385d422c63] This section applies only so far as provided by an order under paragraph 8 of Schedule 13.

(1)[^c12785781][^c20679791] A person other than the accused may give evidence through a live television link in proceedings to which subsection (1A) below applies[^c12785791] if—

- (a)[^c19452221] the witness is outside the United Kingdom; [^c19026842]. . .
- (b)[^c19026841] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

but evidence may not be so given without the leave of the court.

(1A)[^c-a087196012a0c4434ee5583fcfef8d65][^c12785831] This subsection applies—

- (a) to trials on indictment, appeals to the criminal division of the Court of Appeal and hearings of references under section 9 of the Criminal Appeal Act 1995[^c12785841]; and
- (b) to proceedings in youth courts , appeals to the Crown Court arising out of such proceedings and hearings of references under section 11 of the Criminal Appeal Act 1995 so arising[^c12785851].

(2)[^c19027051] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c12785781][^c19452221][^c20679791] A statement made on oath by a witness outside the United Kingdom and given in evidence through a link by virtue of this section shall be treated for the purposes of section 1 of the [^c12785971]Perjury Act 1911 as having been made in the proceedings in which it is given in evidence.
