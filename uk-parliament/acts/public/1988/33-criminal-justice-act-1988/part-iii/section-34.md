## 34. Abolition of requirement of corroboration for unsworn evidence of children.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c12786461] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c12786471] Any requirement whereby at a trial on indictment it is obligatory for the court to give the jury a warning about convicting the accused on the uncorroborated evidence of a child is abrogated [^c12786481]. . ..

(3) Unsworn evidence admitted by virtue of section 56 of the Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999[^c12786491] may corroborate evidence (sworn or unsworn) given by any other person.
