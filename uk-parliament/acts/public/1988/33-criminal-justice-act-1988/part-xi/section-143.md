# Criminal Justice Act 1988, section 143

## 143. Assistance to Isle of Man and Channel Islands.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In subsection (1) of section 2 of the [^c12795701]Criminal Justice Act 1987 (investigation powers of Director of Serious Fraud Office) after the word “above," there shall be inserted the words “or, on a request made by the Attorney General of the Isle of Man, Jersey or Guernsey, under legislation corresponding to that section and having effect in the Island whose Attorney General makes the request,".
