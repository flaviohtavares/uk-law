## 167. Acquisition of easements etc. under Prison Act 1952.

*Extent: E+W*

The power to purchase land conferred on the Secretary of State by section 36 of the [^c12796691]Prison Act 1952 (acquisition of land for prisons) shall include and be deemed always to have included power to purchase easements and other rights over land, including easements and other rights not previously in existence.
