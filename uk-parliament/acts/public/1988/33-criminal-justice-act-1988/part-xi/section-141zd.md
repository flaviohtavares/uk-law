# Criminal Justice Act 1988, section 141ZD

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 141ZD) (Extent: N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note). (not yet in force: Prohibition on importation of offensive weapons: burdens of proof) (Extent: N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c20680041][^c12795561][^c-1bb6df0d22dde4f08d1c81de7d079e3c][^c-09cc1c3982c7b7b568ead291bf60af35][^c-1bb6df0d22dde4f08d1c81de7d079e3c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1) (Extent: N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1bb6df0d22dde4f08d1c81de7d079e3c] *(not yet in force: This section applies for the purposes of proceedings for an offence under the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979 relating to a weapon the importation of which is prohibited by section 141ZB above.)* *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1bb6df0d22dde4f08d1c81de7d079e3c]

((not yet in force: 2) (Extent: N.I.) ((not yet in force: 3) (Extent: N.I.)
