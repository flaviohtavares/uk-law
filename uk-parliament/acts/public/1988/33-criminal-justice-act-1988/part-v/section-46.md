# Criminal Justice Act 1988, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (43).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 46. Maximum term of imprisonment on summary conviction under Prevention of Crime Act 1953 and maximum fine under Restriction of Offensive Weapons Act 1959.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In section 1(1)(a) of the [^c12787351]Prevention of Crime Act 1953 “**six months**” shall be substituted for “three months".

(2) The maximum fine that may be imposed for an offence under section 1 of the [^c12787361]Restriction of Offensive Weapons Act 1959 shall be a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.

(3) This section shall not have effect in relation to anything done before it comes into force.
