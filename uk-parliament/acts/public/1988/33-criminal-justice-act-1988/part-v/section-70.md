# Criminal Justice Act 1988, section 70

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 70. Forfeiture for drug offences.

*Extent: E+W*

In section 27(1) of the [^c12787931]Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 (forfeiture on conviction of an offence under that Act) after the words “under this Act" there shall be inserted the words “or a drug trafficking offence, as defined in section 38(1) of the [^c12787941]Drug Trafficking Offences Act 1986".
