# Norfolk and Suffolk Broads Act 1988, section 17B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/4

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17B. Duty of public bodies etc to contribute to the Broads Plan[^c-e98b5a7a35c0b8b8040a2afa8516e9d4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision—

- (a) requiring a relevant authority other than a devolved Welsh authority to contribute to the implementation or review of the Broads Plan;
- (b) setting out how such a relevant authority may or must do so.

(2) In this section—

- “**devolved Welsh authority**” has the same meaning as in the Government of Wales Act 2006 (see, in particular, section 157A of that Act);
- “**relevant authority**” has the same meaning as in section 17A.
