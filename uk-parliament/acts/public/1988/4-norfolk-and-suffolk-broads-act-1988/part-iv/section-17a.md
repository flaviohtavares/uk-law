# Norfolk and Suffolk Broads Act 1988, section 17A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1988/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17A. General duty of public bodies etc.[^c10506201]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In exercising or performing any functions in relation to, or so as to affect, land in the Broads, a relevant authority must seek to further[^c-ebd55a8f7fcebdb7cc61937c6ede7192] the purposes of—

- (a)[^c-21126eadf35153999b353cedf88525ab] conserving and enhancing the natural beauty, wildlife and cultural heritage of the Broads;
- (b) promoting opportunities for the understanding and enjoyment of the special qualities of the Broads by the public; and
- (c) protecting the interests of navigation.

(1A)[^c-d13dc3a4e5bcf5f9b57a35f4718ad45e] The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision about how a relevant authority is to comply with the duty under subsection (1) (including provision about things that the authority may, must or must not do to comply with the duty).

(2) The following are relevant authorities for the purposes of this section—

- (a) any Minister of the Crown,
- (b) any public body,
- (c) any statutory undertaker,
- (d) any person holding public office.

(3) In subsection (2)—

- “ **public body** ” includes
  - (a) a county council, district council or parish council;
  - (b) a joint planning board within the meaning of section 2 of the [^c10506211] Town and Country Planning Act 1990;
  - (c) a joint committee appointed under section 102(1)(b) of the [^c10506221] Local Government Act 1972;
- “ **public office** ” means—
  - (a) an office under Her Majesty;
  - (b) an office created or continued in existence by a public general Act; or
  - (c) an office the remuneration in respect of which is paid out of money provided by Parliament.
