# Norfolk and Suffolk Broads Act 1988, section 20

## 20. Annual report.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Authority shall, as soon as is reasonably practicable after the end of each financial year—

- (a) prepare and publish a report on the discharge of its functions during that financial year; and
- (b) send a copy of the report to each of the appointing authorities.

(2) The Secretary of State shall lay a copy of every report under this section, together with a copy of the Authority’s statement of accounts for the financial year in question, before each House of Parliament.
