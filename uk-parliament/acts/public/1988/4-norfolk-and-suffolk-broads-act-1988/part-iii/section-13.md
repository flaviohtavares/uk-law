# Norfolk and Suffolk Broads Act 1988, section 13

## 13. Navigation charges.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-ee5efbab316d51554722a1dd51d25316] The Authority may determine and recover tolls in respect of vessels moored, used or navigated on adjacent waters as well as in the navigation area.

(2)[^c-b82720f4972cb9736d6ff90ae5bc1471] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-3f7d9d813db7ee68dc18f3ee860edc36] The Authority shall consult the Navigation Committee before determining the level of any tolls or other charges to be imposed in respect of the navigation area or adjacent waters.

(4) No charges shall be taken or recovered by the Authority in respect of anything (including any farm animal) which—

- (a) is carried on any waterway within the navigation area by the owner of land which abuts such a waterway; and
- (b) is for use in connection with any agricultural activity carried out on other land which he owns and which also abuts that waterway, another such waterway or any part of the Haven.
