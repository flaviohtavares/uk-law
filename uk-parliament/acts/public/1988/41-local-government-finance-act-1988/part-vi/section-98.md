## 98. Other transfers between funds.[^c13072671][^c-4102bd57b7dfa1935e1ada6872724849][^c-0c54dee3428636abb7195dea61c0ea12]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c13072681] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c13072681] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Regulations under section 89(5) above may include provision that—

- (a) any sum to which they relate shall be transferred from an authority’s collection fund to its general fund [^c13072701]. . .;
- (b) the sum so transferred shall be held, invested or otherwise used in such manner as may be prescribed;
- (c) a sum equal to the sum transferred shall be transferred to the authority’s collection fund from its general fund [^c13072701]. . .;
- (d)[^c13072721] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) If the Secretary of State directs it to do so, a billing authority[^c13072731] shall transfer from its collection fund to its general fund [^c13072741]. . . such an amount as is specified in, or calculated in a manner specified in, the direction; and the transfer shall be made at such time as is specified in the direction.

(5) If the Secretary of State directs it to do so, a billing authority shall transfer to its collection fund from its general fund [^c13072761]. . . such an amount as is specified in, or calculated in a manner specified in, the direction; and the transfer shall be made at such time as is specified in the direction.

(6) Different directions may be given to different authorities under subsection (4) or (5)[^c13072771] above.
