## 84P. Information deadlines[^c-f30e0be8042ee7b0528fb9726a5c1d7c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers[^c-ed15ac5430b62c79c223308b08ac26dc] may set a deadline for the receipt of information to be taken into account by them[^c-5faa9d2c81910ef44fce8ed480340d19] when making a calculation —[^c-4e19f86db7ef0cfb1abe0338bfc4cf0a]

- (a) under section 84J(1) or by virtue of section 84J(2), or
- (b) under section 84M(2) or by virtue of section 84M(4).

(2) Different deadlines may be set under subsection (1) in relation to different kinds of information.

(3) A deadline under subsection (1) shall have effect only if the Welsh Ministers notify[^c-1534037d0533d596daf8e95d1d2535fc] each receiving authority concerned of the deadline and of the information to which it relates.

(4) Notification under subsection (3) may be given at any time before the making of the calculation to which the deadline relates, including a time before the making of a determination under section 84F for the year concerned.

(5) When making a calculation in relation to which a deadline under subsection (1) has effect, the Welsh Ministers[^c-a13650ba0b80c3e871f0719b2e8d108d] shall leave information to which the deadline applies out of account if it is received after the passing of the deadline.
