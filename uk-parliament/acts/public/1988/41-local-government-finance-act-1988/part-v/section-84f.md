## 84F. Determination of grant[^c-f30e0be8042ee7b0528fb9726a5c1d7c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers[^c-33a90d2d5d3623167275d06aa7fcb9c0] shall for each financial year make—

- (a) a determination under subsection (2), or
- (b) a determination under each of subsections (3) and (4).

(2) A determination under this subsection shall state—

- (a) the total amount of revenue support grant for the year,
- (b) the amount of the grant the Welsh Ministers propose[^c-c84d33cc21ccb3a392ccd467edee952e] to pay to receiving authorities, and
- (c) the amount of the grant the Welsh Ministers propose[^c-073f28e896d011a9b30681a3353ba764] to pay to each specified body.

(3) A determination under this subsection shall state—

- (a) the total amount of revenue support grant for the year for—
  - (i) receiving authorities other than police and crime commissioners[^c-affcfa00917c0a88136c0eb3c04109f6], and
  - (ii) specified bodies,
- (b) the amount of the grant the Welsh Ministers propose[^c-11ac531706cec46c594b50479b2772d7] to pay to receiving authorities that are not police and crime commissioners, and
- (c) the amount of the grant the Welsh Ministers propose[^c-1108b1e5d1626c779c74d15acdb1571d] to pay to each specified body.

(4) A determination under this subsection shall state the total amount of revenue support grant for the year for police and crime commissioners.

(5) Before making a determination under this section, the Welsh Ministers[^c-cbcca51f6a306238b6b3f33264d7446f] shall consult such representatives of local government as appear to the Welsh Ministers[^c-ae78f206a370bfab10f38a410a86d01b] to be appropriate.

(6) Different amounts may be stated under subsection (2)(c) or (3)(c) in relation to different specified bodies.
