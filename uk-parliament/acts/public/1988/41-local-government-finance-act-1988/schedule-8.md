## SCHEDULE 8 — Non-Domestic Rating: Pooling

Section 60.

### Part I — Non-Domestic Rating Accounts

#### The accounts

**1.** (1) In accordance with this Schedule the Welsh Ministers[^c-7dcf443d3c6eed84dc79d55c23e14e08] shall keep for each chargeable financial year an account (to be called a non-domestic rating account).

(2) The Welsh Ministers[^c-ef758512a4f543af15a69c339b52f317]—

- (a) shall keep each account in such form as they consider appropriate[^c-5c52c93d91b94ba43ddf43a278af4132], and
- (b) shall at such time as they consider appropriate[^c-ae4f945d363838c80df7c86ecaa4e0bb] send copies of each account to the Auditor General for Wales[^c-46afbaffbdfb540ee3884673b865e1a1].

(3)[^c-03af7cca21e9903889ed9ad5d4d53a65] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-4decd0895ea60fe316ac77d4646ae48a] The Auditor General for Wales shall examine, certify and report on any account of which copies are sent to him under sub-paragraph (2) and shall lay copies of the account and of his report before the National Assembly for Wales[^c-a6f865682011c471809f8545a9173436].

#### Credits and debits

**2.**[^c13079701][^c13079711] (1)[^c13079721] For each chargeable financial year the following shall be credited (as items of account) to the account kept for the year—

- (a) sums received by the Welsh Ministers[^c-1c05d8f44bb135c0482488389d0ee703] in the year under section 54 above,
- (b) sums received by them[^c-72432db7fa059ef37fdcc837854e1c3b] in the year under section 59[^c13079731] above, and
- (c) sums received by them[^c-7f347247222ce2547f7d259b678ea0ad] in the year under paragraph 5 below or regulations made under sub-paragraph (15) of that paragraph[^c13079741].

(2) For each chargeable financial year the following shall be debited (as items of account) to the account kept for the year—

- (a) payments made by the Welsh Ministers[^c-028a14c3ec39b8380cb77af6b5cd32a2] in the year under paragraph 5(10) or (14) below[^c13079751], under regulations made for the purpose mentioned in paragraph 4(7) below[^c-b63d203391ceca6e16ffef15137c81d6] or under regulations made under paragraph 5(15) or 6(5) below, and
- (b) payments made by them[^c-f9ec6bb0ce8431dfb67f81a586057b8d] in the year under paragraph 12 or 15 below[^c13079761] (as the case may be).

**3.**[^c-2ae80973ead293f29198f45593f9c0ce] (1) As soon as is reasonably practicable after the end of each chargeable financial year the Welsh Ministers[^c-80ec8e1388885d90a3433869c5f95f6f] shall calculate the following—

- (a) the aggregate of the items of account credited to the account kept for the year, and
- (b) the aggregate of the items of account debited to the account kept for the year.

(2) If the aggregate mentioned in sub-paragraph (1)(a) above exceeds that mentioned in sub-paragraph (1)(b) above, a sum equal to the excess shall be—

- (a) debited (as an item of account) to the account kept for the year, and
- (b) credited (as an item of account) to the account kept for the next financial year.

(3) If the aggregate mentioned in sub-paragraph (1)(b) above exceeds that mentioned in sub-paragraph (1)(a) above, a sum equal to the excess shall be—

- (a) credited (as an item of account) to the account kept for the year, and
- (b) debited (as an item of account) to the account kept for the next financial year.

- (c) specify the basis on which the Welsh Ministers propose[^c-784e2c66fb290513c0e2dff9b6845640] to distribute among those authorities the amount specified under paragraph (b) above.

(2) Before making such a report as is mentioned in sub-paragraph (1) above, the Welsh Ministers[^c-008f2dbcb682e1112a82150f20937317] shall notify the general nature of the basis of distribution proposed to be specified in the report to such representatives of local government as appear to the Welsh Ministers to be appropriate.

**11B.** (1)[^c-2aead2e44ea89abd2cceeb460388aafa] This paragraph applies where—

- (a) the Assembly approves by resolution a local government finance report that is one of two being made by the Welsh Ministers for a particular financial year, and
- (b) as respects that year the Welsh Ministers decide as mentioned in paragraph 9A(1)(b)(ii) above.

(2) The amount specified under paragraph 11A(1)(b) above in the report shall be distributed among and paid to the receiving authorities to which the report relates in accordance with this paragraph and paragraph 12 below.

(3) As soon as is reasonably practicable after the report is approved by the Assembly, the Welsh Ministers shall calculate[^c-8868563f11e02140b92872e41704fea8] in relation to each of those authorities what sum falls to be paid to the authority as its share of the amount specified under paragraph 11A(1)(b) above.

(4) The calculation under sub-paragraph (3) above shall be in accordance with the basis of distribution specified in the report.

(5) The Welsh Ministers[^c-b169ce18b7c5219afa69af772b16eca6] may carry out the sub-paragraph (3) calculation again at any time before the end of the financial year immediately following the one to which the report relates.

(6) The power under sub-paragraph (5) above may only be exercised once and ceases to be exercisable if the Assembly approves by resolution an amending report made by the Welsh Ministers under paragraph 13 below.[^c-c3a206585f78e9652d91b82ad00b0f42]

(7) As soon as is reasonably practicable after making a calculation under sub-paragraph (3) or (5) above, the Welsh Ministers[^c-341c3998d8a76f24d20ca8ea17fa0250] shall inform each receiving authority to which the report relates of the outcome, so far as relating to it.

**11C.** (1) The Welsh Ministers[^c-6d3a7e0b291336cd4ecea4f08e100f6c] may set a deadline for the receipt of information to be taken into account by them[^c-9a4a7a50ad9a939f56e3edcf77ac1de6] when making a calculation under paragraph 11B(3) or (5) above.

(2) Different deadlines may be set under sub-paragraph (1) above in relation to different kinds of information.

(3) A deadline under sub-paragraph (1) above shall only have effect if the Welsh Ministers inform[^c-477560c1257843d60deb8368b7ceb185] each receiving authority concerned of the deadline and of the information to which it relates.

(4) Notification under sub-paragraph (3) above may be given at any time before the making of the calculation to which the deadline relates, including a time before the distributable amount for the year is calculated under paragraph 9 above.

(5) When making a calculation in relation to which a deadline under sub-paragraph (1) above has effect, the Welsh Ministers[^c-412304203fa9433466f04a860a1ca9eb] shall leave information to which the deadline applies out of account if it is received after the passing of the deadline.

**12.**[^c13080311][^c13080321] (1) Where a calculation is made under paragraph 11(3) or 11B(3)[^c-3b976caf7a60287cc42a080124015833] above the Welsh Ministers[^c-b4fe72842966c507607f6d78ba8f0118] shall pay to each receiving authority any sum calculated as falling to be paid to it.

(2) The sum shall be paid in instalments of such amounts, and at such times in the financial year to which the report relates (the financial year concerned), as the Welsh Ministers determine[^c-504f6401053b0b1e1c1fe6f21890bdc0] [^c-e97a04b1f5520648d02ed9bbcb5b4df0]... .

(3) Where a calculation is made under paragraph 11(4) or 11B(5)[^c-19e0aacac00ad93595825de10039d829] above and the sum it shows as falling to be paid to a receiving authority exceeds that shown as falling to be paid to it by the calculation for the financial year concerned under paragraph 11(3) or 11B(3)[^c-f867495aecc8bca70c3bc5cdc41b37b9] above, the Welsh Ministers[^c-159ed1879793f2e335b405afe20c9cdd] shall pay to the authority a sum equal to the difference.

(4) The sum shall be paid at such time, or in instalments of such amounts and at such times, as the Welsh Ministers determine[^c-1166beba1e24b4350e73ef072f19f497][^c-6a39fa2bae146552a3637c619e8afcde]... ; but any such time must fall after the end of the financial year concerned.

(5) Where a calculation is made under paragraph 11(4) or 11B(5)[^c-2b74d4c23bc239b7f016440d7f40553e] above and the sum it shows as falling to be paid to a receiving authority is less than that shown as falling to be paid to it by the calculation for the financial year concerned under paragraph 11(3) or 11B(3)[^c-18acc3d314e173cb4b2a6c8d2fc66719] above, a sum equal to the difference shall be paid by the authority to the Welsh Ministers[^c-791dad2f6d45c182d9633c1111ecd7e4].

(6) The sum shall be paid on such day after the end of the financial year concerned as the Welsh Ministers may specify; and if it is not paid on or before that day it shall be recoverable in a court of competent jurisdiction.

#### Distribution: amending reports[^c13080331]

**13.**[^c13080361] (1) Subject to sub-paragraph (6) below, after a local government finance report has been made the Welsh Ministers[^c-8818ea41d6f5693be845d338adfbb036] may, at any time before the end of the financial year following the financial year concerned, make in relation to the report one or more amending reports under this paragraph.

(2) An amending report under this paragraph shall contain amendments to the basis of distribution specified in the local government finance report.

(3) Before making the report the Welsh Ministers[^c-348dee86208c3a9d494f62ff800888da] shall notify to such representatives of local government as appear to them[^c-44bd3a5f1e3cbce5d191ac5146998aeb] to be appropriate the general nature of the amendments which they propose[^c-1fc3d5c82411d4cc1fb16a6215bb80d7] to make.

(4) The report shall be laid before the Assembly[^c-39871eee700a41fabcdbafdf1db2b947].

(5) As soon as is reasonably practicable after the report is laid before the Assembly[^c-ee542be6fc49ca937f899c3706b0175f], the Welsh Ministers[^c-2c5d22e0c649737bc8bcc051d21df972] shall send a copy of it to each receiving authority.

(6) Where an amending report under this paragraph has been approved by resolution of the Assembly[^c-69b3b5f43c7e033cdc43363485d350c5], the Welsh Ministers[^c-a9c82ec57856025f406a6bbd1137dc6a] may not make a subsequent amending report under this paragraph in relation to the same local government finance report.

**14.**[^c13080371] (1) As soon as is reasonably practicable after an amending report made under paragraph 13 above has been approved by resolution of the Assembly[^c-48cecc2c3d673f1d9a7647ab33d08a7b], the Welsh Ministers[^c-af8774c1d4ae82b16716294d1ba501aa] shall calculate what sum falls to be paid to each receiving authority as its share of the distributable amount for the year in accordance with the basis of distribution specified in the local government finance report as amended by the amending report.

(2) Subject to sub-paragraph (3) below, after making a calculation under sub-paragraph (1) above the Welsh Ministers[^c-c8231e859601bcbdcf882d0633639507] may make one further calculation of what sum falls to be paid to each receiving authority as its share of the distributable amount for the year in accordance with that basis of distribution.

(3) A calculation may not be made under sub-paragraph (2) above after whichever is the later of—

- (a) the end of the financial year following the financial year concerned, and
- (b) the end of the period of 3 months beginning with the day on which the amending report is approved by resolution of the Assembly[^c-d593f54eebe7da85ff0606eb7c566690].

(4) Sub-paragraphs (6) to (8) of paragraph 11 above apply in relation to calculations made under sub-paragraphs (1) and (2) above as they apply in relation to calculations made under sub-paragraphs (3) and (4) of that paragraph.

**14A.**[^c-448c2a83408754d426f11ed84a948ceb] (1) This paragraph applies where—

- (a) a report is made under paragraph 13 above amending a report (“the original report”) that is one of two local government finance reports that the Welsh Ministers make[^c-7bc39334598b807c0226c5a38a6674ad] for a particular financial year, and
- (b) as respects that year the Welsh Ministers decide[^c-6f8776acfe48dfc56e81d8d934e5058b] as mentioned in paragraph 9A(1)(b)(ii) above.

(2) As soon as is reasonably practicable after the amending report is approved by resolution of[^c-02d1f4d1c7d3d86b149d57b6f402b07c] the Assembly, the Welsh Ministers shall calculate[^c-07c6e926d956b71669532fc5d494d8c4] in relation to each of the authorities to which the original report relates what sum falls to be paid to the authority as its share of the amount specified under paragraph 11A(1)(b) above in the original report.

(3) The calculation under sub-paragraph (2) above shall be in accordance with the amended basis of distribution.

(4) The Welsh Ministers may carry out[^c-4e6b40ef4e1fb32399a1b914f5cedf89] the sub-paragraph (2) calculation again at any time before—

- (a) the end of the financial year immediately following the one to which the original report relates, or
- (b) if later, the end of the period of 3 months beginning with the day on which the Assembly approves by resolution[^c-c40151b2791fcdf2e33d878bb3f6a574] the amending report.

(5) The power under sub-paragraph (4) above may only be exercised once.

(6) Paragraphs 11B(7) and 11C above apply in relation to calculations made under sub-paragraphs (2) and (4) above as they apply in relation to calculations made under paragraph 11B(3) and (5) above.

**15.**[^c13080381][^c13080391] (1) This paragraph applies where a calculation (the relevant calculation) is made under paragraph 14(1) or (2) or 14A(2) or (4)[^c-77ed15c0b1dd55eb9c60f84807de0732] above in relation to an amending report.

(2) Where the sum shown by the relevant calculation as falling to be paid to a receiving authority for the financial year concerned exceeds that shown as falling to be paid to it by the relevant previous calculation, the Welsh Ministers[^c-9beb73176d9ed551cfd14899d99fe50b] shall pay to the authority a sum equal to the difference.

(3) The sum shall be paid at such times, or in instalments of such amounts and at such times, as the Welsh Ministers determine[^c-491cc60feda77c2e4c8c187db980e8fa][^c-8c6c54fdc9fa9386848d3f4ac2f5f8c3]...; but any such time must fall after the end of the financial year in which the amending report was made.

(4) Where the sum shown by the relevant calculation as falling to be paid to a receiving authority for the financial year concerned is less than that shown as falling to be paid to it by the relevant previous calculation, a sum equal to the difference shall be paid by the authority to the Welsh Ministers[^c-69de2f8818887996de80d49faa4511b7].

(5) The sum shall be paid on such day after the end of the financial year in which the amending report was made as the Welsh Ministers may specify; and if it is not paid on or before that day it shall be recoverable in a court of competent jurisdiction.

(6) In this paragraph “**the relevant previous calculation**” means—

- (a) in relation to a calculation made under paragraph 14(1) or 14A(2)[^c-2fd872c5f2c383d17ace90296e5668d5] above, the calculation under paragraph 11(3) or 11B(3)[^c-b9ce7e0163a0429f09c83e8c483660db] above or, where a further calculation has been made under paragraph 11(4) or 11B(5)[^c-fafe6cbd50970567fd32353ef59773ae] above, that further calculation;
- (b) in relation to a calculation made under paragraph 14(2) or 14A(4)[^c-73047266b8519819c0e87aaff0b56797] above, the calculation made under paragraph 14(1) or 14A(2) above.

#### Source of payments by Welsh Ministers[^c-d95e6cec4661cc9d0187888aaef66198]

**16.** Sums required for the making of payments by the Welsh Ministers under this Part of this Schedule are to be charged on the Welsh Consolidated Fund.

#### Recovery

##### 16.[^c13080401][^c-c410dbca5d6d1c360b33bee12d7c12f2]

Where an amount has become payable under any provision of this Part of this Schedule, and it has not been paid, it shall be recoverable in a court of competent jurisdiction.
