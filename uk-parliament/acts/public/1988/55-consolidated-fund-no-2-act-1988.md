# Consolidated Fund (No. 2) Act 1988

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Consolidated Fund (No. 2) Act 1988

1988 Chapter 55

An Act to apply certain sums out of the Consolidated Fund to the service of the years ending on 31st March 1989 and 1990.

\[20th December 1988\]

Most Gracious Sovereign,

WE, Your Majesty’s most dutiful and loyal subjects, the Commons of the United Kingdom in Parliament assembled, towards making good the supply which we have cheerfully granted to Your Majesty in this Session of Parliament, have resolved to grant unto Your Majesty the sums hereinafter mentioned; and do therefore most humbly beseech Your Majesty that it may be enacted and be it enacted by the Queen’s most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—

**1.** The Treasury may issue

## 2. Issue out of the Consolidated Fund for the year ending 31st March 1989. out of the Consolidated Fund of the United Kingdom and apply towards making good the supply granted to Her Majesty for the service of the year ending on 31st March 1989 the sum of £1,042,249,000

The Treasury may issue out

## 3. Issue out of the Consolidated Fund for the year ending 31st March 1990. of the Consolidated Fund of the United Kingdom and apply towards making good the supply granted to Her Majesty for the service of the year ending on 31st March 1990 the sum of £50,987,976,000

This Act may be cited as the Consolidated Fund (No. 2) Act 1988.
