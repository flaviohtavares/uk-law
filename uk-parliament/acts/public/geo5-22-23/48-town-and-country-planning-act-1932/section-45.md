# Town and Country Planning Act 1932, section 45

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 45. Saving for Postmaster-General.

The expression " Act of Parliament " in section seven of the [^m00010]Telegraph Act, 1878 (which makes provision as to work done in pursuance of Acts of Parliament which involves alteration in telegraphic lines), shall be construed as including a scheme under this Act.
