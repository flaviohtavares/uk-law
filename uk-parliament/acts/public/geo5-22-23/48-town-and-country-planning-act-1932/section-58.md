# Town and Country Planning Act 1932, section 58

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 58. Short title, commencement and extent.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Town and Country Planning Act, 1932, and, except as otherwise expressly provided, shall come into operation on the first day of April, nineteen hundred and thirty-three.

(2) This Act shall not extend to Northern Ireland.
