# Town and Country Planning Act 1932, section 32

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 32. Application of betterment as capital.

All sums received by a responsible authority by way of betterment, or as proceeds of sale of any land purchased under the powers conferred by this Act, shall be applied in such manner as in the case of the London County Council the Treasury, and in other cases the Minister, may approve towards the discharge of any debt of the authority or otherwise for any purpose for which capital money may be applied.
