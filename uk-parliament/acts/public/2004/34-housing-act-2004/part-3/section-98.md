# Housing Act 2004, section 98

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (145).** Text as at 2026-06-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/34
>

## 98. Other consequences of operating unlicensed houses: restriction on terminating tenancies (affected by repeal, see note) (England) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c2051419][^c-4063d6038ae56a9f4ba9237a25fcaca0][^c-4063d6038ae56a9f4ba9237a25fcaca0][^c-5d1e9d2ca2bc734a2986f1aeb3cca137][^c-4063d6038ae56a9f4ba9237a25fcaca0]

*Extent: W*

(1) No section 21 notice may be given in relation to a shorthold tenancy of the whole or part of an unlicensed house so long as it remains such a house. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4063d6038ae56a9f4ba9237a25fcaca0]

(2) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4063d6038ae56a9f4ba9237a25fcaca0]

- a “**section 21 notice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4063d6038ae56a9f4ba9237a25fcaca0]**” means a notice under section 21(1)(b) or (4)(a) of the Housing Act 1988 (c. 50) (recovery of possession on termination of shorthold tenancy); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4063d6038ae56a9f4ba9237a25fcaca0]
- a “**shorthold tenancy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4063d6038ae56a9f4ba9237a25fcaca0]**” means an assured shorthold tenancy within the meaning of Chapter 2 of Part 1 of that Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4063d6038ae56a9f4ba9237a25fcaca0]
- “**unlicensed house *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4063d6038ae56a9f4ba9237a25fcaca0]**” has the same meaning as in section 96 of this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4063d6038ae56a9f4ba9237a25fcaca0]
