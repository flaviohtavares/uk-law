# Housing Act 2004, section 98A

>

## 98A (Extent: W). Other consequences of operating unlicensed houses: restriction on terminating tenancies (Wales) (Extent: W)[^c-8286c51fe79958821128d43a9f576fe0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) No section 173 notice, no section 186 notice, no paragraph 25B of Schedule 12 notice and no section 194 notice may be given in relation to a standard contract of the whole or part of an unlicensed house as long as it remains such a house. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) In this section— *(Extent: W)*

- “ *(Extent: W)***2016 Act *(Extent: W)***” means the Renting Homes (Wales) Act *(Extent: W)* [2016 (anaw 1) *(Extent: W)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/1); *(Extent: W)*
- “ *(Extent: W)***paragraph 25B of Schedule 12 notice *(Extent: W)***” means a notice under paragraph 25B of Schedule 12 to the 2016 Act (termination of contract by landlord); *(Extent: W)*
- “ *(Extent: W)***section 173 notice *(Extent: W)***” means a notice under section 173 of the 2016 Act (landlord’s notice); *(Extent: W)*
- “ *(Extent: W)***section 186 notice *(Extent: W)***” means a notice under section 186 of the 2016 Act (landlord’s notice in connection with end of term of contract within Schedule 9B); *(Extent: W)*
- “ *(Extent: W)***section 194 notice *(Extent: W)***” means a notice under section 194 of the 2016 Act (landlord’s break clause); *(Extent: W)*
- “ *(Extent: W)***standard contract *(Extent: W)***” has the same meaning as in the 2016 Act (see section 8 of the 2016 Act); *(Extent: W)*
- “ *(Extent: W)***unlicensed house *(Extent: W)***” has the same meaning as in section 96 of this Act. *(Extent: W)*
