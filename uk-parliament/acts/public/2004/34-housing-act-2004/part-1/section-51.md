> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (145).** Text as at 2026-06-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/34

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Repeal of power to improve existing enforcement procedures[^c2051262]

*Extent: E+W*

Omit section 86 of the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996 (c. 53) (power to improve existing enforcement procedures in relation to unfitness for human habitation etc.).
