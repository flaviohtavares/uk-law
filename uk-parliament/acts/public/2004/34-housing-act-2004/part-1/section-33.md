# Housing Act 2004, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (145).** Text as at 2026-06-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/34
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Recovery of possession of premises in order to comply with order[^c2051210]

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in—

- (a) the Rent Act 1977 (c. 42) or the Rent (Agriculture) Act 1976 (c. 80), [^key-992a9dfe037e0059f6a554bfee9b0dc7]...
- (b) Part 1 of the Housing Act 1988 (c. 50), or *(Extent: W)*[^key-7069a857745db79c6a9c611dd9f4a4a2]

*Extent: W*

- (c)[^key-d9dabe06381c3e3073cfeeab8c6779da] Part 9 of the Renting Homes (Wales) Act [2016 (anaw 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/1),

prevents possession being obtained by the owner of any specified premises in relation to which a prohibition order is operative if possession of the premises is necessary for the purpose of complying with the order.
