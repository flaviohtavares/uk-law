# Housing Act 2004, section 75

>

## 75. Other consequences of operating unlicensed HMOs: restriction on terminating tenancies (affected by repeal, see note) (England) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c2051349][^key-65e991b6a05b4e2822bf18307b32d141][^key-65e991b6a05b4e2822bf18307b32d141][^key-232afe92020772c7a72fd18c972c50aa][^key-65e991b6a05b4e2822bf18307b32d141]

*Extent: W*

(1) No section 21 notice may be given in relation to a shorthold tenancy of a part of an unlicensed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-65e991b6a05b4e2822bf18307b32d141] HMO *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-65e991b6a05b4e2822bf18307b32d141] so long as it remains such an HMO. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-65e991b6a05b4e2822bf18307b32d141]

(2) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-65e991b6a05b4e2822bf18307b32d141]

- a “**section 21 notice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-65e991b6a05b4e2822bf18307b32d141]**” means a notice under section 21(1)(b) or (4)(a) of the Housing Act 1988 (c. 50) (recovery of possession on termination of shorthold tenancy); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-65e991b6a05b4e2822bf18307b32d141]
- a “**shorthold tenancy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-65e991b6a05b4e2822bf18307b32d141]**” means an assured shorthold tenancy within the meaning of Chapter 2 of Part 1 of that Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-65e991b6a05b4e2822bf18307b32d141]
- “**unlicensed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-65e991b6a05b4e2822bf18307b32d141] HMO *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-65e991b6a05b4e2822bf18307b32d141]**” has the same meaning as in section 73 of this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-65e991b6a05b4e2822bf18307b32d141]
