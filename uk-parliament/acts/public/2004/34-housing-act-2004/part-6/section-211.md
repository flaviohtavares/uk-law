## 211. Suspension of eviction orders

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c2072489] In section 4(6) of the Caravan Sites Act 1968 (c. 52) (provision for suspension of eviction orders) for the words from “in the following cases” to the end of paragraph (b) substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072489]

> if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072489]
>
> - (a) no site licence under Part 1 of that Act is in force in respect of the site, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072489]
> - (b) paragraph 11 or 11A of Schedule 1 to the Caravan Sites and Control of Development Act 1960 (c. 2) does not apply; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072489]

.

(2) The amendment made by subsection (1) does not apply in relation to proceedings begun before the day on which this section comes into force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072489]
