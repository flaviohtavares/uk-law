# Housing Act 2004, section 215A

## 215A. Statutory periodic tenancies: deposit received before 6 April 2007 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6b584d157e9b20e62cf3051912d526ae][^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7][^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]

- (a) before 6 April 2007, a tenancy deposit has been received by a landlord in connection with a fixed term shorthold tenancy, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]
- (b) on or after that date, a periodic shorthold tenancy is deemed to arise under section 5 of the Housing Act 1988 on the coming to an end of the fixed term tenancy, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]
- (c) on the coming to an end of the fixed term tenancy, all or part of the deposit paid in connection with the fixed term tenancy is held in connection with the periodic tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]
- (d) the requirements of section 213(3), (5) and (6) have not been complied with by the landlord in relation to the deposit held in connection with the periodic tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]

(2) If, on the commencement date— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]

- (a) the periodic tenancy is in existence, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]
- (b) all or part of the deposit paid in connection with the fixed term tenancy continues to be held in connection with the periodic tenancy, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]

section 213 applies in respect of the deposit that continues to be held in connection with the periodic tenancy, and any additional deposit held in connection with that tenancy, with the modifications set out in subsection (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]

(3) The modifications are that, instead of the things referred to in section 213(3) and (5) being required to be done within the time periods set out in section 213(3) and (6)(b), those things are required to be done— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]

- (a) before the end of the period of 90 days beginning with the commencement date, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]
- (b) (if earlier) before the first day after the commencement date on which a court does any of the following in respect of the periodic tenancy— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]
  - (i) determines an application under section 214 or decides an appeal against a determination under that section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7] makes a determination as to whether to make an order for possession in proceedings under section 21 of the Housing Act 1988 or decides an appeal against such a determination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]

(4) If, on the commencement date— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]

- (a) the periodic tenancy is no longer in existence, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]
- (b) no deposit continues to be held in connection with the periodic tenancy, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]

the requirements of section 213(3), (5) and (6) are treated as if they had been complied with by the landlord in relation to any deposit that was held in connection with the periodic tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]

(5) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]**the commencement date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]**” means the date on which the Deregulation Act 2015 is passed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae86811922a729e3b1d19a97df44ba7]
