# Housing Act 2004, section 209

## 209. Protected sites to include sites for gypsies

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c2072487] Section 1 of the Caravan Sites Act 1968 (c. 52) (application of provisions for protection of residential occupiers of caravan sites) is amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072487]

(2) In subsection (2) (under which “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072487]**protected site *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072487]**” includes certain local authority sites) for “paragraph 11 of Schedule 1 to that Act (exemption of land occupied by local authorities) substitute “paragraph 11 or 11A of Schedule 1 to that Act (exemption of gypsy and other local authority sites)”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072487]

(3) The amendment made by subsection (2) above does not affect the operation of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072487]

- (a) section 2 of the Act (minimum length of notice) in relation to any notice given before the day on which this section comes into force, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072487]
- (b) section 3 of the Act (protection from eviction) in relation to any conduct occurring before that day, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072487]
- (c) section 4 of the Act (suspension of eviction orders) in relation to any proceedings begun before that day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072487]

(4) In subsection (3)(b) the reference to section 3 of the Act is to that section whether as amended by section 210 of this Act or otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072487]
