# Housing Act 2004, section 210

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (145).** Text as at 2026-06-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/34

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 210. Extension of protection from harassment for occupiers of mobile homes

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c2072488] Section 3 of the Caravan Sites Act 1968 (protection of occupiers against eviction and harassment) is amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488]

(2) In subsection (1) (offence where person, with the specified intent, does acts calculated to interfere with the peace or comfort of the occupier *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488] etc. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488]) for “calculated to interfere” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488] “likely to interfere” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488].

(3) After subsection (1) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488]

> (1A) Subject to the provisions of this section, the owner of a protected site or his agent shall be guilty of an offence under this section if, whether during the subsistence or after the expiration or determination of a residential contract— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488]
>
> - (a) he does acts likely to interfere with the peace or comfort of the occupier or persons residing with him, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488]
> - (b) he persistently withdraws or withholds services or facilities reasonably required for the occupation of the caravan as a residence on the site, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488]
>
> and (in either case) he knows, or has reasonable cause to believe, that that conduct is likely to cause the occupier to do any of the things mentioned in subsection (1)(c)(i) or (ii) of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488]
>
> (1B) References in subsection (1A) of this section to the owner of a protected site include references to a person with an estate or interest in the site which is superior to that of the owner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488]

(4) In subsection (3) (penalties for offences), for the words from “be liable” onwards substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488]

> be liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488]
>
> - (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months, or to both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488]
> - (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years, or to both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488]

(5) After subsection (4) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488]

> (4A) In proceedings for an offence under subsection (1A) of this section it shall be a defence to prove that the accused had reasonable grounds for doing the acts or withdrawing or withholding the services or facilities in question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488]

(6) The amendments made by this section do not apply in relation to any conduct occurring before the day on which this section comes into force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488]

(7) In the case of an offence committed before *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488] 2 May 2022[^key-917b00695a053e4cbbe6dda6c4be8211] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488], the amendment made by subsection (4) has effect as if for “12 months” there were substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488] “6 months” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2072488].
