# Housing Act 2004, section 215C

## 215C. Sections 215A and 215B: transitional provisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6b584d157e9b20e62cf3051912d526ae][^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541][^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Sections 215A and 215B are treated as having had effect since 6 April 2007, subject to the following provisions of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]

(2) Sections 215A and 215B do not have effect in relation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]

- (a) a claim under section 214 of this Act or section 21 of the Housing Act 1988 in respect of a tenancy which is settled before the commencement date (whether or not proceedings in relation to the claim have been instituted), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]
- (b) proceedings under either of those sections in respect of a tenancy which have been finally determined before the commencement date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]

(3) Subsection (5) applies in respect of a tenancy if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]

- (a) proceedings under section 214 in respect of the tenancy have been instituted before the commencement date but have not been settled or finally determined before that date, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]
- (b) because of section 215A(4) or 215B(2), the court decides— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]
  - (i) not to make an order under section 214(4) in respect of the tenancy, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541] to allow an appeal by the landlord against such an order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]

(4) Subsection (5) also applies in respect of a tenancy if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]

- (a) proceedings for possession under section 21 of the Housing Act 1988 in respect of the tenancy have been instituted before the commencement date but have not been settled or finally determined before that date, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]
- (b) because of section 215A(4) or 215B(2), the court decides— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]
  - (i) to make an order for possession under that section in respect of the tenancy, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541] to allow an appeal by the landlord against a refusal to make such an order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]

(5) Where this subsection applies, the court must not order the tenant or any relevant person (as defined by section 213(10)) to pay the landlord's costs, to the extent that the court reasonably considers those costs are attributable to the proceedings under section 214 of this Act or (as the case may be) section 21 of the Housing Act 1988. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]

(6) Proceedings have been “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]**finally determined *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]**” for the purposes of this section if — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]

- (a) they have been determined by a court, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]
- (b) there is no further right to appeal against the determination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]

(7) There is no further right to appeal against a court determination if there is no right to appeal against the determination, or there is such a right but— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]

- (a) the time limit for making an appeal has expired without an appeal being brought, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]
- (b) an appeal brought within that time limit has been withdrawn. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]

(8) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]**the commencement date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]**” means the date on which the Deregulation Act 2015 is passed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ddcc708c21668a8ec3d1ffc7944f4541]
