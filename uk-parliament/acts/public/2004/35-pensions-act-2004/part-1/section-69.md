# Pensions Act 2004, section 69

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (95).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/35
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 69. Duty to notify the Regulator of certain events

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-206c6d4f2330a3418b0928c48a607ce2] Except where the Regulator otherwise directs, the appropriate person must give notice of any notifiable event to the Regulator.

(2)[^c-8ecdd4b3b03f43a60007de30bac638e3] In subsection (1) “**notifiable event**” means—

- (a) a prescribed event in respect of an eligible scheme, or
- (b) a prescribed event in respect of the employer in relation to an eligible scheme.

(3) For the purposes of subsection (1)—

- (a) in the case of an event within subsection (2)(a), each of the following is “**the appropriate person**”—
  - (i)[^c-206c6d4f2330a3418b0928c48a607ce2] the trustees or managers of the scheme,
  - (ii)[^c-8ecdd4b3b03f43a60007de30bac638e3] a person of a prescribed description, and
- (b) in relation to an event within subsection (2)(b), each of the following is “**the appropriate person**”—
  - (i)[^c-206c6d4f2330a3418b0928c48a607ce2] the employer in relation to the scheme,
  - (ii)[^c-8ecdd4b3b03f43a60007de30bac638e3] a person of a prescribed description.

(4)[^c-206c6d4f2330a3418b0928c48a607ce2] A notice under subsection (1)—

- (a) must be in writing, and
- (b) subject to subsection (5), must be given as soon as reasonably practicable after the person giving it becomes aware of the notifiable event.

(5)[^c-8ecdd4b3b03f43a60007de30bac638e3] Regulations may require a notice under subsection (1) to be given before the beginning of the prescribed period ending with the notifiable event in question.

(6)[^c-206c6d4f2330a3418b0928c48a607ce2] No duty to which a person is subject is to be regarded as contravened merely because of any information or opinion contained in a notice under this section.

This is subject to section 311 (protected items).

(7)[^c-206c6d4f2330a3418b0928c48a607ce2] Where the trustees or managers of a scheme fail to comply with an obligation imposed on them by subsection (1), section 88A (financial penalties)[^c-5086121ccb2237925d5d0e2d46fc7ca9] applies in relation to any trustee or manager who has failed to take all reasonable steps to secure compliance with that subsection.

(8)[^c-206c6d4f2330a3418b0928c48a607ce2] Section 88A[^c-2d4165bc83349c1e761c84025f9f82f4] also applies to any other person who, without reasonable excuse, fails to comply with an obligation imposed on him by subsection (1).

(9)[^c-206c6d4f2330a3418b0928c48a607ce2] In this section—

- “**eligible scheme**” has the meaning given by section 126;
- “**event**” includes a failure to act.
