# Pensions Act 2004, section 93

## 93. The Regulator’s procedure in relation to its regulatory functions

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] The Regulator must determine the procedure that it proposes to follow in relation to the exercise of its regulatory functions.

(2) For the purposes of this Part the “**regulatory functions**” of the Regulator are—

- (a)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] the power to issue an improvement notice under section 13,
- (b)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] the power to issue a third party notice under section 14,
- (ba)[^c-99c722ccaa4bdd45b775172c1a2142d6] the power to appoint a skilled person in relation to a public service pension scheme under section 14A,
- (c)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] the reserved regulatory functions (see Schedule 2),
- (d)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] the power to issue a clearance statement under section 42,
- (e)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] the power to issue a notice under section 45(1) approving the details of arrangements,
- (f)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] the power to issue a clearance statement under section 46,
- (g)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] the power to vary or revoke under section 101 (to the extent that it does not fall within paragraph (c)),
- (h)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] the power to make an order under section 154(8),
- (i)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] the power to make an order under section 219(4),
- (j)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] the power to grant or revoke authorisation under section 288,
- (k)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] the power to grant or revoke approval under section 289,
- (l)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] the power to issue a notice under section 293(5),
- (m)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] the power by direction under section 2(3)(a) of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999 (c. 30) to refuse to register a scheme under section 2 of that Act,
- (n)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] the power to make an order under section 7 of the Pensions Act 1995 (c. 26) appointing a trustee (to the extent that it does not fall within paragraph (c)),
- (o)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] the power to make an order under section 23 of that Act appointing an independent trustee,
- (p)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] the power to give directions under section 72B of that Act (directions facilitating winding up), [^c-efce2a8666118352a1f0b86779d064d7]...
- (pa)[^c-bf08975c7fe35b8d2d94e83a76a0c149] the power to give a direction under section 26(7) of the Pension Schemes Act 2017 (direction to submit implementation strategy for Master Trust scheme[^c-66487650e0a871ee62ab0a0a914933af]), [^c-3e8ff4db29b3f44b4b9c183a885b48da]...
- (pb)[^c-a7beba4309e5a25518edfce9f6524ee8] the power to give a direction under section 23(2) of the Pension Schemes Act 2021 (direction to obtain actuarial valuation or take other steps to remedy or mitigate failure in relation to a collective money purchase scheme),
- (pc) the power to give a notice under section 29(1) , (1A)[^c-7be7b180a8e80bea1cc035326c0d8a05] or (4) of that Act (risk notices),
- (pd) the power to give a direction under section 29(6) of that Act (direction to implement proposals in a resolution plan),
- (pe) the power to give a direction under section 39(4) of that Act (direction to submit implementation strategy), and
- (q)[^c-02fac07de2efa7c883fcd3c571861032] such other functions of the Regulator as may be prescribed.

(3)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] The Determinations Panel must determine the procedure to be followed by it in relation to any exercise by it on behalf of the Regulator of—

- (a) the power to determine whether to exercise a regulatory function, and
- (b) where the Panel so determines to exercise a regulatory function, the power to exercise the function in question.

(4)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] The procedure determined under this section—

- (a) must provide for the procedure required under—
  - (i) section 96 (standard procedure), and
  - (ii) section 98 (special procedure), and
- (b) may include such other procedural requirements as the Regulator or, as the case may be, the Panel considers appropriate.

(5)[^c-3e2278a7720fe64bd32fb5fe07ec22bf] This section is subject to—

- (a) sections 99 to 104 (the remaining provisions concerning the procedure in relation to the regulatory functions), and
- (b) any regulations made by the Secretary of State under paragraph 19 of Schedule 1.
