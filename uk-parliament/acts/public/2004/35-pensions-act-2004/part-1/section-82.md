# Pensions Act 2004, section 82

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (95).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/35
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 82. Restricted information[^c-3d3da1bdb075668ba41698a4ea250203]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Restricted information must not be disclosed—

- (a) by the Regulator, or
- (b) by any person who receives the information directly or indirectly from the Regulator.

(2)[^c-10539156a1d5dc7529720e6a99c8949f] Subsection (1) is subject to—

- (a) subsection (3), and
- (b) sections 71(9), 83 to 88 and 235.

(3) Subject to section 88(5)[^c-dd9cfca1a101c2793ded7aca63d4c502], restricted information may be disclosed with the consent of the person to whom it relates and (if different) the person from whom the Regulator obtained it.

(4) For the purposes of this section and sections 83 to 87, “**restricted information**” means any information obtained by the Regulator in the exercise of its functions which relates to the business or other affairs of any person, except for information—

- (a) which at the time of the disclosure is or has already been made available to the public from other sources, or
- (b) which is in the form of a summary or collection of information so framed as not to enable information relating to any particular person to be ascertained from it.

(5) Any person who discloses information in contravention of this section is guilty of an offence and liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum , or imprisonment for a term not exceeding[^c-230d9accc164fdde9590e560a7f190fc] the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-d07dae09dd2636868e709233f26b47f8], or both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine or imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or both.

(6)[^c-9d87ee1de75e65c5ae4f206d020c1fdc] In relation to an offence under subsection (5) committed before 2 May 2022[^c-883011108f26ce81e72824cf58b2367c] the reference in subsection (5)(a) to the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-291d07710507f24846415d2977383dda] has effect as if it were a reference to six months.

(7) Subsection (6) does not extend to Scotland.
