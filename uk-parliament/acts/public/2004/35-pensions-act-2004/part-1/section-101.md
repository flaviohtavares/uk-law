# Pensions Act 2004, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (95).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/35

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 101. Powers to vary or revoke orders, notices or directions etc

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-58b1c1c005617afebc9dace716590baa] The Regulator may vary or revoke—

- (a) any determination by the Regulator whether to exercise a regulatory function, or
- (b) any order, notice or direction made, issued or given by the Regulator in the exercise of a regulatory function.

(2) Subsection (1)(b) does not apply to—

- (a)[^c-58b1c1c005617afebc9dace716590baa] an order under section 3(3) of the Pensions Act 1995 (c. 26) revoking a prohibition order under that section,
- (b)[^c-58b1c1c005617afebc9dace716590baa] an order under section 4(5) of that Act revoking a suspension order under that section,
- (c)[^c-58b1c1c005617afebc9dace716590baa] a direction under section 2(3) of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999 (c. 30) refusing to register a scheme under section 2 of that Act or removing a scheme from the register of stakeholder pension schemes, or
- (d) such other orders, notices or directions made, issued or given by the Regulator, in the exercise of a regulatory function, as may be prescribed.

(3)[^c-58b1c1c005617afebc9dace716590baa] A variation or revocation of an order, a notice or a direction must be made by an order, a notice or a direction (as the case may be).

(4)[^c-58b1c1c005617afebc9dace716590baa] A variation or revocation made under this section must take effect from a specified time which must not be a time earlier than the time when the variation or revocation is made.

(5)[^c-58b1c1c005617afebc9dace716590baa] The power to vary or revoke under this section—

- (a) is not to be treated for the purposes of subsection (1) as a regulatory function, and
- (b) is in addition to any such power which is conferred on the Regulator by, or by virtue of, this or any other enactment.
