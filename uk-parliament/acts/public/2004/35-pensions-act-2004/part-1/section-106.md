# Pensions Act 2004, section 106

## 106. Legal assistance scheme

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1)[^c-8cf3654bc3205fb31cee9b77b86275dd] The Lord Chancellor may by regulations establish a scheme governing the provision of legal assistance in connection with proceedings before a tribunal in relation to a decision of the Regulator[^c-3123f54cef7ea147209b3b22b786cf7e].

(2)[^c-8cf3654bc3205fb31cee9b77b86275dd] The legal assistance scheme may, in particular, make provision as to—

- (a) the kinds of legal assistance that may be provided;
- (b) the persons by whom legal assistance may be provided;
- (c) the manner in which applications for legal assistance are to be made;
- (d) the criteria on which eligibility for legal assistance is to be determined;
- (e) the persons or bodies by whom applications are to be determined;
- (f) appeals against refusals of applications;
- (g) the revocation or variation of decisions;
- (h) its administration and the enforcement of its provisions.

(3)[^c-8cf3654bc3205fb31cee9b77b86275dd] Legal assistance under the scheme may be provided subject to conditions or restrictions.

(4)[^c-8cf3654bc3205fb31cee9b77b86275dd] Those conditions may include conditions as to the making of contributions by the person to whom the assistance is provided.

(5)[^c-52d930c24afabf55aee4b7a218870510] The Lord Chancellor must fund, out of money provided by Parliament, the costs of the scheme including the costs of legal assistance provided under it.

(6)[^c-52d930c24afabf55aee4b7a218870510] In this Part “**the legal assistance scheme**” means any scheme in force by virtue of subsection (1).
