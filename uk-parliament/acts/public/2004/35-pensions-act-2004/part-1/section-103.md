## 103. References in relation to decisions of Regulator[^key-3230980680bb0e24142a9ea256a476b6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-73f480a5488b058fda376c823cdfca08] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-73f480a5488b058fda376c823cdfca08] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A)[^key-6d75981b4b427b5a5721a1dac744dfb1] This section applies to references to a tribunal in relation to a decision of the Regulator.

(3)[^key-cc6845594ea95d95638d89d066d597e3] On a reference, the tribunal concerned[^key-a8639ee296cf3c504295824996e0e9bb] may consider any evidence relating to the subject-matter of the reference, whether or not it was available to the Regulator at the material time.

(4)[^key-cc6845594ea95d95638d89d066d597e3] On a reference, the tribunal concerned[^key-8c28c37f83d6cce4a703c6ac7501a3b2] must determine what (if any) is the appropriate action for the Regulator to take in relation to the matter referred to it[^key-31f1ceb1b35008ffba1ee0a0b24386de] .

(5)[^key-cc6845594ea95d95638d89d066d597e3] On determining a reference, the tribunal concerned[^key-47ffe747c4dfb443c52c74231d1d164c] must remit the matter to the Regulator with such directions (if any) as it[^key-831519e05fcac1c4068c1fc702d18979] considers appropriate for giving effect to its determination.

(6)[^key-cc6845594ea95d95638d89d066d597e3] Those directions may include directions to the Regulator—

- (a) confirming the Regulator’s determination and any order, notice or direction made, issued or given as a result of it;
- (b) to vary or revoke the Regulator’s determination, and any order, notice or direction made, issued or given as a result of it;
- (c) to substitute a different determination, order, notice or direction;
- (d) to make such savings and transitional provision as the tribunal concerned[^key-21adc07b5937ede197d76fa0b831a6a1] considers appropriate.

(7)[^key-cc6845594ea95d95638d89d066d597e3] The Regulator must act in accordance with the determination of, and any direction given by, the tribunal concerned[^key-800e423093ad55cc51afa45119865c48] (and accordingly sections 96 to 99 (standard and special procedure) do not apply).

(8)[^key-cc6845594ea95d95638d89d066d597e3] The tribunal concerned may, on determining a reference, make recommendations as to the procedure followed by the Regulator or the Determinations Panel.

(9)[^key-cc6845594ea95d95638d89d066d597e3] An order of the tribunal concerned may be enforced—

- (a) as if it were an order of the county court[^key-39b166ec075699d9385a66e139813512], or
- (b) in Scotland, as if it were an order of the Court of Session.
