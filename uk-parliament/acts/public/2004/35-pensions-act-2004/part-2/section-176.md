## 176. Supplementary provisions about pension protection levies[^c-0de9f4069a31a7da6ad3bfba64a1266e][^c-8ae4f61b00045ebee33fee3b22100c82][^c-a7ed8489e179b987a8f0f27af89fb104][^c-982394e9feaf5bce0e802f96ff79d746][^c-8ae4f61b00045ebee33fee3b22100c82][^c-a7ed8489e179b987a8f0f27af89fb104]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Board must consult such persons as it considers appropriate in the prescribed manner before making a determination under section 175(5) in respect of a financial year if—

- (a)[^c-72b9e5e7c6638eb20028ee9ffcba1740] no pension protection levies were imposed in the previous financial year, or
- (b) any of the proposed levy factors or levy rates is different, or applies to a different description of scheme, from the levy factors and levy rates in respect of any pension protection levies[^c-92af8f7ec1e3b4c11411b1890107e49a] imposed in the previous financial year, [^c-3be044c554ade3ed00b0e526df5d27b3]...
- (c)[^c-3be044c554ade3ed00b0e526df5d27b3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-60f8c1e519465477569b92a90b40be0e] The Board must publish in the prescribed manner details of—

- (a) any decision to impose, or not to impose, the levies for a financial year in respect of a description of scheme;
- (b) any determination under section 175(5).
