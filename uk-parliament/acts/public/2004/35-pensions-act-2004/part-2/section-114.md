# Pensions Act 2004, section 114

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (95).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/35
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114. Investment principles[^c-8ae4f61b00045ebee33fee3b22100c82][^c-a7ed8489e179b987a8f0f27af89fb104]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Board must secure—

- (a)[^c-acc5354d064e789ff267795a46a86abb] that a statement of investment principles is prepared and maintained, and
- (b)[^c-7497f53be928e968f686eb4365dfa81c] that the statement is reviewed at such intervals, and on such occasions, as may be prescribed and, if necessary, revised.

(2)[^c-acc5354d064e789ff267795a46a86abb] In this section “**statement of investment principles**” means a written statement of the investment principles governing determinations about investments made by or on behalf of the Board.

(3)[^c-7497f53be928e968f686eb4365dfa81c] Before preparing or revising a statement of investment principles, the Board must comply with any prescribed requirements.

(4)[^c-7497f53be928e968f686eb4365dfa81c] A statement of investment principles must be in the prescribed form and cover, amongst other things, the prescribed matters.
