# Pensions Act 2004, section 107

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 107. The Board of the Pension Protection Fund[^key-ba5ac5eef30e436d006e511890e407a9][^key-a7ed8489e179b987a8f0f27af89fb104][^key-8ae4f61b00045ebee33fee3b22100c82][^key-9f7630100fb0bee3a3dbda1545f54369]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be a body corporate called the Board of the Pension Protection Fund (in this Act referred to as “**the Board**”).
