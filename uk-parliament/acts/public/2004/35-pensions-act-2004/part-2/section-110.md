# Pensions Act 2004, section 110

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (95).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/35
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Board’s functions[^key-a7ed8489e179b987a8f0f27af89fb104][^key-8ae4f61b00045ebee33fee3b22100c82][^key-9f7630100fb0bee3a3dbda1545f54369]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Board must hold, manage and apply, in accordance with this Part and any provision in force in Northern Ireland corresponding to it—

- (a)[^key-9fe98efea81042ff9c79fbeeaef397e7] a fund to be known as the Pension Protection Fund, and
- (b)[^key-e722d6d28f0de796e335ecf3f5e8752f] a fund to be known as the Fraud Compensation Fund.

(2)[^key-437e9371d94271e4d8fade3f53a225fc] Sections 175 and 189 make provision for contributions to those funds to be levied by the Board.

(3)[^key-5f8f677f1444de159cd1f866b35efb2d] The Board also has such other functions as are conferred on it by, or by virtue of, this or any other enactment.
