# Pensions Act 2004, section 129

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (95).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/35
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 129. Applications and notifications for the purposes of section 128[^key-a7ed8489e179b987a8f0f27af89fb104][^key-8ae4f61b00045ebee33fee3b22100c82][^key-a964ec6cdf5c65b0aaba22d6e2b2d2de][^key-a7ed8489e179b987a8f0f27af89fb104][^key-8ae4f61b00045ebee33fee3b22100c82]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where the trustees or managers of an eligible scheme become aware that—

- (a)[^key-182d2ad66255749a152ad7cde61651cd] the employer in relation to the scheme is unlikely to continue as a going concern, and
- (b)[^key-1105a42dd6e83e3caa5b2a3e1e178571] the prescribed requirements are met in relation to the employer,

they must make an application to the Board for it to assume responsibility for the scheme under section 128.

(2)[^key-182d2ad66255749a152ad7cde61651cd] Where the Board receives an application under subsection (1), it must give a copy of the application to—

- (a) the Regulator, and
- (b) the employer.

(3)[^key-1105a42dd6e83e3caa5b2a3e1e178571] An application under subsection (1) must—

- (a) be in the prescribed form and contain the prescribed information, and
- (b) be made within the prescribed period.

(4)[^key-182d2ad66255749a152ad7cde61651cd] Where the Regulator becomes aware that—

- (a) the employer in relation to an eligible scheme is unlikely to continue as a going concern, and
- (b) the requirements mentioned in subsection (1)(b) are met in relation to the employer,

it must give the Board a notice to that effect.

(5)[^key-182d2ad66255749a152ad7cde61651cd] Where the Board receives a notice under subsection (4), it must—

- (a) give the trustees or managers of the scheme a notice to that effect, and
- (b) give the employer a copy of that notice.

(6)[^key-182d2ad66255749a152ad7cde61651cd] The duty imposed by subsection (1) does not apply where the trustees or managers of an eligible scheme become aware as mentioned in that subsection by reason of a notice given to them under subsection (5).

(7)[^key-182d2ad66255749a152ad7cde61651cd] The duty imposed by subsection (4) does not apply where the Regulator becomes aware as mentioned in that subsection by reason of a copy of an application made by the trustees or managers of the eligible scheme in question given to the Regulator under subsection (2).

(8)[^key-1105a42dd6e83e3caa5b2a3e1e178571] Regulations may require notices under this section to be in the prescribed form and contain the prescribed information.
