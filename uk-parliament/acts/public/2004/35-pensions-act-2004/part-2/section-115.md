## 115. Borrowing[^key-8ae4f61b00045ebee33fee3b22100c82][^key-a7ed8489e179b987a8f0f27af89fb104]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-3d8976f4be6595cc28aa5f86373570d6] The Board may—

- (a) borrow from a deposit-taker such sums as it may from time to time require for exercising any of its functions;
- (b) give security for any money borrowed by it.

(2)[^key-3d8976f4be6595cc28aa5f86373570d6] The Board may not borrow under this section[^key-58600a1a39b265e360657bed244aeccb] if the effect would be—

- (a) to take the aggregate amount outstanding in respect of the principal of sums borrowed by it under this section[^key-64c8738fd068be0bc2deae8cc925a083] over its borrowing limit, or
- (b) to increase the amount by which the aggregate amount so outstanding exceeds that limit.

(3)[^key-0ce39c84aff44efc3258b528fcc84e71][^key-95bee3036df9ff7f469ceb7d7ae6aadb] In this section—

- “**borrowing limit**” means such limit as the Secretary of State may specify by order;
- “**deposit-taker**” means—
  - (a) a person who has permission under Part 4A[^key-184b64f840015f1bcfe8b895f35aece1] of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (c. 8) to accept deposits, [^key-ffdd1dbec39fc629562f18bf402d9767]...
  - (b) [^key-ffdd1dbec39fc629562f18bf402d9767]...

(4)[^key-3d8976f4be6595cc28aa5f86373570d6] The definition of “deposit-taker” in subsection (3) must be read with—

- (a) section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000,
- (b) any relevant order under that section, and
- (c) Schedule 2 to that Act.
