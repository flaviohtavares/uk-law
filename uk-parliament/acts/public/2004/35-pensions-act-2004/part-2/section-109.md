## 109. Further provision about the Board[^c-a39ee683a69c323792a84c0d1eda56eb][^c-54db3f4e321fb808cee5fd833134c5d1][^c-a7ed8489e179b987a8f0f27af89fb104][^c-8ae4f61b00045ebee33fee3b22100c82][^c-44c40810bf1eb53658196881ef64a6c3][^c-85793614784c6a2a9d6d4c85fa14bdd6][^c-9f7630100fb0bee3a3dbda1545f54369][^c-12f04822c56b826e547d3d8db8f91345][^c-012cae74a7463118eed592883ed617cb][^c-a7ed8489e179b987a8f0f27af89fb104][^c-8ae4f61b00045ebee33fee3b22100c82][^c-9f7630100fb0bee3a3dbda1545f54369]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 5 makes further provision about the Board, including provision as to—

- the appointment of members,
- the terms of appointment, tenure and remuneration of members,
- the appointment of the Chief Executive and other staff,
- the proceedings of the Board,
- its accounts, and
- the status and liability of the Board, its members and staff.
