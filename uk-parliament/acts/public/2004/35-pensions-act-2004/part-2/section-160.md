## 160. Transfer notice[^c-a7ed8489e179b987a8f0f27af89fb104][^c-8ae4f61b00045ebee33fee3b22100c82][^c-691873c2bfaba20b49f8cb25c7e3363e][^c-a7ed8489e179b987a8f0f27af89fb104][^c-8ae4f61b00045ebee33fee3b22100c82][^c-9f7630100fb0bee3a3dbda1545f54369]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-d043cdd9322451d533f5541c4243644d] This section applies where the Board is required to assume responsibility for a scheme under section 127, 128, 152 or 158.

(2)[^c-d043cdd9322451d533f5541c4243644d] The Board must give the trustees or managers a notice (a “transfer notice”).

(3)[^c-d043cdd9322451d533f5541c4243644d] In a case to which section 127 or 128 applies, a transfer notice may not be given until the determination made or valuation obtained under section 143(2)[^c-94b6d688eb2e9eae8c6da2ef911c3fe2] is binding.

(4)[^c-022114bb4159850f1357e6ffe0fd3b40] In a case to which section 158 applies, a transfer notice may not be given until the determination made or[^c-7e9108ee080cbc9c4b8d4627863afee4] valuation obtained under subsection (3) of that section is binding.

(5)[^c-d043cdd9322451d533f5541c4243644d] A transfer notice may not be given in relation to a scheme during any period when the issue of, or failure to issue, a withdrawal notice under or by virtue of section 146 or 147 (refusal to assume responsibility) is reviewable (see section 149(6)(b)).

(6)[^c-d043cdd9322451d533f5541c4243644d] The Board must give a copy of any notice given under subsection (2) to—

- (a) the Regulator, and
- (b) any insolvency practitioner in relation to the employer or, if there is no such insolvency practitioner, the employer.

(7)[^c-cf7d8c15427984cdb6a1916c6117a058][^c-9760e48011eea0d7d4437e9c2065857e] This section is subject to section 172(2)[^c-066af4ed7fa6cf6fbe96c48743e79419] (no transfer notice [^c-995affa2a33769b25b2cb7721113947e]... when fraud compensation application is pending).
