## 120. Duty to notify insolvency events in respect of employers[^c-a7ed8489e179b987a8f0f27af89fb104][^c-8ae4f61b00045ebee33fee3b22100c82][^c-e25d1842d6b512faa1f51b9bf55e6c53][^c-e25d1842d6b512faa1f51b9bf55e6c53][^c-8ae4f61b00045ebee33fee3b22100c82][^c-a7ed8489e179b987a8f0f27af89fb104]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-a4d410c406f8aa4331b24cb0adde2469] This section applies where, in the case of an occupational pension scheme, an insolvency event occurs in relation to the employer.

(2)[^c-a4d410c406f8aa4331b24cb0adde2469] The insolvency practitioner in relation to the employer must give a notice to that effect within the notification period to—

- (a) the Board,
- (b) the Regulator, and
- (c) the trustees or managers of the scheme.

(3)[^c-cad7ba9a927265fb30387e1dcd9a3318] For the purposes of subsection (2) the “notification period” is the prescribed period beginning with the later of—

- (a) the insolvency date, and
- (b) the date the insolvency practitioner becomes aware of the existence of the scheme.

(4)[^c-cad7ba9a927265fb30387e1dcd9a3318] A notice under this section must be in such form and contain such information as may be prescribed.
