## 254. Representative of non-UK scheme to be treated as trustee[^key-0335152aedcdbe98f4c3b485129936fe][^key-99c28b701f6d213c0a7c1e380a3a2c2a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In the case of an occupational pension scheme that has its main administration outside the[^key-198bcd1a723983d3fd044f2e43cc3558] United Kingdom[^key-543f2ebd02d396775c32e6df082d3132], a reference in pensions legislation to the trustees, or a trustee, of the scheme includes a person who is for the time being appointed by the trustees of the scheme to be a representative of the scheme for the purposes of this section.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to a prescribed reference.

(3) In subsection (1) “**pensions legislation**” means any enactment contained in or made by virtue of—

- (a) the Pension Schemes Act 1993 (c. 48),
- (b) the Pensions Act 1995,
- (c) Parts 1 to 4 of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999 (c. 30), [^key-f32e7d1153620741ed02e55987b08531]...
- (d) this Act,[^key-de75196c5df63f4790d5528b60e90cc2] [^key-04b664c6dd836598a044b53719c90f34]...
- (e) the Pension Schemes Act 2015, or[^key-d92e41a824139e4cd32835084583ac21]
- (f) Part 1 of the Pension Schemes Act 2021.
