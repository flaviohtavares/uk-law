## 251. Payment of surplus to employer: transitional power to amend scheme[^key-ff0ec0d25f74c7323509f799baa7afc2][^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This section applies to a scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]

- (a)[^key-d396778e6109b209016a46fc0714cf6b] which is one to which section 37 of the Pensions Act 1995 applies, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]
- (b) which immediately before the commencement of section 250 was one to which section 37 of the Pensions Act 1995 (c. 26) applied (see subsection (1) of that section, as it then had effect). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]

(2) No payment to the employer may be made out of funds held for the purposes of the scheme except by virtue of a resolution of the trustees under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]

This applies even if the payment is one proposed to be made in fulfilment of an agreement or arrangement entered into before the commencement of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]

(2A)[^key-f6738bd0fb1acf6f0403f3eb15009953] But subsection (2) does not apply in the case of any of the payments listed in paragraphs (c) to (f) of section 175 of the Finance Act 2004 (authorised employer payments other than public service scheme payments or authorised surplus payments). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]

(3) Where the scheme was so expressed as (apart from section 37, as it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5] applied immediately before the commencement of section 250[^key-4bea7a4d4777d8d698f48ac6e0fb9476] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]) to confer power to make payments to the employer out of funds held for the purposes of the scheme otherwise than in pursuance of proposals approved under paragraph 6(1) of Schedule 22 to the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 (c. 1), the trustees may resolve that the power— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]

- (a) shall become exercisable according to its terms, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]
- (b) shall become so exercisable, but only in such circumstances and subject to such conditions as may be specified in the resolution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]

(4) Where the scheme was so expressed as to confer power to make payments to the employer out of funds held for the purposes of the scheme only in pursuance of proposals approved under paragraph 6(1) of Schedule 22 to the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988, the trustees may resolve that the power shall instead be exercisable in such circumstances and subject to such conditions as may be specified in the resolution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]

(5) In either case the trustees must be satisfied that it is in the interests of the members of the scheme that the power is exercised in the manner proposed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]

(6) The power conferred by subsection (3) or (4)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]

- (a) may not be exercised unless notice of the proposal to exercise it has been given, in accordance with prescribed requirements, to the employer and to the members of the scheme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6bf65eb21d730a4ebe2697826b3f3e3b][^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5] may be exercised even if the payments to which it relates are, to any extent, payments to which subsection (2) does not apply, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]
- (b) may *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5] be exercised, after the commencement of section 25 of the Pensions Act 2011, only once (whether or not also exercised before 6 April 2011)[^key-a75aa769ae4a855a82aed46b808fe36d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]
- (c) ceases to be exercisable *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5] on 6 April 2016[^key-4edf48c2fa03e0e6e8e1d707e4478138] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5].

(6A)[^key-c85a6dc949c4f2171868f5733db48f5d] A resolution passed under this section after the commencement of section 25 of the Pensions Act 2011 may amend or revoke a resolution passed under this section before 6 April 2011. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]

(7) The exercise of any power to make payments to the employer by virtue of a resolution under this section is subject to section 37 of the Pensions Act 1995 (c. 26) as substituted by section 250. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ea2f5ee59d494b247a83ea550000f0e5]
