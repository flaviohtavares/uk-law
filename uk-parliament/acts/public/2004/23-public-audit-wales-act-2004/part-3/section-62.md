# Public Audit (Wales) Act 2004, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 62. Co-operation with Welsh Ministers, ... or Care Quality Commission[^c20974491][^c-baea42b88c7b2325b67217ef689dfcd9][^c-8111bfc3b2dcaa97a93986c813388eeb]

*Extent: E+W*

The Auditor General for Wales must co-operate with—

- (a) the Welsh Ministers[^c-f56f248b61c1323a04cbb36d70cdb25e],
- (b)[^c-f9ff52dc8feacb4efee4b9fdf7cf7eab] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c20974931] the Care Quality Commission,

where it seems to him appropriate to do so for the efficient and effective discharge of his functions in relation to Welsh NHS bodies.
