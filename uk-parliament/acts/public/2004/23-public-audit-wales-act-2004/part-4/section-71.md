# Public Audit (Wales) Act 2004, section 71

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 71. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W*

In this Act—

- “**the Assembly**” means the National Assembly for Wales;
- “**the Audit Commission**” means the Audit Commission for Local Authorities and the National Health Service in England [^c20975411]. . . ;
- “**body**” includes office;
- “**financial year**” means the 12 months ending with 31 March.
