# Public Audit (Wales) Act 2004, section 67A

## 67A. Assistance by Auditor General to inspectorates[^c20975121]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Auditor General for Wales may provide assistance to—

- (a) Her Majesty's Chief Inspector of Prisons,
- (b) Her Majesty's Inspectors of Constabulary,
- (c) Her Majesty's Chief Inspector of the Crown Prosecution Service,
- (d) Her Majesty's Inspectorate of Probation for England and Wales[^c20975241],
- (e)[^key-32c183cf3e7759de532b0a6615675d5e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

in the discharge of any of their functions.

(2) Assistance under subsection (1) may be provided on such terms, including terms as to payment, as the Wales Audit Office[^key-138a29206a99d7eb3c833c73f2561be9] and the body or person in question may agree, but any terms as to payment agreed by the Wales Audit Office must be made in accordance with a scheme for charging fees prepared under section 24 of the Public Audit (Wales) Act 2013[^key-aa4efa4694602072d67c959a271eed51].

(3)[^key-85ff605b47c57bb5b4bd2bf087e0f889] Any sums charged in relation to assistance provided under this section may not exceed the full cost of providing that assistance.
