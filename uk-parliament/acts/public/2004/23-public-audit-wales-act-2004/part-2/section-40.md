# Public Audit (Wales) Act 2004, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Documents relating to police and crime commissioners and chief constables[^c-58a8e783b1bb18eb49005f77d5cddfe2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the Auditor General for Wales makes a report under section 22[^c-db09592f45ed6a1fea387716e9edaf2c] and the report relates to a police and crime commissioner for, or the chief constable of a police force maintained under section 2 of the Police Act 1996 for,[^c-e5b569315d301456fb2193575756e183] a police area in Wales, he must send a copy of the report to the Secretary of State and the Welsh Ministers[^c-f45956ea861e71409c781af9d434e3f2].

(2) If the Auditor General for Wales has sent a document (or a copy of a document) relating to one or more police and crime commissioners[^c-eae7b03283737d80ea73007f9c10b19d] for police areas in Wales to a police and crime commissioner[^c-fd8d204ba1f5e95155a14251225f60cd] for a police area in Wales, he may send a copy of the document to the Secretary of State and the Welsh Ministers.

(3)[^c-69675624798f123f7e8c6c0cdcaafd7e] If the Auditor General for Wales has sent a document (or a copy of a document) relating to one or more chief constables of police forces maintained under section 2 of the Police Act 1996 for a police area in Wales, the Auditor General may send a copy of the document to the persons to whom a copy of a document may be sent under subsection (2).
