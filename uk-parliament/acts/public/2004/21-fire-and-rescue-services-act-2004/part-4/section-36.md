# Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 36. Preservation of existing pension scheme[^c-5b0e613dbe2e39382dce7d8403b91b65][^c-a11e521ed7e8b05645fb7a9fe0f4ad3c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-17b1a61ea37b470fc882e898f428c44c] The Secretary of State may by order provide for the scheme established under section 26 of the Fire Services Act 1947 (c. 41) (Firemen’s Pension Scheme) to continue in force despite the repeal by this Act of that section.

(2)[^c-17b1a61ea37b470fc882e898f428c44c] An order under this section may provide for any enactment amended or repealed by this Act to continue in effect in relation to that scheme unmodified or with any modifications specified in the order.

(3)[^c-17b1a61ea37b470fc882e898f428c44c] An order under this section may in particular make new provision for the name of the scheme.

(4) This section does not affect any other power in this Act to make transitional or transitory provision.
