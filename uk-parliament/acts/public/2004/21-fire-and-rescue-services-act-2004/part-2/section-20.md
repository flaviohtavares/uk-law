# Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 20. Exercise of powers at or under sea[^key-88e704b7d847ffa3137e7481c11cbd2c][^key-7d3158131ab2a8dfae9974f233270428]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a fire and rescue authority has power to act, or is required to act, outside the authority’s area, the authority may exercise the power, or perform the duty, at sea or under the sea.

(2) The references in subsection (1) to “sea” are not restricted to the territorial sea of the United Kingdom.
