# Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/21

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 18. Training centres[^c-412852d9049e6eb89f89219293260c12][^c-646b840add1a389e4329f0cf9e2f492f]

*Extent: E+W*

A fire and rescue authority may establish and maintain one or more training centres for providing education and training in matters in relation to which fire and rescue authorities have functions.
