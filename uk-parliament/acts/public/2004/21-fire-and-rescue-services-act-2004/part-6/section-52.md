## 52. Repeal of Fire Services Act 1947[^key-a181ad65959a0564fb7bcc041042e43a][^key-182840e0b187fd039243fe0ca7801308]

*Extent: E+W*

The Fire Services Act 1947 (c. 41) shall cease to have effect.
