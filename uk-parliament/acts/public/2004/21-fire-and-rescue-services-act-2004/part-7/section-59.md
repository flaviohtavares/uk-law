# Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004, section 59

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 59. Financial provision

*Extent: E+W*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenditure incurred by the Secretary of State in consequence of this Act;
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable out of money provided by Parliament under another enactment.
