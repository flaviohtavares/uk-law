# Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004, section 21A

## 21A (Extent: W). Fire and rescue authorities in Wales: performance and governance (Extent: W)[^c-2b0d93359f4458eb9704e1eba0fd6e39]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers may by regulations— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) require a fire and rescue authority for an area in Wales to make a plan in relation to the exercise of the authority's functions; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) impose requirements relating to such a plan. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) The requirements which may be imposed under subsection (1)(b) include requirements about— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) a plan's content; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) its preparation and revision; *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) when it is to be made; *(Extent: W)*
- (d (Extent: W)) the period to which it is to relate; *(Extent: W)*
- (e (Extent: W)) its publication. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) Requirements about a plan's content include requirements to— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) set out an authority's priorities and objectives; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) describe and explain the extent to which the plan reflects the Framework prepared by the Welsh Ministers under section 21; *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) set out actions the authority intends to take in relation to its priorities and objectives; *(Extent: W)*
- (d (Extent: W)) set out how the authority intends to assess its performance. *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers may by regulations make provision (including imposing requirements on an authority) for the purposes of assessing or reporting on the performance of an authority. *(Extent: W)*

(5 (Extent: W)) Before making regulations under subsection (1) or (4) the Welsh Ministers— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) must consult fire and rescue authorities for areas in Wales or persons who the Welsh Ministers consider represent those authorities; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) must consult persons who the Welsh Ministers consider represent employees of fire and rescue authorities for areas in Wales; *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) may consult any other persons the Welsh Ministers consider appropriate. *(Extent: W)*
