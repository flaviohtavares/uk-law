# Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Information[^c-f8ac8c526f6b56b6a5e782b5c7d76956][^c-9f9d7731767a33d155991773300af1d2]

*Extent: E+W*

A fire and rescue authority must—

- (a) submit to the Secretary of State any reports and returns required by him;
- (b) give the Secretary of State any information with respect to its functions required by him.
