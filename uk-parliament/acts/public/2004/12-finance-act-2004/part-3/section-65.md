# Finance Act 2004, section 65

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-07-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Change in control of company registered for gross payment

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where it appears to the Board of Inland Revenue that there has been a change in the control of a company—

- (a) registered for gross payment, or
- (b) applying to be so registered,

the Board may make a direction under section 64(5).

(2) The Board may make regulations requiring the furnishing of information with respect to changes in the control of a company—

- (a) registered for gross payment, or
- (b) applying to be so registered.

(3)[^c-70e18276127cf27c6225dac973f047d7][^c-268a451f2559b447c61ccf476f9b1830] In this section references to a change in the control of a company are references to such a change determined in accordance with section 995 of the Income Tax Act 2007.
