# Finance Act 2004, section 73A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-07-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73A. Designated international organisations: exemption from section 59[^c-089940f637055691458d66b6b1956e2b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Treasury may by order designate for the purposes of this section any international organisation of which the United Kingdom is a member.

(2) Section 59 does not apply to an organisation which is so designated.
