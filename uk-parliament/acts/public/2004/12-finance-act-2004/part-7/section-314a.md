# Finance Act 2004, section 314A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-07-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 314A. Order to disclose[^c-b330ca44e7b8618d350717c2000cfec8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) HMRC may apply to the tribunal[^c-b818d6fe61887755ed337f2ec279031f] for an order that—

- (a) a proposal is notifiable, or
- (b) arrangements are notifiable.

(2) An application must specify—

- (a) the proposal or arrangements in respect of which the order is sought, and
- (b) the promoter.

(3) On an application the tribunal[^c-c47cbb4f664453bf0acd45c972e40234] may make the order only if satisfied that section 306(1)(a) to (c) applies to the relevant arrangements.
