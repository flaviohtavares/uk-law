# Finance Act 2004, section 312B

## 312B. Duty of client to provide information ...[^key-77b280b8907f2842ee9f7fdf993b5d13][^key-3510894bd5b7df883f30b9bbae4c0524]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-fdd724df47f97f607856f7b2d9cedb4b] This section applies where a person (“**the client**”) has been provided with information under section 312(2) or 312ZA(2) (prescribed information about reference number).

(2) The client must, within the prescribed period, provide the person who provided the information[^key-aa9813c278dc4354934d82a4ca0e2035] with prescribed information relating to the client.

(3) The duty under subsection (2) is subject to any exceptions that may be prescribed.
