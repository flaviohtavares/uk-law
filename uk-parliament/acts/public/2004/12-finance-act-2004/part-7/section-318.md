# Finance Act 2004, section 318

## 318. Interpretation of Part 7

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part—

- “**advantage**”, in relation to any tax, means—
  - (a) relief or increased relief from, or repayment or increased repayment of, that tax, or the avoidance or reduction of a charge to that tax or an assessment to that tax or the avoidance of a possible assessment to that tax,
  - (b) the deferral of any payment of tax or the advancement of any repayment of tax, or
  - (c) the avoidance of any obligation to deduct or account for any tax;
- “arrangements” includes any scheme, transaction or series of transactions;
- “[^key-9361103ea485128257beeaed141ccee1]**authorised officer**” means an officer of Revenue and Customs authorised by His Majesty’s Revenue and Customs for the purposes of this Part or, as the case may be, section 100 of TMA 1970;
- “[^key-c272c591b3a3ff7a634bf3a60ab13db3]**company**” has the meaning given by section 1121 of the Corporation Tax Act 2010;
- “**corporation tax**” includes any amount which, by virtue of any of the provisions mentioned in paragraph 1 of Schedule 18 to the Finance Act 1998 (c. 36) (company tax returns, assessments and related matters) is assessable and chargeable as if it were corporation tax;
- “[^key-a4eb4e08aea1d23508a8886b87fbd8cf]**HMRC**” means the Commissioners for Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs;
- “[^key-74b23bc1206a9b9e0f4d540c192845eb]**introducer**”, in relation to a notifiable proposal, has the meaning given by section 307;
- “**make a firm approach**” has the meaning given by section 307(4A);
- “**make a marketing contact**” has the meaning given by section 307(4B);
- “**notifiable arrangements**” has the meaning given by section 306(1);
- “**notifiable proposal**” has the meaning given by section 306(2);
- “**prescribed**”, except in section 306, means prescribed by regulations made by the Board;
- “**promoter**”, in relation to notifiable arrangements or a notifiable proposal, has the meaning given by section 307;
- “[^key-033c591e37b1a20f02ac2edb5f5d3975]**reference number**” means a reference number allocated under section 311;
- [^key-c65c269a83cb8ec5f57d07317a78d2ba]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**tax**” means—
  - (a) income tax,
  - (b) capital gains tax,
  - (c) corporation tax,
  - (d) petroleum revenue tax,
  - (da) apprenticeship levy,[^key-5afb799bf19aab6cd135dfc88f83b4dc]
  - (e) inheritance tax,
  - (f) stamp duty land tax, [^key-1ceb0e7df6ece19e14ce807f624a6c11]...
  - (g) stamp duty reserve tax, or[^key-13e71b82c92497e7d13a89d4583d2562]
  - (h) annual tax on enveloped dwellings.
- “**Taxes Acts**” has the same meaning as in TMA 1970 (see section 118(1) of that Act);
- “[^key-c7fc677651dbc5420f14238966c7f0fe]**trade**” includes every venture in the nature of trade.
- “[^key-8ca5268b52157467f1d073b830da95af]**tribunal**” means the First-tier tribunal, or where determined by or under Tribunal Procedure Rules, the Upper Tribunal.
- “[^key-d6a6311854845637ff13b6643219ba32]**working day**” means a day which is not a Saturday or a Sunday, Christmas Day, Good Friday or a bank holiday under the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 in any part of the United Kingdom.
