## 247. Abolition of income tax charge in respect of employer payments[^key-d117398d5c799734ac948e2e95d177e2][^key-82faf91a8e0d278a23fafc96e1ffb2ca][^key-82faf91a8e0d278a23fafc96e1ffb2ca]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Part 6 of ITEPA 2003, omit Chapter 1 (payments by employer for the provision of benefits for an employee under certain schemes to count as employment income of employee).
