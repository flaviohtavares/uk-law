# Finance Act 2004, section 256

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-07-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/12

## 256. Enhanced ... allowance regulations[^key-8de76a7a7d28c3be4d53965a0df8300d][^key-d0eb2d7c3111f7e61df648d7060c1d95]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to regulations made by the Board of Inland Revenue under—

- (a)[^key-ef0bd12955c73304378a02dd6db35f66] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^key-ef0bd12955c73304378a02dd6db35f66] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^key-ef0bd12955c73304378a02dd6db35f66] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) paragraph 7(1)(b) or 11A(1)(c)[^key-b93a47d6841bfd0df98e234f562fd88c] of Schedule 36 (enhancement of allowances[^key-e2b934e841af37d8ab90caa90d3ffdcb]: primary protection),
- (e) paragraph 12(1) or 15A(1)(b)[^key-e9472f169e64022fc37021e7ef3bb7a6] of that Schedule ([^key-c59d7c864d05ad01116ac627b958e60a]...: enhanced protection), [^key-2dc72ef0d2238be272f7db59f1dded35]...
- (f) paragraph 18(1)(b)[^key-bd5c10785f0748e809966385cb9d58c5] of that Schedule (enhancement of allowances[^key-5022a6606c8616c91400303483d6e718]: pre-commencement pension credits),
- (g)[^key-375a0ef651413d97b123f4d3f65f6375] paragraph 20A(1)(d) of that Schedule (enhancement factor: pension credits from previously crystallised rights),
- (h) paragraph 20B(1)(b) of that Schedule (enhancement factor: non-residence), and
- (i) paragraph 20E(1)(b) of that Schedule (enhancement factor: transfers from recognised overseas pension scheme).

(2) The regulations to which this section applies are referred to in this Part as “**enhanced [^key-e4e6f396dd7b993bf9b8440d4004f729]... allowance regulations**”.

(3) Enhanced [^key-3d9b34a1bad47e06c9c126caf6538302]... allowance regulations may include any provision that appears appropriate for securing that the correct tax is charged—

- (a)[^key-45d7501075e8036c9ea845efeb5eb7d7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) in respect of the payment of lump sums by registered pension schemes.

(4) Enhanced [^key-9816416bdc344a52dbb515f9d98ae634]... allowance regulations may, for that purpose, in particular contain provision—

- (a) requiring any person to produce or make available documents, produce certificates or provide information, and
- (b) for the review from time to time of any matter registered in accordance with the regulations.
