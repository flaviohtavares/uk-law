## 168. Lump sum death benefit rule[^c-d4ba7bb9bdda4e98c24c87b2f2d4cc9d][^c-d6860b22449c0b05d0e88d787e4a6ad4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^c-7055643fff30dd4e1a4ed50a34b655c1]

(1)[^c-776ad80474a4f0f8208f2b86bfc7a7d8] This is the rule relating to the payment of lump sum death benefits by a registered pension scheme in respect of a member of the pension scheme (“the lump sum death benefit rule”).

*Lump sum death benefit rule*

No lump sum death benefit may be paid other than—

- (a) a defined benefits lump sum death benefit,
- (b) a pension protection lump sum death benefit,
- (c) an uncrystallised funds lump sum death benefit,
- (d) an annuity protection lump sum death benefit,
- (e)[^c-7c9a021579fda11b579e66009ee7e3a7] a drawdown pension fund lump sum death benefit,
- (ea)[^c-9bed5b343aa9e523c921ccb9f6a0e836] a flexi-access drawdown fund lump sum death benefit,
- (f) a charity lump sum death benefit , or[^c-f91cc8ecb3e7b8f37e584d3e3ef55c18]
- (g)[^c-15b472539811ab6b4c570eccb45f8e72] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (h) a trivial commutation lump sum death benefit, [^c-36f334c4c46a1a2cbdcb415822c0bbf4]...
- (i)[^c-36f334c4c46a1a2cbdcb415822c0bbf4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) In this Part “**lump sum death benefit**” means a lump sum payable on the death of the member , or a lump sum payable in respect of the member on the subsequent death of a dependant, nominee or successor of the member.[^c-58e0c37fc8e6233912cd92e1b8a46549]

(3) Part 2 of Schedule 29 gives the meaning of expressions used in the lump sum death benefit rule.

(4) Schedule 36 contains (in Part 3) transitional provision about lump sum death benefits.
