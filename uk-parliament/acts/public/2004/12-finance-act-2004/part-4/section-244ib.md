## 244IB. Member’s overseas transfer allowance[^key-cb32c0a5c1f6891b6fc53b1224409c0e][^key-8442a960629e1e99ed567cd792d5924f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A member’s “overseas transfer allowance” is an amount equal to the member’s lump sum and death benefit allowance.
