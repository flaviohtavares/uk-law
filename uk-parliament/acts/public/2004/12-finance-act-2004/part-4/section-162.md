# Finance Act 2004, section 162

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 162. Meaning of “loan”[^c-9d39f90aeb15bbcdd351a7b0c0d32d1e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies for the interpretation of this Chapter.

(2) “**Loan**” does not include the purchase of or subscription to debentures, debenture stock, loan stock, bonds, certificates of deposit or other instruments creating or acknowledging indebtedness which are—

- (a) listed or dealt in on a recognised stock exchange (within the meaning of section 1005 of ITA 2007[^c-4aa5a94f2b3402be075558ad7617f581]), or
- (b) offered to the public.

(3) A guarantee of a loan made to or in respect of a person who is or has been a[^c-0111bdc65803c4150491b5e59c0ff207] member or sponsoring employer of a registered pension scheme , or to or in respect of a person who is connected with a[^c-08335f4bc34ad73a96097b31d3578a33] person who is or has been a member or sponsoring employer of a registered pension scheme but is not such a person[^c-b75db25d9867628d1571a7b6cbc86813], is to be treated as a loan to or in respect of the person who is or has been a member or sponsoring employer of an amount equal to the amount guaranteed.

(4) If a person who is or has been a[^c-21821d70a2175ead21c1a91fc6cc1f82] member or sponsoring employer of a registered pension scheme or a person who is connected with a[^c-799a74f16f86976fa006b45816883c4d] person who is or has been a member or sponsoring employer of a registered pension scheme but is not such a person[^c-6de9d3355a5921380a5e18fa15a73180]—

- (a) is liable to pay a debt, the right to payment of which constitutes an asset held for the purposes of the pension scheme, but
- (b) is not required to pay it by the relevant date,

the debt is to be treated as a loan made by the pension scheme to the person who is or has been a member or sponsoring employer on that date.

(5) The relevant date is the date by which a person at arm’s length from the pension scheme might be expected to be required to pay the debt.

(6)[^c-791db66bcfe172ad9aef6ba239d3b757] For the purposes of this section whether a person is connected with another person is determined in accordance with section 993 of ITA 2007.
