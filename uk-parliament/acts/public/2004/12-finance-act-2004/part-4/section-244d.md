# Finance Act 2004, section 244D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-07-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 244D. Exclusion: receiving scheme is an occupational pension scheme[^key-8442a960629e1e99ed567cd792d5924f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A transfer to a QROPS is excluded from the overseas transfer charge under section 244AC[^key-1f5fd97fd47aa591aaa7105b2aa60584] if—

- (a) the QROPS is an occupational pension scheme, and
- (b) when the transfer is made, the member is an employee of a sponsoring employer of the QROPS.
