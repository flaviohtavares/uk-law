# Finance Act 2004, section 262

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-07-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 262. Enhanced ... allowance regulations: failures to comply[^key-ce0d9f99cfb911e6ce55b612ffd105b3][^key-2c7fe91c5c2cd86a958e16d733161e40][^key-a3f1d29410c0db36c2d6057d5d8c1b1f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An individual who fails—

- (a) to produce or make available any document required to be produced by enhanced [^key-0d4b7f65b2bcfc5d6e210327d17e7edd]... allowance regulations,
- (b) to produce any certificate required to be produced by enhanced [^key-93117fb7b89585cabaa2cb40e50c1b99]... allowance regulations, or
- (c) to provide any information required to be provided by enhanced [^key-d3dd74ef3b2c105ef11d76464c1c37fb]... allowance regulations,

is liable to a penalty not exceeding £3,000.
