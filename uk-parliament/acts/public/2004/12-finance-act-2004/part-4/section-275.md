## 275. Insurance company[^c-8f2ce8d0355b81b485875416a8ec38a5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part “**insurance company**” means—

- (a) a person who has permission under Part 4 of FISMA 2000 to effect or carry out contracts of long-term insurance, [^c-ebe0ee5f92ced535eb4ef61a7e321deb]...
- (b)[^c-f722570fb2ad42ee18d0bae67bf68356] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) “**Contracts of long-term insurance**” means contracts which fall within Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001 (S.I. 2001/544).
