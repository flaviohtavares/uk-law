## 244. Non-UK schemes: application of certain charges[^key-8e17f77cb4361f6d806f5f01dd467106][^key-82faf91a8e0d278a23fafc96e1ffb2ca][^key-82faf91a8e0d278a23fafc96e1ffb2ca]

Schedule 34 contains provision applying certain charges under this Part , and under Part 9 of ITEPA 2003 (pension income),[^key-88188e630e08116d7c3922f5a17c4a2e] in relation to non-UK schemes.
