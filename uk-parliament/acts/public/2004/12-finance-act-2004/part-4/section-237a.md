# Finance Act 2004, section 237A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-07-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 237A. Liability of individual[^key-0ff6f9031c2c3808b9cd33a93159e8ea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^key-7055643fff30dd4e1a4ed50a34b655c1]

(1) The individual is liable to the annual allowance charge.

(2) The individual is liable to the annual allowance charge whether or not—

- (a) the individual, and
- (b) the scheme administrator of the pension scheme or pension schemes concerned,

are resident [^key-d121b4ca763a410e2dc4a1473f833943]... [^key-3a37c51b46a11178c5377b8d5de48e93]... in the United Kingdom.
