# Finance Act 2004, section 175

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-07-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 175. Authorised employer payments[^c-aa4bc8369cac4367f379575f117af6d5][^c-8a7b6ca9a3fcc82c6b924c617b9cfdf2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The only payments which a registered pension scheme that is an occupational pension scheme is authorised to make to or in respect of a person who is or has been a[^c-7a30c03284e6c57806a492328b5875e8] sponsoring employer are—

- (a) public service scheme payments (see section 176),
- (b) authorised surplus payments (see section 177),
- (c) compensation payments (see section 178),
- (d) authorised employer loans (see section 179),
- (e) scheme administration employer payments (see section 180), and
- (f) payments of a description prescribed by regulations made by the Board of Inland Revenue.
