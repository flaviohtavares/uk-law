# Companies (Audit, Investigations and Community Enterprise) Act 2004, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-11-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Ceasing to be a community interest company[^c-4ea255f3c6056969cbc0c5e876d29272][^c-f0ed606106f22bb2e2f10f6553b6b89b][^c-ff9a50a6f7a90aa9833b46b8e1522f5c][^c-6a8c2dc50ce7c3ed6419722e0f916c57]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A community interest company may not cease to be a community interest company except by dissolution or as provided—

- (a) by sections 54 to 55A[^c-6995414bbde600594828aefdf428dc5d] (becoming a charity [^c-f283aed2f5da61fc5444090094c36178]...), or
- (b) if regulations are made under section 56 (becoming a registered society[^c-c659b6c4185d04b9d0fed0fbe02cca2b] ), by the regulations.
