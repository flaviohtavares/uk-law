# Companies (Audit, Investigations and Community Enterprise) Act 2004, section 52

>

## 52. Re-registration[^key-d3a0e94f0be09a070f44f973d41b7e3b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A community interest company is excluded from re-registering under section 102 of the Companies Act 2006[^key-a4539774e01ad99a091732b2646fd304] (re-registration of limited company as unlimited).

(2) If a community interest company which is not a public company re-registers as a public company under section 90 of the Companies Act 2006[^key-b04b311e3470564b8a454f0b325873ea] , or a community interest company which is a public company re-registers as a private company under section 97 of the Companies Act 2006[^key-677f73e5d9447087009b711ea765bd9c] , the certificate of incorporation issued under section 96(2) or 101(2) of the Companies Act 2006[^key-8d0527e3cd54deae76a436504d02a118] is to contain a statement that the company is a community interest company.

(3) The fact that the certificate of incorporation contains such a statement is conclusive evidence that the company is a community interest company.
