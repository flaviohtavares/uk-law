# Companies (Audit, Investigations and Community Enterprise) Act 2004, section 56

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-11-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 56. Becoming a registered society[^c-a3fae34c52128ffc43752af99e80cddb][^c-583a00b4718877102171f19bca426847]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Unless regulations make provision to the contrary, a community interest company may not convert itself into a registered society under section 115 of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014[^c-6b30c2f436933070b95a93c9d55d4bc3] or section 62 of the Industrial and Provident Societies Act (Northern Ireland) 1969[^c-4bed77e610cd74493bf5b6757ab37cbb].

(2) If regulations make provision allowing the conversion of community interest companies under that section they may include provision modifying that section in its application by virtue of the regulations.
