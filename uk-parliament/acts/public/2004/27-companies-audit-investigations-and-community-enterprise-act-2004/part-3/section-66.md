# Companies (Audit, Investigations and Community Enterprise) Act 2004, section 66

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2025-11-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Extent

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any amendment made by this Act has the same extent as the provision to which it relates.

(2) Sections 14, 15(1)(b), (3) and (7) and 16 to[^c-741bafa2064bd709643d29f571f58f54] 18A[^c-54d930c94d667757261f0a4ef34ecb28] and Part 2[^c-e9951aa0c307f9b9984c564111dda462] extend to Northern Ireland.

(3) Subject to that, this Act (apart from section 65, this section and section 67) does not extend to Northern Ireland.
