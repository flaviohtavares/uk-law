## 15. Application of sections 15A to 15E[^M_F_0c8d21ba-680a-480c-b695-dba1e7f15efb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e0b218c7470e8afbd749074c2a804da4] The provisions of sections 15A to 15E have effect in relation to bodies appointed under section 14 (supervision of accounts and reports of issuers of transferable securities). *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e0b218c7470e8afbd749074c2a804da4]

(2 (Extent: N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e0b218c7470e8afbd749074c2a804da4] In those sections— *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e0b218c7470e8afbd749074c2a804da4]

- (a (Extent: N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e0b218c7470e8afbd749074c2a804da4] “prescribed body” means a body appointed under that section; and *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e0b218c7470e8afbd749074c2a804da4]
- (b (Extent: N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e0b218c7470e8afbd749074c2a804da4] references to the functions of a prescribed body are to its functions under that section. *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e0b218c7470e8afbd749074c2a804da4]
