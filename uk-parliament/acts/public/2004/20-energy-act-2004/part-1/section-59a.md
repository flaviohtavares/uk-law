## 59A. Constables serving with National Crime Agency[^c-370411cea830ad0cd762dfd6198f4063][^c-0601754a42baf963a40c072fd6c65b68]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A member of the Constabulary serving with the National Crime Agency[^c-1b248492384c1c9e2d02505f9c15bd27] under arrangements to which subsection (2) applies shall—

- (a) be under the direction and control of the Director General of the National Crime Agency[^c-d43ad97caa5d9fbd6d78bbfe52f61cef], and
- (b) continue to be a constable.

(2) This subsection applies to arrangements made between the Director General of the National Crime Agency[^c-cc393a9658c05eb487b065845357088d] and the chief constable.
