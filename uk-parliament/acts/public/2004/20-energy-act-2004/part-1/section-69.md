# Energy Act 2004, section 69

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-09-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 69. Minor amendments relating to the Constabulary

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-c6d376ba33ac12287d0e86ef190403da][^c-4cde278c506d1969dc5c3ce29c48afa2] Schedule 14 (which contains minor amendments relating to the Constabulary) has effect.

(2)[^c-b8ea6443b2875c06596dfb4145697bfa] The Secretary of State may by order make such modifications of subordinate legislation as appear to him to be appropriate in consequence of any provision of this Chapter.

(3)[^c-b8ea6443b2875c06596dfb4145697bfa] Orders under subsection (2) are subject to the negative resolution procedure.
