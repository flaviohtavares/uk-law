# Energy Act 2004, section 131D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-09-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/20

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131D. Recycled carbon fuel and nuclear-derived fuel[^key-9d519566258aa1c90c978c03ccf7b79a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An RTF order may—

- (a) designate as recycled carbon fuel a description of liquid or gaseous fuel which is produced wholly from waste derived from a fossil source of energy;
- (b) designate as nuclear-derived fuel a description of liquid or gaseous fuel which is produced wholly using, or by a process powered wholly by, nuclear fuel.

(2) Where a designation under subsection (1) is in force, the recycled carbon fuel or nuclear-derived fuel is to be treated for the purposes of this Chapter and any RTF order as renewable transport fuel.
