### Part 1 — Application of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act

#### Application of Schedule B1 provisions

**1.**[^c-3dcc31ab832efa02ee3a366d3c2e3fa0] (1) The provisions of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act specified in paragraph 2 of this Schedule are to have effect in relation to energy administration orders—

- (a) as they have effect in relation to administration orders under that Schedule; but
- (b) with the modifications set out in Part 2 of this Schedule.

(2) Those provisions as modified by Part 2 of this Schedule are to have effect in the case of a non-GB company[^c-d2637db06fca70af838921547e4da6bb] with the further modifications for which provision is made by or under Part 3 of this Schedule.

**2.**[^c-0e4e1acd1e251ee4f5ec5259bfae346f] Those provisions of Schedule B1 to the 1986 Act are paragraphs 1, 40 to 50, 54, 59 to 68, 70 to 75, 79, 83 to 91, 98 to 107 and 109 to 116.
