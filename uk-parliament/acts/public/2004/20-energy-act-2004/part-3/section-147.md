# Energy Act 2004, section 147

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-09-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 147. Consequential amendments of the 1989 Act

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-1e4362b1becc4d8547499de98334389f] The 1989 Act is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-1e4362b1becc4d8547499de98334389f] In section 3A—

- (a) in subsection (1) (principal objective of GEMA in relation to electricity), at the end insert “ or the provision or use of electricity interconnectors ”; and
- (b) in subsection (5)(a) (duty as to carrying out of functions), after “electricity”, where first occurring, insert “ or to participate in the operation of electricity interconnectors ”.

(3)[^key-1e4362b1becc4d8547499de98334389f] In section 29 (regulations)—

- (a) in subsection (1)(b), after “electricity”, where first occurring, insert “ from the use of electricity interconnectors, ”;
- (b) in subsection (2)(b), after “electricity” insert “ or in the use of electricity interconnectors ”;
- (c) in subsection (2)(c), after “electricity” insert “ or to participate in the operation of an electricity interconnector ”.

(4)[^key-1e4362b1becc4d8547499de98334389f] In section 30(2)(a) (duty of inspector to inspect apparatus belonging to licence holders), after “electricity” insert “ or to participate in the operation of electricity interconnectors ”.

(5)[^key-6ef3334cdd401780e22000890304268b] In section 43 (concurrent functions of GEMA and the OFT under the Enterprise Act 2002), in each of subsections (2A) and (3), after “electricity” insert “ or the use of electricity interconnectors ”.

(6)[^key-1e4362b1becc4d8547499de98334389f] In section 58(2) (information protected by directions), after “electricity” insert “ or to participate in the operation of electricity interconnectors ”.

(7)[^key-1e4362b1becc4d8547499de98334389f] In section 64(1) (interpretation), after the definitions of “electricity distributor” and “electricity supplier” insert—

> “**electricity interconnector**” has the meaning given by section 4(3E);

.

(8)[^key-1e4362b1becc4d8547499de98334389f] In section 98(1) (statistical information)—

- (a) after “electricity”, where first occurring, insert “ or the use of electricity interconnectors ”; and
- (b) at the end insert “ or to participate in the operation of electricity interconnectors ”.
