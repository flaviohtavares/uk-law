# Energy Act 2004, section 170

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-09-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 170. Modification of Chapter 3 of Part 3 under Enterprise Act 2002[^c-94755eec454ef40a3d767bb8e4c97f13][^c-6ecbca2562a3c5581953b7d8588ba2fd][^c-9a9ba38510a5e178498ff389b22b0a31][^c-656e02a0185857c795b0c6d274c17389][^c-5f2612a932ea39cbd9eeade118f49d7f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The power to modify or apply enactments conferred on the Secretary of State by each of the sections of the Enterprise Act 2002 (c. 40) mentioned in subsection (2) includes power to make such consequential modifications of this Chapter (including this Chapter as applied by section 96 of the Energy Act 2011[^c-0cb1d218c0060ac15f1eb362bcd807c5] , section 4 of the Smart Meters Act 2018[^c-3e39838cef9e681bbcb5d5ff55133bca] , section 33 of the Nuclear Energy (Finance) Act 2022 or section 44 of the Energy Act 2023[^c-356eeb2008123d2398fcb6a7e2e2b622]) as he considers appropriate in connection with any other provision made under that section.

(2) Those sections are—

- (a) sections 248 and 277 (amendments consequential on that Act); and
- (b) section 254 (power to apply insolvency law to foreign companies).
