## 142. Interpretation of Chapter 1 of Part 3[^c-6a316384ac4cb21c3dea8d63b8c6a51a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Chapter—

- “**transmission licence**” means a licence under section 6(1)(b) of the 1989 Act;

and references to the new trading and transmission arrangements are to be construed in accordance with section 133.
