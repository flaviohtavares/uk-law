# Civil Partnership Act 2004, section 214

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (29).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 214. The general conditions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The general conditions are that, under the relevant law—

- (a) the relationship may not be entered into if either of the parties is already a party to a relationship of that kind or lawfully married,
- (b) the relationship is of indeterminate duration, and

*Extent: S*

- (ba)[^c-c985f7302590e652fec5050d310bcb70] the relationship is not one of marriage,
- (c) the effect of entering into it is that the parties are—
  - (i) treated as a couple either generally or for specified purposes, but are not treated as married *(Extent: S)*[^c-dca1fd9f2ac3d5ab8500f636e657106b]or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dca1fd9f2ac3d5ab8500f636e657106b]
  - (ii (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dca1fd9f2ac3d5ab8500f636e657106b] treated as married. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dca1fd9f2ac3d5ab8500f636e657106b]
