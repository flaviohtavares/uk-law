# Civil Partnership Act 2004, section 220

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 220. Meaning of “the court”[^c20581281]

*Extent: E+W*

In sections 221 to 224 “**the court**” means—

- (a) the High Court, or
- (b)[^c-5bf2b562d2843e0de6678ed55541c346] the family court.
