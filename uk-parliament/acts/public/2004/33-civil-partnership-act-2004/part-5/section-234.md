## 234. Recognition in the UK of overseas dissolution, annulment or separation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The[^c-c5e2c6f427275d06de89d3e85b24ef99] validity of an overseas dissolution, annulment or legal separation is to be recognised in the United Kingdom *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-361e9f49c00f42cddae81727a4e9f393] Scotland *(Extent: S)* if, and only if, it is entitled to recognition by virtue of sections 235 to 237.

(2)[^c-c4dcc5aec4ce01891e9efb450c98c5f1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) For the purposes of subsection (1)[^c-ae1a3d926a935c3accb0cd020545976c] and sections 235 to 237, an overseas dissolution, annulment or legal separation is a dissolution or annulment of a civil partnership or a legal separation of civil partners which has been obtained outside the United Kingdom (whether before or after this section comes into force).
