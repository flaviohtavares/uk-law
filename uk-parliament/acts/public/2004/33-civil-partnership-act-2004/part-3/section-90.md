# Civil Partnership Act 2004, section 90

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (29).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/33
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 90. Publicisation[^c20568441]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where notices of a proposed civil partnership are received by[^c20598921] the district registrar[^c-bf95373a2f61067dee0d58f6e0d4bcb7] , he must, as soon as practicable after such receipt[^c20862941](or, if the two documents are not received[^c20863051] on the same day, after the first is received[^c20862991]), publicise the relevant information and send it to the Registrar General who must also publicise it.

(2) “**The relevant information**” means—

- (a) the names of the intended civil partners, and
- (b) the date on which it is intended to register them as civil partners of each other, being a date more than 28 days[^c-528e1f25e12167e92f9e33b434fab9aa] after publicisation by the district registrar under subsection (1).

(3) Paragraph (b) of subsection (2) is subject to section 91(1)[^c-8c96219e759a32eb73e873a57630e71a] .

(4) The manner in which and means by which relevant information is to be publicised are to be prescribed.
