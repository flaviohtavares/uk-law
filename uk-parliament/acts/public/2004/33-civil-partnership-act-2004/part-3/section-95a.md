## 95A. Validity following entry in civil partnership register[^c20599541]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subsection (2) applies where the particulars set out in a civil partnership schedule signed in accordance with section 85 are entered in the civil partnership register in pursuance of section 95(2) or (3C)[^c-d6e8a1e17b66145c31ab17573742eb03].

(2) The validity of the registration as civil partners to which the schedule relates is not to be questioned in any legal proceedings on the ground of failure to comply with a requirement or restriction imposed by or under this Part.

(3) Subsection (2)—

- (a) is subject to section 85(2), and
- (b) does not prejudice section 100.
