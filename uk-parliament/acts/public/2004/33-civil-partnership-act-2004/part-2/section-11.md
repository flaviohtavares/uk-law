## 11. Meaning of “the waiting period”[^c-30a255610c30acfe21be426b35ad7e74]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Chapter “**the waiting period**”, in relation to a notice of proposed civil partnership, means the period—

- (a) beginning the day after the notice is recorded, and
- (b) subject to section 12, ending at the end of the period of 28[^c-8594174cad5cc47896a7b196fbccedeb] days beginning with that day.
