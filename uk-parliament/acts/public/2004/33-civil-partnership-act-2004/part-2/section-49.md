## 49. Grounds on which civil partnership is void

*Extent: E+W*

Where two people register as civil partners of each other in England and Wales, the civil partnership is void if—

- (a) at the time when they do so, they are not eligible to register as civil partners of each other under Chapter 1 (see section 3), or[^c-ec57526dd49c7f2f5c3b0ee994db8700]
- (b) at the time when they do so they both know—
  - (i) that due notice of proposed civil partnership has not been given,
  - (ii) that the civil partnership document has not been duly issued,
  - (iii) that the civil partnership document is void under section 17(3) or 27(2) (registration after end of time allowed for registering),
  - (iv) that the place of registration is a place other than that specified in the notices (or notice) of proposed civil partnership and the civil partnership document, [^c20587531]. . .
  - (v) that a civil partnership registrar is not present, or
  - (vi)[^c20587571] that the place of registration is on premises that are not approved premises although the registration is purportedly in accordance with section 6(3A)(a), [^c-e3429ed8591c837bc460a087587c73b6]...
