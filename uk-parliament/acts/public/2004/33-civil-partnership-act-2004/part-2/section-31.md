# Civil Partnership Act 2004, section 31

## 31. Offences relating to civil partnership schedule

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person commits an offence if he issues a civil partnership schedule knowing that he does so—

- (a) before the waiting period in relation to each notice of proposed civil partnership has expired, or[^c-78d4b97408b1b0d6286e28f68f8b0f54]
- (b) after the end of the applicable period, [^c-18b89fcc9e4349c5edb939dad5d28104]...
- (c)[^c-e9c866fa36333acecc45491cfba57a07] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) A person commits an offence if, in his actual or purported capacity as a civil partnership registrar, he officiates at the signing of a civil partnership schedule by proposed civil partners knowing that he does so—

- (a) at a place other than the place specified in the notices of proposed civil partnership and the civil partnership schedule,
- (aa)[^c20587121] on premises that are not approved premises although the signing is purportedly in accordance with section 6(3A)(a),
- (ab)[^c-c0a758fae581e1da0407234cc537e21a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) in the absence of a civil partnership registrar,
- (c) before the waiting period in relation to each notice of proposed civil partnership has expired, or
- (d) even though the civil partnership is void under section 49(b) [^c-2a1e0c1eb09d34ac061f20f0f22e52a4]....

(3) A person guilty of an offence under subsection (1) or (2)(a), (aa), (b), (c) or (d)[^c20587191] is liable on conviction on indictment to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 5 years or to a fine (or both).

(3A)[^c20587221] A person guilty of an offence under subsection (2)(ab) is liable on conviction on indictment to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 14 years or to a fine or both.

(4) A prosecution under this section may not be commenced more than 3 years after the commission of the offence.
