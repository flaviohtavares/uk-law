## SCHEDULE 25 — Amendment of certain enactments relating to pensions

*Extent: E+W*

Section 256

### Fire Services Act 1947 (c. 41)

**1.** In section 26 (firemen’s pension scheme), in subsections (1) and (2A), for “widows,” substitute “ surviving spouses, surviving civil partners, ”.

### House of Commons Members' Fund Act 1948 (c. 36)

**2.** In section 4 (provision for cases of special hardship), in subsection (1)(b), for “widowers” substitute “ widowers, surviving civil partners ”.
