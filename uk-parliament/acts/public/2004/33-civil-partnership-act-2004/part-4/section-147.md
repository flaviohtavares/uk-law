# Civil Partnership Act 2004, section 147

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (29).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 147. Corrections and cancellations[^c20569761]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulations under section 159 may make provision for the making of corrections by the Registrar General or any registrar.

(2) The Registrar General must cancel the registration of a void civil partnership or direct the registrar to do so.

(3)[^key-d5b298aa59cf10643d52797e7faacaa1] Subsection (4) applies in relation to a civil partnership formed in Northern Ireland before, on or after 13th January 2020 where, before the civil partnership is formed, a full gender recognition certificate has been issued under the Gender Recognition Act 2004 to either or each of the parties.

(4) The Registrar General, after consulting the parties, may—

- (a) modify, or direct a registrar to modify, the recorded status of either or both of the parties in the registration of the civil partnership, or
- (b) direct a registrar to modify the way in which the status of either or both of the parties is to be recorded when registering the civil partnership,

for the purpose of reducing the risk that the recorded status of the parties in the registration of the civil partnership will disclose the issue of the gender recognition certificate or either of the certificates.
