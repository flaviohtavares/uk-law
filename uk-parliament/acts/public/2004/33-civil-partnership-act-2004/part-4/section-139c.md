# Civil Partnership Act 2004, section 139C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (29).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/33

## 139C. Declaration to accompany information and evidence[^c-b3dc9bb2ae4d63efabba32ec86a466e4]

*Extent: N.I.*

Where information and evidence accompany the civil partnership notice in accordance with sections 139A and 139B that information and evidence must also be accompanied by a declaration in writing and signed by the party who makes it that the party believes all of the information and evidence accompanying the notice to be true.
