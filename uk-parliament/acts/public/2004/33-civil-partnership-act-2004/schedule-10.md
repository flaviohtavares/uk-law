## SCHEDULE 10 — FORBIDDEN DEGREES OF RELATIONSHIP: SCOTLAND[^c-2fc83451eefcd70518a4b4720ad5fc75]

*Extent: S*

(introduced by section 86)

- 1 ***Relationships by consanguinity***
  - Parent
  - Child
  - Grandparent
  - Grandchild
  - Sibling
  - Aunt or uncle
  - Niece or nephew
  - Great-grandparent
  - Great-grandchild
- 2 ***Relationships by affinity referred to in section 86(3)***
  - Child of former spouse
  - Child of former civil partner
  - Former spouse of parent
  - Former civil partner of parent
  - Former spouse of grandparent
  - Former civil partner of grandparent
  - Grandchild of former spouse
  - Grandchild of former civil partner.
