# Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, etc. ) Act 2004, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2024-04-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Money[^key-93f99fc0cd4c7a43927d465f5613f541]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenditure incurred by a Minister of the Crown in connection with this Act, and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable under any other enactment out of money provided by Parliament.
