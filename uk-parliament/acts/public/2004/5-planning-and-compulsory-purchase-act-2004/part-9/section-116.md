# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 116

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 116. Isles of Scilly[^c-218f1cdb2f9b064f831cfb5f7bb815bc][^c-2eff4cbdd607e054037f6df1655ff357]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Act applies to the Isles of Scilly subject to such exceptions, adaptations and modifications as the Secretary of State may by order direct.

(2) An order may in particular provide for—

- (a) the Council of the Isles of Scilly to enter into arrangements in pursuance of section 4;
- (b) the exercise by the Council of the Isles of Scilly of any function exercisable by a local planning authority or minerals and waste planning authority[^c-25d64e61d54ab4ba5b46364e6a4de2dc] under Part 2 or 3[^c-23b5fa09f7bfe711ae7a75b082665198].

(3) But an order must not be made under this section unless the Secretary of State has consulted the Council of the Isles of Scilly.
