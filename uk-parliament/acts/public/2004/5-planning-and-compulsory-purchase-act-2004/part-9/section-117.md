# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 117

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 117. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-7b9cfe94da32376785a07562c2150c8f][^c-5b3a35ee29b0b1ad35df9ba045f51e3e] Expressions used in this Act and in the principal Act have the same meaning in this Act as in that Act.

(2)[^c-7b9cfe94da32376785a07562c2150c8f][^c-5b3a35ee29b0b1ad35df9ba045f51e3e] Expressions used in this Act and in the listed buildings Act have the same meaning in this Act as in that Act.

(3)[^c-7b9cfe94da32376785a07562c2150c8f][^c-5b3a35ee29b0b1ad35df9ba045f51e3e] Expressions used in this Act and in the hazardous substances Act have the same meaning in this Act as in that Act.

(4)[^c-7b9cfe94da32376785a07562c2150c8f][^c-5b3a35ee29b0b1ad35df9ba045f51e3e] The planning Acts are—

- (a) the principal Act;
- (b) the listed buildings Act;
- (c) the hazardous substances Act;
- (d) the Planning (Consequential Provisions) Act 1990 (c. 11).

(5)[^c-7b9cfe94da32376785a07562c2150c8f][^c-5b3a35ee29b0b1ad35df9ba045f51e3e] The principal Act is the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (c. 8).

(6)[^c-7b9cfe94da32376785a07562c2150c8f][^c-5b3a35ee29b0b1ad35df9ba045f51e3e] The listed buildings Act is the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990 (c. 9).

(7)[^c-7b9cfe94da32376785a07562c2150c8f][^c-5b3a35ee29b0b1ad35df9ba045f51e3e] The hazardous substances Act is the Planning (Hazardous Substances) Act 1990 (c. 10).

(8)[^c-5d6e4d5eb23886269b7b626bca159bcf] The Scottish planning Acts are—

- (a) the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997 (c. 8);
- (b) the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) (Scotland) Act 1997 (c. 9);
- (c) the Planning (Hazardous Substances) (Scotland) Act 1997 (c. 10); and
- (d) the Planning (Consequential Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1997 (c. 11).
