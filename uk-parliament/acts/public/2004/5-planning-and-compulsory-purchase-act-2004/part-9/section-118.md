# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 118

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 118. Amendments[^key-da069d97674345d8cee42add7e19ee65]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-9a46df7b07ee79b8409cc8373e7bfd9e] Schedule 6 contains amendments of the planning Acts.

(2)[^key-fd0530c3fcca85bf150f6a2b0c6687c2][^key-4295203fd39e46f5ccb4fc81744ec3da] Schedule 7 contains amendments of other enactments.

(3)[^key-9a46df7b07ee79b8409cc8373e7bfd9e] A reference in Schedule 1 to the National Assembly for Wales (Transfer of Functions) Order 1999 to an enactment amended by this Act must be taken to be a reference to the enactment as so amended.

(4)[^key-9a46df7b07ee79b8409cc8373e7bfd9e] But subsection (3) does not affect such an enactment to the extent that the amendment makes express provision in connection with the exercise of a function in relation to Wales.
