## 119. Transitionals

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-bb4ef1b0608b59689370fdcce0ee3ebd][^c-8629543bc4fbd5db8181f8429eb8805e] Schedule 8 contains transitional provisions relating to Parts 1 and 2.

(2)[^c-5faa2a662a88a72393c14b48962e6f73] The Scottish Ministers may by order make such transitional provision for Scotland, corresponding to the provisions of Schedule 4 and to section 30B of the hazardous substances Act (inserted by section 79(3)), as they consider necessary or expedient.
