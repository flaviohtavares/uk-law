## 114. Examinations[^c-a4b6d9ba45f208c9fc055923559d9ce6][^c-2c6e9c20a42aa3da063ea4f7378772c9]

*Extent: E+W*

An examination of any strategy,[^c-48d4c0713264243f2eaf94c78cc4c7d7] document or plan for the purposes of Part 1A,[^c-5873f048227f6dc4b20c65e731d6fabb] Part 2 or Part 6 of this Act is a statutory inquiry within the meaning of the Tribunals and Inquiries Act 1992 (c. 53).
