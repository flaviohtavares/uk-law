## 111. Crown

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-3a169a91fa976c9f22f91512813206a6] This Act (except Part 8) binds the Crown.

(2)[^c-84071aa3474adda79c3b5a74740af578] The amendment of an enactment by or by virtue of Part 8 applies to the Crown to the extent that the enactment amended so applies.
