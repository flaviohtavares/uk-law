# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 53. Fees and charges[^c-af0a48e24847050b1afb96ae5d378392][^c-7481f82485948597f5291ccf500bac05][^c-d854d30c6a25594b493ffa0881eb77ee]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6] Section 303 (fees for planning applications, etc) of the principal Act is amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]

(2) The following subsections are substituted for subsections (1) and (2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]

> (1) The appropriate authority may by regulations make provision for the payment of a charge or fee to a local planning authority in respect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]
>
> - (a) the performance by the local planning authority of any function they have; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]
> - (b) anything done by them which is calculated to facilitate or is conducive or incidental to the performance of any such function. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]
>
> (2) The regulations may prescribe— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]
>
> - (a) the person by whom the charge or fee is payable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]
> - (b) provision as to the calculation of the charge or fee (including the person by whom it is to be calculated); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]
> - (c) circumstances in which no charge or fee is to be paid; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]
> - (d) circumstances in which a charge or fee is to be transferred from one local planning authority to another. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]
>
> (2A) The appropriate authority is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]
>
> - (a) the Secretary of State in relation to England; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]
> - (b) the National Assembly for Wales in relation to Wales, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]
>
> and in the case of regulations made by the National Assembly for Wales section 333(3) must be ignored. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]

(3) In subsection (4) after the first “prescribed” there is inserted “ charge or ”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]

(4) After subsection (5) there are inserted the following subsections— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]

> (5A) If the local planning authority calculate the amount of fees or charges in pursuance of provision made by regulations under subsection (1) the authority must secure that, taking one financial year with another, the income from the fees or charges does not exceed the cost of the performance of the function or doing of the thing (as the case may be). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]
>
> (5B) A financial year is the period of 12 months beginning with 1 April. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]

(5) Subsection (6) is omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54a3d39536e1467cd26b5eacd65cf6a6]
