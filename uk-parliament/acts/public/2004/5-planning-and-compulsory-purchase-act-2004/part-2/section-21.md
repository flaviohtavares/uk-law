# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-11-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21. Intervention by Secretary of State (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-54ee1d1776b7d0b409d45a583b22211d][^c-db9cc1ed85e2fa2f3da4611e469114ca][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the Secretary of State thinks that a local development document is unsatisfactory— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) he may at any time before the document is adopted under section 23 direct the local planning authority to modify the document in accordance with the direction; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) if he gives such a direction he must state his reasons for doing so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2) The authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) must comply with the direction; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) must not adopt the document unless the Secretary of State gives notice that he is satisfied that they have complied with the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3) But subsection (2) does not apply if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] or to the extent that[^c-609e2c61407fd6cb462765b09a34d80b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] the Secretary of State withdraws the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4) At any time before a development plan document is adopted by a local planning authority the Secretary of State may direct that the document (or any part of it) is submitted to him for his approval. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5) The following paragraphs apply if the Secretary of State gives a direction under subsection (4)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) the authority must not take any step in connection with the adoption of the document until the Secretary of State gives his decision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] , or withdraws the direction[^c-c64ec09a85a4666256e5461334dd8042] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a];
- (b)[^c-c2c1d8f6eff3f436097216af4818c150] if the direction is given, and not withdrawn, before the authority have submitted the document under section 20(1), the Secretary of State must hold an independent examination; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c) if the direction is given after the authority have submitted the document but before the person appointed to carry out the examination has made his recommendations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] , and is not withdrawn before those recommendations are made, the person[^c-1240c23776493516c07a6289d1efc7a6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] must make his recommendations to the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (d)[^c-13ee333edf051a380215310078f765fb] the document has no effect unless the document or (as the case may be) the relevant part of it has been approved by the Secretary of State, or the direction is withdrawn. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5A)[^c-5cbcabc03d19624082469c2790363603] Subsections (4) to (7C) of section 20 apply to an examination held under subsection (5)(b), the reference to the local planning authority in subsection (7C) of that section being read as a reference to the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5B) For the purposes of subsection (5)(d) the “relevant part” of a development plan document is the part that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) is covered by a direction under subsection (4) which refers to only part of the document, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) continues to be covered by a direction under subsection (4) following the partial withdrawal of the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6) The Secretary of State must publish the recommendations made to him by virtue of subsection (5)(b) or (c) and the reasons of the person making the recommendations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(7) In considering a document or part of a document submitted under subsection (4) the Secretary of State may take account of any matter which he thinks is relevant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(8) It is immaterial whether any such matter was taken account of by the authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(9) In relation to a document or part of a document submitted to him under subsection (4) the Secretary of State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) may approve, approve subject to specified modifications or reject the document or part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) must give reasons for his decision under paragraph (a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(9A)[^c-f3f9a9c5cae83154e904a9d024c6182f] The Secretary of State may at any time— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) after a development plan document has been submitted for independent examination under section 20, but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) before it is adopted under section 23, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

direct the local planning authority to withdraw the document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(10) In the exercise of any function under this section the Secretary of State must have regard to the local development scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(11)[^c-26d83fc07a3d116e2e59570127f6e6d0] The local planning authority must reimburse the Secretary of State for any expenditure incurred by the Secretary of State under this section that is specified in a notice given to the authority by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(12)[^c-f63200067b4df17c295bbe569e002c3b] In the case of a joint local development document or a joint development plan document, the Secretary of State may apportion liability for the expenditure on such basis as the Secretary of State thinks just between the local planning authorities who have prepared the document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
