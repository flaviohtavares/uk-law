## 17. Local development documents (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c8815903c68b5403109cf3780d3bd3e1][^c-2875708a8b02f3f987e8a7677eaab1c1][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-e6739171a658d6b0cf280f89243ea385] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-e6739171a658d6b0cf280f89243ea385] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-2a0fca62b3571fbb76101e5d09f9cf8a] The local planning authority's local development documents[^c-e42a5661ce325554410a6807f294e6e6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] must (taken as a whole) set out the authority’s policies (however expressed) relating to the development and use of land in their area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4) In the case of the documents which are included in a minerals and waste development scheme they must also (taken as a whole) set out the authority’s policies (however expressed) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] Where a county council is required to prepare a minerals and waste development scheme in respect of an area, the council's local development documents must (taken as a whole) set out the council's policies (however expressed) for that area[^c-3cadf555e02953d5ed50879b6d50127f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] within the meaning of paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the principal Act (ignoring sub-paragraph (1)(i)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5) If to any extent a policy set out in a local development document conflicts with any other statement or information in the document the conflict must be resolved in favour of the policy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6) The authority must keep under review their local development documents having regard to the results of any review carried out under section 13 or 14. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6A)[^c-8081fa1880b605baea451969cc9adee9] The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision requiring a local planning authority to review a local development document at such times as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6B) If regulations under subsection (6A) require a local planning authority to review a local development document— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) they must consider whether to revise the document following each review, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) if they decide not to do so, they must publish their reasons for considering that no revisions are necessary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6C) Any duty imposed by virtue of subsection (6A) applies in addition to the duty in subsection (6). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(7) Regulations under this section may prescribe— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (za (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-421121643c1a63a4bae96a0ffc776493][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] which descriptions of documents are, or if prepared are, to be prepared as local development documents; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (a) which descriptions of local development documents are development plan documents; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) the form and content of the local development documents; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c) the time at which any step in the preparation of any such document must be taken. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(8) A document is a local development document only in so far as it or any part of it— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) is adopted by resolution of the local planning authority as a local development document; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) is approved by the Secretary of State under section 21 or 27. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c)[^c-0d439c6aaca4bc213805cc54426bef2b] is approved by the Mayor of London under paragraph 2 of Schedule A1; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (d) is approved by a combined authority under paragraph 6 of that Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (e)[^c-1b65154753f21173e8d9fed906c8f2ba] is approved by an upper-tier county council (as defined in that Schedule) under paragraph 7C of that Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
