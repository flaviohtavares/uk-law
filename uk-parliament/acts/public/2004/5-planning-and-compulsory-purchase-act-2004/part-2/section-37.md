# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 37. Interpretation (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-faf9fcc67fd8567ecb78d79ffe8cf2ef][^key-1f0239d5ae537bd4c3290256a0b2108d][^key-c48f5628e722a779fa9ef84a4fc428bf][^key-5b953d266bc35ffa728d179d1e4adc20][^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Local development scheme must be construed in accordance with section 15. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2)[^key-f30d7b1b1e0d0cdefaa6755b149e5839] Local development document must be construed in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] sections 17 and 18(3)[^key-e3e08b5059af2057cf0788fe9c918fdb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a].

(3)[^key-b9cff80f37f56edf1a0173c67eca3476] A development plan document is a local development document which is specified as a development plan document in the local development scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4)[^key-9ae38c634f34b7390b41c29ec5b2cf8a][^key-7e6fd5e4c6c28eb13f6fbfedd6e204c1] Local planning authorities are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) district councils; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) London borough councils; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c) metropolitan district councils; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (d) county councils in relation to any area in England for which there is no district council; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (e) the Broads Authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5)[^key-8164dc04f5321c10fee7a2ec3e5fedd3][^key-c976bb6b1ad39eafb558739d9d05c366] A National Park authority is the local planning authority for the whole of its area and subsection (4) must be construed subject to that. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5ZA (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e453e0f6e04b9dadebb923c35677d266][^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] Subsection (4) must also be construed subject to any order under section 198(2) of the Localism Act 2011 so far as providing that a Mayoral development corporation is, as regards an area, to be the local planning authority for some or all of the purposes of this Part in relation to some or all kinds of development. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5ZB (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] Where such an order makes such provision, that MDC is, in relation to the kinds of development concerned, the local planning authority for the area and purposes concerned in place of any authority who, in relation to those kinds of development, would otherwise be the local planning authority for that area and those purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5A)[^key-9b401c65d888e2474f73e851e6de74ee] Subsection (4) must *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] additionally be construed, and subsection (5ZB) must be construed,[^key-9b1aca1964873c2868112e79f9834293] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] subject to any designation order under section 13 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 (power to make designation orders) providing that the Homes and Communities Agency is to be the local planning authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) for an area specified in the order, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) for all purposes of this Part or any such purposes so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5B) Where such an order makes such provision, the Homes and Communities Agency is the local planning authority for the area and the purposes concerned in place of any authority who would otherwise be the local planning authority for that area and those purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5C)[^key-c49efc07c136679380b5ddae1c09a8a2] Joint local development document must be construed in accordance with section 28(10). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5D) Joint development plan document must be construed in accordance with section 28A(7). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6)[^key-da90e1edb18540cc6778f89f56338e32] Regional strategy” means a regional strategy under Part 5 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6A) “ **Responsible regional authorities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]** ” is to be construed in accordance with Part 5 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(7) This section applies for the purposes of this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
