## 15. Local development scheme (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-7b14a72d35ac2668d777688a7d85fefe][^key-5c7225469a52d22bdee5719dc3b9fe08][^key-98f4c0dbe1b61a5cc2b1fa17f401c961][^key-9833ca95bc5de0c5903e5e25395fa588][^key-5864c7feb43d514b3eac2ec8666285fc][^key-7683a3ece30ec07a2cedfcd596b5985e][^key-e09c57ecb9bee820da3066b6bbd944ba][^key-a4786e1a7152c2ef98727405a7212f1b][^key-809ffa13831c0b21528279546a0b5250][^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^key-7683a3ece30ec07a2cedfcd596b5985e][^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The local planning authority must prepare and maintain a scheme to be known as their local development scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2) The scheme must specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a)[^key-59211d4539a120303c13a22da453ecc0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-d288189dd688b1cbbf3d34d42eba49f8][^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] the local development documents which are to be development plan documents; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) the subject matter and geographical area to which each *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] development plan document[^key-86fc8627b8eb0e15f7ee6e70a49e8e45] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] is to relate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c)[^key-c32490b5eb5618392fe280f11ae5ab34] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] development plan documents[^key-bf9eca9c8c155333870528971c4665cd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] (if any) are to be prepared jointly with one or more other local planning authorities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (e) any matter or area in respect of which the authority have agreed (or propose to agree) to the constitution of a joint committee under section 29; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (f) the timetable for the preparation and revision of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] development plan documents[^key-f17ed4a5d3416d2b462cd6b0460a5f90] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a];
- (g) such other matters as are prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3)[^key-54b45722e524d3d27c2d75b1639c242e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3A)[^key-6d1ec7a63d385741b15dae732d59dd1a] If a local planning authority have not prepared a local development scheme, the Secretary of State or the Mayor of London may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) prepare a local development scheme for the authority, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) direct the authority to bring that scheme into effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4) The Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] or the Mayor of London[^key-34fb654c472ff1751b9c5be6831d5897] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] may direct the local planning authority to make such amendments to the scheme as he thinks appropriate *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] for the purpose of ensuring[^key-7e2d651e22a5af51086dc3a5acfafa6f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] full and effective coverage (both geographically and with regard to subject matter)[^key-a8bc5bab296b3638b3a1cda8f404f4d7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] of the authority's area by the development plan documents (taken as a whole) for that area *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a].

(4A)[^key-c0685fb97a2d64784d63e18629f4383d] The Mayor of London— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) may give a direction under subsection *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] (3A) or[^key-c15bd6975ec78aec78a27d5f771d295a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] (4) only if the local planning authority are a London borough, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) in considering whether to give such a direction, and which amendments to include in the direction, must have regard to any guidance issued by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5) A direction under subsection[^key-5e3c9a950be1dd305feb2ffc6264c257] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] (3A) or[^key-d356a2f59c8c3e1a97dfa7d3434ed167] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] (4) must contain the Secretary of State’s *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] , or (as the case may be) the Mayor of London's,[^key-0eebdd1bddaaf5ac497b078f7220dd49] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] reasons for giving it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6) The local planning authority must comply with a direction given under subsection *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] (3A) or[^key-c7b23f72f2c73fa2785cd38fc2241edc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] (4). In the case of a direction given by the Mayor of London, this subsection is subject to subsections (6A) to (6E).[^key-6dc5fffa11348709eca763b66987347f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6A)[^key-9a71e940b403a1f40a5c1b2a5cde8480] If at any time the Mayor of London gives a direction under subsection *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] (3A) or[^key-1308ec1ac6adf30b552197a56886ba52] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] (4)—

- (a) he must at that time send a copy of the direction to the Secretary of State, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) effect is not to be given to the direction[^key-7f41508fb3afa6409388f289ed848812] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] until such time as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6B) The Secretary of State may, within such time as may be prescribed, direct the local planning authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) to disregard a direction given under subsection *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] (3A) or[^key-55ce8ad1e9e145dd90daa5337ae78b48] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] (4) by the Mayor of London, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) to give effect to the direction with such modifications as may be specified in the Secretary of State's direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6C) Such a direction must contain the Secretary of State's reasons for giving it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6D) If at any time the Secretary of State gives a direction under subsection (6B), the Secretary of State must at that time send a copy of the direction to the Mayor of London. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6E) The local planning authority must comply with any direction given by the Secretary of State under subsection (6B). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(7)[^key-c8603a26a7bc3f35be87e17061fd3541] To bring the scheme into effect, the local planning authority must resolve that the scheme is to have effect and in the resolution specify the date from which the scheme is to have effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(8) The local planning authority must revise their local development scheme— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) at such time as they consider appropriate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) when directed to do so by the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] or the Mayor of London[^key-be6ffac80309d6b96e1dd73c79aae7d7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]. In the case of a direction given by the Mayor of London, paragraph (b) is subject to subsections (8B) to (8F).[^key-3a60150b1691d7426fa9b2d15c67eb52] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(8A)[^key-877929ff088ddd49071c859301d36dc9] The Mayor of London— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) may give a direction under subsection (8) only if the local planning authority are a London borough, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) in considering whether to give such a direction, must have regard to any guidance issued by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(8AA (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-01dc099c7edede41a75b5994df99aa8c][^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] A direction may be given under subsection (8)(b) only if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] —[^key-c3406619e59faa0daa94d8d057415034] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(a) the person giving the direction thinks that revision of the scheme is necessary for the purpose of ensuring *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] full and effective coverage (both geographically and with regard to subject matter)[^key-d31da3944a23be908dd9c00677bed2d7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] of the authority's area by the development plan documents (taken as a whole) for that area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

, or[^key-d46f32f0552f33af94be30073f6e4843] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(b) the Secretary of State has published data standards under section 36(3) which apply to the local development scheme and the person giving the direction thinks that the scheme should be revised so that it complies with the standards. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(8B) If at any time the Mayor of London gives a direction under subsection (8)(b)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) he must at that time send a copy of the direction to the Secretary of State, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) the scheme is not to be revised until such time as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(8C) The Secretary of State may, within such time as may be prescribed, direct the local planning authority to disregard a direction given under subsection (8)(b) by the Mayor of London. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(8D) Such a direction must contain the Secretary of State's reasons for giving it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(8E) If at any time the Secretary of State gives a direction under subsection (8C), the Secretary of State must at that time send a copy of the direction to the Mayor of London. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(8F) The local planning authority must comply with any direction given by the Secretary of State under subsection (8C). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(9) Subsections (2) to (7) apply to the revision of a scheme as they apply to the preparation of the scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(9A)[^key-815a545ce2569683cb1506db575a15f6] The local planning authority must make the following available to the public— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) the up-to-date text of the scheme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) a copy of any amendments made to the scheme, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c) up-to-date information showing the state of the authority's compliance (or non-compliance) with the timetable mentioned in subsection (2)(f). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(10)[^key-a84da090b7103cba1c6d08e7bf9e914c] Section 38(1) of the Greater London Authority Act 1999 (delegation of functions by the Mayor) does not apply to the Mayor of London's functions under this section of giving a direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
