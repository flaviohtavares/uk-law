# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 33A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 33A. Duty to co-operate in relation to planning of sustainable development (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-9e4b6e441e5138aebfdb7ad15db03252][^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Each person who is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) a local planning authority, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) a county council in England that is not a local planning authority, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c) a body, or other person, that is prescribed or of a prescribed description, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

must co-operate with every other person who is within paragraph (a), (b) or (c) or subsection (9) in maximising the effectiveness with which activities within subsection (3) are undertaken. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2) In particular, the duty imposed on a person by subsection (1) requires the person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) to engage constructively, actively and on an ongoing basis in any process by means of which activities within subsection (3) are undertaken, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) to have regard to activities of a person within subsection (9) so far as they are relevant to activities within subsection (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3) The activities within this subsection are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) the preparation of development plan documents, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) the preparation of other local development documents, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c) the preparation of marine plans under the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 for the English inshore region, the English offshore region or any part of either of those regions, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (d) activities that can reasonably be considered to prepare the way for activities within any of paragraphs (a) to (c) that are, or could be, contemplated, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (e) activities that support activities within any of paragraphs (a) to (c), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

so far as relating to a strategic matter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4) For the purposes of subsection (3), each of the following is a “strategic matter”— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) sustainable development or use of land that has or would have a significant impact on at least two planning areas, including (in particular) sustainable development or use of land for or in connection with infrastructure that is strategic and has or would have a significant impact on at least two planning areas, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) sustainable development or use of land in a two-tier area if the development or use— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (i) is a county matter, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] has or would have a significant impact on a county matter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5) In subsection (4)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- “ **county matter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]** ” has the meaning given by paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the principal Act (ignoring sub-paragraph 1(1)(i)), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- “ **planning area *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]** ” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (a) the area of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
    - (i) a district council (including a metropolitan district council), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
    - (ii) a London borough council, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
    - (iii) a county council in England for an area for which there is no district council, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

    but only so far as that area is neither in a National Park nor in the Broads, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (b) a National Park, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (c) the Broads, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (d) the English inshore region, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (e) the English offshore region, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- “ **two-tier area *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]** ” means an area— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (a) for which there is a county council and a district council, but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (b) which is not in a National Park. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6) The engagement required of a person by subsection (2)(a) includes, in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) considering whether to consult on and prepare, and enter into and publish, agreements on joint approaches to the undertaking of activities within subsection (3), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) if the person is a local planning authority, considering whether to agree under section 28 to prepare joint local development documents. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(7) A person subject to the duty under subsection (1) must have regard to any guidance given by the Secretary of State about how the duty is to be complied with. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(8) A person, or description of persons, may be prescribed for the purposes of subsection (1)(c) only if the person, or persons of that description, exercise functions for the purposes of an enactment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(9) A person is within this subsection if the person is a body, or other person, that is prescribed or of a prescribed description. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(10) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- “ **the English inshore region *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]** ” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] **the English offshore region *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]** ” have the same meaning as in the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- “ **land *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]** ” includes the waters within those regions and the bed and subsoil of those waters. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
