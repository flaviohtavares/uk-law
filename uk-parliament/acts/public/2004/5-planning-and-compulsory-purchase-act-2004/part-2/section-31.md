# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 31. Dissolution of joint committee (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-46812a7bd43f3dfc26e0b9c3f3dc9df9][^key-97ed7e820ddfd27150f5b35d80e30394][^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if a constituent authority requests the Secretary of State to revoke an order constituting a joint committee as the local planning authority for any area or in respect of any matter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2) The Secretary of State may revoke the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3) Any step taken by the joint committee in relation to a local development scheme or a local development document must be treated for the purposes of any corresponding scheme or document as a step taken by a successor authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4) A successor authority is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) a local planning authority which were a constituent authority of the joint committee; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) a joint committee constituted by order under section 29 for an area which does not include an area which was not part of the area of the joint committee mentioned in subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5) If the revocation takes effect at any time when an independent examination is being carried out in relation to a local development document the examination must be suspended. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6) But if before the end of the period prescribed for the purposes of this subsection a successor authority falling within subsection (4)(a) requests the Secretary of State to do so he may direct that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) the examination is resumed in relation to the corresponding document; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) any step taken for the purposes of the suspended examination has effect for the purposes of the resumed examination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(7) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision as to what is a corresponding scheme or document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
