# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 21A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-11-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 21A. Temporary direction pending possible use of intervention powers (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9cd255f496860c9fc1ed4a3d0dd224ea][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the Secretary of State is considering whether to give a direction to a local planning authority under section 21 in relation to a development plan document or other local development document, he may direct the authority not to take any step in connection with the adoption of the document— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) until the time (if any) specified in the direction, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) until the direction is withdrawn. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2) A document to which a direction under this section relates has no effect while the direction is in force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3) A direction given under this section in relation to a document ceases to have effect if a direction is given under section 21 in relation to that document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
