# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 16

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 16. Minerals and waste development scheme (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8c05cacf0e9b883779d0830a82f8c23a][^c-eae81633400bcb099ba3f4f0208a5d7c][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A county council in respect of any part of their area for which there is a district council must prepare and maintain a scheme to be known as their minerals and waste development scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2) Section 15 (ignoring subsections (1) and (2)(e)) applies in relation to a minerals and waste development scheme as it applies in relation to a local development scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3) This Part applies to a minerals and waste development scheme as it applies to a local development scheme and for that purpose— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) references to a local development scheme include references to a minerals and waste development scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) references to a local planning authority include references to a county council. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4) But subsection (3) does not apply to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) section 17(3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) section 24(1)(b), (4) and (7); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c) the references in section 24(5) to subsection (4) and the Mayor; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (d) sections 29 to 31. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5)[^c-07ab187210ee502b1c15b91d178ea856] Also, subsection (3)(b) does not apply to section 33A(1)(a) and (b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
