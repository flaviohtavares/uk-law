# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-11-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 27. Secretary of State’s default powers (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9a2fd7478b2526ebfde27250aa0850b3][^c-bc21a3f0efea398369374ea3b1389aad][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if the Secretary of State thinks that a local planning authority are failing or omitting to do anything it is necessary for them to do in connection with the preparation, revision or adoption of a development plan document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2) The Secretary of State may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) prepare or revise (as the case may be) the document, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) give directions to the authority in relation to the preparation or revision of the document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3) The Secretary of State must either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) hold an independent examination, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) direct the authority to submit the document for independent examination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4) The Secretary of State must either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) publish the recommendations and reasons of the person appointed to hold the examination, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) give directions to the authority in relation to publication of those recommendations and reasons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5) The Secretary of State may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) approve the document, or approve it subject to specified modifications, as a local development document, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) direct the authority to consider adopting the document by resolution of the authority as a local development document, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c) (except where it was prepared or revised by the Secretary of State under subsection (2)(a)) reject the document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6) Subsections (4) to (7C) of section 20 apply (subject to subsection (7) below) to an examination held under subsection (3)(a), the reference to the local planning authority in subsection (7C) of that section being read as a reference to the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(7) Subsections (5)(c), (7)(b)(ii) and (7B)(b) of section 20 do not apply to an independent examination held— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) under subsection (3)(a), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) in response to a direction under subsection (3)(b), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

in respect of a document prepared or revised by the Secretary of State under subsection (2)(a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(8) The Secretary of State must give reasons for anything he does in pursuance of subsection (2) or (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(9) The authority must reimburse the Secretary of State for any expenditure he incurs in connection with anything— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) which is done by him under subsection (2)(a), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) which the authority failed or omitted to do as mentioned in subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(10)[^c-8e11a3d034c45d059783c50fdedc3b75] In the case of a joint local development document or a joint development plan document, the Secretary of State may apportion liability for the expenditure on such basis as the Secretary of State thinks just between the local planning authorities for whom the document has been prepared. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
