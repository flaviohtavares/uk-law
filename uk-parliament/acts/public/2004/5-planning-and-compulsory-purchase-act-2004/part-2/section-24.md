## 24. Conformity with regional strategy (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2fb652a200f478b03f0a9c76fc1846d2][^c-4161c52a23358d1e342948e1fe5f947b][^c-3d14b2ed5298c08d6607ea94f16014b6][^c-46ddb1987cc9d82ea3d1e5c918ac9618][^c-07706908100c93b746c4c61d6baa3458][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The local development documents must be in general conformity with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) the regional strategy[^c-e909e879a87e81044a1e1f93cce56ca2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] (if the area of the local planning authority is in a region other than London); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) the spatial development strategy (if the local planning authority are a London borough *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] or a Mayoral development corporation[^c-7e7751ed8fd377d5c1a02eb3d53024d3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] for an area in Greater London[^c-8b95973c753b8144db72ac177ad00758] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]).

(2)[^c-7b586d19342542a785bc66984e026566] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-c69b35f6cd22825812ad3aa189b5aa8e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) A local planning authority which are a London borough *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] or a Mayoral development corporation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] for an area in Greater London[^c-47557b002678c25fcd64127caf0e251f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] —

- (a) must request the opinion in writing of the Mayor of London as to the general conformity of a development plan document with the spatial development strategy; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) may request the opinion in writing of the Mayor as to the general conformity of any other local development document with the spatial development strategy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5) Whether or not the local planning authority make a request mentioned in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] subsection (4), the Mayor may give an opinion as to the general conformity of a local development document with the spatial development strategy[^c-77768622c324c799944942d006fc7e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6)[^c-d5c51d11ba336bb3e3dfa0b69bdf573e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) If in the opinion of the Mayor a document is not in general conformity with the spatial development strategy the Mayor must be taken to have made representations seeking a change to the document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(8)[^c-0be81828c8305d8502831588c2ec20af] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9)[^c-f4041591bd71d042f2035f67b6cc6ec6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
