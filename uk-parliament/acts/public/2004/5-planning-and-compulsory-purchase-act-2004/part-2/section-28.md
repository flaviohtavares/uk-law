# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28. Joint local development documents (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f84b8ea2ca569acca092ae7608557624][^c-42ad800c80b39802edcc846d6931998d][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Two or more local planning authorities may agree to prepare one or more joint local development documents. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2) This Part applies for the purposes of any step which may be or is required to be taken in relation to a joint local development document as it applies for the purposes of any step which may be or is required to be taken in relation to a local development document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3) For the purposes of subsection (2) anything which must be done by or in relation to a local planning authority in connection with a local development document must be done by or in relation to each of the authorities mentioned in subsection (1) in connection with a joint local development document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4) Any requirement of this Part in relation to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] regional strategy[^c-c67b56202d7016691fede6d85f8f9f17] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] is a requirement in relation to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] regional strategy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] for the region in which each authority mentioned in subsection (1) is situated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5) If the authorities mentioned in subsection (1) include one or more London boroughs the requirements of this Part in relation to the spatial development strategy also apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6) Subsections (7) to (9) apply if a local planning authority withdraw from an agreement mentioned in subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(7) Any step taken in relation to the document must be treated as a step taken by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) an authority which were a party to the agreement for the purposes of any corresponding document prepared by them; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) two or more other authorities who were parties to the agreement for the purposes of any corresponding joint local development document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(8) Any independent examination of a local development document to which the agreement relates must be suspended. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(9) If before the end of the period prescribed for the purposes of this subsection an authority which were a party to the agreement request the Secretary of State to do so he may direct that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a)[^c-8c10bc2c99fa2662a3f50a3346974c3b] the examination is resumed in relation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (i) any corresponding document prepared by an authority which were a party to the agreement, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] any corresponding joint local development document prepared by two or more other authorities which were parties to the agreement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) any step taken for the purposes of the suspended examination has effect for the purposes of the resumed examination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(10) A joint local development document is a local development document prepared jointly by two or more local planning authorities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(11) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision as to what is a corresponding document *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] or a corresponding joint local development document for the purposes of this section.[^c-f141cbed4c1e215fb92733739560cfce] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
