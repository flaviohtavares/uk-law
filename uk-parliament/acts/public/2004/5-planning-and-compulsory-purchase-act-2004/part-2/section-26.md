# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 26. Revision of local development documents (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-47d1a3b6c51f9caa445563eae5bc2a12][^c-931a210c9ef01d5927868382a7c10724][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The local planning authority may at any time prepare a revision of a local development document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2) The authority must prepare a revision of a local development document— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) if the Secretary of State directs them to do so, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) in accordance with such timetable as he directs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3) This Part applies to the revision of a local development document as it applies to the preparation of the document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4) Subsection (5) applies if any part of the area of the local planning authority is an area to which an enterprise zone scheme relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5) As soon as practicable after the occurrence of a relevant event— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) the authority must review every local development document in the light of the enterprise zone scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) if they think that any modifications of the document are required in consequence of the scheme they must prepare a revised document containing the modifications. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6) The following are relevant events— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) the making of an order under paragraph 5 of Schedule 32 to the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 (c. 65) (designation of enterprise zone); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) the giving of notification under paragraph 11(1) of that Schedule (approval of modification of enterprise zone scheme). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(7) References to an enterprise zone and an enterprise zone scheme must be construed in accordance with that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
