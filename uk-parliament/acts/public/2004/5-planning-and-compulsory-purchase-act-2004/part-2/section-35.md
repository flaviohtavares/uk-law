## 35. Authorities (affected by repeal, see note) monitoring (affected by repeal, see note) reports (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-984cabd170505e5966e8a32b2896b95f][^key-f11bffc8569cef4f6297414615a586c2][^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^key-6be378129974a2a57e7adef87a181361][^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-ea15225c60cffed1b1154610a039a730] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Every local planning authority must prepare reports containing[^key-39983582d9e3627049f92e7e409cd04d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] such information as is prescribed as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) the implementation of the local development scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) the extent to which the policies set out in the local development documents are being achieved. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3) A report under subsection (2) must—[^key-cfe868dcbe742b31bd195f34f8d1a4a1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) be in respect of a period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (i) which the authority considers appropriate in the interests of transparency, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] which begins with the end of the period covered by the authority's most recent report under subsection (2), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] which is not longer than 12 months or such shorter period as is prescribed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c) be in such form as is prescribed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (d) contain such other matter as is prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3A)[^M_F_7df74dc8-b297-488c-d797-f194785f42f1] Subsection (3B) applies if a London borough council or a Mayoral development corporation have determined in accordance with section 19(1D) that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) policies to address the strategic priorities for the development and use of land in their area are set out in the spatial development strategy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) accordingly, such policies will not to that extent be set out in their development plan documents. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3B) Each report by the council or corporation under subsection (2) must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) indicate that such policies are set out in the spatial development strategy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) specify where in the strategy those policies are set out. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3C) If a combined authority established under section 103 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009 has the function of preparing the spatial development strategy for the authority’s area, subsections (3A) and (3B) also apply in relation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) a local planning authority whose area is within, or the same as, the area of the combined authority, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) the spatial development strategy published by the combined authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4)[^key-fefc7587a4a21cafd15a77aa6a49f5c3] The authority must make the authority's reports under this section available to the public. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
