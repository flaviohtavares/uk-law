# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-11-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 29. Joint committees (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c573989a9ece5e9d9def25cf69910b96][^c-eca4e482c87ac955c1237b2eeb0afc21][^c-7683a3ece30ec07a2cedfcd596b5985e][^c-5cf9b5db5f7de717069362fac874c683][^c-23fbbd78558bfba28a8aa3befddb9553][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^c-7683a3ece30ec07a2cedfcd596b5985e][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if one or more local planning authorities agree with one or more county councils in relation to any area of such a council for which there is also a district council to establish a joint committee to be, for the purposes of this Part, the local planning authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) for the area specified in the agreement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) in respect of such matters as are so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2) The Secretary of State may by order constitute a joint committee to be the local planning authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) for the area; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) in respect of those matters. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3) Such an order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) must specify the authority or authorities and county council or councils (the constituent authorities) which are to constitute the joint committee; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) may make provision as to such other matters as the Secretary of State thinks are necessary or expedient to facilitate the exercise by the joint committee of its functions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4) Provision under subsection (3)(b)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) may include provision corresponding to provisions relating to joint committees in Part 6 of the Local Government Act 1972 (c. 70); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) may apply (with or without modifications) such enactments relating to local authorities as the Secretary of State thinks appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5) If an order under this section is annulled in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) with effect from the date of the resolution the joint committee ceases to be the local planning authority as mentioned in subsection (2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) anything which the joint committee (as the local planning authority) was required to do for the purposes of this Part must be done for their area by each local planning authority which were a constituent authority of the joint committee; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c) each of those local planning authorities must revise their local development scheme accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6) Nothing in this section or section 30 confers on a local planning authority constituted by virtue of an order under this section any function in relation to section 13 or 14. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(7) The policies adopted by the joint committee in the exercise of its functions under this Part must be taken for the purposes of the planning Acts to be the policies of each of the constituent authorities which are a local planning authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(8) Subsection (9) applies to any function— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) which is conferred on a local planning authority (within the meaning of the principal Act) under or by virtue of the planning Acts, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) which relates to the authority’s local development scheme or local development documents. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(9) If the authority is a constituent authority of a joint committee references to the authority’s local development scheme or local development documents must be construed as including references to the scheme or documents of the joint committee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(10) For the purposes of subsection (4) a local authority is any of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) a county council; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) a district council; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c) a London borough council. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
