# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-11-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 23. Adoption of local development documents (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6d033e6e4c6619653d7324640491f43a][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The local planning authority may adopt a local development document (other than a development plan document) either as originally prepared or as modified to take account of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) any representations made in relation to the document; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) any other matter they think is relevant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2)[^c-c748436a9ddfd2e2c04d04226140adf5] If the person appointed to carry out the independent examination of a development plan document recommends that it is adopted, the authority may adopt the document— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) as it is, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) with modifications that (taken together) do not materially affect the policies set out in it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2A) Subsection (3) applies if the person appointed to carry out the independent examination of a development plan document— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) recommends non-adoption, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) under section 20(7C) recommends modifications (“the main modifications”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3) The authority may adopt the document— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) with the main modifications, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) with the main modifications and additional modifications if the additional modifications (taken together) do not materially affect the policies that would be set out in the document if it was adopted with the main modifications but no other modifications. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4) The authority must not adopt a development plan document unless they do so in accordance with subsection (2) or (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5) A document is adopted for the purposes of this section if it is adopted by resolution of the authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
