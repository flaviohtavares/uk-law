# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 19. Preparation of local development documents (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6d7a75349b99bb815cb69612093189d4][^c-35c7880d2721d99fbc3b94c0deca587f][^c-0af79274b55d7a6716686ba2d4da2fce][^c-ed2614ebcdb893eff0e64c521f5ac184][^c-50ce8a10dbcb37832241d09fc72d8e16][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Development plan documents[^c-9d6e66c7f1054faa37fbd06aefceef55] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] must be prepared in accordance with the local development scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(1A)[^c-3a26e59e45dbfbd7f6e246ec5f3de9b8] Development plan documents must (taken as a whole) include policies designed to secure that the development and use of land in the local planning authority's area contribute to the mitigation of, and adaptation to, climate change. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(1B)[^M_F_9c380987-d796-4ba8-bc38-f7f7f714bbc4] Each local planning authority must identify the strategic priorities for the development and use of land in the authority’s area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(1C) Policies to address those priorities must be set out in the local planning authority’s development plan documents (taken as a whole). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(1D) Subsection (1C) does not apply in the case of a London borough council or a Mayoral development corporation if and to the extent that the council or corporation are satisfied that policies to address those priorities are set out in the spatial development strategy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(1E) If a combined authority established under section 103 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009 has the function of preparing the spatial development strategy for the authority’s area, subsection (1D) also applies in relation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) a local planning authority whose area is within, or the same as, the area of the combined authority, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) the spatial development strategy published by the combined authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2) In preparing a local development document the local planning authority must have regard to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) national policies and advice contained in guidance issued by the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-33732e014ef439836c898444ceebda34][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] the local development documents which are to be development plan documents; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) the regional strategy[^c-4e67e89bc69b4046b6496ca6941429cc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] for the region in which the area of the authority is situated, if the area is outside Greater London; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c) the spatial development strategy if the authority are a London borough or if any part of the authority’s area adjoins Greater London; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (d) the regional strategy[^c-9ef2f915ce03a569e5f3eb59fccdd08b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] for any region which adjoins the area of the authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (e) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] National Development Framework for Wales,[^c-9311efedd45c7ad0a7ea3f55a1b6351b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] if any part of the authority’s area adjoins Wales; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (f)[^c-742495016addae64e146364bfd285626] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g)[^c-36698d427b17a17ad027585150e4eab6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (h) any other local development document which has been adopted by the authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (i) the resources likely to be available for implementing the proposals in the document; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (j) such other matters as the Secretary of State prescribes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3) In preparing the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] local development documents (other than their statement of community involvement)[^c-a4cf6d337d81a405c54ce00308017fdd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] the authority must also comply with their statement of community involvement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4) But subsection (3) does not apply at any time before the authority have adopted their statement of community involvement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5) The local planning authority must also— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) carry out an appraisal of the sustainability of the proposals in each *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] development plan document[^c-5fefad0ed95d8692fdb0ed902d3dccfc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a];
- (b) prepare a report of the findings of the appraisal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) as to any further documents which must be prepared by the authority in connection with the preparation of a local development document; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) as to the form and content of such documents. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(7)[^c-c49ea53e95ec60d5caf7713bd41bf1f2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
