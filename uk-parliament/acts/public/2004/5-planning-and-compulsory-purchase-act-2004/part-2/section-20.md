## 20. Independent examination (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-161e515e33d1f0bdbf7bac765a8e7cd9][^c-7c077b187b4e9386a099524af1f25d62][^c-c303dc654464ddbd9d725f2d905f594c][^c-7683a3ece30ec07a2cedfcd596b5985e][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^c-7683a3ece30ec07a2cedfcd596b5985e][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The local planning authority must submit every development plan document to the Secretary of State for independent examination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2) But the authority must not submit such a document unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) they have complied with any relevant requirements contained in regulations under this Part, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) they think the document is ready for independent examination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3) The authority must also send to the Secretary of State (in addition to the development plan document) such other documents (or copies of documents) and such information as is prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4) The examination must be carried out by a person appointed by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5) The purpose of an independent examination is to determine in respect of the development plan document— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) whether it satisfies the requirements of sections 19 and 24(1), regulations under section 17(7) and any regulations under section 36 relating to the preparation of development plan documents; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) whether it is sound. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]; and[^c-b24e3c920bd63079e9538851c006c8cd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c) whether the local planning authority complied with any duty imposed on the authority by section 33A in relation to its preparation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6) Any person who makes representations seeking to change a development plan document must (if he so requests) be given the opportunity to appear before and be heard by the person carrying out the examination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6A)[^c-a931891e5c63c682f76a5364ffb64ea7] The Secretary of State may by notice to the person appointed to carry out the examination— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) direct the person not to take any step, or any further step, in connection with the examination of the development plan document, or of a specified part of it, until a specified time or until the direction is withdrawn; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) require the person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (i) to consider any specified matters; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] to give an opportunity, or further opportunity, to specified persons to appear before and be heard by the person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] to take any specified procedural step in connection with the examination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

In this subsection “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]**specified *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]**” means specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(7)[^c-dc432d31deff5877363630d0d77dcc3f] Where the person appointed to carry out the examination— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) has carried it out, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) considers that, in all the circumstances, it would be reasonable to conclude— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (i) that the document satisfies the requirements mentioned in subsection (5)(a) and is sound, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] that the local planning authority complied with any duty imposed on the authority by section 33A in relation to the document's preparation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

the person must recommend that the document is adopted and give reasons for the recommendation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(7A) Where the person appointed to carry out the examination— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) has carried it out, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) is not required by subsection (7) to recommend that the document is adopted, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

the person must recommend non-adoption of the document and give reasons for the recommendation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(7B) Subsection (7C) applies where the person appointed to carry out the examination— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) does not consider that, in all the circumstances, it would be reasonable to conclude that the document satisfies the requirements mentioned in subsection (5)(a) and is sound, but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) does consider that, in all the circumstances, it would be reasonable to conclude that the local planning authority complied with any duty imposed on the authority by section 33A in relation to the document's preparation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(7C) If asked to do so by the local planning authority, the person appointed to carry out the examination must recommend modifications of the document that would make it one that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) satisfies the requirements mentioned in subsection (5)(a), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) is sound. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(8) The local planning authority must publish the recommendations and the reasons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
