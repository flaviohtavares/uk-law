# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-11-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 36. Regulations (affected by repeal, see note) and standards (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bde52b63d5397f71cfc505cad18c30c7][^c-f10658cdfa54c6b4e439a8e1dd57742f][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^c-b09e1c4951261befc8aceb61ca3114c5][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision in connection with the exercise by any person of functions under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2) The regulations may in particular make provision as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) the procedure to be followed by the local planning authority in carrying out the appraisal under section 19; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) the procedure to be followed in the preparation of local development documents; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c) requirements about the giving of notice and publicity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (d) requirements about inspection by the public of a local development document or any other document; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (e) the nature and extent of consultation with and participation by the public in anything done under this Part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (f) the making of representations about any matter to be included in a local development document; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (g) consideration of any such representations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (h) the remuneration and allowances payable to a person appointed to carry out an independent examination under section 20; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (i) the determination of the time at which anything must be done for the purposes of this Part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (j) the manner of publication of any draft, report or other document published under this Part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (k) monitoring the exercise by local planning authorities of their functions under this Part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (l) the making of reasonable charges for the provision of copies of documents required by or under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3)[^c-974bcc9a127b35c3a04f3b3a046c0e9b] The Secretary of State may from time to time publish data standards for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) local development schemes, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) local development documents, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (c) local development documents of a particular kind. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4) For this purpose a “data standard” is a written standard which contains technical specifications for a scheme or document or the data contained in a scheme or document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5) A local planning authority must comply with the data standards published under subsection (3) in preparing, publishing, maintaining or revising a scheme or document to which the standards apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
