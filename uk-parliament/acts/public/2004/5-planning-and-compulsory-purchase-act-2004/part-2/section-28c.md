# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 28C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28C. Modification or withdrawal of direction under section 28A (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-2a0a7f92698de9a6add944f98230e118][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a][^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may modify or withdraw a direction under section 28A by notice in writing to the authorities to which it was given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(2) The Secretary of State must, when modifying or withdrawing a direction under section 28A, notify the local planning authorities to which it was given of the reasons for the modification or withdrawal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(3) The following provisions of this section apply if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) the Secretary of State withdraws a direction under section 28A, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) the Secretary of State modifies a direction under that section so that it ceases to apply to one or more of the local planning authorities to which it was given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(4) Any step taken in relation to the joint development plan document to which the direction related is to be treated as a step taken by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) a local planning authority to which the direction applied for the purposes of any corresponding document prepared by them, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) two or more local planning authorities to which the direction applied for the purposes of any corresponding joint development plan document prepared by them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(5) Any independent examination of a joint development plan document to which the direction related must be suspended. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(6) If before the end of the period prescribed for the purposes of this subsection a local planning authority to which the direction applied request the Secretary of State to do so, the Secretary of State may direct that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

- (a) the examination is resumed in relation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (i) any corresponding document prepared by a local planning authority to which the direction applied, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a] any corresponding joint development plan document prepared by two or more local planning authorities to which the direction applied, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
- (b) any step taken for the purposes of the suspended examination has effect for the purposes of the resumed examination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]

(7) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision as to what is a corresponding document or a corresponding joint development plan document for the purposes of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-66b206efe3e504a200801217f5a84a3a]
