# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 68A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 68A. Duty to consider whether to review local development plan[^key-f3b0220cb4448d1f71020daa383d35b5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Following the publication of the National Development Framework for Wales or a revised Framework, a local planning authority must consider whether to carry out a review of their local development plan.

(2) Following the adoption or approval of a strategic development plan or revised strategic development plan for all or part of their area, a local planning authority[^key-fdf6bdaf83018d7a68a0b5c2e4d7ce7c] must consider whether to carry out a review of their local development plan.
