# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-11-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5

## 76. Annual monitoring report[^c-00b0e3c93c161d61d01069b0c28dcca6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Every local planning authority must make an annual report to the Assembly.

(2)[^c-b859071cf586cf30986ecf8da8b989c4] The annual report must contain such information as is prescribed as to the extent to which the objectives set out in the local development plan are being achieved.

(3)[^c-b859071cf586cf30986ecf8da8b989c4] The annual report must—

- (a) be made at such time as is prescribed;
- (b) be in such form as is prescribed;
- (c) contain such other matter as is prescribed.
