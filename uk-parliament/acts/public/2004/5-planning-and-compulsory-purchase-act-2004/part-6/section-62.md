# Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (90).** Text as at 2026-11-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/5

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 62. Local development plan

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-45fa60411151e1cbef47c9c1dba27f83] The local planning authority must prepare a plan for their area to be known as a local development plan.

(2)[^c-45fa60411151e1cbef47c9c1dba27f83] The plan must set out—

- (a) the authority’s objectives in relation to the development and use of land in their area;
- (b)[^c-896584651976b1c89f0c0bf46401417e] their general policies for the implementation of those objectives.

(3)[^c-45fa60411151e1cbef47c9c1dba27f83] The plan may also set out specific policies in relation to any part of the area of the authority.

(3A)[^c-0cc63312f0f8893e7dc41328968b77e2] The plan must be in general conformity with—

- (a) the National Development Framework for Wales, and
- (b) the strategic development plan for any [^c-2bc2b5dfaee0ee7eaa8e1d0a631f5fdd]... area that includes all or part of the area of the authority.

(3B)[^c-b3ccaf0335a458a37549d0f0283d9a5d] The plan must specify the period for which it is to have effect.

(4)[^c-9e88c88fc3372e234a17c0b1c742be3c] Regulations under this section may —[^c-23e920bb3eb005a15c684b5510dba574]

- (a) make provision about the period that may be specified under subsection (3B);
- (b) prescribe the form and content of the plan.

(5) In preparing a local development plan the authority must have regard to—

- (a)[^c-45fa60411151e1cbef47c9c1dba27f83] current national policies;
- (b)[^c-45fa60411151e1cbef47c9c1dba27f83] the National Development Framework for Wales;[^c-78204145bfa35881c7ab2260f0c20180]
- (ba) the strategic development plan for any [^c-ec7ddd3c52de3726ceab4d4eb44e1c4f]... area that—
  - (i) includes all or part of the area of the authority, or
  - (ii) adjoins that area;
- (bb)[^c-509490e3bea87777337100d1c94d7d2f] any area statement published under section 11 of the Environment (Wales) Act 2016 for an area that includes all or part of the area of the authority;
- (c)[^c-45fa60411151e1cbef47c9c1dba27f83] the RSS for any region which adjoins the area of the authority;
- (d)[^c-45fa60411151e1cbef47c9c1dba27f83][^c-3a686fbd5f7053e3e9ff42e7b911d991] any relevant local well-being plan[^c-b34d1e7f9b853b66ef7e4bc980195eba];
- (f)[^c-45fa60411151e1cbef47c9c1dba27f83] the resources likely to be available for implementing the plan;
- (g)[^c-9e88c88fc3372e234a17c0b1c742be3c] such other matters as the Assembly prescribes.

(6)[^c-45fa60411151e1cbef47c9c1dba27f83] The authority must also—

- (a) carry out an appraisal of the sustainability of the plan;
- (b) prepare a report of the findings of the appraisal.

(6A)[^c-a929b057f49e4b9506d28be3722ec706] The appraisal must include an assessment of the likely effects of the plan on the use of the Welsh language in the area of the authority.

(7)[^c-45fa60411151e1cbef47c9c1dba27f83][^c-192b2b58d404113909e509c2a93ab1e1] A local well-being plan is relevant if it has been published under section 39 , 44(5) or 47(6) or (11)[^c-3e7e615a75d252786d317bb06b4b89a1] of the Well-being of Future Generations (Wales) Act 2015 (anaw 2) by—

- (a) in the case of an authority which is a county council or county borough council, the public services board of which that authority is a member;
- (b) in the case of an authority which is a National Park Authority, the public services board for an area that includes any part of that authority's area.

(8)[^c-45fa60411151e1cbef47c9c1dba27f83] A plan is a local development plan only in so far as it—

- (a) is adopted by resolution of the local planning authority as a local development plan;
- (b) is approved by the Assembly under section 65 or 71.

(9)[^c-788e7ff923dc80d2f6028049977521de] A plan ceases to be a local development plan on the expiry of the period specified under subsection (3B).
