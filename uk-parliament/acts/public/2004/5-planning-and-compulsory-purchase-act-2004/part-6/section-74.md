## 74. Urban development corporations[^c-688cdff20be41da6eb5d870706e79f0f]

*Extent: E+W*

The Assembly may direct that this Part (except sections 60 to 60C[^c-10b0cfbca1f6301011917f7e483f0e73]) does not apply to the area of an urban development corporation.
