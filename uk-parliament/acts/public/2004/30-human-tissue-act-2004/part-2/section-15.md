# Human Tissue Act 2004, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-09-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/30

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. General functions[^c-e0f75e3cbe8d95d24d73eb37d307102e]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

The Authority shall have the following general functions—

- (a)[^c-10e1f47f3c3db506e3b5335203a78083] maintaining a statement of the general principles which it considers should be followed—
  - (i) in the carrying-on of activities within its remit, and
  - (ii) in the carrying-out of its functions in relation to such activities;
- (b)[^c-062fdc44eb71457bb3aba8601f75909f] providing in relation to activities within its remit such general oversight and guidance as it considers appropriate;
- (c) superintending, in relation to activities within its remit, compliance with—
  - (i) requirements imposed by or under Part 1 or this Part or under the Human Transplantation (Wales) Act 2013 *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-233fcd4c672ceed2c4acb8782ffd2e1b], and
  - (ii)[^c-d801bacffe5e8d410969583e968b6353] codes of practice under this Act;
- (d)[^c-acfdf9c87ffa6d926cf06e2c0d159105] providing to the public, and to persons carrying on activities within its remit, such information and advice as it considers appropriate about the nature and purpose of such activities;
- (e)[^c-fb3353caa81bfadc69decde7cd2fda25] monitoring developments relating to activities within its remit and advising the Secretary of State, the Welsh Ministers *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-01cbd1dcfdf235600847c76befc64980] and the relevant Northern Ireland department on issues relating to such developments;
- (f)[^c-6b6d137c0caa603c9ed03e77497a79c7] advising the Secretary of State, the Welsh Ministers *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-517da086b07fca05acd81f5d0eda8923] or the relevant Northern Ireland department on such other issues relating to activities within its remit as he, the Ministers *(Extent: E+W)* or the department may require.
