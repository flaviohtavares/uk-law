> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-09-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/30
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. The Human Tissue Authority[^c-ab6afb87adad90bc41adacbc4b3ee471]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) There shall be a body corporate to be known as the Human Tissue Authority (referred to in this Act as “**the Authority**”).

(2) Schedule 2 (which makes further provision about the Authority) has effect.
