# Human Tissue Act 2004, section 52

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-09-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/30
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Orders and regulations[^c-e66f13427ecad38c68344eae740f4ad6][^c-6a5eccade643d8887ab8bab3ebfde105][^c-0eb986893d3971aed90e12736272e11c][^c-fc32f868cffe9003c764531dddb4134e][^c-c1c67b1f2a57422edf9ce7714b81d895][^c-1274f4887020a2419869aca23a915df0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any power to make orders or regulations under this Act includes power—

- (a) to make different provision for different cases, and
- (b) to make incidental, supplementary, consequential or transitional provision or savings.

(2) Any power to make orders or regulations under this Act is exercisable by statutory instrument.

(3) A statutory instrument containing an order or regulations under this Act, except sections 1(11), 3(9), *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)*[^c-157243fa230e0823a8b74b953c29d29b] 6, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-19b47584ec574ea20ebb4829a44eb6b2] 6(1), *(Extent: E+W)* *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-19b47584ec574ea20ebb4829a44eb6b2] 7(4), 10(9), 14(4), 16(5), 27(9), 33(3) and (7) [^c-0f37df00ce0afd2427f5eabf007325ef]... and 54(10) and paragraphs 6(2), 12(2) and 13 of Schedule 4, made by the Secretary of State shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(4) No order under section 1(11), 10(9), 14(4), 27(9) or 54(10) or paragraph 13 of Schedule 4, and no regulations under section 3(9), *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)*[^c-8eeb4cbe2f087f40e4f8b0da7213a5ec] 6, 7(4), 16(5) or 33(3) or (7)[^c-cb5bd01dc7e1ef92c467be78fd6668ce] or paragraph 6(2) or 12(2) of Schedule 4, shall be made unless a draft of the statutory instrument containing it, or them, has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

(4A (Extent: E+W))[^c-e762acfdc4085e78c71a169c8579992a] No order under section 27(10) may be made by the Welsh Ministers unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the National Assembly for Wales. *(Extent: E+W)*

*Extent: N.I.*

(4B)[^c-3b9377c82429595a5e3653ad607afb00][^c-a112d1cd3d10345c1facbca1dc719fb7] No regulations may be made under section 3(9A) unless a draft of the regulations has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Northern Ireland Assembly.

(4C (Extent: N.I.)) The power of the Department of Health in Northern Ireland to make regulations under section 3(9A) is exercisable by statutory rule for the purposes of the Statutory Rules (Northern Ireland) Order 1979 and subsection (2) does not apply to that power. *(Extent: N.I.)*

(5) Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply to any power of a court.

(6) Subsections (1) to (3) do not apply to orders under section 58 or 60.

(7) The power under section 14(4) or 16(5)—

- (a) so far as relating to museums in Wales, may only be exercised with the consent of the Welsh Ministers *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-922a207ff8a3f5526d28cb09baea86c9], and
- (b) so far as relating to museums in Northern Ireland, may only be exercised with the consent of the Department of Culture, Arts and Leisure.

(8) The Secretary of State shall consult the Welsh Ministers *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-52f9446a85a33336fd3c5fb59a7bca07] and the relevant Northern Ireland department before acting under any of the following provisions—

- section 1(9)(a) and (11);
- section 4(10)(b);
- section 6 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bd40b21c926a337689d7d05a7ca3b99c] section 6(1) *(Extent: E+W)* *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bd40b21c926a337689d7d05a7ca3b99c];
- section 7(4);
- section 8(4)(d);
- section 10(9);
- section 14(4);
- section 16(3) and (5);
- section 27(9);
- section 33(3) and (7);
- section 34(1);
- [^c-29bb1807caf43fbc2f64be0a98f65f08]...
- section 54(10);
- paragraphs 6(2), 10(b), 12(2) and 13 of Schedule 4;
- paragraph 4(5) of Schedule 5.

(9) Before acting—

- (a) under section 54(10) in order to amend section 54(9) so far as having effect for the purposes of Schedule 4, or
- (b) under paragraph 6(2), 10(b), 12(2) or 13 of Schedule 4,

the Secretary of State shall also consult the Scottish Ministers.

(10) Before acting under any of the following provisions, the Secretary of State shall also consult such other persons as he considers appropriate—
