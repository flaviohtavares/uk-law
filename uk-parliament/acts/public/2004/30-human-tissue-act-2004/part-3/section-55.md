# Human Tissue Act 2004, section 55

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-09-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/30
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Financial provisions[^key-25b7d90ae34a7c63801f25a815f2ec9d]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenditure incurred by the Secretary of State in consequence of this Act, and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable out of money so provided under any other enactment.
