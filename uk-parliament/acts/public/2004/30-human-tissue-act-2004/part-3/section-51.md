# Human Tissue Act 2004, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-09-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/30
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Offences: Northern Ireland[^key-80203394004bfc9c1804eb59bbbaa04b][^key-2b8597af034fd6e7414d39f02e204438][^key-44475ca3f2fa6e4cd4c0e3af86f5c305][^key-80934c129b04dcab2f26af558fe95ef1]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This Act has effect in relation to Northern Ireland with the modifications specified in subsections (2) and (3).

(2) In sections 32(5)(a) and 33(6)(a), for “51 weeks” there is substituted “ 6 months ”.

(3) In section 32(4)(a)(i), for “12 months” there is substituted “ 6 months ”.
