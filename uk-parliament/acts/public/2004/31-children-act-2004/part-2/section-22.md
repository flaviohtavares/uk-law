# Children Act 2004, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/31
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Co-operation and delegation[^c20891181]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Each person or body with functions under any enactment of conducting assessments of children’s services must for the purposes of those assessments co-operate with other persons or bodies with such functions.

(2) A person or body with functions under any enactment of conducting assessments of children’s services may delegate any of those functions to any other person or body with such functions.
