# Children Act 2004, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/31
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Lead member for children’s services

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority[^c20907871] in England must, in making arrangements for the discharge of—

- (a) the functions conferred on or exercisable by the authority specified in section 18(1)(a) and (b), and
- (b) such other functions conferred on or exercisable by the authority as the authority consider appropriate,

designate one of their members as their “lead member for children’s services”.

(2) A local authority in England must have regard to any guidance given to them by the Secretary of State for the purposes of subsection (1).
