# Children Act 2004, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/31
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Inspection of functions under this Part[^c20891501]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-81278803b7a2debc5c097c66f7a30fbf] The Welsh Ministers' functions under Part 8 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (anaw 4) may be exercised as if anything done by a local authority in Wales in the exercise of functions to which this section applies was in the exercise of a social services function of the local authority (within the meaning of that Act).

(2) This section applies to the following functions of a local authority[^c20908141]—

- (a) the authority’s functions under section 25 [^c-d7e4a73b9ffefce2d102b7ae78d38e33]..., except so far as relating to education, training or youth support services (within the meaning of section 123 of the Learning and Skills Act 2000 (c. 21));
- (b) the authority’s functions under section 28;
- (c) any function conferred on the authority under section 29.
