# Children Act 2004, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/31
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Power to establish registration scheme in Wales

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Assembly may by regulations require any person who fosters a child privately in the area of a local authority[^c20907651] in Wales to be registered for private fostering by that authority in accordance with the regulations.

(2) Subsections (2) to (15) of section 45 apply in relation to regulations under this section as they apply in relation to regulations under that section with the substitution for references to a local authority in England of references to a local authority in Wales.

(3) Subsection (16) of that section applies for the purposes of this section.
