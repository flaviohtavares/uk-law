# Children Act 2004, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/31
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Payments to foster parents

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The appropriate person may by order make provision as to the payments to be made—

- (a) by a local authority[^c20903501] in England or Wales or a person exercising functions on its behalf to a local authority foster parent with whom any child is placed by that authority or person under section 22C[^c20903541] of the Children Act 1989 or section 81 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014[^c-d8907dabf9a0e410877d180348c10ee9];
- (b) by a voluntary organisation to any person with whom any child is placed by that organisation under section 59(1)(a) of the Children Act 1989[^c-2f0772be9cf4ea2a16d9d62401dcc234].

(2) In subsection (1)—

- “**appropriate person**” means—
  - (a) the Secretary of State, in relation to a local authority in England;
  - (b) the Assembly, in relation to a local authority in Wales;
- [^c-ae881968016df3f56eefde94ce01697d]...
- “local authority foster parent” has the same meaning as in section 105(1) of the Children Act 1989;[^c-08bb17abd7dc80385db5aafe3ab52da6]
- “voluntary organisation” has the same meaning as in the Children Act 1989.

(3) In section 23(2)(a) of the Children Act 1989, at the end insert . “ (subject to section 49 of the Children Act 2004) ”

(4) In section 59(1)(a) of that Act, at the end insert “ (subject to section 49 of the Children Act 2004) ” .
