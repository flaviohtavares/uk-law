# Children Act 2004, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/31

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 48. Child minding and day care[^c21246771]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 4 (which makes provision amending Part 10A of the Children Act 1989 (c. 41) in relation to child minding and day care) has effect.
