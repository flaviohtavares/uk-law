## 23. The Fund[^key-52ee2c284a14ed9e56b54f147b0e4920]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be a fund maintained under the control and management of the Secretary of State[^key-d509ff9d44388a4f37e16c87930f6031]... and known as the Olympic Lottery Distribution Fund.
