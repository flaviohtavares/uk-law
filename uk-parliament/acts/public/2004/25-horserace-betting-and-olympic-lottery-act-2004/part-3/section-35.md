# Horserace Betting and Olympic Lottery Act 2004, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2014-04-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/25

## 35. Changes in Olympic procedure[^key-8780585687c3f98d424afbddc087a59d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State[^key-e0d4689ee50947c8dbede5159fab6b8e]... may by order make such provision as he thinks necessary or expedient in consequence of a change effected after the passing of this Act in the arrangements made by the International Olympic Committee in relation to the Games of the Olympiad.

(2) An order under subsection (1) may, in particular, amend a provision of—

- (a) this Part, or
- (b) any other enactment.
