## 7. Shadow directors[^c-b4ab909162bcb58b00989074765aa0fe]

*Extent: E+W+S*

While the successor company is wholly owned by the Crown, neither the Secretary of State nor the Treasury shall be treated as a shadow director of the successor company for the purpose of any provision of the Companies Acts (as defined in section 2 of the Companies Act 2006)[^c-db47eb5009103bb715b51d9251a3b4b4].
