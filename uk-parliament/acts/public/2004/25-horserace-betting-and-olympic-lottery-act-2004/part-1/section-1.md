# Horserace Betting and Olympic Lottery Act 2004, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2014-04-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Dissolution of the Tote[^key-4369df1834a59c902a6f75d93c4f25fa]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) On the appointed day the Horserace Totalisator Board shall by virtue of this section cease to exist.

(2)[^key-7c994eb5d9e24614aec32a9e01d3d072][^key-7c994eb5d9e24614aec32a9e01d3d072] In this Part “**the appointed day**” means a day appointed for the purposes of this section by the Secretary of State by order made by statutory instrument.
