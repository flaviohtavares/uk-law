# Horserace Betting and Olympic Lottery Act 2004, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2014-04-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/25

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Commencement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The preceding provisions of this Act shall come into force in accordance with provision made by order of the Secretary of State.

(2) An order under subsection (1)—

- (a) may make provision that has effect generally or only for specified purposes,
- (b) may make different provision for different purposes,
- (c) may include incidental, consequential or transitional provision, and
- (d) shall be made by statutory instrument.

(3) Section 29 shall not be brought into force unless the Secretary of State has made an order under section 22(2).
