# Civil Contingencies Act 2004, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Scotland: consultation[^c2014166]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A Minister of the Crown shall consult the Scottish Ministers before making regulations or an order under this Part in relation to a person or body if or in so far as the person or body exercises functions in relation to Scotland.

(2) The Scottish Ministers shall consult a Minister of the Crown before making regulations or an order under this Part.
