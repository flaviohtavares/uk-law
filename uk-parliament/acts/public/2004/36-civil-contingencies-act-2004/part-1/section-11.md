# Civil Contingencies Act 2004, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Enforcement: Scotland[^c2013043]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any of the following may bring proceedings in the Court of Session in respect of a failure by a person or body listed in Part 2 or 4 of Schedule 1 to comply with section 2(1), 3(3), 4(1) or (8), 5(3), 6(6), 9(4) or 15(7)—

- (a) the Scottish Ministers,
- (b) a person or body listed in Part 2 of Schedule 1, and
- (c) a person or body listed in Part 4 of Schedule 1.

(2) In proceedings under subsection (1) the Court of Session may grant any remedy, or make any order, that it thinks appropriate.
