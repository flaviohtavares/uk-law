# Civil Contingencies Act 2004, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Extent

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act extends to—

- (a) England and Wales,
- (b) Scotland, and
- (c) Northern Ireland.

(2) But where this Act amends or repeals an enactment or a provision of an enactment, the amendment or repeal has the same extent as the enactment or provision.
