# Civil Contingencies Act 2004, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Commencement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c2014724] The preceding provisions of this Act shall come into force in accordance with provision made by a Minister of the Crown by order.

(2)[^c2014730] But the following provisions of this Act shall come into force in accordance with provision made by the Scottish Ministers by order—

- (a) section 1(4) in so far as it relates to the Scottish Ministers,
- (b) sections 2(4) and (6), 3(2), 4(3) and (7), 5(2) and (5), 6(2) and (5), 8, 9(2), 11, 13(2), 14(2), 17(3) and (5), and
- (c) a provision of section 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 13 or 17 in so far as it relates to a provision specified in paragraph (b) above.

(3)[^c2014724][^c2014730] An order under subsection (1) or (2)—

- (a) may make provision generally or for specific purposes only,
- (b) may make different provision for different purposes,
- (c) may make incidental, consequential or transitional provision, and
- (d) shall be made by statutory instrument.
