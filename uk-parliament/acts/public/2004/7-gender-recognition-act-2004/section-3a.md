# Gender Recognition Act 2004, section 3A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/7

## 3A (Extent: E+W+S). Alternative grounds for granting applications (Extent: E+W+S)[^key-15ae595287c97ed3b742ab231f03294d][^key-55a1d11c39d5e089676bc76339d808e0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S)) This section applies to an application under section 1(1)(a) which states that it is an application for a certificate to be granted in accordance with this section. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(2 (Extent: E+W+S)) The Panel must grant the application if satisfied that the applicant complies with the requirements imposed by and under section 3B and meets the conditions in subsections (3) to (6). *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(3 (Extent: E+W+S)) The first condition is that the applicant was a party to a protected marriage or a protected civil partnership on or before the date the application was made. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(4 (Extent: E+W+S)) The second condition is that the applicant— *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) was living in the acquired gender six years before the commencement of section 12 of the Marriage (Same Sex Couples) Act 2013 *(Extent: E+W+S)* (but see subsection (8))[^key-272af79cf1cab769b08d71f37560406b] *(Extent: E+W+S)*, *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) continued to live in the acquired gender until the date the application was made, and *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (c (Extent: E+W+S)) intends to continue to live in the acquired gender until death. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(5 (Extent: E+W+S)) The third condition is that the applicant— *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) has or has had gender dysphoria, or *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) has undergone surgical treatment for the purpose of modifying sexual characteristics. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(6 (Extent: E+W+S)) The fourth condition is that the applicant is ordinarily resident in England, Wales *(Extent: E+W+S)* ,[^key-d21767295298ab53eca3837239661000] *(Extent: E+W+S)* Scotland or Northern Ireland *(Extent: E+W+S)*. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(7 (Extent: E+W+S)) The Panel must reject the application if not required by subsection (2) to grant it. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

*Extent: N.I.*

(8)[^key-29e2ba034e9172073ba0cd2bf4dc30d5] Where the applicant—

- (a) is a party to a protected marriage that is a marriage under the law of Northern Ireland, or
- (b) is a party to a protected civil partnership that is a civil partnership under the law of Northern Ireland, or
- (c) is ordinarily resident in Northern Ireland,

subsection (4)(a) has effect as if for the words after “was living in the acquired gender” there were substituted “on 13 January 2014”. *(Extent: N.I.)*
