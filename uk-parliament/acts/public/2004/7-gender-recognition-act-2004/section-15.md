# Gender Recognition Act 2004, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/7

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Succession etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The fact that a person’s gender has become the acquired gender under this Act does not affect the disposal or devolution of property under a will or other instrument made before the appointed day.
