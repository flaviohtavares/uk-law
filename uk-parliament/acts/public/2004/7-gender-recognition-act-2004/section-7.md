# Gender Recognition Act 2004, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Applications: supplementary

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An application to a Gender Recognition Panel under section 1(1), 4A,[^key-5644363cfd38f285e979156bb07d4093] 4C, *(Extent: S)*[^key-7174cb023a042d75130556ef884836b3] [^key-009a463dac99a66224aacf6bdbd4ee96]... *(Extent: S)* 5(2) , 5A(2)[^c2033154] or 6(1) must be made in a form and manner specified by the Secretary of State after consulting the Scottish Ministers and the Department of Finance and Personnel in Northern Ireland.

(2) The applicant must pay to the Secretary of State a non-refundable fee of an amount prescribed by order made by the Secretary of State unless the application is made in circumstances in which, in accordance with provision made by the order, no fee is payable; and fees of different amounts may be prescribed for different circumstances.
