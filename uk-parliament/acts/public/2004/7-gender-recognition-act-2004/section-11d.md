> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/7

## 11D. Continuity of civil partnership: Scotland[^key-e20d0f667f828df58f51934d510a96ad]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The continuity of a protected Scottish civil partnership is not affected by the issuing of a full gender recognition certificate to either (or both) of the civil partners.[^key-e5bf8934c4f37dc0cc202d4e73465419]
