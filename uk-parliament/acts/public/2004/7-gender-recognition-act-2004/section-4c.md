## 4C. Married person or civil partner with interim certificate: issue of full certificate (Scotland)[^c-437d545d67af51b16eaafe3af4651064][^c-468122cfbe324ad68b6dc8f1951b91fc][^c-46d8366cb9595bf218d0e4a55eaeda9b][^c-1d693a3df99f606a8968ca0190c8c3f9]

*Extent: S*

(1) A Gender Recognition Panel must issue a full gender recognition certificate to a person if, on an application by the person (“[^c-a19b4284adacea09ac7fadcbec4b0902]**the applicant**”), the Panel is satisfied that the conditions set out in subsection (1A) are met.

(1A)[^c-4fbcfd2d891c1e3dde97c5f3a47b8cdb] The conditions referred to in subsection (1) are—

- (a) an interim gender recognition certificate has been issued to the applicant,
- (b) when the interim gender recognition certificate was issued, the applicant and another person (“P”) were the parties to—
  - (i) a protected Scottish marriage, or
  - (ii) a protected Scottish civil partnership,
- (c) the applicant and P are still, or have since become, the parties to—
  - (i) a protected Scottish marriage, or
  - (ii) a protected Scottish civil partnership, and
- (d) P consents to the marriage or civil partnership continuing after the issue of a full gender recognition certificate.

(2)[^c-b8ce40b8549dd5c737c7b622055a25f4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-b8ce40b8549dd5c737c7b622055a25f4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) If, on an application under subsection (1)[^c-2167ccb5c7b122076beca5041aa05bd6], the Panel is not satisfied as mentioned in that subsection, the Panel must reject the application.

(5) An application under subsection (1)[^c-dd30c2b6eb7c7f2bdf6ba3cd5b417322] must be made within the period of six months beginning with the day on which the interim gender recognition certificate is issued.

(6)[^c-56c2229476e2eba13627429def9b794f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) An application under subsection (1)[^c-ac5d5b9d2a5f9752af55fd25eac0fc32] must include a statutory declaration of consent (within the meaning of section 3(6D)(b)(i) or (6G)(b)(i)[^c-30dff85ff9aaf82be7f1e5fa7794be53]) made by the person's spouse or (as the case may be) civil partner[^c-440a6227c3669385ec04f2ab1720889a].

(8)[^c-d7390c2b01184d9dacfd96cfc4086712] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9) If an application is made under this section, the Panel must give the applicant's spouse or (as the case may be) civil partner[^c-6eede6e2784913ed9005389abbe98075]—

- (a) notice of the application, and
- (b) if the Panel grants the application, notice of the issue of the full gender recognition certificate.
