# Gender Recognition Act 2004, section 11B

> Text as at 2026-11-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland; Scotland (the versions of this text differ in extent).

## 11B (Extent: N.I.). Change in gender of civil partner (Extent: N.I.)[^M_E_74ea5e2e-accd-498b-b75b-26337c6504c5][^c-af553a5f986ee99313df1213eb0d5818]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: N.I.)) This section applies in relation to a protected civil partnership if (by virtue of section 4(2)(c) or 4A) a full gender recognition certificate is issued to a party to the partnership. *(Extent: N.I.)*

(2 (Extent: N.I.)) The continuity of the protected civil partnership is not affected by the relevant change in gender. *(Extent: N.I.)*

(3 (Extent: N.I.)) If the protected civil partnership is a protected overseas relationship— *(Extent: N.I.)*

- (a (Extent: N.I.)) the continuity of the civil partnership continues by virtue of subsection (2) notwithstanding any impediment under the relevant law; *(Extent: N.I.)*
- (b (Extent: N.I.)) the relevant law is not affected by the continuation of the civil partnership by virtue of subsection (2). *(Extent: N.I.)*

(4 (Extent: N.I.)) In this section— *(Extent: N.I.)*

- “impediment” means anything which would affect the continuation of the overseas relationship merely by virtue of the relevant change in gender; *(Extent: N.I.)*
- “relevant change in gender” means the change or changes in gender occurring by virtue of the issue of the full gender recognition certificate or certificates; *(Extent: N.I.)*
- “relevant law”, in relation to the protected overseas relationship in question, has the same meaning as in Chapter 2 of Part 5 of the Civil Partnership Act 2004. *(Extent: N.I.)*

## 11B. Change in gender of civil partner[^M_E_f0ca1e49-d390-4d2b-93c7-51196829ddc3][^V_S_cd7d791f-ac5c-01bc-9516-c4d4b95755ef]

*Extent: S*

(1) This section applies in relation to a protected civil partnership if (by virtue of section 4(2)(c) or 4A) a full gender recognition certificate is issued to a party to the partnership.

(2) The continuity of the protected civil partnership is not affected by the relevant change in gender.

(3)[^V_S_5e508ce1-6312-60b9-180f-98f76c4afa0b] If the protected civil partnership is a protected overseas relationship—

- (a) the continuity of the civil partnership continues by virtue of subsection (2) notwithstanding any impediment under the relevant law;
- (b) the relevant law is not affected by the continuation of the civil partnership by virtue of subsection (2).

(4) In this section—

- “**impediment**” means anything which would affect the continuation of the overseas relationship merely by virtue of the relevant change in gender,
- “**relevant change in gender**” means the change or changes in gender occurring by virtue of the issue of the full gender recognition certificate or certificates,
- “**relevant law**”, in relation to the protected overseas relationship in question, has the same meaning as in Chapter 2 of Part 5 of the Civil Partnership Act 2004.
