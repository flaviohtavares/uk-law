# Gender Recognition Act 2004, section 3B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3B (Extent: E+W+S). Evidence for granting applications on alternative grounds (Extent: E+W+S)[^c-3aed8bd230543f14f00f5a0dfca5f6cf][^c-063955e4db6961c415199711ecdd3cec]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S)) This section applies to an application under section 1(1)(a) which states that it is an application for a certificate to be granted in accordance with section 3A. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(2 (Extent: E+W+S)) The application must include either— *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) a report made by a registered medical practitioner, or *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) a report made by a registered psychologist practising in the field of gender dysphoria. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(3 (Extent: E+W+S)) If the application is based on the applicant having or having had gender dysphoria— *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) the reference in subsection (2) to a registered medical practitioner is to one practising in the field of gender dysphoria, and *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) that subsection is not complied with unless the report includes details of the diagnosis of the applicant's gender dysphoria. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(4 (Extent: E+W+S)) Subsection (2) is not complied with in a case where— *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) the applicant has undergone or is undergoing treatment for the purpose of modifying sexual characteristics, or *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) treatment for that purpose has been prescribed or planned for the applicant, *(Extent: E+W+S)*

unless the report required by that subsection includes details of it. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(5 (Extent: E+W+S)) The application must also include a statutory declaration by the applicant that the applicant meets the conditions in section 3A(3) and (4). *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(6 (Extent: E+W+S)) The application must include— *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) a statutory declaration as to whether or not the applicant is married or a civil partner, *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) any other information or evidence required by an order made by the Secretary of State, and *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (c (Extent: E+W+S)) any other information or evidence which the Panel which is to determine the application may require, *(Extent: E+W+S)*

and may include any other information or evidence which the applicant wishes to include. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(7 (Extent: E+W+S)) If the applicant is married, the application must include a statutory declaration as to whether the marriage is a marriage under the law of England and Wales, of Scotland, of Northern Ireland, or of a country or territory outside the United Kingdom. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

*Extent: E+W+S*

(7A)[^c-80bd1b1263d6151f9a2f709d8022856d] If the applicant is a civil partner, the application must include a statutory declaration as to whether the civil partnership is a civil partnership under the law of England and Wales, of Scotland, or of Northern Ireland, or is an overseas relationship that is treated as a civil partnership by virtue of Chapter 2 of Part 5 of the Civil Partnership Act 2004.

(8 (Extent: E+W+S)) If the applicant is married *(Extent: E+W+S)* or a civil partner *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^c-01d8c6c6bf2766ed07a9a5dffca6ae44] *(Extent: E+W+S)*, and the marriage *(Extent: E+W+S)* or civil partnership *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^c-64ee8f6a61af747949751248321f0772] *(Extent: E+W+S)* is a protected marriage *(Extent: E+W+S)* or a protected civil partnership *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^c-2dc585fe1482b203e37ae5d53878e632] *(Extent: E+W+S)*, the application must also include— *(Extent: E+W+S)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+S)) a statutory declaration of consent by the applicant's spouse *(Extent: E+W+S)* or civil partner *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^c-2731bb9fecb5dd6061d1932487e5fde2] *(Extent: E+W+S)* (if the spouse *(Extent: E+W+S)* or partner *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^c-92170544c55e40345aeeb0fa2fb61076] *(Extent: E+W+S)* has made such a declaration), or *(Extent: E+W+S)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+S)) a statutory declaration by the applicant that the applicant's spouse *(Extent: E+W+S)* or civil partner *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^c-e656e845abca52c0ea5d14a0acfd0874] *(Extent: E+W+S)* has not made a statutory declaration of consent (if that is the case). *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(9 (Extent: E+W+S)) If the application includes a statutory declaration of consent by the applicant's spouse *(Extent: E+W+S)* or civil partner *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^c-e65343b2f9043c24c4888bc9913903df] *(Extent: E+W+S)*, the Panel must give the spouse *(Extent: E+W+S)* or partner *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^c-8b8deaabe59cab640285f3f8d82a3bbf] *(Extent: E+W+S)* notice that the application has been made. *(Extent: E+W+S)*

(10 (Extent: E+W+S)) If the Panel which is to determine the application requires information or evidence under subsection (6)(c) it must give reasons for doing so. *(Extent: E+W+S)*
