# Traffic Management Act 2004, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Application of sections 20 to 25 to network management authorities exercising functions jointly[^key-86da321b3eb4a36a537be54e4ad9a25e][^key-47187495dec544a93e786a2d75d18013][^key-ca626fe4cfed112143ddb0545e66df9a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The appropriate national authority may by order made by statutory instrument make provision for the application of sections 20 to 25 (with or without modifications) in cases where to any extent the performance of the duties under sections 16 and 17 is carried out jointly by two or more network management[^key-662a273eca5a39e800b3655f99256e7f] authorities.

(2) A statutory instrument containing an order under this section made by the Secretary of State is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution by either House of Parliament.
