# Traffic Management Act 2004, section 31

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31. Interpretation of Part 2[^key-d8b684646563011a31ac78b49eec9934][^key-69a1145897cbc3ef23ae51b3d7c10fad]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “**appropriate national authority**” means—
  - (a) the Secretary of State, as respects England; and
  - (b) the National Assembly for Wales, as respects Wales;
- “**intervention notice**” means a notice under section 20;
- “**intervention order**” means an order under section 21;
- “**local traffic authority**” means a traffic authority other than the Secretary of State, a strategic highways company[^key-bd2a94dc0d8de07cb4b8c4454199852e] or the National Assembly for Wales;
- “**London authority**” means Transport for London, a London borough Council or the Common Council of the City of London;
- “**Mayor**” means the Mayor of London;
- “**road**” means any length of highway or of any other road to which the public has access, and includes bridges over which a road passes;
- “**road network**”, in relation to a strategic highways company or[^key-7c79cf9985ab5b03dc15dac64233db58] a local traffic authority, means the network of roads for which the company or[^key-523d4242e1f3b1d516aa11c6c59144e6] the authority is the traffic authority;
- “**traffic**” includes pedestrians;
- “**traffic authority**” has the same meaning as in the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 (c. 27);
- “[^key-d8c11dc4a3d432635d771de901074287] **network management authority** ” has the meaning given in section 16(1);
- “**network management duty**” has the meaning given in section 16(3).
