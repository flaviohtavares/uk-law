# Traffic Management Act 2004, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. Uniform[^key-1552b75336f86be43c1caf6100303339][^key-95adb5c26ec3d007cc039ad62f31e2cf]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-c73b90c6d4595dfb18e85c0d1bd17d12] The appropriate national authority shall determine the uniform for traffic officers designated by, or under an authorisation given by, that authority.

(2)[^key-9f1c9680e165f00b576e62101620a7dd] The Secretary of State may delegate his or her function under subsection (1) to a strategic highways company.

(3) A delegation under subsection (2) may specify—

- (a) the extent to which the function is delegated;
- (b) any conditions to which the delegation is subject.
