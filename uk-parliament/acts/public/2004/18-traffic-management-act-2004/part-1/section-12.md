# Traffic Management Act 2004, section 12

## 12. Power to charge for traffic officer services provided on request[^c-64e9e52ff61da9fdb7db18c6b5afc12c][^c-6860e6faf6c7614520ca5dab28784756]

*Extent: E+W*

The appropriate national authority or, as respects England, a strategic highways company[^c-a997d741597324d1a3a17bce96bfe50a] may, at the request of any person, agree to arrange for the services of a traffic officer to be provided to that person subject to the payment of a charge.
