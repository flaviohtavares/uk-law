# Traffic Management Act 2004, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-09-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 51. Restriction on works following substantial road works

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-0670b453b47e320ebe81f74402c203da][^M_I_f1e060e4-a42d-4493-bfb8-033b27cb9d47] Section 58 of the 1991 Act (restriction on works following substantial road works) is amended as specified in subsections (2) to (8).

(2)[^c-0670b453b47e320ebe81f74402c203da][^M_I_f1e060e4-a42d-4493-bfb8-033b27cb9d47] In subsection (1), for the words “twelve months” there is substituted “ prescribed period ”.

(3)[^c-0670b453b47e320ebe81f74402c203da][^M_I_f1e060e4-a42d-4493-bfb8-033b27cb9d47] In subsection (2), after “prescribed” there is inserted “ form and ” and for “three months” there is substituted “ such period as may be prescribed ”.

(4)[^c-d1cd826d25eda19deaa563a8dae39ee3][^c-1b036b6e8c666f69841b501d4de3d3b4] In subsection (3) after paragraph (e) there is inserted

> and
>
> - (f) any other person of a prescribed description;

(and the word “and” after paragraph (d) is omitted).

(5)[^c-0670b453b47e320ebe81f74402c203da][^M_I_f1e060e4-a42d-4493-bfb8-033b27cb9d47] In subsection (4), for paragraphs (a) and (b) there is substituted “ within such period as may be prescribed ”.

(6)[^c-e291e47c62f9da848f18f49b6de665b6][^c-0c6f2ac3e54b81a63a5c64ec117106aa] In subsection (6), at the beginning of paragraph (b) there is inserted “ if he is convicted of an offence under this subsection ”.

(7)[^c-0670b453b47e320ebe81f74402c203da][^M_I_f1e060e4-a42d-4493-bfb8-033b27cb9d47] In subsection (7), for “by arbitration” there is substituted “ in the prescribed manner ”.

(8)[^c-0670b453b47e320ebe81f74402c203da][^M_I_f1e060e4-a42d-4493-bfb8-033b27cb9d47] After that subsection there is inserted—

> (7A) Regulations under subsection (7) may in particular make provision for the question referred to in that subsection to be settled—
>
> - (a) by arbitration;
> - (b) by a person designated by the Secretary of State on appeal by the undertaker.

(9)[^c-0670b453b47e320ebe81f74402c203da][^M_I_f1e060e4-a42d-4493-bfb8-033b27cb9d47] In section 55 of the 1991 Act (notice of starting date of works), in subsection (2), after “works,” there is inserted “ or in cases where the undertaker has been given notice under section 58(1), ”.
