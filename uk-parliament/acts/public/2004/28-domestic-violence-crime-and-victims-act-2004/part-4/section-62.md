# Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (171).** Text as at 2026-09-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/28
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Extent

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the following provisions of this section, Parts 1 to 3 extend to England and Wales only.

(2) The following provisions extend also to Northern Ireland—

- section 5;
- section 9;
- sections 17 to 21;
- Schedule 1;
- section 56;

(3) The following provisions extend to Northern Ireland only—

- section 7;
- section 10(2);
- section 13;
- section 23;
- section 46.

(4)[^c21160871] Nothing in subsection (1) affects the extent of section 8 or of any provision of section 6 or 6A[^c-f4ad3be870f59b9edef406e17633c9ae] as applied by section 8.

(5) An amendment, repeal or revocation in Schedule 3, 7, 8, 10 or 11 has the same extent as the provision to which it relates.
