# Domestic Violence, Crime and Victims Act 2004, section 21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (171).** Text as at 2026-09-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2004/28

## 21. Application of sections 17 to 20 to Northern Ireland

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) In their application to Northern Ireland, sections 17 to 20 have effect subject to the modifications in Schedule 1.

(2)[^c21080321] Sections 17 to 20 do not apply in relation to a trial to which section 5 of the Justice and Security (Northern Ireland) Act 2007 (trials on indictment without a jury) applies.
