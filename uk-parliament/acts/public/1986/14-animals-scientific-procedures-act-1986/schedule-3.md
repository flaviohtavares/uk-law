## SCHEDULE 3 — CONSEQUENTIAL AMENDMENTS

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 27(2).

**1.**[^key-a91c725250aa837de98cb6d0998b81b8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**2.**[^key-11c068827631766c9fc9de0447b8df89] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**3.** In paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the [^c1016591]Protection of Animals (Anaesthetics) Act 1954 for the words “Any experiment duly authorised under the Cruelty to Animals Act 1876” there shall be substituted the words “Any procedure duly authorised under the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986”.

**4.** In section 12 of the [^c1016592]Pests Act 1954 for the words “any experiment duly authorised under the Cruelty to Animals Act 1876” there shall be substituted the words “any procedure duly authorised under the Animals (Scoentific Procedures) Act 1986”.

**5.** In section 19(4)(a) of the [^c1016593]Veterinary Surgeons Act 1966 for the words “any experiment duly authorised under the Cruelty to Animals Act 1876” there shall be substituted the words “any procedure duly authorised under the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986”.

**6.** In section 1(2A)(b) of the [^c1016594]Slaughter of Poultry Act 1967 for the words “an experiment in respect of which restrictions are imposed by the Cruelty to Animals Act 1876, being an experiment performed subject to any restrictions so imposed” there shall be substituted the words “a procedure duly authorised under the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986”.

**7.**[^key-a53b0952f050d3c51df1b6125f09fe61][^M_F_18350fd5-7de1-4638-c210-0ed31950a167] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**8.**[^key-5f4d7584ad616854c4e82eb00a157a2c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**9.**[^c1016598] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**10.** In section 5(4) of the [^c1016599]Dangerous Wild Animals Act 1976 for the words “registered pursuant to the Cruelty to Animals Act 1876 for the purpose of performing experiments” there shall be substituted the words “which is a designated establishment within the meaning of the Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986”.
