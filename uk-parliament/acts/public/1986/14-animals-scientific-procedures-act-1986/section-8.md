# Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-06-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/14

## 8. Fees.[^c1016495]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The holder of a section 2C licence[^c-43efd6392777429bc498306cd527e40c] shall pay such periodical fees to the Secretary of State as may be prescribed by or determined in accordance with an order made by him.
