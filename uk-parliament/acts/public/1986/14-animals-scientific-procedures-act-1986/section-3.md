# Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-06-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Prohibition of unlicensed procedures.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No person shall personally[^key-843958526a58075a62adf492f6bbecb6] apply a regulated procedure to an animal unless—

- (a) he holds a personal licence qualifying him to apply a regulated procedure of that description to an animal of that description;
- (b) the procedure is applied as part of a programme of work specified in a project licence authorising the application, as part of that programme, of a regulated procedure of that description to an animal of that description; and
- (c) the place where the procedure is carried out is a place specified in [^key-99d81f3d317837b2e056f9697305a32f]... the project licence.
