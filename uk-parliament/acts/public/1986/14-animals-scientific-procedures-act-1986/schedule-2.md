# Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986, SCHEDULE 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-06-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/14

## SCHEDULE 2 — ANIMALS THAT ARE “RELEVANT PROTECTED ANIMALS” FOR THE PURPOSES OF SECTION 2B[^key-dd3fd1636d23135aa36d6f6a1c3f4cfc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 2B[^key-13c0cdfdc31411fb0dc5cd30c16dc821] .

- Any mouse of the species[^key-9ee0fadce1ad5c411e4f478440452de4] *Mus musculus*
- Any rat of the species[^key-773b7fda6288ce68fc0edf4baac8a2ac] *Rattus norvegicus*
- Guinea-pig
- Any hamster of the species[^key-e4f5c33a8d5a8daa17b1b53429b97ecc] *Mesocricetus auratus* or *Cricetulus griseus*
- Any rabbit of the species[^key-22feffd341b926ed317c516c5b087a45] *Oryctolagus cuniculus*
- Dog
- Cat
- Primate
- Any bird of the species Coturnix coturnix (quail).[^c1016582]
- Pig, if genetically modified[^c1016583]
- Sheep, if genetically modified
- Ferret
- Any gerbil of the species[^key-ac77bb2384b9a8f158474e8e3bae06c3] *Meriones unguiculatus*
- Any frog of the species[^key-68d35ab33d09583662a594bd8018cb3f] *Xenopus laevis, Xenopus tropicalis, Rana temporaria* or *Rana pipiens*
- Zebra fish
