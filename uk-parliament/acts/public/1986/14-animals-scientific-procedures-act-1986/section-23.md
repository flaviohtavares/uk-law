# Animals (Scientific Procedures) Act 1986, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-06-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. False statements.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person is guilty of an offence if for the purpose of obtaining or assisting another person to obtain a licence [^c-0f373c01d330fa9b453316cefacc1e14]... under this Act he furnishes information which he knows to be false or misleading in a material particular or recklessly furnishes information which is false or misleading in a material particular.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable on summary conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or to a fine not exceeding the fourth level on the standard scale or to both.
