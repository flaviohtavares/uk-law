## 1. Functions of the Financial Conduct Authority and the Prudential Regulation Authority in relation to building societies.[^c15489201][^key-7240ed867a85a3505d7618acbc0ee8d6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The[^key-d57ea20465adbc64d007315e516b2e09] FCA has the following functions under this Act in relation to building socities—

- (a)[^key-39be99bd5e935d457fc6a7f59fcd5ef5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) to administer the system of regulation of building societies provided for by or under this Act; and
- (c) to advise and make recommendations to the Treasury and other government departments on any matter relating to building societies.

(1A)[^key-9d25a9727cd095017c97479871114d02] The PRA has the following functions under this Act in relation to building societies—

- (a) to secure that the principal purpose of building societies remains that of making loans which are secured on residential property and are funded substantially by their members;
- (b) to administer the system of regulation of building societies provided by or under this Act, but only in so far as sections 5, 6, 7 and 9A[^key-15a8d244b77b2fcaf8e98c757e144ece] confer functions on the PRA; and
- (c) to advise and make recommendations to the Treasury and other government departments on any matter relating to building societies.

(2) The FCA[^key-8820ebbab7f412944f1b56fc9066ea95] and the PRA also have , in relation to such societies, the other functions conferred on them respectively[^key-c4485065909fd7ba17351d895d8f2d22] by or under this Act or any other enactment.
