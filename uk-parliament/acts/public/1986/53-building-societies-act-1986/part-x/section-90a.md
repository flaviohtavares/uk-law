# Building Societies Act 1986, section 90A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2025-01-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/53
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90A. Application of other companies insolvency legislation to building societies.[^c-9c0a3777666f7c9afa2e8fc232f6fc61][^c15513831]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purpose of—

- (a) enabling voluntary arrangements to be approved in relation to building societies,
- (b) enabling administration orders to be made in relation to building societies, and
- (c) making provision with respect to persons appointed in England and Wales , Scotland[^c-c22325a304c442c08228c11206b2f801] or Northern Ireland as receivers and managers , or receivers,[^c-c5c3163fad07b1903e1b71cf26dbaf07] of building societies’ property,

the enactments specified in paragraph 1(2) of Schedule 15A to this Act shall apply in relation to building societies with the modifications specified in that Schedule.
