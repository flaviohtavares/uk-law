# Building Societies Act 1986, section 86

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2025-01-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/53
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Modes of dissolution and winding up.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A building society—

- (a) may be dissolved by consent of the members, or
- (b) may be wound up voluntarily or by the court,

in accordance with this Part; and a building society may not, except where it is dissolved by virtue of section 93(5), 94(10) or 97(9), or following building society insolvency or building society special administration,[^key-32e1a37e7d4653aab4c3856e63b16c45] be dissolved or wound up in any other manner.

(2) A building society which is in the course of dissolution by consent, or is being wound up voluntarily, may be wound up by the court.
