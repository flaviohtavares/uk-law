# Building Societies Act 1986, section 116

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2025-01-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/53
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116. Form of documents.[^c15517721]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The FCA[^c-c99a9bbe5bb22bd4e9179efb82ffa3f0] and the PRA may each , by directions under this section, make provision with respect to the form of, and the particulars to be included in, any document to be sent to it under this Act.

(1A)[^c-809c2f7dfbbcc3792d5bec738958cc48] The PRA and the FCA must each consult the other before issuing a direction under this section if the document in question is required to be sent to both of them.

(2) The directions have effect subject to any other provision of or made under this Act.
