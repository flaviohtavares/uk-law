# Building Societies Act 1986, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2025-01-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/53
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Directors: number.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Every building society shall have at at least two directors.

(2) One of the directors shall be appointed to be chairman of the board of directors.
