# Building Societies Act 1986, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2025-01-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/53
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Assistance by building societies to other building societies.[^c15498731]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where it appears to the Commission that a building society is in financial difficulties it may authorise a building society to lend money to that society, and a building society shall have power to do so accordingly.
