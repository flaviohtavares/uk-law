# Building Societies Act 1986, section 78A

## 78A. Signature of auditors' report[^key-530136c5924e87c3024583fd2151b6f3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The auditor’s report must state the name of the auditor (or, where more than one person is appointed as auditor, all of their names)[^key-bd173e20a02f5b3bca358955c45783c3] and be signed and dated.

(2) Where the auditor is an individual, the report must be signed by him.

(2A)[^key-b031afa1e2902a4bc9c74090be23d80d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Where the auditor is a firm, the report must be signed by the senior statutory auditor in his own name, for and on behalf of the auditor.

(4)[^key-9ea00907e195fd5ff2b321aa728ba518] Where more than one person is appointed as auditor, the report must be signed by all those appointed.
