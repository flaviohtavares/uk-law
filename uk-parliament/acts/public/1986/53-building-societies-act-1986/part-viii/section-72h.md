# Building Societies Act 1986, section 72H

## 72H. IAS group accounts[^key-a6611927ea19a53b670c8dc7c87f81b2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the directors of a building society prepare IAS group accounts, they must state in the notes to those accounts that the accounts have been prepared in accordance with UK-adopted international accounting standards[^key-a439b27c225baf77d98d6ae5c92cea96].
