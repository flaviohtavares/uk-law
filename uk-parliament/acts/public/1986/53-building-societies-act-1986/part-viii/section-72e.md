# Building Societies Act 1986, section 72E

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72E. Duty to prepare group accounts[^c-a6611927ea19a53b670c8dc7c87f81b2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If at the end of a financial year a building society has subsidiary undertakings, the directors, as well as preparing individual accounts for the year, shall prepare accounts for the year for the society and those undertakings taken as a whole.Those accounts are referred to in this Part as the society’s “group accounts”.

(2) The group accounts of certain societies are required by section 403(1) of the Companies Act 2006[^c-979d6d881600deb3f3a300810ae7f72c] to be prepared in accordance with UK-adopted international accounting standards[^c-8d944f3e06ee3d086173bc0e51e84a43] (“IAS group accounts”).

(3) The group accounts of other societies may be prepared–

- (a) in accordance with section 72F (“Building Societies Act group accounts”), or
- (b) in accordance with UK-adopted international accounting standards[^c-d8f251838dd9f59fccb8519c1ff86272] (“IAS group accounts”).

This subsection is subject to the following provisions of this section.

(4) After the first financial year in which the directors of a building society prepare IAS group accounts (“the first IAS year”), all subsequent group accounts of the society must be prepared in accordance with UK-adopted international accounting standards[^c-915db4fbcb896c3847462d3757d83afb] unless there is a relevant change of circumstance.

(5) There is a relevant change of circumstance if, at any time during or after the first IAS year, the society ceases to have any securities admitted to trading on a UK[^c-efe82e7bf4b5883624fd547e08c3fa0c] regulated market.

(6) If, having changed to preparing Building Societies Act group accounts following a relevant change of circumstance, the directors again prepare IAS group accounts for the society, subsection (4) and (5) apply again as if the first financial year for which such accounts are again prepared were the first IAS year.
