# Family Law Act 1986, section 61

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2022-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 61. Abolition of right to petition for jactitation of marriage.

*Extent: E+W*

No person shall after the commencement of this Part be entitled to petition the High Court or a county court for jactitation of marriage.
