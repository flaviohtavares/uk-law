# Family Law Act 1986, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2022-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/55

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Jurisdiction in independent proceedings.

*Extent: S*

A court in Scotland may entertain an application for a Part I order[^c13382781] otherwise than in matrimonial or civil partnership[^c-5337733a50bd1b3b829c944fc10120ce] proceedings only if it has jurisdiction under section 9, 10, 12 or 15(2) of this Act.
