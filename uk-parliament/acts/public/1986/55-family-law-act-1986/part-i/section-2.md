# Family Law Act 1986, section 2

## 2. Jurisdiction: general.[^key-e857a87336e46b03217c51559e728baf][^c13381741]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A court in England and Wales shall not make a section 1(1)(a) order with respect to a child unless—

- (a) it has jurisdiction under [^key-aea9fa48abb143c894e4e555994b619d]... the Hague Convention[^key-e60534e9caf100044daa6896c7584f77] , or
- (b) the Hague Convention does not apply[^key-4704d964ea563c400a22edf77beca92a] but—
  - (i) the question of making the order arises in or in connection with matrimonial proceedings or civil partnership proceedings[^key-ba26215db9cfca5712b5fbefdb58b2e2] and the condition in section 2A of this Act is satisfied, or
  - (ii) the condition in section 3 of this Act is satisfied.

(2A)[^key-f990ad6a78f3bf101d321bceb2651367] A court in England and Wales shall not have jurisdiction to make a special guardianship order under the Children Act 1989 unless the condition in section 3 of this Act is satisfied.

(2B) A court in England and Wales shall not have jurisdiction to make an order under section 26 of the Adoption and Children Act 2002 unless the condition in section 3 of this Act is satisfied.

(2C)[^key-4b7baaa0782b96d886e2c32c540d8341] A court in England and Wales shall not have jurisdiction to make an order under section 51A of the Adoption and Children Act 2002 unless—

- (a) it has jurisdiction under [^key-0333abde6608e11ee7ad4c620ca48a5a]... the Hague Convention, or
- (b) the Hague Convention does not apply[^key-75ba80412b161622c1e493963074bc71] but the condition in section 3 of this Act is satisfied.

(3)[^key-164ca108256280ead080de56d219873c] A court in England and Wales shall not make a section 1(1)(d) order unless—

- (a) it has jurisdiction under [^key-5b728958c65878f066a21e0c2e361151]... the Hague Convention[^key-d1c3607c5c009edc548b383605ca000d] , or
- (b) the Hague Convention does not apply[^key-240ac82b751c1d35cb5c7a2b4e7373a9] but—
  - (i) the condition in section 3 of this Act is satisfied, or
  - (ii) the child concerned is present in England and Wales on the relevant date and the court considers that the immediate exercise of its powers is necessary for his protection.
