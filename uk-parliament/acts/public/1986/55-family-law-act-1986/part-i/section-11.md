# Family Law Act 1986, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2022-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Provisions supplementary to sections 9 and 10.

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to subsection (2) below, the jurisdiction of the court to entertain an application for a Part I order[^c13382971] with respect to a child by virtue of section 9, 10 or 15(2) of this Act is excluded if, on the date of the application, matrimonial or civil partnership[^c-d986139d6859f88f450b01ce06582686] proceedings are continuing in a court in any part of the United Kingdom in respect of the marriage or civil partnership[^c-0578aad3f52f65a35f9baa8999450651] of the parents of the child.

(2) Subsection (1) above shall not apply in relation to an application for a Part I order if the court in which the matrimonial or civil partnership[^c-fe18afabf5fededadbfab42b47eedb57] proceedings are continuing has made one of the following orders, that is to say—

- (a) an order under section 2A(4)[^c13382991], 13(6) or 19A(4)[^c13383001] of this Act (not being an order made by virtue of section 13(6)(a)(ii)); or
- (b) an order under section 5(2), 14(2) or 22(2) of this Act which is recorded as made for the purpose of enabling Part I proceedings with respect to[^c13383011] the child concerned to be taken in Scotland or, as the case may be, in another court in Scotland,

and that order is in force.
