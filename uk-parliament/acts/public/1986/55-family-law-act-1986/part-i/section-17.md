# Family Law Act 1986, section 17

> Extent: Scotland.

## 17. Orders for delivery of child.

*Extent: S*

(1) [^c13384231]. . ., an application by one parent of a child for an order for the delivery of the child from the other parent, where the order is not sought to implement a Part I order[^c13384241], may be entertained by the Court of Session or a sheriff if, but only if, the Court of Session or, as the case may be, the sheriff would have jurisdiction under this Chapter to make a Part I order with respect to the child concerned.

(2)[^c13384261] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Subsection (1) above shall apply to an application by one party to a marriage for an order for the delivery of the child concerned from the other party where the child , although not a child of both parties to the marriage, is a child of the family of those parties[^c13384271] as it applies to an application by one parent of a child for an order for the delivery of the child from the other parent.

(4)[^c13384281] In subsection (3) above, “**child of the family**” means any child who has been treated by both parties as a child of their family, except a child who has been placed with those parties as foster parents by a local authority or a voluntary organisation.
