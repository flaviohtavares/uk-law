# Family Law Act 1986, section 19

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Jurisdiction: general.[^c-a6720599534adae9f4fbddae578fca0e][^c13384461]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A court in Northern Ireland shall not make a section 1(1)(c) order with respect to a child unless—

- (a) it has jurisdiction under [^c-8e0211ab5be8fe0778ffdcec6beea444]... the Hague Convention[^c-0fe6533f618aa09e3eaf77a084ed7eec] , or
- (b) the Hague Convention does not apply[^c-967ebdfe06cb31da0d459b0dda1bcea1] but—
  - (i) the question of making the order arises in or in connection with matrimonial proceedings or civil partnership proceedings[^c-d6e1b76a72907c8a7f5d977ecda457cd] and the condition in section 19A of this Act is satisfied, or
  - (ii) the condition in section 20 of this Act is satisfied.

(3)[^c-cc10e5e041db4800cf31176f3b609645] A court in Northern Ireland shall not make a section 1(1)(e) order unless—

- (a) it has jurisdiction under [^c-0b829f8a1dcd6569606af485e36f6b16]... the Hague Convention[^c-df9f5d810630cbb7ddc8d04c536dbdf5] , or
- (b) the Hague Convention does not apply[^c-55137ef50712b3c1d9f8c467641247d0] but—
  - (i) the condition in section 20 of this Act is satisfied, or
  - (ii) the child concerned is present in Northern Ireland on the relevant date and the court considers that the immediate exercise of its powers is necessary for his protection.
