# Family Law Act 1986, section 54

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2022-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Interpretation of Part II.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part—

- “**annulment**” includes any nullity of marriage order or[^c-c9d98a322ab4af200291d7cc13fbb0de] decree or declarator of nullity of marriage, however expressed;
- [^c-bc74f2f8837db5b4c972cd8522d8cde7]...
- “**part of the United Kingdom**” means England and Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland;
- “**proceedings**” means judicial or other proceedings.

(2) In this Part “**country**” includes a colony or other dependent territory of the United Kingdom but for the purposes of this Part a person shall be treated as a national of such a territory only if it has a law of citizenship or nationality separate from that of the United Kingdom and he is a citizen or national of that territory under that law.
