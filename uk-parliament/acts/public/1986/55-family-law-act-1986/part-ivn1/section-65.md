# Family Law Act 1986, section 65

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2022-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 65. Amendments of Child Abduction Act 1984.

*Extent: E+W*

In section 1(2)(b), (3)(a) and (5) of the [^c13387871]Child Abduction Act 1984 (offence of abduction of child by parent etc.), for the words “a court in England and Wales” there shall be substituted the words “a court in the United Kingdom”.
