## 278. Commencement and continuance.[^c15577451]

*Extent: E+W*

The bankruptcy of an individual against whom a bankruptcy order has been made—

- (a) commences with the day on which the order is made, and
- (b) continues until the individual is discharged under [^M_F_2563c3c3-5ecf-469d-f1c5-71f2d175389f]... this Chapter.
