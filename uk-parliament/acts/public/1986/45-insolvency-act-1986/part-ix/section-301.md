# Insolvency Act 1986, section 301

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 301. Creditors’ committee.[^M_C_27fcd6a1-1b01-40ba-f131-dcdc24145780][^c15579141][^c15579151]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject as follows, a bankrupt's creditors[^c-de47cdea1a7137ac5a0a12a0b6175c5b] may, in accordance with the rules, establish a committee (known as “**the creditors’ committee**”) to exercise the functions conferred on it by or under this Act.

(2) The[^c-08dcf588cdecf9cb9185bbecebfe73af] bankrupt’s creditors shall not establish such a committee, or confer any functions on such a committee, at any time when the official receiver is the trustee of the bankrupt’s estate, except in connection with the appointment[^c-60d56770e7252dd9f77219e10e86bd39] of a person to be trustee instead of the official receiver.
