## 4A. Approval of arrangement.[^c-5cd9c8460d5cf34b54cb12e626387718][^c-9700d312d985436a858eae9798d49154][^c15568921]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This section applies to a decision, under section 4, with respect to the approval of a proposed voluntary arrangement.

(2)[^c15568931] The decision has effect if, in accordance with the rules—

- (a) it has been taken by the meeting of the company summoned under section 3 and by the company's creditors pursuant to that section[^c-d8e402eac8fa81974b2816e75719a132], or
- (b) (subject to any order made under subsection (6)[^c-cccd6960719f9e2d1b9ef97db1307c14]) it has been taken by the company's creditors pursuant to[^c-ce2109130a0919a8692f98b6500d209d] that section.

(3) If the decision taken by the company's creditors[^c-6494d7b619f06dd783db187c69296cd1] differs from that taken by the company meeting, a member of the company may apply to the court.

(4) An application under subsection (3) shall not be made after the end of the period of 28 days beginning with—

- (a) the day on which the decision was taken by the company's creditors[^c-6f6d08d13a9f7e959c70c71f5cbfb736], or
- (b) where the decision of the company meeting was taken on a later day, that day.

(5) Where a member of a regulated company, as defined by section A49(13)[^c-58c028409b93be16738f2627ffd4e9a6], applies to the court under subsection (3), the appropriate regulator[^c-e524f17c439fd475b55e93681a01424e] is entitled to be heard on the application.

(5A)[^c-abf5bd7c1bfe07880cc2f7f163a7159a] The appropriate regulator” means—

- (a) where the regulated company is a PRA-regulated company as defined by section A49(13)[^c-8680a9d832deb2c2b5c92188eef1cf7a], the Financial Conduct Authority and the Prudential Regulation Authority, and
- (b) in any other case, the Financial Conduct Authority.

(6) On an application under subsection (3), the court may—

- (a) order the decision of the company meeting to have effect instead of the decision of the company's creditors[^c-11f9cb46155cd49ce2942e920109d1a8], or
- (b) make such other order as it thinks fit.
