> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/45

## 7B. Arrangements coming to an end prematurely.[^c15569391][^c-9700d312d985436a858eae9798d49154]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of this Part, a voluntary arrangement the approval of which has taken effect under section 4A [^c-5d4c302723f1ac38c40d49de66ff0034]... comes to an end prematurely if, when it ceases to have effect, it has not been fully implemented in respect of all persons bound by the arrangement by virtue of section 5(2)(b)(i) [^c-e1cee83713db1be6c5cf372441a6bfa1]....
