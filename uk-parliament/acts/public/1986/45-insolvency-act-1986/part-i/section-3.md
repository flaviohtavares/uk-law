# Insolvency Act 1986, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/45
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3. Consideration of proposal.[^c-5cd9c8460d5cf34b54cb12e626387718][^c-3d90f2e67a92ba04ddc9a2172601063f][^c-9700d312d985436a858eae9798d49154][^c-4ef5ac73646863fda9b67b2bb212dd3d]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where the nominee under section 1 is not the liquidator or administrator, and it has been report to the court under section 2(2) that the proposal should be considered by a meeting of the company and by the company's creditors[^c-8225363335e9cfdc1e5e1dcb46f241d9], the person making the report shall (unless the court otherwise directs)—[^c-0915ef76687199fca74cecf42c8b9b4c]

- (a) summon a meeting of the company to consider the proposal for the time, date and place proposed in the report, and
- (b) seek a decision from the company's creditors as to whether they approve the proposal.

(2) Where the nominee is the liquidator or administrator, he shall—[^c-b43744edb8cb1a38db6ad9527d7f5657]

- (a) summon a meeting of the company to consider the proposal for such time, date and place as he thinks fit, and
- (b) seek a decision from the company's creditors as to whether they approve the proposal.

(3)[^c-0640aeab7221e32e1cbb8342566ab61b] A decision of the company's creditors as to whether they approve the proposal is to be made by a qualifying decision procedure.

(4) Notice of the qualifying decision procedure must be given to every creditor of the company of whose claim and address the person seeking the decision is aware.
