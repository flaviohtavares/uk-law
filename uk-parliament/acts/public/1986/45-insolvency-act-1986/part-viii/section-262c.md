# Insolvency Act 1986, section 262C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/45
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 262C. Arrangements coming to an end prematurely.[^c15576821]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of this Part, a voluntary arrangement approved by a decision of the debtor's creditors pursuant to[^key-fa9a22e39101795f48bb399c5dc80445] section 257 comes to an end prematurely if, when it ceases to have effect, it has not been fully implemented in respect of all persons bound by the arrangement by virtue of section 260(2)(b)(i).
