## 389. Acting without qualification an offence.[^c20438451][^c20438441][^c-e1e3f5ef388f59b096db6d4dfc6ba322][^c-bce653da65b9d1b38f5bf863f469b8e9][^c-27f6a4b363f4fc9f98dc9f53993285e5][^c-67e84b3fde1253471739882cade02486][^c-53e23e5032b55114129bfd4243322aef][^c15583101]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person who acts as an insolvency practitioner in relation to a company or an individual at a time when he is not qualified to do so is liable to imprisonment or a fine, or to both.

(1A)[^c-a9d8675e626adbbf51f05bd491c67e17] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) This section does not apply to the official receiver or the Accountant in Bankruptcy (within the meaning of the Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act[^c15583121] 2016[^c-14691735916cfef08718a2fb8c73ff31])..
