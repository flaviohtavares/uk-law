# Insolvency Act 1986, section 390

## 390. Persons not qualified to act as insolvency practitioners.[^c15583401][^M_C_8a8cc3d8-991e-42ea-9f7f-3abbe555eefd][^M_C_6b28925b-0c0a-492c-c5b7-47d54ee7ce08][^c-29ee3e41a4dc165dced7cfe69b51e3c7][^c-27f6a4b363f4fc9f98dc9f53993285e5][^c-67e84b3fde1253471739882cade02486][^c-53e23e5032b55114129bfd4243322aef]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person who is not an individual is not qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner.

(2)[^c-ffe884e7a62a271a2636c1234a3e104e] A person is not qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner at any time unless at that time the person is appropriately authorised under section 390A.

(3)[^c15583411] A person is not qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner in relation to another person at any time unless—

- (a) there is in force at that time security or, in Scotland, caution for the proper performance of his functions, and
- (b) that security or caution meets the prescribed requirements with respect to his so acting in relation to that other person.

(4) A person is not qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner at any time if at that time—

- (a) he has been made[^c-048494448cde29a86e1c7fac72daabf0] bankrupt under this Act or the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^c-151c3697358aa18fc0587f432d390070] or sequestration of his estate has been awarded and (in either case) he has not been discharged,
- (aa)[^c19758101] a moratorium period under a debt relief order under this Act or the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989[^c-82e9be50fafb325809c5b4f77aa58033] applies in relation of him,
- (b)[^c20453851] he is subject to a disqualification order made or a disqualification undertaking accepted under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986 or the Company Directors Disqualification (Northern Ireland) Order 2002,
- (c) he is a patient within the meaning of [^c18984971]. . . section 329(1) of the Mental Health (Care and Treatment)(Scotland) Act 2003[^c18976801] or has had a guardian appointed to him under the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000 (asp 4).[^c15583471], or[^c18984941]
- (d) he lacks capacity (within the meaning of the Mental Capacity Act 2005) to act as an insolvency practitioner.

(5)[^c-f94755d710dc6c493797a1ccf5407e67] A person is not qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner while there is in force in respect of that person—

- (a) a bankruptcy restrictions order under this Act, the Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 1985 or the Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 2016[^c-a56d7f0395dde02058cf0fe3cae6cd87] or the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989, or
- (b) a debt relief restrictions order under this Act or that Order.
