## 386. Categories of preferential debts.[^c15582271][^c20438291][^c20438321][^c-7dcdcd6c22dfc235b09ff8bc01bb0dd9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A reference in this Act to the preferential debts of a company or an individual is to the debts listed in Schedule 6 to this Act (contributions to occupational pension schemes; remuneration, &c. of employees; levies on coal and steel production[^c15582281] ; debts owed to the Financial Services Compensation Scheme[^c-869d6e5d1d5c2e1fa03a26ae1a63c7aa] ; deposits covered by Financial Services Compensation Scheme[^c-89b7dd8dace595d8a7b089b9bf3f728a] ; other deposits[^c-04d2703bd1844faa7450f65f17c17024] ; certain HMRC debts[^c-04e1db918f0d64373a221675b5fb27d8]); and references to preferential creditors are to be read accordingly.

(1A)[^c-cb76f76746d21d4d3b5786ddb6d6e4a1] A reference in this Act to the “ordinary preferential debts” of a company or an individual is to the preferential debts listed in any of paragraphs 8 to 15B of Schedule 6 to this Act.

(1B) A reference in this Act to the “secondary preferential debts” of a company or an individual is to the preferential debts listed in paragraph 15BA , 15BB or 15D[^c-53d312768185ab1897c1161b4703de13] of Schedule 6 to this Act.

(2) In Schedule 6[^c-985682558b0e430d05a38f767ab3beb3] “**the debtor**” means the company or the individual concerned.

(3) Schedule 6 is to be read with Schedule 4 to the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^c15582291] (occupational pension scheme contributions).
