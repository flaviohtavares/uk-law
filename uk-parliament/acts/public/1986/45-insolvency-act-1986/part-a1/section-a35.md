## A35. Monitoring[^c-2d593eae2f3cccb39a2961eef842bf2c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^M_C_feb355ce-99e3-4780-eebf-8f059572d90c] During a moratorium, the monitor must monitor the company’s affairs for the purpose of forming a view as to whether it remains likely that the moratorium will result in the rescue of the company as a going concern.

(2) In forming the view mentioned in subsection (1), the monitor is entitled to rely on information provided by the company, unless the monitor has reason to doubt its accuracy.
