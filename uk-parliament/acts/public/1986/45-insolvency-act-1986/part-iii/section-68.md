# Insolvency Act 1986, section 68

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/45
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Committee of creditors[^c15571371][^c15571381][^c-708ccdc84b43070106df0201b200b66e]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a receiver has sent or published a report as mentioned in section 67(2) the company's unsecured creditors may, in accordance with the rules[^c-0c0a918d352fb44051a58c34b4ed4ef9], establish a committee (“**the creditors’ committee**”) to exercise the functions conferred on it by or under this Act.

(2) If such a committee is established, the committee may on giving not less than 7 days’ notice require the receiver to attend before it at any reasonable time and furnish it with such information relating to the carrying out by him of his functions as it may reasonably require.
