## 249. “Connected” with a company.[^c15576241][^c18970801][^c18979591][^c19937131][^c19937951][^c19938001][^c20458931][^c-2c7dcf63df12a49ee4ff7b3ae7f530e1][^c-30fd44fe3fe101969e409cfa525dae55][^c-ab06d267d262da9d20d11e21387c4249][^c-285acf5bab96c25d0a8db48040739e86][^c-a7d232e7252f4dd28c814044c5fba54b][^c-98799bec1de66f9e356992cf714fe9f9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of any provision in this Group of Parts, a person is connected with a company if—

- (a) he is a director or shadow director of the company or an associate of such a director or shadow director, or
- (b) he is an associate of the company,

and “**associate**” has the meaning given by section 435 in Part XVIII of this Act.
