# Insolvency Act 1986, section 138

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/45
>

## 138. Appointment of liquidator in Scotland.[^c15572911][^c-ea165c558765d5554df492c8d1b8b8be][^c-3eeb8214fe438f1877f4ab4895630b83]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where a winding-up order is made by the court in Scotland, a liquidator shall be appointed by the court at the time when the order is made.

(2) The liquidator so appointed (here referred to as “**the interim liquidator**”) continues in office until another person becomes liquidator in his place under this section or the next.

(3) The interim liquidator shall (subject to the next subsection) as soon as practicable in the period of 28 days beginning with the day on which the winding-up order was made or such longer period as the court may allow, in accordance with the rules seek nominations from[^c-ef065625d142417256e9211efcdd2137] the company’s creditors and contributories for the purpose of choosing a person (who may be the person who is the interim liquidator) to be liquidator of the company in place of the interim liquidator.

(4) If it appears to the interim liquidator, in any case where a company is being wound up on grounds including its inability to pay its debts, that it would be inappropriate to seek a nomination from the company's contributories under subsection (3), he may seek a nomination only from[^c-373284abb0d2804f6f8264455491bf93] the company’s creditors for the purpose mentioned in that subsection.

(5) If a nomination is sought from the company's creditors, or nominations are sought from the company's creditors and contributories,[^c-c047ea171ccbaf9eb1b4453f2cd28e0b] in pursuance of this section but no person is appointed or nominated as a result[^c-d136861b0db6545d45b10118b046a6b9], the interim liquidator shall make a report to the court which shall appoint either the interim liquidator or some other person to be liquidator of the company.

(6) A person who becomes liquidator of the company in place of the interim liquidator shall, unless he is appointed by the court, forthwith notify the court of that fact.
