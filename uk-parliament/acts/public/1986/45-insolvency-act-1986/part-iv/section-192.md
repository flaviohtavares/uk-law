# Insolvency Act 1986, section 192

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/45
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 192. Information as to pending liquidations.[^c15573761][^c20432811][^c-279af02fdfab5761de4e06ab8935103f][^c-df9d97ccc313b023d5ec679e601c297f][^c-d42c95f368c806f53e80c7334bd5b6c3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-74de95ed19515cfc1c056a10a3beb803] If the winding up of a company is not concluded within one year after its commencement, the liquidator shall, at such intervals as may be prescribed, until the winding up is concluded, send to the registrar of companies a statement in the prescribed form and containing the prescribed particulars with respect to the proceedings in, and position of, the liquidation.

(2) If a liquidator fails to comply with this section, he is liable to a fine and, for continued contravention, to a daily default fine.
