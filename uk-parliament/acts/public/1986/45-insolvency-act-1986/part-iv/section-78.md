# Insolvency Act 1986, section 78

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/45
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Unlimited company formerly limited.[^key-d42c95f368c806f53e80c7334bd5b6c3][^key-2aaac302edc344715e51dad18431623d][^key-d42c95f368c806f53e80c7334bd5b6c3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This section applies in the case of a company being wound up which was at some former time registered as limited but has been re-registered as unlimited [^c20448891]. . . .

(2) A person who, at the time when the application for the company to be re-registered was lodged, was a past member of the company and did not after that again become a member of it is not liable to contribute to the assets of the company more than he would have been liable to contribute had the company not been re-registered.
