# Insolvency Act 1986, section 159

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/45

## 159. Powers of court to be cumulative.[^key-0ce457e91dbe9252d527b55201e2faf6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Powers conferred on the court by this Act[^c20451121] are in addition to, and not in restriction of, any existing powers of instituting proceedings against a contributory or debtor of the company, or the estate of any contributory or debtor, for the recovery of any call or other sums.
