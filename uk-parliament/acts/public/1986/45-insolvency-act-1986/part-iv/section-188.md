# Insolvency Act 1986, section 188

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/45
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 188. Notification that company is in liquidation.[^c15573721][^c20432631][^c20432671][^c-75f99f8aa24095b4abd47e843e99cc48][^c-281face317decc6eef6a0512134b0a6a][^c-d42c95f368c806f53e80c7334bd5b6c3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c18984091] When a company is being wound up, whether by the court or voluntarily—

- (a) every invoice, order for goods or services[^c19945311] , business letter or order form (whether in hard copy, electronic or any other form) issued by or on behalf of the company, or a liquidator of the company or a receiver or manager of the company's property, [^c19945381]. . . and
- (b) all the company's websites,

must contain a statement that the company is being wound up.

(2) If default is made in complying with this section, the company and any of the following persons who knowingly and wilfully authorises or permits the default, namely, any officer of the company, any liquidator of the company and any receiver or manager, is liable to a fine.
