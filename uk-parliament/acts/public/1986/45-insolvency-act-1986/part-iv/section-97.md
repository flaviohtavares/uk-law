# Insolvency Act 1986, section 97

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/45
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. Application of this Chapter.[^c15571661][^key-b7fc24c32b70e3f3ef976aca97e5e1b8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject as follows, this Chapter applies in relation to a creditors’ voluntary winding up.

(2) Sections 99 and 100[^key-5571787fcad0ed44ec86cce9af3662bf] do not apply where, under section 96 in Chapter III, a members’ voluntary winding up has become a creditors’ voluntary winding up.
