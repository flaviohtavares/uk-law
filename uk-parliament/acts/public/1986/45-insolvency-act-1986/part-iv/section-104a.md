# Insolvency Act 1986, section 104A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/45
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 104A. Progress report to company and creditors ... ...[^c-eb4857d2bd84a66b3bb9700ef926548c][^c21414961][^c-55605633bf262eadcccff38c568d182c][^c-70884f26f5197609ed384aa54464b124]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) *(not yet in force: The)*[^c-ea266c051b468ba44f586402e64a1bf1] liquidator must—

- (a) for each prescribed period produce a progress report relating to the prescribed matters; and
- (b) within such period commencing with the end of the period referred to in paragraph (a) as may be prescribed send a copy of the progress report to—
  - (i) the members and creditors , other than opted-out creditors[^c-d06a664d6c798edf03d29ab19b94796e] of the company; and
  - (ii) such other persons as may be prescribed.

(2) A liquidator who fails to comply with this section is liable to a fine.
