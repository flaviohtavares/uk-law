# Insolvency Act 1986, section 80

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/45
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 80. Nature of contributory’s liability.[^c-d42c95f368c806f53e80c7334bd5b6c3][^c-84f8a42097ae2999066f09052b110aef][^c-d42c95f368c806f53e80c7334bd5b6c3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The liability of a contributory creates a debt (in England and Wales in the nature of an ordinary contract debt[^c20448951]) accruing due from him at the time when his liability commenced, but payable at the times when calls are made for enforcing the liability.
