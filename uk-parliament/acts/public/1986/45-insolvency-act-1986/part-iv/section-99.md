# Insolvency Act 1986, section 99

## 99. Directors to lay statement of affairs before creditors.[^c-26f7b3a59f2fe7f263c23d23049ffb7a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-0e6c7df5f66d244f3724f99e2fa500cc] The directors of the company must, before the end of the period of 7 days beginning with the day after the day on which the company passes a resolution for voluntary winding up—

- (a) make out a statement in the prescribed form as to the affairs of the company, and
- (b) send the statement to the company's creditors.

(2) The statement as to the affairs of the company [^c21414741]. . . shall show—

- (a) particulars of the company’s assets, debts and liabilities;
- (b) the names and addresses of the company’s creditors;
- (c) the securities held by them respectively;
- (d) the dates when the securities were respectively given; and
- (e) such further or other information as may be prescribed.

(2A)[^c21414841] The statement as to the affairs of the company shall be verified by some or all of the directors *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2dec1eb2c5bc74c6fcf899df8d6f72d]—

- (a) in the case of a winding up of a company registered in England and Wales, be verified by some or all of the directors[^c-a63e9929abd14e42d186be6b1c2ea216] by a statement of truth; and
- (b) in the case of a winding up of a company registered in Scotland, by affidavit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d22c8a0c0c9aa85e1dd67acc35512e24] contain a statutory declaration by some or all of the directors.

(3)[^c-d012d7facd79f0846e7b0132258525c5] If the directors without reasonable excuse fail to comply with subsection (1), (2) or (2A), they are guilty of an offence and liable to a fine.
