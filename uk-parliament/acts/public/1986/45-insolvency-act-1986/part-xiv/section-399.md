# Insolvency Act 1986, section 399

## 399. Appointment, etc. of official receivers.[^c15583641]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of this Act the official receiver, in relation to any bankruptcy , winding up[^c15583651] , individual voluntary arrangement, debt relief order or application for such an order[^c19758231], is any person who by virtue of the following provisions of this section or section 401 below is authorised to act as the official receiver in relation to that bankruptcy , winding up , individual voluntary arrangement, debt relief order or application for such an order.

(2) The Secretary of State may (subject to the approval of the Treasury as to numbers) appoint persons to the office of official receiver, and a person appointed to that office (whether under this section or section 70 of the [^c15583671]Bankruptcy Act 1914)—

- (a) shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament such salary as the Secretary of State may with the concurrence of the Treasury direct,
- (b) shall hold office on such other terms and conditions as the Secretary of State may with the concurrence of the Treasury direct, and
- (c) may be removed from office by a direction of the Secretary of State.

(3) Where a person holds the office of official receiver, the Secretary of State shall from time to time attach him either to the High Court or to the county court.[^key-2b8f603edfdc4ba1ec795a531b866fbc]

(4) Subject to any directions under subsection (6) below, an official receiver attached to a particular court is the person authorised to act as the official receiver in relation to every bankruptcy , winding up , individual voluntary arrangement, debt relief order or application for such an order[^c19758321] falling within the jurisdiction of that court.

(5) The Secretary of State shall ensure that there is, at all times, at least one official receiver attached to the High Court and at least one attached to the county court[^key-f112a253a770b2fb408c639ac80d2b7d]; but he may attach the same official receiver to both[^key-b82253d6266187f32783dde9c0bb63a4] courts.

(6) The Secretary of State may give directions with respect to the disposal of the business of official receivers, and such directions may, in particular—

- (a) authorise an official receiver attached to one court to act as the official receiver in relation to any case or description of cases falling within the jurisdiction of the other[^key-f790758450d8083d16ad510cfdae0089] court;
- (b) provide, where there is more than one official receiver authorised to act as the official receiver in relation to cases falling within the jurisdiction of any court, for the distribution of their business between or among themselves.

(7) A person who at the coming into force of section 222 of the [^c15583691]Insolvency Act 1985 (replaced by this section) is an official receiver attached to a court shall continue in office after the coming into force of that section as an official receiver attached to that court under this section.
