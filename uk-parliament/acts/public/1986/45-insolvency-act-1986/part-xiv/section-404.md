# Insolvency Act 1986, section 404

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/45
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 404. Investment Account.[^c15583761]

*Extent: E+W*

Any money standing to the credit of the Investment Account (including any money received by the National Debt Commissioners by way of interest on or proceeds of any investment under this section) may be invested by the Commissioners, in accordance with such directions as may be given by the Treasury, in any manner for the time being specified in Part II of Schedule 1 to the [^c15583771]Trustee Investments Act 1961.
