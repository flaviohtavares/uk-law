# Insolvency Act 1986, section 246ZC

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/45
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 246ZC. Proceedings under section 246ZA or 246ZB[^key-45747bd3a2f867979bd970317a8228a4][^key-866dc8f8db0b241adc3c0647fa33842d][^key-ef339195c16f0ad1c33fa908ee5e0cfa][^key-23b0407c483fde68966eb9eeffaf743e][^key-e06d97917dfbc77360d0ca4913ed0ca5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 215 applies for the purposes of an application under section 246ZA or 246ZB as it applies for the purposes of an application under section 213 but as if the reference in subsection (1) of section 215 to the liquidator was a reference to the administrator.
