## 232. Validity of office-holder’s acts.[^c15574921][^c20433891][^c-11ae28cb805d62df79a9506ab417e765][^c-1833ba36373f3ae0946a06692da15a40]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The acts of an individual as [^c18622831]. . . administrative receiver, liquidator or provisional liquidator of a company are valid notwithstanding any defect in his appointment, nomination or qualifications.
