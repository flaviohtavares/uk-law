## 246B. Use of websites[^c-18df2b52075a3fbb24f9ec168f8b9771][^c-70caf8a5f91f02d5c97c3d3059e45d46][^c-fdf7230aeb23385449da1be127d4b008][^c-c3a9e17e88a9f557bfa34a804760a78c][^c-788371096f8da5425d0cd572175725cd][^c-8fba849871c77e6ca9d09a87fb95e2c9][^c21415971][^c-788371096f8da5425d0cd572175725cd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to subsection (2), where any provision of this Act or the rules requires the office-holder to give, deliver, furnish or send a notice or other document or information to any person, that requirement is satisfied by making the notice, document or information available on a website—

- (a) in accordance with the rules, and
- (b) in such circumstances as may be prescribed.

(2)[^c-53c5f87a82bdcd9923a2846077ec6fb1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In this section, “**the office-holder**” means—

- (za)[^c-e17eedec7e82425f365d88c068016d0c] the monitor in relation to a moratorium under Part A1,
- (a) the liquidator, provisional liquidator, administrator, receiver (appointed under section 51),[^c-abfc6ed6595eb89e4f602f26ca5a0c87] or administrative receiver of a company, or
- (b) where a voluntary arrangement in relation to a company is proposed or has taken effect under Part 1, the nominee or the supervisor of the voluntary arrangement.
