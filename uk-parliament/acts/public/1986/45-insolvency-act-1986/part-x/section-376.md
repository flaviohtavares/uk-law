# Insolvency Act 1986, section 376

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/45
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 376. Time-limits.[^c15581461]

*Extent: E+W*

Where by any provision in this Group of Parts or by the rules the time for doing anything (including anything in relation to a bankruptcy application)[^c-651a83027941af726dc71f5971c93f83] is limited, the court may extend the time, either before or after it has expired, on such terms, if any, as it thinks fit.
