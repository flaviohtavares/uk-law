# Insolvency Act 1986, section 372

## 372. Supplies of gas, water, electricity, etc.[^c15581331][^c-370134a71544fcd03e5b04875207a62e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where on any day (“the relevant day”)—

- (a) a bankruptcy order is made against an individual or an interim receiver of an individual’s property is appointed, or
- (b) a voluntary arrangement proposed by an individual is approved under Part VIII, [^c-fc96e201378e6a8778979b56e574c649]...
- (c)[^c-fc96e201378e6a8778979b56e574c649] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

and in this section “**the office-holder**” means the official receiver, the trustee in bankruptcy, the interim receiver or the supervisor of the voluntary arrangement[^c-fd21b6557dcdb841f54e1e7794fc9ff1], as the case may be.

(2) If a request falling within the next subsection is made for the giving after the relevant day of any of the supplies mentioned in subsection (4), the supplier—

- (a) may make it a condition of the giving of the supply that the office-holder personally guarantees the payment of any charges in respect of the supply, but
- (b) shall not make it a condition of the giving of the supply, or do anything which has the effect of making it a condition of the giving of the supply, that any outstanding charges in respect of a supply given to the individual before the relevant day are paid.

(3) A request falls within this subsection if it is made—

- (a) by or with the concurrence of the office-holder, and
- (b) for the purposes of any business which is or has been carried on by the individual, by a firm or partnership of which the individual is or was a member, or by an agent or manager for the individual or for such a firm or partnership.

(4) The supplies referred to in subsection (2) are—

- (a)[^c15581341] a supply of gas by a gas supplier within the meaning of Part I of the Gas Act 1986;
- (aa)[^c-abeb7bbe7b7df4cd5e460f469433af43] a supply of gas by a person within paragraph 1 of Schedule 2A to the Gas Act 1986 (supply by landlords etc.);
- (b)[^c15581351] a supply of electricity by an electricity supplier within the meaning of Part I of the Electricity Act 1989;
- (ba)[^c-0e583926c908a96b87cb6b7d1bd2a1e3] a supply of electricity by a class of person within Class A (small suppliers) or Class B (resale) of Schedule 4 to the Electricity (Class Exemptions from the Requirement for a Licence) Order 2001 ([S.I. 2001/3270](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/3270));
- (c) a supply of water by a water undertaker[^c15581361],
- (ca)[^c-e8f2e677fbcaebb96d7f9b91c938e981] a supply of water by a water supply licensee within the meaning of the Water Industry Act 1991;
- (cb) a supply of water by a person who has an interest in the premises to which the supply is given;
- (d)[^c18412531] a supply of communications services by a provider of a public electronic communications service.
- (e)[^c-c559c3cbb7b633fb9ba557ded767fc02] a supply of communications services by a person who carries on a business which includes giving such supplies;
- (f) a supply of goods or services mentioned in subsection (4A) by a person who carries on a business which includes giving such supplies, where the supply is for the purpose of enabling or facilitating anything to be done by electronic means.

(4A)[^c-4eda1983cc5307ef328a0c3e16e90fb0] The goods and services referred to in subsection (4)(f) are—

- (a) point of sale terminals;
- (b) computer hardware and software;
- (c) information, advice and technical assistance in connection with the use of information technology;
- (d) data storage and processing;
- (e) website hosting.

(5) The following applies to expressions used in subsection (4)—

- (a)[^c15581371] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c15581381] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c18412551] “communications services” do not include electronic communications services to the extent that they are used to broadcast or otherwise transmit programme services (within the meaning of the Communications Act 2003).
