## 373. Jurisdiction in relation to insolvent individuals.[^c15581411]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The High Court and the county court[^key-eda17d79da40ce88e242a1ae49fc185e] have jurisdiction throughout England and Wales for the purposes of the Parts in this Group.

(2) For the purposes of those Parts, the county court[^key-a4c433d39124883f1edf830d7de97a58] has, in addition to its ordinary jurisdiction, all the powers and jurisdiction of the High Court; and the orders of the court may be enforced accordingly in the prescribed manner.

(3) Jurisdiction for the purposes of those Parts is exercised—

- (a) by the High Court or the[^c21428161] county court[^key-38ea4c81e8c3e5231b11a4978066dba6] in relation to the proceedings, which, in accordance with the rules, are allocated to the London insolvency district, and
- (b) by the[^key-1098983b60887f5ff96be122acca086a] county court in relation to the proceedings which are so allocated to any other insolvency district[^key-7196e556faa338c189e6d5c9d639f62d].

(4) Subsection (3) is without prejudice to the transfer of proceedings from one court to another in the manner prescribed by the rules; and nothing in that subsection invalidates any proceedings on the grounds that they were initiated or continued in the wrong court.
