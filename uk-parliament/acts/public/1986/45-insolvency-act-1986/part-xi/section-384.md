# Insolvency Act 1986, section 384

## 384. “Prescribed” and “the rules”.[^c15581611]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to the next subsection and sections 342C(7) and 342F(9) in Chapter V of Part IX[^c15581621], “**prescribed**” means prescribed by the rules; and “**the rules**” means rules made under section 412 in Part XV.

(2) References in this Group of Parts to the amount prescribed for the purposes of any of the following provisions—

- section 251S(4);[^c19757661]
- [^c-fc6089d7c603e022e2771da8070c52b2]...
- section 313A;[^c15581631]
- section 346(3);
- section 354(1) and (2);
- section 358;
- section 360(1);
- section 361(2); [^c19757761]. . .
- section 364(2)(d),
- paragraphs 6 to 8 of Schedule 4ZA,[^c19757721]

and references in those provisions to the prescribed amount are to be read in accordance with section 418 in Part XV and orders made under that section.
