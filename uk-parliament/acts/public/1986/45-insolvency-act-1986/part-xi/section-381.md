> Extent: England, Wales.

## 381. “Bankrupt” and associated terminology.[^c15581521]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) “**Bankrupt**” means an individual who has been made[^c-c140a7378ca7ad81350e9d04a02037c6] bankrupt and, in relation to a bankruptcy order, it means the individual made bankrupt by that order.

(1A)[^c-95a8eb1d67e32f63460777804fac90e0] Bankruptcy application” means an application to an adjudicator for a bankruptcy order.

(2) “**Bankruptcy order**” means an order making[^c-44d1fa2b8d16b1d7ad2c9e30caebc255] an individual bankrupt.

(3) “**Bankruptcy petition**” means a petition to the court for a bankruptcy order.
