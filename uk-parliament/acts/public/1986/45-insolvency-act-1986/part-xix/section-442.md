# Insolvency Act 1986, section 442

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/45

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 442. Extent (other territories).

*Extent: E+W+S*

Her Majesty may, by Order in Council, direct that such of the provisions of this Act as are specified in the Order, being provisions formerly contained in the [^c15586551]Insolvency Act 1985, shall extend to any of the Channel Islands or any colony with such modifications as may be so specified.
