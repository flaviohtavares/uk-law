> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Further provisions with respect to shared ownership leases.

*Extent: E+W*

The provisions of Schedule 4 have effect to exclude certain shared ownership leases from the operation of the provisions of—

- (a) the [^c11871941]Rent Act 1977 and the [^c11871951]Rent (Agriculture) Act 1976, and
- (b) Part I of the [^c11871961]Leasehold Reform Act 1967 (right of long leaseholder to enfranchisement or extension of lease).
