# Financial Services Act 1986, section 24

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Operators and trustees of recognised schemes.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

The operator or trustee of a scheme recognised under section 86 below is an authorised person as respects—

- (a) investment business which consists in operating or acting as trustee in relation to that scheme; and
- (b) any investment business which is carried on by him in connection with or for the purposes of that scheme.
