# Financial Services Act 1986, section 22

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Authorised insurers.[^c1519737]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

A body which is authorised under section 3 or 4 of the [^c1519738]Insurance Companies Act 1982 to carry on insurance business which is investment business and carries on such insurance business in the United Kingdom is an authorised person as respects—

- (a) any insurance business which is investment business; and
- (b) any other investment business which that body may carry on without contravening section 16 of that Act.
