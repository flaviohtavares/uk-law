# Financial Services Act 1986, section 7

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Authorisation by membership of recognised self-regulating organisation.[^c1519702]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1) Subject to subsection (2) below, a member of a recognised self-regulating organisation is an authorised person by virtue of his membership of that organisation.

(2) This section does not apply to a member who is an authorised person by virtue of section 22 or 23 below or an insurance company which is an authorised person by virtue of section 31 below.
