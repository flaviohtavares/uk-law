# Financial Services Act 1986, section 90

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Facilities and information in the United Kingdom.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1) The Secretary of State may make regulations requiring operators of recognised schemes to maintain in the United Kingdom, or in such part or parts of it as may be specified in the regulations, such facilities as he thinks desirable in the interests of participants and as are specified in the regulations.

(2) The Secretary of State may by notice in writing require the operator of any recognised scheme to include such explanatory information as is specified in the notice in any investment advertisement issued or caused to be issued by him in the United Kingdom in which the scheme is named.
