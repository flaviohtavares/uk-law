# Financial Services Act 1986, section 128A

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 128A. Relevance of other controls.[^c1520234]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

In determining—

- (a) in relation to a self-regulating organisation, whether the requirements of Schedule 2 are met, or
- (b) in relation to a professional body, whether the requirements of Schedule 3 are met,

the Secretary of State shall take into account the effect of any other controls to which members of the organisation or body are subject.
