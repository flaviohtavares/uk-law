# Financial Services Act 1986, section 17

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Applications for recognition.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1) A professional body may apply to the Secretary of State for an order declaring it to be a recognised professional body for the purposes of this Act.

(2) Subsections (2) to (6) of section 9 above shall have effect in relation to an application under subsection (1) above as they have effect in relation to an application under subsection (1) of that section.
