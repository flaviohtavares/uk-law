# Financial Services Act 1986, section 24A

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24A. Investment companies with variable capital[^c1519742]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

An investment company with variable capital is an authorised person as respects—

- (a) investment business which consists in operating the collective investment scheme constituted by the company; and
- (b) any investment business which is carried on by the company in connection with or for the purposes of operating that scheme.
