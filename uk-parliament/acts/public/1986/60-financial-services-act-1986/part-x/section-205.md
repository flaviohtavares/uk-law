# Financial Services Act 1986, section 205

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 205. General power to make regulations.[^c1520968][^c1520967]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

The Secretary of State or the Treasury[^c1520969] may make regulations prescribing anything which by this Act is authorised or required to be prescribed.
