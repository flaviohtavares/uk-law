# Financial Services Act 1986, section 209

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 209. Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1) This Act extends to Northern Ireland.

(2)[^c1521114] Subject to any Order made after the passing of this Act by virtue of subsection (1)(a) of section 3 of the [^c1521115]Northern Ireland Constitution Act 1973 the regulation of investment business, the official listing of securities and offers of unlisted securities shall not be transferred matters for the purposes of that Act but shall for the purposes of subsection (2) of that section be treated as specified in Schedule 3 to that Act.
