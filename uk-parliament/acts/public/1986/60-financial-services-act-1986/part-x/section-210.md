# Financial Services Act 1986, section 210

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 210. Expenses and receipts.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1) Any expenses incurred by the Secretary of State under this Act shall be defrayed out of moneys provided by Parliament.

(2) Any fees or other sums received by the Secretary of State under this Act shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund.

(3) Subsections (1) and (2) above apply also to expenses incurred and fees received under this Act by the Friendly Societies Commission[^c1521116].
