# Financial Services Act 1986, section 195

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 195. Offers of short-dated debentures.[^c1520878][^c1520879]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

As respects debentures which, under the terms of issue, must be repaid within less than one year of the date of issue—

- (a) section 79(2) of the [^c1520880]Companies Act 1985 (offer of debentures of oversea company deemed not to be an offer to the public if made to professional investor) shall apply for the purposes of Chapter I of Part III of that Act as well as for those of Chapter II of that Part; and
- (b) Article 89(2) of the [^c1520881]Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 (corresponding provisions for Northern Ireland) shall apply for the purposes of Chapter I of Part IV of that Order as well as for those of Chapter II of that Part.
