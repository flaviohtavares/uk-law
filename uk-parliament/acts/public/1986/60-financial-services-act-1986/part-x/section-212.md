# Financial Services Act 1986, section 212

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 212. Short title, consequential amendments and repeals.[^c1521119]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1) This Act may be cited as the Financial Services Act 1986.

(2) The enactments and instruments mentioned in Schedule 16 to this Act shall have effect with the amendments there specified, being amendments consequential on the provisions of this Act.

(3) The enactments mentioned in Part I of Schedule 17 to this Act and the instruments mentioned in Part II of that Schedule are hereby repealed or revoked to the extent specified in the third column of those Parts.
