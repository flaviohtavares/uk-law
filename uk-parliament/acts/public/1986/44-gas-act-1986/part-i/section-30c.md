# Gas Act 1986, section 30C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (31).** Text as at 2025-11-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/44
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30C. Time limits on the imposition of penalties.[^c-d81e4378756652221004dbe3280ac8b2][^c14421821]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where no final or provisional order has been made in relation to a contravention or failure, the Authority may not impose a penalty in respect of the contravention or failure later than the end of the period of five years[^c-42291039024cb8dcf97181cf5a2d219b] from the time of the contravention or failure, unless before the end of that period—

- (a) the notice under section 30A(3) relating to the penalty is served on the regulated person[^c-927c9c4b77a55005f290a9e1e5719e3f] under section 30A(7), or
- (b) a notice relating to the contravention or failure is served on the regulated person under section 38(1).

(2) Where a final or provisional order has been made in relation to a contravention or failure, the Authority may not impose a penalty in respect of the contravention or failure unless the notice relating to the penalty under section 30A(3) was served on the [^c-927c9c4b77a55005f290a9e1e5719e3f]... under section 30A(7)—

- (a) within three months from the confirmation of the provisional order or the making of the final order, or
- (b) where the provisional order is not confirmed, within six months from the making of the provisional order.
