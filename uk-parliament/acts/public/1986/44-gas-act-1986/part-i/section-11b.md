# Gas Act 1986, section 11B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (31).** Text as at 2025-11-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/44
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11B. Duty of current and prospective LNG import or export facility owners to provide information[^c-3d973d7bdc0265ce6afc649fe3196934]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who is or expects to be the owner of an LNG import or export facility must provide the Authority with such information in such manner and at such times as the Authority may reasonably require to facilitate the performance of its functions under this Act.
