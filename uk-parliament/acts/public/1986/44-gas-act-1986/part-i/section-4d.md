# Gas Act 1986, section 4D

## 4D. Authority to consult and cooperate with other authorities[^key-a1726f3877ce3497af86cab91c82a4cb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) When carrying out its designated regulatory functions[^key-3abfc1562e6d64fd6b15e3a7768af48f], the Authority must, wherever it thinks fit—

- (a) consult and cooperate with the Northern Ireland Authority[^key-dc1195550982f5cdafceb905a5e0ccbb];
- (b) provide the Northern Ireland Authority[^key-70c22b96ec6b22a1a17396c2c5025852] with information it[^key-415c9b00cc42f947176101f2598f5f61] may require in order to carry out its designated regulatory functions[^key-bcba6840ff8e36b166d9ab4caf1f6555]; [^key-a7d316a480d3e8089fbcaa32c821c207]...
- (c) consult relevant national authorities;
- (d)[^key-23e08cbdaef2c0b10fe3ffa802570357] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) In exercising functions in accordance with subsection (1), the Authority must, wherever it thinks fit, cooperate with the Northern Ireland Authority[^key-f5a9002166dab632dd140d9569d3ae9c] with a view [^key-fef118a857deff331044a3ef7ba8ecbe]... to—

- (a)[^key-d45c2a7907843655efd2c1b5d5a37832] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the promotion and facilitation of cooperation between transmission system operators;
- (c) the optimal management of gas networks;
- (d) the promotion of jointly managed trade in gas between Great Britain and Northern Ireland[^key-cb4cdc1bd41745ec017e6eae28bb58ca] and the allocation of capacity between Great Britain and Northern Ireland[^key-4c34cbfd53d3894df93b1e9e9912cb79];
- (e) ensuring an adequate level of interconnection capacity;
- (f)[^key-8c8a56f975934b81dab52aa99c6c967f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . and
- (g) the coordination of the regulation of gas markets, including rules concerning the management of congestion of gas networks.

(3) In this section—

- [^key-5ba031b02654b78a50c696db5d616db5]...
- [^key-b1873fdbe58f215f7e7a08370483c8c2]...
- “relevant national authority” means any of the following—
  - (a) [^key-f97a6ed3283ca4483c97883f640abd85]...
  - (b) the Office of Communications;
  - (c) the[^key-1b543486cf44b2102321cab6c3f0e37f] CMA;
  - (d) the Water Services Regulation Authority.[^key-83a8e1fc9dc527e1ea7c36492b7e1e89]
- [^key-71814f96f89bb70cc46a2f55f7119f1d]...
