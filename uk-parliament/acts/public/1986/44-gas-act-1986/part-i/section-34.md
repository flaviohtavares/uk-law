# Gas Act 1986, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (31).** Text as at 2025-11-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/44
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. General functions.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) It shall be the duty of the Director, so far as it appears to him practicable from time to time, to keep under review the carrying on both within and outside Great Britain of

- (a)[^c14424851] such activities as are mentioned in section 5(1) above or subsection (2A) below[^key-6cab6ccc344edcabc3eb62b75ef6c19e]; and
- (b) activities ancillary to such activities (including in particular the storage of gas, the provision and reading of meters and the provision of pre-payment facilities).

(2) It shall also be the duty of the Director, so far as it appears to him practicable from time to time, to collect information with respect to

- (a)[^c14424861] the carrying on of such activities as are mentioned in subsection (1) above; and
- (b) the persons by whom such activities are carried on,

with a view to his becoming aware of, and ascertaining the circumstances relating to, matters with respect to which his functions are exercisable.

(2A)[^key-df81c8e0f9380c6d202256c256c5b293] The activities referred to in subsection (1)(a) are the matters specified in the following provisions of the Gas Directive as matters to be monitored—

- (a) Article 26(3);
- (b) Article 41(1)(g) to (k), (m), (n) , (r) and[^key-fb32e9937044340852ff2d1c619f4af8] (t);
- (c) where a person is certified on the ground mentioned in subsection (5) of section 8G, Article 41(3)(a) and (b);
- (d) [^key-d1226b4acfff0d19c1db60cd415ce29d]... and
- (e) Article 41(9).

(2B)[^key-cbf50dfcccc79ca9b32521ed16cfb1eb] For the purposes of subsection (2A)(c), the reference to Article 41(3)(a) is to be read as if the reference in that provision to Article 41 were a reference to Article 41 as implemented in Great Britain immediately before IP completion day.

(3) The Secretary of State may give general directions indicating—

- (a) considerations to which the Director should have particular regard in determining the priority in which matters are to be brought under review in the performance of his duty under subsection (1) or (2) above; and
- (b) considerations to which, in cases where it appears to the Director that any of his functions are exercisable, he should have particular regard in determining whether to exercise those functions.

(4) It shall be the duty of the Director, where either he considers it expedient or he is requested by the Secretary of State or the[^key-c482b37c1fb6c16311f5582104c1a1ed] CMA to do so, to give information, advice and assistance to the Secretary of State or the CMA with respect to any matter in respect of which any function of the Director or the Secretary of State.[^c14424871] is exercisable.

(5)[^key-5d4ac9c85631d9b61b0d538c75b3233e] The Authority shall have power to make agreements with the Health and Safety Executive for the Authority to perform on behalf of that Executive (with or without payment) any of the functions of that Executive.
