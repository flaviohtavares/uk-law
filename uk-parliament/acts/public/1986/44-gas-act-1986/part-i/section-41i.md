>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41I. Service by Authority of certain notices on Citizens Advice and Consumer Scotland.[^c14427651][^key-47dbbbdda351683acf9f92d2ce21f53a][^key-04f91f112b3118b9ac2bc2fc3571377f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the Authority is required by any provision of this Part to publish a notice or any other document, the Authority shall send a copy of the document to Citizens Advice[^key-4a475bb2b726b3168b99d19d6b465f09] and Consumer Scotland[^key-28506020908e81d9669f12eda2065c36].
