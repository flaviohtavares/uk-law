# Airports Act 1986, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (51).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/31
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Application of enactments relating to statutory undertakings.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 2 shall have effect with respect to the application of the enactments mentioned in that Schedule (which relate to statutory undertakers etc.) to airports to which this part applies and to relevant airport operators.
