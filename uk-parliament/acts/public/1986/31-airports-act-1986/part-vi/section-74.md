## 74. Restriction on disclosure of information.[^key-8b9408330e4a341d388860585a69f511][^key-964deef7783ad06a68e5b8a1023ae981]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the following provisions of this section, no information with respect to any particular business which has been obtained under or by virtue of the provisions of this Act shall, so long as the business continues to be carried on, be disclosed without the consent of the person for the time being carrying it on.

(2)[^c9346571] Subsection (1) does not apply to any disclosure of information which is made—

- (a) for the purpose of facilitating the performance of any functions under this Act or any of the enactments or subordinate legislation[^c9346581] specified in subsection (3) of any Minister, any Northern Ireland department, the head of any such department, the CAA, the Competition and Markets Authority[^key-9b30f97487f157ba21b17aa550390575] the Water Services Regulation Authority[^key-92d2e9c57d9127e880a36a9259511af2] the Director General of Electricity Supply[^c9346601] the Director General of Electricity Supply for Northern Ireland[^c9346611] or the Director General of Gas for Northern Ireland[^c9346621] the Office of Rail and Road[^M_F_39d9b6ff-5675-4c83-e0f0-3f36e3e06cb6] or a local weights and measures authority in Great Britain;
- (b) in connection with the investigation of any criminal offence or for the purposes of any criminal proceedings;
- (c) for the purposes of any civil proceedings brought under or by virtue of this Act or any of the enactments or subordinate legislation specified in subsection (3);
- (d) in pursuance of any assimilated[^key-871c6026a6b730127801c465afed2e33][^key-1632d4db9d19df0fe9d45714852ecf43] obligation.

(5) Any person who discloses any information in contravention of this section shall be guilty of an offence and liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or to a fine, or to both.
