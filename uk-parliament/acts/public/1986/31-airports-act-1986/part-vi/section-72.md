# Airports Act 1986, section 72

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (51).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/31
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72. Increase in maximum number of members of CAA.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 2(2) of the 1982 Act (which provides that the CAA shall consist of not less than six nor more than twelve members), for “twelve” there shall be substituted “sixteen”.
