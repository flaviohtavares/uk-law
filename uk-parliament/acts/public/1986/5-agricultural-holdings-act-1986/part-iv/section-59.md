# Agricultural Holdings Act 1986, section 59

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 59. Interpretation of Part IV.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In sections 36 to 48 above [^c-877d53c9e88898a7e137f662d2bf4610]...—

- “**the date of death**”,
- “**the deceased**”,
- “**the holding**”,
- “**related holding**”, and
- “**the tenancy**”,

have the meanings given by section 35(2) above; and in those sections “**eligible person**” has the meaning given by section 36(3) above.

(2) In sections 49 to 58 above [^c-ca7e57bfbc176dab26867b85f46174b3]...—

- “**close relative**” of the retiring tenant,
- “**the holding**”,
- “**the nominated successor**”,
- “**related holding**”,
- “**the retirement date**”,
- “**the retirement notice**”,
- “**the retiring tenant**”,
- “**the retiring tenants**”, and
- “**the tenancy**”,

have the meanings given by section 49(3) above; and in those sections “**eligible person**” has the meaning given by section 50(2) above.
