# Agricultural Holdings Act 1986, section 73

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Deterioration of holding: successive tenancies.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c21525821] Where the tenant of an agricultural holding has remained on the holding , or on any agricultural holding which comprised the whole or a substantial part of the land comprised in the holding,[^c21525781] during two or more tenancies his landlord shall not be deprived of his right to compensation under section 71 or 72 above in respect of any dilapidation, deterioration or damage by reason only that the tenancy during which an act or omission occurred which in whole or in part caused the dilapidation, deterioration or damage was a tenancy other than the tenancy at the termination of which the tenant quits the holding.

(2)[^c21525801] Where this Act applies in relation to any tenancy referred to in subsection (1) above by virtue of section 4(1)(g) of the Agricultural Tenancies Act 1995, the reference in that subsection to a substantial part of the land comprised in the holding means a substantial part determined by reference to either area or value.
