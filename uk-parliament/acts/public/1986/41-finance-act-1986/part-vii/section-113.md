> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 113. “Securities” for purposes of Exchange Equalisation Account Act 1979.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

—At the end of section 3 of the [^c13534221]Exchange Equalisation Account Act 1979 (investment of the funds of the Exchange Equalisation Account) there shall be added the following subsection—

> (4) Without prejudice to the reference in subsection (1)(b) above to special drawing rights, the reference in subsection (3) above to currency of any country includes a reference to units of account defined by reference to more than one currency.
