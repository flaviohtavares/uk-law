# Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (32).** Text as at 2024-04-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/47
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Interpretation.

*Extent: S*

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “[^key-a06c99ace08db9f8559ce551d12198cf]**the 2011 Act**” means the Children's Hearings (Scotland) Act 2011 (asp 1);
- “the 2019 Act” means the Age of Criminal Responsibility (Scotland) Act 2019[^key-a29468028e901de571a294c0b4eb8fc6] (asp 7),
- “**advice and assistance**” has the meaning given to it in section 6(1) of this Act;
- “[^c20854641]**adviser**” has the meaning given to it in section 6(2) of this Act;
- “**adviser code**” means the code of practice in relation to the register of advice organisations for the time being in force under Schedule 1A to this Act;
- “**assistance by way of representation**” has the meaning given to it in section 6(1) of this Act;
- “**the Board**” has the meaning given to it in section 1(1) of this Act;
- “[^key-e8bf2cbd707d7caf1f9669eb7a9d92cc]**children's legal aid**” has the meaning given to it in section 28B(2) of this Act;
- “**children's legal assistance**” means—
  - (a) children's legal aid, and
  - (b) advice and assistance in relation to children's hearings, pre-hearing panels (as defined in section 79(2)(a) of the 2011 Act) or proceedings under Part 10 or 15 of the 2011 Act or in relation to proceedings under Part 4 of the 2019 Act[^key-78586ef28381961879adafe6fbe16219];

- “**civil legal aid**” has the meaning given to it in section 13(2) of this Act;
- “[^c14067711]**the code**” means the code of practice in relation to criminal legal assistance for the time being in force under section 25B of this Act;
- “**criminal legal aid**” has the meaning given to it in section 21(1) of this Act;
- “[^c14067721]**criminal legal assistance**” means criminal legal aid and advice and assistance in relation to criminal matters;
- “ **document** ” includes information recorded in any form;
- “**firm**” includes an incorporated practice, a licensed legal services provider,[^key-734df8546693d38e2e6d80d0bce4aa23] a sole solicitor and a law centre;
- “**the Fund**” has the meaning given to it in section 4(1) of this Act;
- “**incorporated practice**” has the same meaning as in the [^c14067731] Solicitors’ (Scotland) Act 1980;
- “[^c14067741]**law centre**” means a body—
  - (a) established for the purpose of providing legal services to the public generally as well as to individual members of the public; and
  - (b) which does not distribute any profits made either to its members or otherwise, but reinvests any such profits for the purposes of the law centre;
- “**the Law Society**” means the Law Society of Scotland;
- “**legal aid**” means civil legal aid, criminal legal aid, children's legal aid[^key-08e0803c802b352a470da5eb6736c1f2] or legal aid given in connection with proceedings for contempt of court [^key-4c7f37dd2547db3b77ef230f8ecf1d1f]...;
- “licensed legal services provider” and “solicitor investor” are to be construed in accordance with Part 2 of the Legal Services (Scotland) Act 2010;[^key-746fb889e5e0198ee396b306ffdaadb7]
- “**person**”
  - (a) [^c14067761] does not include a body corporate or unincorporate, except where such body is acting in a representative, fiduciary or official capacity, so as to authorise legal aid or adive and assistance to be provided to such a body ; and[^c14067771]
  - (b) includes a person under the age of sixteen years.
- “[^c14067781]**the Register**” means the Register established and maintained under section 25A of this Act;
- “[^c20854661]**the register of advice organisations**” means the register established and maintained under section 12A of this Act;
- “ **registered firm** ” means a firm whose name appears on the Register;
- “[^c20854711]**registered organisation**” means an organisation whose name appears on the register of advice organisations;
- “ **registered solicitor** ” means a solicitor whose name appears on the Register;
- “ **sole solicitor** ” means a solicitor practising under his own name or as a single solicitor under a firm name; and
- “**solicitor connected with a firm**” includes a sole solicitor , a solicitor investor in a firm[^key-78beab2b3774628c59ff15329f293c72] and a solicitor who is a partner, director or employee of a firm, and cognate expressions shall be construed accordingly.
