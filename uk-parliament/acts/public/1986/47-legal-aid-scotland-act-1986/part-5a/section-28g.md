# Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986, section 28G

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (32).** Text as at 2024-04-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/47

## 28G. Conditions[^key-b11d80fa18801e4e9485bb4fd2293256]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Board may make the grant of children's legal aid subject to such conditions as the Board considers expedient; and such conditions may be imposed at any time.
