# Legal Aid (Scotland) Act 1986, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (32).** Text as at 2024-04-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1986/47
>

## 8. Availability of advice & assistance.[^c14061981][^c14061971]

*Extent: S*

Subject to any provision made in regulations under section 8A(1)[^c20859521] or 9(1)[^c-9fc72d8524c1f118b39eb873e1e25916] and section 11(2) of this Act, advice and assistance to which this Part applies shall be available in Scotland for any client if—

- (a) his disposable income does not exceed £245[^c-2ea6d2d13d50b800325df6e5415fa014] a week; or
- (b) he is (directly or indirectly) in receipt of universal credit under Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2012,[^c-eba413c71f5fc47a27eed8e177cd0837] income support[^c20845551] , an income-based jobseeker's allowance (payable under the Jobseekers Act 1995) or an income-related allowance under Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2007 (employment and support allowance),[^c20856621],

and his disposable capital does not exceed £1,716[^c-9aa55afcaa6669f3e5c91fb520415403].
