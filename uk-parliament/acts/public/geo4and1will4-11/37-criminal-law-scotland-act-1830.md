Criminal Law (Scotland) Act 1830

1830 c.37

An Act to amend an Act of the ninth year of His late Majesty King George the Fourth, to facilitate Criminal Trials in Scotland, and to abridge the Period now required between the pronouncing of Sentence and Execution thereof, in cases importing a Capital Punishment.

## 7. Officers may cite jurors and witnesses, without witnesses.

It shall be sufficient for the legal citation of any juror or witness in any civil cause or proceeding[^c555269], that such citation be given by any officer of the law duly authorized, without witnesses; and the oath of such officer in support of the execution shall be held and received as sufficient evidence of such citation when the same shall be questioned in a court of law.

## 13. Provisions of 9 Geo. 4. extended to inferior judges, &c. in certain cases

The [^c555275]Circuit Courts (Scotland) Act 1828 in so far as it provides for rendering all inferior judges and magistrates more safe in the execution of their duty, shall extend to all acts done by any such judge or magistrate in apprehending any party, or in regard to any criminal cause or proceeding, or to any prosecution for a pecuniary penalty.
