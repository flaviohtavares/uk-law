# Landlord and Tenant Act 1987, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (41).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1987/31
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Amendments relating to service charges.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Sections 18 to 30 of the 1985 Act (regulation of service charges payable by tenants) shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in Schedule 2 (which include amendments—

- (a) extending the provisions of those sections to dwellings other than flats, and
- (b) introducing certain additional limitations on service charges).

(2)[^c10162591] Sections 45 to 51 of the [^c10162601]Housing Act 1985 (which are, so far as relating to dwellings let on long leases, superseded by sections 18 to 30 of the 1985 Act as amended by Schedule 2) shall cease to have effect in relation to dwellings so let.
