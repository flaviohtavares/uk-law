# Landlord and Tenant Act 1987, section 49

## 49. Extension of circumstances in which notices are sufficiently served.[^key-220132d2d7cc1f814e59526c9efe0089]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 196 of the [^c10162641]Law of Property Act 1925 (regulations respecting notices), any reference in subsection (3) or (4) to the last-known place of abode or business of the person to be served shall have effect, in its application to a notice to be served by a tenant on a landlord of premises to which this Part applies, as if that reference included a reference to—

- (a) the address last furnished to the tenant by the landlord in accordance with section 48, or
- (b) if no address has been so furnished in accordance with section 48, the address last furnished to the tenant by the landlord in accordance with section 47.
