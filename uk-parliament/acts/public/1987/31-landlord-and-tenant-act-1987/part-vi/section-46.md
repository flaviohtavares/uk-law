# Landlord and Tenant Act 1987, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (41).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1987/31
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. Application of Part VI, etc.[^key-a8d90c5e1850bd122993635886f8d2a5][^key-220132d2d7cc1f814e59526c9efe0089]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Part applies to premises which consist of or include a dwelling and are not held under a tenancy to which Part II of the [^c10162631]Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 applies.

(1A)[^key-3d620e64b13e891182dd2ead77117f19] But this Part does not apply if the dwelling is in Wales and is subject to an occupation contract (see section 7 of the Renting Homes (Wales) Act [2016 (anaw 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/1)).

(2) In this Part “**service charge**” has the meaning given by section 18(1) of the 1985 Act.

(3)[^key-84b747bfb20983e62dc87fb363007f10] In this Part “**administration charge**” has the meaning given by paragraph 1 of Schedule 11 to the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002.
