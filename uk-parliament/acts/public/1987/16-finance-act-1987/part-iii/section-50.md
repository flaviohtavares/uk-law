# Finance Act 1987, section 50

> Text as at 2024-02-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Warrants to purchase Government stock, etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where an interest in, a right to an allotment of or to subscribe for, or an option to acquire or to dispose of[^c22513311], exempt securities is transferred to or vested in any person by any instrument, no stamp duty shall be chargeable on the instrument by virtue of Part I[^c22517441] [^c-5c266105cda5b7dc475d257a5bb89534]..., of Schedule 13 to the Finance Act 1999 (conveyance or transfer on sale or otherwise)

(2) No stamp duty under Schedule 15 to the Finance Act 1999 (bearer instruments)[^c22517461] shall be chargeable —

- (a)[^c-aa608ac7abdbb3bd12d94511ba5e4398] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) on the transfer of the interest, right or option constituted by, or transferable by means of, an instrument which relates to an interest, right or option within subsection (1)[^c-9b52e3ef08bca67e3e01456c39b886c3].

(3) For the purposes of this section, “**exempt securities**” means —

- (a) securities the transfer of which is exempt from all stamp duties,
- (b)[^c-f44768574ec0ddb5358f4cefbb7961ec] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) securities the transfer of which is exempt by virtue of section 30 of the Finance Act 1967 or section 7 of the Finance Act (Northern Ireland) 1967 or section 79(2) of the Finance Act 1986[^c22513331] from stamp duty under Schedule 15 to the Finance Act 1999 (stamp duty: bearer instruments)[^c-5804f2780aabce9a5ea9cc50c55fe30c];

and “**securities**” means stock or marketable securities and includes loan capital as defined in section 78(7) of the Finance Act 1986 [^c22188281].
