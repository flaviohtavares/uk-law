> Text as at 2024-02-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Reduced rates of tax.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the [^c11286981]Inheritance Tax Act 1984 (in this Part of this Act referred to as “**the 1984 Act**”) section 8(1) (indexation of rate bands) shall not apply to chargeable transfers made in the year beginning 6th April 1987.

(2) For the Table in Schedule 1 to that Act there shall be substituted the Table set out below:
