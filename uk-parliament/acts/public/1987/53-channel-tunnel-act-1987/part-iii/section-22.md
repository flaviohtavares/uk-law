# Channel Tunnel Act 1987, section 22

> Text as at 2012-11-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Application of Railways Board’s byelaws to their services in the tunnel system.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Byelaws made by the Railways Board under section 67 of the [^c7868761]Transport Act 1962 (general power to make byelaws for their railways and premises) shall apply in relation to persons and goods being conveyed by the Railways Board on any railway of the Concessionaires as they apply in relation to persons and goods being conveyed on any railway of the Board.
