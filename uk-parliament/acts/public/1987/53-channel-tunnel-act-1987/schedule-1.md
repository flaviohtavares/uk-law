### Part IV — Supplementary

#### Interpretation

**1.** In this Schedule—

- “**A20**” means the road from Ashford to Folkestone so classified; and
- “**B2065**” means the road from Hythe to Bishopsbourne so classified.

#### Limits of deviation

**2.** In their construction—

- (a) each scheduled work may deviate from the line or situation shown for that work on the deposited plans to the extent of the limits of deviation so shown;
- (b) Work No. 5 may deviate from the level shown for that work on the deposited sections to the extent of 10 metres upwards and to any extent downwards; and
- (c) each of the scheduled works other than Work No. 5 may deviate from the level so shown for the work in question to the extent of 3 metres upwards and to any extent downwards.
