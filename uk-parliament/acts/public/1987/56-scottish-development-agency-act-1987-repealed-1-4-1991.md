# Scottish Development Agency Act 1987 (repealed 1. 4. 1991)

> Text as at 1991-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

*Extent: S*

Scottish Development Agency Act 1987 [^c802850](repealed 1. 4. 1991)

1987 c. 56

**No longer in force (ended 1991-04-01).**

## 1. Increase of financial limit.

In section 13(3) of the [^c802852]Scottish Development Agency Act 1975 (which sets out the limit on the aggregate of certain amounts outstanding as regards the Scottish Development Agency), for the words “£700 million” (which were substituted by section 2(1) of the [^c802853]Industry Act 1981) there shall be substituted the words “ £1,200 million ”.

## 2. Short title and extent.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Scottish Development Agency Act 1987.

(2) This Act extends to Scotland only.
