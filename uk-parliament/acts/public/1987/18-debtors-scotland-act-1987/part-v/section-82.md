## 82. Appeals from decisions under sections 77, 79 and 80.[^c-09fa6a6b43136490d04a76546705ebb5]

*Extent: S*

[^c-7f94f5c588df7342229d23fbd4e75c8f]...

(1)[^c-2de84f30d99e55d37871f0fc01034f36] An appeal to the Inner House of the Court of Session may be made against any decision of a Lord Ordinary or a sheriff principal under section 77(1),[^c-19f86454c78b74ca6bf3d48ad89510a7] section 79(5) or section 80(1), (2), (3A), (3B), (4), (4A), (6), (6A) or (8)(b)[^c-a6ae6773d8cc65d8ff1a7c861492e507] of this Act

(2)[^c-9027f814e86eae5dac704261d26698a2] The decision of the Inner House on an appeal under subsection (1) shall be final.
