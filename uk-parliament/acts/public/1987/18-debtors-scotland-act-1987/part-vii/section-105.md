# Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987, section 105

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1987/18
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 105. Application to Crown.

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-6befd8445a38c5ad6be23686dd1c0b7f] Without prejudice to the [^c9362641]Crown Proceedings Act 1947 and subject to subsection (2) below[^key-7036e911a9ece7f0adf99b1fb3208b89], this Act shall bind the Crown acting in its capacity as a creditor or employer.

(2)[^key-153b00c16a9441eff90666422bb35787] Section 70B of this Act does not affect Her Majesty in Her private capacity as an employer.
