# Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987, section 97

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1987/18
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 97.

*Extent: S*

In relation to any proceedings before the sheriff under any provision of this Act, the power conferred on the Court of Session by section 32 of the [^c9362391]Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1971 (power of Court of Session to regulate civil procedure in sheriff court) shall extend to the making of rules permitting a party to such proceedings, in such circumstances as may be specified in the rules, to be represented by a person who is neither an advocate nor a solicitor.
