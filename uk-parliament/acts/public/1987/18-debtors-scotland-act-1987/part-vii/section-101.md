# Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1987/18
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 101.

*Extent: S*

It shall not be competent for a creditor to bring an action of adjudication for debt [^c-778607bc08b005eeb93cf6ae9a739ec1]... to enforce a debt payable under a liquid document of debt unless—

- (a) the debt has been constituted by decree; or
- (b) the debt is a debitum fundi; or
- (c) the document of debt or, if the document is a bill of exchange or a promissory note, a protest of the bill or note, has been registered for execution in the Books of Council and Session or in sheriff court books.
