## 106. Interpretation.[^c-a45f4cb415674d3b91a879ec240226bf]

*Extent: S*

In this Act—

- [^c-fe6a69b797b768cd68108d6c14ad8aeb]...
- “**current maintenance**” has the meaning given to it in section 73(1) of this Act;
- “[^c-912937710bf526eb58848f4cc241b0be]**debt advice and information package**” has the meaning given to it in section 47(4) of this Act;
- “**earnings**” has the meaning given to it in section 73(2) of this Act;
- “**employer**” has the meaning given to it in section 73(1) of this Act;
- “[^c-f336426f39413d1c1f238fde00daea5e]**enactment**” includes an Act of the Scottish Parliament and any enactment comprised in subordinate legislation under such an Act;
- “the Hague Convention” means the Convention on the International Recovery of Child Support and other forms of Family Maintenance done at The Hague on 23rd November 2007;[^c-cf670c01cac104ca1f469797bb5bbd23]
- [^c9363341]. . .
- “**maintenance**” means periodical sums payable under a maintenance order;
- “**maintenance order**” means—
  - (a) an order granted by a court in Scotland for payment of a periodical allowance on divorce or on dissolution of a civil partnership[^c-cd4b1a8a3b96407a6c526d7c6b725614] or on the granting of a declarator of nullity of marriage or of nullity of a civil partnership[^c-3f16ceadd3fbbd9df8e07c4a7796c580], or for aliment;
  - (aa) [^c-160ab2a846178aa84c8671b6c803bdf8]...
  - (b) an order under section 43 or 44 of the [^c9363351]National Assistance Act 1948, section 23 or 24 of the [^c9363361]Ministry of Social Security Act 1966, section 80 or 81 of the [^c9363371]Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968, section 11(3) of the [^c9363381]Guardianship Act 1973, section 18 or 19 of the [^c9363391]Supplementary Benefits Act 1976, section 50 or 51 of the [^c9363401]Child Care Act 1980 or section 24 or 25 of the [^c9363411]Social Security Act 1986;
  - (c) an order of a court in England and Wales or Northern Ireland registered in Scotland under Part II of the [^c9363421]Maintenance Orders Act 1950;
  - (d) a provisional order of a reciprocating country which is confirmed by a court in Scotland under Part I of the [^c9363431]Maintenance Orders (Reciprocal Enforcement) Act 1972;
  - (e) an order of a reciprocating country which is registered in Scotland under that Part of that Act;
  - (f) an order registered in Scotland under Part II, or under an Order in Council made in pursuance of Part III, of that Act;
  - (g) an order registered in Scotland under section 5 of the [^c9363441]Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982; [^c9363451]. . .
  - (ga) [^c-760edf0baff8f2082eede8dd40c1c2c8]...
  - (h) an alimentary bond or agreement (including a document providing for the maintenance of one party to a marriage by the other after the marriage has been dissolved or annulled)—
    - (i) registered for execution in the Books of Council and Session or sheriff court books; or
    - (ii) registered in Scotland under an Order in Council made under section 13 of the Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982; [^c-6a2e21bef5d1bf72973027a9a3ea812f]...[^c9363461]
  - (j) a maintenance assessment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_239205a9-9252-42c5-950d-db2b8d241f48] maintenance calculation within the meaning of the Child Support Act 1991 ; or[^c-7beab36091563f418439b9dd89e8261e]
  - (k) a maintenance decision (within the meaning of regulation 2 of the International Recovery of Maintenance (Hague Convention 2007) (Scotland) Regulations 2012 ([S.S.I. 2012/301](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2012/301)) or maintenance arrangement (within the meaning of Article 3(e) of the Hague Convention) which is registered in the sheriff court under the Hague Convention.
- [^c-44eba54943bc2c319a84d06d1a3e5e5d]...
- “**net earnings**” has the meaning given to it in section 73(1) of this Act;
- “**officer of court**” means a messenger-at-arms or a sheriff officer;
- “**ordinary debt**” has the meaning given to it in section 73(1) of this Act;
- “[^c9363471]**summary warrant**” means a summary warrant granted under or, as the case may be, by virtue of—
  - (a) paragraph 7 of Schedule 2 to the Abolition of Domestic Rates Etc. (Scotland) Act 1987;
  - (b) paragraph 2 of Schedule 8 to the [^c9363481]Local Government Finance Act 1992;
  - (c) paragraph 2 of Schedule 10 to the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994; or
  - (cc) [^c-05d8a518a918e650db60d11257ab16fe]...
  - (cca) paragraph 2 of schedule 4 to the Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002 (asp 3);[^c16921161]
  - (d) the enactments (other than the Taxes Management Act 1970)[^c-1f64a03fdb1fcee733fd19697e6c0340] mentioned in Schedule 4 to this Act;
  - (e) section 128 of the Finance Act 2008.[^c-c9358376ac84c1d82b67dc413d77ee91]
  - (f) section 225 of the Revenue Scotland and Tax Powers Act 2014 (asp 16).[^c-a3fed8313eacf750ffe1a99de9e64953]
