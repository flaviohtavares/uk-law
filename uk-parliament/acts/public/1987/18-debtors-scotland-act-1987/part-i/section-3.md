# Debtors (Scotland) Act 1987, section 3

## 3. Variation and recall of time to pay direction and arrestment.

*Extent: S*

(1) The court which granted a decree, or the First-tier Tribunal, where it has made an order,[^key-0c8b6f34ab0be597df59df75e72351f2] containing a time to pay direction may, on an application by the debtor or the creditor—

- (a) vary or recall the direction if it is satisfied that it is reasonable in all the circumstances[^key-210f8ab743a41a988d8fedd971b30496] to do so; or
- (b) if an interim attachment or[^key-0c95279e1258f71f0dde019fcc270dd8] an arrestment in respect of the debt concerned is in effect, recall or restrict the interim attachment or[^key-fc0a8f524b58f57b742d5548ee19f097] arrestment.

(2) If an interim attachment or[^key-c53571d828938ad506e7df8eb599b27c] an arrestment in respect of the debt concerned is in effect, the court or the First-tier Tribunal[^key-bbc09ec3443c46f7e644d637930369bb] may order that any variation, recall or restriction under subsection (1) above shall be subject to the fulfilment by the debtor of such conditions as the court or the First-tier Tribunal thinks fit.

(3) The clerk of court, sheriff clerk or, in relation to time to pay directions made by the First-tier Tribunal, a member of administration staff of the First-tier Tribunal[^key-d91e8a9cd060d84959492593656aea5e] shall as soon as is reasonably practicable intimate a variation under subsection (1) above to the debtor and to the creditor, and the variation shall come into effect on the date of such intimation.
