# Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1987, section 70

> Text as at 2013-01-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Amendments and repeals.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The enactments mentioned in Schedule 1 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments respectively specified in that Schedule, being minor amendments and amendments consequential on the provisions of this Act.

(2) The enactments set out in columns 1 and 2 of Schedule 2 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
