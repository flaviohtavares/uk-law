# Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1987, section 69

> Text as at 2013-01-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 69. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Act—

- “**controlled drug**” has the meaning assigned by section 2 of the [^c933685]Misuse of Drugs Act 1971; and
- “**the 1975 Act**” means the [^c933686]Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1975;

and provision for the construction of the expressions “**administrator**”, “**associate**”, “**authorised government department**”, “**confiscation order**”, “**drug trafficking**”, “**implicative gift**”, “**realisable property**”, “**restraint order**” and “**the 1985 Act**” is made by section 47(1) of this Act.
