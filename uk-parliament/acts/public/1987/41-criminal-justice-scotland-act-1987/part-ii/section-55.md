> Text as at 2013-01-31.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Power to petition for winding up etc. on information obtained under section 52.[^c933629]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The words “**or section 52 of the Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1987**” shall be inserted—

- (a)[^c933630] in section 440 of the Companies Act 1985, after the words “that Act”;
- (b) in section 8(1) of the [^c933631]Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986, after the words “the Financial Services Act 1986”, in the second place where they occur; and
- (c)[^c933632] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
