# Consumer Protection Act 1987, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-10-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1987/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Reports etc.[^c-f41839a7ab4fa298163fe1899b066406]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It shall be the duty of the Secretary of State at least once in every five years to lay before each House of Parliament a report on the exercise during the period to which the report relates of the functions which under Part II of this Act, or under Part IV of this Act in its application for the purposes of the safety provisions, are exercisable by the Secretary of State, weights and measures authorities, district councils in Northern Ireland and persons on whom functions are conferred by regulations made under section 27(2) above.

(2) The Secretary of State may from time to time prepare and lay before each House of Parliament such other reports on the exercise of those functions as he considers appropriate.

(3)[^c9341591] Every weights and measures authority, every district council in Northern Ireland and every person on whom functions are conferred by regulations under subsection (2) of section 27 above shall, whenever the Secretary of State so directs, make a report to the Secretary of State on the exercise of the functions exercisable by that authority or council under that section or by that person by virtue of any such regulations.

(4)[^c9341591] A report under subsection (3) above shall be in such form and shall contain such particulars as are specified in the direction of the Secretary of State.

(5) The first report under subsection (1) above shall be laid before each House of Parliament not more than five years after the laying of the last report under section 8(2) of the [^c9341611]Consumer Safety Act 1978.
