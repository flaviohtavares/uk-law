# Consumer Protection Act 1987, section 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-10-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1987/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 9. Application of Part I to Crown.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to subsection (2) below, this Part shall bind the Crown.

(2) The Crown shall not, as regards the Crown’s liability by virtue of this Part, be bound by this Part further than the Crown is made liable in tort or in reparation under the [^c9337381]Crown Proceedings Act 1947, as that Act has effect from time to time.
