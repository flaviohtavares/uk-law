# Banking Act 1987, section 16

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Statement of principles.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1) The Authority[^c9367091] shall, as soon as practicable after the coming into force of this section, publish in such manner as it thinks appropriate a statement of the principles in accordance with which it is acting or proposing to act—

- (a) in interpreting the criteria specified in Schedule 3 to this Act and the grounds for revocation specified in section 11 above; and
- (b) in exercising its power to grant, revoke or restrict an authorisation.

(2)[^c9367101] If in the course of a financial year of the Authority it makes a material change in the principles in accordance with which it is acting or proposing to act as mentioned in subsection (1) above it shall include a statement of the change in the report made by it for that year under section 1(3) above; and the Authority may, at any time, publish in such manner as it thinks appropriate a statement of the principles in accordance with which it is acting or proposing to act as mentioned in that subsection.
