# Banking Act 1987, section 23

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Objection by direction of the Treasury.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1) The Treasury may direct the Authority[^c9367611] to serve a notice of objection under this section on a person—

- (a) who has given notice under section 21 above of his intention to become a shareholder controller of any description of an institution which is not a credit institution[^c9367621]; or
- (b) who has become such a controller without giving the required notice under that section,

if it appears to the Treasury that, in the event of his becoming or, as the case may be, as a result of his having become, such a controller, a notice could be served on the institution by the Treasury under section 183 of the [^c9367631]Financial Services Act 1986 (disqualification or restriction of persons connected with overseas countries which do not afford reciprocal facilities for financial business).

(2) No direction shall be given in a case within subsection (1)(b) above more than three months after the Treasury becomes aware of the fact that the person concerned has become a controller of the relevant description.

(3) Any notice of objection served by virtue of a direction under this section shall state the grounds on which it is served.
