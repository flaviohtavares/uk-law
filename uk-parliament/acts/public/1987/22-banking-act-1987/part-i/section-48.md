# Banking Act 1987, section 48

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Repayment of unauthorised deposits.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1)[^c9370321][^c9370331] If on the application of the Authority[^c9370341] it appears to the court that a person has accepted deposits in contravention of section 3 above the court may—

- (a) order him and any other person who appears to the court to have been knowingly concerned in the contravention to repay the deposits forthwith or at such time as the court may direct; or
- (b) except in Scotland, appoint a receiver to recover those deposits;

but in deciding whether and, if so, on what terms to make an order under this section the court shall have regard to the effect that repayment in accordance with the order would have on the solvency of the person concerned or otherwise on his ability to carry on his business in a manner satisfactory to his creditors.

(2) The jurisdiction conferred by this section shall be exercisable by the High Court and the Court of Session.
