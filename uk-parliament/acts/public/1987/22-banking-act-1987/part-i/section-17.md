# Banking Act 1987, section 17

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Information as to authorised institutions.[^c9367141]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1) Every report made by the Authority[^c9367151] under section 1(3) above shall contain a list of the institutions which are authorised under this Act at the end of the financial year to which the report relates.

(2) The Authority shall make available to any person on request and on payment of such fee, if any, as the Authority may reasonably require a list of the institutions which are authorised either at the date of the request or at such earlier date, being not more than one month earlier, as may be specified in the list.

(3) The Authority may give public notice of the fact that an institution has ceased to be authorised.
