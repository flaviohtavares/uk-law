# Banking Act 1987, section 110

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Short title and commencement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1) This Act may be cited as the Banking Act 1987.

(2)[^c9389671] Section 91 above shall come into force on the passing of this Act and the other provisions of this Act shall come into force on such day as the Treasury may by order appoint; and different days may be appointed for different provisions or different purposes.
