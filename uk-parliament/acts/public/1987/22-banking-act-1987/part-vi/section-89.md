# Banking Act 1987, section 89

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Electronic transfer of funds.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

After section 187(3) of the [^c9387361]Consumer Credit Act 1974 (arrangements to be disregarded in determining whether a consumer credit agreement is to be treated as entered into in accordance with prior or in contemplation of future arrangements between creditor and supplier) there shall be inserted—

> (3A) Arrangements shall also be disregarded for the purposes of subsections (1) and (2) if they are arrangements for the electronic transfer of funds from a current account at a bank within the meaning of the Bankers’ Books Evidence Act 1879.
