# Banking Act 1987, section 104

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 104. Scottish 1819 savings banks.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1) This section applies to any savings bank established before 28th July 1863 under an Act passed in the 59th year of King George III entitled an Act for the Protection of Banks for Savings in Scotland.

(2) For the purposes of Part II of this Act a savings bank to which this section applies becomes insolvent on the making of a winding-up order against it under Part V of the [^c9388451]Insolvency Act 1986 or on the making of an award of sequestration on the estate of the bank.

(3) A savings bank to which this section applies shall be regarded as a relevant savings bank for the purposes of sections 68 and 69 above.
