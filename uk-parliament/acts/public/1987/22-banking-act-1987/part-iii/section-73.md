# Banking Act 1987, section 73

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Offences under Part III.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

A person who contravenes any provision in this Part of this Act shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding the fifth level on the standard scale or to both and, where the contravention involves a public display or exhibition of any name or description, there shall be a fresh contravention on each day on which the person causes or permits the display or exhibition to continue.
