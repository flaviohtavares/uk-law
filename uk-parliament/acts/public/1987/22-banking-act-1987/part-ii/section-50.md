# Banking Act 1987, section 50

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. The Deposit Protection Board.[^c9370391][^c9370401][^c9370411]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1) The body corporate known as the Deposit Protection Board and the Fund known as the Deposit Protection Fund established by section 21 of the [^c9370421]Banking Act 1979 shall continue to exist.

(2) The Deposit Protection Board (in this Part of this Act referred to as “**the Board**”) shall—

- (a) hold, manage and apply the Fund in accordance with the provisions of this Part of this Act;
- (b) levy contributions for the Fund, in accordance with those provisions, from contributory institutions.[^c9370431]; and
- (c) have such other functions as are conferred on the Board by those provisions.

(3) Schedule 4 to this Act shall have effect with respect to the Board.
