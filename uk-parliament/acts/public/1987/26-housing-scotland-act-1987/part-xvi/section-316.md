## 316. Confirmation of byelaws.

*Extent: S*

For the purposes of section 202 of the [^c12779481]Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 (which relates to the procedure and other matters connected with the making of byelaws) the Secretary of State shall be the person by whom byelaws made under this Act are to be confirmed.
