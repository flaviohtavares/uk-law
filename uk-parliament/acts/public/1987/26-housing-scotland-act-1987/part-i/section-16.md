## 16. Disposal of land for erection of churches, etc.

*Extent: S*

Where a local authority, in the exercise of any power conferred on them by this Act, dispose of land to any person for the erection of a church or other building for religious worship or buildings ancillary thereto, then, unless the parties otherwise agree, such disposal shall be by way of disposition[^key-8939c24942a283169a56c7963fa55dad].
