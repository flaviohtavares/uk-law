# Housing (Scotland) Act 1987, section 19

## 19. Admission to housing list[^c12770181]

*Extent: S*

(1) An applicant for housing held by a social landlord[^key-40a017ce7b96cbae093e18b05218e511] is entitled to be admitted to a housing list unless the applicant is under 16 years of age.

(2) In this section, “**housing list**” means a list of applicants for housing which is kept by any social landlord[^key-44bbc79acdde2225d340ab523b9db0a9] or jointly by or on behalf of any two or more social landlords[^key-ae95dad40de9b59d9f03ede3831e842b] in connection with the allocation of housing held by it or them for housing purposes.

(3)[^key-99a9d31082c0f3e67c86ff59bf8aa455] In this Part, “**social landlord**” means any local authority or any registered social landlord.
