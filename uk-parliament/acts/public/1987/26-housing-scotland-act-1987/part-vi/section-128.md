## 128. Recovery of possession of house subject to statutorily regulated tenancy[^c-bc0894781aa6f646fae7261a4549a35e]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-66eceb02cb36ace2dc9bb661ad1cc481] Nothing in an enactment mentioned in subsection (2)[^c-cbd8db7edd345e94547a023ab3d04cff] shall be deemed to affect the provisions of this Act relating to obtaining possession of a house with respect to which a closing order, or a demolition order has been made or to which a resolution passed under section 125 applies, or to prevent possession being obtained—

- (a) of any house possession of which is required for the purpose of enabling a local authority to exercise their powers under any enactment relating to housing;
- (b) of any house possession of which is required for the purpose of securing compliance with any byelaws made for the prevention of overcrowding;
- (c) of any premises by any owner in a case where an undertaking has been given under this Part that those premises shall not be used for human habitation.

(2)[^c-558e60fd8426ddb814088cb2beacb614] The enactments referred to in subsection (1) are—

- (a) the Rent (Scotland) Act 1984;
- (b) Part II of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1988;
- (c) the Private Housing (Tenancies) (Scotland) Act 2016.
