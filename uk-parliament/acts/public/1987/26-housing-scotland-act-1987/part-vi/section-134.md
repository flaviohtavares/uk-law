## 134. Saving for telecommunication and gas apparatus.

*Extent: S*

Paragraph 23 of Schedule 2 to the [^c12776511]Telecommunications Act 1984 (code for cases where works involve the alteration of apparatus), as applied by paragraph 2(7) of Schedule 7 to the [^c12776521]Gas Act 1986 to gas apparatus, shall apply to a local authority for the purposes of any works which they are authorised to execute under this Part.
