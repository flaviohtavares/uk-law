# Housing (Scotland) Act 1987, section 83

## 83. Members of a person’s family.[^c12775931][^c12775921]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person is a member of another’s family for the purposes of this Act if—

- (a) he is the spouse or civil partner[^key-aa228a1a63d7177b77466d9563b691dc] of that person or he and that person live together as husband and wife or in a relationship which has the characteristics of the relationship between husband and wife except that the persons are of the same sex[^c12775941]; or
- (b) he is that person’s parent, grandparent, child, grandchild, brother, sister, uncle, aunt, nephew or niece.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1)(b)—

- (a) a relationship by marriage or by virtue of civil partnership[^key-370ca2bffbbb19c42aafaa414b1e9268] shall be treated as a relationship by blood;
- (b) a relationship of the half-blood shall be treated as a relationship of the whole blood;
- (c) the stepchild of a person shall be treated as his child; and
- (ca)[^c12775951] a person brought up or treated by another person as if the person were the child of the other person shall be treated as that person’s child;
- (d) a child shall be treated as such whether or not his parents are married.

(3)[^c12775961] Except in subsection (1)(a), references in this Act to a person’s spouse include references to that person’s civil partner or to[^key-a59b35b7ab30683a4ea67500936d5d89] another person living together with that person as husband and wife or in a relationship which has the characteristics of the relationship between husband and wife except that the persons are of the same sex.
