# Family Law Reform Act 1987, section 17

> Text as at 2019-12-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 17. Abolition of affiliation proceedings.

*Extent: E+W*

The [^c1014282]Affiliation Proceedings Act 1957 (the provisions of which are superseded by this Part) shall cease to have effect.
