# Family Law Reform Act 1987, section 31

> Text as at 2019-12-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 31. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W*

In this Act—

- “**the 1953 Act**” means the [^c1014307]Births and Deaths Registration Act 1953;
- “**the 1971 Act**” means the [^c1014308]Guardianship of Minors Act 1971;
- “**the 1973 Act**” means the [^c1014309]Guardianship Act 1973.
