# Colonial Probates (Protected States and Mandated Territories) Act 1927

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Colonial Probates (Protected States and Mandated Territories) Act 1927

1927 c.43

An Act to enable the Colonial Probates Act, 1892, to be applied to certain Protected States and Mandated Territories.

\[22nd December 1927\][^c585910]

## 1. Extension of Colonial Probates Act, 1892, to certain protected states and mandated territories.

The power of His Majesty to apply the [^c585911]Colonial Probates Act, 1892, to British possessions shall include a power to apply the said Act to any territories, being either territories under His Majesty’s protection or territories in respect of which a mandate on behalf of the League of Nations has been accepted by His Majesty, to which it cannot be applied by virtue of the provisions of the [^c585912]Foreign Jurisdiction Acts, 1890 [^c585913]and 1913, and the said Act shall accordingly have effect as if references therein to British possessions included references to such territories as aforesaid.

## 2. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Colonial Probates (Protected States and Mandated Territories) Act, 1927, and the [^c585914]Colonial Probates Act, 1892, and this Act may be cited together as the Colonial Probates Acts, 1892 and 1927.
