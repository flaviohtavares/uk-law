## 7. Interpretation of sections 4 to 6.[^c1004321][^key-23d1b9a206cc7c8efe58762f0585a7b8]

*Extent: S*

(1) In sections 4 to 6 of this Act “**lease**” means a lease of land, whether entered into before or after the commencement of those sections, but does not include a lease of land—

- (a) used wholly or mainly for residential purposes; [^key-253ecfd56305cea3d898a763a4d867ad]...
- (b) comprising [^key-253ecfd56305cea3d898a763a4d867ad]... a croft, the subject of a cottar or the holding of a landholder or a statutory small tenant; or[^key-37bbd32863735cc13eb633b9587fa853]
- (c) where the lease is an agricultural lease.

(2) In subsection (1) above—

- “[^key-fa1828c159db51b939f60fad42ac3428]**agricultural lease**” means a lease constituting a 1991 Act tenancy within the meaning of the Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 11) or a lease constituting a short limited duration tenancy , a limited duration tenancy, a modern limited duration tenancy or a repairing tenancy[^key-59f6e1ebdcd3d8977db5a3bc8b24df50] (within the meaning of that Act);
- “**cottar**” has the same meaning as in section 28(4) of the [^c1004323]Crofters (Scotland) Act 1955;
- “**croft**” has the same meaning as in section 3 of the Crofters (Scotland) Act 1955; and
- “**holding**” (in relation to a landholder or statutory small tenant), “**landholder**” and “**statutory small tenant**” have the same meanings as in the Small Landholders (Scotland) Acts 1886 to 1931.
