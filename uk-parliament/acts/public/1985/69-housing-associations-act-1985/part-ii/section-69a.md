# Housing Associations Act 1985, section 69A

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 69A (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Land subject to housing management agreement. (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ffb89abdd4cc56787b6ff554eabf6a58][^c11838421][^c-ffb89abdd4cc56787b6ff554eabf6a58]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A housing association is not entitled to a *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffb89abdd4cc56787b6ff554eabf6a58] grant under section 50 (housing association grant) or section 51 (revenue deficit grant) of the Housing Act 1988[^c11838431] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffb89abdd4cc56787b6ff554eabf6a58] in respect of land comprised in— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffb89abdd4cc56787b6ff554eabf6a58]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ffb89abdd4cc56787b6ff554eabf6a58] a management agreement within the meaning of the Housing Act 1985 (see sections 27(2) and 27B(4) of that Act: delegation of housing management functions by certain authorities), or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffb89abdd4cc56787b6ff554eabf6a58]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ffb89abdd4cc56787b6ff554eabf6a58] an agreement to which section *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffb89abdd4cc56787b6ff554eabf6a58] 22 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 1987[^c11838441] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffb89abdd4cc56787b6ff554eabf6a58] applies (agreements for exercise by housing co-operatives of certain local authority housing functions). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffb89abdd4cc56787b6ff554eabf6a58]
