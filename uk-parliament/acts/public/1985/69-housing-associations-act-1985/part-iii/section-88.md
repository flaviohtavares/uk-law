# Housing Associations Act 1985, section 88

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 88. Acquisition of land.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers[^key-3397ebfea0e43cfd68c117aacd7ea3ed] may acquire land by agreement for the purpose of—

- (a) selling or leasing it to a registered social landlord[^c11842561] or an unregistered self-build society, or
- (b) providing dwellings (for letting or for sale) or hostels,

and the Welsh Ministers may acquire land compulsorily for any such purpose.[^key-af19fe42c002a014847c0224ca3f71fd]

(2) Land may be so acquired by the Welsh Ministers[^key-dc3b9ec72be2b3dc58a6682d4838743d] notwithstanding that it is not immediately required for any such purpose.

(3)[^key-bf80df81d93f01f8d14f800dbee741b4] The Acquisition of Land Act 1981 applies to the compulsory purchase of land under this section.
