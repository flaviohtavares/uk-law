# Housing Associations Act 1985, section 83

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 83. Power to guarantee loans.[^key-b8f99bf64d39a7744ee2117fb6a42c6a][^key-451df511dd6544f261105d2a40842602]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W))[^key-5eef95c966f2189c567e545236e3424f] The Welsh Ministers may guarantee the repayment of the principal of, and the payment of interest on, sums borrowed by registered social landlords or unregistered self-build societies. *(Extent: E+W)*

(2 (Extent: E+W)) A guarantee may be subject to terms and conditions. *(Extent: E+W)*

(3A)[^c11841081] The aggregate amount outstanding in respect of—

- (a) loans for which the Welsh Ministers (or National Assembly for Wales, Secretary of State or Housing for Wales) have *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-f6b7f484371b65af1f1da37def7f5fe2] given a guarantee under this section, and
- (b) payments made by the Welsh Ministers (or National Assembly for Wales, Secretary of State or Housing for Wales) in *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-9350b3ca10b548273450adfa460978e1] meeting an obligation arising by virtue of such a guarantee and not repaid to the Welsh Ministers (or National Assembly for Wales, Secretary of State or Housing for Wales) *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-4c724524b1199f74b5c4abe95366d83c],

shall not exceed £30 million or such greater sum not exceeding £50 million as the Welsh Ministers may specify by order *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-38e17c6dc898e0c1e8ba8a6b0914d727].

(4 (Extent: E+W))[^key-80fcbc034dfe719c28b4c851f4a31a40] An order under subsection (3A)— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) shall be made by statutory instrument, and *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) shall not be made unless, a draft of the order has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the National Assembly for Wales. *(Extent: E+W)*
