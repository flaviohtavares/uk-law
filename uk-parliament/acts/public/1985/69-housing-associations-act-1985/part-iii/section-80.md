# Housing Associations Act 1985, section 80

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 80. Security for loans to unregistered self-build societies.[^key-7b1e5a748fd604579de41722edd50845][^key-a13f66991737b5a820b81c3458b09dc9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where the Welsh Ministers *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-0771435f75f899bd4077b6f824baa74c]—

- (a) make *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-d5a34de5a1b9ea14bb52be25f11f7c2c] a loan to an unregistered self-build society under section 79(1); and
- (b) under a mortgage [^key-1a434b888931fe7b5ed04673a2d2f80c]... entered into by the society to secure the loan have *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-b70fac321b72ad4dfff531568b8dea13] an interest as mortgagee or creditor in land belonging to the society,

they may *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-fefe890f658d71b7adbb1e6179982193] [^c11840741]. . . give the society directions with respect to the disposal of the land.

(2) The society shall comply with directions so given so long as the Welsh Ministers continue *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-b7e8de5309b5a215d768e85abb3a03c1] to have such an interest in the land.

(3) Directions so given may be varied or revoked by subsequent directions [^c11840761]... .

(3A)[^key-37aeaa2623dca15422c186d62049e704] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The Welsh Ministers shall not give directions under this section requiring a society to transfer its interest in land to them or any other person unless they are satisfied *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-bc6bf62202bbcbe21a8bedc85c79af47] that arrangements have been made which will secure that the members of the society receive fair treatment in connection with the transfer.
