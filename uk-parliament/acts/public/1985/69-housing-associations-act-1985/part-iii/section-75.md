# Housing Associations Act 1985, section 75

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 75. General functions[^key-6cce09e997cc265296748ac467ad7fd1][^key-992c48b8a2e7a2824e1e34151e4ceab1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers[^key-8021c294a0cfcddcd00fb8f9ebca9591] have[^key-5fc8a81d5fd28862984622c6bd67fdef] the following general functions—

- (a)[^c11840031] to facilitate the proper performance of the functions of registered social landlords;
- (b) to maintain a register of social landlords and to exercise supervision and control over such persons;
- (c) to promote and assist the development of self-build societies (other than registered social landlords) and to facilitate the proper performance of the functions, and to publicise the aims and principles, of such societies;
- (d) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c11840041]
- (e) to undertake, to such extent as the Welsh Ministers consider[^key-0fdd66282a2bc5099c749745f2791202] necessary, the provision (by construction, acquisition, conversion, improvement or otherwise) of dwellings for letting or for sale and of hostels, and the management of dwellings or hostels so provided.
- (f)[^key-907435feb165b3caf3ce1299b291c3e6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1A)[^key-afdf3caef2dff4a89e00b2ad7aea4949] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1B)[^key-afdf3caef2dff4a89e00b2ad7aea4949] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The Welsh Ministers[^M_F_a005c2a7-5744-43d4-f18b-46c2b8e3346b] shall exercise their[^key-47e52884d970d995f013c5f96fd493e8] general functions subject to and in accordance with the provisions of this Act and Part I of the Housing Act 1996[^c11840081].

(3) Subsection (1) is without prejudice to specific functions conferred on the Welsh Ministers[^M_F_f932d699-caeb-4087-8d9d-99024c80f5ed] by or under this Act or Part I of the Housing Act 1996[^c11840101].

(4) The Welsh Ministers[^M_F_6b62502a-7c80-4a7a-f7d4-19ac1bee4e36] may do such things and enter into such transactions as are incidental to or conducive to the exercise of any of their[^key-0fc0c85af15584ffd70c77c7e2d2e1f7] functions, general or specific, under this Act or Part I of the Housing Act 1996.

(5)[^c11840131] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^key-f34a07edcbfb48b5d8d2593d100fcbb6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
