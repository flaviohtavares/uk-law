# Housing Associations Act 1985, section 35

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 35. Housing trusts: power to transfer housing to local housing authority.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A housing trust may—

- (a) sell or lease to the local housing authority the houses provided by the trust, or
- (b) make over to the authority the management of the houses.

(2) So far as subsection (1) confers power to dispose of land—

- (za)[^key-f8ac88cf1f516863ba7e1205c9215441] it does not apply to private registered providers of social housing (on whom power to dispose of land is conferred by section 171 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008);
- (a)[^c11836301] it does not apply to registered social landlords (on whom power to dispose of land is conferred by section 8 of the Housing Act 1996);.
- (b) it has effect subject to section 9 (dispositions requiring consent of Corporation[^c11836311]) where the housing trust is an unregistered housing association and the land is grant-aided land (as defined in Schedule 1); and
- (c) it has effect subject to sections 117 to 121 of the Charities Act 2011 (restrictions on dispositions of charity land)[^key-20dafaaa9a582d0d7a50a20a1833a057] where the housing trust is a charity.
