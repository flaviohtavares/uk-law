# Housing Associations Act 1985, section 2B

> Text as at 2023-12-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2B. Meaning of “registered housing association”, “registered social landlord” etc.[^c11831581]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “registered housing association” means a housing association registered in the register of social landlords maintained under[^c11831591] section 20(1) of the Housing (Scotland) Act[^key-2c3d1c94775324d4a661d2023d182996] [2010 (asp 17)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2010/17),
- “registered social landlord” has the same meaning as in Part I of the Housing Act 1996, and[^c11831601]
- “unregistered”, in relation to a housing association, means *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbd9c93c5dafcfae2597e71b453cdcf3] not[^key-2073923e1f8eca6bf4703386dc084701] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbd9c93c5dafcfae2597e71b453cdcf3] registered in the register of social landlords maintained under *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbd9c93c5dafcfae2597e71b453cdcf3] section 20(1) of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2010 (asp 17)[^key-5fec0ee837f81ea2cdc03d9bea1d55f1] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbd9c93c5dafcfae2597e71b453cdcf3]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbd9c93c5dafcfae2597e71b453cdcf3]
- “unregistered”, in relation to a housing association, means— *(Extent: E+W)*
  - (a) not registered as a provider of social housing under Part 2 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, *(Extent: E+W)*
  - (b) not registered as a social landlord under Part 1 of the Housing Act 1996, and *(Extent: E+W)*
  - (c) not registered as a social landlord under *(Extent: E+W)* Part 2 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2010 (asp 17)[^key-ba192c8e7f264c1f9398c65d287c255c] *(Extent: E+W)*. *(Extent: E+W)*
