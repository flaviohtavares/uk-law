## 105. Members of a persons family

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E.W.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3] A person is a member of another's family if— *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3]

- (a (Extent: E.W.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3] he is the spouse *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3] or civil partner *(Extent: S)*[^c-67dd2968c8a4743ec689d22d9a5855f6] *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3] of that person, or he and that person live together as husband and wife, *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3] or in a relationship which has the characteristics of the relationship between civil partners *(Extent: S)*[^c-27a7bf67cab440b1cd6e894125f74d72] *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3] or *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3]
- (b (Extent: E.W.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3] he is that person's parent, grandparent, child, grand- child, brother, sister, uncle, aunt, nephew or niece. *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3]

(2 (Extent: E.W.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3] For the purposes of subsection (1)(b)— *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3]

- (a (Extent: E.W.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3] a relationship by marriage *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3] or civil partnership *(Extent: S)*[^c-e636eb52176a955c746400cc26dd331d] *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3] shall be treated as a relation- ship by blood, *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3]
- (b (Extent: E.W.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3] a relationship of the half-blood shall be treated as a relationship of the whole blood, *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3]
- (c (Extent: E.W.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3] the stepchild of a person shall be treated as his child, and *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3]
- (d (Extent: E.W.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3] an illegitimate child shall be treated as the legitimate child of his mother and reputed father. *(Extent: E.W.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ba1762d9-2620-4c18-87be-f9d155dd5ca3]
