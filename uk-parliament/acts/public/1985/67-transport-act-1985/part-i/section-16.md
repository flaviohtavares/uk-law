# Transport Act 1985, section 16

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (54).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/67

## 16. Taxi licensing: control of numbers.[^c-054eeca6cb27c8d3366d83a16a30c4df]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The provisions of the Town Police Clauses Act 1847 with respect to hackney carriages, as incorporated in any enactment (whenever passed), shall (subject to section 161 of the Equality Act 2010)[^c-58928c22be354ace46baad3502e08d4e] have effect—

- (a) as if in section 37, the words “such number of” and “as they think fit” were omitted; and
- (b) as if they provided that the grant of a licence may be refused, for the purpose of limiting the number of hackney carriages in respect of which licences are granted, if, but only if, the person authorised to grant licences is satisfied that there is no significant demand for the services of hackney carriages (within the area to which the licence would apply) which is unmet.
