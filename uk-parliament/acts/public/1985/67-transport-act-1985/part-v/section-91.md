# Transport Act 1985, section 91

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (54).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/67
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Exceptions from section 89.[^key-eba55380aaf2978907d4dc59f5bdd55a][^key-921c179b3e022dc12590e5aadfd15866][^key-eba55380aaf2978907d4dc59f5bdd55a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c19428581] Regulations may provide for excluding from section 89(1) of this Act agreements of any description specified in the regulations; and any such description may be framed by reference to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]

- (a) the description of service to which the agreement relates; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]
- (b) the description of persons proposing to operate the service; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]
- (c) the period for which the service is to be provided under the agreement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]
- (d) the aggregate amount of the service subsidies provided for under the agreement; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]
- (e) any other relevant circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]

(2) Section 89(1) of this Act shall not apply in any case where it appears to an authority responsible for expenditure on public passenger transport services that action is urgently required for the purpose of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]

- (a) maintaining an existing service; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]
- (b) securing the provision of a service in place of a service which has ceased to operate; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]
- (c) securing the provision of a service to meet any public transport requirement which has arisen unexpectedly and ought in the opinion of the authority to be met without delay; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]

and that it is necessary for that purpose to enter into an agreement providing for service subsidies in order to secure that service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]

(3)[^key-5c6386d433cd25796c0ebb5aea741a8d] Where by virtue of subsection (2) above any such authority enter into an agreement to which section 89(1) of this Act does not apply, the authority shall as soon as possible invite tenders for the provision of the service which is the subject of that agreement for such period and on such basis as may be specified in the invitation to tender; and sections 89(3) to (8) and 90 of this Act shall apply in any such case as if the invitation had been issued under section 89(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]

(4)[^key-5c6386d433cd25796c0ebb5aea741a8d] Any agreement entered into by virtue of subsection (2) above shall be made so as to remain in force no later than the end of the period of three months beginning with the day immediately following the end of the period allowed for the submission of tenders in accordance with the invitation to tender issued under section (3) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]

(5) Subject to the following provisions of this section, where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]

- (a) an invitation to tender for the provision of any service is issued under section 89(2) of this Act or subsection (3) above; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]
- (b) no tender, or no tender which the authority issuing the invitation consider acceptable, is submitted in response to that invitation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]

any power of that authority to enter into an agreement providing for service subsidies in order to secure that service shall cease to be subject to section 89(1) of this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]

(6) Any agreement which by virtue of subsection (5) above is entered into by an authority responsible for expenditure on public passenger transport services otherwise than by acceptance of a tender invited in pursuance of section 89 or subsection (3) above shall be made so as to remain in force no later than the end of the period specified in pursuance of section 90(1) of this Act in the invitation to tender mentioned in subsection (5)(a) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]

(7) On entering into any such agreement an authority shall publish in such manner as may be prescribed either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]

- (a) a statement that no tender was submitted in response to that invitation to tender; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]
- (b) a statement of their reasons for considering that no tender so submitted was acceptable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]

as the case may require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428581]
