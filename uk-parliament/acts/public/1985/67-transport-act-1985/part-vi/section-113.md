# Transport Act 1985, section 113

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (54).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/67
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 113. Repeal of section 24(3) of the 1968 Act.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Section 24(3) of the 1968 Act (which imposes on the National Bus Company, the Railways Board and the Scottish Transport Group a duty to co-operate with one another for the purpose of co-ordinating the passenger transport services provided respectively by them or by their subsidiaries) shall cease to have effect.

(2) In subsection (4) of that section, for the words “Subsections (2) and (3)” there shall be substituted the words “Subsection (2)”.
