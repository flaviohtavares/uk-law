# Transport Act 1985, section 72

## 72. The public transport companies and their controlling authorities.[^key-27890099608df53aa49f7782fbd18013][^key-2900f3636721a83870c26a26d667bb60][^key-27890099608df53aa49f7782fbd18013]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) References in this Part of this Act to a public transport company are references to any of the following—

- (a) any company which was formed under section 59 of this Act by the Passenger Transport Executive for any integrated transport area or *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-f7ffa455446058416a18b471dac8a72e] passenger transport area and is for the time being a subsidiary of that Executive or of the Integrated Transport Authority or *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-867f276f3c02ae7e4277c18b05c65eff] Passenger Transport Authority for that area;
- (b) any company which was formed under section 61 of this Act by the Integrated Transport Authority for any integrated transport area or *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-a37993c9364340b76423548b840cd028] by the Passenger Transport Authority for any passenger transport area and is for the time being a subsidiary of that Authority; and
- (c) any company which was formed by one or more councils under section 67 of this Act and is for the time being under local authority control. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6ea7f89752270d87e66272bed445bc12]

(2) A company formed under section 67 of this Act shall be treated for the purposes of subsection (1)(c) above as under local authority control at any time when either— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc680d726f52451c1a3e57278684f4d6]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-dc680d726f52451c1a3e57278684f4d6] it is a subsidiary of a single district council in England and Wales or, in Scotland, of a single *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc680d726f52451c1a3e57278684f4d6] council (other than the council for Orkney Islands, Shetland Islands or Western Isles)[^c10879981] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc680d726f52451c1a3e57278684f4d6] or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc680d726f52451c1a3e57278684f4d6]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-dc680d726f52451c1a3e57278684f4d6] if two or more such councils who are members of the company were a single body corporate, it would be a subsidiary of that body corporate. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc680d726f52451c1a3e57278684f4d6]

(3) References in this Part of this Act to a public transport company’s controlling authority—

- (a) in relation to a company within subsection (1)(a) or (b) above, are references to the Passenger Transport Executive or (as the case may be) the Integrated Transport Authority or *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-84e3eb2fe104a3b7ec2b5582b394f4ed] the Passenger Transport Authority of whom it is a subsidiary; and
- (b) in relation to a company within subsection (1)(c) above, are references to the council or councils referred to in subsection (2) above. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a4f5a44ed789ce10c8ed6c222a0d8a00]

(4) References in this Part of this Act to a composite authority are references to a controlling authority consisting of two or more such councils as are referred to in subsection (2) above, and the councils concerned are referred to as the component councils of that authority.

(5) For the purposes of this Part of this Act a public transport company is an associated company—

- (a) in relation to an Integrated Transport Authority or *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-ad0cd68f24a2d5f123d93be5408a7d0f] a Passenger Transport Authority if that Authority or the Passenger Transport Executive for that Authority’s area are its controlling authority;
- (b) in relation to a Passenger Transport Executive if that Executive or the Integrated Transport Authority or *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-d6e0b2cf01ced5407345140c4359b022] Passenger Transport Authority for that Executive’s area are its controlling authority; and
- (c) in relation to a district council in England and Wales or, in Scotland, a *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b92fafe3730e6bc8b115df34ee668d50] council (other than the council for Orkney Islands, Shetland Islands or Western Isles) *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b92fafe3730e6bc8b115df34ee668d50] if that council are its controlling authority or one of the component councils of a composite authority who are its controlling authority. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b92fafe3730e6bc8b115df34ee668d50]
