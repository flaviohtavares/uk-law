## 67. Formation of companies to run council bus undertakings.[^key-27890099608df53aa49f7782fbd18013][^key-930bf82a8e2261d421e3d171b3b4ca8d][^key-27890099608df53aa49f7782fbd18013]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] Where an order is made under section 66(2) of this Act, the council or (as the case may be) each of the councils to whom the order applies shall, before the end of that council’s preparatory period, form (whether alone or jointly with any other council operating a bus undertaking) one or more companies for the purpose of carrying on— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] activities of any description included among the activities of the bus undertaking of that council or (as the case may be) of any of the councils concerned in the formation of the company or companies in question; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] activities of any other description included among the activities of any joint undertaking of which that council’s bus undertaking, or (as the case may be) the bus undertaking of any council so concerned, forms part; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] any other activities which appear to the council or (as the case may be) to both or all of the councils so concerned to be incidental to or connected with any activities within paragraph (a) or (b) above or to be capable of being conveniently carried on in association with any such activities. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] Any company formed under this section shall be a company limited by shares registered under the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] [^c10879971]Companies Act 1985. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] Subject to subsections (4) and (5) below, a council’s bus undertaking shall be regarded for the purposes of this Part of this Act as forming part of a joint undertaking if the services for the carriage of passengers by road provided in the course of the activities of the council’s bus undertaking are wholly or mainly provided under any agreement for— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] the provision or operation of those services by a body acting on the joint behalf of that council and one or more other councils; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] the operation of those services by any company operating those services in conjunction with services for the carriage of passengers by road provided by that company. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] Subsection (3)(a) above only applies where the agreement provides for the distribution among the parties on its termination of— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] all assets, or the proceeds of all assets, used or appropriated for use for the purpose of providing services for the carriage of passengers by road under the agreement; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] all such assets or proceeds excluding only land or the proceeds of disposal of land. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

(5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] Subsection (3)(b) above only applies where— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] the agreement includes provision for securing that the parties on termination of the agreement hold shares of equal value in assets required to be brought into account on termination of the agreement; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] those assets include all assets so used or appropriated for use. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

(6 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] References in this Part of this Act, in relation to a council whose bus undertaking forms part of a joint undertaking, to the joint undertaking are references to all activities carried on, or (according to the context) to all property used or appropriated for use and all rights and liabilities subsisting for the purposes of any activities carried on, in pursuance of the agreement by reference to which that council falls within subsection (3) above. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
