## 66. Exclusion of powers of certain councils to run bus undertakings.[^key-27890099608df53aa49f7782fbd18013][^key-930bf82a8e2261d421e3d171b3b4ca8d][^key-27890099608df53aa49f7782fbd18013]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c10879931][^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] Subject to section 71 of this Act and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] subsections (1A) and[^key-78f0964d2abd403aa41389b0c2d81348] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] (2) below *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] and to sections 123O and[^key-efedbfd395f7a0193b649f50fbcab04c] *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-05299a9c4499a523e85e1814036663d7] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] 132C of the Transport Act 2000 *(Extent: E+W)* *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20], but notwithstanding anything in any other statutory provision, a non-metropolitan district council in England *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] [^key-ad22ce955a59bd97967999fc5c405cd5]... or, in Scotland, a *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] council (other than the council for Orkney Islands, Shetland Islands or Western Isles)[^c10879951] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] shall not have power to provide a service for the carriage of passengers by road which requires a PSV operator’s licence. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

(1A (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a6e3abd5925cbcf15c904e1a88abd19d][^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] Subsection (1) does not prevent a non-metropolitan district council in England from forming a company for the purpose of providing a local service. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c10879931][^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] Subsection (1) above shall not have effect in relation to any council who, at the time when this section comes into force, are providing any such service until the end of such period as may be specified by order made by the Secretary of State. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

References below in this Part of this Act to a council operating a bus undertaking are references to any council to whom this subsection applies. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] Any order under subsection (2) above may apply to all councils within that subsection who are not for the time being exempt by virtue of section 71 of this Act from subsection (1) above, to any class of such councils, or to any such council specified in the order; and different periods may be specified by any such order in relation to different councils or classes of councils to whom it applies. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] Any order under subsection (2) above shall, in relation to every period specified in the order for the purposes of that subsection (“the primary period”), specify also a period ending before the primary period as the period allowed to councils to whom the primary period applies for complying with such of the requirements of sections 67 to 69 of this Act as are applicable to them. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

(5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] A period specified by virtue of subsection (4) above in an order under subsection (2) above is referred to in those sections, in relation to any council to whom it applies, as the council’s preparatory period. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

(6 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] Any order amending a previous order under subsection (2) above, in so far as it extends any period specified in the previous order for the purposes of that subsection, may be framed so as to have effect from a date earlier than the making of the order. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

(7 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] For the purposes of this Part of this Act— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] a service for the carriage of passengers by road is a service which requires a PSV operator’s licence if vehicles used in providing the service are used in such circumstances that a PSV operator’s licence is required in respect of that use; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] any council who have made (whether alone or jointly with any other authority or authorities) arrangements under any enactment for the discharge by any other authority or person of that council’s functions with respect to the operation of any such service shall be taken to be providing that service at any time when it is being provided under those arrangements; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] references, in relation to any council operating a bus undertaking, to the council’s bus undertaking are references (according to the context) to— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
  - (i (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] all activities carried on, whether by the council themselves or by any other authority or person in pursuance of any such arrangements as are mentioned in paragraph (b) above or otherwise, in or for the purposes of the provision by the council of any such service; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
  - (ii (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] all property of the council used or appropriated for use and all rights and liabilities of the council subsisting for the purposes of any such activities. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
