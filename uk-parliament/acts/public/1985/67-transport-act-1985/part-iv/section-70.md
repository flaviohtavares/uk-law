## 70. Supplementary provisions with respect to orders under section 69.[^key-27890099608df53aa49f7782fbd18013][^key-930bf82a8e2261d421e3d171b3b4ca8d][^key-27890099608df53aa49f7782fbd18013]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] Any order under section 69 of this Act may contain such supplementary, incidental and consequential provisions as the Secretary of State thinks necessary or expedient for the purposes of the order, and in particular (but without prejudice to the generality of that) may include provision— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] for the assumption by any council participating in the joint undertaking and by any person to whom any property, rights and liabilities are transferred under the order of such liabilities to one another as may be determined by or under the order to be appropriate having regard to the financial arrangements of that council before the severance from the other activities of the council of the joint undertaking or (as the case may be) of any activities of that council for the purposes of the joint undertaking; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] for the settlement by a court or otherwise of any dispute or other matter arising in connection with the order; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] for making in any statutory provision relating to, or to a class of undertakings which includes, the joint undertaking, such amendments or repeals as may appear to the Secretary of State to be required in consequence of any transfer under the order; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
- (d (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] for the making by any person to whom any property, rights and liabilities are transferred under the order to any council participating in the joint undertaking of payments by way of contributions to the cost of any adjustments arising from the severance mentioned in paragraph (a) above; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
- (e (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] with respect to the consideration to be provided by any such person for any transfer under the order. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] Subject to subsection (3) below, any property, rights and liabilities for the transfer of which provision is made by an order under section 69 of this Act shall be transferred and vest in accordance with the order on such date or dates as may be appointed by the order for that purpose. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] Subject to the following provisions of this section, Schedule 4 to the 1968 Act (supplementary provisions as to certain transfers of property, rights and liabilities) shall apply to any transfer under subsection (2) above; and subsection (2) above shall have effect subject to the provisions of that Schedule. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] In Schedule 4 as it applies by virtue of subsection (3) above— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] any reference to a transfer by or a vesting by virtue of that Act shall be read as a reference to a transfer by or a vesting by virtue of the order; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] the reference in paragraph 13(5) to the relevant provisions of that Act shall be read as including a reference to the relevant provisions of this Act. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]

(5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20] Any order under section 69 of this Act may make modifications in Schedule 4 for the purposes of its application to a transfer effected by that order. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e0c5110b95e4b7d04344325a98dce20]
