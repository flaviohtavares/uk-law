# Finance Act 1985, section 3

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Hydrocarbon oil.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 6(1) of the [^c10029321]Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act 1979 (rates of duty on hydrocarbon oil) for “£0.1716” (light oil) and “£0.1448” (heavy oil) there shall be substituted “£0.1794” and “£0.1515” respectively.

(2) This section shall be deemed to have come into force at 6 o’clock in the evening of 19th March 1985.
