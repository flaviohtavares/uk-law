# Finance Act 1985, section 6

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Miscellaneous amendments relating to spirits and beer.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The [^c10029471]Alcoholic Liquor Duties Act 1979 shall have effect subject to the amendments in Schedule 3 to this Act (being amendments relating to spirits and beer).

(2)[^c10029481] Paragraphs 3 and 4 of Schedule 3 to this Act shall come into operation on such day as the Commissioners of Customs and Excise may by order made by statutory instrument appoint; and a different day may be so appointed for each of those paragraphs.
