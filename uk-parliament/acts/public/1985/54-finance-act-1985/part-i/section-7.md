# Finance Act 1985, section 7

> Text as at 2022-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Hydrocarbon oil: mixing etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 4 to this Act (which contains provisions about mixing hydrocarbon oil etc.) shall have effect.

(2)[^c10029491] That Schedule shall come into operation on such day as the Commissioners of Customs and Excise may by order made by statutory instrument appoint.
