# Representation of the People Act 1985, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2024-02-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/50

## 1. Extension of parliamentary franchise.[^c1039729]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694] A person is entitled to vote as an elector at a parliamentary election in any constituency if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

- (a) he qualifies as an overseas elector in respect of that constituency on the date on which he makes a declaration under and in accordance with section 2 of this Act (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]**the relevant date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]**”);
- (b) on that date and on the date of the poll— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
  - (i) he is not subject to any legal incapacity to vote, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694] he is a British citizen; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (c) on the date of the poll he is registered in a register of parliamentary electors for that constituency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

(2) For the purposes of this Act and the principal Act a person qualifies as an overseas elector in respect of a constituency on the relevant date if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

- (a) on that date he is not resident in the United Kingdom, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (b) he satisfies one of the following sets of conditions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

(3)[^key-b17f5f38c6852d46052d01c229fb524e][^key-7a3bbeb4b3ba3daa13e14161d7118a4a][^c1039730] The first set of conditions is that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

- (a) he was included in a register of parliamentary electors in respect of an address at a place that is situated within the constituency concerned, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (b) that entry in the register was made on the basis that he was resident, or to be treated for the purposes of registration as resident, at that address, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (c) that entry in the register was in force at any time falling within the period of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694] 15 years[^c1039731] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694] ending immediately before the relevant date, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (d) subsequent to that entry ceasing to have effect no entry was made in any register of parliamentary electors on the basis that he was resident, or to be treated for the purposes of registration as resident, at any other address. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

(4) The second set of conditions is that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

- (a) he was last resident in the United Kingdom within the period of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694] 15 years *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694] ending immediately before the relevant date, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (b)[^key-b17f5f38c6852d46052d01c229fb524e][^key-a36064cf3784c22f196f893711a9c2a9][^c1039730] he was by reason only of his age incapable of being included in any register of parliamentary electors in force on the last day on which he was resident in the United Kingdom, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (c) the address at which he was resident on that day was at a place that is situated within the constituency concerned and a parent or guardian of his was included, in respect of that address, in a register of parliamentary electors or a register of local government electors in force on that day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

(5) The reference in subsection (1) above to a person being subject to a legal incapacity to vote on the relevant date does not include a reference to his being under the age of 18 on that date; and the reference in subsection (4) above to a register of local government electors includes a reference to a register of electors prepared for the purposes of local elections (within the meaning of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694] [^c1039734]Electoral Law Act (Northern Ireland) 1962). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

(1) A person is entitled to vote as an elector at a parliamentary election in a constituency if—

- (a) on the declaration date, the person—
  - (i) qualifies as an overseas elector in respect of that constituency (see section 1A),
  - (ii) is not subject to any legal incapacity to vote (age apart), and
  - (iii) is a British citizen, and
- (b) on the date of the poll, the person—
  - (i) is not subject to any legal incapacity to vote,
  - (ii) is a British citizen, and
  - (iii) is registered in a register of parliamentary electors for that constituency.

(2) In this section, “**the declaration date**” means—

- (a) the date on which the person makes a declaration under and in accordance with section 1C (overseas elector’s declaration), or
- (b) where the person makes a declaration under and in accordance with section 1E (renewal declaration), the date on which the person makes the declaration.
