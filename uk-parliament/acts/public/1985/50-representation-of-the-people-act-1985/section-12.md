# Representation of the People Act 1985, section 12

## 12. Offences as to declarations, etc.[^c1039930][^c1039931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c1039932][^c1039933][^c1039934][^c1039935][^c2006297] A person who makes a relevant declaration[^c-ef853ca4ae1545767ded33626c860861] or a declaration purporting to be a relevant declaration —

- (a) when he knows that he is subject to a legal incapacity to vote at parliamentary elections (age apart), or
- (b) when he knows that it contains a statement which is false,

is guilty of an offence.

(1A)[^c-dd824e304c4054ffd5107d91842d91e5] In subsection (1) “**relevant declaration**” means—

- (a) an overseas elector’s declaration;
- (b) a renewal declaration (within the meaning given by section 1D(2)).

(2)[^c1039932][^c1039933][^c1039934][^c1039935][^c2006297] A person who attests an overseas elector’s declaration or a declaration purporting to be an overseas elector’s declaration when he knows—

- (a) that he is not authorised to attest such a declaration, or
- (b) that it contains a statement which is false,

is guilty of an offence.

(3)[^c1039940][^c1039941][^c2014909] A person who makes a statement which he knows to be false in any declaration or form used for any of the purposes of sections 5 to 9 of this Act or attests an application under section 6 or 7 of this Act when he knows that he is not authorised to do so or that it contains a statement which is false is guilty of an offence.

(4)[^c1039942][^c1039943][^c1039944][^c1039940][^c1039935][^c2006297][^c2014909] A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
