## 2. Registration of British citizens overseas.[^c1039746][^c2006288][^c1039745][^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person is entitled to be registered in a register of parliamentary electors in pursuance of a declaration made by him under and in accordance with this section (an “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694] **overseas elector’s declaration *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]** ”) if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

- (a) the register is for the constituency orpart of the constituency within which is situated the place in the United Kingdom specified in the declaration in accordance with subsection (4) below as having been the address— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
  - (i) in respect of which he was registered, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694] at which he was resident, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

  as the case may be, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (b) the registration officer concerned is satisfied that, on the relevant date, he qualifies as an overseas elector in respect of that constituency for which that register is prepared. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

(2) A person registered in a register of parliamentary electors in pursuance of an overseas elector’s declaration is entitled to remain so registered until— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

- (a) the end of the period of 12 months beginning with the date when the entry in the register first takes effect, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c2006031][^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694] the registration officer determines in accordance with regulations that the person was not entitled to be registered, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (ab (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c99e9da5bfb23f84e5783a087da971b0][^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694] the registration officer determines in accordance with regulations that the person was registered as the result of an application under section 10ZC of the principal Act made by some other person or that the person's entry has been altered as the result of an application under section 10ZD of that Act made by some other person, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (b)[^c2006031] the declaration is cancelled under subsection (5) below, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (c) any entry made in respect of him in any register of parliamentary electors takes effect otherwise than in pursuance of an overseas elector’s declaration, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

whichever first occurs; and, where the entitlement of such a person to remain so registered terminates by virtue of this subsection, the registration officer concerned shall remove that person’s entry from the register, unless he is entitled to remain registered in pursuance of a further overseas elector’s declaration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

(3) An overseas elector’s declaration must state— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

- (a) the date of the declaration, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (b) that the declarant is a British citizen, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (c) that the declarant is not resident in the United Kingdom on the relevant date, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (d) when he ceased to be so resident or, in the case of a person relying on registration in pursuance of a service declaration, when he ceased to have a service qualification or, if later, ceased to be so resident, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

and must contain such other information and satisfy such other requirements (which may include requirements for declarations to be attested and for the charging of fees in respect of their attestation) as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

(3A)[^M_F_d54fb5a2-ef46-4896-8f52-ade957fab84e] An overseas elector's declaration that specifies an address in Northern Ireland under subsection (4) may, instead of or in addition to including a statement under subsection (3)(b), state that the declarant is an eligible Irish citizen. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

(4) An overseas elector’s declaration must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

- (a) show which set of conditions in section 1 of this Act the declarant claims to satisfy, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (b) in the case of the first set of conditions, specify the address in respect of which he was registered, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (c) in the case of the second set of conditions, specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
  - (i) the date of the declarant’s birth, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694] the address in the United Kingdom at which he was resident, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694] the name of the parent or guardian on whose registration in respect of that address he relies, and whether the person named was a parent or guardian, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

  and may not, in the case of either set of conditions, specify more than one such address; and if the declarant makes more than one such declaration bearing the same date and specifying different addresses in the United Kingdom as the address in respect of which he was registered or, as the case may be, at which he was resident the declarations shall be void. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

(5) An overseas elector’s declaration may be cancelled at any time by the declarant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

(6) An overseas elector’s declaration shall be of no effect unless it is received by the registration officer concerned within the period of three months beginning with the relevant date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

(7) For the purposes of section 1 of this Act, where a person is registered in a register of parliamentary electors for any constituency or part of a constituency in pursuance of an overseas elector’s declaration, it shall be conclusively presumed that he was not resident in the United Kingdom on the relevant date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

(8) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694] **the relevant date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]** ” has the meaning given by section 1(1)(a) of this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

(9)[^M_F_9ecf0f7c-05b4-4427-df44-9fc4dd0c5c87] In this section “eligible Irish citizen” means an Irish citizen who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

- (a) was born in Northern Ireland, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
- (b) qualifies as a British citizen (whether or not he identifies himself as such). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]

(10) A person found abandoned in Northern Ireland as a new-born infant is, unless the contrary is shown, deemed for the purposes of subsection (9) to have been born in Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b84f7ab215f48154d78316df6aada694]
