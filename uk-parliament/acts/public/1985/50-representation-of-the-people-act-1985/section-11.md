> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2024-02-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/50

## 11. Manner of voting: supplementary provision.[^c1039927][^c1039928]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 2 to this Act (which—

- (a) in Part I, makes amendments of the principal Act consequential on the provisions of sections 5 to 9 of this Act,
- (b)[^c1039929] . . . and
- (c) in Part III, makes provision as to absent voting at municipal elections in the City)

shall have effect.
