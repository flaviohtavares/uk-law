## 119. Qualifying period for right to buy.

*Extent: E+W*

(A1)[^key-26c51251319e3fb187746bfbc2d425d8] The[^key-f58f2209998b3b7c628a74358ddf11cf] right to buy does not arise unless the period which, in accordance with Schedule 4, is to be taken into account for the purposes of this section is at least three years.

(1)[^key-7a8c478d4e2c3fdf5620c8b414a8bad5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Where the secure tenancy is a joint tenancy the condition in subsection (A1)[^key-20254410375dbcc7d6af6daa4e1d35c3] [^key-fb0ce0d7c4b2762f0853d811b5a46dde]... need be satisfied with respect to one only of the joint tenants.
