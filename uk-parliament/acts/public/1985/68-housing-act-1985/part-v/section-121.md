## 121. Circumstances in which the right to buy cannot be exercised.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c19905281] The right to buy cannot be exercised if the tenant is subject to an order of the court for possession of the dwelling-house.

(2) The right to buy cannot be exercised if the person, or one of the persons, to whom the right to buy belongs—

- (a) has made a bankruptcy application that has not been determined or[^c-3828c07aaf05db1395bd44f9055fe78b] has a bankruptcy petition pending against him,
- (b)[^c15063821] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) is an undischarged bankrupt, [^c-d12764422033697baea7a7e26da86aef]...
- (d) has made a composition or arrangement with his creditors the terms of which remain to be fulfilled. or[^c-bf817e7669e1f8f701232ebcc0553612]
- (e) is a person in relation to whom a moratorium period under a debt relief order applies (under Part 7A of the Insolvency Act 1986).

(3)[^c19071291] The right to buy cannot be exercised at any time during the suspension period under an order made under section 121A in respect of the secure tenancy.
