## 180. Statutory declarations.[^c15067701]

*Extent: E+W*

A landlord, [^c15067711]. . .or the Secretary of State may, if the landlord, [^c15067711]. . . or Secretary of State thinks fit, accept a statutory declaration made for the purposes of this Part as sufficient evidence of the matters declared in it.
