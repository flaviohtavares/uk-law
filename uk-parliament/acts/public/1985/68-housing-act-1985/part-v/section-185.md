## 185. Meaning of “secure tenancy” and “secure tenant”.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) References in this Part to a secure tenancy or a secure tenant in relation to a time before 26th August 1984 are to a tenancy which would have been a secure tenancy if Chapter II of Part I of the [^c15067861]Housing Act 1980 and Part I of the [^c15067871]Housing and Building Control Act 1984 had then been in force or to a person who would then have been a secure tenant.

(2) For the purpose of determining whether a person would have been a secure tenant and his tenancy a secure tenancy—

- (a) a predecessor of a local authority shall be deemed to have been such an authority, and
- (b) a housing association shall be deemed to have been registered if it is or was a[^c15067881] private registered provider of social housing or[^c-affc5674f26bdf7ae32afd8291d055ad] registered social landlord at any later time.
