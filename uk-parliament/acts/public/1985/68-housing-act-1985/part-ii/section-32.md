## 32. Power to dispose of land held for purposes of this Part.[^c15057591]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Without prejudice to the provisions of Part V (the right to buy) [^c15057601]. . ., a local authority have power by this section, and not otherwise, to dispose of land held by them for the purposes of this Part.

(2)[^c15057611] A disposal under this section may be effected in any manner but, subject to subsections (3) and (3A) *(Extent: W)*[^c-9afbe9dae6a7dc6878982a8198044c88], shall not be made without the consent of the Secretary of State.

(3) No consent is required for the letting of land under a secure tenancy or an introductory tenancy[^c15057621] or under what would be a secure tenancy but for any of paragraphs 2 to 12 of Schedule 1 (tenancies, other than long leases and introductory tenancies, which are not secure).

(3A)[^c-c9316899ba53fe8e52a20af7aa65474e] No consent is required for the letting of land (in Wales) under—

- (a) a secure contract,
- (b) a supported standard contract,
- (c) an introductory standard contract, or
- (d) a standard contract—
  - (i) in relation to which the exception in section 11(2) of the Renting Homes (Wales) Act [2016 (anaw 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/1) applies (first exception to requirement that contract made by a community landlord is a secure contract), and
  - (ii) which is within any of paragraphs 4 and 6 to 14 of Schedule 3 to that Act (occupation contracts made with or adopted by community landlords which may be standard contracts).

(4)[^c15057641] For the purposes of this section the grant of an option to purchase the freehold of, or any other interest in, land is a disposal and a consent given to such a disposal extends to a disposal made in pursuance of the option.

(5) Sections 128 to 132 of the [^c15057651]Lands Clauses Consolidation Act 1845 (which require surplus land first to be offered to the original owner and to adjoining land-owners) do not apply to the sale by a local authority of land held by them for the purposes of this Part.

(6)[^c-0a62f99188fa3c019d6748595ef172d3] The Secretary of State shall consult the Regulator of Social Housing before deciding whether to consent under this section to anything within the Regulator’s remit.
