## 30. Application of provisions to development corporations, etc.[^c19876051]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The following provisions apply in relation to a development[^c19876011] corporation as they apply in relation to a local housing authority—

- section 25 (increase of rent where tenancy not secure), and
- section 26 (financial assistance towards tenants’ removal expenses).

(1A)[^c19876031] Section 25 also applies in relation to the new towns residuary body as it applies in relation to a local housing authority.
