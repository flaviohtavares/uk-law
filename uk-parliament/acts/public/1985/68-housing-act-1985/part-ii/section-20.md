# Housing Act 1985, section 20

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. Application of housing management provisions.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The following provisions of this Part down to[^c15057121] section 27BA[^c15057131] (general provisions on housing management matters) apply in relation to all houses held by a local housing authority for housing purposes.

(2) References in those provisions to an authority’s houses shall be construed accordingly.
