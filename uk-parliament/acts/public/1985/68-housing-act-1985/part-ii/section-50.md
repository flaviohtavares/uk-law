> Extent: England, Wales.

## 50. Offences.[^c15059781][^c15059791][^c15059801][^c15059771]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a person fails without reasonable excuse to perform a duty imposed on him by section 48 . . . [^c15059811] (provision of information, &c.), he commits a summary offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply where the payee is—

- a local authority [^c19880951]. . . ,
- a development[^c19880991] corporation, [^c15059831]. . .
- or[^c19881021]

  the Welsh Ministers
- [^c15059831]. . ..
