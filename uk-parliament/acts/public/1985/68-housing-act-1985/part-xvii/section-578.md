## 578. General enactments relating to compulsory purchase, &c., apply subject to this Part.

*Extent: E+W*

The [^c15082141]Acquisition of Land Act 1981, the [^c15082151]Compulsory Purchase Act 1965 and the [^c15082161]Land Compensation Act 1961 apply to the compulsory purchase of land under this Act subject to the following provisions of this Part.
