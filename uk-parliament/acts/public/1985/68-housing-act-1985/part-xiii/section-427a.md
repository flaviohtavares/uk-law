# Housing Act 1985, section 427A

## 427A. Entitlement to subsidy in case of land subject to management agreement.[^c15076491][^c15076501]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-6b05ac634d2a1d3d4624c7ae34180c60] The fact that a development[^c19910801][^c15076511] corporation [^c15076521] . . . has entered into a management agreement, and any letting of land in connection with such an agreement—

- (a) shall be disregarded in determining that corporation's[^c19911281] reckonable income or expenditure for the purposes of housing subsidy, and
- (b) shall not be regarded as a ground for recovering, withholding or reducing any sum under section 427 (recoupment of housing subsidy).

(2)[^c-8d23b38f204010afcf7053ce70279d0d] Reference in subsection (1) to a management agreement includes a section 247 or 249 arrangement, as defined by section 250A(6) of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008.
