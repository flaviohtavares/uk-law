## 456. Advances under the Small Dwellings Acquisition Acts.

*Extent: E+W*

The provisions of Schedule 18 have effect with respect to advances made under the Small Dwellings Acquisition Acts 1899 to 1923 before the repeal of those Acts by the [^c15079231]Housing (Consequential Provisions) Act 1985.
