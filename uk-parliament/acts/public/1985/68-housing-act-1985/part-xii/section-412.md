## 412. Penalty for obstruction.

*Extent: E+W*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

(1) It is a summary offence for a person intentionally[^c15075701] to obstruct a person acting in the execution of this Part or of any byelaw or warrant made or issued under it.

(2) A person committing such an offence is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3[^c15075711] on the standard scale.
