# Housing Act 1985, section 2

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. The district of a local housing authority[^c15055111]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) References in this Act to the district of a local housing authority are to the area of the council concerned, that is, to the district, London borough, the City of London the Welsh county or county borough,[^c15055121]or the Isles of Scilly, as the case may be.

(2) References in this Act to “**the local housing authority**”, in relation to land, are to the local housing authority in whose district the land is situated.
