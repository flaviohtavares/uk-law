# Housing Act 1985, section 350

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 350. Power to require information for purposes of scheme.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 2006-04-06).**

(1) The local housing authority may—

- (a) for the purpose of ascertaining whether a house . . . [^c15073941] is registrable, and
- (b) for the purpose of ascertaining the particulars to be entered in the register as regards a house . . . [^c15073941],

require the person having control of the house or the person managing the house or any person[^c15073961] who has an estate or interest in, or who lives in, the house . . . [^c15073941] to state in writing any information in his possession which the authority may reasonably require for that purpose.

(2) A person who, having been required in pursuance of this section to give information to a local housing authority, fails to give the information, or knowingly makes a mis-statement in respect of it, commits a summary offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding

- (a)[^c15073981] level 3[^c15073991] on the standard scale in the case of such a failure; or[^c15074001]
- (b) level 5[^c15074011] on the standard scale in the case of such a mis-statement.
