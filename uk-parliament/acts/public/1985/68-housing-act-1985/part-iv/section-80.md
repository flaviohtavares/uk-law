## 80. The landlord condition.[^c19037201][^c19035311][^c15060951]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-1d56df7444e3d09ab74fe4abd1db7b7c][^c-332399d71d25d1f057ff7703f097745c] The landlord condition is that the interest of the landlord belongs to one of the following authorities or bodies—

- a local authority,
- a development[^c19900231] corporation,
- a housing action trust[^c15060961]
- a Mayoral development corporation,[^c-366923b7dc88d7c0336049adb0b3a822]
- an urban development corporation, in the case of a tenancy falling within subsections (2A) to (2E), the Homes and Communities Agency[^c19900341] , the Greater London Authority[^c-8712d5fd969da018b828f841839b1eac] or the Welsh Ministers (as the case may be),
- [^c15060971]. . .
- [^c15060981]. . .
- [^c15060981]. . .
- [^c15061001]. . . housing co-operative to which this section applies.

(2)[^c15061011] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A)[^c19900411] A tenancy falls within this subsection if the interest of the landlord is transferred to—

- (a) the Homes and Communities Agency as mentioned in section 52(1)(a) to (d) of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008,
- (aa)[^c-ffe9311d709748f7b0753c04a847e829] the Greater London Authority as mentioned in section 333ZI(2)(a) to (d) of the Greater London Authority Act 1999, or
- (b) the Welsh Ministers as mentioned in section 36(1)(a)(i) to (iii) of the New Towns Act 1981.

(2B) A tenancy falls within this subsection if it is entered into pursuant to a contract under which the rights and liabilities of the prospective landlord are transferred to the Homes and Communities Agency , the Greater London Authority[^c-54b3129ffe375497af6e2fbb7fc7c0ae] or the Welsh Ministers as mentioned in subsection (2A)(a), (aa)[^c-b7f1d79118bc04c4ecac9fcc30e1c633] or (b) (as the case may be).

(2C) A tenancy falls within this subsection if it is granted by the Homes and Communities Agency , the Greater London Authority[^c-aad747f3651767c6e78f2cdf4da3f795] or the Welsh Ministers to a person (alone or jointly with others) who, immediately before it was entered into, was a secure tenant of the Homes and Communities Agency , the Greater London Authority or the Welsh Ministers (as the case may be).

(2D) A tenancy falls within this subsection if—

- (a) it is granted by the Homes and Communities Agency , the Greater London Authority[^c-4ca8eab97a587235dbba430d1301db1c] or the Welsh Ministers to a person (alone or jointly with others),
- (b) before the grant of the tenancy, an order for possession of a dwelling-house let under a secure tenancy was made against the person (alone or jointly with others) and in favour of the Homes and Communities Agency , the Greater London Authority or the Welsh Ministers (as the case may be) on the court being satisfied as mentioned in section 84(2)(b) or (c), and
- (c) the tenancy is of the premises which constitute the suitable accommodation as to which the court was so satisfied.

(2E) A tenancy falls within this subsection if it is granted by the Homes and Communities Agency , the Greater London Authority[^c-39140f2380f16d044e25eb96494d5174] or the Welsh Ministers pursuant to an obligation under section 554(2A).

(3) If a co-operative housing association ceases to be a private registered provider of social housing or[^c-879623d609222120470d1afca0413974] a registered social landlord[^c15061021], it shall, within the period of 21 days beginning with the date on which it ceases to be such a body[^c-87d041cb4187bd0de764026535f87bd8], notify each of its tenants who thereby becomes a secure tenant, in writing, that he has become a secure tenant.

(4)[^c15061041] This section applies to a housing co-operative within the meaning of section 27B (agreements under certain superseded provisions) where the dwelling-house is comprised in a housing co-operative agreement within the meaning of that section.

(5)[^c19900541] In this Act and in any provision made under this Act, or made by or under any other enactment, a reference to—

- (a) a person within section 80 or 80(1) of this Act, or
- (b) a person who satisfies the landlord condition under this section,

includes a reference to the Homes and Communities Agency , to the Greater London Authority[^c-fafbfb0c4a2f39371842eca3aa22eb9b] or to the Welsh Ministers so far as acting in their capacity as landlord (or, in the case of disposals, former landlord) in respect of a tenancy which falls within subsections (2A) to (2E) above but, subject to this, does not include the Homes and Communities Agency , the Greater London Authority[^c-3ba4bf1d81ee732ad943f0f11edcb724] or the Welsh Ministers.

(6) Subsection (5)—

- (a) applies whether the person is described as an authority, body or landlord or in any other way and whether the reference is otherwise expressed in a different way, and
- (b) is subject to any provision to the contrary.
