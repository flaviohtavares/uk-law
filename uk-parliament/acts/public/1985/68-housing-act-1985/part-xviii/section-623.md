## 623. Minor definitions: Part XVIII

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “[^c15083411]**dwelling-house**” [^c19152741]. . . shall be construed in accordance with subsection (2);
- [^c19152911]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**owner**”, in relation to premises—
  - (a) means a person (other than a mortgagee not in possession) who is for the time being entitled to dispose of the fee simple absolute in the premises, whether in possession or in reversion, and
  - (b) includes also a person holding or entitled to the rents and profits of the premises under a lease of which the unexpired term exceeds three years.

(2)[^c15083421] For the purposes of this Part, “**dwelling-house**” includes any yard, garden, outhouses and appurtenances belonging to it or usually enjoyed with it and section 183 shall have effect to determine whether a dwelling-house is a flat.
