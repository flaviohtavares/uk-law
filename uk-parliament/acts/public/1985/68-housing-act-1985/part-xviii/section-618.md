## 618. The Common Council of the City of London.[^c19151741]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Common Council of the City of London may appoint a committee, consisting of so many persons as they think fit, for any purposes of this Act or the [^c15082771]Housing Accociations Act 1985 which in their opinion may be better regulated and managed by means of a committee.

(2) A committee so appointed—

- (a) shall consist as to a majority of its members of members of the Common Council, and
- (b) shall not be authorised to borrow money or to make a rate,

and shall be subject to any regulations and restrictions which may be imposed by the Common Council.
