# Housing Act 1985, section 612

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (58).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/68
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 612. Exclusion of Rent Act protection.

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in the Rent Acts , or the Renting Homes (Wales) Act[^key-bbe5eaa9a2943df1d75926cee9805d7e] [2016 (anaw 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/1) or Part I of the Housing Act 1988[^c15082741] prevents possession being obtained of a dwelling-house[^c15082751] of which possession is required for the purpose of enabling a local housing authority to exercise their powers under any enactment relating to housing.
