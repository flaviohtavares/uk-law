## 303. Meaning of “listed building”.

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part “**listed building**” means a building included in a list of buildings of special architectural or historic interest under section 1 of the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990[^c15072731] or section 76 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023[^c-df7cd84c49d24217e2edd3ea4e7d81d8].
