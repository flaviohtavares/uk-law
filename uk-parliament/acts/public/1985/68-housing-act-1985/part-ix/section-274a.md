# Housing Act 1985, section 274A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (58).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/68
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 274A. Effect of certain enforcement action under the Housing Act 2004[^c19108481]

*Extent: E+W*

A demolition order which has been made in respect of any premises shall cease to have effect if a management order under Chapter 1 or 2 of Part 4 of the Housing Act 2004 comes into force in relation to the premises.
