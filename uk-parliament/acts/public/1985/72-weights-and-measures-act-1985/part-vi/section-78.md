# Weights and Measures Act 1985, section 78

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-05-03. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/72
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Fees received by inspectors.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Every inspector shall, at such times as the local weights and measures authority may direct, account for and pay over to that authority or as they may direct all fees taken by him for the performance of his duties.
