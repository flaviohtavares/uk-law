# Weights and Measures Act 1985, section 99

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-05-03. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/72
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Short title and commencement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Weights and Measures Act 1985.

(2) [^c13370521]. . . this Act shall come into force at the end of the period of three months beginning with the day on which it is passed.
