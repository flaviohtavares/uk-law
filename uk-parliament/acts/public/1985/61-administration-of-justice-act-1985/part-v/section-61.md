# Administration of Justice Act 1985, section 61

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 61. Constitution of \[youth courts\] in London.[^c2575981]

*Extent: E+W*

In Schedule 2 to the [^c2575991]Children and Young Persons Act 1963 (which by virtue of section 17(1) of that Act has effect as Schedule 2 to the [^c2576001]Children and Young Persons Act 1933), in Part II (constitution of youth courts in Inner London and the City), after paragraph 15 there shall be inserted—

> **15A.**
>
> (1) Where, in the case of any sitting of a youth court, a person nominated under paragraph 15(b) of this Schedule—
>
> - (a) is available to act as chairman; but
> - (b) considers that it would be appropriate for another member of the court to act as chairman,
>
> he may nominate that member to act as chairman at that sitting.
>
> (2) A member of a youth court nominated to act as chairman under sub-paragraph (1) shall only so act while the person making the nomination continues to sit as a member of the court.
