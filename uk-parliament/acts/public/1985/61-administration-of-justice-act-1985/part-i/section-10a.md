# Administration of Justice Act 1985, section 10A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/61
>

## 10A. Penalty for sole solicitor pretending that practice is recognised[^key-5f5274590959bcb3e7f4befcf1a52b01]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A sole solicitor shall not describe or hold out the sole solicitor’s practice as a practice for the time being recognised under section 9 unless it is so recognised.

(2) Any person who contravenes subsection (1) is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale.

(3) In this section “sole solicitor” has the same meaning as in section 9.
