# Administration of Justice Act 1985, section 33A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/61

## 33A. Administration of oaths by licensed conveyancers or licensed CLC practitioners[^c20439151][^key-edd75a7f3c62f0134e8b42a547fc8f6b]

*Extent: E+W*

The Council may make rules prescribing its arrangements for authorising licensed conveyancers or licensed CLC practitioners[^key-8fbe51f8c026e69be47111d86d956470] , for the purposes of the Legal Services Act 2007, to carry on activities which consist of the administration of oaths.
