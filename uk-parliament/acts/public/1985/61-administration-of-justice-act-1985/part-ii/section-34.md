# Administration of Justice Act 1985, section 34

## 34. Modification of existing enactments relating to conveyancing etc.[^c-cdbb8a9acaf3423b6a5b98aee3d96786]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In the following provisions, namely—

- (a) sections 69 and 75(1) of the [^c2574921]Law of Property Act 1925; [^c4401261]. . .
- (b) [^c4401261]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

any reference to a solicitor shall be construed as including a reference to a licensed conveyancer and any reference to a person’s solicitor shall be construed as including a reference to a licensed conveyancer acting for that person.

(2) In the following provisions, namely—

- (a) sections 10(2), 48 and 182 of the Law of Property Act 1925;
- (b) [^c4401331]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) [^c20439241]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) [^c20439241]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) [^c20439241]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f)[^c2574981] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

any reference to a solicitor shall be construed as including a reference to a licensed conveyancer or to a recognised body which is a conveyancing services body[^c-8b1ee94787f640519180dcfed48b82bc] , and any reference to a person’s solicitor shall be construed as including a reference to a licensed conveyancer or such a[^c-741d767944dbb5322affaecf3f43702e] recognised body acting for that person.

(3) The Estate Agents Act 1979 shall not, by virtue of section 1 of that Act, apply to things done in the course of the provision of conveyancing services by a licensed conveyancer or a recognised body which is a conveyancing services body[^c-ea015a7e36b48d292c97d4b92c1afd1f] .

(4)[^c-f45bf8a01dce349d2f9ad0a7e0c96621] In this section “**conveyancing services body**” has the meaning given by section 32A.
