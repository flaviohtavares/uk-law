# Companies Act 1985, section 406

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/6
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 406. Companies to keep copies of instruments creating charges.[^c17407541][^c17407551]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

(1) Every company shall cause a copy of every instrument creating a charge requiring registration under this Chapter to be kept at its registered office.

(2) In the case of a series of uniform debentures, a copy of one debenture of the series is sufficient.
