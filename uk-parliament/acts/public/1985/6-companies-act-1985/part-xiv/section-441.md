# Companies Act 1985, section 441

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/6

## 441. Inspectors’ report to be evidence.[^c17409211][^c17409221][^c-7f27ad17260a9deb0136d5110a4fb638]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A copy of any report of inspectors appointed under this Part[^c17409231], certified by the Secretary of State to be a true copy, is admissible in any legal proceedings as evidence of the opinion of the inspectors in relation to any matter contained in the report and, in proceedings on an application under[^c17409241] section 8 of the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986[^c17409251] or Article 11 of the Company Directors Disqualification (Northern Ireland) Order 2002[^c20489951], as evidence of any fact stated therein.

(2) A document purporting to be such a certificate as is mentioned above shall be received in evidence and be deemed to be such a certificate, unless the contrary is proved.
