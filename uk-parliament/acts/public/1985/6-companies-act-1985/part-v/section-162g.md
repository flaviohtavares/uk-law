# Companies Act 1985, section 162G

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 162G. Treasury shares: penalty for contravention

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

If a company contravenes any provision of sections 162A to 162F every officer of it who is in default is liable to a fine.
