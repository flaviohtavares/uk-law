# Companies Act 1985, section 181

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 181. Definitions for Chapter VII.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

In this Chapter—

- (a) “**distributable profits**”, in relation to the making of any payment by a company, means those profits out of which it could lawfully make a distribution (within the meaning given by section 829 of the Companies Act 2006[^c19871071]) equal in value to the payment, and
- (b) “**permissible capital payment**” means the payment permitted by section 171;

and references to payment out of capital are to be construed in accordance with section 171.
