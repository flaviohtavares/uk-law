> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/6

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 658. Liability for rentcharge on company’s land after dissolution.[^c17418111]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

(1)[^c17418121] Section 180 of the Insolvency Act[^c17418131] shall apply to land in England and Wales which by operation of law vests subject to a rentcharge in the Crown or any other person on the dissolution of a company as it applies to land so vesting on a disclaimer under that section.

(2) In this section “**company**” includes any body corporate.
