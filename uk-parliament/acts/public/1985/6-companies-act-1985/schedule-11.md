# Companies Act 1985, SCHEDULE 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/6
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 11[^c19733651][^c19733651]

*Extent: E+W+S*

### 1.[^c17430881]

**No longer in force (ended 2008-04-06).**

Paragraphs 2 to 6 below apply where a company has prepared accounts in accordance with the special provisions of Part VII relating to banking companies and paragraphs 7 to 13 below apply where a company has prepared accounts in accordance with the special provisions of Part VII relating to insurance companies.

### Modifications where accounts prepared in accordance with special provisions for banking companies[^c17824071]

**No longer in force (ended 2008-04-06).**

#### 2.[^c18728111]

Section 264(2) shall apply as if the reference to paragraph 89 of Schedule 4 therein was a reference to paragraph 85(c) of Part I of Schedule 9.

#### 3.[^c18728121]

Section 269 shall apply as if:

- (a) there were substituted for the words “are shown as an asset” in sub-section (1) the words “are included as an asset”; and
- (b) the reference to paragraph 20 of Schedule 4 in sub-section (2)(b) was to paragraph 27 of Part I of Schedule 9.

#### 4.[^c18728131]

Sections 270(2) and 275 shall apply as if the references therein to paragraphs 88 and 89 of Schedule 4 were to paragraph 85 of Part I of Schedule 9.

#### 5.[^c18728141]

Sections 272 and 273 shall apply as if in section 272(3) there were substituted, for the references to sections 226, 226A and 226B[^c19373231] and Schedule 4, references to section 255 and Part I of Schedule 9.

#### 6.[^c18728151]

Section 276 shall apply as if the references to paragraphs 12(a) and 34(3)(a) of [^c18728231]Schedule 4 were to paragraphs 19(a) and 44(3)(a) of Schedule 9 .

### Modifications where accounts prepared in accordance with special provisions for insurance companies[^c17430961]

**No longer in force (ended 2008-04-06).**

#### 7.[^c17431031]

Section 264(2) shall apply as if for the words in parentheses there were substituted “(“liabilities””to include any provision for other risks and charges within paragraph 84(c) of Part I of Schedule 9A and any amount included under Liabilities items Ba (fund for future appropriations), C (technical provisions) and D (technical provisions for linked liabilities) in a balance sheet drawn up in accordance with the balance sheet format set out in section B of Part I of Schedule 9A).

#### 8.[^c17431041]

Section 269 shall apply as if the reference to paragraph 20 of Schedule 4 in subsection (2)(b) were a reference to paragraph 35 of Part I of Schedule 9A.

#### 9.[^c17431111]

In the case of Companies Act accounts,[^c19373251] sections 270(2) and 275 shall apply as if the reference to provisions of any of the kinds mentioned in paragraphs 88 and 89 of Schedule 4 were a reference to provisions of any of the kinds mentioned in paragraph 84 of Part I of Schedule 9A and to any amount included under Liabilities items Ba (fund for future appropriations), C (technical provisions) and D (technical provisions for linked liabilities) in a balance sheet drawn up in accordance with the balance sheet format set out in section B of Part I of Schedule 9A.

#### 10.[^c17431121]

Sections 272 and 273 shall apply as if the references in section 272(3) to sections 226, 226A and 226B[^c19387361] and Schedule 4 were references to section 255 and Part I of Schedule 9A.

#### 11.[^c17431191]

Section 276 shall apply as if the references to paragraphs 12(a) and 34(3)(a) of Schedule 4 [^c17431201] were references to paragraphs 16(a) and 29(3)(a) of Part I of Schedule 9A.

#### 12.[^c18728241][^c17431281]

**No longer in force (ended 1993-12-19).**

Sections 272 and 273 apply as if in section 272(3)—

- (a) for the references to section 226[^c17431291] and Schedule 4 there were substituted references to section 255 and Part I of Schedule 9A[^c17431311], and
- (b) immediately before paragraph (a) there were inserted “except where the company is entitled to avail itself, and has availed itself, of any of the provisions of paragraph[^c17431321] [^c17431331]. . . 28 of Schedule 9A”.

#### 13.[^c18728251][^c17431381]

**No longer in force (ended 1993-12-19).**

Section 275 applies as if—

- (a) for subsection (1) there were substituted—

  > (1) For purposes of section 263, any provision (within the meaning of Part I of[^c17431391] Schedule 9A[^c17431401]), other than one in respect of any diminution of value of a fixed asset appearing on a revaluation of all the fixed assets of the company, or of all its fixed assets other than goodwill, is to be treated as a realised loss

  ; and
- (b) “fixed assets” were defined to include any other asset which is not a current asset.
