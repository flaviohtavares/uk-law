# Companies Act 1985, section 699B

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 699B. Scope of sections 700 to 703.[^c17419131]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

Sections 700 to 703 shall not apply to any institution to which section 699A applies or to any limited company which is incorporated outside the United Kingdom and Gibraltar and has a branch in the United Kingdom[^c17419141].
