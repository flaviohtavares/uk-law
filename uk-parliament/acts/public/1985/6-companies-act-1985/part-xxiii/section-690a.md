# Companies Act 1985, section 690A

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 690A. Branch registration under the Eleventh Company Law Directive ([89/666/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0666)).[^c17418541]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

(1) This section applies to any limited company which—

- (a) is incorporated outside the United Kingdom and Gibraltar, and
- (b) has a branch in Great Britain.

(2) Schedule 21A to this Act (Branch registration under the Eleventh Company Law Directive ([89/666/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0666))) shall have effect in relation to any company to which this section applies.
