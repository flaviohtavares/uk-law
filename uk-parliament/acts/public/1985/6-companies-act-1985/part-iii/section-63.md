# Companies Act 1985, section 63

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/6
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 63. Prospectus to be dated.[^c17387161][^c17387061]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A prospectus issued by or on behalf of a company, or in relation to an intended company, shall be dated; and that date shall, unless the contrary is proved, be taken as its date of publication.
