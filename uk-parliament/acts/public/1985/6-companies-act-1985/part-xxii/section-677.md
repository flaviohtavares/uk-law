## 677. Companies re-registered with altered status under former Companies Acts.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

(1) This Act applies to every unlimited company registered or re-registered as limited in pursuance of the [^c17418221] Companies Act 1879, section 57 of the [^c17418231]Companies (Consolidation) Act 1908, section 16 of the [^c17418241]Companies Act 1929, section 16 of the [^c17418251]Companies Act 1948 or section 44 of the [^c17418261]Companies Act 1967 as it (this Act) applies to an unlimited company re-registered as limited in pursuance of Part II of this Act.

(2) But reference, express or implied, to the date of registration or re-registration is to be read as referring to the date at which the company was registered or re-registered as a limited company under the relevant enactment.
