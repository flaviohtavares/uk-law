# Local Government Act 1985, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-04-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/51
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Notices.[^c3163581]

*Extent: E+W*

As soon as practicable after receiving notice from a constituent council [^c3163591]. . . that it has made an appointment, or [^c3163591]. . . that it has terminated an appointment, under this Part of this Act, the authority to which the appointment was made shall give public notice of the fact that the appointment has been made or terminated and of the name of the person concerned.
