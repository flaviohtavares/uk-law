# Local Government Act 1985, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-04-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/51
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Integrated Transport Authorities[^key-3594aaf4a30d7422c7b30f897fc6820f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) On the appointed day there shall be established for each metropolitan county a body corporate to be known by the name of that county with the addition of the words “ Integrated Transport Authority ”[^key-5dbe565b59c186397b4889e2f37ebbde].

(2) The authorities[^key-ef9c1ad8ab05cba39919f48e7be2764f] established by this section shall be known as Integrated Transport Authorities[^key-bbb5af905dfbfd4a5c26385452a78d00] .

(3) Each Integrated Transport Authority established under this section[^key-ba7d500add92a1902aba6ba15dc48125] shall consist of members of the constituent councils appointed by them to be members of the authority.

(4) The constituent councils in relation to an Integrated Transport Authority established under this section[^key-dc5230b1e8e94b38ac88832ccb530e40] shall be the councils of the metropolitan districts comprised in the county.
