## 37. Police and fire services.

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 11 to this Act shall have effect, as from the abolition date, for vesting functions relating to [^c3163611]. . . fire services and certain related matters in the police authorities and fire and civil defence authorities established by this Part of this Act.
