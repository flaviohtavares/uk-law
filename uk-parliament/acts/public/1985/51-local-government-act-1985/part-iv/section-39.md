# Local Government Act 1985, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1985/51
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Passenger transport.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 12 to this Act shall have effect for transferring functions relating to passenger transport to the metropolitan county passenger transport authorities and for making provision with respect to the exercise of those functions.

(2) In that Schedule paragraph 3[^c3163651] shall have effect from the abolition date and paragraph 5 shall have effect as provided in sub-paragraph (5) of that paragraph.
