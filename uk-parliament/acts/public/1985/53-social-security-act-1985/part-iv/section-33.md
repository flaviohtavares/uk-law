# Social Security Act 1985, section 33

> Text as at 2007-09-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Citation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Social Security Act 1985.

(2) This Act may be cited together with—

- (a) the Social Security Acts 1975 to 1984; and
- (b) section 25 of the [^c1002885]Health and Social Services and Social Security Adjudications Act 1983 and Schedule 8 to that Act,

as the Social Security Acts 1975 to 1985.
