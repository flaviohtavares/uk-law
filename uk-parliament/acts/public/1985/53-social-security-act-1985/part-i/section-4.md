# Social Security Act 1985, section 4

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Earnings factors and guaranteed minimum pensions.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-02-07).**

The Social Security Pensions Act 1975 and the [^c1002840]Social Security (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1977 shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 3 to this Act in relation to earnings factors and guaranteed minimum pensions.
