# Social Security Act 1985, section 1

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Short service benefit for members of schemes whose employment is terminated before age of 26.[^c1002837]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-02-07).**

Paragraph 6(1)(a) of Schedule 16 to the [^c1002838]Social Security Act 1973 (the effect of which is that an occupational pension scheme need not provide benefit for a member whose service in relevant employment is terminated before he has attained the age of 26) shall cease to have effect.
