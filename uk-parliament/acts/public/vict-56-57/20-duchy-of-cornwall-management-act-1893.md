# Duchy Of Cornwall Management Act 1893

> This law has been repealed or revoked. Ended on 2001-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Duchy Of Cornwall Management Act 1893 [^c575956](repealed 1.2.2001)

1893 c.20

**No longer in force (ended 2001-02-01).**

## 1. Repeal of limit imposed by 26 & 27 Vict. c. 49. for exercise of powers of sale and enfranchisement.[^c575959]

In section three of the Duchy of Cornwall Management Act, 1863, the words “within a period of thirty-one years from the passing of this Act” and in section twenty-two of the same Act the words “not exceeding the term of ninety-nine years” are hereby repealed.

## 2. Extension of power of investment.

The power of investment conferred by the [^c575960]Duchy of Cornwall Management Act 1863 is hereby extended, and shall authorise investments in any of the modes of investment authorised by section one of the[^c575961] [^c575962]Trustee Investments Act 1961, or by any Act amending or extending the same but without the restrictions on wider-range investment imposed by section 2 of the Trustee Investments Act 1961[^c575963], and shall include power to vary any present or future investment for any investment authorised by this Act.

## 3. Short title and construction.

This Act may be cited as the Duchy of Cornwall Management Act 1893 and shall be construed as one with the Duchy of Cornwall Management Acts 1863 to 1868 and those Acts and this Act may be cited collectively as the Duchy of Cornwall Management Acts 1863 to 1893.
