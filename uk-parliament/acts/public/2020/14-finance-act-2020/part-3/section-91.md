# Finance Act 2020, section 91

> Text as at 2026-03-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Amounts of gross gaming yield charged to gaming duty

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 11(2) of FA 1997 (rates of gaming duty), for the table substitute—

> TABLE
>
> | Part of gross gaming yield | Rate |
> | --- | --- |
> | The first £2,471,000 | 15% |
> | The next £1,703,500 | 20% |
> | The next £2,983,000 | 30% |
> | The next £6,296,500 | 40% |
> | The remainder | 50% |

.

(2) The amendment made by this section has effect in relation to accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2020.
