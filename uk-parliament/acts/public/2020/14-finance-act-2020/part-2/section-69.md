# Finance Act 2020, section 69

> Text as at 2026-03-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Recovery of DST liability

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any amount due by way of DST liability is recoverable as a debt due to the Crown.

(2) In this section “**DST liability**” has the same meaning as in section 66.
