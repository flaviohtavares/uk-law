## 23 (Extent: E+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note). Time limits in relation to urgent warrants etc under Investigatory Powers Act[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e] The power in subsection (2) is exercisable if the Investigatory Powers Commissioner notifies the Secretary of State that, in the Commissioner's opinion, the power needs to be exercised in response to the effects that coronavirus is having, or is likely to have, on the capacity of Judicial Commissioners to carry out their functions. *(Extent: E+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e]

(2 (Extent: E+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e] The Secretary of State may by regulations made by statutory instrument modify the Investigatory Powers Act 2016 so as to alter, for the purposes of any of the specified provisions of that Act (see subsection (3)), the length of a period referred to in that Act as “the relevant period”. *(Extent: E+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e]

(3 (Extent: E+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e] The specified provisions are— *(Extent: E+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e]

- (a (Extent: E+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e] sections 24(3), 109(3), 180(3) and 209(3) (period within which Judicial Commissioner must decide whether to approve decision to issue urgent warrant); *(Extent: E+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e]
- (b (Extent: E+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e] sections 32(2)(a), 116(2)(a), 184(2)(a) and 213(2)(a) (period at end of which urgent warrant ceases to have effect); *(Extent: E+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e]
- (c (Extent: E+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e] sections 33(5)(a), 117(5)(a), 185(3)(a) and 214(3)(a) (period during which urgent warrant may be renewed); *(Extent: E+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e]
- (d (Extent: E+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e] sections 38(5), 122(5), 124(3), 147(3), 166(3), 188(3) and 217(3) (period within which Judicial Commissioner or other appropriate person must decide whether to approve decision to make urgent modification of warrant). *(Extent: E+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e]

(4 (Extent: E+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e] A modification made by the regulations may not increase the length of a period so that it ends after the 12th working day after the day on which the warrant was issued or, as the case may be, the modification was made. *(Extent: E+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e]

(5 (Extent: E+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e] The regulations may make consequential, supplementary or transitional provision. *(Extent: E+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e]

(6 (Extent: E+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e] The regulations must provide for them to cease to have effect at the end of the period of 12 months beginning with the day on which they come into force. *(Extent: E+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e]

(7 (Extent: E+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e] A statutory instrument containing regulations under this section is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(Extent: E+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e]

(8 (Extent: E+N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e] In this section— *(Extent: E+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e]

- “ *(Extent: E+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e]**Investigatory Powers Commissioner *(Extent: E+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e]**” has the meaning given in section 263(1) of the Investigatory Powers Act 2016; *(Extent: E+N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67dbd4b5269eaa26536ad86a978c158e]
