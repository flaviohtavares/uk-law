# Coronavirus Act 2020, section 76

## 76. HMRC functions[^M_X_37c67466-a489-4cff-ce29-0dfcc045be9c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs are to have such functions as the Treasury may direct in relation to coronavirus or coronavirus disease.

## Annotations

[^M_X_37c67466-a489-4cff-ce29-0dfcc045be9c]: Editorial: The power conferred under this provision to make a direction may be exercised by means of a notice published in The Gazette
