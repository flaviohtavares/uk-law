# European Union (Withdrawal Agreement) Act 2020, section 15

> Text as at 2024-02-20.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Independent Monitoring Authority for the Citizens' Rights Agreements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-888a8cbb29873aa9c50045045def53c6] A body corporate called the Independent Monitoring Authority for the Citizens' Rights Agreements is established.

(2)[^c-888a8cbb29873aa9c50045045def53c6] In this Part that body is referred to as “**the IMA**”.

(3)[^c-a98751bd4504c3605518280e8fce51bf][^c-2d98411f3c8f799f39bec83d159cf7d2][^c-6949dbba86027ac09e97e93f31e303bb] Schedule 2 contains provision relating to the IMA (including provisions about the IMA's constitution and functions).
