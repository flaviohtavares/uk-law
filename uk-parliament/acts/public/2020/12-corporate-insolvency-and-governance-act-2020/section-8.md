# Corporate Insolvency and Governance Act 2020, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2022-02-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Administration in Great Britain: sales to connected persons

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph 60A of Schedule B1 to the Insolvency Act 1986 (which expired in May 2020) is revived.

(2) For sub-paragraph (10) of that paragraph substitute—

> (10) This paragraph expires at the end of June 2021 unless the power conferred by it is exercised before then.
