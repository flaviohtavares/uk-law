# Agriculture Act 2020, section 54

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Power to make transitional etc provision

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The appropriate authority may by regulations make transitional, transitory or saving provision in connection with the coming into force of any provision of this Act.

(2) The appropriate authority is—

- (a) the Welsh Ministers, for provision in connection with—
  - (i) sections 33 to 35, so far as relating to Wales,
  - (ii) section 36 and Schedule 3, so far as relating to Wales,
  - (iii) sections 38 and 39, so far as relating to Wales,
  - (iv)[^key-bc91a42fd6516a9d44dc76b206d1f6c9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (v)[^key-1a8c1c9ff833e18c13074bcd103b3769] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (vi)[^key-6adb0d0c6f890147109e3521a2efc2b8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the Scottish Ministers, for provision in connection with—
  - (i) sections 33 to 35, so far as relating to Scotland,
  - (ii) sections 38 and 39, so far as relating to Scotland,
- (c) DAERA, for provision in connection with—
  - (i) sections 33 and 34, so far as relating to Northern Ireland,
  - (ii) sections 38 and 39, so far as relating to Northern Ireland,
  - (iii) section 48 and Schedule 6, and
  - (iv) section 52 and Schedule 7 so far as they apply in relation to Northern Ireland, and
- (d) the Secretary of State, for provision in connection with anything not mentioned in paragraphs (a) to (c).
