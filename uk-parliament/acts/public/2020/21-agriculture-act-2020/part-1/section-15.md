# Agriculture Act 2020, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Aid for fruit and vegetable producer organisations[^c24386091]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations modify the following assimilated direct[^key-004b4c5bbbbcb26cf5e7dc43ff3c19eb] legislation for the purpose of securing that it ceases to have effect in relation to England—

- (a) Articles 32 to 38 of the CMO Regulation, which make provision about aid for fruit and vegetable producer organisations (“producer organisations aid”);
- (b) so far as relating to producer organisations aid, Commission Delegated [Regulation (EU) 2017/891](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/0891) of 13 March 2017 supplementing the CMO Regulation with regard to the fruit and vegetable, and processed fruit and vegetable, sectors;
- (c) so far as relating to producer organisations aid, Council Implementing [Regulation (EU) 2017/892](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2017/0892) of 13 March 2017 laying down rules for the application of the CMO Regulation with regard to the fruit and vegetable, and processed fruit and vegetable, sectors.

(2) Regulations under this section are subject to negative resolution procedure (unless section 50(5) applies).
