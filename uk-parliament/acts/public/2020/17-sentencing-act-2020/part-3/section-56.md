# Sentencing Act 2020, section 56

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (303).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Other duties of court to give reasons where certain orders not made[^key-89df20120eaee49bc777d52607d82b49]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For other duties of the court dealing with an offender for an offence to give reasons, in certain circumstances, for a decision not to make an order, see—

<table>
<tbody>
<tr><th><em>Duty to give reasons</em></th><th><em>Type of order</em></th><th><em>Type of offence</em></th></tr>
<tr><th colspan="3">Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001</th></tr>
<tr><td>section 33(2)(c)</td><td>travel restriction order under section 33</td><td>sentence of imprisonment for certain drug-trafficking offences</td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="3">Animal Welfare Act 2006</th></tr>
<tr><td>section 33(6)</td><td>order under section 33 (deprivation)</td><td>certain offences under that Act</td></tr>
<tr><td>section 34(8)</td><td>order under section 34 (disqualification)</td><td>certain offences under that Act</td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="3">Football Spectators Act 1989</th></tr>
<tr><td>section 14A(3)</td><td>banning order under Part 2</td><td>relevant offence within the meaning of that Part</td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="3">Prevention of Social Housing Fraud Act 2013</th></tr>
<tr><td>section 4(4)</td><td>unlawful profit order under section 4</td><td>offence under section 1 or 2</td></tr>
<tr><th colspan="3">Modern Slavery Act 2015</th></tr>
<tr><td>section 8(7)(b)</td><td>slavery and trafficking reparation order</td><td>offence under section 1, 2 or 4</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

(2) Nothing in this section is to be taken to affect—

- (a) any power to make an order mentioned in the table in subsection (1), or
- (b) any requirement to give reasons for a decision not to exercise any power to make an order not mentioned in the table.
