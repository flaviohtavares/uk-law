# Sentencing Act 2020, section 203

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (303).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 203. Restriction on making both community order and suspended sentence order[^key-f246f0c6ebedfba326373dcfc8c4cc21][^key-c6caaa70a95271ece9de9cca3f2f00b9][^key-d9b9ef9268e9664d4b7f53f892934058][^key-2adba769fb09629d9861c174a394b341]

*Extent: E+W*

A court may not make a community order in respect of an offence if it makes a suspended sentence order in respect of—

- (a) the offence,
- (b) any other offence of which the offender is convicted by or before it, or
- (c) any other offence for which it deals with the offender.
