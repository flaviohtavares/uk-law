# Sentencing Act 2020, section 185

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (303).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 185. Youth rehabilitation order: availability of particular requirements[^c-1e1003e3810b89f9cfb2a7858d84e6bd]

*Extent: E+W*

*Extended activity requirement*

(1) An extended activity requirement is not available for a youth rehabilitation order other than a youth rehabilitation order with intensive supervision and surveillance.

*Unpaid work requirement*

(2) An unpaid work requirement is not available for a youth rehabilitation order in respect of an offence unless the offender is aged 16 or 17 when convicted of the offence.

*Fostering requirement*

(3) A fostering requirement is not available for a youth rehabilitation order other than a youth rehabilitation order with fostering.

*Electronic monitoring requirement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-75f01fc39538c820ac2b4d6e31180fea] requirements*

(4) An electronic monitoring requirement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0c01f51963cc0e36f3f4d025fbf06b74] electronic compliance monitoring requirement is not available for a youth rehabilitation order unless the order imposes at least one other youth rehabilitation requirement.

(5)[^c-cff76abc866a64430a65c65c07c63722] An electronic whereabouts monitoring requirement is not available for a youth rehabilitation order in respect of an offence unless the offender was convicted of the offence on or after the day on which paragraph 6 of Schedule 17 to the Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act 2022 first came into force to any extent (ignoring, for these purposes, the coming into force of Part 2 of that Schedule for the purposes of making regulations).
