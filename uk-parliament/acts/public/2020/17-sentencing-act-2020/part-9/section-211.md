# Sentencing Act 2020, section 211

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (303).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 211. Power for Crown Court to direct magistrates' court supervision[^c-62d19cf3fb275bce487f14b4829616ea]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-78aec7d62f7046495ad525bdfaedfc34] Where the Crown Court makes a community order, it may include a direction that the order is to be subject to magistrates' court supervision.

(2)[^c-82cd4f2d4be48548ac13f672ad3bc6d1] Subsection (1) does not apply to a community order that qualifies for special procedures for the purposes of section 217A.
