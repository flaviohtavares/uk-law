# Sentencing Act 2020, section 210

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (303).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 210. Community order to specify offender's home local justice area[^c-cf019f822c2ecf37a9e312af29080a0b][^c-edf2dd12e434c81e7cd05a30995fb966][^c-e436af2943ad91bbad1cc5c6de6ee48e][^c-d0daf0f699b4471240240feb04f758a6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A community order must specify which local justice area is the offender's home local justice area.

(2) The area specified must be the local justice area in which the offender resides or will reside.
