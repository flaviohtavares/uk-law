# Sentencing Act 2020, section 399

## 399. Mandatory sentences[^c-07feead9fc33622a39107bb112fd5987]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of this Code, where a court is dealing with an offender for an offence, a mandatory sentence requirement applies in relation to the offence if—

- (a) the offence is one for which the sentence is fixed by law,
- (b) the court is obliged by one of the following provisions to pass a sentence of detention for life, custody for life or imprisonment for life—
  - (i) section 258, 258A, 274, 274A, 285 or 285A[^c-9cae8f07f664c59ac0a3a1b9fe04aa08] (life sentence for certain [^c-9ff9c4002a27859965608e03e91d89da]... offenders);
  - (ii) section 273 or 283 (life sentence for second listed offence),
- (ba)[^c-44d2892c25db684650fcced86402f42b] the court is obliged by section 268B or 282B to impose a serious terrorism sentence, or
- (c) a sentence is required by one of the following provisions and the court is not of the opinion mentioned in that provision—
  - (i) section 311(2) (minimum sentence for certain offences involving firearms that are prohibited weapons);
  - (ii) section 312(2) or (2A)[^c-33313c21dd23feab37293c57d1beee25] (minimum sentence for offence of threatening with weapon or bladed article);
  - (iii) section 313(2) or (2A)[^c-a302af4b67cc9166a418fa128fa6c63b] (minimum sentence of 7 years for third class A drug trafficking offence);
  - (iv) section 314(2) or (2A)[^c-537cc0d495649db445506e302a37f1d1] (minimum sentence of 3 years for third domestic burglary);
  - (v) section 315(2) or (2A)[^c-709183c1febd950afa6b9355a15713eb] (minimum sentence for repeat offence involving weapon or corrosive substance[^c-5e3bc0c05b44b39ef4a56dbe0fe30211] or bladed article).
