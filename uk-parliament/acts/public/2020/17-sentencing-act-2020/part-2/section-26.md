# Sentencing Act 2020, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (303).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Remission by Crown Court to youth court or other magistrates’ court: custody or bail, and appeals[^c-3415cdd80f1f67f85d975de22debcc0b][^c-048a4a76c03c86623a7826b98f17b7f9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where the Crown Court remits an offender to a youth court under section 25 or a magistrates’ court under section[^c-fde968e50673619210d39ca4c9ae8305] 25A.

(2) The Crown Court may, subject to section 25 of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (restrictions on granting bail), give such directions as appear to be necessary—

- (a) with respect to the custody of the offender, or
- (b) for the offender's release on bail,

until the offender can appear or be brought before the youth court or magistrates’ court[^c-7487d27c1dc9d0d1c38251bd52ffc2c3].

(3) The offender—

- (a) has no right of appeal against the order of remission, but
- (b) has the same right of appeal against an order of the youth court or magistrates’ court[^c-83dacc98b0e2079c93e0b0a4ee59b6dc] as if convicted by that court.
