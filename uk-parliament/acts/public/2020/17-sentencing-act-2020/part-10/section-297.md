# Sentencing Act 2020, section 297

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (303).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 297. Power to direct magistrates' court supervision of order[^c-6541a34a0e0e64f2bb4ce6dbbbb0f95d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-0aefc375f8e51730b07017c03bfd25b2] Where the Crown Court makes a suspended sentence order which imposes any community requirement, it may make a direction that the order is to be subject to magistrates' court supervision.

For the effect of such a direction, see Parts 2 and 3 of Schedule 16 (breach or amendment of a community requirement of a suspended sentence order).

(2)[^c-a8d001a6f2740c67da2222d280bfe0ae] Subsection (1) does not apply to a suspended sentence order that qualifies for special procedures for the purposes of section 293A.
