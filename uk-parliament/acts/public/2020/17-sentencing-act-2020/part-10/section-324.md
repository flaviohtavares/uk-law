# Sentencing Act 2020, section 324

## 324. Life sentences: interpretation[^c-1526b15ab9d5fe0fcb67a62c786b5bf2][^c-cef641cef87d8a92b9a17a7b10b6c7e0]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Chapter—

- “**the early release provisions**” means section 28(5) to (8) of the Crime (Sentences) Act 1997;
- “**life sentence**” means—
  - (a) a sentence of imprisonment for life,
  - (b) a sentence of detention for life or during Her Majesty's pleasure (see sections 250 and 259), or
  - (c) a sentence of custody for life (see sections 272 and 275);
- “minimum term order” and “minimum term” have the meanings given by section 321(4);
- “**whole life order**” has the meaning given by section 321(5).
