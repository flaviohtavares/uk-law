# Sentencing Act 2020, section 298

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (303).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 298. Provision of copies of order and related documents[^c-c1d37303a3fa27ee1dd2d5fad8269c2f][^c-4b902e615ad46b3b1176cebe94a297a8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies on the making by a court of a suspended sentence order which imposes one or more community requirements.

(2) The court must forthwith provide copies of the order—

- (a) to the offender,
- (b) to the responsible officer,
- (c) to an officer of a provider of probation services that is a public sector provider who is acting at the court, and
- (d) if the court does not act in the offender's home local justice area, to a provider of probation services that is a public sector provider and is operating in that area.

(3) If the order imposes any requirement specified in column 1 of the following table the court must also forthwith provide the person specified in the corresponding entry in column 2 with a copy of so much of the order as relates to the requirement.

<table>
<tbody>
<tr><th><em>The requirement</em></th><th><em>The person to whom a copy must be provided</em></th></tr>
<tr><td>An exclusion requirement imposed for the purpose (or partly for the purpose) of protecting a person from being approached by the offender.</td><td>The person intended to be protected</td></tr>
<tr><td>A residence requirement relating to residence in an institution.</td><td>The person in charge of the institution</td></tr>
<tr><td>A mental health treatment requirement.</td><td>The person specified under paragraph 16(3)(b)(iii) of Schedule 9 or the person in charge of the institution or place specified under paragraph 16(3)(b)(i) or (ii) of that Schedule</td></tr>
<tr><td>A drug rehabilitation requirement</td><td>The person in charge of the institution or place specified under paragraph 19(5)(b) or (c) of Schedule 9</td></tr>
<tr><td>An alcohol treatment requirement</td><td>The person in charge of the institution or place specified under paragraph 23(5)(c) or (d) of Schedule 9 or, in the case of practitioner-based treatment, the person specified under paragraph 23(5)(a) of that Schedule</td></tr>
<tr><td rowspan="2">An electronic monitoring requirement</td><td>Any person who by virtue of paragraph 31(1) of Schedule 9 will be responsible for the electronic monitoring</td></tr>
<tr><td>Any person without whose consent the requirement could not be included in the order.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

(4)[^c-0ef73cec48a2ea655e744ce4b2c84d92] If the court does not act in the offender's home local justice area, it must provide the magistrates' court acting in that area with—

- (a) a copy of the order, and
- (b) such documents and information relating to the case as it considers likely to be of assistance to a court acting in that area in the exercise of its functions in relation to the order.

(5) In subsection (2) “**public sector provider**” means—

- (a) a probation trust or other public body, or
- (b) the Secretary of State.
