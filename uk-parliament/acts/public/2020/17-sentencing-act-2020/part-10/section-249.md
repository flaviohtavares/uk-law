# Sentencing Act 2020, section 249

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (303).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 249. Sentence of detention under section 250: availability[^c-bedfc6da0134a29ae285d7f953d2dc34][^c-5ea577179a41689bc10143a3dc691a1c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A sentence of detention under section 250 is available where a person aged under 18 is convicted on indictment of an offence listed in the following table (but the court is not required to pass a sentence of detention under section 252A)[^c-e8a6ece8c899a0d08e57b53809c6186b]—

<table>
<tbody>
<tr><td colspan="2"><em>Offences punishable with imprisonment for at least 14 years</em></td></tr>
<tr><td>(a)</td><td>an offence which—<br>(i) is not an offence for which the sentence is fixed by law, and<br>(ii) is punishable in the case of a person aged 21 or over with imprisonment for 14 years or more;</td></tr>
<tr><td colspan="2"><em>Sexual offences</em></td></tr>
<tr><td>(b)</td><td>an offence under any of the following provisions of the Sexual Offences Act 2003—<br>(i) section 3 (sexual assault);<br>(ii) section 13 (child sex offences committed by children or young persons);<br>(iii) section 25 (sexual activity with a child family member);<br>(iv) section 26 (inciting a child family member to engage in sexual activity);</td></tr>
<tr><td colspan="2"><em>Offences related to firearms</em></td></tr>
<tr><td>(c)</td><td>an offence (other than one within paragraph (a)) which—<br>(i) is listed in Schedule 20 (firearms offences to which minimum sentence applies), and<br>(ii) was committed when the offender was aged 16 or over.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

(2) For circumstances in which a court is required to impose a sentence of detention under section 250, see—

- (a) sections 258 and 258A[^c-86263356e431ce8d0b9c58182491b4ba] (required sentence of detention for life);
- (b) section 311 (minimum sentence for certain offences involving firearms that are prohibited weapons).

(3) Where an offence is found to have been committed—

- (a) over a period of 2 or more days, or
- (b) at some time during a period of 2 or more days,

it is to be taken for the purposes of paragraph (c)(ii) of the table in subsection (1) to have been committed on the last of those days.
