# Sentencing Act 2020, section 320

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 320. Determination of day when offence committed[^c-cf382867e07c33727a7a058a3aefcb61]

*Extent: E+W*

Where an offence is found to have been committed—

- (a) over a period of 2 or more days, or
- (b) at some time during a period of 2 or more days,

it is to be taken for the purposes of sections 311, 312,[^c-51b996e3ffa7cab1d6827f74535a5e58] 313, 314 and 315 to have been committed on the last of those days.
