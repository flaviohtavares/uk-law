# Sentencing Act 2020, section 303

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (303).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 303. Breach or amendment of suspended sentence order, and effect of further conviction[^c-b9db020d895470bacf05d775fa53ad1c]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 16 makes provision about—

- (a) the effect of any further conviction where an offender is subject to a suspended sentence order,
- (aa)[^c-29d54ff61e920499e7181f39f41a0fd3] termination of the supervision period of a suspended sentence order, and
- (b) breach or amendment of the community requirements of a suspended sentence order.
