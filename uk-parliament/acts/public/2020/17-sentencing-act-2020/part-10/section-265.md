# Sentencing Act 2020, section 265

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (303).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 265. Required special sentence for certain offenders of particular concern[^key-42dde7b6c29a9f7061fe4ca2a31e4d07]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where a court imposes a sentence of detention in a young offender institution for an offence where—

- (a) the offence is listed in Schedule 13,
- (aa)[^key-93bbb9ebb3c3430839b0c02b80a786ec] if the offence is listed in Part 3 of that Schedule (offences involving or connected with a threat to national security), the offender was convicted of the offence on or after the day on which section 7(4) of the Sentencing Act 2026 came into force,
- (b) the offender—
  - (i)[^key-7f790231ce7048ec8f7697db9f3625f3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii) is aged at least 18 but[^key-91347f3a08cd01a6347d6dd86d419803] under 21 when convicted of the offence, and
- (c) the court does not impose any[^key-aef6c67059e0c360ffe85bb961cfa8e7] of the following for the offence (or for an offence associated with it)—
  - (i) an extended sentence under section 266,
  - (ia)[^key-a1e84e96d0ef6f41776c3ece3dbdd49e] a serious terrorism sentence under section 268A, or
  - (ii) a sentence of custody for life under section 272.

(1A)[^key-6a129825a5fd2cf0865a1d97e104e937] But this section does not apply if—

- (a) the offender was aged under 18 when the offence was committed, and
- (b) the offence—
  - (i) if listed in Part 1 of Schedule 13 (offences involving or connected with terrorism),[^key-c50d29e5dc1f5e1ebda84e0fd33ff1cc] was committed before the day on which section 22 of the Counter-Terrorism and Sentencing Act 2021 came into force, or
  - (ia)[^key-45c6066edc7b419ec58e5e17d2f769fc] if listed in Part 3 of Schedule 13, was committed before the day on which section 7(4) of the Sentencing Act 2026 came into force,
  - (ii) is listed in Part 2 of Schedule 13 (sexual offences).

(2)[^key-6990ffbad14e214a95b8887d928e390a] The term of the sentence must be equal to the aggregate of—

- (a) the appropriate custodial term, and
- (b) a further period of 1 year for which the offender is to be subject to a licence,

and must not exceed the maximum term of imprisonment with which the offence is punishable in the case of a person aged 21 or over.

(3)[^key-6990ffbad14e214a95b8887d928e390a] For the purposes of subsection (2), the “appropriate custodial term” is the term that, in the opinion of the court, ensures that the sentence is appropriate.

(4)[^key-88c3a313a6e75cff087c551cc2799427] Where an offence is found to have been committed over a period of 2 or more days, or at some time during a period of 2 or more days, it must be taken for the purposes of subsection (1A) to have been committed on the last of those days.

(5)[^key-6a534cabd7c271bf52bae8d2c125126f] In the case of an offence listed in paragraph 4(b) of Schedule 13, this section applies in relation to convictions on or after the day on which paragraph 6 of Schedule 27 to the Crime and Policing Act 2026 comes into force.
