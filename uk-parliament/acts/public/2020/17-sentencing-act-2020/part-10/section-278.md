# Sentencing Act 2020, section 278

## 278. Required special custodial sentence for certain offenders of particular concern[^key-02599b1fb313f9cf7d0dff55f6f681eb]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where the court imposes a sentence of imprisonment for an offence where—

- (a) the offence is listed in Schedule 13,
- (aa)[^key-fcfc7d4a07c36bb9b98f07bea985532b] if the offence is listed in Part 3 of that Schedule (offences involving or connected with a threat to national security), the offender was convicted of the offence on or after the day on which section 7(5) of the Sentencing Act 2026 came into force,
- (b) the person—
  - (i)[^key-ed187ec69e1f9ff95072d74c9e1353e6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii) is aged 21 or over when convicted of the offence, and
- (c) the court does not impose any[^key-ab9d21ce6d9a2eccf432e71751c4294d] of the following for the offence (or for an offence associated with it)—
  - (i) an extended sentence under section 279,
  - (ia)[^key-4ff8ae7edd1a848dd1bd58169df2154f] a serious terrorism sentence under section 282A, or
  - (ii) a sentence of imprisonment for life.

(1A)[^key-60b5c9d3a9e6aa2325efbff07c77f5ac] But this section does not apply if—

- (a) the offender was aged under 18 when the offence was committed, and
- (b) the offence—
  - (i) if listed in Part 1 of Schedule 13 (offences involving or connected with terrorism),[^key-783d7cb1ab1294669384a1a78d930033] was committed before the day on which section 22 of the Counter-Terrorism and Sentencing Act 2021 came into force, or
  - (ia)[^key-fe9596b9a7ecc78e628fef1b2215c2e9] if listed in Part 3 of Schedule 13, was committed before the day on which section 7(5) of the Sentencing Act 2026 came into force,
  - (ii) is listed in Part 2 of Schedule 13 (sexual offences).

(2)[^key-80e7cb3183b949f3d2b7973bbb21c12d] The term of the sentence must be equal to the aggregate of—

- (a) the appropriate custodial term, and
- (b) a further period of 1 year for which the offender is to be subject to a licence,

and must not exceed the maximum term of imprisonment with which the offence is punishable.

(3)[^key-80e7cb3183b949f3d2b7973bbb21c12d] For the purposes of subsection (2), the “appropriate custodial term” is the term that, in the opinion of the court, ensures that the sentence is appropriate.

(4)[^key-b4668d253398eb789ff7c72b8324e198] Where an offence is found to have been committed over a period of 2 or more days, or at some time during a period of 2 or more days, it must be taken for the purposes of subsection (1A) to have been committed on the last of those days.

(5)[^key-d2e53272c77d3020b81f62bd43e7f9d2] In the case of an offence listed in paragraph 4(b) of Schedule 13, this section applies in relation to convictions on or after the day on which paragraph 6 of Schedule 27 to the Crime and Policing Act 2026 comes into force.
