> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (303).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/17

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 378A. Domestic abuse protection orders[^c-148ac12b8fda4f4f603e13aae3665444]

*Extent: E+W*

See Part 3 of the Domestic Abuse Act 2021 (and in particular section 31(3) of that Act) for the power of a court to make a domestic abuse protection order when dealing with an offender for an offence.
