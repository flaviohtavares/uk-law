# United Kingdom Internal Market Act 2020, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-02-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Office for the Internal Market panel and task groups[^key-035174daba080e05add2cb8cc5b8c8f5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The CMA may authorise an Office for the Internal Market task group constituted under Schedule 4 to the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 to do anything required or authorised to be done by the CMA under this Part (and such an authorisation may include authorisation to exercise the power conferred on the CMA by this subsection).

(2) Schedule 3 contains provision about the Office for the Internal Market panel and Office for the Internal Market task groups.
