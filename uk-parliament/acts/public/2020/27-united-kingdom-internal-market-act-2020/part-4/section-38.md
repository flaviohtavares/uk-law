# United Kingdom Internal Market Act 2020, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-02-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2020/27

## 38. Reports under Part 4[^key-e7bb4e42ac42c44b32c5a7b6077e85e1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where under this Part the CMA publishes (or arranges for the publication of) a report it may exclude from the report—

- (a) any information the disclosure of which the CMA thinks is contrary to the public interest;
- (b) commercial information the disclosure of which the CMA thinks might significantly harm the legitimate business interests of any person;
- (c) information relating to the private affairs of an individual the disclosure of which the CMA thinks might significantly harm the individual's interests.
