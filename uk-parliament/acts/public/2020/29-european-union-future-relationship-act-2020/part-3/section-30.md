# European Union (Future Relationship) Act 2020, section 30

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Interpretation of agreements[^key-1ba7e70ed3b8cb364afbdcc203dea0a4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A court or tribunal must have regard to Article 4[^key-1304d910c8efb9c3347a893817e55797] of the Trade and Cooperation Agreement (public international law) when interpreting that agreement or any supplementing agreement.
