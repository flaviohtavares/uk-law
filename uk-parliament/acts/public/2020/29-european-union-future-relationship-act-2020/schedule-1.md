# European Union (Future Relationship) Act 2020, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Information to be included in notification of conviction

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 1(6)

### Introductory

**1.**[^key-b96674349afe2f6d038576022d0bd3fe] (1) This Schedule sets out the information that is required by section 1 to be included in a notification of an individual's conviction.

(2) The information mentioned in paragraphs 4, 8 to 12, 15 and 18 is required to be included only if it is recorded in the criminal records database referred to in subsection (1) or (as the case may be) subsection (2) of that section.

### Information about the individual

**2.**[^key-0b37b772a08520b766268fe5b12ea00e] The individual's name.

**3.**[^key-88bd6d376554182bb27d6d2b9e17c24b] Any previous name of the individual.

**4.**[^key-feced524613c55e81fd314baf8322f2d] Any other name used by the individual.

**5.**[^key-3778f0dfb3f0285743d4ab34983982fc] The individual's gender.

**6.**[^key-f726d963869095b7d70af669794dab09] The individual's date and place of birth.

**7.**[^key-20b56e8dd85e4cc5da0ea33ad565abd9] The individual's nationality or nationalities.

**8.**[^key-7b42b1dbb2cdbe3104d25947cdd84aab] The names of the individual's parents.

**9.**[^key-9371f330fdfb6f832393240bd1c13f9d] The number of any passport held by the individual.

**10.**[^key-d49f021bf6638d61e2c497120b9c3ee6] The issue number (if any) and description of any other identity document (within the meaning of section 7 of the Identity Documents Act 2010) held by the individual.

**11.**[^key-917d470c648a3fcc9f43002efecbd06f] The individual's fingerprints.

**12.**[^key-8a89699c05fd8b94ab28cc6b1dec5d0a] A photograph or other image of the individual's face.

### Information about the conviction

**13.**[^key-69ecef6f3e27db9158766531a7fec856] The date of the conviction.

**14.**[^key-e8098ef92bc15200b7f3c147cdf4c178] (1) In the case of a conviction by or before a court, the court by or before which the individual was convicted.

(2) In any other case, the person or description of person by or before which the individual was convicted.

**15.**[^key-6f7b9ea071644d97b1a2c02e7465089d] The reference number of the conviction.

**16.**[^key-3fa3e6aef0aea095a98b1deb151ccced] The offence of which the individual was convicted.

**17.**[^key-fcbb528c596ae7a6c39f9593e0c181f3] The date on which the offence was committed (or, if the offence was committed over a period of time, that period).

**18.**[^key-71d4772dd8d53e670cc8f9b3b420ffb3] The place where the offence was committed.

**19.**[^key-7f72ae67a326324c4467717388e6e411] Any sentence imposed in respect of the offence.

In this paragraph “**sentence**” includes anything that under section 376(1) and (3) of the Armed Forces Act 2006 (punishments awarded by officers etc) is to be treated as a sentence for the purposes of that Act.

**20.**[^key-019a798927794aca6561d1c1e48d1c75] Any other order made in respect of the offence.
