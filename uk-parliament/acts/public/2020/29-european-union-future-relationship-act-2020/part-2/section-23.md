# European Union (Future Relationship) Act 2020, section 23

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Licences for access to the international road haulage market[^c-62e8fc98254c0a3f1d9b012f9fb3b0bd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Regulation [(EC) No. 1072/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/1072) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 October 2009 on common rules for access to the international road haulage market, for the model licence set out in Annex 2 (UK licence for the Community model) substitute the model licence set out in Part B of Appendix Road.A.1.3 to Annex Road-1 to the Trade and Cooperation Agreement.
