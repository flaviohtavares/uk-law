# Fisheries Act 2020, section 18

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Further provision about licences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act “**sea fishing licence**” means a licence granted under section 15 (licensing of British fishing boats) or section 17 (licensing of foreign fishing boats).

(2) Schedule 3 contains further provision about sea fishing licences.
