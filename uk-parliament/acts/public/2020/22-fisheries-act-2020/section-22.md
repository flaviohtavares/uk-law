# Fisheries Act 2020, section 22

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Minor and consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 4 contains—

- (a) in Part 1, consequential amendments relating to the provision made by section 12;
- (b) in Part 2—
  - (i) minor amendments relating to the licensing of fishing boats, and
  - (ii) consequential amendments relating to the provision made by sections 14 to 21;
- (c) in Part 3, transitional provision relating to the provision made by sections 14 to 21.
