# Fisheries Act 2020, section 47

> Text as at 2024-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Conservation of Seals

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 9 contains amendments of the Conservation of Seals Act 1970 and the Wildlife (Northern Ireland) Order 1985 (1985/171 (N.I. 2)) in connection with prohibiting the killing, injuring or taking of seals.
