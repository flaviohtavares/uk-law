# Fossil Fuel Levy Act 1998

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Fossil Fuel Levy Act 1998 [^c878666](repealed)

1998 c. 5

**No longer in force (ended 2001-10-01).**

## 1. Supplies on which levy charged.

(1) Section 33 of the [^c878667]Electricity Act 1989 (fossil fuel levy) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (8), for the definitions of “leviable electricity” and “non-fossil fuel generating station”, there is substituted—

> “**leviable electricity**” means electricity which—
>
> - (a) is generated by a fossil fuel generating station;
> - (b) is generated by a generating station fuelled by nuclear fuel; or
> - (c) is generated in pursuance of qualifying arrangements by a generating station fuelled or driven otherwise than by a fossil fuel or nuclear fuel;

.

(3) After that subsection there is inserted—

> (9) The Secretary of State may by regulations amend this section so as to—
>
> - (a) omit the word “leviable” in subsection (2) above; and
> - (b) omit the definition of “**leviable electricity**” in subsection (8) above

.

## 2. Short title, commencement and extent.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Fossil Fuel Levy Act 1998.

(2)[^c878668] This Act shall come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order made by statutory instrument appoint.

(3) An order under subsection (2) may include such transitional provisions or savings as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

(4) This Act does not extend to Northern Ireland.
