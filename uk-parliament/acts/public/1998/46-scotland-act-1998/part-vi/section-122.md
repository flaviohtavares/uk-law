# Scotland Act 1998, section 122

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 122. Crown land.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In any provision about the application of any pre-commencement enactment to Crown land—

- (a) references to a Minister of the Crown or government department shall be read as including the Scottish Ministers and the Lord Advocate, and
- (b) references to a Minister of the Crown or government department having the management of the land shall be read as including any member of the Scottish Government[^key-1a6eec0a223077253e974615c6946a62] having the management of the land.

(2) In this section, “**Crown land**” has the meaning given by section 242 of the [^c12395091]Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997.
