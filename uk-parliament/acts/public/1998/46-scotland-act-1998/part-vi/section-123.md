# Scotland Act 1998, section 123

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/46

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123. Stamp duty.[^c12395101]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 55 of the [^c12395111]Finance Act 1987 (Crown exemption from stamp duty) references to a Minister of the Crown shall be read as including the Scottish Ministers, the Lord Advocate and the Parliamentary corporation.
