# Scotland Act 1998, section 94

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94. Private legislation.[^c12394891]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where a pre-commencement enactment makes provision which has the effect of—

- (a) requiring any order to be confirmed by Act of Parliament, or
- (b) requiring any order (within the meaning of the [^c12394901]Statutory Orders (Special Procedure) Act 1945) to be subject to special parliamentary procedure,

and power to make, confirm or approve the order in question is exercisable by the Scottish Ministers by virtue of section 53.

(2) The provision shall have effect, so far as it relates to the exercise of the power to make, confirm or approve the order by virtue of section 53, as if it required the order—

- (a) to be confirmed by an Act of the Scottish Parliament, or
- (b)[^c12394911] (as the case may be) to be subject to such special procedure as may be provided by or under such an Act.
