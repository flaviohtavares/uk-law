## 90A. BBC Trust member for Scotland

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-4126dcae40dda3ad7a51ad5b5bab7f64] A Minister of the Crown must not exercise without the agreement of the Scottish Ministers functions relating to selection for a particular appointment by which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4126dcae40dda3ad7a51ad5b5bab7f64]

- (a) a person is to become a member of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4126dcae40dda3ad7a51ad5b5bab7f64] BBC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4126dcae40dda3ad7a51ad5b5bab7f64] Trust and hold a Scottish post, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4126dcae40dda3ad7a51ad5b5bab7f64]
- (b) an existing member of the Trust is to hold a Scottish post. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4126dcae40dda3ad7a51ad5b5bab7f64]

(2) “**Scottish post *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4126dcae40dda3ad7a51ad5b5bab7f64]**” means a position, held as a member of the Trust, with specific reference to Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4126dcae40dda3ad7a51ad5b5bab7f64]
