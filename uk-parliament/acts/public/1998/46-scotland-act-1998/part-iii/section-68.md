# Scotland Act 1998, section 68

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Borrowing by statutory bodies.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a member of the Scottish Government[^key-1a6eec0a223077253e974615c6946a62] lends money to a body established under any enactment, the rate of interest on the loan shall not be less than the lowest rate determined by the Treasury under section 5 of the [^c12394621]National Loans Act 1968 in respect of similar loans made out of the National Loans Fund on the day the loan is made.

(2) A body established under any enactment shall not, in pursuance of a power conferred by virtue of an Act of the Scottish Parliament, borrow money in a currency other than sterling except with the consent of the Scottish Ministers given with the approval of the Treasury.
