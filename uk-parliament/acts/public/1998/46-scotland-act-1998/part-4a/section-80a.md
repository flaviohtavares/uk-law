# Scotland Act 1998, section 80A

## 80A. Overview of Part 4A[^key-af17c17310905c28ed55bf5db3825706]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part—

- (a) Chapter 2 confers on the Scottish Parliament power to set a rate or rates[^key-565ef6c1bd48004ee71c9a32a474abe8] of income tax to be paid by Scottish taxpayers, and
- (b) The remaining Chapters[^key-3ae464ffcf02b51097c1db1b771216d3] specify the taxes about which the Scottish Parliament may make provision in the exercise of the power conferred by section 28(1).

(2) The power to make provision about a devolved tax is subject to the restrictions imposed by—

- (a) subsection (3), and
- (b) the other provisions of this Part.

(3) A devolved tax may not be imposed where to do so would be incompatible with any international obligations.

(4) In this Act “**devolved tax**” means a tax specified in this Part as a devolved tax.
