# Scotland Act 1998, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Defamatory statements.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of the law of defamation—

- (a) any statement made in proceedings of the Parliament, and
- (b) the publication under the authority of the Parliament of any statement,

shall be absolutely privileged.

(2) In subsection (1), “**statement**” has the same meaning as in the [^c12394171]Defamation Act 1996.
