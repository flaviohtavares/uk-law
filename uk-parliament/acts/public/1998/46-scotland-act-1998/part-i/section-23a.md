# Scotland Act 1998, section 23A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23A. Power to impose requirements on specific bodies[^c-807b663b7efdba5cc0d4ca8c233263df]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 23 applies in relation to requirements imposed on a person in connection with the discharge of the functions of a body mentioned in subsection (2) in relation to Scotland with the omission of—

- (a) the words after paragraph (b) in subsection (1), and
- (b) subsections (2) and (6).

(2) The bodies are—

- (a) the Commissioners of Northern Lighthouses,
- (b) the Office of Communications, and
- (c) the Gas and Electricity Markets Authority.
