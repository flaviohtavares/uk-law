## 32. Submission of Bills for Royal Assent.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It is for the Presiding Officer to submit Bills for Royal Assent.

(2) The Presiding Officer shall not submit a Bill for Royal Assent at any time when—

- (a) the Advocate General, the Lord Advocate or the Attorney General is entitled to make a reference in relation to the Bill under section 32A or[^key-38c9c22217b126f45a4a4ea3a7792c0b] 33,
- (b) any such reference has been made but has not been decided or otherwise disposed of by the Supreme Court[^c20292681], or
- (c) an order may be made in relation to the Bill under section 35.

(2A)[^key-7bb0c82f910ed9ad17c5d0296277f750] The Presiding Officer shall not submit a Bill for Royal Assent if the Supreme Court has decided on a reference made in relation to the Bill under section 32A(2)(b) that any provision of the Bill relates to a protected subject-matter, unless since the decision the Bill has been approved in accordance with standing orders made by virtue of section 36(5).

(3) The Presiding Officer shall not submit a Bill in its unamended form for Royal Assent if—

- (a) the Supreme Court has[^c20292701] decided that the Bill or any provision of it would not be within the legislative competence of the Parliament. [^key-32ed32fe89e25c18a0f8da09a0c72138]...
