## 50. Validity of acts of Scottish Ministers etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The validity of any act of a member of the Scottish Government[^key-1a6eec0a223077253e974615c6946a62] or junior Scottish Minister is not affected by any defect in his nomination by the Parliament or (as the case may be) in the Parliament’s agreement to his appointment.
