# Scotland Act 1998, section 55

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Functions exercisable with agreement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A statutory provision, or any provision not contained in an enactment, which provides for a Minister of the Crown to exercise a function with the agreement of, or after consultation with, any other Minister of the Crown shall cease to have effect in relation to the exercise of the function by a member of the Scottish Government[^key-1a6eec0a223077253e974615c6946a62] by virtue of section 53.

(2) In subsection (1) “**statutory provision**” means any provision in a pre-commencement enactment other than paragraph 5 or 15 of Schedule 32 to the [^c12394371]Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 (designation of enterprise zones).
