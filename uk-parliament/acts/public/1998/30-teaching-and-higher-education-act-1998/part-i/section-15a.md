# Teaching and Higher Education Act 1998, section 15A

## 15A. Supply of information by contractor, agency, &c.[^key-442705315006b8333a47d0eb68f7b5af]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies to arrangements made by one person (the “**agent**”) for a registered teacher (the “teacher”)[^key-46f57bb9ceb302ca8ad21716f9caff5e] to carry out work at the request of or with the consent of a relevant employer (whether or not under a contract).

(2) Subsection (4) applies[^key-0afd35d718210f7da5495f429d2256be] where an agent—

- (a) has terminated the arrangements on a ground mentioned in section 15(1A)[^key-b320e42931a59208b5a4996fc5dc497c],
- (b) might have terminated the arrangements on a ground mentioned in that subsection[^key-829ecfe5ab409db0e9f3eec2b707fb6e] if the worker had not terminated them, or
- (c) might have refrained from making new arrangements for a teacher[^key-e01b9b5a3fa07804a965fe56ba7d0614] on a ground mentioned in that subsection[^key-240f71966eabede9c69816f4e25210ad] if he had not ceased to make himself available for work.

(3)[^key-2b773b36c5639d35e90b0874f75146b6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) [^key-0d8e991cf08ad89a225c53ee90e7aa74]... The agent shall provide prescribed information to the Council[^key-7b724fc1318d431037430bba5354a9a4].

(5)[^key-ffbc5004b99fd21d5d1cef18aaeb0656] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) If the National Assembly thinks that an agent has failed or is likely to fail to comply with a duty arising under subsection (4), the National Assembly may direct the person to comply with the duty.

(7)[^key-39f2c373f3b7255ddb9949c0179ae840] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8) A direction under subsection (6) shall be enforceable, on the application of the National Assembly, by a mandatory order.

(9) Subsection[^key-a523be43af139ff559cf5d5589807aaf] (5) of section 15 shall apply for the purposes of this section as it applies[^key-49f44b793836c64cd1f32fac72986fb5] for the purposes of that section.
