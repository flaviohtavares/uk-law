# Teaching and Higher Education Act 1998, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (29).** Text as at 2026-05-12. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/30
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Power of funding councils in England and Wales to secure provision of certain further education.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Section 3 of the [^c13266301]Further and Higher Education Act 1992 (functions with respect to part-time education and full-time education for those over 18) shall be amended as follows.

(2) For subsection (3) there shall be substituted—

> (3) A council may secure the provision of facilities for education within paragraph (a) or (b) of subsection (1) above—
>
> - (a) for the population of their area, where they are not required to do so by virtue of that subsection, or
> - (b) for other persons.

(3) In subsection (5), for “to which subsection (1) above applies” there shall be substituted “ within paragraph (a) or (b) of subsection (1) above ”.
