## SCHEDULE 13 (affected by repeal, see note) — Control of school premises by governing bodies (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e][^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

### Community and community special schools: general (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

**1.**[^c-d04193ef52f213b5225dc1253e61606e] (1) This paragraph applies to a community or community special school. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

(2) The occupation and use of the premises of the school, both during and outside school hours, shall be under the control of the governing body, subject to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

- (a) any directions given by the local education authority under sub-paragraph (3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]
- (b) any transfer of control agreement entered into by the governing body under paragraph 2; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]
- (c) any requirements of an enactment other than this Act or regulations made under it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

(3) The local education authority may give such directions as to the occupation and use of the premises of a community or community special school as they think fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

(4) In exercising control of the occupation and use of the premises of the school outside school hours the governing body shall have regard to the desirability of those premises being made available for community use. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

### Transfer of control agreement in case of community or community special school (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

**2.** (1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2), the governing body of a community or community special school may enter into a transfer of control agreement with any body or person if their purpose, or one of their purposes, in doing so is to promote community use of the whole or any part of the school premises. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

(2) The governing body shall not enter into any transfer of control agreement which makes or includes provision for the use of the whole or any part of the school premises during school hours unless they have first obtained the local education authority’s consent to the agreement in so far as it makes such provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

(3) A transfer of control agreement shall be taken to include the following terms, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

- (a) that the governing body shall notify the controlling body of any directions given to the governing body under paragraph 1(3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]
- (b) that the controlling body, in exercising control of the use of any premises subject to the agreement— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]
  - (i) shall do so in accordance with any directions from time to time notified to that body in pursuance of paragraph (a); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e] shall have regard to the desirability of the premises being made available for community use; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]
- (c) that, if reasonable notice is given in writing by the governing body to the controlling body that such of the premises subject to the agreement as may be specified in the notice are reasonably required for use by or in connection with the school at such times as may be so specified, then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]
  - (i) the use of the specified premises at those times shall be under the control of the governing body, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e] accordingly, those premises may be used at those times by or in connection with the school for such purposes as may be specified in the notice, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

  even though their use at those times would, apart from this paragraph, be under the control of the controlling body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

(4) Sub-paragraph (5) applies where a transfer of control agreement makes express provision for the use of any school premises which are subject to the agreement to be occasionally under the control of the governing body, instead of the controlling body, in such circumstances, at such times or for such purposes as may be provided by or under the agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

(5) In such a case paragraph (c) of sub-paragraph (3) shall not have effect in relation to the transfer of control agreement if, at the time of entering into it, the governing body were of the opinion that the express provision would be more favourable to the interests of the school than the term that would otherwise be included by virtue of that paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

(6) Where the governing body enter into a transfer of control agreement, they shall so far as reasonably practicable secure that the controlling body exercises control in accordance with any such directions as are notified to that body in pursuance of sub-paragraph (3)(a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

(7) In this paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]

- “**the controlling body *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]**” means the body or person (other than the governing body) which has control of the use of the whole or any part of the school premises under the transfer of control agreement in question; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]
- “**transfer of control agreement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]**” means an agreement which (subject to sub-paragraph (3)) provides for the use of so much of the school premises as may be specified in the agreement to be under the control, at such times as may be so specified, of such body or person as may be so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a608a4ab7e93ce4819dc4fa00912676e]
