# School Standards and Framework Act 1998, section 3

## 3. Payment of grant in connection with reductions in infant class sizes.[^c14007961]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-eae25ad35f8fee81e0dcbbfbb502e970] Regulations shall make provision for the payment by the Secretary of State of grants to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eae25ad35f8fee81e0dcbbfbb502e970] local authorities[^c-570ae5afeec7f2b1ffee877e975cdee5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eae25ad35f8fee81e0dcbbfbb502e970] in respect of expenditure incurred or to be incurred by them for the purpose of securing that any limit imposed under section 1 is complied with in relation to infant classes at schools maintained by them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eae25ad35f8fee81e0dcbbfbb502e970]

(2) Regulations under this section shall provide for the Secretary of State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eae25ad35f8fee81e0dcbbfbb502e970]

- (a) to withhold grants under the regulations from a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eae25ad35f8fee81e0dcbbfbb502e970] local authority[^c-5177265949adaa73e26402711f36dc2e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eae25ad35f8fee81e0dcbbfbb502e970] where no proposed arrangements by that authority have been approved by him under section 2; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eae25ad35f8fee81e0dcbbfbb502e970]
- (b) when determining whether any grant (and, if so, what amount) should be paid by him under the regulations to a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eae25ad35f8fee81e0dcbbfbb502e970] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eae25ad35f8fee81e0dcbbfbb502e970], to have regard to their proposed arrangements as so approved. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eae25ad35f8fee81e0dcbbfbb502e970]

(3) Regulations under this section may provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eae25ad35f8fee81e0dcbbfbb502e970]

- (a) for the payment of grant under the regulations to be dependent on the fulfilment of such conditions as may be determined by or in accordance with the regulations, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eae25ad35f8fee81e0dcbbfbb502e970]
- (b) for requiring *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eae25ad35f8fee81e0dcbbfbb502e970] local authorities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eae25ad35f8fee81e0dcbbfbb502e970] to whom payments have been made under the regulations to comply with such requirements as may be so determined. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eae25ad35f8fee81e0dcbbfbb502e970]
