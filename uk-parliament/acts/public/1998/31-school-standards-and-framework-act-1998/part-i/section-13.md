## 13. Disapplication of pay and conditions order in relation to teachers at participating schools.[^c14008061]

*Extent: E+W*

For section 3 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291] [^c14008071]School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions Act 1991 there shall be substituted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291]

> **3. Special provisions as to schools in education action zones. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c18758291]**
>
> (1) A pay and conditions order shall not apply to the statutory conditions of employment of the school teachers in a participating school the governing body of which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291]
>
> - (a) have, by notice to the Secretary of State, made an application for exemption; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291]
> - (b) pursuant to that application are for the time being exempted from subsections (6) and (7) of section 2 above by virtue of an order under subsection (4) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291]
>
> (2) Before making an application under subsection (1) above, the governing body of the participating school concerned shall consult the school teachers employed at the school with respect to the proposed application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291]
>
> (3) A notice of application under subsection (1) above shall specify a date, at least three months after the date of the notice, with effect from which the governing body of the participating school concerned intend to make their own provision as to the statutory conditions of employment of the school teachers employed at the school. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291]
>
> (4) On receipt of a notice of application under subsection (1) above the Secretary of State may, by statutory instrument, make an order— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291]
>
> - (a) naming the school; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291]
> - (b) specifying, as the date with effect from which, by virtue of the order, subsections (6) and (7) of section 2 above are not to apply, the date specified in the notice of application or such other date as may be agreed between the governing body and the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291]
>
> (5) Where by virtue of an order under subsection (4) above a pay and conditions order ceases to apply in relation to any school, the statutory conditions of employment of the school teachers employed at the school shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291]
>
> - (a) such as may be determined by the governing body, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291]
> - (b) so far as the governing body have not made any determination with respect to any such conditions of employment, those having effect under the order immediately before it ceased to apply; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291]
>
> and (so far as necessary) the local education authority shall give effect to any such determination of the governing body. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291]
>
> (6) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291]**participating school *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291]**” means one of the schools for the time being included in an order under section 10(1) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 establishing an education action zone. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18758291]
