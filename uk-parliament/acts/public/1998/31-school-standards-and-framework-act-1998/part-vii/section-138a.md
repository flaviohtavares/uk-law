# School Standards and Framework Act 1998, section 138A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/31
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 138A. Regulations made by Welsh Ministers under section 71(7A) or sections 89 to 90[^c-82a89e35379ae332ac3d85a5963f7160][^M_F_60fa6a2b-8868-4e14-9557-4316ad033882]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any power of the Welsh Ministers to make regulations under section 71(7A) or[^M_F_ca0c498a-c3ee-4755-e233-dc53dac5c2f1] sections 89 to 90 shall be exercisable by statutory instrument.

(2) A statutory instrument containing any such regulations made by the Welsh Ministers shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of the National Assembly for Wales.

(3) Any such regulations may make different provision for different cases, circumstances or areas and may contain such incidental, supplemental, saving or transitional provisions as the Welsh Ministers think fit.
