## 134. Publication of inspection reports.[^c14010731]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-8e568ae3e8ec55c1d848f69c0667f88c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-b235f98839be0d9340c82ee3e86cc737] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In section 39 of the [^c14010771]Education Act 1997 (reports of inspections of local authorities[^key-570ae5afeec7f2b1ffee877e975cdee5]), at the end of subsection (4) (publication of such reports) there shall be added “; and section 42A(2) to (4) of the School Inspections Act 1996 shall apply in relation to the publication of any such report as they apply in relation to the publication of a report under any of the provisions mentioned in section 42A(2).”
