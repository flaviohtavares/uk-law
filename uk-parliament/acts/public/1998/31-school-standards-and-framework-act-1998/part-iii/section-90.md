## 90. Reference of objections to Welsh Ministers.[^c14009811][^c-19657be0075be231d5f779e249526dc8][^c-ac89bb558437797c44174bba4c077772]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where—

- (a) admission arrangements have been determined by an admission authority under section 89(4), but
- (b)[^c-68cb89636d0d713a285f2db4f25b2dfd] an appropriate body wishes to make an objection about those arrangements, and
- (c) the objection does not fall within any description of objections prescribed for the purposes of this paragraph,

that body may refer the objection to the Welsh Ministers[^c-499a9d0153285d7ee78ecd2beca971fa].

(2) Where—

- (a) admission arrangements have been determined by an admission authority under section 89(4), but
- (b) any parent of a prescribed description wishes to make an objection about those arrangements, and
- (c) the objection falls within any description of objections prescribed for the purposes of this paragraph,

that person may refer the objection to the Welsh Ministers[^c-9231f6626b3625575c8cfe2f5ff350da].

(2A)[^c-ca7af25cbaf3cffce874e05af6d18a01] Where any objection is referred to the Welsh Ministers, they shall decide whether, and (if so) to what extent, the objection should be upheld.

(5)[^c-d25e7641b22675ee84f7501cdf1794d4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

*Extent: E*

(5A)[^c-5e4ef1e894b4af9bd7e1e3891060f066] Where the Welsh Ministers are[^c-fee84910df186db68748abe1678f05d4] required by virtue of (2A)[^c-ed435b4b1e97083cf932280a6b1f63f4] to decide whether to uphold an objection to admission arrangements, they[^c-652653ef363f215d709c9ca8c56b8c10] may consider whether it would be appropriate for changes to be made to any aspect of the admission arrangements, whether or not they would be required to do so for the purpose of determining the objection.

- (d (Extent: E)) their[^c-0947a8bc4d7c988f084157ac0455d29a] *(Extent: E)* reasons for the decisions mentioned in paragraphs (a) *(Extent: E)* and (b)[^c-a8d904537e767a379a1248bd6ac6cd38] *(Extent: E)*. *(Extent: E)*
