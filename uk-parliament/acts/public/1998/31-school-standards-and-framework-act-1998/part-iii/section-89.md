## 89. Procedure for determining admission arrangements.[^c14009781][^c14009791]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-ac1b4a2f28a2c2f337f2a1d55acdee13][^c-d7bfd6ef5a4c715862d14298a96ce80c] The admission authority for a maintained school in Wales[^c-2a36847eb982615fdb0214515e4952af] shall, before the beginning of each school year, determine in accordance with this section the admission arrangements which are to apply for that year.

(1ZA)[^c-cf6cd2632710aeb902e41874981b0722] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1A)[^c-74461718e11d841f4fedaec645f0eecd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-3790b52899d273fe6b33473715cd6b17][^c-95686a7dd55a416325a57bcd6d963a4d][^M_F_3ee2093f-cf09-4aff-fb43-df4b04ea46bb] Before determining the admission arrangements which are to apply for a particular school year, the admission authority shall consult the following about the proposed arrangements, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_3ee2093f-cf09-4aff-fb43-df4b04ea46bb]

- (a) whichever of the governing body and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_3ee2093f-cf09-4aff-fb43-df4b04ea46bb] local authority[^c-5177265949adaa73e26402711f36dc2e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_3ee2093f-cf09-4aff-fb43-df4b04ea46bb] are not the admission authority, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_3ee2093f-cf09-4aff-fb43-df4b04ea46bb]
- (b) the admission authorities for all other maintained schools in the relevant area or for such class of such schools as may be prescribed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_3ee2093f-cf09-4aff-fb43-df4b04ea46bb]
- (c) the governing bodies for all community and voluntary controlled schools in the relevant area (so far as not falling within paragraph (a) or (b)), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_3ee2093f-cf09-4aff-fb43-df4b04ea46bb] [^c-061cdafd577da20140bdfa8a25dd5f23]...
- (d) the admission authorities for maintained schools of any prescribed description; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_3ee2093f-cf09-4aff-fb43-df4b04ea46bb] and *(Extent: E)*[^c-d620c38100baccd919d2b31a97d49fd0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_3ee2093f-cf09-4aff-fb43-df4b04ea46bb]

*Extent: E*

- (e) in the case of a foundation or voluntary school which has a religious character for the purposes of Part 2, such body or person representing the religion or religious denomination in question as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_3ee2093f-cf09-4aff-fb43-df4b04ea46bb]

(2A)[^c-3790b52899d273fe6b33473715cd6b17][^c-95686a7dd55a416325a57bcd6d963a4d] Subsection (2) does not apply in relation to the proposed admission arrangements for a particular school year if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_3ee2093f-cf09-4aff-fb43-df4b04ea46bb]

- (a) the admission authority are the school’s governing body, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_3ee2093f-cf09-4aff-fb43-df4b04ea46bb]
- (b) prescribed conditions are satisfied in relation to that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_3ee2093f-cf09-4aff-fb43-df4b04ea46bb]

(3)[^c-3790b52899d273fe6b33473715cd6b17][^c-95686a7dd55a416325a57bcd6d963a4d] In subsection (2) “**the relevant area**” means—

- (a) the area of the local authority; or
- (b) if regulations so provide, such other area (whether more or less extensive than the area of the local authority) as may be determined by or in accordance with the regulations.

(4)[^c-3790b52899d273fe6b33473715cd6b17][^c-95686a7dd55a416325a57bcd6d963a4d] Once the admission authority have carried out any such consultation, the authority shall—

- (a) determine that their proposed arrangements (either in their original form or with such modifications as the authority think fit) shall be the admission arrangements for the school year in question; and
- (b) (except in such cases as may be prescribed) notify the appropriate bodies *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_0c576b6d-42cd-4610-b44c-7aa59b71d8c2] of those admission arrangements.

(5)[^c-5e25de92ee1b3c88705c85d9ddfe4113][^c-3790b52899d273fe6b33473715cd6b17][^c-95686a7dd55a416325a57bcd6d963a4d] Where an admission authority—

- (a) have in accordance with subsection (4) determined the admission arrangements which are to apply for a particular school year, but
- (b) at any time before the end of that year consider that the arrangements should be varied in view of a major change in circumstances occurring since they were so determined,

the authority shall (except in a case where their proposed variations fall within any description of variations prescribed for the purposes of this subsection) refer the proposed variations to the Welsh Ministers[^c-816fd7ba8a4e6580e52dda0eae358033], and shall (in every case) notify the appropriate bodies *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_0c576b6d-42cd-4610-b44c-7aa59b71d8c2] of the proposed variations.

(6)[^c-5e25de92ee1b3c88705c85d9ddfe4113][^c-3790b52899d273fe6b33473715cd6b17][^c-95686a7dd55a416325a57bcd6d963a4d] The Welsh Ministers[^c-720f2652cba4dd192f1610e3f63cbb86] shall consider whether the arrangements should have effect with those variations until the end of that year; and if they determine[^c-9011084a6954162bd4ef00374f66a630] that the arrangements should so have effect or that they should so have effect subject to such modification of those variations as they may determine[^c-6ab81844aae86a7c374778cb0595e0c3]—

- (a) the arrangements shall have effect accordingly as from the date of their[^c-3ea670f14d3e41c69e5f43d3dc5089a6] determination; and
- (b) the admission authority shall (except in such cases as may be prescribed) notify the appropriate bodies *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_d8851d08-c3e2-4a90-d679-87f7f1775015] of the variations subject to which the arrangements are to have effect.

(7)[^c-6f67c0dbe2b3e32d18294c11410f0706] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8)[^c-3790b52899d273fe6b33473715cd6b17][^c-95686a7dd55a416325a57bcd6d963a4d] Regulations may make provision—

- (a) specifying matters to which any consultation required by subsection (2) is, or is not, to relate;
- (b) as to the manner in which, and the time by which, any such consultation is to be carried out;
- (c) as to the manner in which, and the time by which, any notification required by this section is to be given;
- (d) specifying matters which are, or are not, to constitute major changes in circumstances for the purposes of subsection (5)(b);
- (e) authorising an admission authority, where they have in accordance with subsection (4) determined the admission arrangements which are to apply for a particular school year, to vary those arrangements to such extent or in such circumstances as may be prescribed;
- (f) for the application of any of the requirements of subsections (5) and (6) to variations proposed to be made by virtue of paragraph (e), or to any prescribed description of such variations, as if they were variations proposed to be made under subsection (5);
- (fa (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_16fa15a2-c808-4973-a312-fcdd564f89a3] requiring an admission authority who have made a determination of a prescribed description under this section to publish such information relating to the determination (including information as to the authority’s reasons for making the determination) as may be prescribed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_16fa15a2-c808-4973-a312-fcdd564f89a3]
- (g) as to such other matters connected with the procedure for determining or varying admission arrangements under this section as the Welsh Ministers consider[^c-301f7313a5638b370347b40b5809dbc8] appropriate.

(8A)[^c-3790b52899d273fe6b33473715cd6b17][^c-95686a7dd55a416325a57bcd6d963a4d][^M_F_e635233c-8c0d-43e8-a9d6-fed2344d2465] The power under paragraph (fa) of subsection (8) to require an admission authority to publish information includes power to require them to publish it— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e635233c-8c0d-43e8-a9d6-fed2344d2465]

- (a) by giving a notice containing the information to prescribed persons, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e635233c-8c0d-43e8-a9d6-fed2344d2465]
- (b) in any other prescribed manner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_e635233c-8c0d-43e8-a9d6-fed2344d2465]

(9)[^c-3790b52899d273fe6b33473715cd6b17][^c-95686a7dd55a416325a57bcd6d963a4d][^M_F_9137f97b-95bd-4002-a661-e06840d8f1b6] Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9137f97b-95bd-4002-a661-e06840d8f1b6] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9137f97b-95bd-4002-a661-e06840d8f1b6] are the admission authority for a community or voluntary controlled school, they shall consult the governing body before making any reference under subsection (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_9137f97b-95bd-4002-a661-e06840d8f1b6]

(10)[^c-3790b52899d273fe6b33473715cd6b17][^c-95686a7dd55a416325a57bcd6d963a4d][^c-d319258172d01cd9ddd14ee247fc75a8] In this section, “**the appropriate bodies**”, in relation to an admission authority, means—

- (a) the bodies or persons whom they were required to consult under subsection (2), or would but for subsection (2A) have been required to consult, [^c-7f9a0b2802806cea6b4b9112e2d4f797]...
