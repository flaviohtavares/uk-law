# School Standards and Framework Act 1998, section 88Q

## 88Q. Reports under section 88P: provision of information[^c-fff7d3364a51a0a8358573f4fba9676d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A relevant person must, on request, provide a local authority[^c-5177265949adaa73e26402711f36dc2e] in England with such information as the authority may reasonably require for the purpose of enabling the authority to fulfil their duties under section 88P.

(2) In subsection (1), “**relevant person**”, in relation to a local authority, means—

- (a) an admission authority (other than the local authority) for a maintained school in the area of the local authority;
- (b)[^c-ded67be7264dd987572b11826fe3b184] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) any member of an appeal panel constituted under section 94 by—
  - (i) the local authority, or
  - (ii) the governing body of a foundation or voluntary aided school in the area of the local authority;
- (d) the proprietor of—
  - (i) an Academy school[^c-303b5599ea2482c6ddc6b02147dd69c5],
  - (ii) a city technology college, or
  - (iii) a city college for the technology of the arts,

  in the area of the local authority;
- (e) any other local authority in England;
- (f) such other person as may be prescribed.
