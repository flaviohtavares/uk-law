## 88O. Sharing of information by local authorities[^c-fff7d3364a51a0a8358573f4fba9676d][^c-570ae5afeec7f2b1ffee877e975cdee5]

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State may by regulations require local authorities in England to provide other local authorities with such information as may be required by them in connection with the exercise of any of their functions under this Chapter.
