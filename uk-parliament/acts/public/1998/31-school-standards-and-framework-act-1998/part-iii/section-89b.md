# School Standards and Framework Act 1998, section 89B

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89B. Co-ordination of admission arrangements[^c-bce747c857403a20f075f44561310e86][^c-c49d7ed1049e5c6936b5872546e931ab][^c-d982c640ef3e4a6fb406b5213b8dd1eb]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Regulations may require a local authority[^c-5177265949adaa73e26402711f36dc2e] in Wales[^c-28f160daabffccd576f2730ea01b5519]—

- (a) to formulate, for any academic year in relation to which prescribed conditions are satisfied, a qualifying scheme for co-ordinating the arrangements for the admission of pupils to maintained schools in their area, and
- (b) to take prescribed action with a view to securing the adoption of the scheme by themselves and each governing body who are the admission authority for a maintained school in their area.

(2) Subject to subsection (3), the Welsh Ministers[^c-f76c0bcdd8eea0b79fbc0d3ef5fc7033] may make, in relation to the area of a local authority in Wales[^c-f5bea5d597db77e3568a14b3b62b95b6] and an academic year, a scheme for co-ordinating the arrangements, or assisting in the co-ordination of the arrangements, for the admission of pupils to maintained schools in that area.

(3) A scheme may not be made under subsection (2) in relation to a local authority and an academic year if, before the prescribed date in the year preceding the year in which that academic year commences—

- (a) a scheme formulated by the local authority in accordance with subsection (1) is adopted in the prescribed manner by the persons mentioned in paragraph (b) of that subsection, and
- (b) the authority provide the Welsh Ministers[^c-4e38eed44a25e1308c58b6a8a46f14b6] with a copy of the scheme and inform them[^c-212d0261a992b88e65ac110265a6cea8] that the scheme has been so adopted.

(4) The Welsh Ministers[^c-5b781b9abf3cc190c9bdb2856774c4a5] may by regulations require local authorities[^c-570ae5afeec7f2b1ffee877e975cdee5] in Wales[^c-591fdf5ce7d8372f82a70b8e29a6af2b] to provide other local authorities with such information as may be required by such other authorities[^c-7feaf9bc44a90d842f9bc0b54c5ceee0] in connection with the exercise of any of their functions under this Chapter.

(5) Regulations may provide—

- (a) that each local authority in Wales[^c-da07b2e23f73e859e59e41f2b1aae1cd] shall secure that, subject to such exceptions as may be prescribed, no decision made by any admission authority for a maintained school in their area to offer or refuse a child admission to the school shall be communicated to the parent of the child except on a single day, designated by the local authority, in each year, or
- (b) that, subject to such exceptions as may be prescribed, a decision made by the admission authority for a maintained school in Wales[^c-a4e673f956845d1080c96dc72db9fd05] to offer or refuse a child admission to the school shall not be communicated to the parent of the child except on a prescribed day.

(6) In this section—

- “**academic year**” means a period commencing with 1st August and ending with the next 31st July;
- “**qualifying scheme**” means a scheme that meets prescribed requirements.

(7)[^c-6600f20836414cdcae585ac04b0dc968] Nothing in this section applies in relation to arrangements for the admission to maintained schools in Wales of pupils—

- (a) who—
  - (i) have ceased to be of compulsory school age, or
  - (ii) will have ceased to be of compulsory school age before education is provided for them at the school, or
- (b) for the purpose of receiving sixth form education.
