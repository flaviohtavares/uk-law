### Revision of schemes

**2.**[^key-b004c3b160f003a1dd0adcb7c6bde894] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**2A (Extent: E).** (1 (Extent: E)) A *(Extent: E)* local authority *(Extent: E)* may, in accordance with this paragraph, revise the whole or part of the scheme maintained by them under section 48(1). *(Extent: E)*

(2 (Extent: E)) In revising the scheme, the *(Extent: E)* local authority *(Extent: E)* shall take into account any guidance given by the Secretary of State, whether— *(Extent: E)*

- (a (Extent: E)) generally, or *(Extent: E)*
- (b (Extent: E)) in relation to that authority or any class or description of *(Extent: E)* local authorities[^key-570ae5afeec7f2b1ffee877e975cdee5] *(Extent: E)* to which that authority belongs, *(Extent: E)*

as to the provisions the Secretary of State regards as appropriate for inclusion in the scheme. *(Extent: E)*

(3 (Extent: E)) As regards any proposed variation of the scheme, the authority— *(Extent: E)*

- (a (Extent: E)) shall first consult the governing body and head teacher of every school maintained by the authority (within the meaning of this Chapter), and *(Extent: E)*
- (b (Extent: E)) shall then submit a copy of their proposals to the authority's schools forum for their approval. *(Extent: E)*

(4 (Extent: E))[^key-07ac62bd641aa5abfff665dc5bded92b] The Secretary of State may by a direction revise the whole or any part of a scheme maintained by a local authority in England as from such date as may be specified in the direction. *(Extent: E)*

(5 (Extent: E)) Before giving such a direction the Secretary of State must consult the local authority and such other persons as the Secretary of State thinks fit. *(Extent: E)*

**2B (Extent: E).** (1 (Extent: E)) Regulations may make provision preventing schemes as revised from coming into force unless they are approved in accordance with the regulations by the *(Extent: E)* local authority *(Extent: E)*'s schools forum or by the Secretary of State. *(Extent: E)*

(2 (Extent: E)) The regulations may in particular— *(Extent: E)*

- (a (Extent: E)) prescribe circumstances in which proposals which have been submitted to a *(Extent: E)* local authority *(Extent: E)*'s schools forum may be submitted to the Secretary of State, *(Extent: E)*
- (b (Extent: E)) enable the schools forum or the Secretary of State to approve proposals with modifications, and *(Extent: E)*
- (c (Extent: E)) enable the schools forum or the Secretary of State, in giving their or his approval, to specify the date on which the scheme as revised is to come into force. *(Extent: E)*
