## 118A. Duties of local authority in respect of childcare[^c18425761][^c-5177265949adaa73e26402711f36dc2e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325] A *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325] local authority *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325] shall review annually the sufficiency of childcare provision for their area. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325]

(2 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325] In carrying out a review for the purposes of subsection (1), a *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325] local authority *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325]— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325] may have regard to any facilities which they expect to be available outside their area for providing childcare; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325] shall have regard to any guidance given from time to time by the Secretary of State. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325]

(3 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325] A *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325] local authority *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325] shall also establish and maintain a service providing information to the public relating to the provision of childcare and related services in their area. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325]

(4 (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325] In relation to the function, form and content of a service established and maintained under subsection (3), a *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325] local authority *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325] shall have regard to any guidance given from time to time by the Secretary of State. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5f482ad96f0799ed826b1d3790850325]
