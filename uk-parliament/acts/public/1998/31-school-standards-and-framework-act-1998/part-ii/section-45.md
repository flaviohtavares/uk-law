## 45. Maintained schools to have budget shares.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of the financing of maintained schools by local authorities[^key-570ae5afeec7f2b1ffee877e975cdee5], every such school shall have, for each funding period[^key-72f3211d880b89281e9b2c8bbe5cb485], a budget share which is allocated to it by the authority which maintains it.

(1A)[^key-1a1c2c3bd9fa1745f681ea45ae1cb014][^key-2d3069ffee9472e838cd663a19b7d496] In this Chapter “**maintained school**” means—

- (a) a community, foundation or voluntary school,
- (b) a community or foundation special school, [^key-0789f5d0e970da09880b556e067f389a]...
- (c) a maintained nursery school , or[^key-f177204c7656a603debf7d12fbae6214]
- (d) a pupil referral unit in England.

(1B)[^key-e2c663a70ab820aecbbc67eb06e2ec1d] In this Chapter “**funding period**” means a financial year or such other period as may be prescribed.

(2) Sections 45A to[^key-34a59819fadb22a4f14330a034ad1489] 47 have effect for determining the amount of a school’s budget share for a funding period[^key-cdee98c187e74a7a8a035bb9172e7e13].

(3)[^c14008931] In this Chapter—

- (a) references to schools maintained by a local authority[^key-5177265949adaa73e26402711f36dc2e] do not include pupil referral units[^key-700254ee84c47e5b989951d9f672eec3] in Wales[^key-12a0d0fa8ae66e7d23ecadf97d272731];
- (aa)[^key-3fbd7c7ad931b58d6141dedc065ba70b] references to the governing body of a maintained school or of a school maintained by a local authority shall be read, in relation to a pupil referral unit in England, as references to the management committee for the unit (in spite of paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the Education Act 1996);
- (ab) references to governors shall be read, in relation to a pupil referral unit in England, as references to the members of the management committee for the unit;
- (b) references, in a context referring to a local authority, to a maintained school or to a school maintained by such an authority shall be read as including a new school—
  - (i) which on implementation of proposals under any enactment[^c18366271] will be a community, foundation or voluntary school or a community or foundation special school maintained by the authority, and
  - (ii) which has a temporary governing body; and
- (c) references to the governing body of a maintained school or of a school maintained by a local authority shall accordingly be read as including the temporary governing body of a new school falling within paragraph (b).

(4)[^c14008931] In this Chapter “new school” (without more) has the meaning given by section 72(3).
