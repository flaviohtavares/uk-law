## 23. Charitable status of maintained schools, etc.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The following shall be charities [^c-9289470230743dd52d9b985dc138c8ea]...—

- (a) the governing body of any foundation, voluntary or foundation special school; and
- (b) any foundation body established under section 21;

but no governing body of a community or community special school shall be a charity.

(1A)[^c-c8c58bf4676459c07f82b5774838842c] Any body to which subsection (1)(a) or (b) applies is, as a result of its inclusion in Schedule 3 to the Charities Act 2011, an exempt charity for the purposes of that Act.

(2)[^c-d4a5a5a9a400859eaf27ad8a56da4968] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Any foundation established otherwise than under this Act which has no property other than the premises of any school or schools falling within subsection (1)(a) shall be a charity and is an institution which is to be treated for the purposes of section 31(3) of the Charities Act 2011 as if that provision applied to it.[^c-f0a89fc6db5bd44d885694e74e5af078]

(4) In this section—

- (a)[^c-5d1ef7a9b69ba312c04956e65eb3b4bb] “institution” has the same meaning as in the Charities Act 2011;
- (b) “**premises**” includes a teacher’s dwelling-house.
