# School Standards and Framework Act 1998, section 49

## 49. Maintained schools to have delegated budgets.[^key-058a750876d66622bf461b076d84a231][^key-5f4141f0d4844b4b132345cf521f94a7][^key-bb62fb2dc203dc9f18c0281a7fb8193f][^key-e9760d1fc1f75855c2bc3caf4cf5c3e6][^key-b3df481dcc50656708b3a2c9971d41de][^key-5f4141f0d4844b4b132345cf521f94a7][^key-bb62fb2dc203dc9f18c0281a7fb8193f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Every maintained school shall have a delegated budget.

(2 (Extent: E))[^key-ab2b66761c660601c56db21ef89c9666] A new school shall have a delegated budget from a date determined in accordance with regulations. *(Extent: E)*

(2A (Extent: E)) Regulations under subsection (2) may— *(Extent: E)*

- (a (Extent: E)) enable the date that would otherwise apply by virtue of the regulations to be varied in accordance with the regulations, on the application of the *(Extent: E)* local authority[^key-5177265949adaa73e26402711f36dc2e] *(Extent: E)*, by the authority's schools forum or by the Secretary of State, and *(Extent: E)*
- (b (Extent: E)) make provision about the respective powers of the schools forum and the Secretary of State in relation to any application to vary that date. *(Extent: E)*

(3)[^key-ab2b66761c660601c56db21ef89c9666] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-f8c4ec1192365cb1eaca299cc237dbeb] Subject to—

- (a) section 50 (right of governing body to spend budget share where school has a delegated budget),
- (b) paragraph 4 of Schedule 15 (power of governing body to spend amounts out of budget share where delegation of budget suspended),
- (c) section 489(2) of the [^c14008961]Education Act 1996 (education standards grants), and
- (d) any provisions of the scheme,

a local authority may not delegate to the governing body of any maintained school the power to spend any part of the authority’s non-schools education budget[^key-2f2311f7a1e6d412633143b66774b54a][^M_F_361585ed-8e0f-409a-b801-8ff7af1a3fa2] or schools budget.

(5) Any amount made available by a local authority to the governing body of a maintained school (whether under section 50 or otherwise)—

- (a) shall remain the property of the authority until spent by the governing body or the head teacher; and
- (b) when spent by the governing body or the head teacher, shall be taken to be spent by them or him as the authority’s agent.

(6) Subsection (5)(b) does not apply to any such amount where it is spent—

- (a) by way of repayment of the principal of, or interest on, a loan, or
- (b)[^c14008971] (in the case of a voluntary aided school) to meet expenses payable by the governing body under paragraph 3(1) or (2) of Schedule 3 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18756301] expenditure payable by the governing body under paragraph 3(1) of Schedule 3 ,[^key-d83aeadf216ef7a84e7dcfc22e71d6b4] [^key-47af0b28387e5c9cafa2afd9d2f5abfa]... or paragraph 8 of Schedule 8 to the Education Act 2002 [^key-c1362da5b453b3e753d2ccfd5f40078c]... *(Extent: W)*[^key-f4199ac0745318df749d5e0908ebe348] or paragraph 4 of Schedule 3 to, the School Standards and Organisation (Wales) Act 2013 *(Extent: W)*.

(7)[^key-c57d6aac07c9104cd4340581d36b6682] In this Part—

- (a) references to a school having a delegated budget are references to the governing body of the school being entitled to manage the school’s budget share; and
- (b) where a school has a delegated budget the governing body are accordingly said to have a right to a delegated budget.
