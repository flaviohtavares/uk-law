## 51. Suspension of financial delegation for mismanagement etc.[^key-058a750876d66622bf461b076d84a231][^key-5f4141f0d4844b4b132345cf521f94a7][^key-bb62fb2dc203dc9f18c0281a7fb8193f][^key-e9760d1fc1f75855c2bc3caf4cf5c3e6][^key-b3df481dcc50656708b3a2c9971d41de][^key-5f4141f0d4844b4b132345cf521f94a7][^key-bb62fb2dc203dc9f18c0281a7fb8193f]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 15 (which provides for the suspension by a local authority[^key-5177265949adaa73e26402711f36dc2e] of a governing body’s right to a delegated budget in the case of failure to comply with requirements as to delegation or of financial mismanagement, etc.) shall have effect.
