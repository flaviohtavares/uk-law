## 77. Control of disposals or changes in use of school playing fields.[^c14009581]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c14009591][^c18330141][^key-01071a0bc225dcf377099a75dcb2458d] Subject to subsections (2A) and (2B), except[^key-8b7654e10904801e090f10201e0bf7ed] with the consent of the Secretary of State, a body or trustees[^key-4a94fafed3dc3ecc5401503b6545bcf7] to whom this subsection applies shall not dispose of any playing fields—

- (a) which are, immediately before the date of the disposal, used by a maintained school for the purposes of the school, or
- (b) which are not then so used but have been so used at any time within the period of 10 years ending with that date.

(2)[^c14009601][^key-5157a0a8d0e4efb1bd15eeede57a78ab] Subsection (1) applies to—

- (a) a local authority;
- (b) the governing body of a maintained school;
- (c) a foundation body;
- (d) the trustees of a foundation, voluntary or foundation special school.

(2A)[^key-922b5362ac75fa1c2e9adb4fa63b74f3] Subsection (1) applies in the case of a disposal by the trustees of a foundation, voluntary or foundation special school, only if the disposal is of land falling within paragraph A13(1), (2) or (3) of Schedule 22.

(2B) Subsection (1) does not apply—

- (a) to a disposal in pursuance of a transfer order under paragraph A23 of that Schedule, [^key-842aebeb4880ce19af79d74bead05077]...
- (b) to a disposal to which paragraph 5 or 6 of Schedule 22 (disposals on discontinuance) applies or[^key-1f83915fb16c70703ce1e2fc2fb3d8b5]
- (c) to a disposal in pursuance of—
  - (i) a transfer scheme under paragraph 1 or 2 of Schedule 1 to the Academies Act 2010 (transfer to person concerned with running of Academy), or
  - (ii) a direction under paragraph 10 of Schedule 1 to that Act (direction to transfer to local authority or person concerned with running of Academy school[^key-375c28d0dafb809ed02b5e97eccaa1fb], where Academy order made).

(3) Subject to[^key-b49a1243a27168d8a9617c3f7ffd129d] subsection[^key-2cbecc765e1ea4362eff7f896749c584] (4A), except with the consent of the Secretary of State, a body or trustees to whom subsection (1) applies[^key-bab7f86bd93687d80c1e6737bbb94713] shall not take any action (other than the making of a disposal which falls within subsection (1) or is excluded from that subsection by subsection[^key-3ba451ceb6284f6c5cfdf6c5f249a034] (2B)(a), (b) or (c)[^key-7f0fee48e2cc42afd87e4c5a10e431dd]) which is intended or likely to result in a change of use of any playing fields—

- (a) which are, immediately before the date when the action is taken, used by a maintained school for the purposes of the school, or
- (b) which are not then so used but have been so used at any time within the period of 10 years ending with that date,

whereby the playing fields will be used for purposes which do not consist of or include their use as playing fields by such a school for the purposes of the school , or by an[^key-e657da2460f4fa9707b5d6ef2cbe3add] Academy school[^key-710c0b73d1962d5007846f0a04d68ff9] for the purposes of the Academy school.

(4)[^key-7f12bd921579ee99388914f209630600] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4A)[^key-57f8dfc6ff2dfa33632fa38658b8c998] Subsection (3) applies in relation to the trustees of a foundation, voluntary or foundation special school only if the playing fields in question are land falling within paragraph A13(1), (2) or (3) of Schedule 22.

(4B)[^key-662222e30d3dc4cd424fd6445d79480a] On receiving an application for consent under subsection (1) or (3), the Secretary of State may direct that the playing fields, or any part of them, be transferred to a person concerned with the running of an Academy school[^key-a73ac4cee4b283a99e00e9246b656e20], subject to the payment by that person or the Secretary of State of such sum by way of consideration (if any) as the Secretary of State determines to be appropriate.

(5) For the purposes of this section the Secretary of State’s consent may be given in relation to a particular disposal or change of use or generally in relation to disposals or changes of use of a particular description, and in either case may be given subject to conditions.

(6) This section has effect despite anything in section 123 or 127 of the [^c14009611]Local Government Act 1972 (general power to dispose of land) or in any other enactment; and any consent which a local authority are required to obtain by virtue of this section shall be in addition to any consent required by virtue of either of those sections.

(7) In this section—

- “local authority” includes—[^key-ad5b93faa07d02ac93e37075938cdd0e]
  - (a) a non-metropolitan district council for an area for which there is a county council, and
  - (b) a parish council;
- “[^key-d141aea1d70fd0c8d7dd30c8bdc30284]**maintained school**” includes a maintained nursery school;
- “**playing fields**” means land in the open air which is provided for the purposes of physical education or recreation, other than any prescribed description of such land.

(8) For the purposes of this section any reference to a maintained school includes, in relation to any time falling before the appointed day, a reference to the school as—

- (a) a county, voluntary or maintained special school, or
- (b) a grant-maintained or grant-maintained special school,

within the meaning of the [^c14009621]Education Act 1996.

(9) Nothing in this section applies in relation to Wales.
