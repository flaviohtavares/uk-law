## 78. Rating of maintained schools.

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of Part III of the [^c14009631]Local Government Finance Act 1988 (non-domestic rating) the occupier of any hereditament so far as consisting of the premises of a maintained school shall be taken to be—

- (a) the local authority[^key-5177265949adaa73e26402711f36dc2e], where it is a community, voluntary controlled or community special school; or
- (b) the governing body, where it is a foundation, voluntary aided or foundation special school.
