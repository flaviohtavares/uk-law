## 52. Financial statements: Wales.[^c-a5f5ddca5af9026b2fe12681c1a3299e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Before the beginning of each financial year *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0826fa6fdd5a39368cb975240b04105a] prescribed period a local authority[^c-5177265949adaa73e26402711f36dc2e] in Wales[^c-dcb74f3ef79fed26b0c21d44e1730907] shall prepare a statement containing such information relating to their planned education[^c-cbe02ff90a7d108960f62d2dbc73ce6f] expenditure in that year *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7f6dba21b54e6d4f36d8de5bbcc3f683] that period as may be prescribed.

(1A)[^c-c0dfa99e07ebb82b25faf3a50526dc64] The periods prescribed for the purposes of subsection (1)—

- (a) must consist of one or more funding periods, and
- (b) may include funding periods in respect of which (by virtue of their inclusion in one or more earlier periods prescribed for the purposes of that subsection) information relating to planned education expenditure has previously been required under that subsection.

(2) After the end of each financial year *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8997bf0214eb18669efebe93ec4370ed] prescribed period a local authority in Wales[^c-c1d7d43ea48f120efdb52987e6d136ea] shall prepare a statement containing such information with respect to the following matters as may be prescribed—

- (a) the planned education expenditure in that year *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3726a8c0e64c1950a5746a2d79a50fe3] period specified in the statement prepared by the authority under subsection (1),
- (b) education expenditure actually incurred, or treated by the authority as having been incurred, by them in the year *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3726a8c0e64c1950a5746a2d79a50fe3] period, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4be3d3f1f413a78252c11f48b15c7ad]
- (c) any other resources allocated by the authority in the year *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3726a8c0e64c1950a5746a2d79a50fe3] period to schools maintained by them during any part of the year *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3726a8c0e64c1950a5746a2d79a50fe3] period. and[^c-86b4629deedf2492d952799dbaedd81f]
- (d) accountable resources held, received or expended in the year *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3726a8c0e64c1950a5746a2d79a50fe3] period by any person in relation to a school maintained by the authority.

(2A)[^c-0574902e585b4e76eed1156643e3240b] In subsection (2)(d), “**accountable resources**”, in relation to a maintained school, means any resources which are not provided by the local authority but in respect of which an obligation is imposed on the governing body of the school by virtue of regulations under section 44 of the Education Act 2002 (accounts of maintained schools).

(2B)[^c-8e3c796e5bc8eee239590874f21378e9] The periods prescribed for the purposes of subsection (2) must consist of one or more funding periods.

(3) A statement under this section shall—

- (a) be prepared in such form, and
- (b) be published in such manner and at such times,

as may be prescribed.

(4) The authority shall furnish the governing body and head teacher of each school maintained by them with a copy of each statement prepared by the authority under this section or, if regulations so provide, with a copy of such part or parts of it as may be prescribed.

(5) A governing body provided with such a copy under subsection (4) shall secure that a copy of it is available for inspection (at all reasonable times and free of charge) at the school.

(6) Subsection (5) does not apply to a temporary governing body of a new school at any time before the school opening date.

(7)[^c-5958b0dd175fd053f223b78ee1c6fa8e] In this section “education expenditure” has the same meaning as in section 45A.
