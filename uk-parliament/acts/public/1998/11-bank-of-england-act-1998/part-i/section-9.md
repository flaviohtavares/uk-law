# Bank of England Act 1998, section 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (41).** Text as at 2025-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/11
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Consequential amendments.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 14 of the [^c1119763]National Debt Reduction Act 1786 and section 32 of the [^c1119764]Life Annuities Act 1808, for “deputy governor” there is substituted “ deputy governors ”.

(2) In section 55 of the [^c1119765]National Debt Act 1870, the first reference to the Deputy Governor of the Bank of England shall be treated as a reference to a Deputy Governor of the Bank of England.

(3) In section 3(3) of the [^c1119766]Bank of England Act 1946, after “this Act” there is inserted “ and the Bank of England Act 1998 ”.
