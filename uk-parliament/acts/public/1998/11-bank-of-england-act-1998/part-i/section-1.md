# Bank of England Act 1998, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (41).** Text as at 2025-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/11

## 1. Court of directors.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There shall continue to be a court of directors of the Bank.

(2)[^key-e0e80fab7846f529eb57debccdc47598] The court shall consist of the following directors appointed by Her Majesty—

- (a) a Governor,
- (b) a Deputy Governor for financial stability,
- (ba)[^key-80541bba62a8e5ebd9da21cf912c113e] a Deputy Governor for markets and banking,
- (c) a Deputy Governor for monetary policy,
- (d) a Deputy Governor for prudential regulation, and
- (e) not more than 9 non-executive directors.

(3)[^key-2445ea0db7de994b598ba6a5c7b16ce4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Schedule 1 shall have effect with respect to the court.
