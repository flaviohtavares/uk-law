## 2A. Financial Stability Objective[^key-bd18f41add39639d551a2db4cac528ae]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An objective of the Bank shall be to protect and enhance[^key-8734b5fd971e45bdddb681573bfa3213] the stability of the financial system[^key-6d37e46e01b7a6a275d8bd95a8da2f4b] of the United Kingdom (the “Financial Stability Objective”).

(2) In pursuing the Financial Stability Objective the Bank shall aim to work with other relevant bodies (including the Treasury and the Financial Conduct Authority)[^key-0a875004870aa778e2b17bcb46f659d6].
