## 3A.. Oversight functions of court of directors[^c-3350d6dbf6195318325a41fa1996ff01][^c-220780e4bef2746f48d8c912a4b3c75d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-79d91a45d48bb97469d76cc69994f715] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The oversight functions of the court of directors[^c-1c67d35e282865b06daf4fc4f35a2a7a] are—

- (a) keeping under review the Bank's performance in relation to—
  - (i) the Bank's objectives (that is, the objectives specified in relation to it in this Act , the objectives specified in relation to the Prudential Regulation Authority in Part 1A of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000[^c-d62146841617def482c00cfb182da07e] and the other objectives for the time being determined by the court of directors of the Bank),
  - (ii) the duty of the Financial Policy Committee under section 9C, [^c-093fcf330fa7c631700fe0a6379c08ae]...
  - (iii) the Bank's strategy determined under section 2,[^c-a94a942e2c89855a48b181c753b57b43]
  - (iv) the Bank's financial stability strategy determined under section 9A, and
  - (v) the strategy of the Prudential Regulation Authority determined under section 2E of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000;
- (b) monitoring the extent to which the objectives set by the court of directors of the Bank in relation to the Bank's financial management have been met;
- (c) keeping under review the internal financial controls of the Bank with a view to securing the proper conduct of its financial affairs;
- (d) the functions conferred on the court of directors[^c-ff5a960707ca320b2b862ff03c0c6c7d] by the provisions listed in subsection (4).
