# Bank of England Act 1998, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (41).** Text as at 2025-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/11
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. General interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-c6d1216260a21d5a77e561a9945b77e5] In this Act—

- “the Bank” means the Bank of England;
- “the capital requirements regulation” means Regulation (EU) No. 575/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council.
- “central securities depository” has the meaning given by point (1) of Article 2(1) of Regulation ([^key-83c2fbe46024766027ff95caa1eb58f5]EU) No 909/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 July 2014 on improving securities settlement in the European Union and on central securities depositories;

(2)[^key-9ea1112db360afe1cab186a0992d3f75] References in this Act to an EU regulation are to that regulation as it forms part of domestic law by virtue of section 3 of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018.
