## 37. Restriction on disclosure of information.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 7 (which restricts the disclosure of information obtained for monetary policy or Bank of England levy[^key-8c1275f4ec5e321bce7cc385ba67e24f] purposes) shall have effect.
