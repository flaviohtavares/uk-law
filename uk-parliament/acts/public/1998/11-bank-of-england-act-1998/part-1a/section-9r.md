# Bank of England Act 1998, section 9R

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (41).** Text as at 2025-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/11

## 9R. Recommendations to other persons[^key-9edc8998d9c0b7c4f92d78ed8c779b0d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Financial Policy Committee may make recommendations to persons other than those mentioned in sections 9O to 9Q.

(2) The recommendations must be made or confirmed in writing.
