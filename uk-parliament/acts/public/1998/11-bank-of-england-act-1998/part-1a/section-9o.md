# Bank of England Act 1998, section 9O

## 9O. Making of recommendations within the Bank[^c-9edc8998d9c0b7c4f92d78ed8c779b0d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Financial Policy Committee may make recommendations within the Bank.

(2) The recommendations may, in particular, relate to—

- (a) the provision by the Bank of financial assistance to financial institutions;
- (b) the exercise by the Bank of its functions in relation to payment systems, settlement systems , clearing houses and central securities depositories[^c-8933ce4097b8801a785e18f96ca87da1].

(3) The Committee may not make recommendations about—

- (a) the provision by the Bank of financial assistance in relation to a particular financial institution, or
- (b) the exercise by the Bank of its powers under Parts 1 to 3 of the Banking Act 2009 or Schedule 11 to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2023[^c-e82bc97bc9272f2dd48a3cb357f6d283] in relation to a particular institution.

(4) The recommendations must be made or confirmed in writing.

(5)[^c-f2b2ef715284c591b080fc5e302d121d] Recommendations by the Committee to the PRA are to be made under section 9Q (and not under this section).
