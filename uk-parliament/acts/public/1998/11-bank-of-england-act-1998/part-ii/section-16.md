## 16. Functions of court of directors .[^c-cd12259f6faa4093be7de7786f7685ea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The court of directors of the Bank[^c-fd4b66a79aecde6d8e8b9ced68ff252c] shall keep the procedures followed by the Monetary Policy Committee under review.

(2) In particular, the court's function[^c-7cfeee8adaead341870f6fb9af5f4804] under subsection (1) shall include determining whether the Monetary Policy Committee[^c-6b2d8b8e2d813a4ab846c8b17f6421be] has collected the regional, sectoral and other information necessary for the purposes of formulating monetary policy.
