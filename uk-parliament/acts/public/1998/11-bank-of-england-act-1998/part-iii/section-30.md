# Bank of England Act 1998, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (41).** Text as at 2025-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/11

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Interpretation of Part III.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**the Authority**” means the Financial Services Authority;
- “**transferred functions**” means the functions transferred to the Authority by this Part.
