# National Minimum Wage Act 1998, section 45A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45A. Persons discharging fines by unpaid work[^key-3af8d08e2443618e8d97c4dbcdfd60b4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person does not qualify for the national minimum wage in respect of any work that he does in pursuance of a work order under Schedule 6 to the Courts Act 2003 (discharge of fines by unpaid work).
