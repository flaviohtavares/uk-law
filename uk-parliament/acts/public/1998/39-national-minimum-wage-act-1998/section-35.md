# National Minimum Wage Act 1998, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Home workers who are not otherwise “workers”.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In determining for the purposes of this Act whether a home worker is or is not a worker, section 54(3)(b) below shall have effect as if for the word “personally” there were substituted “ (whether personally or otherwise) ”.

(2)[^c9532251] In this section “**home worker**” means an individual who contracts with a person, for the purposes of that person’s business, for the execution of work to be done in a place not under the control or management of that person.
