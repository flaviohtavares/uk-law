# National Minimum Wage Act 1998, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Amendments relating to remuneration etc of agricultural workers.[^c9532461][^c9532471]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following enactments, that is to say—

- (a) the *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4bc33834cf747d2dc812e726621a8fa] [^c9532481]Agricultural Wages Act 1948, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4bc33834cf747d2dc812e726621a8fa]
- (b) the [^c9532491]Agricultural Wages (Scotland) Act 1949, and
- (c) the [^c9532501]Agricultural Wages (Regulation) (Northern Ireland) Order 1977,

shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 2 to this Act.

(2) The appropriate authority may by regulations amend—

- (a) the *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-990c120e9b72e88887a5538a491dec0a] [^c9532511]Agricultural Wages Act 1948; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-990c120e9b72e88887a5538a491dec0a]
- (b) the [^c9532521]Agricultural Wages (Scotland) Act 1949;
- (c) section 67 of the [^c9532531]Agriculture Act 1967 (sick pay);
- (d) section 46 of the *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-23e9df111e71cfc0e0e89c2e359250d2] [^c9532541]Agriculture (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1968 (further functions of agricultural wages committees); and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-23e9df111e71cfc0e0e89c2e359250d2]
- (e) the [^c9532551]Agricultural Wages (Regulation) (Northern Ireland) Order 1977.

(3) The amendments that may be made under subsection (2) above are any amendments which are consequential on this Act or on regulations under section 1(4), 2 or 3 above.

(4) The appropriate authority may by regulations amend, or make provision in substitution for,—

- (a) section 7 of the *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bd9c5d580c7b074a562cd1a73f42ed5d] [^c9532561]Agricultural Wages Act 1948 (reckoning of benefits and advantages as payment of wages); *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bd9c5d580c7b074a562cd1a73f42ed5d]
- (b) section 7 of the [^c9532571]Agricultural Wages (Scotland) Act 1949 (similar provision for Scotland) *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fd172a5c7b53266b94699d3c9a78fe4d]; or
- (c) Article 4(3) and (5) of the [^c9532581]Agricultural Wages (Regulation) (Northern Ireland) Order 1977.

(5) Subsection (1) above is without prejudice to subsections (2) to (4) above.

(6) In this section “**the appropriate authority**” means—

- (a) in relation to England and Wales, the Secretary of State and the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food acting jointly; *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c30faa3dd67f48fa8d59283063a7f57d]
- (b) in relation to Scotland, the Secretary of State;
- (c) in relation to Northern Ireland, the Department of Agriculture for Northern Ireland.
