# National Minimum Wage Act 1998, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Prisoners.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A prisoner does not qualify for the national minimum wage in respect of any work which he does in pursuance of prison rules.

(2) In this section—

- “**prisoner**” means a person detained in, or on temporary release from, a prison;
- “**prison**” includes any other institution to which prison rules apply;
- “**prison rules**” means—
  - (a) in relation to England and Wales, rules made under section 47 of the [^c9532411]Prison Act 1952;
  - (b) in relation to Scotland, rules made under section 39 of the [^c9532421]Prisons (Scotland) Act 1989; and
  - (c) in relation to Northern Ireland, rules made under section 13 of the [^c9532431]Prison Act Northern Ireland) 1953.
