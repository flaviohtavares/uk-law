> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/39

## 45B. Immigration: detained persons[^c-3ca5164d36efa52b5d0657e1cc25e5e2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 153A of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (c. 33) (persons detained in removal centres) disqualifies certain persons for the national minimum wage.
