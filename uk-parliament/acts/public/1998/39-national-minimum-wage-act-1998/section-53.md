# National Minimum Wage Act 1998, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Repeals and revocations.[^c9532731]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The enactments mentioned in Schedule 3 to this Act are repealed, and the instruments mentioned in that Schedule are revoked, to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
