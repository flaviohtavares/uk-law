## 12. Requests for OFT to examine agreements.[^c8782181][^key-c9066af127774ef83e7803b248abbd7e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] Sections 13 and 14 provide for an agreement to be examined by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] on the application of a party to the agreement who thinks that it may infringe the Chapter I prohibition. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

(2) Schedule 5 provides for the procedure to be followed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

- (a) by any person making such an application; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
- (b) by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874], in considering such an application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

(3) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision as to the application of sections 13 to 16 and Schedule 5, with such modifications (if any) as may be prescribed, in cases where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]—

- (a) has given a direction withdrawing an exclusion; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
- (b) is considering whether to give such a direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
