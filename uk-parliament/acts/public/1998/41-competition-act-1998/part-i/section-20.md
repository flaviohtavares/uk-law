## 20. Requests for OFT to consider conduct.[^key-70811e51577b1f3207c6a46865f300cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] Sections 21 and 22 provide for conduct of a person which that person thinks may infringe the Chapter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] II *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] prohibition to be considered by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] on the application of that person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]

(2) Schedule 6 provides for the procedure to be followed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]

- (a) by any person making an application, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]
- (b) by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385], in considering an application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]
