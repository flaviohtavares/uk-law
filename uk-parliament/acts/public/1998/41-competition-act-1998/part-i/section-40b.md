# Competition Act 1998, section 40B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/41

## 40B. Statement of policy on penalties[^c-f96c3693e4d4c5bdb053fd35f9a32ebf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The CMA must prepare and publish a statement of policy in relation to the exercise of functions[^c-ac16438baa37712115315085e4731665] under section 40ZE[^c-e0a6e1034088b02ad0577ff6a6b3bebc].

(2) The CMA must, in particular, include a statement about the considerations relevant to the determination of the nature and amount of any penalty imposed under section 40ZE[^c-bf3f76ab701c633840c37059dd261037].

(3) The CMA may revise its statement of policy and, where it does so, it must publish the revised statement.

(4) The CMA must consult the Secretary of State and such other persons[^c-21c1f4c7bacdaf7bfc23e2dbb4995dbd] as it considers appropriate when preparing or revising its statement of policy.

(5) If the proposed statement of policy or revision relates to a matter in respect of which a regulator exercises concurrent jurisdiction, those consulted must include that regulator.

(5A)[^c-21717bb8df9f92379b2e1c24845e96b9] A statement or revised statement of policy may not be published under this section without the approval of the Secretary of State.
