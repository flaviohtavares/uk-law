## 4. Individual exemptions.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526] The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526] OFT[^c-e071c7ae7ddc0277ef28d8589af4dd16] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526] may grant an exemption from the Chapter I prohibition with respect to a particular agreement if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526]

- (a) a request for an exemption has been made to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526] under section 14 by a party to the agreement; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526]
- (b) the agreement is one to which section 9 applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526]

(2) An exemption granted under this section is referred to in this Part as an individual exemption. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526]

(3) The exemption— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526]

- (a) may be granted subject to such conditions or obligations as the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526] considers it appropriate to impose; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526]
- (b) has effect for such period as the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526] considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526]

(4) That period must be specified in the grant of the exemption. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526]

(5) An individual exemption may be granted so as to have effect from a date earlier than that on which it is granted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526]

(6) On an application made in such way as may be specified by rules under section 51, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526] may extend the period for which an exemption has effect; but, if the rules so provide, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526] may do so only in specified circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ad368f670cde9e27cf061a8b8aeda526]
