## 31. Decisions following an investigation.[^c-8e48b201e3d06657ab9e15530a7754d8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If as a result of an investigation the CMA[^c-0fab31162b253a5a3cedb4bf39dbf27c] proposes to make a decision, the CMA must—

- (a) give written notice to the person (or persons) likely to be affected by the proposed decision; and
- (b) give that person (or those persons) an opportunity to make representations.

(2) For the purposes of this section and sections 31A and 31B “decision” means a decision of the CMA —

- (a) that the Chapter I prohibition has been infringed; or[^c-fc05a5e81c7b25cb4a2658adb99b3aa5]
- (b) that the Chapter II prohibition has been infringed;
- (c)[^c-4c2b3b50e9a2f8ee7cb1c53733cfc530] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c-15ec1056aeeed90272fd30ad1ef6f631] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
