## 16. Effect of a decision that the Chapter I prohibition has not been infringed.[^c8782221]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] This section applies to an agreement if the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT[^c-3ffbf76d3ddcb148a82db4dfeb89c1f1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] has determined an application under section 14 by making a decision that the agreement has not infringed the Chapter I prohibition. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

(2) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] is to take no further action under this Part with respect to the agreement unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

- (a) it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] has reasonable grounds for believing that there has been a material change of circumstance since *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] gave *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] decision; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
- (b) it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] has a reasonable suspicion that the information on which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] based *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] decision was incomplete, false or misleading in a material particular. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

(3) No penalty may be imposed under this Part in respect of any infringement of the Chapter I prohibition by an agreement to which this section applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

(4) But the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] may remove the immunity given by subsection (3) if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

- (a) it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] takes action under this Part with respect to the agreement in one of the circumstances mentioned in subsection (2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
- (b) it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] considers that it is likely that the agreement will infringe the prohibition; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
- (c) it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] gives notice in writing to the party on whose application the decision was made that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] is removing the immunity as from the date specified in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

(5) If the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] has a reasonable suspicion that information— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

- (a) on which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] based *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] decision, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
- (b) which was provided to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] by a party to the agreement, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

was incomplete, false or misleading in a material particular, the date specified in a notice under subsection (4)(c) may be earlier than the date on which the notice is given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
