## 5. Cancellation etc. of individual exemptions.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] If the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] OFT[^key-9c212abe8fb0d2fd69792b960a29de71] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] has reasonable grounds for believing that there has been a material change of circumstance since *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] granted an individual exemption, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] may by notice in writing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b]

- (a) cancel the exemption; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b]
- (b) vary or remove any condition or obligation; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b]
- (c) impose one or more additional conditions or obligations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b]

(2) If the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] has a reasonable suspicion that the information on which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] based *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] decision to grant an individual exemption was incomplete, false or misleading in a material particular, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] may by notice in writing take any of the steps mentioned in subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b]

(3) Breach of a condition has the effect of cancelling the exemption. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b]

(4) Failure to comply with an obligation allows the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b], by notice in writing, to take any of the steps mentioned in subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b]

(5) Any step taken by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] under subsection (1), (2) or (4) has effect from such time as may be specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b]

(6) If an exemption is cancelled under subsection (2) or (4), the date specified in the notice cancelling it may be earlier than the date on which the notice is given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b]

(7) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] may act under subsection (1), (2) or (4) on *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b] own initiative or on a complaint made by any person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1c49c1b787343f6e5ee5829af30a614b]
