## 47F. Further provision about claims in respect of loss or damage , or for declaratory relief, before a court or the Tribunal[^key-be1155e0f33993d101fc2cdb4fdfeb6d][^key-0ad2a4f0b4f73188bdfbc8ba544b0668]

Schedule 8A makes further provision about claims in respect of loss or damage , or for declarations or declarators,[^key-86a6753d799bc8f839ba90e1dd1ecb51] before a court or the Tribunal.
