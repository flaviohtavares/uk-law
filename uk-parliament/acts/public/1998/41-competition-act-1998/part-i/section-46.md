## 46. Appealable decisions.[^c8782421][^key-424f4e2840d6d1f43d52fcef72ce850c][^key-4f76050dd4f8c3d3ccedafa011dd4bec][^key-3f462f0a8c890d4b15689a8ef5957e80][^key-6508f548385a63afd506c3478b188c58][^key-cb6211e23c8966c083e553d3edd1dc0c][^key-048654116f172587933c75a73ffaf896][^key-7c3214b2746e68f60c345c1c2a21d570][^key-d4a4d0b3638ea17e4a2f71c91d2cfb4a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any party to an agreement in respect of which the CMA[^key-c6349a3a153f049c0529cc583dd37eb7] has made a decision may appeal to the Tribunal[^key-c584f8dfb60e641ed7a7e0e83e42b9cc] against, or with respect to, the decision.

(2) Any person in respect of whose conduct the CMA has made a decision may appeal to the Tribunal against, or with respect to, the decision.

(3)[^key-9f9ef1240f185b40b2ef52a6e060257f] In this section “decision” means a decision of the CMA —

- (a) as to whether the Chapter I prohibition has been infringed,
- (b)[^key-b61e29c46bfd363c7f5db5236eb917bb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) as to whether the Chapter II prohibition has been infringed,
- (d)[^key-571cb12c9ae78e43a99663112f5bf896] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) cancelling a block or assimilated[^key-2ae0d1f7678e4afa960118feb64a8248][^key-a56bd2fb095b9824231f234a6f56764a] exemption,
- (f)[^key-e60eb2c467d61790e1ea4012b51bccf4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g) not releasing commitments pursuant to a request made under section 31A(4)(b)(i),
- (h) releasing commitments under section 31A(4)(b)(ii),
- (ha)[^key-60f9bd53d6358cfef0181f84ced39ae3] to make directions under section 35,
- (hb) not to make directions under section 35,
- (i) as to the imposition of any penalty under section 36 or as to the amount of any such penalty,

and includes a direction under section 32 or 33[^key-0458bb5c761da4194aee9488bb8586b3] and such other decisions under this Part as may be prescribed.

(4) Except in the case of an appeal against the imposition, or the amount, of a penalty, the making of an appeal under this section does not suspend the effect of the decision to which the appeal relates.

(5) Part I of Schedule 8 makes further provision about appeals.
