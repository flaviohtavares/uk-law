# Competition Act 1998, section 31C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31C. Review of commitments[^c-d6ef032bb575192a48440421cd210ff2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the CMA[^c-b236aa8bfb346f22b3ac3f75df2f1011] is reviewing or has reviewed the effectiveness of commitments accepted under section 31A it must, if requested to do so by the Secretary of State, prepare a report of its findings.

(2) The CMA must—

- (a) give any report prepared by it under subsection (1) to the Secretary of State; and
- (b) publish the report.
