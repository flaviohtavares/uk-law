# Competition Act 1998, section 8

## 8. Block exemptions: procedure.[^c-4aa20b48ed5739a583492b7ec067f0ba]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Before making a recommendation under section 6(1), the CMA[^c-11aa00c2db1fb1beb457b65d9ec5a85d] must—

- (a) publish details of its[^c-51427ba9a9cd807ee95f981c0647f3bc] proposed recommendation in such a way as it thinks most suitable for bringing it to the attention of those likely to be affected; and
- (b) consider any representations about it which are made to it.

(2) If the Secretary of State proposes to give effect to such a recommendation subject to modifications, he must inform the CMA of the proposed modifications and take into account any comments made by the CMA .

(3) If, in the opinion of the CMA , it is appropriate to vary or revoke a block exemption order it[^c-cafc267bab7073127fc50b1b599e1d7a] may make a recommendation to that effect to the Secretary of State.

(4) Subsection (1) also applies to any proposed recommendation under subsection (3).

(5) Before exercising its power to vary or revoke a block exemption order (in a case where there has been no recommendation under subsection (3)), the Secretary of State must—

- (a) inform the CMA[^c-6ac6b20373e51cba1a2f8af4dbe2665e] of the proposed variation or revocation; and
- (b) take into account any comments made by the CMA .

(6) A block exemption order may provide for a block exemption to have effect from a date earlier than that on which the order is made.
