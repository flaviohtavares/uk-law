## 33. Directions in relation to conduct.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the CMA[^c-dcbc7d8af4d54bbfd4a11bd18d6c78ac] has made a decision that conduct infringes the Chapter II prohibition [^c-fc07537a308f1bd7ee82722fbf4f871b]... , it[^c-c35256f3d5b3575839ecbac8e2e64c7d] may give to such person or persons as it considers appropriate such directions as it considers appropriate to bring the infringement to an end.

(2) Subsection (1) applies whether the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a23736025ee1ec32dcb76ba6e9345a7] OFT[^c-8fc437eb17fb5ba1fd58ea39d33c0c08] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a23736025ee1ec32dcb76ba6e9345a7]’s decision is made on *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a23736025ee1ec32dcb76ba6e9345a7] its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a23736025ee1ec32dcb76ba6e9345a7] own initiative or on an application made to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a23736025ee1ec32dcb76ba6e9345a7] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a23736025ee1ec32dcb76ba6e9345a7] under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a23736025ee1ec32dcb76ba6e9345a7]

(3) A direction under this section may, in particular, include provision—

- (a) requiring the person concerned to modify the conduct in question; or
- (b) requiring him to cease that conduct.

(4) A direction under this section must be given in writing.
