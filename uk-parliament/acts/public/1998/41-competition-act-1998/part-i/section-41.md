# Competition Act 1998, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Agreements notified to the Commission.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-11646a8c66d8d6a40490afced39d8bfb] This section applies if a party to an agreement which may infringe the Chapter I prohibition has notified the agreement to the Commission for a decision as to whether an exemption will be granted under Article 85 with respect to the agreement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-11646a8c66d8d6a40490afced39d8bfb]

(2) A penalty may not be required to be paid under this Part in respect of any infringement of the Chapter I prohibition after notification but before the Commission determines the matter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-11646a8c66d8d6a40490afced39d8bfb]

(3) If the Commission withdraws the benefit of provisional immunity from penalties with respect to the agreement, subsection (2) ceases to apply as from the date on which that benefit is withdrawn. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-11646a8c66d8d6a40490afced39d8bfb]

(4) The fact that an agreement has been notified to the Commission does not prevent the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-11646a8c66d8d6a40490afced39d8bfb] OFT[^c-bfedcdc11773bf16e0ceb5a320ceef44] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-11646a8c66d8d6a40490afced39d8bfb] from investigating it under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-11646a8c66d8d6a40490afced39d8bfb]

(5) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-11646a8c66d8d6a40490afced39d8bfb]**provisional immunity from penalties *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-11646a8c66d8d6a40490afced39d8bfb]**” has such meaning as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-11646a8c66d8d6a40490afced39d8bfb]
