## 13. Notification for guidance.[^c8782191]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] A party to an agreement who applies for the agreement to be examined under this section must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

- (a) notify the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT[^key-18d3509c13c7c0b8dea70bf35eedd539] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] of the agreement; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
- (b) apply to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] for guidance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

(2) On an application under this section, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] may give the applicant guidance as to whether or not, in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] view, the agreement is likely to infringe the Chapter I prohibition. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

(3) If the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] considers that the agreement is likely to infringe the prohibition if it is not exempt, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] guidance may indicate— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

- (a) whether the agreement is likely to be exempt from the prohibition under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
  - (i) a block exemption; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] a parallel exemption; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] a section 11 exemption; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
- (b) whether *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] would be likely to grant the agreement an individual exemption if asked to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

(4) If an agreement to which the prohibition applies has been notified to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] under this section, no penalty is to be imposed under this Part in respect of any infringement of the prohibition by the agreement which occurs during the period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

- (a) beginning with the date on which notification was given; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
- (b) ending with such date as may be specified in a notice in writing given to the applicant by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] when the application has been determined. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

(5) The date specified in a notice under subsection (4)(b) may not be earlier than the date on which the notice is given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
