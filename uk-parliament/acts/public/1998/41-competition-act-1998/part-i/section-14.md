## 14. Notification for a decision.[^c8782201]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] A party to an agreement who applies for the agreement to be examined under this section must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

- (a) notify the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT[^c-c7dcaf64e115396625e50d2719593af1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] of the agreement; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
- (b) apply to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] the OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] for a decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

(2) On an application under this section, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] may make a decision as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

- (a) whether the Chapter I prohibition has been infringed; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
- (b) if it has not been infringed, whether that is because of the effect of an exclusion or because the agreement is exempt from the prohibition. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

(3) If an agreement is notified to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] under this section, the application may include a request for the agreement to which it relates to be granted an individual exemption. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

(4) If an agreement to which the prohibition applies has been notified to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] under this section, no penalty is to be imposed under this Part in respect of any infringement of the prohibition by the agreement which occurs during the period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

- (a) beginning with the date on which notification was given; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
- (b) ending with such date as may be specified in a notice in writing given to the applicant by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874] when the application has been determined. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]

(5) The date specified in a notice under subsection (4)(b) may not be earlier than the date on which the notice is given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5e7ca9004c6e5a2ec41b10deeb851874]
