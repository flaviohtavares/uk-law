## 23. Effect of guidance.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] This section applies to conduct if the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] OFT[^key-c7b36c84bcba1cd0287682151b4a2c73] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] has determined an application under section 21 by giving guidance that the conduct is unlikely to infringe the Chapter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] II *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] prohibition. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]

(2) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] is to take no further action under this Part with respect to the conduct to which this section applies, unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]

- (a) it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] has reasonable grounds for believing that there has been a material change of circumstance since *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] gave *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] guidance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]
- (b) it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] has a reasonable suspicion that the information on which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] based *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] guidance was incomplete, false or misleading in a material particular; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]
- (c) a complaint about the conduct has been made to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385].

(3) No penalty may be imposed under this Part in respect of any infringement of the Chapter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] II *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] prohibition by conduct to which this section applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]

(4) But the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] may remove the immunity given by subsection (3) if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]

- (a) it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] takes action under this Part with respect to the conduct in one of the circumstances mentioned in subsection (2); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]
- (b) it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] considers that it is likely that the conduct will infringe the prohibition; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]
- (c) it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] gives notice in writing to the undertaking on whose application the guidance was given that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] is removing the immunity as from the date specified in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]

(5) If the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] has a reasonable suspicion that information— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]

- (a) on which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] based *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] guidance, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]
- (b) which was provided to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385] by an undertaking engaging in the conduct, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]

was incomplete, false or misleading in a material particular, the date specified in a notice under subsection (4)(c) may be earlier than the date on which the notice is given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-58555f33e0d44661ac2017eecb12f385]
