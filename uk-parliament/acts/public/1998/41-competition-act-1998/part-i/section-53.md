## 53. Fees.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e] The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e] OFT[^key-2189d1f92d2c6cf7caca1be3041bd223] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e] may charge fees, of specified amounts, in connection with the exercise by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e] of specified functions under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e]

(2) Rules may, in particular, provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e]

- (a) for the amount of any fee to be calculated by reference to matters which may include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e]
  - (i) the turnover of any party to an agreement (determined in such manner as may be specified); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e] the turnover of a person whose conduct the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e] is to consider (determined in that way); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e]
- (b) for different amounts to be specified in connection with different functions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e]
- (c) for the repayment by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e] of the whole or part of a fee in specified circumstances; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e]
- (d) that an application or notice is not to be regarded as duly made or given unless the appropriate fee is paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e]

(3) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e]

- (a) “**rules *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e]**” means rules made by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e] under section 51; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e]
- (b) “**specified *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e]**” means specified in rules. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70e6a780c7d40f33c7ee2895bbd3839e]
