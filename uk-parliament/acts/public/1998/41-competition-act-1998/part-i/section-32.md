## 32. Directions in relation to agreements.[^c-01b3451db2dfce37ea9eb7392699ef39][^c-51f57caaf029e020594cf22f6cbcf485][^c-04f43aa21467c77316cca1a3edecf021][^c-85eef67649de9b90721e7e8fbeeb3330][^c-efe88d050f96988fcb7ee67e1e7c5c30][^c-b3c394eb1c81787a73888292d2c11aa4][^c-d211af56aab62878bb560cab968295a0][^c-9e33835690a6f1bff0dec5552f2283ea][^c-f48208b043f21b2b74b0c0b6a74a29eb][^c-7514ef98d38aa492c46ebd220d52100e][^c-51f57caaf029e020594cf22f6cbcf485]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the CMA[^c-e8e2ee109794a280c1692cf29d0e590e] has made a decision that an agreement infringes the Chapter I prohibition [^c-31af43275e2a88b661c88429a9be4d62]... , it[^c-677741ac433a869745012dcf66de3ec2] may give to such person or persons as it considers appropriate such directions as it considers appropriate to bring the infringement to an end.

(2) Subsection (1) applies whether the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cad0ebf61458ef9e44aa3b884a985ce5] OFT[^c-e58697093850d065af733403ee99027c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cad0ebf61458ef9e44aa3b884a985ce5]’s decision is made on *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cad0ebf61458ef9e44aa3b884a985ce5] its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cad0ebf61458ef9e44aa3b884a985ce5] own initiative or on an application made to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cad0ebf61458ef9e44aa3b884a985ce5] it *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cad0ebf61458ef9e44aa3b884a985ce5] under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cad0ebf61458ef9e44aa3b884a985ce5]

(3) A direction under this section may, in particular, include provision—

- (a) requiring the parties to the agreement to modify the agreement; or
- (b) requiring them to terminate the agreement.

(4) A direction under this section must be given in writing.
