# Competition Act 1998, section 54

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Regulators.[^c8782461][^key-90c54a04a05af379cec36df93929d707]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part “**regulator**” means —[^key-18f30b5e6568a1fe31ead3b882cb4c47]

- (a)[^key-73d8d76fc21dee0e25b10990bbe460e7] the Office of Communications;
- (b) the Gas and Electricity Markets Authority;
- (c)[^key-08160f83d2957747d8edfe4ad5a454f7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) the Water Services Regulation Authority;[^key-c3eaccb9213b918ed591542fe8bdba00]
- (e) the Office of Rail and Road[^key-4c0eb3add22a90f7f2788ae6bbe7d877];
- (f) the Northern Ireland Authority for Utility Regulation;[^key-22176f99ec12bec963144722da5ff943]
- (g) the Civil Aviation Authority ;[^key-1503472cfee27e1824495550701f713d] and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c6e586ce53188e55e4c94059b397b66e]
- (h)[^key-2c945269eac2aa58c351a5c23976b8c3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (i)[^key-60c8f8ee2234017dd369348326c59064] the Payment Systems Regulator established under section 40 of the Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013.
- (j)[^key-4c6411e3f2f55e57c96d745fd6a7c5af] the Financial Conduct Authority.

(2) Parts II and III of Schedule 10 provide for functions of the CMA[^key-7559e5063579b54ac6b96c0efc2424fd] under this Part to be exercisable concurrently by regulators.

(3) Parts IV and V of Schedule 10 make minor and consequential amendments in connection with the regulators’ competition functions.

(4) The Secretary of State may make regulations for the purpose of co-ordinating the performance of functions under this Part (“**Part I functions**”) which are exercisable concurrently by two or more competent persons as a result of any enactment (including any subordinate legislation) whenever passed or made[^key-5486d45a1f5e556c1908862bce4f20c5].

(5)[^key-b9be33e12bf030283ee7d7dc8b41f11d][^key-7b2e54b17fde069ab671130620fd0b73] The regulations may, in particular, make provision—

- (a) as to the procedure to be followed by competent persons when determining who is to exercise Part I functions in a particular case;
- (b) as to the steps which must be taken before a competent person exercises, in a particular case, such Part I functions as may be prescribed;
- (c) as to the procedure for determining, in a particular case, questions arising as to which competent person is to exercise Part I functions in respect of the case;
- (d) for Part I functions in a particular case to be exercised jointly—
  - (i) by the CMA[^key-56f2e4eaeeb6665265782200b2923e98] and one or more regulators, or
  - (ii) by two or more regulators,

  and as to the procedure to be followed in such cases;
- (e) as to the circumstances in which the exercise by a competent person of such Part I functions as may be prescribed is to preclude the exercise of such functions by another such person;
- (f) for cases in respect of which Part I functions are being, or have been, exercised by a competent person to be transferred to another such person;
- (g) for the person (“**A**”) exercising Part I functions in a particular case—
  - (i) to appoint another competent person (“**B**”) to exercise Part I functions on A’s behalf in relation to the case; or
  - (ii) to appoint officers of B (with B’s consent) to act as officers of A in relation to the case;
- (h) for notification as to who is exercising Part I functions in respect of a particular case.

(6)[^key-b9be33e12bf030283ee7d7dc8b41f11d][^key-7b2e54b17fde069ab671130620fd0b73][^key-5fed47479b2e343c75dc1c18601bac39] Provision made by virtue of subsection (5)(c) may provide for questions to be referred to and determined by the Secretary of State , the CMA[^M_F_f994aaf4-26fc-4f82-d479-7fd013cea19e] or by such other person as may —[^key-788e692db98dbc93f2838baaf397749d]

- (a) prescribe circumstances in which the CMA may decide that, in a particular case, it is to exercise Part 1 functions in respect of the case rather than a regulator;
- (b) be prescribed.

(6A)[^key-42d2e73981b1131e24885f3f81eb7e7a] Where the regulations make provision as mentioned in subsection (6)(a), they must—

- (a) include provision requiring the CMA to consult the regulator concerned before making a decision that the CMA is to exercise Part 1 functions in respect of a particular case, and
- (b) provide that, in a case where a regulator has given notice under section 31(1) that it proposes to make a decision (within the meaning given by section 31(2)), the CMA may only decide that it is to exercise Part 1 functions in respect of the case rather than the regulator if the regulator consents.

(6B)[^key-968aafc15b4a2dccc230b03a9f5d8178] The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision requiring arrangements to be made for the sharing of information between competent persons in connection with concurrent cases.

(6C) For the purposes of subsection (6B), “a concurrent case” is a case in respect of which—

- (a) the CMA considers that Part 1 functions are, or (but for provision made under subsection (5)(e)) would be, exercisable by both it and any regulator;
- (b) any regulator considers that Part 1 functions are, or (but for provision made under subsection (5)(e)) would be, exercisable by it.

(7)[^key-b9be33e12bf030283ee7d7dc8b41f11d][^key-7b2e54b17fde069ab671130620fd0b73] “**Competent person**” means the CMA[^key-f5b97f784007af72bb414e4d46c22288] or any of the regulators.

(8)[^key-dabba991ccbe8328e46939c7eef35eea] In this section, “subordinate legislation” has the same meaning as in section 21(1) of the [Interpretation Act 1978 (c 30)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/30) and includes an instrument made under—

- (a) an Act of the Scottish Parliament;
- (b) Northern Ireland legislation.
