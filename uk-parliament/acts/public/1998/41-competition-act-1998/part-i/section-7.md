## 7. Block exemptions: opposition.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f] A block exemption order may provide that a party to an agreement which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f]

- (a) does not qualify for the block exemption created by the order, but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f]
- (b) satisfies specified criteria, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f]

may notify the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f] OFT[^key-a123c927ebe5c05b5a039ab43a1fa5c4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f] of the agreement for the purposes of subsection (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f]

(2) An agreement which is notified under any provision included in a block exemption order by virtue of subsection (1) is to be treated, as from the end of the notice period, as falling within a category specified in a block exemption order unless the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f]—

- (a) is opposed to its being so treated; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f]
- (b) gives notice in writing to the party concerned of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f] its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f] opposition before the end of that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f]

(3) If the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f] gives notice of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f] its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f] opposition under subsection (2), the notification under subsection (1) is to be treated as both notification under section 14 and as a request for an individual exemption made under subsection (3) of that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f]

(4) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f]**notice period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f]**” means such period as may be specified with a view to giving the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f] OFT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f] sufficient time to consider whether to oppose under subsection (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-68d6f51f27e74f6b350a70f2f1e6df8f]
