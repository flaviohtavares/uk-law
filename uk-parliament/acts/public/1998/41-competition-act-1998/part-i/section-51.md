# Competition Act 1998, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Rules.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The CMA[^key-48cf38cc85a763726dcff186f49d364a] may make such rules about procedural and other matters in connection with the carrying into effect of the provisions of this Part as it[^key-201e5d2d082d44cf8e5543e16f8c8f7f] considers appropriate.

(2) Schedule 9 makes further provision about rules made under this section but is not to be taken as restricting the CMA[^key-fe53541de1d92c9d5013fadbab738a85] powers under this section.

(3) If the CMA[^key-9374d8d525bfa5363304a0817d15275b] is preparing rules under this section it[^key-0320e0ddeb11cd01e55ed61e1e57dabd] must consult such persons as he considers appropriate.

(4) If the proposed rules relate to a matter in respect of which a regulator exercises concurrent jurisdiction, those consulted must include that regulator.

(5) No rule made by the CMA[^key-103dd54e1aeef76946b44073fd439066] is to come into operation until it has been approved by an order made by the Secretary of State.

(6) The Secretary of State may approve any rule made by the CMA —

- (a) in the form in which it is submitted; or
- (b) subject to such modifications as he considers appropriate.

(7) If the Secretary of State proposes to approve a rule subject to modifications he must inform the CMA of the proposed modifications and take into account any comments made by the CMA .

(8) Subsections (5) to (7) apply also to any alteration of the rules made by the CMA .

(9) The Secretary of State may, after consulting the CMA , by order vary or revoke any rules made under this section.

(10) If the Secretary of State considers that rules should be made under this section with respect to a particular matter he may direct the CMA to exercise its[^key-5f09522a2235f185202b3cf8d9d35b90] powers under this section and make rules about that matter.
