# Competition Act 1998, section 74

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Amendments, transitional provisions, savings and repeals.[^c8782591]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The minor and consequential amendments set out in Schedule 12 are to have effect.

(2) The transitional provisions and savings set out in Schedule 13 are to have effect.

(3)[^key-b56e7ababb5c851f761e212b5a5c4e7a] The enactments set out in Schedule 14 are repealed.
