# Northern Ireland Act 1998, section 30A

> Text as at 2026-01-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30A. Secretary of State’s powers in relation to exclusion[^c20116091]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f] This section applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]

- (a) the Monitoring Commission has, or members of that Commission have under the agreement establishing it, made a report containing a recommendation about steps the Assembly might consider taking; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]
- (b) the taking of those steps by the Assembly requires the passing by it of a resolution under section 30(1), (1A), (2) or (3) in relation to a Minister, junior Minister or political party; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]
- (c) the first motion for a resolution under that provision in relation to the Minister, junior Minister or political party concerned that is put to the vote after the making of the report does not attract cross-community support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]

(2) Where this section applies because of the failure of a motion for a resolution under section 30(1), the Secretary of State may by direction exclude the Minister or junior Minister concerned from holding office as a Minister or junior Minister for such period of not less than three months, and not more than twelve months, beginning with the date of the direction as the direction may provide (subject to subsection (4)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]

(3) Where this section applies because of the failure of a motion for a resolution under section 30(1A), the Secretary of State may, before the period of exclusion to which the motion related comes to an end, by direction extend it until the end of such period of not less than three months, and not more than twelve months, beginning with the date of the direction as the direction may provide (subject to subsection (4)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]

(4) The Secretary of State may exercise the power under subsection (2) or (3) only if he is satisfied that the Minister or junior Minister concerned— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]

- (a) is not committed to non-violence and exclusively peaceful and democratic means; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]
- (b) has failed to observe any other terms of the pledge of office. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]

(5) Where this section applies because of the failure of a motion for a resolution under section 30(2), the Secretary of State may by direction exclude members of the political party concerned from holding office as Ministers or junior Ministers for such period of not less than six months, and not more than twelve months, beginning with the date of the direction as the direction may provide (subject to subsection (7)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]

(6) Where this section applies because of the failure of a motion for a resolution under section 30(3), the Secretary of State may, before the period of exclusion to which the motion related comes to an end, by direction extend it until the end of such period of not less than six months, and not more than twelve months, beginning with the date of the direction as the direction may provide (subject to subsection (7)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]

(7) The Secretary of State may exercise the power under subsection (5) or (6) only if he is satisfied that the political party concerned— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]

- (a) is not committed to non-violence and exclusively peaceful and democratic means; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]
- (b) is not committed to such of its members as are or might become Ministers or junior Ministers observing the other terms of the pledge of office. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]

(8) A period of exclusion under subsection (2) or (5) shall come to an end if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]

- (a) the Secretary of State by direction so provides; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]
- (b) the Assembly is dissolved. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]

(9) In subsection (1)(a) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]**the Monitoring Commission *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]**” means the Commission mentioned in section 1 of the Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f] etc. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]) Act 2003. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]

(10) In this section a reference to a period of exclusion under any provision is, in the case of a period of exclusion under that provision which has been extended, a reference to that period as extended. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cbbbaafb883c66949661268aa01d276f]
