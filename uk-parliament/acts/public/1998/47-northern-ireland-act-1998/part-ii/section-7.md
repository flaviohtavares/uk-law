# Northern Ireland Act 1998, section 7

> Text as at 2026-01-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Entrenched enactments.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to subsections (2A) and (2B)[^key-db1799402b71462e6c8ba8ca626e04f9], the following enactments shall not be modified by an Act of the Assembly or subordinate legislation made, confirmed or approved by a Minister or Northern Ireland department—

- (a)[^key-b66e2d727731847d447e6f20c62ead16] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the [^c12396411]Human Rights Act 1998; [^key-97639dbcd6a6d5d8d3a3f3c26913a875]...
- (c) section 43(1) to (6) and (8), section 67, sections 84 to 86B[^c20129681], section 95(3) and (4) and section 98 [^key-2cbf70c6f0edcbf71d72f9799459e45b]....[^c12396431]
- (d)[^c12396431] section 1 and section 84 of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002.; [^key-dff75cfdbb7aa263a88d55544f746937]...
- (e)[^key-b595751fe575bced0589fa91bd38196c] the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018; and[^key-9a59846644d88190a4b415181e1d62c0]
- (f) the United Kingdom Internal Market Act 2020.

(2)[^key-d57e935169d06a8664547b33471a457c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A)[^key-6a28dd069c5fd72300eca056df63ad9e] Subsection (1) does not prevent an Act of the Assembly or subordinate legislation modifying—

- (ba)[^key-e8926270318a5d1fbe3bcae498cf1c45] any excluded provision of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018, or
- (c) any regulations made under that Act.

(2B)[^key-329edaf7f2454bbb14576d230faa50f8] For the purposes of subsection (2A)(ba), the following are excluded provisions of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018—

- (a) section 1B(3) and (4),
- (b) sections 8A to 8C,
- (c) section 10(3) and (4),
- (d) sections 13A and 13B,
- (e) sections 15A to 15C,
- (f) Parts 1A to 1C of Schedule 2,
- (g) paragraphs 1(11) and (12), 2(12) and (13) and 8A to 8G of Schedule 7, and
- (h) paragraph 21 of Schedule 8.

(3) In this Act “**Minister**”, unless the context otherwise requires, means the First Minister, the deputy First Minister or a Northern Ireland Minister.
