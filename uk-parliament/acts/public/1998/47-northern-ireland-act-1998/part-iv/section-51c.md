# Northern Ireland Act 1998, section 51C

> Text as at 2026-01-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51C. Section 51A : specified periods and extensions[^c20116601][^key-397e19f943c36968e351cb63f979196c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A period specified under section 51A(1)[^key-5cd395d567f2ff9443ddab541560db0f]...—

- (a) shall begin no earlier than the end of the day when the resolution or direction specifying it is passed or given;
- (b) shall begin no later than the end of the financial year in which that day falls; and
- (c) shall not be longer than 12 months.

(2) The power under section 51A(2) [^key-c5ae586b85de6d0b0ba02681b76f47b7]... to extend a period is a power to extend it until the end of such period of not more than 12 months beginning with the date of the resolution, [^key-c5ae586b85de6d0b0ba02681b76f47b7]..., by which the power is exercised as the resolution [^key-c5ae586b85de6d0b0ba02681b76f47b7]... may provide.
