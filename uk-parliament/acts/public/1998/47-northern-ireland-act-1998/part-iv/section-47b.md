# Northern Ireland Act 1998, section 47B

> Text as at 2026-01-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47B. Secretary of State’s powers in relation to reduction of remuneration[^c20119921][^c20116511]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57] This section applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

- (a) the Monitoring Commission has, or members of that Commission have under the agreement establishing it, made a report containing a recommendation about steps the Assembly might consider taking; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]
- (b) the taking of those steps by the Assembly requires the passing by it of a resolution under section 47A(1), (2), (3) or (4) in relation to a Minister, junior Minister or political party; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]
- (c) the first motion for a resolution under that provision in relation to the Minister, junior Minister or political party concerned that is put to the vote after the making of the report does not attract cross-community support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

(2) Where this section applies because of the failure of a motion for a resolution under section 47A(1), the Secretary of State may, in relation to the salary payable under section 47 to the Minister or junior Minister concerned, by direction provide that the whole or a specified part of the salary payable for a specified period shall not be payable (subject to subsection (4)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

(3) Where this section applies because of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

- (a) the failure of a motion for a resolution under section 47A(3) to extend a period by reference to which a reduction under section 47A(1) falls to be made, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]
- (b) the failure of a motion for a resolution under section 47A(4) to extend a period by reference to which provision for non-payability under subsection (2) applies, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

the Secretary of State may, before the end of that period, by direction extend that period (subject to subsection (4)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

(4) The Secretary of State may exercise the power under subsection (2) or (3) only if he is satisfied that the Minister or junior Minister concerned— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

- (a) is not committed to non-violence and exclusively peaceful and democratic means; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]
- (b) has failed to observe any other terms of the pledge of office. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

(5) Where this section applies because of the failure of a motion for a resolution under section 47A(2), the Secretary of State may, in relation to the salaries payable under section 47 to members of the Assembly who are members of the political party concerned, by direction provide that the whole or a specified part of the salaries payable for a specified period shall not be payable (subject to subsection (7)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

(6) Where this section applies because of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

- (a) the failure of a motion for a resolution under section 47A(3) to extend a period by reference to which a reduction under section 47A(2) falls to be made, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]
- (b) the failure of a motion for a resolution under section 47A(4) to extend a period by reference to which provision for non-payability under subsection (5) applies, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

the Secretary of State may, before the end of that period, by direction extend that period (subject to subsection (7)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

(7) The Secretary of State may exercise the power under subsection (5) or (6) only if he is satisfied that the political party concerned— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

- (a) is not committed to non-violence and exclusively peaceful and democratic means; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]
- (b) is not committed to such of its members as are or might become Ministers or junior Ministers observing the other terms of the pledge of office. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

(8) The period by reference to which provision for non-payability under subsection (2) or (5) applies shall come to an end if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

- (a) the Secretary of State by direction so provides; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]
- (b) the Assembly is dissolved. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

(9) In subsection (1)(a) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]**the Monitoring Commission *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]**” means the Commission mentioned in section 1 of the Northern Ireland (Monitoring Commission *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57] etc. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]) Act 2003. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

(10) In this section a reference to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

- (a) the period by reference to which provision for non-payability under subsection (2) or (5) applies, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]
- (b) the period by reference to which a reduction under section 47A(1) or (2) falls to be made, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]

is, where the period has been extended, a reference to the period as extended. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6fbd9fd87da1143d153e4c604ad18d57]
