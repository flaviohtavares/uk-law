# Petroleum Act 1998, section 52

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Commencement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 5(1) to (4) and (11) and this section shall come into force on the passing of this Act.

(2) The provisions mentioned in subsection (3) shall come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order appoint, and different days may be appointed for different provisions or different purposes.

(3) The provisions referred to in subsection (2) are—

- in Schedule 4, paragraphs 8, 10, 11, 13, 34 and 40; and
- in Schedule 5, the repeals of—

- the [^c12434251]Employment (Continental Shelf) Act 1978;
- section 287(5) of the [^c12434261]Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992; and
- section 201(5) of the [^c12434271]Employment Rights Act 1996.

(4)[^c12434281] Subject to subsections (1) and (2), this Act shall come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order appoint.

(5) Orders under this section shall be made by statutory instrument.
