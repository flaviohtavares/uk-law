# Petroleum Act 1998, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Northern Ireland and Isle of Man shares of petroleum revenue.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where petroleum is delivered to the OGA[^c-1e1da922d6cfcaf1e04de9daf54e09bb] under the terms of a licence granted under section 3 by virtue of subsection (2)(b) of that section, then, for the purposes of section 2 of the [^c12434241]Miscellaneous Financial Provisions Act 1968 (Northern Ireland and Isle of Man shares of revenue from the continental shelf), the proceeds from the licence shall be taken to include the proceeds of the sale of the petroleum less—

- (a) any sums paid by the OGA in respect of the petroleum or the delivery or treatment of the petroleum; and
- (b) any expenses incurred by the OGA in connection with the sale.

(2) In this section “**petroleum**” has the same meaning as in Part I of this Act.
