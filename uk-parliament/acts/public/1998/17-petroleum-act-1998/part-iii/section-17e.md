## 17E. Section 17D: supplemental.[^c12433781]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0] For the purpose of considering an application under section 17D(8), the Director may by notice require the owner to provide him with accounting information and details of the main commercial terms of any significant transactions with associated undertakings. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0] Owners of relevant facilities shall keep their internal accounts in such manner as will enable them to provide accounting information if required to do so by notice under subsection (1). *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0]

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0] The Director shall not disclose to any person information obtained under subsection (1) without the consent of the person by or on behalf of whom it was provided, unless he is required to do so by virtue of any obligation imposed on him by or under any enactment. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0]

(4 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0] Any reference in this section to a right to have gas or gas of any kind stored in a relevant facility includes a reference to a right to introduce into, or take out of, such a facility gas or gas of that kind. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0]

(5 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0] In section 17D “main commercial conditions” means— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0] such information as would enable a potential applicant for a right to have gas stored in a relevant facility to make a reasonable assessment of the cost of, or the method of calculating the cost of, acquiring that right; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0] the other significant terms on which such a right would be granted; and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0]
- (c (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0] such other information as the Director may from time to time specify by notice. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0]

(6 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0] In this section— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0]

- “accounting information” means such accounting records as would be required by *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0] section 386 of the Companies Act 2006[^c-ffbd5aaedaf6cabf4b85428cf324b5db] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0] in respect of the storage activities undertaken by an owner of a relevant facility, if those activities were the only business undertaken by the owner and the owner were a person to whom that section applied; and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0]
- “significant transaction” means— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0]
  - (a) any transaction which relates to rights to have gas stored in a relevant facility; or *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0]
  - (b) any other transaction which is of a description specified by the Director from time to time by notice. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0]

(7 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0] For the purposes of this section an undertaking is an associated undertaking of another undertaking if one of the undertakings has control of the other, or both undertakings are under the control of the same person or persons; and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0] sections 450 and 451(1) to (3) of the Corporation Tax Act 2010[^c-bd273291f362101b810c446ee7786c20] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0] shall apply with any necessary modifications for the purposes of this subsection as they apply for the purposes of *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0] Part 10[^c-acfe3521832e0da21c707fb1f9e87d6b] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0] of that Act. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aad3110679237e1e861df75ea46990f0]
