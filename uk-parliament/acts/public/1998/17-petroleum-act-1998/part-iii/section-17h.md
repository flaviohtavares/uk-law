## 17H. Enforcement of certain duties in sections 17B, 17D and 17E. (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note)Enforcement of duty in section 17GA (Extent: E+W+S)[^c12433811][^key-8bb272a0accf78ef4ee07870a271a2cd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The obligation to comply[^key-143aa5e896d879d7fd03d413bde175a5] with any notice under section 17D(11) *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-da79d9815a17779b2cce6265f973eb9c] or[^key-6cdd41255e4b9509bcd783bcdb4e00b8] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-da79d9815a17779b2cce6265f973eb9c] with a notice given under subsection (6) of section 17GA in a case falling within subsection (7) of that section and the obligation to comply with any duty in section 17B(6) or section 17D(7) *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a1728b1e22be48f64f7ee12c3aa7036] shall be duties *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-64681d1d42a4e20b71467bd5e5e52040] a duty *(Extent: E+W+S)* owed to any person who may be affected by a failure to comply with them *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-22e0b764be609d7efd57f8379d0c8447] it *(Extent: E+W+S)* .

(2) Where a duty is owed by virtue of subsection (1) to any person, any breach of the duty which causes that person to sustain loss or damage shall be actionable at the suit or instance of that person.

(3) In any proceedings brought against a person in pursuance of subsection (2), it shall be a defence for him to prove that he took all reasonable steps and exercised all due diligence to avoid contravening the duty.

(4) Compliance with the duties in sections 17B(1) and (3), 17D(1) and (3) and 17E(2) shall be enforceable by civil proceedings by the Secretary of State for an injunction or interdict or other appropriate relief or remedy. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-86cb97a68a0d655d5e3dddae77aba03d]
