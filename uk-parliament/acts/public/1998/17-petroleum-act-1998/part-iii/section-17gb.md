# Petroleum Act 1998, section 17GB

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17GB. Section 17GA: supplemental[^c-b257bf59875306bda5e3484c4527351c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purpose of considering an application under section 17GA(2), the OGA[^c-57b002e82b69de635f1e78536416a072] may by notice require the owner or the applicant to provide it[^c-e4584ec70e36a3c5f1930ea2d4d2f240] with such information relevant to the application as may be specified or described in the notice.

(2) The OGA[^c-34da93696748a51cb6db09f0daeef97e] shall not disclose to any person any information obtained under subsection (1) without the consent of the person by or on behalf of whom it was provided, unless the disclosure is required[^c-fcd15345d8d0ad38c113e8c059d281f0] by virtue of any obligation imposed [^c-b5d52217e64281b5af169394be559e26]... by or under the Framework Agreement or by or under any enactment.
