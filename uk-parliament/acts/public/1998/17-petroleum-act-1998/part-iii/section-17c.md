(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] An exemption shall be given in writing and may be given— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] so as to have effect for an indefinite period or for a period specified in, or determined under, the exemption; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] unconditionally or subject to such conditions as the Director considers appropriate. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]
- (c (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b6b2e84007acbe05b8a03b5d92c88596][^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] so as to have effect— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]
  - (i (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] in the case of a facility other than a new facility, in relation to the whole of the capacity of the facility; or *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] in the case of a new facility, in relation to the whole of the capacity of the facility or any significant increase in the capacity of the facility. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]

(4 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-09deafee2f9325e7b46fefc364ae8e58][^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]  An exemption may contain provision for its revocation. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]

(5 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-64665d5585977715a39578cf070477c3][^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] The Authority shall give an exemption with respect to a facility (other than a new facility) where it is satisfied that use of the facility by other persons is not necessary for the operation of an economically efficient gas market. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]

(5A (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] The Authority shall give an exemption with respect to a new facility where it is satisfied that either— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] use of the facility by other persons is not necessary for the operation of an economically efficient gas market; or *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] the requirements of subsection (5C) are met. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]

(5B (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] In respect of a facility which is or is to be modified to provide for a significant increase in its capacity, an exemption by virtue of subsection (5A)(b) may only be given in relation to that increase in its capacity. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]

(5C (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] The requirements of this subsection are that— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] the facility or (as the case may be) the significant increase in its capacity will promote security of supply; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] the level of risk is such that the investment to construct the facility or (as the case may be) to modify the facility to provide for a significant increase in its capacity would not be or would not have been made without the exemption; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]
- (c (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] the facility is or is to be owned by a person other than the gas transporter who operates or will operate the pipeline system connected or to be connected to the facility; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]
- (d (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] charges will be levied on users of the facility or (as the case may be) the increase in its capacity; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]
- (e (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] the exemption will not be detrimental to competition, the operation of an economically efficient gas market or the efficient functioning of the pipeline system connected or to be connected to the facility; and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]
- (f (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] the Commission of the European Communities is or will be content with the exemption. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]

(5D (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] Subject to subsection (5E), an exemption may not be given by virtue of subsection (5A)(b) more than once in respect of the same facility. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]

(5E (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] Subsection (5D) does not prevent a further exemption being given by virtue of subsection (5A)(b) in respect of a facility if— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]

- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] the exemption has effect only in relation to that increase in its capacity; and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]
- (c (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] no previous exemption has been given by virtue of subsection (5A)(b) in relation to that increase in its capacity. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]

(5F (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba] The Authority shall publish its decision to give or refuse to give an exemption together with the reasons for its decision in such manner as it considers appropriate. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-732e052f70e4dfe68881b180b8f864ba]
