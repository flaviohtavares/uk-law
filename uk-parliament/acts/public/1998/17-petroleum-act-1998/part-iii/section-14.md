## 14. Construction and use of pipelines.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-86974520aa26d2f3235ac6f7af3b186d] No person shall—

- (a) execute in, under or over any controlled waters any works for the construction of a pipeline; or
- (b) use a controlled pipeline of which the construction was begun on or after 1st January 1976,

except in accordance with an authorisation given in writing by the OGA[^key-89cc2f589712f8a813ef1ac57f3bba1e].

(2) In this Part of this Act—

- “**controlled pipeline**” means so much of any pipeline as is in, under or over controlled waters; and
- “**controlled waters**” means the territorial sea adjacent to the United Kingdom and the sea in any area designated under section 1(7) of the [^c12433701]Continental Shelf Act 1964.
