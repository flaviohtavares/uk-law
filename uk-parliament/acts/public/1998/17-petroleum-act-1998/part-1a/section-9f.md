## 9F. Producing and revising a strategy[^key-e273b266a99dba86f3a0e52d2b715e53][^key-3cf64e08e6cd4abf95571c8bce33f674]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-37fdbd0a79ff026ff3e048d4712d3064] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-358cd214474e612e4aa78e5e1651f756] After the first strategy has been produced, the OGA may—

- (a) produce a new strategy, or
- (b) revise a current strategy,

whenever the OGA thinks appropriate.

(3) The OGA[^key-f66f52c4a8d316ace9f2e5cc0838a360] must review each current strategy before the end of each relevant four year period.

(4)[^key-54e5fce3eaebfccbce3b50f1189d5aac] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) In this section “**relevant four year period**”, in relation to a current strategy, means a period of four years beginning with—

- (a) the date on which the strategy was issued, or
- (b) if later, the date on which the last review under subsection (3) was concluded.
