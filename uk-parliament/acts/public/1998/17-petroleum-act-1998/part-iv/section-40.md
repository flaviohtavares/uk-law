# Petroleum Act 1998, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Offences: penalties.[^key-28bc5944108b762883855a3bd6b5024f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person guilty of an offence under section 28A,[^key-b04356dcd3f971e921ff3d19c0c85191] 30, 33, 36A,[^key-78c7232071be526a5d700f2677eb7168] 37 or 38 shall be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or to a fine, or to both.
