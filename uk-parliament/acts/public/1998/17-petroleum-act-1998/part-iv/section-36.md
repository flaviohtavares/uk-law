# Petroleum Act 1998, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Duty to carry out programmes.[^key-28bc5944108b762883855a3bd6b5024f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where an abandonment programme is approved by the Secretary of State, it shall be the duty of each of the persons who submitted it to secure that it is carried out and that any conditions to which the approval is subject are complied with.
