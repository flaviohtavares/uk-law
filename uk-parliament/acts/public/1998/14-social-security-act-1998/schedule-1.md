# Social Security Act 1998, SCHEDULE 1

> This provision is no longer in force.

## SCHEDULE 1 (affected by repeal, see note) — Appeal tribunals: supplementary provisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d][^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

**No longer in force.**

Sections 5(3) and 7(7). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

### Tenure of office (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

**1.**[^c-c1a58a839be667440e3c10c621de60d4] (1) Subject to the following provisions of this paragraph, the President of appeal tribunals shall hold and vacate office in accordance with the terms of his appointment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

(2) The President shall vacate his office on the day on which he attains the age of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d] 75[^c-7195a58ed4426de2ca062f9970705826] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d].

(3) The President may be removed from office by the Lord Chancellor *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d], with the concurrence of the Lord Chief Justice and the Lord President of the Court of Session,[^c-5ccdbdb52c6cfa9febace05f7436b0cf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d] on the ground of incapacity or misbehaviour. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

(4)[^c-4f55bd24877b143fdc1dc37b4211e46c][^c-34dadc1ac497a4c9474ab2c11c8badf8][^c-baa981b73cc6f2d6b9350754d43d3eb2] Where the Lord Chancellor proposes to exercise a power conferred on him by sub-paragraph (3) above, it shall be his duty to consult the Lord Advocate with respect to the proposal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

### Remuneration etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

**2.**[^c-0913825b0727d894f4ab45dcfd0eaf4d] The Secretary of State may pay, or make such payments towards the provision of, such remuneration, pensions or allowances to or in respect of the President as he may determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

**3.**[^c-e62b0fd5af9169ca387e7c42f0512935] The Secretary of State may pay, or make such payments towards the provision of, such remuneration, pensions or allowances to or in respect of any person appointed under this Chapter to act as a member of an appeal tribunal, or as an expert to such a tribunal, as he may determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

**4.**[^c-31f7e702b35d82fbe236eefe05ed802b] (1) The Secretary of State may pay— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

- (a) to any person required to attend at any proceedings under section 12 of this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d], section 20 of the Child Support Act or paragraph 6 of Schedule 7 to the Child Support, Pensions and Social Security Act 2000[^c-439f65f63f36f948b88730bfd15ea8e8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]
- (b) to any person required under this Part (whether for the purposes of this Part or otherwise) to attend for or to submit himself to medical or other examination or treatment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

such travelling and other allowances as he may determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

(2) In this paragraph references to travelling and other allowances include references to compensation for loss of remunerative time but such compensation shall not be paid to any person in respect of any time during which he is in receipt of remuneration under paragraph 3 above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

**5.**[^c-d1d83cf9a6c405adeab3e16769d36869] (1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2) below, the Secretary of State may pay such other expenses in connection with the work of any person or tribunal appointed or constituted under any provision of this Part as he may determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

(2) Expenses are not payable under sub-paragraph (1) above in connection with the work of a tribunal presided over by a Social Security Commissioner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

### Officers and staff (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

**6.**[^c-55dab31cca3d518723314d8819dcb3a1][^c-9cc26c8e2491abfc847cda99c42f3254] The Secretary of State may appoint such officers and staff as he thinks fit for the President and for appeal tribunals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

### Functions of President (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

**7.**[^c-aa82fd8b0c036e08a70106a7c94545cc][^c-f110a3b9d8b1babb530b50d31754431b][^c-ae3499679989af65fa6d1fff583eb18d] The President shall ensure that appropriate steps are taken by an appeal tribunal to secure the confidentiality, in such circumstances as may be prescribed, of any prescribed material or any prescribed classes or categories of material. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

**8.**[^c-1bc1cf8ddb5f12c2e0088a3e19eae324] (1) The President shall, after the requisite consultation, arrange such training for persons appointed to the panel constituted under section 6 above as he considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

(2) In sub-paragraph (1) above “the requisite consultation” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

- (a) except in the case of medical practitioners, consultation with the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]
- (b) in the case of such practitioners, consultation with the Chief Medical Officers of the Department of Health, the Welsh Office and the Scottish Office. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

**9.**[^c-fa28d365523b502a17bea8b329ea380d] The President shall supply the Secretary of State with such reports and other information with respect to the carrying out of the functions of appeal tribunals as the Secretary of State may require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

**10.**[^c-3b8c71eee0adfa94de3a75c9f99249f7] Each year the President shall make to the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d] and the Child Maintenance and Enforcement Commission[^c-818dfc22965aee1b43269fa59a1322b6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d] a written report, based on the cases coming before appeal tribunals, on the standards achieved by the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d] and the Child Maintenance and Enforcement Commission *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d] in the making of decisions against which an appeal lies to an appeal tribunal; and the Secretary of State shall publish the report. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

### Clerks to appeal tribunals (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

**11.**[^c-38660fdc8bdd6f4678a266c22552772a][^c-71a06e72796678ebbb2368139030b7a2][^c-bf69063e268f002a0eb17a74eaf041bf] The Secretary of State may by regulations provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

- (a) for clerks to be assigned to service appeal tribunals; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]
- (b) for clerks so assigned to be responsible for summoning members of the panel constituted under section 6 above to serve on such tribunals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

### Delegation of certain functions of appeal tribunals (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

**12.**[^c-d4b8f67bd3f6461ffbda6f8e7acb56f0][^c-47aff269da8483d95d896c9b3b0de0a5][^c-06d8ed26c36bcb667d23c3f71f1fa01b] (1) The Secretary of State may by regulations provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

- (a) for officers authorised by the Secretary of State to make any determinations which fall to be made by an appeal tribunal and which do not involve the determination of any appeal, application for leave to appeal or reference; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]
- (b) for the procedure to be followed by such officers in making such determinations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]
- (c) for the manner in which such determinations by such officers may be called in question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

(2) A determination which would have the effect of preventing an appeal, application for leave to appeal or reference being determined by an appeal tribunal is not a determination of the appeal, application or reference for the purposes of sub-paragraph (1) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

### Certificates (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

**13.**[^c-40f89fc6fc70e307a754108cad10967e] A document bearing a certificate which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

- (a) is signed by a person authorised in that behalf by the Secretary of State; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]
- (b) states that the document, apart from the certificate, is a record of a decision of an appeal tribunal or of an officer of the Secretary of State, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]

shall be conclusive evidence of the decision; and a certificate purporting to be so signed shall be deemed to be so signed unless the contrary is proved. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-63664a6a8458de240cf49b4adea02d9d]
