# Social Security Act 1998, section 80

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2024-02-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/14

## 80. Parliamentary control of regulations[^key-18ed02b642c9987f688c01a20b257465]

(1) Subject to the provisions of this section, a statutory instrument containing (whether alone or with other provisions) regulations under—

- (a) section 7,[^key-f58acee4f856639c3e56abd9b3a5613c] 12(2) or (3A)[^key-5c75d34899e8ad59623a583f28d014cc] or 72 above; or
- (b) paragraph 12 of Schedule 1, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-62d599c24818af56103852bd3158a0c7] paragraph 9 of Schedule 2 or paragraph 2 of Schedule 5 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b2fa9ecca9b1d1ac5b7f4061ce2beb3b] to this Act,

shall not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before Parliament and been approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

(2)[^key-ff4783085b459c3b2669178592b46cfd] A statutory instrument—

- (a) which contains (whether alone or with other provisions) regulations made under this Act by the Secretary of State , the Treasury or the Commissioners of Inland Revenue[^key-23572a637598235ab0a7bcce6f89e3c6]; and
- (b) which is not subject to any requirement that a draft of the instrument be laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament,

shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(3)[^key-980cdac8ad9a2583457000a0fc0c43f4] A statutory instrument— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980cdac8ad9a2583457000a0fc0c43f4]

- (a) which contains (whether alone or with other provisions) regulations made under this Act by the Lord Chancellor; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980cdac8ad9a2583457000a0fc0c43f4]
- (b) which is not subject to any requirement that a draft of the instrument be laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980cdac8ad9a2583457000a0fc0c43f4]

shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980cdac8ad9a2583457000a0fc0c43f4]

(4)[^key-8c3f2970a50532ffd34d721ec6b3218c] No order shall be made by the Lord Chancellor under section 7(6A) above unless a draft of the statutory instrument containing the order has been laid before Parliament and been approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980cdac8ad9a2583457000a0fc0c43f4]
