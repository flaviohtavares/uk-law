# Social Security Act 1998, section 84

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2024-02-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/14

## 84. Interpretation: general[^c-53b9698f967f0742068e1e6aba603c0f]

In this Act—

- “the Administration Act” means the [^m00039]Social Security Administration Act 1992;
- “the Child Support Act” means the [^m00040]Child Support Act 1991;
- “the Contributions and Benefits Act” means the [^m00041]Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992;
- “the Jobseekers Act” means the [^m00042]Jobseekers Act 1995;
- “the Vaccine Damage Payments Act” means the [^m00043]Vaccine Damage Payments Act 1979;
- “prescribe” means prescribe by regulations.
