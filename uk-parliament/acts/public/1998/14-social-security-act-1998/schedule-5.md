# Social Security Act 1998, SCHEDULE 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2024-02-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/14

## SCHEDULE 5 — Regulations as to procedure: provision which may be made[^key-6ae0e7a2297f5dce0c909376316cac3b][^key-335f5821a801d9e3ad09f3c478202dbf][^key-ff0935c9479a764f1f3ce6075dffbaf6]

Section 16(1).

**1.**[^key-8358d7d567032af814a9cde5a79f7a42][^key-d35de998bf4426c24db251eb359dbab2][^key-b87d8695f62b7a5808ffafdf58362f08][^key-9193644bebc099c95f3b80779ed66c53][^key-f38728c4920e9a3cd68923de647ad499][^key-09b4eb4b654a3b970d9ea3bb607e9ced] Provision prescribing the procedure to be followed in connection with—

- (a) the making of decisions or determinations by the Secretary of State , an appeal tribunal or a Commissioner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6264ea643ef673cb04f38b08b4f6a478]; and
- (b) the withdrawal of claims, applications, appeals or references falling to be decided or determined by the Secretary of State , an appeal tribunal or a Commissioner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b0060e738d89dd10203961a888fe2961].

**2.**[^key-0e721cccd6efb75f25c9240ff9eabd2f] Provision as to the striking out or reinstatement of proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0e721cccd6efb75f25c9240ff9eabd2f]

**3.**[^key-b28e59d63d00357ccf133e55be034474][^key-480ae0e53306604d042fa5196192f604][^key-4c37aa7a290d76e0f25cc479ed6b3611][^key-3c76270f357e502d44230d1f949fb96c][^key-3ae11c01e8b9bdc9d135a02d90dd2485][^key-f19f0069f4951dc13a2f66acbe119cd1] Provision as to the form which is to be used for any document, the evidence which is to be required and the circumstances in which any official record or certificate is to be sufficient or conclusive evidence.

**4.**[^key-6a831d9356fc5b22fa25b2be6535e782][^key-11234a82bff73f619a990d8cb70d23a9][^key-4537d1e2511baa85bee8d76bf2078e8d][^key-3db22c43b27eda864ca341ec116e42d3][^key-dc362e56a57d33d3643e63a82045af91][^key-418a17d5d711a231aa4e99fe4af7b1af] Provision as to the time within which, or the manner in which—

- (a) any evidence is to be produced; or
- (b) any application, reference or appeal is to be made.

**5.**[^key-bd85e9ee451aadd26341188f25786cf7] Provision for summoning persons to attend and give evidence or produce documents and for authorising the administration of oaths to witnesses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bd85e9ee451aadd26341188f25786cf7]

**6.** Provision with respect to the procedure to be followed on appeals to and in other proceedings before appeal tribunals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bd85e9ee451aadd26341188f25786cf7]

**7.** Provision for authorising an appeal tribunal consisting of two or more members to proceed with any case, with the consent of the claimant, in the absence of any member. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bd85e9ee451aadd26341188f25786cf7]

**8.** Provision for empowering an appeal tribunal to give directions for the disposal of any purported appeal which the tribunal is satisfied that it does not have jurisdiction to entertain. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bd85e9ee451aadd26341188f25786cf7]

**9.**[^key-2dfa2ed34a3680959f5d7c19b5aa1d8d][^key-d9a2c05f877773112a266e3b9a3f8af4][^key-6539e33d4f7e78cff3d29585c18af4e4][^key-786c116c3ba01ccc0dd87195e44d44d5][^key-c6f4c1d685f4d18f3fbf7a3583a865e5][^key-eb10693aeb0f765bbeb4c8db2e85928c] Provision for the non-disclosure to a person of the particulars of any medical advice or medical evidence given or submitted for the purposes of a determination.
