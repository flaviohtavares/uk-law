# Social Security Act 1998, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2024-02-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/14

## 7. Constitution of appeal tribunals[^key-d001c7e4e7dc6b09c9008763047cac72][^key-7c636c1e59343ea5b620d53e20dfd35b][^key-b3ca6d61478442178cc80f442bc83e88][^key-3c74079499c9d5c87338a343c51ab8ec][^key-66c05d8d4a66aeafdb6c68eb239c1017][^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]

(1) Subject to subsection (2) below, an appeal tribunal shall consist of one, two or three members drawn by the President from the panel constituted under section 6 above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]

(2) The member, or (as the case may be) at least one member, of an appeal tribunal must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]

- (a)[^key-e029c655a18754f17dd2acb677fd8d11] be a solicitor of the Senior Courts of England and Wales, a barrister in England and Wales or have a qualification that is specified under subsection (6A); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]
- (b) be an advocate or solicitor in Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]

(3) Where an appeal tribunal has more than one member— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]

- (a) the President shall nominate one of the members as chairman; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]
- (b) decisions shall be taken by a majority of votes; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]
- (c) unless regulations otherwise provide, the chairman shall have any casting vote. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]

(4) Where it appears to an appeal tribunal that a matter before it involves a question of fact of special difficulty, then, unless regulations otherwise provide, the tribunal may require one or more experts to provide assistance to it in dealing with the question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]

(5) In subsection (4) above “expert” means a member of the panel constituted under section 6 above who appears to the appeal tribunal concerned to have knowledge or experience which would be relevant in determining the question of fact of special difficulty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]

(6) Regulations shall make provision with respect to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]

- (a) the composition of appeal tribunals; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]
- (b) the procedure to be followed in allocating cases among differently constituted tribunals; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]
- (c) the manner in which expert assistance is to be given under subsection (4) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]

(6A)[^key-2274f60d8fda0548f52a151bed6dca44] The Lord Chancellor may by order specify a qualification for the purposes of subsection (2)(a) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]

(6B) Subsections (2) to (4), (9) and (10) of section 51 of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 (contents of orders under subsection (1) of that section, and cessation of effect of such orders) shall apply for the purposes of subsection (6A) above as they apply for the purposes of subsection (1) of that section, but as if the reference in subsection (3) of that section to section 50 of that Act were a reference to this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]

(6C) For the purposes of subsection (2)(a) above, a person shall be taken first to become a barrister— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]

- (a) when the person completes pupillage in connection with becoming a barrister, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]
- (b) in the case of a person not required to undertake pupillage in connection with becoming a barrister, when the person is called to the Bar of England and Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]

(6D) For the purposes of subsection (2)(a) above, a person shall be taken not to be a solicitor or a barrister, or not to have a qualification specified under subsection (6A) above, if as a result of disciplinary proceedings he is prevented from practising as a solicitor or (as the case may be) as a barrister or as a holder of the specified qualification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]

(7)[^key-b2d64e2951c847f0ba9ca77eb569a247] Schedule 1 to this Act shall have effect for supplementing this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2be6195dead071e058871e3e9838b3e2]
