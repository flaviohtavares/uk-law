## 28. Correction of errors and setting aside of decisions[^c-b01569fb5986bd6c69b825e7a833e9c1][^c-de2d6a228b3571545775c6befdf7c6f3][^c-81d1213f0649848797688c58571d7b72][^c-9d045246bc494eee71ce22c3d2dc2209][^c-a2c344464fc7cf1cbf4c0e6c790e6e85][^c-362a942f605eedeba04bd8719e5cd62e]

(1)[^c-32e170a515511aa1d83da74b780a66c8][^c-31a8ccc091681ae3934e2e9e4cb79da6][^c-ccfcbfa651889b2cae14d3bde338f8d6][^c-c0083374045e39c19cd283b4d719df56] Regulations may make provision with respect to—

- (a) the correction of accidental errors in any decision of the Secretary of State[^c-2a897c3d014185b4895d1f76f2d0d7af] or record of a decision of the Secretary of State made under any relevant enactment; [^c-166fc13ddc6420b6200460dd71f1c5f1]...
- (b)[^c-166fc13ddc6420b6200460dd71f1c5f1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1A)[^c-fa5e4ebac2d849319d8c87cdcbf02b25] In subsection (1) “**decision**” does not include any decision of the First-tier Tribunal or[^c-cd6ab200fc0cb3e0a5a012a970d62a92] any decision made by an officer of the Inland Revenue, other than a decision under or by virtue of Part III of the Pension Schemes Act 1993.

(2)[^c-32e170a515511aa1d83da74b780a66c8][^c-31a8ccc091681ae3934e2e9e4cb79da6][^c-ccfcbfa651889b2cae14d3bde338f8d6][^c-c0083374045e39c19cd283b4d719df56] Nothing in subsection (1) above shall be construed as derogating from any power to correct errors [^c-fe4d279c78cd019387db42a68839312c]... which is exercisable apart from regulations made by virtue of that subsection.

(3) In this section “relevant enactment” means any enactment contained in—

- (a)[^c-32e170a515511aa1d83da74b780a66c8][^c-31a8ccc091681ae3934e2e9e4cb79da6][^c-ccfcbfa651889b2cae14d3bde338f8d6][^c-c0083374045e39c19cd283b4d719df56] this Chapter;
- (b)[^c-32e170a515511aa1d83da74b780a66c8][^c-31a8ccc091681ae3934e2e9e4cb79da6][^c-ccfcbfa651889b2cae14d3bde338f8d6][^c-c0083374045e39c19cd283b4d719df56] the Contributions and Benefits Act;
- (c)[^c-32e170a515511aa1d83da74b780a66c8] the Pension Schemes Act 1993;
- (d)[^c-c0083374045e39c19cd283b4d719df56] the Jobseekers Act; [^c-8d1b836a6bd240cf3ddf3551df57811c]...
- (e) the Social Security (Recovery of Benefits) Act 1997; [^c-7ba91e787e76e411f8f0623491d5dc95]...
- (f)[^c-b4aeaff4b617e9b13a9556f5825862f6] the State Pension Credit Act 2002; [^c-b54ea5b2db43174415255eb5c16a5f2a]...
- (g)[^c-c75435265dc67c00888c919bd00f8db8] Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2007;
- (h)[^c-46fca2229ea3819f447abf9aa37485d1] Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2012; [^c-ae61b28ecfed007875d3a67c54cf92f6]...
- (i)[^c-5d8c4653bff18375b44d366166c2efea] Part 4 of that Act; [^c-97862229d9713e866cff057ac6862f68]...
- (j)[^c-01568c9f481e4f2eb4b22d6f02e0ff7b] Part 1 of the Pensions Act 2014 or section 30 of that Act[^c-577bceae7b8cf8aa6f9b795331290b4b]; or[^c-e6feac71b87ce342e8f15030fd4ef7f2]
- (k) sections 18 to 21 of the Welfare Reform and Work Act 2016.
