## 34. Determination of claims and reviews[^c-318d7911bf9c70b2780a35fcb171adeb]

(1)[^c-b041dda4d38578f6c28ac45d0d6d8c4d] Regulations shall provide that, where a person claims— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b041dda4d38578f6c28ac45d0d6d8c4d]

- (a) housing benefit; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b041dda4d38578f6c28ac45d0d6d8c4d]
- (b) council tax benefit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b041dda4d38578f6c28ac45d0d6d8c4d]

the authority to whom the claim is made shall notify the person of its determination of the claim. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b041dda4d38578f6c28ac45d0d6d8c4d]

(2) Any such notification shall be given in such form as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b041dda4d38578f6c28ac45d0d6d8c4d]

(3) Regulations may make provision requiring authorities to whom claims for housing benefit or council tax benefit are made by, or in respect of, persons who have been entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance or to income support *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b041dda4d38578f6c28ac45d0d6d8c4d] or state pension credit[^c-a943d51c874764082f9f1f2940cd4c87] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b041dda4d38578f6c28ac45d0d6d8c4d] to give priority, in prescribed circumstances, to those claims over other claims for any such benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b041dda4d38578f6c28ac45d0d6d8c4d]
