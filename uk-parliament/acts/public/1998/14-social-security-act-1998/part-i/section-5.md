## 5. President of appeal tribunals[^c-1d534c8fd40d5bdcb449b3b2bb5aaa9c][^c-1519dcc25d2cf88253609c8ffec0c71e][^c-66c05d8d4a66aeafdb6c68eb239c1017][^c-d38a7cc73d2c48d30508a1feb8323cd0]

(1)[^c-283fa8940315e7e9cfd0570981af1028][^c-7f7fbd1e7a3517fd401fdcf06b1ce6f7][^c-0b1ce75f6b151191c2ab0fd11f151f09] The Lord Chancellor may, after consultation with the Lord Advocate, appoint a President of appeal tribunals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d38a7cc73d2c48d30508a1feb8323cd0]

(2) A person is qualified to be appointed President if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d38a7cc73d2c48d30508a1feb8323cd0]

- (a)[^c-57eb694db8fa9aad02ec5834d8316ab1] he satisfies the judicial-appointment eligibility condition on a 7-year basis; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d38a7cc73d2c48d30508a1feb8323cd0]
- (b) he is an advocate or solicitor in Scotland of at least *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d38a7cc73d2c48d30508a1feb8323cd0] 7[^c-9e116970483d368054e96bb02a7f88e3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d38a7cc73d2c48d30508a1feb8323cd0] years' standing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d38a7cc73d2c48d30508a1feb8323cd0]

(3)[^c-a194895f94175aeb2da82bb59229d94f] Schedule 1 to this Act shall have effect for supplementing this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d38a7cc73d2c48d30508a1feb8323cd0]
