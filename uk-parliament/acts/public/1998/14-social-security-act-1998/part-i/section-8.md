# Social Security Act 1998, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2024-02-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/14

## 8. Decisions by Secretary of State[^c-6ffc5f69a151899c9fa4eb5a40c84de7][^c-4687fc803a1c1b6c340c35ba9d8c4bcd][^c-6ed97458d178c53326546e70e2ff6f16][^c-d914faf44fcc1e22bf10a42282ff1fdf][^c-4f8bc87885b3f0ccf9be2a28373080d9]

(1)[^c-522f97ef908286067b416f73fba7b9e8] Subject to the provisions of this Chapter, it shall be for the Secretary of State—

- (a)[^c-cb94c0856662200ed7db2fbd0c777225][^c-99a8c19b50b15de4012338dcdc2bee3e][^c-0d857846a28b8bfd0523867fa5d2bdbf][^c-204b68a819ce5b86832ee919b90a36bc][^c-8eed48926e44cce407e080fa203b03b3] to decide any claim for a relevant benefit;
- (b)[^c-acbbfada5c9bbf9f11d1c22917233cfc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . and[^c-6f39295e631df2ce5fbce6bee2013ae0]
- (c)[^c-cb94c0856662200ed7db2fbd0c777225][^c-99a8c19b50b15de4012338dcdc2bee3e][^c-0d857846a28b8bfd0523867fa5d2bdbf][^c-204b68a819ce5b86832ee919b90a36bc][^c-8eed48926e44cce407e080fa203b03b3] subject to subsection (5) below, to make any decision that falls to be made under or by virtue of a relevant enactment; [^c-1571c377ea21acadcc5bd063e43f1ea5]...
- (d)[^c-1571c377ea21acadcc5bd063e43f1ea5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-cb94c0856662200ed7db2fbd0c777225][^c-99a8c19b50b15de4012338dcdc2bee3e][^c-0d857846a28b8bfd0523867fa5d2bdbf][^c-8eed48926e44cce407e080fa203b03b3] Where at any time a claim for a relevant benefit is decided by the Secretary of State—

- (a) the claim shall not be regarded as subsisting after that time; and
- (b) accordingly, the claimant shall not (without making a further claim) be entitled to the benefit on the basis of circumstances not obtaining at that time.

(3) In this Chapter “relevant benefit” [^c-b05c27565b7d0ac3e575a8e396b24324]... means any of the following, namely—

- (a)[^c-cb94c0856662200ed7db2fbd0c777225][^c-99a8c19b50b15de4012338dcdc2bee3e][^c-8eed48926e44cce407e080fa203b03b3] benefit under Parts II to V of the Contributions and Benefits Act;
- (aa)[^c-5cd996813aa1574ba112b158956c6796] universal credit;
- (ab)[^c-7c198f14123d0f3980dcffba1d074e71] state pension or a lump sum under Part 1 of the Pensions Act 2014;
- (ac)[^c-f8a15eff3dbbbe97ed3dc850cda484c4] bereavement support payment under section 30 of the Pensions Act 2014;
- (b)[^c-8eed48926e44cce407e080fa203b03b3] a jobseeker’s allowance;
- (ba)[^c-873fdd657c5169915db4d458009f1012] an employment and support allowance;
- (baa)[^c-cbf5606d9e3ca4c73c62d78d91d8ead8] personal independence payment;
- (bb)[^c-1c8deed4d16d23598d1b4ed73ca0c68a] state pension credit;
- (bc)[^c-5c2903f0a3064e3a0d1f69a4def4c67d] a loan under section 18 of the Welfare Reform and Work Act 2016;
- (c) income support;
- (d)[^c-38a787abeef781ea04943509ad96433f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^c-38a787abeef781ea04943509ad96433f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f) a social fund payment mentioned in section 138(1)(a) or (2) of the Contributions and Benefits Act;
- (g)[^c-cb94c0856662200ed7db2fbd0c777225] child benefit;
- (h) such other benefit as may be prescribed.

(4)[^c-cb94c0856662200ed7db2fbd0c777225][^c-99a8c19b50b15de4012338dcdc2bee3e][^c-0d857846a28b8bfd0523867fa5d2bdbf][^c-8eed48926e44cce407e080fa203b03b3] In this section “relevant enactment” means any enactment contained in this Chapter, the Contributions and Benefits Act, the Administration Act, the [^m00013]Social Security (Consequential Provisions) Act 1992, the Jobseekers Act[^c-50f3e46e7a237717b921fce11cec1d96], the State Pension Credit Act 2002[^c-015b6f5352aa4c501f19a53d7edd8b87], Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2007, Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2012[^c-6179b345ec83114a7a34d3afd83bb6e8], Part 4 of that Act or Part 1 of the Pensions Act 2014[^c-f73516478deeaf294987b168dac63596], section 30 of that Act or sections 18 to 21 of the Welfare Reform and Work Act 2016[^c-3b42287d318ad2d911ba7246a5b36a8d], other than one contained in—

- (a) Part VII of the Contributions and Benefits Act so far as relating to housing benefit and council tax benefit;
- (b) Part VIII of the Administration Act (arrangements for housing benefit and council tax benefit and related subsidies).

(5)[^c-cb94c0856662200ed7db2fbd0c777225][^c-99a8c19b50b15de4012338dcdc2bee3e][^c-0d857846a28b8bfd0523867fa5d2bdbf][^c-8eed48926e44cce407e080fa203b03b3][^c-7c49bd41384c7b64bf017d8be0d08c0b] Subsection (1)(c) above does not include any decision which under section 8 of the Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc.) Act 1999 falls to be made by an officer of the Inland Revenue.
