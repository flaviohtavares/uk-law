# Social Security Act 1998, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2024-02-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/14

## 3. Use of information[^key-5dc5e37244d45a233373baa38883eeb7][^key-8a24b2b71140ffb866363cbccac259d5][^key-2fbb8f50dc38f24318d5391c1d4df44e]

(1)[^key-cd1a3d12cc711f49196d6e4f189403ac][^key-acada4528f67aa567f2759084706c754] Subsection (2) below applies to information relating to any of the matters specified in subsection (1A) below[^key-6fce4943438c1d9cf2e875c188413617] which is held—

- (a) by the Secretary of State or the Northern Ireland Department; or
- (b) by a person providing services to the Secretary of State or the Northern Ireland Department in connection with the provision of those services.

(1A)[^key-7c38b9460caaa1cd2df0f19b4b34cf6b] The matters are—

- (a) social security [^key-91fce88a56c9a93ce4bfc4503f197001]... or war pensions;
- (aa)[^key-bded276918a9b94e8e552f9405739c4a] child support [^key-cc4d95e1d71d53dcfa7cd2b6d1bd6907]... ;
- (b) employment or training;
- (c) private pensions policy;
- (d) retirement planning;
- (e)[^key-13939eaca6aa07d7a19b7706901c16ec] the investigation or prosecution of offences relating to tax credits;
- (f)[^key-9c9686e6c5b1386de057346464fd3a28] the Diffuse Mesothelioma Payment Scheme.

(2)[^key-98cd0b2e3b2dc0ecfc9eb06c96359951] Information to which this subsection applies—

- (a)[^key-a624617a8541ccfec8e8ba35d239d030] may be used for the purposes of, or for any purposes connected with, the exercise of functions in relation to any of the matters specified in subsection (1A) above[^key-4aa2fbd61c999f7c6a74557e470ab768]; and
- (b) may be supplied to, or to a person providing services to, the Secretary of State or the Northern Ireland Department for use for those purposes.

(3)[^key-96e39d8d85787e9c9b841a6aa88e1387] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) In this section “the Northern Ireland Department” means the Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland or the Department for Employment and Learning in Northern Ireland[^key-0a691c211d7ac3c95a32ce990a842efc].

(5)[^key-ff9c84249c74271190ee338a877430d7] In this section—

- “**private pensions policy**” means policy relating to—
  - (a) occupational pension schemes or personal pension schemes (within the meaning given by section 1 of the Pension Schemes Act 1993);[^key-47dd7b23f37b6b97d58d5538b5e9ec16] or[^key-860d1b2efcf19f08f1245fd41e33f7d0]
  - (b) occupational pension schemes or private pension schemes within the meaning of Part 1 of the Pensions Act 2008, if they do not fall within paragraph (a);
- “**retirement planning**” means promoting financial planning for retirement.
