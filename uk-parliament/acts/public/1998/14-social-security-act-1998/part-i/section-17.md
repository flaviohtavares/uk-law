# Social Security Act 1998, section 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2024-02-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/14

## 17. Finality of decisions[^c-a51292b8abcaf26f2b57be4e6ca5d530][^c-62b6d06cd639d0d484fa90c7bc1c6d11][^c-f1506cd7f7c4b710af0b0bbe6ad095c2][^c-eb8c580a2c9e546fd861bd4c3d6049bf][^c-ae8052c3813df3165b24285bb5750b98][^c-8eb2f8c163c077cdac38fb5de69864e4][^c-6438bac4326af5159b58ae8250efe476][^c-e57610425a566a8b77ce166ac6ac525e][^c-f0813b872aa1ede3895484893df880d9][^c-900536b46e3d5a19deab4503f285bc60][^c-eb4a6a26125114befaa1af04f086e392]

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Chapter and to any provision made by or under Chapter 2 of Part 1 of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007[^c-83f00f2bcf4b947444824aa739f5649a], any decision made in accordance with the foregoing provisions of this Chapter shall be final; and subject to the provisions of any regulations under section 11 above, any decision made in accordance with those regulations shall be final.

(2) If and to the extent that regulations so provide, any finding of fact or other determination embodied in or necessary to such a decision, or on which such a decision is based, shall be conclusive for the purposes of—

- (a) further such decisions;
- (b) decisions made under the Child Support Act; and
- (c) decisions made under the Vaccine Damage Payments Act.
