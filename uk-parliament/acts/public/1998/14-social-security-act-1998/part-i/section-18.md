# Social Security Act 1998, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2024-02-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/14

## 18. Matters arising as respects decisions[^c-62b6d06cd639d0d484fa90c7bc1c6d11][^c-37881e0e463c88f249ead13ffe8bfdb5][^c-a307116758e33b41b9ccee41da620ca9]

(1)[^c-c61dfb6ad2f59a2883b20b57cda414ee][^c-0824297adf60828b8aa534b7d4f5f54f][^c-5d5605aa5cb16cd6b8602bc3ac7b602d][^c-52619f51f98b7ba983278f76914420dd][^c-08af34f2e91406a5068292ea3e19f928] Regulations may make provision as respects matters arising—

- (a) pending any decision under this Chapter of the Secretary of State or the First-tier Tribunal, or any decision of the Upper Tribunal which relates to any decision under this Chapter of the First-Tier Tribunal,[^c-a8e5de12923d6d7b34a48adb437c5072] which relates to—
  - (i) any claim for a relevant benefit; or[^c-b32dfe1abaa9d3ab09b02d36bafde838]
  - (ii) any person’s entitlement to such a benefit or its receipt; or[^c-da76c6682e30cfc493d0f4d2dbea75b5]
  - (iii)[^c-eee605a751e4162a1e2bbdf1c1dcfcc1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iv)[^c-eee605a751e4162a1e2bbdf1c1dcfcc1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) out of the revision under section 9 above or on appeal of any such decision.

(2)[^c-f7ffe1c3d6f6cb701006bee3bf90350c] Regulations under subsection (1) above as it applies to child benefit may include provision as to the date from which child benefit is to be payable to a person in respect of a child or qualifying young person[^c-56e68e541d541d223e81a54ba6310877] in a case where, before the benefit was awarded to that person, child benefit in respect of the child or qualifying young person[^c-a15b842acfef64173e44aa668ac9c8ce] was awarded to another person.
