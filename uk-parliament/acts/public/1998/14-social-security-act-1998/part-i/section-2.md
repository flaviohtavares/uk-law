# Social Security Act 1998, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2024-02-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/14

## 2. Use of computers[^c-3189c7b8474efed402f647d57abf05ee][^c-92217c00bf80256c5d4f184cdf2399fa]

(1)[^c-49ca4ad109fdd5eefd80670cc2b0228e] Any decision, determination or assessment falling to be made or certificate falling to be issued by the Secretary of State under or by virtue of a relevant enactment, or in relation to a war pension, may be made or issued not only by an officer of his acting under his authority but also—

- (a) by a computer for whose operation such an officer is responsible; and
- (b) in the case of a decision, determination or assessment that may be made or a certificate that may be issued by a person providing services to the Secretary of State, by a computer for whose operation such a person is responsible.

(2) In this section “relevant enactment” means any enactment contained in—

- (a)[^c-69209c66582f56b63be1ff6f197ba8d3][^c-d433bcd9b6381c42341dd7df70cb378d][^c-e8e9a22f46f847942037fc9854715b50][^c-870581c334698349f2479f29fcda9f16] Chapter II of this Part;
- (b)[^c-49ca4ad109fdd5eefd80670cc2b0228e] the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 (“the Contributions and Benefits Act”);
- (c)[^c-49ca4ad109fdd5eefd80670cc2b0228e] the Administration Act;
- (d)[^c-49ca4ad109fdd5eefd80670cc2b0228e] the Child Support Act;
- (e)[^c-49ca4ad109fdd5eefd80670cc2b0228e] the Social Security (Incapacity for Work) Act 1994;
- (f)[^c-49ca4ad109fdd5eefd80670cc2b0228e] the Jobseekers Act 1995 (“the Jobseekers Act”);
- (g)[^c-49ca4ad109fdd5eefd80670cc2b0228e] the Child Support Act 1995; [^c-5f6bdae3ccc4d6d5c46578409f8bf082]...
- (h)[^c-49ca4ad109fdd5eefd80670cc2b0228e] the Social Security (Recovery of Benefits) Act 1997; [^c-48f409feb06c66d083cea89518437b56]...
- (i)[^c-cd158cd6e3d68c3376ae7b6f4b328179] the State Pension Credit Act 2002; [^c-9b1c64ce140002d5794cdecb8817de92]...
- (j)[^c-2a1c94cb441bb7330b233e254d26913b] Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2007;
- (k)[^c-b7d078e32d329f87dbb928a14cfc15db] Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2012; [^c-b59cab082741c83ff8d22eadc6fd6271]...
- (l)[^c-cd2f9a1695daa5a4658100b9b885cee2] Part 4 of that Act;
- (m)[^c-907f62cb9d89df89eaff1a445dcbd86f] Part 1 of the Pensions Act 2014; [^c-fb9d9e8698065f90b3a1be301fc0db4a]...
- (n)[^c-37ef5a447094043e46d2a0446bf96aa0] section 30 of the Pensions Act 2014; or[^c-76b0d752dd7833f1f5b0da67faa280e7]
- (o) sections 18 to 21 of the Welfare Reform and Work Act 2016.

(3)[^c-49ca4ad109fdd5eefd80670cc2b0228e] In this section and section 3 below “war pension” has the same meaning as in section 25 of the Social Security Act 1989 (establishment and functions of war pensions committees).
