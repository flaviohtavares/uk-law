# Social Security Act 1998, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2024-02-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/14

## 1. Transfer of functions to Secretary of State[^c-a87418902f360e3894f3775708bbaac5]

The following functions are hereby transferred to the Secretary of State, namely—

- (a)[^c-a49acb04d0ace31c2c7954098260fe2a][^c-f5f873f11782b795323771749a0d376f][^c-8705fc488d5e9eaac5f57d34fe5cb598][^c-5334e7c80061f8b6df86f519e6b22639][^c-da61658c383e83abef185622d56ac75f] the functions of adjudication officers appointed under section 38 of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 (“the Administration Act”);
- (b) the functions of social fund officers appointed under section 64 of that Act; and
- (c)[^c-92a5374fa3cf914926864a9a0fe349f6] the functions of child support officers appointed under section 13 of the Child Support Act 1991 (“the Child Support Act”).
