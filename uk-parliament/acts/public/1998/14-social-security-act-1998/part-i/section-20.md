# Social Security Act 1998, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2024-02-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/14

## 20. Medical examination required by appeal tribunal[^c-b14c9bfef69034e7bd34f4e689b80802][^c-15397944cc4bb6263008cb0c7d4d5df5][^c-c9c063ca0074a27cdcd175d6f8a179c1]

(1)[^c-b08cdb3e2cf14b9457d599e6aa33d0d0][^c-7fb043dcdeb443c848a1716e4a6e033c][^c-9c5c7b3ef6faa18eebbc14b3ecf39471] This section applies where an appeal has been brought under section 12 above against a decision on a claim for a relevant benefit, or as to a person’s entitlement to such a benefit [^c-db2985be5065f62f8c1311b97d43b427]....

(2)[^c-b08cdb3e2cf14b9457d599e6aa33d0d0][^c-7fb043dcdeb443c848a1716e4a6e033c][^c-9c5c7b3ef6faa18eebbc14b3ecf39471] The First-tier Tribunal may, if conditions prescribed by Tribunal Procedure Rules[^c-4fd74296025e58b666feb47bd1b14f07] are satisfied, refer the person—

- (a) in respect of whom the claim is made; or
- (b) whose entitlement is at issue,

to a health care professional approved by the Secretary of State[^c-971f83d33ce1fa1ce9536bcb93daee43] for such examination and report as appears to the First-tier Tribunal[^c-214247880d67597f2d2d6569c13d177f] to be necessary for the purpose of providing it[^c-4a4045c88c39478fcff92a27c67be73f] with information for use in determining the appeal.

[^c-b99e6cfe0166c998694f536c656affa7]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A)[^c-d511c84e5d107c230519827baf0bbfe5] The power under subsection (2) to refer a person to a health care professional approved by the Secretary of State includes power to specify the description of health care professional to whom the person is to be referred.

(3) At a hearing before the First-tier Tribunal, except in cases or circumstances prescribed by Tribunal Procedure Rules,[^c-601ec35106b081f37e87968bacf16456] the tribunal—

- (a)[^c-b08cdb3e2cf14b9457d599e6aa33d0d0][^c-7fb043dcdeb443c848a1716e4a6e033c][^c-9c5c7b3ef6faa18eebbc14b3ecf39471] may not carry out a physical examination of the person mentioned in subsection (2) above; and
- (b) may not require that person to undergo any physical test for the purpose of determining whether he satisfies the condition mentioned in section 73(1)(a) of the Contributions and Benefits Act.
