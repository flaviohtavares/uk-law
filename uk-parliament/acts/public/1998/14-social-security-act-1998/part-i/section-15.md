# Social Security Act 1998, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2024-02-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/14

## 15. Applications for permission to appeal against a decision of the Upper Tribunal[^c-62b6d06cd639d0d484fa90c7bc1c6d11][^c-0fcb3ffba49e566ab005eceee6c155df][^c-d35302a14812a76ffd30db8c80a94ad7][^c-aeec3285402e51ea1c878c20a11cff66][^c-f1f6b40a268a6c5fa21c61cc09546a4c][^c-acefa1f729a4d0cbef3afe5fb866c326][^c-77a67345168f094930ac834e15c0b0db][^c-5eb259ecebbcb7bc11842c9c493b8e7f][^c-d8526584a0af775631a45095efee9470][^c-1aecc57d7f75350158e9677d1b4f6987][^c-77f16fd9090bc0a1452037c97f141de4]

(1)[^c-0d4ecdadac139a9df975d6265974e39a] Subject to subsections (2) and (3) below, an appeal on a question of law shall lie to the appropriate court from any decision of a Commissioner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0d4ecdadac139a9df975d6265974e39a]

(2)[^c-83de4038c4b2a775aac4a865b534f741] No appeal under this section shall lie from a decision except— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0d4ecdadac139a9df975d6265974e39a]

- (a) with the leave of the Commissioner who gave the decision or, in a prescribed case, with the leave of a Commissioner selected in accordance with regulations; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0d4ecdadac139a9df975d6265974e39a]
- (b) if he refuses leave, with the leave of the appropriate court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0d4ecdadac139a9df975d6265974e39a]

(3)[^c-83de4038c4b2a775aac4a865b534f741] An application for permission to appeal from a decision of the Upper Tribunal in respect of a decision of the First-tier Tribunal under section 12 or 13[^c-c156668584a298c30d6d2d4216c12b5d] may only be made by—

- (a) a person who, before the proceedings before the Upper Tribunal[^c-5d2e921bee34bd448c6dd852f388051d] were begun, was entitled to appeal to the Upper Tribunal from the decision to which the Upper Tribunal’s[^c-fd33ae04badafbe294e6d2e0630f0630] decision relates;
- (b) any other person who was a party to the proceedings in which the first decision mentioned in paragraph (a) above was given;
- (c) any other person who is authorised by regulations to apply for permission[^c-a6e4df47df6fd75fb616542e20ddef9a];

and regulations may make provision with respect to the manner in which and the time within which applications must be made to a Commissioner for leave under this section and with respect to the procedure for dealing with such applications. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f18d56d0bde4f4fbd7199ec80b10d533]

(4)[^c-b65e42d00e6d98a57743e4312a8012a7] On an application to a Commissioner for leave under this section it shall be the duty of the Commissioner to specify as the appropriate court— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b65e42d00e6d98a57743e4312a8012a7]

- (a) the Court of Appeal if it appears to him that the relevant place is in England or Wales; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b65e42d00e6d98a57743e4312a8012a7]
- (b) the Court of Session if it appears to him that the relevant place is in Scotland; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b65e42d00e6d98a57743e4312a8012a7]
- (c) the Court of Appeal in Northern Ireland if it appears to him that the relevant place is in Northern Ireland, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b65e42d00e6d98a57743e4312a8012a7]

except that if it appears to him, having regard to the circumstances of the case and in particular to the convenience of the persons who may be parties to the proposed appeal, that he should specify a different court mentioned in paragraphs (a) to (c) above as the appropriate court, it shall be his duty to specify that court as the appropriate court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b65e42d00e6d98a57743e4312a8012a7]

(5) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b65e42d00e6d98a57743e4312a8012a7]

- “the appropriate court”, except in subsection (4) above, means the court specified in pursuance of that subsection; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b65e42d00e6d98a57743e4312a8012a7]
- “the relevant place”, in relation to an application for leave to appeal from a decision of a Commissioner, means the premises where the person or authority whose decision was the subject of the Commissioner’s decision usually exercises his or its functions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b65e42d00e6d98a57743e4312a8012a7]
