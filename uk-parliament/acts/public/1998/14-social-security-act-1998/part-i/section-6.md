# Social Security Act 1998, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2024-02-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/14

## 6. Panel for appointment to appeal tribunals[^c-fb8cdeefdb67fcbfdba7c20cb48a8cb4][^c-a211fdc35240baa98b769bfb1d72001a][^c-398021f1c44ecbc35fd0a628d76a0986][^c-66c05d8d4a66aeafdb6c68eb239c1017][^c-a8bdb44de0619bdb083f9cec2d3e1a97]

(1) The Lord Chancellor shall constitute a panel of persons to act as members of appeal tribunals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8bdb44de0619bdb083f9cec2d3e1a97]

(2) Subject to subsection (3) below, the panel shall be composed of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8bdb44de0619bdb083f9cec2d3e1a97] persons appointed by the Lord Chancellor[^c-46b8a2de145aa805356dc9957a144cf3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8bdb44de0619bdb083f9cec2d3e1a97].

(3) The panel shall include persons possessing such qualifications as may be prescribed by regulations made with the concurrence of the Lord Chancellor. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8bdb44de0619bdb083f9cec2d3e1a97]

(3A)[^c-38e66084494225f16d416112a95599e3] As part of the selection process for the appointment of a medical practitioner as a member of the panel, the Judicial Appointments Commission shall consult the Chief Medical Officer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8bdb44de0619bdb083f9cec2d3e1a97]

(4) The numbers of persons appointed to the panel, and the terms and conditions of their appointments, shall be determined by the Lord Chancellor with the consent of the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8bdb44de0619bdb083f9cec2d3e1a97]

(5) A person may be removed from the panel by the Lord Chancellor on the ground of incapacity or misbehaviour *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8bdb44de0619bdb083f9cec2d3e1a97]; but the Lord Chancellor may remove such a person only with the concurrence of the appropriate senior judge[^c-c2de092c43a7fb285acce512186459b4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8bdb44de0619bdb083f9cec2d3e1a97].

(5A)[^c-03190e72cea66e3533ba1256b23effc6] The appropriate senior judge is the Lord Chief Justice of England and Wales, unless the person to be removed exercises functions wholly or mainly in Scotland, in which case it is the Lord President of the Court of Session. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8bdb44de0619bdb083f9cec2d3e1a97]

(6) In this section “the Chief Medical Officer” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8bdb44de0619bdb083f9cec2d3e1a97]

- (a) in relation to England, the Chief Medical Officer of the Department of Health; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8bdb44de0619bdb083f9cec2d3e1a97]
- (b) in relation to Wales, the Chief Medical Officer of the Welsh Office; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8bdb44de0619bdb083f9cec2d3e1a97]
- (c) in relation to Scotland, the Chief Medical Officer of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8bdb44de0619bdb083f9cec2d3e1a97] Scottish Administration[^c-25086cc376a1c6d83890066d1ea2cc6a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a8bdb44de0619bdb083f9cec2d3e1a97].
