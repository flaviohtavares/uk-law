to be a Social Security Commissioner (but to be known as a deputy Commissioner) for such period or on such occasions as the Lord Chancellor thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d14187e195a12ae6e45db471be47ea83]

(3) In this paragraph “10 year general qualification” shall be construed in accordance with section 71 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d14187e195a12ae6e45db471be47ea83] [^m00048]Courts and Legal Services Act 1990. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d14187e195a12ae6e45db471be47ea83]
