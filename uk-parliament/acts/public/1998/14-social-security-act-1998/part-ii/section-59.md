## 59. Levy of Class 4 contributions with income tax[^key-3a01b572a479c7695fe2aa032513e259]

(1) Schedule 2 to the Contributions and Benefits Act (levy of Class 4 contributions with income tax) and Schedule 2 to the [^m00032]Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 (corresponding provision for Northern Ireland) shall each be amended as follows.

(2)[^key-1da1c70e47917266b7f30561246c8125] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In paragraph 3, sub-paragraph (1)(b) shall cease to have effect.

(4) In sub-paragraph (2) of paragraph 4, for the words “may either be charged” to the end there shall be substituted the words “shall be charged on him separately”.

(5) In sub-paragraph (1) of paragraph 6—

- (a) for the words from “Sections 86” to “fault)” there shall be substituted the words “Section 86 of the [^m00033]Taxes Management Act 1970 (interest on overdue tax)”;
