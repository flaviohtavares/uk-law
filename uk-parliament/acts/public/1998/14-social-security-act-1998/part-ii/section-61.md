# Social Security Act 1998, section 61

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2024-02-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/14

## 61. Offences and penalties relating to contributions[^key-c81ce23103952685880ef01b27e85364]

For section 114 of the Administration Act there shall be substituted the following sections— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]

> **114. Offences relating to contributions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]**
>
> (1) Any person who is knowingly concerned in the fraudulent evasion of any contributions which he or any other person is liable to pay shall be guilty of an offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
>
> (2) A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
>
> - (a) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding seven years or to a fine or to both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
> - (b) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
>
> **114A. Penalties relating to contributions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]**
>
> (1) If a person fails to pay, at or within the time prescribed for the purpose, any contribution which he is liable to pay, he shall be liable to a penalty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
>
> (2) If a person fails to pay, at or within the time prescribed for the purpose, any sums which he is required by regulations made by virtue of paragraph 6 or 7B of Schedule 1 to the Contributions and Benefits Act to pay, he shall be liable to a penalty under subsection (1) above without proof of his failure so to pay any particular contribution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
>
> (3) For the purposes of subsection (1) above, regulations shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
>
> - (a) prescribe the amount or rate of penalty, or provide for how it is to be ascertained; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
> - (b) provide for the penalty to be imposed by the Secretary of State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
>   - (i) within six years after the date on which the penalty is incurred; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20] where the amount of the penalty is to be ascertained by reference to the amount of any contributions payable, at any later time within three years after the final determination of the amount of those contributions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
> - (c) provide for determining the date on which, for the purposes of paragraph (b) above, the penalty is incurred; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
> - (d) prescribe the means by which the penalty is to be enforced; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
> - (e) provide for enabling the Secretary of State, in his discretion, to mitigate or to remit any such penalty, or to stay or to compound any proceedings for a penalty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
>
> (4) Subsection (1) above does not apply— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
>
> - (a) to any failure by a person in respect of which he has been convicted of an offence under section 114(1) above; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
> - (b) to Class 4 contributions recoverable by the Inland Revenue. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0a23ea9bc4068d30b59d10261753e20]
