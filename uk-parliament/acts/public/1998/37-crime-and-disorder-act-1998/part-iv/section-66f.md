## 66F. Restriction on sentencing powers where youth conditional caution given[^c-eed9528ec4d908e99d734684b7cb93da][^c21397831]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a person who has been given a youth conditional caution is convicted of an offence committed within two years of the giving of the caution, the court by or before which the person is so convicted—

- (a) may not make an order under section 80 of the Sentencing Code[^c-ebaba074eb91e5e81658302ca1c4c74c] (conditional discharge) in respect of the offence unless it is of the opinion that there are exceptional circumstances relating to the offence or the offender which justify its doing so; and
- (b) where it does make such an order, must state in open court that it is of that opinion and why it is.
