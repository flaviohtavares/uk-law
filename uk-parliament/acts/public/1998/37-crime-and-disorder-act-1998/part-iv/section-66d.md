## 66D. Variation of conditions[^c21397831]

*Extent: E+W*

A relevant prosecutor or an authorised person[^c-da994dcd47ebcd148c77b0c72c0c0f68] may, with the consent of the offender, vary the conditions attached to a youth conditional caution by—

- (a) modifying or omitting any of the conditions;
- (b) adding a condition.
