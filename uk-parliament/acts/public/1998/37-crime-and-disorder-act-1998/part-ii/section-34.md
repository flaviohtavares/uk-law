> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (68).** Text as at 2026-06-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/37

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 34. Abolition of rebuttable presumption that a child is doli incapax.[^c9524081]

*Extent: E+W*

The rebuttable presumption of criminal law that a child aged 10 or over is incapable of committing an offence is hereby abolished.
