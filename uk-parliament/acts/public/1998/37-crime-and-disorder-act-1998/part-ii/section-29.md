# Crime and Disorder Act 1998, section 29

## 29. Racially or religiously aggravated assaults.[^c9523831][^c9523821]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person is guilty of an offence under this section if he commits—

- (a) an offence under section 20 of the Offences Against the [^c9523841]Person Act 1861 (malicious wounding or grievous bodily harm);
- (b) an offence under section 47 of that Act (actual bodily harm);
- (ba)[^c-adb49916a57a1aef823c6d49430d2d27] an offence under section 75A of the Serious Crime Act 2015 (strangulation or suffocation); or
- (c) common assault,

which is racially or religiously aggravated[^c9523851] for the purposes of this section.

(2) A person guilty of an offence falling within subsection (1)(a) , (b) or (ba)[^c-e3f3fa8a81530abf011013f5ee54e818] above shall be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or to both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding seven years or to a fine, or to both.

(3) A person guilty of an offence falling within subsection (1)(c) above shall be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or to both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or to a fine, or to both.
