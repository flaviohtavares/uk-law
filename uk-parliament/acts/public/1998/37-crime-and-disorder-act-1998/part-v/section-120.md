# Crime and Disorder Act 1998, section 120

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (68).** Text as at 2026-06-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 120. Transitional provisions, savings and repeals.[^c9527191]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The transitional provisions and savings contained in Schedule 9 to this Act shall have effect; but nothing in this subsection shall be taken as prejudicing the operation of sections 16 and 17 of the [^c9527201]Interpretation Act 1978 (which relate to the effect of repeals).

(2) The enactments specified in Schedule 10 to this Act, which include some that are spent, are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
