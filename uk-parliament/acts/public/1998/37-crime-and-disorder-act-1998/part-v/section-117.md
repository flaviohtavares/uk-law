# Crime and Disorder Act 1998, section 117

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (68).** Text as at 2026-06-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 117. General interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this Act—

- “**the 1933 Act**” means the [^c9526991]Children and Young Persons Act 1933;
- “**the 1969 Act**” means the [^c9527001]Children and Young Persons Act 1969;
- “**the 1973 Act**” means the [^c9527011]Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973;
- “**the 1980 Act**” means the [^c9527021]Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980;
- “**the 1982 Act**” means the [^c9527031]Criminal Justice Act 1982;
- “**the 1984 Act**” means the [^c9527041]Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984;
- “**the 1985 Act**” means the [^c9527051]Prosecution of Offences Act 1985;
- “**the 1989 Act**” means the [^c9527061]Children Act 1989;
- “**the 1991 Act**” means the [^c9527071]Criminal Justice Act 1991;
- “**the 1994 Act**” means the [^c9527081]Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994;
- “**the 1997 Act**” means the [^c9527091]Crime (Sentences) Act 1997;
- “**caution**” has the same meaning as in Part V of the [^c9527101]Police Act 1997;
- “**child**” means a person under the age of 14;
- [^c21392631]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**custodial sentence**” has the meaning given by section 222 of the Sentencing Code[^c-f37a658dccd7962e491a1ce73743c059];
- “**guardian**” has the same meaning as in the 1933 Act;
- “[^c9527131]**local probation board**” means a local probation board established under section 4 of the Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000;
- “**prescribed**” means prescribed by an order made by the Secretary of State;
- “**young person**” means a person who has attained the age of 14 and is under the age of 18;
- “**youth offending team**” means a team established under section 39 above.

(2) In this Act—

- “**the 1993 Act**” means the [^c9527141]Prisoners and Criminal Proceedings (Scotland) Act 1993; and
- “**the 1995 Act**” means the [^c9527151]Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995.

(3) For the purposes of this Act, the age of a person shall be deemed to be that which it appears to the court to be after considering any available evidence.
