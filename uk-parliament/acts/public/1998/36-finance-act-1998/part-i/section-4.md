# Finance Act 1998, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Rates of duty on cider.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 62(1A) of the [^c16419691]Alcoholic Liquor Duties Act 1979 (rates of duty on cider), for paragraphs (b) and (c) there shall be substituted the following paragraphs—

> - (b) £37.92 per hectolitre in the case of cider of a strength exceeding 7.5 per cent. which is not sparkling cider; and
> - (c) £25.27 per hectolitre in any other case.

(2) This section shall come into force on 1st January 1999.
