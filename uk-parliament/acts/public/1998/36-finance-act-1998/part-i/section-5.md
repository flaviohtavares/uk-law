# Finance Act 1998, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Drawback of excise duty on beer.[^c16419701]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 42 of the [^c16419711]Alcoholic Liquor Duties Act 1979 (drawback on exportation, shipment as stores etc.) shall cease to have effect.

(2) Subsection (1) above shall come into force on such day as the Commissioners of Customs and Excise may by order made by statutory instrument appoint.
