# Finance Act 1998, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Rate of duty on beer.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 36(1) of the [^c16419661]Alcoholic Liquor Duties Act 1979 (rate of duty on beer), for “£11.14" there shall be substituted “ £11.50 ”.

(2) This section shall come into force on 1st January 1999.
