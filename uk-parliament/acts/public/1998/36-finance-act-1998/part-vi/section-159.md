# Finance Act 1998, section 159

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 159. Treasury bills.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 8 of the [^c16422881]Treasury Bills Act 1877 (mode of issue of Treasury bills) the following shall be substituted for paragraph (1)—

> (1) Treasury bills shall be issued by the Treasury (either directly or through such agent as the Treasury think fit).

(2)[^c16422891] This section shall apply in relation to issues made on or after such day as the Treasury may appoint by order made by statutory instrument.
