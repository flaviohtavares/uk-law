## 24. Long leases in Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 96(1) of the [^c16419931]Value Added Tax Act 1994, in paragraph (b) of the definition of “major interest" (land in Scotland not held on feudal tenure: lessee’s interest must be for a period exceeding 21 years), for “exceeding 21 years" there shall be substituted “ of not less than 20 years ”.
