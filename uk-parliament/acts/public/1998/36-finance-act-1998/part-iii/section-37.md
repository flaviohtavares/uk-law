# Finance Act 1998, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/36

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Abolition of periodic accounting.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 51B of the Taxes Act 1988 (which enables provision to be made requiring tax on interest on gilt-edged securities to be accounted for periodically) shall cease to have effect.

(2)[^c16420061] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c16420071] The preceding provisions of this section have effect in relation only to payments of interest falling due on or after such day as the Treasury may by order appoint.
