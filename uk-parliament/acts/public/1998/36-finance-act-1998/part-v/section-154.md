# Finance Act 1998, section 154

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 154. Repeal of Customs Duties (Dumping and Subsidies) Act 1969.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The [^c16422861]Customs Duties (Dumping and Subsidies) Act 1969 (which confers powers on the Secretary of State, exercisable in accordance with section 6(5) of the [^c16422871]Finance Act 1978, to charge duties in respect of dumping and to offset subsidies) shall cease to have effect.
