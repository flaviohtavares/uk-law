Regional Development Agencies Act 1998

1998 c. 45

[^c-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]An Act to make provision for regional development agencies in England; to make provision about the Development Commission and the Urban Regeneration Agency; and for connected purposes.

\[19th November 1998\]

[^c11276171]Be it enacted by the Queen’s most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—
