## 15. Audit.[^c11276621][^key-8b36f39a78eaa0ec837168c52af6e1ad][^key-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The accounts of a regional development agency for each accounting period shall be audited by the Comptroller and Auditor General. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]

(2) A copy of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]

- (a) any accounts of a regional development agency which are audited under subsection (1), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]
- (b) the report made on those accounts by the Comptroller and Auditor General, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]

shall be sent to the Secretary of State as soon as reasonably practicable after the report is received by the agency; and the Secretary of State shall lay before each House of Parliament a copy of those accounts and that report. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]

(3) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]

- “**accounting period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]**” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]**accounting records *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]**” have the same meanings as in section 14; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]
- references to accounts, in relation to a regional development agency, include any statement prepared by it under that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]
