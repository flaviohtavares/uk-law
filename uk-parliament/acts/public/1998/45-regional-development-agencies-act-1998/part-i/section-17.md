## 17. Annual report.[^c11276691][^c-95582c1b85a1c5ef01a033614a619c67][^c-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) As soon as reasonably practicable after the end of each accounting period, a regional development agency shall prepare a report on its activities during that period and shall send a copy of that report to the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]

(2) A report under this section shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]

- (a) be in such form and contain such information as the Secretary of State may specify by directions to the agency, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]
- (b) set out any other directions given to the agency under this Part during the period to which the report relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]

(3) Following receipt of a report under this section, the Secretary of State shall lay a copy of it before each House of Parliament and arrange for copies of it to be published in such manner as he considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5a7be3349f6f3e92efa55ada9774411d]
