# Police (Northern Ireland) Act 1998, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/32
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 28. The Police Fund.[^c1371786]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department of Justice[^c-00baf21ab4ddc4434444ee43b8069fb6] may by regulations provide—

- (a) for the establishment, maintenance and operation of the Police Fund; and
- (b) for the payment into that fund of—
  - (i) fines imposed on members of the police force in disciplinary proceedings; and
  - (ii) such other moneys as may be specified in the regulations.

(2) The Department of Justice shall consult both the Board[^c1371787] and the Police Association before making any regulations under this section.
