# Police (Northern Ireland) Act 1998, section 74

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/32
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 74. Amendments, transitional provisions, repeals, etc.[^c1371962]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The statutory provisions mentioned in Schedule 4 shall be amended as provided in that Schedule.

(2) Schedule 5 (which contains transitional provisions and savings) shall have effect.

(3) The statutory provisions mentioned in Schedule 6 are repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.

(4) In the [^c1371963]Northern Ireland (Modification of Enactments – No. 1) Order 1973 the following provisions are revoked, namely—

- (a) Article 6(5) and (6)(c);
- (b) in Schedule 1, the entries relating to—
  - the [^c1371964]Constabulary (Ireland) Act 1836;
  - the [^c1371965]Police (Property) Act 1897;
  - the [^c1371966]Constabulary and Police (Ireland) Act 1919;
  - the [^c1371967]Police Act 1969;
  - the [^c1371968]Constabulary Act Northern Ireland) 1922;
  - the [^c1371969]Police Act Northern Ireland) 1970;
- (c) in Schedule 4, the entry relating to the [^c1371970]Police Act Northern Ireland) 1970; and
- (d) in Schedule 5, paragraphs 11, 21 and 61.
