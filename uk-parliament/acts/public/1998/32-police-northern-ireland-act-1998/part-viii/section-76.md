# Police (Northern Ireland) Act 1998, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Extent.[^c1371972]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Except as provided by subsections (2) and (3), this Act extends to Northern Ireland only.

(2)[^key-3284a418a6cef9432f20e9b79722ecb1] Paragraph 8 of Schedule 3 extends throughout the United Kingdom.

(3) The amendment or repeal by this Act of any provision of—

- (a) the [^c1371973]Police (Overseas Service) Act 1945;
- (b) the [^c1371974]Police (Scotland) Act 1967;
- (c) the [^c1371975]House of Commons Disqualification Act 1975;
- (d) the [^c1371976]Northern Ireland Assembly Disqualification Act 1975;
- (e) the [^c1371977]Ministry of Defence Police Act 1987;
- (f) the [^c1371978]Official Secrets Act 1989;
- (g) the [^c1371979]Police Act 1996;
- (h) the [^c1371980]Police Act 1997,

has the same extent as that provision.
