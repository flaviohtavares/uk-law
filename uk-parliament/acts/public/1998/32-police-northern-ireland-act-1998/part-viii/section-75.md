# Police (Northern Ireland) Act 1998, section 75

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/32

## 75. Commencement.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c1371971] This Act shall come into operation on such day as the Secretary of State may by order appoint.

(2) An order under this section may appoint different days for different purposes and for different provisions.
