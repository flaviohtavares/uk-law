## 42. Publication of reports of inspectors of constabulary.[^c-7319226634a9e3179c65a6101279cc03][^c-035560b6b54c3562d6095d680bd67d0e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to subsection (2), the Department of Justice[^c-5378b88c939089c3dbe7168042a7324f] shall arrange for any report received by it[^c-b757589cbd309563c821cbd40744e951] under section 41(2) [^c-bf67a0ee1535dc4d560a43d2201f5507]...and any report of an inspection under section 41(4)(a)[^c-f6d3b16abe7a055f6b8234be309808bd] to be published in such manner as appears to it to be appropriate.

(2) The Department of Justice[^c-dfdbc131171035d40a0267a43fc027de] may exclude from publication under subsection (1) any part of a report if, in its[^c-3b66c1c18269f97a5f02fc48716bd0fa] opinion, the publication of that part—

- (a) would be [^c-27a1d794219a11b6d1a9028b12b7da41]... prejudicial to public order; or
- (b) might jeopardise the safety of any person.

(3) The Department of Justice[^c-ef1d04b1ad9430850e1d16d81f99a502] shall send a copy of the published report to—

- (a) the Board[^c1371847]; and
- (b) the Chief Constable.

(4) The Board shall invite the Chief Constable to submit comments on the published report to the Board[^c1371849] before such date as it may specify.

(5) The Board shall prepare comments on the published report and shall arrange for—

- (a) its comments;
- (b) any comments submitted by the Chief Constable in accordance with subsection (4); and
- (c) any response which the Board has to the comments submitted by the Chief Constable,

to be published in such manner as appears to the Board to be appropriate.

(6) The Board shall send a copy of any document published under subsection (5) to the Department of Justice[^c-58893429e397b873e9816a1868bcfa6c].

(6A)[^c-2f43104228b943db1ac4fa25e1922a1f] In relation to a report received by the Secretary of State under section 41(3B)—

- (a) subsections (1) to (6) above apply as if references to the Department of Justice were references to the Secretary of State;
- (b) under subsection (2) above, the Secretary of State may also exclude from publication under subsection (1) any part of the report if, in the Secretary of State’s opinion, the publication of that part would be against the interests of national security.
