# Police (Northern Ireland) Act 1998, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1998/32
>

## 46. Expenditure by Department of Justice for police purposes.[^c-9258c0208fab453bb2f5765985fdcfad]

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department of Justice[^c-22193a73b0596f93ec404562b03b03fa] may—

- (a) make such contribution to the provision or maintenance of such organisations, facilities and services; and
- (b) make such other payments,

as the Department of Justice[^c-21cb28c3d9ed76a7f6b898cd486b3f0f] thinks necessary or expedient for promoting the efficiency and effectiveness of —[^c1371868]

- (a) the Police Service of Northern Ireland;
- (b) the Police Service of Northern Ireland Reserve;
- (c) the police support staff; and
- (d) traffic wardens.
