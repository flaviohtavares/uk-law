House Of Commons Offices Act 1856

1856 c.1

An Act to regulate certain Offices of the House of Commons.

\[28th February 1856\][^c561609][^c561610]

## 1. †Appointment of clerk assistant confirmed, and future clerks assistant to be appointed by Her Majesty.[^c561611]

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c561612] the clerk assistant and second clerk assistant of the House of Commons respectively shall be appointed by Her Majesty, by warrant under her Royal Sign Manual; and every clerk assistant and second clerk assistant shall be removable only by Her Majesty, upon an address of the House of Commons to Her Majesty for that purpose.
