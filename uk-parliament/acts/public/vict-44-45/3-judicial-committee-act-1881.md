# Judicial Committee Act 1881

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Judicial Committee Act 1881 (repealed)

1881 c.3

[^c2075984]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## 1. Lords Justices of Appeal to be members of Judicial Committee.

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

Every person holding or who has held in England the office of a Lord Justice of Appeal shall, if a member of Her Majesty’s Privy Council in England, be a member of the Judicial Committee of the Privy Council but no person shall be a member of that Committee by virtue of this section at any time after the day on which he attains the age of seventy-five years.[^c570168]

## 2. Short title.

**No longer in force (ended 2009-10-01).**

This Act may be cited as the Judicial Committee Act 1881.
