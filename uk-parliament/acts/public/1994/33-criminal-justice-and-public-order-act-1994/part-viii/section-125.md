# Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994, section 125

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 125. Interpretation of Chapter III.[^c13104891]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Chapter—

- “**prison**” includes a young offenders centre or remand centre;
- “**prisoner custody officer**” has the meaning given by section 122(1) above;
- “**prison rules**” means rules made under section 13 of the [^c13104901]Prison Act (Northern Ireland) 1953;
- “**prisoner**” means any person for the time being detained in lawful custody as the result of a requirement imposed by a court or otherwise that he be so detained;
- “**prisoner escort arrangements**” has the meaning given by section 118(3) above.

(2) Sections 118, 119(1) and (2)(a), 120 and 122 to 124 above, subsection (1) above and Schedule 7 to this Act shall have effect as if—

- (a) any reference in section 118(1), 119(1), 120 or 124 above to prisoners included a reference to persons remanded or committed to custody in certain premises under section 51, 74 or 75 of the [^c13104911]Children and Young Persons Act (Northern Ireland) 1968 or ordered to be sent to a training school under section 74 or 78 of that Act; and
- (b) any reference in section 118(1)(c) or (d) or (2) above to a prison included a reference to such premises or training school.

(3) In sections 118, 120 and 121 above, “**prison**”—

- (a) so far as relating to the delivery of prisoners to or from a prison situated in England and Wales, includes a remand centre or[^c13104921] young offender institution; and
- (b) so far as relating to the delivery of prisoners to or from a prison situated in Scotland, includes a remand centre or young offenders institution within the meaning of section 19 of the [^c13104931]Prisons (Scotland) Act 1989.
