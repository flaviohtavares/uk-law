# Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994, section 94

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94. Powers and duties of prisoner custody officers acting in pursuance of such arrangements.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For subsection (4) of section 82 of the 1991 Act (powers and duties of prisoner custody officers acting in pursuance of such arrangements) there shall be substituted the following subsection—

> (4) Where a prisoner custody officer acting in pursuance of prisoner escort arrangements is on any premises in which the Crown Court or a magistrates’ court is sitting, it shall be his duty to give effect to any order of that court made—
>
> - (a) in the case of the Crown Court, under section 34A of the 1973 Act (power of Court to order search of persons before it); or
> - (b) in the case of a magistrates’ court, under section 80 of the 1980 Act (application of money found on defaulter).

.

(2) After subsection (2) of section 6 of the [^c13103911]Imprisonment (Temporary Provisions) Act 1980 (detention in the custody of a police constable) there shall be inserted the following subsection—

> (3) Any reference in this section to a constable includes a reference to a prisoner custody officer (within the meaning of Part IV of the [^c13103921]Criminal Justice Act 1991) acting in pursuance of prisoner escort arrangements (within the meaning of that Part).

.
