# Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994, section 68

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Offence of aggravated trespass.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person commits the offence of aggravated trespass if he trespasses on land in the open air *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19809631] and, in relation to any lawful activity which persons are engaging in or are about to engage in on that or adjoining land in the open air *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19809641], does there anything which is intended by him to have the effect—

- (a) of intimidating those persons or any of them so as to deter them or any of them from engaging in that activity,
- (b) of obstructing that activity, or
- (c) of disrupting that activity.

(1A)[^c19810111] The reference in subsection (1) above to trespassing includes, in Scotland, the exercise of access rights (within the meaning of the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 2)) up to the point when they cease to be exercisable by virtue of the commission of the offence under that subsection.

(2) Activity on any occasion on the part of a person or persons on land is “**lawful**” for the purposes of this section if he or they may engage in the activity on the land on that occasion without committing an offence or trespassing on the land.

(3) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale, or both.

(4)[^c19812951] A constable in uniform who reasonably suspects that a person is committing an offence under this section may arrest him without a warrant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19812951]

(5) In this section “**land**” does not include—

- (a)[^key-f486f4bf0897ecfd14908130c598575b] a highway unless it is a footpath, bridleway or byway open to all traffic within the meaning of Part 3 of the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981, is a restricted byway within the meaning of Part 2 of the Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000 or is a cycle track under the Highways Act 1980 or the Cycle Tracks Act 1984;
- (aa) a road within the meaning of the Roads (Scotland) Act 1984 unless it falls within the definitions in section 151(2)(a)(ii) or (b) (footpaths and cycle tracks) of that Act or is a bridleway within the meaning of section 47 of the Countryside (Scotland) Act 1967; or
- (b) a road within the meaning of the [^c13103251]Roads (Northern Ireland) Order 1993.
