> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/33

## 92. Obscene, offensive or annoying telephone calls: increase in penalty.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19817561] In section 43(1) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19817561] [^c13103831]Telecommunications Act 1984 (which makes a person convicted of certain offences relating to improper use of public telecommunication systems liable to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale), for the words “a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale” there shall be substituted the words *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19817561] “ imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale or both ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19817561].

(2) Subsection (1) above does not apply to an offence committed before this section comes into force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19817561]
