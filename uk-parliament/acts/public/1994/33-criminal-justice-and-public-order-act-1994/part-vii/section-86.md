# Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994, section 86

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Indecent photographs of children: sentence of imprisonment.[^c13103701]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) In section 160(3) of the [^c13103711]Criminal Justice Act 1988 (which makes a person convicted of certain offences relating to indecent photographs of children liable to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale) there shall be inserted after the word “to” the words “ imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or ” and at the end the words “ , or both ”.

(2) In Article 15(3) of the [^c13103721]Criminal Justice (Evidence, etc.) (Northern Ireland) Order 1988 (which makes a person convicted in Northern Ireland of certain offences relating to indecent photographs of children liable to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale) there shall be inserted after the word “to” the words “ imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or ” and at the end the words “ , or both ”.
