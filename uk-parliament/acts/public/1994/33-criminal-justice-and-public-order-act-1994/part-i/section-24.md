## 24. Detention of arrested juveniles after charge.

*Extent: E+W*

In section 38(6) of the [^c13100801]Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (detention of arrested juveniles after charge), in paragraph (b), for the words “age of 15 years” there shall be substituted the words “ age of 12 years ”.
