# Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Escort arrangements and officers.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The provisions of Schedule 1 to this Act (which make provision for escort arrangements persons detained in youth detention accommodation[^c19830951]) shall have effect.

(2) The provisions of Schedule 2 to this Act shall have effect with respect to the certification of custody officers.

(3)[^c13100591] In this Part, “**custody officer**” means a person in respect of whom a certificate is for the time being in force certifying—

- (a)[^c19830991] that he has been approved by the Secretary of State for the purpose of performing any of the following—
  - (i) escort functions;
  - (ii) custodial duties at secure training centres;
- (b) that he is accordingly authorised to perform them.
