# Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994, section 149

## 149. Incorporation of the Parole Board.

*Extent: E+W*

In section 32 of the [^c13106081]Criminal Justice Act 1991 (which provides the constitution and basic functions of the Parole Board), for subsection (1), there shall be substituted the following subsection—

> (1) The Parole Board shall be, by that name, a body corporate and as such shall be constituted in accordance with, and have the functions conferred by, this Part.
