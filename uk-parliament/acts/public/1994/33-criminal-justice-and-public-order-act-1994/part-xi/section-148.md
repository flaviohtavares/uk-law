## 148. Amendment of law relating to homosexual acts in Scotland.

*Extent: S*

In section 80(6) of the [^c13106071]Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1980 (which defines “**homosexual act**” for the purpose of section 80), after “gross indecency” there is inserted “ or shameless indecency ”.
