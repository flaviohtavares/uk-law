# Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994, section 19A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19A. Payment for licences by cheque.[^c12508001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may, if he thinks fit, issue a vehicle licence or a trade licence on receipt of a cheque for the amount of the duty payable on it.

(2) In a case where—

- (a) a vehicle licence or a trade licence is issued to a person on receipt of a cheque which is subsequently dishonoured, and
- (b) the Secretary of State sends a notice [^key-de20dacbe465afa2750f10de208da12f]... to the person informing him that the licence is void as from the time when it was granted,

the licence shall be void as from the time when it was granted.

(3) In a case where—

- (a) a vehicle licence or a trade licence is issued to a person on receipt of a cheque which is subsequently dishonoured,
- (b) the Secretary of State sends a notice [^key-7fb57c25540cb641167d9950559b7302]... to the person requiring him to secure that the duty payable on the licence is paid within such reasonable period as is specified in the notice,
- (c) the requirement in the notice is not complied with, and
- (d) the Secretary of State sends a further notice [^key-fc1795749f5620d64ff22d25b67974eb]... to the person informing him that the licence is void as from the time when it was granted,

the licence shall be void as from the time when it was granted.

(4) Section 102 of the [^c12508011]Customs and Excise Management Act 1979 (payment for excise licences by cheque) shall not apply in relation to a vehicle licence or a trade licence.
