# Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Admissions.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where in any proceedings in England and Wales or Northern Ireland for an offence under section 29 , 34 or 43A[^c12510511]—

- (a) it is appropriately proved that there has been served on the accused by post a requirement under section 46(1) or (2) to give information as to the identity of—
  - (i) the driver of, or a person who used, a particular vehicle, or
  - (ii) the person who kept a particular vehicle on a road,

  on the particular occasion on which the offence is alleged to have been committed, and
- (b) a statement in writing is produced to the court purporting to be signed by the accused that he was—
  - (i) the driver of, or a person who used, that vehicle, or
  - (ii) the person who kept that vehicle on a road,

  on that occasion.

(2) Where this section applies, the court may accept the statement as evidence that the accused was—

- (a) the driver of, or a person who used, that vehicle, or
- (b) the person who kept that vehicle on a road,

on that occasion.

(3) In subsection (1) “**appropriately proved**” means proved to the satisfaction of the court—

- (a) on oath, or
- (b) in the manner prescribed—
  - (i) in England and Wales, by Criminal Procedure Rules[^c18054981] , or
  - (ii) in Northern Ireland, by magistrates’ courts rules, as defined by Article 2(3) of the [^c12510531]Magistrates’ Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.
