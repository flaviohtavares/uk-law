# Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/22

## 53. Burden of proof.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where in any proceedings for an offence under section 29, 31A,[^c18044061] 34, 37 or 45 any question arises as to—

- (a) the number of vehicles used,
- (b) the character, weight or cylinder capacity of a vehicle,
- (c) the seating capacity of a vehicle, or
- (d) the purpose for which a vehicle has been used,

the burden of proof in respect of the matter lies on the accused.
