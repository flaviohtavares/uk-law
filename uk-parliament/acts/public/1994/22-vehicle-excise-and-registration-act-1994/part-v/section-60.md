# Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994, section 60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Orders.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any power of the Secretary of State to make an order under this Act is exercisable by statutory instrument.

(2) A statutory instrument containing an order under section 3(3) , paragraph 18(4) of Schedule 1[^c12510981] or paragraph 8 of Schedule 4 is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(3) No order is to be made under section 5(3) or[^c19685071] paragraph 5(5) of Schedule 1 unless a draft of the order has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.
