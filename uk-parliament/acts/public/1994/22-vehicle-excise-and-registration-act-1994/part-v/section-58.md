## 58. Fees prescribed by regulations.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any fee prescribed by regulations under section [^c-ebee7fed16d972fb3b7864372be9722c]... 14(4)(b) or 19C(2)[^c18064461] , and any charge prescribed by regulations under section 25(1), shall be of an amount approved by the Treasury.

(2) Section 128 of the [^c12510951]Finance Act 1990 (power to provide for repayment of fees and charges) applies to any power under this Act to make provision for payment of a fee or charge as it applies to any power to make such provision conferred before that Act was passed.
