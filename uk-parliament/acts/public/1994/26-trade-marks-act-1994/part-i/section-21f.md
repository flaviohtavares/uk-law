# Trade Marks Act 1994, section 21F

> Text as at 2025-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21F. Supplementary: proceedings for delivery up etc.[^c-0a6bb98b6839e4cca75c7e192e6fa27d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 21(1)(b) the reference to proceedings for infringement of a registered trade mark includes a reference to—

- (a) proceedings for an order under section 16 (order for delivery up of infringing goods, material or articles), and
- (b) proceedings for an order under section 19 (order as to disposal of infringing goods, material or articles).
