# Trade Marks Act 1994

> Text as at 2025-07-10.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Trade Marks Act 1994

1994 c. 26

An Act to make new provision for registered trade marks, implementing Council Directive No. [89/104/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1989/104) of 21st December 1988 to approximate the laws of the Member States relating to trade marks; to make provision in connection with Council Regulation [(EC) No. 40/94](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/1994/40) of 20th December 1993 on the Community trade mark; to give effect to the Madrid Protocol Relating to the International Registration of Marks of 27th June 1989, and to certain provisions of the Paris Convention for the Protection of Industrial Property of 20th March 1883, as revised and amended; and for connected purposes.

\[21st July 1994\]

[^c1130328][^c1130329][^c1130330]Be it enacted by the Queen’s most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—[^key-01334f2f0f60c1c4c9650acf2167bdd6][^key-0c0341393963cdf7565ad4916af46f0c]
