# Trade Marks Act 1994, section 51

> Text as at 2025-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Meaning of “European Union trade mark”[^c-15c735da978fb9ddd3e332fa70ce2a8c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

- In this Act—
- “European Union trade mark” has the meaning given by Article 1(1) of the European Union Trade Mark Regulation; and
- “the European Union Trade Mark Regulation” means Regulation ([^c-5bf19f5ad0821305f800ee5582392f9d]EU) 2017/1001 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 June 2017 on the European Union Trade Mark (as it had effect immediately before IP completion day)[^c-a167188188a28f7da786fcc7f1b437b6].
