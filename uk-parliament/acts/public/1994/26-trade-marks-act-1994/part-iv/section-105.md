# Trade Marks Act 1994, section 105

> Text as at 2025-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 105. Transitional provisions.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provisions of Schedule 3 have effect with respect to transitional matters, including the treatment of marks registered under the [^c1130453]Trade Marks Act 1938, and applications for registration and other proceedings pending under that Act, on the commencement of this Act.
