# Trade Marks Act 1994, section 108

> Text as at 2025-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 108. Extent.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act extends to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

(2)[^c1130455] This Act also extends to the Isle of Man, subject to such exceptions and modifications as Her Majesty may specify by Order in Council; and subject to any such Order references in this Act to the United Kingdom shall be construed as including the Isle of Man.
