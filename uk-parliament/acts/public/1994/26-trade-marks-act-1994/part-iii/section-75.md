# Trade Marks Act 1994, section 75

> Text as at 2025-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. The court.[^c-984eeef1e29c84ff7ecb71da3ff64e18][^c-be4098563972af9db3052f3bbc14b49f][^c-f130962c131e3c820d88d02ff2a2aeea][^c-0bb765e61acdf6c3946d231ddf10d907][^c-ac4a0d4ecc79828bfe69fa2a78e96657]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires, “**the court**” means—

- (a) in England and Wales , the High Court *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-1d88b0a16bfa30b94a15540947e87631] , or the county court where it has[^c-262c078a6509ffe618403ece3b17add9] *(Extent: E+W)* jurisdiction by virtue of an order made under section 1 of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990, *(Extent: E+W)*
- (aa (Extent: E+W)) in *(Extent: E+W)* Northern Ireland, the High Court, and
- (b) in Scotland, the Court of Session.
