# Trade Marks Act 1994, section 86

> Text as at 2025-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Use of the term “trade mark attorney”.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No offence is committed under the enactments restricting the use of certain expressions in reference to persons not qualified to act as solicitors by the use of the term “trade mark attorney” in reference to a registered trade mark attorney[^key-7a4fb3cf394235ad833ce5f22e3bbc93].

(2) The enactments referred to in subsection (1) are section 21 of the [^c1130397]Solicitors Act 1974, section 31 of the [^c1130398]Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1980 and Article 22 of the Solicitors (Northern Ireland) Order 1976.
