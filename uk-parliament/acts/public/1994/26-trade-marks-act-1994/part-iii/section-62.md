# Trade Marks Act 1994, section 62

> Text as at 2025-07-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. The registrar.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act “**the registrar**” means the Comptroller-General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks.
