# Police and Magistrates’ Courts Act 1994, section 93

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2008-07-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. Repeals.[^c16321261]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The enactments mentioned in Schedule 9 to this Act (which include spent enactments) are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
