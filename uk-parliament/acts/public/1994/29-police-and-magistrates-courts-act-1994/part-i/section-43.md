# Police and Magistrates’ Courts Act 1994, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2008-07-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Application to police authorities of enactments relating to local authorities etc.[^c16320741]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 4 to this Act (which makes amendments relating to the application of enactments to police authorities, including amendments providing for them to be treated as local authorities for certain purposes) shall have effect.
