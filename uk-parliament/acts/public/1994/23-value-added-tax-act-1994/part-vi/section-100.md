## 100. Savings and transitional provisions, consequential amendments and repeals.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 13 (savings and transitional provisions) and Schedule 14 (consequential amendments) shall have effect.

(2) The enactments and Orders specified in Schedule 15 are hereby repealed to the extent mentioned in the third column of that Schedule.

(3) This section is without prejudice to the operation of sections 15 to 17 of the [^c15296261]Interpretation Act 1978 (which relate to the effect of repeals).
