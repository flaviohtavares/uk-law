# Value Added Tax Act 1994, section 99

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Refund of VAT to Government of Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commissioners shall refund to the Government of Northern Ireland the amount of the VAT charged on the supply of goods or services to that Government [^c-1be9118ceda8775c77c7f27e9a8153a7]... or on the importation of any goods by that Government [^c-04e3fc8befd72ffff0076bb82d2e4d31]..., after deducting therefrom so much of that amount as may be agreed between them and the Department of Finance and Personnel for Northern Ireland as attributable to supplies [^c-d73a572049cd416b9b94508ad5dcdc7f]... and importations for the purpose of a business carried on by the Government of Northern Ireland.
