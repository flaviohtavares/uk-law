## 2. Rate of VAT.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-dc45076ac9f5ced2870bee1178421aad] Subject to the following provisions and to the provisions of section 29A[^M_F_f16b395d-5706-4422-8267-2211c103b3f5] of this section [^c15289381]..., VAT shall be charged at the rate of 20 per cent[^c-bfdfc100f413ff6111a183a77a1b408c] and shall be charged—

- (a) on the supply of goods or services, by reference to the value of the supply as determined under this Act; and
- (b)[^c-3650f0befabde54060839a4a1a93c7cf] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) on the importation of goods [^c-f7764b6746b37d819e68b1db03423d49]..., by reference to the value of the goods as determined under this Act.

(1A)[^c15289391] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1B)[^c15289391] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1C)[^c15289411] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The Treasury may by order increase or decrease the rate of VAT for the time being in force under this section[^M_F_a50cb9cd-fc05-4152-af30-cfee7dc086b2] by such percentage thereof not exceeding 25 per cent. as may be specified in the order, but any such order that has not previously expired or been revoked[^c-425e9337902d79c683d26de7e6648002] shall cease to be in force at the expiration of a period of one year from the date on which it takes effect, unless continued in force by a further order under this subsection.

(3) In relation to an order made under subsection (2) above to continue, vary or replace a previous order, the reference in that subsection to the rate for the time being in force under this section is a reference to the rate which would be in force if no order under that subsection had been made.
