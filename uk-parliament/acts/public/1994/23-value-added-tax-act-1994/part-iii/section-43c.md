# Value Added Tax Act 1994, section 43C

## 43C. Groups: termination of membership.[^key-4a907c06b0ca166b7ee23cd1ded60c7a][^c15291401]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Commissioners may, by notice given to a person[^key-19a032a666b2fae1ff3f501c53786867], terminate its treatment as a member of a group from a date—

- (a) which is specified in the notice, and
- (b) which is, or falls after, the date on which the notice is given.

(2) The Commissioners may give a notice under subsection (1) above only if it appears to them to be necessary for the protection of the revenue.

(3) Where—

- (a) a person[^key-9dd1b853556fc13334280099649d2533] is treated as a member of a group, and
- (b) it appears to the Commissioners that the person[^key-c6403ec8d1cfc0eddd53da90ec221163] is not, or is no longer, eligible by virtue of section 43A[^key-162dc2d0fed7ef431e83fe9df61593e6] to be treated as a member of the group,

the Commissioners shall, by notice given to the person[^key-c51ec32517e03df676b20768b92fe392], terminate its treatment as a member of the group from a date specified in the notice.

(4) The date specified in a notice under subsection (3) above may be earlier than the date on which the notice is given but shall not be earlier than—

- (a) the first date on which, in the opinion of the Commissioners, the person[^key-894299d95ca3ace067df18a6d4022fbd] was not eligible to be treated as a member of the group, or
- (b) the date on which, in the opinion of the Commissioners, the person[^key-fbf0992e5c21b35ff51d4a8bb44a2eff] ceased to be eligible to be treated as a member of the group.
