## 51B. Face-value vouchers issued before 1 January 2019[^key-c475d428e4c25b5f485452729f1c0e85][^key-7c23877d746850e9eb658ca4d402114e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-7263b24ec1d32c07575a81afe6eeefe4] Schedule 10A shall have effect with respect to face-value vouchers.

(2)[^key-5775daa42137884fabec1c2824d57ec1] Schedule 10A does not have effect with respect to a face value voucher (within the meaning of that Schedule) issued on or after 1 January 2019.
