## 52. Trading stamp schemes.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commissioners may by regulations modify section 19 and Schedule 6[^key-a44ad30a6bcacebebc3760d31a31b4aa] for the purpose of providing (in place of the provision for the time being contained in that section and Schedule)[^key-eb0a4391ec66f353acae4fe765141393] for the manner of determining for the purposes of this Act the value of—

- (a) a supply of goods, [^key-a3091bb8b47a2d72cd2f2a61311f4e9c]...
- (b)[^key-a3091bb8b47a2d72cd2f2a61311f4e9c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

in a case where the goods are supplied [^key-d93f2791cc245032f28e860d8aec5cbe]... under a trading stamp scheme (within the meaning of the [^c15291541]Trading Stamps Act 1964 or the [^c15291551]Trading Stamps Act (Northern Ireland) 1965) [^key-2728b605fc097d0a3b117ddcbbd58b29]....
