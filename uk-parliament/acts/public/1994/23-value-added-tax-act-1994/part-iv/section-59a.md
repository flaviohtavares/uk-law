## 59A. Default surcharge: payments on account.[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca][^c15292001]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this section a taxable person shall be regarded as in default in respect of any prescribed accounting period if the period is one in respect of which he is required, by virtue of an order under section 28, to make any payment on account of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] and either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) a payment which he is so required to make in respect of that period has not been received in full by the Commissioners by the day on which it became due; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) he would, but for section 59(1A), be in default in respect of that period for the purposes of section 59. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(2) Subject to subsections (10) and (11) below, subsection (4) below applies in any case where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) a taxable person is in default in respect of a prescribed accounting period; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) the Commissioners serve notice on the taxable person (a “surcharge liability notice”) specifying as a surcharge period for the purposes of this section a period which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
  - (i) begins, subject to subsection (3) below, on the date of the notice; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] ends on the first anniversary of the last day of the period referred to in paragraph (a) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(3) If— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) a surcharge liability notice is served by reason of a default in respect of a prescribed accounting period, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) that period ends at or before the expiry of an existing surcharge period already notified to the taxable person concerned, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

the surcharge period specified in that notice shall be expressed as a continuation of the existing surcharge period; and, accordingly, the existing period and its extension shall be regarded as a single surcharge period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(4) Subject to subsections (7) to (11) below, if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) a taxable person on whom a surcharge liability notice has been served is in default in respect of a prescribed accounting period, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) that prescribed accounting period is one ending within the surcharge period specified in (or extended by) that notice, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (c) the aggregate value of his defaults in respect of that prescribed accounting period is more than nil, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

that person shall be liable to a surcharge equal to whichever is the greater of £30 and the specified percentage of the aggregate value of his defaults in respect of that prescribed accounting period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(5) Subject to subsections (7) to (11) below, the specified percentage referred to in subsection (4) above shall be determined in relation to a prescribed accounting period by reference to the number of such periods during the surcharge period which are periods in respect of which the taxable person is in default and in respect of which the value of his defaults is more than nil, so that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) in relation to the first such prescribed accounting period, the specified percentage is 2 per cent.; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) in relation to the second such period, the specified percentage is 5 per cent.; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (c) in relation to the third such period, the specified percentage is 10 per cent.; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (d) in relation to each such period after the third, the specified percentage is 15 per cent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(6) For the purposes of this section the aggregate value of a person’s defaults in respect of a prescribed accounting period shall be calculated as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) where the whole or any part of a payment in respect of that period on account of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] was not received by the Commissioners by the day on which it became due, an amount equal to that payment or, as the case may be, to that part of it shall be taken to be the value of the default relating to that payment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) if there is more than one default with a value given by paragraph (a) above, those values shall be aggregated; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (c) the total given by paragraph (b) above, or (where there is only one default) the value of the default under paragraph (a) above, shall be taken to be the value for that period of that person’s defaults on payments on account; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (d) the value of any default by that person which is a default falling within subsection (1)(b) above shall be taken to be equal to the amount of any outstanding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] less the amount of unpaid payments on account; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (e) the aggregate value of a person’s defaults in respect of that period shall be taken to be the aggregate of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
  - (i) the value for that period of that person’s defaults (if any) on payments on account; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] the value of any default of his in respect of that period that falls within subsection (1)(b) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(7) In the application of subsection (6) above for the calculation of the aggregate value of a person’s defaults in respect of a prescribed accounting period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) the amount of outstanding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] referred to in paragraph (d) of that subsection is the amount (if any) which would be the amount of that person’s outstanding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] for that period for the purposes of section 59(4); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) the amount of unpaid payments on account referred to in that paragraph is the amount (if any) equal to so much of any payments on account of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] (being payments in respect of that period) as has not been received by the Commissioners by the last day on which that person is required (as mentioned in section 59(1)) to make a return for that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(8) If a person who, apart from this subsection, would be liable to a surcharge under subsection (4) above satisfies the Commissioners or, on appeal, a tribunal— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) in the case of a default that is material for the purposes of the surcharge and falls within subsection (1)(a) above— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
  - (i) that the payment on account of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] was despatched at such a time and in such a manner that it was reasonable to expect that it would be received by the Commissioners by the day on which it became due, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] that there is a reasonable excuse for the payment not having been so despatched, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

  or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) in the case of a default that is material for the purposes of the surcharge and falls within subsection (1)(b) above, that the condition specified in section 59(7)(a) or (b) is satisfied as respects the default, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

he shall not be liable to the surcharge and for the purposes of the preceding provisions of this section he shall be treated as not having been in default in respect of the prescribed accounting period in question (and, accordingly, any surcharge liability notice the service of which depended upon that default shall be deemed not to have been served). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(9) For the purposes of subsection (8) above, a default is material to a surcharge if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) it is the default which, by virtue of subsection (4) above, gives rise to the surcharge; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) it is a default which was taken into account in the service of the surcharge liability notice upon which the surcharge depends and the person concerned has not previously been liable to a surcharge in respect of a prescribed accounting period ending within the surcharge period specified in or extended by that notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(10) In any case where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) the conduct by virtue of which a person is in default in respect of a prescribed accounting period is also conduct falling within section 69(1), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) by reason of that conduct, the person concerned is assessed to a penalty under section 69, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

the default shall be left out of account for the purposes of subsections (2) to (5) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(11) If the Commissioners, after consultation with the Treasury, so direct, a default in respect of a prescribed accounting period specified in the direction shall be left out of account for the purposes of subsections (2) to (5) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(12) For the purposes of this section the Commissioners shall be taken not to receive a payment by the day on which it becomes due unless it is made in such a manner as secures (in a case where the payment is made otherwise than in cash) that, by the last day for the payment of that amount, all the transactions can be completed that need to be completed before the whole amount of the payment becomes available to the Commissioners. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(13) In determining for the purposes of this section whether any person would, but for section 59(1A), be in default in respect of any period for the purposes of section 59, subsection (12) above shall be deemed to apply for the purposes of section 59 as it applies for the purposes of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(14) For the purposes of this section references to a thing’s being done by any day include references to its being done on that day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
