# Value Added Tax Act 1994, section 59

## 59. The default surcharge.[^c-7ea7518aa31059ff19e4a1c72480e865][^c-4fe7d8b857b0a9908591b738f05c032d][^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to subsction (1A) below[^c15291971] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] if, by the last day on which a taxable person is required in accordance with regulations under this Act to furnish a return for a prescribed accounting period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) the Commissioners have not received that return, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) the Commissioners have received that return but have not received the amount of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] shown on the return as payable by him in respect of that period, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

then that person shall be regarded for the purposes of this section as being in default in respect of that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(1A)[^c15291981] A person shall not be regarded for the purposes of this section as being in default in respect of any prescribed accounting period if that period is one in respect of which he is required by virtue of any order under section 28 to make any payment on account of VAT. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(2) Subject to subsections (9) and (10) below, subsection (4) below applies in any case where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) a taxable person is in default in respect of a prescribed accounting period; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) the Commissioners serve notice on the taxable person (a “surcharge liability notice”) specifying as a surcharge period for the purposes of this section a period ending on the first anniversary of the last day of the period referred to in paragraph (a) above and beginning, subject to subsection (3) below, on the date of the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(3) If a surcharge liability notice is served by reason of a default in respect of a prescribed accounting period and that period ends at or before the expiry of an existing surcharge period already notified to the taxable person concerned, the surcharge period specified in that notice shall be expressed as a continuation of the existing surcharge period and, accordingly, for the purposes of this section, that existing period and its extension shall be regarded as a single surcharge period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(4) Subject to subsections (7) to (10) below, if a taxable person on whom a surcharge liability notice has been served— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) is in default in respect of a prescribed accounting period ending within the surcharge period specified in (or extended by) that notice, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) has outstanding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] for that prescribed accounting period, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

he shall be liable to a surcharge equal to whichever is the greater of the following, namely, the specified percentage of his outstanding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] for that prescribed accounting period and £30. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(5) Subject to subsections (7) to (10) below, the specified percentage referred to in subsection (4) above shall be determined in relation to a prescribed accounting period by reference to the number of such periods in respect of which the taxable person is in default during the surcharge period and for which he has outstanding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca], so that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) in relation to the first such prescribed accounting period, the specified percentage is 2 per cent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) in relation to the second such period, the specified percentage is 5 per cent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (c) in relation to the third such period, the specified percentage is 10 per cent; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (d) in relation to each such period after the third, the specified percentage is 15 per cent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(6) For the purposes of subsections (4) and (5) above a person has outstanding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] for a prescribed accounting period if some or all of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] for which he is liable in respect of that period has not been paid by the last day on which he is required (as mentioned in subsection (1) above) to make a return for that period; and the reference in subsection (4) above to a person’s outstanding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] for a prescribed accounting period is to so much of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] for which he is so liable as has not been paid by that day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(7) If a person who, apart from this subsection, would be liable to a surcharge under subsection (4) above satisfies the Commissioners or, on appeal, a tribunal that, in the case of a default which is material to the surcharge— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) the return or, as the case may be, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] shown on the return was despatched at such a time and in such a manner that it was reasonable to expect that it would be received by the Commissioners within the appropriate time limit, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) there is a reasonable excuse for the return or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] not having been so despatched, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

he shall not be liable to the surcharge and for the purposes of the preceding provisions of this section he shall be treated as not having been in default in respect of the prescribed accounting period in question (and, accordingly, any surcharge liability notice the service of which depended upon that default shall be deemed not to have been served). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(8) For the purposes of subsection (7) above, a default is material to a surcharge if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) it is the default which, by virtue of subsection (4) above, gives rise to the surcharge; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) it is a default which was taken into account in the service of the surcharge liability notice upon which the surcharge depends and the person concerned has not previously been liable to a surcharge in respect of a prescribed accounting period ending within the surcharge period specified in or extended by that notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(9) In any case where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) the conduct by virtue of which a person is in default in respect of a prescribed accounting period is also conduct falling within section 69(1), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) by reason of that conduct, the person concerned is assessed to a penalty under that section, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

the default shall be left out of account for the purposes of subsections (2) to (5) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(10) If the Commissioners, after consultation with the Treasury, so direct, a default in respect of a prescribed accounting period specified in the direction shall be left out of account for the purposes of subsections (2) to (5) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(11)[^c15291991] For the purposes of this section references to a thing’s being done by any day include references to its being done on that day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
