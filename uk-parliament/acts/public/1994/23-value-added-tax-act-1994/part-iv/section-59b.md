# Value Added Tax Act 1994, section 59B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/23
>

## 59B. Relationship between sections 59 and 59A.[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca][^c15292011]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies in each of the following cases, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) where a section 28 accounting period ends within a surcharge period begun or extended by the service on a taxable person (whether before or after the coming into force of section 59A) of a surcharge liability notice under section 59; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) where a prescribed accounting period which is not a section 28 accounting period ends within a surcharge period begun or extended by the service on a taxable person of a surcharge liability notice under section 59A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(2) In a case falling within subsection (1)(a) above section 59A shall have effect as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) subject to paragraph (b) below, the section 28 accounting period were deemed to be a period ending within a surcharge period begun or, as the case may be, extended by a notice served under section 59A; but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) any question— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
  - (i) whether a surcharge period was begun or extended by the notice, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] whether the taxable person was in default in respect of any prescribed accounting period which was not a section 28 accounting period but ended within the surcharge period begun or extended by that notice, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

  were to be determined as it would be determined for the purposes of section 59. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(3) In a case falling within subsection (1)(b) above section 59 shall have effect as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

- (a) subject to paragraph (b) below, the prescribed accounting period that is not a section 28 accounting period were deemed to be a period ending within a surcharge period begun or, as the case may be, extended by a notice served under section 59; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (b) any question— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
  - (i) whether a surcharge period was begun or extended by the notice, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] whether the taxable person was in default in respect of any prescribed accounting period which was a section 28 accounting period but ended within the surcharge period begun or extended by that notice, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

  were to be determined as it would be determined for the purposes of section 59A; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
- (c) that person were to be treated as having had outstanding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] VAT *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] for a section 28 accounting period in any case where the aggregate value of his defaults in respect of that period was, for the purposes of section 59A, more than nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]

(4) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca] **a section 28 accounting period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]** ”, in relation to a taxable person, means any prescribed accounting period ending on or after the day on which the Finance Act 1996 was passed in respect of which that person is liable by virtue of an order under section 28 to make any payment on account of VAT. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-61084746b73de20b61a895f048fed1ca]
