# Finance Act 1994, section 199

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/9

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 199. Interpretation and commencement of Chapter III.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Chapter “**the Management Act**” means the [^c14456161]Taxes Management Act 1970.

(2) Unless the contrary intention appears, this Chapter—

- (a) so far as it relates to income tax and capital gains tax, has effect as respects the year 1996-97 and subsequent years of assessment, and
- (b) so far as it relates to corporation tax, has effect as respects accounting periods ending on or after the appointed day.

(3)[^c14456171] For the purposes of this Chapter the appointed day is such day, not earlier than 1st April 1996, as the Treasury may by order appoint.
