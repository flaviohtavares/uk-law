# Finance Act 1994, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Charge to tax.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Tax shall be charged on the receipt of a premium by an insurer if the premium is received—

- (a) under a taxable insurance contract, and
- (b) on or after 1st October 1994.
