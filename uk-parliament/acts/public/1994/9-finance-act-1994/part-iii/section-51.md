# Finance Act 1994, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Rate of tax.[^c14451911]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Tax shall be charged—

- (a) at the higher rate, in the case of a premium which is liable to tax at that rate; and
- (b) at the standard rate, in any other case.

(2) For the purposes of this Part—

- (a) the higher rate is 20 per cent[^c-1730c739148465a5dd50acfc954545de] .; and
- (b) the standard rate is 12 per cent[^c-08be5d406988e07734eb38400625fecd] .
