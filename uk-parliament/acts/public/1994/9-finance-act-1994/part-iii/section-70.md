## 70. Interpretation: taxable insurance contracts.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to subsection (1A) below[^c14453641], any contract of insurance is a taxable insurance contract.

(1A)[^c14453651] A contract is not a taxable insurance contract if it falls within one or more of the paragraphs of Part I of Schedule 7A to this Act.
