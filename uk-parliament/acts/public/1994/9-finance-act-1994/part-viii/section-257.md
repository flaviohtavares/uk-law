## 257. Interpretation and construction.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act “**the Taxes Act 1988**” means the [^c14457481]Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988.

(2) Part V of this Act shall be construed as one with Part I of the [^c14457491]Oil Taxation Act 1975, and in Part V that Act is referred to as “**the principal Act**”.

(3) Part VI of this Act shall be construed as one with the [^c14457501]Stamp Act 1891.
