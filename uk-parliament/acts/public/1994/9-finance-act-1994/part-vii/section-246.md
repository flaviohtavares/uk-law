## 246. Rate bands: no indexation in 1994.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Table substituted by section 72(1) of the [^c14457271]Finance (No. 2) Act 1992 shall apply to chargeable transfers made in the year beginning 6th April 1994, and accordingly section 8(1) of the [^c14457281]Inheritance Tax Act 1984 (indexation of rate bands) shall not apply to such transfers.
