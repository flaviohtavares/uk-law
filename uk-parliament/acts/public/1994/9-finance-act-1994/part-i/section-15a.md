## 15A. Offer of review of relevant decision[^key-43efa83d076042441798d9a93f44b0b6][^key-782ae91f6be1115695df64eed99980ac][^key-5e6e569fe8e24811b9b1033bd9373748][^key-ebe56aa5e2ed527d67c26fa792af3837][^key-caa4baeba220e7921444de4cd8081528][^key-15b377be6cc45d51a887d4e5975ed1b4][^key-d746b298ebc1cf9e04f07e09541bfea1][^key-ddc2c7dd326ff77c0c4ed446936ac18f][^key-4544c6607be234431118abfdd276bf7d][^key-3b44655183932dcf257b79da90fe9313][^key-897928b7f6afdc354d90d7661a6fcbe9][^key-380fa8c295ae426de46a7d4146f99722][^key-85c2075e8365797dcb3c3c7fa1e478d6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If HMRC notify a person (P) of a relevant decision by HMRC, HMRC must at the same time, by notice to P, offer P a review of the decision.

(2) This section does not apply to the notification of the conclusions of a review.
