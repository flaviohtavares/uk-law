# Finance Act 1994, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Hydrocarbon oil.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 6(1) of the [^c14448861]Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act 1979 for “£0.3058” (duty on light oil) and “£0.2514” (duty on heavy oil) there shall be substituted “ £0.3314 ” and “ £0.2770 ” respectively.

(2) In section 11(1) of that Act (rebate on heavy oil) for “£0.0105” (fuel oil) and “£0.0149” (gas oil) there shall be substituted “ £0.0116 ” and “ £0.0164 ” respectively.

(3) In section 14(1) of that Act (rebate on light oil for use as furnace fuel) for “£0.0105” there shall be substituted “ £0.0116 ”.

(4) This section shall be deemed to have come into force at 6 o’clock in the evening of 30th November 1993.
