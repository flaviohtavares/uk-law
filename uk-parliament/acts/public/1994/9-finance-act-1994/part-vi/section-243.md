# Finance Act 1994, section 243

## 243. Agreements to surrender leases.[^c-af4cb430545da2f962928d4c4f0b76a5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where, in pursuance of any agreement, any lease is surrendered (or, in Scotland, renounced) at any time otherwise than by deed, the agreement shall be treated for the purposes of stamp duty[^c14457211] as if it were a deed executed at that time effecting the surrender (or, as the case may be, renunciation).

(2) This section shall apply to any agreement made after 7th December 1993.
