# Drug Trafficking Act 1994, section 69

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2016-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Short title and commencement.[^c8345431]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Drug Trafficking Act 1994..

(2) This Act comes into force at the end of the period of three months beginning with the day on which it is passed.
