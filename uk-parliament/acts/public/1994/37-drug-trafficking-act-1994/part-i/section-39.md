# Drug Trafficking Act 1994, section 39

## 39. Enforcement of external confiscation orders.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff] Her Majesty may by Order in Council— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]

- (a) direct in relation to a country or territory outside the United Kingdom designated by the Order (a “designated country”) that, subject to such modifications as may be specified, the relevant provisions of this Act shall apply to external confiscation orders and to proceedings which have been or are to be instituted in the designated country and may result in an external confiscation order being made there; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]
- (b) make— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]
  - (i) such provision in connection with the taking of action in the designated country with a view to satisfying a confiscation order, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff] such provision as to evidence or proof of any matter for the purposes of this section and section 40 of this Act, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff] such incidental, consequential and transitional provision, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]

  as appears to Her Majesty to be expedient; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]
- (c) (without prejudice to the generality of this subsection) direct that, in such circumstances as may be specified, proceeds which arise out of action taken in the designated country with a view to satisfying a confiscation order shall be treated as reducing the amount payable under the order to such extent as may be specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]

(2) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]**external confiscation order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]**” means an order made by a court in a designated country for the purpose of recovering, or recovering the value of, payments or other rewards received in connection with drug trafficking. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]

(3) An Order in Council under this section may make different provision for different cases or classes of case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]

(4) The power to make an Order in Council under this section includes power to modify the relevant provisions of this Act in such a way as to confer power on a person to exercise a discretion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]

(5) An Order in Council under this section shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]

(6) For the purposes of this section, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]**the relevant provisions of this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]**” are this Part, except sections 10 and 16, and Part IV. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9b3558415cca54423fab43a6a8d478ff]
