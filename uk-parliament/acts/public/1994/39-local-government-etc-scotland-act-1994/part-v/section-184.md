# Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994, section 184

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/39

> Extent: Scotland.

## 184. Short title, commencement and extent[^c12268901]

*Extent: S*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994

(2)[^c12268911][^c12268921] This Act, except section 163, shall come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order made by statutory instrument appoint, and different days may be appointed for different purposes.

(3)[^c12268921] An order under subsection (2) above may contain such transitional provisions and savings as appear to the Secretary of State to be necessary or expedient in connection with the provisions brought into force.

(4) This Act shall extend to Scotland only.
