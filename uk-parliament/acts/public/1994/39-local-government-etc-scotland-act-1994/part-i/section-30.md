# Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/39

## 30. Rating authorities.[^c12265891]

*Extent: S*

The rating authority for any local government area shall be the local authority for that area and, in this Act and in any other enactment (whether passed or made before or after the passing of this Act), the expression “**rating authority**” shall be construed in accordance with this section.
