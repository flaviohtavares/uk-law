> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/17

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Supply of video recordings for use in training to be exempted supply.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In subsection (11) of section 3 of the [^c8619541]Video Recordings Act 1984 (exempted supplies), for “or the [^c8619551]Medical Act 1983” substitute “ the Medical Act 1983, the [^c8619561] Osteopaths Act 1993 or the Chiropractors Act 1994 ”.
