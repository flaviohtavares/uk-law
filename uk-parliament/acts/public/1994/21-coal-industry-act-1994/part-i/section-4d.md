# Coal Industry Act 1994, section 4D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4D (Extent: S). Power of the Authority with respect to coal mine water discharge: Scotland (Extent: S)[^c-344206386d4e1845bacbe970395babd2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S)) The Authority may take such action as it considers appropriate (if any) for the purpose of preventing, or mitigating the effect of, the discharge of water from a coal mine into or on to any land or into the water environment. *(Extent: S)*

(2 (Extent: S)) In this section and sections 4E *(Extent: S)* , 4F and 4G[^c-da32a143bea93f1d3c3ef4b42fe09c9d] *(Extent: S)* below— *(Extent: S)*

- (a (Extent: S)) “ *(Extent: S)***the water environment *(Extent: S)***” has the meaning given by section 3 of the Water Environment and Water Services (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 3); and *(Extent: S)*
- (b (Extent: S)) references to coal mines are to coal mines vested in the Authority. *(Extent: S)*
