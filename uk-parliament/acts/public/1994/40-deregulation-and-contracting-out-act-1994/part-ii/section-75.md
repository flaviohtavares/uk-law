# Deregulation and Contracting Out Act 1994, section 75

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Restrictions on disclosure of information.[^key-71eae83bc398286b46aeb259fc78fee0][^key-410811c48dfd6cb74ed0da0a4a3770c4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 15 to this Act (which contains provisions modifying certain restrictions on the disclosure of information where functions of Ministers, office-holders or local authorities are contracted out) shall have effect.
