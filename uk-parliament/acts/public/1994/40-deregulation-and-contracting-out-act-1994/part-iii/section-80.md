# Deregulation and Contracting Out Act 1994, section 80

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Financial provisions.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any sums required by a Minister of the Crown, an office-holder or the Forestry Commissioners[^c1365831] for making payments under contracts entered into under or by virtue of Part II of this Act;
- (b) any administrative expenses incurred by a Minister of the Crown or office-holder in consequence of the provisions of this Act; and
- (c) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums so payable under any other Act.

(2) In this section—

- “**Minister of the Crown**” has the same meaning as in [^c1365832] the Ministers of the Crown Act 1975;
- “**office-holder**” has the same meaning as in Part II of this Act.
