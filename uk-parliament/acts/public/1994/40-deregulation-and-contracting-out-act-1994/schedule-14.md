# Deregulation and Contracting Out Act 1994, SCHEDULE 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1994/40

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 14 — PSV Operator Licensing etc: Minor and Consequential Amendments

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 68.

**1.**[^c1365946] The 1981 Act shall be amended as follows.

**2.** In section 14(4) for “the provisions of sections 15 and 16” there shall be substituted “ section 16 ”.

**3.** Section 14A(3) shall be omitted.

**4.** In section 16(6) the word “or” immediately before paragraph (b) shall be omitted.

**5.**[^c1365947] (1) Section 17(2)(c) shall be omitted.

(2) In section 17(3)—

- (a) in paragraph (a) the words “intention or” shall be omitted, and
- (b) in paragraph (c) for “section 9 of this Act” there shall be substituted “ section 69 of [^c1365948] the Road Traffic Act 1988 ” and for “subsection (9) of that section” there shall be substituted “ section 71(1)(a) or (b) of that Act arising out of the contravention of such a prohibition ”.

**6.** In section 18(3)(d)—

- (a) after “discs” there shall be inserted “ on their expiry or otherwise ceasing to have effect, ”, and
- (b) for “expiration” there shall be substituted “ on termination ”

**7.** (1) Section 50(2) shall be omitted.

(2) In section 50(4)—

- (a) at the end of paragraph (a) there shall be added “ or any undertaking recorded in it ”, and
- (b) in paragraph (c) the words “or to curtail its period of validity” shall be omitted.

**8.** In section 12 of [^c1365949] the Transport Act 1985 subsection (3) shall be omitted.
