## 5. Powers to improve enforcement procedures.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] If, with respect to any provision made by an enactment, a Minister of the Crown is of the opinion— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] that the effect of the provision is such as to impose, or authorise or require the imposition of, a restriction, requirement or condition affecting any person in the carrying on of any trade, business or profession or otherwise, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] that, by exercising any one or more of the powers conferred by Schedule 1 to this Act, it would be possible, without jeopardising any necessary protection, to improve (so far as fairness, transparency and consistency are concerned) the procedures for enforcing the restriction, requirement or condition, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

he may, subject to the following provisions of this section, by order exercise the power or powers accordingly. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] No order shall be made under this section in any case where the sole or main effect which the restriction, requirement or condition may be expected to have on each person on whom it is imposed is an effect on him in his personal capacity, and not as a person carrying on a trade, business or profession. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] Where the relevant enactment— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] contains a power for the Minister to make regulations or orders; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] provides for that power to be exercisable so as to give effect, with or without modifications, to proposals submitted by some other person, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

the Minister shall consult with that person before he makes an order under this section. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] An order under this section shall be made by statutory instrument and may do all or any of the following— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] make provision as to the consequences of any failure to comply with a provision made by the order; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] contain provisions (including provisions modifying enactments relating to the periods within which proceedings must be brought) which are consequential upon, or supplemental or incidental to, the provisions made by the order; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] contain such transitional provisions and savings as appear to the Minister to be appropriate; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- (d (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] make different provision for different cases or different areas; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

and a statutory instrument containing an order under this section shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

(5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] Nothing in any order made under this section shall— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] preclude an enforcement officer from taking immediate enforcement action against any person, or from requiring any person to take immediate remedial action, in any case where it appears to the officer to be necessary to take such action or impose such a requirement; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] require such an officer to disclose any information the disclosure of which would be contrary to the public interest. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

(6 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] In this section and Schedule 1 to this Act— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

- “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] **enactment *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]** ” means an enactment within the meaning of section 1 above, and any subordinate legislation made under such an enactment; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] **enforcement action *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]** ”— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
  - (a) in relation to any restriction, requirement or condition, means any action taken with a view to or in connection with imposing any sanction (whether criminal or otherwise) for failure to observe or comply with it; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
  - (b) in relation to a restriction, requirement or condition relating to the grant or renewal of licences, includes any refusal to grant, renew or vary a licence, the imposition of any condition on the grant or renewal of a licence and any variation or revocation of a licence; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] **enforcement officer *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]** ” does not include— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
  - (a) the Director of Public Prosecutions; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
  - (b) the Lord Advocate or a procurator fiscal; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
  - (c) the Director of Public Prosecutions for Northern Ireland, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

  but, subject to that, means any person who is authorised, whether by or under the relevant enactment or otherwise, to take enforcement action; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] **licence *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]** ” includes any authorisation (by whatever name called) to do anything which would otherwise be unlawful; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] **Minister of the Crown *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]** ” and “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] **Minister *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]** ” have the same meanings as in section 1 above; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] **the relevant enactment *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]** ” means the enactment containing the provision by which the restriction, requirement or condition is imposed or, as the case may be, is authorised or required to be imposed; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
