## 1. Power to remove or reduce certain statutory burdens on businesses, individuals etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] If, with respect to any provision made by an enactment, a Minister of the Crown is of the opinion— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] that the effect of the provision is such as to impose, or authorise or require the imposition of, a burden affecting any person in the carrying on of any trade, business or profession or otherwise, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] that, by amending or repealing the enactment concerned and, where appropriate, by making such other provision as is referred to in subsection (4)(a) below, it would be possible, without removing any necessary protection, to remove or reduce the burden or, as the case may be, the authorisation or requirement by virtue of which the burden may be imposed, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

he may, subject to the following provisions of this section and sections 2 to 4 below, by order amend or repeal that enactment. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] The reference in subsection (1)(b) above to reducing the authorisation or requirement by virtue of which a burden may be imposed includes a reference to shortening any period of time within which the burden may be so imposed. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] In this section and sections 2 to 4 below, in relation to an order under this section,— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] **the relevant enactment *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]** ” means the enactment containing the existing provision. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] An order under this section shall be made by statutory instrument and may do all or any of the following— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] make provision (whether by amending any enactment or otherwise) creating a burden which relates to the subject matter of, but is less onerous than that imposed by, the existing provision; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] make such modifications of enactments as, in the opinion of the Minister concerned, are consequential upon, or incidental to, the amendment or repeal of the relevant enactment; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] contain such transitional provisions and savings as appear to the Minister to be appropriate; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- (d (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] make different provision for different cases or different areas; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

but no order shall be made under this section unless a draft of the order has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

  - (i (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] any sanction (whether criminal or otherwise) for failure to observe the restriction or to comply with the requirement or condition; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
  - (ii (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] any procedural provisions (including provisions for appeal) relevant to that sanction; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] **enactment *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]** ”, subject to subsection (6) below, means an enactment contained in this Act or in any other Act passed before or in the same Session as this Act, or any provision of an order under this section. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
