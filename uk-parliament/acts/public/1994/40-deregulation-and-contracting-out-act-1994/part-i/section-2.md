## 2. Limitations on the power under section 1.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] If an order under section 1 above creates a new criminal offence, then, subject to subsections (2) and (3) below, that offence shall not be punishable— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] on indictment with imprisonment for a term of more than two years; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] on summary conviction with imprisonment for a term exceeding six months or a fine exceeding level 5 on the standard scale or both. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] In the case of an offence which, if committed by an adult, is triable either on indictment or summarily and is not an offence triable on indictment only by virtue of— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] Part V of *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] [^c1365632] the Criminal Justice Act 1988, or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c1365633][^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] section 292(6) and (7) of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

the reference in subsection (1)(b) above to level 5 on the standard scale shall be construed as a reference to the statutory maximum. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] If an order under section 1 above abolishes an offence contained in the relevant enactment and the maximum penalties for that offence are greater than those specified in subsection (1) above, the order may create a new criminal offence having maximum penalties not exceeding those applicable to the offence which is abolished. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] An order under section 1 above shall not contain any provision— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] providing for any forcible entry, search or seizure, or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06] compelling the giving of evidence, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]

unless, and then only to the extent that, a provision to that effect is contained in the relevant enactment and is abolished by the order. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e85125e13abdb3784a073bc05e52c06]
