Circuit Courts (Scotland) Act 1828

1828 c.29

An Act to authorize additional Circuit Courts of Justiciary to be held, and to facilitate Criminal Trials in Scotland.

## 26. Provisions of 43 Geo. 3. c. 141. extended to all inferior judges.[^c555095]

The provisions of the [^c555096]Justices Protection Act 1803 shall extend to all inferior judges and magistrates in Scotland, in regard to any sentence pronounced or proceeding had in any criminal trial.
