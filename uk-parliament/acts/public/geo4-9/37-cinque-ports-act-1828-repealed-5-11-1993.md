# Cinque Ports Act 1828 ( repealed 5.11.1993)

> Text as at 1993-11-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Cinque Ports Act 1828 [^c555102]( repealed 5.11.1993)

1828 c.37

**No longer in force (ended 1993-11-05).**

## 1.] The deputy warden may appoint Salvage Commissioners, &c.

It shall and may be lawful for the deputy warden of the cinque ports and lieutenant of Dover Castle for the time being, and he is hereby authorized and empowered, to nominate and appoint such commissioners, and to have the same power, and to do all other acts, matters, and things contained in the said recited Act, as the lord warden of the cinque ports for the time being can or may do.
