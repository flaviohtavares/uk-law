# Night Poaching Act 1828

> Text as at 2022-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2022-08-01).**

Night Poaching Act 1828 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc]

1828 c.69

An Act for the more effectual Prevention of Persons going armed by Night for the Destruction of Game. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc]

\[19th July 1828\] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc]

[^c5551151][^c5551161][^c5551171] [^c5551181]... And whereas the Practice of going out by night for the purpose of destroying Game has *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc] [^c5551181]... very much increased of late years, and has in very many instances led to the commission of Murder, and of other grievous offences; and it is expedient *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc] [^c5551181]... to make more effectual Provisions than now by law exist for repressing such practice: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc]

## 1. Persons taking or destroying game by (affected by repeal, see note) Night (affected by repeal, see note) to be committed, for the 1st Offence, for 3 Months, and kept to hard labour, and to find (affected by repeal, see note) Sureties; (affected by repeal, see note) 2d Offence, 6 months, and kept to hard labour, and to find sureties; 3d Offence, to be liable to transportation. (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc][^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc][^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc][^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc][^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-d8a73e4fb94530e8a64f6753b47cc3de] [^c5551281]... If any person shall, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc] [^c5551281]... by night, unlawfully take or destroy any game or rabbits in any land, whether open or enclosed, or shall by night unlawfully enter or be in any land, whether open or enclosed, with any gun, net, engine, or other instrument, for the purpose of taking or destroying game, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc] he shall be liable on summary conviction[^M_F_bfff4fcf-b507-4df0-f7c0-b9667840d109] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc] to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 51 weeks,[^key-356ee2c3a4910659e21abb22c8bca33d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc] to a fine *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc] or to both[^key-da0c9f4e25e94e9620fde5e89dd43b56] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc].

(2)[^key-91dfb70d95acccaaf080939466a23651] In relation to an offence committed before the coming into force of section 281(5) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (alteration of penalties for certain summary offences: England and Wales), the reference in subsection (1) to 51 weeks is to be read as a reference to 6 months. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc]

## 2. Owners or occupiers of land, lords of manors, or their servants, may apprehend offenders. Offenders assaulting or offering violence deemed guilty of misdemeanor, and be liable to be transported for seven years, or imprisoned for two years. (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc]

[^M_F_9340e429-c305-4e27-e2bb-0fe94d9a22fb]... Where any person shall be found upon any land committing any such offence as is herein-before mentioned, it shall be lawful for the owner or occupier of such land *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc] [^c5551301]... , or for the lord of the manor or reputed manor wherein such land may be situate, and also for any gamekeeper or servant of any of the persons herein mentioned, or any person assisting such gamekeeper or servant, to seize and apprehend such offender upon such land, or in case of pursuit being made, in any other place to which he may have escaped therefrom, and to deliver him, as soon as may be, into the custody of a peace officer, in order to his being conveyed before two justices of the peace; and in case such offender shall assault or offer any violence with any gun, crossbow, fire arms, bludgeon, stick, club, or any other offensive weapon whatsoever, towards any person hereby authorized to seize and apprehend him, he shall, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc] be liable on summary conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding[^M_F_64e87937-f457-44fe-bbe3-aa188d2b4066] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc] level 4 on the standard scale[^M_F_99e3bb90-1653-47c0-9179-1eca41b95284] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc], or to both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc]

## 12. What time shall be considered night. (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc]

Provided always, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc] [^M_F_65766810-7e38-4f08-cb92-f2624d0cfb74]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc] that for the purposes of this Act the night shall be considered and is hereby declared to commence at the expiration of the first hour after sunset, and to conclude at the beginning of the last hour before sunrise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_104514d3-37c1-4973-f097-e44d5b35bdfc]
