> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2016/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 244. Oversight by Information Commission in relation to Part 4[^key-913ab08a078ee04aba76c349f2008eb0][^key-3c73a59ed4d99136d89c9bcf5bd447ea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Information Commission must audit compliance with requirements or restrictions imposed by virtue of Part 4 in relation to the integrity, security or destruction of data retained by virtue of that Part.
