# Investigatory Powers Act 2016, section 141

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2016/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 141. Decisions to issue warrants to be taken personally by Secretary of State[^key-944eb613312ea868a42349ed767e8ed3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The decision to issue a bulk interception warrant must be taken personally by the Secretary of State.

(2) Before a bulk interception warrant is issued, it must be signed by the Secretary of State.
