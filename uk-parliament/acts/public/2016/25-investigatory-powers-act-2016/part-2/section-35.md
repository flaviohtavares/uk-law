# Investigatory Powers Act 2016, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2016/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Persons who may make modifications

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-b554f2d736f05fdba3f8e0a82fa0a361] A major modification may be made by—

- (a) the Secretary of State, in the case of a warrant issued by the Secretary of State,
- (b) a member of the Scottish Government, in the case of a warrant issued by the Scottish Ministers, or
- (c) a senior official acting on behalf of the Secretary of State or (as the case may be) the Scottish Ministers.

(2)[^key-b554f2d736f05fdba3f8e0a82fa0a361] A minor modification may be made by—

- (a) the Secretary of State, in the case of a warrant issued by the Secretary of State,
- (b) a member of the Scottish Government, in the case of a warrant issued by the Scottish Ministers,
- (c) a senior official acting on behalf of the Secretary of State or (as the case may be) the Scottish Ministers,
- (d) the person to whom the warrant is addressed, or
- (e) a person who holds a senior position in the same public authority as the person mentioned in paragraph (d).

(3)[^key-b554f2d736f05fdba3f8e0a82fa0a361] But if a person within subsection (2)(d) or (e) considers that there is an urgent need to make a major modification, that person (as well as a person within subsection (1)) may do so.

Section 38 contains provision about the approval of major modifications made in urgent cases.

(4)[^key-b554f2d736f05fdba3f8e0a82fa0a361] Subsections (1) and (3) are subject to section 36(5) and (6) (special rules where any of sections 26 to 29 applies in relation to the making of a major modification).

(5)[^key-b554f2d736f05fdba3f8e0a82fa0a361] Subsections (2)(d) and (e) and (3) do not apply in the case of a mutual assistance warrant addressed to a person falling within section 18(1)(h) (competent authorities of overseas countries or territories).

(6) For the purposes of subsection (2)(e) a person holds a senior position in a public authority if—

- (a)[^key-b554f2d736f05fdba3f8e0a82fa0a361] in the case of any of the intelligence services—
  - (i) the person is a member of the Senior Civil Service or a member of the Senior Management Structure of Her Majesty's Diplomatic Service, or
  - (ii) the person holds a position in the intelligence service of equivalent seniority to such a person;
- (b)[^key-d5bbf35d8cbf2d99c18c3638fc7b1a29] in the case of the National Crime Agency, the person is a National Crime Agency officer of grade 2 or above;
- (c)[^key-d5bbf35d8cbf2d99c18c3638fc7b1a29] in the case of the metropolitan police force, the Police Service of Northern Ireland or the Police Service of Scotland, a person is of or above the rank of superintendent;
- (d)[^key-d5bbf35d8cbf2d99c18c3638fc7b1a29] in the case of Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs, the person is a member of the Senior Civil Service;
- (e)[^key-b554f2d736f05fdba3f8e0a82fa0a361] in the case of the Ministry of Defence—
  - (i) the person is a member of the Senior Civil Service, or
  - (ii) the person is of or above the rank of brigadier, commodore or air commodore.

(7)[^key-b554f2d736f05fdba3f8e0a82fa0a361] In this section “**senior official**” means—

- (a) in the case of a warrant issued by the Secretary of State, a member of the Senior Civil Service or a member of the Senior Management Structure of Her Majesty's Diplomatic Service;
- (b) in the case of a warrant issued by the Scottish Ministers, a member of the staff of the Scottish Administration who is a member of the Senior Civil Service.
