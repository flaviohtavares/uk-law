# Investigatory Powers Act 2016, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2016/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Persons who may apply for issue of a warrant

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Each of the following is an “**intercepting authority**” for the purposes of this Part—

- (a)[^key-10866912af24be23dfcb47d227a6087a] a person who is the head of an intelligence service;
- (b)[^key-2ed36bbd3dcf21787bf8e4f99659ecbe] the Director General of the National Crime Agency;
- (c)[^key-2ed36bbd3dcf21787bf8e4f99659ecbe] the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis;
- (d)[^key-2ed36bbd3dcf21787bf8e4f99659ecbe] the Chief Constable of the Police Service of Northern Ireland;
- (e)[^key-2ed36bbd3dcf21787bf8e4f99659ecbe] the chief constable of the Police Service of Scotland;
- (f)[^key-2ed36bbd3dcf21787bf8e4f99659ecbe] the Commissioners for Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs;
- (g)[^key-10866912af24be23dfcb47d227a6087a] the Chief of Defence Intelligence;
- (h)[^key-2ed36bbd3dcf21787bf8e4f99659ecbe] a person who is the competent authority of a country or territory outside the United Kingdom for the purposes of [^key-b7f7619bc8b39032e9451ed24cb4ae48]... an international mutual assistance agreement.

(2)[^key-10866912af24be23dfcb47d227a6087a] For the meaning of “head of an intelligence service”, see section 263.

(3)[^key-10866912af24be23dfcb47d227a6087a] An application for the issue of a warrant under this Chapter may only be made on behalf of an intercepting authority by a person holding office under the Crown.
