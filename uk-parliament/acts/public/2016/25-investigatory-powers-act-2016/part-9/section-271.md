## 271. Minor and consequential provision[^c22712471]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-be0758857f97c77f10d64b3d2a9747d2][^c-3eea7e3ba1d706092931827bf1d72639][^c-0964e9818bc95d1d3524fd0bd550b933][^c-3dafa251e96696367762cb04f052237f][^c-a82dc8195be8c3c57f7c56ffd6145b0e][^c-67f276a37f2a03c78a71226b50f4115a][^c-14eb874ce804205822dc5a500b82ac5a][^c-d779ff812065a292673aab49c92fa054][^c-4076a9c58ebb5101046aff6ad6970df7][^c-4a31acf93b4ad865d10d325bc39d112d][^c-2e2a7643d26663efd4bd2c0574ca7080][^c-40d0cf3ecb1a12816761066fc9868ec6] Schedule 10 (which contains minor and consequential provision) has effect.

(2) The Secretary of State may by regulations make such provision as the Secretary of State considers appropriate in consequence of this Act.

(3) The power to make regulations under subsection (2) may, in particular, be exercised by modifying any provision made by or under an enactment.

(4) In subsection (3) “**enactment**” does not include any primary legislation passed or made after the end of the Session in which this Act is passed.
