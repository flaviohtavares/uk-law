## 270. Transitional, transitory or saving provision[^c22712461]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-f9cc5e6f032e8149a2e8dfdeff680e41][^c-f31db6f94fe6d23af835d0f8a7b17666][^c-7dba8c6a4a50952883b093c1d0641fb7][^c-119e6997d2a926b7152de2d4aa3793fd] Schedule 9 (which contains transitional, transitory and saving provision including a general saving for lawful conduct) has effect.

(2) The Secretary of State may by regulations make such transitional, transitory or saving provision as the Secretary of State considers appropriate in connection with the coming into force of any provision of this Act.
