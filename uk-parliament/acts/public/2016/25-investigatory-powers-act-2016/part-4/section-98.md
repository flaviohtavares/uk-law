# Investigatory Powers Act 2016, section 98

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2016/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 98. Part 4: interpretation[^key-62472b9ad002158a6a1c18119cf8002a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part—

- “**notice**” means notice in writing,
- “**relevant communications data**” has the meaning given by section 87(11),
- “**retention notice**” has the meaning given by section 87(1).

(2) See also—

- section 261 (telecommunications definitions),
- section 262 (postal definitions),
- section 263 (general definitions),
- section 265 (index of defined expressions).
