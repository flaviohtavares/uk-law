# Investigatory Powers Act 2016, section 95

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2016/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Enforcement of notices and certain other requirements and restrictions[^key-5ffcb74b90c53878f5492bd8e442ae39]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It is the duty of a telecommunications operator on whom a requirement or restriction is imposed by—

- (a) a retention notice, or
- (b) section 92 or 93,

to comply with the requirement or restriction.

(2) A telecommunications operator, or any person employed or engaged for the purposes of the business of a telecommunications operator, must not disclose the existence or contents of a retention notice to any other person.

(3) Any member of the Information Commission, or any of its staff[^key-7cae323e2b00041d001a6fe59f8ca5b8], must not disclose the existence or contents of a retention notice to any other person.

(4) Subsections (2) and (3) do not apply to a disclosure made with the permission of the Secretary of State.

(5) The duty under subsection (1) or (2) , or under section 90(4A),[^key-70c3d7367eff85d6d743a0bd66595700] is enforceable (whether or not the person is in the United Kingdom)[^key-76f9ee3609ad235e7b8630520367daa5] by civil proceedings by the Secretary of State for an injunction, or for specific performance of a statutory duty under section 45 of the Court of Session Act 1988, or for any other appropriate relief.
