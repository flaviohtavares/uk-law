# Investigatory Powers Act 2016, section 199

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2016/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 199. Bulk personal datasets: interpretation[^key-5c1eb383e3de3e376d7c6cb4212213af][^key-0c621ecc9fc62b4dd5b23b5c0049abdf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Part and Part 7A[^key-0b34b48482749d67aa9da32d079512de], an intelligence service retains a bulk personal dataset if—

- (a) the intelligence service obtains a set of information that includes personal data relating to a number of individuals,
- (b) the nature of the set is such that the majority of the individuals are not, and are unlikely to become, of interest to the intelligence service in the exercise of its functions,
- (c) after any initial examination of the contents, the intelligence service retains the set for the purpose of the exercise of its functions, and
- (d) the set is held, or is to be held, electronically for analysis in the exercise of those functions.

(2)[^key-159d2af8505a68997bc83ab60d18a936] In this Part and Part 7A[^key-680bdaf932aeaa49b4222477a9858b07], “**personal data**” means—

- (a) personal data within the meaning of section 3(2) of the Data Protection Act 2018 which is subject to processing described in section 82(1) of that Act by an intelligence service[^key-417a8555d2b8c6dd156c0271341acbb6], and
- (b) data relating to a deceased individual where the data would fall within paragraph (a) if it related to a living individual.
