# Investigatory Powers Act 2016, section 200

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2016/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 200. Requirement for authorisation ...: general[^c-6e594034789e04a6dad0f35b40a5d834]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-0c1ef06f69ca016adc3f67788d20dcbe] An intelligence service may not exercise a power to retain a bulk personal dataset unless the retention of the dataset is authorised

- (a)[^c-26fcd331362eed72ed4aa0028d893281] by a warrant under this Part , or[^c-9cc6c762bc9152b8e668a1de501d8ddb]
- (b) by an individual authorisation under Part 7A (low or no reasonable expectation of privacy) (see section 226B).

(2)[^c-0c1ef06f69ca016adc3f67788d20dcbe] An intelligence service may not exercise a power to examine a bulk personal dataset retained by it unless the examination is authorised

- (a)[^c-3106141bf536ee3eed7cf9694bcd729c] by a warrant under this Part , or[^c-f0345b9e3b4a3c33ae2de8314034ca36]
- (b) by an individual authorisation under Part 7A.

(3)[^c-502a8c67355f33403b14e46953c11dba] For the purposes of this Part, there are two kinds of warrant—

- (a) a warrant, referred to in this Part as “**a class BPD warrant**”, authorising an intelligence service to retain, or to retain and examine, any bulk personal dataset of a class described in the warrant;
- (b) a warrant, referred to in this Part as “**a specific BPD warrant**”, authorising an intelligence service to retain, or to retain and examine, any bulk personal dataset described in the warrant.

(4)[^c-0c1ef06f69ca016adc3f67788d20dcbe] Section 201 sets out exceptions to the restrictions imposed by subsections (1) and (2) of this section.
