# Psychoactive Substances Act 2016, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2023-02-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — EXEMPTED SUBSTANCES

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 3

### Controlled drugs

**1.**[^c-098e7cf0bad4af4de53632407b93fec9] Controlled drugs (within the meaning of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971).

### Medicinal products

**2.**[^c-87d50c6c8ef2851a028906163f6846ff] Medicinal products.

In this paragraph “**medicinal product**” has the same meaning as in the Human Medicines Regulations 2012 (S.I. 2012/1916) (see regulation 2 of those Regulations).

### Alcohol

**3.**[^c-06c0341987efd8b854cba87502d19d4c] Alcohol or alcoholic products.

In this paragraph—

- “**alcohol**” means ethyl alcohol, and
- “**alcoholic product**” means any product which—
  - (a) contains alcohol, and
  - (b) does not contain any psychoactive substance.

### Nicotine and tobacco products

**4.**[^c-f225704bbf18f54105fd24b89ebfd90d] Nicotine.

**5.**[^c-dc17911c64d17473b00c762375a0d71f] Tobacco products.

In this paragraph “**tobacco product**” means—

- (a) anything which is a tobacco product within the meaning of the Tobacco Products Duty Act 1979 (see section 1 of that Act), and
- (b) any other product which—
  - (i) contains nicotine, and
  - (ii) does not contain any psychoactive substance.

### Caffeine

**6.**[^c-13f58e3a263fd89a1506d271a055a900] Caffeine or caffeine products.

In this paragraph “**caffeine product**” means any product which—

- (a) contains caffeine, and
- (b) does not contain any psychoactive substance.

### Food

**7.**[^c-8efb032dbc3a17ab5d44c5c1935793aa] Any substance which—

- (a) is ordinarily consumed as food, and
- (b) does not contain a prohibited ingredient.

In this paragraph—

- “[^c-aa28536b108cb491fb2936b97420538a]**enactment**” includes—
  - (a) an enactment contained in subordinate legislation;
  - (b) an enactment contained in, or in an instrument made under, an Act of the Scottish Parliament;
  - (c) an enactment contained in, or in an instrument made under, a Measure or Act of the National Assembly for Wales;
  - (d) an enactment contained in, or in an instrument made under, Northern Ireland legislation;
- “**food**” includes drink;
- “**prohibited ingredient**”, in relation to a substance, means any psychoactive substance—
  - (a) which is not naturally occurring in the substance, and
  - (b) the use of which in or on food is not authorised by an enactment[^c-4f5772139c753e92a2cf9afba8be2ce0].
