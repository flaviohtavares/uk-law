# Psychoactive Substances Act 2016, section 6

> Text as at 2023-02-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Aggravation of offence under section 5[^c-59544b47a952dce7d1e5142bea158d48][^c-e19fa3ef70f3fc09074567ded4a4713a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c] Subsections (2) to (10) apply[^c-ca8e1f94ac38cbab19ac98f0dbeb84dc] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c] if— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c] a court *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c] in Scotland or Northern Ireland[^c-71d8af980bea64cbea62571b8546c4f6] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c] is considering the seriousness of an offence under section 5, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c] at the time the offence was committed the offender was aged 18 or over. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c] If condition A, B or C is met the court— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c] must treat the fact that the condition is met as an aggravating factor (that is to say, a factor that increases the seriousness of the offence), and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c] must state in open court that the offence is so aggravated. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c] Condition A is that the offence was committed on or in the vicinity of school premises at a relevant time. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c] For the purposes of subsection (3) a “relevant time” is— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c] any time when the school premises are in use by persons under the age of 18; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c] one hour before the start and one hour after the end of any such time. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-54d626f30d2d0dd6294025aa912d4a8c]

(5) In this section—

- “**school premises**” means land used for the purposes of a school, other than any land occupied solely as a dwelling by a person employed at the school;
- “school” has the same meaning—
  - (a) in England and Wales, as in section 4 of the Education Act 1996;
  - (b) in Scotland, as in section 135(1) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980;
  - (c) in Northern Ireland, as in Article 2(2) of the Education and Libraries (Northern Ireland) Order 1986 (S.I. 1986/594 (N.I. 3)).

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06] Condition B is that in connection with the commission of the offence the offender used a courier who, at the time the offence was committed, was under the age of 18. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06] For the purposes of subsection (6) a person (“P”) uses a courier in connection with an offence under section 5 if P causes or permits another person (the courier)— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06] to deliver a substance to a third person, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06] to deliver a drug-related consideration to P or a third person. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]

(8 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06] A drug-related consideration is a consideration of any description which— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06] is obtained in connection with the supply of a psychoactive substance, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06] is intended to be used in connection with obtaining a psychoactive substance. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]

(9 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06] Condition C is that the offence was committed in a custodial institution. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]

(10 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06] In this section— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]

- “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]**custodial institution *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]**” means any of the following— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]
  - (a) a prison; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]
  - (b) a young offender institution, secure training centre, secure college, young offenders institution, young offenders centre, juvenile justice centre or remand centre; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]
  - (c) a removal centre, a short-term holding facility or pre-departure accommodation; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]
  - (d) service custody premises; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]
- “removal centre”, “short-term holding facility” and “pre-departure accommodation” have the meaning given by section 147 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]
- “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]**service custody premises *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]**” has the meaning given by section 300(7) of the Armed Forces Act 2006. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6217412072473fb065d52bae77ba4b06]

(11)[^c-533517d321ba5044dda7e9eecb5baaa6] For the requirement for a court in England and Wales considering the seriousness of an offence under section 5 to treat certain matters as aggravating factors, see section 72 of the Sentencing Code.
