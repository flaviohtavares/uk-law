# Bank of England and Financial Services Act 2016, section 21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2021-12-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2016/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Extension of relevant authorised persons regime to all authorised persons[^c-f9dc4ee16e2fe3a8234337166ed3215a][^c-e6d2ce8dc49e21d5e8bd10cc9ec6bc22][^c-2f278aaf00c9936fd739ac88483d2f39][^c-7bbb53625d8b9de1daa5fb33c9589d10][^c-5b75faea2531f4f5b8a8a4efe72382ba][^c-212a6f1944a2da6dbfc8121391e287ff][^c-bd5971cb7b346902d3d613ef01dac751]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 4 makes provision extending to authorised persons provisions of Part 5 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (performance of regulated activities) which now apply only to relevant authorised persons.
