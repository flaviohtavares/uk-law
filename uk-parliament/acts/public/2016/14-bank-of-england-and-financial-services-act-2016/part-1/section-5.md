> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2021-12-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2016/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Financial stability strategy[^key-a26aff2f15d2534e698eea185a86367a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph 11 of Schedule 1 to the Bank of England Act 1998 (matters which may be delegated by court of directors) is amended as follows.

(2) In sub-paragraph (2) after “paragraph” insert—

> - (a) include duties and powers conferred on the court of directors by section 9A (financial stability strategy), but
> - (b) except as mentioned in paragraph (a),

.

(3) After sub-paragraph (2) insert—

> (3) The court of directors retains responsibility for a duty or power which it delegates under this paragraph.
