# Finance Act 2016, section 167

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-06-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2016/24
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 167. Simple assessments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 23 contains provisions about simple assessments by HMRC.

(2) Paragraphs 1 to 8 of that Schedule have effect in relation to the 2016-17 tax year and subsequent years.

(3) Paragraph 9 of that Schedule comes into force on such day as the Treasury may appoint by regulations made by statutory instrument. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed64ede115b3ecd0f1768e39f3589b95]

(4) Regulations under subsection (3) may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-219cdf94d03da26494a84336ab31314f]

- (a) commence paragraph 9 generally or only for specified purposes, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-219cdf94d03da26494a84336ab31314f]
- (b) appoint different days for different purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-219cdf94d03da26494a84336ab31314f]
