# Finance Act 2016, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-06-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2016/24
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Income tax charge and rates for 2016-17

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Income tax is charged for the tax year 2016-17.

(2) For that tax year—

- (a) the basic rate is 20%,
- (b) the higher rate is 40%, and
- (c) the additional rate is 45%.
