## 199. Objection to division of land

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-cba10fdeddc52319936528c1521945bb] Schedule 17 contains amendments about objecting to the division of land following a notice to treat under section 5 of the Compulsory Purchase Act 1965.

(2)[^c-e584a1de378d7b909ff53adc300dcba5] Schedule 18 contains amendments about objecting to the division of land following a general vesting declaration under section 4 of the Compulsory Purchase (Vesting Declarations) Act 1981.
