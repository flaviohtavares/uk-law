# Housing and Planning Act 2016, section 152

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (34).** Text as at 2026-09-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2016/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 152. Approval condition where development order grants permission for building[^c22569361]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In section 60 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 (permission granted by development order), after subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1), where planning permission is granted by a development order for building operations in England, the order may require the approval of the local planning authority, or the Secretary of State, to be obtained—
>
> - (a) for those operations, or
> - (b) with respect to any matters that relate to those operations, or to the use of the land in question following those operations, and are specified in the order.

(2)[^key-157c561eb7d1019404fa5ad5d4fa2a00] In subsection (2) of that section, after “any buildings” insert “ in Wales ”.

(3)[^key-157c561eb7d1019404fa5ad5d4fa2a00] In subsection (2B) of that section, for “subsection (1)” substitute “ subsections (1) and (1A) ”.

(4)[^key-157c561eb7d1019404fa5ad5d4fa2a00] In section 70A of that Act (power to decline to determine subsequent application), in subsection (5)(b), for “section 60(2)” substitute “ section 60(1A), (2) ”.
