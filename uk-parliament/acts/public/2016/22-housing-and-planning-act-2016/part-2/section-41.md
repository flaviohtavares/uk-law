## 41. Application for rent repayment order[^c-290f6447d1e44fd19166e0ab3c6e0cc7][^c-3163c2da7b1b37e8135ff2fc4c0b68ee]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A tenant or a local housing authority may apply to the First-tier Tribunal for a rent repayment order against a landlord[^c-197cfdea4a12ce0c1a5dfeaef76ff707] who has committed an offence to which this Chapter applies.

(2) A tenant may apply for a rent repayment order only if —

- (a) the offence relates to housing that, at the time of the offence, was let to the tenant, and
- (b) the offence was committed in the period of 2 years[^c-af8b18c52803ee02e5da87b91e1a6310] ending with the day on which the application is made.

(2A)[^c-1dca84525bdf673a1079ed4b6ec4fd97] The requirement in subsection (2)(a) does not apply to an application for a rent repayment order in relation to an offence under section 16J(1) or (2) of the [Housing Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/50).

(3) A local housing authority may apply for a rent repayment order only if—

- (a) the offence relates to housing in the authority's area, and
- (b) the authority has complied with section 42.

(4) In deciding whether to apply for a rent repayment order a local housing authority must have regard to any guidance given by the Secretary of State.
