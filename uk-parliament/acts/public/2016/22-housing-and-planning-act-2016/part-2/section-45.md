## 45. Amount of order: local housing authorities[^c-ef56e51c4f3c487e466d835a4a396dab][^c-77e828066723c3c42e7a1ad7916f0d1a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the First-tier Tribunal decides to make a rent repayment order under section 43 in favour of a local housing authority, the amount is to be determined in accordance with this section.

(2) The amount must relate to universal credit paid in respect of[^c-526f7b228349ba23db5c8bc34adf5466] the period mentioned in the table.

| *In the order is made on the ground that the landlord has committed* | *the amount must relate to universal credit paid in respect of* |
| --- | --- |
| an offence mentioned in row 1 or 2 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d7ddbd30de50b8496c9b6b9c3ff13bb9] , 2, 2A or 9 of the table in section 40(3) | the period of 2 years[^c-6ae7dc35412216c271f831ac238eaead] ending with the date of the offence |
| an offence mentioned in row 2B of the table in section 40(3)[^c-ab5b632c02f0f1901e95ba51771817ec] | the period of 2 years ending with the date of the offence or, if the tenancy ends before that date, the date on which it ends |
| an offence mentioned in row 3, 4, 5, 6 or 7 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b4ce846e31a2748c6d168c6c7cd2d4b] 2C, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 or 10 of the table in section 40(3) | a period, not exceeding 2 years, during which the landlord was committing the offence |

(3) The amount that the landlord may be required to pay[^c-1792781caf26dfafdf6730471a97cec9] in respect of a period must not exceed the amount of universal credit [^c-af977f3f61604e4c21b51b17ec6bd613]... received (directly or indirectly) in respect of rent under the tenancy for that period.

(4) In determining the amount the tribunal must, in particular, take into account—

- (a) the conduct of the landlord,
- (b) the financial circumstances of the landlord, [^c-dd816e5f021aef271de55fec55d8234f]...
- (c) whether the landlord has at any time been convicted of , or received a financial penalty in respect of,[^c-71e11c65bc481cef29e94038cd841f2c] an offence to which this Chapter applies , and[^c-9244c259e572474ccaa631835c5ead78]
- (d) whether the landlord has at any time had a rent repayment order made against them.
