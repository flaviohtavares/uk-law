# Energy Act 2016, section 1

> Text as at 2025-04-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. The OGA

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-0c2d05015e9b2b120913727af280cb05] The company originally incorporated under the Companies Act 2006 as the Oil and Gas Authority Limited is renamed as the Oil and Gas Authority.

(2)[^c-0c2d05015e9b2b120913727af280cb05] In relation to any of its functions—

- (a) the Oil and Gas Authority is not to be regarded as acting on behalf of the Crown, and
- (b) its members, officers and staff are not to be regarded as Crown servants.

(3)[^c-0c2d05015e9b2b120913727af280cb05] The Oil and Gas Authority is exempt from the requirements of the Companies Act 2006 relating to the use of “limited” as part of its name.

(4)[^c-054de6ad92f6962d86c32a5459c71905] In this Act “**the OGA**” means the Oil and Gas Authority.
