# Energy Act 2016, section 66

> Text as at 2025-04-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Disclosure after specified period

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-eaf5876f3206a53dda0a602a4a63b13d] Section 61 does not prohibit protected material obtained by the OGA under Chapter 3 (information and samples) from being—

- (a) published, or
- (b) made available to the public (in a case where the protected material includes samples),

by the OGA or a subsequent holder at such time as may be specified in regulations made by the Secretary of State.

(2)[^c-eaf5876f3206a53dda0a602a4a63b13d] Regulations under subsection (1) may include provision permitting protected material to be published, or made available to the public, immediately after it is provided to a person.

(3)[^c-a16fa75aecbf62438fde6f3eaad34946] Before making regulations under subsection (1), the Secretary of State must consult such persons as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

(4)[^c-a16fa75aecbf62438fde6f3eaad34946] Subsection (3) does not apply if the Secretary of State is satisfied that consultation is unnecessary having regard to consultation carried out by the OGA in relation to what time should be specified in regulations under subsection (1).

(5)[^c-eaf5876f3206a53dda0a602a4a63b13d] In determining the time to be specified in respect of protected material in regulations under subsection (1), the Secretary of State must have regard to the following factors—

- (a) whether the specified time will allow owners of protected material a reasonable period of time to satisfy the main purpose for which they acquired or created the material;
- (b) any potential benefits to the petroleum industry of protected material being published or made available at the specified time;
- (c) any potential risk that the specified time may discourage persons from acquiring or creating petroleum-related information or petroleum-related samples (as defined in section 27);
- (d) any other factors the Secretary of State considers relevant.

(6)[^c-eaf5876f3206a53dda0a602a4a63b13d] In balancing the factors mentioned in subsection (5)(a) to (d), the Secretary of State must take into account the principal objective.

(7)[^c-eaf5876f3206a53dda0a602a4a63b13d] For the purposes of subsection (5)(a), the owner of protected material is the person by whom, or on whose behalf, the protected material was provided to the OGA under Chapter 3 (information and samples).
