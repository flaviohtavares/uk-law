# Energy Act 2016, section 61

> Text as at 2025-04-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Prohibition on disclosure[^key-45df683d61f46eaff05ea273f5729236]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Protected material must not be disclosed—

- (a) by the OGA, or
- (b) by a subsequent holder,

except in accordance with this Chapter.
