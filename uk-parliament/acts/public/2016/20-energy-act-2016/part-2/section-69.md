# Energy Act 2016, section 69

> Text as at 2025-04-15.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Disclosure for purpose of proceedings[^c-34566e706668d9322995b4e0048bd6be]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 61 does not prohibit a disclosure of protected material by the OGA for the purposes of, or in connection with—

- (a) civil proceedings, or
- (b) arbitration proceedings.

(2) Section 61 does not prohibit a disclosure of protected material by the OGA for the purposes of, or in connection with—

- (a) the investigation or prosecution of criminal offences, or
- (b) the prevention of criminal activity.
