# Enterprise Act 2016, section 1

> Text as at 2025-06-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Small Business Commissioner[^c22567751]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-88060f0a3556c54d6b8603e9b40a55bc] A Small Business Commissioner is established.

(2)[^key-6b9a79a7bc7edf05a8eab770a3314cee] The Commissioner's principal functions are—

- (a) to provide general advice and information to small businesses (see section 3), and
- (b) to consider complaints from small businesses relating to payment matters in connection with the supply of goods and services to larger businesses, and make recommendations (see sections 4 to 8).

(3)[^key-88060f0a3556c54d6b8603e9b40a55bc] Schedule 1 makes provision about the Commissioner.
