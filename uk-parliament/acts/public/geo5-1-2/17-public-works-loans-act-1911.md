# Public Works Loans Act 1911

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Public Works Loans Act 1911

1911 c.17

An Act to grant Money for the purpose of certain Local Loans out of the Local Loans Fund, and for other purposes relating to Local Loans.

\[18th August 1911\]

## 1—3 . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c579344]

## 4. Amendment of section 11 of 38 & 39 Vict. c. 89 as to term for repayment of loans.[^c579345]

In section eleven of the Public Works Loans Act, 1875 [^c579346], as amended by section five of the Public Works Loans Act, 1898, fifty years shall be substituted for thirty years as the maximum period for the repayment of a loan in a case where no period for repayment is specified by the special Act relating to the loan.

## 5. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Public Works Loans Act 1911.
