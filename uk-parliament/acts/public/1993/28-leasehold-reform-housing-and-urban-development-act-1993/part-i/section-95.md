# Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993, section 95

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (453).** Text as at 2025-05-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/28

## 95. Saving for National Trust.

*Extent: E+W*

Chapters I and II shall not prejudice the operation of section 21 of the [^c17025521]National Trust Act 1907, and accordingly there shall be no right under Chapter I or II to acquire any interest in or new lease of any property if an interest in the property is under that section vested inalienably in the National Trust for Places of Historic Interest or Natural Beauty.
