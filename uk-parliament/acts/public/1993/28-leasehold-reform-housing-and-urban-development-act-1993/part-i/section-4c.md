# Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993, section 4C

## 4C. RTE companies: regulations[^c17043531]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State shall by regulations make provision about the content and form of the articles of association[^c-dfb5f1c6081f3ca8077fc54ba61e08a8] of RTE companies.

(2) A RTE company may adopt provisions of the regulations for its articles[^c-c4e97f2ab61629c576f33ad8cde0882e].

(3) The regulations may include provision which is to have effect for a RTE company whether or not it is adopted by the company.

(4) A provision of the articles[^c-4aeb836cec6d2c06aa29b2b3bbfe8dac] of a RTE company has no effect to the extent that it is inconsistent with the regulations.

(5) The regulations have effect in relation to articles[^c-52fa03986f7eff68d3a99f6a4e0f28e7]—

- (a) irrespective of the date of the articles[^c-d1a17b67bb8a3a8e513fb705dc8227a0], but
- (b) subject to any transitional provisions of the regulations.

(6)[^c-735fc93fb89aa0c6fecd459dc9e7455d] Section 20 of the Companies Act 2006 (default application of model articles) does not apply to a RTE company.
