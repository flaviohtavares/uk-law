# Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993, section 97

## 97. Registration of notices, applications and orders under Chapters I and II.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) No lease shall be registrable under the [^c17025531]Land Charges Act 1972 or be taken to be an estate contract within the meaning of that Act by reason of any rights or obligations of the tenant or landlord which may arise under Chapter I or II, and any right of a tenant arising from a notice given under section 13 or 42 shall not be capable of falling within paragraph 2 of Schedule 1 or 3 to the Land Registration Act 2002[^c-7ee82d61fdf0faf39fe5c9fee1451d98]; but a notice given under section 13 or 42 shall be registrable under the Land Charges Act 1972, or may be the subject of a notice under the Land Registration Act 2002[^c-55d36a4ea1dc33c077d42c47d10fa218], as if it were an estate contract.

(2) The Land Charges Act 1972 and the Land Registration Act 2002[^c-4b0aa095b4cf7b0e4b3719bc8f6aaa5d] —

- (a) shall apply in relation to an order made under section 26(1) or 50(1) as they apply in relation to an order affecting land which is made by the court for the purpose of enforcing a judgment or recognisance; and
- (b) shall apply in relation to an application for such an order as they apply in relation to other pending land actions.
