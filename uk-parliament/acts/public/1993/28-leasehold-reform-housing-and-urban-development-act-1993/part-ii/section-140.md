## 140. Calculation of Housing Revenue Account subsidy.

*Extent: E+W*

In subsection (1) of section 80 of the [^c17026281]Local Government and Housing Act 1989 (determination of formulae for calculating Housing Revenue Account subsidy), the words “and for any year the first such determination shall be made before the 25th December immediately preceding that year” shall cease to have effect.
