## 175A. Insurer and long-term insurance business[^c11732951]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In this Act “insurer” means—

- (a) a person who has permission under Part 4A[^c-51f2307865376e1be7dd1982d73c3ef5] of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 to effect or carry out contracts of insurance, [^c-a59e6d6293545e7182a4e1392d58ff61]...
- (b)[^c-a59e6d6293545e7182a4e1392d58ff61] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) In this Act “long term insurance business” means business which consists of the effecting or carrying out of contracts of long-term insurance.

(3) This section must be read with—

- (a) section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000;
- (b) any relevant order under that section; and
- (c) Schedule 2 to that Act.
