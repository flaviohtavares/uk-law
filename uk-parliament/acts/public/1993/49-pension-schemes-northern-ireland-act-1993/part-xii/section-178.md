# Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, section 178

## 178. Sub-delegation.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Without prejudice to any specific provisions in this Act, a power conferred by this Act to make regulations or an order (other than regulations and orders made under Chapter I of Part IV, Part VIII so far as it applies for the purposes of Chapter I of Part IV, sections 132 to 134, 135(2) and (3), 136, 137, 138(1) to (4), 139, 149(2), 154(1) to (5), 158, 159 [^c11736581]. . . and 171) includes power to provide for a person to exercise a discretion in dealing with any matter.

(2)[^c11736591] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Regulations under sections [^c-2d911f74aa682d3e4b06c0b736d6e847]... 59(2) and 93(1)[^c11736601] , 97AF(1)[^c-b17558c31b9200f38e7daf1621eef892] and 97I may provide that the values there mentioned shall be calculated and verified [^c11736611]. . .—

- (a) in such manner as may, in the particular case, be approved—
  - (i) by a prescribed person;
  - (ii) by a person with prescribed professional qualifications or experience; or
  - (iii) by a person approved by the Department; or
- (b) in accordance with guidance prepared and revised[^c11736621] by a prescribed body;
