# Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, section 173

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (70).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/49

## 173. Managers of schemes.

*Extent: N.I.*

The Department may by regulations provide—[^c11732851]

- (a) who is to be treated as a manager of an occupational pension scheme for any o f the purposes of the provisions of this Act (except Chapter II of Part VII or section 153), the Social Security Acts (Northern Ireland) 1975 to 1991, [^c11732861]. . . Part VI of the [^c11732871]Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 [^c11732891]. . .[^c11732881][^c17227541]. . . , Article 27D of the Matrimonial Causes (Northern Ireland) Order 1978[^c11732911] Part IV or V of the Welfare Reform and Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1999[^c11732921] [^key-fb7dd19e53d57d774e3c3ff64e1d41c5]...

or[^c11732931]

- (b) who is be treated as a trustee of a scheme for the purposes of Chapter I of Part IV, Chapter I of Part IVA, Part VIII so far as it applies for the purposes of Chapter I of Part IV, sections 149(2), 154(1) to (5), 158, 159 and 171 or articles 22[^c11732941] to 26[^key-1010a123c9fab7acdf8c70621af87d23] of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995.
