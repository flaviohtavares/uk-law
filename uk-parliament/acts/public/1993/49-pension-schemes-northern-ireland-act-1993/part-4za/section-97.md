# Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, section 97

## 97. Supplementary provisions.[^key-920bb60c1ce06dc0c76d1607b21a3fe0][^key-e85fea2e415384a1b766bba1856ecba0]

*Extent: N.I.*

In making any calculation for the purposes of this Chapter—

- (a) any charge or lien on, and
- (b) any set-off against,

the whole or part of a pension shall be disregarded.
