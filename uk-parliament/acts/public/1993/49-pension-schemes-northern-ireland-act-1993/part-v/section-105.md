# Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, section 105

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (70).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/49
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 105. Annual increase of guaranteed minimum pensions.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Whenever the Secretary of State makes an order under section 109 of the [^c11727521]Pension Schemes Act 1993 specifying a percentage by which there is to be an increase of the rate of that part of guaranteed minimum pensions which is attributable to earnings factors for the tax years in the relevant period as defined in that section[^c11727531] for—

- (a) earners who have attained pensionable age; and
- (b) widows, widowers and surviving civil partners[^c-d31af2d28d46ac07bcf91c8c63aeeb90],

the Department may make a corresponding order for Northern Ireland.

(2) Where the benefits mentioned in section 42(1) to (7) are not increased on the day on which an order under this section takes effect, the order shall be treated for the purposes of that section as not taking effect until the day on which those benefits are next increased.
