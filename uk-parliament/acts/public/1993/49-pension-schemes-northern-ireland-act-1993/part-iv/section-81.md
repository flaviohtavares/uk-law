## 81. Revaluation not to apply to substituted benefit.

*Extent: N.I.*

Nothing in this Chapter is to be construed as requiring the revaluation of any pension or other benefit provided by virtue of section 69(2)(b) or 97D(2)(b)[^c11726501] by way of complete substitute for another pension or benefit.
