# Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, section 152

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (70).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/49
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 152. Information for purposes of contracting-out[^c11729971]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department or the Inland Revenue may give to the trustees or managers of an occupational pension scheme or a personal pension scheme which was an appropriate scheme before the abolition date[^key-d97c0334a7a8d23e7638dccb956b7bfb] such information as appears to the Department or Inland Revenue appropriate to give to them for the purpose of enabling them to comply with their obligations under Part III.

(2) The Department or Inland Revenue may also give to such persons as may be prescribed any information that they could give under subsection (1) to trustees or managers of a scheme.
