# Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, section 150

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (70).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/49
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 150. Application of enactments as respects personal pension schemes.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulations may provide that any provision of this Act or of Articles 22 to 26 and 40 of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995[^c11729861] which relates to occupational pension schemes (other than a provision to which subsection (2) applies) shall have effect in relation to personal pension schemes subject to prescribed modifications.

(2) This subsection applies to [^c11729871]. . .section 107 so far as it relates to occupational pension schemes (and Part VIII and section 149 so far as they have effect for the purposes of section 107 as it so relates), sections 113, 119 to 123, 151, 153, 156 and 157.
