# Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, section 151

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (70).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/49

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 151. Requirement to give information to the Department, Secretary of State or Inland Revenue for the purposes of certain provisions.[^c11729941]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulations may require the furnishing by prescribed persons to the Department, the Secretary of State or the Inland Revenue of such information as it or he requires or they require for the purposes of Part III, section 107 (and Part VIII and section 149 so far as they have effect for the purposes of section 107) and section 113[^key-e7a4dea3f154b2e39344fa691796ec46].

(2) In this section “regulations” means regulations made by the Department or, as the case may be, the Secretary of State and “prescribed” shall be construed accordingly.
