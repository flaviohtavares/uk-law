## 37. Reduced rates of Class 1 contributions.[^c11721521]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c11721531][^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4] Subsections (1A) to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4] (1E)[^c11721541] apply where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]

- (a) the earnings paid to or for the benefit of an earner in any tax week are in respect of an employment which is contracted-out employment at the time of the payment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]
- (b) the earner’s service in the employment is service which qualifies him for a pension provided by a salary related contracted-out scheme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]

and in subsections (1A) and (1B) “the relevant part”, in relation to those earnings, means so much of those earnings as exceeds the current lower earnings limit but not *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4] the upper accrual point[^c-7cac8082612f9a949d4f3d093f4b60d1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4] (or the prescribed equivalents if the earner is paid otherwise than weekly). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]

(1ZA (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-546ab67c8113f77fd8fb427eb97bbffb][^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1A)[^c11721551] The amount of any primary Class 1 contribution *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4] attributable to section 8(1)(a) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 (c. 7)[^c17185051] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4] in respect of the earnings shall be reduced by an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4] 1.4 per cent[^c-0567c9600e0dd61106def642d7896ea9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4] of the relevant part of the earnings (“Amount R1”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]

(1B) The amount of any secondary Class 1 contribution in respect of the earnings shall be reduced by an amount equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4] 3.4 per cent[^c-d0021665a133bb7318d7dd4e7fbc6ce2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4] of the relevant part of the earnings (“Amount R2”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]

(1C) The aggregate of Amounts R1 and R2 shall be set off— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]

- (a) first against the aggregate amount which the secondary contributor is liable to pay in respect of the contributions mentioned in subsections (1A) and (1B); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]
- (b) then (as to any balance) against any amount which the secondary contributor is liable to pay in respect of any primary or secondary Class 1 contribution in respect of earnings— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]
  - (i) paid to or for the benefit of any other employed earner (whether in contracted-out employment or not), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4] in relation to which the secondary contributor is such a contributor; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]

  and in this subsection any reference to a liability to pay an amount in respect of a primary Class 1 contribution is a reference to such a liability under paragraph 3 of Schedule 1 to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4] [^c11721571]Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]

(1D) If— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]

- (a) any balance remains, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]
- (b) the secondary contributor makes an application for the purpose to the Inland Revenue, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]

the Inland Revenue shall, in such manner and at such time (or within such period) as may be prescribed by regulations made by the Secretary of State, pay to the secondary contributor an amount equal to the remaining balance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]

But such regulations may make provision for the adjustment of an amount that would otherwise be payable under this subsection so as to avoid the payment of trivial or fractional amounts. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]

(1E) If the Inland Revenue pay any amount under subsection (1D) which they are not required to pay, they may recover that amount from the secondary contributor in such manner and at such time (or within such period) as may be prescribed by such regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]

(2) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]

- (a) an earner has ceased to be employed in an employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]
- (b) earnings are paid to him or for his benefit within the period of 6 weeks, or such other period as may be prescribed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4] by regulations made by the Secretary of State[^c11721581] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4], from the day on which he so ceased, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]

that employment shall be treated for the purposes of subsection (1) as contracted-out employment at the time when the earnings are paid if it was contracted-out employment in relation to the earner when he was last employed in it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]

(3) This section shall not affect the amount of any primary Class 1 contribution which is payable at a reduced rate by virtue of regulations under section 19(4) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4] [^c11721591]Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 (reduced rates for married women and widows). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8b9386d7c8840c6a87d4e9aafe4ef8e4]
