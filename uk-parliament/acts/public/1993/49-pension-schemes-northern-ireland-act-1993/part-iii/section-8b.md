## 8B. Reference scheme.[^c11718331]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] This section applies for the purposes of section 8A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

(2) A reference scheme is an occupational pension scheme which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

- (a) complies with each of subsections (3) and (4), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
- (b) complies with any prescribed requirements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

(3) In relation to earners employed in employed earner’s employment, a reference scheme is one which provides— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

- (a) for them to be entitled to a pension under the scheme commencing at a normal pension age of 65 and continuing for life, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
- (b) for the annual rate of the pension at that age to be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
  - (i) 1/80th of average qualifying earning in the last three tax years preceding the end of service, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

  multiplied by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] the number of years service, not exceeding such number as would produce an annual rate equal to half the earnings on which it is calculated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

(4) In relation to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] widows, widowers or surviving civil partners[^key-53f5e16771244ec2c947ff5258025bb8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2], a reference scheme is one which provides— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

- (a) for the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] widows, widowers or surviving civil partners *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] of earners employed in employed earner’s employment (whether the earners die before or after attaining the age of 65) to be entitled, except in prescribed circumstances, to pensions under the scheme; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
- (b)[^c11718341] for entitlements to those pensions to commence on the day following the death of the earners, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
- (c) except in prescribed circumstances, for the annual rate of those pensions to be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
  - (i) if the earners die on or after their normal pension age, 50 per cent. of the annual rate which a reference scheme was required to provide to the deceased earners immediately before their death, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] if the earners die before their normal pension age, 50 per cent. of the annual rate of pension which a reference scheme would have been required to provide to the deceased earners if the date of their death had been their normal pension age, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
- (d) if those pensions are payable in respect of earners who die— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
  - (i) otherwise than in pensionable service under the scheme, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] before their own entitlements to pensions under the scheme have commenced, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

  for those pensions to be revalued in accordance with section 80 as though they were such benefits as are mentioned in section 79(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]*a*).

(5) For the purposes of this section, an earner’s qualifying earnings in any tax year are 90 per cent. of the amount by which the earner’s earnings— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

- (a) exceed the qualifying earnings factor for that year; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
- (b) do not exceed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] the applicable limit[^key-ef4944d875c552769da8ce968804f536] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2].

(6) Regulations may modify subsections (2) to (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

(7) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

- “[^key-2c9910438c4759da5ad7f67c223592f7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]**the applicable limit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
  - (a) in relation to a tax year before *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] 2009–10[^key-6073226cbe3f65670f9f9da50b4511da] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2], the upper earnings limit for the year multiplied by 53; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
  - (b) in relation to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] 2009–10 or any subsequent tax year, the upper accrual point *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] multiplied by 53[^key-edd3e3ed5018c345ceaf58c85680e57a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2];
- “normal pension age”, in relation to a scheme, means the age specified in the scheme as the earliest age at which pension becomes payable under the scheme (apart from any special provision as to early retirement on grounds of ill-health or otherwise), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
- “qualifying earnings factor”, in relation to a tax year, has the meaning given by section 121(1) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
- “upper earnings limit”, in relation to a tax year, means the amount specified for that year by regulations made by virtue of section 5(3) of that Act as the upper earnings limit for Class 1 contributions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
