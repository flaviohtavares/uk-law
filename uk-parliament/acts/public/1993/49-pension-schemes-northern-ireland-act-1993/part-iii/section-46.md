## 46. Powers of Board to approve arrangements for scheme ceasing to be certified.[^c11723811][^key-b2026f6f505f2abbd6390e64cbe5d5b3]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] In the case of an occupational pension scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] [^key-ef3f0eed4119e344d06efecf96d86fa6]... which is or has been certified as a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] salary related contracted-out[^key-a3df6237f7a5b3ab921bdef168a15f57] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] scheme, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] Inland Revenue[^c11723821] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] may, for the event of, or in connection with, its ceasing to be such a scheme, approve any arrangements made or to be made in relation to the scheme, or for its purposes, for the preservation or transfer— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072]

- (a)[^key-0da410a698cad210a01b390c168dcdf2] of earners’ accrued rights to guaranteed minimum pensions under the scheme or accrued rights to pensions under the scheme attributable to their service on or after the principal appointed day, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072]
- (b) of the liability for the payment of such pensions in respect of persons who have then become entitled to receive them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072]

(1A)[^c11723871] The power of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] Inland Revenue *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] to approve arrangements under this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072]

- (a) includes power to approve arrangements subject to conditions, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072]
- (b) may be exercised either generally or in relation to a particular scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072]

(1B)[^c11723871] Arrangements may not be approved under this section unless any prescribed conditions are met *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072]

(2) If the scheme ceases to be a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] salary related contracted-out[^key-42e4dbcdbdfdcd894a0002c18e3ca226] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] scheme (whether by being wound up or otherwise) and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] Inland Revenue *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072]

- (a) have[^c11723911] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] withdrawn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] their[^c11723921] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] approval of previously approved arrangements relating to it; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072]
- (b) have *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] declined to approve arrangements relating to it, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072]

the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] Inland Revenue *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] may issue a certificate to that effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072]

(3) A certificate issued under subsection (2)(a) or (b) shall be cancelled by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] Inland Revenue *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] they subsequently approve[^c11723961] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] the arrangements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072]

(4) Regulations may provide that where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] Inland Revenue *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] have[^c11723981] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] approved arrangements under subsection (1) in respect of an occupational pension scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] [^key-d95713e297e423fbdb97db542f2e47e4]... any provision of this Part (other than sections 14, 15, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] 27 and 29[^key-472f2ebc0577bbdf98b7598f54b71cb2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] 39 and 41[^key-70aaa424ac472806730b0f973700e9cd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072]) or Chapter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] III *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] of Part IV or Chapter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] II *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] of Part V shall have effect subject to such modifications as may be specified in the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072]

(5) Any such regulations shall have effect in relation to arrangements whenever approved, unless they provide that they are only to have effect in relation to arrangements approved after they come into operation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072]

(6) It is hereby declared that an approval of arrangements relating to an occupational pension scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] to which subsection (1) applies[^key-bde3cdff2ff6658a6fb944910aa2fc64] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072] may be withdrawn at any time, notwithstanding that the scheme has been wound up. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0121afae2d001253346a0966b9c5b072]
