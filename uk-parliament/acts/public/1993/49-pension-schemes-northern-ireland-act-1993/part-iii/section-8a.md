# Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, section 8A

## 8A. The statutory standard.[^c11718311]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] Subject to the provisions of this Part, the scheme must, in relation to the provision of pensions for earners in employed earner’s employment, and for their *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] widows, widowers or surviving civil partners[^c-b0de205cccf1dd73ebf492f860ddbb72] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2], satisfy the statutory standard. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

(2) Subject to regulations made by virtue of section 5(2B)(c)(ii), in applying this section regard must only be had to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

- (a) earners in employed earner’s employment, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
- (b) their *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] widows, widowers or surviving civil partners[^c-ed8adf4ef499e937c808f5195aa12c72] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2],

collectively, and the pensions to be provided for persons falling within paragraph (a) or (b) must be considered as a whole. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

(3) For the purposes of this section, a scheme satisfies the statutory standard if the pensions to be provided for such persons are broadly equivalent to, or better than, the pensions which would be provided for such persons under a reference scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

(4) Regulations may provided for the manner of, and criteria for, determining whether the pensions to be provided for such persons under a scheme are broadly equivalent to, or better than, the pensions which would be provided for such persons under a reference scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

(5) Regulations made by virtue of subsection (4) may provide for the determination to be made in accordance with guidance prepared by a prescribed body *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] [^c-bae4e993efe90da6e6d4fbec2e5fbe7c]....

(6) The pensions to be provided for such persons under a scheme are to be treated as broadly equivalent to or better than the pensions which would be provided for such persons under a reference scheme if and only if an actuary (who, except in prescribed circumstances, must be the actuary appointed for the scheme in pursuance of Article 47 of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995) so certifies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
