## 51. Payment of state scheme premiums on termination of certified status.[^c11724581]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c11724591][^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c11724601] Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

- (a) an earner is serving in employment which is contracted-out employment by reference to an occupational pension scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] [^c-a7caa3f2126bc3963ac8af842e6a3c62]...,
- (b) paragraph (a) ceases to apply, by reason of any of the following circumstances, before the earner attains the scheme’s normal pension age or (if earlier) the end of the tax year preceding that in which the earner attains pensionable age, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (c) the earner has served for less than two years in the employment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

the prescribed person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] shall, if subsection (2B) applies, pay and otherwise[^c11724611] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] may elect to pay a premium under this subsection (referred to in this Act as a “contributions equivalent premium”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(2ZA (affected by repeal, see note))[^c11724621][^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] In this section “prescribed” means prescribed by regulations made by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(2A) The circumstances referred to in subsection (2) are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

- (a) the earner’s service in the employment ceases otherwise than on the earner’s death, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (b) the earner ceases to be a member of the scheme otherwise than on the earner’s death, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (c) the earner’s service in the employment ceases on the earner’s death and the earner dies leaving a widow or widower, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (d) the scheme is wound up, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (e) the scheme ceases to be a contracted-out occupational pension scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

. . .[^c11724631]

(2B)[^c17190541][^c-a2799cb0bd9f05a209a6b0a6c93b8201][^c-4921c10c939b31ee9effc07d00645847][^c11724641] Except in prescribed circumstances, this subsection applies in any case where the earner has no accrued right to any benefit under the scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(2C) Where a contributions equivalent premium is required to be paid in respect of an earner by virtue of subsection (2), the prescribed person must notify the Inland Revenue of that fact within the prescribed period and in the prescribed manner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
