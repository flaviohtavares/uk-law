## 5. Requirements for certification of schemes: general.[^c11717981]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38] Subject to subsection (4), an occupational pension scheme can be contracted-out in relation to an earner’s employment only if it satisfies subsection (2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38] [^key-2930086adaee4074dfbe24b9947c2ab6]....

(2)[^c11717991] An occupational pension scheme satisfies this subsection only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]

- (a) in relation to any earner’s service before the principal appointed day, it satisfies the conditions of subsection (2A); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]
- (b) in relation to any earner’s service on or after that day, it satisfies the conditions of subsection (2B). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]

(2A) The conditions of this subsection are that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]

- (a) the scheme complies in all respects with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38] sections 9 to 20E[^key-dd5a1b8e4a233436d1e337bacaa197b5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38] or, in such cases or classes of case as may be prescribed, with those sections as modified by regulations; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]
- (b) the rules of the scheme applying to guaranteed minimum pensions are framed so as to comply with the relevant requirements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]

(2B) The conditions of this subsection are that the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38] Inland Revenue[^c11718001] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]are satisfied[^c11718011] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38] that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]

- (a) the scheme complies with section 8A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]
- (b) restrictions imposed under Article 40 of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 (restriction on employer-related investments) apply to the scheme and the scheme complies with those restrictions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]
- (c) the scheme satisfies such other requirements as may be prescribed (which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]
  - (i) must include requirements as to the amount of the resources of the scheme, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38] may include a requirement that, if the only members of the scheme were those falling within any prescribed class or description, the scheme would comply with section 8A), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]
- (d) the scheme does not fall within a prescribed class or description; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]

and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38] are satisfied *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38] that the rules of the scheme are framed so as to comply with the relevant requirements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]

(2C) Regulations may modify subsection (2B)(a) and (b) in their application to occupational pension schemes falling within a prescribed class or description. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]

(3)[^key-1a40a49d48c059cc5b28e3029e54bfae] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Where there are two or more occupational pension schemes in force in relation to an earner’s employment, none of which can by itself be a contracted-out scheme, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38] Inland Revenue may, if they think[^c11718061] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38] fit, treat them for contracting-out purposes as a single scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]

(5)[^key-201fced3217ac862073d6dd117074383] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5A) Regulations about pension schemes made under this Chapter may contain provisions framed by reference to whether or not a scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38] is a registered pension scheme under section 153 of the Finance Act 2004[^key-e55052f2999e90472ead9828ee4469e7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38].

(6) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]**relevant requirements *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]

- (a) the requirements of any regulations prescribing the form and content of rules of contracted-out *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38] [^key-1baae03942f067415f5130fcd8bdc539]... schemes; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]
- (b) such other requirements as to form and content (not inconsistent with regulations) as may be imposed by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38] Department[^c11718071] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38] as a condition of contracting-out *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38] [^key-4b05e0a2898067834f3bff613a3141ed]... either generally or in relation to a particular scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dc2fe4222d1b24a3dc6515e447982d38]
