## 64. Unpaid premiums: supplementary.[^c11726251]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] Where a person charged with an offence to which section 63(2) applies is convicted of that offence in his absence under Article 24(2) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] [^c11726261]Magistrates’ Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, then if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

- (a) it is proved to the satisfaction of the court, on oath or by affidavit or in the manner prescribed by magistrates’ courts rules, that notice under section 63(4) has been duly served specifying the other premiums in respect of which the complainant intends to give evidence; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (b) the clerk of petty sessions has received a statement in writing purporting to be made by the accused or by a solicitor acting on his behalf to the effect that if the accused is convicted in his absence of the offence charged he desires to admit failing to pay the other premiums so specified or any of them, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

section 63(3) and (4) shall have effect as if the evidence had been given and the failure so admitted had been proved, and the court shall proceed accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(2) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

- (a) a person is convicted of an offence to which section 63(2) applies; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (b) an order is made under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] [^c11726271]Probation Act (Northern Ireland) 1950 placing the offender on probation or discharging him absolutely or conditionally, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

subsection (1) and section 63(2) to (4) shall apply as if it were a conviction for all purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(3) Any sum which a person is liable to pay under subsection (1) or section 63(2) to (4) shall be recoverable from him as a penalty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(4) contributions equivalent[^c11726281] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] premiums recovered by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] Inland Revenue[^c11726291] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] under those provisions shall be treated for all purposes as premiums paid to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] Inland Revenue *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] in respect of the person in respect of whom they were originally payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
