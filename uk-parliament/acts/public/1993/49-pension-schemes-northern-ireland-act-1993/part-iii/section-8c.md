# Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, section 8C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (70).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/49

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 8C. Transfer, commutation, etc.[^c-ad974af06f0166ec756291b0e7eec08b][^c11718351]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] Regulations may prohibit or restrict— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

- (a) the transfer of any liability— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
  - (i) for the payment of pensions under a relevant scheme, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2] in respect of accrued rights to such pensions, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
- (b) the discharge of any liability to provide pensions under a relevant scheme, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
- (c) the payment of a lump sum instead of a pension payable under a relevant scheme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

except in prescribed circumstances or on prescribed conditions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

(2) In this section, “relevant scheme” means a scheme contracted out by virtue of section 5(2B) and references to pensions and accrued rights under the scheme are to such pensions and rights so far as attributable to an earner’s service on or after the principal appointed day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]

(3) Regulations under subsection (1) may provide that any provision of this Part shall have effect subject to such modifications as may be specified in the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
