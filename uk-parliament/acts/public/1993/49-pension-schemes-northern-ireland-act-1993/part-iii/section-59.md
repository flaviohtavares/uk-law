## 59. Further provisions concerning calculations relating to premiums.[^c11725861]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] The following amounts shall be certified by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] Inland Revenue[^c11725871] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]—

- (b)[^c11725891] the sum of the amounts specified in section 54(4); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (c) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^key-17812756f0a784a6aef28be15649c9ab] the amount mentioned in section 57(2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(2)[^c11725911] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) If the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] Inland Revenue *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]—

- (a)[^c11725931] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) are satisfied[^c11725941] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] that records of earnings relevant for determining the amount mentioned in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] section 54(4)[^c11725951] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] have not been maintained or retained or are otherwise unobtainable, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

then the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] Inland Revenue *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] may for that purpose— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

- (i) compute, in such manner as *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] they think[^c11725971] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] fit, an amount which shall be regarded as the amount of those earnings; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] take their amount to be such sum as *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] they may[^c11725981] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] specify in the particular case, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
