## 8D. Entitlement to benefit.[^c11718361]

*Extent: N.I.*

In the case of a scheme contracted out by virtue of section 5(2B), regulations may make provision as to the ages by reference to which benefits under the scheme are to be paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3dd87e68937c35be4c88affc015d4bc2]
