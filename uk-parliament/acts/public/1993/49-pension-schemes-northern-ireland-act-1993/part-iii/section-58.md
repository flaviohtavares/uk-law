## 58. No recovery of state scheme premiums from earners, etc.[^c11725561]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] Notwithstanding any contract to the contrary, a person shall not be entitled— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

- (a) to recover any part of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] contributions equivalent[^c11725571] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] premium from any earner in respect of whom it is payable; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (b) except in accordance with section 57, to recover or retain any part of such a premium out of any money payable to or for the benefit of the earner or any other person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
