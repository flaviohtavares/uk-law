# Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, section 53

## 53. Elections to pay contributions equivalent premiums.[^c11724951]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] An election under section 51(2) must be made within the prescribed period and in the prescribed manner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(2)[^c-06a39db4625c80f47eed0167a205e61d][^c-7ec21a7b1507a411c5007511ca18197b] Except in such cases as may be prescribed, the prescribed person shall not, in making or abstaining from making elections under that section, discriminate between different earners on any grounds other than their respective lengths of relevant service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(3) In subsection (2) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]**relevant service *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]**” means service in contracted-out employment by reference to the scheme, together with any service in contracted-out employment which in relation to service in that employment is linked qualifying service. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(4) If *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] the Inland Revenue consider[^c11724961] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] that the prescribed person is contravening subsection (2), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] they[^c11724971] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] may cancel any contracting-out certificate held by the earner’s employer in respect of the scheme in question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(5)[^c11724981] In this section “prescribed” means prescribed by regulations made by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
