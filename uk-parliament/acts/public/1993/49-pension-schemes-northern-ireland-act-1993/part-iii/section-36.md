# Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (70).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/49
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Scope of Chapter II.

*Extent: N.I.*

This Chapter has effect for the purpose—

- (a)[^key-4904adc40003009699bd0843439b9bd7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) of providing for contributions to be paid by the Inland Revenue[^c11721281] in respect of earners who are members of money purchase contracted-out schemes and members of[^c11721291] appropriate personal pension schemes; and
- (c) of making provision concerning the payment of certain social security benefits payable in respect of members and former members of schemes that were contracted-out pension schemes[^key-e54fa83764e8f740fefb8e2526676b02].
