## 3. Issue of contracting-out ... certificates.[^c-066e0eaf5091538a998a90b9b89c6b9c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-7c5dbce9675588d63f7c8bf1feb4e49c][^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b] Regulations shall provide for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b] HMRC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b] to issue certificates stating that the employment of an earner in employed earner's employment is contracted-out employment by reference to an occupational pension scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b]

(1A) In this Act such a certificate is referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b]**a contracting-out certificate *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b]**”.

(2) The regulations shall provide for contracting-out certificates to be issued to employers and to specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b]

- (a) the employments which are to be treated, either generally or in relation to any specified description of earners, as contracted-out employments; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b]
- (b) the occupational pension schemes by reference to which those employments are to be so treated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b]

(2A)[^c11717621] The regulations may provide, in the case of contracting-out certificates issued before the principal appointed day, for their cancellation by virtue of the regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b]

- (a) at the end of a prescribed period beginning with (and including) that day, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b]
- (b) if prescribed conditions are not satisfied at any time in that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b]

but for them to continue to have effect until so cancelled; and the regulations may provide that a certificate having effect on and after that day by virtue of this subsection is to have effect, in relation to any earner’s service on or after that day, as if issued on or after that day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b]

(2B) In this Act “the principal appointed day” means the day designated by an order under Article 1 of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 as the principal appointed day for the purposes of Part IV of that Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b]

(3) An occupational pension scheme is a contracted-out scheme in relation to an earner’s employment if it is for the time being specified in a contracting-out certificate in relation to that employment; and references in this Act to the contracting-out of a scheme are references to its inclusion in such a certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b]

(4)[^c-c13268b534ab1e4d6edda921924e7b4d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c-c13268b534ab1e4d6edda921924e7b4d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c-c13268b534ab1e4d6edda921924e7b4d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) Except in prescribed circumstances, no contracting-out certificate *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b] [^c-ea5c9ae0eb2bea7b75695ffb53a94a22]... shall have effect from a date earlier than that on which the certificate is issued. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b]

(8)[^c-19ec165ddf16eb512c0636b7fc01b43e] References in this Act to a contracting-out certificate, a contracted-out scheme and to contracting-out in a context relating to a money purchase contracted-out scheme are to be construed in accordance with section 176A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f75f937b5aa137818d90b65b03de359b]
