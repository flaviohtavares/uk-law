# Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, section 30

## 30. Cancellation, variation, surrender and refusal of certificates.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-a1e0357255764880c98a7334aef55966][^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] Regulations shall provide for the cancellation, variation or surrender of a contracting-out certificate, or the issue of a new certificate— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

- (a) on any change of circumstances affecting the treatment of an employment as contracted-out employment; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]
- (b) where the certificate was issued on or after the principal appointed day, if any employer of persons in the description of employment to which the scheme in question relates, or the actuary of the scheme, fails to provide *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] HMRC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718], at prescribed intervals, with such documents as may be prescribed for the purpose of verifying that the conditions of section 5(2B) are satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

(2) Regulations may enable the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] Inland Revenue[^c11720811] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] to cancel or vary a contracting-out certificate where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

- (a) they have[^c11720831] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] reason to suppose that any employment to which it relates ought not to be treated as contracted-out employment in accordance with the certificate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]
- (b) the employer does not show that it ought to be so treated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

(3) Where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] by or by virtue of any provision of this Part the contracting-out of a scheme in relation to an employment depends on the satisfaction of a particular condition,[^key-fea33270e597dccb51344fe1734d1546] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] the continued contracting-out of the scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] [^key-7821a60798557928624fb52d5dd149c1]... shall be dependent on continued satisfaction of the condition; and if the condition ceases to be satisfied, that shall be a ground (without prejudice to any other) for the cancellation or variation of the contracting-out *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] [^key-0a1cf6744994a571bca4acd5a6fe00f0]... certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

(4) A contracting-out certificate in respect of any employment may be withheld or cancelled by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] Inland Revenue *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] they consider[^c11720851] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] that there are circumstances which make it inexpedient that the employment should be or, as the case may be, continue to be, contracted-out employment by reference to the scheme, notwithstanding that the relevant scheme is one that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] they[^c11720861] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] would otherwise treat as proper to be contracted-out in relation to all earners in that employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

(5)[^key-e9d6102e8c2d88472ca98a12e427caff] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c11720901] Without prejudice to their powers apart from this subsection, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] Inland Revenue *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] may withhold or cancel a contracting-out certificate in respect of a scheme if they consider that the rules of the scheme are such that persons over particular ages may be prevented from participating in the scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

(7)[^c11720921] Without prejudice to the preceding provisions of this section, failure of a scheme to comply with any requirements prescribed by virtue of section 21(2) shall be a ground on which the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] Inland Revenue *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] may, in respect of any employment to which the scheme relates, cancel a contracting-out certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

(8) Except in prescribed circumstances, no cancellation, variation or surrender of a contracting-out certificate *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] [^key-9d605f7133d271101502eb4ac356e231]...shall have effect from a date earlier than that on which the cancellation, variation or surrender is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

(9)[^key-a5fce72a236eda8b8e671b3ac1c099d1] A reference in this section to a contracting-out certificate does not include a reference to a contracting-out certificate issued in respect of a money purchase contracted-out scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]
