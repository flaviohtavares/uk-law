# Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, section 20D

## 20D. Survivors' benefits[^key-340218b5a8267ff4e737d298941b79ed]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac] This section specifies the benefits mentioned in Condition 4 of section 20B. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac]

(2) The first benefit is that if the earner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac] is a man married to a woman or a woman married to a woman in a relevant gender change case, and the earner[^key-156a15dd45fb9672a997b3769e3f5333] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac] dies (whether before or after attaining normal pension age) leaving a widow, she is entitled to a pension of at least half the value of the pension to which the earner would have been entitled by reference to employment during the period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac]

- (a) beginning with 6th April 1978, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac]
- (b) ending with 5th April 1997. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac]

(3) The second benefit is that if the earner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac] is a married woman (other than in a relevant gender change case), a man married to a man, or[^key-e860f7fb0b72039104a414c030237024] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac] (subject to subsection (3A))[^key-023537f1458dad9ee05c50968a7e77c4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac] a civil partner, and the earner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac] dies (whether before or after attaining normal pension age) leaving a widower *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac] , widow[^key-d21df548af9bec5f1524ef969b425c53] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac] or surviving civil partner, he or she is entitled to a pension of at least half the value of the pension to which the earner would have been entitled by reference to employment during the period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac]

- (a) beginning with 6th April 1988, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac]
- (b) ending with 5th April 1997. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac]

(3A)[^key-b341c5dc1945d664c80d8198353bb8c1] The third benefit is that if the earner is a man, or a woman in a relevant gender change case, who is a civil partner and the earner dies (whether before or after attaining normal pension age) leaving a surviving civil partner who is a woman, she is entitled to a pension of at least half the value of the pension to which the earner would have been entitled by reference to employment during the period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac]

- (a) beginning with 6th April 1978, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac]
- (b) ending with 5th April 1997. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac]

(4)[^key-6f6785769744b00d75dbc0e0b5be105e] In relation to an earner who is a woman, a reference in this section to a relevant gender change case is a reference to a case where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac]

- (a) the earner is a woman by virtue of a full gender recognition certificate having been issued under the Gender Recognition Act 2004, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac]
- (b) the marriage of the earner and her widow *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac] , or the civil partnership between the earner and her surviving civil partner,[^key-317de8abf457ed811858c7979d8dc360] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac] (that ends with the earner’s death) subsisted before the time when the certificate was issued. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac]

(5) This section is subject to regulations under section 34A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-52054aa11f32ab4422aa65ca6227d5ac]
