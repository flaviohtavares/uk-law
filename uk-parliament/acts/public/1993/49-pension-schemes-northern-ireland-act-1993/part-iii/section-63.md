## 63. Non-payment of state scheme premiums.[^c11726141]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] If a person fails to pay any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] contributions equivalent[^c11726151] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] premium which is payable by him at or within the time prescribed for the purpose, he shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine of not more than level 3 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(2) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

- (a) a person is convicted of the offence under subsection (1) of failing to pay a premium, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (b) the premium remains unpaid at the date of the conviction, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

he shall be liable to pay to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] Inland Revenue[^c11726161] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] a sum equal to the amount which he failed to pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(3) Subject to subsection (4), where a person is convicted of an offence mentioned in subsection (2), evidence may be given of any previous failure by him to pay *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] contributions equivalent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] premiums within the time prescribed for the purpose; and in that subsection “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]**the conviction *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]**” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]**the offence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]**” mean respectively the conviction referred to in this subsection and the offence of which the person is convicted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(4) Such evidence may be given only if notice of intention to give it is served with the summons or warrant on which the person appeared before the court which convicted him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
