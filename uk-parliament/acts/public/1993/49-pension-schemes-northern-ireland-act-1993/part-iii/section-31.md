## 31. Surrender and cancellation of contracting-out certificates: issue of further certificates.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] This section applies in any case where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

- (a) a contracting-out certificate (“the first certificate”) has been surrendered by an employer or cancelled by the Board; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]
- (b) at any time before the end of the period of 12 months beginning with the date of the surrender or cancellation, that or any connected employer makes an election under section 7 in respect of any employment which was specified by virtue of section 3(2)(a) in the first certificate, with a view to the issue of a further contracting-out certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

(2) This section applies whether or not the scheme specified in the first certificate in relation to the employment concerned is the same as the scheme which would be specified in the further certificate if it were issued. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

(3) The Board shall not give effect to the election referred to in subsection (1) by issuing a further certificate unless they consider that, in all the circumstances of the case, it would be reasonable to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

(4) Regulations may make such supplemental provision in relation to cases falling within this section as the Department considers necessary or expedient. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

(5) For the purposes of subsection (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

- (a) an employment (“the second employment”) in respect of which an election of the kind referred to in subsection (1)(b) has been made; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]
- (b) an employment (“the first employment”) which was specified by virtue of section 3(2)(a) in the first certificate, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

shall be treated as one employment if, in the opinion of the Board— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

- (i) they are substantially the same, however described; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718] the first employment falls wholly or partly within the description of the second employment or the second employment falls wholly or partly within the description of the first employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]

(6) Regulations shall prescribe the cases in which employers are to be treated as connected for the purposes of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-993da76d0f5d6b971a245b470e722718]
