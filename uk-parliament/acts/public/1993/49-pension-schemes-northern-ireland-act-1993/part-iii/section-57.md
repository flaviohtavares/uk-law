## 57. Deduction of contributions equivalent premium from refund of scheme contributions.[^c11725471][^c-b4145d27277f1cb5b9be8d577d390a42]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] This section applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

- (a)[^c11725481] an earner’s service in contracted-out employment ceases or his employment ceases to be contracted-out employment, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (b) he (or, by virtue of a connection with him, any other person) is entitled to a refund of any payments made by or in respect of him towards the provision of benefits under the scheme by reference to which that employment was contracted-out; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (c) a contributions equivalent premium falls to be paid by any person in respect of him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(2) Where this section applies, then, subject to the following provisions of this section, the person by whom the premium falls to be paid shall be entitled on paying it to recover an amount equal to so much of the premium as is attributable to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] any actual reductions of[^c11725491] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] primary Class 1 contributions (and on paying any part of it to recover a proportionate part of that amount) from the person liable for the refund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(3) The amount recoverable under this section shall not exceed the amount of the refund or so much of it as has not been made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(4) Where the period taken into account in arriving at the amount mentioned in subsection (2) does not coincide with that in respect of which the refund is to be made, the sum recoverable under this section shall be determined by reference to so much of that amount and of the refund as are referable to the same period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(5) Where the refund— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

- (a) is made in respect of more than one period of service, and one or more of those periods is a period of previous linked qualifying service; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (b) includes any amount paid under a contracted-out scheme in relation to that service on or in connection with a transfer of accrued rights to another scheme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

the amount which may be recovered under this section shall be increased by such amount as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(6) Where the person liable for the premium is himself liable for the refund, he shall be entitled to retain out of the refund the amount which he could recover under this section from another person liable for the refund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(7) The amount of the refund shall be reduced by the amount recovered or retained under this section; and provision shall be made by regulations for requiring the making of refunds to be delayed for the purpose of enabling any right of recovery or retainer conferred by this section to be exercised, notwithstanding anything in any enactment relating to the making of the refund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(8) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

- (a)[^c11725501] an earner’s service in contracted-out employment ceases or his employment ceases to be contracted-out employment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (b) he (or, by virtue of a connection with him, any other person) is entitled to a refund of any payments made by or in respect of him under the scheme by reference to which that employment was contracted-out in relation to any previous contracted-out employment of his, being payments towards the provision of benefits under that scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (c) a contributions equivalent premium falls to be paid in respect of him; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (d) the period taken into account in arriving at the amount mentioned in subsection (2) includes the period of the previous contracted-out employment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

then the person liable for that premium shall have the like right of recovery from that refund (so far as the premium is not recoverable or retainable out of a refund in respect of a later employment) as a person has under this section where the refund relates to service in the employment on the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] cessation[^c11725511] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] of which the premium falls to be paid (and subsection (7) shall apply accordingly). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(9) This section shall apply in relation to such a refund as is referred to in subsection (1)(b) which becomes payable after the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] cessation[^c11725521] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] of an earner’s service in contracted-out employment as it applies to such a refund becoming payable on the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] cessation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] of an earner’s service in such employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(9A)[^c-3db956f1d76bf582c173e67b62706608] Where under section 97AH the trustees or managers of an occupational pension scheme may pay a contribution refund to a member of the scheme, the member is to be treated for the purposes of this section as being entitled to the contribution refund. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(10)[^c-1e099fc0b85357441570bcb7f3b4098e][^c-edb0026766d67cac45bb7fd0d2781fce] Where the earner (or, by virtue of a connection with him, any other person) becomes entitled to any payment in lieu of benefit, this section shall apply in relation to the payment as if it were such a refund as is referred to in subsection (1)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(11) For the purposes of subsection (10), a payment in lieu of benefit is any payment falling to be made to or for the benefit of, or in respect of, a person by virtue of his being or having been a member of an occupational pension scheme, being a payment which either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

- (a) is made or to be made otherwise than out of the resources of the scheme; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (b) is made or to be made out of those resources but by way of distribution on a winding up; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (c) falls within such other description of payments as may be prescribed for the purposes of that subsection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(12)[^c11725541] In this section “prescribed” means prescribed by regulations made by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
