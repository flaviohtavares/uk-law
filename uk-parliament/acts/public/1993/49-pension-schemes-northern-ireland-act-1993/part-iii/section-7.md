# Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993, section 7

## 7. Elections as to employments covered by contracting-out certificates.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39] Subject to the provisions of this Part, an employment otherwise satisfying the conditions for inclusion in a contracting-out certificate shall be so included if and so long as the employer so elects and not otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]

(2) Subject to subsections (3) and (4), an election may be so made, and an employment so included, either generally or in relation only to a particular description of earners. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]

(3) Except in such cases as may be prescribed, an employer shall not, in making or abstaining from making any election under this section, discriminate between different earners on any grounds other than the nature of their employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]

(4) If the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39] Inland Revenue consider[^c11718251] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39] that an employer is contravening subsection (3) in relation to any scheme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39] they may[^c11718261] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]—

- (a) refuse to give effect to any election made by him in relation to that scheme; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]
- (b) cancel any contracting-out certificate held by him in respect of it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]

(5) Regulations may make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]

- (a) for regulating the manner in which an employer is to make an election with a view to the issue, variation or surrender of a contracting-out certificate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]
- (b) for requiring an employer to give a notice of his intentions in respect of making or abstaining from making any such election in relation to any existing or proposed scheme— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]
  - (i) to employees in any employment to which the scheme applies or to which it is proposed that it should apply; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39] to any independent trade union recognised to any extent for the purpose of collective bargaining in relation to those employees; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39] to the trustees and managers of the scheme; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39] to such other persons as may be prescribed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]
- (c) for requiring an employer, in connection with any such notice, to furnish such information as may be prescribed and to undertake such consultations as may be prescribed with any such trade union as is mentioned in paragraph (b)(ii); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]
- (d) for empowering the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39] Inland Revenue[^c11718271] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39] to refuse to give effect to an election made by an employer unless *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39] they are[^c11718281] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39] satisfied that he has complied with the requirements of the regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]
- (e) for referring to an industrial tribunal any question— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]
  - (i) whether an organisation is such a trade union as is mentioned in paragraph (b)(ii), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39] whether the requirements of the regulations as to consultation have been complied with. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1d9e63d4d3394d93cfad3b039e953e39]
