## 52. Provisions supplementary to s. 51.[^c11724821][^key-83f59f646988a8d0c0d8f5ae311b6a80]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c11724831][^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) A premium under section 51 shall be paid by the prescribed person to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] Inland Revenue[^c11724841] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] within the prescribed period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] [^c11724851]. . ..

(3)[^c11724831] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Where under the rules of the scheme, transfer credits have been allowed—[^key-0f10e8d8ca3806deeb4549d31e17aa96] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

- (a) in respect of the earner’s rights under another scheme, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (b) in respect of the earner by reference to the payment of a cash transfer sum (within the meaning of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] Chapter 2 of Part 4ZA[^key-5f70248c925b221daa0c9e51544c7797] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] ) to the trustees or managers of the scheme by the trustees or managers of another occupational pension scheme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

the reference in section 51(2) to employment which is contracted-out by reference to the scheme shall include references to employment in any period of linked qualifying service which was contracted-out employment by reference to the other scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(5)[^c11724871] [^c17204851]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) Subject to regulations under paragraph 1 of Schedule 1, service in any employment which ceases with the death of the employer shall be treated for the purposes of section 51(2A) as ceasing immediately before the death *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(7)[^c11724891] In this section “prescribed” means prescribed by regulations made by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(8)[^key-cf905445ffac27f6f59e49fbcad70605] Where a premium under section 51 is payable by the Board of the Pension Protection Fund by virtue of a transfer under Article 145 of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005 (effect of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] the Board *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c] assuming responsibility for an occupational pension scheme), then, subject to subsection (9), sections 51 to 64 apply with such modifications as may be prescribed in relation to that premium. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

(9) A premium under section 51 in respect of an earner ceases to be payable if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]

- (a) the liability to pay the premium is transferred to the Board of the Pension Protection Fund by virtue of Article 145 of the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 2005, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
- (b) prescribed requirements are met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-035a64139836ca39069aed480398656c]
