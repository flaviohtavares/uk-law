Section 35.

### Part I — Occupational Pension Schemes[^key-bb08b4c62d64f4ac2f8985134e1ee31b]

#### General regulations: beginning and ending of employment

**1.** (1)[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322] In relation to employments which are or at any time have been contracted-out employments, and to the operation of schemes by reference to which employments are or have been contracted-out, regulations may make provision generally as to the circumstances in which an earner’s employment is or is not to be treated as having begun, or as having come to an end and, in particular, as to the matters mentioned in sub-paragraphs (2) to (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

(2) Regulations may make provision for treating an earner’s employment which ends before a person succeeds to the business of the earner’s employer as having been employment under the employer’s successor. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

(3) Regulations may make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

- (a) for changes in an earner’s employment due to the death of an employer or another cause, or any cesser of contracted-out employment so due, to be disregarded; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]
- (b) for employment under one employer to be treated as a continuation of that under another and any contracting-out certificate issued to, or election made by, the former employer to be treated as issued to or made by the latter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

(4) Regulations may also make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

- (a) for temporary interruptions in an earner’s employment or contracted-out employment to be disregarded; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]
- (b) for the employment in either case to be treated as continuing during the interruption. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

(5) References in this paragraph to an earner’s employment beginning or ending shall include references to his employment becoming or ceasing to be contracted-out employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

**2.** (1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2), regulations may enable the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322] Inland Revenue[^c11736811] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322] to determine in prescribed circumstances that an earner, or any group of earners whose employment falls within a particular category or description of contracted-out employments, has been in such employment from a date (“the determined date”) earlier than would otherwise be the case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

(2) The determined date for any earner may not be earlier than— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

- (a) the date on which his relevant employment began, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]
- (b) the date on which a contracting-out certificate was issued in respect of it, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

whichever is the later. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

**3.** Provision may be made by regulations for requiring an employer to give notice to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322] Inland Revenue[^c11736821] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]—

- (a) when an earner’s employment becomes or ceases to be contracted-out employment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]
- (b) when an earner’s employment in contracted-out employment begins or ends. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

#### Power to modify Part III, etc.

**4.** (1) Regulations may modify the provisions of Part III (other than sections 14, 15 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322] [^c11736861]. . . , 63 and 64[^key-81729c135dc13c2845ffc4de85cef819] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]), Chapter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322] III *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322] of Part IV and Chapter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322] II *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322] of Part V (except, so far as they relate to personal pension schemes, the members of such schemes or rights in respect of them) in their application to cases in which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

- (a) a person is employed at the same time in two or more employments (whether or not under the same employer); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]
- (b) at least one but not all of those employments is contracted-out employment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

with a view to enabling the employments to be treated either separately or together for the purposes of those provisions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

(2) Regulations may also modify those provisions in their application to cases in which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

- (a) any description of benefit under a scheme is subject to a limit (however imposed) operating so as to prevent service beyond a particular length from qualifying for further benefits; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]
- (b) earners qualify for the benefits of a scheme by reference to both— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]
  - (i) service in employment which is contracted-out in relation to them by reference to the scheme; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322] service in the same employment or another employment (whether or not contracted-out employment) before the scheme was contracted-out in relation to them or their employment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

(3) Regulations under this paragraph may include provision for securing that in the cases to which they apply an earner’s employment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322] which, apart from the regulations, would not be contracted-out employment is treated as contracted-out employment where any benefits provided under the scheme are attributable to a period when the scheme was contracted-out[^c11736871] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322].

(4)[^c11736881] In this paragraph “regulations” means regulations made by the Department or, as the case may be, the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b753d9ec367dd2b3595a8dc5a49ed322]

#### . . . premiums[^c11736891]

**5.** (1) Regulations may make provision for requiring persons to furnish the Inland Revenue[^c11737121] [^M_F_2b578e60-ccbf-441a-9348-ec5507aca38c] ... with such information as the Inland Revenue [^M_F_2b578e60-ccbf-441a-9348-ec5507aca38c] ... may require for the purposes of sections 33, 46 to 59 and 155(1) to (3) and (6)[^key-8986ab9d47e95558520c40225087deb3] .

(2) Regulations may provide that for the purposes of sections 46, 51 to 56 and 59 [^c11737181]. . . (except as they so apply) the prescribed person shall be treated as the employer—

- (a) of any employed earners who, in any period of service in contracted-out employment—
  - (i) have been paid earnings in any income tax week by more than one person in respect of different employments; or
  - (ii) have worked under the general control or management of a person other than their immediate employer, or
- (b) of any other employed earners in the case of whom it appears to the Department that such provision is needed.

(3) Regulations may [^c11737191]. . . provide—

- (a) for dispensing with the payment of a premium where its amount would be inconsiderable;
- (b)[^c11737201] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) for treating part of a premium payable in prescribed circumstances in respect of a person as having been paid and for modifying the provisions mentioned in paragraph 4(1) in relation to a case in which such a part is so treated;
- (d) for treating a premium wrongly paid or an overpayment in respect of a premium as paid (wholly or in part) in discharge of a liability for another premium or for contributions under Part I of the [^c11737211]Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992;
- (e) for the return of premiums paid in error or, in prescribed circumstances, of premiums which the Inland Revenue are[^c11737221] satisfied ought to be repaid;
- (f) for the Inland Revenue, in prescribed circumstances where a premium has been paid in respect of a person, to direct the payment out of the National Insurance Fund to that person or his estate of an amount equal to a prescribed part of the premium;
- (g) for any other matters incidental to the payment, collection or return of premiums.

and in this sub-paragraph and the following provisions of this paragraph “premium” means a contributions equivalent premium,[^c11737241]

(3A)[^c11737251] Sub-paragraph (3B) applies in relation to a member of a scheme which was a contracted-out occupational pension scheme and which was being wound up before the second abolition date[^key-b9395b8a35231e64cff684f6f3d947de] if, in the opinion of the Inland Revenue[^c11737261]—

- (a) the resources of the scheme are insufficient to meet the whole of the liability for the cash equivalent of the member’s rights under the scheme, and
- (b) if the resources of the scheme are sufficient to meet a part of that liability, that part is less than the amount required for restoring his State scheme rights.

(3B) Where this sub-paragraph applies—

- (a) regulations may provide for treating the member as if sections 42 to 44 or, as the case may be, section 44A(1) did not apply, or applied only to such extent as is determined in accordance with the regulations, and
- (b) the amount required for restoring the member’s State scheme rights, or a prescribed part of that amount, shall be a debt due from the trustees or managers of the scheme to the Inland Revenue.

(3C) Regulations may make provision—

- (a) for determining the cash equivalent of a member’s rights under a scheme and the extent (if any) to which the resources of the scheme are insufficient to meet the liability for that cash equivalent,
- (b) for the recovery of any debt due under sub-paragraph (3B)(b), and
- (c) for determining the amount required for restoring a member’s State scheme rights including provision requiring the Inland Revenue to apply whichever prescribed actuarial table in force at the appropriate time is applicable.

(3D) Section 151 shall apply as if sub-paragraphs (3A) and (3B), and regulations made by virtue of those sub-paragraphs, were included among the provisions there referred to.

(3E) In sub-paragraphs (3A) and (3B), “State scheme rights”, in relation to a member of a scheme, are the rights for which, if the scheme had not been a contracted-out scheme, the member would have been eligible by virtue of section 44(6) of Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 (earnings factors for additional pension.

(4) The Inland Revenue may accept payments in connection with a case in which a premium or part of it is treated as having been paid.

(4A)[^c11737301] In this paragraph “regulations” means regulations made by the Department or, as the case may be, the Secretary of State and “prescribed” shall be construed accordingly.
