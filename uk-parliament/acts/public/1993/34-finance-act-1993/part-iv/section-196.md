## 196. Rate bands: no indexation in 1993.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Table substituted by section 72(1) of the [^c15156071]Finance (No.2) Act 1992 shall apply to chargeable transfers made in the year beginning 6th April 1993, and accordingly section 8(1) of the [^c15156081]Inheritance Tax Act 1984 (indexation of rate bands)shall not apply to such transfers.
