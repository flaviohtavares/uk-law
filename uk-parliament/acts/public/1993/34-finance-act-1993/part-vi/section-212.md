## 212. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act “**the Taxes Act 1988**” means the [^c15156431]Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 , and “[^c-737d0a2aae2c06e7ea5cefe06342842c]**ITA 2007**” means the Income Tax Act 2007.
