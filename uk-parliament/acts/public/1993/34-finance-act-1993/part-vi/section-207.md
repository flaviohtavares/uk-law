# Finance Act 1993, section 207

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-02-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/34
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 207. Stamp duty.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 50(2) of the [^c15156341]Finance Act 1973 (period of temporary statutory effect of resolution affecting stamp duties)—

- (a) in paragraph (a) (period by reference to twenty-fifth day of Commons sitting) for “twenty-fifth” there shall be substituted “ thirtieth ”;
- (b) in paragraph (d) (period by reference to five months beginning with day resolution takes effect) for “five” there shall be substituted “ six ”.

(2) This section shall apply in relation to resolutions passed after the day on which this Act is passed.
