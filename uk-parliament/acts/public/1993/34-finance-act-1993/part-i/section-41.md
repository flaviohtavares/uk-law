# Finance Act 1993, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-02-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/34

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Commencement.[^c15149951]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter shall come into force on such day as the Commissioners may by order appoint, and different days may be appointed for different provisions or for different purposes.
