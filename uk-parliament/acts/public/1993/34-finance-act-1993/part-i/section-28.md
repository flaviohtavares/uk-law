# Finance Act 1993, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-02-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/34
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. General.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Lottery duty shall be under the care and management of the Commissioners.

(2) Regulations may provide for any matter for which provision appears to the Commissioners to be necessary or expedient for the administration or enforcement of lottery duty or for the protection of the revenue derived from lottery duty.

(3) Where a person[^c15149731] contravenes or does not comply with any regulations under subsection (2) above his contravention or failure to comply shall attract a penalty under section 9 of the Finance Act 1994 (civil penalties).[^c15149741].
