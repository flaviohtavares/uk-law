> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-02-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/34
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Beer duty: rate for new regime.[^c15149061]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 36(1) of the [^c15149071]Alcoholic Liquor Duties Act 1979 (beer duty), as substituted by section 7(1) of the [^c15149081]Finance Act 1991, for “£10.60” there shall be substituted “ £10.45 ”.

(2) This section shall be deemed to have come into force on 1st June 1993.
