## 179B. Conversion to underwriting through partnership or company[^c-4435f32e692306cd7100c6b814c6ba68][^c-1c5d0a45e42ddee8c6eb7ad359259dc9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 20A to this Act (which makes provision for certain reliefs to be available where a member converts to limited liability underwriting or a Lloyd’s partnership converts to underwriting through a company[^c-a747658298cf7a1dc2ef1b594915dcb6]) shall have effect.
