# Finance Act 1993, section 55

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-02-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/34

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Relief for interest.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the year 1993-94 the qualifying maximum defined in section 367(5) of the Taxes Act 1988 (limit on relief for interest on certain loans) shall be £30,000.
