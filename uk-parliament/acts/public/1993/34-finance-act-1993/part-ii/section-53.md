# Finance Act 1993, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-02-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/34
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Charge and rate of corporation tax for 1993.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Corporation tax shall be charged for the financial year 1993 at the rate of 33 per cent.
