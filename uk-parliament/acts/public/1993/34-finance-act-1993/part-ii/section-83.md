## 83. Annual exempt amount: indexation for 1994-95 onwards.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 3(3) of the [^c15150911]Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992 (indexation of annual exempt amount) for “December” (in each place) there shall be substituted “ September ”.

(2) This section shall have effect for the year 1994-95 and subsequent years of assessment.
