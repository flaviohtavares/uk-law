> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Annual exempt amount for 1993-94.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the year 1993-94 section 3 of the [^c15150901]Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992 (annual exempt amount) shall have effect as if the amount specified in subsection (2) were £5,800, and accordingly subsection (3) of that section (indexation) shall not apply for that year.
