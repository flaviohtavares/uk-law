> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120. Pay and file: miscellaneous amendments.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 14 to this Act (which makes various amendments of the [^c15151951]Taxes Management Act 1970, the Taxes Act 1988 and the [^c15151961]Finance Act 1989 with a view to, or in connection with, the introduction of “pay and file”) shall have effect.
