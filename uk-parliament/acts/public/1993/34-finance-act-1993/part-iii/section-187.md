## 187. Returns and information.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In Schedule 2 to the principal Act (management and collection of petroleum revenue tax), other than the Table in paragraph 1 (modifications of the [^c15155991]Taxes Management Act 1970),—

- (a) for the words “an oil field”, in each place where they occur, there shall be substituted “ a taxable field ”; and
- (b) for the words “the oil field”, in each place where they occur, there shall be substituted “ the taxable field ”;

andparagraph 7 (which is superseded by the following provisions of this section) shall be omitted.
