## 5AA. Crofters: residency duty[^c-d982552fa1b68069b96ca7a863e311a3][^c-5d012823728ecdb744765d85eebfc8d4]

*Extent: S*

A crofter must be ordinarily resident on, or within 32 kilometres of, that crofter's croft.
