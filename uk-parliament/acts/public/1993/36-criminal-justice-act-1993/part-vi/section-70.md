# Criminal Justice Act 1993, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Penalties under implementation regulations.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraphs 8(3), 9(2) and 10(3) of Schedule 8 to the Banking Coordination (Second Council Directive) Regulations 1992 shall cease to have effect.

(2) [^c10498501]Regulations under section 2(2) of the European Communities Act 1972 for the purpose of implementing—

- (a)[^key-32ed3ebfab7281806261fb00f0c8c360] Articles 52 and 159 of Directive 2013/36/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013 relating to the activity of credit institutions and the prudential supervision of credit institutions and investment firms, amending Directive [2002/87/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2002/0087) and repealing Directives [2006/48/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0048) and [2006/49/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2006/0049) (which requires the United Kingdom to make provision for the exercise in the United Kingdom by supervisory authorities of other Member States of information and inspection powers in relation to institutions authorised by them), or
- (b) Articles 119(2) and (3), 122 and 124 to 126 of that Directive (which make similar provision in relation to the consolidated supervision of institutions).

may, notwithstanding paragraph 1(1)(d) of Schedule 2 to that Act, create offences punishable in the same way as offences punishable on summary conviction under section 177 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000[^c10498531].

(3) In this section—

- “**the Second Banking Co-ordination Directive**” means the [^c10498541]Community Council Directive No. [89/646/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1989/0646) on the co-ordination of laws, regulations and administrative provisions relating to the taking up and pursuit of the business of credit institutions and amending Directive [77/780/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0780); and
- “**the Supervision of Credit Institutions Directive**” means the [^c10498551]Community Council Directive No. [92/30/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1992/0030) on the supervision of credit institutions on a consolidated basis.

(4) Subsection (1) shall not affect the punishment for an offence committed before that subsection comes into force.
