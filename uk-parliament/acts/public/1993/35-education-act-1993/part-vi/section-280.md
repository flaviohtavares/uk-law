# Education Act 1993, section 280

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 280. Musical instrument tuition.[^c1400578]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

In section 106(3) of the [^c1400579]Education Reform Act 1988 (exceptions from the prohibition of charges in maintained schools) for paragraph (a) there is substituted—

> - (a) tuition in playing any musical instrument, where the tuition is provided either individually or to a group of no more than four pupils; or

.
