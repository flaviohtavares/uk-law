# Education Act 1993, section 286

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 286. Repeal of section 3(4) of the Education Act 1968.[^c1400593]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

Section 3(4) of the [^c1400594]Education Act 1968 (power of local education authority to provide, or assist in providing, temporary accommodation at voluntary school) shall cease to have effect.
