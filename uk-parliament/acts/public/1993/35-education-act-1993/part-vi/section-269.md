# Education Act 1993, section 269

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 269. Investigation by Local Commissioner of decisions of certain appeal committees.[^c1400555]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

In section 25(5) of the [^c1400556]Local Government Act 1974 (certain appeal committees to be subject to investigation by Local Commissioner)—

- (a) for “paragraph 1” there is substituted “ paragraphs 1 and 2 ”, and
- (b) at the end there is added “ or for the purposes of paragraph 5 of Schedule 6 to the Education Act 1993 ”.
