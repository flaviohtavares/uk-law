# Education Act 1993, section 250

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 250. Proceedings.[^c1400525]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

In that Schedule, there is inserted before paragraph 14—

> **13A.**
>
> (1) The chairman of the School Curriculum and Assessment Authority, or a representative of his, shall be entitled to attend and take part in deliberations (but not in decisions) at meetings of the Council or any committee of the Council.
>
> (2) The Council shall provide the chairman of the School Curriculum and Assessment Authority with such copies of any documents distributed to members of the Council or of any such committee as he may require.
