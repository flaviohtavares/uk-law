# Education Act 1993, section 296

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 296. Abolition of requirement to establish education committees.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

Section 6(2) of, and Part II of the First Schedule to, the [^c1400615]Education Act 1944 (education committees) are omitted.
