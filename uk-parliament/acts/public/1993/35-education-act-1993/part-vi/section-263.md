# Education Act 1993, section 263

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 263. Provision of information about CTCs and CCTAs.[^c1400544]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

In section 16(7) of the [^c1400545]Education (Schools) Act 1992 (power to require governing body of school or local education authority to provide information) after “grant-maintained school” there is inserted—

> - (aa) the proprietor of any city technology college or city college for the technology of the arts

.
