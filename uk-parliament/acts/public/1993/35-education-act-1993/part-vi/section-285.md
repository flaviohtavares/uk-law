# Education Act 1993, section 285

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 285. Disapplication of section 123(2) of the Local Government Act 1972.[^c1400591]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

Subsection (2) of section 123 of the [^c1400592]Local Government Act 1972 (which prohibits a local authority from making a disposal of land under that section below market value without the consent of the Secretary of State) shall not apply in the case of a disposal—

- (a) to the governors of an aided or special agreement school, or
- (b) to persons proposing to establish a school which is proposed to be maintained by a local education authority as a voluntary school and to be an aided school.
