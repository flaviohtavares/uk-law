# Education Act 1993, section 11

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Compulsory purchase of land.[^c1400116]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

In section 17(4) of the [^c1400117]Acquisition of Land Act 1981 (statutory undertakers) after paragraph (a) of the definition of “statutory undertakers” there is inserted—

> - (aa) the Funding Agency for Schools,
> - (ab) the Schools Funding Council for Wales

.
