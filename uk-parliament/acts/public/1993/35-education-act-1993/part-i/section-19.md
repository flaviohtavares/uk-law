# Education Act 1993, section 19

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Functions in respect of grants for certain expenditure due to immigrant population.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

(1) The Secretary of State may by order impose on a funding authority the function of paying on his behalf grant under section 11 of the [^c1400124]Local Government Act 1966 (grants in respect of special provision for immigrants) payable by him by virtue of section 211(1)(a) or (b) of the Education Reform Act 1988 (grant-maintained schools, CTCs and CCTAs).

(2) The payments shall be of such amounts and be paid to such persons as the Secretary of State may, in accordance with section 11 of the Local Government Act 1966, determine.
