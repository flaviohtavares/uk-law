# Education Act 1993, section 203

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 203. Exemption where child attains 5 years during term.[^c1400462][^c1400463]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

Where—

- (a) a child attains the age of five years during the school term of a grant-maintained school, and
- (b) arrangements have been made for the admission of the child to that school at the start of the next school term,

then, during the period beginning with his attaining that age and ending with the start of that next school term, section 36 of the [^c1400464]Education Act 1944 (duty of parents to secure the education of their children) and section 192 of this Act shall not apply to the child.
