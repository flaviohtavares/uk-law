# Education Act 1993, section 179

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 179. Remuneration and expenses.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

(1) The Secretary of State may pay to the President, and to any other person in respect of his service as a member of the Tribunal, such remuneration and allowances as he may, with the consent of the Treasury, determine.

(2) The Secretary of State may defray the expenses of the Tribunal to such amount as he may, with the consent of the Treasury, determine.
