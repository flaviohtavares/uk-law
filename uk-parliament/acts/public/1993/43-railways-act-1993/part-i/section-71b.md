# Railways Act 1993, section 71B

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71B. Code of practice for protection of interests of rail users who are disabled.[^c14107661]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State[^c19014711] shall—

- (a) prepare and from time to time revise, and
- (b) publish and otherwise promote the adoption and implementation of,

a code of practice for protecting the interests of users of railway passenger services or station services who are disabled

(2) In preparing or revising the code of practice, the Secretary of State shall consult the Disabled Persons Transport Advisory Committee established under section 125 of the [^c14107671]Transport Act 1985.
