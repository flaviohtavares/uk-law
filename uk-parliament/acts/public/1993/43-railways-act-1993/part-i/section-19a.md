# Railways Act 1993, section 19A

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19A. Review of access charges by the Office of Rail and Road.[^c14100081][^c-54524fb66aa8959d9b5379f30789b05c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 4A to this Act (which contains provision about the review of access charges by the Office of Rail and Road) shall have effect.
