# Railways Act 1993, section 123

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123. No person to be common carrier by railway.

*Extent: E+W+S*

No person shall be regarded as a common carrier by railway.
