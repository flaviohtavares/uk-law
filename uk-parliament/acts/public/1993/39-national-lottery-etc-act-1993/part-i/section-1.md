# National Lottery etc. Act 1993, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (31).** Text as at 2023-02-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. The National Lottery.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act “**the National Lottery**” means all the lotteries that form part of the National Lottery, taken as a whole.

(2) A lottery forms part of the National Lottery if the following conditions are satisfied.

(3) The lottery must be promoted or proposed to be promoted—

- (a) by the person[^key-4564f8498913351d93cf8ac2c0940cbb] licensed to run the National Lottery under section 5, or
- (b) in pursuance of an agreement that has been made between that person[^key-34d5f160448fba4e919a7be5a736061c] and the lottery’s promoter or proposed promoter.

(4) The promotion of the lottery must be authorised by a licence that has been granted to its promoter or proposed promoter under section 6.
