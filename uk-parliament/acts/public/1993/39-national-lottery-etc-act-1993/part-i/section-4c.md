# National Lottery etc. Act 1993, section 4C

## 4C. Wrongful disclosure[^key-aa1c35eeea700645c85067caa5c5e7c6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to a person—

- (a) who is or was an officer or employee of the Gambling[^key-fc97b000a1f77ada2907237a5f3d5105] Commission, or
- (b) who acts or acted on behalf of the Gambling[^key-4daa50d4ecf978472a4a2fcc524ad66d] Commission.

(2) A person to whom this section applies commits an offence if he discloses information received from the Commissioners for Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs in contravention of section 4B(3) and the information relates to a person whose identity—

- (a) is specified in the disclosure, or
- (b) can be deduced from it.

(3) It is a defence for a person charged with an offence under this section of disclosing information to prove that he reasonably believed—

- (a) that the disclosure was lawful, or
- (b) that the information had already and lawfully been made available to the public.

(4) A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable—

- (a) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, to a fine or to both, or
- (b) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-632f22c3d76c4086d4c916147176e45e] the general limit in a magistrates’ court *(Extent: E+W)*, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to both.

(5) In relation to a conviction occurring before 2 May 2022[^key-472a65bdc0cde8a83682eba1cec6c92f] the reference in subsection (4)(b) to 12 months *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7210d7f858204adb9b861bd4f14bee87] the general limit in a magistrates’ court *(Extent: E+W)* shall have effect as if it were a reference to six months.

(6) In the application of this section to Scotland or Northern Ireland the reference in subsection (4)(b) to 12 months shall be taken as a reference to six months.
