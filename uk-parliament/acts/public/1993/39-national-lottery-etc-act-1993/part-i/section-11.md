# National Lottery etc. Act 1993, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (31).** Text as at 2023-02-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Directions to the Director General.[^key-207aeb8a9ea70a4161a9b1a4a94fa597][^key-2d9037b936f8b86a2895c4c4666b6e79]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Director General shall in exercising his functions under sections 5 to 10A[^c1528515] comply with any directions that he may be given by the Secretary of State.

(2) Such directions may deal in particular—

- (a) with the matters that the Director General should take into account in deciding whether or not to grant licences;
- (b) with the conditions that licences should contain.
