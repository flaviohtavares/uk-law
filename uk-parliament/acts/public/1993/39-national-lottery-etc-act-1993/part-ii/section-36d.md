# National Lottery etc. Act 1993, section 36D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (31).** Text as at 2023-02-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/39

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36D. Power to give advice[^key-48f6b853886e48d3ccba40d9df792484]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Big Lottery Fund may give advice about—

- (a) the distribution of money under any provision of this Act;
- (b) inviting, making or considering applications for grants and loans under any provision of this Act;
- (c) the use of money paid under any provision of this Act.
