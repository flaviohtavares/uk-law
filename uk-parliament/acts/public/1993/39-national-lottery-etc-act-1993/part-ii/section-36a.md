# National Lottery etc. Act 1993, section 36A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (31).** Text as at 2023-02-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36A. The Fund[^key-f496eed2e7106fa4ce173f686a15fd98]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There shall be a body corporate known as the Big Lottery Fund.

(2) Schedule 4A (which makes provision in relation to the Fund) shall have effect.
