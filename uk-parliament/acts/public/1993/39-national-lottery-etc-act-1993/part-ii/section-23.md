# National Lottery etc. Act 1993, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (31).** Text as at 2023-02-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. The distributing bodies.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) So much of any sum paid into the Distribution Fund as is allocated for expenditure on or connected with the arts shall be held in the Distribution Fund—

- (a)[^c1528596] as to 69.78 per cent[^c-9686de3a7230f7703973f12c487d00b0]., for distribution by the Arts Council of England,
- (b)[^c-7c1c021ba7d14d3a9bd96f36e589e1ed] as to 8.9 per cent., for distribution by Creative Scotland,
- (c) as to 5 per cent., for distribution by the Arts Council of Wales, [^c1528599]. . .
- (d) as to 2.8 per cent., for distribution by the Arts Council of Northern Ireland[^c1528600] , and[^c-a412431bb84fdef96ca5da8347d8415f] [^c1528608]. . .[^c1528601]
- (e) as to 13.52 per cent[^c-c5e372a2a7843c55aa996df5b7b36191]., for distribution by the The British Film Institute[^c-c081c38d8c1bd9bc7b1dd8c76ac4f700] ...[^c1528609]
- (f)[^c-011b128ca71c313eba1f9671cf3d82f7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-ec276858a425ea302a9b7974f2da6839][^c-5eaa93c517e3eba7bd8e41b9f96c8586][^c-d6e2965503f04ee9563b9d8d1260598e] So much of any sum paid into the Distribution Fund as is allocated for expenditure on or connected with sport shall be held in the Distribution Fund—

- (a) as to 62 per cent[^c-199bc05fdb41a8506c2c74fa2e9044fc] , for distribution by the English Sports Council[^c1528603],
- (b) as to 8.1 per cent.[^c1528604], for distribution by the Scottish Sports Council,
- (c) as to 4.5 per cent.[^c1528605], for distribution by the Sports Council for Wales, and
- (d) as to 2.6 per cent.[^c1528606], for distribution by the Sports Council for Northern Ireland and[^c1528607]
- (e) as to 22.8” per cent[^c-4e1096882948cf897c46500b4dcaec23] ., for distribution by the United Kingdom Sports Council.

(3) So much of any sum paid into the Distribution Fund as is allocated for expenditure on or connected with the national heritage shall be held in the Distribution Fund for distribution by the Trustees of the National Heritage Memorial Fund.

(4)[^c-b9fb8ad6dddd98ee570c0139ab36f558] So much of any sum paid into the Distribution Fund as is allocated under section 22(3)(d) shall be held by the Distribution Fund for distribution by the Big Lottery Fund (established under section 36A).
