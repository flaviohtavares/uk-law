# National Lottery etc. Act 1993, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (31).** Text as at 2023-02-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Expenses.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenses of the Secretary of State or the National Debt Commissioners attributable to this Act, and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable out of money so provided under any other enactment.
