# Prisoners and Criminal Proceedings (Scotland) Act 1993, section 44

## 44. Expenses.

*Extent: S*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any sums required by the Secretary of State for defraying the expenses of the Parole Board for Scotland;
- (b) any expenses incurred by the Secretary of State under section 21(2) of this Act;
- (c) any administrative expenses incurred by the Secretary of State under this Act; and
- (d) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable out of money so provided under any other Act.
