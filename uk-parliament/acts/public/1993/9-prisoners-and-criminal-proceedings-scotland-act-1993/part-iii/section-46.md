# Prisoners and Criminal Proceedings (Scotland) Act 1993, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (38).** Text as at 2026-08-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 46. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Act—

- “[^c12928841]**the 1975 Act**” means the [^c12928851]Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1975;
- “**the 1980 Act**” means the [^c12928861]Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1980; and
- “**the 1989 Act**” means the [^c12928871]Prisons (Scotland) Act 1989.
- “[^c12928881]**the 1995 Act**” means the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995
