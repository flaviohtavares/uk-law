## 14. Supervised release of short-term prisoners.[^c22564401]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) [^c12926401]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c12926411] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c12926421] The Secretary of State shall, not later than thirty days before the date of release of a short-term prisoner who is subject to a supervised release order, designate—

- (a) the local authority for the area where the prisoner proposes to reside after release;
- (b) the local authority for the area where the place from which he is to be released is situated; or
- (c) the justices for the local justice area[^c20988891] where he proposes to reside after release,

as the appropriate authority or, as the case may be, justices for the purposes of the order.

(5)[^c12926421] As soon as practicable after designating a local authority or justices under subsection (4) above the Secretary of State shall—

- (a)[^c12926421] inform the prisoner in writing of the designation; and
- (b) send to the authority or, as the case may be, to the designated officer for[^c20988901] the justices a copy of the supervised release order and of the relevant documents and information received by the Secretary of State by virtue of section 209(6)(b)[^c12926461] of the 1995 Act[^c12926471].
