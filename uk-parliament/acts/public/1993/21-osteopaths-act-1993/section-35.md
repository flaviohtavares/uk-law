# Osteopaths Act 1993, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Rules.[^c10495391]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The approval of the Privy Council shall be required for any exercise by the General Council of a power to make rules under this Act.

(2) Any rules made by the General Council [^c-028ef4d3a38c84ba42d7d83158210444]... under this Act may make different provision with respect to different cases, or classes of case and, in particular, different provision with respect to different categories of osteopath or registered osteopath.

(3)[^c-1fb244640f65fc49958239f2c4443a67] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Nothing in any rules made under this Act shall be taken to oblige or entitle any person to act in breach of the law relating to confidentiality.
