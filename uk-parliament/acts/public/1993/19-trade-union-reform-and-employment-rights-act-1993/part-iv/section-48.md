# Trade Union Reform and Employment Rights Act 1993, section 48

> Text as at 2017-03-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act—

- “**the 1978 Act**” means the [^c1092600]Employment Protection (Consolidation) Act 1978, and
- “**the 1992 Act**” means the [^c1092601]Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992.
