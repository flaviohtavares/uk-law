# Agriculture Act 1993, section 58

> Text as at 2025-06-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Annual report on matters relevant to price support.[^c-279c3481c7251967cfb5adf773835863]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-279c3481c7251967cfb5adf773835863] The Ministers shall publish an annual report on such matters relevant to price support for agricultural produce as they consider appropriate and include in the report such account as they consider appropriate of developments in agricultural policy, so far as relevant to such matters. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-279c3481c7251967cfb5adf773835863]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-279c3481c7251967cfb5adf773835863] In subsection (1) above, the reference to agricultural policy includes policy relating to agriculture and the environment. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-279c3481c7251967cfb5adf773835863]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-279c3481c7251967cfb5adf773835863] In this section— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-279c3481c7251967cfb5adf773835863]

- “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-279c3481c7251967cfb5adf773835863]**agriculture *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-279c3481c7251967cfb5adf773835863]**” and “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-279c3481c7251967cfb5adf773835863]**agricultural *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-279c3481c7251967cfb5adf773835863]**” shall be construed in accordance with section 109(3) of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-279c3481c7251967cfb5adf773835863] [^c1092785]Agriculture Act 1947; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-279c3481c7251967cfb5adf773835863]
