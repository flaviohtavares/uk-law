# Agriculture Act 1993, section 57

> Text as at 2025-06-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. British Wool Marketing Board: power to grant relief.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of section 1157 of the Companies Act 2006[^key-e036013edd09490a24e9ebde3cd9ffa0] (power of court to grant relief in certain cases), the British Wool Marketing Board shall be treated as a company and its members shall be treated as officers of it.
