## 56 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Commercial activities of milk marketing boards: distribution of profits.[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d] It shall be deemed to be an overriding requirement of a milk marketing scheme that any distribution in respect of profits attributable to any relevant commercial activities shall be made so as not to discriminate, as between persons who are registered as producers under the scheme— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d] by reference to the identity of the person to whom milk is sold, or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d] by reference to whether milk is sold in the form of milk or in the form of a product which is wholly or partly derived from milk or which includes milk as an ingredient. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d] The powers conferred by a milk marketing scheme on the board administering the scheme shall be deemed to include whatever powers are necessary for the purpose of giving effect to the requirement under subsection (1) above. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d] For the purposes of subsection (1) above, the following are relevant commercial activities, namely— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d] the separation of milk, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d] the heat treatment of milk, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d] the retail packaging of milk, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d]
- (d (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d] the manufacture of milk products, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d]
- (e (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d] the provision of services for reward, otherwise than under the arrangements for the sale of milk to the board. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d] In that subsection, the reference to a milk marketing scheme is to a scheme having effect under— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d] the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d] [^c1092781]Agricultural Marketing Act 1958, or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d] the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d] [^c1092782]Agricultural Marketing (Northern Ireland) Order 1982, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d]

for the marketing of milk. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d]

(5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d] This section shall apply in relation to any distribution the amount of which is declared on or after the passing of this Act, irrespective of when the profits concerned were made. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-237c1cd704ca2d35657a693da4c1e36d]
