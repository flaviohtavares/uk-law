# Agriculture Act 1993, section 2

> Text as at 2025-06-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Applications for approval.[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] A milk marketing board may, at any time before 1st January 1994, apply to the appropriate authority for approval of a scheme for the reorganisation of the arrangements relating to the marketing of milk in its area (“ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]**scheme of reorganisation *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]**”). *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] An application under this section shall include— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] a copy of the scheme to which the application relates, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] a statement of the applicant’s reasons for believing that the scheme is one which ought to be approved, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] a statement, in relation to each successor body proposed to be engaged in milk trading, of the practices proposed to be adopted by it with respect to such trading, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (d (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] a statement of the applicant’s reasons for believing that those practices satisfy section 3(2)(a)(iii) below. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Subject to subsection (4) below, an application under this section may be amended or withdrawn at any time before the appropriate authority has finally determined it. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] An application under this section may not be amended at any time after the authority has given the board notice under section 4(2) or (3) below. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Where a board has made an application under this section, it may not make a further such application until the previous application has been finally determined or withdrawn. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(6 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] No application under this section may be made by a board which has obtained approval of a scheme of reorganisation under this Part of this Act. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(7 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] The appropriate authority may by order extend the period for making applications under this section. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(8 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] In subsection (2)(c) above, “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]**successor body *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]**” means a body to which property, rights or liabilities of a milk marketing board are, under the scheme to which the application relates, proposed to be transferred under section 11 below. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
