# Agriculture Act 1993, section 9

> Text as at 2025-06-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 9 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Functions of the relevant board.[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] The relevant board may do whatever is necessary for, or conducive to, the carrying out of an approved scheme in the period up to and including the vesting day under the scheme. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] In this section, “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]**the relevant board *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]**”, in relation to an approved scheme, means the board to which the scheme relates. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
