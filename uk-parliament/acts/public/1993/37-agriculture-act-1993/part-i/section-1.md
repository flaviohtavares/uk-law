# Agriculture Act 1993, section 1

> Text as at 2025-06-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Revocation of schemes.[^c1092745][^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Each of the milk marketing schemes having effect under the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] [^c1092746]Agricultural Marketing Act 1958, namely— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] the Milk Marketing Scheme 1933, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] the North of Scotland Milk Marketing Scheme 1934, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] the Aberdeen and District Milk Marketing Scheme 1984, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (d (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] the Scottish Milk Marketing Scheme 1989, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

is hereby revoked. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Subject to subsections (3) and (4) below, subsection (1) above shall come into force— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c1092747][^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] in relation to any of the schemes mentioned in subsection (1) above, other than the scheme mentioned in paragraph (a) of that subsection, on 1st April 1994, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c1092748][^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] in relation to the scheme mentioned in that paragraph, on 1st October 1994. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] The appropriate authority may by order provide that paragraph (a) or (b) of subsection (2) above shall have effect with the substitution for the date mentioned in that paragraph of such later date before 1st January 1996 as may be specified in the order. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Where property, rights or liabilities of a milk marketing board are transferred under section 11 below (statutory transfer on vesting day under approved scheme of reorganisation)— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] subsection (2) above shall not apply in relation to the scheme administered by the board, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] subsection (1) above shall come into force, in relation to that scheme, on the day of the transfer. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Where subsection (4) above applies, the appropriate authority shall by order certify the fact and date of its application. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
