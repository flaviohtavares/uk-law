# Agriculture Act 1993, section 17

> Text as at 2025-06-16.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Levies.[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] This section applies where— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] property, rights or liabilities of a milk marketing board are transferred under section 11 above in accordance with an approved scheme, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] under the scheme, any liabilities of the board are excepted from transfer under that section. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] The appropriate authority may, on the application of the board, by order give effect to a scheme enabling the board to require eligible producers to make contributions for the purpose of enabling it to meet its liabilities. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] The appropriate authority shall not make an order under subsection (2) above unless it is satisfied— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] that the board’s assets are insufficient to meet its liabilities; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] that the board has taken all reasonable steps to minimise the amount required to meet its liabilities; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] that there is no other reasonably practicable way of discharging its liabilities; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (d (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] that the amount proposed to be raised under the scheme is reasonable; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (e (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] that the basis on which contributions are to be assessed under the scheme is reasonable. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] In subsection (2) above, the reference to eligible producers is to the persons who, under the approved scheme, are entitled to participate in the distribution of assets of the board by virtue of their being, or having been, registered producers. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
