# Agriculture Act 1993, section 13

> Text as at 2025-06-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). General.[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The board administering a milk marketing scheme shall not be deemed to be dissolved by reason of the revocation of the scheme by section 1(1) above and so much of the scheme as relates to the winding up of the board shall (subject to any provision of regulations under section 14(2) below) continue in force notwithstanding the revocation. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
