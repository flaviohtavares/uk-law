# Agriculture Act 1993, section 14

> Text as at 2025-06-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Position following reorganisation.[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Subsections (2) to (5) below apply where property, rights or liabilities of a milk marketing board are transferred under section 11 above under an approved scheme. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] The appropriate authority— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] shall by regulations make such provision as it thinks fit for the purpose of giving effect to so much of the approved scheme as relates to the board in the period after the transfer under that section; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] may by regulations make such provision as it thinks fit— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
  - (i (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] in relation to the constitution of the board, or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
  - (ii (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] for the purpose of enabling the board to wind up its affairs. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Regulations under subsection (2) above may— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] provide that, notwithstanding section 1(1) above, such provisions of the relevant marketing scheme as are specified in the regulations shall continue to have effect, subject to such modifications as may be so specified, or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] make such new provision as appears to the authority to be necessary or expedient. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Regulations under subsection (2) above may not confer a power to raise compulsory levies. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] As soon as the appropriate authority is satisfied that the board has carried out any remaining functions, and on being given notice by the board that it has wound up its affairs, the appropriate authority shall, after consultation with the board, make an order dissolving the board on such date as is specified in the order. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(6 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Regulations under subsection (2) above may be made in anticipation of the application of that subsection. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(7 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] In subsection (3)(a) above, “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]**relevant marketing scheme *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]**” means the marketing scheme which constituted the board. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
