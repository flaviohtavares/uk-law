# Agriculture Act 1993, section 18

> Text as at 2025-06-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Power to carry out preparatory work.[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] The functions of a milk marketing board shall be deemed always to have included the function of preparing for the enactment of this Part of this Act. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] In this section, “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]**milk marketing board *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]**” includes the board established under the Milk Marketing Scheme (Northern Ireland) 1989; and, in the application of this section to that board, the reference to the enactment of this Part of this Act shall be construed as a reference to the making of Northern Ireland legislation corresponding to this Part. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
