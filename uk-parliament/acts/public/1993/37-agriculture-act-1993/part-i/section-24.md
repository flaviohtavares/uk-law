## 24 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Interpretation of Part I.[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] In this Part of this Act— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]

- “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]**approved scheme *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]**” means a scheme of reorganisation in relation to which an application under section 2 above, but no application under section 6 above, has been granted (with any variations approved under section 5 above); *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]
- “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]**milk marketing board *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]**” means the board administering a scheme having effect under the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] [^c1092760]Agricultural Marketing Act 1958 for the marketing of milk; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]
- “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]**notice *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]**” means notice in writing; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]
- “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]**registered producers *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]**”, in relation to a milk marketing board, means persons registered as producers under the marketing scheme administered by the board; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]
- “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]**scheme of reorganisation *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]**” has the meaning given by section 2(1) above; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]
- “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]**subsidiary *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]**” has the same meaning as in the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] [^c1092761]Companies Act 1985. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] In this Part of this Act, references to the appropriate authority are— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] in the case of a milk marketing board whose area is in England and Wales, to the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, and, in relation to things done by the appropriate authority, to those ministers acting jointly, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] in the case of a milk marketing board whose area is in Scotland, to the Secretary of State. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]
