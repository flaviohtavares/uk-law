# Agriculture Act 1993, section 6

> Text as at 2025-06-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Withdrawal of approval.[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] The appropriate authority may, on the application of the relevant board before the vesting day under an approved scheme, withdraw the scheme’s approval if it is satisfied that there has been a material change of circumstances since the scheme was approved. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] The appropriate authority shall give an applicant under subsection (1) above notice of its decision in relation to the application. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Where approval in relation to a scheme is withdrawn under subsection (1) above, it shall cease to be an approved scheme with effect from the date of the notice under subsection (2) above. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Where a scheme ceases under this section to be an approved scheme, it shall be disregarded for the purposes of section 2(6) above. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Where the period within which an application under section 2 above may be made has expired before the date on which a scheme ceases under this section to be an approved scheme, the milk marketing board concerned may, subject to subsection (6) below, make an application under that section at any time before the end of the period of three months beginning with that date. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(6 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] A milk marketing board may not make an application by virtue of subsection (5) above within the period of 3 months immediately preceding the day on which the marketing scheme administered by the board will, under subsection (2) of section 1 above, be revoked by subsection (1) of that section. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(7 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] In this section, “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]**the relevant board *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]**”, in relation to an approved scheme, means the milk marketing board to which the scheme relates. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
