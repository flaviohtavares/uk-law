# Agriculture Act 1993, section 22

> Text as at 2025-06-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 22 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Service of documents.[^c1092756][^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] Any document required or authorised under this Part of this Act to be served on any person may be served— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] by delivering it to him or by leaving it at his proper address or by sending it by post to him at that address, or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] if the person is a body corporate, by serving it in accordance with paragraph (a) above on the secretary of that body, or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] if the person is a partnership, by serving it in accordance with paragraph (a) above on a partner or a person having control or management of the partnership business. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] For the purposes of this section and section 7 of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] [^c1092757]Interpretation Act 1978 (which relates to the service of documents by post) in its application to this section, the proper address of any person on whom a document is to be served shall be his last known address, except that— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] in the case of service on a body corporate or its secretary, it shall be the address of the registered or principal office of the body; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] in the case of service on a partnership or a partner or a person having the control or management of a partnership business, it shall be the address of the principal office of the partnership; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]

and for the purposes of this subsection the principal office of a company registered outside the United Kingdom or of a partnership carrying on business outside the United Kingdom is its principal office within the United Kingdom. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] If a person to be served under this Part of this Act with any document by another has specified to that other an address within the United Kingdom other than his proper address (as determined under subsection (2) above) as the one at which he or someone on his behalf will accept documents of the same description as that document, that address shall also be treated as his proper address for the purposes of this section and for the purposes of section 7 of the Interpretation Act 1978 in its application to this section. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] In this section “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]**secretary *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]**”, in relation to a local authority within the meaning of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] [^c1092758]Local Government Act 1972 or the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] [^c1092759]Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973, means the proper officer within the meaning of that Act. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]
