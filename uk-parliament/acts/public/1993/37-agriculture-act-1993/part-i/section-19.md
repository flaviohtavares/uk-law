# Agriculture Act 1993, section 19

> Text as at 2025-06-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Overriding nature of functions under Part I.[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Nothing done in pursuance of this Part of this Act by a milk marketing board, or by any member or officer of such a board, shall be taken to constitute a breach of any duty owed, apart from the provisions of this Part of this Act, to persons who are registered producers. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
