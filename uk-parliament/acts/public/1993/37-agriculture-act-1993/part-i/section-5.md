# Agriculture Act 1993, section 5

> Text as at 2025-06-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Variation of approved scheme.[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Subject to subsection (2) below, the appropriate authority may approve a variation of an approved scheme on the application of the relevant board made before the vesting day under the scheme. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] The appropriate authority shall not approve a variation of an approved scheme unless— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] it is satisfied— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
  - (i (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] that the relevant board has taken reasonable steps to bring the principle of the proposed variation to the attention of persons who are registered producers, or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
  - (ii (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] that the proposed variation is not sufficiently important to require the principle of it to be brought to their attention; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] it is satisfied that its decisions under section 3(2)(a)(ii) and (3)(a) above would not have been different had the scheme included the proposed variation. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] In this section, “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]**the relevant board *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]**”, in relation to an approved scheme, means the milk marketing board to which the scheme relates. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
