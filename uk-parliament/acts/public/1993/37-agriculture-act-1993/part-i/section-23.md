# Agriculture Act 1993, section 23

> Text as at 2025-06-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Consequential amendments.[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] The relevant authority may by order make such modifications of any provision contained in any Act passed, or subordinate legislation made, before the relevant day as appear to it to be necessary or expedient in consequence of the coming into force of section 1(1) above. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] In this section, the reference to the relevant authority is— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] in the case of any modification consequential on the coming into force of section 1(1) above in relation to the England and Wales Milk Marketing Scheme, to the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State for Wales acting jointly, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] in the case of any modification consequential on the coming into force of section 1(1) above in relation to any one or more of the other milk marketing schemes mentioned in that provision, to the Secretary of State, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] in the case of any modification consequential on the coming into force of section 1(1) above in relation to all the milk marketing schemes mentioned in that provision, to the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food, the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales acting jointly. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] For the purposes of this section, the relevant day, in relation to a consequential modification, is— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] in the case of a modification which is consequential on the revocation of one milk marketing scheme, the day on which section 1(1) above comes into force in relation to that scheme, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563] in the case of a modification which is consequential on the revocation of more than one milk marketing scheme, the day on which section 1(1) above comes into force completely in relation to those schemes. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ecd71e0e5bf68b0a19e90be64058d563]
