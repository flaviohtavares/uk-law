# Agriculture Act 1993, section 7

> Text as at 2025-06-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Information.[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] The authority to which an application under section 2, 5 or 6 above is made may by notice require any person to supply to it such information as may be specified in the notice, being information the supply of which the authority considers necessary or desirable for the purpose of enabling it to carry out its functions in relation to the application. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] A notice under subsection (1) above shall require the information to be supplied within such period as may be specified in the notice, being not less than 21 days from the date of the notice. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Where the authority to which an application under section 2, 5 or 6 above is made gives a notice under subsection (1) above to the applicant, the applicant shall be treated as having withdrawn the application unless— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] it complies with the notice, or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] before the end of the period allowed for compliance, it shows to the satisfaction of the authority that it has reasonable grounds for not complying with it. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Where— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] the authority to which an application under section 2, 5 or 6 above is made gives a notice under subsection (1) above to the applicant, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] the applicant purports to comply with the notice, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] it becomes apparent to the authority after the time allowed for compliance with the notice and before the application has been finally determined that the applicant has not in fact complied with it, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

the authority shall give the applicant notice of that fact. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Where, within 14 days of the date of a notice under subsection (4) above, the person to whom the notice is given shows to the satisfaction of the authority by which it is given that the failure to comply with the notice under subsection (1) above was accidental and not attributable to a failure to take reasonable care, subsection (3) above shall be treated as never having had any application in relation to it. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(6 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] If any person other than the applicant under section 2, 5 or 6 above fails without reasonable excuse to comply with a notice under subsection (1) above, he shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(8 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Where an application under section 2, 5 or 6 above is made to the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and the Secretary of State, the powers conferred by this section shall be exercisable by those ministers acting jointly. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
