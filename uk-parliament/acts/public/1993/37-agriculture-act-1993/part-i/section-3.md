## 3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Determination of applications.[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] This section applies where a milk marketing board makes an application under section 2 above for approval of a scheme of reorganisation. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] The authority to which the application is made shall not grant the application unless— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] it is satisfied— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
  - (i (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] that the board has taken reasonable steps to bring the principles of the scheme to the attention of persons who are registered producers, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
  - (ii (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] that the scheme is a qualifying scheme under Schedule 1 to this Act, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
  - (iii (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] that the practices contained in the statement mentioned in section 2(2)(c) above take account of the interests of purchasers of milk; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] it has consulted about the principles of the scheme such persons appearing to it to be representative of the interests of producers, purchasers, retailers and consumers of milk as it considers appropriate. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] Subject to that— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] if the authority is satisfied that the scheme is one which ought to be approved, it shall grant the application, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] if it is not so satisfied, section 4 below shall apply. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] In determining for the purposes of subsection (3) above whether the scheme ought to be approved, the authority shall have regard to all the circumstances and, in particular, to— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] whether the scheme takes account of the interests of consumers of milk and producers of milk; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] whether the scheme makes reasonable provision for the distribution of assets to persons by reference to their being, or having been, registered producers; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] whether it is unlikely that any person to whom a liability is transferred under the scheme will be unable to meet it; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (d (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] whether it is unlikely that the board will be unable to meet retained liabilities out of retained assets; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (e (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] whether the structure of the new arrangements contemplated by the scheme is such as to allow for the development of competition in milk marketing. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] The scheme shall not be taken to be unreasonable in its treatment of the distribution of assets to persons by reference to their being, or having been, registered producers by virtue only of the fact that it— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] specifies a date by reference to which any such distribution is to be made, or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] provides for any such distribution to be made by reference to the occurrence in a specified period (being a period of at least a year) of any relevant matter, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

provided the specified date or, as the case may be, the end of the specified period, is not earlier than 31st March 1993 and not later than the vesting day under the scheme. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(6 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] For the purposes of subsection (5) above, the following are relevant matters— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] the production of milk, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] the sale of milk by the person responsible for producing it. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]

(7 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816] For the purposes of subsection (6)(b) above, milk shall be treated as sold if it is sold in the form of milk or in the form of a product which is wholly or partly derived from milk or which includes milk as an ingredient. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d93d7d502aa9a02b20a8ce255f66816]
