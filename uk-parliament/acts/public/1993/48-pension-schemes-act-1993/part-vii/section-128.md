## 128. Priority in bankruptcy etc.[^c-36c0be76be1b6fd5cec0810cc40b7b90]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 4 shall have effect for the purposes of paragraph 8 of Schedule 6 to the [^c21360851]Insolvency Act 1986 and paragraph 1 of schedule 3 to the Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 2016[^c-bba6461c771913d39eab7deac059c0c4] (by virtue of which sums to which Schedule 4 to this Act applies are preferential or, as the case may be, preferred debts in cases of insolvency).
