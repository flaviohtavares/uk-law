## 24D. Survivors' benefits[^c-37a8d63c6658577dbdfc850f76f1aa0a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda] This section specifies the benefits mentioned in Condition 4 of section 24B. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda]

(2) The first benefit is that if the earner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda] is a man married to a woman or a woman married to a woman in a relevant gender change case, and the earner[^M_F_80966814-efa9-4ec3-b817-488b314afd54] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda] dies (whether before or after attaining normal pension age) leaving a widow, she is entitled to a pension of at least half the value of the pension to which the earner would have been entitled by reference to employment during the period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda]

- (a) beginning with 6th April 1978, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda]
- (b) ending with 5th April 1997. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda]

(3) The second benefit is that if the earner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda] is a married woman (other than in a relevant gender change case), a man married to a man, or a civil partner, and the earner[^M_F_61e9bc7b-969a-47b8-fa44-2e9f8ee35524] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda] dies (whether before or after attaining normal pension age) leaving a widower *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda] , widow[^M_F_49f75ba8-f7bf-495b-815b-5cb47d37e8eb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda] or surviving civil partner, he or she is entitled to a pension of at least half the value of the pension to which the earner would have been entitled by reference to employment during the period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda]

- (a) beginning with 6th April 1988, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda]
- (b) ending with 5th April 1997. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda]

(4)[^M_F_2a979f03-c6b4-4a73-ebe4-fb0e15e84722] In relation to an earner who is a woman, a reference in this section to a relevant gender change case is a reference to a case where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda]

- (a) the earner is a woman by virtue of a full gender recognition certificate having been issued under the Gender Recognition Act 2004, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda]
- (b) the marriage of the earner and her widow (that ends with the earner's death) subsisted before the time when the certificate was issued. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda]

(5) This section is subject to regulations under section 38A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-71596806a8ac7c16e5b867c90d2febda]
