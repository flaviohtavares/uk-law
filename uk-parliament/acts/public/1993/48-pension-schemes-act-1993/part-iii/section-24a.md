## 24A. Conversion of guaranteed minimum pension into other benefits: introduction[^key-37a8d63c6658577dbdfc850f76f1aa0a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-c189b4e7876e2ac4180dc7e63fc53c8a] In this section and sections 24B to 24H—

- (a) the rules specified in sections 13(1)(a) and (b) and 17(1) are referred to as the “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-531295bb7c93aaaada685b55fb8b5baf]**guaranteed minimum pension rules *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-531295bb7c93aaaada685b55fb8b5baf]**”,
- (b) “[^key-6e197fa8aee3ddc40716874915708e86]**GMP conversion *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6e197fa8aee3ddc40716874915708e86]**” means amendment of the scheme in relation to an earner so that it no longer contains the guaranteed minimum pension rules, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6e197fa8aee3ddc40716874915708e86]

  “**GMP conversion**” means—

  - (i) the amendment of a scheme in relation to an earner who was alive immediately before the conversion date so that it no longer contains the rules specified in sections 13(1)(a) and (b) and 17(1), or
  - (ii) the amendment of a scheme in relation to a person who, immediately before the conversion date, was the widow, widower or surviving civil partner of an earner so that it no longer contains the rules specified in section 17(1),
- (ba)[^key-d337204a5e23b353b7e936cc1bef2c32] “**P**” means—
  - (i) in relation to a GMP conversion within paragraph (b)(i), the earner mentioned in that provision;
  - (ii) in relation to a GMP conversion within paragraph (b)(ii), the survivor mentioned in that provision,
- (c) a “GMP-converted scheme” is a scheme which has been subject to GMP conversion, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d7053fa20b89402f9ebd4761f3d84800]
- (d) “**the conversion date**” means the date on which that amendment takes effect,
- (e) “**the pre-conversion benefits**” means the benefits provided under the scheme in relation to P[^key-809a12a4f48ac2a35e9c9daefdf07cd2] immediately before the conversion date (disregarding money purchase benefits) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ed5b736e6dc0b95f808fc825d24956a6],
- (f) “**the post-conversion benefits**” means the benefits which are provided under the converted scheme in relation to P[^key-b28f15d8717d4e2dcba8484244b3e98c] (disregarding money purchase benefits) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9d8e168378559027ff51aaf65b119b46],
- (g) “**the converted scheme**” means the scheme as it has effect immediately after conversion, and
- (h) “**the trustees**” in relation to a scheme means the trustees, managers or other persons responsible under the scheme for effecting amendments of it.

(2)[^key-ae2f96e895ce65ba49ed82b660efbb0b] The Secretary of State must give such guidance (if any) as he or she thinks appropriate about GMP conversion.
