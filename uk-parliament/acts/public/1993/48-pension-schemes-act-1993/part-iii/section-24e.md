# Pension Schemes Act 1993, section 24E

## 24E. Procedural requirements[^key-37a8d63c6658577dbdfc850f76f1aa0a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This section specifies the procedural requirements that must be complied with in order to satisfy Condition 5 of section 24B.

(2) The employer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-98d8a50d635375cf6eb681d46fa885d3]Each relevant person (if any) in relation to the scheme must consent to the GMP conversion in advance.

(2A)[^key-460fb10c8f5d3ca775e18aea00053654] For the purposes of subsection (2) a person is “**relevant**” in relation to a scheme if such conditions as may be prescribed are met in relation to the person and the scheme.

(3) The trustees must take all reasonable steps to—

- (a) consult the earner *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6d741c06fd92a4a61bd0f546ee292f67]P in advance, and
- (b) notify all members, and survivors, affected by the GMP conversion before, or as soon as is reasonably practicable after, the conversion date.

(4) The Commissioners for Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs must be notified on or before the conversion date— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ec841ebbeee4fda37ef0c6c4a849dcf5]

- (a) that the GMP conversion will occur or has occurred, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ec841ebbeee4fda37ef0c6c4a849dcf5]
- (b) that it affects the earner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ec841ebbeee4fda37ef0c6c4a849dcf5]
