# Pension Schemes Act 1993, section 17

## 17. Minimum pensions for widows and widowers.[^c-36c0be76be1b6fd5cec0810cc40b7b90][^M_E_554e4b32-34ef-43f4-94c2-0df33e2332b6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Part, the scheme must provide that if the earner dies leaving a widow, widower or surviving civil partner[^c-adb924f5d184a34f78a002370b104f65] (whether before or after attaining pensionable age), the widow, widower or surviving civil partner will be entitled to a guaranteed minimum pension under the scheme.

(1A)[^c-069e1ec6ac5c6bab8f6fcc7cb7b1e4ca] But a scheme may be amended so as to omit provision of the kind specified in subsection (1) if the conditions specified in section 24B are satisfied.

(2) The scheme must contain a rule to the effect that—

- (a) if the earner is a man , or a woman in a relevant gender change case,[^c-36580737a7008034b964f53970aa8505] who has a guaranteed minimum under section 14, the weekly rate of the widow’s pension will be not less than the widow’s guaranteed minimum;
- (b) if the earner is a woman who has a guaranteed minimum under that section, the weekly rate of the widower’s pension will be not less than the widower’s guaranteed minimum;
- (c)[^c-8408c1b082331b6c70e99dc674d628e2] if the earner is a person who has a guaranteed minimum under that section, the weekly rate of the surviving civil partner’s pension will not be less than the surviving civil partner’s guaranteed minimum;
- (d)[^c-2b461078c15d5251fd09bb8ddfee2e36] if the earner is a man who has a guaranteed minimum under that section, the weekly rate of the widower's pension will not be less than the surviving same sex spouse's guaranteed minimum;
- (e) if the earner is a woman (other than in a relevant gender change case) who has a guaranteed minimum under that section, the weekly rate of the widow's pension will not be less than the surviving same sex spouse's guaranteed minimum.

(3) The widow’s guaranteed minimum shall be half that of the earner.

(4) The widower’s or surviving civil partner's[^c-8756949c1f655e906e4d7420fcb07549] or surviving same sex spouse's[^c-016eafde78c297ddecfc036eb32183c0] guaranteed minimum shall be one-half of that part of the earner’s guaranteed minimum which is attributable to earnings factors for the tax year 1988-89 and subsequent tax years.

(4A)[^c-bcbc295c925919b8f09e63ab09a1d784] Subject to subsection (4B)[^c-d9afa99e28569f2a9acf73dc0fc04ad3] the scheme must provide for the widow's, widower’s or surviving civil partner's[^c-e5b409974386319f0f4f5677825b8d10] pension to be payable to the widow, widower or surviving civil partner[^c-5d9c05cc2f421dd836e9cae5ded45471]—

- (a) for any period for which a Category B retirement pension is payable to the widow, widower or surviving civil partner by virtue of the earner’s contributions or would be so payable but for section 43(1) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 (persons entitled to more than one retirement pension);
- (aa)[^M_F_9f1f5279-d14f-49c6-952e-6cb1ee7f3c79] for life, in a case where—
  - (i) the widow, widower or surviving civil partner attained state pension age on or after the second abolition date, and
  - (ii) the earner died on or after the date on which the widow, widower or surviving civil partner attained state pension age;
- (ab) for any period after the widow, widower or surviving civil partner has attained state pension age, in a case where—
  - (i) the earner died before the widow, widower or surviving civil partner attained state pension age,
  - (ii) the widow, widower or surviving civil partner did not marry or form a civil partnership after the death and before attaining state pension age, and
  - (iii) the widow, widower or surviving civil partner attained state pension age on or after the second abolition date;
- (b) for any period for which widowed parent’s allowance or bereavement allowance is payable to the widow, widower or surviving civil partner by virtue of the earner’s contributions; and
- (c) in the case of a widow, widower or surviving civil partner whose entitlement by virtue of the earner’s contributions to a widowed parent’s allowance or bereavement allowance has come to an end at a time after the widow, widower or surviving civil partner attained the age of 45, for so much of the period beginning with the time when the entitlement came to an end as neither—
  - (i)[^c-af5878e3e0cf9080cdafac4ea0f116c6] comprises a period during which the widow, widower or surviving civil partner and another person are living together[^c-e840b0d0edb3a0a8ad5a55223f85e90d] as if they were a married couple or civil partners[^c-c9269cf225640879dbfdfb8ef805a10b]; nor
  - (ii) falls after the time of any—
    - (a) marriage; or
    - (b) formation of a civil partnership,

    by the widow or widower or surviving civil partner which takes place after the earner’s death.

(4B)[^c-e9f62764ba2525662e547eb61c12ad83] Sub-paragraphs (i)(b) and (ii)(b) of subsection (4A)(c) do not apply where the earner dies before 5th December 2005.

(5) In the case of a woman who is the widow of a man,[^c-4e0cd4ce68562be27e27e0b211e2f923] the scheme must also make provision[^c-1666f93579347163d1b847350aadb894] for the widow’s pension to be payable to her for any period for which a [^c-00727ca3a61ebfda042e068e893e9401]... widowed mother’s allowance or widow’s pension is payable to her by virtue of the earner’s contributions[^c-00727ca3a61ebfda042e068e893e9401]....

(6) In any other case,[^c-03eb14338f63e821073c5f29a5611a84] the scheme must also make provision[^c-d19667bb393a95503ff6b82adef699ec] for the widower’s[^c-74b04c9e930e7b483c7b52dd8670c629] , widow's[^c-3b38f103a5290f7d901dd661eb8d6510] or surviving civil partner’s pension to be payable in the prescribed circumstances and for the prescribed period.

(7) The trustees or managers of the scheme shall supply to the Inland Revenue[^c-dd2e0e2534e833f3cd1ff340903d0915] any such information as the Inland Revenue may require relating to the payment of pensions under the scheme to widows, widowers or surviving civil partners[^c-b45b8f52827d1f6406c80d16d0ffd4b6].

(8)[^c-e9c4ec7c5dab740637f3c9c4cbc35ff1] Where—

- (a) a lump sum is paid to an earner under provisions included in a scheme by virtue of section 21(1), and
- (b) those provisions are of a prescribed description,

the earner shall be treated for the purposes of this section as having any guaranteed minimum under section 14 that he would have had but for that payment.

(9)[^c-50e9dbc3aa4894ed0ba7bec0419637a7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9A)[^M_F_24c8f591-d5c5-44e6-c073-2e167beeb9b6] For the purposes of subsection (4A)(aa) and (ab) a person attains state pension age when he or she attains pensionable age within the meaning given by the rules in paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Pensions Act 1995.

(10)[^c-a07dac588b09910c6e0fee8bd774d743] In relation to an earner who is a woman, a reference in this section to a relevant gender change case is a reference to a case where—

- (a) the earner is a woman by virtue of a full gender recognition certificate having been issued under the Gender Recognition Act 2004, and
- (b) the marriage of the earner and her widow (that ends with the earner's death) subsisted before the time when the certificate was issued.

(11)[^c-8c0c2fbf4f136de1086467c088d77574] This section is subject to regulations under section 38A.

## 17. Minimum pensions for widows and widowers.[^V_S_0bd2d718-b98a-9add-4266-70f3ca923ba6][^M_E_01b344ee-0c22-4d6e-89a6-3896d32edec0]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Part, the scheme must provide that if the earner dies leaving a widow, widower or surviving civil partner[^V_S_9bd8e860-57d4-113b-4a7f-cfc19dabe47f] (whether before or after attaining pensionable age), the widow, widower or surviving civil partner will be entitled to a guaranteed minimum pension under the scheme.

(1A)[^V_S_5d0fb40c-77f1-443c-1a26-4370c0bbd579] But a scheme may be amended so as to omit provision of the kind specified in subsection (1) if the conditions specified in section 24B are satisfied.

(2) The scheme must contain a rule to the effect that—

- (a) if the earner is a man , or a woman in a relevant gender change case,[^c-f214f44bc33a3c15fc0cbb7184a20ba9] who has a guaranteed minimum under section 14, the weekly rate of the widow’s pension will be not less than the widow’s guaranteed minimum;
- (b) if the earner is a woman who has a guaranteed minimum under that section, the weekly rate of the widower’s pension will be not less than the widower’s guaranteed minimum;
- (c)[^V_S_92b40807-4488-fd24-1ff8-4a0ba564a76a] if the earner is a person who has a guaranteed minimum under that section, the weekly rate of the surviving civil partner’s pension will not be less than the surviving civil partner’s guaranteed minimum;
- (d)[^c-ffe0e3363443b68bb7658a3f7a2039e6] if the earner is a man who has a guaranteed minimum under that section, the weekly rate of the widower’s pension will not be less than the surviving same sex spouse’s guaranteed minimum;
- (e) if the earner is a woman (other than in a relevant gender change case) who has a guaranteed minimum under that section, the weekly rate of the widow’s pension will not be less than the surviving same sex spouse’s guaranteed minimum.

(3) The widow’s guaranteed minimum shall be half that of the earner.

(4) The widower’s or surviving civil partner's[^V_S_9719155a-3dcd-2442-a274-e2642f75bbab] or surviving same sex spouse’s[^c-57f518c27b1c2afcee9966160853d54f] guaranteed minimum shall be one-half of that part of the earner’s guaranteed minimum which is attributable to earnings factors for the tax year 1988-89 and subsequent tax years.

(4A)[^V_S_fcb12f46-a32b-bc87-3c9d-629400227aa6] Subject to subsection (4B)[^V_S_7dd1db5d-4136-88c6-0e9d-b6edeabd166a] the scheme must provide for the widow's, widower’s or surviving civil partner's[^V_S_c7041f3b-f586-0999-4d9a-f4fc6089e07d] pension to be payable to the widow, widower or surviving civil partner[^V_S_2e30978b-aa6d-d80c-b1d6-3ba6bafaa4d9]—

- (a) for any period for which a Category B retirement pension is payable to the widow, widower or surviving civil partner by virtue of the earner’s contributions or would be so payable but for section 43(1) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 (persons entitled to more than one retirement pension);
- (aa) for life, in a case where—
  - (i) the widow, widower or surviving civil partner attained state pension age on or after the second abolition date, and
  - (ii) the earner died on or after the date on which the widow, widower or surviving civil partner attained state pension age;
- (ab) for any period after the widow, widower or surviving civil partner has attained state pension age, in a case where—
  - (i) the earner died before the widow, widower or surviving civil partner attained state pension age,
  - (ii) the widow, widower or surviving civil partner did not marry or form a civil partnership after the death and before attaining state pension age, and
  - (iii) the widow, widower or surviving civil partner attained state pension age on or after the second abolition date;
- (b) for any period for which widowed parent’s allowance or bereavement allowance is payable to the widow, widower or surviving civil partner by virtue of the earner’s contributions; and
- (c) in the case of a widow, widower or surviving civil partner whose entitlement by virtue of the earner’s contributions to a widowed parent’s allowance or bereavement allowance has come to an end at a time after the widow, widower or surviving civil partner attained the age of 45, for so much of the period beginning with the time when the entitlement came to an end as neither—
  - (i)[^V_S_718fa54f-f807-88a0-7348-b24264f38b26] comprises a period during which the widow, widower or surviving civil partner and another person are living together as a married couple;[^c-1e4c00519446de73194656cef68a91a4] nor
  - (ii) falls after the time of any—
    - (a) marriage; or
    - (b) formation of a civil partnership,

    by the widow or widower or surviving civil partner which takes place after the earner’s death.

(4B)[^V_S_816c0195-961c-8ee2-5105-8a309a16fce6] Sub-paragraphs (i)(b) and (ii)(b) of subsection (4A)(c) do not apply where the earner dies before 5th December 2005.

(5) In the case of a woman who is the widow of a man,[^c-796e097b0dc8abc5caefa60383c06e4a] the scheme must also make provision[^V_S_93356e97-4f8d-1b50-cba2-60f13da1fa49] for the widow’s pension to be payable to her for any period for which a [^V_S_59d9da5a-34eb-02cf-16ff-bf101eb05790]... widowed mother’s allowance or widow’s pension is payable to her by virtue of the earner’s contributions[^V_S_59d9da5a-34eb-02cf-16ff-bf101eb05790]....

(6) In any other case,[^c-25dfb5528e695aceaf7d7ce9e5bf7e42] the scheme must also make provision[^V_S_0945693d-6035-d4a4-6d4b-cf3e3e22dcaf] for the widower’s[^V_S_b826636c-df85-d309-d743-391f3b3d964a] , widow’s[^c-97a296935d06b3b308c31a5bb682b292] or surviving civil partner’s pension to be payable in the prescribed circumstances and for the prescribed period.

(7) The trustees or managers of the scheme shall supply to the Inland Revenue[^V_S_4eebc273-53a0-a8cd-0462-e82efc5dd7ed] any such information as the Inland Revenue may require relating to the payment of pensions under the scheme to widows, widowers or surviving civil partners[^V_S_03764ebe-198c-d24c-ef5c-4be91f0cc9cb].

(8)[^V_S_1a893604-ec52-0754-0316-bd942957df03] Where—

- (a) a lump sum is paid to an earner under provisions included in a scheme by virtue of section 21(1), and
- (b) those provisions are of a prescribed description,

the earner shall be treated for the purposes of this section as having any guaranteed minimum under section 14 that he would have had but for that payment.

(9)[^c-51c2435beab6a88486ba608b9a305dde] In relation to an earner who is a woman, a reference in this section to a relevant gender change case is a reference to a case where—

- (a) the earner is a woman by virtue of a full gender recognition certificate having been issued under the Gender Recognition Act 2004; and
- (b) the marriage of the earner and her widow (that ends with the earner’s death) subsisted before the time when the certificate was issued.

(9A) For the purposes of subsection (4A)(aa) and (ab) a person attains state pension age when he or she attains pensionable age within the meaning given by the rules in paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Pensions Act 1995.

(11)[^c-8c0c2fbf4f136de1086467c088d77574][^M_F_20052cb1-d0b8-42f9-e54a-e03f2ef04b63] This section is subject to regulations under section 38A.
