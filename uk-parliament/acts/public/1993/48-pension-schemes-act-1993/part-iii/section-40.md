# Pension Schemes Act 1993, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (105).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/48

## 40. Scope of Chapter II.[^key-36c0be76be1b6fd5cec0810cc40b7b90]

*Extent: E+W+S*

This Chapter has effect for the purpose—

- (a)[^key-15b3b08f1be5bec0e88f1aed5e78c036] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) of providing for contributions to be paid by the Inland Revenue[^key-0c7bd2d7ec8de4c23beefd65ba090de1] in respect of earners who are members of money purchase contracted-out schemes and members of[^key-e2cda3c0bc4604225199ea0210bd2606] appropriate personal pension schemes; and
- (c) of making provision concerning the payment of certain social security benefits payable in respect of members and former members of schemes that were contracted-out pension schemes[^key-c00ff2330e0a3a655d35804fee3188ed].
