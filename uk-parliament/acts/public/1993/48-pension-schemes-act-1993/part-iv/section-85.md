# Pension Schemes Act 1993, section 85

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (105).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 85. Revaluation not to apply to substituted benefit.[^c-36c0be76be1b6fd5cec0810cc40b7b90][^c-23c3201cb5e893b4ccca5d9a87e95886]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Nothing in this Chapter is to be construed as requiring the revaluation of any pension or other benefit provided by virtue of section 73(2)(b) or 101D(2)(b)[^c-3e3b9266b8645dbb195e22190adfb1b3] by way of complete substitute for another pension or benefit.
