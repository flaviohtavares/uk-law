## 183. Sub-delegation.[^key-36c0be76be1b6fd5cec0810cc40b7b90]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Without prejudice to any specific provisions in this Act, a power conferred by this Act to make an Order in Council, regulations or an order (other than regulations and orders made under [^key-bd4b8016b38beaade271e8c81da56d81]... Chapter I of Part IV, Part VIII so far as it applies for the purposes of Chapter I of Part IV, sections [^key-dd4c2b6b8687b857cbb30c51edc90e32]... 153(2), 158(1) to (5), 162, 163, [^key-bd4b8016b38beaade271e8c81da56d81]... and 176[^key-bd4b8016b38beaade271e8c81da56d81]...) includes power to provide for a person to exercise a discretion in dealing with any matter.

(2)[^key-a9ea366d74a5e87b2b4db513f35199bb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Regulations under section [^key-d3fdbbefd324a14b1cca75472bd8d300]... 63(2), , 97(1)[^key-c04996358f310ba73ba9d559c46fbc01] , 101AF(1)[^key-8d4ac617d4f4f30cabe5789e1cbd29c7] and 101I may provide that the values there mentioned shall be calculated and verified[^key-cf67f725f798f5e3c597daf3083bb6b4]...—

- (a) in such manner as may, in the particular case, be approved—
  - (i) by a prescribed person;
  - (ii) by a person with prescribed professional qualifications or experience; or
  - (iii) by a person approved by the Secretary of State; or
- (b) in accordance with guidance prepared and from time to time revised[^key-6dc1207e4352ee937c9d323916b03bfb] by a prescribed body.
