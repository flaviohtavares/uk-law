## 178. Trustees and managers of schemes.[^c-36c0be76be1b6fd5cec0810cc40b7b90]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Secretary of State may by regulations provide—

- (a) who is to be treated as a manager of an occupational pension scheme for any of the purposes of the provisions of this Act (except Chapter II of Part VII or section 157), the Social Security Acts 1975 to 1991, or Part VI of the [^c21361421]Social Security Administration Act 1992 [^c-e85df567ee0abe320d295d795e5bd45b][^c-2c540f03639b0938d8ac8352958ea672]... , section 25D of the Matrimonial Causes Act 1973, section 12A of the Family Law (Scotland) Act 1985[^c-7d65308a3611e1ad36d6bf0c85faa7e0] or[^c-7ee4c16ad18b93cf561c08528536b2ef] , Part III or IV of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999[^c-8c723e309b8920fed2b5b2785b075176] [^c-5f3b5988413a8484d82c362c7c0313e4]... ; [^c-24aad0a3f7606f6c53c623ed793f9036]...
- (b) who is to be treated as a trustee of a scheme for the purposes of [^c-fc8ff8e72b6d724350e02eac78cbdea6]... Chapter I of Part IV, Part VIII so far as it applies for the purposes of Chapter I of Part IV, Chapter I of Part IVA,[^c-942af0ee96dca44a6f25ef977dad93dd] sections [^c-1ace6b1ff3bff9c5f96ad64816b918b8]... 153(2), 158(1) to (5), 162, 163, [^c-fc8ff8e72b6d724350e02eac78cbdea6]...and 176 [^c-fc8ff8e72b6d724350e02eac78cbdea6]... or sections 22[^c-1cf375806374dd92aeaf3456d7540736] to 26[^c-4e4eecf749c097cd00602f1521018878] of the Pensions Act 1995.
