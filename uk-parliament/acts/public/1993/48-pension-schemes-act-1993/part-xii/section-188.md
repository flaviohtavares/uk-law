## 188. Repeals.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-3d4bdee5ddb77786ed2b73928b9bd654] The enactments specified in Parts I, II and III of Schedule 5 are repealed to the extent specified in the third column.

(2)[^key-e9f1527dd12ab6d71db2562aa9861b70] The subordinate legislation specified in Part IV of that Schedule is revoked to the extent specified in the third column.

(3)[^key-e9f1527dd12ab6d71db2562aa9861b70] The repeals and revocations have effect subject to any relevant savings in Schedule 6.
