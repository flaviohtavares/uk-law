# Pension Schemes Act 1993, section 174

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (105).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 174. Grants to advisory bodies etc[^key-36c0be76be1b6fd5cec0810cc40b7b90][^key-664308ac6fe677ee1326b10fa4498659]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Regulatory Authority[^key-9d405485dbc49dfb6ca6550d86440a8f] may make grants on such terms and conditions as they think fit to any person or body of persons providing advice or assistance, or carrying out other prescribed functions, in connection with occupational or personal pensions.

(2) The Secretary of State may pay the Regulatory Authority such sums as he may think fit towards any expenditure of theirs in making grants under this section.

(3)[^key-92da7977fc81577c8be1eb73fc8f8c01] The Secretary of State may make grants on such terms and conditions as the Secretary of State thinks fit to any person or body of persons providing advice or assistance, or carrying out other prescribed functions, in connection with occupational or personal pensions.
