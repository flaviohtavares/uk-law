## 171A. Reports by Inland Revenue.[^key-b5a954af5713cd0a4272212fea95d62c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Inland Revenue shall prepare, either annually or at such times or intervals as may be prescribed, a report on the standards achieved by their officers in the making of decisions against which, by virtue of section 170(6), an appeal lies to the First-tier Tribunal[^key-fa8d3624aaf08531c388359fa5470fdb].

(2) Any report under this section—

- (a) may be included in any annual report by the Inland Revenue of which a copy is laid before each House of Parliament, [^key-9b51b0dee96dba033c2b073e7f287c19]...
- (b)[^key-9b51b0dee96dba033c2b073e7f287c19] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) A copy of every report under this section shall be laid before each House of Parliament, unless the report is included in [^key-3f77f13bfc71d09f4d986b8d924a3cf1]... a report of which a copy is so laid.
