## 163. Exemption of certain schemes from rule against perpetuities.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] The rules of law relating to perpetuities shall not apply to the trusts of, or any disposition made under or for the purposes of a personal or occupational pension scheme at any time when this section applies to it. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] This section applies to— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] a public service pension scheme; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] an occupational pension scheme which is a contracted-out scheme in relation to any employment; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8fe58920d9619fd483f15869337182d7][^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . ; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] an occupational or personal pension scheme which satisfies prescribed requirements. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] Subsection (1) applies whether the trusts or dispositions in question are created or made before or after this section first applies to the scheme, but this section does not validate with retrospective effect any trusts or dispositions which the rules of law relating to perpetuities (including, where applicable, section 3(1) of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] [^c21361171]Perpetuities and Accumulations Act 1964 (“wait and see”)) already require to be treated as void before this section applies to the scheme. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] Regulations under subsection (2)(d) may require a scheme— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] to contain provisions in any prescribed form, or to any prescribed effect; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c5311f2271fc3fc113705f0a7e09c8df][^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] to be a registered pension scheme under section 153 of the Finance Act 2004 or to be a scheme that may be expected to satisfy the conditions for registration. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-babdf35c44a6ebac2520ed03c9bd9f9f][^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] Regulations may include provision by which a scheme (other than a public service pension scheme) to which this section ceases to apply may nevertheless be treated as continuing to be a scheme to which it applies for a period of two years from its ceasing to be such a scheme, or for such longer period as the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] [^c-94ea33b5f205cc49a8e53f9661e9125e]... *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] Secretary of State *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] [^c-94ea33b5f205cc49a8e53f9661e9125e]... *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] considers *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] to be reasonable in the case of a particular scheme. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] If this section ceases to apply to a scheme, trusts created and dispositions made under it or for its purposes shall then again be subject to the rules of law relating to perpetuities as if this section had never applied to it. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43]

(8 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] Subsection (7) is without prejudice to any rights which vested while this section applied. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43]

(9 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d0f3668aba75452da44f2e7128c1979c][^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] Regulations may provide for a scheme, whose fund was registered under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d0f3668aba75452da44f2e7128c1979c] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43] [^c21361181]Superannuation and other Trust Funds (Validation) Act 1927 immediately before the repeal of that Act took effect, to retain the benefit of that Act subject to prescribed conditions and either indefinitely or for a prescribed period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d0f3668aba75452da44f2e7128c1979c] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f09726a41022747c0327a49b6680eb43]
