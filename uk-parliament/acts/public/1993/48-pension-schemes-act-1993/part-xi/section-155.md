> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (105).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/48

## 155. Requirement to give information to the Secretary of State or the Board for the purposes of certain provisions.[^c-36c0be76be1b6fd5cec0810cc40b7b90]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Regulations may require the furnishing by prescribed persons to the Secretary of State or the Inland Revenue[^c-a1089aeff9abbd7bec18b8bb24015662] [^c-9627c56ec40c2a7a8d0ffc6522d805ba]... of such information as he or they require[^c-b07e8c881d9747adb9cd10a864d655d3] for the purposes of [^c-04c80405a3b0d341742723f8c66ea2ab]... Part III, section 111 (and Part VIII and section 153 so far as they have effect for the purposes of section 111) and section 117[^M_F_793afae2-bbd7-423b-dfe8-381ca4694357].
