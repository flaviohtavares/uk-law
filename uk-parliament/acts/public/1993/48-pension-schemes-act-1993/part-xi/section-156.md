# Pension Schemes Act 1993, section 156

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (105).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 156. Information for purposes of contracting-out.[^c-36c0be76be1b6fd5cec0810cc40b7b90][^c-3db2451940ad297db1df3fcc663ffb69]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State or the Inland Revenue may give to the trustees or managers of an occupational pension scheme or a personal pension scheme which was an appropriate scheme before[^c-d89ee4b00bf6c0002cf8a1083470f4bf] the first abolition date[^M_F_d9687c34-1ceb-40d5-d782-41c3b315c20a] such information as appears to the Secretary of State or Inland Revenue appropriate to give to them for the purpose of enabling them to comply with their obligations under Part III.

(2) The Secretary of State or Inland Revenue may also give to such persons as may be prescribed any information that they could give under subsection (1) to trustees or managers of a scheme.
