# Pension Schemes Act 1993, section 101M

## 101M. Effect of transfer on trustees’ duties.[^c-2c7a4e535e5067832c6ba0b8235adaec][^c-665a3636dcd1ed2b8438c31258510388]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Compliance with a transfer notice shall have effect to discharge the trustees or managers of a qualifying scheme from any obligation to provide the benefits to which the transfer notice relates[^c-75635ce45e5402c39fed1fb57fa0b76c].
