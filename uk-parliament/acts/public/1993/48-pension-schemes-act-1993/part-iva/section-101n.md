# Pension Schemes Act 1993, section 101N

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (105).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/48

## 101N. Matters to be disregarded in calculations.[^c-665a3636dcd1ed2b8438c31258510388]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In making any calculation for the purposes of this Chapter—

- (a) any charge or lien on, and
- (b) any set-off against,

the whole or part of a pension shall be disregarded.
