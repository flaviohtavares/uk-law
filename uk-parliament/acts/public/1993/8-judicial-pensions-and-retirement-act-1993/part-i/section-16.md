# Judicial Pensions and Retirement Act 1993, section 16

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Disregard of abatement of pension under s.65 of the Social Security Act 1973 etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In making any calculation for the purposes of sections 4 to 8 above, any abatement of a pension falling to be made under any order made under—

- (a)[^c13953981] section 141 of the Pension Schemes Act 1993 (modification of public service schemes) or section 143 of that Act (winding up of public service schemes);
- (b)[^c13953991] section 137 or 139 of the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993 (corresponding Northern Ireland provisions);

shall be left out of account.
