# Judicial Pensions and Retirement Act 1993, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2022-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Transfer of accrued benefits.[^c13954081]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 2 to this Act shall have effect with respect to the transfer of accrued rights into and out of the pension schemes constituted by Part I or section 19 above (other than any such transfer which falls to be made under section 12 or 12A[^c20876611] above).
