# Cardiff Bay Barrage Act 1993, section 3

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Deemed impoundment licence.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A licence under Chapter II of Part II of the [^c861216]Water Resources Act 1991 shall be treated as having been granted by the Natural Resources Body for Wales to[^key-8976a0fc17870e1a10659e34b1c5b761] authorise the obstruction of the flow of water by the construction, and any alteration or replacement, of the barrage.

(2) Schedule 3 to this Act, which specifies requirements deemed to be imposed by the provisions of the licence, shall have effect.
