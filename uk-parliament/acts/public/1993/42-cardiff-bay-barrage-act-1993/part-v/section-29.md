# Cardiff Bay Barrage Act 1993, section 29

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Arbitration.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where under any provision of this Act any difference is to be determined by arbitration it shall be referred to and settled by an arbitrator appointed by agreement between the parties or, in default of agreement, by an arbitrator appointed on the application of either party (after giving to the other not less than one week’s notice in writing) by the President of the Institution of Civil Engineers.
