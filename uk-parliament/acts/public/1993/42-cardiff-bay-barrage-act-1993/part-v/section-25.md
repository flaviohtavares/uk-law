# Cardiff Bay Barrage Act 1993, section 25

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Disapplication of certain statutory requirements.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 109 of the [^c861236]Water Resources Act 1991 (controls on structures in, over or under a main river) shall not apply to any structure constructed under any of the powers conferred by section 1 of this Act.

(2) The inland bay shall not be taken to be a reservoir for the purposes of the [^c861237]Reservoirs Act 1975.
