> Text as at 2017-12-28.

## 19. Operation and management: supplementary.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 6 to this Act, which contains supplementary provisions about the operation and management of the barrage, outer harbour and inland bay etc., shall have effect.
