# Cardiff Bay Barrage Act 1993, section 14

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Development and conservation of flora and fauna.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) When managing the inland bay (including when doing so to comply with a direction given under section 12(1)(b) above) the Development Corporation shall have regard to the desirability of developing and conserving flora and fauna.

(2) The Development Corporation shall consult the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^key-8230370a182c426a41a46389153bb908] to seek their view as to ways in which the inland bay may be managed so as to develop and conserve flora and fauna.
