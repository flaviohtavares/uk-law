# Cardiff Bay Barrage Act 1993, section 21

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Groundwater damage protection.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 7 to this Act, which makes provision for ascertaining, and preventing or repairing, damage to property resulting from any alteration of groundwater levels which may occur in consequence of the construction of the barrage, shall have effect.

(2) The Secretary of State may by regulations made by statutory instrument amend any provision of that Schedule.

(3) Regulations shall not be made under subsection (2) above unless a draft of the instrument containing the regulations has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House.
