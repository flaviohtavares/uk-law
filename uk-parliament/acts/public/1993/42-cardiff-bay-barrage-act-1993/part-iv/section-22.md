# Cardiff Bay Barrage Act 1993, section 22

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Powers to deal with consequences of altered groundwater levels.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The [^c861234]Land Drainage Act 1991 shall have effect as if each of the references to flooding in sections 14, 15 and 66 of that Act included a reference to any other adverse consequence of an alteration of groundwater levels occurring as a result of the construction of the barrage.
