## 19 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Duty to display documents.[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^c-d66e2c88c19ad517f7547ed3c15a5c3a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

At all times while— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d66e2c88c19ad517f7547ed3c15a5c3a]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d66e2c88c19ad517f7547ed3c15a5c3a] a person is registered in respect of any premises under section 7, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d66e2c88c19ad517f7547ed3c15a5c3a]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d66e2c88c19ad517f7547ed3c15a5c3a] an authorisation granted in respect of any premises under section 13(1) or 14 is for the time being in force, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d66e2c88c19ad517f7547ed3c15a5c3a]

the person to whom the registration relates, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d66e2c88c19ad517f7547ed3c15a5c3a] who holds the authorisation[^c-45a83b7ae6ed94a8c183b5567810b8c9] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d66e2c88c19ad517f7547ed3c15a5c3a] , as the case may be, shall cause copies of the certificate of registration or authorisation issued to him under this Act to be kept posted on the premises, in such characters and in such positions as to be conveniently read by persons having duties on those premises which are or may be affected by the matters set out in the certificate. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d66e2c88c19ad517f7547ed3c15a5c3a]
