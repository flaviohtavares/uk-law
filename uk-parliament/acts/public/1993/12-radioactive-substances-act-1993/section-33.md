# Radioactive Substances Act 1993, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-12-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/12
>

## 33 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Offences relating to ss. 19 and 20.[^key-2ac21809531a4a9862c2925061ff3f57][^key-cf71fe49953dc094433e7f720fe58962]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cf71fe49953dc094433e7f720fe58962] Any person who contravenes section 19 shall be guilty of an offence and liable— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cf71fe49953dc094433e7f720fe58962]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cf71fe49953dc094433e7f720fe58962] on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cf71fe49953dc094433e7f720fe58962]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cf71fe49953dc094433e7f720fe58962] on conviction on indictment, to a fine. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cf71fe49953dc094433e7f720fe58962]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cf71fe49953dc094433e7f720fe58962] Any person who without reasonable cause pulls down, injures or defaces any document posted in pursuance of section 19 shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 2 on the standard scale. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cf71fe49953dc094433e7f720fe58962]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cf71fe49953dc094433e7f720fe58962] Any person who fails to comply with a requirement imposed on him under section 20 shall be guilty of an offence and liable— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cf71fe49953dc094433e7f720fe58962]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cf71fe49953dc094433e7f720fe58962] on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months, or both; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cf71fe49953dc094433e7f720fe58962]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-cf71fe49953dc094433e7f720fe58962] on conviction on indictment, to a fine or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or both. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cf71fe49953dc094433e7f720fe58962]
