# Radioactive Substances Act 1993, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-12-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/12
>

## 41 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Service of documents.[^key-2ac21809531a4a9862c2925061ff3f57][^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484] Any notice required or authorised by or under this Act to be served on or given to any person may be served or given by delivering it to him, or by leaving it at his proper address, or by sending it by post to him at that address. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484] Any such notice may— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484] in the case of a body corporate, be served on or given to the secretary or clerk of that body; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484] in the case of a partnership, be served on or given to a partner or a person having the control or management of the partnership business. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484] For the purposes of this section and of section 7 of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484] [^c427007]Interpretation Act 1978 (service of documents by post) in its application to this section, the proper address of any person on or to whom any such notice is to be served or given shall be his last known address, except that— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484] in the case of a body corporate or their secretary or clerk, it shall be the address of the registered or principal office of that body; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484] in the case of a partnership or person having the control or the management of the partnership business, it shall be the principal office of the partnership; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484]

and for the purposes of this subsection the principal office of a company registered outside the United Kingdom or of a partnership carrying on business outside the United Kingdom shall be their principal office within the United Kingdom. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484] If the person to be served with or given any such notice has specified an address in the United Kingdom other than his proper address within the meaning of subsection (3) as the one at which he or someone on his behalf will accept notices of the same description as that notice, that address shall also be treated for the purposes of this section and section 7 of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484] [^c427008]Interpretation Act 1978 as his proper address. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484] The preceding provisions of this section shall apply to the sending or giving of a document as they apply to the giving of a notice. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bbe54a0913ac9a34f2bac7d890460484]
