## 23 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Power of Secretary of State to give directions to chief inspector.[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] The Secretary of State may, if he thinks fit in relation to— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] an application for registration under section 7 or 10, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] an application for an authorisation under section 13 or 14 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] or for the transfer (in whole or in part) or variation of an authorisation[^c-0fe03a695cd805945577bebe8b97f4ff] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e], or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] any such registration or authorisation, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]

give directions to the chief inspector requiring him to take any of the steps mentioned in the following subsections in accordance with the directions. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] A direction under subsection (1) may require the chief inspector so to exercise his powers under this Act as— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] to refuse an application for registration or authorisation *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] or for the transfer (in whole or in part) or variation of an authorisation[^c-df3556d81aacc0aa6089395581bb31b5] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e], *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] to effect or grant a registration or authorisation, attaching such limitations or conditions (if any) as may be specified in the direction, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] to vary a registration or authorisation, as may be so specified, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]
- (ca (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9f2612acecd28e5aa12865d994db8c8f][^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] to grant an application for the transfer (in whole or in part) of an authorisation, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]
- (cb (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] to carry out a review under section 17A, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] to cancel or revoke (or not to cancel or revoke) a registration or authorisation. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] The Secretary of State may give directions to the chief inspector, as respects any registration or authorisation, requiring him to serve a notice under section 21 or 22 in such terms as may be specified in the directions. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] The Secretary of State may give directions requiring the chief inspector to send such written particulars relating to, or to activities carried on in pursuance of, registrations effected or authorisations granted under any provision of this Act as may be specified in the directions to such local authorities as may be so specified. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] In the application of this section to Northern Ireland, references to the Secretary of State shall have effect as references to the Department of the Environment for Northern Ireland. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-24c0a125d5f34faef634a78029ab79c3][^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] In Northern Ireland, where the Department of the Environment gives directions to the chief inspector under this section for the purpose of implementing provisions of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] HASS Directive *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] the following requirements apply— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] any direction shall be published in such manner as the Department considers appropriate for the purpose of bringing the matters to which it relates to the attention of persons likely to be affected by it; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] copies of a direction shall be made available to the public; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] notice of a direction and of where a copy may be obtained shall be given in the Belfast Gazette; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] a direction shall be given only after consultation with the chief inspector; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]
- (e (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] no direction shall be varied or revoked unless, notwithstanding the variation or revocation, the provisions of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] HASS Directive *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e] as they have effect for the time being which were implemented by that direction, continue to be implemented, whether by directions or any other instrument or by any enactment. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dfa9f35236059d162f53fc2bd599ea9e]
