## 1B (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). NORM industrial activities[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^key-72d5d37176b07dc476a4c3ec6de53150]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] Subsection (2) applies to a substance or article which— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] arises from or is used in a NORM industrial activity listed in Part 1 of Table 1; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] is waste which arises from a NORM industrial activity listed in Part 2 of Table 1; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] is contaminated by a substance or article described in paragraph (a) or (b), including where such contamination occurs indirectly through another contaminated substance or article. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] A substance or article to which this subsection applies is radioactive material or radioactive waste where it has a concentration of radioactivity which exceeds the following values in Table 2— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] for a solid substance or article or a relevant liquid substance, the value specified in column 2; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] for any other liquid substance, the value specified in column 3; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] for a gaseous substance, the value specified in column 4. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
