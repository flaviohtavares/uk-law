## 24 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Power of Secretary of State to require certain applications to be determined by him.[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e] The Secretary of State may— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e] give general directions to the chief inspector requiring him to refer applications under this Act for registrations *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e] , authorisations, transfers or variations[^c-66c5ec2bcb6be8d893ebee657358fa85] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e] of any description specified in the directions to the Secretary of State for his determination, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e] give directions to the chief inspector in respect of any particular application requiring him to refer the application to the Secretary of State for his determination. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e] Where an application is referred to the Secretary of State in pursuance of directions given under this section, the Secretary of State may cause a local inquiry to be held in relation to the application. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e] The following provisions shall apply to inquiries in pursuance of subsection (2)— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e] in England and Wales, subsections (2) to (5) of section 250 of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e] [^c426866]Local Government Act 1972 (supplementary provisions about local inquiries under that section) but with the omission, in subsection (4) of that section, of the words “such local authority or”, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e] in Scotland, subsections (2) to (8) of section 210 of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e] [^c426867]Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 (power to direct inquiries), and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e] in Northern Ireland, Schedule 8 to the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e] [^c426868]Health and Personal Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972 (provisions as to inquiries). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e] After determining any application so referred, the Secretary of State may give the chief inspector directions under section 23 as to the steps to be taken by him in respect of the application. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e] In the application of this section to Northern Ireland, references to the Secretary of State shall have effect as references to the Department of the Environment for Northern Ireland. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-70cd763487fc479437f3f2d4f2d1140e]
