# Radioactive Substances Act 1993, section 1E

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-12-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1E (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Radionuclides with a short half-life[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^c-72d5d37176b07dc476a4c3ec6de53150]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A substance or article is not radioactive material or radioactive waste where none of the radionuclides which it contains or which it consists of has a half-life exceeding 100 seconds. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
