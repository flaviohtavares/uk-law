## 38 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Restriction on prosecutions.[^c-2ac21809531a4a9862c2925061ff3f57][^c-1395b85492ab18bd110e319a8d733a4e]

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1395b85492ab18bd110e319a8d733a4e] Proceedings in respect of any offence under this Act shall not be instituted in England or Wales except— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1395b85492ab18bd110e319a8d733a4e]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1395b85492ab18bd110e319a8d733a4e] by the Secretary of State, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1395b85492ab18bd110e319a8d733a4e]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c426988][^c-1395b85492ab18bd110e319a8d733a4e] by the Environment Agency, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1395b85492ab18bd110e319a8d733a4e]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1395b85492ab18bd110e319a8d733a4e] by or with the consent of the Director of Public Prosecutions. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1395b85492ab18bd110e319a8d733a4e]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1395b85492ab18bd110e319a8d733a4e] Proceedings in respect of any offence under this Act shall not be instituted in Northern Ireland except— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1395b85492ab18bd110e319a8d733a4e]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1395b85492ab18bd110e319a8d733a4e] by the head of the Department of the Environment for Northern Ireland, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1395b85492ab18bd110e319a8d733a4e]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1395b85492ab18bd110e319a8d733a4e] by or with the consent of the Attorney General for Northern Ireland. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1395b85492ab18bd110e319a8d733a4e]
