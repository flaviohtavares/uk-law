## 26 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Registrations, authorisations and notices: appeals from decisions of chief inspector.[^key-cbca351a5e6951194c6963cfcbce56dd][^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] Where the chief inspector— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] refuses an application for registration under section 7 or 10, or refuses an application for an authorisation under section 13 or 14, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]
- (aa (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a71c16ebb2810288e0d0e554b882efc3][^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] refuses an application under section 16A or 17 for the transfer (in whole or in part) or variation of such an authorisation, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] attaches any limitations or conditions to such a registration or to such an authorisation, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] varies such a registration or such an authorisation, otherwise than by revoking a limitation or condition subject to which it has effect, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] cancels such a registration or revokes such an authorisation, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]

the person directly concerned may, subject to subsection (3), appeal to the Secretary of State. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] A person on whom a notice under section 21 or 22 is served may, subject to subsections (3) and (4), appeal against the notice to the Secretary of State. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] No appeal shall lie— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] under subsection (1) in relation to authorisations which are subject to section 16(3); *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] under subsection (1) or (2) in respect of any decision taken by the chief inspector in pursuance of a direction of the Secretary of State under section 23 or 24. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] No appeal shall lie under subsection (2) in respect of any notice served in England, Wales or Northern Ireland by the appropriate Minister in exercise of the power under section 21 or 22. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] In this section “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]**the person directly concerned *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]**” means— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] in relation to a registration under section 7 or 10, the person applying for the registration or to whom the registration relates; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] in relation to an authorisation under section 13 or 14, the person applying for the authorisation or to whom it was granted; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0145ece197b9efb4d1de223e1d6ffca8][^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] in relation to an application under section 16A for the transfer of an authorisation, either or both of the persons making the application; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] in relation to an application for a variation under section 17, the person applying for the variation. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]

and any reference to attaching limitations or conditions to a registration or authorisation is a reference to attaching limitations or conditions to it either in effecting or granting it or in exercising any power to vary it. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe] In the application of this section to Northern Ireland, references to the Secretary of State shall have effect as references to the Department of the Environment for Northern Ireland. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4e43d7014159d2032749065af572bcbe]
