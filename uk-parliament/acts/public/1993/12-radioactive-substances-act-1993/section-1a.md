# Radioactive Substances Act 1993, section 1A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-12-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1A (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Meaning of “radioactive material” and “radioactive waste”[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^c-72d5d37176b07dc476a4c3ec6de53150]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act, except as provided by sections 1E, 1F, 1G *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] , 1GA *(Extent: N.I.)*[^c-a9f7002f7cafba6b0093d4d2324d927c] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] and 1H— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]

- “radioactive material” means a substance or article which is not waste, and which satisfies the requirements of any of sections 1B, 1C and 1D as they apply to such a substance or article; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
- “radioactive waste” means a substance or article which is waste, and which satisfies the requirements of any of sections 1B, 1C and 1D; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
- “Table 1”, “Table 2”, “Table 3” and “Table 4” mean the tables with those numbers in Schedule 1A. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
