## 16 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Grant of authorisations.[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] In this section, unless a contrary intention appears, “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]**authorisation *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]**” means an authorisation granted under section 13 or 14. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] Subject to subsection (3), the power to grant authorisations shall be exercisable by the chief inspector. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] In England, Wales and Northern Ireland, the power to grant authorisations under section 13(1) in respect of the disposal of radioactive waste on or from any premises situated on a nuclear site shall be exercisable by the chief inspector and the appropriate Minister; and the disposal of radioactive waste on or from any such premises in England, Wales or Northern Ireland shall not be treated as authorised under section 13(1) unless it is so authorised by both the chief inspector and that Minister. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] Any application for an authorisation shall be accompanied by the prescribed fee. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] Before granting an authorisation under section 13(1) in respect of the disposal of radioactive waste on or from premises situated on a nuclear site, the chief inspector and, where the premises are in England, Wales or Northern Ireland, the appropriate Minister shall each consult with such local authorities, relevant water bodies or other public or local authorities as appear to him to be proper to be consulted by him. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] On *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] receipt of an application[^key-6a03764460089203ca82ee0d1285fdc8] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] , the chief inspector shall, subject to directions under section 25, send a copy of the application to each local authority in whose area, in accordance with the authorisation applied for, radioactive waste is to be disposed of or accumulated. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] An application for an authorisation (other than an application to which subsection (3) applies) which is duly made to the chief inspector may be treated by the applicant as having been refused if it is not determined within the prescribed period for determinations or such longer period as may be agreed with the applicant. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

(8 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] An authorisation may be granted— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] either in respect of radioactive waste generally or in respect of such one or more descriptions of radioactive waste as may be specified in the authorisation, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] subject to such limitations or conditions as the chief inspector or, as the case may be, the chief inspector and the appropriate Minister think fit. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

(8A (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-da18d6b3506b5a3c4cc882de4600572e][^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] In exercising the functions under this Act in relation to radioactive material and radioactive waste, the chief inspector shall observe the requirements of Article 30(4) of the Basic Safety Standards Directive. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

(8B (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] This subsection applies where the chief inspector is exercising functions under this Act in relation to radioactive substances activity where there are no radioactive discharges specified in conditions in the authorisation— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] the chief inspector shall impose appropriate conditions in the authorisation concerning— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]
  - (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] the monitoring, or the evaluation, of radioactive airborne or aqueous discharges into the environment; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] the reporting to the chief inspector of the results of such monitoring or evaluation; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] for the purposes of this subsection, where the chief inspector is exercising functions under this Act in relation to a nuclear power station or nuclear reprocessing plant, the conditions imposed in the authorisation shall require the monitoring of radioactive discharges and reporting to the chief inspector of such information on radioactive discharges as the appropriate Minister directs; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] notification and recording of significant events to ensure compliance with Article 96 of the Basic Safety Standards Directive. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

(9 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] Where any authorisation is granted, the chief inspector— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24d0d12ad495249b8fdc8d1885b0906e][^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] shall furnish the person to whom the authorisation is granted with a certificate which contains all material particulars of the authorisation or gives sufficient information as to the particulars to enable them to be ascertained, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] shall, subject to directions under section 25, send a copy of the certificate— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]
  - (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] to each local authority in whose area, in accordance with the authorisation, radioactive waste is to be disposed of or accumulated, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] in the case of an authorisation to which subsection (5) applies, to any other public or local authority consulted in relation to the authorisation in accordance with that subsection. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

(10 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] An authorisation shall have effect as from such date as may be specified in it; and in fixing that date, in the case of an authorisation where copies of the certificate are required to be sent as mentioned in subsection (9)(b), the chief inspector or, as the case may be, the chief inspector and the appropriate Minister— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] shall have regard to the time at which those copies may be expected to be sent, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea] shall fix a date appearing to him or them to be such as will allow an interval of not less than twenty-eight days after that time before the authorisation has effect, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]

unless in his or their opinion it is necessary that the coming into operation of the authorisation should be immediate or should otherwise be expedited. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d24c9cee593e8f318574354f83084ea]
