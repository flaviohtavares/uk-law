## 3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Meaning of “mobile radioactive apparatus”.[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^key-5695269e6d8710982de9b4d8f0b50456]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5695269e6d8710982de9b4d8f0b50456]**mobile radioactive apparatus *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5695269e6d8710982de9b4d8f0b50456]**” means any apparatus, equipment, appliance or other thing which is radioactive material and— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5695269e6d8710982de9b4d8f0b50456]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5695269e6d8710982de9b4d8f0b50456] is constructed or adapted for being transported from place to place, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5695269e6d8710982de9b4d8f0b50456]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-5695269e6d8710982de9b4d8f0b50456] is portable and designed or intended to be used for releasing radioactive material into the environment or introducing it into organisms. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5695269e6d8710982de9b4d8f0b50456]
