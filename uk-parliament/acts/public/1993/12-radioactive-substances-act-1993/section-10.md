## 10 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Registration of mobile radioactive apparatus.[^c-4bae6b0cd1e7b516ea268b40f9cbab26][^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d] Any application for registration under this section shall be made to the chief inspector and— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d] shall specify— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d]
  - (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d] the apparatus to which the application relates, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d] the manner in which it is proposed to use the apparatus, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d] shall contain such other information as may be prescribed, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d] shall be accompanied by the prescribed fee. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d] Where an application is made to the chief inspector for registration under this section in respect of any apparatus, the chief inspector may register the applicant in respect of that apparatus, either unconditionally or subject to such limitations or conditions as the chief inspector thinks fit, or may refuse the application. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d] On any application being made the chief inspector shall, subject to directions under section 25, send a copy of the application to each local authority in whose area it appears to him the apparatus will be kept or will be used for releasing radioactive material into the environment. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d] An application for registration under this section which is duly made to the chief inspector may be treated by the applicant as having been refused if it is not determined within the prescribed period for determinations or within such longer period as may be agreed with the applicant. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d] On registering a person under this section in respect of any mobile radioactive apparatus, the chief inspector— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1a540c5997c03cbc6e211faffa666850][^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d] shall furnish him with a certificate which contains all material particulars of the registration or gives sufficient information as to the particulars to enable them to be ascertained, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d] shall, subject to directions under section 25, send a copy of the certificate to each local authority in whose area it appears to him the apparatus will be kept or will be used for releasing radioactive material into the environment. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c08bed5051054f8f7e03fcf7e829115d]
