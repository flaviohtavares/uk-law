# Radioactive Substances Act 1993, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-12-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Exemptions from registration under s. 10.[^c-1ee3e4a6cf73c32e0306a03a308dd3a6][^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^c-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] The Secretary of State may by order grant exemptions from registration under section 10, by reference to such classes of persons, and such descriptions of mobile radioactive apparatus, as may be specified in the order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] Any exemption granted by an order under subsection (1) may be granted subject to such limitations or conditions as may be specified in the order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] In the application of this section to Northern Ireland, the reference to the Secretary of State shall have effect as a reference to the Department of the Environment for Northern Ireland. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]
