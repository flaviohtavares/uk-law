## 32 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Offences relating to registration or authorisation.[^key-2ac21809531a4a9862c2925061ff3f57][^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be] Any person who— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be] contravenes section 6, 9, 13(1), (2) or (3) or 14(1), or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be] being a person registered under section 7 or 10, or being (wholly or partly) exempted from registration under either of those sections, does not comply with a limitation or condition subject to which he is so registered or exempted, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be] being a person *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be] who holds an authorisation under section 13 or 14[^key-7fdd5f839f83fc9dd2026ec7b94b2099] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be], does not comply with a limitation or condition subject to which that authorisation has effect, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be] being a person who is registered under section 7 or 10 or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be] who holds an authorisation under section 13 or 14[^key-494f15a8d9169f73b01789c533abc62d] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be], fails to comply with any requirement of a notice served on him under section 21 or 22, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be]

shall be guilty of an offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be] A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be] on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding #20,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or both; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be] on conviction on indictment, to a fine or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years, or both. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c426983][^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be] If the appropriate Agency is of the opinion that proceedings for an offence under subsection (1)(d) would afford an ineffectual remedy against a person who has failed to comply with the requirements of a notice served on him under section 21 or 22, that Agency may take proceedings in the High Court or, in Scotland, in any court of competent jurisdiction, for the purpose of securing compliance with the notice. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ac83f20faa2fe6bd39e03dba37f769be]
