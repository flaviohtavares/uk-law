## 20 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Retention and production of site or disposal records.[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df] The chief inspector may, by notice served on any person to whom a registration under section 7 or 10 relates or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df] who holds an authorisation under section 13 or 14[^key-7f23f93e47ac67ce829b4bac3b8ccfe1] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df], impose on him such requirements authorised by this section in relation to site *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df] , source transfer[^key-7187479b63e4f438b7201075ce0c473f] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df] or disposal records kept by that person as the chief inspector may specify in the notice. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df] The requirements that may be imposed on a person under this section in relation to site or disposal records are— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df] to retain copies of the records for a specified period after he ceases to carry on the activities regulated by his registration or authorisation, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df] to furnish the chief inspector with copies of the records in the event of his registration being cancelled or his authorisation being revoked or in the event of his ceasing to carry on the activities regulated by his registration or authorisation. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df] In relation to authorisations under section 13 so far as the power to grant or revoke such authorisations is exercisable by the chief inspector and the appropriate Minister, references in subsections (1) and (2) of this section to the chief inspector shall be construed as references to the chief inspector and that Minister. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df] In this section, in relation to a registration and the person registered or an authorisation and the person authorised— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]

- “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]**the activities regulated *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]**” by his registration or authorisation means— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]
  - (a) in the case of registration under section 7, the keeping or use of radioactive material, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]
  - (b) in the case of registration under section 10, the keeping, using, lending or hiring of the mobile radioactive apparatus, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]
  - (c) in the case of an authorisation under section 13, the disposal of radioactive waste, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]
  - (d) in the case of an authorisation under section 14, the accumulation of radioactive waste, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]
- “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]**records *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]**” means records required to be kept by virtue of the conditions attached to the registration or authorisation relating to the activities regulated by the registration or authorisation, and “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]**site records *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]**” means records relating to the condition of the premises on which those activities are carried on or, in the case of registration in respect of mobile radioactive apparatus, of any place where the apparatus is kept *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df] , source transfer records” means records relating to the transfer of control of high-activity sources[^key-ae07be1ec599552a8d60cfe3220dc6db] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df] and “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]**disposal records *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]**” means records relating to the disposal of radioactive waste on or from the premises on which the activities are carried on, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]
- “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]**specified *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]**” means specified in a notice under this section. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-efdd105605cc6fe5a35764c612f751df]
