# Radioactive Substances Act 1993, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-12-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/12

## 18 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Functions of public and local authorities in relation to authorisations under s. 13.[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^key-f4103546614c8a2d111d886782f7719a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4103546614c8a2d111d886782f7719a] If, in considering an application for an authorisation under section 13, it appears to the chief inspector (or, in a case where the power to grant the authorisation is exercisable by the chief inspector and the appropriate Minister, it appears to either the chief inspector or that Minister) that the disposal of radioactive waste to which the application relates is likely to involve the need for special precautions to be taken by a local authority, relevant water body or other public or local authority, the chief inspector or the appropriate Minister, as the case may be, shall consult with that public or local authority before granting the authorisation. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4103546614c8a2d111d886782f7719a]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4103546614c8a2d111d886782f7719a] Where a public or local authority take any special precautions in respect of radioactive waste disposed of in accordance with an authorisation granted under section 13, and those precautions are taken— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4103546614c8a2d111d886782f7719a]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4103546614c8a2d111d886782f7719a] in compliance with the conditions subject to which the authorisation was granted, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4103546614c8a2d111d886782f7719a]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4103546614c8a2d111d886782f7719a] with the prior approval of the chief inspector (or, where the authorisation was granted by the chief inspector and the appropriate Minister, with the prior approval of either the chief inspector or that Minister) as being precautions which in the circumstances ought to be taken by that public or local authority, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4103546614c8a2d111d886782f7719a]

the public or local authority shall have power to make such charges, in respect of the taking of those precautions, as may be agreed between that authority and the person to whom the authorisation was granted, or as, in default of such agreement, may be determined by the chief inspector, and to recover the charges so agreed or determined from that person. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4103546614c8a2d111d886782f7719a]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f4103546614c8a2d111d886782f7719a] Where an authorisation granted under section 13 requires or permits radioactive waste to be removed to a place provided by a local authority as a place for the deposit of refuse, it shall be the duty of that local authority to accept any radioactive waste removed to that place in accordance with the authorisation, and, if the authorisation contains any provision as to the manner in which the radioactive waste is to be dealt with after its removal to that place, to deal with it in the manner indicated in the authorisation. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f4103546614c8a2d111d886782f7719a]
