## 34A (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Offences of making false or misleading statements or false entries.[^c-2ac21809531a4a9862c2925061ff3f57][^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee][^c426982]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee] Any person who— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee] for the purpose of obtaining for himself or another any registration under section 7 or 10, any authorisation under section 13 or 14 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee] , any transfer of such an authorisation under section 16A[^c-dda24cfd8125dc7485b8fed7c25527d1] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee] or any variation of such an authorisation under section 17, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee] in purported compliance with a requirement to furnish information imposed under section 31(1)(d), *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee]

makes a statement which he knows to be false or misleading in a material particular, or recklessly makes a statement which is false or misleading in a material particular, shall be guilty of an offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee] Any person who intentionally makes a false entry in any record— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee] which is required to be kept by virtue of a registration under section 7 or 10 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee] , an authorisation under section 13 or 14 or a transfer under section 16A[^c-6a6a300f9822dbd58ece83833be14ae1] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee], or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee] which is kept in purported compliance with a condition which must be complied with if a person is to have the benefit of an exemption under section 8, 11 or 15, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee]

shall be guilty of an offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee] A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee] on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee] on conviction on indictment, to a fine or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or to both. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9c993cdc337f0974eebd9a6a4a5034ee]
