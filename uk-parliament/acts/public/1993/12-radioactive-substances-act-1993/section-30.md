## 30 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Power of Secretary of State to dispose of radioactive waste.[^key-2ac21809531a4a9862c2925061ff3f57][^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c] If there is radioactive waste on any premises, and the Secretary of State is satisfied that— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c] the waste ought to be disposed of, but *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c] by reason that the premises are unoccupied, or that the occupier is absent, or is insolvent, or for any other reason, it is unlikely that the waste will be lawfully disposed of unless the Secretary of State exercises his powers under this section, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c]

the Secretary of State shall have power to dispose of that radioactive waste as the Secretary of State may think fit, and to recover from the occupier of the premises, or, if the premises are unoccupied, from the owner of the premises, any expenses reasonably incurred by the Secretary of State in disposing of it. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c] In the application of subsection (1) to Northern Ireland, references to the Secretary of State shall have effect as references to the Department of the Environment for Northern Ireland. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c] For the purposes of this section in its application to England and Wales and Northern Ireland, the definition of “owner” in section 343 of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c] [^c426972]Public Health Act 1936, and the provisions of section 294 of that Act (which limits the liability of owners who are only agents or trustees), shall apply— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c] with the substitution in section 294 for references to a council of references to the Secretary of State or, in Northern Ireland, the Department of the Environment for Northern Ireland, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c] in relation to Northern Ireland, as if that Act extended to Northern Ireland. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c] For the purposes of this section in its application to Scotland, the definition of “owner” in section 3 of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c] [^c426973]Public Health (Scotland) Act 1897 and the provisions of section 336 of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c] [^c426974]Housing (Scotland) Act 1987 shall apply, with the substitution in section 336 of references to the Secretary of State for references to a local authority. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f68d41b623494681c4e69a918986132c]
