# Radioactive Substances Act 1993, section 40

## 40 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Radioactivity to be disregarded for purposes of certain statutory provisions.[^key-2ac21809531a4a9862c2925061ff3f57][^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] For the purposes of the operation of any statutory provision to which this section applies, and for the purposes of the exercise or performance of any power or duty conferred or imposed by, or for the enforcement of, any such statutory provision, no account shall be taken of any radioactivity possessed by any substance or article or by any part of any premises. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] This section applies— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] to any statutory provision contained in, or for the time being having effect by virtue of, any of the enactments specified in Schedule 3, or any enactment for the time being in force whereby an enactment so specified is amended, extended or superseded, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] to any statutory provision contained in, or for the time being having effect by virtue of, a local enactment whether passed or made before or after the passing of this Act (in whatever terms the provision is expressed) in so far as— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]
  - (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] the disposal or accumulation of waste or any description of waste, or of any substance which is a nuisance, or so as to be a nuisance, or of any substance which is, or so as to be, prejudicial to health, noxious, polluting or of any similar description, is prohibited or restricted by the statutory provision, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] a power or duty is conferred or imposed by the statutory provision on *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] the Environmental Agency or[^c427004] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] SEPA *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] or on *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] any local authority, relevant water body or other public or local authority, or on any officer of a public or local authority, to take any action (whether by way of legal proceedings or otherwise) for preventing, restricting or abating such disposals or accumulations as are mentioned in sub-paragraph (i). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] In this section— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

- “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]**statutory provision *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]**”— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]
  - (a) in relation to Great Britain, means a provision, whether of a general or a special nature, contained in, or in any document made or issued under, any Act *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] or Act of the Scottish Parliament[^key-e30221b24e2108097632733842428e49] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf], whether of a general or a special nature, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]
  - (b) in relation to Northern Ireland, has the meaning given by section 1(f) of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] [^c427005]Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]
- “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]**local enactment *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]**” means— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]
  - (a) a local or private Act (including a local or private Act of the Parliament of Northern Ireland or a local or private Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly), or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]
  - (aa) an Act of the Scottish Parliament the Bill for which was a private Bill for the purposes of the standing orders of the Scottish Parliament,[^key-864517eb5b78c4fd551697db2f887842] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]
  - (b) an order confirmed by *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] the Scottish Parliament,[^key-c83c51dc64dfb6b16315a77b0fbf35ec] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] Parliament (or by the Parliament of Northern Ireland or the Northern Ireland Assembly) or brought into operation in accordance with special parliamentary procedure, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

and any reference to disposal, in relation to a statutory provision, is a reference to discharging or depositing a substance or allowing a substance to escape or to enter a stream or other place, as may be mentioned in that provision. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] The references to provisions of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] [^c427006]Water Resources Act 1991 in Part I of Schedule 3 shall have effect subject to the power c onferred by section 98 of that Act. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]
