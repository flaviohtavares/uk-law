# Radioactive Substances Act 1993, section 1C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-12-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/12
>

## 1C (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Processed radionuclides of natural terrestrial or cosmic origin[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^key-72d5d37176b07dc476a4c3ec6de53150]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A substance or article is radioactive material or radioactive waste where— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] it contains one or more of the radionuclides of natural terrestrial or cosmic origin which are listed in column 1 of Table 3; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] the substance or article— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
  - (i (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] is processed or is intended to be processed for the radioactive, fissile or fertile properties of those radionuclides; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
  - (ii (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] is contaminated by a substance or article to which sub-paragraph (i) applies, including where such contamination occurs indirectly through another contaminated substance or article; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] the substance or article is— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
  - (i (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] a solid or a relevant liquid and it has a concentration of radioactivity which exceeds the value specified in column 2 of Table 3; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
  - (ii (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] any other liquid or a gas. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
