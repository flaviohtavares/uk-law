(1A (Extent: N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8114071953846a999ca86d35f00894f3][^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] The circumstances referred to in subsection (1) are that— *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

(affected by repeal, see note))[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] no radionuclide other than tritium, promethium 147 or radium 226 is present in any clock or watch on the premises (whether or not any radioactive waste arises from it), *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

(1C (Extent: N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] This subsection is satisfied if— *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]
