# Radioactive Substances Act 1993, section 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-12-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/12

## 9 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Prohibition of use of mobile radioactive apparatus without registration.[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^key-a6221f42c5ba7f07b8194b5030354017]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a6221f42c5ba7f07b8194b5030354017] No person shall, for the purpose of any activities to which this section applies— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a6221f42c5ba7f07b8194b5030354017]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a6221f42c5ba7f07b8194b5030354017] keep, use, lend or let on hire mobile radioactive apparatus of any description, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a6221f42c5ba7f07b8194b5030354017]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a6221f42c5ba7f07b8194b5030354017] cause or permit mobile radioactive apparatus of any description to be kept, used, lent or let on hire, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a6221f42c5ba7f07b8194b5030354017]

unless he is registered under section 10 in respect of that apparatus or is exempted from registration under that section in respect of mobile radioactive apparatus of that description. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a6221f42c5ba7f07b8194b5030354017]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a6221f42c5ba7f07b8194b5030354017] This section applies to activities involving the use of the apparatus concerned for— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a6221f42c5ba7f07b8194b5030354017]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a6221f42c5ba7f07b8194b5030354017] testing, measuring or otherwise investigating any of the characteristics of substances or articles,or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a6221f42c5ba7f07b8194b5030354017]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a6221f42c5ba7f07b8194b5030354017] releasing quantities of radioactive material into the environment or introducing such material into organisms. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a6221f42c5ba7f07b8194b5030354017]
