## 46 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Effect of Act on other rights and duties.[^c-2ac21809531a4a9862c2925061ff3f57][^c-dd4222e514fcd2fac6b43c7d4cf4df06]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to the provisions of section 40 of this Act, and of section 18 of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dd4222e514fcd2fac6b43c7d4cf4df06] [^c427023]Interpretation Act 1978 (which relates to offences under two or more laws), nothing in this Act shall be construed as— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dd4222e514fcd2fac6b43c7d4cf4df06]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dd4222e514fcd2fac6b43c7d4cf4df06] conferring a right of action in any civil proceedings (other than proceedings for the recovery of a fine) in respect of any contravention of this Act, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dd4222e514fcd2fac6b43c7d4cf4df06]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dd4222e514fcd2fac6b43c7d4cf4df06] affecting any restriction imposed by or under *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dd4222e514fcd2fac6b43c7d4cf4df06]
  - (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ff802ce8a0f9ded321ebc72e0c3abdab][^c-dd4222e514fcd2fac6b43c7d4cf4df06] any other enactment, whether contained in a public general Act or in a local or private Act, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dd4222e514fcd2fac6b43c7d4cf4df06]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-57cadced4f4302c8acc983e57cea45eb][^c-dd4222e514fcd2fac6b43c7d4cf4df06] any Act of the Scottish Parliament, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dd4222e514fcd2fac6b43c7d4cf4df06]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dd4222e514fcd2fac6b43c7d4cf4df06] derogating from any right of action or other remedy (whether civil or criminal) in proceedings instituted otherwise than under this Act. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dd4222e514fcd2fac6b43c7d4cf4df06]
