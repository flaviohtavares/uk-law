## 42 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Application of Act to Crown.[^key-2ac21809531a4a9862c2925061ff3f57][^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] Subject to the following provisions of this section, the provisions of this Act shall bind the Crown. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to premises— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] occupied on behalf of the Crown for naval, military or air force purposes or for the purposes of the department of the Secretary of State having responsibility for defence, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] occupied by or for the purposes of a visiting force. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] No contravention by the Crown of any provision of this Act shall make the Crown criminally liable; but the High Court or, in Scotland, the Court of Session may, on the application of any authority charged with enforcing that provision, declare unlawful any act or omission of the Crown which constitutes such a contravention. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] Notwithstanding anything in subsection (3), the provisions of this Act shall apply to persons in the public service of the Crown as they apply to other persons. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] If the Secretary of State certifies that it appears to him requisite or expedient in the interests of national security that the powers of entry conferred by section 31 should not be exercisable in relation to any Crown premises specified in the certificate, those powers shall not be exercisable in relation to those premises; and in this subsection “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]**Crown premises *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]**” means premises held or used by or on behalf of the Crown. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] Where, in the case of any such premises as are mentioned in subsection (2)— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] arrangements are made whereby radioactive waste is not to be disposed of from those premises except with the approval of the chief inspector, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] in pursuance of those arrangements the chief inspector proposes to approve, or approves, the removal of radioactive waste from those premises to a place provided by a local authority as a place for the deposit of refuse, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]

the provisions of section 18 shall apply as if the proposal to approve the removal of the waste were an application for an authorisation under section 13 to remove it, or (as the case may be) the approval were such an authorisation. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] Nothing in this section shall be taken as in any way affecting Her Majesty in her private capacity; and this subsection shall be construed as if section 38(3) of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] [^c427018]Crown Proceedings Act 1947 (interpretation of references in that Act to Her Majesty in her private capacity) were contained in this Act. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]

(8 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] In this section “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]**visiting force *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]**” means any such body, contingent or detachment of the forces of any country as is a visiting force for the purposes of any of the provisions of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] [^c427019]Visiting Forces Act 1952. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]

(9 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] In the application of this section to Northern Ireland— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] references to the Crown shall include references to the Crown in right of Her Majesty’s Government in Northern Ireland, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c] the reference in subsection (5) to the Secretary of State shall have effect as a reference to the Department of the Environment for Northern Ireland. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2bd35b954923aa9631b383ed4aaa717c]
