# Radioactive Substances Act 1993, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-12-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Provision of facilities for disposal or accumulation of radioactive waste.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c2d5acf570374c840bd37f46add370a9] If it appears to the Secretary of State that adequate facilities are not available for the safe disposal or accumulation of radioactive waste, the Secretary of State may provide such facilities, or may arrange for their provision by such persons as the Secretary of State may think fit. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d5acf570374c840bd37f46add370a9]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c2d5acf570374c840bd37f46add370a9] Where, in the exercise of the power conferred by this section, the Secretary of State proposes to provide, or to arrange for the provision of, a place for the disposal or accumulation of radioactive waste, the Secretary of State, before carrying out that proposal, shall consult with any local authority in whose area that place would be situated, and with such other public or local authorities (if any) as appear to him to be proper to be consulted by him. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d5acf570374c840bd37f46add370a9]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c2d5acf570374c840bd37f46add370a9] The Secretary of State may make reasonable charges for the use of any facilities provided by him, or in accordance with arrangements made by him, under this section, or, in the case of facilities provided otherwise than by the Secretary of State, may direct that reasonable charges for the use of the facilities may be made by the person providing them in accordance with any such arrangements. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d5acf570374c840bd37f46add370a9]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c2d5acf570374c840bd37f46add370a9] In the application of this section to Northern Ireland, references to the Secretary of State shall have effect as references to the Department of the Environment for Northern Ireland. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d5acf570374c840bd37f46add370a9]
