# Radioactive Substances Act 1993, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-12-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Accumulation of radioactive waste.[^c441382][^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^key-c9bc7aba2a1e173f9daa858b64e4cc72]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c9bc7aba2a1e173f9daa858b64e4cc72] Subject to the provisions of this section and section 15, no person shall, except in accordance with an authorisation granted in that behalf under this section, accumulate any radioactive waste (with a view to its subsequent disposal) on any premises which are used for the purposes of an undertaking carried on by him, or cause or permit any radioactive waste to be so accumulated, if (in any such case) he knows or has reasonable grounds for believing it to be radioactive waste. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c9bc7aba2a1e173f9daa858b64e4cc72]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c9bc7aba2a1e173f9daa858b64e4cc72] Where the disposal of any radioactive waste has been authorised under section 13, and in accordance with that authorisation the waste is required or permitted to be accumulated with a view to its subsequent disposal, no further authorisation under this section shall be required to enable the waste to be accumulated in accordance with the authorisation granted under section 13. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c9bc7aba2a1e173f9daa858b64e4cc72]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c9bc7aba2a1e173f9daa858b64e4cc72] Subsection (1) shall not apply to the accumulation of radioactive waste on any premises situated on a nuclear site. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c9bc7aba2a1e173f9daa858b64e4cc72]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c9bc7aba2a1e173f9daa858b64e4cc72] For the purposes of this section, where radioactive material is produced, kept or used on any premises, and any substance arising from the production, keeping or use of that material is accumulated in a part of the premises appropriated for the purpose, and is retained there for a period of not less than three months, that substance shall, unless the contrary is proved, be presumed— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c9bc7aba2a1e173f9daa858b64e4cc72]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c9bc7aba2a1e173f9daa858b64e4cc72] to be radioactive waste, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c9bc7aba2a1e173f9daa858b64e4cc72]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c9bc7aba2a1e173f9daa858b64e4cc72] to be accumulated on the premises with a view to the subsequent disposal of the substance. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c9bc7aba2a1e173f9daa858b64e4cc72]
