(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] Any application for registration under this section shall be made to the chief inspector and shall— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]

- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] be accompanied by the prescribed fee. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] The particulars referred to in subsection (1)(a) are— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]

- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] the undertaking for the purposes of which those premises are used, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] the description or descriptions of radioactive material proposed to be kept or used on the premises, and the maximum quantity of radioactive material of each such description likely to be kept or used on the premises at any one time, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] the manner (if any) in which radioactive material is proposed to be used on the premises. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] On any application being made under this section, the chief inspector shall, subject to directions under section 25, send a copy of the application to each local authority in whose area the premises are situated. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] Subject to the following provisions of this section, where an application is made to the chief inspector for registration under this section in respect of any premises, the chief inspector may either— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] register the applicant in respect of those premises and in respect of the keeping and use on those premises of radioactive material of the description to which the application relates, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] if the application relates to two or more descriptions of radioactive material, register the applicant in respect of those premises and in respect of the keeping and use on those premises of such one or more of those descriptions of radioactive material as may be specified in the registration, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] refuse the application. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] An application for registration under this section which is duly made to the chief inspector may be treated by the applicant as having been refused if it is not determined within the prescribed period for determinations or within such longer period as may be agreed with the applicant. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] Any registration under this section in respect of any premises may (subject to subsection (7)) be effected subject to such limitations or conditions as the chief inspector thinks fit, and in particular (but without prejudice to the generality of this subsection) may be effected subject to conditions of any of the following descriptions— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] conditions imposing requirements (including, if the chief inspector thinks fit, requirements involving structural or other alterations) in respect of any part of the premises, or in respect of any apparatus, equipment or appliance used or to be used on any part of the premises for the purposes of any use of radioactive material from which radioactive waste is likely to arise, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] conditions requiring the person to whom the registration relates, at such times and in such manner as may be specified in the registration, to furnish the chief inspector with information as to the removal of radioactive material from those premises to any other premises, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] conditions prohibiting radioactive material from being sold or otherwise supplied from those premises unless it (or the container in which it is supplied) bears a label or other mark— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]
  - (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] indicating that it is radioactive material, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] if the conditions so require, indicating the description of radioactive material to which it belongs, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]

  and (in either case) complying with any relevant requirements specified in the conditions. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443] In the exercise of any power conferred on him by subsection (4) or (6), the chief inspector *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443][^c-f0f326cbcb3a0417fc75afa54ee47f15]..., shall have regard exclusively to the amount and character of the radioactive waste likely to arise from the keeping or use of radioactive material on the premises in question. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44ac273b6e72e5b13b2e678f1e0f0443]
