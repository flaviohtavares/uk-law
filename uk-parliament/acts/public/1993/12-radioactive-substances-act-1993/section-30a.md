## 30A (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Recovery and disposal of orphan sources[^c-2ac21809531a4a9862c2925061ff3f57][^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6][^c-c1f79fe83ae3f7532c602e9546db372e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6] The appropriate Agency shall be prepared or have made provision, including assignment of responsibilities, to *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6] control and *(Extent: N.I.)*[^c-8915ea1f6afddc4c98de6447bd99c113] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6] recover any orphan source and shall have drawn up appropriate response plans and measures. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6] The appropriate Agency shall have the power to recover any expenses reasonably incurred by it (or by a person on its behalf) in the recovery and disposal of an orphan source from the holder of that source or from the occupier or owner of the premises where the source is located. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6] For the purposes of paragraph (2), “holder” means the person who is or is required to be registered or authorised under this Act in relation to that orphan source. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6] If the relevant person thinks fit, the relevant person may make available to the appropriate Agency a sum or sums of money in respect of costs and expenses incurred or to be incurred by the appropriate Agency (or by a person on its behalf) in relation to the recovery and disposal of orphan sources where— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6] the amount of such costs and expenses exceeds or is expected to exceed any reasonable provision for such costs and expenses made by the appropriate Agency, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6] the making available of such sum or sums is necessary to enable the recovery and disposal of any orphan source. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6] In subsection (4), “relevant person” means— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6] in relation to England, the Secretary of State, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6] in relation to Wales, the National Assembly for Wales, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6] in relation to Scotland, the Scottish Ministers, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6] in relation to Northern Ireland, the Department of the Environment. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6] In the application of this section to Northern Ireland a reference to the appropriate Agency must be taken to be a reference to the chief inspector. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1e1373bb35d4debaae664a0c598d01d6]
