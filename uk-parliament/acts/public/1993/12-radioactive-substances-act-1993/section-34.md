# Radioactive Substances Act 1993, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-12-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/12

## 34 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Disclosure of trade secrets.[^c-2ac21809531a4a9862c2925061ff3f57][^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59] If any person discloses any information relating to any relevant process or trade secret used in carrying on any particular undertaking which has been given to or obtained by him under this Act or in connection with the execution of this Act, he shall be guilty of an offence, unless the disclosure is made— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59] with the consent of the person carrying on that undertaking, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59] in accordance with any general or special directions given by the Secretary of State, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59]
- (bb (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c426984][^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59] under or by virtue of section 113 of the Environment Act 1995, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59] in connection with the execution of this Act, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59] for the purposes of any legal proceedings arising out of this Act or of any report of any such proceedings. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59] A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59] on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months, or both; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59] on conviction on indictment, to a fine or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or both. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59] In this section “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59]**relevant process *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59]**” means any process applied for the purposes of, or in connection with, the production or use of radioactive material. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59] In the application of this section to Northern Ireland, the reference in subsection (1)(b) to the Secretary of State shall have effect as a reference to the Department of the Environment for Northern Ireland. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dec2e2e68d78615c93576d3e620df59]
