## 16A (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Transfer of authorisations[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f][^key-d6a1d274c9b4f144163ec1b0f5d01baa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] This section applies where— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] a person (“ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]**the transferor *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]**”) holds an authorisation granted under section 13 in respect of the disposal of radioactive waste on or from premises situated on a nuclear site; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] an application is made under this section for a transfer (in whole or in part) of that authorisation to another person (“ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]**the transferee *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]**”). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] An application under this section is one which— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] is made to the authorising authority jointly by the transferor and the transferee; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] is accompanied by the appropriate amount; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] in the case of an application for a transfer relating to part only of the premises, identifies the part in question. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] The appropriate amount for the purposes of subsection (2) is— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] if the application is made to the appropriate Agency, the amount of the charge (if any) that is prescribed for the purpose by a charging scheme under section 41 of the Environment Act 1995; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] if it is made to the chief inspector, the prescribed fee. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] The authorising authority must, on receipt of the application (but subject to directions under section 25 and to subsection (6)), send a copy of the application to every local authority in whose area radioactive waste may be disposed of under the authorisation to which the application relates. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] Before granting the application, the authorising authority must (subject to subsection (6)) consult everyone whom it would have been required to consult under section 16(4A) and (5) if— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] the transferee had applied for the grant of the authorisation that he would hold were the application to be granted; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] in the case of a partial transfer, the transferor had applied for the grant (in place of his existing authorisation) of the authorisation he would hold in those circumstances. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] The authorising authority may proceed with the application without— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] sending a copy of the application to a local authority mentioned in subsection (4), or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] consulting an authority or body mentioned in section 16(5) about the proposed transfer, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

if it appears to the authorising authority that arrangements for the disposal of radioactive waste are unlikely to be changed, as a result of the transfer, in a way that would be of interest to that authority or body. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] The authorising authority may grant the application if, and only if, it is satisfied— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] that the transferee has or will have operational control over the disposals to which the transferred authorisation will relate; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] that he is able and willing to ensure compliance with the limitations and conditions of the authorisation that he will hold if the application is granted; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] that no other grounds exist on which it would be reasonable to refuse to grant the application. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

(8 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] Where the authorising authority grants the application, it must— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] fix the date from which the transfer applied for is to have effect; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-4b4d6efade24adb2569f653c1c9665e4][^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] furnish the transferee with a certificate which contains all material particulars of the authorisation he holds as a result of the transfer or gives sufficient information as to the particulars to enable them to be ascertained; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] in the case of a partial transfer, furnish the transferor with a similar certificate as respects the authorisation he holds as a result of the transfer; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] subject to directions under section 25, send a copy of the certificate furnished to the transferee, and of any certificate furnished to the transferor— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]
  - (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] to every local authority in whose area radioactive waste may be disposed of under the authorisation to which the certificate relates; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] to every person consulted about the transfer under so much of subsection (5) as requires consultation in accordance with section 16(5). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

(9 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] The time fixed as the time from which the transfer is to have effect must be not less than twenty-eight days after the day (if any) on which the authorising authority, when it fixes that time, expects copies of the certificates mentioned in paragraph (d) of subsection (8) to be sent out in accordance with that paragraph. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

(10 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] Subsection (9) does not apply if, in the opinion of the authorising authority, it is necessary for the transfer to have immediate effect or otherwise to be expedited. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

(11 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] In this section “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] **authorising authority *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]** ”— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] in relation to an authorisation having effect in Great Britain, means the appropriate Agency; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f] in relation to an authorisation having effect in Northern Ireland, means the chief inspector. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c760bbf9ba28ce4135c594a6006772f]
