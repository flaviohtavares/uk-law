# Radioactive Substances Act 1993, section 1H

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-12-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/12

## 1H (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Substances or articles after disposal[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^c-72d5d37176b07dc476a4c3ec6de53150]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] Subject to subsections (2) and (3), a substance or article is not radioactive material or radioactive waste during the excluded period where— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] the substance or article has been disposed of lawfully, and at the time of the disposal no further act of disposal is intended in respect of it; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] the substance or article— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
  - (i (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] is contaminated by a substance or article to which paragraph (a) applies, including where such contamination occurs indirectly through another contaminated substance or article; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
  - (ii (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] in the absence of such contamination, would not otherwise be radioactive material or radioactive waste under this Act; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
  - (iii (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] is not contaminated with the intention of using its radioactive, fissile or fertile properties. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] In subsection (1), “the excluded period” means the period— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] beginning at the relevant start time; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] ending in the circumstances specified in subsection (4). *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] The relevant start time is— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] where the substance or article has been disposed of and— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
  - (i (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] is solid at the time of the disposal; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
  - (ii (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] is disposed of by burial (whether underground or otherwise) on premises in relation to which an authorisation under section 13 of this Act is held at the time of disposal; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
  - (iii (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] is disposed of in accordance with that authorisation, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]

  the time of the revocation of that authorisation; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] where the substance or article is contaminated by a substance or article to which paragraph (a) applies, including where such contamination occurs indirectly through another contaminated substance or article, the time of the revocation of the authorisation referred to in paragraph (a)(ii); or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] in relation to any other substance or article— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
  - (i (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] the time of the disposal of it; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
  - (ii (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] where the substance or article is one to which subsection (1)(b) applies, the time of the disposal of the substance or article that caused it, directly or indirectly, to be contaminated. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860] The excluded period ends where, after the beginning of the excluded period, the substance or article is subject to a process which leads to an increase in the radiation exposure of the public or any plant or animal, at the time of that increase. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860]
