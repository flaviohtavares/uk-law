## 50. Repeals.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The enactments and instruments specified in Schedule 6 (which include spent enactments) are repealed or, as the case may be, revoked to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule, but subject to any provision at the end of any Part of that Schedule. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]
