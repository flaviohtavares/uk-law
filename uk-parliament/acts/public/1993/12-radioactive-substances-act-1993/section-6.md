# Radioactive Substances Act 1993, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-12-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1993/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Prohibition of use of radioactive material without registration.[^c-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^c-9fa29dfea73f4ff1f76c6b4646cbfbe8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No person shall, on any premises which are used for the purposes of an undertaking carried on by him, keep or use, or cause or permit to be kept or used, radioactive material of any description, knowing or having reasonable grounds for believing it to be radioactive material, unless either— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9fa29dfea73f4ff1f76c6b4646cbfbe8]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9fa29dfea73f4ff1f76c6b4646cbfbe8] he is registered under section 7 in respect of those premises and in respect of the keeping and use on those premises of radioactive material of that description, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9fa29dfea73f4ff1f76c6b4646cbfbe8]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9fa29dfea73f4ff1f76c6b4646cbfbe8] he is exempted from registration under that section in respect of those premises and in respect of the keeping and use on those premises of radioactive material of that description, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9fa29dfea73f4ff1f76c6b4646cbfbe8]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9fa29dfea73f4ff1f76c6b4646cbfbe8] the radioactive material in question consists of mobile radioactive apparatus in respect of which a person is registered under section 10 or is exempted from registration under that section. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9fa29dfea73f4ff1f76c6b4646cbfbe8]
