## 8 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Exemptions from registration under s. 7.[^key-c4d4961743ab21faedb9b65cf41d0860][^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] At any time while a nuclear site licence is in force in respect of a site, and at any time after the revocation or surrender of such a licence but before the period of responsibility of the licensee has come to an end, the licensee (subject to subsection (2)) is exempted from registration under section 7 in respect of any premises situated on that site and in respect of the keeping and use on those premises of radioactive material of every description. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] Where, in the case of any such premises as are mentioned in subsection (1), it appears to the chief inspector that, if the licensee had been required to apply for registration under section 7 in respect of those premises, the chief inspector would have imposed conditions such as are mentioned in paragraph (b) or (c) of subsection (6) of that section, the chief inspector may direct that the exemption conferred by subsection (1) of this section shall have effect subject to such conditions (being conditions which in the opinion of the chief inspector correspond to those which he would so have imposed) as may be specified in the direction. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] On giving a direction under subsection (2) in respect of any premises, the chief inspector shall furnish the licensee with a copy of the direction. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2b0864485125ce9129840361ec8e95c8][^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2b0864485125ce9129840361ec8e95c8][^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] The Secretary of State may by order grant further exemptions from registration under section 7, by reference to such classes of premises and undertakings, and such descriptions of radioactive material, as may be specified in the order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] Any exemption granted by an order under subsection (6) may be granted subject to such limitations or conditions as may be specified in the order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]

(8 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf] In the application of this section to Northern Ireland, the reference in subsection (6) to the Secretary of State shall have effect as a reference to the Department of the Environment for Northern Ireland. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-be99045f66d431b485bf80fe6e743ddf]
