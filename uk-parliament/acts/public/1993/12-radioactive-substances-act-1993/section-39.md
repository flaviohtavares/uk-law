## 39 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Public access to documents and records.[^c-2ac21809531a4a9862c2925061ff3f57][^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc] The chief inspector shall keep copies of— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc] all applications made to him under any provision of this Act, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc] all documents issued by him under any provision of this Act, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc] all other documents sent by him to any local authority in pursuance of directions of the Secretary of State, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc] such records of convictions under section 32, 33, 34 or 35 as may be prescribed in regulations; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc]

and he shall make copies of those documents available to the public except to the extent that that would involve the disclosure of information relating to any relevant process or trade secret or would involve the disclosure of applications or certificates as respects which the Secretary of State has directed that knowledge should be restricted on grounds of national security. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc] Each local authority shall keep and make available to the public copies of all documents sent to the authority under any provision of this Act unless directed by the chief inspector or, as the case may be, the appropriate Minister and the chief inspector, that all or any part of any such document is not to be available for inspection. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc] Directions under subsection (2) shall only be given for the purpose of preventing disclosure of relevant processes or trade secrets and may be given generally in respect of all, or any description of, documents or in respect of specific documents. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc] The copies of documents required to be made available to the public by this section need not be kept in documentary form. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc] The public shall have the right to inspect the copies of documents required to be made available under this section at all reasonable times and, on payment of a reasonable fee, to be provided with a copy of any such document. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc] In this section “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc]**relevant process *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc]**” has the same meaning as in section 34. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc] In the application of this section to Northern Ireland, references to the Secretary of State shall have effect as references to the Department of the Environment for Northern Ireland. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bf4eee96b2531876e2d74803bc7ff1fc]
