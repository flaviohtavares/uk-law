# Clean Air Act 1993, section 28A

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28A. Guidance for local authorities in England[^c-15a15b1156c7b4a65ec54acf0d2e9008]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A local authority in England must have regard to any guidance published by the Secretary of State about the exercise of the authority’s functions under this Part.
