# Clean Air Act 1993, section 23

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Acquisition and sale of unauthorised fuel in a smoke control area.[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] Any person who— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] acquires any solid fuel for use in a building in a smoke control area otherwise than in a building or fireplace exempted from the operation of section 20 (prohibition of smoke emissions in smoke control area); *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] acquires any solid fuel for use in any fixed boiler or industrial plant in a smoke control area, not being a boiler or plant so exempted; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] sells by retail any solid fuel for delivery by him or on his behalf to— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]
  - (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] a building in a smoke control area; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] premises in such an area in which there is any fixed boiler or industrial plant, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]

shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] In subsection (1), “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]**solid fuel *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]**” means any solid fuel other than an authorised fuel. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] Subsection (1) shall, in its application to a smoke control area in which the operation of section 20 is limited by a smoke control order to specified classes of buildings, boilers or plant, have effect as if references to a building, boiler or plant were references to a building, boiler or plant of a class specified in the order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] The power of the Secretary of State under section 22 (exemptions relating to particular areas) to suspend or relax the operation of section 20 in relation to the whole or any part of a smoke control area includes power to suspend or relax the operation of subsection (1) in relation to the whole or any part of such an area. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] In proceedings for an offence under this section consisting of the sale of fuel for delivery to a building or premises, it shall be a defence for the person accused to prove that he believed and had reasonable grounds for believing— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] that the building was exempted from the operation of section 20 or, in a case where the operation of that section is limited to specified classes of building, was not of a specified class; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] that the fuel was acquired for use in a fireplace, boiler or plant so exempted or, in a case where the operation of that section is limited to specified classes of boilers or plant, in a boiler or plant not of a specified class. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]
