## 20 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Prohibition on emission of smoke in smoke control area.[^c10490481][^c10490491][^c-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] If, on any day, smoke is emitted from a chimney of any building within a smoke control area, the occupier of the building shall be guilty of an offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] If, on any day, smoke is emitted from a chimney (not being a chimney of a building) which serves the furnace of any fixed boiler or industrial plant within a smoke control area, the person having possession of the boiler or plant shall be guilty of an offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] Subsections (1) and (2) have effect— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] subject to any exemptions for the time being in force under section 18, 21 or 22; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] subject to section 51 (duty to notify offences to occupier or other person liable). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] In proceedings for an offence under this section it shall be a defence to prove that the alleged emission was not caused by the use of any fuel other than an authorised fuel. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1] A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-994953bf38659969bbd8142e491d2af1]
