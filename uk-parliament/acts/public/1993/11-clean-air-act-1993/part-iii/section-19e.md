> Text as at 2026-07-01.

## 19E. Penalty for emission of smoke in smoke control area in Wales[^key-4e04277b53d3dc94104038d30bf74f79]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 1A makes provision for financial penalties in relation to the emission of smoke in smoke control areas in Wales.
