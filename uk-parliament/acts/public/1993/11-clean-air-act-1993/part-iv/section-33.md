# Clean Air Act 1993, section 33

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 33. Cable burning.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person who burns insulation from a cable with a view to recovering metal from the cable shall be guilty of an offence unless the burning is part of [^c-eae60e76a17c80d1abff344a140c188f]...an activity subject to regulations under section 2 of the Pollution Prevention and Control Act 1999 or section 18 of the Regulatory Reform (Scotland) Act 2014 *(Extent: S)*[^c-a20a66acd7ae0dbb4180e156e7dd02e2]

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
