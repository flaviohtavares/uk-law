# Clean Air Act 1993, section 66

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 66. Transitory provisions relating to Alkali, &c. Works Regulation Act 1906.[^c10491791]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Until the coming into force of the repeal by the [^c10491801]Environmental Protection Act 1990 of the [^c10491811]Alkali, &c. Works Regulation Act 1906—

- (a) Part I of Schedule 3 shall have effect;
- (b) this Act shall have effect subject to the modifications in Part II of that Schedule; and
- (c) the Alkali, &c. Works Regulation Act 1906 shall continue to have effect as amended by Schedule 2 to the [^c10491821]Clean Air Act 1956 notwithstanding the repeal by this Act of the last-mentioned Act.

(2) On the coming into force of the repeal by the [^c10491831]Environmental Protection Act 1990 of the [^c10491841]Alkali, &c. Works Regulation Act 1906, this section and Schedule 3 shall cease to have effect.
