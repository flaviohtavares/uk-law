# Clean Air Act 1993, section 63

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 63. Regulations and orders.[^c10491501]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any power of the Secretary of State under this Act to make an order or regulations—

- (a) includes power to make different provision in the order or regulations for different circumstances;
- (b) includes power to make such incidental, supplemental and transitional provision as the Secretary of State considers appropriate; and
- (c) is exercisable by statutory instrument except in the case of the powers conferred by sections 19(4) and 60 and paragraph 3 of Schedule 3.

(2) Any statutory instrument containing regulations made under this Act by the Secretary of State[^c-f6af7e887c966b47af1cbb0d49f6f1f2], except an instrument containing regulations a draft of which is required by section 6(3), 10(5) or 47(2) or paragraph 3(4) or 4(6) of Schedule 1A[^c-dee149c15b8cd9942c9afe8e3daa24c8] to be approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament, shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(2A)[^c-2415899586f43d1c3a997c1309177907] Any statutory instrument containing regulations made under this Act by the Welsh Ministers, except an instrument containing regulations a draft of which is required by section 6(3), 10(5) or 47(2) or paragraph 3(5) or 4(7) of Schedule 1A to be approved by a resolution of Senedd Cymru, is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of Senedd Cymru.

(3) Any statutory instrument containing an order under section 19C[^c-52ef4c3432d750fa97f4cbe3ecd1315f] [^c-5a7f0e1813337c27a346262547b3c630]... shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
