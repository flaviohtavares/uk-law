# Clean Air Act 1993, section 41

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Relation to Environmental Protection Act 1990.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c3053e2318e3b92dd211127bf1e80375] Parts I to III shall not apply to any process which is a prescribed process as from the date which is the determination date for that process. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c3053e2318e3b92dd211127bf1e80375]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c3053e2318e3b92dd211127bf1e80375] The “determination date” for a prescribed process is— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c3053e2318e3b92dd211127bf1e80375]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c3053e2318e3b92dd211127bf1e80375] in the case of a process for which an authorisation is granted, the date on which the enforcing authority grants it, whether in pursuance of the application or, on an appeal, of a direction to grant it, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c3053e2318e3b92dd211127bf1e80375]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c3053e2318e3b92dd211127bf1e80375] in the case of a process for which an authorisation is refused, the date of the refusal or, on an appeal, of the affirmation of the refusal. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c3053e2318e3b92dd211127bf1e80375]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c3053e2318e3b92dd211127bf1e80375] In this section “authorisation”, “enforcing authority” and “prescribed process” have the meaning given in section 1 of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 and the reference to an appeal is a reference to an appeal under section 15 of that Act. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c3053e2318e3b92dd211127bf1e80375]
