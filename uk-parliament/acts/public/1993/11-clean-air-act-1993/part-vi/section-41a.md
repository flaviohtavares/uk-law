# Clean Air Act 1993, section 41A

> Text as at 2026-07-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 41A. Relation to the Pollution Prevention and Control Act 1999 and Regulatory Reform (Scotland) Act 2014.[^c10491071][^c-2c5ca4d38baccda836582929482aed14]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where an activity is subject to regulations under section 2 of the Pollution Prevention and Control Act 1999 (regulation of polluting activities) or section 18 of the Regulatory Reform (Scotland) Act 2014[^c-af3249c09d51fff8a58be1a662196681] , Parts I to III of this Act shall not apply as from the determination date for the activity in question.

(2) The “determination date”, for an activity, is—

- (a) in the case of an activity for which a permit or authorisation[^c-e97f074b5058a722b0ee273207ae8c60] is granted, the date on which it is granted, whether in pursuance of the application, or on an appeal, of a direction to grant it;
- (b) in the case of an activity for which a permit or authorisation[^c-426940ba2a310a6186d5abd2ce2bd264] is refused, the date of refusal or, on appeal, of the affirmation of the refusal;

*Extent: E+W*

- (c)[^c-489ca00306e7fc9ca469df6f94f0d1f3] in the case of an activity that is an exempt waste operation, the date of the entry on the register maintained under paragraph 11 of Schedule 2[^c-3793b061f53c8db11952c362833b15ee][^c-c91cfbacfa90c745e9331f0897c84224] (exempt facilities: general) to the Environmental Permitting Regulations of an establishment or undertaking in relation to that operation.

(3) In subsection (2)— *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-c3a79b1d1751ecb01b9a84cd3bab60c3]

- “exempt waste operation” has the meaning given in *(Extent: E+W)* the Environmental Permitting Regulations *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-1cd778032974a09247d8fb0192d61f12] *(Extent: E+W)*; *(Extent: E+W)*
- (a)[^c-89322c5c71c9e999dbd22e8d0c0c1548] “permit” means a permit under regulations under section 2 of the Pollution Prevention and Control Act 1999 and the reference to an appeal is a reference to an appeal under those regulations.

  ; and[^c-c6a33e07aedc8e2f1f10a11e476ee12c]
- (b) “**authorisation**” means an authorisation under regulations made under section 18 of the Regulatory Reform (Scotland) Act 2014, and the reference to an appeal is to an appeal under those regulations.

*Extent: E+W*

(4)[^c-1a10f9922cf2eda18f30d2ec47861fc4] In this section—

- activity” includes—[^c-033e0a0ad34beff37c6a27785f616cc4]
  - (a) a waste operation, or mining waste operation, within the meaning of the Environmental Permitting Regulations;[^c-c0944e5431bdbb5d726adcec44ba0e03], and
  - (b) a mining waste operation within the meaning of those Regulations;
- “the Environmental Permitting Regulations” means[^c-0c8c4503a62e07abdb3d577f63d930dc] the Environmental Permitting (England and Wales) Regulations 2016 ([^c-a3e2ae5f3dfc53464e3055f7aa707e01][S.I. 2016/1154](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/1154)) .
