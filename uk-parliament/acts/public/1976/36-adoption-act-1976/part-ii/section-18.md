# Adoption Act 1976, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2009-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1976/36

## 18. Freeing child for adoption. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c13480161][^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6][^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where, on an application by an adoption agency, an authorised court is satisfied in the case of each parent or guardian of the child that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]

- (a) he freely, and with full understanding of what is involved, agrees generally and unconditionally to the making of an adoption order, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]
- (b) his agreement to the making of an adoption order should be dispensed with on a ground specified in section 16(2), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]

the court shall make an order declaring the child free for adoption. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]

(2) No application shall be made under subsection (1) unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]

- (a) it is made with the consent of a parent or guardian of a child, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]
- (b) the adoption agency is applying for dispensation under subsection (1)(b) of the agreement of each parent or guardian of the child, and the child is in the care of the adoption agency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]

(2A)[^c13480171] For the purposes of subsection (2) a child is in the care of an adoption agency if the adoption agency is a local authority and he is in their care. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]

(3) No agreement required under subsection (1)(a) shall be dispensed with under subsection (1)(b) unless the child is already placed for adoption or the court is satisfied that it is likely that the child will be placed for adoption. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]

(4) An agreement by the mother of the child is ineffective for the purposes of this section if given less than 6 weeks after the child’s birth. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]

(5) On the making of an order under this section, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6] parental responsibility for the child is given to[^c13480191] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6] the adoption agency, and subsections (2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6] to (4)[^c13480201] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6] of section 12 apply as if the order were an adoption order and the agency were the adopters. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]

(6) Before making an order under this section, the court shall satisfy itself, in relation to each parent or guardian *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6] of the child who can be found[^c13480211] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6], that he has been given an opportunity of making, if he so wishes, a declaration that he prefers not to be involved in future questions concerning the adoption of the child; and any such declaration shall be recorded by the court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]

(7)[^c13480221] Before making an order under this section in the case of a child whose father does not have parental responsibility for him, the court shall satisfy itself in relation to any person claiming to be the father that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]

- (a) he has no intention of applying for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]
  - (i) an order under section 4(1) of the Children Act 1989, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6] a residence order under section 10 of that Act, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]
- (b) if he did make any such application, it would be likely to be refused. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]

(8) Subsections (5) and (7) of section 12 apply in relation to the making of an order under this section as they apply in relation to the making of an order under that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eef3a6c5e97ee17bdb8c67f97344d0a6]
