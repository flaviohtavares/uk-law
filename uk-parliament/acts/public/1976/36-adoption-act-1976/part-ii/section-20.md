# Adoption Act 1976, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2009-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1976/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. Revocation of s. 18 order. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1][^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The former parent, at any time more than 12 months after the making of the order under section 18 when— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]

- (a) no adoption order has been made in respect of the child, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]
- (b) the child does not have his home with a person with whom he has been placed for adoption, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]

may apply to the court which made the order for a further order revoking it on the ground that he wishes to resume *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1] parental responsibility[^c13480321] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1].

(2) While the application is pending the adoption agency having *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1] parental responsibility *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1] shall not place the child for adoption without the leave of the court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]

(3)[^c13480341] The revocation of an order under section 18 ( “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]**a section 18 order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]**”) operates— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]

- (a) to extinguish the parental responsibility given to the adoption agency under the section 18 order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]
- (b) to give parental responsibility for the child to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]
  - (i) the child’s mother; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1] where the child’s father and mother were married to each other at the time of his birth, the father; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]
- (c) to revive— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]
  - (i) any parental responsibility agreement, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1] any order under section 4(1) of the Children Act 1989, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]
  - (iia (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-dc41f78305dbafd75b63d3c279b2aece][^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1] any care order, within the meaning of that Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1] any appointment of a guardian in respect of the child (whether made by a court or otherwise), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]

  extinguished by the making of the section 18 order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]

(3A) Subject to subsection (3)(c), the revocation does not— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]

- (a) operate to revive— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]
  - (i) any order under the Children Act 1989, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1] any duty referred to in section 12(3)(b), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]

  extinguished by the making of the section 18 order; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]
- (b) affect any person’s parental responsibility so far as it relates to the period between the making of the section 18 order and the date of revocation of that order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]

(4) Subject to subsection (5), if the application is dismissed on the ground that to allow it would contravene the principle embodied in section 6— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]

- (a) the former parent who made the application shall not be entitled to make any further application under subsection (1) in respect of the child, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]
- (b) the adoption agency is released from the duty of complying further with section 19(3) as respects that parent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]

(5) Subsection (4)(a) shall not apply where the court which dismissed the application gives leave to the former parent to make a further application under subsection (1), but such leave shall not be given unless it appears to the court that because of a change in circumstances or for any other reason it is proper to allow the application to be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]
