# Adoption Act 1976, section 21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2009-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1976/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 21. Variation of section 18 order so as to substitute one adoption agency for another. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c13480381][^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1][^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) On an application to which this section applies, an authorised court may vary an order under section 18 so as to give parental responsibility for the child to another adoption agency (the substitute agency”) in place of the agency for the time being having parental responsibility for the child under the order ( “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]**the existing agency *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]**”).

(2) This section applies to any application made jointly by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]

- (a) the existing agency; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]
- (b) the would-be substitute agency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]

(3) Where an order under section 18 is varied under this section, section 19 shall apply as if the substitute agency had been given responsibility for the child on the making of the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-51ffa7be4230233abff416232bc15ad1]
