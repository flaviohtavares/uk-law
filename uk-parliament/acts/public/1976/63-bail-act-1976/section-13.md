# Bail Act 1976, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-04-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1976/63
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Short title, commencement, application and extent.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This act may be cited as the Bail Act 1976.

(2)[^c8004041] This Act (except this section) shall come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order in a statutory instrument appoint.

(3) Section 1 of this Act applies to bail grantable by the Court Martial Appeal Court[^key-73d7c83fe37529d865e66803a495d4c1] when sitting outside England and Wales and accordingly section 6 of this Act applies to a failure outside England and Wales by a person granted bail by that Court to surrender to custody.

(4) Except as provided by subsection (3) above and with the exception of so much of section 8 as relates to entering into recognizances in Scotland and paragraphs 31 and 46 of Schedule 2 to this Act, this Act does not extend beyond England and Wales.
