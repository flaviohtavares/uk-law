# Bail Act 1976, section 12

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Amendments, repeals and transitional provisions.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c8004021] Schedule 2 to this Act (which contains consequential and minor amendments of enactments) shall have effect.

(2)[^c8004021] The enactments specified in Schedule 3 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.

(3) The transitional provisions contained in Schedule 4 to this Act shall have effect.
