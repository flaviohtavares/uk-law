## 38. Prosecution of offences.

*Extent: E+W*

Without prejudice to section 222 of the [^c13230901]Local Government Act 1972 (power of local authorities to prosecute or defend legal proceedings), proceedings for an offence under any provision of this Act except section 31(6) may be instituted by the housing authority concerned.
