# Rent (Agriculture) Act 1976, section 5

## 5. No statutory tenancy where landlord’s interest belongs to Crown or to local authority, etc.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c13229381] A person shall not at any time be a statutory tenant of a dwelling-house if the interest of his immediate landlord would, at that time—

- (a) belong to Her Majesty in right of the Crown or to a government department, or
- (b) be held in trust for Her Majesty for the purposes of a government department;

except that an interest belonging to Her Majesty in right of the Crown shall not prevent a person from being a statutory tenant if the interest is under the management of the Crown Estate Commissioners.

(2)[^c13229391][^key-e39f51c405b67f6fc1d064e3860be1e1][^key-88f3a07d46853c5eee508df542b82133][^key-a94dc7f350427be581aa96d106c0b581][^key-6fbbaacdc186ebbd1f642e3c83240bf7] A person shall not at any time be a statutory tenant of a dwelling-house if the interest of his immediate landlord would, at that time, belong to any of the bodies specified in subsection (3) below.

(3)[^key-e39f51c405b67f6fc1d064e3860be1e1][^key-88f3a07d46853c5eee508df542b82133][^key-a94dc7f350427be581aa96d106c0b581][^key-6fbbaacdc186ebbd1f642e3c83240bf7] The bodies referred to in subsection (2) above are—

- (a) the council of a county , county borough[^c13229401] or district or, in the application of this Act to the Isles of Scilly, the Council of those Isles;
- (b) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c13229411]the council of a London borough or the Common Council of the City of London;
- (baa)[^key-b469cfaf805a36bcae00ca1804cde1ab][^key-c1b167b6bd7550f9b0edb0750e0f45fd][^key-ce1314743bef33b4087ffa61bb03d603] a police and crime commissioner;
- (ba) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c13229451]
- (bb)[^c13229471][^c13229461] a joint authority established by Part IV of the Local Government Act 1985;
- (bbza)[^key-1aad6d2e6f01a3c48541751dabbaab92] an economic prosperity board established under section 88 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009;
- (bbzb) a combined authority established under section 103 of that Act;
- (bbzc)[^key-11ac1fda49f4da48415b7ea3727558b9] a combined county authority established under section 9(1) of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023;
- (bba)[^key-5cefc953ff399cb8b4419205ea410980] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (bbb)[^key-03b2424db7dc331e0b014954a644d099] the London Fire Commissioner;
- (bbc)[^key-6a88f84a7515788d8e1bc33c14c09399] a fire and rescue authority created by an order under section 4A of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004;
- (bc)[^c13229491] the Broads Authority;
- (bd)[^c13229501] any National Park authority;
- (c) the English new towns residuary body[^key-da57ec9b30a1fd5bee1eec0ade716529]
- (d)[^key-dac23c4328b39fb9a4c33e592a0b694d] the Regulator of Social Housing[^key-6d8a66b55a86e66e66f4f1a5ed48f598];
- (da)[^c13229511] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) a development corporation established by an order made, or having effect as if made, under the [^c13229531] New Towns Act 1981[^c13229521] , and
- (f) a housing trust (as defined in section 15(5) of the[^c13229541] [^c13229551] Rent Act 1977 ) which is a charity [^key-1b32e79ad2a3a0fc63ff2f8b5bcf5538]....

(3A)[^key-38bbdae8966b8ed77459cb80564085d1] In subsection (3)(c) above “ **English new towns residuary body** ” means the Homes and Communities Agency so far as exercising functions in relation to anything transferred (or to be transferred) to it as mentioned in section 52(1)(a) to (d) of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 or the Greater London Authority so far as exercising its new towns and urban development functions[^key-270d2c6039790eabad5619a7284e3a0d].

(4)[^key-e39f51c405b67f6fc1d064e3860be1e1][^key-88f3a07d46853c5eee508df542b82133][^key-a94dc7f350427be581aa96d106c0b581][^key-6fbbaacdc186ebbd1f642e3c83240bf7] If any of the conditions for the time being specified in section 15(4) of the[^c13229571] [^c13229581] Rent Act 1977 (conditions for the operation of the corresponding provision of that Act) is fulfilled, a person shall not be a statutory tenant of a dwelling-house at any time if the interest of his immediate landlord would, at that time, belong to a housing association which—[^c13229591]

- (za)[^key-a064a81df5be0ae8965ac095217c22a7] is a private registered provider of social housing,
- (a) is a registered social landlord within the meaning of the Housing Act 1985 (see section 5(4) and (5) of that Act)[^c13229601], or
- (b) is a co-operative housing association within the meaning of the Housing Associations Act 1985[^c13229611].
