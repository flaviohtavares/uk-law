## 32. Power of local authorities to execute works outside their areas.[^c7226651][^c7226661][^key-ab2576203615571f8919750efa744b14][^key-1791038167e1e8ace060d0987e7ee39b][^key-c126703135e7fb11511d95347608873a][^key-7aa1338860856433e931d46e9c33dde5][^key-82a0a935ffbe2731547f84fa0c3931b1][^key-b9430db05bc0b7f43fc6c9ac179e4400][^key-78ed82617647d83968083bbc0b89b589][^key-7042bb776d3c2577dcbb4e7d450b2248]

*Extent: E+W*

Any power to execute works which is conferred on a local authority by any enactment may, unless the contrary intention appears in that or any other enactment, be exercised outside as well as inside the area of the authority.
