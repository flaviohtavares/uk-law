# Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1976, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1976/57

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 76. Penalties.[^c-202746addf02bed8ee1792b3cb97ea8b]

*Extent: E+W*

Any person who commits an offence against any of the provisions of this Part of this Act in respect of which no penalty is expressly provided shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale[^c7230631].
