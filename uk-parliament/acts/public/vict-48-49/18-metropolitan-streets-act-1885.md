# Metropolitan Streets Act 1885

> This law has been repealed or revoked. Ended on 1993-11-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Metropolitan Streets Act 1885 [^c573040](repealed 5.11.1993)

1885 c.18

**No longer in force (ended 1993-11-05).**

## 1. Short title and construction.

This Act may be cited as the Metropolitan Streets Act, 1885, and shall be construed as one with the [^c573044]Metropolitan Streets Act, 1867, and may be cited together with that Act as the Metropolitan Streets Acts, 1867 and 1885.

## 2.[^c573045]

In section four of the Metropolitan Streets Act, 1867, “six miles” shall be substituted for “four miles,” and the said Act shall take effect and be construed accordingly.
