# Land Drainage Act 1991, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/59
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 70. Confidentiality of information obtained by Agency and Natural Resources Body for Wales etc.[^c1415598][^c-c0ca5f14e299642b390424a0f6e9a8e5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-485637207d16feaf42d06cb509c59df0] Section 204 of the [^c1415599]Water Resources Act 1991 (confidentiality of information) shall have effect in relation to information obtained by virtue of the provisions of this Act so far as they relate to functions exercisable by or in relation to the Agency or the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^c-dff286015fb38c0dc9ce7736fea978fa] as it has effect in relation to the information obtained as mentioned in that section.

(2)[^c-da5b7f0336be3c319c203450edae9b39] Subsection (1) does not apply to information obtained by virtue of section 37A (disclosure of Revenue and Customs information).
