## 154A. Financial assistance to reduce charges[^c-d380506910a9d4192d31a8fdcd8a8dd0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the Secretary of State considers it desirable to do so, the Secretary of State may give financial assistance for the purpose in subsection (2) to—

- (a) an English undertaker, [^c-29c3f3ae490983fdca4ca7dd8e152d7f]...
- (b) a water supply licensee[^c-c878b5fbabba9fca35755b047eb45053] that supplies water to premises in accordance with the licensee's[^c-69587cf57f8d155c08b5c6dca25cda8b] retail authorisation using the supply system of an English undertaker, or[^c-291b0a6addcd70268f8df06cf77474e6]
- (c) a sewerage licensee that serves premises in accordance with the licensee's retail authorisation using the sewerage system of an English undertaker.

(2) The purpose is that of securing the reduction of charges payable by customers in an English undertaker's area for the supply of water or the provision of sewerage services.

(3) The power in subsection (1) may be exercised in relation to all customers in an English undertaker's area or customers of a particular description.

(4) Financial assistance under subsection (1) may be given in any form and in particular may be given by way of—

- (a) grant,
- (b) loan, or
- (c) guarantee.

(5) Financial assistance under subsection (1) may be given on such terms and conditions as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

(6) Financial assistance under subsection (1) may be given in any manner and in particular may be given—

- (a) to an English undertaker by means of an arrangement made by the Secretary of State with another English undertaker, [^c-161cd4ab067833ef846ea547913c5f0a]...
- (b) to a water supply licensee[^c-2e110fde3218fa6124fe73754e721860] by means of an arrangement made by the Secretary of State with an English undertaker that is a water undertaker, or[^c-15a11467fe0bb992638f6c558c14cc44]
- (c) to a sewerage licensee by means of an arrangement made by the Secretary of State with an English undertaker that is a sewerage undertaker.

(7) A reference in this section to a customer in an English undertaker's area is a reference to—

- (a) a person liable to pay charges to the undertaker in respect of the supply of water or the provision of sewerage services, other than a water supply licensee or a sewerage licensee[^c-ad802ac0380e8e55aa1472b0d8c82fa0], [^c-a57548dffeaa42af832be3eea2ba9d10]...
- (b) a person whose premises are supplied with water by a water supply licensee[^c-db44e36e103d0fec93ff679d65e72843] in accordance with the licensee's[^c-b1ccc84a963b690eb4a891325f12a0c3] retail authorisation using the undertaker's supply system, or[^c-20e2dc6540d0686b8f1c6f693bd65354]
- (c) a person whose premises are served by a sewerage licensee in accordance with the licensee's retail authorisation using the undertaker's sewerage system.

(8) In this section a reference to the retail authorisation of a water supply licensee or of a sewerage licensee[^c-ab8d4b98630aab0a8dfb5cb736eda8b0] is to be construed in accordance with Schedule 2A or Schedule 2B, as the case may be[^c-d4dca5641dc4922a43441ab2230f9189].

(9) In this section “**English undertaker**” means a water undertaker or sewerage undertaker whose area is wholly or mainly in England.
