## 48. Interest on sums deposited in pursuance of the deposit condition.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-2e975a7c0668d2c2591e1cb797e8382c] Where for the purposes of subsection (2)(a) of section 47 above any sums have been deposited with a water undertaker by way of security for the discharge of any obligation, the undertaker shall pay interest at such rate as may be determined either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2e975a7c0668d2c2591e1cb797e8382c]

- (a) by the undertaker with the approval of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2e975a7c0668d2c2591e1cb797e8382c] the Authority[^M_F_941a0666-14b3-4384-e969-9d95b05ca6d7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2e975a7c0668d2c2591e1cb797e8382c]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2e975a7c0668d2c2591e1cb797e8382c]
- (b) in default of a determination under paragraph (a) above, by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2e975a7c0668d2c2591e1cb797e8382c] the Authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2e975a7c0668d2c2591e1cb797e8382c],

on every sum of 50p so deposited for every three months during which it remains in the hands of the undertaker. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2e975a7c0668d2c2591e1cb797e8382c]

(2) An approval or determination by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2e975a7c0668d2c2591e1cb797e8382c] the Authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2e975a7c0668d2c2591e1cb797e8382c] for the purposes of this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2e975a7c0668d2c2591e1cb797e8382c]

- (a) may be given or made in relation to a particular case or description of cases or generally; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2e975a7c0668d2c2591e1cb797e8382c]
- (b) may be revoked at any time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2e975a7c0668d2c2591e1cb797e8382c]
