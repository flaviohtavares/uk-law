# Water Industry Act 1991, section 89

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (197).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 89. Consultation ...[^c19906251][^c-7be2f793cbc549f70befa3416a0f1b6f][^c-65d4c2b9d3d54d1a8c49dfa837b5e4e4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Before taking any step mentioned in subsection (2) below, a relevant authority[^c-ccae52bf45a14923501fe9e2bbfff14f] shall—

- (a) consult and ascertain opinion in accordance with regulations made by that authority[^c-d4df3875483f69a45d0137a6429ecfdd]; and
- (b) comply with the requirements set out in regulations made by that authority[^c-98cf9bfaa9b442c0b4a697dc37fcd089].

(2) The steps are—

- (a) requesting a water undertaker to enter into arrangements under section 87(1) above;
- (b) requesting a water undertaker to vary any such arrangements in, or except in, prescribed circumstances or cases;
- (c) giving notice to a water undertaker under section 87C(7) above to terminate any such arrangements;
- (d) maintaining any such arrangements in prescribed circumstances.

(3) Regulations—

- (a) under paragraph (a) of subsection (1) above shall include provision about the process which the relevant authority is[^c-8cd6b75bea615090d05a64d4690940c5] to follow for the purposes of that paragraph;
- (b) under paragraph (b) of that subsection shall include provision about the requirements which must be satisfied (with respect to the outcome of that process or otherwise) before a step mentioned in subsection (2) above may be taken.

(4) Subsection (1) above shall not apply in relation to a proposal by a relevant authority[^c-8f352697bf9ee7b379c85a252689f5cf] to take the step mentioned in subsection (2)(c) above if that authority so directs[^c-86ef08efebb0a4a0e56ebc1a04685224] by an instrument in writing (and such a direction may apply either generally or in relation to a particular proposal).

(5) In this section “appropriate authority”— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6f23555c79a8d152abf5706a8ddd6a87]

- (a) in a case where two or more relevant authorities (one of which is the Assembly) propose to request a particular water undertaker to take a step mentioned in subsection (2)(a), (b) or (c) in respect of arrangements in adjoining areas, means the Secretary of State and the Assembly acting jointly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6f23555c79a8d152abf5706a8ddd6a87]
- (b) in relation to England (except in a case to which paragraph (a) applies), means the Secretary of State; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6f23555c79a8d152abf5706a8ddd6a87]
- (c) in relation to Wales (except in a case to which paragraph (a) applies), means the Assembly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6f23555c79a8d152abf5706a8ddd6a87]
