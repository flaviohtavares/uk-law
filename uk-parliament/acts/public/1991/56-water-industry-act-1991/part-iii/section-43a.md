# Water Industry Act 1991, section 43A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (197).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 43A. Calculation of “discounted aggregate deficit” for the purposes of section 42[^c19799311]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5] For the purposes of section 42 above the discounted aggregate deficit on a water main is the amount equal to the sum of the estimated relevant deficits for each of the twelve years following the provision of the main, in each case discounted in accordance with subsection (6) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5]

(2) The estimated relevant deficit for any year is the amount (if any) by which the estimated revenue in respect of the water main for that year would be exceeded by the annual borrowing costs of a loan of the amount required for the provision of that main. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5]

(3) Subsections (2) to (6), (8) and (9) of section 43 above (which relate to the annual borrowing costs of a loan of the amount required for the provision of a water main) shall apply for the purposes of this section as they apply for the purposes of that. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5]

(4) Any reference in this section to the estimated revenue in respect of a water main for any year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5]

- (a) in relation to premises expected to be connected with the main and supplied with water by a water undertaker, is a reference to so much of the aggregate of any charges expected to be payable to the undertaker for the provision of services in the course of that year as would represent charges— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5]
  - (i) imposed by the undertaker in relation to those premises, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5] reasonably attributable to the provision of a supply of water (whether or not for domestic purposes) to those premises by means of that main; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5]
- (b) in relation to premises expected to be connected with the main and supplied with water by a licensed water supplier, is a reference to so much of the aggregate of any charges expected to be made during the course of that year as would be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5]
  - (i) payable by the supplier to the undertaker in respect of the duty under section 66A(2)(b), 66B(3)(b) or 66C(2)(b)(ii) below; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5] reasonably attributable to the use of that main for the purpose of the supplier’s supplying water to those premises. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5]

(5) For the purpose of calculating estimated revenue under subsection (4) above, a thing is expected to be the case if, at the time the calculation is made, it is reasonably likely to occur. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5]

(6) The estimated relevant deficit for a year mentioned in subsection (1) above shall be discounted in order to determine its net present value by applying such factor, and in accordance with such other provision, as may be determined by the Authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5]

(7) A determination made by the Authority for the purposes of subsection (6) above— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5]

- (a) may be made in relation to the provision of a particular water main or in relation to the provision of water mains generally; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5]
- (b) may be revoked at any time except in relation to a water main in respect of which the conditions referred to in section 42(1) above have already been satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ed32603ce212780fab23a002370149d5]
