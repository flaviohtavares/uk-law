# Water Industry Act 1991, section 92

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (197).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Power to give effect to international obligations etc.[^c-24ff70d6ea1a2045493d0ee5dacff635]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to subsection (2) below, the Secretary of State may by regulations provide that the provisions of Chapters I to III of this Part shall have effect with such modifications as may be prescribed for the purpose of [^c-6032503010db7b890e6776bb3946aebc]...—

- (a)[^c-eaeb91538b80c0213a06d019f59b4a30] giving effect to any assimilated[^c-5d6fd9667d24dfe191e9159da1bc5ef8] obligations, or
- (b) enabling Her Majesty's Government in the United Kingdom to give effect[^c-617ed4514ae18e09bff99774e5e846f0] to any international agreement to which the United Kingdom is for the time being a party.

(2) This section shall not authorise any modification of any of sections 71, 72 and 76 above or of any other provisions of this Part so far as they have effect for the purposes of or in relation to those sections.
