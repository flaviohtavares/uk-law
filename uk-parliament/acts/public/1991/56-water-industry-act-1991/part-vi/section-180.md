## 180. Compensation for damage caused by works etc.[^c-4406f312788ef3df00c4f4335b48d2ef]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 12 to this Act shall have effect for making provision for imposing obligations for the purpose of minimising the damage caused in the exercise of certain powers conferred on undertakers and for imposing obligations as to the payment of compensation.
