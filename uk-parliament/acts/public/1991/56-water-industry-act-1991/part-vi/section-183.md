## 183. Protection for particular undertakings.[^c-27e9673934d61f304d5ee5fa4bff1f0c]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 13 to this Act shall have effect for the protection of particular undertakings in connection with the carrying out of works and other activities by relevant undertakers.
