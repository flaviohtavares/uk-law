# Water Industry Act 1991, section 160

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 160. Power to carry out works for sewerage purposes.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A sewerage undertaker may, by agreement with the owner or occupier of any premises, carry out at that person’s expense—

- (a) any work in connection with the construction, laying, alteration or repair of a sewer or drain which that person is entitled to carry out; or
- (b) any work which the undertaker has required that person to carry out under Part IV of this Act;

and for that purpose the undertaker shall have all such rights as that person would have.

(2) Sections 291, 293 and 294 of the [^c13221351]Public Health Act 1936 (which provide for the means of, and for limitations on, the recovery of expenses incurred by a local authority) shall apply in relation to the recovery by a sewerage undertaker of any sums under this section as they apply in relation to the recovery of expenses under that Act by a local authority.
