# Water Industry Act 1991, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (197).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/56

## 32. Duty to refer merger of water or sewerage undertakings.[^c19751191]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject to sections 33 and 33A below,[^c-7e19b21e6625cab1299d9fbfa65ce31a] it shall be the duty of the CMA[^c-b2634efcdc3d9404bc5cb1ad98b5e6a5] to make a reference to its chair for the constitution of a group under Schedule 4 to the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 if the CMA believes that it is or may be the case—

- (a) that arrangements are in progress which, if carried into effect, will result in a merger of any two or more water enterprises; or
- (b) that such a merger has taken place otherwise than as a result of the carrying into effect of arrangements that have been the subject of a reference by virtue of paragraph (a) above.
