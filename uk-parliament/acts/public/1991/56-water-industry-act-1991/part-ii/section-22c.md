# Water Industry Act 1991, section 22C

## 22C. Time limits on the imposition of financial penalties[^c-2a2b625851f8ca45a4cbb49126f0cbf9][^c19768381]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where no final or provisional order has been made in relation to a contravention or failure, an enforcement authority may not impose a penalty on a person[^c-39530a6b439490f48b1e496c8d90dac3] in respect of the contravention or failure later than the end of the period of five years[^c-c9aece3bd5dffd75b8c37e53fd9946e6] from the time of the contravention or failure, unless before the end of that period—

- (a) the notice under section 22A(4) above relating to the penalty is served on the person[^c-ca5ee5da54b3ebfbea5011124cf39595] under section 22A(8) above; or
- (b) a notice relating to the contravention or failure is served on the person[^c-49414234586660b58c65e58036d42b42] under section 203(2) below.

(2) Where a final or provisional order has been made in relation to a contravention or failure, an enforcement authority may not impose a penalty on a person[^c-3373aafa14d9914a2370da98e4ed5219] in respect of the contravention or failure unless the notice relating to the penalty under section 22A(4) above was served on the person[^c-8871f540ae114cbbfc524aac2cb8e287] under section 22A(8) above—

- (a) within three months from the confirmation of the provisional order or the making of the final order; or
- (b) where the provisional order is not confirmed, within six months from the making of the provisional order.
