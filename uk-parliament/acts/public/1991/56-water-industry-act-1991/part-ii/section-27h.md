# Water Industry Act 1991, section 27H

## 27H. Provision of information to the Council[^c19910201][^c19910051]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Council may direct—

- (a) the Authority;
- (b) a company holding an appointment under Chapter 1 of this Part; [^c-2d5d5439662c57a535075e0157379bba]...
- (c) a water supply licensee, or[^c-59518b5dee9a6a9e057f7c0499a4e588],
- (d)[^c-f847b99fc95bcb72075f4aa98cb30b65] a sewerage licensee,

to supply to it, in such form as it may reasonably specify, such information specified or described in the direction as it may require for the purpose of exercising its functions.

(2) A body or person[^c-581656bfb20553a8c815fae4bcc9097b] to whom a direction under this section is given shall, if the information specified or described in the direction is in its possession, comply with the direction as soon as reasonably practicable.

(3) Before giving a direction under this section and in specifying the form in which any information is to be supplied, the Council shall have regard to the desirability of minimising the costs, or any other detriment, to the body or person[^c-d4db7bbd48217a4d50cbfbc963d9654e] to whom the direction is given.

(4) If a body or person[^c-933956dbe480d6d53c9cbd93dffd1d35] to whom a direction under this section is given fails to comply with the direction the body or person[^c-77495e04213c8b6dde2cd35f27923af0] shall, if so required by the Council, give notice to the Council of the reasons for the[^c-29633aa77ba94f6fa3b267346a42231d] failure.
