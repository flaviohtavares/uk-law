# Water Industry Act 1991, section 93C

## 93C. Publicity of requirements imposed under section 93B.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where, under section 93B(1) above, the Authority[^M_F_941a0666-14b3-4384-e969-9d95b05ca6d7] imposes any requirement on a water undertaker or[^c19808731] water supply licensee[^c-dbc315b6fe7dc96d59eee401ef9466e9], the Authority may arrange for that requirement to be publicised in any such manner as he may consider appropriate for the purpose of bringing it to the attention of that undertaker’s or licensee's[^c-33e5f2fdba256e22d3d3937a9a3d5148] customers.

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1) above, the Authority may arrange for such publicising of the requirement as is mentioned in that subsection by—

- (a) itself publicising the requirement or causing it to be publicised; or
- (b) directing the undertaker or licensee[^c-f260ada614ec0233e010ad99827ea3f7] to inform or arrange to inform its customers of the requirement.
