# Water Industry Act 1991, section 93A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (197).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 93A. Duty to promote the efficient use of water.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) It shall be the duty of every water undertaker or[^c19808441] water supply licensee[^c-ddb7ec790167af2ac22cc00449734d59] to promote the efficient use of water by that person's customers[^c-e029c6553408870ce41abda14e68c25e].

(2) The duty of a water undertaker or water supply licensee[^c-1fc5ae9c371f55ed022bbfb7e37e6162] under this section shall be enforceable under section 18 above—

- (a) by the Secretary of State; or
- (b) with the consent of or in accordance with a general authorisation given by the Secretary of State, by the Authority[^M_F_941a0666-14b3-4384-e969-9d95b05ca6d7].

(3) Nothing in this Part shall have effect to authorise or require a water undertaker or water supply licensee[^c-65687e61c9c7d2506fbd82a665fb3b78] to impose any requirement on any of that person's customers[^c-63aef62bdac1c7db456399d2f73183e1] or potential customers.
