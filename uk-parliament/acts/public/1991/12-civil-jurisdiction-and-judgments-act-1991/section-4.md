# Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1991, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 1992-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Application to the Crown.[^c10320711]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The amendments of the 1982 Act made by this Act bind the Crown in accordance with the provisions of section 51 of that Act.
