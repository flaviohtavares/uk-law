Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1991

1991 c. 12

An Act to give effect to the Convention on jurisdiction and the enforcement of judgments in civil and commercial matters, including the Protocols annexed thereto, opened for signature at Lugano on 16th September 1988; and for purposes connected therewith.

\[9th May 1991\]
