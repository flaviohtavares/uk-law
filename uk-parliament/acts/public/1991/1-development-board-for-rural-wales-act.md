# Development Board for Rural Wales Act

> This law has been repealed or revoked. Ended on 1998-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Development Board for Rural Wales Act [^c931009](Repealed 1.10.1998)

1991 c. 1

**No longer in force (ended 1998-10-01).**

## 1. Increase of financial limit.

In section 12 of the [^c931011]Development of Rural Wales Act 1976 (financial limit for the Development Board for Rural Wales), for subsection (2) there shall be substituted—

> (2) The said limit is £175 million.

;

and section 2(3) of the [^c931012]Industry Act 1981 (which increased the previous limit to £100 million) is hereby repealed.

## 2. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Development Board for Rural Wales Act 1991.
