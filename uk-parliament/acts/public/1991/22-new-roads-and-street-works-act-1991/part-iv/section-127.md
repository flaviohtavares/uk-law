# New Roads and Street Works Act 1991, section 127

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (403).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/22

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 127. Facilities to be afforded to road works authority.

*Extent: S*

(1) An undertaker executing road works shall afford the road works authority reasonable facilities for ascertaining whether he is complying with his duties under this Part.

(2) An undertaker who fails to afford the road works authority such facilities commits an offence in respect of each failure and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4[^c-e53a69ff163e4f81a7c25a3f165144b2] on the standard scale.
