> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (380).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/22

## 170. Commencement.[^c14151101][^c14151111]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The provisions of this Act come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may appoint by order made by statutory instrument; and different days may be appointed for different provisions and different purposes.

(2) An order bringing into force any provision may contain such transitional provisions and savings as appear to the Secretary of State to be necessary or expedient.
