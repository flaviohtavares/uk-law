# New Roads and Street Works Act 1991, section 168

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (380).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 168. Minor and consequential amendments and repeals.[^c14151051]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The enactments specified in Schedule 8 have effect with the amendments specified there which are minor amendments and amendments consequential on the provisions of this Act.

In that Schedule—

- Part I contains amendments of the [^c14151061]Highways Act 1980,
- Part II contains amendments of the [^c14151071]Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984,
- Part III contains amendments of the [^c14151081]Roads (Scotland) Act 1984, and
- Part IV contains amendments of other enactments.

(2) The enactments mentioned in Schedule 9 are repealed to the extent specified there.
