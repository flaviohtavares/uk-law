# Natural Heritage (Scotland) Act 1991, section 13

## 13. Access to open country.[^c1044330]

*Extent: S*

Part II of the [^c1044331]Countryside (Scotland) Act 1967 shall be amended in accordance with Schedule 3 to this Act to enable SNH to enter into access agreements or to make access orders under that Part of that Act.
