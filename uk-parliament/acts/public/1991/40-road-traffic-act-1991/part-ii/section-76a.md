## 76A. Variation of special parking areas by Mayor of London.[^c10860381]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] At any time when an order under section 76 above designating the whole or any part of a London authority’s area as a special parking area is in force, the Mayor of London may by order under this subsection amend the order so as to vary the area which for the time being constitutes the special parking area. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] No order may be made under subsection (1) above without the consent of every London local authority which is the traffic authority for a road which the order has the effect of bringing within, or removing from, the special parking area concerned. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] An order under subsection (1) above must not be such as to bring within a special parking area— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] any area specified in an order under this paragraph made by the Secretary of State; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] the whole or any part of a Royal Park, except with the consent of the Secretary of State. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] No area may be specified in an order under subsection (3)(a) above unless the Secretary of State is satisfied that it is expedient, on grounds of national security, that no part of that area should be included in a special parking area. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] In this section “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]**Royal Park *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]**” means any park to which the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] [^c10860391]Parks Regulation Act 1872 applies (see sections 1 and 3 of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] [^c10860401]Parks Regulation (Amendment) Act 1926). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]
