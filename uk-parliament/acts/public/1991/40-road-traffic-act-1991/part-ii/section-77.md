# Road Traffic Act 1991, section 77

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-11-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Application of provisions in relation to special parking areas.[^c10860411][^c10860421]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] This section applies in relation to any vehicle which is stationary in a special parking area (but which is not in a designated parking place) in circumstances in which an offence would have been committed with respect to the vehicle but for section 76(3) above. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] A penalty charge shall be payable with respect to the vehicle by the owner of the vehicle. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] Section 66 of, and Schedule 6 to, this Act shall apply in relation to penalty charges payable by virtue of subsection (2) above, but subject to such modifications (if any) as the Secretary of State considers it appropriate to make in the order designating the special parking area in question. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] Where a parking attendant has reason to believe that a penalty charge is payable with respect to the vehicle by virtue of subsection (2) above, he or another person acting under his direction may fix an immobilisation device to the vehicle. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] Subsections (2) to (8) of section 69 of this Act shall apply in relation to a device fixed to a vehicle under subsection (4) above, but subject to such modifications (if any) as the Secretary of State considers it appropriate to make in the order designating the special parking area in question. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] An order under section 76 designating a special parking area may make such modifications of any provision of, or amended by, this Part of this Act as the Secretary of State considers appropriate in consequence of the provisions of section 76 or this section or of the order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]
