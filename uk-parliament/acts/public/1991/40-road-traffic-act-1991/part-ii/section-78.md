(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] In this section— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]

- “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]**certificated bailiff *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]**”, means any person authorised to act as such under subsection (6) below; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]
- “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]**a Part II debt *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]**” means any sum which is— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]
  - (a) payable under, or by virtue of, any provision of this Part of this Act; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]
  - (b) recoverable as if it were payable under a county court order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] The Lord Chancellor may by order make provision— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] for warrants of execution in respect of Part II debts, or such class or classes of Part II debts as may be specified in the order, to be executed by certificated bailiffs; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] as to the requirements which must be satisfied before any person takes, with a view to enforcing the payment of— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]
  - (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] a Part II debt; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] such class or classes of Part II debts as may be so specified, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]

  any other step of a kind specified by the order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]
