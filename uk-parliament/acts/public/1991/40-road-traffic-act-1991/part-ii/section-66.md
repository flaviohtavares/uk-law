(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] Where, in the case of a stationary vehicle in a designated parking place, a parking attendant has reason to believe that a penalty charge is payable with respect to the vehicle, he may— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] fix a penalty charge notice to the vehicle; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] give such a notice to the person appearing to him to be in charge of the vehicle. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c10859611][^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] For the purposes of this Part of this Act, a penalty charge is payable with respect to a vehicle, by the owner of the vehicle, if— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] the vehicle has been left— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]
  - (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] otherwise than as authorised by or under any order relating to the designated parking place; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] beyond the period of parking which has been paid for; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] no parking charge payable with respect to the vehicle has been paid; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] there has, with respect to the vehicle, been a contravention of, or failure to comply with, any provision made by or under any order relating to the designated parking place. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] A penalty charge notice must state— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] the grounds on which the parking attendant believes that a penalty charge is payable with respect to the vehicle; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] the amount of the penalty charge which is payable; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] that the penalty charge must be paid before the end of the period of 28 days beginning with the date of the notice; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] that if the penalty charge is paid before the end of the period of 14 days beginning with the date of the notice, the amount of the penalty charge will be reduced by the specified proportion; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]
- (e (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] that, if the penalty charge is not paid before the end of the 28 day period, a notice to owner may be served by the London authority on the person appearing to them to be the owner of the vehicle; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]
- (f (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] the address to which payment of the penalty charge must be sent. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] In subsection (3)(d) above “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]**specified proportion *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]**” means such proportion, applicable to all cases, as may be determined by the London authorities acting through the Joint Committee. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] A penalty charge notice fixed to a vehicle in accordance with this section shall not be removed or interfered with except by or under the authority of— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] the owner, or person in charge, of the vehicle; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] the London authority for the place in which the vehicle in question was found. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] A person contravening subsection (5) above shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 2 on the standard scale. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]
