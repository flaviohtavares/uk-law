## 76. Special parking areas.[^c10860311]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] Where a London authority apply to the Secretary of State for an order to be made under this section, the Secretary of State may make an order designating the whole, or any part, of that authority’s area as a special parking area. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] by Transport for London, to the extent that the special parking area is to consist of *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] GLA *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] roads or trunk roads; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] by a London local authority, to the extent that the special parking area is to consist of roads other than *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] GLA *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] roads and trunk roads. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6] Before making an order under this section, the Secretary of State shall consult the relevant Commissioner or, if appropriate, both Commissioners. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced8bacc24a90f918617984574046c6]
