## 65. Contravention of certain orders relating to parking places in London not to be criminal offence.[^c10859581]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] In section 47 of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 (offences relating to designated parking places) the words *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] “ ;but this subsection does not apply in relation to any designated parking place in Greater London ” *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] shall be added at the end of subsection (1). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] In section 8 of that Act (contravention of orders under section 6 to be an offence), the following subsection shall be inserted after subsection (1)— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]

> (1A (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] Subsection (1) above does not apply in relation to any order under section 6 of this Act so far as it designates any parking places. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] The provisions of section 11 of that Act (contravention of experimental traffic order) shall become subsection (1) of that section and the following subsection shall be inserted as subsection (2)— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]

> (2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794] This section does not apply in relation to any experimental traffic order so far as it designates any parking places in Greater London. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6b922975b3461bec9c8d51cabd44d794]
