(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] Section 69(1) of this Act shall not apply in relation to a vehicle if— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] a current disabled person’s badge is displayed on the vehicle; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

*Extent: E+W*

- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-58d724025cca87ef405f7c95e59fa659][^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] a current recognised badge (within the meaning given by section 21A of the Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons Act 1970) is displayed on the vehicle; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] not more than 15 minutes have elapsed since the end of any period for which the appropriate charge was duly paid at the time of parking; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] not more than 15 minutes have elapsed since the end of any unexpired time (in respect of another vehicle) which is available at the relevant parking meter at the time of parking. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] In any case in which section 69(1) of this Act would apply to a vehicle but for subsection (1)(a) above and the vehicle was not, at the time at which it was parked, being used— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] in accordance with regulations under section 21 of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] [^c10859711]Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons Act 1970; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] in circumstances falling within section 117(1)(b) of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] [^c10859721]Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 (use where a disabled person’s concession would be available), *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

the person in charge of the vehicle at that time shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

*Extent: E+W*

(2A)[^c-0fdbe2cafa62552a0c4fc7db505d7fda] In any case in which section 69(1) of this Act would apply to a vehicle but for subsection (1)(aa) above and the vehicle was not, at the time at which it was parked, being used— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

- (a) in accordance with regulations under section 21A of the Chronically Sick and Disabled Persons Act 1970, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

the person in charge of the vehicle at that time shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]
