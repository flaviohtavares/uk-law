## 74A. Additional parking charges: reserve powers of Secretary of State.[^c-0cfd96a016002a9dcfca09f4685b11b4][^c10860181][^c10860171]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] Where the Mayor of London— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] on a submission under subsection (4) of section 74 above, approves any levels of additional parking charges, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] sets any such levels under subsection (5) of that section, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

he shall notify the Secretary of State of the levels of charges so approved or set. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] Where notification of any levels of charges is required to be given under subsection (1) above, the levels of charges shall not come into force until after the expiration of— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] the period of one month beginning with the day on which the notification is given, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] such shorter period as the Secretary of State may allow. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] If, before the expiration of that period, the Secretary of State gives notice to the Mayor of London that he objects to the levels of charges on the grounds that some or all of them are or may be excessive, those levels of charges shall not come into force unless and until the objection has been withdrawn. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] If, at any time before the levels of charges required to be notified under subsection (1) above to the Secretary of State have come into force, the Secretary of State considers that some or all of them are excessive, he may make regulations setting the levels of charges. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] Levels of charges set under subsection (4) above must be no higher than those notified under subsection (1) above. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] by Transport for London, so far as relating to *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] GLA *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] roads or trunk roads, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] by the London local authorities, so far as relating to other roads. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] Regulations under subsection (4) above are without prejudice to the duties imposed on Transport for London and the London local authorities by section 74(1) above; but where the Secretary of State makes any such regulations— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] Transport for London, if the regulations relate to *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] GLA *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] roads or trunk roads, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60] the London local authorities, if the regulations relate to other roads, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88b2ddeb8784e04bb4f429bc9a392e60]
