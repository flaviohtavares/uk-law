# Road Traffic Act 1991, section 83

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-11-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. Repeals.[^c10861561]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The enactments mentioned in Schedule 8 to this Act (which include enactments which are spent) are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
