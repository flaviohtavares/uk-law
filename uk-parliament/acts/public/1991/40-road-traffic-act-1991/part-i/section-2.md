# Road Traffic Act 1991, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-11-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2. Careless, and inconsiderate, driving.[^c10858101]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For section 3 of the [^c10858111]Road Traffic Act 1988 there shall be substituted—

> **3. Careless, and inconsiderate, driving.**
>
> If a person drives a mechanically propelled vehicle on a road or other public place without due care and attention, or without reasonable consideration for other persons using the road or place, he is guilty of an offence.
