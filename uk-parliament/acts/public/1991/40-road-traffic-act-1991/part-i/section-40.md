# Road Traffic Act 1991, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-11-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Power to install equipment for detection of traffic offences.[^c10858901]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In Part V of the [^c10858911]Highways Act 1980 immediately before section 96 there shall be inserted—

> **95A. Power to install equipment for detection of traffic offences.**
>
> A highway authority may install and maintain on or near a highway structures and equipment for the detection of traffic offences.

(2) In Part IV of the [^c10858921]Roads (Scotland) Act 1984 after section 49 there shall be inserted—

> **Equipment for detection of traffic offences**
>
> **49A. Power to install equipment for detection of traffic offences.**
>
> A roads authority may install and maintain on or near a road structures and equipment for the detection of traffic offences.
