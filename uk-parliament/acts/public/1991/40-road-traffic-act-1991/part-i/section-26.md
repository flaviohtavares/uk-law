## 26. Amendment of Schedule 2 to the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988.[^c10858551]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 2 to this Act, which amends Schedule 2 to the [^c10858561]Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988 (prosecution and punishment of offences), shall have effect.
