# Road Traffic Act 1991, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-11-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Driving under influence of drink or drugs.[^c10858131]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 4 of the Road Traffic Act 1988, in subsections (1), (2) and (3) for the words “motor vehicle” there shall be substituted the words “ mechanically propelled vehicle ”.
