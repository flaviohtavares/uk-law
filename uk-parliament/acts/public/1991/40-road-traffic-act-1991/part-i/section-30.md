# Road Traffic Act 1991, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-11-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/40

## 30. Courses for drink-drive offenders.[^c10858621]

*Extent: E+W+S*

After section 34 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5] [^c10858631]Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988 there shall be inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]

> **34A. Reduced disqualification period for attendance on courses. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]**
>
> (1) This section applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> - (a) a person is convicted of an offence under section 3A (causing death by careless driving when under influence of drink or drugs), 4 (driving or being in charge when under influence of drink or drugs), 5 (driving or being in charge with excess alcohol) or 7 (failing to provide a specimen) of the Road Traffic Act 1988, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
> - (b) the court makes an order under section 34 of this Act disqualifying him for a period of not less than twelve months. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> (2) Where this section applies, the court may make an order that the period of disqualification imposed under section 34 shall be reduced if, by a date specified in the order under this section, the offender satisfactorily completes a course approved by the Secretary of State for the purposes of this section and specified in the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> (3) The reduction made by an order under this section in a period of disqualification imposed under section 34 shall be a period specified in the order of not less than three months and not more than one quarter of the unreduced period (and accordingly where the period imposed under section 34 is twelve months, the reduced period shall be nine months). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> (4) The court shall not make an order under this section unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> - (a) it is satisfied that a place on the course specified in the order will be available for the offender, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
> - (b) the offender appears to the court to be of or over the age of 17, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
> - (c) the court has explained the effect of the order to the offender in ordinary language, and has informed him of the amount of the fees for the course and of the requirement that he must pay them before beginning the course, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
> - (d) the offender has agreed that the order should be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> (5) The date specified in an order under this section as the latest date for completion of a course must be at least two months before the last day of the period of disqualification as reduced by the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> (6) An order under this section shall name the petty sessions area (or in Scotland the sheriff court district or, where an order has been made under this section by a stipendiary magistrate, the commission area) in which the offender resides or will reside. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> **34B. Certificates of completion of courses. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]**
>
> (1) An offender shall be regarded for the purposes of section 34A of this Act as having completed a course satisfactorily if (and only if) a certificate that he has done so is received by the clerk of the supervising court before the end of the period of disqualification imposed under section 34. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> (2) If the certificate referred to in subsection (1) above is received by the clerk of the supervising court before the end of the period of disqualification imposed under section 34 but after the end of the period as it would have been reduced by the order, the order shall have effect as if the reduced period ended with the day on which the certificate is received by the clerk. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> (3) The certificate referred to in subsection (1) above shall be a certificate in such form, containing such particulars, and given by such person, as may be prescribed by, or determined in accordance with, regulations made by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> (4) A course organiser shall give the certificate mentioned in subsection (1) above to the offender not later than fourteen days after the date specified in the order as the latest date for completion of the course, unless the offender fails to make due payment of the fees for the course, fails to attend the course in accordance with the organiser’s reasonable instructions, or fails to comply with any other reasonable requirements of the organiser. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> (5) Where a course organiser decides not to give the certificate mentioned in subsection (1) above, he shall give written notice of his decision to the offender as soon as possible, and in any event not later than fourteen days after the date specified in the order as the latest date for completion of the course. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> (6) An offender to whom a notice is given under subsection (5) above may, within such period as may be prescribed by rules of court, apply to the supervising court for a declaration that the course organiser’s decision not to give a certificate was contrary to subsection (4) above; and if the court grants the application section 34A of this Act shall have effect as if the certificate had been duly received by the clerk of the court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> (7) If fourteen days after the date specified in the order as the latest date for completion of the course the course organiser has given neither the certificate mentioned in subsection (1) above nor a notice under subsection (5) above, the offender may, within such period as may be prescribed by rules of court, apply to the supervising court for a declaration that the course organiser is in default; and if the court grants the application section 34A of this Act shall have effect as if the certificate had been duly received by the clerk of the court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> (8) A notice under subsection (5) above shall specify the ground on which it is given, and the Secretary of State may by regulations make provision as to the form of notices under that subsection and as to the circumstances in which they are to be treated as given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> (9) Where the clerk of a court receives a certificate of the kind referred to in subsection (1) above, or a court grants an application under subsection (6) or (7) above, the clerk or court must send notice of that fact to the Secretary of State; and the notice must be sent in such manner and to such address, and must contain such particulars, as the Secretary of State may determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> **34C. Provisions supplementary to sections 34A and 34B. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]**
>
> (1) The Secretary of State may issue guidance to course organisers, or to any category of course organiser as to the conduct of courses approved for the purposes of section 34A of this Act; and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> - (a) course organisers shall have regard to any guidance given to them under this subsection, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
> - (b) in determining for the purposes of section 34B(6) whether any instructions or requirements of an organiser were reasonable, a court shall have regard to any guidance given to him under this subsection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> (2) In sections 34A and 34B and this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> - “**course organiser *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]**”, in relation to a course, means the person who, in accordance with regulations made by the Secretary of State, is responsible for giving the certificates mentioned in section 34B(1) in respect of the completion of the course; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
> - “**petty sessions area *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]**” has the same meaning as in the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
> - “**supervising court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]**”, in relation to an order under section 34A, means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>   - (a) in England and Wales, a magistrates’ court acting for the petty sessions area named in the order as the area where the offender resides or will reside; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>   - (b) in Scotland, the sheriff court for the district where the offender resides or will reside or, where the order is made by a stipendiary magistrate and the offender resides or will reside within his commission area, the district court for that area, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
>   and any reference to the clerk of a magistrates’ court is a reference to the clerk to the justices for the petty sessions area for which the court acts. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> (3) Any power to make regulations under section 34B or this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
>
> - (a) includes power to make different provision for different cases, and to make such incidental or supplemental provision as appears to the Secretary of State to be necessary or expedient; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
> - (b) shall be exercisable by statutory instrument, which shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cebb1cad78a67f9c0e9fbc5773c8c3b5]
