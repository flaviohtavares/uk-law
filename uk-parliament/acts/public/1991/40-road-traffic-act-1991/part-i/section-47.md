# Road Traffic Act 1991, section 47

## 47. Applications for licences to drive hackney carriages etc.[^c10859111]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c18546311] Part II of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18546311] [^c10859121]Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1976 (including that Part as it applies in any area at the commencement of this section) shall have effect with the insertion of the following subsection after subsection (1) of each of section 51 (licensing of drivers of private hire vehicles) and section 59 (qualifications for drivers of hackney carriages)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18546311]

> (1A) For the purpose of satisfying themselves as to whether an applicant is a fit and proper person to hold a driver’s licence, a council may send to the chief officer of police for the police area in which the council is situated— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18546311]
>
> - (a) a copy of that person’s application, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18546311]
> - (b) a request for the chief officer’s observations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18546311]
>
> and the chief officer shall respond to the request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18546311]

(2) Where any local Act contains a provision requiring a district council to be satisfied as to the fitness of an applicant to hold a licence to drive a private hire vehicle or a hackney carriage, the council may send to the chief officer of police for the police area in which the council is situated— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18546311]

- (a) a copy of that person’s application, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18546311]
- (b) a request for the chief officer’s observations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18546311]

and the chief officer shall respond to the request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18546311]

(3)[^c10859131] In subsection (2), the reference to a district council shall be read in relation to Wales as including a reference to a county council or county borough council. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18546311]
