# Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 1991, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (68).** Text as at 2021-02-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/55

## 76. Power of land improvement companies to advance money.

*Extent: S*

Any company incorporated by Parliament or formed and registered under the Companies Act 2006[^c-a3d36394f00e05b8b530d514679857dd] and having power to advance money for the improvement of land, or for the cultivation and farming of land, may make an advance of money upon a charging order duly made and recorded or registered under this Act, on such terms and conditions as may be agreed upon between the company and the person entitled to the order.
