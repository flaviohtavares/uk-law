## 61B. Clauses in leases as to resolution of disputes[^c-42d10704f7df3e05026b37495b7b5c7b]

*Extent: S*

Any term of—

- (a) a lease of an agricultural holding in relation to which this Act applies; or
- (b) any agreement in connection with such a lease (other than an agreement under section 61(1) of this Act),

that makes provision restricting any right of a landlord or tenant to apply to the Land Court under this Act or under section 116 of the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2016[^c-405e81f346cc615914fd5bebaedbb770] to have a matter determined by the Court shall, in so far as it makes that provision, be null and void.
