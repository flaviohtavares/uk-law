# Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 1991, section 61

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (68).** Text as at 2021-02-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/55

## 61. Agreement to refer matters to arbitration[^c-42d10704f7df3e05026b37495b7b5c7b]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to subsection (2) below, where this Act or section 116 of the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2016[^c-7de5aee5be5ad67ceb9f64357fa6933c] makes provision for any matter to be determined by the Land Court, the matter may, if the landlord and tenant so agree at or after the time when the matter arises, instead of being so determined, be determined by arbitration.

(2) Subsection (1) above does not apply in relation to any matter which may be determined by the Land Court—

- (a) in pursuance of section [^c-db0ff76b3e3aa067d9d254cd6a7bfb1a]... 11, 12, 22, 26, 32, [^c-eb3ba6e3646626d6f2293553959ac64e]... 41(1), 55(7) or 66(2) or (2A) of this Act; or
- (b) on appeal.

(3) In this Act, other than in section 61A, “**arbitration**” includes any other method of resolving the matter; and “**arbiter**” shall be construed accordingly.
