# Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 1991, section 80

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (68).** Text as at 2021-02-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/55

## 80. Determination of matters where Secretary of State is landlord or tenant.

*Extent: S*

(1) This section applies where the Secretary of State is the landlord or the tenant of an agricultural holding.

(2) Where this section applies, any provision of this Act (except section 8)[^key-d34954f19c8e8d6f4a5bf368911fcadf]—

- (a) under which any matter relating to the holding is referred to the decision of the Secretary of State;[^key-50bd197ff46be9cfff68e91e278e5fff]...
- (b)[^key-50bd197ff46be9cfff68e91e278e5fff] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

shall have effect with the substitution for every reference to “the Secretary of State” of a reference to “ the Land Court ”, and any provision referred to in paragraph (a) above which provides for an appeal to an arbiter from the decision of the Secretary of State shall not apply.

(3)[^key-cc9cb0ee585ba2e0b70324d49882b1c5] Where this section applies, section 8 of this Act shall have effect—

- (a) with the substitution for “Scottish Ministers” in subsection (3) of “sheriff”;
- (b) as if subsection (3A) were omitted.
