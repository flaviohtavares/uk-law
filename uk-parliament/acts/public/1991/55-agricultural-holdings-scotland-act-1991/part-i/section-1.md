# Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 1991, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (68).** Text as at 2021-02-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Meaning of “agricultural holding” and “agricultural land”.

*Extent: S*

(1) In this Act (except sections 68 to 72) “**agricultural holding**” means the aggregate of the agricultural land comprised in a lease, not being a lease under which the land is let to the tenant during his continuance in any office, appointment or employment held under the landlord.

(2) In this section and in section 2 of this Act, “**agricultural land**” means land used for agriculture for the purposes of a trade or business, and includes any other land which, by virtue of a designation of the Secretary of State under section 86(1) of the [^c1033691]Agriculture (Scotland) Act 1948, is agricultural land within the meaning of that Act.
