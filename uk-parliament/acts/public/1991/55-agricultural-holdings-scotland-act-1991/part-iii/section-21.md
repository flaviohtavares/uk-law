## 21. Notice to quit and notice of intention to quit.[^key-624e301e415ade638e1f79a25f2c24b3]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-5ce067872080ca3b87f3214ea5c6cc5a] Subject to section 20 and Part 3A[^key-aa0078ad43e457bbe394d681c1044319] of this Act and to subsections (6) and (7) below and to sections[^key-498e0e26b6db6316e444b3e0d44f436d] 2A[^key-c7f1e63b23b7ca8b80b3a97c08174098] and 73 of the Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 2003 (asp 11) a tenancy of an agricultural holding shall not come to an end except by operation of a notice which complies with this subsection notwithstanding any agreement or any provision in the lease to the contrary.

(2)[^key-5ce067872080ca3b87f3214ea5c6cc5a] In this Act, a notice which complies with subsection (1) above is referred to as a “**notice to quit**” if it is given by the landlord to the tenant and as a “**notice of intention to quit**” if it is given by the tenant to the landlord.

(3)[^key-5ce067872080ca3b87f3214ea5c6cc5a] A notice complies with subsection (1) above if—

- (a) it is in writing;
- (b) it is a notice of intention to bring the tenancy to an end;
- (c) where the notice is to take effect at the termination of the stipulated endurance of the lease, it is given not less than one year nor more than 2 years before that date;
- (d) in the case of a lease continued in force by tacit relocation, it gives not less than one year nor more than 2 years’ notice.

(4)[^key-ba83a61838db45b20b2cf283e122ce3c] The provisions of the [^c1033694]Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907 relating to removings shall, in the case of an agricultural holding, have effect subject to this section.

(5)[^key-77a8e0142ad05cb29751a3f2b3e4ee77] Notice to quit shall be given either—

- (a) in the same manner as notice of removal under section 6 of the [^c1033695]Removal Terms (Scotland) Act 1886; or
- (b) in the form and manner prescribed by the [^c1033696]Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1907,

and such notice shall come in place of the notice required by the said Act of 1907.

(6)[^key-ba83a61838db45b20b2cf283e122ce3c] Nothing in this section shall affect the right of the landlord of an agricultural holding to remove a tenant whose estate has been sequestrated under the Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 2016, the[^key-1e21ad6aaa6fadb7d57723f1e3083f71] [^c1033697]Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 1985 or the [^c1033698]Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 1913, or who by failure to pay rent or otherwise has incurred irritancy of his lease or other liability to be removed.

(7)[^key-ba83a61838db45b20b2cf283e122ce3c] This section shall not apply—

- (a) to a notice given in pursuance of a stipulation in a lease entitling the landlord to resume land for building, planting, feuing or other purposes (not being agricultural purposes); or
- (b) in relation to subjects let under a lease for any period less than a year, not being a lease which by virtue of section 2 of this Act takes effect as a lease from year to year.
