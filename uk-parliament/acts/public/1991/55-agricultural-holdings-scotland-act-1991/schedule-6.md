## SCHEDULE 6 — Market Garden Improvements

*Extent: S*

Section 40.

**1.** Planting of fruit trees or bushes permanently set out.

**2.** Planting of strawberry plants.

**3.** Planting of asparagus, rhubarb, and other vegetable crops which continue productive for 2 or more years.

**4.** Erection, alteration or enlargement of buildings for the purpose of the trade or business of a market gardener.
