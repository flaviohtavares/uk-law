Statute Law Revision (Isle of Man) Act 1991

1991 c. 61

An Act to revise the statute law by repealing obsolete, spent, unnecessary or superseded enactments so far as they continue to form part of the law of the Isle of Man.

\[25th July 1991\] [^c932590]

[^c932591]Be it enacted by the Queen’s most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—

## 1. Repeals.

(1) The enactments which were repealed (whether for the whole or any part of the United Kingdom) by the Acts specified in Schedule 1 to this Act are hereby repealed so far as they extend to the Isle of Man.

(2) The Acts specified in Schedule 2 to this Act are hereby repealed so far as they extend to the Isle of Man.

## 2. Short title and extent.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Statute Law Revision (Isle of Man) Act 1991.

(2) This Act extends only to the Isle of Man.
