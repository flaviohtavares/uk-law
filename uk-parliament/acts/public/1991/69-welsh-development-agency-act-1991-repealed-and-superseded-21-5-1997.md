# Welsh Development Agency Act 1991 (repealed and superseded 21.5.1997)

> Text as at 1997-05-21.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Welsh Development Agency Act 1991 [^c932947](repealed and superseded 21.5.1997)

1991 c. 69

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-21).**

## 1. Increase of financial limit.

In section 18(3) of the [^c932950]Welsh Development Agency Act 1975 (financial limit for Agency) for the words “£700 million” there shall be substituted the words “ £950 million ”.

## 2. Short title and repeals.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Welsh Development Agency Act 1991.

(2) Section2(2) of the [^c932951]Industry Act 1981 and the [^c932952]Welsh Development Agency Act 1988 (which relate to the financial limit of the Agency and are now superseded) are hereby repealed.
