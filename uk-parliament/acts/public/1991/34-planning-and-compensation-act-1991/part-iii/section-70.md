# Planning and Compensation Act 1991, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/34
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 70. Further amendments relating to land compensation.[^c15660011][^c15660021]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 15 to this Act, of which—

- (a) Part I contains miscellaneous amendments, and
- (b) Part II contains minor and consequential amendments,

relating to land compensation, shall have effect.
