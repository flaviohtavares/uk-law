# Planning and Compensation Act 1991, section 77

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/34
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 77. Compensation where permission for additional development granted after acquisition.[^c15660521]

*Extent: S*

(1) Schedule 16 to this Act (which revives Part V of the [^c15660531]Land Compensation (Scotland) Act 1963) shall have effect.

(2) This section applies to an acquisition or sale of an interest in land if the date of completion (within the meaning of that Part) falls on or after the day on which this section comes into force.
