# Planning and Compensation Act 1991, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/34
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Simplified planning zones.[^c15658491]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 5 to this Act which—

- (a) modifies the procedure for making simplified planning zones, and
- (b) makes minor and consequential amendments of Schedule 7 to the principal Act,

shall have effect.
