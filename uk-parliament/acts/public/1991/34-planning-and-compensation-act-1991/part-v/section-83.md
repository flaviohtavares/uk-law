# Planning and Compensation Act 1991, section 83

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/34
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. Consequential amendment of section 91A of Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988.[^c15660911]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 91A of the [^c15660921]Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 (waste disposal: restoration payments)—

- (a) in subsection (4)(b) for “any term of a relevant agreement” there is substituted “ any relevant obligation ”; and
- (b) for subsection (7) there is substituted—

  > (7) For the purposes of this section a relevant obligation is—
  >
  > - (a) an obligation arising under an agreement made under—
  >   - (i) section 106 of the [^c15660931]Town and Country Planning Act 1990, as originally enacted;
  >   - (ii) section 50 of the [^c15660941]Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972;
  > - (b) a planning obligation entered into under section 106 of the Act of 1990, as substituted by section 12 of the [^c15660951]Planning and Compensation Act 1991, or under section 299A of the Act of 1990;
  > - (c) an obligation arising under or under an agreement made under any provision—
  >   - (i) corresponding to section 106 of the [^c15660961]Town and Country Planning Act 1990, as originally enacted or as substituted by the Act of 1991 or to section 299A of the Act of 1990; and
  >   - (ii) for the time being in force in Northern Ireland.
