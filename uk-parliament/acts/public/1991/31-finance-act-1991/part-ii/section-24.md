# Finance Act 1991, section 24

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/31
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Charge and rate of corporation tax for 1991.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Corporation tax shall be charged for the financial year 1991 at the rate of 33 per cent.
