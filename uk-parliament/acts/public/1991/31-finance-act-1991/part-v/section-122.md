# Finance Act 1991, section 122

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/31
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 122. Interpretation etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act “the Taxes Act 1988 means the [^c12038431]Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988.

(2) Part II of this Act, so far as it relates to capital gains tax, shall be construed as one with the [^c12038441]Capital Gains Tax Act 1979.

(3) Part III of this Act shall be construed as one with Part I of the [^c12038451]Oil Taxation Act 1975 and in that Part of this Act “the principal Act means that Act.
