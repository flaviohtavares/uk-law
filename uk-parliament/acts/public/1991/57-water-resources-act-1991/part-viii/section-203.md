# Water Resources Act 1991, section 203

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/57

## 203. Exchange of information with respect to pollution incidents etc.[^c18740761][^c-cbc1e4c30f2ed60c375234bcd3a88cb8][^c-097056b5d40e8ee3d9bc78ae4feac216]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) It shall be the duty of the appropriate agency[^c-7290ae0588d46777aeda3443d4530945] to provide a water undertaker with all such information to which this section applies as is in the possession of the appropriate agency and is reasonably requested by the undertaker for purposes connected with the carrying out of its functions.

(1A)[^c-0e0aa0a23e80bfb0f7b95eeb1f016ee1] It shall be the duty of the appropriate agency to provide a water supply licensee[^c-3ea7459a946caf0b87d4c65148b9f9ca] with all such information to which this section applies as is in the possession of the appropriate agency and is reasonably requested by the licensee[^c-28ca2de4696dc46e8b0efbbe7d9ac9d6] for purposes connected with the carrying on of activities under the licensee's licence[^c-737699c38828615139962cbd1fd39efc].

(2) It shall be the duty of every water undertaker to provide the appropriate agency with all such information to which this section applies as is in the possession of the undertaker and is reasonably requested by the appropriate agency for purposes connected with the carrying out of any of its functions.

(2A)[^c-322ff52927406e174ec950881ac3aac4] It shall be the duty of every water supply licensee[^c-9945f3ee0058a464b295ac6ec11b3d35] to provide the appropriate agency with all such information to which this section applies as is in the possession of the licensee[^c-5b0a2565af2c23f0d5bd044c4a966c47] and is reasonably requested by the appropriate agency for purposes connected with the carrying out of any of its functions.

(3)[^c-ec8e1963f523632d47d80fc0d2db5f3a] Information provided to a water undertaker, to a water supply licensee[^c-a0f4c343e9663182437870b6e92a0012] or to the appropriate agency under subsection (1), (1A), (2) or (2A) above shall be provided in such form and in such manner and at such times as the undertaker, the licensee[^c-d3c4e320b0732555a1bce4f14cfcda24] or the appropriate agency, as the case may be, may reasonably require.

(4) Information provided under subsection (1), (1A), (2) or (2A)[^c-632e936b048ef0ea9c5514e969d37ae9] above to a water undertaker , to a[^c-dc3d17c10c1a670fd328e01b3ca2e296] water supply licensee[^c-460d6898344a5fc817935591a236a24b] or to the appropriate agency shall be provided free of charge.

(5) The duties of

- (a)[^c-a534ab3641aba9fc8d3db8d0ff3c7f18] a water undertaker under subsection (2) above; or
- (b) a water supply licensee[^c-c8283c2ddeb33a368d8d051374f1e26c] under subsection (2A) above,

shall be enforceable under section 18 of the [^c18740841]Water Industry Act 1991 by the Secretary of State.

(6) This section applies to information—

- (a) about the quality of any controlled waters or of any other waters; or
- (b) about any incident in which any poisonous, noxious or polluting matter or any [^c-d20a5bef465e82a6581b78282f2f080c]... waste matter has entered any controlled waters or other waters.

(7) In this section “**controlled waters**” has the same meaning as in Part III of this Act.

(8)[^c-f97c7f82f1134088280d3e21b478c8bd] Any reference in this section to a water supply licensee[^c-de2e7b3d54812ac1e6e153adc298c3d9] is a reference to a person[^c-179abae80e1d6316fdbc1af09c8953ef] holding a licence under Chapter 1A of Part 2 of the Water Industry Act 1991.
