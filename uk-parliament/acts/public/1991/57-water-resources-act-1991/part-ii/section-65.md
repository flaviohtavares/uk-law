# Water Resources Act 1991, section 65

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/57

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 65. Licences of right.

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 7 to this Act shall have effect for the purposes of giving effect to provisions conferring an entitlement to licences under this Chapter and with respect to licences granted in pursuance of that entitlement or the entitlement conferred by section 33 of the [^c18731621]Water Resources Act 1963 or paragraph 30 or 31 of Schedule 26 to the [^c18731631]Water Act 1989.
