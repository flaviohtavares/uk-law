# Water Resources Act 1991, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/57
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Curtailment of rights under section 27.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6] The provisions of this section shall have effect where a person (“the occupier") is entitled, by virtue of subsection (6) of section 27 above, to a protected right for the purposes of this Chapter by reason of his being the occupier of such a holding as is mentioned in subsection (4) of that section in relation to an abstraction falling within that subsection (“the holding"). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6]

(2) If it appears to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6] Agency[^c18729851] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6] that the occupier is entitled, as against other occupiers of land contiguous to the inland waters in question, to abstract water from those waters for use on part of the holding (“the relevant part"), but is not so entitled to abstract water for use on other parts of the holding— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6]

- (a) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6] Agency *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6] may serve on him a notice specifying the relevant part of the holding; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6]
- (b) subject to the following provisions of this section, the notice shall have effect so as to require subsections (3) and (4) of section 27 above to be construed in relation to the holding as if the references in subsection (4) to use on the holding were references to use on the part of the holding specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6]

(3) Where a notice is served under subsection (2) above and the occupier objects to the notice on the grounds— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6]

- (a) that he is entitled, as against other occupiers of land contiguous to the inland waters in question, to abstract water from those waters for use on every part of the holding; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6]
- (b) that he is so entitled to abstract water for use on a larger part of the holding than that specified in the notice, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6]

he may, within such period (not being less than twenty-eight days from the date of service of the notice) and in such manner as may be prescribed, appeal to the court against the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6]

(4) On any appeal under subsection (3) above, the court shall determine the matter in dispute and, in accordance with its decision, confirm, quash or vary the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6] Agency’s *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6] notice and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6]

- (a) where the court quashes a notice served under subsection (2) above, paragraph (b) of that subsection shall not have effect; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6]
- (b) where the court varies such a notice, that paragraph shall have effect, but with the substitution, for the reference to the part of the holding specified in the notice, of a reference to the part specified in the notice as varied by the court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6]

(5) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6]

- “**the court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6]**” means the county court for the district in which the holding, or the part of the holding which is contiguous to the inland waters in question, is situated; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6]
- “**entitled *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6]**” (except in subsection (1) above) means entitled apart from this Chapter or any other statutory provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-86ed93d3bff1f2f55cf82211188489d6]
