# Water Resources Act 1991, section 15

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 15. General duties with respect to the water industry.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) It shall be the duty of the Agency and the[^c-1836d9b75fc03d2e2fe84309929d39aa] NRBW, in exercising any of their[^c-b821ccc75e53efeafa15570bcf1f3bfe] powers under any enactment, to have particular regard to the duties imposed, by virtue of the provisions of Parts II to IV of the [^c18728821]Water Industry Act 1991, on any water undertaker or sewerage undertaker which appears to the Agency or the[^c-61934c4ac430c22b450c7042b41b7b94] NRBW, as the case may be, to be or to be likely to be affected by the exercise of the power in question.

(2) It shall be the duty of each of the Ministers, in exercising—

- (a) any power conferred by virtue of the 1995 Act,[^c18728841]this Act, the [^c18728851]Land Drainage Act 1991, the Water Industry Act 1991, the Water Act 1989 or the Natural Resources Body for Wales (Establishment) Order 2012 ([^c-17f11af5ff18f0f6f811a18c659aaf81]S.I.[2012/1903](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/1903)) in relation to, or to decisions of, the Agency or the[^c-184f8aeb73403c2e0ee291f4f4d29157] NRBW; or
- (b) any power which, but for any direction given by one of the Ministers, would fall to be exercised by the Agency or the NRBW,

to take into account the duty imposed on the Agency and the[^c-f2e955fba9e5dace8310b8745e387d00] NRBW by subsection (1) above.
