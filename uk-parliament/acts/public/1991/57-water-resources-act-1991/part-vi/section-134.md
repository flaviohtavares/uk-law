## 134. Raising of general drainage charges.[^c18735781]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to subsection (2) below, the appropriate agency[^key-21423ce0d47eaa650fb851bfceea3f8e] may raise at an amount per hectare of chargeable land in a flood risk management region[^key-b5981a32cf45ee9e65433de1610025ff] a charge to be known as a general drainage charge and to be levied in accordance with sections 135 and 136 below.

(2) The Agency[^key-a8e3b0148f9e8ca61664c9a8ac144727] shall not levy a general drainage charge in respect of any flood risk management region[^key-ea7f4dbefd14dc167d281484d770960d] unless the Regional Flood and Coastal Committee for that region[^key-11ff1e4a15f8873d8df40fbd075e104e] have recommended that such a charge should be raised.
