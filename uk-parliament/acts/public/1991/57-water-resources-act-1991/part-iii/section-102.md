# Water Resources Act 1991, section 102

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/57
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 102. Power to give effect to international obligations etc.[^c-2d982e4f314efd9f2e138305aa19ff79]

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State shall have power by regulations to provide that the water pollution provisions of this Act shall have effect with such modifications as may be prescribed for the purpose of [^c-979b194255cda1d99fac5415fc69fa1b]...—

- (a)[^c-91b0595097dec64c5eb111309c4c60dc] giving effect to any assimilated[^c-0dcbd5514cb2ce653f139999f247f08c] obligations, or
- (b) enabling Her Majesty's Government in the United Kingdom to give effect[^c-e98a3af2259ae081610dd13fa63e59ba] to any international agreement to which the United Kingdom is for the time being a party.
