# Criminal Justice Act 1991, section 86A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/53
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86A. Power of prisoner custody officers to detain suspected offenders[^c19692371]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A prisoner custody officer performing custodial duties at a contracted out prison shall have the following powers in relation to any person who is in or is seeking to enter the prison (other than a prisoner confined in the prison).

(2)[^c19692391] Where the officer has reason to believe that the person is committing or has committed an offence under any of sections 39 to 40D of the Prison Act 1952, the officer may—

- (a) require the person to wait with him for the arrival of a constable for such period as may be necessary (not exceeding two hours); and
- (b) use reasonable force to prevent the person from making off while subject to a requirement under paragraph (a).

(3) A person who makes off while subject to such a requirement is guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.

(4)[^c19693171] In subsection (2), a reference to an offence under a particular provision includes a reference to any offence consisting of an attempt to commit, incitement or conspiracy to commit, or aiding, abetting, counselling or procuring the commission of, an offence under that provision.
