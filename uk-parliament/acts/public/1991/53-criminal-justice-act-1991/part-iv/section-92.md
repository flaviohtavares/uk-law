# Criminal Justice Act 1991, section 92

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/53

## 92. Interpretation of Part IV.[^c13776361]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part unless the context otherwise requires[^c13776371]—

- “**contracted out prison**” and “**the contractor**” have the meanings given by section 84(4)[^c13776381] above;
- “[^c13776391]**contracted out functions**” and “**directly managed prison**” have the meanings given by section 88A(5) above;
- [^c18975351]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^c18975481]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**prison**” includes a young offender institution or remand centre;
- “**prison officer**” means an officer of a directly managed prison;
- “**prison rules**” means rules made under section 47 of the 1952 Act;
- “[^c13776411]**prisoner**” means any person for the time being detained in legal custody as a result of a requirement imposed by a court or otherwise that he be so detained;
- “**prisoner custody officer**” has the meaning given by section 89(1) above;
- “**prisoner escort arrangements**” has the meaning given by section 80(2) above.
- “**sub-contractor**” has the meaning given by section 84(4) above.

(1A)[^c13776431] Any reference in this Part to custodial duties at a contracted out prison includes a reference to custodial duties in relation to a prisoner who is outside such a prison for temporary purposes.

(2)[^c18975521] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Sections 80, 81(1) and (2)(a), 82 and 89 to 91 above, subsection (1) above and Schedule 10 to this Act shall have effect as if—

- (a) any reference in section 80(1), 81(1), 82 or 91 above to prisoners included a reference to persons remanded[^c13776451] to local authority accommodation or youth detention accommodation under section 91 of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012[^c-f35662abe14a9a9236668a8bba67fd44] by virtue of a security requirement imposed under section 23(4) of the 1969 Act (remands and committals to local authority accommodation); and
- (b) any reference in section 80(1)(c) or (e) or (1A) above to a prison included a reference to accommodation in which a person is or is to be accommodated pursuant to such a remand[^c-8468e2c62aff3a94af88af4beb9b64e8].

(4)[^c13776471] In sections 80, 82 and 83 above, “**prison**”—

- (a) so far as relating to the delivery of prisoners to or from a prison situated in Scotland, includes a remand centre or young offenders institution within the meaning of section 19 of the [^c13776481]Prisons (Scotland) Act 1989; and
- (b) so far as relating to the delivery of prisoners to or from a prison situated in Northern Ireland, includes a remand centre or young offenders centre.
