# Criminal Justice Act 1991, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/53
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101. Transitional provisions, savings and repeals.[^c13777441]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The transitional provisions and savings contained in Schedule 12 to this Act shall have effect; but nothing in this subsection shall be taken as prejudicing the operation of sections 16 and 17 of the [^c13777451]Interpretation Act 1978 (which relate to the effect of repeals).

(2) The enactments mentioned in Schedule 13 to this Act (which include some that are spent or no longer of practical utility) are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
