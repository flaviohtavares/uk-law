# Criminal Justice Act 1991, section 36

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Power to release prisoners on compassionate grounds.[^c13769181][^c13769191][^c13769201]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 2005-04-04).**

(1) The Secretary of State may at any time release a short-term or long-term prisoner[^c13769221] on licence if he is satisfied that exceptional circumstances exist which justify the prisoner’s release on compassionate grounds.

(2) Before releasing a long-term [^c13769201]. . . prisoner under subsection (1) above, the Secretary of State shall consult the Board, unless the circumstances are such as to render such consultation impracticable.
