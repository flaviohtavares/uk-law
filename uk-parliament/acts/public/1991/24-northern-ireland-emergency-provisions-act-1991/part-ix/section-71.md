# Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991, section 71

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Short title and extent.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-08-25).**

(1) This Act may be cited as the Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991.

(2) Except for sections 53, to 54A, 55A[^c1324277] and 57, paragraph 19 of Schedule 4, Schedule 5, this Part of this Act so far as relating to those provisions and paragraph 5(2), (3), (7) and (8) of Schedule 7, this Act extends to Northern Ireland only.

(3) Her Majesty may by Order in Council direct that any of the provisions of section 57 and Schedule 5 shall extend, with such modifications as appear to Her Majesty to be appropriate, to any of the Channel Islands or the Isle of Man.
