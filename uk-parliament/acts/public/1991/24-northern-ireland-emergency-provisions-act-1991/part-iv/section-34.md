# Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991, section 34

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 34. Detention orders.[^c1324150][^c1324151]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-08-25).**

Schedule 3 to this Act shall have effect with respect to the detention of terrorists and persons suspected of being terrorists.
