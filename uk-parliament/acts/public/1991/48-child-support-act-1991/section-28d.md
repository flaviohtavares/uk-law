## 28D. Determination of applications.[^key-9bfce69e7ef0477040f3d74c72754258][^key-692be854cd99c9efc52d0eb35ba3f135]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-09a4340d6dcf364342a19fdc58878fa6] Where an application for a variation has not failed, the Secretary of State[^key-b36a0e036e96368c8b70814ca3571bad] shall, in accordance with the relevant provisions of, or made under, this Act—

- (a) either agree or not to a variation, and make a decision under section 11 or 12(1); or
- (b) refer the application to the First-tier Tribunal[^key-6d899c0bff6a3b1d03d69b933959d0df] for the tribunal to determine what variation, if any, is to be made.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1), an application for a variation[^key-246692363aacdc64072db1bcddcd7321] has failed if—

- (a) it has [^key-83abab40e08b8c78cc6bcdbbd5207638]... been withdrawn; or
- (b) the Secretary of State[^key-cb9a41323e582e345cfd873bbf35f30a] has rejected it on completing a preliminary consideration under section 28B; or[^key-90c559bc3e66b1e524306a4c8ddc8cb2]
- (c) the Secretary of State has refused to consider it under section 28C(5).

(2A)[^key-7721ac520d8c3adfe3487d0e0e6a4cc4] Subsection (2B) applies if—

- (a) the application for a variation is made by the person with care or (in the case of an application for a maintenance calculation under section 7) the person with care or the child concerned, and
- (b) it appears to the Secretary of State[^M_F_570d7f8c-7126-4073-80c4-12d44ac26a03] that consideration of further information or evidence may affect the decision under subsection (1)(a) whether or not to agree to a variation.

(2B) Before making the decision under subsection (1)(a) the Secretary of State[^M_F_9e864915-b61e-46a7-8a1d-1606063e7d57] must—

- (a) consider any such further information or evidence that is available to the Secretary of State, and
- (b) where necessary, take such steps as the Secretary of State considers appropriate to obtain any such further information or evidence.

(3) In dealing with an application for a variation which has been referred to it under subsection (1)(b), the First-tier Tribunal[^key-894aa830a41556a358f3bb74dc4753bf] shall have the same powers, and be subject to the same duties, apart from the duty under subsection (2B)[^key-861447de993a5a5b3ed440d4eac7434c], as would the Secretary of State in[^key-c54575bce5dfdcf48a720d3b0ee31744] dealing with the application.
