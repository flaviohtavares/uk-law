# Child Support Act 1991, section 43A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (110).** Text as at 2022-03-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43A. Recovery of arrears from deceased's estate[^M_C_c6e0b2f3-b338-4ce1-b223-859786d4e04d][^c-2ee06e5e50e050360f38451acda9e611]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision for the recovery from the estate of a deceased person of arrears of child support maintenance for which the deceased person was liable immediately before death.

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) may, in particular—

- (a) make provision for arrears of child support maintenance for which a deceased person was so liable to be a debt payable by the deceased's executor or administrator out of the deceased's estate to the Secretary of State[^c-18c87aefcb446593fc293e7f7521a1fb];
- (b) make provision for establishing the amount of any such arrears;
- (c) make provision about procedure in relation to claims under the regulations.

(3) Regulations under subsection (1) may include provision for proceedings (whether by appeal or otherwise) to be instituted, continued or withdrawn by the deceased's executor or administrator.
