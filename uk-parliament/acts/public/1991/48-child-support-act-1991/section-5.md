# Child Support Act 1991, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (110).** Text as at 2022-03-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Child support maintenance: supplemental provisions.[^c21358531]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where—

- (a) there is more than one person with care of a qualifying child; and
- (b) one or more, but not all, of them have parental responsibility for [^key-4738feb9e3069d5424dedfddbdb7e44c]... the child;

no application may be made for a maintenance calculation[^key-fad1874c39e818d578cda5edda000619] with respect to the child by any of those persons who do not have parental responsibility for [^key-4738feb9e3069d5424dedfddbdb7e44c]... the child.

(2) Where more than one application for a maintenance calculation is made with respect to the child concerned, only one of them may be proceeded with.

(3) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision as to which of two or more applications for a maintenance calculation with respect to the same child is to be proceeded with.
