# Child Support Act 1991, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (110).** Text as at 2022-03-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. The duty to maintain.[^c21358471]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) For the purposes of this Act, each parent of a qualifying child is responsible for maintaining him.

(2) For the purposes of this Act, a non-resident parent[^c-485dc06a7a523b0448d6f9bba33653c3] shall be taken to have met his responsibility to maintain any qualifying child of his by making periodical payments of maintenance with respect to the child of such amount, and at such intervals, as may be determined in accordance with the provisions of this Act.

(3) Where a maintenance calculation[^c-fad1874c39e818d578cda5edda000619] made under this Act requires the making of periodical payments, it shall be the duty of the non-resident parent with respect to whom the calculation[^c-4cbdfdc917015a31b2c075a1ef91d78d] was made to make those payments.
