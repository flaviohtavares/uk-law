## 23. Child Support Commissioners for Northern Ireland.[^c21358911]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Her Majesty may from time to time appoint a Chief Child Support Commissioner for Northern Ireland and other Child Support Commissioners for Northern Ireland[^c-d02f1af050976e3f2dd887247f1c9a8c].

(2) The Chief Child Support Commissioner for Northern Ireland and the other Child Support Commissioners for Northern Ireland shall be appointed from among persons who are barristers or solicitors of not less than 7[^c-b3c9f94417a360f53b21ab392113b8e0] years’ standing.
