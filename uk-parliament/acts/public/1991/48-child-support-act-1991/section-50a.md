# Child Support Act 1991, section 50A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (110).** Text as at 2022-03-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50A. Use of computers[^key-32e37bc10c96c8e8af3685c5a996507a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any decision falling to be made under or by virtue of this Act by the Secretary of State[^key-6b8f2ae4d8d5e33e08b99736b56c4083] may be made, not only by a person authorised to exercise the Secretary of State’s[^key-d77fbf7e02af7bce7f41725fbb71db5f] decision-making function, but also by a computer for whose operation such a person is responsible.
