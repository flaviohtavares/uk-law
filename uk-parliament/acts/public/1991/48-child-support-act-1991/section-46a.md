# Child Support Act 1991, section 46A

## 46A. Finality of decisions[^key-3169df508da7684c37243a907c503791]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act and to any provision made by or under Chapter 2 of Part 1 of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007[^key-c107323064925c04b716b2ea449740b5], any decision of [^key-c28648d7255a0777accaa4374c2a08d0]... the Secretary of State or the First-tier Tribunal[^key-c43e49466e2f1366a26d728d7b47f5fc] made in accordance with the foregoing provisions of this Act shall be final.

(2) If and to the extent that regulations so provide, any finding of fact or other determination embodied in or necessary to such a decision, or on which such a decision is based, shall be conclusive for the purposes of—

- (a) further such decisions;
- (b) decisions made in accordance with sections 8 to 16 of the Social Security Act 1998, or with regulations under section 11 of that Act; and
- (c) decisions made under the Vaccine Damage Payments Act 1979.
