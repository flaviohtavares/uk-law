# Child Support Act 1991, section 41B

## 41B. Repayment of overpaid child support maintenance.[^key-c779506ccb6ccf15fb4e7f23593d74aa][^key-29365e5d4c83197df79bfb181207ede2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This section applies where it appears to the Secretary of State[^key-7a5a496c506236b649f534bee35ba0a2] that a non-resident parent[^key-485dc06a7a523b0448d6f9bba33653c3] has made a payment by way of child support maintenance which amounts to an overpayment by him of that maintenance and that—

- (a) it would not be possible for the non-resident parent to recover the amount of the overpayment by way of an adjustment of the amount payable under a maintenance calculation[^key-fad1874c39e818d578cda5edda000619]; or
- (b) it would be inappropriate to rely on an adjustment of the amount payable under a maintenance calculation as the means of enabling the non-resident parent to recover the amount of the overpayment.

(1A)[^key-aabc19789660e684f50664cd93c0f491] This section also applies where the non-resident parent has made a voluntary payment and it appears to the Secretary of State[^key-889a2ca74ea220ea09b87a7dc0d47d82]—

- (a) that he is not liable to pay child support maintenance; or
- (b) that he is liable, but some or all of the payment amounts to an overpayment,

and, in a case falling within paragraph (b), it also appears to the Secretary of State that subsection (1)(a) or (b) applies.

(2) The Secretary of State[^key-91cf231dc99e43e089b96d6d907ad1fd] may make such payment to the non-resident parent by way of reimbursement, or partial reimbursement, of the overpayment as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

(3) Where the Secretary of State[^key-588fea93a7859c310ae0922ee6174435] has made a payment under this section the Secretary of State may, in such circumstances as may be prescribed, require the relevant person to pay to the Secretary of State the whole, or a specified proportion, of the amount of that payment.

(4) Any such requirement shall be imposed by giving the relevant person a written demand for the amount which the Secretary of State[^key-99eb9c1d59e3c8cc015f9888270b8d7b] wishes to recover from him.

(5) Any sum which a person is required to pay to the Secretary of State under this section shall be recoverable from him by the Secretary of State as a debt due to the Crown.

(6) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision in relation to any case in which—

- (a) one or more overpayments of child support maintenance are being reimbursed to the Secretary of State[^key-d6574e055ff2854b1973afc3ef78ccd2] by the relevant person; and
- (b) child support maintenance has continued to be payable by the non-resident parent concerned to the person with care concerned, or again becomes so payable.

(7)[^key-fe4f6d275e6a99e3ba3e08c38849ddde] For the purposes of this section—

- (a) a payment made by a person under a maintenance calculation which was not validly made; and
- (b) a voluntary payment made in the circumstances set out in subsection (1A)(a),

shall be treated as an overpayment of child support maintenance made by a non-resident parent.

(8) In this section “**relevant person**”, in relation to an overpayment, means the person with care to whom the overpayment was made.

(9) Any sum recovered by the Secretary of State[^key-2d04b59e53f590d693405a9248e09432] under this section shall be paid by the Secretary of State into the Consolidated Fund.
