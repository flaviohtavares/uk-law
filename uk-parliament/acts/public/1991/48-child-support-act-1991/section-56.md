## 56. Corresponding provision for and co-ordination with Northern Ireland.[^c21359581][^c21359591]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An Order in Council made under paragraph 1(1)(b) of Schedule 1 to the [^c21359601]Northern Ireland Act 1974 which contains a statement that it is made only for purposes corresponding to those of the provisions of this Act, other than provisions which relate to the appointment of Child Support Commissioners for Northern Ireland—

- (a) shall not be subject to sub-paragraphs (4) and (5) of paragraph 1 of that Schedule (affirmative resolution of both Houses of Parliament); but
- (b) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
