# Child Support Act 1991, section 54

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (110).** Text as at 2022-03-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/48

## 54. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^M_F_ec813147-11ed-46d4-82e3-409490ee063b] In this Act—

- “**non-resident parent[^c-485dc06a7a523b0448d6f9bba33653c3]**”, has the meaning given in section 3(2);
- [^c-3921071d05866f346d18a5454999ec8e]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^c-7047fbee3dac1051e5c0987a41a0852a]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “[^c-72ff49f371ca35a347b585d4d6133c84]**application for a variation[^c-baaed7d512b2d429f3662601ec7550fa]**” means an application under section 28A or 28G[^c-a693ea42200f2eab95b05c567975d984];
- [^c-a15b6c0fe1fb77d7c8055cca7ab0353d]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**benefit Acts**” means the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 and the Social Security Administration Act 1992[^c21359521];
- [^c-3921071d05866f346d18a5454999ec8e]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^c-3921071d05866f346d18a5454999ec8e]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “[^c-4c679cec19c658912f82ebe704c847b0]**charging order**” has the same meaning as in section 1 of the Charging Orders Act 1979;
- “**child benefit**” has the same meaning as in the [^c21359531]Child Benefit Act 1975;
- [^c-3921071d05866f346d18a5454999ec8e]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**child support maintenance**” has the meaning given in section 3(6);
- [^c-3921071d05866f346d18a5454999ec8e]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^c-a15b6c0fe1fb77d7c8055cca7ab0353d]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^c-6d8076080c731cf761f8cbb188846525]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**curfew order**” has the meaning given in section 39H(1);
- “**deduction from earnings order**” has the meaning given in section 31(2);
- “[^c-737c136c4b43444c1d969e87bf630ab5]**default maintenance decision**” has the meaning given in section 12;
- [^c-a15b6c0fe1fb77d7c8055cca7ab0353d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**deposit-taker**” means a person who, in the course of a business, may lawfully accept deposits in the United Kingdom;
- “**disability living allowance**” has the same meaning as in the benefit Acts;
- [^c-a91b24ac7078c6a0aa6204232b42437a]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^c-d2e49e9ea24167bca50777c0d3ab42d0]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “[^c-3cc8e7e3d594b3376625f2f1fa5e0f3f]**income-based jobseeker’s allowance**” has the same meaning as in the Jobseekers Act 1995;
- “[^c-467485cb59f41df97a9b8a9897368d16]**income-related employment and support allowance**” means an income-related allowance under Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2007 (employment and support allowance);
- “**income support**” has the same meaning as in the benefit Acts;
- “**interim maintenance decision[^c-acccd366f5eee8ef3ef231181e5777d7]**” has the meaning given in section 12;
- “**liability order**” has the meaning given in section 33(2);
- “**maintenance agreement**” has the meaning given in section 9(1);
- “[^c-8cf3044fe209e2fd38c8bf80bd43ac03]**maintenance calculation**” means a calculation of maintenance made under this Act and, except in prescribed circumstances, includes a default maintenance decision and an interim maintenance decision;
- “**maintenance order**” has the meaning given in section 8(11);
- [^c-a15b6c0fe1fb77d7c8055cca7ab0353d]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**parent**”, in relation to any child, means any person who is in law the mother or father of the child;
- “**parent with care**” means a person who is, in relation to a child, both a parent and a person with care.
- “[^c-a4c91120350e026d9e5dce696c228d4a]**parental responsibility**”, in the application of this Act—
  - (a) to England and Wales, has the same meaning as in the Children Act 1989; and
  - (b) to Scotland, shall be construed as a reference to “parental responsibilities” within the meaning given by section 1(3) of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995;
- [^c-2d09996354b18eb37c279eca6ee94c2f]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**person with care**” has the meaning given in section 3(3);
- “**prescribed**” means prescribed by regulations made by the Secretary of State;
- “**qualifying child**” has the meaning given in section 3(1);
- “[^c-370d32932ab4d722a41c056a5e1362f9]**voluntary payment**” has the meaning given in section 28J.

(2)[^c-815232601f70f521944cec2b8d1abe82] The definition of “deposit-taker” in subsection (1) is to be read with—

- (a) section 22 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000;
- (b) any relevant order under that section; and
- (c) Schedule 2 to that Act.
