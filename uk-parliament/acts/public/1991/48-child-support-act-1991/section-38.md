# Child Support Act 1991, section 38

## 38. Enforcement of liability orders by diligence: Scotland.[^c21359221][^c-c779506ccb6ccf15fb4e7f23593d74aa][^c-143bcc4c9d00e19d99768b4b07009771]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In Scotland, where a liability order has been made against a person, the order shall be warrant anywhere in Scotland—

- (a) for the Secretary of State[^c-41364e49c738a057a52d66901ee7ef09] to charge the person to pay the appropriate amount and to recover that amount by an attachment[^c-8d6ee2f65f99eaac74e7f028f2284421] and, in connection therewith, for the opening of shut and lockfast places;
- (b) for an arrestment (other than an arrestment of the person’s earnings in the hands of his employers) and action of furthcoming or sale,
- (c)[^c-bc80302af1996ab4174346a2b3db26e3] for an inhibition.

and shall be apt to found a Bill of Inhibition or an action of adjudication at the instance of the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bc80302af1996ab4174346a2b3db26e3]

(2) In subsection (1) the “**appropriate amount**” means the amount in respect of which the order was made, to the extent that it remains unpaid.
