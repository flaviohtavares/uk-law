## 23A. Redetermination of appeals.[^c-f3b56fb0b444ee5f82c7e4012622a35a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where an application is made to the First-tier Tribunal for permission to appeal to the Upper Tribunal from any decision of the First-tier Tribunal under section 20[^c-03c7fc197d5f721f6e33f6053adf6646].

(2)[^c-07c849cd1cb29a1c7c75ce6775e9bb8b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) If each of the principal parties to the case expresses the view that the decision was erroneous in point of law, the First-tier Tribunal[^c-979f201ebe49afc44dde0ef9df3c7452] shall set aside the decision and refer the case for determination by a differently constituted First-tier Tribunal[^c-d4995434586de87dbbb13fc85252522a].

(4) The “principal parties” are—

- (za)[^c-4aefc4721b613dddd89ff239813a64dc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (a) the Secretary of State; and
- (b) those who are qualifying persons for the purposes of section 20(2) in relation to the decision in question.
