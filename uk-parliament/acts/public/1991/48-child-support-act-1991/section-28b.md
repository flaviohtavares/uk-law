# Child Support Act 1991, section 28B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (110).** Text as at 2022-03-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/48

## 28B. Preliminary consideration of applications.[^c-92a577c4d40ae15624e08f5a98c6b96b][^c-8a599091430eae0a639366595737b7c2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where an application for a variation has been duly made to the Secretary of State[^c-00f6dcb8fa9ee107fb78440656988539], the Secretary of State may give it a preliminary consideration.

(2) The Secretary of State may on completing such a[^c-7156fcfa27b876952655b2c8dec38a3c] preliminary consideration, reject the application (and proceed to make a[^c-20b31413e7c1d818b7296d58c072fab5] decision on the application for a maintenance calculation without any variation) if it appears to the[^c-4adb1d277261c7509dbbe2d030fad32d] Secretary of State[^c-7b183dd487d2d8c3b8f00f1a2e79943a]—

- (a) that there are no grounds on which a variation could be agreed to[^c-274f3b3b20fa27f5c936dd68736d7f1f];
- (b) that the Secretary of State[^c-ac8e97d0352c0eccdb523c61d69f82dc] has insufficient information to make a decision on the application for the maintenance calculation under section 11 (apart from any information needed in relation to the application for a variation), and therefore that the Secretary of State’s decision would be made under section 12(1); or
- (c) that other prescribed circumstances apply.
