## 28ZD. Correction of errors and setting aside of decisions[^c-1e29912720ae53adbf65033183657b0a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulations may make provision with respect to—

- (a) the correction of accidental errors in any decision of the Secretary of State[^c-a35b7a99ce092fa90ca122463d7da0fa] or record of a decision of the Secretary of State given under this Act; [^c-3c103aa9b6cc5f713d36716f19c8d9fc]...
- (b)[^c-3c103aa9b6cc5f713d36716f19c8d9fc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Nothing in subsection (1) shall be construed as derogating from any power to correct errors [^c-ccd4e882da320d9708e5f754082588f5]... which is exercisable apart from regulations made by virtue of that subsection.
