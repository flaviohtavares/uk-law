## 20. Appeals to First-tier Tribunal.[^c-601a068a0673422b45a2ce3e33e491cb][^c-b6fcfc5b59f03a1673db7a6d4fea64c2][^c-d5d059c82c328ffc377ec72cd63101f6][^M_C_25c90c92-47e9-438b-dac5-de9179c06874][^c-2922deea82ba2afc4e6931529c5e4e82][^c-c7492dc52bcd45a845e95b1be4f1dff1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A qualifying person has a right of appeal to the First-tier Tribunal[^c-0a3d1cd6f438682b69adccb4b6d94d1f] against—

- (a) a decision of the Secretary of State[^c-2e6bbcd90b0af5c6fdd0ada89952d29a] under section 11, 12 or 17 (whether as originally made or as revised under section 16);
- (b) a decision of the Secretary of State not to make a maintenance calculation under section 11 or not to supersede a decision under section 17;
- (c)[^c-0702adcb89a317bdb9cbb04e0628b12a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) the imposition (by virtue of section 41A) of a requirement to make penalty payments, or their amount;
- (e) the imposition (by virtue of section 47) of a requirement to pay fees.

(2) In subsection (1), “**qualifying person**” means—

- (a) in relation to paragraphs (a) and (b)—
  - (i) the person with care, or non-resident parent, with respect to whom the Secretary of State[^c-efb52b3f866a47c8a66441047486166f] made the decision, or
  - (ii) in a case relating to a maintenance calculation which was applied for under section 7, either of those persons or the child concerned;
- (b)[^c-0ce78284fdbe133d6986555ef149d552] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) in relation to paragraph (d), the parent who has been required to make penalty payments; and
- (d) in relation to paragraph (e), the person required to pay fees.

(2A)[^c-d493d5d3669a1e0dca30e6be9167e09c] Regulations may provide that, in such cases or circumstances as may be prescribed, there is a right of appeal against a decision mentioned in subsection (1)(a) or (b) only if the Secretary of State[^M_F_a58a0b02-21b3-48cb-de90-2f4357c4067b] has considered whether to revise the decision under section 16.

(2B) The regulations may in particular provide that that condition is met only where—

- (a) the consideration by the Secretary of State was on an application,
- (b) the Secretary of State considered issues of a specified description, or
- (c) the consideration by the Secretary of State satisfied any other condition specified in the regulations.

(3)[^c-a9008e0dc96340380cf20f55137a2f8e] A person with a right of appeal under this section shall be given such notice as may be prescribed of—

- (a) that right; and
- (b) the relevant decision, or the imposition of the requirement.

(4)[^c-a9008e0dc96340380cf20f55137a2f8e] Regulations may make—

- (a) provision as to the manner in which, and the time within which, appeals are to be brought; [^c-01591cb967df267a840cb51ea48d302f]...
- (b)[^c-01591cb967df267a840cb51ea48d302f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-b5f624783365482bd76811c486b12348] provision that, where in accordance with regulations under subsection (2A) there is no right of appeal against a decision, any purported appeal may be treated as an application for revision under section 16.

(5)[^c-a9008e0dc96340380cf20f55137a2f8e] The regulations may in particular make any provision of a kind mentioned in Schedule 5 to the Social Security Act 1998.

(6)[^c-fe8e0dff9ee687ec2bada615f85f4e64] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^c-a9008e0dc96340380cf20f55137a2f8e] In deciding an appeal under this section, the First-tier Tribunal[^c-68fd5e6b4f92c7ef86a7ab965fb240c9]—

- (a) need not consider any issue that is not raised by the appeal; and
- (b) shall not take into account any circumstances not obtaining at the time when the Secretary of State[^c-2664093225b5e5afa3a87b85b9bf354d] made the decision or imposed the requirement.

(8)[^c-a9008e0dc96340380cf20f55137a2f8e] If an appeal under this section is allowed, the First-tier Tribunal[^c-842287b0cf31f61888528c208ed565b3] may—

- (a) itself make such decision as it considers appropriate; or
- (b) remit the case to the Secretary of State[^c-efc534641245b4cf2e2c2083d30330ae], together with such directions (if any) as it considers appropriate.
