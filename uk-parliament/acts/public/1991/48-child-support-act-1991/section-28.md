## 28. Power of Secretary of State to initiate or defend actions of declarator: Scotland.[^c-b9b26501a25b7b9316f247d26e7ae20a]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-b41a4a108cbcd0516cef12260b3af745] Subsection (1A) applies in any case where—

- (a) an application for a maintenance calculation[^c-fad1874c39e818d578cda5edda000619] has been made [^c-32f74d6736f53ab58bf13cbc80e9c808]... , or a maintenance calculation is in force, with respect to a person (“the alleged parent”) who denies that he is a parent of a child with respect to whom the application was made[^c-c19b8908f0af94d4a3e4fdf77df0c3f2] [^c-32f74d6736f53ab58bf13cbc80e9c808]... or the calculation was made; and
- (b) the[^c-14cf79683a32e323ef3f97f5ceb461ca] Secretary of State[^c-0d8e439f4d92412d2ca1f77fdfd0ac37] is not satisfied that the case falls within one of those set out in section 26(2).

(1A) In any case where this subsection applies, the Secretary of State[^c-c095cf04a78ff284de91b2b88b9c34fc] may bring an action for declarator of parentage under section 7 of the Law Reform (Parent and Child) (Scotland) Act 1986.

(2) The Secretary of State[^c-0df2404842b0005cb6a75bea1bda676d] may defend an action for declarator of non-parentage or illegitimacy brought by a person named as the alleged parent in an application for a maintenance calculation or in a[^c-4d9bd7d889269105dd40515f4757af0c] maintenance calculation which is in force.

(3) This section applies to Scotland only.
