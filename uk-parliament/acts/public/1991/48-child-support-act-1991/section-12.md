## 12. Default and interim maintenance decisions.[^c-69347c427e5cd1d86248c935b9f472a2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where the Secretary of State[^c-12d68d6f21a9af01408912603ec9ea77]—

- (a) is required to make a maintenance calculation; or
- (b) is proposing to make a decision under section 16 or 17,

and it appears to the[^M_F_33f88975-d193-469d-9ddb-0710db78e5fb] Secretary of State that the Secretary of State[^c-55fd3f05389476e757963ccf39c4b192] does not have sufficient information to enable such a decision to be made[^c-208d9ef621e7aca5714e65c70730cbe8], the Secretary of State[^c-1407b5628ddbd4652eea20049a677430] may make a default maintenance decision.

(2) Where an application for a variation has been made under section 28A(1) in connection with an application for a maintenance calculation [^c-2a617116c05cde2c95d31f70c4fe2e51]... , the Secretary of State[^c-07d65730ea5918378b05786b4cd4ea03] may make an interim maintenance decision.

(3) The amount of child support maintenance fixed by an interim maintenance decision shall be determined in accordance with Part I of Schedule 1.

(4) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision as to default and interim maintenance decisions.

(5) The regulations may, in particular, make provision as to—

- (a) the procedure to be followed in making a default or an interim maintenance decision; and
- (b) a default rate of child support maintenance to apply where a default maintenance decision is made.
