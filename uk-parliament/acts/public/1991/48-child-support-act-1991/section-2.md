# Child Support Act 1991, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (110).** Text as at 2022-03-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1991/48
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Welfare of children: the general principle.[^c21358481]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where, in any case which falls to be dealt with under this Act, the Secretary of State[^c-911006df9f4007d9a292715d65c605bc] [^c-0b966792795620a4b2e05ca9b0415ffb]... is considering the exercise of any discretionary power conferred by this Act, the Secretary of State[^c-adbc49c2dfdcd69c58b658d3e43657fa] shall have regard to the welfare of any child likely to be affected by the[^c-e3e52167662b3118ab1eb7abd499e052] decision.
