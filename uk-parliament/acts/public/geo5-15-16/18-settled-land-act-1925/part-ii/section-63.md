# Settled Land Act 1925, section 63

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo5/15-16/18

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 63. Power to complete predecessor’s contracts.

*Extent: E+W*

A tenant for life may make any disposition which is necessary or proper for giving effect to a contract entered into by a predecessor in title, and which if made by that predecessor would have been valid as against his successors in title.
