# Settled Land Act 1925, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo5/15-16/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 3. Duration of settlements.

*Extent: E+W*

Land which has been subject to a settlement which is a settlement for the purposes of this Act[^c6449971] shall be deemed for the purposes of this Act to remain and be settled land, and the settlement shall be deemed to be a subsisting settlement for the purposes of this Act so long as—

- (a) any limitation, charge, or power of charging under the settlement subsists, or is capable of being exercised; or
- (b) the person who, if of full age, would be entitled as beneficial owner to have that land vested in him for a legal estate is an infant.
