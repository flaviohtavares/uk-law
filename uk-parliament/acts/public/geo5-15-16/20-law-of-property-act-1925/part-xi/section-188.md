# Law of Property Act 1925, section 188

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-04-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo5/15-16/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 188. Power to direct division of chattels.[^c2386101][^c2386091]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c2386111] Where any chattels belong to persons in undivided shares, the persons interested in a moiety or upwards may apply to the court for an order for division of the chattels or any of them, according to a valuation or otherwise, and the court may make such order and give any consequential directions as it thinks fit.

(2)[^c2386121] The county court has jurisdiction under this section where the amount or value of the property or of the interest in the property which is to be dealt with in the court does not exceed £30,000[^c2386131].
