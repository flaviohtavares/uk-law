## 201. Provisions of Act to apply to incorporeal hereditaments.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The provisions of this Act relating to freehold land apply to manors, reputed manors , lordships, advowsons, . . . [^c2387021] perpetual rentcharges, and other incorporeal hereditaments, subject only to the qualifications necessarily arising by reason of the inherent nature of the hereditament affected.

(2) This Act does not affect the special restrictions imposed on dealing with advowsons by the [^c2387031]Benefices Act, 1898, or any other statute or measure, nor affect the limitation of, or authorise any disposition to be made of, a title or dignity of honour which in its nature is inalienable.
