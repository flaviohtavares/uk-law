# Law of Property Act 1925, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-04-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo5/15-16/20

## 39. Transitional provisions in First Schedule.

*Extent: E+W*

For the purpose of effecting the transition from the law existing prior to the commencement of the [^c2382481]Law of Property Act, 1922, to the law enacted by that Act (as amended), the provisions set out in the First Schedule to this Act shall have effect—

(1) for converting existing legal estates, interests and charges not capable under the said Act of taking effect as legal interests into equitable interests;

(2) for discharging, getting in or vesting outstanding legal estates;

(3) for making provision with respect to legal estates vested in infants;

(4) for subjecting land held in undivided shares to trusts[^c2382491];

(5) for dealing with party structures and open spaces held in common;

(6) [^c18367011]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) for converting existing freehold mortgages into mortgages by demise;

(8) for converting existing leasehold mortgages into mortgages by sub-demise.
