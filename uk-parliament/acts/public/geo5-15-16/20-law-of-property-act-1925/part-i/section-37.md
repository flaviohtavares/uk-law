# Law of Property Act 1925, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-04-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo5/15-16/20

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Rights of husband and wife.

*Extent: E+W*

A husband and wife shall, for all purposes of acquisition of any interest in property, under a disposition made or coming into operation after the commencement of this Act, be treated as two persons.
