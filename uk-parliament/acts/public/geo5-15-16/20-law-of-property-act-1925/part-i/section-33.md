# Law of Property Act 1925, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-04-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo5/15-16/20

## 33. Application of Pt. I. to personal representatives.

*Extent: E+W*

The provisions of this Part of this Act relating to trustees of land[^c2382201] apply to personal representatives holding land in trust, but without prejudice to their rights and powers for purposes of administration.
