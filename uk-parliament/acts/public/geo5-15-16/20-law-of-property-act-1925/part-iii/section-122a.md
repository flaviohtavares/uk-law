# Law of Property Act 1925, section 122A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-04-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo5/15-16/20

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 122A. Contrary provision of no effect[^c-ec90e619e8ae021acdba6326a822a09a]

*Extent: E+W*

An instrument creating a rentcharge, or a contract or any other arrangement, (whenever entered into) is of no effect to the extent that it makes provision that is contrary to—

- (a) section 120B, 120C, 121(1A) or 122(1A), or
- (b) regulations under section 120D.
