## 120. Form of discharge of statutory mortgage or charge.

*Extent: E+W*

A statutory mortgage may be surrendered or discharged by a receipt in the form (No. 5) set out in the Fourth Schedule to this Act with such variations and additions, if any, as circumstances may require.
