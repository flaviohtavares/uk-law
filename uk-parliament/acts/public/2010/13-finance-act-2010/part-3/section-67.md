# Finance Act 2010, section 67

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Climate change levy: compatible state aid

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph 42 of Schedule 6 to FA 2000 (amount payable by way of levy), after sub-paragraph (2) insert—

> (3) If a reduced-rate supply is part of an aid scheme within Article 25 of Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 800/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0800), sub-paragraph (4) cites the title and publication reference of that Regulation for the purpose of complying with Article 3(1) of that Regulation.
>
> (4) That citation is Commission Regulation [(EC) No. 800/2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2008/0800) of 6 August 2008 declaring certain categories of aid compatible with the common market in application of Articles 87 and 88 of the Treaty (General block exemption Regulation) (O.J. 2008 No. L214/3) (with the reference to Articles 87 and 88 being read, as a result of the Treaty of Lisbon, as a reference to Articles 107 and 108 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union).
