# Equality Act 2010, section 192

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 192. National security

*Extent: E+W+S*

A person does not contravene this Act only by doing, for the purpose of safeguarding national security, anything it is proportionate to do for that purpose.
