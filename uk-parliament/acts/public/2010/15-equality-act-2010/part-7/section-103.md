# Equality Act 2010, section 103

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 103. Sections 101 and 102: further provision[^c21160451]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A duty to make reasonable adjustments applies to an association.

(2) In the application of section 26 for the purposes of section 101(4) or 102(3), neither of the following is a relevant protected characteristic—

- (a) religion or belief;
- (b) sexual orientation.
