## 40. Employees and applicants: harassment

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An employer (A) must not, in relation to employment by A, harass a person (B)—

- (a) who is an employee of A's;
- (b) who has applied to A for employment.

(1A)[^key-a0932e9760c18890c3471bfd450ea5f2] An employer (A) must not permit a third party to harass a person (B) who is an employee of A.

(1B) For the purposes of subsection (1A), A permits a third party to harass B only if—

- (a) the third party harasses B in the course of B’s employment by A, and
- (b) A failed to take all reasonable steps to prevent the third party from doing so.

(1C) In this section “**third party**” means a person other than—

- (a) A, or
- (b) an employee of A.
