## SCHEDULE 5 — Parliamentary standards: consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 38

### Part 1 — Amendments of the Parliamentary Standards Act 2009

**1.**[^key-7130ed19f483c407b53263f07c652d9f] The Parliamentary Standards Act 2009 is amended as follows.

**2.** In section 2(2) (effect of Act on House of Lords)—

- (a)[^key-9d8dd40ef6636c0a6fc7c94a0df4e982] omit paragraph (a), and
- (b)[^key-d0670a7fda7107d770a180ae846862c2] in paragraph (c) for “paragraphs 4(2) and 8(1)” substitute “ paragraph 8(2) ”.

**3.**[^key-4404b790216706177fca9de5a4b9c252] In section 5(8) (allowances) for the words from “and” to the end substitute “ and in relation to any such allowances, references in this Act to a member of the House of Commons include a former member of that House. ”

**4.** (1)[^key-7b37858e86847b9e003d4611e53eb9e4] Section 12 (interpretation) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a)[^key-7b37858e86847b9e003d4611e53eb9e4] for “ “the Commissioner”” substitute “the Compliance Officer” , and
- (b)[^key-a4a4153ff0191442d684ce353a102047] omit the definition of “the MPs' code of conduct relating to financial interests”.

(3)[^key-fc946e56ddd26587d32737806b3b0803] In subsection (2)—

- (a) after “committee” (in each place) insert “ or officer ”, and
- (b) in paragraph (a) for “its” substitute “ the ”.

**5.** (1)[^key-44af2845d2f3b2eeced603baf76b392f] Section 13 (transitional provision) is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-6c8ad279c86a2b62b66896cc5bfaa1fc] Omit subsection (2)(b) and (c).

(3)[^key-44af2845d2f3b2eeced603baf76b392f] In subsection (4) and (6)(c) for “Commissioner” substitute “ Compliance Officer ”.

**6.**[^key-468e3afee49a5dc818da2648c44ea09e] In section 14 (commencement) omit subsection (2)(d).

**7.** (1)[^key-a751312e195f6936a124b6bd54497a56] Schedule 1 (the IPSA) is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-984aae5667f7acc110c7b6703dbe3d2a] Omit paragraph 10 and the heading above it.

(3) In paragraph 18(1)—

- (a)[^key-a8d51ef6893e020f27fd68492b8c7813] for paragraph (a) substitute—

  > - (a) section 4 (MPs' salaries), so far as relating to the payment (but not the determination) of salaries,

  ,
- (b)[^key-83a1fac90274d3a0a95ea0ac31f5ed61] in paragraph (c), after “claims)” insert “ (except as mentioned in sub-paragraph (2) below) ”, and
- (c)[^key-984aae5667f7acc110c7b6703dbe3d2a] omit the words following paragraph (c).

(4) In paragraph 18(2)—

- (a)[^key-a8d51ef6893e020f27fd68492b8c7813] before paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (za) sections 4 and 4A (MPs' salaries) (except as mentioned in sub-paragraph (1) above),

  ,
- (b)[^key-83a1fac90274d3a0a95ea0ac31f5ed61] after paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (aa) section 6(10) (determining procedures for publication of allowances claims),

  ,
- (c)[^key-984aae5667f7acc110c7b6703dbe3d2a] omit paragraph (b) (and the “and” following it), and
- (d)[^key-fe8f5690e01dd76e8c7b07d634730e03][^key-72fa586a98cab0eab365f86f8eb7d5f4] for paragraph (c) substitute—

  > - (c) section 9(8)(b) and (9) (determining conditions),
  > - (d) section 9A (determining procedures for investigations etc),
  > - (e) paragraphs 1, 2(2), 4(2) and 9(2) of Schedule 2 (appointment and removal of Compliance Officer etc), and
  > - (f) paragraphs 2, 8 and 9 of Schedule 4 (scheme, guidance etc for Compliance Officer).

(5)[^key-83a1fac90274d3a0a95ea0ac31f5ed61] In paragraph 22(8) for “Any repayments” substitute “ Any payments received by the IPSA as a result of a repayment direction under Schedule 4, and any repayments otherwise ”.

### Part 2 — Amendments of other Acts

#### European Parliament (Pay and Pensions) Act 1979 (c. 50)

**8.**[^key-1e6e0df2872fb788426dc716a767d0ff] (1) In section 1(2) (salaries of MEPs) for paragraphs (a) and (b) substitute “ the same as that of the salary payable for that period, under section 4 of the Parliamentary Standards Act 2009, to a Member who does not hold an office or position specified in a resolution of the House of Commons for the purposes of section 4A(2) of that Act (higher salaries for holders of specified offices or positions). ”

(2) Omit section 5 (salary to be used for calculating pension benefits).

(3) In section 8(1) (interpretation) omit the definition of “ “a Member's ordinary salary” and “a Member's pensionable salary””.

#### Parliamentary and other Pensions Act 1987 (c. 45)

**9.**[^key-8a8e01ac4a0b184a12330cd56f95ac26] In section 5(2) (interpretation) for the words from “such resolutions” to the end substitute—

> - (a) section 4 of the Parliamentary Standards Act 2009, or
> - (b) in relation to a time before that section was in force, the resolutions of the House of Commons then in force relating to the remuneration of its members.

#### Ministerial and other Pensions and Salaries Act 1991 (c. 5)

**10.**[^key-8db3b2be27f7f96fd957ae0c797f490e] In section 4 (grants to persons ceasing to hold ministerial and other offices)—

- (a) omit subsection (3),
- (b) after that subsection insert—

  > (3A) The annual amount of the salary paid to a person in respect of the office of Chairman of Ways and Means or Deputy Chairman of Ways and Means is the difference between—
  >
  > - (a) the annual amount of the salary payable under section 4 of the Parliamentary Standards Act 2009 to a person holding that office, and
  > - (b) the annual amount of the salary payable under that section to a member of the House of Commons who does not hold an office or position specified in a resolution of that House for the purposes of section 4A(2) of that Act (higher salaries for holders of specified offices or positions).

  , and
- (c) omit subsection (7).

#### Scotland Act 1998 (c. 46)

**11.**[^key-71e061e2d9380393a97d0cb77319500d] (1) Section 82 (limits on salaries of MSPs) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a) before paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (za) under section 4 of the Parliamentary Standards Act 2009 (salaries of members of the House of Commons),

  , and
- (b) in paragraph (a) for “either House of Parliament” substitute “ the House of Lords ”.

(3) In subsection (2)(b) for “(1)(a)” substitute “ (1)(za), (a) ”.

#### Government of Wales Act 2006 (c. 32)

**12.**[^key-c0d9b945a7f13347a8176db8f6afe379] (1) Section 21 (limits on salaries of Assembly members) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a) before paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (za) under section 4 of the Parliamentary Standards Act 2009 (salaries of members of the House of Commons),

  , and
- (b) in paragraph (a) for “either House of Parliament” substitute “ the House of Lords ”.

(3) In subsection (2)(b) for “(1)(a)” substitute “ (1)(za), (a) ”.
