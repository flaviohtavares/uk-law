# Constitutional Reform and Governance Act 2010, section 30

> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. MPs' allowances scheme[^c-97cbe037dbd230187c2bc86b3c920fc7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 5 of the Parliamentary Standards Act 2009 (MPs' allowances scheme) after subsection (8) insert—

> (8A) Any duty of the IPSA to pay an allowance to a member is subject to anything done in relation to the member in the exercise of the disciplinary powers of the House of Commons.
