# Constitutional Reform and Governance Act 2010, section 46

> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Freedom of information

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-d7f397dc5453705886945f017d1a451f][^c-1c02ff7761d907d78379839498b74f69] Schedule 7 (which makes amendments of the Freedom of Information Act 2000) has effect.

(2)[^c-6304b84232e69df91acb0cfb385f7a83][^c-c054f72870f154c1a7e4904ae4690ae5] The Secretary of State may by order make transitional, transitory or saving provision in connection with the coming into force of paragraph 4 of Schedule 7 (which reduces from 30 years to 20 years the period at the end of which a record becomes a historical record for the purposes of Part 6 of the Freedom of Information Act 2000).

(3)[^c-6304b84232e69df91acb0cfb385f7a83][^c-c054f72870f154c1a7e4904ae4690ae5] An order under subsection (2) may in particular—

- (a) make provision about the time when any records are to become historical records for the purposes of Part 6 of the Freedom of Information Act 2000, and
- (b) make different provision in relation to records of different descriptions.

(4)[^c-6304b84232e69df91acb0cfb385f7a83][^c-c054f72870f154c1a7e4904ae4690ae5] An order under subsection (2) is to be made by statutory instrument.

(5)[^c-6304b84232e69df91acb0cfb385f7a83][^c-c054f72870f154c1a7e4904ae4690ae5] A statutory instrument containing an order under subsection (2) is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
