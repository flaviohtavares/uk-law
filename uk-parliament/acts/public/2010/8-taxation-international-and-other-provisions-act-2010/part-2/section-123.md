# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 123

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123. Interpretation of sections 116 to 122

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In sections 116 to 122 and this section—

- “**company**” means any entity listed as a company in Part A of Annex I[^key-7dec30049cab1a79133915bb71890f44] to the Mergers Directive,
- “**derivative contract**” has the same meaning as in Part 7 of CTA 2009,
- “intangible fixed assets” and “**chargeable intangible assets**”, in relation to any person, have the same meaning as in Part 8 of CTA 2009,
- “**loan relationship**” has the same meaning as in Part 5 of CTA 2009,
- “**the Mergers Directive**” means Council Directive [2009/133/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0133)[^key-97dfa707cfae5724150b29d468a0533d],
- “**proceeds of realisation**”, in relation to intangible fixed assets, has the meaning given in section 739 of CTA 2009, and
- “**recognised for tax purposes**” has the same meaning as in Part 8 of CTA 2009.
