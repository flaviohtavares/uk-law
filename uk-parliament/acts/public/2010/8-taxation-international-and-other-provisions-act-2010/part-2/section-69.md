# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 69

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Dividends paid out of transferred profits

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) a company resident outside the United Kingdom (“company A”) has paid tax under the law of a territory outside the United Kingdom in respect of any of its profits,
- (b) some or all of those profits become profits of another company resident outside the United Kingdom (“company B”) otherwise than as a result of the payment of a dividend to company B, and
- (c) company B pays a dividend out of those profits to another company, wherever resident.

(2) If this section applies, this Part has effect, so far as relating to the determination of underlying tax in relation to any dividend paid—

- (a) by any company resident outside the United Kingdom (whether or not company B),
- (b) to a company resident in the United Kingdom,

as if company B had paid the tax paid by company A in respect of those profits of company A which have become profits of company B as mentioned in subsection (1)(b).

(3) But the amount of relief under this Part which is allowable to a company resident in the United Kingdom is not to exceed the amount which would have been allowable to that company had those profits become profits of company B as a result of the payment of a dividend by company A to company B.
