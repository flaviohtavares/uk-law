# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/8

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Credit not allowed if person elects against credit

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Credit under section 18(2) against income tax, corporation tax or capital gains tax charged on any income or chargeable gains of a person is not allowed if the person elects for credit not to be allowed in respect of that income or those gains.
