# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 34

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Reduction in credit: payment by reference to foreign tax

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsection (2) applies if—

- (a) credit for foreign tax is to be allowed to a person (“P”) under the arrangements, and
- (b)[^key-ad663458ab5da46752ade50cc820774d] a tax authority makes a payment by reference to that tax, and that payment—
  - (i) is made to P or a person connected with P, or
  - (ii) is made to some other person directly or indirectly in consequence of a scheme that has been entered into.

(2) The amount of that credit is to be reduced by an amount equal to that payment.

(3) Whether a person is connected with P is determined in accordance with section 1122 of CTA 2010.

(4)[^key-ad31939d77ca59583adb4b513b391b5c] In subsection (1)(b)(ii) “**scheme**” includes any scheme, arrangement or understanding of any kind, whether or not legally enforceable, involving a single transaction or two or more transactions.
