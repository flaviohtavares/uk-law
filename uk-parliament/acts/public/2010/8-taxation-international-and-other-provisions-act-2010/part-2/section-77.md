# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 77

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 77. Claims for relief under section 73(1)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The excess is to be treated as mentioned in section 73(1) only on a claim.

(2) A claim under subsection (1) must specify—

- (a) the amount (if any) of the excess which is to be treated as mentioned in section 73(1)(a), and
- (b) the amount (if any) of the excess which is to be treated as mentioned in section 73(1)(b).

(3) A claim under subsection (1) must be made not more than—

- (a) 4 years after the end of period A, or
- (b) if later, 1 year after the end of the accounting period in which the foreign tax concerned is paid.
