# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 360

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 360. Treatment of umbrella arrangements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section has effect for the purposes of this Part.

(2) In the case of umbrella arrangements (see section 363)—

- (a) each part of the umbrella arrangements is to be treated as separate arrangements, and
- (b) the umbrella arrangements are to be disregarded.

(3) Subsection (2)(a) is subject to section 361.
