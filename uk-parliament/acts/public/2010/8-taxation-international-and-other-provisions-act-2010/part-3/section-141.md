# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 141

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/8

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 141. Credit under Chapter 2 of Part 2 to be allowed first

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any credit for foreign tax allowed under Chapter 2 of Part 2 against income tax or capital gains tax is to be allowed before effect is given to sections 137 to 140.

(2) In this section “**foreign tax**” has the same meaning as in that Chapter (see section 21).
