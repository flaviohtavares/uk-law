# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 367

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/8

## 367. Leasing arrangements: finance leases and loans

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 3, which inserts—

- (a) a new Part 11A in ITA 2007 (leasing arrangements: finance leases and loans), and
- (b) a new section 37A in TCGA 1992 (consideration on disposal of certain leases),

has effect.
