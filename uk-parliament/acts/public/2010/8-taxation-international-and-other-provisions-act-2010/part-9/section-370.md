# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 370

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 370. UK representatives of non-UK residents

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 6, which inserts—

- (a) new Chapters 2B and 2C in Part 14 of ITA 2007 (income tax: UK representatives of non-UK residents), and
- (b) a new Part 7A in TCGA 1992 (capital gains tax: UK representatives of non-UK residents),

has effect.
