# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 156

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 156. “Losses” and “profits”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part “**losses**” includes amounts which are not losses but in respect of which relief may be given in accordance with the Tax Acts including (for example)[^c-e77bc8168f39d0d801135db28a14b093]—

- (a) section 57 of ITTOIA 2005 (pre-trading expenses),
- (b) section 88 of ITA 2007 (carry forward of certain interest),
- (c) section 61 of CTA 2009 (pre-trading expenses),
- (d) sections 387 to 391 of CTA 2009 (insurance companies: non-trading deficits on loan relationships),
- (e) Chapter 16 or Chapter 16A[^c-6632050e39ea9f49297941a57f5a79d9] of Part 5 of CTA 2009 (non-trading deficits on loan relationships),
- (f) section 1223 of CTA 2009 (excess of management expenses), [^c-89b71708ac4d26a935c7ebf1b303fd54]...
- (g) Part 5 of CTA 2010 (group relief) , or[^c-c985a571134c537cf8ee4ed7776890bc]
- (h) Part 5A of CTA 2010 (group relief for carried-forward losses).

(2) In this Part “**profits**” includes income.
