# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 194

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/8

## 194. Claims under section 192(1): general provisions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A claim under section 192(1) may be made—

- (a) by the guarantor company,
- (b) if there are two or more guarantor companies, by those companies acting together, or
- (c) by the borrower[^key-102e68c9c432dd777d9935cad7878e7f].

(2) A claim made under section 192(1) by the borrower[^key-be8d2dceea75b3436daac512362d35f1] is to be taken to be made on behalf of the guarantor company or companies.

(3) Sections 175 to 177 apply in relation to a claim under section 192(1) made by or on behalf of any person or persons as they apply in relation to a claim under section 174 made by the disadvantaged person, but taking—

- (a) references in sections 176 and 177 to the advantaged person as references to the borrower[^key-e482091a1fa252ba42dd3b1b91dbfbe4], and
- (b) the reference in section 177 to the disadvantaged person as a reference to the guarantor company or companies.
