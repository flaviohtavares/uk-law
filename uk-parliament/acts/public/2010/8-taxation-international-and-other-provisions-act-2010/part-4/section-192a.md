# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 192A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 192A. Provision for cases within Part 6A[^c-fea913129550adae60bc550677d76b9f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsection (2) applies to the extent that—

- (a) there is an amount to be deducted in respect of a payment by the borrower in respect of the borrowing transaction[^c-b3ff2dea2670bac6223b92a8de77b669],
- (b) that amount is required to be reduced (whether or not to nil) under section 147(3) or (5),
- (c) the guarantor company makes a claim under section 192(1) in respect of that reduction, and
- (d) as regards the payment, provision in Part 6A would, but for the reduction, apply in relation to the tax treatment of the borrower[^c-9c72d1898212458f73e83f4683045137] (“the relevant tax treatment”).

(2) The relevant tax treatment is to apply in relation to the guarantor company.
