## 197. Qualifying conditions for purposes of section 198

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Conditions A to F are the qualifying conditions for the purposes of section 198.

(2) Condition A is that one of the affected persons [^c-17cfa069ba778feab6273a2b9281daf4]... has borrowing liabilities[^c-5af52d45c9e79b6213aba244d023814d].

(3) Condition B is that those liabilities are to any extent the subject of a guarantee provided by a company (“the guarantor company”).

(4) Condition C is that, in calculating the profits and losses of the borrower[^c-cb828e4e85036491fc95fcd8b2416572] for tax purposes, the amounts to be deducted in respect of interest or other amounts payable in respect of the borrowing[^c-8990208efcb48f09be1716565a914e09] are required to be reduced (whether or not to nil) under section 147(3) or (5).

(5) Condition D is that that reduction is required because of section section 153A[^c-3c78d6b2fc819fca58f4e70fc287bc8b].

(6) Condition E is that—

- (a) a payment (the “balancing payment”) is made, or
- (b) two or more payments (the “balancing payments”) are made,

by the guarantor company to the borrower[^c-d5ca765446ce53be12cd43431256fdc1].

(7) Condition F is that the sole or main reasons for making that payment or those payments are—

- (a) that section 147(3) or (5) applies because of section section 153A[^c-cb6f6c40a8615a41e9b2f0b1a62a0788], or
- (b) that sections 192 to 194 apply.

(8)[^c-a5ee2eb8ae54777a9bc2a7ca3a4b5005] For the purposes of this Chapter, “borrowing” and “guarantee” have the meanings they have in sections 153A and 153B (see section 154).
