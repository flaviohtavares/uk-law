# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 198

## 198. Balancing payments by guarantor to borrower: no charge to, or relief from, tax[^c-d000e1ffc8e9b183403e84803ee6e45f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If each of the qualifying conditions (see section 197) is met, subsection (2) applies to the balancing payments made by all of the guarantors[^c-06e7e88a7d97ad08e69683a018665cae] if, or so far as, the total amount of those payments does not exceed the total amount of the reductions within section 197(4) that are result of the application of section 153A[^c-1cf02f9fe53cb7acf317207493b3a9e1].

(2) Payments to which this subsection applies—

- (a) are not to be taken into account in calculating for tax purposes[^c-88ee3ec1c4af200e857a2fd2bbdf107a] the profits or losses of the guarantor company or companies or the borrower[^c-6074c1b4045548021d9f41c097114c49], and
- (b) are not for any purpose of the [^c-ba0eb439d168c40f42bdcf1948e08231]... Tax Acts to be regarded as distributions.

(3) In this section, the following expressions have the meaning given by section 197—

- “the balancing payments”,
- “the guarantor company”, and
- [^c-7ce4fff8e022a4d502fa19075ed081a3]...
