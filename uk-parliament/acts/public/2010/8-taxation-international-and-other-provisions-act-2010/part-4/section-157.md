## 157. Direct participation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsection (2) applies for the purposes of—

- (a) this Part (other than for the purposes of section 148A(1)(c))[^c-86a4e286b1225a8d5d0c6aec77dc4d23],
- (b) in Part 2, section 132(7), [^c-59f596665ccd42a22aff1cea66db3903]...
- (c) in Part 5, section 219(2) ,[^c-0225e3f7cb7d8ffcdd20023bf84cff2b] [^c-bbf6034d72784c3fb97a8c029f0253d8]...
- (d) in Part 6A, section 259NB(4) ,[^c-57adf89a5d25c960ad764fb52b27e948] [^c-c207a1c25aa8d7914f072b3edb448cad]... , and[^c-f64e864f1356ae25933a362a69dc0eb1]
- (e) in Part 10, section 463(4)[^c-92060c4c2febe76312e4cb83dae8f6f0]...
- (f)[^c-92060c4c2febe76312e4cb83dae8f6f0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-2e9228f608e049374678508656023325][^c-6ee88a81250de7ce256a83e50cf91d2e][^c-eacaba18186df6a83ecc9e2dc567b148] A person is directly participating in the management, control or capital of another person at a particular time if (and only if) that other person is at that time—

- (a) a body corporate or a firm, and
- (b) controlled by the first person.

(3)[^c-88a9a7b9e3a66b930607ee0ed7c6f17a] See also section 162A, which provides for circumstance in which a person will be regarded as having control of another.
