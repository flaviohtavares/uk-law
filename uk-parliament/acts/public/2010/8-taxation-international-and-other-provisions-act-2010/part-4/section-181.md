# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 181

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 181. Section 182 applies to claims where actual provision relates to borrowing[^c-31f901c778718f0266b2e7b5a3360191]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsection (2) applies if—

- (a)[^c-a6bf6ccf5a71c37f7146721467668e6b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the actual provision is provision in relation to borrowing[^c-e4db87f79a9f705033b2ea62806e720b].

(2) A claim under section 174 may be made in accordance with section 182.

(3) For the purposes of this Part, a “section 182 claim” is a claim under section 174 made in accordance with section 182.

(4)[^c-9623e64c336730383cba2bfc826453d8] For the purposes of this section “**borrowing**” has the meaning it has in sections 153A and 153B (see section 154).
