## 192. Attribution to guarantor company of things done by borrower[^c-312aacf1a10c0f2fdb01143be5d9e91c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) On the making of a claim, the guarantor company is, to the extent of the reduction mentioned in subsection (1)(c) of section 191 (so far as it meets the condition in subsection (1)(d) of that section)[^c-d363b1554f25c266f4879e9362fecb22], to be treated for all purposes of the Taxes Acts as if it (and not the borrower[^c-86bc6ba3a0c3dce8c87932b7ef1820e1])—

- (a)[^c-5d02cd5200b7d69f2728d0bc767816d9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-a97c01a920973d0f3ef0ff9e932cd2ad] was the person that owed the borrowing liabilities under the borrowing transaction, and
- (c) had paid any interest or other amounts paid under it by the borrower[^c-536ae1be36faa60397cba7f1252c96d0].

(2) Subsection (1) is subject to subsection (3).

(3) Where the borrower’s liabilities under the borrowing transaction[^c-e22a9803b500cd08f6f79a70dbfb8d34] are the subject of two or more guarantees (whether or not provided by the same person), TD must not exceed TR, where—

- TD is the total of the amounts brought into account by the guarantor companies because of subsection (1), and
- TR is the total amount of the reductions within section 191(1)(c).

(4) Provision about claims under subsection (1) is made by—

- section 193 (interaction between claims under subsection (1) and claims under section 174), and
- section 194 (general provision about claims under subsection (1)).

(5) In subsection (1) “**the Taxes Acts**” has the meaning given by section 118(1) of TMA 1970.
