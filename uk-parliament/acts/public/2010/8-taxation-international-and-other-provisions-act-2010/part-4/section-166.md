## 166. Exemption for small and medium-sized enterprises[^c-3979b09122da12a1036cf30e789065de]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-c07793c8dc991554264eac7abfcdd829] Section 147(3) and (5) do not apply in calculating for any chargeable period the profits and losses of a potentially advantaged person if that person is a small or medium-sized enterprise for that chargeable period (see section 172).

(2) Exceptions to subsection (1) are provided—

- (a) in the case of a small enterprise, by sections 167 and 167A[^c-2a077440d844b43375da9ba2992a23b1], and
- (b) in the case of a medium-sized enterprise, by sections 167 and 168.
