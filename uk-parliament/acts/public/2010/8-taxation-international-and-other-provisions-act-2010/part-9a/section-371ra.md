# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 371RA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 371RA. Overview of Chapter[^key-629ada2c8a18d82affe2c0e203fa8e18]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Sections 371RB and 371RE set out how to determine for the purposes of this Part if a company is “controlled” by another person or persons.

(2) Sections 371RC and 371RG set[^key-6b45c62544282d94e00d6ddaa5d18708] out certain cases in which a non-UK resident company which would not otherwise be a CFC is to be taken to be a CFC for the purposes of this Part.
