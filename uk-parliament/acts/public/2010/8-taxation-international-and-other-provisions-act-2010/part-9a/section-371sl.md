# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 371SL

## 371SL. Group relief etc[^key-629ada2c8a18d82affe2c0e203fa8e18]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Assume that the CFC is neither a member of a group of companies nor a member of a consortium for the purposes of any provision of the Tax Acts.

(2) Subsection (3) applies if—

- (a) under Part 5 of CTA 2010 (group relief) or Part 5A of that Act (group relief for carried-forward losses)[^key-6fc9c69cf5b16aec29c4628a532e92b0] the CFC actually surrenders any relief which is allowed to another company by way of group relief or group relief for carried-forward losses[^key-935e67dae178ffe21c0a789fec9d3df2], but
- (b) applying the corporation tax assumptions apart from subsection (3), the relief would reduce the CFC's assumed taxable total profits for the relevant accounting period.

(3) Assume that the relief is to be ignored in determining the CFC's assumed taxable total profits for the relevant accounting period.

(4)[^key-22a6d1a30071b6a70517f612cc55e492] This section is subject to section 371SLA (corporate interest restriction).
