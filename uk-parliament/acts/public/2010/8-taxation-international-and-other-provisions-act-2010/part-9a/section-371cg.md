# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 371CG

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 371CG. Does Chapter 8 apply?[^c-629ada2c8a18d82affe2c0e203fa8e18]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Chapter 8 (solo consolidation) applies for a CFC's accounting period if (and only if) condition A or B is met.

(2) Condition A is that, at any time during the accounting period—

- (a) the CFC is a subsidiary undertaking which is the subject of a solo consolidation waiver under section BIPRU 2.1 of the PRA Handbook[^c-f3e9e9d337c34d2ac069a91f10a5aa41] , and
- (b) the CFC's parent undertaking in relation to that waiver is a UK resident company.

(3) Condition B is that, at any time during the accounting period—

- (a) the CFC is controlled (either alone or with other persons) by a UK resident bank which holds shares in the CFC,
- (b) the UK resident bank must meet requirements of the PRA Handbook[^c-0988a2a497254739b413c56ce7974820] in relation to its capital,
- (c) any fall in the value of the shares held in the CFC would be (wholly or mainly) ignored for the purpose of determining if the UK resident bank meets those requirements of the PRA Handbook , and
- (d) the main purpose, or one of the main purposes, of the UK resident bank in holding the shares in the CFC is to obtain a tax advantage for itself or any company connected with it.

(4) In this section—

- “the PRA Handbook” means the Handbook made by the Prudential Regulation Authority under[^c-b59ca720705d605993d0b56f274cb111] FISMA 2000 (as that Handbook has effect from time to time), and
- “**UK resident bank**” means a UK resident person carrying on banking business.

(5) The Treasury may by regulations amend this Chapter or Chapter 8 as they consider appropriate to take account of—

- (a) any changes to the PRA Handbook[^c-d691549d0991a9460b5644009a3f3edb] , or
- (b) any relevant document published by the Financial Conduct Authority or the Prudential Regulation Authority[^c-f9396fbad8cdbb6b6959cf88ab815943] from time to time.

(6) “**Relevant document**” means—

- (a) a document which replaces the PRA Handbook[^c-e03c7e5ddf3d04d722382b7c51286f5a] , or
- (b) a document which changes or replaces a document falling within paragraph (a) or a document which is a relevant document by virtue of this paragraph.
