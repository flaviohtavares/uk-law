# Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010, section 506

## 506. Commencement[^key-e08c22498b9cb18375e5875e84f26769]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act comes into force on 1 April 2010 and has effect—

- (a) for corporation tax purposes, for accounting periods ending on or after that day,
- (b) for income tax and capital gains tax purposes, for the tax year 2010-11 and subsequent tax years, and
- (c) for petroleum revenue tax purposes, for chargeable periods beginning on or after 1 July 2010.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to the following provisions (which therefore come into force on the day on which this Act is passed)—

- (a) section 499[^key-54646150ebcf22bbb4836f3031b53374],
- (b) section 500[^key-84bb3df9f66c74642103196af56d4afc],
- (c) the amendments in TCGA 1992 and ITA 2007 made by Part 13 of Schedule 8,
- (d) section 501[^key-50aae533dfe4e06285f40dfc98bb0caa] so far as relating to those amendments,
- (e)[^key-f8e2f7a58a239c3e2f286ac85662caf0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f)[^key-f8e2f7a58a239c3e2f286ac85662caf0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g) section 502(2) and (3)[^key-a4d2560ffca3bd3a383386a4abe26f40],
- (h) section 505[^key-a3d74211ac493c511d82a164aced9ddd],
- (i) this section, and
- (j) section 507[^key-bf4edf1f3d1681fe61e401de37e7a0e4].
