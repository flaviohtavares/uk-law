## 504. Index of defined expressions[^key-0ce8afaa8279fb75722e514f6ac42086]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 11 (index of defined expressions that apply for purposes of Parts 2 to 10[^key-7f2df48951c94eed54d73710d08fce1a]) has effect.

(2) That Schedule lists the places where some of the expressions used in Parts 2 to 10 are defined or otherwise explained.
