# Flood and Water Management Act 2010, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2025-12-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 25. Money[^c-dd0ec55f512b75e8586ec25e5a5d8fc4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may direct the Environment Agency[^c-36d777cc2b210ae019d9dc0ae55a260e] to pay to or in respect of persons who chair or have chaired Regional Flood and Coastal Committees—

- (a) remuneration,
- (b) allowances,
- (c) sums by way of or in respect of pension, and
- (d) compensation, if the Secretary of State[^c-21cfd4c5b379673cbcdb3c7614d754db] thinks that a person who ceases to chair a Committee should, because of special circumstances, receive compensation.

(2) The Agency[^c-e8a9ce38cd858d8f0bb64c87b6879cf6] may pay allowances to members of Committees.

(3) The Secretary of State[^c-b9339862bd6eefee7bc916397b4f89e8] may determine amounts or maximum amounts to be paid under this section.
