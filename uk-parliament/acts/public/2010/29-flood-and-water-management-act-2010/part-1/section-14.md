# Flood and Water Management Act 2010, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2025-12-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 14. Power to request information[^c-ea8e1d0e2341c8eb12694a5d82cdb6a7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An authority listed in subsection (2) may request a person to provide information in connection with the authority's flood and coastal erosion risk management functions.

(2) The authorities are—

- (a) the Environment Agency, [^c-448e8eea5b53cd6499b4821824de5878]...
- (aa)[^c-cdf2504d2997e831f2c4833db8ec583e] the Natural Resources Body for Wales, and
- (b) lead local flood authorities.

(3) The Welsh Ministers may request a person to provide information in connection with the function under section 8.

(4) Information requested under subsection (1) or (3) must be provided—

- (a) in the form or manner specified in the request, and
- (b) within the period specified in the request.
