# Crime and Security Act 2010, section 22

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Destruction of material taken before commencement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] The Secretary of State must by order make provision for the destruction of— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] fingerprints, samples and impressions of footwear taken prior to the commencement of each of sections 14, 15 and 17 to 21 which would have been destroyed if that section had been in force at the time they were taken, and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] any *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] DNA *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] profile which would been destroyed if that section had been in force at the time the profile was derived. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] If an order under section 113(1) of the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (application of that Act to Armed Forces) makes provision equivalent to sections 64 to 64ZN of that Act as substituted by section 14 above, the Secretary of State must by order make provision for the destruction of— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] fingerprints, samples and impressions of footwear taken prior to the commencement of that order which would have been destroyed if that order had been in force at the time they were taken, and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] any *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] DNA *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] profile which would been destroyed if that order had been in force at the time the profile was derived. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36]

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] In this section— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36]

- “ *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] **DNA *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] profile *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36]** ” means any information derived from a *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] DNA *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] sample; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36]
- “ *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] **DNA *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] sample *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36]** ” means any material that has come from a human body and consists of or includes human cells; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36]
- “ *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] **fingerprints *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36]** ” means a record (in any form and produced by any method) of the skin pattern and other physical characteristics or features of a person's fingers or either of a person's palms. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36]

(4 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] An order under this section must be made by statutory instrument. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36]

(5 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36] A statutory instrument containing an order under this section is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b1de30ddc6775da07a6ead027bbffa36]
