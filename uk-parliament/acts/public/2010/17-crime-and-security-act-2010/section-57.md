# Crime and Security Act 2010, section 57

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2022-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Financial provisions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following are to be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) expenditure incurred by the Secretary of State by virtue of this Act;
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable under any other Act out of money so provided.
