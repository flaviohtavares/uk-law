## 37. Application for variation or discharge of injunction[^key-00b6fc6feb42d7ae19c4cc383d83a70d]

*Extent: E+W*

In the Policing and Crime Act 2009, in section 42 (variation or discharge of injunctions), at the end there is inserted—

> (6) If an application to vary or discharge an injunction under this Part is dismissed, no further application to vary or discharge it may be made by any person without the consent of the court.
