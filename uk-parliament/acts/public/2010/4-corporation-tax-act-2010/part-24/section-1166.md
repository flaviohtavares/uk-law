# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 1166

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1166. Scotland

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the application of the Corporation Tax Acts to Scotland—

- “**assignment**” means assignation,
- “**estate in land**” includes the land,
- “**mortgage**” means—
  - (a) a standard security, or
  - (b) a heritable security, as defined in the Conveyancing (Scotland) Act 1924, but including a security constituted by ex facie absolute disposition or assignation, and
- “**surrender**” includes renunciation.

(2) In the application of the Corporation Tax Acts to Scotland, any reference to property or rights being held on trust or on trusts is a reference to the property or rights being held in trust.
