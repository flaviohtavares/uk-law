# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 1121

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1121. “Company”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the Corporation Tax Acts “**company**” means any body corporate or unincorporated association, but does not include a partnership, a co-ownership scheme (as defined by section 235A of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000),[^c-b24c71b9ef724265640c1a27703e4b90] a local authority or a local authority association.

(2) Subsection (1) needs to be read with section 617 (under which the trustees of an authorised unit trust are treated for certain purposes as a UK resident company).
