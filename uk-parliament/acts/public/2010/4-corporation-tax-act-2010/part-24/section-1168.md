# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 1168

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1168. Payment of dividends

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of the Corporation Tax Acts dividends are to be treated as paid on the date when they become due and payable.

(2) Subsection (1) is subject to any provision to the contrary.
