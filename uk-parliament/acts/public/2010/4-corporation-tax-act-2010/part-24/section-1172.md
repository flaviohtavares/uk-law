# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 1172

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1172. Apportionment to different periods[^key-f4978d6058d0313fd54bd5db95f56034][^key-6fd8cc147333a974a116eaf4eaf6f6dc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any apportionment to different periods which falls to be made under the Corporation Tax Acts is to be made on a time basis according to the respective lengths of the periods.

(2) Subsection (1) is subject to any provision to the contrary.
