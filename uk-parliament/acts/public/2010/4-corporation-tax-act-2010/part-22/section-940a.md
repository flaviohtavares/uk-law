# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 940A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 940A. Overview of Chapter[^key-12f51efafd1459a5dfc85582a17e34f5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter contains rules for cases where a trade is transferred between companies within the charge to tax and certain conditions as to common ownership of the trade are met.

(2) Section 940B[^key-e231265e29b937245a186e849ae1410d] explains when there is a transfer of a trade for the purposes of this Chapter.

(3) Sections 940C[^key-bab3914e2f979689c6019a61e46fb1e8] to 943 contain provision about when this Chapter applies to a transfer of a trade.

(4) Sections 943A[^key-95a00e3dd38f3f012e57b3217329f564] to 950 set out the effects of this Chapter in relation to a transfer to which it applies.

(5) Sections 951 to 953 contain supplementary provision.
