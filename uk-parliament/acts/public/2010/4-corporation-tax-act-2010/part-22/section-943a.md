# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 943A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 943A. Disapplication of section 39[^c-b1c7066c2f20e8ae88c297f157defd71]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If this Chapter applies to a transfer of a trade, section 39 (terminal losses: extension of periods for which relief may be given) does not apply in relation to a claim under section 37 by the predecessor for relief for a loss made in the transferred trade.
