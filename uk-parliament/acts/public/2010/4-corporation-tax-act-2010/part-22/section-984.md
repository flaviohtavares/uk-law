## 984. Local authorities and local authority associations[^key-acadc194b59933183d7d0ce877ee420b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A local authority in the United Kingdom is not liable to corporation tax.

(2) A local authority association in the United Kingdom is not liable to corporation tax.
