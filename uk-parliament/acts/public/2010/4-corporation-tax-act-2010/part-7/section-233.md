# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 233

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 233. Investor must not be accredited

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The investor must not be accredited as a community development finance institution under Chapter 2 of Part 7 of ITA 2007 on the investment date.
