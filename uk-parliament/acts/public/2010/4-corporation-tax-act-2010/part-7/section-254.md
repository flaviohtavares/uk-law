# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 254

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 254. CITR subsequently found not to have been due

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If any CITR has been obtained which is subsequently found not to have been due, the CITR must be withdrawn.
