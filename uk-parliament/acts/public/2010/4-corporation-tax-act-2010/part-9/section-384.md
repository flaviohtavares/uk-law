# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 384

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 384. Amount of income and expense

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The amount of the income under section 383 is calculated in accordance with sections 399 to 407.

(2) The amount of the expense under section 383 is the same as the amount of the income.
