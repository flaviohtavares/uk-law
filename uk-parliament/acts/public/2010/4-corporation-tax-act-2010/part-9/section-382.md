# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 382

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 382. Introduction to Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies if there is a relevant change in the relationship between[^key-5c4cff83e7c4fd15bb39da2e4ff27347] a company carrying on a business of leasing plant or machinery otherwise than in partnership with other persons and a principal company of the company.[^key-1a44cdbedf965bc62a5ba3dc2067f1b6].

(2) For the meaning of “business of leasing plant or machinery”, see sections 387 to 391.

(3) For the meaning of “relevant change in the relationship between a company and a principal company of the company”, see sections 392 to 394.[^key-10689d8001cfccee9c495be5cfab2d79]

(4) As to cases where there is a qualifying change of ownership in relation to a company carrying on a business of leasing plant or machinery in partnership with other persons, see Chapter 4.
