# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 392

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 392. “Relevant change in relationship”[^key-5d4d0999875a1baeb96c06c0dde017f1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of the sales of lessors Chapters there is a relevant change in the relationship between a company (“A”) and a principal company of A on any day in any of the circumstances in section 393 or 394 (qualifying 75% subsidiaries and consortium relationships) or section 394ZA (company joining tonnage tax group)[^key-2f4badb1365851d1c66b4cbb4eb1a570].
