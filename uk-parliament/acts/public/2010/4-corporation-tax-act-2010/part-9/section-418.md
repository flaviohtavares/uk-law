# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 418

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 418. Amount of income and expense

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The amount of the income under section 417 is calculated in accordance with sections 421 to 423.

(2) The amount of the expense of the other company under section 417 is calculated in accordance with section 424.
