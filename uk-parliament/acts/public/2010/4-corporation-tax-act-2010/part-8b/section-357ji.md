## 357JI. Transfers of trade without a change of ownership[^key-daff5b22d7ee6f395950067d2a1daa9d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where—

- (a) Chapter 1 of Part 22 (transfers of trade without a change of ownership) applies to the transfer of a trade, and
- (b) a loss made by the predecessor in the transferred trade is a Northern Ireland loss or a mainstream loss.

(2) Sections 943A to 944C (which modify the application of Chapter 2 of Part 4) have effect as if the references in those sections[^key-b913456e4f163f5a207ff6b470579d76] to a loss made by the predecessor in the transferred trade were to the Northern Ireland loss or mainstream loss.
