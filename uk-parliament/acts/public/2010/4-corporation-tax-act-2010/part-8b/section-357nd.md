# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 357ND

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 357ND. Transactions treated as being on arm's length terms[^key-daff5b22d7ee6f395950067d2a1daa9d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In accordance with the separate enterprise principle, transactions between the company's NIRE and any other part of the company are treated as taking place on such terms as would have been agreed between parties dealing at arm's length.
