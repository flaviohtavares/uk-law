# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 357PA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 357PA. R&D expenditure credit ...[^key-daff5b22d7ee6f395950067d2a1daa9d][^key-bd7937048516a4049122da493f4c04fd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where—

- (a) a company is entitled to an R&D expenditure credit under Chapter 1A of Part 13[^key-ddb071fdb7a4747ee5ee8ff2a873df4e] of CTA 2009 (R&D expenditure credits) for an accounting period in relation to a qualifying trade, and
- (b) the company is a Northern Ireland company in the period.

(2) The R&D expenditure credit forms part of the mainstream profits or mainstream losses of the trade.
