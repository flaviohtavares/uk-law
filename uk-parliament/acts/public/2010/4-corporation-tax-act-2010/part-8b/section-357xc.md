# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 357XC

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 357XC. Investment management[^c-daff5b22d7ee6f395950067d2a1daa9d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A trade is an “excluded trade” if it consists of or includes portfolio management, or risk management, in relation to—

- (a) a UCITS, or
- (b) an AIF.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a)[^c-2092ccfd0c6e72f306dc348f8903960a] “UCITS” means—
  - (i) a UCITS within the meaning given by section 236A of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000; or
  - (ii) an undertaking established in Gibraltar which is a UCITS under the law of Gibraltar which implemented [Directive 2009/65/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/0065) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 on the coordination of laws, regulations and administrative provisions relating to undertakings for collective investment in transferable securities;
- (b) “**AIF**” has the meaning given in regulation 3 of the Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013 (S.I. 2013/1773).
