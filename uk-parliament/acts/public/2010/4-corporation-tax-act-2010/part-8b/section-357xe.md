## 357XE. Re-insurance trade[^key-daff5b22d7ee6f395950067d2a1daa9d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A trade is an “excluded trade” if it consists of re-insurance.

(2) In this Part “**re-insurance**” includes retrocession.
