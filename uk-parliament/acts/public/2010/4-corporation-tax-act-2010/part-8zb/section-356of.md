# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 356OF

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 356OF. Profits and losses[^c-69fcd32a640eaffd104ef40191587e9e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Sections 356OB to 356OE have effect as if they included provision about losses corresponding to the provision they make about profits and gains.

(2) Accordingly, in the following sections of this Part references to a “profit or gain” include a loss.
