## 202. “Charity”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter “**charity**” includes[^key-cfd80af58165076a59eea8dd61aebd09]—

- (a)[^key-c80c150146a08d3568bd817725d310c8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (aa)[^key-3e1c4e89719fceb27b68b4138a25c920] a registered club,
- (b) a scientific research association (as defined in section 469),
- (c) the Trustees of the National Heritage Memorial Fund, or[^key-e61dc3168c881f32272a343cd9fa829b]
- (d) the Historic Buildings and Monuments Commission for England, [^key-d99108ef77be8847a0841fedeabcb714]...
