# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 269ZFA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 269ZFA. “Relevant profits”[^key-b4e0a25bb7e034c68e81bff9d2692e5e][^key-df4edca86bcded2474d7f6ddaaeaa41e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A company’s “relevant profits” for an accounting period are—

- (a) the company’s qualifying profits for the accounting period, less
- (b) the company’s deductions allowance for the accounting period (see section 269ZDA[^key-3ad1f39f2a6ad020d942b5d6c883393a]).

But if the allowance mentioned in paragraph (b) exceeds the profits mentioned in paragraph (a), the company’s “relevant profits” for the accounting period are nil.[^M_F_cb6a5552-2c8d-4de5-cff6-b287cdb31e84]

(2) A company’s “qualifying profits” for an accounting period are—

- (a) the amount given by paragraph (1) of step 1 in section 269ZF(3) in determining the company’s modified total profits[^key-305d5592ce884d00141b904e6722d93a] for the accounting period, less
- (b) the amount given by paragraph (1) of step 2 in section 269ZF(3) which could be relieved against[^key-27f7e9a94108b033f09278296e19435c] those profits for the accounting period.
