# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 269ZZ

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 269ZZ. Company tax return to specify amount of deductions allowance[^key-df4edca86bcded2474d7f6ddaaeaa41e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A company's tax return for an accounting period must specify—

- (a) the amount of the company's deductions allowance for the period,
- (aa)[^key-2c25b371623928a561d0540dcca78e4c] if section 269ZWA (increase of deductions allowance for insolvent companies) applies, what that amount would be without the increase provided for by subsection (2) of that section, and
- (b) if section 269ZX (increase of deductions allowance in connection with onerous or impaired leases[^key-91a3aac86617a7784dbd5d6dc0d4885e]) applies, what that amount would be without the increase provided for by subsection (3) of that section.

(2) But subsection (1) applies only if the company makes for the accounting period a deduction to which section 269ZB(2), 269ZBA(2),[^key-c90cf31339eef1c784ca0655bbb434b0] 269ZC(2), 269ZD(2) or 269ZFC(2)[^key-8ddee850cc6bd8e31467e8ce0ce4d971] applies.
