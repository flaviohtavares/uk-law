# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 269ZM

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4

## 269ZM. Meaning of “solvency shock period”[^key-df4edca86bcded2474d7f6ddaaeaa41e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A period of 12 months is a “**solvency shock period**” in relation to an insurance company if the company has a solvency loss for that period (see section 269ZO) which exceeds the company's shock loss threshold for that period (see section 269ZN).
