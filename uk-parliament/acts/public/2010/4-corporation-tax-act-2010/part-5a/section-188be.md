# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 188BE

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 188BE. Restriction where surrendering company could use losses etc itself[^c-35786d3a1110fc6273ed33554ed64ed1][^c-5f4b4ffe3f8c46c0f1df33d946de3a27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The surrendering company may not surrender under this Chapter any loss or other amount carried forward to the surrender period to the extent that the loss or other amount could be deducted from the total profits of the company for the period at Step 2 of section 4(2).
