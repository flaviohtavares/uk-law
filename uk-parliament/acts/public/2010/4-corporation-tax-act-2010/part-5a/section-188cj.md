# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 188CJ

## 188CJ. Meaning of “UK related” company[^key-5f4b4ffe3f8c46c0f1df33d946de3a27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purpose of sections 188CE to 188CI a company is UK related if—

- (a) it is a UK resident company, or
- (b) it is a non-UK resident company within the charge to corporation tax[^key-2e96fb4bb16f9fe97adad9db49b57b00].
