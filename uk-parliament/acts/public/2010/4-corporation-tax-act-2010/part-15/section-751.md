# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 751

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 751. Interpretation of Part

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-ebf21d3825a71b249cd1a958acf2853d] In this Part—

- “**company**” includes any body corporate,
- “**dividends**” includes references to other [^key-1fb6173fdd367b0cba0b663b599cd3d0]... distributions and to interest,
- “securities”—
  - (a) includes shares and stock, and
  - (b) in relation to a company not limited by shares (whether or not it has a share capital) also includes a reference to the interest of a member of the company as such, whatever the form of that interest,
- “**trading stock**” has the meaning given by section 163 of CTA 2009, and
- “**transaction in securities**” means transactions, of whatever description, relating to securities, and in particular—
  - (a) the purchase, sale or exchange of securities,
  - (b) issuing or securing the issue of new securities,
  - (c) applying or subscribing for new securities, and
  - (d) altering or securing the alteration of the rights attached to securities.

(2)[^key-9ecfa97132904d9c006d1ab823624bd8] In the definition of “dividends” given by subsection (1), “**other distributions**” does not include a distribution which is a distribution for the purposes of the Corporation Tax Acts only because it falls within paragraph C or D in section 1000(1) (redeemable share capital or security issued as bonus in respect of shares in, or securities of, the company).
