# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 731

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 731. Overview of Part

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part makes provision for counteracting corporation tax advantages obtained or obtainable by companies to which section 733 applies in respect of a transaction or transactions in securities.

(2) See section 746 (counteraction notices) for the way in which the corporation tax advantages may be counteracted.
