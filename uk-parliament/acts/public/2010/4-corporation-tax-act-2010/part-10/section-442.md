# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 442

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 442. Particular types of company

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A company is not to be treated as a close company if—

- (a)[^c-6af6eeef0ba0aa5511280f4ba6b28e77] it is non-UK resident,
- (b) it is a registered society[^c-fd736539906f7078d5ee647f5d1fe68c] , or
- (c) it is a building society.
