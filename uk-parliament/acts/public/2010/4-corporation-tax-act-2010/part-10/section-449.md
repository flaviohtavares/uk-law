## 449. “Associated company”[^key-988e90ec235b4d1e8df0d6932dffbfd1][^key-eec46338c649338ca861f06e4281956d][^key-d1878f5e6c4adffaccc6b33681f2f74a][^key-ca577b143bf6f7a39e8d76086441b6e6][^key-988e90ec235b4d1e8df0d6932dffbfd1][^key-eec46338c649338ca861f06e4281956d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Part, a company is another's “associated company” at a particular time if, at that time or at any other time within the preceding 12 months—

- (a) one of them has control of the other, or
- (b) both are under the control of the same person or persons.
