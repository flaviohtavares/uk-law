# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 269CB

## 269CB. Restriction on deductions for non-trading deficits from loan relationships[^key-bb25037da6ed84a2d6df1957eda005ec]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section has effect for determining the taxable total profits of a banking company for an accounting period.

(2) Any deduction made by the company for the accounting period in respect of a pre-2015 carried-forward non-trading deficit may not exceed 25%[^key-4955d165647e8a48b3f88125dc0afdd7] of the company's total relevant non-trading profits[^key-723e2a4f7573103d6fb604d55deb44cc] for the accounting period.

Section 269ZF[^key-af453fed7ded0cf63318deacfc25fc36] contains provision for calculating a company's total relevant non-trading profits for an accounting period (see subsection (2B)[^key-d04fa2a8187af8b8eec59c3ab35b697c] of that section).

(3) But subsection (2) does not apply in relation to a banking company for an accounting period where, in determining the company's[^key-6469daf391c4479da021404f4c05eac8] total relevant non-trading profits[^key-eee58082ea74a97ece9e3fc2e6ea22c3] for the period, the amount given by step 1 in section 269ZF(3) is not greater than nil.

(4) In this Chapter “**pre-2015 carried-forward non-trading deficit**”, in relation to a company and an accounting period (“the current accounting period”), means a non-trading deficit—

- (a) which the company had from its loan relationships under section 301(6) of CTA 2009 for an accounting period ending before 1 April 2015, and
- (b) which is carried forward under section 457 of that Act (carry forward of deficits to accounting periods after deficit period) to be set off against non-trading profits of the current accounting period.

(5) In subsection (4) “**non-trading profits**” has the same meaning as in section 457 of CTA 2009.

(6) See also sections 269CE to 269CH (losses to which restrictions do not apply).
