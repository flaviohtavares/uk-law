## 1100. Certain distributions: right to request a statement[^key-7ea0d7a68359ccc4d39f166dc4bd6efe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a company makes a distribution to which this section applies[^key-e75f35235121b2e270685f2929a5ca7c], the recipient is entitled to ask the company to provide a statement in writing showing—

- (a) the amount or value of the distribution, [^key-ee5cf9b6e668cbdb5e0dc75dfccbe584]...
- (b)[^key-ee5cf9b6e668cbdb5e0dc75dfccbe584] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-13b68decdb7cab17d9035b7cd4eb5be6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The request must be in writing.

(4) The company which makes the distribution has a duty to comply with a request under subsection (1), and that duty is enforceable by the recipient.

(4A)[^key-b5ec52ffbdee207292d5640879334d25] This section applies to any distribution other than one which is a distribution for the purposes of the Corporation Tax Acts only because it falls within paragraph C or D in section 1000(1) (redeemable share capital or security issued as bonus in respect of shares in, or securities of, the company).

(5)[^key-cbf290cc9e6d95d4676e35cd320c421b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) This section does not affect the operation of section 1104 (duty to provide tax certificates).

(7)[^key-57e9825821a18528cb7d6246f384a115] This section , and sections 1101 to 1103, need[^key-da11cfaa6e53cd7a23848be9db18fcc9] to be read with section 396A(2) of ITTOIA 2005 (which treats certain receipts as distributions to which this section applies[^key-f91bd25bfdbd55900fac90116ebf52f6] ).
