## 1034. Requirements as to residence

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The seller must be resident [^c-6c0e0290c522666805024d315c6cf936]... in the United Kingdom in the tax year in which the purchase is made.

(2) If the shares are held through a nominee, the nominee must also be resident [^c-6c0e0290c522666805024d315c6cf936]... in the United Kingdom in the tax year in which the purchase is made.

(3) The residence [^c-3550519452e38169ceda1d439134200b]... of personal representatives are taken for the purposes of this section to be the same as the deceased person's residence [^c-3550519452e38169ceda1d439134200b]... immediately before that person's death.
