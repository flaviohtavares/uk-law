# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 1056

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1056. Dividend or bonus relating to transactions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) a dividend or bonus is granted by a registered society[^key-fd736539906f7078d5ee647f5d1fe68c] , and
- (b) section 132 of CTA 2009 (dividends etc relating to transactions with a registered society[^key-2f547a4548d42f44d33c5f4bdac3c781] ) allows the sum representing the dividend or bonus to be deducted in calculating the profits of a trade.

(2) The dividend, or the bonus, is not a distribution for the purposes of the Corporation Tax Acts.
