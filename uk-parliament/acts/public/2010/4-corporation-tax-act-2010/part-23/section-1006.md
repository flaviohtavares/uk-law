# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 1006

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1006. Distributions exceeding consideration received for issue of security

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No amount is to be regarded for the purposes of paragraph E in section 1000(1) as representing the principal secured by a security so far as it exceeds any new consideration which has been received by the company for the issue of the security.
