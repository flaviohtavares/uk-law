## 1068. Meaning of “participator” in sections 1064 to 1067

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In sections 1064 to 1067 “**participator**” has the same meaning as in Part 10 (see section 454).

(2) Section 1069 extends the meaning given by subsection (1).
