# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 1116

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1116. References to married persons, or civil partners, living together

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Individuals who are married to, or are civil partners of, each other are treated for the purposes of this Part as living together unless—

- (a) they are separated under an order of a court of competent jurisdiction,
- (b) they are separated by a deed of separation, or
- (c) they are in fact separated in circumstances in which the separation is likely to be permanent.
