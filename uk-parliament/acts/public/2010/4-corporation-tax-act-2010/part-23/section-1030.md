# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 1030

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1030. Distribution in respect of share capital in a winding up

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A distribution made in respect of share capital in a winding up is not a distribution of a company for the purposes of the Corporation Tax Acts.
