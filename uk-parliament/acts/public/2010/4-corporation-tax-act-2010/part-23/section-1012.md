# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 1012

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1012. Hedging arrangements

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 1008 does not at a given time apply in relation to a security issued by a company (“the issuing company”) if—

- (a) at that time, or
- (b) at any earlier time after 16 April 2002,

there are or have been any hedging arrangements that relate to some or all of the company's liabilities under the security.

This is subject to section 1013.

(2) If, as a result of this section, section 1008 stops applying at any time in relation to a security, paragraph E in paragraph 1000(1) has effect in relation to the security from that time as it would have had effect if section 1008 had never applied in relation to the security.
