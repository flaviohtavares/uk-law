# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 854

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 854. New lease treated as ending

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Sections 855 to 857 treat the new lease as ending in certain circumstances for the purposes of this Chapter.

(2) If any of those provisions apply in a given case, and the new lease is treated as ending on different dates, it must be treated as ending on the earlier or earliest of them.
