# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 863

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 863. Overview of Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter provides that, in certain circumstances where a payment is made under a lease of a trading asset, corporation tax relief for the payment is restricted.
