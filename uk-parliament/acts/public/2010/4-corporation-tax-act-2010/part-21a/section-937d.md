# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 937D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4

## 937D. Meaning of “the scheme rate, index or value”[^key-3019443254d7be55117d27a28013ee4a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part “ **the scheme rate, index or value** ”, in relation to a risk transfer scheme, means the rate, index or value mentioned in section 937C(3)(a), (b) or (c) in relation to the relevant risk for the scheme.
