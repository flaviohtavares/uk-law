# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 634

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 634. Overview of Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter provides for the receipts of certain types of company being wound up to be charged to corporation tax.

(2) For provision charging the receipts of such companies to income tax, see Chapter 3A of Part 14 of ITA 2007.
