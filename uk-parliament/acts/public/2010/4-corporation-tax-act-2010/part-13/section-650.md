# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 650

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4

## 650. Meaning of “self-build society”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsections (2) and (3) give the meaning of “self-build society” in this Chapter.

(2) In England, Scotland and Wales “**self-build society**” has the same meaning as in the Housing Associations Act 1985 (see section 1(3) of that Act).

(3) In Northern Ireland “**self-build society**” has the same meaning as in Part 2 of the Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1992 (S.I. 1725 (N.I. 15)) (see Article 3 of that Order).
