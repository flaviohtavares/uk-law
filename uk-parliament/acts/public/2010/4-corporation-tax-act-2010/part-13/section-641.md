# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 641

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 641. Interpretation of Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies for the purposes of this Chapter.

(2) Winding up proceedings start against a company at the time when the petition for its winding up by the court is presented.

(3) There is the permanent cessation of a company's trade if—

- (a) the company ceases to carry on the trade, or
- (b) the company ceases to be within the charge to corporation tax in respect of the trade,

whether or not the trade is in fact ceased.

(4) A company is insolvent at any time if at that time—

- (a) it is unable to pay its debts as they fall due, or
- (b) the value of its assets is less than the amount of its liabilities (including its contingent and prospective liabilities).

(5) “**Company**” means—

- (a) a company as defined in section 1(1) of the Companies Act 2006, or
- (b) an unregistered company as defined in section 220 of the Insolvency Act 1986 or Article 184 of the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 (S.I. 1989/2405 (N.I.19)).

(6) For the meaning of “deposit-taking trade” and “winding up receipt”, see sections 635(3) and 636(3) respectively.
