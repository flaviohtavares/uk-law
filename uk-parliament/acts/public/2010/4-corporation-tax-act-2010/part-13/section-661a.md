## 661A. The location condition[^c-4f2b16601c1c7d880ff64c6403b6e292]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A club meets the location condition for the purposes of section 658 if—

- (a) it is established in the United Kingdom[^c-5cba7acf38bc6c0d1ed29a25a95cc641] [^c-0fa4b2517300eec49df8a17ce3db8963]..., and
- (b) the facilities that it provides for eligible sports are all located in the United Kingdom[^c-a9674015c9806820f6390f1da463388e] [^c-5e7d022b95bf51c0d7ceabc58be93081]....

(2)[^c-e2a46346f04fbb0c6dc1b40b2c2e04d3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
