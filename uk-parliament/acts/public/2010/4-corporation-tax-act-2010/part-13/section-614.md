# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 614

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 614. Applicable corporation tax rate[^c-8ccf4eefd731296caf92b5a6d68a953c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The rate of corporation tax in relation to an open-ended investment company for any financial year is the rate at which income tax at the basic rate is charged for the tax year beginning on 6 April in that financial year [^c-818f5ec42ad5a1baf2c90fe62d240f24]... (and sections 18A and 18B (relief for companies with small profits) do not apply)[^c-430c14f858313df2f25b5cb44fbd767b].
