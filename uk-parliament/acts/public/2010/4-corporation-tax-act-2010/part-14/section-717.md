# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 717

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 717. Effect of payment in pursuance of assessment under section 710 or 713

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A payment—

- (a) in pursuance of an assessment under section 710 or 713 (recovery of unpaid corporation tax), or
- (b) of interest under section 87A of TMA 1970 (as that section has effect in accordance with section 716),

is not allowed as a deduction in calculating income, profits or losses for any tax purposes.

(2) A person who makes such a payment may recover an amount equal to the payment—

- (a) in the case of an assessment under section 710 (recovery of tax for accounting period beginning before change), from X, or
- (b) in the case of an assessment under section 713 (recovery of tax for accounting period ending on or after change), from Y or the associated company, depending on which is the company whose unpaid tax gave rise to the assessment.
