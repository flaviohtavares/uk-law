# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 726

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 726. Interpretation of Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- “**ownership**” means beneficial ownership (and references to acquisition and shareholder[^c-a82cc30cb9311cd2a7dda8799449a547] are construed accordingly), and
- “**shares**” includes stock.
