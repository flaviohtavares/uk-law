# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 673

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 673. Introduction to Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies if—

- (a) there is a change in the ownership of a company (“**the company**”), and
- (b) condition A or B is met.

(2) Condition A is that within any period beginning no more than 3 years before the change in ownership occurs which is a period of 5 years in which that change[^c-6955e71ca7f2800cf00893e50c549ec4] occurs there is a major change in the nature or conduct of a trade carried on by the company.

(3) Condition B is that the change in ownership occurs at any time after the scale of the activities in a trade carried on by the company has become small or negligible and before any significant revival of the trade.

(4) In this section “**major change in the nature or conduct of a trade**” includes—

- (a) a major change in the type of property dealt in, or services or facilities provided in, the trade, or
- (b) a major change in customers, outlets or markets of the trade.

This Chapter applies even if the change is the result of a gradual process which began before the period of 5[^c-e3989a99443f3c0895e99ad53127f3d3] years mentioned in subsection (2).

(5) In this Chapter—

- “**the change in ownership**” means the change in ownership mentioned in subsection (1),
- “**the company**” has the same meaning as in this section, and
- “**trade**” includes an office.
