## 938V. Priority[^c-c4d280f057cb36399bbc963345936e18]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Part the following provisions are to be treated as of no effect—

- (a) section 441 of CTA 2009 (loan relationships for unallowable purposes);
- (b) section 690 of that Act (derivative contracts for unallowable purposes);
- (c)[^c-947d98b6a3e1e1933e3269f1db9c6778] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (ca)[^c-96d48bc8ffdd479939b157827f03a3f3] Part 6A of TIOPA 2010 (hybrid and other mismatches);
- (d)[^c-a8a7de12e8ff39b73ec102e9f08eeeae] Part 10 of that Act (corporate interest restriction).
