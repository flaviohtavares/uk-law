# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 938N

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4

## 938N. Priority[^c-8f8689b5248909840ed12e396772012c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Part the following provisions are to be treated as of no effect—

- (a) section 441 of CTA 2009 (loan relationships for unallowable purposes);
- (b) section 690 of that Act (derivative contracts for unallowable purposes);
- (c) Part 4 of TIOPA 2010 (transfer pricing);
- (d)[^c-ade14129220daab659c264ed710469a3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (da)[^c-e7a3973036f542da7fc70a2fd79e738d] Part 6A of that Act (hybrid and other mismatches);
- (e)[^c-eeb1848169180b2b7c3a220b063f4607] Part 10 of that Act (corporate interest restriction).
