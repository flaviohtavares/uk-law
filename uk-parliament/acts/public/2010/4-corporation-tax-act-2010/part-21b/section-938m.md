# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 938M

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 938M. Controlled foreign companies[^c-8f8689b5248909840ed12e396772012c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 371SL(1) of TIOPA 2010 (assumption that a CFC[^c-8b192734e5170a5128d9e952b6501936] is not a member of any group for the purposes of any provision of the Tax Acts) does not apply for the purposes of this Part.

(2) References in this Part to a company bringing amounts into account, or not bringing them into account, as debits or credits for the purposes of Part 5 or 7 of CTA 2009 include bringing amounts into account, or not bringing them into account, as debits or credits under that Part in determining the assumed taxable total profits[^c-5d853f6c50e2ad91c1c1b6a507583bd1] of the company (or in determining that there were no such profits) for the purposes of Part 9A of TIOPA 2010[^c-9fabb75b6f0a7b95884e52fcb3737c9d] (controlled foreign companies).
