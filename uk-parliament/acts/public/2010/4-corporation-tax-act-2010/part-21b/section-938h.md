> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4

## 938H. Meaning of “scheme”[^c-8f8689b5248909840ed12e396772012c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part “ **scheme** ” includes any scheme, arrangements or understanding of any kind whatever, whether or not legally enforceable, involving a single transaction or two or more transactions.
