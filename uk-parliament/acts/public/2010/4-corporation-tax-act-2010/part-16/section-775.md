## 775. Arrangements[^c-a98cfeb4eea0df8a7760be3617edb4d0][^c-e0b8803a04e8f90e50976e5c46bc3362][^c-ec27c36beca9ddf1cc9c524d61d7ec9d][^c-b92ab946325e12c58371924c88513cd0][^c-a98cfeb4eea0df8a7760be3617edb4d0][^c-b92ab946325e12c58371924c88513cd0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A reference in this Chapter to an arrangement includes a reference to an agreement or understanding (whether or not legally enforceable).
