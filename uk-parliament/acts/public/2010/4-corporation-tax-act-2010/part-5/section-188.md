# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 188

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 188. Other definitions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part—

- “**the claimant company** ” has the meaning given by section 130(2) [^c-379247fa4cf604ad8da9b13e803a503e]...,
- “**the claim period**” has the meaning given by section 130(2) [^c-217fb662c2e314968a72395e9274aac2]...,
- “**company**” means any body corporate(except in[^c-07d70d3bb4b4232b5554330baffaa9e7] sections 156(2A) and 169(3)[^c-6130294260b1016d7d13f8d6ca5ef462] ,
- “**group relief**” has the meaning given by section 97(2),
- “**profits**” means income and chargeable gains, except in so far as the context otherwise requires,
- “**the surrenderable amounts**” has the meaning given by section 99(7) [^c-b6ba3f8f7c9380736265c108a704ce59]...,
- “**surrendering company**” has the meaning given by section 99(7) [^c-67fe565ebf9825faedbccb6430efc3e4]...,
- “**the surrender period**” has the meaning given by section 99(7) [^c-389282c50687c7dd436cba0d33fff64a]....

(2) In this Part, except in so far as the context otherwise requires—

- (a) references to a trade include an office, and
- (b) references to carrying on a trade include holding an office.
