# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 138

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 138. Limitation on amount of group relief applying to all claims[^c-11426c16db1c35ae59b1ad3091af5dc4][^c-a46d4907a422975fcbcab9405dd44215][^c-ccc482a714a684685dc8f48c4f3321b4][^c-a46d4907a422975fcbcab9405dd44215]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The amount of group relief to be given on a claim (“the current claim”) is limited to—

- (a) the unused part of the surrenderable amounts (see section 139), or
- (b) if less, the unrelieved part of the claimant company's available total profits of the claim period (see section 140).
