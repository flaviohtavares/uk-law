# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 184

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 184. References to “allowance” in CAA 2001

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

References in CAA 2001 (apart from Parts 6 and 10) to an allowance include references to an allowance which would be made—

- (a) but for the giving of group relief, or
- (b) but for that and for a lack of profits or other income.
