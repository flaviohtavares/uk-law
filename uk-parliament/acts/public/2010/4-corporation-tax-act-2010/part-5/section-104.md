# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 104

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 104. Meaning of “non-trading loss on intangible fixed assets”[^c-4f0d062d21b1b2f2823de23c251955fe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 99(1)(g) “**non-trading loss on intangible fixed assets**” is to be read in accordance with Part 8 of CTA 2009.

(2)[^c-e793a6b03856da1925a7e1ba939438a5] But it does not include a loss treated as a non-trading loss on intangible fixed assets for the surrender period as a result of section 753(3) of CTA 2009.
