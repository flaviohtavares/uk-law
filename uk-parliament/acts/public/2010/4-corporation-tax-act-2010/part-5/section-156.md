# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 156

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 156. Sections 154 and 155: supplementary[^key-4ac1810bf5c49b39b7b42c4d5aa28736][^key-344b18b92b3dcf8b2095c1b7f565e0f6][^key-a46d4907a422975fcbcab9405dd44215][^key-f86fc4de220dd12f31ea4d159ffaf303][^key-a46d4907a422975fcbcab9405dd44215]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies for the purposes of sections 154 to 155B[^key-170046215bccdd1920dba1f5b58cf76f].

(2) “Arrangements”—

- (a) means arrangements of any kind (whether or not in writing), but
- (b) does not include —[^key-f6bc349412cdf651245960940adc913c]
  - (i) a power of a Minister of the Crown, the Scottish Ministers or a Northern Ireland department to give directions to a statutory body as to the disposal of assets belonging to the body or to a subsidiary of the body., or[^key-c9c78945fcdd36ed7e3fd2dfd34c2de5]
  - (ii) a condition or requirement imposed by, or agreed with, a Minister of the Crown, the Scottish Ministers, a Northern Ireland department or a statutory body.

(2A)[^key-74f68be5bcf638d5169cb751f42755f3] In subsection (2) “**statutory body**” means a body (other than a company as defined by section 1(1) of the Companies Act 2006) established by or under a statutory provision for the purpose of carrying out functions conferred on it by or under a statutory provision, except that the Treasury may, by order, specify that a body is or is not to be a statutory body for this purpose.

(3) A company is the successor of another company if it carries on a trade which, in whole or in part, the other company used to carry on and the circumstances are such that—

- (a) Chapter 1 of Part 22 (transfers of trade without a change of ownership) applies in relation to the companies as, respectively, the successor and the predecessor within the meaning of that Chapter, or
- (b) the two companies are connected with each other in accordance with section 1122.
