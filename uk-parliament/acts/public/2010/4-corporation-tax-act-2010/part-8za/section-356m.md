# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 356M

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>

## 356M. Oil contractor activities treated as separate trade[^c-1b6059f3a3d3c0f2097b69e4f6b59574]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the contractor carries on oil contractor activities as part of a trade, those activities are treated for the purposes of the charge to corporation tax on income as a separate trade, distinct from all other activities carried on by the contractor as part of the trade.
