## 356LC. “Lease”[^c-1b6059f3a3d3c0f2097b69e4f6b59574]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part “**lease**” has the meaning given by section 868 and “leased” and “leasing” are to be construed accordingly.
