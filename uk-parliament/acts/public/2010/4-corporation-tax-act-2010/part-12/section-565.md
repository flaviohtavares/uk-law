# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 565

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 565. “The section 565 amount”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of section 564 “the section 565 amount” is found by subtracting D from P.

(2) In the case of a group UK REIT—

- P is 90% of the group's UK profits (as defined by section 530(2)) arising in the accounting period, and
- D is the gross amount of those profits distributed in respect of the period within[^c-244b4d7d634bdf3047a6911aa3d48083] —
  - (a) the deadline set by[^c-ef9efda938d5644f328abe79975c9aa1] section 530(1), or
  - (b) any later deadline set[^c-a2e6856f3b42ce6d115ed0da938e5160] by an officer of Revenue and Customs.

(3) In the case of a company UK REIT—

- P is 90% of the company's profits from property rental business arising in the accounting period, and
- D is the gross amount of those profits distributed in respect of the accounting period within[^c-a816cffdfca9d08c718d64dd2340364d] —
  - (a) the deadline set by[^c-1f779cba654ba8eaadaa1197abbf5c87] section 530(4), or
  - (b) any later deadline set[^c-225b18dbcd76e76efed42a40bda65039] by an officer of Revenue of Customs.

(4)[^c-72430ac41c029bf5a4fe0e531e21c2b2] The definition of “D” in subsections (2) and (3) needs to be read with section 530A (so far as applicable).
