# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 550

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 550. Attribution of distributions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsection (2) applies to—

- (a) distributions made by the principal company of a group UK REIT, and
- (b) distributions made by a company UK REIT.

(2) The distributions are to be attributed—

- (a)[^c-7182654f64a307a545d418dc84e916a9] first, to distributions in satisfaction of the requirement of section 530(1)(a) or 530(4)(a) (as the case may be),
- (aa) second, to distributions in satisfaction of the requirement of section 530(1)(b) or 530(4)(b) (as the case may be),
- (b) third[^c-c1f931cd8d3778b6313fe502979202e8] , so far as the company determines, to distribution of amounts which derive from activities of a kind in respect of which corporation tax is chargeable in relation to income,
- (c) fourth[^c-5c68dbaea1a89fec1008d61ad35b2947] , to distribution of profits of property rental business (calculated in accordance with section 599),
- (d) fifth[^c-6e71b9332db04c333089635da7074300] , to distribution of relevant non-chargeable gains, and
- (e) sixth[^c-466e3025c25dd9d6ba4be7d2a2c84303] , to other distributions.

(3) In subsection (2)(d) “**relevant non-chargeable gains**” means—

- (a) in the case of a group, gains accruing to property rental business of a member of the group, and
- (b) in the case of a company, gains accruing to property rental business of the company,

which as a result of section 535 or 535A[^c-781d0b29ea26ce6daf4b825a8429a2aa] are not chargeable gains.

(4)[^c-c918b7a1c3e564cbf6d6b9780cdb7ada] In determining the amount of relevant non-chargeable gains for the purposes of this section, section 269ZBA (restriction on deductions) is to be ignored.
