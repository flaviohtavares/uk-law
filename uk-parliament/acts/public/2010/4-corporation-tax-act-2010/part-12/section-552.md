# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 552

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 552. “The section 552 amount”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of section 551, the section 552 amount is calculated by taking 3 steps.

(2) *Step 1*: find the amount given by—[^M_F_ab5be5fd-ea29-4048-bbba-ce779a807890]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/4)

where—

DO is—

- (a) in the case of a group UK REIT, the amount of the group's UK profits (as defined by section 530(2)) distributed in respect of ordinary shares in the principal company, and
- (b) in the case of a company UK REIT, the amount of profits of property rental business of the company distributed in respect of ordinary shares in the company,

SO is—

- (a) the percentage of rights in respect of those shares held by the holder of excessive rights, or
- (b) if less, the percentage of rights held by the recipient of the distribution,

in respect of which the distribution is made,

PBRT[^c-51c5ee66cadf26826bda0ac28c8c2010] is the property basic rate[^c-b6f0da32f071ff702a0a5ecdaa670606] of income tax in force at the time the distribution is made, and

MCT is the main rate of corporation tax[^c-9c43a84e7f4eb4c6f6abdadf17c09479].

(3) *Step 2*: find the amount given by—[^M_F_7e58cd5b-a0d5-4d18-c880-bfe1387c5373]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/4)

where—

DP is—

- (a) in the case of a group UK REIT, the amount of the group's UK profits (as defined by section 530(2)) distributed in respect of preference shares in the principal company, and
- (b) in the case of a company UK REIT, the amount of profits of property rental business of the company distributed in respect of preference shares in the company,

SP is—

- (a) the percentage of rights in respect of those shares held by the holder of excessive rights, or
- (b) if less, the percentage of rights held by the recipient of the distribution,

in respect of which the distribution is made, and

PBRT[^c-d6253a7721e24367079782df89e5fb17] and MCT have the same meaning as they have in subsection (2).

(4) *Step 3*: add together the amounts given by steps 1 and 2.

That amount is the section 552 amount.
