## 563. Breach of property rental business condition[^c-989d07433b265042cf71a9cdac53077d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsection (2) applies if the property rental business condition (see section 561(3A))[^c-84fa3ea03bc08b97ae5ce576e709e9bc] is not met in the case of a UK REIT throughout an accounting period of—

- (a) in the case of a group UK REIT, the principal company of the group, and
- (b) in the case of a company UK REIT, the company.

(2) The breach is to be ignored.
