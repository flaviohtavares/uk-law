# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 274

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 274. “Oil-related activities”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part “**oil-related activities**” means—

- (a) oil extraction activities, and
- (b) any activities consisting of the acquisition, enjoyment or exploitation of oil rights.
