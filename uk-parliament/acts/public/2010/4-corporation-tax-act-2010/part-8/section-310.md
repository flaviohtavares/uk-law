## 310. The relevant percentage

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Chapter, the relevant percentage for an accounting period is 10%[^key-1ac3bc294c0481917a966f6640afad9c].

(2) The Treasury may by order vary the percentage for the time being specified in subsection (1) for such accounting periods as may be specified in the order.
