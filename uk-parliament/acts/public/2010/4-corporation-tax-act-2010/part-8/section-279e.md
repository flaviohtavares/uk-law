## 279E. The lower limit and the upper limit[^key-1ef3ee755b0a762d132ac36869e8ff08]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section gives the meaning in this Chapter of “the lower limit” and “**the upper limit**” in relation to an accounting period of a company (“A”).

(2) If A has no associated company[^key-97921ca2e70aa1e3546f1adec32ca2ef] in the accounting period—

- (a) the lower limit is £50,000[^key-a26b686dcf3a3b26c469709c068ed8eb], and
- (b) the upper limit is £250,000[^key-a7011a45106ce65f798ac09e76a484e9].

(3) If A has one or more associated companies[^key-eb76d9c7319955e17149b7ef9ef9152c] in the accounting period—

- (a) the lower limit is—[^key-79ea99f737e260cd49fa9427d596413b]

  [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/4)

  and
- (b) the upper limit is—[^key-5844d023803a4d46f3b3392c6927fe48]

  [formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/4)

  where N is the number of those related 51% group companies.

(4) For an accounting period of less than 12 months the lower limit and the upper limit are proportionately reduced.
