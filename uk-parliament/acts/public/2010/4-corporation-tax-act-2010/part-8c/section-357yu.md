# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 357YU

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 357YU. Interpretation[^key-9a04ada177c581939694de8061a99242]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part “**court**” includes a tribunal.

(2) In this Part “statutory rate” (in relation to interest) means a rate which is equal to a rate specified—

- (a) for purposes relating to taxation, and
- (b) in, or in a provision made under, an Act.
