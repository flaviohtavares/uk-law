# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 357YA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4

## 357YA. Charge to corporation tax on restitution interest

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-cd3612bf2db7657742f79bccf8545701] The charge to corporation tax on income applies to restitution interest arising to a company.

(2)[^c-c10b874c0d5e3e259bdc91e60b305049] In subsection (1) the reference to a company does not include a charitable company.
