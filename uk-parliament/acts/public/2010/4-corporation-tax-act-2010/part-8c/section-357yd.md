# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 357YD

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 357YD. Further provision about amounts included, or not included, in “restitution interest”[^c-9a04ada177c581939694de8061a99242]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Interest paid to a company is not restitution interest for the purposes of this Part if—

- (a) Condition B was not met in relation to the interest until after the interest was paid, and
- (b) the amount paid was limited to simple interest at a rate equivalent to or lower than a[^c-e1742efb3eeb498c0e18b1cf44f6a927] statutory rate

(2) Subsection (1) does not prevent so much of a relevant amount of interest determined to be due, agreed upon or otherwise paid as represents or is calculated by reference to simple interest at a rate equivalent to or lower than a[^c-2de77f6dd120f3d23c2d44756fdcc749] statutory rate from falling within the definition of “restitution interest”.

(3) In subsection (2) “**relevant amount of interest**” means an amount of interest the whole of which was paid before Condition B was met in relation to it.

(4) Section 357YC(7) applies in relation to this section as in relation to section 357YC.
