# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 357YT

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 357YT. Amounts taxed at restitution payments rate to be outside instalment payments regime[^c-9a04ada177c581939694de8061a99242]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of regulations under section 59E of TMA 1970 (further provision as to when corporation tax due and payable), tax charged at the restitution payments rate is to be disregarded in determining the amount of corporation tax payable by a company for an accounting period.
