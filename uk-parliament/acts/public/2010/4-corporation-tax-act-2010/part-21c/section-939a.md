# Corporation Tax Act 2010, section 939A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2010/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 939A. Overview of Part[^key-9cd0abf72d5cd319679444764626ef04]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part makes provision for removing entitlement to corporation tax reliefs where a person makes a relievable charity donation which is a tainted donation.

(2) See Chapter 8 of Part 13 of ITA 2007 and sections 257A and 257B[^key-471520f876792bd74de6f140966f1c03] of TCGA 1992 for the removal of entitlement to other reliefs, and the ways in which other income tax advantages are counteracted, where a person makes a relievable charity donation which is a tainted donation.
