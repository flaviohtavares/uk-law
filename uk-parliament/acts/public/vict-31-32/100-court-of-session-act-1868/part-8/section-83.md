# Court of Session Act 1868, section 83

> Text as at 1994-09-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. In case of default accountant to proceed ex parte.[^c567083]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In case of failure to lodge any note or answer or productions within the time appointed, the accountant shall proceed to dispose of the cause upon the evidence and statements submitted to him; and in case of the failure of one of the parties to attend any diet without reasonable excuse, he shall proceed to dispose of the cause, after hearing the explanations of the other party, according to his opinion on its merits.
