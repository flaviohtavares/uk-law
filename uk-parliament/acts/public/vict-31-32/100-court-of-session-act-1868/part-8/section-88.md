# Court of Session Act 1868, section 88

> Text as at 1994-09-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Procedure when remit to accountant made by the Lord Ordinary.[^c567088]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The whole of the above-mentioned provisions in relation to actions of accounting shall be applicable to causes in dependence before any of the Lords Ordinary.
