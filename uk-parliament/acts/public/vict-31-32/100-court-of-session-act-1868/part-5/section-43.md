# Court of Session Act 1868, section 43

> Text as at 1994-09-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Certain exemptions from serving as jurors abolished.[^c567019]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

All exemptions from liability to serve as jurors in Scotland depending on any Act passed subsequently to the [^c567020]Jurors (Scotland) Act 1825 are hereby abolished.
