# Court of Session Act 1868, section 38

> Text as at 1994-09-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Special case may be substituted for special verdict.[^c567010]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

It shall be lawful to substitute a special case signed by counsel for a special verdict, and thereupon to discharge the order for trial, or the jury, if one has been empannelled, without returning a verdict; and such special case shall have the like force and effect as a special verdict.
