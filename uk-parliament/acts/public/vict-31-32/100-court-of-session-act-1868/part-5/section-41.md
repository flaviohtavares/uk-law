# Court of Session Act 1868, section 41

> Text as at 1994-09-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Provision for payment of deficient stamp duty pursuant to judge’s certificate to be final.[^c567015]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c567016] the deliverance of the judge that the stamp upon any document is sufficient, or that such document does not require a stamp, shall not be subject to review.
