# Court of Session Act 1868, section 47

> Text as at 1994-09-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Jurors to be cited by registered post letter.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The present mode of citing jurors for the trial of civil causes shall be discontinued, and in place thereof the sheriff clerk of the sheriffdom of Lothian and Borders[^c567030], . . . [^c567031], or his depute, shall fill up and sign a proper citation addressed to each such juror, and shall cause the same to be transmitted to him in a registered post letter, directed to him at his place of residence, as stated in the roll of jurors; and a certificate under the hand of such sheriff clerk or his depute of the citation of any jurors or juror in manner herein provided shall have the like force and effect as an execution of citation according to the present law and practice.
