# Court of Session Act 1868, section 45

> Text as at 1994-09-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Mode of returning jurors.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The number of jurors to be cited for the trial of any cause or causes appointed to be tried at Edinburgh, . . . [^c567024], shall be such as is specified in the [^c567025]Jury Trials (Scotland) Act 1815; and a list of such jurors shall be returned by the sheriff principal[^c567026], as provided by the [^c567027]Jurors (Scotland) Act 1825 . . . [^c567028]
