# Court of Session Act 1868, section 93

> Text as at 1994-09-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. Procedure in time of vacation.[^c567094]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Summonses may be called, and defences or other pleadings may be returnable, at any of the box days in vacation or recess; . . . [^c567095]
