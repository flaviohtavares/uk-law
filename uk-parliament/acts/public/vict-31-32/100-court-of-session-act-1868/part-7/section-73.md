# Court of Session Act 1868, section 73

> Text as at 1994-09-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Appeal under s. 40. of 6 G. 4. c. 120.[^c567070][^c567069]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

It shall be lawful, by note of appeal under this Act, to remove to the Court of Session all causes originating in the inferior courts in which the claim is in amount above forty pounds, at the time and for the purpose and subject to the conditions specified in the fortieth section of the [^c567071]Court of Session Act 1825; and such causes may be remitted to the Outer House.
