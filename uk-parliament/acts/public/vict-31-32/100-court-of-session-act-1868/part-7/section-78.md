# Court of Session Act 1868, section 78

> Text as at 1994-09-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Exclusion of review by advocation under special enactments to imply exclusion of review by appeal.[^c567076]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where, by any Statute now in force, the right of review by advocation to the Court of Session is excluded or restricted, such exclusion or restriction of review shall be deemed and taken to apply to review by appeal under this Act.
