# Court of Session Act 1868, section 71

> Text as at 1994-09-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Form of bringing appeals into Court of Session.[^c567066]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Within two days after the appeal shall have been taken the clerk of the inferior court shall transmit the process to one of the clerks of the division of the court to which the appeal is taken, who shall subjoin to the appeal a note of the day on which it is received; . . . [^c567067]
