# Court of Session Act 1868, section 56

> Text as at 1994-09-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. After reclaiming note against a final judgment, cause not to be remitted to Outer House.[^c567043]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After the whole cause has been decided in the Outer House within the meaning of this Act, it shall not in any case be necessary for the Inner House to remit the same back to the Outer House; but the cause, when taken to the Inner House after having been so decided in the Outer House, even though the interlocutor of the Lord Ordinary or any of the procedure shall be held to have been incompetent, shall, except in special circumstances rendering a remit expedient, remain in the Inner House, until it shall be finally and completely decided in the Court of Session.
