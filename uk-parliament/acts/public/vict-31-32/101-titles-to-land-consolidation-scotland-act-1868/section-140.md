# Titles to Land Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1868, section 140

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2018-11-12. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/31-32/101

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 140. Additional sheets may be added to writs.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In all cases where writs or deeds of any description are by this or any other Act permitted or directed to be engrossed on any conveyance or deed, it shall be competent, when necessary, to engross such deeds or writs on a sheet or sheets of paper, or of whatever other material the conveyance itself consists, added to such conveyance, provided that the engrossing of the deed or writ shall be commenced on some part of the conveyance or deed itself on which it is permitted or directed to be engrossed; and the first of such additional sheets shall be chargeable with the stamp duty applicable to the writ or deed partly engrossed thereon [^c-0a5992aac14d5ed95108055140d8ab80]....
