# Titles to Land Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1868, section 138

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2018-11-12. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/31-32/101
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 138. Short clauses of consent to registration may be used in any deed.[^c13455481]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The short clauses of consent to registration for preservation, and for preservation and execution, contained in form No. 1[^c-6c7921a81bbeacd08074331487c4942d] of schedule (B.) hereto annexed, when occurring in any deed or conveyance under this Act, or in any deed or writing or document of whatsoever nature, and whether relating to lands or not, shall unless specially qualified import a consent to registration and a procuratory of registration in the books of council and session, or other judges books competent, therein to remain for preservation; and also, if for execution, that , upon the issue of an extract containing a warrant for execution, all lawful execution shall pass thereon[^c13455491].
