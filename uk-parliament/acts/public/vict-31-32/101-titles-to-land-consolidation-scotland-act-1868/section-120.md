## 120. Securities may be registered during lifetime of grantee, or title completed after his death.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Heritable securities [^c-b800570630d55b6fba44ad30203a51ac]... may be registered in the appropriate register of sasines at any time during the lifetime of the grantee, and shall in competition be preferred according to the date of the registration thereof [^c-b800570630d55b6fba44ad30203a51ac]....
