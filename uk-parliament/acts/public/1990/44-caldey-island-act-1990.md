# Caldey Island Act 1990

Caldey Island Act 1990

1990 c. 44

An Act to provide for the islands of Caldey and St. Margaret’s in the county of Dyfed to be included in the district of South Pembrokeshire for the purposes of local, parliamentary and European elections and for the purposes of local taxation; to include those islands in the districts of the Pembrokeshire coroner and the Pembrokeshire Health Authority; and for connected purposes.

\[1st November 1990\]

[^c930327]Be it enacted by the Queen’s most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—

## 1. Elections.

(1) Caldey Island and St. Margaret’s Island in the county of Dyfed are in[^c930330] this Act together referred to as “**Caldey**”[^c930331]. . ..

(2) St. Margaret’s Island (as well as Caldey Island) shall be part of the electoral division of Tenby for the purposes of the election of councillors for that county; and Caldey shall be part of—

- (a)[^c930332] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the parliamentary constituency of Pembroke; and
- (c) the European Parliamentary constituency of Mid and West Wales.

(3) Caldey shall be a parliamentary polling district and a polling district for the purposes of the election of county [^c930333]. . .councillors.

(4)[^c930334] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c930335] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

## 2. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c930337]

## 3. Coroner’s and health authority districts.

Caldey shall be included in the district of the Pembrokeshire coroner [^c930338]. . ..

## 4. Savings and transitional and supplemental provisions.

(1)[^c-798fec7669fb42364c1afe1444da9fe1] The foregoing provisions of this Act are without prejudice to the powers exercisable under—

- (a)[^c930344] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the [^c930345]Parliamentary Constituencies Act 1986, Schedule 2 to the [^c930346]European Parliamentary Elections Act 1978, and sections 18A and 31 of the Representation of the People Act 1983 (designation of and alteration of polling districts);
- (c) section 4A[^c930347] of the [^c930348]Coroners Act 1988 (alteration of coroners districts); [^c930349]. . .
- (d) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c930344] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c930351] The Secretary of State may by order make such transitional and supplemental provisions with respect to Caldey as he considers necessary or expedient in consequence of the provisions of this Act.

(4) The power to make orders under subsection (3) above shall be exercisable by statutory instrument subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

## 5. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Caldey Island Act 1990.
