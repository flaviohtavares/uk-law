# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 198

## 198. Power to make tree preservation orders.[^c14024111][^c14024121]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If it appears to a local planning authority that it is expedient in the interests of amenity to make provision for the preservation of trees or woodlands in their area, they may for that purpose make an order with respect to such trees, groups of trees or woodlands as may be specified in the order.

(2) An order under subsection (1) is in this Act referred to as a “tree preservation order”.

(3) A tree preservation order may, in particular, make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7139c054f4cf8cda14b96efdaaed5c57]

- (a) for prohibiting (subject to any exemptions for which provision may be made by the order) the cutting down, topping, lopping, uprooting, wilful damage or wilful destruction of trees except with the consent of the local planning authority, and for enabling that authority to give their consent subject to conditions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7139c054f4cf8cda14b96efdaaed5c57]
- (b) for securing the replanting, in such manner as may be prescribed by or under the order, of any part of a woodland area which is felled in the course of forestry operations permitted by or under the order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7139c054f4cf8cda14b96efdaaed5c57]
- (c) for applying, in relation to any consent under the order, and to applications for such consent, any of the provisions of this Act mentioned in subsection (4), subject to such adaptations and modifications as may be specified in the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7139c054f4cf8cda14b96efdaaed5c57]

(4) The provisions referred to in subsection (3)(c) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7139c054f4cf8cda14b96efdaaed5c57]

- (a) the provisions of Part III relating to planning permission and to applications for planning permission, except sections 56, 62, 65 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7139c054f4cf8cda14b96efdaaed5c57] [^c14024131]. . ., 69(3) and (4), 71, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7139c054f4cf8cda14b96efdaaed5c57] [^c14024141]. . ., 91 to 96, 100 and 101 and Schedule 8; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7139c054f4cf8cda14b96efdaaed5c57]
- (b) sections 137 to 141, 143 and 144 (except so far as they relate to purchase notices served in consequence of such orders as are mentioned in section 137(1)(b) or (c)); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7139c054f4cf8cda14b96efdaaed5c57]
- (c) section 316. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7139c054f4cf8cda14b96efdaaed5c57]

(5) A tree preservation order may be made so as to apply, in relation to trees to be planted pursuant to any such conditions as are mentioned in section 197(a), as from the time when those trees are planted.

(6) Without prejudice to any other exemptions for which provision may be made by a tree preservation order, no such order shall apply— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f7807028dfcfd5faf097dc496a7af3b2]

- (a) to the cutting down, uprooting, topping or lopping of trees which are dying or dead or have become dangerous, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f7807028dfcfd5faf097dc496a7af3b2]
- (b) to the cutting down, uprooting, topping or lopping of any trees in compliance with any obligations imposed by or under an Act of Parliament or so far as may be necessary for the prevention or abatement of a nuisance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f7807028dfcfd5faf097dc496a7af3b2]

(7) This section *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1243af8847049702678624798a98c43d] Tree preservation regulations shall have effect subject to—

- (a) section 39(2) of the [^c14024151]Housing and Planning Act 1986 (saving for effect of section 2(4) of the [^c14024161]Opencast Coal Act 1958 on land affected by a tree preservation order despite its repeal); and
- (b) section 15 of the [^c14024171]Forestry Act 1967 (licences under that Act to fell trees comprised in a tree preservation order).

(8)[^c18959511] In relation to an application for consent under a tree preservation order the appropriate authority may by regulations make provision as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-41f4ab9c45cb3c6dd03183c806660508]

- (a) the form and manner in which the application must be made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-41f4ab9c45cb3c6dd03183c806660508]
- (b) particulars of such matters as are to be included in the application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-41f4ab9c45cb3c6dd03183c806660508]
- (c) the documents or other materials as are to accompany the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-41f4ab9c45cb3c6dd03183c806660508]

(9) The appropriate authority is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-41f4ab9c45cb3c6dd03183c806660508]

- (a) the Secretary of State in relation to England; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-41f4ab9c45cb3c6dd03183c806660508]
- (b) the National Assembly for Wales in relation to Wales, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-41f4ab9c45cb3c6dd03183c806660508]

and in the case of regulations made by the National Assembly for Wales section 333(3) must be ignored. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-41f4ab9c45cb3c6dd03183c806660508]
