## 201. Provisional tree preservation orders.[^c14024241][^c14024251]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-b410726660d03d1e85849eea6ba3438a] If it appears to a local planning authority that a tree preservation order proposed to be made by that authority should take effect immediately without previous confirmation, they may include in the order as made by them a direction that this section shall apply to the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b410726660d03d1e85849eea6ba3438a]

(2) Notwithstanding section 199(1), an order which contains such a direction— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b410726660d03d1e85849eea6ba3438a]

- (a) shall take effect provisionally on such date as may be specified in it, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b410726660d03d1e85849eea6ba3438a]
- (b) shall continue in force by virtue of this section until— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b410726660d03d1e85849eea6ba3438a]
  - (i) the expiration of a period of six months beginning with the date on which the order was made; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b410726660d03d1e85849eea6ba3438a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-b410726660d03d1e85849eea6ba3438a] the date on which the order is confirmed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b410726660d03d1e85849eea6ba3438a]

  whichever first occurs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b410726660d03d1e85849eea6ba3438a]
