## 218. Further appeal to the Crown Court: England[^c14024971][^c-0dc07cab350283bcb779cfe89c112b66]

*Extent: E+W*

Where an appeal has been brought to a magistrates' court[^c-72dcc1de1a04b230a9f4df5d684dd006] under section 217, an appeal against the decision of the magistrates’ court on that appeal may be brought to the Crown Court by the appellant or by the local planning authority who served the notice in question under section 215.
