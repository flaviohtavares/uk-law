## 200. Tree preservation orders: Forestry Commissioners and Natural Resources Body for Wales[^c14024201][^c14024211][^c18884391][^key-2e95b96608d98dbab18ffd691c68ae99]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A tree preservation order does not *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-93c1d5d916454bf9c099da25495678cf] Tree preservation regulations do not have effect in respect of anything done—

- (a) by or on behalf of the Forestry Commissioners or the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^key-2116dd8bbd2856228a112a927350e03f] on land placed at their disposal in pursuance of the Forestry Act 1967 or otherwise under their management or supervision;
- (b) by or on behalf of any other person in accordance with a relevant plan which is for the time being in force.

(2) A relevant plan is a plan of operations or other working plan approved by the Forestry Commissioners or the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^key-efbae06d24af3a723f61445543488b6a] under—

- (a) a forestry dedication covenant within the meaning of section 5 of the Forestry Act 1967, or
- (b) conditions of a grant or loan made by the Forestry Commissioners[^key-39ddae87d8e336a15862e3626c7cb53a] under section 1 of the Forestry Act 1979 or made by the Natural Resources Body for Wales under article 10B of the Natural Resources Body for Wales (Establishment) Order 2012 ([^key-b79615feb93813dfc43c31b6fce2c33c]S.I.[2012/1903](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/1903)) for or in connection with the use or management of land for forestry purposes.

(3) A reference to a provision of the Forestry Act 1967 or the Forestry Act 1979 includes a reference to a corresponding provision replaced by that provision or any earlier corresponding provision.
