## 204. Compensation in respect of requirement as to replanting of trees.[^c14024291]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732] This section applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732]

- (a) in pursuance of provision made by a tree preservation order, a direction is given by the local planning authority or the Secretary of State for securing the replanting of all or any part of a woodland area which is felled in the course of forestry operations permitted by or under the order; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732]
- (b) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732] Natural Resources Body for Wales[^c-586fadb16a35834a6d2128bdf0baefa3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732] decide not to make any grant or loan under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732] article 10B of the Natural Resources Body for Wales (Establishment) Order 2012 ([^c-894bad03cea604611dfde079c8660f6a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732]S.I.[2012/1903](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/1903)) in respect of the replanting by reason that the direction frustrates the use of the woodland area for the growing of timber or other forest products for commercial purposes and in accordance with the rules or practice of good forestry. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732]

(2) Where this section applies, the local planning authority exercising functions under the tree preservation order shall be liable, on the making of a claim in accordance with this section, to pay compensation in respect of such loss or damage, if any, as is caused or incurred in consequence of compliance with the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732]

(3) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732] Natural Resources Body for Wales *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732] shall, at the request of the person under a duty to comply with such a direction as is mentioned in subsection (1)(a), give a certificate stating— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732]

- (a) whether they have decided not to make such a grant or loan as is mentioned in subsection (1)(b), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732]
- (b) if so, the grounds for their decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732]

(4) A claim for compensation under this section must be served on the local planning authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732]

- (a) within 12 months from the date on which the direction was given, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732]
- (b) where an appeal has been made to the Secretary of State against the decision of the local planning authority, within 12 months from the date of the decision of the Secretary of State on the appeal, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732]

but subject in either case to such extension of that period as the local planning authority may allow. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732]
