## 202. Power for Secretary of State to make tree preservation orders.[^c14024261][^c14024271]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If it appears to the Secretary of State, after consultation with the local planning authority, to be expedient that a tree preservation order or an order amending or revoking such an order should be made, he may himself make such an order.

(2) Any order so made by the Secretary of State shall have the same effect as if it had been made by the local planning authority and confirmed by them under this Chapter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bff200aafb76ae617f43d4d0d0c2b0ec] , once it has taken effect in accordance with tree preservation regulations, have the same effect as if it had been made by the local planning authority under section 198(1).

(3) The provisions of this Chapter and of any regulations made under it with respect to the procedure to be followed in connection with the making and confirmation of any order to which subsection (1) applies and the service of copies of it as confirmed shall have effect, subject to any necessary modifications— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0ecd86b28bfd0cb0a92f02aadd0b635]

- (a) in relation to any proposal by the Secretary of State to make such an order, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0ecd86b28bfd0cb0a92f02aadd0b635]
- (b) in relation to the making of it by the Secretary of State, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0ecd86b28bfd0cb0a92f02aadd0b635]
- (c) in relation to the service of copies of it as so made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e0ecd86b28bfd0cb0a92f02aadd0b635]
