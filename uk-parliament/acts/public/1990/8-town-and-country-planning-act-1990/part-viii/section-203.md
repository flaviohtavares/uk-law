# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 203

## 203. Compensation in respect of tree preservation orders.[^c14024281]

*Extent: E+W*

A tree preservation order may make provision for the payment by the local planning authority, subject to such exceptions and conditions as may be specified in the order, of compensation in respect of loss or damage caused or incurred in consequence— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732]

- (a) of the refusal of any consent required under the order, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732]
- (b) of the grant of any such consent subject to conditions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6d3a17abf69cefbabc61c2887ca0e732]
