# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 199

## 199. Form of and procedure applicable to orders.[^c14024181][^c14024191]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-b96f59113118672031cb3a35c9abc890] A tree preservation order shall not take effect until it is confirmed by the local planning authority and the local planning authority may confirm any such order either without modification or subject to such modifications as they consider expedient. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b96f59113118672031cb3a35c9abc890]

(2) Provision may be made by regulations under this Act with respect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b96f59113118672031cb3a35c9abc890]

- (a) to the form of tree preservation orders, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b96f59113118672031cb3a35c9abc890]
- (b) to the procedure to be followed in connection with the making and confirmation of such orders. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b96f59113118672031cb3a35c9abc890]

(3) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (2), the regulations may make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b96f59113118672031cb3a35c9abc890]

- (a) that, before a tree preservation order is confirmed by the local planning authority, notice of the making of the order shall be given to the owners and occupiers of land affected by the order and to such other persons, if any, as may be specified in the regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b96f59113118672031cb3a35c9abc890]
- (b) that objections and representations with respect to the order, if duly made in accordance with the regulations, shall be considered before the order is confirmed by the local planning authority; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b96f59113118672031cb3a35c9abc890]
- (c) that copies of the order, when confirmed by the authority, shall be served on such persons as may be specified in the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b96f59113118672031cb3a35c9abc890]
