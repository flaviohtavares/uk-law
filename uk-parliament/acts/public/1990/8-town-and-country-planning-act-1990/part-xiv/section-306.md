# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 306

## 306. Contributions by relevant authorities and statutory undertakers.[^key-0309e0164c9765e7e93d6df413c33ab2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Without prejudice to section 274 of the [^c14030881]Highways Act 1980 (contributions by local authorities to expenses of highway authorities), any relevant authority[^key-fda1200c8f1c2f42c9df22002cf26e65] may contribute towards any expenses incurred by a local highway authority—

- (a) in the acquisition of land under Part IX of this Act or Chapter V of Part I of the [^c14030891]Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990 or Chapter 5 of Part 3 of the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023[^key-e0dc5e6486b4680e583fccedc2494708],
- (b) in the construction or improvement of roads on land so acquired, or
- (c) in connection with any development required in the interests of the proper planning of the area of the relevant authority[^key-783d66314d813bc2f4e9c5d3961df5eb].

(2) Any relevant authority[^key-589095a73505c1e3909890103e1e5ad5] and any statutory undertakers may contribute towards—

- (a)[^c14030901][^c18909641] any expenses incurred by a local planning authority for the purposes of carrying out a review under section 13 or 61 of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 (duty of local planning authority to keep under review certain matters affecting development);
- (ab) any expenses incurred by a minerals and waste planning authority or[^key-268ede9328b88c03b0cf0cd3f8f0290f] county council for the purposes of carrying out a review under section 14 of that Act (duty of minerals and waste planning authority or[^key-82af97933a3b0b42901e059f8035633b] county council to keep under review certain matters affecting development);
- (b) any expenses incurred by a local planning authority or a mineral planning authority in or in connection with the performance of any of their functions under Part III, the provisions of Part VI relating to purchase notices, Part VII except sections 196A and 196B[^c14030931], Part VIII (except section 207), Part IX or Schedule 5 or 9.

(2A)[^key-4bb172fc6769f56b69ebf5f8d46bf882] Each of the persons specified in subsection (2B) may contribute towards any expenses incurred by a corporate joint committee[^key-dbddb713cee5e57b4d23d3991e446c12] for the purposes of carrying out a review under section 60L of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 (corporate joint committees: area survey)[^key-890b1bb400c33155db416ac82e1c5653].

(2B) The persons are—

- (a) a local authority in Wales;
- (b) a statutory undertaker authorised to carry on an undertaking in Wales.

(3) Where any expenses are incurred by a relevant authority[^key-8c74fa4ce34e90018c7b8d481e92e9f5] in the payment of compensation payable in consequence of anything done under Part III, the provisions of Part VI relating to purchase notices, Part VII except sections 196A and 196B, Part VIII, or Schedule 5 or 9, the Secretary of State may, if it appears to him to be expedient to do so, require any other relevant authority to contribute towards those expenses such sum as appears to him to be reasonable, having regard to any benefit accruing to that authority by reason of the proceeding giving rise to the compensation.

(4) Subsection (3) shall apply in relation to payments made by a relevant authority[^key-b73f6cf1e259cd5529627f260fb1b4b0] to any statutory undertakers in accordance with financial arrangements to which effect is given under section 275(5)(c), as it applies in relation to compensation payable by such an authority in consequence of anything done under Part III, Part VIII or Schedule 5 or 9, and the reference in that subsection to the proceeding giving rise to the compensation shall be construed accordingly.

(5) For the purposes of this section, contributions made by a local planning authority towards the expenditure of a joint advisory committee shall be deemed to be expenses incurred by that authority for the purposes for which that expenditure is incurred by the committee.

(6)[^c14030951] This section shall have effect as if the references to a local authority included references to a National Park authority.

(7)[^key-80e98ca75b8215236a1ffb346cd4d560] In this section “**relevant authority**” means—

- (a) a local authority, or
- (b) a non-mayoral combined authority or non-mayoral CCA.
