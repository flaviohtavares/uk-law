## 5. The Broads.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of Chapter I of Part VIII , and sections 90A, 249, 250[^key-86fbf21fdb52e0f66dfac1f086f1b987] , 293B to 293J[^key-db1c75e4940f1cbce7ad4757bd3b99bc] and 300, and Schedule 7A and any other provision of this Act so far as it has effect for the purposes of those provisions, “**local planning authority**”, in relation to land in the Broads, includes the Broads Authority.

(2) For the purposes of the provisions mentioned in subsection (3) the Broads Authority shall be the sole district planning authority for the Broads.

(3) The provisions referred to in subsection (2) are Part II, sections[^c14016271] 61E to 61Q,[^key-4e9bc088bf2ac1dca80ecad7e62f156c] 62, 65, 69 to 72[^c14016281], 76 to 79[^c14016291], 91 to 95, 96A[^c19901841] to 99, 102, 103, 106 to 106BC[^key-8a345c4aabe81b42c21af597f2a3e161], 171C, 172, 173, 173A[^c14016311] 178, 183, 184, 187A, 187B[^c14016321] 188, 191 to 197, 211 to 214, 215[^c14016331], 219 to 221, 224, 294, 295, 297, 299, 299A[^c14016341] 301, 316 and 324(1) and (7)[^c14016351] and Schedules 4B and 4C[^key-31bbe2c7c8c56968429817b3b755e167].
