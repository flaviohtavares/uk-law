# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 71

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 71. Consultations in connection with determinations under s. 70.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-91feedb84cf86b154aadd46f27c96a8c][^c-20b7f264b338a008b994ea2520822759][^c14019861] A development order may provide that a local planning authority shall not determine an application for planning permission or permission in principle[^c-d605311e0b7fd719d9dce43e830bcadd] before the end of such period as may be prescribed.

(2)[^c-4c33b65438119f7edb391d465e4ab645][^c-20b7f264b338a008b994ea2520822759] A development order may require a local planning authority—

- (a) to take into account in determining such an application such representations, made within such period, as may be prescribed; and
- (b) to give to any person whose representations have been taken into account such notice as may be prescribed of their decision.

(2ZA)[^c-4f61bccead4701fe247cf0264293f796] In subsections (1) and (2) references to —[^M_F_23d3676c-1e4f-4cf5-f12a-3fb27520c3d9]

- (a) an application for consent, agreement or approval as mentioned in section 61DB(2), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffb17bfb81a7dbec941da61458195e3e]
- (b) an application for planning permission include references to an application for approval under section 61L(2) , and[^c-c3f3e64a0f0053aa9af8df6ec21a8ff1]
- (c) an application for consent, agreement or approval where that consent, agreement or approval is required by a condition or limitation imposed under section 61QI(1).

(2A) A development order making any provision by virtue of this section may make different provision for different cases or different classes of development.

(3) Before a local planning authority grant planning permission for the use of land as a caravan site, they shall, unless they are also the authority with power to issue a site licence for that land, consult the local authority with that power.

(3A)[^c-78245c6041ae4498dd76dea5691bbf60] Subsection (3) does not apply in relation to planning permission granted by a Mayoral development order or[^M_F_603b3d92-3e5d-41a7-e1a1-d0dc4fc30b39] a neighbourhood development order.

(4) In this section—

- “[^c14019871]**prescribed**” means prescribed by a development order
- “**site licence**” means a licence under Part 1 of the [^c14019881]Caravan Sites and Control of Development Act 1960 authorising the use of land as a caravan site or under Part 2 of the Mobile Homes (Wales) Act 2013 authorising the use of the land as a site for mobile homes (within the meaning of that Act)[^c-bcc212eb8d72b2575b5d0714687bae5e].
