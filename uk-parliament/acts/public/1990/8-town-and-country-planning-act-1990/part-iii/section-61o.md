# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 61O

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (805).** Text as at 2026-11-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 61O. Guidance[^c-085bd6d7b9124e0e88168558f5368a7b][^c-99c58ceb70d9a80231e26e93f5bf37c9]

*Extent: E+W*

Local planning authorities must have regard to any guidance issued by the Secretary of State in the exercise of any function under any provision relating to neighbourhood development orders (including any function under any of sections 61F to 61H).
