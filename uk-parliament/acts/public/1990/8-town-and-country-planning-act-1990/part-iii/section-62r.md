# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 62R

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (816).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/8

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 62R. Power to make provision by development order in respect of applications to Welsh Ministers[^key-44d2d38ffcf687724baaf9bb457f2084]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A development order may make provision for regulating the manner in which an application for planning permission made to the Welsh Ministers under section [^key-564533e8c618f21884ed03f935b5ae60]... 62F, 62M or 62O, or an application for approval made to the Welsh Ministers under section [^key-d647b848ca6c74849f59147f2bf4cb28]... 62M or 62O, is to be dealt with by the Welsh Ministers.

(2) That provision may include provision about—

- (a) consultation to be carried out by the Welsh Ministers;
- (b) the variation of an application.
