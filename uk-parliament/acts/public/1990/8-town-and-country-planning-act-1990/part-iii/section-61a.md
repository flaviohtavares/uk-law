# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 61A

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 61A. Local development orders[^c19081121]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local planning authority may by order (a local development order) make provision to implement policies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19902191]

- (a) in one or more development plan documents (within the meaning of Part 2 of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19902191]
- (b) in a local development plan (within the meaning of Part 6 of that Act). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19902191]

(2) A local planning authority may by order (a local development order)[^c19902221] grant planning permission—

- (a) for development specified in the order;
- (b) for development of any class so specified.

(3) A local development order may relate to—

- (a) all land in the area of the relevant authority;
- (b) any part of that land;
- (c) a site specified in the order.

(4) A local development order may make different provision for different descriptions of land.

(5) But a development order may specify any area or class of development in respect of which a local development order must not be made.

(6) A local planning authority may revoke a local development order at any time.

(7) Schedule 4A makes provision in connection with local development orders.
