# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 62C

## 62C. Notifying parish councils of applications under section 62A(1)[^key-2bf446afabc977549223178c8099989a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If an application is made to the Secretary of State under section 62A(1) and a parish council would be entitled under paragraph 8 of Schedule 1 to be notified of the application were it made to the local planning authority, the Secretary of State must notify the council of—

- (a) the application, and
- (b) any alteration to the application accepted by the Secretary of State.

(2) Paragraph 8(4) and (5) of Schedule 1 apply in relation to duties of the Secretary of State under subsection (1) as they apply to duties of a local planning authority under paragraph 8(1) or (3B)[^key-394373d07a7f27c3dc367e45f17064dc] of that Schedule.

(3) An authority designated for the purposes of section 62A must comply with requests from the Secretary of State for details of requests received by the authority under paragraph 8(1) of Schedule 1 or notifications received by the authority under paragraph 8(3C) or (3D) of that Schedule[^key-85438a3dcdccd59d587b348f9d0d7e87].
