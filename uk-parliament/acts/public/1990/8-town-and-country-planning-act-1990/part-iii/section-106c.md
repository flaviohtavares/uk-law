# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 106C

## 106C. Legal challenges relating to development consent obligations

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A court may entertain proceedings for questioning a failure by the Secretary of State [^c-b6f985dbaa3b51d3b48e8f8c5c9bc52c]... to give notice as mentioned in section 106A(7) or 106BA(9)[^c-7a8921f730ead377610e22f8c4549efe] only if—

- (a) the proceedings are brought by a claim for judicial review, and
- (b) the claim form is filed before the end of[^c-fd809c855cfe8536cfd43879f50d1844] the period of 6 weeks beginning with the day after[^c-7ab03fceafb827d0f74e4abb527eb3fa] the day on which the period prescribed under section 106A(7) or 106BA(9) ends.

(1A)[^c-420aeabf7e7cfa4f32ed2241ed67afa9] If no period is prescribed under section 106BA(9), the period of 6 weeks referred to in subsection (1)(b) that applies in relation to proceedings for failure to give notice as mentioned in subsection (9) of section 106BA begins with the day after[^c-377ad674721d2e5f4f846a15d62202a8] the expiry of the period mentioned in that subsection that applies in the applicant's case.

(2) A court may entertain proceedings for questioning a determination by the Secretary of State [^c-b6f985dbaa3b51d3b48e8f8c5c9bc52c]... that a planning obligation shall continue to have effect without modification only if—

- (a) the proceedings are brought by a claim for judicial review, and
- (b) the claim form is filed before the end of[^c-9b7a9f196f1cc9d497e3a1e58aa59de5] the period of 6 weeks beginning with the day after[^c-46aba88366cf98f22be088da683c65b4] the day on which notice of the determination is given under section 106A(7) or 106BA(9)[^c-3a045c2396d9582301206e83303e90e7].

(3)[^c-f8a6429fb26d43f7c7472bc4859bc292] A court may entertain proceedings for questioning a determination by the Secretary of State on an application under section 106BA that a planning obligation shall be modified otherwise than in accordance with the application only if—

- (a) the proceedings are brought by a claim for judicial review, and
- (b) the claim form is filed before the end of[^c-98cc8e2a834cbe215f8c85295b675d7a] the period of 6 weeks beginning with the day after[^c-a6ed538e6f378f6c3841aab19b8a33f2] the day on which notice of the determination is given under section 106BA(9).
