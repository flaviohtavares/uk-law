# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 61X

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (816).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/8

## 61X. Duty to take account of responses to consultation[^c-6e5b5d2d113f5ea11ee9ceb82f9caaca]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subsection (2) applies where a person—

- (a) has been required by section 61W(1) to carry out consultation on a proposed application for planning permission or permission in principle[^c-abd3d057b2d90892ab6aa87a9047bb63], and
- (b) proposes to go ahead with making an application for planning permission or permission in principle (whether or not in the same terms as the proposed application).

(2) The person must, when deciding whether the application that the person is actually to make should be in the same terms as the proposed application, have regard to any responses to the consultation that the person has received.
