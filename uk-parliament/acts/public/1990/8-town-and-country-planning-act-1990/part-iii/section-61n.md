# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 61N

## 61N. Legal challenges in relation to neighbourhood development orders[^key-326afa5e53ba915f2650fbc76cc8bcc3][^key-99c58ceb70d9a80231e26e93f5bf37c9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A court may entertain proceedings for questioning a decision to act under section 61E(4) or (8) only if—

- (a) the proceedings are brought by a claim for judicial review, and
- (b) the claim form is filed before the end of the period of 6 weeks beginning with the day after[^key-98648db2b125cc39b3adfa186c7b5d31] the day on which the decision is published.

(2) A court may entertain proceedings for questioning a decision under paragraph 12 of Schedule 4B (consideration by local planning authority of recommendations made by examiner etc) or paragraph 13B of that Schedule (intervention powers of Secretary of State)[^key-395da1d07b1b4c010825dc2077700502] only if—

- (a) the proceedings are brought by a claim for judicial review, and
- (b) the claim form is filed before the end of the period of 6 weeks beginning with the day after[^key-0e5bcaef7da08b7f48d88d52be298f6f] the day on which the decision is published.

(3) A court may entertain proceedings for questioning anything relating to a referendum under paragraph 14 or 15 of Schedule 4B only if—

- (a) the proceedings are brought by a claim for judicial review, and
- (b) the claim form is filed before the end of[^key-eb7147b28a9610c826c7e1ecae23b49c] the period of 6 weeks beginning with the day after[^key-fa2560755920457ef3c8b46f70cac6e9] the day on which the result of the referendum is declared.
