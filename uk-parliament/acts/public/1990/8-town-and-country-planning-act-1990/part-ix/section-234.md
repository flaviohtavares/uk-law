## 234. Disposal by Secretary of State of land acquired under s. 228.[^key-dfedd51a4a8b40bec9991c3719980228][^key-17ddad38593dc6ff5b20a84b8f2fb686][^key-90e6f5dfbcbb373542815b6247e6d48e][^key-44a8d51d2a860e87a1c0db548343a1c4]

*Extent: E+W*

[^key-3bdb64a45edf35d7853437c1f715a451][^key-cff962d80373dfb04270f2cfb0917d4a][^key-8bea3a071448d2556d7b70893be9ee03][^key-f07ee5d8abfe595ead0a9ab6e800fc35][^key-22ce916de130821de47a899a6b00b17b]

(1) The Secretary of State may dispose of land held by him and acquired by him or any other Minister under section 228 to such person, in such manner and subject to such conditions as appear to him expedient.

(2) In particular, the Secretary of State may under subsection (1) dispose of land held by him for any purpose in order to secure its use for that purpose.
