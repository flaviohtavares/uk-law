## 227. Acquisition of land by agreement.[^key-140451e10082cdda9b7367f5cf104aca][^key-a1d3512c5dd6969f385d666ec25aff89][^key-280621c9e92898d9540b4eab123cf9e2][^key-69d38099f0936a4c4b9ba27341f67925][^key-8d6a0f2f94dbdb8d46636137de8190bf][^key-90e6f5dfbcbb373542815b6247e6d48e][^key-b1f9dbb3edd7c4a1109ad95f46280358][^key-80d92f6f138bcde72df5f4944c3c68df][^key-44a8d51d2a860e87a1c0db548343a1c4][^key-3bdb64a45edf35d7853437c1f715a451][^key-0a1ca822543fb4d0a45066fc7d615fc9][^key-6083e2f9011bf8dbcc4d5232196eee1c][^key-cff962d80373dfb04270f2cfb0917d4a][^key-8bea3a071448d2556d7b70893be9ee03][^key-f07ee5d8abfe595ead0a9ab6e800fc35][^key-6d65e53718117e781e4bd5db4cdce5df][^key-0a8759743fd5d500ff981f635ce64a17][^key-22ce916de130821de47a899a6b00b17b][^key-6d0a19cd374a7c4edcf90f48210285ca][^key-69d38099f0936a4c4b9ba27341f67925][^key-90e6f5dfbcbb373542815b6247e6d48e][^key-44a8d51d2a860e87a1c0db548343a1c4]

*Extent: E+W*

[^key-3bdb64a45edf35d7853437c1f715a451][^key-cff962d80373dfb04270f2cfb0917d4a][^key-8bea3a071448d2556d7b70893be9ee03][^key-f07ee5d8abfe595ead0a9ab6e800fc35][^key-22ce916de130821de47a899a6b00b17b]

(1) The council of any county, county borough,[^c14025241] district or London borough or any combined authority or CCA[^key-e1fdbd29ec162bea405835e79add892f] may acquire by agreement any land which they require for any purpose for which a local authority or combined authority or CCA[^key-4390b3703c8fa2313c5bdbbe5a0a728f] may be authorised to acquire land under section 226.

(2) The provisions of Part 1 of the [^c14025251]Compulsory Purchase Act 1965 (so far as applicable), other than sections 4 to 8, section 10 and section 31, shall apply in relation to the acquisition of land under this section.
