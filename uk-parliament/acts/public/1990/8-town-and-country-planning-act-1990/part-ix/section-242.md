## 242. Overriding of rights of possession.[^c-90e6f5dfbcbb373542815b6247e6d48e][^c-44a8d51d2a860e87a1c0db548343a1c4]

*Extent: E+W*

[^c-3bdb64a45edf35d7853437c1f715a451][^c-cff962d80373dfb04270f2cfb0917d4a][^c-8bea3a071448d2556d7b70893be9ee03][^c-f07ee5d8abfe595ead0a9ab6e800fc35][^c-22ce916de130821de47a899a6b00b17b]

If the Secretary of State certifies that possession of a house which—

- (a) has been acquired or appropriated by a local authority or a non-mayoral combined authority or non-mayoral CCA[^c-c0a4ec02dbbb12a2a90483e6c36b57af] for planning purposes, and
- (b) is for the time being held by the authority for the purposes for which it was acquired or appropriated,

is immediately required for those purposes, nothing in the [^c14025651]Rent Act 1977 or Part I of the [^c14025661]Housing Act 1988 shall prevent the acquiring or appropriating authority from obtaining possession of the house.
