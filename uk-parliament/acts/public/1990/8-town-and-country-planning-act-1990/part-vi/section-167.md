## 167. No withdrawal of constructive notice to treat.

*Extent: E+W*

Without prejudice to the provisions of section 156(1) and (2), a notice to treat which is deemed to have been served by virtue of this Chapter may not be withdrawn under section 31 of the [^c14021971]Land Compensation Act 1961.
