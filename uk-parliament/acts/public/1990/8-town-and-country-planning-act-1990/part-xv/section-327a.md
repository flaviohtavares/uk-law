# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 327A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (816).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 327A. Applications: compliance with requirements[^c18911341]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies to any application in respect of which this Act or any provision made under it imposes a requirement as to—

- (a) the form or manner in which the application must be made;
- (b) the form or content of any document or other matter which accompanies the application.

(2)[^key-bae7222c9ae6be8a6b471a5adb9af8f7][^key-6c76b57ac4235525bfe5820657dcf8a1] The local planning authority must not entertain such an application if it fails to comply with the requirement.
