# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 320

## 320. Local inquiries.[^c14031331][^c14031321]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State or the Welsh Ministers[^key-d222a9eba745fbd4536a218684010ff6] may cause a local inquiry to be held for the purposes of the exercise of any of his or their[^key-7fd762a2ffa7b67816f808e2880ff86b] functions under any of the provisions of this Act.

(2) Subsections (2) to (5) of section 250 of the [^c14031341]Local Government Act 1972 (local inquiries: evidence and costs) apply to an inquiry caused to be held under this section by the Secretary of State; and subsections (2) and (3) of that section apply to an inquiry caused to be held under this section by the Welsh Ministers[^key-b9eb27b195b28f9d88faa29ed2415f5a].

(3)[^key-df54491b3ad9d808792bed61a4d257bd] In its application by subsection (2) to an inquiry caused to be held by the Secretary of State[^key-ffd2ba1f0b519efce939bcc8c3b9a87f], section 250(4) of that Act has effect as if—

- (a) after “the costs incurred by him in relation to the inquiry” there were inserted “ , or such portion of those costs as he may direct, ”, and
- (b) after “the amount of the costs so incurred” there were inserted “ or, where he directs a portion of them to be paid, the amount of that portion ”.
