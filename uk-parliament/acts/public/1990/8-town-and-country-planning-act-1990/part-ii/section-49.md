# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 49

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Disregarding of representations with respect to development authorised by or under other enactments.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 2004-09-28).**

Notwithstanding anything in the previous provisions of this Chapter, neither the Secretary of State nor a local planning authority need consider representations or objections with respect to a local plan or any proposal to alter, [^c14018291]. . .or replace a structure plan or a local plan if it appears to the Secretary of State or, as the case may be, the authority that those representations or objections are in substance representations or objections with respect to things done or proposed to be done in pursuance of—

- (a) an order or scheme under section 10, 14, 16, 18, 106(1) or (3) or 108(1) of the [^c14018301]Highways Act 1980;
- (b) an order or scheme under any provision replaced by the provisions of the Highways Act 1980 mentioned in paragraph (a) (namely, an order or scheme under section 7, 9, 11, 13 or 20 of the [^c14018311]Highways Act 1959, section 3 of the [^c14018321]Highways (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1961 or section 1 or 10 of the [^c14018331]Highways Act 1971);
- (c) an order under section 1 of the [^c14018341]New Towns Act 1981.
