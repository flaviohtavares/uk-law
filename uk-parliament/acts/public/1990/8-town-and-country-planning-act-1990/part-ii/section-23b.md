# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 23B

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23B. Unitary development plans for National Parks in Wales.[^c14017441]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 2005-10-15).**

(1)[^c18931791] A unitary development plan shall be prepared for each National Park in Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]

(2) A Welsh National Park development plan shall relate to an area prescribed in relation to the National Park in question by order made by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]

(3) The prescribed area in relation to a National Park which falls wholly within, but does not comprise the whole of, the area of a single local planning authority shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]

- (a) where the local planning authority have so elected, the whole of the area of the local planning authority; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]
- (b) in any other case— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]
  - (i) the whole of the area of the National Park; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c18931791] a composite area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]

(4) The prescribed area in relation to any other Welsh National Park shall be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]

- (a) the whole of the area of the National Park; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]
- (b) a composite area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]

(5) For the purposes of this section and section 23C, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]**composite area *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]**”, in relation to a National Park, means an area which consists of the whole of the Park together with any one or more other areas in Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]

(6) The Secretary of State shall not under subsection (2) prescribe an area which is a composite area except with the consent of every local planning authority in whose area the prescribed area or any part of it would fall. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]

(7) Any order made by the Secretary of State under subsection (2) may make such saving or transitional provision as he considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]

(8) Where, by an order under subsection (2), the Secretary of State prescribes a composite area which comprises or includes part only of the area of a local planning authority, the provisions of this Chapter shall apply in relation to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]

- (a) the Welsh National Park development plan in question, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]
- (b) any proposals for its alteration or replacement, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]

subject to such modifications, if any, as may be prescribed by the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]

(9) Subsections (3) and (4) of section 10A do not apply for the purposes of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]

- (a) subsection (3) or (8) of this section, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]
- (b) section 23C(1), (2) or (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]

(10) For the purposes of this Act, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]**Welsh National Park development plan *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]**” means a unitary development plan prepared for a National Park in Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931791]
