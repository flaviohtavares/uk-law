# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 21

## 21. Alteration or replacement of unitary development plan.

*Extent: E+W*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

(1)[^c14017191][^c18931711] A local planning authority may at any time prepare proposals— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931711]

- (a) for alterations to the unitary development plan for their area; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931711]
- (b) for its replacement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931711]

(1A) If the Secretary of State directs them to do so, the authority shall prepare, within such time as he may direct, proposals for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931711]

- (a) such alterations to the unitary development plan as he directs; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931711]
- (b) its replacement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931711]

(1B) An authority shall not, without the consent of the Secretary of State, prepare proposals in respect of a unitary development plan if the plan or any part of it has been approved by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931711]

(2) [^c14017201]. . .Sections 12 to 20 (other than subsection (1) of section 12) shall apply in relation to the making of proposals under this section and to any alteration or replacement so proposed as they apply to the preparation of a unitary development plan under section 12 and to a plan prepared under that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931711]

(3) As soon as practicable after— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931711]

- (a) an order has been made under paragraph 5 of Schedule 32 to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931711] [^c14017211]Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 (designation of enterprise zone); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931711]
- (b) a notification has been given under paragraph 11(1) of that Schedule (approval of modification of enterprise zone scheme), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931711]

the local planning authority for an area in which the zone is wholly or partly situated shall review any unitary development plan for that area in the light of the provisions of the scheme or modified scheme under that Schedule and prepare proposals under this section for any consequential alterations to the plan which they consider necessary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18931711]
