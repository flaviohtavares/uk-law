# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 52

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52. Reviews of plans in enterprise zones.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 2004-09-28).**

(1) As soon as practicable after an order has been made under paragraph 5 of Schedule 32 to the [^c14018831]Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 (adoption of enterprise zone scheme) or a notification has been given under paragraph 11 of that Schedule (modification of such a scheme) any local planning authority for an area in which the enterprise zone is wholly or partly situated shall consider whether they need, in the light of the provisions in the scheme or modified scheme, to prepare proposals for the alteration or replacement of any structure or local plan in relation to which they have power to prepare such proposals.[^c14018841]
