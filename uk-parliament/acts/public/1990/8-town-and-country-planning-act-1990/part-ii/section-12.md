# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 12

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Preparation of unitary development plan.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 2005-10-15).**

(1)[^c19212701] The local planning authority shall, within such period (if any) as the Secretary of State may direct, prepare for their area a plan to be known as a unitary development plan. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]

(2) A unitary development plan shall comprise two parts. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]

(3) Part I of a unitary development plan shall consist of a written statement formulating the authority’s general policies in respect of the development and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701] use of land in their area.[^c14016611] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]

(3A)[^c14016621][^c19899712] The policies shall, subject to subsection (3B), include policies in respect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]

- (a) the conservation of the natural beauty and amenity of the land; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]
- (b) the improvement of the physical environment; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]
- (c) the management of traffic. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]

(3B) Regulations under this section may prescribe the aspects of such development and use with which the general policies in Part I of a unitary development plan are to be concerned, in which case the policies shall be concerned with those aspects and no others. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]

(3C)[^c14016631] In the case of a London borough, Part I of the unitary development plan shall be in general conformity with the spatial development strategy for the time being in force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]

(4) Part II of a unitary development plan shall consist of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]

- (a) a written statement formulating in such detail as the authority think appropriate (and so as to be readily distinguishable from the other contents of the plan) their proposals for the development and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701] [^c14016641]. . . use of land in their area *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701] [^c14016641]. . . ;
- (b) a map showing those proposals on a geographical basis; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]
- (c) a reasoned justification of the general policies in Part I of the plan and of the proposals in Part II of it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]
- (d) such diagrams, illustrations or other descriptive or explanatory matter in respect of the general policies in Part I of the plan or the proposals in Part II of it as the authority think appropriate or as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]

(5) A unitary development plan shall also contain such other matters as may be prescribed or as the Secretary of State may in any particular case direct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]

(6)[^c14016661] In formulating the general policies in Part I of a unitary development plan the authority shall have regard to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]

- (a) any regional or strategic planning guidance given by the Secretary of State to assist them in the preparation of the plan; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]
- (b) current national policies; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]
- (c) the resources likely to be available; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]
- (d) such other matters as the Secretary of State may prescribe or, in a particular case, direct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]

(7) The proposals in Part II of a unitary development plan shall be in general conformity with Part I *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701] and, in the case of a London borough council, with the spatial development strategy[^c14016671] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701].

(7A)[^c14016681] In formulating their proposals in Part II of a unitary development plan, the authority shall have regard to such information and other considerations as the Secretary of State may prescribe or, in a particular case, direct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]

(8) Part II of a unitary development plan may designate any part of the authority’s area as an action area, that is to say, an area which they have selected for the commencement during a prescribed period of comprehensive treatment by development, redevelopment or improvement (or partly by one and partly by another method) and if an area is so designated that Part of the plan shall contain a description of the treatment proposed by the authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]

(9) In preparing a unitary development plan the authority shall take into account the provisions of any scheme under paragraph 3 of Schedule 32 to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701] [^c14016691]Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980 relating to land in their area which has been designated under that Schedule as an enterprise zone. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]

(10)[^c14016701] Regulations under this section may make different provision for different cases and shall be subject to any direction given, in a particular case, by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]

(11)[^c14016711] Any provision made by regulations under this section in its application by virtue of section 10 may differ from that made under this section in its application by virtue of section 10A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19212701]
