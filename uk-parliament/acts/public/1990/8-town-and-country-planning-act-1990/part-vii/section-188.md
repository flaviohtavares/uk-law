## 188. Register of enforcement and stop notices and other enforcement action.[^c14023471][^key-bcd6414590135a9162655cb1a414e98d][^c14023461][^key-cf363dbe18af48ec03ebbdcf55fb2ad2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-e4b458095a6ab904c02e70b00237a987] Every district planning authority , every local planning authority for an area in Wales[^c14023481] and the council of every metropolitan district or London borough shall keep, in such manner as may be prescribed by a development order, a register containing such information as may be so prescribed with respect—

- (za)[^key-8dabb5060f5162ebb32abe2ea37568e5] to planning enforcement orders,
- (zb)[^key-551b946225664664bdec3f2b5ec7a421] to enforcement warning notices under section 172ZA (enforcement warning notice: England),
- (a) to enforcement notices; [^c14023491]. . .
- (aa)[^key-f267c4ae903f9a96ceb9c66f8f60bfec] to enforcement warning notices under section 173ZA (enforcement warning notice: Wales)[^key-35627cd096d1f9f377c3a75fc06ddc78],
- (b) to stop notices, and[^c14023501]
- (c) to breach of condition notices

which relate to land in their area.

(2) A development order may make provision—

- (a) for the entry relating to any planning enforcement order,[^key-07c73bba5f0cee5544ebe946453141eb] enforcement notice, enforcement warning notice under section 172ZA or 173ZA[^key-2eb0bbf364dec9c985c5a630d84d5410], stop notice or breach of condition notice[^c14023511], and everything relating to any planning enforcement order or[^key-5938920ab67af72004c8d3e862441644] any such notice, to be removed from the register in such circumstances as may be specified in the development[^key-75da61bcf4becf9e0caaafa160de729a] order; and
- (b) for requiring a county planning authority to supply to a district planning authority such information as may be so specified with regard to enforcement notices and enforcement warning notices under section 172ZA[^key-bd4404f92dec289c5c2703a2c7068fae] issued and stop notices and breach of condition notices[^c14023521] served by , and planning enforcement orders made on applications made by,[^key-594d0b260661dd29946c4f17964e7f35] the county planning authority.

(3) Every register kept under this section shall be available for inspection by the public at all reasonable hours.

(4)[^key-fdea5cd28b7b400e3a1c1c46398cd9c8] In this section “**planning enforcement order**” means an order under section 171BA(1).
