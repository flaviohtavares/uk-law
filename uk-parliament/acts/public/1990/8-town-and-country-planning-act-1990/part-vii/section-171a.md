# Town and Country Planning Act 1990, section 171A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (816).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 171A. Expressions used in connection with enforcement.[^c14022131]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of this Act—

- (a) carrying out development without the required planning permission; or
- (b) failing to comply with any condition or limitation subject to which planning permission has been granted,

constitutes a breach of planning control.

(2) For the purposes of this Act—

- (za)[^key-f0857c194a84aa7e2bd42c90430b2775] the issue of an enforcement warning notice in relation to land in England under section 172ZA;
- (a) the issue of an enforcement notice (defined in section 172);
- (aa)[^key-fdc662844b6e5b837ef1ad66d8623450] the issue of an enforcement warning notice in relation to land in Wales under section 173ZA[^key-7c156eb5d9d779670f67fdf7cb5311be]; or
- (b) the service of a breach of condition notice (defined in section 187A),

constitutes taking enforcement action.

(3) In this Part “**planning permission**” includes permission under Part III of the 1947 Act, of the 1962 Act or of the 1971 Act.
