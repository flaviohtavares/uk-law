# Aviation and Maritime Security Act 1990, section 51

> Text as at 2021-07-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Extension of Act outside the United Kingdom.[^c10458741]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-5120419a79683d50519cbc5d946c0484] Her Majesty may by Order in Council make provision for extending any of the provisions of section 1, Parts II and III and section 50 of this Act with such exceptions, adaptations or modifications as may be specified in the Order, to any of the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man or any colony.

(2) Section 15 of the [^c10458751]Visiting Forces Act 1952, [^c10458761]. . ., section 7 of the [^c10458771]Suppression of Terrorism Act 1978, section 39(3) of the [^c10458781]Aviation Security Act 1982 and section 108 of the [^c10458791]Civil Aviation Act 1982 (extension to Channel Islands, Isle of Man and other countries) apply respectively to the provisions of this Act amending each of those Acts.
