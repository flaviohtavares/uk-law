# Environmental Protection Act 1990, section 15

## 15. Appeals as respects authorisations and against variation, enforcement and prohibition notices.[^c13733061]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] The following persons, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) a person who has been refused the grant of an authorisation under section 6 above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) a person who is aggrieved by the conditions attached, under any provision of this Part, to his authorisation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (c) a person who has been refused a variation of an authorisation on an application under section 11 above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (d) a person whose authorisation has been revoked under section 12 above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

may appeal against the decision of the enforcing authority to the Secretary of State (except where the decision implements a direction of his). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(2) A person on whom a variation notice, an enforcement notice or a prohibition notice is served may appeal against the notice to the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] (except where the notice implements a direction of his).[^c13733071] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0].

(3)[^c13733081] This section is subject to section 114 of the Environment Act 1995 (delegation or reference of appeals *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] etc *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]).

(4) An appeal under this section shall, if and to the extent required by regulations under subsection (10) below, be advertised in such manner as may be prescribed by regulations under that subsection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(5)[^c13733091] Before determining an appeal under this section, the Secretary of State may, if he thinks fit— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) cause the appeal to take or continue in the form of a hearing (which may, if the person hearing the appeal so decides, be held, or held to any extent, in private); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) cause a local inquiry to be held; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

and the Secretary of State shall act as mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b) above if a request is made by either party to the appeal to be heard with respect to the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(6) On determining an appeal against a decision of an enforcing authority under subsection (1) above, the Secretary of State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) may affirm the decision; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) where the decision was a refusal to grant an authorisation or a variation of an authorisation, may direct the enforcing authority to grant the authorisation or to vary the authorisation, as the case may be; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (c) where the decision was as to the conditions attached to an authorisation, may quash all or any of the conditions of the authorisation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (d) where the decision was to revoke an authorisation, may quash the decision; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

and where he exercises any of the powers in paragraphs (b), (c) or (d) above, he may give directions as to the conditions to be attached to the authorisation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(7) On the determination of an appeal under subsection (2) above the Secretary of State may either quash or affirm the notice and, if he affirms it, may do so either in its original form or with such modifications as he may in the circumstances think fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(8) Where an appeal is brought under subsection (1) above against the revocation of an authorisation, the revocation shall not take effect pending the final determination or the withdrawal of the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(9) Where an appeal is brought under subsection (2) above against a notice, the bringing of the appeal shall not have the effect of suspending the operation of the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(10) Provision may be made by the Secretary of State by regulations with respect to appeals under this section and in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) as to the period within which and the manner in which appeals are to be brought; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) as to the manner in which appeals are to be considered. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]and any such regulations may make different provision for different cases or different circumstances.[^c13733101] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
