# Environmental Protection Act 1990, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1. Preliminary.[^c13732021]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] The following provisions have effect for the interpretation of this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(2) The “environment” consists of all, or any, of the following media, namely, the air, water and land; and the medium of air includes the air within buildings and the air within other natural or man-made structures above or below ground. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(3) “**Pollution of the environment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” means pollution of the environment due to the release (into any environmental medium) from any process of substances which are capable of causing harm to man or any other living organisms supported by the environment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(4) “**Harm *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” means harm to the health of living organisms or other interference with the ecological systems of which they form part and, in the case of man, includes offence caused to any of his senses or harm to his property; and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**harmless *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” has a corresponding meaning. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(5) “**Process *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” means any activities carried on in Great Britain, whether on premises or by means of mobile plant, which are capable of causing pollution of the environment and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**prescribed process *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” means a process prescribed under section 2(1) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(6) For the purposes of subsection (5) above— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- “**activities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” means industrial or commercial activities or activities of any other nature whatsoever (including, with or without other activities, the keeping of a substance); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- “**Great Britain *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” includes so much of the adjacent territorial sea as is, or is treated as, relevant territorial waters for the purposes of \[ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0][^c13732031]Part III of the Water Resources Act 1991\] or, as respects Scotland, Part II of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] [^c13732041]Control of Pollution Act 1974; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- “**mobile plant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” means plant which is designed to move or to be moved whether on roads or otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(7) The “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**enforcing authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**”, in relation to England and Wales, is *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] the Environment Agency or the local authority by which[^c13732051] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0], under section 4 below, the functions conferred or imposed by this Part otherwise than on the Secretary of State are for the time being exercisable in relation respectively to releases of substances into the environment or into the air; and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**local enforcing authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” means any such local authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(8)[^c13732061] In relation to Scotland, references to the “enforcing authority” and a “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**local enforcing authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” are references to the Scottish Environment Protection Agency (in this Part referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**SEPA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**”).

(9) “**Authorisation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” means an authorisation for a process (whether on premises or by means of mobile plant) granted under section 6 below; and a reference to the conditions of an authorisation is a reference to the conditions subject to which at any time the authorisation has effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(10) A substance is “released” into any environmental medium whenever it is released directly into that medium whether it is released into it within or outside Great Britain and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**release *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” includes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) in relation to air, any emission of the substance into the air; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) in relation to water, any entry (including any discharge) of the substance into water; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (c) in relation to land, any deposit, keeping or disposal of the substance in or on land; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

and for this purpose “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**water *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**land *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” shall be construed in accordance with subsections (11) and (12) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(11) For the purpose of determining into what medium a substance is released— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) any release into— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (i) the sea or the surface of the seabed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] any river, watercourse, lake, loch or pond (whether natural or artificial or above or below ground) or reservoir or the surface of the riverbed or of other land supporting such waters, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] ground waters, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

  is a release into water; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) any release into— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (i) land covered by water falling outside paragraph (a) above or the water covering such land; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] the land beneath the surface of the seabed or of other land supporting waters falling within paragraph (a)(ii) above, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

  is a release into land; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (c) any release into a sewer (within the meaning of \[ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0][^c13732071]the Water Industry Act 1991\] or, in relation to Scotland, of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] [^c13732081]Sewerage (Scotland) Act 1968) shall be treated as a release into water; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

but a sewer and its contents shall be disregarded in determining whether there is pollution of the environment at any time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(12) In subsection (11) above “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**ground waters *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” means any waters contained in underground strata, or in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) a well, borehole or similar work sunk into underground strata, including any adit or passage constructed in connection with the well, borehole or work for facilitating the collection of water in the well, borehole or work; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) any excavation into underground strata where the level of water in the excavation depends wholly or mainly on water entering it from the strata. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(13) “**Substance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” shall be treated as including electricity or heat and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**prescribed substance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” has the meaning given by section 2(7) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(14)[^c13732091] In this Part “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**the appropriate Agency *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) in relation to England and Wales, the Environment Agency; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) in relation to Scotland, SEPA. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
