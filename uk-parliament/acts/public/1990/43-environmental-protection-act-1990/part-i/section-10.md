# Environmental Protection Act 1990, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/43

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 10. Variation of authorisations by enforcing authority.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] The enforcing authority may at any time, subject to the requirements of section 7 above, and, in cases to which they apply, the requirements of Part II of Schedule 1 to this Act, vary an authorisation and shall do so if it appears to the authority at that time that that section requires conditions to be included which are different from the subsisting conditions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(2) Where the enforcing authority has decided to vary an authorisation under subsection (1) above the authority shall notify the holder of the authorisation and serve a variation notice on him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(3) In this Part a “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**variation notice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” is a notice served by the enforcing authority on the holder of an authorisation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) specifying variations of the authorisation which the enforcing authority has decided to make; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) specifying the date or dates on which the variations are to take effect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

and, unless the notice is withdrawn *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] or is varied under subsection (3A) below[^c13732991] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0], the variations specified in a variation notice shall take effect on the date or dates so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(3A)[^c13733001] An enforcing authority which has served a variation notice may vary that notice by serving on the holder of the authorisation in question a further notice— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) specifying the variations which the enforcing authority has decided to make to the variation notice; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) specifying the date or dates on which the variations specified in the variation notice, as varied by the further notice, are to take effect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

and any reference in this Part to a variation notice, or to a variation notice served under subsection (2) above, includes a reference to such a notice as varied by a further notice served under this subsection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(4) A variation notice served under subsection (2) above shall also— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) require the holder of the authorisation, within such period as may be specified in the notice, to notify the authority what action (if any) he proposes to take to ensure that the process is carried on in accordance with the authorisation as varied by the notice; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b)[^c13733011] require the holder to pay, within such period as may be specified in the notice,— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (i) in a case where the enforcing authority is the Environment Agency or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] SEPA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0], the charge (if any) prescribed for the purpose by a charging scheme under section 41 of the Environment Act 1995; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] in any other case, the fee (if any) prescribed by a scheme under section 8 above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(5) Where in the opinion of the enforcing authority any action to be taken by the holder of an authorisation in consequence of a variation notice served under subsection (2) above will involve a substantial change in the manner in which the process is being carried on, the enforcing authority shall notify the holder of its opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(6) The Secretary of State may, if he thinks fit in relation to authorisations of any description or particular authorisations, direct the enforcing authorities— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) to exercise their powers under this section, or to do so in such circumstances as may be specified in the directions, in such manner as may be so specified; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) not to exercise those powers, or not to do so in such circumstances or such manner as may be so specified; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

and the Secretary of State shall have the corresponding power of direction in respect of the powers of the enforcing authorities to vary authorisations under section 11 below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(7) In this section and section 11 below a “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**substantial change *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**”, in relation to a prescribed process being carried on under an authorisation, means a substantial change in the substances released from the process or in the amount or any other characteristic of any substance so released; and the Secretary of State may give directions to the enforcing authorities as to what does or does not constitute a substantial change in relation to processes generally, any description of process or any particular process. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(8) In this section and section 11 below— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- “**prescribed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” means prescribed in regulations made by the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- “**vary *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**”
  - (a) [^c13733021], in relation to the subsisting conditions or other provisions of an authorisation, means adding to them or varying or rescinding any of them; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] and[^c13733031] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (b) in relation to a variation notice, means adding to, or varying or rescinding the notice or any of its contents; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

  and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**variation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” shall be construed accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
