# Environmental Protection Act 1990, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/43

## 4. Discharge and scope of functions.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] This section determines the authority by whom the functions conferred or imposed by this Part otherwise than on the Secretary of State are exercisable and the purposes for which they are exercisable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(2) Those functions, in their application to prescribed processes designated for central control, shall be functions of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] the appropriate Agency[^c13732331] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0], and shall be exercisable for the purpose of preventing or minimising pollution of the environment due to the release of substances into any environmental medium. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(3) Subject to subsection (4) below, those functions, in their application to prescribed processes designated for local control, shall be functions of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a)[^c13732341] in the case of a prescribed process carried on (or to be carried on) by means of a mobile plant, where the person carrying on the process has his principal place of business— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (i) in England and Wales, the local authority in whose area that place of business is; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] in Scotland, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] SEPA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0];
- (b) in any other cases, where the prescribed processes are (or are to be) carried on— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (i) in England and Wales, the local authority in whose area they are (or are to be) carried on; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] in Scotland, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] SEPA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0];

and the functions applicable to such processes shall be exercisable for the purpose of preventing or minimising pollution of the environment due to the release of substances into the air (but not into any other environmental medium). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(4) The Secretary of State may, as respects the functions under this Part being exercised by a local authority specified in the direction, direct that those functions shall be exercised instead by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] the Environment Agency[^c13732351] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] while the direction remains in force or during a period specified in the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(4A)[^c13732361] In England and Wales, a local authority, in exercising the functions conferred or imposed on it under this Part by virtue of subsection (3) above, shall have regard to the strategy for the time being published pursuant to section 80 of the Environment Act 1995. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(5) A transfer of functions under subsection (4) above to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] the Environment Agency[^c13732371] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] does not make them exercisable by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] that Agency *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] for the purpose of preventing or minimising pollution of the environment due to releases of substances into any other environmental medium than the air. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(6) A direction under subsection (4) above may transfer those functions as exercisable in relation to all or any description of prescribed processes carried on by all or any description of persons (a “general direction”) or in relation to a prescribed process carried on by a specified person (a “specific direction”). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(7) A direction under subsection (4) above may include such saving and transitional provisions as the Secretary of State considers necessary or expedient. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(8) The Secretary of State, on giving or withdrawing a direction under subsection (4) above, shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) in the case of a general direction— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (i) forthwith serve notice of it on *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] the Environment Agency[^c13732391] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] and on the local enforcing authorities affected by the direction; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] cause notice of it to be published as soon as practicable in the London Gazette *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] [^c13732401]. . . and in at least one newspaper circulating in the area of each authority affected by the direction; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) in the case of a specific direction— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (i) forthwith serve notice of it on *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] the Environment Agency *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0], the local enforcing authority and the person carrying on or appearing to the Secretary of State to be carrying on the process affected, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] cause notice of it to be published as soon as practicable in the London Gazette *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] [^c13732401]. . . and in at least one newspaper circulating in the authority’s area; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

  and any such notice shall specify the date at which the direction is to take (or took) effect and (where appropriate) its duration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(8A)[^c13732431] The requirements of sub-paragraph (ii) of paragraph (a) or, as the case may be, of paragraph (b) of subsection (8) above shall not apply in any case where, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, the publication of notice in accordance with that sub-paragraph would be contrary to the interests of national security. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(8B) Subsections (4) to (8A) above shall not apply to Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(9)[^c13732441] It shall be the duty of local authorities to follow such developments in technology and techniques for preventing or reducing pollution of the environment due to releases of substances from prescribed processes as concern releases into the air of substances from prescribed processes designated for local control. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(10) It shall be the duty of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] the Environment Agency,[^c13732451] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] SEPA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] and the local enforcing authorities to give effect to any directions given to them under any provision of this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(11) In this Part “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” means, subject to subsection (12) below— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) in Greater London, a London borough council, the Common Council of the City of London, the Sub-Treasurer of the Inner Temple and the Under Treasurer of the Middle Temple; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) in England[^c13732461] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] [^c13732461]. . .,outside Greater London, a district council and the Council of the Isles of Scilly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] [^c13732481]. . .
- (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^c13732491][^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] in Wales, a county council or county borough council; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (c)[^c13732481] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(12) Where, by an order under section 2 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] [^c13732511]Public Health (Control of Disease) Act 1984, a port health authority has been constituted for any port health district, the port health authority shall have by virtue of this subsection, as respects its district, the functions conferred or imposed by this Part and no such order shall be made assigning those functions; and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**area *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” shall be construed accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
