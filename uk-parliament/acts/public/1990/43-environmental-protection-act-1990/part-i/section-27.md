## 27. Power of chief inspector to remedy harm.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] Where the commission of an offence under section 23(1)(a) or (c) above causes any harm which it is possible to remedy, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] the appropriate Agency[^c13733441] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] may, subject to subsection (2) below— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) arrange for any reasonable steps to be taken towards remedying the harm; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) recover the cost of taking those steps from any person convicted of that offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(2) The Environment Agency or[^c13733451] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] SEPA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0], as the case may be, shall not exercise its *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] powers under this section except with the approval in writing of the Secretary of State and, where any of the steps are to be taken on or will affect land in the occupation of any person other than the person on whose land the prescribed process is being carried on, with the permission of that person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
