# Environmental Protection Act 1990, section 6

## 6. Authorisations: general provisions.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c13732551][^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] No person shall carry on a prescribed process after the date prescribed or determined for that description of process by or under regulations under section 2(1) above (but subject to any transitional provision made by the regulations) except under an authorisation granted by the enforcing authority and in accordance with the conditions to which it is subject. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(2) An application for an authorisation shall be made to the enforcing authority in accordance with Part I of Schedule 1 to this Act and shall be accompanied by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a)[^c13732561] in a case where, by virtue of section 41 of the Environment Act 1995, a charge prescribed by a charging scheme under that section is required to be paid to the appropriate Agency in respect of the application, the charge so prescribed; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) in any other case, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] the fee prescribed under section 8(2)(a) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(3) Where an application is duly made to the enforcing authority, the authority shall either grant the authorisation subject to the conditions required or authorised to be imposed by section 7 below or refuse the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(4) An application shall not be granted unless the enforcing authority considers that the applicant will be able to carry on the process so as to comply with the conditions which would be included in the authorisation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(5) The Secretary of State may, if he thinks fit in relation to any application for an authorisation, give to the enforcing authority directions as to whether or not the authority should grant the authorisation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(6) Subject to subsection (6A) below,[^c13732571] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] the enforcing authority shall, as respects each authorisation in respect of which it has functions under this Part, from time to time but not less frequently than once in every period of four years, carry out a review of the conditions of the authorisation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(6A)[^c13732581] Subsection (6) above shall not require a review of the conditions of an authorisation to be carried out if– *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef46bc5c0b0063c80519fe389c7b1a75] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ef46bc5c0b0063c80519fe389c7b1a75][^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] the prescribed process covered by the authorisation is carried on in a new Part A installation or by means of a new Part A mobile plant; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef46bc5c0b0063c80519fe389c7b1a75] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ef46bc5c0b0063c80519fe389c7b1a75][^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] the prescribed process covered by the authorisation is carried on in an existing Part A installation or by means of an existing Part A mobile plant and the review would be carried out within the period of two years ending at the beginning of the relevant period for that installation or mobile plant; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef46bc5c0b0063c80519fe389c7b1a75] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ef46bc5c0b0063c80519fe389c7b1a75][^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] the prescribed process covered by the authorisation is carried on in an existing Part B installation or by means of an existing Part B mobile plant and the review would be carried out within the two year period ending on the relevant date for that installation or mobile plant. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef46bc5c0b0063c80519fe389c7b1a75] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

Subsection (6) does not require a review of the conditions of an authorisation to be carried out if the prescribed process covered by the authorisation is— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] a Part A activity, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] a Part B activity, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] a solvent emission activity, or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (d (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] carried out using mobile plant. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(6B) In subsection (6A) above, “new Part A installation”, “existing Part A installation”, “new Part A mobile plant”, “existing Part A mobile plant”, “relevant period”, “existing Part B installation”, “existing Part B mobile plant” and “relevant date” have the meanings given in Schedule 3 to the Pollution Prevention and Control (Scotland) Regulations 2000. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ef46bc5c0b0063c80519fe389c7b1a75] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

In subsection (6A), “Part A activity”, “Part B activity”, “solvent emission activity” and “mobile plant” have the meanings given in the Pollution Prevention and Control (Scotland) Regulations 2012. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(7) The Secretary of State may, by regulations, substitute for the period for the time being specified in subsection (6) above such other period as he thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(8) Schedule 1 to this Act (supplementary provisions) shall have effect in relation to authorisations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
