# Environmental Protection Act 1990, section 26

## 26. Power of court to order cause of offence to be remedied.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] Where a person is convicted of an offence under section 23(1)(a) or (c) above in respect of any matters which appear to the court to be matters which it is in his power to remedy, the court may, in addition to or instead of imposing any punishment, order him, within such time as may be fixed by the order, to take such steps as may be specified in the order for remedying those matters. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(2) The time fixed by an order under subsection (1) above may be extended or further extended by order of the court on an application made before the end of the time as originally fixed or as extended under this subsection, as the case may be. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(3) Where a person is ordered under subsection (1) above to remedy any matters, that person shall not be liable under section 23 above in respect of those matters in so far as they continue during the time fixed by the order or any further time allowed under subsection (2) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
