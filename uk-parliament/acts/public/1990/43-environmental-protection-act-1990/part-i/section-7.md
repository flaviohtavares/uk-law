## 7. Conditions of authorisations.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] There shall be included in an authorisation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) subject to paragraph (b) below, such specific conditions as the enforcing authority considers appropriate, when taken with the general condition implied by subsection (4) below, for achieving the objectives specified in subsection (2) below; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) such conditions as are specified in directions given by the Secretary of State under subsection (3) below; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (c) such other conditions (if any) as appear to the enforcing authority to be appropriate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

but no conditions shall be imposed for the purpose only of securing the health of persons at work (within the meaning of Part I of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] [^c13732711]Health and Safety at Work *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] etc. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] Act 1974). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(2) Those objectives are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) ensuring that, in carrying on a prescribed process, the best available techniques not entailing excessive cost will be used— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (i) for preventing the release of substances prescribed for any environmental medium into that medium or, where that is not practicable by such means, for reducing the release of such substances to a minimum and for rendering harmless any such substances which are so released; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] for rendering harmless any other substances which might cause harm if released into any environmental medium; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) compliance with any directions by the Secretary of State given for the implementation of any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] assimilated[^c-40255a1f2bc178ba938fab90676e6503][^c-66cff26a75880730682ec58b2d22db0f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] obligations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] or international law relating to environmental protection; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (c) compliance with any limits or requirements and achievement of any quality standards or quality objectives prescribed by the Secretary of State under any of the relevant enactments; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (d) compliance with any requirements applicable to the grant of authorisations specified by or under a plan made by the Secretary of State under section 3(5) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(3) Except as respects the general condition implied by subsection (4) below, the Secretary of State may give directions to the enforcing authorities as to the conditions which are, or are not, to be included in all authorisations, in authorisations of any specified description or in any particular authorisation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(4) Subject to subsections (5) and (6) below, there is implied in every authorisation a general condition that, in carrying on the process to which the authorisation applies, the person carrying it on must use the best available techniques not entailing excessive cost— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) for preventing the release of substances prescribed for any environmental medium into that medium or, where that is not practicable by such means, for reducing the release of such substances to a minimum and for rendering harmless any such substances which are so released; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) for rendering harmless any other substances which might cause harm if released into any environmental medium. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(5) In the application of subsections (1) to (4) above to authorisations granted by a local enforcing authority references to the release of substances into any environmental medium are to be read as references to the release of substances into the air. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(6) The obligation implied by virtue of subsection (4) above shall not apply in relation to any aspect of the process in question which is regulated by a condition imposed under subsection (1) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(7) The objectives referred to in subsection (2) above shall, where the process— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) is one designated for central control; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) is likely to involve the release of substances into more than one environmental medium; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

include the objective of ensuring that the best available techniques not entailing excessive cost will be used for minimising the pollution which may be caused to the environment taken as a whole by the releases having regard to the best practicable environmental option available as respects the substances which may be released. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(8) An authorisation for carrying on a prescribed process may, without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1) above, include conditions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) imposing limits on the amount or composition of any substance produced by or utilised in the process in any period; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b) requiring advance notification of any proposed change in the manner of carrying on the process. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(9) This section has effect subject to section 28 below *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] [^c13732721]. . .

(10) References to the best available techniques not entailing excessive cost, in relation to a process, include (in addition to references to any technical means and technology) references to the number, qualifications, training and supervision of persons employed in the process and the design, construction, lay-out and maintenance of the buildings in which it is carried on. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(11) It shall be the duty of enforcing authorities to have regard to any guidance issued to them by the Secretary of State for the purposes of the application of subsections (2) and (7) above as to the techniques and environmental options that are appropriate for any description of prescribed process. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

(12) In subsection (2) above “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**the relevant enactments *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]**” are any enactments or instruments contained in or made for the time being under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]

- (a) section 2 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] [^c13732731]Clean Air Act 1968; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (b)[^c-a2c87abe86b46673b8ec3722e0c047d4] assimilated[^c-3785a3d4555f74ec8213b7a283b02309] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] law; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (c) Part I of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] [^c13732751]Health and Safety at Work *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] etc. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] Act 1974; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (d) Parts II, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] III *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] IV *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] [^c13732761]Control of Pollution Act 1974; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (e)[^c13732771] the Water Resources Act 1991; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19013181] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (f) section 3 of this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] ;[^c13732781] and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19013181] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
- (g) section 87 of the Environment Act 1995. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0] and[^c19013191] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5d0e4a05a380ee60ef9ba9e27d5c83c0]
