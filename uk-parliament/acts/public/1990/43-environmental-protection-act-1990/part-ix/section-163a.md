# Environmental Protection Act 1990, section 163A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 163A. Application of Part VI: England and Wales[^c17542111]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c17542181] The amendments made to the provisions of Part VI by the 2002 Regulations, other than the amendment of section 127(2) as it relates to the continental shelf, have effect in relation to England only, and accordingly, in the application of that Part in relation to Wales, the provisions listed in subsection (2) below continue to have effect without the amendments made by the 2002 Regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c17542181]

(2) The provisions referred to in subsection (1) above are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c17542181]

- (a) section 106(1) and (4) to (6); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c17542181]
- (b) section 107(2), (3), (6), (9) and (11); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c17542181]
- (c) section 111(6); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c17542181]
- (d) section 112(1) and (5); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c17542181]
- (e) section 119(1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c17542181]
- (f) section 123(7); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c17542181]
- (g) section 127(2) in so far as it relates to the territorial sea. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c17542181]

(3) In this section “the 2002 Regulations” means the Genetically Modified Organisms (Deliberate Release) Regulations 2002. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c17542181]
