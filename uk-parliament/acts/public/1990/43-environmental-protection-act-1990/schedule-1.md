## SCHEDULE 1 — AUTHORISATIONS FOR PROCESSES: SUPPLEMENTARY PROVISIONS

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 6.

### Part I — Grant of Authorisations

#### Applications for authorisations

**1.** (1)[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6] An application to the enforcing authority for an authorisation must contain such information, and be made in such manner, as may be prescribed in regulations made by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

(2)[^c13751881] An application to the enforcing authority for an authorisation must also, unless regulations made by the Secretary of State exempt applications of that class, be advertised in such manner as may be prescribed in regulations so made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

(3) The enforcing authority may, by notice in writing to the applicant, require him to furnish such further information specified in the notice, within the period so specified, as the authority may require for the purpose of determining the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

(4) If a person fails to furnish any information required under sub-paragraph (3) above within the period specified thereunder the enforcing authority may refuse to proceed with the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

(5) Regulations under this paragraph may make different provision for different classes of applications. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

#### Determination of applications (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

**2.**[^c13751911] (1)[^c13751921] Subject to sub-paragraph (2) below, the enforcing authority shall give notice of any application for an authorisation, enclosing a copy of the application, to the persons who are prescribed or directed to be consulted under this paragraph and shall do so within the specified period for notification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

(2) The Secretary of State may, by regulations, exempt any class of application from the requirements of this paragraph or exclude any class of information contained in applications for authorisations from those requirements, in all cases or as respects specified classes only of persons to be consulted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

(3) Any representations made by the persons so consulted within the period allowed shall be considered by the enforcing authority in determining the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

(4) For the purposes of sub-paragraph (1) above— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

- (a) persons are prescribed to be consulted on any description of application for an authorisation if they are persons specified for the purposes of applications of that description in regulations made by the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]
- (b) persons are directed to be consulted on any particular application if the Secretary of State specifies them in a direction given to the enforcing authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

and the “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]**specified period for notification *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]**” is the period specified in the regulations or in the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

(5) Any representations made by any other persons within the period allowed shall also be considered by the enforcing authority in determining the application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

(6) Subject to sub-paragraph (7) below, the period allowed for making representations is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

- (a) in the case of persons prescribed or directed to be consulted, the period of twenty-eight days beginning with the date on which notice of the application was given under sub-paragraph (1) above, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]
- (b) in the case of other persons, the period of twenty-eight days beginning with the date on which the making of the application was advertised in pursuance of paragraph 1(2) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

(7) The Secretary of State may, by order, substitute for the period for the time being specified in sub-paragraph (6)(a) or (b) above, such other period as he considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

**3.**[^c13751981] (1) The Secretary of State may give directions to the enforcing authority requiring that any particular application or any class of applications for an authorisation shall be transmitted to him for determination pending a further direction under sub-paragraph (5) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

(2) The enforcing authority shall inform the applicant of the fact that his application is being transmitted to the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

(3) Where an application for an authorisation is referred to him under sub-paragraph (1) above the Secretary of State may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

- (a) cause a local inquiry to be held in relation to the application; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]
- (b) afford the applicant and the authority concerned an opportunity of appearing before and being heard by a person appointed by the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

and he shall exercise one of the powers under this sub-paragraph in any case where, in the manner prescribed by regulations made by the Secretary of State, a request is made to be heard with respect to the application by the applicant or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6] the enforcing authority[^c13751991] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6] concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

(4) Subsections (2) to (5) of section 250 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6] [^c13752001]Local Government Act 1972 (supplementary provisions about local inquiries under that section) or, in relation to Scotland, subsections (2) to (8) of section 210 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6] [^c13752011]Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 (which make similar provision) shall, without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1) of either of those sections, apply to inquiries in pursuance of sub-paragraph (3) above as they apply to inquiries in pursuance of either of those sections and, in relation to England and Wales, as if the reference to a local authority in subsection (4) of the said section 250 included a reference to the enforcing authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

(5) The Secretary of State shall, on determining any application transferred to him under this paragraph, give to the enforcing authority such a direction as he thinks fit as to whether it is to grant the application and, if so, as to the conditions that are to be attached to the authorisation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

**4.** The Secretary of State may give the enforcing authority a direction with respect to any particular application or any class of applications for an authorisation requiring the authority not to determine or not to proceed with the application or applications of that class until the expiry of any such period as may be specified in the direction, or until directed by the Secretary of State that they may do so, as the case may be. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

**5.** (1)[^c13752021] Except in a case where an application has been referred to the Secretary of State under paragraph 3 above and subject to sub-paragraph (3) below, the enforcing authority shall determine an application for an authorisation within the period of four months beginning with the day on which it received the application or within such longer period as may be agreed with the applicant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

(2) If the enforcing authority fails to determine an application for an authorisation within the period allowed by or under this paragraph the application shall, if the applicant notifies the authority in writing that he treats the failure as such, be deemed to have been refused at the end of that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]

(3) The Secretary of State may, by order, substitute for the period for the time being specified in sub-paragraph (1) above such other period as he considers appropriate and different periods may be substituted for different classes of application. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-603f5522887ecd2046825b955bfaf2f6]
