# Environmental Protection Act 1990, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/43

## 44. Offences of making false or misleading statements or false entries.[^c20529021][^c13737551][^key-91aea72ab13f67fe449c29db8a1b9831]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-91aea72ab13f67fe449c29db8a1b9831]

- (a) in purported compliance with a requirement to furnish any information imposed by or under any provision of this Part, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-91aea72ab13f67fe449c29db8a1b9831] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494681] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-91aea72ab13f67fe449c29db8a1b9831] will need corrections *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-91aea72ab13f67fe449c29db8a1b9831]
- (b) for the purpose of obtaining for himself or another any grant of a licence, any modification of the conditions of a licence, any acceptance of the surrender of a licence or any transfer of a licence, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19541861] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-91aea72ab13f67fe449c29db8a1b9831]

makes a statement which he knows to be false or misleading in a material particular, or recklessly makes any statement which is false or misleading in a material particular, commits an offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-91aea72ab13f67fe449c29db8a1b9831]

(2) A person who intentionally makes a false entry in any record required to be kept by virtue of a licence commits an offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19541891] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-91aea72ab13f67fe449c29db8a1b9831]

(3) A person who commits an offence under this section shall be liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-91aea72ab13f67fe449c29db8a1b9831]

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-91aea72ab13f67fe449c29db8a1b9831]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or to both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-91aea72ab13f67fe449c29db8a1b9831]
