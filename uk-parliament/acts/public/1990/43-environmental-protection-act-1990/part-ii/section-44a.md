## 44A. National waste strategy: England and Wales.[^c13737571][^c13737561]

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] The Secretary of State shall as soon as possible prepare a statement (“ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]**the strategy *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]**”) containing his policies in relation to the recovery and disposal of waste in England and Wales. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] The strategy shall consist of or include— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] a statement which relates to the whole of England and Wales; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] Without prejudice to the generality of what may be included in the strategy, the strategy must include— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] a statement of the Secretary of State’s policies for attaining the objectives specified in Schedule 2A to this Act; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] provisions relating to each of the following, that is to say— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]
  - (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] the type, quantity and origin of waste to be recovered or disposed of; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] general technical requirements; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]
  - (iii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] any special requirements for particular wastes. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] Without prejudice to any power to give directions conferred by section 40 of the Environment Act 1995, the Secretary of State may give directions to the Environment Agency requiring it— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]

- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] to carry out a survey of or investigation into— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]
  - (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] the kinds or quantities of waste which it appears to that Agency is likely to be situated in England and Wales, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] the facilities which are or appear to that Agency likely to be available or needed in England and Wales for recovering or disposing of any such waste, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]
  - (iii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] any other matter upon which the Secretary of State wishes to be informed in connection with his preparation of the strategy or any modification of it, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] A direction under subsection (6)(b) above— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]

- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] may make provision in relation to the manner in which— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]
  - (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2] the survey or investigation is to be carried out, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-de10a1ca4cd53435ef03fd9ade668ee2]
