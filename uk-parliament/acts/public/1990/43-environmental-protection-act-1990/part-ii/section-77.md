## 77. Transition from Control of Pollution Act 1974 to this Part.[^c13739631][^c19496451]

*Extent: E+W+S*

[^key-3da51a7ccee498b45f10d3dca8d46bed]

(1) This section has effect for the purposes of the transition from the provisions of Part I of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451] [^c13739641]Control of Pollution Act 1974 (“the 1974 Act”) to the corresponding provisions of this Part of this Act and in this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]

- “**existing disposal licence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]**” means a disposal licence under section 5 of the 1974 Act subsisting on the day appointed under section 164(3) below for the repeal of sections 3 to 10 of the 1974 Act and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]**relevant appointed day for licences *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]**” shall be construed accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]
- “[^c18970121]**existing disposal plan *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970121] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]**” means a plan under section 2 of the 1974 Act subsisting on the day appointed under section 164(3) below for the repeal of that section and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970121] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]**relevant appointed day for plans *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970121] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]**” shall be construed accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970121] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]
- “**relevant part of its undertaking *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970121] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]**”, in relation to an existing disposal authority, has the same meaning as in section 32 above; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970121] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]
- “**the vesting date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970121] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]**”, in relation to an existing disposal authority and its waste disposal contractors, means the vesting date under Schedule 2 to this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970121] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]

(6) Subject to subsection (7) below, as respects any existing disposal authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970161] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]

  - (i) in the case of an authority which transfers the relevant part of its undertaking in accordance with a scheme under Schedule 2 to this Act, until the date which is the vesting date for that authority; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970161] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c18970161][^c19496451] in any other case, until the date on which the authority transfers, or ceases itself to carry on, the relevant part of its undertaking or ceases to provide places at which and plant and equipment by means of which controlled waste can be disposed of or deposited for the purposes of disposal; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970161] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]
- (b) on and after that date, section 14(4) of the 1974 Act shall not authorise the authority to arrange for the disposal of controlled waste except by means of arrangements made (in accordance with Part II of Schedule 2 to this Act) with waste disposal contractors. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970161] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]

(7) The Secretary of State may, as respects any existing disposal authority, direct that the restriction imposed by section 51(1) above shall not apply in the case of that authority until such date as he specifies in the direction and where he does so paragraph (a) of subsection (6) above shall not apply and paragraph (b) shall be read as referring to the date so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970161] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]

(8) In section 14(4) of the 1974 Act, after the words “this subsection”, there shall be inserted the words “but subject to subsection (6) of section 77 of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 as respects any time after the date applicable to the authority under paragraph (a) or (b) of that subsection”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970161] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]

(9) As respects any existing disposal authority, until the date which is, under subsection (6)(a) above, the date until which the restriction imposed by section 51(1) of this Act is disapplied,— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970161] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]

- (a) the powers conferred on a waste disposal authority by section 55(2)(a) and (b) of this Act as respects the recycling of waste and the use of waste to produce heat or electricity shall be treated as powers which the authority may exercise itself; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970161] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]
- (b) the power conferred on a waste disposal authority by section 48(4) of this Act to object to a waste collection authority having waste recycled where the disposal authority has made arrangements with a waste disposal contractor for the contractor to recycle the waste shall be available to the waste disposal authority where it itself has the waste recycled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18970161] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496451]
