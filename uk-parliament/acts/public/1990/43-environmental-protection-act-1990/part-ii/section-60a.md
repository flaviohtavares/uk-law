# Environmental Protection Act 1990, section 60A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/43

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 60A. Waste reduction schemes[^c19922921]

*Extent: E+W*

A waste collection authority whose area is in England may make a waste reduction scheme in accordance with Schedule 2AA to this Act.
