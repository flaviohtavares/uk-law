# Environmental Protection Act 1990, section 44B

## 44B. National waste strategy: Scotland.[^c13737621][^c13737631]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c19494811] SEPA shall as soon as possible prepare a statement (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]**the strategy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]**”) containing its policies in relation to the recovery and disposal of waste in Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

(2) SEPA may from time to time modify the strategy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

(3) Without prejudice to the generality of what may be included in the strategy, the strategy must include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

- (a) a statement of SEPA’s policies for attaining the objectives specified in Schedule 2A to this Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
- (b) provisions relating to each of the following, that is to say— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
  - (i) the type, quantity and origin of waste to be recovered or disposed of; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19494811] general technical requirements; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19494811] any special requirements for particular wastes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

(4) In preparing the strategy or any modification of it SEPA shall consult— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

- (a) such bodies or persons appearing to it to be representative of the interests of industry as it may consider appropriate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
- (b) such local authorities as appear to it to be likely to be affected by the strategy or modification, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

and may consult such other bodies or persons as it considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

(5) Without prejudice to any power to give directions conferred by section 40 of the Environment Act 1995, the Secretary of State may give directions to SEPA— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

- (a) as to the policies which are to be included in the strategy; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
- (b) requiring it to carry out a survey or investigation into— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
  - (i) the kinds or quantities of waste which it appears to it is likely to be situated in Scotland, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19494811] the facilities which are or appear to it likely to be available or needed in Scotland for recovering or disposing of any such waste, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19494811] any other matter which the Secretary of State considers appropriate in connection with its preparation of the strategy or any modifications of it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

(6) A direction under subsection (5)(b) above— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

- (a) shall specify or describe the matters or the areas which are to be the subject of the survey or investigation; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
- (b) may make provision in relation to the manner in which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
  - (i) the survey or investigation is to be carried out, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19494811] the findings are to be reported or made available to other persons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

(7) Where a direction is given under subsection (5)(b) above SEPA shall, in accordance with any requirement of the direction— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

- (a) before carrying out the survey or investigation, consult— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
  - (i) such bodies or persons appearing to it to be representative of planning authorities, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19494811] such bodies or persons appearing to it to be representative of the interests of industry, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

  as it may consider appropriate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
- (b) make its findings available to those authorities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

(8) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

- “**planning authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]**” means an authority within the meaning of section 172 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811] [^c13737641]Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
- “**strategy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]**” includes the strategy as modified from time to time and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]**statement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]**” shall be construed accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

(9) This section makes provision for the purpose of implementing Article 7 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811] [^c13737651]directive of the Council of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811] European Union[^key-77aef96c5ffbbbe18a32622b5b3a935c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811] dated 15th July 1975 on waste, as amended by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]

- (a) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811] [^c13737661]directive of that Council dated 18th March 1991 amending directive *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811] [75/442/EEC *(affected by repeal, see note)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1975/442)[^c19494811] on waste; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
- (b) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811] [^c13737671]directive of that Council dated 23rd December 1991 standardising and rationalising reports on the implementation of certain Directives relating to the environment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19494811]
