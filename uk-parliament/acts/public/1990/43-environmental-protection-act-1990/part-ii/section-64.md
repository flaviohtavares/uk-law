# Environmental Protection Act 1990, section 64

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 64. Public registers.[^c13738861]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c19496421] Subject to sections 65 and 66 below, it shall be the duty of each waste regulation authority to maintain a register containing prescribed particulars of or relating to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]

- (a) current or recently current licences (“licences”) granted by the authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]
- (b) current or recently current applications to the authority for licences; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]
- (c) applications made to the authority under section 37 above for the modification of licences; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]
- (d) notices issued by the authority under section 37 above effecting the modification of licences; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]
- (e) notices issued by the authority under section 38 above effecting the revocation or suspension of licences or imposing requirements on the holders of licences; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]
- (f) appeals under section 43 above relating to decisions of the authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]
- (g) certificates of completion issued by the authority under section 39(9) above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]
- (h) notices issued by the authority imposing requirements on the holders of licences under section 42(5) above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]
- (i) convictions of the holders of licences granted by the authority for any offence under this Part (whether in relation to a licence so granted or not); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]
- (j) the occasions on which the authority has discharged any function under section 42 or 61 above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]
- (k) directions given to the authority under any provision of this Part by the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]
- (l)[^c13738871] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (m) such matters relating to the treatment, keeping or disposal of waste in the area of the authority or any pollution of the environment caused thereby as may be prescribed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]

and any other document or information required to be kept in the register under any provision of this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]

(2) Where information of any description is excluded from any register by virtue of section 66 below, a statement shall be entered in the register indicating the existence of information of that description. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]

(2A)[^c13738881] The Secretary of State may give to a waste regulation authority directions requiring the removal from any register of its of any specified information not prescribed for inclusion under subsection (1) above or which, by virtue of section 65 or 66 below, ought to be excluded from the register. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]

(3) For the purposes of subsection (1) above licences are “recently” current for the period of twelve months after they cease to be in force and applications for licences are “recently” current if they relate to a licence which is current or recently current or, in the case of an application which is rejected, for the period of twelve months beginning with the date on which the waste regulation authority gives notice of rejection or, as the case may be, on which the application is deemed by section 36(9) above to have been rejected. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]

(4) It shall be the duty of each waste collection authority in England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421] or Wales[^c13738891] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]. . . to maintain a register containing prescribed particulars of such information contained in any register maintained under subsection (1) above as relates to the treatment, keeping or disposal of controlled waste in the area of the authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]

(5)[^c13738911] The waste regulation authority in relation to England and Wales shall furnish any waste collection authorities in its area with the particulars necessary to enable them to discharge their duty under subsection (4) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]

(6) Each waste regulation authority and waste collection authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]

- (a)[^c13738921]shall secure that any register maintained under this section is open to inspection *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421] [^c13738931]. . . by members of the public free of charge at all reasonable hours and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]
- (b)shall afford to members of the public reasonable facilities for obtaining, on payment of reasonable charges, copies of entries in the register *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]

and, for the purposes of this subsection, places may be prescribed by the Secretary of State at which any such registers or facilities as are mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b) above are to be available or afforded to the public in pursuance of the paragraph in question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]

(7) Registers under this section may be kept in any form. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]

(8) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]**prescribed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]**” means prescribed in regulations by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19496421]
