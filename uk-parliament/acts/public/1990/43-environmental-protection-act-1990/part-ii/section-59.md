# Environmental Protection Act 1990, section 59

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Powers to require removal of waste unlawfully deposited.[^c19495211][^key-bac9570ce070d78b333bd9284ecf1231]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) If any controlled waste or extractive waste[^c20582261] is deposited in or on any land in the area of a waste regulation authority or waste collection authority in contravention of section 33(1) above or regulation 12 of[^c19496041] the Environmental Permitting Regulations[^c20957931], the authority may, by notice served on him, require the occupier to do either or both of the following, that is—

- (a) to remove the waste from the land within a specified period not less than a period of twenty-one days beginning with the service of the notice;
- (b) to take within such a period specified steps with a view to eliminating or reducing the consequences of the deposit of the waste.

*Extent: S*

(1A)[^key-fc7ab9fac75815b3ef05ffb1335eef57] A waste regulation authority or waste collection authority may only impose a requirement under subsection (1) on an occupier of land if the authority is satisfied that the occupier—

- (a) deposited the waste, or
- (b) knowingly caused or knowingly permitted the deposit of the waste.

(2) A person on whom any requirements are imposed under subsection (1) above may, within the period of twenty-one days mentioned in that subsection, appeal against the requirement to a magistrates’ court or, in Scotland, to the sheriff by way of summary application *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6e1cf8f3e682049018bc5afdf9549ca6]the Scottish Ministers *(Extent: S)*.

(3) On any appeal under subsection (2) above the court or the Scottish Ministers (as the case may be) *(Extent: S)*[^key-e122016e6900aa7ea91addf92117838f] shall quash the requirement if it is or they are *(Extent: S)*[^key-4f56c42cb3fb42d32d6e46245627a8f9] satisfied that—

- (a) the appellant neither deposited nor knowingly caused nor knowingly permitted the deposit of the waste; or
- (b) there is a material defect in the notice;

and in any other case shall either modify the requirement or dismiss the appeal.

(4) Where a person appeals against any requirement imposed under subsection (1) above, the requirement shall be of no effect pending the determination of the appeal; and where the court or the Scottish Ministers (as the case may be) *(Extent: S)*[^key-75f3e712e247ac4691b65f2908aee42e] modifies the requirement or dismisses the appeal it or they *(Extent: S)*[^key-810abf733f609a42dea4025112907bba] may extend the period specified in the notice.

(5) If a person on whom a requirement imposed under subsection (1) above fails, without reasonable excuse, to comply with the requirement he shall be liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale and to a further fine of an amount equal to one-tenth of level 5 on the standard scale *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-31b39cbe91a85267c04c414ff6e1aacf] one-tenth of the greater of £5,000 or level 4 on the standard scale *(Extent: E+W)* for each day on which the failure continues after conviction of the offence and before the authority has begun to exercise its powers under subsection (6) below.

(6) Where a person on whom a requirement has been imposed under subsection (1) above by an authority fails to comply with the requirement the authority may do what that person was required to do and may recover from him any expenses reasonably incurred by the authority in doing it.

(7) If it appears to a waste regulation authority or waste collection authority that waste has been deposited in or on any land in contravention of section 33(1) above or regulation 12 of[^c19496091] the Environmental Permitting Regulations, and that—

- (a) in order to remove or prevent pollution of land, water or air or harm to human health it is necessary that the waste be forthwith removed or other steps taken to eliminate or reduce the consequences of the deposit or both; or
- (b) there is no occupier of the land or the occupier cannot be found without the authority incurring unreasonable expense[^c18970431]; or
- (c) the occupier neither made nor knowingly permitted the deposit of the waste;

the authority may remove the waste from the land or take other steps to eliminate or reduce the consequences of the deposit or, as the case may require, to remove the waste and take those steps.

(8) Where an authority exercises any of the powers conferred on it by subsection (7) above it shall be entitled to recover the cost incurred by it in removing the waste or taking the steps or both and in disposing of the waste—

- (a) in a case falling within subsection (7)(a) above, from the occupier of the land unless he proves that he neither made nor knowingly caused nor knowingly permitted the deposit of the waste;
- (b) in any case, from any person who deposited or knowingly caused or knowingly permitted the deposit of any of the waste;

except such of the cost as the occupier or that person shows was incurred unnecessarily.

(8A)[^c18970361] An authority may not recover costs under subsection (8) above if a compensation order (within the meaning given by section 133 of the Sentencing Code) has been made[^key-4cdef5c2024b7df5ebc146e7a72bac29] in favour of the authority in respect of any part of those costs.

(8B) Subsection (8A) does not apply if the order is set aside on appeal.

*Extent: S*

(8C)[^key-4b56f872939e69b9ed67b959ab3119af] An authority may not recover costs under subsection (8) above if a compensation order has been made under section 249 of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995 in favour of the authority in respect of any part of those costs.

*Extent: S*

(8D) Subsection (8C) does not apply if the compensation order is set aside on appeal.

(9) Any waste removed by an authority under subsection (7) above shall belong to that authority and may be dealt with accordingly.

*Extent: S*

(10)[^key-74ef85ae4c384699a6c537f6f87097ec] The Scottish Ministers may by regulations make further provision about appeals to them under subsection (2), including—

- (a) provision about the manner in which appeals are to be brought,
- (b) provision about the manner in which appeals are to be considered,
- (c) transitional, transitory or saving provision.

*Extent: S*

(11) The Scottish Ministers may issue guidance for waste regulation authorities and waste collection authorities on the operation of this section.

*Extent: S*

(12) Waste regulation authorities and waste collection authorities must have regard to any guidance issued under subsection (11).

*Extent: S*

(13) This section is subject to section 114 of the Environment Act 1995 (delegating or referring of appeals etc).
