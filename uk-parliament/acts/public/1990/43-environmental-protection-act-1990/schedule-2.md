#### Notice of direction (affected by repeal, see note)[^c18997571]

**2.** (1)[^c18999201] The Secretary of State, before giving any directions to any authority or constituent authority, shall give notice of his intention to do so to that authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18999201]

**23.** (1)[^c19023251] Subject to sub-paragraph (3) below, any provision to which this paragraph applies shall be void in so far as it operates— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19023251]

- (a) to preclude the holder of any securities of a waste disposal contractor from disposing of those securities; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19023251]
- (b) to require the holder of any such securities to dispose, or offer to dispose, of those securities to particular persons or to particular classes of persons; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19023251]
- (c) to preclude the holder of any securities from disposing of those securities except— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19023251]
  - (i) at a particular time or at particular times; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19023251]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19023251] on the fulfilment of particular conditions or in other particular circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19023251]

(2) This paragraph applies to any provision relating to any securities of a waste disposal contractor which is controlled by a waste disposal authority or to which the authority has transferred the relevant part of its undertaking and contained in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19023251]

- (a) the memorandum or articles of association of the company or any other instrument purporting to regulate to any extent the respective rights and liabilities of the members of the company; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19023251]
- (b) any resolution of the company; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19023251]
- (c) any instrument issued by the company and embodying terms and conditions on which any such securities are to be held by persons for the time being holding them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19023251]

(3) No provision shall be void by reason of its operating as mentioned in sub-paragraph (1) above if the Secretary of State has given his approval in writing to that provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19023251]
