# Environmental Protection Act 1990, section 144

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 144. Amendments of hazardous substances legislation.[^c13748201][^c13739731]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 13 to this Act (which contains miscellaneous amendments to the legislation relating to hazardous substances) shall have effect.
