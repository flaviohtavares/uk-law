# Environmental Protection Act 1990, section 154

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 154. The Groundwork Foundation: superannuation.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Employment with the Groundwork Foundation shall be and shall be deemed always to have been included among the kinds of employment to which a superannuation scheme under section 1 of the [^c13751271]Superannuation Act 1972 can apply, and accordingly in Schedule 1 to that Act (in which those kinds of employment are listed) the words “**Groundwork Foundation**” shall be inserted after the words “Gaming Board for Great Britain”.
