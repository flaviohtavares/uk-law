> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (25).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/43

## 155. Remuneration of chairman of Inland Waterways Amenity Advisory Council.[^c13739731]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 110 of the [^c13751281]Transport Act [^c13751291]1968 (Inland Waterways Amenity Advisory Council) at the end there shall be inserted—

> (7) The Secretary of State may, with the consent of the Treasury, pay the chairman of the Council out of money provided by Parliament such remuneration as the Secretary of State may determine; and where the chairman is in receipt of such remuneration he shall not be paid any allowance under subsection (6) of this section in respect of loss of remunerative time.
