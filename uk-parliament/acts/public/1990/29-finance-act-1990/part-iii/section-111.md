# Finance Act 1990, section 111

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2013-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 111. General.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In sections 107 to 110 above “**the abolition day**” means such day as may be appointed by the Treasury by order made by statutory instrument.

(2) Sections 107 to 109 above shall be construed as one with the [^c12831531]Stamp Act 1891.
