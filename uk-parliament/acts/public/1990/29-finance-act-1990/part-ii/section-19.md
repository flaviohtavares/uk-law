# Finance Act 1990, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2013-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Charge and rate of corporation tax for 1990.[^c12829501]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Corporation tax shall be charged for the financial year 1990 at the rate of 35 per cent.
