# Finance Act 1990, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2013-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Pool betting duty.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 7(1) of the [^c12829001]Betting and Gaming Duties Act 1981 (which specifies 42½ per cent. as the rate of pool betting duty), for the words “42½ per cent.” there shall be substituted the words “ 40 per cent. ”.

(2) This section shall apply in relation to bets made at any time by reference to an event taking place on or after 6th April 1990.
