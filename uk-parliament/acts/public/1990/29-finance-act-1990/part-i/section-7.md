# Finance Act 1990, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2013-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Entry of goods on importation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 3 to this Act (which amends the provisions of the [^c12829281]Customs and Excise Management Act 1979 about initial and supplementary entries and postponed entry) shall have effect in relation to goods imported on or after the day on which this Act is passed.
