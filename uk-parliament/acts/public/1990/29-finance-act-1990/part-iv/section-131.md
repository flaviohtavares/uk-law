# Finance Act 1990, section 131

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2013-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131. Interpretation etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act “**the Taxes Act 1970**” means the [^c12832071]Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1970 and “**the Taxes Act 1988**” means the [^c12832081]Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988.

(2) Chapter II of Part I of this Act shall be construed as one with the [^c12832091]Value Added Tax Act 1983.

(3) Part II of this Act, so far as it relates to capital gains tax, shall be construed as one with the [^c12832101]Capital Gains Tax Act 1979.
