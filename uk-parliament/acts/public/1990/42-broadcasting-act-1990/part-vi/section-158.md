## 158. International representation by Council of Government interests.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

The functions of the Council shall include representing Her Majesty’s Government in the United Kingdom, at the request of the Secretary of State, on international bodies concerned with setting standards for television programmes.
