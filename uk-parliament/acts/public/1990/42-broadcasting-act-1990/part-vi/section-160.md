## 160. Annual reports.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

(1) As soon as possible after the end of every financial year the Council shall prepare a report of their proceedings during that year, and transmit it to the Secretary of State who shall lay copies of it before each House of Parliament.

(2) The report shall include a report by the Council on the matters specified in section 153(1)(a).

(3) The Council shall send a copy of the report—

- (a) to each broadcasting or regulatory body; and
- (b) to every person providing a licensed service.
