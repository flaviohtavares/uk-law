# Broadcasting Act 1990, section 166

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 166. Defamatory material.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of the law of libel and slander [^c-1a3525f853c7abe4dd18e00f9887fdac]... the publication of words in the course of any programme included in a programme service shall be treated as publication in permanent form.

(2) Subsection (1) above shall apply for the purposes of section 3 of each of the Defamation Acts (slander of title etc.) as it applies for the purposes of the law of libel and slander.

(3)[^c-2239ddb73d836074d4aa3cfa2a90de7c] .........................................................................

(4) In this section “**the Defamation Acts**” means the [^c16548591]Defamation Act 1952 and the [^c16548601]Defamation Act (Northern Ireland) 1955.

(5) Subsections (1) and (2) above do not extend to Scotland.
