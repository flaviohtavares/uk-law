# Broadcasting Act 1990, section 111A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 111A. Enforcement of licences held by BBC companies.[^key-e65bcbfd771c83bac5bed569f3c89be1][^key-5c9662bd913d357a74bd1f30c9d99ddc][^c16548171][^key-5c9662bd913d357a74bd1f30c9d99ddc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where OFCOM[^key-10b458765100d24855639f980ccb363a] —

- (a) serve a notice on a BBC company under any provision of section 109, 110 or 111, or
- (b) receive any written representations from a BBC company under section 109(4), 110(4) or 111(8),

OFCOM shall send a copy of the direction, notice or representations to the Secretary of State.
