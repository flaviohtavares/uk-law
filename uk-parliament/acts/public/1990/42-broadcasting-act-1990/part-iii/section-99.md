# Broadcasting Act 1990, section 99

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Procedure to be followed by Authority in connection with consideration of applications for national licences.[^c-5c9662bd913d357a74bd1f30c9d99ddc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a person has made an application for a national licence in accordance with section 98, OFCOM[^c-6f5eab2131f01d5711bb4ac80d4bed63] shall not proceed to consider whether to award him the licence on the basis of his cash bid in accordance with section 100 unless it appears to them—

- (a)[^c-8383f5b6b6d02dbbbb8aef174086c400] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) that he would be able to maintain the proposed service[^c-40e56ca924ab065c34ec313400ba0ef6] and any proposed simulcast radio service corresponding to that service[^c-4f2411112c681e5f0eecafff2f866c23] throughout the period for which the licence would be in force.

(2) Any reference to an applicant in section 100 (except in section 100(9)(b)) is accordingly a reference to an applicant in whose case it appears to OFCOM that the requirements of subsection (1)[^c-98e6980120e5a7c9cc45ca0378934ed5] above are satisfied.
