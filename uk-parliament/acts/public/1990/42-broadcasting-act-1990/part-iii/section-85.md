## 85. Licensing functions of Authority.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) [^key-43568bd6f06b4e7b4c51ec15f5c11623]... OFCOM[^key-5582a74131ab42f2ff02210d34762785] may, in accordance with the following provisions of this Part, grant licences to provide relevant independent radio services[^key-41d162192ff63965021793081507abbe].

(2)[^key-4503afcb47740a58a35b3e0696108469] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-59146c8840c6b8c9763aa02c49afe784] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-59146c8840c6b8c9763aa02c49afe784] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-3506bfeedec2ef0e8e3a3695802f0f74] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^key-3506bfeedec2ef0e8e3a3695802f0f74] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^key-3506bfeedec2ef0e8e3a3695802f0f74] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8)[^key-93c98780e750b26cc8063d1e3aa3ac30] In this section “**relevant independent radio services**” means the following services so far as they are services falling to be regulated under section 245 of the Communications Act 2003—

- (a) sound broadcasting services;
- (b) radio licensable content services;
- (c) additional radio services.
