# Broadcasting Act 1990, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Announcements of programme schedules.[^key-44913a70087f36fcd98212dcf0eea017]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any Channel 3 licence or licence to provide Channel 4 may include conditions requiring the licence holder to include in the licensed service such announcements concerning relevant programme schedules as OFCOM[^key-ad78a8a3ed9dbf4f30ab7b7ebaf8ec38] may determine.

(2) In this section “**relevant programme schedules**” means—

- (a) in relation to a Channel 3 licence, programme schedules for programmes to be broadcast on Channel 4 and, where any part of the area for which the licensed service is to be provided is in Wales, programme schedules for programmes to be broadcast on S4C Digital[^key-941d686d265f4af6c91450b22133b601]; and
- (b) in relation to the licence to provide Channel 4, programme schedules for programmes to be included in any Channel 3 service.
