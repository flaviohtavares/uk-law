# Broadcasting Act 1990, section 63

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Government control over S4C.[^key-44913a70087f36fcd98212dcf0eea017]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If it appears to him to be necessary or expedient to do so in connection with his functions as such, the Secretary of State or any other Minister of the Crown may at any time by notice require S4C[^key-8a706e0546bc4222d3ad57f9ea17eb90] to broadcast, at such times as may be specified in the notice, any announcement specified in the notice, with or without visual images of any picture, scene or object mentioned in the announcement; and it shall be the duty of S4C[^key-7e82e75621f4ced1ee751012517b3261] to comply with the notice.

(2) Where S4C[^key-1a909916a080657c5ed7d9938db7f557] broadcast any announcement in pursuance of a notice under subsection (1), they may announce that they are doing so in pursuance of such a notice.

(3) The Secretary of State may at any time by notice require S4C[^key-44ee5181a46a032843096c68209f2711] to refrain from broadcasting any matter or classes of matter specified in the notice; and it shall be the duty of S4C[^key-25b03374999db883047117ff1844fc12] to comply with the notice.

(4) Where the Secretary of State has given S4C[^key-905534d0922e6b4477dcaa272fe009cc] a notice under subsection (3), S4C[^key-eb2a4aa70c8648109bea70fc2697b2f3] may broadcast an announcement of the giving of the notice or, when it has been revoked or has expired, of its revocation or expiration.

(5) The powers conferred by this section are in addition to any power specifically conferred on the Secretary of State by any other provision of this Act.
