## 61A. S4C public service fund.[^c16547221][^c-44913a70087f36fcd98212dcf0eea017][^c-4b654852b7762ab2cf3838811622e69b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-e7f5604c6a265b5e9c52177fee9f7e31] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) All amounts received by S4C[^c-35acf9a48f199a7521ea8150fa6df2fd] under section 61 [^c-f62c0e00f53258eb66d94ca9af1a8fc3]... shall be kept by S4C[^c-72cf0fe93cb93ff7cb92f23d6383908d] in a separate fund (in this section referred to as “**the public service fund**”) which may be applied only for the purposes of fulfilling S4C’s public service remit.[^c-691e96fb21a86102f351259edecd5b4b]

(3) No S4C company shall receive any direct or indirect subsidy from the public service fund.

(4) S4C[^c-bf293ecbe69a809f3d6ded727c73f963] shall secure that no audiovisual content[^c-b9104eeb28e3f6662b75ab2d7b6d85d1] which has been wholly or partly financed out of the public service fund is included in a relevant audiovisual service[^c-a3ac85947cd0af8be9d89137554089ad] provided by an S4C company before it is first included in a relevant audiovisual service provided by S4C in fulfilling its public service remit.[^c-b8f34e3afee4d9cf9924806f1b7c390b]

(4A)[^c-5c8f39fb625f9be856ca8f07d907df9f] In subsection (4), “**audiovisual content**” and “**relevant audiovisual service**” have the same meaning as in Part 3 of the Communications Act 2003 (see section 362 of that Act).
