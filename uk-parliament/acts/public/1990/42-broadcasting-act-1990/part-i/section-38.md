# Broadcasting Act 1990, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Promotion of equal opportunities in relation to employment by licence holder.[^key-44913a70087f36fcd98212dcf0eea017]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any Channel 3 licence or licence to provide Channel 4 or Channel 5 shall include conditions requiring the licence holder—

- (a) to make arrangements for promoting, in relation to employment by him, equality of opportunity between men and women and between persons of different racial groups; and
- (b) to review those arrangements from time to time.

(2) In subsection (1) “**racial group**” has the same meaning as in the [^c16546511]Race Relations Act 1976.
