## 60. Advertising on S4C.[^c16547191][^key-44913a70087f36fcd98212dcf0eea017]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-03af713a2251a388978b1216160b821e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-03af713a2251a388978b1216160b821e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-03af713a2251a388978b1216160b821e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) S4C[^key-6267cfd98866726c33a7662a66495082] shall—

- (a) from time to time consult the Secretary of State as to the classes and descriptions of advertisements which must not be broadcast on S4C Digital[^key-5d2f0eb8ccdfdd35fd3503acc9e181b3] and the methods of advertising or sponsorship which must not be employed in, or in connection with, the provision of S4C Digital[^key-3752180087a211ed8a60c4dd2ab512c4];
- (aa)[^key-c337ea6fa75c712a2c29addd9d2271af] from time to time consult the Secretary of State as to the forms and methods of product placement that should not be employed in the provision of S4C Digital[^key-ab71883e481452391858bb6bc265c13e] (including the descriptions of products, services or trade marks for which product placement should not be employed); and
- (b) carry out any directions which he may give to them in respect of such matters.

(5) S4C[^key-a98b2682d8f5bf9aa1d5ca6b6b8df569] shall not act as an advertising agent.

(6)[^key-a6285ffe5cebe03b4da25abe3c8df867] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
