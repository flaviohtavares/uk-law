# Broadcasting Act 1990, section 64

## 64. Audience research by S4C.[^c16547241][^c-44913a70087f36fcd98212dcf0eea017][^c-7b31069d233460fd87317a354ef42a57]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) S4C[^c-101e9777b00172665b7ed48883e6e3c0] shall make arrangements for ascertaining—

- (a) the state of public opinion concerning programmes broadcast on S4C Digital[^c-70ec686e64287cffb5dfbb6623b949c2];
- (b) any effects of such programmes on the attitudes or behaviour of persons who watch them; and
- (c) the types of programme that members of the public would like to be broadcast on S4C Digital[^c-6f5e5f04eec60473c1e27efb1a6dda6f].

(2) Those arrangements shall—

- (a) secure that, so far as is reasonably practicable, any research undertaken in pursuance of the arrangements is undertaken by persons who are neither members nor employees of S4C[^c-4b244f0e5fb25414b2c42cabe7e22eec]; and
- (b) include provision for full consideration by S4C[^c-d768bc819758594f3f9dcfc6defb3cd4] of the results of any such research.
