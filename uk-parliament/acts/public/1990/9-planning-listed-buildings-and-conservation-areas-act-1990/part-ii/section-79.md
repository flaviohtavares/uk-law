## 79. Town scheme agreements.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Commission and one or more local authorities in England [^key-f4a4358479b9b786d8eb0cc9af48a081]... may enter an agreement (in this Act referred to as a “town scheme agreement”) that a specified sum of money shall be set aside for a specified period of years for the purpose of making grants for the repair of buildings which are—

- (a) included in a list compiled for the purposes of such an agreement by the parties to the agreement, or by them and other such authorities, or
- (b) shown on a map prepared for those purposes by the parties, or by them and such other authorities.

(2)[^c21495141] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In this section “**local authority**” means—

- (a) a county council;
- (aa)[^key-2c41f3656f9b3b4656a0794e1fbe6338] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) a district council;
- (c) in relation to any building situated within the Broads, the Broads Authority;
- (ca)[^c12918011] in relation to any building in a National Park for which a National Park authority is the local planning authority, that authority;
- (d) a London borough council or the Common Council of the City of London;
- (e) the Council of the Isles of Scilly.
