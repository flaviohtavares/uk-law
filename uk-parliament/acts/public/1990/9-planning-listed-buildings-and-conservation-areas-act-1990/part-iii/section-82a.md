# Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990, section 82A

## 82A. Application to the Crown[^key-96c0f2fe6c373a3ae79f2b713556c6ce][^c21493571]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Act (except the provisions specified in subsection (2)) binds the Crown.

(2) These are the provisions—

- (a) section 9;
- (b) section 11(6);
- (c) section 21(7);
- (ca)[^key-ad8105f5ea6fbaf21e7ce6935d7ec9f2] section 26J;
- (d) section 42(1), (5) and (6);
- (e) section 43;
- (f) section 44A;
- (fza)[^key-e4daf11864fd76f1d5e982698126cb08] section 44AB;
- (fa)[^key-71293ccea7feb320ee57e9923a2ada5b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g) section 54;
- (h) section 55;
- (i) section 59;
- (j) section 88A.

(3) But subsection (2)(a) does not have effect to prohibit the doing of anything by or on behalf of the Crown which falls within the circumstances described in section 9(3)(a) to (d) and the doing of that thing does not contravene section 7.
