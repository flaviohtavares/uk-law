# Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990, section 89

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (44).** Text as at 2026-08-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/9

## 89. Application of certain general provisions of principal Act.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to subsections (1A) and (2),[^c21492861] the following provisions of the principal Act shall apply for the purposes of this Act as they apply for the purposes of that Act, namely—

- [^c-631519a11fb4cc43d2d7c6a670cde21e]...
- section 320 (local inquiries),
- section 322 (orders as to costs of parties where no inquiry held[^c12918831] [^c-7bfc64400bb3ce9393e5d2c0cb0c727e]...),
- section 322A (orders as to costs: supplementary)[^c12918841],
- [^c-985594acf0590fbda91871bdaded095f]...
- 323 (procedure on certain appeals and applications [^c-5466842d9dd8be4c80e308f3d4be2ad2]...),
- [^c-70fe33ddbca8f88c7772570b077dec5a]...
- section 327ZA (powers as to form and content of applications[^c-2adc0da34ae4d509ca1084421bc55076] [^c-8ad7dd8324a46f02f37b1a422b08624c]...);
- section 327A (compliance with requirements relating to applications),[^c21493661]
- section 329 (service of notices),
- section 329A(1) and (2) (service of notices on the Crown)[^c21493981]
- section 330 (power to require information as to interests in land),
- section 330A(1) to (4) (information as to interests in Crown land)[^c21494001]
- section 331 (offences by corporations).

(1ZB)[^c-6b892e81d4d350a9d8513926fbd78499] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1ZC)[^c-8e10117dc909fb7cd29a6c343bf00e5a] In section 327ZA of the principal Act as applied by this section, references to a planning application are to be read as references to an application under, or for the purposes of, any provision of Chapter 2 of Part 1 of this Act or any subordinate provision made under that Chapter (but are not to be read as including an application made in legal proceedings).

(1A)[^c21492881] [^c-50f569bf5ebabbcd59f28d030d1e7373]... subsection (1)(cc) of section 329 of that Act shall not apply to—

- (a) service of a building preservation notice;
- (b) service of a copy of a listed building enforcement notice by a planning authority;
- (c) giving of notice under section 38 of this Act of the exercise of powers conferred by subsection (5) of that section; or
- (d) service of a listed building enforcement notice issued by the Secretary of State.

(2) Section 331 of that Act shall not apply to offences under section 59 of this Act.

(3)[^c12918851] In the application of section 330 by virtue of this section, references to a local authority include the Commission.
