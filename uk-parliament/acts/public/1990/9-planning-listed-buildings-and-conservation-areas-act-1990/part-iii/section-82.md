## 82. Application of Act to land and works of local planning authorities.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-6d2383bfd2cac8fb535102bc6b7ae2f8] In relation to land of a local planning authority, section 1(1) [^c-80f9abf3db0047078d04ba322e7a584c]... and (4) and sections 2 [^c-bf673e75dc465a95c0f89e7d397d17bd]..., and 39(6)[^c12918071] 41(8)[^c12918081], shall have effect subject to such exceptions and modifications as may be prescribed.

(2)[^c12918091][^c-595144608582ecf4bbc4657459213c10][^c-6a61505c685fea0b607be55463163ba1] The provisions mentioned in subsection (3) shall have effect for the purpose of applications by local planning authorities relating to the execution of works for the demolition, alteration or extension of listed buildings, subject to such exceptions and modifications as may be prescribed.

(3)[^c12918091][^c-595144608582ecf4bbc4657459213c10][^c-6a61505c685fea0b607be55463163ba1] Those provisions are sections 1(3), (5) and (6), [^c-f677ef7ec71f8e60c33bcd81adffcf29]... 3 to 5, 7 to 26, 28,[^c-949c97c5cca0987cf27fbcc15f096080] [^c-6cdc4120b3fc1ef122bb9bacd97697b6]... 32 to 50 (except section 39(6)[^c12918111]), 60(1) to (4) (as it applies as respects the provisions mentioned in this subsection), 62 to 65, 67(2)(b), (6) and (7), 73(1), Schedules 1 [^c-68b5cfce38a949aa73df0a4e43033401]... and 2, paragraph 2 of Schedule 4 (as it applies to Schedule 1) and paragraph 4(1) of Schedule 4 (as it applies as respects the provisions mentioned in this subsection).

(4)[^c12918091][^c-595144608582ecf4bbc4657459213c10] Regulations under this section may in particular provide—

- (a)[^c-6a61505c685fea0b607be55463163ba1] for the making of applications for listed building consent to the Secretary of State; and
- (b)[^c-6d2383bfd2cac8fb535102bc6b7ae2f8] for the issue or service by him of notices under section 2(3) and the provisions mentioned in subsection (3).
