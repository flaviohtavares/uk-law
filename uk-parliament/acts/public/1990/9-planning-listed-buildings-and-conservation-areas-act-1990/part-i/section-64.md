# Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990, section 64

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (44).** Text as at 2026-08-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 64. Validity of listed building enforcement notices.[^c-217b882a1723a1d523e5b7b2ab3b45d5][^c-8b56632f5780766f4f5f6af793ca2ae3][^c12917431]

*Extent: E+W*

The validity of a listed building enforcement notice shall not, except by way of an appeal under section 39, be questioned in any proceedings whatsoever on any of the grounds on which such an appeal may be brought.
