## 46. Enforcement by the Secretary of State.[^c-6ee50766a69d4f399df3e9763d365e5f][^c-b717f0e4d68c249f15048c0eb92cb76c][^c12916841]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If it appears to the Secretary of State to be expedient that a listed building enforcement notice should be issued in respect of any land, he may issue such a notice.

(1A)[^c-44a817f9c2fd83a18ac1162386ee9f90] If it appears to the Secretary of State to be expedient that a temporary stop notice should be issued in respect of any land [^c-df21be15cb85c1fff31c11c245cfb2c4]..., the Secretary of State may issue such a notice.

(2) Before the Secretary of State serves a notice under subsection (1) or (1A)[^c-d19b7739230d1ef9111e3728e02319a1] he shall consult—

- (a) the local planning authority; and
- (b) [^c-17cbf2c802cd92d2c25096eca697500f]... the Commission.

(3) A listed building enforcement notice issued by the Secretary of State shall have the same effect as a notice issued by the local planning authority.

(3A)[^c-3321d27b790e0beefb99bb0674642cf3] A temporary stop notice issued by the Secretary of State shall have the same effect as a notice issued by the local planning authority under section 44AA.

(4) In relation to a listed building enforcement notice issued by the Secretary of State, section 42[^c12916851] shall apply as if for any reference in that section to the local planning authority there were substituted a reference to the Secretary of State.

(5) References in this section to the local planning authority shall in the case of an authority for an area [^c-0a6fa5509c84ee0db6f555f02d2686ad]... outside Greater London be construed as references to the district planning authority.
