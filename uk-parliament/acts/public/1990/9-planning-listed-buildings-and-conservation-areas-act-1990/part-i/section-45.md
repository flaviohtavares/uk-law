# Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (44).** Text as at 2026-08-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45. Commission to have concurrent enforcement functions in London.[^key-792813c46e3c3ed59f62f4ea8cf0caaa][^key-b717f0e4d68c249f15048c0eb92cb76c][^c12916761]

*Extent: E+W*

The Commission shall, as respects any London borough, have concurrently with the council of that borough the functions of a local planning authority under sections 38 to 43 and 44AA to 44AC[^key-0807cdac9a6b110db474e96a7122fe47]; and references to the local planning authority in those provisions , and in any provision of this Act referring to anything done under those provisions,[^key-c34036822c678e6965d059a3cf33f646] shall be construed accordingly.
