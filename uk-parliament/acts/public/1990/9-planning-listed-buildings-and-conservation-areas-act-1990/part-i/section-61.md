# Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990, section 61

## 61. Exceptions for ancient monuments etc.[^c12917351][^c-f358dc88896a8762ffe80d1bc42686ee]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The provisions mentioned in subsection (2) shall not apply to any building for the time being included in the schedule of monuments compiled and maintained under section 1 of the [^c12917361]Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979.

(2) Those provisions are sections 3,[^c-4767eecb9b95759cfd1a339b460bdae5] 4, 7 to 9, 47, 54 and 59.
