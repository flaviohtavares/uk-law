# Food Safety Act 1990, section 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/16

## 17. Enforcement of EU provisions.[^M_E_5c6e66b8-a962-4ad0-898f-ffcb71aa268a][^c-359760d8ae21732919a8329ddd35dde0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State[^c10203481] may by regulations make such provision with respect to food, food sources or contact materials, including in particular provision for prohibiting or regulating the carrying out of commercial operations with respect to food, food sources or contact materials, as appears to him[^c10203491] to be called for by any EU obligation.

(2) As respects any directly applicable EU provision which relates to food, food sources or contact materials and for which, in his opinion, it is appropriate to provide under this Act, the Secretary of State may by regulations—

- (a) make such provision as he considers necessary or expedient for the purpose of securing that the EU provision is administered, executed and enforced under this Act; and
- (b) apply such of the provisions of this Act as may be specified in the regulations in relation to the EU provision with such modifications, if any, as may be so specified.

(3) In subsections (1) and (2) above references to food or food sources shall be construed in accordance with section 16(5) above.

## 17. Enforcement of assimilated provisions.[^M_E_51d31174-a707-4fd8-efcc-75760294fdc2][^V_S_2872d75a-f08e-baf1-7451-588b96a9ffd9][^V_S_76766efc-19a5-284c-30c1-b9292cd1f9b3]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Secretary of State[^V_S_ba9c5fda-55ca-9fc9-1ac3-c41951fed22c] may by regulations make such provision with respect to food, food sources or contact materials, including in particular provision for prohibiting or regulating the carrying out of commercial operations with respect to food, food sources or contact materials, as appears to him[^V_S_ee385c56-2d91-1fe2-d479-520e5c3492af] to be called for by any assimilated[^V_S_f4cd6cad-04d1-6b56-f9a3-4c003b84cdc6] obligation.

(2) As respects any provisions of assimilated direct legislation[^V_S_f83b7366-d884-0fa5-1e1f-5bc2d8be0065] which relates to food, food sources or contact materials and for which, in his opinion, it is appropriate to provide under this Act, the Secretary of State may by regulations—

- (a) make such provision as he considers necessary or expedient for the purpose of securing that the assimilated[^V_S_e671294f-a202-2a49-d5bd-42a34501a88c] provision is administered, executed and enforced under this Act; and
- (b) apply such of the provisions of this Act as may be specified in the regulations in relation to the assimilated[^V_S_4aa0acd6-4735-ca53-ae3b-aeff344f22b0] provision with such modifications, if any, as may be so specified.

(3) In subsections (1) and (2) above references to food or food sources shall be construed in accordance with section 16(5) above.
