## 52. Markets, sugar beet and cold storage.

*Extent: E+W+S*

In the [^c10205481]Food Act 1984 (in this Act referred to as “**the 1984 Act**”)—

- (a) Part III (markets); and
- (b) Part V (sugar beet and cold storage),

shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in Schedule 2 to this Act.
