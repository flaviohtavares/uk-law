# Food Safety Act 1990, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 47. Remuneration of tribunal chairmen.[^c-49bf7baac7c71177e221e7634ff01eb5][^c-4ee4d4209744bb7dd9df8469c3a603da]

*Extent: E+W+S*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament to the chairman of any tribunal constituted in accordance with regulations under this Act such remuneration (by way of salary or fees) and such allowances as the Secretary of State[^c10205361] may with the approval of the Treasury determine.
