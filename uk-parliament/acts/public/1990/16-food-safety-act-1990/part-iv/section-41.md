# Food Safety Act 1990, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 41. Power to require returns.[^c10205101][^c-9e84f6b6a0ae45637bf1dad106b16fe6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-04ed74ad564e9d020f135e7c4a4398ff] Every food authority shall send to the Secretary of State[^c10205111] or to the Food Standards Agency[^c10205121] such reports and returns, and give him or it[^c10205131] such information, with respect to the exercise of the functions conferred on them by or under this Act as he or it may require.

*Extent: S*

(2)[^c-f34e2b6624390bf24c99ea971ffb2331] In the application of this section to Scotland, the references to the Food Standards Agency are to be read as references to Food Standards Scotland.
