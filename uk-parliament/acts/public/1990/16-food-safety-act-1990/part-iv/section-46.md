## 46. Expenses of authorised officers and county councils.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-e106424e77e44abfb559ced1a9869733][^c-af7ab04073a6e36d071a7c7a420a6453][^c-33fad30fbe53ba2103f35eb1c3961080][^c-b58bc344b7f990c8a3c7e91150e5fc7e][^c-6f93914f2c7c1030e22026521a74c37f][^c-59e12b0c24b3acc09eeef1d08f01620a][^c-58bb790d1861dccd80e5060863eb9369][^c-24e6002e28652ea5c339805098662833][^c-1176de6cfb3055ec072aabc257fab754] Any expenses which are incurred under this Act by an authorised officer of a food authority in procuring samples, and causing samples to be analysed or examined, shall be defrayed by that authority.

(2) Any expenses incurred by a county council in the enforcement and execution of any provision of this Act, or of any regulations or orders made under it, shall, if the Secretary of State so directs, be defrayed as expenses for special county purposes charged on such part of the county as may be specified in the direction.
