# Food Safety Act 1990, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/16

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28. Provision of facilities for examinations.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A food authority, or a council constituted under section 2 of the Local Government[^c10204041] etc. (Scotland) Act 1994 in Scotland, may provide facilities for examinations for the purposes of this Act.

(2) In this Act “**examination**” means a microbiological examination and “**examine**” shall be construed accordingly.
