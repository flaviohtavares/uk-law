# Planning (Consequential Provisions) Act 1990, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2014-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/11

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Meaning of “the consolidating Acts”, “the repealed enactments”, etc.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Act—

- “**the consolidating Acts**” means the principal Act, the [^c1048462]Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990, the [^c1048463]Planning (Hazardous Substances) Act 1990 and, so far as it reproduces the effect of the repealed enactments, this Act,
- “**the principal Act**” means the [^c1048464]Town and Country Planning Act 1990, and
- “**the repealed enactments**” means the enactments repealed by this Act.

(2) Expressions used in this Act and in any of the other consolidating Acts have the same meaning as in those Acts.
