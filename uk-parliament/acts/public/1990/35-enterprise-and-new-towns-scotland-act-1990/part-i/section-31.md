# Enterprise and New Towns (Scotland) Act 1990, section 31

> Text as at 2017-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 31. Application and disapplication of certain provisions.

*Extent: S*

(1) Section 9 of the [^c1031125]Industry Act 1975 (the National Enterprise Board and the media) shall apply in relation to Scottish Enterprise as if any reference in that section to the Board were a reference to Scottish Enterprise[^c1031126].

(2)[^c1031127] Section 21 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (restrictions on financial promotion) shall not apply to any invitation or inducement (within the meaning of that section) which Scottish Enterprise communicates in the discharge of its functions.
