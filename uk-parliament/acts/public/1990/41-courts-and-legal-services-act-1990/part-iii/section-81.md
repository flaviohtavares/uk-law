# Courts and Legal Services Act 1990, section 81

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Transfer of accrued rights to and from judicial pension schemes.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

Schedule 13 amends the Judicial Pensions Act 1981 by inserting a new Schedule 1A, which makes provision for the transfer of accrued rights into and out of the judicial pension schemes constituted by that Act and the [^c12477061]Sheriffs’ Pensions (Scotland) Act 1961.
