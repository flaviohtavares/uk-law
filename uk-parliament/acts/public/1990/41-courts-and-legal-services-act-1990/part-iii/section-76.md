# Courts and Legal Services Act 1990, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 76. Judicial oaths.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person holding any of the following offices—

- (a) district judge, including district judge of the principal registry of the Family Division;
- (b) Master of the Queen’s Bench Division;
- (c) Master of the Business and Property Division[^c-40b0bfea2907b8a01db5f7bbfc8fe2e4];
- (d) Insolvency and Companies Court Judge[^c-09481e0e3a71eda1c41b83c924f27385];
- (e) Taxing Master of the Senior Courts[^c20037171];
- (f) Admiralty Registrar,

shall take the oath of allegiance and the judicial oath before a judge of the High Court or a Circuit judge.

(2) The [^c12477011]Promissory Oaths Act 1868 shall have effect as if the offices listed in the Second Part of the Schedule to that Act included those offices.
