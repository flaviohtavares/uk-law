## 95. Appeals against refusal to restore solicitor’s name to roll under section 8 of the 1974 Act.

*Extent: E+W*

In section 8 of the [^c12477341]Solicitors Act 1974 (removal or restoration of name at solicitor’s request) the following subsections shall be added at the end—

> (4) An appeal from any decision of the Society under subsection (2) shall lie to the Master of the Rolls.
>
> (5) The Master of the Rolls may make regulations about appeals to him under this section.
