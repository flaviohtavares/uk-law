# Courts and Legal Services Act 1990, section 115

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 115. Law reports.[^c12478011]

*Extent: E+W*

A report of a case made by a person who is not a barrister but who is a solicitor or has a Senior Courts[^c20037191] qualification (within the meaning of section 71) shall have the same authority as if it had been made by a barrister.
