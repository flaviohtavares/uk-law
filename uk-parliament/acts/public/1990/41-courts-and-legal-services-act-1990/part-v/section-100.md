# Courts and Legal Services Act 1990, section 100

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 100. Specific powers of arbitrator exercisable by High Court.[^c12477431]

*Extent: E+W*

After section 43 of the [^c12477441]Senior Courts Act 1981[^c20500831] there shall be inserted the following section—

> **43A. Specific powers of arbitrator exercisable by High Court.**
>
> In any cause or matter proceeding in the High Court in connection with any contract incorporating an arbitration agreement which confers specific powers upon the arbitrator, the High Court may, if all parties to the agreement agree, exercise any such powers.
