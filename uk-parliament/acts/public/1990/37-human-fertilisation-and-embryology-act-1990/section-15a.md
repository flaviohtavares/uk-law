# Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act 1990, section 15A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-11-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15A. Duties of the Authority in relation to serious adverse events and serious adverse reactions[^c1970102]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Authority shall investigate serious adverse events and serious adverse reactions and take appropriate control measures.

(2) In investigating any serious adverse event or serious adverse reaction, the Authority shall, where it is appropriate to do so, arrange for—

- (a) any premises to which a licence relates and any relevant third party premises to be inspected on its behalf, and
- (b) a report on the inspection to be made to it.

(3) If the Authority, in relation to Northern Ireland, receives a request from a competent authority in an EEA state[^c-70c2d0fd3ce98f2fede82127c6381c1f] to carry out an inspection in relation to a serious adverse event or serious adverse reaction, the Authority must arrange for such an inspection to be carried out, for a report to be made of the inspection and for appropriate control measures to be taken.
