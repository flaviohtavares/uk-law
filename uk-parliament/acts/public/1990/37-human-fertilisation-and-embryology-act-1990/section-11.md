# Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act 1990, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-11-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Licences for treatment, storage and research.[^c271615]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Authority may grant the following and no other licences—

- (a) licences under paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to this Act authorising activities in the course of providing treatment services,
- (aa)[^c1970040] licences under paragraph 1A of that Schedule authorising activities in the course of providing non-medical fertility services,
- (b) licences under that Schedule authorising the storage of gametes, embryos or human admixed embryos[^c-f918f983a12f92aa6df4ac5fb68db26d], and
- (c) licences under paragraph 3 of that Schedule authorising activities for the purposes of a project of research.

(2) Paragraph 4 of that Schedule has effect in the case of all licences.
