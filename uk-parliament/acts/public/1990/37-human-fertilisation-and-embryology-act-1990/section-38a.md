# Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act 1990, section 38A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-11-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38A. Inspection, entry, search and seizure[^key-6ea622d605dd14bccd1e63a12d6c57ca]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 3B (which makes provisions about inspection, entry, search and seizure) has effect.

(2) Nothing in this Act makes it unlawful for a member or employee of the Authority to keep any embryo, human admixed embryo or gametes in pursuance of that person's functions as such.
