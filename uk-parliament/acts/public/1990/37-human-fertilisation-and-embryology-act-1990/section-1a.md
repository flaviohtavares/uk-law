# Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act 1990, section 1A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-11-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1A. Reference to Directives[^c1969979]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act—

- “**the first Directive**” means Directive [2004/23/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/23) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 31 March 2004 on setting standards of quality and safety for the donation, procurement, testing, processing, preservation, storage and distribution of human tissues and cells,
- “**the second Directive**” means Commission Directive [2006/17/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2006/17) of 8 February 2006 implementing Directive [2004/23/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/23) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards certain technical requirements for the donation, procurement and testing of human tissues and cells, as amended by Commission Directive 2012/39/[^c-e73ed54c8c26ae96356853771fea50ed]EU, [^M_F_6cea1650-4648-4706-9c6e-6286d2c128dc]...
- “the third Directive” means—[^c-c890304973c6964e70fe7c28b17714ed]
  - (a) in the application of this Act in relation to Great Britain, Commission [Directive 2006/86/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2006/0086) of 24 October 2006 implementing [Directive 2004/23/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/0023) of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards traceability requirements, notification of serious adverse reactions and events and certain technical requirements for the coding, processing, preservation, storage and distribution of human tissues and cells (“the 2006 Directive”), as it had effect immediately before 29 April 2015 (which is the date on which the amendments made by Commission [Directive 2015/565/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2015/0565) came into force), and
  - (b) in the application of this Act in relation to Northern Ireland, the 2006 Directive as amended by Commission [Directive 2015/565/EU](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2015/0565),
- “the fourth Directive” means Commission Directive 2015/566 of 8 April 2015 implementing [Directive 2004/23/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2004/0023) as regards the procedures for verifying the equivalent standards of quality and safety of imported tissues and cells.
