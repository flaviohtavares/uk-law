# Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act 1990, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-11-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Meaning of “mother”.[^c271632][^key-f5af97cac9964d7b32be4534d0a033a5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The woman who is carrying or has carried a child as a result of the placing in her of an embryo or of sperm and eggs, and no other woman, is to be treated as the mother of the child.

(2)[^c271633][^key-a061ad09370c8c3a3bf7b8bdfe15bfcc][^key-c88ba4d3d1e699ea2c0bc9636e792654] Subsection (1) above does not apply to any child to the extent that the child is treated by virtue of adoption as not being the woman’s child[^c1830832].

(3) Subsection (1) above applies whether the woman was in the United Kingdom or elsewhere at the time of the placing in her of the embryo or the sperm and eggs.
