# National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, section 63

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2022-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1990/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 63. Repeal of remaining provisions of Health Services Act 1976.[^c1358809]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Part III (control of hospital building outside National Health Service) and Part IV (supplementary and general) of the [^c1358810]Health Services Act 1976 shall cease to have effect.

(2) Notwithstanding the repeal of Part III of the Health Services Act 1976 by this Act,—

- (a) that Part shall continue to have effect in relation to any authorisation granted by the Secretary of State under section 13(2) of that Act which is in force when that repeal takes effect; and[^c1358811]
- (b) the amendment made by section 19(4)(b) of that Act shall continue to have effect.
