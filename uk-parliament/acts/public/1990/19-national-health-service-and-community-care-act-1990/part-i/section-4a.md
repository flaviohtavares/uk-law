# National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, section 4A

## 4A. Provision of certain services under NHS contracts.[^c1358061]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c2171053] This section applies to any arrangement under which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053] a Strategic Health Authority,[^c1358062] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]a Primary Care Trust,[^c1358063] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053] a Health Authority or such other health service body as may be prescribed arrange for the provision to them— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]

- (a) by a person on an ophthalmic list, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053] [^c2102099]. . .
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c2102102][^c2171053] by a contractor under a general ophthalmic services contract, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]
- (b) by a person on a pharmaceutical list, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]

of goods or services that they reasonably require for the purposes of functions which they are exercising under Part I of the principal Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053] other than under section 16CD of that Act[^c2102108] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053].

(2) Any such arrangement is to be treated as an NHS contract for the purposes of section 4 (other than subsections (4) and (6)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]

(3) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]

- “general ophthalmic services contract” and “contractor” under such a contract have the meanings given by section 28WA of the principal Act;[^c2102110] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]
- “**health service body *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]**” means a body which is a health service body for the purposes of section 4; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]
- “**ophthalmic list *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]**” means a list published in accordance with regulations made under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]
  - (a) section *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053] 39(1)(a)[^c1358064] of the principal Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]
  - (b) section 26(2)(a) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053] [^c1358065]National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]
  - (c) Article 62(2)(a) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053] [^c1358066]Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]
- “**pharmaceutical list *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]**” means a list published in accordance with regulations made under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]
  - (a) section 42(2)(a) of the principal Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]
  - (b) section 27(2) of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]
  - (c) Article 63(2A)(a) of the 1972 Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]

(4)[^c2102061] In subsection (3), in paragraph (b) of the definition of “ophthalmic list” the reference to a list published in accordance with regulations made under paragraph (a) of section 26(2) of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 is a reference to the first part of the list (referred to in sub-paragraph (i) of that paragraph) which is published in accordance with regulations under that paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c2171053]
