# Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990, section 25

## 25. Rights to conduct litigation and rights of audience. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3c60a154a6c1e25e19ac36c4305dfe69][^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af][^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]

*Extent: S*

(1) Any professional or other body may, for the purpose of enabling any of their members who is a natural person to acquire— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]

- (a) rights to conduct litigation on behalf of members of the public; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]
- (b) rights of audience, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]

make an application in that regard to the Lord President and the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]

(2) An application under subsection (1) above shall include a draft scheme— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]

- (a) specifying— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]
  - (i) the courts; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af] the categories of proceedings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af] the nature of the business; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af] the rights to conduct litigation and the rights of audience, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]

  in relation to which the application is made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]
- (b) describing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]
  - (i) the training requirements which the body would impose upon any of their members who sought to acquire any right such as is mentioned in subsection (1) above; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af] the code of practice which they would impose upon their members in relation to the exercise by those members of any rights acquired by them by virtue of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af] section 27 of this Act[^c-3738606a8c58e9d9fa5aafbb89a05067] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af],

  in the event of the application being granted; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]
- (c) proposing arrangements for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]
  - (i) the indemnification of members of the public against loss suffered by them through the actings of the body’s members in the exercise by those members of any rights acquired by them by virtue of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af] section 27 of this Act in the event of the application being granted[^c-7ac5adce5f882cde64eeb53b42ac2948] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af] the treatment by the body of complaints *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af] remitted to the body under section[^c-91bf7e7dff1c0651de6ee92ce34f17a5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af] 6(2)(a)[^c-a8468dca88b9bdef406f6554225f6925] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af] or 15(5)(a) of the 2007 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af] in relation to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af] [^c-5fb6bab82598e0c46e81588fa88cd4f6]... members of the body exercising rights acquired by virtue of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af] section 27 of this Act in the event of the application being granted[^c-c018b579dc704f3aaaf802bf05b8469f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af],

and shall state that the body have complied with the provisions of Schedule 2 to this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]

(3) A code of practice such as is mentioned in subsection (2)(b)(ii) above shall include provision with regard to revoking, suspending or attaching conditions to the exercise of any right acquired by a member of the body by virtue of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af] section 27 of this Act[^c-36f04b245ac17e06f3f8f1e88c0f6999] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af] in consequence of a breach by that member of that code of practice; and shall in particular include provision enabling the body to comply with the provisions of section 27(4) of this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]

(4) A draft scheme submitted under this section shall also include the proposals of the body in relation to such other matters as may be prescribed by the Secretary of State in regulations made under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]

(5) Regulations under this section shall be made by statutory instrument subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]

(6) Schedule 2 shall have effect in relation to the publication of applications made under subsection (1) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-905edce24ec3c785c063bed4edf284af]
