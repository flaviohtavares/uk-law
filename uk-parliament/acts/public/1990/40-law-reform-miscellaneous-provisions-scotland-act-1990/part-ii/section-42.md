# Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990, section 42

## 42. Review of rules approved by the Secretary of State.[^c13713401]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where the Secretary of State has approved—

- (a) a rule under section [^key-0402ff7085f99638708e888bfdad06b9]... 31(2) of this Act; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-86cf72e5cbfdafeeafba2c443c5e3f4d]
- (b) a draft scheme under section 26(6) of this Act, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-86cf72e5cbfdafeeafba2c443c5e3f4d]

he may and, where the Lord President, in the case of a draft scheme such as is mentioned in paragraph (b), so requests shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f2e6e060e9db2698f2dd95cb98df8f61], require the body which made the rule or, as the case may be, the scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-07fdb016c034513eaedca2aba98950d7] to review its terms.

(2) When they have reviewed a rule or, as the case may be, a scheme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5575ed83ecb894582f08e44ffb17d7f0] following a requirement made under subsection (1) above, the body concerned may revise the rule or scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1839190520815f2cef2d67f2a84c5187] in the light of that review, and shall then submit the rule or scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-1839190520815f2cef2d67f2a84c5187] as revised or, if they have not revised it, as previously approved—

- (a) in the case of a rule such as is mentioned in subsection (1)(a) above, to the Secretary of State; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a51ed12a6f165cf345c5d2377d4f573c]
- (b) in the case of a draft scheme such as is mentioned in subsection (1)(b) above, to the Secretary of State and the Lord President. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a51ed12a6f165cf345c5d2377d4f573c]

(3) Where a rule, whether revised or as previously approved, is submitted to the Secretary of State under subsection (2)(a) above, he may—

- (a) approve the rule as submitted to him; or
- (b) amend the rule in such manner as he considers appropriate,

and (except where the rule remains in the form previously approved) he may direct the body concerned to bring it into operation as soon as is practicable.

(4)[^key-7b35960af5f95f5791cfc43b615cf216] Where the Lord President and the Secretary of State are agreed that the terms of a draft scheme submitted to them under subsection (2)(b) above are satisfactory, the Secretary of State may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7b35960af5f95f5791cfc43b615cf216]

- (a) approve the scheme; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7b35960af5f95f5791cfc43b615cf216]
- (b) (except where the scheme remains in the form previously approved) direct the body concerned to bring the scheme, as so amended, into force as soon as is practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7b35960af5f95f5791cfc43b615cf216]

(5) Where either the Secretary of State or the Lord President is of the view that the terms of any such scheme so submitted to them are not satisfactory, but they do not agree as to what the terms of the scheme should be, the scheme shall continue to have effect as previously approved. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7b35960af5f95f5791cfc43b615cf216]

(6) Where the Secretary of State and the Lord President agree both that the terms of a scheme so submitted to them are not satisfactory, and as to what the terms of the scheme should be, the Secretary of State may amend the scheme in such manner as he and the Lord President consider appropriate; and may direct the body concerned to bring the scheme, as so amended, into force as soon as is practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7b35960af5f95f5791cfc43b615cf216]

(7) The provisions of section 40(1)(b) and (c) of this Act shall apply to rules and schemes *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5911fd19e469a0de931df2fe91f72dd] submitted under subsection (2) of this section as they apply to rules submitted under sections 17(15) and 31(2) and schemes submitted under section 25(1) of this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a58c4a62910d1eac619bea082b540975].
