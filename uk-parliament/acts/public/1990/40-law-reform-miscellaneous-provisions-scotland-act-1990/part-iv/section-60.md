# Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990, section 60

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Criminal jurisdiction of sheriff court.[^c13713671]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

The following subsection shall be inserted at the end of each of sections 3 and 288 of the [^c13713681]Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1975 to form subsection (4) and subsection (5) respectively of these sections—

> - (0) Where an offence is alleged to have been committed in one district in a sheriffdom, it shall be competent to try that offence in a sheriff court in any other district in that sheriffdom.
