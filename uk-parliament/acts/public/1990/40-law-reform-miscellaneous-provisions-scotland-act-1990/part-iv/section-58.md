# Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1990, section 58

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 58. Identification of accused by child.[^c13713621][^c13713631]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

Where a court has, or is deemed to have, granted an application made under section 56 of this Act in relation to a child [^c13713641]. . ., and the child gives evidence that he recalls having identified, prior to the trial, a person alleged to have committed an offence, the evidence of a third party as to the identification of that person by the child prior to the trial shall be admissible as evidence as to such identification.
