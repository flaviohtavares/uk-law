## SCHEDULE 2 (affected by repeal, see note) — Publication of applications made under section 25 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2][^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]

*Extent: S*

Section 25. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]

**1.**[^c-e1ec83a82dae8444d9874edc5d3d656d] Any professional or other body making an application under section 25 of this Act shall, for a period of six weeks beginning with the date on which the application is submitted to the Lord President and the Secretary of State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]

- (a) make a copy of the draft scheme referred to in section 25(2) of this Act available for public inspection at a specified place; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]
- (b) on a request from any person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]
  - (i) send him a copy of the draft scheme; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2] make a copy of the draft scheme available for public inspection at a suitable place in his locality. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]

**2.**[^c-1fc44774d044117121d9242ba799867e] Any person may make written representations concerning any draft scheme submitted under section 25 of this Act, and such representations shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]

- (a) be made to both the Lord President and the Secretary of State; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]
- (b) be delivered to both the Lord President and the Secretary of State before the expiry of the period of six weeks beginning with the date on which the application is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]

**3.**[^c-2c23efbb40192e23b65f0e81b7eea963] At the same time as an application under section 25 is submitted to the Lord President and the Secretary of State, the body making the application shall place an advertisement mentioning the matters referred to in paragraph 4 below in the Edinburgh Gazette and in a daily newspaper circulating throughout Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]

**4.**[^c-4e67db3b7bb24c53fa6d75b4fa014003] An advertisement such as referred to in paragraph 3 above shall state that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]

- (a) a copy of the draft scheme referred to in section 25(2) of this Act will be available for public inspection at a specified place for a period of six weeks beginning with the date on which the advertisement appears; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]
- (b) a copy of the draft scheme will be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]
  - (i) sent, free of charge, to any person on request; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2] made available for public inspection at a suitable place in that person’s locality; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]
- (c) any person may make written representations concerning the draft scheme to the Lord President and the Secretary of State; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]
- (d) any such representations are to be delivered within the period of six weeks beginning with the date on which the application is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-27c14eff59bf9badfbcf0b36ee7363a2]
