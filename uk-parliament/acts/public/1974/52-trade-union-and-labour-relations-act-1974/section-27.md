# Trade Union and Labour Relations Act 1974, section 27

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Expenses.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 1992-10-16).**

There shall be defrayed out of moneys provided by Parliament—

- (a) any administrative expenses incurred by the Secretary of State in consequence of the provisions of this Act; and
- (b) any increase attributable to the provisions of this Act in the sums payable out of moneys so provided under any other enactment.
