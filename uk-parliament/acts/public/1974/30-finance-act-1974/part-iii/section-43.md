## 43. Special rates of charge for development gains accruing to trustees, personal representatives or unit trust schemes.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) *Income arising in a year of assessment by virtue of section* 38(2) *of this Act to trustees or to the personal representatives of a deceased person as such or to an unauthorised unit trust shall* (*unless chargeable to income tax under any of the following provisions of this section*) *be chargeable to income tax at a rate equal to the sum of the basic rate and the additional rate**[^c1702975]for that year.*[^c1702976]. . .
