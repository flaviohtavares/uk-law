# Consumer Credit Act 1974, section 188

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 188. Examples of use of new terminology.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 2 shall have effect for illustrating the use of terminology employed in this Act.

(2) The examples given in Schedule 2 are not exhaustive.

(3) In the case of conflict between Schedule 2 and any other provision of this Act, that other provision shall prevail.

(4) The Treasury[^c-7c21ea5552a896198c5b61449315784b] may by order amend Schedule 2 by adding further examples or in any other way.
