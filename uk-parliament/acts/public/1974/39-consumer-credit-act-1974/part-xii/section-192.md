# Consumer Credit Act 1974, section 192

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 192. Transitional and commencement provisions, amendments and repeals.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The provisions of Schedule 3 shall have effect for the purposes of this Act.

(2)[^c2437681] The appointment of a day for the purposes of any provision of Schedule 3 shall be effected by an order of the Secretary of State made by statutory instrument; and any such order shall include a provision amending Schedule 3 so as to insert an express reference to the day appointed.

(3)[^c2437691] Subject to subsection (4)—

- (a) the enactments specified in Schedule 4 shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in that Schedule (being minor amendments or amendments consequential on the preceding provisions of this Act), and
- (b) the enactments specified in Schedule 5 are hereby repealed to the extent shown in column 3 of that Schedule.

(4)[^c2437701] The Secretary of State shall by order made by statutory instrument provide for the coming into operation of the amendments contained in Schedule 4 and the repeals contained in Schedule 5, and those amendments and repeals shall have effect only as provided by an order so made.
