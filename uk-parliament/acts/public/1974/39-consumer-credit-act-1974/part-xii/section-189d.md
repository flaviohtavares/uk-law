# Consumer Credit Act 1974, section 189D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/39

## 189D. Section 189B: temporary provision[^c-7a1d4a4ada529928b53e8783ca8dd21b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the period beginning on the date when this section comes into force and ending on 31st March 2014, the table in Schedule 2A is to be read as if it included the following entries—

| Section of this Act | References to “debtor” are to be read as references to the… |
| --- | --- |
| Sections 16, 16A, 16B, 16C | \- improver |
| Section 20 | \- improver<br>\- first bill payer |
| Section 40 | \- improver (including an improver who is not an individual)<br>\- current bill payer<br>\- previous bill payer |
| Section 55A | \- improver<br>\- first bill payer, except for the purposes of subsection (1)(b) |
| Section 55B | \- improver<br>\- first bill payer |
| Section 82A | \- improver<br>\- current bill payer<br>\- previous bill payer |
| Section 145 | \- current bill payer<br>\- previous bill payer |
| Section 149 | \- improver (including an improver who is not an individual)<br>\- current bill payer<br>\- previous bill payer |
| Section 160A | \- improver |

(2) This section ceases to have effect on 1st April 2014.
