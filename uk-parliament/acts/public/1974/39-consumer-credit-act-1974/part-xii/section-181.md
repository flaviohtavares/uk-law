## 181. Power to alter monetary limits etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Treasury[^key-ca6ee194c80ca70882e783e21738c1ef] may by order made by statutory instrument amend, or further amend, any of the following provisions of this Act so as to reduce or increase a sum mentioned in that provision, namely, sections [^key-fd4b443104a4d99b699da16ca33f535f]... 17(1), [^c18854421]. . . [^key-fd4b443104a4d99b699da16ca33f535f]... 70(6), 75(3)(b), 77(1), 78(1), 79(1), 84(1), 101(7)(a), 107(1), 108(1), 109(1), 110(1), [^key-fd4b443104a4d99b699da16ca33f535f]... [^c18854421]. . . 140B(6),[^c18854341] 155(1) and 158(1).

(2) An order under subsection (1) amending section [^key-729d9b191e7172c2eeba9efc41cc4ff8]... 17(1), [^c18854441]. . . [^key-729d9b191e7172c2eeba9efc41cc4ff8]... 75(3)(b) [^c18854441]. . . or 140B(6)[^c18854361] shall be of no effect unless a draft of the order has been laid before and approved by each House of Parliament.
