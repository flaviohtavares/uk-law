# Consumer Credit Act 1974, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Duty to supply copy of unexecuted agreement : excluded agreements.[^key-b3ed3d7418de52cbf09593187caac02f][^c20968451]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If in the case of a regulated agreement which is an excluded agreement[^c20968471] the unexecuted agreement is presented personally to the debtor or hirer for his signature, but on the occasion when he signs it the document does not become an executed agreement, a copy of it, and of any other document referred to in it, must be there and then delivered to him.

(2) If the unexecuted agreement is sent to the debtor or hirer for his signature, a copy of it, and of any other document referred to in it, must be sent to him at the same time.

(3) A regulated agreement which is an excluded agreement[^c20968491] is not properly executed if the requirements of this section are not observed.

(4)[^c20968511] In this section, “**excluded agreement**” has the same meaning as in section 61A.
