# Consumer Credit Act 1974, section 55

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Disclosure of information.[^key-a4fac25ec30a4b4665a3d8e5ebeeb9ac]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulations may require specified information to be disclosed in the prescribed manner to the debtor or hirer before a regulated agreement , other than a regulated deferred payment credit agreement,[^key-b9795ea2f0c115c8cbf8dc807042eaba] is made.

(2)[^c20968201] If regulations under subsection (1) are not complied with, the agreement is enforceable against the debtor or hirer on an order of the court only (and for these purposes a retaking of goods or land to which the agreement relates is an enforcement of the agreement).
