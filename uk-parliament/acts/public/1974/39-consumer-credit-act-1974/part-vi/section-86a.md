# Consumer Credit Act 1974, section 86A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86A. FCA to prepare information sheets on arrears and default[^c18854861][^c-3f5deedd18ef8554d12e2cba4a4af73f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The FCA shall prepare and issue[^c-e9be2b0b8b4a322c7e13ed38b64efc17] an arrears information sheet and a default information sheet.

(2) The arrears information sheet shall include information to help debtors and hirers who receive notices under section 86B or 86C.

(3) The default information sheet shall include information to help debtors and hirers who receive default notices.

(4) Regulations may make provision about the information to be included in an information sheet.

(5) An information sheet takes effect for the purposes of this Part at the end of the period of three months beginning with the day on which it is issued[^c-7ebd19117a99255247e9064304a74ea6] or on such later date as the[^c-9174a26cbc56ae2ab8dee3eec9f70630] FCA may specify in relation to the information sheet .

(6)[^c-8630b5a44e82b2da5d90c442641a666d] If the FCA revises an information sheet after it has been issued, it shall issue the revised information sheet.

(7) A revised information sheet takes effect for the purposes of this Part at the end of the period of three months beginning with the day on which it is issued[^c-e7527339a7c046c8b8be4923477f1dd1] or on such later date as the[^c-91355636c7e0965cc3bc340451505478] FCA may specify in relation to the information sheet .
