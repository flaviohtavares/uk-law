## 161. Enforcement authorities.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following authorities ( “enforcement authorities ”) have a duty to enforce this Act and regulations made under it—

- (a)[^c-4a6826cb25faf08395949015daa6a17b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) in Great Britain, the local weights and measures authority,
- (c) in Northern Ireland, the Department of Commerce for Northern Ireland.

(1A)[^c-258597d587280033b8486dd519c7d587] Subsection (1) does not limit any function of the FCA in relation to the enforcement of this Act or regulations made under it.

(1B)[^c-9bc859319ba27a727ed042e3cf253ada] For the investigatory powers available to a local weights and measures authority or the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Investment in Northern Ireland for the purposes of the duty in subsection (1), see Schedule 5 to the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

(2) [^c4984101]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Every local weights and measures authority shall, whenever the FCA[^c-badc0bf5c2b950c1dfc5f8bb743ad112] requires, report to it[^c4984251] in such form and with such particulars as it requires on the exercise of their functions under this Act.
