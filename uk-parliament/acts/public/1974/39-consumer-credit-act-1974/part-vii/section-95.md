# Consumer Credit Act 1974, section 95

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Rebate on early settlement.[^c-ba5dbbfc08f426407c5d65fc0bf5d675][^c-a3b21ac258b89f4f4e76f3732f7f029d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulations may provide for the allowance of a rebate of charges for credit to the debtor under a regulated consumer credit agreeement where, under section 94, on refinancing, on breach of the agreement, or for any other reason, his indebtedness is discharged or is discharged in part[^c20969491] or becomes payable before the time fixed by the agreement, or any sum becomes payable by him before the time so fixed.

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) may provide for calculation of the rebate by reference to any sums paid or payable by the debtor or his relative under or in connection with the agreement (whether to the creditor or some other person), including sums under linked transactions and other items in the total charge for credit.
