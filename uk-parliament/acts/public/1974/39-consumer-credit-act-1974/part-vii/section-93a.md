> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/39

## 93A. Summary diligence not competent in Scotland.[^c18572511]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Summary diligence shall not be competent in Scotland to enforce payment of a debt due under a regulated agreement or under any security related thereto.
