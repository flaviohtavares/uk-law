## 154. Prohibition of canvassing certain ancillary credit services off trade premises.[^c-4f03fcec483f33616d3435a41ca36fef]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

It is an offence to canvass off trade premises the services of a person carrying on a business of credit-brokerage, debt-adjusting , debt-counselling or the provision of credit information services[^c18853811].
