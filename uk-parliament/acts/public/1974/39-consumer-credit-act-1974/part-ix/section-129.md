# Consumer Credit Act 1974, section 129

>

## 129. Time orders.[^c2431881][^c-12f892c4c9eacd7da70ec0529dda59d0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to subsection (3) below,[^c2431891] if it appears to the court just to do so—

- (a) on an application for an enforcement order; or
- (b) on an application made by a debtor or hirer under this paragraph after service on him of—
  - (i) a default notice, or
  - (ii) a notice under section 76(1) or 98(1); or
- (bza)[^c-c59af5f012580dc5fa5ded4580376d65] on an application made by a debtor under this paragraph after the creditor has informed the debtor of the creditor’s intention—
  - (i) to enforce a term of a regulated deferred payment credit agreement by—
    - (aa) demanding earlier payment of any sum,
    - (bb) treating any right conferred on the debtor by the agreement as terminated, restricted or deferred, or
  - (ii) to terminate a regulated deferred payment credit agreement, or
- (ba)[^c18853261] on an application made by a debtor or hirer under this paragraph after he has been given a notice under section 86B or 86C; or
- (c) in an action brought by a creditor or owner to enforce a regulated agreement or any security, or recover possession of any goods or land to which a regulated agreement relates,

the court may make an order under this section (a “time order ”).

(2) A time order shall provide for one or both of the following, as the court considers just—

- (a) the payment by the debtor or hirer or any surety of any sum owed under a regulated agreement or a security by such instalments, payable at such times, as the court, having regard to the means of the debtor or hirer and any surety, considers reasonable;
- (b) the remedying by the debtor or hirer of any breach of a regulated agreement (other than non-payment of money) within such period as the court may specify.

(3)[^c2431901] Where in Scotland a time to pay direction or a time to pay order has been made in relation to a debt, it shall not thereafter be competent to make a time order in relation to the same debt.
