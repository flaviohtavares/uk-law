# Consumer Credit Act 1974, section 136

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 136. Power to vary agreements and securities.[^key-efdda79961ad1ed6a006fb9ee92d6dd3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The court may in an order made by it under this Act include such provision as it considers just for amending any agreement or security in consequence of a term of the order.
