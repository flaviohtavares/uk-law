## 15. Consumer hire agreements.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A consumer hire agreement is an agreement made by a person with an individual (the “hirer ”) for the bailment or (in Scotland) the hiring of goods to the hirer, being an agreement which—

- (a) is not a hire-purchase agreement, and
- (b) is capable of subsisting for more than three months, [^c18818381]. . .
- (c) [^c18818381]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-ffaac92bd3efdd1bb65506861b04d93f] A consumer hire agreement is a regulated agreement with the meaning of this Act if it is a regulated consumer hire agreement for the purposes of Chapter 14B of Part 2 of the Regulated Activities Order.
