# Friendly Societies Act 1974, section 19

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Acknowledgment of registration of amendments of rules.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

On being satisfied that any amendment of the registered rules of a society or branch is not contrary to the provisions of this Act, the FCA[^key-74f0d76602cb0f08a6c7b3e424d0ec6d] shall issue to the society or branch, in respect of that amendment, an acknowledgment of registration which shall be conclusive evidence that the amendment is duly registered.
