# Friendly Societies Act 1974, section 16

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Appeals from refusal to register.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c1073037] If the FCA[^key-66c118d3f9fdb8d04494e08aa8b22e06] refuses to register a branch, the branch may appeal to the High Court or, in the case of a branch whose registered office is to be situated in Scotland, the Court of Session.

(2) If a refusal to register a [^c1073038]. . . branch is overruled on appeal, the FCA shall give an acknowledgment of registration under section 15A[^c1073040] above to the [^c1073038]. . . branch.
