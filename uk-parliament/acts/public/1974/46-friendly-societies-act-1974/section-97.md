# Friendly Societies Act 1974, section 97

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. Notice of proceedings or order to set aside dissolution.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where any person takes proceedings to set aside the dissolution of a society or branch, he shall give notice of the proceedings to the FCA[^key-6d19e9493b21452de4395e9b847fc43a] and, if the society or branch is a PRA-authorised person, the PRA not later than the expiry of whichever of the following periods first expires, namely,—

- (a) the period of seven days after the commencement of the proceedings; and
- (b) the period of three months referred to in section 94(7)(a) above or, as the case may require, section 95(6)(a) or 95A(4)(a)[^c1073546] above.

(2) Where an order is made setting aside the dissolution of a society or branch, the society or branch shall give notice of the order to the FCA and, if the society or branch is a PRA-authorised person, the PRA within seven days after the order is made.
