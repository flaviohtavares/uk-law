# Friendly Societies Act 1974, section 104A

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 104A. Fees for inspection or copying of documents.[^c1073618]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before the FCA[^c-4dadfa8b2dd23d0228a9a7140f33475a] allows any person to inspect any document held by it in connection with this Act, or provides any person with a copy of any such document (or part of such document), it may charge that person a reasonable fee.
