# Friendly Societies Act 1974, section 68

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Devolution of sums due where no nomination.[^c1073301]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If any member of a registered society or branch entitled from the funds thereof to a sum not exceeding £5,000[^c1073302] dies without having made any nomination thereof then subsisting, the society or branch may, without letters of administration or probate of any will or, in Scotland, without any grant of confirmation, distribute the sum among such persons as appear to the committee, upon such evidence as they may deem satisfactory, to be entitled by law to receive that sum.
