# Friendly Societies Act 1974, section 21

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Duty to supply copies of rules.[^c1073054][^c1073053]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Every registered society or branch shall deliver to any person on demand, on payment of a reasonable fee[^c1073055], a copy of the rules of the society or branch.
