# Friendly Societies Act 1974, section 109

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 109. Regulations.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Treasury may make regulations with respect to—

- (a)[^c1073640] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the seal to be used by the[^c1073641] FCA[^key-a2f7c44de91a76b87264d2089cd9eaea] for the registration of any society or document (including any amendment to any document) under this Act, the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014[^key-ffe851ad9d808e858b56f4e55c47d040], the Building Societies Act 1986, or the Friendly Societies Act 1992, or otherwise for sealing any document for the purposes of this Act or any of those Acts;
- (c) the duties and functions of the FCA[^key-92c5046e53f5a0ddaf9aed2b31b4bfd0][^c1073642] and of the PRA under this Act; and
- (d) the inspection of documents kept by the FCA under this Act;

and generally for carrying this Act into effect.

(2) Any power of the Treasury, or the Secretary of State[^c1073644] to make regulations under this Act shall be exercisable by statutory instrument and a statutory instrument made in the exercise of any such power, other than an instrument containing only regulations under section [^c1073645]. . . 42 above, shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
