# Friendly Societies Act 1974, section 47

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Power to set up funds for purchase of Government securities on behalf of members.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c1073257] A registered society or branch may in accordance with its rules set up and administer a fund for the purchase, on behalf of members contributing thereto, of Defence Bonds or National Savings Certificates or such other securities of Her Majesty’s Government in the United Kingdom as the Treasury[^c1073258] may prescribe.

(2) A registered society or branch may, in amending its rules for the purpose of this section, make provision for enabling persons to become members of the society for the purpose only of contributing to the said fund and without being entitled to any rights as members other than rights as contributors to the said fund.
