# Friendly Societies Act 1974, section 39C

> Text as at 2016-10-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39C. Power of appropriate authority to require accounts of past years to be audited.[^key-05f765e250d8397d6fd39a9884c0fa79]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c1073146] The appropriate authority[^key-13b527d84eec504dc1fa378e5bfe28c5] may give a direction to a registered society or branch in respect of any relevant year of account of the society or branch before the year of account in which the direction is given—

- (a) requiring it to appoint a qualified auditor or qualified auditors to audit its accounts and balance sheet for that year, and
- (b) where it has sent to the[^c1073147] FCA[^key-4d4eafb9e159ec9c68078dea6673157f] and, if the registered society or branch is a PRA-authorised person, the PRA its annual return for that year before the date of the direction, requiring it, after its accounts and balance sheet have been audited by a qualified auditor, to send the FCA and, if the registered society or branch is a PRA-authorised person, the PRA within three months from receipt of the direction a further annual return complying with the requirements of this Act (other than that as to time of sending).

(2) A failure by a registered society or branch to comply with any direction given by the appropriate authority[^key-2c84c5ea1d360075b687bc6808409d05] under subsection (1) above shall be an offence under this Act.

(3) For the purposes of subsection (1) above, a year of account of a registered society or branch is relevant year of account if it is one at the end of which there is in force in relation to it a disapplication under section 32A(1) above.
