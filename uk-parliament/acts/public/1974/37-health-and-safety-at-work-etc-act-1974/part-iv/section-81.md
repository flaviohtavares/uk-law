# Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974, section 81

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Expenses and receipts.[^c7815741][^c20459811][^key-8be0b28a95ada425c9187e0e22c48e90]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenses incurred by a Minister of the Crown or government department for the purposes of this Act; and
- (b) any increase attributable to the provisions of this Act in the sums payable under any other Act out of money so provided;

and any sums received by a Minister of the Crown or government department by virtue of this Act shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund.
