Proceedings for an offence under any of the relevant statutory provisions shall not, in England and Wales, be instituted except by an inspector , the Environment Agency or the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^c-f02b19e2c544a3b9fa2befb9b5f7c6ef][^M_F_78b6ddd5-22ef-47a7-c4e6-1f6d1fcef43c] or by or with the consent of the Director of Public Prosecutions.
