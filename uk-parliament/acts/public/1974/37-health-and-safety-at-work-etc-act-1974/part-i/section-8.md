# Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Duty not to interfere with or misuse things provided pursuant to certain provisions.[^c7806831][^c7806841][^c20459251][^key-0fe72d9a6077d9cfd7b83aac041509d3][^key-e7294dca4d75cba999cd802287726b57]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No person shall intentionally or recklessly interfere with or misuse anything provided in the interests of health, safety or welfare in pursuance of any of the relevant statutory provisions.
