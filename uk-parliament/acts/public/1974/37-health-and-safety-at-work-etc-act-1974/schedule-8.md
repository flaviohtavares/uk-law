## SCHEDULE 8 (affected by repeal, see note) — Transitional Provisions with respect to Fire Certificates under Factories Act 1961 or Offices, Shops and Railway Premises Act 1963[^c18928581][^c7815991][^c7816001]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 78.

**1.**[^c18928601] In this Schedule— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928601]

- “**the 1971 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928601]**” means the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928601] [^c7816011]Fire Precautions Act 1971; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928601]
- “**1971 Act certificate *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928601]**” means a fire certificate within the meaning of the 1971 Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928601]
- “**Factories Act certificate *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928601]**” means a certificate under section 40 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928601] [^c7816021]Factories Act 1961 (means of escape in case of fire-certification by fire authority); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928601]
- “**Offices Act certificate *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928601]**” means a fire certificate under section 29 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928601] [^c7816031]Offices, Shops and Railway Premises Act 1963. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928601]

**2.**[^c18928701] (1) Where by virtue of an order under section 1 of the 1971 Act a 1971 Act certificate becomes required in respect of any premises at a time when there is in force in respect of those premises a Factories Act certificate or an Offices Act certificate (“the existing certificate”), the following provisions of this paragraph shall apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928701]

(2) The existing certificate shall continue in force (irrespective of whether the section under which it was issued remains in force) and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928701]

- (a) shall as from the said time be deemed to be a 1971 Act certificate validly issued with respect to the premises with respect to which it was issued and to cover the use or uses to which those premises were being put at that time; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928701]
- (b) may (in particular) be amended, replaced or revoked in accordance with the 1971 Act accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928701]

(3) Without prejudice to sub-paragraph (2)(b) above, the existing certificate, as it has effect by virtue of sub-paragraph (2) above, shall as from the said time be treated as imposing in relation to the premises the like requirements as were previously imposed in relation thereto by the following provisions, that is to say— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928701]

- (a) if the existing certificate is a Factories Act certificate, the following provision of the Factories Act 1961, namely sections 41(1), 48 (except subsections (5), (8) and (9)), 49(1), 51(1) and 52(1) and (4) and, so far as it relates to a proposed increase in the number of persons employed in any premises, section 41(3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928701]
- (b) if the existing certificate is an Offices Act certificate the following provisions of the Offices, Shops and Railway Premises Act 1963, namely section 30(1), 33, 34(1) and (2), 36(1) and 38(1) and, so far as it relates to a proposed increase in the number of persons employed to work in any premises at any one time, section 30(3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18928701]
