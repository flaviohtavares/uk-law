# Solicitors Act 1974, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/47
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7. Entry of name and restoration of name struck off.

*Extent: E+W*

On production—

- (a)[^c19643621] of written evidence of admission of any person as a solicitor by the Society,
- (b) of an order for the restoration to the roll of the name of a person whose name has been struck off it, or[^c13345191]
- (c) of an order under section 47(2)(h) for the restoration of a person’s name to the roll,

and on payment to the Society of such fee [^c13345201]. . . as the Society[^c19643691] may from time to time determine, the Society shall enter the name of that person on the roll.
