# Solicitors Act 1974, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/47
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. Costs where unqualified person acts as solicitor.[^c13347561]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c19794711][^c13347571] No costs in respect of anything done by any unqualified person acting as a solicitor shall be recoverable by him, or by any other person, in any action, suit or matter.

(2) Nothing in subsection (1) shall prevent the recovery of money paid or to be paid by a solicitor on behalf of a client in respect of anything done by the solicitor while acting for the client without holding a practising certificate in force if that money would have been recoverable if he had held such a certificate when so acting.
