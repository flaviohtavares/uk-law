# Solicitors Act 1974, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/47
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Non–British subjects as solicitors. 12 & 13 Will. 3. c. 2.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Nothing in section 3 of the Act of Settlement (which provides among other things that aliens are incapable of enjoying certain offices or places of trust) shall be taken to disqualify a person from becoming or practising as a solicitor of the Senior Courts or of the Court of Judicature[^c19175321].
