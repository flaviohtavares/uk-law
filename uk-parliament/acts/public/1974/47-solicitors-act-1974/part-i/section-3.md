## 3. Admission as solicitor.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) [^key-724d69e1ff01665f9cbfddc179623f23]... No person shall be admitted as a solicitor unless he has obtained a certificate from the Society that the Society—

- (a) is satisfied that he has complied with training regulations, and
- (b) is satisfied as to his character and his suitability to be a solicitor.

(2) Any person who has obtained a certificate that the Society is satisfied as mentioned in subsection (1) may apply to the Society[^c19643071] to be admitted as a solicitor; and if any such person so applies, the Society , [^c13345111]. . ., shall, unless cause to the contrary is shown to its[^c19643131] satifaction, in writing, and in such manner and form as the Society may from time to time think fit, admit that person to be a solicitor.
