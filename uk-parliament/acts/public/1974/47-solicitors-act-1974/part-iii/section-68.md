# Solicitors Act 1974, section 68

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/47
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Power of court to order solicitor to deliver bill, etc.[^c20575091][^c13351481]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The jurisdiction of the High Court to make orders for the delivery by a solicitor of a bill of costs, and for the delivery up of, or otherwise in relation to, any documents in his possession, custody or power, is hereby declared to extend to cases in which no business has been done by him in the High Court.

(2) The county court[^key-c196587d5f68103a95099c199c1b97f7] and the family court each[^key-19fcb21414eac95e182db9b10ca335d3] have the same jurisdiction as the High Court to make orders making such provision as is mentioned in subsection (1) in cases where the bill of costs or the documents relate wholly or partly to contentious business done by the solicitor in the county[^key-b53e790b703134409a93dcc55df6805b] court or (as the case may be) the family court[^key-e17809c6d16d4c659ae450e698c19e9a].

(3) In this section and in sections 69 to 71 “**solicitor**” includes the executors, administrators and assignees of a solicitor.
