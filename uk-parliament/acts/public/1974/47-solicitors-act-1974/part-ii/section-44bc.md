# Solicitors Act 1974, section 44BC

## 44BC. Information offences[^c20571021][^key-326fe26fefaec5a7a8f7b524493607d6][^c19662701]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-05f40bf42f926597a1be03c2ae8d62d2][^c19662901] It is an offence for a person who knows or suspects an investigation into any of the matters mentioned in section 44B(3)(a) to (d) is being or is likely to be conducted—

- (a) to falsify, conceal, destroy or otherwise dispose of a document which the person knows or suspects is or would be relevant to the investigation, or
- (b) to cause or permit the falsification, concealment, destruction or disposal of such a document.

(2)[^key-05f40bf42f926597a1be03c2ae8d62d2][^c19663091] In proceedings for an offence under subsection (1) it is a defence for the accused to show that the accused had no intention of concealing facts disclosed by the documents from the person conducting the investigation.

(3)[^key-c737a902775b11bc1f5bd68c5d08848c][^c19662991] It is an offence for a person, in purported compliance with a requirement imposed on the person under section 44B, 44BA or 44BB—

- (a) to provide information which the person knows to be false or misleading in a material particular, or
- (b) recklessly to provide information which is false or misleading in a material particular.

(4)[^key-83c6eef8ce2ae4f467736b4c066a3e8e][^key-c737a902775b11bc1f5bd68c5d08848c][^c19663091][^c19663121] A person who is guilty of an offence under subsection (1) or (3) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-e4b11fe3e412ac38f8942361aa8252ca] or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or a fine, or both.

(5)[^key-83c6eef8ce2ae4f467736b4c066a3e8e][^key-e2934b646c16525764db9fef846c653a][^c19663091][^c19663121] In relation to an offence under subsection (1) or (3) committed before 2 May 2022[^key-cee210e6011b0c5fc2ef0002bd607914] the reference in subsection (4)(a) to the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-80df515f9314730307ce460833d97bac] is to be read as a reference to 6 months.
