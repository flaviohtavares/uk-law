# Solicitors Act 1974, section 83

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/47
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. Power of Society to inspect file of proceedings in bankruptcy of solicitor.[^c20575361][^c13352161]

*Extent: E+W*

Where proceedings in bankruptcy have been taken against any solicitor, the Society shall be entitled—

- (a) to inspect the file of those proceedings without payment of any fee; and
- (b) to be supplied with office copies of those proceedings on payment of the usual charge.
