# Control of Pollution Act 1974, section 78

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Cable burning.[^c2458391][^c2458401]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 1993-08-27).**

(1)[^c2458411] A person who burns insulation from a cable with a view to recovering metal from the cable shall be guilty of an offence under this subsection unless the burning is part of a process subject to Part I of the Environmental Protection Act 1990 or the[^c2458421] place at which he does so is a work registered in pursuance of section 9 of the Alkali Act.

(2)[^c2458431] A person who contravenes the provisions of the preceding subsection shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale[^c2458441];
