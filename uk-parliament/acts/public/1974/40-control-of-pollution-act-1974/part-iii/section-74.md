## 74. Penalties.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) . . . [^c2457991], a person guilty of an offence against this Part of this Act shall be liable on summary conviction—

- (a) in the case of a first offence against this Part of this Act, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fedabd7ebfcb1a611d9e3b561ebe76e2] to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale[^c2458001]; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8] in the case of a second or subsequent offence against this Part of this Act, to a fine not exceeding *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8] level 5 on the standard scale[^c2458011] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8], *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8]

together, in any case, with a further fine not exceeding £50 for each day on which the offence continues after the conviction. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8] In determining whether an offence is a second or subsequent offence against this Part of this Act, account shall be taken of any offence— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8] under section 24 of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8] [^c2458021]Public Health (Scotland) Act 1897 by way of contravening a decree or interdict relating to noise; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8] under section 95 of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8] [^c2458031]Public Health Act 1936 by way of contravening a nuisance order relating to noise, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8] ; or[^c2458041] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8] under section 80(4) of the Environmental Protection Act 1990, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8]

as if it were an offence against this Part of this Act. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1946f4c1307db24f59e3ee84063267b8]
