# Control of Pollution Act 1974, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/40

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Powers of disposal authorities as respects other waste.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Each disposal authority shall have power to collect information about, and to make arrangements for the disposal of, waste which is situated or likely to be situated in its area and is not controlled waste; but nothing in sections 91 to 94 of this Act shall apply to functions conferred on an authority or information collected by an authority in pursuance of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-706ee603730b04d402aed0e8fe1d080f]
