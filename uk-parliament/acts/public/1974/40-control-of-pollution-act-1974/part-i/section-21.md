# Control of Pollution Act 1974, section 21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/40

## 21. Production of heat and electricity from waste etc.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a] A disposal authority may, subject to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a] subsection (2)[^c2444151] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a] of this section,— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a]

- (a) use waste belonging to the authority for the purpose of producing from it heat or electricity or both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a]
- (b) establish and operate, within or outside its area, such generating stations and other installations as the authority thinks fit for the purpose aforesaid; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a]
- (c) where the authority operates an installation in which waste is usually used as the main fuel for the purpose of producing heat or electricity, then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a]
  - (i) in the case of an installation for producing heat, use other fuel in addition to waste to produce the heat, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a] in the case of an installation for producing electricity, use other fuel to assist in burning the waste to produce the electricity, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a]

  and, in an emergency, use other fuel instead of waste to produce the heat or electricity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a]

and a disposal authority may use, sell or otherwise dispose of any heat *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a] or electricity[^c2444161] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a] produced by the authority by virtue of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a]

(2)[^c2444171] Nothing in subsection (1) of this section shall be construed as exempting a disposal authority from the requirements of Part I of the Electricity Act 1989. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a]

(4) Subsection (6) of section 12 of this Act (except paragraph (b) of that subsection) and subsection (7) of that section (except so much of it as relates to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a] [^c2444181]Pipe-lines Act 1962) shall have effect in relation to a disposal authority as if the reference in the said subsection (6) to the collection of waste in pursuance of that section included the conveying of heat produced by the authority by virtue of this section and of air, steam and water heated by such heat. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a]

(5) It shall be the duty of a disposal authority by which an installation for producing heat is operated in pursuance of this section in any year to furnish to the Secretary of State, as soon as practicable after the end of that year, such particulars relating to the installation and heat produced at it as are prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a]

(6) Nothing in this section *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a] [^c2444191]. . . shall be construed as prejudicing any power exercisable by a disposal authority apart from this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3598b53e6ae11e138bb566d96e08fe8a]
