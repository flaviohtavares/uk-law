## 20. Reclamation of waste.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Without prejudice to the powers of disposal authorities apart from this section, any disposal authority may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-35d148f95cfbc5a714b1f82cd7715c98]

- (a) do such things as the authority considers appropriate for the purpose of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-35d148f95cfbc5a714b1f82cd7715c98]
  - (i) enabling waste belonging to the authority, or belonging to another person who requests the authority to deal with it in pursuance of this section, to be used again, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-35d148f95cfbc5a714b1f82cd7715c98]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-35d148f95cfbc5a714b1f82cd7715c98] enabling substances to be reclaimed from such waste; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-35d148f95cfbc5a714b1f82cd7715c98]
- (b) buy or otherwise acquire waste with a view to its being used again or to the reclamation of substances from it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-35d148f95cfbc5a714b1f82cd7715c98]
- (c) use, sell or otherwise dispose of waste belonging to the authority or anything produced from such waste. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-35d148f95cfbc5a714b1f82cd7715c98]
