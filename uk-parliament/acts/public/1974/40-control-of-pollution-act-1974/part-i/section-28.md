# Control of Pollution Act 1974, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Supplementary provisions relating to pipes.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-48e3201536299f16491eec9e8263429d] Where an authority provides pipes in pursuance of section 12(6), 14(5), 15(2), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-48e3201536299f16491eec9e8263429d] or 21(4)[^c2445441] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-48e3201536299f16491eec9e8263429d] of this Act, it shall be the duty of the authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-48e3201536299f16491eec9e8263429d]

- (a) except where the authority is a collection authority and the pipes are situated in its area, to send to the collection authority in whose area the pipes are situated a map . . . *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-48e3201536299f16491eec9e8263429d] [^c2445461] showing the location of the pipes; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-48e3201536299f16491eec9e8263429d]
- (b) where the authority is a collection authority and the pipes are situated in its area, to prepare such a map; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-48e3201536299f16491eec9e8263429d]

and it shall be the duty of an authority by which a map is received in pursuance of paragraph (a) of this subsection or is prepared in pursuance of paragraph (b) of this subsection to secure that a copy of the map is available at its principal offices for inspection by the public free of charge at all reasonable hours. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-48e3201536299f16491eec9e8263429d]

In the application of this subsection to Scotland, the words “the authority is a collection authority and” in paragraphs (a) and (b) shall be omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-48e3201536299f16491eec9e8263429d]

(2)[^c2445471] Section 25 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-48e3201536299f16491eec9e8263429d] [^c2445481]Public Health Act 1936 (under which the erection of buildings over a sewer or drain may be prevented or controlled by a local authority or, on appeal, by a magistrates’ court) shall have effect as if references to a drain included any pipe provided as mentioned in the preceding subsection and as if the reference to the map of sewers required by that Act to be kept deposited at the offices of an authority included any map required by the preceding subsection to be kept available at the offices of the authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-48e3201536299f16491eec9e8263429d]

(3) Section 21 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-48e3201536299f16491eec9e8263429d] [^c2445491]Sewerage (Scotland) Act 1968 (under which the erection of buldings over a sewer vested in a local authority may be prevented or controlled by the authority or, on appeal, by the sheriff) shall have effect as if the reference to a sewer vested in a local authority included any pipe provided as mentioned in subsection (1) of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-48e3201536299f16491eec9e8263429d]

(4) References to pipes in the preceding provisions of this section include associated works. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-48e3201536299f16491eec9e8263429d]
