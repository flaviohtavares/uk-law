# Control of Pollution Act 1974, section 16

## 16. Removal of waste deposited in breach of licensing provisions.[^c2443911]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49] If any controlled waste is deposited on any land *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49] in contravention of section 3(1) of this Act, any authority to which this section applies may[^c2443921] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49] serve a notice on the occupier of the land requiring him— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

- (a) to remove the waste from the land within a period specified in the notice, which shall not be less than twenty-one days beginning with the date of service of the notice; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]
- (b) to take within such a period such steps as are so specified with a view to eliminating or reducing the consequences of the deposit of the waste, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

or requiring him both to remove the waste as mentioned in paragraph (a) of this subsection and to take such steps as are mentioned in paragraph (b) of this subsection within such a period as aforesaid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

(2) A person served with a notice in pursuance of the preceding subsection may within the twenty-one days aforesaid appeal to a magistrates’ court against the notice; and on any such appeal the court shall quash the notice if it is satisfied that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

- (a) the appellant neither deposited nor caused nor knowingly permitted the deposit of the waste on the land; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]
- (b) service of the notice on the appellant was not authorised by the preceding subsection; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]
- (c) there is a material defect in the notice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

and in any other case shall either modify the notice or dismiss the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

(3) Where a person appeals against a notice in pursuance of this section, the notice shall be of no effect pending the determination of the appeal; and where the court modifies the notice or dismisses the appeal it may extend the period specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

(4) If a person on whom a notice is served in pursuance of subsection (1) of this section fails to comply with the notice, then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

- (a) he shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49] level 5 on the standard scale[^c2443931] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49] and a further fine not exceeding £50 for each day on which the failure continues after conviction for the offence and before the authority which served the notice has begun to exercise its powers in pursuance of the following paragraph; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]
- (b) the said authority may do what that person was required by the notice to do and may recover from him any expenses reasonably incurred by the authority in doing it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

(5) If it appears to such an authority as is mentioned in subsection (1) of this section that waste has been deposited as there mentioned and that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

- (a) in order to remove or prevent pollution of water or danger to public health it is necessary forthwith to remove the waste or to take other steps with a view to eliminating or reducing the consequences of the deposit of it or necessary forthwith to remove the waste and to take such other steps; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]
- (b) there is no occupier of the land in question; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]
- (c) the occupier of the land neither made nor knowingly permitted the deposit of the waste, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

the authority may remove the waste from the land or take such other steps as aforesaid or, as the case may require, may remove it and take such other steps. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

(6) Where an authority exercises in respect of any land a power conferred on it by the preceding subsection it shall be entitled to recover the cost of doing so and of disposing of any waste removed in the exercise of the power— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

- (a) in a case falling within paragraph (a) of that subsection, from the occupier of the land unless he proves that he neither made nor caused nor knowingly permitted the deposit in question; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]
- (b) in any case, from any person who deposited or caused or knowingly permitted the deposit of any of the waste in question on the land, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

except such of the cost as the occupier or other person shows was incurred unnecessarily. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

(7) Any waste removed by an authority in pursuance of this section shall belong to the authority and may be dealt with accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

(8)[^c2443951] The authorities to which this section applies are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]

- (a) the appropriate Agency; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]
- (b) any collection authority in whose area the land mentioned in subsection (1) above is situated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-47253b0c52e88b59f730300d7079ab49]
