# Control of Pollution Act 1974, section 98

## 98. Interpretation of Part V.[^c2460471][^c-30de31ba538ae3fa16a2bd080e35ff2b][^c2460461]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Part of this Act—

- “**functions**” includes powers and duties; and
- “**relevant authority**” means—
  - (a) in England [^c2460481] ., the Secretary of State, a water authority[^c2460491], a county council, . . . [^c2460501], a district council, a London borough council, the Common Council of the City of London, the Sub-Treasurer of the Inner Temple and the Under Treasurer of the Middle Temple and, for the purposes of sections 91 to 93 of this Act, a sewerage undertaker[^c2460511]; and
  - (aa) in Wales, the Secretary of State, a county council or a county borough council and, for the purposes of sections 91 to 93 of this Act, a sewerage undertaker; and[^c2460521]
  - (b) in Scotland—[^c2460531]
    - (i) as respects sections 91 and 92, a council constituted under section 2 of the [^c2460541]Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994; and
    - (ii) as respects this Part other than those sections, the Secretary of State, SEPA or a council constituted under section 2 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994.
