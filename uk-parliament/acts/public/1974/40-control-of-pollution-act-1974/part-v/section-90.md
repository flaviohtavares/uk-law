## 90. Establishment charges and interest in respect of certain expenses of authorities.[^key-e2f165098168d5a3c5c627aa547f8ca7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c2459361] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Where a sum is payable to a[^c2459371] relevant authority by any person by virtue of this Act in respect of the expenses incurred by the authority or by virtue of section 36 of the [^c2459391]Local Government Act 1974 in respect of establishment charges related to such expenses or by virtue of section 193 of the [^c2459401]Local Government (Scotland) Act 1947 in respect of general expenses related to such expenses, then—

- (a) the authority and that person may agree that the sum [^c2459411]... shall be paid in instalments; and
- (b) the authority shall be entitled to receive from that person interest on the sum [^c2459411]... , or on such portion of it as is for the time being unpaid, at such reasonable rate or rates as the authority may determine[^c2459431].

(3) In the application of this section to Scotland, for the references to a water authority there shall be substituted references to SEPA[^c2459441].
