# Control of Pollution Act 1974, section 99

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1974/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 99. Alteration of penalties.[^c2460601]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The enactments mentioned in Schedule 2 to this Act shall have effect subject to the provisions of that Schedule (which alter the penalties for the offences to which those enactments relate).
