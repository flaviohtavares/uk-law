# Local Government Act 1974, Schedule 5A

## Schedule 5A — Matters not subject to investigation under Part 3A[^c-87e5972399b73eef7ae37381e483b52d]

*Extent: E+W*

Section 34B

**1.** A matter which could be the subject of an investigation by a Local Commissioner under Part 3.

**2.** A matter which could be the subject of an investigation by the Health Service Commissioner under the HSCA 1993.

**3.** The commencement or conduct of civil or criminal proceedings before any court of law.

**4.** Action taken in respect of appointments or removals, pay, discipline, superannuation or other personnel matters.
