## 10. Supplementary provisions for Part I.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c779566] The following bodies are local authorities for the purposes of this Part of this Act, namely,—

- (a) the council of a county,
- (b) the Greater London Council,
- (c) the council of a district,
- (d) the council of a London borough,
- (e) the Common Council of the City of London, and
- (f) the Council of the Isles of Scilly,
