## 33. Consultation between Local Commissioner, the Parliamentary Commissioner and the Health Service Commissioners (affected by repeal, see note) and other Commissioners and Ombudsmen.[^c780305][^c-014f1ed4511158e52dc81abbc74ed272]

*Extent: W*

(1) If, at any stage in the course of conducting an investigation under this Part of this Act, a Local Commissioner forms the opinion that the matters which are the subject of the investigation include[^c-6ddbfe3b25aab8cc7bf69a7ac5885e56] a matter which could be the subject of an investigation—

- (a) by the Parliamentary Commissioner, in accordance with section 5 of the Act of 1967,
- (aza)[^c-4c25c98a4975aefb39e75b7d4411a9ce] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (aa)[^c-35131bdefbc0f2bd44b3cd2f87f88f56] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

[^c-d3cc3456f497fe11c6dfb45456e73b33]...

- (b) by the Health Service Commissioner for England [^c-dece1715dbb2a129a3ffe68562f2698a]..., in accordance with the Act of 1993,[^c780307]

  or[^c-3811e94245db974a24644cdd4eadeebe]

*Extent: E*

- (bza)[^c-1a112bd7d528e9567946091ad053cc30] by a housing ombudsman under the Housing Act 1996,
- (ba)[^c-f5f1a60ffc99d69036949e892de7ccd4] by the Public Services Ombudsman for Wales, in accordance with the Public Services Ombudsman (Wales) Act 2005;
- (c) by the Scottish Public Services Ombudsman in accordance with the Act of 2002,

he shall consult with the appropriate Commissioner or[^c780306] [^c-0c33cf1ad914237de993acccfc5cd5c3]... Ombudsman about the matter and, where a complaint was made about the matter, he shall[^c-6131f902e65c10dc29c9f1d951fc8fc8] , if he considers it necessary, inform the person initiating the complaint under this Part of this Act of the steps necessary to initiate a complaint under the Act of 1967 , [^c-173a8165725dacf1ef5ec54f7268954b]... the Act[^c-cf4cb47a23920ca722bee2775243f538] of 1993 , the Public Services Ombudsman (Wales) Act 2005[^c-8f3fc5f6dda96c48fee664982d52b514] or under the Act of 2002 , as the case may be.

(2) Where, by virtue of subsection (1) above, a Local Commissioner consults the Parliamentary Commissioner, a housing ombudsman, *(Extent: E)*[^c-98c6a866a1eacc02eec99b663b0e4932] the Public Services Ombudsman for Wales[^c-407907c4cc35f4682c207352981ff1a4] , the Scottish Public Services Ombudsman[^c-279529a4aaa225956dbe6da2abbbd758] or the Health Service Commissioner for England[^c-c9c6cb790defc3851566cf24b06541ac] in relation to a matter under investigation under this Part of this Act[^c-39e3343cce85288b57effed1768df50a] , he may consult that Commissioner or[^c780312] that Ombudsman[^c-8f2e42add079a5acd7dc9510dc1ceb61] about anything relating to the matter[^c-4aeef9c3cada01670e0ee5fda313843d] , including—

- (a) the conduct of any investigation into the matter[^c-271f0ec7a9498224853e54f6061754e6] , and
- (b) the form, content and publication of any report of the results of such an investigation.

(3)[^c780313] If, at any stage in the course of conducting an investigation under the Act of 1967, the Parliamentary Commissioner forms the opinion that the complaint relates partly to a matter which could be the subject of investigation under this Part of this Act, he shall consult with the appropriate Local Commissioner about the complaint and, if he considers it necessary, inform the person initiating the complaint [^c780314]. . . of the steps necessary to initiate a complaint under this Part of this Act.

*Extent: E*

(3A)[^c-405d7819f71fa9023674c517356cbaca] If at any stage in the course of conducting an investigation under the Housing Act 1996, a housing ombudsman forms the opinion that the complaint relates partly to a matter which could be the subject of an investigation under this Part of the Act, the ombudsman must consult with the appropriate Local Commissioner about the complaint and, if the ombudsman considers it necessary, inform the person initiating the complaint of the steps necessary to initiate a complaint under this Part of this Act.

(4) Where, by virtue of subsection (3) or (3A) *(Extent: E)*[^c-61bb1cf9bd12b4b21587a3b3f6189fc3] above, a Local Commissioner is consulted about a complaint under the Act of 1967 or under the Housing Act 1996 *(Extent: E)*[^c-6b41c4ae4e01e9f84bc9174b8fcf8b33] [^c780315]. . ., subsection (2) above shall apply (with the necessary modifications) as it applies in relation to consultations held by virtue of subsection (1) above.

(5) Nothing in section 11(2) of the Act of 1967, [^c-e1c01405051ac83b9dec90852f048094]... in section 15 of the Act of 1993[^c780316], in[^c780317] section[^c-3818038fae3c9bb8b8a5e5f8aedb0345] 34X[^c-4cd6e782aa9b448db857f2e08282c4ff] of the Public Services Ombudsman (Wales) Act 2005 , in section 19 of[^c-df31bc5eeec58163a781be43eeead9c3] the Act of 2002 or in section 32(2) of this Act (restrictions of disclosure of information) shall apply in relation to the disclosure of information . . .[^c780318] in the course of consultations held in accordance with this section.

(6) In this section the “**Act of 1967**” means the [^c780319]Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967 and the “[^c780320]**Act of 1993**” means the Health Service Commissioners Act 1993. and[^c-1d125c9c632f064fcb62a9e0a16b002b] the Act of 2002” means the Scottish Public Services Ombudsman Act 2002
