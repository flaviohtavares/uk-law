## 23A. Annual reports for representatives etc.[^c779614][^c779613]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the financial year ending in 1990 and for each subsequent financial year, the Commission[^c-5bd1e4b423031cd3cc26fda8c10dbe9f] shall prepare a general report on the discharge of their functions (other than those under Part 3A of this Act)[^c-6d69555555c4abac808e0255dd70db8d] (an “annual report”)[^c-77d666a8ba66fa0c60f0c49b347708b7] and shall submit it—

- (a) to such persons as appear to the Commission to represent authorities [^c-b053e24d5b0d697f1eb405cb6c00760a]... to which this Part of this Act applies, and
- (b) in the case of such authorities as are not so represented, to those authorities.

(2) The annual report[^c-86ad131553fb9873dfa929f058f27b38] shall be submitted as soon as may be after the Commission have received the reports for the year from Local Commissioners under section 23(11) above, and the Commission[^c-a1ef37b70e09c155cb9b26e8eb5f95e7] shall submit copies of those reports, together with their annual report[^c-b3ba906a089551eb0504d0a9704fc08f] .

(3) The Commission[^c-3582599cae9ae865e0c66c34daf78e26] shall arrange for the publication of the annual report[^c-1e9b16a499789c52d258a791d3b8cd6b] and of the reports of which copies are submitted by them under subsection (2) above.

(3A)[^c-196356321faab19d2a55dae9f9019206] The Commission must lay a copy of the annual report before Parliament.
