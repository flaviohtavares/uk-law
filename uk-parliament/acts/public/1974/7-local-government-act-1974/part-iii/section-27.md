## 27. Members of the public .[^c780063][^key-623ee9f1eaffdf4a3d5b600ed7dc458c][^c780062]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part of this Act, “[^key-585d08e6514895a632ce568dad9d5c10]**member of the public**” means an individual or a body of persons, whether incorporated or not, other than—

- (a) a local authority or other authority or body constituted for purposes of the public service or of local government (including[^c780064] the Welsh Ministers, the National Assembly for Wales Commission or the[^key-a6e8a933d03c06fe7b4bfd04bca77903] the National Assembly for Wales), or for the purposes of carrying on under national ownership any industry or undertaking or part of an industry or undertaking;
- (b) any other authority or body whose members are appointed by Her Majesty or any Minister of the Crown or government department or by the[^c780065] Welsh Ministers[^key-f095e96a677461190428d00e4b496d56] , or whose revenues consist wholly or mainly of moneys provided by Parliament or the Welsh Ministers .
