## 33ZA. Collaborative working between Local Commissioners and other Commissioners[^c-e1adfeecff7c916d30593966bc4f7486]

*Extent: W*

(1) If at any stage in the course of conducting an investigation under this Part of this[^M_F_7eb7d1fb-a3b5-427b-d3d3-59b0fa8ff956] Act a Local Commissioner forms the opinion that the matters which are the subject of the investigation include[^M_F_17687ce2-4127-4435-8b2e-80abb3f60cc3] a matter within the jurisdiction of —

- (a) the Parliamentary Commissioner,
- (b) the Health Service Commissioner for England, or
- (c) both *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b7b42e04-b686-4a99-e09f-052c83fd26ff] a housing ombudsman *(Extent: E)*,

he may, subject to subsection (2) below, conduct an investigation under this Part of this Act jointly with that Commissioner or those Commissioners. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_84f18be7-7de4-4f95-b3d7-b52461d3de8c] jointly with any also-involved ombudsman or jointly with any two or more also-involved ombudsmen. *(Extent: E)*

(1A (Extent: E))[^M_F_4135ffdd-1c4b-4ed8-b501-a29ddd392f9c] In subsection (1) “also-involved ombudsman” means a person within subsection (1)(a), (b) or (c) who, in the opinion of the Local Commissioner concerned, has jurisdiction in relation to a matter that is included among the matters which are the subject of the Local Commissioner’s investigation. *(Extent: E)*

(2) A Local Commissioner must obtain the consent of the person affected[^M_F_3bb2cb31-2504-4664-f76f-6b50891add83] or the complainant (if any)[^M_F_2a468f08-fbb6-4188-dec2-95334d81c788] before agreeing to a joint investigation referred to in subsection (1) above.

(3) If a Local Commissioner forms the opinion that a complaint which is being investigated by—

- (a) the Parliamentary Commissioner,
- (b) the Health Service Commissioner, or
- (c) both *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_757e6fe9-2095-41f0-cf75-dfab71527271] a housing ombudsman *(Extent: E)*,

relates partly to a matter within his jurisdiction by virtue of this Part[^M_F_eb0d8671-4bf7-4b27-819e-b068b9631d9e], he may conduct an investigation under this Part of this[^M_F_7ade4b79-e2f2-463c-f0f5-d80b19d90549] Act jointly with that Commissioner or those Commissioners. *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ae8b5645-1173-470e-b06b-4245ff45ffc0] jointly with a person within paragraph (a), (b) or (c) who is investigating the complaint or jointly with any two or more such persons. *(Extent: E)*

(4) If a Local Commissioner conducts an investigation [^M_F_cdb9bbc9-e250-4046-b6de-9e7a77a48eb6]... jointly with another person, the requirements of section 30(1) (so far as relating to a case where the Local Commissioner conducts an investigation under this Part of this[^M_F_de2c4e1f-27c8-410e-d506-21d09dc77165] Act) may be satisfied by a report made jointly with that person.

(5) In relation to an investigation which has been conducted jointly with another person a direction given by the Local Commissioner under section 30(7) of this Act may relate only to those parts of a joint report prepared by virtue of subsection (4) above which are specified in the direction.
