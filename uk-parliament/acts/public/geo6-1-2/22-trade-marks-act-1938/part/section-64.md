# Trade Marks Act 1938, section 64

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Trusts and equities.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-10-31).**

(1) There shall not be entered in the register any notice of any trust express, implied or constructive, nor shall any such notice be receivable by the Registrar.

(2) Subject to the provisions of this Act, equities in respect of a trade mark may be enforced in like manner as in respect of any other personal property.
