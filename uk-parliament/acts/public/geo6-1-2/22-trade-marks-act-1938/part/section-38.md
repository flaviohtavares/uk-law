# Trade Marks Act 1938, section 38

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Sheffield marks.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-10-31).**

The provisions of the Second Schedule to this Act shall have effect with respect to the master, wardens, searchers, assistants, and commonalty of the Company of Cutlers in Hallamshire, in the county of York (in this Act called “the Cutlers’ Company”), and the marks or devices assigned or registered by the master, wardens, searchers and assistants of that Company.
