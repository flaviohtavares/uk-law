# Trade Marks Act 1938, section 44

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Power of Registrar to award costs.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-10-31).**

In all proceedings before the Registrar under this Act, the Registrar shall have power to award to any party such costs as he may consider reasonable, and to direct how and by what parties they are to be paid, and any such order may, by leave of the Court or a judge thereof, be enforced in the same manner as a judgment or order of the Court to the same effect.
