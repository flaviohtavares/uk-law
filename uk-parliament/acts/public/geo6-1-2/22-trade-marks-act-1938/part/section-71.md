# Trade Marks Act 1938, section 71

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Short title and extent.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-10-31).**

(1) This Act may be cited as the Trade Marks Act 1938.

(2)[^c610008] *This Act shall come into operation on the date fixed by order made under subsection (5) of section thirty-three of the [^c610009]Trade Marks (Amendment) Act, 1937, for the coming into operation of that Act (in this Act referred to as “**the appointed day**”), immediately after the coming into operation of that Act.*

(3) It is hereby declared that this Act extends to Northern Ireland.

(4) This Act shall extend to the Isle of Man.
