# Trade Marks Act 1938, section 30

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Use of one of associated or substantially identical trade marks equivalent to use of another.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-10-31).**

(1) Where under the provisions of this Act use of a registered trade mark is required to be proved for any purpose, the tribunal may, if and so far as the tribunal thinks right, accept use of an associated registered trade mark, or of the trade mark with additions or alterations not substantially affecting its identity, as an equivalent for the use required to be proved.

(2) The use of the whole of a registered trade mark shall for the purposes of this Act be deemed to be also a use of any registered trade mark, being a part thereof, registered in the name of the same proprietor by virtue of subsection (1) of section twenty-one of this Act.
