# Trade Marks Act 1938, section 56

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Evidence of orders, &c. of Board of Trade.[^c609981]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-10-31).**

(1) All documents purporting to be orders made by the Board of Trade and to be sealed with the seal of the Board, or to be signed by a secretary or an under-secretary or an assistant secretary of the Board, or by any person authorised in that behalf by the President of the Board, shall be received in evidence, and shall be deemed to be such orders without further proof, unless the contrary is shown.

(2) A certificate, signed by the President of the Board of Trade, that any order made or act done is the order or act of the Board, shall be conclusive evidence of the fact so certified.
