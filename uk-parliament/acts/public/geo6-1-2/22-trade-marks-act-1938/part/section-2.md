# Trade Marks Act 1938, section 2

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. No action for infringement of unregistered trade mark.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-10-31).**

No person shall be entitled to institute any proceeding to prevent, or to recover damages for, the infringement of an unregistered trade mark, but nothing in this Act shall be deemed to affect rights of action against any person for passing off . . . [^c609931] or the remedies in respect thereof.
