# Trade Marks Act 1938, section 45

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Annual reports of Registrar.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-10-31).**

The Comptroller-General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks shall, in his annual report on the execution by or under him of the [^c609977]Patents and Designs Act 1907, and Acts amending that Act, include a report respecting the execution by or under him of this Act as if it formed a part of or was included in those Acts.
