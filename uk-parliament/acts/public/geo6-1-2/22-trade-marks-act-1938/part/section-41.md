# Trade Marks Act 1938, section 41

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Fees.[^c609976]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-10-31).**

There shall be paid in respect of applications and registration and other matters under this Act such fees as may be, with the sanction of the Treasury, prescribed by the Board of Trade.
