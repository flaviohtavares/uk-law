# Trade Marks Act 1938, section 65

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Saving for jurisdiction of courts in Scotland, Northern Ireland and Isle of Man.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-10-31).**

Where by this Act any act has to be done by or to any person in connection with a trade mark or proposed trade mark or any procedure relating thereto, the act may under and in accordance with the rules, or in particular cases by special leave of the Board of Trade, be done by or to an agent of that person duly authorised in the prescribed manner.
