## 8. Superannuation allowances.[^c574333]

The principal civil service pension scheme within the meaning of section 2 of the [^c574334]Superannuation Act 1972 and for the time being in force shall apply in relation to persons appointed to offices in the Court of Session as it applies in relation to persons to whom section 1 of that Act applies.
