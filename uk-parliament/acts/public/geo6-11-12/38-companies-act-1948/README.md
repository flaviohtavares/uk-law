# Companies Act 1948

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

Companies Act 1948

1948 Chapter 38

An Act to consolidate the Companies Act, 1929, the Companies Act, 1947 (other than the provisions thereof relating to the registration of business names, bankruptcy and the prevention of fraud in connection with unit trusts), and certain other enactments amending the first-mentioned Act.

\[30th June 1948\]

Be it enacted by the King's most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—
