### PART II — Limitations on Operation of Part I.

**1.** Where in the case of a company limited by guarantee and having a share capital or an unlimited company having a share capital, an increase of share capital is made at the same time as an increase of membership, the company shall pay whichever fee is the higher, but not both.

**2.** The total of the fees payable by any company by reference to its membership shall in no case exceed twenty pounds.

**3.** The total of the fees payable by any company by reference to its share capital or of the fees payable by it by reference to its membership and the fees payable by it by reference to its share capital, shall in no case exceed fifty pounds.
