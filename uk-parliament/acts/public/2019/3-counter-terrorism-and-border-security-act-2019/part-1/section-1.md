# Counter-Terrorism and Border Security Act 2019, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2023-12-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2019/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Expressions of support for a proscribed organisation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 12 of the Terrorism Act 2000 (support), after subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) A person commits an offence if the person—
>
> - (a) expresses an opinion or belief that is supportive of a proscribed organisation, and
> - (b) in doing so is reckless as to whether a person to whom the expression is directed will be encouraged to support a proscribed organisation.
