# Counter-Terrorism and Border Security Act 2019, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2023-12-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2019/3
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Serious crime prevention orders

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 1 to the Serious Crime Act 2007 (serious offences for the purposes of serious crime prevention orders) is amended as follows.

(2) In Part 1 (serious offences in England and Wales), after paragraph 2 insert—

> **2A. Terrorism**
>
> An offence for the time being listed in section 41(1) of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (offences to which Part 4 of that Act applies: terrorism offences).

(3) In Part 1A (serious offences in Scotland), after paragraph 16B insert—

> **16BA. Terrorism**
>
> An offence for the time being listed in section 41(1) of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (offences to which Part 4 of that Act applies: terrorism offences).

(4) In Part 2 (serious offences in Northern Ireland), after paragraph 18 insert—

> **18A. Terrorism**
>
> An offence for the time being listed in section 41(1) of the Counter-Terrorism Act 2008 (offences to which Part 4 of that Act applies: terrorism offences).
