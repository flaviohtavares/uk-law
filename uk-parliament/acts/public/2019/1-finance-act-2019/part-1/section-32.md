# Finance Act 2019, section 32

> Text as at 2026-05-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Temporary increase in annual investment allowance

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-643de1167917772f50ad8975626cd920] In relation to expenditure incurred during *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-643de1167917772f50ad8975626cd920] the period beginning with 1 January 2019 and ending with 31 March 2023[^key-63a363853f93f02e0db5a384112ff4d4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-643de1167917772f50ad8975626cd920], section 51A of CAA 2001 (entitlement to annual investment allowance) has effect as if in subsection (5) the amount specified as the maximum allowance were £1,000,000. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-643de1167917772f50ad8975626cd920]

(2) Schedule 13 contains provision about chargeable periods which straddle 1 January 2019 or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-643de1167917772f50ad8975626cd920] 1 April 2023[^key-182a74d51b08ffe8a06b791437a8a2ca] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-643de1167917772f50ad8975626cd920].
