# Extradition Act 2003, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Withdrawal of warrant while appeal to Supreme Court pending[^c2087051][^c2087990][^c2087990]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if at any time in the relevant period the Supreme Court[^c2087983][^c2087983] is informed by the designated authority that a Part 1 warrant issued in respect of a person has been withdrawn.

(2) The relevant period is the period—

- (a) starting when leave to appeal to the Supreme Court[^c2087983] is granted to the person or the authority which issued the warrant;
- (b) ending when proceedings on the appeal are discontinued or the Supreme Court[^c2087983] makes its decision on the appeal.

(3) If the appeal is brought by the person in respect of whom the warrant was issued the Supreme Court[^c2087983] must—

- (a) order the person’s discharge;
- (b) quash the order for his extradition, in a case where the appeal was against a decision of the High Court to dismiss an appeal under section 26.

(4) If the appeal is brought by the authority which issued the warrant the Supreme Court[^c2087983] must dismiss the appeal.

(5) If the person is not before the Supreme Court[^c2087983] at the time it orders his discharge, the Supreme Court[^c2087983] must inform him of the order as soon as practicable.
