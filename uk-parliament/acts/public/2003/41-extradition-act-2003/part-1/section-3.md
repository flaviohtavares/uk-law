## 3. Arrest under certified Part 1 warrant[^c2087003]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if a certificate is issued under section 2 in respect of a Part 1 warrant issued in respect of a person.

(2) The warrant may be executed by a constable or a customs officer in any part of the United Kingdom.

(3)[^c2087916] The warrant may be executed by a service policeman anywhere, but only if the person is subject to service law or is a civilian subject to service discipline.

(5)[^c2087916] The warrant may be executed even if neither the warrant nor a copy of it is in the possession of the person executing it at the time of the arrest.
