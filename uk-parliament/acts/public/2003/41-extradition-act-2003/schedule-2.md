# Extradition Act 2003, SCHEDULE 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/41

## SCHEDULE 2 — Trade and Cooperation Agreement list[^c2087349][^c-63954a168c233f84c287a9507cd245f1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 215

**1.**[^c2087350] Participation in a criminal organisation.

**2.**[^c2087351] Terrorism.

**3.**[^c2087352] Trafficking in human beings.

**4.**[^c2087353] Sexual exploitation of children and child pornography.

**5.**[^c2087354] Illicit trafficking in narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances.

**6.**[^c2087355] Illicit trafficking in weapons, munitions and explosives.

**7.**[^c2087356] Corruption , including bribery[^c-1a0a24c7dab76edeee67803c4b65cdf0].

**8.**[^c2087357] Fraud, including that affecting the financial interests of the European Union[^c-da8d32033eb2ae9270f6786bda9e1844] within the meaning of the Convention of 26 July 1995 on the protection of the European Union financial interests.

**9.**[^c2087358] Laundering of the proceeds of crime.

**10.**[^c2087359] Counterfeiting currency, including of the euro.

**11.**[^c2087360] Computer-related crime.

**12.**[^c2087361] Environmental crime, including illicit trafficking in endangered animal species and in endangered plant species and varieties.

**13.**[^c2087362] Facilitation of unauthorised entry and residence.

**14.**[^c2087363] Murder, grievous bodily injury.

**15.**[^c2087364] Illicit trade in human organs and tissue.

**16.**[^c2087365] Kidnapping, illegal restraint and hostage-taking.

**17.**[^c2087366] Racism and xenophobia.

**18.**[^c2087367] Organised or armed robbery.

**19.**[^c2087368] Illicit trafficking in cultural goods, including antiques and works of art.

**20.**[^c2087369] Swindling.

**21.**[^c2087370] Racketeering and extortion.

**22.**[^c2087371] Counterfeiting and piracy of products.

**23.**[^c2087372] Forgery of administrative documents and trafficking therein.

**24.**[^c2087373] Forgery of means of payment.

**25.**[^c2087374] Illicit trafficking in hormonal substances and other growth promoters.

**26.**[^c2087375] Illicit trafficking in nuclear or radioactive materials.

**27.**[^c2087376] Trafficking in stolen vehicles.

**28.**[^c2087377] Rape.

**29.**[^c2087378] Arson.

**30.**[^c2087379] Crimes within the jurisdiction of the International Criminal Court.

**31.**[^c2087380] Unlawful seizure of aircraft/ships/spacecraft[^c-870c49d4e82d43018912d16998ade85d].

**32.**[^c2087381] Sabotage.
