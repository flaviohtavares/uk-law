> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 209. Reasonable force[^c2087296]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person may use reasonable force, if necessary, in the exercise of a power conferred by this Act.
