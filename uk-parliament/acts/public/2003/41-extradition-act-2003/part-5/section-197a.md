# Extradition Act 2003, section 197A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 197A. Extradition of serving prisoner[^c2087767]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If an order is made under Part 1 or 2 for the extradition of a person who is in custody[^c2088197][^c2088197] serving a sentence of imprisonment or another form of detention in the United Kingdom, the order is sufficient authority for the person to be removed from the prison or other institution where he is detained.
