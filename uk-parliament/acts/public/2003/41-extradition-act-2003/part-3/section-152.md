# Extradition Act 2003, section 152

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 152. Remission of punishment for other offences[^c2087221]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) a person is extradited to the United Kingdom from a territory;[^c2088184][^c2088184]
- (b) before his extradition he has been convicted of an offence in the United Kingdom;
- (c) he has not been extradited in respect of that offence.

(2) The sentence for the offence must be treated as served[^c2088186][^c2088186] but the person’s conviction for the offence must be treated as a conviction for all other purposes.
