# Extradition Act 2003, section 98

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 98. Deferral: person serving sentence in United Kingdom[^c2087147]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) the appropriate judge sends a case to the Secretary of State under this Part for his decision whether a person is to be extradited;
- (b) the person is in custody[^c2088169][^c2088169] serving a sentence of imprisonment or another form of detention in the United Kingdom.

(2) The Secretary of State may defer making a decision with regard to the person’s extradition until the person is released from detention pursuant to the sentence (whether on licence or otherwise)[^c2088171][^c2088171].
