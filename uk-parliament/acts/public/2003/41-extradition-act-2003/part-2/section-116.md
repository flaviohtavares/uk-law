# Extradition Act 2003, section 116

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116. Appeals: general[^c2087167]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-61db8fdf066046d537ac2186d85135aa] A decision under this Part of the judge or the Secretary of State may be questioned in legal proceedings only by means of an appeal under this Part.

(2)[^c-7f83021743ef38295f5d048709f790b3] Subsection (1) does not prevent an appeal against a determination of a devolution issue.

(3) In this Part “ **devolution issue** ” has the same meaning as in Schedule 6 to the Scotland Act 1998.
