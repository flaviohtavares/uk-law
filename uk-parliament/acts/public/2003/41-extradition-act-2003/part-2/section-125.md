# Extradition Act 2003, section 125

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 125. Withdrawal of request while appeal to Supreme Court pending[^c2087178][^c2088041][^c2088041]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if at any time in the relevant period the Supreme Court[^c2088042][^c2088042] is informed by the Secretary of State that a request for a person’s extradition has been withdrawn.

(2) The relevant period is the period—

- (a) starting when leave to appeal to the Supreme Court[^c2088042] is granted to the person whose extradition is requested or a person acting on behalf of the category 2 territory to which his extradition is requested;
- (b) ending when proceedings on the appeal are discontinued or the Supreme Court[^c2088042] makes its decision on the appeal.

(3) If the appeal is brought by the person whose extradition is requested the Supreme Court[^c2088042] must—

- (a) order the person’s discharge;
- (b) quash the order for his extradition, in a case where the appeal was against a decision of the High Court to dismiss an appeal under section 103 or 108.

(4) If the appeal is brought by a person acting on behalf of the category 2 territory the Supreme Court[^c2088042] must dismiss the appeal.

(5) If the person whose extradition is requested is not before the Supreme Court[^c2088042] at the time it orders his discharge, the Supreme Court[^c2088042] must inform him of the order as soon as practicable.
