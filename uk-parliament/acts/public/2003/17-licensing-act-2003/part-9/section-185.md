# Licensing Act 2003, section 185

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/17

## 185. Provision of information

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies to

- (a)[^c-d1d20eeca785008bc09bdc84f0d09d92] information which is held by or on behalf of a licensing authority or a responsible authority (including information obtained by or on behalf of the authority before the coming into force of this section) , and[^c-08a17cfa0dea00cd0a7edae58dc12af3]
- (b) information which is held by or on behalf of the Mayor of London in connection with the Mayor’s functions under this Act.

(2) Information to which this section applies may be supplied—

- (a) to a licensing authority,[^c-52b0f464df1c30f590f3ae04f442ab1f]...
- (b) to a responsible authority, or[^c-af78ef136f0aa7ef7721fde398fa630d]
- (c) to the Mayor of London,

for the purposes of facilitating the exercise of the authority’s functions or the Mayor’s functions[^c-3dc49e6e43759c56ff14377a39bd687d] under this Act.

(3) Information obtained by virtue of this section must not be further disclosed except to a licensing authority , responsible authority or the Mayor of London[^c-346c314b99b7995656dd9d7b12d43dff] for the purposes mentioned in subsection (2).

(4) In this section “**responsible authority**” means a responsible authority within the meaning of Part 3 or 4.
