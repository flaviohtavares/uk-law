# Licensing Act 2003, section 175

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 175. Exemption for incidental ... lottery[^c22729991][^c-f742c55b7933f2fb9a5a1698cbe7e59d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The promotion of a lottery to which this section applies shall not constitute a licensable activity by reason only of one or more of the prizes in the lottery consisting of or including alcohol, provided that the alcohol is in a sealed container.

(2) This section applies to an incidental [^c-8962f34d6ecf1ab017f198db9093c35a]... lottery (within the meaning of Part 1 of Schedule 11 to the Gambling Act 2005).
