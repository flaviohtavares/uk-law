# Licensing Act 2003, section 181

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/17

> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 181. Appeals against decisions of licensing authorities or the Mayor of London[^c21020121][^key-485b391a04df1685c406371d44ceca3c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Schedule 5 (which makes provision for appeals against decisions of licensing authorities or the Mayor of London[^key-7ec5f4f12cad6e3d5a7d73c9733e2817]) has effect.

(2) On an appeal in accordance with that Schedule against a decision of a licensing authority or the Mayor of London[^key-a6c8ff1b2127780ba8b872b48cdb23ed], a magistrates' court may—

- (a) dismiss the appeal,
- (b) substitute for the decision appealed against any other decision which could have been made by the licensing authority or (as the case may be) the Mayor[^key-d5980fe437e71d03815446da8ced4f27], or
- (c) remit the case to the licensing authority or (as the case may be) the Mayor[^key-ee521a3baa0c7ab7b9d442ca51947db9] to dispose of it in accordance with the direction of the court,

and may make such order as to costs as it thinks fit.
