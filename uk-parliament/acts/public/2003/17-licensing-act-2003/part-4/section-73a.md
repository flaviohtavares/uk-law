# Licensing Act 2003, section 73A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 73A. Mandatory conditions relating to the supply of alcohol to members or guests[^c21016171]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a club premises certificate authorises the supply of alcohol to members or guests, the certificate must include any conditions specified in an order under section 73B and applicable to the certificate.
