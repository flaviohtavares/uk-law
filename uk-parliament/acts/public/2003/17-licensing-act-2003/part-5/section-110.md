## 110. Theft, loss, etc. of temporary event notice or statement of conditions[^c21019711][^key-0592638b11759398d1a1588cf04ade9a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a temporary event notice acknowledged under section 102 is lost, stolen, damaged or destroyed, the premises user may apply to the licensing authority which acknowledged the notice (or, if there is more than one such authority, any of them) for a copy of the notice.

(1A)[^key-13cbd29f613fd999fadd2251a8e048bd] Where a statement of conditions that is given under section 106A(3) is lost, stolen, damaged or destroyed, the premises user may apply to the licensing authority which gave the statement for a copy of the statement.

(2) No application may be made under this section more than one month after the end of the event period specified in the notice.

(3) The application must be accompanied by the prescribed fee.

(4) Where a licensing authority receives an application under this section, it must issue the premises user with a copy of the notice or statement[^key-3997e85da6af55b17ea43363a3e7080e] (certified by the authority to be a true copy) if it is satisfied that—

- (a) the notice or statement[^key-affe015bd1534df4fa52a12166a234ee] has been lost, stolen, damaged or destroyed, [^key-60cbc9ede11b464e56fc1639bd966960]...
- (b)[^key-60cbc9ede11b464e56fc1639bd966960] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) The copy issued under this section must be a copy of the notice or statement[^key-7835140bdabf9d13a359346b4ad5b3e3] in the form it existed immediately before it was lost, stolen, damaged or destroyed.

(6) This Act applies in relation to a copy issued under this section as it applies in relation to an original notice or statement[^key-419562c1a642c8a31d87ebfc4dcb6b45].
