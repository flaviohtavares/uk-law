## 104. Objection to notice by a relevant person[^key-0a811706bb20b8bb76e159ca991c6f7d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-48ca451fad2a1cd77f86cd82b2becac7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1A)[^key-109216b8378c6d8ab607e631bda9d895] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-89369138996454a1598ae6c8b7831b63] Where a relevant person who is given a temporary event notice is satisfied that allowing the premises to be used in accordance with the notice would undermine a licensing objective, the relevant person must give a notice stating the reasons for being so satisfied (an “objection notice”)—

- (a) to the relevant licensing authority,
- (b) to the premises user, and
- (c) to every other relevant person.

(3) The objection notice must be given before the end of the[^c20999881] third[^key-ffe4c8254901687a9448af0dc14a22bf] working day following the day on which the relevant person[^key-dc0293ee93499a2d48f5bbb188352a03] is given the temporary event notice[^key-93b16f3cbe06929eabf7494188a8b4d3].

(4) Subsection (2) does not apply at any time after the relevant person[^key-b78096b713380a279186b4d49fd52906] has received a copy of a counter notice under section 107 in respect of the temporary event notice.

(5)[^key-ca5e2396457589f6d8c6b269b6f97cdb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
