## 106. Modification of standard temporary event notice following ... objection[^key-e7071b7284d4cce3f9b1ca0d3955197c][^key-f5d5b6db5745afe03026b37d6cf65c7b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where a relevant person[^key-131e37773835f59eb9e8b171206b1e21] has given an objection notice under section 104(2) in respect of a standard[^key-513ec23b7c98f6f2d8d1ae25c2430898] temporary event notice (and the objection notice has not been withdrawn).

(2) At any time before a hearing is held or dispensed with under section 105(2), the relevant person[^key-9caa96724be4d61beb6ce4fa65456c0c] may, with the agreement of the premises user and each other relevant person[^key-747d7a15ea9709cdce96d90685355082], modify the temporary event notice by making changes to it[^key-1d0d2dbc93b6dd7093f88ebf96ea71bd].

(3) Where a temporary event notice is modified under subsection (2)—

- (a) the objection notice is to be treated for the purposes of this Act as having been withdrawn from the time the temporary event notice is modified, and
- (b) from that time—
  - (i) this Act has effect as if the temporary event notice given under section 100 had been the notice as modified under that subsection, and
  - (ii) to the extent that the conditions of section 98 are satisfied in relation to the unmodified notice they are to be treated as satisfied in relation to the notice as modified under that subsection.

(4) A copy of the temporary event notice as modified under subsection (2) must be sent or delivered by the relevant person[^key-2607d74e8499f740a045a199a0dcfbe8] to the relevant licensing authority before a hearing is held or dispensed with under section 105(2).

(5)[^key-d74c0e276befe82859d0695435fba5fd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) This section does not apply if a counter notice has been given under section 107.

(7) In this section “**objection notice**” has the same meaning as in section 104(2).
