## 117. Application for grant ... of personal licence[^c-e071474f115e20bf17a04bc81635a99e]

(2) An application by an individual[^c-5818c8f5fc715e2cffbfe86150e6dc8e] for the grant of a personal licence—

- (a) must, if the applicant is ordinarily resident in the area of a licensing authority, be made to that authority, and
- (b) may, in any other case, be made to any licensing authority.
