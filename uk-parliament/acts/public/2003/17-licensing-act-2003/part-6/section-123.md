# Licensing Act 2003, section 123

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 123. Duty to notify licensing authority of convictions etc during application period[^c-a058cb8f57f3e7706f877082d663470d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where an applicant for the grant [^c-a89933973e27747dbf301989199c9ca8]... of a personal licence is convicted of a relevant offence or a foreign offence during the application period, or is required to pay an immigration penalty during that period[^c-d03652b9a039c6b4cf8f6191c7c7f9fc], he must as soon as reasonably practicable notify the conviction or the requirement to pay (as the case may be)[^c-b6482684e857d3d186b3915590f96da8] to the authority to which the application is made.

(2) A person commits an offence if he fails, without reasonable excuse, to comply with subsection (1).

(3) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale.

(4) In this section “**the application period**” means the period that—

- (a) begins when the application for grant [^c-c2cc2e245f409028fb782a73edfa6e78]... is made, and
- (b) ends when the application is determined or withdrawn.
