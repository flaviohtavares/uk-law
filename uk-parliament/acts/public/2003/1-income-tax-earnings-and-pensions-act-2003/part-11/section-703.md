# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 703

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 703. Introduction

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter provides—

- (a) for employers to make agreements with Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs[^c-39f2618a78119c4266c5b2f8c88275fc] (“PAYE settlement agreements”) under which they agree to be accountable to the Commissioners[^c-e07ab60d9b31e39fdada3876c34274b7] for sums in respect of income tax on general earnings of their employees; and
- (b) for such earnings to be treated for certain purposes of the Income Tax Acts as excluded from the employees' income.
