# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 711

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 711. Right to make a return

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person who has PAYE income for a tax year in respect of which deductions or repayments are made under PAYE regulations may by notice require an officer of Revenue and Customs[^c-4f3a0614079ff004d8042e82d44ecd6f] to give that person a notice under section 8 of TMA 1970 (personal return) for the tax year.

(2) A notice to an officer of Revenue and Customs under subsection (1) must be given no later than 3 years[^c-2b446896c40f4f9466c03b8c28541d8c] after the 31st October next following the tax year.
