# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 79

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Voucher issued under approved scheme

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter does not apply to a cash voucher received by an employee if—

- (a) it is issued under a scheme, and
- (b) at the time when it is received the scheme is a scheme approved by an officer of Revenue and Customs[^key-4f3a0614079ff004d8042e82d44ecd6f] for the purposes of this section.

(2) An officer of Revenue and Customs must not approve a scheme for the purposes of this section unless the officer[^key-dbd0549179cc04574cf33538a1b3f114] is[^key-00cf4a5abc057c51733ffd92968bdb0a] satisfied that it is practicable for income tax in respect of all payments made in exchange for vouchers issued under the scheme to be deducted in accordance with PAYE regulations.
