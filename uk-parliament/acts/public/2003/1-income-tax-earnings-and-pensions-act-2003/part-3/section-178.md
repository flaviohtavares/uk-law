# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 178

## 178. Exception for loans where interest qualifies for tax relief

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A loan is not a taxable cheap loan in relation to a particular tax year if, assuming interest is paid on the loan for that year (whether or not it is in fact paid), the whole of that interest—

- (a) is eligible for relief under section 353 of ICTA or section 383 of ITA 2007 (relief for interest payments)[^c-143ec167bbd427130353de1557057cd3],
- (b) would be eligible for relief under section 353 of[^c-85021f3d57fa4ac3fa2a154420fc0f8c] ICTA but for the fact that it is a payment of relevant loan interest to which section 369 of ICTA applies (mortgage interest payable under deduction of tax),
- (c) is deductible in computing the amount of the profits to be charged to tax[^c-91252a3bea48312f155f0ce183ccc5df] in respect of a trade, profession or vocation carried on wholly or partly in the United Kingdom[^c-4f5cac4877b26ed2aa752048703c753f] by the person to whom the loan is made, or
- (d) is deductible in computing the amount of the profits to be charged to tax in respect of a[^c-8e7ceb278cae2bd0c93d79f269506943] UK property business (within the meaning of Chapter 2 of Part 3 of[^c-873ab622eb1c74c41a1d69d18518f8ec] ITTOIA 2005 or Chapter 2 of Part 4 of CTA 2009) carried on by that person.
