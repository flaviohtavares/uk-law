# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 222

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 222. Payments by employer on account of tax where deduction not possible

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) an employer is treated by virtue of sections 687, 687A,[^c-8eb6c60671d20f2bd0f59466f0b00f4e] 689 , 689A[^c-c9331c7ac628871432489828b515c1b1] and 693 to 700 as having made a payment of income of an employee (“the notional payment”),
- (b) the employer is required by virtue of section 710(4) to account to an officer of Revenue and Customs[^c-4f3a0614079ff004d8042e82d44ecd6f] for an amount of income tax (“the due amount”) in respect of the notional payment, and
- (c) the employee does not, before the end of the period of 90 days[^c-1157d01c423cb7edf7d984bddb3e1c3a] after the end of the tax year in which the relevant date falls[^c-7ad02b770691ba2d4b89c9de7118a716], make good the due amount to the employer.

(2) The due amount is to be treated as earnings from the employment for the tax year in which the relevant date[^c-ef9599c6c6ee42464efe89ed914a8d6d] falls.

(3) In this section “**employer**”, in relation to any provision of sections 687, 687A,[^c-ad21cc1ce7225c09819c82f9962d68ac] 689, 693 to 700 or 710, means the person taken to be the employer for the purposes of that provision.

It also includes a person who is treated as making a payment of PAYE income by virtue of section 689(2) (payments by person for whom employee works but who is not the employer) or section 689A(3) (deemed payments of[^c-2650f487ad26e355418ddcedd5893bdc] PAYE income of continental shelf workers by person other than employer) .

(4)[^c-bcfe89a5e71ac3eba37e717f2af8fc72] In this section “**the relevant date**” means—

- (a) if the employer is treated by virtue of any Act as making the notional payment before the date on which the Act is passed, that date, and
- (b) in any other case, the date on which the employer is treated as making the notional payment.
