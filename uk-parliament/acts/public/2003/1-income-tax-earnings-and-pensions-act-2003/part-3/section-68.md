# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 68

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Meaning of “material interest” in a company

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of the benefits code a person has a material interest in a company if condition A or B is met.

(2) Condition A is that the person (with or without one or more associates) or any associate of that person (with or without one or more other such associates[^key-664adb60e2ffe6b588570609f6cb3ebf]) is—

- (a) the beneficial owner of, or
- (b) able to control, directly or through the medium of other companies or by any other indirect means,

more than 5% of the ordinary share capital of the company.

(3) Condition B is that, in the case of a close company, the person (with or without one or more associates) or any associate of that person (with or without one or more other such associates[^key-453f373a364ec4f182091ba233e1b37c]), possesses or is entitled to acquire, such rights as would—

- (a) in the event of the winding-up of the company, or
- (b) in any other circumstances,

give an entitlement to receive more than 5% of the assets which would then be available for distribution among the participators.

(4) In this section—

- “**associate**” has the meaning given by section 448 of CTA 2010[^key-9761c677c4e9219dfa6aba02a47d3031] except that, for this purpose, “**relative**” in section 448(1)[^key-351c5f03b300eb189d5f8fe4ba5ac847] has the meaning given by subsection (5) below, and
- “**participator**” has the meaning given by section 454 of CTA 2010[^key-97136d91f31ef98b5d17164c5fe93f0f].

(5) For the purposes of this section a person (“A”) is a relative of another (“B”) if A is—

- (a) B’s spouse or civil partner[^key-62659ee37187ac1c70182ca9cbb02a1f],
- (b) a parent, child or remoter relation in the direct line either of B or of B’s spouse or civil partner,
- (c) a brother or sister of B or of B’s spouse or civil partner, or
- (d) the spouse or civil partner of a person falling within paragraph (b) or (c).
