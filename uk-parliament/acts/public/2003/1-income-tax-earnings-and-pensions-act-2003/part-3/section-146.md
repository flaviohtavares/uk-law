# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 146

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1

## 146. Cars that run on road fuel gas

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies for the purposes of sections 121 and 121B[^c-d30db470b0391389671171231bc3e185] if the car—

- (a) has been manufactured so as to be capable of running on road fuel gas, and
- (b) is not a car to which section 137 (different CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure for bi-fuel cars) applies.

(2) The price of the car found under step 1 of section 121(1) or (as the case may be) step 1 of section 121B(1)[^c-c57f2bee4a8ab2068a35d318324f459f] is to be reduced by so much of that price as it is reasonable to attribute to the car being manufactured in such a way as to be capable of running on road fuel gas rather than in such a way as to be capable of running only on petrol.
