# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 137A

## 137A. Car with a CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure: bi-fuel cars registered on or after IP completion day[^c-d7164c2b280ddfad67f449b9b9e31618]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to a car first registered on or after IP completion day if it is so registered on the basis of a qualifying emissions certificate which specifies separate CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figures in terms of grams per kilometre driven for different fuels.

(2) The car’s CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure is—

- (a) the lowest figure specified, or
- (b) if there is more than one figure specified in relation to each fuel, the lowest CO<sub>2</sub> emissions (combined) figure specified.

(3) For the purpose of determining the car’s CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure ignore any values specified in the qualifying emissions certificate that are not WLTP (worldwide harmonised light vehicles test procedures) values.

(4) Subsection (2) is subject to section 138 (automatic car for a disabled employee).
