## 119. Where alternative to benefit of low emission car or van offered[^key-7bd3f7aa7edcb5c2672461116b19a3b2][^key-fc2dc951a367b0ee414dc1ffd7623642]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-995ab1eaad9a729da9e6d369359a3e3f] This section applies where in a tax year—

- (a) a car is made available as mentioned in section 114(1),
- (b) the car's CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure (see sections 133 to 138) does not exceed 75 grams per kilometre, and
- (c) an alternative to the benefit of the car is offered.

(2) The mere fact that the alternative is offered does not result in an amount in respect of the benefit constituting earnings by virtue of Chapter 1 of this Part (earnings).
