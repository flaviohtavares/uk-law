# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 121A

## 121A. Optional remuneration arrangements: method of calculating relevant amount[^key-8fc70087b019bc61502973036b26a21a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) To find the relevant amount for the purposes of section 120A, take the following steps—

- *Step 1[^key-d6ecaa2b6d4091c0daebc32c36222e2a]* Take the total foregone amount in connection with the car for the tax year (see section 120A(4)). .
- *Step 2* Make any deduction under section 132A in respect of capital contributions made by the employee to the cost of the car or accessories.

  The resulting amount is the provisional sum.
- *Step 3* Make any deduction from the provisional sum under section 144 in respect of payments by the employee for the private use of the car.

  The result is the “**relevant amount**” for the purposes of section 120A.

(2) Where it is necessary, for the purpose of determining the “[^key-28175e7ae678a744a33f61a445fd8bae]**total foregone amount**” for the purposes of step 1 of subsection (1), to apportion an amount of earnings to a benefit mentioned in section 120A(4)(a) or (b)[^key-4ab5e81ef130193a75419bf0fed00dad] for the tax year, the apportionment is to be made on a just and reasonable basis.

In this subsection “earnings” is to be interpreted in accordance with section 69B(5).
