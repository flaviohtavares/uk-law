# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 144

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 144. Deduction for payments for private use

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A deduction is to be made from the provisional sum (see subsection (1A))[^key-57401e016185ad574f1e3d0779e04204] if, as a condition of the car being available for the employee’s private use, the employee—

- (a) is required in the tax year in question to pay (whether by way of deduction from earnings or otherwise) an amount of money for that use, and
- (b)[^key-08c6c4aa5976a1aeb136d29e18d7483f] pays that amount on or before 6 July following[^key-21ab7c512074fc7da49157fd2de6be12] that year.

(1A)[^key-f86485fa66d3358316a1c105ff8d1747] In this section “**the provisional sum**” means the provisional sum calculated under—

- (a) step 7 of section 121(1) (method of calculating the cash equivalent of the benefit of a car), or
- (b) step 2 of section 121A(1) (optional remuneration arrangements: method of calculating relevant amount).

(2) If the amount paid as mentioned in subsection (1)(b)[^key-7203e7a3dbc23e30a99e27456d7d4243] by the employee in respect of that year is equal to or exceeds the provisional sum, the provisional sum is reduced so that—[^key-31db1c963cf757e7c72a848e57213756]

- (a) in a case within subsection (1A)(a), the cash equivalent of the benefit of the car for the year is nil, or
- (b) in a case within subsection (1A)(b), the relevant amount for the purposes of section 120A is nil.

(3) Where subsection (2) does not apply,[^key-76404703b6541b7cb718a8f6c579fe0b] the amount paid as mentioned in subsection (1)(b)[^key-d59077efee2cc4b05c53645303e6a86a] by the employee in respect of the year is deducted from the provisional sum in order to give—[^key-357e67662f5bb501825c436ed0d6c2a7]

- (a) in a case within subsection (1A)(a), the cash equivalent of the benefit of the car for the year, or
- (b) in a case within subsection (1A)(b), the relevant amount for the purposes of section 120A.

(4) In this section the reference to the car being available for the employee’s private use includes a reference to the car being available for the private use of a member of the employee’s family or household.

(5) This section is subject to section 145 (modification where car temporarily replaced).
