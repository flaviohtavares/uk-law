## 180. Threshold for benefit of loan to be treated as earnings

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 175 does not have effect in relation to an employee and a tax year—[^key-bc414b71317c3e7e26828b13a8bb94a8]

- (a) if the normal £10,000[^key-816217dd30f51bebf5fd00d543e1b790] threshold is not exceeded, or
- (b) where the loan is a non-qualifying loan and that threshold is exceeded, if the £10,000 threshold for non-qualifying loans is not exceeded.

(2) The normal £10,000[^key-c1685fa1114645e5f8d9f4a05780d777] threshold is not exceeded if at all times in the year the amount outstanding on the loan (or, if two or more employment-related loans which are taxable cheap loans are outstanding in the year, the aggregate of the amount outstanding on them) does not exceed £10,000.

(3) The £10,000 threshold for non-qualifying loans is not exceeded if at all times in the year the amount outstanding on the loan (or if two or more employment-related loans which are non-qualifying loans are outstanding in the year, the aggregate of the amounts outstanding on them) does not exceed £10,000.

(4) In this section a “**non-qualifying loan**” means a taxable cheap loan which is not a qualifying loan.

(5) For the purposes of this section a loan is a “**qualifying loan**” in relation to a particular tax year if, assuming interest is paid on the loan for that year (whether or not it is in fact paid), the whole or part of that interest—

- (a) is eligible for relief under section 353 of ICTA or section 383 of ITA 2007 (relief for interest payments)[^key-8e20ae457347aad7034a80cf3567bf11],
- (b) would be eligible for relief under section 353 of[^key-53554f9073114c070f18b7a8ea9eb48f] ICTA but for the fact that it is a payment of relevant loan interest to which section 369 of ICTA applies (mortgage interest payable under deduction of tax),
- (c) is deductible in computing the amount of the profits to be charged to tax[^key-cd19b53d51a31db6e57e311bbda9e02a] in respect of a trade, profession or vocation carried on wholly or partly in the United Kingdom[^key-10cf769025e7154c3b6ad79e17f0374a] by the person to whom the loan is made, or
- (d) is deductible in computing the amount of the profits to be charged to tax in respect of a[^key-2e01e28690f52acd1daf93ccef4b86a8] UK property business (within the meaning of Chapter 2 of Part 3 of[^key-f0f3963bd800020a4444d0ec6e3b2f68] ITTOIA 2005 or Chapter 2 of Part 4 of CTA 2009) carried on by that person.
