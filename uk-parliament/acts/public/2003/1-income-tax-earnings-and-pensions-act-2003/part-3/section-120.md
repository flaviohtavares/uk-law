# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 120

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120. Benefit of car treated as earnings

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If this Chapter applies to a car in relation to a particular tax year, the cash equivalent of the benefit of the car is to be treated as earnings from the employment for that year.

(2) In such a case (including a case where the cash equivalent of the benefit of the car is nil)[^key-f6a58ba55b751d49c1ba5535c42938a7] the employee is referred to in this Chapter as being chargeable to tax in respect of the car in that year.

(3)[^key-23905cd98e23ddac9937aa87371a14d9] Any reference in this Act to a case where the cash equivalent of the benefit of a car is treated as the employee's earnings for a year by virtue of this section includes a case where the cash equivalent is nil.

(4)[^key-6ce6f6fd4ac5f3a61929ce5a0f85614d] This section is subject to section 120A.
