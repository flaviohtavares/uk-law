# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 154

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 154. Benefit of van treated as earnings

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If this Chapter applies to a van in relation to a particular tax year, the cash equivalent of the benefit of the van is to be treated as earnings from the employment for that year.[^c-a0d635d2ca19e4e170e79bf155211791]

(2)[^c-7cf1969b26a6120e1544d39644f54b03] In such a case (including a case where the cash equivalent of the benefit of the van is nil) the employee is referred to in this Chapter as being chargeable to tax in respect of the van for that year.

(3) Any reference in this Act to a case where the cash equivalent of the benefit of a van is treated as the employee's earnings for a year by virtue of this section includes a case where the cash equivalent is nil.

(4)[^c-ed6fc72b54cddbd5d446ce60ce0b7014] This section is subject to section 154A.
