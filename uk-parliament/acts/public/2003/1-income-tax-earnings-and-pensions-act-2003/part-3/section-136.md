# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 136

## 136. Car with a CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure: registration from 1st October 1999 to IP completion day[^key-91a641bc13e0817f9dcc7822bebbcb3d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to a car first registered on or after 1st October 1999 but before IP completion day[^key-24c6afa26b448a02fe80f93fd5e883cd] if it is so registered on the basis of—

- (a) an EC certificate of conformity (see section 171(1)), or
- (b) a UK approval certificate (see section 171(1)),

which specifies a CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure in terms of grams per kilometre driven.

(2) The car’s CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure is that specified figure unless more than one figure is specified, in which case the car’s CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure is the figure specified as the CO<sub>2</sub> emissions (combined) figure.

(2A)[^key-cd04b140ad01c94d71bedf6f1a7c13c4] For the purpose of determining the car's CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure in a case where the car is first registered before 6 April 2020[^key-b0d91c1475cc1309c5c77d5eb09ea8c1], ignore any WLTP (worldwide harmonised light[^key-537a47cf730810155c58b1c74f74a0a1] vehicles test procedures) values specified in the EC certificate of conformity or UK approval certificate[^key-b87920bc4158d3f90fba16c278a2d04d].

(2B)[^key-a53ebad1be54971758418ee8ea58ac70] For the purpose of determining the car's CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure in a case where the car is first registered on or after 6 April 2020, ignore any values specified in the EC certificate of conformity or UK approval certificate that are not WLTP (worldwide harmonised light vehicle test procedures) values.

(3) Subsection (2)[^key-2ef6773ef104650344675bed831cb87e] is subject to—

- (a) section 137 (bi-fuel cars : registration from 1st January 2000 to IP completion day[^key-73e0e6352d2ca41cd213831d84d6d6e5]), and
- (b) section 138 (automatic car for a disabled employee).
