## 215. Limitation of exemption for scholarship income in section 776(1) of ITTOIA 2005[^key-8ecaf8150656ba7621bf023793feddcc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If an employment-related benefit consists in the provision of a scholarship, section 776(1) of[^key-84fa8dfc27d4aebe380860efead401df] ITTOIA 2005 (exemption for scholarship income) applies only in relation to the holder of the scholarship.
