# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 136A

## 136A. Car with a CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure: registration on or after IP completion day[^key-9ba42e121c94b5ce40115b687983260a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to a car first registered on or after IP completion day if it is so registered on the basis of a qualifying emissions certificate.

(2) The car’s CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure is the figure specified in the qualifying emissions certificate unless more than one figure is specified, in which case the car’s CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure is the figure specified as the CO<sub>2</sub> emissions (combined) figure.

(3) For the purpose of determining the car’s CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure ignore any values specified in the qualifying emissions certificate that are not WLTP (worldwide harmonised light vehicles test procedures) values.

(4) Subsection (2) is subject to—

- (a) section 137A (bi-fuel cars registered after IP completion day), and
- (b) section 138 (automatic car for a disabled employee).

(5)[^key-3b3c19d735a4e104ef324877049a8515] Subsection (2) is also subject to section 138A (certain cars with a CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure and an electric range figure).
