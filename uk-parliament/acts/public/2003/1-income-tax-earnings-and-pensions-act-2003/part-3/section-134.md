# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 134

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1

## 134. Meaning of car with or without a CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Chapter a “**car with a CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure**” means—

- (a) a car first registered on or after 1st January 1998 but before 1st October 1999 to which section 135 applies,
- (b) a car first registered on or after 1st October 1999 but before IP completion day[^c-41a4fe6f7d18546ed7107374da872275] to which section 136 (registration from 1st October 1999 to IP completion day)[^c-ce48c21e1af32d5482fd69fd48375198] applies, ...
- (ba)[^c-95731881bb7ade42033b92f77c3a5a46] a car first registered on or after IP completion day to which section 136A (registration on or after IP completion day) applies,
- (c) a car first registered on or after 1st January 2000 but before IP completion day[^c-43d69797bf608548932aeb185bbdaac7] which is a car to which section 137 (bi-fuel cars : registration from 1st January 2000 to IP completion day[^c-d0561621cd704ffa3464c6f0b0f5f195]) applies , or[^c-6651850468c4a48c1297e561bb14db23]
- (d) a car first registered on or after IP completion day to which section 137A (bi-fuel cars: registration on or after IP completion day) applies.

(2) In this Chapter a “**car without a CO<sub>2</sub> emissions figure**” means any other car first registered on or after 1st January 1998.
