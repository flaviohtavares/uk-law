## 122. The price of the car

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-8ea93f259961cb73c7a1990870d37b53] For the purposes of this Chapter the price of a car means—

This is subject to section 124A (automatic car for a disabled employee).

- (a) its list price, if it has one, or
- (b) its notional price, if it has no list price.

(2)[^key-ce11515fe3f93538c161ac87ca67b5aa] This is subject to section 124A (automatic car for a disabled employee).
