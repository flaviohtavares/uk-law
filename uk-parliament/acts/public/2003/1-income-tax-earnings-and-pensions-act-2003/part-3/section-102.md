## 102. Benefit of living accommodation treated as earnings

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if living accommodation to which this Chapter applies is provided in any period (“the taxable period”) —[^c-897470a1c4e67cf3b0eb471d87174905]

- (a) which consists of the whole or part of a tax year, and
- (b) throughout which the employee holds the employment,

(1A)[^c-43ab43db6b305dfb1168eafa90694a41] the cash equivalent of the benefit of the accommodation is to be treated as earnings from the employment for that year.

(1B)[^c-4bfd754ee986636ffd1428237cfbb384] If the benefit of the accommodation is provided pursuant to optional remuneration arrangements—

- (a) subsection (1A) does not apply, and
- (b) the relevant amount is to be treated as earnings from the employment for that tax year.
