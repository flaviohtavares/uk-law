- (a) Part 1 of Schedule 7D to TCGA 1992 (which provides for relief from capital gains tax for the trustees and for participants in relation to a Schedule 2[^c-72a77c75e9a221d39e533077e22ea4f1] SIP in certain circumstances, including where shares cease to be subject to the plan), ...
- (b) section 95 of FA 2001 (which contains relief from stamp duty and stamp duty reserve tax for transfers of partnership or dividend shares) ,[^c-fa9e73529226a806973e484f0dc437e7] ...
- (c) sections 392 to 395 and 405 to 408 of ITTOIA 2005 (SIPs: special rules for charges under Chapters 3 and 4 of Part 4 of that Act (dividends etc. from UK or non-UK resident companies etc.)) and section 770 of that Act (exemption for amounts applied by SIP trustees acquiring dividend shares or retained for reinvestment),[^c-b80b2903a9e8cf17c39fa6022efda7d4] ...
- (d) Chapter 5 of Part 9 of ITA 2007 (which provides for section 479 of that Act not to apply to income of the trustees of a Schedule 2[^c-8e4eb124a96573b5d785980c6f94f0cf] SIP in certain circumstances), and[^c-1fc96844b0d224efc56f59983498e20e]
