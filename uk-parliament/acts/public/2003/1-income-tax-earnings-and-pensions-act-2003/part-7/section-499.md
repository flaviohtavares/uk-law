# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 499

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 499. No charge in respect of incidental expenditure

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An employee is not liable to income tax in respect of incidental expenditure of—

- (a) the trustees,
- (b) the company which established the plan, or
- (c) (if different) the employer,

in operating the plan.
