# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 435

## 435. Application of this Chapter[^key-74ea5d0d19e54792ecd1cf8a6a2ae033]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter applies to employment-related securities if they are—

- (a) convertible securities, or
- (b) an interest in convertible securities,

at the time of the acquisition.
