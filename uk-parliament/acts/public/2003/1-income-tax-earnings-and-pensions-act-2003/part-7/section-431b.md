# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 431B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 431B. Securities acquired for purpose of avoidance[^key-7c3e52e40e7075e355d4186171230687][^key-070a79afdd5048639efb19720520cf9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where employment-related securities are restricted securities or a restricted interest in securities, the employer and the employee are to be treated as making an election under section 431(1) in relation to the employment-related securities if

- (a)[^key-952fe2436b633d8964965ee56b9849fa] the main purpose (or one of the main purposes) of the arrangements under which the right or opportunity to acquire the employment-related securities is made available is the avoidance of tax or national insurance contributions , and[^key-9b2cf0e5c9f47d69896905eef138eb1b]
- (b)[^key-30c0328633ac4a8ac739a3394bd437d9] at the time of the acquisition, the earnings from the employment are (or would be if there were any) general earnings to which any of the charging provisions of Chapters 4 and 5 of Part 2 applies.
