# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 446V

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1

## 446V. Chapter to be additional to other income tax charges[^c-50a0c1b5a35931f04337ef6a4d73c721]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter does not affect any liability to income tax arising in respect of the acquisition under—

- (a) Chapter 1 of Part 3 (earnings),
- (b) Chapter 10 of Part 3 (taxable benefits: residual liability to charge),
- (ba)[^c-ca31160499c076d7ba7f5cf248e6de8e] section 226A (employee shareholder shares: amount treated as earnings),
- (c) Chapter 3 of this Part (acquisition by conversion),
- (d) Chapter 3A of this Part (securities with artificially depressed market value), ...
- (e) Chapter 5 of this Part (acquisition of securities pursuant to securities option) , or[^c-7a922e30df6e55fd720eda32debbe9de]
- (f) Chapter 2 of Part 7A (employment income provided through third parties)
