# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 511

## 511. PAYE deductions to be made by trustees on shares ceasing to be subject to plan

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if, as a result of any shares ceasing to be subject to a Schedule 2[^key-1c7ce2e508ee8d97a5e6afdaab0184c7] SIP—

- (a) there is an amount that counts as employment income of a participant by virtue of the SIP code, and
- (b) condition A or B is met.

(2) Condition A is that an officer of Revenue and Customs[^key-4f3a0614079ff004d8042e82d44ecd6f] —

- (a) is[^key-f6659aee431ba57feb2eb7ea1b9e14e1] of the opinion that it is impracticable for the employer company (within the meaning of section 510) to make a PAYE deduction, and
- (b) accordingly directs[^key-21a9dc1a46e9d2e4aed58dfd0e803553] that this section is to apply.

(3) Condition B is that there is no company that qualifies as the employer company (within the meaning of that section).

(4) If this section applies—

- (a) section 510(2) does not apply, and
- (b) the trustees must make a PAYE deduction in respect of the taxable equivalent as if the participant were a former employee of the trustees.

(5) The “**taxable equivalent**” means an amount equal to that mentioned in subsection (1).

(6) If this section applies, section 689 (employee of non-UK employer) does not apply.
