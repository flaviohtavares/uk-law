# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 422

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1

## 422. Application of this Chapter[^c-070a79afdd5048639efb19720520cf9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter applies to employment-related securities if they are—

- (a) restricted securities, or
- (b) a restricted interest in securities,

at the time of the acquisition.
