# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 431A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 431A. Shares under tax advantaged plan or scheme[^key-c3593f22e3d3c7346fa9f59b120e340a][^key-070a79afdd5048639efb19720520cf9b][^key-c796af208eac5564c6ce2bee86c7850f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where employment-related securities are restricted securities or a restricted interest in securities, the employer and the employee are to be treated as making an election under section 431(1) in relation to the employment-related securities if they are shares, or an interest in shares, to which this subsection applies.

(2) Subsection (1) applies to—

- (a) shares awarded or acquired under a Schedule 2[^key-f7da57b39a60ccb4cc9eba8bfb1f0c7d] share incentive plan (within the meaning of Chapter 6 of this Part) in circumstances in which (in accordance with section 490) no liability to income tax arises,
- (b) shares acquired by the exercise of a share option granted under a Schedule 3[^key-bde5af6d496f4cb6ab616565467ec08e] SAYE option scheme (within the meaning of Chapter 7 of this Part) in circumstances in which (in accordance with section 519) no liability to income tax arises,
- (c) shares acquired by the exercise of a share option granted under a Schedule 4[^key-5b594d58bab65aa1b16f8f2356b3ac2f] CSOP scheme (within the meaning of Chapter 8 of this Part) in circumstances in which (in accordance with section 524) no liability to income tax arises, and
- (d) shares acquired by the exercise of a qualifying option within the meaning of section 527(4) (enterprise management incentives) in circumstances in which (in accordance with section 530) no liability to income tax arises.
