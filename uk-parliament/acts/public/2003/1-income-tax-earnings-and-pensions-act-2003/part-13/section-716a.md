# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 716A

## 716A. Priority rule for dividends etc. of UK resident companies etc.[^c-0c2d1fdc329b17b33c228d7474a333dc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-71995d669f36d5f9732d97b9cdeb7c8a] Any income, so far as it falls within—

- (a) Part 2, 9 or 10 of this Act, and
- (b) Chapter 3 of Part 4 of ITTOIA 2005 (dividends etc. from UK resident companies etc.),

is dealt with under Chapter 3 of Part 4 of ITTOIA 2005.

(2)[^c-6faf545035d5121346edc5a2d8386405] Subsection (1) is subject to section 554Z2(2).
