# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 681I

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 681I. Winter fuel payment charge[^c-3b56b801aa4491f07be8914c523ba8b1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person (“**P**”) is liable to a charge to income tax for a tax year if—

- (a) P is entitled to a winter fuel payment in respect of the qualifying week, and
- (b) P’s total income for the tax year exceeds £35,000.

(2) The charge is to be known as the “winter fuel payment charge”.

(3) The amount of the charge is equal to the amount of the winter fuel payment.

(4) But P is not liable for the charge if P is entitled to a relevant benefit on any day in the qualifying week.

(5) The following are “relevant benefits”—

- (a) income support under section 124 of SSCBA 1992 or section 123 of SSCB(NI)A 1992;
- (b) an income-based jobseeker’s allowance under section 1 of JSA 1995 or Article 3 of JS(NI)O 1995;
- (c) state pension credit under section 1 of SPCA 2002 or section 1 of SPCA(NI) 2002;
- (d) an income-related employment and support allowance under section 1(2)(b) of WRA 2007 or [Part 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2007/2/part/1) of the [Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2007/2/contents);
- (e) universal credit under Part 1 of WRA 2012 or [Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2015/2006/part/2) of the [Welfare Reform (Northern Ireland) Order 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/2015/2006/contents).

(6) In this section—

- (a) a “**winter fuel payment**” means a payment under the [Social Fund Winter Fuel Payment Regulations 2025](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/969/contents), the [Winter Heating Assistance (Pension Age) (Scotland) Regulations 2024](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2024/351/contents) or the [Social Fund Winter Fuel Payment Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2025](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2025/142/contents);
- (b) the “**qualifying week**”, in relation to a tax year, means the week beginning on the third Monday in the September of that tax year.
