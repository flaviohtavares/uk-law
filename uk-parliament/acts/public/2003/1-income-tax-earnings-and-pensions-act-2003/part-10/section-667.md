# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 667

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 667. Amounts in excess of taxable maximum

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the amount of income support and relevant welfare supplementary payments[^key-1f84f9d54ae10539d68ef943c8ca4f2a] paid to a person (“**the claimant**”) for a week or a part of a week exceeds the claimant’s taxable maximum for that period, no liability to income tax arises on the excess.

(2)[^key-24105859162a035c57900b4c2e84d116] The claimant’s taxable maximum for a period is determined–

- (a) under section 668(1), (2) and (3) where the claimant is a member of a couple, and
- (b) under section 668(2A) and (3) where the claimant is not a member of a couple.
