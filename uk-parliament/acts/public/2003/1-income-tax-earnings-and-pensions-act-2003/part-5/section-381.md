# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 381

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 381. Taking account of other deductions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of sections 379 and 380, the amount of the earnings from an employment for a tax year is the amount remaining after any deductions under—

- (a) section 232 (giving effect to mileage allowance relief),
- (b) Chapter 2, 3, 4 or 5 of this Part,
- (c)[^key-43fc532689ba770a89048cd881af7a06] section 262 of CAA 2001 (capital allowances to be given effect by treating them as deductions from earnings), and
- (d) sections 188 to 194 of FA 2004 (contributions to registered pension schemes).
