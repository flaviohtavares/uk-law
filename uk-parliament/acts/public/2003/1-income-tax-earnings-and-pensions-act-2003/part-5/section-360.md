# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 360

## 360. Disallowance of certain accommodation expenses of MPs and other representatives

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No deduction from earnings is allowed under this Chapter or section 373 (non-resident or qualifying new resident[^c-4af1044e8168f2cdfaca7ce1f0f28acf] employee’s travel costs and expenses where duties performed in UK) for accommodation expenses incurred by a member of—

- (a) the House of Commons,
- (b) the Scottish Parliament,
- (c) the National Assembly for Wales, or
- (d) the Northern Ireland Assembly.

(2) In this section “**accommodation expenses**” means expenses incurred in, or in connection with, the provision or use of residential or overnight accommodation to enable the member to perform duties as a member of the Parliament or Assembly in or about—

- (a) the place where it sits, or
- (b) the constituency or region which the member represents.

(3)[^c-430e3c00ade51c9c9b761345be9cfd25] In relation to a member of the House of Commons, subsection (3) of section 292 applies for the purposes of this section as it applies for the purposes of that section.
