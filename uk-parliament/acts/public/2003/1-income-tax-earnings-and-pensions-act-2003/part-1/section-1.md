# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Overview of contents of this Act

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act imposes charges to income tax on—

- (a) employment income (see Parts 2 to 7A[^c-1a4e9823c1b58cbb423eca79f21e4a77] ),
- (b) pension income (see Part 9), and
- (c) social security income (see Chapters 1 to 7 of[^c-ba715fcd2f5f89038dd0082ef2fec974] Part 10).

(2) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) This Act also—

- (a) confers certain reliefs in respect of liabilities of former employees (see Part 8),
- (aa)[^c-1e1daa2424c3796d46f888f1d21e5bd4] makes provision for the high income child benefit charge (see Chapter 8 of Part 10),
- (ab)[^c-4988411133c8484f226646739e88c069] makes provision for the winter fuel payment charge (see Chapter 9 of Part 10),
- (b) provides for the assessment, collection and recovery of income tax in respect of employment, pension or social security income that is PAYE income (see Part 11), ...
- (ba)[^c-1f3d8841eb13d16aa289a7b19d84b305] allows deductions to be made from such income in respect of certain debts payable to the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs (see Part 11), and
- (c) allows deductions to be made from such income in respect of payroll giving (see Part 12).
