## 10. Meaning of “taxable earnings” and “taxable specific income”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section explains what is meant by “taxable earnings” and “taxable specific income” in the employment income Parts.

(2) “Taxable earnings from an employment in a tax year are to be determined in accordance with Chapters 4 and 5 of this Part[^c-7543c33d2a8e486d316261fc176eec48].

(3) “Taxable specific income from an employment for a tax year means the full amount of any specific employment income which, by virtue of Part 6 , 7 or 7A[^c-ee0e20de8a7284caff9ee0ec460c9266] or any other enactment, counts as employment income for that year in respect of the employment.

(4)[^c-24cf1e0d099fa6894b182951bc63162a] Subsection (3) is subject to Chapter 5B (taxable specific income from employment-related securities[^c-3bcdcc32ef4c7cfe489b59396be5f867] etc: internationally mobile employees).

(5)[^c-8878b92913e35c0dafc289c3512153ca] Subsection (3) is also subject to sections 554Z9 to 554Z11 (employment income under Part 7A: remittance basis).
