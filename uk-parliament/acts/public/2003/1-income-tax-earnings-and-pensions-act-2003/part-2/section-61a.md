# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 61A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61A. Scope of this Chapter[^key-87da422853c02c2b89725efce6f34cf9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter has effect with respect to the provision of services by a managed service company.

(2) Nothing in this Chapter—

- (a) affects the operation of Chapter 7 of this Part (agency workers), or
- (b) applies to payments or transfers to which section 966(3) or (4) of ITA 2007 applies (visiting performers: duty to deduct and account for sums representing income tax).

(3)[^key-31a289e4e42569876f1039d1d49f425b] See also section 61D(4A) (disapplication of this Chapter if Chapter 10 applies).
