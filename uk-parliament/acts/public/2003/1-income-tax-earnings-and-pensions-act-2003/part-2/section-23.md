# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 23

## 23. Calculation of “chargeable overseas earnings”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies for calculating how much of an employee’s general earnings for a tax year are “**chargeable overseas earnings**” for the purposes of section[^key-9e474aa8c23139576f57bffd43790769] 22.

(1A)[^key-037ace5a27abca85761b8ec546e50d23] But none of an employee's general earnings from an employment for a tax year are to be “chargeable overseas earnings” if section 24A applies in relation to the employment for the tax year.

(2) General earnings for a tax year are “overseas earnings” for that year if—

- (a)[^key-94ffcaa90fad7e9771f525c5e1ea6cf7] section 809B, 809D or 809E of ITA 2007 (remittance basis) applied[^key-0cdb3bd2d0b1a8db3edda0b0adc16017] to the employee for that year,
- (aa)[^key-182bb32ec8bd1a60141514ce3a3df5b0] the employee did not[^key-54dd919ffb254d846f71188c3fdabde8] meet the requirement of section 26A for that year,
- (b) the employment was[^key-56679d51dbe1904f783b55acb560a190] with a foreign employer, and
- (c) the duties of the employment were[^key-5d1d10b922144b88da5b4a3ee6c60d40] performed wholly outside the United Kingdom.

(3) To calculate the amount of “chargeable overseas earnings” for a tax year—

*Step 1[^key-de069085545d9ec2d01056761e00bf7b]*

Identify—

- (a) in the case of a tax year that is not a split year, the full amount of the overseas earnings for that year, and
- (b) in the case of a split year, so much of the full amount of the overseas earnings for that year as is attributable to the UK part of the year.

*Step 2*

Subtract any amounts that would (assuming they were taxable earnings) be allowed to be deducted from the earnings identified under step 1[^key-f897af0a9f7f6f6be8e283481d72a8d1] under—

- (a) section 232 or Part 5 (deductions allowed from earnings),
- (b)[^key-b075ce95d86996b53d9fa85d6da318ac] sections 188 to 194 of FA 2004 (contributions to registered pension schemes), or
- (d) section 262 of CAA 2001 (capital allowances to be given effect by treating them as deductions from earnings).

  *Step 3*

  Apply any limit imposed by section 24 (limit where duties of associated employment performed in UK).

  The result is the chargeable overseas earnings for the tax year.

(4)[^key-465e2354f750e19d520d1747535d496a] Any attribution required for the purposes of step 1 or step 2 in subsection (3) is to be done on a just and reasonable basis.
