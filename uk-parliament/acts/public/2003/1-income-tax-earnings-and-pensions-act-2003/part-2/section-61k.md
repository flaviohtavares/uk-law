# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 61K

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61K. Scope of this Chapter[^key-c5ed4a8550671f87f70e726d21948b20]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter has effect with respect to the provision of services through an intermediary in a case where the services are provided to a person who—[^key-c80d11c5b2150164b8fd6780cf315882]

- (a) is a public authority, or
- (b) qualifies as medium or large and has a UK connection for a tax year

(2) Nothing in this Chapter—

- (a) affects the operation of Chapter 7 of this Part (agency workers), or
- (b) applies to payments or transfers to which section 966(3) or (4) of ITA 2007 applies (visiting performers: duty to deduct and account for sums representing income tax).

(3)[^key-3d4c29a53966ebd695faa0fa3bbf36ba] For the purposes of this Chapter a person qualifies as medium or large for a tax year if the person does not qualify as small for the tax year for the purposes of Chapter 8 of this Part (see sections 60A to 60G).

(4) Section 60I (when a person has a UK connection for a tax year) applies for the purposes of this Chapter.
