# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Scope of this Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter has effect with respect to the provision of services through an intermediary in a case where the services are provided to a person who is not a public authority and who either—[^key-d5a5d3fa9a86dcc9684d4f70e1500004]

- (a) qualifies as small for a tax year, or
- (b) does not have a UK connection for a tax year.

(2) Nothing in this Chapter—

- (a) affects the operation of Chapter 7 of this Part,
- (aa)[^key-aaddb3914b96b3a05e00203179fe5533] applies to services provided by a managed service company (within the meaning of Chapter 9 of this Part), or
- (b) applies to payments or transfers to which section 966(3) or (4) of ITA 2007 applies (visiting performers: duty to deduct and account for sums representing income tax)[^key-dd8546a085cb62c89d12eb24b2757016] .

(3)[^key-7d3936c217f3fe370fd581cfc06f48b8] In this Chapter “**public authority**” has the same meaning as in Chapter 10 of this Part (see section 61L).

(4)[^key-f61e08b92be41ba2973839dd161628fb] For provisions determining when a person qualifies as small for a tax year, see sections 60A to 60G.

(5) For provision determining when a person has a UK connection for a tax year, see section 60I.
