# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 52

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Conditions of liability where intermediary is a partnership

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the intermediary is a partnership the conditions are as follows.

(2) In relation to any payment or benefit received or receivable by the worker as a member of the partnership the conditions are—

- (a) that the worker, alone or with one or more relatives, is entitled to 60% or more of the profits of the partnership; or
- (b) that most of the profits of the partnership concerned derive from the provision of services under engagements to which one or other of this Chapter and Chapter 10[^c-fcb540fa370b644eb707dc40bb3fdf93] applies—
  - (i) to a single client, or
  - (ii) to a single client together with associates of that client; or
- (c) that under the profit sharing arrangements the income of any of the partners is based on the amount of income generated by that partner by the provision of services under engagements to which one or other of this Chapter and Chapter 10[^c-2c3e653f3153caa5499cf9b20a30d0f5] applies.

  In paragraph (a) “**relative**” means spouse or civil partner[^c-5e51cc93d7b5895410d66f79e6013581] , parent or child or remoter relation in the direct line, or brother or sister.

(3) In relation to any payment or benefit received or receivable by the worker otherwise than as a member of the partnership, the conditions are that the payment or benefit—

- (a) is received or receivable by the worker directly from the intermediary, and
- (b) can reasonably be taken to represent remuneration for services provided by the worker to the client.
