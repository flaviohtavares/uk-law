## 14. Taxable earnings under this Chapter: introduction

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter sets out for the purposes of this Part what are taxable earnings from an employment in a tax year in cases where section 15 (earnings for year when employee UK[^key-cdad05803c2a80919eafea070fdbd8d2] resident) applies to general earnings for a tax year.

(2) In this Chapter—

- (a) sections 16 and 17 deal with the year for which general earnings are earned, and
- (b) sections 18 and 19 deal with the time when general earnings are received.

(3) In the employment income Parts any reference to the charging provisions of this Chapter is a reference to section 15.
