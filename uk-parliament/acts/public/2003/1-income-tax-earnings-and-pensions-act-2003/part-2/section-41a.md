## 41A. Taxable specific income from employment-related securities: effect of remittance basis (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2][^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

- (a) an amount within subsection (2) counts as employment income of an individual for a tax year in respect of an employment (“the securities income”), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
- (b) any part of the relevant period (see section 41B) is within a tax year for which section 809B, 809D or 809E of ITA 2007 (remittance basis) applies to the individual. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(2) An amount is within this subsection if it counts as employment income under any provision of any of Chapters 2, 3 and 3C to 5 of Part 7 (employment-related securities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2] etc *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]) except section 446UA. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(3) The reference in subsection (2) to an amount that counts as employment income under any of the provisions mentioned there does not include an amount which counts as employment income by virtue of any provision of Chapter 3A or 3B of Part 7. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(4) An amount equal to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

is an amount of “taxable specific income” from the employment for the tax year mentioned in subsection (1)(a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(5) In subsection (4)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

- (a) SI *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2] is the amount of the securities income, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
- (b) FSI is the amount of the securities income that is “foreign” (see sections 41C to 41E). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(6) The full amount of any of the foreign securities income which is remitted to the United Kingdom in a tax year is an amount of “taxable specific income” from the employment for that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(7) Subsection (6) applies whether or not the employment is held when the foreign securities income is remitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(8) For the purposes of Chapter A1 of Part 14 of ITA 2007 (remittance basis), treat the relevant securities or securities option as deriving from the foreign securities income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(9) But where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

- (a) the chargeable event is the disposal of the relevant securities or the assignment or release of the relevant securities option, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
- (b) the individual receives consideration for the disposal, assignment or release of an amount equal to or exceeding the market value of the relevant securities or securities option, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

for the purposes of that Chapter treat the consideration (and not the relevant securities or securities option) as deriving from the foreign securities income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(10) In this section and section 41B— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

- “**the chargeable event *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]**” means the event giving rise to the securities income, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
- “**the relevant securities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]**” or “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]**the relevant securities option *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]**” means the employment-related securities or employment-related securities option by virtue of which the amount mentioned in subsection (1)(a) counts as employment income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(11) See Chapter A1 of Part 14 of ITA 2007 for the meaning of “remitted to the United Kingdom” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2] etc. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
