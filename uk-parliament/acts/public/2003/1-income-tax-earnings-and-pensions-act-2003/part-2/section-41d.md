## 41D. Limit on foreign securities income where duties of associated employment performed in (affected by repeal, see note) UK (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2][^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2][^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section imposes a limit on the extent to which section 41C(3) applies in relation to a period when— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

- (a) the individual holds associated employments as well as the employment in relation to which section 41C(4) applies, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
- (b) the duties of the associated employments are not performed wholly outside the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(2) The amount of the securities income for the period that is to be regarded as “foreign” is limited to such amount as is just and reasonable, having regard to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

- (a) the employment income for the period from all the employments mentioned in subsection (1)(a), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
- (b) the proportion of that income that is general earnings to which section 22 applies (chargeable overseas earnings), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
- (c) the nature of and time devoted to the duties performed outside the United Kingdom, and those performed in the United Kingdom, in the period, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
- (d) all other relevant circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(3) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]**associated employments *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]**” means employments with the same employer or with associated employers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(4) Section 24(5) and (6) (meaning of “associated employer”) apply for the purposes of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
