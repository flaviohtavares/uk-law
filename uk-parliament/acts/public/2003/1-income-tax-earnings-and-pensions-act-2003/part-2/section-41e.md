# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 41E

## 41E. Foreign securities income: just and reasonable apportionment (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2][^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if the proportion of the securities income that would otherwise be regarded as “foreign” is not, having regard to all the circumstances, one that is just and reasonable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(2) The amount of the securities income that is “foreign” is such amount as is just and reasonable (rather than the amount calculated in accordance with section 41C). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
