# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 61U

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61U. Information to be provided by worker or intermediary and consequences of failure[^key-c5ed4a8550671f87f70e726d21948b20][^key-afe8eb4e1e9b2455d0c57dcf1e1a0f94]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the case of an engagement to which this Chapter applies, the relevant person[^key-258a0547af256586d67c4e390a50e497] must inform the potential deemed employer of which one of the following is applicable—

- (a) that one of conditions A to C in section 61N is met in the case;
- (b) that none of conditions A to C in section 61N is met in the case.

(2) If the relevant person[^key-b7277ad0d5b49860b9bc8692f74b3f45] has not complied with subsection (1) then, for the purposes of section 61N(1), one of conditions A to C in section 61N is to be treated as met.

(3) In this section —[^key-939ff26838fcd8b0f268fb8541cc2dc7]

- “**relevant person**” means the worker or, in a case where the worker has not complied with subsection (1), the intermediary;
- “the potential deemed employer” is the person who, if one of conditions A to C in section 61N were met, would be treated as making a deemed direct payment to the worker under section 61N(3).
