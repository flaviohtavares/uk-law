# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 41B

## 41B. Section 41A: the relevant period (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2][^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) “The relevant period” is to be determined as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(2) In the case of an amount that counts as employment income by virtue of Chapter 2 (restricted securities) or Chapter 3 (convertible securities), the relevant period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

- (a) begins with the day of the acquisition, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
- (b) ends with the day of the chargeable event. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(3) In the case of an amount that counts as employment income by virtue of section 446U (securities acquired for less than market value: discharge of notional loan)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

- (a) if the relevant securities were acquired by virtue of the exercise of a securities option (“the option”), the relevant period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
  - (i) begins with the day of the acquisition of the option, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2] ends with the day the option vests, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
- (b) otherwise, the relevant period is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
  - (i) the tax year in which the notional loan (within the meaning of Chapter 3C) is treated as made, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2] if the chargeable event occurs in that year, the period beginning at the beginning of that year and ending with the day of that event. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(4) In the case of an amount that counts as employment income by virtue of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

- (a) Chapter 3D (securities disposed of for more than market value), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
- (b) Chapter 4 (post-acquisition benefits from securities), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

the relevant period is the tax year in which the chargeable event occurs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(5) In the case of an amount that counts as employment income by virtue of Chapter 5 (employment-related securities options), the relevant period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

- (a) begins with the day of the acquisition, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
- (b) ends with the day of the chargeable event or, if earlier, the day the relevant securities option vests. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(6) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]**the acquisition *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]**” has the same meaning as in Chapters 2 to 4 or Chapter 5 (see section 421B or 471). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(7) For the purposes of this section an option “vests” when it is first capable of being exercised. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]

(8) References in this section to a Chapter are to a Chapter of Part 7. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_ce1f8881-03d5-4e0d-9ad5-d2b31d7824e2]
