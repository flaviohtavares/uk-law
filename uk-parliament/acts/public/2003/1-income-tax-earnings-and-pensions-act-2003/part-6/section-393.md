# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 393

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1

## 393. Application of this Chapter[^c-451098f3cc102fdb92e5b1be1c47a168]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter applies to relevant benefits provided under an employer-financed retirement benefits scheme.

(2) Section 393A defines “employer-financed retirement benefits scheme” and section 393B defines “relevant benefits”.
