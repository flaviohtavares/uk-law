## 408. Exception for contributions to registered pension schemes[^c-62783cdeb5d9a6c5467ccee8edf0b4cd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter does not apply to a contribution to a registered pension scheme[^c-38a0b513f4df0bb9bdc8ba081155be12] or an employer-financed retirement benefit scheme[^c-a272d681b1b889a7a452b206ff2ba418] if the contribution is made—

- (a) as part of an arrangement relating to the termination of a person’s employment, and
- (b) in order to provide benefits for the person in accordance with the terms of the scheme or approved personal pension arrangements.
