# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 579D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 579D. Interpretation[^key-924eb0dbf22499b73e3c2b7a7ab1edaf][^key-cc94000b3530d1b96b38cfce476e5e9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- “**income withdrawal**” has the meaning given by paragraph 7 of that Schedule;
- “nominees' income withdrawal" has the meaning given by paragraph 27D of that Schedule;[^key-8c0474094896ad1c82e574f5673db028]
- “successors' income withdrawal" has the meaning given by paragraph 27J of Schedule 28 to FA 2004.
- “**pension under a registered pension scheme**” includes—
  - (a) an annuity under, or purchased with sums or assets held for the purposes of, or representing acquired rights under, a registered pension scheme, and
  - (b) income withdrawal or dependants' income withdrawal , or nominees' income withdrawal or successors' income withdrawal,[^key-adbdab0c9831606adae823096fc66499] under a registered pension scheme.
