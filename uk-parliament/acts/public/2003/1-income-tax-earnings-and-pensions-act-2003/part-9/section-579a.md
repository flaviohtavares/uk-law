# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 579A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 579A. Pensions[^c-cc94000b3530d1b96b38cfce476e5e9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to any pension under a registered pension scheme (but subject to subsection (2) and section 579CZA[^c-b9af4fddccefd93e5da7263c067bc1a6]).

(2) This section does not apply to a pension under a registered pension scheme if and to the extent that, when it is paid, a liability to the unauthorised payments charge arises in respect of the amount of the payment (see section 208 of FA 2004).

(3)[^c-14b049d7290643d208a45fc08990e8c6] Chapter 17 of this Part provides exemptions for certain annuities (see sections 646B and 646C: certain beneficiaries' annuities purchased out of unused or drawdown funds).
