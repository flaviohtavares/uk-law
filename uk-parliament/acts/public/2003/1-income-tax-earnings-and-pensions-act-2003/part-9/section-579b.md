## 579B. Taxable pension income[^key-cc94000b3530d1b96b38cfce476e5e9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If section 579A applies, the taxable pension income for a tax year is the full amount of the pension under the registered pension scheme that accrues in that year irrespective of when any amount is actually paid This is subject to section 579CA.[^key-3e4a9fe937d7b87ca8db8d701416a782]
