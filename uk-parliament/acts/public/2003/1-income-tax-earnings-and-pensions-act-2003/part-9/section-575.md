# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 575

## 575. Taxable pension income

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If section 573 applies, the taxable pension income for a tax year is the full amount of the pension income arising in the tax year, but subject to subsections[^key-de00292ac9c369f6d91f52120a00105a] (1A)[^key-58158431c43224d896f70fab10a7edb5] ... and (3) and section 576A[^key-f99da7517ccc490cfea5aac5e5162246].

(1A)[^key-50dc99bbab30865d5fce0d9ede3a7c11] If the person liable for the tax under this Part is an individual and the tax year is a split year as respects that individual, the taxable pension income for the tax year is the full amount of the pension income arising in the UK part of the year, subject to subsection[^key-80487eb88fcc6b65be9b379757a313bc] (3) and section 576A.

(2)[^key-98000e7f0edbc234f3514025418d9030] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The full amount of the pension income arising in the tax year, or (as the case may be) the[^key-334a19c0bf9495dadf093269a5393a0a] UK part of the tax year, is treated as relevant foreign income for the purposes of Chapters 2 and 3 of Part 8 of ITTOIA[^key-63a5a911ead4c377385af0439586dce9] 2005 (relevant foreign income: remittance basis and deductions and reliefs).

(4) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) See also Chapter 4 of that Part (unremittable income).
