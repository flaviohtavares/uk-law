# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 640A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 640A. Lump sums provided under armed forces early departure scheme[^key-cfefbea24d0706f91623bb9cf82d4daf][^key-3a82cfde06c91c5bd7f715e60f47b40c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No liability to income tax arises on a lump sum provided under a scheme established by the Armed Forces Early Departure Payments Scheme Order 2005 (S.I. 2005/437) or the Armed Forces Early Departure Payments Scheme Regulations 2014 ([^key-6c8eb364f9c7befdabab88ddbef06d69]S.I. 2014/2328).
