# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 307

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 307. Death or retirement benefit provision

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No liability to income tax arises by virtue of Chapter 10 of Part 3 (taxable benefits: residual liability to charge) in respect of provision made by an employee’s employer under a registered pension scheme or otherwise[^c-4427b174e05bbf5d265011a521c71ee6] for a retirement or death benefit.

(1A)[^c-080a806fc1c9b0ad25190d7bcc8a926b] Subsection (1) does not apply to provision made for insuring against the risk that a retirement or death benefit under an employer-financed retirement benefits scheme cannot be paid or given because of the employer’s insolvency.

(1B) In subsection (1A) “**employer-financed retirement benefits scheme**” has the same meaning as in Chapter 2 of Part 6 (see section 393A).

(2) In this section[^c-8fdd9afc5dc9a7e1de4d950acf05a061] “**retirement or death benefit**” means a pension, annuity, lump sum, gratuity or other similar benefit which will be paid or given to the employee , or paid or given in respect of the employee to any other individual or to a charity,[^c-0c455aebc63d12d26b19737ad3c30dde] in the event of the employee’s retirement or death.
