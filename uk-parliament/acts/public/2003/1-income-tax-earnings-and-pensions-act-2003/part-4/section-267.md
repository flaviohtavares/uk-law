# Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003, section 267

## 267. Exemption of credit-tokens used for exempt benefits

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No liability to income tax arises by virtue of Chapter 4 of Part 3 (taxable benefits: vouchers and credit-tokens) in respect of a credit-token if or to the extent that the token is used to obtain anything the direct provision of which—

- (a) would fall within one of the provisions specified in subsection (2), or
- (b) would do so if the employee were not in lower-paid employment as a minister of religion[^c-1936d7a452f837ddbe34dcf347ff4d2f].

(2) Those provisions are—

- (a) section 237(1) (parking provision),
- (b) section 245 (travelling and subsistence during public transport strikes),
- (c) section 246 (transport between home and work for disabled employees: general),
- (d) section 247 (provision of cars for disabled employees),
- (e) section 248 (transport home: late night working and failure of car-sharing arrangements), ...
- (f) section 265 (third party entertainment).
- (g)[^c-46593df832b1f82af848d81da7532b57] section 319 (mobile telephones). , ...
- (h)[^c-08912ffcc659427f191738a016c580c9] section 320A (eye tests and special corrective appliances) ...[^c-a9334ce256307c7d30fc1120f7fe689e]
- (i) section 320B (health screening and medical check-ups) , and[^c-09f13fa7c8f726f69db60ab837e086d4]
- (j) section 320D (flu vaccinations).
