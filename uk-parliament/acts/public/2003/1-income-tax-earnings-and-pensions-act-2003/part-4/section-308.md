## 308. Exemption of contributions to registered pension scheme[^c-02290f1a592d995110ba7aab6239b649]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No liability to income tax arises in respect of earnings where an employee’s employer makes contributions under a registered pension scheme in respect of the employee[^c-4251abda62f510df17a264a55e2f48f5].
