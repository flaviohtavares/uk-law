## 297B. Armed forces: the Council Tax Relief[^c-c11eb6696c4b03b4e797eb0a6f49769f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No liability to income tax arises in respect of payments of the Council Tax Relief to members of the armed forces of the Crown.

(2) Payments of the Council Tax Relief are payments designated as such under a Royal Warrant made under section 333 of the Armed Forces Act 2006[^c-371ff3695fc7fcff62f9460195f0c6c9] .
