# Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003, section 34

## 34. Compulsory police services agreement

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-9785e8952d3f51c35d57175a7d50c1cb] The Secretary of State may by order—

- (a) require a person who provides railway services to enter into a police services agreement;
- (b) require each member of a class of persons providing railway services to enter into a police services agreement.

(2)[^c-5f7204c2bf01b8997fab244e28443895] A person commits an offence if—

- (a) he is subject to a requirement under subsection (1), and
- (b) he provides railway services without having entered into a police services agreement in respect of the railway or railway property in connection with which he provides the services.

(3)[^c-5f7204c2bf01b8997fab244e28443895] A person who is guilty of an offence under subsection (2) shall be liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine.

(4)[^c-5f7204c2bf01b8997fab244e28443895] The Authority shall take reasonable steps to facilitate compliance by a person with a requirement imposed under subsection (1).

(5)[^c-41fa3d011acdea5544e49b8c13c8c9cf] Before imposing a requirement on a person or class under subsection (1) the Secretary of State shall consult the person or each member of the class.
