## 27. Civilian employees[^c-81ed091a34d656b96def2ca4c1d254e1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Authority may employ persons to assist the Police Force.

(2) A person appointed under this section shall be under the direction and control of either the Chief Constable or the Authority, in accordance with his terms of employment.

(3)[^c-09a2b060492ce13670b03cb17d0ebedb] Subsection (2) is subject to any provision included in a police force collaboration agreement by virtue of section 23(4) of the Police Act 1996.
