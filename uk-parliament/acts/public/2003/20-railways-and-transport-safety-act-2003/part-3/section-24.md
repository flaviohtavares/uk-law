# Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003, section 24

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Constables[^key-a4dc7d252cf15be6edab67d16bd5a372]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Authority shall appoint and employ constables of the Police Force.

(2) A constable of the Police Force shall be under the direction and control of the Chief Constable.

(3) Section 29 of the Police Act 1996 (attestation) shall apply to a constable of the Police Force appointed in England or Wales (with the omission of the words in paragraph (b) “having jurisdiction within the police area”).

(4)[^key-c603ea17645572a014cf23fd31c112f5] Section 10 of the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 (declaration) shall apply to a constable of the Police Force appointed in Scotland.

(5)[^key-7000baa7635ba5b4b042f8bf94b9e778] Subsection (2) is subject to any provision included in a police force collaboration agreement by virtue of section 23(4) of the Police Act 1996.
