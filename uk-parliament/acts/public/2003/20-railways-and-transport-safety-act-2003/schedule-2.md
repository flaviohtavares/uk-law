## SCHEDULE 2 — Abolition of Rail Regulator: Consequential Amendments

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 16

### Part 1 — Railways Act 1993 (c. 43)

**1.**[^c-8ea74b9a556e9537d8620d0d3e1b837c] The Railways Act 1993 shall be amended as follows.

**2.**[^c-dbe755047e20758414092cc988a7bef1] Section 1 and Schedule 1 (the Rail Regulator) shall cease to have effect.

**3.**[^c-f3d078fb808b966abb66cc4cc3c786ca] Subject to the following paragraphs of this Schedule, in the provisions specified in the following table—

- (a) for “the Regulator” substitute “ the Office of Rail Regulation ”,
- (b) for “he” and “him”, where they refer only to the Regulator, substitute “ it ”, and
- (c) for “his”, where it refers to the Regulator, substitute “ its ”.
