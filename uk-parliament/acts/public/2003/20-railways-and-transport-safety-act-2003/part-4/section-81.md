# Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003, section 81

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Prescribed limit[^c-ced151ae536884a95505af74c6656458]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The prescribed limit of alcohol for the purposes of this Part is—

- (a) in the case of breath, 25[^c-f73f52149cfb69a6c0783928db60e98e] microgrammes of alcohol in 100 millilitres,
- (b) in the case of blood, 50[^c-57fc769f78c0ae9a5dc3071575c68888] milligrammes of alcohol in 100 millilitres, and
- (c) in the case of urine, 67[^c-04e0de41092dccc444d20aecdb9176f7] milligrammes of alcohol in 100 millilitres.

(2) The Secretary of State may make regulations amending subsection (1).
