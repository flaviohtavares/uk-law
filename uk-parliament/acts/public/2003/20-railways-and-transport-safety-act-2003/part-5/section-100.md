# Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003, section 100

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 100. Crown application[^c-5cdd13834df1d38906bfef5f0356072b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part shall apply to a function or activity performed or carried out in relation to an aircraft which belongs to or is employed in the service of the Crown.

(2) This section is subject to section 101.
