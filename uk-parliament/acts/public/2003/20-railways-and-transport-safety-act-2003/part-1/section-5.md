# Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/20
>

## 5. Assistance to others[^key-483cee24500abffad08a23c521cf3a01]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Chief Inspector of Rail Accidents may arrange for the Rail Accident Investigation Branch to assist any person; in particular, assistance—

- (a) may be provided with or without charge;
- (b) may be provided inside or outside the United Kingdom.
