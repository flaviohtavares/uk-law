# Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Establishment[^c-b63e60e570d6ff6695484b34f3d0e9b4]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) There shall be a body corporate to be known as the Office of Rail and Road[^c-c9960c57308b20ff80fd4ade6b0f46c2].

(2) Schedule 1 (which makes provision about the Office) shall have effect.
