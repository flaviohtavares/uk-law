# Local Government Act 2003, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Temporary borrowing[^c-48c0adac8085c709510f8509ce262649][^c-29f0ffdb208c59e721b760e1edb0dcd2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to subsection (2), any limit for the time being determined by or for a local authority under section 3, or applicable to it under section 4 or 12A[^c-6ca383b6ed046b29e51904d45c20b555], shall be treated for the purposes of this Chapter as increased by the amount of any payment which—

- (a) is due to the authority in the period to which the limit relates, but
- (b) has not yet been received by it.

(2) In the case of a limit determined under section 3, or set under section 4(2) or 12A[^c-06479bb3c7d22e82b08df7caebd1b828], subsection (1) shall not apply to any payment whose delayed receipt was taken into account in arriving at the limit.
