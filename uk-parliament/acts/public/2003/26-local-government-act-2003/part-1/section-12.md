## 12. Power to invest[^c-46e9940a1440841273709e4131f87462][^c-97326cfd4ce06467fd5277ea0d1d7890]

*Extent: E+W*

A local authority may invest—

- (a) for any purpose relevant to its functions under any enactment, or
- (b) for the purposes of the prudent management of its financial affairs.

This is subject to a direction under section 12A (risk-mitigation directions).[^c-23e99694610cb376e82645b4d77e3d5f]
