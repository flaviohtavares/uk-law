## 7. “Credit arrangements”[^c-06998675d5886c916c10501e24464b5f][^c-a731a2acd702985dc4c87006c5d67fa2][^c-ef58782d519242b5c76d4c3dea50c8de][^c-4d7be73f71b6b96e71027ddd1f955392]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-ada7c4389f75cbb9835e6ec0d2ad688a] For the purposes of this Chapter, a local authority shall be taken to have entered into a credit arrangement where—

- (a) it enters into a transaction which gives rise to a liability on its part, and
- (b) the liability is a qualifying liability.

(2) A transaction entered into by a local authority is to be taken for the purposes of subsection (1) as giving rise to a liability on the part of the authority if—

- (a)[^c-ada7c4389f75cbb9835e6ec0d2ad688a] it falls in accordance with proper practices to be treated for the purposes of the authority’s accounts as giving rise to such a liability, or
- (b)[^c-3ee847a7f31a1511d29c0fc1469e9e83] it falls in accordance with regulations made by the Secretary of State to be treated as falling within paragraph (a).

(3) The reference in subsection (1)(b) to a qualifying liability is to any liability other than—

- (a)[^c-ada7c4389f75cbb9835e6ec0d2ad688a] a liability to repay money,
- (b)[^c-ada7c4389f75cbb9835e6ec0d2ad688a] a liability in respect of which the date for performance is less than 12 months after the date on which the transaction giving rise to the liability is entered into, and
- (c)[^c-3ee847a7f31a1511d29c0fc1469e9e83] a liability of a description specified for the purposes of this provision by regulations made by the Secretary of State.
