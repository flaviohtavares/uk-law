# Local Government Act 2003, section 24

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 24. Wales[^c-12792766ebab130a94ad82eb97ca4278]

*Extent: E+W*

In its application to Wales, this Part, except section 19 and Schedule 1, has effect as if for any reference to the Secretary of State there were substituted a reference to the Welsh Ministers[^c-cee650e722493b32a13df6fb472bfd79].

(1A)[^c-5fb2fb87530186436b9d43571fdbd0e9] Subsection (1) does not apply to section 21 to the extent that it confers functions on the Secretary of State in relation to a person or body that—

- (a) is a relevant authority for the purposes of the Local Audit and Accountability Act 2014, and
- (b) exercises functions in relation to an area that is partly in England and partly in Wales.
