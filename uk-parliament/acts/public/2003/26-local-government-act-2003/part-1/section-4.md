# Local Government Act 2003, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Imposition of borrowing limits[^key-ecb6bd11e24a2b2099ee29fd98a16ab6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-e9b855c06dc75a8ca37943bcd4a641af] The Secretary of State may for national economic reasons by regulations set limits in relation to the borrowing of money by local authorities.

(2)[^key-e9b855c06dc75a8ca37943bcd4a641af] The Secretary of State may by direction set limits in relation to the borrowing of money by a particular local authority for the purpose of ensuring that the authority does not borrow more than it can afford.

(3)[^key-e9b855c06dc75a8ca37943bcd4a641af] Different limits may be set under subsection (1) or (2) in relation to different kinds of borrowing.

(4)[^key-8b1cee27b06c2bd630b69793b439a616] A local authority subject to a limit set under subsection (1) may transfer any headroom it has in relation to the limit to another local authority subject to a corresponding limit.

(5)[^key-e9b855c06dc75a8ca37943bcd4a641af] The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision about the exercise of the right under subsection (4) and may, in particular, make provision about—

- (a) the circumstances in which a local authority is to be regarded as having headroom for the purposes of that subsection, and
- (b) the amount of headroom which it has for those purposes.

(6)[^key-8b1cee27b06c2bd630b69793b439a616] Where an amount is transferred under subsection (4), this Chapter shall have effect—

- (a) in relation to the transferor, as if the limit in relation to which the headroom exists were reduced by that amount, and
- (b) in relation to the transferee, as if the corresponding limit to which it is subject were increased by that amount.
