# Local Government Act 2003, section 67

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Exemptions for agricultural buildings

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-d889c8d60167ea90bee47f452d4679ae][^c-96f9f11e8eb57d30531cbbae90eb9a77] Schedule 5 to the 1988 Act (exemptions from non-domestic rating) is amended as follows.

(2)[^c-02867187c5415f6d18110cbbf7756016][^c-4876456e878358322379df8892e0f7c6] For paragraph 3(a) (which provides that a building is an agricultural building if it is occupied together with agricultural land and is used solely in connection with agricultural operations on the land) there is substituted—

> - (a) it is occupied together with agricultural land and is used solely in connection with agricultural operations on that or other agricultural land, or.

(3)[^c-d889c8d60167ea90bee47f452d4679ae][^c-96f9f11e8eb57d30531cbbae90eb9a77] After paragraph 7(1)(b) there is inserted

> , and
>
> - (c) the members who are occupiers of the land together have control of the body.

(4)[^c-d889c8d60167ea90bee47f452d4679ae][^c-96f9f11e8eb57d30531cbbae90eb9a77] For paragraph 7(3) there is substituted—

> (3) This sub-paragraph applies if—
>
> - (a) the building in question is occupied by a body corporate any of whose members are, or are together with the body, the occupiers of the building or buildings mentioned in sub-paragraph (2)(a) above, and
> - (b) the members who are occupiers of the land together have control of the body.

(5)[^c-d889c8d60167ea90bee47f452d4679ae][^c-96f9f11e8eb57d30531cbbae90eb9a77] After paragraph 7(8) there is inserted—

> (9) In this paragraph “**control**” shall be construed in accordance with section 416(2) to (6) of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988.
