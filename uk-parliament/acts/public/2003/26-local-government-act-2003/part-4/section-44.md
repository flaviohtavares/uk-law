# Local Government Act 2003, section 44

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Duty to comply with arrangements[^key-c03fe670d30a87a23c14247a1278537c][^key-36f954aa8c1d0fbd6380266ee00ea19c][^key-3e2b937793637045d4b193e54290d011][^key-7abf4ff0c5290df3c559c4985d6c1887]

*Extent: E+W*

Where BID arrangements are in force, the billing authority which made the arrangements must comply with them.
