# Local Government Act 2003, section 96

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Regulation of trading powers

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The appropriate person may by order impose conditions in relation to the exercise by a relevant authority[^c-7206ec3a139857dfbb09e0f7853c4eb6] of—

- (a) a power to do anything for a commercial purpose, or
- (b) a power to do anything for such a purpose through a company.

(2) In exercising such a power as is mentioned in subsection (1), a relevant authority[^c-22ca9f4192fa056d901e39bc0de3170e] shall have regard to such guidance as the appropriate person may issue.

(3) An order under this section may be made in relation to—

- (a) all relevant authorities[^c-deb10256faa8953341ec96c62660a53f],
- (b) particular relevant authorities[^c-cb6ee97112ae581b1523bae52226f5b9], or
- (c) particular descriptions of relevant authority[^c-b568aebfa397b547a94e50a0c5dd6810].

(4)[^c-d73fc75e14a5a3797b9714b26ef4b923] In this section, “**relevant authority**” has the meaning given in section 95.

(5) In subsection (1)(b), “**company**” has the same meaning as in Part 5 of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989.
