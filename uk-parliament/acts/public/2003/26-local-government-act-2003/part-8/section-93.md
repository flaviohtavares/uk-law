# Local Government Act 2003, section 93

## 93. Power to charge for discretionary services[^key-36756b1345a68f5d11ab72dae26e1ce4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-a356cc0f2dc57332d4f210990feacbce][^key-3ea2b4fbb6bbae384a6625426a208608][^key-5e36c434323b8fa6177d85cedeeeb292] Subject to the following provisions, a relevant authority[^key-553860775fb96f1e24600ab77d0d9912] may charge a person for providing a service to him if—

- (a) the authority is authorised, but not required, by an enactment to provide the service to him, and
- (b) he has agreed to its provision.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply if the authority—

- (a) has power apart from this section to charge for the provision of the service, or
- (b) is expressly prohibited from charging for the provision of the service.

(3) The power under subsection (1) is subject to a duty to secure that, taking one financial year with another, the income from charges under that subsection does not exceed the costs of provision.

(4) The duty under subsection (3) shall apply separately in relation to each kind of service.

(5) Within the framework set by subsections (3) and (4), a relevant authority[^key-83c3bcd72ad98d4ccb1bc9a44fe0bb37] may set charges as it thinks fit and may, in particular—

- (a) charge only some persons for providing a service;
- (b) charge different persons different amounts for the provision of a service.

(6) In carrying out functions under this section, a relevant authority[^key-1fbb4cbc689f860854d519812c629ae9] shall have regard to such guidance as the appropriate person may issue.

(7) The following shall be disregarded for the purposes of subsection (2)(b)—

- (a) section 111(3) of the Local Government Act 1972 (c. 70) (subsidiary powers of local authorities not to include power to raise money),
- (b) section 34(2) of the Greater London Authority Act 1999 (c. 29) (corresponding provision for Greater London Authority), [^key-083431e1d8fdcde5cbd7b7a574beb2d9]...
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f46f8324d7858ec008bc3fd83cff6ace] section 3(2) of the Local Government Act 2000 (c. 22) (well-being powers not to include power to raise money). *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f46f8324d7858ec008bc3fd83cff6ace]
- (d)[^key-a142b02d1f57c5757ccbae2e8a75304d] section 100(2) of the Local Transport Act 2008 (well-being powers of Integrated Transport Authorities , combined authorities and combined county authorities[^key-953cc6471b2ef87084a21a6f36846ff4]),
- (e) section 102C(4) of that Act (Integrated Transport Authorities),
- (f) section 10B(4) of the Transport Act 1968 (Passenger Transport Executives), [^key-e9d4bcdb4749bd903020488d0c5b5212]...
- (g) section 113B(4) of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009 (economic prosperity boards and combined authorities), and[^key-5a149702f36f0fb1832e79561e199b0c]
- (h) section 50(4) of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023 (combined county authorities).

(8) In subsection (1), “**enactment**” includes an enactment comprised in subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act 1978 (c. 30)).

(9)[^key-b9b35ecadf0cc5b229c25615defc9489] In this section, “ **relevant authority** ” means—

- (a) a best value authority;
- (aa)[^key-bd939af9a2d0e7ec1250744322a4f847] a county council or county borough council in Wales;
- (ab)[^key-d513950a14b2e7618bc228b50b0533be] the Passenger Transport Executive of an integrated transport area in England;
- (ac)[^key-af8662ae7d54e2d9bb14495546e6885f] a National Park authority for a National Park in Wales;
- (b) a parish council;
- (c) a parish meeting of a parish which does not have a separate parish council; or
- (d) a community council.
