# Local Government Act 2003, section 127

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 127. Minor and consequential amendments and repeals[^c522450]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-a7ff3ab90e1907fc7f5cbb3649f13132][^c-c0bbf8e57f24d0b2b9d30be180ffc9fa][^c-ccb523d8f1a585422a5ecbd8aa597d49][^c-b4ca6736b495151c2d58c0c39c9951da][^c-7905710e352ff03cf72d6ede79bf9144][^c-fd9635d2e888fabf2a5556f7b9e44535][^c-71a5d565d2fcf89f7ee04d56bfd0c53c][^c-646327aa3ed5639fa9d51881b3c11fa5][^c-a92a33da5237ae494fd14ad67b83c3fc] Schedule 7 (minor and consequential amendments) has effect.

(2)[^c-553514a1b8151c5aa145cf3c2e96ce77][^c-575a5deeb02d092dedd974d5868ecea9][^c-fb99cbcb17a5f87b1efaf0d89f27fca9][^c-35af70dc2ab8a00e80c5848067392300][^c-7bb6f62a67e730f0a6ff05fdcc2fe4d6][^c-c1d238714da11dd4e2ebd5598c78c211][^c-06dfb78854e88a4f1f76c89ed20baa58][^c-5513e7e825fbb41e9bfd96889c4a7bb1][^c-5a04889af1de86a3ef4188d0f3640e80] The enactments and instruments specified in Schedule 8 are hereby repealed or revoked to the extent specified there.

(3)[^c-8a75542f8237e1ca435bafb632949e6d][^c-e037f4a34275d87f7cf5087032f6d628] The Secretary of State may by order make provision consequential on this Act amending, repealing or revoking (with or without savings) any provision of an Act passed before or in the same session as this Act, or of an instrument made under an Act before the passing of this Act.

(4)[^c-8a75542f8237e1ca435bafb632949e6d][^c-e037f4a34275d87f7cf5087032f6d628] In this section, “**Act**” includes a private or local Act.
