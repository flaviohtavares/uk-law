# Local Government Act 2003, section 105

## 105. The Valuation Tribunal Service

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-87e00425390a0df1fbdf71ca745bda42] There shall be a body corporate to be known as the Valuation Tribunal Service (referred to in this section, section 106 and Schedules 4 and 5 as “the Service”).

(2)[^c-87e00425390a0df1fbdf71ca745bda42][^c-36135674d8841cebc64374202295b868] The Service shall have the following functions in relation to the Valuation Tribunal for England (referred to in this section and Schedule 4 as “the Tribunal”)[^c-b9d682dc01f6abe75ed7783aea6bea48]—

- (a) providing, or arranging for the provision of, the services required for the operation of the Tribunal[^c-63a17ce7871f528d22a5acd3c1ceaf6d], in particular—
  - (i) accommodation,
  - (ii) staff (including the clerk of the Tribunal[^c-e02c7005c6a717393eaedf26c9685c43]),
  - (iii) information technology,
  - (iv) equipment, and
  - (v) training for members and staff of the Tribunal (including the clerk of the Tribunal)[^c-9d9aa260a0966c7d45e56b77c74b06a4];
- (aa)[^c-1b4ff13e851ed170ba6f29f77808f52b] making payments in accordance with paragraph A14 or A18A(8)[^c-d181794c52948d787d9e3db16351fc04] of Schedule 11 to the Local Government Finance Act 1988;
- (b) giving general advice about procedure in relation to proceedings before the Tribunal[^c-f4da341b800bd4c65120b8ba72f5d020].

(3)[^c-87e00425390a0df1fbdf71ca745bda42] The Service shall provide the Secretary of State with such information, advice and assistance as he may require.

(4)[^c-87e00425390a0df1fbdf71ca745bda42] The Service may do anything which it considers is calculated to facilitate, or is conducive or incidental to, the carrying-out of its functions.

(5)[^c-87e00425390a0df1fbdf71ca745bda42] The Service shall carry out its functions with respect to the Tribunal[^c-e1bcea4a37d51a8a87d16b6930a16608] in the manner which it considers best calculated to secure its[^c-ba693c2a80d030972e0afdb0a7ccdec9] efficient and independent operation.

(6)[^c-87e00425390a0df1fbdf71ca745bda42] The Service shall, in relation to its functions with respect to the Tribunal[^c-b00b70a91589670daf180b27576f3324], consult the President of the Tribunal[^c-209d9e01f33d48fc6e79f58dd1ccb9a3] about the carrying-out of its functions.

(7)[^c-87e00425390a0df1fbdf71ca745bda42] The Secretary of State may—

- (a) after consultation with the Service, give directions to it for the purpose of securing the effective carrying-out of its functions, and
- (b) issue guidance to the Service about the carrying-out of its functions.

(8)[^c-87e00425390a0df1fbdf71ca745bda42] The Service shall, in carrying out its functions—

- (a) comply with any directions under subsection (7)(a), and
- (b) have regard to any guidance under subsection (7)(b).

(9)[^c-9922d55ca54e384d6fc66575ed00ba17][^c-a416158d31b4af2433a5d547a5fc7af5] Schedule 4 (which makes further provision about the Service) has effect.
