# Local Government Act 2003, section 94

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94. Power to disapply section 93(1)[^key-212a788869786990a1b93440ebba640d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The appropriate person may by order disapply section 93(1)—

- (a) in relation to particular descriptions of relevant authority[^key-18fd2506214ab5939abd6e513b41255a] or particular relevant authorities[^M_F_335784ff-788e-4518-bfcb-4aae72a2722a];
- (b) in relation to the provision of a particular kind of service by—
  - (i) all relevant authorities[^key-df20e2c5a593ad372ea422d3f17189b0],
  - (ii) particular relevant authorities[^key-b5d0b9921c7a32d4511ca36c062c1969], or
  - (iii) particular descriptions of relevant authority[^key-f642dd70026366ba14c69ee40e1c6df0].

(2) The power under subsection (1) includes power to disapply for a particular period.

(3)[^key-ceacff390a2ef0e41fbaaf9693e0936f] In this section, “**relevant authority**” has the meaning given in section 93.
