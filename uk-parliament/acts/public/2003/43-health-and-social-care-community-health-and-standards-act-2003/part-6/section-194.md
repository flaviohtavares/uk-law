## 194. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act—

- “[^c-0cd4ab3c6e635ee461487a68000e23d4]**the 2006 Act**” means the National Health Service Act 2006;
- “**the Assembly**” means the National Assembly for Wales.
