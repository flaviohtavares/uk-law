## 114. Complaints about social services: England[^c552114][^c-3dbd63107c2f92baf424f21792458841]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-aad502bfd41cb68cb6d656bdbaf3cc7f] The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision about the handling and consideration of complaints made under the regulations about—

- (a) the discharge by a local authority in England of any of its social services functions;
- (b) the provision of services by another person pursuant to arrangements made by such an authority in the discharge of those functions;
- (c) the provision of services by such an authority or any other person in pursuance of arrangements made by the authority under section 75 of the National Health Service Act 2006 or section 33 of the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^c-d027348419e20d98bef847732f9c8d4a] in relation to the functions of an NHS body (within the meaning of that section).

(2)[^c-aad502bfd41cb68cb6d656bdbaf3cc7f] Regulations under this section[^c-5f27464a855580ffe7415fd80b32ec98] may provide for a complaint to be considered by one or more of the following—

- (a) the local authority in respect of whose functions the complaint is made;
- (b)[^c-9bb7ac3a112076f698e04c7798dadb97] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) an independent panel established under the regulations;
- (d) any other person or body.

(3)[^c-31f7a30c1d6a15e3691f001d653e216e][^c-d44a028c965a5d6801104da205b01b52] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-31f7a30c1d6a15e3691f001d653e216e][^c-d44a028c965a5d6801104da205b01b52] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c-6e2f5873192ed226da8870ef18970794][^c-aad502bfd41cb68cb6d656bdbaf3cc7f] Regulations under this section may provide for a complaint or any matter raised by a complaint—

- (a) [^c-62d09b16373987c02a2c145844034d66]... to be referred to a Local Commissioner under Part 3 of the Local Government Act 1974 (c. 7) [^c-5fa43f538bd772b91b889bc80eb2b27e]... for him to consider whether to investigate the complaint or matter under that Part (and for the complaint to be treated as satisfying sections 26A and 26B of that Act)[^c-f51b7ece9204bffb9af5d9bdca1c8b3f];
- (b)[^c-1984e860232eabe44ea5d5713a8357b8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) to be referred to any other person or body for him or it to consider whether to take any action otherwise than under the regulations.

(6)[^c-aad502bfd41cb68cb6d656bdbaf3cc7f] Regulations under this section may not make provision about complaints capable of being considered as representations under section 24D or section 26 of the Children Act 1989 (c. 41).
