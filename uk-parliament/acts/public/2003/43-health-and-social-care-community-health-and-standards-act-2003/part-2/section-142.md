# Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003, section 142

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 142. Annual reports of Assembly[^c-a1e82e8a7153fd48c126a1ffca435d07]

*Extent: E+W*

As soon as possible after the end of each financial year of the Assembly, the Assembly must make and publish a report or reports on—

- (a) what it has found during the year in the course of exercising—
  - (i) its functions under Chapter 4 [^c-d864427e9d070aa5decbf72f5d995b58]... of this Part (other than any function of making regulations);
  - (ii) its functions exercisable by virtue of section 5(1)(b)[^c-16e4bd2cfea2bf9771c43c09d047e0f9] and 8(1) to (3) of the Care Standards Act 2000 (c. 14);
- (b) the way in which the Assembly has during the year exercised those functions.
