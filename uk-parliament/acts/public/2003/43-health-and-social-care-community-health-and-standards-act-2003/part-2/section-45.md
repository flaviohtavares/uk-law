## 45. Quality in health care[^key-e56cb5c3e5706a8ef8b87828959a4da8][^key-d82b3d356ac07174819a59aa0d3bcce4][^key-d7c79ec6ebf80dabffb58d568612ccdc]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-86c4bf24e2c36ea2ebd23978c75c73f7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) In this Part “**health care**” means—

- (a) services provided to individuals for or in connection with the prevention, diagnosis or treatment of illness; and
- (b) the promotion and protection of public health.

(3) In subsection (2)(a), “**illness**” has the meaning given by section 275 of the 2006 Act[^key-dd4922f888c6db5ea5ca8631814bf468].

(4)[^key-7b806df6c11de544cfe50e3452ec531f] In this Chapter references to the provision of health care for a body include in the case of an English NHS body references to the provision of health care in respect of which direct payments are made by that body under section 12A(1), or under regulations under section 12A(4), of the National Health Service Act 2006.
