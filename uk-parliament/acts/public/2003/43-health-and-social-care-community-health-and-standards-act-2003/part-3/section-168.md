# Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003, section 168

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 168. Interpretation of Part 3[^c552136][^c-14df0cd57c6ee67638caa1f7f4c5ff87][^c-fd27c2f8ae8cd7958706fd506327c692]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Part—

- “**the 1978 Act**” means the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 (c. 29);
- “**compensation payment**” has the meaning given in section 150;
- “**health service hospital**” means a health service hospital within the meaning of the 2006 Act, the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^c-df64262024b5bcaccb62a4b58c68bb95] or the 1978 Act;
- “**injured person**” has the meaning given in section 150(1);
- “**NHS ambulance services**” means ambulance services provided under section 3(1)(c) of the 2006 Act, section 3(1)(c) of the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^c-5dec7cd4fd655e99362d40061857cacb] or section 45 of the 1978 Act;
- “**NHS treatment**” has the meaning given in section 150(7);
- “**prescribed**” means prescribed by regulations.
