# Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003, section 169

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 169. Consequential and minor repeals[^c-b0c9ff9bc6a07a1abe490d766b5a7d5c][^c-745ee61a0c81b888c3d2b89d290d7745]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Road Traffic (NHS Charges) Act 1999 (c. 3) shall cease to have effect.

(2) In the Road Traffic Act 1988, in section 161(1), in the definition of “hospital”, paragraph (b) is omitted.
