# Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003, section 15

## 15. Demoted assured shorthold tenancies (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-5d1715e870b625604918b279ce91e457][^c-1c0b74546e35b9ab5b351c0cbaddfe8a][^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a][^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In the Housing Act 1988 (c. 50) after section 20A (duty of landlord to provide statement of terms for certain tenancies) there is inserted the following section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]

> **20B. Demoted assured shorthold tenancies (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]**
>
> (1) An assured tenancy is an assured shorthold tenancy to which this section applies (a demoted assured shorthold tenancy) if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]
>
> - (a) the tenancy is created by virtue of an order of the court under section 82A of the Housing Act 1985 or section 6A of this Act (a demotion order), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]
> - (b) the landlord is a registered social landlord. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]
>
> (2) At the end of the period of one year starting with the day when the demotion order takes effect a demoted assured shorthold tenancy ceases to be an assured shorthold tenancy unless subsection (3) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]
>
> (3) This subsection applies if before the end of the period mentioned in subsection (2) the landlord gives notice of proceedings for possession of the dwelling house. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]
>
> (4) If subsection (3) applies the tenancy continues to be a demoted assured shorthold tenancy until the end of the period mentioned in subsection (2) or (if later) until one of the following occurs— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]
>
> - (a) the notice of proceedings for possession is withdrawn; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]
> - (b) the proceedings are determined in favour of the tenant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]
> - (c) the period of six months beginning with the date on which the notice is given ends and no proceedings for possession have been brought. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]
>
> (5) Registered social landlord has the same meaning as in Part 1 of the Housing Act 1996. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]

(2) In section 21 of that Act (recovery of possession on expiry or termination of assured shorthold tenancy) after subsection (5) there is inserted the following subsection— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]

> (5A) Subsection (5) above does not apply to an assured shorthold tenancy to which section 20B (demoted assured shorthold tenancies) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]

(3) In Schedule 2A to that Act (assured tenancies which are not shorthold tenancies) after paragraph 5 (former secure tenancies) there is inserted the following paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]

> **5A. Former demoted tenancies (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]**
>
> An assured tenancy which ceases to be an assured shorthold tenancy by virtue of section 20B(2) or (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-322c69360d39223b9ba7d492dfe59e4a]
