# Anti-social Behaviour Act 2003, section 84

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/38
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 84. Crown application[^key-e1ae18e639dacad6cdb6a02428e3373f][^key-df0a4ae2095dea9606f11f66a2f081b4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Part and any provision made under it bind the Crown.

(2) This section does not impose criminal liability on the Crown.

(3) Subsection (2) does not affect the criminal liability of persons in the service of the Crown.
