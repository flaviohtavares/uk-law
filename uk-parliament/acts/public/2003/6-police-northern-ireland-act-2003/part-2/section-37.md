# Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2003, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (13).** Text as at 2016-05-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/6
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 37. Code of ethics

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Department of Justice[^key-082ab78d229f78d9ceadf2922b5c4afb] may by order apply the code of ethics issued by the Board under section 52 of the Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2000 (c. 32) to persons designated under section 30 , 30A[^key-2a99f3e1773de218f4fd749412a8e3ee] or 31.

(2) An order under subsection (1) may apply the code with such modifications as are specified in the order.

(3) If an order is made under subsection (1) applying the code to a person designated under section 30 , 30A[^key-4d3eb1c7a972863ace8b3b513688f48e] or 31 he must be guided by the code as it applies to him in carrying out the functions for the purposes of which any power or duty is conferred or imposed on him by his designation.
