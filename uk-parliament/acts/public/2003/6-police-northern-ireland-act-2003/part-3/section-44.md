# Police (Northern Ireland) Act 2003, section 44

## 44. Orders and regulations

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Any power of the Secretary of State or the Department of Justice[^c-a92f2c35838af2cb3229b69912bd5924] to make orders or regulations under this Act is exercisable by statutory rule for the purposes of the Statutory Rules (Northern Ireland) Order 1979 (S.I. 1979/1573 (N.I. 12)).

(2) Orders or regulations under this Act—

- (a) may make different provision for different purposes;
- (b) may include supplementary, incidental, saving or transitional provisions.

(3) No order may be made under section [^c-4e310b807745c59f95bafb9de1232b7a]... 23(7) unless a draft of the statutory rule containing the order to be made has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House.

(4)[^c-489c08dd5464cef15f1455190f47b70f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) A statutory rule made by the Secretary of State[^c-33b0dcb6b23b599d4fcfdf90028b1625] under this Act (other than one containing an order under section [^c-f4f7085fd7eeda99387c6a200225b32c]... 23(7), 26(4) [^c-79777514a8dc78f79678d169a80788e4]... or 42(6)) is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament in like manner as a statutory instrument and section 5 of the Statutory Instruments Act 1946 (c. 36) applies accordingly.

(6)[^c-c5c02c0fab5a80988015b9aa0228ebbc] An order or regulations made by the Department of Justice under this Act (other than an order under section 41(2))[^c-a5104ff53a8327231bf2352ec2f90996] shall be subject to negative resolution (within the meaning of section 41(6) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954).
