# Courts Act 2003, section 99

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 99. High Court writs of execution[^c19095701]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Schedule 7 contains provisions about High Court writs of execution and about warrants issued in connection with the compulsory acquisition of land[^c19549881] .

(2) Any rule of law requiring a writ of execution issued from the High Court to be directed to a sheriff is abolished.
