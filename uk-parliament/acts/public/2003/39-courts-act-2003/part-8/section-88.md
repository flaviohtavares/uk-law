# Courts Act 2003, section 88

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 88. Extension of time for criminal appeals to Supreme Court[^c19287081]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Amend section 2 of the Administration of Justice Act 1960 (c. 65) (applications for leave to appeal to Supreme Court[^c19287091]) as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a) for “fourteen” (in both places) substitute “ 28 ”, and
- (b) for “date of the decision of that court” substitute “ relevant date ”.

(3) After subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) In subsection (1), “**the relevant date**” means—
>
> - (a) the date of the decision of the court below, or
> - (b) if later, the date on which that court gives reasons for its decision.

(4) Amend section 34 of the 1968 Act (applications for leave to appeal to the Supreme Court[^c19287101]) as follows.

(5) In subsection (1)—

- (a) for “fourteen” (in both places) substitute “ 28 ”, and
- (b) for “date of the decision of the Court of Appeal[^c19287121]” substitute “ relevant date ”.

(6) After subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) In subsection (1), “**the relevant date**” means—
>
> - (a) the date of the Court of Appeal’s decision, or
> - (b) if later, the date on which the Court of Appeal[^c19287131] gives reasons for its decision.
