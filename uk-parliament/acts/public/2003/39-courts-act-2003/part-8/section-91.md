# Courts Act 2003, section 91

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Extension of time for appeals from Courts-Martial Appeal Court

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Amend section 40 of the Courts-Martial (Appeals) Act 1968 (c. 20) (applications for leave to appeal to Supreme Court[^c19153551]) as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a) for “fourteen” (in both places) substitute “ 28 ”, and
- (b) for “date of the decision of the Appeal Court[^c19153591]” substitute “ relevant date ”.

(3) After subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) In subsection (1), “**the relevant date**” means—
>
> - (a) the date of the Appeal Court’s decision, or
> - (b) if later, the date on which the Appeal Court[^c19153651] gives reasons for its decision.
