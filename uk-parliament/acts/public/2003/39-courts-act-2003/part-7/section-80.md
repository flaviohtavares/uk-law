# Courts Act 2003, section 80

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Power to amend legislation in connection with the rules[^c19113141][^c-18108ff4e8c57790597b50e1f9d8ad3a]

*Extent: E+W*

1[^c19152761] The Lord Chancellor may , after consulting the Lord Chief Justice,[^c19152791] by order amend, repeal or revoke any enactment to the extent that he considers necessary or desirable—

- (a) in order to facilitate the making of Family Procedure Rules, or
- (b) in consequence of section 75, 76 or 79 or Family Procedure Rules.

(2)[^c19152881] The Lord Chief Justice may nominate a judicial office holder (as defined in section 109(4) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005) to exercise his functions under this section.
