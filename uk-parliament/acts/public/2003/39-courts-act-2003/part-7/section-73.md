# Courts Act 2003, section 73

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/39
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Power to amend legislation in connection with the rules[^c19094451]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c19553821][^c19150131] The Lord Chancellor may, [^c19553831]... after consulting the Lord Chief Justice[^c19150161], by order amend, repeal or revoke any enactment to the extent that he considers necessary or desirable—

- (a) in order to facilitate the making of Criminal Procedure Rules, or
- (b) in consequence of section 69 or 72 or Criminal Procedure Rules.

(2)[^c19150221] The Lord Chief Justice may nominate a judicial office holder (as defined in section 109(4) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005) to exercise his functions under this section.
