# Courts Act 2003, section 11

## 11. Resignation and removal of lay justices

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A lay justice may resign his office at any time.

(2) The Lord Chancellor may , with the concurrence of the Lord Chief Justice,[^c19137181] remove a lay justice from his office by an instrument on behalf and in the name of Her Majesty—

- (a) on the ground of incapacity or misbehaviour,
- (b) on the ground of a persistent failure to meet such standards of competence as are prescribed by a direction given by the Lord Chancellor with the concurrence of the Lord Chief Justice[^c19137211] , or
- (c) if he is satisfied that the lay justice is declining or neglecting to take a proper part in the exercise of his functions as a justice of the peace.
