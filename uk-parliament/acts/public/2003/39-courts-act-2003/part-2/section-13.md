## 13. Entry of names in the supplemental list

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to subsection[^c-ca352072b03f5f6764cdd9fb50251983] (3), the name of a lay justice who has reached 75[^c-68f28bac43605019e282685bc0f8367f] must be entered in the supplemental list.

(2)[^c-6be5dbbcd55bec7f200d040c8f03e15d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Where—

- (a) proceedings are, or are expected to be, in progress on the day on which the lay justice reaches 75[^c-f355e71b0b85ee3b41ee5332681b696f], and
- (b) the lay justice is exercising functions in those proceedings as a justice of the peace,

the Lord Chief Justice may, with the concurrence of the Lord Chancellor,[^c19192061] direct that the name of the lay justice need not be entered in the supplemental list until the proceedings have ended.

(4) The name of a lay justice must be entered in the supplemental list if—

- (a) he applies for it to be entered, and
- (b) the application is approved by the Lord Chancellor.

(5) The Lord Chancellor may , with the concurrence of the Lord Chief Justice,[^c19192071] direct that the name of a lay justice is to be entered in the supplemental list on the ground of incapacity.

(6)[^c19192081] The Lord Chief Justice may nominate a judicial office holder (as defined in section 109(4) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005) to exercise his functions under subsection (3).
