# Courts Act 2003, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/39

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Senior District Judge (Chief Magistrate)

*Extent: E+W*

Her Majesty[^c19142071]

- (a) may designate one of the District Judges (Magistrates' Courts) to be Senior District Judge (Chief Magistrate), and
- (b) if she[^c19142101] does so, may designate another of them to be the deputy of the Senior District Judge (Chief Magistrate).
