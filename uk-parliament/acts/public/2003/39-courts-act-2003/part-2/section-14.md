# Courts Act 2003, section 14

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 14. Removal of names from the supplemental list

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person’s name must be removed from the supplemental list if he ceases to be a justice of the peace.

(2)[^c-cf49f2f133963eb6e62053e02b1f28ec] A person’s name must be removed from the supplemental list if—

- (a) his name is in the list as a result of section 13(4) or (5), and
- (b) the Lord Chancellor , with the concurrence of the Lord Chief Justice,[^c19137521] directs its removal.
