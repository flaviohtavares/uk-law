# Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2022-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Duty of appropriate Minister to inform competent authority[^key-7bb5ed5a4c9219a2bbe873d9162eaaa5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where a competent authority of the Republic of Ireland[^key-6ff88e45c678d8b3d3867ffff637cacd] gives the appropriate Minister a notice under the specified agreement on driving disqualifications[^key-b64f050599c288d81dde8df393934e61] in respect of any person.

(2) If the appropriate Minister gives a notice under section 57 to that person, he must give the competent authority particulars of the disqualification which arises by virtue of that section.

(3) If the appropriate Minister does not give such a notice, he must give his reasons to the competent authority.
