# Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2022-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/32

## 58. Notice under section 57[^c-d94e6e83de78bf36d32229c0901e6556]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A notice under section 57 must—

- (a) give particulars of the offence in respect of which the Republic of Ireland disqualification[^c-e9df4c6018fad30315373d38641b008c] was imposed and the period of that disqualification,
- (b) state that the offender is disqualified in each part of the United Kingdom for a period equal to the unexpired period of the Republic of Ireland disqualification,
- (c) state the date from which, and period for which, he is disqualified,
- (d) give particulars of any relevant condition mentioned in section 57(4)(b),
- (e) give details of his right to appeal under section 59.

(2) A notice under section 57 must be in writing.

(3) A notice under section 57 may contain such other information as the appropriate Minister considers appropriate.
