# Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003, section 66

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2022-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/32

## 66. Effect of disqualification by virtue of section 57[^key-f461bd558e54752423fa5b0a84f98e6d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the holder of a Great Britain licence or Northern Ireland licence is disqualified by virtue of section 57, the licence is to be treated as revoked with effect from the beginning of the period of disqualification.
