# Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003, section 72

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2022-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/32

## 72. Regulations: Great Britain[^c-d4c23eac08f35e034f65d9982e57f62e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any power to make regulations conferred by this Chapter on the Secretary of State is exercisable by statutory instrument.

(2) A statutory instrument containing any such regulations is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament, subject to subsection (2A)[^c-2aeb9ae609e15e7510d42daf75580015].

(2A)[^c-1ff9884fb05cd1db993e6e738fb2457f] A statutory instrument containing regulations under section 54(6), 56(10) or 71A may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

(3) The regulations may make different provision for different purposes.
