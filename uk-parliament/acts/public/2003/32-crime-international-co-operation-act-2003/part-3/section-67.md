# Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003, section 67

## 67. Rule for determining end of period of disqualification[^key-b041f510be8af9c44cda5b43d2d5111f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In determining the expiration of the period for which a person is disqualified by virtue of section 57, any time during which—

- (a) the disqualification is suspended, or
- (b) he is not disqualified,

is to be disregarded.
