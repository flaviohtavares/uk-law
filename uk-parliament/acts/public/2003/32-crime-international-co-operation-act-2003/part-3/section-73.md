# Crime (International Co-operation) Act 2003, section 73

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2022-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/32

## 73. Regulations: Northern Ireland[^c-54b98bff6299b35b8f3ab209f8ec2a3e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any power to make regulations conferred by this Chapter on the Department is exercisable by statutory rule for the purposes of the Statutory Rules (Northern Ireland) Order 1979 (S.I. 1979/ 1573 (N.I. 12)).

(2) Any such regulations are subject to negative resolution (within the meaning of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 (c. 33 (N.I.)), subject to subsection (2A)[^c-0630ce6bd85ec6b4ae08cfe9515abe6b].

(2A)[^c-8e33e9f4fcc55cf405a202a386c9d53d] Regulations made under section 54(7) may not be made unless a draft of the regulations has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Northern Ireland Assembly.

(3) The regulations may make different provision for different purposes.
