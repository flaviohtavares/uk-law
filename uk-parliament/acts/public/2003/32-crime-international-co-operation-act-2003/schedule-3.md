## SCHEDULE 3 — Offences for the purposes of section 54: Great Britain[^c-2b82dd0f8d6074722532c58150fe63d4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 54

### Part 1 — Offences where order of disqualification for a minimum period unnecessary

**1.**[^c-71a0d6181b869676a45d56294c5c0f45] (1) Manslaughter or culpable homicide by the driver of a motor vehicle.

(2)[^c-d6b08e9de7786608e9f7ffeeca1a13f2] “Driver” has the same meaning as in the Road Traffic Act 1988.

**2.**[^c-ef36f11c724fd32ae6e555d59a95f2fe] An offence under section 89(1) of the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984 (c. 27) [^c-a71fcad078ebf10800169c16cf27e10e]... (exceeding speed limit).

**3.**[^c-f8fe4708dc308d37bee50d79081d1ee9] [^c-64d645653b6f80e71e553fd5f5920ca2]An offence under any of the following sections of the Road Traffic Act 1988 ...—

- (a) section 1 [^c-749ba3b4dc955fc4fe71f0efca16ab6b]... (causing death by dangerous driving),
- (aa)[^c-7e4a9513159f28311351b3a4c2cf4788] section 1A (causing serious injury by dangerous driving),
- (b) section 2 [^c-f7331b3e40b26b4938c29679a79bd4db]... (dangerous driving),
- (ba)[^c-470bf9510811529a3678b38a02384892] section 2B (causing death by careless, or inconsiderate, driving),
- (bb)[^c-69cca0a4401644f6ca3f0fa4a68dd6b3] section 2C (causing serious injury by careless, or inconsiderate, driving),
- (c) section 3 [^c-8bf1684941cf6638e0056edde04c0776]... (careless, and inconsiderate, driving),
- (ca)[^c-428cf1855d35bfacc5469a8d4ded0445] section 3ZB (causing death by driving: unlicensed[^c-6df3b271b0d3b716aae7f593f6b5f9bc]... or uninsured drivers),
- (cb)[^c-4b5d43e1643076c47d59a46838322e8e] section 3ZC (causing death by driving: disqualified drivers),
- (cc) section 3ZD (causing serious injury by driving: disqualified drivers),
- (d) section 3A [^c-d40f3ae41fbb9bc32933a2a493045006]... (causing death by careless driving when under influence of drink or drugs),
- (e) section 4 [^c-0d480fda456faf2649a50b1b1ec6a231]... (driving, or being in charge, when under influence of drink or drugs),
- (f) section 5 [^c-2acc6da81e902a99e30196246e0f8a0b]... (driving, or being in charge, of a motor vehicle with alcohol concentration above prescribed limit),
- (fa)[^c-bde18795495dc3f68d4f9a4fe20b2093] section 5A (driving, or being in charge, of a motor vehicle with concentration of specified controlled drug above specified limit),
- (g) section 6 [^c-8376523826f3c84dd65baea46fa22673]... (failing to provide a specimen of breath for a breath test),
- (h) section 7 [^c-7c6ad450bda96e81201789836a491550]... (failing to provide specimen for analysis or laboratory test).

**4.**[^c-dd28a441e75a2bf52e4f33a98dcd6756] An offence under section 12 of the Road Traffic Act 1988 (motor racing and speed trials on public ways).

**5.**[^c-a800bd63edd33d991db8d8e35384884b] An offence under section 103(1)(b) of the Road Traffic Act 1988 [^c-f31f9fa402b469bb22240751511b3904]... (driving while disqualified).

**6.**[^c-d7c75cdb834a2bbbe95bafbf4936da47] An offence under section 170(4) of the Road Traffic Act 1988 [^c-8a470f4db225f75d2aaf028f447189c5]... (failing to stop after accident and give particulars or report of accident).

### Part 2 — Offences where order of disqualification for minimum period necessary

**7.**[^c-45dac332203b20622ea72d2e215d74c7] An offence which—

- (a) is mentioned in Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988 (c. 53) [^c-873c11b18ac8957a7538958411cd9e7e]... , but
- (b) is not an offence mentioned in Part 1 of this Schedule.
