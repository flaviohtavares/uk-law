# Finance Act 2003, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. No prosecution after demand notice for penalty under section 26[^key-2d59feafd27a4148ee3edd5fb73dd5d3][^key-a59d45db876ca5682f6d477df2aff6d9][^key-2aa33b44296caac0f00136612182ac47][^key-6d3fb379168fa318b8e49c16fb890824]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-1590f315d306fb8fa353b1e753be4afb] Where a demand notice is given demanding payment of an amount due by way of penalty under section 26 in respect of any conduct of a person, no proceedings may be brought against that person for any offence constituted by that conduct (whether or not the demand notice is subsequently withdrawn).

(2)[^key-27a6f8462925444278b46a0e390d967e] Nothing in subsection (1) prevents the bringing of proceedings against a person for an offence under section 20A(1A), 22A(1A) or 25A(1A) of the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979 in circumstances where it is alleged that the person is liable to a penalty of an enhanced amount.
