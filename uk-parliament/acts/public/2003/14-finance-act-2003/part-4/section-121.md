## 121. Minor definitions[^c5522411]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- [^key-9270be84f811c82d18451b97586146c1]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^key-078c0a608d68d09c949bf39d10da4b9e]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**employee**” includes an office-holder and related expressions have a corresponding meaning;
- “[^key-f6fdb8eab223527eafa81ccd13125773]**HMRC**” means Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs;
- “**jointly entitled**” means—
  - (a) in England [^key-a78a6d4d258b4bccfdcd5f831e9742e6]... , beneficially entitled as joint tenants or tenants in common,
  - (b) [^key-03e6490d0ef76e43564720e0ce5269e9]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (c) in Northern Ireland, beneficially entitled as joint tenants, tenants in common or coparceners;
- “**land**” includes—
  - (a) buildings and structures, and
  - (b) land covered by water;
- [^key-848914055e21fa4327bab40c0fdbb799]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^key-60db8320a921d177699220a019320f5f]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**statutory provision**” means any provision made by or under an Act of Parliament, an Act of the Scottish Parliament or any Northern Ireland legislation;
- [^key-4fbd7caca2276e1170aecb854f34a4c4]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**tax**”, unless the context otherwise requires, means tax under this Part.
- “[^key-fb933ef778f3fa869cee90f597560e72]**tribunal**” means the First-tier Tribunal or, where determined by or under Tribunal Procedure Rules, the Upper Tribunal.
