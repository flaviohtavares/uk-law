# Finance Act 2003, section 118

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 118. Meaning of “market value”[^c5522381]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-b8482ef7f99653dd984df08d6d60e545] For the purposes of this Part “**market value**” shall be determined as for the purposes of the Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992 (c. 12) (see sections 272 to 274 of that Act).

(2)[^key-87c5ff3dd1888455936edb45fb29918f] This is subject to paragraphs 7(7) and 17(7) of Schedule 7A (which define “market value” for certain purposes of PAIF seeding relief and co-ownership scheme[^key-7950f434fc01e27f0de54741460a25d4] seeding relief).
