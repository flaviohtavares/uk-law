# Finance Act 2003, section 107

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/14

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 107. Crown application[^c5522271]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-974d8e847a7babb029951a026251a344] This Part binds the Crown, subject to the following provisions of this section.

(2) A land transaction under which the purchaser is any of the following is exempt from charge:

<table>
<tbody>
<tr><td><table><tbody><tr><th><em>Government</em></th></tr><tr><td>A Minister of the Crown</td></tr><tr><td>The Scottish Ministers</td></tr><tr><td>A Northern Ireland department</td></tr><tr><td>The Welsh Ministers, the First Minister for Wales and the Counsel General to the Welsh Government</td></tr></tbody></table></td></tr>
<tr><td><table><tbody><tr><th><em>Parliament etc</em></th></tr><tr><td>The Corporate Officer of the House of Lords</td></tr><tr><td>The Corporate Officer of the House of Commons</td></tr><tr><td>The Scottish Parliamentary Corporate Body</td></tr><tr><td>The Northern Ireland Assembly Commission</td></tr><tr><td>The National Assembly for Wales Commission</td></tr></tbody></table></td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

[^c-1ff03881e866d88be5c3b2c6fe6c9a47][^c-b464a8943e3444be33acb702c265f329]

(3) The powers conferred by Part 7 of Schedule 13 (entry with warrant to obtain information) are not exercisable in relation to premises occupied for the purposes of the Crown.

(4)[^c-24997faa2a4cefa931da96170ec120df] Nothing in this section shall be read as making the Crown liable to prosecution for an offence.
