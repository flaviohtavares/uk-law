# Finance Act 2003, section 77

## 77. Notifiable transactions[^key-839c1f52271025a15983ac691425d035]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A land transaction is notifiable if it is—

- (a) an acquisition of a major interest in land that does not fall within one or more of the exceptions in section 77A,
- (b) an acquisition of a chargeable interest other than a major interest in land where there is chargeable consideration in respect of any part of which tax is chargeable at a rate of more than 0%[^key-25e03939392e75cbf1b3ff2a730b08d6] or would be so chargeable but for a relief,
- (c) a land transaction that a person is treated as entering into by virtue of section 44A(3), [^key-07add5127be4fb99bafd0f7f7acc24cd]...
- (d) a notional land transaction under section 75A or[^key-161228a4379311c88cab532a7f5444dc]
- (e) a notional or additional land transaction under paragraph 5 of Schedule 2A.

(2) This section has effect subject to—

- (a) sections 71A(7) [^key-e73c61cbe054304b9e69db75cc382a93]..., and
- (b) paragraph 30 of Schedule 15.

(3) In this section “**relief**” does not include an exemption from charge under Schedule 3.
