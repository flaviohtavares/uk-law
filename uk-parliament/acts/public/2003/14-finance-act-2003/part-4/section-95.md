## 95. Offence of fraudulent evasion of tax[^c5522151]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person commits an offence if he is knowingly concerned in the fraudulent evasion of tax by him or any other person.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 14[^key-8341575b9e1a8ad4614b64e5411e605d] years or a fine, or both.
