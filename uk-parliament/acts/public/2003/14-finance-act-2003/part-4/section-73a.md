## 73A. Sections 71A to 73: relationship with Schedule 7[^key-6b7a0d6e47ac838897180fd9ce1f1897][^key-f7af00443a0143c8543428f345a47c32]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Sections 71A to 73 do not apply to arrangements in which the first transaction is exempt from charge by virtue of Schedule 7.
