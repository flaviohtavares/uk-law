- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] in England and Wales or Northern Ireland, has been cautioned in respect of such an offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] The offences are— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] an offence within paragraph 93 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] or 93A[^c18948792] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] of that Schedule, if— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
  - (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] the corresponding civil offence is an offence within any of paragraphs 13 to 15 of that Schedule; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] the corresponding civil offence is an offence within paragraph 31 of that Schedule, and the intended offence was an offence against a person *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] under 18 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
  - (iii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] the corresponding civil offence is an offence within any of paragraphs 1 to 12, 16 to 30 and 32 to 35 of that Schedule, and the victim of the offence was *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] under 18 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] at the time of the offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] an offence within any other paragraph of that Schedule, if the victim of the offence was *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] under 18 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] at the time of the offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

(2A (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c18948812][^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] In subsection (2)(c) references to the corresponding civil offence are to be read, in relation to an offence within paragraph 93A of Schedule 3, as references to the corresponding offence under the law of England and Wales. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] such a court has made in respect of a relevant offence a finding equivalent to a finding that he is under a disability and did the act charged against him in respect of the offence, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] he has been cautioned in respect of a relevant offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] In subsection (3), “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]**relevant offence *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]**” means an act which— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] An act punishable under the law in force in a country outside the United Kingdom constitutes an offence under that law for the purposes of subsection (4), however it is described in that law. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] showing his grounds for that opinion, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
