## 88. Section 87: interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsections (2) to (4) *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-52a606e09d0f6849bee639c2387cf2c5] Subsections (2) and (2A) *(Extent: S)* apply for the purposes of section 87.

(2) “**Photograph**” includes any process by means of which an image may be produced.

(2A)[^c18950071] “**Relevant physical data**” has the meaning given by section 18(7A) of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995.

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-43c0c9de804e7a69a6496b5a91719bf3] “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-43c0c9de804e7a69a6496b5a91719bf3]**Local police area *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-43c0c9de804e7a69a6496b5a91719bf3]**” means, in relation to a person— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-43c0c9de804e7a69a6496b5a91719bf3]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-43c0c9de804e7a69a6496b5a91719bf3] the police area in which his home address is situated; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-43c0c9de804e7a69a6496b5a91719bf3]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-43c0c9de804e7a69a6496b5a91719bf3] in the absence of a home address, the police area in which the home address last notified is situated; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-43c0c9de804e7a69a6496b5a91719bf3]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-43c0c9de804e7a69a6496b5a91719bf3] in the absence of a home address and of any such notification, the police area in which the court which last dealt with the person in a way mentioned in subsection (4) is situated. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-43c0c9de804e7a69a6496b5a91719bf3]

(4) The ways are— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42] dealing with a person in respect of an offence listed in Schedule 3 or a finding in relation to such an offence; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42] dealing with a person in respect of an offence under section 128 or a finding in relation to such an offence; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42] making, in respect of a person, a notification order, interim notification order, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42] sexual harm prevention order, interim sexual harm prevention order,[^c-f4c83d129a307812243198e75eb3382a] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42] sexual offences prevention order or interim sexual offences prevention order *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42] , or an order under Chapter 2 of Part 11 of the Sentencing Code (sexual harm prevention orders on conviction)[^c-d38caeb002fc1a80a64f9df466a156d4] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42]; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42]
- (d (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42] making, in respect of a person, an order under section 2, 2A or 20 of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998 (c. 37) (sex offender orders and interim orders made in England and Wales or Scotland) or Article 6 or 6A of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 ( *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42]S.I. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42] 1998/2839 (N.I. 20)) (sex offender orders and interim orders made in Northern Ireland); *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42]

and in paragraphs (a) and (b), “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42]**finding *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42]**” in relation to an offence means a finding of not guilty of the offence by reason of insanity or a finding that the person was under a disability and did the act or omission charged against him in respect of the offence. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42]

(5) Subsection (3) applies as if Northern Ireland were a police area. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7dbf0a04059856755d45e8225da35f42]
