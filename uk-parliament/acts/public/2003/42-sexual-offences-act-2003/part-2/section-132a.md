> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (154).** Text as at 2026-09-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/42

## 132A. Disapplication of time limit for complaints[^c20931691]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Section 127 of the Magistrates' Courts Act 1980 (time limits) does not apply to a complaint under any provision of this Part.
