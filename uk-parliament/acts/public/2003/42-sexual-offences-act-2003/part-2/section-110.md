## 110 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Appeals in relation to SOPOs and interim SOPOs: Northern Ireland (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_81ead728-d537-4d61-8f8d-def4b771156b][^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690][^c-b126c6c6d2bc3809d43e6b360550c50f][^c-12f011d2c77e11f02549c20ede30da60][^c-df99c325224174918305e50de88d7c70][^c-336977800e2b65a4def4215e9e99c61b][^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] A defendant may appeal against the making of a sexual offences prevention order— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] where section 104(2) applied to him, as if the order were a sentence passed on him for the offence; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] where section 104(3) (but not section 104(2)) applied to him, as if he had been convicted of the offence and the order were a sentence passed on him for that offence; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] where the order was made on an application under section 104(5), to *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] a county court[^c-1fe2f67d050c41dbd961cc7848744ac6] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] A defendant may appeal to *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] a county court[^c-61463dd9c73483555d5c52e7baf9db85] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] against the making of an interim sexual offences prevention order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] A defendant may appeal against the making of an order under section 108, or the refusal to make such an order— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] where the application for such an order was made to the Crown Court, to the Court of Appeal; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] in any other case, to *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] a county court[^c-ca415d49ca2f5150b0ea9bdee47dae83] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] On an appeal under subsection (1)(c), (2) or (3)(b), *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] the county court[^c-a2355efc6990f656f729dc1b00a40c91] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] may make such orders as may be necessary to give effect to its determination of the appeal, and may also make such incidental or consequential orders as appear to it to be just. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-f090b015de570df263ed7fe076146e4d][^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] Any order made by a county court on an appeal under subsection (1)(c) or (2) (other than an order directing that an application be re-heard by a court of summary jurisdiction) is for the purposes of section 108(7) or 109(6) (respectively) to be treated as if it were an order of the court from which the appeal was brought (and not an order of the county court). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
