# Sexual Offences Act 2003, section 122J

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (154).** Text as at 2026-09-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/42

## 122J. Sexual risk orders and interim sexual risk orders: guidance[^key-c92bef4baad8882688e94afe22e1faca]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State must issue guidance to chief officers of police, the Director General of the National Crime Agency, the chief constable of the British Transport Police Force and the chief constable of the Ministry of Defence Police[^key-a2f1fc462511ac414d315a04ccf2fedd] in relation to the exercise by them of their powers with regard to sexual risk orders and interim sexual risk orders.

(2) The Secretary of State may, from time to time, revise the guidance issued under subsection (1).

(3) The Secretary of State must arrange for any guidance issued or revised under this section to be published in such manner as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.
