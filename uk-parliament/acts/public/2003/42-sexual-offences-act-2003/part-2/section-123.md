## 123 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Risk of sexual harm orders: applications, grounds and effect (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c][^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] The Chief Constable of the Police Service of Northern Ireland[^key-c1cb5729c775278567f2540446b489e6] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] may by complaint to *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] a court of summary jurisdiction[^key-3e6156ad900a2dcc7d68fdab7c3be27c] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] apply for an order under this section (a “risk of sexual harm order”) in respect of a person aged 18 or over (“ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]**the defendant *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]**”) who resides in *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] Northern Ireland[^key-f508b21defe30d1d99b632381d7d1b37] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] or who *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] the Chief Constable[^key-b5c407892c04f4bb6059ab78cdc72161] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] believes is in, or is intending to come to, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] Northern Ireland *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] if it appears to *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] the Chief Constable *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] that— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] the defendant has on at least two occasions, whether before or after the commencement of this Part, done an act within subsection (3), and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] as a result of those acts, there is reasonable cause to believe that it is necessary for such an order to be made. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-91d0d5ee2902a797ef1ca05dbf09842d][^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] The acts are— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] engaging in sexual activity involving a child or in the presence of a child; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] causing or inciting a child to watch a person engaging in sexual activity or to look at a moving or still image that is sexual; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] giving a child anything that relates to sexual activity or contains a reference to such activity; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] communicating with a child, where any part of the communication is sexual. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] On the application, the court may make a risk of sexual harm order if it is satisfied that— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] the defendant has on at least two occasions, whether before or after the commencement of this section, done an act within subsection (3); and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] it is necessary to make such an order, for the purpose of protecting children generally or any child from harm from the defendant. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] Such an order— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] prohibits the defendant from doing anything described in the order; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] has effect for a fixed period (not less than 2 years) specified in the order or until further order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] The only prohibitions that may be imposed are those necessary for the purpose of protecting children generally or any child from harm from the defendant. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] Where a court makes a risk of sexual harm order in relation to a person already subject to such an order (whether made by that court or another), the earlier order ceases to have effect. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]
