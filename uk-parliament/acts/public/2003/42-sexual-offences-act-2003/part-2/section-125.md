## 125 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). RSHOs: variations, renewals and discharges (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c][^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] A person within subsection (2) may by complaint to *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] the appropriate court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6160e4405a83ab47d4370d0ea9c11a46] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] a court of summary jurisdiction *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] apply for an order varying, renewing or discharging a risk of sexual harm order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] The persons are— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] the defendant; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d05a01da5d94062e0bf3a8602fc456d5][^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] the Chief Constable of the Police Service of Northern Ireland. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] Subject to subsections (4) and (5), on the application the court, after hearing the person making the application *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c], and the other person mentioned in subsection (2) (if that person wishes to be heard)[^c-56aa2a61927c1a427761ebac0b3e729f] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c], may make any order, varying, renewing or discharging the risk of sexual harm order, that the court considers appropriate. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] An order may be renewed, or varied so as to impose additional prohibitions on the defendant, only if it is necessary to do so for the purpose of protecting children generally or any child from harm from the defendant (and any renewed or varied order may contain only such prohibitions as are necessary for this purpose). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] The court must not discharge an order before the end of 2 years beginning with the day on which the order was made, without the consent of the defendant and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] the Chief Constable of the Police Service of Northern Ireland[^c-c59764f41c2b9764f5713219ec798e6b] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] Section 124(2) applies for the purposes of this section. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

(7 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5daba32bac4e3768dd04341059d961e6][^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5daba32bac4e3768dd04341059d961e6] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]**the appropriate court *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5daba32bac4e3768dd04341059d961e6] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5daba32bac4e3768dd04341059d961e6] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] the court which made the risk of sexual harm order; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5daba32bac4e3768dd04341059d961e6] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-18ee65fcc9c3928216d9fd83de3f6c58][^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] a court of summary jurisdiction for the petty sessions district which includes the area where the defendant resides; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5daba32bac4e3768dd04341059d961e6] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c] where the application is made by the Chief Constable of the Police Service of Northern Ireland, any court of summary jurisdiction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5daba32bac4e3768dd04341059d961e6] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-30898360abe68d9ccbf000961635935c]
