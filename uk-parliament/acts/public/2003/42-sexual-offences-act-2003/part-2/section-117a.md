## 117A (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Surrender of passports: Northern Ireland (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c20931541][^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690][^key-4ede35ee0d2d8d4933ccc905cee46162][^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] This section applies in relation to a foreign travel order which contains a prohibition within section 117(2)(c). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] The order must require the defendant to surrender all of the defendant's passports, at a police station *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] in Northern Ireland[^key-15e0c4b43add98f6685d0e764a74e4c8] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] specified in the order— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] on or before the date when the prohibition takes effect, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] within a period specified in the order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] Any passports surrendered must be returned as soon as reasonably practicable after the person ceases to be subject to a foreign travel order containing a prohibition within section 117(2)(c) *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] (unless the person is subject to an equivalent prohibition under another order)[^key-4e881e1a57beab486595a4c08485bf7d] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] Subsection (3) does not apply in relation to— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] a passport issued by or on behalf of the authorities of a country outside the United Kingdom if the passport has been returned to those authorities; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] a passport issued by or on behalf of an international organisation if the passport has been returned to that organisation. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] In this section “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]**passport *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]**” means— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] a United Kingdom passport within the meaning of the Immigration Act 1971; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] a passport issued by or on behalf of the authorities of a country outside the United Kingdom, or by or on behalf of an international organisation; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] a document that can be used (in some or all circumstances) instead of a passport. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
