## 119 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Appeals in relation to foreign travel orders: Northern Ireland (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690][^c-e1591889261fbcd9bc87a7efb2396028][^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] A defendant may appeal to *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] a county court[^c-2bf7f2ca1d54fe8bb76333cd2292cf47] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] against the making of a foreign travel order; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] against the making of an order under section 118, or the refusal to make such an order. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] On any such appeal, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] the county court[^c-92f18afd5d13dd79b26b64e23588159c] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] may make such orders as may be necessary to give effect to its determination of the appeal, and may also make such incidental or consequential orders as appear to it to be just. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9c507d89ae3e9aafc1b4fd1818e1f5cc][^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690] Any order made by a county court on an appeal under subsection (1)(a) (other than an order directing that an application be re-heard by a court of summary jurisdiction) is for the purposes of section 118(5) to be treated as if it were an order of the court from which the appeal was brought (and not an order of the county court). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8216d299001d1851e1b7db26cb0e3690]
