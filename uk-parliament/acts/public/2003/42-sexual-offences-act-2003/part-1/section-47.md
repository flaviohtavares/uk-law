## 47. Paying for sexual services of a child

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c20916511] A person (A) commits an offence if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]

- (a) he intentionally obtains for himself the sexual services of another person (B), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]
- (b) before obtaining those services, he has made or promised payment for those services to B or a third person, or knows that another person has made or promised such a payment, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]
- (c) either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]
  - (i) B is under 18, and A does not reasonably believe that B is 18 or over, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20916511] B is under 13. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]

(2) In this section, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]**payment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]**” means any financial advantage, including the discharge of an obligation to pay or the provision of goods or services (including sexual services) gratuitously or at a discount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]

(3) A person guilty of an offence under this section against a person under 13, where subsection (6) applies, is liable on conviction on indictment to imprisonment for life. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]

(4) Unless subsection (3) applies, a person guilty of an offence under this section against a person under 16 is liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]

- (a) where subsection (6) applies, on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 14 years; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]
- (b) in any other case— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]
  - (i) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20916511] on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 14 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]

(5) Unless subsection (3) or (4) applies, a person guilty of an offence under this section is liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 7 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]

(6) This subsection applies where the offence involved— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]

- (a) penetration of B’s anus or vagina with a part of A’s body or anything else, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]
- (b) penetration of B’s mouth with A’s penis, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]
- (c) penetration of A’s anus or vagina with a part of B’s body or by B with anything else, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]
- (d) penetration of A’s mouth with B’s penis. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916511]
