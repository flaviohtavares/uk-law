## 24. Sections 16 to 19: sexual relationships which pre-date position of trust

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c20916501] Conduct by a person (A) which would otherwise be an offence under any of sections 16 to 19 against another person (B) is not an offence under that section if, immediately before the position of trust arose, a sexual relationship existed between A and B. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916501]

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply if at that time sexual intercourse between A and B would have been unlawful. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916501]

(3) In proceedings for an offence under any of sections 16 to 19 it is for the defendant to prove that such a relationship existed at that time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916501]
