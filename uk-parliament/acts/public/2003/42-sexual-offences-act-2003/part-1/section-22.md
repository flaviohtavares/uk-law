## 22. Positions of trust: interpretation

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c20916481] The following provisions apply for the purposes of section 21. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]

(2) Subject to subsection (3), a person looks after persons under 18 if he is regularly involved in caring for, training, supervising or being in sole charge of such persons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]

(3) A person (A) looks after another person (B) on an individual basis if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]

- (a) A is regularly involved in caring for, training or supervising B, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]
- (b) in the course of his involvement, A regularly has unsupervised contact with B (whether face to face or by any other means). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]

(4) A person receives education at an educational institution if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]

- (a) he is registered or otherwise enrolled as a pupil or student at the institution, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]
- (b) he receives education at the institution under arrangements with another educational institution at which he is so registered or otherwise enrolled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]

(6)[^c20943781] The services referred to in paragraph (b)(i) of the definition of “hospital” are as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]

- (a) medical treatment under anaesthesia or intravenously administered sedation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]
- (b) dental treatment under general anaesthesia; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]
- (c) obstetric services and, in connection with childbirth, medical services; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]
- (d) termination of pregnancies; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]
- (e) cosmetic surgery, other than— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]

  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20916481] tattooing; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20916481] the subcutaneous injection of a substance or substances into the skin for cosmetic purposes; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20916481] the removal of hair roots or small blemishes on the skin by the application of heat using an electric current. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916481]
