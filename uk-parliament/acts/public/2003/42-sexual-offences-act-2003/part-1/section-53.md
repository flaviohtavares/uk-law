## 53. Controlling prostitution for gain

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c20916571] A person commits an offence if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916571]

- (a) he intentionally controls any of the activities of another person relating to that person’s prostitution in any part of the world, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916571]
- (b) he does so for or in the expectation of gain for himself or a third person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916571]

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916571]

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916571]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 7 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916571]
