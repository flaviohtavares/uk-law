## 48. Causing or inciting sexual exploitation of a child[^key-6a934ad897976d1b65b368206a5bd979]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c20916521] A person (A) commits an offence if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916521]

- (a) he intentionally causes or incites another person (B) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916521] to be sexually exploited[^key-a9c2b28b8602ef648227a0d58498aabf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916521] in any part of the world, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916521]
- (b) either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916521]
  - (i) B is under 18, and A does not reasonably believe that B is 18 or over, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916521]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20916521] B is under 13. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916521]

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916521]

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916521]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 14 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916521]
