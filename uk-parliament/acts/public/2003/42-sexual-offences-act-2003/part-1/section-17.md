# Sexual Offences Act 2003, section 17

## 17. Abuse of position of trust: causing or inciting a child to engage in sexual activity

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c20916431] A person aged 18 or over (A) commits an offence if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]

- (a) he intentionally causes or incites another person (B) to engage in an activity, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]
- (b) the activity is sexual, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]
- (c) A is in a position of trust in relation to B, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]
- (d) where subsection (2) applies, A knows or could reasonably be expected to know of the circumstances by virtue of which he is in a position of trust in relation to B, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]
- (e) either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]
  - (i) B is under 18 and A does not reasonably believe that B is 18 or over, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20916431] B is under 13. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]

(2) This subsection applies where A— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]

- (a) is in a position of trust in relation to B by virtue of circumstances within section 21(2), (3), (4) or (5), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]
- (b) is not in such a position of trust by virtue of other circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]

(3) Where in proceedings for an offence under this section it is proved that the other person was under 18, the defendant is to be taken not to have reasonably believed that that person was 18 or over unless sufficient evidence is adduced to raise an issue as to whether he reasonably believed it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]

(4) Where in proceedings for an offence under this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]

- (a) it is proved that the defendant was in a position of trust in relation to the other person by virtue of circumstances within section 21(2), (3), (4) or (5), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]
- (b) it is not proved that he was in such a position of trust by virtue of other circumstances, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]

it is to be taken that the defendant knew or could reasonably have been expected to know of the circumstances by virtue of which he was in such a position of trust unless sufficient evidence is adduced to raise an issue as to whether he knew or could reasonably have been expected to know of those circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]

(5) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 5 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916431]
