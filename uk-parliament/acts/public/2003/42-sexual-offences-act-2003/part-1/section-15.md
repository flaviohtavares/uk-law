## 15. Meeting a child following sexual grooming etc.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c20916411] A person aged 18 or over (A) commits an offence if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]

- (a)[^c20910181] A has met or communicated with another person (B) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411] on one or more occasions[^c-1a115d8ebd761554d1173e5cdb15eace] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411] and subsequently— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]
  - (i) A intentionally meets B, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20916411] A travels with the intention of meeting B in any part of the world or arranges to meet B in any part of the world, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20916411] B travels with the intention of meeting A in any part of the world, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]
- (b) A intends to do anything to or in respect of B, during or after the meeting mentioned in paragraph (a)(i) to (iii) and in any part of the world, which if done will involve the commission by A of a relevant offence, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]
- (c) B is under 16, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]
- (d) A does not reasonably believe that B is 16 or over. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]

(2) In subsection (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]

- (a) the reference to A having met or communicated with B is a reference to A having met B in any part of the world or having communicated with B by any means from, to or in any part of the world; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]
- (b) “**relevant offence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]
  - (i) an offence under this Part, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20916411] [^c20927741]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20916411] anything done outside England and Wales *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411] [^c20927761]. . . which is not an offence within sub-paragraph (i) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411] [^c20927761]. . . but would be an offence within sub-paragraph (i) if done in England and Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]

(3) [^c20927791]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 10 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916411]
