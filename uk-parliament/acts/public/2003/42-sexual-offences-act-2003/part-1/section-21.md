## 21. Positions of trust

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c20916471] For the purposes of sections 16 to 19, a person (A) is in a position of trust in relation to another person (B) if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

- (a) any of the following subsections applies, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]
- (b) any condition specified in an order made by the Secretary of State is met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

(2) This subsection applies if A looks after persons under 18 who are detained in an institution by virtue of a court order or under an enactment, and B is so detained in that institution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

(3) This subsection applies if A looks after persons under 18 who are resident in a home or other place in which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

- (a) accommodation and maintenance are provided by an authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471] in accordance with section 22C(6)[^c20948421] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471] of the Children Act 1989 (c. 41) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471] or section 81(6) of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014[^key-444d42a662e78f5b37e3512f85a4aef8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471] [^c20927861]..., or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]
- (b) accommodation is provided by a voluntary organisation under section 59(1) of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471] the Children Act 1989[^key-1d5f8ac91ebfd05068756121faa441f9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471] [^c20927881]...,

and B is resident, and is so provided with accommodation and maintenance or accommodation, in that place. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

(4) This subsection applies if A looks after persons under 18 who are accommodated and cared for in one of the following institutions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

- (a) a hospital, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]
- (b) in Wales,[^c20943721] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471] an independent clinic, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]
- (c) a care home, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471] [^c20927901]...
- (d) a community home, voluntary home or children’s home, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471] or[^c20927921] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]
- (e) a home provided under section 82(5) of the Children Act 1989, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471] [^c20928891]...
- (f)[^c20928911] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g)[^M_F_12815eaf-026c-47ff-96dc-7548283664d7] a place in Wales at which a care home service is provided, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]
- (h)[^key-6aa755d9161e9b1c449afc5d839bf16d] premises in Wales at which a secure accommodation service is provided, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

and B is accommodated and cared for in that institution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

(5) This subsection applies if A looks after persons under 18 who are receiving education at an educational institution and B is receiving, and A is not receiving, education at that institution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

(6)[^c20928931] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) This subsection applies if A is engaged in the provision of services under, or pursuant to anything done under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

- (a) sections 8 to 10 of the Employment and Training Act 1973 (c. 50), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]
- (b)[^c20916121] section 68, 70(1)(b) or 74 of the Education and Skills Act 2008, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

and, in that capacity, looks after B on an individual basis. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

(8) This subsection applies if A regularly has unsupervised contact with B (whether face to face or by any other means)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

- (a) in the exercise of functions of a local authority under section 20 or 21 of the Children Act 1989 (c. 41) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471] or section 76 or 77 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014[^key-256f24d091883e7c407e7ad08b3f9ce2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471], [^c20929331]...
- (b)[^c20929331] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9) This subsection applies if A, as a person who is to report to the court under section 7 of the Children Act 1989 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471] [^c20929361]... on matters relating to the welfare of B, regularly has unsupervised contact with B (whether face to face or by any other means). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

(10) This subsection applies if A is a personal adviser appointed for B under— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

- (a) section 23B(2) of, or paragraph 19C of Schedule 2 to, the Children Act 1989, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471] [^c20929441]... or[^key-c3cf1aa706b18202c0825e39e1b4d9d1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-aa75d990d7486b7c8d215e94925b1bba][^c20916471] section 106(1) of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 in respect of category 1 or 2 young persons within the meaning of that Act, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]
- (b)[^c20929441] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

and, in that capacity, looks after B on an individual basis. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

(11) This subsection applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

- (a) B is subject to a care order, a supervision order or an education supervision order, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]
- (b) in the exercise of functions conferred by virtue of the order on an authorised person or the authority designated by the order, A looks after B on an individual basis. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

(12) This subsection applies if A— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

- (a) is an officer of the Service *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471] or Welsh family proceedings officer (within the meaning given by section 35 of the Children Act 2004)[^c18943571] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471] appointed for B under section 41(1) of the Children Act 1989, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]
- (b) is appointed a children’s guardian of B under rule 6 or rule 18 of the Adoption Rules 1984 ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]S.I. 1984/265), [^key-fdad95c0c8aa7ba9ee32c61bd6aa7b06]...
- (c) is appointed to be the guardian ad litem of B under rule 9.5 of the Family Proceedings Rules 1991 (S. I. 1991/1247) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471] [^c20929451]..., or[^key-0efac97433d0ac5bfc78f477b0f1de92] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]
- (d)[^key-370eb8bb4086e0ed0ae55ab67ed920f2] is appointed to be the children’s guardian of B under rule 59 of the Family Procedure (Adoption) Rules 2005 ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471][S.I. 2005/2795](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/2795)) or rule 16.3(1)(ii) or rule 16.4 of the Family Procedure Rules 2010 ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471][S.I. 2010/2955](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/2955)),

and, in that capacity, regularly has unsupervised contact with B (whether face to face or by any other means). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

(13) This subsection applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]

- (a) B is subject to requirements imposed by or under an enactment on his release from detention for a criminal offence, or is subject to requirements imposed by a court order made in criminal proceedings, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]
- (b) A looks after B on an individual basis in pursuance of the requirements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916471]
