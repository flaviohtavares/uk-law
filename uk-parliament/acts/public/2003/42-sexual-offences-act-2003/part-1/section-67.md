# Sexual Offences Act 2003, section 67

## 67. Voyeurism

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c20916601] A person commits an offence if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916601]

- (a) for the purpose of obtaining sexual gratification, he observes another person doing a private act, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916601]
- (b) he knows that the other person does not consent to being observed for his sexual gratification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916601]

(2) A person commits an offence if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916601]

- (a) he operates equipment with the intention of enabling another person to observe, for the purpose of obtaining sexual gratification, a third person (B) doing a private act, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916601]
- (b) he knows that B does not consent to his operating equipment with that intention. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916601]

(3) A person commits an offence if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916601]

- (a) he records another person (B) doing a private act, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916601]
- (b) he does so with the intention that he or a third person will, for the purpose of obtaining sexual gratification, look at an image of B doing the act, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916601]
- (c) he knows that B does not consent to his recording the act with that intention. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916601]

(4) A person commits an offence if he instals equipment, or constructs or adapts a structure or part of a structure, with the intention of enabling himself or another person to commit an offence under subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916601]

(5) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916601]

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916601]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916601]
