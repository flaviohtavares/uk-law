# Sexual Offences Act 2003, section 23

## 23. Sections 16 to 19: exception for spouses and civil partners[^c18943901]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c20916491] Conduct by a person (A) which would otherwise be an offence under any of sections 16 to 19 against another person (B) is not an offence under that section if at the time — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916491]

- (a) B is 16 or over, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916491]
- (b) A and B are lawfully married *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916491] or civil partners of each other[^c18943851] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916491].

(2) In proceedings for such an offence it is for the defendant to prove that A and B *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916491] were at the time lawfully married or civil partners of each other[^c18943871] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916491].
