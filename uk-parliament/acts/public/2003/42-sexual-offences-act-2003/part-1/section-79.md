# Sexual Offences Act 2003, section 79

## 79. Part 1: general interpretation

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c20916671] The following apply for the purposes of this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]

(2) Penetration is a continuing act from entry to withdrawal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]

(3) References to a part of the body include references to a part surgically constructed (in particular, through gender reassignment surgery). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]

(4) “**Image *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]**” means a moving or still image and includes an image produced by any means and, where the context permits, a three-dimensional image. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]

(5) References to an image of a person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671] (except in sections 66E, 66F[^c-4d8bf845fa930d0069ec382d650bb3be] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671] , 66G and 66I[^c-2c4e569fcf3068b1011a72487b91b148] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]) include references to an image of an imaginary person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]

(6) “**Mental disorder *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]**” has the meaning given by section 1 of the Mental Health Act 1983 (c. 20). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]

(7) References to observation (however expressed) are to observation whether direct or by looking at an image. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]

(8) Touching includes touching— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]

- (a) with any part of the body, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]
- (b) with anything else, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]
- (c) through anything, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]

and in particular includes touching amounting to penetration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]

(9) “**Vagina *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]**” includes vulva. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916671]
