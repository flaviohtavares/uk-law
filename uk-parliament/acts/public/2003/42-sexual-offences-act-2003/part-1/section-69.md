# Sexual Offences Act 2003, section 69

## 69. Sexual activity with an animal (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-c05c555f94ccc0ef46b6e8df7cf1744c][^c20916621][^c20916621]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person commits an offence if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916621]

- (a) the person intentionally touches an animal (whether living or dead), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916621]
- (b) the person knows that, or is reckless as to whether, that is what is touched, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916621]
- (c) the touching is sexual. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916621]

(2) For the purposes of this section, touching is sexual if a reasonable person would consider that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916621]

- (a) because of its nature it may be sexual, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916621]
- (b) because of its circumstances or the purpose of any person in relation to it (or both) it is sexual. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916621]

(3) A person who commits an offence under this section is liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916621]

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court or a fine (or both); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916621]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916621]
