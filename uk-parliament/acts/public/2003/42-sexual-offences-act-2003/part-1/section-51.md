## 51. Sections 48 to 50: interpretation

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-610925f707daa1fd6ab7a79cb72d1a03][^c20916551] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-4352ce120970c9fbaa28727a03605c28] For the purposes of sections 48 to 50, a person (B) is sexually exploited if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916551]

- (a) on at least one occasion and whether or not compelled to do so, B offers or provides sexual services to another person in return for payment or a promise of payment to B or a third person, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916551]
- (b) an indecent image of B is recorded *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916551] or streamed or otherwise transmitted[^key-24cefaab5f4e3f1ea1d49afdff02deff] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916551] ;

and “sexual exploitation” is to be interpreted accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916551]

(3) In subsection (2), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916551]**payment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916551]**” means any financial advantage, including the discharge of an obligation to pay or the provision of goods or services (including sexual services) gratuitously or at a discount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916551]
