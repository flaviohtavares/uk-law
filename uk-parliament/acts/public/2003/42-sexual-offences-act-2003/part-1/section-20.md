## 20. Abuse of position of trust: acts done in Scotland

*Extent: E+W*

Anything which, if done in England and Wales *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916461] [^c20927811]. . . , would constitute an offence under any of sections 16 to 19 also constitutes that offence if done in Scotland *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916461] or Northern Ireland[^c20927831] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916461].
