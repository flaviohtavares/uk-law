# Sexual Offences Act 2003, section 78

## 78. “Sexual”

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of this Part ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916661]except sections 15A[^key-bf97c77c05e54c3c23cb7a2cbe42bc68] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916661] , 66B to 66D[^key-1342af945ca13797b63f4f4151b23a13] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916661] , 69[^key-47e382b5e21170ac2fba41a780a7f11b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916661] and 71 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916661] ), penetration, touching or any other activity is sexual if a reasonable person would consider that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916661]

- (a) whatever its circumstances or any person’s purpose in relation to it, it is because of its nature sexual, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916661]
- (b) because of its nature it may be sexual and because of its circumstances or the purpose of any person in relation to it (or both) it is sexual. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916661]
