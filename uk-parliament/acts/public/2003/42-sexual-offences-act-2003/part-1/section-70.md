# Sexual Offences Act 2003, section 70

## 70. Sexual activity with a corpse (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-7c2052227feb82f4d86e82eb793258e0][^c20916631][^c20916631]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person commits an offence if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916631]

- (a) the person intentionally performs an act of touching (with a part of their body or anything else), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916631]
- (b) what is touched is a part of the body of a dead person, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916631]
- (c) the person knows that, or is reckless as to whether, that is what is touched, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916631]
- (d) the touching is sexual. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916631]

(2) A person who commits an offence under this section is liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916631]

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court or a fine (or both); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916631]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916631]
  - (i) if the touching involved penetration of a part of the body mentioned in subsection (1)(b), 7 years; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916631]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20916631] otherwise, 5 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916631]
