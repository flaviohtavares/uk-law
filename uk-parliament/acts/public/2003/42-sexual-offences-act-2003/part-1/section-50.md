## 50. Arranging or facilitating sexual exploitation of a child[^c-6313185cfe1800f0e43359801fd954e6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c20916541] A person (A) commits an offence if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916541]

- (a) he intentionally arranges or facilitates the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916541] sexual exploitation[^c-14f017aceabcafaefb154059d14b82b9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916541] in any part of the world of another person (B), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916541]
- (b) either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916541]
  - (i) B is under 18, and A does not reasonably believe that B is 18 or over, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916541]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c20916541] B is under 13. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916541]

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916541]

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916541]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 14 years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916541]
