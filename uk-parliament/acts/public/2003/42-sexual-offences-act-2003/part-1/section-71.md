# Sexual Offences Act 2003, section 71

## 71. Sexual activity in a public lavatory

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c20916641] A person commits an offence if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916641]

- (a) he is in a lavatory to which the public or a section of the public has or is permitted to have access, whether on payment or otherwise, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916641]
- (b) he intentionally engages in an activity, and, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916641]
- (c) the activity is sexual. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916641]

(2) For the purposes of this section, an activity is sexual if a reasonable person would, in all the circumstances but regardless of any person’s purpose, consider it to be sexual. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916641]

(3) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale or both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20916641]
