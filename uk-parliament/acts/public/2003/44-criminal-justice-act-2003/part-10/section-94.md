# Criminal Justice Act 2003, section 94

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (151).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/44
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94. Armed Forces: Part 10[^c19384851]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c19211621] Section 323 of the Armed Forces Act 2006 (provision in consequence of criminal justice enactments) applies in relation to an enactment contained in this Part so far as relating to matters not specified in subsection (2) of section 324 of that Act as it applies in relation to a criminal justice enactment (within the meaning given by that section).

(2) The power under section 323 of that Act[^c19211641] to make provision equivalent to that made in relation to qualifying offences by an enactment contained in this Part (with or without modifications) includes power to make such provision in relation to such service offences as the Secretary of State thinks fit.

(3)[^c19211661] In subsection (2) “**service offence**” has the same meaning as in the Armed Forces Act 2006.
