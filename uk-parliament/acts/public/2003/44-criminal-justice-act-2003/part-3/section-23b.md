# Criminal Justice Act 2003, section 23B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (151).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/44

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23B. Variation of conditions[^c20226431]

*Extent: E+W*

A relevant prosecutor or an authorised person[^c-b5f001b7dcab4afb2a4eeae8e47ce576] may, with the consent of the offender, vary the conditions attached to a conditional caution by—

- (a) modifying or omitting any of the conditions;
- (b) adding a condition.
