# Criminal Justice Act 2003, section 27

## 27. Interpretation of Part 3[^c19080701]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “**authorised person**” has the meaning given by section 22(4),
- “**conditional caution**” has the meaning given by section 22(2),
- “**investigating officer**” means an officer of Revenue and Customs, appointed in accordance with section 2(1) of the Commissioners for Revenue and Customs Act 2005, or[^c19163741] a person designated as a policing support officer or a policing support volunteer[^c-bbb0ffa9652742b2bb0fee2327cb064c] under section 38 of the Police Reform Act 2002 (c. 30),
- “**the offender**” has the meaning given by section 22(1),
- “**relevant prosecutor**” means—
  - (a) the Attorney General,
  - (b) the Director of the Serious Fraud Office,
  - (ba) [^c-d56c0ccb2c9d5f6c8e784154a922e5ee]...
  - (c) the Director of Public Prosecutions,
  - (d) a Secretary of State,
  - (e) [^c19403491]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (f) [^c19403491]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (g) a person who is specified in an order made by the Secretary of State as being a relevant prosecutor for the purposes of this Part.
