## 328. Criminal record certificates: amendments of Part 5 of Police Act 1997[^c19403231][^c19079392]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Schedule 35 (which contains amendments of Part 5 of the Police Act 1997 (c. 50)) shall have effect.
