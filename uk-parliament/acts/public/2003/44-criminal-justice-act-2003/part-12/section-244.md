## 244. Duty to release prisoners not subject to special provision for release[^c19069591][^c-3ad5aa8d78ba17800a2fb76fa1b0d9df][^c-ad9c2bc53516bdc69645a8f71e932e46][^c-155c0f69a8116c840c0366b45e57112f][^c-1685a02ce653a5293667f4152e268d38][^c-5549d7a1efe3245b08b1a9eb9b263946]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) As soon as a fixed-term prisoner, other than a prisoner to whom section 243A[^c-785e8755354e67d4d21db68aea0c3a6b] ,[^c-b29c8aa7232ce8b7f447a76a7cd17e73] 244ZA,[^c-d0ac4416eb709c41b02c7345fd99331a] 244A , 246A[^c-811fdbfbb1b902792d695b5bebafaa5d] , 247 or 247A[^c-f5e5bc84d18f5b95edaefb41dcb9a335] applies, has served the requisite custodial period for the purposes of this section[^c-6fc4d437e876770c6945fbeb528f7e38], it is the duty of the Secretary of State to release him on licence under this section.

(1ZA)[^c-bb5930b372b88e1d1593ef6a857f9c0c] Subsection (1) does not apply if—

- (a) the prisoner’s case has been referred to the Board under section 244ZB, or
- (b) a notice given to the prisoner under subsection (4) of that section is in force.

(1A)[^c-6a96c491d6b059d68835a9d903afabe3] Subsection (1) does not apply if the prisoner has been released on licence under section 246 or 248[^c-ba9f1848a2cdb679a5344939269bc120] and recalled under section 254 (provision for the release of such persons being made by sections 255B to[^c-d5763e72d07cce8c9587da83223864de] 255C).

(2)[^c-f2f67525cf3cf1941d2079081f56a8ca] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) For the purposes of this section[^c-7a7198ea4ff09df77281ba1b19aa6753] “**the requisite custodial period**” means—

- (a)[^c-81e6f6a5539116d9589a33b179a4f43e][^c-d9322f59ecef6c9aa06717203aad076b] in relation to a prisoner serving one sentence[^c-ff736edc116a0b2fd59600cefe3508cc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9322f59ecef6c9aa06717203aad076b], one-half of his sentence, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9322f59ecef6c9aa06717203aad076b]
- (a) in relation to a prisoner serving one sentence imposed under section 91 of the PCC(S)A 2000 or section 250 of the Sentencing Code, one-half of the sentence,
- (aa) in relation to a prisoner serving one sentence of any other kind, one-third of the sentence,
- (b)[^c-7714608378105e7619e5e3a1c9a511af] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-7714608378105e7619e5e3a1c9a511af] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) in relation to a person serving two or more concurrent or consecutive sentences [^c-4cb1ce16ce770473f5415bae2ce1ea4d]... , the period determined under sections 263(2) and 264(2B) or (2E)[^c-a7ee1a0955dc70d05578f0f21f77d183].

(4)[^c-8ddf08fc55ad979a2ce0dcb2ad335d94] This section is subject to paragraphs 5, 6, 8, 25 and 28 of Schedule 20B (transitional cases).
