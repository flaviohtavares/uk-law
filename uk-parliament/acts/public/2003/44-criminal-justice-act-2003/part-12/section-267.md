# Criminal Justice Act 2003, section 267

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (151).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/44
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 267. Alteration by order of relevant proportion of sentence

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State may by order provide that any reference in section 243A(3)(a),[^c-0eb2324764cb63fefed91be9d33334f5] section 244(3)(a), section 247(2) or section 264(6)(d)[^c-26136682c349d399b494d7d23c0a8e0a] to a particular proportion of a prisoner’s sentence is to be read as a reference to such other proportion of a prisoner’s sentence as may be specified in the order.
