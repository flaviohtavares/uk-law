# Criminal Justice Act 2003, section 264B

## 264B. Consecutive terms: supplementary[^M_C_df5e507d-911d-4c12-f668-64d6220e0ae3][^M_C_c2911633-81ba-4836-9e2b-b763d432a36b][^M_C_3d54cede-59f3-45cf-aea2-88a29387600a][^key-e85bf666aed00e84ad9f4fb18ba9b518][^key-7366982145a790f12baf2e9cc1c1df44]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies in a case in which section 264 applies where—

- (a) the offender is released on licence under this Chapter,
- (b) the aggregate length of the terms of imprisonment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6a721a17474451cbcb64bb60077b2da0] mentioned in section 264(1)(a) is less than 12 months, and
- (c) those terms include one or more terms of imprisonment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-aced9d54b5afbca3f757eba437d8ec07] (“short transitional terms”) which were imposed under section 250 of the Sentencing Code[^key-01646cca71eb7dda9e3aae3df08413c2] in respect of an offence committed before the day on which section 1 of the Offender Rehabilitation Act 2014 came into force, as well as one or more terms imposed (whether or not under section 250 of the Code)[^key-2a9a5fc5ed3b11b44843eea84ec4fc07] in respect of an offence committed on or after that day.

(2) The offender is to be on licence until the offender would, but for the release, have served a term equal in length to the aggregate of—

- (a) the minimum[^key-c44559f15a7851acc668e8ecde965086] custodial period in relation to each of the short transitional terms, and
- (b) the full length of each of the other terms.

(3) In this section “**custodial period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4c79f5b915f909ff7ed9fea51c88a838] minimum custodial period**” has the same meaning as in section 264.
