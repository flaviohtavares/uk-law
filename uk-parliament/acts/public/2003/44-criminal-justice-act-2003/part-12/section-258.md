# Criminal Justice Act 2003, section 258

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 258. Early release of fine defaulters and contemnors[^c19388091][^c-c67d6a7d826023921b82e5652161c04d][^c-c67d6a7d826023921b82e5652161c04d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies in relation to a person committed to prison—

- (a) in default of payment of a sum adjudged to be paid by a conviction, or
- (b) for contempt of court or any kindred offence.

(2) As soon as a person to whom this section applies has served one-half of the term for which he was committed, it is the duty of the Secretary of State to release him unconditionally.

(2A)[^c-b8633ace3847026b113e09522738178a] Subsection (2) is subject to paragraph 35 of Schedule 20B (transitional cases).

(2B)[^c-fb579d6418ee1692b8ecf671113340fd] Subsection (2) does not apply to a person within subsection (1)(a) if the sum in question is a sum of more than £10 million ordered to be paid under a confiscation order made under Part 2 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002.

(2C) The Secretary of State may by order amend the amount for the time being specified in subsection (2B).

(3) Where a person to whom this section applies is also serving one or more sentences of imprisonment, nothing in this section or in paragraph 35 of Schedule 20B[^c-768b643de0935f3ed5604abc2d1352c4] requires the Secretary of State to release him until he is also required to release him in respect of that sentence or each of those sentences.

(3A)[^c-91d1942346a2cca2d98901aafe618863] The reference in subsection (3) to sentences of imprisonment includes sentences of detention under section 91 or 96 of the PCC(S)A 2000, under section 250[^c-092342e1051bd55bcc34c924fd5407e0] , 252A[^c-87844d82fc804339f65e42d3698e5980], 254, 262, 265 , 266 or 268A[^c-88b47024541d715cd3a3e77b841eb554] of the Sentencing Code or under section 226A, 226B,[^c-3d1d24c4fab44db11387752900cc944d] 227 , 228 or 236A[^c-aa7667405524ccd63d8372447a9e7f85] of this Act.

(4) The Secretary of State may at any time release unconditionally a person to whom this section applies if he is satisfied that exceptional circumstances exist which justify the person’s release on compassionate grounds.
