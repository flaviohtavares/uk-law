## 302. Execution of process between England and Wales and Scotland[^c19076261][^key-fe22253cfcc097d6963237d8d4bb5e05]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 4 of the Summary Jurisdiction (Process) Act 1881 (c. 24) (execution of process of English and Welsh courts in Scotland) applies to any process issued by a magistrates' court under—

- [^key-5968f41d95199c51b5044175f8938079]...[^key-34b4dccba4f2aea42673e4322aab28b1]
- section 256C(1) or (3),
- [^key-3c27e5697d3d5b7ec55e8871f0084813]...
- [^key-3c27e5697d3d5b7ec55e8871f0084813]...
- [^key-3c27e5697d3d5b7ec55e8871f0084813]...
- [^key-3c27e5697d3d5b7ec55e8871f0084813]...
- [^key-af907583eb978979cb1f074a52cbf9cb]...

as it applies to process issued under the Magistrates' Courts Act 1980 by a magistrates' court.
