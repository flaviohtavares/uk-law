# Criminal Justice Act 2003, section 259A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## (not yet in force: 259A). (not yet in force: Persons eligible for removal from the United Kingdom)[^c20166701]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: For the purposes of this Chapter, to be “)****(not yet in force: eligible for removal from the United Kingdom)****(not yet in force: ” a person must show, to the satisfaction of the Secretary of State, that the condition in subsection (2) is met.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The condition is that the person has the settled intention of residing permanently outside the United Kingdom if removed from prison under section 260.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: The person must not be one who is liable to removal from the United Kingdom.)*
