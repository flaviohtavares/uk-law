## 241. Effect of section 240ZA or direction under section 240A or under section 325 of the Sentencing Code on release on licence[^c19069601][^c19124511][^c-0c780b6184e67c3ada348d3a114af260][^c-ad9c2bc53516bdc69645a8f71e932e46][^c-155c0f69a8116c840c0366b45e57112f][^c-766e12bc6fb26e8601d934be1ea18143][^c-f8ac351131e545d9580d76e84791989a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In determining for the purposes of this Chapter [^c-3e68c00a6d78f49fc18d54258313a11d]... whether a person to whom section 240ZA applies or a direction under section 240A[^c-d47c0a24f7b74f512fe7c0dc9469fcce] or under section 325 of the Sentencing Code[^c-d1bc6889572abc8906178897dccef6ed] relates —

- (a) has served, or would (but for his release) have served, a particular proportion of his sentence, or
- (b) has served a particular period,

the number of days specified in section 240ZA or in the direction under section 240A[^c-8395a7bde0126d3eca47b6ca2ac3b803] or under section 325 of the Sentencing Code[^c-de9bfee98641ae6748f769577e3185db] are to be treated as having been served by him as part of that sentence or period.

(1A)[^c19241481] In subsection (1) the reference to section 240ZA includes[^c-d4972ea1f984d3e6c94cd9002c1d1965] section 246 of the Armed Forces Act 2006.
