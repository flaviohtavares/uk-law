## 268. Interpretation of Chapter 6[^c19076251]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-00441551636be0d2eb81e42ef6298a78] In this Chapter —

- “**the 1997 Act**” means the Crime (Sentences) Act 1997 (c. 43);
- “**the Board**” means the Parole Board;
- “[^c-dc176d60b3bc63f548819bd8a82ed852]**fixed-term prisoner**” and “**fixed-term sentence**” have the meaning given by section 237(1) (as extended by section 237(1B));[^c19241721]
- [^c-4717248c1243072bc684a96e38ccbd4b]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^c-8899633d04e8ad312446af272c7427cf]...
- “prison” and “prisoner” are to be read in accordance with section 237(2);
- [^c-88a90f0785dc384b3f954291e253f5d1]...
- [^c-9975d76bded7ea593f53b2c22e86b2be]...
- “the supervisor”—[^c-bec3a9eef6d00570023f311bb88ff393]
  - ((a)) [^c-a9544edd3c651ed141806259047421bb]...
  - ((b)) in relation to an offender subject to supervision requirements under section 256B, means the person who provides supervision under that section;
- [^c-4717248c1243072bc684a96e38ccbd4b]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^c-4717248c1243072bc684a96e38ccbd4b]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1A)[^c-775cd92ff373406e74b719c3e1f6343a] In this Chapter, “**the requisite custodial period**” means (except where it has the meaning given by section 247A(8))[^c-943c83f6e23e9f2ba87dcffa18814759] —

- (a) in relation to a person serving an extended sentence imposed under section 226A or 226B or under section 254, 266 or 279 of the Sentencing Code[^c-35b6d727a17e295bed269761a355982c], the requisite custodial period for the purposes of section 246A;
- (b) in relation to a person serving an extended sentence imposed under section 227 or 228, the requisite custodial period for the purposes of section 247;
- (c) in relation to a person serving a sentence imposed under section 236A or under section[^c-ecb8cfc8bf061d6d63d5322395e02132] 252A,[^c-9d38ad097eaae672ef443e25b0d576da] 265 or 278 of the Sentencing Code, the requisite custodial period for the purposes of section 244A;
- (ca)[^c-10455db8ce9c903d073615389aa28d18] in relation to a prisoner whose case has been referred to the Parole Board under section 244ZB, the requisite custodial period for the purposes of section 244ZC;
- (d) in relation to any other fixed-term prisoner, the requisite custodial period for the purposes of section 243A , 244 or 244ZA[^c-5f51589842419430bc80422100eea979] (as appropriate).

(2)[^c-78b78bfdd1e47729a79507ebfcfe4f5f] For the purposes of sections 243A(1A), [^c-20b90ab6abeb6983de772a1dd35abbe0]... 256B(1A) and 264B(1), where an offence is found to have been committed over a period of 2 or more days, or at some time during a period of 2 or more days, it must be taken to have been committed on the last of those days.
