## 247. Release on licence of prisoner serving extended sentence under section 227 or 228[^c19388081][^key-ad9c2bc53516bdc69645a8f71e932e46][^key-155c0f69a8116c840c0366b45e57112f][^key-1685a02ce653a5293667f4152e268d38]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies to a prisoner who is serving an extended sentence imposed under section 227 or 228, other than a prisoner to whom section 247A applies[^key-4852f768889f0b4d56ec71ec96d1df36].

(2) As soon as—

- (a) a prisoner to whom this section applies has served the requisite custodial period[^key-3b0df52ed935f57cbed05b2b4a4aae89], [^c20145821]. . .
- (b) [^c20145831]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

it is the duty of the Secretary of State to release him on licence.

(3) [^c20145851]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) [^c20145861]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) [^c20145871]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) [^c20145881]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^key-32ec25b5309dc02733e0111658225376] In this section—

- “**the appropriate custodial term**” means the period determined by the court as the appropriate custodial term under section 227 or 228;
- “**the requisite custodial period**” means—
  - (a) in relation to a person serving one sentence, one-half of the appropriate custodial term, and
  - (b) in relation to a person serving two or more concurrent or consecutive sentences, the period determined under sections 263(2) and 264(2B) or (2E)[^key-2e9138af18979dddd6dcd551ea5180cc].

(8)[^key-e154e8532062b40882a7548f4da96248] In its application to a person serving a sentence imposed before 14 July 2008, this section is subject to the modifications set out in paragraph 15 of Schedule 20B (transitional cases).
