# Communications Act 2003, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Information requested by EU bodies[^key-c2a921202776f43e9bb564f29150e522][^key-a81629c573347a5c628ce4bb81bddabb][^key-1e9e1c16aa8becf4fd9e582dfb8cba6d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) the European Commission or BEREC requests[^key-f98d886cb22406faa81ae5a4eb0ccde1] OFCOM to provide it with information for the purpose of enabling it to perform any of its functions in relation to electronic communications networks, electronic communications services or associated facilities; and
- (b) the information is information obtained by OFCOM in the course of carrying out any of their functions under—
  - (i) Part 2; [^key-17498121520e189de6ebdee4c09c7aac]...
  - (ii) the enactments relating to the management of the radio spectrum that are not contained in that Part ; or[^key-7eddf677cd093e8ab0a6af68ab839e3d]
  - (iii)[^key-e37753ded133d4efd543584c4efee939] any other enactment relating to the regulation of electronic communications networks and electronic communications services.

(2)[^key-59fc4c4779f6bac9e2dd246e1263a611] OFCOM may comply with the request if and to the extent that—

- (a) they consider it appropriate to do so in connection with their functions, and
- (b) in the case of information relating to a particular business, they are satisfied that arrangements exist for preserving confidentiality.

(3) If information provided to the European Commission or BEREC[^key-4b0d9b1646ce532e714f415f7da001af] under this section has been obtained by OFCOM from a person who is or, at the time the information was obtained from him, was—

- (a) a communications provider, or
- (b) a person making associated facilities available,

OFCOM must notify him that they have provided the information to the Commission or, as the case may be, to BEREC[^key-c9c988b019c72be2ab942a49d5c2edff].

(4) It shall be for OFCOM to determine the manner in which a notification is given under subsection (3).
