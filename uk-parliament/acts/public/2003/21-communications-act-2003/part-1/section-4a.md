# Communications Act 2003, section 4A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21

## 4A. ... European Commission recommendations for harmonisation[^c-79559b4344b44bb6d1e36b704b37c02a][^c-4a5c9ee73c5d56799c491b000fc5ada3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to the following functions of OFCOM—

- (a) their functions under Chapter 1 of Part 2;
- (b) their functions under the enactments relating to the management of the radio spectrum;
- (c) their functions under Chapter 3 of Part 2 in relation to disputes referred to them under section 185;
- (d) their functions under sections 24 and 25 so far as they relate to information required for purposes connected with matters in relation to which functions specified in this subsection are conferred on OFCOM; and
- (e) their functions under section 26 so far as they are carried out for the purpose of making information available to persons mentioned in subsection (2)(a) to (c) of that section.

(2)[^c-0161e5225037c9fe25002da6766691e1] In carrying out those functions, OFCOM may take account of recommendations issued by the European Commission under Article 19(1) of the Framework Directive or Article 38(1) of the EECC Directive (including recommendations issued before the coming into force of this section or after IP completion day) if the recommendations appear to OFCOM to be relevant to those functions.
