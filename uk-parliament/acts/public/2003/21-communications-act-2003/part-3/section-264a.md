# Communications Act 2003, section 264A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 264A. OFCOM reports: wider review and reporting obligations[^c-d30098e1b5fcf1174533389bda012154]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) When carrying out a review under section 264 for a period, OFCOM must also carry out a review of the extent to which material included in media services during that period (taken together over the period as a whole) contributed towards the fulfilment of the public service requirements[^c-18d7eb534010cc6683541928023fed8a].

(2) Every report under section 264 must—

- (a) include a report on the matters found on the review under this section,
- (b) specify, and comment on, whatever changes appear to OFCOM to have occurred, during the period to which the report relates, in the extent to which the public service requirements[^c-d25783d0e31c534f938744590046deee] have been fulfilled,
- (c) specify, and comment on, whatever changes appear to OFCOM to have occurred, during that period, in the manner in which those requirements[^c-f81283c404507ed7c66eceba367b42b6] are fulfilled, and
- (d) set out OFCOM's conclusions on the current state of material included in media services.

(3)[^c-9a0baa9ff2013d104f3c860fa173aa08] “The public service requirements” are the requirements set out in paragraphs (a) to (d) of section 264(5), as modified by subsection (4).

(4) Section 264(5) has effect for the purposes of subsection (3) as if references to audiovisual content made available by the public service broadcasters, or to programmes included in that content, were references to material included in media services.

(5) In this section—

- “**material**” does not include advertisements;
- “**media services**” means any of the following services that are available to members of the public in all or part of the United Kingdom—
  - (a) television and radio services,
  - (b) on-demand programme services,
  - (ba) non-UK on-demand programme services,[^c-1ac5c952411f97be088dffee3c068515] and
  - (c) other services provided by means of the internet where there is a person who exercises editorial control over the material included in the service.

(6) The services that are to be taken for the purposes of this section to be available to members of the public include any service which—

- (a) is available for reception by members of the public (within the meaning of section 361); or
- (b) is available for use by members of the public (within the meaning of section 368R(4)).
