# Communications Act 2003, section 203

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 203. Function of OFCOM in relation to S4C[^key-7caa2f15faa0c0c1a4f5f1ca102aba86][^key-1bbfcc5c3d948ae777c36179cddd4812]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

It shall be a function of OFCOM, to the extent that provision for them to do so is contained in this Act and Part 5 of the 1996 Act, to regulate the services provided by S4C[^key-6332f60032f295cb20591bcac7820ed3].
