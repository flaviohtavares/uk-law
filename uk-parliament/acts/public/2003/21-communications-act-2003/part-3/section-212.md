# Communications Act 2003, section 212

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 212. Abolition of function of assigning television frequencies[^key-efb9d8016039b70cc8ad4a4400e970e7][^key-3ed08a781151dcbd15b42c46f8dd5828][^key-45cb27ae430e1c2da44e8f6a76ec7deb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State shall cease to have any function under the 1990 Act or the 1996 Act of assigning frequencies for the purposes of any of the following—

- (a) services falling to be licensed under Part 1 of the 1990 Act;
- (b) S4C; or
- (c) television multiplex services falling to be licensed under Part 1 of the 1996 Act.
