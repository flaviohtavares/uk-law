# Communications Act 2003, section 343

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 343. Provision of information by S4C[^key-8267c33365b869b1940fb45901b8218b][^key-313e6ac1bf3fe6b56dd57a11ec03f9f2][^key-5eda645746f64e1df2b4f6d030a98335]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It shall be the duty of S4C[^key-aaf4a02fb1849ed7063ecec8999035f3] to comply with every direction given to them by OFCOM to provide OFCOM with information falling within subsection (2).

(2) The information that S4C[^key-10e8b9a01c4be993cba2e3c609f352b7] may be directed to provide is any information which OFCOM may reasonably require for the purposes of carrying out their functions in relation to S4C under this Act, the 1990 Act or the 1996 Act.

(3) Information that is required to be provided by a direction under this section must be provided in such manner and at such times as may be required by the direction.
