# Communications Act 2003, section 335B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21

## 335B. Maintenance of list of providers[^key-b7dd044246dc229f5d551de48a93c782]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) OFCOM must establish and maintain an up to date list of persons providing—

- (a) a television programme service, or
- (b) a digital additional television service,

which are subject to regulation by OFCOM as a result of one of the provisions listed in subsection (1A)[^key-c61bb406837e4d445325a07ca05d8b87].

(1A)[^key-d74cd6d6ccc0c1eb209d4f74e2dea868] The provisions are—

- (a) section 198 (regulation of the BBC by OFCOM);
- (b) section 203 (regulation of S4C by OFCOM);
- (c) section 211 (regulation of independent television services).

(2)[^key-9f514c19fbac3561813b0d2a8a9791fc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-5998dd532325a35606eb594cb572c4df] OFCOM must publish the up to date list on a publicly accessible part of their website.

(4) The regulatory regime for each service mentioned in subsection (1) (other than any service provided by the BBC or S4C)[^key-2e112c21efc7c5d09eb23015e7089236] must include the condition that the persons providing the service must notify OFCOM of any changes that may affect whether or not the service falls to be regulated by OFCOM under section 211.[^key-8d0bea1747d1fb8806b6eb563a9537da]
