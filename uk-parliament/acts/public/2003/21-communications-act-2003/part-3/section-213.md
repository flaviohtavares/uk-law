# Communications Act 2003, section 213

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 213. Abolition of licensing for local cable systems[^c-984429597cc1ebedd8ee4a898cde1ccd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

On and after the television transfer date no licence shall be required under Part 2 of the 1990 Act for the provision of a local delivery service.
