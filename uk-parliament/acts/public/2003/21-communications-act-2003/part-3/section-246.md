# Communications Act 2003, section 246

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21

## 246. Abolition of function of assigning radio frequencies[^c-a1f1ecd72139cb2d249222c8b616e579]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State shall cease to have any function under the 1990 Act or the 1996 Act of assigning frequencies—

- (a) for any of the purposes of Part 3 of the 1990 Act (regulation of radio services); or
- (b) for the purposes of the provision of any radio multiplex services.
