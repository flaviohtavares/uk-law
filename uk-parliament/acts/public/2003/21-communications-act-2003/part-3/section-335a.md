# Communications Act 2003, section 335A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 335A. Co-operation with other parties to European Convention on Transfrontier Television[^key-fcab10939ff11791400db75a66b85735]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

OFCOM may do any of the things that paragraph 3 of Article 19 of the European Convention on Transfrontier Television requires to be done by an authority designated under paragraph 2 of that Article.
