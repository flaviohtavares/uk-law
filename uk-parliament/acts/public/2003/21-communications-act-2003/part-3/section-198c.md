# Communications Act 2003, section 198C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 198C. OFCOM reports on C4C's media content duties and commissioning duties[^key-f2f8eaad4bb362b2e8e7af6dbf7b0602][^key-27cdb184ce7bf929f5747200802018f1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For each relevant period, OFCOM must—

- (a) carry out a review of the extent to which C4C have discharged their duties under section sections 198A and 198AA[^key-f6e3f8a4a5a48d1daaba72e632a7fcbd], and
- (b) prepare a report on the matters found on the review.

(2) OFCOM must publish each report under this section—

- (a) as soon as practicable after its preparation is complete, and
- (b) in such manner as they consider appropriate.

(3) “**Relevant period**” means each period selected by OFCOM for the purposes of section 264(1)(b) that ends after this section comes into force.
