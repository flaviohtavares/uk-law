## 338. Corresponding rules for the BBC and S4C[^key-bafdabb16d70e2a332f2795cb46fc187][^key-24020f884051073e6f89b5cffd0f51b5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 12 (which provides for the imposition on the BBC and S4C[^key-f5a655b8a4d8f370a64a50c40115f792] of obligations corresponding to obligations included in the regulatory regime for licensed providers) shall have effect.
