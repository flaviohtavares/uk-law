## 368BZA. Maintenance of list of providers[^key-46fd344e8af6416a1841fde9e0602f45][^key-8b5116708328654f497f918c49a3b13d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) OFCOM must establish and maintain an up to date list of persons who have—[^key-4538f84b85de8dd19708498e169f5e79]

- (a) given a notification to OFCOM under section 368BA(1) of their intention to provide an on-demand programme service, and
- (b) not given a further notification under section 368BA(2)(b) of their intention to cease to provide it.

(2)[^key-e74f1179baabd3179fa64afb624c52d9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-50bfbd8ca425e004fbcfb19201c1803e] OFCOM must publish the up to date list on a publicly accessible part of their website.
