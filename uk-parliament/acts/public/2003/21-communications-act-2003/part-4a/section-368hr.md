# Communications Act 2003, section 368HR

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 368HR. Application of Chapter 3[^key-0077064997c0c904f94b6f95c9ba51d9][^key-8b5116708328654f497f918c49a3b13d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The duties of providers of Tier 1 services under or by virtue of this Chapter apply in relation to those services only so far as they are made available for use by members of the public in the United Kingdom.
