# Communications Act 2003, section 368ZA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 368ZA. Audiovisual programmes[^c-06831c2e7b2bca180ab3972f43604c35][^c-8b5116708328654f497f918c49a3b13d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part applies in relation to the provision of programmes with or without sounds which consist of moving or still images, or of legible text, or of a combination of those things.
