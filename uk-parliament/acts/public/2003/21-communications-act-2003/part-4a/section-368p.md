## 368P. Application of Part 4A in relation to the BBC[^key-8b5116708328654f497f918c49a3b13d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1)[^key-313e2cb2b01e2f9bc931b2abccce2fda] Section 368BA (advance notification) does not apply in relation to an on-demand programme service provided or to be provided by the BBC.

(1)[^key-299f1dcd23bcf9ac8707ec02671d32d5] The following provisions do not apply to the BBC—

- (a) section 368D(3)(za) and (zb) (duties of providers of on-demand programme services);[^key-9814648eac7e457424cdff25cd2a24b6]
- (b) section 368F (advertising);
- (c) section 368G (sponsorship);
- (d) section 368NA (fees).

(2) In the following provisions references to a provider of an on-demand programme service do not include references to the BBC—

- (a) section 368C(4) (codes of conduct: food and beverage promotion and children’s programmes)[^key-8dd76a7c062ae9ede888043ca6fe34bf];
- (b)[^key-222b2a09dd4babb92b30cc4c9ba2c60e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^key-222b2a09dd4babb92b30cc4c9ba2c60e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^key-101f5247df42d14e61075cd6895e3086] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) section 368K (suspension or restriction of service for contraventions);
- (f) section 368L (suspension or restriction of service for inciting crime or disorder);
- (g)[^key-2097a815b48af6d51107c019937d42ff] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Paragraph 2(2)(b) of Schedule 12 includes provision imposing obligations on the BBC in relation to on-demand programme services.
