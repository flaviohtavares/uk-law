# Communications Act 2003, section 368N

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21

## 368N. Enforcement of directions under section 368K or 368L[^key-8b5116708328654f497f918c49a3b13d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person (“P”) is guilty of an offence if P provides an on-demand programme service or a non-UK on-demand programme service that is a Tier 1 service[^key-90a1740fbff84e9d426392b65e6a9d9c]—

- (a) while P’s entitlement to do so is suspended by a direction under section 368K or 368L, or
- (b) in contravention of a restriction contained in such a direction.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine.
