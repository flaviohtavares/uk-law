# Communications Act 2003, section 105M

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 105M. General duty of OFCOM to ensure compliance with security duties[^c-61d3a75dc500e830735194548dc0d8e2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

OFCOM must seek to ensure that providers of public electronic communications networks and public electronic communications services comply with the duties imposed on them by or under sections 105A to 105D, 105J and 105K.
