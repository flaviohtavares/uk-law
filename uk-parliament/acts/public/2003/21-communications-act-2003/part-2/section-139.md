## 139. Penalties for contravention of information requirements[^c-087545bffb125470413b66e82847d427][^c-6a259782e7c2fe0eb09a2661a23e9131][^c-e990b5a51aa38de73d089a34b5cc2060]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-0ea7655c2d434e69a46b54caae2c8afb] This section applies where a person is given a notification under section 138 which specifies a proposed penalty.

(2) OFCOM may specify a penalty[^c-7308a66a06f928024c08c832565b073c] if—

- (a)[^c-18f4eb99bae3c7d899e05663f0d1e0d1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-18f4eb99bae3c7d899e05663f0d1e0d1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) no proceedings for an offence under section 144 have been brought against the notified person in respect of the contravention.

(3) Where a notification under section 138 relates to more than one contravention, a separate penalty may be specified[^c-9e8307b6c6e3513b6ea07a7fd84f925f] in respect of each contravention.

(4) Where such a notification relates to a continuing contravention, no more than one penalty may be specified[^c-7e5a6dae62f0414416eb750f0d17255f] in respect of the period of contravention specified in the notification.

(4A)[^c-a24d4be1144dbf8bf9718cf315c97737] But, in relation to a continuing contravention, a penalty may be specified in respect of each day on which the contravention continues after—

- (a) the giving of a confirmation decision under section 139A(4)(c) which requires immediate action; or
- (b) the expiry of any period specified in the confirmation decision for complying with a requirement so specified.

(4B) The amount of a penalty under subsection (4A) is to be such amount not exceeding £500 per day as OFCOM determine to be—

- (a) appropriate; and
- (b) proportionate to the contravention in respect of which it is imposed.

(5) The amount of any other penalty notified[^c-55e72d2e6eab4de03f8116f16110504b] under this section is to be such amount not exceeding £2,000,000[^c-463abdc3fed5b85d328b3ba0a3e769b1] as OFCOM determine to be both—

- (a) appropriate; and
- (b) proportionate to the contravention in respect of which it is imposed.

(6)[^c-d73cdbd4ae1dcf411213ae85ea0fb17e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^c-d73cdbd4ae1dcf411213ae85ea0fb17e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8)[^c-d73cdbd4ae1dcf411213ae85ea0fb17e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9) The Secretary of State may by order amend this section so as to substitute a different maximum penalty for the maximum penalty for the time being specified in subsection (5).

(10) No order is to be made containing provision authorised by subsection (9) unless a draft of the order has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House.
