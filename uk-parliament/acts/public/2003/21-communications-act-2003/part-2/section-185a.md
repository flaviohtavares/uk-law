# Communications Act 2003, section 185A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 185A. Power of OFCOM to invite parties to refer dispute[^key-2a7b74e6cc3ad2157197fdb8de122126][^key-899275045cd85695240370892b436865]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

OFCOM may invite any one or more of the parties to a dispute falling within section 185(1) to refer the dispute to OFCOM under section 185(3).
