# Communications Act 2003, section 368Z21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 368Z21. Interpretation[^key-02dd9f734f9de221efb556998936ddc9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**appropriate regulatory authority**” is to be construed in accordance with section 368Z19;
- “**less healthy**”, in relation to a food or drink product, has the meaning given by section 368Z14(5)(e).
