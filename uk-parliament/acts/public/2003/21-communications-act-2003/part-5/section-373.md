# Communications Act 2003, section 373

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 373. Repeal of existing newspaper merger regime[^key-6693d0ce0bd321b93e877db77780d088]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Sections 57 to 62 of the Fair Trading Act 1973 (c. 41) (newspaper merger references) shall cease to have effect.
