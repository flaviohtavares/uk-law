# Communications Act 2003, section 374

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2003/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 374. Repeal of exclusion for newspaper mergers from general merger controls[^c-ffd5065548d16024abbf6e781e562393]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 69 of the Enterprise Act 2002 (c. 40) (exclusion of newspaper mergers from references under Part 3 of that Act) shall cease to have effect.
