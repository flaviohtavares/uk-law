# Electricity Act 1947

> Text as at 2001-11-09.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Electricity Act 1947

1947 c.54

An Act to provide for the establishment of a British Electricity Authority and Area Electricity Boards and for the exercise and performance by that Authority and those Boards and the North of Scotland Hydro-Electric Board of functions relating to the supply of electricity and certain other matters; for the transfer to the said Authority or any such Board as aforesaid of property, rights, obligations and liabilities of electricity undertakers and other bodies; to amend the law relating to the supply of electricity; to make certain consequential provision as to income tax; and for purposes connected with the matters aforesaid.

\[13th August 1947\][^c634035][^c634036][^c634037][^c634038][^c634039][^c634040][^c634041][^c634042]
