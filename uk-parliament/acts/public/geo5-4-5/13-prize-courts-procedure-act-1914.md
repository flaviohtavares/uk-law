Prize Courts (Procedure) Act 1914

1914 c.13

An Act to amend the Law relating to Procedure in Prize Courts.

\[5th August 1914\]

## 1. Procedure in prize courts.

(1)[^c579895] . . . [^c579896] nothing in such repeal shall have the effect of extending section sixteen of that Act to ships of war or military aircraft[^c579897] taken as prize, [^c579898]and accordingly that section shall have effect as if the following words were inserted therein:—“Nothing in this section shall apply to ships of war taken as prize.”

(2) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c579899]

## 2. Short title and construction.

This Act may be cited as the [^c579900]Prize Courts (Procedure) Act 1914 and shall be construed as one with the Naval Prize Act 1864; and that Act and the [^c579901]Prize Courts Act 1894 and this Act may be cited together as the Naval Prize Acts, 1864 to 1914.
