# Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Complainants in proceedings for sexual offences.[^c9545931][^key-14fb982f9d044c1ef29da254b4d6a1fa]

*Extent: E+W*

No person charged with a sexual offence may in any criminal proceedings cross-examine in person a witness who is the complainant, either—

- (a) in connection with that offence, or
- (b) in connection with any other offence (of whatever nature) with which that person is charged in the proceedings.
