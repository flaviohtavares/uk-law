## 49. Offences under Chapter IV.[^c9546391][^c-9a57009ee054cfd23ec6de6182b00ab1][^c-c72b793c02fc1d87d6bc61d92133e0f0][^c-88ad9ff1624e30fc7f7c2c42b18c032e][^c-ca8c072901a176deda083ad5c0567b63][^c-28c092a2a0a20df9e03c8ca740f8b1ac][^c-33ff00480060565c43a5a1081aa798d9][^c-6247684a1241b9fd5bd3bee4c3046fd3][^c-42fac34869c91a9681dd8bf09d1263f2][^c-bd8b4413087f3e7e48c6e11d1649fcd7][^c-8989f77e07f13292f2c3ba416ae01616][^c-b96ca74dff02753fb5f36e3d69b30725]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-97bac955c49fd719d9815ce1df02a6ad] This section applies if a publication—

- (a) includes any matter in contravention of section 44(2) or of a direction under section 45(3) or 46(2); or
- (b) includes a report in contravention of section 47.

(1A)[^c-e2d780d9768d88da71f58a044e58485d] This section also applies—

- (a) in England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland, if a publication includes any matter in contravention of a direction under section 45A(2) made by a service court;
- (b) in England and Wales, if a publication includes any matter in contravention of a direction under section 45A(2) made by a court other than a service court.

(2)[^c-97bac955c49fd719d9815ce1df02a6ad] Where the publication is a newspaper or periodical, any proprietor, any editor and any publisher of the newspaper or periodical is guilty of an offence.

(3)[^c-97bac955c49fd719d9815ce1df02a6ad] Where the publication is a relevant programme—

- (a) any body corporate or Scottish partnership engaged in providing the programme service in which the programme is included, and
- (b) any person having functions in relation to the programme corresponding to those of an editor of a newspaper,

is guilty of an offence.

(4)[^c-97bac955c49fd719d9815ce1df02a6ad] In the case of any other publication, any person publishing it is guilty of an offence.

(5)[^c-97bac955c49fd719d9815ce1df02a6ad] A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.

(6) Proceedings for an offence under this section in respect of a publication falling within subsection (1)(b) may not be instituted—

- (a) in England and Wales otherwise than by or with the consent of the Attorney General, or
- (b) in Northern Ireland otherwise than by or with the consent of the Attorney General for Northern Ireland.

(7)[^c-e35443b96501f630aba5ab61860764e2] Schedule 2A makes special provision in connection with the operation of this section, so far as it relates to a publication that includes matter in contravention of a direction under section 45A(2), in relation to persons providing information society services.
