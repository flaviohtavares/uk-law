# Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999, section 33C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33C. Saving[^c-5001947544a5df99528acaaf59b47187][^c-af01f9421f5e9374414856a231f32d9a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Chapter affects—

- (a) any power of a court to make an order, give directions or give leave of any description in relation to any witness (including an accused), or
- (b) the operation of any rule of law relating to evidence in criminal proceedings.
