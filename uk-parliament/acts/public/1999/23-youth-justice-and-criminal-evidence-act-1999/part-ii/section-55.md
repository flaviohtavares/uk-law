# Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999, section 55

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 55. Determining whether witness to be sworn.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-2de1b8802e254c8287900e69c7611447] Any question whether a witness in criminal proceedings may be sworn for the purpose of giving evidence on oath, whether raised—

- (a) by a party to the proceedings, or
- (b) by the court of its own motion,

shall be determined by the court in accordance with this section.

(2)[^c-2de1b8802e254c8287900e69c7611447] The witness may not be sworn for that purpose unless—

- (a) he has attained the age of 14, and
- (b) he has a sufficient appreciation of the solemnity of the occasion and of the particular responsibility to tell the truth which is involved in taking an oath.

(3)[^c-2de1b8802e254c8287900e69c7611447] The witness shall, if he is able to give intelligible testimony, be presumed to have a sufficient appreciation of those matters if no evidence tending to show the contrary is adduced (by any party).

(4)[^c-2de1b8802e254c8287900e69c7611447] If any such evidence is adduced, it is for the party seeking to have the witness sworn to satisfy the court that, on a balance of probabilities, the witness has attained the age of 14 and has a sufficient appreciation of the matters mentioned in subsection (2)(b).

(5)[^c-239bf11b2a3f17dcedf4dc9ade6362cb] Any proceedings held for the determination of the question mentioned in subsection (1) shall take place in the absence of the jury (if there is one).

(6)[^c-485216abec38c9719ab3e2f78a0b467e] Expert evidence may be received on the question.

(7)[^c-485216abec38c9719ab3e2f78a0b467e] Any questioning of the witness (where the court considers that necessary) shall be conducted by the court in the presence of the parties.

(8)[^c-485216abec38c9719ab3e2f78a0b467e] For the purposes of this section a person is able to give intelligible testimony if he is able to—

- (a) understand questions put to him as a witness, and
- (b) give answers to them which can be understood.
