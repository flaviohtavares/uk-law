# Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Amendments relating to other reporting restrictions.[^c9546331]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 2, which contains amendments relating to reporting restrictions under—

- (a)[^key-530e443e5a25c521560294c6b194cdf7] the [^c9546341]Children and Young Persons Act 1933,
- (b)[^key-b62bfe0ee6c7241990c2d2c61ff0448d] the [^c9546351]Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act 1976,
- (c)[^key-8154bf4448439a5db310bc8797dd9a26] the [^c9546361]Sexual Offences (Northern Ireland) Order 1978,
- (d)[^M_I_bcd722e7-9a93-4810-b575-c691a233ee3b][^key-7e814bb291b2fa53547456d60b41b398] the [^c9546371]Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act 1992, and
- (e)[^M_I_076a24f5-b33a-472b-f571-ef771a80afce] the [^c9546381]Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1994,

shall have effect.
