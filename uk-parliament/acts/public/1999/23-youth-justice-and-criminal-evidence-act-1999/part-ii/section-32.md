# Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/23

## 32. Warning to jury.[^c9545901]

*Extent: E+W*

Where on a trial on indictment with a jury[^key-b14d5bfe9e562cd260529c1c671be532] evidence has been given in accordance with a special measures direction, the judge must give the jury such warning (if any) as the judge considers necessary to ensure that the fact that the direction was given in relation to the witness does not prejudice the accused.
