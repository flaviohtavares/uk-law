# Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999, section 60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Removal of restriction on use of evidence from computer records.

*Extent: E+W*

Section 69 of the [^c9546531]Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (evidence from computer records inadmissible unless conditions relating to proper use and operation of computer shown to be satisfied) shall cease to have effect.
