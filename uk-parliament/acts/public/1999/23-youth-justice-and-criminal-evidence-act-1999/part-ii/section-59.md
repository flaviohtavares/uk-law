# Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999, section 59

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Restriction on use of answers etc. obtained under compulsion.[^c9546521]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 3, which amends enactments providing for the use of answers and statements given under compulsion so as to restrict in criminal proceedings their use in evidence against the persons giving them, shall have effect.
