# Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, section 92

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (172).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Enforcement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If it appears to the Commissioner that a person—

- (a) is providing immigration advice or immigration services in contravention of section 84 or of a restraining order, and
- (b) is likely to continue to do so unless restrained,

the Commissioner may apply to a county court in Northern Ireland or the county court in England and Wales[^key-235b0bf1cb06a18af23d318c430e23d8] for an injunction, or to the sheriff for an interdict, restraining him from doing so.

(2) If the court is satisfied that the application is well-founded, it may grant the injunction or interdict in the terms applied for or in more limited terms.

(3) “**Restraining order**” has the meaning given by section 91.
