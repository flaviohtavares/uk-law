## 34. Defences to claim that penalty is due under section 32.[^c11432702][^c11432691][^key-860cd73ee6afc92641a3de2b1987cfb3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66] This section applies if it is alleged that a person (“the carrier”) is liable to a penalty under section 32. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]

(1) A person (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]**the carrier *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]**”) shall not be liable to the imposition of a penalty under section 32(2) if he has a defence under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]

(2) It is a defence for the carrier to show that he, or an employee of his who was directly responsible for allowing the clandestine entrant to be concealed, was acting under duress. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]

(3)[^key-ece9a49c630e292ed815fb8485c3236f] It is also a defence for the carrier to show that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ece9a49c630e292ed815fb8485c3236f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]

- (a) he did not know, and had no reasonable grounds for suspecting, that a clandestine entrant was, or might be, concealed in the transporter; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ece9a49c630e292ed815fb8485c3236f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]
- (b) an effective system for preventing the carriage of clandestine entrants was in operation in relation to the transporter; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ece9a49c630e292ed815fb8485c3236f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]
- (c) that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-90b92dafe0e3d3a758c1968cc45e8c0d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ece9a49c630e292ed815fb8485c3236f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66] on the occasion in question the person or persons responsible for operating that system did so properly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ece9a49c630e292ed815fb8485c3236f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]

(3A)[^key-79119f37e6469f2a8b69421b3cf143b5] It is also a defence for the carrier to show that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]

- (a) he knew or suspected that a clandestine entrant was or might be concealed in a rail freight wagon, having boarded after the wagon began its journey to the United Kingdom; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]
- (b) he could not stop the train or shuttle-train of which the wagon formed part without endangering safety; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66] and[^key-2691f9fc212b96193dc2f49269002443] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]
- (c)[^key-b7a883f04bf587db2863e83da5c563ed] an effective system for preventing the carriage of clandestine entrants was in operation in relation to the train or shuttle-train; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b7a883f04bf587db2863e83da5c563ed] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]
- (c) the carrier had taken the actions specified in regulations under subsection (3B) in relation to the securing of the wagon against unauthorised access. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]
- (d)[^key-2665ad30f528c6cc72660b66806128fe] on the occasion in question the person or persons responsible for operating the system did so properly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2665ad30f528c6cc72660b66806128fe] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]

(3B)[^key-17f2377eff5c03ab5616b6f16a291ce8] The Secretary of State must specify in regulations the actions to be taken for the purposes of subsection (3A)(c) in relation to the securing of a rail freight wagon against unauthorised access. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]

(3C) The actions that may be specified in regulations under subsection (3B) include, in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]

- (a) actions in relation to checking a person has not gained unauthorised access to the wagon, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]
- (b) actions in relation to the reporting of any unauthorised access to the wagon, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]
- (c) actions in relation to the keeping of records to establish that other actions specified in the regulations have been taken. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]

(3D) Before making regulations under subsection (3B), the Secretary of State must consult such persons as the Secretary of State considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]

(4)[^key-8dfca35a21825151f182cafeec9283f4] In determining, for the purposes of this section, whether a particular system is effective, regard is to be had to the code of practice issued by the Secretary of State under section 33. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8dfca35a21825151f182cafeec9283f4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]

(5)[^key-8bd56177abb0402d3fe695ed86cac368] If there are two or more persons responsible for a clandestine entrant, the fact that one or more of them has a defence under subsection (3) does not affect the liability of the others. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8bd56177abb0402d3fe695ed86cac368] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]

(6)[^key-20a2f2df7bc3c80389e958f3eaf8d2d1] But if a person responsible for a clandestine entrant has a defence under subsection (2), the liability of any other person responsible for that entrant is discharged. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-20a2f2df7bc3c80389e958f3eaf8d2d1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e96f8de8f4391a3001e3d7aefaa31e66]

(6) Where a person has a defence under subsection (2) in respect of a clandestine entrant, every other responsible person in respect of the clandestine entrant is also entitled to the benefit of the defence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-20a2f2df7bc3c80389e958f3eaf8d2d1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-20a2f2df7bc3c80389e958f3eaf8d2d1]
