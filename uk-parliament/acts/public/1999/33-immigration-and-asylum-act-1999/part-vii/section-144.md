# Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, section 144

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (172).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 144. Other methods of collecting data about physical characteristics.[^key-91b4d37b295ce691686859e9f2bd8a59]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-4d9f0b3325d1780e8944d56371ea8377] The Secretary of State may make regulations containing provisions equivalent to sections 141 and 142[^key-d8a815a324aeb7a81e51c928fb31442e] , or to provision made under section 141,[^key-db18163cea30f12594acb25ccce035fa] in relation to such other methods of collecting biometric information[^key-2c2614e14e5db51e31ef2fe320268264] as may be prescribed.

(2)[^key-026ed70d9368e42a6c3099c65821230b] “**Biometric information**” has the meaning given by section 15 of the UK Borders Act 2007.
