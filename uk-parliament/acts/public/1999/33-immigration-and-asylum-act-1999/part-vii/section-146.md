# Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, section 146

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (172).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 146. Use of force.[^c11434571][^key-0ae8d79f336e9d6f159fc6e8d1c1b11e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An immigration officer exercising any power conferred on him by the Immigration Acts[^key-5395873d53459c589d9f43bd1e4d380b] may, if necessary, use reasonable force.

(2)[^c17547921] A person exercising a power under any of the following may if necessary use reasonable force—

- (a) section 28CA, 28FA or 28FB of the 1971 Act (business premises: entry to arrest or search),
- (aa)[^key-1cc487a6d68b47c8957e7617003e0d88] paragraph 25CA, 25CB or 25CC of Schedule 2 to the 1971 Act (powers to search for and seize driving licences),
- (b) section 141 or 142 of this Act, and
- (c) regulations under section 144 of this Act.
