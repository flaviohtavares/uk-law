# Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, section 164

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (172).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 164. Institution of proceedings.[^key-b51a57688d7e86a0de03b1eddbdeb84b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 3(2) of the [^c11434951]Prosecution of Offences Act 1985 (proceedings which must be conducted by the Director of Public Prosecutions), after paragraph (a) insert—

> - (aa) to take over the conduct of any criminal proceedings instituted by an immigration officer (as defined for the purposes of the [^c11434961]Immigration Act 1971) acting in his capacity as such an officer;

.
