# Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, section 152

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (172).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 152. Visiting Committees and inspections.[^c11434741]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-660455f369a82b42842cd5ba7197bc12] The Secretary of State must appoint a committee (to be known as the Visiting Committee) for each removal centre[^c-f45d9d049495f9b5718f9c28e2db7f58].

(2)[^c-660455f369a82b42842cd5ba7197bc12] The functions of the Visiting Committee for a removal centre are to be such as may be prescribed by the removal centre rules.

(3)[^c-660455f369a82b42842cd5ba7197bc12] Those rules must include provision—

- (a) as to the making of visits to the centre by members of the Visiting Committee;
- (b) for the hearing of complaints made by persons detained in the centre;
- (c) requiring the making of reports by the Visiting Committee to the Secretary of State.

(4)[^c-660455f369a82b42842cd5ba7197bc12] Every member of the Visiting Committee for a removal centre may at any time enter the centre and have free access to every part of it and to every person detained there.

(5) In section 5A of the [^c11434751]Prison Act 1952 (which deals with the appointment and functions of Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Prisons), after subsection (5), insert—

> (5A) Subsections (2) to (5) apply to removal centres (as defined by section 147 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 and including any in Scotland) and persons detained in such removal centres as they apply to prisons and prisoners.
