# Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, section 153A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (172).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/33

## 153A. Detained persons: national minimum wage[^c-204fb84c69ceb6b3035b3c48489991aa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A detained person does not qualify for the national minimum wage in respect of work which he does in pursuance of removal centre rules.
