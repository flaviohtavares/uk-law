# Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, section 157

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (172).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 157. Short-term holding facilities.[^c11434841]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations extend any provision made by or under this Part in relation to removal centres[^c-903d5d9aef735cfa5bba2343c892d424] (other than one mentioned in subsection (2)) to short-term holding facilities.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to section 150.

(3) The Secretary of State may make rules for the regulation and management of short-term holding facilities.
