# Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, section 153

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (172).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 153. Removal centre rules.[^c11434761][^key-f208b085b08c42694c553b87e7228453]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State must make rules for the regulation and management of removal centres.

(2) Removal centre rules may, among other things, make provision with respect to the safety, care, activities, discipline and control of detained persons.
