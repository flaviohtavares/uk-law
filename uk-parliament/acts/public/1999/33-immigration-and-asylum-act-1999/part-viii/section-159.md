# Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, section 159

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (172).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 159. Power of constable to act outside his jurisdiction.[^key-f2e871fac58c1bfa26856cf3271af88c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purpose of taking a person to or from a removal centre[^key-57b6ba32d0c4a83a94503d2db4a6f32c] under the order of any authority competent to give the order, a constable may act outside the area of his jurisdiction.

(2) When acting under this section, the constable concerned retains all the powers, authority, protection and privileges of his office.
