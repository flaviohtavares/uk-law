# Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, section 99

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (172).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Provision of support by local authorities.[^c11433911]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A local authority or Northern Ireland authority[^c18871531] may provide support for persons[^c-ddfaf5041604d38046469b2dfb11f8f1] in accordance with arrangements made by the Secretary of State under section 4,[^c-a70e18fec2226caad1af8488d80e7d54] 95 or 98[^c18871591].

(2)[^c18871611] Support may be provided by an authority in accordance with arrangements made with the authority or with another person.

(3) Support may be provided by an authority in accordance with arrangements made under section 95 only in one or more of the ways mentioned in section 96(1) and (2).

(4) An authority[^c18871631] may incur reasonable expenditure in connection with the preparation of proposals for entering into arrangements under section 4,[^c-135a4c12125395868b303ae6c2c95c45] 95 or 98[^c18871651].

(5) The powers conferred on an authority[^c18871671] by this section include power to—

- (a) provide services outside their area;
- (b) provide services jointly with one or more other bodies[^c18871691];
- (c) form a company for the purpose of providing services;
- (d) tender for contracts (whether alone or with any other person).
