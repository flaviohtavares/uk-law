# Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, section 109A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (172).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/33

## 109A. Arrest[^key-21100f45c716f159bdf1f681c6e29471]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An immigration officer may arrest without warrant a person whom the immigration officer reasonably suspects has committed an offence under section 105 or 106.
