# Immigration and Asylum Act 1999, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (172).** Text as at 2026-04-07. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/33
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Accommodation[^c17546621][^c17632971]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c17632951][^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5] The Secretary of State may provide, or arrange for the provision of, facilities for the accommodation of persons— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]

- (a) temporarily admitted to the United Kingdom under paragraph 21 of Schedule 2 to the 1971 Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]
- (b) released from detention under that paragraph; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]
- (c) released on bail from detention under any provision of the Immigration Acts. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]

(2)[^c17632931] The Secretary of State may provide, or arrange for the provision of, facilities for the accommodation of a person if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]

- (a) he was (but is no longer) an asylum-seeker, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]
- (b) his claim for asylum was rejected *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5] or declared inadmissible (see sections 80A and 80B of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002)[^c-6060f8fc3de561e89befb08486afda30] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5].

(3) The Secretary of State may provide, or arrange for the provision of, facilities for the accommodation of a dependant of a person for whom facilities may be provided under subsection (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]

(4) The following expressions have the same meaning in this section as in Part VI of this Act (as defined in section 94)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]

- (a) asylum-seeker, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]
- (b) claim for asylum, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]
- (c) dependant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]

(5)[^c-3ddcd7b88143231e65236923a4a45272] The Secretary of State may make regulations specifying criteria to be used in determining— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]

- (a) whether or not to provide accommodation, or arrange for the provision of accommodation, for a person under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]
- (b) whether or not to continue to provide accommodation, or arrange for the provision of accommodation, for a person under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]

(6) The regulations may, in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]

- (a) provide for the continuation of the provision of accommodation for a person to be conditional upon his performance of or participation in community activities in accordance with arrangements made by the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]
- (b) provide for the continuation of the provision of accommodation to be subject to other conditions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]
- (c) provide for the provision of accommodation (or the continuation of the provision of accommodation) to be a matter for the Secretary of State’s discretion to a specified extent or in a specified class of case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]

(7) For the purposes of subsection (6)(a)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]

- (a) “**community activities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]**” means activities that appear to the Secretary of State to be beneficial to the public or a section of the public, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]
- (b) the Secretary of State may, in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]
  - (i) appoint one person to supervise or manage the performance of or participation in activities by another person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5] enter into a contract (with a local authority or any other person) for the provision of services by way of making arrangements for community activities in accordance with this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5] pay, or arrange for the payment of, allowances to a person performing or participating in community activities in accordance with arrangements under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]

(8) Regulations by virtue of subsection (6)(a) may, in particular, provide for a condition requiring the performance of or participation in community activities to apply to a person only if the Secretary of State has made arrangements for community activities in an area that includes the place where accommodation is provided for the person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]

(9) A local authority or other person may undertake to manage or participate in arrangements for community activities in accordance with this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]

(10)[^c-48676c2a67a24a34327bd50feeb9797e] The Secretary of State may make regulations permitting a person who is provided with accommodation under this section to be supplied also with services or facilities of a specified kind. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]

(11) Regulations under subsection (10)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]

- (a) may, in particular, permit a person to be supplied with a voucher which may be exchanged for goods or services, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]
- (b) may not permit a person to be supplied with money, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]
- (c) may restrict the extent or value of services or facilities to be provided, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]
- (d) may confer a discretion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13a710cf301cd1a9eb5133b21ba9bee5]
