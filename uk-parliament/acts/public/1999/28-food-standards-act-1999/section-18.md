# Food Standards Act 1999, section 18

> Text as at 2022-06-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Functions under other enactments.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 3 (which contains provisions conferring functions under certain enactments on the Agency) has effect.

(2) Any amendment made by Schedule 3 which extends to Scotland is to be taken as a pre-commencement enactment for the purposes of the [^c1312458]Scotland Act 1998.
