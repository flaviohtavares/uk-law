# Food Standards Act 1999, section 30

> Text as at 2022-06-28.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Animal feedingstuffs: Great Britain.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1] The Ministers may, for the purpose of regulating any animal feedingstuff or anything done to or in relation to, or with a view to the production of, any animal feedingstuff, make an order under this section. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1] An order under this section is one which applies, or makes provision corresponding to, any provisions of the 1990 Act (including any power to make subordinate legislation or to give directions), with or without modifications. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1] Such an order may be made by reference to the 1990 Act as it stands immediately before this Act is passed or as it stands following any amendment or repeal made by this Act. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1] Such an order under this section may make provision with a view to protecting animal health, protecting human health or for any other purpose which appears to the Ministers to be appropriate. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1]

(5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1] The provision which may be made in an order under this section by virtue of section 37(1)(a) includes provision amending or repealing any enactment or subordinate legislation. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1]

(6 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1] Before making such an order, the Ministers shall— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1] consult such organisations as appear to them to be representative of interests likely to be substantially affected by the order; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1] have regard to any advice given by the Agency. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1]

(7 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1] Any consultation undertaken before the commencement of subsection (6) shall be as effective, for the purposes of that subsection, as if undertaken after that commencement; and any consultation undertaken by the Agency may be treated by the Ministers as being as effective for those purposes as if it had been undertaken by them. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1] in the case of an order extending to England and Wales, the Secretary of State *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1] [^c1312474]. . .; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1] in the case of an order extending to Scotland, the Scottish Ministers. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e1ebf50d8f7eeff3362a1bbdf90870b1]
