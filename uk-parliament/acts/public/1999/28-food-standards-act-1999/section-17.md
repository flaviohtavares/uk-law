> Text as at 2022-06-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Delegation of powers to make emergency orders.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077] Arrangements may be made between the Secretary of State and the Agency authorising the Agency to exercise on behalf of the Secretary of State the power to make orders under— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077] section 1(1) of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077] [^c1312456]Food and Environment Protection Act 1985 (emergency orders); and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077] section 13(1) of the 1990 Act (emergency control orders). *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077] The authority given by any such arrangements is subject to any limitations and conditions provided for in the arrangements. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077] Where by virtue of any such arrangements the Agency is authorised to exercise a power, anything done or omitted to be done by the Agency in the exercise or purported exercise of the power shall be treated as done or omitted by the Secretary of State. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077] Nothing in any such arrangements prevents the Secretary of State exercising any power. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077]

(5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077] This section applies with the necessary modifications— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077] to any power mentioned in subsection (1) so far as it is exercisable by the National Assembly for Wales or the Scottish Ministers, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077] to the power of a Northern Ireland Department to make orders under section 1(1) of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077] [^c1312457]Food and Environment Protection Act 1985 or Article 12(1) of the 1991 Order, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077]

as it applies to a power exercisable by the Secretary of State. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d7814f11cd9e3ed4791d70462378077]
