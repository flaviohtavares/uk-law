## 27. Notification of tests for food-borne disease.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f] Regulations may make provision for requiring the notification of information about tests on samples taken from individuals (whether living or dead) for the presence of— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f] organisms of a description specified in the regulations; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f] any substances produced by or in response to the presence of organisms of a description so specified. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f] A description of organisms may be specified in the regulations only if it appears to the authority making the regulations that those organisms or any substances produced by them— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f] are capable of causing disease in humans; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f] are commonly transmitted to humans through the consumption of food. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f] The power to make the regulations is exercisable for the purpose of facilitating the carrying out of functions of the Agency or any other public authority which relate to the protection of public health. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f] The regulations shall, as respects each specified description of organisms— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f] specify the information to be notified about them and the form and manner in which it is to be notified; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f] make provision for identifying the person by whom that information is to be notified; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f] specify the person to whom that information is to be notified; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f]

but the regulations may not require a person to notify information which is not in his possession, or otherwise available to him, by virtue of his position. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f]

- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f] restrict the purposes for which any information may be used (whether by the person so specified or by any other person); *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-644f27304021d2b0bd078d73bf17e52f]
