# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 107

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 107. Judicial review.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Section 66 of the [^c12801941]Local Government Finance Act 1992 (matters which are not to be questioned except on judicial review) shall be amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (2) (which specifies the matters) after paragraph (c) there shall be inserted—

> - (cc) a calculation made in accordance with any of sections 85 to 90 of the Greater London Authority Act 1999;
> - (cd) a substitute calculation made in accordance with any of sections 85, 86 and 88 to 90 of, and Schedule 7 to, that Act;

.
