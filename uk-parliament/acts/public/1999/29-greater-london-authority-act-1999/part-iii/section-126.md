## 126. Interpretation of Chapter IV.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-4e5b18c45850d11542126d492ef69570] In this Chapter—

- “**capital expenditure**” and “**capital receipt**” have the same meaning as in Chapter 1 of Part 1 of the Local Government Act 2003 (capital finance);
- “**capital spending plan**” means a capital spending plan under section 122 above.

(2) References in this Chapter to credit arrangements, or to entering into credit arrangements, shall be construed in accordance with Chapter 1 of Part 1 of the Local Government Act 2003 (see section 7[^key-58b06c68de1696b65ad7cb210565bd34] of that Act).
