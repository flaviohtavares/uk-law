> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 137. Council tax: no Crown exemption for Authority or functional bodies.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Section 19 of the [^c12802541]Local Government Finance Act 1992 (exclusion from Crown exemption in certain cases) shall be amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (3) (which specifies the excluded bodies) after paragraph (b) there shall be inserted—

> - (bb) the Greater London Authority;
> - (bc) any functional body, within the meaning of the Greater London Authority Act 1999;

.
