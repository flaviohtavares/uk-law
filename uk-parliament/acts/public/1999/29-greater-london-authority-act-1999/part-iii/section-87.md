## 87. Procedure for determining the council tax requirements.[^c-a3321d90b92db948b1f25ad50da5c42f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 6 to this Act (which contains procedural requirements for determining the component council tax requirements and the consolidated council tax requirement) shall have effect.
