## 99. Interpretation of Chapter I.[^c12801771][^c-a30853f2730b2e958f1c57f6d81c507e][^c-b9d4d117916802281818a9ee2936292b][^c-58f69270f56a0e61f6e567d1144d1bd2][^c-5c8ea2e43f9b7d210a863a2443afc871][^c-e189452662ba3fd2c6b2860a860c582d][^c-9fb9d3c1e9fe81cb763b7483a418a49b][^c-ab6b6cf3eeb5c19740201aeb9fb57229][^c-3c9c5839871753ac2333ebf05e8d5e6d][^c-7460944bcadfaa0f95f6ddbeecec9684]

In this Part—

- **BID levy[^c-dd7e1dd3dd378d7b8dce6615a626d6ee]**” has the same meaning as in Part 4 of the Local Government Act 2003;
- “ *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-d4a77d9290753d26178933ca293f2661]**budgetary provisions *(Extent: E+W)***” has the meaning given in section 85(14) above; *(Extent: E+W)*
- component council tax[^c-467ce389cbce705ac5e1068bbfb2add2] requirement has the meaning given in section 85(6) above;
- consolidated council tax requirement has the meaning given in section 85(8) above;
- constituent body has the meaning given in section 85(3) above;
- local government finance report means such a report under paragraph 5(1) of Schedule 7B to[^c-75f7d1615986aba6f47c16dab00ea2c5] the [^c12801781]Local Government Finance Act 1988;
