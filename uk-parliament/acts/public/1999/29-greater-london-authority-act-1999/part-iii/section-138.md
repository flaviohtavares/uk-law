# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 138

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 138. No discretionary rate relief for functional bodies.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Section 47 of the [^c12802551]Local Government Finance Act 1988 (discretionary relief) shall be amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (9) (which provides that a hereditament is an excepted hereditament, and accordingly not eligible for relief, if all or part of it is occupied by any body there mentioned) at the end of paragraph (b) (which relates to precepting authorities) there shall be added

> ; or
>
> - (c) a functional body, within the meaning of the Greater London Authority Act 1999

.
