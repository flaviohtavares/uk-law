## 14. Declaration of vacancy in certain cases.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the Mayor—

- (a) ceases to be qualified to be the Mayor,
- (b) becomes disqualified from being the Mayor otherwise than—
  - (i)[^c-4779640cc1c81eedf4b9839176f8fdba] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ia)[^c-0a66961ae321045b174d118bc43c8d68] under section 34 of the Localism Act 2011,
  - (ib)[^c-f298b9c113224cacd5849b3484af8639] by virtue of an order under section 30 of the Elections Act 2022 (disqualification of offenders for holding elective office etc),
  - (ii) by virtue of a conviction, or
  - (iii) by virtue of a breach of any provision of Part II of the Representation of the [^c12800031]People Act 1983, or
- (c) ceases to be the Mayor by reason of failure to attend meetings of the Assembly,

the proper officer of the Authority shall forthwith declare his office to be vacant, unless it has been declared vacant by the High Court.
