# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29

## 13.— Failure to attend meetings.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c12800001] If the Mayor fails on six consecutive occasions to attend meetings of the Assembly held pursuant to section 52(3) below, he shall cease to be the Mayor.

(2)[^c12800011] Any meeting of the Assembly which the Mayor is unable to attend because he is suspended or partially suspended under section 66, [^c-57c4b7fefa9e10b642d4bc26dad58acd]... 73, 78[^c-3a119a6b134de9da95c959e3a39034ba] [^c-57c4b7fefa9e10b642d4bc26dad58acd]... or 79 of the Local Government Act 2000 , or suspended under section 31(4) of the Elections Act 2022,[^c-89e2ac3d48fdbafaddacff71dd5925dc] shall be disregarded for the purposes of subsection (1) above.
