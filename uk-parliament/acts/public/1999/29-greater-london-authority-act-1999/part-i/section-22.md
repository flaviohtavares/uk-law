# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 22. Validity of acts done by unqualified persons.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The acts and proceedings of any person elected to an office under this Act and acting in that office shall, notwithstanding his disqualification or want of qualification, be as valid and effectual as if he had been qualified.
