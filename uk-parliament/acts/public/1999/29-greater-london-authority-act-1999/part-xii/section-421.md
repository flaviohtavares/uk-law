# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 421

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 421. Directions.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any direction given under this Act shall be in writing.

(2) Any power conferred by this Act to give a direction shall include power to vary or revoke the direction.
