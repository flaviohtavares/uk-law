# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 419

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 419. Taxation.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The following bodies, namely—

- (a) Transport for London,
- (b) the Mayor's Office for Policing and Crime[^key-544a2fe25c286f31a145c7106c95da40], and
- (c) the London Fire and Emergency Planning Authority, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8cdab3d60e14d57220410d1608a7e2a0]

  the London Fire Commissioner,

shall each be treated as a local authority for the purposes of the enactments mentioned in subsection (2) below.

(2) The enactments are—

- (a)[^key-b30362680930cf612b4266b551bb0785] section 984 of the Corporation Tax Act 2010 (exemption of local authorities from corporation tax);
- (aa)[^key-f5af254bf357d4abb5a42936b687226c] section 838 of the Income Tax Act 2007 (exemption of local authorities from income tax); and
- (b) section 271 of the [^c12808691]Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992 (exemption of local authorities from capital gains tax).

(3) In sections 170 to 181 of the [^c12808701]Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992 (groups of companies) references to a company do not apply to Transport for London.

(4) Schedule 33 to this Act (which makes further provision about taxation) shall have effect.
