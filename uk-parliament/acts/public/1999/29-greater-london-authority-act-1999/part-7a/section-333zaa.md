>

## 333ZAA. Acquisition of land by agreement[^key-6e32089fed4881f2b8aee95b1ab9b209][^key-c9d70079c6471977e41bcfe904bb1eb9][^key-a761f600a6cde50131649a6bffe0cf99]

*Extent: E+W*

The Authority may acquire land in Greater London by agreement for the purposes of housing or regeneration.
