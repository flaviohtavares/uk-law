## 333D. Duties of the Authority and local authorities[^c-a761f600a6cde50131649a6bffe0cf99][^c-dd7dcba10a35049f17059ec83583b19a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-fa06d419014676c3effb11f97162968d] In exercising any function relating to[^c-f04122af447fb83246d4cb20e6bf8e59] housing or regeneration, the Authority[^c-013ac975cd2f4501cfef2a211a29bd34] shall have regard to the London housing strategy.

(2) Any local housing strategy prepared by a local housing authority in Greater London must be in general conformity with the London housing strategy.

(3) In subsection (2)—

- “**local housing strategy**” means—
  - (a) [^c-3d034a1058b061ab08d8380e41fbac3f]...
  - (b) any [^c-bc34c0a437538693f1f290aa14f21809]... statement of the local housing authority's policies or proposals relating to housing;
- “**local housing authority**” has the same meaning as in section 333A above.
