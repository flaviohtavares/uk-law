## 228. Same person as PPP arbiter and Rail Regulator: duties of staff.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-92811f9848e7ed96acc57ffcec1d0050] Subsections (2) and (3) shall apply while a member of the Office of Rail and Road[^c-956e34a1daa753ead475ba7829a078c1] holds the office of PPP arbiter.

(2) Where this subsection applies, any member of the Office of Rail and Road’s staff may (in addition to discharging duties of that employment) be required also to discharge duties as if he were a member of the PPP arbiter’s staff of similar status.

(3) Where this subsection applies, any member of the PPP arbiter’s staff may (in addition to discharging duties of that employment) be required also to discharge duties as if he were a member of the Office of Rail and Road’s staff of similar status.

(4) Subsections (2) and (3) above apply notwithstanding anything in the terms or conditions of employment of the member of staff concerned.
