# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 192

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 192. Publication.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c12803181] The Mayor shall publish the guidance document.

(2)[^c12803181] Where the Mayor revises the guidance document he shall publish it as revised.

(3)[^c12803181] In this Act, references to the guidance document include, except where the context otherwise requires, a reference to the current version of the guidance document.

(4)[^c12803211] The guidance document must be published no later than 180 days after the Mayor has published the transport strategy prepared by him under section 142(1) above.

(5)[^c12803221] The Mayor shall send to the Common Council and to each London borough council a copy of the current version of the guidance document.

(6) A copy of the current version of the guidance document shall be kept available by the Mayor for inspection by any person on request free of charge—

- (a) at the principal offices of the Authority, and
- (b) at such other places as the Mayor considers appropriate,

at reasonable hours.

(7) A copy of the current version of the guidance document, or any part of it, shall be supplied to any person on request for such reasonable fee as the Mayor may determine.

(8) Any reference in this section to the current version of the guidance document is a reference to the guidance document as last published, whether originally or as revised.
