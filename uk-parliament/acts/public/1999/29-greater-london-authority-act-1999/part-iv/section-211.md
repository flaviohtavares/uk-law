# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 211

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 211. Public sector operators.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this Chapter public sector operator means—

- (a) any Minister of the Crown, government department or other emanation of the Crown;
- (b) any local authority;
- (c) any Integrated Transport Authority for an integrated transport area in England[^c-673c368bc080dbb2c88bb7e506eae80c];
- (ca)[^c-c0baa609edc69ab83b24d8ef39c06607] any combined authority established under section 103 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009;

*Extent: EW*

- (cb)[^c-ab4c08adfa6f6e226ccf1dce1abec514] any combined county authority established under section 9(1) of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023;
- (d) any body corporate whose members are appointed by a Minister of the Crown, a government department, a local authority, an Integrated Transport Authority for an integrated transport area in England[^c-f4aa851a17f5d36bea7caa57e2dadf06] or a combined authority *(Extent: E.W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-794aa56a00faa656ba451d8b3ce75553] , combined authority or combined county authority *(Extent: E.W)* or by a body corporate whose members are so appointed;
- (e) a company—
  - (i) a majority of whose issued shares are held by or on behalf of any of the bodies or persons falling within paragraphs (a) to (d) above;
  - (ii) in which the majority of the voting rights are held by or on behalf of any of those bodies or persons;
  - (iii) a majority of whose board of directors can be appointed or removed by any of those bodies or persons; or
  - (iv) in which the majority of the voting rights are controlled by any of those bodies or persons, pursuant to an agreement with other persons;
- (f) a subsidiary of a company falling within paragraph (e) above.

(2)[^c-887954ae5234e0de9d1f846068e9dbdf] Expressions used in sub-paragraphs (i) to (iv) of subsection (1)(e) above that are defined for the purposes of the Companies Acts (see section 1174 of, and Schedule 8 to, the Companies Act 2006) have the same meaning in those sub-paragraphs.
