# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 246

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 246. Abolition of the London Regional Passengers’ Committee.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 40 of the [^c12805691]London Regional Transport Act 1984 (which established the London Regional Passengers’ Committee) shall cease to have effect.

(2) Any appointment to the London Regional Passengers’ Committee in pursuance of that section shall cease to have effect.
