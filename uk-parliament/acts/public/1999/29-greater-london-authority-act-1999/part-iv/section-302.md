# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 302

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29

## 302. Dissolution of London Regional Transport.[^c12806821]

*Extent: E+W+S*

When the Secretary of State is satisfied that provision has been made for the transfer of all property, rights and liabilities of London Regional Transport, he may by order provide for the dissolution of London Regional Transport.
