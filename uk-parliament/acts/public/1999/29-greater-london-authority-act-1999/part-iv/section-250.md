# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 250

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 250. Recommendations and reports etc.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c12805801] Copies of the minutes, conclusions and recommendations of the Committee with respect to any matter shall be sent—

- (a) to the Assembly,
- (b) to the Mayor, and
- (c) to Transport for London.

(2) The Committee shall make an annual report to the Assembly and the Secretary of State[^c-43d857a58b2c03bf9e68ad94651be2b7] .

(3) Where the Assembly, the Mayor or Transport for London reach a decision with respect to matters dealt with in any recommendation received under subsection (1) above, the decision shall be notified to the Committee.
