## 257. The Woolwich Ferry.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The duty of the Secretary of State under section 16 of the [^c12805941]Metropolitan Board of Works (Various Powers) Act 1885 to work a ferry-boat across the River Thames is transferred to Transport for London by this section.
