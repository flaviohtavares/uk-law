# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 202

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 202. Authority and Transport for London not to be railway franchisees.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Section 25(1) of the [^c12803611]Railways Act 1993 (which prevents public sector operators, as defined in the paragraphs of that subsection, from being franchisees) shall be amended as follows.

(2) After paragraph (b) there shall be inserted—

> - (bb) the Greater London Authority;
> - (bc) Transport for London;

.

(3) In paragraph (d) (bodies corporate whose members are appointed by certain other bodies or persons) after a local authority there shall be inserted “ , the Greater London Authority, Transport for London ”.
