# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 323

## 323. Alteration of the metropolitan police district.

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 76 of the [^c12807191]London Government Act 1963, for subsection (1) (which defines the metropolitan police district so as to include certain areas of Essex, Hertfordshire and Surrey) there shall be substituted—

> (1) The metropolitan police district shall consist of Greater London, excluding the City of London, the Inner Temple and the Middle Temple.
