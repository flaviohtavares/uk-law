## 311. Assimilation of general functions to those of other police authorities.

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 6 of the [^c12806971]Police Act 1996 (general functions of a police authority to include securing the maintenance of an efficient and effective police force for its area) after subsection (4) there shall be inserted—

> (5) This section shall apply in relation to the Metropolitan Police Authority as it applies in relation to a police authority established under section 3.
