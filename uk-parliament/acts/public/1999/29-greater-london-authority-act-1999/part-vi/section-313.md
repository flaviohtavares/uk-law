# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 313

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 313. Openness.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Section 100J of the [^c12806991]Local Government Act 1972 (access to meetings and documents: application of Part VA to certain authorities as to principal councils) shall be amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1) (which lists the bodies to which the Part is applied) after paragraph (e) there shall be inserted—

> - (eza) the Metropolitan Police Authority;

.

(3) In subsection (3) (modifications of section 100A(6)(a) relating to council premises in the case of certain bodies specified in paragraphs of subsection (1)) after “(e),” there shall be inserted “ (eza), ”.

(4) In subsection (4) (application of section 100G(1)(a) relating to registers of members in the [^c12807001]case of certain bodies specified in subsection (1)) in paragraph (a), after “Police Act 1996” there shall be inserted “ or the Metropolitan Police Authority ”.
