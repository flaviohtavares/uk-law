> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 71. Disclosure by employees of interests in contracts.[^c12800811][^c12800821]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 117 of the [^c12800831]Local Government Act 1972 (disclosure by officers of interests in contracts) shall apply in relation to employees of the Authority as if the Authority were a local authority and its employees were officers employed by that local authority.
