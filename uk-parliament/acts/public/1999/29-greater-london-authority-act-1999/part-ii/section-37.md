# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 37. Discharge during vacancy or temporary incapacity of Mayor.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 4 to this Act shall have effect with respect to the discharge of functions during periods when—

- (a) there is a vacancy in the office of Mayor; or
- (b) there is no such vacancy but the Mayor is unable to act in his office.
