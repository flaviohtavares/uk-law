## 80. Public supply or works contracts.

*Extent: E+W+S*

In Schedule 2 to the [^c12801191]Local Government Act 1988 (which specifies the public authorities to which section 17 of that Act applies) after the entry A local authority there shall be inserted—

> The Greater London Authority.
