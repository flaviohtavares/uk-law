# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 355

## 355. Duties of waste collection authorities etc.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-1b5404f4604b2d73c906e2fb09186c2a] In exercising any function under Part II of the [^c12807591]Environmental Protection Act 1990 (waste on land)—

- (a) each of the waste collection authorities in Greater London, and
- (b) each of the waste disposal authorities in Greater London,

shall act in general conformity with[^c-a8fa8393fa50642071f5cf4484ff5cd1] the provisions of the London Environment Strategy dealing with municipal waste management[^c-2e81d933aca2635cdad23e45bdaef362].

*Extent: E+W*

(2)[^c-a11bf74f1b6ea89e736b667444f7ac89] Subsection (1) above has effect only to the extent that compliance by an authority with the requirements of that subsection does not impose excessive additional costs on the authority.

*Extent: E+W*

(3)[^c-dcac0eb1107db443291e3932931c7d0e] For the purposes of this section, the Secretary of State may issue guidance for determining what is to be regarded as—

- (a) acting in general conformity with the provisions of the London Environment Strategy dealing with municipal waste management[^c-ee77fb24c9ce08acf533a4c458d0510e], or
- (b) imposing excessive additional costs on an authority.

(4 (Extent: E+W)) In discharging the duties imposed upon it by subsection (1) above (as read with subsection (2) above), an authority must act in accordance with any guidance issued under subsection (3) above. *(Extent: E+W)*

(5 (Extent: E+W)) Any guidance issued under subsection (3) above shall be published by the Secretary of State in such manner as he considers appropriate. *(Extent: E+W)*

(6 (Extent: E+W)) Nothing in this section, or in any guidance issued under it, requires an authority— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) to terminate a waste contract before the expiry of the term of the contract, or *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) to do anything which would result in a breach of any term of a waste contract. *(Extent: E+W)*

(7 (Extent: E+W))[^c-0812f0edb1822690dc25c00a531cd1a3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W)*

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(8)[^c-72ad652d9523f8390d58b0c4d1ed4504] In any case where—

- (a) an authority is required to comply with the Procurement Act 2023[^c-1e1306c10d3fbf8bded69b03fe3f7a5f] in the awarding of a waste contract,
- (b)[^c-7e8b104c054ec2a236abf583f4e71fec] in compliance with that Act the authority publishes or provides the tender notice relating to the awarding of that contract, and
- (c) after the authority publishes or provides[^c-cd3a05462bae05d34571a4bb87feeb0c] that notice, the Mayor revises the provisions of the London Environment Strategy dealing with municipal waste management,
