## 364. Duty of local authorities in Greater London.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In exercising any function under Part IV of the [^c12807761]Environment Act 1995 (air quality) a local authority in Greater London shall have regard to the provisions of the London Environment Strategy dealing with air quality[^key-c35688f4d704a2f4ffd10ab24a54569b].
