# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 369

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 369. Consultation with the Mayor.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In paragraph 1 of Schedule 11 to the [^c12807811]Environment Act 1995 (duty of local authorities to consult certain persons in the exercise of their functions under Part IV of that Act), after sub-paragraph (2) there shall be inserted—

> (2A) A local authority specified in sub-paragraph (2B) below shall in carrying out the functions falling within sub-paragraph (1)(a) to (c) above also consult the Mayor of London.
>
> (2B) The local authorities mentioned in sub-paragraph (2A) above are—
>
> - (a) any local authority in Greater London,
> - (b) any local authority whose area is contiguous to the area of Greater London.
