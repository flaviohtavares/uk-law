## 352. The London Environment Strategy: biodiversity.[^key-d2244251bd20fc02fa5feb89ce16f3ea]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-aaa004ad14e8309c1c7ec09a7c27b3cc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The provisions of the London Environment Strategy dealing with biodiversity[^key-e154c95c21907fe700b134aebc7477ff] shall contain information about—

- (a) the ecology of Greater London,
- (b) the wildlife of Greater London and its habitat,
- (c) any proposals for the conservation and promotion by the Mayor of biodiversity within Greater London, which have been agreed between the Mayor and any person or body he has consulted or intends to consult[^key-563e6910f8b34c4ca78f7013a3192a1f] in relation to those provisions of the London Environment Strategy, and
- (d) any commitments as to the conservation and promotion of biodiversity within Greater London made by any person or body who who the Mayor has consulted or intends to consult[^key-00add3dbf4ae7a08b572665f1947f10a] in relation to those provisions of the London Environment Strategy.

(3)[^key-637042e6d3998fe985f8376569d3ce18] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) In preparing or revising the the provisions of the London Environment Strategy dealing with biodiversity[^key-5131103ecaf80abe2a4f88025cf60755] the Mayor shall also—

- (a) have regard to any plans relating to biodiversity prepared by a London borough council or the Common Council, [^key-772c52b203dc51c1e9f09014c9f5971a]...
- (b)[^key-772c52b203dc51c1e9f09014c9f5971a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-4946fca48c591682cbc9d78516f5f164] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
