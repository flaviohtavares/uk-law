# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 354

## 354. Directions by the Secretary of State.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where the Secretary of State considers that the condition[^c-08072f7b76377d2f3349310d84031555] specified in subsection (2) below is satisfied, he may give the Mayor a direction about the content of the provisions of the London Environment Strategy dealing with municipal waste management[^c-502b85e261f2abc6b8dab8219c5094fd].

(2) The conditions mentioned in subsection (1) above are—

- (a)[^c-0ea13f8877244cb90dc18dae3ecbaba1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) that a direction about the content of the provisions of the London Environment Strategy dealing with municipal waste management[^c-6674013af8d29def9f10602f7e542657] is required for the purposes of the implementation of the policies contained in the national waste management plan *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-145759d208bdfe4571662737884c44a0] or of the policies contained in the strategy required by section 17 of the Waste and Emissions Trading Act 2003 (landfill strategy for England) *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-326f5f380d6ce3eccf8ad95855b3e709] .

(3) The power of the Secretary of State to give a direction to the Mayor under subsection (1) above—

- (a) may be exercised either generally or specially, and
- (b) may only be exercised after consultation with the Mayor.

(4) Where the Secretary of State gives the Mayor a direction under subsection (1) above, the Mayor shall comply with the direction.
