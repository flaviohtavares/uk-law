# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 356

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29

## 356. Directions by the Mayor.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where the Mayor considers that it is necessary for the purposes of the implementation of the provisions of the London Environment Strategy dealing with municipal waste management[^key-d8bf706e035fe0c36696b11b3731f975], he may give to a waste collection authority in Greater London, or a waste disposal authority in Greater London, a direction requiring the authority to exercise a function in a manner specified in the direction.

(2) The Mayor may not give to an authority a direction under subsection (1) above requiring the authority—

- (a) to terminate a waste contract before the expiry of the term of the contract; or
- (b) to do anything which would result in a breach of any term of a waste contract.

(3)[^key-f39b45726e836fdfb248a1396d6a9ffa] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(3A)[^key-e56536ecbe53ee4eca44862a4c75046c] The Mayor may not give to an authority a direction under subsection (1) above requiring the authority to exercise a function in relation to the awarding of a waste contract if—

- (a) the authority is required to comply with the Procurement Act 2023[^key-3b9dd5f75e9187efa983c89f0a089357] in awarding that contract, and
- (b)[^key-20a720a6e08fc9f59cbc0c15ab6b57cb] in compliance with that Act the authority publishes or provides the tender notice relating to the awarding of that contract.

(4 (Extent: E+W+N.I.)) The power of the Mayor to give a direction to an authority under subsection (1) above— *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)*

- (a (Extent: E+W+N.I.)) may be exercised either generally or specially, and *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)*
- (b (Extent: E+W+N.I.)) may only be exercised after consultation with the authority concerned. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)*

(5 (Extent: E+W+N.I.)) Where the Mayor gives an authority a direction under subsection (1) above, the authority to whom the direction is given shall comply with the direction. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)*
