# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 363

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 363. Directions by the Secretary of State.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where the Secretary of State considers that the condition[^c-04a1ba5101f217e834d27321d09672b6] specified in subsection (2) below is satisfied, he may give the Mayor a direction about the content of the London air quality strategy.

(2) The conditions mentioned in subsection (1) above are—

- (a)[^c-0a0c5b483a4482dc98cbe3aa6814816d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) that a direction about the content of the provisions of the London Environment Strategy dealing with air quality[^c-4af823fe8c1f669a15d026654e4da9c6] is required for the purposes of the implementation of the policies contained in the strategy prepared and published by the Secretary of State in accordance with section 80 of the [^c12807751]Environment Act 1995 (national air quality strategy).

(3) The power of the Secretary of State to give a direction to the Mayor under subsection (1) above—

- (a) may be exercised either generally or specially, and
- (b) may only be exercised after consultation with the Mayor.

(4) Where the Secretary of State gives the Mayor a direction under subsection (1) above, the Mayor shall comply with the direction.
