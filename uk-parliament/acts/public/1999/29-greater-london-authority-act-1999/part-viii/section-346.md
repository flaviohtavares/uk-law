# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 346

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 346. Monitoring and data collection.

*Extent: E+W+S*

It shall be the duty of the Mayor—

- (a) to monitor the implementation of the spatial development strategy;
- (b) to monitor the local plan, any document which is or forms part of a minerals and waste plan and any supplementary plans[^c-42d0cb7b175d288ed33ac7ff1162cf80][^c-7a7d6e5734d0d59c4ea8dfd526eeb772] (within the meaning of Part 2 of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004) of each London borough council; and
- (c) to monitor, and collect information about, matters relevant to the preparation, review, alteration, replacement or implementation of the spatial development strategy.
