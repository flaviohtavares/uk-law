## 349. Abolition of joint planning committee for Greater London.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The joint planning committee for Greater London established under section 5 of the [^c12807521]Local Government Act 1985 and continued by section 3 of the [^c12807531]Town and Country Planning Act 1990 is abolished by this section.
