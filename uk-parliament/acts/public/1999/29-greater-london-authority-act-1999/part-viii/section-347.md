# Greater London Authority Act 1999, section 347

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (55).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 347. Functional bodies to have regard to the strategy.

*Extent: E+W+S*

In exercising any function, each of the functional bodies shall have regard to the spatial development strategy , but this is without prejudice to[^c-cf5dd113e5aa04f4750d21d2f76d7555] sections 15CA(2) and 15CC(7)[^c-024731cdc77b7dc2b5369c984770f3e6] of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 (which require local plans, minerals and waste plans and supplementary plans[^c-f23c3a2ede51e35508cc8f70a98255bf] to be in general conformity with the strategy).
