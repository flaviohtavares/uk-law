## 3. Duty of employers to facilitate access to stakeholder pension schemes.[^c1314192]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Except in so far as regulations otherwise provide, it shall be the duty of an employer of relevant employees to comply with the requirement in subsection (5)[^key-4749d67038c7b6e534ee4c460cb35624].

(1A)[^key-2876bd4e16361926e76297d6dcaa5302] A relevant employee, in relation to an employer, is an employee of the employer who, on the relevant date, satisfies the conditions in subsection (1B).

(1B) The conditions are that—

- (a) the employee is a member of a stakeholder pension scheme;
- (b) the employee made a request under subsection (5) before the relevant date and that request has not been withdrawn;
- (c) the employee pays contributions (which are deducted in accordance with that request) to the scheme at regular intervals;
- (d) at least one deduction has been made before the relevant date in accordance with that request.

(1C) A person ceases to be a relevant employee—

- (a) on ceasing to be employed by the employer;
- (b) on withdrawing a request under subsection (5);
- (c) on ceasing to pay contributions at regular intervals.

(2)[^key-a5e17b1f3d9b6aecd52ef5d45e7240d3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-a5e17b1f3d9b6aecd52ef5d45e7240d3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-a5e17b1f3d9b6aecd52ef5d45e7240d3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) The [^key-1e79c7c4505a0ea5dc1197e8664af137]... requirement is that, subject to such exceptions and qualifications as may be prescribed, the employer shall, if he is requested to do so by a relevant employee [^key-eca4b481f9eae8075c17dca863ef9658]...—

- (a) deduct the employee’s contributions to the stakeholder pension scheme[^key-32c77a4eeab9b8135b3a22ba1c6d93fc] from his remuneration; and
- (b) pay them to the trustees or managers of the scheme or, if regulations so provide, to a prescribed person.

(5A)[^key-9791f8382a380cd23e4485294fcf11c2] That requirement only applies in relation to a request to make deductions made before the relevant date (whether or not that request is varied after that date).

(6)[^key-9a34c12e93aed7c5b4ec896a63fa2195] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) Section 10 of the 1995 Act (civil penalties) applies to an employer who fails to comply with the requirement[^key-f72ede4cc9d58f1bfa428e0c7c27c399] set out above.

(8) An employer is not, while subject to the requirement in subsection (5)[^key-a913b2ab808e9969d40a455b4b1de0c4], under any duty—

- (a) to make any enquiries, or act on any information, about the scheme for any purpose not connected with—
  - (i) ascertaining whether the scheme is for the time being registered under section 2,
  - (ii)[^key-02346e4eee6be6f3eea681dd4d253d2b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii)[^key-dc5ba38351ab7d546f38be2ab1dd0446] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) in particular, to investigate or monitor, or make any judgment as to, the past, present or future performance of the scheme.

(9) In this section—

- “**employer**” means any employer, whether or not resident or incorporated in any part of the United Kingdom;
- “[^key-8cca518ab27e1852084be4481cc5aea1] **relevant date** ” means the date on which section 87 of the Pensions Act 2008 comes into force.
