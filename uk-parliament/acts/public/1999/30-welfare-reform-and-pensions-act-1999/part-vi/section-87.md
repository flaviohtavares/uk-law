# Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999, section 87

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (41).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/30

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Corresponding provisions for Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

An Order in Council under paragraph 1(1)(b) of Schedule 1 to the [^c1314394]Northern Ireland Act 1974 (legislation for Northern Ireland in the interim period) which contains a statement that it is made only for purposes corresponding to those of this Act—

- (a) shall not be subject to paragraph 1(4) and (5) of that Schedule (affirmative resolution of both Houses of Parliament), but
- (b) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
