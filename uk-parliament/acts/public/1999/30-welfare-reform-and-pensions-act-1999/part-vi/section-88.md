# Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999, section 88

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (41).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/30
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Repeals.[^c1314395]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The enactments specified in Schedule 13 (which include certain enactments no longer of practical utility) are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
