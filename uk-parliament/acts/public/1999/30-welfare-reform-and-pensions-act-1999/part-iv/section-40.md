## 40. Other pension schemes.[^c1314299][^c-6f10ff536cd0494d9d204e72ec6f200c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision for a pension to which subsection (2) applies to be increased, as a minimum, by reference to relevant percentage increases[^c-27abfcf73b59fed26c6e84b28c963a51], so far as not exceeding the maximum percentage[^c-8a06b9f7df59f0d5f8c059a3f10940fe] per annum.

(2) Subject to subsection (2A), this[^c-3032b6f0a20efa285742a12dbe14ed1a] subsection applies to—

- (a) a pension provided to give effect to eligible pension credit rights of a member under a qualifying occupational pension scheme, [^c-7759689cc0a3fcaedef26a910a86981b]...
- (b)[^c-7759689cc0a3fcaedef26a910a86981b][^c-7759689cc0a3fcaedef26a910a86981b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A)[^c-8f969cd2b3586c0daac8b8cc9c95104d] Subsection (2) does not apply to pensions which—

- (a) are money purchase benefits, and
- (b) become pensions in payment on or after the commencement day.

(2AA)[^c-fb5ee04a1509c56387ca2eac8abbbe62] In subsection (1) “ **relevant percentage increases** ” means percentage increases estimated by the Secretary of State from time to time for the purposes of paragraph 2(3)(a) of Schedule 3 to the Pension Schemes Act 1993 for revaluation periods of 12 months.

(2B) For the purposes of subsection (1) the “ **maximum percentage** ” means—

- (a) 5% in a case where—
  - (i) the pension is in payment before the commencement day, or
  - (ii) the pension is not in payment before the commencement day but the entitlement to the relevant pension credit arose before that day, and
- (b) 2.5% in a case where the entitlement to the relevant pension credit arises on or after the commencement day.

(3)[^c-74f10551720c155ffda1143b48fa2746] In this section—

- “[^c-83367176f90ebeae4c85bb6a0fb66056] **commencement day** ” means the day appointed for the coming into force of section 280 of the Pensions Act 2004 (amendments to section 40);
- “**eligible**”, in relation to pension credit rights, means of a description prescribed by regulations made by the Secretary of State;
- “ **money purchase benefit** ” has the meaning given by section 181(1) of the Pension Schemes Act 1993;
- “**pension credit rights**”, in relation to an occupational pension scheme, means rights to future benefits under the scheme which are attributable (directly or indirectly) to a credit under section 29(1)(b) or under corresponding Northern Ireland legislation;
- “**qualifying occupational pension scheme**” means an occupational pension scheme which is not a public service pension scheme;
- “ **relevant pension credit** ” means the pension credit to which the eligible pension credit rights [^c-a19497fecfbc422ec43cb61683af8270]... are (directly or indirectly) attributable;
- “**safeguarded rights**” has the meaning given in section 68A of the [^c1314300]Pension Schemes Act 1993.
