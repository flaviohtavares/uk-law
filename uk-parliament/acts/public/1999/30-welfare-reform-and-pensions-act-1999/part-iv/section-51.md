# Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (41).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/30
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 51. Interpretation of Chapter II.[^c1314336][^c-2c05d80e180d4c302731ad682df897dd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-6b6814c188b36077287aefae1501d794] In this Chapter—

- “shareable state scheme rights”, and related expressions, have the meaning given by section 47;[^c-a0424a12c77e09bf4bc849bad98186bc]
- “**tax year**” has the meaning given by section 122(1) of the Contributions and Benefits Act.

(2)[^c-9de6b52a05bb1d7ea5b351bd803a540a] For the purposes of this Chapter—

- (a) a person is in the old state pension system if the person reached pensionable age before 6 April 2016 (or would have done so if the person had lived until pensionable age), and
- (b) a person is in the new state pension system if the person reached pensionable age on or after 6 April 2016 (or will do so if the person lives until pensionable age).
