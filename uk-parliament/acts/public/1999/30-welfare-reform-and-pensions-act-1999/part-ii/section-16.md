## 16. Excessive pension contributions made by persons who have become bankrupt: Scotland.[^c1314228]

*Extent: S*

For sections 36A to 36C of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606] [^c1314229]Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 1985 there shall be substituted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]

> **36A. Recovery of excessive pension contributions. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]**
>
> (1) Where a debtor’s estate has been sequestrated and he— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> - (a) has rights under an approved pension arrangement, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (b) has excluded rights under an unapproved pension arrangement, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> the permanent trustee may apply to the court for an order under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (2) If the court is satisfied— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> - (a) that the rights under the arrangement are to any extent, and whether directly or indirectly, the fruits of relevant contributions, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (b) that the making of any of the relevant contributions (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]**the excessive contributions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]**”) has unfairly prejudiced the debtor’s creditors, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> the court may make such order as it thinks fit for restoring the position to what it would have been had the excessive contributions not been made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (3) Subsection (4) applies where the court is satisfied that the value of the rights under the arrangement is, as a result of rights of the debtor under the arrangement or any other pension arrangement having at any time become subject to a debit under section 29(1)(a) of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999 (debits giving effect to pension-sharing), less than it would otherwise have been. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (4) Where this subsection applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> - (a) any relevant contributions which were represented by the rights which became subject to the debit shall, for the purposes of subsection (2), be taken to be contributions of which the rights under the arrangement are the fruits, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (b) where the relevant contributions represented by the rights under the arrangement (including those so represented by virtue of paragraph (a)) are not all excessive contributions, relevant contributions which are represented by the rights under the arrangement otherwise than by virtue of paragraph (a) shall be treated as excessive contributions before any which are so represented by virtue of that paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (5) In subsections (2) to (4) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]**relevant contributions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]**” means contributions to the arrangement or any other pension arrangement— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> - (a) which the debtor has at any time made on his own behalf, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (b) which have at any time been made on his behalf. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (6) The court shall, in determining whether it is satisfied under subsection (2)(b), consider in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> - (a) whether any of the contributions were made for the purpose of putting assets beyond the reach of the debtor’s creditors or any of them, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (b) whether the total amount of any contributions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>   - (i) made by or on behalf of the debtor to pension arrangements, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606] represented (whether directly or indirectly) by rights under approved pension arrangements or excluded rights under unapproved pensions arrangements, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
>   is an amount which is excessive in view of the debtor’s circumstances when those contributions were made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (7) For the purposes of this section and sections 36B and 36C (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]**the recovery provisions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]**”), rights of a debtor under an unapproved pension arrangement are excluded rights if they are rights which are excluded from his estate by virtue of regulations under section 12 of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (8) In the recovery provisions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> - “**approved pension arrangement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]**” has the same meaning as in section 11 of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - “**unapproved pension arrangement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]**” has the same meaning as in section 12 of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> **36B. Orders under section 36A. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]**
>
> (1) Without prejudice to the generality of section 36A(2) an order under section 36A may include provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> - (a) requiring the person responsible for the arrangement to pay an amount to the permanent trustee, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (b) adjusting the liabilities of the arrangement in respect of the debtor, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (c) adjusting any liabilities of the arrangement in respect of any other person that derive, directly or indirectly, from rights of the debtor under the arrangement, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (d) for the recovery by the person responsible for the arrangement (whether by deduction from any amount which that person is ordered to pay or otherwise) of costs incurred by that person in complying in the debtor’s case with any requirement under section 36C(1) or in giving effect to the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (2) In subsection (1), references to adjusting the liabilities of the arrangement in respect of a person include (in particular) reducing the amount of any benefit or future benefit to which that person is entitled under the arrangement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (3) In subsection (1)(c), the reference to liabilities of the arrangement does not include liabilities in respect of a person which result from giving effect to an order or provision falling within section 28(1) of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999 (pension sharing orders and agreements). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (4) The maximum amount which the person responsible for an arrangement may be required to pay by an order under section 36A is the lesser of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> - (a) the amount of the excessive contributions, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (b) the value of the debtor’s rights under the arrangement (if the arrangement is an approved pension arrangement) or of his excluded rights under the arrangement (if the arrangement is an unapproved pension arrangement). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (5) An order under section 36A which requires the person responsible for an arrangement to pay an amount (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]**the restoration amount *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]**”) to the permanent trustee must provide for the liabilities of the arrangement to be correspondingly reduced. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (6) For the purposes of subsection (5), liabilities are correspondingly reduced if the difference between— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> - (a) the amount of the liabilities immediately before the reduction, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (b) the amount of the liabilities immediately after the reduction, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> is equal to the restoration amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (7) An order under section 36A in respect of an arrangement— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> - (a) shall be binding on the person responsible for the arrangement; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (b) overrides provisions of the arrangement to the extent that they conflict with the provisions of the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> **36C. Orders under section 36A: supplementary. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]**
>
> (1) The person responsible for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> - (a) an approved pension arrangement under which a debtor has rights, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (b) an unapproved pension arrangement under which a debtor has excluded rights, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (c) a pension arrangement under which a debtor has at any time had rights, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> shall, on the permanent trustee making a written request, provide the permanent trustee with such information about the arrangement and rights as the permanent trustee may reasonably require for, or in connection with, the making of applications under section 36A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (2) Nothing in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> - (a) any provision of section 159 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606] [^c1314230]Pensions Schemes Act 1993 or section 91 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606] [^c1314231]Pensions Act 1995 (which prevent assignation and the making of orders that restrain a person from receiving anything which he is prevented from assigning), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (b) any provision of any enactment (whether passed or made before or after the passing of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999) corresponding to any of the provisions mentioned in paragraph (a), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (c) any provision of the arrangement in question corresponding to any of those provisions, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> applies to a court exercising its powers under section 36A. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (3) Where any sum is required by an order under section 36A to be paid to the permanent trustee, that sum shall be comprised in the debtor’s estate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (4) Regulations may, for the purposes of the recovery provisions, make provision about the calculation and verification of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> - (a) any such value as is mentioned in section 36B(4)(b); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (b) any such amounts as are mentioned in section 36B(6)(a) and (b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (5) The power conferred by subsection (4) includes power to provide for calculation or verification— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> - (a) in such manner as may, in the particular case, be approved by a prescribed person; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (b) in accordance with guidance— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>   - (i) from time to time prepared by a prescribed person, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606] approved by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (6) References in the recovery provisions to the person responsible for a pension arrangement are to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> - (a) the trustees, managers or provider of the arrangement, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - (b) the person having functions in relation to the arrangement corresponding to those of a trustee, manager or provider. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (7) In this section and sections 36A and 36B— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> - “**the recovery provisions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]**” means this section and sections 36A and 36B; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
> - “**regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]**” means regulations made by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
>
> (8) Regulations under the recovery provisions may contain such incidental, supplemental and transitional provisions as appear to the Secretary of State necessary or expedient. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0216efbe416da5d0d9a42c245f1f1606]
