# Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc. ) Act 1999, section 27

> Text as at 2026-07-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act, unless a contrary intention appears—

- “**the Board**” means the Commissioners of Inland Revenue;
- “**contributions**” means contributions under Part I of the [^c11211141]Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992.
