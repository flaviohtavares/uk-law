# Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc. ) Act 1999, section 5

> Text as at 2026-07-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Powers relating to enforcement.[^c11210081]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 5 to this Act (which relates to the enforcement powers of the Board in relation to functions transferred to them by this Act) shall have effect.
