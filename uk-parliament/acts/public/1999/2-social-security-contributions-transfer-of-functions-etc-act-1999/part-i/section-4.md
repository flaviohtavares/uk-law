# Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc. ) Act 1999, section 4

> Text as at 2026-07-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Recovery of contributions where income tax recovery provisions not applicable.[^c11210011]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provisions of Schedule 4 shall have effect with respect to the recovery of—

- (a) those Class 1, Class 1A and Class 1B[^key-c8e613afd4ef3c86265711636fb42f18] contributions to which regulations under paragraph 6 or 7BZA[^c21652511] of Schedule 1 to the [^c11210021]Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 or paragraph 6[^c11210031] or 7BZA of Schedule 1 to the [^c11210041]Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 (power to combine collection of contributions with income tax) do not apply,
- (aa)[^key-09e31b1eb265648f673766b042e4cc07] those Class 2 contributions in relation to which—
  - (i) the regulations mentioned in paragraph (a), and
  - (ii) Part 6 of the Taxes Management Act 1970 (collection and recovery),

  do not apply,
- (b) [^c21652541]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) interest or penalties payable under regulations made under paragraph 7A [^c21652781]. . . of Schedule 1 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 or paragraph 7A[^c11210071] [^c21652781]. . . of Schedule 1 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, and[^c21652811]
- (d) interest or penalties—
  - (i) payable under regulations made under paragraph 7B of Schedule 1 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 and to which regulations under paragraph 7BZA of that Schedule do not apply, or
  - (ii) payable under regulations made under paragraph 7B of Schedule 1 to the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 and to which regulations under paragraph 7BZA of that Schedule do not apply.
