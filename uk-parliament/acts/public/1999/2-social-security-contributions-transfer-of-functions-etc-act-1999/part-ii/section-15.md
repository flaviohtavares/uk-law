# Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc. ) Act 1999, section 15

> Text as at 2026-07-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15. Power to make provision for period before commencement of new social security appeal provisions.[^c11210681]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations modify any of the enactments to which this subsection applies during any period in which section 8 of this Act is in force but Chapter II of Part I of the [^c11210691]Social Security Act 1998 (social security decisions and appeals) is not yet wholly in force.

(2) Subsection (1) above applies to—

- (a) Part II of the [^c11210701]Social Security Administration Act 1992 (adjudication), and
- (b) the Acts amended by section 16 of, and Schedule 7 to, this Act.
