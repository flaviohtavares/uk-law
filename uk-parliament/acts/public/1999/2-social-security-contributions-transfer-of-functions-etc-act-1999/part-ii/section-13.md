# Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc. ) Act 1999, section 13

> Text as at 2026-07-08.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 13. Regulations with respect to appeals.[^c11210651]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c11210661] The Board may, by regulations made with the concurrence of the Lord Chancellor and the Lord Advocate, make provision with respect to appeals to the tribunal[^c21649651] under this Part.

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) above may, in particular—

- (a) make provision with respect to any of the matters dealt with in the following provisions of the Taxes Management Act 1970 —
  - (i) [^c21649671]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii) sections 48 to 54 (appeals to the tribunal[^c21649741] under the Taxes Acts), and
  - (iii) section 56 (payment of tax where there is a further appeal)[^c21649761], or
- (b) provide for any of those provisions of that Act to apply, with such modifications as may be specified in the regulations, in relation to an appeal to the tribunal[^c21649861] under this Part.

(2A)[^c21649941] Regulations under subsection (1) above may provide for sections 11(2) and 13(2) of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 [^c21649981] to apply with such modifications as may be specified in the regulations in relation to an appeal to the tribunal under this Part.
