# Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc. ) Act 1999, section 9

> Text as at 2026-07-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Regulations with respect to decisions.[^c11210421]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Part and of the Social Security Administration Act 1992, provision may be made by the Board by regulations as to the making by their officer of any decision under or in connection with the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, the Social Security Administration Act 1992 or the [^c11210431]Jobseekers Act 1995 which falls to be made by such an officer.

(2) Where it appears to an officer of the Board that a matter before him involves a question of fact requiring special expertise, he may direct that in dealing with that matter he shall have the assistance of one or more experts.

(3) In subsection (2) above “**expert**” means a person appearing to the officer of the Board to have knowledge or experience which would be relevant in determining the question of fact requiring special expertise.
