## 64. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act—

- [^c21047051]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**the 1978 Act**” means the [^c4667171]National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978,
- [^c21047061]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^c21035511]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**enactment**” includes an enactment whenever passed or made,
- “**NHS trust**” has the same meaning—
  - (a) [^c21047081]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (b) in Part II of this Act as in the 1978 Act.
