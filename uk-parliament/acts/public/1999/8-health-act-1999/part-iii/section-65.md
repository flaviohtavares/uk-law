# Health Act 1999, section 65

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2022-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Amendments and repeals.[^c4667191]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 4 (amendments of enactments) is to have effect.

(2) The repeals set out in Schedule 5 (which include the repeal of an enactment which is spent) are to have effect.
