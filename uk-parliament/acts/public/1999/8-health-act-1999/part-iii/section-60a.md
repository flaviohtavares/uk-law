# Health Act 1999, section 60A

## 60A. Standard of proof in fitness to practise proceedings[^c21251471]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The standard of proof applicable to any proceedings to which this subsection applies is that applicable to civil proceedings.

(2) Subsection (1) applies to any proceedings before—

- (a)[^c-881c4ed639bfaeb3fa22bb907d3cbfe5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) a committee of a regulatory body, a regulatory body itself or any officer of a regulatory body,

which relate to a matter specified in subsection (2A)[^c-0e2f0c4bab7623ae6c24a69ad154bcfc].

(2A)[^c-0cfafd8e7dc0c86efb129f43940d02d9] The matters are—

- (a) a person's fitness to practise a profession to which section 60(2) applies;
- (b)[^c-ed40101f274a98486252ec0ff29a6299] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) a person's suitability to remain registered as a social care worker in England (within the meaning given by section 60[^c-002ed3061f52e505aad06ce3df4b92bb]).

(3) In subsection (2) “**regulatory body**” means the body (or main body) responsible for the regulation of a profession to which section 60(2) applies [^c-93374f91b0cb0783b467da4b48409687]... .

(4) An Order in Council under section 60 may not—

- (a) amend this section, or
- (b) make any provision that is inconsistent with subsection (1).
