# Health Act 1999, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2022-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Repeal of law about fund-holding practices.[^c4666231]

*Extent: E+W*

In the [^c4666241]National Health Service and Community Care Act 1990, sections 14 to 17 (which make provision in relation to fund-holding practices) are to cease to have effect.
