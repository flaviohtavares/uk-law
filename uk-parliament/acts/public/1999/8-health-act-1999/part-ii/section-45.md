# Health Act 1999, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2022-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Repeal of law about fund-holding practices.

*Extent: S*

In the [^c4666991]National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, sections 87A to 87C (which make provision in relation to fund-holding practices in Scotland) are to cease to have effect.
