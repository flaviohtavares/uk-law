# Finance Act 1999, section 103

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 103. Business assets: roll-over relief.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 193 of the [^c1126321]Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992 (roll-over relief not available for gains on oil licences) shall cease to have effect.

(2) This section has effect in relation to—

- (a) a disposal of a licence or an interest in a licence which occurs on or after 1st July 1999;
- (b) an acquisition of a licence or an interest in a licence which occurs on or after 1st July 1999.
