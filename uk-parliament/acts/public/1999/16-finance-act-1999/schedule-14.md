## SCHEDULE 14 — Stamp duty: amendments consequential on section 112

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 112(4).

### General amendments

**1.** (1) Any reference (express or implied) in any enactment, instrument or other document to any of the headings in Schedule 1 to the [^c1126490]Stamp Act 1891 (other than the heading “**Bearer Instrument**”) shall be construed, so far as is required for continuing its effect, as being or, as the case may require, including a reference to the corresponding provision of Schedule 13 to this Act.

(2) Sub-paragraph (1)—

- (a) has effect subject to any express amendment made by this Act, and
- (b) is without prejudice to the general application of section 17(2) of the [^c1126491]Interpretation Act 1978 (general effect of repeal and re-enactment).

**2.** In the enactments relating to stamp duty for “lease or tack”, wherever occurring, substitute “ lease ”.
