# Finance Act 1999, section 131

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131. Economic and monetary union: taxes and duties.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commissioners of Inland Revenue and the Commissioners of Customs and Excise may incur expenditure in order to secure that, if the United Kingdom were to move to the third stage of economic and monetary union, they would be able to exercise their functions relating to taxes and duties (including agricultural levies of the European Union[^key-f7ee7a0e6dee750a9d87622b8df7de8e] ).
