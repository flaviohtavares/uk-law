> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/16

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Preparations etc. of meat, yeast or egg.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 8 to the [^c1126217]Value Added Tax Act 1994 (zero-rating) shall have effect, and be deemed always to have had effect, as if in Group 1 (food), in Note (6) (which provides that certain items which override the exceptions listed in that Group relate only to item 4 of the excepted items (non-alcoholic beverages)) for “Items 4 to 6” there were substituted “ Items 4 to 7 ”.
