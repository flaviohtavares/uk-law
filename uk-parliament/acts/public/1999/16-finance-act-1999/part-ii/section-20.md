# Finance Act 1999, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Meaning of “business”.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 94(3) of the Value Added Tax Act 1994 (meaning of “**business**”: public organisations) shall cease to have effect.

(2)[^c1126223] This section shall come into force in accordance with such provision as the Commissioners of Customs and Excise may make by order made by statutory instrument.
