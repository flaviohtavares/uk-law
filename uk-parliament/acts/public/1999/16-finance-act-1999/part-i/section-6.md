# Finance Act 1999, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Rate of pool betting duty.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 7(1) of the [^c1126208]Betting and Gaming Duties Act 1981 (rate of pool betting duty), for “26.50 per cent.” there shall be substituted “ 17.50 per cent. ”

(2) This section has effect in relation to bets the stake money on which is or has been paid on or after 28th March 1999.
