# Finance Act 1999, section 124

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 124. Rate of landfill tax.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 42 of the [^c1126373]Finance Act 1996 (amount of landfill tax), in subsections (1)(a) and (2), for “£7”, in each place where it occurs, there shall be substituted “ £10 ”.

(2) This section has effect in relation to taxable disposals made, or treated as made, on or after 1st April 1999.
