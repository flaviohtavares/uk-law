## 6. Unfair dismissal connected with recognition: interim relief.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

In sections 128(1)(b) and 129(1) of the [^c12486091]Employment Rights Act 1996 (interim relief) after “103” there shall be inserted “ or in paragraph 161(2) of Schedule A1 to the [^c12486101]Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 ”.
