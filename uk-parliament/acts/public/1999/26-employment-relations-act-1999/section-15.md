# Employment Relations Act 1999, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/26

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. National security employees.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Sections 10 to 13 of this Act shall not apply in relation to a person employed for the purposes of—

- (a) the Security Service,
- (b) the Secret Intelligence Service, or
- (c) the Government Communications Headquarters.
