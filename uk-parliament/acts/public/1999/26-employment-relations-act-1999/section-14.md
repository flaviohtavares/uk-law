# Employment Relations Act 1999, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/26

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 14. Contracting out and conciliation.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Sections 10 to 13 of this Act shall be treated as provisions of Part V of the [^c12486171]Employment Rights Act 1996 for the purposes of—

- (a) section 203(1), (2)(e) and (f), (3) and (4) of that Act (restrictions on contracting out), and
- (b) section 18(1)(b)[^c-ad2b7927d834d4614308987a6111a78a] of the [^c12486181]Employment Tribunals Act 1996 (conciliation).
