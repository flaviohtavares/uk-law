## 55. Second appeals.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where an appeal is made to the county court[^key-8ce430d9781a6b70663a8a390a29878e] , the family court[^key-99cd6ced4bc16e06e96b69d0223b475a] or the High Court in relation to any matter, and on hearing the appeal the court makes a decision in relation to that matter, no appeal may be made to the Court of Appeal from that decision unless the Court of Appeal considers that—

- (a) the appeal would raise an important point of principle or practice, or
- (b) there is some other compelling reason for the Court of Appeal to hear it.

(2) This section does not apply in relation to an appeal in a criminal cause or matter.
