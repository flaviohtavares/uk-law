# Access to Justice Act 1999, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1999/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Areas: consequential provision.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Lord Mayor and aldermen of the City of London shall not be justices of the peace unless appointed by the Lord Chancellor in accordance with the [^c11266551]Justices of the Peace Act 1997.

(2) Schedule 10 (which contains other provisions consequential on sections 74 and 75) has effect.
