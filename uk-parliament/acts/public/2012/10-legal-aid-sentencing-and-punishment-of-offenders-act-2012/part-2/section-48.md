# Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (57).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/10

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Sections 44 and 46 and diffuse mesothelioma proceedings

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Sections 44 and 46 may not be brought into force in relation to proceedings relating to a claim for damages in respect of diffuse mesothelioma until the Lord Chancellor has—

- (a) carried out a review of the likely effect of those sections in relation to such proceedings, and
- (b) published a report of the conclusions of the review.

(2) In this section “**diffuse mesothelioma**” has the same meaning as in the Pneumoconiosis etc (Workers' Compensation) Act 1979.
