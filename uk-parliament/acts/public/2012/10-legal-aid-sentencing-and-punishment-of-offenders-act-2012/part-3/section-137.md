# Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012, section 137

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (57).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/10

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 137. Youth conditional cautions: references to youth offending teams

*Extent: E+W*

In section 66A of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998 (youth conditional cautions) after subsection (6) insert—

> (6A) If an authorised person gives a youth conditional caution to an offender, the authorised person must as soon as practicable refer the offender to a youth offending team.
