# Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012, section 143

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (57).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/10
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 143. Offence of causing serious injury by dangerous driving

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Road Traffic Act 1988 is amended as follows.

(2) After section 1 insert—

> **1A. Causing serious injury by dangerous driving**
>
> (1) A person who causes serious injury to another person by driving a mechanically propelled vehicle dangerously on a road or other public place is guilty of an offence.
>
> (2) In this section “**serious injury**” means—
>
> - (a) in England and Wales, physical harm which amounts to grievous bodily harm for the purposes of the Offences against the Person Act 1861, and
> - (b) in Scotland, severe physical injury.

(3) In section 2A (meaning of dangerous driving) in subsections (1) and (2) after “sections 1” insert “ , 1A ”.

(4) Section 1A inserted by subsection (2) has effect only in relation to driving occurring after that subsection comes into force.

(5) In Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988 (prosecution and punishment of offences under the Traffic Acts) in the appropriate place insert—

> <table>
> <tbody>
> <tr><td rowspan="2">RTA section 1A</td><td rowspan="2">Causing serious injury by dangerous driving.</td><td>(a) Summarily.</td><td>(a) 12 months or the statutory maximum or both.</td><td rowspan="2">Obligatory.</td><td rowspan="2">Obligatory.</td><td rowspan="2">3-11.”</td></tr>
> <tr><td>(b) On indictment.</td><td>(b) 5 years or a fine or both.</td></tr>
> </tbody>
> </table>

(6) In the entry inserted by subsection (5), in relation to an offence committed before 2 May 2022[^key-2009038085e54aaa3a120a963de39535] “the general limit in a magistrates’ court” is to be read as “6 months[^key-cd5acf77da1561f4fb210cc56a66e8cf] (in England and Wales) or 12 months (in Scotland)”.

(7) Schedule 27 (causing serious injury by dangerous driving: minor and consequential amendments) has effect.
