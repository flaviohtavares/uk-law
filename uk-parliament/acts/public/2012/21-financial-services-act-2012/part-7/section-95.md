## 95. Consequential repeal[^key-f7276776e0b6f164d9c61cfa75ca596c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 397 of FSMA 2000 (which relates to misleading statements and practices and is superseded by the provisions of this Part) is repealed.
