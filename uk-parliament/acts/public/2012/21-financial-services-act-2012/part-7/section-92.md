# Financial Services Act 2012, section 92

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-07-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Penalties[^key-f3161fcf5d3eb9817b349d460ec69a25]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person guilty of an offence under this Part is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the applicable maximum term or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 10 years[^key-f2f5809772f695aa0a807992722a1321] or a fine, or both.

(2) For the purpose of subsection (1)(a) “the applicable maximum term” is—

- (a) in England and Wales, the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-c60e3d748d729d7af45c2a3b56517a8c] (or 6 months, if the offence was committed before 2 May 2022[^key-6623eb544b7763e06649b0d822ddcbd9]);
- (b) in Scotland, 12 months;
- (c) in Northern Ireland, 6 months.
