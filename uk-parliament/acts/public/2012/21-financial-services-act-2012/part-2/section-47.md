# Financial Services Act 2012, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-07-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. International obligations[^key-c6011705bfa2ba1a0b5a79d755d27caf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 410 of FSMA 2000 (international obligations), in subsection (4), for paragraph (a) substitute—

> - (a) the FCA;
> - (aa) the PRA;
> - (ab) the Bank of England when exercising functions conferred on it by Part 18;

.
