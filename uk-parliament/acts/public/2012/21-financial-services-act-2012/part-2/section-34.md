# Financial Services Act 2012, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-07-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Repeal of special competition regime[^key-4d2b4ea0883a45702e89485e16ca011a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Part 18 of FSMA 2000 (recognised investment exchanges and clearing houses)—

- (a) omit Chapter 2 (competition scrutiny), and
- (b) omit Chapter 3 (exclusion from the Competition Act 1998).
