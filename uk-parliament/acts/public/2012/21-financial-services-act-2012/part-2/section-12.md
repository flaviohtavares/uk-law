> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-07-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Passporting: exercise of EEA rights and Treaty rights[^c-39b047bb35dec19d974659e3457b908c][^c-3efe3158eed473724eae97014eee6164][^c-f77e01d24e4919c111d9f73a80e6fd0f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 4 contains amendments of the following provisions of FSMA 2000—

- (a) Schedule 3 (EEA passport rights),
- (b) Schedule 4 (Treaty rights),
- (c) sections 34 and 35 (EEA firms and Treaty firms), and
- (d) Part 13 (incoming firms: powers of intervention).
