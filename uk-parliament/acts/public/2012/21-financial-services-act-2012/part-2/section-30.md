# Financial Services Act 2012, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-07-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Recognition requirements: power of FCA and Bank to make rules[^c-edea58557e5f84aa98b7f352b11cfdcc][^c-039a75091123021580d43b8323df04d3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 286 of FSMA 2000 (qualification for recognition), after subsection (4E) insert—

> (4F) Regulations under subsection (1) may confer power on the appropriate regulator to make rules for the purposes of the regulations or of any specified provision made by the regulations.
