# Financial Services Act 2012, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-07-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Discipline and enforcement[^c-67448b72f5663a082e7d692d4daa8c5b][^c-0d0c44c565ecc3ce672c13dd9e9ecace]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-c8597a19af4e9ffc52e008cb7a3a2ff6] Schedule 9 contains miscellaneous amendments of FSMA 2000 relating to discipline and enforcement.

(2)[^c-85b7874252c97ef8293dba9091e4c549] If the Treasury consider that it is in the public interest to do so, the Treasury may by order—

- (a) amend section 391 of FSMA 2000 by substituting for subsections (1) to (1ZB) the following—

  > (1) Neither the regulator giving a warning notice nor a person to whom it is given or copied may publish the notice or any details concerning it.

  , and
- (b) repeal section 395(1)(d) and (2)(b) and (c) of that Act.
