# Health and Social Care Act 2012, section 230

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 230. Consequential provisions and savings, etc.[^c2191346]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-4ebbbedf9af63bd7ef16a432e4b7f51a][^key-8e02377ad7aea53f8040d963678e2999] Parts 1 to 3 of Schedule 15 (which contain minor and consequential amendments and savings relating to the preceding provisions of this Part) have effect.

(2)[^key-4fa9461cf33a4a001e56fef37884997e][^key-8e02377ad7aea53f8040d963678e2999] The Privy Council may by order make transitional, transitory or saving provision in connection with the commencement of the preceding provisions of this Part.

(3)[^key-4fa9461cf33a4a001e56fef37884997e][^key-8e02377ad7aea53f8040d963678e2999] The quorum for the exercise of the power under subsection (2) is two.

(4)[^key-4fa9461cf33a4a001e56fef37884997e][^key-8e02377ad7aea53f8040d963678e2999] Anything done by the Privy Council under subsection (2) is sufficiently signified by an instrument signed by the Clerk of the Council.

(5)[^key-4fa9461cf33a4a001e56fef37884997e] In section 38 of the National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002 (regulations and orders), after subsection (4) insert—

> (4A) The quorum for the exercise by the Privy Council of the power under section 25A, 27 or 28 or paragraph 6 of Schedule 7 is two; and anything done by the Privy Council under either of those sections or that paragraph is sufficiently signified by an instrument signed by the Clerk of the Council.

(6)[^key-4fa9461cf33a4a001e56fef37884997e][^key-8e02377ad7aea53f8040d963678e2999] The amendments made by this Part to an Order in Council under section 60 of the Health Act 1999 do not affect the power to make a further Order in Council under that section amending or revoking provision made by those amendments.
