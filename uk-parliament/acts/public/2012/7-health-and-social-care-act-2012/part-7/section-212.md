# Health and Social Care Act 2012, section 212

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 212. Abolition of the General Social Care Council

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-29b2ba8d7e5af6507d380f4baf115a24] The General Social Care Council is abolished.

(2)[^key-7a1196c7d662cfc978b441b382e72672] In section 54 of the Care Standards Act 2000 (the Care Councils)—

- (a) in subsection (1), omit paragraph (a) and the “and” following it,
- (b) in that subsection, for “conferred on them” substitute “conferred on it”,
- (c) omit subsection (2),
- (d) in subsection (4), for “Each Council” substitute “The Welsh Council”,
- (e) in subsection (6), for “a Council” substitute “the Welsh Council”, and
- (f) omit subsection (7).

(3)[^key-7a1196c7d662cfc978b441b382e72672] For the title to that section substitute “The Care Council for Wales”.
