# Health and Social Care Act 2012, section 298

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 298. Advice or assistance to public authorities in the Isle of Man or Channel Islands[^key-7b58181962af1579d99303bb23b2078d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) NHS England[^key-ad6639707841b7b53477ccef12ea9bde] or an integrated care board[^key-a04b99f1540d808974d087144940b70c] may provide advice or assistance to any public authority in the Isle of Man or Channel Islands.

(2) Advice or assistance under subsection (1) may be provided on such terms, including terms as to payment, as NHS England or (as the case may be) the integrated care board[^key-5688a0f5e1914821d67b80e16b226e06] considers appropriate.
