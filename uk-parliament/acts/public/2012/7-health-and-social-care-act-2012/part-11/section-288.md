# Health and Social Care Act 2012, section 288

## 288. Monitor: duty to co-operate with Care Quality Commission

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-cbe7010c107615fc75965e4d978a0720] NHS England[^c-cbbfba190ffe1d5c0c438e1849db7f4d] must co-operate with the Care Quality Commission in the exercise of their respective functions.

(2) In particular NHS England[^c-b8c7c750f562d4de35caaf2c20c476f9] must—

- (a)[^c-cbe7010c107615fc75965e4d978a0720] give the Commission any relevant[^c-058b3dd007a5cc382ee3bc21a17aa7bf] information NHS England has about the provision of health care services which NHS England or the Commission considers would assist the Commission in the exercise of its functions,
- (b)[^c-afc2642d9d55055fa835fef58147f9c9] make arrangements with the Commission to ensure that—
  - (i) a person applying both for a licence under Chapter 3 of Part 3 and to be registered under the Health and Social Care Act 2008 may do so by way of a single application form,
  - (ii) such a person is granted a licence under that Chapter and registration under that Act by way of a single document, and
- (c)[^c-fe82172027c4b2b795faf607ef6e3961] seek to secure that the conditions included in a licence under that Chapter in a case within paragraph (b) are consistent with any conditions on the person's registration under that Act.

(2A)[^c-3055fbc241fdcfe9702eb744f2819121] In subsection (2)(a) “**relevant information**” means information held by NHS England in connection with—

- (a) its regulatory functions, within the meaning given by section 13SB(2) of the National Health Service Act 2006, or
- (b) its functions under section 27A of the National Health Service Act 2006 Act (NHS trusts: oversight and support).

(3)[^c-bf8fb9fbfc8481e7a6cb12c29f0c14e3][^c-afc2642d9d55055fa835fef58147f9c9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-afc2642d9d55055fa835fef58147f9c9] In subsection (2), references to registration under the Health and Social Care Act 2008 are references to registration under Chapter 2 of Part 1 of that Act.
