# Health and Social Care Act 2012, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/7

## 37. Fluoridation of water supplies: transitional provision[^key-6da2ff050664f97d3f0ecc0cbda5d1d4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-8c29499c3568047166dc632c67deb6f4] In relation to any time on or after the commencement of section 35, any relevant arrangements which have effect immediately before its commencement are to be treated for the purposes of Chapter 4 of Part 3 of the Water Industry Act 1991 as if they were arrangements entered into by the water undertaker with the Secretary of State under section 87(1) of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c29499c3568047166dc632c67deb6f4]

(2) In subsection (1) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c29499c3568047166dc632c67deb6f4]**relevant arrangements *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c29499c3568047166dc632c67deb6f4]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c29499c3568047166dc632c67deb6f4]

- (a) any arrangements entered into by a water undertaker with a Strategic Health Authority under section 87(1) of the Water Industry Act 1991, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c29499c3568047166dc632c67deb6f4]
- (b) any arrangements which are treated as arrangements falling within paragraph (a) by virtue of section 91 of that Act (as it had effect immediately before the commencement of this section). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c29499c3568047166dc632c67deb6f4]

(3) In its application to arrangements which are treated by virtue of subsection (1) as arrangements entered into by a water undertaker with the Secretary of State under section 87(1) of the Water Industry Act 1991, section 88H of that Act applies as if for subsection (3) there were substituted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c29499c3568047166dc632c67deb6f4]

> (3) The amount to be paid by each of the affected local authorities is to be determined by agreement between the local authorities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c29499c3568047166dc632c67deb6f4]

.

(4) Section 91 of the Water Industry Act 1991 (pre-1985 fluoridation schemes) ceases to have effect in relation to arrangements which are (by virtue of subsection (1)) treated as if they were arrangements entered into by a water undertaker with the Secretary of State under section 87(1) of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8c29499c3568047166dc632c67deb6f4]
