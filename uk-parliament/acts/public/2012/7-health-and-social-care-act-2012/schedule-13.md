# Health and Social Care Act 2012, SCHEDULE 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 13 — Part 3: minor and consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 150

### General

**1.**[^key-8e3116b5904ef9d1772c0ded06fdfe54] (1) Any reference in an instrument or document to the Independent Regulator of NHS Foundation Trusts is to be read, in relation to any time after the commencement of section 61, as a reference to Monitor.

(2) Any reference in this Act or in any other enactment, instrument or document to Monitor is to be read, in relation to any time before that commencement, as a reference to the Independent Regulator of NHS Foundation Trusts.

### Public Bodies (Admission to Meetings) Act 1960 (c. 67)

*Extent: E+W*

**2.**[^key-d1de5cf288674d9ceba17c8ab427a662] In the Schedule to the Public Bodies (Admission to Meetings) Act 1960, after paragraph (bj) insert—

> - (bk) Monitor;

.

### Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967 (c. 13)

*Extent: E+W*

**3.**[^key-e30be4a1ad8991b13b5bce92de5b8e94] (1) In Schedule 2 to the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967 (departments etc. subject to investigation)—

(2) In consequence of the repeal made by sub-paragraph (1)(b), omit paragraph 17 of Schedule 2 to the Health and Social Care (Community Health and Standards) Act 2003 (which inserted the entry in question).
