# Health and Social Care Act 2012, section 156

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/7

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 156. Annual report and forward plan[^c2191329]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-b71119ef3a72060a88d2645ca2f789aa] In sub-paragraph (2) of paragraph 26 of Schedule 7 to the National Health Service Act 2006 (information that must be included in annual report), after paragraph (aa) (inserted by section 151(8)) insert—

> - (ab) information on the corporation's policy on pay and on the work of the committee established under paragraph 18(2) and such other procedures as the corporation has on pay,
> - (ac) information on the remuneration of the directors and on the expenses of the governors and the directors,

.

(2)[^key-b71119ef3a72060a88d2645ca2f789aa] After that sub-paragraph insert—

> (2A) Before imposing a requirement under sub-paragraph (2)(b) that the regulator considers is sufficiently significant to justify consultation, the regulator must consult such persons as it considers appropriate.

(3)[^key-a045df41f6a201ca54148f231448a026] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-ffeac773a685acbb9c296e51101741aa] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-46f98ee53b6b3032209a32e63105a89d] Omit section 39(2)(e) of that Act (requirement for copy of forward plan to be on register).

(6)[^key-46f98ee53b6b3032209a32e63105a89d] In paragraph 22(1) of Schedule 7, omit paragraph (e) (duty to make forward plan available to the public).
