# Health and Social Care Act 2012, section 269

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 269. Power to confer functions in relation to identification of GPs[^c-384880c93529b880b92c4f009fdfc732][^c2191364]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Regulations may make provision conferring functions on NHS England[^c-5189727da95a9f14c9f87bea05e92ef6] in connection with the verification of the identity of general medical practitioners for purposes connected with the health service in England.

(2) In subsection (1) “**general medical practitioners**” means persons registered in the General Practitioner Register kept by the General Medical Council.
