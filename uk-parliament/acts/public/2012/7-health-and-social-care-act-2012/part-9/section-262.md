## 262. Other dissemination: directions and requests under sections 254 and 255[^c-486b87b0ad5beefa75b512f500956e6b][^c-15f76a582be5f9711683064608f4bda9][^c2191361]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-2bf1b58d13cbee8d0a20832cd2d314b9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-2bf1b58d13cbee8d0a20832cd2d314b9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-2bf1b58d13cbee8d0a20832cd2d314b9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) A request under section 255 may request NHS England[^c-0a55d6a96bdd8809ce5eb89518305766] to exercise—

- (a) the power conferred by section 261(1) or (4) in relation to information which it obtains by complying with the request, or
- (b) any other power it has to disseminate such information under or by virtue of any other provision of this or any other Act.

(5) A [^c-e5a48625d7de7330d8ccd69fd1ea577b]... request under section 255 may request, NHS England[^c-801bc2d96086939c1f559f34f79606ad] not to exercise the power conferred by section 261(1) or (4) in relation to information which it obtains by complying with the [^c-360a35cb93833924c257b73f7973fc4b]... request.

(6) Section 255(3) does not apply in relation to anything included in a mandatory request by virtue of subsection (4) or (5).

(7)[^c-b4e0bc98916359610d48cdf57d72516a] A request made to NHS England in accordance with this section to disseminate information may include a request about the persons to whom the information is to be disseminated and the form, manner and timing of dissemination.
