## 262A. Publication and other dissemination: supplementary[^c-292a152c1929cde4b4da8d9d8708c3db][^c-15f76a582be5f9711683064608f4bda9]

*Extent: E+W*

In exercising any function [^c-70b1321ef23cf14099571de00da8e076]... of publishing or otherwise disseminating information obtained in connection with the exercise of its relevant data functions, NHS England[^c-365f9abb03b981103ec6a24e5086729a] must have regard to any advice given to it by the committee appointed by the Health Research Authority under paragraph 8(1) of Schedule 7 to the Care Act 2014 (committee to advise in connection with information dissemination etc).
