## 264. Information Register[^c-79386af894fd6d7199165cc54390f230][^c-15f76a582be5f9711683064608f4bda9]

*Extent: E+W*

NHS England[^c-4327eca99ca36699721d750085b3300c] must maintain and publish a register containing descriptions of the information which has been obtained by virtue of this Chapter.
