# Health and Social Care Act 2012, section 85

## 85. Application for licence[^c-57c3336a35aa99f1f366fe8ee70735bc][^c-b0a5c5770e4eceed8f64ee311bf12114]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person seeking to hold a licence under this Chapter must make an application to NHS England[^c-3f7131badfe397bf1fbe0204e5b7b13b].

(2) The application must be made in such form, and contain or be accompanied by such information, as NHS England[^c-65cf67bf6cf18c8a69e12d94ad9b52ab] requires.
