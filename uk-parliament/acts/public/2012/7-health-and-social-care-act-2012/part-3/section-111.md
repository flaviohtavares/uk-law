## 111. Imposition of licence conditions on (affected by repeal, see note) NHS (affected by repeal, see note) foundation trusts (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-11ccdd6a270892a17fad499f21107ba8][^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd][^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd][^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd][^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] NHS England[^key-18bc74856b1edf38e5fb29a83edf9e92] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] is satisfied that the governance of an NHS foundation trust is such that the trust will fail to comply with the conditions of its licence, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] NHS England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] may include in the licence such conditions relating to governance as it considers appropriate for the purpose of reducing that risk. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]

(2) The circumstances in which *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] NHS England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] may be satisfied as mentioned in subsection (1) include circumstances where it is satisfied that the council of governors, the board of directors or the council of governors and board of directors taken together are failing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]

- (a) to secure compliance with conditions in the trust’s licence, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]
- (b) to take steps to reduce the risk of a breach of a condition in the trust’s licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]

(2A)[^M_F_7092adf3-701e-4668-c423-749ff9b01473] Where a warning notice under section 29A of the Health and Social Care Act 2008 is given to an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] NHS *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] foundation trust, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] NHS England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] may include in the trust's licence such conditions as it considers appropriate in connection with the matters to which the notice relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]

(3) A condition included under subsection (1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] or (2A)[^M_F_245fadfa-533c-44dc-d866-15277eab333c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] has effect until this section ceases, by virtue of section 112, to have effect in relation to the trust. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]

(4) NHS England[^key-2152a052b623c5156487bdb9bb359084] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] may modify a condition included under subsection (1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] or (2A) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd].

(5) Where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] NHS England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] is satisfied that the trust has breached or is breaching a condition included under subsection (1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] or (2A) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd], NHS England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] may by notice require the trust to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]

- (a) remove one or more of the directors or members of the council of governors and appoint interim directors or members of the council; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]
- (b) suspend one or more of the directors or members of the council from office as a director or member for a specified period; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]
- (c) disqualify one or more of the directors or members of the council from holding office as a director or member for a specified period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]

(6) Where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] NHS England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] is satisfied that a person has failed or is failing to comply with a notice under subsection *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] (5), NHS England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] may do one or more of the things which it may require the trust to do under that subsection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]

(7) Subsection *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] (5) does not prevent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] NHS England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] from exercising in relation to a condition included in a licence under subsection (1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] or (2A)[^M_F_23e2c69a-c353-481f-dc47-f826d9c1a217] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] the powers conferred by sections 105 and 106 (breach of licence condition etc: enforcement powers which apply during and after period in which this section and sections 112 to 114 have effect). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]

(8) Where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] NHS England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] includes a condition under subsection (1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] or (2A) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd], it may also make such incidental or consequential modifications as it considers necessary or expedient of any other condition of the licence concerned which is affected. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]

(9) Where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] NHS England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] includes a condition under subsection (1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] or (2A) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd] by modifying a standard condition of the licence concerned, the modification does not prevent any other part of the condition from continuing to be regarded as a standard condition for the purposes of this Chapter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]

(10) In this section, a reference to failing to discharge functions includes a reference to failing to discharge those functions properly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]

(11) Omit section 52 of the National Health Service Act 2006 (failing NHS foundation trusts); and in consequence of that, omit— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]

- (a) section 39(2)(f) of that Act (copy of notice under section 52 of that Act to be on register), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]
- (b) paragraph 22(1)(f) of Schedule 7 to that Act (copy of that notice to be available for public inspection). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_b0310fea-a9db-4812-ba53-d8e67cf26efd]
