## 98. Conditions relating to the continuation of the provision of services etc.[^key-0ffe840e35791584ad5f9caa0718aee1][^c2191304]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-d1e9aa756b92c3651295342b85856dda] The things which a licence holder may be required to do by a condition under section 97(1)(i)(i) include, in particular—

- (a) providing information to the commissioners of services to which the condition applies and to such other persons as NHS England[^key-319dd4a96a78770bdcff64034ef17c2a] may direct,
- (b) allowing NHS England[^key-0adc6498dd929c9f1582284f188ca08e] to enter premises owned or controlled by the licence holder and to inspect the premises and anything on them, and
- (c) co-operating with such persons as NHS England[^key-4dcc6f8229e92f097a13bf2d2ef183ce] may appoint to assist in the management of the licence holder's affairs, business and property.

(2)[^key-d1e9aa756b92c3651295342b85856dda] A commissioner of services to which a condition under section 97(1)(i), (j) or (k) applies must co-operate with persons appointed under subsection (1)(c) in their provision of the assistance that they have been appointed to provide.

(3) Where a licence includes a condition under section 97(1)(i), (j) or (k), NHS England[^key-d534ad43a702710c6da23f97d9156782] must carry out an ongoing assessment of the risks to the continued provision of services to which the condition applies.

(4)[^key-1edaf86969a897dbe171ecb3cdca7c91] NHS England[^key-bb0bb0eb0f9e878cee208dd2c7674ada] must publish guidance—

- (a) for commissioners of a service to which a condition under section 97(1)(i), (j) or (k) applies about the exercise of their functions in connection with the licence holders who provide the service, and
- (b) for such licence holders about the conduct of their affairs, business and property at a time at which such a condition applies.

(5) A commissioner of services to which a condition under section 97(1)(i), (j), or (k) applies must have regard to guidance under subsection (4)(a).

(5A)[^key-d9845eecc6c63f191debb5249ec68401] In subsections (4)(a) and (5) “**commissioner**” does not include NHS England.

(6) NHS England[^key-e420fb787e8452347c1e74025b2ef440] may revise guidance under subsection (4) and, if it does so, must publish the guidance as revised.

(7)[^key-1edaf86969a897dbe171ecb3cdca7c91] Before publishing guidance under subsection (4) or (6), NHS England[^key-71ad30185efbbc3a9cc2f5f1bb8089fb] must obtain the approval of—

- (a) the Secretary of State, [^key-7d690ff5eca7f330399bd69e90d346d0]...
