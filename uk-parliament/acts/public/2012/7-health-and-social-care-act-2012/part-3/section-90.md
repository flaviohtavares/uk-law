# Health and Social Care Act 2012, section 90

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Right to make representations

*Extent: E+W*

(1) NHS England[^key-e0d1c14745ba804fec6ed4d7668ff00f] must give notice—

- (a)[^key-f91c7a86d8abafeebd641f55aa1a7f82] to an applicant for a licence under this Chapter of a proposal to refuse the application;
- (b)[^key-fe9ec33a0b52c679973cfe813886f146] to the licence holder of a proposal to revoke a licence under section 89(b).

(2)[^key-fe9ec33a0b52c679973cfe813886f146] A notice under this section must—

- (a) set out NHS England’s[^key-6bc4af4d3d7ca44834b468951581cf2c] reasons for its proposal;
- (b) specify the period within which representations with respect to the proposal may be made to NHS England[^key-c86a513634a0f3c1b19f4ad29bb015b7].

(3)[^key-fe9ec33a0b52c679973cfe813886f146] The period so specified must be not less than 28 days beginning with the day after that on which the notice is received.
