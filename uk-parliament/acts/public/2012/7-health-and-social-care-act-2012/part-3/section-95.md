# Health and Social Care Act 2012, section 95

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/7

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Special conditions[^key-93d6448c76c62eba90981100b625b118]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) NHS England[^key-37530fa7ed235d1079e82171b1e65f69] may—

- (a)[^key-cd2ff92cf1421e0da7a112b35b3c3b43] [^key-d1ed01d0a950cd90a1a092fe8b5ff21f]... include a special condition in a licence under this Chapter, and
- (b)[^key-c3b4e6df7dd319c3a6f20ec92e42e279] [^key-2b44dda3e2230344ddd1d1d6cd779f1b]... modify a special condition of a licence.

(2)[^key-cd2ff92cf1421e0da7a112b35b3c3b43] Before including a special condition or making such modifications NHS England[^key-027812d3f95daa5b0f8c5e1b732584a9] must give notice to—

- (a) the applicant or the licence holder (as the case may be),
- (b) the Secretary of State,
- (c)[^key-d98a8948eb25022c642ae3b5f97d9f64] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) such integrated care boards[^key-47db158deb5003df18fbc52cd92aa8e3] as are likely to be affected by the proposed inclusion or modifications, and
- (e) the Care Quality Commission and its Healthwatch England committee.

(3)[^key-cd2ff92cf1421e0da7a112b35b3c3b43] NHS England[^key-a401d6f910efaabbaf19c643b62a969b] must also publish the notice under subsection (2).

(4)[^key-cd2ff92cf1421e0da7a112b35b3c3b43] The notice under subsection (2) must—

- (a) state that NHS England[^key-48b1ffba40a577aa2299111992d551b1] proposes to include the special condition or make the modifications and set out its or their proposed effect,
- (b) set out NHS England’s[^key-487c30df84e759013d9d3c3f5110f0fe] reasons for the proposal, and
- (c) specify the period (“**the notice period**”) within which representations with respect to the proposal may be made to NHS England[^key-b9a5d1f8feef21342632504d5e94eebd].

(5)[^key-cd2ff92cf1421e0da7a112b35b3c3b43] The notice period must be not less than 28 days beginning with the day after that on which the notice is published under subsection (3).

(6)[^key-c3b4e6df7dd319c3a6f20ec92e42e279] In this section, a reference to modifying a condition includes a reference to amending, omitting or adding a condition.
