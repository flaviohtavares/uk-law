# Health and Social Care Act 2012, section 92

## 92. Appeals to the Tribunal

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An appeal lies to the First-tier Tribunal against a decision of NHS England[^key-acb24362fb0d9fce10ec17dd65c1f2c2] to—

- (a)[^key-0ff7551d120db50178dba0833d54c2a9] refuse an application for a licence under section 87, or
- (b)[^key-3f532f94828a1f70c0c7219ab5e4047a] revoke a licence under section 89(b).

(2)[^key-3f532f94828a1f70c0c7219ab5e4047a] The grounds for an appeal under this section are that the decision was—

- (a) based on an error of fact,
- (b) wrong in law, or
- (c) unreasonable.

(3)[^key-3f532f94828a1f70c0c7219ab5e4047a] On an appeal under this section, the First-tier Tribunal may—

- (a) confirm NHS England’s[^key-87238a9bcf44e3615c2a8447613cbadd] decision,
- (b) direct that the decision is not to have effect, or
- (c) remit the decision to NHS England[^key-25dde052df3a0eece5dbc58283880d5a].
