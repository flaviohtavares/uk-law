# Health and Social Care Act 2012, section 114

>

## 114. Repeal of sections 112 and 113 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-bf7b8d3f211b60696733571e61eaceab][^M_F_77143082-7a48-4a3d-a572-e95da5963239][^M_F_77143082-7a48-4a3d-a572-e95da5963239]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Sections 112 and 113 are repealed immediately after section 111 is repealed; and in consequence of that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_77143082-7a48-4a3d-a572-e95da5963239]

- (a) in section 67(2)(a), omit “or under sections 111 and 113 of this Act (imposition of licence conditions on NHS foundation trusts during transitional period)”, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_77143082-7a48-4a3d-a572-e95da5963239]
- (b) omit section 67(3), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_77143082-7a48-4a3d-a572-e95da5963239]
- (c) in section 87(4), after paragraph (a) insert “and”, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_77143082-7a48-4a3d-a572-e95da5963239]
- (d) in section 87(4), omit paragraph (c) and the preceding “and”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_77143082-7a48-4a3d-a572-e95da5963239]

(2) This section is repealed immediately after sections 112 and 113 are repealed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_77143082-7a48-4a3d-a572-e95da5963239]
