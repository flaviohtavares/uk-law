# Health and Social Care Act 2012, section 108

## 108. Guidance as to use of enforcement powers

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-870df751ef6056e1723fdcac86532134][^c-e16d71fecf6740497310efd67be913ad] NHS England[^c-bef94fb9436812684a2fead681e72835] must publish guidance about how it intends to exercise its functions under sections 105 and 106 and Schedule 11.

(2)[^c-870df751ef6056e1723fdcac86532134][^c-e16d71fecf6740497310efd67be913ad] NHS England may revise the guidance and, if it does so, must publish the guidance as revised.

(3)[^c-870df751ef6056e1723fdcac86532134][^c-e16d71fecf6740497310efd67be913ad] NHS England must consult such persons as it considers appropriate before publishing or revising the guidance.

(4)[^c-870df751ef6056e1723fdcac86532134][^c-e16d71fecf6740497310efd67be913ad] Guidance relating to NHS England’s[^c-579082bd098f9638f504bdce572080b7] functions under section 105 must include information about—

- (a) the circumstances in which NHS England[^c-29c292523edd095398e8c01895cb66e4] is likely to impose a discretionary requirement,
- (b) the circumstances in which NHS England[^c-acba3028de89064406fd811db5111a06] may not impose a discretionary requirement,
- (c) the matters likely to be taken into account by NHS England[^c-eb9c43dc8f2437d8366292781d1d2732] in determining the amount of any variable monetary penalty to be imposed (including, where relevant, any discounts for voluntary reporting of breaches in respect of which a penalty may be imposed), and
- (d) rights to make representations and rights of appeal.

(5)[^c-3d4e100cc7066c834dda030aec9fa1df] NHS England[^c-38e8990c0bd9a927d2a8c2b454dcc0b5] must have regard to the guidance or (as the case may be) revised guidance in exercising its functions under sections 105 and 106 and Schedule 11.
