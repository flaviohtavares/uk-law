# Health and Social Care Act 2012, section 112

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 112. Duration of transitional period (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-3de1e579bf1e1dc2a66e20053d4738d6][^M_F_1e6e68e3-bf1a-4895-cc7c-731b9d698999][^c2191309][^M_F_1e6e68e3-bf1a-4895-cc7c-731b9d698999]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 111 ceases to have effect in relation to an NHS foundation trust on such day as the Secretary of State may by order specify. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1e6e68e3-bf1a-4895-cc7c-731b9d698999]

(2) Different days may be appointed in relation to different NHS foundation trusts. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1e6e68e3-bf1a-4895-cc7c-731b9d698999]

(3) A day specified under subsection (1) must not— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1e6e68e3-bf1a-4895-cc7c-731b9d698999]

- (a) in the case of an NHS foundation trust authorised on or before 1 April 2014, be before 1 April 2016; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1e6e68e3-bf1a-4895-cc7c-731b9d698999]
- (b) in the case of an NHS foundation trust authorised after 1 April 2014, be before the end of the period of two years beginning with the day on which the trust was authorised. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1e6e68e3-bf1a-4895-cc7c-731b9d698999]

(4) In this section, a reference to being authorised is a reference to being given an authorisation under section 35 of the National Health Service Act 2006. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1e6e68e3-bf1a-4895-cc7c-731b9d698999]

(5) Section 111 is repealed as soon as there are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1e6e68e3-bf1a-4895-cc7c-731b9d698999]

- (a) no NHS foundation trusts in relation to which it has effect, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1e6e68e3-bf1a-4895-cc7c-731b9d698999]
- (b) no NHS trusts in existence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_1e6e68e3-bf1a-4895-cc7c-731b9d698999] [^c-501950d98a7e08771d94a5c393f98f21]....
