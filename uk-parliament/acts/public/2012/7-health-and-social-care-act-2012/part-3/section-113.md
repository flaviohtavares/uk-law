## 113. Orders under section 112: criteria for deciding applicable trusts (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-16853a6be53d3f175b6e316fb6f2b3c4][^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2][^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the Secretary of State proposes to make an order under section 112, the Secretary of State must notify *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2] NHS England[^key-00036ec122be88bddda3e7c57a3922de] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2].

(2) NHS England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2], having received a notification under subsection (1), must set the criteria that are to be applied for the purpose of determining to which NHS foundation trusts the order should apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2]

(3) Before setting criteria under subsection (2), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2] NHS England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2] must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2]

- (a) consult the Care Quality Commission and such other persons as *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2] NHS England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2] considers appropriate, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2]
- (b) obtain the approval of the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2]

(4) If the Secretary of State approves the proposed criteria, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2] NHS England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2] must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2]

- (a) publish the criteria, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2]
- (b) determine, by applying the criteria, to which trusts the order should apply, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2]
- (c) notify the Secretary of State of its determination, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2]
- (d) publish a list of the trusts concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2]

(5) If the Secretary of State does not approve the proposed criteria, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2] NHS England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2] must propose revised criteria; and subsections (3)(b) and (4) apply in relation to the proposed revised criteria as they apply in relation to the criteria previously proposed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2]

(6) The Secretary of State, having received a notification under subsection (4)(c), must review *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2] NHS England’s[^key-919c4b6f431475b128a3c2c41660532d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2] determination under subsection (4)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_f2b60220-1db5-4ca4-ef69-385ce7adabd2]
