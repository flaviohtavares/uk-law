# Protection of Freedoms Act 2012, section 87

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Formation and constitution of DBS[^M_C_39f802de-b288-483b-961e-de5b83b224de][^key-0b1d8e3e18daeae7f9e4dccc16b0b4ab][^key-de7eec67c2cfa50a56c4db52f69197cc]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1)[^key-406e40c6e23f360f858a886e21002139] There is to be a body corporate known as the Disclosure and Barring Service.

(2)[^key-406e40c6e23f360f858a886e21002139] In this Chapter “**DBS**” means the Disclosure and Barring Service.

(3)[^key-3027c8fa3edfe3c72d79108bec1ee6a5][^key-a2a5a55385cb3f048fa0bf0d022ad722] Schedule 8 (which makes further provision about DBS) has effect.
