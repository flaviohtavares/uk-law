# Protection of Freedoms Act 2012, section 108

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 108. Removal of Secretary of State approval for staff numbers, terms etc.[^c-84cbcb87e3edbf842f60f0e958eb936b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Paragraph 4 of Schedule 5 to the Data Protection Act 1998 (appointment of officers and staff of the Information Commissioner) is amended as follows.

(2) After sub-paragraph (4) insert—

> (4A) In making appointments under this paragraph, the Commissioner must have regard to the principle of selection on merit on the basis of fair and open competition.

(3) Omit sub-paragraph (5) (approval of Secretary of State required for number, and terms and conditions, of persons to be appointed).
