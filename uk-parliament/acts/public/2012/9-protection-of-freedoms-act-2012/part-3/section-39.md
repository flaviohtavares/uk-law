## 39. Repealing etc. unnecessary or inappropriate powers of entry[^c2142433]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-9b192b85de6c028f6ccafd967dd00afc] The appropriate national authority may by order repeal any power of entry or associated power which the appropriate national authority considers to be unnecessary or inappropriate.

(2) Schedule 2 (which contains repeals etc. of certain powers of entry) has effect.
