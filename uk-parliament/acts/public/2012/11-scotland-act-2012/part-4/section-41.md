# Scotland Act 2012, section 41

> Text as at 2018-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Orders

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any power to make an order conferred by this Act is exercisable by statutory instrument.
