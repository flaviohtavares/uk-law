# Scotland Act 2012, section 18

> Text as at 2018-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Crown Estate Commissioner with special responsibility for Scotland[^c-82d3dbc750c991c20050fa0fc1f17b53][^c-37bfb3b820f276c1047663884445ced4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In Schedule 1 to the Crown Estate Act 1961 (constitution etc of Crown Estate Commissioners), paragraph 1 is amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-37bfb3b820f276c1047663884445ced4]

(2) After sub-paragraph (3) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-37bfb3b820f276c1047663884445ced4]

> (3A) One of the Commissioners shall be appointed as the Crown Estate Commissioner with special responsibility for Scotland, who must be a person who knows about conditions in Scotland as they relate to the functions of the Commissioners. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-37bfb3b820f276c1047663884445ced4]

(3) After sub-paragraph (4) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-37bfb3b820f276c1047663884445ced4]

> (4A) The Crown Estate Commissioner with special responsibility for Scotland shall be appointed on the recommendation of the Chancellor of the Exchequer, who shall consult the Scottish Ministers before making that recommendation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-37bfb3b820f276c1047663884445ced4]
