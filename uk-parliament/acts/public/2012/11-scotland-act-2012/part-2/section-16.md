# Scotland Act 2012, section 16

> Text as at 2018-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Trust member for Scotland

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the 1998 Act, after section 90 insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b48723b719f63acc553f47551eb375f]

> **The BBC (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0b48723b719f63acc553f47551eb375f]**
>
> **90A. BBC Trust member for Scotland (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0b48723b719f63acc553f47551eb375f]**
>
> (1) A Minister of the Crown must not exercise without the agreement of the Scottish Ministers functions relating to selection for a particular appointment by which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b48723b719f63acc553f47551eb375f]
>
> - (a) a person is to become a member of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b48723b719f63acc553f47551eb375f] BBC *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b48723b719f63acc553f47551eb375f] Trust and hold a Scottish post, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b48723b719f63acc553f47551eb375f]
> - (b) an existing member of the Trust is to hold a Scottish post. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b48723b719f63acc553f47551eb375f]
>
> (2) “**Scottish post *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b48723b719f63acc553f47551eb375f]**” means a position, held as a member of the Trust, with specific reference to Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b48723b719f63acc553f47551eb375f]
