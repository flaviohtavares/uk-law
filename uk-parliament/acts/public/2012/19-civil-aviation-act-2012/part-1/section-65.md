# Civil Aviation Act 2012, section 65

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Power to modify CAA's competition powers[^c-707212be72396a40687db49b360249ac]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations modify sections 60(2) and 62(2) by providing that the functions of the Competition and Markets Authority[^c-ad0c562d2587a5eda8aa28abd42d8e69] mentioned in those provisions—

- (a) do not include functions relating to the provision of particular airport operation services, or
- (b) include functions relating to the provision of particular services at an airport that are not airport operation services.

(2) The regulations may make consequential, incidental or supplementary provision amending this Chapter.
