# Civil Aviation Act 2012, section 62

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Functions under Competition Act 1998[^c-c07c53ef1adf3e49564c32bca803280d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The functions of the Competition and Markets Authority (“the CMA”)[^c-ae25ce62ce29e88f650406fe20cb5208] specified in subsection (2) are to be concurrent functions of the CAA and the CMA[^c-4545b33ad09c22ec62abf6cc39f9dd5b].

(2) Those functions are the CMA’s[^c-6c577baa31ecda9a2da5666f6defeb23] functions under Part 1 of the Competition Act 1998 (competition) so far as they relate to anything which—

- (a) is mentioned in subsection (3)(a) to (c)[^c-ef615a188f8ddb26fb9a159e431718bd], and
- (b) relates to the provision of airport operation services,

other than functions under sections 31D(1) to (6) , 35C[^c-d98b7f67f776625930cf728b3f3f89d0] , 38(1) to (6) , 40B(1) to (4)[^c-b66dd5228717c6d7bbe45a754b47082a] and 51.

(3) Those things are—

- (a) agreements, decisions or concerted practices of the kind mentioned in section 2(1) of that Act,
- (b) conduct of the kind mentioned in section 18(1) of that Act, or[^c-8aed132ec15b079efa7239ca7542a4e8]
- (c)[^c-9c941dc1eaf409c69661f52fa42034b3] transferred EU anti-trust commitments or transferred EU anti-trust directions (as defined in section 40ZA of that Act).

(4) References to the CMA[^c-d541c2f85d1a221aa66125fc7a085a50] in Part 1 of the Competition Act 1998 (including references in provisions of the Enterprise Act 2002 applied by that Part)[^c-c45ddb7e0288addfd4e60eb0a1755c1a], other than in sections 31D(1) to (6) , 35C[^c-7d35f3f3aa7a348697b22d6dabac1847], 38(1) to (6) , 40B(1) to (4) and 51, are to be read as including a reference to the CAA.

(5) But subsection (4) applies—

- (a) only so far as it is consequential on subsections (1) and (2), and
- (b) only if the context does not otherwise require.
