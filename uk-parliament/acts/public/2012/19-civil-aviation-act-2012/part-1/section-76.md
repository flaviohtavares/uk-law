# Civil Aviation Act 2012, section 76

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Minor, consequential and transitional provision[^c2171994]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-2ceda8a4a3e9fac2c2fc58efbfef7776][^c-a888fe5ab0f8c0dd49ce7f45c68e29b9] Part 4 of the Airports Act 1986 (economic regulation of airports) is repealed.

(2)[^c-65f02d68ab23380031ea44feee21ec4e] Part 4 of the Airports (Northern Ireland) Order 1994 (S.I. 1994/426 (N.I. 1)) (economic regulation of airports) is revoked.

(3)[^c-65f02d68ab23380031ea44feee21ec4e] Schedule 8 (status of airport operators as statutory undertakers etc) has effect.

(4)[^c-987c22cededa063f9d0d7109fbf21730][^c-c26562c813942590e8f7737281221344] Schedule 9 (regulation of operators of dominant airports: consequential provision) has effect.

(5)[^c-2ea698710607cbd2e052bdca1dca322b] Schedule 10 (regulation of operators of dominant airports: transitional provision) has effect.
