# Civil Aviation Act 2012, section 24

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Appeal to Competition and Markets Authority : conditions of new licences[^c-205af98ad826bacea8254d76d469bd8d][^c-6f69f693c9d255921faca13282eebf83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An appeal lies to the Competition and Markets Authority[^c-977e17e9ab0f24a27c579ed21e5e7d07] against a decision by the CAA under section 15 to include, or not to include, a condition in a licence when it is granted.

(2) An appeal may be brought under this section only by—

- (a) the holder of the licence, or
- (b) a provider of air transport services whose interests are materially affected by the decision.

(3) An appeal may be brought under this section only with the permission of the Competition and Markets Authority .

(4) An application for permission to appeal under this section may be made only by a person who, if permission is granted, will be entitled to bring the appeal.

(5) The Competition and Markets Authority[^c-bd7a1866dd49252b3fa6336d59b8ebe2] may refuse permission to appeal under this section only on one of the following grounds—

- (a) that the appeal is brought for reasons that are trivial or vexatious, or
- (b) that the appeal does not have a reasonable prospect of success.
