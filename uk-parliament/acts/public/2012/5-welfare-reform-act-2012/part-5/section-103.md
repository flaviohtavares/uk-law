# Welfare Reform Act 2012, section 103

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (68).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 103. Supersession of decisions of former appellate bodies

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Schedule 12 contains amendments reinstating powers to make decisions superseding decisions made by appellate bodies before their functions were transferred to the First-tier Tribunal and Upper Tribunal.

(2) The following have effect as if they had come into force on 3 November 2008—

- (a) the amendments made by Schedule 12, and
- (b) if regulations made in the exercise of the powers conferred by virtue of those amendments so provide, those regulations.
