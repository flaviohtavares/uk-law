## 122. Tax credit fraud: investigation[^c-87349b31bab3a43d195a80df8eca9540]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 109A of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 (authorisations for investigators), at the end there is inserted—

> (9) This section and sections 109B to 109C below apply as if—
>
> - (a) the Tax Credits Act 2002 were relevant social security legislation, and
> - (b) accordingly, child tax credit and working tax credit were relevant social security benefits for the purposes of the definition of “benefit offence“.
