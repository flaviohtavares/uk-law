# Welfare Reform Act 2012, section 97

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (68).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 97. Benefit cap: supplementary

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-bcdd0d0a3880fd510234e07cf4d92e4e] Regulations under section 96 or 96A[^c-a24543a2c4d4a791be614bcfa99997d0] may make different provision for different purposes or cases.

(2)[^c-bcdd0d0a3880fd510234e07cf4d92e4e] Regulations under section 96 or 96A[^c-07e3c848f944342d88eac93002bfe247] must be made by statutory instrument.

(3)[^c-fe07d9c3740bf55310a255ad69e7198c][^c-bcdd0d0a3880fd510234e07cf4d92e4e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-bcdd0d0a3880fd510234e07cf4d92e4e] A statutory instrument containing [^c-fd8deca4a93faca84fbd980099f1a6fa]... regulations under section 96 is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(4A)[^c-7d245168ac65a81e288c2a3ae58ca8ca] A statutory instrument containing regulations under section 96A may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

(5)[^c-34cba822bdc890440245b9f19609eaff][^c-e22f179eef5fcc3a65a55658a695c695] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c-e22f179eef5fcc3a65a55658a695c695] In Schedule 2 to the Social Security Act 1998 (decisions against which no appeal lies) after paragraph 8 there is inserted—

> **8A. Reduction on application of benefit cap**
>
> A decision to apply the benefit cap in accordance with regulations under section 96 of the Welfare Reform Act 2012.
