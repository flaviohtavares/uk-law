# Welfare Reform Act 2012, section 133

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (68).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 133. Sections 130 to 132: supplementary

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-775ee59547542ebc067cfb5bae7bf483] In sections 130 and 131—

- “**benefit**” includes any allowance, payment, credit or loan;
- “**local authority**” means—
  - (a) a county or district council in England;
  - (b) an eligible parish council (within the meaning of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of the Localism Act 2011);
  - (c) a London borough council;
  - (d) the Common Council of the City of London in its capacity as a local authority;
  - (e) the Council of the Isles of Scilly;
  - (f) a county or county borough council in Wales;
  - (g) a council constituted under section 2 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994;
- “**prescribed**” means prescribed in regulations made by the Secretary of State.

(2)[^key-775ee59547542ebc067cfb5bae7bf483] Any power to make regulations under sections 130 and 131 includes power—

- (a) to make different provision for different purposes, cases and areas;
- (b) to make such incidental, supplemental, consequential, transitional or saving provision as the Secretary of State thinks necessary or expedient.

(3)[^key-775ee59547542ebc067cfb5bae7bf483] Regulations under sections 130 and 131 must be made by statutory instrument.

(4)[^key-775ee59547542ebc067cfb5bae7bf483] A statutory instrument containing regulations under section 130 or 131 is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(5) Until the coming into force of provision for identifying eligible parish councils within the meaning of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of the Localism Act 2011, the reference in subsection (1) to an eligible parish council within the meaning of that Chapter is to be read as a reference to an eligible parish council within the meaning of Part 1 of the Local Government Act 2000.

(6)[^key-c6fca8da68b88d04a995ef8b69345064] The following are repealed—

- (a) sections 42 and 43 of the Welfare Reform Act 2007;
- (b) section 69(2)(a) of that Act.
