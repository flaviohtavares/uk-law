## 39. Couples[^c-7a6361c018fdc5edd9974a58eb7e3c74][^c-f0c316d9d1b0dfd0e226e5ef4e030922]

*Extent: E+W+S*

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-aca0727f07b8f2bd47b31b1c8ab2250e][^c-cbfb1c1180d9e69f061353cb67793fff] In this Part “couple” means—

- (a) two people who are married to, or civil partners of, each other and are members of the same household; or
- (b) two people who are not married to, or civil partners of, each other but are living together as if they were a married couple or civil partners[^c-7e0dfba5a127445e26b4aef1ca64cff7].

(2)[^c-aa699bc5471b8339e37729dd8c65ddf5][^c-8cc5e215dd459410daa155d096cf45b0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) For the purposes of this section regulations may prescribe—

- (a)[^c-e008e0b6b32904291c5bf3772a2eb65b] circumstances in which the fact that two persons are [^c-f5f64ed5bdf4c878b4c61418eb456d1e]married *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-df129f2dd98d538de3c4b2df1984557a] or are civil partners is to be disregarded;
- (b)[^c-7a6361c018fdc5edd9974a58eb7e3c74] circumstances in which [^c-38cda835a02b90860aa230279c5956d6]two people are to be treated as living together *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-a69936c7790b898ebdb03ecff1a32cc4] as if they were a married couple or civil partners[^c-c2e709352934264b1305bb6845bbb4f4] *(Extent: E+W)*;
- (c)[^c-7a6361c018fdc5edd9974a58eb7e3c74] circumstances in which people are to be treated as being or not being members of the same household.
