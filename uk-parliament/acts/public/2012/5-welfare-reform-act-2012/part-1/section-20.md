## 20. Claimants subject to work-focused interview requirement only[^key-10f9bfed7abb62f19af779183a0c8763][^key-f0c316d9d1b0dfd0e226e5ef4e030922][^key-0f025a2bc299c78363b7b33332bde658][^key-c88f1de1d8ff9fbac4c477088e0bbd6d][^key-f19771921b216e68934968fcc7d90744][^key-c6cdb114344b88aaa0351235e899debc][^key-ae50b7c3253e53341836de44c72403de][^key-d24329294c997e28dcc3aeb9f4560abd][^key-817c5158908b2680aba90ced859fb1fc][^key-d66cc66f8a4ba5357c95f26d659a1b3a][^key-f4512e1ada5599a1d1e25f6e7ea3d688][^key-34b1352e3d96726860394cbdbf636cdc][^key-75f5803f05395f16cd16482a8f435bb6][^key-a3f3bfac3981bc33a304a34eae3bf2bf][^key-465b0c25ae7993d0d48d505de93305c9][^key-ad8e9c100292a8d6ddbcea4b7a38edce][^key-7a0addc65ae5c7c7a9b6196f2701b198][^key-46dd1bc0c595a948696c62f41017b902][^key-f0c316d9d1b0dfd0e226e5ef4e030922]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-b21a11643029f38e83e21b44b6fc5f25] A claimant falls within this section if—

- (a) the claimant is the responsible carer for a child who is aged 1[^key-13a4fde338f6cc3d2992582822bcc35e], or
- (b) the claimant is of a prescribed description.

(2) The Secretary of State may, subject to this Part, impose a work-focused interview requirement on a claimant falling within this section.

(3) The Secretary of State may not impose any other work-related requirement on a claimant falling within this section (and, where a claimant falls within this section, any other work-related requirement previously applying to the claimant ceases to have effect).
