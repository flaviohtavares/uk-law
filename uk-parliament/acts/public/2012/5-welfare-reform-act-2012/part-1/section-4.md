## 4. Basic conditions[^key-f0c316d9d1b0dfd0e226e5ef4e030922]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-8c0bc67bf4e2d84601d26d4f510c429d][^key-73f90fc07ee123401db2e1063a1fd6bf][^key-26b9b62413564c9884149b88d8b4084f][^key-87537b296a6320beec15dc3862f899be][^key-74f76255c5d4411a5eae8bf94275c503][^key-cd7d1176b671212392fb65886b8b0f37][^key-ecc2211bf30ac3ad005b9b28c5941671][^key-7516e0003b0f2e44814375d3d752424a][^key-25dce913c1ffc7616c12d0c7fce4c74a][^key-886b9caa7e3f5574fb4903f94d2feb91][^key-5c0908a1e5e0d139a0e9c1071529e9a7][^key-f0f2cf324346329324ee1b34e5e965f2][^key-42b0006e1d1082a96f911c34f0873862][^key-98a5b3e5b7a7b1a5f51ba62cd29d5d7a][^key-5720373aeab9de67787c8206b92004fe][^key-06bcdc1530362007201b4d95504fe152][^key-141fb4a87f80027c3ae8ec815e138b4f] For the purposes of section 3, a person meets the basic conditions who—

- (a)[^key-0c391747a6eb37df6c781aea0655d881] is at least 18 years old,
- (b) has not reached the qualifying age for state pension credit,
- (c)[^key-caae4ac314e51020af67bb47c85c613c] is in Great Britain,
- (d)[^key-1de272be2370ad316fa5949c1dbc6325][^key-d90d9bc5884cc3ace7ced35685f097fa][^key-ed2156e7ae1fad2945bc7cb1e17dbd77] is not receiving education, and
- (e) has accepted a claimant commitment.

(2)[^key-e0831cd1f6adbef07c6749566e9e32b4] Regulations may provide for exceptions to the requirement to meet any of the basic conditions (and, for joint claimants, may provide for an exception for one or both).

(3)[^key-e0831cd1f6adbef07c6749566e9e32b4] For the basic condition in subsection (1)(a) regulations may specify a different minimum age for prescribed cases.

(4)[^key-8c0bc67bf4e2d84601d26d4f510c429d][^key-73f90fc07ee123401db2e1063a1fd6bf][^key-26b9b62413564c9884149b88d8b4084f][^key-87537b296a6320beec15dc3862f899be][^key-74f76255c5d4411a5eae8bf94275c503][^key-cd7d1176b671212392fb65886b8b0f37][^key-ecc2211bf30ac3ad005b9b28c5941671][^key-7516e0003b0f2e44814375d3d752424a][^key-25dce913c1ffc7616c12d0c7fce4c74a][^key-886b9caa7e3f5574fb4903f94d2feb91][^key-5c0908a1e5e0d139a0e9c1071529e9a7][^key-f0f2cf324346329324ee1b34e5e965f2][^key-42b0006e1d1082a96f911c34f0873862][^key-98a5b3e5b7a7b1a5f51ba62cd29d5d7a][^key-5720373aeab9de67787c8206b92004fe][^key-06bcdc1530362007201b4d95504fe152][^key-141fb4a87f80027c3ae8ec815e138b4f] For the basic condition in subsection (1)(b), the qualifying age for state pension credit is that referred to in section 1(6) of the State Pension Credit Act 2002.

(5)[^key-e0831cd1f6adbef07c6749566e9e32b4] For the basic condition in subsection (1)(c) regulations may—

- (a) specify circumstances in which a person is to be treated as being or not being in Great Britain;
- (b) specify circumstances in which temporary absence from Great Britain is disregarded;
- (c) modify the application of this Part in relation to a person not in Great Britain who is by virtue of paragraph (b) entitled to universal credit.

(6)[^key-e0831cd1f6adbef07c6749566e9e32b4] For the basic condition in subsection (1)(d) regulations may—

- (a) specify what “**receiving education**” means;
- (b) specify circumstances in which a person is to be treated as receiving or not receiving education.

(7)[^key-e0831cd1f6adbef07c6749566e9e32b4] For the basic condition in subsection (1)(e) regulations may specify circumstances in which a person is to be treated as having accepted or not accepted a claimant commitment.
