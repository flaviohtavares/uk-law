## 9. Standard allowance[^c-f0c316d9d1b0dfd0e226e5ef4e030922]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-50acdab3de4e122092fab23027bec07d][^c-ebcae5b8794a34d674adf682ff754e9e][^c-8275d9b23cd26ac1abbc0e73adf925b1][^c-371217fde96c7b28d995e823aff7cdf2][^c-6c8811c75e609554197e8c14442b22ae][^c-1363a1c27674da8f91d0401d9e9204de][^c-fb3e5f25a3aea1ef4d485fa22a098fd3][^c-d4557adcc2073fd1a2459fc4f4426dcf][^c-fd9ecd133c8b87d86b74acb082429290][^c-1ebf17457e5c587230e32917fe119c43][^c-82349a363f029a5d865cd78c7f6bc5cb][^c-dc68e6d7cca8bdc67836ccbd144faa9e][^c-d7bd7d62df709e43a1e776b777a075ea][^c-81c58f122ed343514d4f2a5a3ab98727][^c-a9f9abb02b0c637ed2720a42daf695d1][^c-4705424b9966ac730ed83b921e573fc1][^c-0407da9c40c5632094be837c3df1fd58] The calculation of an award of universal credit is to include an amount by way of an allowance for—

- (a) a single claimant, or
- (b) joint claimants.

(2)[^c-ce690a7895c13b15ceceb8590f81c380] Regulations are to specify the amount to be included under subsection (1).

(3)[^c-ce690a7895c13b15ceceb8590f81c380] Regulations may provide for exceptions to subsection (1).
