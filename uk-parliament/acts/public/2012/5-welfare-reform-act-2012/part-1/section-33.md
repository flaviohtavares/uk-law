## 33. Abolition of benefits[^key-f0c316d9d1b0dfd0e226e5ef4e030922]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following benefits are abolished—

- (a)[^key-8db024e89a5b5e1b237d44f5987b8d1d][^key-3ab619d7004279c618eeb3780ef1206e][^key-fa66f5f127420602cd9ad2c55397f854][^key-14625944ed2cb25d8f4c583b4ac76235][^key-76d086069b4094fa0e08ea07dc16166b][^key-c1c74ff51d8d56d7494982294e76edbb][^key-54347b106e76f0cbd95f5ac95a7aaecd][^key-af3bcccc57b93fe090814cb6d8d6f2a5][^key-7f546e89f955d4433f3e1d2b67d32d31][^key-d837627d620a7c0067d48bbc1b954658][^key-20ca30fb78845607150cfe1ffe06d10c][^key-aa0f8aa5e5cd3b90a5c5a6c5aa7cd1f2][^key-288ecaa4783f29002e9999ced959dafe][^key-e1501f008ae0391698cf580fff2d2c4d][^key-65cf7b48691967217f9c7d8624a9579c][^key-3804d1a25326ac5a39095ddb3d611706][^key-ac18f2dda4768cf1a99636953104d78d][^key-89304f9431d2ab31f4bcce58a8776a6c][^key-a91957e39a33f9f143820bcd1ea40e44][^key-4a5d698a7a1158bb329d8b3b94b93b48] income-based jobseeker's allowance under the Jobseekers Act 1995;
- (b)[^key-8db024e89a5b5e1b237d44f5987b8d1d][^key-3ab619d7004279c618eeb3780ef1206e][^key-fa66f5f127420602cd9ad2c55397f854][^key-14625944ed2cb25d8f4c583b4ac76235][^key-76d086069b4094fa0e08ea07dc16166b][^key-c1c74ff51d8d56d7494982294e76edbb][^key-54347b106e76f0cbd95f5ac95a7aaecd][^key-af3bcccc57b93fe090814cb6d8d6f2a5][^key-7f546e89f955d4433f3e1d2b67d32d31][^key-d837627d620a7c0067d48bbc1b954658][^key-20ca30fb78845607150cfe1ffe06d10c][^key-aa0f8aa5e5cd3b90a5c5a6c5aa7cd1f2][^key-288ecaa4783f29002e9999ced959dafe][^key-e1501f008ae0391698cf580fff2d2c4d][^key-65cf7b48691967217f9c7d8624a9579c][^key-3804d1a25326ac5a39095ddb3d611706][^key-ac18f2dda4768cf1a99636953104d78d][^key-89304f9431d2ab31f4bcce58a8776a6c][^key-a91957e39a33f9f143820bcd1ea40e44][^key-4a5d698a7a1158bb329d8b3b94b93b48] income-related employment and support allowance under Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2007;
- (c)[^key-9e229e4012ee0cf26b6c8f4d4d1953dd] income support under section 124 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992;
- (d)[^key-b540c8d11d9295f71435fc5206796b3d][^key-3b53b038b7f9f5cd490f80ef8895bb2c] housing benefit under section 130 of that Act;
- (e)[^key-524c049b8857c3a88a7aaa9b0e0f1a0b] council tax benefit under section 131 of that Act;
- (f)[^key-0696484efe39e20eab041ba89d56ed61] child tax credit and working tax credit under the Tax Credits Act 2002.

(2)[^key-8db024e89a5b5e1b237d44f5987b8d1d][^key-3ab619d7004279c618eeb3780ef1206e][^key-fa66f5f127420602cd9ad2c55397f854][^key-14625944ed2cb25d8f4c583b4ac76235][^key-76d086069b4094fa0e08ea07dc16166b][^key-c1c74ff51d8d56d7494982294e76edbb][^key-54347b106e76f0cbd95f5ac95a7aaecd][^key-af3bcccc57b93fe090814cb6d8d6f2a5][^key-7f546e89f955d4433f3e1d2b67d32d31][^key-d837627d620a7c0067d48bbc1b954658][^key-20ca30fb78845607150cfe1ffe06d10c][^key-aa0f8aa5e5cd3b90a5c5a6c5aa7cd1f2][^key-288ecaa4783f29002e9999ced959dafe][^key-e1501f008ae0391698cf580fff2d2c4d][^key-65cf7b48691967217f9c7d8624a9579c][^key-3804d1a25326ac5a39095ddb3d611706][^key-ac18f2dda4768cf1a99636953104d78d][^key-89304f9431d2ab31f4bcce58a8776a6c][^key-a91957e39a33f9f143820bcd1ea40e44][^key-4a5d698a7a1158bb329d8b3b94b93b48] In subsection (1)—

- (a) “**income-based jobseeker's allowance**” has the same meaning as in the Jobseekers Act 1995;
- (b) “**income-related employment and support allowance**” means an employment and support allowance entitlement to which is based on section 1(2)(b) of the Welfare Reform Act 2007.
