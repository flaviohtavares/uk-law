## 10. Responsibility for children and young persons[^c-f0c316d9d1b0dfd0e226e5ef4e030922]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-3351afdb1550171d557ef6cc65bcf97b][^c-d2c5f94c18e161e2e5b4d7f6e38c92e1][^c-1d40a9107bbe04464ff44839a67d69b7][^c-945edadda293ffc92b2ef5bc7a5e5a64][^c-3af9a80147dde2b69b6ec0bbeb21ed91][^c-e1b005f2d6b5dd21a21b2fdbdfacb55b][^c-c723163e239a7ece79d6e82ed2a9a231][^c-69a64b1b9263786c308e7841ca7a64f1][^c-30c5bab98b624fde8dce8d4bd8c87e42][^c-f32dfc1726f5aafa477ae5bf042c78da][^c-b22c08273b1a9960a1f8b3b09b003479][^c-6c8a64f8a2227abf6da1b1ce16bb96f3][^c-582f1b55ce9bffc9980fcee74f13feec][^c-fd15ead3b040f690536f975c3559b4ab][^c-3b486a7d2c0820c9086b361117771ccd][^c-bac950a32858add0e6a8906f70f9c1c8][^c-25d4fc50bcec8b2df5a18f01a5d5cd77] The calculation of an award of universal credit is to include an amount for each child or qualifying young person for whom a claimant is responsible.

(1A)[^c-0627bbcc611e2251532692da54060647] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-d55eee51f8d8fe800a2dc41b5d699cb4] Regulations may make provision for the inclusion of an additional amount for each[^c-8197c3a23c87c677f699c4f8f0359ca6] child or qualifying young person for whom a claimant is responsible who[^c-3174c39ab88958559fe8b36da4bdf048] is disabled.

(3)[^c-d55eee51f8d8fe800a2dc41b5d699cb4] Regulations are to specify, or provide for the calculation of, amounts to be included under subsection (1) or (2).

(4)[^c-d55eee51f8d8fe800a2dc41b5d699cb4] Regulations may provide for exceptions to subsection (1) [^c-62ffe4ad68fb509205561e6d404157f0]....

(5)[^c-d55eee51f8d8fe800a2dc41b5d699cb4] In this Part, “**qualifying young person**” means a person of a prescribed description.
