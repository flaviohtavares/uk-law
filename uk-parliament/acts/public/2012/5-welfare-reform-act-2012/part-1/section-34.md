# Welfare Reform Act 2012, section 34

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 34. Universal credit and state pension credit[^c-f0c316d9d1b0dfd0e226e5ef4e030922]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

Schedule 4 provides for a housing element of state pension credit in consequence of the abolition of housing benefit by section 33.
