## 7. Basis of awards[^c-f0c316d9d1b0dfd0e226e5ef4e030922]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-770f68c29d37efb3e202dc0648584129][^c-dbd237179f3d6d7e50841b7c4c28f3d4][^c-c7bda5a6bfee83a9f78694ffab140316][^c-6fff777eb477c56d08ab23719cb1136a][^c-1c8c4eef89c9e19bfd8201fb391a218c][^c-efc6b2e48f16ecb996d1a7ee1fcdb794][^c-0392668c1103e670031f3fee6791f69b][^c-14d09a7d6f2cb8166b552ae44c4101b3][^c-7949d9d9fdbd11b8949e49d086a236f8][^c-878152578f66b15275dff10d57cb5a5c][^c-fede272209cebfbc2e43197df3ff010d][^c-29d0fb02b4a85f8eb64bbfb07114a2cc][^c-aee5a0bd7c8c62ba92b7c9650ef724b9][^c-9be3cb5a8fa081d166866bd371db168c][^c-834767482adb97eb4eacf4e56de2ee51][^c-5ff5b38e6d22cf7c5bfad905292e1767][^c-06044da6787b71953668fc9eeeafd640] Universal credit is payable in respect of each complete assessment period within a period of entitlement.

(2)[^c-ce4aaecbaa1b157b00d2227d845c0313] In this Part an “assessment period” is a period of a prescribed duration.

(3)[^c-ce4aaecbaa1b157b00d2227d845c0313] Regulations may make provision—

- (a) about when an assessment period is to start;
- (b) for universal credit to be payable in respect of a period shorter than an assessment period;
- (c) about the amount payable in respect of a period shorter than an assessment period.

(4)[^c-770f68c29d37efb3e202dc0648584129][^c-dbd237179f3d6d7e50841b7c4c28f3d4][^c-c7bda5a6bfee83a9f78694ffab140316][^c-6fff777eb477c56d08ab23719cb1136a][^c-1c8c4eef89c9e19bfd8201fb391a218c][^c-efc6b2e48f16ecb996d1a7ee1fcdb794][^c-0392668c1103e670031f3fee6791f69b][^c-14d09a7d6f2cb8166b552ae44c4101b3][^c-7949d9d9fdbd11b8949e49d086a236f8][^c-878152578f66b15275dff10d57cb5a5c][^c-fede272209cebfbc2e43197df3ff010d][^c-29d0fb02b4a85f8eb64bbfb07114a2cc][^c-aee5a0bd7c8c62ba92b7c9650ef724b9][^c-9be3cb5a8fa081d166866bd371db168c][^c-834767482adb97eb4eacf4e56de2ee51][^c-5ff5b38e6d22cf7c5bfad905292e1767][^c-06044da6787b71953668fc9eeeafd640] In subsection (1) “**period of entitlement**” means a period during which entitlement to universal credit subsists.
