## 19. Claimants subject to no work-related requirements[^c-f0c316d9d1b0dfd0e226e5ef4e030922]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-6bcc736d6cbfd6bff567f02f61b1850d][^c-9110241453da1f235cca724816074388][^c-3194e6b4b48ecb9e5881cf44801a68d0][^c-f6563021803fb8c243c554d6b8df30f4][^c-c8bd062eb6438d929b0c1f16b28ef809][^c-dad2e7fc24965e5f436c2747392d6169][^c-72edb8251874f570b1a25367e981ef55][^c-a397db9fb45830a310c64e0a88daed91][^c-0e5f7425d0a73313899be2c508a26267][^c-9f299acf0d8e4d11678e2c1ce65d80d7][^c-6d6e45e07b07a2ca179d79c5ab98204c][^c-6c3d072f016d6ce7433dd5dd22b1b628][^c-c4465c88ed2ca560af24babffeb97d03][^c-10203d5295cc6d7c7c16286093713e42][^c-f2f0a6b49af227fbdf5a56ec630c1d83][^c-dcf9d059e909322ff16344b0fa5be2b9][^c-08076e9a85cd0268db8e04f87b378371] The Secretary of State may not impose any work-related requirement on a claimant falling within this section.

(2) A claimant falls within this section if—

- (a)[^c-6bcc736d6cbfd6bff567f02f61b1850d][^c-9110241453da1f235cca724816074388][^c-3194e6b4b48ecb9e5881cf44801a68d0][^c-f6563021803fb8c243c554d6b8df30f4][^c-c8bd062eb6438d929b0c1f16b28ef809][^c-dad2e7fc24965e5f436c2747392d6169][^c-72edb8251874f570b1a25367e981ef55][^c-a397db9fb45830a310c64e0a88daed91][^c-0e5f7425d0a73313899be2c508a26267][^c-9f299acf0d8e4d11678e2c1ce65d80d7][^c-6d6e45e07b07a2ca179d79c5ab98204c][^c-6c3d072f016d6ce7433dd5dd22b1b628][^c-c4465c88ed2ca560af24babffeb97d03][^c-10203d5295cc6d7c7c16286093713e42][^c-f2f0a6b49af227fbdf5a56ec630c1d83][^c-dcf9d059e909322ff16344b0fa5be2b9][^c-08076e9a85cd0268db8e04f87b378371] the claimant has limited capability for work and work-related activity,
- (b)[^c-6bcc736d6cbfd6bff567f02f61b1850d][^c-9110241453da1f235cca724816074388][^c-3194e6b4b48ecb9e5881cf44801a68d0][^c-f6563021803fb8c243c554d6b8df30f4][^c-c8bd062eb6438d929b0c1f16b28ef809][^c-dad2e7fc24965e5f436c2747392d6169][^c-72edb8251874f570b1a25367e981ef55][^c-a397db9fb45830a310c64e0a88daed91][^c-0e5f7425d0a73313899be2c508a26267][^c-9f299acf0d8e4d11678e2c1ce65d80d7][^c-6d6e45e07b07a2ca179d79c5ab98204c][^c-6c3d072f016d6ce7433dd5dd22b1b628][^c-c4465c88ed2ca560af24babffeb97d03][^c-10203d5295cc6d7c7c16286093713e42][^c-f2f0a6b49af227fbdf5a56ec630c1d83][^c-dcf9d059e909322ff16344b0fa5be2b9][^c-08076e9a85cd0268db8e04f87b378371] the claimant has regular and substantial caring responsibilities for a severely disabled person,
- (c)[^c-6bcc736d6cbfd6bff567f02f61b1850d][^c-9110241453da1f235cca724816074388][^c-3194e6b4b48ecb9e5881cf44801a68d0][^c-f6563021803fb8c243c554d6b8df30f4][^c-c8bd062eb6438d929b0c1f16b28ef809][^c-dad2e7fc24965e5f436c2747392d6169][^c-72edb8251874f570b1a25367e981ef55][^c-a397db9fb45830a310c64e0a88daed91][^c-0e5f7425d0a73313899be2c508a26267][^c-9f299acf0d8e4d11678e2c1ce65d80d7][^c-6d6e45e07b07a2ca179d79c5ab98204c][^c-6c3d072f016d6ce7433dd5dd22b1b628][^c-c4465c88ed2ca560af24babffeb97d03][^c-10203d5295cc6d7c7c16286093713e42][^c-f2f0a6b49af227fbdf5a56ec630c1d83][^c-dcf9d059e909322ff16344b0fa5be2b9][^c-08076e9a85cd0268db8e04f87b378371] the claimant is the responsible carer for a child under the age of 1, or
- (d)[^c-405d548eb1e6e3a47f4ebbf5f0c0da30] the claimant is of a prescribed description.

(3)[^c-405d548eb1e6e3a47f4ebbf5f0c0da30] Regulations under subsection (2)(d) may in particular make provision by reference to one or more of the following—

- (a) hours worked;
- (b) earnings or income;
- (c) the amount of universal credit payable.

(4)[^c-405d548eb1e6e3a47f4ebbf5f0c0da30] Regulations under subsection (3) may—

- (a) in the case of a claimant who is a member of the couple, make provision by reference to the claimant alone or by reference to the members of the couple together;
- (b) make provision for estimating or calculating any matter for the purpose of the regulations.

(5)[^c-6bcc736d6cbfd6bff567f02f61b1850d][^c-9110241453da1f235cca724816074388][^c-3194e6b4b48ecb9e5881cf44801a68d0][^c-f6563021803fb8c243c554d6b8df30f4][^c-c8bd062eb6438d929b0c1f16b28ef809][^c-dad2e7fc24965e5f436c2747392d6169][^c-72edb8251874f570b1a25367e981ef55][^c-a397db9fb45830a310c64e0a88daed91][^c-0e5f7425d0a73313899be2c508a26267][^c-9f299acf0d8e4d11678e2c1ce65d80d7][^c-6d6e45e07b07a2ca179d79c5ab98204c][^c-6c3d072f016d6ce7433dd5dd22b1b628][^c-c4465c88ed2ca560af24babffeb97d03][^c-10203d5295cc6d7c7c16286093713e42][^c-f2f0a6b49af227fbdf5a56ec630c1d83][^c-dcf9d059e909322ff16344b0fa5be2b9][^c-08076e9a85cd0268db8e04f87b378371] Where a claimant falls within this section, any work-related requirement previously applying to the claimant ceases to have effect.

(6)[^c-6bcc736d6cbfd6bff567f02f61b1850d][^c-9110241453da1f235cca724816074388][^c-3194e6b4b48ecb9e5881cf44801a68d0][^c-f6563021803fb8c243c554d6b8df30f4][^c-c8bd062eb6438d929b0c1f16b28ef809][^c-dad2e7fc24965e5f436c2747392d6169][^c-72edb8251874f570b1a25367e981ef55][^c-a397db9fb45830a310c64e0a88daed91][^c-0e5f7425d0a73313899be2c508a26267][^c-9f299acf0d8e4d11678e2c1ce65d80d7][^c-6d6e45e07b07a2ca179d79c5ab98204c][^c-6c3d072f016d6ce7433dd5dd22b1b628][^c-c4465c88ed2ca560af24babffeb97d03][^c-10203d5295cc6d7c7c16286093713e42][^c-f2f0a6b49af227fbdf5a56ec630c1d83][^c-dcf9d059e909322ff16344b0fa5be2b9][^c-08076e9a85cd0268db8e04f87b378371] In this Part “**responsible carer**”, in relation to a child means—

- (a) a single person who is responsible for the child, or
- (b) a person who is a member of a couple where—
  - (i) the person or the other member of the couple is responsible for the child, and
  - (ii) the person has been nominated by the couple jointly as responsible for the child.
