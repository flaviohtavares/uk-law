## 2. Claims[^key-f0c316d9d1b0dfd0e226e5ef4e030922]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-f72d7b57c59c398d35e8661f53977b62][^key-7a253d6c4d14f150cea047515c5f2e80][^key-ad32f56e4115dd236f98c7552a6661cc][^key-61ba2c44614f1525e4573cef4783e2c4][^key-7b1ed8658c242387655f321dce023d01][^key-cb1a8c5341854ec816fe79c77842825e][^key-30379bd091ab22f3c6ad28208b59d316][^key-10d55900750edd74474cb437093a1a84][^key-97586622617b3e77f37da821c10dad0e][^key-d6629ce4bbd389e9453df460fb6fa8e0][^key-b23b52a723e385b54b3caa51b7a428fb][^key-c11b093c0343f15e2d4b907e973811c0][^key-0c3b6d818dd70980b966720c59fcbdc4][^key-cd3d0cc8b70e2ecf70e4ecd6cb006478][^key-a3c23b30cc59556db1303d81bf79413e][^key-1a102141d5cf93913e60fc022ae62987][^key-4702f5f7c6d3658238e7b6e49539a371] A claim may be made for universal credit by—

- (a) a single person, or
- (b) members of a couple jointly.

(2)[^key-e6504a94dd75cc525e874197464f635e] Regulations may specify circumstances in which a member of a couple may make a claim as a single person.
