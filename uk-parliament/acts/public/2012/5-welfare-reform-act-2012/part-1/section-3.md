## 3. Entitlement[^key-2e760ff8e402253f53b759cf19d4184f][^key-f0c316d9d1b0dfd0e226e5ef4e030922][^key-ac7d9bc2c8f52586b008037fac853d03][^key-6a652efa14a5eed105fb77180da143e4][^key-a648ebe3789be16757d067ce584ea56e][^key-00c664821700c13d09f99995d426faac][^key-70f81b09b13556fb106f38caac7019c8][^key-6f18631e1be688c045e7e12a0d842d79][^key-3d40bbac1a7eeeb295bd7b6e096822b0][^key-8cc0d711e06d0889eb6765dbd81b9056][^key-edc5a370cb1d4d054de19f67e9735fe5][^key-f5ad57c4af44275f53947dab7c5bf5c6][^key-872cae4b1d9cbfd74fbad3dcaf92455e][^key-ce6ebaee8b22196e84fe251f234b0768][^key-e313fbd1ac2c6af279ec35e0ee24cc05][^key-63acabe6de438a8ad83874724061a12d][^key-1af7f6a705875e64a9d4962eaf1bad6c][^key-8b0a87ebeb4666dd5876152231276d7f][^key-f0c316d9d1b0dfd0e226e5ef4e030922]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A single claimant is entitled to universal credit if the claimant meets—

- (a) the basic conditions, and
- (b) the financial conditions for a single claimant.

(2) Joint claimants are jointly entitled to universal credit if—

- (a) each of them meets the basic conditions, and
- (b) they meet the financial conditions for joint claimants.
