# Welfare Reform Act 2012, section 77

## 77. Personal independence payment

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-15eefc1f44827f17142bf7e86f757adf][^c-305155b676b9232912bac925c56cb53f] An allowance known as personal independence payment is payable in accordance with this Part.

(2)[^c-15eefc1f44827f17142bf7e86f757adf][^c-305155b676b9232912bac925c56cb53f] A person's entitlement to personal independence payment may be an entitlement to—

- (a) the daily living component (see section 78);
- (b) the mobility component (see section 79); or
- (c) both those components.

(3)[^c-b381752e0a8a5673579411438536c67e][^c-15eefc1f44827f17142bf7e86f757adf][^c-305155b676b9232912bac925c56cb53f] A person is not entitled to personal independence payment unless the person meets prescribed conditions relating to residence and presence in Great Britain.

(4)[^c-88f2ace89d846b6a2236297e355eb38b] A person is not entitled to personal independence payment while they are entitled to Scottish adult disability living allowance,[^c-950e7370671b99b7b33fa0106c8b12a7] pension age disability payment,[^c-5ea6c517d66dbb1a113ac8ef252fe57b] adult disability payment or child disability payment[^c-33d15df3ddfbe88f5c9a971c555d4d63].
