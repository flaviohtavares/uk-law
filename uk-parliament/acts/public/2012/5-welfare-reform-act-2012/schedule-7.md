## SCHEDULE 7 — Jobseeker's allowance in interim period: consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 48

### Jobseekers Act 1995 (c. 18)

**1.**[^c-d864e4732690b06f366dbeba978a963e] The Jobseekers Act 1995 is amended as follows.

**2.**[^c-f0927b838e6f9086d1ee753f559479aa] (1)[^c-37b742083e1eb844c2fcadbfa5c894f8] Section 8 (attendance etc) is amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-37b742083e1eb844c2fcadbfa5c894f8]

(2) In subsection (1A)(a) (as originally enacted), for “the Secretary of State” there is substituted “an employment officer“. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-37b742083e1eb844c2fcadbfa5c894f8]

(3) In subsection (2), for paragraphs (a) to (c) there is substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-37b742083e1eb844c2fcadbfa5c894f8] “ provide for entitlement to a jobseeker's allowance to cease at such time as may be determined in accordance with any such regulations if, when a person fails to comply with such regulations, that person (or, if that person is a member of a joint-claim couple, either member of the couple) does not make prescribed contact with an employment officer within a prescribed period of the failure. ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-37b742083e1eb844c2fcadbfa5c894f8]

**3.**[^c-a9315d5a824bcf57d185bbea1392fb63] In section 16(3)(b) and 17(4) (in both places) for “good cause” there is substituted “ a good reason ”.

**Not yet in force.**

**4.** In section 17A (employment schemes), in subsection (5)(c) for “jobseeker's agreement to which a person is a party” there is substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f15f40fd144252b78ba109319b32e8f3] “ claimant commitment accepted by a person ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f15f40fd144252b78ba109319b32e8f3].

**5.**[^c-faf58c6c0212343a4aef2f6966c8d36f] Section 20 (exemptions from section 19) is amended as follows— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e01711a03a57cd054ddcfbe78aa1305]

- (a) in the heading, after “19” there is inserted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e01711a03a57cd054ddcfbe78aa1305] “ and 19A ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e01711a03a57cd054ddcfbe78aa1305];
- (b) in subsection (1), for “prevent payment” there is substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e01711a03a57cd054ddcfbe78aa1305] “ authorise reduction ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e01711a03a57cd054ddcfbe78aa1305];
- (c) in subsection (2), for “19” there is substituted “19A” and for “(5)” there is substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e01711a03a57cd054ddcfbe78aa1305] “ (2)(c) to (g) ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e01711a03a57cd054ddcfbe78aa1305];
- (d) in subsection (3), for “19(6)(b) or (d)” there is substituted “19(2)(b) or (d)” and for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e01711a03a57cd054ddcfbe78aa1305] “ just cause ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e01711a03a57cd054ddcfbe78aa1305] there is substituted “good reason”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e01711a03a57cd054ddcfbe78aa1305]

**6.**[^c-ead2d404ce0eccb601bdb3a42e9c8331] Sections 20C and 20D (uncommenced provision relating to sanctions for violent conduct etc) are repealed.

**7.**[^c-00e9386a0d930a6a4ba26484355f4b02] In section 22 (members of the forces), in subsection (2), for “section 19(6)(b)” there is substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-697fbdac94a282b0d08d6647c87bccf9] “ section 19(2)(b) ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-697fbdac94a282b0d08d6647c87bccf9].

**8.**[^c-9d340fdff0aaac343a429ca318dd5e4e] In section 35 (interpretation), in subsection (1), before the definition of “jobseeking period” there is inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-26e29792fc383e4bad73d82dd146abbc]

> “**jobseeker's direction *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-26e29792fc383e4bad73d82dd146abbc]**” has the meaning given by section 19A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-26e29792fc383e4bad73d82dd146abbc]

.

**9.**[^c-e1f482767aeed0987874cb32129fe44b] In section 36 (regulations and orders), after subsection (1) there is inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-df35899951ff708585dc43f29b8c10f0]

> (1A) Subsection (1) does not apply to an order under section 35(1) in relation to employment officers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-df35899951ff708585dc43f29b8c10f0]

**10.** (1)[^c-77dfddf730d74e161f4887de92af879c] Schedule 1 (supplementary) is amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e8c85d6ff2094791072c24b25a2cd0bf]

(2) In paragraph 8(b), for “entered into a jobseeker's agreement” there is substituted “accepted a claimant commitment“.

(3)[^c-6bed0e2a74b9cc05f0b43579df56293e] In paragraph 10, at the end there is inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c75f69bd1a1edf0e2b824ffbf7c27708]

> (6) References in sub-paragraphs (1) and (2) to an income-based jobseeker's allowance include a payment by way of such an allowance under section 19C. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c75f69bd1a1edf0e2b824ffbf7c27708]

### Social Security Fraud Act 2001 (c. 11)

- (a) at the beginning there is inserted “Except in prescribed circumstances”;

  .
