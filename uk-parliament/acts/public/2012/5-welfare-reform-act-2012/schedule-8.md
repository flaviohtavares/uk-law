# Welfare Reform Act 2012, SCHEDULE 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (68).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 8 — Social fund discretionary payments: consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 70

**1.**[^c-b6d0f2534d4f43d37f4f59642ebb9360] The Social Security Administration Act 1992 is amended as follows.

**2.**[^c-5c7a912520cdc1ea2f569b26b93f895e] In section 78(4) (recovery of funeral payments out of social fund), after “Payments” there is inserted “ out of the social fund ”.

**3.**[^c-e3144f8409bdd886ac876d87e5e8eb66] In section 79 (recovery of Northern Ireland payments), in paragraph (c), for the words from “under subsection” to the end there is substituted “ under section 71(8) ”.

**4.**[^c-d0a2c95620965a8385010dcd99b81a8a] In Part 1 of Schedule 4 (persons employed in social security administration), under the heading “Former officers”, after the entry for “A social fund officer” there is inserted—

> The social fund Commissioner.
>
> A social fund inspector.
>
> A member of any staff employed in connection with the social fund.
