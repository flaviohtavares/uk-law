## 46. Sanctions

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-47d06843957111854bcb9950399178ef][^key-01e3ae232303bc8b84b31489895718f2][^key-9a14f61c2e001e48d73f7bf71a24c5a3] For section 19 of the Jobseekers Act 1995 (circumstances in which a jobseeker's allowance is not payable) there is substituted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]

> **19. Higher-level sanctions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]**
>
> (1) The amount of an award of a jobseeker's allowance is to be reduced in accordance with this section in the event of a failure by the claimant which is sanctionable under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (2) It is a failure sanctionable under this section if a claimant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) through misconduct loses employment as an employed earner; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) without a good reason voluntarily leaves such employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (c) without a good reason refuses or fails to apply for, or accept if offered, a situation in any employment which an employment officer has informed him is vacant or about to become vacant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (d) without a good reason neglects to avail himself of a reasonable opportunity of employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (e) without a good reason fails to participate in any scheme within section 17A(1) which is prescribed for the purposes of this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (3) For the purposes of subsection (2)(b), in such circumstances as may be prescribed, including in particular where a person has been dismissed by his employer by reason of redundancy within the meaning of section 139(1) of the Employment Rights Act 1996 after volunteering or agreeing to be so dismissed, a person who might otherwise be treated as having left his employment voluntarily is to be treated as not having left voluntarily. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (4) Regulations are to provide for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) the amount of a reduction under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) the period for which such a reduction has effect, not exceeding three years in relation to any failure sanctionable under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (5) Regulations under subsection (4)(b) may in particular provide for the period of a reduction to depend on either or both of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) the number of failures by the claimant sanctionable under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) the period between such failures. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (6) Regulations may provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) for cases in which no reduction is to be made under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) for a reduction under this section made in relation to an award that is terminated to be applied to any new award made within a prescribed period of the termination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (7) During any period for which the amount of a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance is reduced under this section by virtue of a failure by one of the claimants which is sanctionable under this section, the allowance is payable to the other member of the couple. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> **19A. Other sanctions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]**
>
> (1) The amount of an award of a jobseeker's allowance is to be reduced in accordance with this section in the event of a failure by the claimant which is sanctionable under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (2) It is a failure sanctionable under this section if a claimant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) without a good reason fails to comply with regulations under section 8(1) or (1A); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) without a good reason fails to comply with regulations under section 17A; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (c) without a good reason refuses or fails to carry out a jobseeker's direction which was reasonable having regard to his circumstances; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (d) without a good reason neglects to avail himself of a reasonable opportunity of a place on a training scheme or employment programme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (e) without a good reason refuses or fails to apply for, or accept if offered, a place on such a scheme or programme which an employment officer has informed him is vacant or about to become vacant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (f) without a good reason gives up a place on such a scheme or programme or fails to attend such a scheme or programme having been given a place on it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (g) through misconduct loses a place on such a scheme or programme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (3) But a failure is not sanctionable under this section if it is also sanctionable under section 19. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (4) Regulations are to provide for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) the amount of a reduction under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) the period for which such a reduction has effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (5) Regulations under subsection (4)(b) may provide that a reduction under this section in relation to any failure is to have effect for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) a period continuing until the claimant meets a compliance condition specified by the Secretary of State, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) a fixed period not exceeding 26 weeks which is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>   - (i) specified in the regulations, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8] determined in any case by the Secretary of State, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (c) a combination of both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (6) In subsection (5)(a) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]**compliance condition *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) a condition that the failure ceases, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) a condition relating to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>   - (i) future compliance with a jobseeker's direction or any requirement imposed under section 8(1) or (1A) or 17A of this Act, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8] future avoidance of the failures referred to in subsection (2)(d) to (g). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (7) A compliance condition specified under subsection (5)(a) may be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) revoked or varied by the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) notified to the claimant in such manner as the Secretary of State may determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (8) The period fixed under subsection (5)(b) may in particular depend on either or both of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) the number of failures by the claimant sanctionable under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) the period between such failures. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (9) Regulations may provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) for cases in which no reduction is to be made under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) for a reduction under this section made in relation to an award that is terminated to be applied to any new award made within a prescribed period of the termination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (10) During any period for which the amount of a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance is reduced under this section by virtue of a failure by one of the claimants which is sanctionable under this section, the allowance is payable to the other member of the couple. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (11) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) “**jobseeker's direction *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]**” means a direction given by an employment officer (in such manner as he thinks fit) with a view to achieving one or both of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>   - (i) assisting the claimant to find employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8] improving the claimant's prospects of being employed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) “training scheme“ and “employment programme” have such meaning as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> **19B. Claimants ceasing to be available for employment etc (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]**
>
> (1) Regulations may make provision for reduction of the amount of an award of a jobseeker's allowance other than a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance if the claimant— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) was previously entitled to such an allowance or was a member of a couple entitled to a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) ceased to be so entitled by failing to comply with the condition in section 1(2)(a) or (c) (availability for employment and actively seeking employment). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (2) Regulations may make provision for reduction of the amount of a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance if one of the claimants— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) was previously entitled to a jobseeker's allowance other than a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) ceased to be so entitled by failing to comply with the condition in section 1(2)(a) or (c). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (3) Regulations may make provision for reduction of the amount of an award of joint-claim jobseeker's allowance if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) the couple were previously entitled to a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance but ceased to be so entitled by either or both of them failing to comply with the condition in section 1(2)(a) or (c), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) either member of the couple was a member of another couple previously entitled to such an allowance and that couple ceased to be so entitled by that person failing to comply with the condition in section 1(2)(a) or (c). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (4) Regulations are to provide for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) the amount of a reduction under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) the period for which such a reduction has effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (5) The period referred to in subsection (4)(b) must not include any period after the end of the period of 13 weeks beginning with the day on which the claimant's previous entitlement ceased. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (6) Regulations under subsection (4)(b) may in particular provide for the period of a reduction to depend on either or both of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) the number of occasions on which a claimant's entitlement has ceased as specified in subsection (1), (2) or (3); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) the period between such occasions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (7) Regulations may provide for a reduction under this section made in relation to an award that is terminated to be applied to any new award made within a prescribed period of the termination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (8) During any period for which the amount of a joint-claim jobseeker's allowance is reduced under this section by virtue of a failure by one of the claimants to comply with the condition in section 1(2)(a) or (c), the allowance is payable to the other member of the couple. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> **19C. Hardship payments (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]**
>
> (1) Regulations may make provision for the making of payments (“hardship payments”) by way of a jobseeker's allowance to a claimant where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) the amount of the claimant's award is reduced under sections 19 to 19B, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) the claimant is or will be in hardship. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> (2) Regulations under this section may in particular make provision as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
>
> - (a) circumstances in which a claimant is to be treated as being or not being in hardship; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (b) matters to be taken into account in determining whether a claimant is or will be in hardship; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (c) requirements or conditions to be met by a claimant in order to receive hardship payments; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (d) the amount or rate of hardship payments; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (e) the period for which hardship payments may be made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
> - (f) whether hardship payments are recoverable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b5bb36bfd3d0d2c9fbfa316465b1aad8]
