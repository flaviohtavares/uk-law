## 55. Work experience etc[^key-4b7d1f5c50be17b0ee144aec11771987]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 13 of the Welfare Reform Act 2007 (work-related activity), after subsection (7) there is inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9b63e0a5d2569587b58f5a518878a616]

> (8) The reference to activity in subsection (7) includes work experience or a work placement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9b63e0a5d2569587b58f5a518878a616]
