# Welfare Reform Act 2012, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (68).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2012/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 44. Claimant commitment for jobseeker's allowance

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-bc402000139b995c23beade0604465b0] The Jobseekers Act 1995 is amended as follows.

(2)[^c-c2d01551c8f267a6678a09c5445d5a73] In section 1 (the jobseeker's allowance), in subsection (2)(b) for “entered into a jobseeker's agreement which remains in force” there is substituted “ accepted a claimant commitment ”.

(3) For section 9 (the jobseeker's agreement) there is substituted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]

> **9. Claimant commitment (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]**
>
> (1) For the purposes of this Act a “claimant commitment” is a record of a claimant's responsibilities in relation to an award of a jobseeker's allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> (2) A claimant commitment shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> - (a) be prepared by an employment officer, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
> - (b) be in such form as the Secretary of State thinks fit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
> - (c) include any prescribed information, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
> - (d) include any other information an employment officer or the Secretary of State considers it appropriate to include. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> (3) Information included in a claimant commitment under subsection (2)(d) may include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> - (a) information in respect of the conditions mentioned in section 1(2)(a) and (c); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
> - (b) details of any requirement imposed on the claimant by virtue of regulations under section 8 or 17A, or under a jobseeker's direction; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
> - (c) details of any consequences of a failure to comply with such a requirement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> (4) A claimant shall not be invited to accept a claimant commitment by an employment officer unless, in the opinion of the employment officer, the conditions mentioned in section 1(2)(a) and (c) would be satisfied with respect to the claimant if he were to act in accordance with, or be treated as acting in accordance with, the proposed claimant commitment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> (5) The employment officer may, and if asked to do so by the claimant shall forthwith, refer a proposed claimant commitment to the Secretary of State for him to determine— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> - (a) whether, if the claimant were to act in accordance with the proposed claimant commitment, he would satisfy— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>   - (i) the condition mentioned in section 1(2)(a), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45] the condition mentioned in section 1(2)(c), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
> - (b) whether it is reasonable to expect the claimant to have to act in accordance with the proposed claimant commitment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> (6) A reference under subsection (5) may only relate to information included in the proposed claimant commitment under subsection (3)(a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> (7) On a reference under subsection (5) the Secretary of State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> - (a) shall, so far as practicable, dispose of it in accordance with this section before the end of the period of 14 days from the date of the reference; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
> - (b) may give such directions, with respect to the terms of the proposed claimant commitment, as the Secretary of State considers appropriate; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
> - (c) may direct that, if such conditions as he considers appropriate are satisfied, the proposed claimant commitment is to be treated (if accepted) as having been accepted by the claimant on such date as may be specified in the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> (8) Regulations may provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> - (a) for such matters as may be prescribed to be taken into account by the Secretary of State in giving a direction under subsection (7)(c), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
> - (b) for such persons as may be prescribed to be notified of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>   - (i) any determination of the Secretary of State under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45] any direction given by the Secretary of State under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> (9) Regulations may provide that, in prescribed circumstances, a claimant is to be treated as having satisfied the condition mentioned in section 1(2)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> (10) For the purposes of this Act a claimant accepts a claimant commitment if, and only if, the claimant accepts the most up-to-date version of it in such manner as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]

(4) For section 10 (variation of jobseeker's agreement) there is substituted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]

> **10. Variation of claimant commitment (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]**
>
> (1) A claimant commitment may be varied by an employment officer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> (2) An employment officer shall not vary a claimant commitment unless, in the opinion of the employment officer, the conditions mentioned in section 1(2)(a) and (c) would continue to be satisfied with respect to the claimant if he were to act in accordance with, or be treated as acting in accordance with, the varied claimant commitment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> (3) An employment officer shall, before making a relevant variation of a claimant commitment, notify the claimant of the proposed variation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> (4) For the purposes of this section a “relevant variation” of a claimant commitment means a variation which relates to information to be included in the claimant commitment in respect of the conditions mentioned in section 1(2)(a) and (c). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> (5) The employment officer may, and if asked to do so by the claimant in prescribed circumstances, shall forthwith refer a relevant variation of a claimant commitment proposed by the employment officer or requested by the claimant to the Secretary of State to determine— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> - (a) whether, if the claimant were to act in accordance with the claimant commitment as proposed to be varied, he would satisfy— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>   - (i) the condition mentioned in section 1(2)(a), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45] the condition mentioned in section 1(2)(c), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
> - (b) in the case of a variation proposed by the employment officer, whether it is reasonable to expect the claimant to have to act in accordance with the claimant commitment as proposed to be varied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> (6) On a reference under subsection (5) the Secretary of State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> - (a) shall, so far as practicable, dispose of it in accordance with this section before the end of the period of 14 days from the date of the reference, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
> - (b) shall give such directions as he considers appropriate as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>   - (i) whether the claimant commitment should be varied, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45] if so, the terms on which the claimant is to accept the varied claimant commitment, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
> - (c) may direct that, if such conditions as he considers appropriate are satisfied, the claimant commitment, as proposed to be varied, is to be treated (if accepted) as having been accepted by the claimant on such date as may be specified in the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> (7) Regulations may provide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>
> - (a) for such matters as may be prescribed to be taken into account by the Secretary of State in giving a direction under subsection (6)(b) or (c), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
> - (b) for such persons as may be prescribed to be notified of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>   - (i) any determination of the Secretary of State under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45] any direction given by the Secretary of State under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]

(5)[^c-e9b7ac5da0170435026bf86c51bd0795] In section 35 (interpretation), in subsection (1), after the definition of “employment” there is inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]

> “employment officer”, for any purpose of this Act, means an officer of the Secretary of State or such other person as may be designated for that purpose by an order made by the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72140115f405a820d23de7f1eb021a45]
