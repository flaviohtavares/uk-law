## 56. Hardship payments[^c-821c7adc41ba339f42bb72df17dfed54]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In the Welfare Reform Act 2007 after section 16 there is inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-402e37cd0dcd7c0cd20e67bee012b3df]

> **16A. Hardship payments (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-402e37cd0dcd7c0cd20e67bee012b3df]**
>
> (1) Regulations may make provision for the making of payments (“hardship payments”) by way of an employment and support allowance to a person where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-402e37cd0dcd7c0cd20e67bee012b3df]
>
> - (a) the amount otherwise payable to the person in respect of an employment and support allowance is reduced by virtue of regulations under section 11(3), 12(3) or 13(3), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-402e37cd0dcd7c0cd20e67bee012b3df]
> - (b) the person is or will be in hardship. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-402e37cd0dcd7c0cd20e67bee012b3df]
>
> (2) Regulations under this section may in particular make provision as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-402e37cd0dcd7c0cd20e67bee012b3df]
>
> - (a) circumstances in which a person is to be treated as being or not being in hardship; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-402e37cd0dcd7c0cd20e67bee012b3df]
> - (b) matters to be taken into account in determining whether a person is or will be in hardship; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-402e37cd0dcd7c0cd20e67bee012b3df]
> - (c) requirements or conditions to be met by a person in order to receive hardship payments; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-402e37cd0dcd7c0cd20e67bee012b3df]
> - (d) the amount or rate of hardship payments; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-402e37cd0dcd7c0cd20e67bee012b3df]
> - (e) the period for which hardship payments may be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-402e37cd0dcd7c0cd20e67bee012b3df]
