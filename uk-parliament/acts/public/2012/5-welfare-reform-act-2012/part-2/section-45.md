## 45. Interviews[^c-2c0f035d36f6c5363cb1ae834e661552]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 8 of the Jobseekers Act 1995 (attendance, information and evidence), in subsections (1)(a) and (1A)(a) for “attend at such place and at such time” there is substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae298e8c1a1413e2acaeb1cebe12637] “ participate in an interview in such manner, time and place ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6ae298e8c1a1413e2acaeb1cebe12637].
