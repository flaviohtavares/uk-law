# Welfare Reform Act 2012, section 54

## 54. Claimant commitment for employment and support allowance

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-6f63527c2447f2b1d973e6f8a8bca430][^key-a07c5745e43c708529b7677a6469072c] The Welfare Reform Act 2007 is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-7c85ab8753e57c7809c00cb9e5ac2dac] In section 1(3) (employment and support allowance: basic conditions) after paragraph (a) there is inserted—

> - (aa) has accepted a claimant commitment,

.

(3) After section 1B (as inserted by section 52 above) there is inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980e9c500443ce6e45dbe958ad43333c]

> **1C. Claimant commitment (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-980e9c500443ce6e45dbe958ad43333c]**
>
> (1) For the purposes of this Part a “claimant commitment” is a record of the claimant's responsibilities in relation to an award of an employment and support allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980e9c500443ce6e45dbe958ad43333c]
>
> (2) A claimant commitment is to be prepared by the Secretary of State and may be reviewed and updated as the Secretary of State thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980e9c500443ce6e45dbe958ad43333c]
>
> (3) A claimant commitment is to be in such form as the Secretary of State thinks fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980e9c500443ce6e45dbe958ad43333c]
>
> (4) A claimant commitment is to include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980e9c500443ce6e45dbe958ad43333c]
>
> - (a) any prescribed information, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980e9c500443ce6e45dbe958ad43333c]
> - (b) any other information the Secretary of State considers it appropriate to include. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980e9c500443ce6e45dbe958ad43333c]
>
> (5) For the purposes of this Part a claimant accepts a claimant commitment if, and only if, the claimant accepts the most up-to-date version of it in such manner as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980e9c500443ce6e45dbe958ad43333c]
>
> (6) Regulations may provide that, in prescribed circumstances, a claimant is to be treated as having satisfied the condition mentioned in section 1(3)(aa). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980e9c500443ce6e45dbe958ad43333c]

(4) In section 15(2)(b) (directions about work-related activity) for the words from “by” to “14” there is substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980e9c500443ce6e45dbe958ad43333c] “ in such manner as the Secretary of State thinks fit ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980e9c500443ce6e45dbe958ad43333c].

(5) In section 16(1) (contracting out) before paragraph (a) there is inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980e9c500443ce6e45dbe958ad43333c]

> - (za (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-980e9c500443ce6e45dbe958ad43333c] any function under section 1C in relation to a claimant commitment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-980e9c500443ce6e45dbe958ad43333c]

.

(6)[^key-c6dcd8b6c7849a6fceb7950fed1abf2f] In Schedule 2 (supplementary provisions) after paragraph 4 there is inserted—

> **4A.**
>
> Regulations may prescribe circumstances in which a person may be entitled to employment and support allowance without having accepted a claimant commitment.

(7) In section 31(2) of the Welfare Reform Act 2009 (action plans: well-being of children), in subsection (5) inserted into section 14 of the Welfare Reform Act 2007, after “preparing any” there is inserted “ claimant commitment or ”.
