## 71. Purposes of discretionary payments

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 138 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 (payments out of the social fund), in subsection (1)(b), for “to meet other needs” there is substituted

> to meet—
>
> - (i) other needs, and
> - (ii) in the case of payments by way of budgeting loan, those needs for which provision is made by paragraph (a),

.
