# Carriage of Goods by Road Act 1965, section 5

> Text as at 1997-01-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Contribution between carriers.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a carrier under a contract to which the Convention applies is liable in respect of any loss or damage for which compensation is payable under the Convention, nothing in section 1 of the[^c657623] [^c657624]Civil Liability (Contribution) Act 1978, or section 3(2) of the [^c657625]Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1940 shall confer on him any right to recover contribution in respect of that loss or damage from any other carrier who, in accordance with article 34 in the Schedule to this Act, is a party to the contract of carriage.

(2) The preceding subsection shall be without prejudice to the operation of article 37 in the Schedule to this Act.
