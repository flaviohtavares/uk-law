(5) In accordance with the foregoing provisions of this section—

- (a) section 1 of the [^c665792]Armed Forces (Housing Loans) Act 1949 shall be amended as provided by Part I of the [^c665793]Schedule to this Act [^c665794]. . .
- (b)[^c665795] in the Armed Forces (Housing Loans) Act 1958, in section 2(1) for the words “the defence services” there shall be substituted the words “the service of the Ministry of Defence” . . . [^c665796]

## 2. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Armed Forces (Housing Loans) Act 1965.
