#### 36. Graduated retirement benefit. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c6658521][^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95][^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of the Act, graduated retirement benefit shall be payable to any person who is over pensionable age and who *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95] is entitled to a retirement pension[^M_F_35019fde-c785-44e4-e119-5f54d340440e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95], and shall be an increase in the weekly rate of his retirement pension equal to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95] 18.51[^c-f279c534fb7af8f13cf8a644d61d0366] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95] pence for each unit, ascertained in accordance with subsections (2) and (3) of this section, of the graduated contributions properly paid by him as an insured person, the result being rounded to the nearest whole penny, taking ½p as nearest to the next whole penny above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

(2) For the purpose of graduated retirement benefit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95] , a unit of graduated contributions shall be £7.50.[^c21264101] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

(3) Where a person’s graduated contributions calculated at the said rate do not make an exact number of units any incomplete fraction of a unit shall, if it is one-half or more, be treated as a complete unit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

(4)[^M_C_8ea8d787-ccdf-4191-8827-987aac7d72fe][^c21263671] Where a person's entitlement to graduated retirement benefit is deferred— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

- (a) Schedule 2 to the Social Security (Graduated Retirement Benefit) (No.2) Regulations 1978; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]
- (b) Schedule 1 to the 2005 Regulations, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

shall have effect and both those Schedules shall be construed and have effect as if they were part of this subsection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

(4A) For the purposes of subsection (4), a person's entitlement to graduated retirement benefit is deferred— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

- (a) where he would be entitled to a Category A or Category B retirement pension but for the fact that his entitlement is deferred within the meaning in section 55(3) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, if and so long as his entitlement to such a pension is deferred; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]
- (b) where he is treated under subsection (7) as receiving a Category A or a Category B retirement pension at a nominal weekly rate, if and so long as he does not become entitled to graduated retirement benefit by reason only of not satisfying the conditions in section 1 of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 (entitlement to benefit dependent on claim), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

and in relation to graduated retirement benefit, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]**period of deferment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]**” shall be construed accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

(5) For the purposes of subsection (4) of this section, the Secretary of State may by regulations provide for treating all or any of the graduated contributions paid by a person in the tax year in which he attained pensionable age as having been paid before, or as having been paid after, the day on which he attained that age, whether or not the contribution in question was so paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

(6)[^c6658591] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) A person who *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95] attained pensionable age before 6 April 2016[^c-d691a04eab86727a8b95d7b1a7587004] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95] and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95] has claimed[^c6658601] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95], but is not entitled to a retirement pension *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95] (except a person who is not so entitled because of an election under section 54(1) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 or because he has withdrawn his claim)[^c6658611] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95], shall be treated for the purposes of the foregoing provisions of this section as receiving a retirement pension at a nominal weekly rate: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

Provided that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

- (a) this subsection shall not confer any right to graduated retirement benefit on a person who would be entitled to a retirement pension but for some provision of the Act or of regulations disqualifying him for receipt of it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]
- (b) regulations may provide that any right by virtue of this subsection to benefit at less than a specified weekly rate shall be satisfied either altogether or for a specified period by the making of a single payment of the prescribed amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

(8) In this section and in section 37 below— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

- “**graduated contributions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]**” means graduated contributions under the National Insurance Act 1965 or the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95] [^c6658621]National Insurance Act 1959; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]
- “**insured person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]**” means insured person under the National Insurance Act 1965 or the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95] [^c6658631]National Insurance Act 1946; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]
- “**retirement pension *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]**” means retirement pension of any category *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95] under the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^c-d69e9198370c29cd68c42ced00a9bc1d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95];
- “**the Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]**” means the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95] [^c6658641]Social Security Act 1975; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]
- “the 2005 Regulations” means the Social Security (Graduated Retirement Benefit) Regulations 2005,[^M_F_6c9b169a-ea86-4802-a7b9-e6d8ac5dd0e7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

and any reference in section 37 below to “section 36 of this Act” or to any of its subsections is a reference to that section or subsection as it is here set out. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

(9) This section and section 37 below and the Act shall be construed and have effect as if this section and section 37 below were included in Chapter I of Part II or that Act (contributory benefits); and references to that Chapter, that Part of that Act in any other enactment or in any instrument shall be construed accordingly: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]

Provided that nothing in this subsection shall affect the construction of any reference to section 36 or 37 of this Act or of that Act or to any of the subsections of those sections; and any increase in the weekly rate of a person’s retirement pension, to the extent that it is attributable to subsection (4) of this section, shall be left out of account in determining the weekly rate of that pension for the purposes of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95] section 30B(3) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, regulations 11(1) and 18(7) of the Social Security (Incapacity Benefit) (Transitional) Regulations 1995 and[^M_F_90375b32-8487-4339-e971-43aee2177210] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95] regulation 10(6) of the Jobseeker’s Allowance (Transitional Provisions) Regulations 1996[^M_F_5029fa23-2f9a-4acf-9cb7-9aaa38ac23e1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95] (rates of incapacity benefit and jobseeker’s allowance in transitional cases for persons over pensionable age). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_4478ee51-3d8b-4b64-b804-17cda44a6d95]
