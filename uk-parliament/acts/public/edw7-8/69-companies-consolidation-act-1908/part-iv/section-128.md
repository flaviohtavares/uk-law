# Companies (Consolidation) Act 1908, section 128

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 128. Provision as to married women.

(1) The husband of a female contributory married before the date of the commencement of the [^m00007]Married Women's Property Act, 1882, or the [^m00008]Married Women's Property (Scotland) Act, 1881, as the case may be, shall, during the continuance of the marriage, be liable, as respects any liability attaching to any shares acquired by her before that date, to contribute to the assets of the company the same sum as she would have been liable to contribute if she had not married, and he shall be a contributory accordingly.

(2) Subject as aforesaid, nothing in this Act shall affect the provisions of the Married Women's Property Act, 1882, or the Married Women's Property (Scotland) Act, 1881.
