# Companies (Consolidation) Act 1908, section 234

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 234. Annual accounts of English, winding up.

(1) The Treasury shall annually cause to be prepared and laid before both Houses of Parliament an account for the year ending with the thirty-first day of March, showing the receipts and expenditure during that year in respect of proceedings under this Act in relation to the winding up of companies in England, and the provisions of section twenty-eight of the [^m00019]Supreme Court of Judicature Act, 1875, shall apply to the account as if the account had been required by that section.

(2) The accounts of the Board of Trade under this Act in relation to the winding up of companies in England shall be audited in such, manner as the Treasury direct, and, for the purpose of the account to be laid before Parliament, the Board shall make such returns and give such information as the Treasury direct.
