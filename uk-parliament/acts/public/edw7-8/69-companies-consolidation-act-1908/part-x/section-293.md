# Companies (Consolidation) Act 1908, section 293

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 293. Saving for Life Assurance Companies Acts.

Nothing in this Act shall affect the provisions of the [^m00033][^m00034][^m00035]Life Assurance Companies Acts, 1870 to 1872, except that references in those Acts to any provision of the Companies Act, 1862, shall be read as references to the corresponding provision of this Act.
