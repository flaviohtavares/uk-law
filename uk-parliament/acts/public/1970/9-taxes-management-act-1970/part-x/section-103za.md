# Taxes Management Act 1970, section 103ZA

## 103ZA. Disapplication of sections 100 to 103 in the case of certain penalties[^c-7ed28ea8ec21a8339dd750fe1375945f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Sections 100 to 103 do not apply to a penalty under—

- (a) Schedule 24 to FA 2007 (penalties for errors),
- (b) Schedule 36 to FA 2008 (information and inspection powers),
- (c) Schedule 41 to that Act (penalties for failure to notify and certain other wrongdoing),
- (d) Schedule 55 to FA 2009 (penalties for failure to make returns etc), [^c-0755c6b9248592f1273aec4c1ed36545]...
- (e) Schedule 56 to that Act (penalties for failure to make payments on time),[^c-58e7f09767f7d631222f2f7363cc0ae1] [^c-686e60788ee23ac7eb6981fe6c8245e7]...
- (f) Schedule 23 to FA 2011 (data-gathering powers),[^c-52dca6b276af8fedf36980a9cdfc27c2] [^c-a2c6a7c81149fd6102923f294242937e]...
- (g) Schedule 38 to FA 2012 (tax advisers: sanctionable conduct[^c-c293089022e84170c6b702440e846549]),[^c-6fab6e6f7a686dcafc929fb58fa0bfd6] [^c-16595f56e548a01e81345d0909def04d]...
- (ga)[^c-85273384246225c1f62d161b16b772d4] section 212A of the Finance Act 2013 (general anti-abuse rule), [^c-f2b0fb9c03d72e23624bb9b40a78afbf]...
- (h) Part 4 of the Finance Act 2014 (follower notices and accelerated payments) [^c-eb7d219ccdec1586e2c6a0fbede08c4e]...
- (i)[^c-cff259627a1d57c0d05335f912f96ab6] Part 5 of Schedule 18 to the Finance Act 2016 (serial tax avoidance),[^c-f68a12ba3082720cd5a685b9ea4a2668] [^c-550030590753aae68c433f2efdcbed3d]...
- (j) Schedule 22 to the Finance Act 2016 (asset-based penalty) [^c-ce6d3b3226d9515405298e29958b6f05]...
- (k)[^c-a28b65850d4ebd61ece04713a4652a71] paragraph 1 or 45 of Schedule 16 to the Finance (No. 2) Act 2017 (enablers of defeated tax avoidance etc) ,[^c-58761a92c668dc9e8fe9c74c638c3a9f] [^c-7bf3f030008244bf1b03fca831a4ea62]...
- (l) Schedule 18 to the Finance Act 2017.
- (m)[^c-798614895056e4a65a5f7cc2570172af] Schedule 24 to the Finance Act 2021 (penalties for failure to make returns etc),
- (n) Schedule 25 to that Act (penalties for deliberately withholding information), or
- (o) Schedule 26 to that Act (penalties for failure to pay tax).
