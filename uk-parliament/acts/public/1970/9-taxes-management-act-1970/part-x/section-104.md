## 104. Saving for criminal proceedings.[^c846276][^c846278][^M_C_7d342d51-90e9-4406-a57c-8615896de558][^M_C_e2237751-8e7e-4fb3-dc50-8431157f4d98][^key-5095515806397f8835c7049abaf59d44][^key-3e58d10626b2006e4baf47f27e8e361f][^key-785503ec1872b9dfad4afbf7a2a3dd5c][^key-9ae3284aa427cc21ac3eb532b496d050][^key-b6faa3164a22b879d94a998e9661035c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provisions of the Taxes Acts shall not, save so far as is otherwise provided, affect any criminal proceedings for any misdemeanour.
