## 99A. Certificates of non-liability to income tax.[^c2172543]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a person who gives a certificate of non-liability to income tax in pursuance of regulations under section 852 of ITA 2007[^key-0f3c8a5f09b05fd3c67e6c1c83139df3]—

- (a) gives the certificate fraudulently or negligently, or
- (b) fails to comply with any undertaking contained in the certificate in pursuance of the regulations,

he shall be liable to a penalty not exceeding £3,000.
