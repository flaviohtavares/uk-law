## 103. Time limits for penalties.[^M_C_3235826d-381c-4f80-bc8c-a5bdb44ea42a][^M_C_7d342d51-90e9-4406-a57c-8615896de558][^M_C_e2237751-8e7e-4fb3-dc50-8431157f4d98][^c-e508b615e2787afb2f77a2205bec22b3][^c-71964cdbc22e5061d84ab4c78b6b30f2][^c-4c42985ca78361edc26b8f96cb794e5b][^c-cf1f8fd296e3afbb0a5ec74f7373ebe1][^c-05276ffc135847c515d41ab15d2877d7][^c-9354c32199625adf384ef5834232ff6e][^c-0fdd282e491aa79765ac5a047776af77][^c846274]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to subsection (2) below, where the amount of a penalty is to be ascertained by reference to tax payable by a person for any period, the penalty may be determined by an officer of the Board, or proceedings for the penalty may be commenced before the tribunal[^c-fc838c6a4f5109aaaa56fc5e3eaaebc3] or a Court—

- (a) at any time within six years after the date on which the penalty was incurred, or
- (b) at any later time within three years after the final determination of the amount of tax by reference to which the amount of the penalty is to be ascertained.

(2)[^c-d97d4a61c39271294ea9a4e878869f3f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-e738675a3e052a47c2d04ce4f694b889][^c-d656224491c98e28542c62d5b1a3f409] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-2d52ca0822f8c2e8d54b2f8e82877b1b][^c-e31bba9b8e8752bae9ae6d3d4edd98cb][^c-28c61237d42fd3624576cdfdd374682b][^c-af02483cb4326acf05bc711ff28b6371][^c-706b5e90c5ed6858e812f2cbb2e6020c][^c-d656224491c98e28542c62d5b1a3f409][^c-7280c85f908cb6a5e10ff6895545bfaa][^c-dd1608d8748fb95c26d1044089523de1] A penalty to which subsection (1) does not apply[^c-b5a2409da97e162ca3eb83f3b5ddcb00] may be so determined, or proceedings for such a penalty may be commenced before the tribunal[^c-bc7401149c58b31453f511c272c02973] or a court, at any time within six years after the date on which the penalty was incurred or began to be incurred.
