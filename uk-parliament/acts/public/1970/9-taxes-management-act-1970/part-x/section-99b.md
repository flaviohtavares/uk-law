# Taxes Management Act 1970, section 99B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (149).** Text as at 2026-06-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1970/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99B. Declarations under Chapter 2 of Part 15 of ITA 2007[^c-4135f23f03ad642b216db582b46a6374]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a person fraudulently or negligently gives any incorrect information in a declaration under any of sections 858 to 861 of ITA 2007, the person shall be liable to a penalty not exceeding £3,000.
