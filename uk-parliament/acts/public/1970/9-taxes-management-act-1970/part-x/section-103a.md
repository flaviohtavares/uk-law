## 103A. Interest on penalties.[^M_C_7d342d51-90e9-4406-a57c-8615896de558][^M_C_e2237751-8e7e-4fb3-dc50-8431157f4d98][^c-8f219e66b5adc7aec01c5015415e140a][^c-74477f2b7ba84eaaeb463ca5d157dcd5][^M_C_2b61e8f6-8afe-41be-ebbe-0aefb6ac6cea][^c-014f54a0143b4284d9d26ed243e75994][^c-6cf5d5ac1b90bf944e11fddef00aac5b][^c-da88e07f20e586eb717430d81af499eb][^c-f36e0bee40a884b4a639bd998738e4ea][^c-ffe950901345e6ce84d68ea6fd6ace59]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A penalty under any of the provisions of Part II, IV or VA[^c-52069e8ef20862a2da160d6e184db283] or this Part of this Act [^c-2f20e1f452ed19ee94a96a29dc303e71]... , or Schedule 18 to the Finance Act 1998,[^c-8a65f446f626bc4a805d60a4f17ccca6] shall carry interest at the rate applicable under section 178 of the Finance Act 1989 from the date on which it becomes due and payable until payment.
