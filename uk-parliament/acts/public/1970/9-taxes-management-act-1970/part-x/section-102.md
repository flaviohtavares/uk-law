Mitigation of penalties.[^M_C_7d342d51-90e9-4406-a57c-8615896de558][^M_C_e2237751-8e7e-4fb3-dc50-8431157f4d98][^key-2fefdebb63621c91de4ceb8017feed51][^key-ef33aba14f670c41a48732a8f79e3983][^key-dfa9085f0f012ed64c1216a072b164e6][^key-7560d5bae99e29c7698becf7de85c06e][^key-ac3e9e4919bd51da2571b411cbd42adb][^key-fff60b20de4ddfcdfb4b3170e94e8bce][^key-ebbdcf6dc75f933e4e6dc6d1bf3d805c][^key-f73e16ae29e778ea30e366f741dbbf89][^key-1e53788b451c4890f8a294efe9455d00][^key-4cebcbe7b312e4ae7e1233acc3dcc9f7][^key-71964cdbc22e5061d84ab4c78b6b30f2][^key-4c42985ca78361edc26b8f96cb794e5b][^key-cf1f8fd296e3afbb0a5ec74f7373ebe1][^key-05276ffc135847c515d41ab15d2877d7][^key-9354c32199625adf384ef5834232ff6e][^key-86d4b9b031f73495a9f7967bfaae277c][^key-740c87e9274e29f5c4ca0fc71c280b62][^key-0fdd282e491aa79765ac5a047776af77]

The Board may in their discretion mitigate any penalty, or stay or compound any proceedings for a penalty[^c846270], and may also, after judgment, further mitigate or entirely remit the penalty.
