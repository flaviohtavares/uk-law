## 8B. Withdrawal by HMRC of notice under section 8 or 8A[^c-09856b23f401423d97e7b25320c9bd4f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^c-ba7ef8e5103359a508757112823db135][^c-245985e79becf8d645b2f0608b6bd803]

(1) This section applies to a person who is given a notice under section 8 or 8A.

(2) Before the end of the withdrawal period, HMRC[^c-ee7cf4f29a999e556256dae609eb14f8] may withdraw the notice (whether at the request of the person or otherwise).

(3) But the notice may not be withdrawn[^c-4da81f0a9ad7428388bfa947d61ab45e] if—

- (a) the person has made a return under section 8 or 8A in pursuance of the notice under that section, or
- (b) the person has been served with notice of a determination under section 28C by virtue of the notice under section 8 or 8A having been given to the person.

(4) If [^c-5e13b5b54cff0bcec9ac5a4d237ab32f]... HMRC decide to withdraw the notice under section 8 or 8A they must do so by giving the person a notice under this section.

(5) A notice under this section must specify the date on which the notice under section 8 or 8A is withdrawn.

(6) For the purposes of subsection (2) “**the withdrawal period**” means—

- (a) the period of 2 years beginning with the end of the year of assessment to which the notice under section 8 or 8A relates, or
- (b) in exceptional circumstances, such extended period as HMRC may determine[^c-7dea2a58e4eb9e7ec158bade3e874ccb].

(7) Withdrawal of a notice given to a person under section 8 or 8A in relation to a year of assessment does not prevent HMRC from giving the person a further notice under that section in relation to that year.

(8)[^c-f26cd3e0373a4d799cc7e565a4366481] See paragraph 17A of Schedule 55 to FA 2009 as to the cancellation of liability to a penalty under any paragraph of that Schedule by including provision in a notice under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f26cd3e0373a4d799cc7e565a4366481]

(8) See paragraph 21 of Schedule 24 and paragraph 16 of Schedule 25 to the Finance Act 2021 as to the cancellation of liabilities under those Schedules by including provision in a notice under this section.
