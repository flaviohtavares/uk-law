## 9ZA. Amendment of personal or trustee return by taxpayer[^key-2e793825ad58a1db5684c86b6ec09ee7][^key-71dcb458a85b4537a7b81c373c7f746a][^key-1935179ebb18467cd75e2c0aef84292f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^key-ba7ef8e5103359a508757112823db135][^key-245985e79becf8d645b2f0608b6bd803]

(1) A person may amend his return under section 8 or 8A of this Act by notice to an officer of the Board.

(2) An amendment may not be made more than twelve months after the filing date.

(3)[^key-6d6d5d91bc6fb165a813827e37f069cd] In this section “the filing date”, in respect of a return for a year of assessment (Year 1), means—

- (a) 31st January of Year 2, or
- (b) if the notice under section 8 or 8A is given after 31st October of Year 2, the last day of the period of three months beginning with the date of the notice.
