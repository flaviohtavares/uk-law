## 20D. Interpretation of ss.20 to 20C.[^c-e938280e3af47d031888671d4579c697][^c844923]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of section 20BA[^c-254e135d3296cec7c75411d7b3c09900] above, “**the appropriate judicial authority**” is—

- (a) in England and Wales, a Circuit judge or a District Judge (Magistrates' Courts)[^c-3ff44079101bbabac8c8a7ecb714e060];
- (b) in Scotland, a sheriff; and
- (c) in Northern Ireland, a county court judge.
