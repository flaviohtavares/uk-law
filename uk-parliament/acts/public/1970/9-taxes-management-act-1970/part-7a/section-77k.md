## 77K. Other definitions in Part 7A[^c-61aba4278aa9fa949294f1e689a38a66][^c-2cd5b8fa2cc596a6eccc150237a29d5e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(2) “**Licence**” has the meaning given by section 77B(7).
