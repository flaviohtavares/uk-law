# Taxes Management Act 1970, section 37A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (149).** Text as at 2026-06-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1970/9

## 37A. Effect of assessment where allowances transferred.[^c845252][^key-d0fddfb7eadd90c6e9d9364a7c371fb3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where an assessment is made on any person in a case falling within section 36(1) or (1A)[^key-2941789ccd03f77cb95fd6c5468a48ad] or 36A[^key-9b12253977d233ce648d680ef57d9d47], the fact that the person’s liability to income tax or[^key-f2a0a85d71aa0501f1059b968927251c] total income for any year of assessment is assessed as greater than it was previously taken to be shall not affect the validity of any[^key-fed63063c5af09cc8872df586a609cea] deduction from net income or tax reduction[^key-598710f83efd07123b1dce1c4cbd3e6b] made in the case of that person’s spouse or civil partner[^key-9c6fb3c41d13931eb14c4e2747439131] [^key-5e25257320aaeca645902b76c950088c]... by virtue of [^key-e806e46557f88f71977d0495d3bbbdf1]... section 39, 51 or 52 of ITA 2007[^key-787f128b395a7f98f287812d98581048] [^key-5e25257320aaeca645902b76c950088c]... ; and the entitlement in that case of the first-mentioned person for the year in question to any[^key-0f01206de682d624df6685940275cf7a] deduction from net income or tax reduction shall be treated as correspondingly reduced.
