## 40. Assessment on personal representatives.[^c845269][^c-70c6d2ab9d6c81aef73f897d20823f17][^c-d349934a9a3944a2445295e9f716df93][^c-1033b8ede48250d4e9f807d99d02d33f][^c-9c445c63da1c81a3ceb63f4962cd221f][^c-d0fddfb7eadd90c6e9d9364a7c371fb3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c845270][^c845271][^c-e69c980fcbf2606e176ebaa8d2e7d439][^c-1c491115a9a7309eb35b7f388d7ba510][^c-a6fe368c55785598209b10fb07c9e159] For the purpose of the charge of tax on the executors or administrators of a deceased person in respect of the income, or chargeable gains, which arose or accrued to him before his death, the time allowed by section 34, 35, 36 or 36A[^c-cf49fe4102d55a4cf0b4380a4590a7ce] above shall in no case extend more than 4 years after the end of[^c-054ad1b0130389d2b7048d9dbfbbb864] the year of assessment[^c-b3828c95a132c75c3a2367de2f2524dc] in which the deceased died.

(2)[^c845270][^c845271][^c-e69c980fcbf2606e176ebaa8d2e7d439][^c-1c491115a9a7309eb35b7f388d7ba510][^c-a6fe368c55785598209b10fb07c9e159] In a case involving a loss of tax brought about carelessly or deliberately by a person who has died (or another person acting on that person's behalf before that person's death)[^c-23283f5b000324e80906531843475ddd], an assessment on his personal representatives to tax for any year of assessment ending not earlier than six years before his death may be made at any time not more than 4 years after the end of[^c-d81baae2a33b5b882ab18a533b0386c8] the year of assessment in which he died.

(3) In this section[^c845279] “**tax**” means income tax or capital gains tax.

(4)[^c845281] Any act or omission such as is mentioned in section 98B below, on the part of a grouping (as defined in that section) or member of a grouping shall be deemed for the purposes of subsection (2) above to be the act or omission of each member of the grouping.
