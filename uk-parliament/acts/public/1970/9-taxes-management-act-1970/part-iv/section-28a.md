## 28A. Completion of enquiry into personal or trustee return ...[^key-d4510da372edc689ce6e7daa7db58635][^key-bb0ab2280388a228579c185e202c9b35][^key-d0fddfb7eadd90c6e9d9364a7c371fb3][^key-691423f3ed16b82e34e36ed3c81905e6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-33d7e35559e3a06cffbf90b819519fce] This section applies in relation to an enquiry under section 9A(1) [^key-a69d9ea2c722f3c3e26685cd56b6841a]... of this Act.

(1A) Any matter to which the enquiry relates is completed when an officer of Revenue and Customs informs the taxpayer by notice (a “partial closure notice”) that the officer has completed his enquiries into that matter.

(1B) The enquiry is completed when an officer of Revenue and Customs informs the taxpayer by notice (a “final closure notice”) —

- (a) in a case where no partial closure notice has been given, that the officer has completed his enquiries, or
- (b) in a case where one or more partial closure notices have been given, that the officer has completed his remaining enquiries.

(2) A partial or final closure notice[^key-6ff15e61919a10bc1d82d786652144d7] must state the officer's conclusions and[^key-3e2552a9f33440bfca8fa50a5f583587]—

- (a) state that in the officer’s opinion no amendment of the return is required, or
- (b) make the amendments of the return required to give effect to his conclusions.

(3) A partial or final closure notice[^key-254d85342904e54241d5a7f452850d35] takes effect when it is issued.

(4) The taxpayer may apply to the tribunal[^key-c6575a10afdcb6d8f04d05aed4304acc] for a direction requiring an officer of the Board to issue a partial or final closure notice[^key-7730284d65710e065e65318dd3c7f309] within a specified period.

(5)[^key-5f170900d5da41887b8df7e79abdaedf] Any such application is to be subject to the relevant provisions of Part 5 of this Act (see, in particular, section 48(2)(b)).

(6) The tribunal[^key-d46f2ba368fd61af8c1848123c0787dd] shall give the direction applied for unless [^key-fd4aa2e0fce34d5f6ba35d0d1f519a4c]... satisfied that there are reasonable grounds for not issuing the partial or final closure notice[^key-16e3126a883db53c8cb4c05437ad9275] within a specified period.

(7)[^key-d67b4e624d2928a077b14e90663630f8] In this section “**the taxpayer**” means the person to whom notice of enquiry was given.

(8) In the Taxes Acts, references to a closure notice under this section are to a partial or final closure notice under this section.
