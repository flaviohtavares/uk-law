## 34. Ordinary time limit of 4 years.[^c845213][^M_C_c7b4cb45-5d9f-47d1-ba05-7753ab9b9ae3][^c845210][^c-27d26ca91e3fe8a6f1a4f1fe6e2a93b6][^c-6cb698b3aee4f6b9dae70edc10f1aecb][^c-399337c4dd1a2e38bf64e4520922364f][^c-65668519a05656ccade2e85a5e23beac][^c-6162cc039ec879dd57975d8db31d67ff][^c-d0fddfb7eadd90c6e9d9364a7c371fb3][^c-1e7e00dc2f616a0f0176f2a0fc1c2141][^c-d0fddfb7eadd90c6e9d9364a7c371fb3][^c-9ff173d150b9c92cc2413804de969f5e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c845211][^c845212][^c-ab3f4e0b17c21649f1b257db0115eaf7][^c-1d98420e3f14a5e984dc5436ef8f5f59][^c-b0401ce19d1ebea51d3fd538032ccd61][^c-c2a1d3d312bdd9615e4f467d4853d066] Subject to the following provisions of this Act, and to any other provisions of the Taxes Acts allowing a longer period in any particular class of case, an assessment to income tax or capital gains tax may be made at any time[^c-5ba012fb274327414233b627a018592a] not more than 4 years after the end of[^c-69db7faf6b0adf35b731f6b058efd00e] the year of assessment to which it relates.

(1A)[^c-0fad792d2ec8fae62239cf45624ae76d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) An objection to the making of any assessment on the ground that the time limit for making it has expired shall only be made on an appeal against the assessment.

(3)[^c-484a682413fb0da8b5a0ad9cb5686508] In this section “**assessment**” does not include a self-assessment.
