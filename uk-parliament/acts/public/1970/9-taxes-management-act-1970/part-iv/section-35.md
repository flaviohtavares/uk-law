# Taxes Management Act 1970, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (149).** Text as at 2026-06-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1970/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Time limit: income received after year for which it is assessable[^key-1e165d80d080f6792f82c5e142a60102][^key-d0fddfb7eadd90c6e9d9364a7c371fb3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where income to which this section applies is received in a year of assessment subsequent to that for which it is assessable, an assessment to income tax as respects that income may be made at any time not more than 4 years after the end of[^key-d5cb97db93f05e667740258e9d073540] the year of assessment in which it was received.

(2) This section applies to—

- (a) employment income,
- (b) pension income, and
- (c) social security income.
