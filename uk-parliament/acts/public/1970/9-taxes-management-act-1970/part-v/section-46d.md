# Taxes Management Act 1970, section 46D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (149).** Text as at 2026-06-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1970/9

## 46D. Questions to be determined by the relevant tribunal.[^c-b7c179d64b77f7dc716703fc232c6d0d][^c-3632ada1ed2357ebee72c95c27a06a1b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In so far as the question in dispute on an appeal to which this section applies—

- (a) is a question of the value of any land or of a lease of land, and
- (b) arises in relation to the taxation of chargeable gains (whether under capital gains tax or corporation tax) or in relation to a claim under the 1992 Act,

the question shall be determined by the relevant tribunal[^c-e80fe457ef49e5f345b63eb2df105d68].

(2) This section applies to—

- (a)[^c-4710598a4092458ec5450034bd79feec] an appeal against an amendment of a self-assessment under section 9C of this Act or paragraph 30 of Schedule 18 to the Finance Act 1998;
- (aa) an appeal against an amendment of a return under paragraph 34[^c-ebcd8c8507f83ca0e44591b36f181846] of Schedule 18 to the Finance Act 1998;
- (b) an appeal against a conclusion stated or amendment made by a closure notice under section 28A or 28B of this Act;
- (c) an appeal against an amendment of a partnership return under section 30B(1) of this Act;
- (d) an appeal against an assessment to tax which is not a self-assessment;
- (e) an appeal against a conclusion stated or amendment made by a closure notice under paragraph 7(2) of Schedule 1A to this Act relating to[^c-5fd499afddc2baf661986f700a5de346] a claim or election made otherwise than by being included in a return;
- (f) an appeal against a decision contained in a closure notice under paragraph 7(3)[^c-0b3a6707ab973072b3e4a916cc141ee9] of Schedule 1A to this Act disallowing in whole or in part a claim or election made otherwise than by being included in a return.

(3) In this section “**the relevant tribunal[^c-cc21a70144d723396b46d5fa460c30c1]**” means—

- (a) in relation to land in England and Wales, the Upper Tribunal[^c-7424ffb5c0b95208ba55edff7be541db];
- (b) in relation to land in Scotland, the Lands Tribunal for Scotland;
- (c) in relation to land in Northern Ireland, the Lands Tribunal for Northern Ireland.
