# Taxes Management Act 1970, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (149).** Text as at 2026-06-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1970/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Responsibility for certain taxes[^M_C_1b6fc11b-df48-40b5-e25c-1151781c83c0][^key-59662ace7bb39276d9c3fcc223995fa3][^key-37053add6960d99b9f4b374f23da42cd][^key-8c03b32c372228901db88d8a1bdcc28c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs shall be responsible for the collection and management of—

- (a) income tax,
- (b) corporation tax, and
- (c) capital gains tax.
