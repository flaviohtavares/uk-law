## 118. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1)[^c846476][^key-3b4d67a9f9bfee11a9cef92d5cceda82][^key-bf22085ff602e75583b818b310cf8d85] In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “**Act**” includes an Act of the Parliament of Northern Ireland and “**enactment**” shall be construed accordingly,
- “**the Board**” means the Commissioners of Inland Revenue,
- “**body of persons**” means any body politic, corporate or collegiate, and any company, fraternity, fellowship and society of persons, whether corporate or not corporate,
- [^key-05e2bd37bfbea78949af47873bba578e]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**chargeable gain**” has the same meaning as in [^M_F_dac9c097-1430-4749-e637-d8f2ce1145df]the 1992 Act[^c846458],
- “**chargeable period**” means a year of assessment or a company’s accounting period,
- “**collector**” means any collector of taxes,
- “**company**” has the meaning given by section 1121(1) of CTA 2010 (with section 617 of that Act)[^key-5210eea33c544c4a7a93e89d4a4e02b8] [^c846464]... ,
- “[^key-0c7ea0494e75657ef087d4796f03fe4c]**CTA 2009**” means the Corporation Tax Act 2009,
- “[^key-7fc9f2b6797a6063e035b5a644ec37ac]**CTA 2010**” means the Corporation Tax Act 2010,
- [^key-da144aa1d5227c5efdbe91b35365d870] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “HMRC” means Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs;[^key-5d5a7f06c047d7f462ab56e9520834eb]
- [^key-d8bcc6aef931fb9136b61c1a1f5021eb]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^key-d8bcc6aef931fb9136b61c1a1f5021eb]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**inspector**” means any inspector of taxes,
- “ITEPA 2003” means the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003,[^key-7288691a2c7b4165a6924f0f1c482a2b]
- “ITTOIA 2005” means the Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005,[^key-82d2c9667b359d110f23877c9e80e02b]
- “[^key-203280f3b95b4083a5f575b793256d5e]**ITA 2007**” means the Income Tax Act 2007,
- [^M_F_11aeba3d-70ed-48d4-eff7-ac33f001de8e]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^key-43362bf326dcd39039bca858da93742c]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “[^key-bc33c2b513a0e95238eaafeff078a05d]**partner**” is to be construed in accordance with section 12AA(10B) of this Act;
- “[^key-1bf1f85aa7b13fb606a9add01c278e6f]**partnership return**” has the meaning given by section 12AA(10A) of this Act,
- “**the principal Act**” means the Income and Corporation Taxes Act [^c846462]1988,
- “[^key-9a4893c8ab64767ede0d307d84817e2f]**the relevant trustees**”, in relation to a settlement, shall be construed in accordance with section 7(9) of this Act.
- “**return**” includes any statement or declaration under the Taxes Acts,
- [^key-e4eeb67291cb10abadbea4f23bf79c4a]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “[^key-b5e33f6efe442d27b79ff76e2073b396]**successor**”, in relation to a person who is required to make and deliver, or has made and delivered, a partnership return[^key-3d97e63f841f8c170ffcdd3b2d011643], and “**predecessor**” and “**successor**”, in relation to the successor of such a person, shall be construed in accordance with section 12AA(11) of this Act;
- “**tax**”, where neither income tax nor capital gains tax nor corporation tax [^c846470]nor development land tax is specified, means any of those taxes [^key-d37ed414e2e922925a1a76b8209da300]... ,
- [^c846472]“**the Taxes Acts**” means this Act and—
  - (a) the Tax Acts [^M_F_a1f3fe2a-c02a-4342-80e3-727b3040648b]... and
  - (b) the Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992[^c846473] and all other enactments relating to capital gains tax,
  - (c) [^c846474]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “the 1992 Act ” means the Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992[^c846475]
- “[^key-4c57a563f04189088bfdd7f9eedf9d09]**TIOPA 2010**” means the Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010,
- “the TCEA 2007” means the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007;
- [^c846476]“**trade**” includes every trade, manufacture, adventure or concern in the nature of trade .
- “the tribunal” is to be read in accordance with section 47C;

(2)[^c846476][^key-07fd40da0642f0ebb7d650af47274702][^key-1742f727590d2fcfd9f2a9569b485873][^key-0f14674facc7955bb3b8a491dcf9af6f][^key-7bc08ab51e0329bed87e9ded65ddef05][^key-5984b9d30ee7e3c88cd53c72ee0d7887][^key-6df847c38e788a799f3bcf7e0a7a5706][^key-dec4bbd46f0447ad0503a15cbc1fb73c][^key-b66ab632e6ce9f50ab47a2104c4ad8c1][^key-d34944e87e2106803b0b8f3dad05f6c3][^key-f672b4fe7a5585ab0d441b5052224053][^key-bed24270e3690e67de7132841e74cdd5][^key-5d7bc07b090d1c249ae532a37180f23a] For the purposes of this Act, a person shall be deemed not to have failed to do anything required to be done within a limited time if he did it within such further time, if any, as the Board or the tribunal[^key-faa677519051cd1535a7e756999f49b6] or officer concerned may have allowed; and where a person had a reasonable excuse for not doing anything required to be done he shall be deemed [^c846478]not to have failed to do it unless the excuse ceased and, after the excuse ceased, he shall be deemed not to have failed to do it if he did it without unreasonable delay after the excuse had ceased:

[^c846479]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-4b38b4d40474870ef1bbd1a51c5463c7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-07fd40da0642f0ebb7d650af47274702] For the purposes of this Act, the amount of tax covered by any assessment shall not be deemed to be finally determined until that assessment can no longer be varied, whether by the tribunal on an appeal notified to it[^key-006517413fcb0b7d713ac61ba1813e02] or by the order of any court.

(5)[^key-62448289d101239eef2470b21a8df6da][^key-31d3584d6e9be8fee961680acb3ad864][^key-72196db682ad761a86b28d6a9e42da08][^key-7df11c2c0ab8b7827e68e88e7c13ce51][^key-9cc8d70cbbed0e7bfbaa2f54e8fae4a3][^key-98d9017072bdbbe9ee6707ff1c9e2120][^key-8ecb714859c7d806e8465c7a1e601baf][^key-369992fbc7d6cf7ed547d4eb58595cf6] For the purposes of this Act a loss of tax or a situation is brought about carelessly by a person if the person fails to take reasonable care to avoid bringing about that loss or situation.

(6)[^key-72196db682ad761a86b28d6a9e42da08][^key-62448289d101239eef2470b21a8df6da][^key-31d3584d6e9be8fee961680acb3ad864][^key-7df11c2c0ab8b7827e68e88e7c13ce51][^key-9cc8d70cbbed0e7bfbaa2f54e8fae4a3][^key-98d9017072bdbbe9ee6707ff1c9e2120][^key-8ecb714859c7d806e8465c7a1e601baf] Where—

- (a) information is provided to Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs,
- (b) the person who provided the information, or the person on whose behalf the information was provided, discovers some time later that the information was inaccurate, and
- (c) that person fails to take reasonable steps to inform Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs,

any loss of tax or situation brought about by the inaccuracy shall be treated for the purposes of this Act as having been brought about carelessly by that person.

(7)[^key-72196db682ad761a86b28d6a9e42da08][^key-62448289d101239eef2470b21a8df6da][^key-31d3584d6e9be8fee961680acb3ad864][^key-7df11c2c0ab8b7827e68e88e7c13ce51][^key-9cc8d70cbbed0e7bfbaa2f54e8fae4a3][^key-98d9017072bdbbe9ee6707ff1c9e2120][^key-8ecb714859c7d806e8465c7a1e601baf] In this Act references to a loss of tax or a situation brought about deliberately by a person include a loss of tax or a situation that arises as a result of a deliberate inaccuracy in a document given to Her Majesty's Revenue and Customs by or on behalf of that person.
