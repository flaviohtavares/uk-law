# Conveyancing and Feudal Reform (Scotland) Act 1970, section 17

## 17. Discharge of standard security.[^c21482411]

*Extent: S*

A standard security duly registered or[^key-f69862ccec3bab3956bb8388c050620a] recorded may be discharged, and the land or real right[^c8427451] in land burdened by that security may be disburdened thereof, in whole or in part, by a discharge in conformity with Form F of Schedule 4 to this Act, duly registered or recorded.
