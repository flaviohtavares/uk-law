## 29. Procedure.[^c21482661]

*Extent: S*

(1) The court for the purposes of this Part of this Act, and for the operation of section 11 of the [^c8427971]Heritable Securities (Scotland) Act 1894 (application by *pari passu* creditor to sell), in relation to a standard security, shall be the sheriff having jurisdiction over any part of the security subjects, and the sheriff shall be deemed to have such jurisdiction whatever the value of the subjects.
