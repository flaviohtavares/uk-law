## 51. Application to Crown.

*Extent: S*

This Act shall, subject to any exceptions stated therein, apply to land owned by the Crown or by[^c8428321] the Prince and Steward of Scotland, and to land in which there is any other interest belonging to Her Majesty in right of the Crown or to a Government department, or held on behalf of Her Majesty for the purposes of a Government department, in like manner as it applies to other land.
