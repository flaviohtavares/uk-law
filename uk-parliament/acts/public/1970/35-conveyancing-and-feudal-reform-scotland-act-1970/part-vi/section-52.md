# Conveyancing and Feudal Reform (Scotland) Act 1970, section 52

## 52. Saving, amendment and repeal.

*Extent: S*

(1) Any procedure, notice, advertisement, certificate or warrant instituted, given or granted, or any other thing done under any enactment amended or disapplied by this Act, shall not be invalidated by the coming into force of that amendment or disapplication, but it and any sale or other proceedings dependent thereon shall have effect as if this Act had not come into operation.

(2)[^c8428331] The enactments specified in Schedule 10 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in that Schedule, being minor amendments and amendments consequential on the provisions of this Act.

(3)[^c8428331] The enactments specified in Schedule 11 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in relation thereto in that Schedule.
