## 39. Application of s. 38.[^c11525991]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The persons to whom section 38 above applies are (subject to subsection (3) below)—

- (a) any member of the Regular Reserve[^c-a4a6117152a70855c16dfaf06fa247f5] or the Air Force Reserve who became such a member on or after 1st April 1967 otherwise than in consequence of his having enlisted in the regular army or the regular air force *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bea67b4061052c6ae14f1835ec6a0a23] Royal Air Force before that day;
- (b) any member of the Army Reserve[^c-67963aace79d0fea2cbe4c142624c9b5] who became such a member on or after 1st April 1967 by enlistment or re-engagement or by becoming an officer; and
