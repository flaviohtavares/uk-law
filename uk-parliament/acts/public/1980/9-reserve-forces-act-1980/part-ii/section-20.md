## 20. Permanent service call out of Air Force Reserve.[^c11524931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c11524941] Any man of the Air Force Reserve shall be liable to be called out for permanent service on home defence service whenever an order under section 52(1)(b) of the Reserve Forces Act 1996 authorising his call-out is in force.

(1A)[^c11524951] Sections 50(6) and 52(3) to (6) of the Reserve Forces Act 1996 shall apply to persons subject to this section as if they were persons to whom section 52 of that Act applied.
