## 34. Liability of certain former soldiers to recall.[^c11525761]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any former soldier to whom this section applies may be recalled for service by the Secretary of State by notice under section 35 below[^c11525771] at any time when men of the Regular Reserve[^key-a4a6117152a70855c16dfaf06fa247f5] are called out for permanent service under a call-out order under section 52 of the Reserve Forces Act 1996[^c11525781].

(2) This section applies to any person who is not a woman and who is for the time being under the age of 45, and—

- (a) who is not—
  - (i) a member of the armed forces of the Crown apart from this section;
  - (ii) liable to be recalled to service under section 31 above;
  - (iii) such a person as is mentioned in Schedule 2 to this Act;
- (b) who enlisted in pursuance of regulations made under section 2 of the [^c11525791]Armed Forces Act 1966;
- (c) who has not been discharged in respect of that enlistment under section 14 of the [^c11525801]Army Act 1955, or under any regulations made by virtue of section 2 of the Armed Forces Act 1966 conferring a right to discharge by purchase;
- (d) who has not been granted a commission.

(3) A person recalled for service by such a notice as is referred to in subsection (1) shall be deemed to be enlisted in the regular forces within the meaning of the Army Act 1955 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-05b7000340336172aecfede3fa22b906] regular army for the period—

- (a) beginning with the time specified in the notice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c9a1d348c99191970f19f35ca0d6011e] he is accepted into service , and
- (b)[^c11525811] ending (unless he is previously discharged) with the date on which there is no longer a call-out order under section 52 of the Reserve Forces Act 1996 in force.
