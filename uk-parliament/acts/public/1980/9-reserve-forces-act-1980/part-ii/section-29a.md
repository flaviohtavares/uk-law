# Reserve Forces Act 1980, section 29A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2016-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29A. Application of provisions relating to recall[^c11525371]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provisions of this Act relating to recall shall not apply to any person to whom section 66 of the Reserve Forces Act 1996 applies.
