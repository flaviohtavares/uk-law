# Reserve Forces Act 1980, SCHEDULE 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2016-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/9

## SCHEDULE 2 — Army and Air Force Pensioners and other Former Soldiers not Liable to be Recalled for Service

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Sections 31 and 34.

**1.** A man in holy orders or a regular minister of any religious denomination.

**2.**[^c11529381] A person who is receiving treatment for mental disorder as an in-patient in any establishment in the United Kingdom and is under the supervision of a registered medical practitioner.

**2A.**[^key-ba7453181b11f3fc55cce4312a970841] A person registered as severely sight-impaired in a register kept under section 77(1) of the Care Act 2014 or section 18(1) of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-5119b0344ba2b087e03b8cdbd1eebe9c] .

**3.** A person certified to be registered as a blind person—

- (a) by a local authority, as defined for the purposes of Part III of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-478e93bd2dd27b3157611334b8d52554] [^c11529391]National Assistance Act 1948, under arrangements made by the authority under section 29 of that Act; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-478e93bd2dd27b3157611334b8d52554]
- (b) by a Health and Social Services Board in Northern Ireland under arrangements made under Article 15(1) of the [^c11529401]Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972.
