# Reserve Forces Act 1980, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2016-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Void enlistment in Royal Marines or regular army .[^key-e3f6f69cf6244f3a2279b18446321db7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where any officer or other person enlists a man to serve in the Royal Marines or the regular army[^key-f351e78585f6625f53d4f498710d7ac3] who at the time of such enlisting is entered to serve as a man of the Royal Naval Reserve, Royal Fleet Reserve or Royal Marines Reserve that enlisting shall be null and void.
