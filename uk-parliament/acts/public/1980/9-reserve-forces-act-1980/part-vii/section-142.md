# Reserve Forces Act 1980, section 142

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2016-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 142. Application of Army Act.[^c11528321]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

References in Parts II to V of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5] [^c11528331]Army Act 1955 to the regular forces shall include references to persons of the Ulster Defence Regiment while subject to military law, but any other references in that Act to the regular forces shall not include references to the Ulster Defence Regiment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5]
