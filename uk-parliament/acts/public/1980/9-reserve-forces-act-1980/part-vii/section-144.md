## 144. Assistance in desertion, etc.[^c11528481]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5] Any person who, in Northern Ireland or elsewhere— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5]

- (a) procures or persuades any member of the Ulster Defence Regiment to desert within the meaning of section 37 of the Army Act 1955 or to absent himself without leave, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5]
- (b) knowing that any member of that force is about to desert as mentioned in paragraph (a) above or to absent himself without leave, assists him in so doing, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5]
- (c) knowing any person to be a deserter within the meaning of that section 37 or an absentee without leave from that force, procures or persuades or assists him to remain such a deserter or absentee, or assists in his rescue from custody. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5]

shall be liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5]

- (i) on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months or to both; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c11528491]
- (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5] on conviction on indictment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5] [^c11528501]to a fine or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or to both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5]
