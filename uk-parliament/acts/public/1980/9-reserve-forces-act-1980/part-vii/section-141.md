# Reserve Forces Act 1980, section 141

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2016-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/9

## 141. Membership of armed forces and application of military law.[^c11528291]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Persons of the Ulster Defence Regiment shall be members of the armed forces of the Crown, and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5]

- (a) any holder of a land forces commission who is for the time being assigned for duty with the Ulster Defence Regiment, and any other member of that force when serving on its permanent staff, shall be subject to military law; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5]
- (b) any member of the Ulster Defence Regiment to whom paragraph (a) above does not apply shall be subject to military law— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5]
  - (i) at all times when called out for service under sections 10, 24, 25 and 44 above or when undergoing training whether in pursuance of an obligation under those sections or not; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5] at any other time when he is in possession, or when, in pursuance of any order given or permission granted by a superior officer of his, he is required or authorised to be in possession, of any arms or ammunition or of any prescribed description of equipment, being arms, ammunition or equipment belonging to Her Majesty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44648ee843329913d4c9c3e972049bb5]
