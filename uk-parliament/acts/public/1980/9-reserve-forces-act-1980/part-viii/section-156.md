# Reserve Forces Act 1980, section 156

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2016-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 156. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act, except where the context otherwise requires—

- [^c11529211]. . .
- “**home defence service**” means military or air force service in any place in the United Kingdom, the Channel Islands or the Isle of Man in defence of the United Kingdom or those islands against actual or apprehended attack;
- “**man**”, in relation to—
- (a) the naval forces, means a person of or below the rate of warrant officer;
- (b) the military or air forces, includes a warrant officer and non-commissioned officer;

- [^c11529221]. . .
- “**prescribed**” (except in subsections (3) to (5) and (7) of sections 19A and 21A and in section 140)[^key-9002b708c10ed2cf7703e3adc497fafa] means prescribed by orders or regulations made under the Reserve Forces Act 1996[^c11529231];
- “[^key-42117d723bcab35cce8ce84e380e10ab]**regular air force *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42117d723bcab35cce8ce84e380e10ab]**” has the same meaning as in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42117d723bcab35cce8ce84e380e10ab] [^c11529241]Air Force Act 1955. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-42117d723bcab35cce8ce84e380e10ab]
- “[^key-a7a00a67fc72ac85dca37fcda25e46d3]**regular army *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7a00a67fc72ac85dca37fcda25e46d3]**” means the regular forces within the meaning of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7a00a67fc72ac85dca37fcda25e46d3] [^c11529251]Army Act 1955, but in sections 11(4) and (5), 15, 39(2), 83(1) and (2) above, and paragraph 19 of Schedule 8 to this Act, does not include the Royal Marines; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7a00a67fc72ac85dca37fcda25e46d3]

  “**the regular army**” has the meaning given by section 374 of the Armed Forces Act 2006.
- . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c11529261]

(2) Except where the context otherwise requires, other expressions in this Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdd0fc602f95f4e97c71ddda76972a96]

- (a) relating to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdd0fc602f95f4e97c71ddda76972a96] Regular Reserve[^key-a4a6117152a70855c16dfaf06fa247f5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdd0fc602f95f4e97c71ddda76972a96] and the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdd0fc602f95f4e97c71ddda76972a96] Army Reserve[^key-67963aace79d0fea2cbe4c142624c9b5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdd0fc602f95f4e97c71ddda76972a96] , have the same meanings as in the Army Act 1955; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdd0fc602f95f4e97c71ddda76972a96]
- (b) relating to the Air Force Reserve and the Royal Auxiliary Air Force, have the same meanings as in the Air Force Act 1955. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdd0fc602f95f4e97c71ddda76972a96]

(3) This Act, except so much of it as relates to the Royal Fleet Reserve and the Royal Marines Reserve[^c11529271], applies to women as it applies to men.

(4) In this Act—

- (a) in relation to the definition of “**home defence service**” in subsection (1) above, and
- (b) for the purposes of sections 10(6), 68(3) and 96(3) above,

service on any flight of which the points of departure and intended return are within the boundaries of the United Kingdom, the Channel Islands, and the Isle of Man, or of the territorial waters of the United Kingdom and those islands, shall be deemed to be service within the United Kingdom notwithstanding that the flight may in its course extend beyond those boundaries.

(5) The expression “magistrates’ court”, in the application of this Act—

- (a) to Scotland, shall be construed as a reference to the sheriff sitting as a court of summary jurisdiction;
- (b) to Northern Ireland, shall be construed as a reference to a court of summary jurisdiction.
