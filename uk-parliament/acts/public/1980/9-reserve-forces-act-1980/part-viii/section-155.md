## 155. Amendment of subordinate legislation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 14 of the [^c11528971]Interpretation Act 1978 applies to this Act as if in paragraph (b) of that section there were no requirement that Orders in Council, orders or other subordinate legislation should be made by statutory instrument.
