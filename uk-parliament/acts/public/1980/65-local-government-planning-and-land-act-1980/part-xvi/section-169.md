# Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980, section 169

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/65
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 169. Ecclesiastical property.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where any ecclesiastical property is vested in the incumbent of a benefice which is vacant[^key-ec05c3bce17b03f8991f0b32c4ce8be3], it shall be treated for the purposes of a compulsory acquisition of the property under this Part of this Act as being vested in the Diocesan Board of Finance for the diocese in which the land is situated[^key-09820122607a2ea4d9e6542104842dcc], and any notice to treat shall be served, or be deemed to have been served, accordingly.

(2) Where under this Part of this Act any notice, other than a notice to treat, is required to be served on an owner of land, and the land is ecclesiastical property, a like notice shall be served on the Diocesan Board of Finance for the diocese in which the land is situated.

(3) This section does not extend to Scotland.
