# Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980, section 171

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/65
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 171. Interpretation: general.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-941b455a8beb20346db5677fbc9886c5] In this Part of this Act, except in so far as the context otherwise requires—

- “**ecclesiastical property**” means land belonging to an ecclesiastical benefice of the Church of England[^c-0ee92ff920eaa9ffae78600e266e70c2], or being or forming part of a church subject to the jurisdiction of a bishop, of any diocese of the Church of England or the site of such a church, or being or forming part of a burial ground subject to such jurisdiction;
- “[^c13881861]**the 1981 Act**” means the [^c13881871]Acquisition of Land Act 1981
- “**the 1947 Act**” means the [^c13881881]Acquisition of Land (Authorisation Procedure) (Scotland) Act 1947;
- “**the 1990[^c13881891] Act**” means the Town and Country Planning Act 1990
- “**the 1997[^c13881911] Act**” means the [^c13881921]Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997;
- “[^c-b6a840add141cff8aa6f0c7e48e5477f]**heat networks**” has the meaning given by [section 216](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/52/section/216) of the [Energy Act 2023](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/52/contents);
- “[^c13881941]**urban development area**” means so much of an area designated by an order under subsection (1) of section 134 above as is not excluded from it by an order under subsection (3A) of that section;
- “**urban development corporation**” means a corporation established by an order under section 135 above.

(2)[^c-48288828e48f7090391017e56dbcebf9] In this Part as it applies in relation to England, references to the regeneration of an area are to be read as references to the regeneration or development of the area.
