# Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/65
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101. Repeal.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The [^c13875771]Community Land Act 1975 shall cease to have effect in accordance with Schedule 17 below.

(2) This section and Schedule 17 below shall not extend to Northern Ireland (except so far as they repeal any enactment so extending).
