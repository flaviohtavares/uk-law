## 177. Interpretation of Part XVII.

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Part of this Act—

- “**the Act of 1960**” means the [^c13882011]Caravan Sites and Control of Development Act 1960;
- “**the Act of 1968**” means the [^c13882021]Caravan Sites Act 1968;
- “**caravan**” has the same meaning as in the Act of 1960; and
- “**gipsy**” has the same meaning as in the Act of 1968.
