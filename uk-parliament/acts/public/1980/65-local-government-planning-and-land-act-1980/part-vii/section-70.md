## 70 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note). Grants in respect of caravan sites for gipsies (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c13873481][^c13873481]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c13873481] This section applies to expenditure of a capital nature incurred by any local authority under section 24 of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c13873481] [^c13873491]Caravan Sites and Control of Development Act 1960 (provision of caravan sites by local authorities) in respect of caravan sites provided for the accommodation of *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c13873481] persons to whom subsection (8A) of that section applies[^c13873501] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c13873481]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c13873481]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c13873481] The Secretary of State may, with the approval of the Treasury, make out money provided by Parliament grants in respect of expenditure which, in his opinion, is expenditure to which this section applies. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c13873481]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c13873481] Any grants under this section shall be made on such terms and conditions (if any) as the Secretary of State may, with the approval of the Treasury, determine. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c13873481]
