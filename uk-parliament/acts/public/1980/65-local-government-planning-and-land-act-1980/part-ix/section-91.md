## 91. Amendments relating to compulsory acquisition.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c13874041] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Where a compulsory purchase order has been made, or a binding contract has been entered into to acquire land, before the passing of this Act, sections 112 and 119 of the [^c13874051]Town and Country Planning Act 1971 shall apply as they applied immediately before the passing of this Act, which shall for this purpose be treated as not having been passed.
