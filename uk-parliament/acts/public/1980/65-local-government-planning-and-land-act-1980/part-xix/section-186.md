> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/65
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 186. Amendment of s. 94 of the Public Health Acts (Amendment) Act 1907.[^c13882341]

*Extent: E+W*

The following subsections shall be added at the end of section 94 of the [^c13882351]Public Health Acts (Amendment) Act 1907—

> (8) No licence under this section shall be required in respect of pleasure boats and pleasure vessels on any canal owned or managed by the British Waterways Board.
>
> (9) In subsections (1) and (3) of this section “**let for hire**” means let for hire to the public.

.
