## 38. Citation, construction, repeals and extent.[^c990715]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Education Act 1980.

(2)[^c990716] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c990717] This Act shall, in its application to England and Wales, be construed as one with the Education Act 1996.

(5)[^c990716] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5A)[^c990720] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c990716] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
