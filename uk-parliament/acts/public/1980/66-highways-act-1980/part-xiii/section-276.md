# Highways Act 1980, section 276

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (125).** Text as at 2026-09-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/66
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 276. Contributions to land drainage works which will benefit trunk roads.

*Extent: E+W*

Where it appears to the Minister that the execution or maintenance by the Environment Agency, the Natural Resources Body for Wales or an internal[^c-5374c36d3bbb82799aa89f0688b175ec] drainage board of any drainage works is desirable for the protection or enjoyment of a trunk road, he may make such contributions as he thinks fit towards any expenses incurred by that body[^c-faa53c95b4d0f28bbd52c8f131385dcb] in the execution or maintenance of those works.
