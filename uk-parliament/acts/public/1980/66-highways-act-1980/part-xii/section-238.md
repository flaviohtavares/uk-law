# Highways Act 1980, section 238

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 238. Interpretation etc. of Part XII.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any power under sections 239 to 246 below to acquire land, except the power under section 246(2), is exercisable compulsorily or by agreement.

(2) In this Part of this Act “**common**”, “**fuel or field garden allotment**” and “**open space**” have the same meaning respectively as in the Acquisition of Land Act of 1981[^c13429981].
