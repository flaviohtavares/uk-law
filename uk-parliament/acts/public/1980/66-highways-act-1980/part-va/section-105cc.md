## 105CC. Timing of conclusion and decision[^c-4da1fb64128d6c93404a2cdf5336c23d][^c13422031]

*Extent: E+W*

The project authority must—

- (a) reach the reasoned conclusion required by section 105CA(1)(b), and
- (b) decide whether or not to proceed with the relevant construction or improvement,

within a reasonable period of time (having regard to the nature and complexity of the project) beginning on the day on which the authority begins the consideration required by section 105CA(1)(a).
