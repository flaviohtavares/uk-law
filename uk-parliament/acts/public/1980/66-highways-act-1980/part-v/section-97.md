# Highways Act 1980, section 97

## 97. Lighting of highways.[^c-1efdc6a46c474d566828d6a2371acf0b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A[^c-13ac16808ed7015c0d1ad3b2baba8dba] highway authority may provide lighting for the purposes of any highway or proposed highway for which they are or will be the highway authority, and may for that purpose—

- (a) contract with any persons for the supply of gas, electricity or other means of lighting; and
- (b) construct and maintain such lamps, posts and other works as they consider necessary.

(2) A highway authority may alter or remove any works constructed by them under this section or vested in them under Part III of the [^c13421781]Local Government Act 1966 or section 270 below.

(3) A highway authority shall pay compensation to any person who sustains damage by reason of the execution of works under this section.

(4) Section 45 of the [^c13421791]Public Health Act 1961 (attachment of street lamps to buildings) and section 81 of that Act (summary recovery of damages for negligence) apply to a highway authority who are not a council of a kind therein mentioned as they apply to such a council.
