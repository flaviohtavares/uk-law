# Highways Act 1980, section 95A

## 95A. Power to install equipment for detection of traffic offences etc.[^c13421771][^key-236e97e360b64a77c8ee911887aa2d25]

*Extent: E+W*

A highway authority may install and maintain on or near a highway structures and equipment for the detection of traffic offences or offences under section 11 of the[^key-37235a651600bbc1e8444eae51a88ce8] HGV Road User Levy Act 2013 (using or keeping heavy goods vehicle if HGV road user levy not paid).
