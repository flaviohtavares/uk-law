## 284A. Trunk roads: miscellaneous functions of Secretary of State and strategic highways company .[^c13431321][^key-64a374cc47f1bad39cd6c9578c2de6e4]

*Extent: E+W*

The persons who may exercise the functions conferred by—

- (a) section 40 of the Public Health Acts Amendment Act [^c13431331] 1890 (cabmen’s shelters);
- (b) section 42 of that Act (statutes and monuments);
- (c) section 14 of the [^c13431341] Public Health Act 1925 (public drinking fountains, seats, etc. ),

shall, in relation to a trunk road for which the Secretary of State is highway authority, include the Secretary of State and, in relation to a trunk road for which a strategic highways company is the highway authority, include that company.[^key-e72d5c2d9fe45a6bb799588ae9c902d7]
