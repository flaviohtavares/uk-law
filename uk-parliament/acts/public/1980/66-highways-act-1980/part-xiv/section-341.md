# Highways Act 1980, section 341

## 341. Amendment of section 303 of Public Health Act 1875.

*Extent: E+W*

In section 303 of the [^c13439331]Public Health Act 1875 (power of Secretary of State to repeal and alter local Acts by means of provisional orders), the reference to any local Act which relates to the same subject matters as that Act is to be construed as including a reference to any local Act which relates to the same subject matters as the following provisions of this Act, namely, sections 288, 294, 312, 338, 339 and the provisions specified in Schedule 22 to this Act.
