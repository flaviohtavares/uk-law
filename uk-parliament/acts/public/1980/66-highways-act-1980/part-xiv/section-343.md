## 343. Transitional provisions, amendments and repeals.[^c13439361]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Schedule 23 to this Act, which contains transitional provisions, has effect.

(2)[^c13439371] The enactments specified in Schedule 24 to this Act are, in consequence of this Act, amended as there provided.

(3)[^c13439371] The enactments specified in Schedule 25 to this Act are repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
