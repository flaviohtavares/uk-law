# Highways Act 1980, section 284

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (125).** Text as at 2026-09-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/66

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 284. Powers of Minister or strategic highways company in relation to privately maintainable parts of trunk roads.[^key-195c8f83aa9e4ad587d2f67ee048d3d5]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a trunk road comprises a highway which a person is liable to maintain under a charter or special enactment or by reason of tenure, enclosure or prescription, the Minister or a strategic highways company, whichever is highway authority for the trunk road,[^key-cdc4d5fbe0ce47707ed1ddc276e7e48e] is entitled to exercise in relation to that highway any power which he would be entitled to exercise in relation thereto if that highway were a highway maintainable at the public expense.
