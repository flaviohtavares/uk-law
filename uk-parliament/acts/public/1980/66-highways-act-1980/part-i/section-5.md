# Highways Act 1980, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (125).** Text as at 2026-09-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/66
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Agreement for local highway authority to maintain and improve certain highways constructed or to be constructed by Minister or strategic highways company.[^c-2d57f9dc0861404427fcb93ba0974b46]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The local highway authority may by agreement with the Minister or a strategic highways company[^c-1480020baa107e827844da665bed51cd] undertake the maintenance and improvement of a highway in the local highway authority's[^c-43e6d48034fdfafeda9de8a0ae3638bd] area, being a highway (other than a trunk road) which the Minister or the company[^c-04870c152a7ab255d5022f88d42f3fe8] proposes to construct or has, whether before or after the commencement of this Act, constructed.

(2) Where an agreement is made under this section the council who are a party to the agreement shall, on such date as may be provided by the agreement, become the highway authority for the highway to which the agreement relates.
