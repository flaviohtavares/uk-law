# Child Care Act 1980, section 6

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Appeal to the High Court.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-10-14).**

An appeal shall lie to the High Court from the making by a juvenile court of an order under section 3(6) or 5(4) of this Act or from the refusal by a juvenile court to make such an order.
