# Child Care Act 1980, section 86

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Provisions as to copies of orders.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-10-14).**

A document purporting to be a copy—

- (a) of an order made by a court under or by virtue of any of the provisions contained in sections 47, 48 and 49 of this Act, or
- (b) of an affiliation order referred to in an order under section 49 of this Act,

shall, if it purports to be certified as a true copy by the clerk of the court, be evidence of the order.
