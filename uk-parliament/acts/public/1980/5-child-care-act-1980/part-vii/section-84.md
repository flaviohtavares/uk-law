# Child Care Act 1980, section 84

> This provision is no longer in force.

## 84. Prosecution of offences.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-10-14).**

A local authority may institute proceedings for any offence under section 9(4) or 13(1) of this Act and any offence under Part VI of this Act.
