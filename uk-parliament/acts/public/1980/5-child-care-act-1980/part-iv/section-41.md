# Child Care Act 1980, section 41

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Controlled and assisted community homes exempted from certain provisions of Part VI.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-10-14).**

While a voluntary home is a controlled or assisted community home, sections 57 to 60 of this Act shall not apply in relation to it.
