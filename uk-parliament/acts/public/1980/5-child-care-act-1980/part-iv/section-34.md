# Child Care Act 1980, section 34

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Provision of community homes by local authorities.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-10-14).**

Where a regional plan for a planning area includes provision for a community home to be provided by one of the relevant authorities, it shall be the duty of the local authority concerned to provide, manage, equip and maintain that home.
