# Child Care Act 1980, section 17

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Children to whom Part III applies.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-10-14).**

Except where the contrary intention appears, any reference in this Part of this Act to a child who is or was in the care of a local authority is a reference to a child who is or was in the care of the authority under section 2 of this Act or by virtue of a care order or a warrant under section 23(1) of the [^c739159]Children and Young Persons Act 1969 (which relates to remands to the care of local authorities).
