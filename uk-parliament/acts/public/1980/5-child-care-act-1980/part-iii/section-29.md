# Child Care Act 1980, section 29

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29. Power of local authority to visit and assist persons formerly in their care.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-10-14).**

Where a person was at or after the time when he attained the age of seventeen in the care of a local authority under section 2 of this Act but has ceased to be in their care, then, while he is under the age of twenty-one, the local authority, if so requested by him, may cause him to be visited, advised and befriended and, in exceptional circumstances, to be given financial assistance.
