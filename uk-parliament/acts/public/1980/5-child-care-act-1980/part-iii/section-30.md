# Child Care Act 1980, section 30

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30. Allocation of functions as between local authority and local education authority.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-10-14).**

The Secretary of State for Social Services, the Secretary of State for Education and Science and the Secretary of State for Wales acting jointly may make regulations for providing, where a local authority under this Part of this Act and a local education authority as such have concurrent functions, by which authority the functions are to be exercised, and for determining as respects any functions of a local education authority specified in the regulations whether a child in the care of a local authority is to be treated as a child of parents of sufficient resources or a child of parents without resources.
