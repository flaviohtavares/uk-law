# Child Care Act 1980, section 52

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52. Appeals from orders made under Part V.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-10-14).**

(1) An appeal to the Crown Court from an order made by a magistrates’ court under this Part of this Act may be brought in the following cases and by the following persons, that is to say—

- (a) in the case of an order requiring a person to contribute in respect of himself or any other person (whether a contribution order or an arrears order), by the person required to contribute;
- (b) in the case of an order requiring all or any part of the payments accruing due under an affiliation order to be paid to some other person, by the person who would but for the order be entitled to the payments.

(2) Nothing in this section shall be construed as affecting any other right of appeal conferred by this or any other Act.
