# Child Care Act 1980, section 70

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 70. Returns of information by voluntary organisations.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-10-14).**

Every voluntary organisation shall, at such times and in such form as the Secretary of State may direct, transmit to him such particulars as he may require with respect to the children who are accommodated and maintained in voluntary homes provided by the organisation or who have been boarded out by the organisation.
