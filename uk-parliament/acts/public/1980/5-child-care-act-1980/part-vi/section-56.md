# Child Care Act 1980, section 56

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 56. Definition of voluntary home.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-10-14).**

In this Act, except where otherwise indicated, the expression “**voluntary home**” means any home or other institution for the boarding, care and maintenance of poor children, being either—

- (a) a home or other institution supported wholly or partly by voluntary contributions, or
- (b) a home or other institution supported wholly or partly by endowments, not being a school within the meaning of the [^c739170]Education Act 1944,

but does not in either case include a mental nursing home within the meaning of the [^c739171]Nursing Homes Act 1975 or a residential home for mentally disordered persons within the meaning of the Residential Homes Act 1980.
