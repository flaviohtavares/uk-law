# Housing Act 1980, section 143

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2014-10-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/51
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 143. Apportionment of rents.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 20(1) of the [^c1152096]Landlord and Tenant Act 1927 (apportionment of certain rents and other payments) has effect as respects applications for apportionment made under that section after the passing of this Act with the substitution in the proviso of “£5” for “two pounds”.

(2) The Secretary of State may by order vary the amount there mentioned.

(3)[^c1152097] After section 20(1) of the said Act there is inserted the following subsection—

> (1A) An order of apportionment under sections 10 to 14 of the said Act of 1854 may provide for the amount apportioned to any part of the land in respect of which the rent or payment is payable to be nil.
