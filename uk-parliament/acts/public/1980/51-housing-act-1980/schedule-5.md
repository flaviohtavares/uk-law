# Housing Act 1980, SCHEDULE 5

>

## SCHEDULE 5 (affected by repeal, see note) — Application of Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 to Assured Tenancies (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b][^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

*Extent: E+W*

**1.** The exceptions and modifications referred to in section 58(1) and (2) of this Act are as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

**2.** Sections 23, 43 and 56 to 60B do not apply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

**3.** In relation to an assured tenancy the expression “the holding” (which is defined for the purposes of Part II in section 23(3) means the property comprised in the tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

**4.** (1) Section 30 applies as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

- (a) for paragraph (d) in subsection (1) there were substituted the following paragraph— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

“(d) that the landlord has offered and is willing to provide or secure the provision of suitable alternative accommodation for the tenant,”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

- (b) in subsection (2) for the words from “a tenancy” to the end there were substituted the words “an assured tenancy or successive assured tenancies”; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
- (c) at the end there were added the subsections set out in sub-paragraph (2) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

(2) The following are the subsections added to section 30 in its application to assured tenancies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

> (4) Accommodation shall be deemed to be suitable if it consists of either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
>
> - (a) premises which are to be let as a separate dwelling such that they will then be let on an assured tenancy or on a protected or secure tenancy, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
> - (b) premises to be let as a separate dwelling on terms which will, in the opinion of the court, afford to the tenant security of tenure reasonably equivalent to that afforded by this Part of this Act in the case of an assured tenancy, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
>
> and, in the opinion of the court, the accommodation fulfils the conditions mentioned below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
>
> (5) The conditions are that the accommodation is reasonably suitable to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
>
> - (a) the needs of the tenant and his family as regards proximity to place of work; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
> - (b) the means of the tenant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
> - (c) the needs of the tenant and his family as regards extent and character; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
>
> that if any furniture was provided for use under the assured tenancy in question, furniture is provide for use in the accommodation which is either similar to that so provided or is reasonably suitable to the needs of the tenant and his family. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
>
> (6) Accommodation shall not be deemed to be suitable to the needs of the tenant and his family if the result of their occupation of the accommodation would be that it would be an overcrowded dwelling-house for the purposes of the Housing Act 1957. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
>
> (7) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
>
> - “**assured tenancy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]**” has the same meaning as in section 56 of the Housing Act 1980; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
> - “**protected tenancy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]**” means a protected tenancy within the meaning of the Rent Act 1977, other than one under which the landlord might recover possession of the dwelling-house under one of the Cases in Part II of Schedule 15 to that Act (cases where the court must order possession); and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
> - “**secure tenancy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]**” has the same meaning as in section 28 of the Act of 1980. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

.

**5.** Section 31A applies as if in subsection (1)(a) for the words “for the purposes of the business carried on by the tenant” there were substituted “as a residence for the tenant and his family”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

**6.** Section 34 applies as if in subsection (2)(b) for the words from “tenancies” to the end there were substituted the words “assured tenancies (within the meaning of section 56 of the Housing Act 1980); and”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

**7.** Section 37 applies as if for subsections (2) and (3) there were substituted the following subsection— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

> (2) The said amount shall be *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b] the product of the appropriate multiplier and[^c1152120] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b] the rateable value of the holding. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

.

**7A.**[^c1152121] The power to prescribe a multiplier conferred by sub-section (8) of that section includes power to prescribe a multiplier in relation to assured tenancies different from that prescribed in relation to other tenancies to which Part II of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b] [^c1152122]Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 applies.  *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

**8.**[^c1152123] Section 38 applies as if the following provisions were omitted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

- (a) in subsection (1), the words “(except as provided by subsection (4) of this section)”; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
- (b) in subsection (2), the words from the beginning to the end of paragraph (b); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
- (c) subsections (3) and (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]

**9.** Section 63(7)(a) applies as if reference to section 23(3) of the Act of 1954 were a reference to paragraph 3 of this Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_bacfc434-db4f-4773-a75d-e9064b91c06b]
