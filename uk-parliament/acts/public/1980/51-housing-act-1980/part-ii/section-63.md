# Housing Act 1980, section 63

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2014-10-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/51
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Repeal of sections 48 and 50 of Rent Act 1977.[^c1152053]

*Extent: E+W*

Section 48 of the 1977 Act (increase, on account of improvements, of recoverable rent for statutory periods before registration) and section 50 of that Act (private street works to count as improvements) are hereby repealed.
