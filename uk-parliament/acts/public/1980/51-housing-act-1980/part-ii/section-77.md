# Housing Act 1980, section 77

## 77. Amendment of Part VI of Rent Act 1977.

*Extent: E+W*

Part VI of the 1977 Act (rent limit for dwellings let by housing associations, housing trusts and the Housing Corporation) is amended in accordance with the provisions of Schedule 10 to this Act.
