# Housing Act 1980, section 64

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2014-10-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/51
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Conversion of controlled tenancies into regulated tenancies.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) At the commencement of this section every controlled tenancy shall cease to be a controlled tenancy and become a regulated tenancy, except in the case mentioned in subsection (2) below.

(2) If the controlled tenancy is one to which Part II of the [^c1152054]Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 would apply, apart from section 24(2) of the 1977 Act, or would so apply if it were a tenancy within the meaning of the Act of 1954, it shall, when it ceases to be a controlled tenancy, be treated as a tenancy continuing by virtue of section 24 of the Act of 1954 after the expiry of a term of years certain.
