# Housing Act 1980, section 85

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 85. Interpretation and application of Part III.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part of this Act any expression used [^c1152082] in the 1977 Act has the same meaning as in [^c1152082] that Act.

(2) This Part of this Act applies to tenancies granted before as well as tenancies granted after the commencement of this Part of this Act.
