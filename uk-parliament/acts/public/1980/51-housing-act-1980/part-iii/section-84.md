> Extent: England, Wales.

## 84. Exclusion of certain housing associations from Part III.

*Extent: E+W*

This Part of this Act does not apply in relation to a housing association which falls within paragraph (d) of section 15(3) of the 1977 Act (certain societies registered under the [^c1152081]Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1965).
