# Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1980, section 74

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 74. Drunkenness at sporting event.

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-04-01).**

Any person who—

- (a) is drunk in; or
- (b) while drunk, attempts to enter,

the relevant area of a designated sports ground at any time during the period of a designated sporting event shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 2 on the standard scale[^c10849241].
