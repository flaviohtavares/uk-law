# Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1980, section 71

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Defences in connection with carriage of alcohol.

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-04-01).**

Where a person is charged with an offence under section 69(*b*) , 70 or 70A(4)[^c10849191] of this Act, it shall be a defence for him to prove that the alcohol was carried on the vehicle without his consent or connivance and that he did all he reasonably could to prevent such carriage.
