# Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1980, section 57

> Text as at 2013-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 57. Penalty for second conviction of assault on constable.[^c10848821]

*Extent: S*

In section 41(1)(ii) of the [^c10848831]Police (Scotland) Act 1967 (assaults on constables, etc.), at the end there shall be added the words “ or to a fine not exceeding the prescribed sum within the meaning of section 289B of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1975, or to both. ”.
