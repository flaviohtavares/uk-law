# Criminal Justice (Scotland) Act 1980, section 60

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Payment under compensation order.[^c10848921]

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-04-01).**

(1)[^c10848931] Payment of any amount under a compensation order shall be made to the clerk of the court who shall account for the amount to the person entitled thereto.

(2) Only the court shall have power to enforce a compensation order.
