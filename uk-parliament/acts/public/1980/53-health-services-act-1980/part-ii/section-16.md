# Health Services Act 1980, section 16

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2007-03-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/53
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 16. Nursing homes and mental nursing homes, etc.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The enactments specified in Schedule 4 to this Act (which relate to the registration of nursing homes, mental nursing homes and[^c8086491] private hospitals within the meaning of the [^c8086501]Mental Health (Scotland) Act 1960) shall have effect subject to the amendments provided for in that Schedule.
