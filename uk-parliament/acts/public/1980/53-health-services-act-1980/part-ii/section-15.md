# Health Services Act 1980, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2007-03-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/53
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15. Minor and consequential amendments of the Act of 1976.[^c8086471][^c8086481]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Act of 1976 shall have effect subject to the amendments provided for in Schedule 3 to this Act, being minor amendments and amendments consequential on the amendments made by sections 12 to 14 above.
