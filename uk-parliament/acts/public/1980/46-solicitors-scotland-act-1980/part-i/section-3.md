# Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1980, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (349).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/46

## 3. Establishment and functions of Council of Law Society.

*Extent: S*

(1) The business of the Society shall continue to be conducted by the Council of the Society (referred to in this Act as “**the Council**”) the members of which shall be elected , co-opted or appointed[^c-51bda2f27f91731dfd84ed1e0e83a2d5] in accordance with the provisions of the scheme made under paragraph 2 of Schedule 1.

(2) The Council shall have the functions conferred upon them by this Act and sections 16 to 23 of the 1990 Act[^c20643101].
