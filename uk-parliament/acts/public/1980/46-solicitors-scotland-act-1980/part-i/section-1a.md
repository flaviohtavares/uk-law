# Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1980, section 1A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (349).** Text as at 2026-07-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/46

## 1A. Power to act as statutory regulator[^c-e67e30f153508a70f6561106fd94871b]

*Extent: S*

The Society may—

- (a) act as an approved regulator within the meaning of Part 2 of the 2010 Act,
- (b) do anything that is necessary or expedient for the purposes of doing so.
