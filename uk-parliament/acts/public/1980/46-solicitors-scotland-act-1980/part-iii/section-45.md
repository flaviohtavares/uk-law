# Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1980, section 45

## 45. Safeguarding interests of clients of solicitor struck off or suspended.[^c20645251]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139] The following provisions of this section *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139] (except subsection (4A))[^c20655731] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139] shall have effect in relation to the practice of a solicitor whose name is struck off the roll or who is suspended from practice as a solicitor under any provision of this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139] and, in relation to any incorporated practice, the recognition under section 34(1A) of which is revoked[^c8122621] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139].

(2) In the case of a solicitor,[^c8122631] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139] the solicitor shall within 21 days of the material date satisfy the Council that he has made suitable arrangements for making available to his clients or to some other solicitor or solicitors *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139] or incorporated practice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139] instructed by his clients or by himself— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]

- (a) all deeds, wills, securities, papers, books of accounts, records, vouchers and other documents in his or his firm’s possession or control which are held on behalf of his clients or which relate to any trust of which he is sole trustee or co-trustee only with one or more of his partners or employees, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]
- (b) all sums of money due from him or his firm or held by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]

him or his firm on behalf of his clients or subject to any such trust as aforesaid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]

- (2A)[^c8122651] In the case of an incorporated practice, it shall within 21 days of the material date satisfy the Council that it has made suitable arrangements for making available to its clients or to some other solicitor or solicitors or incorporated practice instructed by its clients or itself— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]
- (a) all deeds, wills, securities, papers, books of accounts, records, vouchers and other documents in its possession or control which are held on behalf of its clients or which relate to any trust of which it is sole trustee or co-trustee only with one or more of its employees; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]
- (b) all sums of money due from it or held by it on behalf of its clients or subject to any trust as aforesaid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]

(3A)[^c20655771] If the solicitor or, as the case may be, the incorporated practice fails so to satisfy the Council, the Council may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]

- (a) require the production or delivery to any person appointed by them at a time and place fixed by them of the documents mentioned in subsection (3B); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]
- (b) take possession of all such documents; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]
- (c) apply to the Court for an order that no payment be made by any banker, building society or other body named in the order out of any banking account or any sum deposited in the name of the solicitor or his firm or, as the case may be, the incorporated practice without the leave of the Court and the Court may make such order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]

(3B) The documents are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]

- (a) all books, accounts, deeds, securities, papers and other documents in the possession or control of the solicitor or his firm or, as the case may be, the incorporated practice; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]
- (b) all books, accounts, deeds, securities, papers and other documents relating to any trust of which the solicitor is a sole trustee or is a co-trustee only with one or more of his partners or employees or, as the case may be, of which the incorporated practice or one of its employees is a sole trustee or of which the practice is a co-trustee only with one or more of its employees. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]

(4) If the solicitor, immediately before the striking off or, as the case may be, the suspension,was a sole solicitor, the right to operate on, or otherwise deal with, any client account in the name of the solicitor or his firm shall on the occurrence of that event vest in the Society (notwithstanding any enactment or rule of law to the contrary) to the exclusion of any other person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]

(4A)[^c20655091] Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]

- (a) a solicitor is restricted from acting as a principal; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]
- (b) immediately before the restriction the solicitor was a sole solicitor, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]

the right to operate on, or otherwise deal with, any client account in the name of the solicitor or the solicitor's firm shall on the occurrence of those circumstances vest in the Society (notwithstanding any enactment or rule of law to the contrary) to the exclusion of any other person until such time as the Council have approved acceptable other arrangements in respect of the client account. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]

(4B)[^c20655791] Part II of Schedule 3 has effect in relation to the powers of the Council under subsection (3A). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]

(5) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]

- “**material date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]**” means whichever is the latest of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]
  - (a) the date when the order of the Tribunal or Court by or in pursuance of which the solicitor is struck off the roll or suspended from practice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139] or, as the case may be, the recognition under section 34(1A) is revoked.[^c8122691] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139] is to take effect; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]
  - (b) the last date on which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]
    - (i) an appeal against that order may be lodged or an application may be made to the Court under section 54(2), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]
    - (ii) an appeal against a decision of the Council under section 40 may be lodged; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]
  - (c) the date on which any such appeal is dismissed or abandoned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139] [^c20655831]. . .
- “[^c20655111]**principal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]**” means a solicitor who is a sole practitioner or is a partner in a firm of two or more solicitors or is a director of an incorporated practice which is a company or a solicitor who is a member of a multi-national practice having its principal place of business in Scotland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]
- “**sole solicitor *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]**” means a solicitor practising under his own name or as a single solicitor under a firm name. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6f7568dcbe77ec2b5d937e2db8eef139]
