# Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1980, section 41

## 41. Appointment of judicial factor.[^c20644361]

*Extent: S*

Where the Council, in exercise of any power conferred on them by rules made by virtue of section 34(1D) or[^key-b97a1465ee9b55644770d355831fec06] the accounts rules, have caused an investigation to be made of the books, accounts and other documents of a solicitor or an[^c8121831] incorporated practice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-59e6f3b3e90ed499d3a8991fc223eb57] authorised legal business, and, on consideration of the report of the investigation, the Council are satisfied—

- (a) that the solicitor or, as the case may be, the[^c8121841] incorporated practice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-34010b5bcce39a1834bdbad7a0f74ed3] authorised legal business has failed. . . [^c8121851] to comply with the provisions of those rules, and
- (b) that, , in the case of a solicitor, in connection with his practice as such[^c8121861], either—
  - (i) his liabilities exceed his assets in the business, or
  - (ii) his books, accounts and other documents are in such a condition that it is not reasonably practicable to ascertain definitely whether his liabilities exceed his assets, or
  - (iii) there is reasonable ground for apprehending that a claim on the Guarantee Fund may arise ; or[^c8121871]
- (c) that, in the case of an incorporated practice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e1bd88d96c51e4fc4ec40decedc656da] authorised legal business, either—
  - (i) its liabilities exceed its assets, or
  - (ii) its books, accounts and other documents are in such a condition that it is not reasonably practicable to ascertain definitely whether its liabilities exceed its assets, or
  - (iii) there is reasonable ground for apprehending that a claim on the guarantee fund may arise.

the Council may apply to the Court for the appointment of a judicial factor on the estate of the solicitor or, as the case may be, of the[^c8121881] incorporated practice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0a98e1fa737ba6464948f4e0a3dcc2c0] authorised legal business; and the Court, on consideration of the said report and after giving the solicitor or, as the case may be, the[^c8121891] incorporated practice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0a98e1fa737ba6464948f4e0a3dcc2c0] authorised legal business an opportunity of being heard, may appoint a judicial factor on such[^c8121901] estate, or do otherwise as seems proper to it.
