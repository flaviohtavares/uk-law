# Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1980, section 9

## 9. Removal of name from roll on request.

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-d98e9bc6d882129522daa0c49731b86d] An enrolled solicitor who wishes his name , or any annotation made against his name under section 25A(3),[^c8119061] to be removed from the roll of solicitors may make an application to the Council in that behalf, and the Council shall [^key-49655f800f2612817c4b7ba98aaae659]... remove the name of that solicitor or, as the case may be, the annotation against his name,[^c8119081] from the roll.

(2)[^key-8ceb790e1bf448e67cb593522ad1cac7] But the Council are required to remove the name or annotation only if they are satisfied that—

- (a) the solicitor has made adequate arrangements with respect to the business which the solicitor then has in hand, and
- (b) it is otherwise appropriate to do so.
