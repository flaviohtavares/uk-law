# Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1980, section 30

## 30. Liability for fees of other solicitor.[^c20644111]

*Extent: S*

Where a solicitor or an incorporated practice[^c8120271], authorised by and acting for a client employs another solicitor or incorporated practice[^c8120281] or a licensed legal services provider[^c-31b512b119cf6a3c1d01cdb746be62b9] he or, as the case may be, it[^c8120291] shall (whether or not he or, as the case may be, it discloses the client) be liable to the employed party[^c-73c4892416603d2a760ab4a87af9cdf1] for that party's[^c-5ae3de4fc12e749b01969856c64d7c6c] fees and outlays, unless at the time of the employment he or, as the case may be, it expressly disclaims any such liability.
