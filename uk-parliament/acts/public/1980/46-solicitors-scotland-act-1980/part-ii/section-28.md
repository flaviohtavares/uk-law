# Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1980, section 28

## 28. Offence for solicitors who are disqualified to seek employment without informing employer.

*Extent: S*

Any person who—

- (a) has been struck off the roll; or
- (b) suspended from practice as a solicitor ; or[^c8120221]
- (c) has had his registration as a registered European lawyer withdrawn; or
- (d) has been suspended from practice as a registered European lawyer; or[^c20647161]
- (e) has had his registration as a registered foreign lawyer withdrawn; or
- (f) has been suspended from practice as a registered foreign lawyer,

and while so disqualified from practice seeks or accepts employment by a solicitor in connection with that solicitors practice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2cbad7e869483ea0bf6b06da11d83a8a] or by an incorporated practice[^c8120231] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2cbad7e869483ea0bf6b06da11d83a8a]or multi-national practice[^c20647181] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2cbad7e869483ea0bf6b06da11d83a8a] an authorised legal business without previously informing him *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8c39875336cbbc6b7c4101fe2527153] or, as the case may be,[^c8120241] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8c39875336cbbc6b7c4101fe2527153] it that he is so disqualified, shall be guilty of an offence.
