# Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1980, section 33

## 33. Unqualified persons not entitled to fees, etc.[^c20652301]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-9c6999fd45ddbf0d54ce393ed054d892] Subject to the provisions of regulations 12 and 13 of the European Communities (Lawyer’s Practice) (Scotland) Regulations 2000[^c8120751] as those regulations have effect by virtue of regulation 6 or 7 of the Services of Lawyers and Lawyer's Practice (EU Exit) (Scotland) (Amendment etc.) Regulations 2019[^key-341cd3b1b0ca6cde91f1be55bdb8a152], no fee, reward, outlay or expenses on account of or in relation to any act or proceeding done or taken by any person who—

- (a) acts as a solicitor or as a notary public without being duly qualified so to act; or
- (b) not being so qualified, [^c8120761]. . . frames or draws any writs to which section 32 applies,

shall be recoverable by any person in any action or matter.

(2)[^key-1b0c4ac12b7e6fbb87bb317a02af5f87] This section does not apply to an incorporated practice[^c8120771] or a licensed legal services provider[^key-8693a5c20644fc5f2abd8075c5b3259e] or in relation to writs framed or drawn by a person who is, by virtue of an act of sederunt made under section 32 of the Sheriff Courts (Scotland) Act 1971, permitted to represent a party to a summary cause.[^c8120781]
