# Education (Scotland) Act 1980, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/44

## 31. School age.[^c9694191][^c9694201][^c9694211]

*Extent: S*

Subject to sections 32(3) and 33(2) and (4) of this Act, a person is of school age if he has attained the age of five years and has not attained the age of sixteen years.
