## 28G. Application of sections 28A to 28F and Schedule A1 to young persons.[^c-cafc016c45f8c536017dcfe381ec7c77]

*Extent: S*

—Sections 28A to 28F of this Act and Schedule A1 to this Act shall apply in relation to a young person who is a pupil and in that application references in those sections to the parent of a qualifying[^c16022271] child as well as references to the qualifying child himself shall be construed as references to the young person.
