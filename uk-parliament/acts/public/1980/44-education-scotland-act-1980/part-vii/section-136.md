# Education (Scotland) Act 1980, section 136

## 136. Transitional and savings provisions, amendments and repeals.

*Extent: S*

(1) The transitional and savings provisions set out in Schedule 3 to this Act shall have effect.

(2)[^c9704511] The enactments specified in Schedule 4 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in that Schedule, being amendments consequential on the consolidation under this Act.

(3)[^c9704511] The enactments set out in Schedule 5 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
