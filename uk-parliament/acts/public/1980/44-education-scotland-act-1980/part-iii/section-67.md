## 67. Local inquiries.

*Extent: S*

The Secretary of State may cause a local inquiry to be held for the purpose of the exercise of any of his functions under this Act or any other enactment relating to education[^c9696961], and the provisions of Schedule 1 to this Act shall have effect with regard to any such inquiry.
