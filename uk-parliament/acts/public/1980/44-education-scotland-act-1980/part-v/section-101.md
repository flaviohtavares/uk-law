# Education (Scotland) Act 1980, section 101

## 101. Enforcement of orders.

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-5bd18abee771db1d6069eba814345e61] Any person who carries on an independent school which is not registered shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or to both such fine and such imprisonment.

(2) If any person uses

- (a)[^c-cd91c70429dd2eeb7ba0775f5883b76d] school premises which are or any part of such premises which is; or
- (b) any accommodation provided at such premises which is,

disqualified by virtue of any order made under this Part of this Act, that person shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale[^c9698621], or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or to both such fine and such imprisonment.

(3) If any person acts as the proprietor of an independent school, [^c-80adfa5b0deb41d37d4592dd6639cada]..., while he is disqualified from so acting [^c-80adfa5b0deb41d37d4592dd6639cada]... by any such order as aforesaid, he shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale, or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or to both such fine and such imprisonment.

(3A)[^c-a80d977c642369cc6f5b70620ff7f706] If any person who is, by virtue of this Part of this Act, disqualified from being a teacher in any school—

- (a) endeavours to obtain a position as a teacher;
- (b) accepts such a position; or
- (c) teaches,

in any school, that person shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or to both such fine and such imprisonment.

(4) For the purposes of the foregoing provisions of this Part of this Act, a person who is subject to or deemed to be subject to a direction given under section 142 of the Education Act 2002 (c. 32) shall be deemed to be disqualified[^c-625fa3b90e7f45b2d4dce0aae3f4091d] from being the proprietor of an independent school or from being a teacher in any school [^c-6c1aad419f05edb05c4262bef9e92321]... by an order made under this Part of this Act.
