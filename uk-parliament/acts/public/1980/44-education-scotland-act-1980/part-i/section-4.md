# Education (Scotland) Act 1980, section 4

## 4. Duty of education authorities to provide child guidance service.

*Extent: S*

It shall be the duty of every education authority to provide for their area \[[^c9691831][^c9691841]. . .\] psychological service [^key-812e7238793ca03b5fcd8e7e30c45d11]..., and the functions of that service shall include— [^c9691851]

- (a) the study of children having additional support needs[^key-432014ebe26b42dcd897d5e31c964d7c];\]
- (b) the giving of advice to parents and teachers as to appropriate methods of education. . . [^c9691861] for such children;
- (c) in suitable cases,. . . [^c9691871] provision for the[^c9691881] additional support needs[^key-d79253a32f8cf76a3e73b085f9aca25a] of such children [^key-5c133f7da4e4fa726e67a39d5b7a85aa]... [^M_F_2a811957-9c9a-4239-fa06-0c61aca0a327]... [^key-5c133f7da4e4fa726e67a39d5b7a85aa]...;
- (d) the giving of advice to a local authority within the meaning of the [^c9691901]Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968 regarding the assessment of the needs of any child for the purposes of any of the provisions of that or any other enactment.
