# Water (Scotland) Act 1980, section 54

## 54. Register of meter to be evidence.

*Extent: S*

(1) Where Scottish Water supplies[^c18219311] water by meter, the register of the meter shall be sufficient[^c13461401] evidence of the quantity of water supplied

(2)[^c13461411] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) If the meter on being tested is proved to register incorrectly to any degree exceeding five per cent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-314dd7600b2ca43371e1cf7b35e19eec] the relevant percentage—

- (a) the meter shall be deemed to have registered incorrectly to that degree since the last occasion but one before the date of the test on which a reading of the index of the meter was taken by Scottish Water[^c18219351] , unless it is proved to have begun to register incorrectly on some later date; and
- (b) the amount of any refund to be made to, or of any extra payment to be made by, the consumer shall be paid or allowed by Scottish Water or paid by the consumer, as the case may be, [^c13461421].

(4)[^c-6a604b061ef5152381d05deebcfdf5a6] In subsection (3), “**the relevant percentage**” means—

- (a) in the case of a high flow rate, 2.5%,
- (b) in the case of a low flow rate, 6%.

(5) The Scottish Ministers, by regulations—

- (a) must define “high flow rate” and “low flow rate” for the purpose of subsection (4),
- (b) may amend a percentage figure appearing in subsection (4).
