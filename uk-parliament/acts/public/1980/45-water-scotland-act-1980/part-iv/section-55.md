## 55. Charges for water supplied by meter.

*Extent: S*

(1) Scottish Water[^c18219401] shall prepare and maintain a schedule of the terms and conditions , other than as respects charges,[^c13461441] on which it is[^c18219421] prepared in general to give a supply of water by meter or otherwise, and that schedule shall be published in such manner as in the opinion of Scottish Water[^c18219441] will secure adequate publicity for it.

(2) Nothing in the foregoing subsection shall be construed as prejudicing the rights or duties under section 9 of Scottish Water[^c18219461] or of any person supplied or proposed to be supplied by it[^c18219481] under that section.
