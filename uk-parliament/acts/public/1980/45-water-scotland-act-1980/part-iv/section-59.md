# Water (Scotland) Act 1980, section 59

## 59. Limitation of liability of water authority to supply water on special terms.

*Extent: S*

(1) Notwithstanding anything in any such obligation as is mentioned in section 58(4) Scottish Water[^c18235821] shall not be liable under the obligation to provide to any premises in any year a quantity of water greater than the quantitiy supplied thereto in the year immediately preceding 27th October 1948, or to provide to any premises not provided by them with a supply of water on that day a supply otherwise than on the terms on which the supply would, apart from the obligation, have been provided under the [^c13461611]Water (Scotland) Act 1946 and the [^c13461621]Water (Scotland) Act 1949.

(2) Any question arising under the foregoing subsection shall in case of dispute be determined by arbitration.
