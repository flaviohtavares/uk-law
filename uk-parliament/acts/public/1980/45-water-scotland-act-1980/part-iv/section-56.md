# Water (Scotland) Act 1980, section 56

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2018-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/45
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 56. Provisions as to supply of water to agricultural subjects.

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a supply of water for purposes other than domestic purposes is provided for premises being agricultural lands and heritages, Scottish Water[^c18235521] shall require the supply to be taken either by meter or on other specified terms as it[^c18235541] may from time to time in its[^c18235561] discretion determine.

(2) Where Scottish Water supplies[^c18235581] water by meter for purposes other than domestic purposes to any premises being agricultural lands and heritages and also supplies[^c18235601] water for domestic purposes to any dwelling house comprised in such premises, Scottish Water[^c18235621] shall, if it is reasonably practicable so to do, provide the whole supply of water to such premises and dwelling house through a single meter.
