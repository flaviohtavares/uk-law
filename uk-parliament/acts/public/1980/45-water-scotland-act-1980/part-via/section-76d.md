# Water (Scotland) Act 1980, section 76D

## 76D. Provision of water where piped supplies insufficient or unwholesome.

*Extent: S*

(1) Where—

- (a) it is not practicable at reasonable cost for Scottish Water[^c18306671] , by supplying water in pipes, to provide or maintain such a supply of wholesome water to any particular premises in its limits of supply as (so far as those premises are concerned) is sufficient for domestic purposes;
- (b) it is practicable at reasonable cost for Scottish Water[^c18306691] to provide such a supply to those premises otherwise than in pipes;
- (c) the insufficiency or unwholesomeness of the supply of water for domestic purposes to those premises is such as to cause a danger to life or health; and
- (d) the local authority in whose area those premises are situated notify Scottish Water[^c18306711] of that danger and require Scottish Water to provide a supply otherwise than in pipes, it shall be the duty of Scottish Water , for such period as may be required by that local authority, to provide any supply to those premises which it is practicable at reasonable cost to provide otherwise than in pipes and which it is required to provide by that local authority.

(2) Where under subsection (1) above a local authority require the provision by Scottish Water[^c18306761] of a supply of water to any premises, that local authority—

- (a) shall be liable to Scottish Water[^c18306781] for any charges payable in respect of the provision of that supply; but
- (b) shall have power to recover the whole or any part of any charges paid by virtue of this subsection from the owner or occupier of the premises to which the supply is provided.

(3) In this section references to the provision of a supply of water to any premises otherwise than in pipes shall have effect, in a case in which it is practicable at reasonable cost to provide a supply (whether or not in pipes) to a place within a reasonable distance of those premises, as including references to the provision of a supply to that place.
