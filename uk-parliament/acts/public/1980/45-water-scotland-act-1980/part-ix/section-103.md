# Water (Scotland) Act 1980, section 103

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2018-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/45

## 103. Notices to be in writing.

*Extent: S*

All notices, consents, approvals, demands and other documents authorised or required by or under this Act or any local enactment incorporating any provisions of Schedule 4 to be given, made or issued by the Secretary of State or any local authority[^c13463631], or Scottish Water[^c18307661][^c13463641], and all notices and applications authorised or required by or under this Act or any such local enactment to be given or made to the Secretary of State or to, or to any officer of, any local authority, or Scottish Water[^c13463641], shall , subject to section 23(4) of and paragraph 4(1) of Schedule 3 to this Act,[^c13463671] be in writing.
