## 100. Orders.

*Extent: S*

(1) Any power to make orders under this Act may be exercisable by statutory instrument.

(2)[^c13463541] Before making, on his own initiative, an order under section 107, the Secretary of State shall consult Scottish Water[^c18307611] .
