# Water (Scotland) Act 1980, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2018-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/45
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 1. General duties of Secretary of State and of water authorities.[^c13457431]

*Extent: S*

It shall be the duty of the Secretary of State and of Scottish Water[^c18054751] when exercising their respective functions or powers under or by virtue of this Act—

- (a) to promote the conservation and effective use of the water resources of, and the provision of adequate water supplies throughout, Scotland; and
- (b) to secure the collection, preparation, publication and dissemination of information and statistics relating to such resources and supplies.
