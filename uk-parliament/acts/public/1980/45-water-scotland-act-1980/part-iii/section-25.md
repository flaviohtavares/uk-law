# Water (Scotland) Act 1980, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2018-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/45
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 25. Power of authority to provide public wells.

*Extent: S*

(1) A local authority[^c13459471] or Scottish Water[^c18177201] may construct and maintain public pumps, wells, stand-pipes or other works for supplying water for the use of the inhabitants within their area or, as the case may be, its[^c18177221] limits of supply.

(2) A local authority[^c13459491] may erect and maintain or authorise to be erected and maintained in any road[^c13459501] or public place within their area [^c13459511] any drinking fountain or trough; but where the Secretary of State is the roads authority[^c13459521] there shall be no such erection by the[^c13459531] the local authority[^c13459541] without his[^c13459551] consent which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld.
