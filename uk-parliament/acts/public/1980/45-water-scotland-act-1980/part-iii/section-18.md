# Water (Scotland) Act 1980, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2018-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/45
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 18. Compulsory acquisition of land for water works.

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this section, an order under section 17 may authorise Scottish Water[^c18137671] to acquire compulsorily such land as may be necessary for the purposes of the order, being land which Scottish Water[^c18137681][^c13459031] could be authorised to acquire under section 47 of the Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002 (asp 3)[^c18137691] .

(2) The provisions of Schedule 2 shall have effect in relation to an order under section 17 which authorises any such acquisition of land.
