# Water (Scotland) Act 1980, section 36

## 36. Power to execute work on behalf of owner or occupier.

*Extent: S*

Scottish Water[^c18217061] may by agreement with the owner or occupier of any premises execute at his expense any work in connection with the laying, maintenance, repair, alteration, renewal or removal of any water pipe, cock or meter which he is required or entitled to execute by or under this Act or any local enactment incorporating any provisions of Schedule 4, and for that purpose shall have all such rights as he has and may provide necessary materials.
