# Water (Scotland) Act 1980, section 13A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2018-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/45
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 13A. Supply of water for use outwith Scotland.[^c13458791]

*Extent: S*

(1) Scottish Water[^c18122341] may, if for the time being it is[^c18122371] satisfied that such supplies of water as are available to it[^c18122621] are likely to be more than sufficient to enable it to fulfil its[^c18123031] duties as respects the supply of water to premises in Scotland, enter into an agreement with any other person to give him, on such terms and conditions as it thinks[^c18122651] and whether or not in bulk, a supply of water for use outwith Scotland.

(2) For the purposes of laying any pipes or installing any apparatus connected therewith, being pipes or apparatus required for giving a supply of water in pursuance of an agreement entered into under subsection (1) above, Scottish Water[^c19562901] may exercise, [^c19553811]. . . , the like powers with respect to laying mains or breaking open roads as are exercisable by it[^c18122661] under this Act for the purposes of laying mains, but subject to the like conditions and obligations.
