# Water (Scotland) Act 1980, section 24A

## 24A. Keeping of map showing water mains, etc.[^c13459441]

*Extent: S*

(1) Scottish Water[^c18176121] shall keep deposited at its[^c18176131] principal office a map showing and distinguishing so far as is reasonably practicable all water mains, communication pipes and supply pipes which are vested in it[^c18176141] by virtue of this Act or of the Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002 (asp 3)[^c18176151]; and Scottish Water[^c18176161] shall provide reasonable facilities at that office for inspection of the map by any person and shall permit a copy of the map, or of an extract of it, to be taken by a person on his paying such reasonable amount as Scottish Water may determine.

(2) Scottish Water[^c18176521] shall keep deposited at such of its[^c18176701] offices, other than its principal office, as it considers[^c18176761] appropriate, a copy relevant to the office in question of part of the map mentioned in subsection (1) above; and Scottish Water[^c18176771] shall provide the like facilities and permission in relation to the copy part, at the office at which that copy is deposited, as, under subsection (1) above, it does[^c18176781] in relation to the map mentioned in that subsection at its principal office.

(3) For the purposes of subsection (2) above, a copy is relevant to an office if it is of such part of the map mentioned in subsection (1) above as appears to Scottish Water[^c18176791] to be appropriate having regard to the geographical location of that office.
