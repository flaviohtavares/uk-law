## 22. Power to break open streets.

*Extent: S*

(1)[^key-068c888193d2a52a94d996e96412a7ec] The provisions of Part I of Schedule 3 shall have effect in relation to the breaking open of roads[^c13459111] by Scottish Water[^c18156931][^c13459121] for the purpose of carrying out any works which it is[^c18156941] authorised to execute by or under this Act or any local enactment incorporating any provisions of Schedule 4.

(2)[^key-d8b65c77785e3c87c8d2ac57c340d5dc] Paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 has effect in relation to works carried out by any person—

- (a) in pursuance of an authorisation under section 23A(1), or
- (b) in connection with the management, maintenance or renewal of a main or communication pipe laid by that person in pursuance of such an authorisation and vested in that person,

as it has in relation to works carried out by Scottish Water.
