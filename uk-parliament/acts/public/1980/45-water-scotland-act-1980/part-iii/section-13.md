# Water (Scotland) Act 1980, section 13

## 13. Supply of water in bulk.

*Extent: S*

(1) Scottish Water[^c18122001][^c13458631] may enter into an agreement with any other persons [^c18122041]. . . , for the giving by those other persons, and the taking by Scottish Water[^c18122051][^c13458631], of a supply of water in bulk for any period and on any terms and conditions, [^c18122061]. . .

(2) [^c18122081]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) For the purpose of laying any pipes or installing any apparatus connected therewith, being pipes or apparatus required for giving or taking a supply of water in pursuance of an agreement [^c18122091]. . . made under this section, Scottish Water[^c18122121][^c13458731] may exercise, [^c18122131]. . . [^c13458731] the like powers with respect to laying mains and breaking open roads[^c13458751] as are exercisable by it[^c18122141] under this Act for the purpose of laying mains, but subject to the like conditions and obligations.

(4) [^c18122171]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) [^c18122171]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) Nothing in this section shall affect any right of a navigation authority under any enactment to prohibit, or impose restrictions on, the supply of water in bulk by Scottish Water[^c18122191][^c13458781].
