# Water (Scotland) Act 1980, section 8

## 8. Water supplied for domestic purposes to be wholesome.

*Extent: S*

Scottish Water[^c18085931] shall provide in its[^c18085971] mains and communication pipes a supply of wholesome water sufficient for the domestic purposes of all owners and occupiers of premises within its limits of supply who are entitled to a supply for those purposes.
