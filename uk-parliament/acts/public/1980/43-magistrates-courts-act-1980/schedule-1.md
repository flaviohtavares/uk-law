## SCHEDULE 1 — OFFENCES TRIABLE EITHER WAY BY VIRTUE OF SECTION 17

*Extent: E+W*

Section 17.

**1.** Offences at common law of public nuisance.

**1A.**[^c19472331] An offence at common law of outraging public decency.

**2.** [^c19978781]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**3.** Offences consisting in contravention of section 13 of the [^c18264451]Statutory Declarations Act 1835 (administration by a person of an oath etc. touching matters in which he has no jurisdiction).

**4.** Offences under section 36 of the [^c18264461]Malicious Damage Act 1861 (obstructing engines or carriages on railways).

**5.** Offences under the following provisions of the Offences against the [^c18264471]Person Act 1861—

- (a) section 16 (threats to kill);
- (b) section 20 (inflicting bodily injury, with or without a weapon);
- (c) section 26 (not providing apprentices or servants with food etc.);
- (d) section 27 (abandoning or exposing child);
- (e) section 34 (doing or omitting to do anything so as to endanger railway passengers);
- (f) section 36 (assaulting a clergyman at a place of worship etc.);
- (g) section 38 (assault with intent to resist apprehension);
- (h) section 47 (assault occasioning bodily harm. . . [^c18264481]);
- (i) section 57 (bigamy);
- (j) section 60 (concealing the birth of a child).

**6.** Offences under section 20 of the [^c18264491]Telegraph Act 1868 (disclosing or intercepting messages).

**7.** Offences under section 13 of the [^c18264501]Debtors Act 1869 (transactions intended to defraud creditors).

**8.** Offences under section 5 of the [^c18264511]Public Stores Act 1875 (obliteration of marks with intent to conceal).

**9.** Offences under section 12 of the [^c18264521]Corn Returns Act 1882 (false returns).

**10.** . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c18264531]

**11.** Offences under section 3 of the [^c18264541]Submarine Telegraph Act 1885 (damaging submarine cables).

**12.** Offences under section 13 of the [^c18264551]Stamp Duties Management Act 1891 (offences in relation to dies and stamps).

**13.** Offences under section 8(2) of the [^c18264561]Cremation Act 1902 (making false representations etc. with a view to procuring the burning of any human remains).

**14.** All offences under the [^c18264571]Perjury Act 1911 except offences under—

- (a) section 1 (perjury in judicial proceedings);
- (b) section 3 (false statements etc. with reference to marriage);
- (c) section 4 (false statements etc. as to births or deaths).

**15.** . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c18264581]

**16.**[^c-a4f5e89cfae6db83a3e22240ee77c73d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**17.** . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c18264601]

**18.** Offences under section 8(2) of the [^c18264611]Census Act 1920 (disclosing census information).

**19.** Offences under section 36 of the [^c18264621]Criminal Justice Act 1925 (forgery of passports etc.).

**20.** Offences under section 11 of the [^c18264631]Agricultural Credits Act 1928 (frauds by farmers).

**21.** . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c18264641]

**22.**[^c18264661] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**23.** [^c19472801]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**24.** . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c18264681]

**25.** . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c18264691]

**26.** The following offences under the [^c18264701]Criminal Law Act 1967—

- (a) offences under section 4(1) (assisting offenders); and
- (b) offences under section 5(1) (concealing arrestable offences and giving false information),

where the offence to which they relate is triable either way.

**27.** [^c19472771]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**28.** All indictable offences under the [^c18264721]Theft Act 1968 except:—

- (a) robbery, aggravated burglary, blackmail and assault with intent to rob;
- (b) burglary comprising the commission of, or an intention to commit, an offence which is triable only on indictment;
- (c) burglary in a dwelling if any person in the dwelling was subjected to violence or the threat of violence.

**29.** Offences under the following provisions of the [^c18264731]Criminal Damage Act 1971—

- section 1(1) (destroying or damaging property);
- section 1(1) and (3) (arson);
- section 2 (threats to destroy or damage property);
- section 3 (possessing anything with intent to destroy or damage property).

**30.** Offences in relation to stamps issued for the purpose of national insurance under the provisions of any enactments as applied to those stamps.

**31.** Uttering any forged document the forgery of which is an offence listed in this Schedule.

**32.** [^c19472831]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**33.** Aiding, abetting, counselling or procuring the commission of any offence listed in the preceding paragraphs of this Schedule except paragraph 26.
