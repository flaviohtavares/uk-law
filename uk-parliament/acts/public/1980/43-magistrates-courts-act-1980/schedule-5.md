## SCHEDULE 5 — TRANSFER OF REMAND HEARINGS

*Extent: E+W*

Section 130.

**1.** A court which, on adjourning a case, makes an order under section 130(1) of this Act is not required at that time to fix the time and place at which the case is to be resumed but shall do so as soon as practicable after the order ceases to be in force.

**2.**[^c-1b5f4b5f099bd06adba96cb56b3cf827] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

**3.** A court making an order under subsection (1) of section 130 of this Act or remanding the accused under subsection (4) shall at once notify the terms of the order or remand to the court before which the accused is to be brought for the hearing on any application for a subsequent remand or, as the case may be, before which any such application is to be made without his being brought before it[^c18265251]

**4.** A person to whom an order under section 130(1) of this Act applies shall, if released on bail, be bailed to appear before the court which made the order.

**5.** Section 130 of this Act and this Schedule have effect notwithstanding anything in sections 10, 17C, 18(4) or 24C[^c-4cfdbdcf41e59045b96b6be25abfe1bd] of this Act.
