# Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Maximum penalties on summary conviction in pursuance of section 22.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where in pursuance of subsection (2) of section 22 above a magistrates’ court proceeds to the summary trial of an information, then, if the accused is summarily convicted of the offence—

- (a) subject to subsection (3) below[^c18241991] the court shall not have power to impose on him in respect of that offence imprisonment for more than 3 months or a fine greater than level 4 on the standard scale[^c18242001]; and
- (b) section 3 of the Powers of Criminal Courts (Sentencing) Act 2000[^c18242011] shall not apply as regards that offence.

(2) In subsection (1) above “**fine**” includes a pecuniary penalty but does not include a pecuniary forfeiture or pecuniary compensation.

(3)[^c18242021] Paragraph (a) of subsection (1) above does not apply to an offence under section 12A of the Theft Act 1968 (aggravated vehicle-taking).
