## 46. Corporations.[^c20643911][^c-98bdbc347ccf412e0f64922117da0fa4][^c-c593c90c6ac42bc9045462145cddb976][^c-fe867572d2b5e17a85aa4d06df2479d6]

*Extent: E+W*

The provisions of Schedule 3 to this Act shall have effect where a corporation is charged with an offence before a magistrates’ court.
