# Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980, section 96A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 96A. Application of Part III to persons aged 17 to 20.[^c18254071]

*Extent: E+W*

This Part of this Act shall have effect in relation to a person aged 18[^c18254081] or over but less than 21 as if any reference to committing a person to prison, or fixing a term of imprisonment for a default, were a reference to committing the person to, or, as the case may be, to fixing a term of, detention under section 108 of the[^c18254091] [^c18254101]Powers of Criminal Courts (Sentencing) Act 2000; and any reference to warrants of commitment, or to periods of imprisonment imposed for default, shall be construed accordingly.
