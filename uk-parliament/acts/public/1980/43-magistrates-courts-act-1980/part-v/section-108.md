# Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980, section 108

## 108. Right of appeal to the Crown Court.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c18256471] A person convicted by a magistrates’ court may appeal to the Crown Court—

- (a) if he pleaded guilty, against his sentence;
- (b)[^c18256481][^c-98b34f9aafcc25508b043b1cf02e680c][^c-5464fbf8a1ddf09730b7eac7d5730c51][^c-56e52daf6f3de9a19eb5a41e670cfca1] if he did not, against the conviction or sentence.

(1A)[^c18256491] Section 82 of the Sentencing Code[^c-ef06eaca7e9f6c104159cf17a2a154de] (under which a conviction of an offence for which [^c18256521]... an order for conditional or absolute discharge is made is deemed not to be a conviction except for certain purposes) shall not prevent an appeal under this Act, whether against conviction or otherwise.

(2) A person sentenced by a magistrates’ court for an offence in respect of which [^c18256531]... an order for conditional discharge has been previously made may appeal to the Crown Court against the sentence.

(2A)[^c-b30f6b83c917beedd882ca2f86025329] A person convicted under section 16H may not appeal under this section against the conviction or sentence, except a sentence imposed under section 16M(5)(b).

(3) In this section “**sentence**” includes any order made on conviction by a magistrates’ court, not being—

- (a)[^c18256541] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) an order for the payment of costs;
- (c) an order under [^c18256551]... section 37(1) of the Animal Welfare Act 2006[^c19492302] (which enables a court to order the destruction of an animal); or
- (d) an order made in pursuance of any enactment under which the court has no discretion as to the making of the order or its terms

and also includes a[^c18256571] declaration of relevance, within the meaning of section 23 of[^c19492351] the Football Spectators Act 1989.

(4)[^c19867211] Subsection (3)(d) above does not prevent an appeal against a surcharge imposed under section 42 of the Sentencing Code[^c-eb312ff086331f94a16bcd4252dede02].

(5)[^c-df0e0b0f959fb0a56a43d14535c0a9c4] Subsection (3) does not prevent an appeal against an order under section 46 of the Sentencing Code[^c-f77fb0f02927ccf20b49cf6df532ade5] (criminal courts charge).
