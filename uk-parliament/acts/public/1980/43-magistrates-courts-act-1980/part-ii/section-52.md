# Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980, section 52

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Jurisdiction to deal with complaints.[^c19351181]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A magistrates' court has jurisdiction to hear any complaint.

(2) But subsection (1) is subject to provision made by any enactment.
