## 56. Non-appearance of complainant.[^c-275a26e81fc79304e5da60fc07cb2f77]

*Extent: E+W*

[^c-6ab85b391824bc234f96b0448cc5e5cf]... Where[^c-517f65fcc050d5fc0af4beb1c11d77db] at the time and place appointed for the hearing or adjourned hearing of a complaint the defendant appears but the complainant does not, the court may dismiss the complaint or, if evidence has been received on a previous occasion, proceed in the absence of the complainant.
