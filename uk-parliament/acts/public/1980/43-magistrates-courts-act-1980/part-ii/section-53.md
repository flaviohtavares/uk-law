## 53. Procedure on hearing.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c18242971] On the hearing of a complaint, the court shall, if the defendant appears, state to him the substance of the complaint.

(2)[^c18242971] The court, after hearing the evidence and the parties, shall make the order for which the complaint is made or dismiss the complaint.

(3)[^c18242991] Where a complaint is for an order for the payment of a sum recoverable summarily as a civil debt, or for the variation of the rate of any periodical payments ordered by a magistrates’ court to be made, or for such other matter as may be prescribed, the court may make the order with the consent of the defendant without hearing evidence.
