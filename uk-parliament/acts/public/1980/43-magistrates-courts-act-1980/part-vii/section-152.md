# Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980, section 152

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 152. Saving for juvenile courts.

*Extent: E+W*

The provisions of this Act relating to the constitution, place of sitting and procedure of magistrates’ courts shall, in their application to juvenile courts, have effect subject to any provision contained in rules of court[^c19471281] or any enactment regulating the constitution, place of sitting or procedure of juvenile courts.
