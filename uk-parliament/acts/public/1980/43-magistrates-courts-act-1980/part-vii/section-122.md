# Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980, section 122

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 122. Appearance by counsel or solicitor.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A party to any proceedings before a magistrates’ court may be represented by a legal representative[^c18256811].

(2) Subject to subsection(3) below, an absent party so represented shall be deemed not to be absent.

(3) Appearance of a party by a legal representative shall not satisfy any provision of any enactment or any condition of a recognizance expressly requiring his presence.
