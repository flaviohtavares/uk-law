# Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980, section 155

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1980/43

## 155. Short title, extent and commencement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980.

(2) The following provisions of this Act extend to Scotland—

- (a) sections [^c-9e6393c988fd1f734674cb7b441707a7]..., 12(13)[^c18264401], 83(3), 90 and 91 and this section; and
- (b) section 154 and Schedules 7, 8 and 9 so far as they relate to any enactment extending to Scotland.

(3) The following provisions of this Act extend to Northern Ireland—

- (a) sections 83(3), 90 and 91 and this section; and
- (b) section 154 and Schedules 7, 8 and 9 so far as they relate to an enactment extending to Northern Ireland.

(4) The provisions of section 126 above have the same extent as the section[^c19471431] of the [^c18264411]Indictable Offences Act 1848 to which they refer.

(5)[^c18264421] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) Except as stated in subsections (2) to (5) above, and except so far as relates to the interpretation or commencement of the provisions mentioned in those subsections, this Act extends to England and Wales only.

(7)[^c18264431] This Act shall come into force on such date as the Secretary of State may appoint by order made by statutory instrument.
