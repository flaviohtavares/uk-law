## 143. Power to alter sums specified in certain provisions.[^key-19f31a7dee3573b2dadda8d2ef954c34][^c18261911]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1)[^c18261921] If it appears to the Secretary of State that there has been a change in the value of money since the relevant date, he may by order substitute for the sum or sums for the time being specified in any provision mentioned in subsection (2) below such other sum or sums as appear to him justified by the change.

(2)[^c18261931] The said provisions are—

- (a) section 22 (1) above;

- (aza)[^key-d1cc9f806b0d6fa46823c0d2c1edab58] section 22A(3)(a) above;
- (aa)[^c18261941] section 24(3) and (4) above;
- (b) the definition of “the prescribed sum” in section 32(9) above;
- (c) paragraph (a) of section 33(1) above
- (ca)[^key-4e7f319ec06d61c05fbd34c65a648aaa] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c18262111] In subsection (1) above the “**relevant date**” means—

- (a) the date of the coming into force of section 17 of the Criminal Justice Act 1991 (increase of certain maxima); or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e1189f4faf7092d3bfee59cd650aebae]

- (b) where the sums specified in a provision mentioned in subsection (2) above have been substituted by an order under subsection (1) above [^key-8c561268cc2bf6a35dc68e651d494ed7]..., the date of that order.

(4), (5) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c18262121]

(6) An order under subsection (1) [^c18262131] above—

- (a) shall be made by statutory instrument subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament and may be revoked by a subsequent order thereunder; and
- (b) shall not affect the punishment for an offence committed before that order comes into force.
