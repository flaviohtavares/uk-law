# Statute Law Revision (Substituted Enactments) Act 1876

> This law has been repealed or revoked. Ended on 1993-11-05.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Statute Law Revision (Substituted Enactments) Act 1876 [^c569419](repealed 5.11.1993)

1876 c.20

**No longer in force (ended 1993-11-05).**

## 1. Substitution of Summary Jurisdiction Act for repealed Act 7 & 8 Geo. 4. c. 30. in certain sections of the Inclosure Acts.

. . . [^c569423] Any offence under section ten of the [^c569424]Inclosure Act 1848 and under section ten of the [^c569425]Inclosure Act 1849 and under section thirty-three of the [^c569426]Inclosure Act 1852 shall be deemed to be an offence punishable on summary conviction under the Summary Jurisdiction . . . [^c569427] Acts . . . [^c569427]: Provided . . . [^c569429] that any information in relation to any such offence as is mentioned in this section shall be heard, tried, determined, and adjudged before two justices.
