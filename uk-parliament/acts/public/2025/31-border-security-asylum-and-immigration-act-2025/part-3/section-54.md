# Border Security, Asylum and Immigration Act 2025, section 54

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Confiscation of assets[^c-cef6c53afd99ed1ca09ff8916e131949]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 is amended as follows.

(2) In Schedule 2 (criminal lifestyle offences in England and Wales)—

- (a) before paragraph 9A insert—

  > Offences involving gangmasters

  ;
- (b) after paragraph 9A insert—

  > **9B. Offences relating to things for use in serious crime**
  >
  > An offence under section 52 of the Border Security, Asylum and Immigration Act 2025 (articles for use in serious crime).

(3) In Schedule 4 (criminal lifestyle offences in Scotland), after paragraph 9F insert—

> **9G. Offences relating to things for use in serious crime**
>
> An offence under section 52 of the Border Security, Asylum and Immigration Act 2025 (articles for use in serious crime).

(4) In Schedule 5 (criminal lifestyle offences in Northern Ireland), after paragraph 9A insert—

> **9B. Offences relating to things for use in serious crime**
>
> An offence under section 52 of the Border Security, Asylum and Immigration Act 2025 (articles for use in serious crime).
