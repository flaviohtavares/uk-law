# Renters’ Rights Act 2025, section 98

> Text as at 2026-06-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 98. Rent repayment orders for offences under the [Housing Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/50) and sections 67 and 92 of this Act[^key-043234e85281095949720eddaac47c93]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-6c37efd521a1cb27043021cf36863123] The [Housing and Planning Act 2016](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2016/22) is amended as follows.

(2) In section 40 (introduction and key definitions), in subsection (3)—

- (a)[^key-6c37efd521a1cb27043021cf36863123] at the end of line 2 of the table in that subsection insert—

  > <tr><td>2B</td><td>section 16J(2)</td><td>Breach of restriction on letting or marketing dwelling-house</td></tr>

- (c)[^key-6c37efd521a1cb27043021cf36863123] in line 6 of the table in that subsection, in the third column, for “control or management of unlicensed house” substitute “offences relating to unlicensed houses”;

  > </tbody>
  > </table>

  .

(3)[^key-6c37efd521a1cb27043021cf36863123] In section 41 (application for rent repayment order)—

- (a) in subsection (2)(b), for “12 months” substitute “2 years”;
- (b) after subsection (2) insert—

  > (2A) The requirement in subsection (2)(a) does not apply to an application for a rent repayment order in relation to an offence under section 16J(1) or (2) of the [Housing Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/50).

(4)[^key-6c37efd521a1cb27043021cf36863123] In section 42 (notice of intended proceedings), in subsection (5), for “12 months” substitute “2 years”.

(5) In section 44 (amount of order: tenants)—

- (a)[^key-accd87e9730a7cc2844a50cf67c5f041] in subsection (2), in the first column of the table—
  - (i) in the first row, for “or 2” substitute “, 2, 2A or 9”, and
  - (ii) in the second row, for “3” to “7” substitute “2C, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 or 10”;
- (b)[^key-6c37efd521a1cb27043021cf36863123] in subsection (2), after the first row of the table insert—

  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | an offence mentioned in row 2B of the table in section 40(3) | the period of 2 years ending with the date of the offence or, if the tenancy ends before that date, the date on which it ends |

  ;
- (c)[^key-6c37efd521a1cb27043021cf36863123] in subsection (4)—
  - (i) omit the “and” at the end of paragraph (b),
  - (ii) in paragraph (c), after “of” insert “, or received a financial penalty in respect of,”, and
  - (iii) at the end of paragraph (c) insert

    > , and
    >
    > - (d) whether the landlord has at any time had a rent repayment order made against them.

(6) In section 45 (amount of order: local housing authorities)—

- (a)[^key-accd87e9730a7cc2844a50cf67c5f041] in subsection (2), in the first column of the table —
  - (i) in the first row, for “or 2” substitute “, 2, 2A or 9”, and
  - (ii) in the second row, for “3” to “7” substitute “2C, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 or 10”;
- (b)[^key-6c37efd521a1cb27043021cf36863123] in subsection (2), after the first row of the table insert—

  > |  |  |
  > | --- | --- |
  > | an offence mentioned in row 2B of the table in section 40(3) | the period of 2 years ending with the date of the offence or, if the tenancy ends before that date, the date on which it ends |

  ;
- (c)[^key-6c37efd521a1cb27043021cf36863123] in subsection (4)—
  - (i) omit the “and” at the end of paragraph (b),
  - (ii) in paragraph (c), after “of” insert “, or received a financial penalty in respect of,”, and
  - (iii) at the end of paragraph (c) insert

    > , and
    >
    > - (d) whether the landlord has at any time had a rent repayment order made against them.

(7)[^key-6c37efd521a1cb27043021cf36863123] In section 46 (amount of order following conviction)—

- (a) in subsection (1), for “both” substitute “either”;
- (b) for subsection (3) substitute—

  > (3) Condition 2 is that the order is made against a landlord in relation to an offence (the “relevant offence”) where the landlord has at any time (whether or not in relation to the same tenancy or housing)—
  >
  > - (a) been convicted of another offence which is the same offence as the relevant offence,
  > - (b) received a financial penalty in respect of another offence which is the same offence as the relevant offence, or
  > - (c) had a rent repayment order made against them in respect of another offence which is the same offence as the relevant offence.

  ;
- (c) after subsection (4) insert—

  > (4A) For the purposes of subsection (3), an offence under section 72(1) of the [Housing Act 2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/34) is to be treated as the same offence as an offence under section 95(1) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/34) (and vice versa).
