# Renters’ Rights Act 2025, section 133

> Text as at 2026-06-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 133. Additional powers of seizure under [Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16)[^c-ec60ed882b7ae58fc27c2c5cfe3c8d1b]

*Extent: E+W*

In Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the [Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/16), at the end insert—

> **73W. Renters’ Rights Act 2025**
>
> Each of the powers of seizure conferred by section 122(1)(b) and section 123 of the Renters’ Rights Act 2025.
