# Renters’ Rights Act 2025, section 10

> Text as at 2026-06-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. Repayment of rent paid for days after end of tenancy[^key-ea9cdc869809ee315c7e4e1b0c0fb9c6][^key-b5dedb0c7252277496f8e6a8375bda65]

*Extent: E+W*

In [the 1988 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/50), after section 14ZB (inserted by section 7 of this Act) insert—

> **14ZC. Repayment of rent paid for days after end of tenancy**
>
> (1) A person who paid rent as a tenant under an assured tenancy is entitled to be repaid any part of that rent that relates to days falling after the end of the tenancy.
>
> (2) Subsection (1) does not affect any other entitlement to payment arising at the end of an assured tenancy.
