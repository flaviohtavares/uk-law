# Renters’ Rights Act 2025, section 28

> Text as at 2026-06-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Liability of tenants under assured tenancies for council tax[^c-91a6890958025958c8cdce413f8e7a29][^c-e5adf987dc43331aac5e1c88f878f1af]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 6(6) of the [Local Government Finance Act 1992](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/14), in the definition of “material interest”—

- (a) for “or a” substitute “, a”;
- (b) after “more” insert “or a tenancy that is or was previously an assured tenancy within the meaning of the Housing Act 1988”.
