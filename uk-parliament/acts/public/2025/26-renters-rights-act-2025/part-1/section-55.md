# Renters’ Rights Act 2025, section 55

> Text as at 2026-06-22.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 55. Interpretation of Chapter 5[^c-db9cfffb1eda048e32edd07c905625ce][^c-8361da769a8ba836f0a9725ccdd0a2de]

*Extent: S*

In this Chapter—

- “**benefits claimant**” means a person who—
  - (a) is entitled to payments (including payments made directly to a landlord) under or by virtue of benefits and welfare legislation or would be so entitled were the person to become a tenant under a private residential tenancy, or
  - (b) is entitled, or would (on application or otherwise), if the person were to rent the property, be entitled, by virtue of section 80 of the [Local Government Finance Act 1992](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/14), to a reduction in the amount of council tax payable in respect of the property on the basis of income or an entitlement to a payment mentioned in paragraph (a);
- “**benefits and welfare legislation**” means—
  - (a) the [Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4);
  - (b) the [Jobseekers Act 1995](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1995/18);
  - (c) the [State Pension Credit Act 2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/16);
  - (d) the [Tax Credits Act 2002](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/21);
  - (e) the [Welfare Reform Act 2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/5);
  - (f) the [Welfare Reform Act 2012](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2012/5);
  - (g) the [Pensions Act 2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/19);
  - (h) the [Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (asp 9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2018/9);
- “**child**” means a person under the age of 18;
- “**relevant tenancy**” means—
  - (a) a private residential tenancy under the [Private Housing (Tenancies) (Scotland) Act 2016 (asp 19);](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2016/19)
  - (b) an assured tenancy under the [Housing (Scotland) Act 1988](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/43);
  - (c) a protected or statutory tenancy under the [Rent (Scotland) Act 1984](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1984/58);
- “**tenant**” includes sub-tenant.
