# Renters’ Rights Act 2025, section 23

> Text as at 2026-06-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Assured agricultural occupancies: grounds for possession[^c-d41588995a6508cf7f226bd3d6aaed43][^c-d141afbf36777e779b0cf6329ed3b817]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 25 of [the 1988 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/50) (security of tenure in relation to assured agricultural occupancies)—

- (a) omit subsection (1);
- (b) in subsection (2)—
  - (i) for “Part II” substitute “Part 1”;
  - (ii) for “Ground 16” substitute “Grounds 2ZA to 2ZD, 5A and 5C”.
