# Renters’ Rights Act 2025, section 45

> Text as at 2026-06-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 45. Amendment of short title of [Renting Homes (Fees etc.) (Wales) Act 2019](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2019/2)[^key-4dfdbd7f64e56d9e8e8b3a3579b8bc6f][^key-b1e1725c68320f20a1d09b6dddcc9d01]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Deddf Rhentu Cartrefi (Ffioedd etc.) (Cymru) 2019 may be cited as Deddf Rhentu Cartrefi (Ffioedd, Gwahaniaethu etc.) (Cymru) 2019.

(2) The [Renting Homes (Fees etc.) (Wales) Act 2019 (anaw 2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2019/2) may be cited as the Renting Homes (Fees, Discrimination etc.) (Wales) Act 2019.

(3) In the Welsh language text of the following provisions, for “Deddf Rhentu Cartrefi (Ffioedd etc.) (Cymru) 2019” substitute “Deddf Rhentu Cartrefi (Ffioedd, Gwahaniaethu etc.) (Cymru) 2019”—

- (a) section 41(2A) of the [Housing (Wales) Act 2014 (anaw 7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2014/7);
- (b) in Schedule 9A to the [Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016 (anaw 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/1)—
  - (i) the italic heading before paragraph 5;
  - (ii) paragraph 5(1)(a);
  - (iii) paragraph 5(2)(a);
- (c) in regulation 2 of the [Renting Homes (Rent Determination) (Converted Contracts) (Wales) Regulations 2022](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2022/781) ([S.I. 2022/781 (W. 170)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2022/781)), paragraph (b) of the definition of “rhent”;
- (d) in Schedule 2 to the [Renting Homes (Model Written Statements of Contract) (Wales) Regulations 2022](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2022/28) ([S.I. 2022/28 (W. 13)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2022/28)), in Part 3 of the model written statement, in term 68—
  - (i) the heading;
  - (ii) paragraphs (1)(a) and (2)(a).

(4) In the English language text of the following provisions, for “Renting Homes (Fees etc.) (Wales) Act 2019” substitute “Renting Homes (Fees, Discrimination etc.) (Wales) Act 2019”—

- (a) section 41(2A) of the [Housing (Wales) Act 2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2014/7);
- (b) in Schedule 9A to the [Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/1)—
  - (i) the italic heading before paragraph 5;
  - (ii) paragraph 5(1)(a);
  - (iii) paragraph 5(2)(a);
- (c) in regulation 2 of the [Renting Homes (Rent Determination) (Converted Contracts) (Wales) Regulations 2022](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2022/781), paragraph (b) of the definition of “rent”;
- (d) in Schedule 2 to the [Renting Homes (Model Written Statements of Contract) (Wales) Regulations 2022](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/wsi/2022/28), in Part 3 of the model written statement, in term 68—
  - (i) the heading;
  - (ii) paragraphs (1)(a) and (2)(a).

(5) In section 31 of the [Renting Homes (Fees etc.) (Wales) Act 2019](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2019/2)—

- (a) in the Welsh language text after “Ffioedd” insert “, Gwahaniaethu”;
- (b) in the English language text after “Fees” insert “, Discrimination”.
