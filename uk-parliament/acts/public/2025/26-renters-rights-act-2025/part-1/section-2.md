# Renters’ Rights Act 2025, section 2

> Text as at 2026-06-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Abolition of assured shorthold tenancies[^key-66f2ade4c6ffc3cf7070943311cee70a][^key-f11a4378aa93ebb5b4cd2672d6965a68]

*Extent: E+W*

In [the 1988 Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/50)—

- (a) omit section 6A (demotion to assured shorthold tenancy because of anti-social behaviour);
- (b) omit Chapter 2 of Part 1 (assured shorthold tenancies).
