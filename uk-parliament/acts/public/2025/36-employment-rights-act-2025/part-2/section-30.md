# Employment Rights Act 2025, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2025/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 30. Collective redundancy consultation: protected period[^c-9f8df1f10460fe4f6b76ae5027cbdbd3]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-4ef0a18d796bf38493b09e779e39543c][^c-9d72209332794faed2963459518c0353] Chapter 2 of Part 4 of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 (procedure for handling redundancies) is amended as follows.

(2)[^c-218c21add40762e7c1cbb9236739fcf5] In section 189 (duty to consult representatives: complaint and protective award), in subsection (4), in the words after paragraph (b), for “90” substitute “180”.

(3)[^c-922d78f43656f2c7b5a126153f9b109a] In section 197 (power to vary provisions), in subsection (1)(b), for “periods” substitute “period”.
