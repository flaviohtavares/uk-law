# Employment Rights Act 2025, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2025/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Protection of disclosures relating to sexual harassment[^key-5a4c9589c7bd848b75c3f511c7dadd10][^key-6606f3b42e4a172be0d652a971142745]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) [Part 4A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/18/part/4A) of the [Employment Rights Act 1996](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/18/contents) (protected disclosures) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 43B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/18/section/43B) (disclosures qualifying for protection), in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/18/section/43B), after [paragraph (d)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/18/section/43B) insert—

> - (da) that sexual harassment has occurred, is occurring or is likely to occur,

.

(3) In [section 43L](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/18/section/43L)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/18/section/43L) (other interpretative provisions), after the definition of “the relevant failure” insert—

> - “**sexual harassment**” means harassment of the kind described in [section 26](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/15/section/26)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/15/section/26) of the [Equality Act 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/15/contents) (unwanted conduct of a sexual nature).
