# Employment Rights Act 2025, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2025/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Statutory sick pay in Northern Ireland: removal of waiting period[^c-7a9cfca13640f26429ee23feeaeaf52e][^c-cbd77de2e2bdb48e258587c28ede858f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) [Part 11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7/part/11) of the [Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7/contents) (statutory sick pay) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 147](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7/section/147)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7/section/147) (employer’s liability), for “sections 148 to 150” substitute “sections 149 and 150”.

(3) In [section 148](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7/section/148) (period of incapacity for work)—

- (a) omit [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7/section/148);
- (b) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7/section/148), for the words from “any” to “is” substitute “a period of one day which is, or of two or more consecutive days each of which is,”.

(4) In [section 149](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7/section/149)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7/section/149) (period of entitlement), for “second” substitute “first”.

(5) In [section 150](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7/section/150)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7/section/150) (qualifying days), for “third” substitute “second”.

(6) In [section 151](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7/section/151) (limitations on entitlement), omit [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7/section/151).

(7) In [section 152](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7/section/152)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7/section/152) (notification of incapacity for work), omit [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/7/section/152) (and the “or” at the end of paragraph (a)).
