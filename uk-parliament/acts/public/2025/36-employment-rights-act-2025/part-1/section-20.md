# Employment Rights Act 2025, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2025/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 20. Employers to take all reasonable steps to prevent sexual harassment[^c-4b6bc75516a4ed28e0e4e774ce8e61af][^c-f4939ecfb70aee7705737b418955d9b5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 40A of the Equality Act 2010 (employer duty to prevent sexual harassment of employees), in subsection (1), before “reasonable steps” insert “all”.
