# Employment Rights Act 2025, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2025/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 10. Statutory sick pay in Great Britain: removal of waiting period[^c-51ea481c530e45bc9ed2fe43b9bf7ec7][^c-c41f5ded90c90148188adea92a3dda3f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) [Part 11](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4/part/11) of the [Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4/contents) (statutory sick pay) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 151](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4/section/151)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4/section/151) (employer’s liability), for “sections 152 to 154” substitute “sections 153 and 154”.

(3) In [section 152](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4/section/152) (period of incapacity for work)—

- (a) omit [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4/section/152);
- (b) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4/section/152), for the words from “any” to “is” substitute “a period of one day which is, or of two or more consecutive days each of which is,”.

(4) In [section 153](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4/section/153)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4/section/153) (period of entitlement), for “second” substitute “first”.

(5) In [section 154](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4/section/154)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4/section/154) (qualifying days), for “third” substitute “second”.

(6) In [section 155](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4/section/155) (limitations on entitlement), omit [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4/section/155).

(7) In [section 156](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4/section/156)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4/section/156) (notification of incapacity for work), omit [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/4/section/156) (and the “or” at the end of paragraph (a)).
