## 67. Meaning of “relevant infringement”[^key-b3d0b8bf0759ec9a0d9356d145a3fd76][^key-429abb80985d1a5d56092c64dfec4c35][^key-d109a8e9f44d1e44a816873ac152103e][^key-bf88963c22fd83dad15e5f0003af1630][^key-466393604b83c89ad84d3c8403a979e6][^key-fac38141529f8829f1bf4ee016010b31]
