## 5. Establishment of the IFR[^key-b2f7a4a2968c4db2ed74d3d49fdcd7e4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A body corporate called the Independent Football Regulator is established.

(2) Schedule 2 contains further provision about the IFR.
