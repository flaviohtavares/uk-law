# Public Authorities (Fraud, Error and Recovery) Act 2025, section 81

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 81. Entry, search and seizure in Scotland[^c-07aeb7c6ee6e68f79aa2acec6d4a40b5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Social Security Administration Act 1992 is amended as follows.

(2) After section 109D (inserted by section 80 of this Act) insert—

> **109E. Powers of entry, search and seizure in Scotland**
>
> Schedule 3ZD confers functions that are exercisable only in relation to premises and items in Scotland and that correspond to the functions conferred by section 109D in relation to England and Wales.

(3) After Schedule 3ZC (inserted by section 80(3) of this Act) insert, as Schedule 3ZD, the Schedule in paragraph 2 of Schedule 4 to this Act.
