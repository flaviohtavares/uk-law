# Public Authorities (Fraud, Error and Recovery) Act 2025, section 85

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 85. Amendments to the Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001[^key-db59f838fca2076283e562854f434129]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001 is amended as follows.

(2) In section 57 (retention of seized items), in subsection (1), after paragraph (w) (inserted by the Football Governance Act 2025) insert—

> - (x) paragraph 4(3) of Schedule 3ZD to the Social Security Administration Act 1992.

(3) In section 63 (copies), in subsection (2), at the end insert—

> - (j) paragraph 2(1)(e) and (f) of Schedule 3ZD to the Social Security Administration Act 1992.

(4) In Part 1 of Schedule 1 (powers of seizure to which section 50 of the Act applies), after paragraph 56 insert—

> **56ZA. Social Security Administration Act 1992**
>
> The power of seizure conferred by paragraph 2(1)(d) of Schedule 3ZD to the Social Security Administration Act 1992.
