# Public Authorities (Fraud, Error and Recovery) Act 2025, section 91

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 91. Giving notices etc[^c-96c5da975cbe52700bd14f1ac897b8a1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

In the Social Security Administration Act 1992, in section 121DA (interpretation of Part relating to enforcement), after subsection (7) insert—

> (8) In this Part, references to giving a notice or other document (however expressed) include sending the notice or document by post.
