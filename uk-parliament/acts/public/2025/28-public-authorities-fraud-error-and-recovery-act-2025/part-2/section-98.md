# Public Authorities (Fraud, Error and Recovery) Act 2025, section 98

> Text as at 2026-10-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 98. Rights of audience[^c-f24b6e42ad25abdee9401150c9b5ebfc][^c-ec31f3008df03e4af6c9f6518ed771dd]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In the Social Security Administration Act 1992, after section 80E (inserted by section 97 of this Act) insert—

> **80F. Rights of audience**
>
> (1) An officer of the Secretary of State who is authorised by the Secretary of State for the purposes of this section has a right of audience and the right to conduct litigation in relation to proceedings for or in connection with the recovery of overpayments under a provision of this Part before—
>
> - (a) a magistrates’ court,
> - (b) the county court, and
> - (c) the Crown Court.
>
> (2) In this section, “**right of audience**” and “**right to conduct litigation**” have the same meanings as in section 119 of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990.
