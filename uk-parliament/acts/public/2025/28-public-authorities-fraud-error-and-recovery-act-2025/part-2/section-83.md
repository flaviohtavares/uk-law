# Public Authorities (Fraud, Error and Recovery) Act 2025, section 83

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 83. Offence of delay, obstruction etc[^c-0301cd0b83f5dba6686fbe4c902a5966]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

In the Social Security Administration Act 1992, in section 111 (delay, obstruction etc of inspector)—

- (a) in paragraph (a) of subsection (1), after “officer” insert “, or authorised investigator,”, and
- (b) after subsection (2) insert—

  > (2A) In subsection (1)(a), “**authorised investigator**” means an individual who is authorised by the Secretary of State to exercise the powers conferred by section 109D or Schedule 3ZD.
