# Public Authorities (Fraud, Error and Recovery) Act 2025, section 33

> Text as at 2026-10-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 33. Cessation on death of liable person[^key-eedb50ef4b58404256c1fd97aae140b5][^key-cc818da740791d563a98ac7d22e0f1c4]

*Extent: E+W*

A bank ceases to be subject to a direct deduction order on becoming aware of the liable person’s death.
