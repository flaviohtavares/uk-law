# Public Authorities (Fraud, Error and Recovery) Act 2025, section 15

> Text as at 2026-10-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15. Payable amounts[^key-92839dd1d2d582ab493e74ba2bb82df9][^key-b3af3797a4f655d3845ace3f6a05713a]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part, references to a “payable amount” are to—

- (a) a recoverable amount (see section 1(3)), or
- (b) an amount within section 13(1).
