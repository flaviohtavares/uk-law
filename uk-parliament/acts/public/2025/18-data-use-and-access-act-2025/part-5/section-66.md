# Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, section 66

> Text as at 2026-09-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. The 2018 Act and the UK GDPR[^key-7a3c44b5d590fbda931f1180f0f40054]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- “**the 2018 Act**” means the Data Protection Act 2018;
- “**the UK GDPR**” means [Regulation (EU) 2016/679](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2016/0679) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data.
