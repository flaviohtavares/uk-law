## 109. The PEC Regulations[^c-7108dca8124834995ad99da6e44fc529][^c-eb3cad2e8114fe8c3d034bcf60ca664b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter, “**the PEC Regulations**” means the Privacy and Electronic Communications (EC Directive) Regulations 2003 ([S.I. 2003/2426](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2426)).
