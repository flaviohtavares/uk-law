# Mental Health Act 2025, section 7

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Grounds for discharge by tribunal[^c-93f9fdf9e16b335e64fd818c7aa33966]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The [Mental Health Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 72](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/72) (powers of tribunals)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/72)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/72), for [sub-paragraphs (i)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/72) and [(ii)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/72) substitute “that the grounds in section 2(2) are made out;”;
- (b) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/72)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/72), for [sub-paragraphs (i) to (iia)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/72) (but not the “or” at the end) substitute—

  > - (i) that the conditions in section 20(4) are met;

  ;
- (c) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/72)[(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/72), for [sub-paragraphs (i) to (iv)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/72) (but not the “or” at the end) substitute—

  > - (i) that the criteria in section 17A(5) are met;

  ;
- (d) in [subsection (1A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/72), for “whether the criterion in subsection (1)(c)(iii) above” substitute “for the purposes of subsection (1)(c)(i) whether the criterion in section 17A(5)(e)”.

(3) In [section 73](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/73) (power to discharge restricted patients), in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/73)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/73), for “as to the matters mentioned in paragraph (b)(i), (ii) or (iia) of section 72(1) above” substitute “that the conditions in section 20(4) are met”.

(4) The amendments made by this section—

- (a) so far as relating to persons who are liable to be detained by virtue of [Part 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/part/3) of the [Mental Health Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents), apply in relation to such a person whether the person became so liable before or after the coming into force of this section;
- (b) so far as relating to persons who are subject to community treatment orders (within the meaning given by [section 17A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/17A) of the [Mental Health Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents)) by virtue of [Part 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/part/3) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents), apply in relation to such a person whether the person became subject to a community treatment order before or after the coming into force of this section.
