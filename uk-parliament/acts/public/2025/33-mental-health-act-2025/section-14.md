# Mental Health Act 2025, section 14

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Medicine etc: treatment in other circumstances[^c-61c3fb291feeff905ea5669fbe72ebd7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In [section 58](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58) of the [Mental Health Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents) (treatment requiring consent or a second opinion)—

- (a) in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58), for “three” substitute “two”;
- (b) in [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58), after “has consented to it” insert “, that the treatment constitutes appropriate medical treatment”;
- (c) in [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58), for “(3)(b) above the registered medical practitioner concerned” substitute “(3) the person giving the certificate”.
