# Mental Health Act 2025, section 10

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Nomination of the responsible clinician[^c-8fbf2e359b7a105ce0c115bfd46dd4d4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The [Mental Health Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/34)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/34) (interpretation of Part 2)—

- (a) at the appropriate place insert—

  > - “**the relevant hospital**” means—
  >   - (a) in relation to a patient who is liable to be detained in a hospital, that hospital;
  >   - (b) in relation to a community patient, the responsible hospital;

  ;
- (b) in the definition of “the responsible clinician”, in [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/34), for “with” substitute “nominated by the managers of the relevant hospital to have”.

(3) In the following places, after “would” insert “be nominated by the managers of the hospital to”—

- [section 36](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/36)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/36) (remand for treatment only if admission is arranged);
- [section 37](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/37)[(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/37) (hospital order to be made only if admission is arranged);
- [section 38](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/38)[(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/38) (interim hospital order to be made only if admission is arranged);
- [section 44](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/44)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/44) (evidence for admission to hospital by magistrates’ court);
- [section 45A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/45A)[(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/45A) (hospital direction and limitation direction to be made only if admission is arranged).

(4) In [section 55](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/55)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/55) (interpretation of Part 3), in the definition of “responsible clinician”, for “with” substitute “nominated by the managers of the hospital to have”.

(5) In [section 64](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/64) (supplementary provisions for Part 4), for [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/64) substitute—

> (1) In this Part—
>
> - “**hospital**” includes a registered establishment;
> - “**responsible clinician**” means the responsible clinician within the meaning of Part 2 (see section 34(1)).

(6) In [section 134](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/134)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/134), in the words after [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/134), for “with” substitute “nominated by the managers of the hospital to have”.
