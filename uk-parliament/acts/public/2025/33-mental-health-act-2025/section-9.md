# Mental Health Act 2025, section 9

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Remission or release of prisoners etc from hospital: treatment condition[^c-f16a3edb4c5828927233009371554e78]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In the Mental Health Act 1983, in the following places, for “effective” substitute “appropriate medical”—

-
  - section 50(1) (prisoners under sentence);
  - section 51(3)(b) and (4)(b) (detained persons);
  - section 52(5)(b) (persons remanded by magistrates’ courts);
  - section 53(2)(b) (civil prisoners and persons detained under the Immigration Acts).
