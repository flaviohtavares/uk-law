# Mental Health Act 2025, section 22

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Consultation of the community clinician[^c-8b6e5d6a21705ac6d3e0a9cdf75d895c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The [Mental Health Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 17A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/17A)[(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/17A) (grounds for making community treatment orders)—

- (a) omit the “and” at the end of [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/17A);
- (b) for [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/17A) substitute—

  > - (b) an approved mental health professional states in writing—
  >   - (i) that they agree that the relevant criteria are met; and
  >   - (ii) that it is appropriate to make the order; and
  > - (c) where the responsible clinician is not the community clinician, the community clinician states in writing that they agree that the relevant criteria are met.

(3) In [section 17B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/17B) (conditions of community treatment orders)—

- (a) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/17B), for the words from “approved” to “above” substitute “relevant professionals”;
- (b) after [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/17B) insert—

  > (5A) Where the responsible clinician is not the community clinician, the responsible clinician must consult the community clinician before varying or suspending conditions specified in a community treatment order, unless consultation would involve unreasonable delay.

  ;
- (c) after [subsection (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/17B) insert—

  > (8) In this section “**the relevant professionals**” means—
  >
  > - (a) the approved mental health professional making the statement required by section 17A(4)(b), and
  > - (b) where section 17A(4)(c) applies, the community clinician.

(4) In [section 17E](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/17E) (power to recall a community patient to hospital), after [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/17E) insert—

> (2A) Where the responsible clinician is not the community clinician, the responsible clinician must consult the community clinician before recalling a community patient to hospital, unless consultation would involve unreasonable delay.

(5) In [section 17F](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/17F) (powers in respect of recalled patients), after [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/17F) insert—

> (4A) Where the responsible clinician is not the community clinician, the responsible clinician must consult the community clinician before revoking a community treatment order, unless consultation would involve unreasonable delay.

(6) In [section 20A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/20A) (community treatment period)—

- (a) in [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/20A)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/20A), for “under subsection (8) below is made,” substitute

  > has been made—
  >
  > - (i) under subsection (8), and
  > - (ii) where the responsible clinician is not the community clinician, under subsection (8A),

  ;
- (b) in [subsection (8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/20A), after “(4)” insert “(b)(i)”;
- (c) after [subsection (8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/20A) insert—

  > (8A) The statement referred to in subsection (4)(b)(ii) is a statement in writing by the community clinician that it appears to the community clinician that the conditions set out in subsection (6) are satisfied.

  ;
- (d) omit [subsection (9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/20A).

(7) In [section 34](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/34)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/34) (interpretation of Part 2), at the appropriate place insert—

> - “**the community clinician**” means—
>   - (a) in relation to a patient who is liable to be detained in a hospital in pursuance of an application for admission for treatment, the approved clinician who would oversee the patient’s care if they were to become a community patient;
>   - (b) in relation to a community patient, the approved clinician overseeing the patient’s care as a community patient;

.

(8) In [section 80C](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/80C) (removal of patients subject to compulsion in the community from Scotland)—

- (a) in [subsection (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/80C), for “an approved mental health professional agrees” substitute “the relevant professionals agree”;
- (b) after [subsection (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/80C) insert—

  > (7) In this section “**the relevant professionals**” means—
  >
  > - (a) an approved mental health professional, and
  > - (b) where the responsible clinician is not the community clinician, the community clinician.

(9) In [section 85ZA](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/85ZA) (responsibility for community patients transferred from Channel Islands or Isle of Man)—

- (a) in [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/85ZA), for “an approved mental health professional agrees” substitute “the relevant professionals agree”;
- (b) after [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/85ZA) insert—

  > (6) In this section “**the relevant professionals**” means—
  >
  > - (a) an approved mental health professional, and
  > - (b) where the responsible clinician is not the community clinician, the community clinician.

(10) In [section 92](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/92) (interpretation of Part 6), after [subsection (1A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/92) insert—

> (1B) References in this Part to the community clinician are to be construed as references to the community clinician within the meaning of Part 2.
