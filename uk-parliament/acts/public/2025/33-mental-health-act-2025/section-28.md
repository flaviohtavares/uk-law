# Mental Health Act 2025, section 28

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Transfer of patients: role of nominated person[^c-f51a4cfc7b3170d4ff9ad61c23a7175b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

In section 19 of the Mental Health Act 1983 (transfer of patients), after subsection (3) insert—

> (3A) Before deciding to transfer a patient between hospitals in pursuance of regulations under subsection (1), or in pursuance of subsection (3), the person responsible for taking that decision must consult the patient’s nominated person (if any), unless consultation—
>
> - (a) is not reasonably practicable, or
> - (b) would involve unreasonable delay.
