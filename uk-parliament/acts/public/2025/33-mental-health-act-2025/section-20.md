# Mental Health Act 2025, section 20

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Capacity to consent to treatment[^c-5943341044e3d591c357f5c542350b41]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The [Mental Health Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 57](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/57) (treatment requiring consent and a second opinion), in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/57)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/57), for “is capable of understanding the nature, purpose and likely effects of” substitute “has capacity to consent to”.

(3) In [section 58](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58) (treatment requiring consent or a second opinion), in [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58)—

- (a) in [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58), for “is capable of understanding its nature, purpose and likely effects” substitute “has capacity to consent to it”;
- (b) in [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58), for “is not capable of understanding the nature, purpose and likely effects of” substitute “lacks capacity to consent to”.

(4) In [section 58A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58A) (electro-convulsive therapy etc)—

- (a) in [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58A)[(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58A), for “is capable of understanding the nature, purpose and likely effects of” substitute “has capacity to consent to”;
- (b) in [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58A)[(c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58A), for [sub-paragraph (i)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58A) (but not the “and” at the end) substitute—

  > - (i) that the patient has capacity to consent to the treatment and has consented to it,

  ;
- (c) in [subsection (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58A), for the words from “is not” to the end substitute “lacks capacity to consent to the treatment”;
- (d) omit [subsection (9)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/58A).

(5) In [section 60](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/60) (withdrawal of consent)—

- (a) in [subsection (1A)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/60)[(b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/60), for the words from “be” to the end substitute “have capacity to consent to the treatment”;
- (b) in [subsection (1C)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/60)—
  - (i) in [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/60), for the words from “is not” to “effects of” substitute “lacks capacity to consent to”;
  - (ii) in [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/60), for the words from “becomes” to the end substitute “gains capacity to consent to that treatment”.

(6) In [section 64](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/64) (supplementary provisions for Part 4), after [subsection (1B)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/64) insert—

> (1BA) In relation to a patient who is aged under 16, references in this Part to capacity are to be read as references to competence.
>
> (1BB) In relation to a patient who is aged 16 or over—
>
> - (a) references in this Part to lacking capacity are to lacking capacity within the meaning of the [Mental Capacity Act 2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9/contents), and
> - (b) references in this Part to having, ceasing to have or gaining capacity are to be read accordingly.
>
> (1BC) References in this Part—
>
> - (a) to an advance decision are to an advance decision (within the meaning of the [Mental Capacity Act 2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9/contents)) made by the patient;
> - (b) to a donee are to a donee of a lasting power of attorney (within the meaning of [section 9](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9/section/9) of the [Mental Capacity Act 2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9/contents)) created by the patient, where the donee is acting within the scope of their authority and in accordance with [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9/contents);
> - (c) to a deputy are to a deputy appointed for the patient by the Court of Protection under [section 16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9/section/16) of the [Mental Capacity Act 2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9/contents), where the deputy is acting within the scope of their authority and in accordance with [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9/contents).
>
> (1BD) In this Part “**valid and applicable**”, in relation to an advance decision, means valid and applicable to the treatment in question in accordance with [section 25](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9/section/25) of the [Mental Capacity Act 2005](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/9/contents).
