# Mental Health Act 2025, section 5

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Grounds for detention[^c-0d751566862115be9659eb0a2455ff29]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The [Mental Health Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/2) (admission for assessment), in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/2)—

- (a) omit the “and” at the end of [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/2);
- (b) for [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/2) substitute—

  > - (b) serious harm may be caused to the health or safety of the patient or of another person unless the patient is so detained; and
  > - (c) given the nature, degree and likelihood of the harm, the patient ought to be so detained.

(3) In [section 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/3) (admission for treatment)—

- (a) in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/3), for [paragraphs (c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/3) and [(d)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/3) substitute—

  > - (b) serious harm may be caused to the health or safety of the patient or of another person unless the patient receives medical treatment,
  > - (c) it is necessary, given the nature, degree and likelihood of the harm, for the patient to receive medical treatment,
  > - (d) the necessary treatment cannot be provided unless the patient is detained under this Act, and
  > - (e) appropriate medical treatment is available for the patient.

  ;
- (b) in [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/3)—
  - (i) in [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/3), for “(d)” substitute “(e)”;
  - (ii) in [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/3), for “(c)” substitute “(b) to (d)”.

(4) In [section 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/5)[(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/5) (detention for six hours pending application for admission), for [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/5) (but not the “and” at the end) substitute—

> - (a) that the patient is suffering from mental disorder to such a degree that serious harm may be caused to the health or safety of the patient or of another person unless the patient is immediately restrained from leaving the hospital;

.

(5) In [section 20](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/20) (renewal of authority for detention of patient detained in pursuance of application for admission for treatment etc), in [subsection (4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/20), for [paragraphs (c)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/20) and [(d)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/20) substitute—

> - (b) serious harm may be caused to the health or safety of the patient or of another person unless the patient receives medical treatment,
> - (c) it is necessary, given the nature, degree and likelihood of the harm, for the patient to receive medical treatment,
> - (d) the necessary treatment cannot be provided unless the patient continues to be liable to be detained, and
> - (e) appropriate medical treatment is available for the patient.

(6) The amendment made by subsection (5), so far as relating to persons who are liable to be detained by virtue of [Part 3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/part/3) of the [Mental Health Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents), applies in relation to such a person whether the person became so liable before or after the coming into force of this section.
