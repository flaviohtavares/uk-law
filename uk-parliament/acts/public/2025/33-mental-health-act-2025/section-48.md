# Mental Health Act 2025, section 48

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Removal of police stations and prisons as places of safety[^c-8b93a9d939e75abdf4ce81cb8e4c2f59]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The [Mental Health Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents) is amended as follows.

(2) In [section 55](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/55) (interpretation of Part 3), in [subsection (1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/55), for the definition of “place of safety” substitute—

> - “**place of safety**”—
>   - (a) in relation to a person who is not a child or young person, means any hospital the managers of which are willing temporarily to receive that person;
>   - (b) in relation to a child or young person, has the same meaning as in the Children and Young Persons Act 1933 except that it does not include a police station;

.

(3) The amendment made by subsection (2) does not apply in relation to any directions given under [sections 35](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/35)[(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/35), [36](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/36)[(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/36), [37](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/37)[(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/37), [38](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/38)[(4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/38) or [45A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/45A)[(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/45A) before the coming into force of [that subsection](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/45A).

(4) In [section 135](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/135) (warrant to search for and remove patients)—

- (a) in [subsection (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/135) omit “a police station,”;
- (b) in [subsection (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/135), before [paragraph (a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/135) insert—

  > - (za) a police station may not be regarded as a suitable place;

  ;
- (c) omit [subsection (8)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/135).

(5) In [section 136](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/136) (removal etc of mentally disordered persons without a warrant) omit [subsection (5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/136).

(6) Omit [section 136A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/136A) (use of police stations as places of safety).

(7) In [section 136B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/136B) (extension of detention) omit [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/136B).
