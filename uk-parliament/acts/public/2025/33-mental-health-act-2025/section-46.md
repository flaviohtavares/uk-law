# Mental Health Act 2025, section 46

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. After-care services[^key-889866a3a85b26f6736feb03be5bc9ad]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) [Section 117](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/117) of the [Mental Health Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents) (after-care) is amended as follows.

(2) In [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/117), after “authority”, in the second place it occurs, insert “jointly give notice in writing to the person stating that they”.

(3) After [subsection (3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/117) insert—

> (3A) In applying subsection (3) for the purpose of determining the local social services authority in relation to a person—
>
> - (a) [section 105](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/105)[(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/section/105) of the [Children Act 1989](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/41/contents)—
>   - (i) applies for the purpose of determining the person’s ordinary residence at any time when they were aged under 18, and
>   - (ii) in its application for that purpose, is to be read as if there were inserted, after paragraph (c)—
>
>     > - (d) while the child is being provided with accommodation under [section 117](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/117) of the [Mental Health Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents); or
>     > - (e) while the child is being provided with accommodation under any of the following—
>     >   - the [National Health Service Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/41/contents);
>     >   - the [National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/42/contents);
>     >   - the [National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/29/contents);
>     >   - the [Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1972](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1972/1265/contents) ([S.I. 1972/1265 (N.I. 14)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1972/1265));
>     >   - the [Health and Social Care (Reform) Act (Northern Ireland) 2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nia/2009/1/contents); or
>     > - (f) which is referred to in section 194(6) of the [Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (anaw‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌ 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2014/4/contents).
>
>     ;
> - (b) the following provisions apply for the purpose of determining the person’s ordinary residence at any time when they were aged 18 or over—
>   - (i) [section 39](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/23/section/39)[(1) to (6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/23/section/39) of, and [paragraphs 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/23/schedule/1/paragraph/1)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/23/schedule/1/paragraph/1), [2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/23/schedule/1/paragraph/2)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/23/schedule/1/paragraph/2) and [(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/23/schedule/1/paragraph/2) and [8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/23/schedule/1/paragraph/8) of [Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/23/schedule/1) to, the [Care Act 2014](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2014/23/contents);
>   - (ii) section 194(1) to (3) of the [Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (anaw‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌ 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2014/4/contents).
