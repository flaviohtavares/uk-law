# Mental Health Act 2025, section 38

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Transfer directions for persons detained in youth detention accommodation[^key-838e3cac9b57a873364a91875b44cf17]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In [section 48](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/48) of the [Mental Health Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents) (removal to hospital of other prisoners), in [subsection (2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/48)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/48), for “remand centre” substitute “remanded to youth detention accommodation under section 91 of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012”.

(2) In [Schedule 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/43/schedule/8) to the [Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/43/contents) (repeals), in the table, omit the entry relating to [section 48](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/48)[(2)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/48)[(a)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/section/48) of the [Mental Health Act 1983](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1983/20/contents).
