# Planning and Infrastructure Act 2025, section 112

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (9).** Text as at 2026-11-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2025/34
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 112. Temporary possession of land in connection with compulsory purchase[^key-55f05f3f6329b28553506bcec08c0812]

*Extent: E+W*

In [section 18](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/20/section/18) of the [Neighbourhood Planning Act 2017](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2017/20/contents) (power to take temporary possession of land), for subsection (3) substitute—

> (3) Subject to—
>
> - (a) any express provision in another Act;
> - (b) any provision in an order granting development consent made under [section 114](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/29/section/114) of the [Planning Act 2008](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/29/contents);
> - (c) any provision in an order made under [section 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/42/section/1) or [3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/42/section/3) of the [Transport and Works Act 1992](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/42/contents);
> - (d) any provision in an order granting infrastructure consent made under [section 60](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2024/3/section/60) of the [Infrastructure (Wales) Act 2024 (asc 3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asc/2024/3/contents);
>
> the power in subsection (2) is the only power under which a person may take temporary possession of land compulsorily.
