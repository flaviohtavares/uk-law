## 46. Extension to Scotland of certain amendments[^key-5b62e3e5be8db6c008fb6ac2483ddc9b]

*Extent: S*

The amendments made to the Transport and Works Act 1992 by the following provisions are extended to Scotland—

- (a) Schedule 3 to the Environmental Impact Assessment (Miscellaneous Amendments Relating to Harbours, Highways and Transport) Regulations 2017 ([S.I. 2017/1070](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2017/1070));
- (b) regulation 4(3) and (4) of the Merchant Shipping and Other Transport (Environmental Protection) (Amendment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 ([S.I. 2019/311](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/311)).
