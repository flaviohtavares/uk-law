## 28. Electricity transmission systems: extension of commissioning period[^c-7899730ea60b7547eb0eba8504b30686]

*Extent: E+W*

In [section 6G](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/29/section/6G)[(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/29/section/6G) of the [Electricity Act 1989](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/29/contents) (meaning of “**commissioning period**” for the purposes of determining whether a person is to be regarded as participating in the transmission of electricity), in [paragraph (b)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/29/section/6G), for “18 months” substitute “27 months”.
