# Finance Act 2025, section 17

> Text as at 2026-04-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Extending the period for which levy has effect

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 1 of the [Energy (Oil and Gas) Profits Levy Act 2022](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/40) (charge to tax), in subsection (3) (which sets out the accounting periods by reference to which the tax is charged), in paragraph (b), for “2028” substitute “2030”.

(2) In consequence of the amendment made by subsection (1)—

- (a) in section 7(2) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/40) (when investment expenditure is incurred), for “2028” substitute “2030”, and
- (b) in section 16 of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/40) (transitional provision for accounting periods straddling 31 March 2028), for “2028”, in each place (including the heading), substitute “2030”.
