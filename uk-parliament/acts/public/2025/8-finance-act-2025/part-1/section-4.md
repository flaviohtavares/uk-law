# Finance Act 2025, section 4

> Text as at 2026-04-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Freezing starting rate limit for savings for tax year 2025-26

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the tax year 2025-26 the amount specified in section 12(3) of [ITA 2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/3) (the starting rate limit for savings) is “£5,000”.

(2) Accordingly, section 21 of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/3) (indexation) does not apply in relation to the starting rate limit for savings for the tax year 2025-26.
