# Finance Act 2025, section 62

> Text as at 2026-04-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. National Savings Bank: statements from HMRC no longer to be required

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the National Savings Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/623](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/623)), omit regulation 55 (inheritance tax chargeable on death of depositors).

(2) In the National Savings (No. 2) Regulations 2015 ([S.I. 2015/624](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/624)), omit regulation 90 (inheritance tax chargeable on death of holders of stock or certificates).
