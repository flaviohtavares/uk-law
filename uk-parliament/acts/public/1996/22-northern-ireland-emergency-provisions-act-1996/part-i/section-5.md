# Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1996, section 5

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Maximum period of remand in custody in case of scheduled offences.

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-02-19).**

Notwithstanding Article 47(2) and (3) of the [^c1180666]Magistrates’ Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981, the period for which a person charged with a scheduled offence (or with a scheduled offence and another offence which is not a scheduled offence) may be remanded in custody by a magistrates’ court shall be a period of not more than twenty-eight days beginning with the day following that on which he is so remanded.
