# Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1996, section 31

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Display of support in public for a proscribed organisation.[^c1180730][^c1180731]

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-02-19).**

Any person who in a public place—

- (a) wears any item of dress; or
- (b) wears, carries or displays any article, in such a way or in such circumstances as to arouse reasonable apprehension that he is a member or supporter of a proscribed organisation is guilty of an offence and liable—
  - (i) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year or a fine or both;
  - (ii) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or both.
