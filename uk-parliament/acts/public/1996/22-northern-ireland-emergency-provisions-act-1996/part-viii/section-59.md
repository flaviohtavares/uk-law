# Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1996, section 59

## 59. Repeal of Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991.

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2000-08-25).**

The [^c1180763]Northern Ireland (Emergency Provisions) Act 1991 shall cease to have effect.
