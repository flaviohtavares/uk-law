## (not yet in force: 5B). (not yet in force: Members of employment tribunals: removal from office)[^c19528781]

*Extent: E+W+S*

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Any power by which the President of the Employment Tribunals (England and Wales) may be removed from that office may be exercised only with the concurrence of the Lord Chief Justice of England and Wales.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Any power by which the President of the Employment Tribunals (Scotland) may be removed from that office may be exercised only with the concurrence of the Lord President of the Court of Session.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Any power by which a member of a panel may be removed from membership of the panel—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: may, if the person exercises functions wholly or mainly in Scotland, be exercised only with the concurrence of the Lord President of the Court of Session;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: may, if paragraph (a) does not apply, be exercised only with the concurrence of the Lord Chief Justice of England and Wales.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: In subsection (3) “)****(not yet in force: panel)****(not yet in force: ” means—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: a panel of)* *(not yet in force: Employment Judges[^key-d56c42ffcfe4aa0cf9dc02599e070b3e])* *(not yet in force: , or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: any other panel of members of employment tribunals,)*

*(not yet in force: which is appointed in accordance with regulations made under section 1(1).)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: The Lord Chief Justice of England and Wales may nominate a judicial office holder (as defined in section 109(4) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005) to exercise his functions under this section.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: The Lord President of the Court of Session may nominate a judge of the Court of Session who is a member of the First or Second Division of the Inner House of that Court to exercise his functions under this section.)*
