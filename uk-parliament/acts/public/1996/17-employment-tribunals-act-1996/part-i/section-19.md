## 19. Conciliation procedure.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c19006301] Procedure Rules must[^key-1ef860287dae3f6103dfb9a21f51ae56] include in relation to employment tribunal[^c19006311] proceedings in the case of which any enactment makes provision for conciliation—

- (a) provisions requiring a copy of the application by which the proceedings are instituted, and a copy of any notice relating to it which is lodged by or on behalf of the person against whom the proceedings are brought, to be sent to a conciliation officer, and[^c19524161]
- (b) provisions securing that the applicant and the person against whom the proceedings are brought are notified that the services of a conciliation officer are available to them, [^c19006381]. . .
