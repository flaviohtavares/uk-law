## 7. Practice and procedure: general[^c19002301][^c-b6d7b46d1c0e890b3130266a2b5e73bb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Procedure Rules (see section 37QA) are to govern the practice and procedure to be followed in employment tribunals.
