# Employment Tribunals Act 1996, section 7A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (29).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/17

## 7A. Practice directions[^c19002581]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(A1)[^c-eb1ef70fb270ce77b26618f8a0884007] The Senior President of Tribunals may make directions about the practice and[^c-61bdd7594efff6fb915532998e5d1b94] procedure of employment tribunals.

(1)[^c-210af843f34242b144b7d1f789444935] The territorial President may make directions about the practice and procedure of employment tribunals.

(2)[^c-40ae8ce1100954920585ad925187c9c1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A)[^c-62bc8a077e225b06ba2c41dd00f250c5] The powers under subsections (A1) and (1) include[^c-62cfcdfb44a2b508c6b27b8d4ec0df48]—

- (a) power to vary or revoke directions made in exercise of the power, and
- (b) power to make different provision for different purposes (including different provision for different areas).

(2B) Directions under subsection (A1) may not be made without the approval of the Lord Chancellor.

(2C) Directions under subsection (1)[^c-fdd416293fb5d7c364ec6246f3cdb841] may not be made without the approval of—

- (a) the Senior President of Tribunals, and
- (b) the Lord Chancellor.

(2D) Subsections (2B) and (2C)(b) do not apply to directions to the extent that they consist of guidance about any of the following—

- (a) the application or interpretation of the law;
- (b) the making of decisions by members of an employment tribunal.

(2E) Subsections (2B) and (2C)(b) do not apply to directions to the extent that they consist of criteria for determining which members of employment tribunals may be selected to decide particular categories of matter; but the directions may, to that extent, be made only after consulting the Lord Chancellor.

(3) In this section, references to the territorial[^c-77d3d40f70963801d0dac8facfe55185] President are to a person appointed in accordance with regulations under section 1(1) as—

- (a) President of the Employment Tribunals (England and Wales), or
- (b) President of the Employment Tribunals (Scotland).
