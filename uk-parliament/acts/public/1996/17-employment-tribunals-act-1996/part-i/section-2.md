# Employment Tribunals Act 1996, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (29).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 2. Enactments conferring jurisdiction on employment tribunals.[^key-cd3f3d301ab67d5ee4dfbd227d8d362e][^c19000871]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Employment tribunals shall exercise the jurisdiction conferred on them by or by virtue of this Act or any other Act, whether passed before or after this Act.
