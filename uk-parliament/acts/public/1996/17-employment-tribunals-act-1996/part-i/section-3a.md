# Employment Tribunals Act 1996, section 3A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (29).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 3A. Meaning of “Employment Judge”[^c19523861]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A person who is a member of a panel of Employment Judges[^c-42bbf05493bcb530df4bba9790c4f530] which is appointed in accordance with regulations under section 1(1) may be referred to as an Employment Judge.
