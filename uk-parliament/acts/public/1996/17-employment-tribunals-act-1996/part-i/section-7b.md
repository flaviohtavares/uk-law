# Employment Tribunals Act 1996, section 7B

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## (not yet in force: 7B). (not yet in force: Mediation)[^c19528931]

*Extent: E+W+S*

((not yet in force: A1))[^c-ad82a51bdb413126e7a9fbe658166b8a] *(not yet in force: A person exercising power to make Procedure Rules or give practice directions must, when making provision in relation to mediation, have regard to the following principles—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: mediation of matters in dispute between parties to proceedings is to take place only by agreement between those parties;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: where parties to proceedings fail to mediate, or where mediation between parties to proceedings fails to resolve disputed matters, the failure is not to affect the outcome of the proceedings.)*

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Practice directions under section 7A may[^c-87072f32729fc1d03e2c34e9a32849b7])* *(not yet in force: provide for members to act as mediators in relation to disputed matters in a case that is the subject of proceedings.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The provision that may be)* *(not yet in force: made[^c-fe417fd9687f8a9ab6f358470070c678])* *(not yet in force: by virtue of subsection (1) includes provision for)* [^c-a7c6375fb736b051be560dec416e54ad]*(not yet in force: ... a member to act as mediator in relation to disputed matters in a case even though the member has been selected to decide matters in the case.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Once a member has begun to act as mediator in relation to a disputed matter in a case that is the subject of proceedings, the member may decide matters in the case only with the consent of the parties.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Staff appointed under section 40(1) of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 (staff for employment and other tribunals) may, subject to their terms of appointment, act as mediators in relation to disputed matters in a case that is the subject of proceedings.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Before making a practice direction that makes provision in relation to mediation, the person making the direction must consult)* *(not yet in force: ACAS[^c-0f57e909d4277728de35e39081e03b23])* *(not yet in force: .)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: In this section—)*

- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: member)****(not yet in force: ” means a member of a panel of members of employment tribunals (whether or not a panel of)* *(not yet in force: Employment Judges[^c-c8cc08f42158a4a536405c895502d07f])* *(not yet in force: );)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: practice direction)****(not yet in force: ” means a direction under section 7A;)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: proceedings)****(not yet in force: ” means proceedings before an employment tribunal.)*
