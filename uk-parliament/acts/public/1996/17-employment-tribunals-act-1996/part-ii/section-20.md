# Employment Tribunals Act 1996, section 20

## 20. The Appeal Tribunal.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Employment Appeal Tribunal (“the Appeal Tribunal") shall continue in existence.

(2) The Appeal Tribunal shall have a central office in London but may sit at any time and in any place in Great Britain.

(3) The Appeal Tribunal shall be a superior court of record and shall have an official seal which shall be judicially noticed.

(4)[^c19006401] Subsection (2) is subject to regulation 34 of the Transnational Information and Consultation of Employees Regulations 1999,[^c19516761] regulation 46(1) of the European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations[^c19516491] 2004,[^c19516841] regulation 36(1) of the Information and Consultation of Employees Regulations[^c19516801] 2004,[^c19516881] regulation 37(1) of the European Cooperative Society (Involvement of Employees) Regulations[^M_F_de7e79f0-a470-43ae-83de-82c7dd718834] 2006,[^c-6efdbc00bec137b57401067bb81bf11d] regulation 58(1) of the Companies (Cross-Border Mergers) Regulations 2007[^M_F_c0eec901-7aa3-4614-bbdd-f350859497af] and regulation 33(1) of the European Public Limited-Liability Company (Employee Involvement) (Great Britain) Regulations 2009([^c-771cb2fd1f64ccc1c07786214ab0d5fc][S.I. 2009/2401](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2401)).
