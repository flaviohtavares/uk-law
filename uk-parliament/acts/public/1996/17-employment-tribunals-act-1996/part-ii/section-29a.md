## (not yet in force: 29A). (not yet in force: Practice directions)[^c19531491]

*Extent: E+W+S*

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Directions about the)* *(not yet in force: practice and[^key-075cff9c81ccd037cb8f7294689dbb69])* *(not yet in force: procedure of the Appeal Tribunal may be given—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: by the Senior President of Tribunals, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: by the President of the Appeal Tribunal.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: A power under subsection (1) includes—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: power to vary or revoke directions given in exercise of the power, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: power to make different provision for different purposes.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Directions under subsection (1)(a) may not be given without the approval of the Lord Chancellor.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Directions under subsection (1)(b) may not be given without the approval of—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the Senior President of Tribunals, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the Lord Chancellor.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Subsection (1) does not prejudice any power apart from that subsection to give directions about the)* *(not yet in force: practice or[^key-2510eff2ccd75c95c6b4f0d938e7c55a])* *(not yet in force: procedure of the Appeal Tribunal.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: Directions may not be given in exercise of any such power as is mentioned in subsection (5) without the approval of—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the Senior President of Tribunals, and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the Lord Chancellor.)*

((not yet in force: 7)) *(not yet in force: Subsections (3), (4)(b) and (6)(b) do not apply to directions to the extent that they consist of guidance about any of the following—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the application or interpretation of the law;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the making of decisions by members of the Appeal Tribunal.)*

((not yet in force: 8)) *(not yet in force: Subsections (3), (4)(b) and (6)(b) do not apply to directions to the extent that they consist of criteria for determining which members of the Appeal Tribunal may be chosen to decide particular categories of matter; but the directions may, to that extent, be given only after consulting the Lord Chancellor.)*

((not yet in force: 9)) *(not yet in force: Subsections (4) and (6) do not apply to directions given in a particular case for the purposes of that case only.)*

((not yet in force: 10)) *(not yet in force: Subsection (6) does not apply to directions under section 28(1).)*
