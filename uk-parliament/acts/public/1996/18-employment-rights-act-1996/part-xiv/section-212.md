## 212. Weeks counting in computing period.[^c-021857c09bfca348de00157312468209][^c-1ea05877e335e92192e96a8d5eb26d2f][^c-99307445b30dd5592a3e60b5d8ffa0d1][^c-e8b4503cc39f98c52d86cf6f8d86e1a7][^c-8765a22d85ae2b200c6f288d49c338d2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any week during the whole or part of which an employee’s relations with his employer are governed by a contract of employment counts in computing the employee’s period of employment.

(2)[^c16336461] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Subject to subsection (4), any week (not within subsection (1)) during the whole or part of which an employee is—

- (a) incapable of work in consequence of sickness or injury,
- (b) absent from work on account of a temporary cessation of work, or[^c16336471]
- (c) absent from work in circumstances such that, by arrangement or custom, he is regarded as continuing in the employment of his employer for any purpose, [^c16336481]. . .
- (d)[^c16336461] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

counts in computing the employee’s period of employment.

(4) Not more than twenty-six weeks count under subsection (3)(a) [^c16336481]. . . between any periods falling under subsection (1).
