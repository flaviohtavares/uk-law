# Employment Rights Act 1996, section 231

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (150).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/18

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 231. Associated employers.[^c16985741]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of this Act any two employers shall be treated as associated if—

- (a) one is a company of which the other (directly or indirectly) has control, or
- (b) both are companies of which a third person (directly or indirectly) has control;

and “**associated employer**” shall be construed accordingly.
