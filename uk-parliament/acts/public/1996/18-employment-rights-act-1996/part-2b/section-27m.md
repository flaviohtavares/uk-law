# Employment Rights Act 1996, section 27M

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (150).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 27M. Compensation: dealing with tips etc[^c-5a6fbdeae32e6954ef0f0f9eacf5c885]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) If an employment tribunal makes a declaration under section 27L(1)(a), it may order the employer or agent (as the case may be) to pay to the complainant such amount, not exceeding £5,366[^c-93436e1544a9b479e8a58fce1449ef0b], as the tribunal considers appropriate in all the circumstances to compensate the complainant for any financial loss sustained by the complainant which is attributable to the matter complained of.

(2) The tribunal may make an order under this section whether or not an order under section 27L(1)(b) has been made.
