## 3. Note about disciplinary procedures and pensions.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A statement under section 1 shall include a note—

- (a) specifying any disciplinary rules applicable to the worker[^c-0b268e3a53114ce700ffa2eff2a547cc] or referring the worker to the provisions of a document specifying such rules which is reasonably accessible to the worker,
- (aa)[^c19978471] specifying any procedure applicable to the taking of disciplinary decisions relating to the worker[^c-e702f5b991d70fc479e0f167d7188e3a], or to a decision to dismiss the worker, or referring the worker to the provisions of a document specifying such a procedure which is reasonably accessible to the worker,
- (b) specifying (by description or otherwise)—
  - (i) a person to whom the worker[^c-855dd2b25d96bfe1cf08f045eaa5f650] can apply if dissatisfied with any disciplinary decision relating to him or any decision to dismiss him[^c19978551], and
  - (ii) a person to whom the worker[^c-8e0f1e3e8a4c3f867b0ae25a47f9d58c] can apply for the purpose of seeking redress of any grievance relating to his employment,

  and the manner in which any such application should be made, and
- (c) where there are further steps consequent on any such application, explaining those steps or referring to the provisions of a document explaining them which is reasonably accessible to the worker[^c-219956382686ca71f29530ad0adf644d].

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to rules, disciplinary decisions, decisions to dismiss[^c19978571] grievances or procedures relating to health or safety at work.
