# Employment Rights Act 1996, section 7B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (150).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7B. Giving of alternative documents before start of employment[^c19978761]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A document in the form of a contract of employment or other worker’s contract[^key-c891094788495f4a7f0e837460fc8b3b] or letter of engagement given by an employer to a worker[^key-0916a1bf657389f285f7bc28f3160108] before the beginning of the worker’s[^key-ae3985dbc9f6c486846a329e1df7cfa8] employment with the employer shall, when the employment begins, be treated for the purposes of section 7A as having been given at that time.
