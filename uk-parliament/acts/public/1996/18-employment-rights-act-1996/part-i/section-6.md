# Employment Rights Act 1996, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (150).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Reasonably accessible document or collective agreement.

*Extent: E+W+S*

In sections 2 to 4 references to a document or collective agreement which is reasonably accessible to a worker[^key-c9b790581fbba9cc6d795f74a8093167] are references to a document or collective agreement which—

- (a) the worker[^key-e812edefb173b7e34901a20983dda7f0] has reasonable opportunities of reading in the course of his employment, or
- (b) is made reasonably accessible to the worker[^key-99d99342eafbf131f430dcb04a87fa07] in some other way.
