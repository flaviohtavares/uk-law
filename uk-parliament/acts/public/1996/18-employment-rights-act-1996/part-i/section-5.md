# Employment Rights Act 1996, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (150).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Exclusion from rights to statements.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Sections 1 to 4 apply to a worker[^c-690e7121f1d62a370bd8b9c027cebee2] who at any time comes or ceases to come within the exceptions from those sections provided by section[^c16324591] 199, and under section 209, as if his employment with his employer terminated or began at that time.

(2) The fact that section 1 is directed by subsection (1) to apply to a worker[^c-c24ff49f57a0ee51a6c1401effe93bcb] as if his employment began on his ceasing to come within the exceptions referred to in that subsection does not affect the obligation under section 1(3)(b) to specify the date on which his employment actually began.
