## 186. Limit on amount payable under section 182.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The total amount payable to an employee in respect of any debt to which this Part applies, where the amount of the debt is referable to a period of time, shall not exceed—

- (a)[^c16334061] £751[^c-dee024eb453a617d748ec7b21383f409] in respect of any one week, or
- (b)[^c16334061] in respect of a shorter period, an amount bearing the same proportion to £751[^c-d4f0f1ae2afd8bc2d8708772079caab3] as that shorter period bears to a week.
