# Employment Rights Act 1996, section 43K

## 43K. Extension of meaning of “worker” etc. for Part IVA.[^c16325111]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) For the purposes of this Part “ **worker** ” includes an individual who is not a worker as defined by section 230(3) but who—

- (a) works or worked for a person in circumstances in which—
  - (i) he is or was introduced or supplied to do that work by a third person, and
  - (ii) the terms on which he is or was engaged to do the work are or were in practice substantially determined not by him but by the person for whom he works or worked, by the third person or by both of them,
- (b) contracts or contracted with a person, for the purposes of that person’s business, for the execution of work to be done in a place not under the control or management of that person and would fall within section 230(3)(b) if for “personally” in that provision there were substituted “(whether personally or otherwise)”,
- (ba)[^c20058171][^c20058191][^c20055011] works or worked as a person performing services under a contract entered into by him with NHS England[^c-0a3f2af7f0c680dfe9a5b1e4ea198777] under[^c20096711] section 83(2), 84, 92, 100, 107, 115(4), 117 or 134 of, or Schedule 12 to,[^c-7b3cb6d587ff4d89fe89460c3d97aafb] the National Health Service Act 2006 or with a Local Health Board under section 41(2)(b), 42, 50, 57, 64 or 92 of, or Schedule 7 to,[^c-5f3ae41a5cfd53dd968b1c69de39c943] the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006,
- (bb)[^c20058231][^c20058211] works or worked as a person performing services under a contract entered into by him with a Health Board under section 17J or 17Q[^c-d8eef084fbd22406cf99e7bce8ffc114] of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978,
- (c) works or worked as a person providing services[^c-487b5a2d4145aa12c0de6548509c3c42] in accordance with arrangements made—
  - (i) by NHS England[^c16512781] under section 126 of the National Health Service Act 2006,[^c20096771] or Local Health Board[^c20368641] under section 71 or 80 of the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^c20096811], or
  - (ii)[^c-3d28e8bc9d5e54336f5dfbb47904ca2b] by a Health Board under section 2C, 17AA, 17C,[^c-ecd6e432489b7304abc7c6a385d4673d] [^c20058291] . . . 25, 26 or 27 of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, or
- (cb)[^c-361e1ad1288fecf2cd84e3fb4d6db6f9] is or was provided with work experience provided pursuant to a course of education or training approved by, or under arrangements with, the Nursing and Midwifery Council in accordance with article 15(6)(a) of the Nursing and Midwifery Order 2001 ([S.I. 2002/253](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2002/253)), or
- (d) is or was provided with work experience provided pursuant to a training course or programme or with training for employment (or with both) otherwise than—
  - (i) under a contract of employment, or
  - (ii) by an educational establishment on a course run by that establishment;

  and any reference to a worker’s contract, to employment or to a worker being “ **employed** ” shall be construed accordingly.

(2) For the purposes of this Part “ **employer** ” includes—

- (a) in relation to a worker falling within paragraph (a) of subsection (1), the person who substantially determines or determined the terms on which he is or was engaged,
- (aa)[^c20056951] in relation to a worker falling within paragraph (ba) of that subsection, NHS England[^c-1420353db3bfaa0a0a3cb1c0e6308034], or the Local Health Board referred to in that paragraph,
- (ab)[^c20058251] in relation to a worker falling within paragraph (bb) of that subsection, the Health Board referred to in that paragraph,
- (b) in relation to a worker falling within paragraph (c) of that subsection, NHS England or the board[^c-09168e7742d157e71e4e654cd0ec4571] referred to in that paragraph, and
- (c) in relation to a worker falling within paragraph (cb) or[^c-05dd7b56be4dcb3503dc63d3331b52f3] (d) of that subsection, the person providing the work experience or training.

(3) In this section “ **educational establishment** ” includes any university, college, school or other educational establishment.

(4)[^c-878e6d7ab8906942f271e8e503b9bc79] The Secretary of State may by order make amendments to this section as to what individuals count as “ **workers** ” for the purposes of this Part (despite not being within the definition in section 230(3)).

(5)[^c-878e6d7ab8906942f271e8e503b9bc79] An order under subsection (4) may not make an amendment that has the effect of removing a category of individual unless the Secretary of State is satisfied that there are no longer any individuals in that category.
