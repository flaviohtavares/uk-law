# Employment Rights Act 1996, section 205

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (150).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 205. Remedy for infringement of certain rights.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c20111821] The remedy of an employee for infringement of any of the rights conferred by section 8, Part III, Parts V to VIII, Part 8B,[^key-5ee5640c653c83a3370e5e1d55bf457e] section 92, Part X and Part XII is, where provision is made for a complaint or the reference of a question to an employment tribunal[^c16336281], by way of such a complaint or reference and not otherwise.

(1YA)[^key-badd56e52516f4e37004582844742733] In relation to the right conferred by section 44(1A), the reference in subsection (1) to an employee has effect as a reference to a worker.

(1A)[^c16336291] In relation to the right conferred by section 47B, the reference in subsection (1) to an employee has effect as a reference to a worker.

(1ZA)[^c16336301] In relation to the right conferred by section 45A, the reference in subsection (1) to an employee has effect as a reference to a worker.

(2) The remedy of a worker in respect of any contravention of section 13, 15, 18(1) or 21(1) is by way of a complaint under section 23 and not otherwise.
