# Employment Rights Act 1996, section 126

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (150).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/18

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 126. Acts which are both unfair dismissal and discrimination.[^c16332561]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This section applies where compensation falls to be awarded in respect of any act both under—

- (a) the provisions of this Act relating to unfair dismissal, and
- (b)[^c20892061] the Equality Act 2010.

(2) An employment tribunal[^c16332601] shall not award compensation under either of those Acts[^c20892081] in respect of any loss or other matter which is or has been taken into account under the other[^c20892101] by the tribunal (or another employment tribunal) in awarding compensation on the same or another complaint in respect of that act.
