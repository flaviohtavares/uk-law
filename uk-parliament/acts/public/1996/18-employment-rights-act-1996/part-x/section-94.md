# Employment Rights Act 1996, section 94

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (150).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 94. The right.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An employee has the right not to be unfairly dismissed by his employer.

(2) Subsection (1) has effect subject to the following provisions of this Part (in particular sections 108 to 110) and to the provisions of the [^c16329061]Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 (in particular sections 237 to 239).
