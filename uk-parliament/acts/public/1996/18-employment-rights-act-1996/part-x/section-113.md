# Employment Rights Act 1996, section 113

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (150).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 113. The orders.[^c16331171]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An order under this section may be—

- (a) an order for reinstatement (in accordance with section 114), or
- (b) an order for re-engagement (in accordance with section 115),

as the tribunal may decide.
