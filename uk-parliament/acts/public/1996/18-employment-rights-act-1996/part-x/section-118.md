## 118. General.[^c16331911]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) [^c16331921]. . .Where a tribunal makes an award of compensation for unfair dismissal under section 112(4) or 117(3)(a) the award shall consist of—

- (a) a basic award (calculated in accordance with sections 119 to 122 and 126), and
- (b) a compensatory award (calculated in accordance with sections 123, 124, 124A and 126[^c20013761]).
