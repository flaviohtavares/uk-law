# Employment Rights Act 1996, section 104

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (150).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 104. Assertion of statutory right.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An employee who is dismissed shall be regarded for the purposes of this Part as unfairly dismissed if the reason (or, if more than one, the principal reason) for the dismissal is that the employee—

- (a) brought proceedings against the employer to enforce a right of his which is a relevant statutory right, or
- (b) alleged that the employer had infringed a right of his which is a relevant statutory right.

(2) It is immaterial for the purposes of subsection (1)—

- (a) whether or not the employee has the right, or
- (b) whether or not the right has been infringed;

but, for that subsection to apply, the claim to the right and that it has been infringed must be made in good faith.

(3) It is sufficient for subsection (1) to apply that the employee, without specifying the right, made it reasonably clear to the employer what the right claimed to have been infringed was.

(4) The following are relevant statutory rights for the purposes of this section—

- (a) any right conferred by this Act for which the remedy for its infringement is by way of a complaint or reference to an employment tribunal[^c20260711],
- (b) the right conferred by section 86 of this Act, [^c20260721]. . .
- (c) the rights conferred by sections 68, 86, 145A, 145B,[^c20260731] 146, 168, 168A,[^c20260741] 168B,[^c-fafe71f73b332f3758b7dd667bde8cb3] 169 , 170 and 236A[^c-b718da4b88ba71f1d505580d24f2e607] of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 (deductions from pay, union activities and time off) [^c20270781]...
- (d)[^c20260771] the rights conferred by the Working Time Regulations 1998, the Merchant Shipping (Maritime Labour Convention) (Hours of Work) Regulations 2018 ([^c-85d91da85699d543d68ac6cfce31d37c][S.I. 2018/58](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/58)), the Merchant Shipping (Working Time: Inland Waterway) Regulations 2003 , the Fishing Vessels (Working Time: Sea-fisherman) Regulations 2004 or the Cross-border Railway Services (Working Time) Regulations 2008[^c20372211]. , and[^c20270791]
- (e) the rights conferred by the Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) Regulations 2006.

(5)[^c20260781] In this section any reference to an employer includes, where the right in question is conferred by section 63A, the principal (within the meaning of section 63A(3)).
