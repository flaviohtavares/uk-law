# Employment Rights Act 1996, section 124A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (150).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 124A. Adjustments under the Employment Act 2002[^c20017131]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where an award of compensation for unfair dismissal falls to be—

- (a) reduced or increased under section 207A of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 (effect of failure to comply with Code: adjustment of awards)[^c20371721], or
- (b) increased under section 38 of that Act (failure to give statement of employment particulars),

the adjustment shall be in the amount awarded under section 118(1)(b) and shall be applied immediately before any reduction under section 123(6) or (7).
