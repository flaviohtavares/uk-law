# Broadcasting Act 1996, section 137

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 137. Avoidance of certain terms relating to use for purpose of news reporting of visual images from broadcast or cable programme.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any provision in an agreement is void in so far as it purports to prohibit or restrict relevant dealing with a broadcast [^c-3dbac74eb0c38cf0958094547263b91f]... in any circumstances where by virtue of section 30(2) of the [^c11523141]Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (fair dealing for the purpose of reporting current events) copyright in the broadcast [^c-3dbac74eb0c38cf0958094547263b91f]... is not infringed.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a)[^c-83c27e3a29f42598c66641c87abc9e9b] “relevant dealing”, in relation to a broadcast, means dealing by communicating to the public any visual images taken from that broadcast, and
- (b) “**broadcast**” and “ **communicating to the public[^c-02bb3498c0da211191c0124d8d60cdb0]** ” have the same meaning as in Part I of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988.
