## 61. Duration and conditions of digital sound programme licence.[^key-6784f938de3b26fd1e4f0a168e6d608e][^key-c38b90ba4e151e8ed04fdde574f976b0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Part and to section 111 of the 1990 Act as applied by section 62(10), a digital sound programme licence shall continue in force until it is surrendered by its holder.

(2) A digital sound programme licence shall include such conditions as appear to OFCOM[^key-83f2a1b8483e1ca20d9c2c018d314235] to be appropriate for requiring the holder of the licence—

- (a) on entering into any agreement with the provider of a radio multiplex service, of a television multiplex service or of a general multiplex service[^key-df01568bbb722641c762449c2d2bc1c9] for the provision of a digital sound programme service to be broadcast by means of the multiplex service[^key-898835b578932aaa9f170b35f3e11b9d] , to notify OFCOM —
  - (i) of the identity of the multiplex service[^key-ac0c3fcd4bd44fd8712a459c9b0206f3] ,
  - (ii) of the characteristics of the digital sound programme service to which the agreement relates, and
  - (iii) of the period during which it will be provided,
- (b) when any such agreement is varied so far as it relates to any of the matters mentioned in paragraph (a)(i), (ii) or (iii), to notify OFCOM of the variation so far as relating to those matters, and
- (c) where he is providing a digital sound programme service to the provider of a radio multiplex service, of a television multiplex service or of a general multiplex service[^key-72459bed5693e795635e7802c4da1866] in accordance with such an agreement as is mentioned in paragraph (a) but intends to cease doing so, to notify OFCOM of that fact.
