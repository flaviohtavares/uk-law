# Broadcasting Act 1996, section 63

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Digital additional services.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part “**digital additional service**” means any service which—

- (a)[^c-0e427ed65cbe95bc9eb1c4e54e81abf3] is provided by a person with a view to its being broadcast in digital form (whether by him or some other person) so as to be available for reception by members of the public;
- (aa) is so provided with a view to the broadcasting being by means of a radio multiplex service or by means of a general multiplex service; and
- (b) is not a digital sound programme service, a simulcast radio service, a television licensable content service, an ancillary service, a relevant ancillary service within the meaning of section 232 of the Communications Act 2003[^c-0b1b424fe77eb26955a06514682372f1] or a technical service.

(2) In this Part “**ancillary service**” (except in the expression “relevant ancillary service”)[^c-0e7670f7b7fa027cad20fb5f693c1227] means any service which is provided by the holder of a digital sound programme licence or by an independent national broadcaster and consists in the provision of any service (other than advertising) which—

- (a) is ancillary to programmes included in a digital sound programme service or simulcast radio service provided by him and is directly related to their contents, or
- (b) relates to the promotion or listing of such programmes.

(3) In this Part “**technical service**” means a service which—

- (a) is provided for technical purposes connected with the encryption or decryption of one or more digital sound programme services or digital additional services , television licensable content services[^c-1d1f8c6cd40bf4a8975e9e24d9759245] , and
- (b) is of a description specified in an order made by the Secretary of State.

(3A)[^c-f4f637fe4020280292309d71d93cc2c6] In this section “**available for reception by members of the public**” shall be construed in accordance with section 361 of the Communications Act 2003.

(4) An order under subsection (3) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
