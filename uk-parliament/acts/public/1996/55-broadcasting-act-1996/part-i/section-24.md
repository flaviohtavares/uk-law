# Broadcasting Act 1996, section 24

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Digital additional services.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part “**digital additional service**” means any service (or dissociable section of a service)[^c-fae05df954551df2963a809d748223b8] which—

- (a)[^c-8afeda547e16520235fca48e8aaf6d43] is provided by a person with a view to its being broadcast in digital form (whether by him or some other person) so as to be available for reception by members of the public;
- (b) is so provided with a view either—
  - (i) to the broadcasting being by means of a television multiplex service or by means of a general multiplex service; or
  - (ii) to the members of the public in question being or including members of the public in a CTT State[^c-c69ce0a6e20be0ef034d127cde4b3eca] other than the United Kingdom, or in an area of such a State;

  and
- (c) is not a Channel 3 service, Channel 4, Channel 5, a public television service of S4C[^c-4234bec910243fa44a416251d39a5665], [^c-f4b96bd8bb59150de92e158b0210e150]... a digital programme service, a digital sound programme service, an ancillary service or a technical service.

(2) In this Part “**ancillary service**” means any service which is provided by the holder of a digital programme licence or by an a relevant public service broadcaster[^c-69bda59863dd23bb52f693869ca03d3c] and consists in the provision of—

- (a)[^c-66383c46fc0e92a876d53aa7a6f0caaf] assistance for disabled people in relation to some or all of the programmes included in a digital programme service or qualifying service provided by him;
- (b) a service (apart from advertising) that relates to the promotion or listing of programmes included in such a service or in a digital sound programme service so provided; or
- (c) any other service (apart from advertising) that is ancillary to one or more programmes so included, and relates directly to their contents.

(3) In this Part “**technical service**” means a service which—

- (a) is provided for technical purposes connected with the encryption or decryption of one or more digital programme services , digital sound programme services[^c-5ee2032c217e66b9479e7c1d38113f50] or digital additional services, and
- (b) is of a description specified in an order made by the Secretary of State.

(3A)[^c-841649adb9f785ee4e0e73653c631e92] In this section—

“**assistance for disabled people**” has the same meaning as in Part 3 of the Communications Act 2003;

“**available for reception by members of the public**” shall be construed in accordance with section 361 of that Act;

“**public television service of S4C[^c-e0729e10ab8f0f3630ae3ef3bd4255d1]**” means—

- (a) S4C Digital; or
- (b) any television programme service provided in digital form by S4C in fulfilling its public service remit[^c-082d7ef5e6f72543810ed948ffda6641];

“**relevant public service broadcaster**” means any of the following—

- (a) a person licensed under Part 1 of the 1990 Act to provide a Channel 3 service;
- (b) the Channel 4 Corporation;
- (c) a person licensed under Part 1 of the 1990 Act to provide Channel 5;
- (d) the BBC;
- (e) S4C[^c-afebee04caef998168811f0e7e788c34];
- (f)[^c-e41144aa4b7f0ec66aa16505b95dcf0d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) An order under subsection (3) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
