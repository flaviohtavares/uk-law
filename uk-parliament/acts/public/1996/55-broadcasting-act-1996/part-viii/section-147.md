# Broadcasting Act 1996, section 147

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 147. General interpretation.[^c11523191]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c11523201] In this Act—

- “**the 1990 Act**” means the [^c11523211]Broadcasting Act 1990;
- “**the BBC**” means the British Broadcasting Corporation.
- “[^key-3299cd9691942c28d4345145ae14a2ab] **OFCOM**” means the Office of Communications;

(2) The 1990 Act and the following provisions of this Act—

- (a) Parts I and II and Schedule 1,
- (b) Part IV,
- (c) Part V and Schedules 3 and 4, and
- (d) sections 142 to 146,

shall be construed as if those provisions were contained in that Act.
