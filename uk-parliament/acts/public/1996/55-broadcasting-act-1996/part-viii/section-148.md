# Broadcasting Act 1996, section 148

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/55

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 148. Minor and consequential amendments, repeals and revocations.[^c11523221]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 10 (which makes minor and consequential amendments) shall have effect.

(2) The enactments and instruments mentioned in Schedule 11 are hereby repealed or, as the case may be, revoked to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
