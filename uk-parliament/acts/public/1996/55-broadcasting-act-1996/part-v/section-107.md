# Broadcasting Act 1996, section 107

## 107. Preparation by OFCOM of code relating to avoidance of unjust or unfair treatment or interference with privacy.[^c11523041][^key-7496167aff6bc98a7406c3f0bbcdc25c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It shall be the duty of OFCOM to draw up, and from time to time review, a code giving guidance as to principles to be observed, and practices to be followed, in connection with the avoidance of—

- (a) unjust or unfair treatment in programmes to which subsection (5)[^key-d3f34e9fde6257f0457c389d6ed4db72] applies, or
- (b) unwarranted infringement of privacy in, or in connection with the obtaining of material included in, such programmes.

(1A)[^key-608e814948e6a1d079da2a15c76e9d3c] OFCOM must also draw up, and from time to time review, a code giving guidance as to the principles to be observed, and practices to be followed, in connection with the avoidance of—

- (a) unjust or unfair treatment in any programme that is included in a Tier 1 service (within the meaning given by section 368HA of the Communications Act 2003), or
- (b) unwarranted infringement of privacy in, or in connection with the obtaining of material included in, such programmes.

(1B) Subsection (1A) applies in relation to a Tier 1 service only so far as the service is made available for use by members of the public in the United Kingdom.

(2)[^key-4534f65b51e7e7f396bf00eba665ea27] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) OFCOM shall from time to time publish a code under this section[^key-5ba8b1de07a55b87016b6186e635352f] (as for the time being in force).

(4) Before drawing up or revising a code under this section[^key-1ecaf90285f878a9d0ad5eec5c813db3], OFCOM shall consult—

- (a) each broadcasting [^key-aedc68b602e346adcbda671cf1612443]... body, and
- (b) such other persons as appear to OFCOM to be appropriate.

(5) This subsection[^key-de04ff45853cdac1afe0d95303cd084a] applies to—

- (a) any programme broadcast by the BBC,
- (b) any programme broadcast by S4C[^key-485161e522753559723359fbf5c7d705] or included in any public service of[^key-923ea27343839e5dab00cb66485da04f] S4C (within the meaning of Part 2 of Schedule 12 to the Communications Act 2003), and
- (c) any programme included in a licensed service.
