# Broadcasting Act 1996, section 114

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/55
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114. Supplementary provisions as to making of complaints of either kind.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A fairness complaint [^key-c635a5e55b33f1bcde4c71af1d1ca6ee]... must be in writing, or in such other form as OFCOM[^key-b5d316a8c995509a55b894d03bf2495f] may allow, and must give particulars of the matters complained of.

(2) The OFCOM shall not entertain, or proceed with the consideration of, a fairness complaint [^key-a8cd905766ff9a34285985016c71b33c]... if it appears to them—

- (a) that the matter complained of is the subject of proceedings in a court of law in the United Kingdom, or
- (b) that the matter complained of is a matter in respect of which the complainant or[^key-94d7a5d8f874a2d0d1868425e97bb8c8]... the person affected has a remedy by way of proceedings in a court of law in the United Kingdom, and that in the particular circumstances it is not appropriate for OFCOM to consider a complaint about it, or
- (c) that the complaint is frivolous, or
- (d) that for any other reason it is inappropriate for them to entertain, or proceed with the consideration of, the complaint.
