## 317. Duties of governing body or (affected by repeal, see note) local authority (affected by repeal, see note) in relation to pupils with special educational needs. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c15246751][^key-83403ac1c888729628a0b8b62067b52e][^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^key-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^key-df82d42709314c7296748c08b3554faa][^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-db6edbaccc44d2ecd0cd012971116179][^key-02727d6395a4369d99a8f2bf2d8d6794] The governing body of a community, foundation or voluntary school or a maintained nursery school shall[^key-7de30dd1bafefbebebf2e15252601b38] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] —

- (a) use their best endeavours, in exercising their functions in relation to the school, to secure that, if any registered pupil has special educational needs, the special educational provision which his learning difficulty calls for is made, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) secure that, where the responsible person has been informed by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority[^key-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] that a registered pupil has special educational needs, those needs are made known to all who are likely to teach him, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c)[^key-f2f2414a8af1cb3df51b07ff6d0bb125] secure that the teachers in the school are aware of the importance of identifying, and providing for, those registered pupils who have special educational needs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2)[^key-02727d6395a4369d99a8f2bf2d8d6794][^key-99bccf5b07babfc0ec85db7b916a4f1c] In subsection (1)(b) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**the responsible person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**” means the head teacher or the appropriate governor (that is, the chairman of the governing body or, where the governing body have designated another governor for the purposes of this subsection, that other governor). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3)[^key-02727d6395a4369d99a8f2bf2d8d6794] To the extent that it appears necessary or desirable for the purpose of co-ordinating provision for children with special educational needs— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) the governing bodies of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] community, foundation and voluntary schools[^c15246781] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] and maintained nursery schools[^key-0f7502f528d34e37e1088aa115267a0a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] shall, in exercising functions relating to the provision for such children, consult the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c15246791]. . . and the governing bodies of other such schools, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^key-d169e457ce852976d80572e12d729f5e]...
- (b)[^key-d169e457ce852976d80572e12d729f5e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3A)[^key-a5e194a90d27c2e13bad0bf0d2acf4d1] The governing body of a community, foundation or voluntary school or a maintained nursery school shall designate a member of the staff at the school (to be known as the “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**special educational needs co-ordinator *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**”) as having responsibility for co-ordinating the provision for pupils with special educational needs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3B) Regulations may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) require the governing bodies of schools falling within subsection (3A) to ensure that special educational needs co-ordinators have prescribed qualifications or prescribed experience (or both), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) confer on the governing bodies of those schools other functions relating to special educational needs co-ordinators. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4)[^key-7bd576b570b330f9e3e6e3cc8d7a37f3][^key-77a9065672f45fb071bd0eafb92524f4] Where a child who has special educational needs is being educated in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] a community, foundation or voluntary school[^c15246811] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] or a maintained nursery school, those concerned with making special educational provision for the child shall secure, so far as is reasonably practicable and is compatible with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) the child receiving the special educational provision which his learning difficulty calls for, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) the provision of efficient education for the children with whom he will be educated, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) the efficient use of resources, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

that the child engages in the activities of the school together with children who do not have special educational needs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(5)[^key-3a32eac9d00e8e2549342c3b1c827d08][^key-7001981bf3e692ef582f768e25dae749] The governing body of a community, foundation or voluntary school, a maintained nursery school, or a community or foundation special school shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] include special needs information in the report prepared under section 30(1) of the Education Act 2002 (governors' report).[^key-11cf9fd404cb9f8dfd710ed073d722f0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a)[^key-b86f752b736d7f65cd4d10a224eaf8f2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^key-294eb707a1c28ee5d9146ba61d93c2cf] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) In subsection (5) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**special needs information *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) such information as may be prescribed about the implementation of the governing body's policy for pupils with special educational needs, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) information as to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (i) the arrangements for the admission of disabled persons as pupils at the school, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] the steps taken to prevent disabled pupils from being treated less favourably than other pupils, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] the facilities provided to assist access to the school by disabled pupils, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] the plan prepared by the governing body under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] paragraph 3 of Schedule 10 to the Equality Act 2010 (“the[^key-84265fbdbb68eef4120e919535a06953] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] 2010 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]”).

(6A) In subsection (6)(b) “disabled person” means a person who is a disabled person for the purposes of the[^key-fab8575028142fd5cb6d42d53934a1ae] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] 2010 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]; and section 89 (interpretation of Part 6) of, and paragraph 6 of Schedule 10 (supplementary provisions for Schedule 10) to, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] 2010 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] apply for the purposes of subsection (6)(b) as they apply for the purposes of Part 6 of and Schedule 10 to that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
