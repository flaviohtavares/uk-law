# Education Act 1996, section 332BA

>

## 332BA (affected by repeal, see note). Resolution of disputes (affected by repeal, see note) ...[^c-5bea103247869b921e6718f856e900ef][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-32cfaf8953297d5cde6a9b1f0253e715]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-0fd975d8883817b83247e420fb6d54ab]... must make arrangements with a view to avoiding or resolving disagreements between— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) authorities and children in their area about the exercise by authorities of functions under this Part, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) authorities and parents of children in their area about the exercise by authorities of functions under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) A local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-21e9a94e3b00870f66943bc38032f417]... must also make arrangements with a view to avoiding or resolving, in each relevant school, disagreements between— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) a relevant child and the proprietor of the school about the special educational provision made for that child, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) the parents of a relevant child and the proprietor of the school about the special educational provision made for that child. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) The arrangements must provide for the appointment of independent persons with the functions of facilitating the avoidance or resolution of such disagreements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) In making the arrangements, the authority must have regard to any guidance given by the Welsh Ministers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(5) The arrangements must comply with any provisions made in regulations by the Welsh Ministers that relate to the arrangements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(6) The authority must take such steps as they consider appropriate for making the arrangements under subsections (1) and (2) known to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) children in their area, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) parents of children in their area, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) head teachers and proprietors of schools in their area, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (d) such other persons as they consider appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(7) The arrangements cannot affect the entitlement of a child or a parent of a child to appeal to the Tribunal, and the authority must take such steps as they consider appropriate to make that fact known to children, to parents of children and to case friends for children in their area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(8) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- “**authorities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**” means the governing bodies of maintained schools and the local authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- “**relevant child *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**” means a child who has special educational needs and is a registered pupil at a relevant school. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(9) For the purposes of this section a school is a relevant school in relation to a child if it is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) a maintained school or a maintained nursery school, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) a pupil referral unit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) an independent school named in the statement maintained for the child under section 324, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (d) a school approved under section 342. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
