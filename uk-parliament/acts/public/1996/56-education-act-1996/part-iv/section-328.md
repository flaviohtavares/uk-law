## 328. Reviews of educational needs. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Regulations may prescribe the frequency with which assessments under section 323 are to be repeated in respect of children for whom statements are maintained under section 324. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) the parent of a child for whom a statement is maintained under section 324 asks the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] to arrange for an assessment to be made in respect of the child under section 323, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) no such assessment has been made within the period of six months ending with the date on which the request is made, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) it is necessary for the authority to make a further assessment under section 323, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

the authority shall comply with the request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) If in any case where subsection (2)(a) and (b) applies the authority determine not to comply with the request— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) they shall give *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] notice in writing of that fact[^c15247331] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] to the child’s parent, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) the parent may appeal to the Tribunal against the determination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3A)[^c15247341] A notice under subsection (3)(a) must inform the parent of the right of appeal under subsection (3)(b) and contain such other information as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3B) Regulations may provide that where a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] are under a duty under this section to serve any notice, the duty must be performed within the prescribed period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) On an appeal under subsection (3) the Tribunal may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) dismiss the appeal, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) order the authority to arrange for an assessment to be made in respect of the child under section 323. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(5) A statement under section 324 shall be reviewed by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]—

- (a) on the making of an assessment in respect of the child concerned under section 323, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2fed8d65e302c476864d96942e4e4d13][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] where the child concerned— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (i) has been subject to a detention order, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] immediately before release was detained in relevant youth accommodation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

  on the child's release from detention, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) in any event, within the period of 12 months beginning with the making of the statement or, as the case may be, with the previous review. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(6) Regulations may make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) as to the manner in which reviews of such statements are to be conducted, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) as to the participation in such reviews of such persons as may be prescribed, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) in connection with such other matters relating to such reviews as the Secretary of State considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
