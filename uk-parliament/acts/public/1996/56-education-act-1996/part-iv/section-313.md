## 313. Code of Practice. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] Welsh Ministers[^c-7c227004ba90fcad35025efc8389038d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] shall issue, and may from time to time revise, a code of practice giving practical guidance in respect of the discharge by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authorities[^c-df82d42709314c7296748c08b3554faa] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] and the governing bodies of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] maintained schools[^c15246571] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] and maintained nursery schools[^c-9495ed1478e15de4e65100d50939c7e2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] of their functions under this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2)[^c-79974d7ff0ebc75fc2822218addb9ca8] It shall be the duty of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) local authorities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41], and such governing bodies, exercising functions under this Part, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) any other person exercising any function for the purpose of the discharge by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authorities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41], and such governing bodies, of functions under this Part, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

to have regard to the provisions of the code. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) On any appeal under this Part to the Tribunal, the Tribunal shall have regard to any provision of the code which appears to the Tribunal to be relevant to any question arising on the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] Welsh Ministers[^c-19d8858b4d2c4bdb80e77e42a5c69a70] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] shall publish the code as for the time being in force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(5)[^c-d44cbdd77c68ca623066e505c0e6eb0f] In this Part *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-2c402490e7196453cdd82b3d240f1d05]..., “**the Tribunal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**”, in relation to an appeal, means *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] the Special Educational Needs Tribunal for Wales.[^c-d7d427bacc5913bf6dcab967b12c8669] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a)[^c-6ed5654ca48296e2c258a54175aefa95] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-99f1d4e41459f8b41cc1a5ca622fd4f4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
