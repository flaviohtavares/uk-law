## 329A. Review or assessment of educational needs at request of responsible body (affected by repeal, see note)[^c19120791][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-80df082308f18fb22a0666fc5b9cb1e3][^c15247371][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) a child is a registered pupil at a relevant school (whether or not he is a child in respect of whom a statement is maintained under section 324), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) the responsible body asks the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] to arrange for an assessment to be made in respect of him under section 323, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) no such assessment has been made within the period of six months ending with the date on which the request is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) If it is necessary for the authority to make an assessment or further assessment under section 323, they must comply with the request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) Before deciding whether to comply with the request, the authority must serve on the child’s parent a notice informing him— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) that they are considering whether to make an assessment of the child’s educational needs, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) of the procedure to be followed in making the assessment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) of the name of their officer from whom further information may be obtained, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (d) of the parent’s right to make representations, and submit written evidence, to them before the end of the period specified in the notice (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**the specified period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**”).

(4) The specified period must not be less than 29 days beginning with the date on which the notice is served. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(5) The authority may not decide whether to comply with the request until the specified period has expired. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(6) The authority must take into account any representations made, and any evidence submitted, to them in response to the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(7) If, as a result of this section, a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] decide to make an assessment under section 323, they must give written notice to the child’s parent and to the responsible body which made the request, of the decision and of their reasons for making it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(8) If, after serving a notice under subsection (3), the authority decide not to assess the educational needs of the child— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) they must give written notice of the decision and of their reasons for making it to his parent and to the responsible body which made the request, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) the parent may appeal to the Tribunal against the decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(9) A notice given under subsection (8)(a) to the child’s parent must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) inform the parent of his right to appeal, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) contain such other information (if any) as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(10) On an appeal under subsection (8) the Tribunal may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) dismiss it, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) order the authority to arrange for an assessment to be made in respect of the child under section 323. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(11) This section applies to a child for whom *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] relevant early years education[^c-9e0f112aed5295792520121ce4f9fcb3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] is provided as it applies to a child who is a registered pupil at a relevant school. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(12) “**Relevant school *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) a maintained school, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) a maintained nursery school, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) a pupil referral unit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (d) an independent school, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (da (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-2aa6da49f6216133d09e0166bbd435f9][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] an alternative provision Academy that is not an independent school, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (e) a school approved under section 342. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(13) “**The responsible body *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a)[^c-13e607b060cc3b4413ded046a3ae4463] in relation to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-6c851f2ee5d16d3a6f128baddb181df2]... a pupil referral unit, the head teacher, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) in relation to any other relevant school, the proprietor or head teacher, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) in relation to a provider of relevant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] early years[^c-8a769b04bc8c7b2c01906f62187067e1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] education, the person or body of persons responsible for the management of the provision of that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] early years *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] education. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(14)[^c-4140b46bbf360dc9d2e2e7a98a829bf7] “Relevant early years education” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] has the same meaning as it has (in relation to Wales) in section 123 of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 except that it does not include early years education provided by a local authority at a maintained nursery school.[^c-5dbd5f09abb03c5ca474ab6fc346ce2e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a)[^c-58272c47c0a404ed014f2445b0e901e5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-aa4e52ceb219ded27031a7d98a35b3d2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(15) “**Prescribed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**”[^c-f5bfa8c03b2eea114eea207542d96157]... means prescribed in regulations made by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] Welsh Ministers[^c-4e807403f1b7c62a43a6810f9d369e25] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41].
