# Education Act 1996, section 332ZB

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 332ZB (affected by repeal, see note). Notice and service of documents on a child (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d8531cb725783b16a199891179173d86][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if a local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-6e9e25a69d3592f201edd05292a239d3]... is required to give notice to or serve a document on a parent of a child under any of the following provisions— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) section 325 (appeal against decision not to make statement); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) section 328 (reviews of educational needs); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) section 329 (assessment of educational needs at request of child's parent); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (d) section 329A(8) (review or assessment of educational needs at request of responsible body); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (e) paragraph 3 of Schedule 26 (manner and timing of assessments); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (f) paragraph 2A of Schedule 27 (amendments to a statement); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (g) paragraph 2B(2) of Schedule 27 (provision of additional information); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (h) paragraph 6 of Schedule 27 (service of statement); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (i) paragraph 8 of Schedule 27 (change of named school); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (j) paragraph 11 of Schedule 27 (ceasing to maintain a statement). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) The local authority must give notice to, or serve the document on, the child as well as on the parent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) Any provision applicable to notices given to or documents served on a parent applies equally to notices given to or documents served on a child. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
