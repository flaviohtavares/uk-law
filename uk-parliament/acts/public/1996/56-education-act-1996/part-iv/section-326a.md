## 326A. Unopposed appeals (affected by repeal, see note)[^c15247311][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a)[^c-ab08eb78f7d6114e9b761bccf88e9096] the parent of a child, or a child, has appealed to the Tribunal under section 325, 328, 329 or 329A or paragraph 8(3) of Schedule 27 against a decision of a local authority, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) the authority notifies the Tribunal that they have determined that they will not, or will no longer, oppose the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) The appeal is to be treated as having been determined in favour of the appellant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) If an appeal is treated as determined in favour of the appellant as a result of subsection (2), the Tribunal is not required to make any order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) Before the end of the prescribed period, the authority must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) in the case of an appeal under section 325, make a statement under section 324 of the child’s educational needs, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) in the case of an appeal under section 328, 329 or 329A, make an assessment of the child’s educational needs, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) in the case of an appeal under paragraph 8(3) of Schedule 27 against a determination of the authority not to comply with the parent’s request, comply with the request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(5) An authority required by subsection (4)(a) to make a statement under section 324 must maintain the statement under that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(6)[^c-6ea7af896b6c9ad5f892f3bdb991cccf] In this section, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**prescribed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**” means prescribed by regulations made *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] by the Welsh Ministers[^c-95717914bcc95c06940e98e5bca33906] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]—

- (a)[^c-e4e4189af6c4137132be6ad6fed58463] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-001f74c9aea0758e43aac6e0447c10c6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
