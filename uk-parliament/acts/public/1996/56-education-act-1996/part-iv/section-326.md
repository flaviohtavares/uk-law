## 326. Appeal against contents of statement. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c15247291] The parent of a child for whom a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] maintain a statement under section 324 may appeal to the Tribunal— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) when the statement is first made, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) if an amendment is made to the statement, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) if, after conducting an assessment under section 323, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] determine not to amend the statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(1A) An appeal under this section may be against any of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) the description in the statement of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]’s assessment of the child’s special educational needs, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) the special educational provision specified in the statement (including the name of a school so specified), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) if no school is specified in the statement, that fact. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) Subsection (1)(b) does not apply where the amendment is made in pursuance of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) paragraph 8 (change of named school) or 11(3)(b) (amendment ordered by Tribunal) of Schedule 27, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) directions under section 442 (revocation of school attendance order); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

and subsection (1)(c) does not apply to a determination made following the service of notice under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] paragraph 2A[^c15247301] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] (amendment by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority[^c-df82d42709314c7296748c08b3554faa] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]) of Schedule 27 of a proposal to amend the statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3)[^c19120641] On an appeal under this section, the Tribunal may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) dismiss the appeal, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) order the authority to amend the statement, so far as it describes the authority’s assessment of the child’s special educational needs or specifies the special educational provision, and make such other consequential amendments to the statement as the Tribunal think fit, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) order the authority to cease to maintain the statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) On an appeal under this section the Tribunal shall not order the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] to specify the name of any school in the statement (either in substitution for an existing name or in a case where no school is named) unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) the parent has expressed a preference for the school in pursuance of arrangements under paragraph 3 (choice of school) of Schedule 27, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) in the proceedings the parent, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41], or both have proposed the school *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] , or *(Extent: W)*[^c-cfb28f244ab7eff65cb744154de27fa4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-710ef3676e6d299382bf11c3f042f0f2]...

*Extent: W*

- (c)[^c-251a75001376229f422de86852664cb2] in the proceedings the child has proposed the school[^c-c11be50b7ba7d4c628f8406059c7e9c8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] (whether or not the parent, the local authority or both have also proposed the school). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(5) Before determining any appeal under this section the Tribunal may, with the agreement of the parties, correct any deficiency in the statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
