## 329. Assessment of educational needs at request of child’s parent. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) the parent of a child for whom a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] are responsible but for whom no statement is maintained under section 324 asks the authority to arrange for an assessment to be made in respect of the child under section 323, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) no such assessment has been made within the period of six months ending with the date on which the request is made, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) it is necessary for the authority to make an assessment under that section, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

the authority shall comply with the request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) If in any case where subsection (1)(a) and (b) applies the authority determine not to comply with the request— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) they shall give *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] notice in writing of that fact[^c15247351] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] to the child’s parent, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) the parent may appeal to the Tribunal against the determination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2A)[^c15247361] A notice under subsection (2)(a) must inform the parent of the right of appeal under subsection (2)(b) and contain such other information as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) On an appeal under subsection (2) the Tribunal may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) dismiss the appeal, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) order the authority to arrange for an assessment to be made in respect of the child under section 323. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
