# Education Act 1996, section 332AA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 332AA (affected by repeal, see note). Advice and information (affected by repeal, see note) ...[^c-5c22fb7259c43f2de3e9d2457f44945e][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-0fcc56503e773fd28b3d982fa1ac62f1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-cc33f911c9138cb082cde58085bd5fd3]... must arrange for any child in their area with special educational needs, for a parent of any such child and for a case friend for any such child, to be provided with advice and information about matters relating to those needs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) In making the arrangements, the authority must have regard to any guidance given by the Welsh Ministers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) The arrangements must comply with any provisions made in regulations by the Welsh Ministers that relate to the arrangements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) The authority must take such steps as they consider appropriate for making the services provided under subsection (1) known to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) children in their area, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) parents of children in their area, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) head teachers and proprietors of schools in their area, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (d) such other persons as they consider appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
