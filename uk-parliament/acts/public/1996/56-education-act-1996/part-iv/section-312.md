## 312. Meaning of “special educational needs” and “special educational provision” (affected by repeal, see note) etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A child *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] in the area of a local authority in Wales[^c-05e71c99cac10d9daadb83edba3d010a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] has “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**special educational needs *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**” for the purposes of this Act if he has a learning difficulty which calls for special educational provision to be made for him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) Subject to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] subsections (3) and (3A)[^c-4428ac49f3599bdcfb53272a92d35d90] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] a child *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] in the area of a local authority in Wales[^c-e5d694d7fa560a03254b24a310537ef7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] has a “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**learning difficulty *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**” for the purposes of this Act if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) he has a significantly greater difficulty in learning than the majority of children of his age, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) he has a disability which either prevents or hinders him from making use of educational facilities of a kind generally provided for children of his age in schools within the area of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] , or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) he is under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] compulsory school age[^c15246481] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] and is, or would be if special educational provision were not made for him, likely to fall within paragraph (a) or (b) when of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c15246491]. . . that age. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) A child is not to be taken as having a learning difficulty solely because the language (or form of the language) in which he is, or will be, taught is different from a language (or form of a language) which has at any time been spoken in his home. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3A)[^c-6552b0327364498c7621cb05c2a04cef] Subsection (2) does not apply— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) for the purposes of sections *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-8a0aecb09473d2a44a54bb00643a705d]... , 15A and 15B[^c-041b77f05ecea0fadb7849b6d26953c9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41], or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) for the purposes of sections 18A and 562H (except for the purpose of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] a local authority in Wales[^c-adf6b944ffe165dac1c3d70a4af16005] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] determining, for the purposes of those sections, whether a child has special educational needs). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) In this Act “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**special educational provision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) in relation to a child who has attained the age of two, educational provision which is additional to, or otherwise different from, the educational provision made generally for children of his age in schools maintained by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] (other than special schools) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c15246501]. . ., and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) in relation to a child under that age, educational provision of any kind. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(5) In this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- “**child *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**” includes any person who has not attained the age of 19 and is a registered pupil at a school; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- “[^c15246511]**maintained school *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**” means any community, foundation or voluntary school or any community or foundation special school not established in a hospital. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
