# Education Act 1996, section 324

## 324. Statement of special educational needs. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^key-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If, in the light of an assessment under section 323 of any child’s educational needs and of any representations made by the child’s parent in pursuance of Schedule 27, it is necessary for the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority[^key-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] to determine the special educational provision which any learning difficulty he may have calls for, the authority shall make and maintain a statement of his special educational needs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) The statement shall be in such form and contain such information as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) In particular, the statement shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) give details of the authority’s assessment of the child’s special educational needs, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) specify the special educational provision to be made for the purpose of meeting those needs, including the particulars required by subsection (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) The statement shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) specify the type of school or other institution which the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] consider would be appropriate for the child, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) if they are not required under Schedule 27 to specify the name of any school in the statement, specify the name of any school or institution (whether in the United Kingdom or elsewhere) which they consider would be appropriate for the child and should be specified in the statement, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) specify any provision for the child for which they make arrangements under section 319 and which they consider should be specified in the statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4A)[^c15247161] Subsection (4)(b) does not require the name of a school or institution to be specified if the child’s parent has made suitable arrangements for the special educational provision specified in the statement to be made for the child. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(5) Where a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] maintain a statement under this section, then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) unless the child’s parent has made suitable arrangements, the authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (i) shall arrange that the special educational provision specified in the statement is made for the child, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] may arrange that any non-educational provision specified in the statement is made for him in such manner as they consider appropriate, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b)[^c15247171][^key-c14e7b2451e29f4dc71101b8044c7e22][^key-9db65a943b97b05faf53160fbba27205] if the name of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] maintained school[^c15247181] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] or maintained nursery school[^key-7be9c83ec0c71da923a41f51a0161479] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] is specified in the statement, the governing body of the school shall admit the child to the school. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(5A)[^key-b5e2344b9914c26ead18995661d600f2][^c15247171][^key-64b0b5c75a6ce72899877b1c2432d99a][^c15247191] Subsection (5)(b) has effect regardless of any duty imposed on the governing body of a school by section 1(6) of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(6) Subsection (5)(b) does not affect any power to exclude from a school a pupil who is already a registered pupil there. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(7) Schedule 27 has effect in relation to the making and maintenance of statements under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
