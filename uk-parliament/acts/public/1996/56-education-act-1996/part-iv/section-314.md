## 314. Making and approval of code. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] Welsh Ministers propose[^c-3e2564be502c164e9830a32063ccb1a6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] to issue or revise a code of practice, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] they[^c-18af3446e18a6af78d5fb9a8f7718c9a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] shall prepare a draft of the code (or revised code). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] Welsh Ministers[^c-bed6d1584a5b7c9116b8650ac952a0f0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] shall consult such persons about the draft as *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] they think[^c-81a935e4847039a5393bde2223aded03] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] fit and shall consider any representations made by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] those persons[^c-78b72c82db7a9d250a4d48744cf70925] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41].

(3)[^c-9214be6f7c38633dcf8e5d1c389d6e63] If the Welsh Ministers determine to proceed with the draft (either in its original form or with such modifications as they think fit) they shall lay it before the National Assembly for Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) If the draft is approved by resolution of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] the National Assembly for Wales, the Welsh Ministers[^c-9418819e30a6cbd55d8076250f90f2b8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] shall issue the code in the form of the draft, and the code shall come into effect on such day as *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] the Welsh Ministers may[^c-0bbbf2ab576eedd953fb57ec3b0ba732] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] by order appoint. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
