# Education Act 1996, section 332BB

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 332BB (affected by repeal, see note). Independent advocacy services (affected by repeal, see note) ...[^c-b48e8ddf5f8419b0e2643d87a4585af5][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-c2efe585f988f8e696832ecc9f647b78]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Every local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-32e057bdbaf1a6f29b88927cbcc676f3]... must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) make arrangements for the provision of independent advocacy services in their area; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) refer any child in their area who requests independent advocacy services to a service provider; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) refer any person who is a case friend for a child in their area and who requests independent advocacy services to a service provider. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) In this section “independent advocacy services” are services providing advice and assistance (by way of representation or otherwise) to a child— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) making, or intending to make an appeal to the Tribunal under section 332ZA, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) considering whether to appeal to the Tribunal under that section, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) taking part in or intending to take part in dispute resolution arrangements made under section 332BA. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) In making arrangements under this section, every local authority must have regard to the principle that any services provided under the arrangements must be independent of any person who is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) the subject of an appeal to the Tribunal, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) involved in investigating or adjudicating on such an appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) The arrangements must comply with any provisions made in regulations by the Welsh Ministers that relate to the arrangements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(5) Every local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-016505fb9575a7fe3fd5fe1a436210e8]... must take such steps as they consider appropriate for making the arrangements under this section known to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) children in their area, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) parents of children in their area, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) head teachers and proprietors of schools in their area, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (d) such other persons as they consider appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(6) The arrangements may include provision for payments to be made to, or in relation to, any person carrying out functions in accordance with the arrangements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(7) A local authority must have regard to any guidance given from time to time by the Welsh Ministers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
