# Education Act 1996, section 332ZC

## 332ZC (affected by repeal, see note). Case friends (affected by repeal, see note) ...[^c-74e06f33b7f3770c0766f91b7e000906][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-06284793e140ec229b316bb815434d54]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may provide by regulations for a child to have a person (referred to in this Part as a “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**case friend *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**”)—

- (a) to make representations on behalf of a child with a view to avoiding or resolving disagreements about the exercise by local education authorities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-893fca1000792834c026ede25264573f]... of functions under this Part, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) to exercise the rights of a child under section 332ZA on the child's behalf. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) A child's case friend must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) make representations and exercise rights fairly and competently, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) have no interest adverse to that of the child; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) ensure that all steps and decisions taken by the case friend are for the benefit of the child and take account of the child's views. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) Regulations made under this section may (among other things)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) confer functions on the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-350aa3cc24ddc9e2a60fefb1806f78f2]... Tribunal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) make provision about procedures in relation to case friends; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) make provision about the appointment and removal of case friends; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (d) specify the circumstances in which a person may or may not act as a case friend; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (e) specify the circumstances in which a child must have a case friend; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (f) specify further requirements in respect of the conduct of case friends. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
