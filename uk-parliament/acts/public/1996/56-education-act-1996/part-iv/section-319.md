# Education Act 1996, section 319

## 319. Special educational provision otherwise than in schools. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^key-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority[^key-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] are satisfied that it would be inappropriate for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) the special educational provision which a learning difficulty of a child in their area calls for, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) any part of any such provision, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

to be made in a school, they may arrange for the provision (or, as the case may be, for that part of it) to be made otherwise than in a school. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) Before making an arrangement under this section, a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] shall consult the child’s parent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
