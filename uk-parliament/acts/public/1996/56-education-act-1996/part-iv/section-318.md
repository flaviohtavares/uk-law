## 318. Provision of goods and services in connection with special educational needs. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] may, for the purpose only of assisting— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) the governing bodies of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] community, foundation or voluntary schools[^c15246991] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] or maintained nursery schools[^c-e386655ebd2f2e24098f097d19aea480] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] (in their or any other area) in the performance of the governing bodies’ duties under section 317(1)(a), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) the governing bodies of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] community or foundation special schools *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] (in their or any other area) in the performance of the governing bodies’ duties, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

supply goods or services to those bodies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) The terms on which goods or services are supplied by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authorities[^c-df82d42709314c7296748c08b3554faa] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] this section to the governing bodies of community, foundation or voluntary schools[^c15247011] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] , maintained nursery schools[^c-aa7f9d89d2fb672939e0b771a14816e1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] or community or foundation special schools in any other area *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] may, in such circumstances as may be prescribed, include such terms as to payment as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3)[^c-1b72399957ecafc8c69b4c66d157d71d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3A)[^c-e87d877c0a2f550211b88c7bf66eac06] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3B)[^c18415311] A local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-a0b7d4374ff03de85671a9401e96433b]... may supply goods and services to any authority in Wales or other person (other than a governing body within subsection(1)) for the purpose of assisting them in making for a child any special educational provision which any learning difficulty of the child calls for. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) This section is without prejudice to the generality of any other power of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authorities *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] to supply goods or services. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
