# Education Act 1996, section 325

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 325. Appeal against decision not to make statement. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If, after making an assessment under section 323 of the educational needs of any child for whom no statement is maintained under section 324, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] do not propose to make such a statement, they shall give notice in writing of their decision, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c15247251]. . . to the child’s parent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) In such a case, the child’s parent may appeal to the Tribunal against the decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2A)[^c15247261] A notice under subsection (1) must inform the parent of the right of appeal under subsection (2) and contain such other information as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2B) Regulations may provide that where a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] are under a duty under this section to serve any notice, the duty must be performed within the prescribed period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) On an appeal under this section, the Tribunal may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) dismiss the appeal, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) order the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] to make and maintain such a statement, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) remit the case to the authority for them to reconsider whether, having regard to any observations made by the Tribunal, it is necessary for the authority to determine the special educational provision which any learning difficulty the child may have calls for. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
