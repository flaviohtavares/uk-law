## 323. Assessment of educational needs. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] are of the opinion that a child for whom they are responsible falls, or probably falls, within subsection (2), they shall serve a notice on the child’s parent informing him— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) that they *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] are considering whether[^c15247141] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] to make an assessment of the child’s educational needs, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) of the procedure to be followed in making the assessment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) of the name of the officer of the authority from whom further information may be obtained, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (d) of the parent’s right to make representations, and submit written evidence, to the authority within such period (which must not be less than 29 days beginning with the date on which the notice is served) as may be specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) A child falls within this subsection if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) he has special educational needs, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) it is necessary for the authority to determine the special educational provision which any learning difficulty he may have calls for. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) a local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] have served a notice under subsection (1) and the period specified in the notice in accordance with subsection (1)(d) has expired, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) the authority remain of the opinion, after taking into account any representations made and any evidence submitted to them in response to the notice, that the child falls, or probably falls, within subsection (2), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

they shall make an assessment of his educational needs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) Where a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] decide to make an assessment under this section, they shall give notice in writing to the child’s parent of that decision and of their reasons for making it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(5) Schedule 26 has effect in relation to the making of assessments under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(6) Where, at any time after serving a notice under subsection (1), a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] decide not to assess the educational needs of the child concerned they shall give notice in writing to the child’s parent of their decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
