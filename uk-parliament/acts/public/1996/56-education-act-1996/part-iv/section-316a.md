## 316A. Education otherwise than in mainstream schools (affected by repeal, see note)[^c15246611][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-c6bce8363a273a6c90e0a466c0377fd3][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 316 does not prevent a child from being educated in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) an independent school which is not a mainstream school, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) a school approved under section 342, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

if the cost is met otherwise than by a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41].

(2)[^M_C_41b13530-a0fd-47f1-af48-994646e56b1e] Section 316(2) does not require a child to be educated in a mainstream school during any period in which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) he is admitted to a special school for the purposes of an assessment under section 323 of his educational needs and his admission to that school is with the agreement of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (i) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41],
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0ff173522feb378ae583d98f54b72a68][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] the governing body of the school or, if the school is in England, its head teacher, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] his parent, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (iv (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] any person whose advice is to be sought in accordance with regulations made under paragraph 2 of Schedule 26; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) he remains admitted to a special school, in prescribed circumstances, following an assessment under section 323 at that school; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c)[^c-199931d06a18bebe62a381670583a847] he is admitted to a special school, following a change in his circumstances, with the agreement of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (i) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41],
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-141bc4861408b12b8416be1069797fef][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] the governing body of the school or, if the school is in England, its head teacher, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] his parent; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (d) he is admitted to a community or foundation special school which is established in a hospital. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) Section 316 does not affect the operation of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) section 348, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) paragraph 3 of Schedule 27. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) If a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] decide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) to make a statement for a child under section 324, but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) not to name in the statement the school for which a parent has expressed a preference under paragraph 3 of Schedule 27, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

they shall, in making the statement, comply with section 316(3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(5) A local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] may, in relation to their mainstream schools taken as a whole, rely on the exception in section 316(3)(b) only if they show that there are no reasonable steps that they could take to prevent the incompatibility. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(6) An authority in relation to a particular mainstream school may rely on the exception in section 316(3)(b) only if it shows that there are no reasonable steps that it or another authority in relation to the school could take to prevent the incompatibility. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(7) The exception in section 316(3)(b) does not permit a governing body to fail to comply with the duty imposed by section 324(5)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(8) An authority must have regard to guidance about section 316 and this section issued *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] by the Welsh Ministers[^c-6082e9d13369bdb3de9fcab59a0a9f1d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]—

- (a)[^c-459b6805ddd18ff85e9c08f58038031f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c-38c0b2b5b512bf78b6039ca81689aecb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9) That guidance shall, in particular, relate to steps which may, or may not, be regarded as reasonable for the purposes of subsections (5) and (6). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(10) “**Prescribed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**”[^c-1fdfa48ade468968da55b0d30aa06ce9]... means prescribed in regulations made by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] Welsh Ministers[^c-57201e2d89a4cc7407012a49bd825667] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41].

(11) “**Authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**”—

- (a) in relation to a maintained school *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] or maintained nursery school[^c-c801a23cca4845eca4fbf34893e2e3c7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41], means each of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (i) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41],
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] the school’s governing body, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) in relation to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-1ccbb3904909b124fbf583edfc571b16]... a pupil referral unit, means the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41].
