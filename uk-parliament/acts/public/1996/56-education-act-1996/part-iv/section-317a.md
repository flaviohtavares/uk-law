# Education Act 1996, section 317A

## 317A. Duty to inform parent where special educational provision made (affected by repeal, see note)[^c15246951][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-31070d92ae13f0897b8906ca28410340] This section applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) a child for whom no statement is maintained under section 324 is a registered pupil at— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (i) a community, foundation or voluntary school *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] or a maintained nursery school[^c-7e7e9b66ffe9dad1486afc67f344a259] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] , or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] a pupil referral unit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) special educational provision is made for him at the school because it is considered that he has special educational needs, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) his parent has not previously been informed under this section of special educational provision made for him at the school. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) If the school is a pupil referral unit, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] must secure that the head teacher informs the child’s parent that special educational provision is being made for him at the school because it is considered that he has special educational needs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) In any other case, the governing body must inform the child’s parent that special educational provision is being made for him there because it is considered that he has special educational needs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
