## 322. Duty of certain bodies to help local authority. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-0bb5c0d640d199df46672514d2ea17c5][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where it appears to a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] another local authority,[^c-55ee7894764ccd58bebcd8c04a3aff69] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] NHS England[^c-d37f0349cdf2936cf3a64c4b58fe9e8f][^c-8f633fc58aa4e0e6ec772390eecf2e62] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41], an integrated care board[^c-1ce5063225a5e0450f7e538df00d764c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] a Local Health Board *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-ebd76d45719e1d1e2070dbcbadea29e3]... could, by taking any specified action, help in the exercise of any of their functions under this Part, they may request the help of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] that body[^c-6bcdbc0684cd81dee562c1ba638ee2ff] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] , specifying the action in question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) A body[^c-a60e008340457cf267038bf0789eb9b4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] whose help is so requested shall comply with the request unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) they consider that the help requested is not necessary for the purpose of the exercise *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] of those functions by the local authority that made the request[^c-f3f0cb0ca59f037045d0380af75f58e3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] , or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) subsection (3) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) This subsection applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] a case where the request is made[^c-74e7d0cffe36055d481ce7c894eaf849] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] NHS England[^c-076d8efca80da6b1b8005449c85ea222] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41], an integrated care board[^c-aa6d386900377c58c59bfc78d6d495d6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] a Local Health Board[^c-e619a982623b2bf15e0edbb0a93c5992] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-9be51ba4119d8a576bcf7bc72a4d3263]..., if *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] that body[^c-f9645b8e7974cfbff3139fc543cac5b4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] consider that, having regard to the resources available to them for the purpose of the exercise of their functions under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c15247121] National Health Service Act 2006 or the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^c-3720d22fe263520f37b42154fbb77a10] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] , it is not reasonable for them to comply with the request, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] a case where the request is made *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] of a local authority, if that authority consider that the request is not compatible with their own statutory or other duties and obligations or unduly prejudices the discharge of any of their functions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) Regulations may provide that, where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] a local authority,[^c-f06573a0cbdc69d66fa067178b8d28d0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] NHS England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41], an integrated care board[^c-15482b28f2c9ea9b5f60f7ce00484356] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] or a Local Health Board *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-5fd5b07806f934555650c8674df0e2f5]... are under a duty by virtue of subsection (2) to comply with a request to help a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] in the making of an assessment under section 323 or a statement under section 324 of this Act, they must, subject to prescribed exceptions, comply with the request within the prescribed period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
