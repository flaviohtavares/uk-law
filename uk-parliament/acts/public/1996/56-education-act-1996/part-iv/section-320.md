# Education Act 1996, section 320

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 320. Provision outside England and Wales for certain children. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] may make such arrangements as they think fit to enable a child for whom they maintain a statement under section 324 to attend an institution outside England and Wales which specialises in providing for children with special needs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) In subsection (1) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**children with special needs *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**” means children who have particular needs which would be special educational needs if those children were in England and Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) Where a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] make arrangements under this section in respect of a child, those arrangements may in particular include contributing to or paying— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) fees charged by the institution, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) expenses reasonably incurred in maintaining him while he is at the institution or travelling to or from it, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) his travelling expenses, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (d) expenses reasonably incurred by any person accompanying him while he is travelling or staying at the institution. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) This section is without prejudice to any other powers of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41].
