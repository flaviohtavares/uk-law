## 332. Duty of (affected by repeal, see note) Local Health Board (affected by repeal, see note) or National Health Service trust to notify parent (affected by repeal, see note) etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-442675bbd548991389bf350f37a7b9d4][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] an integrated care board,[^c-06ebf0c85c95c2abcb0ee2c57d26ccd1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] a Local Health Board[^c-b37c6758511550ecbe3cd14d92dbd48a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] [^c-3329604cbb0206ab0aaa1b7a8591c80d]... , a National Health Service trust or an[^c-1ee004433357327bf40dcd4f4b7f4acf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] NHS *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] foundation trust *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] , in the course of exercising any of their functions in relation to a child who is under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] compulsory school age[^c15247401] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41], form the opinion that he has (or probably has) special educational needs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] Board[^c-81f8a6622c69f812a6b325a814a69834] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] other body[^c-948f63478e8608a57e30eb0a2576d6ff] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] —

- (a) shall inform the child’s parent of their opinion and of their duty under paragraph (b), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) after giving the parent an opportunity to discuss that opinion with an officer of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] Board *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] other body *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] , shall bring it to the attention of the appropriate *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41].

(3) If the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] Board[^c-88404481d933d6c2c323debd7795c9d1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] other body[^c-318a262f222d433ddd167e378e928cf3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] are of the opinion that a particular voluntary organisation is likely to be able to give the parent advice or assistance in connection with any special educational needs that the child may have, they shall inform the parent accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
