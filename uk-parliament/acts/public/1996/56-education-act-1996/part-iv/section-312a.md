# Education Act 1996, section 312A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2026-11-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 312A. Children subject to detention (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-1c7722658c395e2af88fce6f88778fa7][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) No provision of, or made under, this Part applies in relation to a child who is subject to a detention order and detained in relevant youth accommodation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) The following provisions of this section apply where a child who has been subject to a detention order is released having, immediately before release, been detained in relevant youth accommodation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) Subject to subsection (6), a statement which was maintained for the child by a local authority under section 324 immediately before the beginning of the detention is, from the child's release, to be treated as being maintained by that authority under section 324. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) In subsection (3) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**the beginning of the detention *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) the beginning of the period of detention in relevant youth accommodation, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) where that period is part of a continuous period, comprising periods of detention in relevant youth accommodation and in other accommodation, the beginning of that continuous period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(5) For the purposes of subsection (4), it is immaterial whether or not a period of detention is pursuant to a single order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(6) Where, on the child's release, a local authority (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**the new authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**”) other than the authority mentioned in subsection (3) (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**the old authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**”) becomes responsible for the child for the purposes of this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) the old authority must transfer the statement to the new authority, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) from the child's release, the statement is to be treated as being maintained by the new authority under section 324. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
