# Education Act 1996, section 316

## 316. Duty to educate children with special educational needs in mainstream schools (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0e98c54cb1e5b90b21fa391d877e43fb][^c15246601][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies to a child with special educational needs who should be educated in a school. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2)[^c19112921] If no statement is maintained under section 324 for the child, he must be educated in a mainstream school. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3)[^c19112921] If a statement is maintained under section 324 for the child, he must be educated in a mainstream school unless that is incompatible with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) the wishes of his parent, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) the provision of efficient education for other children. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(4) In this section and section 316A “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**mainstream school *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]**” means any school other than— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) a special school, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) an independent school which is not— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (i) a city technology college, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] a city college for the technology of the arts, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] an Academy[^c18307241] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41].
