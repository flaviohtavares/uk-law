## 321. General duty of (affected by repeal, see note) local authority (affected by repeal, see note) towards children for whom they are responsible. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-a26091f02a6f098b8797ebca0f491930][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41][^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] shall exercise their powers with a view to securing that, of the children for whom they are responsible, they identify those to whom subsection (2) below applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(2) This subsection applies to a child if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) he has special educational needs, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (b) it is necessary for the authority to determine the special educational provision which any learning difficulty he may have calls for. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

(3) For the purposes of this Part a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] are responsible for a child if he is in their area and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]

- (a) he is a registered pupil at a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] maintained school[^c15247041] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] or maintained nursery school[^c-643d569b5b6c84399f9a56d559ab6106] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] ,
- (b)[^c15247051] education is provided for him at a school which is not a maintained school *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] or maintained nursery school *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41] but is so provided at the expense of the authority, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (c) he does not come within paragraph (a) or (b) above but is a registered pupil at a school and has been brought to the authority’s attention as having (or probably having) special educational needs, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
- (d) he is not a registered pupil at a school but is not under the age of two or over compulsory school age and has been brought to their attention as having (or probably having) special educational needs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44c659fdf3ae27bfa95ab072d6157d41]
