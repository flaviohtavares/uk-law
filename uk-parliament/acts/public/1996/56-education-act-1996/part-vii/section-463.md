## 463. Alteration to definition of “independent school”[^c-2d1721a060639ee27cf87abcc3c2f7e6][^c-76e4bb25cdd4f0f533c78e328d8e5afb]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Act “**independent school**” means any school at which full-time education is provided for—

- (a) five or more pupils of compulsory school age, or
- (b) at least one pupil of that age for whom an EHC plan[^c-29e1192500180dd90d3f222d4d9f6076] is maintained or for whom *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1a703ac252733aeed845336a82029049] a statement is maintained under section 324 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1a703ac252733aeed845336a82029049] or an individual development plan is maintained, or who is looked after by a local authority (within the meaning of section 22 of the Children Act 1989 or section 74 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014[^c-37c2cca80fc37027b472761de087e0a0]),

and which is not a school maintained by a local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] non-maintained special school[^c-fa9fc2b554c4c70fd5896624173177dc].

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1)(a) and (b) it is immaterial if full-time education is also provided at the school for pupils under or over compulsory school age.
