## 483A. City colleges and academies: special educational needs and additional learning needs.[^c15251141][^key-7eaf7238bb3571c6939e057b899f9450]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies in relation to any child falling within subsection (2) if the condition in subsection (3) is satisfied *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7de19cdee13b6efc45702bd690e361e7].

(2) A child falls within this subsection if—

- (a) he is a child for whom an EHC plan[^key-14e411ae7c7bd356fb112f398b310817] or a statement under section 324 is maintained *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ded942bc97700129b5b6042c9867391e] is maintained by a local authority in England, or for whom an individual development plan is maintained by a local authority in Wales, and
- (b) he attends (or proposes to attend) a school which is a city technology college, a city college for the technology of the arts or an Academy[^c18523811] .

(3)[^key-13ee9b9aa0ea3e537bd0d648c79006c0] The condition in this subsection is satisfied if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ee9b9aa0ea3e537bd0d648c79006c0]

- (a)[^key-fe6a44f57b8e71d3b2ac0ba94886845a] the EHC plan[^key-e15ede7174bb6835058a7dbeaec2e594] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ee9b9aa0ea3e537bd0d648c79006c0] is maintained by a local authority in England, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ee9b9aa0ea3e537bd0d648c79006c0]
- (b) the statement is maintained by a local authority in Wales and the Welsh Ministers consent to the child being educated at the school. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ee9b9aa0ea3e537bd0d648c79006c0]

(4) The appropriate national authority[^key-2a653e6ad34dd1e36c925a3721e18df7] may by regulations make provision for securing that arrangements are made—

- (a) for making the special educational provision specified in the plan or the statement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e57f0bf790cf3a1a43bafbee28915ef3] the EHC plan, or for making the additional learning provision specified in the individual development plan (as the case may be);
- (b) for making any non-educational provision specified in the plan or the statement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-32b1b165f08edab3ffcf9e5f92f513a1] the EHC plan or the individual development plan.

(5) Regulations under subsection (4) may require or authorise a local authority[^key-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41]—

- (a) to make payments to the school in respect of the child, or
- (b) to provide any other assistance to the school in respect of the child.

(6) No condition or requirement imposed by virtue of section 482(4)(a) is to prevent a local authority making payments or providing assistance by virtue of subsection (5).

(6A)[^key-7ec7546bf8b0ffb13cece72840987b6c] In subsection (4) “**the appropriate national authority**” means—

- (a) in relation to a school in England, the Secretary of State;
- (b) in relation to a school in Wales, the Welsh Ministers.

(7) This section does not apply to schools in Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c18523841]
