# Education Act 1996, section 459

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 459. Provision of information.[^c-06dd79d0f477f057e7be418e2e48584c]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations may require, in relation to every maintained school, the local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41], the governing body or the head teacher to make available either generally or to prescribed persons, in such form and manner and at such times as may be prescribed—

- (a) such information relevant for the purposes of this Chapter as to the school hours at the school, and
- (b) such information as to the policies determined under section 457 which apply in relation to the school,

as may be prescribed.
