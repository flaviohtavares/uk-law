# Education Act 1996, section 450

## 450. Prohibition of charges for admission.[^c-06dd79d0f477f057e7be418e2e48584c][^c-4839a717c0315cbd636d69a4a6ea4ee6][^c-06dd79d0f477f057e7be418e2e48584c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) No charge shall be made in respect of admission to a maintained school.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to the admission of any person to any maintained school for the purpose of—

- (a) part-time education suitable to the requirements of persons of any age over compulsory school age;
- (b) full-time education suitable to the requirements of persons who have attained the age of 19; or
- (c)[^c-a5c246d9018ab31281b4a54654f7e03e] training for members of the school workforce.

(3)[^c-ac2cceb6aaab28859a5bfd831d5834c1] In subsection (2)(c), the reference to training for members of the school workforce is to be read in accordance with sections 96(1) and 100[^c-c96e483e97d4200da9709274324325f4] of the Education Act 2005.
