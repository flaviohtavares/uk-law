# Education Act 1996, section 441

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/56

## 441. Choice of school: child with EHC plan or statement of special educational needs (affected by repeal, see note).[^key-b2ef312e530f4f2b774a484e3e2d0db3][^key-9d28bd1b897d37c3c64976ef0437089d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subsections (2) and (3) apply where a local authority[^key-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] in England[^key-d2e30477ab170c02108fc92b53f1dc7d] are required by virtue of section 437(3) to serve a school attendance order in respect of a child for whom they maintain an EHC plan[^key-62164c61ef1461246f316f0d548b5b29] (in the case of a local authority in England) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dec0ebbdd4631a94bfd930a402d4be2c] a statement under section 324 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dec0ebbdd4631a94bfd930a402d4be2c] (in the case of a local authority in Wales)[^key-fbd65d22499e255b27c554678b67fa05] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-dec0ebbdd4631a94bfd930a402d4be2c].

(2) Where the EHC plan[^key-51ed5becb2d5462c44c77cf7b29b6abb] or statement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3b5e30bd6d23977882f1f49f63257b63] specifies the name of a school, that school shall be named in the order.

(3) Where the EHC plan[^key-3ff9f8af0453d8307c6ebc92d6981aa2] or statement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a5248d7a4daaa42e678cbee2d470d2a2] does not specify the name of a school—

- (a) the authority shall, [^c15250381]. . . , amend the EHC plan[^key-dedce134e194a8a15315383cf026a666] or statement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7d92e994252c3ae59302ed4df1759b3d] so that it specifies the name of a school, and
- (b) that school shall then be named in the order.

(3A)[^c15250391] An amendment to a statement required to be made under subsection (3)(a) shall be treated for the purposes of Schedule 27 as if it were an amendment proposed following a periodic review (within the meaning of that Schedule). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-36b3793f76f50b4b6aec18c8abb00136]

(3B)[^key-0c2e03e08e48d06f84f6c9bb66a20656] An amendment to an EHC plan required to be made under subsection (3)(a) shall be treated as if it were an amendment made following a review under section 44 of the Children and Families Act 2014, and that section and regulations made under it apply accordingly.

(4) Where—

- (a) a school attendance order is in force in respect of a child for whom the local authority maintain an EHC plan[^key-b82468c978bb5006e8849a642c1b26fc] or a statement under section 324 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e07e9e87f1eea66aea5f4e309f95699], and
- (b) the name of the school specified in the plan[^key-67b98acda1e59fb4167965fad34705ef] or statement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a0bd3e77fee1fd907aecd9369abef01d] is changed,

the local authority shall amend the order accordingly.
