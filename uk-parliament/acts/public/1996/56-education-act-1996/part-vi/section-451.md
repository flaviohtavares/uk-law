## 451. Prohibition of charges for provision of education.[^c-06dd79d0f477f057e7be418e2e48584c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-0f3f3d2130c0cc2ebe5230b8261c65b2] [^c15250541]... This section applies in relation to education provided at any maintained school for a registered pupil at the school.

(2)[^c-133ecc2efd7b587965558fd99474a712] Where the education is provided for the pupil during school hours no charge shall be made in respect of it.

(2A)[^c-60f6c472af43f6824d61b5869e139a7d] Regulations may, in relation to England, prescribe circumstances in which subsection (2) does not apply in relation to education which is early years provision (as defined by section 20 of the Childcare Act 2006) other than —

- (a) early years provision provided in pursuance of the duty imposed by section 7 of that Act, [^c-9faea3793c8248856f02ea5dbf2b9494]...
- (b) early years provision for a pupil who is of compulsory school age. or[^c-a8376d88f90e70782275654b19fd38b7]
- (c) early years provision provided under arrangements made by a local authority in pursuance of any duty imposed under section 2 of the Childcare Act 2016 (whether or not the local authority provides the early years provision).

(3) Subsection (2) does not apply in relation to tuition in playing a musical instrument where the tuition is provided either individually or to a group of not more than four pupils, unless the tuition is— *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5b58d0e9270ae511bbbafc88801bdb62]

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5b58d0e9270ae511bbbafc88801bdb62] required as part of a syllabus for a prescribed public examination which is a syllabus for which the pupil is being prepared at the school, or *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5b58d0e9270ae511bbbafc88801bdb62]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-5b58d0e9270ae511bbbafc88801bdb62] provided in pursuance of a duty imposed by *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5b58d0e9270ae511bbbafc88801bdb62] or under the Curriculum and Assessment (Wales) Act 2021.[^c-cfc461656df61559a175607d1ddef7e7] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-5b58d0e9270ae511bbbafc88801bdb62]

Regulations may prescribe circumstances in which subsection (2) does not apply in relation to tuition in singing or in playing a musical instrument. *(Extent: E)*

(4) Where the education is provided for the pupil outside school hours no charge shall be made in respect of it if it is—

- (a) required as part of a syllabus for a prescribed public examination which is a syllabus for which the pupil is being prepared at the school, or
- (b) provided in pursuance of a duty imposed by section 88[^c18515821] [^c-3cfcf414b4a13a216ef296eafb5f7a0d]... of the Education Act 2002 or section 69 of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^c15250561] or[^c-7b8100cbc5ef0bf6508ae9130c2b4626]
- (c) provided in pursuance of the duty imposed by section 7 of the Childcare Act 2006 , or[^c-c4aa813edc0926a33073d34cd62c0b8e]
- (d) provided in pursuance of a duty imposed by or under the Curriculum and Assessment (Wales) Act 2021.
