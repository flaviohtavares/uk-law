# Education Act 1996, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Primary schools, secondary schools and middle schools.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Act “**primary school**” means (subject to regulations under subsection (4)) a school for providing primary education, whether or not it also provides part-time education suitable to the requirements of junior pupils or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9e086b323661afa9f3995703fe47d442] further education.

(2) In this Act “**secondary school**” means (subject to regulations under subsection (4)) a school for providing secondary education, whether or not it also provides further education.

(3)[^key-a0fa5ecdb590ed6c538bcb0a5a11a38a] In this Act “**middle school**” means a school which, in pursuance of proposals published under any of the enactments specified in subsection (3A), has been established as, or altered so as to become, a school for providing full-time education suitable to the requirements of pupils who—

- (a) have attained a specified age below 10 years and six months, and
- (b) are under a specified age above 12 years.

(3A) The enactments mentioned in subsection (3) are—

- (a) in relation to England—
  - (i) section 28 or 28A of, or paragraph 5 of Schedule 7 to, the School Standards and Framework Act 1998,
  - (ii) section 66 of, or paragraph 7 of Schedule 11 to, the Education Act 2005, and
  - (iii) section 7, 10, 11 or 19 of the Education and Inspections Act 2006;
- (b) in relation to Wales,

  *Extent: W*

  - (i)[^key-130a3a2e2b0a32075339a656c1e338e0] section 28 of, or paragraph 5 of Schedule 7 to, the School Standards and Framework Act 1998, and[^key-f782ef53af14866181828649f998b160]

  *Extent: W*

  - (ii) section 48, 59 or 68 of the School Standards and Organisation (Wales) Act 2013

(4) The Secretary of State shall make regulations for determining, or enabling him to determine, whether a middle school is to be treated for the purposes of this Act and the other enactments relating to education as a primary school or as a secondary school.

(5) The powers conferred by the enactments mentioned in subsection (3) (so far as relating to the establishment of middle schools) and the powers conferred by subsection[^key-68b45bf62ac5063f63e714985361d8c0] (4) above are exercisable—

- (a) notwithstanding anything in this Act (and in particular section 1); but
- (b) without prejudice to the exercise of any other power conferred by this Act.
