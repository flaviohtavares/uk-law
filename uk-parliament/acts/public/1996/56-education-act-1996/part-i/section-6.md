## 6. Nursery schools ... .[^c-375b7a4f93c6908c85ed177694362f8c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A primary school is a nursery school if it is used wholly or[^c-25d000732757f5f84d5548013f339fd8] mainly for the purpose of providing education for children who have attained the age of two but are under compulsory school age[^c15241321].
