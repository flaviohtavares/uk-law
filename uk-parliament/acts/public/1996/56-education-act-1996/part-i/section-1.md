## 1. The stages of education.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The statutory system of public education consists of three progressive stages: primary education, secondary education and further education.

(2) This Part—

- (a) confers functions on the Secretary of State and local authorities[^c-df82d42709314c7296748c08b3554faa] with respect to primary, secondary and further education; [^c15241101]. . .
- (b)[^c15241101] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Part I of the [^c15241121]Further and Higher Education Act 1992 makes provision with respect to further education.[^c15241131].

(4)[^c-8c120bcd4e30bd872e7b4edefa91995e] Apart from section 10 (general duty of Secretary of State), nothing in this Act confers any functions with respect to higher education.
