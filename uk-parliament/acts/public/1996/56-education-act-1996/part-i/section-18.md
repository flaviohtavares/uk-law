## 18. Power to arrange provision of education at non-maintained schools.

*Extent: E+W*

A local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] may make arrangements for the provision of primary and secondary education for pupils at schools not maintained by them or another local authority.
