# Education Act 1996, section 562C

## 562C. Detained persons with statement of special educational needs[^key-31959a9a3bc9ef552adf830b7c0e93a6][^key-e7e116145194886f0e32d13b2a197268][^key-4d5b190cf3d82dd15b2272d1a46f06de]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where, immediately before the beginning of the detention, a local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-31959a9a3bc9ef552adf830b7c0e93a6] in Wales[^key-36c2257e685ea938277797ca27265a01] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-31959a9a3bc9ef552adf830b7c0e93a6] were maintaining a statement under section 324 for a detained person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-31959a9a3bc9ef552adf830b7c0e93a6]

(2) The authority must keep the statement while the person is detained in relevant youth accommodation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-31959a9a3bc9ef552adf830b7c0e93a6]

(3) The host authority must use best endeavours to secure that appropriate special educational provision is made for the detained person while the person is detained in relevant youth accommodation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-31959a9a3bc9ef552adf830b7c0e93a6]

(4) For the purposes of subsection (3), appropriate special educational provision is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-31959a9a3bc9ef552adf830b7c0e93a6]

- (a) the special educational provision that, immediately before the beginning of the detention, was specified in the statement, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-31959a9a3bc9ef552adf830b7c0e93a6]
- (b) educational provision corresponding as closely as practicable to the special educational provision so specified, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-31959a9a3bc9ef552adf830b7c0e93a6]
- (c) if it appears to the host authority that the special educational provision so specified is no longer appropriate for the person, such special educational provision as reasonably appears to the host authority to be appropriate for the person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-31959a9a3bc9ef552adf830b7c0e93a6]
