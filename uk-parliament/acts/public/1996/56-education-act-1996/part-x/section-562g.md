# Education Act 1996, section 562G

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/56

## 562G. Information to be provided where statement of special educational needs previously maintained[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba][^c-e7e116145194886f0e32d13b2a197268]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies in relation to a detained person if, immediately before the beginning of the detention, a local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba] in Wales[^c-20d468876abc7ab0fc8ed4ef68cd1ae3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba] were maintaining a statement under section 324 for the person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]

(2) Subsections (3) and (4) apply where the home authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba] , where they are a local authority in Wales,[^c-a9aa49e6e479e7a4d50a8a09b3be631a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba] become aware (whether by notice under section 39A(2) of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998 (detention of child or young person: local authorities to be notified) or otherwise)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]

- (a) that the person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]
  - (i) has become subject to a detention order, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba] is detained in relevant youth accommodation, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]
- (b) that the person has been transferred from one place of accommodation to another place of accommodation which is relevant youth accommodation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]

(3) If, immediately before the beginning of the detention, the home authority were maintaining the statement, they must send a copy of the statement to the host authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]

(4) If the home authority are or become aware that, immediately before the beginning of the detention, another local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba] in Wales[^c-e49a0d4cd7252250eada7f0ed7839386] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba] were maintaining a statement for the person under section 324, they must notify the host authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]

- (a) of that fact, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]
- (b) of the identity of that other local authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]

(5) The local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba] in Wales[^c-897a49aa8d9866ac1780c9f595b3f24d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba] who, immediately before the beginning of the detention, were maintaining the statement must, on a request by the host authority, send a copy of the statement to the host authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]

(6) Subsections (7) and (8) apply where the person is released from detention in relevant youth accommodation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]

(7) The host authority must notify the following of the person's release— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]

- (a) the home authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba] , where they are a local authority in Wales[^c-af2a1c3ad23e963b57f2255030a523f8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]
- (b) if different, the authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba] in Wales[^c-44b3c5f1953b025d70804e595f4bc6a5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba] who, immediately before the beginning of the detention, were maintaining the statement under section 324. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]

(8) If the home authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba], where they are a local authority in Wales[^c-99b0dc5a20baea5c27a5b4529d5ae35d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba] are not the authority who, immediately before the beginning of the detention, were maintaining the statement, the host authority must also notify the home authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]

- (a) of the fact that immediately before the beginning of the detention a statement was being maintained for the person by a local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba] in Wales[^c-4439cfe5c507e05d650c3cf970baf3e0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba] under section 324, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]
- (b) of the identity of that authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]

(9) Nothing in this section requires any local authority to notify another authority of any matter of which the other authority are already aware, or to send a copy of any statement to another authority who already have a copy of it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-88d9a86f9182b350bfdbf5169015c3ba]
