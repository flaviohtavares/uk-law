# Education Act 1996, section 562D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/56

## 562D. Appropriate special educational provision: arrangements between local authorities[^key-a8eba91d421d8e7f41896fde739d7d80][^key-e7e116145194886f0e32d13b2a197268][^key-062d27e760c356996de33ee6a2c9d7a4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where special educational provision is secured for a person in circumstances where section 562C applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8eba91d421d8e7f41896fde739d7d80]

(2) A local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8eba91d421d8e7f41896fde739d7d80] in Wales[^key-692e76df6d29071eace9285388bb3cb7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8eba91d421d8e7f41896fde739d7d80] may supply goods and services to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8eba91d421d8e7f41896fde739d7d80]

- (a) the host authority, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8eba91d421d8e7f41896fde739d7d80]
- (b) any other person making the special educational provision in question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8eba91d421d8e7f41896fde739d7d80]

(3) Goods and services may be supplied under subsection (2) only for the purpose of assisting the making or securing of that special educational provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a8eba91d421d8e7f41896fde739d7d80]
