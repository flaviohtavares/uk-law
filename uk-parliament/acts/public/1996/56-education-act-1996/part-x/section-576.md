## 576. Meaning of “parent”.[^key-204172708455abbb4c2dc99c76de6ce6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c15254561][^c15254571] In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires, “**parent**”, in relation to a child or young person, includes any person—

- (a) who is not a parent of his but who has parental responsibility for him, or
- (b) who has care of him,

except that in section 499(8)[^c15254581] it only includes such a person if he is an individual.

(2)[^c15254591] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In subsection (1) “**parental responsibility**” has the same meaning as in the [^c15254601]Children Act 1989.

(4) In determining for the purposes of subsection (1) whether an individual has care of a child or young person, any absence of the child or young person at a hospital or boarding school and any other temporary absence shall be disregarded.
