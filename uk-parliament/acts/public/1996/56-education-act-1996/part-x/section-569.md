# Education Act 1996, section 569

## 569. Regulations.[^c15254411][^c-2af21dd79b207ba21cb5d8b9cf3eb133]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any power of the Secretary of State or the Welsh Ministers *(Extent: W)*[^c-50fc9fea2c0a597c5c85e50e6382f856] to make regulations under this Act shall be exercised by statutory instrument.

(2) A statutory instrument containing regulations under this Act made by the Secretary of State *(Extent: W)*[^c-8c6f3f5ecc79b85b4299821086d68468], other than one falling within subsection (2A),[^c-f597d65871e41f6e2c1a885508ca8afe] [^c-f8009ac84b312712f25107b59a116fc9]..., shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(2A)[^c-de7957e9770181595f5c45d8f20b6060] A statutory instrument which contains (whether alone or with other provision) regulations under section 434B,[^c-ed5438ce4d595ea5f7d309c952b52def] 436B(6), 436C(1)(g), 436C(2), 436C(4), 436E(1)(a), 436E(7), 436E(9), 436F(1), 436F(2),[^c-de50051b2d424ab29bf7eb9564a87c34] 550ZA(3)(f) or 550ZC(7)[^c-a1bbe26d635ee6d94adce2c635458f5b] , or under paragraph 5 of Schedule 31A[^c-0f44cc6590bc3cf9e560b13cce4044e2] may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

*Extent: W*

(2B)[^c-a2838d2a6f3f9b477f7c2a2d2976e660] A statutory instrument containing *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-37a78c4078d7d7237fc74f70ae64fe48] regulations under sections 29(6A), *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-5e6f16a0aad1d81ee082c3246414f0d0] 332ZC, 332AA, 332BA, 332BB , 336,[^c-d0b669b40389e2d98542db99b39ec672] 397[^M_F_8c42f214-1f6d-4b1d-dda0-eee37cc658eb] , 436I(6)[^c-6ba0c3f1f2a930e56e01dd624fb6e948], 444A , 444B or (unless subsection (2BA) applies) Chapter 5A[^c-85af254faeea1d82a6210e6587cb44a5] , or under paragraph 6B or 6C of Schedule 1,[^M_F_618362d6-e30b-4c3d-bf28-ff431d8dede4] made by the Welsh Ministers is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of the National Assembly for Wales *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2108a99a48236c45dbde6a8f06d68009] are subject to the Senedd annulment procedure (see section 37E of the Legislation (Wales) Act 2019 (anaw 4)).

(2BA (Extent: W))[^c-3f3d844c4b88c45538e07a5fb52eab72] A statutory instrument which contains (whether alone or with other provision) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-72a1635559c2d9037b4123ea62a49522] *(Extent: W)* regulations made by the Welsh Ministers under both section 562J(4) and section 39(2) of the Additional Learning Needs and Education Tribunal (Wales) Act 2018 *(Extent: W)* may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by resolution of, the National Assembly for Wales *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9fa4cbdb4cc307121db7828455cad1be] *(Extent: W)* are subject to the Senedd approval procedure (see section 37C of the Legislation (Wales) Act 2019). *(Extent: W)*

(2BB (Extent: W)) A statutory instrument which contains (whether alone or with other provision) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-06fc328282b3a3668382bcac974d6a0f] *(Extent: W)* regulations under section *(Extent: W)* 434B or[^c-b2f3ac5194b3cdfd813ddfa8c0a404f6] *(Extent: W)* 436B(6), 436C(1)(g), 436C(2), 436C(4), 436E(1)(a), 436E(7), 436E(9), 436F(1), 436F(2) or[^c-e1cb877646b0950905830f6b959c28b8] *(Extent: W)* 579(3C) *(Extent: W)* , or under paragraph 5 of Schedule 31A[^c-b6b68469d558a63a274b446413703867] *(Extent: W)* may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by resolution of, the National Assembly for Wales may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by resolution of, the National Assembly for Wales *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc87620a23b3e935398a69a7c80c1889] *(Extent: W)* are subject to the Senedd approval procedure (see section 37C of the Legislation (Wales) Act 2019). *(Extent: W)*

(2C (Extent: W)) Paragraphs 33 to 35 of Schedule 11 to the Government of Wales Act 2006 make provision about the National Assembly for Wales procedures that apply to any statutory instrument containing regulations or an order made in exercise of functions conferred upon the Secretary of State or the National Assembly for Wales by this Act that have been transferred to the Welsh Ministers by virtue of paragraph 30 of that Schedule. *(Extent: W)*

(3)[^c-3987615c3a1c15fa7d66590f27a2f315] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Regulations under this Act may make different provision for different cases, circumstances or areas and may contain such incidental, supplemental, saving or transitional provisions as the Secretary of State thinks fit or the Welsh Ministers think fit *(Extent: W)*[^c-1dda179546a4ad76c47e1a645f098040].

(5) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (4), regulations under this Act may make in relation to Wales provision different from that made in relation to England.

(6) Subsection (5) does not apply to regulations under section 579(4).
