## 562H. Release of detained person appearing to host authority to require assessment[^c-af270f67007d08e44d0c8876f80b68c7][^c-e7e116145194886f0e32d13b2a197268]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies in relation to the release from detention in relevant youth accommodation of a detained person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af270f67007d08e44d0c8876f80b68c7]—[^c-77a11034e777a95c2dca8460e9c26c88] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af270f67007d08e44d0c8876f80b68c7]

- (a) in relation to whom section 562G does not apply *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af270f67007d08e44d0c8876f80b68c7], and[^c-8adeba68214898f3cbf11f45f0f2d202] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af270f67007d08e44d0c8876f80b68c7]
- (b) for whom the home authority are a local authority in Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af270f67007d08e44d0c8876f80b68c7]

(2) Subsection (3) applies where it appears to the host authority that the detained person will, on release, be a child within the meaning of Part 4. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af270f67007d08e44d0c8876f80b68c7]

(3) If the host authority are of the opinion that the person has, or may have, special educational needs, they must, on the person's release, notify the home authority of their opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af270f67007d08e44d0c8876f80b68c7]

(4) Subsection (6) applies[^c-0f4a1e9841b80e5ccff3073d50315633] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af270f67007d08e44d0c8876f80b68c7] where, on release, the detained person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af270f67007d08e44d0c8876f80b68c7]

- (a) will be over compulsory school age, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af270f67007d08e44d0c8876f80b68c7]
- (b) will cease to be of compulsory school age within one year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af270f67007d08e44d0c8876f80b68c7]

(5)[^c-9011d811c5c35933186ad00fe89f1f81] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) If— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af270f67007d08e44d0c8876f80b68c7]

- (a) the host authority are of the opinion that the person has, or may have, a learning difficulty (within the meaning of section 41 of the Learning and Skills Act 2000 (assessments relating to learning difficulties: Wales)) and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af270f67007d08e44d0c8876f80b68c7]
- (b)[^c-e957a945032b3e5d01610ec2ba3d54d7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

the host authority must, on the person's release, notify the Welsh Ministers of their opinion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-af270f67007d08e44d0c8876f80b68c7]
