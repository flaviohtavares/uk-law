# Education Act 1996, section 564

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 564. Certificates of birth and registrars’ returns.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the age of any person is required to be proved for the purposes of this Act or of any enactment relating to the employment of children or young persons, the registrar having the custody of the register of birth and deaths containing the entry relating to the birth of that person shall—

- (a) on being presented by any person (“**the applicant**”) with a written requisition in such form and containing such particulars as may be determined by regulations, and
- (b)[^c-09ded70b547f6217a2db0f846910b220] on payment of the appropriate fee,

supply the applicant with a copy of the entry certified under his hand.

(2) A registrar shall, on being requested so to do, supply free of charge a form of requisition for the purposes of subsection (1).

(3) A registrar shall supply to a local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] such particulars of the entries contained in any register of births and deaths in his custody, and in such form, as (subject to regulations) the authority may from time to time require for the purpose of the exercise of their education functions[^c-8e9f7d2771b4150db5b273f89cf9b0c6].

(4) In this section—

- “[^c-9eddd67e3705c151dc8e1ffae3d90c17]**the appropriate fee**” means the fee payable to the registrar having custody of the register concerned for a certified copy of an entry in the register by virtue of section 38A of the Births and Deaths Registration Act 1953;
- “**register of births and deaths**” means a register of births and deaths kept under that Act[^c-0047f9a49d6f5528fd8acf609a314c3c], and
- “**registrar**” includes a registrar of births and deaths and a superintendent registrar.
