## 560A. Work experience for persons over compulsory school age: England[^key-a2718015a8cd86d6994a5b1658a236a5][^key-23027649c18a6719ac081ccc1b53295a][^key-b0dc78a1b62a4a3410858ac27a53c304][^key-1e532050e655f46723f314d62fb726c9][^key-94dcac464f21d10c27a4665b1eca32e4][^key-a5df82da4af40089bed8dabd6e1158ef][^key-b38fc4577ef6940b9d1d8ce15af2fa19][^key-805ec8d5ef5f1213be3e3e38bf2a2b8f][^key-59cf5bf8640002e9e47ecccd63ffe49e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority[^key-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] in England may secure the provision of work experience for persons in their area—

- (a) who are over compulsory school age but under 19, or
- (b) who are aged 19 or over and for whom an EHC plan is maintained[^key-43b5fb08987cb1bb41b8cf56aaaee5a8].

(2) A local authority in England must—

- (a) encourage participation in work experience by persons in their area who are within subsection (1)(a) or (b);
- (b) encourage employers to participate in the provision of work experience for such persons.
