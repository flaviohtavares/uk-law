# Education Act 1996, section 410

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/56

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 410. Application of Part V in relation to nursery education.

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in this Part applies in relation to a nursery school or in relation to a nursery class in a primary school. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6e87e9f63c2ec25b976712ea68c41594]
