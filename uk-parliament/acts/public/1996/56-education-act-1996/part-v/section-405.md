# Education Act 1996, section 405

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 405. Exemption from sex education.[^c-7e37d602293b8cbc6fea3dbcce5efbab][^c-d446d8149c0c6662aee7135a13243e65][^c-eec4beb675f4c017f6c224381ab5d90a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-0591d81fb7264a0fdc93989ac3c86afc] If the parent of any pupil in attendance at a maintained school requests that he may be wholly or partly excused from receiving sex education at the school, the pupil shall, except so far as such education is comprised in the National Curriculum, be so excused accordingly until the request is withdrawn.

(2)[^c-8d8ffe479150a3bab2f0877589df63af] In subsection (1) the reference to sex education does not include sex education provided at a maintained school in England as part of statutory relationships and sex education.

(3) If the parent of any pupil in attendance at a maintained school in England requests that the pupil may be wholly or partly excused from sex education provided as part of statutory relationships and sex education, the pupil must be so excused until the request is withdrawn, unless or to the extent that the head teacher considers that the pupil should not be so excused.

(4) In this section “statutory relationships and sex education” means education required to be provided at a school in England under section 80(1)(d) of the Education Act 2002.
