## 408. Provision of information.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Regulations may require, in relation to every maintained school, the local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41], the governing body or the head teacher to make available either generally or to prescribed persons, in such form and manner and at such times as may be prescribed—

- (a) such information (including information as to the matters mentioned in subsection (2)) relevant for the purposes of any of the relevant provisions of this Part or Part V of the Education Act 1997[^c15248931] or section 96 of the Learning and Skills Act 2000[^c15248941] or the relevant provisions of the Education Act 2002[^c18409501] or the provisions of[^c-c11f6e6e7f2c4bf114ca82f5e12af0db] Part 7[^c-1c3cd8641d7df8c9bb73b36461b90073] of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 or the provisions of the Qualifications Wales Act 2015[^c-4372536fc901256a9885c17576c9634d] or the provisions of the Curriculum and Assessment (Wales) Act 2021[^c-91aae5bb308a28611e84c47cb2ffa4e1], and
- (b)[^c15248951] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

as may be prescribed.

(2) The matters referred to in subsection (1)(a) are—

- (a) the curriculum for maintained schools,
- (b) the educational provision made by the school for pupils at the school and any syllabuses to be followed by those pupils,
- (c) the educational achievements of pupils at the school (including the results of any assessments of those pupils, whether under this Part or otherwise, for the purpose of ascertaining those achievements), and
- (d) the educational achievements of such classes or descriptions of pupils[^c15248961] as may be prescribed (including results of the kind mentioned in paragraph (c)). , and[^c15248971]
- (e) arrangements relating to relevant[^c-7d3f2d1189e752fcd14c5433f4d0c5ee] qualifications (within the meaning given by section 96(5) of the Learning and Skills Act 2000) which are approved under section 98[^c-8c86cf69cb82b16946b8e6be8592dcd2] [^c-2dcc4b8d859a8c59c0147609b54ad584]... of that Act and to courses leading to such qualifications.
- (f)[^c-4e7ad0934c113b70d79c951b458213e0] arrangements relating to qualifications within the meaning given in section 56 of the Qualifications Wales Act 2015 which are approved under Part 4 of that Act and to courses of education or training leading to such qualifications.

(3)[^c15248991] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) For the purposes of subsection (1) the relevant provisions of this Part are—

- (a)[^c18456061] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c15249001] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c15249001] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) sections 390[^c15249011] to 392;
- (e) sections 394 to 396A[^c-9fe715df6454f889dac25e19bc546f04];
- (f) sections 398 [^c15249021]... and 405; [^c-9d5635bfc22010cb1b8509d3a9fc0508]...
- (g) section 409 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-95c1f81948f27d72de4e4cd03509e5c7] ; [^c-f09fb68b181c0c0218f47b4aedd48638]...
- (h)[^c-f09fb68b181c0c0218f47b4aedd48638] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4A)[^c18456071] For the purposes of subsection (1) the relevant provisions of the Education Act 2002 are—

- (a) Part 6 (the curriculum in England), [^c-b2b45c594494dee9be6c3fce0f552329]...
- (b)[^c-b2b45c594494dee9be6c3fce0f552329] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) Before making any regulations under this section, the Secretary of State shall consult any persons with whom consultation appears to him to be desirable.

(6) Regulations under this section shall not require information as to the results of an individual pupil’s assessment (whether under Part 6[^c18456151] [^c-ad31daa014c156f7ce2371c6600ea92f]... of the Education Act 2002 or Part 4 of the Curriculum and Assessment (Wales) Act 2021[^c-0e25cecd53ab9e9d233600275e0fab58] or otherwise) to be made available to any persons other than—

- (a) the parents of the pupil concerned,
- (b) the pupil concerned,
- (c) in the case of a pupil who has transferred to a different school, the head teacher of that school,
- (d) the governing body of the school, or
- (e) the local authority;

[^c-b881f5810bd2e526caa2c19946720f79]...

(6A)[^c-9946e9166ca3651a62a3eb941f103d10] Regulations under this section shall not require information as is mentioned in subsection (6) to be made available—

- (a) to a governing body except where relevant for the purposes of the performance of any of their functions;
- (b) to a head teacher except where relevant for the purposes of the performance of any of the head teacher’s functions;
- (c) to a local authority except where relevant for the purposes of the performance of any of their education functions.

(7) Regulations under this section may authorise local authorities[^c-df82d42709314c7296748c08b3554faa], governing bodies and head teachers to make a charge (not exceeding the cost of supply) for any documents supplied by them in pursuance of the regulations.

(8) In relation to any maintained school, the local authority and the governing body shall exercise their functions with a view to securing that the head teacher complies with any regulations made under this section.

(8A)[^c-b37833c0282e5e7005386baedc29ba6d] In exercising their functions under subsection (1), the Welsh Ministers must have regard to the desirability of information being available to parents and others about whether, and if so how, any parts of the curriculum and any educational provision at maintained schools (other than maintained nursery schools) promote the purpose of the Violence against Women, Domestic Abuse and Sexual Violence (Wales) Act 2015 (see section 1 of that Act).

(9)[^c-aa5696393eb413197cb5c54e12158dc3] In this section “**maintained school**” includes a maintained nursery school.
