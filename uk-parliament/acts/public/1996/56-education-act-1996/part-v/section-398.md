# Education Act 1996, section 398

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 398. No requirement of attendance at Sunday school etc.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-99f56a81e9ff82c41d4251c354fe6c71] It shall not be required, as a condition of—

- (a) a pupil attending a maintained school, or
- (b) a person attending such a school to receive further education or any training for members of the school workforce[^key-83810a23ad69eebeb8a86748692611e4] ,

that he must attend or abstain from attending a Sunday school or a place of religious worship.

(2)[^key-31aa08f36d7e49803cad79638948dad2] In subsection (1)(b), the reference to training for members of the school workforce is to be read in accordance with sections 96(1) and 100[^key-de5d38f350ee7561fd1eeaf232052c0b] of the Education Act 2005.
