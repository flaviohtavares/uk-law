## 409. Complaints and enforcement: maintained schools in Wales.[^c15249181][^c-383757fb797adfe7eb8c89ddb002fecf]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] A local education authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] in Wales[^c-7aa3b3f4d43e7898c3c9f5c676d6a475] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] shall, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] [^c18461471]... after consultation with governing bodies *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] of foundation and voluntary aided schools[^c15249191] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] in Wales *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4], make arrangements for the consideration and disposal of any complaint to which subsection (2) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4]

(2) This subsection applies to any complaint which is to the effect that the authority, or the governing body of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] any community, foundation or voluntary school maintained by the authority[^c15249201] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] , any maintained nursery school so maintained[^c-d8eaeb7d1400e2e2b71c15027adfac36] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] or any community *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] or foundation special *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-62ececada804d9422a19836a32f57638] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] school *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] so maintained which is not established in a hospital— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4]

- (a) have acted or are proposing to act unreasonably in relation to the exercise of a power conferred on them by or under a relevant enactment, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4]
- (b) have acted or are proposing to act unreasonably in relation to the performance of, or have failed to discharge, a duty imposed on them by or under a relevant enactment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4]

(3) In subsection (2) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4]**relevant enactment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4]

- (a) any provision which by virtue of section 408(4) is a relevant provision of this Part for the purposes of section 408(1), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] [^c-95d2acad7767e56872de9b07b36eb151]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4]
- (b) any other enactment (whether contained in this Part *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] or the Curriculum and Assessment (Wales) Act 2021[^c-2b9549c0df32bb0e35a48f3b5d2e6774] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] or otherwise) so far as relating to the curriculum for, or religious worship in, maintained schools *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] [^c15249211]....

(4) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] Welsh Ministers[^c-60f0c3b348b5089d5f83dccc8bf9e850] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] shall not entertain under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] section 496 (power to prevent unreasonable exercise of functions) or 497 (powers where a local education authority or governing body fail to discharge their duties) *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-20f5cc18b6a5e621f05469961af6a438] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] Chapter 1 or 2 of Part 2 of the School Standards and Organisation (Wales) Act 2013 (intervention in conduct of maintained schools and local authorities) *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4] any complaint to which subsection (2) applies, unless a complaint concerning the same matter has been made and disposed of in accordance with arrangements made under subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_394f486a-e8cc-4c59-91be-b64f33a3c7a4]
