## 512ZA. Power to charge for meals etc.[^key-9d0e84b41fdcfa85100756123eda3ca6][^key-9db060f1e807ec0c7538e1743b95f495]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-a2362bbf1a7c026e19a2a164c91a5967] A local authority[^key-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] may[^key-97ffe513d289d84569a034bd7ec01688] charge for anything provided by them under subsection (1) or (3) of section 512.

(1A)[^key-26ee751907b31401945a355d5618d91a] Where a local authority in England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cfc51a535aad3798d3c5c242741f7f88] exercise the power to charge under subsection (1), the price they charge for an item must not exceed the cost of providing that item.

(2)[^key-a2362bbf1a7c026e19a2a164c91a5967][^key-b55eb07a51bdbe230d2d224540412822] Where a[^key-5746a69609c643e0b7554fe4ae14310c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b55eb07a51bdbe230d2d224540412822] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b55eb07a51bdbe230d2d224540412822] exercise the power to charge under subsection (1), they must *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b55eb07a51bdbe230d2d224540412822] charge every person the same price for the same quantity of the same item. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b55eb07a51bdbe230d2d224540412822]

(3) This section is subject to section 512ZB.
