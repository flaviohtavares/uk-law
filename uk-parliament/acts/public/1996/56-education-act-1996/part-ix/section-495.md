# Education Act 1996, section 495

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (69).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 495. Determination of disputes.[^c15251491][^c-56f00ec6623f3f267774f7282c90b477]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c15251501][^c-74ef4cb1ed0ee626fcaffba08c66721b] Except where this Act expressly provides otherwise, any dispute between a local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] and the governing body of a school as to the exercise of any power conferred or the performance of any duty imposed by or under this Act may be referred to the Secretary of State (despite any enactment which makes the exercise of the power or the performance of the duty contingent upon the opinion of the authority or of the governing body).

(2)[^c-74ef4cb1ed0ee626fcaffba08c66721b] The Secretary of State shall determine any dispute referred to him under subsection (1).

(3) Any dispute between two or more local authorities as to which of them is responsible for the provision of education for any pupil shall be determined by the Secretary of State.
