# Education Act 1996, section 529

## 529. Power to accept gifts on trust for educational purposes.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] may accept, hold and administer any property on trust for purposes connected with education.

(1A)[^c-c12378cc65970f98d4ee3ab9b861f8ce] Any intention on the part of a local authority in England that a school should be vested in the authority as trustees shall be treated for the purposes of sections 7 and 10[^c-874818c9ffd2d48cd00517ecf2cb05f0] of the Education and Inspections Act 2006 as an intention to establish a new community school, community special school or maintained nursery school (so that proposals for that purpose shall be published in accordance with those sections); and Schedule 2 to that Act (proposals for establishment or discontinuance of schools in England) shall apply accordingly.

(2) Any intention on the part of a local authority in Wales[^c-53b6cf884e56740b12858ed080284081] that a school [^c-b0eb979260564ad49a54ab5ac04c9b50]... should be vested in the authority as trustees shall be treated for for the purposes of sections[^c-889f88ccaca3e1719e1b4068a7ef03e2] 28 and 31 of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-792722805962088de5216043b39eba07] 41 and 44 of the School Standards and Organisation (Wales) Act 2013 *(Extent: W)* as an intention to establish a new community school, community special school or maintained nursery school (so that proposals for that purpose shall be published as required by those sections); and Schedule 6 to that Act (statutory proposals concerning schools in Wales *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-21b65f311667322b73adf574f80f848e] and sections 48 to 55 of, and Schedule 3 to, that Act (school organisation proposals *(Extent: W)* : procedure and implementation) shall apply accordingly.

(3) Any school which in accordance with subsection (1A) or[^c-80498988b15b64d028c834a5f5cacc1a] (2) is vested in a local authority as trustees shall be a community school[^c15253131]. , a community special school or a maintained nursery school.[^c-5d849c201a4dd14a3ba9bd2de4f49f23]
