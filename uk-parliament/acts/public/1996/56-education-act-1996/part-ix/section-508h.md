## 508H. Guidance: sections 508F and 508G[^c-5d50d94044696f4d87eddb6cfd876d19]

*Extent: E+W*

In making arrangements under section 508F(1) and preparing and publishing a statement under section 508G, a local authority[^c-6f7ad3e2cc8ee9e0ff2977c70c6c2e41] must have regard to any guidance issued by the Secretary of State under this section.
