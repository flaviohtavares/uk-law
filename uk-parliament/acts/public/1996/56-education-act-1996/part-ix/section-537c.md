# Education Act 1996, section 537C

## 537C. Disclosure of information by or to principal regulators of exempt charities[^c-34ac9b31dafa74c7f56869809a811a67]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Nothing in any provision made by or under section 537, 537A or 537B requires or authorises the provision of information—

- (a) by the principal regulator of an exempt charity (within the meaning of section 25 of the Charities Act 2011)[^c-cd22cd2a02a3da00029f1f2cb7455c7f], if the information was received by that principal regulator in its capacity as such; or
- (b) to the principal regulator of an exempt charity in its capacity as such.

(2) Nothing in subsection (1) prevents the disclosure of information under section 56 or 57 of the Charities Act 2011[^c-46d94e9b207961f7e1590f4c2f84ccab].
