# Reserve Forces Act 1996, section 127

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 127. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act—

“**additional duties commitment**” means a commitment under section 25;

“**call-out order**” has the meaning given by section 64;

“the ex-regular reserve forces” has the meaning given by section 2(2);

“[^key-d5e41ed7c770153bee1db2a079b925fc]**full-time service commitment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5e41ed7c770153bee1db2a079b925fc]**” means a commitment under section 24; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-d5e41ed7c770153bee1db2a079b925fc]

“man” (except in Part VII) has the meaning given by section 2(4);

[^c8319931]. . .

“**prescribed**” means (except in Part VII) prescribed by orders or regulations under section 4;

“recall” and “recall order” have the meanings given by section 77;

[^key-7bb2b2bb6155041c4e644747d8ed2478]...

“[^key-bc17d57c9ed4cd026b8268d0e5a74a4c]**the regular army**” has the meaning given by section 374 of the Armed Forces Act 2006;

“**regular services**” means the Royal Navy, the Royal Marines, the regular army or the Royal Air Force[^key-86904dde9d041d778a3a8dcc10781996];

“**the reserve forces**”, “**the reserve naval and marine forces**”, “**the reserve land forces**” and “**the reserve air forces**” have the meaning given by section 1(2);

“**reserve marine force**” means the Royal Marines Reserve;

“[^key-29d87d71c7627a88d6c18fba2983fcc7] **section 24 commitment** ” means a commitment under section 24;

“[^key-7a4424dbc5367898a31e373ae8ac1c02]**service offence**” has the meaning given by section 50 of the Armed Forces Act 2006;

“**special member**” means a member of a reserve force who is, by virtue of Part V, a special member of that force;

“term of compulsory service” has the meaning given by section 13(7);

“the volunteer reserve forces” has the meaning given by section 2(3).

(2) Any reference in this Act to a member of a reserve force or a member of the reserve forces is to an officer in, or a man of, that force or any of those forces, as the case may be.

(3)[^key-db834edadbdabdc8e0c9a714fc5377a1] The officer who is the “commanding officer” of a person for the purposes of any provision of this Act shall be determined by or under regulations made by the Defence Council under this subsection.

(4) Section 164(2) and (3) of the Armed Forces Act 2006 apply in relation to section 95(2)(a) of, and paragraph 5(3) of Schedule 1 to, this Act.
