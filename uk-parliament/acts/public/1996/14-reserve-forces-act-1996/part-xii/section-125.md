# Reserve Forces Act 1996, section 125

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 125. Absence for voting.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No member of a reserve force shall be liable, when not in permanent service, to any penalty or punishment on account of his absence from duty in the United Kingdom for voting at—

- (a) any election of a Member of Parliament a Member of the Scottish Parliament[^c8319901] , a Member of the National Assembly for Wales,[^c8319911] or a Member of the Northern Ireland Assembly[^c-a5a83973975e8f697401a9c94c050c50], or
- (b) any local election,

or going to or returning from such voting.
