## 90. Appointment of panel of chairmen.[^c8319521]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c8319531][^c8319541] There shall be a panel of chairmen of reserve forces appeal tribunals appointed by the Lord Chancellor [^c8319551]. . ..

(1A)[^key-52b71313009ab0358e70bfdfb9774bbd] The Lord Chancellor may not appoint a member of the panel unless the appropriate senior judge concurs.

(2) No person may be appointed to the panel unless he is—

- (a) a person who has a 10 year general qualification (within the meaning of section 71 of the [^c8319561]Courts and Legal Services Act 1990);
- (b) an advocate or solicitor in Scotland of at least 10 years’ standing; or
- (c) a member of the Bar of Northern Ireland or solicitor of the Court of Judicature of Northern Ireland[^key-a5af3edeeb66a6093f6521b31c2e746d] of at least 10 years’ standing.

(3)[^c8319531][^c8319541] The appointment of a person to the panel shall be for such term as may be determined by the Lord Chancellor [^c8319551]. . ..

(4) A member of the panel shall vacate his office on the day on which he attains the age of 75[^key-2297eb1ecbae1fd73721ea1f644cafdc].

(5) There shall be paid to members of the panel such fees, allowances and expenses (if any) as the Secretary of State may determine.
