## 105. Trial of offences by civil court.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any offence to which this section applies is (as well as being triable by the Court Martial)[^c-441a8478a53465497a2e8c4dd4aae8da] triable summarily by a civil court and punishable with imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months or a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale (or both).

(2)[^c-a46111c31bae4cfa884de966650806ad] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-6dcb2d0611d9c2348cbce3498a3f73cf] This section applies to any service offence (other than an offence under this Act or an offence mentioned in section 98(1))[^c-cb6c71c16d03be61c7518abadaae230a] committed by a member of a reserve force when not in permanent service under Part IV or Part V or under a call-out order.
