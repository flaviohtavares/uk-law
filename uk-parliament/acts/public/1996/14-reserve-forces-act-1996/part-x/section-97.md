# Reserve Forces Act 1996, section 97

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. Failure to attend for duty or training.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A member of a reserve force who has entered into a full-time service *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-b95b510ac2bf37e3c00a2c4f23bcbe34] section 24 commitment or an additional duties commitment and, without leave lawfully granted or reasonable excuse, fails to appear at the time and place at which he is required to attend—

- (a) in the case of a full-time service *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-00b2188a83a3672def202fd657d118d7] section 24 commitment, to begin the period of full-time *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-83942a3e77f1b623b5c7efbc57496bac] service contemplated by the commitment;
- (b) in the case of an additional duties commitment, to begin a period of service under the commitment,

is guilty of desertion (if section 8(2)(a) or (b) of the Armed Forces Act 2006 applies to him) or absence without leave (if neither of those provisions applies to him).[^c-248ea13bd77f8a8915e5b84cba5da34f]

(2) A member of a reserve force who—

- (a) is required to undergo a period of training in accordance with section 22, a special agreement or an employee agreement (or any other requirement applicable to special members), and
- (b) fails, without leave lawfully granted or reasonable excuse, to appear at any time and place at which he is required to attend,

is guilty of absence without leave.

(3) An offence under this section is triable summarily by a civil court (as well as being triable by the Court Martial).[^c-23e51668fb8cc0d58ced87581f48e7fe]
