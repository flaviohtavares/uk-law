## 107. Time for institution of proceedings.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-9ac2fe7a5a96b64b86f425d286025a4f] Proceedings against a person before [^key-93f47cba354162da23112924a7e70077]...

- (a)[^key-93f47cba354162da23112924a7e70077] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) a civil court,

in respect of an offence under this Act or a service offence[^key-f67784bf0266d182e4fe6da500d72c1e] and alleged to have been committed by him during his period of service in a reserve force may be instituted whether or not he has ceased to be a member of that reserve force.

(2) Such proceedings may, notwithstanding anything in any other enactment, be instituted at any time within 2 months after—

- (a) the time at which the offence becomes known to his commanding officer; or
- (b) the time at which he is apprehended,

whichever is later.

(3)[^key-9fbc7fd679719d49a8091560e97428e8][^key-56b8c1ce6f989367e7fcf43b30860956] Subsection (2) applies to proceedings for an offence under section 96(1) committed by a person liable to recall as it applies to proceedings mentioned in subsection (1) of this section.
