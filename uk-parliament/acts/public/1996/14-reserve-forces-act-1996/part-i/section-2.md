# Reserve Forces Act 1996, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Membership of the reserve forces.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The reserve forces shall each consist of officers and men.

(2) The men of the Royal Fleet Reserve, the Regular Reserve[^key-85c8f4d18dab50242e6e5c40ebacdd8c] and the Air Force Reserve (referred to in this Act as “the ex-regular reserve forces”) may only be—

- (a) men transferred to that force under regulations made under section 331 of the Armed Forces Act 2006;[^key-9b0b563e2bc5e9d21505da048b80906d]
- (b) men enlisted or re-engaged in that force.

(3) The men of the Royal Naval Reserve, the Royal Marines Reserve, the Army Reserve[^key-d33cdaf006f8a5629a1ffd9c03fe5b52] and the Royal Auxiliary Air Force (referred to in this Act as “the volunteer reserve forces”) may only be men enlisted or re-engaged in that force.

(4) In this Act, any reference (however expressed) to a man of any of the reserve forces is a reference to a person of either sex who is a member of that force and is of or below the rate or rank of warrant officer.
