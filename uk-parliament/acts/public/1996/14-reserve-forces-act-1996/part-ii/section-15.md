# Reserve Forces Act 1996, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Discharge by commanding officer.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A man of a reserve force may be discharged by his commanding officer[^key-bb2a705a5ac8da6f3fad85eb0e48421d], in such manner and on such grounds as may be prescribed.

(2) A man discharged by his commanding officer may appeal to the Defence Council, who may give such directions in his case (including a direction annulling the discharge) as they consider appropriate.
