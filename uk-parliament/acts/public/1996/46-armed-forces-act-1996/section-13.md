# Armed Forces Act 1996, section 13

> Text as at 2014-03-10.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Application of Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The [^c1127419]Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 shall be amended as follows.

(2) In section 2 (rehabilitation of persons dealt with in service disciplinary proceedings), subsections (2) to (4) shall cease to have effect.

(3) In section 6 (the rehabilitation period)— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7901fbe8b3c21a0f7998e304bdc16fae]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7901fbe8b3c21a0f7998e304bdc16fae] in subsection (6) the words “Subject to subsection (7) below" shall cease to have effect; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7901fbe8b3c21a0f7998e304bdc16fae]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7901fbe8b3c21a0f7998e304bdc16fae] after subsection (6)(b) there shall be inserted the following paragraph— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7901fbe8b3c21a0f7998e304bdc16fae]

  > - (bb (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7901fbe8b3c21a0f7998e304bdc16fae] any conviction in service disciplinary proceedings for an offence listed in the Schedule to this Act; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7901fbe8b3c21a0f7998e304bdc16fae]

  ; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7901fbe8b3c21a0f7998e304bdc16fae]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7901fbe8b3c21a0f7998e304bdc16fae] in subsection (7) for the words “Notwithstanding subsection (6) above" there shall be substituted the words *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7901fbe8b3c21a0f7998e304bdc16fae] “ Except as provided by subsection (6)(bb) above ” *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7901fbe8b3c21a0f7998e304bdc16fae]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7901fbe8b3c21a0f7998e304bdc16fae]

(4) The provisions set out in Schedule 4 to this Act shall be inserted as the Schedule to the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7901fbe8b3c21a0f7998e304bdc16fae] [^c1127420]Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7901fbe8b3c21a0f7998e304bdc16fae]

(5) The Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 shall apply to convictions before the commencement date as if the amendments made by this section had always had effect; but this subsection does not apply to convictions to which section 2(1) of that Act applied before that date.

(6) Where by virtue of subsection (5) above a rehabilitation period applicable to a conviction would have ended before the commencement date, the individual concerned shall (subject to section 1(2) of the Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974) be treated as a rehabilitated person in respect of the conviction, and the conviction shall be regarded as spent, on and after that date.

(7) In this section “**the commencement date**” means the date on which this section comes into force.
