## 24. Equal treatment: Great Britain.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] Section 1(9) of the *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] [^c1127490]Equal Pay Act 1970 (exclusion of armed forces from requirement of equal treatment for women and men in same employment) shall cease to have effect. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] For section 7 of that Act (service pay) there shall be substituted the following section— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]

>
> (1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] Sections 1 and 6 above shall apply, with the modifications mentioned in subsection (2) below and any other necessary modifications, to service by a woman in any of the armed forces as they apply to employment by a private person. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
>
> (2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] In the application of those sections to service by a woman in any of the armed forces— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
>
> - (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] references to a contract of employment shall be regarded as references to the terms of service; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
> - (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] in section 1, in subsection (6), paragraph (c) and the words “or any associated employer" and subsections (8) to (11) (which have no application) shall be omitted; and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
> - (c (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] references to an equality clause shall be regarded as referring to a corresponding term of service capable of requiring the terms of service applicable in her case to be treated as modified or as including other terms. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
>
> (3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] Any claim in respect of the contravention of a term of service modified or included, in relation to a woman’s service in any of the armed forces, by a term corresponding to an equality clause in a contract of employment (including a claim for arrears of pay or damages in respect of the contravention) may be presented by way of complaint to an *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] employment tribunal[^c1127491] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
>
> Any such contravention shall be regarded for the purposes of a claim under this subsection as if it were a breach of contract. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
>
> (4 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] Subsections (5) to (10) below apply in relation to any claim by a woman (“the claimant") arising from a contravention of a term of service referred to in subsection (3) above. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
>
> (5 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] No complaint in respect of the claim shall be presented to an *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] employment tribunal *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] unless— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
>
> - (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] the claimant has made a complaint to an officer under the service redress procedures applicable to her and has submitted that complaint to the Defence Council under those procedures; and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
> - (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] the Defence Council have made a determination with respect to the complaint. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
>
> (6 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] Regulations may make provision enabling a complaint in respect of the claim to be presented to an *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] employment tribunal *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] in such circumstances as may be specified by the regulations, notwithstanding that subsection (5) above would otherwise preclude its presentation. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
>
> (7 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] Where a complaint is presented to an *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] employment tribunal *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] by virtue of regulations under subsection (6) above, the service redress procedures may continue after the complaint is presented. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
>
> (8 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] No complaint in respect of the claim shall be presented to an *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] employment tribunal *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] if the period of service during which the claim arose ended more than nine months before the date of the presentation of the complaint to the tribunal. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
>
> (9 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] A woman shall not be entitled, in proceedings on a complaint in respect of the claim, to be awarded any payment by way of arrears of pay or damages in respect of a time earlier than two years before the date on which her complaint under the service redress procedures was made. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
>
> (10 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] Section 2A above shall apply in relation to a complaint in respect of the claim as it applies to a complaint presented to an *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] employment tribunal *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] under section 2(1) above. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
>
> (11 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] Regulations under subsection (6) above shall be made by statutory instrument which shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
>

> - “ *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]**the service redress procedures *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]**” means the procedures, excluding those which relate to the making of a report on a complaint to Her Majesty, referred to in section 180 of the *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] [^c1127497]Army Act 1955, section 180 of the *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] [^c1127498]Air Force Act 1955 and section 130 of the *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920] [^c1127499]Naval Discipline Act 1957. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cbd9f94081a587646b8010c6785b5920]
