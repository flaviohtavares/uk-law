# Family Law Act 1996, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-03-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Rights concerning home where one spouse or civil partner has no estate, etc.[^c20996821]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) one spouse or civil partner (“A”)[^c20994631] is entitled to occupy a dwelling-house by virtue of—
  - (i) a beneficial estate or interest or contract; or
  - (ii) any enactment giving A[^c20994791] the right to remain in occupation; and
- (b) the other spouse or civil partner (“B”)[^c20994831] is not so entitled.

(2) Subject to the provisions of this Part, B[^c20994871] has the following rights (“home rights”[^c20994901])—

- (a) if in occupation, a right not to be evicted or excluded from the dwelling-house or any part of it by A[^c20994921] except with the leave of the court given by an order under section 33;
- (b) if not in occupation, a right with the leave of the court so given to enter into and occupy the dwelling-house.

(3) If B[^c20994941] is entitled under this section to occupy a dwelling-house or any part of a dwelling-house, any payment or tender made or other thing done by B in or towards satisfaction of any liability of A[^c20994971] in respect of rent, mortgage payments or other outgoings affecting the dwelling-house is, whether or not it is made or done in pursuance of an order under section 40, as good as if made or done by A.

(4) B's[^c20995021] occupation by virtue of this section—

- (a) is to be treated, for the purposes of the [^c12122501]Rent (Agriculture) Act 1976 and the [^c12122511]Rent Act 1977 (other than Part V and sections 103 to 106 of that Act), as occupation by A as A's[^c20995041] residence, and
- (b) if B occupies the dwelling-house as B's[^c20995101] only or principal home, is to be treated, for the purposes of the [^c12122521]Housing Act 1985 , Part I of the Housing Act 1988[^c12122531] , Chapter 1 of Part 5 of the Housing Act 1996[^key-ab20ebb8fcf6e6c208555b8ee4b69a0c] ,[^key-26f3315e5d8c3931891977132943f12f] the Prevention of Social Housing Fraud Act 2013 and the Renting Homes (Wales) Act[^key-46098a4d0e88c37125cff55c9209914c] [2016 (anaw 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/1), as occupation by A as A's[^c20995121] only or principal home.

(5) If B[^c20995161])—

- (a) is entitled under this section to occupy a dwelling-house or any part of a dwelling-house, and
- (b) makes any payment in or towards satisfaction of any liability of A[^c20995841] in respect of mortgage payments affecting the dwelling-house,

the person to whom the payment is made may treat it as having been made by A[^c20995261], but the fact that that person has treated any such payment as having been so made does not affect any claim of B against A[^c20995301] to an interest in the dwelling-house by virtue of the payment.

(6) If B[^c20995901] is entitled under this section to occupy a dwelling-house or part of a dwelling-house by reason of an interest of A[^c20995921] under a trust, all the provisions of subsections (3) to (5) apply in relation to the trustees as they apply in relation to A.

(7) This section does not apply to a dwelling-house which—[^c20995961]

- (a) in the case of spouses, has at no time been, and was at no time intended by them to be, a matrimonial home of theirs; and
- (b) in the case of civil partners, has at no time been, and was at no time intended by them to be, a civil partnership home of theirs.

(8) B’s home rights[^c20995991] continue—

- (a) only so long as the marriage or civil partnership[^c20996011] subsists, except to the extent that an order under section 33(5) otherwise provides; and
- (b) only so long as A[^c20996031] is entitled as mentioned in subsection (1) to occupy the dwelling-house, except where provision is made by section 31 for those rights to be a charge on an estate or interest in the dwelling-house.

(9) It is hereby declared that a person[^c20996731]—

- (a) who has an equitable interest in a dwelling-house or in its proceeds of sale, but
- (b) is not a person in whom there is vested (whether solely or as joint tenant) a legal estate in fee simple or a legal term of years absolute in the dwelling-house,

is to be treated, only for the purpose of determining whether he has home rights[^c20996801], as not being entitled to occupy the dwelling-house by virtue of that interest.
