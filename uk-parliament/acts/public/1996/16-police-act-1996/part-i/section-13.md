## 13. Other members of police forces.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-560f3692cfca8650d660ca6d0781343a] The ranks that may be held in a police force maintained under section 2 shall be such as may be prescribed by regulations under section 50 and the ranks so prescribed shall include, in addition to chief constable *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-560f3692cfca8650d660ca6d0781343a] , deputy chief constable[^c18805561] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-560f3692cfca8650d660ca6d0781343a] and assistant chief constable, the ranks of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-560f3692cfca8650d660ca6d0781343a] chief superintendent[^c18805571] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-560f3692cfca8650d660ca6d0781343a] superintendent, chief inspector, inspector, sergeant and constable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-560f3692cfca8650d660ca6d0781343a]

(2) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Appointments and promotions to any rank below that of assistant chief constable in any police force maintained under section 2 shall be made, in accordance with regulations under section 50, by the chief constable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-560f3692cfca8650d660ca6d0781343a]
