## 23B. Collaboration agreements: payments[^c-0a484dd46d77de1090776766308edb39][^c-0d80583ecb11921312f235e3b9a88fc9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A collaboration agreement may provide for payments between parties to the agreement[^c-1f70159265b0bb6aaf19746d28297310].

(2) Provision under subsection (1) may in the case of policing bodies or chief officers of police who are parties to the agreement[^c-95bfe4c646d7d298cd733ea7fd2a618c], in particular—

- (a) specify the policing bodies or chief officers of police[^c-572ce7b051bf1402a651a0bea59139bb] by which and to which a payment is to be made or the manner in which those policing bodies or chief officers of police are to be determined;
- (b) specify the amount of any payment or the manner in which it is to be determined.

(3) A policing body or chief officer of police[^c-2e9e14536d02052d45c10e712fa5f2f2] must make any payments required by provision made under subsection (1).
