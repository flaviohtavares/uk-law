# Police Act 1996, section 9H

## 9H. Other members of the metropolitan police force.[^c18802831]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-501a64cbd36865abc1477d70ae6b9bc9] The ranks that may be held in the metropolitan police force shall be such as may be prescribed by regulations under section 50. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-501a64cbd36865abc1477d70ae6b9bc9]

(2) The ranks so prescribed in the case of the metropolitan police force shall include, in addition to the ranks of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-501a64cbd36865abc1477d70ae6b9bc9]

- (a) Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-501a64cbd36865abc1477d70ae6b9bc9]
- (b) Deputy Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-501a64cbd36865abc1477d70ae6b9bc9]
- (c) Assistant Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-501a64cbd36865abc1477d70ae6b9bc9]
- (ca (affected by repeal, see note))[^c18804411][^c-501a64cbd36865abc1477d70ae6b9bc9] Deputy Assistant Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-501a64cbd36865abc1477d70ae6b9bc9]
- (d) Commander, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-501a64cbd36865abc1477d70ae6b9bc9]

those of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-501a64cbd36865abc1477d70ae6b9bc9] chief superintendent,[^c18804421] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-501a64cbd36865abc1477d70ae6b9bc9] superintendent, chief inspector, inspector, sergeant and constable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-501a64cbd36865abc1477d70ae6b9bc9]

(3) In the metropolitan police force, appointments and promotions to any rank below that of Commander shall be made in accordance with regulations under section 50 by the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-501a64cbd36865abc1477d70ae6b9bc9]
