## 29. Attestation of constables.[^key-fb7221dfdf933385afa63183e7b8bdca][^key-17f550d09c0c4ee3484924de544395fd][^key-d3f42f8177776f10402985d54ded48a0]

*Extent: E+W*

Every member of a police force maintained for a police area and every special constable appointed for a police area shall, on appointment, be attested as a constable by making a declaration in the form set out in Schedule 4—

- (a)[^c16427601] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) [^c16427611]. . . before a justice of the peace having jurisdiction within the police area.
