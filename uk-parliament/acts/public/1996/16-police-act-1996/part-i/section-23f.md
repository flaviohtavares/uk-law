# Police Act 1996, section 23F

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-09-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23F. Collaboration agreements: guidance[^key-0a484dd46d77de1090776766308edb39][^key-0d80583ecb11921312f235e3b9a88fc9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may give chief officers or policing bodies[^key-2fe5c88169b1f8981e7a085cc3e36686] guidance about collaboration agreements or related matters.

(2) In discharging their functions, chief officers and policing bodies must have regard to the guidance.

(3)[^key-b2f6dee29a548546de9a268d36a38085] The Secretary of State may give other persons who exercise functions of a public nature guidance about collaboration agreements or related matters, and those persons must have regard to the guidance in exercising such functions.
