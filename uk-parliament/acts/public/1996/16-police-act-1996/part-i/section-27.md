# Police Act 1996, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-09-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Special constables.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The chief officer of police of the police force maintained for a police area may, in accordance with regulations under section 51, appoint special constables for that area.

(2) Subject to provision included in a[^key-bb58b8b1ac2594a45a05647375a57381] collaboration agreement[^key-e68cd87ea0e2520ab3ae7a09ba71edaf] by virtue of section 23(4), section 24(3) and[^key-10fc8e464ebb1dfd80387d2d1ba6ca19] regulations under section 51, all special constables appointed for a police area shall be under the direction and control of, and subject to dismissal by, the chief officer of police.
