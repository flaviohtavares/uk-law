## 18. Supply of goods and services.[^c19032331]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subsections (1) to (3) of section 1 of the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) Act 1970 (c. 39)(supply of goods and services by local authorities)—

- (a)[^c-5dbadcacc882b28118b1012c8684ea15] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) shall,[^c-06d982918d151a03166e850ebe7277cf] in their application to the Common Council of the City of London in its capacity as the police authority for the City of London police force , apply with the modification set out in subsection (2)[^c-d3465fecfa0b2203a5fd2fbb0bd73c0d].

(2) The modification is that references in those subsections to a public body shall be read as references to any person.

(3)[^c-1a39e7d100740fdadae15f1feafe01e2] The Common Council in its capacity as police authority[^c-abdbbe2fd021ce5f0ef535388efa25ec] may not enter into an agreement with another local policing body[^c-024dcb7683a990e9265be8723d337955] under section 1 of the 1970 Act in respect of a matter which could be the subject of force collaboration provision in a collaboration agreement[^c-7b0a6b7ae463afe411dc5d04471c51ed] .
