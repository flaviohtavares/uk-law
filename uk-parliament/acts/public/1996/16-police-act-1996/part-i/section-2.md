## 2. Maintenance of police forces.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-e406c010282e50854f58946c4ce46f95] A police force shall be maintained for every police area for the time being listed in Schedule 1.

(2)[^c-67747b958336a233a27545a5496f2f62] For further provision about the maintenance of those police forces, see Chapter 1 of Part 1 of the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011.
