## 5A. Maintenance of the metropolitan police force.[^c16426731]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-7f4500902b4a93cffec0d8ed9405dd5d] A police force shall be maintained for the metropolitan police district.

(2)[^key-1cc9d1c73d4dd66468bd65d428b7387f] For further provision about the maintenance of the metropolitan police force, see Chapter 2 of Part 1 of the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011.
