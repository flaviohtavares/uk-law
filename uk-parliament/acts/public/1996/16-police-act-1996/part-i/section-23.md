## 23. Collaboration agreements involving police forces[^c-0a484dd46d77de1090776766308edb39][^c-0d80583ecb11921312f235e3b9a88fc9][^c-636d0ff1cf5d63dfb3223c5f07dae43d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-d2407dc1bea2e979bc23575e9899de09] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Force collaboration provision may, in particular, consist of provision—[^c-339a6eda733b291d282cd6ff0971bfba]

- (a) for the joint discharge of functions by members of police forces;
- (b) for members of a police force to discharge functions in another force's area;
- (c) for members of a police force to be provided to another force.

(3)[^c-bc256b697720182c40481ea394028677] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) A collaboration agreement[^c-5fc1ae479a69267e3ad79703767e28ac] may provide for a member of a police force, or a civilian employee, to be under the direction and control of a chief officer specified in or determined in accordance with the agreement.

(5) A chief officer may make a collaboration agreement[^c-81468799411457bd2db12383fcedfe8a] only if the chief officer thinks that the agreement is in the interests of the efficiency or effectiveness of one or more police forces.
