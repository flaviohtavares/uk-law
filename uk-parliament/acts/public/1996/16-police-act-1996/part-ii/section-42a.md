# Police Act 1996, section 42A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-09-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/16

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42A. Procedure in relation to exercise of powers under section 42[^c19076391][^key-ad8546ae92db965bfbe4fe4d1ec7bc8f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision as to the procedure to be followed in the exercise of any power conferred or duty imposed by section [^key-57a0661b57e971013220c662cc67ab07]... 42.

(2) Before making any regulations under this section, the Secretary of State shall consult with—

- (a)[^key-c08c846d9c1c3138825e14ac15bb9eae] the Mayor's Office for Policing and Crime;
- (b) the National Police Chiefs' Council[^key-f843e8db1a1894707c76814148e2e036]; and
- (c) such other persons as he thinks fit.

(3) Regulations under this section may make different provision for different cases and circumstances.

(4) A statutory instrument containing regulations under this section shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
