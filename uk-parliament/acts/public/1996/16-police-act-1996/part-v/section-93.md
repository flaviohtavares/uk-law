## 93. Acceptance of gifts and loans.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local policing body[^key-3970da696bb5a7bfb10a555bc0fbc98c] may, in connection with the discharge of any of its functions, accept gifts of money, and gifts or loans of other property, on such terms as appear to the body[^key-a91ee3cce0c2e49a1f5f333723a837f9] to be appropriate.

(2) The terms on which gifts or loans are accepted under subsection (1) may include terms providing for the commercial sponsorship of any activity of the local policing body[^key-8e09ee5f22b982d7a6998be31a767880] or of the police force maintained by it.
