# Police Act 1996, section 100B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-09-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/16

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 100B. Disclosure of information to College of Policing[^key-8638da1e49f1182fb471eb198f05fc7e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who, apart from this section, would not have power to disclose information to the College of Policing has power to do so where the disclosure is made for the purposes of the exercise by the College of any of its functions.
