# Police Act 1996, section 94

## 94. Financing of new police and crime commissioners .[^c-354fee52f19519b911ff959dfb61adbb][^c-d9132faef3cc13ca691c619555852ba3][^c-474ac107d4342b3957d95739f8a31c2c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may make grants to any police and crime commissioner[^c-5dd21e7731148b4806475eb50525f8c0] in respect of expenditure incurred (or to be incurred) by the commissioner[^c-a128b367b97fdab3ba47260e2da2cfb9] at any time before the beginning of its first precepting year.

(2) Without prejudice to any other powers to borrow, a police and crime commissioner[^c-fa901263570c0335e70f5cec325fab8d] may borrow by way of temporary loan or overdraft from a bank or otherwise any sums which the commissioner[^c-212f32b2b334f9d4edaffc0147e66a73] may require for the purpose of meeting the commissioner's[^c-306d518ae51447f45a070777b0224ef0] expenditure before the beginning of the commissioner's first precepting year.

(3) The sums borrowed by a commissioner[^c-dac7e66fda54959afb455dd68ae2a8e6] under this section shall not exceed such amount as the Secretary of State may determine, and shall be repaid before the end of the commissioner's[^c-a46349abc0cf0bb6caba55f20f51a7ad] first precepting year.

(4) For the purposes of this section the “first precepting year” of a police and crime commissioner[^c-84b43a194cba985dd99e8a8766909944] is the financial year in which revenue is first received by the commissioner[^c-f25af1fe13fc84ee7fd342d5cb516909] as a result of a precept issued by the commissioner under Part I of the [^c16429531]Local Government Finance Act 1992.
