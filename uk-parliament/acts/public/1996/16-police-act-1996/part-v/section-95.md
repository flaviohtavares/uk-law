# Police Act 1996, section 95

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 95. The City of London police Fund.[^c16429541]

*Extent: E+W*

There shall be paid out of [^c16429551]. . . the City of London police fund [^c16429561]. . .any expenditure incurred under this Act in respect of—

- (a) any special constables appointed for [^c16429551]. . . the City of London police area; and
- (b) any police cadets appointed in relation to [^c16429551]. . . the City of London police force.
