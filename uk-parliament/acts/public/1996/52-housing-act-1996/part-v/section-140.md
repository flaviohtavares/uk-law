# Housing Act 1996, section 140

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 140. Members of a person’s family: Chapter I.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person is a member of another’s family within the meaning of this Chapter if—

- (a) he is the spouse or civil partner[^M_F_2088eaae-1468-4322-a8dc-4651669efef0] of that person, or he and that person live together as if they were a married couple or[^c-1ec8e5a7ab437cde3f9e35271a8b9b6e] civil partners[^c-6fb42032b439eee07068e174292240b1], or
- (b) he is that person’s parent, grandparent, child, grandchild, brother, sister, uncle, aunt, nephew or niece.

(2) For the purpose of subsection (1)(b)—

- (a) a relationship by marriage or civil partnership[^c-c29588ccf871d5cd0bbe83d3d99a5672] shall be treated as a relationship by blood,
- (b) a relationship of the half-blood shall be treated as a relationship of the whole blood, and
- (c) the stepchild of a person shall be treated as his child.
