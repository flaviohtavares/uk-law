## 135. Right to carry out repairs.

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State may by regulations under section 96 of the [^c13152281]Housing Act 1985 (secure tenants: right to carry out repairs) apply to introductory tenants any provision made under that section in relation to secure tenants.
