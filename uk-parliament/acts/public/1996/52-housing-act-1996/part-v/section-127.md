## 127. Proceedings for possession.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The landlord may only bring an introductory tenancy to an end by obtaining —[^key-dc6927b9c9aa1ea70a393ce794ebcfdf]

- (a) an order of the court for the possession of the dwelling-house, and
- (b) the execution of the order.

(1A)[^key-ed5e51cf2cd451a4b9b63d06cceda562] In such a case, the tenancy ends when the order is executed.

(2) The court shall make an order of the kind mentioned in subsection (1)(a)[^key-f81f47f7c9f95e1530938fddf5c7db57] unless the provisions of section 128 apply.
