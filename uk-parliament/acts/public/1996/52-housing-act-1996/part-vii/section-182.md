# Housing Act 1996, section 182

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 182. Guidance by the Secretary of State.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In the exercise of their functions relating to homelessness and the prevention of homelessness, a local housing authority or social services authority in England[^c-6e451d5712933b125b23ccbc7e94dd22] shall have regard to such guidance as may from time to time be given by the Secretary of State.

(2) The Secretary of State may give guidance either generally or to specified descriptions of authorities.
