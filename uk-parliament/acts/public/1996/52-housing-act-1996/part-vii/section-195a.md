## 195A. Re-application after private rented sector offer[^key-26ee06b6dfe0354778b690b3719776c3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-fa9e1f6513d0c00b47087fd73f70e73d] If within two years beginning with the date on which an applicant accepts an offer under section 193(7AA) (private rented sector offer), the applicant re-applies for accommodation, or for assistance in obtaining accommodation, and the local housing authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fa9e1f6513d0c00b47087fd73f70e73d]

- (a) is satisfied that the applicant is homeless and eligible for assistance, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fa9e1f6513d0c00b47087fd73f70e73d]
- (b) is not satisfied that the applicant became homeless intentionally, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fa9e1f6513d0c00b47087fd73f70e73d]

the duty under section 193(2) applies regardless of whether the applicant has a priority need. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fa9e1f6513d0c00b47087fd73f70e73d]

(2) For the purpose of subsection (1), an applicant in respect of whom a valid notice under section 21 of the Housing Act 1988 (orders for possession on expiry or termination of assured shorthold tenancy) has been given is to be treated as homeless from the date on which that notice expires. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fa9e1f6513d0c00b47087fd73f70e73d]

(4)[^key-7779f9afa6bf4068dd3bbd19f228d664] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) Subsection (1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fa9e1f6513d0c00b47087fd73f70e73d] [^key-8a3feb940fda42dbb0b284e5f5a4d726]... does not apply to a case where the local housing authority would not be satisfied as mentioned in that subsection without having regard to a restricted person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fa9e1f6513d0c00b47087fd73f70e73d]

(6) Subsection (1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fa9e1f6513d0c00b47087fd73f70e73d] [^key-5e3cb4d51018df4cc5ed186dcdd24fd2]... does not apply to a re-application by an applicant for accommodation, or for assistance in obtaining accommodation, if the immediately preceding application made by that applicant was one to which subsection (1) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fa9e1f6513d0c00b47087fd73f70e73d] [^key-5e3cb4d51018df4cc5ed186dcdd24fd2]... applied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fa9e1f6513d0c00b47087fd73f70e73d]
