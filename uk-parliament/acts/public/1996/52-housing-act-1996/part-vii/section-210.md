# Housing Act 1996, section 210

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 210. Suitability of accommodation.[^c13154661][^c13154671][^c13154681]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In determining for the purposes of this Part whether accommodation is suitable for a person, the local housing authority shall have regard to Parts 9 and 10[^key-527c03f430327234d7786d37c820205b] of the Housing Act 1985 (slum clearance and overcrowding) and Parts 1 to 4 of the Housing Act 2004[^key-bebd62c19838088adc805e15754ea266] .

(2) The Secretary of State may by order specify—

- (a) circumstances in which accommodation is or is not to be regarded as suitable for a person, and
- (b) matters to be taken into account or disregarded in determining whether accommodation is suitable for a person.
