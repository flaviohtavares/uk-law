## SCHEDULE 7 (affected by repeal, see note) — Assured tenancies: schedule inserted after Schedule 2 to the Housing Act 1988 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c13158231][^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830][^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]

Section 96. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]

> **SCHEDULE 2A (affected by repeal, see note) — Assured Tenancies: Non-Shortholds (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830][^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]**
>
> **Tenancies excluded by notice (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]**
>
> **1.** (1) An assured tenancy in respect of which a notice is served as mentioned in sub-paragraph (2) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> (2) The notice referred to in sub-paragraph (1) above is one which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> - (a) is served before the assured tenancy is entered into, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
> - (b) is served by the person who is to be the landlord under the assured tenancy on the person who is to be the tenant under that tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
> - (c) states that the assured tenancy to which it relates is not to be an assured shorthold tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> **2.** (1) An assured tenancy in respect of which a notice is served as mentioned in sub-paragraph (2) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> (2) The notice referred to in sub-paragraph (1) above is one which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> - (a) is served after the assured tenancy has been entered into, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
> - (b) is served by the landlord under the assured tenancy on the tenant under that tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
> - (c) states that the assured tenancy to which it relates is no longer an assured shorthold tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> **Tenancies containing exclusionary provision (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]**
>
> **3.** An assured tenancy which contains a provision to the effect that the tenancy is not an assured shorthold tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> **Tenancies under section 39 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]**
>
> **4.** An assured tenancy arising by virtue of section 39 above, other than one to which subsection (7) of that section applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> **Former secure tenancies (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]**
>
> **5.** An assured tenancy which became an assured tenancy on ceasing to be a secure tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> **Tenancies under Schedule 10 to the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]**
>
> **6.** An assured tenancy arising by virtue of Schedule 10 to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830] [^c13158241]Local Government and Housing Act 1989 (security of tenure on ending of long residential tenancies). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> **Tenancies replacing non-shortholds (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]**
>
> **7.** (1) An assured tenancy which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> - (a) is granted to a person (alone or jointly with others) who, immediately before the tenancy was granted, was the tenant (or, in the case of joint tenants, one of the tenants) under an assured tenancy other than a shorthold tenancy (“the old tenancy”), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
> - (b) is granted (alone or jointly with others) by a person who was at that time the landlord (or one of the joint landlords) under the old tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
> - (c) is not one in respect of which a notice is served as mentioned in sub-paragraph (2) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> (2) The notice referred to in sub-paragraph (1)(c) above is one which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> - (a) is in such form as may be prescribed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
> - (b) is served before the assured tenancy is entered into, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
> - (c) is served by the person who is to be the tenant under the assured tenancy on the person who is to be the landlord under that tenancy (or, in the case of joint landlords, on at least one of the persons who are to be joint landlords), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
> - (d) states that the assured tenancy to which it relates is to be a shorthold tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> **8.** An assured tenancy which comes into being by virtue of section 5 above on the coming to an end of an assured tenancy which is not a shorthold tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> **Assured agricultural occupancies (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]**
>
> **9.** (1) An assured tenancy— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> - (a) in the case of which the agricultural worker condition is, by virtue of any provision of Schedule 3 to this Act, for the time being fulfilled with respect to the dwelling-house subject to the tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
> - (b) which does not fall within sub-paragraph (2) or (4) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> (2) An assured tenancy falls within this sub-paragraph if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> - (a) before it is entered into, a notice— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>   - (i) in such form as may be prescribed, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830] stating that the tenancy is to be a shorthold tenancy, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
>   is served by the person who is to be the landlord under the tenancy on the person who is to be the tenant under it, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
> - (b) it is not an excepted tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> (3) For the purposes of sub-paragraph (2)(b) above, an assured tenancy is an excepted tenancy if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> - (a) the person to whom it is granted or, as the case may be, at least one of the persons to whom it is granted was, immediately before it is granted, a tenant or licensee under an assured agricultural occupancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
> - (b) the person by whom it is granted or, as the case may be, at least one of the persons by whom it is granted was, immediately before it is granted, a landlord or licensor under the assured agricultural occupancy referred to in paragraph (a) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
>
> (4) An assured tenancy falls within this sub-paragraph if it comes into being by virtue of section 5 above on the coming to an end of a tenancy falling within sub-paragraph (2) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-aabe13b54877bbcdda92b8dcc0314830]
