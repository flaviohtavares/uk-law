# Housing Act 1996, section 230

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 230. Minor definitions: general.

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Act—

- “assured tenancy” , “assured shorthold tenancy” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4c38be38bfe86ce33799248fd32f227e] and “assured agricultural occupancy” have the same meaning as in Part I of the [^c13155041]Housing Act 1988;
- “[^key-4c54764675ebe4c7a21e60bf85e548ab]**contract-holder**” has the same meaning as in the Renting Homes (Wales) Act [2016 (anaw 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/1) (see section 7 (see also section 48) of that Act);
- “enactment” includes an enactment comprised in subordinate legislation (within the meaning of the [^c13155051]Interpretation Act 1978);
- “housing action trust” has the same meaning as in the Housing Act 1988;
- “housing association” has the same meaning as in the [^c13155061]Housing Associations Act 1985;
- “**introductory standard contract**” has the same meaning as in the Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016 (see section 16 of that Act);
- “introductory tenancy” and “introductory tenant” have the same meaning as in Chapter I of Part V of this Act;
- “local housing authority” has the same meaning as in the [^c13155071]Housing Act 1985;
- “registered social landlord” has the same meaning as in Part I of this Act;
- “**secure contract**” has the same meaning as in the Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016 (see section 8 of that Act);
- “secure tenancy” and “secure tenant” have the same meaning as in Part IV of the Housing Act 1985.
- “**standard contract**” has the same meaning as in the Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016 (see section 8 of that Act).
