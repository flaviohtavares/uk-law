## 50O (Extent: W). Grounds for award (Extent: W)[^key-39816a421eadf3232720efa6b5b834b0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers may require a registered social landlord to pay compensation if they are satisfied that— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) either of the following cases applies, and *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) the award of compensation is appropriate (whether or not as part of a response including other action). *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) Case 1 is where the registered social landlord has failed to meet a standard under section 33A. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) Case 2 is where the registered social landlord has given an undertaking under section 6A and failed to comply with it. *(Extent: W)*
