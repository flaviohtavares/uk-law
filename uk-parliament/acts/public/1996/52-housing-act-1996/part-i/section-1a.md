# Housing Act 1996, section 1A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1A. Welsh bodies[^M_C_f9f7041a-760b-4dfb-b746-e04ccf09abe8][^c-29a802100fa149c79643c131e2e4b748]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter “**Welsh body**” means a body which is—

- (a) a registered charity whose address, for the purposes of registration by the Charity Commission for England and Wales, is in Wales,
- (b) a registered society within the meaning of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014 (in this Part, a “registered society”) whose registered office for the purposes of that Act is in Wales,[^c-c9080246232836eba97c70abfc8a2f58] or
- (c) a company within the meaning of the Companies Act 2006 which has its registered office for the purposes of that Act in Wales.
