# Housing Act 1996, section 33A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33A (Extent: W). Standards of performance (Extent: W)[^c-888dfd06ef6f746ce5d6f82bd3922103]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers may set standards to be met by registered social landlords in connection with— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) their functions relating to the provision of housing, and *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) matters relating to their governance and financial management. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) In setting standards the Welsh Ministers must have regard to the desirability of registered providers being free to choose how to provide services and conduct business. *(Extent: W)*

(2A (Extent: W))[^c-e6cf700cf42b10e7e1f0306e0d990282] Standards set under subsection (1) may require registered social landlords to comply with rules specified in the standards. *(Extent: W)*

(2B (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers may— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) revise the standards by issuing further standards under this section; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) withdraw the standards by issuing further standards under this section or by notice. *(Extent: W)*

(2C (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers must publish any standards or notice under this section. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) This section does not apply in relation to a registered social landlord's provision of housing in England. *(Extent: W)*
