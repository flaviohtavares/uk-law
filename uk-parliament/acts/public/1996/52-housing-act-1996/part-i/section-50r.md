# Housing Act 1996, section 50R

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50R (Extent: W). Impact (Extent: W)[^key-b58d6d1af50922f76c3dbd47ac225e89]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) This section applies when the Welsh Ministers are considering— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) whether to award compensation, or *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) the amount of compensation to award. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers must take account of any information available to them about the financial situation of the registered social landlord. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers must consider the likely impact of the compensation on the registered social landlord's ability to provide services. *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) In particular, the Welsh Minsters must aim to avoid— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) jeopardising the financial viability of the registered social landlord, *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) preventing the registered social landlord from honouring financial commitments, or *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) preventing the registered social landlord from taking action to remedy the matters on the grounds of which the compensation might be awarded. *(Extent: W)*
