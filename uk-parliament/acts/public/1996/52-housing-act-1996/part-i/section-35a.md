# Housing Act 1996, section 35A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35A (Extent: W). Guidance about complaints about performance (Extent: W)[^key-06439ffe679d8a6a1ebe51f569aea898]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers may publish guidance about complaints to the Welsh Ministers about the performance of registered social landlords. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) The guidance may specify (among other things)— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) the procedure to be followed in making a complaint; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) the criteria used by the Welsh Ministers in deciding whether to investigate a complaint; *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) periods within which the Welsh Ministers aim to inform complainants of the result of complaints. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers may revise or withdraw the guidance. *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) This section does not apply in relation to complaints about a registered social landlord's provision of housing in England. *(Extent: W)*
