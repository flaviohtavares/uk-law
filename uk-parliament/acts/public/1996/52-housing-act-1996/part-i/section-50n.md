## 50N (Extent: W). Appeal (Extent: W)[^key-5639f2863a6cfac51f9cd09b97189d57]

*Extent: E+W*

A registered social landlord who is given a penalty notice may appeal to the High Court against— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) the imposition of the penalty, *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) its amount, or *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) both. *(Extent: W)*
