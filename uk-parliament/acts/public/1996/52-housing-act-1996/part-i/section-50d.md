## 50D (Extent: W). Content (Extent: W)[^c-7ff58dd11ee757eb5b1d5f99c65af532]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) An enforcement notice must— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) specify the grounds on which it is given, *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) specify the action the Welsh Ministers want the registered social landlord to take in response to the notice, *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) specify when the action is to be taken (which may be immediately on receipt of the notice), and *(Extent: W)*
- (d (Extent: W)) explain the effect of sections 50E to 50G. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) The action specified in an enforcement notice may include publishing the notice in a specified manner. *(Extent: W)*
