# Housing Act 1996, section 61

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Meaning of “associate”.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part “**associate**”, in relation to a registered social landlord, means—

- (a) any body of which the landlord is a subsidiary, and
- (b) any other subsidiary of such a body.

(2) In this section “subsidiary” has the same meaning as in the Companies Acts (see section 1159 of the Companies Act 2006)[^c-8aa0add546281de15aca55b897fc4a6e] or Part 7 of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014[^c-2be10b9eb854b842f6e488157dbdb8df] or, in the case of a body which is itself a registered social landlord, has the meaning given by section 60.
