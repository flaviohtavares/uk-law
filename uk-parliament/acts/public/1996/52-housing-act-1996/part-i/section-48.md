## 48. Powers of the manager: transfer of engagements.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An order under section 46(1) may, where the landlord is a registered society[^key-e38244ecf50e288b05b415e28262a2c3], give the manager power to make and execute on behalf of the society an instrument transferring the engagements of the society.

(2) Any such instrument has the same effect as a transfer of engagements under section 110 or 112 of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014[^key-b01a66b41d9f6f1e3876985eca985a30] (transfer of engagements by special resolution to another society or a company).

In particular, it does not prejudice any right of a creditor of the society.[^key-b3ce4e5952dda6b1844dad64f556566e]

(3) A copy of the instrument, signed by the manager, shall be sent to the Financial Conduct Authority[^key-9750b654304f47b5c94c9d5a57e3188f][^c13150011] and registered by it; and until that copy is so registered the instrument shall not take effect.

(4) It is the duty of the manager to send a copy for registration within 14 days from the day on which the instrument is executed; but this does not invalidate registration after that time.
