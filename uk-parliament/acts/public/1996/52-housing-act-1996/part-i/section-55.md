# Housing Act 1996, section 55

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Minor and consequential amendments: Part I.[^c13150251]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The enactments mentioned in Schedule 3 have effect with the minor amendments specified there.

(2) The Welsh Ministers[^c-2fd5237b0bbed88821155d731bd1dc3c] may by order make such amendments or repeals of any enactment as appear to them[^c-c2f006c81ed9be0c10e5252d72e25795] necessary or expedient in consequence of the provisions of this Part.

(3) Any such order shall be made by statutory instrument which shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of the National Assembly for Wales[^c-85c4b072ee736fc4fc1899f25a75507a] .
