# Housing Act 1996, section 50S

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50S (Extent: W). Warning (Extent: W)[^key-1c2b314e366c702a1f9c4164d59fad06]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) Before giving a compensation notice to a registered social landlord the Welsh Ministers must give the landlord a notice (a “pre-compensation warning”)— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) specifying grounds on which the Welsh Ministers think compensation could be awarded, *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) warning the landlord that the Welsh Ministers are considering awarding compensation to a specified person, *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) including any indication that the Welsh Ministers are able to give of the likely amount of any compensation, and *(Extent: W)*
- (d (Extent: W)) explaining the effect of sections 50T, 50U(1) and (3) and 50V. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) Before giving a pre-compensation warning the Welsh Ministers must consult the Public Services Ombudsman for Wales. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers must send a copy of a pre-compensation warning to any person they think appropriate (having regard, in particular, to any person who provided information as a result of which the pre-compensation warning is given). *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) A pre-compensation warning must— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) refer to section 6A, and *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) indicate whether or to what extent the Welsh Ministers would accept a voluntary undertaking instead of, or in mitigation of, awarding compensation. *(Extent: W)*

(5 (Extent: W)) A pre-compensation warning may be combined with notice under one or more of the following— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) section 50K, *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) paragraphs 15C, 15E and 15G of Schedule 1. *(Extent: W)*
