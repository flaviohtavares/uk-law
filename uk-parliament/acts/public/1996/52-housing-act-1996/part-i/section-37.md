# Housing Act 1996, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Powers of entry.[^c13149301]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where it appears to the Welsh Ministers[^key-056e78dee416c01b82be7c499a9de4ee] that a registered social landlord may be failing to maintain or repair any premises in accordance with standards set under section 33A or *(Extent: W)*[^key-c4bbc2f43998cc5f0adc8352f1cbeac8] guidance issued under section 36.

(2) A person authorised by the Welsh Ministers may at any reasonable time, on giving not less than 28 days’ notice of his intention to the landlord concerned, enter any such premises for the purpose of survey and examination.

(3) Where such notice is given to the landlord, the landlord shall give the occupier or occupiers of the premises not less than seven days’ notice of the proposed survey and examination.

*Extent: W*

(3A)[^key-237338ff915b8f01bf40a118a92789d9] A landlord who fails, without reasonable excuse, to give the required notice in relation to premises in Wales[^key-99f5c4f34757889e7b56141a99fbb549] commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.

*Extent: W*

(3B)[^key-ce8d49b82fdb0dd71e4404da4a6b4e47] A landlord who fails to give the required notice in relation to premises in England commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.

(4) Proceedings for an offence under subsection (3A) or (3B) *(Extent: W)*[^key-c72a1ba355dcef3553f20203612f3c86] may be brought only by or with the consent of the Welsh Ministers or the Director of Public Prosecutions.

(5) An authorisation for the purposes of this section shall be in writing stating the particular purpose or purposes for which the entry is authorised and shall, if so required, be produced for inspection by the occupier or anyone acting on his behalf.

(6) The Relevant Authority[^c13149311] shall give a copy of any survey carried out in exercise of the powers conferred by this section to the landlord concerned.

(7) The Relevant Authority may require the landlord concerned to pay to it such amount as the Welsh Ministers may determine towards the costs of carrying out any survey under this section.
