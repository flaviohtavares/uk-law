# Housing Act 1996, section 50H

## 50H (Extent: W). Grounds for imposition (Extent: W)[^c-fc18597ad50ad4f79789b89d008b0c1a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers may require a registered social landlord to pay a penalty if they are satisfied that— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) any of the following cases applies, and *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) the imposition of a penalty is appropriate (whether or not as part of a response including other action). *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) Case 1 is where the registered social landlord has failed to meet a standard under section 33A. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W))[^c-119044dc249a3e3a5a48946d2c832ea8] Case 2 is where the registered social landlord has failed to comply with a requirement imposed by or under an enactment. *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) Case 3 is where the registered social landlord has failed to comply with an enforcement notice. *(Extent: W)*

(5 (Extent: W)) Case 4 is where the registered social landlord has given an undertaking under section 6A and failed to comply with it. *(Extent: W)*

(6 (Extent: W)) Case 5 is where an offence under this Part has been committed by the registered social landlord. *(Extent: W)*

(6A (Extent: W))[^c-b04cfefa15cbbd9f888737d695828b23] But Case 2 is not to be treated as applying if any of the other cases listed in this section applies. *(Extent: W)*

(7 (Extent: W)) Where the Welsh Ministers are satisfied that an offence under this Part has been committed in respect of a registered social landlord but by another person (such as a member, employee or agent of the registered social landlord)— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) Case 5 applies, *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) the Welsh Ministers may require the other person to pay a penalty, and *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) this Chapter applies with the substitution of references to that other person for references to the registered social landlord. *(Extent: W)*

(8 (Extent: W)) In order to rely on Case 5 the Welsh Ministers must be satisfied beyond reasonable doubt that it applies. *(Extent: W)*
