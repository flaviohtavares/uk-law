## 19. Land subject to housing management agreement.[^M_C_f9f7041a-760b-4dfb-b746-e04ccf09abe8]

*Extent: E+W*

A registered social landlord is not entitled to a grant under section 18 (social housing grant) in respect of land comprised in a management agreement within the meaning of the [^c13148151]Housing Act 1985 (see sections 27(2) and 27B(4) of that Act: delegation of housing management functions by certain authorities).
