# Housing Act 1996, section 50Q

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 50Q (Extent: W). Award (Extent: W)[^c-7a4c147b05866759cf98214b14af929b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) Compensation is awarded by the Welsh Ministers giving notice (a “compensation notice”) to— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) the registered social landlord, and *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) the person to be compensated. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) The notice must specify— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) the grounds on which the compensation is awarded, *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) the amount of the compensation, *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) the person to be compensated, *(Extent: W)*
- (d (Extent: W)) a period within which it must be paid, and *(Extent: W)*
- (e (Extent: W)) any interest or additional compensation which, by virtue of section 50U(2), is payable in the event of late payment. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) The notice may require the registered social landlord to publish information about the compensation award in a specified manner. *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) The notice must explain the effect of sections 50U(1) and (3) and 50V. *(Extent: W)*
