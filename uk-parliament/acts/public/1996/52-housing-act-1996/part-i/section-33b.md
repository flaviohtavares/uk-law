## 33B (Extent: W). Guidance on standards of performance (Extent: W)[^c-3850905ab889b1e567995aa4cb741544]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers may issue guidance that— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) relates to a matter addressed by a standard, and *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) amplifies the standard. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) In considering whether standards have been met the Welsh Ministers may have regard to the guidance. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W))[^c-c3ed6fe852704cb9dfbceb6f4b834f8a] The Welsh Ministers may— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) revise the guidance by issuing further guidance under this section; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) withdraw the guidance by issuing further guidance under this section or by notice. *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W))[^c-9cacf08e7f98ce4ff2c2fcafc3bc6216] The Welsh Ministers must publish any guidance or notice under this section. *(Extent: W)*
