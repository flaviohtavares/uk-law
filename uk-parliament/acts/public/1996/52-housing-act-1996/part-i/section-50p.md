## 50P (Extent: W). Persons to whom compensation may be awarded (Extent: W)[^key-4fd81b0c894c4cddea03289691291c70]

*Extent: E+W*

Compensation in respect of a failure may be awarded to one or more persons who have suffered as a result of the failure. *(Extent: W)*
