# Housing Act 1996, section 6A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6A (Extent: W). Voluntary undertakings (Extent: W)[^c-fcf4763e68b9d8c9ef075fd0c9d749c8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) A registered social landlord may give the Welsh Ministers an undertaking in respect of any matter concerning housing. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers may prescribe a procedure to be followed in giving an undertaking. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers must have regard to any undertaking offered or given in exercising a regulatory or enforcement power. *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers may base a decision about whether to exercise a regulatory or enforcement power wholly or partly on the extent to which an undertaking has been honoured. *(Extent: W)*

(5 (Extent: W)) In this section, “ *(Extent: W)***regulatory or enforcement power *(Extent: W)***” means a power exercisable under any of the following provisions— *(Extent: W)*

- section 35, *(Extent: W)*
- section 37, *(Extent: W)*
- section 38, *(Extent: W)*
- Chapter 4A of this Part, *(Extent: W)*
- paragraphs 4 and 6 to 15H of Part 2 of Schedule 1, *(Extent: W)*
- Part 3A of Schedule 1, *(Extent: W)*
- Part 4 of Schedule 1. *(Extent: W)*

(6 (Extent: W)) This section does not apply in relation to a registered social landlord's provision of housing in England. *(Extent: W)*
