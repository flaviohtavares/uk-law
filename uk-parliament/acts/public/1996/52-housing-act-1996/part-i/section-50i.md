# Housing Act 1996, section 50I

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50I (Extent: W). Imposition (Extent: W)[^key-f5c01c740dffce4cb246e7921ab2d48c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) A penalty is imposed by the Welsh Ministers giving notice (a “penalty notice”) to the registered social landlord. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) The notice must specify— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) the grounds on which the penalty is imposed, *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) the amount of the penalty, *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) how the penalty must be paid, *(Extent: W)*
- (d (Extent: W)) a period within which it must be paid, and *(Extent: W)*
- (e (Extent: W)) any interest or additional penalty which, by virtue of section 50M, is payable in the event of late payment. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) The notice may require the registered social landlord to publish information about the penalty in a specified manner. *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) The notice must explain the effect of sections 50M(1), (3) and (5) and 50N. *(Extent: W)*
