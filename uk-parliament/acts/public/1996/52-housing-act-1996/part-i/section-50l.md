# Housing Act 1996, section 50L

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50L (Extent: W). Representations (Extent: W)[^c-fa9db4b6860d9a27cac28756fef5c1d6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) A pre-penalty warning must specify a period during which the registered social landlord may make representations to the Welsh Ministers. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) The period must— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) be a period of at least 28 days, and *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) begin with the date on which the registered social landlord receives the pre-penalty warning. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) Representations may address— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) whether a penalty should be imposed; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) the amount of any penalty that may be imposed. *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) After the end of the period specified under subsection (1) the Welsh Ministers must— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) consider any representations made, and *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) decide whether to impose a penalty. *(Extent: W)*
