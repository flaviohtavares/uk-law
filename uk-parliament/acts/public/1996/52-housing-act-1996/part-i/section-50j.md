# Housing Act 1996, section 50J

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50J (Extent: W). Amount (Extent: W)[^key-2a48a4121e29ab5b048e8f00f33ed7e9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) The amount of a penalty imposed on the ground specified in Case 5 of section 50H may not exceed the maximum amount *(Extent: W)* (if any)[^key-f47578320d17ef1c6ba86e608174d501] *(Extent: W)* of fine that a magistrates' court could impose for the relevant offence. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) The amount of a penalty imposed on the ground specified in any other Case of that section may not exceed £5,000. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers may by order amend the amount specified in subsection (2). *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) An order under subsection (3) is to be made by statutory instrument and must not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the National Assembly for Wales. *(Extent: W)*
