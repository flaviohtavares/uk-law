# Housing Act 1996, section 50T

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 50T (Extent: W). Representations (Extent: W)[^c-97e26fec73d264c12ba18f4e8cb4f6c4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) A pre-compensation warning must specify a period during which the registered social landlord may make representations to the Welsh Ministers. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) The period must— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) be a period of at least 28 days, and *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) begin with the date on which the registered social landlord receives the pre-compensation warning. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) Representations may address— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) whether compensation should be awarded; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) the amount of any compensation that may be awarded. *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) After the end of the period specified under subsection (1) the Welsh Ministers must— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) consider any representations made, and *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) decide whether to award compensation. *(Extent: W)*
