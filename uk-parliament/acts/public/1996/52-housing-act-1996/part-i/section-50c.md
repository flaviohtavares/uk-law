# Housing Act 1996, section 50C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50C (Extent: W). Grounds for giving notice (Extent: W)[^key-4cd2e9e2f6fb75c074b158d2dab530ec]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers may give an enforcement notice to a registered social landlord if they are satisfied that— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) any of the following cases applies, and *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) giving an enforcement notice is appropriate (whether it is likely to be sufficient in itself or a prelude to further action). *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) Case 1 is where the registered social landlord has failed to meet a standard applicable to it under section 33A. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W))[^key-98948c88562be66fff2124a9eee091fd] Case 2 is where the registered social landlord has failed to comply with a requirement imposed by or under an enactment. *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) Case 3 is where the registered social landlord has failed to comply with an earlier enforcement notice. *(Extent: W)*

(5 (Extent: W)) Case 4 is where the registered social landlord has failed to publish information in accordance with a requirement under section 50I(3) or 50Q(3). *(Extent: W)*

(6 (Extent: W)) Case 5 is where the interests of tenants of the registered social landlord require protection. *(Extent: W)*

(7 (Extent: W)) Case 6 is where the registered social landlord's assets require protection. *(Extent: W)*

(8 (Extent: W)) Case 7 is where the registered social landlord has given an undertaking under section 6A and failed to comply with it. *(Extent: W)*

(9 (Extent: W)) Case 8 is where an offence under this Part has been committed by the registered social landlord. *(Extent: W)*

(10 (Extent: W)) Case 9 is where the registered social landlord has failed to implement a recommendation made by the Public Services Ombudsman for Wales in a report prepared under section 16 of the Public Services Ombudsman (Wales) Act 2005 *(Extent: W)* or section 23 of the Public Services Ombudsman (Wales) Act 2019[^key-fc273df327ce9b44915d34b2646e2e94] *(Extent: W)*. *(Extent: W)*

(10A (Extent: W))[^key-db35cb44d0fde945c43ab124ee1d6868] But Case 2 is not to be treated as applying if any of the other cases listed in this section applies. *(Extent: W)*

(11 (Extent: W)) Where the Welsh Ministers are satisfied that an offence under this Part has been committed in respect of a registered social landlord but by another person (such as a member, employee or agent of the registered social landlord)— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) Case 8 applies, *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) the Welsh Ministers may give an enforcement notice to the other person, and *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) this Chapter applies with the substitution of references to that other person for references to the registered social landlord. *(Extent: W)*
