# Housing Act 1996, section 50M

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 50M (Extent: W). Enforcement (Extent: W)[^c-62716cc27131833a6d8fd3061f18de82]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) A penalty is to be treated as a debt owed to the Welsh Ministers. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers may— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) charge interest on a penalty not paid during the period specified under section 50I(2)(d); *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) impose one or more additional penalties where a penalty is not paid during that period. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) Interest and additional penalty are to be treated as penalty (and may have the effect of increasing the penalty above a limit set by section 50J). *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) A penalty notice may include provision allowing a discount if the penalty is paid on or before a date specified in the notice (falling within the period specified under section 50I(2)(d)). *(Extent: W)*

(5 (Extent: W)) A person to whom a penalty notice is given on the ground in Case 5 of section 50H may not be prosecuted for the offence by reference to which the penalty notice was given. *(Extent: W)*
