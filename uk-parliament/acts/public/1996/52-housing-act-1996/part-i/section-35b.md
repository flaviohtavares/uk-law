## 35B (Extent: W). Consultation (Extent: W)[^c-ab3bc1ebdd59b56f79ee561f5da512e0]

*Extent: E+W*

Before publishing, revising or withdrawing guidance under section 35A the Welsh Ministers must consult— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) one or more bodies appearing to them to represent the interests of registered social landlords, *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) one or more bodies appearing to them to represent the interests of tenants, *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) one or more bodies appearing to them to represent the interests of local housing authorities, and *(Extent: W)*
- (d (Extent: W)) the Auditor General for Wales. *(Extent: W)*
