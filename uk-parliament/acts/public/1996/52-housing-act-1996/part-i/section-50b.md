## 50B (Extent: W). Exercise of enforcement powers (Extent: W)[^c-a5b524a2fd674e2060165d26fbb35606]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) This section applies where the Welsh Ministers are deciding— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) whether to exercise an enforcement power, *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) which enforcement power to exercise, or *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) how to exercise an enforcement power. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers must consider— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) the desirability of registered social landlords being free to choose how to provide services and conduct business; *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) whether the failure or other problem concerned is serious or trivial; *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) whether the failure or other problem is a recurrent or isolated incident; *(Extent: W)*
- (d (Extent: W)) the speed with which the failure or other problem needs to be addressed. *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) In subsection (1), an “ *(Extent: W)***enforcement power *(Extent: W)***” means a power exercisable under any of the following provisions— *(Extent: W)*

- this Chapter, *(Extent: W)*
- paragraphs 4, 6 to 8, 14 to 15B, 15D, 15F and 15H of Part 2 of Schedule 1, *(Extent: W)*
- paragraphs 20 to 27 of Part 4 of Schedule 1. *(Extent: W)*
