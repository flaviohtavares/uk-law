# Housing Act 1996, section 50K

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 50K (Extent: W). Warning (Extent: W)[^c-8aa397df29c55c500546da6104db70b0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: W)) Before giving a penalty notice to a registered social landlord the Welsh Ministers must give the landlord a notice (a “pre-penalty warning”)— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) specifying grounds on which the Welsh Ministers think a penalty could be imposed, *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) warning the landlord that the Welsh Ministers are considering imposing a penalty, *(Extent: W)*
- (c (Extent: W)) including any indication that the Welsh Ministers are able to give of the likely amount of any penalty, and *(Extent: W)*
- (d (Extent: W)) explaining the effect of sections 50L, 50M(1), (3) and (5) and 50N. *(Extent: W)*

(2 (Extent: W)) The Welsh Ministers must send a copy of a pre-penalty warning to any person they think appropriate (having regard, in particular, to any person who provided information as a result of which the pre-penalty warning is given). *(Extent: W)*

(3 (Extent: W)) A pre-penalty warning must— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) refer to section 6A, and *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) indicate whether or to what extent the Welsh Ministers would accept a voluntary undertaking instead of, or in mitigation of, a penalty. *(Extent: W)*

(4 (Extent: W)) A pre-penalty warning may be combined with notice under one or more of the following— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) section 50S, *(Extent: W)*
- (b (Extent: W)) paragraphs 15C, 15E and 15G of Schedule 1. *(Extent: W)*
