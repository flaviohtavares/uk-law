# Housing Act 1996, section 62

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Members of a person’s family: Part I.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person is a member of another’s family within the meaning of this Part if—

- (a) he is the spouse or civil partner[^key-e6dde0539ee382b911f70c446f98b8d2] of that person, or he and that person live together as if they were a married couple or[^key-8be11e5cbe61e04a4465862e21877611] civil partners[^M_F_e251f999-3815-4011-caca-5001e79375bd], or
- (b) he is that person’s parent, grandparent, child, grandchild, brother, sister, uncle, aunt, nephew or niece.

(2) For the purpose of subsection (1)(b)—

- (a) a relationship by marriage or civil partnership[^key-8b86dea5bb622ae82b2a868245f5c4a0] shall be treated as a relationship by blood,
- (b) a relationship of the half-blood shall be treated as a relationship of the whole blood, and
- (c) the stepchild of a person shall be treated as his child.
