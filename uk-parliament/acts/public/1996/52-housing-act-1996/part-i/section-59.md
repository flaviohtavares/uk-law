# Housing Act 1996, section 59

## 59. Meaning of “officer” of registered social landlord.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) References in this Part to an officer of a registered social landlord are—

- (a) in the case of a registered charity which is not a company[^c-ee929a43ffa1f3a55e01e7155adcc7dd] , to any trustee, secretary or treasurer of the charity;
- (b) in the case of a registered society[^c-e38244ecf50e288b05b415e28262a2c3], to any officer of the society as defined in section 149 of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014[^c-0ca976e4b541257608d96247cb2c5c8a]; and
- (c) in the case of a company (including a company that is a registered charity)[^c-d5636923f05dd1a65eb713ca2a009917] , to any director or other officer of the company within the meaning of the Companies Acts (see sections 250 and 1173(1) of the Companies Act 2006)[^c-9fa9acbf29befc45c378dd0412e3130c] .

(2) Any such reference includes, in the case of a registered society, a co-opted member of the committee of the society.
