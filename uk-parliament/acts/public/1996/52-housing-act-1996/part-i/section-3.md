## 3. Registration.[^M_C_f9f7041a-760b-4dfb-b746-e04ccf09abe8][^c13146701]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Relevant Authority[^c13146711] may register as a social landlord any Welsh body[^c-7e619d530dd508c2b1b30abd8c108b90] which is eligible for such registration.

(2) An application for registration shall be made in such manner, and shall be accompanied by such fee (if any), as the Welsh Ministers[^c-056e78dee416c01b82be7c499a9de4ee] may determine.

(3) As soon as may be after registering a body as a social landlord the Welsh Ministers shall give notice of the registration—

- (a) in the case of a registered charity, to the Charity Commission[^c-4a551643141793a8a9f36aa859e028d9],
- (b) in the case of a registered society[^c-e38244ecf50e288b05b415e28262a2c3], to the Financial Conduct Authority[^c-1e5c4d1d0997bbc0121fff115f046665], and
- (c) in the case of a company (including a company that is a registered charity)[^c-53a82b7f557313f7c090b234a8605aa1], to the registrar of companies,

who shall record the registration.

(4) A Welsh body[^c-0a6d76a73bfb82c183cddefa4a6c39f0] which at any time is, or was, registered as a social landlord shall, for all purposes other than rectification of the register, be conclusively presumed to be, or to have been, at that time a body eligible for registration as a social landlord.
