## 96. Tenancies which are assured shorthold tenancies.[^c13151461]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719] In Chapter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719] II *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719] of Part I of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719] [^c13151471]Housing Act 1988 (assured shorthold tenancies) there shall be inserted at the beginning— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]

> **19A. Assured shorthold tenancies: post-Housing Act 1996 tenancies. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]**
>
> An assured tenancy which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> - (a) is entered into on or after the day on which section 96 of the Housing Act 1996 comes into force (otherwise than pursuant to a contract made before that day), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
> - (b) comes into being by virtue of section 5 above on the coming to an end of an assured tenancy within paragraph (a) above, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> is an assured shorthold tenancy unless it falls within any paragraph in Schedule 2A to this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]

.

(2) After Schedule 2 to that Act there shall be inserted the Schedule set out in Schedule 7 to this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
