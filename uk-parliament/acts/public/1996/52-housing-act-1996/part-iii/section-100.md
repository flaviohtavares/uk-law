## 100. Applications for determination of rent: time limit.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719] Section 22 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719] [^c13151501]Housing Act 1988 (reference of excessive rents to rent assessment committee) shall be amended as follows. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]

(2) In subsection (2) (circumstances in which no application under the section may be made) after paragraph (a) there shall be inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]

> - (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719] the tenancy is one to which section 19A above applies and more than six months have elapsed since the beginning of the tenancy or, in the case of a replacement tenancy, since the beginning of the original tenancy; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]

.

(3) At the end there shall be inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]

> (6) In subsection (2)(aa) above, the references to the original tenancy and to a replacement tenancy shall be construed in accordance with subsections (6) and (7) respectively of section 21 above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]

.
