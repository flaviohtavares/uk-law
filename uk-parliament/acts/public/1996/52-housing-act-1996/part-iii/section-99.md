## 99. Restriction on recovery of possession on expiry or termination.

*Extent: E+W*

In section 21 of the Housing Act 1988 there shall be inserted at the end— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]

> (5) Where an order for possession under subsection (1) or (4) above is made in relation to a dwelling-house let on a tenancy to which section 19A above applies, the order may not be made so as to take effect earlier than— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> - (a) in the case of a tenancy which is not a replacement tenancy, six months after the beginning of the tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
> - (b) in the case of a replacement tenancy, six months after the beginning of the original tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> (6) In subsection (5)(b) above, the reference to the original tenancy is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> - (a) where the replacement tenancy came into being on the coming to an end of a tenancy which was not a replacement tenancy, to the immediately preceding tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
> - (b) where there have been successive replacement tenancies, to the tenancy immediately preceding the first in the succession of replacement tenancies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> (7) For the purposes of this section, a replacement tenancy is a tenancy— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> - (a) which comes into being on the coming to an end of an assured shorthold tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
> - (b) under which, on its coming into being— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>   - (i) the landlord and tenant are the same as under the earlier tenancy as at its coming to an end, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>   - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719] the premises let are the same or substantially the same as those let under the earlier tenancy as at that time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]

.
