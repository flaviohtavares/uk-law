## 97. Duty of landlord to provide statement of terms of assured shorthold tenancy.

*Extent: E+W*

After section 20 of the Housing Act 1988 there shall be inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]

> **20A. Post-Housing Act 1996 tenancies: duty of landlord to provide statement as to terms of tenancy. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]**
>
> (1) Subject to subsection (3) below, a tenant under an assured shorthold tenancy to which section 19A above applies may, by notice in writing, require the landlord under that tenancy to provide him with a written statement of any term of the tenancy which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> - (a) falls within subsection (2) below, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
> - (b) is not evidenced in writing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> (2) The following terms of a tenancy fall within this subsection, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> - (a) the date on which the tenancy began or, if it is a statutory periodic tenancy or a tenancy to which section 39(7) below applies, the date on which the tenancy came into being, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
> - (b) the rent payable under the tenancy and the dates on which that rent is payable, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
> - (c) any term providing for a review of the rent payable under the tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
> - (d) in the case of a fixed term tenancy, the length of the fixed term. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> (3) No notice may be given under subsection (1) above in relation to a term of the tenancy if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> - (a) the landlord under the tenancy has provided a statement of that term in response to an earlier notice under that subsection given by the tenant under the tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
> - (b) the term has not been varied since the provision of the statement referred to in paragraph (a) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> (4) A landlord who fails, without reasonable excuse, to comply with a notice under subsection (1) above within the period of 28 days beginning with the date on which he received the notice is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> (5) A statement provided for the purposes of subsection (1) above shall not be regarded as conclusive evidence of what was agreed by the parties to the tenancy in question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> (6) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> - (a) a term of a statutory periodic tenancy is one which has effect by virtue of section 5(3)(e) above, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
> - (b) a term of a tenancy to which subsection (7) of section 39 below applies is one which has effect by virtue of subsection (6)(e) of that section, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> subsection (1) above shall have effect in relation to it as if paragraph (b) related to the term of the tenancy from which it derives. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> (7) In subsections (1) and (3) above— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
>
> - (a) references to the tenant under the tenancy shall, in the case of joint tenants, be taken to be references to any of the tenants, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
> - (b) references to the landlord under the tenancy shall, in the case of joint landlords, be taken to be references to any of the landlords. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ee998ba4abf04101e363ba678cf1c719]
