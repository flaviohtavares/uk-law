# Housing Act 1996, section 90

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Notice required to be given by landlord making disposal.[^c13151341]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In section 4(2) of the [^c13151351]Landlord and Tenant Act 1987 (disposals which are not relevant disposals for the purposes of Part I of that Act), for paragraph (l) substitute—

> - (l) a disposal by a body corporate to a company which has been an associated company of that body for at least two years.

.

(2) The above amendment does not apply to a disposal made in pursuance of an obligation entered into before the commencement of this section.
