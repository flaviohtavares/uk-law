## 108. Collective enfranchisement: removal of need for professional valuation of interests to be acquired.[^c13151801]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 13 of the [^c13151811]Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993 (notice by qualifying tenants of claim to exercise right to collective enfranchisement) subsection (6) (tenants to obtain professional valuation of interests proposed to be acquired before giving notice) shall cease to have effect.
