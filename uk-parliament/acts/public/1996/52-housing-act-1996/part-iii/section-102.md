# Housing Act 1996, section 102

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (127).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Recovery of possession where grant induced by false statement.[^c13151521]

*Extent: E+W*

In Part II of Schedule 2 to the Housing Act 1988 (grounds on which court may order possession) there shall be inserted at the end—

> **Ground 17**
>
> The tenant is the person, or one of the persons, to whom the tenancy was granted and the landlord was induced to grant the tenancy by a false statement made knowingly or recklessly by—
>
> - (a) the tenant, or
> - (b) a person acting at the tenant’s instigation.
