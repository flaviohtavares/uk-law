# Housing Act 1996, section 170

## 170. Co-operation between certain social landlords and local housing authorities.[^key-a8c8b16f4d60e28af6b88f715dd6d23b]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a local housing authority so request, a private registered provider of social housing or[^key-b996e7c29e607758413f003fc10eafb3] registered social landlord shall co-operate to such extent as is reasonable in the circumstances in offering accommodation to people with priority under the authority’s allocation scheme[^c13153071].
