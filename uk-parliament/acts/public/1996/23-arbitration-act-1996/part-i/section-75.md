# Arbitration Act 1996, section 75

> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Charge to secure payment of solicitors’ costs.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

The powers of the court to make declarations and orders under section 73 of the [^c833527]Solicitors Act 1974 or Article 71H of the [^c833528]Solicitors (Northern Ireland) Order 1976 (power to charge property recovered in the proceedings with the payment of solicitors’ costs) may be exercised in relation to arbitral proceedings as if those proceedings were proceedings in the court.
