# Arbitration Act 1996, section 99

> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Continuation of Part II of the Arbitration Act 1950.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Part II of the [^c833556]Arbitration Act 1950 (enforcement of certain foreign awards) continues to apply in relation to foreign awards within the meaning of that Part which are not also New York Convention awards.
