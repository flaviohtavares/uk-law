# Arbitration Act 1996, section 92

> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 92. Exclusion of Part I in relation to small claims arbitration in the county court.

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in Part I of this Act applies to arbitration under section 64 of the [^c833552]County Courts Act 1984.
