# Arbitration Act 1996, section 90

> Text as at 2025-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Part applies where consumer is a legal person[^key-b71480c07570eae6c59469dbb9611ae5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Part applies where the consumer is a legal person as it applies where the consumer is an individual.
