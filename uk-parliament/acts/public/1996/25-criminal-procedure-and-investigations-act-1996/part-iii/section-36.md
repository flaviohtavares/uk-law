# Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2025-10-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Appeals to Supreme Court.[^key-8f0c4db8ff21d70516780a2184f399b4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In the [^c11039381]Criminal Appeal Act 1968, in—

- (a) section 33(1) (right of appeal to House of Lords), and
- (b) section 36 (bail),

after “1987” there shall be inserted “ or section 35 of the Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996 ”.

(2) The judge may continue a preparatory hearing notwithstanding that leave to appeal has been granted under Part II of the Criminal Appeal Act 1968, but the preparatory hearing shall not be concluded[^c19058261] until after the appeal has been determined or abandoned.
