# Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2025-10-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Start of trial and arraignment.

*Extent: E+W*

If a judge orders a preparatory hearing—

- (a) the trial shall start with that hearing, and
- (b) arraignment shall take place at the start of that hearing, unless it has taken place before then.
