# Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2025-10-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Meaning of pre-trial hearing.[^c11039471]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Part a hearing is a pre-trial hearing if it relates to a trial on indictment and it takes place—

- (a) after the accused has been sent for trial for the offence, and[^c-22e9500393913443f8a344e0af546730]
- (b) before the start of the trial.

(2) For the purposes of this Part a hearing is also a pre-trial hearing if—

- (a) it relates to a trial on indictment to be held in pursuance of a bill of indictment preferred under the authority of section 2(2)(b) or (ba)[^c-cdf203785f1d3d73d1d628e9b7682173] of the [^c11039491]Administration of Justice (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1933 (bill preferred by direction of Court of Appeal, or by direction or with consent of a judge), and
- (b) it takes place after the bill of indictment has been preferred and before the start of the trial.

(3) For the purposes of this section the start of a trial on indictment occurs at the time when a jury is sworn[^c19061701] to consider the issue of guilt or fitness to plead or, if the court accepts a plea of guilty before the time when a jury is sworn[^c19061721] , when that plea is accepted; but this is subject to section 8 of the [^c11039501]Criminal Justice Act 1987 and section 30 of this Act (preparatory hearings).

(4)[^c19061821] The references in subsection (3) to the time when a jury is sworn include the time when that jury would be sworn but for the making of an order under Part 7 of the Criminal Justice Act 2003.
