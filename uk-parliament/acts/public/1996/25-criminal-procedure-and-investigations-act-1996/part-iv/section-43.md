# Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2025-10-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Application of this Part.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) This Part applies in relation to pre-trial hearings beginning on or after the appointed day.

(2)[^c11039541] The reference in subsection (1) to the appointed day is to such day as is appointed for the purposes of this section by the Secretary of State by order.
