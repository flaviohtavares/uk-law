# Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996, section 6D

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 6D). (not yet in force: Notification of names of experts instructed by accused)[^c18987811][^c-8c1811d7ba0c092c4e68960ff664e626]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: If the accused instructs a person with a view to his providing any expert opinion for possible use as evidence at the trial of the accused, he must give to the court and the prosecutor a notice specifying the person’s name and address.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: A notice does not have to be given under this section specifying the name and address of a person whose name and address have already been given under section 6C.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: A notice under this section must be given during the period which, by virtue of section 12, is the relevant period for this section.)*
