# Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996, section 6B

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 6B). (not yet in force: Updated disclosure by accused)[^c18987791][^c-8c1811d7ba0c092c4e68960ff664e626]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Where the accused has, before the beginning of the relevant period for this section, given a defence statement under section 5 or 6, he must during that period give to the court and the prosecutor either—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: a defence statement under this section (an “updated defence statement”), or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: a statement of the kind mentioned in subsection (4).)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: The relevant period for this section is determined under section 12.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: An updated defence statement must comply with the requirements imposed by or under section 6A by reference to the state of affairs at the time when the statement is given.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Instead of an updated defence statement, the accused may give a written statement stating that he has no changes to make to the defence statement which was given under section 5 or 6.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Where there are other accused in the proceedings and the court so orders, the accused must also give either an updated defence statement or a statement of the kind mentioned in subsection (4), within such period as may be specified by the court, to each other accused so specified.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: The court may make an order under subsection (5) either of its own motion or on the application of any party.)*
