*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

**1.** The entries in Schedule 11 to the 1994 Act are those relating to the following—

- (a) sections 13(3) and 49(2) of the [^c11041111]Criminal Justice Act 1925;
- (b) section 1 of the [^c11041121]Criminal Procedure (Attendance of Witnesses) Act 1965;
- (c) section 7 of the [^c11041131]Criminal Justice Act 1967 and in section 36(1) of that Act the definition of “**committal proceedings**”;
- (d) in paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the [^c11041141]Criminal Appeal Act 1968 the words from “section 13(3)” to “but”;
- (e) in section 46(1) of the [^c11041151]Criminal Justice Act 1972 the words “Section 102 of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 and”, “which respectively allow”, “committal proceedings and in other”, “and section 106 of the said Act of 1980”, “which punish the making of”, “102 or” and “, as the case may be”, and section 46(2) of that Act;
- (f) in section 32(1)(b) of the [^c11041161]Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973 the words “tried or”;
- (g) in Schedule 1 to the [^c11041171]Interpretation Act 1978, paragraph (a) of the definition of “**Committed for trial**”;
- (h) in section 97(1) of the [^c11041181]Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 the words from “at an inquiry” to “be) or”, sections 102, 103, 105, 106 and 145(1)(e) of that Act, in section 150(1) of that Act the definition of “**committal proceedings**”, and paragraph 2 of Schedule 5 to that Act;
- (i) in section 2(2)(g) of the [^c11041191]Criminal Attempts Act 1981 the words “or committed for trial”;
- (j) in section 1(2) of the [^c11041201]Criminal Justice Act 1982 the words “trial or”;
- (k) paragraphs 10 and 11 of Schedule 2 to the [^c11041211]Criminal Justice Act 1987;
- (l) in section 20(4)(a) of the [^c11041221]Legal Aid Act 1988 the words “trial or”, and section 20(4)(bb) and (5) of that Act;
- (m) in section 1(4) of the [^c11041231]War Crimes Act 1991 the words “England, Wales or”, and Part I of the Schedule to that Act.

**2.** The repeals under this paragraph (reinstatement of certain provisions) have effect in accordance with section 44 of this Act.
