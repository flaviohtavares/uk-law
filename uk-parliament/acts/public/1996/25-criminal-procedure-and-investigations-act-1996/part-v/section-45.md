# Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2025-10-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Notices of transfer.[^c11039691]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1)[^key-13ba994cda5d2bf0891fffac4bf0b322] Section 5 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ba994cda5d2bf0891fffac4bf0b322] [^c11039701]Criminal Justice Act 1987 (notices of transfer in cases of serious or complex fraud) shall be amended as mentioned in subsections (2) and (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ba994cda5d2bf0891fffac4bf0b322]

(2) In subsection (9)(a) (regulations) for the words “a statement of the evidence” there shall be substituted “copies of the documents containing the evidence (including oral evidence)”. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ba994cda5d2bf0891fffac4bf0b322]

(3) The following subsection shall be inserted after subsection (9)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ba994cda5d2bf0891fffac4bf0b322]

> (9A) Regulations under subsection (9)(a) above may provide that there shall be no requirement for copies of documents to accompany the copy of the notice of transfer if they are referred to, in documents sent with the notice of transfer, as having already been supplied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ba994cda5d2bf0891fffac4bf0b322]

(4) In Schedule 6 to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ba994cda5d2bf0891fffac4bf0b322] [^c11039711]Criminal Justice Act 1991 (notices of transfer in certain cases involving children) paragraph 4 (regulations) shall be amended as mentioned in subsections (5) and (6). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ba994cda5d2bf0891fffac4bf0b322]

(5) In sub-paragraph (1)(a) for the words “a statement of the evidence” there shall be substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ba994cda5d2bf0891fffac4bf0b322] “ copies of the documents containing the evidence (including oral evidence) ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ba994cda5d2bf0891fffac4bf0b322].

(6) The following sub-paragraph shall be inserted after sub-paragraph (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ba994cda5d2bf0891fffac4bf0b322]

> (1A) Regulations under sub-paragraph (1)(a) above may provide that there shall be no requirement for copies of documents to accompany the copy of the notice of transfer if they are referred to, in documents sent with the notice of transfer, as having already been supplied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ba994cda5d2bf0891fffac4bf0b322]

(7) In paragraph 6 of Schedule 6 to the 1991 Act (reporting restrictions) in sub-paragraph (8) for the words “sub-paragraphs (5) and (6)” there shall be substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ba994cda5d2bf0891fffac4bf0b322] “ sub-paragraphs (5) and (7) ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ba994cda5d2bf0891fffac4bf0b322].

(8) This section applies where a notice of transfer is given under section 4 of the 1987 Act or served under section 53 of the 1991 Act (as the case may be) on or after the appointed day. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ba994cda5d2bf0891fffac4bf0b322]

(9)[^c11039721] The reference in subsection (8) to the appointed day is to such day as is appointed for the purposes of this section by the Secretary of State by order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-13ba994cda5d2bf0891fffac4bf0b322]
