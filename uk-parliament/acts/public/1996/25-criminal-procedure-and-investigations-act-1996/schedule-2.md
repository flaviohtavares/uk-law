## SCHEDULE 2 (affected by repeal, see note) — Statements and depositions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd][^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

Section 68. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

### Statements (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

**1.**[^c11040891] (1) Sub-paragraph (2) applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

- (a) a written statement has been admitted in evidence in proceedings before a magistrates’ court inquiring into an offence as examining justices, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]
- (b) in those proceedings a person has been committed for trial, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]
- (c) for the purposes of section 5A of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd] [^c11040901]Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 the statement complied with section 5B of that Act prior to the committal for trial, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]
- (d) the statement purports to be signed by a justice of the peace, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]
- (e) sub-paragraph (3) does not prevent sub-paragraph (2) applying. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

(2) Where this sub-paragraph applies the statement may without further proof be read as evidence on the trial of the accused, whether for the offence for which he was committed for trial or for any other offence arising out of the same transaction or set of circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

(3) Sub-paragraph (2) does not apply if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

- (a) it is proved that the statement was not signed by the justice by whom it purports to have been signed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]
- (b) the court of trial at its discretion orders that sub-paragraph (2) shall not apply, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]
- (c) a party to the proceedings objects to sub-paragraph (2) applying. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

(4) If a party to the proceedings objects to sub-paragraph (2) applying the court of trial may order that the objection shall have no effect if the court considers it to be in the interests of justice so to order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

### Depositions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

**2.**[^c11040911] (1) Sub-paragraph (2) applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

- (a) in pursuance of section 97A of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 (summons or warrant to have evidence taken as a deposition *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd] etc. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]) a person has had his evidence taken as a deposition for the purposes of proceedings before a magistrates’ court inquiring into an offence as examining justices, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]
- (b) the deposition has been admitted in evidence in those proceedings, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]
- (c) in those proceedings a person has been committed for trial, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]
- (d) for the purposes of section 5A of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 the deposition complied with section 5C of that Act prior to the committal for trial, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]
- (e) the deposition purports to be signed by the justice before whom it purports to have been taken, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]
- (f) sub-paragraph (3) does not prevent sub-paragraph (2) applying. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

(2) Where this sub-paragraph applies the deposition may without further proof be read as evidence on the trial of the accused, whether for the offence for which he was committed for trial or for any other offence arising out of the same transaction or set of circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

(3) Sub-paragraph (2) does not apply if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

- (a) it is proved that the deposition was not signed by the justice by whom it purports to have been signed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]
- (b) the court of trial at its discretion orders that sub-paragraph (2) shall not apply, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]
- (c) a party to the proceedings objects to sub-paragraph (2) applying. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

(4) If a party to the proceedings objects to sub-paragraph (2) applying the court of trial may order that the objection shall have no effect if the court considers it to be in the interests of justice so to order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

### Signatures (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

**3.** (1) A justice who signs a certificate authenticating one or more relevant statements or depositions shall be treated for the purposes of paragraphs 1 and 2 as signing the statement or deposition or (as the case may be) each of them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

(2) For this purpose— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

- (a) a relevant statement is a written statement made by a person for the purposes of proceedings before a magistrates’ court inquiring into an offence as examining justices; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]
- (b) a relevant deposition is a deposition made in pursuance of section 97A of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd] [^c11040921]Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 for the purposes of such proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

### Time limit for objection (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

**4.** Criminal Procedure Rules may[^c19068291] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd] make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

- (a) requiring an objection under paragraph 1(3)(c) or 2(3)(c) to be made within a period prescribed in the rules; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]
- (b) allowing the court of trial at its discretion to permit such an objection to be made outside any such period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

### Retrial (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

**5.** In Schedule 2 to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd] [^c11040941]Criminal Appeal Act 1968 (procedural and other provisions applicable on order for retrial) in paragraph 1 for the words from “section 13(3)” to “before the original trial” there shall be substituted *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd] “ paragraphs 1 and 2 of Schedule 2 to the Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996 (use of written statements and depositions) shall not apply to any written statement or deposition read as evidence at the original trial ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd].

### Repeals (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

**6.** (1) Section 13(3) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd] [^c11040951]Criminal Justice Act 1925 (which relates to depositions taken before examining justices and is superseded by paragraph 2 above) shall be omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

(2) Section 7 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd] [^c11040961]Criminal Justice Act 1967 (which is superseded by paragraph 3 above) shall be omitted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

### Commencement (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]

**7.**[^c11040971] This Schedule shall have effect in accordance with provision made by the Secretary of State by order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c129d846641c33f9316bd80a3568b0dd]
