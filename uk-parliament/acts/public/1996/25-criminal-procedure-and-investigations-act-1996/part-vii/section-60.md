## 60. Reporting of assertions: offences.[^c-c9b29eef99a139fd7063e7767890c605]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] If an assertion is published or included in a relevant programme in contravention of section 59, each of the following persons is guilty of an offence— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] in the case of publication in a newspaper or periodical, any proprietor, any editor and any publisher of the newspaper or periodical; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] in the case of publication in any other form, the person publishing the assertion; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (c (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] in the case of an assertion included in a relevant programme, any body corporate engaged in providing the service in which the programme is included and any person having functions in relation to the programme corresponding to those of an editor of a newspaper. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine of an amount not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] Where a person is charged with an offence under this section it is a defence to prove that at the time of the alleged offence— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] he was not aware, and neither suspected nor had reason to suspect, that an order under section 58(7) or (8) had effect at that time, or *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] he was not aware, and neither suspected nor had reason to suspect, that the publication or programme in question was of, or (as the case may be) included, the assertion in question. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

(4 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] Where an offence under this section committed by a body corporate is proved to have been committed with the consent or connivance of, or to be attributable to any neglect on the part of— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] a director, manager, secretary or other similar officer of the body corporate, or *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] a person purporting to act in any such capacity, *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

he as well as the body corporate is guilty of the offence and liable to be proceeded against and punished accordingly. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

(5 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] In relation to a body corporate whose affairs are managed by its members “ *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]**director *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]**” in subsection (4) means a member of the body corporate. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

(6 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] Subsections (2) and (3) of section 59 apply for the purposes of this section as they apply for the purposes of that. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
