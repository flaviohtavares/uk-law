## 58. Orders in respect of certain assertions.[^c11040011][^c-c9b29eef99a139fd7063e7767890c605]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] This section applies where a person has been convicted of an offence and a speech in mitigation is made by him or on his behalf before— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] a court *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] in Northern Ireland[^c-c1730c8ea2c89f8946b99932ba658b13] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] determining what sentence should be passed on him in respect of the offence, *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] [^c-e4db358619921525ab559c4d22dc6b86]... *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-e4db358619921525ab559c4d22dc6b86][^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] This section also applies where a sentence has been passed on a person in respect of an offence and a submission relating to the sentence is made by him or on his behalf before— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] a court *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] in Northern Ireland[^c-ac8a8131d84ef5a0b9d055efe7828743] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] hearing an appeal against or reviewing the sentence, or *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] a court *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] in Northern Ireland[^c-3614baa8a673a8f11be6344fd64e82d5] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] determining whether to grant leave to appeal against the sentence. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] Where it appears to the court that there is a real possibility that an order under subsection (8) will be made in relation to the assertion, the court may make an order under subsection (7) in relation to the assertion. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

(4 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] Where there are substantial grounds for believing— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] that an assertion forming part of the speech or submission is derogatory to a person’s character (for instance, because it suggests that his conduct is or has been criminal, immoral or improper), and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] that the assertion is false or that the facts asserted are irrelevant to the sentence, *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

the court may make an order under subsection (8) in relation to the assertion. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

(5 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] An order under subsection (7) or (8) must not be made in relation to an assertion if it appears to the court that the assertion was previously made— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] at the trial at which the person was convicted of the offence, or *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] during any other proceedings relating to the offence. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

(6 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] Section 59 has effect where a court makes an order under subsection (7) or (8). *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

(7 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] An order under this subsection— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] may be made at any time before the court has made a determination with regard to sentencing; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] may be revoked at any time by the court; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (c (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] subject to paragraph (b), shall cease to have effect when the court makes a determination with regard to sentencing. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

(8 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] An order under this subsection— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] may be made after the court has made a determination with regard to sentencing, but only if it is made as soon as is reasonably practicable after the making of the determination; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] may be revoked at any time by the court; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (c (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] subject to paragraph (b), shall cease to have effect at the end of the period of 12 months beginning with the day on which it is made; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (d (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] may be made whether or not an order has been made under subsection (7) with regard to the case concerned. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

(9 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] For the purposes of subsections (7) and (8) the court makes a determination with regard to sentencing— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] when it determines what sentence should be passed (where this section applies by virtue of subsection (1)(a)); *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-79c89b814eda0015632916eb32d5147f][^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (c (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] when it determines what the sentence should be (where this section applies by virtue of subsection (2)(a)); *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (d (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] when it determines whether to grant leave to appeal (where this section applies by virtue of subsection (2)(b)). *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
