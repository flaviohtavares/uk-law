# Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996, section 64

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2025-10-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Checks against fingerprints etc.[^c11040191]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1)[^c19585361] In section 63A of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361] [^c11040201]Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 the following subsections shall be substituted for subsection (1) (checks against fingerprints *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361] etc. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361] where a person has been arrested on suspicion of being involved in a recordable offence)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361]

> (1) Where a person has been arrested on suspicion of being involved in a recordable offence or has been charged with such an offence or has been informed that he will be reported for such an offence, fingerprints or samples or the information derived from samples taken under any power conferred by this Part of this Act from the person may be checked against— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361]
>
> - (a) other fingerprints or samples to which the person seeking to check has access and which are held by or on behalf of a police force (or police forces) falling within subsection (1A) below or are held in connection with or as a result of an investigation of an offence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361]
> - (b) information derived from other samples if the information is contained in records to which the person seeking to check has access and which are held as mentioned in paragraph (a) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361]
>
> (1A) Each of the following police forces falls within this subsection— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361]
>
> - (a) a police force within the meaning given by section 62 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361] [^c11040211]Police Act 1964 (which relates to England and Wales); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361]
> - (b) a police force within the meaning given by section 50 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361] [^c11040221]Police (Scotland) Act 1967; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361]
> - (c) the Royal Ulster Constabulary and the Royal Ulster Constabulary Reserve; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361]
> - (d) the States of Jersey Police Force; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361]
> - (e) the salaried police force of the Island of Guernsey; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361]
> - (f) the Isle of Man Constabulary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361]

(2) This section applies where a person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361]

- (a) is arrested on suspicion of being involved in a recordable offence, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361]
- (b) is charged with a recordable offence, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361]
- (c) is informed that he will be reported for a recordable offence, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361]

after the day on which this Act is passed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19585361]
