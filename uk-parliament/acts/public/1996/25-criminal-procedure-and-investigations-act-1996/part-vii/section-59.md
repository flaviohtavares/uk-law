## 59. Restriction on reporting of assertions.[^c11040021][^c-c9b29eef99a139fd7063e7767890c605]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] Where a court makes an order under section 58(7) or (8) in relation to any assertion, at any time when the order has effect the assertion must not— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] be published in *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] Northern Ireland[^c-4691e60847ca20c8e3e0fb4a2366827f] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] in a written publication available to the public, or *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] be included in a relevant programme for reception in *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] Northern Ireland[^c-3e4e0e84e9b1e1081f1133ea0c5058a8] *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] In this section— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

- “ *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]**relevant programme *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]**” means a programme included in a programme service, within the meaning of the *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] [^c11040031]Broadcasting Act 1990; *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- “ *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]**written publication *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]**” includes a film, a soundtrack and any other record in permanent form but does not include an indictment or other document prepared for use in particular legal proceedings. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] For the purposes of this section an assertion is published or included in a programme if the material published or included— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] names the person about whom the assertion is made or, without naming him, contains enough to make it likely that members of the public will identify him as the person about whom it is made, and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db] reproduces the actual wording of the matter asserted or contains its substance. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36e558b89920072b2f10f661280f47db]
