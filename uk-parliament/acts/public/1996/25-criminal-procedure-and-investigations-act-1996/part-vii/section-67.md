# Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996, section 67

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2025-10-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Witness summons: securing attendance of witness.[^c11040291][^c11040301]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In section 4(1) of the [^c11040311]Criminal Procedure (Attendance of Witnesses) Act 1965 (judge of High Court may issue warrant to arrest witness in respect of whom witness summons is in force) for the words “High Court” there shall be substituted “ Crown Court ”.

(2) This section shall have effect in accordance with provision made by the Secretary of State by order.
