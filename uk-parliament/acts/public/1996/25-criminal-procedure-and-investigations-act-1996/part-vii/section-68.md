# Criminal Procedure and Investigations Act 1996, section 68

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2025-10-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/25

## 68. Use of written statements and depositions at trial.

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 2 to this Act (which relates to the use at the trial of written statements and depositions admitted in evidence in committal proceedings) shall have effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-cdd07c91305179d3825013c0dd83155e]
