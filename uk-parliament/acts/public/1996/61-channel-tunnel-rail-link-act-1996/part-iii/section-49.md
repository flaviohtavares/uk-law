# Channel Tunnel Rail Link Act 1996, section 49

> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Noise insulation regulations: procedure.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 20 of the [^c1252050]Land Compensation Act 1973 (sound-proofing of buildings affected by public works) shall have effect, in relation to regulations under that section which relate only to noise caused or expected to be caused by the construction or use of works authorised by this Act, with the substitution for subsection (9) of—

> (9) An instrument containing regulations under this section shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
