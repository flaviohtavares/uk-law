# Channel Tunnel Rail Link Act 1996, section 14

> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Operation and use of authorised works.[^c1251940]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The nominated undertaker may, in relation to the works authorised by this Part of this Act—

- (a) operate them for the purpose of providing infrastructure services, and
- (b) use them for the purpose of providing services for the carriage of passengers or goods.

(2) In subsection (1) above, the reference to infrastructure services is to services which are network services or station services for the purposes of Part I of the [^c1251941]Railways Act 1993.
