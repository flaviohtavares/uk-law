# Channel Tunnel Rail Link Act 1996, section 42A

> Text as at 2026-04-29.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42A. Strategic Rail Authority as agent of Secretary of State.[^c1252034]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36cd2ff35f99e48d830bb989f8c09981] The Strategic Rail Authority may do anything which it arranges with the Secretary of State to do on his behalf in connection with any agreement or other arrangement made by him for the purpose of securing the design, construction, financing, maintenance or operation of the rail link or any of the other works authorised by this Part of this Act. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36cd2ff35f99e48d830bb989f8c09981]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36cd2ff35f99e48d830bb989f8c09981] Subsection (1) above— *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36cd2ff35f99e48d830bb989f8c09981]

- (a (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36cd2ff35f99e48d830bb989f8c09981] does not authorise the Strategic Rail Authority to exercise any function conferred or imposed by or by virtue of any enactment, and *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36cd2ff35f99e48d830bb989f8c09981]
- (b (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36cd2ff35f99e48d830bb989f8c09981] is subject to the terms of the agreement or other arrangement. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36cd2ff35f99e48d830bb989f8c09981]

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-36cd2ff35f99e48d830bb989f8c09981] Sections 207 and 208 of the Transport Act 2000 do not apply to the power conferred by this section. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-36cd2ff35f99e48d830bb989f8c09981]
