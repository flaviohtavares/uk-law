# Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996, section 146

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/53
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 146. Orders, regulations and directions.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Orders, regulations and directions under this Act may make different provision for different cases or descriptions of case, including different provision for different areas.

(2) Orders and regulations under this Act may contain such incidental, supplementary or transitional provisions and savings as the the authority making them[^key-f2e7b6cde6d4cfe3409e6f13696ebea1] considers appropriate.

(3) Orders and regulations under this Act shall be made by statutory instrument which, except for—

- (a) orders and regulations subject to affirmative resolution procedure (see sections 104(4), 105(4), 106(4) , 106A[^key-073f5d571117daa814ba4dabbc210816] and 114(5)),
- (b) orders under section 150(3), or
- (c) regulations which only prescribe forms or particulars to be contained in forms,

shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
