# Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996, section 19

## 19. Disabled facilities grants: owner’s and tenant’s applications.[^c11253241]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local housing authority shall not entertain an application for a [^c17512471]. . . grant unless they are satisfied—

- (a) that the applicant has, or proposes to acquire, an owner’s interest in every parcel of land on which the relevant works are to be carried out, or
- (b) that the applicant is a tenant (alone or jointly with others)—
  - (i) in the case of an application in respect of works to a dwelling, of the dwelling, or
  - (ii) in the case of a common parts application, of a flat in the building,

  and, in either case, does not have or propose to acquire such an owner’s interest as is mentioned in paragraph (a) , or[^c17512931]
- (c)[^key-3fdd0be5bd41dd2003e9af92a0737e28] that the applicant is an occupier (alone or jointly with others) of a qualifying houseboat or a caravan and, in the case of a caravan, that at the time the application was made the caravan was stationed on land within the authority’s area.

(2) References in this Chapter to an “**owner's application**” or a “**tenant's application**” or an “occupier’s application”[^c17513221], in relation to a [^c17513241]. . . grant, shall be construed accordingly.

(3) In accordance with directions given by the Secretary of State, a local housing authority may treat the condition in subsection (1)(a) as met by a person who has, or proposes to acquire, an owner’s interest in only part of the land concerned.

(4) In this Chapter, in relation to an application for a [^c17513131]. . . grant—

- “**qualifying owner’s interest**” means an owner’s interest meeting the condition in subsection (1)(a) or treated by virtue of subsection (3) as meeting that condition; and
- “**qualifying tenant**” means a tenant who meets the conditions in subsection (1)(b).

(5) In this Chapter “**tenant**”, in relation to a [^c17513151]. . . grant, includes—

- (a) a secure tenant, introductory tenant or statutory tenant,
- (aa)[^key-4e315c39a49e7c5f470e5b5a029c8a90] a tenant or licensee under a secure contract within the meaning of the Renting Homes (Wales) Act [2016 (anaw 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2016/1) (see section 8 of that Act),
- (ab) a tenant or licensee under an introductory standard contract within the meaning of the Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016 (see section 16 of that Act),
- (b) a protected occupier under the [^c11253251]Rent (Agriculture) Act 1976 or a person in occupation under an assured agricultural occupancy within the meaning of Part I of the [^c11253261]Housing Act 1988,
- (c) an employee (whether full-time or part-time) who occupies the dwelling or flat concerned for the better performance of his duties, and
- (d) a person having a licence to occupy the dwelling or flat concerned which satisfies such conditions as may be specified by order of the Secretary of State;

and other expressions relating to tenancies, in the context of an application for [^c17513161]. . . grant, shall be construed accordingly.
