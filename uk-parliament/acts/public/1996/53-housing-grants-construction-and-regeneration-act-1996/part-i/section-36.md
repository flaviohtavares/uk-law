# Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/53
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Delayed payment of mandatory grant.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The local housing authority may approve an application for a grant[^c17532761] on terms that payment of the grant, or part of it, will not be made before a date specified in the notification of their decision on the application.

(2) That date shall not be more than twelve months, or such other period as may be specified by order of the Secretary of State, after the date of the application.
