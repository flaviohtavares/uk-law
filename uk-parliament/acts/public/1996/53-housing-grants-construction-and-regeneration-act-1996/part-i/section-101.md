# Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/53

## 101. Minor definitions: Part I.[^c11254161]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “development corporation” has the same meaning as in the Housing Act 1985;[^c-3e44d98d95a14cd985f05a4295a5bb9b]
- “**dwelling**” means a building or part of a building occupied or intended to be occupied as a separate dwelling, together with any yard, garden, outhouses and appurtenances belonging to it or usually enjoyed with it;
- [^c17636051]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^c17636051]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**housing action trust**” means a housing action trust established under Part III of the [^c11254181]Housing Act 1988 and includes any body established by order under section 88 of the Housing Act 1988;
- [^c17636051]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**introductory tenancy**” and “**introductory tenant**” have the same meaning as in Chapter I of Part V of the [^c11254191]Housing Act 1996;
- “**local authority**” and “**local housing authority**” have the same meaning as in the [^c11254201]Housing Act 1985;
- [^c17636051]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- [^c-c1ed4c3a72e85bc7fae40d997d519027]...
- “**owner**”, in relation to a dwelling, has the meaning given by section 99 [^c17636111]. . . ;
- “**owner’s interest**”, in relation to any premises, means—
  - (a) an estate in fee simple absolute in possession, or
  - (b) a term of years absolute of which not less than five years remain unexpired at the date of the application,

  whether held by the applicant alone or jointly with others;
- [^c17636051]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**prescribed**” means prescribed by regulations made by the Secretary of State;
- “**registered social landlord**” has the same meaning as in Part I of the Housing Act 1996;
- “**renewal area**” has the same meaning as in Part VII of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989;
- “secure tenant” has[^c17641321] the same meaning as in Part IV of the [^c11254221]Housing Act 1985;
- “statutory tenant” means a[^c17641341] statutory tenant within the meaning of the [^c11254231]Rent Act 1977 or the [^c11254241]Rent (Agriculture) Act 1976;
- “**tenancy**” includes a sub-tenancy and an agreement for a tenancy or sub-tenancy;
- “**tenant**” includes a sub-tenant and any person deriving title under the original tenant or sub-tenant;
- “**urban development corporation**” has the same meaning as in the Housing Act 1985 and includes any body established by order under section 165B of the [^c11254251]Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980.
