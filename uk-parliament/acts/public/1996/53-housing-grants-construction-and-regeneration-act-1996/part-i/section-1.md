## 1. Grants for improvements and repairs, &c.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Grants are available from local housing authorities in accordance with this Chapter towards the cost of works required for—

- (a) [^c17267171]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) [^c17267171]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) the provision of facilities for disabled persons
  - (i)[^c17272581] in dwellings, qualifying houseboats and caravans[^key-672ed3f2f08b750ab306b5bc358d729f], and
  - (ii) in the common parts of buildings containing one or more flats.

(2) [^c17273011]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) [^c17273011]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) [^c17273011]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) [^c17273011]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) In the following provisions of this Chapter the expression “**grant**”means a grant under subsection (1) above[^c17511231].
