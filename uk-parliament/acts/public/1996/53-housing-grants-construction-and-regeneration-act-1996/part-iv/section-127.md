# Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996, section 127

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 127. Regeneration and development: forms of assistance.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Financial assistance under section 126 (powers of Secretary of State to give financial assistance) may be given in any form.

(2) Assistance may, in particular, be given by way of—

- (a) grants,
- (b) loans,
- (c) guarantees, or
- (d) incurring expenditure for the benefit of the person assisted.

(3) The Secretary of State must not, in giving financial assistance under section 126, purchase loan or share capital in a company.
