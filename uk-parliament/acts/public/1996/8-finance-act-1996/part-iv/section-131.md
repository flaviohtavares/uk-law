# Finance Act 1996, section 131

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131. Interest on overdue tax.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 110 of the [^c14612211]Finance Act 1995 (interest on overdue tax) shall be deemed to have been enacted with the insertion after subsection (3) of the following subsection—

> (4) So far as it relates to partnerships whose trades, professions or businesses were set up and commenced before 6th April 1994, subsection (1) above has effect as respects the year 1997-98 and subsequent years of assessment.

(2) In subsection (3) of section 86 of the [^c14612221]Taxes Management Act 1970 (which was substituted by the said section 110), for the words “section 93” there shall be substituted the words “ section 92 ”.

(3) In Schedule 19 to the [^c14612231]Finance Act 1994, paragraph 23 (which is superseded by the said section 110) shall cease to have effect.
