# Finance Act 1996, section 176

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 176. Retirement relief: age limits.[^c14612971]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In each of sections 163 and 164 of, and paragraph 5 of Schedule 6 to, the [^c14612981]Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992 (retirement relief), for “the age of 55”, wherever occurring, there shall be substituted “ the age of 50 ”.

(2) The amendments made by this section shall apply in relation to disposals on or after 28th November 1995.
