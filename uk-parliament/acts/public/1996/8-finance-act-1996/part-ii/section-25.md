## 25. EC Second VAT Simplification Directive.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Sections 26 to 29 of and Schedule 3 to this Act are for the purpose of giving effect to requirements of the directive of the Council of the European Communities dated 17th May 1977 No.[77/388/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1977/0388) and the amendments of that directive by the directive of that Council dated 10th April 1995 No.[95/7/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/1995/0007) (amendments with a view to introducing new simplification measures with regard to value added tax).
