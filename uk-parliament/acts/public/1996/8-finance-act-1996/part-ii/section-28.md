## 28. Adaptation of aircraft and hovercraft.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 22 of the [^c14609871]Value Added Tax Act 1994 shall be omitted.

(2) This section shall apply to supplies made on or after 1st January 1996.
