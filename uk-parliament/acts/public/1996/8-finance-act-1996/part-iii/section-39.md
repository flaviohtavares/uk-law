# Finance Act 1996, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Landfill tax.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A tax, to be known as landfill tax, shall be charged in accordance with this Part.

(2) The tax shall be under the care and management of the Commissioners of Customs and Excise.
