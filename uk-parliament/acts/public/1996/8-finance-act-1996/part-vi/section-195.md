## 195. Regulations concerning administration: sub-delegation to the Board.[^c14613481]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 98 of the [^c14613491]Finance Act 1986 (Treasury regulations with respect to administration etc) after subsection (1) there shall be inserted—

> (1A) The power conferred on the Treasury by subsection (1) above includes power to make provision conferring or imposing on the Board functions which involve the exercise of a discretion.
