# Finance Act 1996, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Spirits: rate of duty.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 5 of the [^c14609371]Alcoholic Liquor Duties Act 1979 (spirits), for “£20.60” there shall be substituted “ £19.78 ”.

(2) This section shall be deemed to have come into force at 6 o’clock in the evening of 28th November 1995.
