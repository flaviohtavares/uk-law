> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Increase in general rate.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In Schedule 1 to the [^c14609601]Vehicle Excise and Registration Act 1994 (annual rates of duty), in paragraph 1(2) (the general rate), for “£135” there shall be substituted “ £140 ”.

(2) Subsection (1) above applies in relation to licences taken out after 28th November 1995.
