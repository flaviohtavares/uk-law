# Finance Act 1996, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-03-18. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1996/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Relief for marine voyages.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following provisions of the [^c14609531]Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act 1979 are hereby repealed—

- (a) section 18 (fuel for ships in home waters), and
- (b) in subsection (1) of section 19 (fuel used in fishing boats, etc.), paragraph(a) and the words from “by the owner” to “be”.

(2)[^c14609541] This section shall come into force on such day as the Commissioners of Customs and Excise may by order made by statutory instrument appoint.
