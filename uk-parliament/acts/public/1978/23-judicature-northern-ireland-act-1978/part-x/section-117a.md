# Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978, section 117A

## 117A. Allowances for judges.[^c-cce151b1a136d4e92f8e4cf9a7291c69][^c13728061]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Department of Justice shall pay[^c-e7eff63a0cbbb5a650e98024feb50710] to any judge of the Court of Appeal or of the High Court, in addition to his salary, such allowances as may be determined by the Lord Chancellor with the concurrence of the Treasury.
