# Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978, section 105

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (33).** Text as at 2026-09-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 105. Solicitors to be officers of court.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any person duly admitted as a solicitor in Northern Ireland or entitled to practise therein as a solicitor shall be an officer of the Court of Judicature[^c20733531], but nothing in this subsection shall affect the operation of any statutory provision (including a provision contained in this Act) which imposes on officers of a court restrictions as to practice as solicitors.

(2) References in any statutory provision in force in Northern Ireland to attorneys or proctors shall be construed as references to solicitors of the Court of Judicature.
