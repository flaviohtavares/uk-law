# Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978, section 79

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (33).** Text as at 2026-09-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/23

## 79. Accountant General to keep bank account.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-a0e98ce90db8f7035d930e464284f5c0][^key-be7f66543539a36855cf652097ef55f7] The Accountant General shall maintain an account at the Bank of Ireland or at such other bank or banks as the Lord Chancellor may, with the concurrence of the Treasury, designate and shall pay all sums received by him into that account and all sums payable by him out of it.

(2) References to the Bank of Ireland in any statutory provision relating to sums in the Court of Judicature[^c20732721] or the county court shall, when a designation is made under subsection (1), be construed, in connection with the sums to which that provision relates, as references to a bank so designated.
