# Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978, section 84

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (33).** Text as at 2026-09-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/23

## 84. Statutory deposits.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 80(1) shall apply to a statutory deposit as it applies to the payment of money into the Court of Judicature[^c20732861]; and section 80(2) and (3) shall apply to the deposit with the Accountant General of securities in lieu of money in exercise of a right to make such a deposit conferred by section 29 of the [^c13727461]Tramways (Ireland) Act 1860 or by rules made by virtue of subsection (2).

(2) Sections 81 and 82 shall apply to a statutory deposit and to securities which are in the hands of the Accountant General in consequence of a person’s having availed himself of such a right as is mentioned in subsection (1) and rules made under section 82 may include separate provisions with respect to such deposits and securities and, in particular, may authorise the deposit, under the statutory provisions mentioned in subsection (3), of securities in lieu of money.

(3) In this section and sections 83 and 85 “**statutory deposit**” means money deposited with the Accountant General under or by virtue of—

- (a) section 29 of the [^c13727471]Tramways (Ireland) Act 1860;
- (b)[^c13727481] Article 7 of the Industrial Assurance (Northern Ireland) Order 1979;
- (c) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c13727491]
- (d)[^c13727501] Article 90(2)(*b*) or 93(1)(*a*) of the Road Traffic (Northern Ireland) Order 1981,

or any statutory provision which was re-enacted by any of those provisions.
