# Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978, section 77

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (33).** Text as at 2026-09-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/23

## 77. Accountant General.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-b8ee248565a7d81b090876e6580ef74a] Such person as the Lord Chancellor may designate shall be the Accountant General of the Court of Judicature[^c20732671].

(2) There shall be maintained in or near the Royal Courts of Justice an office for the business of the Accountant General under this Part.
