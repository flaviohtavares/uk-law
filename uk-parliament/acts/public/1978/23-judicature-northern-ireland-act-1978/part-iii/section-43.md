# Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (33).** Text as at 2026-09-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/23

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Appeals to Supreme Court from High Court.[^c20732011]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Part of this Act affects the operation of Part II of the [^c13725631]Administration of Justice Act 1969 (which provides that an appeal from the High Court shall in certain circumstances lie direct to the Supreme Court[^c20732051]).
