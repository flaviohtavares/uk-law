# Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978, section 52

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (33).** Text as at 2026-09-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Crown Court rules.[^c13726311]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c20722421] Subject to any statutory provision, Crown Court rules may be made in accordance with section 53A for the purpose of regulating and prescribing—

- (a) the practice and procedure to be followed in the Crown Court; and
- (b) the form and content of indictments,

and in this or any other statutory provision having effect in Northern Ireland “**Crown Court rules**” means rules so made.

(2) Sections 1 and 2 of the [^c13726321]Indictments Act (Northern Ireland) 1945 shall cease to have effect on such date as may be appointed in Crown Court rules made for the purpose mentioned in subsection (1)(b).
