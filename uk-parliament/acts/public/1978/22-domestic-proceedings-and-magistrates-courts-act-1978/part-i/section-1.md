# Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates’ Courts Act 1978, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2014-04-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/22

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Grounds of application for financial provision.

*Extent: E+W*

Either party to a marriage may apply to the family court[^c-b770ed990b7f0bfb66d6fbb89e3175e4] for an order under section 2 of this Act on the ground that the other party to the marriage [^c3518591]... —

- (a) has failed to provide reasonable maintenance for the applicant; or
- (b) has failed to provide, or to make a proper contribution towards, reasonable maintenance for any child of the family; or
- (c) has behaved in such a way that the applicant cannot reasonably be expected to live with the respondent; or
- (d) has deserted the applicant.
