# Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates’ Courts Act 1978, section 87

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2014-04-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Expenses.

*Extent: E+W+S*

There shall be defrayed out of moneys provided by Parliament any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable out of moneys so provided under any other enactment.
