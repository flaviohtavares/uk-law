# Employment Protection (Consolidation) Act 1978, SCHEDULE 5

> Text as at 2007-08-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 5[^c1793916]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

### 1.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-04-01).**

A Regional Health Authority, District Health Authority[^c1793917] Family Practitioner Committee[^c1793918], preserved Board (within the meaning of section 15(6) of the [^c1793919]National Health Service Reorganisation Act 1973), special health authority, Health Board or the Common Services Agency for the Scottish Health Service.

### 2.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-04-01).**

The Dental Estimates Board.

### 3.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-04-01).**

Any joint committee constituted under section 2(11) of the[^c1793920] [^c1793921]National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978.

### 4.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-04-01).**

The Public Health Laboratory Service Board.

### 5.[^c1793922]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 1991-04-01).**

A State Hospital Management Committee.
