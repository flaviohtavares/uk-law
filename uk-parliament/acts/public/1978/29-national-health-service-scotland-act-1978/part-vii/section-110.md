# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 110

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (106).** Text as at 2026-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/29

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Citation, extent and commencement.

*Extent: S*

(1) This Act may be cited as the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978.

(2) Subject to subsection (3)[^c14175701]subsections (2A) and (3), this Act extends only to Scotland.

(2A)[^c14175721] Section 87B(3) extends also to England and Wales.

(3) Paragraph 3 of Schedule 10 extends to other parts of the United Kingdom.

(4) This Act shall come into force on 1st January 1979.
