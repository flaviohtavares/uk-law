# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (106).** Text as at 2026-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101. Protection of Health Boards and Common Services Agency.[^c14172551][^c14172531][^c14172541]

*Extent: S*

Section 166 of the [^c14172561]Public Health (Scotland) Act 1897 (which relates to the protection of local authorities and their officers) shall apply in relation to a Health Board , an[^c14172571] NHS trust and the Agency in like manner as the said section applies in relation to a local authority, but with the substitution for any reference to that Act of a reference to this Act.
