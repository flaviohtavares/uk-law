# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 17T

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 17T). (not yet in force: Payments by Health Boards under pharmaceutical care services contracts)[^c20973211]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The Scottish Ministers may give directions as to payments to be made under pharmaceutical care services contracts.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: A pharmaceutical care services contract must require payments to be made under it in accordance with directions for the time being in force under this section.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: A direction under subsection (1) may in particular—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: provide for payments to be made by reference to compliance with standards or the achievement of levels of performance;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: provide for payments to be made by reference to—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: any scheme or scale specified in the direction;)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: a determination made by any person in accordance with factors specified in the direction;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: provide that the whole or any part of a payment is subject to conditions (including a condition that the whole or any part of a payment is liable to be paid by a Health Board only if they are satisfied as to such conditions as may be specified in the direction);)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: make provision having effect from a date before the date of the direction, provided that, having regard to the direction as a whole, the provision is not detrimental to the persons to whose remuneration it relates.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Before giving a direction under subsection (1), the Scottish Ministers—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: must consult any body appearing to them to be representative of persons to whose remuneration the direction would relate; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: may consult such other persons as they think appropriate.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: The Scottish Ministers must publish directions given by them under subsection (1) in the Drug Tariff or in such other manner as they consider appropriate.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: References in this section to payments include fees, allowances and reimbursements.)*
