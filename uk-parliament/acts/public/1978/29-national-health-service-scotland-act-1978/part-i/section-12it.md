# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 12IT

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (106).** Text as at 2026-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/29

## 12IT. HIS: duty on Health Boards to assist staffing functions[^key-8dd9cf4ba13d19266fa9ee22221f8518]

*Extent: S*

Every Health Board, relevant Special Health Board and the Agency must give such assistance to HIS as it requires in the performance of its functions under sections 12IP to 12IS.
