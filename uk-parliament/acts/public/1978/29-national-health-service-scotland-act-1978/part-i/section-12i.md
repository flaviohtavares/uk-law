# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 12I

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (106).** Text as at 2026-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12I. Duty in relation to governance of staff[^c20957581]

*Extent: S*

It shall be the duty of every Health Board and Special Health Board and of HIS[^c-b3ed9b085fb4a73dd88af0e1880353a9] and the Agency to put and keep in place arrangements for the purposes of—

- (a) improving the management of the officers employed by it;
- (b) monitoring such management; and
- (c) workforce planning.
