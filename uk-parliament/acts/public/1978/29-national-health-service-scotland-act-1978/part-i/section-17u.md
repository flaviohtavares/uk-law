# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 17U

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## (not yet in force: 17U). (not yet in force: Other mandatory contract terms: pharmaceutical care services contracts)[^c20973211]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: A pharmaceutical care services contract must include (in addition to provisions required by or under other provisions of this Part) such provision as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Regulations under subsection (1) may in particular—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: make provision as to the manner in which, and the standards to which, services must be provided;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: provide that the Scottish Ministers may give directions as to the manner in which, and the standards to which, services must be provided;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: make provision as to—)*
  - ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: the persons who are to perform services;)*
  - ((not yet in force: ii)) *(not yet in force: the area in which services are to be provided;)*
  - ((not yet in force: iii)) *(not yet in force: the persons to whom services are to be provided;)*
  - ((not yet in force: iv)) *(not yet in force: requirements to be complied with where a contractor provides any pharmaceutical care services indirectly (including requirements as to the pharmaceutical care services which may or may not be so provided);)*
  - ((not yet in force: v)) *(not yet in force: the variation of terms of the contract (except terms required by or under this Part);)*
  - ((not yet in force: vi)) *(not yet in force: rights of entry and inspection (including inspection of clinical records and other documents);)*
  - ((not yet in force: vii)) *(not yet in force: the circumstances in which, and the manner in which, the contract may be terminated;)*
  - ((not yet in force: viii)) *(not yet in force: enforcement;)*
  - ((not yet in force: ix)) *(not yet in force: the adjudication of disputes.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Regulations making provision in pursuance of subsection (2)(c)(iii) may make provision as to the circumstances in which a contractor—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: must, or may, accept a person as a person to whom services are provided under the contract;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: may decline to accept a person as such a person; or)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: may terminate the contractor's responsibility for a person.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Regulations making provision in pursuance of subsection (2)(c)(v) may—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: make provision as to the circumstances in which a Health Board may unilaterally vary the terms of a contract;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: make provision suspending or terminating any duty under the contract to provide services of a prescribed description.)*

((not yet in force: 5)) *(not yet in force: Regulations making provision of the kind described in subsection (4)(b) may prescribe services by reference to the manner or circumstances in which they are provided.)*

((not yet in force: 6)) *(not yet in force: A pharmaceutical care services contract must contain provision requiring the contractor to comply with directions for the time being in force given by the Scottish Ministers under regulations under subsection (1).)*
