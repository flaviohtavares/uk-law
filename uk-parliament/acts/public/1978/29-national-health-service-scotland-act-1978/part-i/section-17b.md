# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 17B

## 17B. Reimbursement of Health Board’s costs.[^c14161241][^c-046ff196cf20fcbfdda9dbc11f657411]

*Extent: S*

(1) Where a Health Board provide goods or services under this Act for an individual for whose health care it is not their function to provide by virtue of section 2(1), in circumstances where the condition of the individual is such that he needs those goods or services and, having regard to his condition, it is not practicable, before providing them, to enter into an NHS contract for their provision, that Health Board shall be remunerated in respect of that provision by the Health Board or Health and Social Care trust[^c-6886e38b8db630a1b4cb6f7a9484be47] which has the function, or the Health Authority or Special Health Authority[^c14161251] which has the primary functions, of providing those goods or services to that individual.

(2) The rate of any remuneration payable by virtue of subsection (1) shall be calculated in such manner or on such basis as may be determined by the Secretary of State.

(3) In any case where—

- (a) a Health Board provide goods or services for the benefit of an individual; and
- (b) the provision of those goods and services is not pursuant to an NHS contract; and
- (c) the individual is resident outside the United Kingdom and is of a description (being a description associating the individual with another country) specified for the purposes of this subsection by a direction made by the Secretary of State,

the Health Board shall be remunerated by the Secretary of State in respect of the provision of the goods or services at such rate or rates as he considers appropriate.

(4)[^c-16d789c404ce6c8b84875083024e553d] In subsection (1)—

- (a) “**Health and Social Care trust**” means a Health and Social Care trust established under the Health and Personal Social Services (Northern Ireland) Order 1991 ([S.I. 1991/194 (N.I. 1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisi/1991/194)); and
- (b) the reference to a function of a Health and Social Care trust is a reference to a function of such a trust mentioned in Article 10(1) of that Order.
