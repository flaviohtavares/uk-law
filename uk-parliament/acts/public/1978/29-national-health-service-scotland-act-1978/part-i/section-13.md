# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (106).** Text as at 2026-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Co-operation between Health Boards and other authorities.[^c14159401][^c-fa2bffbd2313fdc5c56ee38730f3da15][^c-046ff196cf20fcbfdda9dbc11f657411][^c-046ff196cf20fcbfdda9dbc11f657411][^c14159391]

*Extent: S*

In exercising their respective functions, Health Boards, HIS (as respects its health service functions only),[^c-fbef6efe438993ee17733b6eabe55a5e]NHS[^c14159411] trusts, local authorities , integration joint boards[^c-5ac9cb368a65c9be8529d5726534d43d] and education authorities shall co-operate with one another in order to secure and advance the health of the people of Scotland.
