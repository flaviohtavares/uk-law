# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 17I

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (106).** Text as at 2026-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/29
>

## 17I. Use of accommodation.[^c14161501][^key-046ff196cf20fcbfdda9dbc11f657411]

*Extent: S*

If the Secretary of State considers that any accommodation provided by him by virtue of this Act is suitable for use in connection with the provision of primary medical services[^c20954591] or personal dental services in accordance with section 17C arrangements, he may make the accommodation available on such terms as he thinks fit to persons providing those services.
