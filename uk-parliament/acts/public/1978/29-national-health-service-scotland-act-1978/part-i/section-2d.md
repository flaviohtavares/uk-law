# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 2D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (106).** Text as at 2026-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2D. Equal opportunities[^c20957471]

*Extent: S*

(1) Health Boards, Special Health Boards , the Agency and (as respects its health service functions only) HIS[^key-c00308da3bac070ae722166ed2762a82] must discharge their functions in a manner that encourages equal opportunities and in particular the observance of the equal opportunity requirements.

(2) In this section “ **equal opportunities** ” and “ **equal opportunity requirements** ” have the same meaning as in Section L2 (equal opportunities) of Part II of Schedule 5 to the Scotland Act 1998 (c. 46).
