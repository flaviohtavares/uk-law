# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 17S

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## (not yet in force: 17S). (not yet in force: Eligibility to be contractor under pharmaceutical care services contract)[^c20973211]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: A Health Board may, subject to such conditions as may be prescribed, enter into a pharmaceutical care services contract with—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: a registered pharmacist; or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: a person other than a registered pharmacist who, by virtue of section 69 of the Medicines Act 1968 (c. 67), is taken to be a person lawfully conducting a retail pharmacy business in accordance with that section,)*

*(not yet in force: who undertakes that all pharmaceutical care services provided under the contract will be provided by, or under the supervision of, a registered pharmacist.)*

((not yet in force: (not yet in force: 1A)))[^c20973391] *(not yet in force: *(not yet in force: In such circumstances, and subject to such conditions, as may be prescribed, subsection (1) has effect with the omission of the words from “who undertakes” to the end.)*)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Regulations may make provision as to the effect on a pharmaceutical care services contract entered into with a partnership of a change in the membership of the partnership.)*
