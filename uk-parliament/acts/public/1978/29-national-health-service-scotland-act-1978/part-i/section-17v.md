# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 17V

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## (not yet in force: 17V). (not yet in force: Resolution of disputes and entry into) (not yet in force: NHS) (not yet in force: contracts: pharmaceutical care services contracts)[^c20973211]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Regulations may make provision for the resolution of disputes as to the terms of a proposed pharmaceutical care services contract, including, without prejudice to that generality, provision for—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the referral of the terms of the proposed contract to the Scottish Ministers; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: the Scottish Ministers, or a person or panel of persons appointed by them, to determine the terms on which the contract may be entered into.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Regulations may make provision for any person entering, or who has entered, into a pharmaceutical care services contract to be regarded as a health service body for any purposes of section 17A, in circumstances where the person so elects.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Where a person is to be regarded as a health service body for any purposes of section 17A by reason only of an election by virtue of subsection (2) of this section, that section has effect in relation to that person with the omission of the words “under any enactment” in subsection (1) and with such other modifications (if any) as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 4)) *(not yet in force: Regulations under subsection (2) may include provision as to the application of section 17A in cases where—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: a partnership is to be regarded as a health service body; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: there is a change in the membership of the partnership.)*
