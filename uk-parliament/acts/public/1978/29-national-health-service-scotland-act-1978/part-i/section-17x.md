# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 17X

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## (not yet in force: 17X). (not yet in force: Persons performing pharmaceutical care services)[^c20973281]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: Regulations may provide that a registered pharmacist may not perform any pharmaceutical care service which a Health Board is, under section 2CA(1), under a duty to provide or secure the provision of unless that pharmacist is included in a list maintained under the regulations by the Health Board.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Regulations under subsection (1) may make provision in relation to such lists and in particular as to—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: the preparation, maintenance and publication of a list;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: eligibility for inclusion in a list;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: applications for inclusion (including provision for the procedure for applications to be made and dealt with and documents to be supplied on application);)*
- ((not yet in force: d)) *(not yet in force: the grounds on which an application for inclusion must be granted or refused;)*
- ((not yet in force: e)) *(not yet in force: requirements with which a person included in a list must comply (including requirements as to standards of performance and patient care and as to declarations, consents or undertakings);)*
- ((not yet in force: f)) *(not yet in force: suspension or removal from a list (including the grounds for and consequences of suspension or removal);)*
- ((not yet in force: g)) *(not yet in force: circumstances in which a person included in a list may not withdraw from it;)*
- ((not yet in force: h)) *(not yet in force: payments to be made by a Health Board in respect of a person suspended from the list (including provision for the amount of, or the method of calculating, the payment to be determined by the Scottish Ministers);)*
- ((not yet in force: i)) *(not yet in force: criteria to be applied in making decisions under the regulations;)*
- ((not yet in force: j)) *(not yet in force: disclosure of information about applicants for inclusion, refusals of applications, or suspensions, removals or references to the Tribunal.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: Regulations making provision as to the matters referred to in subsection (2)(j) may in particular authorise the disclosure of information—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: by a Health Board to the Scottish Ministers; and)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: by the Scottish Ministers to a Health Board.)*
