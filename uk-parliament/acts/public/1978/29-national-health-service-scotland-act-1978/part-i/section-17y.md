# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 17Y

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## (not yet in force: 17Y). (not yet in force: Assistance and support: primary medical services and pharmaceutical care services)[^c20973301]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: A Health Board may provide assistance and support to—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: any person providing, or proposing to provide, primary medical services under a general medical services contract;)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: any person providing, or proposing to provide, such services in accordance with section 17C arrangements;)*
- ((not yet in force: c)) *(not yet in force: any person providing, or proposing to provide, pharmaceutical care services under a pharmaceutical care services contract.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Assistance and support provided by a Health Board under subsection (1) is to be provided on such terms, including terms as to payment, as the Board think fit.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: In this section, “)* ***(not yet in force: assistance)*** *(not yet in force: ” includes financial assistance.)*
