# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 12K

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (106).** Text as at 2026-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12K. Power of Health Board to carry out other Health Board's functions[^key-37963615cd9a5ebccd2e6eec752b525b][^key-046ff196cf20fcbfdda9dbc11f657411][^key-dafddae47aa5b77e42d5706ab1fca194][^key-046ff196cf20fcbfdda9dbc11f657411]

*Extent: S*

A Health Board may, with the agreement of another Health Board and the Scottish Ministers, carry out on behalf of that other Health Board any function of that other Health Board.
