# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 17R

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## (not yet in force: 17R). (not yet in force: Mandatory contract term: provision of prescribed pharmaceutical care services)[^c20973211]

*Extent: S*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: A pharmaceutical care services contract must require the contractor to provide as respects the area of the Health Board pharmaceutical care services of such descriptions as may be prescribed.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Regulations under subsection (1) may in particular describe the pharmaceutical care services by reference to the manner or circumstances in which they are provided.)*
