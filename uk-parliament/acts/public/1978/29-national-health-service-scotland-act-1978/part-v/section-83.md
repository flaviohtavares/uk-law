# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 83

## 83. Power of Health Boards and local health councils to hold property on trust.

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c14169711][^c14169721][^c-de47d5ee9956edf8c86de96335782a56] A Health Board , and HIS,[^c-ae645f4370649e55ef4cf9ed300e35b6] shall have power to accept, hold and administer any property on trust for purposes relating to any service which it is their function to make arrangements for, administer or provide, or to their functions with respect to research.

(1ZA)[^c-56ee753b9f24971fd48f24482e9589f5] Any reference in subsection (1) to a function of HIS is to be construed as a reference to that body's health service functions only.

(1A)[^c-de47d5ee9956edf8c86de96335782a56][^c20962241] A Health Board shall have power to accept, hold and administer any property on trust for purposes relating to any service which it is the function of any NHS trust in the area of the Health Board to make arrangements for, administer or provide.

(2) A local health council shall have power to accept, hold and administer any property on trust for purposes relating to any function conferred upon them by or under any enactment.
