# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 40

## 40. Vaccination and immunisation.

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-07aa9d27b25fc9808f1350ca4325ac72] The Scottish Ministers may make arrangements for the vaccination or immunisation of persons against any disease.

(2)[^c20963251] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The Scottish Ministers[^c-9ce9a9af9270aaf45feaf393297621af] may, either directly or by another person, supply free of charge to persons[^c-811a90000fdba77afa33fd926e5d9dcb] providing services under this section, vaccines, sera or other preparations for vaccinating or immunising persons against any disease.
