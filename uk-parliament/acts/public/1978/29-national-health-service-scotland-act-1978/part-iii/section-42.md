# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (106).** Text as at 2026-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/29

## 42. Health education.[^c14168391][^c14168381]

*Extent: S*

The Secretary of State shall have power to disseminate, by whatever means, information relating to the promotion and maintenance of health and the prevention of illness.
