# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 38B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (106).** Text as at 2026-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/29

## 38B. Detection of vision problems in children[^c20971561]

*Extent: S*

(1) It is the duty of the Scottish Ministers, to such extent as they consider necessary to meet all reasonable requirements, to provide for the detection of vision problems in children.

(2) In this section, “**children**” means persons under the age of 16 years.
