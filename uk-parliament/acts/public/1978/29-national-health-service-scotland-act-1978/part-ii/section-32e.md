# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 32E

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (106).** Text as at 2026-07-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1978/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32E. Payments in consequence of suspension.[^c14168001][^c20991571]

*Extent: S*

(1) Regulations may provide for the making to persons to whom section 32A(3) or 32D(3)[^c20964441] applies of payments in consequence of the application of that provision.

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) above may provide for the determination by the Secretary of State in a prescribed manner of anything for which provision may be made by regulations under that subsection.
