## 32B. Suspension pending appeal.[^c14167831][^c20991571][^c14167841]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c14167831][^c20952701] Where, on disposing of a case under section 29B, the Tribunal make a [^c20964841]. . . disqualification, they may, if they consider that either of the conditions mentioned in section 32A(2A) is satisfied, direct that section 32A(3) shall apply or, if a direction has been given under section 32A(2), shall continue to apply to him as respects services of the kind to which the disqualification relates.

(2) A direction under subsection (1) above shall cease to have effect—

- (a) where no appeal against the [^c20964841]. . . disqualification[^c14167861] is brought, at the end of the period for bringing an appeal, and
- (b) where an appeal against the disqualification is brought, when the appeal process has been exhausted.

(3) [^c20964861]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c14167901] In subsection (1) above, the reference to patients is to persons to whom services of the kind to which the direction under section 29(3)(b) relates are, or may be, provided under this Part of this Act.
