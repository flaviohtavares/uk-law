# National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978, section 28

## 28. Persons authorised to provide pharmaceutical services.

*Extent: S*

(1) Except as may be provided by or under[^c14166711] regulations, no arrangements shall be made by a Health Board with a medical practitioner or dental practitioner under which he is required or agrees to provide pharmaceutical services to any person to whom he is rendering primary medical services under Part 1[^c20955611] or general dental services.

(2) Except as may be provided by or under[^c14166721] regulations, no arrangements for the dispensing of medicines or the provision of pharmaceutical services[^c14166731] shall be made with persons other than persons who are registered pharmacists or are persons lawfully conducting a retail pharmacy business in accordance with section 69 of the [^c14166741]Medicines Act 1968, and who undertake

- (a)[^c14166751] that all medicines provided[^c14166761] by them under the arrangements made under this Part shall be dispensed , and[^c14166771]
- (b) that
  - (i)[^c14166781] all services mentioned in section 27(1)(d) provided by them under those arrangements
  - (ii)[^c14166791] all additional pharmaceutical services provided in accordance with a direction under section 27A,

  shall be provided either by, or under the direct supervision of, a registered pharmacist.

(2A)[^c20965281] Regulations shall provide for the preparation and publication by a Health Board of one or more lists of medical practitioners who undertake to supply drugs and appliances in the Board’s area.

(2B) The regulations shall include provision for the removal of an entry from a list in prescribed circumstances.

(2C)[^c20971491] Where—

- (a) arrangements have been made under this Part with a registered pharmacist; and
- (b) the registration of that pharmacist is suspended by virtue of any direction or order under the Pharmacy Order 2010,
