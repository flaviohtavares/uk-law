## 29B. Powers of NHS Tribunal.[^c14167121][^c20991571]

*Extent: S*

(1) Subsection (2) applies where the Tribunal are of the opinion—

- (a) on inquiring into an efficiency case, that the person meets the first condition for disqualification;
- (b) on inquiring into a fraud case, that the person meets the second condition for disqualification;[^c20963191]
- (c) on inquiring into an unsuitability case, that the person meets the third condition for disqualification.

(2)[^c20963211] The Tribunal shall disqualify him for inclusion in—

- (a) the list to which the case relates;
- (b) all lists within the same paragraph of subsection (8) of section 29 as that list; and
- (c) where the list to which the case relates is a list referred to in—
  - (i) paragraph (c) of that subsection, all lists within paragraph (cc) of that subsection;
  - (ii) that paragraph (cc), all lists within that paragraph (c).

(3) [^c20964571]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The Tribunal shall not make a[^c20963231] disqualification [^c20964601]. . . under this section if they are of the opinion that it would be unjust to do so.

(5) A disqualification under this section shall have effect when the case is finally concluded.

(6) If a person is disqualified for inclusion in any list prepared by a Health Board, the Board must not enter him in the list and (if he is already included in the list) must remove him from the list.
