# Judicial Committee Amendment Act 1895

> Text as at 2004-07-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Judicial Committee Amendment Act 1895

1895 c.44

An Act to amend the Law relating to the Judicial Committee of Her Majesty’s Privy Council.

\[6th July 1895\]

## 1. Provision as to persons being or having been Colonial Chief Justices or Judges.[^c576499]

(1) If any person being or having been Chief Justice or a Judge of the Supreme Court [^c576500]. . ., of any of the Australasian colonies mentioned in the schedule to this Act, [^c576501]. . . or of any other Superior Court in Her Majesty’s Dominions named in that behalf by Her Majesty in Council, is a member of Her Majesty’s Privy Council, he shall be a member of the Judicial Committee of the Privy Council.

(2)[^c576502] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The provisions of this Act shall be in addition to, and shall not affect, any other enactment for the appointment of or relating to members of the Judicial Committee.

## 2. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Judicial Committee Amendment Act 1895.
