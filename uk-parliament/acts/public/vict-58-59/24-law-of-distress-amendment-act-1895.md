# Law of Distress Amendment Act 1895

> Text as at 2014-04-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Law of Distress Amendment Act 1895

1895 c.24

An Act to amend the Law of Distress Amendment Act 1888.

\[6th July 1895\]

## 1. Power to cancel bailiffs’ certificates.

A certificate granted to a bailiff by a judge of the county court[^c-60b5fe145eef67b9231031e106cf6668] under the [^c576490]Law of Distress Amendment Act 1888, may at any time be cancelled or declared void by a judge of the county court[^c-32a8846890886266f5bdcb38b735ee34] , . . . [^c576491]

## 2. Penalty for acting without certificate.

If any person not holding a certificate for the time being in force under the Law of Distress Amendment Act 1888, levies a distress contrary to the provisions of that Act, he shall without prejudice to any civil liability be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale[^c576492].

## 3. Duration of certificates.

The power to make rules under the [^c576493]Law of Distress Amendment Act 1888 shall extend to making provision for fixing the duration of certificates granted, or to be hereafter granted, to bailiffs.

## 4. Unlawful distress.

A court of summary jurisdiction, on complaint that goods or chattels exempt under section four of the [^c576494]Law of Distress Amendment Act 1888 from distress for rent, have been taken under such distress, may, by summary order, direct that the goods and chattels so taken, if not sold, be restored; or, if they have been sold, that such sum as the court may determine to be the value thereof shall be paid to the complainant by the person who levied the distress or directed it to be levied.

## 5. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c576495]

## 6. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Law of Distress Amendment Act 1895.
