Metropolitan Police (Receiver) Act 1895

1895 c.12

An Act to make provision for the temporary absence of the Receiver for the Metropolitan Police District.

\[14th May 1895\] [^c576392]

## 1. Power to provide for temporary absence of receiver.

If and whilst the receiver for the metropolitan police district is temporarily unable to act whether by reason of absence or otherwise[^c576393] a person appointed by warrant under the hand of the Secretary of State may temporarily act as and shall be deemed for all purposes to be such receiver.

## 2. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Metropolitan Police (Receiver) Act 1895, and shall be read with the Metropolitan Police Acts 1829 to 1890.
