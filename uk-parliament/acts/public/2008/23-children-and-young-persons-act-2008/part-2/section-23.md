# Children and Young Persons Act 2008, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 23. Extension of entitlements to personal adviser and to assistance in connection with education or training

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-e8a8014c72a6391c551b18f4ec9623a9][^key-bebf6845fd2623775e99f30e977a57e1] In section 23D(1) of the 1989 Act (power to make provision for personal advisers for certain young persons)—

- (a) for “twenty-one” substitute “ twenty-five ”; and
- (b) after paragraph (c) insert

  > ; or
  >
  > - “(d) persons to whom section 23CA applies.

(2)[^key-2da47940b60838435921821de8b80094][^key-e9902d4762d9debb4b5c9fed4add07c4] In section 24B of that Act (assistance in connection with employment, education and training), in subsection (3)(a) for “twenty-four” substitute “ twenty-five ”.
