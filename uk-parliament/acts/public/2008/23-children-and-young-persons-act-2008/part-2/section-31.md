# Children and Young Persons Act 2008, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 31. Supply of information concerning the death of children to Local Safeguarding Children Boards in England and Safeguarding Children Boards in Wales[^c-b8e52c01555b19377bfdc8f885d6cfc2][^c-480527a5410e15bef295eccb3ea7fa46]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subsection (2) applies if, under the 1953 Act, a registrar of births and deaths registers the death of a person and the registrar believes that the deceased was or may have been under the age of 18 at the time of death.

(2) The registrar must, before the end of the required period, secure that the appropriate authority[^c-4efa80e178c0ba16f06ec57de3aab8f2] is notified of the particulars of the death entered in the register.

(3) Subsection (4) applies if, under the 1953 Act, an entry in a register kept for a sub-district concerning a death is corrected and the person making the correction believes that the entry relates to a person who was or may have been under the age of 18 at the time of death.

(4) The person who makes the correction must, before the end of the required period, secure that the appropriate authority[^c-06f0e521f0c6f9230a5f39722ce64c0d] is notified of the particulars of the death as corrected.

(5) Subsections (5A) and (6) apply[^c-509797b6fecce14250da8edbf3ab4d5c] if, under the 1953 Act, a registrar of births and deaths issues a certificate to the effect that a death is not required by law to be registered in England or Wales but the registrar believes that the deceased was or may have been under the age of 18 at the time of death.

(5A)[^c-7ce6dc69f53d9cd7fa0bba534cbac695] Where the registrar's sub-district is in England, the registrar must, before the end of the required period, secure that the appropriate authority is notified—

- (a) of the issuing of the certificate; and
- (b) of the registrar's belief and the grounds for it.

(6) Where the registrar's sub-district is in Wales,[^c-7dc4030a9f04b023efd10e799f873c7e] the registrar must, before the end of the required period, secure that the [^c-edb46308b6609f636ec84e5ffdf7d39c]... Safeguarding Children Board in Wales[^c-7187b9199dc881a5a67b4e06d79e5729] established by the local authority[^c-2a4c070c9425eee63a31d05064729821] within whose area the registrar's sub-district is situated is notified—

- (a) of the issuing of the certificate; and
- (b) of the registrar's belief and the grounds for it.

(7) The required period is the period of seven days beginning with the day after—

- (a) for the purposes of subsection (2), the day on which the death was registered;
- (b) for the purposes of subsection (4), the day on which the correction was made; and
- (c) for the purposes of subsections (5A) and[^c-ac53bc045dd318996ab9e984fe5a3920] (6), the day on which the certificate was issued.

(8) The requirements of this section do not apply if the death occurred before 1 April 2008.

(8A)[^c-0ea0432d9a11a1f5a5cbf43ad4c1bf60] The child death review partners for each local authority area in England must—

- (a) make arrangements for the receipt by them of notifications under this section; and
- (b) publish those arrangements.

(9) [^c-abf6e85227af1274a4ed55c42b8b9a8b]... each Safeguarding Children Board in Wales[^c-e6812f4b48269c2764adfa619fda5cfd] must—

- (a) make arrangements for the receipt by it of notifications under this section; and
- (b) publish those arrangements.

(10) In this section—

- “**the 1953 Act**” means the Births and Deaths Registration Act 1953 (c. 20);
- “the appropriate[^c-af1c9f593d96d1c61becba6c9ecc8c17] authority[^c-07a044ba23a7f109f13203f0379b09fa] ” means—
  - (a) in relation to a register kept for a sub-district in England, the child death review partners for the local authority area within which[^c-9477334b26549e6215e49ea8528b12cc] is situated the sub-district for which the register is kept; or
  - (b) in relation to a register kept for a sub-district in Wales,[^c-1c7c101cb2330fceb4f2e50d5e178dd6] the Safeguarding Children Board in Wales for the Safeguarding Board area within which is situated the sub-district for which the register is kept,

  and “Safeguarding Board area” has the meaning given by section 142 of the 2014 Act.;
- “[^c-09407af410f77b29506df2600e011fef]**child death review partners**” has the meaning given by section 16Q(2) of the Children Act 2004;
- “local authority” has[^c-16ed7f2938463ccadc1465edb313e1da] the same meaning as in the Children Act 2004 (c. 31).

- [^c-3164158314139282e4f44711182b34e0]...
- “Safeguarding Children Board in Wales” means a Board established under section 134(4) of the 2014 Act.[^c-00ef44ebb4bbb6aac34803beb4833aa5]
