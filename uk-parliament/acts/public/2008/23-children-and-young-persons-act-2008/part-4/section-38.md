# Children and Young Persons Act 2008, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 38. Entitlement of relative to apply for a special guardianship order[^c-868515ef1ebd30181715c1aff0e6f188][^c-b6895fa6b9ffa27a192423e390c62c8a]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 14A(5) of the 1989 Act (persons entitled to apply for special guardianship order), after paragraph (d) insert

> ;
>
> - (e) a relative with whom the child has lived for a period of at least one year immediately preceding the application.
