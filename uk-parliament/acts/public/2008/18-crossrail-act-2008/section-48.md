# Crossrail Act 2008, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-04-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Application of Act to extensions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1)[^key-4646012fd276c352ad338a325e87d399] Development consent under the Planning Act 2008 is not required for—

- (a) an extension of Crossrail, or
- (b) the provision, otherwise than as part of an extension of Crossrail, of a railway facility for use for the purposes of or in connection with Crossrail.

(1) Subsection (2) applies to an order under section 1 of the Transport and Works Act 1992 (c. 42) (orders as to railways etc.) which relates to a matter mentioned in subsection (A1)(a) or (b).[^key-2fdbd76c353f5e39ef379db36581dfce]

(2) An order to which this subsection applies may apply any provision of this Act, with any modifications, in relation to anything authorised by the order, so far as relating to a matter mentioned in subsection (A1)[^key-1dfd1b9f497c5d406e59a4ed5646e0f1](a) or (b).

(3) An order under section 1 of the Transport and Works Act 1992 which relates to an extension of Crossrail may also provide for any provision of this Act to have effect as if Crossrail included the extension.

(4) The following provisions are excepted from the power conferred by subsection (2)—

- section 6(7) and (8),
- paragraphs 18 and 19 of Schedule 6,
- Schedule 9, and
- Schedule 10.

(5) In subsection (A1)[^key-c94b741bc0f84776512777907125836b](b), “**railway facility**” has the same meaning as in Part 1 of the Railways Act 1993 (c. 43).
