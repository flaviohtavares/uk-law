# Climate Change Act 2008, section 93

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/27

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. Measurement of emissions etc by reference to carbon dioxide equivalent

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Act greenhouse gas emissions, reductions of such emissions and removals of greenhouse gas from the atmosphere shall be measured or calculated in tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent.

(2) A “**tonne of carbon dioxide equivalent**” means one metric tonne of carbon dioxide or an amount of any other greenhouse gas with an equivalent global warming potential (calculated consistently with international carbon reporting practice).
