# Climate Change Act 2008, section 61

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Guidance by Secretary of State to reporting authorities

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may issue guidance to reporting authorities about—

- (a) assessing the current and predicted impact of climate change in relation to the authorities' functions,
- (b) preparing proposals and policies for adapting to climate change in the exercise of their functions, and
- (c) co-operating with other reporting authorities for that purpose.

(2) This section does not apply to devolved functions.
