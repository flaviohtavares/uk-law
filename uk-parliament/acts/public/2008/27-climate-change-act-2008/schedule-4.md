## SCHEDULE 4 — Trading schemes: powers to require information

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 50

### Introductory

**1.** (1)[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715] The powers conferred by this Schedule are exercisable by the following authorities— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

- (a) the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (b) the Scottish Ministers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (c) the relevant Northern Ireland department; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (d) the Welsh Ministers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (e) the Environment Agency; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (f) the Scottish Environment Protection Agency. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

(2) References in this Schedule to an “environmental authority” are to any of those authorities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

### Information from electricity suppliers and distributors

**2.** (1) An environmental authority may, for the purposes of enabling a trading scheme to be established, by notice require an electricity supplier or electricity distributor to provide any of the following information— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

- (a) information about the electricity meters and metering systems for which the supplier or distributor is responsible, including (in particular) their locations and any identifying features; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (b) information about the persons to whom electricity measured by those meters or systems is supplied or who purchase such electricity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (c) information about the consumption by those persons of that electricity; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (d) any other information that the environmental authority considers necessary for identifying the potential participants in the scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

(2) An “electricity supplier”— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

- (a) in relation to England and Wales and Scotland means an authorised supplier within the meaning of the Electricity Act 1989 (c. 29) (see section 64(1) of that Act); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (b) in relation to Northern Ireland means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
  - (i) an electricity supplier within the meaning of the Electricity (Northern Ireland) Order 1992 (S.I. 1992/231) (N.I. 1) (see Article 3 of that Order), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715] a person who may supply electricity to premises without a licence by virtue of an exemption under Article 9 of that Order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

(3) An “electricity distributor”— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

- (a) in relation to England and Wales and Scotland means an authorised distributor within the meaning of the Electricity Act 1989 (see section 64(1) of that Act); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (b) in relation to Northern Ireland means an electricity distributor within the meaning of the Electricity (Northern Ireland) Order 1992 (see Article 3 of that Order). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

(4) References in this Schedule to an electricity supplier or electricity distributor include an agent of such a supplier or distributor. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

### Information from potential participants in a trading scheme

**3.** (1) An environmental authority may, for the purposes of enabling a trading scheme to be established, by notice require a potential participant in the scheme to provide any of the following information— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

- (a) information about whether the criteria specified in the notice are met by the potential participant, either alone or together with any other person or persons; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (b) information identifying any potential co-participant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (c) contact details for the potential participant and any potential co-participant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (d) information about the meters that measure electricity supplied to or purchased by the potential participant or any potential co-participant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (e) information about the consumption of electricity by the potential participant and any potential co-participant; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (f) information about any climate change agreement (within the meaning of Schedule 6 to the Finance Act 2000 (c. 17)) entered into by or on behalf of the potential participant or any potential co-participant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

(2) A “potential participant”, in relation to a trading scheme, means a person who the environmental authority considers— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

- (a) will or may be a participant in the scheme, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (b) will or may fall to be treated together with any other person or persons (a “potential co-participant”) as such a participant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

### Requirements for a valid notice

**4.** (1) A notice under this Schedule must comply with the following requirements. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

(2) The notice must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

- (a) be in writing, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (b) specify the information to be provided, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (c) specify the name and address of the person to whom the information is to be provided, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (d) specify the date by which the information is to be provided, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (e) explain the consequences of failure to comply with the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

(3) An environmental authority must not give a notice requiring information from a person unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

- (a) the authority has previously sent the person a request in writing for the information, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (b) the person has failed to provide the information within the period of 28 days beginning with the day on which the request was sent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

### Failure to comply with notice etc an offence

**5.** (1) A person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

- (a) fails without reasonable excuse to comply with a notice under this Schedule, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (b) provides information in response to such a notice that the person knows or suspects to be false or misleading, commits an offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]
- (2) A person guilty of such an offence is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a64abdad5bb52dc1961e6938537a0715]

### Disclosure of information

**6.** (1) This paragraph applies to information obtained by an environmental authority (whether or not pursuant to a notice under this Schedule) from—

- (a) an electricity supplier or electricity distributor, or
- (b) a potential participant,

for the purposes of enabling a trading scheme to be established.

(2) The information may be disclosed for the purposes of or in connection with the establishment, operation or enforcement of a trading scheme—

- (a) by an environmental authority to another environmental authority or the administrator of the scheme, or
- (b) by the administrator of the scheme to any other administrator of the scheme or an environmental authority.

(3) This does not affect any other right to disclose information within sub-paragraph (1) apart from this paragraph.
