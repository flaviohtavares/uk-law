# Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008, section 150

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-11-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 150. Financial provisions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There is to be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenditure incurred by virtue of this Act by a Minister of the Crown; and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable under any other Act out of money so provided.
