## 67. Penalties etc. for possession of extreme pornographic images[^key-e202e514fd761c46a8a41df67f3e115e]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) This section has effect where a person is guilty of an offence under section 63.

(2) If the offence relates to an image that portrays any relevant act (with or without other acts)[^key-a217878b4c5762d2fc15e632a47b1b63] , the offender is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the relevant period or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 years or a fine or both.

(3) If the offence relates to an image that does not portray any relevant act[^key-673531db6fecd7dce040a0649e752ca2], the offender is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the relevant period or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or a fine or both.

(4) In subsection (2)(a) or (3)(a) “**the relevant period**” means—

- (a) in relation to England and Wales, the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-73d80f4f4b468984ff2f957acd74f1ae];
- (b) in relation to Northern Ireland, 6 months.

(5)[^key-84a8794e8370ed1483368d8662f03098] In this section “**relevant act**” means—

- (a) in relation to England and Wales, an act within section 63(7)(a) or (b) or (7A)(a) or (b);
- (b) in relation to Northern Ireland, an act within section 63(7)(a) or (b) or (7A)(a) or (b) *(Extent: N.I.)*[^key-4bc3ad5c7b1309e157332f9aeaaee060].
