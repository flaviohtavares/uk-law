## 68. Special rules relating to providers of information society services[^c-c7d6614abf0e60928bc9d71cdbd1444e]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Schedule 14 makes special provision in connection with the operation of sections 63, 67A[^c-60092e25418e45dbd4c85c4b71feeb46] , 67E[^c-09378bb9e91d5ab77085da2257984e6e] and 67H[^c-d791fc3d3f948cd87b7fac96da8101a4] in relation to persons providing information society services within the meaning of that Schedule.
