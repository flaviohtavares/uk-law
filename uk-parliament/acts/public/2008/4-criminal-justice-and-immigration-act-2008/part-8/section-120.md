# Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008, section 120

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120. Power to remove person causing nuisance or disturbance

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-25f3a51c5a3fd4fce82ded0935bf0e29][^key-d500946fa5dfa3cf524a2dd9301185f5] If a constable reasonably suspects that a person is committing or has committed an offence under section 119, the constable may remove the person from the NHS premises concerned.

(2)[^key-25f3a51c5a3fd4fce82ded0935bf0e29][^key-d500946fa5dfa3cf524a2dd9301185f5] If an authorised officer reasonably suspects that a person is committing or has committed an offence under section 119, the authorised officer may—

- (a) remove the person from the NHS premises concerned, or
- (b) authorise an appropriate NHS staff member to do so.

(3)[^key-25f3a51c5a3fd4fce82ded0935bf0e29][^key-d500946fa5dfa3cf524a2dd9301185f5] Any person removing another person from NHS premises under this section may use reasonable force (if necessary).

(4)[^key-25f3a51c5a3fd4fce82ded0935bf0e29][^key-d500946fa5dfa3cf524a2dd9301185f5] An authorised officer cannot remove a person under this section or authorise another person to do so if the authorised officer has reason to believe that—

- (a) the person to be removed requires medical advice, treatment or care for himself or herself, or
- (b) the removal of the person would endanger the person's physical or mental health.

(5)[^key-56072634885de94a1fe16926bbac7ae3][^key-b5f1895663edf306c69e38561d42784b] In this section—

- “appropriate NHS staff member”—
  - (a) in relation to English NHS premises, means an English NHS staff member, and
  - (b) in relation to Welsh NHS premises, means a Welsh NHS staff member,
- “authorised officer”—
  - (a) in relation to English NHS premises, means any English NHS staff member authorised by a relevant English NHS body to exercise the powers which are conferred by this section on an authorised officer in respect of English NHS premises, and
  - (b) in relation to Welsh NHS premises, means any Welsh NHS staff member authorised by a relevant Welsh NHS body to exercise the powers which are conferred by this section on an authorised officer in respect of Welsh NHS premises,
- “**English NHS staff member**” means a person employed by a relevant English NHS body or otherwise working for it (whether as or on behalf of a contractor, as a volunteer or otherwise),
- “**Welsh NHS staff member**” means a person employed by a relevant Welsh NHS body or otherwise working for it (whether as or on behalf of a contractor, as a volunteer or otherwise).

(6)[^key-56072634885de94a1fe16926bbac7ae3][^key-b5f1895663edf306c69e38561d42784b] Terms defined in section 119 have the same meaning in this section as in that section.
