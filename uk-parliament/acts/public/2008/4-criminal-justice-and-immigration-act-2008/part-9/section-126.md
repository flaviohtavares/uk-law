## 126. Police misconduct and performance procedures

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-acd65f5056553c757490d9e70478ce4c][^c-2d898713d0c9d788ca25a561602cd8ad][^c-68e3dcecdf7b24af891f385c8dd957c4] Part 1 of Schedule 22—

- (a) amends the Police Act 1996 (c. 16) to make provision for or in connection with disciplinary and other proceedings in respect of the conduct and performance of members of police forces and special constables, and
- (b) makes other minor amendments to that Act.

(2)[^c-09402bf5704da2d6c3823ca19bdbd2a2] Part 2 of that Schedule makes equivalent amendments to the Ministry of Defence Police Act 1987 (c. 4) for the purposes of the Ministry of Defence Police.

(3)[^c-68e3dcecdf7b24af891f385c8dd957c4] Part 3 of that Schedule makes equivalent amendments to the Railways and Transport Safety Act 2003 (c. 20) for the purposes of the British Transport Police.
