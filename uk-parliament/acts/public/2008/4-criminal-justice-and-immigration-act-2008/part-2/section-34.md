## 34. Removal under Criminal Justice Act 2003

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-dd97e28a4d1a6e0e07db199328072043] In Part 12 of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (c. 44) (sentencing) Chapter 6 (release on licence) is amended as follows.

(2)[^c-777c4ed04e9ddfdc22ad7313e9e2f4c6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-dd97e28a4d1a6e0e07db199328072043] Section 260 (early removal of prisoners liable to removal from United Kingdom) is amended as follows.

(4) In subsection (1) (the power of removal)—

- (a)[^c-dd97e28a4d1a6e0e07db199328072043] for “subsections (2) and (3)” substitute “ subsection (2) ”, and
- (b)[^c-621fb0e9513988c1ce65a46407563f27] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c-dd97e28a4d1a6e0e07db199328072043] For subsection (2) (conditions relating to time) substitute—

> (2) Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to a prisoner unless he has served at least one-half of the requisite custodial period.

(6)[^c-dd97e28a4d1a6e0e07db199328072043] Subsections (3) and (3A) (cases where subsection (1) does not apply) cease to have effect.

(7)[^c-000986511e4cb201ae2245a3c23b41d2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8)[^c-dd97e28a4d1a6e0e07db199328072043] In subsection (6) (order-making powers)—

- (a) in paragraph (a) omit “or (3)(e)”,
- (b) omit paragraph (b), and
- (c) in paragraph (c) for “subsection (2)(b)(ii)” substitute “ subsection (2) ”.

(9)[^c-dd97e28a4d1a6e0e07db199328072043] For subsection (7) (meaning of “requisite custodial period”) substitute—

> (7) In this section “**requisite custodial period**”—
>
> - (a) in relation to a prisoner serving an extended sentence imposed under section 227 or 228, means one-half of the appropriate custodial term (determined by the court under that section);
> - (b) in any other case, has the meaning given by paragraph (a), (b) or (d) of section 244(3).
