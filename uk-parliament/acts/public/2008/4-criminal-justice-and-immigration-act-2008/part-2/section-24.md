## 24. Minimum conditions for early release under section 246(1) of Criminal Justice Act 2003[^c-20229f0516f0f67138c54fa2dc4ad724]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 246(2) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (c. 44) (minimum conditions for early release of fixed-term prisoner other than intermittent custody prisoner) for paragraph (b) substitute

> and
>
> - (b) he has served—
>   - (i) at least 4 weeks of that period, and
>   - (ii) at least one-half of that period.
