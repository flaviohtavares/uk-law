> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2023-09-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/6
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Minor and consequential amendments[^c2175985]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-e145be3cb8fde5c4488c4af8a060952b][^c-5fb5f5bda9c02414965b040432e9c470] Schedule 7 (which makes minor and consequential amendments) has effect.

(2) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision consequential on this Act amending, repealing or revoking any provision of—

- (a) an Act passed on or before the last day of the Session in which this Act is passed, or
- (b) an instrument made under an Act before the passing of this Act.
