# Child Maintenance and Other Payments Act 2008, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2023-09-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/6
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 18. Determination of applications for a variation[^c2172041][^c-9f22b7f8f04cfef710d344dcc77b6f43][^c-d11585090e42607044f219858f27190a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Section 28D of the Child Support Act 1991 is amended as follows.

(2) After subsection (2) insert—

> (2A) Subsection (2B) applies if—
>
> - (a) the application for a variation is made by the person with care or (in the case of an application for a maintenance calculation under section 7) the person with care or the child concerned, and
> - (b) it appears to the Secretary of State[^c2172044] that consideration of further information or evidence may affect the[^c2172046] decision under subsection (1)(a) whether or not to agree to a variation.
>
> (2B) Before making the decision under subsection (1)(a) the Secretary of State[^c2172048] must—
>
> - (a) consider any such further information or evidence that is available to the Secretary of State[^c2172051], and
> - (b) where necessary, take such steps as the Secretary of State considers appropriate to obtain any such further information or evidence.

(3) In subsection (3), after “duties” insert “ , apart from the duty under subsection (2B) ”.
