# Planning Act 2008, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (75).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. When development consent is required[^key-99d821655beb5f5cb3e475d4779908b6]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-9bbb6f0dfe5270e54f8d11127049eabc] Consent under this Act (“development consent”) is required for development to the extent that the development is or forms part of a nationally significant infrastructure project.

(2)[^key-bb8ea49d6db95578b1042b790f65b734] But see section 35B (power for the Secretary of State to give a direction disapplying the requirement for development consent).
