# Planning Act 2008, section 123

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (75).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123. Land to which authorisation of compulsory acquisition can relate[^c19534301][^key-eb1255ea7a6147c6f7685d7b422b1c5e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An order granting development consent may include provision authorising the compulsory acquisition of land only if the Secretary of State[^key-d0fae85a2017475f3ff59560ac50c165] is satisfied that one of the conditions in subsections (2) to (4) is met.

(2) The condition is that the application for the order included a request for compulsory acquisition of the land to be authorised.

(3) The condition is that all persons with an interest in the land consent to the inclusion of the provision.

(4) The condition is that the prescribed procedure has been followed in relation to the land.
