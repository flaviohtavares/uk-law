## 158. Nuisance: statutory authority[^key-983336d119c993a54fbddd0a2995944c][^key-220542a1bf2dc0c7e3eb920c57759702]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-9371b74c1f1bbd6be0f30d14d6c374fa][^key-e5d5a28c89583083e62bddf4649deb23] This subsection confers statutory authority for—

- (a) carrying out development for which consent is granted by an order granting development consent;
- (b) doing anything else authorised by an order granting development consent.

(2)[^key-e15effe80e2262471541397ae36b946f][^key-a54c0c643559d732397a526cef5018b2][^key-64fb034b7407333fc2142ae4d5304112][^key-32f82fba281f29559ca7fe51b28d2648][^key-e5d5a28c89583083e62bddf4649deb23][^key-cfa190d8427f1b257396f20a76381611][^key-9e88af65b9615122ff297594de1b6053][^key-8786eaccef59b7efc12d3852e4ba20da] Statutory authority under subsection (1) is conferred only for the purpose of providing a defence in civil or criminal proceedings for nuisance.

(3) Subsections (1) and (2) are subject to any contrary provision made in any particular case by an order granting development consent.
