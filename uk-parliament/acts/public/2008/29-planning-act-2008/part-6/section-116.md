## 116. Reasons for decision to grant or refuse development consent[^key-fc038fe09db06f3227029e66755c4777]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State[^key-4c3bf96db1dcb78847e03323eb3122ce] must prepare a statement of the Secretary of State's[^key-106dc4f309d38d386572e3521c5bedc4] reasons for deciding to—

- (a) make an order granting development consent, or
- (b) refuse development consent.

(2) The Secretary of State[^key-e377d030fb703c21b00a0ff5fab26be7] must provide a copy of the statement to each person who is an interested party in relation to the application for the purposes of Chapter 4 (see section 102).

(3) The Secretary of State[^key-ce63c8c529f0d150540ae6b3bb7884af] must publish the statement in such manner as the Secretary of State[^key-f6460fb0c26366c5f03e4dd46aa2aa26] thinks appropriate.
