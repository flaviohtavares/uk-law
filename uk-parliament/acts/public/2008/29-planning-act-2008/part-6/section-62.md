# Planning Act 2008, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (75).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 62. Switching from single appointed person to Panel[^c-ad45762f069983f8a65adf896d2d1b65][^c-195eab878e66b89851fcd8052a70ec7e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subsection (2) applies where an application for an order granting development consent is being handled by a single appointed person[^c-b969507546a66ca082937fc45f9a5979] under Chapter 3.

(2)[^c-3c6c411014ac8a49694dfcced22529c9] The Secretary of State may decide that the application should instead be handled by a Panel under Chapter 2.

(3) The Secretary of State must publish the criteria that are to be applied in making decisions under subsection (2).
