# Planning Act 2008, section 82

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (75).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Appointment of replacement single appointed person[^c-16aae0fccf92952c93e55879144e0d31][^c-bec5b659225736dba374f0ffc15b956a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where a person ceases to be the single appointed person, a new appointment of a person to handle the application must be made under section 79.

(2) Where that happens, the new single appointed person may, so far as may be appropriate, decide to treat things done by or in relation to any previous single appointed person as done by or in relation to the new single appointed person.

(3) Where the single appointed person makes a decision under subsection (2), the single appointed person is under a duty to acquire the necessary knowledge of the previous proceedings on the application.
