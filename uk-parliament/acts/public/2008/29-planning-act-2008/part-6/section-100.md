# Planning Act 2008, section 100

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (75).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 100. Assessors[^key-fb1c704326c399a10bcb267b2a5cb485]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State[^key-f9c2286747debe100d773147fc6421a0] may, at the request of the Examining authority, appoint a person to act as an assessor to assist the Examining authority in the Examining authority's examination of the application.

(2) A person may be appointed as an assessor only if it appears to the Secretary of State[^key-1217c7db83014c5715ea390e140644fc] that the person has expertise that makes the person a suitable person to provide assistance to the Examining authority.
