# Planning Act 2008, section 114

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (75).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/29

## 114. Grant or refusal of development consent[^c19535491][^c-f5bd7ec9ae0e3dc3d02f8940be17cd72]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) When the Secretary of State[^c-310ca4087721081969ea35585abf80a4] has decided an application for an order granting development consent, the Secretary of State[^c-a025d12c344652d60d858c822dce841b] must either—

- (a) make an order granting development consent, or
- (b) refuse development consent.

(2) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision regulating the procedure to be followed if the Secretary of State[^c-9d54d0a2b54ca800f0fb1ee5b8b37d71] proposes to make an order granting development consent on terms which are materially different from those proposed in the application.
