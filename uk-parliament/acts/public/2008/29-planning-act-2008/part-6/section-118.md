# Planning Act 2008, section 118

## 118. Legal challenges relating to applications for orders granting development consent[^key-dc5f50dfb301e345216d6a54d4fd6539][^key-5756db59c25cde027d689c9cff57f90f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A court may entertain proceedings for questioning an order granting development consent only if—

- (a) the proceedings are brought by a claim for judicial review, and
- (b) the claim form is filed before the end of[^key-1dc48148735ad9aabea3d4a2d4b92782] the period of 6 weeks beginning with the day after[^key-99de61f6416f5f4b0c176beaa980271f] —
  - (i) the day on which the order is published, or
  - (ii) if later, the day on which the statement of reasons for making the order is published.

(2) A court may entertain proceedings for questioning a refusal of development consent only if—

- (a) the proceedings are brought by a claim for judicial review, and
- (b) the claim form is filed before the end of[^key-6677d04636e433e0839dd470306d8d71] the period of 6 weeks beginning with the day after[^key-5ab578e53e23e310a67b2a2534ff5952] the day on which the statement of reasons for the refusal is published.

(3) A court may entertain proceedings for questioning a decision of the Secretary of State[^key-88ac6003759fbf8d3c0d1094e71fa453] under section 55 not to accept an application for an order granting development consent only if—

- (a) the proceedings are brought by a claim for judicial review, and
- (b) the claim form is filed before the end of[^key-41271f56221f3ae1b6adc06319753a04] the period of 6 weeks beginning with the day after[^key-a0998e3c004c35e056a464cfa2961a1f] the day on which the Secretary of State provides the copy of the statement of reasons for the decision to the applicant as required by subsection (7)(b)[^key-be739248128337271f1257bcc5dd3990] of that section.

(4) A court may entertain proceedings for questioning a decision under paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 in relation to an error or omission in a decision document only if—

- (a) the proceedings are brought by a claim for judicial review, and
- (b) the claim form is filed before the end of[^key-7d049c163164bdf3d7433c9df189e203] the period of 6 weeks beginning with the day after the day[^key-2b54efb77b22c770d75654eed3ad2bc1] on which a correction notice in respect of the error or omission is issued under paragraph 2 of that Schedule or, if the correction is required to be made by order contained in a statutory instrument, the day after the day on which the order is published.

(5) A court may entertain proceedings for questioning a decision under paragraph 2(1) of Schedule 6 to make a change to an order granting development consent only if—

- (a) the proceedings are brought by a claim for judicial review, and
- (b) the claim form is filed before the end of[^key-75a72851ee909b8f8de11ff5b9426192] the period of 6 weeks beginning with the day after the day[^key-a5fcc62c5e6218be32852899ce7eae45] on which notice of the change is given under paragraph 2(12)(b) of that Schedule or, if the change to the order is required to be made by order contained in a statutory instrument, the day after the day on which the order making the change is published.

(6) A court may entertain proceedings for questioning a decision under paragraph 3(1) of Schedule 6 to make a change to, or revoke, an order granting development consent only if—

- (a) the proceedings are brought by a claim for judicial review, and
- (b) the claim form is filed before the end of[^key-34f8387b8a035977778b390c5a2d955d] the period of 6 weeks beginning with the day after the day[^key-c74c8d18850382bad5748e2b941c2c77] on which notice of the change or revocation is given under paragraph 4(6) of that Schedule or, if the change or revocation is required to be made by order contained in a statutory instrument, the day after the day on which the order making the change or revocation is published.

(7) A court may entertain proceedings for questioning anything else done, or omitted to be done, by the Secretary of State [^key-d72256cdacdb083ddbf18747fb9b80a9]... in relation to an application for an order granting development consent only if—

- (a) the proceedings are brought by a claim for judicial review, and
- (b) the claim form is filed before the end of[^key-d495b16f39960a66b8bd90a12460fd78] the period of 6 weeks beginning with the day after[^key-c41436fbe442ad544557d3499ae7a538] the relevant day.

(8) “**The relevant day**”, in relation to an application for an order granting development consent, means the day on which—

- (a) the application is withdrawn,
- (b) the order granting development consent is published or (if later) the statement of reasons for making the order is published, or
- (c) the statement of reasons for the refusal of development consent is published.

(9) Subsections (7) and (8) do not apply in relation to—

- (a) a failure to decide an application for an order granting development consent, or
- (b) anything which delays (or is likely to delay) the decision on such an application.
