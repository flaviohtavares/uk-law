# Planning Act 2008, section 75

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (75).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 75. Decision-making by the Panel[^c-371042771a15002255d5fa2ddd67802b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(A1)[^c-c2ecc306bb9b46c999fa76fb6bdc2eba] If the members of a Panel with two members disagree as to a proposed decision by the Panel, the view of the lead member is to prevail.

(1) The making of a decision by a Panel with three or more members[^c-1af8d5db9edc6902dd6acc53f1daf2eb] requires the agreement of a majority of its members.

(2) The lead member has a second (or casting) vote in the event that the number of members of the Panel agreeing to a proposed decision is the same as the number of members not so agreeing.
