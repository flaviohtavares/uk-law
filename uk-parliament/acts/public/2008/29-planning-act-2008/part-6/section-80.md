## 80. Ceasing to be the single appointed person[^key-62f441ba305713d0fb65e0fd31cbd2cc][^key-fe7e25229890e6fba2c2f1e00eacdd80]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-5250d4ef1329e9361e165fcb9801b954] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) A person may resign from being the single appointed person[^key-00cd2193a16c36d9c1abf08612d4abff] by giving notice in writing to the Secretary of State[^key-4c030e7b9768b9813944bc18ea645010].

(3) The Secretary of State[^key-e9605bf93591d53c4a63297ed156829c] may remove a person (“**the appointee**”) from being the single appointed person[^key-79e12501a610551ccfe0a435d80fef3d] if the Secretary of State[^key-2e18dd7e413b9488c3394aed4e7b9ffb] is satisfied that the appointee is unable, unwilling or unfit to perform the duties of the single appointed person.
