# Planning Act 2008, section 107

## 107. Timetable for decisions[^c-962eeeaa884bf685ccbb3ad32641b830]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State[^c-dd42db32bca514d240608c70858a6815] is under a duty to decide an application for an order granting development consent by the end of the period of 3 months beginning with—[^c-4977a0f7b021288d73ce3dceda967a6d]

- (a) the deadline under section 98(3)[^c-3d9f6232c9b98668d8ec68c22b274c55], or[^c-483b091645d880756bc3019ffeb68960]
- (b) (if earlier) the end of the day on which the Secretary of State receives a report on the application under section 74(2)(b) or 83(1)(b).

(2)[^c-5edef0596a53ae4a0cac3c4dd62fec06] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The Secretary of State[^c-7ca5a6791fb80a5804c1dea6145f9a09] may set a date for the deadline under subsection (1) that is later than the date for the time being set.

(4)[^c-586c895dcd76c98f8628f2e64e742ca8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) The power under subsection (3) may be exercised—

- (a) more than once in relation to the same deadline;
- (b) after the date for the time being set for the deadline.

(6)[^c-29eed6c827cdca2e634c4c4bd73b1c30] Subsection (7) applies where the power under subsection (3) is exercised.

(7) The Secretary of State exercising the power must make a statement, to the House of Parliament of which that Secretary of State is a member, announcing the new deadline.

(8) A statement under subsection (7) must be published in such form and manner as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

(8A) A statement under subsection (7) may be written or oral.
