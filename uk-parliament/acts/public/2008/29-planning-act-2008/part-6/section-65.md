## 65. Appointment of members, and lead member, of Panel[^key-5c185348ea5548348fd989e06d4cb274]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-79ad5a8dd294fb354ad6e8b09be0ab80] The Secretary of State must appoint—

- (a) two,[^key-d7ace574d6c9665ee80b5a37af82a285] three, four or five persons to be members of the Panel, and
- (b) one of those persons to chair the Panel.

(2) In this Chapter “**the lead member**” means the person who for the time being is appointed to chair the Panel.
