# Planning Act 2008, section 64

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (75).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Panel for each application to be handled under this Chapter[^key-f73359a6d1b9f6fd4d037785f5e42c6f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This Chapter applies where—

- (a) the Secretary of State[^key-b94600251fa566aedb29e3b350fd8762] accepts an application for an order granting development consent, and
- (b) under section 61(2) or 62(2), it is decided that the application is to be handled by a Panel under this Chapter.

(2) There is to be a Panel (referred to in this Chapter as “**the Panel**”) to handle the application.
