## 66. Ceasing to be member, or lead member, of Panel[^c-164153c43e03ba9ed5d219bcc243acb9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-097e20607963b596e1dbfd3ebedc3c7e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The person appointed to be the lead member ceases to hold that office if the person ceases to be a member of the Panel.

(3) A person may resign from membership of the Panel by giving notice in writing to the Secretary of State[^c-881e5083048f3714e0af2411c39f9453].

(4) The lead member may resign that office, without also resigning from membership of the Panel, by giving notice in writing to the Secretary of State[^c-7648f528f1939b18db12feb50e367476].

(5) The Secretary of State[^c-82d1dcd68aecdd80eb8b96c2fdb25511]—

- (a) may remove a person (“the Panel member”) from membership of the Panel if the Secretary of State[^c-8d5b530f02ccc62ed71bbe2c2df3ea8f] is satisfied that the Panel member is unable, unwilling or unfit to perform the duties of Panel membership;
- (b) may remove the lead member from that office, without also removing the lead member from membership of the Panel, if the Secretary of State[^c-fa760451e752236eda13aff33e330aba] is satisfied that the lead member is unable, unwilling or unfit to perform the duties of the office.
