## 117. Orders granting development consent: formalities[^c-f321d4320104cf3be7314ac079ae4de5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This section applies in relation to an order granting development consent.

(2)[^c-444043d583255993b529765415de7b5b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Except in a case within subsection (4), the Secretary of State[^c-d8af96fe00983166d1b71d506af75d66] must publish the order in such manner as the Secretary of State[^c-199ab9e49ca5673bf1717e7fabae51bd] thinks appropriate.

(4)[^c-da6c4614403cb856232e0df0fa8fe7b2] If the order includes provision—

- (a) made under section 120(3) for or relating to any of the matters listed in paragraphs 32A and 32B of Schedule 5, or
- (b) made in the exercise of any of the powers conferred by section 120(5)(a) or (b),

the order must be contained in a statutory instrument.

(5)[^c-f9d77cfbc30c6ff6c3b54d1a1cdcee78] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) As soon as practicable after the instrument containing the order is made, the Secretary of State[^c-bad585b401df116883c889026327da27] must deposit in the office of the Clerk of the Parliaments a copy of—

- (a) the instrument,
- (b) the latest version of any plan supplied by the applicant in connection with the application for the order contained in the instrument, and
- (c) the statement of reasons prepared under section 116(1).

(7)[^c-8852cc2587cd0fa5a19a6896e5863deb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
