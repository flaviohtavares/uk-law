# Planning Act 2008, section 106

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (75).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/29

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 106. Matters that may be disregarded when deciding application[^key-c8f81822eb9cd8859884b10099db57cc]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In deciding an application for an order granting development consent, the Secretary of State[^key-47f31e79b3aa1589575a7c309f15d9fc] may disregard representations if the Secretary of State considers that the representations—

- (a) are vexatious or frivolous,
- (b) relate to the merits of policy set out in a national policy statement, or
- (c) relate to compensation for compulsory acquisition of land or of an interest in or right over land.

(2) In this section “**representation**” includes evidence.
