**9.**[^c-464443490b8b4d4f0eec59e00d3e2625] Freeing land from any restriction imposed on it by or under the Green Belt (London and Home Counties) Act 1938 (c. xciii), or by a covenant or other agreement entered into for the purposes of that Act.

**10.**[^c-1d173e6a37989f07098eb3b026c43197] The protection of the property or interests of any person.

**11.**[^c-8f6a24e8376a9adca3c2d72c87b8e349] The imposition or exclusion of obligations or liability in respect of acts or omissions.

**25.**[^c-3001964a7b7d41c887fb34cdc3e56ac9] Entering into an agreement for the provision of police services.

**26.**[^c-e35c8f010a8faaa70d95b7dcb8a75597] The discharge of water into inland waters or underground strata.

**32.**[^c-c5430f9a88c75be9cfecc8ffec9570ce] Changing the powers and duties of a harbour authority.

- (a) non-payment of tolls, fares or other charges,
- (b) a person's failure to give the person's name or address in accordance with provision relating to penalty fares,
- (c) enforcement of byelaws, or
- (d) construction, improvement, maintenance or management of a harbour.

(2) An offence is within this sub-paragraph if—

- (a) it is triable only summarily,
- (b) a person guilty of the offence is not liable to imprisonment, and
- (c) any fine to which a person guilty of the offence may be liable cannot be higher than level 3 on the standard scale.

**36.**[^c-50b7217be61f848e193ae2eeebad3c64] The payment of compensation.

**37.**[^c-a3ff886fc4e9408bf823dc105d417aaf] The submission of disputes to arbitration.

**38.**[^c-d93788b3b65156f58b52319a3b9153d0] The alteration of borrowing limits.

### Part 2 — Interpretation

**39.**[^c-5be5ee01904ae6a3c7f1b4dc24540c0e] (1) This paragraph applies for the purposes of this Schedule.

(2) “**Transport system**” means any of the following—

- (a) a railway,
- (b) a tramway,
- (c) a trolley vehicle system,
- (d) a system using a mode of guided transport prescribed by order under section 2 of the Transport and Works Act 1992 (c. 42).

(3) “**Maintenance**”, in relation to a transport system, includes the inspection, repair, adjustment, alteration, removal, reconstruction or replacement of the system.

(4) The following terms have the meanings given by section 67(1) (interpretation) of the Transport and Works Act 1992 (c. 42)—

- “guided transport”,
- “tramway”,
- “trolley vehicle system”.
