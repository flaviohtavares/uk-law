## 161. Breach of terms of order granting development consent[^c19535511][^c-8e7a3fb7f059ccc5f0436b51988027f3][^c-50870833b5becb6b67ab99ce821c70ab]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-edf00614fd17a886004e503dd697bc24][^c-6cf9dd47df24941d83e9dfdaa910c927] A person commits an offence if without reasonable excuse the person—

- (a) carries out, or causes to be carried out, development in breach of the terms of an order granting development consent, or
- (b) otherwise fails to comply with the terms of an order granting development consent.

(2) Subsection (1) is subject to section 149A(4)[^c-4e3f2cb3c2edae8f73fac61c3ba68e62] .

(3) It is a defence for a person charged with an offence under this section to prove that—

- (a) the breach or failure to comply occurred only because of an error or omission in the order, and
- (b) a correction notice specifying the correction of the error or omission has been issued under paragraph 2 of Schedule 4.

(4) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13f88683b90a7e68bf3d35df725e4aaa]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-13f88683b90a7e68bf3d35df725e4aaa] on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding £50,000, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13f88683b90a7e68bf3d35df725e4aaa]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-13f88683b90a7e68bf3d35df725e4aaa] on conviction on indictment, to a fine. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-13f88683b90a7e68bf3d35df725e4aaa] on summary conviction, or on conviction on indictment, to a fine *(Extent: E+W)*

(5) The Secretary of State may by order amend subsection (4)(a) to increase the level of the fine for the time being specified in that provision. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-2d9dd33e7a1323dda5fde4e108b479b3]
