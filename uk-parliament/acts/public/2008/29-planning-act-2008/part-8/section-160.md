## 160. Development without development consent[^c19535501][^key-7001c53bde05ff16861fb2e3fe597ac0][^key-7c352d36a2b2da77ef429b5e410cb5b7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person commits an offence if the person carries out, or causes to be carried out, development for which development consent is required at a time when no development consent is in force in respect of the development.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db1fe5511fabc2e764d4435d2705dc5]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6db1fe5511fabc2e764d4435d2705dc5] on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding £50,000, or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db1fe5511fabc2e764d4435d2705dc5]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-6db1fe5511fabc2e764d4435d2705dc5] on conviction on indictment, to a fine. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-6db1fe5511fabc2e764d4435d2705dc5] on summary conviction, or on conviction on indictment, to a fine *(Extent: E+W)*

(3) The Secretary of State may by order amend subsection (2)(a) to increase the level of the fine for the time being specified in that provision. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-283187a6b9dc8e65d896b7c9c4547979]
