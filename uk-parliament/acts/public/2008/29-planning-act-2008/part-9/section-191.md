## 191. Validity of orders, decisions and directions

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-887334029702d2c4a513c4bc713a13f2] Section 284(3) of TCPA 1990 (validity of certain actions on the part of the Secretary of State) is amended as follows.

(2)[^c-57b36c5a2f126c3cae982ec2516c3294] Before paragraph (a) insert—

> - (za) any decision on an application referred to the Secretary of State under section 76A;

.

(3)[^c-887334029702d2c4a513c4bc713a13f2] In paragraph (a) omit “for planning permission”.
