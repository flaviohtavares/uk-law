# Planning Act 2008, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (75).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Nationally significant infrastructure projects: general[^c19534141]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this Act “**nationally significant infrastructure project**” means a project which consists of any of the following—

- (a)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] the construction or extension of a generating station;
- (b)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] the installation of an electric line above ground;
- (c)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] development relating to underground gas storage facilities;
- (d)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] the construction or alteration of an LNG facility;
- (e)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] the construction or alteration of a gas reception facility;
- (f)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] the construction of a pipe-line by a gas transporter;
- (g)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] the construction of a pipe-line other than by a gas transporter;
- (h)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] highway-related development;
- (i)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] airport-related development;
- (j)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] the construction or alteration of harbour facilities;
- (k)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] the construction or alteration of a railway;
- (l)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] the construction or alteration of a rail freight interchange;
- (m)[^c-04082d71397c177ff840eb7ee5e36365] the construction or alteration of a dam or reservoir;
- (n)[^c-fbe533690ddcc0bce3e6ce8a0926caec] development relating to the transfer of water resources;
- (na)[^c-f1589fc1fc0651d9d6f27aa174be2c58] the construction or alteration of a desalination plant;
- (o)[^c-6def54f370b1a8f95867a47210cfbde6] the construction or alteration of a waste water treatment plant or of infrastructure for the transfer or storage of waste water[^c-5431eadd5387dbc5087416eb45625d87] ;
- (p)[^c-cb4bcb814b212e2bf58f9550bf18ca46] the construction or alteration of a hazardous waste facility ;[^c-61da44e8c344511b6c611f8938b09641]
- (q) development relating to a radioactive waste geological disposal facility.

(2)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] Subsection (1) is subject to sections 15 to 30A[^c-7d2c54f60d1013b02610a505da4b74dc] .

(3)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] The Secretary of State may by order—

- (a) amend subsection (1) to add a new type of project or vary or remove an existing type of project;
- (b) make further provision, or amend or repeal existing provision, about the types of project which are, and are not, within subsection (1).

(3A)[^c-01b4f4f323954e4a65f37bbf2ab19288] An order under subsection (3)(a) may also amend section 6(7)(a) of the Public Order Act 2023 (obstruction etc of major transport works).

(4)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] An order under subsection (3)(b) may amend this Act.

(5)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] The power conferred by subsection (3) may be exercised to add a new type of project to subsection (1) only if—

- (a) a project of the new type is a project for the carrying out of works in one or more of the fields specified in subsection (6), and
- (b) the works are to be carried out wholly in one or more of the areas specified in subsection (7).

(6)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] The fields are—

- (a) energy;
- (b) transport;
- (c) water;
- (d) waste water;
- (e) waste.

(7)[^c-66adf0bf99e059845aa6af9589979974] The areas are—

- (a) England;
- (b) waters adjacent to England up to the seaward limits of the territorial sea;
- (c) in the case of a project for the carrying out of works in the field of energy, a Renewable Energy Zone, except any part of a Renewable Energy Zone in relation to which the Scottish Ministers have functions.
