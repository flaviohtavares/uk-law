# Regulatory Enforcement and Sanctions Act 2008, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/13
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Relationship with other regulators

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State[^c-51caf72c6d6ecdb30cd5038960ef591c] and a regulator to which this section applies must enter into a memorandum of understanding with each other as to how they will work together in the exercise of their respective functions.

(2) This section applies to the following regulators—

- (a) the Environment Agency;
- (b) the Food Standards Agency;
- (c) the Gambling Commission;
- (d) the Health and Safety Executive;
- (e) the Competition and Markets Authority[^c-3fab8f4f21f0340d42d61c4ae36326c6] .

(3)[^c-10f49e2ae78c51c9c86db1b6c57908c9] The Secretary of State must consult the Welsh Ministers about—

- (a) revising an existing memorandum of understanding, or
- (b) entering into a new one,

which relates to a devolved Welsh[^c-3aac9b9b0984448bd3d830df8f07edfb] matter.
