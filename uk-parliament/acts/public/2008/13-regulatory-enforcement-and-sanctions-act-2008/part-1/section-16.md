## 16. Guidance or directions by Welsh Ministers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Welsh Ministers may give the Secretary of State[^c-c3e8e35119a4d62f4fa535c181d563b0] —

- (a) guidance, [^c-ade6d5301aad5502a0c698d922841c11]...
- (b)[^c-ade6d5301aad5502a0c698d922841c11] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

as to the exercise in relation to Wales of any of the Secretary of State’s functions under this Act so far as they relate[^c-620acc426a4c265538e2847b3e6c5c6b] to a devolved Welsh[^c-e9771c2ed389e4687b79b7788a666ff9] matter.

(2) Before giving any guidance [^c-b1ad9ac1d13cd4b7f1ce2da6e5af1dda]... under this section the Welsh Ministers must consult—

- (a) the Secretary of State[^c-4f2f63a6a866d9c28ae990460686b65f] , and
- (b) such other persons as the Welsh Ministers consider likely to be affected by the guidance [^c-b1ad9ac1d13cd4b7f1ce2da6e5af1dda]..., or persons representative of such persons.

(3) The Welsh Ministers must—

- (a) publish (in such manner as they consider appropriate) any guidance [^c-f5ef93736f0ec1384ce224355d1237b6]... given under this section, and
- (b) lay a copy of any such guidance [^c-f5ef93736f0ec1384ce224355d1237b6]... before the National Assembly for Wales.

(4) The Secretary of State[^c-4c48a65f1197e009efabc0fca9493b37] must have regard to any guidance[^c-b6a39823e4a122aab84fdb75afab2682]... given under this section.

(5) The Welsh Ministers may vary or revoke any guidance [^c-ebf7c70029510f02ef46c99363e96354]... given under this section by further guidance [^c-ebf7c70029510f02ef46c99363e96354]... under this section.

(6)[^c-3a984e3ff24bf060406176995d764cb6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^c-3a984e3ff24bf060406176995d764cb6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
