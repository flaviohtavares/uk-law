# Regulatory Enforcement and Sanctions Act 2008, section 5

## 5. Objective relating to general functions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In exercising their functions under sections 6 to 10 the Secretary of State and the Welsh Ministers have[^key-4e2b56307bbea4b8df429c38b9a686dd] the objective of securing that local authorities in England and Wales exercise their relevant functions—

- (a) effectively,
- (b) in a way which does not give rise to unnecessary burdens, and
- (c) in a way which conforms with the principles in subsection (2).

(2) Those principles are that—

- (a) regulatory activities should be carried out in a way which is transparent, accountable, proportionate and consistent;
- (b) regulatory activities should be targeted only at cases in which action is needed.
