# Regulatory Enforcement and Sanctions Act 2008, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/13
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Enforcement priorities

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-6cf66a4f547513654aa9eb1581a354fc] The Secretary of State must prepare and publish a list specifying those matters to which a local authority in England should give priority when allocating resources to its relevant functions.

(1A) The Welsh Ministers must prepare and publish a list specifying those matters to which a local authority in Wales should give priority when allocating resources to its relevant functions.

(2) A local authority in England or Wales must have regard to the appropriate list under subsection (1) or (1A)[^c-cfc13fce92c7d66d18132a17ad40dc25] when allocating resources to its relevant functions.

(3) Before publishing a list under subsection (1) the person preparing the list[^c-7b1593497ef3994d5660c50d471c30c7] must consult such persons as that person[^c-563981a8b25401f7ad34b79eb98fa65a] considers appropriate.

(4) The person preparing the list[^c-482578fd56352d8e9f76b98cfb93c483] must publish details of any representations made [^c-41a39c693f5dc088bbcdf4fe9012098c]... pursuant to subsection (3).

(5)[^c-60b3153195f28dc75929177cf59ce0f2] Before publishing a list under subsection (1A), the Welsh Ministers must consult the Secretary of State.

(6)[^c-872ee426d58def1c8f46d22bc92612e8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^c-d3cafa48bfdc4a3602218c35f8ab460d] A list published under this section must be reviewed from time to time by the person who published it.

(8) A list revised as a result of a review under subsection (7) must be published (and subsections (2) to (7) have effect in relation to it).
