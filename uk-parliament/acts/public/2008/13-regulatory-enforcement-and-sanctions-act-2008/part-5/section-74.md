## 74. General interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act—

- **devolved Welsh matter[^key-2e4324160825b7afd30610ce71b29ac2]**” means —
  - (a) a matter within the legislative competence of the National Assembly for Wales (see section 108A[^key-00401d517bbfef8dd5c6db7580b6cf09] of the Government of Wales Act 2006), or
  - (b) a matter in relation to Wales in respect of which functions are exercisable by the Welsh Ministers,

  and in this definition “**Wales**” has the same meaning as in the Government of Wales Act 2006;
- “**Minister of the Crown**” has the same meaning as in the Ministers of the Crown Act 1975 (c. 26);
- “**regulatory function**” has the same meaning as in the Legislative and Regulatory Reform Act 2006 (c. 51);
