# Regulatory Enforcement and Sanctions Act 2008, section 66

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/13
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Compliance with regulatory principles[^key-7171958a5e47e86b2f842d12e0834895][^key-7048cbd581f0fb7d39c93120d38d83fd][^key-97bd540a18b53e87a8b46c97ae10901e][^key-7d7b65a091dababca67cd1fa8b80039a][^key-e5dae438812b5161c5fb9329d2ee2b90]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The relevant authority may not make any provision under or by virtue of this Part conferring power on a regulator to impose a civil sanction in relation to an offence unless the authority is satisfied that the regulator will act in accordance with the principles referred to in section 5(2) in exercising that power.
