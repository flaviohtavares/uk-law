# Regulatory Enforcement and Sanctions Act 2008, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/13

## 36. Power to make orders providing for civil sanctions[^key-c2863f7ad44c621776e331627b0e20a2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A Minister of the Crown may by order in accordance with this Part make—

- (a) the provision specified in section 39 (fixed monetary penalties);
- (b) the provision specified in section 42 (discretionary requirements);
- (c) the provision specified in section 46 (stop notices);
- (d) the provision specified in section 50 (enforcement undertakings).

(2) The Welsh Ministers may by order in accordance with this Part make any such provision, where the provision relates to a devolved Welsh[^key-a3a65e6b197cbd4fd7e70c6992600652] matter.

(3) An order under this Part is to be made by statutory instrument.
