# Regulatory Enforcement and Sanctions Act 2008, section 59

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/13

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Consultation and consent: Wales

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A Minister of the Crown must consult the Welsh Ministers before making an order under this Part in relation to an offence which applies in or in relation to Wales.

(2) A Minister of the Crown must obtain the consent of the Welsh Ministers before making an order under this Part containing provision which relates to a devolved Welsh[^c-fe393dcd4bbb5adcf1ca9dacc058d1a9] matter.

(3)[^c-469addd1965877497f2faffc54eda711] The Welsh Ministers must consult the Secretary of State before making an order under this Part.
