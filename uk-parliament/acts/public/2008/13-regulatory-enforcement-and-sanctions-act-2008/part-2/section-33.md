# Regulatory Enforcement and Sanctions Act 2008, section 33

## 33. Guidance from the Secretary of State[^M_F_a28eb310-b0a6-4915-8e6f-06de54c52817]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State[^key-8e6bc760eb182f6f3037267dc6ff69f0] may give guidance to any one or more local authorities about the operation of this Part.

(2) The guidance may include, in particular, guidance to local authorities about—

- (a) arrangements under section 27(2);
- (b) notification of inspection plans under section 30(6);
- (c) the charging of fees under section 31.

(3) A local authority must have regard to any guidance given to it under this section.

(4) Before giving guidance under this section the Secretary of State[^key-a09244c02790b219ea9435d451b421d4] must consult such persons as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

(5) The Secretary of State[^key-fb7649a6ccf8ee87241f8d4d18f26a40] may not give guidance under subsection (2)(c)—

- (a)[^key-709e813a89388138026ac036dafac96d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) without having consulted the Welsh Ministers.

(6) The Secretary of State[^key-8fc17f6584ff6cd2bc384f065689836e] must publish (in such manner as the Secretary of State[^key-fd5c61f37984c8e381b3e3d110a40f93] considers appropriate) any guidance given [^key-529c73e5f02599441a1cb58b2cae9e78]... under this section.

(7) The Secretary of State[^key-8505d170b757579ee4a5852232d219a7] may at any time vary or revoke any guidance given under this section by further guidance under this section.
