# Regulatory Enforcement and Sanctions Act 2008, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/13

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Power to charge

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The primary authority may charge the regulated person such fees as it considers to represent the costs reasonably incurred by it in the exercise of its functions under this Part in relation to the regulated person.
