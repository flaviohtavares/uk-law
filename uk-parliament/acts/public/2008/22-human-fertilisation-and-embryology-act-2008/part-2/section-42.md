# Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act 2008, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2022-01-27. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Woman in civil partnership or marriage to a woman (Extent: E+W) at time of treatment[^key-15ba8b571c963299da3297619de1f2c2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If at the time of the placing in her of the embryo or the sperm and eggs or of her artificial insemination, W was a party to a civil partnership with another woman *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^key-fab485fedad53ad76e3eb9cc8b7a5700] or a marriage with another woman[^key-eacae7702445601d6087308d6cec0cc3], then subject to section 45(2) to (4), the other party to the civil partnership or marriage[^key-1994f01965e4f93b36f772ee939db606] is to be treated as a parent of the child unless it is shown that she did not consent to the placing in W of the embryo or the sperm and eggs or to her artificial insemination (as the case may be).

(2) This section applies whether W was in the United Kingdom or elsewhere at the time mentioned in subsection (1).
