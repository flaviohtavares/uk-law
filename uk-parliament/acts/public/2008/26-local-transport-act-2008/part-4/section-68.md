# Local Transport Act 2008, section 68

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-04-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Subsidy to secure passenger transport services in other areas

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-e68fd680e63f3941b88582d9a0b5eb91][^c-eecda40ba35ecb976634bf2e9c0fc10a][^c-aaea4497aea99be3a4cfd079475c5e92] Section 63 of the TA 1985 (functions of local councils with respect to passenger transport in areas other than passenger transport areas) is amended as follows.

(2)[^c-2e13922856f36c991a0c98abb18acf83][^c-517b09c309cd931b97269b3b7cfb250b] In subsection (5) (power to enter into agreements providing for service subsidies) at the beginning of paragraph (a) insert

> in England and Wales, shall be exercisable only where the service in question would not be provided, or would not be provided to a particular standard, without subsidy;
>
> - (aa) in Scotland,

.

(3)[^c-a8d4689da45d69580b508796b30537a6][^c-c89e9a0ca9f8825b1ab8eca7a0e77b04] After subsection (5) insert—

> (5A) The reference in subsection (5)(a) above to the standard to which a service is provided includes—
>
> - (a) the frequency or timing of the service;
> - (b) the days, or times of day, when the service is provided;
> - (c) the vehicles used to provide the service.

.
