# Local Transport Act 2008, section 89

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-04-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Contravention of an order under section 88[^c1952781][^key-d4f831ae2ddf16898892a34c20244318]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An order under section 88 may provide that, if an authority exercises any power in contravention of a direction under such an order, the directing authority may take such steps as it considers appropriate to reverse or modify the effect of the exercise of the power.

(2)[^key-cd005df32f58a73a1832ff84c8c4e6ed] For the purposes of subsection (1), the directing authority has power to exercise any power of the authority subject to the direction on behalf of that authority.

(3)[^key-cd005df32f58a73a1832ff84c8c4e6ed] Any reasonable expenses incurred by the directing authority in taking any steps under subsection (1) are recoverable from the authority subject to the direction as a civil debt.
