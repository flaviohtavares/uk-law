# Local Transport Act 2008, section 112

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-04-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 112. Charges

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-b9bafa9599682b0db317c1dfe96e2807][^key-c202a87cdea4851cd02ca8d22443e9d5] In section 171(5) of the TA 2000 (different charges for different cases) after paragraph (e) insert

> , and
>
> - (f) different methods or means of recording, administering, collecting or paying the charge.

.

(2)[^key-9b0fd42efc4220da4ae8008ffb84bd77] In Schedule 23 to the GLA Act 1999 (road user charging) in paragraph 10(4) (the charges)—

- (a) after “different charges (which may be no charge) for” insert “ different cases, including (in particular) ”;
- (b) after paragraph (e) insert—

  > - (f) different methods or means of recording, administering, collecting or paying the charge.

  .
