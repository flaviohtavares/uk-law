# Local Transport Act 2008, section 105

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-04-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 105. Joint local charging schemes to implement policies of ITAs[^c-a812225c4b9f6b92f92d15d38801f0f3][^c-d3070ed19dd17a2711b3fc1212b18b08]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 165 of the TA 2000 (joint local charging schemes) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (2)—

- (a) after “A joint local charging scheme” insert “ which has effect wholly outside an integrated transport area ”;
- (b) for “policies in the charging authorities' local transport plans” substitute “ local transport policies of the charging authorities ”.

(3) After subsection (2) insert—

> (3) A joint local charging scheme which has effect wholly or partly within an integrated transport area may only be made if it appears desirable for the purpose of directly or indirectly facilitating the achievement of local transport policies of—
>
> - (a) the charging authorities, and
> - (b) the Integrated Transport Authority for the integrated transport area.
>
> (4) Section 164(4) has effect for the purposes of this section.
