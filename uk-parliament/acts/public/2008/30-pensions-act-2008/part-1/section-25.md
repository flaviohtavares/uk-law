## 25. Quality requirement: non-UK occupational pension schemes[^c19537451]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision as to the quality requirement to be satisfied in the case of an occupational pension scheme within section 18(c)[^c-0d3522f1ef2906bde7b1eed58434ced9].
