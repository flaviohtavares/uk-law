## 18. Occupational pension schemes[^c19537301]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of this Part, each of these is an occupational pension scheme—

- (a) an occupational pension scheme within the meaning of section 1(1) of the Pension Schemes Act 1993 (c. 48) that has its main administration in the United Kingdom;
- (b)[^key-658ea7a54dd5205233d4a6cfdbc13214] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) a pension scheme that is prescribed or is of a prescribed description and that has its main administration elsewhere than in the United Kingdom[^key-2048ebada03cdfc28725bd0e78178d79].
