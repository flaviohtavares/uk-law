# Pensions Act 2008, section 13

## 13. Qualifying earnings[^c19537251][^c-0844e1fc13967af159ad4d1761437624]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person's qualifying earnings in a pay reference period of 12 months are the part (if any) of the gross earnings payable to that person in that period that is—

- (a) more than £6,240[^c-33318fb97164380a4e0e22c96c0d344c], and
- (b) not more than £50,270[^c-9d174ce756d87272d4e4e1249a4397de].

(2)[^c21677661][^c-2efefbc919b0da09017f31dfec66f17a][^c-8e6b0521f7917c22c55383a26660985a][^c-3864e4489f642d05dc4da327a5174b5e][^c-ea12129da6709df556ec0b3bab400785][^c-99a7cc614248c9e060e206be8db9b84b][^c-07e865d7345ecfc00b37fbb45c2ffcfd][^c-8676e7ba824acc495c03d3d5466f5464] In the case of a pay reference period of less or more than 12 months, subsection (1) applies as if the amounts in paragraphs (a) and (b) were proportionately less or more.

(3) In this section, “**earnings**”, in relation to a person, means sums of any of the following descriptions that are payable to the person in connection with the person's employment—

- (a) salary, wages, commission, bonuses and overtime;
- (b) statutory sick pay under Part 11 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 (c. 4);
- (c) statutory maternity pay under Part 12 of that Act;
- (d) statutory paternity pay[^c-08abba2fec8d67dbc56915c6c9ed56cf] under Part 12ZA of that Act;
- (e) statutory adoption pay under Part 12ZB of that Act;
- (ea)[^c-ad3e3c7ecddf8fe051e4667278f45783] statutory shared parental pay under Part 12ZC of that Act;
- (eb)[^c-ef0499d8ef496f6bf928e6e9059699e9] statutory parental bereavement pay under Part 12ZD of that Act;
- (ec)[^c-f24f654bada4a31d2006ed3493789351] statutory neonatal care pay under Part 12ZE of that Act;
- (f) sums prescribed for the purposes of this section.
