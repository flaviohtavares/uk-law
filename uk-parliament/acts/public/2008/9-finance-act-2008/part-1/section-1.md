# Finance Act 2008, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (12).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Charge and main rates for 2008-09

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Income tax is charged for the tax year 2008-09.

(2) For that tax year—

- (a) the basic rate is 20%, and
- (b) the higher rate is 40%.
