# Energy Act 2008, section 25

> Text as at 2026-10-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Failure to comply with a direction under section 24[^key-8843165f8e0d4e868642587cf256bb97]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It is an offence for a person to fail to comply with a direction under section 24, unless the person proves that due diligence was exercised in order to avoid the failure.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under subsection (1) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding £50,000 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-b265ce153a092ae18bf164037ebb78da]a fine *(Extent: E+W)*, or
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or to a fine, or both.
