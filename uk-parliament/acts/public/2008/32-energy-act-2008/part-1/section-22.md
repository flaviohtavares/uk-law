# Energy Act 2008, section 22

> Text as at 2026-10-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Offence to carry on unlicensed activities[^c-d5c36bb6f4c5041a770995f9717fb531]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It is an offence for a person to carry on an activity within section 17(2) at a controlled place unless, at the time the activity is carried on, that person—

- (a) has a licence for the carrying on of the activity at that place, or
- (b) is carrying on the activity on behalf of a person who has such a licence.

(2) It is an offence for a person to cause or permit another person to commit the offence in subsection (1).

(3) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding £50,000 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-24ca5f2ec0461b65bf4646dced3f8cc9]a fine *(Extent: E+W)*, or
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or to a fine, or both.

(4) If the activity constituting the offence falls within section 17(2)(c), or relates to the establishment or maintenance of an installation for the purposes of an activity mentioned in that provision, subsection (3) has effect as if—

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-05ba02fbe08ed259ae0fc499fd82fc4f] the reference to £50,000 were a reference to the statutory maximum, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-05ba02fbe08ed259ae0fc499fd82fc4f]
- (b) the reference to imprisonment were omitted.

(4A (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-1fd43de03d969be2174583bff3fcd86d][^c-30cd6486c7d6d9b387ec5e26fdeb5f49] If the activity constituting the offence is carried on at an English controlled place, a Welsh controlled place or a Northern Ireland controlled place, subsection (3) has effect as if the reference to £50,000 were a reference to the statutory maximum. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-1fd43de03d969be2174583bff3fcd86d]

(5)[^c-2b706161e382d6ed828eb47a743fb69e] If the activity constituting the offence is carried out in a Scottish controlled place other than a place within the area of the territorial sea, subsection (3)(a) has effect as if the reference to £50,000 were a reference to £5,000.
