# Energy Act 2008, section 7

> Text as at 2026-10-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Model clauses[^key-0843b4670cf24f9a5acb492eca5a05ec]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may make regulations prescribing model clauses for licences.

(2) Subject to subsection (3), the model clauses, as they have effect at the time a licence is granted, are deemed to be incorporated into the licence.

(3) The OGA[^key-7e2b1e3a49f11da3ecfdd86412a7ae4c] may decide to exclude or modify one or more of those model clauses in the case of a particular licence.

(4)[^key-8d012e364a787916e6884635e58fa4b6] Before making any regulations under this section, the Secretary of State must consult the OGA.
