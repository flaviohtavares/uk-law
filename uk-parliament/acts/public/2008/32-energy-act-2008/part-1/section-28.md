# Energy Act 2008, section 28

> Text as at 2026-10-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Criminal proceedings[^key-cfe6de1eb84aba6dfc6d58062b467723]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Proceedings for a relevant offence may be taken, and the offence may for all incidental purposes be treated as having been committed, in any place in the United Kingdom.

(2) Section 3 of the Territorial Waters Jurisdiction Act 1878 (c. 73) (restriction on prosecutions) does not apply to any proceedings for a relevant offence.

(3) Proceedings for a relevant offence alleged to have been committed in an offshore controlled place[^key-f9c62e37aa5b7a1fb8f0d6a11573342a] may not be instituted in England and Wales except—

- (a) by the Secretary of State or a person authorised by the Secretary of State, or
- (b) by or with the consent of the Director of Public Prosecutions.

(4) Proceedings for a relevant offence alleged to have been committed in an offshore controlled place[^key-8393356d03719fd1773493e89a82f830] may not be instituted in Northern Ireland except—

- (a) by the Secretary of State or a person authorised by the Secretary of State, or
- (b) by or with the consent of the Director of Public Prosecutions for Northern Ireland.

(5) In the application of subsection (3) or (4) to an offence created by regulations under section 27—

- (a) the words “alleged to have been committed in an offshore controlled place[^key-690249a2f91500c55f54b5296d9c2b16]” are to be omitted, and
- (b) except in the case of an offence that relates to functions of the OGA under this Chapter,[^key-56d3dc70dbc1165e64b7e75d29c1f266] the references to a person authorised by the Secretary of State are to be read as references to an inspector appointed under that section.

(6) In this section “**relevant offence**” means an offence under this Chapter or created by regulations under section 27.
