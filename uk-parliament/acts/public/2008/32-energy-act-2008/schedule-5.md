# Energy Act 2008, SCHEDULE 5

> Text as at 2026-10-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 5 — Minor and consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 107

### Pipe-lines Act 1962 (c. 58)

**1.**[^key-3854066674380bd571c7bcbcba855153] In section 54 of the Pipe-lines Act 1962 (offences by corporations), in subsection (1) after “any of the provisions of this Act” insert “ (other than section 10H) ”.

### Gas Act 1986 (c. 44)

**2.**[^key-92ad72f006b6c382db2dc5e2d825fabc] In section 64(2) of the Gas Act 1986 (orders) after “41C” insert “ , 41HA, ”.

### Electricity Act 1989 (c. 29)

**3.**[^key-7d7f73c32e86210184ce8d7a1df7f2f3] In section 25 of the Electricity Act 1989 (orders for securing compliance), in subsection (8), in the definition of “relevant requirement” for “sections 32 to 32C” substitute “ sections 32 to 32M ”.

**4.**[^key-0730f98796449c79e5a208922e716bae] In section 47 of that Act (general functions of the Authority)—

- (a) in subsection (1A) after “microgeneration” insert “ or small-scale low-carbon generation ”, and
- (b) for subsection (1B) substitute—

  > (1B) In subsection (1A)—
  >
  > - “**microgeneration**” has the same meaning as in the Climate Change and Sustainable Energy Act 2006;
  > - “**small-scale low-carbon generation**” has the same meaning as in section 41 of the Energy Act 2008.

**5.** In section 106 of that Act (regulations and orders), after subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) Any power of the Scottish Ministers to make orders under section 32 is exercisable by statutory instrument.

**6.**[^key-903bee476bd8636b090e0df5975d3836] In section 106(2)(b) of that Act (regulations and orders) for “or 56A” substitute “ , 56A or 56FA ”.

### Petroleum Act 1998 (c. 17)

**7.**[^key-0408fee0234b0cec4eea570e31728e79] The Petroleum Act 1998 is amended as follows.

**8.**[^key-0096747bd36ac79d95dad9b4a27edac7] In section 5(9) (alteration of licences) for “an instrument under seal” substitute “ deed ”.

**9.**[^key-44f3d201283c6dc3bcb9e769cf6d8ec2] In section 31(1) (notices: supplementary provision) omit “(d) or”.

**10.**[^key-3f5de0ae06d0ad7bb57b4df9a389480b] In section 34 (revision of programmes)—

- (a) in subsection (2)(a), after “(b)” insert “ (ba), ”, and
- (b) in subsection (3) omit “(d) or”.

**11.**[^key-aa314004955ea9f550a8d0cc163aabf1] In section 45 (interpretation), in the definition of “submarine pipeline” after “which is” insert “ , or is intended to be established, ”.

### Utilities Act 2000 (c. 27)

**12.**[^key-c1330fe1fc349da1efd06b0a938580c5] The Utilities Act 2000 is amended as follows.

**13.**[^key-2aaec655d431b192f76af160289b12c7] In section 33 (standard conditions of electricity licences), in subsection (1)—

- (a) after paragraph (b) omit “, or”, and
- (b) after paragraph (c) insert

  > , or
  >
  > - (d) under section 41, 84, 88 or 97 of the Energy Act 2008 (power to amend licence conditions).

**14.**[^key-e6c7e92f732aa956b3f71fbd9bb0a688] In section 81 (standard conditions of gas licences), in subsection (2) for “or under the Energy Act 2004” substitute “ , under the Energy Act 2004 or under section 88 or 94 of the Energy Act 2008 (power to amend licence conditions) ”.

**15.** In section 105 (general restrictions on disclosure of information)—

- (a)[^key-d013143dcb9710552914b9b40a2f9db1] in subsection (1)(a), omit the words from “or section 184(5)” to the end and insert “ , section 184(5) or 185(5) of the Energy Act 2004 or section 41 or 100 of the Energy Act 2008; ”,
- (b)[^key-d013143dcb9710552914b9b40a2f9db1] in subsection (3)(a), after “2004” insert “ sections 41 to 43 or section 100 of the Energy Act 2008 ”, and
- (c)[^key-b2258a741b1208b404a136d07914196c] in subsection (8)(a) after “section” (in the second place) insert “ 32J or ”.

### Energy Act 2004 (c. 20)

**16.**[^key-cf8521135c65028d7ac59e63ecea25ee] The Energy Act 2004 is amended as follows.

**17.**[^key-f3bc23ed75a0ab5f86292afc17ca0b43] Omit section 105(9) (power to require provision of information in connection with decommissioning programme).

**18.**[^key-82b860cd62de8a9708be6c9b1953b6d0] Omit section 107(5) to (7) (power to require provision of information in connection with decommissioning programme).

**19.**[^key-a2c0ba78549b84d8c1854bb9e6ada8d9] In section 132 (interpretation of Chapter 5 of Part 2), in subsection (4), in the definition of “fossil fuel” for “section 32” substitute “ section 32M ”.

**20.**[^key-04b8d78fa309199342b4ba3dbcef1cc4] In section 185 (adjustment of transmission charges), in subsection (14), in the definition of “renewable sources” for “evidence” to the end substitute “ renewables obligation certificates (within the meaning of section 32B of that Act) in respect of electricity generated from those sources is capable of satisfying a renewables obligation imposed by a renewables obligation order (within the meaning of section 32 of that Act); ”.

**21.**[^key-29b81ac8fb1688bd14d071093ab27d5b] In Schedule 13 (directions given to the Civil Nuclear Police Authority), in paragraph 2(1)(h) for “officers of the Secretary of State's department” substitute “ persons authorised by the Secretary of State ”.
