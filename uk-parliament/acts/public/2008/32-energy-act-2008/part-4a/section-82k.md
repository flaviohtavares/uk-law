# Energy Act 2008, section 82K

> Text as at 2026-10-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82K. Failure to comply with direction under section 82E[^c-c6edb742658f858855d9a38ca3936ae8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It is an offence for a person to fail to comply with a direction under section 82E, unless the person proves that due diligence was exercised in order to avoid the failure.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding £50,000 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-48870c62b2d714226e12451a521f0332]a fine *(Extent: E+W)*, or
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or to a fine, or both.
