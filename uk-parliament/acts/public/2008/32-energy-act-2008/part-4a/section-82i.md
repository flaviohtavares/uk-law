# Energy Act 2008, section 82I

> Text as at 2026-10-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82I. Carrying out operation without consent etc[^key-c6edb742658f858855d9a38ca3936ae8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It is an offence for a person—

- (a) to carry out an operation to which subsection (1) of section 82A applies without the written consent of the Secretary of State under that subsection, or
- (b) to fail to comply with a condition of such a consent.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding £50,000 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-939b735edffdce7798cddff85e3aeaa2]a fine *(Extent: E+W)*, or
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or to a fine, or both.
