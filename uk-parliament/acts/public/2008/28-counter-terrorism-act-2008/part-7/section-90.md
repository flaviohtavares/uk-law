# Counter-Terrorism Act 2008, section 90

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/28
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Application of provisions to costs incurred before commencement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Sections 85 to 89 apply in relation to costs incurred in the period—

- (a) beginning with 16th January 2007, and
- (b) ending with the day before those sections come into force,

as they apply in relation to costs incurred on or after that day.
