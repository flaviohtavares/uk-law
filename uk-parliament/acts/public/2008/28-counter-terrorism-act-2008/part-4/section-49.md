# Counter-Terrorism Act 2008, section 49

## 49. Periodic re-notification[^key-4fcad8839279b186d679620f13b82484]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person to whom the notification requirements apply must, within the applicable period[^key-d78e93f7013be0bee7f179e9cfb1d5b3] after last notifying the police in accordance with—

- (a) section 47 (initial notification),
- (b) section 48 (notification of change : general[^key-33b398fe9b8c4a78132869cce07bb62e]),
- (ba)[^key-bb9979ec21a91b65b85bb27ce8b55146] section 48A (notification of changes: financial information and information about identification documents),
- (c) this section, or
- (d) section 56 (notification on return after absence from UK),

re-notify to the police the information mentioned in section 47(2).

(1A)[^key-4d8d7e3ca92d1d33f75f6aba58c260e1] In this section the “**applicable period**” means—

- (a) in the case of a person who has no sole or main residence in the United Kingdom, the period of one week, and
- (b) in any other case, the period of one year.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply if the applicable period[^key-b014f1a01d21d853cf563047a4f83ec8] ends at a time when the person is—

- (a) remanded in or committed to custody by an order of a court,
- (b) serving a sentence of imprisonment or detention,
- (c) detained in a hospital, or
- (d) detained under the Immigration Acts.

(3) In that case section 48(4) and (10) (duty to notify of release and to re-notify other information) apply when the person is released.
