(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] This section applies where a court *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] in Northern Ireland[^c-d63c906e0d1deea962002ed201ebcd01] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] is considering for the purposes of sentence the seriousness of an offence *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] specified in Schedule 2 (offences where terrorist connection to be considered) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-31748271dc41caf60f662a2809fc8177] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] within subsection (5A) or (5B) *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] If having regard to the material before it for the purposes of sentencing it appears to the court that the offence has or may have a terrorist connection, the court must determine whether that is the case. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] For that purpose the court may hear evidence, and must take account of any representations made by the prosecution and the defence, as in the case of any other matter relevant for the purposes of sentence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] If the court determines that the offence has a terrorist connection, the court— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] must treat that fact as an aggravating factor, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] must state in open court that the offence was so aggravated. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] In this section “ *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]**sentence *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]**”, in relation to an offence, includes any order made by a court when dealing with a person in respect of the offence. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]

(5A (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c71a0b2ba570ab48d8bfdb2dfb35e228][^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] An offence is within this subsection if it— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] was committed on or after the day on which section 1 of the Counter-Terrorism and Sentencing Act 2021 came into force, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] is punishable on indictment with imprisonment for more than 2 years (or would be so punishable in the case of an offender aged at least 21), and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] is not specified in Schedule 1A. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]

(5B (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] An offence is within this subsection if it— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] was committed— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]
  - (i (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] on or after 12 April 2019 (being the date on which section 8 of the Counter-Terrorism and Border Security Act 2019, which extended this section to Northern Ireland, came into force), but *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]
  - (ii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] before the day on which section 1 of the Counter-Terrorism and Sentencing Act 2021 came into force, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] is specified in Schedule 2. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]

(5C (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3] Where an offence is found to have been committed over a period of 2 or more days, or at some time during a period of 2 or more days, it must be taken for the purposes of subsections (5A) and (5B) to have been committed on the last of those days. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-686853986395f2e7691d8e01c4beefb3]
