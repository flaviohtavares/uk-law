# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 116

## 116. Entry in the register: voluntary registration[^key-4e9fdbe5d85145ab9c4a19193312eb59]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-80f991175d1f1a15c2a36a8cbdede897] The regulator shall register anyone who—

- (a) is eligible for registration under section 112(1)[^key-3420a571b6692014b9093f1abb18c859], and
- (b) applies to be registered.

(2)[^key-ea4f48c97b2ab82a6d6274f16d0a0d22] The regulator may make provision about—

- (a) the form of an application;
- (b) the information to be contained in it or provided with it;
- (c) the manner in which an application is to submitted;
- (d) the consequences of failure to comply with provision under paragraphs (a) to (c).

(2A)[^key-d037833e19992bb73f3e584fa87ede41] The regulator must notify a body of the outcome of its application.

(3)[^key-80f991175d1f1a15c2a36a8cbdede897] This section is subject to section 117 (fees).

(4)[^key-80f991175d1f1a15c2a36a8cbdede897] Once entered in the register under this section[^key-e95604dc805e0e55f0ccbf796ed74e28] a body remains registered unless and until removed under section 118 or 119.

(5)[^key-80f991175d1f1a15c2a36a8cbdede897] It shall be presumed for all purposes that a person entered in the register under this section[^key-c684feb197f2020ed52ef62756ca225d] is eligible for registration while the registration lasts (irrespective of whether and why the person is later removed from the register).

(6)[^key-4b0bf5eb53a99945284d845d922ffcf1] This section deals with voluntary registration; for the registration of local authorities[^key-10316cd297d011acda8f816c21953039], see section 114.
