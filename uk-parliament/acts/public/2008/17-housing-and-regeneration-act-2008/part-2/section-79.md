## 79. English bodies[^key-da684a792d6a7327c0c1392a7000ff75][^key-7ecd7da575801839765e1a682a607229]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part “**English body**” means—

- (a) a registered charity whose address for the purposes of registration by the Charity Commission is in England,
- (b) a registered society[^key-a85a2bd2407dae5b1dab913643127dde] whose registered office for the purposes of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014[^key-2b7d81b76d65f1baec9adb9d76f3135e] is in England,
- (c) a registered company which has its registered office in England,
- (d)[^key-c5eb2c55ccdc36b689b70ebfb3a44564] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^key-abe2c28e3e31cec7fcb7fbd28b5354b9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . and[^key-eadd4cfad7099de36135ed311d767bd7]
- (f) a limited liability partnership which has its registered office in England.
