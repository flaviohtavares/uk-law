## 267. Section 266: supplemental[^c-742eba10e352f29d18428df92d6c4726]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Before making an order under section 266 in respect of an officer the regulator must take all reasonable steps to give at least 14 days' notice to—

- (a) the officer, and
- (b) the registered provider.

(1A)[^c-7a974bc4c6a4e25603341b2eff51ec9b] If the regulator makes an order, the regulator must—

- (a) take all reasonable steps to notify the person removed, and
- (b) notify the registered provider.

(1B)[^c-82f4bb4e8ce1609a1dfa2dc6c0161d37] The regulator must notify the Charity Commission if it makes an order removing an officer of a registered charity.
