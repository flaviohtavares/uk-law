## 112. Eligibility for voluntary registration[^key-f50120cb609fcf8c8cfde21048708b44]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-493baa0f7a89b7b1249d42bc9bacd837] A body is eligible for registration if—

- (a) it is an English body, and
- (b) it satisfies the following conditions.

(2)[^key-140a928c2dd5ec173bb5883194c3f3b4] Condition 1 is that the body—

- (a) is a provider of social housing in England, or
- (b) intends to become a provider of social housing in England.

(3)[^key-99f5315dc38122b7a5af98d70ba34fdf][^key-0afbb1933f622b9f211a47fbae9d6157] Condition 2 is that the body satisfies any relevant criteria set by the regulator [^key-ad2b8ace1b4206b2411c9f70143a344b]...—

- (a) as to[^key-90f5414f08c995a21ffc06c539b1c4b1] its financial situation,
- (b) as to[^key-399fa678dd4b6a5ed03e44c81c58ad49] its constitution, [^key-9ebecb86dfce11322bd767aaf9999772]...
- (c) as to[^key-8df935e0bb749a5feb5bb102dcb81e47] other arrangements for its management , and[^key-5149d7f0a296ab5c1c7ba1ca51759dbe]
- (d) to secure that the body would meet, on registration, standards set by the regulator under sections 193, 194, 194A and 194C.

(4)[^key-99f5315dc38122b7a5af98d70ba34fdf][^key-0afbb1933f622b9f211a47fbae9d6157] Before setting criteria the regulator must consult—

- (a)[^key-92a7d391ddccce3748ee3d1ef3a9b863] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (za)[^key-6e538fd88fe5858e0595c2d805be80cd] the HCA,
- (aa)[^key-d83001f88abcb9d8ecb0e6f59390fc30] the Greater London Authority,
- (b) one or more bodies appearing to it to represent the interests of private registered providers[^key-027f425c174b45903acd3b291dd519a5],
- (ba)[^key-19df055f917d2f5bc2b7883be5165107] any body for the time being nominated under section 278A,

  and
- (c) one or more other[^key-b5956057078a585974907966c8fe8b45] bodies appearing to it to represent the interests of tenants.

(5)[^key-e49e25024dcc938d397976d193fcbd41] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
