## 266. Removal of officers[^c-0d9253d77077ca8f6baa667dd19f56a6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The regulator may by order remove an officer of a private[^c-f172c2b6f6c67e1f9a5a3d3237e8869e] registered provider if a Case listed in this section applies to the officer.

(2) Case 1 applies to a person who has been made[^c-3910101fe16e7f99a0220e974762961e] bankrupt.

(3) Case 2 applies to a person who has made an arrangement with creditors.

(4) Case 3 applies to a person who is subject to a disqualification order or a disqualification undertaking under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986 (c. 46) or equivalent legislation in Northern Ireland.

(5) Case 4 applies to a person who is subject to an order under section 429(2) of the Insolvency Act 1986 (c. 45) (disabilities on revocation of county court administration order).

(6) Case 5 applies to a person who is disqualified under section 178 of the Charities Act 2011[^c-6870d42590c261ac719a890c4d735807] from being a charity trustee.

(7) Case 6 applies to a person who is incapable of acting by reason of mental disorder.

(8) Case 7 applies to a person who is impeding the proper management of the registered provider by reason of absence or failure to act.

(9)[^c-ddb639bb651eab0d1a7283f4abe4cc57] Case 8 applies to a person who is obstructing the regulator, or failing to co-operate with the regulator, in the performance of the regulator’s functions under this Part.
