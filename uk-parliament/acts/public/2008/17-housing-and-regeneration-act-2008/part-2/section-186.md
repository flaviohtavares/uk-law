> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/17

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 186. Former registered providers[^key-08efe795fe9ef331ccbc4291b52fa481]

*Extent: E+W*

Where a person ceases to be a private registered provider[^key-9516b0e42e4b486f3933d16de9f3671c], sections 171 and 176 (apart from section 176(2))[^key-eef31e05dc0ab065ba961e89c4d5cc8b] continue to apply in respect of any property owned by the person at any time when it was registered.
