# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 93

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. General[^key-0826a720047cb89dabc3c480d5183546][^key-037efec79a087c29d98732ade627fac9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The regulator may do anything it thinks necessary or expedient for the purpose of or in connection with the performance of a function of the regulator[^key-04ccecc322076c692b40d4e6304141a4].

(2) In particular, the regulator may do anything it thinks appropriate for advancing its fundamental objectives.
