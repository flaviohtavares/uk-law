# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 223

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 223. Appeal[^c-827a2208560090487f851aaf00e07f54]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-cd6ffad5e1bbfd5b87bc1c45d05d7ca3] A registered provider who is given an enforcement notice may appeal to the High Court.

(2)[^c-aa2a052ba0edc8477936773cb390bb98] An appeal under this section must be brought within the period of 28 days beginning with the day on which the registered provider is given the enforcement notice.
