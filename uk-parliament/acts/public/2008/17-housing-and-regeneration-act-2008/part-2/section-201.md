## 201. Inspections[^c-c90a01aef752bd60066a4ee12f19d992]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The regulator may arrange for a person to inspect—

- (a) a registered provider's performance of its functions in relation to the provision of social housing, or
- (b) the financial or other affairs of a registered provider.

(2)[^c-ec5d16b803460ef54eef01853a1aad1b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A)[^c-7b983d34d834e328d017b26f21e2a9e2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-cd86f477b8754db2492d62bf72547a47] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The regulator may direct a person carrying out an inspection to discontinue it.

(5) An inspection may be general or specific.

(6)[^c-12cfe32a2bf1aca5b4df6e2bb3f1c990] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^c-9036d598d4a1a9dbccfe8028f9b4d118] Arrangements for a person other than a member of the regulator's staff to carry out an inspection may include provision about payments.
