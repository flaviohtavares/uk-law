# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 181

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 181. Interpretation: “publicly funded”[^c-5730d184446ab2bab24f2598e9084bc8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The provision of a dwelling was publicly funded if any of the following conditions is satisfied.

(2) Condition 1 is that—

- (a) the dwelling was provided by a person in fulfilment of a condition imposed by the HCA when giving assistance to the person, and
- (b) before giving the assistance the HCA notified the person that if it did so the provision of the dwelling would be regarded as publicly funded.

(2A)[^c-8c14ec61eb6cf148820d9d22dfa95d63] Condition 2 is that—

- (a) the dwelling was provided wholly or partly by a person using an amount for purposes for which the amount was required to be used by an HCA direction under section 32(4), and
- (b) before giving the direction the HCA notified the person that any dwelling so provided would be regarded as publicly funded.

(3) Condition 3[^c-c788a427075f68a14728aa35afbd45b3] is that the dwelling was provided wholly or partly by using sums in the disposal proceeds fund of—

- (a) a private registered provider[^c-d8c7fa17796156f7818daa78310ed1a4], or
- (b) a registered social landlord.

(3A)[^c-6eff80a7827a1b55a5284968ebe3dd3a] In relation to a private registered provider, the reference in subsection (3) to its disposal proceeds fund is to its disposal proceeds fund before the abolition of that fund by Part 3 of Schedule 4 to the Housing and Planning Act 2016.

(4) Condition 4[^c-1a2dbea264c5657fe1bb04b98215d6d9] is that —

- (a) the dwelling was acquired by a private registered provider[^c-4d7885c4f5bacb75193c9857f6760f16], or a registered social landlord, on a disposal by a public sector landlord,
- (b) the disposal was made on or after 1st April 1997, and
- (c) at the time of the disposal the dwelling was capable of being let as a separate dwelling.

(5) Condition 4 is not satisfied if the dwelling was acquired in pursuance of a contract made, or option created, before 1st April 1997.

(6) Condition 5[^c-ad32a30b08aea4218bc7a7b305e6aac2] is that—

- (a) the dwelling was provided wholly or partly by means of a grant under section 18 or 27A of the Housing Act 1996 (c. 52), and
- (b) when the grant was made the recipient was notified by the Welsh Ministers[^c-6855ee5675e61e115b7307c6a9a4f16f] that the dwelling was to be regarded as funded by means of such a grant.
