## 76. Leaving the social housing stock: regulator's direction[^key-c02ecbd01cce7c1b3fee023439117718][^key-91ff7fbbf920f9c82e11f15aa8044d2f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The regulator may direct that a specified dwelling is to cease to be social housing.

(2) The regulator may make a direction only on the application of the provider.

(3)[^key-ec77c068a9097d2134c45adbb11818c8] A direction may not be made if the provider is a local authority.
