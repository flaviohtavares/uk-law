# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 118

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 118. De-registration: compulsory[^c-0d0dd5817a281076d8dbbb4ca0a980b9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The regulator may remove from the register a private registered provider[^c-399d49bb9882950970291f9e102ea268] which the regulator thinks—

- (a) is no longer eligible for registration,
- (aa)[^c-e707f0f6bd5f2f6e9359f4b30563ffde] has failed to meet a standard under section 193, 194 or 194C,
- (b) has ceased to carry out activities, or
- (c) has ceased to exist.

(2)[^c-565981a87889208540daa81750df2325] Before acting under subsection (1)(a), (aa) or (b) the regulator must—

- (a) give the private registered provider a notice—
  - (i) warning the provider that the regulator is considering action under the provision concerned, and
  - (ii) specifying a period (which must be at least 14 days beginning with the day the provider receives the notice) within which the provider may make representations, and
- (b) consider any representations made during that period.

(3)[^c-02a0a2dee5d79e51cced07b4e570ec57] The regulator must notify a private registered provider of a decision to remove it from the register under subsection (1)(a), (aa) or (b).

(4)[^c-6670461130a4ce0abed9ea0397ad2520] The regulator shall remove a local authority from the register if the regulator becomes aware that the authority—

- (a) is no longer a provider of social housing, or
- (b) no longer intends to become a provider of social housing (in the case of an authority which intended to become one but did not in fact do so).
