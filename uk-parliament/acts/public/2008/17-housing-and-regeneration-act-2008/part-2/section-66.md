## 66. Interim arrangements[^c-c1ce8f3b787f31cb16d33879087566f4]

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State may by notice require the Housing Corporation to provide staff, premises, facilities or other assistance to—

- (a) the regulator, or
- (b) the HCA.
