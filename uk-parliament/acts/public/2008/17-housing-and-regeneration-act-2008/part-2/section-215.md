## 215. Use of intervention powers

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-a544eedec9ce42f14d926cc271966c6f] The regulator shall publish—

- (a)[^c-81aecb1b40616816c459f50feca7c46b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) guidance about how it uses and intends to use powers under this Chapter and Chapter 7.

(1A)[^c-06bb59a977ccbc6528fbefc382ee2db2] In determining whether the regulator has complied with subsection (1) in relation to its power to arrange for inspections under section 201(1), a plan published under section 201A may be taken into account.

(2)[^c-1a1cefa1ad32940c0726d547ae579f1f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A)[^c-0c56bbf789a88c0b8ab76005aa6f1f34] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-f3b234a5ddb0f9374e917fe5a6c6617f] The regulator shall have regard to guidance under this section.
