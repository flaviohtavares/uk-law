# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 119

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 119. De-registration: voluntary

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-0004ae88cabce573bd08f4bb6a39fce9] A private registered provider[^key-930b8c8abe8b8a711783cb3b4b728cbf] may ask the regulator to remove it from the register.

(2)[^key-2c4b506b73dfd03102c52c5c0b0ef6ac][^key-55143230a1e3f9ae1b39da94d87e948e] The regulator may comply with a request—

- (a) on the grounds that the registered provider no longer is or intends to be a provider of social housing in England,
- (b) on the grounds that the registered provider is subject to regulation by another authority whose control is likely to be sufficient, or
- (c) on the grounds that the registered provider meets any relevant criteria for de-registration set by the regulator.

(3)[^key-0004ae88cabce573bd08f4bb6a39fce9] Before deciding whether or not to comply, the regulator must consult such local authorities in whose area the registered provider acts as it thinks appropriate.

(4)[^key-0004ae88cabce573bd08f4bb6a39fce9] The regulator shall not comply with a request by a non-profit registered provider if it thinks that removal is sought with a view to enabling the registered provider to distribute assets to members.

(5)[^key-0004ae88cabce573bd08f4bb6a39fce9] In deciding whether or not to comply, the regulator must (in particular) have regard to—

- (a)[^key-406fbcfdfb4cc701685fedb6c50ee990] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) any conditions imposed in connection with financial assistance given to the registered provider under any enactment.

(6)[^key-0004ae88cabce573bd08f4bb6a39fce9] Having decided whether or not to remove the registered provider the regulator must notify—

- (a) the provider, and
- (b) any authority consulted.

(7)[^key-2c4b506b73dfd03102c52c5c0b0ef6ac][^key-55143230a1e3f9ae1b39da94d87e948e] The regulator shall publish criteria set for the purposes of subsection (2)(c).
