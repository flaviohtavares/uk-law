## 169C. Companies: change of articles etc[^c-2860e877450d39a905f3f6c5ffa5edd2]

*Extent: E+W*

A [^c-452d8fe6007947aa7c013fd41985f735]... registered provider that is a registered company must notify the regulator of—

- (a) any amendment of the company's articles of association,
- (b) any change to its name or registered office.
