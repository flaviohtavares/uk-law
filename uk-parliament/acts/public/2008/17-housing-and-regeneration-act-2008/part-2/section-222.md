## 222. Notifying HCA etc[^c-f38383a201e35b1010a9922465392457][^c-b3e46886ad0905a5fa74638e7f72132d]

*Extent: E+W*

If the regulator gives an enforcement notice it must send a copy—

- (a)[^c-2a35003b4f9dc8b37aa786c36fb25242] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (za)[^c-44e39e0dd2a37a1e84be9998331ac7be] to the HCA,
- (aa)[^c-f54d4a07b871719f52ff678755765d49] in the case of an enforcement notice given to a registered provider who owns land in Greater London, the Greater London Authority, and
- (b) in the case of an enforcement notice given to a local authority, to the Secretary of State.
