## 180. Right to acquire[^c-cc2f9116cc8eb8d38e9b198f43b0232c][^c-4cabd11e9d1a9afe227c4747ad209503]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The tenant of a dwelling in England has a right to acquire the dwelling if—

- (a) the landlord is a private registered provider[^c-1190b61863b667ad800643f24bed07a8] or a registered social landlord,
- (b) the tenancy is within subsection (2),
- (c) the provision of the dwelling was publicly funded,
- (d) the dwelling has remained in the social rented sector ever since that provision, and
- (e) the tenant satisfies any qualifying conditions applicable under Part V of the Housing Act 1985 (c. 68) (as it applies by virtue of section 184).

(2)[^c-8eaa6eb72831a6546fb4b462dd9118a7] A tenancy is within this subsection if it is—

- (a) an assured tenancy, other than *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8ca762bb4e2965d05100eff4cb9fc879] [^c-964e6843cea881ebcc0bede80e183e7d]... a long tenancy *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8ca762bb4e2965d05100eff4cb9fc879], or
- (b) a secure tenancy.

(2A)[^c-5cdd8992746c673e166d0221cfb728d7] The Secretary of State may by regulations provide that an assured shorthold *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3effc7647bb4961726d0d7f47e011cdf] tenancy of a description specified in the regulations is not a tenancy within subsection (2).

(3) The reference in subsection (1)(a) to a private registered provider[^c-4c10d8e3dac103d2d6510a241a79c573] includes—

- (a) a person who provided the dwelling in fulfilment of a condition imposed by the HCA when giving assistance to the person;
- (b) a person who provided the dwelling wholly or partly by means of a grant under section 27A of the Housing Act 1996 (c. 52).
