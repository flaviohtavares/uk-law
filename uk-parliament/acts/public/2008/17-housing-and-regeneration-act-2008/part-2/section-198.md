# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 198

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 198. Supplemental

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-4f1951fe78efc388f7e995a3f766f303] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-87a53a86a22133e13649d552a95da642] The regulator shall make arrangements for bringing standards to the attention of registered providers.

(3)[^key-87a53a86a22133e13649d552a95da642][^key-df186e9ecfe4e4302fe694b5045b8a6a] The regulator may revise or withdraw standards; and section 196 applies to revising or withdrawing standards as to setting standards.

(4)[^key-87a53a86a22133e13649d552a95da642] Standards may be expressed by reference to documents prepared by others.

(5)[^key-87a53a86a22133e13649d552a95da642] Standards—

- (a) may make provision generally or only in relation to specified cases, circumstances or areas, and
- (b) may make different provision for different cases, circumstances or areas.

(6)[^key-118ee7955bbb426e49bdc9064dea06fe] In setting standards the regulator must have regard to the desirability of registered providers being free to choose how to provide services and conduct business.
