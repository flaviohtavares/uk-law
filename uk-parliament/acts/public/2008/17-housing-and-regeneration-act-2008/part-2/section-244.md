# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 244

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 244. Enforcement

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-8774187ad103bf790f29b789e58740b0] Compensation shall be treated as a debt owed to the person to whom it is awarded.

(2)[^c-588507d703b92e221d4f82f7369dd5cc][^c-08227bc8607f98217a6f2c0c61a4d8be] The Treasury may make regulations authorising the regulator—

- (a) to award interest on compensation not paid during the period specified under section 240(2)(e);
- (b) to award additional compensation where compensation is not paid during that period.

(3)[^c-8774187ad103bf790f29b789e58740b0] Interest and additional compensation shall be treated as compensation.

(4)[^c-588507d703b92e221d4f82f7369dd5cc][^c-08227bc8607f98217a6f2c0c61a4d8be] Regulations under subsection (2)(a) may provide for an interest rate to be—

- (a) set by a specified person, or
- (b) determined in accordance with the regulations.
