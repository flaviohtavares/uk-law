# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 96

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/17

## 96. Evidence[^c-b3b354a72162c3a590b42215eeb001cd][^c-77e6e72fc6771565e8d311b1b7d997d0]

*Extent: E+W*

In considering whether to exercise a power under this Part the regulator may have regard to information or opinions from any source including, in particular, from—

- (a) tenants,
- (b) bodies representing tenants,
- (c) local housing authorities, or
- (d) the Commission for Local Administration in England or[^c-acfb5970d997924198841a0dbb0d5f1b] a housing ombudsman[^c-4967d79cdec5f2d8dbc171c93125f7b4].
