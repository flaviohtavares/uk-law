# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 216

## 216. Consultation[^c-6088e527c214238a666f109f92b1bbfb]

*Extent: E+W*

Before giving guidance under section 215 the regulator must consult—

- (za)[^c-1b0134f744402035b5fb7d8733bbb9e0] the Secretary of State,
- (a) one or more bodies appearing to it to represent the interests of registered providers,
- (aa)[^c-5b202324348ee4432fb6d9b2bf0d53b6] any body for the time being nominated under section 278A,
- (b) one or more other[^c-741ca787f0d8d12b7a90bb0549c8f303] bodies appearing to it to represent the interests of tenants of social housing[^c-0069dbc38d0714e336ebec51b3a955c5],
- (ba)[^c-1c16e0557e51bd8a544ce84ba3f27643] the Greater London Authority,
- (c) one or more bodies appearing to it to represent the interests of local housing authorities,
- (d)[^c-71240ee256bfcf3aba1fec7602cb28d0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^c-1ec74e54adc1f4a51462189e299c2b06] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f)[^c-4e8ac5c317828bcb78e66730015f321a] the HCA.
