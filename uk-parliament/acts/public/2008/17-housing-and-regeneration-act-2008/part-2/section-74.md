# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 74

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 74. Leaving the social housing stock: ending of lease[^c-1d180d642f31cfa1745764528d785fd2][^c-7a52041f40fde4e3b2fd1f543675622b][^c-e8f97ef712cb0d71b176a87f1e51934b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A dwelling ceases to be social housing if—

- (a) the provider holds a leasehold interest in the dwelling, and
- (b) the leasehold interest determines (whether by effluxion of time or in any other way)[^c-5fb871023bc296115260ee333081cd15].

(2)[^c-1f5d8af3ffa88a16bd40a88dfcfa31b1] But subsection (1) does not apply if, immediately before the leasehold interest determines, the lessor was—

- (a) an associate or subsidiary of the provider, or
- (b) a registered provider.
