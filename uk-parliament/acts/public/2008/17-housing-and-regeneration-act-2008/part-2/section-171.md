# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 171

## 171. Power to dispose[^c-b36b6cdb6e1be282619f2ab3ec05513d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A private registered provider[^c-9a1efba3ebdf8e8e9e468b86f71e9de6] may dispose of land.

(2) But a non-profit registered provider may dispose of the landlord's interest under a secure tenancy only to another non-profit registered provider or to a local authority which is a registered provider[^c-b36c347f6ba60e2ea4d4e9ee03beaf92].

(3) Subsection (1) is subject to the following provisions of this Chapter [^c-a4b460c5080721b897ee61b41d2e46b9]....
