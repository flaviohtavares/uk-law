# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 129

## 129. Companies or limited liability partnerships exempt from audit[^c-bc6941eb460feb191ec6b37086ba930e][^c-8cf0ab3e77e973ff976af64b5f2bd46b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies in relation to a registered provider which—

- (a) is a registered company other than a charity or is a limited liability partnership[^c-e3efbbd72c3e895603bd66201e1bf200], and
- (b) is exempt from the audit requirements of the Companies Act 2006 (c. 46) by virtue of section 477 of that Act (small companies' exemption).

(2) The directors of the company or members of the limited liability partnership[^c-809f2784acc97653462c88ed0f6679a9] shall cause a report to be prepared in accordance with section 130 and made to the registered provider’s[^c-a5b5e1abe4f6b56bd4f306d7adf7dc83] members in respect of the registered provider’s individual accounts for any year in which the registered provider[^c-565c07ee1e5d51d21cf69f5f9d2b0782] takes advantage of its exemption from audit.

(3) “**Individual accounts**” means accounts prepared in accordance with[^c-00cef21c2dcdd1328315da1b593af569] section 396 of the Companies Act 2006.
