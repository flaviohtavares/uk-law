# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 120

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 120. Notice[^c-26d74164c9042ce2d31799d60dcbeab7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) As soon as is reasonably practicable after registering or de-registering a body the regulator shall notify—

- (a) in the case of a registered charity, the Charity Commission,
- (b) in the case of a registered society[^c-a85a2bd2407dae5b1dab913643127dde], the Financial Conduct Authority[^c-417de34906570567493371c60fea0636], [^c-34757758e3ba3644d511eea27e56dca2]...
- (c) in the case of a registered company (whether or not also a registered charity) or a limited liability partnership[^c-4f2c90af4a0b51a92e1d4eede02ba02b], the registrar of companies for England and Wales , and[^c-67b3948adefc22b11c1261c1a9af61f5]
- (d) in the case of a local authority, the Secretary of State.

(2) Except in the case of a local authority,[^c-450c05a3d9de0141d9b175d948d87d12] a notice of registration shall specify whether the person registered is designated as a non-profit or profit-making organisation.

(3) If the designation changes, the regulator shall notify any person notified of the registration.

(4) A person to whom notice is given under this section must keep a record of it.
