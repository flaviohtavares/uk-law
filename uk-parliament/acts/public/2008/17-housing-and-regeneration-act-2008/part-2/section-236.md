## 236. Overview[^c-d2d378b81c8b30022fa2bbaf27f29f6d]

*Extent: E+W*

This group of sections allows the regulator to award compensation to a victim of a failure on the part of a private registered provider[^c-9e447f3b867cbd9f7555a91278206ef8].
