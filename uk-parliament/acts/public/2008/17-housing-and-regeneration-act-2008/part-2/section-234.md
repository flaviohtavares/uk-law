# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 234

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 234. Enforcement

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-fde5b3fddf41695e7aa477a552b27b2b] A penalty shall be treated as a debt owed to the regulator.

(2)[^c-26d19a069513b785efd0c1297139bdfc][^c-f8ecd5a30acb16b53439073d6cef9b8a] The Treasury may make regulations authorising the regulator—

- (a) to charge interest on penalty not paid during the period specified under section 228(2)(d);
- (b) to impose one or more additional penalties where a penalty is not paid during that period.

(3)[^c-fde5b3fddf41695e7aa477a552b27b2b] Interest and additional penalty shall be treated as penalty (and may have the effect of increasing the penalty above a limit set by section 229).

(4)[^c-26d19a069513b785efd0c1297139bdfc][^c-f8ecd5a30acb16b53439073d6cef9b8a] Regulations under subsection (2)(a) may provide for an interest rate to be—

- (a) set by a specified person, or
- (b) determined in accordance with the regulations.

(5)[^c-fde5b3fddf41695e7aa477a552b27b2b] A penalty notice may include provision allowing a discount if the penalty is paid on or before a date specified in the notice (falling within the period specified under section 228(2)(d)).

(6)[^c-fde5b3fddf41695e7aa477a552b27b2b] A person to whom a penalty notice is given on the ground in Case 6 of section 227 may not be prosecuted for the offence by reference to which the penalty notice was given.
