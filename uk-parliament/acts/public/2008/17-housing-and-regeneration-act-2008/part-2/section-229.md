## 229. Amount[^key-4f8425c35ad880b2b4cbfec4348d9316]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The amount of a penalty imposed on the ground specified in Case 6 of section 227 may not exceed the maximum amount (if any)[^M_F_87863064-9b83-4f68-c7b9-bd01adbf726b] of fine that a magistrates' court could impose for the relevant offence.
