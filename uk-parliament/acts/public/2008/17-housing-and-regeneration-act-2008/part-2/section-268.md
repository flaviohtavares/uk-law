# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 268

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/17

## 268. Appeal against removal or suspension[^key-26b98b6cad54d5cb36b880e70400bcac]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-99b5e6f7fa3e00dca8b88090820f518d] A person removed or suspended under section 259, 260 or 266 may appeal to the High Court.

(2)[^key-88344a29f4219b9f5832c9e2d556353a] An appeal under this section must be brought within the period of 28 days beginning with the day on which the registered provider concerned is notified of the removal or suspension.
