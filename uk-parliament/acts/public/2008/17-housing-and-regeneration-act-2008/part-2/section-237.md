## 237. Grounds for award[^c-4e818369b1f1d06b5d7e9a290f1a7869]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The regulator may require a private registered provider[^c-c10988bcc3119224dee035fa7dcc21fe] to pay compensation if the regulator is satisfied that—

- (a) any[^c-ca098fa6686346750e0ec600a3187f5e] of the following cases applies, and
- (b) the award of compensation is appropriate (whether or not as part of a response including other action).

(2) Case 1 is where the registered provider has failed to meet a standard under section 193, 194 or[^c-b6421a346ffb2e83a7084cb006b68e98] 194C.

(3) Case 2 is where the registered provider has given an undertaking under section 125 and failed to comply with it.

(4)[^c-28e7540e1682cc5534235d2c19ca1577] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c-ddf8bc7356f4524fa7d5ea82354e9a1b] Case 4 is where the registered provider has failed to comply with a performance improvement plan notice.

(6) Case 5 is where the registered provider has failed to comply with the duty in section 218B(4) (duty to provide copy of performance improvement plan).
