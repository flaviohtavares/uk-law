# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 264

## 264. Acting while disqualified: offence[^key-4bf8dc0fbd4a8a510cdfef5b01fa7266]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) It is an offence for a person to act as an officer of a registered provider in respect of which the person is disqualified under section 262.

(2) A person guilty of an offence is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-a02b0b908807cdf12fd7be14ca0aeec1] or a fine [^key-4b94349dd6f01dbaa93084c8a6369dd9]... or both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or a fine or both.

(3) Proceedings for an offence may be brought only by or with the consent of—

- (a) the regulator, or
- (b) the Director of Public Prosecutions.

(4) In relation to an offence committed before 2 May 2022[^key-112468bb9f8f60811f8357ab6ae8f7c9] the reference in subsection (2)(a) to the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-8634c675fe955cb7bb8ab2dcae9d87bc] shall have effect as if it were a reference to 6 months.
