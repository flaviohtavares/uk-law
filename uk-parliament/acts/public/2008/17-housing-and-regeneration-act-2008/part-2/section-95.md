# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 95

## 95. Financial assistance

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-7787de2b0cab49525682ecb0d61b6b84][^c-72da0a8ccba09fadc4d41c535535672d] The regulator may, where it thinks it advances a fundamental objective, give financial assistance to a person in connection with—

- (a) undertaking research,
- (b) preparing guidance,
- (c) developing and publicising best practice, or
- (d) facilitating the management of social housing by tenants.

(2)[^c-7787de2b0cab49525682ecb0d61b6b84][^c-72da0a8ccba09fadc4d41c535535672d] Financial assistance under subsection (1) may be given—

- (a) by way of grant,
- (b) by way of loan,
- (c) by defraying expenditure on behalf of a person, or
- (d) in any other way except purchasing loan or share capital of a body corporate or giving a guarantee or indemnity.

(3)[^c-b5d95bfc5426597cc2950a00fc6e5d49] The regulator may, where it thinks it advances a fundamental objective, give financial assistance to a private registered provider[^c-fe6640ee2b9f763aa801491466254fc1] by—

- (a) lending money to or in respect of the registered provider, or
- (b) giving a guarantee or indemnity in respect of the registered provider.

(4)[^c-b5d95bfc5426597cc2950a00fc6e5d49] Financial assistance may be given under subsection (3) only with the consent of the Secretary of State (given with the approval of the Treasury).

(5)[^c-7787de2b0cab49525682ecb0d61b6b84][^c-72da0a8ccba09fadc4d41c535535672d] Financial assistance under subsection (1) or (3) may be given on conditions (which may include provision for repayment, with or without interest).
