## 195. Code of practice[^c-e6b28563374eff88d5f5d1fbd5480668]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The regulator may issue a code of practice which—

- (a) relates to a matter addressed by a standard [^c-4c8673fabc9b9d1f6b53d9b71e479765]..., and
- (b) amplifies the standard.

(2) In considering whether standards [^c-b338d725818a0a9b2e0cdf7ecd23e5f9]... have been met the regulator may have regard to a code of practice.

(3) The regulator may revise or withdraw a code of practice.

(4) The regulator shall make arrangements for bringing a code of practice to the attention of registered providers.
