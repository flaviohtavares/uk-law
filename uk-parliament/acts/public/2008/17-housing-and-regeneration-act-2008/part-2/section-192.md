## 192. Overview[^c-ed49a558b7174fd4a669998ac88fddd7]

*Extent: E+W*

This Chapter—

- (a) allows the regulator to set standards for the provision of social housing (sections 193 to 198[^c-e181a8460f6f92361b0a5ab99d183782]),
- (b) gives the regulator powers to monitor compliance (sections 199 to to 210A[^c-17173794aca78976ad10c54ff53bd090]),
- (c)[^c-e4c778fa9e0ae10e0cde10891532b054] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) requires the regulator to give guidance [^c-79956df8c984b8f71ff8cede4abac055]... about the use of its powers under this Chapter and Chapter 7 (sections 215 and 216), [^c-cb2342909a8e6fda778e31dbd03d6f45]...
- (e) allows the regulator to arrange for the accreditation of managers of social housing (section 217) , and[^c-0b7bf9e65422504bc171e8ae31e4d563]
- (f) makes provision about terms to be implied into management services agreements (section 217A).
