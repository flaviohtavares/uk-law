(1) The HCA or a strategic authority outside London[^c-0fea520dd7ce8f85b2421ac79410c67d] may regenerate or develop land.

(2) The HCA or a strategic authority outside London[^c-7fa4614266adc25d0a95514844d983fb] may bring about the more effective use of land.

(3) The HCA or a strategic authority outside London[^c-cd2f9c36717be9efbb74098ad9c50a6e] may facilitate—

- (a) the regeneration or development of land, or
- (b) the more effective use of land.
