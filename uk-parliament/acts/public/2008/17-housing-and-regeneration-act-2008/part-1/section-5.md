[^c-b473cad868dfc5e1b004231e22893571][^c-96396265e555cef2018631befff89943][^c-c4e8158af0841c8a243df83312f08196][^c-4b7e64ac437c7d6ba3ab98113c4de111][^c-2a91ca5c7b3af245caf592d9d7b10948]

(1) The HCA or a strategic authority outside London[^c-9471d302a718eb30c19a439c9a395ecb] may provide housing or other land.

(2) The HCA or a strategic authority outside London[^c-c6ed5e61ffbc1b6d2ecbf20ef8f7b0be] may facilitate the provision of housing or other land.

(3) In this section “**provide**” includes provide by way of acquisition, construction, conversion, improvement or repair (and “provision” is to be read in the same way).
