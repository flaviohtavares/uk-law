## 43. Acting with, or for, other persons: general[^c-7599d56b00f8dbb13f0b27c5cc036d25]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The HCA may act with other persons (whether in partnership or otherwise).

(2) The HCA may act as agent for other persons.
