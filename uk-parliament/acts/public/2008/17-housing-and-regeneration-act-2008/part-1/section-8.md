[^key-b473cad868dfc5e1b004231e22893571][^key-96396265e555cef2018631befff89943][^key-c4e8158af0841c8a243df83312f08196][^key-4b7e64ac437c7d6ba3ab98113c4de111][^key-2a91ca5c7b3af245caf592d9d7b10948]

The HCA or a strategic authority outside London[^key-3d942129b7b246215a01aadbf55584fc] may carry out, or facilitate the carrying out of, any of the following activities in relation to land—

- (a) acquiring, holding, improving, managing, reclaiming, repairing or disposing of housing,
- (b) acquiring, holding, improving, managing, reclaiming, repairing or disposing of other land, plant, machinery, equipment or other property, and
- (c) carrying out building and other operations (including converting or demolishing buildings).
