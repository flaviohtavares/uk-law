# Housing and Regeneration Act 2008, section 50

## 50. Abolition of the Commission for the New Towns

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-8a738208499af0889625aa51548742a2][^c-c6fee477bf5738c6425255a02a66f690] The Commission for the New Towns shall cease to exist on such day as the Secretary of State may by order appoint.

(2)[^c-eb19ae203e694d3608a1ac52739bbabb][^c-0e737b51e3bcc40b4d173d9adc18fa75] Schedule 5 (which transfers Welsh functions of the Commission to the Welsh Ministers and makes other amendments of the New Towns Act 1981 (c. 64)) has effect.
