# Education and Skills Act 2008, section 87

## 87. Benefit and training information[^c-f182d7913b89c83bbc88cab22a91908e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Information within subsection (2) or (3) may be—

- (a) used in connection with the exercise of an assessment function of the Secretary of State or a devolved authority, or
- (b) disclosed to a person for use in connection with the exercise of an assessment function of the Secretary of State or a devolved authority.

(2) Information is within this subsection if—

- (a) it is about an individual [^c-10404809200954da681dac12ff6f4483]..., and
- (b) it is held by the Secretary of State for the purposes of any function of the Secretary of State relating to social security.

(3) Information is within this subsection if—

- (a) it is about an individual [^c-bca5e711b155a2b1294137abfa71353f]...,
- (b) it is held by the Secretary of State or a devolved authority, and
- (c) it relates to any training or course of education undertaken by the individual [^c-e21ad243bb1d988b7e3b0ba14453a295]....

(4) For the purposes of this section and sections 88 to 90, “**assessment function**” means any of the following functions—

- (a) evaluating the effectiveness of training or education [^c-825827d2b82f37421e24289afbbbbfe1]...;
- (b) assessing policy in relation to the provision of [^c-e6dd2e59fd7178c370991a788a1fb095]... training or education;
- (c) assessing policy in relation to social security or employment as it affects the provision of or participation in [^c-2a8c5f313f1ae6ed099c282b81d846d9]... training or education.
