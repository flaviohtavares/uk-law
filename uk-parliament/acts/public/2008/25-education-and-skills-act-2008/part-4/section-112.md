# Education and Skills Act 2008, section 112

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 112. Failure to pay fees

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-84e07d87834df466642baafcdde9728e] Where the proprietor of an institution fails to pay a fee in accordance with regulations under section 111, the Secretary of State may remove the institution from the register.

(2)[^c-84e07d87834df466642baafcdde9728e] The Secretary of State must notify the proprietor of an institution of any decision to remove it from the register under this section.

(3)[^c-5378388d6f96d39bd536368892ef6f16] The Secretary of State's decision does not have effect during the period in which—

- (a) an appeal may be brought under section 124 against the decision, or
- (b) where such an appeal has been brought, the appeal has not been determined, withdrawn or otherwise disposed of.
