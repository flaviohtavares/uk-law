# Education and Skills Act 2008, section 140

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (56).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 140. The register and fees[^key-d1b7a380b5c9dd2d5d10c3596d43effe]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-c92b23af6b3110487a1c546032442b5d] On the coming into force of section 95, the register of independent schools in England becomes the register of independent educational institutions in England.

(2)[^key-cbb68bf43d03dd27c4d88afb01a5b9b4] The power in section 111(3)(d) to make provision prescribing circumstances in which the amount of a fee may be waived includes power to make such provision in relation to a fee (or any part of a fee) payable by virtue of section 162B(6) of the Education Act 2002 (fees payable to Chief Inspector).
