# Education and Skills Act 2008, section 117

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 117. “Relevant restriction”

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-38b8520351109be5957f71944a9e2e67] In this Chapter a “relevant restriction” imposed on the proprietor of a registered independent educational institution is a requirement that the proprietor take one or more of the following steps by a specified time or by specified times—

- (a) to cease to use any part of the institution's premises for all purposes or specified purposes;
- (b) to close any part of the institution's operation;
- (c) to cease to admit any new students or new students of specified descriptions.

(2) In subsection (1) “**specified**” means—

- (a)[^c-fe46f53ddccea7349e20a0f639ef3259] in the case of a relevant restriction imposed by the Secretary of State under section 116(1)(a), specified by the Secretary of State;
- (b)[^c-fe46f53ddccea7349e20a0f639ef3259] in the case of a relevant restriction imposed by an order of a justice of the peace under section 120, specified in the order;
- (c)[^c-38b8520351109be5957f71944a9e2e67] in the case of a relevant restriction imposed by an order of the Tribunal under section 124, 125 or 126, specified in the order.
