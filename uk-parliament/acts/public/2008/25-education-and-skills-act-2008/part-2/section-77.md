# Education and Skills Act 2008, section 77

## 77. Supply of information by public bodies[^c-303b71690d4dc0975cd8f52c0188a654]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any of the persons or bodies mentioned in subsection (2) may supply information about a young person or relevant young adult to any person or body involved in the provision of services in pursuance of section 68 or 70(1)(b) for the purpose of the provision of those services.

(2) Those persons and bodies are—

- (a) a local authority,
- (aa)[^c-7d87088de1b5b161460d5f93d8ff6ef5] a non-metropolitan district council for an area for which there is a county council,
- (b)[^c-3444a00ac0e94fe2bac4c504b5909eb3] the Secretary of State[^c-855d24157c9cb86cc8288409c3d2c7cf],
- (ba)[^c-2f2759346b624eb34826a85e185f8b10] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-62fd97f1c1414aa7c4baeb9179e46eca] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c-561cdc08abdf3521c7af0a0cd552fc40] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (da)[^c-391044e3b68b9e93cfff4113aeab02ff] an integrated care board,
- (e) a chief officer of police,
- (f) a provider of probation services,
- (g) a local probation board, and
- (h) a youth offending team.

(3) In this section—

- [^c-ed25d7ff02fb62ab04e5ebdf8f4e5235]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**local probation board**” means a local probation board established under section 4 of the Criminal Justice and Court Services Act 2000 (c. 43);
- “**youth offending team**” means a team established under section 39 of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998 (c. 37).
