# Education and Skills Act 2008, section 72

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72. Educational institutions: duty to provide information[^key-6f10fe91f3dbcf1785b97854a69ac799]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Relevant information about a pupil or student who is attending an educational institution in England must be provided by the responsible person to a person involved in the provision of services in pursuance of section 68 or 70(1)(b), on a request by that person.

(2) A request under subsection (1) by a person involved in the provision of services in pursuance of 68 or 70(1)(b) may be made only for the purposes of the provision of those services.

(3) In subsection (1), “**relevant information**” means—

- (a) the name, address and date of birth of the pupil or student;
- (b) the name and address of a parent of the pupil or student;
- (c) information in the institution's possession about the pupil or student.

(4) Information within subsection (3)(c) must not be provided under subsection (1) if—

- (a) the pupil or student concerned, in the case of a pupil or student who has attained the age of 16, or
- (b) a parent of the pupil or student concerned, in the case of a pupil or student who has not attained the age of 16,

has instructed the responsible person not to provide information of that kind under this section.

(5) In this section—

- “**educational institution**” means—
  - (a) a community, foundation or voluntary school,
  - (b) a community or foundation special school,
  - (c) a city technology college, a city college for the technology of the arts or an Academy,
  - (d) a pupil referral unit,
  - (e) an institution within the further education sector, or
  - (f) an institution (other than one within any of paragraphs (a) to (e)) in receipt of funding from a local authority,[^key-09bc66631d4c1334e3ddf78ba56ec6cf] or the Secretary of State[^key-55f467422b2d3dfbd0b8ba1ad7898dbf];
- “**responsible person**” means—
  - (a) in relation to a school within paragraph (a) or (b) of the definition of “educational institution”, the governing body;
  - (b) in relation to an institution within paragraph (c) or (f) of that definition, the proprietor;
  - (c) in relation to a pupil referral unit, the local authority[^key-a3ed5f043e1b57c6ebf3ecae0a50cdd6] by which it is maintained;
  - (d) in relation to an institution within the further education sector, the governing body within the meaning given by section 90 of the Further and Higher Education Act 1992 (c. 13).
