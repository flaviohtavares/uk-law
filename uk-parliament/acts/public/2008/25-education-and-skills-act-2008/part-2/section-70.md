# Education and Skills Act 2008, section 70

>

## 70. local authorities: supplementary powers[^key-cc8ab37fc4908f769f5cbccfed89b8c2][^key-5d2ca0f2dc38f5a0cd1a91f1111b1287][^key-ed1a0956b91d488387e2f03d0973e676][^key-5ba49064a3b2d1702726e009db60daf1][^key-2bd5223411172ccbf01f82634dc42c60][^key-93b12afc0ec05fb020cc3fbc787bbb7e][^key-a3ed5f043e1b57c6ebf3ecae0a50cdd6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority in England—

- (a) may provide, secure the provision of or participate in the provision of services under arrangements entered into by it with another local authority in pursuance of any function of that other local authority under section 68, and
- (b) may provide, secure the provision of or participate in the provision of services for encouraging, enabling or assisting the effective participation in education or training of—
  - (i) young persons, or
  - (ii) relevant young adults,

  (including such persons from other areas) otherwise than in accordance with section 68 or paragraph (a).

(2) Nothing in or done under section 68 [^key-a396015fc42ce3a3fb5dfdfa06b25ebd]... or this section is to be taken to prejudice any powers which a local authority has with respect to the exercise of its functions otherwise than under those sections.
