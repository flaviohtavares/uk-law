# Education and Skills Act 2008, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (56).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 6. Relevant training or education[^c-42fcb4740e64437617ab54b7f2c9e7af][^c-b3583109d3cecfc841d501275986a6b0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part, “**relevant training or education**” means training or education towards a regulated qualification[^c-1b2ea3e2428341b6a81a375751c5b98d] provided by a course or courses.

(2)[^c-4c7f7b852817aa079ca1f66fcb7c9b2e] “Regulated qualification” has the same meaning as in Part 7 of the Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009 (see section 130 of that Act).
