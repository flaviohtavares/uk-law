## 18. Guidance[^key-bf1d73ef59a6bc024768c4ce2cd2354f]

*Extent: E+W*

In exercising its functions under this Part, a local authority[^key-a3ed5f043e1b57c6ebf3ecae0a50cdd6] must have regard to any guidance given by the Secretary of State.
