# Education and Skills Act 2008, section 43

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Parenting orders: appeals

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) An appeal lies to the Crown Court against the making of a parenting order under section 41.

(2) Subsections (2) and (3) of section 10 of the Crime and Disorder Act 1998 (appeals against parenting orders) apply in relation to an appeal under this section as they apply in relation to an appeal under subsection (1)(b) of that section.
