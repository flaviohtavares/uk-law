# Health and Social Care Act 2008, section 35

## 35. Contravention of regulations[^c1942176][^c-1135b50d3aa9994ec6d0e02d2b30b4c6]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations under this Chapter may provide that a contravention of or failure to comply with any specified provision of the regulations is to be an offence, but may not provide for an offence to be triable on indictment or to be punishable with imprisonment or, except in the case of regulations under section 20, with a fine exceeding level 4 on the standard scale.[^c-8979184e880808dc45d79da977dd878c]
