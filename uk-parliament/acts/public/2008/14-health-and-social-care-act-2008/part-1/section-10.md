# Health and Social Care Act 2008, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Requirement to register as a service provider[^c1942170][^key-1e28ec148890d74561a7d1afd4597ac8][^key-34d30c48fe83376a82b39dfc49e02f81]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-6a8cfbff5febaf82b3fa754eac61e990][^key-ddfee63f6d444fd162aa40df1edb65b5][^key-aa6aa3f051e5468917760d0f0a7a6ce6][^key-d4f829531dbc5d1184c524e6574238ea][^key-b9648f6e8aa96d3e8dc24615c25a4431] Any person who carries on a regulated activity without being registered under this Chapter in respect of the carrying on of that activity is guilty of an offence.

(2)[^key-6a8cfbff5febaf82b3fa754eac61e990] The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision for the purposes of this Chapter for determining, in relation to a regulated activity carried on by two or more persons acting in different capacities, which of those persons is to be regarded as the person who carries on the activity.

(3)[^key-84418c4cb2086ea6909a3f62f6cf1984] In the following provisions of this Part, the registration of a person under this Chapter in respect of the carrying on of a regulated activity by that person is referred to as registration “as a service provider” in respect of that activity.

(4)[^key-6a8cfbff5febaf82b3fa754eac61e990] A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine[^key-4af5ab197ee31139d69ac601710168a6], or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-12ea4a35bcf35086176d7ec1b6ef0739], or to both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine, or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months, or to both.

(5)[^key-6a8cfbff5febaf82b3fa754eac61e990] In relation to an offence committed before 2 May 2022[^key-1d3798a6f22b721c105f7bf5ba3831a1], the reference in subsection (4)(a) to the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-de05501baef396a255f5e45bccfec20a] is to be read as a reference to 6 months.
