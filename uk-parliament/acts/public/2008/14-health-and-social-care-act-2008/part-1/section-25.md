# Health and Social Care Act 2008, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Effect of code under s. 21 or 21A and guidance under s. 23[^c-2c791c3b5c31544f0b4e3fa5a5973290][^c-6781eff8cd539373083ddfadfd746256][^c-8768e24e2d51c652eaae3adb5ccb76e6]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Codes of practice under sections 21 and 21A[^c-ad34a9be8a0fa5c231a846b2806dfdc4] and guidance under section 23 are to be taken into account—

- (a) in the making of any decision by the Commission under this Chapter;
- (b) in any proceedings for the making of an order under section 30;
- (c) in any proceedings on an appeal against such a decision or order;
- (d) in any proceedings for an offence under section 33 or under regulations under section 20.

(2) Codes of practice under sections 21 and 21A and[^c-df91663dbcf503323a0e23816ab93ca0] guidance under section 23 are[^c-c990e2e18e21abddc3ff7856c4b0bf22] also admissible in evidence in other criminal or civil proceedings.

(3) A failure to observe any provision of a code of practice under section 21 or 21A[^c-288d9fd5a4c7024ea34ff756c98b2b2f] or guidance under section 23 does not of itself make a person liable to any criminal or civil proceedings.
