# Health and Social Care Act 2008, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Applications for registration as a manager[^key-b52d526483043a1c72297396fae43692][^key-1e28ec148890d74561a7d1afd4597ac8][^key-34d30c48fe83376a82b39dfc49e02f81][^key-1e28ec148890d74561a7d1afd4597ac8][^key-34d30c48fe83376a82b39dfc49e02f81]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-f18d8b40d58a45dcc504a7e5242b7ed9] A person seeking to be registered under this Chapter as a manager in respect of a regulated activity in respect of which a registered manager condition has, or is to have, effect must make an application to the Commission.

(2)[^key-378ed8f248b08add72bd31b809e7ba8f] The application must be made in such form, and contain or be accompanied by such information, as the Commission requires.

(3)[^key-d2e350c7751706723b2962c9ea2ddace] In such cases as the Commission may determine, a person seeking to be registered as a manager in respect of two or more regulated activities carried on by a person registered as a service provider may make a single application in respect of them.
