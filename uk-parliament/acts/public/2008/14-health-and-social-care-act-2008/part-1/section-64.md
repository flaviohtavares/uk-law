## 64. Power to require documents and information etc.[^key-4f581b0433a062ac35452d248c22e3b9][^key-aa1b08da46e2810a502a5156ab61dc94][^key-1e28ec148890d74561a7d1afd4597ac8][^key-34d30c48fe83376a82b39dfc49e02f81][^key-b16da6eaed635ff12ade35cc76428fb6][^key-b42e4cf68648cf17d05a79d28725124a][^key-1e28ec148890d74561a7d1afd4597ac8][^key-34d30c48fe83376a82b39dfc49e02f81]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Commission may require any person mentioned in subsection (2) to provide it with any information, documents, records (including personal and medical records) or other items which the Commission considers it necessary or expedient to have for the purposes of any of its regulatory functions.

(2) The persons are—

- (a) an English NHS body,
- (b) a person providing health care commissioned by —[^key-7b0cbfe1f2500b9a9c4bae4d0e65b106]
- (i) NHS England[^key-1f3f7a6f5cec0810995f802f57d1bbcb],
- (ii)[^key-3011b92f639553514691a30aaba30248] an integrated care board, or
- (iii) [^key-4161c6e1bb5b272a1b292a848756d607]...
- (c) an English local authority,
- (d) a person providing adult social services commissioned by an English local authority, [^key-247f1dad2c1f90157b7f07cee5dc8d2b] ...
- (e) a person who carries on or manages a regulated activity, [^M_F_3a8606c7-30ec-4a60-c48f-5eab3bed2ba9]...
- (f)[^M_F_3a8606c7-30ec-4a60-c48f-5eab3bed2ba9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The power in subsection (1) to require the provision of information, documents or records includes, in relation to information, documents or records kept by means of a computer, power to require the provision of the information, documents or records in legible form.

(4) A person who without reasonable excuse fails to comply with a requirement imposed under this section is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale.
