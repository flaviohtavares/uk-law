# Health and Social Care Act 2008, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Applications for registration as a service provider[^key-1e4cb6faf417b9490f4fb7d801069d44][^key-656439f98124e4bc7282e8677e0ff58e][^key-1e28ec148890d74561a7d1afd4597ac8][^key-34d30c48fe83376a82b39dfc49e02f81]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person seeking to be registered under this Chapter as a service provider must make an application to the Commission.

(2) The application must be made in such form, and contain or be accompanied by such information, as the Commission requires.

(3)[^key-6237acd2a7cdc19c56571d14d94de994][^key-fdf698708ccaa3ec0013c6f4472dd6ce] In such cases as the Commission may determine, a person seeking to be registered as a service provider in respect of two or more regulated activities may make a single application in respect of them.
