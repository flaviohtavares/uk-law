# Health and Social Care Act 2008, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Interpretation of Chapter 2[^key-bc6db6d010268844a35387e98aa9d652][^key-49a663ac0c216480367499519dcd9355][^key-1e28ec148890d74561a7d1afd4597ac8][^key-34d30c48fe83376a82b39dfc49e02f81][^key-1e28ec148890d74561a7d1afd4597ac8][^key-34d30c48fe83376a82b39dfc49e02f81]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Chapter—

- “**health care associated infection**” is to be read in accordance with section 20(6) and (7);
- “**registered manager condition**” is to be read in accordance with section 13(3).
