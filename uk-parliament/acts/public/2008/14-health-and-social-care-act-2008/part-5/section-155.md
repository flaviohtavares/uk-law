> Extent: England, Wales.

## 155. Power to form company[^c-763a6c221472a2ec3613fc07e0513e3c]

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State may form, or participate in forming, one or more companies with a view to making arrangements under section 154(1) or (1A)[^c-84a9dbfa091ee159a8afeb0d73cb41a6] with the companies for financial assistance under section 149 to be given, or other functions relating to such assistance to be exercised, by the company.
