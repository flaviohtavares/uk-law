# Health and Social Care Act 2008, section 154

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2023-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2008/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 154. Arrangements with other third parties[^c-a4ca3c4946a1b58ed8dd11eb2400af09]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may make arrangements for—

- (a) financial assistance under section 149 (1)[^c-7c54cc700f3531c6303b00f55ff0624d] to be given, or
- (b) other functions relating to such assistance to be exercised,

by a person other than a body mentioned in section 153(1) or an English local authority.

(1A)[^c-6acd76dbe7f780ee40ce760b0300d70a] The Secretary of State may make arrangements for—

- (a) financial assistance under section 149(1A) to be given, or
- (b) other functions relating to such assistance to be exercised,

by a person other than a Special Health Authority (as to Special Health Authorities, see section 153(2)).

(2) A person with whom the Secretary of State makes arrangements under subsection (1) or (1A)[^c-9b321df622ad1d31f1f280476663ca16] is referred to in this section as P.

(3) Arrangements under subsection (1) or (1A)[^c-44433c948003ca3f7224df08b3186d7a] may provide for the functions concerned to be exercised by P—

- (a) either wholly or to such extent as may be specified in the arrangements, and
- (b) either generally or in such cases or circumstances as may be so specified.

(4) Arrangements under subsection (1) or (1A)[^c-dfe9b3446deded89ecad3102f48b5a96] may make provision—

- (a) subject to section 151(3), as to the forms of financial assistance which may be given by P, and
- (b) as to the terms on which financial assistance may be given by P.

(5) Arrangements under subsection (1) or (1A)[^c-fbb1c06b5985769feec8f6c7d85f25b1] may—

- (a) provide for the Secretary of State to make payments to P, and
- (b) make provision as to the circumstances in which any such payments are to be repaid to the Secretary of State.

(6) In subsection (1) “**English local authority**” includes a non-metropolitan district council for an area for which there is a county council.
