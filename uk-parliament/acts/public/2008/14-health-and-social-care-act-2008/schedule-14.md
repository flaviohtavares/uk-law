### Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 (c. 47)

**Not yet in force.**

**8.** In section 6 of the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006 (regulated activity providers), after subsection (8) insert—

> (8A) An authority that is a local authority for the purposes of section 17A of the Children Act 1989 , section 57 of the Health and Social Care Act 2001 or sections 31 to 33 of the Care Act 2014[^key-7ab055017370bb6ff7268c216ced0231] (direct payments) does not make arrangements for another to engage in a regulated activity by virtue of anything the authority does under any of those sections[^key-3f4161f8e0f2aea8bae80d960ea303eb].
>
> (8B) A person (S) who is someone's surrogate within the meaning of section 57 of the Health and Social Care Act 2001 does not make arrangements for another to engage in a regulated activity by virtue of anything that S does under subsection (1B)(b) or (1C)(b) of that section.
>
> (8C)[^key-18b6c1ce1eaedd344ed92f104b036c62] A person (S) who is authorised as mentioned in subsection (4)(a) of section 32 of the Care Act 2014 does not make arrangements for another to engage in a regulated activity by virtue of anything that S does under subsection (4)(b) of that section.
