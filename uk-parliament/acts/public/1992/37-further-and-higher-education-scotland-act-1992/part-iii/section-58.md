# Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992, section 58

> Text as at 2026-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Stamp duty.[^c1309728]

*Extent: S*

Stamp duty shall not be chargeable in respect of any agreement made or any transfer effected under or by virtue of any of the provisions of this Act.
