# Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992, section 61

> Text as at 2026-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Interpretation.[^c1309735]

*Extent: S*

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “**the 1980 Act**” means the [^c1309736]Education (Scotland) Act 1980
- “[^key-5bc2ff369ac27ff63bbb4a91f2723563]**the Council**" means the Scottish Further and Higher Education Funding Council; and
- “ **prescribed** ” means prescribed by order or by regulations made by the Secretary of State.
