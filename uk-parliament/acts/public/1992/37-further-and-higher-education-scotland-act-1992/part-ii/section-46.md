# Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992, section 46

> Text as at 2026-02-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 46. Establishment of new institutions by the Secretary of State.[^c1309713]

*Extent: S*

The Secretary of State may by order establish institutions for the provision of any form of higher education.
