# Transport and Works Act 1992, section 69

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 69. Expenses.[^c8596841]

*Extent: E+W+S*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenses incurred by the Secretary of State under this Act, and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable out of money so provided under any other enactment.
