# Transport and Works Act 1992, section 68

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 68. Repeals.[^c8596811]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The enactments mentioned in Schedule 4 to this Act (which include spent enactments) are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.

(2) The repeal by this Act of the [^c8596821]Notice of Accidents Act 1894 shall not affect section 75 of the [^c8596831]Civil Aviation Act 1982 (by virtue of which regulations may include provisions applying section 3 of the 1894 Act).
