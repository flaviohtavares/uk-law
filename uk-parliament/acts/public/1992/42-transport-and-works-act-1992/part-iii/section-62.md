## 62. Exclusion of hackney carriage legislation.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In section 4 of the [^c8596521]Metropolitan Public Carriage Act 1869 (interpretation) in the definition of “hackney carriage”, for the words “not a stage carriage” there shall be substituted the words “ neither a stage carriage nor a tramcar ”.

(2)[^c-d6ff988e305017649e96de416de5ac84] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In section 80 of the [^c8596541]Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1976 (interpretation) in subsection (1) in the definition of “private hire vehicle”, after the words “London cab” there shall be inserted the words “ or tramcar ”.
