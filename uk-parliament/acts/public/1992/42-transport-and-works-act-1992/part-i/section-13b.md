# Transport and Works Act 1992, section 13B

## 13B (Extent: E+W). Application or proposal for an (Extent: E+W) EIA (Extent: E+W) order: reasoned conclusion (Extent: E+W)[^c-5e8539210b81156305f5db290646ab7a][^c-6fe8eaba423f786f7a8af6d3aece3b38]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W)) Before making a determination under section 13(1) in respect of an application or proposal for an *(Extent: E+W)* EIA *(Extent: E+W)* order, the Secretary of State— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) must consider the *(Extent: E+W)* EIA *(Extent: E+W)* information, and *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) following that consideration, must reach a reasoned conclusion about the likely significant effects of the proposed works or other projects on the environment. *(Extent: E+W)*

(2 (Extent: E+W)) The Secretary of State must obtain such expert advice as appears to the Secretary of State to be necessary for the purposes of considering the environmental statement. *(Extent: E+W)*

(3 (Extent: E+W)) When making a determination under section 13(1) in respect of an application or proposal for an *(Extent: E+W)* EIA *(Extent: E+W)* order, the Secretary of State must take into account the reasoned conclusion. *(Extent: E+W)*

(4 (Extent: E+W)) But the Secretary of State may determine to make an *(Extent: E+W)* EIA *(Extent: E+W)* order (whether with or without modifications) only if satisfied that the reasoned conclusion is up to date. *(Extent: E+W)*
