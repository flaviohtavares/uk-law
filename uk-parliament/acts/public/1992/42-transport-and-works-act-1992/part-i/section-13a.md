## 13A (Extent: E+W). Environmental impact assessment: definitions (Extent: E+W)[^c-5e8539210b81156305f5db290646ab7a][^c-6fe8eaba423f786f7a8af6d3aece3b38]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W)) This section defines certain terms used in this Part. *(Extent: E+W)*

(2 (Extent: E+W)) “The *(Extent: E+W)* EIA *(Extent: E+W)* Directive” means *(Extent: E+W)* [Directive 2011/92/EU *(Extent: E+W)*](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2011/0092) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 December 2011 on the assessment of the effects of certain public and private projects on the environment. *(Extent: E+W)*

(3 (Extent: E+W)) “ *(Extent: E+W)*EIA *(Extent: E+W)* information”, in relation to an application under section 6, or proposal under section 7, for an *(Extent: E+W)* EIA *(Extent: E+W)* order, means— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) the environmental statement, *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) any other information which the Secretary of State reasonably requires for the purpose of reaching a reasoned conclusion (see section 13B), and *(Extent: E+W)*
- (c (Extent: E+W)) any representations made by any person about the environmental effects of the proposed works or other projects. *(Extent: E+W)*

(4 (Extent: E+W)) “ *(Extent: E+W)*EIA *(Extent: E+W)* order” means an order authorising works or other projects— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) which are— *(Extent: E+W)*
  - (i (Extent: E+W)) in a class listed in Annex I to the *(Extent: E+W)* EIA *(Extent: E+W)* Directive, or *(Extent: E+W)*
  - (ii (Extent: E+W)) in a class listed in Annex *(Extent: E+W)* II *(Extent: E+W)* to the *(Extent: E+W)* EIA *(Extent: E+W)* Directive and, by virtue of their nature, size or location, likely to have significant effects on the environment, and *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) which are not exempt works. *(Extent: E+W)*

(5 (Extent: E+W)) For the purposes of subsection (4), works or other projects are exempt if the Secretary of State directs or decides (in accordance with rules made under section 6) that an environmental impact assessment is not required in respect of those works or projects. *(Extent: E+W)*

(6 (Extent: E+W)) “Environmental statement” means a statement which, by rules under section 6, is required— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) to accompany an application for, or be prepared in connection with the publication of a proposal to make, an *(Extent: E+W)* EIA *(Extent: E+W)* order, and *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) amongst other matters, to set out the likely significant effects of the implementation of the *(Extent: E+W)* EIA *(Extent: E+W)* order applied for or proposed on the environment. *(Extent: E+W)*

(7 (Extent: E+W)) “Reasoned conclusion” means a reasoned conclusion under section 13B(1)(b). *(Extent: E+W)*
