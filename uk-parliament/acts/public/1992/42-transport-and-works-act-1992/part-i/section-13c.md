# Transport and Works Act 1992, section 13C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 13C (Extent: E+W). EIA (Extent: E+W) orders: monitoring measures and remedial action (Extent: E+W)[^key-5e8539210b81156305f5db290646ab7a][^key-6fe8eaba423f786f7a8af6d3aece3b38]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W)) If the Secretary of State proposes to make an *(Extent: E+W)* EIA *(Extent: E+W)* order (whether with or without modifications), the Secretary of State must consider whether monitoring of the significant adverse effects of the works or other projects on the environment to be authorised by the order is appropriate and, if so, must consider— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) whether it is appropriate to impose a monitoring measure, and *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) whether it is appropriate to impose a requirement to take remedial action. *(Extent: E+W)*

(2 (Extent: E+W)) In this section, “monitoring measure”, in relation to proposed works or other projects, means a requirement to monitor any significant adverse effects of the works or projects on the environment. *(Extent: E+W)*

(3 (Extent: E+W)) For the purposes of subsection (1)(a), the Secretary of State must take into account any monitoring arrangements which are required to be carried out under the law of any part of the United Kingdom (other than a provision *(Extent: E+W)* which implemented[^key-0e9133967460e10595c303577c824700] *(Extent: E+W)* the *(Extent: E+W)* EIA *(Extent: E+W)* Directive). *(Extent: E+W)*

(4 (Extent: E+W)) The Secretary of State may impose a monitoring measure or a requirement to take remedial action in the *(Extent: E+W)* EIA *(Extent: E+W)* order or, if the Secretary of State gives a direction under section 90(2A) of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 on the making of the *(Extent: E+W)* EIA *(Extent: E+W)* order, by way of condition specified in that direction. *(Extent: E+W)*

(5 (Extent: E+W)) But the Secretary of State may impose a monitoring measure only if satisfied that the type of parameters which will be required to be monitored and the duration of such monitoring are proportionate having regard to— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) the nature, location and size of the proposed works or other projects, and *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) the significance of the effects of the works or other projects on the environment. *(Extent: E+W)*
