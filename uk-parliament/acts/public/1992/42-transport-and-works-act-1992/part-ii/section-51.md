# Transport and Works Act 1992, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 51. Amendment of Level Crossings Act 1983.[^c8596391]

*Extent: E+W+S*

—In section 1 of the [^c8596401]Level Crossings Act 1983 (safety arrangements at level crossings) in subsection (11), for the definition of “operator” there shall be substituted—

> “**operator**”, in relation to a crossing, means any person carrying on an undertaking which includes maintaining the permanent way;
