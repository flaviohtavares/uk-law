# Transport and Works Act 1992, SCHEDULE 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Matters within sections 1 and 3[^c8596861]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 5.

**1.**[^c8596871] The construction, alteration, repair, maintenance, demolition and removal of railways, tramways, trolley vehicle systems and other transport systems within section 1(1) of this Act, waterways, roads, watercourses, buildings and other structures.

**2.**[^c8596881] The carrying out of any other civil engineering or other works.

**3.**[^c8596891] The acquisition of land, whether compulsorily or by agreement.

**4.**[^c8596901] The creation and extinguishment of rights over land (including rights of navigation over water), whether compulsorily or by agreement.

**5.**[^c8596911] The abrogation and modification of agreements relating to land.

**6.**[^c8596921] The conferring on persons providing transport services of rights to use systems belonging to others.

**7.**[^c8596931] The protection of the property or interests of any person.

**8.**[^c8596941] The imposition and exclusion of obligations or of liability in respect of any acts or omissions.

**9.**[^c8596951] The making of agreements to secure the provision of police services.

**10.**[^c8596961] The carrying out of surveys and the taking of soil samples.

**11.**[^c8596971] The payment of compensation.

**12.**[^c8596981] The charging of tolls, fares (including penalty fares) and other charges, and the creation of summary offences in connection with non-payment (or in connection with a person’s failure to give his name or address in accordance with provisions relating to penalty fares).

**13.**[^c8596991] The making of byelaws by any person and their enforcement, including the creation of summary offences.

**14.**[^c8597001] The payment of rates.

**15.**[^c8597011] The transfer, leasing, discontinuance and revival of undertakings.

**16.**[^c8597021] The submission of disputes to arbitration.

**17.**[^c8597031] The imposition of requirements to obtain the consent of the Secretary of State.
