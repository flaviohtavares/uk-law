## 135. Awards by social fund officers.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c16395041][^c-ffd0bf530a56989c54023473ecce3aae] Whether a payment mentioned in section 134(1)(b) above is to be awarded, and how much it is to be, shall be determined by an appropriate officer, that is to say, an officer of the Department who, acting under its authority, is exercising functions of the Department in relation to payments so mentioned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffd0bf530a56989c54023473ecce3aae]

(2) An appropriate officer[^c16395051] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffd0bf530a56989c54023473ecce3aae] may determine that an award shall be payable in specified instalments at specified times. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffd0bf530a56989c54023473ecce3aae]

(3)[^c16395061] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) An award *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffd0bf530a56989c54023473ecce3aae] of a crisis loan or a budgeting loan[^c16395071] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffd0bf530a56989c54023473ecce3aae] shall be repayable upon such terms and conditions as before the award is paid the Department notifies to the person by or on behalf of whom the application for it was made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffd0bf530a56989c54023473ecce3aae]

(5) Payment of an award shall be made to the applicant unless *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffd0bf530a56989c54023473ecce3aae] the appropriate officer[^c16395081] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffd0bf530a56989c54023473ecce3aae] determines otherwise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ffd0bf530a56989c54023473ecce3aae]
