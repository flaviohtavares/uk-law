# Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 134

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (205).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/7
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 134. Payments out of the social fund.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c16395001] There may be made out of the social fund, in accordance with this Part of this Act—

- (a) payments of prescribed amounts, whether in respect of prescribed items or otherwise, to meet, in prescribed circumstances, maternity expenses and funeral expenses; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e8590a8c9a5f104fac792b086ca000d5]
- (b) payments by way of community care grant, crisis loan or budgeting loan *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-91d5243e39002a4ba0cd21f0e9b94a4c] to meet—[^c-84167bf91f408195e11809a10d95f175] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-91d5243e39002a4ba0cd21f0e9b94a4c]
  - (i) other needs, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-91d5243e39002a4ba0cd21f0e9b94a4c]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-91d5243e39002a4ba0cd21f0e9b94a4c] in the case of payments by way of budgeting loan, those needs for which provision is made by paragraph (a), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-91d5243e39002a4ba0cd21f0e9b94a4c]

  in accordance with directions given or guidance issued by the Department. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-91d5243e39002a4ba0cd21f0e9b94a4c]

(2) Payments may also be made out of that fund, in accordance with this Part of this Act, of a prescribed amount or a number of prescribed amounts to prescribed descriptions of persons, in prescribed circumstances to meet expenses for heating which appear to the Department to have been or to be likely to be incurred in cold weather.

(3) The power to make a payment out of the social fund such as is mentioned in subsection (1)(b) above may be exercised by making a payment to a third party with a view to the third party providing, or arranging for the provision of, goods or services for the applicant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a718dda10badcb74e6864598c7756cd0]

(4) In this section “prescribed” means specified in or determined in accordance with regulations.

**No longer in force (ended 2016-11-28).**

(5)[^c16395011] In this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a718dda10badcb74e6864598c7756cd0]

- “budgeting loan” means a loan awarded in circumstances specified in directions issued by the Department for the purpose of defraying an intermittent expense; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a718dda10badcb74e6864598c7756cd0]
- “community care grant” means a grant awarded in circumstances so specified for the purpose of meeting a need for community care; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a718dda10badcb74e6864598c7756cd0]
- “crisis loan” means a loan awarded in circumstances so specified for the purpose of meeting an immediate short term need, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a718dda10badcb74e6864598c7756cd0]

and any reference in this subsection to meeting a need or defraying an expense includes a reference to helping to meet the need or to defray the expense. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a718dda10badcb74e6864598c7756cd0]
