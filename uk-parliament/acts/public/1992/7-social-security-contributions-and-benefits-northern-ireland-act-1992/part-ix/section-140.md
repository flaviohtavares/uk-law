## 140. Exclusions and priority.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-37d45e07f4722cb43230788a146902c0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Schedule 9 to this Act shall have effect for excluding entitlement to child benefit [^key-26effe0ba1e256167293cf27da990706]...

(3) Where, apart from this subsection, two or more persons would be entitled to child benefit in respect of the same child or qualifying young person[^key-9f254deb1488f1887035ec03cfb61f14] for the same week, one of them only shall be entitled; and the question which of them is entitled shall be determined in accordance with Schedule 10 to this Act.
