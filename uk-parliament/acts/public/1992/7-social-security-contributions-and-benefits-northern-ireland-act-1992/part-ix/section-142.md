# Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 142

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (205).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/7
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 142. Presence in Northern Ireland.[^c-6b61d20ffaf9c4cfcd37f7b95fab456f]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) No child benefit shall be payable in respect of a child or qualifying young person[^c-4047244455168b4e8c161dca77cec67f] for a week unless he is in Northern Ireland in that week.

(2) No person shall be entitled to child benefit for a week unless he is in Northern Ireland in that week.

(3) Circumstances may be prescribed in which any[^c-72474b2ec5aded0ce73f88bfe939789b] person is to be treated for the purposes of subsection (1) or (2) above[^c-ff0ebbda6f4cec1bcdb4712d476371ae] as being, or as not being, in Northern Ireland.
