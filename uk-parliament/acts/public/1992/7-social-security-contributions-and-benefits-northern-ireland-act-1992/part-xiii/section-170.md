# Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 170

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (205).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 170. Interpretation.

*Extent: N.I.*

In this Act—

- “**the 1975 Act**” means the [^c16397371]Social Security (Northern Ireland) Act 1975;
- “**the 1986 Order**” means the [^c16397381]Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1986;
- “**the Administration Act**” means the [^c16397391]Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992;
- “**the Consequential Provisions Act**” means the [^c16397401]Social Security (Consequential Provisions) (Northern Ireland) Act 1992;
- “**the Department**” means (except in Parts XIIZA[^c19105371] , 12ZB[^key-b82fcae52ea17246c9ab39567bd85ede] , 12ZC and 12ZD[^key-93a6727b02c06bf4a4c8b78b59d9d283] above) the Department of Health and Social Services for Northern Ireland;
- “**the Department of Economic Development**” means the Department of Economic Development in Northern Ireland;
- “**the Department of the Environment**” means the Department of the Environment for Northern Ireland;
- “**the Department of Finance and Personnel**” means the Department of Finance and Personnel in Northern Ireland;
- the Employment Rights Order” means the Employment Rights (Northern Ireland) Order 1996;[^key-63a6550cd3afd53a10c7132de0febf95]
- “**the Great Britain Administration Act**” means the [^c16397411]Social Security Administration Act 1992;
- “**the Great Britain Contributions and Benefits Act**” means the [^c16397421]Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992;
- “**the National Insurance Fund**” means the Northern Ireland National Insurance Fund;
- “**the Old Cases Act**” means the [^c16397431]Industrial Injuries and Diseases (Northern Ireland Old Cases) Act 1975;
- “[^c16397441]**the Pensions Act**” means the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993;
- “**the Pensions Order**” means the [^c16397451]Social Security Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1975;
- “**statutory provision**” has the meaning assigned by section 1(f) of the [^c16397461]Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954.
