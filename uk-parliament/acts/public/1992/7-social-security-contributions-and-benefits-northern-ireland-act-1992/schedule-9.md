## SCHEDULE 9 — Exclusions from Entitlement to Child Benefit

*Extent: N.I.*

Section 140(2).

### Children and qualifying young persons in detention, care, etc.[^key-af36dcc0f604a61e9fdf65aed450896c]

**1.** (1) Except where regulations otherwise provide, no person shall be entitled to child benefit in respect of a child or qualifying young person[^key-e3c9619b916c7ee5539114cf87eb46c8] for any week if in that week the child or qualifying young person—

- (a) is undergoing imprisonment or detention in a young offenders centre;
- (b) is authorised under the Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^c16407091] to be detained in a juvenile justice centre, and is not out under supervision[^c16407101];
- (c) is liable to be detained by virtue of Article 45 of the Criminal Justice (Children) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^c16407111] and is not discharged on licence;
- (d) is the subject of an order under that Order of 1998[^c16407121] committing him to custody in any place to which he may be committed on remand;
- (e)[^c16407131] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f) is—
  - (i) in the care of the Department; or
  - (ii) the subject of a parental rights order under section 104 of that Act of 1968,

  in such circumstances as may be prescribed.

(2) In sub-paragraph (1) above the reference to a child or qualifying young person[^key-46368b052c5eedb018ffe960d7d35fbf] in the care of the Department includes a reference to a child or qualifying young person in the care of [^key-a9abbd34b195ed62806dbe51690d4a6b]... a Health and Social Care trust.[^key-12ea2183ca8db5d04908cd9964a42db5]

### ...[^key-aa3fbb0ac21b5a626d4b90144dc1471b]

**2.**[^key-e8d3b3c6cbe49e779f0a4b59f574f80f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

### Married children and qualifying young persons[^key-79cf186f99a236455f8e2121419195b4]

**3.** Except where regulations otherwise provide, no person shall be entitled to child benefit in respect of a child or qualifying young person[^key-d15521a57ffd8c90a6dbf9c9abcb90e0] who is married or is a civil partner[^key-1d14f68eb48148c6c8dacd802befe5bc].
