# Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 91

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (205).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Effect of trade disputes on entitlement to increases.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A beneficiary shall not be entitled—

- (a) to an increase in any benefit under or by virtue of sections 82 to 88 above[^c16389791]; or
- (b) to an increase in benefit [^key-166c86d1c1afe793153a639b3d570ebe]... by virtue of regulations under section 90 above,

if the person in respect of whom he would be entitled to the increase falls within subsection (2) below.

(2)[^c16389801] A person falls within the subsection if—

- (a) he is prevented for being entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance by Article 16 of the Jobseekers (Northern Ireland) Order 1995 (trade disputes); or
- (b) he would be so prevented if he were otherwise entitled to that benefit.
