# Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 88

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (205).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/7

## 88. Increases to be in respect of only one adult dependant.[^c16389711]

*Extent: N.I.*

A person shall not by virtue of section 86A[^key-04598eef6cfb82ea693690053fa27bf8] above be entitled for the same period to an increase of benefit in respect of more than one person.
