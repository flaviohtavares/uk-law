# Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 87

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (205).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/7

## 87. Rate of increase where associated retirement pension is attributable to reduced contributions.[^c16389661]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Where a person—

- (a)[^c16389671] is entitled to short-term incapacity benefit under section 30A(2)(b) above; and
- (b) would have been entitled only by virtue of section 60(1) above to the retirement pension by reference to which the rate of that benefit [^c16389681]. . . is determined,

the amount of any increase of the benefit attributable to sections 82 to 86A above shall be determined in accordance with regulations under this section.[^c16389691]

(2) The regulations shall not provide for any such increase in a case where the retirement pension by reference to which the rate of the said benefit [^c16389681]. . . is determined—

- (a) would have been payable only by virtue of section 60 above; and
- (b) would, in consequence of a failure to satisfy a contribution condition, have contained no basic pension.
