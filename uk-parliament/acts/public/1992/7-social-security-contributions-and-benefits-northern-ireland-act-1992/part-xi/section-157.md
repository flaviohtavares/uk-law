# Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 157

## 157. Crown employment - Part XI.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to subsection (2) below, the provisions of this Part of this Act apply in relation to persons employed by or under the Crown as they apply in relation to persons employed otherwise than by or under the Crown.

(2) The provisions of this Part of this Act do not apply in relation to persons serving as members of Her Majesty’s forces, in their capacity as such.

(3) For the purposes of this section Her Majesty’s forces shall be taken to consist of such establishments and organisations as may be prescribed by regulations made by the Secretary of State with the concurrence of the Treasury[^c16396451], being establishments and organisations in which persons serve under the control of the Defence Council.
