# Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 18

## 18. Class 4 contributions recoverable under regulations.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Inland Revenue may by regulations make provision[^c16381721] so that where—

- (a) an earner, in respect of any one or more employments of his, is treated by regulations under section 2(2)(b) above as being self-employed; and
- (b) in any tax year he has earnings from any such employment (one or more) which fall within paragraph (b)(i) of subsection (8)[^c-8a91566463bcf50a14d725b93e93e4a5] of section 11 above but is not liable for a higher weekly rate of Class 2 contributions by virtue of regulations under that subsection; and
- (c) the total of those earnings exceeds £12,570[^c-3816246bbebdafe94b76952f4fbd9585],

he is to be liable, in respect of those earnings, to pay a Class 4 contribution [^c19101711]... .

(1A)[^c19153971] The amount of a Class 4 contribution payable by virtue of regulations under this section is equal to the aggregate of—

- (a) the main Class 4 percentage of so much of the total of the earnings referred to in subsection (1)(b) above as exceeds £12,570[^c-cc84fb3968b6c81994e47ad5dbfd2aa7] but does not exceed £50,270[^c-7bca964dfb8442b0fb90565e8de975d1]; and
- (b) the additional Class 4 percentage of so much of that total as exceeds £50,270;

but the figures specified in this subsection are subject to alteration under section 129 of the Administration Act.

(2) In relation to Class 4 contributions payable by virtue of regulations under this section[^c16381781], regulations made by the Inland Revenue may—

- (a) apply any of the provisions of Schedule 1 to this Act (except a provision conferring power to make regulations); and
- (b) make any such provision as may be made by regulations under that Schedule, except paragraph 6 or 7BZA[^c-c21b03c237e0c5cf008dbf72188b4eae].
