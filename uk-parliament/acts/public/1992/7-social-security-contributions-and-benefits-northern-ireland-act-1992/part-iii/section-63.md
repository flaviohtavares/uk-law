# Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 63

## 63. Descriptions of non-contributory benefits.

*Extent: N.I.*

Non-contributory benefits under this Part of this Act are of the following descriptions, namely—

- (a) attendance allowance;
- (b)[^c16387881] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) carer’s allowance[^c19118281] [^key-737a90d9e7c27270d2d98a082cd65512]... ;
- (d) disability living allowance;
- (e) guardian’s allowance;
- (f) retirement pensions of the following categories—
  - (i) Category C, payable in certain cases to a widow whose husband was over pensionable age on 5 July 1948 or to a woman whose marriage to a husband who was over pensionable age on that date was terminated otherwise than by his death[^key-f397a975d9310f2dc1a8987614de0c3d] (with increase for adult [^key-88b5f72c94c7f640a62c4541cd25b9f6]... dependants), and
  - (ii) Category D, payable to persons over the age of 80;
- (g) age addition payable, in the case of persons over the age of 80, by way of increase of a retirement pension of any category under this Act[^key-7749ec89e91f75fc4ed8840153d76f4d] or of a pension or allowance to which section 79(2) below applies.
