# Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 122

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (205).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 122. Income-related benefits.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Prescribed schemes shall provide for the following benefits (in this Act referred to as “**income-related benefits**”)—

- (a) income support;
- (b)[^c-c8efaca0fae395e92ed9bbada118d4eb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-c8efaca0fae395e92ed9bbada118d4eb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) housing benefit.

(2)[^c16392381] The Department shall make copies of schemes prescribed under subsection (1)(a), (b) or (c) above available for public inspection at social security offices of the Department at all reasonable hours without payment.

(3)[^c16392391] The Department of the Environment and the Executive—

- (a) shall take such steps as appear to them appropriate for the purpose of securing that persons who may be entitled to housing benefit become aware that they may be entitled to it; and
- (b) shall make copies of the housing benefit scheme available for public inspection at their offices at all reasonable hours without payment.
