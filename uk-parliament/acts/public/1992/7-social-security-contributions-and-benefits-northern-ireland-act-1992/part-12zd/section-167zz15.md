## 167ZZ15. Crown employment[^key-625aea7685965b53f692f6806889f367][^key-603f634d6b71939bbf7898b28d6e6428][^key-42e0682e3e71e6783b3e83a114db9dd7][^key-bac34cb91b2df0c5d2f2cf7ac582873d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provisions of this Part apply in relation to persons employed by or under the Crown as they apply in relation to persons employed otherwise than by or under the Crown.
