# Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 120

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (205).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/7
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120. Treatment of certain marriages.[^c16390701]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Regulations made by the Treasury with the concurrence of the Department[^c16390711] may provide—

- (a) for a voidable marriage which has been annulled, whether before or after the date when the regulations come into force, to be treated for the purposes of the provisions to which this subsection applies as if it had been a valid marriage which was terminated by divorce at the date of annulment;
- (aa)[^c-f28af7621c36a1a390e158fff5cd4d0c] for a voidable civil partnership which has been annulled, whether before or after the date when the regulations come into force, to be treated for the purposes of the provisions to which this subsection applies as if it had been a valid civil partnership which was dissolved at the date of annulment;
- (b) as to the circumstances in which, for the purposes of the enactments to which this section applies, a marriage during the subsistence of which a party to it is at any time married to more than one person is to be treated as having, or as not having, the same consequences as any other marriage.[^c16390721]

(2) Subsection (1) above applies—

- (a) to any enactment contained in Parts I to V or this Part of this Act; and
- (b) to regulations under any such enactment.
