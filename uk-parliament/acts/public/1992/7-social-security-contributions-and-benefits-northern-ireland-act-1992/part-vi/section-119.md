# Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 119

## 119. Persons outside Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

The Treasury may with the concurrence of the Department[^c16390631] make regulations modifying Parts I to V of this Act and Part III of the Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions, etc.) (Northern Ireland) Order 1999[^c16390641], and any provisions of Chapter II of Part II of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 which correspond to[^c16390651] provisions of Part III of the 1975 Act, in such manner as the Treasury think[^c16390661] proper, in their application to persons who are or have been outside Northern Ireland at any prescribed time or in any prescribed circumstances.
