# Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 118

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (205).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/7

## 118. Married women and widows.

*Extent: N.I.*

The Treasury may with the concurrence of the Department[^c16390571] make regulations modifying any of the following provisions of this Act, namely—

- (a) Part I;
- (b) Part II (except section 60); and
- (c) Parts III and IV,

in such manner as the Treasury think[^c16390581] proper, in their application to women who are or have been married.
