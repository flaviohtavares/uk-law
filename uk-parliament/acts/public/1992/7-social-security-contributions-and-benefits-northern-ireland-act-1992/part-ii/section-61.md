# Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 61

## 61. Exclusion of increase of benefit for failure to satisfy contribution condition.[^c16387671]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A Category A or Category B retirement pension which is payable by virtue of section 60(1) above and a widowed mother’s allowance or widowed parent’s allowance[^c16387681] which is so payable shall not be increased under section 47(1) above or under Part IV below [^c-8f68a1a1a96736bd5b79a67d57995db7]... if the pension or allowance contains no basic pension in consequence of a failure to satisfy a contribution condition.

(2)[^c16387691] Where a person is entitled to short-term incapacity benefit at a rate determined under section 30B(3) above and the retirement pension by reference to which the rate of the benefit is determined—

- (a) would have been payable only by virtue of section 60 above, and
- (b) would, in consequence of a failure to satisfy a contribution condition, have contained no basic pension,

the benefit shall not be increased under section 47(1) above or under Part IV below[^c-8f68a1a1a96736bd5b79a67d57995db7]... .
