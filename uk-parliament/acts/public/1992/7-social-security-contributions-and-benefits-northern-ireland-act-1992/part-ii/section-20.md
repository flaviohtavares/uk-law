## 20. Descriptions of contributory benefits.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Contributory benefits under this Part of this Act are of the following descriptions, namely—

- (a)[^c16382271] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c16382281] incapacity benefit, comprising—
  - (i) short-term incapacity benefit; and
  - (ii) long-term incapacity benefit;
- (d) maternity allowance [^key-017792d9fe41ce9031a5c6cfbc83f8f7]... ;
- (e) widow’s benefit, comprising—
  - (i)[^c16382291] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii) widowed mother’s allowance[^key-58fb35c7d2599c4d8c8c09e001f30f3f]...;
  - (iii) widow’s pension;
- (ea)[^key-add739828478044bc52a5912a9c466e1] widowed parent's allowance;
- (f) retirement pensions of the following categories—
  - (i) Category A, payable to a person by virtue of his own contributions (with increase for adult [^key-b6bfad474778e1d13f24ca929e0efdc4]... dependants); and
  - (ii)[^c16382311] Category B, payable to a person by virtue of the contributions of a spouse or civil partner[^key-052682cf7fef23819c67e4d70f4a7ebe] [^key-58fb35c7d2599c4d8c8c09e001f30f3f]... ;
- (fa)[^c16382321] shared additional pensions;
- (g) for existing beneficiaries only, child’s special allowance.

(2) In this Act—

- “**long-term benefit**” means—
  - (a) long-term incapacity benefit;[^c16382331]
  - (b) a widowed mother’s allowance;
  - (ba) a widowed parent’s allowance;[^c16382341]
  - (bb) [^key-7d2e18f1fcebe24032ff7d0f2444cbf5]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (c) a widow’s pension; and
  - (d) a Category A or Category B retirement pension; and
  - (e) a shared additional pension;[^c16382351]

- “**short-term benefit**” means—
  - (a) [^c16382361]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (b) short-term incapacity benefit; and[^c16382371]
  - (c) maternity allowance.

(3) The provisions of this Part of this Act are subject to the provisions of Chapter II of Part III of the Pensions Act[^c16382381] (reduction in benefits for members of schemes that were contracted-out)[^key-c05bac84926f3926483371c44bbb1407].
