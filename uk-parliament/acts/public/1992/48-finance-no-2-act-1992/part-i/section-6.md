# Finance (No. 2) Act 1992, section 6

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Abolition of duties on matches and mechanical lighters.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The [^c13516731]Matches and Mechanical Lighters Duties Act 1979 shall cease to have effect.

(2) This section shall come into force on 1st January 1993.
