> Text as at 2026-04-01.

## 19. Lower rate: further provisions.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 7(4) of the [^c13517501]Taxes Management Act 1970 for “basic rate" there shall be substituted “ the basic rate or the lower rate ”.

(2) In each of the provisions to which this subsection applies, after “basic rate" there shall be inserted “ or the lower rate ”; and this subsection applies to section 91(3)(c) of the Taxes Management Act 1970 and to sections [^c-0fa8767b32cfe2cd8c269cf370fd9d51]... 599A(7) of the Taxes Act 1988.
