# Finance (No. 2) Act 1992, section 25

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Companies ceasing to be members of groups.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c13517631] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Subject to the repeals made by the [^c13517641]Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992, in relation to a company which ceases to be a member of a group of companies on or after 15th November 1991 section 278 of the [^c13517651]Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1970 (deemed sale etc. where company ceases to be member of a group) shall have effect, and be deemed to have had effect, with the substitution in subsection (1) of the words “ in consequence of another member of the group ceasing to exist ” for the words from “by being wound up" to the end of the subsection.
