> Text as at 2026-04-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Exchange of securities.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c13517761] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Subject to the repeals made by the Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992, in relation to exchanges made on or after 1st January 1992 section 85 of the [^c13517771]Capital Gains Tax Act 1979 (exchange of securities for those in another company) shall have effect, and be deemed to have had effect, with the insertion after subsection (1)(b) of

> or
>
> - (c) company A holds, or in consequence of the exchange will hold, the greater part of the voting power in company B

.
