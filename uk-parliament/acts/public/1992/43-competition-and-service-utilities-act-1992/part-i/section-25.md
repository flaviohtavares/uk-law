# Competition and Service (Utilities) Act 1992, section 25

> Text as at 2010-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Determination of disputes by Director: interim directions.[^c859790]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The power of the Director General of Electricity Supply, under section 23(2) of the [^c859791]Electricity Act 1989, to require a public electricity supplier to continue a supply of electricity pending the Director’s determination of a dispute shall be extended to enable the Director to require the giving of a supply of electricity pending such a determination.

(2) Accordingly, in section 23(2) of that Act, the words “to continue” shall be omitted, in both places where they occur.
