# Competition and Service (Utilities) Act 1992, section 24

> Text as at 2010-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Compliance with standards of overall performance.[^c859788]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 40 of the [^c859789]Electricity Act 1989 (electricity supply: overall performance), the following subsection shall be added at the end—

> (3) It shall be the duty of every public electricity supplier to conduct his business in such a way as can reasonably be expected to lead to his achieving the standards set under this section.
