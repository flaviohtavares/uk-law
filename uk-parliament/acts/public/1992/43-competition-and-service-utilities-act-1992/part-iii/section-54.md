# Competition and Service (Utilities) Act 1992, section 54

> Text as at 2010-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Corresponding provision for Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An Order in Council under paragraph 1(1)(b) of Schedule 1 to the [^c859841]Northern Ireland Act 1974 (legislation for Northern Ireland in the interim period) which contains a statement that it is made only for purposes corresponding to purposes of any of the provisions mentioned in subsection (2)—

- (a) shall not be subject to paragraph 1(4) and (5) of that Schedule (affirmative resolution of both Houses of Parliament); but
- (b) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(2) The provisions are—

- (a) sections 20 to 25;
- (b) paragraphs 11 to 16 of Schedule 1; and
- (c) the entries in Schedule 2 relating to the [^c859842]Electricity Act 1989.
