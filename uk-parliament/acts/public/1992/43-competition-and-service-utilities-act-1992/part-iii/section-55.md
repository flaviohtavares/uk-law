# Competition and Service (Utilities) Act 1992, section 55

> Text as at 2010-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Expenses.[^c859843]

*Extent: E+W+S*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament any increase attributable to the provisions of this Act in the sums payable out of money so provided under any other Act.
