# Charities Act 1992, section 60B

## 60B. Exclusion of lower-paid collectors from provisions of section 60A[^c-fe85dba891f4a663141735540402beae]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 60(1) and (2) do not apply (by virtue of section 60A(1)) to a person who is under the earnings limit in subsection (2) below.

(2) A person is under the earnings limit in this subsection if he does not receive—

- (a) more than—
  - (i) £15[^c-47ccbf50fe804654cb28da6e4d8a33da] per day, or
  - (ii) £1,500[^c-a1e397fc70e1a14091211b044746cfd8] per year,

  by way of remuneration for acting as a collector in relation to relevant collections, or
- (b) more than £1,500[^c-5f6597e433ff126b8d624f99d9b916d8] by way of remuneration for acting as a collector in relation to the collection mentioned in section 60A(1).

(3) In subsection (2) “ **relevant collections** ” means public charitable collections conducted for the benefit of—

- (a) the charitable institution or institutions, or
- (b) the charitable, benevolent or philanthropic purposes,

for whose benefit the collection mentioned in section 60A(1) is conducted.

(4) A person is not within section 60A(6) if he is under the earnings limit in subsection (5) below.

(5) A person is under the earnings limit in this subsection if the remuneration received by him as mentioned in section 60A(6)(c)—

- (a) is not more than—
  - (i) £15[^c-5c171048914a4d9aba9b76dc47d81f85] per day, or
  - (ii) £1,500[^c-6239decfb23691ab0355d95de366cc84] per year, or
- (b) if a lump sum, is not more than £1,500[^c-47e51f16673dff17c85354db93d5ee23].

(6) The Secretary of State[^c-e6d34150bd79e33ba9035f6c7ed175e3] may by order amend subsections (2) and (5) by substituting a different sum for any sum for the time being specified there.
