# Charities Act 1992, section 63

## 63. False statements relating to institutions which are not registered charities.[^c10232751]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where—

- (a) a person solicits money or other property for the benefit of an institution in association with a representation that the institution is a registered charity, and
- (b) the institution is not such a charity,

he shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding the fifth level on the standard scale.

(1A)[^c10232761] In any proceedings for an offence under subsection (1), it shall be a defence for the accused to prove that he believed on reasonable grounds that the institution was a registered charity.

(2) In this section[^c10232771] “**registered charity**” means a charity which is for the time being registered in the register of charities kept under section 29 of the Charities Act 2011[^key-2d25f21dd2f26b5208ca14339ab32364].
