# Charities Act 1992, section 77

## 77. Regulations and orders.[^c10232981]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any regulations or order of the Secretary of State[^c-e061e3111d68792ca6e130ecabb9e058] under this Act—

- (a) shall be made by statutory instrument; and
- (b) (subject to subsections (2) and (2A)[^c-881d185692aeadf3b030be1c221fb40c]) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(2) Subsection (1)(b) does not apply—

- (a)[^c10232991] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c10232991] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c10232991] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) to an order under section 79(2).

(2A)[^c-d3d79dc64d9dd2821819bd6519026c77] Subsection (1)(b) does not apply to regulations under section 64A, and no such regulations may be made unless a draft of the statutory instrument containing the regulations has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

(3)[^c10233021] Any regulations or order of the Secretary of State[^c-90f006b23092cf895e011c1cfdcb8b0d] under this Act may make—

- (a) different provision for different cases; and
- (b) such supplemental, incidental, consequential or transitional provision or savings as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

(4) Before making any regulations under section [^c10233031]. . . 64 or 64A[^c-5f453b4c7131d39b640480f25894f52e] [^c-436105877911271f80121fdf040db563]... the Secretary of State[^c-5802e8546843535364ccc0e271330b9b] shall consult such persons or bodies of persons as he considers appropriate and, in the case of regulations made by virtue of section 64B or 64C, shall in particular consult the Charity Commission. *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-a84fa77f5bfb41aeacdb36ad22c23bf9]
