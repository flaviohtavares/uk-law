# Charities Act 1992, section 78

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Minor and consequential amendments and repeals.[^c10233041]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The enactments mentioned in Schedule 6 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments there specified (which are either minor amendments or amendments consequential on the provisions of this Act).

(2) The enactments mentioned in Schedule 7 to this Act (which include some that are already spent or are no longer of practical utility) are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
