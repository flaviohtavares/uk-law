# Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992, section 87H

## 87H. Meaning of “close member of the settlor's family”[^key-2b06b41d071467dc0d80c31111ee0590]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of sections 87D and 87G[^key-9fea6c0c51dd88fb37d74b199ff56fda] as they apply in relation to a settlement, a person is a close member of the settlor's family at any time if the settlor is living at that time and—

- (a) the person is the settlor's spouse or civil partner at that time, or
- (b) the person—
  - (i) is a child of the settlor, or of a person who at that time is the settlor's spouse or civil partner, and
  - (ii) at that time has not reached the age of 18.

(2)[^key-323d0e9c9a9e97f75682650aceb60013] For the purposes of subsection (1), two people living together as if they were a married couple or civil partners are treated as if they were spouses or civil partners of each other.
