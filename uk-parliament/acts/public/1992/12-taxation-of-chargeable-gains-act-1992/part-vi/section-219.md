# Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992, section 219

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 219. Disposals by housing related bodies.[^c-f8e6f2ce59f0a8b091b4cdfea51c85d2][^c-82560af1f75e63558ace0c1d2a55ce27][^c-4d3e34955c8affdae901ac7cd97936b5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In any case where—

- (a) a housing regulator[^c-e59a4108c7031637c1540ec154348715] or the Homes and Communities Agency[^c-7abd99145bf64b735704b4aa648a5a3e] disposes of any land to a relevant housing provider[^c-68c707cf196001e624811382982ed310], or
- (b) a relevant housing provider disposes of any land to another relevant housing provider, or
- (c) in pursuance of a direction of a housing regulator[^c-6b6e0cbf14015cb9ca67e906bc2cd5b5] given under section 167 of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008[^c-b6752d905a28e4b5903ce37dbb787874], section 106 of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2010[^c-beb44ad0af6d57302f767268ccf92a6a], Part I of the Housing Act 1996 or Part I of the Housing Associations Act 1985 (as the case may be) requiring it to do so, a relevant housing provider disposes of any of its property, other than land, to another relevant housing provider, or
- (d) a relevant housing provider or an unregistered self-build society disposes of any land to a housing regulator[^c-d4f174813e8386ae67593a2c78569f53], the Homes and Communities Agency or the Greater London Authority[^c-020cfc6e33b55a0f9054a1f86c57ea89],

both parties to the disposal shall be treated for the purposes of tax on chargeable gains as if the land or property disposed of were acquired from the housing regulator[^c-de9ea958403d09d9f4d648c3ce616d92], the Homes and Communities Agency[^c-8baf9c33b103ef614f427c73b56e4056], relevant housing provider or unregistered self-build society making the disposal for a consideration of such an amount as would secure that on the disposal neither a gain nor a loss accrued to the housing regulator, the Homes and Communities Agency[^c-a55da6849a98946a7f978b1b3b9d8069] or, as the case may be, that relevant housing provider or society.

(2) In this section—

- “housing regulator” means the Regulator of Social Housing[^c-d121465409679f40342ae8ac4f549dfe], the Scottish Housing Regulator[^c-faf5491163dc57b3accbb3d4667f3747], the Secretary of State[^c-7a4e502c85426e730ba92096d5704f2d] or Scottish Homes;
- “[^c-e273258b8a9c23cf9e3318ada97930d5]**relevant housing provider**” means—
  - (a) a non-profit registered provider of social housing,
  - (b) a registered social landlord within the meaning of Part 1 of the Housing Act 1996, or
  - (c) a body registered in the register maintained under section 20(1) of the Housing (Scotland) Act 2010[^c-69d90dd0096429959e3a6a515c887b9a].
- “unregistered self-build society” has the same meaning as in the Housing Associations Act 1985.
