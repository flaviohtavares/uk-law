# Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992, section 192

## 192. Tax exempt distributions.[^key-c42008dae329e95a470c4862d376625e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section has effect for facilitating certain transactions whereby trading activities carried on by a single company or group are divided so as to be carried on by 2 or more companies not belonging to the same group or by 2 or more independent groups.

(2) Where a company makes a distribution which is exempt by virtue of section 1076 of CTA 2010[^key-ca4c125d0d999dbfed64fb8e99023d00]—

- (a) the distribution shall not be a capital distribution for the purposes of section 122; and
- (b) sections 126 to 130 shall, with the necessary modifications, apply as if that company and the subsidiary whose shares are transferred were the same company and the distribution were a reorganisation of its share capital.

(3) Subject to subsection (4) below, section 179 shall not[^key-6a1cf12afd7b05a3cc2f592a775e1995] apply in a case where a company ceases to be a member of a group by reason only of an exempt distribution.

(4) Subsection (3) does not apply if within 5 years after the making of the exempt distribution there is chargeable payment; and the time for making an assessment under section [^key-d221057aa86217906eb5753d3c2e2e9a]... 179 by virtue of this subsection shall not expire before the end of 3 years after the making of the chargeable payment.

(5) In this section—

- “**chargeable payment**” has the meaning given in section 1088 of CTA 2010[^key-7c66e97e42e99bbf85e1100c91d94e8e];
- “**exempt distribution**” means a distribution which is exempt by virtue of section 1076 or 1077 of CTA 2010[^key-377cca624cf222c755b2031de3f9ed81]; and
- “**group**” means a company which has one or more 75 per cent. subsidiaries together with that or those subsidiaries.

(6) In determining for the purposes of this section whether one company is a 75 per cent. subsidiary of another, the other company shall be treated as not being the owner of—

- (a) any share capital which it owns directly in a body corporate if a profit on a sale of the shares would be treated as a trading receipt of its trade; or
- (b) any share capital which it owns indirectly and which is owned directly by a body corporate for which a profit on the sale of the shares would be a trading receipt.
