# Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992, section 166

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 166. Gifts to non-residents.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to section 167A, section 165(4)[^key-2ddd9bb8bd7a05d4ca0740c9bb5c6ee1] shall not apply where the transferee is not resident[^key-3181a49b013c04b2490b9542acf8ee5d] in the United Kingdom.

(2) Section 165(4) shall not apply where the transferee is an individual [^key-1ca81e0ab084e135cffc4d55b14bea58]... if that individual [^key-1ca81e0ab084e135cffc4d55b14bea58]... —

- (a) though resident [^key-8852c4c9555f1bc69d32478d975f8a7e]... in the United Kingdom, is regarded for the purposes of any double taxation relief arrangements as resident in a territory outside the United Kingdom, and
- (b) by virtue of the arrangements would not be liable in the United Kingdom to tax on a gain arising on a disposal of the asset occurring immediately after its acquisition.
