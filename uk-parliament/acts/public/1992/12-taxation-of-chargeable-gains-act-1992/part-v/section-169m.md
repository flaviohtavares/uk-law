# Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992, section 169M

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 169M. Relief to be claimed[^key-62210e0dd95858649d5b0850e81240b3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Business asset disposal relief[^key-81bc625811da9cbe5b35aca85a72dfb8] is to be given only on the making of a claim.

(2)[^key-b20be849a431a61de4b433c082e75a02] A claim for business asset disposal relief in respect of a qualifying business disposal must be made—

- (a) in the case of a disposal of trust business assets, jointly by the trustees and the qualifying beneficiary, and
- (b) otherwise, by the individual.

(3)[^key-b20be849a431a61de4b433c082e75a02] A claim for business asset disposal relief in respect of a qualifying business disposal must be made on or before the first anniversary of the 31 January following the tax year in which the qualifying business disposal is made.

(4) A claim for business asset disposal relief in respect of a qualifying business disposal may only be made if the amount resulting under section 169N(1) is a positive amount.
