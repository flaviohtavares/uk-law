## 169SB. Overview of Chapter[^key-54aca3dbf6a607f6e276424c1fec282b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter makes provision about an individual claiming business asset disposal relief[^key-81bc625811da9cbe5b35aca85a72dfb8] in certain cases where relief would otherwise become unavailable because of a company ceasing to be the individual's personal company.
