# Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992, section 226B

## 226B. Exception to section 226A[^key-ea1f146812f30fa1026da94cc76dcfa6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 226A shall not apply in relation to a later disposal made by the trustees of a settlement if the trustees have elected that section 508 of ITA 2007 (trustees’ election in respect of income arising from heritage maintenance property)[^key-16ad4e7043dced91aa97c5351046b99d] shall have effect in the case of—

- (a) the settlement, or
- (b) any part of the settlement,

in relation to each year of assessment in which a relevant earlier disposal is made.

(2) In this section “**relevant earlier disposal**”, in relation to a later disposal, means an earlier disposal in respect of which a claim mentioned in section 226A(1)(c) is made.

(3) This section is to be construed as one with section 226A.
