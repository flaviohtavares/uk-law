## 270. Chevening Estate.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The enactments relating to capital gains tax (apart from this section) shall not apply in respect of property held on the trusts of the trust instrument set out in the Schedule to the [^c2138420]Chevening Estate Act 1959.
