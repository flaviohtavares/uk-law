# Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992, section 261C

## 261C. Meaning of “the maximum amount” for purposes of section 261B[^key-114e475e441da9bc64e129ef09833188]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of section 261B “the maximum amount” is the amount on which the person would be chargeable to capital gains tax for the year of assessment if—

- (a) the provisions mentioned below were ignored, and
- (b) no account were taken of the event mentioned below.

(2) The provisions are—

- (a)[^key-60dae785482e6e22ae8a8d4c1878a18c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) section 1K(1)[^key-ba03e3df1fe133da4384344ea779187f] (annual exempt amount), and
- (c) section 261B.

(3) The event is any event—

- (a) which occurs after the date on which the relevant amount (see section 261B(3)) can no longer be varied by the tribunal[^key-201a829100757df3413f9804a16e3e39] on appeal or on the order of a court, and
- (b) in consequence of which the amount chargeable to capital gains tax is reduced as a result of an enactment relating to capital gains tax.
