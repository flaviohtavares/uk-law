# Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992, section 112

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 112. Parallel pooling regulations: corporation tax.[^c-c9715726df2f0cfa77ee85ceffbcc5cf][^c-fe7dc1150b20310078b4e7112c67a125][^c-be9144c7827a5159dc0c9dca424bf439]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The [^c2138206]Capital Gains Tax (Parallel Pooling) Regulations 1986 made by the Treasury under paragraph 21 of Schedule 19 to the [^c2138207]Finance Act 1985 shall continue to have effect notwithstanding the repeal by this Act of that Schedule, and for the purposes of section 14 of the [^c2138208]Interpretation Act 1978 that paragraph shall be deemed not to have been repealed.

(2) An election under Schedule 6 to the [^c2138209]Finance Act 1983 which has not been revoked before 6th April 1992 shall not have effect in relation to any disposal after 5th April 1992 and may, if the Board allow, be revoked by notice to the inspector.

(3) All such adjustments shall be made, whether by way of discharge or repayment of tax, or the making of assessments or otherwise, as are required in consequence of a revocation under subsection (2) above.
