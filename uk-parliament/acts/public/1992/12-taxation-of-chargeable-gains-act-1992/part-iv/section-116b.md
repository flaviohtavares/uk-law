# Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992, section 116B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 116B. Shares beginning or ceasing to be shares to which section 521B of CTA 2009 applies[^key-1eef8e7f1c7d6a8de2e10b41fa094b67][^key-bca43ff72075129ba5ba54d83e2b9bdb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-5e193b47c09b886f97c3763f3465be02] If at any time section 521B[^key-622175623c83a1c0f0939bc46ed746d8] of CTA 2009 (application of Part 5 of that Act to certain shares as rights under a creditor relationship) begins or ceases to apply in the case of a share held by the investing company it is treated for the purposes of this Act—

- (a) as having disposed of the share immediately before that time for consideration of an amount equal to the notional carrying value of the share[^key-244dabc16ff7c647159c7a74256471c1] at that time, and
- (b) as having immediately reacquired it for consideration of the same amount.

(2) In this section—

- “[^key-e72024702cc06837beb040e230c07b15]**notional carrying value**” has the same meaning as in subsection (2) of section 521F of CTA 2009 (see subsection (3) of that section),
- “**investing company**” has the same meaning as it has for the purposes of Chapter 6A[^key-97549df711002e3032581dfbce499233] of Part 6 of that Act (shares accounted for as liabilities) (see section 521A(3)[^key-a5d5cd115d2204f6cc40ae8c23039025] of that Act).
