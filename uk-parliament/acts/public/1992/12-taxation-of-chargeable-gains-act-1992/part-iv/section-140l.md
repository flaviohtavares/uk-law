# Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992, section 140L

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/12

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 140L. Interpretation[^c-c3931a3fbf29b771177918e042ddf65c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In sections 140A to 140K and this section[^c-7967e8e224dce50fea3be6254981126f], unless the contrary intention appears—

- (a) “the Mergers Directive” means Council Directive 2009/133/EC,[^c-a5c75822af8ebe34935cdac8b07d1561]
- (b) “company” means an entity listed as a company in Part A of Annex I[^c-55af64c64f8fb4400f10a160a6fb58b9] to the Mergers Directive,
- (ba)[^c-44b9044be4695a0da6acc77637526eb2] “relevant state” means the United Kingdom or a member State, and
- (c) “transparent entity” means an entity which is resident in a member State [^c-ac68ffad46fe8ca070dbbb9afb093aba]... and is listed as a company in Part A of Annex I to the Mergers Directive, but—
  - (i) does not have an ordinary share capital (within the meaning given by section 1119 of CTA 2010[^c-a91684c7f56b11883b3731294c32d6b2]), and
  - (ii) if it were resident in the United Kingdom, would not be capable of being a company within the meaning given by the Companies Act 2006.

(2)[^c-b0cccac121ee40603dd457c6b36a7de8] For the purposes of those sections and subsection (1) above, a company is resident in a relevant state if—

- (a) it is within a charge to tax under the law of the relevant state as being resident for that purpose, and
- (b) it is not regarded, for the purposes of any double taxation relief arrangements to which the relevant state is a party, as resident in a territory not within a relevant state.
