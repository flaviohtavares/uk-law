# Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (23).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/12

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Deferred charges on gains before 31st March 1982.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 4, which provides for the reduction of a deferred charge to corporation tax in respect of chargeable gains[^key-e4262ba6b241e8d7f295427884d88577] where the charge is wholly or partly attributable to an increase in the value of an asset before 31st March 1982, shall have effect.
