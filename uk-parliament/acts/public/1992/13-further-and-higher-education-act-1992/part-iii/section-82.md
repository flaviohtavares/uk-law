# Further and Higher Education Act 1992, section 82

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/13
>

## 82. Joint exercise of functions.[^c11066371][^c11066381][^c11066391][^c11066401]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-efafcb802a322013fae447cc357d3092] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1A)[^c-efafcb802a322013fae447cc357d3092] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1B)[^c-efafcb802a322013fae447cc357d3092] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) A relevant authority must, if directed to do so by the Secretary of State, make provision jointly with another relevant authority or with the Secretary of State[^c-f9c11a991b4cc4bf6df85a35386e4897] for the assessment by a person appointed by them of matters relating to the arrangements made by each institution in Wales and Scotland[^c-e1e606287077a3b3afed2ede2eaa3c13] which is within the higher education sector for maintaining academic standards in the institution.

(2A)[^M_F_9ccddb54-0675-47d6-affb-5cebd4550de8] The power of the Secretary of State to give directions under subsection (2) above shall, as regards the Scottish Further and[^c-5b05a643c54955cc56209d60324af959] Higher Education Funding Council, be treated as exercisable in or as regards Scotland and may be exercised separately.

(3) In this section—

- (a)[^c-b19b2e7c77c3eebd2e6f1fd2aa8543a1] “relevant authority” means the Commission for Tertiary Education and Research[^M_F_5bf351dd-4376-425f-f6dd-b3a8fe93636c] or the Scottish Further and[^c-f8fe09dbb4217ca14381cc39ebd0f1bf] Higher Education Funding Council, and
- (b) references to institutions within the higher education sector include institutions within the higher education sector within the meaning of Part II of the [^c11066421]Further and Higher Education (Scotland) Act 1992.
