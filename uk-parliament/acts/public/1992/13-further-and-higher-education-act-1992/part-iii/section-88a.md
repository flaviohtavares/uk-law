## 88A. Stamp duty land tax[^key-b472ee15d9e8d1172f404a7ad270c02b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A land transaction effected under or by virtue of section [^key-fc4d5c37b0f4dd71f9cefc3d3bba755f]... 27B, 27C[^key-321be94e4b55e1063a290697ffc9ae2f], [^key-ca6fd181bace920db6532f5b0820c30e]... 33P[^key-6db7f170be123049b85ec833ca375384] or 34 of this Act is exempt from charge for the purposes of stamp duty land tax.

(2) Relief under this section must be claimed in a land transaction return or an amendment of such a return.

(3) In this section—

- “land transaction” has the meaning given by section 43(1) of the Finance Act 2003;
- “land transaction return” has the meaning given by section 76(1) of that Act.
