# Further and Higher Education Act 1992, section 85

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/13
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 85. Finance and government of locally funded further and higher education.[^c11066521]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Chapter III of Part II of the [^c11066531]Education Reform Act 1988 (finance and government of locally funded further and higher education) shall cease to have effect; and section 156 of that Act (government and conduct of certain further and higher education institutions) shall cease to have effect in relation to designated assisted institutions.

(2) A local authority[^c-5ca14e1c5c9fb4ff32ae914fdd63af76] shall have the following powers in relation to any institution, not within the further education sector or the higher education sector, which is maintained by them in the exercise of their further or higher education functions.

(3) The authority may—

- (a) make such provision as they think fit in respect of the government of the institution (including replacing any instrument of government or articles of government of the institution made under that Chapter or that section), and
- (b) delegate to the governing body of the institution such functions relating to the management of the finances of the institution, and such other functions relating to the management of the institution (including the appointment and dismissal of staff), as the authority may determine.
