## 33M. Charitable status of a sixth form college corporation[^key-1dc714bc06c48a6f744ef1dcb4317329]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A sixth form college corporation is a charity [^key-f6fa8b1ef685c1e68898ea432c93a2b4]... (and, as a result of its inclusion in Schedule 3 to the Charities Act 2011, is an exempt charity for the purposes of that Act)[^key-549b99f246b9e974a157344aaba8cedf].
