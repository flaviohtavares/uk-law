# Further and Higher Education Act 1992, section 21

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/13
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Initial instruments and articles.[^c11062881]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) As from the date on which a further education corporation is established, the instrument of government and articles of government—

- (a)[^c11062891] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

[^c11062901]. . . shall be such as is prescribed by regulations.

(2) Such [^c11062911]. . . regulations—

- (a) may provide for all or any of the persons who, on the date on which a corporation is established to conduct the [^c11062921]. . . existing institution, are the members of the governing body of the institution to be the initial members of the corporation, and
- (b) may make such other provision in relation to [^c11062931]. . . existing institutions as appears to the Secretary of State necessary or desirable to secure continuity in their government.

(3) In the case of a further education corporation established to conduct an institution which, on the date the corporation was established, was a maintained school, the governing body incorporated under[^c11062941] section 19 of the Education Act 2002[^key-b10e788aca5a1b2fc7f222e1b44b3521] shall, on the operative date, be dissolved.
