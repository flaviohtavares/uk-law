# Further and Higher Education Act 1992, section 33K

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/13

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 33K. Instrument and articles of new sixth form college corporations[^c-1dc714bc06c48a6f744ef1dcb4317329]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The first instrument of government and articles of government of a sixth form college corporation established under section 33C are to be made by the Secretary of State by order[^c-01c740a3ee60e7e584b2091874243b11] .

(2)[^c-362e29323965b8388f93b5d08663195e] An order under subsection (1) may not be made unless—

- (a) the Secretary of State has consulted the corporation, and
- (b) in the case of a sixth form college corporation to which section 33J applies, the trustees of the relevant sixth form college have given their consent.
