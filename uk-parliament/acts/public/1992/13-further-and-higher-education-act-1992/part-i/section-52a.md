# Further and Higher Education Act 1992, section 52A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (28).** Text as at 2026-07-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/13
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52A. Duty to safeguard pupils receiving secondary education.[^c11064921]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where secondary education is provided to persons of compulsory school age[^c17235921]—

- (a) by a further education corporation by virtue of section 18(1)(aa) or (ab) of this Act[^c17235971],
- (aa)[^key-9e7a07104f29a4a3111db54de12b01fe] by a sixth form college corporation by virtue of section 33E(1)(b) or (c) of this Act, or
- (b) by a designated institution in pursuance of arrangements made—
  - (i) by a local authority[^key-5ca14e1c5c9fb4ff32ae914fdd63af76], or
  - (ii) by the governing body of a school on behalf of such an authority.

(2) The governing body of the corporation or institution shall secure that, except in such circumstances as may be prescribed by regulations, no education is provided to a person who has attained the age of nineteen years in a room in which any persons of compulsory school age[^c17235991] are for the time being receiving secondary education.
