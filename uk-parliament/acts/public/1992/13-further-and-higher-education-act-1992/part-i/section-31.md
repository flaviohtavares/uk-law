## 31. Designated institutions conducted by companies.[^c11063531]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545] This section has effect in relation to any designated institution *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545] in Wales[^c-1ab678ffdac031ebbb49a39f708bf739] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545] conducted by a company. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545]

(2) The articles of association of the company shall incorporate— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545]

- (a) provision with respect to the constitution of a governing body of the institution (to be known as the instrument of government of the institution), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545]
- (b) provision with respect to the conduct of the institution (to be known as the articles of government of the institution). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545]

(2A)[^c-46938bc53a331c313ef9f3c4eab19558] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The Secretary of State may give to the persons who appear to him to have effective control over the company such directions as he thinks fit for securing that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545]

- (a) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545] articles of association[^c-faa2f87aef42636dd7a4a158d04706a3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545] of the company, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545]
- (b) any rules or bye-laws made in pursuance of any power conferred by the articles of association of the company, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545]

are amended in such manner as he may specify in the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545]

(4) No amendment of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545] articles of association[^c-bfc07f32c5f890be373245ad3844a22b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545] of the company (other than one required under subsection (3)(a) above) shall take effect until it has been submitted to the Secretary of State for his approval and he has notified his approval to the company. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545]

(5) Before giving any directions under subsection (3) above the Secretary of State shall consult the persons who appear to him to have effective control over the company. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6bb88b7cc14017e9bfd326a429f50545]
