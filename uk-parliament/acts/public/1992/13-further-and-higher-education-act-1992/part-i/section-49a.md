## 49A. Consultation[^M_F_bd575cbf-fc8f-4174-8527-6b26ecf45ddd]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-123694d03d8990e29a5a9842d8ca0eb2] In exercising their functions the governing body of an institution within the further education sector *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-123694d03d8990e29a5a9842d8ca0eb2] in Wales[^c-46fcb3d0d35362bcc6b7d18028eb9f97] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-123694d03d8990e29a5a9842d8ca0eb2] must have regard to any guidance given from time to time by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-123694d03d8990e29a5a9842d8ca0eb2] Welsh Ministers *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-123694d03d8990e29a5a9842d8ca0eb2] about consultation with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-123694d03d8990e29a5a9842d8ca0eb2]

- (a) persons who are or are likely to become students of the institution, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-123694d03d8990e29a5a9842d8ca0eb2]
- (b) employers, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-123694d03d8990e29a5a9842d8ca0eb2]

in connection with the taking of decisions affecting them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-123694d03d8990e29a5a9842d8ca0eb2]

(2) Any guidance under this section about consultation with persons falling within paragraph (a) of subsection (1) must provide for the views of such a person to be considered in the light of his age and understanding. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-123694d03d8990e29a5a9842d8ca0eb2]
