## 5. Transitional provisions and savings.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The transitional provisions and savings in Schedule 3 to this Act shall have effect.

(2) Nothing in that Schedule affects the general operation of section 16 of the [^c12203731]Interpretation Act 1978 (general savings implied on repeal) or of the previous provisions of this Act.
