# Social Security (Consequential Provisions) Act 1992, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2016-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/6
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 1. Meaning of “the consolidating Acts”.

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Act—

- “**the consolidating Acts**” means the [^c12203691]Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 (“the Contributions and Benefits Act”), the [^c12203701]Social Security Administration Act 1992 (“the Administration Act”) and, so far as it reproduces the effect of the repealed enactments, this Act; and
- “**the repealed enactments**” means the enactments repealed by this Act.
