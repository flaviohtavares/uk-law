## 111. Delay, obstruction etc. of inspector[^M_C_fb721847-124f-41b2-cadb-5567784fc1cf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a person—

- (a) intentionally delays or obstructs an authorised officer[^key-4ab341a683d3d15f6522a1e2376c248e] in the exercise of any power under this Act other than an Inland Revenue Power[^c24146021];
- (ab)[^key-db9a9e72c7f6f782ae9688499da332d3] refuses or neglects to comply with any requirement under section109BA or 110AA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-18a896917f9944b05c6030575e0363a7] or with the requirements of any arrangements entered into in accordance with subsection (1) of that section, or
- (b) refuses or neglects to answer any question or to furnish any information or to produce any document when required to do so under this Act otherwise than[^M_F_b2d53015-53b7-4896-9c30-98e2ce5aba27] under Schedule 3ZA, Schedule 3B or[^key-b287f8bd2b9ba04e2e45b1b8f000922d] in the exercise of an Inland Revenue Power,

he shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.

(2) Where a person is convicted of an offence under subsection (1)(ab) or (b)[^key-39702b417b0900763fc23eb9cff0b3ed] above and the refusal or neglect is continued by him after his conviction, he shall be guilty of a further offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding £40 for each day on which it is continued.

(3)[^M_F_2eabf5a9-4b43-45d3-e260-08e813e5d65d] In subsection (1) “**Inland Revenue power**” means any power conferred on an officer of the Inland Revenue by virtue of[^key-bf022e2aa5b7fe1bbdea34321f4014a2] section 110ZA above or by virtue of an authorisation granted under section 109A[^c24146131] or 110A *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7df805f8c1ea2d33590d15279c406e20] above.

(4)[^key-88b7d5225657bc19357db7ea6a5532e6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
