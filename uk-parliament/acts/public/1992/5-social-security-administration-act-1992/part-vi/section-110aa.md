# Social Security Administration Act 1992, section 110AA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (192).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110AA. Power of local authority to require electronic access to information[^c-0222464b2e6b2d9ce76832de77c3df02]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b] Subject to subsection (2) below, where it appears to an authority administering housing benefit or council tax benefit- *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]

- (a) that a person falling within section 109B(2A) keeps any electronic records, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]
- (b) that the records contain or are likely, from time to time, to contain information about any matter that is relevant for any one or more of the purposes mentioned in section 110A(2) above, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]
- (c) that facilities exist under which electronic access to those records is being provided, or is capable of being provided, by that person to other persons, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]

that authority may require that person to enter into arrangements under which authorised officers are allowed such access to those records. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]

(2) An authorised officer— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]

- (a) shall be entitled to obtain information in accordance with arrangements entered into under subsection (1) above only if his authorisation states that his authorisation applies for the purposes of that subsection; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]
- (b) shall not seek to obtain any information in accordance with any such arrangements other than information which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]
  - (i) relates to a particular person; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b] could be the subject of any such requirement under section 109B above as may be imposed in exercise of the powers conferred by section 110A(8) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]

(3) The matters that may be included in the arrangements that a person is required to enter into under subsection (1) above may include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]

- (a) requirements as to the electronic access to records that is to be made available to authorised officers; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]
- (b) requirements as to the keeping of records of the use that is made of the arrangements; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]
- (c) requirements restricting the disclosure of information about the use that is made of the arrangements; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]
- (d) such other incidental requirements as the authority in question considers appropriate in connection with allowing access to records to authorised officers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]

(4) An authorised officer who is allowed access in accordance with any arrangements entered into under subsection (1) above shall be entitled to make copies of, and to take extracts from, any records containing information which he is entitled to make the subject of a requirement such as is mentioned in subsection (2)(b) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]

(5) An authority administering housing benefit or council tax benefit shall not- *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]

- (a) require any person to enter into arrangements for allowing authorised officers to have electronic access to any records; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]
- (b) otherwise than in pursuance of a requirement under this section, enter into any arrangements with a person specified in section 109B(2A) above for allowing anyone acting on behalf of the authority for purposes connected with any benefit to have electronic access to any private information contained in any records, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]

except with the consent of the Secretary of State and subject to any conditions imposed by the Secretary of State by the provisions of the consent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]

(6) A consent for the purposes of subsection (5) may be given in relation to a particular case, or in relation to any case that falls within a particular description of cases. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]

(7) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]**private information *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]**”, in relation to an authority administering housing benefit or council tax benefit, means any information held by a person who is not entitled to disclose it to that authority except in compliance with a requirement imposed by the authority in exercise of their statutory powers. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2d98a34ae3f02acf73f56c6c7cebf8b]
