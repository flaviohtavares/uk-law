# Social Security Administration Act 1992, section 120

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (192).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120. Proof of previous offences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to and in accordance with subsections (2) to (5) below, where a person is convicted of an offence mentioned in section 119(1) [^c24147151]... above, evidence may be given of any previous failure by him to pay contributions within the time prescribed for the purpose; and in those subsections “**the conviction**” and “**the offence**” mean respectively the conviction referred to in this subsection and the offence of which the person is convicted.

(2) Such evidence may be given only if notice of intention to give it is served with the summons or warrant or, in Scotland, the complaint on which the person appeared before the court which convicted him.

(3) If the offence is one of failure to pay a Class 1 contribution, evidence may be given of failure on his part to pay (whether or not in respect of the same person) such contributions or any Class 1A or Class 1B[^c24147161] contributions or contributions equivalent premiums[^c24147181] on the date of the offence, or during the 6 years[^c24147211] preceding that date.

(4) If the offence is one of failure to pay Class 1A contribution, evidence may be given of failure on his part to pay (whether or not in respect of the same person or the same amount[^c24147231]) such contributions, or any Class 1 or Class 1B contributions or contributions equivalent premiums, on the date of the offence, or during the 6 years preceding that date.

(4A)[^c24147321] If the offence is one of failure to pay a Class 1B contribution, evidence may be given of failure on his part to pay such contributions, or any Class 1 or Class 1A contributions or contributions equivalent premiums, on the date of the offence, or during the 6 years preceding that date.

(5) If the offence—

- (a) is one of failure to pay Class 2 contributions; [^c24147341]...
- (b)[^c24147341] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

evidence may be given of his failure to pay such contributions during those 6 years[^M_F_36ce28b8-7071-4217-b8ba-590d4eb9a175].

(6) On proof of any matter of which evidence may be given under subsection (3), (4), (4A)[^c24147381] or (5) above, the person convicted shall be liable to pay to the Inland Revenue[^M_F_cfa7d14c-f9d9-4a7a-d268-67fe8c2e3d07] a sum equal to the total of all amounts which he is so proved to have failed to pay and which remain unpaid at the date of the conviction.
