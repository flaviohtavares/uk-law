# Social Security Administration Act 1992, section 145

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 145. Power to alter primary and secondary contributions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c24151761] For the purpose of adjusting amounts payable by way of primary Class 1 contributions, the Treasury[^M_F_7e05ef21-e624-4549-f7fd-0b1a2fa291be] may at any time make an order altering the percentage rate specified as the main primary percentage in section 8(2)(a)[^c-993ad7a3802c6f80648b2674db637ec0] of the Contributions and Benefits Act.

(2) For the purpose of adjusting amounts payable by way of secondary Class 1 contributions, the Treasury may at any time make an order altering the percentage rate specified as the secondary percentage in section 9(2) of the Contributions and Benefits Act.

(3)[^c-3c2f5ae7010b5045baeb76733ce89a98] No order shall be made under this section so as to increase for any tax year the main[^c-3324280f0484ac52afada1b7e9314018] primary percentage, or the secondary percentage, to a percentage rate more than 0.25 per cent higher than that applicable at the end of the preceding tax year.

(4) Without prejudice to section 141 or 143 above, the Treasury may make such order—

- (a) amending section[^c-ecb6374e36b041b66ed6600e54b9929d] 11(6)[^c-28fa60f2bcf916ca77d6f1a37e00d277] of the Contributions and Benefits Act by altering the figure specified [^c-8ec20deb4f1d83cf6db136c14c50aaba]... as the weekly rate of Class 2 contributions;
- (b) amending section 13(1) of that Act by altering the figure specified in that subsection as the amount of a Class 3 contribution,

as the Treasury think[^M_F_bea13b59-deea-4e91-932a-dfbd98be9798] fit in consequence of the coming into force of an order made or proposed to be made under subsection (1) above.
