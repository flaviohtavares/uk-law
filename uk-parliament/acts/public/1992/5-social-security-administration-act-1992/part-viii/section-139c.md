# Social Security Administration Act 1992, section 139C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (192).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 139C. Reports (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_f98c1356-4f68-4d74-824b-4bbb2a4d5185][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A report about an authority by a person authorised under section 139A(1) above may include recommendations about improvements which could be made by that authority in its administration of benefit and, in particular, in the prevention and detection of fraud relating to benefit and, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] in particular—[^M_F_dcd779f0-6b26-41e3-f63d-9e9e70e9e7e9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) in the prevention and detention of fraud relating to benefit, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) for the purposes of complying with requirements of Part I of the Local Government Act 1999 (best value) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] or the performance requirements set out in section 89 of the Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021[^key-a6a197863c945c28a3bf54881d06b28b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2].

(2) When the Secretary of State receives a report about an authority from a person authorised under section 139A(1) above, he shall send a copy to the authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
