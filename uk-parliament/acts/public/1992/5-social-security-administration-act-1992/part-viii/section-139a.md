# Social Security Administration Act 1992, section 139A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (192).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 139A. Persons to report on administration (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_f98c1356-4f68-4d74-824b-4bbb2a4d5185][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^M_F_4ae67605-294c-43f4-e08e-b625595c52d7] The Secretary of State may authorise persons to consider and report to him on the administration by authorities of housing benefit and council tax benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(2) The Secretary of State may ask persons authorised under subsection (1) to consider in particular– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) authorities’ performance in the prevention and detection of fraud relating to housing benefit and council tax benefit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) authorities compliance with the requirements of Part I of the Local Government Act 1999 (best value) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] or the performance requirements set out in section 89 of the Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021[^key-970a86048e9fdc379d04719d7561febc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2].

(2A) A person may be authorised under subsection (1)– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) on such terms and for such period as the Secretary of State thinks fit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) to act generally or in relation to a specified authority or authorities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (c) to report on administration generally or on specified matters. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(3) In sections 139B and 139C below— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- “benefit” means housing benefit or council tax benefit; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- “authority” means an authority which is administering either of those benefits. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
