# Social Security Administration Act 1992, section 139DA

## 139DA (affected by repeal, see note). Directions: variation and revocation (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_893a3fd5-4c1b-4d61-b20e-eff58564fd23][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State may at any time in accordance with this section vary or revoke a direction under section 139D above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(2) A direction may be varied or revoked only if the Secretary of State thinks it is necessary to do so— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) in consequence of representations made by the authority to which the direction is addressed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) to rectify an omission or error, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (c) in consequence of a material change in circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(3) The Secretary of State must not vary a direction unless he first— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) sends a copy of the proposed variation to the authority concerned, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) gives the authority his reasons for making the variation, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (c) gives the authority an opportunity to make representations about the proposed variation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(4) The Secretary of State may specify a period of not less than one month within which representations mentioned in subsection (3)(c) above must be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(5) The Secretary of State may extend a period specified under subsection (4) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
