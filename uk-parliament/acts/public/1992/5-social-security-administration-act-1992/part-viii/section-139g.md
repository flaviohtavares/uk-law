# Social Security Administration Act 1992, section 139G

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (192).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/5

## 139G. Enforcement determinations (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_bf9c9eab-a0d7-4a92-f3d1-86d3939bd7a0][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where, after the time specified in the notice under section 139F above has expired, the Secretary of State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) is not satisfied that the authority has attained the standards *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] or taken the action[^M_F_f416205a-08d5-4d4d-dfca-da5c4c02425f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] in question; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) is not satisfied that the authority is likely to attain those standards *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] or take that action[^M_F_02199704-e9bf-4174-a633-c45c389edaed] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] within the time specified in the directions, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

he may make a determination under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(2) The determination may be made whether or not the authority has responded to the notice under section 139F above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(3) The determination shall be designed to secure the attainment of the standards *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] or the taking of the action[^M_F_1325419d-0a06-41b6-c519-316efd525a0c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] in question and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) shall include provision such as is specified in subsection (4) below; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) may also include provision such as is specified in subsection (5) below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(4) The provision referred to in paragraph (a) of subsection (3) above is provision that the authority must comply with specified requirements as to inviting, preparing, considering and accepting bids to carry out any work which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) falls to be carried out in pursuance of the authority’s functions relating to the administration of benefit; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) is of a description specified in the determination. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(5) The provision referred to in paragraph (b) of that subsection is provision of any one or more of the following kinds relating to the work, or any specified category of the work, to which the determination relates— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) provision that it may not be carried out by the authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) provision that it may not be carried out by any person (other than the authority) who has been carrying it out; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (c) provision that any contract made by the authority with any person for carrying it out shall include terms requiring a level of performance which will secure, or contribute to securing, the attainment of the standards *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] or the taking of the action[^M_F_af1a2d90-82af-41e2-d3a4-5297e88cf30f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] in question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(6) In this section “benefit” means housing benefit or council tax benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
