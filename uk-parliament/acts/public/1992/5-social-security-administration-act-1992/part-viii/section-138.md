## 138. Nature of benefits (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(1)[^M_F_b4eb5dcf-1abf-4b21-8c34-c626c7237ee2] Regulations shall provide that where a person is entitled to council tax benefit in respect of council tax payable to a billing authority or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] local authority in Scotland[^M_F_12157c2a-8e3c-4a44-c0d6-9aa8b297f7a8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] the benefit shall take such of the following forms as is prescribed in the case of the person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (c) both such payment or payments and such reduction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
