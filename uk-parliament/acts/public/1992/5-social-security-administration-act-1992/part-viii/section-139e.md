## 139E. Information about attainment of standards (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_bf9c9eab-a0d7-4a92-f3d1-86d3939bd7a0][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where directions have been given to an authority under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] section 139D(3A) or (3B)[^M_F_d2dc7455-d5ad-4fce-eeb6-45b612057193] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] above, the Secretary of State may require the authority to supply to him any information which he considers may assist him in deciding— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) whether the authority has attained the standards which it has been directed to attain; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_8ab8128d-ac1f-4e93-e9e4-f147b963d15f][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] whether the authority has taken the action which it has been directed to take; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) whether the authority is likely to attain those standards *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] or take that action[^M_F_d390a67f-da36-4478-f275-3b816744190f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] within the time specified in the directions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(2) Information shall be supplied under subsection (1) above in such manner and form as the Secretary of State may require. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
