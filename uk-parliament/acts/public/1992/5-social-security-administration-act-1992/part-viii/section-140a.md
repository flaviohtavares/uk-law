# Social Security Administration Act 1992, section 140A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (192).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 140A. Subsidy (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_29c38a12-82bb-4169-b53c-df2f913609fd][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For each year the Secretary of State shall pay a subsidy to each authority administering housing benefit or council tax benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(2) He shall pay— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) rent rebate subsidy to each housing authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) rent allowance subsidy to each local authority; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (c) council tax benefit subsidy to each billing authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] and to each local authority in Scotland[^M_F_aabe96c3-5ebd-47eb-ad6f-6b9fe04187b1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2].

(3) In the following provisions of this Part “subsidy”, without more, refers to subsidy of any of those descriptions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
