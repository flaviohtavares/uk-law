> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (192).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/5

## 139. Arrangements for community charge benefits (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Council tax benefit[^M_F_c1668ee2-924e-49ce-8716-37cf70612871] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] provided for by virtue of a scheme under section 123 of the Contributions and Benefits Act (in this Act referred to as a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] council tax benefit scheme[^M_F_b8a9b5e2-b3a3-4dd3-f42b-a74e442b82b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]) is to be administered by the appropriate authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(2)[^M_F_f03748d4-87ec-4c63-a54d-4004f9ef1558] For the purposes of this section the appropriate authority is the billing authority or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] local authority in Scotland[^M_F_bc26e351-9939-415c-f7a5-56071f873075] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] which levied the council tax as regards which a person is entitled to the benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(4)[^M_F_10221739-f7ae-4463-db20-1384581a7ad0] Nothing in this section shall be read as excluding the general provisions of the Local Government Act 1972 or the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 from applying in relation to the council tax benefit functions of a local authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(6) A billing authority[^M_F_765e8069-acbb-42c5-f979-b35253d7093a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] local authority in Scotland[^M_F_f9a1488c-66ec-4c32-a4ae-5ea8a0bc9739] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] may modify any part of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] council tax benefit scheme[^M_F_585ed31c-ae1b-49be-dc47-a2ddcb4e68c2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] administered by the authority— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) so as to provide for disregarding, in determining a person’s income, the whole or part of any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] prescribed[^M_F_f4c0a109-df8e-4f69-a24c-7feef0370283] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] war disablement pension or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] prescribed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] war widow’s pension payable to that person or to his partner or to a person to whom he is polygamously married; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) to such extent in other respects as may be prescribed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

and any such modifications may be adopted by resolution of an authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(7) Modifications other than such modifications as are mentioned in subsection (6)(a) above shall be so framed as to secure that, in the estimate of the authority adopting them, the total of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] the amount of benefit which will be paid by them in any year will not exceed the permitted total or any subsidiary limit specified by order of the Secretary of State[^M_F_ccb31866-74fc-43f2-da6b-61acfdc61247] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2].

(8) An authority which has adopted modifications may by resolution revoke or vary them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(9) If the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] council tax benefit scheme[^M_F_d12f1764-3898-4d9d-95e8-f07451ebf0d5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] includes power for an authority to exercise a discretion in allowing *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] council tax benefit[^M_F_c2d14d64-a76a-48d9-a7f6-b0c2893f03b4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2], the authority shall not exercise that discretion so that the total of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] the amount of benefit paid by them in any year exceeds the permitted total or any subsidiary limit specified by order of the Secretary of State.[^M_F_1600373c-3c9b-4b2a-cb88-709d0c79d9f9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(10)[^M_F_51d4dca4-9539-4848-fcca-369c537ed1eb] The Secretary of State– *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) shall by order specify the permitted total of council tax benefit payable by any authority in any year; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) may by order specify one or more subsidiary limits on the amount of council tax benefit payable by any authority in any year in respect of any matter or matters specified in the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

The power to specify the permitted total or a subsidiary limit may be exercised by fixing an amount or by providing rules for its calculation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(11) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- “modifications” includes additions, omissions and amendments, and related expressions shall be construed accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- “partner”, in relation to a person, means the other member of the couple concerned; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- [^M_F_09787390-afc2-46bd-fee4-54a2afc3969d]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “war widow's pension” includes any corresponding pension payable to a widower or surviving civil partner[^M_F_346523b9-8583-405d-ecd5-40505a1f66b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2].
