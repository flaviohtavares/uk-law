## 140B. Calculation of amount of subsidy (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_C_1c4c844b-d642-4832-99aa-d2e679fc5cdd][^M_F_29c38a12-82bb-4169-b53c-df2f913609fd][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The amount of subsidy to be paid to an authority shall be *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] determined in accordance with an[^M_F_b76b089f-0b4d-4230-ff87-669fa13eb138] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] order made by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(2) Subject as follows, the amount of subsidy shall be calculated by reference to the amount of relevant benefit paid by the authority during the year *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] [^M_F_11ebec2a-4e78-4da8-ca88-f48560ae5f8a]...

[^M_F_23a60a54-fed6-4078-8804-d0d7e63dd58d]...

(3) The order may provide that the amount of subsidy in respect of any matter shall be a fixed sum or shall be nil. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(4)[^M_F_7c8f59e3-7cdb-457f-87b2-c26138fbe7d0] The Secretary of State may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) pay as part of subsidy an additional amount specified by, or calculated in a manner specified by, the order; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) deduct from the amount which would otherwise be payable by way of subsidy an amount specified by, or calculated in a manner specified by, the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(4A) The additional amounts which may be paid by virtue of subsection (4)(a) above include amounts in respect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) the costs of administering the relevant benefit; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) success in preventing or detecting fraud relating to the relevant benefit or action to be taken with a view to preventing or detecting such fraud. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(5) The Secretary of State may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) where an application is made by an authority on his invitation, pay to the authority as part of the subsidy such additional amount as he considers appropriate in respect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
  - (i) success in preventing or detecting fraud relating to the relevant benefit; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] action to be taken with a view to preventing or detecting such fraud; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) deduct from the subsidy which would otherwise be payable to an authority such amount as he considers it unreasonable to pay by way of subsidy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(5A) The amounts which may be deducted by virtue of subsection (4)(b) or (5)(b) above include amounts in respect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) a failure to comply with directions under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] section 139D(3A) or (3B)[^M_F_3067780f-6af2-4a0c-83e3-f7333dc218ec] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] above; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) other failures in preventing or detecting fraud relating to the relevant benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(6) In this section “relevant benefit” means housing benefit or council tax benefit, as the case may be. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(7)[^M_F_6aad0641-4b18-4cbf-8835-13c5398084ee] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8) The amount of subsidy payable to an authority shall be calculated to the nearest pound, disregarding an odd amount of 50 pence or less and treating an odd amount exceeding 50 pence as a whole pound. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
