## 139F. Enforcement notices (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_bf9c9eab-a0d7-4a92-f3d1-86d3939bd7a0][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where directions have been given to an authority under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] section 139D(3A) or (3B)[^M_F_50816b98-a88b-4b7b-e918-a3e432980452] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] above and the Secretary of State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) is not satisfied that the authority has attained the standards which it has been directed to attain; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_7c9e5173-17fa-48d7-c9fe-01294dfcd675][^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] is not satisfied that the authority has taken the action which it has been directed to take; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) is not satisfied that the authority is likely to attain those standards *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] or take that action[^M_F_4f63de7c-ab62-45ad-a920-351a950da8f1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] within the time specified in the directions, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

he may serve on the authority a written notice under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(2) The notice shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) identify the directions and state why the Secretary of State is not satisfied as mentioned in paragraph (a) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2], (aa)[^M_F_a264a273-68d0-44c7-d811-90ac1fc0b12a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] or (b) of subsection (1) above; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) require the authority to submit a written response to the Secretary of State within a time specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(3) If any person (other than the authority) carrying out work relating to the administration of benefit may be affected by any determination which may be made under section 139G below, the authority shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) consult that person before submitting its response; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) include in its response any relevant observations made by that person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(4) If the notice identifies directions under section 139D(3A),[^M_F_6d198c3c-329b-4b27-b51b-dce59e2f7b0f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2] the authority’s response shall either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) state that the authority has attained the standards, or is likely to attain them within the time specified in the directions, and justify that statement; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) state that the authority has not attained the standards, or is not likely to attain them within that time, and (if the authority wishes) give reasons why a determination under section 139G below should not be made or should not include any particular provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(4A)[^M_F_56badff9-24b4-4ef9-fe67-7fa021eb0462] If the notice identifies directions under section 139D(3B), the authority’s response shall either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

- (a) state that the authority has taken the action, or is likely to take it within the time specified in the directions, and justify that statement; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
- (b) state that the authority has not taken the action, or is not likely to take it within that time, and (if the authority wishes) give reasons why a determination under section 139G below should not be made or should not include any particular provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(5) The notice may relate to any one or more matters covered by the directions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(6) The serving of a notice under this section relating to any directions or matter does not prevent the serving of further notices under this section relating to the same directions or matter. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]

(7) In this section “benefit” means housing benefit or council tax benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_a83c7a24-87b0-4ff7-a744-d05145be56f2]
