## 128A. Disclosure of information by authorities[^M_F_e1c36f24-5716-4485-dba1-362ca7b54c67]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^M_F_01d7f55b-6bac-4ead-a9e8-40cbf6f97af4] Regulations may make provision requiring the disclosure by one authority (“the disclosing authority”) to another authority (“the receiving authority”), in prescribed circumstances, of information of a prescribed description obtained by the disclosing authority in respect of persons who have been entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance or to income support. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_01d7f55b-6bac-4ead-a9e8-40cbf6f97af4]

(2) The regulations may in particular provide for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_01d7f55b-6bac-4ead-a9e8-40cbf6f97af4]

- (a) information to be disclosed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_01d7f55b-6bac-4ead-a9e8-40cbf6f97af4]
  - (i) at the request of the receiving authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_01d7f55b-6bac-4ead-a9e8-40cbf6f97af4]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_01d7f55b-6bac-4ead-a9e8-40cbf6f97af4] at the request of any person who falls within a prescribed category; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_01d7f55b-6bac-4ead-a9e8-40cbf6f97af4]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_01d7f55b-6bac-4ead-a9e8-40cbf6f97af4] otherwise than in response to such a request; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_01d7f55b-6bac-4ead-a9e8-40cbf6f97af4]
- (b) the period within which information is to be disclosed; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_01d7f55b-6bac-4ead-a9e8-40cbf6f97af4]
- (c) information to be disclosed only if it has been obtained by the disclosing authority in the exercise of any of their functions in relation to housing benefit or council tax benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_01d7f55b-6bac-4ead-a9e8-40cbf6f97af4]
