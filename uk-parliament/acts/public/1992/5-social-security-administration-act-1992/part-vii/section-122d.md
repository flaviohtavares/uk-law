## 122D. Supply of information by authorities administering benefit (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_C_2297c20a-1278-4448-f40c-45c401873543][^M_C_f5601360-2966-4b24-e1ef-ddb7a71aaba1][^M_C_d9e6fa0d-5fb1-409a-9a42-f87ae3331040][^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f][^M_F_90753993-0164-476c-9b7a-1e42a7f9fa89][^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State or the Northern Ireland Department may require— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

- (a) an authority administering housing benefit or council tax benefit; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
- (b) a person authorised to exercise any function of such an authority relating to such a benefit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

to supply *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] relevant benefit information[^M_F_2d3bd994-1c99-41ee-f3d4-56c9e74b0dbe] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] held by the authority or other person to, or to a person providing services to, the Secretary of State or the Northern Ireland Department for use for any purpose relating to social security *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f], child support or war pensions,[^M_F_44107dff-5c8a-4555-acd3-08fdeab44a29] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] employment or training, private pensions policy or retirement planning[^M_F_4fd53ea6-2417-4cca-93c5-083f60095047] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f].

(2) The Secretary of State or the Northern Ireland Department may require— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

- (a) an authority administering housing benefit or council tax benefit; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
- (b) a person authorised to exercise any function of such an authority relating to such a benefit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

to supply benefit policy information held by the authority or other person to, or to a person providing services to, the Secretary of State or the Northern Ireland Department. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

(2A)[^M_F_4d6acded-8499-41b3-b1e3-ec3787e004ec] Information supplied under subsection (2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f], in addition to any other purpose for which the information may be used,[^M_F_347ee015-167c-4001-d492-0d686e5531b3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] may be used for any purpose relating to private pensions policy or retirement planning. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

(3) Information shall be supplied under subsection (1) or (2) above in such manner and form, and in accordance with such requirements, as may be *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] specified in directions given by the Secretary of State or, as the case may be, the Northern Ireland Department[^M_F_b085cdb4-0743-4f11-d4f5-a0c271651656] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f].

(4)[^M_F_791d5420-bb10-406e-dc4f-7a0094f5b48e] In subsection (1) “relevant benefit information”, in relation to an authority or other person, means any information which is relevant to the exercise of any function relating to a relevant social security benefit by the authority or other person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

(5) In subsection (2) above “benefit policy information” means any information which may be relevant to the Secretary of State or the Northern Ireland Department— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

- (a) in preparing estimates of likely future expenditure on *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] any relevant social security benefit[^M_F_a3c74455-040c-4daa-9205-359fbc79e3b6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
- (b) in developing policy relating to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] any relevant social security benefit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f].

(6)[^M_F_e77ed7cd-d6b5-4fc4-8068-49832b3b452e] In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

- “private pensions policy” means policy relating to occupational pension schemes or personal pension schemes (within the meaning given by section 1 of the Pension Schemes Act 1993); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
- “relevant social security benefit” has the same meaning as in section 121DA above;[^M_F_d7615f88-bab5-4415-e322-d0ae02728a8e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
- “retirement planning” means promoting financial planning for retirement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
