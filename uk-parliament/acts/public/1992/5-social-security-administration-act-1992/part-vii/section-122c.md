# Social Security Administration Act 1992, section 122C

## 122C. Supply of information to authorities administering benefit[^M_C_b18c58e1-a0d7-4b88-fc71-a360eb546e8c][^M_C_d9e6fa0d-5fb1-409a-9a42-f87ae3331040][^M_F_90753993-0164-476c-9b7a-1e42a7f9fa89]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] This section applies to information relating to social security *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f], child support or war pensions, or employment or training,[^M_F_8f4d3445-150f-4d55-f1c3-805d46932eb7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] which is held— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

- (a) by the Secretary of State or the Northern Ireland Department; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
- (b) by a person providing services to the Secretary of State or the Northern Ireland Department in connection with the provision of those services. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

(2) Information to which this section applies may be supplied to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

- (a) an authority administering housing benefit or council tax benefit; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
- (b) a person authorised to exercise any function of such an authority relating to such a benefit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

for use in the administration of such a benefit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] or for the purposes of anything the authority is permitted to do in relation to any other benefit by virtue of section 110A or 116A above[^M_F_1e31e84c-c46d-4ee0-e98f-532d7ebdbdfd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f].

(3) But where information to which this section applies has been supplied to the Secretary of State, the Northern Ireland Department or the person providing services under section 122 or 122B above, it may only be supplied under subsection (2) above— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

- (a) for use in the prevention, detection, investigation or prosecution of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] benefit offences (within the meaning of Part 6 above)[^M_F_cbce6278-d73c-4038-f044-6a11e86e81f5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
- (b) for use in checking the accuracy of information relating to housing benefit or to council tax benefit and (where appropriate) amending or supplementing such information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

(4) The Secretary of State or the Northern Ireland Department— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

- (a) may impose conditions on the use of information supplied under subsection (2) above; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
- (b) may charge a reasonable fee in respect of the cost of supplying information under that subsection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

(5) Where information is supplied to an authority or other person under subsection (2) above, the authority or other person shall have regard to it in the exercise of any function relating to housing benefit or council tax benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

(6) Information supplied under subsection (2) above shall not be supplied by the recipient to any other person or body unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

- (a) it is supplied— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
  - (i) by an authority to a person authorised to exercise any function of the authority relating to housing benefit or council tax benefit; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] by a person authorised to exercise any function of an authority relating to such a benefit to the authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
- (b) it is supplied for the purposes of any civil or criminal proceedings relating to the Contributions and Benefits Act, the Jobseekers Act 1995 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f], Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2007[^M_F_a9529b0e-6896-4819-e216-886c2cba929e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f], Part 4 of the Welfare Reform Act 2012[^key-e80001c9a67771740cc9eb24f9b63709] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] or this Act or to any provision of Northern Ireland legislation corresponding to any of them; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
- (c) it is supplied under section 122D or 122E below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

(7) This section does not limit the circumstances in which information may be supplied apart from this section (in particular by reason of section 122(4) or 122B(4) above). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

(8)[^M_F_e8fd0236-c628-48ff-e0f1-7ef38ba49a8a] In this section and section 122D below “war pension” has the same meaning as in section 25 of the Social Security Act 1989. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
