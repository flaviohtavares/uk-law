# Social Security Administration Act 1992, section 121E

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (192).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/5

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 121E. Supply of contributions etc. information held by Inland Revenue[^c24148341]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to information which is held for the purposes of functions relating to contributions, health in pregnancy grant,[^c-6402cfaf1c94c3d6c100e5a708903e0d] statutory sick pay or statutory maternity pay or functions under Part III of the Pensions Act[^c24148151]—

- (a) by the Inland Revenue, or
- (b) by a person providing services to them, in connection with the provision of those services.

(2)[^c-20225a10199f769aaff101cb4cd4a77f] Information to which this section applies may, and [^M_F_6a6f8617-de3a-45e0-d471-75a664a45176]... must if an authorised officer so requires, be supplied—

- (a) to the Secretary of State, or
- (b) to a person providing services to the Secretary of State,

for use for the purposes of functions relating to [^M_F_6a6f8617-de3a-45e0-d471-75a664a45176]... war pensions [^M_F_6a6f8617-de3a-45e0-d471-75a664a45176]...

(2ZA) Information to which this section applies may, and [^M_F_1ceaf1ba-fdd5-438e-b9d8-366d23740e48]... must if an authorised officer so requires, be supplied—

- (a) to the Northern Ireland Department, or
- (b) to a person providing services to that Department,

for use for the purposes of functions relating to [^M_F_1ceaf1ba-fdd5-438e-b9d8-366d23740e48]... child support [^M_F_1ceaf1ba-fdd5-438e-b9d8-366d23740e48]...

(2A)[^M_F_bb155eef-ba17-441b-8887-18ca67c21281] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In this section[^c-8f56ec54353b95fa839b17e5a05343ba] “**authorised officer**” means an officer of the Secretary of State or the Northern Ireland Department authorised for the purposes of this section by the Secretary of State or the Northern Ireland Department.
