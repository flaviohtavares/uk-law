## 122E. Supply of information between authorities administering benefit (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_C_60db814b-85ac-487a-91ed-6a47f1842023][^M_C_d9e6fa0d-5fb1-409a-9a42-f87ae3331040][^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f][^M_F_90753993-0164-476c-9b7a-1e42a7f9fa89][^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] relevant benefit information[^M_F_666e26d2-2277-49ef-cdcc-aa5fc5db94e3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] which is held by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

- (a) an authority administering housing benefit or council tax benefit; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
- (b) a person authorised to exercise any function of such an authority relating to such a benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

(2) Information to which this section applies may be supplied to another such authority or person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

- (a) for use in the prevention, detection, investigation or prosecution of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] benefit offences (within the meaning of Part 6 above)[^M_F_f4bd7e0f-d4fd-48ea-d578-d91e2c2d6584] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
- (b) for use in checking the accuracy of information relating to housing benefit or to council tax benefit and (where appropriate) amending or supplementing such information. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

(3) The Secretary of State or the Northern Ireland Department may require information to which this section applies and which is of a prescribed description to be supplied in prescribed circumstances to another such authority or person for use in the administration of housing benefit or council tax benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

(4) Information shall be supplied under subsection (3) above in such manner and form, and in accordance with such requirements, as may be *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f] specified in directions given by the Secretary of State or, as the case may be, the Northern Ireland Department[^M_F_4f72df23-ed7c-400f-804d-5847681bbea7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f].

(5) Where information supplied under subsection (2) or (3) above has been used in amending or supplementing other information, it is lawful for it to be— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

- (a) supplied to any person or body to whom that other information could be supplied; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
- (b) used for any purpose for which that other information could be used. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

(6)[^M_F_992ec0b9-c5b2-4b4d-95da-ae8ba2bc7d53] In this section “relevant benefit information”, in relation to an authority or other person, means any information which is relevant to the exercise of any function relating to a relevant social security benefit (within the meaning of section 121DA above) by the authority or other person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]

(7) This section does not limit the circumstances in which information may be supplied apart from this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_2994fb96-33ef-4f6e-d9f5-c1e74d194a5f]
