## 6. Regulations about community charge benefits administration

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613] Regulations may provide as follows as regards *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613] council tax benefit[^M_F_8d701a45-8bb2-4777-f003-bd62f0a48f64] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]—

- (a) for requiring a claim for a benefit to be made by such person, in such manner and within such time as may be prescribed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (b) for treating a claim made in such circumstances as may be prescribed as having been made at such date earlier or later than that at which it is made as may be prescribed; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (c) for permitting a claim to be made, or treated as if made, for a period wholly or partly after the date on which it is made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (d) for permitting an award on a claim to be made for such a period subject to the condition that the claimant satisfies the requirements for entitlement when benefit becomes payable, or any right to a reduction *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613] [^M_F_a9b312d6-fb6b-49e5-c0d4-81b6f72787f4]... becomes available, under the award; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (e) for a review of any award if those requirements are found not to have been satisfied; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (f) for the disallowance on any ground of a person's claim for a benefit to be treated as a disallowance of any further claim by that person for that benefit until the grounds of the original disallowance have ceased to exist; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (g) for enabling one person to act for another in relation to a claim for a benefit and for enabling such a claim to be made and proceeded with in the name of a person who has died; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (h) for requiring any information or evidence needed for the determination of a claim or of any question arising in connection with a claim to be furnished by such person as may be prescribed in accordance with the regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (hh (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_22ffab36-81e4-4518-9766-49e87304cada][^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613] for requiring such person as may be prescribed in accordance with the regulations to furnish any information or evidence needed for a determination whether a decision on an award of a benefit— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
  - (i) should be revised under paragraph 3 of Schedule 7 to the Child Support, Pensions and Social Security Act 2000; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613] should be superseded under paragraph 4 of that Schedule; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (i) for the time when and manner in which any benefit (or part) which takes the form of a payment is to be paid, and for the information and evidence to be furnished in connection with the payment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (j) for the time when the right to make a reduction *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613] [^M_F_b94283b8-22bf-410e-8a9b-9f07a4a294c8]... may be exercised; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (k) for notice to be given of any change of circumstances affecting the continuance of entitlement to a benefit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (l) for the day on which entitlement to a benefit is to begin or end; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (m) for calculating the amount of a benefit according to a prescribed scale or otherwise adjusting it so as to avoid fractional amounts or facilitate computation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (n)[^M_F_01d161c4-ffdc-4c7f-e252-6dda9cdcddb8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (o)[^M_F_aa1dedba-3045-48d4-d669-52dddfaa9381] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (p) in the case of any benefit (or part) which takes the form of a payment, for payment or distribution to or among persons claiming to be entitled on the death of any person, and for dispensing with strict proof of their title; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (q) in the case of any benefit (or part) which takes the form of a payment, for the circumstances and manner in which payment may be made to one person on behalf of another for any purpose, which may be to discharge, in whole or in part, an obligation of the person entitled to the benefit or any other person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (r) for making a payment on account of a benefit, or conferring a right to make a reduction *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613] [^M_F_148dbf33-cd6f-4b36-b615-1d2e958216e1]... on account, where no claim has been made and it is impracticable for one to be made immediately; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (s) for making a payment on account of a benefit, or conferring a right to make a reduction *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613] [^M_F_afd1ab81-9966-4ad5-c44e-2a89d97205b7]... on account, where a claim has been made but it is impracticable for the claim or an appeal, reference, review or application relating to it to be determined immediately; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (t) for making a payment on account of a benefit, or conferring a right to make a reduction *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613] [^M_F_c956ba26-4162-4cad-908a-d083f643945e]... on account, where an award has been made but it is impracticable to institute the benefit immediately; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
- (u) generally as to administration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]

(2) Regulations under this section may include *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613] provision in relation to council tax benefit that prescribed provisions shall apply instead of prescribed provisions of Part I or II of the Local Government Finance Act 1992, or that prescribed provisions of either of those Parts shall not apply[^M_F_a3a9527c-12a7-434f-da40-2303aa8ada2c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613] or shall apply subject to prescribed amendments or adaptations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]

(3)[^M_F_9ea3f873-9265-4648-b994-81b01b4cabd8] References in subsection (2) above to either of the Parts there mentioned include references to regulations made under the Part concerned. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3263974f9b2cce5f09583cf0f32f4613]
