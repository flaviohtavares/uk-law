## 7. Relationship between community charge benefits and other (affected by repeal, see note) benefits[^c-910175ab547afe4a6e11a541ceb7de41]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulations may provide for a claim for one relevant benefit to be treated, either in the alternative or in addition, as a claim for any other relevant benefit that may be prescribed.

(2)[^c-c49eef04aba2fae319874ba3d3328e03] Regulations may provide for treating a payment made or right conferred by virtue of regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c49eef04aba2fae319874ba3d3328e03]

- (a)[^c-d5d4aa20068ada1c139d8c702ab48b4c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) under section 6(l)(r) to (t) above, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c49eef04aba2fae319874ba3d3328e03]

as made or conferred on account of any relevant benefit that is subsequently awarded or paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c49eef04aba2fae319874ba3d3328e03]

(3) For the purposes of subsection (1)[^c-77a413e3fb2504bb7afabda5d328932d] above relevant benefits are—

- (a) any benefit to which section 5 above applies; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fe9e141509a0fe1417ac701ce11c5d9d]
- (b) council tax benefit[^c24142391] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fe9e141509a0fe1417ac701ce11c5d9d].
