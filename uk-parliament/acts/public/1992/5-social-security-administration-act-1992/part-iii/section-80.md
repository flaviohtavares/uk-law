## 80. Child benefit—overlap with benefits under legislation of ... member States[^c-ab0eea340dd7835b00e121d3eb75c650]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations may provide for adjusting child benefit payable in respect of any child or qualifying young person[^c-2e8b6693f89c8fc72676fed9a519cff7] in respect of whom any benefit is payable under the legislation of any member State[^c-e15b4ed4ad5492f152a00f142a9bd052].
