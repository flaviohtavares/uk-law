## 181. Impersonation of officers

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If any person, with intent to deceive, falsely represents himself to be a person authorised by the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions[^key-5e66b64617d2bf34dd0a8544b48ece54] to act in any capacity (whether under this Act or otherwise) he shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale.
