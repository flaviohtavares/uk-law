# Social Security Administration Act 1992, section 182C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (192).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/5
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 182C. Requirement to apply for national insurance number[^c24156771]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulations may make provision requiring a person to apply for a national insurance number to be allocated to him.

(1A)[^c24156741] Regulations under subsection (1) above may require the application to be made to the Secretary of State or to the Inland Revenue.

(2) An application required by regulations under subsection (1) above shall be accompanied by information or evidence enabling such a number to be allocated.
