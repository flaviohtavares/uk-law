# Local Government Finance Act 1992, section 113

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/14

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 113. Orders and regulations.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any power of the Secretary of State [^key-cf1493167db4c835a1a907f00f2b164f]...  , the Treasury or the[^c18816581] Welsh Ministers[^key-bdd2ac9808a8dcfe35fc0e79d14e0c1c] under this Act to make orders or regulations (other than the power to make orders under section 52X(6)[^c12081701] above) may be so exercised as to make different provision for different cases or descriptions of case, including different provision for different areas or for different authorities.

(2) Any power of the Secretary of State [^key-3b9fa970b689ea79142fb60e2117d6f5]...  , the Treasury or the Welsh Ministers[^key-c668465448b782c0495a7826fe2b6a31] under this Act to make orders or regulations includes power to make such incidental, consequential, transitional or supplementary provision as he or they think[^key-83236e0e7cd355fdb4a9bd99140e1b7f] necessary or expedient.

(3) Any power of the Secretary of State [^key-b2aa0ee3d0578dd2e5979d69c4b57d08]... or the Treasury under this Act to make orders or regulations shall be exercisable by statutory instrument which, except in the case of regulations under section[^key-bd49e4f788ee40be8de8a25f8d335d08] 11D(3), *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-4d556c7295d16c2b288cc8b3c45abf2f] 14A, 14B, 14C or *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-12c730eca0268a6366b438e604bb29d9] 52ZQ above or orders under—

- (a) section 5(4), 11(3), 22B(1A),[^c18859741] [^key-406409158ef4ac49bcbe8703a2cb9556]... 74(3) or 79(3) above;
- (b) section 119(2) below; or
- (c) paragraph 1 of Schedule 12 to this Act,

shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(4)[^c18816771] Any power of the Welsh Ministers[^key-cd2cf65dede89e7e66b592509340d428] under this Act to make orders or regulations shall be exercisable by statutory instrument.

*Extent: W*

(5)[^key-38c51c4003d3382dd2a29b1aa9666719] Paragraphs 33 and 34 of Schedule 11 to the Government of Wales Act 2006 make provision about the Senedd Cymru procedures that apply to any statutory instrument containing regulations or an order made in exercise of functions conferred upon the Secretary of State or the National Assembly for Wales by this Act that have been transferred to the Welsh Ministers by virtue of paragraph 30 of that Schedule.
