# Local Government Finance Act 1992, section 52W

## 52W. Meaning of budget requirement.[^c12078881]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any reference in this Chapter to the amount calculated (or already calculated) by a major precepting authority [^key-f3e360729522384e96c942e73e9d91ba]... as its budget requirement for a financial year is a reference to the amount calculated by it in relation to the year under section 43(4) above.

(2)[^key-a58dbd6264a6ff288cc0aeafd9fcc64d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Any reference in this Chapter to the amount calculated (or already calculated) by a billing authority as its budget requirement for a financial year is a reference to the amount calculated by it in relation to the year under section 32(4) above; but this is subject to section 52X below.
