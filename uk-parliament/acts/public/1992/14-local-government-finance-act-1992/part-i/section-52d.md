# Local Government Finance Act 1992, section 52D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/14

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 52D. Designation or nomination.[^c12078881]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if in the Welsh Ministers'[^c-0569469358414d94f4dc0e42f4d6b471] opinion (reached after applying section 52B above) the amount calculated by an authority as its budget requirement for the year under consideration is excessive.

(2) In such a case they[^c-edb1f429709d4bb8e223935225809e1e] may—

- (a) designate the authority as regards the year under consideration, or
- (b) nominate the authority.

(3) The Welsh Ministers[^c-47fa54737c0f0062f4ab7edce67a990e] may proceed under different paragraphs of subsection (2) above in relation to different authorities.
