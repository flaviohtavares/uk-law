# Local Government Finance Act 1992, section 52A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 52A. Interpretation.[^c12078881]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Chapter a reference to an authority is to—

- (a) a billing authority in Wales[^c-91ac315c7363cf4c796f0ec28ef1ac13], or
- (b) a major precepting authority in Wales[^c-dd1a8664edd8347fc8f6b1b227e7e473].

(2) Sections 52W and 52X below contain other interpretative provisions for the purposes of this Chapter.
