# Local Government Finance Act 1992, section 52H

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 52H. No challenge or acceptance.[^c12078881]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if the period mentioned in section 52E(5) above ends without an authority designated under section 52D(2)(a) above informing the Welsh Ministers[^key-1ca45265b6e41296d7010789b9c5ae46] by notice in writing under paragraph (a) or (b) of section 52E(5).

(2) As soon as is reasonably practicable after the period ends the Welsh Ministers[^key-d5c12e37db87e2c1b9a832f46c90bc6c] shall make an order stating the amount which the amount calculated by the authority as its budget requirement for the year is not to exceed; and the amount stated shall be that stated in the notice under section 52E(2)(d) above.

(3) An order under this section—

- (a) shall not be made unless a draft of it has been laid before and approved by resolution of the National Assembly for Wales[^key-1fa0294e4d560636c43564fb0407f8fa];
- (b) may relate to two or more authorities.

(4) As soon as is reasonably practicable after an order under this section is made the Welsh Ministers[^key-f4756cdbfb903dff91a150cf9be8cf26] shall serve on the authority (or each authority) a notice stating the amount stated in the case of the authority concerned in the order.

(5) When they serve[^key-48c86918830bef901bfb85139ba28cae] a notice under subsection (4) above on a precepting authority the Welsh Ministers[^key-a833e9b9e2bb4d1e908d93847968d6ca] shall also serve a copy of it on each billing authority to which the precepting authority has power to issue a precept.
