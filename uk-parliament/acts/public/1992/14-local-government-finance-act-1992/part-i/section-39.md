## 39. Precepting and precepted authorities.[^c12076951][^key-d90517246149da64fabb511dec0113fa][^key-f0b320cd782925eff458a347aa70a3e6][^key-bb7f6b996e608a693d568e2b4cc6cc54]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Each of the following is a major precepting authority for the purposes of this Part, namely—

- (a) a county council in England[^c12076961];
- (aa)[^c12076971] the Greater London Authority;
- (ab)[^key-4eebcc4da8ca948802c4f51da01d8b7a][^key-538901fa375b5ed2f17cdf83cda65a49] a mayoral combined authority, as defined by section 107A(8) of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009;
- (ac)[^key-1a45a37e0ead09035cc716c9963fe2ff] a mayoral CCA, as defined by section 27(8) of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023 (mayoral combined county authorities);
- (b)[^key-99a8033b359ac9f5ca31fa8877a2f5c0] a police and crime commissioner;
- (d) a metropolitan county fire and civil defence authority;
- (da)[^c18856811] a fire and rescue authority in England constituted by a scheme under section 2 of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004 or a scheme to which section 4 of that Act applies;
- (db)[^key-88bfc402a1b346082ebdde9dcae625c8] a fire and rescue authority created by an order under section 4A of that Act;
- (e)[^c12077001] the London Fire and Civil Defence Authority; and
- (f)[^c12077011] the Receiver for the Metropolitan Police District.

(2) Each of the following is a local precepting authority for the purposes of this Part, namely—

- (a) the sub-treasurer of the Inner Temple;
- (b) the under-treasurer of the Middle Temple;
- (c) a parish or community council;
- (d) the chairman of a parish meeting; and
- (e) charter trustees.

(3) A precept may only be issued to an appropriate billing authority.

(4) If the whole or part of a billing authority’s area falls within a precepting authority’s area, it is an appropriate billing authority in relation to the precepting authority to the extent of the area which so falls.

(5)[^c12077021] In respect of the financial year beginning on 1st April 2000, and subsequent financial years, for the purposes of Chapter IV of this Part,

- (a) the Receiver for the Metropolitan Police District’s area shall be the area of the Inner London boroughs;
- (b) the Receiver shall only issue precepts to the councils of the Inner London boroughs.
