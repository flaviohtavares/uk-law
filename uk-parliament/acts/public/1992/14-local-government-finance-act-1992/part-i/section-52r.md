# Local Government Finance Act 1992, section 52R

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/14

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 52R. Acceptance of maximum amount.[^c12078881]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) an authority designated under section 52M above informs the Welsh Ministers[^key-856b16bd92a2eb68b31afe59c446d661] by notice in writing under subsection (8)(b) of that section, or
- (b) an authority designated under section 52P above informs the Welsh Ministers[^key-1679be3339a4d188e00fe926fe1ae9c2] by notice in writing under subsection (8)(b) of that section.

(2) As soon as is reasonably practicable after they receive[^key-ee9d4dd0b8916b3e09db8fcf4e28df60] the notice the Welsh Ministers[^key-79f05cb6e6ff22915878a5542733197a] shall serve on the authority a notice stating the amount which the amount calculated by it as its budget requirement for the year is not to exceed; and the amount stated shall be that stated in the notice under section 52M(4)(b) or 52P(4)(b) above (as the case may be).

(3) When they serve[^key-16c03637c53ed1d2f1f7a85551779ee2] a notice under subsection (2) above on a precepting authority the Welsh Ministers[^key-2d73130ee905a0a347dd5974c6396cc1] shall also serve a copy of it on each billing authority to which the precepting authority has power to issue a precept.
