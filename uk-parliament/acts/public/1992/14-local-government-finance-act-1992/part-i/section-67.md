## 67. Functions to be discharged only by authority.[^c-8d3a5cc104e8bb2fea7476f4d74a6994][^c-90cd387516e88f9820eea0caf466e4ea][^c-8d3a5cc104e8bb2fea7476f4d74a6994][^c-7e53f966bffc21336e2f9fd9385d6c74][^c-90cd387516e88f9820eea0caf466e4ea]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to subsections (2A) to (3A) below,[^c18796431] each of the functions of an authority mentioned in subsection (2) below shall be discharged only by the authority.

(2) The functions are—

- (a) making a determination under section 8(2) , 11A[^c18796571] , 11B[^c-5d2f905d46788f737f1a66856feb4302] , 11C[^c-db648931db3cf4bf34e740596fb1ee0a] ,[^c-4dcd8dffd074801ecb218efd3a092541] 11F[^c-ab5e560da19c935140c1e7b136cb67e4], 12A or 12B above;
- (aa)[^c-ed2366b7046d3a2651ce9f29e4a8143b] making or revising a council tax reduction scheme under section 13A(2);
- (b) making a calculation in accordance with any of sections 31A[^c-11bc874eab8a7ce1fcc4f91429be32e3] to 37 or section 52ZF,[^c-4b821530266ea0f4b5631125d7109519] 52I or 52T[^c12079711] or any of sections 42A[^c-81f6a839ce702fcce2f4dd67881ccb5f] to 51 or section 52ZJ,[^c-0f6bb522c4f2d33a7fcd737f6279594e] 52J or 52U above, whether originally or by way of substitute;
- (bb)[^c12079731] making a calculation in accordance with any of sections 85 to 90 of the Greater London Authority Act 1999;
- (bc) making a substitute calculation in accordance with any of sections 85, 86 and 88 to 90 of, and Schedule 7 to, that Act;
- (c) setting an amount of council tax for a financial year under Chapter III of this Part, whether originally or by way of substitute; and
- (d) issuing a precept under Chapter IV of this Part, whether originally or by way of substitute.

(2A)[^c18796461] Subsection (1) does not apply to the following functions—

- (za)[^c-311f2484ff5893c0f30cf4fe576781ec] the determination of an amount for item T in section 31B(1) above;
- (a) the determination of an amount for item T in section 33(1) above;
- (b) the determination of an amount for item TP in section 34(3) above;
- (ba)[^c-437cad12cfccbc73c1aa52a1ad072913] the determination of an amount for item T in section 42B(1) above;
- (c) the determination of an amount for item T in section 44(1) above;
- (d) the determination of an amount for item TP in section 45(3) above;
- (e) the determination of an amount for item TP in section 48(3) or (4) above;
- (f) the determination of an amount for item T in section 88(2) of the Greater London Authority Act 1999 (c. 29);
- (g) the determination of an amount for item TP2 in section 89(4) of that Act;
- (h) the determination of an amount required for determining an amount for the item mentioned in paragraph (c), (d), (f) or (g) above.

(3) Subject to subsection (3B) below,[^c12079741] the functions of an authority mentioned in subsection (2)(ba) or (c)[^c-595dd4956cdfee54f84a38a1f253e47b] above may, if the authority so directs, be exercised by a committee of the authority appointed by it for that purpose; and as respects a committee so appointed—

- (a) the number of members and their term of office shall be fixed by the authority; and
- (b) each member shall be a member of the authority.

(3A)[^c12079751] In the case of the Greater London Authority, the functions mentioned in subsection (2) above shall be discharged on behalf of the Authority in accordance with the provisions of the Greater London Authority Act 1999 but only by the Mayor of London, the London Assembly or the Mayor and Assembly acting jointly.

(3B) Subsection (3) above does not apply in relation to the Greater London Authority, but where Schedule 6 to the Greater London Authority Act 1999 makes provision enabling a function to be discharged by a committee or other representatives of the London Assembly, the function may be discharged by such a committee or representatives in accordance with the provisions of that Schedule.

(4) Part VA (access to meetings and documents of certain authorities, committees and sub-committees) of the [^c12079761]Local Government Act 1972 shall apply in relation to a committee appointed under subsection (3) above as it applies in relation to a committee appointed under section 102 of that Act.
