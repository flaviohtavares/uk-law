# Local Government Finance Act 1992, section 52G

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 52G. Acceptance of maximum amount.[^c12078881]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if an authority designated under section 52D(2)(a) above informs the Welsh Ministers[^c-2bfd5625b406b3d630de3a1dff0c42c6] by notice in writing under section 52E(5)(b) above.

(2) As soon as is reasonably practicable after they receive[^c-5387c2ec4ec483b5e98024b2f328c686] the notice the Welsh Ministers[^c-8c725104c58ea303436534bd6d2d8a3c] shall serve on the authority a notice stating the amount which the amount calculated by it as its budget requirement for the year is not to exceed; and the amount stated shall be that stated in the notice under section 52E(2)(d) above.

(3) When they serve[^c-8aae1f88ce4aa5e3d1753ef2e30db4d3] a notice under subsection (2) above on a precepting authority the Welsh Ministers[^c-da7e9acbf68aa6f1af433a1a0e1483f5] shall also serve a copy of it on each billing authority to which the precepting authority has power to issue a precept.
