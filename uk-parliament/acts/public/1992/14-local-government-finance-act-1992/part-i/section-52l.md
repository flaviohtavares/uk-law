# Local Government Finance Act 1992, section 52L

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/14

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 52L. Nomination.[^c12078881]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if the Welsh Ministers nominate[^key-8df62b71996276c42d6929a46705a614] an authority under section 52D(2)(b) above.

(2) They[^key-2074cfff125628abc6f45428335a681f] shall notify the authority in writing of—

- (a) the nomination;
- (b) the set of principles determined for the authority under section 52B above;
- (c) the category in which the authority falls (if they determine[^key-2ede602f38010a0444d8eda87d122227] categories under section 52B above);
- (d) the amount which they[^key-5dc66bc4881a29b7c8d2f7508efdcc41] would have proposed as the target amount for the year under consideration if they had designated the authority as regards that year under section 52D(2)(a) above.

(3) A nomination under section 52D(2)(b) above —

- (a) is invalid unless subsection (2) above is complied with;
- (b) shall be treated as made at the beginning of the day on which the authority receives a notification under that subsection.

(4) After nominating all the authorities they intend[^key-f51d150bca562e44a4f66709be6be8aa] to nominate the Welsh Ministers[^key-e06ee7c0d479ba1eb9524509d2c78536] —

- (a) shall decide, in the case of each authority nominated, whether to proceed under section 52M or under section 52N below in relation to it;
- (b) may decide to proceed under different sections in relation to different authorities.
