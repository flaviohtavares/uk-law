# Local Government Finance Act 1992, section 52S

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 52S. No challenge or acceptance.[^c12078881]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) the period mentioned in section 52M(8) above ends without an authority designated under section 52M above informing the Welsh Ministers[^key-9c9f5f6bd950decd0c3023a1a9af3449] by notice in writing under paragraph (a) or (b) of section 52M(8), or
- (b) the period mentioned in section 52P(8) above ends without an authority designated under section 52P above informing the Welsh Ministers[^key-c6382f94b1d562482da49ff66ab9732a] by notice in writing under paragraph (a) or (b) of section 52P(8).

(2) As soon as is reasonably practicable after the period ends the Welsh Ministers[^key-89ec0927452ec308e219493e6b77c04d] shall make an order stating the amount which the amount calculated by the authority as its budget requirement for the year is not to exceed; and the amount stated shall be that stated in the notice under section 52M(4)(b) or 52P(4)(b) above (as the case may be).

(3) An order under this section—

- (a) shall not be made unless a draft of it has been laid before and approved by resolution of the National Assembly for Wales[^key-a5b586cd6c5c41da7a7b1c0b604d8c49] ;
- (b) may relate to two or more authorities.

(4) As soon as is reasonably practicable after an order under this section is made the Welsh Ministers[^key-96cce171e0dafd9286ea6ce58cd3ce06] shall serve on the authority (or each authority) a notice stating the amount stated in the case of the authority concerned in the order.

(5) When they serve[^key-40e0579af69a365f6b46c787178f5c9f] a notice under subsection (4) above on a precepting authority the Welsh Ministers[^key-0d0008ef2618db03b0b73df852495ee0] shall also serve a copy of it on each billing authority to which the precepting authority has power to issue a precept.
