# Local Government Finance Act 1992, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (40).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/14

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 101. Transitory exemption for school leavers.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) After paragraph 5 of Schedule 1 to the [^c12081361]1988 Act (personal community charge: exemption) there shall be inserted the following paragraph—

> **5A. School leavers**
>
> A person is an exempt individual on a particular day if—
>
> - (a) he is aged under 20 on the day,
> - (b) the day falls within the period of 6 months beginning with 1 May 1992,
> - (c) immediately before that date he was undertaking a qualifying course of education, and
> - (d) the course was not undertaken in consequence of an office or employment held by him.

(2) After sub-paragraph (1) of paragraph 6A of Schedule 1A to the [^c12081371]1987 Act (personal community charge: exemption) there shall be inserted the following sub-paragraph—

> (1A) If such a person as is mentioned in sub-paragraph (1) above ceases to undertake such a course of education on or after 30th April 1992, he shall continue to be exempt until the start of the earlier of the following days—
>
> - (a) 1st November 1992,
> - (b) his twentieth birthday.
