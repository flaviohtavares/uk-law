## 144. Exclusions and priority.

(3) Where, apart from this subsection, two or more persons would be entitled to child benefit in respect of the same child or qualifying young person[^key-47a4ff6541451e0a47bb0d9ca0b559f2] for the same week, one of them only shall be entitled; and the question which of them is entitled shall be determined in accordance with Schedule 10 to this Act.
