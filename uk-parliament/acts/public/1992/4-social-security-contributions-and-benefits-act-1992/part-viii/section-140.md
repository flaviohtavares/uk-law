## 140. Principles of determination. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f][^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In determining whether to make an award *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] of a community care grant or a crisis loan[^c-cae619615fabb598cd2eb1a87808518f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] to the applicant or the amount or value to be awarded *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] an appropriate officer[^c-b687fd0e1717c05045c0cd59e8375305] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] shall have regard, subject to subsection (2) below, to all the circumstances of the case and, in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]

- (a) the nature, extent and urgency of the need; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]
- (b) the existence of resources from which the need may be met; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]
- (c) the possibility that some other person or body may wholly or partly meet it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]
- (d) where the payment is repayable, the likelihood of repayment and the time within which repayment is likely; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]
- (e) any relevant allocation under section 168(1) to (4) of the Administration Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]

(1A)[^c-963e5da1c38f9bbe46e952c9b7b4729e] Subject to subsection (2) below, in determining whether to make an award of a budgeting loan to the applicant, or the amount or value to be awarded, an appropriate officer shall have regard to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]

- (a) such of the applicant’s personal circumstances as are of a description specified in directions issued by the Secretary of State; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]
- (b) the criteria specified in paragraphs *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] (b), (d) and (e)[^c-56111adbcdd82b2862ebb7aeabb2b9d1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] of subsection (1) above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]

[^c-1f6a7eb8b820ec0eb942225d932b4955]....

(2) An appropriate officer[^c-9a51a4bd26f6f4602a5fa1872f019c16] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] shall determine any question in accordance with any general directions issued by the Secretary of State and in determining any question shall take account of any general guidance issued by him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]

(3)[^c-c112948b44a5accc4503ee7e037a143a] Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (2), the Secretary of State may issue directions under that subsection for the purpose of securing that allocations under section 168 of the Administration Act are not exceeded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]

(4) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (2) above, the power to issue general directions conferred on the Secretary of State by that subsection includes power to direct— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]

- (a) that in circumstances specified in the direction *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] an appropriate officer[^c-33f6caccdb396fdfca214609d7dad28b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] shall not determine an application and, without prejudice to the generality of this paragraph, that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] an appropriate officer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] shall not determine an application which is made before the end of a specified period after the making of an application by the same person for a payment such as is mentioned in section 138(1)(b) above to meet the same need and without there having been any relevant change of circumstances since the previous application; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-88e61aa8122f8cd80d65a0fc1133a596][^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] that in circumstances specified in the direction an application for an award of a community care grant may be treated as an application for an award of a crisis loan, and vice versa; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]
- (b) that for a category of need specified in the direction *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] an appropriate officer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] shall not award less than an amount specified in the direction; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]
- (c) that for a category of need specified in the direction *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] an appropriate officer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] shall not award more than an amount so specified; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]
- (d) that payments to meet a category of need specified in the direction shall in all cases or in no case be made by instalments; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]
- (da (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-7033e24d929e0aaf73bc20c73577adb7][^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] that the amount or value of a budgeting loan is not to exceed a sum specified or determined as specified in the direction; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]
- (e)[^c-08f9d32c8ed365a1b0291b47972a3fc0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f) that a payment such as is mentioned in section 138(1)(b) above shall only be awarded to a person if either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]
  - (i) he is in receipt of a benefit which is specified in the direction and the circumstances are such as are so specified; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] in a case where the conditions specified in sub-paragraph (i) above are not satisfied, the circumstances are such as are specified in the direction, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]

and the power to issue general guidance conferred on him by that subsection includes power to give *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] appropriate officers[^c-c136fb45fb7ef38f8c8f157035e064e0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] guidance as to any matter to which directions under that subsection may relate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]

(4ZA (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-53e4551b4d46b22fb263cd9b0e7c7a1f][^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] A direction under subsection (4)(da) may require the sum to be determined by applying, or by a method that includes applying, a multiplier specified in the direction in circumstances specified in the direction to the most recent relevant sum published by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]

(4ZB (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] A relevant sum is a sum determined from time to time by reference to so much of any relevant allocation under section 168(1) to (4) of the Administration Act as is available for making payments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f]

(5) In determining a question *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] an appropriate officer[^c-b8fa39b786ccb86448fd269840f986cc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] shall take account (subject to any directions or guidance issued by the Secretary of State under this section) of any guidance issued by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f] the appropriate officer nominated for his area under section 36 of the Social Security Act 1998[^c-7c60db9f28de8ccfdcaceb553bbe257c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a07a28927ba8f767112c2d53c0afdb8f].
