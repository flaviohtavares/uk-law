## 15. Class 4 contributions recoverable under the Income Tax Acts.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Class 4 contributions shall be payable for any tax year in respect of all profits[^key-df596a240859bd9fc485ca22bffc3bbc] which—

- (a) are immediately derived from the carrying on or exercise of one or more trades, professions or vocations, [^key-d0cc909d07e939418a62663571e5c289]...
- (b) are profits chargeable to income tax under Chapter 2 of Part 2 of the Income Tax (Trading and Other Income) Act 2005[^key-35e38af46f7387c3c1ebd490e9199f5d] for the year of assessment corresponding to that tax year and[^key-39849319e3f62cf189e8a26d7eeddb17]
- (c) are not profits of a trade, profession or vocation carried on wholly outside the United Kingdom.

(2) Class 4 contributions in respect of profits [^key-84f48bb5f2d9c810975ad6c9ea48c125]... shall be payable—

- (a) in the same manner as any income tax which is, or would be, chargeable in respect of those profits [^key-84f48bb5f2d9c810975ad6c9ea48c125]... (whether or not income tax in fact falls to be paid), and
- (b) by the person on whom the income tax is (or would be) charged,

in accordance with assessments made from time to time under the Income Tax Acts.

(3)[^c21367661][^key-0133f165d8fd5562f49f217d25d78e3f] The amount of a Class 4 contribution under this section for any tax year is equal to the aggregate of—

- (a) the main Class 4 percentage of so much of the profits [^key-0195d670eb528e539faa426ab597a12f]... referred to in subsection (1) above (computed in accordance with Schedule 2 to this Act) as exceeds £12,570[^key-f53a2ee4cf1be3ca22e9c78cb6ebf908] but does not exceed £50,270[^key-7a4e3f8a0a42304f30c7d7bd1c4e2cf0]; and
- (b) the additional Class 4 percentage of so much of those profits [^key-0195d670eb528e539faa426ab597a12f]... as exceeds £50,270;

but the figures specified in this subsection are subject to alteration under section 141 of the Administration Act.

(3ZA) For the purposes of this Act—

- (a)[^M_C_dc9f090e-66ba-430e-d7bc-48de0a25a082] the main Class 4 percentage is 6%[^key-3fd7ddbe07b1ecef50e324a1f0a5dd66]; and
- (b) the additional Class 4 percentage is 2[^key-4b56820109a8093f0cdcf070c2187898] per cent;

but the main Class 4 percentage is subject to alteration under section 143 of the Administration Act.

(3A)[^M_F_e452383e-71dc-467c-946d-fcba909a6619] Where income tax is (or would be) charged on a member of a limited liability partnership in respect of profits [^key-39573e61075417458a9c0c9e3277f7d7]... arising from the carrying on of a trade or profession by the limited liability partnership, Class 4 contributions shall be payable by him if they would be payable were the trade or profession carried on in partnership by the members.

(4)[^key-384dc0d5628e8a453a06062cf392920b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) For the purposes of this section the year of assessment which corresponds to a tax year is the year of assessment (within the meaning of the Tax Acts) which consists of the same period as that tax year.
