## 19B. Extended meaning of “benefit” etc in Part 1[^c-494d4ca53b5e1948b574155d6193adf5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Part references to “benefit” or “contributory benefit” include benefit under Part 1 of the Pensions Act 2014.
