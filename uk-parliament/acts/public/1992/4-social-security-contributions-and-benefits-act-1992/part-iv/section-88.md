# Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, section 88

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (139).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Increases to be in respect of only one adult dependant.[^c-83c43d0b0b91be4f0f1e714f102809e9]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A person shall not by virtue of section 86A[^M_F_445c3339-985b-4918-f427-754fa430b1b6] above be entitled for the same period to an increase of benefit in respect of more than one person.
