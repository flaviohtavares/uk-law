# Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, section 63

## 63. Descriptions of non-contributory benefits.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Non-contributory benefits under this Part of this Act are of the following descriptions, namely—

- (a) attendance allowance;
- (b)[^key-8aa1cc8b3a63845a2fb881926ccc0e4a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) carer’s allowance[^key-c9cd82f2b375d3d6ccd6619cfbb5a524][^key-0cea4f2fa955bfc16f709feaa7d35754]...;
- (d) disability living allowance;
- (e) guardian’s allowance;
- (f) retirement pensions of the following categories—
  - (i) Category C, payable in certain cases to a widow whose husband was over pensionable age on 5 July 1948 or to a woman whose marriage to a husband who was over pensionable age on that date was terminated otherwise than by his death[^key-844ba6d2eabcd7022e8da1ec6381c5d2] (with increase for adult [^key-c69151ffcbba39a57d375e7f5a75cdd6]... dependants), and
  - (ii) Category D, payable to persons over the age of 80;
- (g) age addition payable, in the case of persons over the age of 80, by way of increase of a retirement pension of any category under this Act[^key-232f1b13d8d9cc308170227ea0a1ebdc] or of some other pension or allowance from the Secretary of State.
