# Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, section 171ZK

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (139).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 171ZK. Power to apply Part 12ZA ...[^key-ac4c76973becf2fd657f54571283e0b3][^key-6d9a8a314dd15a6c410f60b2d11d09fa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-642f7e79d579384cccb9bf5bebfecdf0] The Secretary of State may by regulations provide for this Part to have effect in relation to cases which involve adoption, but not the placement of a child for adoption under the law of any part of the United Kingdom, with such modifications as the regulations may prescribe.

(2)[^key-79777cdc48cf2638e733f4d42c963330] The Secretary of State may by regulations provide for this Part to have effect in relation to cases which involve a person who has applied, or intends to apply, with another person for a parental order under section 54 of the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act 2008 and a child who is, or will be, the subject of the order, with such modifications as the regulations may prescribe.
