# Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, section 174

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (139).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 174. References to Acts.

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Act—

- “**the 1975 Act**” means the [^c21369701]Social Security Act 1975;
- “**the 1986 Act**” means the [^c21369711]Social Security Act 1986;
- “**the Administration Act**” means the [^c21369721]Social Security Administration Act 1992;
- “**the Consequential Provisions Act**” means the [^c21369731]Social Security (Consequential Provisions) Act 1992;
- “**the Northern Ireland Contributions and Benefits Act**” [^c21369741]means the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992;
- “**the Old Cases Act**” means the [^c21369751]Industrial Injuries and Diseases (Old Cases) Act 1975; and
- “**the Pensions Act**” means the Pension Schemes Act 1993[^c-58dbeb0fe83a394a6673c57acaea48a2].
