> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (139).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/4

## 140B. Amount[^c-0c6737bb4694458cd4b296a8f68b5175]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Health in pregnancy grant is to be of an amount prescribed by regulations made by the Treasury.

(2) Different amounts may be prescribed in relation to different cases.
