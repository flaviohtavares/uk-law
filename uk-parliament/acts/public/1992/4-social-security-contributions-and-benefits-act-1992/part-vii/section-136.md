# Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, section 136

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (139).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 136. Income and capital. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d512b0988533f9e1d3adbd5c001edea2][^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e][^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where a person claiming an income-related benefit is a member of a family, the income and capital of any member of that family shall, except in prescribed circumstances, be treated as the income and capital of that person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(2) Regulations may provide that capital not exceeding the amount prescribed under section 134(1) above but exceeding a prescribed lower amount shall be treated, to a prescribed extent, as if it were income of a prescribed amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(3) Income and capital shall be calculated or estimated in such manner as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(4) A person’s income in respect of a week shall be calculated in accordance with prescribed rules; and the rules may provide for the calculation to be made by reference to an average over a period (which need not include the week concerned). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(5) Circumstances may be prescribed in which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a) a person is treated as possessing capital or income which he does not possess; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) capital or income which a person does possess is to be disregarded; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (c) income is to be treated as capital; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (d) capital is to be treated as income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
