# Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, section 127

## 127. Effect of return to work. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e][^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

If a person returns to work with the same employer after a period during which section 126 above applies to him, and whether or not his return is before the end of any stoppage of work in relation to which he is or would be *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] prevented from being entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance[^c-52fcd4cf9c881d496e9bb584615ae5c5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]—

- (a) that section shall cease to apply to him at the commencement of the day on which he returns to work; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) until the end of the period of 15 days beginning with that day, section 124(1) above shall have effect in relation to him as if the following paragraph were substituted for paragraph (c)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

  > (c) in the case of a member of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] couple[^c-3d3babc54748071b2f80147f640ff5eb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e], the other member is not engaged in remunerative work; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

  ; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (c) any sum paid by way of income support for that period of 15 days to him or, where he is a member of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] couple *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e], to the other member of that couple, shall be recoverable in accordance with the regulations from the person to whom it was paid or from any prescribed person or, where the person to whom it was paid is a member of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] couple *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e], from the other member of the couple. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
