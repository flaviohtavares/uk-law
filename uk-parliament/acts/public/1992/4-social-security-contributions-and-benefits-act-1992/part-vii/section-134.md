# Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, section 134

## 134. Exclusions from benefit. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e][^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-489541a76d6a87891dee0a1a795daed4] No person shall be entitled to an income-related benefit if his capital or a prescribed part of it exceeds the prescribed amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(2)[^key-489541a76d6a87891dee0a1a795daed4] Except in prescribed circumstances the entitlement of one member of a family to any one income-related benefit excludes entitlement to that benefit for any other member for the same period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(3)[^key-de1e489ecd7310f62521f3c102177182] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Where the amount of any income-related benefit would be less than a prescribed amount, it shall not be payable except in prescribed circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
