# Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, section 130A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (139).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/4

## 130A. Appropriate maximum housing benefit[^c-a94fb1378dbceafa8eddcc014e0cd4d5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] For the purposes of section 130 above, the appropriate maximum housing benefit (in this section referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**the AMHB *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**”) is determined in accordance with this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(2) Regulations must prescribe the manner in which the AMHB is to be determined. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(3) The provision which may be made by the regulations includes provision[^c-f997a90ae6db2393577014a1b09a2060] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] for the AMHB to be ascertained in the prescribed manner by reference to rent officer determinations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(4) The regulations may require an authority administering housing benefit in any prescribed case— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a) to apply for a rent officer determination, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) to do so within such time as may be specified in the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(5)[^c-b56cf310a443f5f1cb44a6019d7c4a55] The regulations may, for the purpose of determining the AMHB, provide for the amount of the liability mentioned in section 130(1)(a) above to be taken to be an amount other than the actual amount of that liability (and, without prejudice to the generality of this subsection, may provide for it to be taken to be the amount of a rent officer determination). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(6) The regulations may, for that purpose, make provision for determining the amount of liability under section 130(1)(a) above which a person is treated as having by virtue of regulations under section 137(2)(j) below (and, without prejudice to the generality of this subsection, may provide for that amount to be the amount of a rent officer determination). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(7) A rent officer determination is a determination made by a rent officer in the exercise of functions under section 122 of the Housing Act 1996. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
