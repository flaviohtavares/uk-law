## 130. Housing benefit. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e][^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person is entitled to housing benefit if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a) he is liable to make payments in respect of a dwelling in Great Britain which he occupies as his home; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) there is an appropriate maximum housing benefit in his case; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (c) either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
  - (i) he has no income or his income does not exceed the applicable amount; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] his income exceeds that amount, but only by so much that there is an amount remaining if the deduction for which subsection (3)(b) below provides is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(2) In subsection (1) above “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**payments in respect of a dwelling *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**” means such payments as may be prescribed, but the power to prescribe payments does not include power to prescribe— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a)[^key-38f2a8a2f6e4f8697815d056b20cde61] payments to a billing authority or to a local authority in Scotland in respect of council tax; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b)[^c21368921] mortgage payments, or, in relation to Scotland, payments under heritable securities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(3) Where a person is entitled to housing benefit, then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a) if he has no income or his income does not exceed the applicable amount, the amount of the housing benefit shall be the amount which is the appropriate maximum housing benefit in his case; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) if his income exceeds the applicable amount, the amount of the housing benefit shall be what remains after the deduction from the appropriate maximum housing benefit of prescribed percentages of the excess of his income over the applicable amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
