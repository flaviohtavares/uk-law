## 137. Interpretation of Part VII and supplementary provisions. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e][^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this Part of this Act, unless the context otherwise requires— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- “[^c-c159e6cb8f16e7f3a59b955071d746ce] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**billing authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**” has the same meaning as in Part I of the Local Government Finance Act 1992; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- “**child *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**” means a person under the age of 16; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- “couple” means— *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-814ac94fd60fdee5a38e6f042ae7c238] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
  - (a) two people who are married to, or civil partners of, each other and are members of the same household; or *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
  - (b) two people who are not married to, or civil partners of, each other but are living together *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] as if they were a married couple or civil partners[^c-aac6f47d9eda0747d137a21d36d95618] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] otherwise than in prescribed circumstances; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- [^c-7a8205b6ced1f73fc048451db16fd793]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**dwelling *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**” means any residential accommodation, whether or not consisting of the whole or part of a building and whether or not comprising separate and self-contained premises; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- “**family *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
  - (a) a couple[^c-4567876663904c4d1a6a6d6220ddc322] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e];
  - (b) a couple *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] and a member of the same household for whom one of them is or both are responsible and who is a child or a person of a prescribed description; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
  - (c) except in prescribed circumstances, a person who is not a member of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] couple *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] and a member of the same household for whom that person is responsible and who is a child or a person of a prescribed description; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- “[^c-04ac19e823384eb8130bbd797dee147e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**income-based jobseeker’s allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**” has the same meaning as in the Jobseekers Act 1995; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- “**industrial injuries scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**” means a scheme made under Schedule 8 to this Act or section 159 of the 1975 Act or under the Old Cases Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- “[^c-da1fa1bd149335831a1a267722d2b937] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**” in relation to Scotland means a council constituted under section 2 of the Local Government *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] etc. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] (Scotland) Act 1994; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- “[^c-b378cf1fd87e0e4307ea671e1339fc16] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**pensionable age *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**” has the meaning given by the rules in paragraph 1 of Schedule 4 to the Pensions Act 1995 (c. 26); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- “**prescribed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**”means specified in or determined in accordance with regulations; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- “**the qualifying age for state pension credit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**” is (in accordance with section 1(2)(b) and (6) of the State Pension Credit Act 2002)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
  - in the case of a woman, pensionable age; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
  - in the case of a man, the age which is pensionable age in the case of a woman born on the same day as the man; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- “**state pension credit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**” means state pension credit under the State Pension Credit Act 2002; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(2) Regulations may make provision for the purposes of this Part of this Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a) as to circumstances in which a person is to be treated as being or not being in Great Britain; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) continuing a person’s entitlement to benefit during periods of temporary absence from Great Britain; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (c) as to what is or is not to be treated as remunerative work or as employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (d)[^c-1c44aaa4ddd3467ee6cd3765fb3ca5f1] as to circumstances in which a person is or is not to be treated as engaged or normally engaged in remunerative work; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (e) as to what is or is not to be treated as relevant education; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (f) as to circumstances in which a person is or is not to be treated as receiving relevant education; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (g) specifying the descriptions of pension increases under war pension schemes or industrial injuries schemes that are analogous to the benefits mentioned in section 129(2)(b)(i) to (iii) above; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (h) as to circumstances in which a person is or is not to be treated as occupying a dwelling as his home; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (i) for treating any person who is liable to make payments in respect of a dwelling as if he were not so liable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (j) for treating any person who is not liable to make payments in respect of a dwelling as if he were so liable; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (k) for treating as included in a dwelling any land used for the purposes of the dwelling; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (l) as to circumstances in which persons are to be treated as being or not being members of the same household; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (m) as to circumstances in which one person is to be treated as responsible or not responsible for another. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
