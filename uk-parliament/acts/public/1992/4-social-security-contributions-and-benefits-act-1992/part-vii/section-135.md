## 135. The applicable amount. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e][^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-be72c8778bdd5affed8368c741c35f4e][^key-9eb023191b5bd79e93d1a73432e58396][^key-d470794ad8e4c335f2e4ea81e63d669b] The applicable amount, in relation to any income-related benefit, shall be such amount or the aggregate of such amounts as may be prescribed in relation to that benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(2)[^key-9eb023191b5bd79e93d1a73432e58396] The power to prescribe applicable amounts conferred by subsection (1) above includes power to prescribe nil as an applicable amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(3)[^key-c01a58d04bac18f80f9698bac150e86c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-c01a58d04bac18f80f9698bac150e86c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) [^key-845e913165cdf7c5dd07452dc1328441]... the applicable amount for a severely disabled person shall include an amount in respect of his being a severely disabled person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(6) Regulations may specify circumstances in which persons are to be treated as being or as not being severely disabled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
