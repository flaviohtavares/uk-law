## 124. Income support. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e][^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person in Great Britain is entitled to income support if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a)[^c-2fb017929d2bf9f21b3689436f197c52] he is of or over the age of 16; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-16d6ca3943e26f20469d9d650942ab2a][^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] he has not attained the qualifying age for state pension credit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) he has no income or his income does not exceed the applicable amount; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (c) he is not engaged in remunerative work and, if he is a member of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] couple[^c-50b7bf9932b3b5a98e821acde5b02896] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e], the other member is not so engaged; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] [^c-590186a43da26b0ac15e81b4dd834cf4]...
- (d)[^c-27f77d468531f9fbffdbdb930f4b2f22] except in such circumstances as may be prescribed, he is not receiving relevant education; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (e)[^c-504f04bc9f0864779d1e958990de5a96][^M_F_6e2e5566-1a25-4960-9f65-e4cabfcda7f8] he falls within a prescribed category of person; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] [^c-70357ee0c3c600234e4ac8e362c0a70a]...
- (f) he is not entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance and, if he is a member of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] couple[^c-a46fd7bb4d64a81957d13ace9bff0732] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e], the other member of the couple is not *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e], and the couple are not,[^c-576dcc9986bd6d4555193a2b0b63f3fc] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] entitled to an income-based jobseeker’s allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]; [^c-2aaa0c1ff0f08774f49f2cfddacaeecc]...
- (g)[^c-6956cc8489ad287bc3e4d1ce224ab99b] if he is a member of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] couple[^c-a929b1837b7ab891d793720dd8536504] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e], the other member of the couple is not entitled to state pension credit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]; and[^c-06bdd6b3cdbc3c678e5dde6f551771d9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (h) he is not entitled to an employment and support allowance and, if he is a member of a couple, the other member of the couple is not entitled to an income-related employment and support allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(1A)[^c-47c7b145caca8e6577cabedfbad13f4f] Regulations under paragraph (e) of subsection (1) must secure that a person who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a) is not a member of a couple, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) is responsible for, and a member of the same household as, a child under the age of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] 5[^M_F_5097590f-1abf-4caa-b662-d6cb7ada77d0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e],

falls within a category of person prescribed under that paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(1B) Subsection (1A) does not apply if regulations under subsection (4)(c) of section 1A of the Jobseekers Act 1995 containing the provision mentioned in subsection (5) of that section are in force. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(2)[^c-8c7e3e8a51fc351d5adf0ad245ba2597] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-8c7e3e8a51fc351d5adf0ad245ba2597] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Subject to subsection (5) below, where a person is entitled to income support, then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a) if he has no income, the amount shall be the applicable amount; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) if he has income, the amount shall be the difference between his income and the applicable amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(5) Where a person is entitled to income support for a period to which this subsection applies, the amount payable for that period shall be calculated in such manner as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(6) Subsection (5) above applies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a) to a period of less than a week which is the whole period for which income support is payable; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) to any other period of less than a week for which it is payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(7)[^c-bd3f12b212f67aa09bdf1b6bfb4baf2b] In this section, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**income-related employment and support allowance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**” means an income-related allowance under Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act 2007 (employment and support allowance). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
