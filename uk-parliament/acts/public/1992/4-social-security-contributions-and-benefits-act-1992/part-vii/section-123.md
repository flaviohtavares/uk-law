# Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, section 123

## 123. Income-related benefits. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c21368741][^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e][^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Prescribed schemes shall provide for the following benefits (in this Act referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**income-related benefits *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**”)—

- (a) income support; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b)[^c-cbe3d8f9df76c6d6bb2bf2c38a81a799] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-cbe3d8f9df76c6d6bb2bf2c38a81a799] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) housing benefit; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (e)[^c-0ff7ac14a4895c8fe67af14215ab2e7b] council tax benefit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(2) The Secretary of State shall make copies of schemes prescribed under subsection (1)(a), (b) or (c) above available for public inspection at local offices of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] the Department for Work and Pensions[^c-7cb73ff4ee0aac342f76dfc949dfb0da] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] at all reasonable hours without payment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(3) Every authority granting housing benefit— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a) shall take such steps as appear to them appropriate for the purpose of securing that persons who may be entitled to housing benefit from the authority become aware that they may be entitled to it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) shall make copies of the housing benefit scheme, with any modifications adopted by them under the Administration Act, available for public inspection at their principal office at all reasonable hours without payment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(4)[^c-6c1559358cc4c4b9ade90809bb8fb1d9] Each billing authority and in Scotland each local authority[^c-55b8cd5a07283705da4fc406d9f36367] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]—

- (a) shall take such steps as appear to it appropriate for the purpose of securing that any person who may be entitled to council tax benefit in respect of council tax payable to the authority becomes aware that he may be entitled to it; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) shall make copies of the council tax benefit scheme, with any modifications adopted by it under the Administration Act, available for public inspection at its principal office at all reasonable hours without payment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
