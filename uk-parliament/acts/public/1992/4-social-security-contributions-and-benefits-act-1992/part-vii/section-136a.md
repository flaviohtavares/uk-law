# Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, section 136A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (139).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 136A. Effect of attaining qualifying age for state pension credit (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-92fd9207b53360c9e1de62a076a9e9ee][^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e][^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subsections (2) and (3) below apply in relation to housing benefit and council tax benefit in the case of any person who has attained the qualifying age for state pension credit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(2) Regulations may make provision for section 134(1) or any provision of section 136 above not to have effect in relation to those benefits in the case of any such person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(3) In relation to those benefits, regulations may make provision for the determination of the income and capital of any such person; and any such regulations may include provision applying (with such modifications as the Secretary of State thinks fit)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a) section 5 of the State Pension Credit Act 2002 (provision for treating income of spouse as income of claimant, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] etc *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) section 15 of that Act (determination of income and capital for purposes of state pension credit). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(4) Regulations under subsection (3) above may also include provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a) authorising or requiring the use of any calculation or estimate of a person’s income or capital made by the Secretary of State for the purposes of the State Pension Credit Act 2002; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) requiring that, if and so long as an assessed income period is in force under section 6 of that Act in respect of a person falling within subsection (1) above,— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
  - (i) the assessed amount of any element of his retirement provision shall be treated as the amount of that element for the purposes of housing benefit or council tax benefit; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] his income shall be taken for those purposes not to include any element of retirement provision which it is taken not to include for the purposes of state pension credit by virtue of a determination under subsection (5) of section 7 of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(5) In subsection (4) above “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**assessed amount *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**”, “**element *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**retirement provision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**” have the same meaning as in the State Pension Credit Act 2002. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(6) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision for the preceding provisions of this section to apply with modifications in cases to which section 12 of the State Pension Credit Act 2002 (polygamous marriages) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(7) The provision that may be made by regulations under subsection (6) above includes any provision that may be made by regulations under section 133 above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
