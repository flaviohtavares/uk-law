# Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, section 126

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (139).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 126. Trade disputes. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e][^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This section applies to a person, other than a child or a person of a prescribed description— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a) who *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] is prevented from being entitled to a jobseeker’s allowance by section 14 of the Jobseekers Act 1995 (trade disputes)[^c-1a5deae39b0843609347e82a4a726ff1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) who would be so *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] prevented[^c-265f724c6e3da15f95c666f845605f22] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] if otherwise entitled to that benefit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

except during any period shown by the person to be a period of incapacity for work *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] [^c-3ba8a6c951b1042bdbe37f13d791cbc3]... or to be within the maternity period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(2) In subsection (1) above “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**the maternity period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**” means the period commencing at the beginning of the 6th week before the expected week of confinement and ending at the end of the 7th week after the week in which confinement takes place. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(3) For the purpose of calculating income support— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a) so long as this section applies to a person who is not a member of a family, the applicable amount shall be disregarded; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) so long as it applies to a person who is a member of a family but is not a member of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] couple[^c-823d4182f421b6c7521bf583e162250c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e], the portion of the applicable amount which is included in respect of him shall be disregarded; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (c) so long as it applies to one of the members of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] couple[^c-6b60624a58fc48c217311cca2cd6ca91] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]—
  - (i) if the applicable amount consists only of an amount in respect of them, it shall be reduced to one half; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] if it includes other amounts, the portion of it which is included in respect of them shall be reduced to one-half and any further portion of it which is included in respect of the member of the couple to whom this section applies shall be disregarded; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (d) so long as it applies to both the members of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] couple[^c-7bc6f169cc99cd82a1efacafca1f18b4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]—
  - (i) if neither of them is responsible for a child or person of a prescribed description who is a member of the same household, the applicable amount shall be disregarded; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] in any other case, the portion of the applicable amount which is included in respect of them and any further portion of it which is included in respect of either of them shall be disregarded. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(4) Where a reduction under subsection (3)(c) above would not produce a sum which is a multiple of 5p, the reduction shall be to the nearest lower sum which is such a multiple. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(5) Where this section applies to a person for any period, then, except so far as regulations provide otherwise— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a) in calculating the entitlement to income support of that person or a member of his family the following shall be treated as his income and shall not be disregarded— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
  - (i) any payment which he or a member of his family receives or is entitled to obtain by reason of the person to whom this section applies being without employment for that period; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] without prejudice to the generality of sub-paragraph (i) above, any amount which becomes or would on an application duly made become available to him in that period by way of repayment of income tax deducted from his *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] taxable earnings (as defined by section 10 of the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003) under PAYE regulations[^c-53982c47e27dfb2a193ea83ee6fce5bd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) any payment by way of income support for that period or any part of it which apart from this paragraph would be made to him, or to a person whose applicable amount is aggregated with his— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
  - (i) shall not be made if the weekly rate of payment is equal to or less than the relevant sum; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] if it is more than the relevant sum, shall be at a weekly rate equal to the difference. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(6) In respect of any period less than a week, subsection (5) above shall have effect subject to such modifications as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

(7) Subject to subsection (8) below, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**the relevant sum *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]**” for the purposes of subsection (5) above shall be *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] £53.00[^c-45fe398889c47b13c4f9a779aad8c15b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e].

(8) If an order under section 150 of the Administration Act (annual up-rating) has the effect of increasing payments of income support, from the time when the order comes into force there shall be substituted, in subsection (5)(b) above, for the references to the sum for the time being mentioned in it references to a sum arrived at by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

- (a) increasing that sum by the percentage by which the personal allowance under paragraph 1(1) of Part I of Schedule 2 to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e] [^c21368811]Income Support (General) Regulations 1987 for a single person aged not less than 25 has been increased by the order; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
- (b) if the sum as so increased is not a multiple of 50p, disregarding the remainder if it is 25p and, if it is not, rounding it up or down to the nearest 50p, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]

and the order shall state the substituted sum. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-67b23406b28006f5c07c2e1260fcd11e]
