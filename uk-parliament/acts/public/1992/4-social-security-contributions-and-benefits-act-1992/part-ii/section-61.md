## 61. Exclusion of increase of benefit for failure to satisfy contribution condition.[^c-af5d75715c4594ff1a02b60397b11bfb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A Category A or Category B retirement pension which is payable by virtue of section 60(1) above and a widowed mother’s allowance or widowed parent’s allowance[^c-bc57bd05c71cb7bf35dd3d4dec376f83] which is so payable shall not be increased under section 47(1) above or under Part IV below [^c-9da9289d9683948d785ee0fbbb2085b6]... if the pension or allowance contains no basic pension in consequence of a failure to satisfy a contribution condition.

(2)[^c-ad24b4c34693bc573b48d94f4bf95a2f] Where a person is entitled to short-term incapacity benefit at a rate determined under section 30B(3) above and the retirement pension by reference to which the rate of the benefit is determined—

- (a) would have been payable only by virtue of section 60 above, and
- (b) would, in consequence of a failure to satisfy a contribution condition, have contained no basic pension,

the benefit shall not be increased under section 47(1) above or under Part IV below [^c-9da9289d9683948d785ee0fbbb2085b6]...
