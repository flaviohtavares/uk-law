# Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, section 20

## 20. Descriptions of contributory benefits.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Contributory benefits under this Part of this Act are of the following descriptions, namely—

- (a)[^M_F_548a2f5f-9d18-4868-c057-5349e2180d4b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^M_F_b91a1d56-d897-4b20-9bd1-85fbba051a0a] incapacity benefit, comprising—
  - (i) short-term incapacity benefit, and
  - (ii) long-term incapacity benefit;
- (d) maternity allowance [^c-54df58bc2d6930444426bc8564e392e1]... ;
- (e) widow’s benefit, comprising—
  - (i)[^c-8ce4fe63e71d336f7689ab9aa217d6c9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii) widowed mother’s allowance [^c-3ee217689031a8083905906f623547e5]... ;
  - (iii) widow’s pension;
- (ea)[^c-87f74ca62a2ebea08db20278068356a6] widowed parent's allowance;
- (f) retirement pensions of the following categories—
  - (i) Category A, payable to a person by virtue of his own contributions (with increase for adult [^c-4f143afbfdec025c752805a8f89161f5]... dependants); and
  - (ii)[^c-7c60fe3bb4b5a6a8644c66d6b2b165bb] Category B, payable to a person by virtue of the contributions of a spouse [^c-3ee217689031a8083905906f623547e5]... or civil partner[^c-61616c461da67eae49db008056c6a8ce]
- (fa)[^M_F_576d6d3d-e8dd-443e-ccf6-68f3e7caa6f1] shared additional pensions;
- (g) for existing beneficiaries only, child’s special allowance.

(2) In this Act—

- “**long-term benefit**” means—
  - (a) long-term incapacity benefit;[^M_F_24f395c0-a858-4536-e3ce-cbde9330cc8f]
  - (b) a widowed mother’s allowance;
  - (ba) a widowed parent’s allowance;[^c-e0513c94b79bc7ca10f0a8cc18ce22b8]
  - (bb) [^c-a69c8b9999e01a2de8d7220dab989025]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (c) a widow’s pension; and
  - (d) a Category A or Category B retirement pension
  - (e) a shared additional pension;[^M_F_8ba6f8e8-4952-41a6-dfc3-64f2c16302aa]; and
- “**short-term benefit**” means—
  - (a) [^M_F_ca590686-d91d-411d-d6b2-4f096bf9530d]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (b) short-term incapacity benefit; and[^M_F_f057ce8d-de8e-4b93-91d1-dc1eafb1e53b]
  - (c) maternity allowance.

(3) The provisions of this Part of this Act are subject to the provisions of Chapter II of Part III of the Pensions Act[^M_F_a7b039d2-6017-4eba-cab6-eb0573743cda] (reduction in benefits for members of schemes that were contracted-out)[^c-ad6a69c3479ca7afa56b3790ab6dcd8c].
