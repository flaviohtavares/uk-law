# Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, section 118

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (139).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/4

## 118. Married women and widows.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Treasury may with the concurrence of the Secretary of State[^key-66240a282ee61ef55cbc5575ddd9e130] make regulations modifying any of the following provisions of this Act, namely—

- (a) Part I;
- (b) Part II (except section 60); and
- (c) Parts III and IV,

in such manner as the Treasury think[^key-270ae5123900855dbc79f6b0c04fa156] proper, in their application to women who are or have been married.
