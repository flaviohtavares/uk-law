# Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, section 119

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (139).** Text as at 2026-07-08. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/4

## 119. Persons outside Great Britain.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Treasury may with the concurrence of the Secretary of State[^c-7f2939604406b3f985860f4cb9b326f1] make regulations modifying Parts I to V of this Act and Part II of the Social Security Contributions (Transfer of Functions,[^c-eda532f44ffc5713657f187f826fc238] etc.) Act 1999, and any provisions of Chapter II of Part I of the Social Security Act 1998 which correspond to[^c-997f6f59e7b9f8e16db926d063806179] provisions of Part III of the 1975 Act, in such manner as the Treasury think[^c-3e5bfd84ed3a6961cd5737b40bfb4449] proper, in their application to persons who are or have been outside Great Britain at any prescribed time or in any prescribed circumstances.
