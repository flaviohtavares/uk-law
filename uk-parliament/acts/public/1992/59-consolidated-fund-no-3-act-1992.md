# Consolidated Fund (No. 3) Act 1992

> This law has been repealed or revoked. Ended on 1995-07-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Consolidated Fund (No. 3) Act 1992 [^c860202](repealed 19.7.1995)

1992 c. 59

**No longer in force (ended 1995-07-19).**

## 1. Issue out of the Consolidated Fund for the year ending 31st March 1993.

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund of the United Kingdom and apply towards making good the supply granted to Her Majesty for the service of the year ending on 31st March 1993 the sum of £4,384,141,000.

## 2. Issue out of the Consolidated Fund for the year ending 31st March 1994.

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund of the United Kingdom and apply towards making good the supply granted to Her Majesty for the service of the year ending on 31st March 1994 the sum of £83,737,475,000.

## 3. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Consolidated Fund (No. 3) Act 1992.
