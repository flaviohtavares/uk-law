# Friendly Societies Act 1992, section 103

## 103. Power to modify Part VI in relation to particular friendly societies.[^c15387161]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The appropriate authority[^c-d4343cfa61c78932586566ee2367aa06] may, on the application or with the consent of a friendly society, [^c15387181]. . . direct that all or any of the provisions of Part [^c15387191]. . . VI of this Act , or any provision of regulations made for the purposes of that Part,[^c15387201] shall not apply to the society or shall apply to it with such modifications as may be specified in the direction[^c15387211].

(2) A direction[^c15387221] under this section may be subject to conditions.

(3) A direction[^c15387231] under this section may be revoked by the appropriate authority at any time; and the appropriate authority, may at any time vary any such direction[^c15387261] on the application or with the consent of the society to which it applies.

(3A)[^c-aba30977429060b7dd2c817d0b1b12cf] The PRA must consult the FCA before making, varying or revoking a direction under this section.

(3B) The PRA must send the FCA a copy of any direction, variation or revocation under this section.

(4)[^c15387271] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c15387271] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c15387271] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^c-afd81b96cdbd414c56874285da03d298] The FCA must keep in a register kept by it for the purposes of this subsection a copy of any direction, variation or revocation under this section.

(8)[^c15387301] The register kept for the purposes of subsection (7) above shall be available for inspection on reasonable notice by members of the public.

(9)[^c15387301] The FCA[^c-2753f28c31c234ced2daf45f65fad983][^c15387351] shall keep a copy of—

- (a) any direction [^c-80d1a3f1d25990fd22cc4f854f0f869d]... under this section[^c15387361], and
- (b) any revocation or variation of any such direction,

and the central office shall keep the copy[^c15387371] in the public file of the society to which it relates.
