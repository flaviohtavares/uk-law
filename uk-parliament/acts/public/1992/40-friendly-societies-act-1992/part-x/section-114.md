# Friendly Societies Act 1992, section 114

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 114. Form of documents.[^c15387631]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Each of the FCA and the PRA may[^key-3aa9abe8738d5a2ef884148deeadb10d], by directions under this section, make provision with respect to the form of, and the particulars to be included in , and the authentication of,[^key-2966d5f642b32fb1a98c7a6c15f9c0f9] any document to be sent to it under this Act or the 1974 Act.

(1A)[^key-12ea5683dac7e5c02d961009e6a93e60] The PRA and the FCA must each consult the other before issuing a direction under this section if the document in question is required to be sent to both of them.

(2) The directions have effect subject to any other provision of or made under this Act.

(3)[^key-5e01b2f57502c0d03719aef58222e33d] As regards the authentication of a document sent to it[^key-dfc9e7bed917128600c29c4b4db8192d] electronically, each of the FCA and the PRA[^key-8dd0be2e74477a79b8ecea15625ab80d] may—

- (a) require the document to be authenticated by a particular person or a person of a particular description;
- (b) specify the means of authentication;
- (c) require the document to contain or be accompanied by the name or registered number of the society to which it relates (or both).

(4) As regards the procedure to be followed in sending a document electronically, each of the FCA and the PRA[^key-be08dd9aa0b97b42e36ed4c5ecde74fb] may specify requirements as to the hardware and software to be used, and technical specifications (for example, matters relating to protocol, security, anti-virus protection or encryption).

(5) Directions made by the FCA or the PRA[^key-d41fb3bdcca1b8757b28b42f2345301c] under this section must not require documents to be sent electronically.

(6) In this section, a document is sent electronically if is it sent by electronic means or in electronic form.
