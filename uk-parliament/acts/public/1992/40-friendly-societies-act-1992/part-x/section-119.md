# Friendly Societies Act 1992, section 119

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 119. General interpretation.[^c15388881]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “**the 1974 Act**” means the [^c15388891]Friendly Societies Act 1974;
- “**actuary**” means an actuary possessing such qualifications, if any, as may be specified in rules made by the Authority under section 340 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (and subsections (3) to (6) of that section apply in relation to an actuary appointed by virtue of any provision of this Act as they apply in relation to an actuary appointed in compliance with such rules)[^c15388901];
- “**annuities on human life**” does not include superannuation allowances and annuities payable out of any fund applicable solely to the relief and maintenance of persons engaged or who have been engaged in any particular profession, trade or employment, or of the dependants of such persons;
- [^c-f9e6577faf9d5cc708c08313f94fb503]...
- “**appointed actuary**” means the actuary appointed in accordance with rules made under section 340 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000[^c15388921];
- “**the appropriate actuary**” means—
  - (a) if the society is under a duty imposed by rules made by the Authority under section 340 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000[^c15388931], the society’s appointed actuary; and
  - (b) if it is not under such a[^c15388941] duty, an actuary appointed to perform the function in question;
- “the appropriate authority” means—[^c-714df5cd7ddfb323858eb1ba887daf5c]
  - (a) in relation to a society which is a PRA-authorised person, the PRA; and
  - (b) in relation to a society which is not a PRA-authorised person, the FCA;
- [^c15388951]...
- [^c15388951]...
- [^c15388951]...
- [^c15388951]...
- “**committee of management**” means the committee of management or other directing body of a society or branch;
- [^c-43821b7c9d32b4f836d0d1dfd2ddc368]...
- “**contract of insurance**” includes any contract the effecting of which constitutes the carrying on of insurance business by virtue of section 117 above;
- “controller” has the meaning given by section 55A above;[^c15388991]
- “**the court**” except in relation to the winding-up of an incorporated friendly society, means—
  - (a) in the case of a body whose registered office is situated in England and Wales, the county court;[^c-ef2f6186c415390b7d2f85a3853118f2]
  - (aa) in the case of a body whose registered office is situated in Northern Ireland, the county court for the district in which the office is situated;
  - (b) in the case of a body whose registered office is situated in Scotland, the sheriff in whose jurisdiction the office is situated;

  and, in relation to the winding-up of an incorporated friendly society, means the court which has jurisdiction under the applicable winding-up legislation to wind-up the society;
- [^c15388951]...
- [^c-a6396bc988eb9295acb0bf86f263f5e0]...

  “EEA State” has the meaning given by Schedule 1 to the Interpretation Act 1978 (c. 30);[^c-8d6088bf1d12cc906b6f3be4b9d673ed]

  “EFTA State” means an EEA State which is not a member State;[^c15389011]
- “electronic address” means any number or address used for the purposes of sending or receiving documents or information by electronic means;[^c-58468bd0195ac21a4e45e11eecbaf079]
- “the FCA” means the Financial Conduct Authority;
- “**financial year**” is to be construed in accordance with section 118;
- [^c-a0c1d2c023f8e0c5f50ea44e8eb4134c]...
- [^c-5f53ab289bce299a929fd8aead302f1e]...
- [^c-5f9dd452be9fbb5e93172284976cfe9e]...
- “**group business**” is to be construed in accordance with section 11 above;
- “**jointly controlled body**” is to be construed in accordance with section 13 above;
- [^c-5f53ab289bce299a929fd8aead302f1e]...
- “manager”, in relation to a friendly society to which section 37(2) or (3) above applies, means any person (other than an employee of a society) appointed by the society to manage any part of its insurance business, or any employee of the society (other than a chief executive) who, under the immediate authority of a member of the committee of management or chief executive of the society–[^c15389071]
  - (a) exercises managerial functions, or is responsible for maintaining accounts or other records of the society; and
  - (b) is not a person whose functions relate exclusively to business conducted from a place of business which is not in the United Kingdom or Gibraltar[^c-d4e0848a840e9abe0143173edb8f50c5];
- “**memorandum**” has the meaning given by paragraph 4(3) of Schedule 3 to this Act;
- “**modifications**”, in relation to enactments, includes additions, omissions and amendments and cognate expressions are to be construed accordingly[^c-1f15d3d3c422c9de24ae08444d756bea];
- “**non-insurance business**” means business falling within head C of Schedule 2 to this Act;
- “**notice**” means written notice and “**notice to**” a person means notice given to that person, and “**notify**” shall be construed accordingly;
- [^c15388951]...
- “**officer**” means—
  - (a) in relation to a registered friendly society or a registered branch—
    - (i) a trustee;
    - (ii) the treasurer, secretary and chief executive (however described);
    - (iii) a member of the committee of management; and
    - (iv) a person appointed by the society or branch to sue or be sued on its behalf; or
  - (b) in relation to an incorporated friendly society, a member of the committee of management, the chief executive (however described) and the secretary;
- “the PRA” means the Prudential Regulation Authority;
- “PRA-authorised person” has the meaning in section 2B of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000;
- “**the public file**”, in relation to a friendly society, means the file relating to the society which the FCA[^c-d65a5380638a912e3128aab81a723156] is required to maintain under section 104 above;
- “**registered address**”, in relation to a member of an incorporated friendly society, has the meaning given by paragraph 14(6) of Schedule 3 to this Act;
- [^c-a177b51c2fbbb8b6369da9cf27211575]...
- [^c-5f53ab289bce299a929fd8aead302f1e]...
- “[^c-d3b70ce4a7c44193317798c6a18a1252]**the Solvency 2 Directive**” means Directive [2009/138/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2009/0138) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2009 on the taking-up and pursuit of the business of Insurance and Reinsurance (Solvency II);
- “**special resolution**” has the meaning given by paragraph 7 of Schedule 12 to this Act;
- “**subscription**” includes any premium or other sum (however described) payable, in respect of the provision of benefits, by (or on behalf of) a member of a friendly society under the rules of the society;
- “**subsidiary**” is to be construed in accordance with section 13 above; and
