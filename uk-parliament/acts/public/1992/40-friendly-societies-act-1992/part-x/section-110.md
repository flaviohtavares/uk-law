# Friendly Societies Act 1992, section 110

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110 (Extent: N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note). Jurisdiction of magistrates' courts in Northern Ireland.[^key-9cdb2a79f5daa9da5df7dc278d916159]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9cdb2a79f5daa9da5df7dc278d916159] In Northern Ireland, a friendly society or an officer of a friendly society may be prosecuted for a summary offence under this Act before a magistrates' court acting for the county court division in which the registered office of the society is situated. *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9cdb2a79f5daa9da5df7dc278d916159]

(2 (Extent: N.I.) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9cdb2a79f5daa9da5df7dc278d916159] Subsection (1) is without prejudice to the provisions of the Magistrates' Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 as to the jurisdiction of a magistrates' court. *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9cdb2a79f5daa9da5df7dc278d916159]
