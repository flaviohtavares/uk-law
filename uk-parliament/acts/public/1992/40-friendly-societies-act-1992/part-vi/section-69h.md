# Friendly Societies Act 1992, section 69H

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/40

## 69H. IAS group accounts[^key-7a0dce157dc8e73694c580d29bac2f89]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where the committee of management of a friendly society prepares IAS group accounts, it must state in the notes to those accounts that the accounts have been prepared in accordance with UK-adopted international accounting standards[^key-90720a734e7892df9d7545dfe2d26346].
