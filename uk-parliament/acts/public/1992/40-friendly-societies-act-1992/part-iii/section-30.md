# Friendly Societies Act 1992, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Meetings and resolutions.[^c15378111]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 12 to this Act shall have effect with respect to meetings and resolutions of friendly societies and registered branches.
