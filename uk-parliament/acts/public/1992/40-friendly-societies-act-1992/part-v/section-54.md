# Friendly Societies Act 1992, section 54

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Supervision of activities of subsidiaries etc.[^c15380441]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this section “**friendly society group**” means an incorporated friendly society, subsidiaries of that society and bodies jointly controlled by it.

(2) If it appears to the FCA or the PRA[^c-2efb536c0463d1dcf75f322c28aef706] that the activities of subsidiaries of an incorporated friendly society or bodies jointly controlled by it are or may become disproportionate to those of the friendly society group as a whole, it may direct the society—

- (a) to take or refrain from taking steps specified in the direction with a view to securing that the activities in question cease to be or do not become disproportionate; or
- (b) to take steps so specified with a view to securing—
  - (i) that it ceases to have control or joint control of any subsidiary or jointly controlled body in question; or
  - (ii) that any such subsidiary or jointly controlled body is wound up.

(3) If it appears to the FCA or the PRA that any activity of a subsidiary of an incorporated friendly society or of a body jointly controlled by such a society is unsuitable for a member of a friendly society group, it may direct the society—

- (a) to take steps specified in the direction with a view to securing that that activity ceases; or
- (b) to take steps so specified with a view to securing—
  - (i) that it ceases to have control or joint control of the subsidiary or jointly controlled body; or
  - (ii) that the subsidiary or jointly controlled body is wound up.

(4) A direction under this section may specify that the society is to comply with it—.[^c15380471]

- (a) immediately on receipt of a final notice in relation to the direction;
- (b) before the end of such period as may be specified in the direction, beginning with the giving of a final notice in relation to the direction; or
- (c) on the happening of an event subsequent to the giving of such a notice.

(5) A society given a direction under this section must—

- (a) comply with the direction; or
- (b) convert itself into a company in accordance with Part VIII of this Act.

(6)[^c15380481] The FCA or the PRA[^c-953f81ef554276c102fe12c4abf927af] may by written notice to the society—

- (a) vary a direction issued by it[^c-2b7d7675465d6e7dcda11d1281f77286] under this section at the request of the society; or
- (b) revoke a direction issued by it under this section.

(6A)[^c-527f1888bc42c124dafc12962c13ac8f] The FCA must consult the PRA before issuing a direction under this section to a PRA-authorised person or varying such a direction.

(6B) The PRA must consult the FCA before issuing or varying a direction under this section.

(7)[^c-9939c66169340f6f595c00c7e21e8ba0] If a society requests the FCA or the PRA to notify the society as to whether, in the opinion of that authority, it has complied with a direction issued by that authority, the FCA or the PRA (as the case may be) must comply with the request.

(7A) The PRA must send a copy to the FCA of any direction, notice, final notice or notification it issues under this section.

(8)[^c15380521] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9) The FCA[^c-7fa92abdc3ff147c171682a139dfffd0][^c15380531] shall keep a copy—

- (a) of a direction under this section;
- (b) of a notice under subsection (6) above; or
- (ba)[^c15380541] of a final notice varying a direction under this section;
- (c) of a notification under subsection (7) above;

[^c15380551]. . . in the public file of the society.

(10)[^c15380561] “Final notice" means a final notice given under section 390 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, as applied by section 58A(6) below.
