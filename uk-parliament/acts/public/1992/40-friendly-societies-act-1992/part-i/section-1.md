# Friendly Societies Act 1992, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Functions of the Financial Conduct Authority and the Prudential Regulation Authority in relation to friendly societies[^c15377231][^key-8c6e2de657c343de6593e4db804742f0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Financial Conduct Authority (“the FCA”)[^key-0cb83a6bc26fc614046cc204513ae7ee] has the following functions under this Act and the 1974 Act in relation to friendly societies—

- (a) to secure that the purposes of each friendly society are inconformity with this Act and any other enactment regulating the purposes of friendly societies;
- (b) to administer the system of regulation of the activities of friendly societies provided for by or under this Act and the 1974 Act; and
- (c) to advise and make recommendations to the Treasury and other government departments on any matter relating to friendly societies.

(1A)[^key-233f5d6b7e1b0740ce04b3bba3d7a4c8] The function in subsection (1)(c) is also a function of the Prudential Regulation Authority (“the PRA”).

(2) The FCA and the PRA also have[^key-1f3a199644ac9ccee23bcd9d7f843b4a], in relation to such societies, the other functions conferred on them respectively[^key-05a40aa864aa8687e2d7bdd4cb53def6] by or under this Act or any other enactment.
