# Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 158A

>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 158A. Return of social security post.[^c14906201]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A social security authority may require a postal operator (within the meaning of[^c14906211] Part 3 of the Postal Services Act 2011[^c-0afdf9ca198ea66edfed85b5431cad2d] ) to return to the sender social security post sent by or on behalf of the authority which would otherwise be redirected.

(2) A social security authority shall make payments of such amount as the Department considers reasonable in respect of the return of social security post in compliance with a requirement imposed by the authority under subsection (1) above.

(3) In subsections (1) and (2) above “**social security authority**” means—

- (a) the Department;
- (b) the Housing Executive;
- (c) the Secretary of State; or
- (d) any local or other authority administering housing benefit or council tax benefit (other than the Housing Executive).

(4) In subsections (1) and (2) above “**social security post**” means postal packets—

- (a) the contents of which relate to any benefit, contributions or national insurance number or to any other matter relating to social security; and
- (b) which are marked, in a manner approved by the postal operator concerned[^c14906221], with the name and address of the sender and with an indication that they are to be returned rather than redirected.

(5) In this section—

- (a) “**redirected**”, in relation to any postal packet, means delivered to an address other than that indicated by the sender on the packet; and
- (b) “**postal packet**” has the same meaning as in Part 3 of the Postal Services Act 2011[^c-e7f093e9725c4913d47acf07c88bae64] .

(6) Any requirement imposed under subsection (1) above has effect subject to any order under—

- (a) Article 342 of the [^c14906241]Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 or section 371 of the [^c14906251]Insolvency Act 1986 (redirection of bankrupt’s letters to trustee in bankruptcy);
- (b) paragraph 15 of Schedule 1 to the [^c14906261]Solicitors (Northern Ireland) Order 1976 or paragraph 10 of Schedule 1 to the [^c14906271]Solicitors Act 1974 (redirection of letters following intervention by Law Society); or
- (c) paragraph 10 of Schedule 5 to the [^c14906281]Administration of Justice Act 1985 (redirection of letters following intervention by Council for Licensed Conveyancers).
