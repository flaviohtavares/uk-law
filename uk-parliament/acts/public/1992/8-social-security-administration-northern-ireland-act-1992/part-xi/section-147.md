# Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 147

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (186).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/8

## 147. Allocations from social fund.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2] The Department shall allocate amounts for payments from the social fund such as are mentioned in section 134(1)(b) of the Contributions and Benefits Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2] (in this section referred to as “[^c-9cb1a250bc8dfb98a9ddc2263cc52917] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2]**section 134(1)(b) payments *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2]**”) .

(2) The Department may specify the amounts either as sums of money or by reference to money falling into the social fund on the repayment or partial repayment of loans, or partly in the former and partly in the latter manner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2]

(3) Allocations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2]

- (a)[^c-d55de386e95611e867b9aa272592c173] may be for all section 134(1)(b) payments or for any description of such payments; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2]
- (b) may be of different amounts for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2] payments of different descriptions[^c-2df3be20dfdb2f4e90d5d53e603e0b06] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2] ;
- (c) may be made at such time or times as the Department considers appropriate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2]
- (d) may be in addition to any other allocation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2][^c-29925912662d18ec6e11cafc5c362734]....

(3A)[^c-cb79b9b7844de2e02a2a7b8996137604] Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (3)(a) above, descriptions of section 134(1)(b) payments may, in particular, be framed by reference to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2]

- (a) the purposes for which payments are made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2]
- (b) the persons by whom payments are made (including where such persons are located); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2]
- (c) the persons to whom payments are made (including where such persons are located). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2]

(4) The Department may at any time re-allocate amounts previously allocated, and subsections (2) and (3) above shall have effect in relation to a re-allocation as they have effect in relation to an allocation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2]

(5) The Department may give general directions to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2] appropriate officers[^c14904611] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2] or groups of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2] appropriate officers *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2], or to any class of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2] appropriate officers *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2], with respect to the control and management by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2] appropriate officers *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2] or groups of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2] appropriate officers *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2] of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2] any amounts allocated to them[^c-f537a62d9dea0740cc53d9e1842774c6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2] under this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2]

(6)[^c14904661] In this section “appropriate officer” means an officer of the Department who, acting under its authority, is exercising functions of the Department in relation to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2] section 134(1)(b) payments[^c-bd4b1b94dd66cbc620a057a4741319b5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6a1ffc25eda73f95b1bcf9aacd3745b2] .
