# Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 69ZA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (186).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/8

## 69ZA. Overpayments out of the social fund.[^c14895801][^c14895791]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Subject to subsection (2) below, section 69 above shall apply in relation to social fund payments to which this section applies as it applies in relation to payments made in respect of benefits to which that section applies.

(2) Section 69 above as it so applies shall have effect as if—

- (a) in [^c-293e290f677304c6d6e9dc7f88ca4261]... subsection (5A), for the words “reversed or varied on an appeal or has been revised under Article 10 or superseded under Article 11” there were substituted the words “revised on a review under Article 38”;
- (b)[^c-c2641b2ad926d228d6cb28af9052b2d0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) subsections (7) and (10A) were omitted.

(2A)[^c-88203331df0187f3ba7aea07b0657cad] Subsection (9A) of section 69 as it so applies shall have effect as if the reference to amounts recoverable under the provisions mentioned in subsection (8) of that section were to amounts recoverable under subsections (1) and (4) of that section by virtue of subsection (1) above.

(3) This section applies to social fund payments such as are mentioned in section 134(1)(b) of the Contributions and Benefits Act.
