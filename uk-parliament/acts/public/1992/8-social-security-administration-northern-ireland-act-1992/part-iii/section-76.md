## 76. Child benefit - overlap with benefits under legislation of ... member States.[^c-1e2592248f9b1b5fd117a726ef20b901][^c-b244c758d46bfca9e250cfab1c1eb7fb]

*Extent: N.I.*

Regulations may provide for adjusting child benefit payable in respect of any child or qualifying young person[^c-697dda9b52f065822686244f710a064b] in respect of whom any benefit is payable under the legislation of any member State[^c-db87b37b9a92c2f19541b311c14d4e53] .
