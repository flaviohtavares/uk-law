# Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 50

> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 50. Appointment of Commissioners.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Her Majesty may from time to time appoint for Northern Ireland, from among persons who are barristers or solicitors of at least 10 years’ standing—

- (a) a Chief Social Security Commissioner; and
- (b) other Social Security Commissioners[^c-d0a763bc6c355315351249c4adbbe848].

(2) In order to facilitate the disposal of the business of Social Security Commissioners, the Northern Ireland Judicial Appointments Commission may appoint[^c-2938c3bc40e2e8877aedd212d596d90f] —

- (a) a member of the bar of Northern Ireland or solicitor of the Court of Judicature of Northern Ireland[^c-876676368ad8b2e36a40be31bc9b9d97] of at least 10 years’ standing;
- (b) a person who has a 10 year general qualification within the meaning of section 71 of the [^c14894531]Courts and Legal Services Act 1990; or
- (c) an advocate or solicitor in Scotland of at least 10 years’ standing,

to be a Social Security Commissioner (but to be known as a deputy Commissioner) for such period or on such occasions as the Commission determines with the agreement of the[^c-a411a348e08dc2d815c1a93587940949] Department of Justice[^c-0fdc50e136d57da7d83301a7a17ac275].

(3) Schedule 2 to this Act shall have effect for supplementing this section.
