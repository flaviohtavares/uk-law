# Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 167

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (186).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/8

## 167. Interpretation.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-613454dddc91380b19499f6da80ea273] In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “**the Assembly**” means the Northern Ireland Assembly;
- “**the 1975 Act**” means the [^c14910451]Social Security (Northern Ireland) Act 1975;
- “**benefit**” means benefit under the Contributions and Benefits Act and includes[^c14910461] universal credit,[^key-8da0f11bc472b44cb6e4fdfe7a7d6287] state pension under Part 1 of the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2015,[^key-f67717076b86acd5f188b5caf907dea3] a jobseeker’s allowance state pension credit, an employment and support allowance and personal independence payment[^key-da89529630e0d349ed0708dbb334fa34] and bereavement support payment under section 29 of the Pensions Act (Northern Ireland) 2015[^key-d69542118d461190b5ebfa272ee3da99] ;
- “**Christmas bonus**” means a payment under Part X of the Contributions and Benefits Act;
- “**claimant**” (in relation to contributions under Part I and to benefit under Parts II to IV of the Contributions and Benefits Act) means—
  - (a) a person whose right to be excepted from liability to pay, or to have his liability deferred for, or to be credited with, a contribution, is in question;
  - (b) a person who has claimed benefit;

  and includes, in relation to an award or decision, a beneficiary under the award or affected by the decision;
- “**claim**” is to be construed in accordance with “**claimant**”;
- “**claimant**” (in relation to industrial injuries benefit) means a person who has claimed such a benefit and includes—
  - (a) an applicant for a declaration under Article 29 of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998[^c14910471] that an accident was or was not an industrial accident; and
  - (b) in relation to an award or decision, a beneficiary under the award or affected by the decision;
- “[^c14910481]**Commissioner**” means the Chief Social Security Commissioner or any other Social Security Commissioner and includes a tribunal of 2 or more Commissioners constituted under Article 16(7) of the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998;
- [^c14910491]. . .
- [^c14910491]. . .
- “**the Consequential Provisions Act**” means the [^c14910511]Social Security (Consequential Provisions) (Northern Ireland) Act 1992;
- “**Consolidated Fund**” means the Consolidated Fund of Northern Ireland;
- “[^c14910521]**contribution**” means a contribution under Part I of the Contributions and Benefits Act;
- “[^c14910531]**contribution-based jobseeker’s allowance**” has the same meaning as in the Jobseekers (Northern Ireland) Order 1995;
- “**contribution card**” has the meaning assigned to it by section 108(6) above;
- “**the Contributions and Benefits Act**” means the [^c14910541]Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992;
- “[^key-7118a3293d3d67f8c1efdcd106998bcc]**contributory employment and support allowance**” means a contributory allowance under Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007 (employment and support allowance);
- “[^c19111431]**the Department**” means the Department for Social Development but—
  - (a) in sections 109A, 116 and 116B to 116D also includes the Department of Finance and Personnel; and
  - (b) in sections[^c19111461] 2E, 2F, 2G (except in the second reference in subsection (11)(c))[^key-1e80404226d84fc62164eaa19b5ca153] 115D, 115E, 116, 116ZA, 116C and 116D also includes the Department for Employment and Learning;
- “**the Department of the Environment**” means the Department of the Environment for Northern Ireland;
- “**the Department of Finance and Personnel**” means the Department of Finance and Personnel in Northern Ireland; but in sections 116 and 116B also includes the Department of the Environment;[^c14910561]
- “**disablement benefit**” is to be construed in accordance with section 94(2)(a) of the Contributions and Benefits Act;
- “[^c14910571]**the disablement questions**” is to be construed in accordance with section 43 above;
- “**dwelling**” means any residential accommodation, whether or not consisting of the whole or part of a building and whether or not comprising separate and self-contained premises;
- “**the Great Britain Administration Act**” means the [^c14910581]Social Security Administration Act 1992;
- “**the Great Britain Contributions and Benefits Act**” means the [^c14910591]Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992;
- “**the Housing Executive**” means the Northern Ireland Housing Executive;
- “income-based jobseeker’s allowance” has the same meaning as in the Jobseekers (Northern Ireland) Order 1995;[^c14910601]
- “**income-related benefit**” means—
- (a) income support;
- (b)[^key-be9de58d3191c809999a8f5c6e92c72b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^key-be9de58d3191c809999a8f5c6e92c72b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) housing benefit;

- "[^key-d7d6643c4262e110c3cca3a264e1cb26]**income-related employment and support allowance**" means an income-related allowance under Part 1 of the Welfare Reform Act (Northern Ireland) 2007 (employment and support allowance);
- “**industrial injuries benefit**” means benefit under Part V of the Contributions and Benefits Act[^key-709017c66e79b0392f43eed78beb338b]...;
- “[^c14910631]**Inland Revenue**” means the Commissioners of Inland Revenue;
- [^c14910641]. . .
- [^c14910651]. . .
- “**medical examination**” includes bacteriological and radiographical tests and similar investigations, and “**medically examined**” has a corresponding meaning;
- “**medical practitioner**” means—
- (a) a registered medical practitioner; or
- (b) a person outside the United Kingdom who is not a registered medical practitioner, but has qualifications corresponding (in the Department’s opinion) to those of a registered medical practitioner;

- “**medical treatment**” means medical, surgical or rehabilitative treatment (including any course of diet or other regimen), and references to a person receiving or submitting himself to medical treatment are to be construed accordingly;
- “money purchase contracted-out scheme” is to be construed in accordance with[^key-9ed9dc8942539b39a13875fd427fac70][^c14910661] section 3B[^key-a305080f7b5c19c3d01d5cdffeaedcdf] of the Pensions Act;
- “**National Insurance Fund**” means the Northern Ireland National Insurance Fund;
- “**occupational pension scheme**” has the same meaning as in section 1 of the Pensions Act[^c14910671];
- “**the Old Cases Act**” means the [^c14910681]Industrial Injuries and Diseases (Northern Ireland Old Cases) Act 1975;
- [^key-709017c66e79b0392f43eed78beb338b]...
- “**the 1986 Order**” means the [^c14910691]Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1986;
- “pensionable age” has the meaning given by the rules in paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1995;[^c14910701]
- “[^c14910711]**the Pensions Act**” means the Pension Schemes (Northern Ireland) Act 1993;
- “**the Pensions Order**” means the [^c14910721]Social Security Pensions (Northern Ireland) Order 1975;
- “**personal pension scheme**” has the meaning assigned to it by section 1 of the Pensions Act[^c14910731] and “[^c14910741]**appropriate**”, in relation to such a scheme, shall be construed in accordance with section 3B(6)[^key-e427e3fbcef6ffe0763acab8cd901942] of that Act;
- “**prescribe**” means prescribe by regulations and “prescribed” must be construed accordingly[^key-b013b3b1163b7c4bc7d41fe86cba8b3a] ;
- “[^c14910751]**President**” means the President of social security appeal tribunals, medical appeal tribunals and disability appeal tribunals;
- “**regulations**” means regulations made by the Department or the Lord Chancellor under this Act;
- “[^c19111501]**state pension credit**” means state pension credit under the State Pension Credit Act (Northern Ireland) 2002;
- “**statutory provision**” has the meaning assigned to it by section 1(f) of the [^c14910761]Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 ;
- “**tax year**” means the 12 months beginning with 6th April in any year;
- “**widow’s benefit**” has the meaning assigned to it by section 20(1)(e) of the Contributions and Benefits Act.

(2) For the purposes of Part III of the [^c14910771]Northern Ireland Constitution Act 1973 (validity of Measures of the Northern Ireland Assembly, including Orders in Council under the [^c14910781]Northern Ireland Act 1974), provisions of this Act which re-enact provisions of a Measure of the Assembly or such an Order are to be treated as provisions of such a Measure or Order.
