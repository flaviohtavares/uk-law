# Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 85

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Insolvency.

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-10-06).**

Where the intended recipient has been adjudged bankrupt, nothing in the [^c14896421]Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 shall affect the operation of the recoupment provisions.
