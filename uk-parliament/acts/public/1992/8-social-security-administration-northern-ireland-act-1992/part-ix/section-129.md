## 129. Amendments following alterations in Great Britain.

*Extent: N.I.*

Whenever the Treasury[^c14901661] makes an order under section 141, 143, [^c14901671]. . . or 145[^c14901681] of the Great Britain Administration Act (alteration of contributions), the order may also make corresponding provision[^c14901691] for Northern Ireland.
