# Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 130

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (186).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/8

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 130. Revaluation of earnings factors.[^c14901741]

*Extent: N.I.*

Whenever the Secretary of State makes an order under section 148 of the Great Britain Administration Act (revaluation of earnings factors), the Department may make a corresponding order for Northern Ireland[^c14901751].
