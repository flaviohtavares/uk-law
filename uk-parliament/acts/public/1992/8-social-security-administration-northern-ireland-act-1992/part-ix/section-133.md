# Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 133

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (186).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/8

## 133. Rectification of mistakes in up-rating orders.[^c-35b9db277fcb6d1ca4c06eddbe65ba21]

*Extent: N.I.*

Whenever the Secretary of State or the Treasury[^c-924a9cf03980b376ae80a1e2be9364bf] makes an order under section 152 of the Great Britain Administration Act, the Department may make a corresponding order for Northern Ireland.
