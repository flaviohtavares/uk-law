# Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 130AD

## 130AD. Revaluation of new state scheme pension debits and credits[^key-4ce7f39be5a1f31f58d7a5f038fb5cfb]

*Extent: N.I.*

Whenever the Secretary of State makes an order under section 148AD of the Great Britain Administration Act, the Department may make a corresponding order for Northern Ireland.
