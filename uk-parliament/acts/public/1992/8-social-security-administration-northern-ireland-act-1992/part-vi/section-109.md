## 109. Offences by bodies corporate.[^key-b0bf0e3286eb8c2b83da859b640332be]

*Extent: N.I.*

Section 20(2) and (3) of the [^c14898201]Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 (offences by bodies corporate) shall apply to this Act as if it were an enactment within the meaning of section 1(b) of that Act.
