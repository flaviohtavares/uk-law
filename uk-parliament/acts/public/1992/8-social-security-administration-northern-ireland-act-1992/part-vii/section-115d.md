## 115D. Supply of contributions, etc. information held by Inland Revenue.[^c14899551][^c14899561][^c14899531]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This section applies to information which is held for the purposes of functions relating to contributions, health in pregnancy grant,[^key-fca3c07ebc0de0f107195a713ca91cb1] statutory sick pay or statutory maternity pay or functions under Part III of the Pensions Act[^c14899571]—

- (a) by the Inland Revenue, or
- (b) by a person providing services to them, in connection with the provision of those services.

(2)[^key-34e1b5f49279a4a1f73bba3ee81da0a2] Information to which this section applies may, and [^key-827b6aa59d5364db8f1bb5d9fca5907d]... must if an authorised officer so requires, be supplied—

- (a) to the Department, or
- (b) to a person providing services to the Department,

for use for the purposes of functions relating to [^key-827b6aa59d5364db8f1bb5d9fca5907d]... child support [^key-827b6aa59d5364db8f1bb5d9fca5907d]....

(2ZA)[^key-7ecc33673520f213f7fc5aba3fa6f657] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A)[^key-3ad831a6a6901b2b49e42054d90809a4][^c18384541] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) In this section[^c18384561]“authorised officer” means an officer of the Department or the Secretary of State authorised for the purposes of this section by the Department or the Secretary of State.
