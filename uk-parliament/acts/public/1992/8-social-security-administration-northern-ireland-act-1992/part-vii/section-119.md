# Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, section 119

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (186).** Text as at 2026-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/8
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 119. Personal representatives to give information about the estate of a deceased person who was in receipt of income support or supplementary benefit.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The personal representatives of a person who was in receipt of universal credit[^c-774577228e126cafaf30cb87faa1328e] income support an income-based jobseeker’s allowance[^c14900871], state pension credit[^c18409621], an income-related employment and support allowance[^c-b00e7d040d846c3d04e77711418c938a] or supplementary benefit at any time before his death shall provide the Department with such information as it may require relating to the assets and liabilities of that person’s estate.

(2) If the personal representatives fail to supply any information within 28 days of being required to do so under subsection (1) above, then—

- (a) the county court may, on the application of the Department, make an order directing them to supply that information within such time as may be specified in the order, and
- (b) any such order may provide that all costs of and incidental to the application shall be borne personally by any of the personal representatives.
