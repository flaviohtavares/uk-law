# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 298

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (156).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/52

## 298. Minor definitions: general.

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

- act and action each includes omission, and references to doing an act or taking action shall be construed accordingly;
- “agency worker” has the meaning given in regulation 3 of the Agency Workers Regulations 2010;[^c-d5a219e86421010851ca36365c2674b0]
- “[^c20398251]**certificate of independence**” means a certificate issued under—
  - (a) section 6(6), or
  - (b) section 101A(4);
- contravention includes a failure to comply, and cognate expressions shall be construed accordingly;
- dismiss, dismissal and effective date of termination, in relation to an employee, shall be construed in accordance with Part X of the Employment Rights Act 1996[^c14523881];
- “legal professional privilege”, as respects Scotland, means confidentiality of communications;[^c-da79b1046530d359da892a7cc2071e96]
- [^c14523891]. . .
- tort, as respects Scotland, means delict, and cognate expressions shall be construed accordingly.
