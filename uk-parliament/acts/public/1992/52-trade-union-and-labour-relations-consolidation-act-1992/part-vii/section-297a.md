## 297A. Meaning of “voting”[^c-95bcbe52a06b8ba4de59a8df5bfd2028]

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of this Act, the number of persons voting in a ballot includes those who return ballot papers (whether by post or otherwise) or submit electronic voting forms[^c-f30024e6c82db2671d7944ac7b20ccd2] that are spoiled or otherwise invalid.
