# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 289

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (156).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/52

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 289. Employment governed by foreign law.

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of this Act it is immaterial whether the law which (apart from this Act) governs any person’s employment is the law of the United Kingdom, or of a part of the United Kingdom, or not.
