# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 212B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (156).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/52

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 212B. Dismissal procedures agreements.[^c14520051]

*Extent: E+W+S*

ACAS may, in accordance with any dismissal procedures agreement (within the meaning of the [^c14520061]Employment Rights Act 1996), refer any matter to the arbitration of a person appointed by ACAS for the purpose (not being an officer or employee of ACAS).
