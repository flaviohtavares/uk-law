# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 193A

## 193A. Application of section 193 in certain cases involving redundancies of ships’ crew[^c-4e86d3d393a3cf4078a0e1953d9d076a][^c-e1da9903495758e1efdeb6579b1887d5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Section 193 applies with the modifications set out in subsections (2) and (3)[^c-83f03da75ff8f5e08ab533c138bd6c81] if—

- (a) the duty under section 193(1) or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-835f8389b499b229c0ad57e4bfc1204b] 193(2) applies to a proposal to dismiss employees as redundant, and
- (b) some or all of[^c-0fa233feba8c9a3a08c1d9c4de5e4140] the employees concerned are members of the crew of a seagoing ship[^c-216ae98155e587bd7665ef4187a73085] which is registered at a port outside Great Britain.

(2) The employer shall give the notification required by section 193(1) or (2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0b3ae7153da473e7514185be6fae953a] section 193(2) , so far as relating to the members of crew of a ship within subsection (1)(b),[^c-192b56396e8f9d98c20b726aa0fe0227] to the competent authority of the state where the ship[^c-6e4c6a187fe250ae82ca7c4c75bf8166] is registered (as well as[^c-2195c2450aa6bc30da6931e5179303d8] to the Secretary of State).

(3)[^c-2aff7148960752f9590c20026ca94750] Where this subsection applies, section 193 is to be read as if references in subsections (4) and (6) to a notice were to the notice that is required to be given to the Secretary of State.

(4) In this section “**ship**” includes—

- (a) any kind of vessel used in navigation, and
- (b) hovercraft.
