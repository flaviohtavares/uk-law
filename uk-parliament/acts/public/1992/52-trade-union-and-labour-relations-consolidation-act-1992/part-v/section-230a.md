# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 230A

## 230A. Electronic voting[^c-c1e967a51e9c0eba4b2863e25dd559b8]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where electronic voting is a selected means of voting for a ballot, Parts 1 and 2 of Schedule A1A apply in relation to electronic voting in the ballot (and Part 4 of that Schedule applies for the purposes of Parts 1 and 2).
