## 235. Construction of references to contract of employment in sections 226 to 234A.[^c14520881][^key-88d2a0de79b6c7c8a8d0c6482341ee86]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In sections 226 to 234A[^key-7f635aa514142aac637f6fad25f5c353] references to a contract of employment include any contract under which one person personally does work or performs services for another; and “employer" and other related expressions[^c14520891] shall be construed accordingly.
