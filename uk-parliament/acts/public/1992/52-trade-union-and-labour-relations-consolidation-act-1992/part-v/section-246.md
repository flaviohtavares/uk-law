# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 246

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (156).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 246. Minor definitions.[^c14521721]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Part—

- “**date of the ballot**” means, in the case of a ballot in which votes may be cast on more than one day, the last of those days;
- [^c14521731]...
- “**strike**” means (except[^c14521741] for the purposes of section 229(2) (see section 229(2A))[^key-3b3d63f8793a13079e4372e90e19f6b1] any concerted stoppage of work;
- “**working hours**”, in relation to a person, means any time when under his contract of employment, or other contract personally to do work or perform services, he is required to be at work.
