# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 126

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (156).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 126. Appeal against decision of Certification Officer.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An organisation aggrieved by the refusal of the Certification Officer to enter its name in the list of employers’ associations, or by a decision of his to remove its name from the list, may appeal to the Employment Appeal Tribunal on any appealable question[^c20383661].

(2) [^c20383671]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) For the purposes of this section, an appealable question is any question of law[^c20383681] arising in the proceedings before, or arising from the decision of, the Certification Officer.
