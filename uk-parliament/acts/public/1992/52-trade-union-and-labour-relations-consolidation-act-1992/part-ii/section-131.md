# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 131

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (27).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 131. Administrative provisions applying to employers’ associations.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The following provisions of Chapter III of Part I of this Act apply to an employers’ association as in relation to a trade union—

- section 27 (duty to supply copy of rules),

  section 28 (duty to keep accounting records),

  section 32(1), (2), (3)(a), (b) and (c) and (4) to (6)[^c14516101] [^c-e045e8576bd3ec8b396debdade4f09c0]... and sections 33 to 37 (annual return, accounts and audit),
- [^c14516111]sections 37A to 37E (investigation of financial affairs),
- sections 38 to 42 (members’ superannuation schemes),

  section 43(1) (exemption for newly-formed organisations),

  section 44(1),(2) and (4) (discharge of duties in case of organisation having branches or sections), and

  sections 45 and 45A[^c14516121] (offences).

(2) Sections 33 to 35 (appointment and removal of auditors) do not apply to an employers’ association which is registered as a company under the Companies Act 2006[^c20418891]; and sections 36 and 37 (rights and duties of auditors) apply to the auditors appointed by such an association under [^c20413811]. . . Chapter 2 of Part 16 of[^c20413791] that Act[^c20418911].
