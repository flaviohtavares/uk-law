# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 133

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (156).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 133. Amalgamations and transfers of engagements.[^c14516171]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to subsection (2), the provisions of Chapter VII of Part I of this Act (amalgamations and similar matters) apply to unincorporated employers’ associations as in relation to trade unions.

(2) In its application to such associations that Chapter shall have effect—

- (a) as if in section 99(1) for the words from “that voting materials[^c-305b3e079f0ef2f7eca9d0af404d7fe3]" to “accompanied by" there were substituted the words “that, not less than seven days before the ballot on the resolution to approve the instrument of amalgamation or transfer is held, every member is supplied with",
- (b) as if the requirements imposed by sections 100ZA to 100F[^c-3114d9e68c413f1f7547061c7613d32c] consisted only of those specified in sections 100B , 100C(3)(a) and 100CC(1)[^c-71923fdc3fe100fc9b8a66376802f96f] together with the requirement that every member must, so far as is reasonably possible, be given a fair opportunity of voting, [^c20383721]. . ,
- (bza)[^c-4bb43623a11437a141dca4017dcb6f77] as if in section 100CC(1), for “eligible to vote by postal voting” there were substituted “entitled to vote”,
- (ba)[^c20383771] as if the references in sections 101A and 101B to the list of trade unions were to the list of employers' associations, and
- (c) with the omission of sections 101(3) , 101A(3) and (4)[^c20383791] , 103(2A) and (6) to (9)[^c14516181] and 107.
