# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 173

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (156).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 173. Intepretation and other supplementary provisions.[^c14517891][^c20414031][^c-bacec50c18ceaabad5078ca8dab87778]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) For the purposes of sections 168 , 168A[^c14517901] , 168B[^c-4a38e80b4bd3f88ff1dea37fbb7a3d17] and 170 the working hours of an employee shall be taken to be any time when in accordance with his contract of employment he is required to be at work.

(2) The remedy of an employee for infringement of the rights conferred on him by section 168, 168A,[^c14517911] 168B,[^c-bd328f925cf57b5746d3024321235003] 169 or 170 is by way of complaint to an employment tribunal[^c14517921] in accordance with this Part, and not otherwise.

(3)[^c14517931] The Secretary of State may by order made by statutory instrument amend section 168A or 168B[^c-1b8eda466c5d13a933d9023a5c8c3dc0] for the purpose of changing the purposes for which an employee may take time off under that section.

(4) No order may be made under subsection (3) unless a draft of the order has been laid before and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament.
