# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 171

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (27).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/52

## 171. Time limit for proceedings.[^c14517811][^c20414001][^c-bacec50c18ceaabad5078ca8dab87778]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-2003a5b15e4bcf5ba0d48c323d7d46ab] An employment tribunal[^c14517821] shall not consider a complaint under section 168, 168A,[^c14517831] 168B,[^c-9cf3527691a013df216c4ea36794caf9] 169 or 170 unless it is presented to the tribunal—

- (a) within six[^c-c3413b355dd1e61ec2dda0a0cc6c1f6d] months of the date when the failure occurred, or
- (b) where the tribunal is satisfied that it was not reasonably practicable for the complaint to be presented within that period, within such further period as the tribunal considers reasonable.

(2)[^c-b1a427bad9c9ce6c7522993ae0209ed8] Section 292A (extension of time limits to facilitate conciliation before institution of proceedings) applies for the purposes of subsection (1)(a).
