## 264. Awards of the Committee.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Central Arbitration Committee may correct in any award ,or in any decision or declaration of the Committee under Schedule A1,[^c14522091] any clerical mistake or error arising from an accidental slip or omission.

(2) If a question arises as to the interpretation of an award of the Committee or of a decision or declaration of the Committee under Schedule A1,[^c14522101], any party may apply to the Committee for a decision; and the Committee shall decide the question after hearing the parties or, if the parties consent, without a hearing, and shall notify the parties.

(3) Decisions of the Committee in the exercise of any of its functions shall be published.
