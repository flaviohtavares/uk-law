# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 264

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (27).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/52

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 264. Awards etc of the Committee.[^c-70d7e10575f08c65f0ba041d4aee052b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Central Arbitration Committee may correct in any award ,[^c14522091] in any determination, declaration, order or other decision of the Committee under Chapter 5ZA of Part 1,[^c-e2b1be423d0a16255bf60b151a2bfa98] or in any decision or declaration of the Committee under Schedule A1, any clerical mistake or error arising from an accidental slip or omission.

(2) If a question arises as to the interpretation of an award of the Committee or of a decision or declaration of the Committee under Schedule A1,[^c14522101], any party may apply to the Committee for a decision; and the Committee shall decide the question after hearing the parties or, if the parties consent, without a hearing, and shall notify the parties.

(2A)[^c-ad34c12ae9389a6715e25779263ac3da] Subsection (2) does not apply in relation to Chapter 5ZA of Part 1.

(3) Decisions of the Committee in the exercise of any of its functions shall be published.
