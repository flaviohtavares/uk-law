> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (156).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/52

## 56A. Appeals from Certification Officer.[^c14514381]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An appeal lies to the Employment Appeal Tribunal on any question[^c-af2b73cce349f8a14f8d6dd695b1168f] of law[^c-8590ffe8d6f1a6994c8de031bef53161] arising in proceedings before or arising from any decision of the Certification Officer under section 55.
