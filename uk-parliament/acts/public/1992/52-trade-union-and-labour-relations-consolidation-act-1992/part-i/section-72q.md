# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 72Q

## 72Q. Application of sections 73 to 78[^c-06732482af4a141d32acaa360f1d4b74]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Sections 73 to 78 apply in relation to a trade union with a branch or section in Northern Ireland where the head or main office of the trade union is outside Northern Ireland.
