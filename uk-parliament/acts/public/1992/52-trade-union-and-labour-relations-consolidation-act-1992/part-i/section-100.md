# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 100

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (156).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 100. Requirement of ballot on resolution.[^c14515571]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A resolution approving the instrument of amalgamation or transfer must be passed on a ballot of the members of the trade union held in accordance with , so far as applicable,[^key-cea11a8767282296165450cc13f5f18e] sections 100ZA to 100F and Schedule A1A[^key-d006b04fea18253caff700443beeef1e].

(2) A simple majority of those voting is sufficient to pass such a resolution unless the rules of the trade union expressly require it to be approved by a greater majority or by a specified proportion of the members of the union.
