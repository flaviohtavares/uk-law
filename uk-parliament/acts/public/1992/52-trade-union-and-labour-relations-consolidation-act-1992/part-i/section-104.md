# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 104

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (156).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 104. Appeal from decision of Certification Officer.

*Extent: E+W+S*

An appeal lies to the Employment Appeal Tribunal, at the instance of the complainant or the trade union, on any question[^c-b3dce53a691d518921b7fff2d4e0d002] of law[^c-1c0f0c94210e0db8efdf4e2ce0f10667] arising in any proceedings before, or arising from any decision of, the Certification Officer under section 103.
