# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (156).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Appeal against decision of Certification Officer.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) An organisation aggrieved by the refusal of the Certification Officer to enter its name in the list of trade unions, or by a decision of his to remove its name from the list, may appeal to the Employment Appeal Tribunal on any appealable question[^c20381981].

(2) A trade union aggrieved by the refusal of the Certification Officer to issue it with a certificate of independence, or by a decision of his to withdraw its certificate, may appeal to the Employment Appeal Tribunal on any appealable question.

(3) [^c20382031]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) For the purposes of this section, an appealable question is any question of law[^c20382011] arising in the proceedings before, or arising from the decision of, the Certification Officer.
