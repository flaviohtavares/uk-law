# Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (27).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1992/52

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 27. Duty to supply copy of rules.

*Extent: E+W+S*

A trade union shall at the request of any person supply him with a copy of its rules either free of charge or on payment of a reasonable charge.
