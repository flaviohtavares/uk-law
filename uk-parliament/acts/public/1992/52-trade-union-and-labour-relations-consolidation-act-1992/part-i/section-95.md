## 95. Appeals from Certification Officer.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An appeal lies to the Employment Appeal Tribunal on any question[^c-8252ad25fc9d9153c4cb252df9788293] of law[^c-6ecc6ddb252f6401015a870734b74035] arising in proceedings before or arising from any decision of the Certification Officer under this Chapter.
