## 45D. Appeals from Certification Officer.[^c14514141]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An appeal lies to the Employment Appeal Tribunal on any question[^key-3a87d850e4b598be00d77c5955d2e432] of law[^key-436edf40e122328b76a434b4dba547ac] arising in proceedings before or arising from any decision of the Certification Officer under section 24B,[^key-51710062d64e3f45a9d07f1981cb3408] [^key-27ebe5e5e2d37ceaa2f48e7b247ec0ae]... 25, 31 [^key-dd648a9d30d46c8b451119844d9c659d]... or 45C [^key-be09ab09dd39b00d37dea7506f96af03]....
