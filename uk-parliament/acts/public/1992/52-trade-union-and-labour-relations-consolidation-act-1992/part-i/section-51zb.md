## 51ZB. Postal voting[^c-792a0a593f141c0fb302988cb4ace1ba]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where postal voting is a selected means of voting for an election, the requirements in sections 51ZC and 51A apply in relation to postal voting at the election.
