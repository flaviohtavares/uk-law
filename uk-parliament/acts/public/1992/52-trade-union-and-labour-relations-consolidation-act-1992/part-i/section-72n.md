## 72N. Hybrid voting[^key-1a25d560e20f6072872377d3956955df]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where hybrid voting is a selected means of voting for a ballot, Parts 1 and 3 of Schedule A1A apply in relation to hybrid voting in the ballot (and Part 4 of that Schedule applies for the purposes of Parts 1 and 3).
