## 51B. Electronic voting[^c-e0e8f4ab4b79ffa4b9566a86ae8ff6f2]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where electronic voting is a selected means of voting for an election, Parts 1 and 2 of Schedule A1A apply in relation to electronic voting at the election (and Part 4 of that Schedule applies for the purposes of Parts 1 and 2).
