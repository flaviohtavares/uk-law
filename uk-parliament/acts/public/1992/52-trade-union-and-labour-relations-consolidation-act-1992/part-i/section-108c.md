## 108C. Appeals from Certification Officer.[^c14515761]

*Extent: E+W+S*

An appeal lies to the Employment Appeal Tribunal on any question[^c-6e2fc8fc7dae6fdd7f9d7b1a07caf378] of law[^c-6f566e214e31525da2778c66c2ccde33] arising in proceedings before or arising from any decision of the Certification Officer under this Chapter.
