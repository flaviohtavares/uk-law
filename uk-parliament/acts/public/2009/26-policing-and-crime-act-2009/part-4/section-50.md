# Policing and Crime Act 2009, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Review of operation of this Part[^c-20b179c791d13b979e6a33780a125cfb]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State must—

- (a) review the operation of this Part, and
- (b) prepare and publish a report on the outcome of the review.

(2) The report must be published before the end of the period of 3 years beginning with the day on which this Part comes into force.

(3) The Secretary of State must lay the report before Parliament.
