## 48. Supplemental[^c-27332a138d68fd4f2f32e7ea2a2c82f3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-6210376fe19d293b15d251963029291c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Rules of court may provide that an appeal from a decision [^c-6d933a3966e69abdc550f99965f18c7d]... to which this subsection applies may be made without notice being given to the respondent.

(3) Subsection (2) applies—[^c-a4207cbe9492f61013c0412948898bcc]

- (a) to a decision under section 39(4)(a) that an application without notice be dismissed, and
- (b) to a decision to refuse to grant an interim injunction under section 41.

(4)[^c-eb3b551766b280c38b2b5661de08e806] In relation to a respondent attaining the age of 18 after the commencement of proceedings under this Part, rules of court may—

- (a) provide for the transfer of the proceedings from a youth court to the High Court or the county court;
- (b) prescribe circumstances in which the proceedings may or must remain in a youth court.
