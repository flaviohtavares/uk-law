# Policing and Crime Act 2009, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2020-12-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Selling alcohol to children[^c-218af64c3c5782e77bbfcfd69f78beb5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 147A(1)(a) of the Licensing Act 2003 (c. 17) (offence of selling alcohol to children on different occasions) for “3 or more different occasions” substitute “ 2 or more different occasions ”.
