# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1198

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1198. UK expenditure

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-c5491f787f3831d37b10c2135ee26fa0] At least 10%[^c-410e74bd79cd894c68445b8e40442947] of the core expenditure on the film incurred—

- (a) in the case of a British film other than a qualifying co-production, by the company, and
- (b) in the case of a qualifying co-production, by the co-producers,

must be UK expenditure.

(2) The Treasury may by regulations amend the percentage specified in subsection (1).
