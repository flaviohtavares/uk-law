# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 518

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 518. Investment bond arrangements: treatment as securities

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Investment bond arrangements are securities for the purposes of the Corporation Tax Acts.

(2) For those purposes—

- (a) a reference in an enactment to redemption is to be taken as a reference to making the redemption payment, [^c-3d8b4c65157ce2f7cdb279ee12338419]...
- (b) a reference in an enactment to interest is to be taken as a reference to alternative finance return, and[^c-cac3baebc9bdca993621fb636891f147]
- (c) the bond-issuer is to be treated for the purposes of Chapter 4 of Part 13 of CTA 2010 (securitisation companies)[^c-ab91bf0a099f9b2a0069f4a4571ae940] as being party as debtor to a capital market arrangement.

(3) In subsection (2) “**the redemption payment**” has the same meaning as in section 507 (see subsection (1)(d)(ii) of that section).
