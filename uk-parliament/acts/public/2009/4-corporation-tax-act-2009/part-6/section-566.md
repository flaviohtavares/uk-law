# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 566

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 566. Introduction

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section and sections 567 to 569 apply if the relevant company was a party to an investment life insurance contract immediately before the beginning of the first accounting period of the company beginning on or after 1 April 2008.

(2) In those sections—

- “**the deemed surrender**” means the surrender of all the rights under that contract that the relevant company was deemed for the purposes of Chapter 2 of Part 13 of ICTA (life policies etc) to have made [^c-839ea65865772be5a8fe3ddde6a402b3]... under paragraph 6(1) of Schedule 13 to FA 2008 [^c-ef89838b0153916e03d758ff92b7d354]...,
- “**the first accounting period**” means the first accounting period of the company beginning on or after 1 April 2008[^c-6801ea92dc84b0f2d700e4e6b3cad1b9], and
- “**the old contract**” means the contract mentioned in subsection (1).
