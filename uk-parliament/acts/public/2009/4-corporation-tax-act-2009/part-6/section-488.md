## 488. Meaning of “open-ended investment company” etc

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Sections 613 and 615(3) of CTA 2010[^c-c1c67c58e665ddda68e6ab4300839446] (meaning of “open-ended investment company” and “company” and application to parts of umbrella companies) apply for the purposes of this Chapter as they apply for the purposes of Chapter 2 of Part 13 of that Act[^c-f8826188443ef03adee895ab97c40f37].

(2) In this Chapter “**umbrella company**” has the meaning given by section 615 of CTA 2010[^c-a53833bf0c6a015cdc4dbf1cf767356a].
