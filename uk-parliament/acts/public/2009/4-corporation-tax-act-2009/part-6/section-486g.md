# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 486G

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4

## 486G. Consideration to be treated as loan relationship[^key-6336e6020770237a754d0b0028f2374e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Part—

- (a) the consideration for the transfer of the right to relevant receipts is to be treated as a money debt which is owed to the transferee by the person by whom the relevant receipts fall to be paid, and
- (b) the transfer is to be treated as a transaction for the lending of money from which that debt is treated as arising.

(2) For the meaning of “relevant receipts” see section 752(2) of CTA 2010[^key-385eb43ac8041d8a18e2980bc147bb03] or section 809AZA(2) of ITA 2007.
