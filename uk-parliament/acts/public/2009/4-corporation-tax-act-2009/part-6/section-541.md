# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 541

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 541. Debits for deemed interest under stock lending arrangements disallowed[^key-b8e08b823becd40f06c8c1bf8b9c95bf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if a company is the borrower under a stock lending arrangement for the purposes of section 812 of CTA 2010[^key-03db97a5a8a191eb998faa8e6e8da3e6] (which treats such a borrower as having made a payment representative of interest for the purposes of this Chapter).

(2) In accordance with subsection (3) of that section[^key-a3234f55ddb5f82a9b69b35a32b04321] (which prevents deductions or group relief for the borrower in stock lending cases), the company may not bring debits into account for the purposes of Part 5 of this Act[^key-cb98fb26b6852d6c3eed963f7b7086ff] in respect of the representative payment which is treated as having been made under subsection (2) of that section[^key-f6531459407009f67c8fe13efe8f69ba].
