## 1217RB. The UK expenditure condition[^c-ae72997d7de0e438453f66dfd6fd5589][^c-17fdd6343f0b8ecaa90c007672312113]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The “UK[^c-922b2d1f18cd1e709876bb086655b447] expenditure condition” is that at least 10%[^c-0cd5ae50d2594d06563a13a4cc2feee0] of the core expenditure on the production of the concert or concert series incurred by the company is UK expenditure.

(2)[^c-03193a2760846acd84b1c61d9358d5e6] In this Part “**UK expenditure**” means expenditure on goods or services that are used or consumed in the United Kingdom.

(3) Any apportionment of expenditure as between expenditure that is and is not UK expenditure[^c-c9ccf2a10e278798df8f3a509f539ff6] for the purposes of this Part is to be made on a just and reasonable basis.

(4) The Treasury may by regulations—

- (a) amend the percentage specified in subsection (1);
- (b) amend subsection (2).

(5) See also sections 1217T and 1217TA (which are about the giving of relief provisionally on the basis that the UK[^c-9dc830ceee4275fc901b3191fe52b1fe] expenditure condition will be met).
