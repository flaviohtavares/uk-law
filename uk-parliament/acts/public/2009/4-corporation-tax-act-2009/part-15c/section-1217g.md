## 1217G. How a company qualifies for relief[^key-8ee7e720cfeb865939dadc9ef1b64284]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A company qualifies for relief in relation to a theatrical production if—

- (a) it is the production company in relation to the production, and
- (b) the commercial purpose condition (see section 1217GA) and the UK[^key-8bd3d4b1769497a4164e5558fd0d1a53] expenditure condition (see section 1217GB) are met.

(2) There is further provision relating to subsection (1) in section 1217LA (tax avoidance arrangements).
