# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1217I

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>

## 1217I. Introduction to sections 1217IA to 1217IF[^c-8ee7e720cfeb865939dadc9ef1b64284]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a company is treated under section 1217H(3)(a) as carrying on a separate trade (“the separate theatrical trade”), the profits or losses of the trade are calculated for corporation tax purposes in accordance with sections 1217IA to 1217IF.
