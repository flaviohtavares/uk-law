# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1139A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>

## 1139A. Expenditure incurred on payments[^M_F_3b019f05-17f0-4328-8708-f732956e1863]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) References in this Part to expenditure incurred on payments (however expressed) are references to expenditure incurred on payments made before the making of a claim under this Part in relation to that expenditure.
