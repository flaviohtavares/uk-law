> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4

## 1062A. Insurance company to be treated as large company[^c-17243f0557091778ff55ddd001390d4a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An insurance company that carries on life assurance business in an accounting period is not to be treated for the purposes of this Chapter as a small or medium-sized enterprise in relation to that period.
