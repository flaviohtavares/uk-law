# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1062

## 1062. Restriction on losses carried forward where tax credit claimed

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if a company claims an R&D tax credit to which it is entitled for an accounting period.

(2)[^key-609d78f9cd249c2e328a136095ac1e5c] For the purposes of sections 45, 45A and 45B[^key-ec40fcaeea2d4544fea5e9848b663508][^key-8b3b7ec6f99099041a1510da79a3e4df] of CTA 2010 (relief of trading losses against future [^key-798f2062d25d9500c16375019c8bc4c9]... profits) the company's trading loss for the period is treated as reduced by the amount of the surrendered loss for the period.

(3)[^key-e547b900f737d874f647d93daea8b0cc] The “**amount of the surrendered loss**” for the period means the amount of the Chapter 2 surrenderable loss in respect of which the company claims an R&D tax credit for the period.
