# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1142B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>

## 1142B. “R&D claim”[^c-3d59001dc1ab31583cb03a0ab10ae278][^M_F_b17ef39a-e907-4db3-cfc5-793a3f3fad83]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Part an “R&D claim” means a claim under—

- (a) section 1042C[^c-c5cf733d08beb4de6ae2a91909f59c24] (R&D expenditure credits),
- (b) section 1044 (relief for SMEs: additional deduction), or
- (c) section 1054 (entitlement to R&D tax credit).
