# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1119

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1119. “Small or medium-sized enterprise”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part “**small or medium-sized enterprise**” means a micro, small or medium-sized enterprise as defined in Commission Recommendation [(EC) No 2003/361](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/19361/2003), but subject to the qualifications in section 1120 (and see[^M_F_269ae1f6-37aa-4a6d-dbe6-5868771c0b50] sections 1120A and 1120B).

(2) The Treasury may by order amend this section or sections 1120 to[^M_F_524d902a-cadd-4730-a35d-da84ecd19357] 1120B to substitute a different definition of “**small or medium-sized enterprise**” for the purposes of this Part.

(3) This section is subject to section 1081 (insurance companies to be treated as large companies for purposes of Chapter 2[^c-605906dd36f2ab2fbc2021c40cc4526f]).
