# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1126

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1126. Software , data licences, cloud computing services or consumable items: attributable expenditure[^c-42da71d8fb3112c466a31698dbb49b26][^M_F_56a2d94a-1ea0-495d-b75b-2b638b49f8dc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies for the purposes of this Part to identify when expenditure on software , data licences, cloud computing services[^M_F_100b32d1-2491-4782-96c2-f1d3e9f09230] or consumable items is attributable to relevant research and development.

(2) Expenditure on software , data licences, cloud computing services[^M_F_4083f28f-476b-4c39-bf40-0b2e505bd168] or consumable items is so attributable if the software , data licences, cloud computing services or consumable items are employed directly in relevant research and development.

(3) Subsection (4) applies if software , data licences, cloud computing services[^M_F_028ded33-66b6-4c15-fe7f-646916a70df0] or consumable items are partly employed directly in relevant research and development.

(4) The appropriate proportion of the expenditure on the software , data licences, cloud computing services[^M_F_eb3e1481-45a6-4e43-fe65-1a50b33b58ad] or consumable items is treated as attributable to relevant research and development.

(5) Subsection (6) applies if software , data licences, cloud computing services[^M_F_b615a754-57b3-4d86-fd0e-114967b489ba] or consumable items are employed in the provision of services, such as secretarial or administrative services, in support of other activities.

(6) The software , data licences, cloud computing services[^M_F_bf964c98-af54-4aae-fcab-7d1732a26ac3] or consumable items are not, as a result of their employment in the provision of those services, to be treated as themselves directly employed in those activities.

(7)[^c-10eea0638ef25f84d8fcd762a32310de] This section is subject to sections 1126A and 1126B.
