# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1044

## 1044. Additional deduction in calculating profits of trade

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A company is entitled to corporation tax relief for an accounting period if it meets each of conditions A to F[^key-2bcda51596f07e723069ee0bad6dfb4f].

(2) Condition A is that the company is a small or medium-sized enterprise in the period.

(2A)[^key-158b72a9e28a2e11e8a5e5aadea1dc07] Condition B is that the company—

- (a) meets the R&D intensity condition in the period, or
- (b) obtained relief under this Chapter for its most recent prior accounting period of 12 months’ duration, having met the R&D intensity condition in that period.

(3)[^key-330ca7dbbb78de75b77a515866817725] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Condition C is that the company carries on a trade in the period.

(5) Condition D is that the company has qualifying Chapter 2 expenditure which is allowable as a deduction in calculating for corporation tax purposes the profits of the trade for the period.

(5A)[^key-465a74d6f24aa3483bd234e430bb7065] Condition E is that the company makes a loss in the trade in the period.

(5B) Condition F is that the company is not an ineligible company (see section 1142).

(6) For the company to obtain the relief it must make a claim (see Part 9A of Schedule 18 to the FA 1998, and also sections 1045A and[^key-4f284cb7193e5050e450a48fec7e7fc9] 1112F).

[^key-28fd858a3816a4a4a6a0db7083aed90c]...

(7) The relief is an additional deduction in calculating the profits of the trade for the period. The deduction is, in particular, additional to any given under section 87.[^key-a132a9e238bde3345e5ef501f2ac472b]

(8) The amount of the additional deduction is 86%[^key-ee6308d910c1baa967e657a51a66421b] of the qualifying Chapter 2 expenditure.

(9)[^key-4fcafa03ecb1e3fb31fa2adcecf5b7dc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(10) For the meaning of “qualifying Chapter 2 expenditure” see section 1051.
