# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1140A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1140A. Groups[^key-7fee368f9bbe5b6e0d96cb3ab8ddb5af]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Part, a company is in the same group as another company if those companies are in the same group for the purposes of Part 5 of CTA 2010.
