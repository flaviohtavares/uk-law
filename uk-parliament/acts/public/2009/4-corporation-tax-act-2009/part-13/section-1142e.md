## 1142E. Orders and regulations: ancillary provision[^key-adabde9898e72a5ec69cd45cce058666]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any order or regulations under this Part may—

- (a) contain incidental, supplemental, consequential and transitional provision and savings;
- (b) make different provision for different purposes [^key-f7fee011735501831a14bf80ad186626]....
