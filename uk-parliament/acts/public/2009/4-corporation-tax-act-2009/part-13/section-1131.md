# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1131

## 1131. Qualifying expenditure on externally provided workers: other cases[^key-aaa261863707e804147e8f2ff4d2a44a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) a company makes a staff provision payment,
- (b)[^key-21f837137e45bc8080bef605465d1a7a] the company, the staff provider and (if different) the staff controller (or staff controllers) are not all connected, and
- (c) no election is made under section 1130.

(2) The company's qualifying expenditure on externally provided workers is 65% of so much of the staff provision payment as is attributable to qualifying earnings of externally provided workers[^key-27afc21843f841a93cbc4dd1ede1da61].

(3)[^key-3cbe41245fabd18aa9aaa8de250bc42e] In subsection (2) the reference to the staff provision payment is to that payment before any deduction is made from the payment under—

- (a) section 61S of ITEPA 2003,
- (b) regulation 19 of the Social Security Contributions (Intermediaries) Regulations 2000, or
- (c) regulation 19 of the Social Security Contributions (Intermediaries) (Northern Ireland) Regulations 2000.

(4)[^key-0420dd1d91e2dfb13fef37cd60580328] Any apportionment of expenditure of the company necessary for the purposes of this section is to be made on a just and reasonable basis.
