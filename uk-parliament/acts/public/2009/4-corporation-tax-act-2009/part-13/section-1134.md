# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1134

## 1134. Qualifying element of contractor payment: connected persons[^key-3facb8a37b6938b83abafac576597f5b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a)[^key-2d70c7fe7ef92e511982d8278cc3b0c7] a company (“**A**”) makes a contractor payment to another person (“**B**”),
- (b) A and B are connected, and
- (c) in accordance with generally accepted accounting practice, the whole of the [^key-deeb9da15ffb7def4a1da898db9bb8d3]... payment and all of B’s[^key-403212dd50baca68f6f8a2a00079566f] relevant expenditure have been brought into account in determining B’s profit or loss for a relevant period.

(2) The qualifying element of the [^key-a40fb0b5478704811d1445b8627a8d0f]... payment is—

- (a) the entire payment, or
- (b) if less, an amount equal to B’s[^key-b9440faed44bcd2b069a5c88c66781ba] relevant expenditure.

(3) “**Relevant expenditure**” of B[^key-2319d1a7cf516e57dbaae4a45573ddca] means expenditure that—

- (a) is incurred by B[^key-736032a3d09ef2d68c13d29be7c3bada] in carrying on, on behalf of the company, the activities to which the [^key-37a05f9dfc14f9ee9c93e890258e6735]... payment relates,
- (b) is not of a capital nature,
- (c) is incurred on staffing costs, software , data licences, cloud computing services[^M_F_eaef101d-d6ac-4fe1-cb20-dfa75b296b59] or consumable items or relevant payments to the subjects of a clinical trial or is qualifying expenditure on externally provided workers, and
- (e)[^key-c97fb3d0a495b208f6ad7a94165c77f8] is incurred in respect of—
  - (i) research and development that is undertaken in the United Kingdom, or
  - (ii) research and development that is undertaken outside the United Kingdom and to which section 1138A applies.

(4) “**Relevant period**” means a period—

- (a) for which accounts are drawn up for B[^key-b71f384442f69da45393a454a21f5d86], and
- (b) that ends not more than 12 months after the end of A’s[^key-5aabc77c3800212c2b6c48e85ad01c6a] period of account in which the contractor[^key-9edf4837574dc47923271327dea550a7] payment is, in accordance with generally accepted accounting practice, brought into account in determining A’s profit or loss.

(5)[^key-7692eaad31926c542d7f2405bb156603] In section 1123 (staffing costs) and sections 1127 to 1131 (qualifying expenditure on externally provided workers) as they apply for the purposes of subsection (3)(c), references to a company are to be read as references to B.

(6) Any apportionment of expenditure of A or B[^key-bd5898ff1cc16950c596017c74a0c3cc] necessary for the purposes of this section is to be made on a just and reasonable basis.
