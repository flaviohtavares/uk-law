# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1316

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1316. Meaning of “connected” persons and “control”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 1122 of CTA 2010[^key-25bf1e70f9797e4d188468b2aca6f536] (how to tell whether persons are connected) applies for the purposes of this Act unless otherwise indicated (whether expressly or by implication).

(2) Section 1124 of CTA 2010[^key-c82cb456717fd569518573f0bc17d929] (meaning of control in relation to a body corporate) applies for the purposes of this Act unless otherwise indicated (whether expressly or by implication).
