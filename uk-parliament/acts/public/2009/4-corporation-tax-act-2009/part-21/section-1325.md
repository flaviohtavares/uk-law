## 1325. Transitional provisions and savings[^c1967858]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 2 (transitionals and savings) has effect.

(2) The Treasury may by order make transitional or saving provision in connection with the coming into force of this Act [^key-5d3c6c8f1feff399b1e77a8c25f006d9]... .
