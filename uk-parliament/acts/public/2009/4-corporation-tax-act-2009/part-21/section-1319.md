## 1319. Other definitions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act, except where the context otherwise requires—

- [^c-4a6524c026fefea7353a81007a8fb114]...
- [^c-4a6524c026fefea7353a81007a8fb114]...
- “**credit union**” means a society registered as a credit union under the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014[^c-1f87efeb6a1d700fab05045aa7f587b7] or the Credit Unions (Northern Ireland) Order 1985 (S.I. 1985/1205 (N.I. 12)),
- “**dividend ordinary rate**” means the rate of income tax specified in section 8(1) of ITA 2007,
- “**houseboat**” means a boat or similar structure designed or adapted for use as a place of human habitation,
- “**the Mergers Directive**” means Council Directive [2009/133/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2009/133)[^c-ed3289c598b34f4c48cc7f76fc00d22f],
- “**national insurance contributions**” means any contributions under—
  - (a) Part 1 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 (c. 4), or
  - (b) Part 1 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 (c. 7),
- [^c-4a6524c026fefea7353a81007a8fb114]...
- “**normal self-assessment filing date**”, in relation to a tax year, means the 31 January following the tax year,
- [^c-4a6524c026fefea7353a81007a8fb114]...
- “**SCE**” means a European Cooperative Society within the meaning of Council Regulation [(EC) No. 1435/2003](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2003/1435) on the Statute for a European Cooperative Society,
- “**SE**” means a European public limited-liability company (or Societas Europaea) within the meaning of Council Regulation [(EC) No. 2157/2001](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2001/2157) on the Statute for a European company,
- “[^c-29bb49b750aeaaa54b1ddec6229da364]**statutory insolvency arrangement**” means—
  - a voluntary arrangement that has taken effect under, or as a result of, the Insolvency Act 1986, the Insolvency (Northern Ireland) Order 1989 or schedule 4 to the Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 2016[^c-f58f52324f4d90f3e87eb9fef8d20141],
  - a compromise or arrangement that has taken effect under Part 26 or 26A[^c-a9414c035fb9c1ff2545d171aba84651] of the Companies Act 2006, or
  - an arrangement or compromise of a kind corresponding to any of those mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b) that has taken effect under, or as a result of, the law of a country or territory outside the United Kingdom,
- [^c-4a6524c026fefea7353a81007a8fb114]...
- [^c-4a6524c026fefea7353a81007a8fb114]...
- [^c-4a6524c026fefea7353a81007a8fb114]...
