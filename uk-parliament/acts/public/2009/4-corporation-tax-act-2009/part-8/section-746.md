# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 746

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 746. “Non-trading credits” and “non-trading debits”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part credits and debits in respect of intangible fixed assets that are not within sections 747 to 749 are referred to respectively as “non-trading credits” and “non-trading debits”.

(2) See also—

- (a) section 781(5) (character of credits and debits brought into account as a result of section 780),
- (b)[^c-80873a5ce6b7533ab652a891e4ecdfbe] section 793A (effect of election to reallocate charge within group),
- (ba)[^c-33557ba5271336daa5531981fca6d461] sections 879C(3), 879I(3), 879K(5) and 879O(3)(b) (debits in respect of goodwill and certain other assets treated as non-trading debits), and
- (c) section 901[^c-a698f128f1cafdc1cbc818a6bb392c01] (insurance companies: effect of application of the I minus E basis: non-trading amounts).
