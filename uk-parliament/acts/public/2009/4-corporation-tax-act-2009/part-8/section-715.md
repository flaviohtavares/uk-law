# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 715

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 715. Application of this Part to goodwill

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part applies to goodwill as it applies to an intangible fixed asset.

(2) Subsection (1) is subject to any indication to the contrary (see, in particular,[^c-d93210ebe6d7dd544c89e9c611f349c4] Chapter 15A (debits in respect of goodwill and certain other assets)).[^c-fe9d7026c76d763eaf60613f870b8e64]

(3) In this Part “**goodwill**” has the meaning it has for accounting purposes (and includes internally-generated goodwill)[^c-8a29788cf2c34bf0d64e3b610d57d456].

(4)[^c-26d3c008963f1945051b29b4f6702453] For the purposes of this Part, goodwill is treated as created in the course of carrying on the business in question.
