# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 803

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 803. Non-commercial purposes etc

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part does not apply to an intangible fixed asset so far as it is held—

- (a) for a purpose that is not a business or other commercial purpose of the company, or
- (b) for the purpose of activities in respect of which the company is not within the charge to corporation tax , otherwise than as a result of Chapter 3A of Part 2[^key-ac97488c09339938d9c133f77ea84e1d].
