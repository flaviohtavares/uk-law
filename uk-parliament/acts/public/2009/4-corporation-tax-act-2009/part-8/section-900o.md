# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 900O

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 900O. Interpretation[^c-7877849576a67a8a66d940ccbb3715fc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- “**restricted asset**” has the same meaning as in Chapter 16A, and
- “**standard intangible fixed asset**” means an intangible fixed asset that is neither a pre-FA 2002 asset nor a restricted asset.
