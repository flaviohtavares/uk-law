## 841. “Participator” and “associate”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Chapter “**participator**”, in relation to a close company, has the meaning given by section 454 of CTA 2010[^c-75977f241282625bb87c24be6f2e03e3], except as provided in subsection (2).

(2) “**Participator**” does not include a person just because the person is a loan creditor of the company within the meaning given by section 453 of CTA 2010[^c-0085806f6d2b0262242d5d2a8d87a314].

(3) In this Chapter “**associate**”, in relation to a participator in a close company, has the meaning given by section 448 of CTA 2010[^c-796ceee290d0371cd4a2c56a3d68aa7d].
