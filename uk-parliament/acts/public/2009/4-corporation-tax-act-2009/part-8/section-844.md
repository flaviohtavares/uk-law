## 844. Overview of Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter sets out special rules relating to transactions between related parties.

(2) Sections 845 to 849A[^key-a030527ef49c2c25afb9f51fd0bdf17d] are about the rule that transfers between a company and a related party are treated as being at market value.

(2ZA)[^key-efac6925ca64a21b8166a8baed02fda2] Sections 849AB to 849AD make provision for the grant of a licence or other right by a company to a related party, or vice versa, to be treated as being at market value.

(2A)[^key-d03c24824f6fe4737be8dd85ac0ebd18] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Sections 850 and 851 set out other rules for transactions involving related parties.

(4) See Chapter 12 for the meaning of “related parties”.
