# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 997

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 997. No deduction for expenses in providing dividend shares

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No deduction is allowed to a company for expenses in providing shares that are acquired on behalf of employees under a Schedule 2[^key-057951852bf9488ddf30faa0a7591740] share incentive plan as dividend shares.

(2) This is subject to section 988.
