# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1256

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1256. Overview of Part

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part contains some special rules about partnerships.

(2) For restrictions that in some circumstances affect relief for losses, and certain other reliefs, for a company that is a member of a partnership see Chapter 3 of Part 22 of CTA 2010 (transfer of relief within partnerships)[^key-affc8f4e310b5e58ac82cd4066efe51a].
