# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1262

## 1262. Allocation of firm's profits or losses between partners[^c-10b4f0d2f8be14be567fb9833f85804f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-c00957c1f2b96e3b32667e5e25922cb1] For any accounting period of a firm a partner's share of a profit or loss of a trade carried on by the firm is determined for corporation tax purposes in accordance with the firm's profit-sharing arrangements during that period.

This is subject to sections 1263 to 1264A[^c-699d176ef694944a620adcb3d714cda7] and section 12ABZB of[^c-044d5057ebf8d03354cd60f954d458ab] TMA 1970 (partnership return is conclusive).

(2) If a firm makes qualifying charitable donations[^c-f1cc9e76107c20aebca6b740f9b911fd], a partner's share of the donations[^c-daa926e1e143a7ed005174c0e7b58fbb] is determined for corporation tax purposes in accordance with the firm's profit-sharing arrangements during the accounting period of the firm in which the donations are made[^c-3df3803aaf22f7e35416761a19c98ee3].

(3)[^c-5ddfffdec882ceb381a83e65918e54c2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) In this section and sections 1263 and 1264 “**profit-sharing arrangements**” means the rights of the partners to share in the profits of the trade and the liabilities of the partners to share in the losses of the trade.
