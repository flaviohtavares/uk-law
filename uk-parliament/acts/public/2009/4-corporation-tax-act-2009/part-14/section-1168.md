## 1168. Restriction on carrying forward expenses payable where tax credit claimed

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if a company claims a BLAGAB tax credit[^c-4747d1254b190481b1563a9870b4eeb8] to which it is entitled for an accounting period.

(2) For the purposes of section 73 of FA 2012[^c-1371824938bdfefeda6b0d6efe7ce642] the amount which may be—

- (a) carried forward from the accounting period under that section as excess BLAGAB expenses[^c-8c34d4f443758c89bb2a3968afc8c59d], and
- (b) brought into account in accordance with step 5 in section 76 of FA 2012[^c-3a1fbb781ec651307f58aede1699594d],

is treated as reduced by the amount of the surrendered loss for the period.

(3) The “**amount of the surrendered loss**” for the period means the amount of the qualifying BLAGAB loss[^c-97aa53f36f2791eacbdbdae60574b194] in respect of which the land remediation tax credit is claimed for the period.
