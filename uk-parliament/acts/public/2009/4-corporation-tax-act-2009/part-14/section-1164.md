# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1164

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1164. Entitlement to tax credit

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A company is entitled to a BLAGAB tax credit[^key-877c907dd7cc3a139579b9f06b9f1082] for an accounting period if it has a qualifying BLAGAB loss[^key-e186e9e801bc74d93c5fdbe7e36f226a] in the period (see section 1165).

(2) For the company to obtain a BLAGAB tax credit[^key-d785313bd2c1495aa1cd89b9ffeae713] in respect of all or part of the qualifying BLAGAB loss[^key-c9a19946e19cea1f49886433527ecf4b] it must make a claim.

(3) The amount of a BLAGAB tax credit[^key-364ec197311b6674d73a6e7b434cf697] to which the company is entitled is determined in accordance with section 1166.

(4) See also section 1168, which restricts the carry forward of expenses payable where a company claims a BLAGAB tax credit[^key-298855ce43618185625da091f9e635f1].
