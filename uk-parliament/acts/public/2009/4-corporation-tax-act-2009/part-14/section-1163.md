# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1163

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1163. No relief if company responsible for contamination or dereliction or polluter has interest[^c-f705cf89d4e549d0de68de71e1470366]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-430a83cc2c8bf3ead0d25dc2da6194b9] A company is not entitled to relief under section 1161 or 1162[^c-0a5ba0852254173e3e1f3ef916beaf6a] in respect of expenditure on land all or part of which is in a contaminated or derelict state if the land is in a contaminated or derelict[^c-e36dbf13d37bd70e8db73fe914300bb1] state wholly or partly as a result of any thing done, or omitted to be done, at any time by—

- (a) the company, or
- (b) a person with a relevant connection to the company (see section 1178).

(2)[^c-f36c9b463d9ba56fd75261f998ce1174] A company is not entitled to relief under this Chapter in respect of expenditure on land all or part of which is in a contaminated or derelict state if—

- (a) the land is in that state wholly or partly as a result of any thing done, or omitted to be done, by a person not within subsection (1), and
- (b) that person, or a person connected with that person, has a relevant interest in the land.

(3) For the purposes of subsection (2) a person has a relevant interest in land if—

- (a) the person holds any interest in, right over or licence to occupy the land (including an option to acquire any such interest, right or licence in any circumstances), or
- (b) has disposed of any estate or interest in the land for a consideration that to any extent reflects the impact, or likely impact, on the value of the land of the remediation of its contamination or dereliction.
