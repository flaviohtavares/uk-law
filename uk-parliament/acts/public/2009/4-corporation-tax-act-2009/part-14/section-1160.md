## 1160. Provision in respect of I minus E basis

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter applies[^c-7d81fd44b40c62de2ff802d48bede0fd] if, for an accounting period, an insurance company is charged to tax in respect of its basic life assurance and general annuity business in accordance with the I - E rules[^c-409cd76c9101325bdb170a1667c40c8e].
