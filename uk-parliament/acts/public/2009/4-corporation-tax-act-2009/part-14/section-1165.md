## 1165. Meaning of “qualifying BLAGAB loss”[^key-0ec9de2268096aa6a471a2a6863f6214]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Chapter a company has a “qualifying BLAGAB loss”[^key-39fffd31eac077fa0185a71a79f7870d] in an accounting period (“**the relevant accounting period**”) if in the period—

- (a) it is entitled to relief under section 1161 or 1162[^key-f81f247c31844bc09cdefa48f96c2063], and
- (b) an amount falls to be carried forward to a subsequent accounting period under section 73 of FA 2012 as excess BLAGAB expenses[^key-29264f2c483cbf16b801f9856919e950].

(2) In determining for the purposes of subsection (1)(b) whether there is an amount which falls to be carried forward to a subsequent accounting period under section 73 of FA 2012 as excess BLAGAB expenses[^key-f8a83e5e2192c04adc434a54e668c6e6], no account is to be taken of the amounts specified in subsection (3).

(3) Those amounts are amounts—

- (a) brought forward from an earlier accounting period, and
- (b)[^key-3455b0ca197f80b8d239e365ef83f315] taken into account in calculating for the purposes of section 73 of FA 2012 the amount of adjusted BLAGAB management expenses of the company for the relevant accounting period as a result of—
  - (i) the previous application of section 73 or 93 of FA 2012, or
  - (ii) the carry forward to the relevant accounting period of an amount under section 391 of this Act (surplus deficit).

(4) The amount of the qualifying BLAGAB loss[^key-107eef8b3ac4822584916617d43d8a29] is—

- (a) the amount which falls to be carried forward as mentioned in subsection (1)(b), or
- (b) if less, 150% of the qualifying Chapter 4 expenditure in respect of which the relief was obtained.
