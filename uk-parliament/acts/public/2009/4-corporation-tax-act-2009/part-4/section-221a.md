# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 221A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 221A. Sums to which sections 217 to 221 do not apply[^c-f3d6caca13fbdb8c5ce7cabbcbed95fb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if a grant of a lease constitutes a disposal of an asset for the purposes of section 758(2)(b) or 763(2)(a) of CTA 2010 (disposals under finance arrangements).

(2) Sections 217 to 221 do not apply in relation to a premium paid in respect of the grant.
