## 931H. Distributions derived from transactions not designed to reduce tax[^c-c2daeff2dc2f1c78d3413089f450a47e][^c-9e383ed2ef5a61864aa58309ce713efe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A dividend or other distribution[^c-24e150952209ee13ad521a9ba5a1da9e] falls into an exempt class if it is made[^c-884b251b97e68b2fc1c3197642f8f82a] in respect of relevant profits.

(2) In this section “**relevant profits**” means any profits available for distribution at the time that the distribution is made[^c-00fa4b394131032f839c67d105eed312], other than profits that reflect the results of a transaction, or of one or more of a series of transactions, where—

- (a) the transaction or series of transactions achieve a reduction (other than a negligible reduction) in United Kingdom tax, and
- (b) the purpose or one of the main purposes of that transaction or series of transactions is to achieve that reduction.

(3) A distribution[^c-bbfb875ff40e152d72b6a1befe9eb4d6] that falls into an exempt class otherwise than by virtue of this section is for the purposes of this section treated, so far as possible, as made[^c-d373f49a32f8c5ee5315a6874096b2fb] in respect of relevant profits.

(4) Any other distribution[^c-6116f66020f0de7895a3f077fc11a98f] is for the purposes of this section treated, so far as possible, as made[^c-69fa229e003189ae04e0860a30e47cb4] in respect of profits other than relevant profits.

(5) Where by virtue of subsection (4) part of a distribution[^c-58ad2e383cf0ef6d4714e6fef238c5ef] is treated as made[^c-153d2b31976c353b8a7ac81a46f282b0] in respect of relevant profits and part is treated as made in respect of profits other than relevant profits, the two parts are treated for the purposes of this Part and Part 2 of TIOPA 2010[^c-4e6084595bd268aed6a7fa2d48213406] (double taxation relief) as separate distributions[^c-2e6cd59f95444124df49d7d2bf3d057d].
