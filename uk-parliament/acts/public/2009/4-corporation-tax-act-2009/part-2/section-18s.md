# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 18S

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18S. Other interpretation[^key-7ecf91942442bc685d5c039773680a41]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- “**company tax return**” has the same meaning as in Schedule 18 to FA 1998 (see paragraph 3(1));
- “**double taxation arrangements**” means arrangements that have effect under section 2(1) of TIOPA 2010;
- “**the OECD model**” means the Model Tax Convention on Income and on Capital approved by the OECD Council on 18 November 2025[^key-0a6e77183f0f279edc22b072e949756a] as it may be amended or replaced from time to time[^key-b48e9c23a6cba921b021d4054d7f3de1];
- “**small company**” means a micro or small enterprise, as defined in the Annex to Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC of 6 May 2003.
