# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Exclusion of charge to income tax

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-259890defd57d3272a284512c8b16960] The provisions of the Income Tax Acts relating to the charge to income tax do not apply to income of a company if—

- (a) the company is UK resident, or
- (b)[^c-4c4f7b4a28c367795b9e077d296293c4] the company is not UK resident and it is chargeable to corporation tax in respect of the income, or would be so chargeable but[^c-d3ddc745ae83a46b6b03287ca00f952b] is not as a result of—[^c-562335573cd3eedbe1c67d060b7de425]
- (i) an exemption, or
- (ii) the application of sections 1142 to 1144 of CTA 2010 (circumstances in which a company is not regarded as having a permanent establishment).

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to income accruing to a company in a fiduciary or representative capacity.
