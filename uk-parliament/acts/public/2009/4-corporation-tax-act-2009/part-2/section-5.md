# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Territorial scope of charge

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A UK resident company is chargeable to corporation tax on income[^c-75dabe389329f7acd2304ba446907841] on all its profits wherever arising (but see Chapter 3A for an exemption from charge in respect of profits of foreign permanent establishments)[^c-569de9c29cbe08fb60c27eff2e3a9c47].

(2)[^c-f3d5d2aab5eecec357dc2e69bc541bb4] A non-UK resident company is within the charge to corporation tax on income[^c-6cb1b2ef1d202af55dfe76e2fce9a938] only if—

- (a) it carries on a trade of dealing in or developing UK land (see section 5B), [^c-9591699123ffdf697e7da48742ea3e8e]...
- (b) it carries on a trade in the United Kingdom (other than a trade of dealing in or developing UK land) through a permanent establishment in the United Kingdom,[^c-575158e90f4a817bf06044693cf10b99]
- (c) it carries on a UK property business, or
- (d) it has other UK property income.

(2A)[^c-d645a54a0bc491e8810b084f748e7809] A non-UK resident company which carries on a trade of dealing in or developing UK land is chargeable to corporation tax on income[^c-fbf2043dd76e2076715a534c7430db98] on all its profits wherever arising that are profits of that trade.

(3) A non-UK resident company which carries on a trade in the United Kingdom through a permanent establishment in the United Kingdom is chargeable to corporation tax on income[^c-a9b7efcb5b8a175dbd316d91d1f99126] on all its profits wherever arising that are chargeable profits as defined in section 19 (profits attributable to its permanent establishment in the United Kingdom).

(3A)[^c-c6059c09f3b8b5e824afe233d6b83a25] A non-UK resident company which carries on a UK property business is chargeable to corporation tax on income on all its profits that are—

- (a) profits of that business, or
- (b) profits arising from loan relationships or derivative contracts that the company is a party to for the purposes of that business.

(3B) A non-UK resident company which has other UK property income is chargeable to corporation tax on income on all its profits that—

- (a) consist of that income, or
- (b) are profits arising from loan relationships or derivative contracts that the company is a party to for the purposes of enabling it to generate that income.

(4) Subsections (1) and (2A) to (3B)[^c-dbbc4d4051ab92b24b191277558b2467] are subject to any exceptions provided for by the Corporation Tax Acts.

(5)[^c-d0b0f5d5891f6c797b8a65fd8fa40a85] The territorial scope of the charge to corporation tax on chargeable gains is given by section 2B of TCGA 1992.

(6)[^c-a34dd152157634e537f6facc3662e6c4] In this Part “other UK property income” means income dealt with by any of the following Chapters of Part 4—

- (a) Chapter 7 (rent receivable in connection with a UK section 39(4) concern);
- (b) Chapter 8 (rent receivable for UK electric-line wayleaves);
- (c) Chapter 9 (post-cessation receipts arising from a UK property business).
