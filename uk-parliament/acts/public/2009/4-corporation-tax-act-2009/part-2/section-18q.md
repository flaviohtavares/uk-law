## 18Q. Insurance companies[^key-7ecf91942442bc685d5c039773680a41]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) So much of the profits or losses of a company as consists of profits or losses arising from basic life assurance and general annuity business [^key-39ae5eab029b340d856a978c8646363d]... is not to be regarded as forming part of a relevant profits amount or relevant losses amount of the company for the purposes of this Chapter.
