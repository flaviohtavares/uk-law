## 35. Charge to tax on trade profits[^c-4dc037818e414b2b9d243b6857714bf9][^c-49cfdcab754b45946ce9eaa1e382928e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The charge to corporation tax on income applies to the profits of a trade.
