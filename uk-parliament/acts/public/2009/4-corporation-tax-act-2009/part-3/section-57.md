## 57. Car ... hire: supplementary[^c-66702631827c2dc2795fa233b16c2f8e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 56 “**car [^c-0d9f9a7445c463edc2838a6798bceda9]...**” means a mechanically propelled road vehicle other than [^c-70d9c70ac0897104da95006d211d3cd3]...—

- (za)[^c-4ec0b64162e6247b4fb8f0c9d6685fe5] a motor cycle (within the meaning of section 185(1) of the Road Traffic Act 1988),
- (a) a vehicle[^c-7d2d2447df539fe19db3f36c321c7ec1] of a construction primarily suited for the conveyance of goods or burden of any description, or
- (b) a vehicle[^c-7519a5859789cc59e11817c5ddca56b6] of a type not commonly used as a private vehicle and unsuitable for such use.

(1A)[^c-caf2b9b12417f5f67f18a2b81e85f3c2] In section 56—

- “ **a car that has low CO2 emissions** ” has the same meaning as in section 104AA of CAA 2001 (special rate expenditure: main rate car);
- “ **electrically propelled** ” has the meaning given in section 268B of that Act.

(2) In section 56 “**a qualifying hire car [^c-5b413addfa0235dec654a0fcba4a882c]...**” means a car [^c-5b413addfa0235dec654a0fcba4a882c]... which—

- (a) is hired under a hire-purchase agreement [^c-43785b1c4eac622b3f45ac098a31a2c9]... under which there is no option to purchase,
- (b) is hired under a hire-purchase agreement under which there is an option to purchase exercisable on the payment of a sum equal to not more than 1% of the retail price of the car [^c-5b413addfa0235dec654a0fcba4a882c]... when new, or
- (c)[^c-c146827d564d66606154ddd4f90b52eb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c-153b2b6f93c6bfccda63138e86e26b3d] is leased under a long-funding lease (within the meaning of section 70G of CAA 2001).

(3)[^c-d6095eaa36ea46e21a983aeeb5b7e79f] For this purpose “**hire-purchase agreement**” has the meaning given by section 1129 of CTA 2010.

(6) In this section [^c-4e11c0f88efd4531a563236a1f22e60e]... “**new**” means unused and not second-hand.
