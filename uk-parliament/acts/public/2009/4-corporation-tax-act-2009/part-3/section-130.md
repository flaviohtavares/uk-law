# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 130

## 130. Insurers receiving distributions etc[^c-389920447f0bb0cc80f7651ed5fc6ef4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies for the purpose of calculating the trading profits of—

- (a) insurance business other than business in relation to which section 111 of FA 2012 applies[^c-1565b802ef0d3b449f79f0271adac134], or
- (b) any category of such business.

(2) A receipt that is exempt for the purposes of Part 9A (company distributions) is not brought into account in calculating the profits of the trade.
