# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 439

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 439. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Chapter—

- “**company**” means any entity listed as a company in Part A of Annex I[^key-f7c45250bfb6fa596e4bc1cabd1520cb] to the Mergers Directive, and
- “**co-operative society**” means a society registered under the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014[^key-1f87efeb6a1d700fab05045aa7f587b7] or a similar society governed by the law of a member State [^key-cdfecf57706512b06cb67593124ede84]....

(2) For the purposes of this Chapter, a company is resident in a relevant state[^key-45eae59c8a235612080a2eef3c848bf7] if—

- (a) it is within a charge to tax under the law of the relevant state[^key-4dd4cf58f8d183d5d245b057cb9fad31] as being resident for that purpose, and
- (b) it is not regarded, for the purpose of any double taxation relief arrangements to which the relevant state is a party, as resident in a territory not within a relevant state.
