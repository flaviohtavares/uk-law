## 362. Parties becoming connected where creditor's rights subject to impairment adjustment etc[^key-4e5eac5855cfaed5973f003d77485d79][^key-012322b966bc7a6dfe94c065f3862387]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) a company (“D”) is a party to a loan relationship as debtor, and[^key-42f81265273b0fcb5e3d695015cee051]
- (b) another company (“C”) which—
  - (i) is a party to the loan relationship as creditor, and
  - (ii) is not connected with D,

  becomes connected with D, [^M_F_44bc4e91-668b-43c4-b02e-17925c10b4e1]...
- (c)[^key-b70dc9b158a69f0b4ea4549d1b30e37f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) C is treated as releasing its rights under the loan relationship when C and D become connected.

(3)[^key-6fdee70f080cce9a0b302cb1ee613308] The amount treated as released is the amount (if any) by which the pre-connection carrying value in D's accounts exceeds the pre-connection carrying value in C's accounts.

(4) In subsection (3)—

- “**the pre-connection carrying value in D's accounts**” means the amount that would be the carrying value of the liability representing the loan relationship in D's accounts if a period of account had ended immediately before C and D became connected, and
- “**the pre-connection carrying value in C's accounts**” means—
  - (a) in any case where C was a party to the loan relationship as creditor on the last day of the period of account ending immediately before the one in which C and D became connected, the cost of the asset representing the loan relationship which would be given on that day on an amortised cost basis of accounting, and
  - (b) in any other case, the amount or value of any consideration given by C for the acquisition of the asset representing the loan relationship.

(5) For the purposes of subsection (4) no account is to be taken of—[^key-95f0edb7380ae02c7cc05bced5b17a1c]

- (a) accrued amounts, or[^key-4e40ce16fc243554716a26332748834a]
- (b) amounts paid or received in advance, [^key-8f028321319795d3d1f572446f51fb32]...
- (c)[^key-8f028321319795d3d1f572446f51fb32] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^key-8c5d4dd3dbe760f532c6c85a982bbb22] Subsections (2) and (3) are subject to section 362A (corporate rescue: debt released shortly after connection arises).
