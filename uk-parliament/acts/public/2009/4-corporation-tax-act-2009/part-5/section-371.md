# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 371

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 371. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Chapter—

- “[^c-ca1edce0ad2c773990533529a542bb75]**consortium company**” means a trading company, as defined by section 185(1) of CTA 2010, that is owned by a consortium or a holding company that is so owned,
- “**debtor consortium company**” has the same meaning as in section 364 (see section 364(2)),
- “**group accounting period**” is to be read in accordance with section 370,
- “**group member**” has the same meaning as in section 364 (see section 364(2)),
- “[^c-e4cda6e483e257d01f2a543f62e8ed35]**group relief**” has the meaning given by section 364(4),
- “[^c-fe563a08ebb2bf31b1978cbb1ab79774]**holding company**” has the same meaning as in Part 5 of CTA 2010 (see section 185(2) of that Act),
- “**member**”, in relation to a consortium, has the same meaning as in Part 5 of CTA 2010 (see section 153(2) of that Act)[^c-318ab4d699c525b441783e553a804aa8],
- “**member company**” has the same meaning as in section 364 (see section 364(2)),
- “**net consortium debit**” is to be read in accordance with section 365(2) and (3),
- “**relevant consortium creditor relationship**” is to be read in accordance with section 364(2), and
- “**subsidiary**”, in relation to a company which is a holding company, means a trading company (as defined by section 185(1) of CTA 2010) that, by reference to that holding company, is owned by a consortium by virtue of section 153(3) of that Act[^c-e97fea9f89b35a5e807c1cabb24c0581].

(2) Any reference in this Chapter to a company being owned by a consortium is to be read in accordance with section 153 of CTA 2010[^c-74643a853244067481bdccb423a49b35].

(3) Any reference in this Chapter to two companies being members of the same group of companies is a reference to those companies being members of the same group of companies for the purposes of Part 5 of CTA 2010 (group relief) (see section 152 of that Act)[^c-7fce4b2fa2ac30f6cc3c33d481ef15cc].
