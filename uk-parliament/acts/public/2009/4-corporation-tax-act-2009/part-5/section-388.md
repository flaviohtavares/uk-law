# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 388

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 388. Basic rule: deficit set off against income and gains of deficit period[^c-4e5eac5855cfaed5973f003d77485d79]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The basic rule is that the deficit must be set off against any income and gains of the deficit period which are referable to BLAGAB.

(2) The income and gains are reduced accordingly.

(3) Any such reduction is made in accordance with step 4 in section 73 of FA 2012 (that is to say, before any deduction for the adjusted BLAGAB management expenses of the company for the deficit period)[^c-aa2daa257f02c6fb7b2b8c9ab632c1ae].
