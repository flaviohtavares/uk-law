# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 418A

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 418A. Cases involving host contract (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-10bb57fe9b78acf480b0e046593e5ef0][^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b][^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where the debtor or the creditor, in accordance with generally accepted accounting practice, treats the rights and liabilities under the loan relationship as divided between— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

- (a) rights and liabilities under a loan relationship (“the host contract”), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]
- (b) rights and liabilities under one or more derivative financial instruments or equity instruments. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

(2) Where the debtor, in accordance with generally accepted accounting practice, treats the rights and liabilities under the loan relationship as so divided, section 418 has effect as if the reference to the loan relationship in subsection (3)(a) were to the host contract. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

(3) Where the creditor, in accordance with generally accepted accounting practice, treats the rights and liabilities under the loan relationship as so divided, section 418 has effect as if the reference to the loan relationship in subsection (3)(b) were to the host contract. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

(4) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b] **the debtor *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]** ” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b] **the creditor *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]** ” have the same meaning as in section 418. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]
