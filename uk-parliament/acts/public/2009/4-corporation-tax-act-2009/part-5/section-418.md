## 418. Loan relationships (affected by repeal, see note) involving connected debtor and creditor where debits exceed credits (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b][^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b][^c-342749c589f8a02602b6bff69aa34093][^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

- (a) two connected companies are party to a loan relationship, one (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]**the debtor *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]**”) as debtor and the other (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]**the creditor *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]**”) as creditor, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]
- (b) conditions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b] A and B[^c-311b8dbf3d6632e4566d9506dfaba34c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b] are met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

(2)[^c-a08b7e118cd0bb0037bbbb4a3478128c] Condition A is that the rights under the loan relationship include provision by virtue of which the creditor company *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b] or any company connected with it[^M_F_c8468f93-a744-489e-aa27-5c899c7fb2b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]—

- (a) is or may become entitled, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]
- (b) is or may be required, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

to acquire (whether by conversion or exchange or otherwise) any shares in any company. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

(3) Condition B is that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

- (a) the debits brought into account by the debtor under this Part in respect of the loan relationship for any accounting period, exceed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]
- (b) the credits brought into account (otherwise than as a result of this section) by the creditor in respect of the loan relationship for the corresponding accounting period or periods of the creditor. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

(5) The creditor is treated for the purposes of this Part as bringing into account for the corresponding accounting period or periods additional credits in respect of the loan relationship of an amount equal to the excess. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

(6) But if the creditor is a party to the loan relationship as creditor during only part of the corresponding accounting period (or any of the corresponding periods), it is treated for the purposes of this Part as bringing into account for the period only such part of the excess as is just and reasonable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

(6A)[^c-3e3d94c7a318314ba82ccdc194425910] For the purposes of this section the creditor is to be treated as continuing to be a party to the loan relationship even though the creditor has disposed of the creditor's rights under the loan relationship to another person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

- (a) under a repo or stock lending arrangement, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]
- (b) under a transaction which is treated as not involving any disposal as a result of section 26 of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b] TCGA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b] 1992 (mortgages and charges not to be treated as disposals). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

(6B) For the purposes of this section the creditor is to be treated as continuing to be a party to the loan relationship even though the creditor has disposed of the creditor's rights under the loan relationship to another person if the disposal was made with the relevant avoidance intention. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

(6C) The relevant avoidance intention is the intention of eliminating or reducing the credits to be brought into account for the purposes of this Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]

(7) Sections 418A and 419 supplement[^c-247eb27ea991c3ae5b975a58ffa4fb0c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b] this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eab1801d8c7d87ff307fcee7b623e86b]
