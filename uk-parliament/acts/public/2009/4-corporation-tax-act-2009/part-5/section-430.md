# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 430

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 430. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Chapter “**company**” means any entity listed as a company in Part A of Annex I[^c-9760ad828b5836ed126548665f2517e9] to the Mergers Directive.

(2) For the purposes of this Chapter, a company is resident in a relevant state[^c-648e75e4aa1f74fd560ae4525c705cfe] if—

- (a) it is within a charge to tax under the law of the relevant state[^c-3231350768b54f4dfc75dabc3d7bd64d] as being resident for that purpose, and
- (b) it is not regarded, for the purpose of any double taxation relief arrangements to which the relevant state is a party, as resident in a territory not within a relevant state.
