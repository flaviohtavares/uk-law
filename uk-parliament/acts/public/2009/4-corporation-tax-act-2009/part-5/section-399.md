# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 399

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 399. Basic rules[^key-4e5eac5855cfaed5973f003d77485d79][^key-e91c3567c6456d81e3fb59199a2fdf54]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if a loan relationship is represented by an index-linked gilt-edged security.

(2) The amounts to be brought into account for the purposes of this Part are to be determined using fair value accounting.

(3)[^key-b61db0108272212b396e55d828f7c25f] For provision requiring adjustments to be made to amounts determined under subsection (2), see sections 400 to 400C (adjustments for changes in index).

(4)[^key-637797d1b29fca2091f3885666c1cd55] In this section and sections 400 to 400C—

- “**index-linked gilt-edged securities**” means any gilt-edged securities under which the amounts of the payments are determined wholly or partly by reference to an index of prices published by the Statistics Board;
- “**relevant prices index**”, in relation to an index-linked gilt-edged security, means the index of prices by reference to which the amounts of the payments under the security are wholly or partly determined.

(5) For the meaning of “gilt-edged securities”, see section 476(1).

(6)[^key-bde33fc7268a567118ca266c37db83d0] In the case of insurance companies, the application of sections 400 to 400C is subject to section 112 of FA 2012.
