# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 429

## 429. Disapplication of Chapter where transparent entities involved

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter does not apply in relation to the transfer of business if the transferor is a transparent entity.

(2) If any transferee is a transparent entity, sections 424 and 425 (reorganisations involving loan relationships) do not apply.

(3) In this section “**transparent entity**” means a company which is resident in a member State [^key-21e24ec1d6cff5e0b9f257f915c8cb6c]... and does not have an ordinary share capital.

(4) For the meaning of “resident in a relevant state[^key-b0dab1ff194d14cff5ba76046fb3f009]”, see section 430.
