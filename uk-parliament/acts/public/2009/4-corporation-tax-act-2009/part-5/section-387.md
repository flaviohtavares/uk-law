# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 387

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 387. Treatment of deficit on basic life assurance and general annuity business: introduction[^c-4e5eac5855cfaed5973f003d77485d79]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Sections 388 to 391 apply for the purposes of the I - E rules[^c-94ed33e2c466d2a7c59e0f030033f379] instead of Chapters 16 and 16A[^c-17896e24c08e240fc8bd2695a1df9273] (non-trading deficits) if a company has a non-trading deficit from its loan relationships for BLAGAB for any accounting period.

(2) In those sections “the deficit” and “the deficit period” mean that deficit and that period respectively.
