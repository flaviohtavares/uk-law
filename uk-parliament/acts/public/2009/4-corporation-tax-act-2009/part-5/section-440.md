# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 440

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 440. Overview of Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter contains rules connected with tax avoidance.

(2) In particular—

- (a) for rules about unallowable purposes [^c-b5f914222a88bd50ab026c6c95303bbe]..., see sections 441 and 442[^c-8eaf4c53439a45696ebd468bde0a8eb1],
- (b) for rules relating to credits and debits where transactions are not at arm's length (other than credits and debits relating to exchange gains and losses), see sections 444 to 446,
- (c) for rules relating to credits and debits relating to exchange gains and losses where transactions are not at arm's length, see sections 448 and[^c-ce8bfd043ea290ac3215f6a7c89b5905] 452,
- (d) for rules about connected parties deriving benefit from creditor relationships, see section 453,
- (e) for rules dealing with tax advantages from resetting interest rates, see section 454, [^c-3a9943f9ec3e2da571b416a76e23d845]...
- (f)[^c-ae496274f82004c214cfc3212f0bb77e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g)[^c-63eb90fafdbe67c0bbefbe13536cc1b2] for rules about debits arising as a result of the derecognition of creditor relationships, see section 455A, and[^c-13004d85c94eac1653d045b707e42065]
- (h) for rules dealing with tax avoidance arrangements, see sections 455B to 455D.
