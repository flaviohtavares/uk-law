# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1179DR

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1179DR. Expenditure that qualifies for credit[^c-2f0eac37ba158de991604259402344ab]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-e149cb3c3210efbaa7dd86ddf8414a3a] Expenditure incurred by the production company for a film or television programme counts as “**relevant production expenditure**” for the purposes of section 1179CA(2) if—

- (a) it is core expenditure in relation to that film or television programme (see section 1179DS), and
- (b) it is not excluded expenditure (see sections 1179DT and 1179DU).

(2)[^c-ca1e6226b1536f4c40af92bde6225d4c] But for the purposes of step 1 in section 1179CA(1) as it applies in relation to a certified low-budget film (see section 1179DJA(9))—

- (a) no more than £15 million can count towards the total of the production company’s relevant global expenditure, and
- (b) UK expenditure counts towards that total before other expenditure.
