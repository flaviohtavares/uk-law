# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1301A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4

## 1301A. Restriction of deductions for interest[^c-5631fd76feb4e697b297c2070cfcfbe3][^c-9b5532513456c4c663f7db23a17273a9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In calculating a company's income from any source for corporation tax purposes, no deduction is allowed for interest otherwise than under Part 5 (loan relationships).
