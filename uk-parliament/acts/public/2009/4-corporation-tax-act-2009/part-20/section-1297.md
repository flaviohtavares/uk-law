## 1297. Basic life assurance and general annuity business[^c-66b5b5c77425a6316031a92f73f73fef]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if the employer is a company in relation to which the I - E rules apply[^c-dbabc021de11f6ce8260b0b3e11259b9].

(2)[^c-f58b700c4d01a413b2881a122e7d6f22] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-8497d246dda768421881253628966048] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) For the purpose of calculating the adjusted BLAGAB management expenses of the company for the purposes of section 73 of FA 2012[^c-c72b45178e3e00b9328a254f02e1ac69], the employee benefit contributions are treated as expenses debited, in accordance with generally accepted accounting practice, in the accounts drawn up by the company for that period[^c-2f63e42c5277a03a57da2c0ee9a77bd7].

(5) For the purposes of sections 1290 to 1296—

- (a) any reference to a deduction for employee benefit contributions is to be read as a reference to an amount constituting ordinary BLAGAB management expenses of the company for the purposes of section 76 of FA 2012[^c-b71e26b6a43f2f0132f7883affea6725], and
- (b) references to deduction are to be read in that light.
