# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1301B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4

## 1301B. Qualifying charitable donations[^c-85ba9f96dc39d92754c42c6686aace5f][^c-9b5532513456c4c663f7db23a17273a9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In calculating a company's income from any source for corporation tax purposes, no deduction is allowed in respect of qualifying charitable donations.
