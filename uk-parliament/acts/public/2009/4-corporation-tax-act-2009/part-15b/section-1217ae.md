# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1217AE

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1217AE. “European expenditure” etc[^c-430657b7d7e90131044eca9771949694][^c-42660dad717cf3f61f8a3a52df3b1558]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-4d0026f4b8e5ce08f3b6fd72100f132b] In this Part, “**European expenditure**”, in relation to a video game, means expenditure on goods or services that are provided from within the United Kingdom or[^c-0a49eca0763967936cb5fae0edb5d7d1] the European Economic Area.

(2) Any apportionment of expenditure as between European expenditure[^c-5cb7f3508fd613068d9502e67be7066b] and non-European expenditure[^c-e84502641dd51c64586b9fb959488c14] for the purposes of this Part is to be made on a just and reasonable basis.

(3) The Treasury may by regulations amend subsection (1).
