## 1217CE. European expenditure[^key-430657b7d7e90131044eca9771949694][^key-353f2041eae3a91aa871a18b6e9d7276]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) At least 25% of the core expenditure on the video game incurred by the company must be European expenditure[^key-18c0640542b5631330d5c57119871df5].

(2) The Treasury may by regulations amend the percentage specified in subsection (1).
