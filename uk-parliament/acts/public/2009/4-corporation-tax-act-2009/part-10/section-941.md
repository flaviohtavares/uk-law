# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 941

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 941. UK estates

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the case of a UK estate, the charge to tax under section 934 is a charge[^c-92d0412c6ffccdfab795540b6e6be09c] on the amount of estate income treated as arising in the accounting period.

(2) That amount is the basic amount of that income for the accounting period (see subsection (4)), grossed up by reference to the applicable rate [^M_F_7071247d-0295-4e3a-e021-7b423d855476]... (see section 946).

(3) The gross amount is treated as having borne income tax by deduction at that rate.

(4) In this Chapter “**the basic amount**”, in relation to estate income, has the meaning given by—

- (a) section 943 (basic amount of estate income: absolute interests),
- (b) section 944 (basic amount of estate income: limited interests),
- (c) section 945 (basic amount of estate income: discretionary interests), and
- (d) section 958 (basic amount of estate income: successive limited interests).
