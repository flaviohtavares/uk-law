# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 1233

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1233. Excess capital allowances[^c-0b1a0b4ee8713404ae4df700d83f1496][^c-8bb6e9a26bdf2f0de08c86643b90a3af]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if a company with investment business is entitled to allowances by virtue of section 15(1)(g) or 270CA(f)[^c-38c9b01cd391d204963b7419f87e1797] of CAA 2001 (qualifying activities include managing investments).

(2) So far as effect cannot be given to the allowances under section 253(2) or 270HE(2) (as the case may be)[^c-ed2ffef11bbcd70778567e57f6710924] of CAA 2001, the allowances are treated for the purposes of Chapter 2—

- (a) as expenses of management, and
- (b) as referable to the accounting period for which the company is entitled to the allowances.
