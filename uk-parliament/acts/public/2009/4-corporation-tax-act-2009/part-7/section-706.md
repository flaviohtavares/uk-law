# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 706

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 706. “Excluded body”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part “**excluded body**” means—

- an authorised unit trust,
- an investment trust,
- an open-ended investment company, or
- a venture capital trust.
