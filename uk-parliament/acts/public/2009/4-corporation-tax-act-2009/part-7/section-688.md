# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 688

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (22).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/4
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 688. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Chapter—

- “**company**” means any entity listed as a company in Part A of Annex I[^c-49496d9840ea5a8e179ea58774183ebb] to the Mergers Directive, and
- “**co-operative society**” means a society registered under the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014[^c-1f87efeb6a1d700fab05045aa7f587b7] or a similar society governed by the law of a member State [^c-f3bc409fb3f7292091ea3fc6a712a49f]....

(2) For the purposes of this Chapter, a company is resident in a relevant state[^c-ce0e7534d9722385c6dd9e8d3d975974] if—

- (a) it is within a charge to tax under the law of the relevant state[^c-2ebd99f1441460e460781399e6aad4c1] as being resident for that purpose, and
- (b) it is not regarded, for the purpose of any double taxation relief arrangements to which the relevant state is a party, as resident in a territory not within a relevant state.
