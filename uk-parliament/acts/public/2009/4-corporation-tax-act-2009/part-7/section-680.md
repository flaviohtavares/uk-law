## 680. Disapplication of Chapter where transparent entities involved

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter does not apply in relation to the transfer of business if the transferor is a transparent entity.

(2) In this section “**transparent entity**” means a company which is resident in a member State [^key-f77cf3e5b1ecd809fdaf4ad974865726]... and which does not have an ordinary share capital.
