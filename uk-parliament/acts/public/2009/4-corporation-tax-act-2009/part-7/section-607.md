# Corporation Tax Act 2009, section 607

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 607. Pre-contract or abortive expenses

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) a company may enter into a derivative contract or related transaction but has not yet done so,
- (b) it incurs any expenses for purposes connected—
  - (i) with entering into it, or
  - (ii) with giving effect to any obligation which might arise under it, and
- (c) had the company entered into the contract or transaction, the expenses would be expenses within section 594A(1)(b)[^c-56526db53a2eb53efb1e22efc9f884f1].

(2) The expenses are treated as expenses in relation to which debits may be brought into account in accordance with section 595(2)[^c-58c21e607dd3b6747c5f16651c4668c6] to the same extent as if the company had entered into the contract or transaction.
