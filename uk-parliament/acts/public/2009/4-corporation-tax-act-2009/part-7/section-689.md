## 689. Overview of Chapter

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Chapter contains rules connected with tax avoidance.

(2) In particular—

- (a) for rules about unallowable purposes, see sections 690 to 692,
- (b) for rules relating to credits and debits where transactions are not at arm's length, see sections 693 to 695,
- (c) for rules relating to credits and debits in the case of transactions with non-UK residents, see sections 696 and 697, [^key-1f6291474670e30a5c92588e2d44e255]...
- (d)[^key-609f9c6ae4e86635fc6e51e3d686ec8b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^key-040f6422b076b4540152fcbf8e4e304d] for rules about debits arising as a result of the derecognition of derivative contracts, see section 698A, and[^key-b81d0ce1a66a55227ee47756fbab16f7]
- (f) for rules dealing with tax avoidance arrangements, see sections 698B to 698D.
