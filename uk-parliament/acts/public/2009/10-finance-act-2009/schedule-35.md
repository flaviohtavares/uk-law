### Special annual allowance charge (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f4438b640929b644742064e473d0448b]

**1.** (1) A charge to income tax, to be known as the special annual allowance charge, arises where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4438b640929b644742064e473d0448b]

- (a) the total adjusted pension input amount for a tax year in the case of a high-income individual who is a member of one or more registered pension schemes, exceeds *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4438b640929b644742064e473d0448b]
- (b) the amount of the special annual allowance. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4438b640929b644742064e473d0448b]

(2) The individual is a high-income individual if the individual's relevant income for the tax year is £150,000 or more. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4438b640929b644742064e473d0448b]

Paragraph 2 makes provision for calculating the individual's relevant income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4438b640929b644742064e473d0448b]

(3) Paragraphs 3 to 16 explain what is the total adjusted pension input amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4438b640929b644742064e473d0448b]

(4) The special annual allowance is £20,000 (but subject to paragraph 17). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4438b640929b644742064e473d0448b]

(5) But if, in calculating the total adjusted pension input amount of the individual for the tax year, a deduction is made in respect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4438b640929b644742064e473d0448b]

(6) The person liable to the special annual allowance charge is the individual. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f4438b640929b644742064e473d0448b]
