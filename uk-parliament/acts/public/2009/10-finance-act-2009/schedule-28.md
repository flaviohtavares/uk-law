## SCHEDULE 28 — Taxable benefits: cars

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 53

### Introduction

**1.** Chapter 6 of Part 3 of ITEPA 2003 (taxable benefits: cars) is amended as follows.

### Abolition of “price cap”

**2.** (1) Section 121(1) (method of calculating cash equivalent of benefit of car) is amended as follows.

(2) In step 3, insert at the end— “ The resulting amount is the interim sum. ”

(3) Omit step 4 (interim sum to be £80,000 if step 3 amount exceeds £80,000).

**3.** In section 145(5) (modifications of provisions where car temporarily replaced), for “step 4” substitute “ step 3 ”.

**4.** In section 147(1) and (2) (classic cars), for “amount carried forward from” substitute “ interim sum calculated under ”.

**5.** In section 170(1) (Treasury orders increasing various amounts), omit paragraph (a) (amount in step 4 of section 121(1)).
