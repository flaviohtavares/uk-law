# Northern Ireland Act 2009, section 2

> Text as at 2010-04-12.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Judicial appointments and removals[^c2036650]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-70cb3278258fa3647149a541a68954f3] For sections 12 and 12B of the Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978 (c. 23) substitute the sections 12 to 12C set out in Schedule 2.

(2)[^c-70cb3278258fa3647149a541a68954f3] The Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002 (c. 26) is amended as set out in Schedule 3.

(3)[^c-f61114d5891eba0be51198679bb43fcf] Schedule 4 (which transfers appointment and other related functions from the Lord Chancellor to the Northern Ireland Judicial Appointments Commission etc) has effect.

(4)[^c-70cb3278258fa3647149a541a68954f3] Schedule 5 (which contains consequential amendments and transitional provision) has effect.

(5)[^c-70cb3278258fa3647149a541a68954f3] Schedule 6 (which makes provision for reviewing arrangements for judicial appointments and removals etc) has effect.
