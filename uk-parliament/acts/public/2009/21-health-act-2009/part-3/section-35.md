## 35. Investigation of complaints about privately arranged or funded adult social care[^c-bac549c894bcdabca9d84ac241f3559d]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 5 (which inserts a new Part 3A into the Local Government Act 1974 (c. 7) to give the Commission for Local Administration in England powers to investigate complaints about privately arranged or funded adult social care and which makes consequential amendments) has effect.
