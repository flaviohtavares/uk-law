## 27. Pharmaceutical lists: minor amendment[^key-1b29cb756b4a59c727318babde94204d]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 129(6) of the National Health Service Act 2006 (c. 41) (regulations as to pharmaceutical services), in paragraph (d), for “such an application” substitute “ an application to a Primary Care Trust ”.
