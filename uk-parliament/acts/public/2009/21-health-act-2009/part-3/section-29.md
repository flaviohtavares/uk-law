## 29. LPS schemes: powers of Primary Care Trusts and Strategic Health Authorities[^key-4ec81115a5bd219018f9c388993b736c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The National Health Service Act 2006 is amended as follows.

(2) In section 15 (Strategic Health Authorities' directions), in subsection (2), after “section 107 arrangements” insert “ or LPS schemes ”.

(3) In section 16 (section 92 arrangements and section 107 arrangements)—

- (a) in subsection (1), after “section 107 arrangements” insert “ and LPS schemes ”;
- (b) in the heading, after “section 107 arrangements” insert “ and LPS schemes ”.

(4)[^key-177ab38073924a8dcc2d254f4d59ca5b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) Schedule 12 (LPS schemes) is amended as follows.

(6) Paragraph 1 is amended as follows.

(7)[^key-e74e7a0f3d4bd62bd540f9279208675f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8) In sub-paragraph (2)—

- (a)[^key-32cbc497e3b870f6741fd5766b04a318] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) in paragraph (b), for “Primary Care Trust),” substitute “ commissioning body). ”;
- (c)[^key-221da6000169a067ea487fe3731c2f07] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9) After sub-paragraph (2) insert—

> (2A) A Strategic Health Authority may establish an LPS scheme only where the only other parties are Primary Care Trusts.
>
> (2B) A Primary Care Trust may provide local pharmaceutical services under an LPS scheme (where it is not the commissioning body), but only in prescribed circumstances.

(13) Paragraph 3 is amended as follows.

(14) In sub-paragraph (2), for “Primary Care Trusts” substitute “ the commissioning body ”.
