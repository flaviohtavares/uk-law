# Political Parties and Elections Act 2009, section 12

> Text as at 2022-04-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Defence to charge of failing to return donation from impermissible donor[^key-49fc3ac509bd1e0bebd33382c4e9339e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 56 of the 2000 Act (acceptance or return of donations: general), after subsection (3) there is inserted—

> (3A) Where a party or its treasurer is charged with an offence under subsection (3), it shall be a defence to prove that—
>
> - (a) all reasonable steps were taken by or on behalf of the party to verify (or ascertain) whether the donor was a permissible donor, and
> - (b) as a result, the treasurer believed the donor to be a permissible donor.
