Political Parties and Elections Act 2009

2009 c. 12

An Act to make provision in connection with the Electoral Commission; to make provision about political donations, loans and related transactions and about political expenditure; and to make provision about elections and electoral registration.

\[21st July 2009\]

Be it enacted by the Queen's most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—[^c-c605663f4318d18e91197ae43158ebbe][^c-bb582274ccb3e3e696ecaaacae22abc7][^c-74fbedb05bba6f97f08b9ac4937b8bff][^c-c6274913fb1a78128c4b0a875c4ef901][^c-b6e85c79253f64d1323293fcc6aacb3b]
