## 18. Maximum duration of agreement[^c-1dfbcaebc0fcbb115a9a3f10a0b36ae3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The maximum period for which an agreement may authorise the MMO or an eligible body to perform a function is 20 years.
