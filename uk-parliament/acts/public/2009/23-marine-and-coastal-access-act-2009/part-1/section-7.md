# Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Regulations supplementary to sections 4 and 4A[^c-f6e159f11c2ad3e74bf9568b28d935b2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In any regulations made under section 4B of the Sea Fish (Conservation) Act 1967 (c. 84) any reference to the Secretary of State, or which includes a reference to the Secretary of State, is to be read, in relation to the exercise by the MMO of functions under or by virtue of section [^c-4e044fdf043be0cdc980479c17e76dad]... 4A of that Act (licensing of fishing boats and trans-shipment licences for vessels), as a reference to the MMO or, as the case may be, as including instead a reference to the MMO.
