# Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Grants[^key-0232461025de7d4380e351592258dd08]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may make payments by way of grant to the MMO.

(2) Any payments under subsection (1) are to be—

- (a) of such amounts,
- (b) at such times, and
- (c) subject to such conditions (if any),

as the Secretary of State may determine.
