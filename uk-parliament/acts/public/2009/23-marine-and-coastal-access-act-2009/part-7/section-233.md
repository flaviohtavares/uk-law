# Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009, section 233

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 233. Consequential and supplementary amendments

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-3de9300ae6bd793a296506790349b2cb][^c-7865d4467a2266d985450fea91a96ee5] Schedule 16 (which contains consequential and supplementary amendments relating to this Chapter) has effect.

(2)[^c-d39bbe89b201858f086a0f0c4963053e] The following provisions of the Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries Act 1975 (c. 51) (which are obsolete or no longer of practical utility) are omitted—

- (a) in section 4 (poisonous matter etc), subsection (2);
- (b) section 23 (export of salmon and trout);
- (c) section 24 (consignment of salmon and trout).
