# Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009, section 65

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Requirement for licence[^c-b8293a172ad128e62a93ac04b55b6ff0][^c-f06a7a336496a9933b358b2475d64d0a][^c-e241fbef02a7a474fe663f951bb6dc5f][^c-b8edb0507b833f17adc39da03d3f6379]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No person may—

- (a) carry on a licensable marine activity, or
- (b) cause or permit any other person to carry on such an activity,

except in accordance with a marine licence granted by the appropriate licensing authority.

(2) Subsection (1) is subject to any provision made by or under sections 74 to 77 (exemptions).
