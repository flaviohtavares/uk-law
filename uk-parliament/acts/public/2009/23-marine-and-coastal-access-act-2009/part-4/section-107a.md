# Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009, section 107A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 107A (Extent: E+W). Deposits on account of fees payable to the (Extent: E+W) Secretary of State, the Scottish Ministers or the (Extent: E+W) Welsh Ministers (Extent: E+W)[^c-7a81b25675feb0a191c0d0a5a3144a5c][^c-2b118d90169706959a93b15bf515e707]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W)) This section applies where the *(Extent: E+W)* appropriate licensing authority is the Secretary of State, the Scottish Ministers or the Welsh Ministers.[^c-053dbd00cafb036678020ffb2f8bafc8] *(Extent: E+W)*

(2 (Extent: E+W)) Where a person is required to pay a fee to the licensing authority under this Part, the licensing authority may require the person to pay a deposit on account of the fee. *(Extent: E+W)*

(3 (Extent: E+W)) The amount that a person may be required to pay under subsection (2) is to be determined by or in accordance with regulations made by the licensing authority. *(Extent: E+W)*

(4 (Extent: E+W)) If a licensee fails to comply with a requirement to pay a deposit charged under subsection (2) on account of a fee charged under section 72A(2)(a) or (b), the licensing authority may by notice vary, suspend or revoke the licence. *(Extent: E+W)*

(5 (Extent: E+W)) The suspension of a licence under subsection (4) continues in effect until the deposit is paid (but this is subject to any provision made under section 108(3)(b) in relation to notices under that subsection). *(Extent: E+W)*

(6 (Extent: E+W)) If a person who has applied for a licence under section 67 or for a variation, suspension, revocation or transfer of a licence under section 72 fails to comply with a requirement to pay a deposit charged under subsection (2) in connection with that application, the licensing authority may— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) refuse to proceed with the application, or *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) refuse to proceed with it until the failure is remedied. *(Extent: E+W)*
