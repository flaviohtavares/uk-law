# Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009, section 72A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72A (Extent: E+W). Further fees chargeable where the (Extent: E+W) appropriate licensing authority is the Secretary of State, the Scottish Ministers or the Welsh Ministers (Extent: E+W)[^c-f6d266c6d49dab202ca839d237b82620][^c-e241fbef02a7a474fe663f951bb6dc5f][^c-b8edb0507b833f17adc39da03d3f6379][^c-e2ba5e108e7f114fb8feb49a9f9c09b4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W)) This section applies where the *(Extent: E+W)* appropriate licensing authority in relation to a marine licence granted under this Part is the Secretary of State, the Scottish Ministers or the Welsh Ministers.[^c-f200079bcc7017f9bc347722bdf580b8] *(Extent: E+W)*

(2 (Extent: E+W)) The licensing authority may require the licensee to pay a fee for— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) monitoring an activity authorised by the licence, *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) assessing and interpreting the results of any monitoring of an activity authorised by the licence, or *(Extent: E+W)*
- (c (Extent: E+W)) where the Welsh Ministers are the licensing authority,[^c-fa7f7691778188fa6fd558b9683915b1] *(Extent: E+W)* dealing with an application by the licensee for a variation, suspension, revocation or transfer of the licence under section 72. *(Extent: E+W)*

(2A (Extent: E+W))[^c-4b45da79755bf6062bd116d9e492b5ab] Where the licensing authority is the Secretary of State or the Scottish Ministers, the authority may charge a fee for dealing with— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) a variation of the licence under section 72(3) (whether or not on an application), or *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) a transfer and variation of the licence under section 72(7). *(Extent: E+W)*

(3 (Extent: E+W)) In subsection (2) “ *(Extent: E+W)***monitoring *(Extent: E+W)***”, in relation to a licence, means monitoring carried out for the purposes of enabling the licensing authority to determine— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) the environmental, economic or social consequences of any activity authorised by the licence, or *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) whether the licensee is complying with any conditions attached to that licence. *(Extent: E+W)*

(4 (Extent: E+W)) The fees that may be charged under *(Extent: E+W)* subsections (2) and (2A)[^c-cdce835894f793a286ee9377ed3e9525] *(Extent: E+W)* are to be determined by or in accordance with regulations made by the licensing authority. *(Extent: E+W)*

(5 (Extent: E+W)) Regulations under subsection (4) may provide for different fees for different cases. *(Extent: E+W)*

(6 (Extent: E+W)) If the licensing authority carries out any investigation, examination or test which in its opinion is necessary or expedient to enable it to determine— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W))[^c-da98d8dc5f115d482dd7a515d1a828d4] where the Welsh Ministers are the licensing authority,[^c-82a84abb6e498028eab9621b43b6e919] *(Extent: E+W)* an application by a licensee for a variation, suspension, revocation or transfer of a licence under section 72, *(Extent: E+W)* or[^c-4b3e794c92faa3b091a74e7feab7074f] *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) where the licensing authority is the Secretary of State or the Scottish Ministers, an application for a variation of a licence under section 72(3) or a transfer and variation of a licence under section 72(7), *(Extent: E+W)*

the authority may require the licensee *(Extent: E+W)* or (as the case may be) other applicant[^c-e29995de4a60b9ce661612aa8717a2ee] *(Extent: E+W)* to pay a fee towards the reasonable expenses of that investigation, examination or test. *(Extent: E+W)*

(7 (Extent: E+W)) If a licensee fails to comply with a requirement to pay a fee charged under subsection (2)(a) or (b), the licensing authority may by notice vary, suspend or revoke the licence. *(Extent: E+W)*

(8 (Extent: E+W)) The suspension of a licence under subsection (7) continues in effect until the fee is paid (but this is subject to any provision made under section 108(3)(b) in relation to notices under that subsection). *(Extent: E+W)*

(9 (Extent: E+W)) If a licensee *(Extent: E+W)* or other applicant[^c-ce21a6777d8538760d317122f12a9969] *(Extent: E+W)* who has applied for a variation, suspension, revocation or transfer of a licence under section 72 fails to comply with a requirement to pay a fee charged under this section in connection with that application, the licensing authority may— *(Extent: E+W)*

- (a (Extent: E+W)) refuse to proceed with the application, or *(Extent: E+W)*
- (b (Extent: E+W)) refuse to proceed with it until the failure is remedied. *(Extent: E+W)*
