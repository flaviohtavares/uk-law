# Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-20. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/23

## 76. Dredging in the Scottish zone[^key-75d631405400211c44d6b01a7a8f6aef]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Nothing in this Part applies to anything done, in the exercise of a function falling within subsection (2), in relation to the extraction of minerals by dredging in the Scottish zone.

(2) The functions are—

- (a) any function exercisable under[^key-3e720b43d2605b0a879eaf9106da5918] assimilated[^key-3302a2be25c4a04fcc1c41a680a9c458] law;
- (b) any of Her Majesty's prerogative and other executive functions which is exercisable on behalf of Her Majesty by the Scottish Ministers.
