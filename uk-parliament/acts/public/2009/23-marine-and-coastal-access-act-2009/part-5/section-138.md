# Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009, section 138

## 138. Hearings ...[^c2007909][^key-9780d2aa71278aff1f48e3bf6d269c45][^key-abb0f2511ea32c491cef7fe18a3d9d06][^key-abb0f2511ea32c491cef7fe18a3d9d06][^key-978de3330abdf62d8b6ef9aeaa01e0d4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where the Secretary of State has the function of—

- (a) deciding (under section 130(8)) whether to confirm a byelaw made under this Chapter[^key-7d62f4d2f9dbe554e0f4ea6d0c0f78fa];
- (b) deciding (under section 131(5)) whether to revoke an emergency byelaw;
- (c) deciding (under section 132(8)) whether to revoke an interim byelaw.

(2) This section also applies where the Welsh Ministers have the function of—

- (a) deciding whether to make an order under section 134 , 134A or 134B[^key-af4945ebad762a287111142deeb5370f];
- (b) deciding whether to make an interim order under section 136(1) or (1A)[^key-7626c345a61dd27ef65884f086412e5f].

(2A)[^key-27fd6650b037248b1ed495d7c1d089f4] This section also applies where the Scottish Ministers have the function of—

- (a) deciding whether to make an order under section 137A;
- (b) deciding whether to make an order under section 137C.

(2B) This section also applies where the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs in Northern Ireland has the function of —

- (a) deciding whether to make an order under section 137E;
- (b) deciding whether to make an order under section 137G.

(3) The Secretary of State or (as the case may be) the Welsh Ministers , the Scottish Ministers or the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs in Northern Ireland[^key-7896db07173c0d4de67501571b6bbeb3] may, before making that decision, give to any person the opportunity of—

- (a) appearing before and being heard by a person appointed for that purpose;
- (b) providing written representations to such a person.

(4) The Secretary of State or (as the case may be) the Welsh Ministers , the Scottish Ministers or the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs in Northern Ireland[^key-1f9a762c72ab035f309b183114d50908] may make regulations providing for the procedure to be followed (including decisions as to costs) at hearings held under subsection (3).

(5) A person appointed under subsection (3) must make a report to the Secretary of State or (as the case may be) the Welsh Ministers , the Scottish Ministers or the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs in Northern Ireland[^key-5306a023576c064217a4dd07536dc5f9] of any oral or written representations made under that subsection.
