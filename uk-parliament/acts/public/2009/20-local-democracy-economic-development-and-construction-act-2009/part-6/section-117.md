# Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009, section 117

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (44).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/20

## 117. Orders and regulations[^key-e6c9c88e3d58b4c7ddb5fc126a9b878c][^key-cbbc43322c7fe7fe0e74333a6d377b29]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Orders and regulations[^key-e4c79db908c4e42b2f0b0746c081ef43] under this Part must be made by statutory instrument.

*Extent: E+W*

(1A)[^key-03f322d03a7ba5c78edb671d0cbf6cc2] An order or regulations[^key-2f1e1948416d0e36673e27467003280a] under this Part may make different provision for different authorities or descriptions of authority or otherwise for different purposes.

(2)[^key-ac618a206dbe5ecfe6f7a8d71de08ab2] Subject to subsections (2A) and (3), an order under this Part[^key-39109670884444bf8c7c2a93d3e6f16b] may not be made unless a draft of the statutory instrument containing the order (whether alone or with other provisions) has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

(2A) Subsection (2) does not apply to—[^key-e266cf73974004004e687f4e31d25ff7]

- (za)[^key-e14d4d9cc7974045245efc2011e9c6b4] an order under section 107FA,
- (a) an order under section 113C(1) that is made only for the purpose mentioned in section 113C(5)(b),
- (b) an order under section 113C(2) that is made only for that purpose or for imposing conditions on the doing of things for a commercial purpose, [^key-7a8c921793f7cac98c713f113ff7e789]...
- (ba)[^key-28243667714603e1c7a651d77e79f978] an order under section 113E,
- (c) an order under section 116 that amends or revokes provision contained in an instrument subject to annulment by resolution of either House of Parliament , or[^key-6c46618614ba0432a9040c6a27f2efea]
- (d) an order under Schedule 5BA.

(3) A statutory instrument that—

- (a) contains an order or regulations[^key-4dbf9641861e3eab184882066244c806] under this Part, and
- (b) is not subject to any requirement that a draft of the instrument be laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament,

is subject to annulment by resolution of either House of Parliament.

(3A)[^key-17f143105cd091e8b6e73f0dcd872008] A statutory instrument that contains (whether alone or with any other provisions) regulations under section 104C(1), 104C(4), or 107K(1) or Schedule 5AA[^key-ce784c7995dd5837c39b55eabf177e8c] may not be made unless a draft of the instrument containing the regulations has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

(3B) A statutory instrument that—

- (a) contains regulations under section 109A(8) or 112A(10), and
- (b) is not by virtue of subsection (3A) subject to a requirement that a draft of the instrument be laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament,

is subject to annulment by resolution of either House of Parliament.

(4) If a draft of an order under this Part or of regulations under section 104C(1) or (4)[^key-c83bec20c3d23e205a25f3db0908cbd9] would, apart from this subsection, be treated for the purposes of the standing orders of either House of Parliament as a hybrid instrument, it is to proceed in that House as if it were not a hybrid instrument.

*Extent: E+W*

(5)[^key-03fc85d490e4d4d143c1065a020e294a] An order under any provision of this Part, other than an order under section 116 or an order mentioned in subsection (2A)(za) to (ba) or (2A)(d)[^key-6658a53dc8a9b9139fd2bc2fa00eeed9], may include provision amending, applying (with or without modifications), disapplying, repealing or revoking any enactment whenever passed or made.
