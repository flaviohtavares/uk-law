## 113ZC. Relevant consenting authorities in relation to orders under section 107[^key-6e2e4fc03a8fc2d5fc278360fdfff694]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The “**relevant consenting authorities**” in relation to an order under section 107 to dissolve or abolish a combined authority means—

- (a) a majority of the relevant councils;
- (b) if the combined authority that is to be abolished is a mayoral combined authority, the mayor of that authority.

(2) The “**relevant councils**” for the purposes of this section are—

- (a) a county council whose area, or part of whose area, is within the area of the combined authority;
- (b) a unitary district council whose area is within the area of the combined authority.
