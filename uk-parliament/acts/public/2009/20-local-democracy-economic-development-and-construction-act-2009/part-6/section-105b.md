# Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009, section 105B

## 105B. Section 105A orders: procedure[^key-6b225919cea879081e8943a336250541]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may make an order under section 105A only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-018ce81c17a05f7d683d7aecec5c8284]

- (a) a proposal for the making of the order in relation to the combined authority has been made to the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-018ce81c17a05f7d683d7aecec5c8284] —[^M_F_d6e1c228-f3fd-4a2c-c70b-d3b436c019b9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-018ce81c17a05f7d683d7aecec5c8284]
  - (i) as part of a proposal under section 109A, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-018ce81c17a05f7d683d7aecec5c8284]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-018ce81c17a05f7d683d7aecec5c8284] in accordance with section 112A, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-018ce81c17a05f7d683d7aecec5c8284] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-018ce81c17a05f7d683d7aecec5c8284]
- (b) the appropriate consent is given and the Secretary of State considers that the making of the order is likely to improve *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-018ce81c17a05f7d683d7aecec5c8284] the economic, social and environmental well-being of some or all of the people who live or work in the area or areas to which the order relates[^M_F_f0b4d2bf-71e5-4869-938e-d58e10e603b0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-018ce81c17a05f7d683d7aecec5c8284].

The Secretary of State may make an order under section 105A in relation to an existing combined authority’s area only if—

- (a) a proposal for the making of the order in relation to the combined authority has been submitted to the Secretary of State in accordance with section 112A, or
- (b) the appropriate consent is given.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1)(b), the appropriate consent is given to the making of an order under section 105A only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eb65d9571a4a0d4d7898a2cec4091d07]

- (a) in the case of an order in relation to an existing combined authority, each appropriate authority consents; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eb65d9571a4a0d4d7898a2cec4091d07]
- (b) in any other case, each constituent council consents. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eb65d9571a4a0d4d7898a2cec4091d07]

Paragraph (a) is subject to subsections (3) and (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-eb65d9571a4a0d4d7898a2cec4091d07]

For the purposes of subsection (1)(b), the appropriate consent is given to the making of an order under section 105A only if—

- (a) each constituent council consents, and
- (b) the combined authority consents.

(3) Subsection (4) applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3319be71546b397c86fd0cd8dfb42a60]

- (a) an order under section 105A in relation to an existing combined authority is the first such order to be made in relation to that authority, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3319be71546b397c86fd0cd8dfb42a60]
- (b) the authority is not a mayoral combined authority, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3319be71546b397c86fd0cd8dfb42a60]
- (c) there are one or more constituent councils who do not consent to the making of the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3319be71546b397c86fd0cd8dfb42a60]

(4) For the purposes of subsection (1)(b), the appropriate consent is given to the making of the order if the combined authority and at least two constituent councils consent to the making of the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3319be71546b397c86fd0cd8dfb42a60]

(5) Where an order under section 105A is made by virtue of subsection (4) of this section, the Secretary of State must make an order under section 106 to remove the area of each non-consenting constituent council from the existing area of the combined authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3319be71546b397c86fd0cd8dfb42a60]

(5A)[^key-9b31db7f6751b2c5f707af3b9cd1b20d] The requirements in subsection (1) do not apply where the order is made under sections 105A and 107D in relation to an existing mayoral combined authority and provides for a function—

- (a) to be a function of the combined authority, and
- (b) to be a function exercisable only by the mayor.

See section 107DA in relation to an order of this kind.

(6) The requirement in subsection (1)(b) for the appropriate consent to be given to the making of an order under section 105A does not apply where—

- (a) the order revokes (in whole or in part), or otherwise amends, a previous order under that section, and
- (b) the only purpose of the order is to provide for a health service function of a combined authority to cease to be exercisable by the authority.

(7) In subsection (6)(b), “**health service function of a combined authority**” means a function which—

- (a) relates to the health service, as defined by section 275(1) of the National Health Service Act 2006, and
- (b) is exercisable by the combined authority by virtue of an order under section 105A.

(8) The requirement in subsection (1)(b) for the appropriate consent to be given is subject to section 106A.

(9) At the same time as laying a draft of a statutory instrument containing an order under this section before Parliament, the Secretary of State must lay before Parliament a report explaining the effect of the order and why the Secretary of State considers it appropriate to make the order.

(10) The report must include—

- (a) a description of any consultation taken into account by the Secretary of State,
- (b) information about any representations considered by the Secretary of State in connection with the order, and
- (c) any other evidence or contextual information that the Secretary of State considers it appropriate to include.

(11)[^key-3663eca4e5e60c22f57d20c91e0e0d52] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(12) For the purposes of this section “the appropriate authorities” are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-321d51aea64430357a49ceb3b4efdf7b]

- (a) each county council the whole or any part of whose area is within the area for which the combined authority is, or is to be, established, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-321d51aea64430357a49ceb3b4efdf7b]
- (b) each district council whose area is within the area for which the combined authority is, or is to be, established, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-321d51aea64430357a49ceb3b4efdf7b]
- (c) in the case of an order in relation to an existing combined authority, the combined authority, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-321d51aea64430357a49ceb3b4efdf7b]

  and a “constituent council” is a council within paragraph (a) or (b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-321d51aea64430357a49ceb3b4efdf7b]
