# Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009, section 120

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (44).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 120. Interpretation[^key-d8ea5cf9b5a0d3b3cbbca7e8876b2793]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “[^key-7f677014ffa6f9d7173efef0b38a1ed2]**associate member**” has the meaning given by section 104B(1);
- “**combined authority**” means an authority established under section 103(1);
- “[^key-dd08c7352e005add5d848038b016d9d3]**combined county authority**” means a combined county authority established under section 9(1) of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023;
- “[^key-d2989aa5c21bfcf8c1d8d33ed020f5e6]**deputy mayor**” has the meaning given by section 107C(7);
- “[^key-6d851e09448f4a42e0b5a4311fab7967]**established mayoral strategic authority**” means a mayoral combined authority designated by the Secretary of State under section 106B;
- “**general functions**” has the meaning given by section 107D(2);
- “**EPB**” has the meaning given by section 88(1);
- “[^key-b0ba7c0087759880a980ca2d90ce1a06]**fire and rescue authority**” means a fire and rescue authority under the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004;
- “**ITA**” has the meaning given by section 104(1);
- “**local government area**” has the meaning given by section 88(7);
- “[^key-13bd3790f22c588fd5fa2451949ecf05]**mayor**”, in relation to the area of a combined authority, means the mayor for the area of the authority by virtue of an order under section 107A(1);
- “**mayoral combined authority**” has the meaning given by section 107A(8);
- “**nominating body**” means a body designated under section 104A(1);
- “**non-constituent member**” has the meaning given by section 104A(3);
- “[^key-eb6ac7d75130bf3fc1c3569c23ab9413]**non-mayoral combined authority**” means a combined authority that is not a mayoral combined authority;
- “**PCC functions**” has the meaning given by section 107F(3);
- “**unitary district council**” has the meaning given by section 90(4).
