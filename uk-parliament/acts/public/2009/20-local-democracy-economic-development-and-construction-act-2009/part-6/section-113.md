# Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009, section 113

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (44).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/20

## 113. Requirements in connection with changes to existing combined arrangements[^key-5ab30c4afc9ecf5049a577e412b3659d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-f65ea297c4237303f13ee19b324eccac][^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914] The Secretary of State may make an order under section 104, 105, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914] 105A, 106, 107, 107A, 107D or 107F[^key-faed78a1f02a83743290b36bcc044c4c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914] in relation to an existing combined authority only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]

- (a)[^key-c1bdfae1db6765b706bdb97b0197d887] the Secretary of State considers that to do so is likely to improve the economic, social and environmental well-being of some or all of the people who live or work in the area, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914] the Secretary of State considers that to do so is appropriate having regard to the need— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]
  - (i) to secure effective and convenient local government, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914] to reflect the identities and interests of local communities, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]
- (ab (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914] where a proposal for the making of the order has been submitted under section 112A, the Secretary of State considers that making the order will achieve the purposes specified under subsection (9) of that section, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]
- (b) any consultation required by subsection (2) has been carried out. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]

(1A)[^key-49c28d41bd34098a2b528261d7b9f32c] If a proposal for the making of the order has been submitted under section 112A, the Secretary of State must have regard to the proposal in making the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]

(2) The Secretary of State must carry out a public consultation unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]

- (a)[^key-1203c74e908980533642caff17f128aa] a proposal has been prepared under section 112A, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]
- (b) a public consultation has been carried out in connection with the proposal and the Secretary of State has been provided with a summary of the consultation responses, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]
- (c) the Secretary of State considers that no further consultation is necessary. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]

(2A)[^key-54490ba1cbbede1b8e66dc061f91183f] Subsection (2B) applies where the Secretary of State is considering whether to make an order under section 106 and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]

- (a) part of the area to be created is separated from the rest of it by one or more local government areas that are not within the area, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]
- (b) a local government area that is not within the area to be created is surrounded by local government areas that are within the area. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]

(2B) In deciding whether to make the order under section 106, the Secretary of State must have regard to the likely effect of the change to the combined authority's area on the exercise of functions equivalent to those of the combined authority's functions in each local government area that is next to any part of the area to be created by the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]

(3)[^key-c6dcbe9ed00dddfddc5b4ffe9c008974] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-d175f2b65f82c976472019e7c969f0c4] This section does not apply to an order under section 106(1)(b) that is made as a result of the duty in section 105B(5) or 107B(4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2dc916d1522daf5ea0c39fe60227e914]

(1) The Secretary of State may make an order under any of the relevant sections in relation to an existing combined authority only if—

- (a) the Secretary of State considers that the statutory test is met, and
- (b) any consultation required by subsection (3) or (4) has been carried out.

(2) The “relevant sections” are—

- (a) section 104 (constitution and functions: transport);
- (b) section 105 (constitution and functions: local authority functions generally);
- (c) section 105A (other public authority functions);
- (d) section 107A (power to provide for election of mayor);
- (e) section 107D (functions of mayors: general).

(3) If the order implements a proposal submitted to the Secretary of State in accordance with section 112A, the Secretary of State must consult such persons (if any) as the Secretary of State considers it necessary to consult about the proposal.

(4) In any other case, the Secretary of State must consult the relevant consultees.

(5) Subsection (4) does not apply if the order is made in accordance with the requirements under section 107B(3).

(6) The “**relevant consultees**” for the purposes of subsection (4)—

- (a) in relation to a mayoral combined authority means—
  - (i) the constituent councils, and
  - (ii) the mayor;
- (b) in relation a non-mayoral combined authority means—
  - (i) the constituent councils, and
  - (ii) the combined authority.

(7) In this section, “**statutory test**” has the meaning given by section 110(6).
