## 114. Incidental etc provision[^key-7ab4aaf9a7d4a4869e547cea315aefef]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-94749ef75f397ea6a528ef0941269664][^key-9657bd2b80719fb7873e779ba575893b] The Secretary of State may by order make incidental, consequential, transitional or supplementary provision for the purposes of, or in consequence of, an order under this Part or for giving full effect to such an order.

*Extent: E+W*

(1A)[^key-582c3312de67b8704363ee8f5c7f30ee] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-07a81558b9ae887ce51b20fbebc31d89] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The provision that may be included in an order under this section by virtue of section 117(5) *(Extent: E+W)*[^key-6db92ae8611102e4195b385a0e6b0150] includes provision applying, with modifications, or disapplying any enactment amended by Schedule 6.

(4) An order under this section may not include provision amending or disapplying sections 15 to 17 of, and Schedule 1 to, the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 (c. 42) (political balance on local authority committees etc).
