# Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009, section 139

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (44).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/20

## 139. Requirement for construction contracts to be in writing[^key-43690f71ddce1d815e4405520159df90][^key-60719d7e0dd4723324313c6b12ac188d][^key-d794430d79149aa7f1b3d2f3d4bb925c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996, section 107 (provisions applicable only to contracts in writing) is repealed.

(2) In section 108 of that Act (right to refer disputes to adjudication)—

- (a) in subsection (2), after “The contract shall” insert “ include provision in writing so as to ”;
- (b) in subsections (3) and (4), after “provide” insert “ in writing ”.
