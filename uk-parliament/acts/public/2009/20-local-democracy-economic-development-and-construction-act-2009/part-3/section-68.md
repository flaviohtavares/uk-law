## 68. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “**the Electoral Commission's Boundary Committee**” has the meaning given by section 60(4);
- “**electoral arrangements**” has the meaning given in section 56(4) and (6);
- “**principal council**” has the meaning given in section 56(3).
