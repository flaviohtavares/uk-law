# Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009, section 39

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 39. Power to appoint replacement auditor

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Where a person appointed by the Auditor General for Wales[^c-e5bd668c884b65701e88d5fa8f8cf502] under this Chapter in relation to an entity for a financial year dies, is dismissed or is unable or unwilling to act, the Auditor General for Wales[^c-265cecd99ed7894884972bb5ce9535e2] may (subject to this Chapter) appoint a replacement in relation to that entity for that financial year.

(2) Before making an appointment under this section the Auditor General for Wales must consult the entity.

(3) After making an appointment under this section the Auditor General for Wales must notify the local authority with which the entity is connected.
