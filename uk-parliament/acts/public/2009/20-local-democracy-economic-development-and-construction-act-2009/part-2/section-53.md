# Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009, section 53

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Regulations

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) Regulations under section 36(5)(b)[^key-d76d644b14a1fe6353d73fac9fc18dad] may provide for any expression used in formulating a condition specified in the regulations to have the meaning for the time being given by a relevant document identified in the regulations.

(2) In subsection (1), “relevant document”—

- (a) means a document that (at the time the regulations are made) is a document identified for the purposes of section 21(2)(b) of the Local Government Act 2003 (c. 26) by regulations made under that provision, and
- (b) includes a document so identified by virtue of section 21(5) of that Act.

(3) Regulations under this Chapter must be made by statutory instrument.

(4)[^key-28e6b47cbc7a651f3cfa48176fa6c9a1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) A statutory instrument containing regulations under this Chapter [^key-3c2b677539dbc22d6ca01f31b13102e3]... is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of the National Assembly for Wales.
