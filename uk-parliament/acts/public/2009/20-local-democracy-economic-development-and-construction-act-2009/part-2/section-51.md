# Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009, section 51

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Power of Auditor General for Wales to require information[^key-cfe0a7604da5badac16548f57d8213d1]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) At any time after the appointment of a person under this Chapter in relation to an entity, the Auditor General for Wales[^key-efdf27c67aaa099d63cffb66c396306c] may for the purpose specified in subsection (2) require the entity to produce to the Auditor General for Wales[^key-4045e923ee6a6b65fa8d8af6e1d5138f] —

- (a) the accounts audited by the person pursuant to section 43 or under section 44, or
- (b) any other document or information relating to the entity to which the person has or had a right of access under or pursuant to this Chapter.

(2) The purpose referred to in subsection (1) is to enable the Auditor General for Wales[^key-ef88c7e6aec959e519e1b0236d3e0f18] to secure that persons appointed by the Auditor General for Wales[^key-c9fb3e7dc0e1ff555b398e80189b5628] under this Chapter maintain proper standards.
