# Banking Act 2009, section 108

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 108. Removal by court[^c-b9b3a628e64b22bafaacb7d06418e0bf]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A bank liquidator may be removed by order of the court on the application of—

- (a) the liquidation committee,
- (b) the PRA[^c-5aa88bb57f2feec10db3990ab4e2c662], or
- (c) the Bank of England.

(2) Before making an application the PRA[^c-8446e3464a7c20efde0b8ae376bc5a47] must consult [^c-5c54fd3c20c681310f96f4f17cadaf2d]... the FCA[^c-ad16aadd2bdd9abe4512727fbdc27d64].

(3) Before making an application the Bank of England must consult [^c-296130b2c1be6aa32660ff819f304d5e]...the FCA[^c-f09276e83443ac5438fa78f98b86ef9f].

(4) A bank liquidator removed by order has release with effect from a time determined by—

- (a) the Secretary of State, or
- (b) in the case of a bank liquidator in Scotland, the Accountant of Court.
