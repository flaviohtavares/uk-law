# Banking Act 2009, section 110

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Disqualification[^key-114c86ea226fdbda28efaf785a774f4b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a bank liquidator ceases to be qualified to act as an insolvency practitioner in relation to the bank[^key-8aa09a5b2b82a5e3bfd50e47bb84e327], the appointment lapses.

(2) A bank liquidator whose appointment lapses under subsection (1) has release with effect from a time determined by—

- (a) the Secretary of State, or
- (b) in the case of a bank liquidator in Scotland, the Accountant of Court.
