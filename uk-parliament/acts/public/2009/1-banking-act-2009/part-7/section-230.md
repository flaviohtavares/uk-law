# Banking Act 2009, section 230

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1

## 230. “Financial institution”[^M_I_8cee577b-5eca-4045-e3b3-3041aeb10eaa][^key-b4c941cf5755069372aad43b01279595]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Treasury may by order provide that a specified institution, or an institution of a specified class, is or is not to be treated as a financial institution for the purposes of section 228 or 229.

(2) An order—

- (a) shall be made by statutory instrument, and
- (b) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
