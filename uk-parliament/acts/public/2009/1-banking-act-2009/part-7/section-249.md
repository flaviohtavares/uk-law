## 249. Functions[^M_I_846a2ef0-c534-46b7-d38e-242c28a55af4][^key-0c4feca6ae1be5e22f88f7c572a2c091]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A reference in an enactment to functions conferred on the Prudential Regulation Authority or the Financial Conduct Authority[^key-a9cec52874dceecdf2214d949e8f85fc] by or under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (or any part of it) includes a reference to functions conferred on that authority[^key-7cbe5ae2743ce1c7c7611a86de5dc708] by or under this Act.

(2) A reference in an enactment to functions of the Prudential Regulation Authority or the Financial Conduct Authority[^key-48b094e2ab0a7b51ebd4782a79260809] includes a reference to functions conferred by or under this Act (irrespective of whether the enactment was passed or made before or after the commencement of this Act).
