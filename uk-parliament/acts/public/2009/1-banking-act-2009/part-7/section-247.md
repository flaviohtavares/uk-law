## 247. Bank of England Act 1946[^key-5a138ee2ac1598be126175f3da0b6a67]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Act affects the generality of section 4 of the Bank of England Act 1946 (directions and relations with other banks).
