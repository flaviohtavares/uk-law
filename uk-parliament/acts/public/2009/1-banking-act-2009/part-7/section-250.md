# Banking Act 2009, section 250

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 250. Information[^key-27b62b794b31f8e1a7c826fe1a139453]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Prudential Regulation Authority[^key-1dc748d1c2eeb24db3e86dd240b2d7ef] shall collect information that it thinks is or may be relevant to the stability of—

- (a) individual financial institutions, or
- (b) one or more aspects of the financial systems of the United Kingdom.

(2) The Authority may perform its function under subsection (1) by the exercise of the power in section 165 or 165A[^key-ee10a75003ff16679be76864c244450a] of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (power to require information–as qualified, in the case of the section 165 power,[^key-9debc7c4d5e6de33ab62ea5fb1bdb9e3] by section 249 above) or in any other way.
