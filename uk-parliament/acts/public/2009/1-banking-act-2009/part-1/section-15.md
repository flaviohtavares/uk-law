# Banking Act 2009, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Share transfer instrument[^c-d7c7ab827ab358ecfc569f87ef112b81]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A share transfer instrument is an instrument which—

- (a) provides for securities issued by a specified bank to be transferred;
- (b) makes other provision for the purposes of, or in connection with, the transfer of securities issued by a specified bank (whether or not the transfer has been or is to be effected by that instrument, by another share transfer instrument or otherwise).

(1A)[^c-0407988d59e0c34c14e9f13143e738a7] Where the Bank of England requires a recapitalisation payment to be made under section 214E of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 in respect of a specified bank, a share transfer instrument may include provision requiring the specified bank to issue securities.

(2) A share transfer instrument may relate to—

- (a) specified securities, or
- (b) securities of a specified description.
