# Banking Act 2009, section 7A

## 7A. Effect on other group members, financial stability in UK etc[^key-086c34153a07468b639ededac580bfb3][^key-af92c22cccebac0efade25be6695a6c4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the Bank of England is considering the imposition of a requirement under section 3A(2), (4), (4B)(b),[^key-20353519f283844e8fb38737efdaee69] (5) or (6), the Bank must consult the PRA and the FCA, and have regard to the potential impact of the requirement on—

- (a) the institution in question,
- (b) the market for financial services within the United Kingdom[^key-a7326744581f5ce804eac147983f9d4f], and
- (c) the financial stability of the United Kingdom[^key-e0cb732d6b8faeed1c48dd0f173b99a7].

(1A)[^key-8415378b4b7d706125dbe95c6a466820] Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to a requirement under section 3A(4) for a person to maintain (but not issue) a particular kind of bail-in liability.

(2) Where the Bank of England is considering the exercise of a stabilisation power in respect of a bank which is a member of a group, the Bank must have regard to—

- (a) the need to minimise the effect of the exercise of the power on other undertakings in the same group,
- (b) the need to minimise any adverse effects on the financial stability of the United Kingdom[^key-95d61f4eefb20a68f8c0519d2bd6f55d], and
- (c) the potential effect of the exercise of the power on the financial stability of countries other than the United Kingdom[^key-e3cc29dc32b3c9a8fb66aeef84e81d24] (particularly those [^key-b4f7e63e76548a267bb2fd802bbf57a9]... countries in which any member of that group is operating).

(3) In this section “group” has the meaning given by section 474 of the Companies Act 2006.
