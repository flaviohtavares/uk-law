# Banking Act 2009, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. International obligation notice: general[^c-055597f2eb626c67c98be855f48db310]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Bank of England may not exercise the power to make a resolution administrator appointment instrument or a mandatory reduction instrument or[^c-70421460c5d948620f468b731a917709] a stabilisation power in respect of a bank if the Treasury notify the Bank that the exercise would be likely to contravene an international obligation of the United Kingdom.

(2) A notice under subsection (1)—

- (a) must be in writing, and
- (b) may be withdrawn (generally, partially or conditionally).

(3) If the Treasury give a notice under subsection (1) the Bank of England must consider other exercises of the power to make a resolution administrator appointment instrument or a mandatory reduction instrument or[^c-2da82ef2398a8c7f34e82b4fb04df264] the stabilisation powers with a view to—

- (a) pursuing the special resolution objectives, and
- (b) avoiding the objections on which the Treasury's notice was based.

(4) The Treasury may by notice to the Bank of England disapply subsection (3) in respect of a bank; and a notice may be revoked by further notice.

(5)[^c-f739d15af25e668d061a5cf182044600] “Resolution administrator appointment instrument” means an instrument under section 62B(2)(a).
