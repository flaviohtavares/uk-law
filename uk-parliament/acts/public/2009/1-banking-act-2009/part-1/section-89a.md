# Banking Act 2009, section 89A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89A. Application to investment firms[^c-4c03dfce479c88084d2f8797729abbe9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part applies to investment firms as it applies to banks, subject to the modifications in the following Table—[^c-284fca4662d9eae0992babbf7abfeb9e]

| Provision | Modification |
| --- | --- |
| Section 1 | Ignore subsection (2)(b). |
| Section 3[^c-c53065a832899c9fc61d84aa2055a939] | In subsection (1), in the definition of “normal insolvency proceedings” ignore the reference to the bank insolvency procedure. |
| Section 4 | Ignore subsections (2)(b), (6) and (7)(b). |
| Section 5 | Ignore subsection (1)(b). |
| Section 7 | Ignore subsection (7). |
| [^c-7065ef166e1e0d983ccf433b42683e9f]. . . | [^c-7065ef166e1e0d983ccf433b42683e9f]. . . |
| Section 8ZA | In subsection (5), ignore the reference to the bank insolvency procedure. |
| Section 12AA | References to normal insolvency proceedings do not include a reference to the bank insolvency procedure.[^c-6065f0921c2804194513a10002a47742] |
| Section 14 | Ignore subsection (5). |
| Section 60 | In subsection (3)(c), ignore the reference to bank insolvency. |
| Section 60B | In subsection (4), ignore the reference to bank insolvency. |
| Section 63 | In subsection (1A), ignore the reference to bank insolvency. |
| Section 66 | In subsection (1ZA), ignore the reference to bank insolvency. |

(2)[^c-edd679bec93c55467fd43099ed110008] In the case of investment firms which are FCA-regulated investment firms, in subsection (1) the reference to this Part is a reference to this Part as it applies to FCA-regulated banks by virtue of section 83A.

(3) In this section—

- “FCA-regulated bank” has the meaning given by section 83A(2);
- “FCA-regulated investment firm” means an investment firm which does not carry on any activity which is a PRA-regulated activity for the purposes of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.
