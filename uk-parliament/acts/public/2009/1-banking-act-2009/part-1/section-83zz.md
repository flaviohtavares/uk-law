# Banking Act 2009, section 83ZZ

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1

## 83ZZ. Co-operation[^key-078a3a5196c0cbc0c6c8cf9b32d7d4e1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In connection with the exercise of its powers to impose sanctions under section 83ZR, the Bank of England must take such steps as it considers appropriate to co-operate with—

- (a) the FCA , and[^key-7a2ef932b87e940a84df9bc4eaee12fe]
- (b)[^key-8ce23026941b9f719094b8f2574141a7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) any person who exercises functions outside the United Kingdom equivalent to those exercisable by the Bank under this Part or any other enactment which, immediately before IP completion day, gave[^key-b59adc4d975d65ccb2078a9e34900554] effect to the recovery and resolution directive.
