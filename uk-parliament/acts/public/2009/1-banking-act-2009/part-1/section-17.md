# Banking Act 2009, section 17

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Effect[^M_I_0902add3-085c-46a4-cf46-0cf0af67285a][^key-96f0dcdf7dc19cf2e1055574a0604982]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this section “**transfer**” means a transfer provided for by a share transfer instrument or order, by a mandatory reduction instrument[^key-539f94a2e5523759cfd11ca3f6268032] or by a resolution instrument[^key-60e8ba1ca6ad56a7d9245ded83e1e40d].

(2) A transfer takes effect by virtue of the instrument or order (and in accordance with its provisions as to timing or other ancillary matters).

(3) A transfer takes effect despite any restriction arising by virtue of contract or legislation or in any other way.

(4) In subsection (3) “**restriction**” includes—

- (a) any restriction, inability or incapacity affecting what can and cannot be assigned or transferred (whether generally or by a particular person), and
- (b) a requirement for consent (by any name).

(5) A share transfer instrument or order, a mandatory reduction instrument[^key-d449127691edc207e30f3f787ccfe854] or a resolution instrument[^key-7a8831cce2332341ecc376a8e04ebb4f] may provide for a transfer to take effect free from any trust, liability or other encumbrance (and may include provision about their extinguishment).

(6) A share transfer instrument or order, a mandatory reduction instrument[^key-ba1e3fb990e5af96db9e2a14862178b3] or a resolution instrument[^key-49c3be06be3ccf555dd58fdf2e671a54] may extinguish rights to acquire securities falling within Class 1 or 2 in section 14.
