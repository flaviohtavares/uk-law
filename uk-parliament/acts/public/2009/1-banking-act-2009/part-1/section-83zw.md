# Banking Act 2009, section 83ZW

## 83ZW. Appeal[^key-078a3a5196c0cbc0c6c8cf9b32d7d4e1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the Bank of England decides to impose a sanction on a person under section 83ZR, the person may appeal to the Upper Tribunal.

(2)[^key-89e44db0fc5252ae57f34e88c8f51ba8] The Bank of England may not impose a sanction while an appeal under this section could be brought or is pending.
