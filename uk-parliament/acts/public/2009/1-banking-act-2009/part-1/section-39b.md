# Banking Act 2009, section 39B

## 39B. Property transfer instrument: delisting[^c-23aeedc2a3ebeb314fa2e9741f5eae5b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A property transfer instrument may provide for the listing of securities, under section 74 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, to be discontinued or suspended.

(2) Where the listing of securities is suspended in accordance with a property transfer instrument, those securities are to be treated for the purposes of section 96 of, and paragraph 23(6) of Schedule 1ZA to, the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 as still being listed.
