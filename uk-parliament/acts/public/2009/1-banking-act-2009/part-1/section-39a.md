# Banking Act 2009, section 39A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39A. Banks which are recognised central counterparties[^c-388fa4afca4c752c2e23fa4e5becb39c][^c-70686850b02e755850ca69a527404d82]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Paragraphs 59, 60 and 108 of Schedule 11 to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2023[^c-249a07e23ee89a07ef0c109e9986fc01] (recognised central counterparty rules, membership and recognition) apply in relation to a bank which would be a recognised central counterparty[^c-74676b2a944cb71d46e9f40f9b50ff70] but for paragraph 155(2) of Schedule 11 to that Act[^c-40419c7a500e5bf53996c2fb23dc3b40] (exclusion of banks etc from definition of recognised central counterparty) as they apply in relation to a recognised central counterparty.
