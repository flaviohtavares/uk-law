# Banking Act 2009, section 53

## 53. Onward and reverse transfers etc[^M_I_1979030b-384f-4664-a2fe-ae19e1ee7bcc][^c-ef20f5788d864ad7ae7f2d772cd6da97][^c-69b8a60e4ed5d0a069b0947588415ef2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where—

- (zza)[^c-39274ba0a99bfe7a523948b05740ff2f] the Bank of England makes a supplemental share transfer instrument under section 26,
- (zzb)[^c-be1b0272fe2684b4d5264dc5fff006a6] the Bank of England makes an onward share transfer instrument under section 26ZA,
- (za)[^c-e0f659f44b18388483bb41be55cf2688] the Bank of England makes a [^c-8b66b08d92adca32e19ad0c37d48d4b2]... reverse share transfer instrument under section 26A,
- (zb)[^c-90cf64d31a7eb1b9162f2cbd8105af87] the Treasury makes a supplemental share transfer order under section 27,
- (a) the Treasury make an onward share transfer order under section 28,
- (b) the Treasury makes a reverse share transfer order under section 29,
- (c) the Bank of England makes a resolution company[^c-0c6e14e3880524fd9b8e117266978eb7] share transfer instrument under section 30,
- (d) the Bank of England makes a resolution company[^c-d33a4117573ff424e5bf4ed34ac13eea] reverse share transfer instrument under section 31,
- (dza)[^c-8e1a66e7ff29ccb307071e2379189b06] the Bank of England makes a supplemental property transfer instrument under section 42,
- (da)[^c-0c956ab6c5abc0826f7fb074def0eb5d] the Bank of England makes a private sector reverse property transfer instrument under section 42A,
- (e) the Bank of England makes an onward property transfer instrument under section 43,
- (f) the Bank of England makes a reverse property transfer instrument under section 44,
- (fa)[^c-4aea54d6436b8fc4725349aaabe7517e] the Bank of England makes a reverse property transfer instrument under section 44A(2),
- (fb)[^c-7b0b334f6207142fe16b92a7ed4b0adb] the Bank of England makes a bridge bank supplemental property transfer instrument under section 44D,
- (fc) the Bank of England makes a bridge bank supplemental reverse property transfer instrument under section 44E,
- (g) the Treasury make a property transfer order under section 45, [^c-9d714a3c0f5398e6f75e2cc3edb88a3e]...
- (h) the Treasury make a reverse property transfer order under section 46.
- (i)[^c-0478b71268546a70667c281e228bf768] the Bank of England makes a supplemental resolution instrument under section 48U,
- (j) the Bank of England makes an onward transfer resolution instrument under section 48V(2), or
- (k) the Bank of England makes a reverse transfer resolution instrument under section 48W(2) or (3).

(2) The Treasury may make—

- (a) a compensation scheme order;
- (b) a third party compensation order.
