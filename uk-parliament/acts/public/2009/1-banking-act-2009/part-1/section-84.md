# Banking Act 2009, section 84

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84. Application of Part 1: general[^key-ab4ba26ddf3ca2bbf833094310890f72]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part applies to building societies (within the meaning of section 119 of the Building Societies Act 1986) as it applies to banks, subject to—[^key-ab10939e0d5bae14c861615cc585bbe3]

- (a) the provisions of the Table, and
- (b) sections 84ZA[^key-8832124211c527c92c1a634433166696] to 84D (which relate to the second and fourth stabilisation options[^key-90f7d04750c89ef6fee0a64e8826effc] ).
