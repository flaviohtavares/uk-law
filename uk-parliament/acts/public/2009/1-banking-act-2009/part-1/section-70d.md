# Banking Act 2009, section 70D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70D. Suspension: general provisions[^key-13365eb4956bbeb16c14fa33a9936bc5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of sections 70A to 70C—

- “business day” means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday, or a day which is a bank holiday under the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 in any part of the United Kingdom;
- “excluded person” means—
  - (a) a person who has been declared to be, or who is an operator of, a designated system under regulation 4 of the Financial Markets and Insolvency (Settlement Finality) Regulations 1999,
  - (b) [^key-94259488bf9cff25ab9a9998a7d2a2fc]...
  - (c) a recognised central counterparty [^key-33a6cd0eff1ab33a4547acff5105363b]... or third country central counterparty, or
  - (d) a central bank.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1) [^key-ff51376d5ad824a366cdbd429c3a95a7]..., “recognised central counterparty” and “third country central counterparty” have the meaning given in section 285 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.
