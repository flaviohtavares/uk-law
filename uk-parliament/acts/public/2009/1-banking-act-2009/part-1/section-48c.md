# Banking Act 2009, section 48C

## 48C. Meaning of “protected deposit”[^key-e08285c6098396b69338da688e9dbcc0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-7ae67a87f69b1070841fe94c94fba423] A deposit is “protected” so far as it is covered by the FSCS[^key-903f32b80af68514ab6707c20eacc03b].

(4) In subsection (1)[^key-9f96e28502fa0b4521541ccc486e7e82] and section 48B(8)(a), “**deposit**” has the meaning given by article 5(2) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities) Order 2001 (S.I. 2001/544), but ignoring the exclusions in article 6.
