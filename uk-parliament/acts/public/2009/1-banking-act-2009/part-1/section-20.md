# Banking Act 2009, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Directors and senior managers[^M_I_9c93fdc5-bc6b-4c1c-ade6-98b3c6ae4453][^c-c5d6267513e0e8df943e326ef5098b5e][^c-94663893b2362f00c953ddccf322d531]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A share transfer instrument may enable the Bank of England—

- (a) to remove a director or senior manager[^c-6f1ad46dc89b6c92cc68bbd87fd8cbaf] of a specified bank;
- (b) to vary the service contract of a director or senior manager of a specified bank;
- (c) to terminate the service contract of a director or senior manager of a specified bank;
- (d) to appoint a director or senior manager of a specified bank.

(1A)[^c-a87c2de212f60db5bc6554838dd68fda] Subsection (1) also applies to a director or senior manager[^c-1334c57111b844175c052cafe6ebbe80] of any undertaking which is a banking group company in respect of a specified bank.

(2) A share transfer order may enable the Treasury—

- (a) to remove a director or senior manager[^c-2e2616bec5f8b16f9ffa4b1e09a012e8] of a specified bank;
- (b) to vary the service contract of a director or senior manager of a specified bank;
- (c) to terminate the service contract of a director or senior manager of a specified bank;
- (d) to appoint a director or senior manager of a specified bank.

(3) Appointments under subsection (1)(d) are to be on terms and conditions agreed with the Bank of England.

(4) Appointments under subsection (2)(d) are to be on terms and conditions agreed with the Treasury.

(5)[^c-b616d039e676ce5119093f2185a6c5c5] In this section “senior manager” means a person who—

- (a) exercises executive functions within a specified bank or banking group company, and
- (b) is responsible, and directly accountable to the directors, for the day to day management of that bank or banking group company.
