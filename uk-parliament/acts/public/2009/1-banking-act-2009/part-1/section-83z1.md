## 83Z1. Delegation of enforcement functions[^key-078a3a5196c0cbc0c6c8cf9b32d7d4e1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Bank of England may, by agreement made with the FCA[^key-b4d49ab519839770b6882ec8fcb9a72b], delegate to the FCA[^key-043e471b1490312ea371e19521e2bdb2] its enforcement functions, subject to such restrictions and conditions, and for such period, as may be specified in the agreement.

(2) For the purposes of this section—

- (a) “enforcement functions” of the Bank of England are its functions under, or by virtue of—
  - (i) section 83ZD;
  - (ii) section 83ZF;
  - (iii) section 83ZJ;
  - (iv) section 83ZL;
  - (v) section 83ZO;
  - (vi) sections 83ZQ to 83ZZ;
- (b)[^key-6d8ac48d5c24863c75ba9ccb9243fd03] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The Bank of England must make provision for the reimbursement of any expenses incurred by the FCA[^key-420779117910dd447c9bbe89b76f19fb] in the performance, in accordance with the terms of any agreement, of any functions delegated under this section.
