# Banking Act 2009, section 153

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 153. Successful rescue[^key-136ebf0b60a044129784d34064f507f6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) the Bank of England has given an Objective 1 Achievement Notice, and
- (b) the bank administrator has pursued Objective 2(a) in section 140 and believes that it has been achieved.

(2)[^key-168f7db9c1bab838752445069cbbd5b0] The bank administrator shall make an application under paragraph 79 of Schedule B1 to the Insolvency Act 1986 (court ending administration on achievement of objectives).

(3) A bank administrator who makes an application in accordance with subsection (2) must send a copy to the PRA and the FCA[^key-b14969d3351a875466380bda978f0c65].

(4) Failure without reasonable excuse to comply with subsection (3) is an offence.
