# Banking Act 2009, section 264

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 264. Extent

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act extends to—

- (a) England and Wales,
- (b) Scotland, and
- (c) Northern Ireland.

(2) But—

- (a) sections 253 and 254 extend to Scotland only, and
- (b) an amendment of an enactment has the same extent as the enactment (or the relevant part).
