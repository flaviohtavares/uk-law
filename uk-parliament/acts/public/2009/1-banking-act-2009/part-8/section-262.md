# Banking Act 2009, section 262

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 262. Repeal[^M_I_f7d994cb-25af-4784-bab7-27f2a483cc36][^c-96214f19dedf39cf26e1df3bdd3f9186]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Treasury may by order repeal the Banking (Special Provisions) Act 2008.

(2) An order—

- (a) may include savings, and
- (b) shall be made by statutory instrument.

(3) Subsection (2)(a) is without prejudice to the generality of, or the application to this section of, section 259.
