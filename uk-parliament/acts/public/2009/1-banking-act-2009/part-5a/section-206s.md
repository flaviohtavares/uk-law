# Banking Act 2009, section 206S

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 206S. Publication[^c-ae09a7768d77aba2c7df294dec95488a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Bank of England may publish details of—

- (a) a compliance failure by a recognised person;
- (b) a sanction imposed under sections 206T to 206V.
