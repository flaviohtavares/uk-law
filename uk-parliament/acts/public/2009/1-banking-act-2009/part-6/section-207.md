# Banking Act 2009, section 207

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 207. Overview[^c-d70802a918556324b211cbfa54bf9d86]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part—

- (a) repeals existing provisions about permission to issue banknotes in Scotland and Northern Ireland, and
- (b) replaces the provisions, but only for authorised banks (see section 210).[^c-d268300f93033b90cf9a13b3b6f83799]
