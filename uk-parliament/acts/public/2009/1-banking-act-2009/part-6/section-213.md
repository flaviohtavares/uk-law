# Banking Act 2009, section 213

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 213. Authorisation to issue banknotes[^c-7cd5c2cc1e2efec99d6494751f4b3ee9][^c-3e4fb35f8b322e4677ff8d6c4a5ff931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-fc4c2f39b17d00cfc52881cb7437868a] An authorised bank within section 210(a)[^c-974fc5ac12a7cb411232300983f501df] may continue to issue banknotes after commencement, but only—

- (a) in accordance with the provisions of this Part, and
- (b) in the part of the United Kingdom in which it was authorised to issue banknotes before commencement.

(2)[^c-862789f8e87c4f7aae3700b5bade46eb] An authorised bank within section 210(b) may issue banknotes, but only—

- (a) in accordance with the provisions of this Part, and
- (b) in the part of the United Kingdom which is specified in relation to the bank in regulations under section 214A(1)(b).
