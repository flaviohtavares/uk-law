# Banking Act 2009, section 225

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 225. Organisation[^key-ad7e4395ebe8716e20c7d96e01a10a2a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Expenses incurred and sums received by the Bank of England in connection with its functions under this Part are to be treated as expenses and receipts of the Issue Department.
