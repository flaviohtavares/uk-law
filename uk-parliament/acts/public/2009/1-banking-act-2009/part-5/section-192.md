## 192. Role of FCA and PRA[^c-302a2700e1a84e6646a74750ffb4b3f5][^c-62234a9eb4de65629d2338c1901b65ad]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In exercising powers under this Part the Bank of England shall have regard to any action that the FCA or the PRA[^c-4974d825d8b89236aced830f5b3c91cd] has taken or could take.

(2)[^c-6bd5a1128cd6b84fe454d445dbc2b4e2] The Bank of England—

- (a) must consult the FCA before taking action under this Part in respect of a recognised [^c-9dfda2e65d9538f7dc665f0bdc46b807]... payment system the operator of which satisfies section 186(2)(a) , a service provider in relation to such a system or a service provider which itself satisfies section 186(2)(a)[^c-364c7681c518c72e7db420fa4690b6d1] and
- (b) must consult the PRA before taking action under this Part in respect of a recognised [^c-9dfda2e65d9538f7dc665f0bdc46b807]... payment system the operator of which satisfies section 186(2)(b) , a service provider in relation to such a system or a service provider which itself satisfies section 186(2)(b)[^c-d3f4d15c457a6893e791ebf1c954c446].

(3) If the FCA or the PRA[^c-be554adf6d610971d31b795cdb860a10] gives the Bank of England notice that it[^c-5d7b2745465c1ff312d9fe863add7b87] is considering taking action in respect of the operator of a recognised [^c-52d44df2308bca2a49e0032fc16ba717]... payment system who satisfies section 186(2)(a) or (b)[^c-c5b4d30a08a7e14477943e744d53703b] , a service provider in relation to such a system or a service provider which itself satisfies section 186(2)(a) or (b)[^c-d4d407c435d926ad6e43fdc09c606b4f] the Bank may not take action under this Part in respect of the operator or service provider[^c-4ae087a75c5cf62a4e030ac9f89851f5] unless—

- (a) the FCA or (as the case may be) the PRA[^c-884abc5524d46dff26023fb7b4b7ac66] consents, or
- (b) the notice is withdrawn.
