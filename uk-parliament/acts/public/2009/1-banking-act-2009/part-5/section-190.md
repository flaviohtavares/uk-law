# Banking Act 2009, section 190

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 190. System rules[^key-c8bf069f4f8bd8aa072cd8dfcd1f5dea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Bank of England may require the operator of a recognised [^key-01d5f7980ad5ce237b4349921899a70b]... payment system—

- (a) to establish rules for the operation of the system including the operation of services that form part of the arrangements constituting[^key-7006aa6f5b01523541eb03d3024f041c] , or connected with,[^key-cc3ac155cdb479f4307fa9d9d75aa03e] the system and are provided by a service provider , or a DSA service provider[^key-c49755085e356c97709e8eda495653e9];
- (b) to change the rules in a specified way or so as to achieve a specified purpose;
- (c) to notify the Bank of any proposed change to the rules;
- (d) not to change the rules without the approval of the Bank.

(2) A requirement under subsection (1)(c) or (d) may be general or specific.
