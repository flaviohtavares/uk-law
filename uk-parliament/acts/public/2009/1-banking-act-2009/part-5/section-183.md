# Banking Act 2009, section 183

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 183. Interpretation: other expressions[^c-ead4662254920ed211883fc8a5549545]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- (a) a reference to the “**operator**” of a[^c-25e0995eba8156d18c9146a092dfc968] payment system is a reference to any person with responsibility under the system for managing or operating it,
- (b) a reference to the operation of a system includes a reference to its management,
- (ba)[^c-7a4144dd8c63fe4919cffc0201c36f69] a reference to a “service provider” is to be construed in accordance with section 206A(2),
- (c) “**the UK financial system**” has the meaning given [^c-d3d7350751bc1c58c0eed07ca0dce104]... by section 1I[^c-8f55ae5571450391ec773c178fb9d58c] of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 [^c-ff11ab8e38d66a4f0b448fa9902b3697]...,
- (d) a reference to the Bank of England's role as a monetary authority is to be construed in accordance with section 244(2)(c),
- (e)[^c-f4485393ea6d4724a6df1e500ce08b52] the FCA” means the Financial Conduct Authority,
- (f) “**Part 4A permission**” has the meaning given by section 55A of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000,
- (g) “**the PRA**” means the Prudential Regulation Authority,
- (h) “**PRA-regulated activity**” has the meaning given by section 22A of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, [^c-fc8935f30f52116876dfc642a5333895]...
- (i) “**recognised investment exchange**” has the meaning given by section 285 of that Act.
- (j)[^c-44ae2f7b7605c8315cb9a29dde221aaa] “the Payment Systems Regulator” means the Payment Systems Regulator established under section 40 of the Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013, and
- (k) in sections 188 to 199 (regulation and enforcement), references to the provision of services by a service provider to a payment system or to a DSA service provider[^c-7128a2a8926f629c5592ad1e79c139e8] include references to—
  - (i) services provided by the service provider which form part of the arrangements constituting , or connected with,[^c-b24774d8101e95482d33fd8ae008b42a] the system, and
  - (ii) the service provider’s arrangements for governance or risk management, or for any other matters which may affect the provision of the services by the service provider.
