# Banking Act 2009, section 191

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 191. Directions[^key-8658387667b7957621e67d9473ed7049]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Bank of England may give directions in writing to—

- (a)[^key-2bb25ebddd0415c92de618f3d21a0636] the operator of a recognised [^key-d17465d014240bace81458883cd2f3ea]... payment[^key-12dff169c093f215665fd98c79c2397f] system,
- (b)[^key-e902f9b4286b1b74f052f3a5d674801d] to a recognised DSA service provider, or
- (c)[^key-928fe3b0e2574cef440246470d9f3353] a service provider in relation to such a system[^key-c34a6195df513f75a9bae2d4bc8d1c7d] or to such DSA service providers[^key-f14ef121beab42bdf0ca2dcc57f93ae0].

(2) A direction may—

- (a) require or prohibit the taking of specified action in the operation of the system or the provision of services[^key-082b0985eba09964c50a57bd84279938] [^key-31f5f3376af6fe13de068464e33faccf]...;
- (b) set standards to be met in the operation of the system or the provision of services[^key-b5e697aab4833c701a5674f08e455dab] [^key-1d893490e0355bc88cdf8a0a6e9ddadc]....

(3) If a direction is given for the purpose of resolving or reducing a threat to the stability of the UK financial system, the operator , DSA service provider[^key-e8c2575b01c79f5d6f48c9336bd3b21e] or service provider[^key-186da17355628f5f80636d5f2c4d4cb5] (including the operator’s[^key-d754943503c4a4d6b4a1b282c2f59c03] , DSA service provider’s[^key-eb4774383ca8c91377b7879d21acb31e] or service provider’s officers and staff) has immunity from liability in damages in respect of action or inaction in accordance with the direction.

(4) A direction given for the purpose mentioned in subsection (3) must—

- (a) include a statement that it is given for that purpose, and
- (b) inform the operator , DSA service provider[^key-62e61ec8415ab0b31b3de491b60e6efd] or service provider[^key-edab1cb5a98eac80ad4c861812152238] of the effect of that subsection.

(5) The Treasury may by order confer immunity on any person from liability in damages in respect of action or inaction in accordance with a direction (including a direction given for the purpose mentioned in subsection (3)).

(6) An order—

- (a) is to be made by statutory instrument, and
- (b) is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(7) An immunity conferred by or under this section does not extend to action or inaction—

- (a) in bad faith, or
- (b) in contravention of section 6(1) of the Human Rights Act 1998.
