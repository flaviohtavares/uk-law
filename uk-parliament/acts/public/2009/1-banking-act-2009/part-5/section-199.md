## 199. Closure[^key-af381779208bbf14b3cde92e95b0cfe9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if the Bank of England thinks that a compliance failure—

- (a) threatens the stability of, or confidence in, the UK financial system, or
- (b) has serious consequences for business or other interests throughout the United Kingdom.

(2) The Bank may give the operator of the [^key-ce82a9104589275e3954eb4c141a9b9c]... payment system concerned , the DSA service provider concerned[^key-a8087e0d5b1fbe6a9d30b920861b1e8f] , or the service provider concerned,[^key-fd12be050cb1a7e09174277d365e2fbf] an order to stop operating the system , providing services,[^key-be91b8a5749aaacd7c441f8db8d6f6c3] or (as the case may be) providing services to a recognised payment system[^key-09b3c184ea2f2531924faf757189a80b] or recognised DSA service provider[^key-b11b7e1b9435a82c6c2f3da5f192e4df] (a “closure order”)—

- (a) for a specified period,
- (b) until further notice, or
- (c) permanently.

(3) A closure order may apply to—

- (a) all activities of the payment system , or DSA service provider[^key-5e8ace696e0c149ae6eb8379fffb32f8] or all services provided to a recognised payment system[^key-e6aa5810ed057d0c6fe0776f525e853f] , or a recognised DSA service provider[^key-240a24ae2d574298c7e57586e81a9ff3] by the service provider, or
- (b) specified activities or specified services[^key-fef2d87ebc8d861ca581e7983beddd3d].

(3A)[^key-8c3e8d50c0ba7ae70537c33ed11067eb] Before giving a closure order to a service provider, the Bank must have regard to the public interest in the continued operation of each recognised payment system , or of each recognised DSA service provider,[^key-01a3abf36af0bc4afb2be985516b4b08] in relation to which the service provider is specified under section 206A(2)(b) or 206A(2A)(b) (as the case may be).[^key-ae70304fc6f2b2d8432aad7632e9a6d7]

(4) An operator , DSA service provider,[^key-be1dc091c196aab31eae1c9414d785e6] or service provider[^key-f338084707a182a4f862690ca07ef8c2] who fails to comply with a closure order commits an offence.

(5) A person guilty of an offence is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine.
