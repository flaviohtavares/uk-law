# Banking Act 2009, section 196

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 196. Compliance failure[^key-ce863d633320921e926d5841464511c5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part “**compliance failure**” means a failure by the operator of a recognised [^key-3e22c9eecf18f47d3494676e7255246c]... payment system , a recognised DSA service provider[^key-af0767cbbdda2d1c2bae95fa02703a36] , or a service provider in relation to such a system,[^key-96f79ba0f139aa7fd3cc374aac7a3b4a] or such a DSA service provider[^key-656763fa13f3ac245301c5f39a740954] to—

- (a) comply with a code of practice under section 189,
- (b) comply with a requirement under section 190,
- (c) comply with a direction under section 191, or
- (d) ensure compliance with a requirement under section 195.
