# Banking Act 2009, section 193

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 193. Inspection[^key-dc92c1caa3a133f37e05d8430dc08097]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Bank of England may appoint one or more persons to inspect the operation of a recognised [^key-61658525fd125fb83555fa826e56afe6]... payment system , a recognised DSA service provider[^key-542cc9cb9c6d65b76256ee174712a772] or the provision of services to such a system[^key-c8970262a1abdcea2c7f02d94e3b19e0] or such a DSA service provider[^key-24014bf43edac8223948c91815f22cf8] by a service provider.

(2) The operator of a recognised [^key-61658525fd125fb83555fa826e56afe6]... payment system , or a recognised DSA service provider[^key-62f01254a9f4201a28838e8c216dd914] , or a service provider in relation to such a system[^key-b3ce2501b20b8ee831c2833036011832] or such a DSA service provider[^key-59cbc8a156878b2c4a151eabb5beecd3], must—

- (a) grant an inspector access, on request and at any reasonable time, to premises on or from which any part of the system is operated or (as the case may be) premises on or from which any part of the services is provided[^key-02b5fdbb62e172eb582be7bf728441b8], and
- (b) otherwise co-operate with an inspector.
