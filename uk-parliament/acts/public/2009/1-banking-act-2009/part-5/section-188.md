# Banking Act 2009, section 188

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 188. Principles[^c-e838ddaa4aa56e046b442373256dc55e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Bank of England may publish—

- (a)[^c-5510d137b8ea53c3f930db9b092238a1] principles to which operators of recognised [^c-42b44352c340019e1d4a7caef77578a8]... payment systems are to have regard in operating the systems and[^c-f5b1f6a27826575c4b668df2ebeb06b8]
- (b)[^c-56ef180aae7bfc55e90733ff49acc3d2] principles to which recognised DSA service providers are to have regard in the provision of services to payment systems (whether or not recognised),
- (c)[^c-1c8c6847fe08dbc65ae7aa116f0e5b35] principles to which service providers are to have regard in the provision of services to such systems or to such DSA service providers[^c-a9da3889ca687927d78cb9839503d096].

(2) Before publishing principles the Bank must obtain the approval of the Treasury.
