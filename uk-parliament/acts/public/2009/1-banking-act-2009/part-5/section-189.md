# Banking Act 2009, section 189

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1

## 189. Codes of practice[^key-22813bd5500621cf5163bffdc0236da0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Bank of England may publish codes of practice about—

- (a)[^key-c19213ec3dc924bb0984e0db4e5507f2] the operation of recognised [^key-6abaa55455196794c5157b36e2f2e6de]... payment systems; [^key-d496d8f7981153224412d259b9c608a8]...
- (b)[^key-a9e5dee400890c8ce2826a5cfd0d2325] the provision of services by DSA service providers in relation to payment systems (whether or not recognised), or
- (c)[^key-6dd7081a8bbf5d37fcced08ba4154220] the provision of services by service providers to such systems or to such DSA service providers[^key-8daff22de65ef0c5cce62ce6a57a3815].
