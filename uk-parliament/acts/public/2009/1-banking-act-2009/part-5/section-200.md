# Banking Act 2009, section 200

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 200. Management disqualification[^c-a1dc5fb458da87b3986b079851ff3359]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Bank of England may by order prohibit a specified person from being an operator of a recognised [^c-0dbc3f7a05468065d0347d70da12bfb9]... payment system or from being a DSA service provider[^c-19dd99f179b0462765999d31eb271355]—

- (a) for a specified period,
- (b) until further notice, or
- (c) permanently.

(2) The Bank may by order prohibit a specified person from holding an office or position involving responsibility for taking decisions about the management of a recognised [^c-0dbc3f7a05468065d0347d70da12bfb9]... payment system , or a recognised DSA service provider,[^c-b47e0c56822dfe65a976d901daee536f] or about the management of a service provider in relation to such a system[^c-9ad5e61fc1cdfb58e146d9b6db165d65] or such a DSA service provider[^c-606efdfdc8af4aa606d580bfaff6c312]—

- (a) for a specified period,
- (b) until further notice, or
- (c) permanently.

(2A)[^c-1fc836937ebbdd6bbfbd75e72e619678] Before making an order under subsection (2) in respect of a service provider, the Bank must have regard to the public interest in the continued operation of each recognised payment system , or of each recognised DSA service provider,[^c-f4e9e1f8d67dc4448c3b80a0c96d16cc] in relation to which the service provider is specified under section 206A(2)(b). or 206A(2A)(b) (as the case may be).[^c-0b0650e4fe224a246e8c9d38cca44c5f]

(3) A person who breaches a prohibition under subsection (1) or (2) commits an offence.

(4) A person guilty of an offence is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine.
