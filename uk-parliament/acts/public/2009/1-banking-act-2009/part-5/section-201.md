# Banking Act 2009, section 201

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 201. Warning[^c-0f95f332a6e4915baae7cf53987ba3de][^c-003a8ed7a372697deceb4b3e5fb7ee89]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Before imposing a sanction on the operator of a[^c-32fb4db2a45fdad26343113c227ee3c9] payment system , on a DSA service provider,[^c-4ebd6acbc1a7ef7699007d2bd8743a01] , on a service provider in relation to such a system[^c-889fdd545bef039927a38688fac00f92] or such a DSA service provider[^c-ed889a6403621f402e49843fa74d5c5d] or on another person the Bank of England must—

- (a) give the operator , DSA service provider[^c-1f8f28b4827689feb7d666e0f2d0d69c] , service provider[^c-c82e46b26242e012ae57e63244f1bb8f] or other person a notice (a “warning notice”),
- (b) give the operator , DSA service provider[^c-45473800df19f237c26bb267319897cc] , service provider[^c-2307de6dd9f286136d6e05dfdadfa2b0] or other person at least 21 days to make representations,
- (c) consider any representations made, and
- (d) as soon as is reasonably practicable, give the operator , DSA service provider[^c-93ae3296ab0886caea8e917510b5db17] , service provider[^c-7a4a537f386af8cf72d3703b3e2e79f5] or other person a notice stating whether or not the Bank intends to impose the sanction.

(1A)[^c-bccaf0deb352c98a6a03ce2d8875e61e] Before imposing a sanction on a person who is a service provider in relation to a recognised payment system or recognised DSA service provider[^c-aa4cd163a56f2a2f1ec20e231846704d] the Bank must also—

- (a) give the operator of the payment system , or DSA service provider[^c-7aee37457412b62877a1c73ae28f7b43] a notice (a “warning notice”),
- (b) give the operator , or DSA service provider[^c-bca1fe95dfcb9cfb206666a8169bd88d] at least 21 days to make representations,
- (c) consider any representations made, and
- (d) as soon as reasonably practicable, give the operator or DSA service provider[^c-b7d301ca9c42ee5322e7d535d7406376] a notice stating whether the Bank intends to impose the sanction.

(2) In subsections (1) and (1A)[^c-cf3585e55085229fe8e0dcd52647b708] “**imposing a sanction**” means—

- (a) publishing details under section 197(1),
- (b) requiring the payment of a penalty under section 198,
- (c) giving a closure order under section 199, or
- (d) making an order under section 200.

(3) Despite subsections (1) and (1A)[^c-983e4cc7597f06e6251d3cbc7841ae1e], if satisfied that it is necessary the Bank may without notice—

- (a) give a closure order under section 199, or
- (b) make an order under section 200.
