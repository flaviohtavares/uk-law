# Banking Act 2009, section 197

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 197. Publication[^key-ddbd96d2c7a9bd96523f9926485d1c8e][^key-a1e2c46f89a21c1c2dd195c29ff99b54]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Bank of England may publish details of a compliance failure by the operator of a recognised [^key-b45d8be3bc6cca5267213679083f597d]... payment system , a recognised DSA service provider[^key-c86716369bb3810bdd922c3759835580] or a service provider in relation to such a system[^key-8d37d78279837f7fdad3d21cea9f3276] or such a DSA service provider[^key-be607a011266cc2fa6d9bf7d05ccf320].

(2) The Bank may publish details of a sanction imposed under sections 198 to 200.
