# Banking Act 2009, section 205

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 205. Pretending to be recognised[^c-b5ea17a43639fbf0487c6da4a6e0064c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It is an offence for the operator of a non-recognised [^c-fd64b0d864026e6b5cc7c34b748a3d13]... payment system or DSA service provider[^c-9f8e65f5324ec6606be3f1c805cc9abb]—

- (a) to assert that the system or provider[^c-7cfe8ccc44824f7dd500d13111f8b85c] is recognised, or
- (b) to do anything which suggests that the system or provider[^c-1e21c41aaec9ee983f979af29e5ef718] is recognised.

(1A)[^c-b8822f76d5a1c4a3a9f57c8144f09859] It is an offence for a person who is not a service provider in relation to a recognised payment system or recognised DSA service provider[^c-0b653f5330ae0c3d2683663aac0829ab]—

- (a) to assert that the person is such a service provider, or
- (b) to do anything which suggests that the person is such a service provider.

(2) A person guilty of an offence is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine.
