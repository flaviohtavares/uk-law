# Banking Act 2009, section 187

## 187. De-recognition[^c-4935c97586077c3b124fe24417f02469]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Treasury may revoke a recognition order.

(2) The Treasury must revoke a recognition order if not satisfied—

- (a)[^c-74930d728a6407e944108bfe40b972cb] that the criteria in section 185 are met in respect of the recognised [^c-2d62bfb14d66393d8db9dca6d6794cb2]... payment system. , or[^c-99c9e5a534d794dbffe2a5b9cc590ac2]
- (b) that the criteria in section 185A are met in respect of the recognised DSA service provider.

(3) Before revoking a recognition order the Treasury must—

- (a) consult the Bank of England and the Payment Systems Regulator[^c-93a7a1c2826c57f9e3cbe125aecb4af0],
- (aa)[^c-34f3ab4388aad2e33569ce84961f6eaf] in the case of a recognition order in respect of a DSA service provider, consult the FCA,
- (b) notify the operator of the recognised [^c-fd86ee37f432a777a44118d4f0417135]... payment system or the recognised DSA service provider (as appropriate)[^c-584f02141d679d0a16009c2f3c38fc05], and
- (c) consider any representations made.

(4)[^c-2fe366103a8bb9b0b287658fb56c4657] In addition, the Treasury—

- (a) must consult the FCA before revoking a recognition order in respect of a payment system or a DSA service provider, where the operator of the system or the provider[^c-f1fbf2259b56e3ba5278e66d29c8c672] —
  - (i) is, or has applied to become, a recognised investment exchange, or
  - (ii) has, or has applied for, a Part 4A permission, and
- (b) if the operator or provider[^c-454033b8b5d18aae6a5cf39aeacab8e1] has, or has applied for, a Part 4A permission for the carrying on of a PRA-regulated activity, must also consult the PRA.

(5) The Treasury must consider any request by the operator of a recognised [^c-9f880c433b63befe313a4c0c7bc6f516]... payment system , or by a recognised DSA service provider[^c-6e19930c3b8d29f764bd558edc5eb9db] for the revocation of its recognition order.
