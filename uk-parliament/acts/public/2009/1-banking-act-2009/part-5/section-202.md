# Banking Act 2009, section 202

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 202. Appeal[^c-57863e87290e0906af1fa6a0f7304bad][^c-00dbaef829c8957d1a34b82bc817bbec]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the Bank of England notifies a person under section 201(1)(d) or (1A)(d)[^c-d1fa521c4abb4db72a223a8abfa7ffd4] that the Bank intends to impose a sanction, the person may appeal to the Upper Tribunal[^c-78efc6a076b3e56b7289075732e760f8].

(2) Where the Bank of England imposes a sanction on a person without notice in reliance on section 201(3), the person and, if the person is a service provider in relation to a recognised payment system[^c-da9841042f3d51ae2088f0e9db4761f5] or recognised DSA service provider[^c-ac1d7f95ffd1605d4a9875f24562b1c7], the operator of the payment system or DSA service provider (as the case may be)[^c-a66e86231840c141f83b3026a830e059], may appeal to the Upper Tribunal[^c-4358cec410ace609c78eca8013ba7346].

(3)[^c-8a67ce338eb8243690efd8b190286ba2] The Bank of England may not impose a sanction while an appeal under this section could be brought or is pending.
