# Banking Act 2009, section 204

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 204. Information

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Bank of England may by notice in writing require a person to provide information—

- (a)[^c-4378d8571c7233c58b96c19ff69c09bc] which the Bank thinks will help the Treasury in determining whether to make a recognition order or an order under section 206A[^c-194b582d8bd2af9edbcbdc019d459129] , or to specify a person under section 206A(2)(b)[^c-bbb13e1cd9346296f4a5ed734a638a22] or 206A(2A)(b)[^c-631ee2ade4fc8cbde59a7dcb6d7f0073], or
- (b)[^c-3af4830a2f1d248c69c74282e78fd67b] which the Bank otherwise requires in connection with its functions under this Part.

(1A)[^c-9dacf32eceb894ff92b4c96fb23cde58] The Bank of England may by notice in writing require the operator of a recognised [^c-e9a1f2471ca81e5cf022e083f284bb39]... payment system , a recognised DSA service provider,[^c-b826f43166f152d644aa7f390920ce70] or a service provider in relation to such a system[^c-97e729f73638af33955806c57fe2f0ea] or such a DSA service provider[^c-ed7614d37f43bc192a7e364fe59d161c] to provide information which the Bank requires in connection with the exercise of its functions (whether under this Part or otherwise) in pursuance of its financial stability objective.

(2)[^c-4378d8571c7233c58b96c19ff69c09bc] In particular, a notice under subsection (1) or (1A)[^c-ab9bfe172b1a58d7b9675885a37c3004] may require the operator of a recognised [^c-28b3829f9b8c482a685b50555d1e1323]... payment system , a recognised DSA service provider,[^c-3c79244874d476ec8f0119044b75fa2d] or a service provider in relation to such a system[^c-a9ff4f8220555689823fd104db2ff0ed] or such a DSA service provider[^c-e0306c7e32a4ddefcd14f3cceeb54e9b] to notify the Bank if events of a specified kind occur.

(3)[^c-4378d8571c7233c58b96c19ff69c09bc] A notice under subsection (1) or (1A)[^c-dc0890ba03f117ec24fafa98596145e0] may require information to be provided—

- (a) in a specified form or manner;
- (b) at a specified time;
- (c) in respect of a specified period.

(4) The Bank may disclose information obtained by virtue of this section to—

- (a)[^c-4378d8571c7233c58b96c19ff69c09bc] the Treasury;
- (b)[^c-4378d8571c7233c58b96c19ff69c09bc][^c-89a6a4d7872b2b47122a2ff79380b2e1] the FCA;
- (ba)[^c-700bdb736e2580507f4d47bc492187f5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-3af4830a2f1d248c69c74282e78fd67b] an authority in a country or territory outside the United Kingdom which exercises functions similar to those of the Treasury, the Bank of England , the FCA or the PRA[^c-8b3fd067c3d6c415c18e98e2a4c1243a] in relation to [^c-bb3c914b3aef32eee09791979643595f]... payment systems;
- (d)[^c-aedcac837437b403fbcddd901b1be80b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^c-3af4830a2f1d248c69c74282e78fd67b] the Bank for International Settlements.

(5)[^c-4378d8571c7233c58b96c19ff69c09bc] Subsection (4)—

- (a) overrides a contractual or other requirement to keep information in confidence, and
- (b) is without prejudice to any other power to disclose information.

(6)[^c-4378d8571c7233c58b96c19ff69c09bc] The Treasury may by regulations permit the disclosure of information obtained by virtue of this section to a specified person.

(7)[^c-3af4830a2f1d248c69c74282e78fd67b] The Bank may publish information obtained by virtue of this section.

(8)[^c-4378d8571c7233c58b96c19ff69c09bc] The Treasury may make regulations about the manner and extent of publication under subsection (7).

(9)[^c-4378d8571c7233c58b96c19ff69c09bc] Regulations under this section—

- (a) shall be made by statutory instrument, and
- (b) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(10)[^c-4378d8571c7233c58b96c19ff69c09bc] It is an offence—

- (a) to fail without reasonable excuse to comply with a requirement under this section;
- (b) knowingly or recklessly to give false information in pursuance of this section.

(11)[^c-4378d8571c7233c58b96c19ff69c09bc] A person guilty of an offence is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to a fine.
