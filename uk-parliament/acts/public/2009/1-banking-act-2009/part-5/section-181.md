# Banking Act 2009, section 181

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 181. Overview[^key-a5872e494b83cdf4b27d616bf6a33f40]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Part enables the Bank of England to oversee certain systems for transferring money[^key-8f3d4841600ee8d999b5e3aa9d9bf4d2] and certain persons who provide services[^key-805996bda26dae40684ab2bb462a6ab7] , including[^key-905c5f8b7c994c0712ed4d104131c4c5] in relation to such systems.
