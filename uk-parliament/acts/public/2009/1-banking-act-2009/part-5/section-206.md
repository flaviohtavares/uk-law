# Banking Act 2009, section 206

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-01-19. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/1
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 206. Saving for informal oversight[^c-a728e1aed1beac723b5ef5df083eab3d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Nothing in this Part prevents the Bank of England from having dealings with the operators of payment systems , DSA service providers[^c-804d8331e85d09d159e99561adcaf3f7] , or persons who provide services in relation to payment systems,[^c-1f48578d6684d1d2b0f922a0daffbc89] or DSA service providers[^c-451056f8c6aa26ad73fbb97f117fb3b4] to which this Part does not apply.

(2) Nothing in this Part prevents the Bank from having dealings, other than through the provisions of this Part, with the operators of payment systems , DSA service providers[^c-1446c4b4f15a5229b6c6c0004bf062a9] , or persons who provide services in relation to payment systems,[^c-c8c1273f910a4a8baaefeb6911666a32] or DSA service providers[^c-c0f256a8031796ece7f6ec0825e0a518] to which this Part does apply.
