# Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009, section 122

## 122. Sharing of information for education and training purposes[^c-28d6eb04687dba229fcc15d7820a503c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person within subsection (3) may provide information to another person within that subsection, or a person within subsection (4), for the purpose of enabling or facilitating the exercise of any relevant function.

(2) A person within subsection (4) may provide information to a person within subsection (3) for the purpose of enabling or facilitating the exercise of any relevant function.

(3) The persons within this subsection are—

- (a)[^c-1b9fc2464ce6e3781ff5b44fee63e440] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the Secretary of State[^c-512daa5fba6b8665804eb7b5563c123b];
- (c)[^c-03a44b7e99343c396f945ed75354504d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c-b6bd2aedb306d8ce4891d29a9d0a28a5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^c-7c8246db9b5585cc999c816b3d138b3c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f) a person providing services to the Secretary of State[^c-3a0d14ace7cffb3d8943be367219d144]
- (fza)[^c-1c950635e9b581b9bca5fbf47423b6ef] a local authority in England exercising functions under Part 4 by virtue of regulations made under section 16(1) of the Cities and Local Government Devolution Act 2016;
- (fzb) a person providing services to a local authority in England in connection with functions exercisable by the local authority under Part 4 by virtue of regulations made under section 16(1) of the Cities and Local Government Devolution Act 2016;
- (fa)[^c-6520a0247cb103bf5135155435640172] a combined authority established under section 103 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009;
- (fb) a person providing services to a combined authority;
- (fc)[^c-0ee66738838f38b5f5281de9708fc188] a combined county authority established under section 9(1) of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023;
- (fd) a person providing services to a combined county authority;
- (fe)[^c-7176188d4ec194b1d57db1724d1d8126] the Mayor of London;
- (ff) a person providing services to the Mayor of London;
- (fg) the Greater London Authority;
- (fh) a person providing services to the Greater London Authority;
- (g)[^c-9259a0002f5e14d0cad458313a206276] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The persons within this subsection are—

- (a) a local authority[^c-539a148a152b679b0b2f5402f58a05e0] in England;
- (b) a person providing services to a local authority in England in connection with[^c-519930aedde7dab1a74367b3455763a8] a relevant function of the authority[^c-a948d2a89224cab96760e2c4fdc108d3] .

(5) In this section[^c-15d33f3727feeac2cba46bd444d13136], “**relevant function**” means—

- (a)[^c-e1262c9a917fdeb16c8084685c1f5cfb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) any function of the Secretary of State relating to education or training[^c-9bc8fe3565704fe5411b1ad75ed37341], [^c-b0afd1f9121cee1d88958a1626a222b3]...
- (ba)[^c-97b878ca69e16be232acb7a3e5cbde10] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) any education function of a local authority in England, [^c-1bd95f0625dfb228e967c53b6ec3cf1d]...
- (d)[^c-2bb336f2e2fdadbc2fa1f9e09a62dd23] any function of a combined authority under Part 4,
- (e) any function of a combined county authority under Part 4,
- (f) any function of the Mayor of London under Part 4,
- (g) any function of the Greater London Authority under Part 4, or

other than a function under this section.

(6)[^c-63654c2eb17d3f3bc090078b5f859384] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) Nothing in this section—

- (a) affects any power to disclose information that exists apart from this section, or
- (b) authorises the disclosure of any information in contravention of any provision made by or under any Act which prevents disclosure of the information.
