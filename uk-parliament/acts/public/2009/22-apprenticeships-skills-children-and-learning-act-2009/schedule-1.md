## Schedule 1 — Employee study and training: minor and consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 40

### Employment Rights Act 1996 (c. 18)

**1.**[^c-4a6000d5791818177e75ec830a7a1c5e] The Employment Rights Act 1996 is amended as follows.

**2.**[^c-024b7e58ef906cc07e5237911587cc44] In section 48 (right to present complaint of detriment to employment tribunal), in subsection (1) for “or 47E” substitute “ , 47E or 47F ”.

**3.**[^c-5bd39de19c29f12e7434824b52d0b81d] In section 105 (unfair dismissal: redundancy), after subsection (7BA) insert—

> (7BB) This subsection applies if the reason (or, if more than one, the principal reason) for which the employee was selected for dismissal was one of those specified in section 104E.

**4.**[^c-84f28d01c51a0cdc66a312f6c6f0957c] In section 108(3) (exceptions to one year qualifying period of continuous employment for claims for unfair dismissal), after paragraph (gj) insert—

> - (gk) section 104E applies,

.

**5.**[^c-787ab8394e378a39067a1ef6cd12649f] In section 194 (House of Lords staff), in subsection (2)(e) before “VII” insert “ 6A, ”.

**6.**[^c-d5de29be3b17711359d48adef5260e3a] In section 195 (House of Commons staff), in subsection (2)(e) before “VII” insert “ 6A, ”.

**7.**[^c-270d2b0a609809b50a0a62efe31461de] In section 199 (mariners)—

- (a) in subsection (2), after “47E,” insert “ 47F, ”;
- (b) in that subsection, before “VII” insert “ 6A, ”;
- (c) in subsection (8)(d), before “VII” insert “ 6A, ”.

**8.**[^c-0ddca8fd63e5ce5411acc6d98055cc7d] In section 225 (how to calculate a week's pay in relation to rights during employment) after subsection (4A) insert—

> (4B) Where the calculation is for the purposes of section 63J, the calculation date is the day on which the section 63D application was made.

**9.**[^c-bb56095d6d2042c9240feae99ada7e73] In section 227(1) (maximum amount of week's pay) before paragraph (za) insert—

> - (zza) an award of compensation under section 63J(1)(b),

.

**10.**[^c-31c9f0e22592885254244d1e0f93050d] In section 235(1) (other definitions) at the appropriate place insert—

> “**section 63D application**” has the meaning given by section 63D(2);

.

**11.**[^c-a65838da3fc64d8da571409ed7d5da63] In section 236(3) (orders and regulations subject to affirmative Parliamentary procedure), after “47C,” insert “ 63D, 63F(7), ”.

### Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 (c. 52)

**12.**[^c-3dba9447f8a09b13a1bc2ff6ca722820] The Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 is amended as follows.

**13.**[^c-9f4ffea4daaad9e2bc1d593a435e746f] In section 212A (arbitration scheme for unfair dismissal cases etc.), in subsection (1)—

- (a) before paragraph (za) insert—

  > - (zza) section 63F(4), (5) or (6) or 63I(1)(b) of the Employment Rights Act 1996 (study and training);

  ;
- (b) in paragraph (za) for “the Employment Rights Act 1996” substitute “ that Act ”.

**14.**[^c-a1c9b0db3befadcb0594f8564270296a] In section 237(1A)(a) (cases where employee may complain of unfair dismissal despite participation in unofficial industrial action)—

- (a) for “or 104D” substitute “ , 104D or 104E ”;
- (b) for “and pension scheme membership” substitute “ , pension scheme membership, and study and training ”.

**15.**[^c-4047940968beae28d40773646772d691] In section 238(2A)(a) (cases where employment tribunal to determine whether dismissal of an employee is unfair despite limitation in subsection (2) of that section)—

- (a) for “or 104D” substitute “ , 104D or 104E ”;
- (b) for “and pension scheme membership” substitute “ , pension scheme membership, and study and training ”.

### Employment Tribunals Act 1996 (c. 17)

**16.**[^c-0f5bf4b9c128ffacf2066cbd3f298e42] In section 18 of the Employment Tribunals Act 1996 (conciliation), in subsection (1)(d) after “28,” insert “ 63F(4), (5) or (6), 63I(1)(b), ”.
