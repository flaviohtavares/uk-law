# Apprenticeships, Skills, Children and Learning Act 2009, section 268

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2026-04-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 268. Extent

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This Act extends to England and Wales only, subject to subsections (2) to (4).

(2) Section 40, Part 1A, sections[^c-6fb0567737f842c8f646cbec951b683d] 68, 69, 107, 108 and this Part also extend to Scotland.

(3) Part 1A, sections[^c-bae5898b8fd03ed853fff1c047faf3ac] 68, 70, 107, 109, Part 7, sections 257 and 258 and this Part also extend to Northern Ireland.

(4) An amendment, repeal or revocation made by this Act has the same extent as the provision to which it relates.
