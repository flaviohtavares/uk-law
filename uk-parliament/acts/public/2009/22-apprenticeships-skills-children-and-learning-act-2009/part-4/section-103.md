(1) For the purpose of the exercise of the powers under section 100(1)(c), (d) or (e), the Secretary of State[^c-f439340d7718795c0df88690e03e8f8e] may—

- (a) carry out means tests;
- (b) arrange for other persons to carry out means tests.

(1A)[^c-fe1f863c93a7df7d43942b0b239e77f7] For the purpose of the exercise of the powers under section 100(1)(c), (d) or (e), the Greater London Authority, a combined authority or CCA, or a district council or county council that is a strategic authority, may—

- (a) carry out means tests;
- (b) arrange for other persons to carry out means tests.
