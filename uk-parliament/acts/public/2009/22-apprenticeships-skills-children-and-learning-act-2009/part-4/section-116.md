## 116. Persons subject to adult detention[^c-3e675ea15ebbe3d4b2443092d850eefa]

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State[^c-7802ad8d0ccd36c144798738d9d09fe0] must, in performing functions under this Part[^c-91996f0d7cb7e4b689eb409aa61f315d], have regard to the needs of persons subject to adult detention.
