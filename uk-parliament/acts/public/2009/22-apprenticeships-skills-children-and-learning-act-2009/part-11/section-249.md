*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-b5aa4ebd216d3a35825394ca8b7d01e3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-b5aa4ebd216d3a35825394ca8b7d01e3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-8f45d151ac61ae5519816e50e2abad41] After paragraph 3 of Schedule 1 to the Education Act 1996 (c. 56) (short stay schools: further provision) insert—

> **3A.**
>
> Regulations may also—
>
> - (a) require a local authority[^c-539a148a152b679b0b2f5402f58a05e0] in England to obtain the consent of the Secretary of State, in specified circumstances, to the closure of a short stay school;
> - (b) confer a power on the Secretary of State to give directions to a local authority in England about the exercise of—
>   - (i) their functions under section 19;
>   - (ii) their functions under any enactment applied to short stay schools (with or without modifications) by regulations under paragraph 3;
>   - (iii) any other function connected with short stay schools;
> - (c) require a local authority to comply with such directions.
