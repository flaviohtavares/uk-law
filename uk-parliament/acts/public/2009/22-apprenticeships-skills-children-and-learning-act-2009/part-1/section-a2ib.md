## A2IB. Availability of approved technical education qualifications outside England[^c-cd630c10bf0c97bf07c0889b2bbb1608][^c-bd21d3a6f3ee27e142e1ef9106f67af7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Secretary of State[^c-f54c3f597d2119a9871cfed72e7d34d2] may provide advice and assistance, or take other steps that the Secretary of State[^c-d965ab802f4b9fedf30f81d3231fdbd8] considers appropriate, for the purpose of enabling approved technical education qualifications to be made available to be obtained by persons outside England.
