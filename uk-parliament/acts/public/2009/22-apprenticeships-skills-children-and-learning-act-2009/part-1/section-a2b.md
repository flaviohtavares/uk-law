## A2B. Evaluation of quality of apprenticeship assessments[^c-8df6c6e0f4ffa1f30a29b88180f40b9d][^c-bd21d3a6f3ee27e142e1ef9106f67af7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State[^c-1c1cd09d86234cc517d90c44e4ef1556] must secure that evaluations are carried out of the quality of apprenticeship assessments provided by persons in relation to assessment plans published under section A2.

(2) “**Apprenticeship assessment**” means the assessment of a person's attainment of the outcomes set out in the standard to which the apprenticeship[^c-6551accb44aa96c54b9a80b2f82292f1] assessment plan relates.

(3) For the purposes of subsection (1) the Secretary of State[^c-aee27bd1d2f9d598f829232b6552fa44] may carry out evaluations or[^c-cebde50436b1fa1494c0441c7079c836] approve or make arrangements for other persons to carry out evaluations.
