## A2IA. Transfer of copyright relating to technical education qualifications[^c-3b279e0dd6d1e6d50fe0abee68cd3875][^c-bd21d3a6f3ee27e142e1ef9106f67af7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This section applies where a technical education qualification is approved under section A2D3[^c-0e448dcb4a050ba236fc64a6889aa41c].

(2) The right or interest in any copyright in a relevant course document is, by virtue of this section, transferred from the person to the Secretary of State[^c-095eae111680154f9e36ab5fa6232bab] at the time the approval is given.

(3) The Secretary of State may assign to another person, or grant a licence to another person in respect of, any right or interest transferred to the Institute by virtue of this section.

(4) In this section “**relevant course document**” means a document in relation to which the Secretary of State has made a determination under section A2D3(4)(a)[^c-fce8e94cc662730c37c7d1c80f13d982].
