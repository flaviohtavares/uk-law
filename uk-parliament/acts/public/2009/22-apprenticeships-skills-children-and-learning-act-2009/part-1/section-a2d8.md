## A2D8. Reviews of approval of technical education qualifications[^c-b241be60524f651d63e714a395508278]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State[^c-071e49ffa1da45e64a1ba1b55e95d8bd] must maintain arrangements for reviewing approved technical education qualifications [^c-9eb72f6ce5d9702399468f3533858bad]... with a view to determining, for each qualification, whether—

- (a) it should continue to be approved,
- (b) it should be revised, or
- (c) approval should be withdrawn.
