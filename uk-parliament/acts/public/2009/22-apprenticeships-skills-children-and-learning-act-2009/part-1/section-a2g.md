## A2G. Examinations by independent third parties[^c-8df6c6e0f4ffa1f30a29b88180f40b9d][^c-bd21d3a6f3ee27e142e1ef9106f67af7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-e7c0bfa500d931d38396382f2a3fd4da] The Secretary of State may at any time make arrangements for an independent third party to carry out an examination of—

- (a) a standard, or
- (b) an apprenticeship assessment plan.

(3) Where an examination of a standard or apprenticeship[^c-d6a716f59f859865214fd8a9b0c41753] assessment plan is carried out under this section, the Secretary of State[^c-a58c619c62edc8fb8e83691ec5a78d2d] must take account of the finding of the examination in exercising the Secretary of State’s[^c-76cbc38af508297397c4fa99d4ad785c] functions in relation to the standard or apprenticeship plan under this Chapter.

(4)[^c-97ab5a942f851b9b71f6dd1c35f5585c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
