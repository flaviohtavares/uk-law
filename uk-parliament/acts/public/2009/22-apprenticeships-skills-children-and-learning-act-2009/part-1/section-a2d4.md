## A2D4. Additional steps towards occupational competence[^c-1a12eb64bb96e2665ff25cf8f9db3f93][^c-241e6620f23257dccd5326b438436cbc]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Where there is a[^c-d8011f8a3ee7d46ec981451a13694b63] technical education qualification that is approved under section A2D3[^c-30e5f1a64cc187033875f469751cbf5b] in respect of one or more occupations, the Secretary of State[^c-007716037d24f0e49b514ddfd8d4dd67] may from time to time determine other steps that the Secretary of State[^c-a4a6a6f514568bf39b3d1197fed3e919] considers it would be appropriate for a person to take in order to progress towards being a person who is competent to work in those occupations.

(2) Steps determined under this section may include the obtaining of other qualifications.

(3) The Secretary of State may make a further determination under this section.

(4) The Secretary of State must publish a statement of any steps the Secretary of State[^c-58b48e9bb8a4737e087e4832cf80a4fd] determines under this section.

(5) In this Chapter steps that the Secretary of State determines under this section are referred to as “**approved steps towards occupational competence**”.
