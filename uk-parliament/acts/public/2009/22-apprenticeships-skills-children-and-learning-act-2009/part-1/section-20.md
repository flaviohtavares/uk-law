## 20. Recognised Welsh frameworks: notification and publication[^key-dcfd5d1a70666b62242c2a3a9d67a4b2][^key-86e3546cc5697f54eb7036d249310bac]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) On issuing an apprenticeship framework under section 19(1), the Welsh issuing authority must—

- (a) publish the framework;
- (b) if the issuing authority is not the Welsh Ministers,[^key-bee00858f976bc36314331de5e497f4f] notify the Welsh Ministers of the issue of the framework.

(2) A notice given under subsection (1)(b) must be accompanied by a copy of the framework.

(3) A Welsh issuing authority which[^key-e725eb18b5da5a740549d7b64ed0ebbc] withdraws recognition of an apprenticeship framework under section 19(2) must—

- (a) publish a notice stating that recognition of the framework has been withdrawn;
- (b) in the case where the issuing authority are not the Welsh Ministers,[^key-6f967d5e52d4fa1764907230503e6a8a] notify the Welsh Ministers of the withdrawal.

(4) Where this section imposes a duty on a person to publish a framework or notice, the publication may be in such manner as the person thinks fit.
