## 54. Transport policy statements for persons of sixth form age: consultation[^key-fb85c0305070ada844b4251ac0c94b0e]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 509AB(6) of the Education Act 1996 (people to be consulted when preparing transport policy statements for persons of sixth form age in England), in paragraph (c), before “and” insert—

> - (ca) persons in the local authority[^key-539a148a152b679b0b2f5402f58a05e0]'s area who will be of sixth form age when the statement has effect, and their parents,

.
