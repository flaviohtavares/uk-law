## 53. Provision of transport etc for persons of sixth form age: duty to have regard to section 15ZA duty[^key-d6c482122f08c2a55a23393b7298219f]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 509AB(3) of the Education Act 1996 (c. 56) (provision of transport etc for persons of sixth form age in England: matters to which local authorities[^key-062d27e760c356996de33ee6a2c9d7a4] must have regard) after paragraph (b) insert—

> - (ba) what they are required to do under section 15ZA(1) in relation to persons of sixth form age,

.
