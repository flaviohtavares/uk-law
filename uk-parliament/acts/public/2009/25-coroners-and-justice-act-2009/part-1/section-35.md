## 35. Chief Coroner and Deputy Chief Coroners[^c-66e24c0ac2027a17b307698d8b391226]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Schedule 8 makes provision about the appointment etc of the Chief Coroner and Deputy Chief Coroners.

(2) The Lord Chief Justice may nominate a judicial office holder (as defined in section 109(4) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (c. 4)) to exercise any of the functions of the Lord Chief Justice under Schedule 8.
