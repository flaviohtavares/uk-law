## 111. Effect of admission of video recording[^c-f9ac515eefad88ded1415cf11d1c169b]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 138 of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (c. 44) (video evidence: further provisions), omit subsection (1) (no evidence in chief on matter dealt with adequately in recorded account).
