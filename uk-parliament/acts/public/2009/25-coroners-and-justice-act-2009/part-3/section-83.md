# Coroners and Justice Act 2009, section 83

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-03-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 83. Review[^c-7eacaecd7f5a61bb5df8a36fa31f9c50]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State must review the operation of this Chapter in England and Wales[^c-eea2093c800671c5fc8d8a70a6c63cc7] and prepare a report of that review.

(2) The Secretary of State must lay a copy of the report before Parliament before the end of the period of 2 years beginning with the day on which section 77 comes into force.

(3)[^c-b5c9403ae0164393199c45c2011d9718][^c-a1483d256c3bed2ed3819e5a846e6a92] The Department of Justice in Northern Ireland must review the operation of this Chapter in Northern Ireland and prepare a report of that review.

(4)[^c-b5c9403ae0164393199c45c2011d9718] The Department of Justice must lay a copy of the report before the Northern Ireland Assembly before the end of the period of 2 years beginning with the day on which section 77 comes into force.

(5)[^c-b5c9403ae0164393199c45c2011d9718] Section 41(3) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 applies for the purposes of subsection (4) in relation to the laying of a copy of a report as it applies in relation to the laying of a statutory document under an enactment.
