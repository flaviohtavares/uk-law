## 134. Entrenchment of Lord Chancellor's functions[^c-5ef81b11c3544b81913c66f51c699f23]

*Extent: E+W*

In Schedule 7 to the Constitutional Reform Act 2005 (c. 4) (protected functions of the Lord Chancellor), in Part A of paragraph 4—

- (a) for the entry for the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (c. 44) substitute—

  > *Criminal Justice Act 2003 (c. 44)*
  >
  > Section 174(4)
  >
  > Section 269(6) and (7)

  , and
- (b) after the entry for the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 (c. 15) insert—

  > *Coroners and Justice Act 2009 (c. 25)*
  >
  > Section 119(1) and (2)
  >
  > Section 120(6)
  >
  > Section 122(4)
  >
  > Section 124(1)
  >
  > Section 131(2)
  >
  > Section 132(1), (4) and (6)
  >
  > Section 133
  >
  > Schedule 15
