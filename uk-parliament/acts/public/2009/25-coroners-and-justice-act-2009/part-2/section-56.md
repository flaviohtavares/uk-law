# Coroners and Justice Act 2009, section 56

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2026-03-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2009/25
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Abolition of common law defence of provocation

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1)[^c-457579ba401e2c3a3f78e1f49c4f322b][^c-729b2a711b5b258c4d341756fcd66380] The common law defence of provocation is abolished and replaced by sections 54 and 55.

(2) Accordingly, the following provisions cease to have effect—

- (a)[^c-703829001a4479db6a78af9981aa9103] section 3 of the Homicide Act 1957 (c. 11) (questions of provocation to be left to the jury);
- (b)[^c-729b2a711b5b258c4d341756fcd66380] section 7 of the Criminal Justice Act (Northern Ireland) 1966 (c. 20) (questions of provocation to be left to the jury).
