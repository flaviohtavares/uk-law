# Welfare Reform Act 2009, section 30

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 30. Good cause for failure to comply with regulations etc.

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) In Schedule 1 to the Jobseekers Act 1995 (supplementary provisions), after paragraph 14A insert—

> **14B. Good or just cause for acts or omissions**
>
> (1) This paragraph applies to any regulations made under this Act that prescribe matters to be taken into account in determining whether a person has good cause or just cause for any act or omission (including any failure to comply with the regulations).
>
> (2) The provision made by the regulations prescribing those matters must include provision relating to—
>
> - (a) the person's physical or mental health or condition;
> - (b) the availability of childcare.

(2) In Schedule 2 to the Welfare Reform Act 2007 (c. 5) (employment and support allowance: supplementary provisions), after paragraph 10 insert—

> **10A. Good cause for failure to comply with certain regulations**
>
> (1) This paragraph applies to any regulations made under section 11, 12 or 13 that prescribe matters to be taken into account in determining whether a person has good cause for any failure to comply with the regulations.
>
> (2) The provision made by the regulations prescribing those matters must include provision relating to—
>
> - (a) the person's physical or mental health or condition;
> - (b) the availability of childcare.
