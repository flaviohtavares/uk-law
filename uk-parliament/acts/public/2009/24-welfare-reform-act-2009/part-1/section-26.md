> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 26. Repeal of sections 62 to 66 of the Child Support, Pensions and Social Security Act 2000[^c-bed9e55d73f24d8e133c8fc217994b32]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In the Child Support, Pensions and Social Security Act 2000 (c. 19), omit sections 62 to 66 (loss of benefit for breach of community order).
