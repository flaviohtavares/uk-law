## 6. General betting duty

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 1 to this Act (which makes provision about general betting duty) has effect.

(2)[^c12449861] This section shall come into force in accordance with such provision as the Commissioners of Customs and Excise may make by order made by statutory instrument.
