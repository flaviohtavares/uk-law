# International Criminal Court Act 2001, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-01-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/17

## 35. Orders for exhumation

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Proceedings before the ICC in respect of an ICC crime are criminal proceedings for the purposes of paragraph 6 of Schedule 5 to the Coroners and Justice Act 2009[^c-081e448dd6a66866c746d72f97a08bbc] or section 11 of the Coroners Act (Northern Ireland) 1959 (c.15(N.I.)) (power of coroner to order exhumation).
