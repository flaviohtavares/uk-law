International Criminal Court Act 2001

2001 c. 17

An Act to give effect to the Statute of the International Criminal Court; to provide for offences under the law of England and Wales and Northern Ireland corresponding to offences within the jurisdiction of that Court; and for connected purposes.

\[11th May 2001\]

Be it enacted by the Queen’s most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—[^c-eb58b2a658624e1912354ebb46306895][^c-6759cab840c4ae2a1d91f3e3d582fed0][^c-0a766dc383f66b6d977c8f8b85a5278a]
