# International Criminal Court Act 2001, section 69

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2024-01-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/17

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. References to acts to include omissions, etc

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

In this Part “**act**”, except where the context otherwise requires, includes an omission, and references to conduct have a corresponding meaning.
