## 313. Meaning of “the residue of qualifying expenditure”[^c-f2a0bd7f41e825092b2dd81790951dcc][^c-c5bbfb77c8756f47440cfe730130f778][^c-9110db95a01c0c29a5bddba26486e553][^c-f2a0bd7f41e825092b2dd81790951dcc][^c-628a5f5fa45465234e045e67079bed4f][^c-3507198e4e311b901b0c302c85d4f748]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The residue of qualifying expenditure is the qualifying expenditure that has not yet been written off in accordance with Chapter 8.
