# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 455

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 455. Excluded expenditure

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Expenditure on the acquisition of know-how is not qualifying expenditure to the extent that it is otherwise deducted for tax purposes.

(2) Expenditure on the acquisition of know-how is not qualifying expenditure if—

- (a) the buyer is a body of persons over whom the seller has control,
- (b) the seller is a body of persons over whom the buyer has control, or
- (c) the buyer and the seller are both bodies of persons and another person has control over both of them.

(3) In subsection (2) “**body of persons**” includes a partnership.

(4) Expenditure on the acquisition of know-how is not qualifying expenditure if it is treated as a payment for goodwill under section 194(3) of[^c-e2eafc49be3447d0d5c2e7f08af16390] ITTOIA 2005 or under section 178(3) of CTA 2009[^c-12ddca6ce0ce60002647fc5da8ba472e] (consideration for know-how on disposal of trade to be treated as payment for goodwill, unless parties otherwise elect etc.).
