# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 387

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 387. Overall limit on balancing charge[^key-9f70844198f57536148e142a2f693811]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The amount of a balancing charge made on a person in respect of any qualifying expenditure must not exceed the total allowances made under this Part to the person in respect of the expenditure for chargeable periods ending before that in which the balancing event occurs.
