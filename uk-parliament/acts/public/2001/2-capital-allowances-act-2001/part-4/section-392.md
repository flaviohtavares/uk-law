# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 392

## 392. UK property businesses[^c-431d30933e9d163e8f747ca5fdaf763f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if a person who is entitled or liable to an allowance or charge for a chargeable period was not carrying on a trade in that period.

(2) If the person was carrying on a[^c-893b9cfb0eff92d85b1a5129a06af0f3] UK property business [^c-896d66e4999946432d94d75754abf0ac]... at any time in that period, the allowance or charge is to be given effect in calculating the profits of that business, by treating—

- (a) the allowance as an expense of that business, and
- (b) the charge as a receipt of that business.

(2A)[^c-18c4c371c79d00c28205d76c2e8a60b1] If the person[^c-80ad289ce9c73fa59142c67e9e83ec8d]... was not carrying on a UK property business at any time in that period, the allowance or charge is to be given effect by treating the person as having carried on[^c-0c7b1bb3c8f5bf609141f8f939661312] such a business in that period and as if—

- (a) the allowance were an expense of that business, and
- (b) the charge were a receipt of that business.
