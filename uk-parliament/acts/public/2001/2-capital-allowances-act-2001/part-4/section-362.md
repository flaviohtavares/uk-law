## 362. Meaning of “husbandry”[^key-9f70844198f57536148e142a2f693811]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part “**husbandry**” includes—

- (a) any method of intensive rearing of livestock or fish on a commercial basis for the production of food for human consumption, and
- (b) the cultivation of short rotation coppice.

(2) “**Short rotation coppice**” has the meaning given by section 1125(6) of CTA 2010 (meaning for corporation[^key-b88b6edff38eca20740b3fa0894d6295] tax purposes: tree species planted at high density where stems harvested at intervals of less than 10 years).
