# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 378

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2

## 378. Allowance limited to residue of qualifying expenditure[^c-9f70844198f57536148e142a2f693811]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The amount of a writing-down allowance for a chargeable period is limited to the residue of qualifying expenditure immediately before it is made or would, apart from this section, be made.

(2) The residue of qualifying expenditure is calculated in accordance with section 386.
