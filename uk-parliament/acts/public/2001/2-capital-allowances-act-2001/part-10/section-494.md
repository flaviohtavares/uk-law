# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 494

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 494. Introduction

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter identifies, in a case where a person has incurred expenditure on the construction of a building which is to be or include a qualifying dwelling-house—

- (a) the relevant interest in the building, and
- (b) the relevant interest in a dwelling-house comprised in the building.
