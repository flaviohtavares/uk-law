# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 529

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 529. Giving effect to allowances and charges

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a person who is entitled or liable to an allowance or charge for a chargeable period was carrying on a[^c-3e3c3c1e45d8218f5094b58fc333d963] UK property business[^c-1c3477f28cc8c1e85970ce4f1f02a6f3]... at any time in that period, the allowance or charge is to be given effect in calculating the profits of that business, by treating—

- (a) the allowance as an expense of that business, and
- (b) the charge as a receipt of that business.

(1A)[^c-fd3ca7ea9aa5416c2498c37b53211d75] If the person entitled or liable to an allowance or charge for a chargeable period[^c-845d51215b65422db166af7c1dbe9f0a]... was not carrying on a UK property business at any time in that period, the allowance or charge is to be given effect by treating the person as having carried on[^c-540920ebe470e7bc43ca3aaaffd60466] such a business in that period and as if—

- (a) the allowance were an expense of that business, and
- (b) the charge were a receipt of that business.
