# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 500

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2

## 500. The relevant interest in the dwelling-house

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The relevant interest in a dwelling-house comprised in a building is the relevant interest in the building, to the extent that it subsists in the dwelling-house.
