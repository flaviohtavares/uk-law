# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 34A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34A. Expenditure on plant or machinery for long funding leasing not qualifying expenditure[^key-99fcf7a96214f78746665c591bb1ea99]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Expenditure is not qualifying expenditure if it is incurred on the provision of plant or machinery for leasing under a long funding lease (see Chapter 6A).
