# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 248

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 248. ...UK property businesses[^c-aa62985035873ed73f713e8d0d03ef08][^c-cadbb395f14f0350999870ddc1be80be][^c-603626374eae6c2eddb1fc8e689a87c8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the qualifying activity of a person who is entitled or liable to an allowance or charge for a chargeable period is a[^c-1155e19163a89ff1b0a5e08e24ab4938] UK[^c-4c09a0a1f0f0b90b16678dc8da357224] property[^c-2facae28ccaf05f054b9c47e7c232924] business, the allowance or charge is to be given effect in calculating the profits of that business, by treating—

- (a) the allowance as an expense of that business, and
- (b) the charge as a receipt of that business.
