# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 63

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Cases in which disposal value is nil

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a person disposes of plant or machinery by way of gift in circumstances such that there is a charge to tax under ITEPA[^c-3b459038fa8d27c972ccd05e2f4be353] 2003, the disposal value of the plant or machinery is nil.

(2)[^c1896196] If a person carrying on a relevant qualifying activity makes a gift of plant or machinery used in the course of the activity—

- (a) to a charitable trust[^c-87c9fbd008cbf2772bb10e18bc9dcf0e] [^c-c27120aa190404e42e6b329b2fe88388]...,
- (aa)[^c-a61cb3b75f7dfa94ffda79774c56f96b] to a charitable company [^c-66665668828084f487debe997899ba9c]...,
- (ab) to a registered club within the meaning of Chapter 9 of Part 13 of CTA 2010 (community amateur sports clubs),
- (b) to a body listed in section 468 of CTA 2010[^c-46559e551fbe453aad8a22bcc0b516e6] (various heritage bodies and museums), or
- (c) for the purposes of a designated educational establishment within the meaning of section 110 of[^c-dc02973a1dd0263f64ab8cd8cbb02510] ITTOIA 2005 or section 106 of CTA 2009[^c-34ce6e3bbe8cbb31b4d42fd668003f19] (gifts to educational establishments),

the disposal value of the plant or machinery is nil.

(3) In subsection (2) “**relevant qualifying activity**” means a qualifying activity consisting of—

- (a) a trade,
- (b) a[^c-0bfae1eb7ea4a8465f3ff93d83b6911e] UK[^c-14d519eaaafa8012091a20865fb93ed4] property[^c-a97f867434cd91cc9bcb27538dd2a2c6] business,
- (c)[^c-e4d80e7da6397ef44853a459b57d29c5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) an [^c-54dd5cd145a4f8f8aab9bcb198a4bba5]... overseas[^c-f3ace2506f558bebbc80ea4fa09b77d2] property business, [^c-ea69fa99ef8e09c46d35f6fd0687bbc1]...
- (da) [^c-e6cfa8ac88ea4731da0d433e74b77d74]... or
- (e) a profession or vocation.

(4) Subsection (2) —[^c-a6e0679d626e287fb38b4d4078f09f55]

- (a) needs to be read with section 109 of[^c-36420893ab1559010f08fbc0fbc651ea] ITTOIA 2005 and section 108 of CTA 2009[^c-ec22e6d6b5d0e4852180c9e508cd2fe0] (which provide for a charge to tax if subsection (2) applies in circumstances in which the donor or a connected person receives a benefit attributable to the gift), and[^c-ef8e2fe3b6e0c9d655b8ba0dc29f666d]
- (b) is subject to sections 809ZM and 809ZMB[^c-3ccce61df2bab983ae90da73db6ef045] of ITA 2007 and sections 939F and 939FB[^c-4bfa3272936f4d7b50f338cc53cf6b89] of CTA 2010 (removal of tax relief in respect of tainted charity donations etc).

(5) If expenditure is treated under section 27(2) (expenditure on thermal insulation, safety measures, etc.) as having been incurred on plant or machinery, the disposal value of the plant or machinery is nil.
