# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 84

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84. Cases in which short-life asset treatment is ruled out

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Treatment of plant or machinery as a short-life asset is ruled out in any of the cases listed in column 1 of the Table, unless an exception listed in column 2 applies.

Table

Short-life asset treatment

| *1. Short-life asset treatment ruled out* | *2. Exception (if any)* |
| --- | --- |
| 1\. The expenditure is treated as incurred for the purposes of a qualifying activity under—<br>- (a) section 13 (use for qualifying activity of plant or machinery provided for other purposes), or<br>- (aa) section 13A (use for other purposes of plant or machinery provided for long funding leasing), or[^c-82992c781f10013c7d971574c066992f]<br>- (b) section 14 (use for qualifying activity of plant or machinery which is a gift). |  |
| 2\. The plant or machinery is the subject of special leasing (as defined by section 19). |  |
| 3\. The plant or machinery is a car (as defined by section 268A[^c-02542b6711baa19d2d1cd500649d757d]). | The car is a hire car for a disabled person (as defined by section 268D).[^c-0dfac1e2dfb3d1a194eb09e099853bc9] |
| 4\. The expenditure is special rate expenditure (see Chapter 10A).[^c-6005147e986860b1827ae8b0b1b13704] | The expenditure is incurred on the provision of a car which is a hire car for a disabled person (as defined by section 268D)[^c-5c0705e02d3934e4d791c69a887fb073] |
| 5\. The plant or machinery is provided for leasing. | The plant or machinery is a car which is a hire car for a disabled person (as defined by section 268D)[^c-5fea13f2b0c59573fdbd964a0f2b3ab4].<br>The plant or machinery will be used within the designated period (as defined by section 106) for a qualifying purpose (as defined by sections 122 to 125). |
| 6\. Section 109 provides only a 10% writing-down allowance in respect of expenditure on the plant or machinery. |  |
| 7\. The plant or machinery is leased to two or more persons jointly in circumstances such that section 116 applies. |  |
| 8\. The plant or machinery is a ship. |  |
| 9\. The expenditure was incurred partly for the purposes of a qualifying activity and partly for other purposes (see Chapter 15). |  |
| 10\. The expenditure is required to be allocated to a single asset pool under section 211 (partial depreciation subsidy). |  |
