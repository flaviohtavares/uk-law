# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 250

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 250. ... Overseas property businesses[^c-cb94271eca3b209d08b8f3bfcf4703c7][^c-1399507517b80ce315311c25f46725f8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the qualifying activity of a person who is entitled or liable to an allowance or charge for a chargeable period is an [^c-1dd12aa8584f7ec42e3a7628588accef]... overseas[^c-1fa126942dbf2f9d2ee3f66ec21365d6] property business, the allowance or charge is to be given effect in calculating the profits of that business, by treating—

- (a) the allowance as an expense of that business, and
- (b) the charge as a receipt of that business.
