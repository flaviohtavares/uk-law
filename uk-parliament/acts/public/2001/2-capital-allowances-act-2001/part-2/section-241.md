# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 241

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2

## 241. No annual investment allowance or first-year allowance in respect of additional VAT liability[^key-1b416d05d0ddd6b58883f19d43bb1a0b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) one person (“**B**”) enters into a transaction with another person (“**S**”) which is a relevant transaction for the purposes of Chapter 17 (anti-avoidance), and
- (b) an annual investment allowance or[^key-d0546be2b575c75096a7469c73d0d50e] a first-year allowance in respect of B’s expenditure under the relevant transaction is prohibited by section 217(1)[^key-c47613436febbae7700ff7fbdc658d27]....

(2) No annual investment allowance or first-year allowance is[^key-8ac6bfe1cc63c493ba4899b7a30f7470] to be made in respect of any additional VAT liability incurred by B in respect of his expenditure under the relevant transaction.

(3) Any annual investment allowance or[^key-f6c3d96f969229d0a39990296087857e] first-year allowance which is prohibited by subsection (2), but which has already been made, is to be withdrawn.
