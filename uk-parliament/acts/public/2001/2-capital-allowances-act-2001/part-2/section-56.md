# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 56

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 56. Amount of allowances and charges

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The amount of the writing-down allowance to which a person is entitled for a chargeable period is 14%[^key-62f2653317df257251b5a56757870bd3] of the amount by which AQE exceeds TDR.

(1A)[^key-1de598151d62d4ddbe9c49f70a5d4000] But in relation to qualifying expenditure incurred wholly for the purposes of a ring fence trade in respect of which tax is chargeable under section 330(1) of CTA 2010[^key-44050f455763219865c96d9166e7826b] (supplementary charge in respect of ring fence trades), the amount of the writing-down allowance to which a person is entitled for a chargeable period is 25% of the amount by which AQE exceeds TDR.

(2) Subsections (1) and (1A) are[^key-15c7b693fe614c12c7654a1fd8b6711e] subject to—

- (za)[^key-0e7cb23b0298a609bde6cad5a73cfd14] section 56A (small main pools and special rate pools),
- (a)[^key-ede73a84ab2f7d7798b6486a49c31e02] section 104D (special rate expenditure: 6%[^key-b9aa3a655d9b0ee26983d85a29089491][^key-0e9eb8065e8a701132a0789ddeea2822] or 10%), and
- (b) section 109 (overseas leasing: 10%).

(3) If the chargeable period is more or less than a year, the amount is proportionately increased or reduced.

(4) If the qualifying activity has been carried on for part only of the chargeable period, the amount is proportionately reduced.

(5) A person claiming a writing-down allowance may require the allowance to be reduced to a specified amount.

(6) The amount of the balancing charge to which a person is liable for a chargeable period is the amount by which TDR exceeds AQE.

(7) The amount of the balancing allowance to which a person is entitled for the final chargeable period is the amount by which AQE exceeds TDR.
