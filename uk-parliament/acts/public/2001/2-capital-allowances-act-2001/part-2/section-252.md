# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 252

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 252. Mines, transport undertakings etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the qualifying activity of a person who is entitled or liable to an allowance or charge for a chargeable period is a concern listed in section 12(4) of[^key-98e8e03061ffd5862a626bca9c2c82dd] ITTOIA 2005 or section 39(4) of CTA 2009[^key-560e33240cd53b97ee351e0018fba4ed] (mines, transport undertakings etc.) the allowance or charge is to be given effect in calculating the profits of the concern under Chapter 2 of Part 2 of[^key-a061e1e5380365cdf7d59ec1028ee56c] ITTOIA 2005 or, as the case may be, under Case I of Schedule D, by treating—

- (a) the allowance as an expense of the concern, and
- (b) the charge as a receipt of the concern.
