# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 261

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 261. Special leasing: long-term business[^c-a06c4aa16ec85448648d33dfda47524e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the case of a company which is carrying on any long-term business[^c-2e666ff358efbb160876f3685b154b02]—

- (a) subsections (3) to (6) of section 260, and
- (b) sections 99 and 113 of CTA 2010[^c-053743dcb7ba4c609992c37ce31401da] (group relief),

do not apply in relation to an allowance to which the company is entitled under section 19 (special leasing of plant or machinery).
