## 159. Meaning of “mineral extraction trade” etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter—

- “**mineral extraction trade**”, and
- “**mineral exploration and access**”

have the same meaning as in Part 5 (mineral extraction allowances).
