## 39. First-year allowances available for certain types of qualifying expenditure only

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A first-year allowance is not available unless the qualifying expenditure is first-year qualifying expenditure under any of the following provisions[^key-7393162b8c3b9a1d10d34dba89b528a1]—
