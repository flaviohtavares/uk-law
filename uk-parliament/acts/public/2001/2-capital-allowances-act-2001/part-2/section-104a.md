# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 104A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2

## 104A. Special rate expenditure[^c-c44cf10a55ef184e8b0ac49262ed6a26]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) “**Special rate expenditure**” means—

- (a) expenditure incurred on or after the first[^c-dcb46b8f9fd53a06db76222b7b2e7604] relevant date to which section 28 (thermal insulation) applies,
- (b) expenditure incurred on or after that date to which section 33A (integral features) applies,
- (c) long-life asset expenditure (within the meaning of Chapter 10) incurred on or after that date, [^c-6f24fab235eaaaa39d476495a0eaeacf]...
- (d) long-life asset expenditure (within the meaning of that Chapter) incurred before that date but allocated to a pool in a chargeable period beginning on or after that date,[^c-88d76fc2f806e47f00df33d7cba1d4b1] [^c-14429afd68f3d821087182b87748ce67]...
- (e) expenditure incurred on or after the second relevant date on the provision of a car that is not a main rate car [^M_F_a5975f49-5870-461f-ffee-cff68e28b6fe]...[^c-382a6a8e5930b5520ee93e1b27472c94]
- (f) expenditure incurred on or after 1 April 2010 on the provision of cushion gas (within the meaning given by section 70J(7)), and[^c-1223749fa3b5bb8ec94aa2d694090c6d]
- (g) expenditure incurred on or after the third relevant date on the provision of solar panels.

(2) The first[^c-010d66ca5e7db64d756c1a4d7fb6a319] relevant date is—

- (a) for corporation tax purposes, 1 April 2008, and
- (b) for income tax purposes, 6 April 2008.

(3)[^c-3927369579afae5f2a8b17decb47c57d] The second relevant date is—

- (a) for corporation tax purposes, 1 April 2009, and
- (b) for income tax purposes, 6 April 2009.

(3A)[^M_F_3b495e06-e14b-4aff-959a-c2e61087af26] The third relevant date is—

- (a) for corporation tax purposes, 1 April 2012, and
- (b) for income tax purposes, 6 April 2012.

(4) In this section—

- “**car**” has the meaning given in section 268A;
- “**main rate car**” has the meaning given in section 104AA.
