# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 247

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 247. Trades

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-2c15482cae255426637205eddad0dbe4] If the qualifying activity of a person who is entitled or liable to an allowance or charge for a chargeable period is a trade, the allowance or charge is to be given effect in calculating the profits of that person’s trade, by treating—

- (a) the allowance as an expense of the trade, and
- (b) the charge as a receipt of the trade.

(1A)[^key-48fbe4ca4673335bcdbf77e41f60e5ca] Subsection (1) is subject to section 6E (giving effect to allowances and charges: NI rate activity cases).

(2)[^key-6175ba5a668b757585f23f7fd6aa1733] See Chapter 16A for provision restricting in certain circumstances the ways in which effect may be given to an allowance by virtue of subsection (1)(a).
