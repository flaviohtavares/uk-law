# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 217

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 217. No annual investment allowance or first-year allowance for B’s expenditure[^key-153181e9ae0104b6aae86b2d8f4d58dd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If this section applies as a result of section 214, 215 or 216, no annual investment allowance or first-year allowance is[^key-67aafd1086e78fbe9625dc0c3501bf5e] to be made in respect of B’s expenditure under the relevant transaction.

(2) Any annual investment allowance or[^key-ee768ae63422c4c3b0a9cf2edebee2be] first-year allowance which is prohibited by subsection (1), but which has already been made, is to be withdrawn.

(3)[^key-2380cf4ab63a0bb2b6c806ae3afbc46e] This section does not apply if plant or machinery is the subject of a sale and finance leaseback (as defined in section 221).
