# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 158

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2

## 158. Members of same group

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Chapter two companies are members of the same group at any time if they would be treated as members of the same group of companies at that time for the purposes of Part 5 of CTA 2010[^c-2d73cd5ff1d7d28953c3444170cf8deb] (group relief).
