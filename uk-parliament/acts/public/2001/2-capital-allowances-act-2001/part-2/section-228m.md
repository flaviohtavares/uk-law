# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 228M

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 228M. Other definitions for the purposes of s.228K[^key-a8de3bbcd99736e9202c62ea14f5c99d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies for the purposes of section 228K.

(2) “Business of leasing plant or machinery”—

- (a) has the same meaning as in Chapter 3 of Part 9 of CTA 2010[^key-b02c1662f460f7737e08dee6ed5aab7e] (if the business is carried on otherwise than in partnership), or
- (b) has the same meaning as in Chapter 4 of that Part[^key-1fe492d5320d35a128181c1bcdf8dd25] (if the business is carried on in partnership).

(3) “**Lease**” includes—

- (a) an underlease, sublease, tenancy or licence, and
- (b) an agreement for any of those things.

(4) “**Relevant plant or machinery**”, in relation to a business of leasing plant or machinery, means plant or machinery on whose provision expenditure is incurred wholly or partly for the purposes of the business.
