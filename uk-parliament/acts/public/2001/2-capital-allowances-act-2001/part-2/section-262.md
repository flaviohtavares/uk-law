# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 262

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 262. Employments and offices

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the qualifying activity of a person who is entitled or liable to an allowance or charge for a chargeable period is an employment or office, the allowance or charge is to be given effect, by treating—

- (a) the allowance as a deduction from the taxable earnings from[^key-e7a94b5c41740542cc394326e8f933f0] the employment or office, and
- (b) the charge as earnings[^key-9cb1841e7871662c243b02cdefd572cb] of the employment or office.
