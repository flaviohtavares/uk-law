# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2

## 18. Managing the investments of a company with investment business

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Part, managing the investments of a company with investment business consists of pursuing those purposes expenditure on which would be treated as expenses of management within section 1219 of CTA 2009[^key-a0a2b4475aa9da5f78017e07fd8bc764].

(2) In this Part “company with investment business” has the meaning given by section 1218B[^key-aa53555ad17e3e7a4a56d93ae2dbef6c] of CTA 2009.
