## 99. The monetary limit

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c1902104] The monetary limit in the case of a chargeable period of 12 months is £100,000.

(2)[^c1902105] If, in the case of an individual or partnership, the chargeable period is longer or shorter than 12 months, the monetary limit is the amount given by a proportional increase or reduction of £100,000.

(3)[^c1902106] If, in the case of a company, the chargeable period is shorter than 12 months, the monetary limit is the amount given by a proportional reduction of £100,000.

(4) If, in a chargeable period, a company has one or more associated companies[^c-f895fdbe051593ad7ac50919df778e72], the monetary limit for that period is—

[formula: see source](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2001/2)

where—

- L is the monetary limit applicable under subsection (1) or (3), and
- N is the number of associated[^c-f05576e9a7679ed3bb162ca21ba6cd59] companies.

(4A)[^c-40b5918e616e548578e259b8748d3e3c] The rules in Part 3A of CTA 2010 (see sections 18E to 18J) which apply for determining whether a company is another company's associated company in an accounting period for the purposes of section 18D of that Act apply for the purposes of subsection (4).
