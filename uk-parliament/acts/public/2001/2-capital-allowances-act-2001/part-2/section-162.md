# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 162

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 162. Ring fence trade a separate qualifying activity

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a person carries on a ring fence trade, it is a separate qualifying activity for the purposes of this Part.

(2) In this Chapter “**ring fence trade**” means activities which—

- (a) fall within the definition of “oil-related activities” in section 16(2) of[^key-0ca17d8d976b1f5d6bc107b8bc5c7208] ITTOIA 2005 or section 274 of CTA 2010[^key-fd69975620192e5bf4ab408890fabf9c], and
- (b) constitute a separate trade (whether as a result of section 16(1) of[^key-b416b1f9b15bdb51edce907cf8a1966d] ITTOIA 2005 or section 16(1) of ITTOIA 2005 or section 279 of CTA 2010[^key-00443bb2c0604d6cb4bab8f5284aed4a] or otherwise).
