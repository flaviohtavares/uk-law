# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 212C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 212C. When there is qualifying change in relation to C[^c-298a75262b587039f349fdea6d235f62]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There is a qualifying change in relation to C on the relevant day if one or more of conditions A to D is met.

(2) Condition A is that—

- (a) the principal company or companies of C at the beginning of the relevant day is not, or are not, the same as at the end of that day, or
- (b) there is no principal company of C at the beginning of the relevant day but there is one, or are more than one, at the end of the relevant day.

(3) Condition B is that—

- (a) any principal company of C is a consortium principal company (“CPC”), and
- (b) CPC's ownership proportion at the end of the relevant day is more than at the beginning of the relevant day.

(4) Condition C is that the relevant activity is a trade (within the meaning of this Part) and[^c-4e862d5dac851ff43f9c8eb800453336] on the relevant day—

- (a) C ceases to carry on the whole or part of the relevant activity[^c-d4fababf048b86034928c6c40410004b], and
- (b) it begins to be carried on in partnership by two or more companies,

in circumstances in which Chapter 1 of Part 22 of CTA 2010 (transfers of trade without change of ownership) applies in relation to the transfer of the relevant activity.

(5) Condition D is that—

- (a) the relevant activity[^c-b7d25480c2aa18ca4f10fd1609754636] is, at the beginning of the relevant day, carried on by C in partnership, and
- (b) C's relevant percentage share in the relevant activity at the end of the relevant day is less than at the beginning of the relevant day (or is nil).
