# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 61

## 61. Disposal events and disposal values[^c-60b87b3eecec6bc036b844083741e3ac][^c-9bb7757a77a954d166adf76c83bb46a2][^c-3b8e879fd9c9eef23b9a8070691774a9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person who has incurred qualifying expenditure is required to bring the disposal value of the plant or machinery into account for the chargeable period in which—

- (a) the person ceases to own the plant or machinery;
- (b) the person loses possession of the plant or machinery in circumstances where it is reasonable to assume that the loss is permanent;
- (c) the plant or machinery has been in use for mineral exploration and access and the person abandons it at the site where it was in use for that purpose;
- (d) the plant or machinery ceases to exist as such (as a result of destruction, dismantling or otherwise);
- (e) the plant or machinery begins to be used wholly or partly for purposes other than those of the qualifying activity;
- (ee)[^c-8318f2e182c9c0dccfd804ff01b12513] the plant or machinery begins to be leased under a long funding lease (see Chapter 6A);
- (f) the qualifying activity is permanently discontinued.

(2)[^c-4d0ac7afe0ce14db0a647a03ad37a917] The disposal value to be brought into account depends on the disposal event, as shown in the Table—

Table

Disposal values: general

| *1. Disposal event* | *2. Disposal value* |
| --- | --- |
| 1\. Sale of the plant or machinery, except in a case where item 2 or 2A[^c-15b50c96fde239e702096525cd563eaa] applies. | The net proceeds of the sale, together with—<br>- (a) any insurance money received in respect of the plant or machinery as a result of an event affecting the price obtainable on the sale, and<br>- (b) any other compensation of any description so received, so far as it consists of capital sums. |
| 2\. Sale of the plant or machinery where—<br>- (a) the sale is at less than market value,<br>- (b) there is no charge to tax under ITEPA[^c-bdd7ecd05dc8969d8636da99e4cfae2f] 2003, and<br>- (c) the condition in subsection (4) is met by the buyer. | The market value of the plant or machinery at the time of the sale. |
| 2A. Sale of the plant or machinery where—[^c-5cf56e99557cf7b21c23af2b92984a18]<br>- (a) the sale is at less than market value,<br>- (b) the condition in subsection (4A) is met by the seller, and<br>- (c) the condition in subsection (4B) is met by the buyer. | The market value of the plant or machinery at the time of the sale. |
| 3\. Demolition or destruction of the plant or machinery. | The net amount received for the remains of the plant or machinery, together with—<br>- (a) any insurance money received in respect of the demolition or destruction, and<br>- (b) any other compensation of any description so received, so far as it consists of capital sums. |
| 4\. Permanent loss of the plant or machinery otherwise than as a result of its demolition or destruction. | Any insurance money received in respect of the loss and, so far as it consists of capital sums, any other compensation of any description so received. |
| 5\. Abandonment of the plant or machinery which has been in use for mineral exploration and access at the site where it was in use for that purpose. | Any insurance money received in respect of the abandonment and, so far as it consists of capital sums, any other compensation of any description so received. |
| 5A. Commencement of the term of a long funding finance lease of the plant or machinery.[^c-3daa16c7cacc4d4863f3f82b2afd2b2f] | The greater of—<br>- (a) the market value of the plant or machinery at the commencement of the term of the lease, and<br>- (b) the qualifying lease payments. |
| 5B. Commencement of the term of a long funding operating lease of the plant or machinery.[^M_F_70591e6b-d33f-401e-c376-0200eda50d32] | An amount equal to the market value of the plant or machinery at the commencement of the term of the lease. |
| 6\. Permanent discontinuance of the qualifying activity followed by the occurrence of an event within any of items 1 to 5B[^c-7ff96792a2e985f4433fba14e4b940ec]. | The disposal value for the item in question. |
| 6A. Disposal event to which section 62A applies.[^c-58afba9de681706157b2a4429b491488] | The relevant transition value (see section 62A). |
| 7\. Any event not falling within any of items 1 to 6A[^c-9d5e61ab2a21c602223d8fd8cd4c2d4c]. | The market value of the plant or machinery at the time of the event. |

(3)[^c-4d0ac7afe0ce14db0a647a03ad37a917] The amounts referred to in column 2 of the Table are those received by the person required to bring the disposal value into account.

(4)[^c-4d0ac7afe0ce14db0a647a03ad37a917] The condition referred to in item 2 of the Table is met by the buyer if—

- (a) the buyer’s expenditure on the acquisition of the plant or machinery cannot be qualifying expenditure under this Part or Part 6 (research and development allowances), or
- (b) the buyer is a dual resident investing company which is connected with the seller.

(4A)[^c-de38ca45c71159846458287ff8b757d9] The condition referred to in paragraph (b) of item 2A in the Table is met by the seller if—

- (a) the seller is—
  - (i) a company, or
  - (ii) a partnership whose partners include one or more companies, and
- (b) before the sale the plant or machinery is used wholly or partly for the purposes of a qualifying activity that is not an NI rate activity.

(4B) The condition referred to in paragraph (c) of item 2A in the Table is met by the buyer if—

- (a) the buyer is an[^c-ce6662ee812ba9fb040f5a5ebfa84dc7] SME (Northern Ireland employer) company, a NIRE company or a Northern Ireland firm in the chargeable period of the buyer in which the plant or machinery is bought,
- (b) the buyer's expenditure on the acquisition of the plant or machinery is qualifying expenditure under this Part or Part 6 (research and development allowances), and
- (c) the plant or machinery is used by the buyer wholly or partly for the purposes of an NI rate activity.

(5) In this section “**mineral exploration and access**” has the same meaning as in Chapter 13 (provisions affecting the mining and oil industries) and Part 5 (mineral extraction allowances).

(5A)[^c-b502d04c3caa5de9c0396bb6acf6e87e] In item 5A of the Table “**qualifying lease payments**” means the minimum payments under the lease (including any initial payment), excluding the following—

- (a) so much of any payment as, under generally accepted accounting practice, falls (or would fall) to be treated as the gross return on investment in respect of the lease,
- (b) so much of any payment as represents charges for services, and
- (c) so much of any payment as represents qualifying UK or foreign tax (within the meaning of section 70YE) to be paid by the lessor.
