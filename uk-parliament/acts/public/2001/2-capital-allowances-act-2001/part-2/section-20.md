# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 20

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Employments and offices

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 15(1)(i) “**employment**” does not include an employment the performance of the duties of which is treated as the carrying on of a trade under section 15 of[^c-993b1502f58ad01b5f0ea5d733d6f8bc] ITTOIA 2005 (divers and diving supervisors in the North Sea etc.).

(2) Subsection (3) applies if the earnings[^c-5ab27247360c4f23535fbc97c7d33584] for any duties of an employment or office fall within section 22 or 26 of[^c-dc844aceb951b31f6cbff23f06e82f20] ITEPA 2003.

(3) This Part applies in relation to—

- (a) those earnings[^c-062fe5138c9def68ae08c0e85d614d88] , or
- (b) any other taxable earnings (as defined by section 10 of[^c-a159517b901c63f29ba5793e30b5af0a] ITEPA 2003) of the employment or office,

as if the performance of the duties did not belong to that employment or office.
