# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 156

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 156. Connected persons

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of the deferment rules a person (“**B**”) is connected with another person (“**A**”) at any time if, at that time—

- (a) B is connected (in the sense given in section 575[^c-f9039621dc3fad2edc2c38314add6de4]) with A,
- (b) B is carrying on a qualifying activity previously carried on by A and the condition in subsection (2) is met, or
- (c) B is connected (in the sense given in section 575[^c-fa472df2e39aab1a0d355b307ec8e734]) with a person who is carrying on a qualifying activity previously carried on by A and the condition in subsection (2) is met.

(2)[^c-8a9c6a75f447f344d2c47785b3c0a4f0] The condition is that the only changes in the persons carrying on the qualifying activity since A carried it on are changes—

- (a) which do not involve all of the persons carrying it on before the changes permanently ceasing to carry it on, or
- (b) in respect of which the qualifying activity is treated as continuing under section 948 of CTA 2010[^c-9314c126c5ecd18a98bf39987473fb60].

(3) If expenditure is incurred by a person who is not the shipowner, the persons connected with him at any time include any person connected with the shipowner at that time as a result of subsection (1).
