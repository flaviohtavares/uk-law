## 545. Investment assets

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if a company which is carrying on any long-term business[^key-94ab6a5e85ee8f5a01f63f68c956cc2f] holds an asset for purposes other than the management of that business.

(2) “**Investment asset**” means an asset that is within subsection (1).

(3)[^key-248d383c4877f1c5d49a2e09932c67e8] No allowance in respect of an investment asset is to be taken into account in calculating for corporation tax purposes the profits of any non-BLAGAB long-term business carried on by the company.
