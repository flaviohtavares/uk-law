# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 561

## 561. Transfer or division of UK business[^c-e53f7b725f027efe8ebea0aeae2e7d11]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-b38dac996743c038d3a5d0efd837c3ec] This section applies if and in so far as—

- (a) a qualifying company resident in one relevant state[^c-b6d09abae9e5e5e81568a5e547926152] (“the transferor”) transfers the whole or part of a business carried on by it in the United Kingdom to one or more qualifying companies resident in one or more other relevant states[^c-984e344419ffd6ac882805128dc8e73b] (“the transferee” or “the transferees”),
- (b) section 140A of TCGA 1992 (transfer of assets treated as no-gain no-loss disposal) applies in relation to the transfer, and
- (c) immediately after the transfer the transferee (or one or more of the transferees)—
  - (i) is resident in the United Kingdom, or
  - (ii) carries on in the United Kingdom through a permanent establishment a business which consists of, or includes, the business or part of the business transferred.

(2) If this section applies—

- (a) the transfer itself does not give rise to any allowances or charges under this Act, and
- (b) in relation to assets included in the transfer, anything done to or by the transferor[^c-7f7a15b9349921929f7ccbff79e4abfe] before the transfer is to be treated after the transfer as having been done to or by the transferee (or each transferee)[^c-26b218e57921dd7eda42f6e7beb86680].

(3) If, for the purposes of subsection (2)(b), expenditure falls to be apportioned between assets included in the transfer and other assets, the apportionment is to be made in a just and reasonable manner.

(4) In this section “ **qualifying company** ” means a body incorporated under the law of a relevant state[^c-2de63a436e686b28be207d37810c2899].

(4A)[^c-ab23b303b4f24e1bb5652b34851b15e4] In this section “relevant state” means the United Kingdom or a member State.

(5) If this section applies, section 948 of CTA 2010 (modified application of[^c-cbf6380388875c508e0a9ed694cf6c6c] CAA 2001 in relation to trade transfers without a change of ownership) does not apply.
