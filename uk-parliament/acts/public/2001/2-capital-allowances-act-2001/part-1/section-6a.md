# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 6A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2

## 6A. “NIRE company” and “SME (Northern Ireland employer) company”[^c-8affe371a4c0f1618fd4056236544418][^c-484f45d7dc7f815d7cf2e4b9d04288fe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act—

- “**NIRE company**” means a company that is a Northern Ireland company for the purposes of Part 8B of CTA 2010 by virtue of the SME (election) condition or[^c-87ca6ae16949367b2890a9fecfa8818d] the large company condition in section 357KA of that Act;
- “[^c-f7a792722fe44d07ccf970d67e9132a0]**SME (Northern Ireland employer) company**” means a company that is a Northern Ireland company for the purposes of Part 8B of CTA 2010 by virtue of the SME (Northern Ireland employer) condition[^c-9e9c956ad7b6f6771d4d82e4a150ad22] in section 357KA of that Act.
