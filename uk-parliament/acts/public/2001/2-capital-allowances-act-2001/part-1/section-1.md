# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2

## 1. Capital allowances

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act provides for allowances in respect of capital expenditure (and for charges in connection with those allowances).

(2) The allowances for which this Act provides are those under—

- (a) Part 2 (plant and machinery allowances);
- (aa)[^c-e6e0a95c4c51af0b368cf4d81f9d4f2e] Part 2A (structures and buildings allowances);
- (b)[^c-24dc17adcf81e92ee4c81f23cfc1e72f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (ba)[^c-aa5a7f6eaaa151918ae7b8b0cc1d006f] Part 3A (business premises renovation allowances)
- (c)[^c-5e62ab5df9b4b1f642edfb5367f83cea] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (ca)[^c-0f9bf016275ad13dc6168c02315321cf] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) Part 5 (mineral extraction allowances);
- (e) Part 6 (research and development allowances);
- (f) Part 7 (know-how allowances);
- (g) Part 8 (patent allowances);
- (h) Part 9 (dredging allowances);
- (i) Part 10 (assured tenancy allowances).

(3) This Act also provides for allowances in respect of contributions to expenditure incurred on plant or machinery[^c-e2c99cc497530d8ac816bbf18f55d736]... for the purposes of a mineral extraction trade or on dredging (see Part 11).

(4)[^c-84ac925b654001c8a5b8805b889970a3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
