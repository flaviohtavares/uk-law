# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 360Y

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2

## 360Y. Additional VAT rebates and writing off qualifying expenditure[^c-e55c9e0cbb84c78796990128c4d86b99]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If an additional VAT rebate is made in respect of qualifying expenditure, an amount equal to the rebate is written off at the time when the rebate accrues.
