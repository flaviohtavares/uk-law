# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 360K

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2

## 360K. Meaning of “ the residue of qualifying expenditure ”[^c-e55c9e0cbb84c78796990128c4d86b99]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The residue of qualifying expenditure is the qualifying expenditure that has not yet been written off in accordance with Chapter 9.
