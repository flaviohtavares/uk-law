# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 360W

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2

## 360W. Additional VAT liabilities and writing off initial allowances[^c-e55c9e0cbb84c78796990128c4d86b99]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If an initial allowance is made in respect of an additional VAT liability incurred after the qualifying business premises are first used or suitable for letting for business use, the amount of the allowance is written off at the time when the liability accrues.
