# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 397

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 397. Meaning of “mineral asset”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part “**mineral asset**” means—

- (a) any mineral deposits or land comprising mineral deposits, or
- (b) any interest in or right over such deposits or land.
