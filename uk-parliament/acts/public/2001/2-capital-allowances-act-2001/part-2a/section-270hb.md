# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 270HB

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 270HB. ... UK property businesses and ... overseas property businesses[^key-63b259cb6f7aef6fc5b59b4dde260ee2][^key-255e8157557ffc7249f3c468e397bed4][^key-255e8157557ffc7249f3c468e397bed4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the qualifying activity of a person who is entitled to an allowance for a chargeable period is—

- (a) a[^key-5c4cda66a4ffe03a29603b81846797ec] UK property business, or
- (b) an [^key-f3ecaabbe15154e2c3a5c7af122f33c4]... overseas property business,

the allowance is to be given effect in calculating the profits of that business by treating the allowance as an expense of that business.
