# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 270EA

## 270EA. Proportionate adjustment in certain cases[^c-63b259cb6f7aef6fc5b59b4dde260ee2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if a person is entitled to an allowance under section 270AA(2) for a chargeable period.

(2) If the chargeable period is more or less than one year, the allowance is proportionately increased or reduced.

(3) If—

- (a) the conditions in section 270AA(2)(a) and (b)(i)[^M_F_a3a32353-80eb-4100-904d-45b15e125bcf] are met on some, but not all, days during the chargeable period,
- (aa)[^M_F_e656cfce-3ee1-4057-a100-77da7c500c51] the period mentioned in section 270AA(2)(b)(ii) expires part way through the chargeable period, or
- (b) entitlement to the allowance ceases under section 270AA(4) on any day during the chargeable period,

the allowance is proportionately reduced.
