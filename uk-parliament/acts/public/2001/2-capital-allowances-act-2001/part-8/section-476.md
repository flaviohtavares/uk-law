# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 476

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 476. Disposal value of patent rights

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Chapter “**disposal receipt**” means a disposal value that a person is required to bring into account in accordance with—

- (a) this section, or
- (b) section 614BS of ITA 2007[^c-9619dce2b2e02c8e76594b300da1cd74] or section 918 of CTA 2010 (cases where expenditure taken into account under Part 2, 5 or 8 of this Act) or[^c-b76068c3311223dc792fa346caa707dd] any other enactment.

(2) A person is required to bring a disposal value into account for the chargeable period in which he sells the whole or a part of any patent rights on which he has incurred qualifying expenditure.

(3) Subject to section 477, the disposal value to be brought into account is the net proceeds of the sale, so far as they consist of capital sums.
