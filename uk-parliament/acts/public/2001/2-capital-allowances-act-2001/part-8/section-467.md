# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 467

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 467. Qualifying expenditure

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Expenditure is qualifying expenditure only if it is—

- (a) qualifying trade expenditure, or
- (b) qualifying non-trade expenditure.
