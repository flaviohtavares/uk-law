# Capital Allowances Act 2001, section 483

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (8).** Text as at 2026-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/2

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 483. Meaning of “income from patents”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Part a person’s “**income from patents**” means—

- (a) royalties or other sums paid in respect of the use of a patent,
- (b) balancing charges to which the person is liable under this Part, and
- (c) amounts on which tax is payable under section 587, 593 or 594 of[^key-33da6b31c39802a44e0e54f288ecbae9] ITTOIA 2005 or under section 912 or 918 of CTA 2009[^key-00d4fc3b182499df503650976bb62c10] (taxation of receipts from sale of patent rights).
