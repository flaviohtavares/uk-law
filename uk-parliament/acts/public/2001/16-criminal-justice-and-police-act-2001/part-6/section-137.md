# Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001, section 137

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (33).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/16

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 137. Repeals[^c8874521]

*Extent: E+W*

The enactments and instruments mentioned in Schedule 7 (which include spent provisions) are hereby repealed or (as the case may be) revoked to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
