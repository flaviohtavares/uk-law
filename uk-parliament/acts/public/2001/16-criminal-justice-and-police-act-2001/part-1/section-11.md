# Criminal Justice and Police Act 2001, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (33).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Interpretation of Chapter 1

*Extent: E+W*

In this Chapter—

- “[^c-0fc2bc03b70c59cd3adeb9bd39b70395]**approved educational course**” has the meaning given in section 2(4);
- “**chief officer of police**” includes the Chief Constable of the British Transport Police;
- “**defaulter**” has the meaning given in section 8(5);
- “[^c-b03c0d809b047b7a761f0baa26f64d8a]**educational course scheme**” has the meaning given in section 2(4);
- “**penalty notice**” has the meaning given in section 2(4);
- “[^c-7a7ef05b2f98caab1c7443472de1f67a]**penalty notice with an education option**” has the meaning given in section 2(4);
- “**penalty offence**” has the meaning given in section 1(1);
- “**registrable sum**” has the meaning given in section 8(4).
