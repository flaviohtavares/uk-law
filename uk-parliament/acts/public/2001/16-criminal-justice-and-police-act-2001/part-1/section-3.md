## 3. Amount of penalty and form of penalty notice

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The penalty payable in respect of a penalty offence is such amount as the Secretary of State may specify by order.

(1A)[^key-be1cf03f607a19f78faffabc964e4e49] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) But the Secretary of State may not specify an amount which is more than a quarter of the amount of the maximum fine for which a person is liable on summary[^c19315771] conviction of the offence plus a half of the relevant surcharge[^key-589c8bb74df69f7940137498b3817c5b].

(2A)[^key-7252c0404ce9eae8bb6616394295a118] The “**relevant surcharge**”, in relation to a person of a given age, is the amount payable by way of surcharge under section 42 of the Sentencing Code[^key-d4c8965e6bcf1a9016274c270a6bca5a] by a person of that age who is fined the maximum amount for the offence.

(3) A penalty notice must—

- (a)[^key-a0622b66fa78014c15b5110eb3d98b6f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) state the alleged offence;
- (c) give such particulars of the circumstances alleged to constitute the offence as are necessary to provide reasonable information about it;
- (d) specify the suspended enforcement period (as to which see section 5) and explain its effect;
- (e) state the amount of the penalty;
- (f) state the designated officer for a local justice area[^c19315801] to whom, and the address at which, the penalty may be paid; and
- (g) inform the person to whom it is given of his right to ask to be tried for the alleged offence and explain how that right may be exercised.

(3A)[^key-b867d29580e46decb0c4642b40d310b2] The Secretary of State may by regulations require information in addition to that mentioned in subsection (3) to be included in, or to be provided with, a penalty notice with an education option.
