## 2. Penalty notices

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A constable who has reason to believe that a person aged 18[^c-6ad49ba2ba503c3044417f527d70a5b4] or over has committed a penalty offence may give him a penalty notice in respect of the offence.

(1A)[^c-802d2506b1cd75abaac3b327cdbceb1f] If the offence mentioned in subsection (1) is a relevant penalty offence, the constable may give the person a penalty notice with an education option.

(2)[^c-941e6dd84012745d5dd5a98be228136b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-8d7a1a54988fa4187b6c3cb7afd311d2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) In this Chapter —[^c-6b5a2d99baa77167f1eb059dd0dd1e3a]

“**approved educational course**” means an educational course run as part of an educational course scheme established by—

- (a) in the case of a notice given by a constable of the British Transport Police Force, the Chief Constable of that force, and
- (b) in any other case, the chief officer of police for the area in which the notice is given;

“**educational course scheme**” means a scheme established by a chief officer of police under section 2A;

“**penalty notice**” means a notice offering the opportunity, by paying a penalty in accordance with this Chapter, to discharge any liability to be convicted of the offence to which the notice relates ;[^c-4c25ae0a5cf0ebb8c36c4815dc6a3736]

“**penalty notice with an education option**” means a penalty notice that also offers the opportunity to discharge any liability to be convicted of the offence to which the notice relates by—

- (a) completing an approved educational course, and
- (b) paying the course fee;

(4A)[^c-1fc40260f4aef24861aa7ea8e4f46927] In this section, “**relevant penalty offence**” means a penalty offence in relation to which there is an approved educational course.

(4B) The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision about the revocation of penalty notices.
