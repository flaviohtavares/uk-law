# Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001, section 126

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-04-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/24
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 126. Expenses

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenditure incurred by a Minister of the Crown by virtue of this Act, and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable out of money so provided under any other enactment.
