# Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001, section 55

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-04-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/24

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Consent to prosecutions[^c19553571]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Proceedings for an offence under section 47 or 50 shall not be instituted—

- (a) in England and Wales, except by or with the consent of the Attorney General;
- (b) in Northern Ireland, except by or with the consent of the Advocate General for Northern Ireland[^key-27e8fea1133fa8feaa2b32d4b9c18fa7] .
