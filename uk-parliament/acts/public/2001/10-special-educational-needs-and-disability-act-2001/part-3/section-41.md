# Special Educational Needs and Disability Act 2001, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2021-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2001/10
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 41. Expenses of Secretary of State[^c417835]

*Extent: E+W+S*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament any increase attributable to this Act in the sums so payable under any other enactment.
