# Armed Forces Act 2001, section 36

> This provision is no longer in force.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Application to Channel Islands and Isle of Man

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-03-28).**

(1)[^key-b1cb7f0492d45baafc4628e54cf8f4a5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
