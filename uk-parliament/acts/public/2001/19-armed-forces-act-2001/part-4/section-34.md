# Armed Forces Act 2001, section 34

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Miscellaneous amendments[^c1420044][^c-5b346da68196d37f0e209299194369b4]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-03-28).**

Schedule 6 (which contains miscellaneous amendments relating to the armed forces) shall have effect.
