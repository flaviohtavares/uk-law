# Armed Forces Act 2001, section 38

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Repeals[^c1420046]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-03-28).**

The enactments specified in Schedule 7 (which includes enactments that are spent in consequence of section 21(5) of the Human Rights Act 1998 (c. 42) or section 60 of the Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999 (c.23)) are repealed to the extent specified in that Schedule.
