# Armed Forces Act 2001, section 28

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Provisions supplementary to ss. 26 and 27[^c-1742a2158921d14ca6243ad06d6bf050]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2009-03-28).**

(1)[^c-bab264b63d61d2f6ccd094f2c7f75b25][^c-2a12f832d69e2bd2611ecd8ac1f73dc4][^c-d82668652d73d01dc49d7d1e2a59b0b7][^c-f0ca6834feb57d7a1387939b70281264] Where any of Her Majesty’s forces incurs costs in respect of the exercise by the Director of Service Prosecutions (“**the Director**”) of his functions as a party to proceedings before a court mentioned in section 27(1), those costs shall, subject to any provision made by virtue of subsection (2), be taken for the purposes of sections 26 and 27 to have been incurred by the Director.

(2)[^c-8b397e2318f4cd726a27a1265e27871f] Regulations under section 26 or 27 may make provision—

- (a) as to the costs incurred by any of Her Majesty’s forces which are or are not to be taken for the purposes of that section to have been incurred by the Director, and
- (b) as to the person to whom, or account into which, any payment in respect of costs incurred by the Director is to be made.
