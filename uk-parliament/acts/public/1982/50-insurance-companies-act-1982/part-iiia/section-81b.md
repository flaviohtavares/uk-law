# Insurance Companies Act 1982, section 81B

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81B. Recognition in other EEA States of UK insurers.[^c1483039]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

Schedule 2G to this Act (which makes provision for or in connection with the recognition in other EEA States of UK insurers) shall have effect.
