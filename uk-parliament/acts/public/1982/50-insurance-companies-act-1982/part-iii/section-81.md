# Insurance Companies Act 1982, section 81

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Penalties and offences under Part III.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1) Any person guilty of an offence under section 72, 73 or 74 above shall be liable—

- (a) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or to a fine, or to both;
- (b) on summary conviction—
  - (i) in England and Wales [^c1483005], to a fine not exceeding £1,000 or, if it is greater, the prescribed sum within the meaning of section 32 of the [^c1483006]Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980;
  - (ii) in Scotland, to a fine not exceeding £1,000 or, if it is greater, the prescribed sum within the meaning of section 225(8)of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995[^c1483007],
  - (iii)[^c1483008] in Northern Ireland, to a fine not exceeding £1,000 or, if it is greater, the prescribed sum within the meaning of Article 4 of the Fines and Penalties (Northern Ireland) Order 1984.

(2) Any person who makes default in complying with, or with a requirement imposed under, any other provision of this Part of this Act shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction in England and Wales and Scotland to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale and, on summary conviction in Northern Ireland to a fine not exceeding £400.
