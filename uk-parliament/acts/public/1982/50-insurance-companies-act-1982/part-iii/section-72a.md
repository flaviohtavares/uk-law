# Insurance Companies Act 1982, section 72A

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72A. Information for policy holders of UK insurers and EC companies.[^c1482955]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

Schedule 2E to this Act (which makes provision with respect to information for policy holders of UK insurers and EC companies) shall have effect.
