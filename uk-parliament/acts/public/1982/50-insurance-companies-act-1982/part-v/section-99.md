# Insurance Companies Act 1982, section 99

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Savings, transitionals, consequential amendments and repeals.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1) The saving and transitional provisions specified in Schedule 4 to this Act shall have effect.

(2)[^c1483654] The enactments mentioned in Schedule 5 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments there specified, being amendments consequential on the provisions of that Act.

(3)[^c1483654] The enactments mentioned Schedule 6 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
