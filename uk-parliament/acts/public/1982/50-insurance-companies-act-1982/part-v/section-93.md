# Insurance Companies Act 1982, section 93

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. Restriction on institution of prosecutions.[^c1483176]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

Proceedings in respect of an offence under this Act shall not be instituted—

- (a) in England or Wales, except by or with the consent of the Treasury,[^c1483177] the Secretary of State, the Industrial Assurance Commissioner or the Director of Public Prosecutions,
- (b) in Northern Ireland, except by or with the consent of the Treasury,the Secretary of State, the Department of Commerce for Northern Ireland or the Director of Public Prosecutions for Northern Ireland.
