# Insurance Companies Act 1982, section 66

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 66. Documents deposited in Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

Any insurance company which is required to prepare and deliver accounts under Articles 235 and 249 or Article 649 of the Companies (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^c1482835] shall deposit with the registrar of companies in Northern Ireland one copy of—

- (a) any document deposited with the Treasury[^c1482836] under section 22(1), [^c1482837]. . .22(6), or 42(4) above or paragraph 5(5) of Schedule 2C to this Act[^c1482838];
- (b) any document supplied by the company to the Treasury under section 22(5) above.
