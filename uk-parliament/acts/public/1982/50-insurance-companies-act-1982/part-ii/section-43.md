# Insurance Companies Act 1982, section 43

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Acceleration of information required by accounting provisions.[^c1482248]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1) The Treasury[^c1482249] may require any documents which under section 22 above are required to be deposited with them[^c1482250] by a company within the period specified in that section to be deposited with them on or before a specified date before the end of that period, being a date not earlier than three months before the end of that period and not earlier than one month after the date on which the requirement is imposed.

(2) The Treasury may require any statement which under section 25 above is required to be deposited with them by a company within a period prescribed under that section to be deposited with them on or before a specified date before the end of that period.
