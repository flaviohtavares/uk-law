# Insurance Companies Act 1982, section 84

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84. Lloyd’s underwriters —financial resources.[^c1483121]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1) Subject to such modifications as may be prescribed and to any determination made by the Treasury[^c1483122] in accordance with regulations, sections 32, 33 and 35 above shall apply to the members of Lloyd’s taken together as they apply to an insurance company to which Part II of this Act applies and whose head office is in the United Kingdom.

(2) The powers conferred on the Treasury or[^c1483123] the Secretary of State by sections 38 to 41, 44 and 45 above shall be exercisable in relation to the members of Lloyd’s if there is a breach of an obligation imposed by virtue of subsection (1) above.
