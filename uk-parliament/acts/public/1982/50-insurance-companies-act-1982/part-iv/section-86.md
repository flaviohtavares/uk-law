# Insurance Companies Act 1982, section 86

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Statement of business by Committee of Lloyd’s.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-12-01).**

(1)[^c1483140] The Committee of Lloyd’s shall deposit every year with the Treasury[^c1483141] a statement in the prescribed form summarising the extent and character of the insurance business done by the members of Lloyd’s in the twelve months to which the statement relates.

(2) Regulations made for the purposes of this section may require the statement to deal separately with such classes or descriptions of business as may be specified in the regulations.
