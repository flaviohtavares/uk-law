# Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982, section 68

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 68. Expenses.

*Extent: S*

There shall be defrayed out of moneys provided by Parliament—

- (a) any administrative expenses incurred by any Government department in consequence of the provisions of this Act; and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable out of moneys so provided under any other enactment.
