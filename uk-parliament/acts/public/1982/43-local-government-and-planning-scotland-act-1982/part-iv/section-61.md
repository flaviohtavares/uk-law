# Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982, section 61

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 61. Abolition of Amenity Committee.

*Extent: S*

The Amenity Committee appointed under subsection (2) of section 9 of the [^c794991]Hydro-Electric Development (Scotland) Act 1943 (a subsection which among other things provided that the Secretary of State was to appoint such a committee for the purpose of giving advice and assistance to him and to the Scottish Electricity Boards and which is re-enacted as subsection (2) of section 5 of the [^c794992]Electricity (Scotland) Act 1979) is hereby abolished.
