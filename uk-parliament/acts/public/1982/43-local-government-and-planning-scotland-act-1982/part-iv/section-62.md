# Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982, section 62

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 62. Abolition of Staff Commission for Scotland.

*Extent: S*

The Staff Commission for Scotland is hereby abolished; and accordingly, in the 1973 Act, section 218 (which relates to the establishment, functions, procedure and expenses of that Commission) shall cease to have effect.
