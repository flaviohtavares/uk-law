# Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982, section 66

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 66. Amendments and repeals.[^c794993]

*Extent: S*

(1) The enactments specified in Schedule 3 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in that Schedule, being minor amendments or amendments consequential upon the provisions of this Act.

(2) The enactments specified—

- (a) in Part I of Schedule 4 to this Act (which include certain spent enactments) ; and
- (b) in Part 11 of that Schedule (which relate to powers of entry),

are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
