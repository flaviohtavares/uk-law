# Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982, section 67

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 67. Interpretation.

*Extent: S*

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “[^c794996]**local authority**” means a council constituted under section 2 of the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994
- “**the1972 Act**” means the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1972; and
- “**the1973 Act**” means the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973.
