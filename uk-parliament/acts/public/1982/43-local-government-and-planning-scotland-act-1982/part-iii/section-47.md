# Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982, section 47

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 47. Restriction on further appeal following decision of Secretary of State on appeal under section 63A, 85 or 93 of the 1972 Act.[^c794973]

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

In subsection (3) of section 231 of the 1972 Act (which, among other things, relates to appeals against certain decisions of the Secretary of State)—

- (a) after paragraph (e) there shall be inserted the following paragraph—

  > - (ee) any decision of the Secretary of State on an appeal to him under section 63A of this Act against a waste land notice ;

  ;
- (b) in paragraph (f), the words “(1)(a), (f) or (g)” shall cease to have effect ; and
- (c) for paragraph (h) there shall be substituted the following paragraph—

  > - (h) any decision of the Secretary of State on an appeal to him under section 93 of this Act against a listed building enforcement notice ;

  .
