# Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982, section 38

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 38. Inquiries with respect to local plans.[^c794960]

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

In subsection (1) of section 11 of the [^c794961]1972 Act (which relates, among other things, to inquiries with respect to local plans)—

- (a) after the word “shall”, where it occurs for the first time, there shall be inserted the word “if” ; and
- (b) after the word “Act”, where it occurs for the first time, there shall be inserted the words “an objector so requires,”.
