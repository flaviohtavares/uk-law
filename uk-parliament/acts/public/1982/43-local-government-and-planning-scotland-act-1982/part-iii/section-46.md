# Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982, section 46

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 46. Power of planning authorities to vary planning permission.[^c794972]

*Extent: S*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-05-27).**

After section 31 of the 1972 Act there shall be inserted the following section—

> **31A. Power of planning authorities to vary planning permission.**
>
> Notwithstanding any other provision of this Part of this Act, a planning authority may, at the request of the grantee or of a person acting with his consent, vary any planning permission granted by them, if it appears to them that the variation sought is not material.
