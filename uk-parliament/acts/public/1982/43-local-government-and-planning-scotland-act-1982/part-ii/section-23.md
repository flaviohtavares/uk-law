# Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982, section 23

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 23. General powers of local authority to assist community council.[^c794911]

*Extent: S*

In section 55 of the [^c794912]1973 Act (which empowers a regional, islands or district council to contribute towards the expenses etc. of a community council in their area), the word “Regional,” shall cease to have effect.
