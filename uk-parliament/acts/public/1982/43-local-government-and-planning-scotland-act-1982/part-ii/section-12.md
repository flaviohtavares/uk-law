> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 12. Regional or islands council’s functions in affording assistance for rural bus services.[^c794867]

*Extent: S*

In section 34(1) of the [^c794868]Transport Act 1968 (which empowers regional, islands or district councils to afford financial assistance for rural bus services), for the words “, islands or district” there shall be substituted the words “or islands ”.
