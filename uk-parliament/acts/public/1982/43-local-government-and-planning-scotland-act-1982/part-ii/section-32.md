# Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982, section 32

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 32. Delegation of functions by sub-committees of local authorities.

*Extent: S*

For section 56(2) of the [^c794950]1973 Act (which permits a committee of a local authority to arrange for a sub-committee or an officer of the authority to discharge functions of the authority) there shall be substituted the following subsection—

> (2) Where by virtue of this section any function of a local authority may be discharged by any committee or sub-committee of theirs, then, unless the local authority otherwise direct—
>
> - (a) the committee may arrange for the discharge of any of those functions by a sub-committee or an officer of the authority ; and
> - (b) the sub-committee, whether assigned the discharge of functions by the authority or by a committee, may arrange for the discharge of any such functions by an officer of the authority.

.
