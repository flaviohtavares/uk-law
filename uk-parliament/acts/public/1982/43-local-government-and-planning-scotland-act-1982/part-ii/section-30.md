# Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982, section 30

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 30. Power of islands and district councils to provide and maintain roadside seats.

*Extent: S*

(1) Without prejudice to any existing power which they have in that regard, a local authority may, with the consent of the roads authority[^c794946], for the use of the public, provide and maintain seats on any footway in their area; and they may maintain any such seats donated to them.

(2)[^c794947] In subsection (1) above, “**footway**” and “**roads authority**” have the same meanings as in the Roads (Scotland) Act 1984.
