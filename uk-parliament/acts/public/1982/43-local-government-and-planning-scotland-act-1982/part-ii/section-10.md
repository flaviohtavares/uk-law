# Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982, section 10

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 10. Re-allocation of responsibility for the establishment of nature reserves.[^c794863]

*Extent: S*

In section 21 (1) of the [^c794864]National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act 1949 (which relates to the establishment of nature reserves), for the words “a regional, islands or district council” there shall be substituted the words “a general or district planning authority ”.
