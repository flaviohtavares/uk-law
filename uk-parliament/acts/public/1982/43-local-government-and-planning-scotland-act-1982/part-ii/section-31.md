# Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982, section 31

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 31. Cessation of regional, council’s functions as regards war memorials.[^c794948]

*Extent: S*

In section 170 of the 1973 Act (which designates regional, islands and district councils as local authorities for the purposes of the [^c794949]War Memorials (Local Authorities’ Powers) Act 1923) for the words “local authority within the meaning of this Act” there shall be substituted the words “islands or district council ”.
