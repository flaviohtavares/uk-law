# Local Government and Planning (Scotland) Act 1982, section 28

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28. Islands or district council’s functions in relation to the provision of clocks.

*Extent: S*

A local authority[^c794942]—

- (a) may provide a clock for public purposes and may maintain, illuminate or remove such clock; and
- (b) where a clock is not owned by the authority[^c794943] but is open to public view, may for the purposes of securing its maintenance or illumination enter into an agreement with its owner.
