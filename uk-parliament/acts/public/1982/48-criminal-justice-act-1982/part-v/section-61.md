## 61. Legal representation in case of committal on written statements.[^c-3351bb94f1d055bdb3c086554353db9e]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 6(2)(a) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3351bb94f1d055bdb3c086554353db9e] [^c19339211]Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 for the words “is not represented by counsel or a solicitor" there shall be substituted the words “has no solicitor acting for him in the case (whether present in court or not)". *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3351bb94f1d055bdb3c086554353db9e]
