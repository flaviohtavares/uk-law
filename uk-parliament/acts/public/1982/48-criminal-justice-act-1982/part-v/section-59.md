# Criminal Justice Act 1982, section 59

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 59. Remand in custody in absence of accused.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The [^c19339171]Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in Schedule 9 to this Act, being amendments to modify the requirement that a person may not be remanded in custody without being brought before the court.

(2) Nothing in this section shall affect the operation of section 2 of the [^c19339181]Imprisonment (Temporary Provisions)Act 1980.
