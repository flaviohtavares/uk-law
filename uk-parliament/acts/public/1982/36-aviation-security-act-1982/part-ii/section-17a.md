# Aviation Security Act 1982, section 17A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-03-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1982/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17A. Copies of directions etc for CAA[^key-6e28d6882ef42b66bba12f5d4567f026]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State must give the CAA a copy of—

- (a) each direction under section 12, 13, 13A or 14, and
- (b) each direction varying or revoking such a direction.

(2) The Secretary of State must inform the CAA of each notification given under section 17.
