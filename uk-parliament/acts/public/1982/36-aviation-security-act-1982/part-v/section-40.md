# Aviation Security Act 1982, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-03-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1982/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Consequential amendments, savings and repeals.[^c794808]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 2 to this Act (which contains consequential amendments and savings) shall have effect; and the provisions of that Schedule are without prejudice to sections 16 and 17 of the [^c794809] Interpretation Act 1978 (which relate to repeals).

(2) Subject to the provisions of Schedule 2 to this Act, the enactments specified in Schedule 3 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of Schedule 3.
