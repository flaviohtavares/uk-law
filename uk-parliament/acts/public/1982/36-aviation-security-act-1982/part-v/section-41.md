# Aviation Security Act 1982, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2024-03-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1982/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Short title and commencement.[^c794810]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Aviation Security Act 1982.

(2) This Act shall come into force on the expiration of the period of three months beginning with its passing.
