# Aviation Security Act 1982, section 24AL

## 24AL. Period for establishment of aerodrome groups[^key-60eb6c2fea269d569f8c613a4608a22d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The risk advisory group and the security executive group for an aerodrome must each be established before the relevant day.

(2) In the case of an aerodrome to which this Part applies on the commencement date, the relevant day is —

- (a) in relation to the risk advisory group, the end of the period of 1 month beginning with the commencement date, and
- (b) in relation to the security executive group, the end of the period of 3 months beginning with the commencement date.

(3) In subsection (2), “ **commencement date** ” means the date on which section 79 of the Policing and Crime Act 2009 comes into force.

(4) In the case of any other aerodrome to which this Part applies, the relevant day is—

- (a) in relation to the risk advisory group, the end of the period of 1 month beginning with the date on which the aerodrome becomes one to which this Part applies, and
- (b) in relation to the security executive group, the end of the period of 3 months beginning with that date.
