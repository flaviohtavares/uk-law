# Finance Act 1982, section 1

> Text as at 2026-04-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Duties on spirits, beer, wine, made-wine and cider.[^c13231781]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 5 of the [^c13231791]Alcoholic Liquor Duties Act 1979 (excise duty on spirits) for the words from “at the rates” to the end of the section there shall be substituted the words “ at the rate of £14.47 per litre of alcohol in the spirits ”.

(2) In section 36 of that Act (excise duty on beer) for “£18.00” and “£0.60” there shall be substituted “ £20.40 ” and “ £0.68 ” respectively.

(3)[^c-68ab74d86a6e98e6cd6c075f7a0e4685] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c13231801]

(5) In section 62(1) of that Act (excise duty on cider) for “£7.20” there shall be substituted “ £8.16 ”.

(6) This section shall be deemed to have come into force on 10th March 1982.
