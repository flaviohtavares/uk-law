# Civil Aviation Act 1982, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (29).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1982/16

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Matters arising in connection with original transfer to CAA of property, staff, etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 3 to this Act shall have effect with respect to the matters there dealt with, being matters arising out of the transfer by virtue of paragraph 1 of Schedule 2 to the [^c2535201]Civil Aviation Act 1971 to the CAA of the aerodromes mentioned in the said Schedule 2 and of the other property, rights and liabilities there mentioned and out of the transfer of staff to the CAA.
