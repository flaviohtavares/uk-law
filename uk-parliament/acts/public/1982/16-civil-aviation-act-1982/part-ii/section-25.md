# Civil Aviation Act 1982, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (29).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1982/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Secretary of State’s power to provide aerodromes.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may for the purposes of civil aviation establish and maintain aerodromes and provide and maintain in connection therewith roads, approaches, apparatus, equipment and buildings and other accommodation.

(2) The Secretary of State shall appoint for each aerodrome vested in him an officer who shall be responsible to the Secretary of State for all services (including signalling services, flying control services and services connected with the execution of works) provided on the aerodrome on the Secretary of State’s behalf.
