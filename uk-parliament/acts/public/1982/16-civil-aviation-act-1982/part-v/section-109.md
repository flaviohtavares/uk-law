# Civil Aviation Act 1982, section 109

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (29).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1982/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 109. Transitional provisions, consequential amendments, repeals, etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 14 to this Act (which contains transitional and transitory provisions and savings) shall have effect; and the provisions of that Schedule are without prejudice to sections 16 and 17 of the [^c2545441]Interpretation Act 1978 (which relate to repeals).

(2)[^c2545451] The enactments specified in Schedule 15 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments there specified, being amendments consequential on the provisions of this Act.

(3) Subject to the provisions of the said Schedule 14, the enactments and instruments specified in Schedule 16 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of the said Schedule 16.
