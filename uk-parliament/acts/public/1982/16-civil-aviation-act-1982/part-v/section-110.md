# Civil Aviation Act 1982, section 110

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (29).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1982/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Citation and commencement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19378851] This Act may be cited as the Civil Aviation Act 1982.

(2) This Act shall come into force at the expiration of the period of three months beginning with its passing.
