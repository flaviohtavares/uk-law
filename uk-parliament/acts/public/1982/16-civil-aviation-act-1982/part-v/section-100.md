# Civil Aviation Act 1982, section 100

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (29).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1982/16
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 100. Application of Act to hovercraft.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The enactments and instruments with respect to which provision may be made by Order in Council in pursuance of section 1(1)(h) of the [^c2542931]Hovercraft Act 1968 (power to apply enactments and instruments in relation to hovercraft etc.) shall include this Act and any instrument made under it.
