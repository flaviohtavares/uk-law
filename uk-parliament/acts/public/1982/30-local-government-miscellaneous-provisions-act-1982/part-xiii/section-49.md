## 49. Citation and extent.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1982.

(2) Subject to sections [^c-6e73a1ffb0e9365712caeb588ab15e8e]... 38(3) and 47(4) above, and to paragraph 8(2) of Schedule 6 to this Act, this Act extends to England and Wales only.
