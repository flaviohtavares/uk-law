# Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1982, section 45

## 45. Arrangements under Employment and Training Act 1973.[^c2553091][^key-a785940574c8d6e68817443ec04bcdb9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority to whom this section applies shall have power and shall be deemed always to have had power to enter into arrangements with [^c2553101]... the Secretary of State under any provision of the Employment and Training Act 1973.

(2) The local authorities to whom this section applies are—

- (a) a local authority as defined in section 270(1) of the [^c2553111]Local Government Act 1972;
- (aa)[^c2553121] an authority established by Part [^c2553131]... IV of the Local Government Act 1985 or by an order under section 10 of that Act;
- (ab) a body corporate established by an order under section 67 of that Act..
- (b) a board constituted in pursuance of section 2 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^c2553141] [^M_F_86f3bb09-51e3-4cb9-a590-689f19b3e329]. . .; [^c2553161]...
- (bb)[^c2553171] the Broads Authority; [^c2553161]...
- (c) the Common Council of the City of London.
- (ca)[^key-ab0e7d7394ac6725456567de00af765f] a fire and rescue authority created by an order under section 4A of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004; and
- (d)[^key-88c43c0a06edac53ac27c24d68c0db21] the London Fire Commissioner.
