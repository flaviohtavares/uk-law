# Canada Act 1982, section 2

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Termination of power to legislate for Canada[^c742964]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No Act of the Parliament of the United Kingdom passed after the Constitution Act, 1982 comes into force shall extend to Canada as part of its law.

## Annotations

[^c742964]: Modification: [S. 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1982/11/section/2): By proclamation made at Ottawa 17.4.1982 it was declared that the Constitution Act 1982 should, subject to section 59 thereof, come into force on 17.4.1982
