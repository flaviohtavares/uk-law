# Civic Government (Scotland) Act 1982, section 105

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-03-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1982/45

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 105. Contents of notices.

*Extent: S*

Except where otherwise expressly provided under this Part of this Act, any notice issued or served by a local authority under this said Part regarding the doing of any thing in relation to land or premises shall, so far as necessary and reasonably practicable, specify—

- (a) details, including the location, of the land or premises;
- (b) the nature of any works which have to be carried out and of any requirements which have to be met; and
- (c) the period within which the notice has to be complied with.
