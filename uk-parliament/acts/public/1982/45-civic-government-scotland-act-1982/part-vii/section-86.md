## 86. Interpretation and Crown application of this Part.

*Extent: S*

(1) In this Part of this Act, “**chief constable**” means the chief constable of the Police Service of Scotland.[^c-973bd92b531d9cc06884f7fc22d32943]

(2) This Part of this Act binds the Crown.
