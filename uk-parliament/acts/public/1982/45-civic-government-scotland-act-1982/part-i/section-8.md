## 8. Interpretation of Parts I and II.

*Extent: S*

In this Part and in Part II of this Act except where the context otherwise requires—

- [^key-3b6d3ad44e8dd66494b9f449ee006045]...
- “[^key-d404302eb0924ba8df9fcd0bde48883d]**authorised civilian employee**” means a person—
  - (a) appointed by the Scottish Police Authority under section 26(1) of the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 (asp 8); and[^key-0f70dd26bb86577fd5b4b6b94450958c]
  - (b) authorised by the chief constable for the purposes of sections 5 and 11 of this Act;
- “[^key-b5c2ef781d5392d1dbc2c5d808cdb595]**chief constable**” means the chief constable of the Police Service of Scotland;
- [^key-fc253c6d595a24685fd65c03d155fde4]...
- “**licence**” means a licence granted under this Part and Part II of this Act, and cognate expressions shall be construed accordingly;
- “**premises**” includes land.
