## 47. Urinating etc.

*Extent: S*

Any person who urinates or defecates in such circumstances as to cause, or to be likely to cause, annoyance to any other person shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 2 on the standard scale[^c13373591].
