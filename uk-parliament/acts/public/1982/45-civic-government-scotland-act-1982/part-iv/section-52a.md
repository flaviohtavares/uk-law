# Civic Government (Scotland) Act 1982, section 52A

## 52A. Possession of indecent photographs of children.[^c13374431]

*Extent: S*

(1) It is an offence for a person to have any indecent photograph or pseudo-photograph of a child[^c13374441] in his possession.

(2) Where a person is charged with an offence under subsection (1), it shall be a defence for him to prove—

- (a) that he had a legitimate reason for having the photograph or pseudo-photograph[^c13374451] in his possession; or
- (b) that he had not himself seen the photograph or pseudo-photograph and did not know, nor had any cause to suspect, it to be indecent; or
- (c) that the photograph or pseudo-photograph was sent to him without any prior request made by him or on his behalf and that he did not keep it for an unreasonable time.

(3) A person shall be liable

- (a)[^c-58da5ed17909fa38fbd3bd8571db8009] on summary conviction of an offence under this section to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 6 months or to[^c13374481]a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale “or to both.”[^c13374491].
- (b)[^c-cfd3086f013440ddd02ab0f55c2fbc64] on conviction on indictment of such an offence to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 5 years or to a fine or to both.

(4) Subsections (2) to (2C)[^c13374501] and (8) to (10)[^c-1ecb49ea42ac7d4c6e29a05cbbe75d85] of section 52 of this Act shall have effect for the purposes of this section as they have for the purposes of that section.
