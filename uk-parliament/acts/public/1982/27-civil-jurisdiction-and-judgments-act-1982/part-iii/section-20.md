## 20. Rules as to jurisdiction in Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to [^c-9c33dc669f3b549877044a9881eaadb2]... Parts I and II and to the following provisions of this Part, Schedule 8 has effect to determine in what circumstances a person may be sued in civil proceedings in the Court of Session or in a sheriff court.

(2) Nothing in Schedule 8 affects the competence as respects subject-matter or value of the Court of Session or of the sheriff court.

(3) Section 43 of the Courts Reform (Scotland) Act 2014 does not apply—[^c-00d690b1b2ce312a1518f2dab6af59b8]

- (a)[^c21651311] [^c-0614797b5bd5bbb7496324be8cf6254c]...in relation to any matter to which Schedule 8 applies.[^c-46264e846a5f646ca900632384bc5e30]...
- (b)[^c-46264e846a5f646ca900632384bc5e30] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c15857121] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) In determining any question as to the meaning or effect of any provision contained in Schedule 8 [^c15857131] . . .—

- (a) regard shall be had to any relevant principles laid down by the European Court in connection with Title II of the 1968 Convention or Chapter[^c15857141] II of the Regulation and to any relevant decision of that court as to the meaning or effect of any provision of that Title or that Chapter[^c15857151]; and
- (b) without prejudice to the generality of paragraph (a), the expert reports relating to the 1968 Convention[^c-8569ef6f6415d6b23777467d67e0a286] may be considered and shall, so far as relevant, be given such weight as is appropriate in the circumstances.

(6)[^c-43056ae57b0e6cc4001ee661ab4a6b69] The requirement in subsection (5)(a) applies only in relation to principles laid down, or decisions made, by the European Court before IP completion day.
