# Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982, SCHEDULE 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1982/27

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 9 — PROCEEDINGS EXCLUDED FROM SCHEDULE 8[^c22747061]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 21.

**1.** Proceedings concerning the status or legal capacity of natural persons (including proceedings for separation) other than proceedings which consist solely of proceedings [^c15860391]. . . of affiliation and aliment.

**2.** Proceedings for regulating the custody of children.

**2A.**[^c15860401] Proceedings relating to parental responsibilities within the meaning of section 1(3) of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995 or parental rights within the meaning of section 2(4) of that Act.

**3.** Proceedings relating to guardianship of children[^c15860421] and all proceedings relating to the management of the affairs of persons who are incapable of managing their own affairs.

**4.** Proceedings in respect of sequestration in bankruptcy; or the winding up of a company or other legal person; or proceedings in respect of a judicial arrangement or judicial composition with creditors.

**5.** Proceedings relating to a company where, by any enactment, jurisdiction in respect of those proceedings is conferred on the court having jurisdiction to wind it up.

**6.** Admiralty actions[^c21528931] in so far as the jurisdiction is based on arrestment *in rem* or *ad fundandam jurisdictionem* of a ship, cargo or freight.

**7.** Commissary proceedings.

**8.** Proceedings for the rectification of the register of aircraft mortgages kept by the Civil Aviation Authority.

**9.** Proceedings under section 7(3) of the [^c15860431]Civil Aviation (Eurocontrol) Act 1962 (recovery of charges for air navigation services and proceedings for damages against Eurocontrol).

**10.** Proceedings brought in pursuance of an order under section 11 of the Petroleum Act 1998[^c15860451].

**11.** Proceedings under section 6 of the [^c15860461]Protection of Trading Interests Act 1980 (recovery of sums paid or obtained pursuant to a judgment for multiple damages).

**12.** Appeals from or review of decisions of tribunals.

**13.** Proceedings which are not in substance proceedings in which a decree against any person is sought.

**14.** Proceedings brought in any court in pursuance of—

- (a) any statutory provision which, in the case of any convention to which the United Kingdom is or may become a party and which governs jurisdiction or recognition and enforcement of judgments in relation to a particular matter[^key-7fc5515c18eaf50216b18c9b6754e89e], implements the convention; and
- (b) any rule of law so far as it has the effect of implementing any such convention.
