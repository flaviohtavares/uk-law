# Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982, section 15A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1982/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15A. Scope of sections 15B to 15E[^c-59cf4b4d784fe6deb5c8fcaeb9468fc7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Sections 15B to 15E make provision about the jurisdiction of courts in the United Kingdom—

- (a) in matters relating to consumer contracts where the consumer is domiciled in the United Kingdom;
- (b) in matters relating to individual contracts of employment.

(2) Sections 15B and 15C apply only if the subject-matter of the proceedings and the nature of the proceedings are within the scope of the Regulation as determined by Article 1 of the Regulation (whether or not the Regulation would have had effect before IP completion day[^M_F_af2b77cd-7014-445e-86b7-e431c3f3ce88] in relation to the proceedings).

(3) Sections 15B to 15E do not apply to proceedings of a description listed in Schedule 5 or to proceedings in Scotland under an enactment which confers jurisdiction on a Scottish court in respect of a specific subject-matter on specific grounds.
