## 28. Application of s. 1 of Administration of Justice (Scotland) Act 1972.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c21551601] When any proceedings have been brought, or are likely to be brought, [^c-3ab76b20848dfbe2a049a721f86a4808]... in a *(Extent: S)*[^c-4984dd45a22d3a4d2b57280fa4e26cda] 2005 Hague Convention *(Extent: S)* State *(Extent: S)* or in England and Wales or Northern Ireland in respect of any matter which is within the scope of the Regulation[^c15857621] as determined in Article 1, the Court of Session shall have the like power to make an order under section 1 of the [^c15857631]Administration of Justice (Scotland) Act 1972 as amended by the Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1985[^c15857641] as if the proceedings in question had been brought, or were likely to be brought, in that court.
