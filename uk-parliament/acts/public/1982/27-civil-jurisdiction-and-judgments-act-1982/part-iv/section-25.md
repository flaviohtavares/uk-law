# Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982, section 25

## 25. Interim relief in England and Wales and Northern Ireland in the absence of substantive proceedings.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The High Court in England and Wales or Northern Ireland shall have power to grant interim relief where—

- (a) proceedings have been or are to be commenced in [^c-196a20d3f4b7c31b6af7d4e035ec9efe]... a *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)*[^c-9a33dfd6a7c16ffe4d1be36c6d58d943] 2005 Hague Convention *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* State *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* [^c-effeaa8497e7c5f7021ddee4e6a0d6a5]... [^c-0c6e1af3d9abf9b47c0476808d660111]... other than the United Kingdom or in a part of the United Kingdom other than that in which the High Court in question exercises jurisdiction; and
- (b (Extent: E.W.N.I.))[^c-bccc7b44f46ee97abaf4d4f351085dae] they are or will be proceedings whose subject-matter is *(Extent: E.W.N.I.)* [^c-8353f83a766904246283996977c49fb7]... *(Extent: E.W.N.I.)* [^c-931b76051f6b9465f61187e6b204b089]... *(Extent: E.W.N.I.)* [^c-77c739883f8176274e99f6dae8dc8f45]... within scope of the *(Extent: E.W.N.I.)* 2005 Hague Convention *(Extent: E.W.N.I.)* as determined by Articles 1 and 2 of the *(Extent: E.W.N.I.)* 2005 Hague Convention *(Extent: E.W.N.I.)* (whether or not *(Extent: E.W.N.I.)* [^c-a1b73d047c7f67014d6657ace80173f4]... *(Extent: E.W.N.I.)*[^c-8a1cdf45c95a38a4bf21529f9a383e30]... *(Extent: E.W.N.I.)* [^c-2c7c5c3958df2a254c9776178219a64e]... the *(Extent: E.W.N.I.)* 2005 Hague Convention *(Extent: E.W.N.I.)* has effect in relation to the proceedings). *(Extent: E.W.N.I.)*

(2) On an application for any interim relief under subsection (1) the court may refuse to grant that relief if, in the opinion of the court, the fact that the court has no jurisdiction apart from this section in relation to the subject-matter of the proceedings in question makes it inexpedient for the court to grant it.

(3)[^c-93ac1cef6daa3ff15e44b5c932ff5718] Her Majesty may by Order in Council extend the power to grant interim relief conferred by subsection (1) so as to make it exercisable in relation to proceedings of any of the following descriptions, namely—

- (a) proceedings commenced or to be commenced otherwise than in [^c-29c475ab6b59f30834dd974001459aff]... a *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)*[^c-640cb2b96153a4994e44bd7b4965b9ab] 2005 Hague Convention *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* State *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* [^c-bc99e23b85e73050409a24a3d91d0514]... [^c-89f5bc36773d645ca095009f7261e817]...;
- (b) proceedings whose subject-matter is not within the scope [^c21551281]. . . of [^c-940750cdd2df66880c3106027ea14964]... [^c-346f1d86ae0f6f1f0bc0f72495dd9edb]..., [^c-925c8ee14b5ba673dfb1fdfd459b4af5]... the *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)*[^c-c5dbbf5f689b5ba3c7e46ea33b30eaa7] 2005 Hague Convention *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* as determined by Articles 1 and 2 of the *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)* 2005 Hague Convention *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)*. *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)*
- (c)[^c15857391] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) An Order in Council under subsection (3)—

- (a) may confer power to grant only specified descriptions of interim relief;
- (b) may make different provision for different classes of proceedings, for proceedings pending in different countries or courts outside the United Kingdom or in different parts of the United Kingdom, and for other different circumstances; and
- (c) may impose conditions or restrictions on the exercise of any power conferred by the Order.

(5)[^c15857401] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) Any Order in Council under subsection (3) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(7) In this section “**interim relief**”, in relation to the High Court in England and Wales or Northern Ireland, means interim relief of any kind which that court has power to grant in proceedings relating to matters within its jurisdiction, other than—

- (a) a warrant for the arrest of property; or
- (b) provision for obtaining evidence.
