# Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1982/27

## 15. Interpretation of Part I and consequential amendments.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “**judgment**” has the meaning given by [^c-ba583b8f725f49d9c3a2c58571fb0d09]... Article 4(1) of the[^c-9f8b9f2ae0e48020e942b9e76a03ce76] 2005 Hague Convention or, as the case may be, Article 3(1) of the 2019 Hague Convention[^c-3bb1b55db8f1ccf2e961222ce991938b];
- [^c-b362af1651b1722e427d08e452c17f25]...
- [^c-44dece438c2888a4394dc15c9d2a4b67]...
- “**prescribed**” means prescribed by rules of court.

(2)[^c15855891] References in this Part to a judgment registered under sections[^c21525291] [^c-52bbe6b5c6054a93e15b456987e0ca74]... 4B[^c-28b60751fc0b1379178d920609bcc922][^c-53495ec9accebf974f69c54adca7b4e6]... , 4C, 6B or 6C[^c-80cae3ce4bdf89025f732ed9797273d1] include, to the extent of its registration, references to a judgment so registered to a limited extent only.

(3)[^c-fd38433b8db2acde53a84804e3237792][^c15855891] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c15855921] The enactments specified in Part I of Schedule 12 shall have effect with the amendments specified there, being amendments consequential on this Part.
