# Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1982/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Provision for issue of copies of, and certificates in connection with, U.K. judgments.[^c15855791]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Rules of court may make provision for enabling any interested party wishing to secure under [^c-244fa4a5495131814ec3017793f1d2b6]... the[^c-531d0fe2202451774ae3fed0d27332ac] 2005 Hague Convention or the 2019 Hague Convention[^c-2c5f3091bb79c5b20a942a60ccf6b545] the recognition or enforcement in another Contracting State of a judgment given by a court in the United Kingdom to obtain, subject to any conditions specified in the rules—

- (a) a copy of the judgment; and
- (b) a certificate giving particulars relating to the judgment and the proceedings in which it was given.
