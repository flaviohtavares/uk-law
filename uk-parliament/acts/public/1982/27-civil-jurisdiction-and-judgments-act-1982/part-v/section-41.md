## 41. Domicile of individuals.[^c15858521][^c-858408dcb06bb92df179b909668909c3][^c-6c67494432eaf59adffd4104e983e294][^c-6c9d35ba943488f29ccfd668af6f6c45][^c-bdc6dfddcf61098c51cb732e82e9661d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) [^c-fc07d1409395856ce8661236ced38afc]... The following provisions of this section determine, for the purposes of [^c-58db6b33d54f4ad2385c9ceee47a3bc6]...this Act, whether an individual is domiciled in the United Kingdom or in a particular part of, or place in, the United Kingdom [^c-59d630863fa299591adc50e17c57d16b]....

(2)[^c-79bba93f182ab4599f24d7b023a7c857] An individual is domiciled in the United Kingdom if and only if—

- (a) he is resident in the United Kingdom; and
- (b) the nature and circumstances of his residence indicate that he has a substantial connection with the United Kingdom.

(3)[^c-79bba93f182ab4599f24d7b023a7c857] Subject to subsection (5), an individual is domiciled in a particular part of the United Kingdom if and only if—

- (a) he is resident in that part; and
- (b) the nature and circumstances of his residence indicate that he has a substantial connection with that part.

(4) An individual is domiciled in a particular place in the United Kingdom if and only if he—

- (a) is domiciled in the part of the United Kingdom in which that place is situated; and
- (b) is resident in that place.

(5)[^c-1c8fd2250871249110932aa359f3399a] An individual who is domiciled in the United Kingdom but in whose case the requirements of subsection (3)(b) are not satisfied in relation to any particular part of the United Kingdom shall be treated as domiciled in the part of the United Kingdom in which he is resident.

(6)[^c-1c8fd2250871249110932aa359f3399a] In the case of an individual who—

- (a) is resident in the United Kingdom, or in a particular part of the United Kingdom; and
- (b) has been so resident for the last three months or more,

the requirements of subsection (2)(b) or, as the case may be, subsection (3)(b) shall be presumed to be fulfilled unless the contrary is proved.
