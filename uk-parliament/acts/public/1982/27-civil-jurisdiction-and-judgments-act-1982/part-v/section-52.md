# Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1982, section 52

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2025-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1982/27
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Extent.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act extends to Northern Ireland.

(2)[^c-d1768670d8db9555a4f781e9efd6e7e9][^c-e95999dc1c22daddd448f99c1dc101af] Without prejudice to the power conferred by section 39, Her Majesty may by Order in Council direct that all or any of the provisions of this Act apart from that section shall extend, subject to such modifications as may be specified in the Order, to any of the following territories, that is to say—

- (a) the Isle of Man;
- (b) any of the Channel Islands;
- (c)[^c15859201] any colony.
