## 15. Interest on debts and damages.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c2571091] The section set out in Part I of Schedule 1 to this Act shall be inserted after section 35 of the [^c2571101]Senior Courts Act 1981[^c20828031]

(2) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c2571111]

(3)[^c2571091] The [^c2571131]Crown Proceedings Act 1947 shall accordingly have effect subject to the amendment in Part III of that Schedule, being an amendment consequential on subsections (1) and (2) above.

(4) The provisions mentioned in subsection (5) below (which this section supersedes so far as they apply to the High Court and county courts) shall cease to have effect in relation to those courts.

(5) The provisions are—

- (a) section 3 of the [^c2571141]Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1934; and
- (b) in the [^c2571151]Administration of Justice Act 1969—
  - (i) section 22; and
  - (ii) in section 34(3) the words from “and section 22” onwards.
