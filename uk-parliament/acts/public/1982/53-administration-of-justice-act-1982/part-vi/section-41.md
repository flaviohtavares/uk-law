# Administration of Justice Act 1982, section 41

> Text as at 2023-07-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Transfer of funds in court to Official Custodian for Charities and appropriate authority.[^c20823711]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any funds for the time being vested in the Accountant General and held by him in trust for any charity or in trust for any ecclesiastical corporation in the Church of England may, if the Accountant General on an application made in that behalf to him by the Charity Commission[^c20827861] or the appropriate authority thinks fit so to direct, be transferred to the Official Custodian for Charities or the appropriate authority, as the case may be.

(2) Any funds transferred by virtue of a direction given under subsection (1) above shall be vested in and held by the Official Custodian for Charities or the appropriate authority respectively in trust for the charity or ecclesiastical corporation upon the trusts upon which the funds were held before the transfer.

(3)[^c2571561] In this section “**ecclesiastical corporation**” means a capitular body within the meaning of the [^c2571571]Cathedrals Measure 1963 or the incumbent of a benefice and “[^c20823771]**appropriate authority**” means, in the case of funds held in trust for a cathedral, the corporate body of that cathedral established under section 9(1)(a) of the Cathedrals Measure 1999 and in the case of funds held in trust for a benefice the Diocesan Board of Finance for the diocese in which that benefice is situated.

(4)[^c-563f028b022d07b08bd0dca60071d4ab] The preceding provisions of this section cease to apply to the case of funds held in trust for a cathedral on the commencement in relation to that cathedral of the charity provisions within the meaning of section 53(7) of the Cathedrals Measure 2021
