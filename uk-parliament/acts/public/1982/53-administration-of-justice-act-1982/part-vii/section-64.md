# Administration of Justice Act 1982, section 64

> Text as at 2023-07-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Temporary vacancies in Law Commission.[^c2572401]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

In section 1(1) of the [^c2572411]Law Commissions Act 1965 (by virtue of which the Law Commission consists of a Chairman and four other Commissioners) after the word “consisting” there shall be inserted the words “ (except during any temporary vacancy) ”.
