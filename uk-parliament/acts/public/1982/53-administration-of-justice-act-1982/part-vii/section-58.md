# Administration of Justice Act 1982, section 58

> Text as at 2023-07-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Assistance by Recorders for transaction of business of High Court.[^c2572271]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

At the end of the Table in section 9(1) of the [^c2572281]Senior Courts Act 1981[^c20828101](under which certain persons may be requested to act as judges in specified courts) there shall be added— “ 6. A Recorder. The High Court. ”.
