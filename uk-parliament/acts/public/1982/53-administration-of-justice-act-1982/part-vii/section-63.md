# Administration of Justice Act 1982, section 63

> Text as at 2023-07-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Consents to prosecutions under the Explosive Substances Act 1883.[^c2572381]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following subsection shall be substituted for section 7(1) of the [^c2572391]Explosive Substances Act 1883—

> (1) Proceedings for a crime under this Act shall not be instituted except by or with the consent of the Attorney General.

.

(2) In section 9(2) of that Act (application to Scotland) the following paragraph shall be inserted before the paragraph relating to the expression “Attorney General”—

“ Section 7(1) shall be omitted. ”
