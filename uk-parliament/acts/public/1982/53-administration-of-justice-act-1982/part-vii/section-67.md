## 67. Jurisdiction.[^c2572471]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Land Registration Act 1925 shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in Schedule 5 to this Act.

(2) The [^c2572481]Land Registration Rules 1967 are revoked.
