# Administration of Justice Act 1982, section 69

> Text as at 2023-07-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Interest on debts and damages.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c2572491] The section set out in Part I of Schedule 7 to this Act shall be inserted after section 33 of the [^c2572501]Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978.

(2)[^c2572491] The Article set out in Part II of that Schedule shall be inserted after Article 45 of the [^c2572521]County Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1980.

(3) The enactments specified in Part III of that Schedule shall have effect subject to the amendments there specified being amendments consequential on subsections (1) and (2) above.
