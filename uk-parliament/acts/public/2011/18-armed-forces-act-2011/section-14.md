# Armed Forces Act 2011, section 14

> Text as at 2019-07-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Court Martial sentencing powers[^c-d34312593ca909f44168882c7440de1d]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For section 165 of AFA 2006 substitute—

> **165. Sentencing powers of Court Martial where election for trial by that court instead of CO**
>
> Schedule 3A (sentencing powers of Court Martial where election for trial by that court instead of CO) has effect.

(2) After Schedule 3 to AFA 2006 insert the Schedule set out in Schedule 1.
