# Energy Act 2011, section 37

> Text as at 2022-12-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Preparatory expenditure: framework regulations

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Secretary of State may, before the framework regulations are made, incur expenditure for the purpose of, or in connection with, preparing for a scheme of the kind provided for by section 3.
