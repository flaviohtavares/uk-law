# Finance Act 2011, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/11
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Charge and main rates for 2011-12

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Income tax is charged for the tax year 2011-12.

(2) For that tax year—

- (a) the basic rate is 20%,
- (b) the higher rate is 40%, and
- (c) the additional rate is 50%.
