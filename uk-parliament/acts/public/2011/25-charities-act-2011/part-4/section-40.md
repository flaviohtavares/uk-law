# Charities Act 2011, section 40

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Power to alter sum specified in s.39(1)

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State[^c-906ba6679c7cdbb665894b34c2c987ef] may by order amend section 39(1) by substituting a different sum for the sum for the time being specified there.
