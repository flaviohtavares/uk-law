# Charities Act 2011, section 33

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 33. Power to repeal provisions relating to excepted charities[^c-b3d11263c7517094c51fed742720ddec]

*Extent: E+W*

The following provisions—

- (a) section 30(2)(b) and (c) and (3)(a),
- (b) section 31,
- (c) section 32(1)(a), (2)(a) and (3), and
- (d) this section,

cease to have effect on such day as the Secretary of State[^c-c6a7fd605e8cdbd34952a40ecf24cfb4] may by order appoint for the purposes of this section.
