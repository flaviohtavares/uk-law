# Charities Act 2011, section 350

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 350. Connected person: child, spouse and civil partner

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In sections 118(2)(c), 188(1)(a), 200(1)(a) , 249(2)(a) and 280B(3)(a)[^c-ce28393cfa4beae11a56eb3d488c29ee], “**child**” includes a stepchild [^c-d1711f8847dc60ab83527fbcf33d40a9]....

(2)[^c-633f3caad1bee3da1a66515a14c2563f] For the purposes of sections 118(2)(e), 188(1)(b), 200(1)(b) , 249(2)(b) and 280B(3)(b)[^c-e97509d37c5e93b74ee2cb8d497c30a1], where two people are not married to, or civil partners of, each other but live together as if they were a married couple or civil partners, each of them is to be treated as the spouse or civil partner of the other.
