# Charities Act 2011, section 348

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 348. Regulations subject to affirmative procedure etc.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 347(2) (negative procedure) does not apply to—

- (a) regulations under section 19 (fees and other amounts payable to Commission) which require the payment of a fee in respect of any matter for which no fee was previously payable;
- (b) regulations under section 25 (meaning of “principal regulator”) which amend any provision of an Act;
- (ba)[^c-b2021bb99b96ad69df102d90216da29a] regulations under section 178A(4) (offences specified for automatic disqualification of charity trustees);
- (ba)[^c-f0a850085051cbda32e99d944c3e3f34] regulations under section 181A(8) (conditions for disqualification by order);
- (c) regulations under section 245 (regulations about winding up, insolvency and dissolution of CIOs).
- (d)[^c-df0aff9d8661d8e458d8c6f4e7f2eb29] regulations under section 285(3) (power to amend period or multiplier specified);
- (e)[^c-f67cc45a6d62a6ef2f471f87d6d55105] regulations under section 352A (power to amend definition of “connected person”).

(2) No regulations within subsection (1)(a) , (ba)[^c-7f5ef3d5f2d2235074edbf749e8a7482] , (bb)[^c-bc7b663bad25557b4c9b13e95008d4d1] , (c), (d)[^c-676e5c2f9d927e8b5569c6df1a103a8e] or (e)[^c-7fd6d09e096f581c24c607dbba5dcd6d] may be made unless a draft of the regulations has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

(3) No regulations within subsection (1)(b) may be made (whether alone or with other provisions) unless a draft of the regulations has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

(4)[^c-f7d7d58f18354480c064937e5c5d4029] Before making any regulations under—

- (za)[^c-d0a414633d610f61365162a4aba5af8d] section 178A(4), if the regulations add an offence,
- (zb)[^c-adb4d6e2e512eb3261ce12faaee81837] section 181A(8), if the regulations add a condition,
- (a) Part 8 (charity accounts, reports and returns), or
- (b) section 245 or 246 (certain powers to make regulations about CIOs),

the Secretary of State[^c-a29431c76fdf317a716ba85bd37fba24] must consult such persons or bodies of persons as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.
