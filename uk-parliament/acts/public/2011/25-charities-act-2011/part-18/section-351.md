# Charities Act 2011, section 351

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 351. Connected person: controlled institution

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of sections 118(2)(g), 157(1)(a), 188(1)(d), 200(1)(d) , 249(2)(d) and 280B(3)(d)[^key-38e5272c8f9742323c751be93d8ed652], a person controls an institution if the person is able to secure that the affairs of the institution are conducted in accordance with the person's wishes.
