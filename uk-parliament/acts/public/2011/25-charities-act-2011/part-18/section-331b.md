# Charities Act 2011, section 331B

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 331B. Power to alter sums specified in s.331A[^key-7bf507ca6cef725c401c11d008dae025]

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State may by regulations amend section 331A(6) (relevant income thresholds) by substituting a different sum for any sum for the time being specified in that provision.
