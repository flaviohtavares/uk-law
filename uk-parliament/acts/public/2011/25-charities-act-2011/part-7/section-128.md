> Text as at 2026-10-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 128. Power to alter sum specified in s.127(2)

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State[^c-20fb18f96e802f4ceb2b52c68b8af541] may by order amend section 127(2) by substituting a different sum for the sum for the time being specified there.
