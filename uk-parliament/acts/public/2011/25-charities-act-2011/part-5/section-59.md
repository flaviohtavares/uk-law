# Charities Act 2011, section 59

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Disclosure: supplementary

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-9eb3eff0810318438f9fc54b9b1aebc5] Nothing in sections 54 to 57 (or in those sections as applied by section 58(1) to (4)) authorises the making of a disclosure which—

- (a) contravenes the data protection legislation[^key-879186ca2169a9448f3e0e7d1bf7125e], or
- (b) is prohibited by any of Parts 1 to 7 or Chapter 1 of Part 9 of the Investigatory Powers Act 2016[^key-6355d262a99d39f098989a56d96aca5d].

(2)[^key-6eebac7b7dbbd913037475ef82f1caa8] In this section, “**the data protection legislation**” has the same meaning as in the Data Protection Act 2018 (see section 3 of that Act).
