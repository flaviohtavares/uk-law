# Charities Act 2011, section 323

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 323. Remission of matters to Commission

*Extent: E+W*

References in column 3 of Schedule 6 to the power to remit a matter to the Commission are to the power to remit the matter—

- (a) generally, or
- (b) for determination in accordance with a finding made or direction given by the Tribunal.
