# Charities Act 2011, section 257

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 257. Requirement to be met before certificate is granted

*Extent: E+W*

Before a certificate of incorporation is granted under this Part, charity trustees of the charity must have been effectually appointed to the satisfaction of the Commission.
