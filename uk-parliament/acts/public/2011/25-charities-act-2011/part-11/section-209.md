# Charities Act 2011, section 209

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 209. Registration of CIO

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the Commission grants an application under section 207 it must register the CIO to which the application relates as a charity in the register of charities.

(2) The entry relating to the charity's registration in the register of charities must include—

- (a) the date of the charity's registration, and
- (b) a note saying that it is constituted as a CIO.

(3) A copy of the entry in the register must be sent to the charity at the principal office of the CIO.
