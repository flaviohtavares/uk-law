# Charities Act 2011, section 215

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 215. Offence of holding out that a body is a CIO

*Extent: E+W*

(1) It is an offence for a person (in whatever way) to hold any body out as being a CIO when it is not.

(2) It is a defence where a person is charged with an offence under subsection (1) to prove that the person believed on reasonable grounds that the body was a CIO.

(3) A person guilty of an offence under subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
