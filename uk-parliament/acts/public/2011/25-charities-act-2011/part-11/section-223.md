# Charities Act 2011, section 223

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 223. Regulations about procedure of CIOs

*Extent: E+W*

(1) CIO regulations may make provision about the procedure of CIOs.

(2) Subject to—

- (a) any such regulations,
- (b) any other requirement imposed by or by virtue of this Act or any other enactment, and
- (c) anything in the CIO's constitution,

a CIO may regulate its own procedure.

(3) But a CIO's procedure must include provision for the holding of a general meeting of its members, and the regulations referred to in subsection (1) may in particular make provision about such meetings.
