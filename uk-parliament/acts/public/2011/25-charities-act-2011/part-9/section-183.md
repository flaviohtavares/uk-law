# Charities Act 2011, section 183

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 183. Criminal consequences of acting while disqualified

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to subsection (2), it is an offence for any person to act as a charity trustee or trustee for a charity or to hold an office or employment[^c-56d8a0d2f3e053acccf9ea83d430ffb9] while disqualified from being such a trustee or from holding that office or employment[^c-05cb8c8838d75422ee929937d58b874f] by virtue of section 178 or an order under section 181A[^c-0957cb624a17b1d10423897ad875f493].

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply if—

- (a) the charity concerned is a company or a CIO[^c-ba844cb91a6885c38b5a2a818cb79701], and
- (b) the disqualified person is disqualified by virtue only of Case B , F or G[^c-1f8602ff27e01864b8895e8e38804d26] in section 178[^c-f78467fea4c456f882d1a4a912817adf].

(3) A person guilty of an offence under subsection (1) is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-77a57bd596e2c90b9772b87d712b5818] or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or both;
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or to a fine, or both.
