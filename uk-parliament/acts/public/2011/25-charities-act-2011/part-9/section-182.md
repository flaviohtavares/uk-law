# Charities Act 2011, section 182

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 182. Records of persons removed from office

*Extent: E+W*

(1) For the purposes of sections 178 to 181A[^c-b9e4cd89b17f6fe7419f0afffb1b9953] the Commission must keep, in such manner as it thinks fit, a register of the following.[^c-eb9eb2574cd2371f715016c4048bdc92]

(1A)[^c-5b07740c95b292ea4ac851d3ca31f533] The register must include all persons who have been removed from office as mentioned in Case D—[^c-da2c40dd8d2b3f59b1d7d45c61afa140]

- (a) by an order of the Commission or the Commissioners made before or after the commencement of section 178(1), or
- (b) by an order of the High Court made after the commencement of section 45(1) of the Charities Act 1992;

and, where any person is so removed from office by an order of the High Court, the court must notify the Commission of the person's removal.

(1B)[^c-ffb863c7f4d96f2d5c28d6f5865f2331] The register must include all persons who have been disqualified by an order of the Commission under section 181A.

(1C) The register must include all persons who have been removed from office by an order of the Commission under section 79A (removal of disqualified trustee).

(2) The entries in the register kept under subsection (1) must be available for public inspection in legible form at all reasonable times.

(3) In this section “**the Commissioners**” means the Charity Commissioners for England and Wales.
