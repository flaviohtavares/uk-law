# Charities Act 2011, section 180

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 180. Disqualification: exceptions in relation to charitable companies

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where (apart from this subsection) a person (“P”) is disqualified under Case B or G[^c-f8e0e5f4bb6bfe67c07f1747df1e0504] from being a charity trustee or trustee for a charitable company or a CIO[^c-aec0ae9baeb97c3ac5ff013482f2cb99], P is not so disqualified if leave has been granted under section 11 of the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986 (undischarged bankrupts) for P to act as director of the company or charity trustee of the CIO (as the case may be)[^c-e42b7d2747d3d9f2858dcf5eb70588af].

(2) Similarly, a person (“P”) is not disqualified under Case F from being a charity trustee or trustee for a charitable company or a CIO[^c-2bd7c3d255f06d23b3a8b36f3e336664] if, in a case set out in the first column of the table, leave has been granted as mentioned in the second column for P to act as director of the company or charity trustee of the CIO (as the case may be)[^c-bdec2a11b973d1d7235845d31332ac3d]—

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| P is subject to a disqualification order or disqualification undertaking under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986. | Leave has been granted for the purposes of section 1(1)(a) or 1A(1)(a) of the 1986 Act. |
| P is subject to a disqualification order or disqualification undertaking under the Company Directors Disqualification (Northern Ireland) Order 2002 (S.I. 2002/3150 (N.I.4)). | Leave has been granted by the High Court in Northern Ireland. |
| P is subject to an order under section 429(2) of the Insolvency Act 1986. | Leave has been granted by the court which made the order. |
