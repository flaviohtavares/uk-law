# Charities Act 2011, section 199

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 199. Meaning of “benefit” in s.198(2)

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of section 198(2)(c) “**benefit**” means a direct or indirect benefit of any nature, except that it does not include—[^c-322e11a9561d879bf5fb6db7928ff174]

- (a) any remuneration whose receipt may be authorised under section 185, or
- (b) the purchase of any insurance which may be authorised under section 189.
