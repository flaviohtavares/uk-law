# Charities Act 2011, section 193

> Text as at 2026-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 193. Meaning of “charitable company”

*Extent: E+W*

In this Act “**charitable company**” means a charity which is a company.
