> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. No indemnification for civil penalties[^c2114371][^key-f8599e759c71d3ada039275c4b4f2915]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 256 of the Pensions Act 2004 (no indemnification for fines or civil penalties) in subsection (1)(b)—

- (a) for “or section” substitute “ , section ”;
- (b) after “1993 (c. 48)” insert “ or section 40 or 41 of the Pensions Act 2008 ”.
