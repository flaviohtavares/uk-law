# Pensions Act 2011, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2023-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Power to make further provision

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may by regulations amend for any purpose—[^key-9b5b831eb3c0522ed71650b846b4cf86]

- (a) the definition of “money purchase benefit” in the Pension Schemes Act 1993, the Pensions Act 2008 or Schedule 10A to the Building Societies Act 1986 , or[^key-c8bf1dc407ae49e221a22a796b8aaeb7]
- (b) section 2 of the Pension Schemes Act 2021 (collective money purchase benefits: meaning of “qualifying benefits”).

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) may in particular amend the provisions inserted by section 29 above or by Schedule 1 to the Pension Schemes Act 2021[^key-b291d5174c46072e6c31e00bd4778d89].

(3) Regulations under this section may include transitional, consequential or supplementary provision.
