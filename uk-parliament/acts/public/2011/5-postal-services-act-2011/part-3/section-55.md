# Postal Services Act 2011, section 55

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Information[^key-5bfee820ce43b6ee36460f34d6f705bc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 8 makes provision about the provision of information[^key-a5f1e3641960871d689bffa8fb547626] to OFCOM for the purpose of carrying out their functions in relation to postal services or for related purposes.

(2) In that Schedule—

- (a) Part 1 makes provision authorising OFCOM to require information to be provided to them,
- (b) Part 2 makes provision for the enforcement of those requirements [^key-be0ee109763e27f5ee36d1a31a43429b]..., and
- (c) Part 3 contains supplementary provision.

(3) Nothing in Schedule 7 to the Postal Services Act 2000 (disclosure of information) prevents the Postal Services Commission from disclosing information to OFCOM for the purposes of OFCOM's functions in relation to postal services.
