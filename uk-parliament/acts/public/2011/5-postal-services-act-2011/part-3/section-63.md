# Postal Services Act 2011, section 63

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Orders, schemes and regulations made by OFCOM[^key-b7dca8ddda04bdcf49d821434b393547]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 403 of the Communications Act 2003 (regulations and orders made by OFCOM) applies to any power of OFCOM under this Part to make an order, a scheme or regulations.
