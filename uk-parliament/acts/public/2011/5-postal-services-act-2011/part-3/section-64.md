# Postal Services Act 2011, section 64

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Abolition of Postal Services Commission etc

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-d277dc82f7e76695a33d515b28101775] The Postal Services Commission is abolished.

(2)[^c-99700973dbfa78a35832d5aebf680ac6] In section 30 of, and Schedule 2 to, the Communications Act 2003 (transfers of property etc from pre-commencement regulators), each reference to a pre-commencement regulator includes the Postal Services Commission.

(3)[^c-99700973dbfa78a35832d5aebf680ac6] The following provisions apply in relation to a transfer scheme made by virtue of subsection (2).

(4)[^c-99700973dbfa78a35832d5aebf680ac6] The Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) Regulations 2006 apply to a transfer, by virtue of a transfer scheme, of rights and liabilities under a contract of employment (whether or not the transfer would otherwise be a relevant transfer for the purposes of those regulations).

(5)[^c-99700973dbfa78a35832d5aebf680ac6] Where an employee of the Postal Services Commission becomes an employee of OFCOM by virtue of a transfer scheme—

- (a) a period of employment with that Commission is to be treated as a period of employment with OFCOM, and
- (b) the transfer to OFCOM is not to be treated as a break in service.

(6)[^c-99700973dbfa78a35832d5aebf680ac6] The transfer of functions, property, rights or liabilities from the Postal Services Commission to OFCOM under or by virtue of this Act is not to be treated as a merger for accounting purposes.
