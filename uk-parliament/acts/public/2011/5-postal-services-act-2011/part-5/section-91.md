# Postal Services Act 2011, section 91

> Text as at 2025-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Minor and consequential amendments[^c2093182][^key-665bd5e07f99c69b52eb53c9b2d0bc1b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-d44361e7fd44e509fd4672f07282e0b3] Schedule 12 contains minor and consequential amendments (including repeals).

(2)[^key-d44361e7fd44e509fd4672f07282e0b3] In that Schedule—

- Part 1 makes provision in relation to the Postal Services Act 2000,
- Part 2 makes provision in relation to the Communications Act 2003, and
- Part 3 makes provision in relation to other enactments.

(3) The Minister for the Civil Service, the[^key-ec276fe71c100d4e804d1d88dd916a15] Secretary of State or the Treasury may by order make such other provision amending, repealing, revoking or otherwise modifying any enactment as they consider necessary or expedient in consequence of any provision made by this Act.

(4) An order under subsection (3) is subject to negative resolution procedure.
