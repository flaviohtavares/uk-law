# Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011, section 24

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/13
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Police grant

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-c52cfd8e737b5c7c28bfb11dc9c60274] Section 46 of the Police Act 1996 (police grant) is amended as follows.

(2)[^c-249c6b748ff4d80c0a711f72e6a52e24] In subsection (1)—

- (a)[^c-6678b41b729e97cc835a6c952d509a3a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) for the words after paragraph (b) substitute—

  > and in those provisions a reference to a grant recipient is a reference to a police and crime commissioner, the Common Council or the Greater London Authority.

  .

(3)[^c-249c6b748ff4d80c0a711f72e6a52e24] In subsection (2)(b), for “authority” substitute “ grant recipient ”.

(4)[^c-249c6b748ff4d80c0a711f72e6a52e24] In subsection (4), for “police authorities” substitute “ grant recipients ”.

(5)[^c-249c6b748ff4d80c0a711f72e6a52e24] In subsection (5), for “different authorities or different classes of authority” substitute “ different grant recipients or different classes of grant recipient ”.

(6)[^c-249c6b748ff4d80c0a711f72e6a52e24] In subsection (7), for “police authority” substitute “ grant recipient ”.

(7)[^c-c52cfd8e737b5c7c28bfb11dc9c60274] In subsection (7A)—

- (a) for “Metropolitan Police Authority” substitute “ Mayor's Office for Policing and Crime ”;
- (b) for “that Authority” substitute “ that Office ”.

(8)[^c-249c6b748ff4d80c0a711f72e6a52e24] In subsection (8)—

- (a) for “an authority's” substitute “ a grant recipient's ”;
- (b) for “the authority” substitute “ the grant recipient ”;
- (c) for “an authority” substitute “ a grant recipient ”.

(9)[^c-c52cfd8e737b5c7c28bfb11dc9c60274] In subsection (9), for “Metropolitan Police Authority” substitute “ Mayor's Office for Policing and Crime ”.
