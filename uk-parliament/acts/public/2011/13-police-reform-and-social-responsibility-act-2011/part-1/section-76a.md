## 76A. Exercise of PCC functions by elected mayors[^c-e79a0a2d2600b1bf7b19c8e1b1098df7]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 10A makes provision about cases where a mayor is to exercise functions in accordance with—

- (a) section 107F(1A) of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009, or
- (b) section 33(1A) of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023.
