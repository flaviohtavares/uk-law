## 75. The appropriate officer[^c-3a376a619eb9cf0995a8aaf1f394c20d][^c-549b8ed23f32aed0da470fe6c1cf71de]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-a4b781aa5754c65f9e628a77638ed187] For each police area, other than the metropolitan police district, the Secretary of State [^c-6c9bec6ac6590ca616214db35ad2a8a4]... must by order designate a local authority.

(2) In this Chapter, the “**appropriate officer**”, means—[^c-c491368d3b63c9513fcfba945e37914f]

- (a) in relation to a police area in England, the head of paid service of the local authority designated for that police area;
- (b) in relation to a police area in Wales, the chief executive of the local authority designated for that police area.

(3) In this section—

- “ *(Extent: W)*[^c-c8225447ac038b1e468f01c1b26469b0]**chief executive *(Extent: W)***” means the person appointed by a county council or county borough council in Wales under section 54 of the Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021; *(Extent: W)*
- “**local authority**” means—
  - (a) a district council,
  - (b) a county council in England for a county in which there are no district councils,
  - (c) the Council of the Isles of Scilly,
  - (d) a county council or county borough council in Wales;
- “**head of paid service**”, in relation to a council in England *(Extent: W)*[^c-c2ae7ef7ff194fce0376a3312c9e09d0], means the person designated by the council under section 4(1)(a) of the Local Government Act 1989.
