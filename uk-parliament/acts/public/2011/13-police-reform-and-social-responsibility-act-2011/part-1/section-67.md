# Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011, section 67

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/13
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Disqualification of person holding office as police and crime commissioner[^c-14fd6a1dab7910db76f9585b89f5ab3c][^c-0dcf683e5f97f882b10d269fa48f2b67]

*Extent: E+W*

A person becomes disqualified from being a police and crime commissioner upon becoming a member of—

- (a) the House of Commons;
- (b) the Scottish Parliament;
- (c) the National Assembly for Wales;
- (d) the Northern Ireland Assembly;
- (e) the European Parliament.
