# Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/13
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Other grants etc

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-304d2aaada82ec157cac11002abf5744] Section 155 of the Local Government and Housing Act 1989 (emergency financial assistance to local authorities) is amended in accordance with subsections (2) and (3).

(2)[^c-304d2aaada82ec157cac11002abf5744] In subsection (1A) (grants to GLA functional bodies), for paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) the Mayor's Office for Policing and Crime, or

.

(3)[^c-9df6b9805786788207c9a1d6a3600499] In subsection (4) (meaning of local authority), for paragraph (ea) substitute—

> - (ea) a police and crime commissioner;

.

(4)[^c-9df6b9805786788207c9a1d6a3600499] In section 33 of the Local Government Act 2003 (interpretation of Chapter 1 of Part 3: expenditure grant), in subsection (1) (meaning of local authority), for paragraph (m) substitute—

> - (m) a police and crime commissioner.

.
