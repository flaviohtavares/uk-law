# Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/13
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Other grants etc under Police Act 1996

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-eb974c86fbe26e7af14463539b75ef20] The Police Act 1996 is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-eb974c86fbe26e7af14463539b75ef20] In section 47 (grants for capital expenditure)—

- (a) in subsection (1), for the words from “by” to “Authority”, substitute “ by local policing bodies ”;
- (b) in subsection (4), for “by virtue of subsection (1)(b)” substitute “ under subsection (1) in respect of expenditure incurred (or to be incurred) by the Mayor's Office for Policing and Crime ”;
- (c) in subsection (5)—
  - (i) for “Metropolitan Police Authority” substitute “ Mayor's Office for Policing and Crime ”;
  - (ii) for “that Authority” substitute “ that Office ”.

(3)[^key-eb974c86fbe26e7af14463539b75ef20] In section 48 (grants for expenditure on safeguarding national security)—

- (a) in subsection (1), for the words from “by” to “security”, substitute “ by local policing bodies in connection with safeguarding national security ”;
- (b) in subsection (4), for “by virtue of subsection (1)(b)” substitute “ under subsection (1) in respect of expenditure incurred (or to be incurred) by the Mayor's Office for Policing and Crime ”;
- (c) in subsection (5)—
  - (i) for “Metropolitan Police Authority” substitute “ Mayor's Office for Policing and Crime ”;
  - (ii) for “that Authority” substitute “ that Office ”.

(4) In section 92 (grants by local authorities)—

- (a)[^key-dcd2b508d93976659177556a673e8639] in subsection (1), for “police authority established under section 3” substitute “ police and crime commissioner ”;
- (b)[^key-eb974c86fbe26e7af14463539b75ef20] in subsection (2), for “Metropolitan Police Authority” substitute “ Mayor's Office for Policing and Crime ”.

(5)[^key-eb974c86fbe26e7af14463539b75ef20] In section 93 (acceptance of gifts or loans)—

- (a) in subsection (1), for “police authority” substitute “ local policing body ”;
- (b) in subsection (2), for “police authority” substitute “ local policing body ”.

(6)[^key-dcd2b508d93976659177556a673e8639] Section 94 (financing of new police authorities) is amended as follows.

(7)[^key-dcd2b508d93976659177556a673e8639] In the title, for “**police authorities**” substitute “ **police and crime commissioners** ”.

(8)[^key-dcd2b508d93976659177556a673e8639] In subsection (1)—

- (a) for “police authority established under section 3” substitute “ police and crime commissioner ”;
- (b) for “it” substitute “ the commissioner ”.

(9)[^key-dcd2b508d93976659177556a673e8639] In subsection (2)—

- (a) for “police authority established under section 3” substitute “ police and crime commissioner ”;
- (b) for “it” substitute “ the commissioner ”;
- (c) for “its” (in both places) substitute “ the commissioner's ”.

(10)[^key-dcd2b508d93976659177556a673e8639] In subsection (3)—

- (a) for “an authority” substitute “ a commissioner ”;
- (b) for “its” substitute “ the commissioner's ”.

(11)[^key-dcd2b508d93976659177556a673e8639] In subsection (4)—

- (a) for “a police authority” substitute “ a police and crime commissioner ”;
- (b) for “it” (in both places) substitute “ the commissioner ”.
