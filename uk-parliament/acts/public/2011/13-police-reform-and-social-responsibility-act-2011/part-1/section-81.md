# Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011, section 81

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/13
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Abolition of certain powers of Secretary of State

*Extent: E+W*

In the Police Act 1996, omit—

- (a)[^key-394d4d342dbef7ac136784374581ac92] section 38 (performance targets for police strategic priorities);
- (b)[^key-755547020d9207ff1bbc4152c8c4718a] section 39 (codes of practice for police authorities);
- (c)[^key-755547020d9207ff1bbc4152c8c4718a] section 43 (reports from police authorities to Secretary of State).
