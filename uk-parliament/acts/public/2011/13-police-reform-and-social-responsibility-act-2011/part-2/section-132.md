# Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011, section 132

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/13
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 132. Introduction of late night levy requirement[^key-caafc817bbadd56f6bbec5361f3c4578]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a licensing authority decides under section 125 that a late night levy requirement[^key-78a85b992f99226e8ff6ee67bd97b5c9] is to apply [^key-61846cdfe09b732bb3490785b1b543d5]..., it must also decide—

- (a) the date on which the late night levy requirement is first to apply, and
- (b) for the first levy year and, subject to section 133, each subsequent levy year—
  - (i) the late night supply period or periods (as to which see section 126(3A))[^key-b31d84263038314d9687078011e5485f];
  - (ii) the permitted exemption categories (if any) that are to apply [^key-4d6a4fed7b59358bd742a36243c601e7]...;
  - (iii) the permitted reduction categories (if any) that are to apply [^key-19f9bd54757d8d0ad3a880995596ed96]...;
  - (iv) the proportion of the net amount of levy payments that is to be paid to the relevant local policing body under section 131.

(2) Subsection (1)(b)(i) is subject to section 126(4) and (5).

(3) Subsection (1)(b)(iv) is subject to section 131(4).

(4) Regulations under section 134 apply in relation to any decision of a licensing authority under section 125 or this section.
