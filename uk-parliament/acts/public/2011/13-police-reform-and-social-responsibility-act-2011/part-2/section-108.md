# Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011, section 108

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-09-21. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/13
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 108. Club premises certificates: who may apply for review

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-013b7e4b1b1dbca6824e27510ff5b378][^c-022b582618e93eb96eb370063a4bcb17] The Licensing Act 2003 is amended as set out in subsections (2) to (4).

(2)[^c-a1c3cbc8286cda6528d30fc7a2ff5418] In section 87 (application for review of club premises certificate), for subsection (1) substitute—

> (1) Where a club holds a club premises certificate, a responsible authority or any other person may apply to the relevant licensing authority for a review of the certificate.

.

(3)[^c-013b7e4b1b1dbca6824e27510ff5b378][^c-022b582618e93eb96eb370063a4bcb17] In section 87(3) (application for review of club premises certificate)—

- (a) in paragraph (b), after “to the authority” insert “ by the club, responsible authorities and other persons ”, and
- (b) in paragraph (c), for “interested party” substitute “ other person ”.

(4)[^c-a1c3cbc8286cda6528d30fc7a2ff5418] In section 88(8) (determination of application for review of club premises certificate)—

- (a) in paragraph (a), for “an interested party” substitute “ any other person ”, and
- (b) in paragraph (c), for “an interested party (who is not also a responsible authority)” substitute “ a person who is not a responsible authority ”.

(5)[^c-a1c3cbc8286cda6528d30fc7a2ff5418] The amendments made by this section apply in relation to applications for review that are made on or after the commencement of this section.
