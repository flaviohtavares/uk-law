# Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011, section 146

## 146. Power of court on conviction[^c-b5116cb62c95143ac4c635c1a183f18c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The court may do either or both of the following on the conviction of a person (“P”) of an offence under section 143—

- (a) make an order providing for the forfeiture of any item of a kind mentioned in subsection (2) of that section that was used in the commission of the offence;
- (b) make such other order as the court considers appropriate for the purpose of preventing P from engaging in any prohibited activity in a relevant area[^c-cd8cec379e7b95b496138cb1ec348f10].

(2) An order under subsection (1)(b) may (in particular) require P not to enter a relevant area[^c-ad71241ad21e1d67cc741a8136cab7bd] for such period as may be specified in the order.

(2A)[^c-7b54bf584f30157f63dfc832d1be0d2f] In this section “**relevant area**” means an area consisting of either or both of the following areas—

- (a) the controlled area of Parliament Square , and
- (b) the Palace of Westminster controlled area.

(3) Power of the court to make an order under this section is in addition to the court's power to impose a fine under section 143(8).
