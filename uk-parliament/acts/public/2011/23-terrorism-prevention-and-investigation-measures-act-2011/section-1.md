# Terrorism Prevention and Investigation Measures Act 2011, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/23

## 1. Abolition of control orders

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Prevention of Terrorism Act 2005 (which gives powers to impose control orders) is repealed.
