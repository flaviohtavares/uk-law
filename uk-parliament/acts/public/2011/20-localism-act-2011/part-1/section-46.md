## 46. Repeal of provisions about petitions to local authorities[^c-15063f4ea42414ac6cbc9df85ae31229][^c-cac66c15a412089ad697588b48c063ff]

*Extent: E+W*

Chapter 2 of Part 1 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009 (petitions to local authorities) is repealed.
