# Localism Act 2011, section 4

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 4. Limits on doing things for commercial purpose in exercise of general power[^c-1f2d3a62be961c57dc155a6a2e9f7b93]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The general power confers power on a local authority , combined authority, CCA[^c-8f9518f6e3f55cbf939561421c5a82be] , or English National Park authority, or the Broads Authority,[^c-537c550e5e5149257be260b02d5f4a58] to do things for a commercial purpose only if they are things which the authority may, in exercise of the general power, do otherwise than for a commercial purpose.

(2) Where, in exercise of the general power, a local authority , combined authority, CCA[^c-a8f6cb3f2d24c77823ea27bbc01e32c4] , or English National Park authority, or the Broads Authority,[^c-0375c8d2f9dfd839872614d7e42279b5] does things for a commercial purpose, the authority must do them through a company.

(3) A local authority , combined authority, CCA[^c-1c3ffdc7092bd96ae0a177f7ddad5835] , or English National Park authority, or the Broads Authority,[^c-b6d038acfafc0f693b90dca7505fe7c3] may not, in exercise of the general power, do things for a commercial purpose in relation to a person if a statutory provision requires the authority to do those things in relation to the person.

(4) In this section “**company**” means—

- (a) a company within the meaning given by section 1(1) of the Companies Act 2006, or
- (b) a registered society within the meaning of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014 or a society registered or deemed to be registered under[^c-eb69892a02d36a2440a261710c7f940e] the Industrial and Provident Societies Act (Northern Ireland) 1969.
