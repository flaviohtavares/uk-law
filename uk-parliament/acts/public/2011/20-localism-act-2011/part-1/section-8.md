# Localism Act 2011, section 8

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Interpretation of Chapter

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-a2346f61979b7d856c2fe670ded66545] In this Chapter—

- “[^c-a123cc5e9ece302be8b5a986c9c32530]**CCA**” means a combined county authority established under section 9(1) of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023;
- “**combined authority**” means a combined authority established under section 103 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009;
- “[^c-0c7b126802005a07fcd64a4cd1cf302b]**English National Park authority**” means a National Park authority for a National Park in England;
- “**the general power**” means the power conferred by section 1(1);
- “**local authority**” means—
  - (a) a county council in England,
  - (b) a district council,
  - (c) a London borough council,
  - (d) the Common Council of the City of London in its capacity as a local authority,
  - (e) the Council of the Isles of Scilly, or
  - (f) an eligible parish council;
- “[^M_F_0fcdd154-2c07-46e1-ed51-fcd111903d89]**mayor**”, in relation to a mayoral combined authority or mayoral CCA, means the mayor for the area of that authority;
- “**mayoral CCA**” has the same meaning as in Chapter 1 of Part 1 of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023 — see section 57 of that Act;
- “**mayoral combined authority**” has the same meaning as in Part 6 of the Local Democracy, Economic Development and Construction Act 2009 — see section 120 of that Act;
- “**statutory provision**” means a provision of an Act or of an instrument made under an Act.

(2)[^c-9e87f043c4099f39a07c55b0e33534bd] A parish council is “**eligible**” for the purposes of this Chapter if the council meets the conditions prescribed by the Secretary of State by order for the purposes of this section.
