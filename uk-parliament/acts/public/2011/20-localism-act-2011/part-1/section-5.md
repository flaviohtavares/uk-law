# Localism Act 2011, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (48).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 5. Powers to make supplemental provision[^c-d2951ee1fcb2002d681963f6e7983718]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If the Secretary of State thinks that a statutory provision (whenever passed or made) prevents or restricts local authorities , combined authorities, CCAs[^c-ffabbaee4ed6f11b527629ebbf53f715] , or English National Park authorities, or the Broads Authority,[^c-24e8343695b8470bcaf070231536dd27] from exercising the general power, the Secretary of State may by order amend, repeal, revoke or disapply that provision.

(2) If the Secretary of State thinks that the general power is overlapped (to any extent) by another power then, for the purpose of removing or reducing that overlap, the Secretary of State may by order amend, repeal, revoke or disapply any statutory provision (whenever passed or made).

(3) The Secretary of State may by order make provision preventing local authorities , combined authorities, CCAs[^c-3dab3f6acc157ddded59e0f216e0948b] , or English National Park authorities, or the Broads Authority,[^c-2f4b3e5ef53bde4729f64a113bf42186] from doing, in exercise of the general power, anything which is specified, or is of a description specified, in the order.

(4) The Secretary of State may by order provide for the exercise of the general power by local authorities , combined authorities, CCAs[^c-cba13dc049760b08eff6126854d7727d] , or English National Park authorities, or the Broads Authority,[^c-214793d7b400b2241b9f0ed2aa1a2072] to be subject to conditions, whether generally or in relation to doing anything specified, or of a description specified, in the order.

(5) The power under subsection (1), (2), (3) or (4) may be exercised in relation to—

- (a) all local authorities , combined authorities, CCAs[^c-b2754f3ec0f2b890d0f5d1d6607f60e1] , or English National Park authorities[^c-56c4b5cb405b1fb6c5a6111d563f2d2e],
- (b) particular local authorities , combined authorities, CCAs[^c-644239cec7c8b8a33fa2b8d3c9b0080e] , or English National Park authorities[^c-b54b1d2a331855a4d36291aa4023a529], or
- (c) particular descriptions of local authority , combined authority, CCA[^c-b02dd8626e486b7c44ed100e82a9152b] , or English National Park authority[^c-c947a4af88001b151f15e49288b43320].

(6) The power under subsection (1) or (2) to amend or disapply a statutory provision includes power to amend or disapply a statutory provision for a particular period.

(7) Before making an order under subsection (1), (2), (3) or (4) the Secretary of State must consult—

- (a) such local authorities , combined authorities, CCAs[^c-d69a7080d72e98cce926037490c57d73] , or English National Park authorities[^c-08ff05933194eb592735c69699de32fc],
- (b) such representatives of local government, and
- (c) such other persons (if any),

as the Secretary of State considers appropriate.

(7A)[^c-b7cfa79d240a550bc82542d0fd2faead] Before making an order under subsection (1), (2), (3) or (4) in relation to the Broads Authority, the Secretary of State must consult the Authority if the Secretary of State considers it appropriate.

(8) Before making an order under subsection (1) that has effect in relation to Wales, the Secretary of State must consult the Welsh Ministers.
