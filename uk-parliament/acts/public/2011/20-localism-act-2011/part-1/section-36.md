# Localism Act 2011, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (48).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Amendment of section 27 following abolition of police authorities[^c-58cdb5c29ad7d390ecb9fb56e69dbc84][^c-c0ace28f03994219e94a7009a419888f]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 27(6) (which defines “**relevant authority**” for the purposes of this Chapter) omit—

- (a) paragraph (f) (the Metropolitan Police Authority), and
- (b) paragraph (k) (police authorities).
