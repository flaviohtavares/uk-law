# Localism Act 2011, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (48).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 42. Exercise of functions[^c2116563][^c-b58067a373012aa40f479ffae21bf226]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-3069f459a7cbb5417058cc4ba7b21ab0][^c-450ea5bf6ebbdac5560bbf8a46563e5b] The functions conferred on a relevant authority by this Chapter are not to be the responsibility of an executive of the authority under executive arrangements.

(2)[^c-3069f459a7cbb5417058cc4ba7b21ab0][^c-450ea5bf6ebbdac5560bbf8a46563e5b] Section 101 of the Local Government Act 1972 (arrangements for discharge of functions by local authorities) does not apply to the function of passing a resolution under this Chapter.

(2A)[^c-65474c4526a6d703c3af7c0a31d1adcd] In the case of a relevant authority which is a corporate joint committee, regulation 13 of the Corporate Joint Committees (General) (No. 2) (Wales) Regulations 2021 (arrangements for the discharge of functions) does not apply to the function of passing a resolution under this Chapter.

(3) The function of a fire and rescue authority within section 43(1)(i) of passing a resolution under this Chapter may not be delegated by the authority.
