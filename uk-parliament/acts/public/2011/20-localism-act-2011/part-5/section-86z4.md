## 86Z4. Meaning of “local authority”[^M_F_cfd61e8c-7bd9-4ac0-ed6a-a99e40d73d62]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Chapter “**local authority**” means—

- (a) a district council,
- (b) a county council for an area in England for which there are no district councils,
- (c) a London borough council,
- (d) the Common Council of the City of London, or
- (e) the Council of the Isles of Scilly.

(2) The Secretary of State may by regulations amend this section for the purpose of changing the meaning in this Chapter of “**local authority**” in relation to England.
