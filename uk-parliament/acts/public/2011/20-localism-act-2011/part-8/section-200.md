## 200. Transfers of property etc to a Mayoral development corporation[^c-3375e98e1835a6eefd480721b74ed6ec][^c-f91fb68bfa381e4512559dd53f5fda40][^c-eada4f88f4769f792731d2fc31c590a2][^c-ff9972770fbc68d9022c1923bea95cf9][^c-250b381cc6b3a829195738e959b9cac8][^c-3375e98e1835a6eefd480721b74ed6ec]

*Extent: E+W*

[^c-eada4f88f4769f792731d2fc31c590a2][^c-250b381cc6b3a829195738e959b9cac8][^c-1f33aaa1bfaaf2324ee23081679fe3cb][^c-548922647a36fd8f18f9c9731a0d9de8]

(1) The Secretary of State may at any time make a scheme transferring to an MDC property, rights and liabilities of an eligible transferor[^M_F_8a1a0862-8b2e-4f34-ed7e-e16d1f43d899].

(1A)[^M_F_970b1a7e-6911-47e5-cdfc-8d3e2ad69970] In the case of an MDC for an area in Greater London, “**eligible transferor**” means—

- (a) a London borough council,
- (b) the Common Council of the City of London in its capacity as a local authority,
- (c) any company whose members—
  - (i) include the Mayor of London and a Minister of the Crown, and
  - (ii) do not include anyone who is neither the Mayor or London nor a Minister of the Crown, or
- (d) a person within subsection (3).

(1B) In the case of an MDC for an area in the area of a combined authority, “**eligible transferor**” means a person within subsection (3).

(1C) In the case of an MDC for an area in the area of a CCA, “**eligible transferor**” means—

- (a) any district council whose local authority area is within the area of the CCA, or
- (b) a person within subsection (3).

(2) Before making a scheme under subsection (1), the Secretary of State must consult—

- (a) the person whose property, rights or liabilities would be transferred, and
- (b) the Mayor.

(3) A person is within this subsection if the person is—

- (a)[^M_F_813b6d13-76ac-4a8d-b110-18c59a96e145] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^M_F_813b6d13-76ac-4a8d-b110-18c59a96e145] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) the Homes and Communities Agency,
- (d) a development corporation established under the New Towns Act 1981 for a new town all or part of whose area is in the strategic authority area[^M_F_74bec24c-88f1-44d3-d87a-60c363c479ce],
- (e) an urban development corporation for an urban development area all or part of which is in the strategic authority area[^M_F_282ebe21-4dda-4bee-a893-0dac37a3ee0b],
- (f)[^c-dc5daf9898e022a322c50d81ac05eebb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g)[^c-dc5daf9898e022a322c50d81ac05eebb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (h)[^c-dc5daf9898e022a322c50d81ac05eebb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (i) a Minister of the Crown or a government department,
- (j) any company all the shares in which are held by a Minister of the Crown, or
- (k)[^M_F_2b69414f-2dba-4a2f-d9cb-8ecd58a08f96] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The Mayor may at any time make a scheme transferring to an MDC property, rights and liabilities of an eligible transferee.[^M_F_6bee95f0-024a-4bac-ba30-f1a4dbfc2a91]

(4A) In the case of an MDC for an area in Greater London, “eligible transferee” means—

- (a) the Greater London Authority,
- (b) a functional body other than that MDC, or
- (c) a company that is a subsidiary of the Greater London Authority.

(4A)[^M_F_cc6c9e4c-0cca-48cd-e9dc-a91d349bac13] In the case of an MDC for an area in the area of a combined authority or CCA, “**eligible transferee**” means—

- (a) the combined authority or CCA, or
- (b) a company that is a subsidiary of the combined authority or CCA.

(5) The Mayor must publish a scheme under subsection (4) as soon after it is made as is reasonably practicable.

(6) The Secretary of State may by order specify another person, or a description of other persons, from whom property, rights or liabilities may be transferred under subsection (1) or (4).

(7)[^c-4692cadeea90b746530536b16e41bf3d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8)[^c-4692cadeea90b746530536b16e41bf3d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9) In subsection (4)(c) or (4A)(b)[^M_F_02d54f1f-13e3-4afd-bcb7-b8bdf2a5865c]—

- “**company**” means—
  - (a) a company within the meaning given by section 1(1) of the Companies Act 2006, or
  - (b) a registered society within the meaning of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014 or a society registered or deemed to be registered under[^c-45fa0bfab6ddeece5ff9e1eb3f18c253] the Industrial and Provident Societies Act (Northern Ireland) 1969, and
- “**subsidiary**” has the meaning given by section 1159 of the Companies Act 2006.

(10) In this section—

- “**functional body**” has the meaning given by section 424(1) of the Greater London Authority Act 1999;
- “**Minister of the Crown**” has the same meaning as in the Ministers of the Crown Act 1975;
- “**public authority**” means a public body or a Minister of the Crown or other holder of a public office;
- “**urban development corporation**” means a corporation established by an order under section 135 of the Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980.
