# Localism Act 2011, section 191

## 191. Abolition of London Development Agency and transfer of its property etc

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-506b27c1c8081bf112d4dc7e89995be4] The London Development Agency ceases to exist on the day on which this subsection comes into force.

(2)[^c-33517c4a05a6f50467496c545be9dcd7] The Secretary of State may at any time make a scheme (a “transfer scheme”) transferring the property, rights and liabilities of the London Development Agency that are specified in the scheme to—

- (a) the Greater London Authority,
- (b) a functional body,
- (c) a company that is a subsidiary of the Greater London Authority,
- (d) the Secretary of State,
- (e) a London borough council, or
- (f) the Common Council of the City of London.

(3)[^c-33517c4a05a6f50467496c545be9dcd7] Before making a transfer scheme, the Secretary of State must consult the Mayor of London.

(4)[^c-33517c4a05a6f50467496c545be9dcd7] The Secretary of State may by order specify another person, or a description of other persons, to whom property, rights or liabilities of the London Development Agency may be transferred by a transfer scheme.

(5)[^c-33517c4a05a6f50467496c545be9dcd7] In this section—

- “**company**” means—
  - (a) a company within the meaning given by section 1(1) of the Companies Act 2006, or
  - (b) a registered society within the meaning of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014 or a society registered or deemed to be registered under[^c-5b44db532715f4959324bec4ec0e9a0c] the Industrial and Provident Societies Act (Northern Ireland) 1969;
- “**functional body**” has the meaning given by section 424(1) of the Greater London Authority Act 1999;
- “rights” and “liabilities” include rights, or (as the case may be) liabilities, in relation to a contract of employment;
- “**subsidiary**” has the meaning given by section 1159 of the Companies Act 2006.
