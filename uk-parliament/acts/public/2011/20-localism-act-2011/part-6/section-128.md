# Localism Act 2011, section 128

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (48).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 128. Abolition of Infrastructure Planning Commission

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-09662d9005f23d875bc5432d848ad12d] The Infrastructure Planning Commission ceases to exist on the day on which this subsection comes into force.

(2)[^c-7026d1b94a85f1bbb239d11603c6c91f][^c-617bde2ca46af27c1c04a74aaaee8d0c] Schedule 13 (amendments in consequence of Commission's abolition, including amendments transferring its functions to Secretary of State) has effect.

(3)[^c-09662d9005f23d875bc5432d848ad12d] On the coming into force of this subsection, the property, rights and liabilities of the Infrastructure Planning Commission vest by virtue of this subsection in the Secretary of State.

(4)[^c-09662d9005f23d875bc5432d848ad12d] Subsection (3) operates in relation to property, rights and liabilities—

- (a) whether or not they would otherwise be capable of being transferred,
- (b) without any instrument or other formality being required, and
- (c) irrespective of any requirement for consent that would otherwise apply.

(5)[^c-09662d9005f23d875bc5432d848ad12d] The transfer by virtue of subsections (2) to (4) is to be treated as a relevant transfer for the purposes of the Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) Regulations 2006 (S.I. 2006/246) if it would not otherwise be a relevant transfer for those purposes.

(6)[^c-09662d9005f23d875bc5432d848ad12d] Subsections (3) and (4) do not affect the operation of those Regulations in relation to that transfer.
