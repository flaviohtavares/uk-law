# Localism Act 2011, section 174

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (48).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 174. Capital receipts from disposal of housing land

*Extent: E+W*

In section 11 of the Local Government Act 2003 (use of capital receipts by a local authority) after subsection (5) insert—

> (6) The Secretary of State and a local authority in England may enter into an agreement with the effect that a requirement imposed under subsection (2)(b) does not apply to, or is modified in its application to, capital receipts of the authority that are specified or described in the agreement.
