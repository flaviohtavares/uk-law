# Localism Act 2011, section 164

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (48).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/20

## 164. Assured shorthold tenancies: notice requirements (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-15bf4e6528866440cd4b1597794f0bea][^key-2e14f6b8e52880c4b95e3932604e6985][^key-2e14f6b8e52880c4b95e3932604e6985]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In section 21 of the Housing Act 1988 (recovery of possession on expiry or termination of assured shorthold tenancy) after subsection (1) insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2e14f6b8e52880c4b95e3932604e6985]

> (1A) Subsection (1B) applies to an assured shorthold tenancy of a dwelling-house in England if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2e14f6b8e52880c4b95e3932604e6985]
>
> - (a) it is a fixed term tenancy for a term certain of not less than two years, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2e14f6b8e52880c4b95e3932604e6985]
> - (b) the landlord is a private registered provider of social housing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2e14f6b8e52880c4b95e3932604e6985]
>
> (1B) The court may not make an order for possession of the dwelling-house let on the tenancy unless the landlord has given to the tenant not less than six months' notice in writing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2e14f6b8e52880c4b95e3932604e6985]
>
> - (a) stating that the landlord does not propose to grant another tenancy on the expiry of the fixed term tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2e14f6b8e52880c4b95e3932604e6985]
> - (b) informing the tenant of how to obtain help or advice about the notice and, in particular, of any obligation of the landlord to provide help or advice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2e14f6b8e52880c4b95e3932604e6985]

(2) The amendments made by this section do not apply in relation to an assured shorthold tenancy that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2e14f6b8e52880c4b95e3932604e6985]

- (a) was granted before the day on which this section comes into force, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2e14f6b8e52880c4b95e3932604e6985]
- (b) came into being by virtue of section 5 of the Housing Act 1988 (periodic tenancy arising on termination of fixed term) on the coming to an end of an assured shorthold tenancy within paragraph (a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-2e14f6b8e52880c4b95e3932604e6985]
