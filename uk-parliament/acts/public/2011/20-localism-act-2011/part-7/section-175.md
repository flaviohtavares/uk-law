## 175. Interpretation[^key-298e18e012ea996e91260dbd888027b9][^key-7b822ef3bd7f6c1139e5ef953eb9a67e]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Chapter “**local housing authority**” has the same meaning as in the Housing Act 1985.
