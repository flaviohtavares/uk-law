## 163. Assured shorthold tenancy following demoted or family intervention tenancy (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6405d6a408f822fced67b11d30455389][^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344][^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In section 20B(2) of the Housing Act 1988 (demoted assured shorthold tenancy lasts for one year unless subsection (3) applies) after “subsection (3) applies” insert *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344] “ , but see section 20C ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344].

(2) After section 20B of the Housing Act 1988 insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]

> **20C. Assured shorthold tenancies following demoted tenancies (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]**
>
> (1) Subsection (2) applies if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
>
> - (a) section 20B applies to an assured shorthold tenancy of a dwelling-house in England (“the demoted tenancy”), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
> - (b) the landlord is a private registered provider of social housing, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
> - (c) the demoted tenancy was created by an order under section 6A made after the coming into force of section 163(2) of the Localism Act 2011, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
> - (d) the assured tenancy that was terminated by that order was an assured shorthold tenancy that, whether or not it was a fixed term tenancy when terminated by the order, was granted for a term certain of not less than two years, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
> - (e) apart from subsection (2), the demoted tenancy would cease to be an assured shorthold tenancy by virtue of section 20B(2) or (4), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
> - (f) the landlord has served a notice within subsection (3) on the tenant before the demoted tenancy ceases to be an assured shorthold tenancy by virtue of section 20B(2) or (4). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
>
> (2) The demoted tenancy does not cease to be an assured shorthold tenancy by virtue of section 20B(2) or (4), and at the time when it would otherwise cease to be an assured shorthold tenancy by virtue of section 20B(2) to (4)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
>
> - (a) it becomes an assured shorthold tenancy which is a fixed term tenancy for a term certain, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
> - (b) section 20B ceases to apply to it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
>
> (3) The notice must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
>
> - (a) state that, on ceasing to be a demoted assured shorthold tenancy, the tenancy will become an assured shorthold tenancy which is a fixed term tenancy for a term certain of the length specified in the notice, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
> - (b) specify a period of at least two years as the length of the term of the tenancy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
> - (c) set out the other express terms of the tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
>
> (4) Where an assured shorthold tenancy becomes a fixed term tenancy by virtue of subsection (2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
>
> - (a) the length of its term is that specified in the notice under subsection (3), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
> - (b) its other express terms are those set out in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]

(3) Before section 21 of the Housing Act 1988 insert— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]

> **20D. Assured shorthold tenancies following family intervention tenancies (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]**
>
> (1) An assured tenancy that arises by virtue of a notice under paragraph 12ZA(2) of Schedule 1 in respect of a family intervention tenancy is an assured shorthold tenancy if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
>
> - (a) the landlord under the assured tenancy is a private registered provider of social housing, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
> - (b) the dwelling-house is in England, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
> - (c) the family intervention tenancy was granted to a person on the coming to an end of an assured shorthold tenancy under which the person was a tenant, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
> - (d) the notice states that the family intervention tenancy is to be regarded as an assured shorthold tenancy. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
>
> (2) This section does not apply if the family intervention tenancy was granted before the coming into force of section 163(3) of the Localism Act 2011. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa086ff80bf42e813000cbc991e8344]
