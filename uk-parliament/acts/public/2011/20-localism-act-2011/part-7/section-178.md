# Localism Act 2011, section 178

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (48).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 178. Transfer of functions from the Office for Tenants and Social Landlords to the Homes and Communities Agency[^c-e4e7983a5c13f57b77696c36ba808ec0][^c-9e28c960f66cbcc5daeeb7eac2e78453]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Schedule 16 (transfer of functions from the Office for Tenants and Social Landlords to the Homes and Communities Agency) has effect.

(2) In that Schedule—

- Part 1 amends the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 (regulation of social housing) so as to—
  - (a) abolish the Office for Tenants and Social Landlords (“the Office”),
  - (b) create the Regulation Committee of the Homes and Communities Agency (“the HCA”), and
  - (c) transfer the functions of the Office to the HCA acting through the Committee,
- Part 2 makes consequential amendments to other enactments,
- Part 3 contains provision transferring property, rights and liabilities of the Office to the HCA, and
- Part 4 contains transitional and saving provisions.
