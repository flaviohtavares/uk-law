## SCHEDULE 18 — Home information packs: consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W*

Section 183

### Terrorism Act 2000 (c. 11)

**1.** In Schedule 3A to the Terrorism Act 2000 (regulated sector and supervisory authorities) in paragraph 2(1) (excluded activities)—

- (a) at the end of paragraph (d) insert “ or ”, and
- (b) omit paragraph (f).

### Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (c. 29)

**2.** In Schedule 9 to the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (regulated sector and supervisory authorities) in paragraph 2(1) (excluded activities)—

- (a) at the end of paragraph (d) insert “ or ”, and
- (b) omit paragraph (f).

### Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 (c. 17)

**3.** In section 290(2)(e) of the Housing and Regeneration Act 2008 (power to make regulations) omit “made by virtue of Part 5 of the Housing Act 2004 (c. 34) (home information packs) or”.
