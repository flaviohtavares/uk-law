## SCHEDULE 20 — Abolition of London Development Agency: consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W*

Section 195

### Local Authorities (Goods and Services) Act 1970 (c. 39)

**1.**[^key-25dc05bf37470d2bd81a0c5ed50551da] In section 1(4) of the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) Act 1970, in the definition of “local authority” for “, Transport for London and the London Development Agency” substitute “ and Transport for London ”.

### Local Government Finance Act 1988 (c. 41)

**2.**[^key-2b97f5ddfeb80336e5152ee2068001a0] In section 115(4A) of the Local Government Finance Act 1988 (duties as regards reports under section 114)—

- (a) omit “the London Development Agency or”, and
- (b) for the words from “neither” to “shall” substitute “ paragraph 7 of Schedule 10 to the 1999 Act (delegation by Transport for London) shall not ”.

### Audit Commission Act 1998 (c. 18)

**3.**[^key-053cf6cf5c9b0f1f989c979fd1afe7c5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

### Greater London Authority Act 1999 (c. 29)

**4.**[^key-5defd0b9e3b6a9a0a60681482049a792] The Greater London Authority Act 1999 is amended as follows.

**5.**[^key-f39d48544bbb5189397227318ec0420a] In section 38(8) (application of section 101 of the Local Government Act 1972) after paragraph (a) insert “ or ”.

**6.**[^key-19a5bd792552f52fb0519ef1c6639276] In section 127(4) (“officers” in section 127(2)(b) includes, in the case of Transport for London or the London Development Agency, its members) for the words after “in the case of Transport for London” substitute “ , includes a reference to its members. ”

**7.**[^key-11950b6726e13bbd65acc344ed39df79] In section 362(3)(b) (air quality strategy to contain information about measures to be taken by the Authority, Transport for London and the London Development Agency) for “, Transport for London and the London Development Agency” substitute “ and Transport for London ”.

**8.**[^key-51a96f814beb48be2a698205cd671867] In section 380(10) (application of section 101 of the Local Government Act 1972) after paragraph (a) insert “ or ”.

### London Olympic Games and Paralympic Games Act 2006 (c. 12)

**9.**[^key-4f2d7016e3845348f5d69ce76f10ac94] In section 10(4) of the London Olympic Games and Paralympic Games Act 2006 (matters to which regard to be had when Olympic Transport Plan being prepared or revised) for paragraph (c) (the London Development Agency's strategy) substitute—

> - (c) the Economic development strategy for London prepared in accordance with section 333F of that Act.
