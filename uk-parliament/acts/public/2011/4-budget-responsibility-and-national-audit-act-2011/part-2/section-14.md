# Budget Responsibility and National Audit Act 2011, section 14

> Text as at 2026-03-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Resignation or removal[^c-4001643e2dfe3f277a98d136f4a45490]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Comptroller and Auditor General may resign from office by giving written notice to the Prime Minister.

(2) Her Majesty may remove the Comptroller and Auditor General from office on an address of both Houses of Parliament.
