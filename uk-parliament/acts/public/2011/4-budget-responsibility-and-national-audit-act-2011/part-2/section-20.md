# Budget Responsibility and National Audit Act 2011, section 20

> Text as at 2026-03-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Incorporation of NAO

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-1142dfd4d0f3d8e86c548e4d089e3f05] There is to be a body corporate called the National Audit Office (or, in Welsh, Y Swyddfa Archwilio Genedlaethol).

(2)[^c-1142dfd4d0f3d8e86c548e4d089e3f05] In this Part “**NAO**” means the National Audit Office.

(3)[^c-e43cf631fe730727b82a3dc03fef871c][^c-05f32fe86158a9654146d26b99c13093] Schedule 2 contains provision about NAO.
