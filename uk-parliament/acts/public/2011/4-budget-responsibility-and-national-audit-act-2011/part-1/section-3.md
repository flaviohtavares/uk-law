## 3. Office for Budget Responsibility[^key-0c994cf45544e77f11610a7e2d1b381f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There is to be a body corporate called the Office for Budget Responsibility.

(2) Schedule 1 contains provision about the Office.
