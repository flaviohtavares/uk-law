# Education Act 2011, SCHEDULE 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2018-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 15 — Academies: minor amendments

*Extent: E+W*

Section 65

**1.**[^key-166ba070ad023b62faf1a255b5d6b9e1] In Schedule 4 to the Finance Act 2003 (stamp duty land tax: chargeable consideration), in paragraph 17(2)(e) (arrangements involving public or educational bodies) for “section 482 of the Education Act 1996” substitute “ section 1 of the Academies Act 2010 ”.

**2.**[^key-64f457710eac5caaa27393aaa9c70ebc] In Schedule 17 to the Equality Act 2010 (disabled pupils: enforcement), in paragraph 13(4) (admissions) for paragraph (b) substitute—

> - (b) Academy arrangements (as defined in section 1 of the Academies Act 2010) between the responsible body for an Academy and the Secretary of State,

.

**3.**[^key-c518cfc7eb0d49a8c46ff25b8f33a3b1] In section 2 of AA 2010 (payments under Academy arrangements), omit subsection (5).
