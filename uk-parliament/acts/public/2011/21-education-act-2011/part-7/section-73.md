# Education Act 2011, section 73

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2018-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Functions of the Chief Executive of Skills Funding

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-ed1fb295775bd1587ae535e14e046b61][^key-3c5ec8fb7a51be6c90a92d558bd4166b][^key-f033cb5b5c9cd147985e85539b4b705a] Part 4 of ASCLA 2009 (the Chief Executive of Skills Funding) is amended as follows.

(2) In section 88 (learning aims for persons aged 19 or over: payment of tuition fees)—

- (a)[^key-5ff430a555c19296c0514a25cbc91740] in subsection (1), for “paragraph 1” substitute “ paragraph 1(a) or (b) ”;
- (b)[^key-5ff430a555c19296c0514a25cbc91740] after subsection (2), insert—

  > (2A) Functions under this Part must be exercised by the Chief Executive so as to secure that a course of study for a qualification to which paragraph 1(c) of Schedule 5 applies is free to a person falling within subsection (4) if it is provided for the person by virtue of facilities whose provision is secured under section 87.

  ;
- (c)[^key-c578c5594c6fe6c78d1aac9c09f9c674][^key-f033cb5b5c9cd147985e85539b4b705a] in subsection (4)(a), for “25” substitute “ 24 ”.

(3)[^key-31177cd43a48ebe350a6e10aa461b11c] Sections 112 to 114 (Secretary of State's power to specify bodies to formulate strategy for the Chief Executive) are repealed.
