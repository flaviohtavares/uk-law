# Education Act 2011, section 66

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 66. Abolition of the Young People's Learning Agency for England[^c-86fd77c7094881df9f3771e2b93a05cb]

*Extent: E+W*

Sections 60 to 80 of, and Schedule 3 to, ASCLA 2009 (the Young People's Learning Agency for England) are repealed.
