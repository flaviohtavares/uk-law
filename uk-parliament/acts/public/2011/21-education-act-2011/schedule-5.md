## SCHEDULE 5 — Abolition of the TDA: consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W*

Section 16

### Public Records Act 1958 (c. 51)

**1.**[^c-662ea2083a1cb0ec3300f01be61f72b3] In paragraph 3 of Schedule 1 to the Public Records Act 1958, in Part 2 of the Table (definition of public records: other establishments and organisations) omit “Training and Development Agency for Schools.” and “Teacher Training Agency.”

### Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967 (c. 13)

**2.**[^c-0bb27bd3b7f582a1b0f3df41845ad4cc] In Schedule 2 to the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967 (departments etc subject to investigation) omit “Training and Development Agency for Schools.”

### Superannuation Act 1972 (c. 11)

**3.**[^c-1c085cf33adfb233210672607cc486cb] In Schedule 1 to the Superannuation Act 1972 (kinds of employment, etc, referred to in section 1 of that Act) omit “Training and Development Agency for Schools.”

### House of Commons Disqualification Act 1975 (c. 24)

**4.**[^c-8fba42e584be4c02e968f5184a75225a] In Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the House of Commons Disqualification Act 1975 (other disqualifying offices) omit the entry relating to members of the Training and Development Agency for Schools in receipt of remuneration.

### Education (Fees and Awards) Act 1983 (c. 40)

**5.**[^c-4590d95cb3bb7023aeeb1f085a297900] In section 1(3) of the Education (Fees and Awards) Act 1983 (institutions in respect of which regulations may provide for differential fees), in paragraph (e), for “support under section 78 of that Act from the Training and Development Agency for Schools” substitute

> assistance—
>
> - (i) from the Secretary of State or the Welsh Ministers under section 14 of the Education Act 2002, or
> - (ii) from a person who is receiving financial assistance under that section;

.

### EA 1994

**6.**[^c-ddf1ca242b6da2aa6056adbea35742ba] Part 1 of EA 1994 (teacher training) is amended as follows.

**7.**[^c-e05a742d72e9965839457c6bbfdad874] Section 11A (general duty of Secretary of State and Welsh Ministers with respect to teacher training) is repealed.

**8.**[^c-21da3f505b27739071782bb8a22549c6] In section 18B (inspection of teacher training), in subsections (3) and (8), for “Training and Development Agency for Schools” substitute “ Secretary of State ”.

**9.**[^c-1e522826db245426a3cd881066093737] In section 18C (inspection of teacher training in Wales), in subsection (12)(b), omit “or the Training and Development Agency for Schools”.

### EA 1996

### THEA 1998

- (a) omit “81(1), 82(1) or”;

- (c) omit “78 or”.

- (a) omit “or the Training and Development Agency for Schools”;

### Government of Wales Act 1998 (c. 38)

(3) Omit subsection (1A).

(4) In subsection (2), for “Subsections (1) and (1A) do” substitute “ Subsection (1) does ”.

(5) In subsection (3), omit “or (1A)”.

### Freedom of Information Act 2000 (c. 36)

### EA 2002
