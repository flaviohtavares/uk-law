# Education Act 2011, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2018-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 32. Duty to prepare and publish school profile[^c-5c8e0edd1624d6b2ebfcb7d7d6f06a3e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 30A of EA 2002 (duty of governing body of maintained school in England to prepare and publish school profile) is repealed.

(2) In consequence of subsection (1), section 104 of EA 2005 (insertion of section 30A of EA 2002) is repealed.
