# Education Act 2011, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2018-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Financing of pupil referral units[^c-6d860812e1b6ef8977a86657de137d58]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 45 of SSFA 1998 (financing of maintained schools: maintained schools to have budget shares) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1A), omit “or” at the end of paragraph (b), and after paragraph (c) insert

> , or
>
> - (d) a pupil referral unit in England.

(3) In subsection (3)—

- (a) in paragraph (a), after “pupil referral units” insert “ in Wales ”;
- (b) after paragraph (a) insert—

  > - (aa) references to the governing body of a maintained school or of a school maintained by a local authority shall be read, in relation to a pupil referral unit in England, as references to the management committee for the unit (in spite of paragraph 1 of Schedule 1 to the Education Act 1996);
  > - (ab) references to governors shall be read, in relation to a pupil referral unit in England, as references to the members of the management committee for the unit;

  .
