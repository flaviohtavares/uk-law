> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. Requirement for schools to participate in international surveys[^c-096c9daa2aea60c5afaf59e86ba3c72f]

*Extent: E+W*

In Chapter 4 of Part 9 of EA 1996 (provision of information by governing bodies etc), after section 538, insert—

> **538A. Power to direct participation in international surveys**
>
> The Secretary of State may direct the governing body of a community, foundation or voluntary school in England to secure that the school participates in such international education surveys as may be specified in the direction.
