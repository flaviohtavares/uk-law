# Education Act 2011, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2018-07-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2011/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 28. Education and training support services in England

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-fdad2d706e1ba51add296c3bab3694d8] Part 2 of ESA 2008 (education and training support services in England) is amended as set out in subsections (2) to (5).

(2)[^c-fdad2d706e1ba51add296c3bab3694d8] Section 69 (Secretary of State directions in relation to education and training support services) is repealed.

(3)[^c-fdad2d706e1ba51add296c3bab3694d8] In consequence of subsection (2)—

- (a) in section 68 (provision of support services by local authorities)—
  - (i) omit subsection (2);
  - (ii) in subsection (3), omit “and section 69”;
  - (iii) in subsection (4), omit paragraph (a) (and the “and” after it);
- (b) in section 70(2) (local authorities: supplementary powers), omit “or 69”;
- (c) in section 71(8) (provision of support on conditional basis), for “68(2) and (4)” substitute “ 68(4) ”.

(4)[^c-fdad2d706e1ba51add296c3bab3694d8] Section 73 (duty on schools and FE institutions to permit access by persons involved in providing education and training support services) is repealed.

(5)[^c-53da2a063b7014c01852f2d62ef41ca0] In section 76A (supply of information obtained in connection with education and training support services), omit subsection (5) (information not to be disclosed in a way that reveals identity of individual to whom it relates).

(6)[^c-fdad2d706e1ba51add296c3bab3694d8] In consequence of the amendment made by subsection (2), omit section 255(2) of ASCLA 2009.
