## 56. The Passengers’ Council and London Transport Users’ Committee.[^c4795451][^c4795461][^c4795471][^c4795481][^c4795491][^c4795501][^c4795511][^c-eebb458b1cd8b3056e8c92d73c215cd1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c4795521] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c4795521] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c4795521] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c4795551][^c4795561][^c4795571][^c4795581][^c4795591] Subject to the following provisions of this section, it shall be the duty of the[^c4795601] Passengers’ Council[^c-3d036ebe8c910178bc80fd760933b165] and the London Transport Users' Committee[^c-a74ba90479ac952785a4d38922b17a8f] to consider and, where it appears to them to be desirable, make recommendations with respect to any matter affecting the services and facilities provided by any of the Boards—

- (a) which has been the subject of representations (other than representations appearing to the committee to be frivolous) made to the committee by or on behalf of users of those services or facilities, or
- (b) which has been referred to the[^c4795611] Passengers’ Council or the London Transport Users' Committee[^c-29df8572886eae2da2425229572ec9f6] by the Minister or by a Board, or
- (c) which appears to the Passengers’ Council or the London Transport Users' Committee to be a matter to which consideration ought to be given;

[^c-019f49879cdbc2e9f6e46b47d09ac9e0]...

(5)[^c-76be5c0d030e9beb8ef9987ae25e93f3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c4795581][^c4795591][^c4795551] Where the Minister receives a recommendation under subsection (4) of this section he may give to the Board concerned such directions as he thinks fit with respect to the matters dealt with in the recommendation.

(6ZA)[^c4795751] If the Secretary of State so directs [^c-9ae621f49c72465bce3a0601c74f27a4]..., subsections (4) to (6) of this section shall have effect in relation to [^c-9ae621f49c72465bce3a0601c74f27a4]... the[^c4795771] Passengers’ Council and the London Transport Users' Committee[^c-07750114f61d7e7a6e89ab8ce60e7060] as if the reference in subsection (4) of this section to services and facilities provided by any of the Boards included a reference to any such ferry service as may be specified in the direction, whether provided by a Board or by some other person; and, in the application of subsections (4) to (6) of this section in relation to any such ferry service, any reference in those subsections to a Board shall be taken to include a reference to the person providing the ferry service.

(6A)[^c4795591][^c4795781] Each of the[^c-95cb9dd3c682b0e96682191e2f19d3ee] Passengers’ Council and the London Transport Users' Committee may consider, and if they think fit object to, any proposal for the discontinuance of railway services made in an application for an order under section 1 of the Transport and Works Act 1992 or made by the Secretary of State by virtue of section 7 of that Act.
