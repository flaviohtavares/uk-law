# Transport Act 1962, section 89

> Text as at 2024-10-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 89. Duty to give effect to Minister’s directions.[^c4802051]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It shall be the duty of the Boards or of any other person to whom the Minister or, as the case may be, the Scottish Ministers[^c4802061] or any other authority gives directions under this Act to give effect to those directions.

(2) Any direction given by the Minister or, as the case may be, the Scottish Ministers[^c4802071] under this Act shall be in writing.
