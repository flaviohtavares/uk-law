# Transport Act 1962, section 75

> Text as at 2024-10-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 75. Transitional provisions relating to pensions.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Part IV of the Seventh Schedule to this Act (which contains transitional provisions relating to pensions) shall have effect for the purposes of this Act.
