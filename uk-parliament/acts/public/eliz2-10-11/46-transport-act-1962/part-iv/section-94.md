# Transport Act 1962, section 94

> Text as at 2024-10-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94. Minister’s expenses.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any expenses incurred by the Minister under or in consequence of the provisions of this Act shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament.
