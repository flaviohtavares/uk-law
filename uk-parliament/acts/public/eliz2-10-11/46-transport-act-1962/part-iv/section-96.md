# Transport Act 1962, section 96

> Text as at 2024-10-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Short title and commencement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Transport Act 1962.

(2)[^c4802571] Subject to any other provision of this Act, this Act shall come into force on such date as the Minister may by order contained in a statutory instrument appoint, and the Minister may under this subsection appoint different dates for different provisions of this Act.

## Annotations

[^c4802571]: Modification: Power of appointment conferred by s. 96(2) fully exercised see S.I. 1962/1788, 2634, 1964/2025
