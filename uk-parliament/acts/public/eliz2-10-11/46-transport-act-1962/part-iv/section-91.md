# Transport Act 1962, section 91

> Text as at 2024-10-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Repeal of local enactments.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Minister or, in the case of the British Waterways Board in respect of any local enactment in Scotland, the Scottish Ministers[^c4802361] may by order repeal any local enactment which in his or, as the case may be, their[^c4802371] opinion has ceased to have effect in consequence of the provisions of the [^c4802381]Transport Act 1947, or this Act.

(2) An order under this section shall be made by statutory instrument which shall be subject to annulment in the case of an order made by the Minister,[^c4802391] in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament and in the case of an order made by the Scottish Ministers, in pursuance of a resolution of the Scottish Parliament.[^c4802401].
