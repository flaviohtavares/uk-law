> Text as at 2024-10-30.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. †Port charges and conditions at harbours.[^c-22953f81e0f430c071c8c11169c68e71][^c4793101]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The provisions of the Ninth Schedule to this Act shall have effect as regards the powers of the Boards and Canal & River Trust[^c-9337ceddd253d119042c095e72238eff] to make . . . [^c4793111] charges at the harbours specified in that Schedule.

(2) Subsection (3) of section forty-three of this Act shall not authorise the Boards or Canal & River Trust[^c-605931b9e17ef1847fb51e7624945478] to make the use of their services and facilities at the said harbours subject to any term or condition which the Boards or Canal & River Trust would not be able to impose apart from that subsection.
