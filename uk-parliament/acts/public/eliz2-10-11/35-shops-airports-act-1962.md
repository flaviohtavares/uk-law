# Shops (Airports) Act 1962

> This law has been repealed or revoked. Ended on 1994-12-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Shops (Airports) Act 1962 ([^c627390]repealed 1.12.1994)

1962 c.35

**No longer in force (ended 1994-12-01).**

## 1. Exemption of traders at certain airports from Part I of Shops Act 1950.[^c627399][^c627398]

(1) The provisions of Part I of the [^c627400]Shops Act 1950 (which relate to hours of closing) and of Part IV of that Act (which relates to Sunday trading)[^c627401]shall not apply—

- (a) to any shop at a designated airport which is situated in a part of the airport to which this Act applies, or
- (b) to the sale (otherwise than at a shop) of any goods at a designated airport, where the sale takes place in a part of the airport to which this Act applies, and is effected by or on behalf of a person carrying on a retail trade or business at a shop situated in such a part of the airport.

(2) This Act applies to every part of a designated airport, except any part which is not ordinarily used by persons travelling by air to or from the airport; and in this Act “**designated airport**” means an airport designated for the purposes of this Act by an order made by the Board of Trade[^c627402], as being an airport at which there appears to the Board to be a substantial amount of international passenger traffic.

(3) The power conferred by subsection (2) of this section to make orders shall include power to vary or revoke any order made thereunder by a subsequent order.

(4) Any power to make orders under this Act shall be exercisable by statutory instrument.

(5) Expressions used in this Act and in the [^c627404]Shops Act 1950, have the same meanings in this Act as in that Act.

## 2. Short title, citation and extent.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Shops (Airports) Act 1962; and the Shops Act 1950, and this Act may be cited together as the Shops Acts 1950 and 1962.

(2) This Act shall not extend to Northern Ireland.
