# Law Reform (Husband and Wife) Act 1962

> Text as at 2006-05-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Law Reform (Husband and Wife) Act 1962

1962 c.48

An Act to amend the law with respect to civil proceedings between husband and wife. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37]

\[1st August 1962\]

## 1. Actions in tort between husband and wife.

*Extent: E+W*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37] Subject to the provisions of this section, each of the parties to a marriage shall have the like right of action in tort against the other as if they were not married. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37] Where an action in tort is brought by one of the parties to a marriage against the other during the subsistence of the marriage, the court may stay the action if it appears— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37] that no substantial benefit would accrue to either party from the continuation of the proceedings; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37] that the question or questions in issue could more conveniently be disposed of on an application made under section seventeen of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37] [^c627579]Married Women’s Property Act 1882 (determination of questions between husband and wife as to the title to or possession of property); *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37]

and without prejudice to paragraph (b) of this subsection the court may, in such an action, either exercise any power which could be exercised on an application under the said section seventeen, or give such directions as it thinks fit for the disposal under that section of any question arising in the proceedings. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c627580][^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37] This section does not extend to Scotland. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37]

## 2. Proceedings between husband and wife in respect of delict.

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this section, each of the parties to a marriage shall have the like right to bring proceedings against the other in respect of a wrongful or negligent act or omission, or for the prevention of a wrongful act, as if they were not married. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37]

(2)[^c627581] Where any such proceedings are brought by one of the parties to a marriage against the other during the subsistence of the marriage, the court may dismiss the proceedings if it appears that no substantial benefit would accrue to either party from the continuation therof; and it shall be the duty of the court to consider at an early stage of the proceedings whether the power to dismiss the proceedings under this subsection should or should not be exercised. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37]

(3) This section extends to Scotland only. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37]

## 3. Short title, repeal, interpretation, saving and extent.

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37] This Act may be cited as the Law Reform (Husband and Wife) Act 1962. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37] [^c627582]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37] The references in subsection (1) of section one and subsection (1) of section two of this Act to the parties to a marriage include references to the persons who were parties to a marriage which has been dissolved. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37] This Act does not apply to any cause of action which arose, or would but for the subsistence of a marriage have arisen, before the commencement of this Act. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37]

(5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37] This Act does not extend to Northern Ireland. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be6b8152a6c2e2f41544eeaa18dbed37]
