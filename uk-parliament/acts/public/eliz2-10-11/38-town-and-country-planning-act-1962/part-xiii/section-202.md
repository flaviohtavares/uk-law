# Town and Country Planning Act 1962, section 202

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 202. Application of Act to London

The provisions of the Ninth Schedule to this Act shall have effect for the purposes of the application of this Act to the administrative county of London.
