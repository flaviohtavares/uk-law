# Town and Country Planning Act 1962, section 203

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 203. Application of Act to Isles of Scilly

(1) The Minister shall, after consultation with the Council of the Isles of Scilly, by order provide for the application to those Isles of the provisions of this Act specified in para-giaph 1 of the Eighth Schedule thereto as if those Isles were a separate county; and any such order may provide for the application of those provisions of this Act to those Isles subject to such modifications as may be specified in the order.

(2) In relation to land in the Isles of Scilly, the provisions of this Act specified in paragraph 2 of the Eighth Schedule thereto shall have effect as if those Isles were a county district, and the Council of the Isles were the council of that district.
