# Town and Country Planning Act 1962, section 115

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 115. Amount recovered not to be deducted from unexpended balance

(1) Where an amount has become recoverable under section one hundred and thirteen of this Act in respect of the compensation specified in a compensation notice, the following provisions of this section shall have effect for the purpose of determining any question as to the unexpended balance of established development value of any land at any subsequent time.

(2) Except where, and to the extent that, payment of that amount has been remitted under the last preceding section, so much (if any) of that compensation as is attributable to that land shall, for the purpose mentioned in the preceding subsection, be treated as not having become payable, and accordingly (notwithstanding anything in section ninety-four of this Act) shall not be deducted from that balance.
