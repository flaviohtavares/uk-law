# Town and Country Planning Act 1962, section 222

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 222. Consequential amendments

The enactments specified in the Twelfth Schedule to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in that Schedule, being amendments consequential upon the provisions of this Act.
