# Town and Country Planning Act 1962, section 224

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 224. Saving for Interpretation Act, 1889, s.38

The inclusion in this Act of any express saving, transitional provision or amendment shall not be taken as prejudicing the operation of section thirty-eight of the Interpretation Act, 1889 (which relates to the effect of repeals).
