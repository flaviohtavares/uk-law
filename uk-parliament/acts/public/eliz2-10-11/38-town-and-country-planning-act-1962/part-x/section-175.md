# Town and Country Planning Act 1962, section 175

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 175. Special provisions as to statutory undertakers who are local planning authorities

In relation to statutory undertakers who are local planning authorities, the last preceding section and the provisions specified in subsection (2) thereof shall have effect subject to such exceptions and modifications as may be prescribed by regulations made under this Act.
