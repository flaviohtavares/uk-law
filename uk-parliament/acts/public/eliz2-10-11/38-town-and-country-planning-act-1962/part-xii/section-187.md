# Town and Country Planning Act 1962, section 187

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 187. Loans to local authorities

The power of the Public Works Loan Commissioners to make loans under section nine of the Public Works Loans Act, 1875, shall include power to make loans to a local authority for the purpose of the discharge by them of their functions under any of the provisions of this Act specified in paragraph 1 of the Eighth Schedule thereto.
