# Town and Country Planning Act 1962, section 195

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 195. General provision as to receipts of Minister

Without prejudice to the last preceding section, and subject to the provisions of section one hundred and twenty-two of this Act, any sums received by the Minister—

- (a) under any provision of this Act, or
- (b) in respect of the payment of a development charge,

shall be paid into the Exchequer.
