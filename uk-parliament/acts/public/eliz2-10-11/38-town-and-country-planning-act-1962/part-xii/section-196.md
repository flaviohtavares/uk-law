# Town and Country Planning Act 1962, section 196

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 196. Expenses of county councils

The council of any county may direct that any expenses incurred by them under the provisions of this Act specified in paragraph 1 of the Eighth Schedule thereto shall be treated as expenses for special county purposes chargeable upon such part of the county as may be specified in the directions.
