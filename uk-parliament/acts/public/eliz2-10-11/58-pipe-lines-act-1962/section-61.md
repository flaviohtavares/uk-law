# Pipe-Lines Act 1962, section 61

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2025-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Eliz2/10-11/58

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 61. Exclusion of application of Act to, and in relation to, dock, &c., pipe-lines.

*Extent: E+W+S*

References in sections one to forty . . . [^c7381451] of this Act to a pipe-line and to pipe-line works shall be construed as respectively not including references to a pipe-line wholly situate in premises to which certain provisions of the [^c7381461]Factories Act 1961, apply by virtue of subsection (1) of section one hundred and twenty-five (docks, &c.) of that Act, and to pipe-line works executed in connection with a pipe-line that is or will be wholly so situate.
