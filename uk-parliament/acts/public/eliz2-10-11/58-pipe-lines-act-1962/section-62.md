# Pipe-Lines Act 1962, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2025-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Eliz2/10-11/58
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 62.

*Extent: E+W+S*

References in sections twenty-seven and thirty-one of this Act to a pipe-line shall be construed as not including references to a pipe-line that is a government oil pipe-line within the meaning of the [^c7381471]Requisitioned Land and War Works Act 1948, or to a pipe-line that was laid under a wayleave order made under section fourteen of the [^c7381481]Land Powers (Defence) Act 1958.
