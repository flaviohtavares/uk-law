# Pipe-Lines Act 1962, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2025-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Eliz2/10-11/58

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 70. Short title, extent and commencement.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Pipe-lines Act 1962.

(2) This Act shall not extend to Northern Ireland.

(3)[^c7382711] Section forty-one of this Act shall come into operation on the passing of this Act, and the remainder of this Act shall come into operation on such day as Her Majesty may by Order in Council appoint.
