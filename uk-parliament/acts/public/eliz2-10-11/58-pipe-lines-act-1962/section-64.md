# Pipe-Lines Act 1962, section 64

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2025-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Eliz2/10-11/58
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 64. Power of Minister to exclude application ofsection one in relation to certain pipe-lines in particular localities.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Minister, on an application in that behalf being made to him, and after causing if he thinks fit a public inquiry to be held, may by order (made by statutory instrument which shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament) direct that section one[^c7381491] of this Act shall not apply to works executed for the construction of a pipe-line designed for the conveyance of a thing of a particular kind specified in the order or things of a class so specified, being a pipe-line wholly situate within such area as may be specified in, or described by, the order.
