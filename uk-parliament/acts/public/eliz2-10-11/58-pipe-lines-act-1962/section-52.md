# Pipe-Lines Act 1962, section 52

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2025-05-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Eliz2/10-11/58

## 52. Reckoning of periods.

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of this Act in reckoning any period which is therein expressed to be a period before or from a given date, that date shall be excluded.
