# Criminal Law Act 1977, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1977/45
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 40. Transfer of fine orders.[^c4765501][^c4765491][^c4765481][^c4765471]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 7 to this Act (which confers powers on courts in Northern Ireland, and extends the existing powers of courts in England, Wales and Scotland, to make transfer of fine orders) shall have effect.
