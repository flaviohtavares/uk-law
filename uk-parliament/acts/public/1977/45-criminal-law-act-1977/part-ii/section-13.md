# Criminal Law Act 1977, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1977/45
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Abolitions and repeals.[^c4764391]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The offence of forcible entry and any offence of forcible detainer at common law are hereby abolished for all purposes not relating to offences committed before the coming into force of this Part of this Act.

(2) The following enactments shall cease to have effect—

- (a) the [^c4764821]Forcible Entry Act 1381;
- (b) chapter 2 of 15 Ric. 2 (1391);
- (c) the [^c4764831]Forcible Entry Act 1429;
- (d) the [^c4764841]Forcible Entry Act 1588; and
- (e) the [^c4764851]Forcible Entry Act 1623.
