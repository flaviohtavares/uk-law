# Rent Act 1977, section 153

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 153. Application to Isles of Scilly.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) With the exception of Part V, and sections 102A to 106A[^c16042091], of this Act (which do not apply to the Isles of Scilly) this Act applies to the Isles subject to such exceptions, adaptations and modifications as the Secretary of State may by order direct.

(2) The power to make an order under this section shall be exercisable by statutory instrument which shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(3) An order under this section may be varied or revoked by a subsequent order.
