# Rent Act 1977, section 72A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1977/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 72A. Amounts attributable to services.[^c16039271]

*Extent: E+W*

In order to assist authorities to give effect to the housing benefit scheme under Part VII of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 or to assist the Secretary of State in the administration of universal credit[^key-0242974e2ce15ddb72f9bf6609532d43] , where a rent is registered, there shall be noted on the register the amount (if any) of the registered rent which, in the opinion of the rent officer or appropriate tribunal[^key-fac4101c83942fc697fc2c3bf8b36301] , is fairly attributable to the provision of services, except any amount which is negligible in the opinion of the officer or, as the case may be, the tribunal[^key-7e1788601961603290e258ecc80d36c3] .
