## 86. Tenancies to which Part VI applies.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part of this Act “**housing association tenancy**” means a tenancy to which this Part of this Act applies.

(2) This Part of this Act applies to a tenancy (other than a co-ownership tenancy)[^c16040021] where—

- (a)[^key-d6eab6a9b001f876f7cd9b8352e7c10a] the interest of the landlord under that tenancy belongs to a housing association or housing trust, or to the Regulator of Social Housing[^key-a00435c95c77a2232807b4af2240dcf3] or to the Secretary of State where that interest belongs to him as the result of the exercise by him of functions under Part III of the Housing Associations Act 1985[^c16040031]. . ., and
- (b)[^c16040051] the tenancy would be a protected tenancy but for section 13 or[^c16040061] 15 or 16 of this Act, and is not a tenancy to which Part II of the [^c16040071]Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 applies.

(3) In this Part of this Act “**housing association**”has the same meaning as in the Housing Associations Act 1985[^c16040081]

- (a) restrict membership to persons who are tenants or prospective tenants of the association, and
- (b) preclude the granting or assigning of tenancies to persons other than members.

(3A)[^c16040091] For the purposes of this section a tenancy is a “co-ownership tenancy” if—

- (a) it was granted by a housing association which is a co-operative housing association within the meaning of the Housing Associations Act 1985[^c16040101]; and
- (b) the tenant (or his personal representatives) will, under the terms of the tenancy agreement or of the agreement under which he became a member of the association, be entitled, on his ceasing to be a member and subject to any conditions stated in either agreement, to a sum calculated by reference directly or indirectly to the value of the dwelling-house.

(4)[^c16040111] In this Part of this Act “housing trust” has the same meaning as in section 15 of this Act.
