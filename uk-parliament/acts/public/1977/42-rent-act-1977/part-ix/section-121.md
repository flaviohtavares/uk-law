# Rent Act 1977, section 121

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1977/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 121. Tenancies which became regulated by virtue of Counter-Inflation Act 1973.

*Extent: E+W*

Part II of Schedule 18 to this Act shall have effect where a premium was lawfully required and paid on the grant, renewal or continuance of a regulated tenancy—

- (a) which was granted before 8th March 1973, and
- (b) which would not have been a regulated tenancy, but for section 14(1) of the [^c16041011]Counter-Inflation Act 1973 (which brought certain tenancies of dwelling-houses with high rateable values within the protection of the [^c16041021]Rent Act 1968).
