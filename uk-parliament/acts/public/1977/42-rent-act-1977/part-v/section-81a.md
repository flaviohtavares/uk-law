## 81A. Cancellation of registration of rent.[^c16039751]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where the rent payable for any dwelling is entered in the register under section 79 or 79A[^key-0ba1d2b404b0eb13febb964e507845a3] of this Act, the appropriate tribunal[^key-4730ab804f01574411173b21f93211ee] shall cancel the registration of entry, on an application made under this section, if—

- (a) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c16039761]
- (b) the dwelling is not for the time being subject to a restricted contract; and
- (c) the application is made by the person who would be the lessor if the dwelling were subject to a restricted contract.

(2) An application under this section must be in the prescribed form, and contain the prescribed particulars.

(3) Cancellation of the registration shall be without prejudice to a further registration of a rent at any time after the cancellation.

(4) The appropriate[^key-86c07ee23d99c807865e2e5d934de471] tribunal shall notify the applicant of their decision to grant, or to refuse, any application under this section.
