# Rent Act 1977, section 102A

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 102A. Restricted application of sections 103 to 106.[^c16040681]

*Extent: E+W*

Sections 103 to 106 of this Act apply only to restricted contracts entered into before the commencement of section 69 of the [^c16040691] Housing Act 1980.
