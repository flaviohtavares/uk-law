# Rent Act 1977, section 18A

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18A. Modification of Act for controlled tenancies converted into regulated tenancies.[^c16036871]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 17 to this Act applies for the purpose of modifying the provisions of this Act in relation to a tenancy Which, by virtue of any of the following enactments, was converted from a controlled tenancy into a regulated tenancy, that is to say—

- (a) section 18(3) of this Act;
- (b) paragraph 5 of Schedule 2 to the [^c16036881] Rent Act 1968 (which was superseded by section 18(3));
- (c) Part VIII of this Act;
- (d) Part 111 of the Housing Finance Act 1972 (which was superseded by Part VIII);
- (e) Part IV of the Act of 1972 (conversion by reference to rateable values);
- (f) section 64 of the Housing Act 1980 (conversion of remaining controlled tenancies into regulated tenancies).
