# Patents Act 1977, section 121

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1977/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 121. Comptroller’s annual report.[^c8021531]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before 1st December[^key-966601619fe328ba66bedf127b4dcac8] in every financial year[^key-bc2a88191d54b909d33981fb53cc7d87] the comptroller shall cause to be laid before both Houses of Parliament a report with respect to the execution of this Act and the discharge of his functions under the European Patent Convention, [^key-028939519ed3a65e96daa88484afa51a]... and the Patent Co-operation Treaty, and every such report shall include an account of all fees, salaries and allowances, and other money received and paid by him under this Act, that convention[^key-90b2a0062c1f371cb735c78f97bc854a] and that treaty during the previous financial year .
