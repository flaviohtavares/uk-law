# Patents Act 1977, section 128B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1977/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 128B. Supplementary protection certificates[^c-95c188da4c93d6bb7ca0fb586408bedd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 4A contains provision about the application of this Act in relation to supplementary protection certificates and other provision about such certificates.

(2) In this Act a “supplementary protection certificate” means a certificate issued under—

- (a) Regulation[^c-94769147fc24f0e0479bb27b88f3e501] [(EC) No 469/2009](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/2009/0469) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 6th May 2009 concerning the supplementary protection certificate for medicinal products, or
- (b) Regulation [(EC) No 1610/96](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/regulation/1996/1610) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 July 1996 concerning the creation of a supplementary protection certificate for plant protection products.
