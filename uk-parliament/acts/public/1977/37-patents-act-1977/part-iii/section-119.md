# Patents Act 1977, section 119

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1977/37

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 119. Service by post.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any notice required or authorised to be given by this Act or rules, and any application or other document so authorised or required to be made or filed, may be given, made or filed by post.
