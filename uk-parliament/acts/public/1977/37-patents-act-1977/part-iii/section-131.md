# Patents Act 1977, section 131

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1977/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 131. Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the application of this Act to Northern Ireland—

- (a) “**enactment**” includes an enactment of the Parliament of Northern Ireland and a Measure of the Northern Ireland Assembly;
- (b) any reference to a government department includes a reference to a Department of the Government of Northern Ireland;
- (c) any reference to the Crown includes a reference to the Crown in right of Her Majesty’s Government in Northern Ireland;
- (d) any reference to the Companies Act 1985[^c8022141] includes a reference to the corresponding enactments in force in Northern Ireland; and
- (e)[^c8022151] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f)[^c-aeaf6448d88974103bbc99257c386053] any reference to a claimant includes a reference to a plaintiff.
