# Patents Act 1977, section 95

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1977/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 95. Financial provisions.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There shall be paid out of moneys provided by Parliament any sums required by any Minister of the Crown or government department to meet any financial obligation of the United Kingdom under the European Patent Convention, [^c-f6c8c05b8c9ae33ab50e742cc0ec198f]... or the Patent Co-operation Treaty.

(2) Any sums received by any Minister of the Crown or government department in pursuance of that convention[^c-2f00234e658998d6abf8ed76b4bc376e] or that treaty shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund.
