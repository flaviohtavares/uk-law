# Patents Act 1977, section 93

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1977/37

## 93. Enforcement of orders for costs.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the European Patent Office orders the payment of costs in any proceedings before it—

- (a) in England and Wales the costs shall, if the county court[^c-cec2375409a1ec8ab787375183e77815] so orders, be recoverable under section 85 of the County Courts Act 1984[^c-57bf6598bd3f5c1f6949a1031e42e73a] or otherwise as if they were payable under an order of that court;
- (b) in Scotland the order may be enforced in like manner as a recorded decree arbitral[^c8021261] an extract registered decree arbitral bearing a warrant for execution issued by the sheriff court of any sheriffdom in Scotland.
- (c) in Northern Ireland the order may be enforced as if it were a money judgment.
