# Life Assurance Companies (Payment Into Court) Act 1896

> Text as at 2009-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Life Assurance Companies (Payment Into Court) Act 1896

1896 c.8

An Act to enable Life Assurance Companies to pay Money into Court in certain Cases.

\[21st May 1896\][^c-e5faffdf5309308bd4a0a67ab02be819]\[21st May 1896\][^c-4c6bcde476c9dac5ac34951b21e31dfe]

## 1. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Life Assurance Companies (Payment into Court) Act 1896.

## 2. Interpretation.

In this Act—

- The expression “**life assurance company**” means any corporation, company, or society carrying on the business of life assurance, not being a society registered under the Acts relating to friendly societies;
- The expression “**life policy**” includes any policy not foreign to the business of life assurance.

## 3. Power to pay money into court.

Subject to rules of court[^c576706] rules made under section 55 of the [^c576708]Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978 any life assurance company may pay into the Supreme Court . . . [^c576710][^c576709], any moneys payable by them under a life policy in respect of which, in the opinion of their board of directors, no sufficient discharge can otherwise be obtained.

## 4. Receipt of officer sufficient discharge.

The receipt or certificate of the proper officer shall be a sufficient discharge to the company for the moneys so paid into court, and such moneys shall, subject to rules of court[^c576711] rules made under section 55 of the [^c576713]Judicature (Northern Ireland) Act 1978 be dealt with according to the orders of the High Court[^c576714] Supreme Court . . . [^c576716]

## 5. Extent of Act.

This Act does not extend to Scotland.
