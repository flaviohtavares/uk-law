# Public Health Act 1875, section 160

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 160. Incorporation of certain provisions of 10 & 11 Vict. c. 34.[^c665137][^c665138]

*Extent: E+W*

The provisions of the [^c665139]Towns Improvement Clauses Act 1847, with respect to the following matters, that is to say,

(1)[^c665140] With respect to naming the streets and numbering the houses; and

(2)—(4)[^c665141] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

shall, for the purpose of regulating such matters in districts,[^c665142] be incorporated with this Act.
