# Public Health Act 1875, section 341

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 341. Powers of Act to be cumulative.[^c665325]

*Extent: E+W*

All powers given by this Act shall be deemed to be in addition to and not in derogation of any other powers conferred by Act of Parliament law or custom, and such other powers may be exercised in the same manner as if this Act had not passed; [^c665326]. . .
