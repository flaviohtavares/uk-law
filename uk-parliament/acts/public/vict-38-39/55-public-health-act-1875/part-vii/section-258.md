# Public Health Act 1875, section 258

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 258. Justices may act though members of local authority or liable to contribute.[^c665225]

*Extent: E+W*

No justice of the peace shall be deemed incapable of acting in cases arising under this Act . . . [^c665226] by reason of his being as one of several ratepayers, or as one of any other class of persons liable in common with the others to contribute to, or to be benefited by any rate or fund, out of which any expenses incurred by such authority are under this Act to be defrayed.
