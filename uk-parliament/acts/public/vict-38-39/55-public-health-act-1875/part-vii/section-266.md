# Public Health Act 1875, section 266

> Text as at 2023-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 266. Notices, &c. may be printed or written.[^c665239]

*Extent: E+W*

Notices orders and other such documents under this Act may be in writing or print, or partly in writing and partly in print; and if the same require authentication by the local authority, the signature thereof by the proper officer of the authority[^c665240] shall be sufficient authentication.
