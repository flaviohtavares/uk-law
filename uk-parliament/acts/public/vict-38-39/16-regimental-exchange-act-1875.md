# Regimental Exchange Act 1875

> This law has been repealed or revoked. Ended on 1995-11-08.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regimental Exchange Act 1875 [^c568559](repealed 8.11.1995)

1875 c.16

**No longer in force (ended 1995-11-08).**

## 1. Short title Act.

This Act may be cited for all purposes as “The Regimental Exchange Act, 1875.”

## 2. Authorised exchanges exempted from Army Brokerage Acts.

Her Majesty may, from time to time, by regulation authorise exchanges to be made by officers in Her Majesty’s Regular Forces from one regiment or corps to another regiment or corps, on such conditions as to Her Majesty may for the time being seem expedient; and nothing contained in the Army Brokerage Acts shall extend to any exchanges made in manner authorised by any regulation of Her Majesty for the time being in force.

## 3. Definition of Army Brokerage Acts.

The expression “**The Army Brokerage Acts**” means the Acts following:

The [^c568565]Sale of Offices Act, 1551, and the [^c568566]Sale of Offices Act, 1809.
