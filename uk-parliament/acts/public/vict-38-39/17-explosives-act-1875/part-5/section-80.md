## 80. Penalty for throwing fireworks in thoroughfare.[^c5689391]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If any person throw, cast, or fire any fireworks in or into any highway, street, thoroughfare,[^c5689401]road or public place, he shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale[^c5689421]
