## 97. Exemption of Government factories, &c. from the Act.[^c5689681][^c5689691][^c5689671]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Act shall not apply—

(1) To any factory, magazine, store, premises, wharf, place, or explosive under the control of the Secretary of State, . . . [^c5689701], or other department of the Government or otherwise held for the service of the Crown, or to the manufacture, keeping, or importation of such explosive; or

(2) To any of Her Majesty’s ships, boats, or carriages; or

(3)[^key-e356fa14e59a2a1cdbe933bb78c93c3e][^key-bcc1edcdb4776d4285053949e13275c6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-e356fa14e59a2a1cdbe933bb78c93c3e][^key-bcc1edcdb4776d4285053949e13275c6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c5689771][^c5689781] To the conveyance of any explosive under the control of a Secretary of State, . . . [^c5689701], or other department of the Government or to the conveyance of any explosive otherwise held for the service of the Crown when the same is being conveyed in accordance with the regulations of a Secreatary of State of . . . [^c5689701] other department of the Government:
