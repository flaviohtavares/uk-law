# Explosives Act 1875, section 91

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2015-08-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/38-39/17

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Prosecution of offences either summarily or on indictment.[^c5689581]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Every offence under this Act may be prosecuted and every penalty under this Act may be recovered, and all explosives and ingredients liable to be forfeited under this Act may be forfeited either on indictment or before a court of summary jurisdiction, in manner directed by the Summary Jurisdiction Acts.

[^key-4421b3d8cf5911cb6cf8bde3eeea5876]...

All costs and money directed to be recovered as penalties may be recovered before a court of summary jurisdiction in manner directed by the Summary Jurisdiction Acts.

A court of summary jurisdiction may by order prohibit a person from doing any act for doing which such person has twice been convicted under this Act, and may order any person disobeying such summary order to be imprisoned for any period not exceeding six months.
