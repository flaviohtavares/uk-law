## 67. Definition of local authority.[^c5688771][^c5688761]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The local authority for the purposes of this Act, shall be—

(1) In the city of London, [^c-d94e63b6526dbb8133a3cd664efb745c]... the court of the Lord Mayor and aldermen of the said city; and

(1A)[^c-637c68ad9f7ebc32ef8a746253043810] In a London borough, the council of the borough; and

(2)[^c5688781] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c5688821] Outside Greater London and a metropolitan county[^c5688801] , the council of a county or county borough[^c5688811]; and

(3A) In a metropolitan county, the fire and rescue authority[^c-37d0edcffdedcc9b3134b4ff02de4f05]; [^c-0f255e801ba6fc59078b8582f7a18f59]...

(4)[^c-0f255e801ba6fc59078b8582f7a18f59] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c5688831] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
