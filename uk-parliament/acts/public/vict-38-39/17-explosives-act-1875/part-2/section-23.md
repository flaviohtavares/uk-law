# Explosives Act 1875, section 23

## 23 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note). Precautions against unauthorised access (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-67a5fa1858e7690f590280652b544488][^key-67a5fa1858e7690f590280652b544488]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67a5fa1858e7690f590280652b544488] The occupier of every premises at which gunpowder is manufactured or stored shall take all due precaution for preventing unauthorised persons having access to the premises or to the gunpowder therein. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67a5fa1858e7690f590280652b544488]

(2 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67a5fa1858e7690f590280652b544488] In the event of any breach (by any act or default) of subsection (1), the occupier shall be guilty of an offence. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67a5fa1858e7690f590280652b544488]

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67a5fa1858e7690f590280652b544488] In this section, “premises” has the meaning given in section 53 of the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67a5fa1858e7690f590280652b544488]

(3 (Extent: E+W+S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-67a5fa1858e7690f590280652b544488][^M_F_71e8a478-19e8-4098-c5d4-1d564f56eb9d] In this section, “premises” has the meaning given by Article 2(2) of the Health and Safety at Work (Northern Ireland) Order 1978. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-67a5fa1858e7690f590280652b544488]
