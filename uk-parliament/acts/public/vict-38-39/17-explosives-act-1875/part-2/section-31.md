# Explosives Act 1875, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2015-08-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/38-39/17

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Penalty for sale of gunpowder to children.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-6a945864056dc306c0ed092a62cadde8] Gunpowder shall not be sold to any person apparently under the age of sixteen[^c5687141] years; and any person selling gunpowder in contravention of this section shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale[^c5687151]

(2)[^c-300723c03f1ad240f06223175b10e7c8] Subsection (1) does not apply to—

- (a) pyrotechnic articles within the meaning set out in regulation 3 of the Pyrotechnic Articles (Safety) Regulations 2015; or
- (b) percussion caps intended specifically for toys within the meaning set out in regulation 4(2) of the Toys (Safety) Regulations 2011 ([S.I. 2011/1881](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2011/1881)).
