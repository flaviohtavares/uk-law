# Police Act 1964, section 55

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 55. Abolition of fees.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-08-22).**

Section 23 of the [^c11165191]Police Act 1890 (fees payable to constables), and any other enactment or rule of law whereby constables are authorised or required to take a fee for any act done in the course of their duty as such, shall cease to have effect.
