# Police Act 1964, section 2

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 2. Maintenance of police forces.[^c11162041]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-08-22).**

A police force shall be maintained for every police area for the time being listed in Schedule 1A to this Act.
