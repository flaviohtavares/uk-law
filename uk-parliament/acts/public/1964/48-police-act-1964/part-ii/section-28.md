# Police Act 1964, section 28

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28. General duty of Secretary of State.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-08-22).**

The Secretary of State shall exercise his powers under this Act in such manner and to such extent as appears to him to be best calculated to promote the efficiency and effectiveness[^c11163841] of the police.
