# Harbours Act 1964, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1964/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 58. Drainage and river authorities not to be harbour authorities for purposes of this Act if not possessing exceptional powers.

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of this Act neither an internal drainage board, the Environment Agency, the Natural Resources Body for Wales, a water[^c-752ba120fe7cc69fd9b82550e2ace9c6] undertaker[^c7744101][^c7744091], the Scottish Environment Protection Agency[^c7744111] a district board nor an improvement committee shall be taken to be a harbour authority if, apart from this provision, they would only be taken to be such by reason of the fact that river works powers or duties are vested in them; and any reference in this Act to functions of a harbour authority relating to a harbour shall be construed as not including a reference to such powers or duties.
