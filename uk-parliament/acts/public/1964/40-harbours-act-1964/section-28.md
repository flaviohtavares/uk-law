# Harbours Act 1964, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-09-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1964/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28. Repeal of provisions limiting discretion of nationalised transport bodies as to ship, passenger and merchandise dues chargeable at certain harbours owned or managed by them.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 9 to the [^c7738341]Transport Act 1962 shall cease to have effect in so far as it limits the discretion of the Boards as to the ship, passenger and merchandise dues chargeable by them at the harbours specified in that Schedule.
