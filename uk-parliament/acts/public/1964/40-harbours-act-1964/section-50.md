# Harbours Act 1964, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1964/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 50. Reckoning of periods.

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of this Act, in reckoning any period which is therein, or in an order thereunder, expressed to be a period from a given date, that date shall be excluded.
