## 54A. “Developments of national significance” etc.: special procedure[^c-2c52ed7b917f11f39b9c8a9e88bece89]

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-6e5eafa27a65fa4f6b0ba5fb204c36a5] Subsection (4) below applies to a harbour revision order or harbour empowerment order which falls within subsection (2) below.

(2) An order[^c-03fa14b343e4c9ec086c5783c73aabf7] falls within this subsection if—

- (a) it[^c-0a1b082f58e7588de03d20625ba83ff6] authorises the carrying out of work which would constitute a national development; or
- (b) it[^c-099d206161af4d2fa2aa89fd8d6ba8a7] is the subject of a direction by the Scottish Ministers under this paragraph.

(3) In subsection (2) above and subsection (5) below, references to a “national development” are to any development (within the meaning of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997) for the time being designated under section 3A(4)(b) of that Act as a national development.

(4)[^c-65117747551735328455683181c88fb6] The order is subject to the affirmative procedure.

(5) An order (“the new order”)[^c-3a8153c7afd8d817354aad9c2d5f4e89] which revokes, amends or re-enacts an order (“the old order”) which was subject to the affirmative procedure by virtue[^c-89287e73a07bb2797f8720043f3bdc35] of subsection (4) above is subject to the procedure in that subsection only if—

- (a) the new[^c-57dedf5a6e6c3e5757bba83d294e3bd9] order authorises the carrying out of work which would constitute a national development (other than a national development to which the old order[^c-42a050ca8a335f2c20fcae53f468d892] relates); or
- (b) the Scottish Ministers so direct.
