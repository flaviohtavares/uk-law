# Harbours Act 1964, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-10-23. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1964/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 45. Penalisation of furnishing false information.

*Extent: E+W+S*

A person who—

- (a) [^c7740391]. . . In purported compliance with a requirement imposed under section [^c7740401]. . . 41 of this Act, gives any information which he knows to be false in a material particular or makes a statement which he knows to be so false or recklessly gives any information which is so false or recklessly makes any statement which is so false; [^c7740411]. . .
- (b)[^c7740421] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable—

- (i) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months[^c7740431] or to a fine not exceeding £100, or to both;
- (ii) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or to a fine, or to both.
