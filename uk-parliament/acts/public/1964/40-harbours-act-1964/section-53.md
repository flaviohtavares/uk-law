# Harbours Act 1964, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-11-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1964/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 53. Saving for telegraphic lines.[^c7740981][^c7740991]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Part 10 of Schedule 3A to the Communications Act 2003 (the electronic communications code)[^c-79612c49b986a8b4d0f685a0ef6a09cb] (which provides a procedure for certain cases where works involve the alteration of electronic communications apparatus[^c-e191750b5f294c603f4864331ea70518]) shall apply, for the purposes of works in pursuance of a harbour revision order and works authorised by a harbour empowerment order, to the person authorised to execute those works.
