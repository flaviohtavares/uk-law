# British North America Act 1867, section 88

> Text as at 1991-02-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Constitutions of Legislatures of Nova Scotia and New Brunswick.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The constitution of the Legislature of each of the provinces of Nova Scotia and New Brunswick shall, subject to the provisions of this Act, continue as it exists at the union, until altered under the authority of this Act; . . . [^c5657671]
