# British North America Act 1867, section 73

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Qualification of legislative councillors.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The qualifications of the legislative councillors of Quebec shall be the same as those of the senators for Quebec.
