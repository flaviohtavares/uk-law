# British North America Act 1867, section 27

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Reduction of Senate to normal number.[^c5657601]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In case of such Addition being at any Time made, the Governor General shall not summon any Person to the Senate, except on a further like Direction by the Queen on the like Recommendation, until each of the Three Divisions of Canada is represented by Twenty-four Senators and no more.

## Annotations

[^c5657601]: Modification: Number of divisions increased to four by [British North America Act 1915 (c. 45)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1915/45), [s. 1(1)(iv)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1915/45/section/1/1/iv)
