# British North America Act 1867, section 21

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21. Number of senators.[^c5657561]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Senate shall, subject to the Provisions of this Act, consist of Seventy-Two members, who shall be styled Senators.

## Annotations

[^c5657561]: Modification: Number of divisions increased from three to four, and provision for fourth division made by [British North America Act 1915 (c. 45)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1915/45), [s. 1(1)(ii)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1915/45/section/1/1/ii)
