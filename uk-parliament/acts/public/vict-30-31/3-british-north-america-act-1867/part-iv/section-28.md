# British North America Act 1867, section 28

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Maximum number of senators.[^c5657611]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Number of Senators shall not at any Time exceed Seventy-eight.

## Annotations

[^c5657611]: Modification: Maximum number of senators increased to one hundred and four by [British North America Act 1915 (c. 45)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1915/45), [s. 1(1)(v)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1915/45/section/1/1/v)
