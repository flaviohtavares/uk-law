# British North America Act 1867, section 30

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Resignation of place in Senate.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A Senator may by Writing under his Hand addressed to the Governor General of resign his Place in the Senate, and thereupon the same shall be vacant.
