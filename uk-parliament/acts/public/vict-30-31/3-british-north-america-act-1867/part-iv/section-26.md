# British North America Act 1867, section 26

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Addition of senators in certain cases.[^c5657591]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If at any time on the Recommendation of the Governor General the Queen thinks fit to direct that Three or Six Members be added to the Senate, the Governor General may by Summons to Three or Six qualified Persons (as the Case may be), representing equally the Three Divisions of Canada, add to the Senate accordingly.

## Annotations

[^c5657591]: Modification: Number of members increased to four or eight, representing equally the four divisions, by [British North America Act 1915 (c. 45)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1915/45), [s. 1(1)(iii)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1915/45/section/1/1/iii)
