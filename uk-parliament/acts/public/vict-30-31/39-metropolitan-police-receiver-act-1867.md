Metropolitan Police (Receiver) Act 1867

1867 c.39

An Act for amending the Law with respect to the Accounts of the Receiver for the Metropolitan Police District; and for other Purposes relating to the Metropolitan Police.

\[15th July 1867\][^c5658081][^c5658091]

## 1. As to the Accounts to be laid before Parliament.[^c5658101]

The amount of all monies received and expended for the purposes of the Metropolitan Police Act 1829, shall be made up to the thirty-first day of March [^c5658111]. . . in each year [^c5658121]. . .
