# Protection of Animals (Anaesthetics) Act 1954

> Text as at 2007-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Protection of Animals (Anaesthetics) Act 1954

1954 c.46

An Act to repeal the Animals (Anaesthetics) Act, 1919, and to extend the provisions of the Protection of Animals Acts in relation to the performance of operations on animals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976]

\[30th July 1954\][^c6194581][^c6194591][^c6194601][^key-aff2e7722e59d1c0f9f42d584f820690]

## 1. Use of anaesthetics in operations on animals.

(1) If any operation to which this section applies is performed on any animal without the use of an anaesthetic so administered as to prevent any pain during the operation, that operation shall be deemed for the purposes of the principal Act to be an operation which is performed without due care and humanity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976]

*Extent: S*

(1A)[^key-ee21f8c2ea0c1b4dd40f0e70624a1c59] An operation to which this section applies that is performed on an animal without the use of an anaesthetic administered so as to prevent pain during the operation is, for the purposes of section 21 of the Animal Health and Welfare (Scotland) Act 2006 (asp 11), to be regarded as being performed without due care and humanity. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976]

(2) This section applies to any operation with or without the use of instruments which involves interference with the sensitive tissues or the bone structure of an animal, other than— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976]

- (a) the making of injections or extractions by means of a hollow needle; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976]
- (b) an operation included in the First Schedule to this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976]

(3)[^c6194611][^c6194621] The Minister of Agriculture and Fisheries and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976] the Secretary of State for Scotland and the Secretary of State for Wales[^c6194631] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976], acting jointly, may, after consultation with the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons and with such persons appearing to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976] those Ministers *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976] to represent agricultural interests as may appear to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976] those Ministers *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976] appropriate, by order amend any of the ages specified in paragraph 6 of the said First Schedule; and any order under this subsection— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976]

- (a) shall be made by statutory instrument, which shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976]
- (b) may be varied or revoked by a subsequent order thereunder. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976]

(4) This Act shall be construed as one with the principal Act, so, however, that in this Act the expression “animal” shall not include a fowl or other bird, fish or reptile. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976]

(5) In this section, the expression “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976]**the principal Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976]

- (a) in relation to England and Wales, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976] [^c6194661]Protection of Animals Act, 1911; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976]
- (b)[^key-8447781a4b9c9b36a637c14b78a640b6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

as amended by or under any other enactment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a7b3729727a25a574fdc9f8ada2d3976]
