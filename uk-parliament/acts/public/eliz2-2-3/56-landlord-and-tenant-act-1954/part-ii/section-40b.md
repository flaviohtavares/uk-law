## 40B. Proceedings for breach of duties to give information[^c-15214738abf923ddd589b8f88abac917]

*Extent: E+W*

A claim that a person has broken any duty imposed by section 40 of this Act may be made the subject of civil proceedings for breach of statutory duty; and in any such proceedings a court may order that person to comply with that duty and may make an award of damages.
