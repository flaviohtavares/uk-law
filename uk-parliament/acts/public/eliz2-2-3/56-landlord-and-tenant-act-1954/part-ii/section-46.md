# Landlord and Tenant Act 1954, section 46

## 46. Interpretation of Part II.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-3f9ae37ecf5578ce2d1dc6706ed47d24] In this Part of this Act:—

- “ **business** ” has the meaning assigned to it by subsection (2) of section twenty-three of this Act;
- “current tenancy” means the tenancy under which the tenant holds for the time being;[^key-975b7db11ce3651d15be18575280beb9]
- “ **date of termination** ” has the meaning assigned to it by subsection (1) of section twenty-five of this Act;
- subject to the provisions of section thirty-two of this Act, “ **the holding** ” has the meaning assigned to it by subsection (3) of section twenty-three of this Act;
- “interim rent” has the meaning given by section 24A(1) of this Act;[^key-3f564a83731de0d06024e4c9c30a8af8]
- “ **mining lease** ” has the same meaning as in the [^c318928] Landlord and Tenant Act 1927.

(2)[^key-912b3623480e7613a2c7718c0f69b71b] For the purposes of this Part of this Act, a person has a controlling interest in a company, if, had he been a company, the other company would have been its subsidiary; and in this Part—

- “company” has the meaning given by section 1(1) of the Companies Act 2006[^key-68c7a21787f6ca60b13f34bf5a5197a7]; and
- “subsidiary” has the meaning given by section 1159 of that Act[^key-3e9961c813659d252ec86c1d4431298f].
