# Landlord and Tenant Act 1954, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-07-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Eliz2/2-3/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Protection of residential tenants on termination of long tenancies at low rents.[^c318760][^c318759]

*Extent: E+W*

On the termination in accordance with the provisions of this Part of this Act of a tenancy to which this section applies the tenant shall be entitled to the protection of the Rent Act[^c318761] subject to and in accordance with those provisions.
