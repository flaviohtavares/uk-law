# Mines and Quarries Act 1954, section 35

> Text as at 2015-05-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 35. Height and width of travelling roads.[^c650726]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Every length of road in a mine which is used at the beginning or end of a shift by not less than ten persons for the purpose of walking to or from their working places in the mine, being a length made after the commencement of this Act, shall be not less than 1.7 metres[^c650727] high throughout:

Provided that—

- (a) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c650728]
- (b) an inspector may, by notice served on the manager of a particular mine, exempt from those provisions any such length of road as aforesaid in that mine or any part of any such length of road.
