# Mines and Quarries Act 1954, section 166

> Text as at 2015-05-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 166. Liability of parents for unlawful employment of children.

*Extent: E+W+S*

For the purposes of any proceedings under this Act in respect of the employment of children in contravention of . . . [^c651129], section one hundred and thirty six of the[^c651130] [^c651131]Education (Scotland) Act 1962, or section one of the [^c651132]Employment of Women, Young Persons and Children Act 1920 (being enactments which prohibit the employment of children in factories and[^c651133], mines [^c651134]. . . and, so far as they relate to mines [^c651134]. . ., are incorporated with this Act), the references in section one hundred and sixty of this Act to a young person shall be construed as including references to a child within the meaning of . . . [^c651129] the said section seventeen or the said section one, as the case may be.
