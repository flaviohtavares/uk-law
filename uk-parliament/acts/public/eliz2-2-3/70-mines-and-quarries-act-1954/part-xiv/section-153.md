# Mines and Quarries Act 1954, section 153

> Text as at 2015-05-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 153. Accessories.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Without prejudice to the operation—

- (a) as respects England and Wales, of section eight of the [^c651074]Accessories and Abettors Act 1861, and section 44 of the[^c651075] [^c651076]Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980; and
- (b) as respects Scotland, of section sixty-one of the [^c651077]Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1887, and section two of the [^c651078]Summary Jurisdiction (Scotland) Act 1954;

any person who induces or procures, or consents to or connives at, the commission of an offence under this Act, shall be guilty of an offence.
