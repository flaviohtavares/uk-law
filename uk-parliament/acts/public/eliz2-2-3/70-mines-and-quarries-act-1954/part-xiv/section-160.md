# Mines and Quarries Act 1954, section 160

> Text as at 2015-05-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 160. Liability of parents for unlawful employment of young persons

*Extent: E+W+S*

If a young person is employed at a mine [^c651110]. . . in contravention of the provisions of this Act, the parent of the young person shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale[^c651111], unless it appears to the court that the contravention occurred without the consent, connivance or wilful default of the parent.
