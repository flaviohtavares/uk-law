# Mines and Quarries Act 1954, section 193

> Text as at 2015-05-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 193. Saving for common law rights of workmen, &c.

*Extent: E+W+S*

No provision of this Act, of any order made thereunder or of regulations shall be construed as derogating from any rule of law with respect to the duties owed by masters to their servants (including, in particular, but without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing words, the duty to provide a safe system of working), and section one of this Act shall not be construed as derogating from any obligation imposed by or by virtue of any other provision of this Act upon the owner of a mine [^c651334]. . ..
