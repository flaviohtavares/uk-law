# Mines and Quarries Act 1954, section 179

> Text as at 2015-05-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 179. Application to the Crown.[^c651167]

*Extent: E+W+S*

This Act shall apply to mines [^c651168]. . . belonging to Her Majesty in right of the Crown or to a Government department or held in trust for Her Majesty for the purposes of a Government department. [^c651167]
