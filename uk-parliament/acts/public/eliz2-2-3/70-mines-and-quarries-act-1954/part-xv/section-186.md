# Mines and Quarries Act 1954, section 186

> Text as at 2015-05-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 186. Repeal of s. 21 of 16 & 17 Geo. 5. c. 28.[^c651310]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section twenty-one of the Mining Industry Act, 1926 (which enablesprovision to be made for the establishment of joint committees forcoal mines) shall cease to have effect.
