> Text as at 2015-05-26.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 173. Revocation and variation of orders and rules.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any power conferred by this Act to make a . . . [^c651145] a rule shall be construed as including a power, exercisable in the like manner and subject to the like conditions, if any, to revoke or vary the . . . [^c651145] or rule.
