## 32. Orders and regulations.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any power conferred by any provision of this Act to make an order or regulations shall be exercisable by statutory instrument.

(2) [^c4854561]. . ., any statutory instrument made under any power conferred by this Act shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of the House of Commons.

(3)[^c19495021] But in the case of an order under section 26H(4) which has the effect of adding to the class of activities in respect of which remote gaming duty is chargeable—

- (a) subsection (2) above shall not apply, and
- (b) the order may not be made unless a draft has been laid before and approved by resolution of the House of Commons.
