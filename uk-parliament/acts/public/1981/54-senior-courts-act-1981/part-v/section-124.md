# Senior Courts Act 1981, section 124

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/54

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 124. Place for deposit of original wills and other documents.

*Extent: E+W*

All original wills and other documents which are under the control of the High Court in the Principal Registry or in any district probate registry shall be deposited and preserved in such places as may be provided for in directions given in accordance with Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the Constitutional Reform Act 2005[^c20693001]; and any wills or other documents so deposited shall, subject to the control of the High Court and to probate rules, be open to inspection.
