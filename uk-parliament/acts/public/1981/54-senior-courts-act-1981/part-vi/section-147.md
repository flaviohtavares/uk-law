# Senior Courts Act 1981, section 147

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 147. Amendment of Solicitors Act 1974.[^c12337031]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 50 of the [^c12337041]Solicitors Act 1974 (jurisdiction of Senior Courts[^c20725901] over solicitors), after subsection (2) there shall be inserted—

> (3) An appeal shall lie to the Court of Appeal from any order made against a solicitor by the High Court or the Crown Court in the exercise of its jurisdiction in respect of solicitors under subsection (2).

.
