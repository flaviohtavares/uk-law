# Senior Courts Act 1981, section 86B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 86B. Rules to be made if required by Lord Chancellor[^c20690831]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if the Lord Chancellor gives the Lord Chief Justice[^key-b0e68a254c465d09c74e249224af142f] written notice that he thinks it is expedient for Crown Court rules to include provision that would achieve a purpose specified in the notice.

(2) The Lord Chief Justice[^key-5190f10be1f8b109c09d4666933ccce2] must make such Crown Court rules as the Lord Chief Justice[^key-0f091f6195351cc1b787f48f00877f0c] considers necessary to achieve the specified purpose.

(3) Those rules must be—

- (a) made within a reasonable period after the Lord Chancellor gives notice to the Lord Chief Justice[^key-9d1e5ddf91086380453075043c8d9078];
- (b) made in accordance with section 86A.
