# Senior Courts Act 1981, section 80

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Process to compel appearance.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any direction to appear and any condition of a recognizance to appear before the Crown Court, and any summons or order to appear before that court, may be so framed as to require appearance at such time and place as may be directed by the Crown Court, and if a time or place is specified in the direction, condition, summons or order, it may be varied by any subsequent direction of the Crown Court.

(2) Where an indictment has been signed although the person charged has not been sent[^c20642681] for trial, the Crown Court may issue a summons requiring that person to appear before the Crown Court, or may issue a warrant for his arrest.

(3) Section 4 of the [^c12334651]Summary Jurisdiction (Process) Act 1881 (execution of process of English courts in Scotland) shall apply to process issued under this section as it applies to process issued under the [^c12334661]Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 by a magistrates’ court.
