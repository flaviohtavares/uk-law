# Senior Courts Act 1981, section 93

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. Status of officers for purposes of salary and pension.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subject to subsection (2), any person who holds an office listed in column 1 of any Part of Schedule 2 or the office of Accountant General of the[^c12335971] Senior Courts[^c20715151] and is not employed in the civil service of the State shall be deemed to be so employed for the purposes of salary and pension.

(2) Subsection (1), so far as it relates to pension, shall not apply to a person holding qualifying judicial office, within the meaning of the[^c12335981] [^c12335991]Judicial Pensions and Retirement Act 1993.
