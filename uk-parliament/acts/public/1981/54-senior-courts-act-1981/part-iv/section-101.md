## 101. Power of one district registrar to act for another.[^c20692911]

*Extent: E+W*

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

(1) A district judge[^c12336141] of any registry shall be capable of acting in any other district registry for a district judge of that registry; and, where a district judge is so acting, the district judge of the other registry may divide the duties of his office as he thinks fit between himself and the district judge acting for him.
