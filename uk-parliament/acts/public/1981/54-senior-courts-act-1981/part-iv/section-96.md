# Senior Courts Act 1981, section 96

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 96. Central Office.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Central Office of the Senior Courts[^c20715161] shall perform such business as the Lord Chief Justice may, with the concurrence of the Lord Chancellor,[^c20691351] direct.

(2) Subject to any direction under subsection (1)[^c20691371], the Central Office shall perform such business as it performed immediately before the commencement of this Act.

(3)[^c20691391] The Lord Chief Justice may nominate a judicial office holder (as defined in section 109(4) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005) to exercise his functions under this section.
