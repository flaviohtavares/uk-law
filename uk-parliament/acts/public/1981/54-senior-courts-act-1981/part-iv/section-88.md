# Senior Courts Act 1981, section 88

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Qualification for office.

*Extent: E+W*

A person shall not be qualified for appointment to any office in the Senior Courts[^c20714951] listed in column 1 of any Part of Schedule 2 unless he is a person of any description specified in relation to that office in column 2 of that Part.
