# Judicial Pensions Act 1981, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Recommendation of a Minister required in certain cases.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The grant under this Part of this Act of a lump sum or widow's, widower's, surviving civil partner's[^c20867401] or children’s pension conditional on eligibility for a pension requiring the recommendation to the Treasury or any Minister of the Crown[^c10349191] shall require the like recommendation.
