# Judicial Pensions Act 1981, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Widows', widowers' and surviving civil partners' pensions[^c10348321][^c20866601]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No widow's, widower's or surviving civil partner's[^c20866811] pension may be granted if the marriage or formation of the civil partnership[^c20866671] with the deceased took place after he or she retired from relevant service.

(2) A widow's, widower's or surviving civil partner's[^c20866781] pension shall come to an end on the death of the widow, widower or surviving civil partner[^c20866701].

(3) Where a widow's, widower's or surviving civil partner's[^c20866721] pension is payable the Treasury may, on or at any time after

- (a)[^c20866741] the remarriage of, or formation of a civil partnership by, the widow or widower, or
- (b) the formation of a subsequent civil partnership by, or the marriage of, the surviving civil partner,

direct that it shall cease to be payable.

(4) Where such a direction has been given the Treasury may at any time direct that payment of the pension is to be resumed.

(5) The annual amount of a widow's, widower's or surviving civil partner's[^c20866761] pension may be one half of the annual amount of the personal pension.
