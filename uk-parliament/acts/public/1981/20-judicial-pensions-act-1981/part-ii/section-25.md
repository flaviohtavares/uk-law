# Judicial Pensions Act 1981, section 25

## 25. Persons serving again after retirement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where any person after retirement from service in a judicial office resumes [^c10349071]. . . service, that retirement shall be left out of account for all the purposes of this Part of this Act except that—

- (a) if a lump sum was granted on that retirement without any contribution by virtue of section 23[^c-4b5e75119a60de9aba7045bd5c417e74] being made, then unless that person on resumption of [^c10349071]. . . service, or within three months of [^c10349071]. . . marrying or forming a civil partnership[^c20867321] while again serving, refunds by way of contribution such sum, not exceeding three quarters of the lump sum granted on the prior retirement as the Treasury[^c10349101] may determine, no pension shall be granted to any widow, widower[^c10349111] , surviving civil partner[^c20867341] or child of that person, and
- (b) any lump sum granted on that retirement, less any refund, shall be set off against any lump sum to be granted in respect of that person’s[^c10349121] service.

(2)[^c10349131] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Where a refund is made under subsection (1) above, all such adjustments shall be made, including payments out of the Consolidated Fund or out of money provided by Parliament and payments into the Exchequer, as will secure that the position is the same as if the lump sum had been reduced by an amount equal to the refund and no refund had been made.
