# Judicial Pensions Act 1981, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/20

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Disregard of abatement of pension under s. 65 of Social Security Act 1973.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In making any calculation under this Part of this Act any abatement of a pension falling to be made under any order made under section 65 of the [^c10349141]Social Security Act 1973 shall be left out of account.
