# Judicial Pensions Act 1981, section 18A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18A. Widowers’ and surviving civil partners' pensions[^c10348301][^c20866481]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 18 above shall have effect in relation to the death of a female person who has, or has had, a husband or civil partner[^c20866501] as it has effect in relation to the death of a male person who has, or has had, a wife or civil partner[^c20866521] but as if—

- (a) for the words “widow”, “widow’s pension” and “wife” there were substituted “widower”, “widower’s pension” and “husband”; and
- (b) for the words “his”, “he” and “him” there were substituted “hers”, “she” and “her”.

(2) The transitional provisions in Part IV of Schedule 2 to this Act shall have effect in relation to widowers’ pensions.

(2A)[^c20866561] The transitional provisions in Part 5 of Schedule 2 to this Act shall have effect in relation to surviving civil partners' pensions.
