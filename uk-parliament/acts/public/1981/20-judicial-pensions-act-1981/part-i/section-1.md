# Judicial Pensions Act 1981, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Chapter “**high judicial office**” means any of the offices listed in the first column of the Table below, and “**relevant service**”, in relation to high judicial office, has the meaning given in the second column of that Table.

Table

| *Office* | *Relevant service* |
| --- | --- |
| Judge of the Supreme Court[^c20869461]. | Service as a judge of the Supreme Court[^c20869481] or as a judge of the Senior Courts[^c20869551], the Court of Session or the Court of Judicature[^c20869591]. |
| Judge of the Senior Courts[^c20867911]. . . . | Service as a judge of the Supreme Court or as any judge of the Senior Courts. |
| Judge of the Court of Session. | Service as a judge of the Supreme Court or as any judge of the Court of Session. |
| Judge of the Court of Judicature. | Service as a judge of the Supreme Court or as any judge of the Court of Judicature. |
