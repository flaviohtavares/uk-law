# Judicial Pensions Act 1981, section 4

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/20

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Provision against double pensions.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Not more than one pension shall be paid to the same person under this Chapter, or under this Chapter and the [^c10347731]Lord Chancellor’s Pension Act 1832.
