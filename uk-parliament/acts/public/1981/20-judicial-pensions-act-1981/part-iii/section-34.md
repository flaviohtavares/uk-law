# Judicial Pensions Act 1981, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/20

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. County court registrars and assistant registrars.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Act the annual salary of a county court registrar or assistant registrar who is also a district registrar of the High Court shall be deemed to include any salary payable in respect of his services as district registrar.

(2)[^c10350301] Nothing in this Act shall apply to a part-time registrar or part-time assistant registrar within the meaning of subsection (3) of section 10 of the County Courts Act 1984[^c10350311] (restrictions on practice).
