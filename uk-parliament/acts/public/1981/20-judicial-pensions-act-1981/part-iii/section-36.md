# Judicial Pensions Act 1981, section 36

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Transitory provisions, amendments and repeals.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedules 2 and 3 to this Act (transitory provisions and consequential amendments) shall have effect.

(2)[^c10350331] The enactments and instruments specified in Schedule 4 to this Act shall be repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
