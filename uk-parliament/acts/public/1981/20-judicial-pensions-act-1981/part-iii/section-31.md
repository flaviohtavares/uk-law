# Judicial Pensions Act 1981, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Payments charged on Consolidated Fund.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be charged on, and paid out of, the Consolidated Fund—

- (a) any pension under Chapter I of Part I, or section 5 or 7, of this Act, and
- (b) any lump sum, or widow's, widower's, surviving civil partner's[^c20867451] or children’s pension, under Part II of this Act conditional on eligibility for a pension payable out of the Consolidated Fund.
