# Judicial Pensions Act 1981, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2020-12-31. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Short title and commencement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Judicial Pensions Act 1981.

(2) This Act shall come into force at the expiration of a period of one month beginning with the date on which it is passed.
