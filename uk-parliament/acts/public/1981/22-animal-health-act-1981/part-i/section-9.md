# Animal Health Act 1981, section 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/22

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 9. Prohibition in specific cases.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The Ministers may make such orders as they think fit for prohibiting the conveyance of animals by any specified vessel or aircraft to or from any port or aerodrome in the United Kingdom or by any specified vehicle through the tunnel system as defined in the Channel Tunnel Act 1987[^c10345011] for such time as the Ministers may consider expedient.
