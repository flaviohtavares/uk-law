## 71A. Prosecutions: time limit[^key-7c1b7e41c04c819f288b8c814588980c][^key-4a04c5adb3de78e8f1e8b4745a17ab66][^key-91306986e98f5fa40ca8c60f8f1512ce][^key-097931145a065e4ba3b0b2454c13d2b1][^key-33f2afcc9b3aea455bbf82f962090b95][^key-aa4c5635f0864648ab2b2f44d18b32a2][^key-fd65699cd4f0019de5de142ad729bdd8][^key-d62b061515cf806ee0cfc90fe1cb65e1][^key-1e57394a11ed637a8333751ccc957797][^key-00752d2abac17158647f14fd51f27675][^key-dfc953c51a626a1b6736417f947df256][^key-90430ec8ec5f44d14a24ca2c47b70c1a][^key-71c3c60a52b3fea1b1781acb97f6f505][^key-de099887aabd8aae939a2b6bfc3028b1][^key-6172fb2117243977d2bb3e51f3b34a62][^key-6d2ebbd38e23cdd7c80b4f88e60d0c84][^c10346611]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Despite anything in section 127(1) of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 an information relating to an offence under this Act which is triable by a magistrates’ court in England and Wales may be so tried if it is laid at any time—

- (a) within the period of three years starting with the date of the commission of the offence, and
- (b) within the period of six months starting with the day on which evidence which the prosecutor thinks is sufficient to justify the proceedings comes to his knowledge.

(2) A certificate by the prosecutor as to the date on which such evidence came to his knowledge is conclusive evidence of that fact.
