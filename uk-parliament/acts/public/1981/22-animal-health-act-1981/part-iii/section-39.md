## 39. Export of animals generally.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa] The Ministers may by order provide in the interests of animal welfare for regulating the exportation from Great Britain of animals, and in particular— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa] for prohibiting exportation without such certificate or licence as may be prescribed by the order; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa] as to the circumstances in which and conditions on which a certificate or licence may be obtained. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa] Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1) above, an order under this section may include provision for requiring persons proposing to export animals from Great Britain to furnish information about— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa] the intended ultimate destination of the animals; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa] the arrangements for conveying them to that destination; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa] any other matters which may be specified in the order. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa]
