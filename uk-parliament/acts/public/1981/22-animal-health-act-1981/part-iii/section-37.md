## 37. Prevention of suffering.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa] The Ministers may make such orders as they think fit for the purpose of protecting animals from unnecessary suffering— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa] during inland transit, including transit by an aircraft on a flight beginning and ending in Great Britain; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa] while exposed for sale; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa] while awaiting removal after being exposed for sale. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa] The Ministers may make such orders as they think fit— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa] for ensuring for animals carried by sea or by air proper ventilation during the passage and on landing; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa] for protecting them from unnecessary suffering during the passage and on landing. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0405ae0e34af380d36979a2b80a7c4fa]
