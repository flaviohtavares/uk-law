# Animal Health Act 1981, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Additional staff and expenses.

*Extent: E+W+S*

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c-f002ace48e77cbf61e3ed57790ed019b] The Minister may, for the purposes of his powers under this Act relating to the slaughter by him of animals, birds or amphibians,[^c-1b6ce052557af469c7f4e1108511456e] employ such additional inspectors, valuers and other persons, and at such remuneration, and may incur such expenses, as, subject to the approval of the Treasury[^c10345841], he thinks necessary.

*Extent: S*

(2)[^c-2007c7a6f23113b04c18440aa406383f] In this section, “**animals**” means any kind of mammal (except man).
