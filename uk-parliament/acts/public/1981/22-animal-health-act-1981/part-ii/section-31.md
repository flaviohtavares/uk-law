# Animal Health Act 1981, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Slaughter in certain diseases.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Schedule 3 to this Act has effect as to the slaughter of animals in relation to—

- (a) cattle plague;
- (b) pleuro-pneumonia;
- (c) foot-and-mouth disease;
- (d)[^c-d13551af2038816e89567556ea0791b5] swine-fever; and
- (e) diseases of poultry.
