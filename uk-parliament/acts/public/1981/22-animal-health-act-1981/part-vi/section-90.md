# Animal Health Act 1981, section 90

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Application to hovercraft.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The enactments and instruments with respect to which provision may be made by Order in Council in pursuance of section 1(1)(h) of the [^c10347211]Hovercraft Act 1968 include this Act and any instrument made or having effect as if made under it.

This section is without prejudice to section 17 of the [^c10347221]Interpretation Act 1978 (repeal and re-enactment).
