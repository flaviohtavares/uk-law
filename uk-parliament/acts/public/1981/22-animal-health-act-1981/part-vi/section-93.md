# Animal Health Act 1981, section 93

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/22

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. Communications to and from Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In order to secure uniformity of action—

- (a) every order of the Minister shall with all practicable speed be communicated to the Department of Agriculture for Northern Ireland; and
- (b) every order made by that Department under the enactments in Northern Ireland relating to diseases of animals shall with all practicable speed be communicated to the Minister.
