## 82. Information from Agricultural Marketing Boards.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Every board administering a scheme under the [^c10346881]Agricultural Marketing Act 1958 shall give to the appropriate Minister such information as he may reasonably require for the purposes of his functions under this Act.
