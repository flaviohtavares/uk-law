> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Registration of minors.[^c19269961][^c-928bcb5c552237a98d1bc1a7bafb5a6c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^c-f4ff7a503f07ac4defce21139e55fe83]... If while a person is a minor an application is made for his registration as a British subject, the Secretary of State may, if he thinks fit, cause him to be registered as a British subject.
