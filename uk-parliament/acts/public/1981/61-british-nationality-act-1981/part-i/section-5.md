# British Nationality Act 1981, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2025-12-02. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/61
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Acquisition by registration: British overseas territories citizens having connection with Gibraltar[^c19269421][^c-08a05b90060c77ede7e0beee96aa0369][^c-1f5029ba94e993a09036c5586300adfd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^c-3fd834fbd92c186e2f9d2d9eaf25b8b4]... A person who is a British overseas territories citizen by virtue of a connection with Gibraltar is entitled to be registered as a British citizen if an application is made for their registration as such a citizen.
