# British Nationality Act 1981, section 4E

## 4E. The general conditions[^key-916470acf2beb9d58eb41842c93b59b8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of sections 4F to 4I, a person (“P”) meets the general conditions if—

- (a)[^key-749eb3952fc01f490eb858cf163819bb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) at the time of P's birth, P's mother—
  - (i) was not married, or
  - (ii) was married to a person other than P's natural father;
- (c) no person is treated as the father of P under section 28 of the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act 1990 or under section 35 or 36 of the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act 2008[^key-d64995f90f220347c88c4ef37ab2954c];
- (ca)[^key-f5178ca02de31d390e77079ccc0ea86d] no person is treated as a parent of P under section 42 or 43 of the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Act 2008; and
- (d) P has never been a British citizen.
