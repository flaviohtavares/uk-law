# British Nationality Act 1981, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2025-12-02. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/61
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Citizens of U.K. and Colonies who are to become British Overseas citizens at commencement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any person who was a citizen of the United Kingdom and Colonies immediately before commencement and who does not at commencement become either a British citizen or a British overseas territories citizen[^c16763151] shall at commencement become a British Overseas citizen.
