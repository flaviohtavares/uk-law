# British Nationality Act 1981, section 44A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2025-12-02. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/61
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44A. Waiver of requirement for full capacity[^c19270601]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a provision of this Act requires an applicant to be of full capacity, the Secretary of State may waive the requirement in respect of a specified applicant if he thinks it in the applicant's best interests.
