# Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981, section 88

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/14

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 88. Transitional provisions, consequential amendments and repeals.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This Act shall have effect subject to the transitional provisions set out in Schedule 6 to this Act.

(2)[^c2523741] The enactments specified in Schedule 7 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments specified in that Schedule, being amendments consequential on the provisions of this Act.

(3)[^c2523741] The enactments specified in Schedule 8 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
