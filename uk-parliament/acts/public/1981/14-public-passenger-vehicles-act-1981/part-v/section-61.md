## 61. Exercise of regulation making powers and parliamentary control thereof.[^key-a5c1dd3b809606d7796c398308b389d2][^key-a5bdc9b4c34d3bea05a5ac5475d699ae]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any power conferred by this Act upon the Secretary of State to make regulations shall be exercisable by statutory instrument which shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(2)[^key-982b563c16b4e54ecfe2e53ed1d9b607] Before making any regulations under . . . [^c2521571] this Act the Secretary of State shall consult with such representative organisations as he thinks fit.
