# Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981, section 56

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/14

## 56. Records of licences.[^c2520671]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Each traffic commissioner[^c-9300d43d2cea069abaac0668fa363bef] [^c2520691]... shall keep a record in such form and containing such particulars as may be prescribed of all licences granted by [^c2520701]... him under this Act and shall allow the record to be inspected at all reasonable times by members of the public[^c2520711].

(2)[^c2520721] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) A record kept under this section shall be admissible in evidence of the matters required under this Act to be entered therein, and a copy of an entry made in such a record in pursuance of this section purporting to be signed by or on behalf of the authority by whom the record is kept and to be certified to be a true copy shall be evidence of the matters stated in that entry without proof of the signature or authority of the person signing the same.
