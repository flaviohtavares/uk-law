## 86. Saving for sections 16 and 17 of the Interpretation Act 1978.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The inclusion in this Act of any express saving or amendment shall not be taken as prejudicing the operation of section 16 or 17 of the [^c2523711]Interpretation Act 1978 (which relate to the effect of repeals).
