## 53. Payment of expenses.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) There shall be paid in every year out of moneys provided by Parliament such sums as the Secretary of State may, with the consent of the Minister for the Civil Service, direct in respect of the salaries, remuneration, establishment charges, and other expenses of . . . [^c2520551] traffic commissioners, [^c2520561]. . ., and any other officer or servant appointed by the Secretary of State for the purposes of Part I, or[^c2520571] II . . . [^c2520581] of this Act, including any expenses incurred in connection with the employment of police officers as [^c2520561]. . . vehicle examiners.
