## 69. Restriction on institution in England and Wales of proceedings under Part II or III.[^c2522341]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this section proceedings for an offence under Part II . . . [^c2522351] of this Act shall not, in England or Wales, be instituted except by or on behalf of the Director of Public Prosecutions or by a person authorised in that behalf by a traffic commissioner[^c2522361], a chief officer of police, or the council of a county or district.

(2) Subsection (1) above shall not apply to proceedings for the breach of regulations having effect by virtue of section 25 or 26 of this Act.
