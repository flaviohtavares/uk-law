# Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981, section 66A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 66A. Issue of false documents.[^c16626381]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) If a person issues

- (a) a certificate of initial fitness under section 6 of this Act; or[^c2522121]
- (b) a notice removing a prohibition under section 9(1) of this Act;

which he knows to be false in a material particular, he shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale[^c2522131].

(2) If a constable, an examiner appointed under section 66A of the Road Traffic Act 1988[^c2522141] or an authorised inspector has reasonable cause to believe that a document produced to him or carried on a vehicle by its driver is a document in relation to which an offence has been committed under this section, he may seize the document.

(3) The power to seize a document under subsection (2) above includes power to detach a document carried on a vehicle from the vehicle.
