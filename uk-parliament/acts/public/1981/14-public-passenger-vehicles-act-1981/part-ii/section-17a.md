# Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981, section 17A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 17A. Assessors to assist traffic commissioners.[^c16021911]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In considering any financial question which appears to him to arise in relation to the exercise of his functions under section 14, 16 or 17 of this Act or under the[^c-25690863572a78441ecfd0d61b37ff03] 2009 Regulation, a traffic commissioner may be assisted by an assessor drawn from a panel of persons appointed by the Secretary of State for the purposes of this section.

(2) A traffic commissioner shall pay to any such assessor, in respect of his services, such remuneration as may be determined by the Secretary of State with the consent of the Treasury.
