# Public Passenger Vehicles Act 1981, section 16A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2026-08-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/14

## 16A. Conditions as to matters required to be notified.[^c16021901]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) On issuing a standard licence, a traffic commissioner shall attach to it the following conditions, namely—

- (a) a condition requiring the licence-holder to inform a traffic commissioner[^key-bbda760bb84a7fe043368a62c865d8a5] of any event which could affect the fulfilment by the licence-holder of any of the requirements of section 14ZA(2)[^key-8006b69cd4c5bc69b9e11c21778238c7] of this Act, and to do so within 28 days of the event; and
- (b) a condition requiring the licence-holder to inform a traffic commissioner of any event which could affect the fulfilment by a [^key-ffd35f5b707b0d7074bc50f4325aec2a]... transport manager of the requirements mentioned in section 14ZA(3)(a) and (b)[^key-fdf96db1ec0d5536952ed48214ef953e] of this Act, and to do so within 28 days of the event coming to the licence-holder’s knowledge.

(2)[^key-c9e235cdadce21a7f079543f37828b36] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Any person who contravenes any condition attached under this section to a licence of which he is the holder is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale.
