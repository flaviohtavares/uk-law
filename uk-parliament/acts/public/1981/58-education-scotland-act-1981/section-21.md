# Education (Scotland) Act 1981, section 21

> Text as at 2012-04-02.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 21. Amendment of enactments, transitional provisions and repeals.

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c751808] The enactments specified in Schedule 7 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments there specified being minor amendments and amendments consequential upon the provisions of this Act.

(2) The transitional provisions set out in Schedule 8 to this Act shall have effect.

(3)[^c751808] The enactments specified in Schedule 9 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
