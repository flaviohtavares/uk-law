# Finance Act 1981, section 128

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2019-01-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/35
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 128. Supplementary Provisions.[^c1606374]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 16 to this Act shall have effect with respect to the management and collection of duty.

(2)[^c1606379] In section 1(1) of the Provisional Collection of Taxes Act 1968 after the words “petroleum revenue tax” there shall be inserted the words “ supplementary petroleum duty ”.

(3) This Part of this Act shall be included in the Oil Taxation Acts for the purposes of section 108 of the Finance Act 1980 (gas banking schemes).
