# Finance Act 1981, section 107

## 107. Sale of houses at discount by local authorities etc.[^c1606309]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a conveyance or transfer to which this section applies is subject contingently to the payment of any money (whether by virtue of that conveyance or transfer or otherwise), then, notwithstanding section 57 of the [^c1606310]Stamp Act 1891, that money shall not be deemed to be part of the consideration in respect of which the conveyance or transfer is chargeable with ad valorem duty.

(2) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c1606311]

(3) This section applies to any conveyance or transfer on sale of a dwelling-house (including the grant of a lease) at a discount by—

- (a) any Minister of the Crown or Northern Ireland department;
- (b) a local housing authority within the meaning of the Housing Act 1985[^c1606312], a county council, a district council within the meaning of the [^c1606313]Local Government Act (Northern Ireland) 1972 or in Scotland a council constituted under section 2 of the Local Government[^c1606314] etc. (Scotland) Act 1994, the common good of such a council or any trust under its control;
- (c) the Housing Corporation;
- (ca)[^c1606315] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) the Scottish Homes[^c1606317];
- (e) the Northern Ireland Housing Executive;
- (ea)[^c1606318] a registered social landlord within the meaning of Part I of the Housing Act 1996;
- (f) a housing association registered—;[^c1606319]
  - (i) in Scotland, under the Housing Associations Act 1985, or
  - (ii) in Northern Ireland, under Part II of the Housing (Northern Ireland) Order 1992;
- (ff)[^c1606320] a housing action trust established under Part III of the Housing Act 1988;
- (g) a development corporation established by an order made or having effect as if made under the [^c1606321]New Towns Act 1965 or the [^c1606322]New Towns (Scotland) Act 1968 or an urban development corporation established by an order made under section 135 of the [^c1606323]Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980;
- (h) the Commission for the New Towns or a new town commission established under section 7 of the [^c1606324]New Towns Act (Northern Ireland) 1965;
- (i)[^c1606325] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (j) the Council of the Isles of Scilly;
- (k) a local policing body[^key-58f1ac0b3bcea3591b542a412dd5f72b] within the meaning of section 101(1) of the Police Act[^c1606326] 1996,[^key-aea48683740a8ac3301631fd80a8eb4d] the Scottish Police Authority[^key-27e03a9b24b1f01cb8036d3ef0450577] , or the Northern Ireland Policing Board[^c1606328];
- (ka) [^c1719954]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (l) an Education and Libraries Board established under the [^c1606331]Education and Libraries (Northern Ireland) Order 1972;
- (m) any person mentioned in paragraph (e), (i), (j) or (l) of section 1(10) of the [^c1606332]Tenants’ Rights, Etc. (Scotland) Act 1980.
- (n)[^c1606333] the United Kingdom Atomic Energy Authority
- (o)[^c1606334] such other body as the Treasury may, by order made by statutory instument, prescribe for the purposes of this section

(3A)[^c1606335] This section also applies to any conveyance or transfer on sale of a dwelling house where the conveyance or transfer is made pursuant to a sub-sale made at a discount by a body falling within subsection (3)(ea) or (f)[^c1606336]above.

(3B)[^c1606337] This section also applies to a conveyance or transfer on sale (including the grant of a lease) by a person against whom the right to buy under Part V of the Housing Act 1985 is exercisable by virtue of section 171A of that Act (preservation of right to buy on disposal to private sector landlord) to a person who is the qualifying person for the purposes of the preserved right to buy and in relation to whom that dwelling-house is the qualifying dwelling-house.

(3C)[^key-26f0ea7931e043db879d310c3d6ae957] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) This section applies to instruments executed on or after 23rd March 1981 and shall be deemed to have come into force on that date.
