## 9. Betting and gaming duties.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c1605916] In section 1(2)(*b*) of the [^c1817604]Betting and Gaming Duties Act 1972 and section 17(1)(*b*) of the [^c1605917]Miscellaneous Transferred Excise Duties Act (Northern Ireland) 1972 (general betting duty on bets other than on-course bets) for the words “7½ percent.” there shall be substituted the words “8 per cent.”.

(2)—(5)[^c1605918] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c1605919] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^c1605920] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8)[^c1605916] Subsection (1) above shall be deemed to have come into force on 12th July 1981, . . . [^c1605922] and (6) above shall come into force on 1st October 1981.
