# Finance Act 1981, section 135

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2019-01-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/35
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 135. Chevening Estate.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The enactments relating to [^c1606384]. . . capital transfer tax[^c1606385] shall not apply in respect of property held on the trusts of the trust instrument set out in the [^c1606386]Schedule to Chevening Estate Act 1959.

(2) This section shall be deemed always to have had effect.
