# Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1981, section 20

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 20. Interpretation of Part II.[^c809952]

*Extent: S*

In the foregoing provisions of this part of this Act “year”, and “year” followed by a reference to two calender years, have the same meanings as in the [^c809953] Local Government (Financial Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1963.
