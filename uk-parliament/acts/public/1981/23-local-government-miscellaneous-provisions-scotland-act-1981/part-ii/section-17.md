# Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1981, section 17

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 17. Effect of sections 14 to 16.[^c809950]

*Extent: S*

The amendments made by sections 14 to 16 of this Act have effect as regards rate support grants payable for the year 1981—82 or for any year thereafter.
