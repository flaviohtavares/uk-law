## 24. Powers of Commissioner for Local Administration in Scotland.[^c809955]

*Extent: S*

The [^c809956]1975 Act shall, as regards the powers of the Commissioner for Local Administration in Scotland, have effect subject to the amendments specified in Schedule 1 to this Act.
