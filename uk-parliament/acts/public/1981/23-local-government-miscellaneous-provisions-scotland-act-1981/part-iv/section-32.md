# Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1981, section 32

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 32. Abolition of advisory committee on conservation and provision of water supplies etc.[^c809973]

*Extent: S*

The advisory committee appointed under section 1 of the [^c809974]Water (Scotland) Act 1946 (a section which among other things provided that the Secretary of State was to appoint such a committee to advise him as regards matters concerning the conservation and provision of water supplies etc. and which is re-enacted as section 1 of the [^c809975]Water (Scotland) Act 1980) is hereby abolished; and accordingly paragraph (c) of section 1 of the said Act of 1980 shall cease to have effect.
