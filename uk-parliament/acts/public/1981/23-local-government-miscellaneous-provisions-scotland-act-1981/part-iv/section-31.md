# Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1981, section 31

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 31. Abolition of Scottish River Purification Advisory Committee.[^c809971]

*Extent: S*

The Scottish River Purification Advisory Committee is hereby abolished; and accordingly, in the [^c809972]Rivers (Prevention of Pollution) (Scotland) Act 1951, subsections (2), (3) and (4) of section 1 (which relate to the appointment, constitution and functions of that committee) shall cease to have effect.
