# Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1981, section 28

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 28. Repeal of special licensing provisions in respect of new towns.[^c809965]

*Extent: S*

Part IV of, and Schedule 3 to, the [^c809966]Licensing (Scotland) Act 1976 (which make special provision for new towns as regards licensing with respect to alcoholic liquor) shall cease to have effect.
