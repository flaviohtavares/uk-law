# Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1981, section 30

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 30. Abolition of Scottish Food Hygiene Council.[^c809969]

*Extent: S*

The Scottish Food Hygiene Council is hereby abolished; and accordingly, in the [^c809970]Food and Drugs (Scotland) Act 1956, section 25 (which relates to the appointment, constitution and functions of the Council) shall cease to have effect.
