# Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1981, section 29

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 29. Abolition of Advisory Council on Social Work.[^c809967]

*Extent: S*

The Advisory Council on Social Work is hereby abolished; and accordingly, in the [^c809968]Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968, section 7 (which relates to the appointment, constitution and functions etc. of the Council) shall cease to have effect.
