# Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1981, section 42

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 42. Interpretation.

*Extent: S*

In this Act—

- “**the 1956 Act**” means the [^c809986]Valuation and Rating (Scotland) Act 1956;
- “**the 1966 Act**” means the [^c809987]Local Government (Scotland) Act 1966;
- “**the 1973 Act**” means the [^c809988]Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973;
- “**the 1975 Act**” means the [^c809989]Local Government (Scotland) Act 1975; and
- “**the 1978 Act**” means the [^c809990]Housing (Financial Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1978.
