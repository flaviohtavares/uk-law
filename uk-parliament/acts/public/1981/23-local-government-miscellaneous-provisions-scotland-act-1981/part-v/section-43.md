# Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1981, section 43

> Text as at 2016-04-01.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 43. Short title, commencement and extent.

*Extent: S*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Scotland) Act 1981.

(2) Section 11 of this Act shall come into force on 1st January 1982.

(3) Sections 4 to 10 of this Act and, in so far as relating to section 4(2) of the [^c809991]Local Government (Financial Provisions etc.) (Scotland) Act 1962, Schedule 4 to this Act shall come into force on 1st April 1982.

(4)[^c809992] Sections 29 and 37 of this Act and, in so far as relating to section 7 of the [^c809993]Social Work (Scotland) Act 1968, Schedule 4 to this Act shall come into force on such date as the Secretary of State may appoint by order made by statutory instrument.

(5) This Act extends to Scotland only.
