# Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981, section 19A

## 19A. Evidence in Scotland in certain proceedings.[^c14000911][^c-3ad924d3026ff10595f70b150e4007ef]

*Extent: S*

In any proceedings in Scotland for any of the following offences[^c-0b39b212fddb7acee0c5905765c9b19e], the accused may be convicted on the evidence of one witness —[^c-efb68863e9de166ad2a580fdd537e68b]

- (a) an offence under section 1(1)(a) in relation to a grouse, partridge, pheasant or ptarmigan included in Part I of Schedule 2;
- (b) an offence under section 1(1)(c);
- (c) an offence under section 6(1) in relation to a grouse, partridge or pheasant included in Part IA of Schedule 3;
- (d) an offence under section 6(2) in relation to a grouse, partridge, pheasant or ptarmigan included in Part IIA of that Schedule;
- (e) an offence under section 10A(1), 11G(1) or 11I(1).
