# Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (48).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/69
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Power to vary Schedules.[^c19461461]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may by order, either generally or with respect to particular provisions of this Part, particular areas of Great Britain or particular times of the year, add any bird to, or remove any bird from, any of or any Part of Schedules ZA1 to 4[^c19482491].

(2) An order under subsection (1) adding any bird to Part II of Schedule 1 or Part I of Schedule 2 may prescribe a close season in the case of that bird for the purposes of sections 1 and 2; and any close season so prescribed shall commence on a date not later than 21st February and end on a date not earlier than 31st August.

(3) The Secretary of State may, on a representation made to him by the GB conservation bodies acting through the Joint Nature Conservation Committee in accordance with Part 2 of the 2006 Act[^c19482561], by order, either generally or with respect to particular provisions of this Part, particular areas of Great Britain or particular times of the year—

- (a) add to Schedule 5 or Schedule 8 any animal or plant which, in his opinion, is in danger of extinction in Great Britain or is likely to become so endangered unless conservation measures are taken; and
- (b) remove from Schedule 5 or Schedule 8 any animal or plant which, in his opinion, is no longer so endangered or likely to become so endangered

[^c19482611]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3A)[^c19482581] The 2006 Act means the Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006.

(4) The Secretary of State may, for the purpose of complying with an international obligation, by order, either generally or with respect to particular provisions of this Part or particular times of the year—

- (a) add any animals to, or remove any animals from, Schedule 5 or Schedule 6; and
- (b) add any plants to, or remove any plants from, Schedule 8.

(5) The Secretary of State may by order, either generally or with respect to particular areas of Great Britain—

- (a) add any animals to, or remove any animals from, Part I , IA or IB[^c-145a7fff445eedcfa8148aaa797dceb0] of Schedule 9; and
- (b) add any plants to, or remove any plants from, Part II of that Schedule.

(6)[^c-067a755aa6923731db09d59ed16f7dad] The Secretary of State may, for the purpose of complying with the international trapping standards agreement, by order add any animal to, or remove any animal from, Schedule 6ZA.

(7) In subsection (6), “the international trapping standards agreement” has the meaning given by section 16(3ZK).

## 22. Power to vary Schedules.[^c19461471]

*Extent: S*

(1) The Secretary of State may by order, either generally or with respect to particular provisions of this Part, particular areas of Great Britain or particular times of the year

- (a)[^c19460551] add any bird to, or remove any bird from, any of or any Part of Schedules A1[^c19460601] to 4.
- (b)[^c19460621] add any animal to, or remove any animal from, Schedule 5 , 5A, 6 or 6A[^c-8775ae53bd5fc41f0ab63a752b750838] [^c-2a0aa0543b0e25205cc58c50c6216678]...;
- (c) add any plant to, or remove any plant from, Schedule 8 [^c-cc392cc9f3d5eb88f6c2563e0dded599]....

(2) An order under subsection (1) adding any bird to Part II of Schedule 1 or Part I of Schedule 2 may prescribe a close season in the case of that bird for the purposes of sections 1 and 2; and any close season so prescribed shall commence on a date not later than 21st February and end on a date not earlier than 31st August.

(2ZA)[^c-603d41a3807305c4fd90457b868b0eed] An order under subsection (1) adding any animal to Schedule 5A may prescribe a close season in the case of that animal for the purposes of section 10A.

(2A)[^c19460671] Before making an order under subsection (1) the Scottish Ministers shall consult Scottish Natural Heritage.

(3) The Secretary of State may, on a representation made to him by the GB conservation bodies acting through the Joint Nature Conservation Committee in accordance with Part 2 of the 2006 Act[^c19482621], by order, either generally or with respect to particular provisions of this Part, particular areas of Great Britain or particular times of the year—

- (a) add to Schedule 5 or Schedule 8 any animal or plant which, in his opinion, is in danger of extinction in Great Britain or is likely to become so endangered unless conservation measures are taken; and
- (b) remove from Schedule 5 or Schedule 8 any animal or plant which, in his opinion, is no longer so endangered or likely to become so endangered
