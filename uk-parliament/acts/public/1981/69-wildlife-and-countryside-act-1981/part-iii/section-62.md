## 62. Appointment of wardens for public rights of way.[^c19462631][^c19497071]

*Extent: E+W*

A local authority may appoint such number of persons as appears to the authority to be necessary or expedient to act as wardens as respects a footpath, bridleway , restricted byway[^c22562311] or byway open to all traffic which is both in the countryside and in the area of the authority, and the purpose for which the wardens may be so appointed is to advise and assist the public in connection with the use of the path or way.
