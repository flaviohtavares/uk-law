# Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (48).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/69
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 70. Financial provisions.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) There shall be defrayed out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any administrative expenses incurred by any Minister of the Crown under this Act; and
- (b) any increase attributable to the provisions of this Act in the sums payable out of money so provided under any other enactment.

(2) Any sums received by a Minister of the Crown under this Act shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund.
