## 45. Power to vary order designating National Park.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c4947021] Natural England[^c19488311](as well as the Secretary of State) shall have power to make an order amending an order made under section 5 of the 1949 Act designating a National Park [^c19488291]. . . , and—

- (a) section 7(5) and (6) of that Act (consultation and publicity in connection with orders under section 5 or 7) shall apply to an order under this section as they apply to an order under section 7(4) of that Act with the substitution for the reference in section 7(5) to the Secretary of State of a reference to Natural England ; and
- (b) Schedule 1 to that Act (procedure in connection with the making and confirmation of orders under section 5 or 7) shall apply to an order under this section as it applies to an order designating a National Park.
