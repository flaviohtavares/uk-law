# Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (48).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/69

## 33. Ministerial guidance as respects areas of special scientific interest.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c19462491] The Ministers shall from time to time, after consultation with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462491] Natural England[^c19495981] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462491] and such persons appearing to them to represent other interests concerned as they consider appropriate— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462491]

- (a) prepare codes containing such recommendations, advice and information as they consider proper for the guidance of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462491]
  - (i) persons exercising functions under sections 28 to 32; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462491]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19462491] persons affected or likely to be affected by the exercise of any of those functions; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462491]
- (b) revise any such code by revoking, varying, amending or adding to the provisions of the code in such manner as the Ministers think fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462491]

(2) A code prepared in pursuance of subsection (1) and any alterations proposed to be made on a revision of such a code shall be laid before both Houses of Parliament forthwith after being prepared; and the code or revised code, as the case may be, shall not be issued until the code or the proposed alterations have been approved by both Houses. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462491]

(3) Subject to subsection (2), the Ministers shall cause every code prepared or revised in pursuance of subsection (1) to be printed, and may cause copies of it to be put on sale to the public at such price as the Ministers may determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462491]
