## 41. Duties of agriculture Ministers with respect to the countryside.[^c4946061]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c4946071][^c19462571] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) In the exercise of his general duty under section 4(2) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571] [^c4946081]Small Landholders (Scotland) Act 1911 of promoting the interests of agriculture and other rural industries, and without prejudice to the generality of that duty, the Secretary of State shall make provision, through such organisation as he considers appropriate, for the giving of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]

- (a)[^c4946091] advice to persons carrying on agricultural businesses on the conservation and enhancement of the natural beauty and amenity of the countryside; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]
- (b) advice to such persons on diversification into other enterprises of benefit to the rural economy; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]
- (c) advice to government departments and other bodies exercising statutory functions on the promotion and furtherance of such diversification as is mentioned in paragraph ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]*b*).

(3) Where an application for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571] a farm capital grant[^c4946101] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571] is made as respects expenditure incurred or to be incurred for the purposes of activities on land which is in a National Park *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571] (including a National Park in Scotland)[^c4946111] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]or an area specified for the purposes of this subsection by the Ministers, the appropriate Minister— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]

- (a) shall, so far as may be consistent with the purposes of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571] the grant provisions[^c4946121] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571], so exercise his functions thereunder as to further the conservation and enhancement of the natural beauty and amenity of the countryside and to promote its enjoyment by the public; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]
- (b) where the relevant authority have objected to the making of the grant on the ground that the activities in question have had or will have an adverse effect on the natural beauty or amenity of the countryside or its enjoyment by the public, shall not make the grant except after considering the objection and, in the case of land in England, after consulting with the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]

[^c19487961]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Where, in consequence of an objection by the relevant authority, an application for a grant as respects expenditure to be incurred is refused on the ground that the activities in question will have such an effect as is mentioned in subsection (3)(b), the relevant authority shall, within three months of their receiving notice of the appropriate Minister’s decision, offer to enter into, in the terms of a draft submitted to the applicant, a management agreement— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]

- (a) imposing restrictions as respects those activities; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]
- (b) providing for the making by them of payments to the applicant. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]

(5) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]

- “[^c4946131]**agricultural business *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]**” has the same meaning as in section 29 of the Agriculture Act 1970; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]
- “**the appropriate Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]**”, “**farm capital grant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]**” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]**grant provisions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]**” have the same meanings as in section 32; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]
- “[^c19487981]**management agreement *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]
  - (a) in relation to England, an agreement under section 39 or under section 7 of the Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]
  - (b) in relation to Wales, an agreement under section 39 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571] or under section 16 of the Environment (Wales) Act 2016 *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-f0eca5684581bc436d96d249a1c98123] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571];
- “**the relevant authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]**” has the same meaning as in section 39 except that in relation to England it also includes Natural England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571] and in relation to Wales it also includes the Natural Resources Body for Wales *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-a760146dd7bb98ce1afcae9763d16c1e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571]

(5A)[^c4946161] For the purposes of this section the Broads shall be treated as a National Park *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571] (and, as respects land within the Broads, any reference in this section to the relevant authority is accordingly a reference to the Broads Authority).[^c4946171] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19462571].
