> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (48).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/69

## 41A. Application of sections 42 to 45 in Wales[^c19896771]

*Extent: E+W*

In relation to land in Wales, sections 42 to 45 (which relate to National Parks) have effect as if references to Natural England were references to the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^key-00616b1c5d97a4c5b2d93540c3f46311].
