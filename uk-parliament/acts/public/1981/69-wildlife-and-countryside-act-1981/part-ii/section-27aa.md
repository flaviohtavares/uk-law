## 27AA. Application of sections 28 to 34 in Wales[^c19493441]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-8eecb480341afb8a9eb28452dac108b0] In relation to land in Wales, sections 28 to 34 (which relate to sites of special scientific interest and limestone pavements) have effect as if references to Natural England were references to the Natural Resources Body for Wales and as if section 28D(2)(d) were omitted[^c-39754871a5e90105ef120170e0be43d1].

(2)[^c-b1a3f81bd7cc9389767c2b69aa1851a9] Subsection (3) applies where—

- (a) any provision of sections 28 to 34 requires the Natural Resources Body for Wales to give a notification or notice to the local planning authority in whose area land is situated, and
- (b) all or part of the land is included in a strategic planning area designated under section 60D of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eeb2e35104341516cf6c62e4c0a76032] the area of a corporate joint committee to which Part 6 of the Planning and Compulsory Purchase Act 2004 applies by virtue of regulations under Part 5 of the Local Government and Elections (Wales) Act 2021 .

(3) The Natural Resources Body for Wales must also give the notification or notice to the strategic planning panel for the strategic planning area *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3d0986bdd9ae0ead4071b27a9b2a9140] that corporate joint committee .
