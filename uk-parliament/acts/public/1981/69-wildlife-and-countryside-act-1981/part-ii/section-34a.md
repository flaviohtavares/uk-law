# Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981, section 34A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (48).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/69
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34A. Meaning of “appropriate conservation body”[^c19762741]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In the following provisions of this Part “**the appropriate conservation body**” means—

- (a) in relation to England, Natural England;
- (b) in relation to Wales, the Natural Resources Body for Wales[^c-45451707129c6368c94b1b6aec104fd6];
- (c) in relation to Scotland, Scottish Natural Heritage.
