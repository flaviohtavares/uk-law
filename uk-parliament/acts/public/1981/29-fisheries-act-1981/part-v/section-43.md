# Fisheries Act 1981, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Financial provisions.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There shall be paid out of moneys provided by Parliament any sums required by any Minister or Ministers—

- (a) for making payments to or in respect of members of the Sea Fish Industry Authority and former members of the bodies replaced by that Authority;
- (b) for providing financial assistance to the Authority (by way of loan, grant or guarantee) under Part I of this Act;
- (c)[^c-792772b6cda6d9741dda9c6df949e775] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) for exercising the powers conferred by section 32 above; or
- (e) for administrative expenses under this Act.

(2) Any sums received by any Minister or Ministers under this Act shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund.

(3)[^c8102911] Any sums received by the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development under this Act shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund of Northern Ireland.
