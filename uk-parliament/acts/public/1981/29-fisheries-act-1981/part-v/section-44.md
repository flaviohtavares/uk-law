# Fisheries Act 1981, section 44

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. General interpretation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act—

- “**salmon**” includes any fish of the salmon species;
- “**shellfish**” includes crustaceans and molluscs of any kind;
- “**migratory trout**” means any species of trout which migrates to and from the sea.
