# Fisheries Act 1981, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Offences by bodies corporate.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where an offence under Part I[^c-30c2c2db71c5e06c6ef41ca1fb46665e]... of this Act which has been committed by a body corporate is proved to have been committed with the consent or connivance of or to be attributable to any neglect on the part of any director, manager, secretary or other similar officer of the body corporate, or any person who was purporting to act in any such capacity, he as well as the body corporate shall be deemed to be guilty of that offence and shall be liable to be proceeded against and punished accordingly.
