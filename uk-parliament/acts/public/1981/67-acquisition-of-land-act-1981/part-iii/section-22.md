# Acquisition of Land Act 1981, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-09-14. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/67
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Notice of giving of certificate.

*Extent: E+W*

As soon as may be after the giving of a certificate under this Part of this Act, the acquiring authority shall publish—

- (a)[^c-17ab3a823231977c9898f1c7c1d5fd61] in one or more local newspapers circulating in the locality in which the land comprised in the order is situated , and[^c-0a0306f274ac4ad085b309c5358fbb00]
- (b) on an appropriate website, until the end of the period of 6 weeks beginning with the day on which the certificate is given,

a notice in the prescribed form stating that the certificate has been given.
