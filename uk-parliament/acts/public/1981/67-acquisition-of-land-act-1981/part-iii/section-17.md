## 17. Local authority and statutory undertakers’ land.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies to land which—

- (a) is the property of a local authority, or
- (b) has been acquired by statutory undertakers, who are not a local authority, for the purposes of their undertaking.

(2) Subject to subsection (3) below, a compulsory purchase order shall, in so far as it authorises the compulsory purchase of land to which this section applies, be subject to special parliamentary procedure in any case where an objection to the compulsory purchase of the land[^c-4eacf233f8329d24b217adf2b36e7fce] has been made by the local authority, or as the case may be the statutory undertakers, and has not been withdrawn.

(2A)[^c3313761] Subsection (3) of section 16 above applies in relation to subsections (1) and (2) above as it applies in relation to the preceding provisions of that section.

(3)[^c3313771] Subsection (2) above shall not apply to the compulsory acquisition of an interest in land where the person acquiring the interest is a local authority (as defined in subsection (4) below) a National Park authority[^c3313781], an urban development corporation[^c3313791] , a Mayoral development corporation[^c-814764d40afc7d6118f1033efeade8ce] ,[^c3313801] [^c-81ba385aa72b94bdf8cf0c8f1e9e2e86]...,. . . [^c3313821]. . ., a Welsh planning board,[^c3313831] any statutory undertakers or a Minister.

(4)[^c3313841] In subsection (3) above—

- “**local authority**” means—
  - (a) in relation to England, the council of a county or district the Broads Authority[^c3313851], the council of a London borough, the Common Council of the City of London, a fire and rescue authority created by an order under section 4A of the Fire and Rescue Services Act 2004,[^c-12f4a0fb230cbaa93ab8436db0165e7c] the London Fire Commissioner,[^c-ca91dc17a80db9833d39e3d116f2e56a] [^c-132cefbd60707436f8b4dcc826240f70].... . . . . .[^c3313911] [^c3313921], a joint authority established by Part 4 of the Local Government Act 1985 , a combined authority established under section 103 of that Act or a combined county authority established under section 9(1) of the Levelling-up and Regeneration Act 2023[^c-e13a74825a0a0ed8ad77950accafca73],
  - (b) in relation to Wales, the council of a county or county borough[^c3313941], or a police authority established under[^c3313951] section 3 of the Police Act 1996[^c3313881]. . .

  and this definition applies to the Isles of Scilly as if the Council of those Isles were the council of a county;
- “**statutory undertakers**” includes—
  - (a) [^c3313991]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (aa) a National Health Service trust established under[^c3314001] section 25 of the National Health Service Act 2006 or section 18 of the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^c-40d32e79da50be1f57d2b739393d5fe3], and
  - (aab) an[^c-d6ca877b6b421ba582ea9a7351bb9cf0] NHS foundation trust,
  - (ab) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c3314011]
  - (ac) the Schools Funding Council for Wales,
  - (ad) [^c-811c31306b96c163e4942713b2096bca]...
  - (ae) NHS England[^c-0b560ede7290af46ec02d47014d8d7c2],
  - (af) an integrated care board established under section[^c-6470a43d6a513aa082bc6683891726ca] 14Z25 of the National Health Service Act 2006,
  - (b) any other authority, body or undertakers specified in an order made by the Secretary of State under this paragraph.
- “[^c3314031]**a Welsh planning board**” means a board constituted under—
  - (a) section 2(1B) of the [^c3314041]Town and Country Planning Act 1990; [^c3314051]
  - (b) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) An order under paragraph (b) of the definition of “**statutory undertakers**” in subsection (4) above shall be made by statutory instrument and shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
