## 12. Notices to owners, lessees , occupiers and others.[^c3310581][^c-a94ecca293e6571eb58af60540455b96]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The acquiring authority shall serve on every qualifying person[^c-15aaf51612ec64337fccc962450d5af0] a notice in the prescribed form—

- (a) stating the effect of the order,
- (b) stating that it is about to be submitted for confirmation, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e80f0d56893217f973ede8eba06dc3fc]
- (ba)[^c-d8b872a9f3ad815d1787efeadaa3b3cc] (subject to section 11(2A)) naming a place within the locality where a copy of the order and of the map referred to in it may be inspected,
- (bb) specifying a website on which those copies may be viewed, and
- (c)[^c-062bd8788ad8166858176c4ce6cd121d] specifying the time (not being less than twenty-one days from service of the notice) within which, and the manner in which, objections to the order can be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-062bd8788ad8166858176c4ce6cd121d]
- (c) specifying the final day for making objections to the order, and the manner in which objections can be made.

(2) A person is a qualifying person, in relation to land comprised in an order, if—[^c-0af781816bdef318b75a0e94763be7b8]

- (a) he is an owner, lessee, tenant (whatever the tenancy period) or occupier of the land, [^c-4614c134d9264ff58eb4c5f916f165f1]...
- (b) he falls within subsection (2A) , or[^c-4c1b29332d90e4a702976f5680796100]
- (c) the person is entitled to the benefit of an obligation under a conservation covenant (within the meaning of Part 7 of the Environment Act 2021) relating to the land.

(2A) A person falls within this subsection if he is—

- (a) a person to whom the acquiring authority would, if proceeding under section 5(1) of the Compulsory Purchase Act 1965, be required to give a notice to treat, or
- (b) a person the acquiring authority thinks is likely to be entitled to make a relevant claim if the order is confirmed and the compulsory purchase takes place, so far as he is known to the acquiring authority after making diligent inquiry.

(2B) A relevant claim is a claim for compensation under section 10 of the Compulsory Purchase Act 1965 (compensation for injurious affection).

(3)[^c3310591] Where under this section any notice is required to be served on an owner of land, and the land is ecclesiastical property, a like notice shall be served on the Church Commissioners *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-747c835bb20ac8162f28042fb7a3f23f] Diocesan Board of Finance for the diocese in which the land is situated *(Extent: E)* .

In this subsection “**ecclesiastical property**” means land belonging to any ecclesiastical benefice of the Church of England *(Extent: E)*[^c-a52aa73b70f0b112ae1e18e83450f713] , or being or forming part of a church subject to the jurisdiction of the bishop of any diocese of the Church of England *(Extent: E)* or the site of such a church, or being or forming part of a burial ground subject to such jurisdiction or being diocesan glebe land within the meaning of the Endowments and Glebe Measure 1976 *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-cc28dfc7ef3c6f68e731ae913ec391e6][^c3310641].
