## 28. Acquisition of rights over land by the creation of new rights.

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 3 to this Act applies to the compulsory acquisition under this Act of rights over land by the creation of new rights by virtue of—

- (a) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c3314501]
- (aa)[^c3314511] section 21A of the [^c3314521]Welsh Development Agency Act 1975,
- (b) section 13(1) of the [^c3314531]Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1976,
- (c)[^c3314541] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) section 142(4) of the [^c3314551]Local Government, Planning and Land Act 1980,
- (e) section 250 of the [^c3314561]Highways Act 1980.
- (f)[^c-f6e0ba6b0cd572bcd2f349eb0f6a787e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g)[^c3314581] paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to the Gas Act 1986.
- (h)[^c3314591] paragraph 1 of Schedule 3 to the Electricity Act 1989.
- (i)[^c-c1e21c8566212de381d9c98a3945bc08] paragraph 3(3) of Schedule 4 to the Communications Act 2003.
