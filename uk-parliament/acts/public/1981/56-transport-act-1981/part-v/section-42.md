# Transport Act 1981, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2017-09-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Expenses.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Expenses incurred by any government department in consequence of the provisions of this Act shall be defrayed out of money provided by Parliament.
