# Transport Act 1981, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2017-09-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/56
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Road humps.[^c858829]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The provisions of Schedule 10 have effect with respect to road humps.

(2)[^c858830] This section and Schedule 10 come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may appoint by order made by statutory instrument, and different days may be so appointed for different purposes.
