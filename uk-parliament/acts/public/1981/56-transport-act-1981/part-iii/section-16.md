## 16. Levy of contributions from harbour authorities.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State shall levy from harbour authorities such contributions as appear to him necessary to meet the expenses specified in subsection (2) [^key-88ebd0523559861ecf8d8795fa363fa4]....

(2) The expenses referred to in subsection (1) are—

- (a)[^key-9d47fcd1360c67221e0d5e4fab3c6c52] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^key-9d47fcd1360c67221e0d5e4fab3c6c52] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) expenses incurred by him under paragraph [^key-50d8959ef9eeb816885739f51a56ca7a]... 8(4)(b) or 9(5) of Schedule 5.

(3)[^key-1a81f17651b6f74488c2ca6c4337a5ba] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Sums received by the Secretary of State by virtue of this section shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund.

(5) In this section and section 17 “**harbour authority**” means a person engaged (whether or not in the exercise of statutory functions) in improving, maintaining or managing a harbour within the meaning of the [^c858825]Harbours Act 1964.
