## 67. Accounts of ... development corporations.[^key-d5dabfc3ae9dc0548f6be2d4896b43d7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) [^key-d2d53f5a52713e1708cb3219cd565c47]... every development corporation—

- (a) shall keep proper accounts and other records in relation to those accounts, and
- (b)[^c253262] shall [^key-1775f33c309efb9b1dc90ae435a5f6bd]... prepare in respect of each financial year annual accounts in such form as the Secretary of State may with the Treasury’s approval direct, [^key-d330dc023d6cadbb1563fde5b657a801]...

[^key-d330dc023d6cadbb1563fde5b657a801]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1A)[^key-116b3b62a23cded41917243222f29d98] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The financial year [^key-6ce0ae178a5b49af87449321c13efd86]... of every development corporation shall begin with 1st April but, if the Secretary of State, with the approval of the Treasury, so directs in a case where a development corporation[^c253263] [^key-e0d008899a3bf9046fbdcda20a8b6988]... is to be dissolved, the final financial year of the corporation [^key-e0d008899a3bf9046fbdcda20a8b6988]... shall be such period as is specified in the direction, and references in this Act to a financial year in relation to a development corporation [^key-e0d008899a3bf9046fbdcda20a8b6988]... shall be construed accordingly.

(3) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1) above, the Secretary of State may, with the Treasury’s approval, give directions to [^key-1b508a9f6678a0fccf00ec8673669bb1]... a development corporation as to—

- (a) the kind or number of accounts which it is[^key-11f00b78e59409af9e89286df2a8d00e] to keep.
- (b) the amounts which are or are not to be credited or debited to any account,
- (c) the manner of rectifying any account,
- (d) provision for working balances,

and any such direction may be a general direction or a direction for a particular case.
