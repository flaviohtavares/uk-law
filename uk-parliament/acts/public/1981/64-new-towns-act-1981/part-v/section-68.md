# New Towns Act 1981, section 68

## 68. Audit.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The accounts [^c-404882018041bb1091d07e989658159e]... of every development corporation shall be audited by an auditor to be appointed annually by the Secretary of State in relation to the [^c-a111c1fa572593d5e86c90df6ee34d54]... corporation.

(2)[^c253265] A person shall not be so appointed auditor unless he is eligible for appointment as a statutory auditor under Part 42 of the Companies Act 2006[^c-d7beec710acc44af9b63dc4fb841fbae].

(2A)[^c-dee62d157e2ce21fccbfbc4f5d35d996] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) As soon as the annual accounts of[^c-b9ad35a293f21e238d8195d3d5d34fdd] a development corporation for any financial year have been audited, the corporation shall send to the Secretary of State a copy of the accounts prepared by it[^c-89c173c57a82637cb3b58745c2807d9f] for that year in accordance with this section, together with a copy of any report made by the auditor on those accounts.
