# New Towns Act 1981, section 71

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (37).** Text as at 2026-06-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1981/64
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Information.[^key-1bcae416254c3b9f24873df5ab6ccb2d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Without prejudice to the requirements imposed by section 70 above, every development corporation shall[^key-71ca7dc76ff744283509703dc6ee60a7] provide the Secretary of State with such information relating to its[^key-9475db26b70d9ddbf873c8359ac07fab] undertaking as the Secretary of State may from time to time require.

(2) For that purpose [^key-47e70dd8b5bbada15c6fad74186b6224]... every development corporation—

- (a) shall permit any person authorised by the Secretary of State in that behalf to inspect and copy the accounts, books, documents or papers of the corporation[^key-4c92b2b1dbbe744f2abdcc6e0d74e966], and
- (b) shall afford such explanation of those accounts, books, documents or papers as that person or the Secretary of State may reasonably require.
