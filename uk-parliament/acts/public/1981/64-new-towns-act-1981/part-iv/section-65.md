# New Towns Act 1981, section 65

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 65. Disposal of surplus funds.[^c-2de029b5362e28f0fab509c03c4b1f4e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where it appears to the Secretary of State, after consultation with the Treasury and any development corporation, that the corporation has a surplus whether on capital or on revenue account after making allowance by way of transfer to reserve or otherwise for its future requirements[^c-75e3eb7845912717d5f1c809ef0e2860].

(2) That corporation[^c-ba46f8fe816e4385042b33bdaa995ead] shall, if the Secretary of State after such consultation so directs, pay to the Secretary of State such sum not exceeding the amount of that surplus as may be specified in the direction, and any sum so received by him shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund, subject to section 66(2) below.
