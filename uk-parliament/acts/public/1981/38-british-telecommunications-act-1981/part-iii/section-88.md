# British Telecommunications Act 1981, section 88

> Text as at 2009-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Transitional provisions.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The general transitional provisions contained in Schedule 4[^M_F_a41991bd-056c-4d61-dee6-ebd03ff463f1]... shall have effect.

(2) This section and Schedules 4 [^M_F_41da3b37-4c3e-4bde-cb0f-edfac84df50e]... shall extend to the Isle of Man, and this section and Schedule 4 shall extend to the Channel Islands.
