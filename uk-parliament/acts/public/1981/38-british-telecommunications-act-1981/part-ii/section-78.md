# British Telecommunications Act 1981, section 78

> Text as at 2009-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Interpretation and extent of Part II.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part shall be construed as one with Part III of the 1969 Act.

(2) This Part, except sections 59 and 63 to 70, shall extend to the Isle of Man and the Channel Islands.
