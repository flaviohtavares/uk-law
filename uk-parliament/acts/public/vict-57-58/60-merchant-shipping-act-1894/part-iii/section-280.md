# Merchant Shipping Act 1894, section 280

> Text as at 2006-12-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 280. Delivery up of certificate.[^c16229421]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Board of Trade may require a passenger steamer’s certificate, which has expired or been cancelled, to be delivered up as they direct.

(2) If any owner or master fails without reasonable cause to comply with such requirement, he shall for each offence be liable to a fine not exceeding £50[^c16229441][^c16229431]level 2 on the standard scale.
