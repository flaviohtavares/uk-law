# Merchant Shipping Act 1894, section 540

> Text as at 2006-12-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 540. Marine store dealer not to purchase from person under sixteen.[^c16233381][^c16233391]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A marine store dealer shall not by himself or his agents purchase marine stores of any description from any person apparently under the age of sixteen years.

(2) If a marine store dealer so purchases any marine store, he shall be liable to a fine for the first offence not exceeding five pounds[^c16233401]£25 and for every subsequent offence not exceeding twenty pounds[^c16233421]£50.
