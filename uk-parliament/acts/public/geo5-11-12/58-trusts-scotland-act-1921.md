## 3. What trusts shall be held to include. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]

All trusts shall be held to include the following powers and provisions unless the contrary be expressed (that is to say):— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]

- (a) Power to any trustee to resign the office of trustee; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]
- (b) Power to the trustee, if there be only one, or to the trustees, if there be more than one, or to a quorum of the trustees, if there be more than two, to assume new trustees; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]
- (c) A provision that a majority of the trustees accepting and surviving shall be a quorum; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]
- (d) A provision that each trustee shall be liable only for his own acts and intromissions and shall not be liable for the acts and intromissions of co-trustees and shall not be liable for omissions: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]

Provided that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]

- (1) A sole trustee shall not be entitled to resign his office by virtue of this Act unless either (1) he has assumed new trustees and they have declared their acceptance of office, or (2) the court shall have appointed new trustees or a judicial factor as hereinafter in this Act provided; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]
- (2) A trustee who has accepted any legacy or bequest or annuity expressly given on condition of the recipient thereof accepting the office of trustee under the trust shall not be entitled to resign the office of trustee by virtue of this Act, unless otherwise expressly declared in the trust deed, nor shall any trustee appointed to the office of trustee on the footing of receiving remuneration for his services be entitled so to resign that office in the absence of an express power to resign; but it shall be competent to the court, on the petition of any trustee to whom the foregoing provisions of this proviso apply, to grant authority to such trustee to resign the office of trustee on such conditions (if any) with respect to repayment or otherwise of his legacy as the court may think just; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]
- (3) A judicial factor shall not, by virtue of this Act, have the power of assumption, nor shall he have the power by virtue of this Act to resign his office without judicial authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]

Nothing in this section shall affect any liability incurred by any trustee prior to the date of any resignation or assumption under the provisions of this Act or of any Act repealed by this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]

## 30. Trustee not to be chargeable with breach of trust for lending money on security of any property on certain conditions. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6][^c5825741]

(1) Any trustee lending money on the security of any property shall not be chargeable with breach of trust by reason only of the proportion borne by the amount of the loan to the value of such property at the time when the loan was made, provided that it shall appear to the court that in making such loan the trustee was acting upon a report as to the value of the property made by a person whom the trustee reasonably believed to be an able practical valuator instructed and employed independently of any owner of the property, whether such valuator carried on business in the locality where the property is situated or elsewhere, and that the amount of the loan by itself or in combination with any other loan or loans upon the property ranking prior to or pari passu with the loan in question does not exceed two equal third parts of the value of the property as stated in such report, and this section shall apply to a loan upon any property on which the trustees can lawfully lend. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]

(2) This section shall apply to transfers of existing securities as well as to new securities, and in its application to a partial transfer of an existing security the expression “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]**the amount of the loan *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]**” shall include the amount of any other loan or loans upon the property ranking prior to or pari passu with the loan in question. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]

## 31. Power of court to make orders in case of breach of trust. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]

Where a trustee shall have committed a breach of trust at the instigation or request or with the consent in writing of a beneficiary, the court may, if it shall think fit, make such order as to the court shall seem just for applying all or any part of the interest of the beneficiary in the trust estate by way of indemnity to the trustee or person claiming through him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]

## 32. Court may relieve trustee from personal liability. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6]

## 33. Investment ceasing to be an authorised investment. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d808d934a112a18bc367e27142280cf6][^c5825751]
