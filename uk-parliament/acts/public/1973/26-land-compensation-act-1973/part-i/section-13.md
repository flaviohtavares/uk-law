## 13. Ecclesiastical property.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any compensation payable under this Part of this Act in respect of land which is ecclesiastical property shall be paid

- (a (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_5373c228-272d-40e5-b518-2a5452bb70ad][^key-24092998fbb6c9b28c6db8fafdb27b8f] in the case of land which is not diocesan glebe land, to the Church Commissioners; and *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24092998fbb6c9b28c6db8fafdb27b8f]
- (b (Extent: E) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-24092998fbb6c9b28c6db8fafdb27b8f] in the case of diocesan glebe land, to the Diocesan Board of Finance in which the land is vested, *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-24092998fbb6c9b28c6db8fafdb27b8f]

to the Diocesan Board of Finance for the diocese in which the land is situated *(Extent: E)* and (in either case) *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-53229fe2c3aa2d70df8ef23a07b9eb87] shall be applied for the purposes for which the proceeds of a sale by agreement of the land would be applicable under any enactment or Measure authorising, or disposing of the proceeds of, such a sale.

(2) In this section “**ecclesiastical property**” means land belonging to an ecclesiastical benefice of the Church of England, or being or forming part of a church subject to the jurisdiction of a bishop of any diocese of the Church of England or the site of such a church, or being or forming part of a burial ground subject to such jurisdiction or being diocesan glebe land; and “[^c776122] *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f81c5baf87c49693231c812d3f5965e9]**Diocesan Board of Finance *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f81c5baf87c49693231c812d3f5965e9]**” and “ *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f81c5baf87c49693231c812d3f5965e9]**diocesan glebe land *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f81c5baf87c49693231c812d3f5965e9]**” have the same meaning as in the *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f81c5baf87c49693231c812d3f5965e9] [^c776123]Endowments and Glebe Measure 1976 *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f81c5baf87c49693231c812d3f5965e9].
