# Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973, section 60

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 60. Short title and commencement.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 2000-08-25).**

(1) This Act may be cited as the Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973.

(2)[^c11221801] This Act shall come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order appoint, and different days may be so appointed for different provisions; and any reference in any provision of this Act to the commencement of this Act shall be construed as a reference to the day so appointed for the coming into force of that provision.

## Annotations

[^c11221801]: Modification: Power of appointment conferred by s. 60(2) not fully exercised
