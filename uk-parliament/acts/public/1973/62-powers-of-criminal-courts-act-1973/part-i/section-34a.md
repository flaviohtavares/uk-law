# Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973, section 34A

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34A. Power of Crown Court to order search of persons before it.[^c11219251]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 2000-08-25).**

(1) Where—

- (a) the Crown Court imposes a fine on a person or forfeits his recognizance;
- (b) the Crown Court makes against a person any such order as is mentioned in paragraph 3, 4 or 9 of Schedule 9 to the [^c11219261]Administration of Justice Act 1970 (orders for the payment of costs);
- (c) the Crown Court makes against a person any such order as is mentioned in paragraph 12 of that Schedule (fines etc. payable by parents or guardians) . . . [^c11219271] or
- (cc)[^c11219281] the Crown Court makes an order against a person under section 35 of this Act;
- (d) on the determination of an appeal brought by a person under section 83 of the [^c11219291]Magistrates’ Courts Act 1952 a sum is payable by him, whether by virtue of an order of the Crown Court or by virtue of a conviction or order of the magistrates’ court against whose decision the appeal was brought,

then, if that person is before it, the Crown Court may order him to be searched.

(2) Any money found on a person in a search under this section may be applied, unless the court otherwise directs, towards payment of the fine or other sum payable by him; and the balance, if any, shall be returned to him.
