# Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973, section 34

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 34. Power of Crown Court to allow time for payment, or payment by instalments, of costs and compensation.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 2000-08-25).**

Where the Crown Court makes any such order as is mentioned in Part I of Schedule 9 to the [^c11219241]Administration of Justice Act 1970 (orders against accused for the payment of costs or compensation), the court may—

- (a) allow time for the payment of the sum due under the order;
- (b) direct payment of that sum by instalments of such amounts and on such dates respectively as the court may specify.
