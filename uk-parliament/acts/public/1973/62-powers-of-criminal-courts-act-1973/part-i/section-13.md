# Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973, section 13

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Effects of probation and discharge.[^c11215341][^c11215351][^c11215361][^c11215371][^c11215331]

*Extent: E+W+S*

**No longer in force (ended 1992-10-01).**

(1)[^c11215381] Subject to subsection (2) below, and to section 50(1A) of the Criminal Appeal Act 1968 and section 108(1A) of the Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980,[^c11215391] a conviction of an offence for which an order is made under this Part of this Act placing the offender on probation or discharging him absolutely or conditionally shall be deemed not to be a conviction for any purpose other than the purposes of the proceedings in which the order is made and of any subsequent proceedings which may be taken against the offender under the preceding provisions of this Act and the purposes of section 1(2)([^c11215401]*bb*) of the Children and Young Persons Act 1969.

(2) Where the offender was of or over seventeen years of age at the time of his conviction of the offence in question and is subsequently sentenced under this Part of this Act for that offence, subsection (1) above shall cease to apply to the conviction.

(3)[^c11215411] Without prejudice to the preceding provisions of this section, the conviction of an offender who is placed on probation or discharged absolutely or conditionally under this Part of this Act shall in any event be disregarded for the purposes of any enactment or instrument which imposes any disqualification or disability upon convicted persons, or authorises or requires the imposition of any such disqualification or disability.

(4) The preceding provisions of this section shall not affect—

- (a) any right of any offender placed on probation or discharged absolutely or conditionally under this Part of this Act to rely on his conviction[^c11215421] in bar of any subsequent proceedings for the same offence;
- (b) the restoration of any property in consequence of the conviction of any such offender; or
- (c) the operation, in relation to any such offender, of any enactment or instrument in force at the commencement of this Act which is expressed to extend to persons dealt with under section 1(1) of the [^c11215431]Probation of Offenders Act 1907 as well as to convicted persons.

(5) In this section “**enactment**” includes an enactment contained in a local Act and “**instrument**” means an instrument having effect by virtue of an Act.
