# Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973, section 30

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. General power of Crown Court to fine offender convicted on indictment.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 2000-08-25).**

(1) Where a person is convicted on indictment of any offence other than an offence for which the sentence is fixed by law or falls to be imposed under section 2(2), 3(2) or 4(2) of the Crime (Sentences) Act 1997[^c11217681], the court, if not precluded from sentencing the offender by its exercise of some other power [^c11217691]. . ., may impose a fine in lieu of or in addition to dealing with him in any other way in which the court has power to deal with him, subject however to any enactment . . . [^c11217701] requiring the offender to be dealt with in a particular way.
