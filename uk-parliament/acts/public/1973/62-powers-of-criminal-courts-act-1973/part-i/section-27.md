# Powers of Criminal Courts Act 1973, section 27

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Breach of requirement of suspended sentence supervision order.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 2000-08-25).**

(1) If at any time while a supervision order is in force in respect of an offender it appears on information to a justice of the peace acting for the petty sessions area for the time being specified in the order that the offender has failed to comply with any of the requirements of section 26(4) of this Act, the justice may issue a summons requiring the offender to appear at the place and time specified therein, or may, if the information is in writing and on oath, issue a warrant for his arrest.

(2) Any summons or warrant issued under this section shall direct the offender to appear or be brought before a magistrates’ court acting for the petty sessions area for the time being specified in the supervision order.

(3)[^c11217461][^c11217471] If it is proved to the satisfaction of the court before which an offender appears or is brought under this section that he has failed without reasonable cause to comply with any of the requirements of section 26(4) the court may, without prejudice to the continuance of the order, impose on him a fine not exceeding £1000[^c11217481].

(4)[^c11217491] A fine imposed under subsection (3) above shall be deemed, for the purposes of any enactment, to be a sum adjudged to be paid by a conviction.
