# Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973, section 123

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2025-08-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1973/65
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 123. Education authorities.[^c9679351]

*Extent: S*

The education authority for the purposes of the [^c9679361]Education (Scotland) Act 1980 and any other enactment conferring functions on the education authority shall be a local authority.
