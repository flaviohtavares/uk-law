# Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973, section 155

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2025-08-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1973/65
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 155. Factories.

*Extent: S*

(1)[^c9681491] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c9681501] In section 46(6) (bye-laws), for the words “301 to 303” there shall be substituted the words “ 201 to 204 ” and for the word “1947”, where it twice occurs, there shall be substituted the word “ 1973 ”.

(3)[^c9681501] In section 47(1) (means of escape), the word “either” and the words from “or, where” to the end shall cease to have effect.

(4)[^c9681521] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^c9681501] In section 153(3) (provisions as to councils), the words “a county council and” shall cease to have effect.

(6)[^c9681501] Section 181(3) (definition of “district council” for certain purposes), shall cease to have effect.

(7)[^c9681501] In section 182 (application to Scotland), subsection (2) shall cease to have effect, and in subsection (9) for the words “county and town” there shall be substituted the words “ islands and district ”.
