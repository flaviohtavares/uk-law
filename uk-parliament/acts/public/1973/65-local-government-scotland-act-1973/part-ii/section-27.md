## 27. Consultation with Boundaries Scotland on schemes for community councils[^c-81623015cfba3271a31f3cce533ccda5]

*Extent: S*

In considering the framing or approval of a scheme or of an amendment to a scheme under Part IV of this Act, a local authority or the Secretary of State may consult Boundaries Scotland[^c-220ebf15fac82638daf0ed5ca97ef765] on any matter relating to the boundaries of the area of a community council.
