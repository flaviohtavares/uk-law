## 12. Boundaries Scotland[^c-12d5ee2834aeed0b4a378fb76f02f168]

*Extent: S*

(1) There shall be a commission to be known as Boundaries Scotland which is to carry out the functions conferred on it by or under this Act or any other enactment.[^c-5f7df2b4ca1ad42f51d621afed01c49e]

(2) The provisions of Schedule 4 to this Act shall have effect with respect to Boundaries Scotland[^c-17a0f0c4483a86af2807d7fdf212596d].
