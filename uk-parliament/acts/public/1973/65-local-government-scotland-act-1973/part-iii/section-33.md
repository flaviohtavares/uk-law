# Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973, section 33

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2025-08-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1973/65
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Validity of acts done by unqualified persons.[^c9669101]

*Extent: S*

The acts and proceedings of any person elected to an office under the Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994[^c9669111] and acting in that office shall, notwithstanding any question as to the validity of his election or his disqualification or want of qualification, be as valid and effectual as if he had been duly elected and qualified.
