## 236. Savings.

*Extent: S*

(1) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c9685851]

(2) [^c9685861]. . . Nothing in sections 70 to 78 of this Act shall affect any provision relating to the acquisition by agreement or compulsorily, appropriation, letting or disposal of land, the erection of buildings or the execution of works by a local authority contained in any of the following enactments or any instrument made thereunder—

- (a)[^key-ea614516111799d8622c7394386f498b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) The Military Lands Acts 1892 to 1903;
- (c)[^key-ea614516111799d8622c7394386f498b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) The Housing (Scotland) Act 1987[^c9685881];
- (e)[^c9685891] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f) The Education (Scotland) Acts 1939 to 1973;
- (g)[^key-4a2e9ed622c560ab1d15510359af775b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (h) Any local Act.

(3) Any enabling provision contained in this Act shall be in addition to, and not in derogation of, any powers exercisable by Her Majesty by virtue of her royal prerogative.
