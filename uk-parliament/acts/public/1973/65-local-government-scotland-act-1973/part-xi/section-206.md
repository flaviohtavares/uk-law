# Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973, section 206

## 206. Admission of honorary freemen.

*Extent: S*

(1) A local authority[^c9684201] may, by resolution passed by not less than two-thirds of the members voting thereon at a meeting of the authority the notice of which specifies the proposed admission as an item of business, admit to be honorary freemen of their area[^c9684221] persons of distinction and any persons who have rendered eminent service to their area.

(2) An officer designated for the purpose by local authority[^c9684241] shall keep a roll containing the names of persons admitted to be freemen under this section.
