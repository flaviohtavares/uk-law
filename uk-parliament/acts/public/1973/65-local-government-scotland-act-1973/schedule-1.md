### Part IV

**May not be in force at this date. Check the source.**

#### Provisions as to boundaries

##### 1.

The boundaries of the new local government areas shall be mered by Ordnance Survey.

##### 2.

Any such boundary defined on the map annexed to any order under Part VI of the [^c9685971]Local Government (Scotland) Act 1947 by reference to proposed works shall, until such works are carried out, be mered as if the boundary had not been so defined.

##### 3.

In this Schedule references to existing polling districts shall mean those districts as bounded as at 16th May 1973.
