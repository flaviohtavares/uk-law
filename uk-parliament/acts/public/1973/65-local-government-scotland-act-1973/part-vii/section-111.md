# Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973, section 111

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2025-08-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1973/65
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 111. Secretary of State may make regulations with respect to rates.

*Extent: S*

(1) The Secretary of State may, after consultation with such associations of local authorities as appear to him to be concerned, make regulations—

- (a)[^c9678971] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^c9678971] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) making provision for any matter with respect to which he is empowered or obliged by this Act, or by any other enactment, to make provision in regulations under this section;
- (d)[^c9678971] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e) providing for the payment of interest, at such rate as may be prescribed, by a local authority to another local authority, to a committee, joint committee or joint board all the members of which, other than any ex officio members, are appointed by one or more local authorities, [^c9679001]. . . in a case where any amount due in respect of a requisition made under any enactment is not paid on or before such date as may be prescribed.
- (f) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c9679011]

(2) A statutory instrument containing regulations made by the Secretary of State under this section shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
