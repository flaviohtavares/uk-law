## 187. Authorities for purposes of betting, gaming and lotteries, etc.

*Extent: S*

The provisions of the [^c9682841]Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963 and of the [^c9682851]Gaming Act 1968 relating to the authorities for the purposes of permits, licences and registration under those Acts shall have effect subject to the modifications and amendments set out in Part II of Schedule 24 to this Act.
