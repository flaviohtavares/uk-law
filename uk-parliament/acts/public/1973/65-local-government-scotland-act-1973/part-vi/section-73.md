# Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973, section 73

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2025-08-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1973/65
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 73. Appropriation of land.

*Extent: S*

(1) Subject to Part II of the [^c9672741]Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1959 and to the following provisions of this section, a local authority may appropriate for the purpose of any function, whether statutory or otherwise, land vested in them for the purpose of any other such function.

(2) A local authority may [^key-f731e840a5a1c0e36049aec2b6ad6e69]... exercise their power of appropriation under subsection (1) above with respect to any land specified in subsection (3)[^c9672751] below subject to sections 117 and 118 of the Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015[^key-6265e028c82236aac6d1a0f84546916b].

(3) The land to which subsection (2) above applies is land which[^c9672761] is held for use as allotments (within the meaning of section 107 of that Act)[^key-127f679a04bffb7ef1e21cf9153cec57].
