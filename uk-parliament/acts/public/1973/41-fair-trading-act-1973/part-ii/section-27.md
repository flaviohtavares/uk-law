# Fair Trading Act 1973, section 27

> Text as at 2026-01-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Enforcing authorities.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It shall be the duty of every local weights and measures authority to enforce within their area the provisions of any order made under section 22 of this Act; . . . [^c10698441]

(2) Nothing in subsection (1) shall be taken as authorising a local weights and measures authority in Scotland to institute proceedings for an offence.
