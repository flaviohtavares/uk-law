# Fair Trading Act 1973, section 31

> Text as at 2026-01-19.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Notice of test.[^key-b1f5c5315b3ca483c62b16a4591386d9][^key-ddbe52b7fdfcacc41efc5778af16e006][^key-ddbe52b7fdfcacc41efc5778af16e006]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where any goods seized or purchased by a person in pursuance of this Part of this Act are submitted to a test, then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ed6a77a8ae75869139bbe98f0df9495e]

- (a) if the goods were seized, he shall inform any such person as is mentioned in section 29(2) of this Act of the result of the test; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ed6a77a8ae75869139bbe98f0df9495e]
- (b) if the goods were purchased and the test leads to the institution of proceedings for an offence under section 23 of this Act, he shall inform the person from whom the goods were purchased, or, in the case of goods sold through a vending machine, the person mentioned in relation to such goods in section 29(2) of this Act, of the result of the test; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ed6a77a8ae75869139bbe98f0df9495e]

and where, as a result of the test, proceedings for an offence under section 23 of this Act are instituted against any person, he shall allow that person to have the goods tested on his behalf if it is reasonably practicable to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ed6a77a8ae75869139bbe98f0df9495e]
