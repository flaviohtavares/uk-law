# Hallmarking Act 1973, section 24

> Text as at 2025-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Short title, commencement and extent.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Hallmarking Act 1973.

(2) Section 13 of and Schedule 4 to this Act shall come into operation on 1st January 1974 and the remainder of this Act shall come into operation on 1st January 1975.

(3) This Act extends to Northern Ireland . . . [^c4449201].
