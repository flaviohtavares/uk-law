# International Sugar Organisation Act 1973

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

International Sugar Organisation Act 1973

1973 c.68

An Act to provide for the continued application to the International Sugar Organisation of section 1 of the International Organisations Act 1968.

\[19th December 1973\]

## 1. Application of s. 1 of the International Organisations Act 1968.

The termination of the membership of Her Majesty’s Government in the United Kingdom of the International Sugar Organisation shall not affect the application to that Organisation of section 1 of the [^c705072]International Organisations Act 1968.

## 2. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the International Sugar Organisation Act 1973.
