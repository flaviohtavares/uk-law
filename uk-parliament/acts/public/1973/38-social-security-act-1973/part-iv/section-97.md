# Social Security Act 1973, section 97

> Text as at 1996-09-24.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. Parliamentary control of orders and regulations.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1), (2) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c8801001]

(3)[^c8801021][^c8801011] All regulations and[^c8801031] orders made under this Act by the Secretary of State shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(4)[^c8801031] In the case of any regulations laid before Parliament at a time when Parliament is not sitting, the requirements of section . . . [^c8801051]68(2) of this Act shall be deemed to be satisfied as respects either House of Parliament if a copy of the report and the statement referred to in that[^c8801061] subsection are laid before that House not later than the second day on which the House sits after the laying of the regulations.
