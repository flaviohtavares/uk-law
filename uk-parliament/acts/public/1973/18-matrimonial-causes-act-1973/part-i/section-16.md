## 16. Effect of annulment in case of voidable marriage.[^c-ad8372c0be09bd966dc84d57c89587ce]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-4829b16da6e89d901d5e01a50cbcfd4b] A nullity of marriage order granted[^c-f60c2b968ef6182fa301841729691c1e] in respect of a voidable marriage shall operate to annul the marriage only as respects any time after the order has been made final,[^c-9a79a98f17c15f8ad2fcb1531cc8bb6a] and the marriage shall, notwithstanding the order,[^c-58d7ae88f6c706cfb0fb225605a3d927] be treated as if it had existed up to that time.

(2)[^c-7d2c9f8a0ab8ba0baa35b1015a805098] Subsection (1) has effect in relation to a decree of nullity granted after 31 July 1971 as it has effect in relation to a nullity of marriage order, but with the substitution—

- (a) for “order has been made final,” of “decree has been made absolute,” and
- (b) for “notwithstanding the order,” of “notwithstanding the decree,
