# Matrimonial Causes Act 1973, section 22

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-08-02. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1973/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Maintenance pending suit.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-9527d5bb0a3951275b0b1a7534ed86d0] On an application for a divorce, nullity of marriage or judicial separation order,[^c-3e3d8fb2d95c343904b696516fe70e79] the court may make an order for maintenance pending suit, that is to say, an order requiring either party to the marriage to make to the other such periodical payments for his or her maintenance and for such term, being a term beginning not earlier than the date of the making of the application[^c-cc3c4e12037a4ec7f9aaf2509407e9ec] and ending with the date of the determination of the suit, as the court thinks reasonable.

(2)[^c-27ba2c4d439f07aa852daf9ccb1bd030] An order under this section may not require a party to a marriage to pay to the other party any amount in respect of legal services for the purposes of the proceedings.

(3) In subsection (2) “**legal services**” has the same meaning as in section 22ZA.
