## 39. Settlement, etc. made in compliance with a property adjustment order may be avoided on bankruptcy of settlor.[^c8900761]

*Extent: E+W*

The fact that a settlement or transfer of property had to be made in order to comply with a property adjustment order shall not prevent that settlement or transfer from being a transaction in respect of which an order may be made under[^c8900771] section 339 or 340 of the[^c8900781] [^c8900791]Insolvency Act 1986 (transactions at an undervalue and preferences.)
