# Matrimonial Causes Act 1973, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-08-02. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1973/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Commencement of proceedings for ancillary relief, etc.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where an application for a divorce, nullity of marriage or judicial separation order has been made,[^c-abba03ad8ac6ee0d3ae30b364a2b4fd1] then, subject to subsection (2) below, proceedings for maintenance pending suit under section 22 above, for a financial provision order under section 23 above, or for a property adjustment order may be begun, subject to and in accordance with rules of court, at any time after the presentation of the application[^c-a244db7aa97c9d47941a9f4a42b1755b].

(2) Rules of court may provide, in such cases as may be prescribed by the rules—

- (a) that applications for any such relief as is mentioned in subsection (1) above shall be made in the application or response[^c-b30c81a64f768d343b7a2ec8f3e619e7]; and
- (b) that applications for any such relief which are not so made, or are not made until after the expiration of such period following the presentation of the application or filing of the response[^c-dee8aa73a427a7de10920f6098ab04c6] as may be so prescribed, shall be made only with the leave of the court.
