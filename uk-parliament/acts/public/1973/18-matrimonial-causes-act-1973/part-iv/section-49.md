## 49. Parties to proceedings under this Act.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-528041458c34056d82eccbed07181ac7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-528041458c34056d82eccbed07181ac7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-528041458c34056d82eccbed07181ac7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Rules of court may make provision[^key-ad4ce74c5621c6b9906fbcb9f6e7810b]... with respect to the joinder as parties to proceedings under this Act of persons involved in allegations of [^key-754ac96783efa9f264a4a5dbe5abd685]... improper conduct made in those proceedings, and with respect to the dismissal from such proceedings of any parties so joined; and rules of court made by virtue of this subsection may make different provision for different cases.

(5) In every case[^key-905cf32997910588d0cfa2ea968681ec] in which the court considers, in the interest of any person not already a party to the suit, that that person should be made a party to the suit, the court may if it thinks fit allow that person to intervene upon such terms, if any, as the court thinks just.
