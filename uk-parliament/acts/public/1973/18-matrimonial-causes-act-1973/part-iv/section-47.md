# Matrimonial Causes Act 1973, section 47

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Matrimonial relief and declarations of validity in respect of polygamous marriages.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A court in England and Wales shall not be precluded from granting matrimonial relief or making a declaration concerning the validity of a marriage by reason only that either party to the marriage is, or has during the subsistence of the marriage been, married to more than one person[^c8901191].

(2) In this section “**matrimonial relief**” means—

- (a) any order[^c-56b90058d3d805f85f4e75c650671fa5] under Part I of this Act;
- (b) a financial provision order under section 27 above;
- (c) an order under section 35 above altering a maintenance agreement;
- (d) an order under any provision of this Act which confers a power exercisable in connection with, or in connection with proceedings for, any such [^c-a48521a66d0da13aa99f1c13adefba68]... order as is mentioned in paragraphs (a) to (c) above;
- (dd)[^c8901201] an order under Part III of the Matrimonial and Family Proceedings Act 1984;
- (e) an order under Part I of the[^c8901211] [^c8901221]Domestic Proceedings and Magistrates’ Courts Act 1978.

(3)[^c8901231] In this section “**a declaration concerning the validity of a marriage**” means any declaration under Part III of the Family Law Act 1986 involving a determination as to the validity of a marriage

(4)[^c8901241] Provision may be made by rules of court—

- (a) for requiring notice of proceedings brought by virtue of this section to be served on any additional spouse of a party to the marriage in question; and
- (b) for conferring on any such additional spouse the right to be heard in the proceedings,

in such cases as may be specified in the rules.
