# Matrimonial Causes Act 1973, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (2).** Text as at 2024-08-02. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1973/18

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Transitional provisions and savings.

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 1 to this Act shall have effect for the purpose of—

- (a) the transition to the provisions of this Act from the law in force before the commencement of this Act;
- (b) the preservation for limited purposes of certain provisions superseded by provisions of this Act or by enactments repealed and replaced by this Act; and
- (c) the assimilation in certain respects to orders under this Act of orders made, or deemed to have been made, under the [^c8902411]Matrimonial Causes Act 1965.
