# Domestic Abuse Act 2021, section 61

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Interpretation of Part 4[^key-8278e921a52f0f26e1c1b1fecd677162]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “**local authority**” means—
  - (a) a relevant local authority;
  - (b) a district council for an area for which there is a county council;
  - (c) a London borough council;
  - (d) the Common Council of the City of London in its capacity as a local authority;
- “**relevant local authority**” means—
  - (a) a county council;
  - (b) a district council for an area for which there is no county council;
  - (c) the Greater London Authority;
  - (d) the Council of the Isles of Scilly.
