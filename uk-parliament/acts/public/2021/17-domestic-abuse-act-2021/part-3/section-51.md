# Domestic Abuse Act 2021, section 51

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-03-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2021/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Data from electronic monitoring: code of practice[^c-5edb52bff3ab88e8af1c8fe8939b1e8e][^c-6da239816b93a0c5b59cbba56193520c][^c-60f797d7f327d1beedb4e4ef2d8d505a][^c-3795bbf73861bd0a573c4aaa8975ad93]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State must issue a code of practice relating to the processing of data gathered in the course of electronic monitoring of individuals under electronic monitoring requirements imposed by domestic abuse protection orders.

(2) A failure to act in accordance with a code issued under this section does not of itself make a person liable to any criminal or civil proceedings.
