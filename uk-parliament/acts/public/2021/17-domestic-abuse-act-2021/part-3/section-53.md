# Domestic Abuse Act 2021, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-03-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2021/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Proceedings not to be subject to conditional fee agreements[^c-4fb21ae17b45daafd7d76b4fd6391de1][^c-cda0341d8482df08a3a1078e80e8883d][^c-95407a726d09a89b0290c9f06b8d6652][^c-94734917575b9b1bd5b61bd0b3f6bc70]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 58A of the Courts and Legal Services Act 1990 (conditional fee agreements: supplementary), in subsection (2), after paragraph (fd) (but before the “and” following it) insert—

> - (fe) proceedings under Part 3 of the Domestic Abuse Act 2021 (proceedings for domestic abuse protection order), where the proceedings are in the family court or the Family Division of the High Court;

.
