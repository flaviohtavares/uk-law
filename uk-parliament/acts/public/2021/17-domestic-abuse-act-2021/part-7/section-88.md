# Domestic Abuse Act 2021, section 88

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-03-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2021/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Financial provision

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There is to be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenditure incurred by a Minister of the Crown under or by virtue of this Act, and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable under any other Act out of money so provided.
