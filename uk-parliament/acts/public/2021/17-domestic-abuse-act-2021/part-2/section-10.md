# Domestic Abuse Act 2021, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-03-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2021/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Incidental powers[^key-b83e42c20897a5300be447b9ace99a35]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Commissioner may do anything which the Commissioner considers will facilitate, or is incidental or conducive to, the carrying out of the Commissioner's functions.

(2) But the Commissioner may not borrow money.
