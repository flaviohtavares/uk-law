# Financial Services Act 2021, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2024-11-22. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2021/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Appointment of chief executive of FCA

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 1ZA to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Conduct Authority) is amended as follows.

(2) In paragraph 2A—

- (a) after sub-paragraph (1) insert—

  > (1A) Appointment as chief executive under paragraph 2(2)(b) is to be for a period of 5 years.

  , and
- (b) in sub-paragraph (2), for “Sub-paragraph (1) does” substitute “ Sub-paragraphs (1) and (1A) do ”.

(3) After paragraph 2A insert—

> **2B.**
>
> (1) A person may not be appointed as chief executive under paragraph 2(2)(b) more than twice.
>
> (2) For this purpose an appointment as chief executive on an acting basis, pending a further appointment being made, is to be ignored.
