# Environment Act 2021, section 19

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 19. Policy statement on environmental principles: effect[^c-cb018806797599ae58dd0a18f63347d1]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-fe4b441b75f1618ae8f275a0feda17ab] A Minister of the Crown must, when making policy, have due regard to the policy statement on environmental principles currently in effect.

(2)[^c-fe4b441b75f1618ae8f275a0feda17ab] Nothing in subsection (1) requires a Minister to do anything (or refrain from doing anything) if doing it (or refraining from doing it)—

- (a) would have no significant environmental benefit, or
- (b) would be in any other way disproportionate to the environmental benefit.

(3)[^c-fe4b441b75f1618ae8f275a0feda17ab] Subsection (1) does not apply to policy so far as relating to—

- (a) the armed forces, defence or national security,
- (b) taxation, spending or the allocation of resources within government, or
- (c) Wales.

(4)[^c-fe4b441b75f1618ae8f275a0feda17ab] Subsection (1) applies to policy relating to Scotland only so far as relating to reserved matters.

(5)[^c-0915a4ef702062da65cc3d4387cf689b] Section 14(2) of the UK Withdrawal from the European Union (Continuity) (Scotland) Act [2021 (asp 4)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/asp/2021/4) (UK Ministers must have regard to guiding principles on the environment in making policies extending to Scotland) does not apply to policies so far as relating to reserved matters.

(6)[^c-0915a4ef702062da65cc3d4387cf689b] In this section “**reserved matters**” has the same meaning as in the Scotland Act 1998.
