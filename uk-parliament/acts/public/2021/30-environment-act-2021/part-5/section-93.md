# Environment Act 2021, section 93

> Text as at 2026-05-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 93. Water quality: interpretation[^key-aba8828ed17651e3d6e2455bc1c317c6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In sections 89 to 92—

- “**groundwater**” has the same meaning as in the Water Framework Directive;
- “**river basin district**” means an area identified as such by or under any legislation to which the section in question applies;
- “**surface water**” has the same meaning as in the Water Framework Directive;
- “**Water Framework Directive**” means [Directive 2000/60/EC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/european/directive/2000/0060) of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a framework for Community action in the field of water policy.
