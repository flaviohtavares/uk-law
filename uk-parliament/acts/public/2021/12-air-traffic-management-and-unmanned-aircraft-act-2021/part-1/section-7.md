# Air Traffic Management and Unmanned Aircraft Act 2021, section 7

> Text as at 2026-03-03.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Appeals and enforcement[^c24533811]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-91281f5921c148d14b61f4a76f903ef1] Schedule 1 makes provision for appeals against decisions to give or vary directions under sections 2 and 3.

(2)[^key-1079195fede619de79a05777e3525427] Schedule 2 makes provision for—

- (a) the CAA to enforce directions, and
- (b) connected appeals.
