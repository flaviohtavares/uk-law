> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 129. Further provision relating to pension schemes: Northern Ireland

*Extent: N.I.*

Schedule 11 contains—

- (a)[^c-596689899c38154a032990bdf4542ba6] in Part 1, provision for Northern Ireland corresponding to the provision made for England and Wales and Scotland in Schedule 10, and
- (b)[^c-19b2e7ae7e97a683d8d0f6a9fbac67a5][^c-fbea8ac06983b1b67e6d1d7207a0be69][^M_I_f5a7e604-a9af-4e9b-9d51-861770379d1c][^M_I_43b319ca-235d-4b80-9064-0b5fb8d504e8][^M_I_2b896876-4737-4da1-d09e-8f0046710a4a][^c-2ad7bf4bea1ba4ba76ca887d0e63bffa][^c-1a33df2de0951ed82a4ac821f3bbf37a] in Part 2, provision for Northern Ireland corresponding to the provision made for England and Wales and Scotland in sections 124 to 128.
