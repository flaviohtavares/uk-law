# Pension Schemes Act 2021, section 128

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 128. Pension Schemes Act 2015: repeals

*Extent: E+W+S*

In the Pension Schemes Act 2015, omit—

- (a)[^key-ab464be1fe014ca779ef67adca72379d] sections 1 to 7 (Part 1: categories of pension scheme);
- (b)[^key-ab464be1fe014ca779ef67adca72379d] sections 8 to 35 (Part 2: pension schemes providing collective benefits);
- (c)[^key-9188a8dbe0c17b67a84fc6412decb9fd] sections 36, 37, 38(2), (3) and (5) to (7) and 39 to 45 (general changes to legislation about pension schemes);
- (d)[^key-ab464be1fe014ca779ef67adca72379d] Schedule 1 (early leavers: revaluation of accrued benefits);
- (e)[^key-ab464be1fe014ca779ef67adca72379d] in Schedule 2 (other amendments to do with Parts 1 and 2)—
  - (i) paragraphs 1 to 22;
  - (ii) paragraphs 25 to 29;
  - (iii) paragraphs 31 and 32;
  - (iv) paragraphs 34 and 35;
  - (v) paragraphs 37 to 51.
