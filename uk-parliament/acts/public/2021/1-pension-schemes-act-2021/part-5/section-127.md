# Pension Schemes Act 2021, section 127

> Text as at 2026-07-31.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 127. Administration charges

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In section 1 of the Welfare Reform and Pensions Act 1999 (meaning of “stakeholder pension scheme”), in subsection (5) (fourth condition: charge cap)—

- (a) for “result in” substitute “ constitute ”;
- (b) after “members” insert “ or the making of transfer payments with a view to acquiring rights or entitlements to pension benefits under different pension schemes ”.

(2)[^c-62e2589b71b730583cedcf2b08aea2ec] In Schedule 18 to the Pensions Act 2014 (power to restrict charges or impose requirements in relation to schemes), in paragraph 1(5), in the definition of “administration charge”—

- (a) for “result in” substitute “ constitute ”;
- (b) after “members” insert “ or the making of transfer payments with a view to acquiring rights or entitlements to pension benefits under different pension schemes ”.

(3)[^c-20045366d01673e4060b36ad3be80a27] In section 113 of the Pension Schemes Act 1993 (disclosure of information about schemes to members etc)—

- (a) in subsection (6)(b), after “the” insert “ other ”;
- (b) in subsection (7), after “or” insert “ other ”.

(4)[^c-20045366d01673e4060b36ad3be80a27] In section 137FA of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (FCA general rules: disclosure of information about pension scheme transaction costs etc)—

- (a) in subsection (3)(b), after “the” insert “ other ”;
- (b) in subsection (4), after “or” insert “ other ”;
- (c) in subsection (7), after “costs or” insert “ other ”.
