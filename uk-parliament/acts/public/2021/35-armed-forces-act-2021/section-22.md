> Text as at 2023-11-22.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Minor amendments[^c-5783cca7f40deee3534d4adf2dc05515][^c-f1e455829750e863b7fff245b4038d8b][^c-f18321da6743368a89ac978d2d94e3dd][^c-549f2e522f8fd5333bb7a9ecfebad27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-5783cca7f40deee3534d4adf2dc05515][^c-f1e455829750e863b7fff245b4038d8b] AFA 2006 is amended as follows.

(2)[^c-f18321da6743368a89ac978d2d94e3dd] In section 61(1) (sections 55 to 60: exceptions to time limits for charging and interpretation) for “section 120 or 122” substitute “any of sections 120 to 122”.

(3)[^c-549f2e522f8fd5333bb7a9ecfebad27e] In section 373(3) (orders, regulations and rules: affirmative procedure) after “A statutory instrument containing” insert “(whether alone or with other provision)”.
