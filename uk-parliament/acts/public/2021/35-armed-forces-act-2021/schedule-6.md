## Schedule 6 — Deprivation and driving disqualification orders: minor and consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 16

### 1. Armed Forces Act 1991[^key-d50d5ef29bb05864d1ee609931c9d5e3][^key-ecc03ee599d99547111b05d7557b708e][^key-a78da76f2a80134bbb49aa6305ac652c]

The Armed Forces Act 1991 is amended as follows.

**2.**[^key-fb2c4b1ab22b4840e0243b1862d6d9a1][^key-40424b515654fd16e3b45a8a9819c0ce][^key-28c5d63ef252824c4c3af0b298bc0422] In section 18 (intentional obstruction)—

- (a) in subsection (8A) for “12” substitute “14”;
- (b) in subsection (8B)—
  - (i) for “12” substitute “14”;
  - (ii) for “7” substitute “9”;
- (c) in subsection (8C)—
  - (i) for “12” substitute “14”;
  - (ii) for “10” substitute “12”.

**3.**[^key-65e5a4d429ff5aef4ca25e567c588fe2][^key-58e801f87d675a8b0bcbb1c8f4eb034e][^key-058fe635e90172cd33f0d1d8c0939166] In section 20 (intentional obstruction or failure to comply with exclusion requirement)—

- (a) in subsection (9A) for “12” substitute “14”;
- (b) in subsection (9B)—
  - (i) for “12” substitute “14”;
  - (ii) for “7” substitute “9”;
- (c) in subsection (9C)—
  - (i) for “12” substitute “14”;
  - (ii) for “10” substitute “12”.

### 4. Reserve Forces Act 1996[^key-e56252a7fc3243261e1b2024d3de65e7][^key-e1099d15176b2bec306695fe715778d3][^key-309b5ebc72489d826ec6cadf8de339ed]

The Reserve Forces Act 1996 is amended as follows.

**5.**[^key-d1c95b5c49b0db6c2f6b0018d610314e][^key-65d806c493ce61d2df0883c2b4a914ca][^key-b8184c6ea51fcc347152ad55fc90fcb9] In section 95 (offences against orders and regulations under section 4)—

- (a) in subsection (2)(a)(i) for “12” substitute “14”;
- (b) in subsection (2A)—
  - (i) for “12” substitute “14”;
  - (ii) for “10” substitute “12”.

**6.**[^key-70eb6871db335744803f5b5cfc74e9c2][^key-3ab9da600a609877cddc42ca8392329c][^key-ecd21e44930ba076514ba84b7b305be6] In paragraph 5 of Schedule 1 (false answer to question in attestation paper)—

- (a) in sub-paragraph (3) for “12” substitute “14”;
- (b) in sub-paragraph (4)—
  - (i) for “12” substitute “14”;
  - (ii) for “10” substitute “12”.

### 7. Armed Forces Act 2006[^key-3baa26b5c2cb49080d9862b69e54b5a0][^key-f083c982558980cb8f76bec2447715a0][^key-fc484de37b011d80b47235a6207b78c1]

AFA 2006 is amended as follows.

**8.**[^key-64be45fe098884aca35c01b86bdb13fc][^key-25dd0908b4d1878bbb2f6e7d1d174b7e][^key-88375aa206349733fa9652114be99e67] In the following provisions, for “12” substitute “14”—

- (a) section 25(2) (penalty for misapplying public property etc);
- (b) section 35(3) (penalty for annoyance by flying);
- (c) section 42(3)(b) (penalties for criminal conduct offences);
- (d) section 328(4)(a) (enlistment, terms of service etc);
- (e) section 343(5)(a) (service inquiries).

**9.**[^key-61573439df0bba891eb4ffc87897b648][^key-b33c3ab6a835bb67e62b55afa000819d][^key-fcb47db8bea2d01268e84068c926549e] In section 185(4) (conditional or absolute discharge (civilians only)), at the end insert “, deprivation order or driving disqualification order”.

**10.**[^key-9d60230bfec885a5fe3287f1e5c3c552][^key-ea0fe62964999b2ed836143677b3e1f3][^key-065db5128ff67bfa80aaf2cc81ffdc21] In Schedule 3 (civilians etc: modifications of Court Martial sentencing powers)—

- (a) in paragraph 2—
  - (i) for “12” substitute “14”;
  - (ii) for “7” substitute “9”;
- (b) in paragraph 4—
  - (i) for “12”, in each place it occurs, substitute “14”;
  - (ii) for “10”, in each place it occurs, substitute “12”.
