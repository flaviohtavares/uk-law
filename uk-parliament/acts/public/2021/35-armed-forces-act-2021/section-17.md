# Armed Forces Act 2021, section 17

> Text as at 2023-11-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Removal of requirement to take into account offences in member States[^key-98ed189e2a102afa95e607f967827fd7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-e7b5f446b6adccfdfe859454016b2755] AFA 2006 is amended as follows.

(2)[^key-e7b5f446b6adccfdfe859454016b2755] In section 238 (deciding the seriousness of an offence)—

- (a) in subsection (3)—
  - (i) in paragraph (a) at the end insert “or”;
  - (ii) omit paragraphs (c) and (d);
- (b) for subsection (4) substitute—

  > (4) Nothing in this section prevents the court or officer from treating a previous conviction by a court outside the British Islands as an aggravating factor in any case where the court or officer considers it appropriate to do so.

  ;
- (c) omit subsection (5).

(3)[^key-e7b5f446b6adccfdfe859454016b2755] In section 263 (restriction on imposing custodial sentence or service detention on unrepresented offender)—

- (a) in subsection (2)(b) omit the words from “, or sentenced to detention” to the end;
- (b) in subsection (6) omit paragraphs (c) and (d).

(4)[^key-e7b5f446b6adccfdfe859454016b2755] In section 270A (exception to restrictions on community punishments)—

- (a) in subsection (3)—
  - (i) in paragraph (a) omit “, or member State service offence,” and at the end insert “or”;
  - (ii) in paragraph (b) omit “or”;
  - (iii) omit paragraph (c);
- (b) omit subsection (8).

(5)[^key-98ed189e2a102afa95e607f967827fd7] In section 415 of the Sentencing Act 2020 (armed forces provisions: extent to Channel Islands, Isle of Man and British overseas territories), after subsection (6) insert—

> (7) In subsection (6) references to this Act include this Act as amended by the [Taking Account of Convictions (EU Exit) (Amendment) Regulations 2020 (S.I. 2020/1520)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/1520).
