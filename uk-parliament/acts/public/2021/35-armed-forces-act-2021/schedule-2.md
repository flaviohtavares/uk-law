# Armed Forces Act 2021, Schedule 2

> Text as at 2023-11-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## Schedule 2 — Reserve forces: flexibility of commitments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 9

### Part 1 — Consequential amendments

#### 1. Reserve Forces Act 1996[^c-caaef11fe539f909026e2e548cdf3081][^c-cac786e23d531275089b307a02e2d9de]

The Reserve Forces Act 1996 is amended as follows.

**2.**[^c-ef949acd19696002d79977e2552fc05b][^c-1330faa4ecccc3e30409f5e045b7f838] (1) Section 17 (postponement of discharge) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1), for “full-time service under a full-time service commitment” substitute “service under a section 24 commitment”.

(3) In subsection (2), for “full-time service under a full-time service commitment” substitute “service under a section 24 commitment”.

(4) In subsection (4), for “full-time service under a full-time service commitment” substitute “service under a section 24 commitment”.

**3.**[^c-a00284c967df6e54149a36a73a3b6aba][^c-33985b8fede8c58e73f30c181e62967f] (1) Section 26 (parliamentary control of commitments) is amended as follows.

(2) For subsection (1)(a) substitute—

> - (a) in service under section 24 commitments; or

.

(3) In subsection (2), for “full-time service” substitute “service under section 24 commitments”.

(4) In subsection (3), for “full-time service” substitute “service under section 24 commitments”.

**4.**[^c-3c3cd0c9c52d8afd3a7080ac3133bb83][^c-662930f0ee97504e2de358be4c662535] (1) Section 97 (failure to attend for duty or training) is amended as follows.

(2) In subsection (1)—

- (a) in the words before paragraph (a), for “full-time service” substitute “section 24”;
- (b) in paragraph (a), for “full-time service”, in the first place it occurs, substitute “section 24”, and omit “full-time” in the second place it occurs.

**5.**[^c-2af0e05164590e0dfc835c3d405176a9][^c-7a304b271a8c2a8fee41a8845285219a] In subsection (1) of section 127 (interpretation)—

- (a) omit the definition of “full-time service commitment”, and
- (b) at the appropriate place, insert—

  > - “**section 24 commitment**” means a commitment under section 24;

  .

**6.**[^c-4b94e79d9d198a737dc4cc8d84c8f560][^c-1e5e61d4f37a3ba3024b9334d5a049a6] In Schedule 9 (application of Act to members of transitional classes), in paragraph 11, in the words before sub-paragraph (a), for “full-time service commitment” substitute “commitment under that section”.

#### 7. Armed Forces Act 2006[^c-6bbdb66276ebb09714ce573ede10625e][^c-a6445de0080dec753fee8ebe6b179487]

AFA 2006 is amended as follows.

**8.**[^c-92cdb965b6a07d98b8b106238bc2b895][^c-6d145a706dbec460cab87f96906ce6f7] In section 62 (time limit for charging Reserve Forces Act offences)—

- (a) in subsection (3)(c)(ii), for “full-time service” substitute “service under a commitment entered into under section 24 of the Reserve Forces Act 1996”;
- (b) omit subsection (3)(d).

**9.**[^c-a83d906d85ab204e96825f0910bb4fc5][^c-579331b8993b84580f11382ff58a23d1] In section 367 (persons subject to service law: regular and reserve forces), in subsection (2)(c), omit “full-time”.

### Part 2 — Transitional provision

**10.**[^c-4e0065ab12d514d9c6f57d76a0300d9f][^c-94e5d51bae02fbedd66b99f553e3a774] Regulations under section 24(4) may provide that any provision of section 9 or Part 1 of this Schedule is to have effect in relation to commitments under section 24 or 25 of the Reserve Forces Act 1996 entered into before section 9 comes into force.
