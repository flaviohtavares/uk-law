# Armed Forces Act 2021, section 20

> Text as at 2023-11-22.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Power of British overseas territories to apply AFA 2006 etc[^key-edda45e6a129be77d886c09dcbda3ef1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 357 of AFA 2006 (power of British overseas territory to apply Act, etc), after paragraph (b) insert—

> and in particular nothing prevents a law of a British overseas territory to which this section does not extend applying provisions of this Act in reliance on paragraph (b).

(2) Subsection (1) has effect in relation to a law of a British overseas territory, whenever passed or made.
