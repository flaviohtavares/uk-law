# Finance Act 2021, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2021/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Payment[^key-35a7634e7216a47c1ee31089a632ebb0][^key-a856046dda3071e0f6b2b75504ff8a15][^key-469a62b9d55a3a2abfb4e208c186572b][^key-469a62b9d55a3a2abfb4e208c186572b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Plastic packaging tax is to be paid by reference to accounting periods determined in accordance with regulations under section 61(1) (regulations about the payment, collection and recovery of plastic packaging tax).

(2) References in this Part to “accounting periods” are to those accounting periods.
