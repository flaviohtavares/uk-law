> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2021/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Plastic packaging tax[^key-c4c1cf6244d61d78c54bbb346a888fe6][^key-c6f1824594fe096350c51e8e71d6f139][^key-469a62b9d55a3a2abfb4e208c186572b][^key-469a62b9d55a3a2abfb4e208c186572b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A tax called “plastic packaging tax” is to be charged in accordance with this Part.

(2) The Commissioners are responsible for the collection and management of plastic packaging tax.
