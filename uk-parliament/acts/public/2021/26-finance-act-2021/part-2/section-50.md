# Finance Act 2021, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2021/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Time of importation[^c-df22855cb9d06ee2dcfceee130ab9864][^c-f1d4f908bebc20b071de1264874c27c3][^c-469a62b9d55a3a2abfb4e208c186572b][^c-469a62b9d55a3a2abfb4e208c186572b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A chargeable plastic packaging component is imported into the United Kingdom—

- (a) in the case of a component that is subject to customs formalities within the meaning given by section 1(1) of CEMA 1979, as soon as all such formalities have been complied with in respect of the component, and
- (b) in any other case, at the time of importation for the purposes of the customs and excise Acts.

(2) Subsection (1)[^c-1c057e5eaa1b97f7dabe9ebc4f67f601] is subject to section 76 (Isle of Man: import and export of chargeable plastic packaging components).

(3)[^c-4ac2aeb366400877956460f89decf03a] The Commissioners may by regulations make provision about when a chargeable plastic packaging component is imported into the United Kingdom for the purposes of plastic packaging tax.

(4) Regulations under subsection (3) may amend this Part.
