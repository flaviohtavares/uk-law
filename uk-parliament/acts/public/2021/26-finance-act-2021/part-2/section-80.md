# Finance Act 2021, section 80

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2021/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Penalty for contravening relevant requirements[^key-3b32168245ab76380c5d7cb46658eee0][^key-469a62b9d55a3a2abfb4e208c186572b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] Where a person (P) fails to comply with a relevant requirement, P is liable to—

- (a) a fixed penalty of £500, and
- (b) a daily penalty of £40 for each day, after the first, on which the person continues to fail to comply.

(2)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] Where P is liable to a daily penalty in respect of a continuing failure to comply with a relevant requirement P is not liable to a further fixed penalty in respect of that failure.

(3)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] P is not liable to a penalty under this section in respect of an act or omission in respect of which P—

- (a) has been convicted of an offence, or
- (b) is liable to a penalty other than under this section.

(4)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] P is not liable to a penalty under this section if P satisfies the Commissioners or (on appeal) the appeal tribunal within the meaning of Schedule 11 that there is a reasonable excuse for the failure.

(5)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] For the purposes of subsection (4)—

- (a) an insufficiency of funds is not a reasonable excuse, unless it is attributable to events outside P's control,
- (b) where P relies on any other person to do anything, that is not a reasonable excuse unless P took reasonable care to avoid the failure, and
- (c) where P had a reasonable excuse for the failure but the excuse has ceased, P is to be treated as having continued to have the excuse if the failure is remedied without unreasonable delay after the excuse ceased.

(6)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] Where P is liable to a penalty under this section—

- (a) the Commissioners or, on appeal, the appeal tribunal within the meaning of Schedule 11, may reduce the penalty to such amount (including nil) as they think proper;
- (b) on an appeal relating to any penalty reduced by the Commissioners, the appeal tribunal may cancel the whole or any part of the Commissioners' reduction.

(7) In this section, “**relevant requirement**” means an obligation or a requirement imposed by or under—

- (a)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] section 58 (variation and correction of the register);
- (b)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] section 61 (payment, collection and recovery);
- (c)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] section 63 (records);
- (d)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] section 65 (security for tax);
- (e) section 68 (statements);
- (f)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] section 69 (tax representatives);
- (g)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] section 74 (death, incapacity or insolvency of person carrying on a business);
- (h)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] section 75 (transfer of business as a going concern);
- (i)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] Schedule 9 (secondary liability and assessment notices and joint and several liability notices);
- (j)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] Schedule 13 (groups of companies).

(8)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] The Treasury may by regulations amend subsection (1) so as to substitute for the amounts for the time being specified there amounts taking account of inflation.

(9)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] The Treasury may by regulations amend subsection (7) so as to add or remove a requirement relating to plastic packaging tax as a “relevant requirement”.

(10)[^key-52669ea2702e96e1b6924fcdb9dae93d] Schedule 14 makes provision about the assessment of penalties under this section.
