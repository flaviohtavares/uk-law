# Finance Act 2021, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2021/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Rate[^c-459ae5a640226639126c9a8817b284d2][^c-cefc90b7c3d11048866a1147ca0b92ea][^c-469a62b9d55a3a2abfb4e208c186572b][^c-469a62b9d55a3a2abfb4e208c186572b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Plastic packaging tax is charged at the rate of £228.82[^c-a7bf8178ff9f0294b09d2e5374bf9195] per metric tonne of chargeable plastic packaging components of a single specification.

(2) The amount charged on part of a tonne is the proportionately reduced amount.
