# Finance Act 2021, section 68

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2021/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Statements for business customers[^c-469a62b9d55a3a2abfb4e208c186572b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-6d2b872ab90fb2a78f47648d00d5fea0] A person who—

- (a) supplies to a business customer a plastic packaging component in respect of which a charge to plastic packaging tax has arisen, and
- (b) is liable to pay plastic packaging tax on that component,

must, when invoicing that customer in respect of that component, include with that invoice a statement of the amount of plastic packaging tax arising in relation to that component (a “PPT statement”).

(2)[^c-6d2b872ab90fb2a78f47648d00d5fea0] The reference in subsection (1)(a) to supplying a plastic packaging component to a business customer includes supplying that component by virtue of supplying other goods, such as goods that are contained within the component.

(3)[^c-c818854b9aad4577df6567a20fa267c2] A PPT statement must contain such particulars as the Commissioners may prescribe in regulations.

(4)[^c-6d2b872ab90fb2a78f47648d00d5fea0] In this section, “**business customer**” means a person who is supplied with a plastic packaging component in the course of their carrying out a business (within the meaning of section 43(2)).
