## 15. Extension of temporary increase in annual investment allowance (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-bc02bbad5d1aaba82d4343255b1293ba][^key-bc02bbad5d1aaba82d4343255b1293ba]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 32(1) of FA 2019 (which increases the maximum amount of the annual investment allowance to £1,000,000 for the period of two years beginning with 1 January 2019), for “two years” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bc02bbad5d1aaba82d4343255b1293ba] “ three years ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bc02bbad5d1aaba82d4343255b1293ba].

(2) In consequence of the amendment made by subsection (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bc02bbad5d1aaba82d4343255b1293ba]

- (a) in section 32(2) of that Act, for “2021” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bc02bbad5d1aaba82d4343255b1293ba] “ 2022 ”,
- (b) in paragraph 2 of Schedule 13 to that Act and the heading before that paragraph, for “2021” (in each place) substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bc02bbad5d1aaba82d4343255b1293ba] “ 2022 ”,
- (c) in paragraph 3(3)(b) of that Schedule, for “two years” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bc02bbad5d1aaba82d4343255b1293ba] “ three years ” *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bc02bbad5d1aaba82d4343255b1293ba], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bc02bbad5d1aaba82d4343255b1293ba]
- (d) in the heading for that Schedule, for “2021” substitute *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bc02bbad5d1aaba82d4343255b1293ba] “ 2022 ”.
