# Finance Act 2021, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-04-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2021/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Basic rate limit and personal allowance for future tax years

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the tax years 2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25 , 2025-26, 2026-27[^key-a421ca7002d038a80a596fb4279524db] , 2027-28, 2028-29, 2029-30 and 2030-31[^key-9d32cd4e001e91db49a1ff1ffa155cf0] , the amount specified in section 10(5) of ITA 2007 (basic rate limit) is “£37,700”.

(2) For the tax years 2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25 , 2025-26, 2026-27[^key-290713075fc96822cc2ecf1382809d61] , 2027-28, 2028-29, 2029-30 and 2030-31[^key-f10481ffc78f683026cb19ea939e279a] , the amount specified in section 35(1) of ITA 2007 (personal allowance) is “£12,570”.

(3) Accordingly—

- (a) section 21 of ITA 2007 (indexation of basic rate limit) does not apply in relation to the basic rate limit, and
- (b) section 57 of ITA 2007 (indexation of allowances) does not apply in relation to the amount specified in section 35(1) of that Act,

for the tax years 2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25 , 2025-26, 2026-27[^key-0cf1f6d13914697c9d4f80fd43bca52a] , 2027-28, 2028-29, 2029-30 and 2030-31[^key-49a65c726ea5ec9478028f4058cf9812] .
