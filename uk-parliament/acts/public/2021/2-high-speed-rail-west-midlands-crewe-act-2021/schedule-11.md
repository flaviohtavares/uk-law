# High Speed Rail (West Midlands - Crewe) Act 2021, SCHEDULE 11

> Text as at 2024-10-30.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 11 — Land where powers of acquisition are limited to sub-surface acquisition

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 7

### Restriction of power of compulsory acquisition to land under the surface

**1.** In the case of land specified in the following table, the power under section 4(1) may be exercised only in relation to so much of the subsoil or under-surface of the land as lies more than 9 metres below the level of the surface.

| *Area* | *Number of land shown on deposited plans* |
| --- | --- |
| Borough of Newcastle-under-Lyme<br>Parish of Whitmore | 74, 77, 79, 82, 85, 87, 89, 92, 94, 96, 98, 100, 103, 105, 107, 109, 110, 178 to 181 |
| Parish of Madeley | 142, 145, 158, 160, 164, 166 |

### Supplementary

**2.** (1) Paragraph 1 is subject to the power to impose restrictive covenants by virtue of section 5(1)(b).

(2) Paragraph 1 does not restrict the exercise of the power under section 4(1) in relation to a cellar, vault, arch or other construction in, on or under a highway which forms part of a building fronting on to the highway where—

- (a) the building is within the Act limits, and
- (b) the power under section 4(1) is exercisable in relation to the building.
