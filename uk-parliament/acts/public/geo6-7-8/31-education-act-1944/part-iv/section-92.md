# Education Act 1944, section 92

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 92. Reports and returns.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

Every local education authority shall make to the Secretary of State for Education and science[^c957505] such reports and returns and give to him such information as he may require for the purpose of the exercise of his functions under this Act.
