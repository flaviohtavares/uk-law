# Education Act 1944, section 76

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 76. Pupils to be educated in accordance with the wishes of their parents.[^c957338]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

In the exercise and performance of all powers and duties conferred and imposed on them by the Education Acts 1944 to[^c957339] 1996[^c957340] the Secretary of State for Education and Science[^c957341] the funding authorities[^c957342] and local education authorities shall have regard to the general principle that, so far as is compatible with the provision of efficient instruction and training and the avoidance of unreasonable public expenditure, pupils are to be educated in accordance with the wishes of their parents.
