# Education Act 1944, section 83

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 83. Powers of local education authorities as to educational conferences.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c957470] A local education authority may organise, or participate in the organisation of, conferences for the discussion of questions relating to education, and may expend such sums as may be reasonable in paying or contributing towards any expenditure incurred in connection with conferences for the discussion of such questions, including the expenses of any person authorised by them to attend any such conference.
