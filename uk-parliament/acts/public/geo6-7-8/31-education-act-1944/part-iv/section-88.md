# Education Act 1944, section 88

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 88. Appointment of chief education officers of local education authorities.[^c957478]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

The duties of a local education authority with respect to the appointment of officers under the provisions of the [^c957480]Local Government Act, 1972[^c957479], shall, without prejudice to the generality of those provisions, include the duty of appointing a fit person to be the chief education officer of the authority, . . . [^c957481]
