# Education Act 1944, section 63

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 63. Exemption from building byelaws of buildings approved by the Minister.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

(1) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c957146]

(2)[^c957147][^c957148][^c957149] Where plans for or particulars in respect of[^c957150] any building required for the purposes of any school or other educational establishment are approved by the Secretary of State for Education and Science[^c957151], he may by order direct that any provision of any local Act or of any byelaw made under such an Act shall not apply in relation to the building or shall apply in relation thereto with such modifications as may be specified in the order.
