# Education Act 1944, section 68

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 68. †Power of Minister to prevent unreasonable exercise of functions.[^c957195][^c957196][^c957197][^c957198][^c957199][^c957200][^c957201][^c957202][^c957203][^c957193][^c957194]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

If the Secretary of State for Education and Science[^c957204] is satisfied, either on complaint by any person or otherwise, that any local education authority or the . . . [^c957205] governors of any county or voluntary school have acted or are proposing to act unreasonably with respect to the exercise of any power conferred or the performance of any duty imposed by or under this Act, he may, notwithstanding any enactment rendering the exercise of the power or the performance of the duty contingent upon the opinion of the authority or of the . . . [^c957205] governors, give such directions as to the exercise of the power or the performance of the duty as appear to him to be expedient.

In this section, references to a local education authority shall be construed as including references to any body of persons authorised, in accordance with the First Schedule to this Act, . . .[^c957207] [^c957208], to exercise functions of such an authority.
