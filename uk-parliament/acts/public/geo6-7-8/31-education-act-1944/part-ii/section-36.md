# Education Act 1944, section 36

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. Duty of parents to secure the education of their children.[^c956908][^c956907]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

It shall be the duty of the parent of every child of compulsory school age to cause him to receive efficient full-time education suitable to his age, ability, and aptitude and to any special educational needs he may have[^c956909], either by regular attendance at school or otherwise.
