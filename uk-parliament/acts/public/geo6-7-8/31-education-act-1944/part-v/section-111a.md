# Education Act 1944, section 111A

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 111A. Regulations: different provision for Wales.[^c957745][^c957744]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

(1) Regulations under this Act may make in relation to Wales provision different from that made in relation to England.

(2) Subsection (1) above is without prejudice to any express or implied power to make different provision for different cases or circumstances.
