# Education Act 1944, section 118

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 118. Application to Isles of Scilly.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

The Minister shall by order provide for the application of this Act to the Isles of Scilly as if those isles were a separate non-metropolitan county[^c958457], and any such order may provide for the application of this Act to those isles subject to such modifications as may be specified in the order.
