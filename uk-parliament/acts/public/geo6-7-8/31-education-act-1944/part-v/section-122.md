# Education Act 1944, section 122

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 122. Short title and extent.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

(1) This Act may be cited as the Education Act 1944.

(2) This Act shall not extend to Scotland or to Northern Ireland.
