# Education Act 1944, section 113

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 113. Notices.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

Any order, notice or other document[^c957746] required or authorised by this Act to be served upon any person may be served by delivering it to that person, or by leaving it at his usual or last known place of residence, or by sending it in a pre-paid letter addressed to him at that place.
