# Education Act 1944, section 112

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 112. Regulations to be laid before Parliament.

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 1996-11-01).**

All regulations made under this Act shall be laid before Parliament as soon as may be after they are made, and if either House of Parliament, within the period of forty days beginning with the day on which any such regulations are laid before it, resolves that the regulations be annulled, the regulations shall cease to have effect, but without prejudice to anything previously done thereunder or to the making of any new regulations.

In reckoning any such period of forty days, no account shall be taken of any time during which Parliament is dissolved or prorogued or during which both Houses are adjourned for more than four days.
