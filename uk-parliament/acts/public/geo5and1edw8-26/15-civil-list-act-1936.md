## 8. Payments by Treasury in respect of retired allowances.

(1) The Treasury may undertake the payment of any retired allowances granted, on a scale and in accordance with conditions approved by the Treasury, by His late Majesty or by His present Majesty to or in respect of persons who have been members of the Household of His late Majesty or of His present Majesty.

## 12. Adjustments in respect of parts of years.

Where any of the yearly payments, . . . [^c588265], mentioned in this Act fall to be made in respect only of a part of a year, such adjustments of and in relation to those payments . . . [^c588265] shall be made as may in the circumstances of the case appear to the Treasury to be required.
