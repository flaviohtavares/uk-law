> This law has been repealed or revoked. Ended on 1998-11-19.

Government of India Act 1935 ([^c588217]repealed 19.11.1998)

1935 c.2

**No longer in force (ended 1998-11-19).**

## Part I — INTRODUCTORY

### 1. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Government of India Act 1935.

### Interpretation

#### 311. Interpretation, &c.

(1)—(3) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c588221]

(4) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c588222]

(5) Any Act of Parliament containing references to India or any part thereof, to countries other than or situate outside India or other than or situate outside British India, to His Majesty’s dominions, to a British possession, to the Secretary of State in Council, to the Governor-General in Council, to a Governor in Council or to Legislatures, courts, or authorities in, or to matters relating to the government or administration of, India or British India shall have effect subject to such adaptations and modifications as His Majesty in Council may direct, being adaptations and modifications which appear to His Majesty in Council to be necessary or expedient in consequence of the provisions of this Act or the [^c588223]Government of Burma Act 1935.

Any power of any legislature under this Act to repeal or amend any Act adapted or modified by an Order in Council under this subsection shall extend to the repeal or amendment of that Order, and any reference in this Act to an Act of Parliament shall be construed as including a reference to any such Order.
