## 10. Borrowing powers of port health authorities and joint boards.[^c611307]

*Extent: E+W*

A . . . [^c611308] joint board constituted under this Part of this Act shall, subject to the provisions of the order by which they were constituted, have the like powers of borrowing for the purposes of their functions under the order as a local authority have of borrowing for the purposes of their functions under this Act.
