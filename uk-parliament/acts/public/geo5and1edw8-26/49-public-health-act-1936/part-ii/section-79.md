# Public Health Act 1936, section 79

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo5and1Edw8/26/49
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 79. \[Power to require removal of noxious matter by occupier of premises in urban district.[^c-305f389a2f6a38878e74b184cf391969][^c-c7ef330912e4c76f8e1cd5f859cdf1aa][^c-ec4d0036aafe7c4596ccce2ce6ac59ed][^c611486][^c611487]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If in a borough or district,[^c611488] or in a rural district or contributory place in which section forty-nine of the [^c611489]Public Health Act 1875, was in force immediately before the commencement of this Act, it appears to the proper officer of the authority[^c611490] that any accumulation of noxious matter ought to be removed, he shall serve notice on the owner thereof, or on the occupier of the premises on which it is found, requiring him to remove it, and, if the notice is not complied with within twenty-four hours after service thereof, the inspector may remove the matter referred to.

(2) A local authority may recover the expenses of any action reasonably taken by their inspector under the preceding sub-section from the owner or occupier in default.\]
