## 33. Application of 27 & 28 Vict. c. 114 to works for supply of sewage.

*Extent: E+W*

Works for the supply of sewage to land for agricultural purposes shall be deemed to be an improvement of land authorised by the [^c611405]Improvement of Land Act 1864 and the provisions of that Act shall apply accordingly.
