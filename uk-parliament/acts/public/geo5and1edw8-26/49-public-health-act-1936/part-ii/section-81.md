## 81. Byelaws for the prevention of certain nuisances.[^key-d0640c1ec96ca57f579bf849297d023f][^key-9c3d61ad9747ecd1d27651025cee71db][^key-b57ed8f3b692c5bb92848b2f7e644423][^key-57545d88981984a1dd16da5c03b0efe4][^key-14d78d43b9d11cf1fb6b4c963525fb43][^key-46c1d8ad956b8f7fe640b529c101dca4][^key-2f4e231cdbd3ae325376be575cdbf8f9][^c611496]

*Extent: E+W*

A local authority may make byelaws for preventing—

- (a) the occurrence of nuisances from snow, filth, dust, ashes and rubbish;
- (b) the keeping of animals so as to be prejudicial to health.
