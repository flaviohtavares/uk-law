## 229. Power of statutory undertakers to supply water, gas or electricity to baths, &c., on favourable terms.

*Extent: E+W*

Any statutory undertakers supplying water, [^c611643]. . . or electricity may supply water, [^c611643]. . . or electricity to any public baths, washhouse, swimming bath or bathing place, either without charge or on such other favourable terms as they think fit.
