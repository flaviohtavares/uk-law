# Public Health Act 1936, section 324

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo5and1Edw8/26/49

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 324. Provisions as to exercise by Minister of functions of body in default.[^c611879][^c611880][^c611881][^c611878]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where under the last but one preceding section the Minister has by order transferred to himself any functions of a council . . . [^c611882] or joint board, any expenses incurred by him in discharging the said functions shall be paid in the first instance out of moneys provided by Parliament, but the amount of those expenses as certified by the Minister shall on demand be paid to him by the body in default, and shall be recoverable by him from them as a debt due to the Crown, and that body shall have the like power of raising the money required as they have of raising money for defraying expenses incurred directly by them.

(2) The payment of any such expenses as aforesaid shall, to such extent as may be sanctioned by the Minister, be a purpose for which a local authority . . . [^c611882] or joint board may borrow money in accordance with the statutory provisions relating to borrowing by such an authority or board.
