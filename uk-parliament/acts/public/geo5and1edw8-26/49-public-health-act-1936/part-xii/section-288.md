# Public Health Act 1936, section 288

## 288. Penalty for obstructing execution of Act.[^c611778][^c611779][^key-9ea787d5e4411c91e34953be6d376c16][^c611777]

*Extent: E+W*

A person who wilfully obstructs any person acting in the execution of this Act or of any byelaw building regulation[^c611780], order or warrant made or issued thereunder shall, in any case for which no other provision is made by this Act, be liable to a fine not exceeding £10 for a first offence and £20 for a second or subsequent offence[^c611781].
