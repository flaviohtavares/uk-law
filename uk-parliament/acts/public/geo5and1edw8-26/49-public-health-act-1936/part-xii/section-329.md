## 329. †Saving for certain provisions of the Land Charges Act, 1925.[^c611908]

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in this Act with respect to the recovery of expenses from owners of premises affects the provisions of the [^c611910]Local Land Charges Act 1975[^c611909].
