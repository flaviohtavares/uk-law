## 305. Protection of members and officers of certain local authorities from personal liability[^c611836][^c611835]

*Extent: E+W*

Section two hundred and sixty-five of the [^c611837]Public Health Act 1875, (which relates to the protection of members and officers of certain authorities) shall apply to local authorities, joint boards and port health authorities under this Act as if any reference in that section to the said Act of 1875 were a reference to this Act.
