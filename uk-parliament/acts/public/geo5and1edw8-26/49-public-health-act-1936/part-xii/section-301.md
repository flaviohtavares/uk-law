# Public Health Act 1936, section 301

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo5and1Edw8/26/49
>

## 301. Appeals to quarter sessions against decisions of justices.[^c611824][^c611825][^c611826][^c-cbf256d5ac1d7055fef3f56dec0eb8bf][^c611823]

*Extent: E+W*

Subject as hereinafter provided, where a person aggrieved by any order, determination or other decision of a court of summary jurisdiction under this Act is not by any other enactment authorised to appeal to the Crown Court[^c611827] he may appeal to such a court:

Provided that nothing in this section shall be construed as conferring a right of appeal from the decision of a court of summary jurisdiction in any case if each of the parties concerned might under this Act have required that the dispute should be determined by arbitration instead of by such a court.
