## 282. Application of 38 & 39 Vict. c. 55 s. 153.

*Extent: E+W*

Section one hundred and fifty-three of the [^c611743]Public Health Act 1875 (which relates to the power to require gas and water pipes to be moved), shall apply for the purposes of this Act as it applies for the purposes of that Act.
