# Public Health Act 1936, section 317

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo5and1Edw8/26/49

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 317. Amendment of 38 & 39 Vict. c. 55. s. 303.[^c611863]

*Extent: E+W*

In section three hundred and three of the [^c611864]Public Health Act 1875 (which relates to the power of the Minister to repeal and alter local Acts by means of provisional orders), the reference to any local Act which relates to the same subject-matters as that Act shall be construed as including a reference to any local Act which relates to the same subject-matters as this Act.
