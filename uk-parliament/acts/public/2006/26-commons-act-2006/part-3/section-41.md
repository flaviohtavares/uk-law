# Commons Act 2006, section 41

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 41. Enforcement[^c1912166][^c1968672][^c-62c4ab2afdd5fb165bd1700a97d441bc][^c-4b03453e03c527d1ed1366e23a5f87cc][^c-d199c4c6da03df078ae960db7f4e5a8c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where any works are carried out on land to which section 38 applies in contravention of subsection (1) of that section, any person may apply to the county court [^c-276e791881f8a676a06777224396441f]....

(2) On an application under this section the court may make an order—

- (a) in any case, for removal of the works and restoration of the land to the condition it was in before the works were carried out;
- (b) in a case where consent has been given under section 38(1) but the works have not been carried out in accordance with any term of that consent, for the works to be carried out in such manner and subject to such conditions as the order may specify.
