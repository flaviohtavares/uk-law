# Commons Act 2006, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 42. Schemes[^c1968281]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-a3c84cf035ef069ef6d7f3282e1b7c96] This section applies in relation to works on relevant land where, by virtue of section 38(1), the works may not be carried out without the consent of the appropriate national authority.

(2)[^key-a3c84cf035ef069ef6d7f3282e1b7c96] In subsection (1) “**relevant land**” means land which is subject to—

- (a) a scheme under the Metropolitan Commons Act 1866 (c. 122) which is in force at the commencement of this section; or
- (b) a scheme under the Commons Act 1899 (c. 30) which is in force at the commencement of this section.

(3)[^key-a3c84cf035ef069ef6d7f3282e1b7c96] Where—

- (a) any provision of the scheme referred to in subsection (2) would also prohibit the carrying out of the works, and
- (b) the scheme does not allow for any person to consent to the works to be carried out,

the works do not contravene that provision if they are carried out with (and in accordance with the terms of) the consent of the appropriate national authority under section 38(1) and of any owner of the land (if not the person carrying out the works).

(4)[^key-07018eb6a8b31ad2f0a2011078edb656] Regulations may make provision as to the procedure to be followed in obtaining the consent of an owner under subsection (3) (and may include provision for the consent of an owner to be regarded as having been given where he has not objected within a period of time specified in the regulations).

(5)[^key-a3c84cf035ef069ef6d7f3282e1b7c96] Where any provision of the scheme referred to in subsection (2) would also prohibit the carrying out of the works without the consent of the appropriate national authority—

- (a) consent given under section 38(1) is to be regarded as consent given under the scheme; and
- (b) consent may not be sought separately under the scheme.
