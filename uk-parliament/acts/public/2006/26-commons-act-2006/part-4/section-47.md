# Commons Act 2006, section 47

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 47. Approvement[^c1912178]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Commons Act 1285 (13 Edw 1 c. 46) (power of approvement) shall cease to have effect.

(2) Any power of approvement of a common which subsists at common law is abolished.
