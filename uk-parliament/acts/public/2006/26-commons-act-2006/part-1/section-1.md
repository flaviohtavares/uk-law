# Commons Act 2006, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Registers of common land and greens[^key-54dfee4f58942cd88351b19f7c40f49f][^key-bc18e37391b7075c551067b99ee9e7d5]

*Extent: E+W*

Each commons registration authority shall continue to keep—

- (a) a register known as a register of common land; and
- (b) a register known as a register of town or village greens.
