# Commons Act 2006, section 21

## 21. Official copies[^c1968241][^c-58c8692c24f23cd9a647a0fe4e2ad536][^c-da7480f2db02bc1a2874745d76206dec]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-c04fa355bf52cc501bba79172e700d99] An official copy of, or of any part of—

- (a) a register of common land or town or village greens,
- (b) any document kept by a commons registration authority which is referred to in such a register, or
- (c) any other document kept by a commons registration authority which relates to an application made at any time in relation to such a register,

is admissible in evidence to the same extent as the original.

(2)[^c-929412a5838cb885bb039e5544144b31] Regulations may make provision for the issue of official copies and may in particular make provision about—

- (a) the form of official copies;
- (b) who may issue official copies;
- (c) applications for official copies;
- (d) the conditions to be met by applicants for official copies.

(3)[^c-929412a5838cb885bb039e5544144b31] Conditions under subsection (2)(d) may include conditions requiring the payment of a fee (which may be a fee determined by a commons registration authority).
