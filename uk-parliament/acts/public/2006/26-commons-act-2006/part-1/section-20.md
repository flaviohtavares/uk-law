# Commons Act 2006, section 20

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. Inspection[^c1968240][^c-c535427d484fffffd10cca7bf5def706][^c-2f4aad6b939fea80be5c1bac8efdfd4a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-9eca5be5901fda3aac878b133b1040c9] Any person may inspect and make copies of, or of any part of—

- (a) a register of common land or town or village greens;
- (b) any document kept by a commons registration authority which is referred to in such a register;
- (c) any other document kept by a commons registration authority which relates to an application made at any time in relation to such a register.

(2)[^c-92127b7d2c6f766fdeb090d77597804c] The right in subsection (1) is subject to regulations which may, in particular—

- (a) provide for exceptions to the right;
- (b) impose conditions on its exercise.

(3)[^c-92127b7d2c6f766fdeb090d77597804c] Conditions under subsection (2)(b) may include conditions requiring the payment of a fee (which may be a fee determined by a commons registration authority).
