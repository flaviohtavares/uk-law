# Commons Act 2006, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23. Transitional[^c1912189][^c-980a1287cfd009b7d64e87a36b7eb18c][^c-ba69dea671995dd9ac0e50b0aee4347d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Schedule 3 (transitional provision) has effect.

(2) Nothing in Schedule 3 affects the power to make transitional provision and savings in an order under section 56; and an order under that section may modify any provision made by that Schedule.
