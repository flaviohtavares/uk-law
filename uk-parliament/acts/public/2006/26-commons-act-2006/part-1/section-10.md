# Commons Act 2006, section 10

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. Attachment[^key-79685af62b15db90b9859b35d802965c][^key-d13741a150c99fde554664d1dd0b366e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies to any right of common which is registered in a register of common land or town or village greens but is not registered as attached to any land.

(2) The owner of the right may apply to the commons registration authority for the right to be registered in that register as attached to any land, provided that—

- (a) he is entitled to occupy the land; or
- (b) the person entitled to occupy the land has consented to the application.
