> Extent: England, Wales.

## 56. Commencement

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c1917288] The preceding provisions of this Act, except section 9 and Schedule 1 and sections 54 and 55, come into force in accordance with provision made by order by the appropriate national authority.

(2) Sections 54 and 55 come into force at the end of the period of two months beginning with the day on which this Act is passed.
