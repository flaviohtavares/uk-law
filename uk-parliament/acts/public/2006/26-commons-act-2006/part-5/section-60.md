# Commons Act 2006, section 60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-03-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 60. Crown application

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This Act (and any provision made under it) binds the Crown.

(2) This section does not impose criminal liability on the Crown in relation to an offence under section 34(1).

(3) Subsection (2) does not affect the criminal liability of persons in the service of the Crown.
