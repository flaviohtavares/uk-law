> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (5).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/48

## 10. Police bail[^c-4c909c453b7e0923f844b8509abbb598]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 6, which amends provisions in the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (c. 60) that relate to bail—

- (a) granted by a constable elsewhere than at a police station, or
- (b) granted at a police station,

has effect.
