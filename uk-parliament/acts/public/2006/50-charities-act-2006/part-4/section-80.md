# Charities Act 2006, section 80

> Text as at 2023-02-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Short title and extent

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Charities Act 2006.

(2) Subject to subsections (3) to (7), this Act extends to England and Wales only.

(3) The following provisions extend also to Scotland—

- (a)[^key-b220f8f9de48be076635801868927178] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^key-b220f8f9de48be076635801868927178] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) sections 72 and 74,
- (d)[^key-b220f8f9de48be076635801868927178] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . and
- (e) section 75(4) and (5), sections 77[^key-2787afe641a7b1801920bcd485d6eee1] to 79 and this section.

(4)[^key-b16a3567c5493f746691646cac915575] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) The following provisions extend also to Northern Ireland—

- (a)[^key-da73c481899c78c922851f3b906c53b6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^key-da73c481899c78c922851f3b906c53b6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^key-da73c481899c78c922851f3b906c53b6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) sections 72 and 74,
- (e)[^key-da73c481899c78c922851f3b906c53b6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . and
- (f) section 75(4) and (5), sections 77[^key-f1e9134b235b953a9c6447b09bead49e] to 79 and this section.

(6)[^key-dad48e91aad2aadb728a273258c52624] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) Any amendment, repeal or revocation made by this Act has the same extent as the enactment to which it relates.

(8)[^key-816de20e85ed1706fb3f80357c509984] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9) Subsection (7) [^key-20e4481e5c2377931256450a64d9f84b]... does not apply to—

- (a)[^key-9a135afc0c5b219dc6f4076a180a7928] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the amendments[^key-68ef888004b25510a9eaad39cb68ff7f] made by Schedule 8 in the Police, Factories, &c. (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1916 (c. 31), or
- (c) the repeal made in that Act by Schedule 9,

which extend to England and Wales only.
