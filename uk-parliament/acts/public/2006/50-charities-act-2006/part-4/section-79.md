# Charities Act 2006, section 79

> Text as at 2023-02-07.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Commencement[^c-cdc81290ca6459fda283c5bccf50d374]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following provisions come into force on the day on which this Act is passed—

- (a)[^c-8a519d58df7cee8a336c8461bdb015fc] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) section 74,
- (c) section 75(4) and (5),
- (d) section 78,
- (e) section 77,
- (f) this section and section 80, and
- (g) the following provisions of Schedule 8—
  - paragraph 90(2),
  - [^c-813c1a07e4315a9ac702cf5a06555cef]...

  and section 75(1) so far as relating to those provisions.

(2) Otherwise, this Act comes into force on such day as the Secretary of State[^c-d1354a43f988c5d1d12f9005989de3b4] may by order appoint.

(3) An order under subsection (2)—

- (a) may appoint different days for different purposes or different areas;
- (b) make such provision as the Secretary of State[^c-d8a4f44f68c28e59501ae5f0b3a07ceb] considers necessary or expedient for transitory, transitional or saving purposes in connection with the coming into force of any provision of this Act.
