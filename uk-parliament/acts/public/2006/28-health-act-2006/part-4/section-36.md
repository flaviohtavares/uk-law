# Health Act 2006, section 36

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 36. Arrangements for dispensing of medicines

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) In section 43 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4511b6a2d9d73afdc7f73adb27b73729] 1977 Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4511b6a2d9d73afdc7f73adb27b73729] (persons authorised to provide pharmaceutical services), for subsection (2) substitute— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4511b6a2d9d73afdc7f73adb27b73729]

> (2) Except as may be provided for by or under regulations, no arrangements for the dispensing of medicines shall be made under this Part of this Act with persons other than persons who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4511b6a2d9d73afdc7f73adb27b73729]
>
> - (a) are registered pharmacists or persons lawfully conducting a retail pharmacy business in accordance with section 69 of the Medicines Act 1968; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4511b6a2d9d73afdc7f73adb27b73729]
> - (b) undertake that all medicines supplied by them under the arrangements will be dispensed either by or under the supervision of a registered pharmacist. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-4511b6a2d9d73afdc7f73adb27b73729]

(2) In section 17S of the National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978 (c. 29) (eligibility to be contractor under pharmaceutical care services contract), after subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) In such circumstances, and subject to such conditions, as may be prescribed, subsection (1) has effect with the omission of the words from “who undertakes” to the end.
