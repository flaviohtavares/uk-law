## 4. Additional smoke-free places[^c16418371][^c-49bca0a5bb9ba9b66388948c4f07bb4b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State[^c-0e6b1aa96975551c26ed567e61dcbd94] may make regulations designating as smoke-free any place in England[^c-77fe3c07a3ffa91b66e2539c57ad6b63] or description of place in England that is not smoke-free under section 2.

(2) The place, or places falling within the description, need not be enclosed or substantially enclosed.

(3) The Secretary of State[^c-26dee74e98db7c48a07d65b3bebe9c48] may designate a place or description of place under this section only if in the Secretary of State's[^c-1ac908935b91e41a7bb7597337f40012] opinion there is a significant risk that, without a designation, persons present there would be exposed to significant quantities of smoke.

(4) The regulations may provide for such places, or places falling within the description, to be smoke-free only—

- (a) in specified circumstances,
- (b) at specified times,
- (c) if specified conditions are satisfied,
- (d) in specified areas,

or any combination of those.
