# Health Act 2006, section 81

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (41).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/28
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Expenses

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenditure incurred by the Secretary of State by virtue of this Act;
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable out of money so provided under any other enactment.
