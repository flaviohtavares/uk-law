## 78. Penalties for offences: transitional modification for England and Wales[^key-64a904304af68cdaed24a2bdb8b279b5][^key-a8ac1f3629d83955edbcc5302574f859][^key-95f956b720913ebf3d91d90928f181bf][^key-e8b31a10383610fe29a456bb139c8662][^key-e3dd41eb81f9f05d7eee72a475316c76]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section contains transitional modifications in respect of penalties for certain offences committed in England and Wales.

(2) In relation to an offence committed before 2 May 2022[^key-9520b5d6add46e3b00aad03153ec6bf7], the references in section 21(2)(b)[^key-c120a7ca66b8954175b8bab7fe9e617b] of this Act to periods of imprisonment of the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-30d124d496a113fc336bc43d7bf7c9d4] are to be read as references to periods of imprisonment of 6 months.
