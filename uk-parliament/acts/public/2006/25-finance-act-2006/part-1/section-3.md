## 3. Rate of duty on beer

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 36(1AA)(a) of ALDA 1979 (rate of duty on beer) for “£12.92” substitute “ £13.26 ”.

(2) This section shall be deemed to have come into force at midnight on 26th March 2006.
