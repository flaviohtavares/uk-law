# London Olympic Games and Paralympic Games Act 2006, section 28

> Text as at 2014-12-02.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Enforcement: power of entry[^c-cb7ce020d43b7f60f95bf0ade664774e][^c-8094366cf57d1e6190cc96e193790115]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A constable [^c-ceee8cf8e0d83c4e3e1edcfeada92264]... may—

- (a) enter land or premises on which they reasonably believe a contravention of regulations under section 25 is occurring;
- (b) remove any infringing article;
- (c) when entering land under paragraph (a), be accompanied by one or more persons for the purpose of taking action under paragraph (b);
- (d) use, or authorise the use of, reasonable force for the purpose of taking action under this subsection.

(2) The power to remove an article may be exercised only if the constable [^c-7a56946cf5561185cb00facc3de5606a]... thinks it necessary for the purpose of—

- (a) ending the contravention of regulations under section 25,
- (b) preventing a future contravention of the regulations, or *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^c-1dd6aa1c614cc792aaffc2d552766a83]
- (c) enabling the article to be used as evidence in proceedings for an offence under section 27, or *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0c196ce6c3614f9f05880c60edcb63]
- (d) enabling the article to be forfeited in accordance with section 143 of the Powers of Criminal Courts (Sentencing) Act 2000 (c. 6). *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-dc0c196ce6c3614f9f05880c60edcb63]

(3) An article removed shall be returned when retention is no longer justified by a matter specified in subsection (2)(a) to (d); but this subsection does not apply to perishable articles which have ceased to be usable for trade. *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c6bfeeed9eca97145c30982409527959]

(4)[^c-be6fb66ad528b60313821d4756917b1a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) Having exercised a power under this section a constable [^c-03734df031bcbe86164069d4651912c6]...—

- (a) shall take reasonable steps to leave the land or premises secure, and
- (b) shall comply with any provision of regulations under section 25 about informing specified persons of what the constable [^c-03734df031bcbe86164069d4651912c6]... has done.

(6) Regulations under section 25 shall include provision enabling a person whose property is damaged in the course of the exercise or purported exercise of a power under this section (other than a person responsible for a contravention of the regulations) to obtain compensation from a local policing body[^c-af878964be6c2b5369da383b99a35350] or a police authority[^c-edba7226da65cd1273e31a00ae65f863]; and the regulations may, in particular, include provision—

- (a) conferring jurisdiction on a court or tribunal;
- (b) about appeals.

(7) A local policing body[^c-061be0748d4fa80b91dfc56eeafed92b] or a police authority may recover from a person responsible for the contravention of regulations under section 25, as if it were a debt, the reasonable costs of taking action under this section.

(8) In this section—

- [^c-26f068c2593b727dcc820217afd1a00c]...
- “**infringing article**” means—
  - (a) an article or animal *(Extent: E+W+S)*[^c-0b5390d5df97176b064773339265d49d] that is being offered for trade in contravention of regulations under section 25 or is otherwise being used in connection with a contravention of the regulations, and
  - (b) anything (other than a vehicle) containing an article or animal *(Extent: E+W+S)* to which paragraph (a) applies.
