# Electoral Administration Act 2006, section 47

> Text as at 2025-07-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 47. Miscellaneous amendments of the 1983 Act[^c18924391]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Part 5 of Schedule 1 (which contains miscellaneous amendments of the 1983 Act relating to the conduct of elections) has effect.
