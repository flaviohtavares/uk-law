## 77. Commencement

(2)[^c19307161][^c19702431][^c19702961][^c20067971][^c20068161][^c20191161] Otherwise, this Act comes into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order made by statutory instrument appoint.

(3) Different days may be appointed for different purposes.

(4) An order under subsection (2) may make—

- (a) any supplementary, incidental or consequential provision, and
- (b) any transitory, transitional or saving provision,

as the Secretary of State considers necessary or expedient in connection with the order.
