# National Health Service Act 2006, section 187

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (147).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 187. Charges for designated services or facilities

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations may provide for the making and recovery of charges in respect of services or facilities of a kind mentioned in[^c-ddbb462d003e0bf89152111e2aa6ecd1] section 3(1)(e) or (f)[^c-ed59e62a0ee92f4357bfe6bd99c231d4] (whether provided in pursuance of those provisions or any other provision of this Act).
