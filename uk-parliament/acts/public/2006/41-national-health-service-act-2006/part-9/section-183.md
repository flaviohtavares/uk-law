## 183. Payment of travelling expenses

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations may provide in relation to prescribed descriptions of persons—

- (a)[^c-e4ff7c9215c5a7cf0a9ad10f9a8c6a11] for the payment by the Secretary of State ,[^c-61f878222cf04710bf021d84a7245f42] NHS England[^c-c26379f595629655ada8a57e0d3d2bfd], an integrated care board[^c-12fed841c71563d07a17da9828efd5d8], [^c-57854dd06935cd427bb3f0fd2e62338a]... an NHS trust or an NHS foundation trust, in such cases as may be prescribed, of travelling expenses (including the travelling expenses of a companion) incurred or to be incurred for the purpose of their obtaining—
  - (i) any services provided under this Act,
  - (ii)[^c-df3bb0f3c87f8420d39e663d714df811] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (iii)[^c-b02ccc0872345672b413c1a59510c567] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) for the reimbursement by NHS England[^c-67a0c6f93d770374b9dbe59853ea0db5], [^c-3c241b715e2662826a4d5967fb3886c0]... or[^c-484a98cfb01438045c9dcad098293df4] an integrated care board[^c-f6cdaf7c2ee27f9100f91f3936e4734c] to an NHS trust or an NHS foundation trust [^c-e967d4c96aae6464e2d163b9f62c2329]... of such payments,
- (c) for the reimbursement by NHS England[^c-3f70699f03782bef2e8595711c833dcd], [^c-557bfd5e4ca7109c916662dcb49ea535]... or[^c-d0e0b33f5b669471c42d13c362de8302] an integrated care board[^c-87fbdb4e57e1b2bfcfb6124c79c9624b] to an NHS trust and, in such cases as may be prescribed, to a Local Health Board, of payments made by virtue of section 131(a) of the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006 (c. 42).
