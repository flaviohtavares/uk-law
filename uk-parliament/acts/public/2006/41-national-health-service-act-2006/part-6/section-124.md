# National Health Service Act 2006, section 124

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (147).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/41

## 124. Assistance and support: primary ophthalmic services

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Board[^c-d430093f82c5da21e80d654fa38d97b9] may provide assistance or support to any person providing or proposing to provide primary ophthalmic services under a general ophthalmic services contract or primary ophthalmic services that fall within section 115(4)[^c-6978cda6e16646817f987f0f0a839b1d].

(2) Assistance or support provided by the Board[^c-6f2be74136392222432b28b424916892] under subsection (1) is provided on such terms, including terms as to payment, as the Board[^c-577261cb91fd872cc110a951dee437e2] considers appropriate.

(3) “**Assistance**” includes financial assistance.
