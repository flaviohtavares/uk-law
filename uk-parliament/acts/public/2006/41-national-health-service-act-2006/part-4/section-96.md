# National Health Service Act 2006, section 96.

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (147).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 96.. Assistance and support: primary medical services

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Board[^c-2c596b3984e10064c6528255c7331109] may provide assistance or support to any person providing or proposing to provide—

- (za)[^c-96afc90fc37560fe214e30236e3ecd81] primary medical services pursuant to section 83(2),
- (a) primary medical services under a general medical services contract, or
- (b) primary medical services in accordance with section 92 arrangements.

(2) Assistance or support provided by the Board[^c-fbbd48acdac09eda9b4f73800fd51547] under subsection (1) is provided on such terms, including terms as to payment, as the Board[^c-4ee3b9cd13bdd7af1f89ec43e1c8deb1] considers appropriate.

(3) “**Assistance**” includes financial assistance.
