## 84.. General medical services contracts: introductory

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Board[^c-566da53f3713e2baf83b4df35399c4df] may enter into a contract under which primary medical services are provided in accordance with the following provisions of this Part.

(2) A contract under this section is called in this Act a “general medical services contract”.

(3) A general medical services contract may make such provision as may be agreed between the Board[^c-590ae1ae31eee6f6db2dfc5390fdbc9f] and the contractor or contractors in relation to—

- (a) the services to be provided under the contract,
- (b) remuneration under the contract, and
- (c) any other matters.

(4) The services to be provided under a general medical services contract may include—

- (a) services which are not primary medical services,
- (b)[^c-decddaaadb5897737dcf7524b0d448a9] services to be performed outside England.

(5) In this Part, “**contractor**”, in relation to a general medical services contract, means any person entering into the contract with the Board[^c-7a691f8a306b269f89e3a4c39e0ccfd4].
