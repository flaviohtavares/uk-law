# National Health Service Act 2006, section 13G

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (147).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/41

## 13G. Duty as to reducing inequalities[^c-c17c605fbd7569f0644650e6ed66435c]

*Extent: E+W*

NHS England[^c-c26379f595629655ada8a57e0d3d2bfd] must, in the exercise of its functions, have regard to the need to—

- (a) reduce inequalities between persons[^c-9dbf64ae9808f5a92ea5b4cfc1acf729] with respect to their ability to access health services, and
- (b) reduce inequalities between patients with respect to the outcomes achieved for them by the provision of health services (including the outcomes described in section 13E(3))[^c-8f9263d0a24d6e229eecd71a04b67be5].
