# National Health Service Act 2006, section 13L

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (147).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/41

## 13L. Duty in respect of research[^key-c17c605fbd7569f0644650e6ed66435c]

*Extent: E+W*

NHS England[^key-c26379f595629655ada8a57e0d3d2bfd] must, in the exercise of its functions, facilitate or otherwise[^key-13079d7bb5c45cfe9060d3e22a08329b] promote—

- (a) research on matters relevant to the health service, and
- (b) the use in the health service of evidence obtained from research.
