## 73. Directions and regulations under this Part

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies to directions and regulations under any of—

- (a) section 7,
- (aa)[^c-cd64de778e1a2d0811303a8b22833860] section 7B,
- (ab)[^c-a7ee45b5062ffc2e28ef93153223216b] section 7C,
- (b) section 8,
- (ba)[^c-daee82167eb11112090613ab47e6435b] section 13YB,
- (bb)[^c-8fa8f4c0906ae7984aa16b3501d04c29] section 27B,
- (c)[^c-4a0ca27b7f57a2a54268f3466c1ea765] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c-4a0ca27b7f57a2a54268f3466c1ea765] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (e)[^c-4a0ca27b7f57a2a54268f3466c1ea765] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (f)[^c-4a0ca27b7f57a2a54268f3466c1ea765] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (g) section 29.

(2) Except in prescribed cases, the directions and regulations must not preclude a person or body by whom the function is exercisable apart from the directions or regulations from exercising the function.
