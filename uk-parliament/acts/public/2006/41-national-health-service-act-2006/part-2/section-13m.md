## 13M. Duty as to promoting education and training[^key-c17c605fbd7569f0644650e6ed66435c]

*Extent: E+W*

NHS England[^key-c26379f595629655ada8a57e0d3d2bfd] must, in exercising its functions other than its education and training functions[^key-5d5dd9e2dcfebf56c74893d9949a122c], have regard to the need to promote education and training for the persons mentioned in section 1F(1) so as to assist [^key-94ec61ae97742cfb2d2c2b4af082a1d5]... in the discharge of the duty under that section.
