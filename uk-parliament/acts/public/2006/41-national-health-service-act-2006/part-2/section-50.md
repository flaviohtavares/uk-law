## 50. Fees[^key-aa544180a5f7723c5ae2af2dc7701463]

*Extent: E+W*

An NHS foundation trust must pay to NHS England[^key-3c875ce8549d0bdb84a6328e80cb2c32] such fee as NHS England may determine in respect of its exercise of functions under—

- (a) section 39;
- (b) section 39A.
