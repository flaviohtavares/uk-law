## 72. Co-operation between NHS bodies

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-8030aff55c3d4f317023ef903afff49f] It is the duty of NHS bodies to co-operate with each other in exercising their functions.

(1A)[^key-08491a49fa51626af6bfb22c26e38f48] The Secretary of State may publish guidance on the discharge of the duty under subsection (1) in relation to England.

(1B) An NHS body other than a Welsh NHS body must have regard to any guidance published under subsection (1A).

(2)[^key-26c180233e8eda66a3a00f60c3a17770] For the purposes of this section, NICE is an NHS body.

(3)[^M_F_77afc991-32b2-41ea-dda9-f9ef1e904e9c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-5d5f76d716ca8cc53ecf7bd2c74c6d3c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-b8dd3a46338ee190fd4af166b75d908b] In this section “**Welsh NHS body**” means—

- (a) an NHS trust established under the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006,
- (b) a Special Health Authority established under that Act, or
- (c) a Local Health Board.
