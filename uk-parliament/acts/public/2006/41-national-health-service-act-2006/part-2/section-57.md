## 57. Sections 56 to 56B: supplementary[^c-4938cd78e4ae1d19b7a1821803d9165e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where an application is granted under section 56 or 56B[^c-1acb12af64f539975cdcac3c2ed91dc7], NHS England[^c-f1dc0ed49da6e9cc149cd7029f7a37f2] must specify the property and liabilities to be transferred to the new NHS foundation trust or trusts[^c-d5c4af44fd5a57d43c5d3ee23759b348].

(2) Where such an application is granted,[^c-b4cbd1aebab32f66562105e50681ff37] NHS England[^c-32657c497f5ff29e1ce4107722f6abc5] must make an order—

- (a) dissolving the trust or[^c-efb94110a6b1bd8bb8e843b0563fd891] trusts in question, and
- (b) transferring, or providing for the transfer of, the property and liabilities specified by NHS England to the new NHS foundation trust or trusts[^c-c9e9122952f6c5a33f93c7d3d02e293a].

(2A)[^c-c951d8556d293adb8147c69bac444e06] An order under section 56 or 56B is conclusive evidence of incorporation and conclusive evidence that the corporation is an NHS foundation trust.

(3) The order may—

- (a) transfer, or provide for the transfer of, any of the remaining property or liabilities to another[^c-9b9d09e9cb4e36455f8323718c00f77e] NHS foundation trust, an NHS trust established under section 25 or the Secretary of State,
- (b)[^c-e888b018739aa8ca882686d8b00c9da3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3A)[^c-24a208cb4e23eb3a184dd3f9cc97bc1c] The order may include provision for the transfer of employees of the trust or trusts dissolved by the order.

(4) In sections 56(2) and 56B(3)[^c-33a0dbd5dffe8a71314c5e7c233ba0d0], and subsections (1) and (2) of this section, “**liabilities**” includes criminal liabilities; and an order under subsection (3) of this section may transfer any remaining criminal liabilities to another[^c-e1ec8af0082459320b52b8fe248e6e89] NHS foundation trust or an NHS trust established under section 25.

(5) Where one of the parties to an application under section 56 or 56A[^c-dd115be5d8c2745c4bdac17c63b60f35] is an NHS trust, the powers conferred on the Secretary of State or NHS England[^c-aec72d27488f1a80f0912c1a60cc5f99] by Part 3 of Schedule 4 are not exercisable in relation to the trust.

(6)[^c-a916bb5246a225cdb435f64d72d1ce07] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
