## 35. Authorisation of NHS foundation trusts

*Extent: E+W*

(1) NHS England[^c-fcbf3a0b1c633b13b2d4346aaf52e1b4] may give an authorisation under this section—

- (a) to an NHS trust which has applied under section 33, [^c-dd5e7b86bd8e5712f88d1fee3bbeb1c1]...
- (b)[^c-dd5e7b86bd8e5712f88d1fee3bbeb1c1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

if the Secretary of State approves the authorisation and[^c-2859e5ce601d58faacff8d233452914e] NHS England[^c-88471760d690ad38b60ed89dc8a47b98] is satisfied as to the following matters.

(2) The matters are that—

- (a) the applicant's constitution will be in accordance with Schedule 7 and will otherwise be appropriate,
- (b) the applicant has taken steps to secure that (taken as a whole) the actual membership of any public constituency, and (if there is one) of the patients' constituency, will be representative of those eligible for such membership,
- (c) there will be a council of governors[^c-98b89f5a3ab9b004893c4739ee4c8aae], and a board of directors, constituted in accordance with the constitution,
- (d) the steps necessary to prepare for NHS foundation trust status have been taken,
- (e)[^c-fbdaad96b36abb0c6da02b991cb4183b] the applicant will be able to provide goods and services for the purposes of the health service in England,
- (f) any other requirements which NHS England[^c-8e9d0fc1e843ae0f9f728e42738e2e8f] considers appropriate are met.

(3) In deciding whether it is satisfied as to the matters referred to in subsection (2)(e), NHS England[^c-d6702cd7314ff7a16a9303f3a04f7224] must consider (among other things)—

- (a) any report or recommendation in respect of the applicant made by the Care Quality Commission[^c-16ef9d4aeb9a3764e813d04e36f2a7b5],
- (b) the financial position of the applicant.

(4)[^c-f62711c6857cffe685f564a1a5e89df0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) NHS England[^c-75bffd3b43e609c7d6461dfc8e604fb5] must not give an authorisation unless it is satisfied that the applicant has sought the views about the application of the following—

- (a)[^c-771d76e1cabfb4d7838f743cdfae6900] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) individuals who live in any area specified in the proposed constitution as the area for a public constituency,
- (c) any local authority that would be authorised by the proposed constitution to appoint a member of the council of governors[^c-4ac914e2298c74a1425c0d12923663ff],
- (d) if the proposed constitution provides for a patients' constituency, individuals who would be able to apply to become members of that constituency,
- (e) any prescribed persons.

(6) If regulations make provision about consultation, NHS England[^c-439a3a4c74d5a6dd6e6d83c24fea8ccc] may not give an authorisation unless it is satisfied that the applicant has complied with the regulations.

(7)[^c-1062f6e3862c3870b268ea71b5802930] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
