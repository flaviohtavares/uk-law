## 247. Application to the City of London

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-0316a6a0df7b3848b0fde329383fea36] This section applies to a committee of the Common Council appointed to exercise functions that the Council has under regulations under section 244(2) to (2ZC).

(2) Section 245(2)(b) and (c) applies[^c-7fbab98aff91e81e624f9624e4b09611] as if such a committee were an overview and scrutiny committee [^c-004064426a8db5085a5d2dcc57f3f91e]... .

(3) Section 9F[^c-0622e12dd5449263e1c66ee657b71a8d] of the Local Government Act 2000 applies as if such a committee were an overview and scrutiny committee and as if the Common Council were a local authority, but with the omission—

- (a) of subsections (1) to (4)[^c-48d942e60e35e249b367b7c773632514],
- (b)[^c-ec6450c85f53b0680df6163d3174dba1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^c-ec6450c85f53b0680df6163d3174dba1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d)[^c-ec6450c85f53b0680df6163d3174dba1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3A)[^c-b2de051500943bb87c53223c5d510902] Section 9FA of the Local Government Act 2000 applies as if such a committee were an overview and scrutiny committee and as if the Common Council were a local authority, but with the omission—

- (a) of subsection (3),
- (b) in subsection (6), of paragraph (b), and
- (c) in subsection (8)(a), of the reference to members of the executive.

(4) In the provisions applied by subsections (3) and (3A)[^c-41ac7ef8b035ed9054fe54f0b796fccb], references to functions under any provision of section 9F(2)[^c-769e8b2b505be934ff5307d6c8e2f44c] of the 2000 Act are, in the case of a committee to which this section applies, references to functions under regulations under section 244(2) to (2ZC) which are exercisable by the committee.[^c-e7a4f6f862b1e2096072f3fe75f5b8ff]

(5) “**The Common Council**” means the Common Council of the City of London.
