## 77. Care Trusts

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where—

- (a) [^key-678f0d76d87d22f85525e4d8c29e677d]... an integrated care board, an NHS trust[^key-77fba55620abf90a40e9b7cd3597f9f3] or an[^key-67a6e6b80285d689976bf22c71b1e615] NHS foundation trust is, or will be, a party to any existing or proposed LA delegation arrangements, [^key-c74f561536f71d15cd24a308c92014cb]...
- (b) the body and the local authority concerned consider[^key-8a62d5a41bccbb5258d77ea4d42b5843] that designation of the body as a Care Trust would be likely to promote the effective exercise by the body of prescribed health-related functions of the local authority[^key-12e2dfa7a54a5d141a3b6763c0b3b166] (in accordance with the arrangements) in conjunction with prescribed NHS functions of the body , and[^key-7d145eebd155b0e3bb16909a13de0a08]
- (c) the requirements in subsection (1A) are satisfied,

the body and the local authority may jointly[^M_F_e8f67bd7-eead-4bd2-c0f1-18d6afc041ed] designate the body as a Care Trust.

(1A)[^key-c4d313ca71601fe60b816a594b3f4217] The body and the local authority must, before designating the body as a Care Trust under this section—

- (a) publish in the prescribed form and manner—
  - (i) the reasons why they consider that the proposed designation would be likely to have the result mentioned in subsection (1)(b), and
  - (ii) information about the proposed governance arrangements of the Care Trust, and
- (b) consult on the proposed designation in accordance with regulations.

(1B) Where a body has been designated as a Care Trust under this section, the body and the local authority must notify prescribed persons of the designation.

(2)[^key-4b25c75c8c40b0506678ea2fbd199b8f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-4b25c75c8c40b0506678ea2fbd199b8f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) A body designated as a Care Trust under this section[^key-98a14b200969c78eb4a24e53e2125799] may (in addition to exercising health-related functions of the local authority as mentioned in subsection (1)(b)) exercise such prescribed health-related functions of the local authority as are agreed[^key-cf2b63575e06bbdf57e764f5425bb352] in relation to persons in any area so agreed[^key-96deff98913b77a62bb80de3ea671617], even though it does not exercise any NHS functions in relation to persons in that area; and “[^key-3e005b0b339d6c148181a1a933691b97]**agreed**” means agreed by the body and the local authority.

(5)[^key-24feb8a8238049cbe039130ad68eb5af] Where a body is designated as a Care Trust under this section, the body and the local authority may jointly revoke that designation.

(5A) Before revoking a designation as a Care Trust under this section, the body and the local authority must consult on the proposed revocation of the designation in accordance with regulations.

(5B) Where the designation of a body as a Care Trust under this section has been revoked, the body and the local authority must notify prescribed persons of the revocation.

(5C)[^key-baab28e3f6f100aeffc6a9d684441efa] Regulations under subsection (1A)(b) or (5A) may include provision requiring a body and a local authority to publish prescribed information following a consultation.

(5D)[^key-ed31badde823d9830a577967e8f7841d] Where a duty is imposed by or by virtue of this section on a body and a local authority, they may make arrangements for the function to be discharged—

- (a) by both of them acting jointly,
- (b) by each of them acting separately, or
- (c) by one of them acting on behalf of both of them.

(6)[^key-c55c77407b9e969781caea5551805eda] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^key-b681fb29ea9e920af06ac837d50e9a6c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8) Regulations may make such incidental, supplementary or consequential provision (including provision amending, repealing or revoking enactments) as the Secretary of State considers expedient in connection with the preceding provisions of this section.

(9) Regulations under subsection (8) may, in particular, make provision—

- (a)[^key-ef0582ff6137c8955b5616d47253a17b] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^key-5998f5028fac500b8e01eee8a51e9839] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^key-145683528f6cb02c3f633f898949daf5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) for supplementing or modifying, in connection with the operation of subsection (4)[^key-6c15a2cbf5e8e7ee9f9bb3b3ece5db78], any provision made by regulations under section 75.

(10) The designation of a body as a Care Trust under this section does not affect any of the functions, rights or liabilities of that body in its capacity as [^key-1009095ce4533369f8983326db7bf46d]... an integrated care board, NHS trust[^key-663f111ef363f1f608b6e8c57b7b253b] or[^key-9c8e04925b855b4858115791aff7cbe3] NHS foundation trust.

(11) In connection with the exercise by a body so designated of any relevant social services functions of a local authority in England[^key-7a6eda733627e9c4ff056c2ce3223118] under LA delegation arrangements—

- (a) section 7 of the Local Authority Social Services Act 1970 (c. 42) (authorities to exercise social services functions under guidance), and
- (b) section 7A of that Act (directions as to exercise of such functions),

apply to the body as if it were a local authority within the meaning of that Act.

(11A)[^key-12b051c3cd52a9869ea09b36ce0ddd16] In connection with the exercise by a body so designated of any relevant social services functions under LA delegation arrangements, sections 145 to 149 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (codes on the exercise of social services functions) apply to the body as if it were a local authority within the meaning of that Act.

(12) In this section—

- “**health-related functions**” has the meaning given by section 75(8),
- “**LA delegation arrangements**” means arrangements falling within section 75(2)(b), whether or not made in conjunction with any pooled fund arrangements,
- “**NHS functions**” means functions exercisable by [^key-9e4d2c191303ab9190c4bcb24f132d7c]... an integrated care board, NHS trust[^key-f565c0ddad8024f13c342aa4c5850e6e] or[^key-4dc014d1aac70ac3eb324745441861bb] NHS foundation trust in its capacity as such,
- “**pooled fund arrangements**” means arrangements falling within section 75(2)(a),
- “relevant social services functions” means—[^key-cc8e19d86955ae29a8f70f9fc4f76f08]
  - (a) in relation to a local authority in England, health-related functions which are social services functions within the meaning of the Local Authority Social Services Act 1970;
  - (b) in relation to a local authority in Wales, health-related functions which are social services functions within the meaning of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014.
