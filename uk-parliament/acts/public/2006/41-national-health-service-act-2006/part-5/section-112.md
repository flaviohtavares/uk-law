# National Health Service Act 2006, section 112

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (147).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 112. Assistance and support: primary dental services

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Board[^key-6b7f533eaf9b5832fc16787bed694515] may provide assistance or support to any person providing or proposing to provide—

- (a) primary dental services under a general dental services contract, or
- (b) primary dental services in accordance with section 107 arrangements.

(2) Assistance or support provided by the Board[^key-d0294614a3bf957614f402e916688963] under subsection (1) is provided on such terms, including terms as to payment, as the Board[^key-eafd8c81aee5a18502a43f0b6dffce36] considers appropriate.

(3) “**Assistance**” includes financial assistance.
