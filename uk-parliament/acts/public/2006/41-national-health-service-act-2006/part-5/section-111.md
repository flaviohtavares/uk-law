# National Health Service Act 2006, section 111

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (147).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 111. Dental public health

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A local authority[^key-4530c01d1cde0d054a1cea6beb4b1f2a] has such functions in relation to dental public health in England as may be prescribed.

(2) The functions of a local authority[^key-930c57a2dc9fba7af2f07af0b03e74f7] under this section may be discharged—

- (a) by the local authority itself,
- (b) by the local authority and one or more other local authorities[^key-c0d30e86df5e0c0e088f6df41dfe660e] acting jointly, or
- (c) by any other person or body in accordance with arrangements made by the local authority.

(3)[^key-e2a86d80382f01d6d6745b924cde3d8e] In this section, “**local authority**” has the same meaning as in section 2B.
