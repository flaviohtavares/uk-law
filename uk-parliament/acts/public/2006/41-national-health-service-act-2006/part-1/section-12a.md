## 12A. Direct payments for health care[^key-5fa311cd640bc6dd3f28029e1e18e6cd][^key-e8a263d827f1e306f985b06a04ef3783]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State ,[^key-bd4bcc2be1ca4ac9fbc69e5b368274fb] NHS England[^key-c26379f595629655ada8a57e0d3d2bfd], an integrated care board[^key-bcbefc4b980747945fd3f08a5911997c] or a local authority may, for the purpose of securing the provision to a patient of anything to which this subsection applies, make payments, with the patient's consent, to the patient or to a person nominated by the patient.

(2) Subsection (1) applies to—

- (a)[^key-e623b5e3b238df96f9f234bc6e5fdb23] anything that the Secretary of State or a local authority has a duty or power to provide or arrange under section 2A or 2B or Schedule 1;
- (aa)[^key-d044e3bd46eebe7b06fe6698ed11b507] anything that NHS England or an integrated care board[^key-3e14739be4ab0469d62538076752fec8] may or must arrange for the provision of under this Act or any other enactment.
- (b)[^key-58a98e698a94b3bfae41d01d4374f601] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)[^key-58a98e698a94b3bfae41d01d4374f601] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Subsection (1) is subject to any provision made by regulations under section 12B.

(4) If regulations so provide, an integrated care board[^key-40e41b9ccb811bb2e26b630925dfd3ea] may, for the purpose of securing the provision for a patient of services that the board[^key-fb3ba4002a07a100fb6490c9c72c0797] must provide under section 117 of the Mental Health Act 1983 (after-care), make payments, with the patient's consent, to the patient or to a person nominated by the patient ; and the references in this subsection to[^key-bd225929fbfac3363286572599c5be5d] an integrated care board are, so far as necessary for the purposes of regulations under subsection (2E) of that section, to be read as references to NHS England.

(5) A payment under subsection (1) [^key-ed60c0a07d690e703eea10d354228a4b]... is referred to in this Part as a “**direct payment**”.

(6)[^key-5e0d097aecca09fb595374bab69e1f1a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^key-4446a739c900157706c362774c9c4daa] In this section and sections 12B to 12D, “**local authority**” has the same meaning as in section 2B.
