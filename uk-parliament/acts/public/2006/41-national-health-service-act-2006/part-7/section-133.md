# National Health Service Act 2006, section 133

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (147).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 133. Inadequate provision of pharmaceutical services

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subsection (2) applies if the Secretary of State is satisfied, after such inquiry as he considers appropriate, that—

- (a) as respects any part of England[^key-f579f890fb018f1ea14e0b70bad3dbd6], the persons whose names are included in any pharmaceutical list are not such as to secure the adequate provision of pharmaceutical services in that [^key-8e88421ea3b0e482cf0076ca66acf0d8]... part, or
- (b) for any other reason any considerable number of persons in any part of England[^key-02c0968307985111999bc3209e1ceeb2] are not receiving satisfactory services under the arrangements in force under this Chapter.

(2) Where this subsection applies, the Secretary of State—

- (a) may authorise NHS England[^key-c26379f595629655ada8a57e0d3d2bfd] to make such other arrangements as he may approve, or may himself make such other arrangements, and
- (b) may dispense with any of the requirements of regulations made under this Part (other than Chapters 2 to 4) so far as appears to him necessary to meet exceptional circumstances and enable such arrangements to be made.
