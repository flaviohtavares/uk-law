## 160. Notification of decisions

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations may require NHS England[^c-c26379f595629655ada8a57e0d3d2bfd] to notify prescribed persons, or persons of prescribed descriptions, of any decision it makes under this Chapter, and of any information relevant to the decision which it considers appropriate to include in the notification.
