## 144. Local pharmaceutical services schemes

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 12 makes provision with respect to the provision of local pharmaceutical services in accordance with schemes made by NHS England[^key-c26379f595629655ada8a57e0d3d2bfd][^key-970880b2c60478af3cc6ffe0778605e1] or the Secretary of State [^key-91c8ac5ec477c0a22bead117d5aeacd5]... .
