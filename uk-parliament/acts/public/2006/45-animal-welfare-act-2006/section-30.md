# Animal Welfare Act 2006, section 30

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 30. Power of local authority to prosecute offences[^c-6be53699ba16eb6616f75e46e80cc609][^c-9d53801a5ebef865b6b731ec1e6e5cd2]

*Extent: E+W*

A local authority in England or Wales may prosecute proceedings for any offence under this Act.
