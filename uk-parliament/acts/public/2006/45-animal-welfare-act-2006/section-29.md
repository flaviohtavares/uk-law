# Animal Welfare Act 2006, section 29

> Text as at 2026-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29. Inspection relating to assimilated law[^c-c6952c93fe3357f18a8a3dc69e4816a3][^c-16af57f61fcf77a144e525552b28fced][^c-b462a5e39492df36406b93ed59c08933][^c-49ec3bbb236a6a6559d980fcad27fd34]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An inspector may carry out an inspection in order to check compliance with regulations under section 12 which are[^c-63f11d145a3c7c1626d1cc8ab4407bc3] assimilated law.

(2) An inspector may enter any premises in order to carry out an inspection under subsection (1).

(3) Subsection (2) does not authorise entry to any part of premises which is used as a private dwelling.
