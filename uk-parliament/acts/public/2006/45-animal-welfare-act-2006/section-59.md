# Animal Welfare Act 2006, section 59

> Text as at 2026-04-06.

## 59. Fishing[^key-10623995d874327ba4fbd60afa2917ad][^key-8b17a098e3def8b6c0ad599c0528f2b3]

*Extent: E+W*

Nothing in this Act applies in relation to anything which occurs in the normal course of fishing.
