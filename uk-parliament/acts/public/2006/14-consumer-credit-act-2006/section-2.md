> Text as at 2015-10-01.

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Removal of financial limits etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-5b7a8b3a24866756bf2fcfc6f3bbe56f][^c-ceda690e7f169bfdebcd694667da47a8] In section 8 of the 1974 Act (which defines consumer credit agreements)—

- (a) in subsection (1) for “personal” substitute “ consumer ”;
- (b) subsection (2) shall cease to have effect.

(2)[^c-54cd8f43035e738316ede6521b9d1ce5] In section 15(1) of that Act (which defines consumer hire agreements) paragraph (c) and the “and” immediately preceding it shall cease to have effect.
