# Consumer Credit Act 2006, section 14

> Text as at 2015-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Default notices

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-cac9056ab08807001b0a004ea662552a] In subsections (2) and (3) of section 88 of the 1974 Act (contents and effect of default notice) for “seven” wherever occurring substitute “ 14 ”.

(2)[^key-1d9633e6414f1c218ae585acc7226d91] In subsection (4) of that section after “it” insert “ and any other prescribed matters relating to the agreement ”.

(3)[^key-a7c58513c94e27a5d157c8c97627bc51] After that subsection insert—

> (4A) The default notice must also include a copy of the current default information sheet under section 86A.
