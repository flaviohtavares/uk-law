# Consumer Credit Act 2006, section 67

> Text as at 2015-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Interpretation[^key-c3882574361c7dc0b205a5bfe299977b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act—

- “**the 1974 Act**” means the Consumer Credit Act 1974 (c. 39);
- “**the 2000 Act**” means the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (c. 8).
