# Consumer Credit Act 2006, section 25

> Text as at 2015-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25. Credit information services

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-512aa1e82fbc5aa4b42db251c5d1769d] In subsection (1) of section 145 of the 1974 Act (types of ancillary credit business) before paragraph (e) insert—

> - (db) the provision of credit information services, or

.

(2)[^c-c417999fd0093cfd7c91c87793140ff3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-e56b26cb1832c8db430f14bf4ce5a4ae] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-512aa1e82fbc5aa4b42db251c5d1769d] In each of the following provisions of that Act for “or debt-counselling” substitute “ , debt-counselling or the provision of credit information services ”

- (a) section 152(1)(application of sections 52 to 54 to ancillary credit businesses);
- (b) section 154 (prohibition of canvassing ancillary credit business off trade premises);
- (c) section 156 (regulations about agreements entered into for ancillary credit businesses).

(5)[^c-79725a479928b762e8b0d17b39eb8b83] In section 189(1) of that Act (definitions) after the definition of “credit brokerage” insert—

> ‘credit information services’ has the meaning given by section 145(7B).
