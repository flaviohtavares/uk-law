# Consumer Credit Act 2006, section 15

> Text as at 2015-10-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Enforceability of regulated agreements[^c-f30f44ec70751561eb0b562b10821f2a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 127 of the 1974 Act (enforcement orders in cases of infringement) subsections (3) to (5) shall cease to have effect.
