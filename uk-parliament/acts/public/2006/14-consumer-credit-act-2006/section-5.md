- (a) in subsection (1) for “personal” wherever occurring substitute “ consumer ”;

(5)[^key-202b2c38afc41cf791bff9cb64c384b4] In subsection (1) of section 158 of that Act (duty of credit reference agency to disclose filed information) for paragraph (a) substitute—

> - (a) a request in writing to that effect from a consumer,

.

(6)[^key-202b2c38afc41cf791bff9cb64c384b4] After subsection (4) of that section insert—

> (4A) In this section ‘consumer’ means—
>
> - (a) a partnership consisting of two or three persons not all of whom are bodies corporate; or
> - (b) an unincorporated body of persons which does not consist entirely of bodies corporate and is not a partnership.

(7)[^key-1a252332a009ef93136670a17481aeb7] In section 181(1) and (2) of that Act (power to alter monetary limits etc.) for “8(2), 15(1)(c)” substitute “ 16B(1) ”.

(8)[^key-202b2c38afc41cf791bff9cb64c384b4] In subsection (5) of section 185 of that Act (agreement with more than one debtor or hirer)—

- (a) in paragraph (b) for “a body corporate” substitute “ not an individual ”;
- (b) for “the body corporate or bodies corporate” substitute “ each person within paragraph (b) ”.

(9)[^key-202b2c38afc41cf791bff9cb64c384b4] In subsection (6) of that section after “a body corporate” insert “ within paragraph (b) of that subsection ”.
