## 30. Minimum sentences for certain firearms offences[^c-e59a806e6c3b99ce5c6c29500af66a66][^c-b0f0a56d1d0456a271359425fcf9dd42]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The 1968 Act is amended as follows.

(2) In section 51A (which imposes minimum sentence requirements for certain offences involving the possession of various firearms), in subsection (1)— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a] in paragraph (a)(ii), for “and” substitute *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a] “ or ” *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a]; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a] after paragraph (a)(ii) insert— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a]

  > - (iii (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a] an offence under any of the provisions of this Act listed in subsection (1A) in respect of a firearm or ammunition specified in section 5(1)(a), (ab), (aba), (ac), (ad), (ae), (af) or (c) or section 5(1A)(a) of this Act, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a]

  . *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a]

(3) After that subsection insert— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a]

> (1A (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a] The provisions are— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a]
>
> - (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a] section 16 (possession of firearm with intent to injure); *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a]
> - (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a] section 16A (possession of firearm with intent to cause fear of violence); *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a]
> - (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a] section 17 (use of firearm to resist arrest); *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a]
> - (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a] section 18 (carrying firearm with criminal intent); *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a]
> - (e (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a] section 19 (carrying a firearm in a public place); *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a]
> - (f (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a] section 20(1) (trespassing in a building with firearm). *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8275a073b2210fface143466f754f75a]

(4) In Schedule 6 (prosecution and punishment of offences) in column 3, in paragraph (a) of the entries relating to sections 19 and 20(1), after “Summary”, in each place, insert “ except if the firearm is a firearm specified in section 5(1)(a), (ab), (aba), (ac), (ad), (ae) or (af) or section 5(1A)(a) of this Act. ”

(5) This section applies only to offences committed after the commencement of this section.
