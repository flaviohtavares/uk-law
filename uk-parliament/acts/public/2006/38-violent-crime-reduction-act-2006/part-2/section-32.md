# Violent Crime Reduction Act 2006, section 32

## 32. Sales of air weapons by way of trade or business to be face to face[^c-bf7eac6322890fe4b56b6cee88e711eb]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632] This section applies where a person sells an air weapon by way of trade or business to an individual in Great Britain who is not registered as a firearms dealer. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632] A person is guilty of an offence if, for the purposes of the sale, he transfers possession of the air weapon to the buyer otherwise than at a time when both— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632] the buyer, and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632] either the seller or a representative of his, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632]

are present in person. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632] The reference in subsection (2) to a representative of the seller is a reference to— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632] a person who is employed by the seller in his business as a registered firearms dealer; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632] a registered firearms dealer who has been authorised by the seller to act on his behalf in relation to the sale; or *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632]
- (c (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632] a person who is employed by a person falling within paragraph (b) in his business as a registered firearms dealer. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632] A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632] on summary conviction in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 51 weeks or to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, or to both; and *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632] on summary conviction in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, or to both. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632]

(5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632] In relation to an offence committed before the commencement of section 281(5) of the Criminal Justice Act 2003 (c. 44), the reference in subsection (4)(a) of this section to 51 weeks is to be read as a reference to 6 months. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a6ccbea008efb36cb9ff957fbe227632]
