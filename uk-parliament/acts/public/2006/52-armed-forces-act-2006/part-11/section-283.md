# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 283

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 283. Imprisonment: maximum term[^key-267f1270e8f80904608273b4c69f6f3d][^key-6086d5cb695c86d1cfa4a33cfc40ca20]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Service Civilian Court may not impose imprisonment for more than 6 months[^key-090c6815106e24ec30bce3417470905b] in respect of any one offence.

(2) Where the Service Civilian Court imposes two or more terms of imprisonment to run consecutively their aggregate must not exceed 12 months[^key-9b97f385e190f1a01801611b73a127b8].

(3)[^key-fcca614afd3c4be43bdc4d63a2172c8a][^key-23817a24ca927999b04a3171737024da] In this section, references to imprisonment include detention in a young offender institution.
