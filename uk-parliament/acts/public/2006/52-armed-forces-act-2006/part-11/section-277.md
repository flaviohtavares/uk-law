# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 277

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 277. The Service Civilian Court[^key-9cdbc9e1b393da36c5ad2d8bfc21f356][^key-51a3ec77400637f80d262dc80962a68b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There shall be a court, to be known as the Service Civilian Court.

(2)[^key-2a005616ab118d799985a7241a689f30] The Service Civilian Court may sit in any place, whether within or outside the United Kingdom.
