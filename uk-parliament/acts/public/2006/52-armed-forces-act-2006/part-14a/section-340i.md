# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 340I

## 340I. Procedure on Commissioner investigations of service complaints[^key-90ae8b9d401b11d41781b0bdcd186097][^key-4de84de62be261f80cba59bf4644047b][^key-5aceba8f3a218f0b1644bafb113337cd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) It is for the Armed Forces Commissioner[^key-ca06eda3fd5c3ab46857ec7ef30a912c] to determine—

- (a) whether to begin, continue or discontinue an investigation under section 340H[^key-c09d5f567006a6cced20e89ec4b4402f];
- (b) whether to investigate a service complaint, or an allegation, as a whole or only in particular respects.

(2) The Secretary of State may make regulations about the procedure to be followed in an investigation under section 340H[^key-ee5f929dd1cba82d898744271985c1ec].

(3) Subject to subsection (2), the procedure for carrying out an investigation under section 340H[^key-7b5c794c8213b60b8e9a87a4d3227e5d] is to be such as the Commissioner[^key-d32c727d01026499e462ce8e4f0d0c8e] considers appropriate in the circumstances.

(4) In particular, the Commissioner[^key-3abf8dca89070c7050f7d9f33ffd6d68] may make such inquiries as the Commissioner considers appropriate.

(5) The Secretary of State may by regulations—

- (a) impose time limits for the taking by the Commissioner[^key-b241e8e598cfa94da2ac3507ac54fff7] or the complainant of any step specified in the regulations;
- (b) specify circumstances in which a time limit does not apply.

(6) Where—

- (a) the Commissioner[^key-2ee1076bce28ef2e534b00adb083a328] carries out an investigation under section 340H(1)(b) in connection with the handling of a service complaint, and
- (b) before the complaint was made, the matter to which it relates was considered in accordance with a process for dealing with matters of that kind,

the Commissioner may for the purposes of the investigation consider any alleged maladministration in connection with that process.
