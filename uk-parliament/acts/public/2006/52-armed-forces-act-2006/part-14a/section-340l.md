# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 340L

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52

## 340L. Report and recommendations : service complaints investigations[^c-90ae8b9d401b11d41781b0bdcd186097][^c-0994fce3c8b8ea221b1a2500ad656495]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Armed Forces Commissioner[^c-0d907ce93a37d85b92c6c89737b6106d] must, after carrying out an investigation under section 340H[^c-19bd627e08e97c40941f757e44eeb780], prepare a report setting out—

- (a) the Commissioner’s[^c-8a0e7776390adb27b61faf7454668bda] findings, and
- (b) any recommendations referred to in subsection (2).

(2) Those recommendations are—

- (a) on an investigation under section 340H(1)(a) where the Commissioner[^c-a2041ce660c69c0e7beaec45023a13a5] finds that the service complaint to which the investigation relates is well-founded, the Commissioner’s[^c-aee72ce70a97d133538d84e3b8b254a9] recommendations (if any) on what redress would be appropriate;
- (b) on an investigation under section 340H(1)(b), (c) or (d) where the Commissioner[^c-d56de17e784839cbd77fe3d63aec1874] finds that the allegation to which the investigation relates is well-founded, the Commissioner’s[^c-660089d08ae8b26b88ae82dbf21d475d] recommendations (if any) as a result of that finding;
- (c) where, by virtue of section 340H(7), the Commissioner[^c-00579f235878045b0c18d09dc5557107] finds maladministration in connection with the handling of a service complaint, the Commissioner’s[^c-3a1bffa0b4555c212fcf80af14961408] recommendations (if any) as a result of that finding.

(3) The Commissioner[^c-64dd98df8deaf65fe5a80c63e0466736] may for the purposes of subsection (2)(b) or (c) make any recommendations that the Commissioner considers appropriate, including recommendations for the purpose of remedying—

- (a) the maladministration or undue delay to which the finding relates, and
- (b) any injustice that the Commissioner considers has or could have been sustained, in consequence of the maladministration or undue delay, by the complainant.

(4) The report must include the Commissioner’s[^c-7ab107d34ad7cd0f5339486835f144b5] reasons for each of the findings and recommendations.

(5) The Commissioner[^c-445037ca8a9b9965c00014e57df7f12f] must send a copy of the report to—

- (a) the Defence Council,
- (b) the complainant, and
- (c) any other persons specified, or of a description specified, in regulations made by the Secretary of State.

(6) The Commissioner[^c-8c9ab29574f38f0de7575bcd4b7db4b9] may send a copy of the report to any other persons the Commissioner considers appropriate.

(7) The Secretary of State may make regulations with respect to reports under this section; and the regulations may in particular include—

- (a) further provision about the preparation of reports;
- (b) provision for the correction of accidental errors in reports;
- (c) provision about obligations (including obligations of confidentiality) that may be imposed on persons to whom reports are sent.
