## 340N. Referral of certain allegations[^c-90ae8b9d401b11d41781b0bdcd186097]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the Armed Forces Commissioner[^c-39c64780d633e8a2ddf3ca43ab15d296] considers that a communication made to the Commissioner[^c-2e7a66f21507c833bd4975b0f3d04f9c] alleges that a person named in the communication (“P”)[^c-c893074b0fa45b185d02e955d1497092]—

- (a) is subject to service law and has been wronged in any matter relating to P’s[^c-09487411444e6a2b693b4cf0bbf68850] service, or
- (b) was wronged in any matter relating to P’s[^c-4d6c19bb9e5f0425355d5c3c140eec82] service which occurred while P[^c-578fb300c0cdc5001555f55a79d4750b] was subject to service law,

the Commissioner may refer the allegation to the appropriate person[^c-f7b74807f3a8f2720aeb57ee2420e37e].

(2) “The appropriate person[^c-e12cb3971cded583c57bad841893692c]” is the person whom the Commissioner[^c-17872512345f762b96542afedb2ed1aa] considers to be the person to whom a service complaint made by P[^c-5bec683dd937c49d837671c4390fec24] in respect of the alleged wrong is (under service complaints regulations) to be made.

(3) If an allegation is referred under subsection (1), the appropriate person[^c-781fe8b338335930967bad55b4d0f66d] must as soon as is reasonably practicable—

- (a) inform P[^c-7bce806c1f7d6e967a5a6a59dc0722d2] that the allegation has been so referred,
- (b) ensure that P[^c-d1747f90e4cc1ee7a5d4fb448465787c] is aware of—
  - (i) the procedure for making a service complaint, and
  - (ii) the effect on the making of service complaints of provision made by virtue of section 340B(2)(c) (time limit), and
- (c) ascertain whether P[^c-2ae328417f8e1203f511c88d9f53fc87] wishes to make a service complaint in respect of the alleged wrong.

(4) Regulations made by the Secretary of State must make provision—

- (a) about matters that must be notified to the Commissioner[^c-e415132e0a49b0486a8d8cdbdd8c0980] where an allegation is referred under subsection (1), and
- (b) about the time by which notification must be given.
