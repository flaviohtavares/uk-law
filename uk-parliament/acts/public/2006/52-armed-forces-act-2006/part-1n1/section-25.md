## 25. Misapplying or wasting public or service property[^c-f72eb6f5bade355f6ace9f3ce5f218f7][^c-a1ffddcaa9445234a970f6d404b7b744]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person subject to service law commits an offence if he misapplies or wastes any public or service property.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable to any punishment mentioned in rows 2 to 14[^c-e99fb43e86b2018f0e0d695eec87e149] of the Table in section 164.
