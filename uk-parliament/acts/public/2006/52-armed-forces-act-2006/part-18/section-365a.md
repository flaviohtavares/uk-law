# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 365A

## 365A. Provost Marshals: appointment[^c-735734224c34d03299001d2b3ce7c3d9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No appointment of a person to be Provost Marshal of a service police force , or to be Provost Marshal for serious crime,[^c-8f786e2e9b0574a1669d086a7587511e] may be made except by Her Majesty.

(2) To be eligible for appointment as a Provost Marshal, a person must be a provost officer.
