# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 363

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 363. Court administration officer[^c-cea3bb76b677db9b15041276a595eb2b][^c-328ec982bdf60c568869c1fb3f767ea0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There shall be a court administration officer for the Court Martial, the Service Civilian Court and the Summary Appeal Court.

(2) The court administration officer is to be appointed by the Defence Council.
