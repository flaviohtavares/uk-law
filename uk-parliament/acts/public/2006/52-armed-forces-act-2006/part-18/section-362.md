# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 362

## 362. Judge advocates[^c-ead1fe143beab93845d83d02fe601719][^c-4a202efc8d074f6d1c410c3d79d4e939]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act “**judge advocate**” means—

- (a) the Judge Advocate General;
- (b) a person appointed under section 30(1)(a) or (b) or (2) of the Courts-Martial (Appeals) Act 1951 (c. 46) (assistants to the Judge Advocate General); or
- (c) a puisne judge of the High Court in England and Wales , or a Circuit judge,[^c-a49a501f5bbba19e823f66322d383285] who (following a request by the Judge Advocate General) is nominated by or on behalf of the Lord Chief Justice of England and Wales to sit as a judge advocate.
