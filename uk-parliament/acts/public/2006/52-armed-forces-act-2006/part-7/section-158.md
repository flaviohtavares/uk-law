## 158. Open court[^c-a2af44834251d85626e611c25ce3a092][^c-b05cf063c95174bcbcfdfe7ff1926024][^c-0d41ee8c11ad8debab6c4f5d46328640]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to any provision made by Court Martial rules, the Court Martial must sit in open court.
