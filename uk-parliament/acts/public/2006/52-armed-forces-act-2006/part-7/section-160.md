## 160. Decisions of Court Martial: finding and sentence[^c-9a8b85c5eae84d74f2bc11412a20b1cb][^c-01425f3fe6bc3d1155efff9f393400ae]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1)[^c-4cb31225c164222e28668f5a6a88c0fe] The finding of the Court Martial on a charge must be determined by votes of the members of the Court Martial other than the judge advocate (the “lay members”) and—

- (a) where there are three lay members, must be a finding with which no fewer than two of them agree;
- (b) where there are four lay members, must be a finding with which no fewer than three of them agree;
- (c) where there are five lay members, must be a finding with which no fewer than four of them agree;
- (d) where there are six lay members, must be a finding with which no fewer than five of them agree.

(1) Subject to subsection (4), any sentence passed by the Court Martial[^c-0ca0f32ba414935b03e2049eff1664a0] must be determined by a majority of the votes of the members of the court.

(2)[^c-355d13173d32a95e48b230e2fd505440] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-355d13173d32a95e48b230e2fd505440] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) In the case of an equality of votes on the sentence, the judge advocate has a casting vote.
