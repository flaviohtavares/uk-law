# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 154

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 154. The Court Martial[^key-d7ce510c96682c54a8db5b682c5ec929][^key-b1299d01f04ae3b7a4cb083dc3f0ae03]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There shall be a court, to be known as the Court Martial.

(2) The Court Martial may sit in any place, whether within or outside the United Kingdom.
