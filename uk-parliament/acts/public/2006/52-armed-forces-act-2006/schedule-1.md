## SCHEDULE 1 — Criminal conduct offences that may be dealt with at a summary hearing

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Sections 53, 54

### Part 1 — Offences that may be dealt with without permission

**1.**[^key-903250ffbaa37ebc66fd6aa9db40fbf7][^key-a74827151eb8176515689c93355cf839] An offence under section 1 of the Theft Act 1968 (c. 60) (theft).

**2.**[^key-93e9555043a62b3cb43ae16525d0aa97][^key-63fd79c3e59384d64a26b371317ea3a3] An offence under section 12 of that Act (taking vehicle etc without consent).

**3.**[^key-fc8b2911be6711d1e429724c16bc19b8][^key-5f56f64180d7a6c90411ff87cdf36696] An offence under section 5(2) of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 (c. 38) (possession of controlled drug).

**4.**[^key-0f039fe091117dbc4c38e662714eb425][^key-de0388f5dd3dd1fc1615ccb3bbb69db0] An offence under section 1(1) of the Criminal Damage Act 1971 (c. 48) (criminal damage).

**5.**[^key-f9b5a13325e907b767dcf7e86255f3fc][^key-52f4ba28c7cbaa2899e95c7e6c3d4e74] An offence under section 3 of the Theft Act 1978 (c. 31) (making off without payment) where the payment required or expected did not exceed £100.

**6.**[^key-9dced08e4a853860ebf4a596ce375b69][^key-f7ea97a9d3e6b68083b1b872f46c93e0] An offence under section 9 of the Criminal Attempts Act 1981 (c. 47) (interference with vehicles).

**7.**[^key-cfe4b515f3b241caac8a4353bebc947d][^key-dc8122fbf99c27704030910d341fe555] An offence under section 39 of the Criminal Justice Act 1988 (c. 33) (assault and battery).

**8.**[^key-1c65d5a062d29d228937a6ec72a8a665][^key-003258d910e46a4a6ab9e0f27397dd83] An offence under section 3 of the Road Traffic Act 1988 (c. 52) (careless driving etc).

**9.**[^key-f1eb25c2466b92bbce186baa6d695399][^key-4956af308c5bf4f87031a3aca5fae584] An offence under section 5 of that Act (driving a vehicle where driver has consumed excessive amount of alcohol etc).

**9A.**[^key-88fa1a51b61297f90e266d00c6d8e2ce] An offence under section 5A of that Act (driving a vehicle with concentration of specified controlled drug above specified limit).

**10.**[^key-fb16917107f0dfa2a143f114d24e699f][^key-48932ea19921559e17089a8383c40586] An offence under section 25 of that Act (tampering with vehicles etc) where the vehicle was on a road.

**11.**[^key-e4bf382912bf83bef369957d620e57b1][^key-4668dd6b82e535ac879af9d5efb2bbe1] An offence under section 28 of that Act (dangerous cycling).

**12.**[^key-1805830b60569c6dddac436b73688886][^key-afdd922844bd2b68409d6fbcc1ce7e1f] An offence under section 29 of that Act (careless cycling etc).

### Part 2 — Offences that may be dealt with only with permission

**13.**[^key-d161be5a39d0102e21a30264905665b7][^key-7d8795adc987cd8fb030adceb02f4498] An offence under section 47 of the Offences against the Person Act 1861 (c. 100) (assault occasioning actual bodily harm).

**14.**[^key-a81bc1e391e17bec3adb31390378ab4f][^key-d4cc16643017aeef3452351aee677e3d] An offence under section 1 of the Prevention of Crime Act 1953 (c. 14) (possession in public place of offensive weapon).

**14A.**[^key-f19ba883564af2f9d153d67fae5378d8] An offence under section 1(1A) of the Restriction of Offensive Weapons Act 1959 (possession of flick knife, flick gun or gravity knife).

**15.**[^key-564ac5036552c7e96691fe5b4ba95fc0][^key-76d815defe3093a05bac673b69ecc746] An offence under section 13 of the Theft Act 1968 (c. 60) (abstracting of electricity).

**16.**[^key-548dba164f8c4479c9b181966b1c1634][^key-18038f801042e7d4ff7e7722d1ab3ab5] An offence under section 139 of the Criminal Justice Act 1988 (c. 33) (possession in public place of point or blade).

**16A.**[^key-f980d63b0e2a2ccf13a9eae7b02683da] An offence under section 141(1A) of the Criminal Justice Act 1988 (possession of certain offensive weapons).

**17.**[^key-6d14b29785cad101f9920d6c11c060c0][^key-2b28ee7a9e783d330fad7258105049a4] An offence under section 125 of the Communications Act 2003 (c. 21) (dishonestly obtaining electronic communications services).

**18.**[^key-2699586bff8b010bc68c517e5c0ff45b][^key-b9940231db8c38a42de84c1bd47c31a7] An offence under section 126 of that Act (possession or supply of apparatus etc for contravening s.125 of that Act).

**19.**[^key-39551f3e5c65be17de2b78a468799c47][^key-cd0bb69f7ee434b1e9d7aa419b2c35ac] An offence under section 1 of the Fraud Act 2006 (fraud).

**20.**[^key-b225618740641a3a0d699e011bd5a93e][^key-ccdef46bd437427b1f8271aa3ae49014] An offence under section 11 of that Act (dishonestly obtaining services).

**21.**[^key-d3030abee1f93456212c9f376b16a651] An offence under section 1(1) of the Offensive Weapons Act 2019 (sale of corrosive product to person under 18).

**22.** An offence under section 3(2) or (3) of the Offensive Weapons Act 2019 (delivery of corrosive product to residential premises or locker).

**23.** An offence under section 6(1) of the Offensive Weapons Act 2019 (possession of a corrosive substance in a public place).

**24.** An offence under section 38(2) or (3) of the Offensive Weapons Act 2019 (delivery of bladed product to residential premises or locker).
