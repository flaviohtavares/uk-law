# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 373

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 373. Orders, regulations and rules

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any power to make orders, regulations or rules conferred by this Act on the Secretary of State is exercisable by statutory instrument.

(1A)[^c-1dc96e50e8edb033028d55702b11f1af] The powers conferred by paragraphs 6 and 7 of Schedule 2A on the Lord Chancellor to make regulations are exercisable by statutory instrument.

(2) The powers conferred by sections 20A,[^c-9862f62aa4591096ac4893003f18754a] 36, 93AA,[^c-ef12ca61a5c5175b6bfbb4a8b25554d1] 93F,[^c-7c722780e75cc271a88a37e5354a44a7] 132, 135, 164, 173, 174, 328 to 331 , 340 and 340B[^c-17b25cbf926396c37bf5111a9e0047b1] on the Defence Council to make regulations are exercisable by statutory instrument; and the Statutory Instruments Act 1946 (c. 36) applies in relation to those powers as if the Defence Council were a Minister of the Crown.

(3) A statutory instrument containing (whether alone or with other provision)[^c-f7925679def8b757ced6bd09b809b4d1]—

- (a) an order under section 53, 89, 113, 276A(7),[^c-1d4104cdb1aba924c22966f0f9eca33f] 324(2)(h), 379 or 381,
- (b) an order under section 323 which by virtue of section 323(4)(c) makes any provision adding to, replacing or omitting any part of the text of an Act,
- (c) regulations under section 128 which make provision of a kind mentioned in section 128(2)(c) or (e) or prescribe documents for the purposes of section 118(2)(b),
- (d) regulations under section 20A,[^c-3fea2ba4dbf1074e3ae59f5a7bd4c496] 93AA(2),[^c-23c7b7224de5c7a19957073881817287] 177J(8),[^c-2d311b82d37a12efdbda7ca56eeaa05d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-bb1fa322e7d2e73ff074800a5977a5e3] 177J(8A) 271, 340A(4), 340E(1) or 340N[^c-7dcd8c854b2ec08941280e4dd381eef6],
- (e) regulations under section 328 which make provision of a kind mentioned in section 328(2)(c),
- (ea)[^c-e14541141dcf0d43c8ccbfde1023d993] regulations under section 329(1) which make provision of a kind mentioned in section 329(2)(ha), (i), or (j),
- (eb) regulations under section 329(3A),
- (eba)[^c-bd02f8ae5b8f394077d4a0031850c446] regulations under section 340IA(4) or (5)(e),
- (ec)[^c-cb6a9ed56b0bdfd7fb01274c48e9aa04] the first regulations under each of sections 340P(1), 340Q and 340R,
- (ed) regulations under section 340P(3),
- (ee)[^c-e1ba27e5b1737773b88d21776a615829] regulations under section 343AE(4),
- (ef) regulations under section 343AF,
- (eg) regulations under section 343B(4A),
- (f) rules under section 163 which—
  - (i) by virtue of section 155 make provision about the constitution of the Court Martial, or
  - (ii) make provision authorised by section 165, or
- (g) rules under section 232F[^c-5dc7b57a78498a15b4538e04ca603f27] [^c-c1e2e8cb5484e68d3bda68972f339981]...,

may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by resolution of, each House of Parliament.

(4) Any other statutory instrument under this Act, except one containing only an order under any of sections 382 to 384, shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(5)[^c-31a9e89367b0a3e888d63d22105070a4] Except for regulations made under section[^c-8d005b47f7ce90f1482e97edcf2189e5] 177J(8)[^c-49edc34d074e21415383c94e95fe49dc] 177J(8A), any order, regulations or rules made under this Act may—

- (a) contain incidental, supplemental, consequential, transitional, transitory and saving provision;
- (b) make different provision for different cases.

(5A)[^c-ef8637331ba241345d60aec8acfe8e90] Regulations made under section 177J(8)[^c-5a9b5552f8469c6a3da8b3ed632b6741] 177J(8A) may contain transitional, transitory and saving provision.

(6)[^c-aa85134eaf1befc84023d9f4df7061ec] Where a provision of this Act confers a power to make an order containing provision equivalent to a provision of PACE, the order may apply that provision of PACE with modifications.
