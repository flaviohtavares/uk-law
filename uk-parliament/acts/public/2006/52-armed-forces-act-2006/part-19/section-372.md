# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 372

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>

## 372. Evidence in proceedings before civilian courts[^c-bc6c81317f8919ec5ceec3e350dab149][^c-30d18a474815b84ee66dca7063452a49]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State may by regulations make provision with respect to evidence, including the admissibility of evidence, in proceedings for an offence created by or under this Act before a civilian court in—

- (a) any part of the United Kingdom;
- (b) the Isle of Man; or
- (c) a British overseas territory other than Gibraltar[^c-14ec6d5da6c6d886dd6ed4225b9e5b9f].
