# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 304

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 304. Sentences passed by civilian courts[^key-bdddcea984ed75daf82e703275e95d9b][^key-290662c0c5698e3be1bb35fa7c1c85e1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part any reference to a custodial sentence passed in respect of a service offence excludes any sentence[^key-3d7bcfe6cbb03837655bcbb6a48b6bf3] passed by a civilian court in respect of such an offence.

(2) In each of sections 297(2) and 298(2) the reference to an order excludes an order made by a civilian court.
