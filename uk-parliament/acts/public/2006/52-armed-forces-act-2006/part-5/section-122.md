# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 122

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 122. Charges brought by or at direction of DSP[^c-fcfff5c39caec076a7c5d950f9010cc1][^c-b75a9d964d14fcc0749e211ad99abcae][^c-3488119f94d57f71b6478665beffde65]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a direction under section 121(2) is given to an officer, he must bring the charge or charges specified in the direction.

(2) A charge brought by the Director of Service Prosecutions (“[^c-a975063712d3ce94b4e89a06a1212015]**the Director**”) under section 121(1A) or by an officer as a result of a direction under section 121(2)—

- (a) is to be regarded for the purposes of this Part as allocated for Service Civilian Court trial if the Director allocated it (under section 121(1B) or (3))[^c-0c9d933d465d98e479f59d3d34adef29] for trial by that court;
- (b) otherwise, is to be regarded for the purposes of this Part as allocated for Court Martial trial.
