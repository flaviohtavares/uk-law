# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 113

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 113. CO to ensure service police aware of possibility serious offence committed[^key-15a388b8e809d49f0c42109253172c82][^key-d2d8b0a040f127de8fcd79503c8652fe]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If an officer becomes aware of an allegation or circumstances within subsection (2), he must as soon as is reasonably practicable ensure that a service police force or the tri-service serious crime unit[^key-5c4d462a8e7fd472c05bfd4090529b77] is aware of the matter.

(2) An allegation is, or circumstances are, within this subsection if it or they would indicate to a reasonable person that a Schedule 2 offence has or may have been committed by a relevant person.

(3) In subsection (2) “**relevant person**” means a person whose commanding officer is the officer mentioned in subsection (1).

(4) In this Chapter “**Schedule 2 offence**” means a service offence listed in Schedule 2.

(5) The Secretary of State may by order amend Schedule 2.
