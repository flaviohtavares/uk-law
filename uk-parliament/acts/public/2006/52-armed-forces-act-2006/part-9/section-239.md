# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 239

## 239. Reduction in sentences for guilty pleas[^c-16e81dd80c759d8ddab456ef0bf338ea][^c-d7f0988c67ceccff4d1a4d8ac835989b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where an offender—

- (a) has pleaded guilty to a service offence in proceedings before a court; or
- (b) at a summary hearing in respect of a service offence, has admitted the offence.

(2) In determining what sentence to pass on the offender, the court or officer dealing with him for his offence must take into account—

- (a) the stage in the proceedings for the offence at which he indicated his intention to plead guilty or his intention to admit the offence at a summary hearing; and
- (b) the circumstances in which this indication was given.

(3) In subsection (2) “**sentence**” includes any order made when dealing with the offender in respect of his offence.

(4) Subsection (5) applies in the case of an offence the sentence for which, as a result of section 225(2) or 226(2) of this Act (required custodial sentences), falls to be imposed under section 313(2A) or 314(2A)[^c-f1d5ae3181e5f82531d7b666f6e27576] of the Sentencing Code[^c-baac6b868c18f3a2b4a36c7bb577727e].

(5) Nothing in section 313(2A) or 314(2A)[^c-9483976a1a41cb95c72bbc8a211a231f] of that Code[^c-e7a0baf34d4835dcc16ccb45c5f68cbe] prevents the court, after taking into account any matter mentioned in subsection (2) above, from imposing any sentence which is at least 80% of that specified in section 313(2A) or 314(2A) of that Code.

(6)[^c-ba9b43e660fac880854e88756965d4f6][^c-3de4ea0722d106eaa07d24af15347f41] Nothing in section 227A(1A) or (2)[^c-9d730bca8d93424bb50ea30044de3821] prevents the court, after taking into account any matter mentioned in subsection (2) of this section, from imposing any sentence which is at least 80% of that specified in section 227A(1A) or (2).
