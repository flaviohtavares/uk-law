## 262. Power to recommend licence conditions[^c-30a8473837fa60a77fd658a3eeb5354b][^c-9220e2303450380300fd1afb53792c93]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 328 of the Sentencing Code[^c-1da6ae6d37155667f5ef23258d2c8553] (court imposing prison term of 12 months or more may recommend licence conditions) “**court**” includes a court dealing with an offender for a service offence.
