# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 257

## 257. Pre-sentence reports: supplementary[^c-deb6453534941aad490709aa6f3ff8cd][^c-a7506d502723dd54d91ff5de18134d9a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In section 256 and this section “**pre-sentence report**” has the meaning given by section 31(1) of the Sentencing Code[^c-2a89e93010c4f6101101f93a42080752].

(2) In section 31(1) of that Code[^c-358e1602e31a1e10f34ee3edb75b1239] as applied by this section, “**an appropriate officer**” includes any registered social worker (as well as any person who is an appropriate officer within the meaning given by section 31(2) of that Code[^c-7b9586b1c4761f18af588fb39bef1b54]).

(3) In this section “**registered social worker**” means a person registered as a social worker in a register maintained by—

- (a)[^c-10d3fe34957b9ebd5809cea75b0e9676] Social Work England;
- (b) the Care Council for Wales;
- (c) the Scottish Social Services Council; or
- (d) the Northern Ireland Social Care Council.

(4) Section 32(1) to (4) and (6) of the Sentencing Code[^c-28ab90c35894087306d3e0212af02469] (disclosure of reports) apply in relation to a pre-sentence report obtained by a court for the purposes of section 256 of this Act as they apply in relation to a report obtained by a court for the purposes of section 30 of that Code[^c-6cb8be48eab037f3cb42f562e7c10aa6].
