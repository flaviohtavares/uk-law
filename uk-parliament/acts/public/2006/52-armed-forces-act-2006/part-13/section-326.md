> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 326. Exclusion of enactments requiring consent of Attorney General or DPP[^key-d71aaeb869b1dc8f64b24a7d09f194d9][^key-da9ae3abe682f6dbd284a46321c11622]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to section 61(2), no enactment requiring the consent of the Attorney General or the Director of Public Prosecutions in connection with any proceedings has effect in relation to proceedings under this Act for a service offence.
