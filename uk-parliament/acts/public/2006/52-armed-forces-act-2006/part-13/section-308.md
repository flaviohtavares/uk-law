# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 308

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (189).** Text as at 2026-11-03. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 308. Section 305: supplementary[^c-955d9558ae075a51908f0019a917cf67][^c-8744ee72e783dd2373e39a69bac293c7][^c-cd03dca47e6228e5046ddbb59f5c8e54]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Defence Council may by regulations make provision about the obtaining of samples under section 305(1)[^c-d062fe533b7cb3ea6a3c6dbd6ccf6af6] and the analysis of such samples, including in particular provision—

- (a) as to the number of samples which a person may be required to provide;
- (b)[^c-590f2f3003e5ba2a9b50e3c1e0ce5d4c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) enabling the person imposing a requirement under section 305(1) [^c-2ad98ce57d306f955272a8e287ed6db8]... to specify the way in which the sample is to be provided;
- (d) prescribing circumstances in which a requirement under section 305(1) [^c-1168e8d887e278162b73c4b1a090af80]... may not be imposed;
- (e) as to the equipment to be used, and the procedures to be followed, in obtaining or analysing samples;
- (f) as to the qualifications and training of any persons engaged in obtaining or analysing samples.

(2)[^c-ac850e5125fc2b9993b0329f3d45802f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The results of any analysis of a sample provided pursuant to a requirement imposed under section 305(1) [^c-3cd291cb8c9766b14330c0c1b0029e1a]...are not admissible in evidence against any person in proceedings in respect of a service offence.

(4) Nothing in this Chapter limits the powers conferred by—

- (za)[^c-c050c09404b14a319c8cbef16a15173e] Chapter 3A of Part 3,
- (a) any provision of the Road Traffic Act 1988 (c. 52), or
- (b) any provision of an order under section 113(1) of PACE which makes provision equivalent to any provision of Part 5 of PACE (questioning and treatment of persons by police),

or affects the admissibility in any proceedings of evidence obtained under those powers.
