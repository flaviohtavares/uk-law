# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 318

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 318. Arrest by civilian police of persons unlawfully at large[^c-f0efeafd131badde9dd02eafef5d225e][^c-3e9ba5e2b9b47c2398f6af93f7fb9715]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person who has been sentenced to service detention and who is unlawfully at large—

- (a) may be arrested without a warrant by a civilian policeman[^c-ddc4e651b5cef8e37540cb146af17c88]; and
- (b) may be taken to the place in which he is required in accordance with law to be detained.

(2) Section 301(4) (cases where persons temporarily released from service detention are unlawfully at large) applies for the purposes of this section.

(3)[^c-93350d65470e9a73022ae769d8482457] In this section “**civilian policeman**” has the same meaning as in section 314.
