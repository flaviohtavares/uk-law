# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 320

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52

## 320. Power to use reasonable force[^c-09a4cfd23ff946edcbc31a9f9b164ae1][^c-d0973773320ce95d12c9ccd7026f0d27]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a power of arrest is conferred on any person by virtue of this Chapter, he may use reasonable force, if necessary, in the exercise of the power.
