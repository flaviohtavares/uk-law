# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 140

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52

## 140. The Summary Appeal Court[^c-1edf8b9eb249ae1c94e816cab1c03bd8][^c-9cdc80ce54a0e15485c5169d842d5d01]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There shall be a court, to be known as the Summary Appeal Court.

(2) The Summary Appeal Court may sit in any place, whether within or outside the United Kingdom.
