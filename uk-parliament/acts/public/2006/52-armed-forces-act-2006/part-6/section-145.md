## 145. Open court[^c-06784d9b66a89544f6d52b43a8d9f8e8][^c-318e5d3156868ea81fd4f242edf55962]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Subject to any provision made by SAC rules, the Summary Appeal Court must sit in open court.
