# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 150

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52

## 150. Privileges of witnesses and others[^key-b9c7f74e6bd8c1bec96eb7b04eb63b54][^key-4e1f67059c86976dae3705108edeed8e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A witness before the Summary Appeal Court or any other person whose duty it is to attend the court is entitled to the same immunities and privileges as a witness before the High Court in England and Wales.
