# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 97

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. Power to use reasonable force[^key-91cf5e8236086b9c43e2f8a9ab151dd0][^key-6f364de624aaf617dcb5a7e10240669e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-06843cc240fe0c1fdf92e4041a0f1909] Where a power is conferred on any person by or under this Part, he may use reasonable force, if necessary, in the exercise of the power.

(2)[^key-f4e6393a93521cd4c79a89a7a531e573] Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to powers conferred by Chapter 3A of this Part.
