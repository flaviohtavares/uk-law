# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 354

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 354. Extension of powers of command dependent on rank or rate[^key-75df192b98a8c0ff23768ef39a652d21][^key-1555435acb916096e859b1cf0fb1a57a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An officer, warrant officer or non-commissioned officer of a regular or reserve force who is subject to service law (“A”) has, over members of any other such force who are of inferior rank or rate to A, such powers of command as are dependent on rank or rate.
