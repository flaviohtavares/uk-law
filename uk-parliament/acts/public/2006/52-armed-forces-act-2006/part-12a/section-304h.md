# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 304H

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 304H. Meaning of “sentence”[^key-bdb59840e419b2c9c45946b5c53e890c][^key-99a976f6c0eb62a36cf6e9caec64ec3b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- (a) “**sentence**” includes any order made by a court when dealing with an offender in respect of an offence; and
- (b) a reference to a greater or lesser sentence must be interpreted accordingly.
