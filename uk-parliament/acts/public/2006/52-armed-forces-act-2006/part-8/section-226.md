# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 226

## 226. Third domestic burglary[^c-ef45cf6b44c004d09afa901f3368acdb][^c-61439bf3bf89cf038f67d8d13a0b48d0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where—

- (a) a person aged over 18 is convicted by the Court Martial of an offence under section 42 (criminal conduct); and
- (b) if his conviction had been by a civilian court in England and Wales of the corresponding offence under the law of England and Wales, section 314 of the Sentencing Code[^c-6f75c1c62048a8fae8e705bef5bf1e18] (third domestic burglary) would apply.

(2) The Court Martial must impose the sentence required by section 314(2A)[^c-dd9af62d7e38a693bac135510607af2d][^c-5ad160771a3d98b057840ad7a9a598e2] of that Code, unless it is of the opinion that there are exceptional circumstances[^c-1801b6efecbd432d0acf71a2aaf0c9dc] which—

- (a) relate to any of the offences or to the offender; and
- (b)[^c-790d256c1e3344badb98e7b7c718805f] justify not doing so.
