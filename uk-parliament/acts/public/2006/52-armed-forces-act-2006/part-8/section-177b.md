## 177B. Deprivation orders: interpretation[^key-b6456bc364a337471eeaa9dd5ac010d5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A deprivation order is an order made under this Act which—

- (a) is made in respect of an offender for an offence, and
- (b) deprives the offender of any rights in the property to which the order relates.

(2) In sections 177C and 177E “the decision maker”, in relation to an offender, means—

- (a) the court by which the person is convicted, or
- (b) the commanding officer who records a finding that the charge has been proved.

(3) Nothing in subsection (2) prevents section 376 from applying in relation to this section and sections 177C to 177F.
