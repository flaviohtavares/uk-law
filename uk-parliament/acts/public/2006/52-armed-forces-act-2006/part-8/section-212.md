# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 212

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 212. Term of detention and training order: general[^c-a79c90724e76e249a38f374ea204ea18]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-1447a8b0e6c1fce6fbc4d6aa86afbf32] The term of an order made under section 211 in respect of an offence—

- (a) shall be 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 18 or 24 months; and
- (b) may not exceed the maximum term of imprisonment with which the offence is punishable in the case of a person aged 21[^c-226a15b957913c56bf9f47d97bbf4258] or over.

(2) Where—

- (a) the offence is an offence under section 42 (criminal conduct),
- (b) the corresponding offence under the law of England and Wales is under that law a summary offence, and
- (c) the maximum term of imprisonment with which that offence is punishable in the case of a person aged 21[^c-03903ceb7e4d6cb97d69be76d40b5c46] or over is 51 weeks,

the term of the order may not exceed 6 months.

(3)[^c-e319d151ab9261aa8b0303eecac2a726] An order under section 211 takes effect at the beginning of the day on which it is made, unless the court orders otherwise under section 237 of the Sentencing Code[^c-4c1bf89f4b6da03bb8fc499ae5eaf778] (as applied by section 213 of this Act).
