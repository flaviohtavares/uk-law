# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 215

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 215. Section 214: definitions etc[^key-0449fe1bd2050efeafa7adb5188b440a][^key-f6645a69c4f8d53c78414a928f99dbf0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 238(3) of the Sentencing Code[^key-1243d7c0da2ab92e9c2a50c0dd67da20] (treatment of concurrent and consecutive terms) applies for the purposes of the reference in section 214(1)(a) of this Act to the term of an order.

(2) Where the new offence (within the meaning of section 214) is found to have been committed over a period of two or more days, or at some time during a period of two or more days, it shall be taken for the purposes of that section to have been committed on the last of those days.

(3) In section 214 “[^key-a037d0c11942b93bb8c6972061181723]**youth detention accommodation**” has the meaning given by section 248 of the Sentencing Code[^key-b52ab6c3c7ca17697ed30612878e3d96].
