# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 208

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (189).** Text as at 2026-11-03. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 208. Prohibition on imposing imprisonment on persons under 21[^c-daa6a98c2aceeddb2dacc4397d25684c][^c-2b298e4f8ac46ee937c9a1fba470317f][^c-9df71e4ce18f3b5c556664c62e10ad10]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person who is aged under 21[^c-27a83b6b6e57d609671e3febc57b34f5] when convicted of an offence by the Court Martial or the Service Civilian Court shall not be sentenced to imprisonment for the offence.
