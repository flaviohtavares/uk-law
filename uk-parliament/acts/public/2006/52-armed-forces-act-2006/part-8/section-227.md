# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 227

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (204).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 227. Firearms offences[^c-1dbf0bedb353cc57b95a2b3e5eb6e3bc][^c-48d4a42dbf396ab15fb360141250465a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) a person is convicted by the Court Martial of an offence under section 42 (criminal conduct);
- (aa)[^c-ff3010f92bb02bfc5504877583177dcb] the corresponding offence under the law of England and Wales is an offence listed in any of paragraphs 1 to 4 of Schedule 20 to the Sentencing Code; and
- (b) if his conviction had been by a civilian court in England and Wales of that corresponding offence, section 311 of the Sentencing Code[^c-4ae2bb2fb8d4a77bd3e3448a20725574] (minimum sentences for certain firearms offences) would apply.

(2) The Court Martial must impose the sentence required by section 311(2) of the Sentencing Code[^c-896adecd1683d5b4d30ee2b539837911] (as that provision has effect in relation to England and Wales), unless it is of the opinion that there are exceptional circumstances relating to the offence or to the offender which justify its not doing so.

(3) In section 311(3)(a) of that Code[^c-abca71b6824bfe69789e4637ca19c14e], as applied by this section, the reference to a sentence of detention under section 250[^c-aeb7015b743d923f46930cd6a107972f] or 252A[^c-3b7cfab4a28e848eb97daef844caec08] of that Code is to be read as a reference to a sentence of detention under section 209 of this Act.
