## 177H. Driving disqualification: availability[^key-99e777f3a0dc9a87a1663915164c8f7a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a driving disqualification order is available to a court, the court may make a driving disqualification order whether or not it also deals with the offender for the offence in any other way (including where the court makes a service community order, an overseas community order or a suspended sentence order which imposes a driving prohibition requirement).[^key-8c432313cc77b568b4c84ce8a989d094]
