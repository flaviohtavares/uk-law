## 221. Life sentence for certain dangerous offenders aged under 18[^key-b9f655d8d9328e12ce8339af88835c31][^key-442654be8065992cdff325e89fd23b4f][^key-2e543c7155b575249cd26f3e578d2442]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where —[^key-242cef028ea44fbe6c1e4407d07a2e0e]

- (a) a person aged under 18 is convicted by the Court Martial of an offence under section 42 (criminal conduct) which was committed on or after 4 April 2005[^key-7aeca6f6cc5409fa05bef2d64b71aa99],
- (b) the corresponding offence under the law of England and Wales is a Schedule 19 offence within the meaning of Part 10 of the Sentencing Code (see section 307 of that Code)[^key-4635b3b3efd024ffe94fd5c3ce39cdfb],
- (ba)[^key-e9ef31455b247705cd1e2f97effb27ea] the court considers that the seriousness of—
  - (i) the offence under section 42, or
  - (ii) that offence and one or more offences associated with it,

  is such as to justify the imposition of a sentence of detention for life, and
- (c) the court is of the required opinion (defined by section 223).

(2)[^key-a49ef47fed5175a806d04d05d46e04bb] Section 258(2) of the Sentencing Code (duty to impose detention for life)[^key-ddd26bf16c821057f9e95218092e6374] applies in relation to the offender.

(3)[^key-6aa2948e90e44ef46b110a1f21790c43] In section 258(2) of the Sentencing Code (as applied by subsection (2)), the reference to section 250 of that Code is to be read as a reference to section 209 of this Act.

(4)[^key-f70c9afb16cbddcd46a41d2e700744b8] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-9ff6a03952e25c8ee570ad81a500232c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) A sentence under section 258(2) of the Sentencing Code[^key-fa4183308e483094976c3ba831d297b5] passed as a result of this section is not to be regarded as a sentence fixed by law.
