# Armed Forces Act 2006, section 202

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (189).** Text as at 2026-11-03. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/52

## 202. Order with community requirements: disapplication of certain provisions[^c-15c0f42b5fd05a3f531ebbc266230ca0][^c-ac4705c498d6e67af862070622ca7f7f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions of the Sentencing Code[^c-c8ffdb6c36def33222eb23c4ddcedab6] do not apply in relation to a suspended sentence order with community requirements made by a relevant service court—

- paragraph 17(2)(c) of Schedule[^c-46d3b06dd005e65988c46690b732cd18] (condition for mental health treatment requirement);
- section 298(4)[^c-1e566d396dc64b2dfae407a60e9d97d5] (requirement to give copy of order to magistrates' court).
