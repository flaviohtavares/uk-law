# Equality Act 2006, section 34

> Text as at 2024-10-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 34. Unlawful[^key-d4634b5ce3ef1b9f50a07dd505b82e88]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this Part (except section 30(3)) “**unlawful**” means contrary to a provision of the Equality Act 2010[^key-3eef64c6147f11f536d70acda89f72f9].

(2) But action is not unlawful for the purposes of this Part by reason only of the fact that it contravenes a duty under or by virtue of any of the following provisions of the Equality Act 2010[^key-cc45f8f83b251fde689c90cb4be6f381]—

- (a)[^key-98de407dc3853d8bf015faa8c2b55604] section 1 (public sector duty regarding socio-economic inequalities),
- (b) section 149, 153 or 154 (public sector equality duty),
- (c) Part 12 (disabled persons: transport), or
- (d) section 190 (disability: improvements to let dwelling houses).
