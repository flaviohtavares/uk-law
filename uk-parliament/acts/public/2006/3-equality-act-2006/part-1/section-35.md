# Equality Act 2006, section 35

> Text as at 2024-10-26.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 35. General

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Part—

- “**act**” includes deliberate omission,
- “**groups**” has the meaning given by section 10,
- “**the Commission**” means the Commission for Equality and Human Rights,
- “**disabled person**” has the meaning given by section 8,
- “**human rights**” has the meaning given by section 9,
- [^c-d7b688431a3b056bf2487d9581f4a834]...
- “**race**” includes colour, nationality, ethnic origin and national origin,
- “**religion or belief**” has the same meaning as in section 10 of the Equality Act 2010[^c-6768fdd33091a62b1841b76b1c7b4198], and
- “**sexual orientation**” has the same meaning as in section 12 of the Equality Act 2010.[^c-9352e0d3e9302d4cfb4d3dde90080a14]
  - (a) persons of the same sex as him or her,
  - (b) persons of the opposite sex, or
  - (c) both.
