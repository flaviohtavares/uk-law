# Childcare Act 2006, section 30

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2024-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 30. Interpretation of Part 2[^key-81aa20196c3b83fa7c8afdd5be61d90a]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Part—

- “**childcare**” means—
  - (a) child minding or day care within the meaning of Part 10A of the Children Act 1989 (c. 41) *(Extent: E)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-4fcdcc380065cd6841856aae1032d7c3] Part 2 of the Children and Families (Wales) Measure 2010 *(Extent: W)* in respect of which the provider is required to be registered under that Part;
  - (b) care provided by a person of a description approved in accordance with a scheme made by the Assembly under section 12(5) of the Tax Credits Act 2002 (c. 21);
- “**prescribed**” means prescribed by regulations made by the Assembly.
