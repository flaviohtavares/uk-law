## 28. Inspection[^c-bf55f7960e83ce8a87a3f067eb0b0729]

*Extent: E+W*

For the purposes of section 38 of the Education Act 1997 (c. 44) (inspection of local education authorities), the functions conferred on a Welsh local authority by or under this Part are to be regarded as education functions (as defined in section 579(1) of the Education Act 1996)[^c-6bb4b5489b1b0a0b3eba935f4cc43ed0].
