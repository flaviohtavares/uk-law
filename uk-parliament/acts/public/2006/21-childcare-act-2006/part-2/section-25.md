# Childcare Act 2006, section 25

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2024-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/21

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 25. Charges where local authority provide childcare[^c-9c5dd4974081c556cfcb849deb95d01d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A Welsh local authority may enter into an agreement under which payments are made to the authority for the provision by the authority of childcare for a child.

(2)[^c-c46f9c5f0b3677767ac852ea6c6b8d43] Subsection (1) does not apply to childcare provided under sections 37 to 39 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014 (meeting care and support needs of children), provision as to charges for such care being made by Part 5 of that Act.
