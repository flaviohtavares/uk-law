# Childcare Act 2006, section 37

## 37. Entry on the register and certificates[^key-5e43037699e950aa669756a05f629700][^key-16f1de366463ada7ef2ccff41839a21d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If an application under section 35(1) (a)[^key-8784e94c9f86f15fcf3fa57a41502570] is granted, the Chief Inspector must—

- (a) register the applicant in the early years register as an early years childminder with domestic premises[^key-5ce909c737e8546c94dfe8f81f35a292], and
- (b) give the applicant a certificate of registration stating that he is so registered.

(2) If an application under section 36(1)[^key-a2fd8485cbe5566af76cb006cf075032](a) or (1A)(a)[^key-0341cd4aeda0b712a56f5f4490e16be5] is granted, the Chief Inspector must—

- (a) register the applicant in the early years register as an early years provider other than a childminder (even if, in the case of an application under section 36(1)(a), the early years provision is or includes early years childminding)[^key-574c060e00eeb069a6a63574f6198fb2] [^key-396a47803d1b67d1a1e8a321983d101b]..., and
- (b) give the applicant a certificate of registration stating that he is so registered.

(2A)[^key-336d5567ff8a935ac72e54bc15b9a3c1] If an application under section 36(1)(b)(i) is granted, the Chief Inspector must—

- (a) register the applicant in the early years register as an early years childminder without domestic premises, and
- (b) give the applicant a certificate of registration stating that the applicant is so registered.

(3) A certificate of registration given to the applicant in pursuance of subsection (1) , (2) or[^key-110617a6bfee6f20239f6c652e49595e] (2A) must contain prescribed information about prescribed matters.

(4) If there is a change of circumstances which requires the amendment of a certificate of registration, the Chief Inspector must give the registered early years provider an amended certificate.

(5) If the Chief Inspector is satisfied that a certificate of registration has been lost or destroyed, the Chief Inspector must give the registered early years provider a copy, on payment by the provider of any prescribed fee.
