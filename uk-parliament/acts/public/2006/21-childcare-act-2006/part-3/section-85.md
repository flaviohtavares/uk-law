## 85. Offence of making false or misleading statement[^key-186d39f2f717a472574bd54bc92edad5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person commits an offence if, in an application for registration under any of Chapters 2 to 4, the person[^key-9325b089119ed0319b00f00a1068dce3] knowingly makes a statement which is false or misleading in a material particular.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
