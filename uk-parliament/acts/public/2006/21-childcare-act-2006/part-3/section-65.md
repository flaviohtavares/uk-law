# Childcare Act 2006, section 65

## 65. Special procedure for persons already registered in a childcare register[^c-9b399b0664d59a12ae1eed0e04109028][^c-3ccf60fcb42324d0796b934a9309e56e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a person who is registered as an early years childminder with or without domestic premises in the early years register, or as a later years childminder with or without domestic premises in Part A of the general childcare register,[^c-cb9c6a03b840663d1e7ca8ed284be3d5] gives notice to the Chief Inspector that he wishes to be registered in Part B of the general childcare register, the Chief Inspector must—

- (a) register the person in Part B of the general childcare register as a childminder (as the case may be, with or without domestic premises)[^c-19ff9dfe04c618e71ad077218647aed0], and
- (b) give the applicant a certificate of registration stating that he is so registered.

(2) If a person who is registered (otherwise than as a childminder) in the early years register or in Part A of the general childcare register [^c-df1622253213c784a555e558b47e8f71]... gives notice to the Chief Inspector that he wishes to be registered in Part B of the general childcare register [^c-df1622253213c784a555e558b47e8f71]..., the Chief Inspector must—

- (a) register the person in Part B of the general childcare register as a provider of childcare other than a childminder [^c-df1622253213c784a555e558b47e8f71]... , and
- (b) give the person a certificate of registration stating that he is so registered.

(3) Subsections (3) to (5) of section 64 apply in relation to a certificate of registration given in pursuance of subsection (1) or (2) of this section as they apply in relation to a certificate of registration given in pursuance of subsection (1) or (2) of that section.
