## 79. Power of constable to assist in exercise of powers of entry[^c-bfa4dbfc31581d1755aacc553e21531e]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Chief Inspector[^c-72ab9e5832caf5b0dff18be0d7c6fd30] may apply to a court for a warrant under this section.

(2) If it appears to the court that the Chief Inspector[^c-314605d8e97fbed7c0c03c8fce10c171]—

- (a) has attempted to exercise a power conferred on him by section 77 or 78A[^c-de4416c66a0180492601eb654ce81901] but has been prevented from doing so, or
- (b) is likely to be prevented from exercising any such power,

the court may issue a warrant authorising any constable to assist the Chief Inspector[^c-6c301662238d972f757935fcbfee3073] in the exercise of the power, using reasonable force if necessary.

(3) A warrant issued under this section must be addressed to, and executed by, a constable.

(4)[^c-6fd8248429eafac3e9810a500cb4804d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) In this section, “**court**” means the High Court or the family court[^c-c155e4131d803a8e75663ccb318a109f].
