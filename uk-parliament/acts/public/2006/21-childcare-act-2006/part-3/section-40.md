## 40. Duty to implement Early Years Foundation Stage[^key-3106da8a93448e7c82cdb93f8895205d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies to—

- (a) early years providers providing early years provision in respect of which they are registered under this Chapter, and
- (b) early years providers providing early years provision in respect of which, but for section 34(2) (exemption for provision for children aged 2[^key-b1577f459d7edef16a5dd8992dd0a834] or over at certain schools), they would be required to be registered under this Chapter.

(2) An early years provider to whom this section applies—

- (a) must secure that the early years provision meets the learning and development requirements, and
- (b) must comply with the welfare requirements.
