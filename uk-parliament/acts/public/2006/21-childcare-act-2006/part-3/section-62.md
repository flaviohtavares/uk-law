# Childcare Act 2006, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2024-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 62. Applications for registration on the general register: childminders with domestic premises[^c-362a20c06131c354a11afb073e38d02b][^c-984d554589fec6706e5c4bcfcb777b90]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person who provides or proposes to provide in England—

- (a)[^c-4ba22dc74c36ea77d7911e86bb916f6d] later years childminding for a child who has attained the age of eight, or
- (b)[^c-b3a7ffb30abcdc72f8259ff6527ca31e] early years childminding or later years childminding for a child who has not attained that age but in respect of which the person is not required to be registered under Chapter 2 or 3,

where some or all of the childminding is (or is to be) provided on domestic premises,[^c-21676fc3797ea8138aaf867639981ab2] may make an application to the Chief Inspector for registration in Part B of the general childcare register as a childminder with domestic premises[^c-d7954dcb1d1aad20d56a01ad9f676fae].

(2)[^c-4ba22dc74c36ea77d7911e86bb916f6d] An application under subsection (1) must—

- (a) give any prescribed information about prescribed matters,
- (b) give any other information which the Chief Inspector reasonably requires the applicant to give, and
- (c) be accompanied by any prescribed fee.

(3)[^c-4ba22dc74c36ea77d7911e86bb916f6d] The Chief Inspector must grant an application under subsection (1) if—

- (a) the applicant is not disqualified from registration by regulations under section 75, and
- (b) it appears to the Chief Inspector that any requirements prescribed for the purposes of this subsection (“the prescribed requirements for registration”) are satisfied and are likely to continue to be satisfied.

(4)[^c-4ba22dc74c36ea77d7911e86bb916f6d] The Chief Inspector must refuse any application under subsection (1) which subsection (3) does not require him to grant.

(5)[^c-4ba22dc74c36ea77d7911e86bb916f6d] The prescribed requirements for registration may include requirements relating to—

- (a) the applicant;
- (b) the premises on which the childminding is being (or is to be) provided;
- (c) the arrangements for childminding on those premises;
- (d) any person who may be caring for children on those premises;
- (e) any other person who may be on those premises.
