# Childcare Act 2006, section 34

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2024-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 34. Requirement to register: other early years providers[^M_C_f67eb870-6d04-4fec-cd78-ef1161bb1eb7][^c-cdca9cac4b29317cbfd87a8db15f1ecf][^c-3c27ee2f146aae8188c7d1d000aed5b2][^c-31686ff62f1b31b338f5af57180b4db4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-f922881ca4b02c4702dd32ae99afda34] A person may not provide early years provision on non-domestic premises in England unless—

- (a) the person is registered in the early years register as an early years provider other than a childminder (whether or not the provision is or includes early years childminding), or
- (b) the provision is early years childminding, none of which is provided on domestic premises, and the person is registered as an early years childminder without domestic premises—
  - (i) in the early years register, or
  - (ii) with an early years childminder agency.

(1ZA) Subsection (1)(a) does not apply to early years provision in respect of which the person providing it is required to be registered under section 33(1) or under subsection (1A).

(1A) A person may not provide early years provision [^c-3410641fe348de3f306aa2ab10d9c67e]... in England which would be early years childminding but for section 96(5) , and some or all of which is provided on domestic premises,[^c-89dccac090dcb4bd24d973a21e540143] unless the person is registered as an early years provider other than a childminder[^c-538b81519b4fc833c484f3d7fc287e96]—

- (a) in the early years register [^c-afbf00e5c60f529374ffbf86d1349dea]..., or
- (b) with an early years childminder agency [^c-afbf00e5c60f529374ffbf86d1349dea]....

(2) Subsections (1) and (1A) do[^c-983cac2fae90f52a199c41ab4967f10a] not apply in relation to early years provision for a child or children who has (or have) attained the age of two[^c-31e130c4f53d4c3f9ebf680fafc33d58] if—

- (a) the provision is made at any of the following institutions[^c-e467930e6f07dfa5f8fe22b12c80e461] as part of the institution's activities—
  - (i) a maintained school,
  - (ii) a school approved[^c-4b766eea7c6865848367df6337b3e04a] under section 342 of the Education Act 1996 (c. 56) (approval of non-maintained special schools), or
  - (iii) an independent educational institution[^c-5199d50213cd1deed14a3cdf260efca9],
- (b) the provision is made by the proprietor of the institution[^c-60498d6d5670c3e35d10f92973390792] or a person employed to work at the institution, and
- (c)[^c-625b48217ea6a2a171b70356bd28c69d] where the provision is made at a school (including a school that is an independent educational institution)—
  - (i) the child is a registered pupil at the school, or
  - (ii) if the provision is made for more than one child, at least one of the children is a registered pupil at the school.

(3) The Secretary of State may by order provide that, in circumstances specified in the order, subsections (1) and (1A) do[^c-ae5bc978ff4b02f0cc897f9403c099b7] not apply in relation to early years provision.

(4) The circumstances specified in an order under subsection (3) may relate to one or more of the following matters (among others)—

- (a) the person providing the early years provision;
- (b) the child or children for whom it is provided;
- (c) the nature of the early years provision;
- (d) the premises on which it is provided;
- (e) the times during which it is provided;
- (f) the arrangements under which it is provided.

(5) A person commits an offence if, without reasonable excuse, he provides early years provision in contravention of subsection (1) or (1A)[^c-1c949570aa79b966ecee49406b502a51].

(6) A person guilty of an offence under subsection (5) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
