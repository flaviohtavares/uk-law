# Childcare Act 2006, section 89

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2024-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 89. Fees[^c-f3c02f7263ee6c46d1afbc0314d6dee2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Regulations may require persons registered under any of Chapters 2 to 4 in the early years register or the general childcare register[^c-9dc0f60d544a60a67553f5e8d21363f5] to pay to the Chief Inspector at or by prescribed times fees of the prescribed amounts in respect of the discharge by the Chief Inspector of his functions under this Part.

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) may prescribe circumstances in which—

- (a) the amount of a fee payable under the regulations may be varied in accordance with the regulations;
- (b) a fee payable under the regulations may be waived.
