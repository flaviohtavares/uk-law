# Companies Act 2006, section 1109

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1109. Transliteration of names and addresses: voluntary transliteration into Roman characters[^c20604611][^c20619091]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a name or address is or has been delivered to the registrar in a permitted form using other than Roman characters, the company (or other body) to which the document relates[^c20614281] may deliver to the registrar a transliteration into Roman characters.

(2) The power of the registrar to impose requirements as to the form and manner of delivery includes power to impose requirements as to the identification of the original document and the delivery of the transliteration in a form and manner enabling it to be associated with the original.
