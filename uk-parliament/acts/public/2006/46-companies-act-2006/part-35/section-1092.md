# Companies Act 2006, section 1092

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1092. Issue of process for production of records kept by the registrar[^c18906941][^c20604481][^c20619071]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No process for compelling the production of a record kept by the registrar shall issue from any court except with the permission of the court.

(2) Any such process shall bear on it a statement that it is issued with the permission of the court.
