# Companies Act 2006, section 1110E

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1110E. Disclosure to the registrar[^key-4ced1b47f2d6e07fac12d46f183ffc99][^key-22da977a74fe18f02009f029b9cfb833][^key-4b4644097c99afcb8ff3075833ea0ef5]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any person may disclose information to the registrar for the purposes of the exercise of any of the registrar’s functions.
