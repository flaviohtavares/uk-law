# Companies Act 2006, section 1062

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1062. The registrar's official seal[^c20603911][^c20618791]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The registrar shall have an official seal for the authentication of documents in connection with the performance of the registrar's functions.
