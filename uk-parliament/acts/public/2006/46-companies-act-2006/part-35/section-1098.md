# Companies Act 2006, section 1098

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1098. Public notice of removal of certain material from the register[^c19692881][^c19692851][^key-9a1c937076be99aa73340b27d64d6187]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The registrar must cause to be published—

- (a) in the Gazette, or
- (b) in accordance with section 1116 (alternative means of giving public notice),

notice of the removal from the register of an enhanced disclosure document[^key-c4e6978360936642aab309421f3cb7e5] (see section 1078) or of any material derived from such a document.

(2) The notice must state the name and registered number of the company, the description of document and the date of receipt.
