# Companies Act 2006, section 1062A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1062A. Analysis of information for the purposes of crime prevention or detection[^c-188e79355328c61041868877d6f7f85b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The registrar must carry out such analysis of information within the registrar’s possession as the registrar considers appropriate for the purposes of preventing or detecting crime.

(2) See also section 1110F (which, among other things, allows the registrar to disclose information to other public authorities).
