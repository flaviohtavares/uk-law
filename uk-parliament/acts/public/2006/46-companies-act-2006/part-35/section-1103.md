# Companies Act 2006, section 1103

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1103. Documents to be drawn up and delivered in English[^c19692541][^c18908341][^c20604551][^c-e87ee998d097fe20f7da65c8e84129d3][^c-cc1eae72fc13cdec81f6bf11a77c1f74][^c-4022001a0aa9b8cc060a56cdce7c53f3][^c-c59157e8e7ecf080acf5175b63b9d7ee]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The general rule is that all documents required to be delivered to the registrar must be drawn up and delivered in English.

(2) This is subject to—

- section 1104 (documents relating to Welsh companies) and
- section 1105 (documents that may be drawn up and delivered in other languages).
