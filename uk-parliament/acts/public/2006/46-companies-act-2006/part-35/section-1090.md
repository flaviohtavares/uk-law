## 1090. Form and manner in which copies to be provided[^c19692821][^c19692811][^c18906901][^c20619051][^M_C_e5229ead-c6c4-4b0d-faec-3eaca2225cf8][^key-ba21a89787335a78ce2e4609451ae38c][^key-eafcf3137aeb2cf3ed63ff8012d15266][^key-157342b37e8681f73e7a1f5fbca70ec7][^key-95f129779d7ebf9734bdf5bc0f4331a4][^key-13eec0d48e7eff76c8c30aca530f40d3]

The registrar may determine the form and manner in which copies are to be provided under section 1086.
