# Companies Act 2006, section 382

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 382. Companies qualifying as small: general[^key-03581c00420f53f102d9b36eb3a3d6be][^key-ecfb06268564ce710a5438ebc6c48111][^key-df98015af57f2b5f6c569f1a3f9331db]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A company qualifies as small in relation to its first financial year if the qualifying conditions are met in that year.

(1A)[^key-c73f6017c578f747ccb025271e6c400a] Subject to subsection (2), a company qualifies as small in relation to a subsequent financial year if the qualifying conditions are met in that year.

(2)[^key-943a7793d900477bb2313bba4a1a35c9] In relation to a subsequent financial year, where on its balance sheet date a company meets or ceases to meet the qualifying conditions, that affects its qualification as a small company only if it occurs in two consecutive financial years.

(3) The qualifying conditions are met by a company in a year in which it satisfies two or more of the following requirements—

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| 1\. Turnover | Not more than £15 million[^key-2d492562c6b6f5d86b05a6b2feaf22ff] |
| 2\. Balance sheet total | Not more than £7.5 million[^key-4e81185cad6d294e2bd488e9bfeddfd4] |
| 3\. Number of employees | Not more than 50 |

(4)[^key-b41444d7c0475f93542a6180a9350578] For a period that is a company's financial year but not in fact a year the maximum figures for turnover must be proportionately adjusted.

(5)[^key-b41444d7c0475f93542a6180a9350578] The balance sheet total means the aggregate of the amounts shown as assets in the company's balance sheet.

(6)[^key-b41444d7c0475f93542a6180a9350578] The number of employees means the average number of persons employed by the company in the year, determined as follows—

- (a) find for each month in the financial year the number of persons employed under contracts of service by the company in that month (whether throughout the month or not),
- (b) add together the monthly totals, and
- (c) divide by the number of months in the financial year.

(7) This section is subject to section 383 (companies qualifying as small: parent companies).
