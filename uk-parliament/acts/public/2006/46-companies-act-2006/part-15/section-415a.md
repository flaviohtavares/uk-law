# Companies Act 2006, section 415A

## 415A. Directors' report: small companies exemption[^key-c95b4b808c6a78d163c68c51364fecaf][^c20617021][^M_C_f310395c-32ae-4a68-f312-c9b9c0ba619f][^c19883651]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A company is entitled to small companies exemption in relation to the directors' report for a financial year if—

- (a) it is entitled to prepare accounts for the year in accordance with the small companies regime, or
- (b) it would be so entitled but for being or having been a member of an ineligible group.

(2)[^key-c4b789467607f299d9ad48537457e0b7] The exemption is relevant to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4b789467607f299d9ad48537457e0b7]

- section 416(3) (contents of report: statement of amount recommended by way of dividend), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4b789467607f299d9ad48537457e0b7]and[^key-bb0a164fd132f447a298459b6e082afd] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4b789467607f299d9ad48537457e0b7]
- [^key-bfbe24287bf9ad7b1bbc4840984cdc3e]...
- sections 444 to 446 (filing obligations of different descriptions of company). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c4b789467607f299d9ad48537457e0b7]

(2) The exemption is relevant to section 416(3) (contents of report: statement of amount recommended by way of dividend).
