# Companies Act 2006, section 441

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 441. Duty to file accounts and reports with the registrar[^c19893181][^c19893091][^c-399d4fe5700d23f0a5a75b8fd03c4f17][^c19894891][^c19894951][^c19894971][^c20613341][^c20613351][^c-fcac9059691376c74e264811c62b598f][^c-5ac6eb138f947ab6eb816e6903621f74]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The directors of a company must deliver to the registrar for each financial year the accounts and reports required by—

- section 443A (filing obligations of micro-entities),[^c-cc4801573b7e87e8e0a9a6bf69fb6e12]
- section 444 (filing obligations of companies subject to small companies regime *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-db9e8cf39c79a4d489dc07d19074eaea] small companies other than micro-entities),
- section 444A (filing obligations of companies entitled to small companies exemption in relation to directors' report),[^c19894991] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a4362c432e8595b1ed4f8fb1a6053bed]
- section 445 (filing obligations of medium-sized companies),
- section 446 (filing obligations of unquoted companies), or
- section 447 (filing obligations of quoted companies).

(2)[^c21737481] This is subject to—

- section 448 (unlimited companies exempt from filing obligations), and
- section 448A (dormant subsidiaries exempt from filing obligations).
