# Companies Act 2006, section 444A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 444A. Filing obligations of companies entitled to small companies exemption in relation to directors' report (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-fcac9059691376c74e264811c62b598f][^c19893231][^c19894941][^c19892861][^key-5869171d3be1a6cc17f64d6a72927bea][^key-5869171d3be1a6cc17f64d6a72927bea]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^key-016cd5a86776d76aca52b18fc6b93113]

(1) The directors of a company that is entitled to small companies exemption in relation to the directors' report for a financial year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5869171d3be1a6cc17f64d6a72927bea]

- (a) must deliver to the registrar a copy of the company's annual accounts for that year, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5869171d3be1a6cc17f64d6a72927bea]
- (b) may also deliver to the registrar a copy of the directors' report. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5869171d3be1a6cc17f64d6a72927bea]

(2) The directors must also deliver to the registrar a copy of the auditor's report on the accounts (and any directors' report) that it delivers. This does not apply if the company is exempt from audit and the directors have taken advantage of that exception. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5869171d3be1a6cc17f64d6a72927bea]

(3) The copies of the balance sheet and directors' report delivered to the registrar under this section must state the name of the person who signed it on behalf of the board. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5869171d3be1a6cc17f64d6a72927bea]

(4)[^c20592621] The copy of the auditor's report delivered to the registrar under this section must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5869171d3be1a6cc17f64d6a72927bea]

- (a) state the name of the auditor and (where the auditor is a firm) the name of the person who signed it as senior statutory auditor, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5869171d3be1a6cc17f64d6a72927bea]
- (b) if the conditions in section 506 (circumstances in which names may be omitted) are met, state that a resolution has been passed and notified to the Secretary of State in accordance with that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5869171d3be1a6cc17f64d6a72927bea]

(4A)[^key-2f9e00f41af165158c4ef55672db23a7] If more than one person is appointed as auditor, the reference in subsection (4)(a) to the name of the auditor is to be read as a reference to the names of all the auditors. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5869171d3be1a6cc17f64d6a72927bea]

(5) This section does not apply to companies within section 444 (filing obligations of companies subject to the small companies regime). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5869171d3be1a6cc17f64d6a72927bea]
