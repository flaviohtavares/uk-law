# Companies Act 2006, section 419A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 419A. Approval and signing of separate corporate governance statement[^c-cf4ebce29fd685e932ca184707f8da40][^c20617061][^c20592481]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any separate corporate governance statement must be approved by the board of directors and signed on behalf of the board by a director or the secretary of the company.
