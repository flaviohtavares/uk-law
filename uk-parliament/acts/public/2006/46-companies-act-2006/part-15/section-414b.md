# Companies Act 2006, section 414B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46

## 414B. Strategic report: small companies exemption[^c-dad63577b1944f4e84da593288ed18df]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^c-bc472f1eab209e28ccdd03d205cc9047]

A company is entitled to the[^c-6242268a3dfd78938c02f14e5d5cc1b8] small companies exemption in relation to the strategic report for a financial year if—

- (a) it is entitled to prepare accounts for the year in accordance with the small companies regime, or
- (b) it would be so entitled but for being or having been a member of an ineligible group.
