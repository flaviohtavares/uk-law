## 444. Filing obligations of companies subject to small companies regime (affected by repeal, see note) Filing obligations of small companies other than micro-entities[^c19893221][^c19893131][^c19894931][^c19684041][^key-fcac9059691376c74e264811c62b598f][^M_C_98f5950d-cd21-45f3-bf1d-eac76e84f20b][^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^key-016cd5a86776d76aca52b18fc6b93113]

(1) The directors of a company subject to the small companies regime— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

- (a) must deliver to the registrar for each financial year a copy of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6] the balance sheet[^key-9239a58e8bde024283e65ee533353eb4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6] drawn up as at the last day of that year, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]
- (b) may also deliver to the registrar— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]
  - (i) a copy of the company's profit and loss account for that year, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6] a copy of the directors' report for that year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

(2) Where the directors deliver to the registrar a copy of the company’s profit and loss account under subsection (1)(b)(i),[^key-6eb5ea910e262fbc1e0bad052eabe4bf] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6] the directors must also deliver to the registrar a copy of the auditor's report on *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6] the accounts (and any directors' report) that it delivers[^c19895031] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6].

This does not apply if the company is exempt from audit and the directors have taken advantage of that exemption. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

(2A)[^key-ae5dddd5ce7052348dbb3f6284f12e0e] Where the balance sheet or profit and loss account is abridged pursuant to paragraph 1A of Schedule 1 to the Small Companies and Groups (Accounts and Directors’ Report) Regulations ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6] [S.I. 2008/409](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/409) ) , the directors must also deliver to the registrar a statement by the company that all the members of the company have consented to the abridgement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

(3)[^key-63212721f86fd74f10bcb0037e538367] [^key-b64515cfa6f373458a9ec58b15343864] ... the copies of accounts and reports delivered to the registrar must be copies of the company’s annual accounts and reports. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

(3A)[^key-9db54e51fb560e97d72c2ea736fbd2c4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3B)[^key-9db54e51fb560e97d72c2ea736fbd2c4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-2d868cf985676a5f67153f67cc6c30d7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) Where the directors of a company subject to the small companies regime *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6] [^key-1a3a1ee41e9c2284fc3da7e48a4d81ee]...—

- (a) do not deliver to the registrar a copy of the company's profit and loss account, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]
- (b) do not deliver to the registrar a copy of the directors' report, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

the copy of the balance sheet delivered to the registrar must contain in a prominent position a statement that the company's annual accounts and reports have been delivered in accordance with the provisions applicable to companies subject to the small companies regime. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

(5A)[^key-37846de3cd4c55ab9c0ec9b882265ba1] Subject to subsection (5C), where the directors of a company subject to the small companies regime do not deliver to the registrar a copy of the company’s profit and loss account— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

- (a) the copy of the balance sheet delivered to the registrar must disclose that fact, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]
- (b) unless the company is exempt from audit and the directors have taken advantage of that exemption, the notes to the balance sheet delivered must satisfy the requirements in subsection (5B). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

(5B) Those requirements are that the notes to the balance sheet must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

- (a) state whether the auditor’s report was qualified or unqualified, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]
- (b) where that report was qualified, disclose the basis of the qualification (reproducing any statement under section 498(2)(a) or (b) or section 498(3), if applicable), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]
- (c) where that report was unqualified, include a reference to any matters to which the auditor drew attention by way of emphasis, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]
- (d) state— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]
  - (i) the name of the auditor and (where the auditor is a firm) the name of the person who signed the auditor’s report as senior statutory auditor, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6] if the conditions in section 506 (circumstances in which names may be omitted) are met, that a resolution has been passed and notified to the Secretary of State in accordance with that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

(5C) Subsection (5A) does not apply in relation to a company if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

- (a) the company qualifies as a micro-entity (see sections 384A and 384B) in relation to a financial year, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]
- (b) the company’s accounts are prepared for that year in accordance with any of the micro-entity provisions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

(6) The copies of the balance sheet and any directors' report delivered to the registrar under this section must state the name of the person who signed it on behalf of the board. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

(7) The copy of the auditor's report delivered to the registrar under this section must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

- (a) state the name of the auditor and (where the auditor is a firm) the name of the person who signed it as senior statutory auditor, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]
- (b) if the conditions in section 506 (circumstances in which names may be omitted) are met, state that a resolution has been passed and notified to the Secretary of State in accordance with that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

(8)[^key-21e84463bd8687867cc6629b1db79b02] If more than one person is appointed as auditor, the references in subsections (5B)(d)(i) and (7)(a) to the name of the auditor are to be read as references to the names of all the auditors. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f7e8ef59c088348ad30a137086b1c1d6]

(1) The directors of a company that is subject to the small companies regime in relation to a financial year, or that would be so subject but for being or having been a member of an ineligible group, must deliver to the registrar a copy of—

- (a) the company’s annual accounts, and
- (b) the directors’ report.

(2) The directors must also deliver to the registrar a copy of the auditor’s report on those accounts (and on the directors’ report).

This does not apply if the company is exempt from audit and the directors have taken advantage of that exemption.

(3) The copies of the balance sheet and directors’ report delivered to the registrar under this section must state the name of the person who signed it on behalf of the board.

(4) The copy of the auditor’s report delivered to the registrar under this section must—

- (a) state the name of the auditor and (where the auditor is a firm) the name of the person who signed it as senior statutory auditor, or
- (b) if the conditions in section 506 (circumstances in which names may be omitted) are met, state that a resolution has been passed and notified to the Secretary of State in accordance with that section.

(5) If more than one person is appointed as auditor, the reference in subsection (4)(a) to the name of the auditor is to be read as a reference to the names of all the auditors.

(6) This section does not apply to companies within section 443A (filing obligations of companies that qualify as micro-entities).
