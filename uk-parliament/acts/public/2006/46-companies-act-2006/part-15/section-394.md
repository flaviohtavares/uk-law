# Companies Act 2006, section 394

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 394. Duty to prepare individual accounts[^c20613121][^c20613161][^c-ecfb06268564ce710a5438ebc6c48111][^c-958faf4bd0d97e262c63dfd254f8cb99]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^c-016cd5a86776d76aca52b18fc6b93113]

The directors of every company must prepare accounts for the company for each of its financial years unless the company is exempt from that requirement under section 394A[^c21737441].

Those accounts are referred to as the company's “individual accounts”.
