# Companies Act 2006, section 384B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 384B. Companies excluded from being treated as micro-entities[^c-ae00493d12d46f2701fe378393a10498][^c-9b8d54bfccd677426a882207af0b9411][^c-338337304d3958064e1060c033e705f2]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The micro-entity provisions do not apply in relation to a company’s accounts for a particular financial year if the company [^c-4fd0d8efcd6dd34e8ffad1aa37752e64]... at any time within that year—

- (a) was[^c-ee0e25ffb0d2d824902c847ead4e1e32] a company excluded from the small companies regime by virtue of section 384,
- (b) would have been[^c-286c4cf36f242ecc56a42ddeb64a139d] an investment undertaking as defined in Article 2(14) of Directive 2013/34/ EU of 26 June 2013 on the annual financial statements etc. of certain types of undertakings were the United Kingdom a member State[^c-e95719f65827fc8174fa20878f12b746],
- (c) would have been[^c-e1f56044e67f37426ee37607afcb2168] a financial holding undertaking as defined in Article 2(15) of that Directive were the United Kingdom a member State[^c-3261f88e44635eb5ded9d94cb64e210e],
- (d)[^c-60bbec7d7712d661a9dd66036b6869bf] a credit institution within the meaning given by Article 4(1)(1) of Regulation (EU) No. 575/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council, which is a CRR firm within the meaning of Article 4(1)(2A) of that Regulation,[^c-5f7ec2236808fc479daf963f556c15ea]
- (e) would have been[^c-b752784ffe180f6d522f2ef8c9dd9416] an insurance undertaking as defined in Article 2(1) of Council [Directive 91/674/EEC](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/1991/0674) of 19 December 1991 on the annual accounts of insurance undertakings were the United Kingdom a member State[^c-5ae69ea42511222f833ced042f818881], or
- (f)[^c-c4da0ac633d66ee29bd6e374c5803b2d] was[^c-5bd74bc7375528a8a12c415671044079] a charity.

(2) The micro-entity provisions also do not apply in relation to a company’s accounts for a financial year if —

- (a) the company is a parent company which prepares group accounts for that year as permitted by section 399(4)[^c-cb3c2ea95dca880633a738c9f6d2fead], or
- (b) the company is not a parent company but its accounts are included in consolidated group accounts for that year.
