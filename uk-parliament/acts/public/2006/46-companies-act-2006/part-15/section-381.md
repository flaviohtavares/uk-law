# Companies Act 2006, section 381

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 381. Companies subject to the small companies regime[^key-03581c00420f53f102d9b36eb3a3d6be][^key-ecfb06268564ce710a5438ebc6c48111]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The small companies regime [^c19881591]. . . applies to a company for a financial year in relation to which the company—

- (a) qualifies as small (see sections 382 and 383), and
- (b) is not excluded from the regime (see section 384).
