# Companies Act 2006, section 436

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 436. Meaning of “publication” in relation to accounts and reports[^c19887131][^c19887221][^c-5b0d30286d51b591f2eb1f40d0434213][^c19886991][^c19887011][^c20598541][^c20598551][^c20598561][^c20598571][^c-60504bf6a9a26fdc6f079589576080e8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section has effect for the purposes of—

- section 433 (name of signatory to be stated in published copies of accounts and reports),
- section 434 (requirements in connection with publication of statutory accounts), and
- section 435 (requirements in connection with publication of non-statutory accounts).

(2)[^c19887031][^c19887051][^c-4736503422cae6927b7d827833186dae] For the purposes of those sections a company is regarded as publishing a document if it publishes, issues or circulates it or otherwise makes it available for public inspection in a manner calculated to invite members of the public generally, or any class of members of the public, to read it.
