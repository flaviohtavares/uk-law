# Companies Act 2006, section 115

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 115. Index of members[^M_F_32097bcf-1d10-4b5d-da62-14d6beb1b863]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Every company having more than 50 members must keep an index of the names of the members of the company, unless the register of members is in such a form as to constitute in itself an index. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_32097bcf-1d10-4b5d-da62-14d6beb1b863]

(2) The company must make any necessary alteration in the index within 14 days after the date on which any alteration is made in the register of members. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_32097bcf-1d10-4b5d-da62-14d6beb1b863]

(3) The index must contain, in respect of each member, a sufficient indication to enable the account of that member in the register to be readily found. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_32097bcf-1d10-4b5d-da62-14d6beb1b863]

(4) The index must be at all times kept available for inspection at the same place as the register of members. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_32097bcf-1d10-4b5d-da62-14d6beb1b863]

(4A)[^M_F_9cb78552-c7a0-4d9e-aade-9cb3d26c4d9c] Subsection (4) is subject to any restriction imposed by regulations under section 120A (protected material). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_32097bcf-1d10-4b5d-da62-14d6beb1b863]

(5) If default is made in complying with this section, an offence is committed by— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_32097bcf-1d10-4b5d-da62-14d6beb1b863]

- (a) the company, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_32097bcf-1d10-4b5d-da62-14d6beb1b863]
- (b) every officer of the company who is in default. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_32097bcf-1d10-4b5d-da62-14d6beb1b863]

(6) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale and, for continued contravention, a daily default fine not exceeding one-tenth of level 3 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_32097bcf-1d10-4b5d-da62-14d6beb1b863]
