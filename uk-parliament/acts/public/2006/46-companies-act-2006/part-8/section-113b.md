# Companies Act 2006, section 113B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46

## 113B. Required information about members: corporate members and firms[^c-2bee0a678fac62c933b326f672a4edd6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The required information about a member that is a body corporate, or a firm that is a legal person under the law by which it is governed, is—

- (a) corporate or firm name;
- (b) a service address.
