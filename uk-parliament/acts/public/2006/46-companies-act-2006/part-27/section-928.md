# Companies Act 2006, section 928

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46

## 928. Approval of articles of new transferee company (division)

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-05acb2118caa67fef513914c1afff0e9] The articles of every new transferee company, or a draft of them, must be approved by ordinary resolution of the transferor company.

(2)[^key-953889814f6f69b9af35d459ceedc13a] Subsection (1) does not apply in the case of a compromise or arrangement to be sanctioned under Part 26A.
