# Companies Act 2006, section 941

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 941. Meaning of “liabilities” and “property”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**liabilities**” includes duties;
- “**property**” includes property, rights and powers of every description.
