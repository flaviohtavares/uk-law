# Companies Act 2006, section 922

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 922. Approval of members of companies involved in the division

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The scheme[^c-effedf4ae6427ae3b76109a33085a9c3] must be approved by a majority in number, representing 75% in value, of each class of members of each of the companies involved in the division, present and voting either in person or by proxy at a meeting.

(2) This requirement is subject to sections 931, 931A[^c-d7b4e3af531ad899aed932252c40bbb4] and 932 (circumstances in which meeting of members not required).
