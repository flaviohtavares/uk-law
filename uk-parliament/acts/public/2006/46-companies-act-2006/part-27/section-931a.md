# Companies Act 2006, section 931A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>

## 931A. Other circumstances in which meeting of members of transferor company not required (division)[^c-8ee885bb38fefa83a30db53a15fbd63c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the case of a compromise or arrangement to be sanctioned under Part 26A, it is not necessary for the scheme to be approved by the members of the transferor company.
