# Companies Act 2006, section 1179

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1179. Repeal of requirement that Secretary of State prepare annual report[^c18962061]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 729 of the Companies Act 1985 (annual report to Parliament by Secretary of State on matters within the Companies Acts) shall cease to have effect.
