# Companies Act 2006, section 1178

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1178. Repeal of requirement that certain companies publish periodical statement[^c18962031]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following provisions shall cease to have effect—

- section 720 of the Companies Act 1985 (c. 6) (certain companies to publish periodical statement), and
- Schedule 23 to that Act (form of statement under section 720).
