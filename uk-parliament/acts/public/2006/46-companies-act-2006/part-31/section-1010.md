# Companies Act 2006, section 1010

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1010. Withdrawal of application[^key-b7ee5108f25df87dc609ac61990623cc]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An application under section 1003 is withdrawn by notice to the registrar.
