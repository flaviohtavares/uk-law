# Companies Act 2006, section 308

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 308. Manner in which notice to be given[^c18911331]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^key-060fbe922df595816e9e5552acee9a77]

Notice of a general meeting of a company must be given—

- (a) in hard copy form,
- (b) in electronic form, or
- (c) by means of a website (see section 309),

or partly by one such means and partly by another.
