# Companies Act 2006, section 360C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 360C. Meaning of “traded company”[^c20628501]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part, “ **traded company** ” means a company any shares of which—

- (a) carry rights to vote at general meetings, and
- (b) are admitted to trading on a UK regulated market or an EU regulated market[^c-88922a9cad7c65a745309b2dd98e5b08] by or with the consent of the company.
