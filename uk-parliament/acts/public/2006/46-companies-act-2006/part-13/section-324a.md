# Companies Act 2006, section 324A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 324A. Obligation of proxy to vote in accordance with instructions[^c20624781]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A proxy must vote in accordance with any instructions given by the member by whom the proxy is appointed.
