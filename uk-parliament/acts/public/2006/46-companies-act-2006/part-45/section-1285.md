# Companies Act 2006, section 1285

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1285. Extension of GB enactments relating to UK Societas[^key-005a66e5e13505e19adddaf053be3f25]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^key-ff4eaed48a5ae4fa8dab711c669b592c]

(1) The enactments in force in Great Britain relating to UK Societas[^key-bd8a101896b440d92318667bdf92c8bd] extend to Northern Ireland.

(2) The following enactments shall cease to have effect accordingly—

- (a) the European Public Limited-Liability Company Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 (SR 2004/417), and
- (b) the European Public Limited-Liability Company (Fees) Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2004 (SR 2004/418).

(3)[^key-ce1f610dda4d4be2a40f155c09d99d76] In this section “**UK Societas**” means a United Kingdom Societas within the meaning of Council Regulation 2157/2001/EC of 8 October 2001 on the Statute for a European Company.
