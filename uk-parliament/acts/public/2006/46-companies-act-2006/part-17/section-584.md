# Companies Act 2006, section 584

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 584. Public companies: shares taken by subscribers of memorandum[^c20590801][^c21177771]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Shares taken by a subscriber to the memorandum of a public company in pursuance of an undertaking of his in the memorandum, and any premium on the shares, must be paid up in cash.
