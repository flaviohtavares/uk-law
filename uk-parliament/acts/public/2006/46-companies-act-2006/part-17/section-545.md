# Companies Act 2006, section 545

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 545. Companies having a share capital[^c19667401]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

References in the Companies Acts to a company having a share capital are to a company that has power under its constitution to issue shares.
