## 595. Exception to valuation requirement: merger or division[^c21175821]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 593 (valuation of non-cash consideration) does not apply to the allotment of shares by a company as part of a scheme to which Part 27 (mergers and divisions of public companies) applies if—[^c21175751]

- (a) in the case of a scheme involving a merger, an expert's report is drawn up as required by section 909, or
- (b) in the case of a scheme involving a division, an expert's report is drawn up as required by section 924.
