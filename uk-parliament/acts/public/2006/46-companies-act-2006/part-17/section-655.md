# Companies Act 2006, section 655

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 655. Shares no bar to damages against company

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person is not debarred from obtaining damages or other compensation from a company by reason only of his holding or having held shares in the company or any right to apply or subscribe for shares or to be included in the company's register of members [^c-80582319888a8e0cf64aeab0df42e9a2]... in respect of shares.
