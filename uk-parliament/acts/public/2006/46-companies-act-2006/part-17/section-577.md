# Companies Act 2006, section 577

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46

## 577. Provisions about pre-emption not applicable to shares taken on formation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provisions of this Chapter have no application in relation to the taking of shares by the subscribers to the memorandum on the formation of the company.
