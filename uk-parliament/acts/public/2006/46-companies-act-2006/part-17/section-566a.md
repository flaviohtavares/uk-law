# Companies Act 2006, section 566A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>

## 566A. Exception to pre-emption right: companies in financial difficulty[^key-eab91a6d0eb1d52a959e8300c73af649]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 561(1) (existing shareholders’ right of pre-emption) does not apply to an allotment of equity securities that is carried out as part of a compromise or arrangement sanctioned in accordance with Part 26A (arrangements and reconstructions: companies in financial difficulty).
