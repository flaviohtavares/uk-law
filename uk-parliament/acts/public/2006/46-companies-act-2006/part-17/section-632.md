# Companies Act 2006, section 632

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 632. Variation of class rights: saving for court's powers under other provisions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in section 630 or 631 (variation of class rights) affects the power of the court under—

- section 98 (application to cancel resolution for public company to be re-registered as private),
- Part 26 (arrangements and reconstructions: general[^c-7d3e0056e0dca4f4f5ba538d757ce9ad])
- Part 26A (arrangements and reconstructions: companies in financial difficulty)[^c-353996c313818b85ede5adc09a07c7ee], or
- Part 30 (protection of members against unfair prejudice).
