# Companies Act 2006, section 558

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 558. When shares are allotted[^c19708391][^c19639761]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of the Companies Acts shares in a company are taken to be allotted when a person acquires the unconditional right to be included in the company's register of members [^c-0243aee7fa776f1c54c103d965e67c2e]... in respect of the shares.
