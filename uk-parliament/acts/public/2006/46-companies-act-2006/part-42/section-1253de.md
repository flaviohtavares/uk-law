# Companies Act 2006, section 1253DE

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1253DE. Transfer by means of inspection[^c20896461][^c19704071]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies in the case of a transfer of audit working papers and investigation reports[^c-0d49f7c53f51367d7451f5134bc5072e] if—

- (a) it is a transfer to an approved third country competent authority [^c-9e6075e9003239258ae32c742d2d099d]...,
- (b) it is a transfer under section 1253DA or 1253DB, and
- (c) it is to take place by means of an inspection in the United Kingdom by the authority.

(2) The Secretary of State must participate in the inspection.

(3) The inspection must be under the leadership of the Secretary of State unless the Secretary of State otherwise permits.
