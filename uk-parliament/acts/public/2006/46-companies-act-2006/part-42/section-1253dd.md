## 1253DD. Agreement of third country competent authority[^c20896461][^c19704071][^key-3cc4d20727060a7be34f2775fb8c2253]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where—

- (a) an approved third country competent authority makes a request to the Secretary of State for the transfer of audit working papers and investigation reports[^key-41d5909fb94c5ac1fe66776a97cd5ca3] which relate to the audit of the consolidated accounts of a group, and
- (b) the audit working papers and investigation reports that are the subject of the request—
  - (i) have been created by the auditor of a subsidiary that is located in an equivalent third country or transitional third country[^key-d4fe9d353eaed52b7395089ab8aa4607] in relation to the audit of that subsidiary, and
  - (ii) are in the possession of a statutory auditor.

(2) In the case of a transfer by the Secretary of State under section 1253DA, the transfer must not take place unless the third country competent authority[^key-602ca5afcda2141595f0f5bd6dfa1861] responsible for the auditor of the subsidiary has given its express agreement to the transfer.

(3) In the case of a transfer by a statutory auditor under section 1253DB, the Secretary of State must not approve the transfer unless the third country competent authority[^key-caa729d2fa5bf5b710de146e2f3cc8e5] responsible for the auditor of the subsidiary has given its express agreement to the transfer.
