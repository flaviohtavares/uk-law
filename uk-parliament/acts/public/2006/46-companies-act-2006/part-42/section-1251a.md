# Companies Act 2006, section 1251A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1251A. Duty of the Secretary of State to report on inspections[^c19703111]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State must, at least once in every calendar year, publish a report containing a summary of the results of inspections that are delivered to him—

- (a) by the Independent Supervisor under section 1229(5A);
- (b) by a recognised supervisory body under paragraph 13(12)[^key-0c2c3fa9d81cb23d906ebb9c9847c75a] of Schedule 10.
