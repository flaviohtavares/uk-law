## 1241. Meaning of “registered third country auditor” and “UK-traded third country company”[^c18959541][^c19702541][^c-7992ae50ffef387c453ff573f43d5779]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part—

- [^c19702561]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- “**registered third country auditor**” means a third country auditor who is entered in the register kept in accordance with regulations under section 1239(1).

(2) In this Part “[^c19702581]UK-traded third country company[^c-abe2827e1a693e3efc53ed5500041ff5]” means a body corporate—

- (a) which is incorporated or formed under the law of a third country[^c19702611] ,
- (b) whose transferable securities are admitted to trading on a UK regulated market[^M_F_ee19e8f2-a642-43ee-dc4d-1e82715dfb92] [^c-e44f7dcafcc05481b03493cfbad0df52]..., and
- (c) which has not been excluded, or is not of a description of bodies corporate which has been excluded, from this definition by an order made by the Secretary of State.

(3)[^c-79718bee75539c70da72d2db69ce5de4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) An order under this section is subject to negative resolution procedure.
