# Companies Act 2006, section 1223A

>

## 1223A. Notification of matters relevant to approved third country competent authorities[^c19700311][^key-dd8dd79bfcea79fcac3c73fa98cf7b39]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-c977ca2c39e455db27e3379aa943367a] A recognised supervisory body must notify the Secretary of State—

- (a) if a notifiable person becomes eligible for appointment as a statutory auditor, unless the notifiable person is an individual;
- (b) if a notifiable person’s eligibility for appointment as a statutory auditor is withdrawn;
- (c) of the reasons for any such withdrawal.

(2) A recognised supervisory body must also notify the Secretary of State of any reasonable grounds it has for suspecting that—

- (a) a person has contravened the law of the United Kingdom, or an equivalent third country or transitional third country[^key-0cba04df8a477fb4d1383e129665d7ca], and
- (b) the act or omission constituting that contravention took place on the territory of an equivalent third country or transitional third country[^key-7e6a3a0f817ae437570220d9cc95ac0e].

(3) In this section “ **notifiable person** ” means a member of the recognised supervisory body in question—

- (a) who is also a third country auditor that has been approved by an approved third country competent authority[^key-6e104c10fe7492f530f9012ae898ba2a]; and
- (b) in respect of whom the approved third country[^key-48253fc2d6a17b89ec8423528f18ea66] competent authority is not the recognised supervisory body itself.
