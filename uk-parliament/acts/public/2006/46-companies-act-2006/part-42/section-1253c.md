# Companies Act 2006, section 1253C

## 1253C. Notification to approved third country competent authorities...[^c19704041][^key-3dc1c0b710484efbfcb00b18164fe8f6][^key-355a88691b3b008ecd847683609859ef]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-e88c1aab6704a59d7503c67fdd670426] If the Secretary of State receives notice from a recognised supervisory body under section 1223A(1) (notification of matters relevant to approved third country competent authorities[^key-21d130e86376dbe8f2c3a1e7f954cfbb]) that—

- (a) a person has become eligible for appointment as a statutory auditor, or
- (b) a person’s eligibility for appointment as a statutory auditor has been withdrawn,

the Secretary of State must notify the relevant approved third country competent authority[^key-6abca33acf28c161a8bb08f2dbb9ebe6].

(2) In subsection (1) “the relevant approved third country competent authority[^key-5533df68554198e42d612e5d76b0685a]” means the approved third country competent authority which has approved the person concerned [^key-adab288012d9dc7fa46cb3acf9a2780d]... to carry out audits of annual accounts or consolidated accounts [^key-51546a97414e77877df712b4dbaeee30]....

(3) The notification under subsection (1) must include the name of the person concerned and , in a case where a person’s eligibility for appointment as a statutory auditor has been withdrawn, the reasons for the withdrawal[^key-31c8cb3aadb3ebea79282f1cc2373084].

(4) The Secretary of State must notify the relevant approved third country competent authority[^key-f1b7dffe6c088c68d59a3c301bf9943c] if he has reasonable grounds for suspecting that—

- (a) a person has contravened the law of the United Kingdom, or any equivalent third country or transitional third country, relating to audit[^key-1338db87c54ba9a22bd7d998e1dfcf16], and
- (b) the act or omission constituting that contravention took place on the territory of an equivalent third country or transitional third country[^key-a59e921d831509fc388f94417f54de48].

(5) In subsection (4) “the relevant approved third country competent authority[^key-fb08fd43e5ebc18f788ee1d31e43eb2b]” means the approved third country competent authority for the country or territory[^key-afcdf8bd51a47b04733f6eb12e0104c8] in which the suspected contravention took place.

(6) The notification under subsection (4) must include the name of the person concerned and the grounds for the Secretary of State's suspicion.
