## 1253A. Requests to foreign competent authorities[^c19704041]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State may request from [^key-03f4b58ee7a00dacf4367d26ba58d986]... a third country competent authority such assistance, information or investigation as he may reasonably require in connection with the exercise of his functions under this Part.
