# Companies Act 2006, section 1211

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1211. Eligibility for appointment as a statutory auditor: overview

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person is eligible for appointment as a statutory auditor only if the person is so eligible—

- (a) by virtue of Chapter 2 (individuals and firms), or
- (b) by virtue of Chapter 3 (Comptroller and Auditor General, etc).
