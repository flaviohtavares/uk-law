# Companies Act 2006, section 849

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 849. Restriction on application of unrealised profits[^c19684651]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A company must not apply an unrealised profit in paying up debentures or any amounts unpaid on its issued shares.
