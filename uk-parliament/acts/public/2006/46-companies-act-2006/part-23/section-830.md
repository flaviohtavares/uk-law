# Companies Act 2006, section 830

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 830. Distributions to be made only out of profits available for the purpose[^c19684381]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A company may only make a distribution out of profits available for the purpose.

(2) A company's profits available for distribution are its accumulated, realised profits, so far as not previously utilised by distribution or capitalisation, less its accumulated, realised losses, so far as not previously written off in a reduction or reorganisation of capital duly made.

(3) Subsection (2) has effect subject to sections 832 , 833A[^key-da97e00b09c67ecba1315477a965bac7] and 835 (investment companies and Solvency 2 insurance companies[^key-84d6185fce8dace15489ffb32c5e3673]).
