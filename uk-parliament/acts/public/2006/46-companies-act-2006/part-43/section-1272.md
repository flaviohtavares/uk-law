## 1272. Transparency obligations and related matters: minor and consequential amendments

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 15 to this Act makes minor and consequential amendments in connection with the provision made by this Part.

(2) In that Schedule–

- Part 1 contains amendments of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (c. 8);
- Part 2 contains amendments of the Companies (Audit, Investigations and Community Enterprise) Act 2004 (c. 27).
