# Companies Act 2006, section 853BA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 853BA. Duty to confirm lawful purpose[^c-2fcaca5c7f45362cdcb9528ab0a886a4][^c-1f4fc7b4fcb2fac6807c00b55c57a549][^c-dab1cdfb2b6132781635ad1a36cc2f04][^c-ea9d2d7fdd0e2a9fd896dc8bfade6b46]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a company makes a confirmation statement it must at the same time deliver to the registrar a statement that the intended future activities of the company are lawful.
