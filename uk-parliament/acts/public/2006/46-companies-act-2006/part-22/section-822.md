# Companies Act 2006, section 822

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 822. Interest in shares: family interests[^c18920301]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) For the purposes of this Part a person is taken to be interested in shares in which—

- (a) his spouse or civil partner, or
- (b) any infant child or step-child of his,

is interested.

(2) In relation to Scotland “**infant**” means a person under the age of 18 years.
