# Companies Act 2006, section 1047

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1047. Registered name of overseas company[^c20261571]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulations under section 1046 (duty to register particulars) must require an overseas company that is required to register particulars to register its name.

(2) This may be—

- (a) the company's corporate name (that is, its name under the law of the country or territory in which it is incorporated) or
- (b) an alternative name specified in accordance with section 1048.

(3)[^key-7d6f23b2a073bd83996ca20cae0f3014] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) [^key-f558851652605bf79218315f3b216860]... The following provisions of Part 5 (a company's name) apply in relation to the registration of the name of an overseas company—

- (a) section 53 (prohibited names);
- (aa)[^key-d95584df840f46205e982203c58ed3c1] section 53A (names for criminal purposes);
- (b) sections 54 to 56 (sensitive words and expressions);
- (bza)[^key-627c9531f151942c9e0a63a93747ef33] section 56A (names suggesting connection with foreign governments etc);
- (ba)[^key-5999b8ace4751c059eb774f60d5c1d32] section 57 (permitted characters etc);
- (bb)[^key-a7c5c47d0220a1a09c3e6cf2b1f88891] section 57A (names containing computer code);
- (bc)[^key-2051e05bc101d5d6e384e51d80f5a573] section 57B (restriction on re-registering name following direction).
- (bd)[^key-087169f2e114577baccb267e1cb0ac3d] section 57C (names that another company has been directed to change);
- (c) section 65 (inappropriate use of indications of company type or legal form);
- (d) sections 66 to 74 (similarity to other names);
- (e) section 75 (provision of misleading information etc);
- (f) section 76 (misleading indication of activities).
- (g)[^key-b9ca7be240c55bdce50a8a44005329de] section 76A (power to direct change of name used for criminal purposes);
- (h)[^key-de25b83c136fccc951c62eb7960e787e] section 76B (direction to change name wrongly registered);
- (i)[^key-cc85c6ec7b778d0fe4596a9f00dd0d8a] section 76C (registrar’s power to change name containing computer code);
- (j) section 76D (registrar’s power to change name for failure to comply with direction).

(5)[^key-5915e001d0de0a30768eca9936596766] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) Any reference in the provisions mentioned in subsection (4) [^key-9cfdd50d63784ec03fa2a2da73f42f37]... to a change of name shall be read as a reference to registration of a different name under section 1048.
