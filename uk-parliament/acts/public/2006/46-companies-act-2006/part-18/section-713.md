# Companies Act 2006, section 713

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 713. Requirements for payment out of capital

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A payment out of capital by a private company for the redemption or purchase of its own shares is not lawful unless the requirements of the following sections are met—

- section 714 (directors' statement and auditor's report);
- section 716 (approval by special resolution);
- section 719 (public notice of proposed payment);
- section 720 (directors' statement and auditor's report to be available for inspection).

(2) This is subject to section 720A and[^c21792791] to any order of the court under section 721 (power of court to extend period for compliance on application by persons objecting to payment).
