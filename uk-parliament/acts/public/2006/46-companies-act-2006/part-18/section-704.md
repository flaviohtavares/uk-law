# Companies Act 2006, section 704

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 704. No assignment of company's right to purchase own shares

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The rights of a company under a contract authorised under—

- (za)[^c21792751] section 693A (authority for off-market purchase for the purposes of or pursuant to an employees' share scheme),
- (a) section 694 (authority for off-market purchase), or
- (b) section 701 (authority for market purchase)

are not capable of being assigned.
