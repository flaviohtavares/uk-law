# Companies Act 2006, section 691

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 691. Payment for purchase of own shares

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A limited company may not purchase its own shares unless they are fully paid.

(2) Where a limited company purchases its own shares, the shares must be paid for on purchase.

(3)[^c21787211] But subsection (2) does not apply in a case where a private limited company is purchasing shares for the purposes of or pursuant to an employees' share scheme.
