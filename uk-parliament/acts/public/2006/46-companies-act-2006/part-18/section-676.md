# Companies Act 2006, section 676

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 676. Application of provisions to directors

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

For the purposes of this Chapter references to “employer” and “employee”, in the context of a pension scheme or employees' share scheme, shall be read as if a director of a company were employed by it.
