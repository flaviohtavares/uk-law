# Companies Act 2006, section 790B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46

## 790B. Companies to which this Part applies[^key-25bb38e9575e6451e141c16a39f97d46]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Part applies to companies other than—

- (a)[^key-2776bbfe5658e46566b8b0743a3045a1] companies with voting shares admitted to trading on a UK regulated market or an EU regulated market[^key-f371c8937579479c3603bf006358cd47], and
- (b) companies of any description specified by the Secretary of State by regulations.

(2) In deciding whether to specify a description of company, the Secretary of State is to have regard to the extent to which companies of that description are bound by disclosure and transparency rules (in the United Kingdom or elsewhere) which are contained in international standards and are equivalent to those applicable to companies referred to in subsection (1)(a)[^key-5780c6e6089b1ab80a87a139df90ff39] .

(3)[^key-410522ba9783447173ef93f121ef715d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Regulations under this section are subject to affirmative resolution procedure.

(5)[^key-a86424c0b439aa57ee5062a0f7bbf270] In this section—

- “voting shares” means shares carrying voting rights;
- “voting rights” means rights to vote at general meetings of the company in question, including rights that arise only in certain circumstances.
