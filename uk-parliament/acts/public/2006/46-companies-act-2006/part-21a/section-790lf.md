# Companies Act 2006, section 790LF

## 790LF. Duty to notify registrar when person ceases to have significant control[^key-068c01c0f0a0e297b864a24c15d67e52][^key-6780c977ffde3a9008df6f6e7b8d341d][^key-77330d37e1fc27737fb7170e5b780fe7][^key-25bb38e9575e6451e141c16a39f97d46]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A company must give a notice to the registrar if it—

- (a) has had confirmation that a person has ceased to be a registrable person or a registrable relevant legal entity in relation to it, and
- (b) has had confirmation of the date on which the person so ceased.

(2)[^key-7bbffe4838c305039be1fcfe865b800f] A notice under subsection (1) must state—

- (a) the person’s name,
- (b) the date on which the person ceased to be a registrable person or a registrable relevant legal entity in relation to the company, and
- (c) the date on which the company had confirmation as mentioned in subsection (1).

(3) A notice under subsection (1) must be given within the period of 14 days beginning with the day on which the company had confirmation as mentioned in that subsection.

(4)[^key-406f97830872c4d7dffebba4fe5194c8] In this section “**name**” means, in relation to an individual, their forename and surname.
