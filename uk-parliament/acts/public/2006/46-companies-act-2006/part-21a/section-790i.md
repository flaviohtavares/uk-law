# Companies Act 2006, section 790I

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 790I. Enforcement of disclosure requirements[^key-752ea6c80060394bfb21beb3c0b6167a][^key-fb5c9a067e3b29816257cf0999bb3307][^key-b4628131ea1d1b3d8ec36ea2ac5a4544][^key-25bb38e9575e6451e141c16a39f97d46]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 1B contains provisions for when a person (whether an individual or a legal entity) fails to comply with—[^key-44de92772c086e1c3c8ec644f307a28b]

(a) a notice under section 790D, 790DA, 790E or 790EA, or

(b) a duty under section 790G, 790H or 790HA.
