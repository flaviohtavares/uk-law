# Companies Act 2006, section 790CB

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 790CB. Duty to find out about persons with significant control[^c-b4628131ea1d1b3d8ec36ea2ac5a4544][^c-902764ee754cba9029d27b4d72aa25b7][^c-25bb38e9575e6451e141c16a39f97d46]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A company to which this Part applies must take reasonable steps to find out if there is anyone who is a registrable person or a registrable relevant legal entity in relation to the company and, if so, to identify them.
