# Companies Act 2006, section 790F

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 790F. Failure by company to comply with information duties[^c-752ea6c80060394bfb21beb3c0b6167a][^c-fb5c9a067e3b29816257cf0999bb3307][^c-b4628131ea1d1b3d8ec36ea2ac5a4544][^c-25bb38e9575e6451e141c16a39f97d46]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-8aa11cff31ad7e546e874fb957a3006e] If a company fails, without reasonable excuse, to comply with a duty under section 790CB, 790D, 790DA(3), 790E, 790EA, 790EB, 790EC, 790ED, 790EE or 790EF[^c-b3526ea85b1fbb2d416c163000c28435] to take steps or give a notice, an offence is committed by—

- (a) the company, and
- (b) every officer of the company who is in default.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable—

- (a) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction—
  - (i) in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding twelve months or a fine (or both);
  - (ii) in Scotland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding twelve months or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
  - (iii) in Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both).
