# Companies Act 2006, section 183

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 183. Offence of failure to declare interest[^c20597231][^c-89bc6c9ec9997e9afa588ca19aaf7ff2][^c-aade5f854be447d29468937ac3512719][^c-bb005100625034b2d1aa854df0101845][^c19877451][^c19877391][^c19877311]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A director who fails to comply with the requirements of section 182 (declaration of interest in existing transaction or arrangement) commits an offence.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable—

- (a) on conviction on indictment, to a fine;
- (b) on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum.
