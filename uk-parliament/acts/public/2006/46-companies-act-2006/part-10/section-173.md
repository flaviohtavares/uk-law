# Companies Act 2006, section 173

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 173. Duty to exercise independent judgment[^c19641881][^c20597161][^key-5f0dd66041994a15aee593304fae9938][^key-fc05d016211fde38dc7765021aed3735][^key-f8eade1256f89ad5d3015137e9e401aa][^c19876961][^c19877051][^c19877141]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A director of a company must exercise independent judgment.

(2) This duty is not infringed by his acting—

- (a) in accordance with an agreement duly entered into by the company that restricts the future exercise of discretion by its directors, or
- (b) in a way authorised by the company's constitution.
