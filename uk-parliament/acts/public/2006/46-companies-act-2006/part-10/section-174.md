# Companies Act 2006, section 174

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 174. Duty to exercise reasonable care, skill and diligence[^c19641891][^c20597171][^c-5f0dd66041994a15aee593304fae9938][^c-fc05d016211fde38dc7765021aed3735][^c-f8eade1256f89ad5d3015137e9e401aa][^c19876971][^c19877061][^c19877151]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A director of a company must exercise reasonable care, skill and diligence.

(2)[^c-ec9a9c2eac14578d7033aaca0b8367c1] This means the care, skill and diligence that would be exercised by a reasonably diligent person with—

- (a) the general knowledge, skill and experience that may reasonably be expected of a person carrying out the functions carried out by the director in relation to the company, and
- (b) the general knowledge, skill and experience that the director has.
