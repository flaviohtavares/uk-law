# Companies Act 2006, section 226C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 226C. Loss of office payments[^key-f3c4339cb80075dc81295f5c317ae996]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) No payment for loss of office may be made by any person to a person who is, or has been, a director of a quoted company [^key-90edb17fe485096f19e7ca1a191d836a]... unless—

- (a) the payment is consistent with the approved directors' remuneration policy, or
- (b)[^key-a149be034b2f9f6ca8d3d5b064584ded] the payment is approved by resolution of the members of the company.

(2) The approved directors' remuneration policy is the most recent remuneration policy to have been approved by a resolution passed by the members of the company in general meeting.
