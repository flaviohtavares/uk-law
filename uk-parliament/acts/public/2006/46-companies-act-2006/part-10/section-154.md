# Companies Act 2006, section 154

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 154. Companies required to have directors

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A private company must have at least one director.

(2) A public company must have at least two directors.
