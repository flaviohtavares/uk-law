# Companies Act 2006, section 230

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 230. Directors' service contracts: application of provisions to shadow directors[^c20597651][^c19880111][^c19880061][^c19879901]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A shadow director is treated as a director for the purposes of the provisions of this Chapter.
