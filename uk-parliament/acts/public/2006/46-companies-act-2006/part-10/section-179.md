# Companies Act 2006, section 179

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 179. Cases within more than one of the general duties[^c19642591][^key-fc05d016211fde38dc7765021aed3735]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Except as otherwise provided, more than one of the general duties may apply in any given case.
