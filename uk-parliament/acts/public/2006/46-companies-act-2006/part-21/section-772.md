## 772. Transfer of shares on application of transferor

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

On the application of the transferor of any share or interest in a company, the company shall enter in its register of members the name of the transferee [^key-cec9eb44f75e0f55b15035941a4dda01]... in the same manner and subject to the same conditions as if the application for the entry [^key-24d2770954a077631411bc036b11bd87]... were made by the transferee.
