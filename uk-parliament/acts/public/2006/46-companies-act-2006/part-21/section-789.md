# Companies Act 2006, section 789

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 789. Duty to consult

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Before making—

- (a) regulations under this Chapter, or
- (b) any order under section 787,

the authority having power to make regulations under this Chapter must carry out such consultation as appears to it to be appropriate.
