## 1207. Application of general provisions about offences

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provisions of sections 1121 to 1123 (liability of officer in default) and 1125 to 1131 (general provisions about offences) apply in relation to offences under this Part as in relation to offences under the Companies Acts.
