# Companies Act 2006, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Common seal

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1)[^c20599791][^c20616111][^c21178231] A company may have a common seal, but need not have one.

(2) A company which has a common seal shall have its name engraved in legible characters on the seal.

(3)[^c21737841] If a company fails to comply with subsection (2) an offence is committed by—

- (a) the company, and
- (b) every officer of the company who is in default.

(4)[^c21737841] An officer of a company, or a person acting on behalf of a company, commits an offence if he uses, or authorises the use of, a seal purporting to be a seal of the company on which its name is not engraved as required by subsection (2).

(5)[^c21737841] A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.

(6) This section does not form part of the law of Scotland.
