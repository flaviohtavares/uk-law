# Companies Act 2006, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 53. Prohibited names[^c20584981][^key-2d4cacf4a69194082b69182a740f5f10]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A company must not be registered under this Act by a name if, in the opinion of the Secretary of State—

- (a) its use by the company would constitute an offence, or
- (b) it is offensive.
