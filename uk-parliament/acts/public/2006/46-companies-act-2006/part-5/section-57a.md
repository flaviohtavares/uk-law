# Companies Act 2006, section 57A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57A. Names containing computer code[^key-7caf1bd2b5f355fa9a47ef136f9ca6a0][^key-7f1c912a51d968d42d3ca676960fab84]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A company must not be registered under this Act by a name that, in the opinion of the Secretary of State, consists of or includes computer code.
