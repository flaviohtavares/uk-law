# Companies Act 2006, section 945

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 945. Rulings

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^key-016cd5a86776d76aca52b18fc6b93113][^key-2f0806497efa0de1046c25b6d14d5a49]

(1) The Panel may give rulings on the interpretation, application or effect of rules.

(2) To the extent and in the circumstances specified in rules, and subject to any review or appeal, a ruling has binding effect.
