## 538A. Meaning of “corporate governance statement” etc[^c20691161][^c20594901]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part “ **corporate governance statement** ” means the statement required by rules 7.2.1 to 7.2.11 in the Disclosure Rules and Transparency Rules sourcebook made by the Financial Conduct Authority[^c22574281] .

(2)[^c22574291] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) A “separate” corporate governance statement means one that is not included in the directors' report.
