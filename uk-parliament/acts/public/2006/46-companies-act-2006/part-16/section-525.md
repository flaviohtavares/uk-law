## 525. Meaning of “appropriate audit authority” ...[^c20618111][^c-257cda1ba9318d5d8dcec59dfc9a3d4e][^c-3c14f982f29ef9c0d9162757fc70f9a6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In sections 522, 523 and 524 “**appropriate audit authority**” means—

- (a) in relation to an auditor of a public interest company (other than an Auditor General)[^c-ef68cf2d009bfd0642729c3e4775a921]—
  - (i) the Secretary of State, or
  - (ii) if the Secretary of State has delegated functions under section 1252 to a body whose functions include receiving the statement or[^c-8a3794fa374a6e2d4bfc174643942b3a] notice in question, that body;
- (a)[^c21353531] in the case of a major audit (other than one conducted by an Auditor General), the Financial Reporting Council Limited;
- (b) in relation to an auditor of a non-public interest company (other than an Auditor General)[^c-23cbfa6dbdf0ae47f24373c47024b419], the relevant supervisory body.
- (c)[^c19699301] in relation to[^c-4ae87cc7aacc4a217b69187c168c4aa1] an Auditor General, the Independent Supervisor.

“[^c19699321]**Supervisory body**” and “**Independent Supervisor**” have the same meaning as in Part 42 (statutory auditors) (see sections 1217 and 1228[^c19699341]).
