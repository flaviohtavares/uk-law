# Companies Act 2006, section 481

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 481. Companies excluded from dormant companies exemption[^c20617671][^c-6719bcdb774bb72d603dc9a45284362a][^M_C_14dc6b49-96e3-418f-d709-f78765e0c397]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A company is not entitled to the exemption conferred by section 480 (dormant companies) if it was at any time within the financial year in question a company that—

- (za)[^c-e27d3df9ce33a97f3cefc180d147b8ba] is a traded company as defined in section 474(1),
- (a) is an authorised insurance company, a banking company, an e-money issuer, a MiFID investment firm[^c19693691] or a UCITS management company, or
- (b) carries on insurance market activity.
