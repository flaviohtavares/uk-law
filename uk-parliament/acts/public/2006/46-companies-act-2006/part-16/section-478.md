# Companies Act 2006, section 478

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 478. Companies excluded from small companies exemption[^key-73968589633aa8e37174ac5e510948cc][^c20617641]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A company is not entitled to the exemption conferred by section 477 (small companies) if it was at any time within the financial year in question—

- (a) a public company,
- (b) a company that—
  - (i) is an authorised insurance company, a banking company, an e-money issuer, a MiFID investment firm[^c19693671] or a UCITS management company, or *(Extent: E+W+S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bd737744b8dc8abe9f29f19866d8a7e1]
  - (ii) carries on insurance market activity, or

  *Extent: E+W+S*

  - (iii)[^key-15b06673fab2a4f08170a956b8d2756e] is a scheme funder of a Master Trust scheme within the meanings given by section 39(1) of the Pension Schemes Act 2017 or section 39(1) of the Pension Schemes Act (Northern Ireland) 2021 *(Extent: N.I.)*[^key-ca8f799402fde1e73507f7323e68a181] (interpretation of Part 1), or
- (c) a special register body as defined in section 117(1) of the Trade Union and Labour Relations (Consolidation) Act 1992 (c. 52) or an employers' association as defined in section 122 of that Act or Article 4 of the Industrial Relations (Northern Ireland) Order 1992 (S.I. 1992/807 (N.I. 5)).
