# Companies Act 2006, section 516

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 516. Resignation of auditor[^c19906721][^c20618021]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An auditor of a company may resign his office by sending a notice to that effect to the company[^key-687eda9786db2042da7c8b833ad0905a].

(2) Where the company is a public interest company, the[^key-a29e62bb00f4261aeae1c6985b359f3d] notice is not effective unless it is accompanied by the statement required by section 519.

(3) An effective notice of resignation operates to bring the auditor's term of office to an end as of the date on which the notice is received[^key-a442feef8b41a4cc4dadc335c3b1e3c2] or on such later date as may be specified in it.
