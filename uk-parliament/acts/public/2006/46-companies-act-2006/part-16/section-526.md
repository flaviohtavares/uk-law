# Companies Act 2006, section 526

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 526. Effect of casual vacancies[^c20618121][^key-257cda1ba9318d5d8dcec59dfc9a3d4e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If an auditor ceases to hold office for any reason, any surviving or continuing auditor or auditors may continue to act.
