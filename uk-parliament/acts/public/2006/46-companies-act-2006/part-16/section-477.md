## 477. Small companies: conditions for exemption from audit[^c-73968589633aa8e37174ac5e510948cc][^c20617631]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A company that qualifies as a small company in relation to[^c21737241] a financial year is exempt from the requirements of this Act relating to the audit of accounts for that year.

(4) For the purposes of this section—

- (a) whether a company qualifies as a small company shall be determined in accordance with section 382(1) to (6), [^c21737311]. . .
- (b) [^c21737311]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) This section has effect subject to—

- section 475(2) and (3) (requirements as to statements to be contained in balance sheet),
- section 476 (right of members to require audit),
- section 478 (companies excluded from small companies exemption), and
- section 479 (availability of small companies exemption in case of group company).
