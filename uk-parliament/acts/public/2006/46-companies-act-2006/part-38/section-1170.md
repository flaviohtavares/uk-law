## 1170. Meaning of “EEA State” and related expressions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Companies Acts—

- “[^c19708911] **EEA State** ” has the meaning given by Schedule 1 to the Interpretation Act 1978;
- “ EEA company” and “ EEA undertaking” mean a company or undertaking governed by the law of an EEA State.
