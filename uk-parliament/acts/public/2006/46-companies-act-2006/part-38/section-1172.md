# Companies Act 2006, section 1172

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1172. References to requirements of this Act[^c19920851][^c20606161]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

References in the company law provisions of this Act to the requirements of this Act include the requirements of regulations and orders made under it.
