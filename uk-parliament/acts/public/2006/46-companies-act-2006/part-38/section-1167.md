# Companies Act 2006, section 1167

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1167. Meaning of “prescribed”[^c18956711]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Companies Acts “**prescribed**” means prescribed (by order or by regulations) by the Secretary of State.
