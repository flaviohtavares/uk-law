# Companies Act 2006, section 1158

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46

## 1158. Meaning of “UK-registered company”[^c19679451]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In the Companies Acts“**UK-registered company**” means a company registered under this Act. The expression does not include an overseas company that has registered particulars under section 1046.
