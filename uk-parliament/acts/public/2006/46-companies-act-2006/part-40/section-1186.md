# Companies Act 2006, section 1186

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1186. Offence of breach of disqualification[^c20262821]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulations under section 1184 may provide that a person disqualified under this Part who acts in breach of the disqualification commits an offence.

(2) The regulations may provide that a person guilty of such an offence is liable—

- (a) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years or a fine (or both);
- (b) on summary conviction—
  - (i) in England and Wales, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-8eb4135e1ceaa4a67c3f37d5e6d12496] or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both);
  - (ii) in Scotland or Northern Ireland, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum (or both).

(3) In relation to an offence committed before 2 May 2022[^key-3e5e80b0f53a37f764d53671c16310b7], for “the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-80467bebb03fd101234382c1a737053e]” in subsection (2)(b)(i) substitute “ six months ”.
