# Companies Act 2006, section 1133

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1133. Transitional provision[^c18930831][^c20605391]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The provisions of this Part except section 1132 do not apply to offences committed before the commencement of the relevant provision.
