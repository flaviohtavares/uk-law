# Companies Act 2006, section 1129

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1129. Legal professional privilege[^c18930561][^c19692651][^c19919431][^c20605291][^c21737821][^c21737891][^c-d515d435a0afc443cc8913abbf110fae][^c-505fb694e0084a31d0d4c7960f1f6bec]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In proceedings against a person for an offence under the Companies Acts, nothing in those Acts is to be taken to require any person to disclose any information that he is entitled to refuse to disclose on grounds of legal professional privilege (in Scotland, confidentiality of communications).
