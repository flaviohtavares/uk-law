# Companies Act 2006, section 1131

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1131. Imprisonment on summary conviction in England and Wales: transitory provision[^c18930661][^c19919451][^c20605341][^c21737851][^c21737901]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to any provision of the Companies Acts that provides that a person guilty of an offence is liable on summary conviction in England and Wales to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-e5a591b7eed75584902266a72027fcb8].

(2) In relation to an offence committed before 2 May 2022[^c-f45265a624ce501934c298727db340f0], for “the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^c-56aa85a12663074eb677a55b5bd3880d]” substitute “six months”.
