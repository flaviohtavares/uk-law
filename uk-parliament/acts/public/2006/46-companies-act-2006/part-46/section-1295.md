# Companies Act 2006, section 1295

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1295. Repeals[^c18964661]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The enactments specified in Schedule 16, which include enactments that are no longer of practical utility, are repealed to the extent specified.
