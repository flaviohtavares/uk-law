# Companies Act 2006, section 1292A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46

## 1292A. “Bank of England”[^c-18e4c845a2b70d5ef94d5c86636bd6e3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act, except in section 796, references to the Bank of England do not include the Bank acting in its capacity as the Prudential Regulation Authority.
