# Companies Act 2006, section 1288

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (20).** Text as at 2026-07-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/46
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1288. Regulations and orders: statutory instrument[^c19931991][^c20607251]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Except as otherwise provided, regulations and orders under this Act shall be made by statutory instrument.
