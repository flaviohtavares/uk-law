# Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006, section 47

> Text as at 2026-06-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 47. Protection for nests of certain birds which re-use their nests[^c-50191a71f486225aa9ce108fafed2086]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Amend the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 (c. 69) as follows.

(2) In section 1 (protection of wild birds, their nests and eggs), in subsection (1), after paragraph (a) insert—

> - (aa) takes, damages or destroys the nest of a wild bird included in Schedule ZA1;

.

(3) At the beginning of the Schedules insert—

> **SCHEDULE ZA1 — Birds which re-use their nests**
>
> <table>
> <tfoot>
> <tr><td colspan="2">NOTE: The common name or names given in the first column of this Schedule are included by way of guidance only; in the event of any dispute or proceedings, the common name or names shall not be taken into account.</td></tr>
> </tfoot>
> <tbody>
> <tr><th><em>Common name</em></th><th><em>Scientific name</em></th></tr>
> <tr><td>Eagle, Golden</td><td>Aquila chrysaetos</td></tr>
> <tr><td>Eagle, White-tailed</td><td>Haliaetus albicilla</td></tr>
> <tr><td>Osprey</td><td>Pandion haliaetus</td></tr>
> </tbody>
> </table>

(4) In—

- (a) section 4 (exceptions to sections 1 and 3), in subsection (1)(c), and
- (b) section 7 (registration etc. of certain captive birds), in subsection (3A)(a)(i) and (c)(i),

after “Schedule” insert “ ZA1 or ”.

(5) In section 22 (power to vary Schedules), in subsection (1), for “Schedules 1 to 4” substitute “ Schedules ZA1 to 4 ”.
