# Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006, section 82

> Text as at 2026-06-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Maximum duration of agreement[^key-20ccfc2b124b1d365d78f52c6686f6b8]

*Extent: E+W*

The maximum period for which an agreement may authorise a designated body to perform—

- (a) a DEFRA or DESNZ[^key-2b3d8e06cd11470aeb3245fb34e7b34f] function, or
- (b) a function that is related to or connected with a DEFRA or DESNZ[^key-b57d4535f944d1544a88db5602c660eb] function,

is 20 years.
