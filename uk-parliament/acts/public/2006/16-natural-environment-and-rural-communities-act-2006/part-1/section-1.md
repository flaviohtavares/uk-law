# Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006, section 1

> Text as at 2026-06-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Constitution

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-f6f99a76ef5e900682ed640946be4b44] There is to be a body known as Natural England.

(2)[^key-f6f99a76ef5e900682ed640946be4b44] Natural England is to have the functions conferred on it by or under this Act or any other enactment.

(3)[^key-f6f99a76ef5e900682ed640946be4b44] Except where otherwise expressly provided, Natural England's functions are exercisable in relation to England (including, where the context requires, the territorial sea adjacent to England)[^key-81e2d031af9381494c982c17bd06d79e] only.

(3A)[^key-cd392fcd362a0c1617ca6563cc58aa34] An order or Order in Council made—

- (a) under section 158(3) of the Government of Wales Act 2006 for the purposes of determining which waters are treated as being adjacent to Wales, or
- (b) under section 126(2) of the Scotland Act 1998 for the purposes of determining which waters are treated as being adjacent to Scotland,

applies for the purposes of this section as it applies for the purposes of the Act under which it is made.

(4)[^key-ce3990eecf5890af9bcae0c2bca9ee81] English Nature and the Countryside Agency are dissolved and their functions are (subject to the provisions of this Act) transferred to Natural England.

(5)[^key-f6f99a76ef5e900682ed640946be4b44] Schedule 1 contains provisions about the constitution of Natural England and related matters.
