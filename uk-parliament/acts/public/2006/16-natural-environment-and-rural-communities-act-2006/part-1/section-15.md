# Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006, section 15

> Text as at 2026-06-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Guidance

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-7ca21cb7399aa3107192f5ac8b8ef42f] The Secretary of State must give Natural England guidance as to the exercise of any functions of Natural England that relate to or affect regional planning and associated matters.

(2)[^key-f88e0ec67a43fb2b2f84cb1e86e5ec0a] The Secretary of State may give Natural England guidance as to the exercise of its other functions.

(3)[^key-f88e0ec67a43fb2b2f84cb1e86e5ec0a] Before giving guidance under this section the Secretary of State must consult—

- (a) Natural England,
- (b) the Environment Agency, and
- (c) such other persons as the Secretary of State thinks appropriate.

(4)[^key-f88e0ec67a43fb2b2f84cb1e86e5ec0a] The Secretary of State must publish any guidance given under this section as soon as is reasonably practicable after giving the guidance.

(5)[^key-f88e0ec67a43fb2b2f84cb1e86e5ec0a] The power to give guidance under this section includes power to vary or revoke it.

(6)[^key-f88e0ec67a43fb2b2f84cb1e86e5ec0a] In discharging its functions, Natural England must have regard to guidance given under this section.
