> Text as at 2026-06-17.

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Power to bring criminal proceedings[^c-fad7af103fbb80581c45f966aab9e979]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Natural England may institute criminal proceedings.

(2) A person who is authorised by Natural England to prosecute on its behalf in proceedings before a magistrates' court is entitled to prosecute in such proceedings [^c-8e0f67d0ccff917831e80d1787f19bce]....
