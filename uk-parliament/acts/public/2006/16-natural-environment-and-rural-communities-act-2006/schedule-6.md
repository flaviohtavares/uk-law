# Natural Environment and Rural Communities Act 2006, SCHEDULE 6

> Text as at 2026-06-17.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 6 — Wildlife offences: time limits for proceedings

*Extent: E+W*

Section 53

### Destructive Imported Animals Act 1932 (c. 12)

**1.**[^key-081716d5cd5cc13351cba4de7b9b7d71] In section 6 (offences relating to importation etc. of musk rats without licence etc.), after subsection (2) insert—

> (3) Proceedings in England and Wales for an offence under this section may be brought within the period of 6 months beginning with the date on which evidence sufficient in the opinion of the prosecutor to warrant the proceedings came to his knowledge.
>
> (4) But subsection (3) does not authorise the commencement of proceedings for an offence more than 2 years after the date on which the offence was committed.
>
> (5) For the purposes of subsection (3), a certificate signed by or on behalf of the prosecutor and stating the date on which evidence sufficient in his opinion to warrant the proceedings came to his knowledge shall be conclusive evidence of that fact.
>
> (6) A certificate stating that matter and purporting to be so signed shall be deemed to be so signed unless the contrary is proved.
