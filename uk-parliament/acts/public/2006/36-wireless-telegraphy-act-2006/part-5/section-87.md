# Wireless Telegraphy Act 2006, section 87

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Procuring person to commit offence abroad

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person commits an offence if he procures, in the United Kingdom, another person to do, outside the United Kingdom, anything that would have constituted an offence under sections 82 to 85 had the other person done it in the United Kingdom.
