# Wireless Telegraphy Act 2006, section 94

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/36

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94. Saving for certain broadcasts

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Nothing in this Part makes it unlawful to do anything under and in accordance with a wireless telegraphy licence, or to procure anything to be so done.
