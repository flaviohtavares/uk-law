# Wireless Telegraphy Act 2006, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Part 4: interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part—

- “**advertisement**” includes a catalogue, a circular and a price list;
- “**relevant apparatus**” means wireless telegraphy apparatus or apparatus designed or adapted for use in connection with wireless telegraphy apparatus.
