# Wireless Telegraphy Act 2006, section 60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. Obstruction and failure to assist

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person commits an offence if—

- (a) he intentionally obstructs a person in the exercise of the powers conferred on him under section 59; or
- (b) he fails or refuses, without reasonable excuse, to give to such a person any assistance which, under that section, he is under a duty to give to him.

(2) A person who commits an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
