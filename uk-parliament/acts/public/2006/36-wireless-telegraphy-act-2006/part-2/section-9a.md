# Wireless Telegraphy Act 2006, section 9A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/36

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9A. Notice to satellite uplinkers[^key-94d416996dc61e53b0d930fa4e5da4a3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-06829dec0aa1549880b2ea8108780c62] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) OFCOM may give a notice in writing under this section to a satellite uplinker in relation to a relevant regulated television service if OFCOM are satisfied that the service is provided—

- (a) in contravention of a licence under Part 1 of the Broadcasting Act 1990 or Part 1 of the Broadcasting Act 1996; or
- (b) otherwise than pursuant to such a licence.

(3) OFCOM may give a notice in writing under this section to a satellite uplinker in relation to an on-demand programme service , or a non-UK on-demand programme service that is a Tier 1 service,[^key-433611da24e8a7b25e67a5cf1077875d] if OFCOM are satisfied that the service is provided —[^key-efa34a4fcac72ed720e1ce99e29067cd]

- (a) in contravention of a requirement of Part 4A of the Communications Act 2003; or[^key-71dd8416a8b8ea0dabc0dff9dd17f5bf]
- (b) otherwise than pursuant to a notification under section 368BA of the Communications Act 2003.

(4) Where a notice is given to a satellite uplinker under subsection (2)(a) or (3)(a)[^key-8ae9c8b66060c633307e561b4b00d5d1] the notice must—

- (a) name the service;
- (b) specify the reasons why OFCOM consider that subsection (2)(a) or (3) (a) is satisfied; and
- (c) specify—
  - (i) the date by which the satellite uplinker must cease the uplinking of the service; or
  - (ii) a period during which the satellite uplinker must suspend the uplinking of the service.

(5) Where a notice is given to a satellite uplinker under subsection (2)(b)or (3)(b)[^key-a6d503560aedec2d8155adcc98519c35] the notice must—

- (a) name the service; and
- (b) specify—
  - (i) the date by which the satellite uplinker must cease the uplinking of the service; or
  - (ii) a period during which the satellite uplinker must suspend the uplinking of the service.

(6) In this section—

- “relevant regulated television service” has the same meaning as in section 13 of the Broadcasting Act 1990;
- “on-demand programme service , “non-UK on-demand programme service” and “Tier 1 service” each[^key-b6cdbdf61c5dc64e209a0fe60b01cc0c] has the same meaning as in the Communications Act 2003 (see sections 368A,[^key-ce3dc80c43fc7518d24e64b2a4f5d992] 368AA and 368HA of that Act).
