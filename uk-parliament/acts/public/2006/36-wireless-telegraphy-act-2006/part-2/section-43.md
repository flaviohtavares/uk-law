# Wireless Telegraphy Act 2006, section 43

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/36
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 43. Amount of penalty under section 42

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The amount of a penalty imposed under section 42 for a relevant multiplex contravention (see subsection (7) of that section)[^key-fc826a76a7e9559d8120fa5cd8cef03a] is to be such amount as OFCOM think fit.

(2) But the amount of the penalty may not exceed the greater of—

- (a) £250,000; and
- (b) 5 per cent. of the relevant amount of gross revenue.

(2A)[^key-fc674dd86a25ac358dfa66e41fe2563d] The amount of a penalty imposed under section 42 for a contravention that is not a relevant multiplex contravention is to be such amount not exceeding 10 per cent of the relevant amount of gross revenue as OFCOM think—

- (a) appropriate; and
- (b) proportionate to the contravention in respect of which it is imposed.

(3) In this section[^key-a4e3873474f34be3c8c8584200a51313] “**the relevant amount of gross revenue**” means the amount specified in section 44.

(4) The Secretary of State may by order amend this section so as to substitute a different amount for the amount for the time being specified in subsection (2)(a).

(5) No order is to be made containing provision authorised by subsection (4) unless a draft of the order has been laid before Parliament and approved by a resolution of each House.
