## 4. Power to vary date of ordinary general election[^c16526401]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-2879a8efebd41d41c25f18fc7653606f] Subject to section 3(1A), the Presiding Officer may propose, for the holding of the poll at an ordinary general election, a day which is not more than one month earlier, nor more than one month later, than the first Thursday in May.

(2) If the Presiding Officer proposes a day under subsection (1), Her Majesty may by proclamation under the Welsh Seal—

- (a) dissolve the Senedd[^key-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962],
- (b) require the poll at the election to be held on the day proposed, and
- (c)[^key-2dcb5601218a8864c753a72b25464c55] require the Senedd to meet within the period of fourteen[^key-d53a210798a958d317715890a2f1bebe] days beginning immediately after the day of the poll.

(2A) Where a day is specified by an order under section 3(1B), subsection (1) applies as if the reference to the first Thursday in May were a reference to that day.

(3) In calculating any period of days for the purposes of provision made by virtue of subsection (2)(c)[^key-3073ab0cd5529410fef653f3b8ff63c1], the following days are to be disregarded—

- (a) Saturday and Sunday,
- (b) Good Friday,
- (c) any day which is a bank holiday in Wales under the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 (c. 80), and
- (d) any day appointed for public thanksgiving or mourning.

(4) The Welsh Ministers may by order[^key-1d1c78058790ec0e6282247598832209] make provision for—

- (a) any provision of, or made under, the Representation of the People Acts, or
- (b) any other enactment relating to the election of Members of the Senedd[^key-959ca092134c70e47fe7789224d88f33] ,

to have effect with such modifications or exceptions as the Welsh Ministers consider[^key-c2e99487b7d9efc6590fa74d7ba029c5] appropriate in connection with the alteration of the day of the poll under this section[^key-4c9ab06fa56f017a920ce8947bfd1a27] .

(5)[^key-72c252f393e50d5fe22184cbe8c9164c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) A statutory instrument containing an order under this section is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of the[^key-b0a12807d6487b20ab5370ab65941e27] Senedd.
