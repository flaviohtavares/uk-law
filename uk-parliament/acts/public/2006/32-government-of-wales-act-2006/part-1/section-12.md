## 12. Entitlement to vote[^c16526481]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The persons entitled to vote at an election of Members of the Senedd[^c-959ca092134c70e47fe7789224d88f33] [^c-7247c2f4c8a1d5d62001ec664fa385bb]... in a Senedd[^c-70d8b16fe610814bb3ba5aa76d90a5f3] constituency are those who on the day of the poll—

- (a) would be entitled to vote as electors at a local government election in an electoral area wholly or partly included in the Senedd[^c-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962] constituency [^c-b0fdeb41df5153d21f187c7577d6fad6]..., and
- (b) are registered in the register of local government electors at an address within the Senedd constituency.

(1A)[^c-df3995ada60039b80591a78a7a148d4c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1B)[^c-c609d29cc4dcc5d41c4ce38c49003fc4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) But a person is not entitled as an elector—

- (a) to cast more than one vote[^c-63e6157307d06ee2df2467549bd5ad3c] in the same Senedd constituency at any general election, or[^c-efc95febd1af6f28d2a5eabfb3ed62ec]
- (b) to vote in more than one Senedd constituency at any general election, [^c-1bfab251467fba9e0be95ea5e77f77f6]...
- (c)[^c-1bfab251467fba9e0be95ea5e77f77f6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
