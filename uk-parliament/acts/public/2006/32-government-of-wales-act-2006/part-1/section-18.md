## 18. Effect of disqualification[^c16526541]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1)[^M_F_5d555675-05cf-4970-cdee-a56a508974f5] If a person who is disqualified from being a candidate to be a Member of the Senedd (see section 16(A1)) is nominated as a candidate at a general election of Members of the Senedd [^key-554314e2f461f3e8e087f56949fc63ab]..., the person's nomination is void.

(1) If a person who is disqualified from being a Member of the Senedd[^key-9348d22b2d042136af6e1508e7843105] is returned as a Member of the Senedd, the person's return is void and the person's seat is vacant.

(2)[^M_F_51f00126-0683-4fb8-fb1d-4e0227daf1c2] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) If a person who is a Member of the Senedd becomes disqualified—

- (a) from being a Member of the Senedd, [^M_F_75d2520f-5449-4f1a-a7cc-7101f902efbd]...
- (b)[^M_F_75d2520f-5449-4f1a-a7cc-7101f902efbd] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

the person ceases to be a Member of the Senedd (so that the person's seat is vacant).

(4) Subsections (1) to (3) have effect subject to any resolution of the Senedd[^key-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962] under section 17(3).

(5) In addition, subsection (3) has effect subject to—

- (a)[^key-dd015fa5f797ca607f199fbef06dc493] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (aa)[^key-bf5820a6a8cd8e8a9d89355804df8fd9] section 31 of the Elections Act 2022 (disqualification of offenders for holding elective office etc);
- (b) section 427 of the Insolvency Act 1986 (c. 45) (bankruptcy etc.).

(6) If, in consequence of either of the provisions[^key-784519354e569c5fdcd47ba6058ac513] mentioned in subsection (5), the seat of a person who is disqualified from being a Member of the Senedd is not vacant, the person does not cease to be a Member of the Senedd until the person's seat becomes vacant.

(7) But for any period for which the person is disqualified but the person's seat is not vacant—

- (a) the person must not participate in any Senedd proceedings, and
- (b) any of the person's other rights and privileges as a Member of the Senedd may be withdrawn by the Senedd.

(8) The validity of any Senedd proceedings is not affected by the disqualification of any person—

- (a) from being a Member of the Senedd, [^M_F_deccb4a2-05f7-45c6-eea4-5691ffc8398e]...
- (b)[^M_F_deccb4a2-05f7-45c6-eea4-5691ffc8398e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
