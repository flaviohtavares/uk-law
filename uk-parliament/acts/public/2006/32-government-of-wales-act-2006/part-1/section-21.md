## 21. Limit on salaries of Members of the Senedd[^c16526571][^c-959ca092134c70e47fe7789224d88f33]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Senedd[^c-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962] must make provision to ensure that the amount of the salary payable to a Member of the Senedd[^c-9348d22b2d042136af6e1508e7843105] in accordance with section 20 is reduced if a salary is payable to the Member of the Senedd[^c-77862aa11ba8e490f1db71c8f7e15ec1] —

- (za)[^c-ce22956a3ee2c0ddca6bb2e3398aa1e0] under section 4 of the Parliamentary Standards Act 2009 (salaries of members of the House of Commons), or[^c-99d05a833ab7f8c7b046762196a4ec3e]
- (a) pursuant to a resolution (or combination of resolutions) of the House of Lords[^c-88f92aa070c274329985757897c52411] relating to the remuneration of members of that House, [^c-d494f7013ee0d8f044b23214630ec449]...
- (b)[^c-d494f7013ee0d8f044b23214630ec449] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The provision made must ensure that the amount of salary is reduced—

- (a) to a particular proportion of what it otherwise would be or to a particular amount, or
- (b) by the amount of any salary payable to the Member of the Senedd as mentioned in subsection (1)(za) or (a)[^c-bcbfc3166a3d9ef597a00c1fc1d5b6e8], by a particular proportion of that amount or by some other particular amount.

(3) Provision may be made under this section by—

- (a) the standing orders, or
- (b) resolutions of the Senedd,

and may include provision conferring functions on the Senedd Commission.
