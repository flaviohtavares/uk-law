# Government of Wales Act 2006, section 41

## 41. Proceedings by or against Senedd etc.[^c16526771][^c-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Proceedings by or against the Senedd are to be instituted by or against the Senedd Commission on behalf of the Senedd.

(2) Proceedings by or against—

- (a) the Presiding Officer or a Deputy Presiding Officer[^c-ec939773e19e135d4e88521f6b793b41], or
- (b) a member of the staff of the Senedd,

are (unless instituted against or by the Senedd Commission) to be instituted by or against the Senedd Commission on behalf of the Presiding Officer, Deputy Presiding Officer or member of staff.

(3) In any proceedings against the Senedd the court must not grant a mandatory, prohibiting or quashing order or an injunction, make an order for specific performance or stay the proceedings but may instead make a declaration.

(4) In any proceedings against—

- (a) any Member of the Senedd[^c-77862aa11ba8e490f1db71c8f7e15ec1],
- (b) the Presiding Officer or a Deputy Presiding Officer[^c-a7707d5fda6a156f2cdbfe3ef8cc1c9c],
- (c) any member of the staff of the Senedd, or
- (d) the Senedd Commission,

the court must not grant a mandatory, prohibiting or quashing order or an injunction, make an order for specific performance or stay the proceedings if the effect of doing so would be to give any relief against the Senedd which could not have been given in proceedings against the Senedd.

(5) References in this section to an order include an order which is not final.
