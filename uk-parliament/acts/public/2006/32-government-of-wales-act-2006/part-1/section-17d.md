# Government of Wales Act 2006, section 17D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/32

## 17D. Exception from disqualification by virtue of being a councillor: recently elected members[^M_F_499fe501-5d2f-48e9-e61e-079842aabd32]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person returned at an election as a Member of the Senedd is not disqualified under section 16(1)(zc) (disqualification by virtue of being a member of the council of a county or county borough or community council[^c-6878087cafdd60ed5992914ade3b42af] in Wales) at any time before the person purports to take the oath of allegiance (or make the corresponding affirmation) in compliance with section 23(1) or 55(2).

(2) A Member of the Senedd who is returned at an election as a member of the council of a county or county borough or community council[^c-d721202c5bd0b44150f458da9140b3e5] in Wales is not disqualified under section 16(1)(zc) at any time before the person makes a declaration of acceptance of office under section 83 of the [Local Government Act 1972 (c. 70)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1972/70).
