## 14. Term of office of Members of the Senedd[^c16526501][^key-959ca092134c70e47fe7789224d88f33]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The term of office of a Member of the Senedd[^key-9348d22b2d042136af6e1508e7843105] —

- (a) begins when the Member of the Senedd[^key-77862aa11ba8e490f1db71c8f7e15ec1] is declared to be returned, and
- (b) ends with the dissolution of the Senedd[^key-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962].
