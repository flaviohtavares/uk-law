## 17. Relief from disqualification[^c16526531][^M_F_7c89983a-974d-41bf-804e-557a22cf67e8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^M_F_029123e9-be53-4faf-e431-fbf4d180236f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^M_F_029123e9-be53-4faf-e431-fbf4d180236f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The Senedd[^key-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962] may resolve that the disqualification of any person who was, or is alleged to have been, disqualified from being a Member of the Senedd[^key-9348d22b2d042136af6e1508e7843105] on a ground within section 16(1) [^M_F_e6129e40-0ba2-4827-fb37-8f48ba3d9d16]... is to be disregarded if it appears to the Senedd —

- (a) that the ground has been removed, and
- (b) that it is proper so to resolve.

(4) A resolution under subsection (3) does not—

- (a) affect any proceedings under Part 3 of the Representation of the People Act 1983 (c. 2) as applied by or incorporated in an order under section 13, or
- (b) enable the Senedd to disregard any disqualification which has been established in such proceedings or in proceedings under section 19.
