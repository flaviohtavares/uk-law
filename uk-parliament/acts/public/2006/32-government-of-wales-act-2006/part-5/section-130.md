# Government of Wales Act 2006, section 130

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/32

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 130. Payments in by mistake[^c16542951]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a sum is paid into the Welsh Consolidated Fund which should not or need not have been paid into the Fund, the Auditor General may grant an approval to draw a payment equal to the amount of that sum out of the Fund.
