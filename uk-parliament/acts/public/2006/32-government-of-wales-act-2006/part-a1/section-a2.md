# Government of Wales Act 2006, section A2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/32

## A2. Recognition of Welsh law[^c-e4c869f0e410843665ebd1bf8f9a5298]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The law that applies in Wales includes a body of Welsh law made by the Senedd[^c-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962] and the Welsh Ministers.

(2) The purpose of this section is, with due regard to the other provisions of this Act, to recognise the ability of the Senedd and the Welsh Ministers to make law forming part of the law of England and Wales.
