# Government of Wales Act 2006, section 116A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/32

## 116A. Overview of Part 4A[^c-072cc4442b55bad2cd354d03f2953ea6]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part—[^c-2d3f356b0e89fdb74921eb5748e212b3]

- (a) Chapter 2 confers on the Senedd[^c-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962] power to set rates of income tax to be paid by Welsh taxpayers, and
- (b) Chapters 3 and 4 specify particular taxes as devolved taxes about which the Senedd may make provision in the exercise of the power conferred by section 107(1).

(2) The power to make provision about a devolved tax is subject to the restrictions imposed by—

- (a) subsection (3), and
- (b) the other provisions of this Part.

(3) A devolved tax may not be imposed where to do so would be incompatible with any international obligations.

(4) In this Act “ **devolved tax** ” means a tax specified in this Part as a devolved tax.
