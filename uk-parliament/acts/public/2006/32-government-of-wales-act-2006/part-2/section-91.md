# Government of Wales Act 2006, section 91

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Validity of acts[^c16527271]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The validity of any act of a person as First Minister is not affected by any defect in the person's nomination by the Senedd[^c-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962] .

(2) The validity of any act of a person as the Counsel General is not affected by any defect in the Senedd's agreement to the person's appointment.
