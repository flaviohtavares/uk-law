> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/32

## 54. Remuneration: supplementary[^c16526901]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Different provision may be made under section 53 for different cases.

(2) The[^c20932261] Senedd[^c-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962] Commission must ensure that information concerning—

- (a) the amounts paid to each person to whom section 53 applies as salary and allowances, and
- (b) the total amount paid to such persons as salaries and allowances,

is published for each financial year [^c21073601]. . .

(3)[^c20932281] The Senedd Commission must lay before the Senedd every determination made by the Board under section 53(7) as soon as is reasonably practicable after it is made.

(4) Provision made under section 53(3) does not affect pensions or allowances in payment before the provision was made.
