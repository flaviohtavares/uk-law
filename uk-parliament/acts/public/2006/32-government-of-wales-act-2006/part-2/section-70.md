# Government of Wales Act 2006, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Financial assistance[^c16527061]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The First Minister[^key-750ba55fe9cd4a7854d34d48ae5eb0a6] may give financial assistance (whether by way of grant, loan or guarantee) to any person engaged in any activity which the First Minister considers[^key-14f3d6527fc561dbcf70497003207a37] will secure, or help to secure, the attainment of any objective which the Minister aims[^key-f5f40df559942300810447563152759a] to attain in the exercise of any of the Minister's functions[^key-00d2f90b6997fcd2eb50d4889c4ea37d].

(2) The First Minister[^key-d83baad6c56398be099063fde316db2b] may attach conditions to the giving of financial assistance by the Minister[^key-d44298fcf0c75e0d272c4b2d2bb155dd]; and the conditions which may be attached include, in particular, conditions requiring the repayment of the whole or any part of a grant, or the making of any other payments, in any circumstances.

(3)[^key-f52baa6c974de60102f3c64c8c318854] This section applies in relation to the Counsel General as in relation to the First Minister.

(As regards the Welsh Ministers, see section 58A.)
