## 156. English and Welsh texts of legislation[^c16543201]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The English and Welsh texts of—

- (a) any Assembly Measure or Act of the Senedd[^c-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962] which is in both English and Welsh when it is enacted, or
- (b) any subordinate legislation which is in both English and Welsh when it is made,

are to be treated for all purposes as being of equal standing.

(1A)[^c-e29fb8e1d5ecd19c1071120ff39d5a39] Subsection (1) does not apply to any legislation to which Part 2 of the Legislation (Wales) Act 2019 applies (but section 5 of that Act makes corresponding provision in relation to legislation to which that Part applies).
