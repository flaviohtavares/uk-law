# Government of Wales Act 2006, section 165

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 165. Extent

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following provisions—

- section 36(7) to (9),
- section 39, and
- section 40(2) and (3),

extend only to England and Wales.

(2) The amendments, and repeals and revocations, made by this Act have the same extent as the enactments amended or repealed or revoked.
