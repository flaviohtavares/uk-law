## 150A. Translation of references to Senedd Cymru etc.[^key-4f1f75607fb2ef1d39ba916f38063c77][^key-c3fed792f5af903ad71310584c0f2e90]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-916eaa61c8e2ab77e6ae2348c480104d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Unless the context requires otherwise, a reference to the National Assembly for Wales (or Cynulliad Cenedlaethol Cymru), the National Assembly for Wales Commission (or Comisiwn Cynulliad Cenedlaethol Cymru) or Acts of the National Assembly for Wales (or Deddfau Cynulliad Cenedlaethol Cymru) (as the case may be)[^key-093b56fcf1cc6a69c56502a47de943d3], in—

- (a) any enactment (including any enactment comprised in or made under this Act) or prerogative instrument, or
- (b) any other instrument or document,

is to be read as, or as including, a reference to the name given by Part 2 of the Senedd and Elections (Wales) Act 2020[^key-ac214f2f6ebf361d2f0215b702a5682c].

(3)[^key-efc1ed89fa2eb1f4b63f5a438dcca24b] Unless the context requires otherwise, a reference to Senedd Cymru or the Welsh Parliament, the Senedd Commission (or Comisiwn y Senedd) or Acts of Senedd Cymru (or Deddfau Senedd Cymru) (as the case may be), in—

- (a) any enactment (including any enactment comprised in or made under this Act) or prerogative instrument, or
- (b) any other instrument or document,

is to be read as including a reference to the previous name.
