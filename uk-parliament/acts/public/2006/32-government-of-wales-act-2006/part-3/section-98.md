# Government of Wales Act 2006, section 98

## 98. Proceedings on proposed Assembly Measures (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-70d8b16fe610814bb3ba5aa76d90a5f3][^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960][^c16527341][^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The standing orders must include provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) for general debate on a proposed Assembly Measure with an opportunity for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Members of the Senedd[^c-959ca092134c70e47fe7789224d88f33] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] to vote on its general principles, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) for the consideration of, and an opportunity for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Members of the Senedd *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] to vote on, the details of a proposed Assembly Measure, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (c) for a final stage at which a proposed Assembly Measure can be passed or rejected. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(2) Subsection (1) does not prevent the standing orders making provision to enable the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd[^c-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] to expedite proceedings in relation to a particular proposed Assembly Measure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(3) The standing orders may make provision different from that required by subsection (1) for the procedure applicable to proposed Assembly Measures of any of the following kinds— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) proposed Assembly Measures which restate the law, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) proposed Assembly Measures which repeal or revoke spent enactments, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (c) private proposed Assembly Measures. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(4) The standing orders must include provision for securing that the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] may only pass a proposed Assembly Measure containing provisions which would, if contained in a Bill for an Act of Parliament, require the consent of Her Majesty or the Duke of Cornwall if such consent has been signified in accordance with the standing orders. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(5) The standing orders must include provision for securing that the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] may only pass a proposed Assembly Measure if the text of the proposed Assembly Measure is in both English and Welsh, unless the circumstances are such as are specified by the standing orders as any in which the text need not be in both languages. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(6) The standing orders must provide for an opportunity for the reconsideration of a proposed Assembly Measure after its passing if (and only if)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) the Supreme Court decides on a reference made in relation to the proposed Assembly Measure under section 99 that the proposed Assembly Measure or any provision of it would not be within the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd's *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] legislative competence, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) a reference made in relation to the proposed Assembly Measure under section 99 is withdrawn following a request for withdrawal of the reference under section 100(2)(b), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (c) an order is made in relation to the proposed Assembly Measure under section 101. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(7) The standing orders must, in particular, ensure that any proposed Assembly Measure amended on reconsideration is subject to a final stage at which it can be approved or rejected. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(8) References in subsections (4), (5) and (6) of this section and sections 93(2) and 95(10) to the passing of a proposed Assembly Measure are, in the case of a proposed Assembly Measure which has been amended on reconsideration, to be read as references to its approval. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
