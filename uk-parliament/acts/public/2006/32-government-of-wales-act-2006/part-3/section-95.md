## 95. Legislative competence: supplementary (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-70d8b16fe610814bb3ba5aa76d90a5f3][^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960][^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Her Majesty may by Order in Council— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) amend Part 1 of Schedule 5 to add a matter which relates to one or more of the fields listed in that Part, or to vary or remove any matter, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) amend that Part to add a new field or to vary or remove any field, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (c) amend Part 2 or 3 of that Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(2) An Order in Council under this section does not have effect to amend Part 1 of Schedule 5 by adding a field if, at the time when the amendment comes into force, no functions in the field are exercisable by the Welsh Ministers, the First Minister or the Counsel General. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(3) An Order in Council under this section may make such modifications of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) any enactment (including any enactment comprised in or made under this Act) or prerogative instrument, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) any other instrument or document, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

as Her Majesty considers appropriate in connection with the provision made by the Order in Council. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(4) An Order in Council under this section may make provision having retrospective effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(5) No recommendation is to be made to Her Majesty in Council to make an Order in Council under this section unless a draft of the statutory instrument containing the Order in Council— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd[^c-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) having been so approved, has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(6) As soon as is reasonably practicable after the draft of an Order in Council under this section has been approved by a resolution of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960], the First Minister must ensure that— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) notice in writing of the resolution, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) a copy of the draft, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

is sent to the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(7) The Secretary of State must, before the end of the period of 60 days beginning immediately after the day on which notice of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd's *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] resolution is received, either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) lay the draft before each House of Parliament, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) give notice in writing to the First Minister of the Secretary of State's refusal to do so and the reasons for that refusal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(8) As soon as is reasonably practicable after the First Minister receives notice of the Secretary of State's refusal to lay the draft before each House of Parliament and the reasons for that refusal— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) the First Minister must lay a copy of the notice before the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] must ensure that it is published. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(9) In reckoning the period of 60 days mentioned in subsection (7) no account is to be taken of any period during which Parliament is dissolved or prorogued or both Houses are adjourned for more than four days. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(10) The amendment of Schedule 5 by an Order in Council under this section does not affect— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) the validity of an Assembly Measure passed before the amendment comes into force, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) the previous or continuing operation of such an Assembly Measure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
