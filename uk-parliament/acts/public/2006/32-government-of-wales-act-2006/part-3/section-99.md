# Government of Wales Act 2006, section 99

## 99. Scrutiny of proposed Assembly Measures by Supreme Court (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-70d8b16fe610814bb3ba5aa76d90a5f3][^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960][^c16527351][^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Counsel General or the Attorney General may refer the question whether a proposed Assembly Measure, or any provision of a proposed Assembly Measure, would be within the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd's[^c-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] legislative competence to the Supreme Court for decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(2) Subject to subsection (3), the Counsel General or the Attorney General may make a reference in relation to a proposed Assembly Measure at any time during— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) the period of four weeks beginning with the passing of the proposed Assembly Measure, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) any period of four weeks beginning with any subsequent approval of the proposed Assembly Measure in accordance with provision included in the standing orders in compliance with section 98(7). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(3) No reference may be made in relation to a proposed Assembly Measure— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) by the Counsel General if the Counsel General has notified the Clerk that no reference is to be made in relation to it by the Counsel General, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) by the Attorney General if the Attorney General has notified the Clerk that no reference is to be made in relation to it by the Attorney General. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(4) But subsection (3) does not apply if the proposed Assembly Measure has been approved as mentioned in subsection (2)(b) since the notification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
