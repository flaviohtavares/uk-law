## 97. Introduction of proposed Assembly Measures (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-70d8b16fe610814bb3ba5aa76d90a5f3][^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960][^c16527331][^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A proposed Assembly Measure may, subject to the standing orders, be introduced in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd[^c-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] —

- (a) by the First Minister, any Welsh Minister appointed under section 48, any Deputy Welsh Minister or the Counsel General, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) by any other *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Member of the Senedd[^c-77862aa11ba8e490f1db71c8f7e15ec1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] .

(2) The person in charge of a proposed Assembly Measure must, on or before the introduction of the proposed Assembly Measure, state that, in that person's view, its provisions would be within the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd's *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] legislative competence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(3) The Presiding Officer must, on or before the introduction of a proposed Assembly Measure in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] —

- (a) decide whether or not, in the view of the Presiding Officer, the provisions of the proposed Assembly Measure would be within the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd's *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] legislative competence, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) state that decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(4) A statement under this section must be made in both English and Welsh; but, subject to that, the form of the statement and the manner in which it is to be made are to be determined under the standing orders. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(5) The standing orders— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) may provide for a statement under this section to be published, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) if they do so, must provide for it to be published in both English and Welsh. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
