## 96. Scrutiny of proposed Orders in Council (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-70d8b16fe610814bb3ba5aa76d90a5f3][^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960][^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Counsel General or the Attorney General may refer to the Supreme Court for decision the question whether a matter which a proposed Order in Council under section 95 proposes to add to Part 1 of Schedule 5 relates to a field listed in that Part. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
