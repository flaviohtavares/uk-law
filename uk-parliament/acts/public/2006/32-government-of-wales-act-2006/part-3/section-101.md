# Government of Wales Act 2006, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-09-16. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/32

## 101. Power to intervene in certain cases (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-70d8b16fe610814bb3ba5aa76d90a5f3][^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960][^c16527371][^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies if a proposed Assembly Measure contains provisions which the Secretary of State has reasonable grounds to believe— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) would have an adverse effect on any matter which is not specified in Part 1 of Schedule 5 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] (or falls within any of the exceptions specified in paragraph A1 of Part 2 of that Schedule)[^c20307301] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960],
- (b) might have a serious adverse impact on water resources in England, water supply in England or the quality of water in England, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (c) would have an adverse effect on the operation of the law as it applies in England, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (d) would be incompatible with any international obligation or the interests of defence or national security. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(2) The Secretary of State may make an order prohibiting the Clerk from submitting the proposed Assembly Measure for approval by Her Majesty in Council. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(3) The order must identify the proposed Assembly Measure and the provisions in question and state the reasons for making the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(4) The order may be made at any time during— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) the period of four weeks beginning with the passing of the proposed Assembly Measure, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) any period of four weeks beginning with any subsequent approval of the proposed Assembly Measure in accordance with provision included in the standing orders in compliance with section 98(7), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (c) if a reference is made in relation to the proposed Assembly Measure under section 99, the period of four weeks beginning with the reference being decided or otherwise disposed of by the Supreme Court. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(5) The Secretary of State must not make an order in relation to a proposed Assembly Measure if the Secretary of State has notified the Clerk that no order is to be made in relation to the proposed Assembly Measure. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(6) Subsection (5) does not apply if the proposed Assembly Measure has been approved as mentioned in subsection (4)(b) since the notification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(7) An order in force under this section at a time when such approval is given ceases to have effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(8) A statutory instrument containing an order under this section is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
