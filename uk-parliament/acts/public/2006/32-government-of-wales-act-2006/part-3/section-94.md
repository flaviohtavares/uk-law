# Government of Wales Act 2006, section 94

## 94. Legislative competence (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960][^c16527301][^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Part, an Assembly Measure may make any provision that could be made by an Act of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(2) An Assembly Measure is not law so far as any provision of the Assembly Measure is outside the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd's[^c-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] legislative competence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(3) A provision of an Assembly Measure is within the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd's *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] legislative competence only if it falls within subsection (4) or (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(4) A provision of an Assembly Measure falls within this subsection if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) it relates to one or more of the matters specified in Part 1 of Schedule 5 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] and does not fall within any of the exceptions specified in paragraph A1 of Part 2 of that Schedule (whether or not the exception is under a heading corresponding to the field which includes the matter).[^c20307131] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) it neither applies otherwise than in relation to Wales nor confers, imposes, modifies or removes (or gives power to confer, impose, modify or remove) functions exercisable otherwise than in relation to Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(5) A provision of an Assembly Measure falls within this subsection if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) it provides for the enforcement of a provision (of that or any other Assembly Measure) which falls within subsection (4) or it is otherwise appropriate for making such a provision effective, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) it is otherwise incidental to, or consequential on, such a provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(6) But a provision which falls within subsection (4) or (5) is outside the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd's *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] legislative competence if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) it breaches any of the restrictions in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] paragraphs 1 to 6 of[^c20307241] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Part 2 of Schedule 5, having regard to any exception in Part 3 of that Schedule from those restrictions, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b)[^c20926071] it extends otherwise than only to England and Wales, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (c) it is incompatible with the Convention rights or with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] EU[^c-9044639e532e57354bf1856bd34f04ef] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] law. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(7) For the purposes of this section the question whether a provision of an Assembly Measure relates to one or more of the matters specified in Part 1 of Schedule 5 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] (or falls within any of the exceptions specified in paragraph A1 of Part 2 of that Schedule)[^c20307261] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] is to be determined by reference to the purpose of the provision, having regard (among other things) to its effect in all the circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
