## 100. ECJ (affected by repeal, see note) references (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-70d8b16fe610814bb3ba5aa76d90a5f3][^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960][^c16527361][^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960][^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) a reference has been made in relation to a proposed Assembly Measure under section 99, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) a reference for a preliminary European Court ruling has been made by the Supreme Court in connection with that reference, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (c) neither of those references has been decided or otherwise disposed of. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(2) If the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd[^key-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] resolves that it wishes to reconsider the proposed Assembly Measure— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

- (a) the Clerk must notify the Counsel General and the Attorney General of that fact, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
- (b) the person who made the reference in relation to the proposed Assembly Measure under section 99 must request the withdrawal of the reference. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(3) In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] **a reference for a preliminary European Court ruling *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]** ” means a reference of a question to the European Court under Article 234 of the Treaty establishing the European Community, Article 41 of the Treaty establishing the European Coal and Steel Community or Article 150 of the Treaty establishing the European Atomic Energy Community. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
