## 93. Assembly Measures (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960][^c16527291][^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd[^c-60edd79c70d763519861630d1afb7962] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] may make laws, to be known as Measures of the National *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] for Wales or Mesurau Cynulliad Cenedlaethol Cymru (referred to in this Act as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] **Assembly Measures *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]** ”).

(2) A proposed Assembly Measure is enacted by being passed by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] and approved by Her Majesty in Council. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(3) The validity of an Assembly Measure is not affected by any invalidity in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] Senedd *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960] proceedings leading to its enactment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(4) Every Assembly Measure is to be judicially noticed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]

(5) This Part does not affect the power of the Parliament of the United Kingdom to make laws for Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-ccae0853747c790d8d5bafab32b97960]
