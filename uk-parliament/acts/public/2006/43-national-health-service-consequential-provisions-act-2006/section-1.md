# National Health Service (Consequential Provisions) Act 2006, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/43
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 1. Meaning of “the consolidating Acts”

*Extent: E+W*

In this Act “**the consolidating Acts**” means—

- (a) the National Health Service Act 2006 (c. 41),
- (b) the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006 (c. 42), and
- (c) this Act, to the extent that it reproduces the effect of provisions repealed or revoked by this Act.
