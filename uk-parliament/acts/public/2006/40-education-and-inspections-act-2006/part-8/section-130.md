## 130. Action plans following area inspections[^c-3321cbb217b5b2a554f2926ffd5c549d][^c17507831]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where the Chief Inspector publishes a report of an area inspection conducted under section 128.

(2)[^c-ac67acacb921085c45dd079807f4c2ff] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The Secretary of State may direct a local authority in England[^c-aa3f6e92700f80e15a9483a78e04859b] whose area is wholly or partly within the area covered by the report to prepare a written statement of—

- (a) the action which they propose to take in the light of the report, and
- (b) the period within which they propose to take that action.

(4) In preparing a statement under subsection[^c-8ccfe5ec5370b6c220c40dca26834e66]... (3) [^c-26ce21b15eb9b4afc8bd665ffc831641]... the authority must consult such persons as the Secretary of State may direct.

(5) The [^c-43f0d7443be0ab5986dba9ba4a837b68]... authority must—

- (a) publish the statement within such period, and in such manner, as may be prescribed by regulations made by the Secretary of State; and
- (b) send copies of it to such persons as may be so prescribed.

(6)[^c-e8c42aaca36d70a0d4bc020e351a5739] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
