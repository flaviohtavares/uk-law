# Education and Inspections Act 2006, section 116

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 116. Functions of the Office

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Office has the following functions—

- (a)[^c-bdaf0e63675baca61ce14c677734d5e3][^c-a35d9e67eb8ca6b74a921950f09cde9c] to determine strategic priorities for the Chief Inspector in connection with the performance of his functions;
- (b)[^c-bdaf0e63675baca61ce14c677734d5e3] to determine strategic objectives and targets relating to such priorities; and
- (c)[^c-78059ce1e76cf09006f4fa3e53277099] to secure that the Chief Inspector's functions are performed efficiently and effectively.

(2)[^c-78059ce1e76cf09006f4fa3e53277099] The Office is to have such other functions in connection with the performance of the Chief Inspector's functions as may be assigned to it by the Secretary of State.
