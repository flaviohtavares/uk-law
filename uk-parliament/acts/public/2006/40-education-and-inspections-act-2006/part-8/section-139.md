## 139. Power of entry[^c-e67834758d51fc14941e9c69cc3a3cd2][^c-43916382b3301898fbcc3bf9932c82ac]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies to—

- (a) any inspection conducted by the Chief Inspector under section 136, [^c-bf5664f3d647240a3841be9fdfa64683]...
- (b)[^c-bf5664f3d647240a3841be9fdfa64683] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) The Chief Inspector may, at any reasonable time, enter any premises for the purposes of the inspection [^c-47176e0b74f6b9303730702c69416c28]..., other than any premises excluded by subsection (3).

(3) The premises excluded by this subsection are any domestic premises that are not a school (within the meaning of EA 1996).
