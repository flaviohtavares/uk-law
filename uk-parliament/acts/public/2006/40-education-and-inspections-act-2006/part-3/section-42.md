# Education and Inspections Act 2006, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Support for parental preferences[^key-357ae4347b1363a14afec7fe9b3c39fd]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 86 of SSFA 1998 (parental preferences) after subsection (1) insert—

> (1A) A local authority[^key-0ea7acc4831332590d0c9da4689eca3c] in England shall provide advice and assistance to parents of children in the area of the authority in connection with the preferences expressed or to be expressed by them in accordance with the arrangements made under subsection (1).
