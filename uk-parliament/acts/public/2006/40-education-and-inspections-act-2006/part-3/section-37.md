# Education and Inspections Act 2006, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Staff at foundation or voluntary schools with religious character[^key-342ad36b20eba7bc5885680c23d1bdb8]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-e9519bd97a01013d29a540354525320e] In section 58 of SSFA 1998 (appointment and dismissal of certain teachers at schools with a religious character), omit subsection (4) (which prevents the head teacher of a foundation or voluntary controlled school being a reserved teacher).

(2) In section 60 of SSFA 1998 (staff at foundation or voluntary school with religious character)—

- (a)[^key-e9519bd97a01013d29a540354525320e] in subsection (4), after “(whether foundation or voluntary controlled)” insert “ in a case where the head teacher is not to be a reserved teacher ”, and
- (b) in subsection (6), after “voluntary aided school” insert “ in Wales ”.
