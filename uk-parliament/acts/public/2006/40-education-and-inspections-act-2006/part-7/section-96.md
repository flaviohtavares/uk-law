> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 96. Repeals consequential on provisions of Chapter 1[^c-c362c097853dbcc46bb8b6dd21ec8b2c]

*Extent: E+W*

The following provisions (which are superseded by sections 88 to 93) cease to have effect—

- (a)[^c-95b0e4583eb517e2556c6a8091da1a82] sections 550A and 550B of EA 1996;
- (b)[^c-761a1b6a4e3aae1e071d6b6f7115eb49][^c-638439cbfe8825d183e1487a95abdca0] section 61 of SSFA 1998.
