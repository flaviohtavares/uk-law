# Education and Inspections Act 2006, section 110

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Sums received under section 444A of EA 1996[^c-1ecfe20d39672ae36cc99edfa72eed16]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 444A of EA 1996 (penalty notice in respect of failure to secure regular attendance at school of registered pupil) for subsection (6) substitute—

> (6) Sums received by a local authority[^c-0ea7acc4831332590d0c9da4689eca3c] under this section may be used by the authority for the purposes of any of its functions which may be specified in regulations but, to the extent that they are not so used, must be paid in accordance with regulations to the Secretary of State.
