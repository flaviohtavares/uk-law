# Education and Inspections Act 2006, section 181

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/40

## 181. Orders and regulations: general provisions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any power to make an order or regulations conferred by this Act on the Secretary of State or the Assembly is exercisable by statutory instrument. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-faa94b994272b6f392c35e85a38767bb]

Any power of the Secretary of State or the Welsh Ministers to make an order or regulations under this Act is exercisable by statutory instrument. *(Extent: E+W)*

(2) Any power of the Secretary of State or the Assembly *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0a2d10287e2a27db2ca0d1d2b9525124] the Welsh Ministers *(Extent: E+W)* to make an order or regulations under this Act includes power—

- (a) to make different provision for different cases or areas,
- (b) to make provision generally or in relation to specific cases, and
- (c) to make such incidental, supplementary, transitional or saving provision as the Secretary of State or the Assembly thinks *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-44d22c0062e96543729365427b0b5547] the Welsh Ministers think *(Extent: E+W)* fit.
