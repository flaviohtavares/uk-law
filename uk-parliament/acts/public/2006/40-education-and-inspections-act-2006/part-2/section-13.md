# Education and Inspections Act 2006, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 13. Schools established outside area of relevant local authority[^c-29492e189a950926b580c8fcd2f8dd89][^c-6674810d9345c8ca96c6dfbe40cc8d46][^c-81ffb0eb20e62c98df124b83aeaf3944]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations may modify the provisions of sections 7 to 12 and Schedule 2 in their application to cases where—

- (a) in the case of proposals published under section 7, the school is proposed to be established in an area in England other than that of the local authority[^c-0ea7acc4831332590d0c9da4689eca3c] who published the notice under that section, or
- (b) in the case of proposals published under section 10 [^c-2e561e78d94f8ceb15290b791c476a7d]..., the school is proposed to be established in an area in England other than that of the local authority who it is proposed should maintain the school.
