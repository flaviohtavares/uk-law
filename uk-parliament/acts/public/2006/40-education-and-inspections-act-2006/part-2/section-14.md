> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/40

## 14. Local authority in England not to establish school in Wales[^key-2ded9caa4f8d29790b3e17e70c9310f7][^key-81ffb0eb20e62c98df124b83aeaf3944]

*Extent: E+W*

No proposals may be published under this Part or any other enactment for the establishment of a school in Wales which is proposed to be maintained by a local authority[^key-0ea7acc4831332590d0c9da4689eca3c] in England.
