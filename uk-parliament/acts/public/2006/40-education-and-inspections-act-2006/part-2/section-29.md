# Education and Inspections Act 2006, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 29. Abolition of school organisation committees[^c-0e701413d86936506f2e1d7a1f5edcd0]

*Extent: E+W*

Section 24 of, and Schedule 4 to, SSFA 1998 (which require local authorities[^c-0ea7acc4831332590d0c9da4689eca3c] in England to establish a school organisation committee for their area) cease to have effect.
