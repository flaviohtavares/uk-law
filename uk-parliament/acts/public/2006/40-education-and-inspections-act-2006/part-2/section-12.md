# Education and Inspections Act 2006, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/40

## 12. Establishment of school as federated school[^key-fe2b536719c2cc40f198ddac2322528b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Proposals under—

- (a) section 7 or 10[^key-60fec8188c37a4d57a669474d4d8df41], [^key-63dafc861e1759726771e8704759393f] ...
- (b)[^key-63dafc861e1759726771e8704759393f] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

for the establishment of a new maintained[^key-fd16b51272711d58f158dcf5c55c49e8] school in England may relate to the establishment of the school as a federated school.

(2) In this section “**federated school**” has the meaning given by section 24(2) of EA 2002.
