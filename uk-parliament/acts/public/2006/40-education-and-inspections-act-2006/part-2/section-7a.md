# Education and Inspections Act 2006, section 7A

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 7A. Withdrawal of notices under section 7[^c-d535ab96b773d5df4ee7136544e86159]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where a local authority have published a notice under section 7.

(2) At any time before the date specified in the notice—

- (a) the local authority may withdraw it [^c-a508b36f16c720e24d10e5a2e52ff58d]..., or
- (b) the Secretary of State may direct the local authority to withdraw it.

(3)[^c-9bbb8e7c27ce3f583e45d90d44f8bbc6] If the local authority withdraw the notice in accordance with subsection (2)(a) or (b), the local authority must notify the Secretary of State.
