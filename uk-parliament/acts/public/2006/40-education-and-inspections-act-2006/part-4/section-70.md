## 70. Governing bodies consisting of interim executive members[^key-696498abe72bf8d911c6015d95233156]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 6 has effect in relation to any school in respect of which a notice has been given—

- (a) under section 65(1) by the local authority[^key-0ea7acc4831332590d0c9da4689eca3c], or
- (b) under section 69(1) by the Secretary of State.
