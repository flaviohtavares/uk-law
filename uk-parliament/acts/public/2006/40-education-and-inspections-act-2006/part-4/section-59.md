# Education and Inspections Act 2006, section 59

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Meaning of “maintained school” and “eligible for intervention”[^c-dd39aea309b5f9e601bcb634a9d17e0d]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part “**maintained school**” means any of the following schools in England—

- (a) a community, foundation or voluntary school,
- (b) a community or foundation special school, or
- (c) a maintained nursery school.

(2) In this Part, references to a school being “eligible for intervention” are to be read in accordance with—

- section 60 (performance standards and safety warning notice[^c-3f591d860b3fcd24287358d92a239cbc]),
- section 60A (teachers' pay and conditions warning notice),[^M_F_259fba99-ecd7-4fb9-827d-cfb504b7bffb]
- section 60B (coasting schools),[^c-5b55353f7d9180a8f1793af818aa9578]
- section 61 (school requiring significant improvement), and
- section 62 (school requiring special measures).
