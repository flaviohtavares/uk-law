# Education and Inspections Act 2006, section 65

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (19).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Power of local authority to provide for governing body to consist of interim executive members[^c-6e16dc01fa36b732d0cf3792802158de][^c-81ffb0eb20e62c98df124b83aeaf3944]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If at any time a maintained school is eligible for intervention, the local authority[^c-0ea7acc4831332590d0c9da4689eca3c] may, with the consent of the Secretary of State, give the governing body a notice in writing stating that, as from a date specified in the notice, the governing body are to be constituted in accordance with Schedule 6 (governing bodies consisting of interim executive members).

(2) Before exercising the power conferred by subsection (1), the local authority must consult—

- (a) the governing body of the school,
- (b) in the case of a foundation or voluntary school which is a Church of England school or a Roman Catholic Church school, the appropriate diocesan authority, and
- (c) in the case of any other foundation or voluntary school, the person or persons by whom the foundation governors are appointed.
