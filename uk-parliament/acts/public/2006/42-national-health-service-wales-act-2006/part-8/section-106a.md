# National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006, section 106A

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## (not yet in force: 106A). (not yet in force: Notices and penalties)[^c20965301]

*Extent: E+W*

**Not yet in force.**

((not yet in force: 1)) *(not yet in force: The Welsh Ministers may by regulations provide that where a practitioner who provides pharmaceutical services or general ophthalmic services under arrangements with a Local Health Board breaches a term of those arrangements, the Local Health Board may—)*

- ((not yet in force: a)) *(not yet in force: by a notice require the practitioner to do, or not do, specified things or things of a specified description within a specified period, or)*
- ((not yet in force: b)) *(not yet in force: in prescribed circumstances or for a prescribed period, withhold all or part of a payment due to the practitioner under the arrangements.)*

((not yet in force: 2)) *(not yet in force: Regulations under this section must include provision conferring on such persons as may be prescribed rights of appeal from decisions of Local Health Boards made by virtue of this section.)*

((not yet in force: 3)) *(not yet in force: In this section—)*

- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: practitioner)****(not yet in force: ” means a person included in an ophthalmic list or a pharmaceutical list, and)*
- *(not yet in force: “)****(not yet in force: specified)****(not yet in force: ” means specified in a notice under paragraph (a) of subsection (1).)*
