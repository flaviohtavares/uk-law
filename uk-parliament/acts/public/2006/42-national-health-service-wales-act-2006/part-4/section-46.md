# National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (24).** Text as at 2026-03-28. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. GMS contracts: prescription of drugs, etc

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A general medical services contract must contain provision requiring the contractor or contractors to comply with any directions given by the Welsh Ministers for the purposes of this section as to the drugs, medicines or other substances which may or may not be ordered for patients in the provision of medical services under the contract.

(2) A direction under this section must, subject to subsection (3), be given by regulations.

(3) A direction under this section may be given by an instrument in writing where it gives effect to a request made in writing to the Welsh Ministers by a person who is a holder of a UK[^c-2c12589dbb9b4cf9d6a281e10ac1c5f2] marketing authorisation in respect of the drug, medicine or other substance to which the request relates.

(4)[^c-3eee7a5c836b88238d00f5eec34e3a34] “**UK marketing authorisation**” has the meaning given by regulation 8(1) of the Human Medicines Regulations 2012 (S.I. 2012/1916).
