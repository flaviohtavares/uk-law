## 48. Supervisory authorities: notification of barring &c. in respect of children

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) a person is [^key-05ba59b2fa699ce0f790fbf0ea1fc7b4]... included in the children's barred list,
- (b) DBS[^key-199ae15e29852868c6d2ba94a0e46aad] becomes aware that a person is subject to a relevant children's disqualification, [^key-839f33d512042513c92b56aebe4f952a]...
- (c)[^key-839f33d512042513c92b56aebe4f952a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) DBS[^key-d1e2e856d5c2bdc4cbd6338f4921ba77] must notify every interested supervisory authority of the circumstance mentioned in paragraph (a), (b) or (c) (as the case may be) of subsection (1).

(3) A supervisory authority is an interested supervisory authority only if—

- (a) it has applied to DBS[^key-8bc31f7a10603186681ec25537060a75] to be notified if any of the circumstances mentioned in subsection (1) occurs in relation to the person, and
- (b) the application has not been withdrawn.

(4) A supervisory authority may apply to DBS[^key-b8de537bb858ee94fbdafcb4ad975ee3] under subsection (3)(a) only if the notification is required in connection with the exercise of a function of the supervisory authority mentioned in section 45(7).

(5) For the purposes of subsection (3)(b) an application is withdrawn if the supervisory authority notifies DBS[^key-56327ef923ef726725d2b1ed285cbb1c] that it no longer wishes to be notified if any of the circumstances mentioned in subsection (1) occurs in relation to the person.

(6)[^key-f37fa557f9fe99b47301ad407d8021f3] A person is subject to a relevant children's disqualification if he is included in a list maintained under the law of Scotland or Northern Ireland which the Secretary of State specifies by order as corresponding to the children's barred list.

(7)[^key-5de7c1953d5aa5907aa407d7845ffc47] The Secretary of State may provide that in prescribed circumstances a supervisory authority is not an interested supervisory authority for the purposes of this section.

(8)[^key-5de7c1953d5aa5907aa407d7845ffc47] DBS[^key-106c52e44a6f58bebad661ff22d7d3e8] may determine[^key-4787b209f1dd3f8dbf8e143a1d7987f3] the form, manner and contents of an application for the purposes of this section.
