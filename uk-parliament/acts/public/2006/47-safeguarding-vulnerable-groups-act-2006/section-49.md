## 49. Supervisory authorities: notification of barring &c. in respect of vulnerable adults

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) a person is [^c-c3a94c1bf2926f4f9450b437ee24f7a7]... included in the adults' barred list,
- (b) DBS[^c-129bb1ca2bb26c7f604e2bb3ce902c31] becomes aware that a person is subject to a relevant adults' disqualification, [^c-537f14e064d020330f3946a80d82928e]...
- (c)[^c-537f14e064d020330f3946a80d82928e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) DBS[^c-860cbd2eb6efda96a89c50cf40472a76] must notify every interested supervisory authority of the circumstance mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b)[^c-7e8fd7201867def028c6b320ab168437] (as the case may be) of subsection (1).

(3) A supervisory authority is an interested supervisory authority only if—

- (a) it has applied to DBS[^c-99d93522889cfcb6cdc3214641aaefc2] to be notified if any of the circumstances mentioned in subsection (1) occurs in relation to the person, and
- (b) the application has not been withdrawn.

(4) A supervisory authority may apply to DBS[^c-ba91b82a8a1a18257c840fcceff2ef5f] under subsection (3)(a) only if the notification is required in connection with the exercise of a function of the supervisory authority mentioned in section 45(7).

(5) For the purposes of subsection (3)(b) an application is withdrawn if the supervisory authority notifies DBS[^c-80ff97fae97ca6592aeb8b995259476a] that it no longer wishes to be notified if any of the circumstances mentioned in subsection (1) occurs in relation to the person.

(6)[^c-08c1aad5030c4eb9c7b9b8d394465604] A person is subject to a relevant adults' disqualification if he is included in a list maintained under the law of Scotland or Northern Ireland which the Secretary of State specifies by order as corresponding to the adults' barred list.

(7)[^c-d2f47f2e54d8003b9b731be7e8a2904a] The Secretary of State may provide that in prescribed circumstances a supervisory authority is not an interested supervisory authority for the purposes of this section.

(8)[^c-d2f47f2e54d8003b9b731be7e8a2904a] DBS[^c-69d7aa0b0fe65bcb84caf76826b88463] may determine[^c-ff3ee7737b07343636b55109460cf3e6] the form, manner and contents of an application for the purposes of this section.
