# Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006, section 37

## 37. Regulated activity providers: duty to provide information on request &c.[^c-9a081d0a125c6ba23faa8b3b12e063da][^c-8a4d9038beffaebaa5b069a05ba87dd7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if DBS[^c-fa68ad77cea91818ef85b5394098bd51] is considering—

- (a) whether to include any person in a barred list;
- (b) whether to remove any person from a barred list.

(2)[^c-9a6aefaddbe5bf5123d4d0b1d3dca341] DBS may require—

- (a) any regulated activity provider who has made arrangements for that person to engage in regulated activity (whether or not the arrangements are still in place),
- (b)[^c-91e529f2b6355a3fbf95eaf82c184516] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) any personnel supplier which is an employment agency or employment business and which acts for or has acted for that person, or
- (d) any personnel supplier which is an educational institution and which has supplied that person to another person for him to engage in regulated [^c-ab50ae2722c88771911e620c3c58a256]... activity,

to provide DBS with any prescribed information he or it holds relating to the person.

(3) An employment agency acts for a person if it makes arrangements with him with a view to—

- (a) finding him relevant employment with an employer, or
- (b) supplying him to employers for relevant employment by them.

(4) Relevant employment is employment which consists in or involves engaging in regulated [^c-4619123074b0766cdc15a6abc77e51e3]... activity.

(5) An employment business acts for a person if it employs him to engage in regulated [^c-9f7a8c173410ca348bedd0c8d4326cef]... activity for and under the control of other persons.

(6) In this section “**employment**” has the same meaning as in the Employment Agencies Act 1973 (c. 35).
