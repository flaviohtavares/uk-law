# Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006, section 38

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/47
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Duty to provide information: offences[^c-bf487563c36a14c6d6f02d775def8423][^c-599e73567050070350fcabdff2872f9e][^c-0cbf3889169b6f8057f39643b4e514d0]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person commits an offence if—

- (a) he is required under section 35 or 36 or in pursuance of section 37 to provide information to DBS[^c-fa68ad77cea91818ef85b5394098bd51] , and
- (b) he fails, without reasonable excuse, to provide the information.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
