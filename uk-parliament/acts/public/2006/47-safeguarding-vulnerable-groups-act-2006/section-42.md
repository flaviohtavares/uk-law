# Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/47
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Registers: duty to provide information on request[^key-7b60f88920c495fb2bd270c02b5a825e][^key-12f2901d89a6bf1a9021648b69c9be48]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if DBS[^key-fa68ad77cea91818ef85b5394098bd51] is considering—

- (a) whether to include in a barred list a person who appears on a relevant register;
- (b) whether to remove such a person from a barred list.

(2)[^key-8a81d21b65cf6cf254177fc2a5362afc] DBS may require the keeper of the register to provide it with any prescribed information he holds relating to the person.

(3) The keeper of the register must comply with a requirement under subsection (2).

(4) References to a relevant register and the keeper of a relevant register must be construed in accordance with section 41(7).
