# Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/47
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Offences: companies &c.[^c-99077cb47cd661538799f7d68e3beb51]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If an offence under section 9 [^c-2d11ead380c9a8b8cad9d1f5f4b37143]... or 38 [^c-89c89a45e486d1ca4db283cd822fcf37]... is committed by a body corporate and is proved to have been committed with the consent or connivance of, or to be attributable to neglect on the part of—

- (a) a director, manager, secretary or other similar officer of the body, or
- (b) a person purporting to act in such a capacity,

he (as well as the body) commits the offence.

(2) If an offence under section 9 [^c-7b277ebe8a97ed12568c0ce2ae5680ed]... or 38 [^c-6c402bb9f851be741aa3e9f59afc4c87]... is committed by a partnership (whether or not a limited partnership) and is proved to have been committed with the consent or connivance of, or to be attributable to neglect on the part of—

- (a) a partner, or
- (b) a person purporting to act as a partner,

he (as well as the partnership) commits the offence.

(3) In subsection (1), “**director**”, in relation to a body corporate whose affairs are managed by its members, means a member of the body.
