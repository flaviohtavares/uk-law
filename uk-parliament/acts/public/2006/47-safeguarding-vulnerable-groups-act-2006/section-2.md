# Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/47
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Barred lists[^key-3ae26f6280110a65adb8c95ea5a4f6de][^key-cf1a13496cedde67afce6d013b7fb7c7][^key-5cfeadf889ed8107ac9ace5f074d91c1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) DBS[^key-acf6b2c73399eff8a1b8af43d2cacdbd] must [^key-6d2f38fd8130b694d5476860015fc476]... maintain—

- (a) the children's barred list;
- (b) the adults' barred list.

(2)[^key-0b553e749610ee422c8c79addbd2b17b][^key-f0d281dc3a4af38cd94395aecdf61677] Part 1 of Schedule 3 applies for the purpose of determining whether an individual is included in the children's barred list.

(3)[^key-0b553e749610ee422c8c79addbd2b17b][^key-f0d281dc3a4af38cd94395aecdf61677] Part 2 of that Schedule applies for the purpose of determining whether an individual is included in the adults' barred list.

(4)[^key-de5c33c609249bc01af587d8b3f3a6f5][^key-f0d281dc3a4af38cd94395aecdf61677][^key-49a9208c97500bdab35ded8dbc402dfb] Part 3 of that Schedule contains supplementary provision.

(5)[^key-cb295536c562e90d393ef16aa137e124] In respect of an individual who is included in a barred list, DBS must keep other information of such description as is prescribed.
