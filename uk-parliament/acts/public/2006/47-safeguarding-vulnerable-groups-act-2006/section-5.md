# Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (46).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2006/47

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Regulated activity[^key-2102b99f39dd363d0fbd4cd129e74483]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-9314cf8b70baab25fb058d8531bdeb4d][^key-c3d3fd5700f8d8d1355d5c0ddea48a48] A reference to regulated activity relating to children must be construed in accordance with Part 1 of Schedule 4.

(2)[^key-9314cf8b70baab25fb058d8531bdeb4d][^key-c3d3fd5700f8d8d1355d5c0ddea48a48] A reference to regulated activity relating to vulnerable adults must be construed in accordance with Part 2 of that Schedule.

(3)[^key-a1305d8d8a1c7a28ec577e374c5f1004] The Secretary of State may by order amend that Schedule, or any of the modifications of that Schedule in the provisions mentioned in subsection (4), so as to vary the meaning of—

- (a) regulated activity relating to children;
- (b) regulated activity relating to vulnerable adults.

(4)[^key-a1305d8d8a1c7a28ec577e374c5f1004] The provisions are—

- section 7(5);
- section 9(5);
- [^key-c6e97cc5be80de4547fe9ca1a6f893d0]...
- section 19(8);
- [^key-04fd0922667e56e1db6795836cafea69]....
