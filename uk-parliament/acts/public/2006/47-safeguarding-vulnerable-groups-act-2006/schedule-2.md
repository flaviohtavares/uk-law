### Staff transfer schemes (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]

**1.**[^c-92d88401276f628a8f09f05da44b73a8] (1) The Secretary of State may make a scheme (a “staff transfer scheme”) providing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]

- (a) for a person employed in the civil service of the state to become an employee of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a] ISA[^c-41c70181ded147c8fe9b4d92597c485d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a];
- (b) for his terms of employment to have effect (subject to any necessary modifications) as the terms of his contract of employment with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a] ISA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a];
- (c) for the transfer to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a] ISA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a] of the rights, powers, duties and liabilities of the Crown under or in connection with his terms of employment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]
- (d) for anything done (or having effect as if done) before that transfer by or in relation to the Crown in respect of the terms of employment or the person to be treated as having been done by or in relation to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a] ISA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a].

(2) A staff transfer scheme may provide for a period before a person became an employee of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a] ISA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a] to count as a period during which he was such an employee (and for the operation of the scheme not to be treated as having interrupted the continuity of that period). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]

(3) A staff transfer scheme may provide for a person in the civil service of the state who would otherwise become an employee of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a] ISA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a] not to become such an employee if he gives notice objecting to the operation of the scheme in relation to him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]

(4) A staff transfer scheme may provide for any person who would be treated (whether by an enactment or otherwise) as having his employment terminated by the operation of the scheme not to be so treated. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]

### Property transfer schemes (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]

**2.**[^c-6dbd00f3d98001220538fd3f4adccc08] (1) The Secretary of State may make a scheme (a “property transfer scheme”) providing for the transfer to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a] ISA[^c-2022ecd76dbcec0d0ac916a3c2eb7c8d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a] of any property, rights or liabilities of the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]

(2) The things that may be transferred by a property transfer scheme include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]

- (a) property, rights and liabilities that could not otherwise be transferred; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]
- (b) property acquired, and rights and liabilities arising, after the making of the scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]

(3) A property transfer scheme may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]

- (a) create interests in, or rights in relation to, anything that is or could be transferred by the scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]
- (b) impose liabilities in relation to anything that is or could be transferred or created by the scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]
- (c) apportion property, rights and liabilities; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]
- (d) provide for things done by or in relation to the Secretary of State in connection with any property, rights or liabilities transferred by the scheme to be treated as done, or to be continued, by or in relation to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a] ISA *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a];
- (e) make provision about the continuation of legal proceedings. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]

### Schemes: supplementary (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]

**3.**[^c-eae1c12ecc1eb01ee36baf58da96d32c] A staff transfer scheme, or property transfer scheme, may make supplementary, incidental, transitional and consequential provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-be55ff9ad974a673a4a73128cdd8bd6a]
