## 4. Appeals[^c-91a718ff6cf27f83cd227391ef9c0428][^c-05545bbb71c5a225cd98fc7ee6c2f365][^c-d5b479788ac65ef43028d69bdff627f7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-e87ee813f94071b47e7b0a98b49ac5cf] An individual who is included in a barred list may appeal to the Upper[^c-121aff50487990d5096b7cfaf7a1aca1] Tribunal against—

- (a)[^c-29410aba6f9dad2dff401e8ac14fd896] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) a decision under paragraph 2,[^c-bf6f1b01aaf16f00ca604c8ae531f281] 3, 5, 8,[^c-ba042ec8ee569a5e51401c99f1539475] 9 or 11 of Schedule 3[^c-4005f6824ef89c5ce29bba6a6bfbc129] to include him in the list;
- (c) a decision under paragraph 17 , 18 or 18A[^c-2eaaf3443e9f6ca77559301daf2a80f5] of that Schedule not to remove him from the list.

(2)[^c-e87ee813f94071b47e7b0a98b49ac5cf] An appeal under subsection (1) may be made only on the grounds that DBS[^c-fb5201c95a3e716beb0d04673e70d86f] has made a mistake—

- (a) on any point of law;
- (b) in any finding of fact which it has made and on which the decision mentioned in that subsection was based.

(3)[^c-e87ee813f94071b47e7b0a98b49ac5cf] For the purposes of subsection (2), the decision whether or not it is appropriate for an individual to be included in a barred list is not a question of law or fact.

(4)[^c-e87ee813f94071b47e7b0a98b49ac5cf] An appeal under subsection (1) may be made only with the permission of the Upper Tribunal.

(5)[^c-e87ee813f94071b47e7b0a98b49ac5cf] Unless the Upper Tribunal finds that DBS has made a mistake of law or fact, it must confirm the decision of DBS .

(6)[^c-e87ee813f94071b47e7b0a98b49ac5cf] If the Upper Tribunal finds that DBS has made such a mistake it must—

- (a) direct DBS to remove the person from the list, or
- (b) remit the matter to DBS for a new decision.

(7)[^c-e87ee813f94071b47e7b0a98b49ac5cf] If the Upper Tribunal remits a matter to DBS under subsection (6)(b)—

- (a) the Upper Tribunal may set out any findings of fact which it has made (on which DBS must base its new decision); and
- (b) the person must be removed from the list until DBS makes its new decision, unless the Upper Tribunal directs otherwise.
