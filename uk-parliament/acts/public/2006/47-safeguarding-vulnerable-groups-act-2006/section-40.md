# Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006, section 40

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Local authorities: duty to provide information on request[^c-0bacffa893602d0616029b1f213b7cb6][^c-b575787795dc27ce16a8b084f3594a8f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if DBS[^c-fa68ad77cea91818ef85b5394098bd51] is considering—

- (a) whether to include any person in a barred list;
- (b) whether to remove any person from a barred list.

(2)[^c-543ef55e962c7ea9e6bb70fbc6a42234] If DBS thinks that a local authority hold any prescribed information relating to the person, it may require the authority to provide it with the information.

(3) The local authority must comply with a requirement under subsection (2).

(4) “**Local authority**” has the same meaning as in section 1 of the Local Authorities (Goods and Services) Act 1970.
