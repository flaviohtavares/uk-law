## 19. Offences: other persons

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-c23ac882e8cecdb5a494600941734ee9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^key-73bf6c7a626b053e9a0b34a33fc0ce69] A person commits an offence if, in the course of acting or appearing to act on behalf of a personnel supplier—

- (a) he supplies an individual (B) to another (P),
- (b) he knows or has reason to believe that P will make arrangements for B to engage in regulated activity from which B is barred, and
- (c) he knows or has reason to believe that B is barred from the activity.

(3)[^key-6c0568fa5e6ecddba384ef3457c4e0a5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-6c0568fa5e6ecddba384ef3457c4e0a5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5)[^key-73bf6c7a626b053e9a0b34a33fc0ce69] A person guilty of an offence under subsection (2) is liable—

- (a) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years, or to a fine, or to both;
- (b) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the general limit in a magistrates’ court[^key-9da1532dda9a763bb95e3ef8ba06b19b], or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or to both.
