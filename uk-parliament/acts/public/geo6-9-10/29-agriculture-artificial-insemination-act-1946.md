# Agriculture (Artificial Insemination) Act 1946

> Text as at 2025-06-16.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

*Extent: E+W+S*

Agriculture (Artificial Insemination) Act 1946 *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d6f41f4740545ab59959279260a3689b]

1946 c. 29 *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d6f41f4740545ab59959279260a3689b]

An Act to enable the Minister of Agriculture and Fisheries and the Secretary of State to make contributions and establish centres for the purposes of research as to the practice of artificial insemination of livestock; to provide for the payment of grants out of moneys provided by Parliament in respect of initial losses incurred in the operation of certain centres for the artificial insemination of cattle; and for purposes connected therewith. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d6f41f4740545ab59959279260a3689b]

\[6th March 1946\] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d6f41f4740545ab59959279260a3689b][^c6324751][^c-127d1e1279fe4fdbe0aa64772639256f]

## 1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note). Power of Minister of Agriculture and Fisheries and Secretary of State to make contributions and establish centres for the purposes of research as to the artificial insemination of livestock. (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note)[^c6324771][^c-845d8a6cb8d4f63b40acbc9bc4618b7a][^c-d6f41f4740545ab59959279260a3689b][^c-d6f41f4740545ab59959279260a3689b][^c6324761]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d6f41f4740545ab59959279260a3689b] With a view to developing and improving the breeding of livestock, including poultry and bees, by the practice of artificial insemination, the Minister may— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d6f41f4740545ab59959279260a3689b]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d6f41f4740545ab59959279260a3689b] contribute towards any expenditure incurred, with his approval, by any society or person in the conduct of research tions and establish centres for the purposes of research or experiment in matters affecting that practice;” *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d6f41f4740545ab59959279260a3689b]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d6f41f4740545ab59959279260a3689b] establish and operate such centres providing services of artificial insemination for any such livestock as aforesaid as he thinks fit for the purposes of such research and experiment. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d6f41f4740545ab59959279260a3689b]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d6f41f4740545ab59959279260a3689b] Any expenses incurred by the Minister under this section, to such amount as may be sanctioned by the Treasury, shall be defrayed out of moneys provided by Parliament, and any fees or other sums received by the Minister in respect of the operations of any centre established under this section shall be paid into the Exchequer. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d6f41f4740545ab59959279260a3689b]
