# Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963, section 25

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 25. General powers and duties of Levy Board.[^c-cb934a3822f750ff1fab745dbd575ee0][^c948664]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] The Levy Board shall have power— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

- (a) with the approval of, and subject to any conditions imposed by, the Secretary of State, to engage in any activity connected with any of the matters specified in section 24(1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]*a*) to ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]*c*) of this Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (b) to acquire and hold such land as may be reasonably required for the purposes of any of their functions and to sell or lease any land held by them which is not required for those purposes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (c) to borrow for the purposes of any of their functions and to give security for any moneys borrowed by them; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (d) to lend or invest money for the purposes of or in connection with any activity in which they have power under paragraph ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]*a*) of this subsection to engage; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (e)[^c948665] to make such other investments as— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
  - (i) they judge desirable for the proper conduct of their affairs, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] a trustee would be able to make under the general power of investment in section 3 of the Trustee Act 2000 (as restricted by sections 4 and 5 of that Act); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (f) to do all such things as are incidental to, or conducive to the attainment of the purposes of, any of their functions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(2) The Levy Board shall apply any moneys from time to time available in their hands— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

- (a) in providing for the payment of rates, taxes, charges, expenses and other outgoings, including any sums which they are required or authorised to pay by virtue of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] any enactment[^c948666] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29];
- (b) in retaining such sums and making provision for such matters as they think proper in connection with any of their functions; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (c) in making such payments as they think fit for charitable purposes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (d) subject to the foregoing paragraphs of this subsection, in making payments, in accordance with schemes from time to time prepared by the Levy Board and approved with or without modifications by the Secretary of State, for such purposes as are mentioned in section 24(1) of this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
