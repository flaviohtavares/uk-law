# Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963, section 12

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 12. Horserace Totalisator Board.[^c-aa1750f9a0cfe710de28ae83b0bcfd94]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] There shall be a Horserace Totalisator Board (in this Act referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]**the Totalisator Board *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]**”) which shall be a body corporate and have perpetual succession and a common seal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(2) The Totalisator Board shall consist of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] a chairman and such number of other members as the Secretary of State may determine, all of whom[^c948624] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] shall be appointed by the Secretary of State and hold and vacate office in accordance with the terms of the respective instruments under which they are appointed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(3) The Totalisator Board may pay to any member of the Board such remuneration, and travelling, subsistence or other allowances at such rates, as the Board may with the approval of the Secretary of State determine: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

Provided that the Board shall not by virtue of this subsection have power to pay remuneration to any member of the Board who is for the time being a member of, or nominated as a candidate for election to, the House of Commons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(3A)[^c948625] The Board may pay such pension or gratuity to or in respect of any member of the Board as the Board may, with the approval of the Secretary of State, determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(4) The Totalisator Board may appoint officers, servants and agents on such terms as to remuneration, pensions or otherwise as the Board may determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(5) The Totalisator Board may regulate their own procedure and make standing orders governing the conduct of their business. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(6) No act or proceeding of the Totalisator Board shall be questioned on account of any vacancy in the number of the members thereof or on account of the appointment of any member having been defective. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(7) Except where the context otherwise requires, any reference in any Act or other document to the Racecourse Betting Control Board shall be construed as a reference to the Totalisator Board. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
