# Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963, section 27

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 27. The levy[^c-cb934a3822f750ff1fab745dbd575ee0][^c948667][^c-93a9efd1852c8d1fefefa0f7738a1e1c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] The contributions such as are mentioned in section 24(1) of this Act to be made by bookmakers *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] and betting exchange providers[^c-46199e25e8761cb4380a35b9cc4a9bd3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] shall be paid by way of a levy in respect of each levy period in accordance with *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] this section[^c-2db9001ff6fc60a4b075011bef0e884b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29].

(1A)[^c-4bd5362f63b1472afb1075e341efe9cb] The levy is charged at the rate of 10% of the amount by which a person’s profits for a levy period exceed the exempt amount (see section 27D). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(1B) The person’s profits for a levy period are the sum of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

- (a) the person’s profits as a bookmaker on leviable bets for the period (see section 27A), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (b) the commission charged by the person as a betting exchange provider on leviable bets during the period (see section 27C). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(1C) The persons by whom the levy is payable include those bookmakers and betting exchange providers who are required to hold remote operating licences. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(1D) In subsection (1C) “remote operating licence” has the same meaning as in the Gambling Act 2005 (see section 67 of that Act). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
