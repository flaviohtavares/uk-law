# Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963, section 29A

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 29A. Meaning of “leviable bet”[^c-9a2ea6148c863450d16fc189d9184b5b]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) In this Part “leviable bet” means a bet that—

- (a) relates to horse racing in Great Britain, and
- (b) is made (whether in person or remotely) by a person who is in Great Britain when the bet is made.

(2) The following are not “horse racing” for the purposes of subsection (1)(a)—

- (a) point to point racing;
- (b) harness racing;
- (c) trotting events.
