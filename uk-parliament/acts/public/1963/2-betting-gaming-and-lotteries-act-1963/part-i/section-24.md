# Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963, section 24

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 24. Horserace Betting Levy Board.[^key-cb934a3822f750ff1fab745dbd575ee0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] There shall be a Horserace Betting Levy Board (in this Act referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]**the Levy Board *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]**”) which shall be charged with the duty of assessing and collecting in accordance with the subsequent provisions of this Part of this Act, and of applying, subject to those provisions, for purposes conducive to any one or more of the following, that is to say— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

- (a) the improvement of breeds of horses; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (b) the advancement or encouragement of veterinary science or veterinary education; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (c) the improvement of horse racing, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

monetary contributions from bookmakers *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] and betting exchange providers[^key-6da24e2a0c0135da01dbf8adc5f52018] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29].

(2) The Levy Board shall consist of a chairman and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] six other members[^key-82a886654fbc91b7e1a4e3a110f796de] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] of whom— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

- (a) the chairman and two other members shall be appointed by the Secretary of State and be persons who the Secretary of State is satisfied have no interests connected with horse racing which might hinder them from discharging their functions as members of the Board in an impartial manner; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (b)[^c948657] three members shall be appointed by the Jockey Club (incorporating the National Hunt Committee) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (d)[^key-3c6441e43bd8f5a2ec89ebd538169561] one member appointed by the Association of British Bookmakers and the Remote Gambling Association, acting jointly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (e)[^key-f903bb4969802f3c60c2f09a55cdb87c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2A)[^key-58d8e760a90d77464b6d0ef8ab2083c9] If the Association of British Bookmakers and the Remote Gambling Association fail to appoint a member under subsection (2)(d) within a reasonable time, the appointment may be made by the chairman of the Levy Board. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(3) Any person appointed to be a member of the Levy Board under subsection (2)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]*a*) of this section shall hold and vacate office in accordance with the terms of the instrument under which he was appointed; and any person appointed to be a member of the Board under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] subsection (2)([^c948658] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]*b*) of this section may be removed from the Board at any time by the body by whom he was appointed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(3A)[^key-1ac78c5c9974d661ed3cb7de1b2337ba] Any person appointed to be a member of the Board under subsection (2)(d) may be removed from the Board at any time by the Association of British Bookmakers and the Remote Gambling Association, acting jointly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(4) The Jockey Club, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] (incorporating the National Hunt Committee) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] [^key-99d2e4819608074046bf04413872da3c]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] may from time to time appoint a person to act in the place of such a member of the Levy Board as is mentioned in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] subsection (2)(b)[^key-35867103c4e7cdfb0a48abbc5aebe24c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] [^key-5353bad52d7172b199fa4c4bacf7addc]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] [^key-5353bad52d7172b199fa4c4bacf7addc]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] of this section at any meeting of the Levy Board at which that member is unable to be present, and while so acting any such person shall be deemed for the purposes of any act or proceeding of the Levy Board to be a member of that Board. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(4A)[^key-6ac30e02e7ba46107ee47f0728e2d385] The Association of British Bookmakers and the Remote Gambling Association, acting jointly, may from time to time appoint a person to act in the place of such a person appointed to be a member of the Levy Board as is mentioned in subsection (2)(d) at any meeting of the Levy Board at which that member is unable to be present, and while so acting any such person shall be deemed for the purposes of any act or proceeding of the Levy Board to be a member of that Board. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(5) The Levy Board shall be a body corporate and shall have perpetual succession and a common seal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(6) The Levy Board may pay to the chairman and the two other members appointed by the Secretary of State such remuneration, and to any member of the Board travelling, subsistence or other allowances at such rates, as the Board may with the approval of the Secretary of State determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(6A)[^c948661] The Levy Board may pay such pension or gratuity as the Board may, with the approval of the Secretary of State, determine, to or in respect of the chairman and any other members appointed by the Secretary of State. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(7) The Levy Board may appoint officers, servants and agents on such terms as to remuneration, pensions or otherwise as the Board may determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(8) The Levy Board may regulate their own procedure and make standing orders governing the conduct of their business. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(9) No act or proceeding of the Levy Board shall be questioned on account of any vacancy in the number of the members thereof or on account of the appointment of any member having been defective. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
