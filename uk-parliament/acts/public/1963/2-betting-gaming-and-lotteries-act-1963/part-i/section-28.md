# Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963, section 28

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 28. Assessment of ... levy.[^c-cb934a3822f750ff1fab745dbd575ee0][^c-e65e37d4e4f8fe899cbc18d38298511c]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)–(4)[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c948669]

(4A)[^c-8fe5ddf69630b02cae3b6b9f97a1d618] As soon as practicable after the end of a levy period, the Levy Board must serve an assessment notice on every person they have assessed as liable to pay the levy under section 27 for that period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(4B) The assessment notice must state— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

- (a) the amount that the Levy Board have assessed that the person is liable to pay, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (b) the basis on which that amount has been calculated, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (c) when and how the amount must be paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(5) An assessment notice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] served on any person by the Levy Board[^c-e6b4e53e6515bf81ca6881a7885a1fcb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] for any levy period shall be conclusive as to his liability to the levy for that period and the amount payable by him by way thereof unless not later than twenty-eight days after the notice is served on him he gives to the Board notice in writing of appeal therefrom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(6) On receiving any notice of appeal under the last foregoing subsection, the Levy Board shall refer the appeal to an appeal tribunal established in pursuance of section 29 of this Act, which shall have power to confirm, increase or reduce the assessment, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] [^c-8dc05e989a960e80347736107cad19b7]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] but which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

- (a) shall not reduce the assessment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] [^c-ec526e1113424a1e6aca9be6ec8e0608]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] unless the appellant has afforded the tribunal all the facilities it may have required for the investigation of his case; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (b) shall confirm the assessment unless the tribunal is satisfied that, on all the evidence made available to it, the assessment should be varied or rescinded; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

and any decision of the tribunal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] [^c-2a3a23d01af80cf8727eb0a9a0b6b421]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] shall be final. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(7) Any amount assessed as payable by any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] person[^c-6ea48616b50089a1fc22b76d56848fa4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] by way of the levy in respect of any levy period shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] (except to the extent of any payments on account)[^c948670] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] become due twenty-eight days after notice of the assessment has been served on the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] or, if he appeals therefrom in pursuance of the foregoing provisions of this section, on the determination or abandonment of the appeal, and shall be recoverable by the Levy Board as a debt due to them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(8) Upon the discharge by a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] person[^c-c857b6cfc714428369df044c64c71748] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] of his liability by way of the levy in respect of any levy period *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] in accordance with the notice of assessment issued in his case[^c948671] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29], the Levy Board shall issue to him a certificate in writing to the effect that he has done so, and any such certificate shall be conclusive evidence of the facts stated therein. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(9) An assessment notice may be served on any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] person[^c-7748af1aea883db137e5af9d0100c577] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] either by serving it on him personally or by sending it to him by post at his usual or last-known residence or place of business in the United Kingdom or, if the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] is a company, at the company’s registered office. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(10)[^c-7aaff7d8a62577a656788c3ebca680d1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
