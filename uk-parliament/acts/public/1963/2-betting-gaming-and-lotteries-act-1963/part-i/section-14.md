# Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963, section 14

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 14. Functions of Totalisator Board with respect to betting on horse races.[^c948630][^c948631][^c948629][^c-aa1750f9a0cfe710de28ae83b0bcfd94]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] The Totalisator Board *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] shall have the exclusive right[^c948632] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] to do, and to authorise other persons to do, either of the following things, that is to say— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

- (a) to carry on pool betting business in any form on a recognised horse race; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (b) by way of business to receive or negotiate bets on a recognised horse race on terms that the winnings or any part thereof shall be calculated or regulated directly or indirectly by reference to the amounts or rates of any payments or distributions in respect of winning bets on that race made by way of sponsored pool betting; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

and in giving any authority under this subsection the Board may do so on such terms, including terms as to payments to the Board, as the Board may think fit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(2) Any infringement of the right conferred on the Totalisator Board by the foregoing subsection shall be actionable at the suit of the Board; and in any action for such an infringement all such relief, by way of damages, injunction, accounts or otherwise, shall be available to the Board as is available to the plaintiff in any corresponding proceedings in respect of infringements of proprietary rights and, notwithstanding anything to the contrary in any enactment or rule of law relating to the jurisdiction of county courts, a county court may, on the application of the Board, grant an injunction restraining an infringement or apprehended infringement of the right aforesaid whether or not any other relief in claimed; and for the purposes of this subsection the right aforesaid is infringed by any person who, without the authority of the Board— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

- (a) does or authorises any other person to do anything such as is mentioned in subsection (1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]*a*) or ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]*b*) of this section; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (b) by way of business holds himself out as willing to enter into any pool betting transaction on a recognised horse race, whether by way of sponsored pool betting or otherwise, or to receive or negotiate a bet on a recognised horse race on such terms as are mentioned in the said subsection (1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]*b*).

In the application of this subsection to Scotland, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]**suit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]**” means instance, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]**injunction *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]**” means an interdict, “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]**accounts *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]**” means count, reckoning and payment, and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]**plaintiff *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]**” means pursuer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(3) Where bets on a race or combination of races are made by way of sponsored pool betting, the Totalisator Board shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

- (a) cause to be deducted from the aggregate amount staked either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
  - (i) such percentage of that amount as may be determined from time to time by the Board, either generally or with respect to any particular racecourse; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] if so determined as aforesaid, such percentage of such part of that amount as may be determined as aforesaid; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (b) cause the whole of the remainder of that amount to be distributed among the persons making such of those bets as are winning bets. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(4) Where faciltites for sponsored pool betting are being provided on an approved horse racecourse by the persons having the management of that racecourse, any amount deducted by those persons under subsection (3)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]*a*) of this section shall be paid to the Totalisator Board, but the Board may pay to those persons the amount of the expenses shown to the satisfaction of the Board to have been properly incurred by those persons in connection with the provision of those facilities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(5) Without prejudice to section 12(4) of this Act, the Totalisator Board may remunerate any person, by the payment of commissions or otherwise, in respect of the negotiation, receipt or transmission by that person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

- (a) of bets to be made by way of sponsored pool betting; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (b) of bets such as are mentioned in subsection (1)( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]*b*) of this section to be made with the Board or, under the authority of the Board, with the persons having the management of an approved horse racecourse, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

and may provide facilities on any such racecourse for persons engaged in receiving bets to be so made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(6) Nothing in this Act shall be construed as restricting the betting transactions which may be effected by way of sponsored pool betting to betting transactions upon the result of a single race, or upon the results of races run on a particular racecourse or on a particular day, or as preventing the Totalisator Board from giving credit in any betting transaction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
