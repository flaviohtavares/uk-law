# Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963, section 29

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 29. Levy appeal tribunals.[^key-cb934a3822f750ff1fab745dbd575ee0][^c948682]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] There shall be established for the purposes of section 28 of this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] and section 2 of the Horserace Betting Levy Act 1981[^c948683] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]—

- (a) one or more appeal tribunals for England and Wales; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (b) one or more appeal tribunals for Scotland; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

and an appeal under section 28(5) of this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] or section 2(1) of the said Act of 1981[^c948684] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] shall be referred to a tribunal established for Scotland if the appellant is the holder of a bookmaker’s permit which was last granted or renewed by an authority in Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(2) Each such tribunal shall consist of a chairman and two other members of whom— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

- (a) the chairman, who shall be *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
  - (i)[^key-68e4738951ae817c4646b4804a11d0fe][^c948685] a person who satisfies the judicial-appointment eligibility condition on a 5-year basis; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] an advocate or solicitor in Scotland of at least *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] 5[^key-7d519f3f739762bd5bfbd5ed7937b9d6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] years’ standing, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

  shall be appointed by the Lord Chancellor or, in the case of a tribunal established for Scotland, by the Lord President of the Court of Session; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (b)[^c948686] the other members shall be appointed by the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

and each member of any such tribunal shall hold office in accordance with the terms of the instrument under which he was appointed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] , but subject, in the case of the chairman, to subsection (2A) of this section[^c948687] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29].

(2A)[^c948688] The chairman of any such tribunal shall vacate his office on the day on which he attains the age of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] 75[^key-30e3ed0ef9a26aadfb3d8b07990d6d9e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29].

(3) The procedure of any such tribunal shall be such as the Lord Chancellor or, in the case of a tribunal established for Scotland, the Lord President of the Court of Session may by rules prescribe; and any such rules shall be made by statutory instrument, which shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(4)[^c948686] The Levy Board shall pay to the members of any such tribunal such remuneration as the Board may with the approval of the Secretary of State determine and any travelling and other expenses reasonably incurred by them as members of the tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(5) The Levy Board shall provide any such tribunal with such secretarial and other facilities as may appear to the Board to be necessary or expedient, and, without prejudice to subsection (7) of this section, shall pay any expenses incurred by the tribunal for the purposes of their functions with the approval, whether general or special, of the Levy Board. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(6) If any such tribunal thinks it just so to direct in allowing any appeal by a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] person[^key-8407e63823c183d92b6f696cd5cfbe24] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29], the Levy Board shall pay to that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] such amount as the tribunal may specify towards expenses appearing to the tribunal to have been reasonably incurred by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] in connection with the appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(7) If any such tribunal, in dismissing any appeal by a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] person[^key-24b9a92924daf6f97aa65abca8ea1c61] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29], or on the abandonment of any appeal after the tribunal has taken some action towards its determination, thinks it just that the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] should make a payment towards expenses incurred by the tribunal in connection with the appeal, the tribunal may certify accordingly and the Levy Board shall be entitled to recover from the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] as a debt due to them the amount specified in the certificate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
