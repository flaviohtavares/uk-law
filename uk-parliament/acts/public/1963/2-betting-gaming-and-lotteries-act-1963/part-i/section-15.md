# Betting, Gaming and Lotteries Act 1963, section 15

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 15. Additional powers and duties of Totalisator Board.[^key-aa1750f9a0cfe710de28ae83b0bcfd94]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] The Totalisator Board shall have power— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

- (a) to acquire and hold such land as may be reasonably required for the purposes of any of their functions and to sell or lease any land held by them which is not required for those purposes; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (b) to borrow for the purposes of any of their functions and to give security for any moneys borrowed by them; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (c) to make such loans or investments as they judge desirable for the proper conduct of their affairs, being loans or investments either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
  - (i) such as, under the enactments for the time being in force, a trustee would by authorised to make out of trust funds; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29] approved, or of a description approved, by the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (d) to do all such things as are incidental to, or conducive to the attainment of the purposes of, any of their functions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

(2) Subject to section 14(3) to (5) of this Act, the Totalisator Board shall apply any moneys from time to time available in their hands— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]

- (a) in providing for the payment of rates, taxes, charges, expenses and other outgoings; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (b) in making provision for the payment of any contribution for the time being payable by them under section 30 of this Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
- (c) in making such other provision in connection with any of their functions as they think proper. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-3e354a2f0fdd029aa30601328d372f29]
