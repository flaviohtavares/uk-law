# Children and Young Persons Act 1963, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2015-04-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1963/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Jurisdiction of magistrates’ courts in certain cases involving children and young persons.

*Extent: E+W*

Notwithstanding section 46(1) of the principal Act (which restricts the jurisdiction of magistrates’ courts which are not youth courts[^c6538591] in cases where a child or young person is charged with an offence) a magistrates’ court which is not a youth court may hear an information against a child or young person if he is charged—

- (a) with aiding, abetting, causing, procuring, allowing or permitting an offence with which a person who has attained the age of eighteen[^c6538611] is charged at the same time; or
- (b) with an offence arising out of circumstances which are the same as or connected with those giving rise to an offence with which a person who has attained the age of eighteen is charged at the same time.
