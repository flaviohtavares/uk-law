# Children and Young Persons Act 1963, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2015-04-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1963/37
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Medical evidence by certificate.

*Extent: E+W*

In any proceedings, other than proceedings for an offence, before a youth court[^c6538861], and on any appeal from a decision of a youth court in any such proceedings, any document purporting to be a certificate of a fully registered medical practitioner who holds a licence to practise[^c-491e8a05e7aef90f1eefc1b2bb15c848] as to any person’s physical or mental condition shall be admissible as evidence of that condition.
