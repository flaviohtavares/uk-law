Finance Act 1899

1899 c.9

An Act to grant certain duties of Customs and Inland Revenue, to alter other duties, and to amend the Law relating to Customs and Inland Revenue, and to make other provision for the financial arrangements of the year.[^c5776511]

\[20th June 1899\].

### 5. Extension of stamp duty on share warrants and stock certificates to bearer.

(1) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c5776561]

(2) [^c22106261]. . . section one hundred and nine of the Stamp Act, 1891 [^c22106281] (which relates to the penalty for issuing stock certificates unstamped), shall apply to any instrument chargeable with stamp duty [^c22106271]. . . as a stock certificate to bearer in the same manner as it applies to the stock certificates to bearer named in that section, and as if “company or body of persons” were mentioned in sub-section one of that section as well as “ local authority.”
