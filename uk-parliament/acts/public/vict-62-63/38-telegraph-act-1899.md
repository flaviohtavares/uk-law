## 2. Payment of expenses of exercise of powers under telephone licence.

(1)[^c21000081] Where the council of a borough or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21000081] a district[^c5776141] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21000081] are licensed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21000081] to run a public telecommunication system (within the meaning of the Telecommunications Act 1984)[^c5776151] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21000081], they may . . . *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21000081] [^c5776161] borrow money for the purpose . . . *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21000081] [^c5776161] and the council may, subject to the provisions . . . *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21000081] [^c5776181]of the licence, exercise their powers under the licence throughout the area for which it is granted, although part of that area may be outside the borough or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21000081] district. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21000081]

(2) In the application of this Act to Scotland, a royal or parliamentary burgh shall be substituted for a borough, and a police burgh for *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21000081] a district *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c21000081] . . . [^c5776211]
