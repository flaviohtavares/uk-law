# Juries (Scotland) Act 1826

> Text as at 1991-02-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Juries (Scotland) Act 1826

1826 c.8

An Act to amend so much of an Act of the last Session of Parliament, for regulating the Qualification and the Manner of enrolling Jurors in Scotland, and of choosing Jurors in Criminal Trials there, and to unite counties for the purposes of Trial in Cases of High Treason in Scotland, as relates to the Qualification of Special Jurors.

\[22d March 1826\][^c554965][^c554966]

## 1—3. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c554967]

## 4. Names of Jurors dying or becoming disqualified may be passed over.[^c554968]

In case any person whose name shall have been entered . . . [^c554969] in the list of potential jurors[^c554970] . . . [^c554969] shall die, or cease to be qualified to serve as a juror[^c554972], it shall and may be lawful for the sheriff, in making returns of jurors, as directed by the said recited Act, to pass over the name of every such person, provided the date at which the name of such person shall have been so passed over, and the reason thereof, shall be entered at the time in the list of potential jurors . . . [^c554969]

## 5. This Act and recited Act 6 Geo. 4. c. 22. to be construed together.[^c554975]

This Act, and said recited Act passed in the last sessions of Parliament, shall be construed and receive effect together, in the same manner as if this Act had made part of the said recited Act.
