# Lunacy Regulation (Ireland) Act 1871, section 96

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 96. Powers to extend to colonies, &c.

The powers and authorities given by this Act to the Lord Chancellor intrusted as aforesaid shall extend to all land and stock within any of the dominions, plantations, and colonies of Her Majesty (except England, Wales, and Scotland).
