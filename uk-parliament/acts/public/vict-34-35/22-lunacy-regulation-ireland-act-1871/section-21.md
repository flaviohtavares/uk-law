# Lunacy Regulation (Ireland) Act 1871, section 21

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 21. Nothing to preclude the Lord Chancellor from issuing a special commission.

Nothing in this Act contained shall be taken to preclude the Lord Chancellor intrusted as aforesaid from, issuing a commission specially in any case of alleged lunacy if he shall upon any occasion deem it proper to do so, and the foregoing provisions shall be deemed to extend to every commission so issued specially, or so directed as aforesaid, so far as they may be applicable.
