# Lunacy Regulation (Ireland) Act 1871, section 102

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 102. Commission may be superseded on conditions.

If it shall appear to the Lord Chancellor that it is not expedient or for the benefit of the lunatic that the Commission, should be unconditionally superseded, but that same should be superseded on terms and conditions, he may, upon the consent of the lunatic and such other persons, if any, whose consent he may deem necessary, order the Commission to be superseded upon such terms and conditions as he shall think proper.
