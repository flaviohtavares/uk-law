# Parliamentary Costs Act 1871

> This law has been repealed or revoked.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Parliamentary Costs Act 1871(repealed)

1871 c.3

## 1. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c567746]

**No longer in force (ended 2007-04-01).**

## 2. Power to Select Committees on Bills confirming Provisional Orders to award costs.

**No longer in force (ended 2007-04-01).**

Any Select Committee of either House of Parliament to which any Bill for confirming or giving effect to Provisional Orders has been referred, in relation to any Provisional Order therein contained, may award costs, in like manner and under the same conditions under which costs may be awarded by any Select Committee under the [^c567747]Parliamentary Costs Act 1865; and the provisions of the said Act, so far as they are applicable, shall have effect accordingly.

## 3. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c567748]

**No longer in force (ended 2007-04-01).**

## 4. Interpretation of “Provisional Order”.

**No longer in force (ended 2007-04-01).**

For the purpose of this Act, the words “**Provisional Order**” shall include . . . [^c567749] , schemes, and orders in the nature of Provisional Orders, made under the authority of any statute, and requiring to be confirmed, sanctioned, or carried into effect by Act of Parliament.
