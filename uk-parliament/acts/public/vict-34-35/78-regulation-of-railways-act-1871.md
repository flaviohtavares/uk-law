## Preliminary (affected by repeal, see note)[^c5719301]

### 1. Construction of Act and short title. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c5719301]

This Act so far as is consistent with the tenor thereof shall be construed as one with the Acts mentioned in Schedule Two to this Act and with the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301] [^c5719391]Regulation of Railways Act 1868 and those Acts and this Act may be cited together as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]**The Regulation of Railways Acts 1840 to 1871 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]**”; and this Act and each of the Acts mentioned in Schedule Two to this Act may be cited as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]**The Regulation of Railways Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]**” of the year in which it was passed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]

## Inspection of Railways (affected by repeal, see note)[^c5719301]

### 3. Board of Trade may appoint inspectors of railways.[^c5719751][^c5719741]

The Board of Trade may from time to time appoint any person to be inspector for the purpose of inspecting any railway or station and of making any inquiry with respect to any railway or station or into the cause of any railway accident or any accident at any station[^c5719761] which the Board of Trade are authorized to make or direct, and of enabling the Board of Trade to carry the provisions of any general Act relating to railways into execution, or for any of such purposes: Provided that no person so appointed shall exercise any powers of interference in the affairs of any company[^c5719771].

### 4. Powers of inspectors of railways. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c5719811][^c5719301][^c5719801]

Every inspector under this Act shall for the purposes of any inspection or inquiry which he is directed by the Board of Trade to make or conduct have the following powers; (that is to say,) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]

(1)[^c5719821] He may enter and inspect any railway or station and any works, buildings or offices belonging thereto and may inspect any stock, plant or machinery used in connection with any railway or station; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]

(2) He may by summons under his hand require the attendance of any person who is engaged in the management, service, or employment of a company as defined by this Act, and whom he thinks fit to call before him and examine for the said purpose, and may require answers or returns to such inquiries for the said purpose as he thinks fit to make from such person or company: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]

(3) He may require and enforce the production of all books, papers, and documents of a company which he considers important for the said purpose. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]

### 5. Extension of 5 & 6 Vict. c. 55. ss. 4 to 6 to new works. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c5719831][^c5719301]

The provisions of the Regulation of Railways Act, 1842, and the Acts amending the same, with respect to the opening of any railway, shall extend to the opening of any additional line of railway, deviation line, station, junction, or crossing on the level which forms a portion of or is directly connected with a railway on which passengers are conveyed, and has been constructed subsequently to the inspection of such railway on behalf of the Board of Trade previous to the original opening of such railway: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719831] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]

Provided always, that the Board of Trade may, with respect to any of the works in this section mentioned, from time to time upon the application of any railway company dispense with any notice which, under the provisions of the said Acts, is required to be given to the Board of Trade previous to opening any railway. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719831] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]

## Accidents (affected by repeal, see note)[^c5719301]

### 6. Companies to make returns of accidents to Board of Trade. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c5719921][^c5719931][^c5719971][^c5719301]

Where in or about any railway or station or any of the works or buildings connected with such railway or station, or any building or place, whether open or enclosed, occupied by the company working such railway or operating such station, any of the following accidents takes place in the course of working any railway or operating any station[^c5719941] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719971] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]; (that is to say,) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719971] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]

(1) Any accident attended with loss of life or personal injury to any person whomsoever; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719971] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]

(2) Any collision where one of the trains is a passenger train; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719971] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]

(3) Any passenger train or any part of a passenger train accidentally leaving the rails; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719971] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]

(4) Any accident of a kind not comprised in the foregoing descriptions, but which is of such a kind as to have caused or to be likely to cause loss of life or personal injury, and which may be specified in that behalf by any order to be made from time to time by the Board of Trade, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719971] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]

the company working such railway or operating such station, and also, if the accident happens to a train operated by any other company[^c5719951] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719971] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301], such last-mentioned company shall send notice of such accident and of the loss of life or personal injury (if any) occasioned thereby to the Board of Trade. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719971] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]

Such notice shall be in such form and shall contain such particulars as the Board of Trade may from time to time direct, and shall be sent by the earliest practicable post after the accident takes place. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719971] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]

The Board of Trade may from time to time by order direct that notice of any class of accidents shall be sent to them by telegraph, and may revoke any such order. Notice of every such order shall be sent to every railway company, and while it is in force notice of every accident of the class to which the order relates shall be sent to the Board of Trade by telegraph immediately after the accident takes place. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719971] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]

Every company who fail to comply with the provisions of this section shall be liable for each offence to a penalty not exceeding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719971] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301] level 2 on the standard scale[^c5719961] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719971] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301].

### 11. Disobedience to or obstruction of inspector or court. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c5719301]

If any person, without reasonable excuse (proof whereof shall lie on him), does any of the following things; namely, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]

### 12. Limitation of liability of companies on sea voyages in certain cases. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c5720281][^c5719301]

Where a railway company under a contract for carrying persons, animals, or goods by sea procure the same to be carried in a vessel not belonging to the railway company, the railway company shall be answerable in damages in respect of loss of life or personal injury, or in respect of loss of or damage to animals or goods, in like manner and to the same amount as the railway company would be answerable if the vessel had belonged to the railway company; provided that such loss of life or personal injury, or loss or damage to animals or goods, happens to the person, animals, or goods (as the case may be) during the carriage of the same in such vessel, the proof to the contrary to lie upon the railway company. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5720281] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c5719301]
