# Architects (Registration) Act 1931, section 17

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Defence for business under control and management of registered person[^c607769][^c607768]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-07-21).**

(1)[^c607770]Nothing in this Act shall prevent a body corporate, firm or partnership from carrying on business under the style or title of Architect[^c607771]:

- (a) if the business of the body corporate, firm or partnership so far as it relates to architecture is under the control and management of a registered person[^c607772] who does not act at the same time in a similar capacity for any other body corporate firm or partnership; and
- (b) if in every premises where such business as aforesaid is carried on it is carried on by or under the supervision of[^c607773] a registered person.

(2)[^c607774] In relation to an offence under subsection (1)—

- (a) section 127(1) of the [^c607775]Magistrates’ Courts Act 1980 (information to be laid within six months of offence);
- (b) Article 19(1) of the [^c607776]Magistrates’ Courts (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 (complaint to be made within that time); and
- (c) section 136(1) of the [^c607777]Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995 (proceedings to be commenced within that time),

shall have effect as if for the references in them to six months there were substituted references to two years.
