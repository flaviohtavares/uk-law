# Architects (Registration) Act 1931, section 12

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Penalty for obtaining registration by false representation.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-07-21).**

If any person shall intentionally[^c607753] procure or attempt to procure himself to be registered under this Act by making or producing or causing to be made or produced any false or fraudulent representation or declaration, either verbally or in writing, the person so offending, shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale[^c607754].
