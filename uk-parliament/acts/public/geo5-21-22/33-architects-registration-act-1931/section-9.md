# Architects (Registration) Act 1931, section 9

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Appeals.[^c607744][^c607743]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-07-21).**

Any person aggrieved by—[^c607745]

- (a) his name not being re-entered in, or being removed from, the Register by virtue of section 6C(1);
- (b) the making of a disciplinary order in relation to him; or
- (c) the Board ordering the Registrar to remove his name from the Register under section 7A,

may appeal to the High Court or the Court of Session within three months from the date on which notice of the decision or order concerned is served on him; and on an appeal under this section the Court may make any order which appears appropriate, and no appeal shall lie from any decision of the Court on such an appeal.
