# Architects (Registration) Act 1931, section 15

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Supply of rules and forms.[^c607759]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-07-21).**

The Registrar[^c607760] shall on payment of the prescribed charges supply a copy of any rules made under this Act and of any forms prescribed by such rules to any person applying for the same.
