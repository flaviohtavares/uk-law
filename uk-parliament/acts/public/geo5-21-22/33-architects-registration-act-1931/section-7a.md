# Architects (Registration) Act 1931, section 7A

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7A. Removal of name from Register: disqualification in another member State.[^c607734]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1997-07-21).**

(1)[^c607735] If a person’s name was entered on the Register in pursuance of section 6A [^c607736]. . . at a time when there was a disqualifying decision in another member State in force in respect of that person, and if at that time the Board was[^c607737] unaware of that fact, the Board, on[^c607738] being satisfied that the person was at that time and still is subject to that disqualifying decision, may order the Registrar to remove his name[^c607739] from the Register.

(2) If a person who is registered under this Act in pursuance of section 6A becomes subject to a disqualifying decision in another member State expressed to be made on the ground that he has committed a criminal offence he shall be deemed for the purposes of section 7ZA(1)[^c607740], to have been convicted of that offence.

(3)[^c607741] Where the Board orders the Registrar to remove a person’s name from the Register under this section, the Registrar shall serve written notice of the removal on the person as soon as is reasonably practicable.
