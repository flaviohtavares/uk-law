# Employment Act 2002, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2025-01-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Supply of information held by the Secretary of State

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to information which is held for the purposes of functions relating to statutory paternity pay,[^c-e94330a3a92b8180ad4617a6d21b5b9b] statutory adoption pay , statutory shared parental pay[^c-39433f4141f53190fff552b19f1196b5] , statutory parental bereavement pay or statutory neonatal care pay[^c-9de3117db51d4ad24fff2a9a46e0d82f]—

- (a) by the Secretary of State or the Department, or
- (b) by a person providing services to the Secretary of State or the Department, in connection with the provision of those services.

(2) Information to which this section applies may be supplied—

- (a) to the Board, or
- (b) to a person providing services to the Board,

for use for the purposes of functions relating to statutory paternity pay,[^c-0fe81f34f782663e708f115016afd8e6] statutory adoption pay , statutory shared parental pay[^c-b3e0d4bed6914eaf0ba7c02823b1905f] , statutory parental bereavement pay or statutory neonatal care pay[^c-38bc3244dc32bd9d0fd374f0d3aa32c8].
