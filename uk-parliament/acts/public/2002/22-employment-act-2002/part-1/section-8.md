# Employment Act 2002, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2025-01-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Regulations about payment

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may make regulations with respect to the payment by employers of statutory paternity pay,[^c-0ddfa7fc6a6c63d1a35a2066732c34eb] statutory adoption pay , statutory shared parental pay[^c-389ba0936810f1241a798720f57b21ee] , statutory parental bereavement pay and statutory neonatal care pay[^c-6f1f60c8d267086b85f87ce7de9ad734].

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) may, in particular, include provision—

- (a) about the records to be kept by employers in relation to payments of statutory paternity pay and statutory paternity pay,[^c-33d76ba865706881fc9f2bdb6cb3f0dd] , statutory shared parental pay[^c-8750e14bf2a3fa723b0d00d41664dbc3] , statutory parental bereavement pay and statutory neonatal care pay[^c-4d1f1a78be78eef9a318c88be4d650e6], including the length of time for which they are to be retained;
- (b) for the production of wages sheets and other documents and records to officers of the Board for the purpose of enabling them to satisfy themselves that statutory paternity pay,[^c-9fd64f6634b4095892afe9950e13a6b2] statutory adoption pay , statutory shared parental pay[^c-acefd263cb2d1599a7701643addc8032] , statutory parental bereavement pay and statutory neonatal care pay[^c-f10cb63038405be03858b56093815063] have been paid and are being paid, in accordance with the regulations, to employees who are entitled to them;
- (c) for requiring employers to provide information to employees (in their itemised pay statements or otherwise);
- (d) for requiring employers to make returns to the Board containing such particulars with respect to payments of statutory paternity pay,[^c-58c7a48863357a300b7944dd890f9d35] statutory adoption pay , statutory shared parental pay[^c-5ee86c8694efcc1188fd23b979c9f2fa] , statutory parental bereavement pay and statutory neonatal care pay[^c-a3ba83811af3fd9c49d6dd705e93977e] as the regulations may provide.

(3) Regulations under subsection (1) must be made with the concurrence of the Board.
