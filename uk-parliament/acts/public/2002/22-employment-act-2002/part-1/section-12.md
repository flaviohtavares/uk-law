# Employment Act 2002, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2025-01-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Penalties: fraud etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a person fraudulently or negligently—

- (a) makes any incorrect statement or declaration in connection with establishing entitlement to  [^key-a26e4c40213689367da8b44d3199a6ab][^key-b39e978d033a1d656e64829e82b137a7]... statutory paternity pay or statutory parental bereavement pay[^key-7a94f9b9df112a3462a2cbe989de4711], or
- (b) provides any incorrect information or document of a kind mentioned in regulations under section 10(1) so far as relating to  [^key-266e9c41c9c0bba3b8dff34be00d9506]... statutory paternity pay, or statutory parental bereavement pay[^key-77ed77030147abd2aa23a6a86a58ee9d]

he shall be liable to a penalty not exceeding £300.

(2) Where a person fraudulently or negligently—

- (a) makes any incorrect statement or declaration in connection with establishing entitlement to statutory adoption pay , statutory shared parental pay or statutory neonatal care pay[^key-859728620db05e2146e7faf6fda8fdba], or
- (b) provides any incorrect information or document of a kind mentioned in regulations under section 10(1) so far as relating to statutory adoption pay , statutory shared parental pay or statutory neonatal care pay[^key-0fc2e118c1b5706d344c8cdb729c6910],

he shall be liable to a penalty not exceeding £3,000.

(3) Where an employer fraudulently or negligently makes incorrect payments of  [^key-78e4982efd11c57b0cc3df22ca3aa136][^key-09dfb1b02135421785e8d4ceed82de69]... statutory paternity pay or statutory parental bereavement pay[^key-9d1b8df4c26bac3094429237522538d9], he shall be liable to a penalty not exceeding £300.

(4) Where an employer fraudulently or negligently makes incorrect payments of statutory adoption pay , statutory shared parental pay or statutory neonatal care pay[^key-50dea37f97655f64d02238d101684218], he shall be liable to a penalty not exceeding £3,000.

(5) Where an employer fraudulently or negligently—

- (a) produces any incorrect document or record, provides any incorrect information or makes any incorrect return, of a kind mentioned in regulations under section 8È, or
- (b) receives incorrect payments in pursuance of regulations under section 7,

he shall be liable to a penalty not exceeding £3,000 or, if the offence relates only to  [^key-6802e5b92665ac690c7a520f0e0ac38b][^key-17ac84c9fbcf0835b356842a58028f1a]... statutory paternity pay or statutory parental bereavement pay[^key-e520e52dc90bf76e420c02878c353a1a], £300.

(6) Schedule 1 (penalties: procedure and appeals) has effect in relation to penalties under this section.
