# Employment Act 2002, section 13

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2025-01-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/22

## 13. Supply of information held by the Board

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies to information which is held for the purposes of functions relating to statutory paternity pay,[^key-72e4587afd59ef5d9f454d5952b2d47c] statutory adoption pay , statutory shared parental pay[^key-37a76bb0a17a4812d547639559c3e0fd] , statutory parental bereavement pay or statutory neonatal care pay[^key-509150ca2fc8d8aff4b63422995268ef]—

- (a) by the Board, or
- (b) by a person providing services to the Board, in connection with the provision of those services.

(2) Information to which this section applies may be supplied—

- (a) to the Secretary of State, [^key-41b52ec1bde57aa08b63d44c0a251d20]... or the Department, or
- (b) to a person providing services to the Secretary of State, [^key-41b52ec1bde57aa08b63d44c0a251d20]... or the Department,

for use for the purposes of functions relating to social security, child support or war pensions [^key-a785d448896d4271cfb70d8cc18420b4]....
