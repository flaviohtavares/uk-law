# Employment Act 2002, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2025-01-17. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Removal of exemption for small employers[^key-1f89d521b732957af130237b4166259a]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In section 3 of the Employment Rights Act 1996 (c. 18) (note about disciplinary rules and procedures), subsections (3) and (4) (exemptions for undertakings with less than 20 employees) shall cease to have effect.
