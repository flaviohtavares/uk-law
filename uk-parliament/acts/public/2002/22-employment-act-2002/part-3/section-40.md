## 40. Interpretation of Part 3[^c-b91660e709e6a2a91d04ca96a8e796f0]

*Extent: E+W+S*

In this Part—

- “**employer**” and “**employee**” have the same meanings as in the Employment Rights Act 1996 (c. 18);
- “worker” has the same meaning as in the Employment Rights Act 1996.[^c-4fde86da7319defb8b60dae5eafe39bd]
