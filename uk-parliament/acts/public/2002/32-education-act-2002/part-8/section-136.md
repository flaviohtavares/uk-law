# Education Act 2002, section 136

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (67).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 136. Provision of education[^key-fe25528f5a4c7f4f3bcda7e868193fc6][^key-8e32e6ebae6f363b3dae1a5882c43eaf]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations may—

- (a) prohibit the provision of education at a further education institution *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bac6b55810bd1d1f7afa13a9492620e6] in Wales[^key-bf345f9c6fae22fdd1f3cd592f050b5f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bac6b55810bd1d1f7afa13a9492620e6] by a person who does not have a specified qualification; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-bac6b55810bd1d1f7afa13a9492620e6]
- (b) prohibit the provision of education at a further education institution *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8d468e429a758210b90f8754a910e3e7] in Wales[^key-2422d1b61aa8b2c9814f491dcf9d06b0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8d468e429a758210b90f8754a910e3e7] by a person unless he is serving or has served a probationary period; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8d468e429a758210b90f8754a910e3e7]
- (c) specify conditions to be complied with by or in respect of persons providing education at a further education institution.
