# Education Act 2002, section 146

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 146. Repeal of sections 218 and 218A of Education Reform Act 1988[^c415697][^key-41934672dbf167b071c65f79b0bc2a5f][^key-850c396406648e52c3700a6d8d6cd0b3][^key-794fb91df0e55c847589c46a7d30a5a4][^key-6ff192890812ac2740e523138b3f0ec5][^key-29f9ec83e32460ce0fab55e1b74103d9][^key-7c02c155e97069252dd9d6b6287395ca][^key-cb7dd597282ebf76da003ad5bf412fad]

*Extent: E+W*

Sections 218 and 218A of the Education Reform Act 1988 (c. 40) (school and further and higher education regulations) shall cease to have effect.
