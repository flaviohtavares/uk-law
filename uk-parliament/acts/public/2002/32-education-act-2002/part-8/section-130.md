# Education Act 2002, section 130

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (67).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/32

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 130. Repeal of School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions Act 1991[^c382438][^key-2e9b1a2a97b961542ad2a6c5985f0079]

*Extent: E+W*

The School Teachers’ Pay and Conditions Act 1991 (c. 49) shall cease to have effect.
