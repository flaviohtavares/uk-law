# Education Act 2002, section 142

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (67).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 142. Prohibition from teaching, etc.[^c415694][^key-3e84fa2790b6c3a2c7abcd82297f6856][^key-0e72e449adc3820354a93936fbde81a5][^key-54ebd3c5c2b0f1db4ee1608b8e5800b2][^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State, in relation to England, or the Secretary of State and the National Assembly for Wales concurrently, in relation to Wales, may direct that a person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

- (a) may not carry out work to which this section applies; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (b) may carry out work to which this section applies only in circumstances specified in the direction; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (c) may carry out work to which this section applies only if conditions specified in the direction are satisfied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

(2) This section applies to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

- (a) providing education at a school, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (b) providing education at a further education institution, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (c) providing education under a contract of employment or for services where the other party to the contract is a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62] local authority[^key-379c0a8ab791aad4cda0b42dbea5f020] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62] or a person exercising a function relating to the provision of education on behalf of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (d) taking part in the management of an independent school. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

(3) This section also applies to work of a kind which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

- (a) brings a person regularly into contact with children, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (b) is carried out at the request of or with the consent of a relevant employer (whether or not under a contract). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

(4) A direction under this section may be given in respect of a person only— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

- (a) on the grounds that the person is included (otherwise than provisionally) in the list kept under section 1 of the Protection of Children Act 1999 (c. 14) (list of individuals considered unsuitable to work with children), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (b) on the grounds that the person is unsuitable to work with children, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (c) on grounds relating to the person’s misconduct, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (d) on grounds relating to the person’s health, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (e) in the case of a direction given by virtue of subsection (2)(d), on grounds relating to the person’s professional incompetence (or on a ground mentioned in any of paragraphs (a) to (d)). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

(5) The Secretary of State, in relation to England, or the Secretary of State and the National Assembly for Wales concurrently, in relation to Wales, may by regulations prescribe the procedure for giving a direction under this section (including provision about notification of persons who are subject to directions). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

(6) The Secretary of State, in relation to England, or the Secretary of State and the National Assembly for Wales concurrently, in relation to Wales, may vary or revoke a direction under this section except in a case where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

- (a) the direction was given on the grounds that a person is unsuitable to work with children, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (b) the person claims that he is no longer unsuitable to work with children. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

(7) The Secretary of State, in relation to England, or the Secretary of State and the National Assembly for Wales concurrently, in relation to Wales, may by regulations prescribe the grounds on which a person subject to a direction under this section may seek to have it varied or revoked under subsection (6). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

(8) Where a person is subject to a direction under this section, a relevant employer shall not use the person to carry out work in contravention of the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

(9) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

- “**child *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]**” means a person who has not attained the age of 18 years, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- “**education *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]**” includes vocational, social, physical and recreational training, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- “**further education institution *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]**” has the meaning given by section 140, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- “**relevant employer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
  - (a) local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62] exercising an education function[^key-d4f88404c4456042f134583744582952] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62],
  - (b) a person exercising a function relating to the provision of education on behalf of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62],
  - (c) the proprietor of a school, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
  - (d) the governing body of a further education institution, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- “**school *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]**” includes an independent school. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
