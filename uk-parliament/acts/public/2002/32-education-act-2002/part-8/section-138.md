## 138. Training in provision of further education[^key-88870217b251bd87d21356e9b947a81c][^key-e9663033aa1b403371c0117eb616b8ba]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-876e0f36720b1054cdf91deae11fd856] This section applies to a course which is designed to lead to the award of a qualification specified under section 136 or 137(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-876e0f36720b1054cdf91deae11fd856]

(2)[^key-7e1b7ada49499a90488dc1123787b7e1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) The National Assembly for Wales may by regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-876e0f36720b1054cdf91deae11fd856]

- (a) prohibit the provision by a further or higher education institution in Wales of a course to which this section applies without the approval of the National Assembly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-876e0f36720b1054cdf91deae11fd856]
- (b) enable the National Assembly to determine the number of persons who may undertake a specified course to which this section applies at a further or higher education institution in Wales; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-876e0f36720b1054cdf91deae11fd856]
- (c) enable the National Assembly to determine the number of persons in different categories who may undertake a specified course to which this section applies at a further or higher education institution in Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-876e0f36720b1054cdf91deae11fd856]
