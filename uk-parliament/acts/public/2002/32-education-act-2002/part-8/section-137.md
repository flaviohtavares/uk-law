## 137. Principals of further education institutions[^c-a84ae8e65e16d04c7e02867f67f97016][^c-9ea3a12f73db6e1543dfd5513f9ce99f]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-f90ce25bb6f8074e0daccd8477cff027] Regulations may provide that a person may serve as the principal of a further education institution *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f90ce25bb6f8074e0daccd8477cff027] in Wales[^c-0dc1ff7b1b102e22a0261cf77fe94bfe] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f90ce25bb6f8074e0daccd8477cff027] only if he has a specified qualification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f90ce25bb6f8074e0daccd8477cff027]

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) shall not prevent a person from serving as the principal of an institution while he is following a course or programme which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f90ce25bb6f8074e0daccd8477cff027]

- (a) is of a kind specified in the regulations, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f90ce25bb6f8074e0daccd8477cff027]
- (b) is designed to lead to the award of a qualification specified under subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f90ce25bb6f8074e0daccd8477cff027]

(2A)[^c-d68b8b723336816f307deb0c171cb5f7] Regulations under subsection (1) may limit the period of time during which a person may serve as the principal of an institution in reliance on subsection (2). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f90ce25bb6f8074e0daccd8477cff027]

(3) A provision of regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f90ce25bb6f8074e0daccd8477cff027] made under subsection (1) by the Welsh Ministers in relation to Wales[^c-2bef779456cc87537b85b0946c3c54a3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f90ce25bb6f8074e0daccd8477cff027] shall not apply to a person who has been appointed as the principal of an institution *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f90ce25bb6f8074e0daccd8477cff027] in England or Wales ”[^c-b1a5faf30f5812838b3d0dbd908981d8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f90ce25bb6f8074e0daccd8477cff027] before the commencement of the provision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f90ce25bb6f8074e0daccd8477cff027]

(4) Regulations under subsection (1) shall not prevent a person from carrying out the functions of the principal of an institution— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f90ce25bb6f8074e0daccd8477cff027]

- (a) pending the appointment of a principal, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f90ce25bb6f8074e0daccd8477cff027]
- (b) in the absence of the principal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f90ce25bb6f8074e0daccd8477cff027]
