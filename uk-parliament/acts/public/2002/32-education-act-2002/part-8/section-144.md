# Education Act 2002, section 144

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (67).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/32

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 144. Directions under section 142: appeal[^c415696][^c-5a4a84d527d3b1ab972d7c33667fc9cf][^c-1d0d9ecfeba96ed3be54d1a35a1f64eb][^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person in respect of whom a direction has been given under section 142 may appeal to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62] First-tier Tribunal[^c-2f0acd9da3b53b9b0e62de0b0f420b7b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]—

- (a) against the decision to give the direction; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (b) against a decision not to vary or revoke the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

(2) In a case to which subsection (3) applies, the Tribunal may, on an application for a review of a direction under section 142, revoke the direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

(3) This subsection applies to a case where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

- (a) the direction was given on the grounds that the applicant is unsuitable to work with children, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (b) the applicant has obtained the leave of the Tribunal to apply for a review of the direction, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (c) the Tribunal is satisfied that the applicant is no longer unsuitable to work with children. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

(4) The Secretary of State, in relation to England, or the Secretary of State and the National Assembly for Wales concurrently, in relation to Wales, may by regulations— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]

- (a) provide that the Tribunal may not entertain an appeal under this section in so far as the appellant’s case is inconsistent with his having been convicted of an offence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (b) prescribe circumstances in which the Tribunal shall allow an appeal under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (c) prescribe the powers available to the Tribunal on allowing an appeal under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (d) prescribe circumstances in which the Tribunal shall grant an application for leave under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (e) prescribe circumstances in which the Tribunal shall grant an application for a review under this section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
- (f) prescribe the powers available to the Tribunal on revoking a direction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-03763d466a2f2055bc62702237a26f62]
