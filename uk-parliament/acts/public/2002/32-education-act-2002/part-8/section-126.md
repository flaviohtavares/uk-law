# Education Act 2002, section 126

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (67).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 126. Consultation by Secretary of State[^key-055a743c82b0c923050460bd59fecc56][^key-fe6e9afc83362434a95d7373c7bd3ab5]

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State may not make an order under section 122 , 122A[^key-952bbdc1198aa91ba8998838fe750615] or 125(4)(a) unless he has consulted such of the following as appear to him to be appropriate to consult having regard to the content of the order—

- (a) associations of local authorities[^key-0b83dc4852763b67d3e91bf8700f3c44],
- (b) local authorities,
- (ba)[^key-1e507f59521ef5b61a4e55a8c2d387ab] bodies representing the interests of proprietors of Academies,
- (c) bodies representing the interests of governing bodies of schools, and
- (d) bodies representing the interests of teachers.
