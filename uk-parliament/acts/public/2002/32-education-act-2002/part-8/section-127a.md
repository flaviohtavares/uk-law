# Education Act 2002, section 127A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (67).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 127A. References to “Academy” and “Academy arrangements”[^c-5ab61d9add76cf33e7f57d56c372ea87]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In sections 121 to 127, a reference to an Academy—

- (a) includes a reference to a city technology college and a city college for the technology of the arts, and
- (b) does not include a reference to a 16 to 19 Academy.

(2) A reference in any of those sections to Academy arrangements includes a reference to an agreement under [section 482](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/section/482) of the [Education Act 1996](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/56/contents) (city colleges).
