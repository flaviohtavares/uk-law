# Education Act 2002, section 189

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (67).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/32

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 189. Amendments of Part 5 of Education Act 1997[^c382451]

*Extent: E+W*

Schedule 17 (which contains amendments of Part 5 of the Education Act 1997 (c. 44) relating to the Qualifications and Curriculum Authority and the Qualifications, Curriculum and Assessment Authority for Wales) shall have effect.
