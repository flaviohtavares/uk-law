# Education Act 2002, section 49

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (67).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/32
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 49. Repeal of power to make certain special arrangements for preserving religious character[^c385174]

*Extent: E+W*

Section 91 of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 (c. 31) (special arrangements to preserve religious character of foundation or voluntary aided school) shall cease to have effect.
