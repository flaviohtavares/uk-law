## 35. Staffing of community, voluntary controlled, community special and maintained nursery schools[^key-01ea481baf6a711ec90801be24b89a44]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-41deec34d2d5458024539d64d4b61ab2] This section applies to—

- (a) community schools,
- (b) voluntary controlled schools,
- (c) community special schools, and
- (d) maintained nursery schools.

(2)[^key-41deec34d2d5458024539d64d4b61ab2] Any teacher or other member of staff who is appointed to work under a contract of employment at a school to which this section applies is to be employed by the local authority[^key-379c0a8ab791aad4cda0b42dbea5f020].

(3)[^key-41deec34d2d5458024539d64d4b61ab2] The teaching staff of any school to which this section applies shall include—

- (a) a person appointed as head teacher, or
- (b) a person appointed to carry out the functions of the head teacher of the school—
  - (i) pending the appointment of a head teacher, or
  - (ii) in the absence of the head teacher.

(4)[^key-be2fa4d2ad60dd33d8f3826783ada994] Regulations may make further provision with respect to the staffing of schools to which this section applies.

(5)[^key-be2fa4d2ad60dd33d8f3826783ada994] Regulations under subsection (4) may, in particular—

- (a) make provision with respect to the appointment, discipline, suspension and dismissal of teachers and other staff,
- (b) make provision with respect to the appointment of teachers and other staff to work at a school otherwise than under a contract of employment,
- (c) make provision with respect to staff employed, or engaged otherwise than under a contract of employment, wholly or partly for the purposes of—
  - (i) the provision of facilities and services under section 27, or
  - (ii) any other activities which are not school activities but are carried on on the school premises under the management or control of the governing body, and
- (d) confer functions on local authorities[^key-0b83dc4852763b67d3e91bf8700f3c44], governing bodies and head teachers.

(6)[^key-41deec34d2d5458024539d64d4b61ab2] In relation to teachers at a voluntary controlled school who are reserved teachers within the meaning of section 58 of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 (c. 31) (appointment and dismissal of certain teachers at schools with a religious character), regulations under subsection (4) shall have effect subject to the provisions of that section.

(7)[^key-41deec34d2d5458024539d64d4b61ab2][^key-0597eada5f8f1e04b45426f0797251ea] If at any time a school to which this section applies does not have a delegated budget by virtue of any suspension under[^key-da74a7c7dc729a87bd0de21a5c9d9123]... Schedule 15 to, the School Standards and Framework Act 1998, or section 66 of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^key-61c7b32761cd0704eff7d6417eee4141], or section 8 of the School Standards and Organisation (Wales) Act 2013[^key-b0d8714a2ab72cf1d6055559872098e4], —

- (a) regulations under subsection (4) shall not apply, and
- (b) the provisions of Part 1 of Schedule 2 shall apply instead.

(8)[^key-41deec34d2d5458024539d64d4b61ab2] In discharging any function conferred by regulations under subsection (4), a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e4e148ae240c6a4df8690941e026160] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e4e148ae240c6a4df8690941e026160] in Wales[^key-c0abe0d4d49bd18eddb6a42cf1ca90e4] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e4e148ae240c6a4df8690941e026160] or the governing body or head teacher of a maintained school *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e4e148ae240c6a4df8690941e026160] in Wales[^key-0d82c3664c0fe0cd9c21c953abb81349] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e4e148ae240c6a4df8690941e026160] shall have regard to any guidance given from time to time— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e4e148ae240c6a4df8690941e026160]

- (a)[^key-20be2587c531cb06fd4decd7ec3ccd69] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) [^key-d08fb90adf306bf70e102e69e44a14a8]...by the National Assembly for Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-5e4e148ae240c6a4df8690941e026160]
