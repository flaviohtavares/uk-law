## 32. Responsibility for fixing dates of terms and holidays and times of sessions: England[^c-8346aad34dfab170a4f03ac106a26a67][^c-308d6712d14e5c3066441f1a684825c1][^c-3112d6801fffc7e7e92fd5552b9cb0f4][^c-2304b7339066533fb69166adf110df59][^c-fd450334b1d61a21d830c9c310bd0396][^c-8b81f85a39717bdde4f40a6bf0f1aad1]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In the case of a community, voluntary controlled or community special school in England[^c-5039e6a615a4f46285674744875c6012] or a maintained nursery school in England —

- (a) the local authority[^c-379c0a8ab791aad4cda0b42dbea5f020] shall determine the dates when the school terms and holidays are to begin and end, and
- (b)[^c-e0def43fee85ee0500f9ad85c6c7de19] [^c-989756dbd2e3feaeed92a1f5d36be156]...the governing body shall determine the times of the school sessions.

(2) In the case of a foundation, voluntary aided or foundation special school in England[^c-6fc594ddf7def4c88fd8ef14578891d4] the governing body shall determine—

- (a) the dates and times when the school terms and holidays are to begin and end, and
- (b) [^c-1b0765bc0977e673811bc7512c5626f2]... the times of the school sessions.

(3) Regulations may make provision—

- (a) as to the procedure to be followed where the governing body of a school within subsection (1) propose to make any change in the time of the school sessions;
- (b) as to the implementation of any such proposal;
- (c) for enabling the local authority to determine, for any purposes of the regulations, whether any person is to be treated as a parent of a registered pupil at the school.

(4) In this section “**the times of the school sessions**” means the times at which each of the school sessions (or, if there is only one, the school session) is to begin and end on any day.
