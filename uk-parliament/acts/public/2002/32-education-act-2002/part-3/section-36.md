## 36. Staffing of foundation, voluntary aided and foundation special schools[^c-14e7108c860845cda9417de02284999a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-aba09f0f7bb7a12dc3ad51c9a85433e2] This section applies to—

- (a) foundation schools,
- (b) voluntary aided schools, and
- (c) foundation special schools.

(2)[^c-aba09f0f7bb7a12dc3ad51c9a85433e2] Except as provided by regulations under subsection (4), any teacher or other member of staff who is appointed to work under a contract of employment at a school to which this section applies is to be employed by the governing body of the school.

(3)[^c-aba09f0f7bb7a12dc3ad51c9a85433e2] The teaching staff of any school to which this section applies shall include—

- (a) a person appointed as head teacher, or
- (b) a person appointed to carry out the functions of the head teacher of the school—
  - (i) pending the appointment of a head teacher, or
  - (ii) in the absence of the head teacher.

(4)[^c-3dbeffc2ea8227ba3b3a7f3aa6afa5a8] Regulations may make further provision with respect to the staffing of schools to which this section applies.

(5)[^c-3dbeffc2ea8227ba3b3a7f3aa6afa5a8] Regulations under subsection (4) may, in particular—

- (a) make provision with respect to the appointment, discipline, suspension and dismissal of teachers and other staff,
- (b) make provision with respect to the appointment of teachers and other staff to work at a school otherwise than under a contract of employment,
- (c) make provision with respect to staff employed, or engaged otherwise than under a contract of employment, wholly or partly for the purposes of—
  - (i) the provision of facilities and services under section 27, or
  - (ii) any other activities which are not school activities but are carried on on the school premises under the management or control of the governing body,
- (d) enable teachers and other staff to be employed by the local authority[^c-379c0a8ab791aad4cda0b42dbea5f020] in prescribed cases, and
- (e) confer functions on local authorities[^c-0b83dc4852763b67d3e91bf8700f3c44], governing bodies and head teachers.

(6)[^c-aba09f0f7bb7a12dc3ad51c9a85433e2] Regulations under subsection (4) shall have effect subject to section 58 of the School Standards and Framework Act 1998 (c. 31) (appointment and dismissal of certain teachers at schools with a religious character).

(7)[^c-aba09f0f7bb7a12dc3ad51c9a85433e2] If at any time a school to which this section applies does not have a delegated budget by virtue of any suspension under[^c-aa2de1c40d8ba18d319adaa0940adfbd]... Schedule 15 to, the School Standards and Framework Act 1998, or section 66 of the Education and Inspections Act 2006[^c-15e721042654cf447c9187d501634ac7], or section 8 of the School Standards and Organisation (Wales) Act 2013[^c-600929972a67ab5f5ec11130d51f8389], regulations under subsection (4) shall have effect subject to the provisions of Part 2 of Schedule 2.

(8)[^c-aba09f0f7bb7a12dc3ad51c9a85433e2] In discharging any function conferred by regulations under subsection (4), a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e350791d9a270960a373d80754b1fc03] local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e350791d9a270960a373d80754b1fc03] in Wales[^c-785dcc952f6c3dc41ce3ae9ab3b76c8f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e350791d9a270960a373d80754b1fc03] or the governing body or head teacher of a maintained school *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e350791d9a270960a373d80754b1fc03] in Wales[^c-c6824f052eac646d9af1e8805fd0809b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e350791d9a270960a373d80754b1fc03] shall have regard to any guidance given from time to time— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e350791d9a270960a373d80754b1fc03]

- (a)[^c-5465b0ac5b92eeae004a8cd0045ed731] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) [^c-338c37abbb3b6af94b239f513790771b]... by the National Assembly for Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-e350791d9a270960a373d80754b1fc03]
