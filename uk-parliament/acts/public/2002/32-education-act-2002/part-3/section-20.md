# Education Act 2002, section 20

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 20. Instruments of government

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-845330742f45f498b6c4ffada3f33a8b][^key-5fb9d9fc951e2ee0a9b96fca25aa666f][^key-296560a9427f3f3b75b17bfa990bd63c] For every maintained school there shall be an instrument (known as the instrument of government) which determines the constitution of the governing body and other matters relating to the school.

(2)[^key-447aec9a26900ea8c8eefd10c0b7f639][^key-296560a9427f3f3b75b17bfa990bd63c] Regulations shall make provision with respect to the making of instruments of government, the matters to be dealt with in such instruments, the form of such instruments, and the review and variation of such instruments.

(3)[^key-447aec9a26900ea8c8eefd10c0b7f639][^key-296560a9427f3f3b75b17bfa990bd63c] Regulations under subsection (2) may require any person responsible for the making, review or variation of an instrument of government to have regard to any guidance given from time to time (in relation to England) by the Secretary of State or (in relation to Wales) by the National Assembly for Wales.

(4)[^key-b98d8a691e275f30a5288e47fb4400a2][^key-db02101b1bb2964ad35bee8db80a6e54][^key-30641d30594b6883b817ed2ee6a10f0b] In relation to maintained schools in England,[^key-e7f945b801dda449f0a21d9afaefa96b] subsection (1) has effect subject to section 24; and regulations under subsection (2) may include provision with respect to instruments of government for federations.

(4A)[^key-bd585f8c79a0eb1239c631e1475fd811] In relation to maintained schools in Wales, subsection (1) has effect subject to Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Education (Wales) Measure 2011; and regulations under subsection (2) may include provision with respect to instruments of government for federations (within the meaning of section 21(1) of that Measure).

(5)[^key-845330742f45f498b6c4ffada3f33a8b][^key-296560a9427f3f3b75b17bfa990bd63c] The governing body of a maintained school shall not conduct the school under a name other than the one for the time being set out in the school’s instrument of government.
