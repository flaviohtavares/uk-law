## 40. Amendments of Part 2 of School Standards and Framework Act 1998[^c381697][^c-a486bc92fcaf344f44531e78e6f33687][^c-7c2697608c44fa1ae1a365d8c0bab37a][^c-fcdef681ecbd29811ff8ae56e1228c55]
