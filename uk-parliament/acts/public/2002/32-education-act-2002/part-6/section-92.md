# Education Act 2002, section 92

## 92. Pupils with EHC plans[^key-79d184a82f7b5656d3a5d501522b2313]

*Extent: E+W*

The special educational provision for any pupil specified in an EHC plan maintained for the pupil[^key-88b792eda40d04c980c0a0da8af5d902] may include provision—

- (a) excluding the application of the National Curriculum for England, or
- (b) applying the National Curriculum for England with such modifications as may be specified in the plan[^key-d1a224e92fc1f8604177deac1ebda1e9].
