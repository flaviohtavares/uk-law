## 171. Interpretation of Chapter 1[^key-118b742d46c62ea3e7f12cc0b37335dc][^key-b45f4040ff46a149ff89b82c37dd9e34]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Chapter—

- “**appeal period**” has the meaning given by section 165;
- “**Chief Inspector**” means—
  - (a) [^key-df22774285fe519a4cd410a0c9556d69]...
  - (b) [^key-7dddc012b34b3062d7973cb58173567c]... Her Majesty’s Chief Inspector of Education and Training in Wales;
- [^key-143f3bc04416ac13666e516a610a493f]...
- “**independent school standards**” has the meaning given by section 157;
- “**the register**” means—
  - (a) [^key-1fde6a02480af11847f8d4da174dec10]...
  - (b) [^key-1b558de7cc6a3259608206d9d7f74c06]... the register of independent schools in Wales;
- “**registered**” means entered in the register;
- [^key-090a59f859487f0e66c40d49456ea418]...
- “**registration authority**” means the Welsh Ministers[^key-96794f0669f41684e54745e96e5f390a].
