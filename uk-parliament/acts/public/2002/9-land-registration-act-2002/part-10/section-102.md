# Land Registration Act 2002, section 102

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 102. Fee orders[^c4271261]

*Extent: E+W*

The Secretary of State[^c-f6089df4c616bfb5298d0618b8366b9e] may with the advice and assistance of the body referred to in section 127(2) (the Rule Committee), and the consent of the Treasury, by order—

- (a) prescribe fees to be paid in respect of dealings with the land registry, except under section 69(3)(b) or 105;
- (b) make provision about the payment of prescribed fees.
