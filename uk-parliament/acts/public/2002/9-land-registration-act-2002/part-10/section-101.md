# Land Registration Act 2002, section 101

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 101. Annual report

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The registrar must make an annual report on the business of the land registry to the Secretary of State[^c-01acba9c1a3e3d2f77ddb6fc8e49cc51].

(2) The registrar must publish every report under this section and may do so in such manner as he thinks fit.

(3) The Secretary of State[^c-979863eeeb255524d521e737c5086d97] must lay copies of every report under this section before Parliament.
