# Land Registration Act 2002, section 88

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/9
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 88. Incorporeal hereditaments

*Extent: E+W*

In its application to—

- (a) rentcharges,
- (b) franchises,
- (c) profits a prendre in gross, or
- (d) manors,

this Act shall have effect subject to such modification as rules may provide.
