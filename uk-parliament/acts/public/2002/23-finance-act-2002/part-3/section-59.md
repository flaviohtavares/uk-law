## 59. Cars with low carbon dioxide emissions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 19 to this Act (first-year allowances in respect of expenditure on cars with low CO<sub>2</sub> emissions and exemption from single asset pool rules) has effect in relation to expenditure incurred on or after 17th April 2002.
