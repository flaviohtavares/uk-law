## 142. Interpretation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act “**the Taxes Act 1988**” means the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 (c. 1), and “[^c-deaf483eda91b75efc439f5ebad23fb9]**ITA 2007**” means the Income Tax Act 2007”.
