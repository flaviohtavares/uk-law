# Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002, section 69

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (51).** Text as at 2025-03-03. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 69. Interpretation[^c21529931]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In this Part—

- “[^c-f1fc47401daf46edd4793f938dee390e]**higher-risk building**” has the meaning given by section 115 of the Building Safety Act 2022,
- “**higher-risk commonhold**” means a commonhold in England that includes all or any part of a higher-risk building,
- “**instrument**” includes any document, and
- “[^c21535021]**object**”, in relation to a commonhold association, means an object stated in the association's articles of association (see section 31 of the Companies Act 2006);

(2) In this Part—

- (a) a reference to a duty to insure includes a reference to a duty to use the proceeds of insurance for the purpose of rebuilding or reinstating, and
- (b) a reference to maintaining property includes a reference to decorating it and to putting it into sound condition.

(3) A provision of the Law of Property Act 1925 (c. 20), the Companies Act 2006[^c21535051] or the Land Registration Act 2002 (c.9) defining an expression shall apply to the use of the expression in this Part unless the contrary intention appears.
