# Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (51).** Text as at 2025-03-03. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/15

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 12. Unit-holder

*Extent: E+W*

A person is the unit-holder of a commonhold unit if he is entitled to be registered as the proprietor of the freehold estate in the unit (whether or not he is registered).
