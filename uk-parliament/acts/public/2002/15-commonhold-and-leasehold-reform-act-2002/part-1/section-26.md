# Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002, section 26

## 26. Use , maintenance and building safety[^c-52ec56edf4fd79f55ed41cd07dc90378]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-2ecf6174f2582cae8c09a2f3e6dd3098] A commonhold community statement must make provision—

- (a) regulating the use of the common parts;
- (b) requiring the commonhold association to insure the common parts;
- (c) requiring the commonhold association to repair and maintain the common parts.

(2)[^c-f6aa104f4cfcbc427dc03ec56b5a0fa6] A commonhold community statement for a higher-risk commonhold must make provision requiring the commonhold association to comply with its duties under Part 4 of the Building Safety Act 2022, or regulations made under that Part of that Act, in relation to the common parts.
