# Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-11-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 18. Leasing: non-residential[^c4026661]

*Extent: E+W*

An instrument or agreement which creates a term of years absolute in a commonhold unit which is not residential (within the meaning of section 17) shall have effect subject to any provision of the commonhold community statement.
