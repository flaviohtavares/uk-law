# Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-11-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 62. Advice etc.[^c21534451]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State[^c-2925ed828a6ab9eaf0041bc00198dd56] may give financial assistance to a person in relation to the provision by that person of information, training or[^c21534391] general advice about, or a dispute resolution service in connection with—[^c21534411]

- (a) any aspect of the law of commonhold land, so far as relating to residential matters , or[^c21534431]
- (b) any other matter relating to commonhold land and residential matters.

(2) Financial assistance under this section may be given in such form and on such terms as the Secretary of State[^c-4eae71e580a4dba2a391ed023a955d28] thinks appropriate.

(3) The terms may, in particular, require repayment in specified circumstances.
