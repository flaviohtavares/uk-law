# Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002, section 119

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (51).** Text as at 2025-03-03. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 119. Proportion of tenants required to participate[^c3654821]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 13(2)(b) of the 1993 Act (persons by whom initial notice must be given), omit sub-paragraph (i) (initial notice to be given by at least two-thirds of qualifying tenants of flats contained in premises).
