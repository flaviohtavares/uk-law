# Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002, section 120

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-11-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 120. Abolition of residence condition[^c3654831]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 13(2) of the 1993 Act, omit the words following paragraph (b) (which require at least one-half of the qualifying tenants by whom the initial notice is given to satisfy the residence condition).
