# Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002, section 134

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (51).** Text as at 2025-03-03. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 134. Valuation date[^c3654971]

*Extent: E+W*

In Schedule 13 to the 1993 Act (premium and other amounts payable by tenant on grant of new lease), for “the valuation date” (in each place) substitute “ the relevant date ”.
