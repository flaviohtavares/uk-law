# Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002, section 127

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (51).** Text as at 2025-03-03. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/15
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 127. Freeholder’s share of marriage value[^c3654881]

*Extent: E+W*

In paragraph 4(1) of Schedule 6 to the 1993 Act (freeholder’s share of marriage value), for the words after “freeholder’s share of the marriage value is” substitute “ 50 per cent. of that amount ”.
