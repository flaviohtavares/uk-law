## 175. Appeals[^c21533121]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A party to proceedings before a leasehold valuation tribunal may appeal to the Upper Tribunal[^c21534531] from a decision of the leasehold valuation tribunal.

(2) But the appeal may be made only with the permission of—

- (a) the leasehold valuation tribunal, or
- (b) the Upper Tribunal.

(3) [^c21534601]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) On the appeal the Upper Tribunal may exercise any power which was available to the leasehold valuation tribunal.

(5) And a decision of the Upper Tribunal on the appeal may be enforced in the same way as a decision of the leasehold valuation tribunal.
