# Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002, section 135

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (45).** Text as at 2026-11-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/15

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 135. Landlord’s share of marriage value[^c3654981]

*Extent: E+W*

In paragraph 4(1) of Schedule 13 to the 1993 Act (landlord’s share of marriage value), for the words after “landlord’s share of the marriage value is” substitute “ 50 per cent. of that amount ”.
