## SCHEDULE 4 — Development rights

*Extent: E+W*

Section 58

### 1. Introductory

This Schedule sets out the matters which are development business for the purposes of section 58.

### 2. Works

The completion or execution of works on—

- (a) a commonhold,
- (b) land which is or may be added to a commonhold, or
- (c) land which has been removed from a commonhold.

### 3. Marketing

(1) Transactions in commonhold units.

(2) Advertising and other activities designed to promote transactions in commonhold units.

### 4. Variation

The addition of land to a commonhold.

**5.** The removal of land from a commonhold.

**6.** Amendment of a commonhold community statement (including amendment to redefine the extent of a commonhold unit).

### 7. Commonhold association

Appointment and removal of directors of a commonhold association.
