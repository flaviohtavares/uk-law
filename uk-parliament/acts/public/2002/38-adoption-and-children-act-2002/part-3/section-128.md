# Adoption and Children Act 2002, section 128

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2022-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/38

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 128. Supply of information for the register[^c-6626e6039d11e912d7dccfc6444b0c21]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] Regulations[^c-0279d4c106f767f7e03fa18772076099] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] may require adoption agencies to give prescribed information to the Secretary of State or the registration organisation for entry in the register. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(2) Information is to be given to the Secretary of State or the registration organisation when required by *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] regulations[^c-c9050c9122a0e0b7da22398c125c9658] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] and in the prescribed form and manner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(3) Regulations[^c-763076297b65a36ab3d8a88c51c3f7e7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] may require an agency giving information which is entered on the register to pay a prescribed fee to the Secretary of State or the registration organisation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(4) But an adoption agency is not to disclose any information to the Secretary of State or the registration organisation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

- (a) about prospective adopters who are suitable to adopt a child, or persons who were included in the register as such prospective adopters, without their consent, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]
- (b) about children suitable for adoption *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] or for whom a local authority in England are considering adoption[^c-b9db0efc085dd4c6e761330bf798233b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c], or persons who were included in the register as such children, without the consent of the prescribed person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(5) Consent under subsection (4) is to be given in the prescribed form. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]
