# Adoption and Children Act 2002, section 125

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2022-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/38

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 125. Adoption and Children Act Register[^c-c2282eaa4c951c40e34e4c62c6ec3918]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] The Secretary of State may[^c-3011afb2396afa43e6e7576317a3a49d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] establish and maintain a register, to be called the Adoption and Children Act Register, containing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

- (a) prescribed information about children who are suitable for adoption *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c], children for whom a local authority in England are considering adoption[^c-52c706ccb1e1dc717a8a7fac125012d0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] and prospective adopters who are suitable to adopt a child, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]
- (b) prescribed information about persons included in the register in pursuance of paragraph (a) in respect of things occurring after their inclusion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(1A)[^c-fa2a4df7f8b85f9942de296e017de277] Regulations may provide that the register may contain— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

- (a) prescribed information about children who a Welsh, Scottish or Northern Irish adoption agency is satisfied are suitable for adoption, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]
- (b) prescribed information about prospective adopters who a Welsh, Scottish or Northern Irish adoption agency is satisfied are suitable to adopt a child, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]
- (c) prescribed information about persons included in the register in pursuance of paragraph (a) or (b) in respect of things occurring after their inclusion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(2) For the purpose of giving assistance in finding persons with whom children may be placed for purposes other than adoption, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] regulations[^c-889f4c597635f85fc65544a869faa5a3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

- (a) provide for the register to contain information about such persons and the children who may be placed with them, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]
- (b) apply any of the other provisions of this group of sections (that is, this section and sections 126 to 131), with or without modifications. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(3) The register is not to be open to public inspection or search *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] (subject to regulations under section 128A)[^c-c2d33545c9b0ed4315730037bf556ea7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c].

(4) Regulations[^c-bc200f11d30734b82303f87321fbb508] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] may make provision about the retention of information in the register. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(5) Information is to be kept in the register in any form the Secretary of State considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]
