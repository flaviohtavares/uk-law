## 127. Use of an organisation as agency for payments[^c-8d8fe2d4a2ab67606baa896bec2795dc]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] Regulations[^c-613e5b3abdf57e9d6016425a90501a7c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] may authorise an organisation with which an arrangement is made under section 126 to act as agent for the payment or receipt of sums payable by adoption agencies to other adoption agencies and may require adoption agencies to pay or receive such sums through the organisation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(2) The organisation is to perform the functions exercisable by virtue of this section in accordance with any directions given by the Secretary of State; and the directions may be of general application *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c][^c-6d5171c73bb4432130504e8854071ba0]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] or be special directions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]
