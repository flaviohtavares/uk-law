# Adoption and Children Act 2002, section 129

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2022-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/38
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 129. Disclosure of information[^key-5287e8711b18740a7682dcf5adcfc2be]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] Information entered in the register, or compiled from information entered in the register, may only be disclosed under subsection (2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c], (2A) or (3) or section 128A[^key-bb2db245b7e1a07c54318ad9bb3559db] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c].

(2) Prescribed information entered in the register may be disclosed by the Secretary of State or the registration organisation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

- (a) where an adoption agency is acting on behalf of a child who is suitable for adoption *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] or for whom a local authority in England is considering adoption[^key-466d430b5abb9aab26522640a018aa4a] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c], to the agency to assist in finding prospective adopters with whom it would be appropriate for the child to be placed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]
- (b) where an adoption agency is acting on behalf of prospective adopters who are suitable to adopt a child, to the agency to assist in finding a child appropriate for adoption by them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(2A)[^key-70e57bd12d4fb88b6a879954df789b74] Regulations may make provision permitting the disclosure of prescribed information entered in the register, or compiled from information entered in the register— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

- (a) to an adoption agency or to a Welsh, Scottish or Northern Irish adoption agency for any prescribed purpose, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]
- (b) for the purpose of enabling the information to be entered in a register which is maintained in respect of Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland and which contains information about children who are suitable for adoption or prospective adopters who are suitable to adopt a child. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(3) Prescribed information entered in the register, or compiled from information entered in the register, may be disclosed by the Secretary of State or the registration organisation to any prescribed person for use for statistical or research purposes, or for other prescribed purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(4) Regulations[^key-48546b668b24f5dbf60d977a063e4bf8] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] may prescribe the steps to be taken by adoption agencies in respect of information received by them by virtue of subsection (2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] or (2A)[^key-cae556eaf0664174eb1c9462ef82228e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c].

(5) Subsection (1) does not apply — *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

- (a) to a disclosure of information with the authority of the Secretary of State, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c][^key-2b8eb7d3733287f8aa8a14e29852ef3c]...
- (b)[^key-2b8eb7d3733287f8aa8a14e29852ef3c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) Information disclosed to any person under subsection (2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c], (2A)[^key-08143eca767f8b5cc3bf3b768c835575] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] or (3) may be given on any prescribed terms or conditions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(7) Regulations[^key-0c02d9c42e0b1167835e862eab9f80e5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] may, in prescribed circumstances, require a prescribed fee to be paid to the Secretary of State or the registration organisation— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

- (a) by a prescribed adoption agency in respect of information disclosed under subsection (2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] or (2A)[^key-2d813a010957797450a67829065d1b35] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c],
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-f680da18fd90f3dc4e3354def4b4c29f][^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] by a prescribed Welsh, Scottish or Northern Irish adoption agency in respect of information disclosed under subsection (2A), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]
- (b) by a person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] in respect of information disclosed under subsection (2A) or (3)[^key-2c990b93d98adb96de3dafa6b0a320f3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c].

(8) If any information entered in the register is disclosed to a person in contravention of subsection (1), the person disclosing it is guilty of an offence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(9) A person guilty of an offence under subsection (8) is liable on summary conviction to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months, or a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, or both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]
