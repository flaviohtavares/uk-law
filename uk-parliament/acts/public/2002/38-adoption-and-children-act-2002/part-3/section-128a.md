# Adoption and Children Act 2002, section 128A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2022-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/38
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 128A. Search and inspection of the register by prospective adopters[^key-9ca22f65b66528e9f825773396add0a5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c] Regulations may make provision enabling prospective adopters who are suitable to adopt a child to search and inspect the register, for the purposes of assisting them to find a child for whom they would be appropriate adopters. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) may make provision enabling prospective adopters to search and inspect only prescribed parts of the register, or prescribed content on the register. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(3) Access to the register for the purpose of searching and inspecting it may be granted on any prescribed terms and conditions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(4) Regulations may prescribe the steps to be taken by prospective adopters in respect of information received by them as a result of searching or inspecting the register. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(5) Regulations may make provision requiring prospective adopters, in prescribed circumstances, to pay a prescribed fee to the Secretary of State or the registration organisation in respect of searching or inspecting the register. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

(6) On the occasion of the first exercise of the power to make regulations under this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]

- (a) the statutory instrument containing the regulations is not to be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]
- (b) accordingly section 140(2) does not apply to the instrument. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f8942c0ce2b3c145387acc8dc8de4f6c]
