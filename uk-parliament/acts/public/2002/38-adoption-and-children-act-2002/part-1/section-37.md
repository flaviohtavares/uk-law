# Adoption and Children Act 2002, section 37

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-11-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/38
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 37. Applicants for adoption[^c-e63bd4f85d31e855e82d1b9c32388239][^c-d85f0ddb028eb8c66f35e3f0341f2342][^c-2cb31dadcb0e15fe8ccda632685a614b]

*Extent: E+W*

If section 36(1)(a) applies, the following persons may remove the child—

- (a) a person who has the court’s leave,
- (b) a local authority or other person in the exercise of a power conferred by any enactment, other than section 20(8) of the 1989 Act or section 76(5) of the[^c-6b5a8e882f407dbe76a58494035120de] 2014 Act.
