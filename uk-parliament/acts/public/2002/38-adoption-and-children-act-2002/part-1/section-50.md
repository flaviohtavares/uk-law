# Adoption and Children Act 2002, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2022-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/38
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Adoption by couple[^key-fd74ff1ad4741379a85ce7f42a66ed54]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-993e93193c608427c5012f7117737ca9] An adoption order may be made on the application of a couple where both of them have attained the age of 21 years.

(2)[^key-993e93193c608427c5012f7117737ca9] An adoption order may be made on the application of a couple where—

- (a) one of the couple is the mother or the father of the person to be adopted and has attained the age of 18 years, and
- (b) the other has attained the age of 21 years.
