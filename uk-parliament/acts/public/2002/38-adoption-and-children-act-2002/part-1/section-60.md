# Adoption and Children Act 2002, section 60

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2022-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/38

## 60. Disclosing information to adopted adult

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-4d15f28e6fa7d7e268795156bef3f752][^c-89be013c9aac36006df44cde79e443a0][^c-d5ae39fd1501c8e07a1477ea5129993c] This section applies to an adopted person who has attained the age of 18 years.

(2)[^c-e3bfdd325c97d47db2f9b1cdad7c1e42][^c-314070e456296db604eb33db5a217ed3][^c-3db7a3459a316991f6c6d71387f4ebad][^c-2b1a872fc3eb26cb2d9c4c1bc785d5cc] The adopted person has the right, at his request, to receive from the appropriate adoption agency—

- (a) any information which would enable him to obtain a certified copy of the record of his birth, unless the High Court or family court[^c-a2a58ebbfb4da9813ae40d2805a5f4bb] orders otherwise,
- (b) any prescribed information disclosed to the adopters by the agency by virtue of section 54.

(3)[^c-4d15f28e6fa7d7e268795156bef3f752][^c-89be013c9aac36006df44cde79e443a0] The High Court or family court[^c-d85b616e891fb0cef3543b32122ff7c7] may make an order under subsection (2)(a), on an application by the appropriate adoption agency, if satisfied that the circumstances are exceptional.

(4)[^c-e3bfdd325c97d47db2f9b1cdad7c1e42][^c-314070e456296db604eb33db5a217ed3][^c-3db7a3459a316991f6c6d71387f4ebad][^c-2b1a872fc3eb26cb2d9c4c1bc785d5cc][^c-66d09bca2d791e97cc54c4ab7cc61c0e] The adopted person also has the right, at his request, to receive from the court which made the adoption order a copy of any prescribed document or prescribed order relating to the adoption.

(5)[^c-4d15f28e6fa7d7e268795156bef3f752][^c-89be013c9aac36006df44cde79e443a0] Subsection (4) does not apply to a document or order so far as it contains information which is protected information.
