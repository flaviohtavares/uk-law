# Adoption and Children Act 2002, section 40

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-11-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/38
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 40. Other non-agency cases[^c-c65684eb741b44d53ebea5fcdf67b653][^c-d85f0ddb028eb8c66f35e3f0341f2342]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In any case where sections 37 to 39 do not apply but—

- (a) the people concerned have given notice of intention to adopt, or
- (b) the people concerned have applied for leave under section 42(6) and the application has not been disposed of,

the following persons may remove the child.

(2) They are—

- (a) a person who has the court’s leave,
- (b) a local authority or other person in the exercise of a power conferred by any enactment, other than section 20(8) of the 1989 Act or section 76(5) of the[^c-c7af3ac1306e242750a5285bb178900f] 2014 Act.
