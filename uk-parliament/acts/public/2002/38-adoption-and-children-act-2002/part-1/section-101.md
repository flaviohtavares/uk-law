# Adoption and Children Act 2002, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2022-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/38
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101. Privacy[^key-7a9a4c2c1b409be97eca4c4c21d45f75]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-9d109ed114604f68bd38d133a0e8a7d2] Proceedings under this Act in the High Court or the family court[^key-132717369a8021580679eb6cd27142a7] may be heard and determined in private.

(2) In section 12 of the Administration of Justice Act 1960 (c. 65) (publication of information relating to proceedings in private), in subsection (1)(a)(ii), after “1989” there is inserted “ or the Adoption and Children Act 2002 ”.

(3) In section 97 of the 1989 Act (privacy for children involved in certain proceedings), after “this Act” in subsections (1) and (2) there is inserted “ or the Adoption and Children Act 2002 ”.
