## 55. Revocation of adoptions on legitimation[^c-8e4dc8717250d59526b221940c79f11a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where any child adopted by one natural parent as sole adoptive parent subsequently becomes a legitimated person on the marriage of, or formation of a civil partnership by,[^c-48ed25794d14d240996e1e0b7fbce424] the natural parents, the court by which the adoption order was made may, on the application of any of the parties concerned, revoke the order.
