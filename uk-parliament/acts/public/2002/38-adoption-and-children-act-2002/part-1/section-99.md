# Adoption and Children Act 2002, section 99

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2022-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/38
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Proceedings for offences[^key-d60cfe032c88c8840682b3a3bc670b99]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Proceedings for an offence by virtue of section 9 or 59 may not, without the written consent of the Attorney General, be taken by any person other than Her Majesty's Chief Inspector of Education, Children's Services and Skills[^key-d48bc6a82b69b2e391343f1750e37b8a] or the Assembly.
