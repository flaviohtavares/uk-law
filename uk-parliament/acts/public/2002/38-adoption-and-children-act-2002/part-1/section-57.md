# Adoption and Children Act 2002, section 57

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2022-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/38
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Restrictions on disclosure of protected etc. information[^c-25c6f33f2023be807280494379fed0f5][^c-93aa83502ac78ff0e712b1f5f05203c2]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-2d802f4595a7c450a8ab2bf9b3882676] Any section 56 information kept by an adoption agency which—

- (a) is about an adopted person or any other person, and
- (b) is or includes identifying information about the person in question,

may only be disclosed by the agency to a person (other than the person the information is about) in pursuance of this group of sections.

(2)[^c-2d802f4595a7c450a8ab2bf9b3882676] Any information kept by an adoption agency—

- (a) which the agency has obtained from the Registrar General on an application under section 79(5) and any other information which would enable the adopted person to obtain a certified copy of the record of his birth, or
- (b) which is information about an entry relating to the adopted person in the Adoption Contact Register,

may only be disclosed to a person by the agency in pursuance of this group of sections.

(3)[^c-2d802f4595a7c450a8ab2bf9b3882676] In this group of sections, information the disclosure of which to a person is restricted by virtue of subsection (1) or (2) is referred to (in relation to him) as protected information.

(4)[^c-2d802f4595a7c450a8ab2bf9b3882676] Identifying information about a person means information which, whether taken on its own or together with other information disclosed by an adoption agency, identifies the person or enables the person to be identified.

(5)[^c-b768b590738451e06140f6b77865f601][^c-b2a7d39c301b38221c092f87500d162e] This section does not prevent the disclosure of protected information in pursuance of a prescribed agreement to which the adoption agency is a party.

(6)[^c-6328060653164e6ec101fe62212e7932][^c-2d802f4595a7c450a8ab2bf9b3882676] Regulations may authorise or require an adoption agency to disclose protected information to a person who is not an adopted person.
