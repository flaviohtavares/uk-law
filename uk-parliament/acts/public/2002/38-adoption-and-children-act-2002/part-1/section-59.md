# Adoption and Children Act 2002, section 59

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (16).** Text as at 2022-11-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/38
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Offence[^key-033d14fb7593b55125c37670963714e9][^key-882152c9e04f52ecd8a82a5ecf7838f0][^key-420e0b251bf8f289775eec83c2a1c421]

*Extent: E+W*

Regulations may provide that a registered adoption society which discloses any information in contravention of section 57 is to be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
