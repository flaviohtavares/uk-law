# Adoption and Children Act 2002, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (10).** Text as at 2026-11-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/38
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Disclosure of other information[^c-3e3b39ed68ec68634c2ff931ff07528a][^c-1f8143fde4baca80087f94acd35e16d7]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c-ffdbfbf2be6c2003db932d85cf194be8] This section applies to any section 56 information other than protected information.

(2)[^c-dd4f19f301ca70cec2546bee83b867c1][^c-31f417d03af0ef5654a840ea53ae95ee] An adoption agency may for the purposes of its functions disclose to any person in accordance with prescribed arrangements any information to which this section applies.

(3)[^c-dd4f19f301ca70cec2546bee83b867c1][^c-31f417d03af0ef5654a840ea53ae95ee] An adoption agency must, in prescribed circumstances, disclose prescribed information to a prescribed person.
