## 113. Section 8 orders: local authority foster parents[^c-49695c659c329ea137302337ce282218]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 9 of the 1989 Act (restrictions on making section 8 orders)—

- (a) in subsection (3)(c), for “three years” there is substituted “ one year ”, and
- (b) subsection (4) is omitted.
