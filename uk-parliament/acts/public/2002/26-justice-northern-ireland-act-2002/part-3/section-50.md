# Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002, section 50

## 50. Law Commission

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) There is to be a body corporate known as the Northern Ireland Law Commission.

(2) The Commission is to consist of—

- (a) a chairman, and
- (b) four other Commissioners,

appointed by the Department of Justice[^c-9e7a862399768c2a086bc108095dd987].

(3) The chairman is to be a person who holds the office of judge of the High Court.

(4) Of the other Commissioners—

- (a) one is to be a person appearing to the Department of Justice[^c-f53d09fda3d935d904d9279504d747f8] to be suitably qualified to be a Commissioner by experience as a barrister,
- (b) one is to be a person appearing to the Department of Justice[^c-9317d3dac4da6a02637c14dbf0f60db5] to be suitably qualified to be a Commissioner by experience as a solicitor,
- (c) one is to be a person appearing to the Department of Justice[^c-29af6162694360cb2ac9fbb6defcf6d4] to be suitably qualified to be a Commissioner by experience as a teacher of law in a university, and
- (d) the other is to be a person who does not hold (and has never held) judicial office and is not (and has never been) a barrister, solicitor or teacher of law in a university.

(5) Before appointing a person to be a Commissioner the Department of Justice[^c-b01d99a8e5b59bb95c13809058bb0e1c] must consult—

- (a)[^c-2e9e7c45afe391920db4bba087745fd5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the First Minister and deputy First Minister, and
- (c) the Attorney General for Northern Ireland.

(6) In appointing persons to be Commissioners, the Department of Justice[^c-3fcded67a90a5ed64919d23f9a4707c6] must so far as possible secure that the Commissioners (taken together) are representative of the community in Northern Ireland.

(7) Schedule 9 makes further provision about the Commission.
