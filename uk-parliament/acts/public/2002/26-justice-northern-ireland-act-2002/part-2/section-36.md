# Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2024-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 36. Exercise of functions by and on behalf of Service[^c-f96d3fcb4a48aa58e8fbbbbda60782e2][^c-0535911609b7543431e95d42f38ed050][^c-993223bf77b558fc832e7af78249f130]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) The Director may delegate any of his powers (to such extent as he determines) to—

- (a) any Public Prosecutor, or
- (b) any other member of staff of the Public Prosecution Service for Northern Ireland.

(2) The Director may at any time appoint a person who is not a member of staff of the Service but who is a barrister or solicitor in Northern Ireland to institute or take over the conduct of criminal proceedings or extradition proceedings[^c20689781] assigned to him by the Director.

(3) A person conducting proceedings assigned to him under subsection (2) has all the powers of a Public Prosecutor but must exercise them subject to any instructions given to him by a Public Prosecutor.
