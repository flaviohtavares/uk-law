# Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002, section 9A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2024-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9A. Judicial Appointments Ombudsman[^c20697151]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) There is to be a Northern Ireland Judicial Appointments Ombudsman.

(2)[^c-1e1c32c810f597fc8579c45272cc68a9][^c-cdcd94d5aaaa5d74d5186f14286ac1e1][^c-9e82759435586a11510a30bef8443383] The Northern Ireland Public Services Ombudsman is, by virtue of holding that office, the Northern Ireland Judicial Appointments Ombudsman.

(3) Schedule 3A makes further provision about the Ombudsman.
