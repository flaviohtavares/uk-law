## 63. Extension of youth justice system to 17 year olds[^c20726481]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) Schedule 11 makes amendments of enactments and instruments for extending the youth justice system to 17 year olds.

(2) The Department of Justice[^c-0b9c77ee2f4db225dcc2f78cfd8bf669] may by order make provision amending any other enactments or instruments (whenever passed or made) for, or in connection with, extending the youth justice system to 17 year olds.
