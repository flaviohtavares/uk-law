# Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002, section 90

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2024-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Statutory rules

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any power of—

- (a) the Lord Chancellor,
- (b) the Secretary of State, or
- (c) the First Minister and deputy First Minister or the[^c-15bacc74badae89bed8ef2ada270644d] Department of Justice[^c-01b896d63ad3718f4fe914f7ef0af3aa],

to make an order or a scheme (or alterations to a scheme) under this Act shall be exercisable by statutory rule for the purposes of the Statutory Rules (Northern Ireland) Order 1979 (S.I. 1979/1573 (N.I. 12)).

(1A)[^c-0ab219fea75a8fbe8cb19e2c6013f49f] No order containing (whether or not together with other provision) provision made by virtue of section 2(2)(b), 9(4) *(Extent: N.I.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8f67296841135e3bbc36322245236cde], 19(4)(b), 46(6)(a) or (b) or 72(1), (3), (7) or (8) shall be made unless a draft of the order has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, the Northern Ireland Assembly.

(1B) Section 41(3) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 applies for the purposes of subsection (1A) in relation to the laying of a draft as it applies in relation to the laying of a statutory document under an enactment.

(2) [^c20701041]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) [^c20701041]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) No order containing (whether or not together with other provision) provision made by virtue of section 78[^c-5fef37c08dc79c9a67636b259a4057f8] shall be made unless a draft of the order has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

(5) The following instruments—

- (a) an order under section 2(2)(a) or (c),[^c20685841]5A(6),[^c20701261] 10(4), 12(3), 19(4)(a) or (c), [^c-089c05452de4c4ac3cf903ebdf621e25]... 46(6)(c), 63(2), 72(2) or 85(2), Schedule 4 or paragraph 7(3) of Schedule 8, and
- (b) a scheme (or alterations to a scheme) under section 68 or 69A[^c20701101],

shall, unless a draft has been approved by a resolution of the Northern Ireland Assembly, be subject to negative resolution (within the meaning of section 41(6) of the Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954)[^c-c7e435960d4b9d827f123841b409a7fa].

(6)[^c-f7112d0c79b4e12f0b5dc22c670baea3] An order under section 28(2) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament in the same manner as a statutory instrument; and section 5 of the Statutory Instruments Act 1946 applies accordingly.
