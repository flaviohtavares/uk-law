# Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002, section 85

## 85. Minor and consequential amendments[^c2627681]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^key-7d2c224d26c5d05729f0152bdca57f0d] Schedule 12 makes minor and consequential amendments in enactments and instruments.

(2) The Department of Justice[^key-6916319f3f0a56e33e496835fb597c47] may by order make in any other enactment or instrument (whenever passed or made) such amendments as appear to the Department of Justice[^key-1f87529484efb9b9f7a165897cf74b56] to be appropriate in consequence of any provision made by Part 4.
