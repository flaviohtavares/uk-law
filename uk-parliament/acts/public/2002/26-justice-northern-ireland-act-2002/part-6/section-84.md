# Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002, section 84

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2024-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 84. Assembly Acts about judiciary, law officers and prosecutions[^c20728841]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) In section 7(1) of the Northern Ireland Act 1998 (entrenched enactments), insert at the end

> and
>
> - (d) section 1 and section 84 of the Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002.

(2)[^key-79a4f143452dbfe36c748159d26d1d5d] A Bill containing any provision which deals (otherwise than incidentally) with—

- (a) appointment to, or removal from, a protected judicial office (including the subject matter of section 19),
- (b) the subject matter of section 12(1), or
- (c) the subject matter of Part 2,

may not be passed by the Assembly without cross-community support.

(3)[^key-79a4f143452dbfe36c748159d26d1d5d] “**Cross-community support**” has the meaning given by section 4(5) of the Northern Ireland Act 1998 (c. 47).

(4)[^key-79a4f143452dbfe36c748159d26d1d5d] “**Passed**”, in relation to a Bill, means passed at the final stage (at which the Bill can be passed or rejected but not amended).
