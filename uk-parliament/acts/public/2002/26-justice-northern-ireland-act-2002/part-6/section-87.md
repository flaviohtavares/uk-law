# Justice (Northern Ireland) Act 2002, section 87

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2024-11-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Commencement[^c2627701]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The preceding provisions of this Act (with the Schedules) shall not come into force until such day as the Secretary of State or the Department of Justice[^key-2ed6d9f015b891c018ab0f73366d7812] may by order appoint.

(2) An order may appoint different days for different purposes.
