# Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002, section 155

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (99).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 155. Sections 153 and 154: supplemental

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following shall be added at the end of section 28L of the Immigration Act 1971 (c. 77) (interpretation) (which becomes subsection (1))—

> (2) In this Part “**business premises**” means premises (or any part of premises) not used as a dwelling.
>
> (3) In this Part “**employee records**” means records which show an employee’s—
>
> - (a) name,
> - (b) date of birth,
> - (c) address,
> - (d) length of service,
> - (e) rate of pay, or
> - (f) nationality or citizenship.
>
> (4) The Secretary of State may by order amend section 28CA(3)(a) to reflect a change in nomenclature.
>
> (5) An order under subsection (4)—
>
> - (a) must be made by statutory instrument, and
> - (b) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
