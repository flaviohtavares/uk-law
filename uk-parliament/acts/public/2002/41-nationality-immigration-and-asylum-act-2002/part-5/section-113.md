## 113. Interpretation[^key-0f07315b8f619cf09d9da6a24813b15b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part, unless a contrary intention appears—

- “**asylum claim**” means a claim made by a person to the Secretary of State at a place designated by the Secretary of State *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-8334e9558fe58b488d9159129c593072] that to remove the person from or require him to leave the United Kingdom would breach the United Kingdom’s obligations under the Refugee Convention,
- [^key-a17a91223985c4d04c0352b7a01c09ee]...
- “**human rights claim**” means a claim made by a person to the Secretary of State at a place designated by the Secretary of State that to remove the person from or require him to leave the United Kingdom or to refuse him entry into the United Kingdom[^key-319c9fe0872aa05f777676f89f5bd90c] would be unlawful under section 6 of the Human Rights Act 1998 (c. 42) (public authority not to act contrary to Convention) [^key-7ca366a4d3aecb8fbe801ecc2c8bd5f7]...,
- “**the Human Rights Convention**” has the same meaning as “**the Convention**” in the Human Rights Act 1998 and “**Convention rights**” shall be construed in accordance with section 1 of that Act,
- “[^key-990b7395ff6573b266959c1c63a89f75] **humanitarian protection** ” has the meaning given in section 82(2);
- [^key-a17a91223985c4d04c0352b7a01c09ee]...
- “**immigration rules**” means rules under section 1(4) of the Immigration Act 1971[^key-719decfbdc6efaf6f3d57a425e7560ce] (general immigration rules),
- [^key-a17a91223985c4d04c0352b7a01c09ee]...
- “ **protection claim** ” has the meaning given in section 82(2)
- “ **protection status** ” has the meaning given in section 82(2)
- “**the Refugee Convention**” means the Convention relating to the Status of Refugees done at Geneva on 28th July 1951 and its Protocol,
- [^key-a17a91223985c4d04c0352b7a01c09ee]...
- [^key-a17a91223985c4d04c0352b7a01c09ee]...
