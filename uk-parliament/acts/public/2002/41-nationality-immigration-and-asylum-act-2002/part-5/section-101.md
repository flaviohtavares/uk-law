# Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002, section 101

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (99).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 101. Appeal to Tribunal[^c19202911]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19202831][^c19227551] A party to an appeal to an adjudicator under section 82 or 83 may, with the permission of the Immigration Appeal Tribunal, appeal to the Tribunal against the adjudicator’s determination on a point of law. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227551]

(2)[^c19202871] A party to an application to the Tribunal for permission to appeal under subsection (1) may apply to the High Court or, in Scotland, to the Court of Session for a review of the Tribunal’s decision on the ground that the Tribunal made an error of law. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227551]

(3)[^c19202871] Where an application is made under subsection (2)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227551]

- (a) it shall be determined by a single judge by reference only to written submissions, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227551]
- (b) the judge may affirm or reverse the Tribunal’s decision, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227551]
- (c) the judge’s decision shall be final, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227551]
- (d) if, in an application to the High Court, the judge thinks the application had no merit he shall issue a certificate under this paragraph (which shall be dealt with in accordance with Civil Procedure Rules). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227551]

(4) The Lord Chancellor may by order repeal subsections (2) and (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227551]
