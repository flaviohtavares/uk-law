# Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002, section 85

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (99).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/41

## 85. Matters to be considered[^M_C_56201297-9735-47eb-afc1-2374a9ce446b][^c3925081][^c-e2e40fd133cddcf13e47322e73e83875]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An appeal under section 82(1) against a decision shall be treated by the Tribunal[^c19214071] as including an appeal against any decision in respect of which the appellant has a right of appeal under section 82(1).

(2)[^c-a4aa48850779e6eaf358d4c50cdd2ff0][^c-dc82462051eeed314ea7e8c31fa34991] If an appellant under section 82(1) makes a statement under section 120, the Tribunal shall consider any matter raised in the statement which constitutes a ground of appeal of a kind listed in section 84[^c-0020d1d66687bab95f6080e80029c000] against the decision appealed against.

(3) Subsection (2) applies to a statement made under section 120 whether the statement was made before or after the appeal was commenced.

(4)[^c3925091] On an appeal under section 82(1) [^c-bdb4fec02f1618159e39dd3e94540e23]... against a decision the Tribunal[^c19213941] may consider [^c-64ccc6636460741fbb5fb8eed85bbb69]... any matter which it[^c19214111] thinks relevant to the substance of the decision, including [^c-08ff1587f7c39af689cb35a77922f90c]... a matter arising after the date of the decision.

(5)[^c-fdfd8ec056066eb115736334d5dff069] But the Tribunal must not consider a new matter unless the Secretary of State has given the Tribunal consent to do so.

(6) A matter is a “new matter” if—

- (a)[^c-dc82462051eeed314ea7e8c31fa34991] it constitutes a ground of appeal of a kind listed in section 84, and
- (b) the Secretary of State has not previously considered the matter in the context of—
  - (i) the decision mentioned in section 82(1), or
  - (ii) a statement made by the appellant under section 120.
