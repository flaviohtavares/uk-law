# Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002, section 110

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (99).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Grants[^c3925171][^c3925181]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19258581] The Secretary of State may make a grant to a voluntary organisation which provides— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258581]

- (a) advice or assistance to persons who have a right of appeal under this Part; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258581]
- (b) other services for the welfare of those persons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258581]

(2) A grant under this section may be subject to terms or conditions (which may include conditions as to repayment). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258581]
