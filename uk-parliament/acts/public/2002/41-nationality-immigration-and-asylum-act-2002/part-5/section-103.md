# Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002, section 103

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (99).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 103. Appeal from Tribunal[^c19202941]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19228721] Where the Immigration Appeal Tribunal determines an appeal under section 101 a party to the appeal may bring a further appeal on a point of law— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228721]

- (a) where the original decision of the adjudicator was made in Scotland, to the Court of Session, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228721]
- (b) in any other case, to the Court of Appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228721]

(2) An appeal under this section may be brought only with the permission of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228721]

- (a) the Tribunal, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228721]
- (b) if the Tribunal refuses permission, the court referred to in subsection (1)(a) or (b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228721]

(3) The remittal of an appeal to an adjudicator under section 102(1)(c) is not a determination of the appeal for the purposes of subsection (1) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19228721]
