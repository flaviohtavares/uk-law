## 99. Section 97 : appeal in progress[^c19202681][^key-6b03294761aea942b83238f43034e56a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where a certificate is issued under section [^key-172b7b5732b117f4388a9f2cf9f32e6d]... 97 [^key-da0dd1fde004e5ffd32a6d38a69d5822]... in respect of a pending appeal.

(2) The appeal shall lapse.
