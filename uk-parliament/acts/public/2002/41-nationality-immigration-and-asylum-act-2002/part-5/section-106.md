# Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002, section 106

## 106. Rules[^c3925151][^c-bae3f3f3165fc7236ae5ea8c63193c67]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-201f31687ec0a869f3afa1a2b693fb83] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(1A)[^c-03e521a3466394a4c8e9af6a5b49557a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-e6e24300e29be48cb2845161dc32eb5d] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-158b8ff2d3510caf298000a71f58f566] In the case of an appeal under section 82 [^c-0b19b69dde1b9fbc0b8215387b8ac996]... [^c-8e44d923415dbe92f352e1797cbcec3f]..., Tribunal Procedure Rules may enable the Tribunal to certify that the appeal had no merit (and shall make provision for the consequences of the issue of a certificate).

(4) A person commits an offence if without reasonable excuse he fails to comply with a requirement imposed in accordance with Tribunal Procedure Rules in connection with proceedings under section 82[^c-232204ef2645a6a67b3a7f78ea095dce] [^c-114b8555ec6048fd433d2770d093b827]... [^c-4901badf35b50918675ae9d6f5d1282c]... to attend before [^c19216881]. . . the Tribunal—

- (a) to give evidence, or
- (b) to produce a document.

(5) A person who is guilty of an offence under subsection (4) shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
