# Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002, section 108

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (99).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/41

## 108. Forged document: proceedings in private[^c-09b9a7fa7f1e356a86bfcccccfc2283e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where it is alleged—

- (a) that a document relied on by a party to an appeal under section 82 [^c-b087096fc638a52c0ba180e8648002ac]... is a forgery, and
- (b) that disclosure to that party of a matter relating to the detection of the forgery would be contrary to the public interest.

(2) The Tribunal[^c19217231]—

- (a) must investigate the allegation in private, and
- (b) may proceed in private so far as necessary to prevent disclosure of the matter referred to in subsection (1)(b).
