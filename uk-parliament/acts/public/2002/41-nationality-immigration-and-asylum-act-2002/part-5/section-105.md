# Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002, section 105

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (99).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/41

## 105. Notice of immigration decision[^c3925141][^key-3a90d8ca4466a8fe27c022b7f042d0da]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may make regulations requiring a person to be given written notice where an appealable[^key-31a01eca2fcfb422a259df8c2ec3496b] decision is taken in respect of him.

(2) The regulations may, in particular, provide that a notice under subsection (1) of an appealable decision[^key-2f7ed7001021191e61b5456f9613ec85] must state—

- (a) that there is a right of appeal under section 82[^key-4ec92f7db024520379053fabc0d19fcf], and
- (b) how and when that right may be exercised.

(3) The regulations may make provision (which may include presumptions) about service.

(4)[^key-fc4c8d847521a1a9192bfa417aeca120] In this section “**appealable decision**” means a decision mentioned in section 82(1).
