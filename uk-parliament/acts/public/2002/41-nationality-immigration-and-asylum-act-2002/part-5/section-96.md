## 96. Earlier right of appeal[^c3925121][^c19212681]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person may not bring an appeal under section 82 against a decision (“the new decision”)[^key-e8e9b351b6f270ecb761447ac63a87df] if the Secretary of State or an immigration officer certifies—

- (a)[^c19202731] that the person was notified of a right of appeal under that section against another [^key-c837465c0b18398145b22ba2df498c5c]... decision (“the old decision”) (whether or not an appeal was brought and whether or not any appeal brought has been determined),
- (b) that the claim or application to which the new decision relates relies on aground[^key-2e01cb544ef5c00e3ede728c5e387604] that could have been raised in an appeal against the old decision, and
- (c) that, in the opinion of the Secretary of State or the immigration officer, there is no satisfactory reason for that ground[^key-97fefeb0c999243049ac7cd8c6dad07d] not having been raised in an appeal against the old decision.

(2)[^key-ac3e84a0708a875aa675d3e25dcae39a] A person may not bring an appeal under section 82 if the Secretary of State or an immigration officer certifies—

- (a) that the person has received a notice under section 120(2),
- (b) that the appeal relies on a ground that should have been, but has not been, raised in a statement made under section 120(2) or (5), and
- (c) that, in the opinion of the Secretary of State or the immigration officer, there is no satisfactory reason for that ground not having been raised in a statement under section 120(2) or (5).

(4) In subsection (1) “**notified**” means notified in accordance with regulations under section 105.

(5) Subsections (1) and (2) apply to prevent[^c19212711] a person’s right of appeal whether or not he has been outside the United Kingdom since an earlier right of appeal arose or since a requirement under section 120 was imposed.

(6) In this section a reference to an appeal under section 82(1) includes a reference to an appeal under section 2 of the Special Immigration Appeals Commission Act 1997 (c. 68) which is or could be brought by reference to an appeal under section 82(1).

(7)[^c19212731] A certificate under subsection (1) or (2) shall have no effect in relation to an appeal instituted before the certificate is issued.
