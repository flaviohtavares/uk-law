# Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002, section 102

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (99).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Decision[^c19202931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19227591] On an appeal under section 101 the Immigration Appeal Tribunal may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227591]

- (a) affirm the adjudicator’s decision; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227591]
- (b) make any decision which the adjudicator could have made; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227591]
- (c) remit the appeal to an adjudicator; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227591]
- (d) affirm a direction given by the adjudicator under section 87; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227591]
- (e) vary a direction given by the adjudicator under that section; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227591]
- (f) give any direction which the adjudicator could have given under that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227591]

(2) In reaching their decision on an appeal under section 101 the Tribunal may consider evidence about any matter which they think relevant to the adjudicator’s decision, including evidence which concerns a matter arising after the adjudicator’s decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227591]

(3) But where the appeal under section 82 was against refusal of entry clearance or refusal of a certificate of entitlement— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227591]

- (a) subsection (2) shall not apply, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227591]
- (b) the Tribunal may consider only the circumstances appertaining at the time of the decision to refuse. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227591]

(4) In remitting an appeal to an adjudicator under subsection (1)(c) the Tribunal may, in particular— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227591]

- (a) require the adjudicator to determine the appeal in accordance with directions of the Tribunal; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227591]
- (b) require the adjudicator to take additional evidence with a view to the appeal being determined by the Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227591]
