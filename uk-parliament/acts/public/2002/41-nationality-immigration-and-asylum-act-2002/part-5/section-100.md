## 100. Immigration Appeal Tribunal

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19227531] There shall continue to be an Immigration Appeal Tribunal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227531]

(2) Schedule 5 (which makes provision about the Tribunal) shall have effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19227531]
