# Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002, section 158

## 158. Interpretation: “the Immigration Acts”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19219011][^c19260501] A reference to “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19260501]**the Immigration Acts *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19260501]**” shall be construed in accordance with section 44 of the Asylum and Immigration (Treatment of Claimants, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19260501] etc. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19260501]) Act 2004. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19260501]

(3) The following shall be substituted for section 32(5) of the Immigration Act 1971— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19260501]

> (5) In subsection (4) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19260501]**the Immigration Acts *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19260501]**” has the meaning given by section 158 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19260501]

(4) The following shall be substituted for the definition of “the Immigration Acts” in section 167(1) of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19260501]

> “**the Immigration Acts *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19260501]**” has the meaning given by section 158 of the Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19260501]
