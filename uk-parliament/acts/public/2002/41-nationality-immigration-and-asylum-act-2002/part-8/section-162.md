# Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002, section 162

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (99).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 162. Commencement

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c3925211][^c19438851][^c19460301] Subject to subsections (2) to (5), the preceding provisions of this Act shall come into force in accordance with provision made by the Secretary of State by order.

(2) The following provisions shall come into force on the passing of this Act—

- (a) section 6,
- (b) section 7,
- (c) section 10(1) to (4) and (6),
- (d) section 11,
- (e) section 15 (and Schedule 2),
- (f) section 16,
- (g) section 35(1)(h),
- (h) section 38,
- (i) section 40(1),
- (j) section 41(1),
- (k) section 42,
- (l) section 43,
- (m) section 48,
- (n) section 49,
- (o) section 50,
- (p) section 56,
- (q) section 58,
- (r) section 59,
- (s) section 61,
- (t) section 67,
- (u) section 69,
- (v) section 70,
- (w) section 115 and paragraph 29 of Schedule 7 (and the relevant entry in Schedule 9),
- (x) section 157, and
- (y) section 160.

(3) Section 5 shall have effect in relation to—

- (a) an application made after the passing of this Act, and
- (b) an application made, but not determined, before the passing of this Act.

(4)[^c19206561] Section 8 shall have effect in relation to—

- (a) an application made on or after a date appointed by the Secretary of State by order, and
- (b) an application made, but not determined, before that date.

(5)[^c19460301] Section 9 shall have effect in relation to a child born on or after a date appointed by the Secretary of State by order.

(6)[^c3925211] An order under subsection (1) may—

- (a) make provision generally or for a specified purpose only (which may include the purpose of the application of a provision to or in relation to a particular place or area);
- (b) make different provision for different purposes;
- (c) include transitional provision;
- (d) include savings;
- (e) include consequential provision;
- (f) include incidental provision.

(7) An order under this section must be made by statutory instrument.
