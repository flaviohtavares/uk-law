## 127. Physical data: voluntary provision[^M_C_eaed66b3-10c5-4193-aeca-49085a5cd52c][^c-2677bea1378dfb4c9433293280267634]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may operate a scheme under which an individual may supply, or submit to the obtaining or recording of, biometric information[^c-ff9dbea9be01b74a087348a85096a1c5] to be used (wholly or partly) in connection with entry to the United Kingdom.

(2) In particular, the Secretary of State may—

- (a) require an authorised person to use biometric[^c-23cc34a2b77646ed52b4bb65b4a8e044] information supplied under a scheme;
- (b) make provision about the collection, use and retention of biometric information supplied under a scheme (which may include provision requiring an authorised person to have regard to a code);
- (c) charge for participation in a scheme.

(3) In this section the following expressions have the same meaning as in section 126—

- (a) “**authorised person**”,
- (aa)[^c-13beac2923a70b834c6734031f2dea4d] biometric information”, and
- (b) “**code**”, [^c-ddc3db354916df727c0ebc07056af61a]...
- (c)[^c-bf41d3b734321677d134efdf4d3437d0] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
