# Nationality, Immigration and Asylum Act 2002, section 122

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (99).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/41

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 122. Fee for work permit, &c.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19258871] The Secretary of State may by regulations require an application for an immigration employment document to be accompanied by a fee prescribed in the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]

(2) In subsection (1) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]**immigration employment document *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]

- (a) a work permit, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]
- (b) any other document which relates to employment and is issued for a purpose of immigration rules or in connection with leave to enter or remain in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]

(3) Regulations under subsection (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]

- (a) may make provision which applies generally or only in specified cases or circumstances (or except in specified cases or circumstances), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]
- (b) may make different provision for different cases or circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]

(4) In particular, regulations by virtue of subsection (3)(a) which create an exception may make provision by reference to an arrangement with the Secretary of State under which a payment is made in respect of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]

- (a) a specified number or class of applications, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]
- (b) a specified period of time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]

(5) Regulations under subsection (1)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]

- (a) must be made by statutory instrument, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]
- (b) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]

(6) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]

- “**immigration rules *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]**” has the meaning given by section 33(1) of the Immigration Act 1971 (c. 77) (interpretation), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]
- “**work permit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]**” has the meaning given by that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19258871]
