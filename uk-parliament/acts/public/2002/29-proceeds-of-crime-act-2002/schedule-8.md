## SCHEDULE 8 — Forms of declarations[^c-d40714360119dd6601950788b093c7c1]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 325

### ...[^c-0134b5c95325c616e51c6ba05287fc10]

### Members of SOCA's Staff[^c-318a21be8d2e10f570f74aa41462c411]

“I, A.B., do solemnly declare that I will not disclose any information received by me in carrying out the functions under Part 6 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 which I may from time to time be assigned by[^c-aa45c407bf245ca31aaee458268afa22] the National Crime Agency[^c-9d78dae7d11d1cc0d4be6f092d02ac53] to carry out except for the purposes of those functions, or to to[^c-df5a911e9bca92e03adacb692e89e496] that Agency[^c-2dfe7451bac314f3050c58a7dd828612] or in accordance with that Agency's[^c-04f5e571482b3dc64b1f70341b99520c] instructions, or for the purposes of any prosecution for an offence relating to inland revenue, or in such other cases as may be required or permitted by law.”
