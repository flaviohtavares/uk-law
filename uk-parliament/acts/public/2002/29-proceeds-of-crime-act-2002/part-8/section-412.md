# Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, section 412

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 412. Interpretation[^key-fb8d12136f9b6a4d96d0db9d779733c8]

*Extent: S*

In this Chapter, unless the context otherwise requires—

- “**appropriate person**” means—
  - (a) the procurator fiscal, in relation to a confiscation investigation or a money laundering investigation,
  - (b) the Scottish Ministers, in relation to a civil recovery investigation , a detained cash investigation, a detained property investigation[^key-188fc50c6f1ea10f08780f3cc808e48a] , a frozen funds investigation or a cryptoasset investigation[^key-466d3457f69cfcd9e64656391ce310f4];

  references to a “**constable**” include references to an officer of Revenue and Customs[^key-4dbafa4395c2b1239a20bbf7ebb1a3eb] and to an immigration officer[^key-e450d562ccf3e78e8b969e8197b44a2c];
- “**legal privilege**” means protection in legal proceedings from disclosure, by virtue of any rule of law relating to the confidentiality of communications; and “**items subject to legal privilege**” are—
  - (a) communications between a professional legal adviser and his client, or
  - (b) communications made in connection with or in contemplation of legal proceedings and for the purposes of those proceedings,

  which would be so protected.
- “**premises**” include any place and, in particular, include—
  - (a) any vehicle, vessel, aircraft or hovercraft;
  - (b) any offshore installation within the meaning of section 1 of the Mineral Workings (Offshore Installations) Act 1971 (c. 61) and any tent or movable structure;
- “**proper person**” means—
  - (a) a constable, in relation to a confiscation investigation or a money laundering investigation;
  - (b) the Scottish Ministers or a person named by them, in relation to a civil recovery investigation , a detained cash investigation, a detained property investigation , a frozen funds investigation or a cryptoasset investigation[^key-07b543891d3852ebe07a8532e3643ee8].
