## 344. Courts[^c-99ceec5ffdfd552def0594fadf7a0cca]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

In this Chapter references to the court are to—

- (a) the Crown Court, in relation to an order for the purposes of a confiscation investigation , a money laundering investigation[^c-6420b353ad6e53fa9232ad3a9f67f7b3] , a detained cash investigation, a detained property investigation[^c-4aa7677008b715d3f4ede7fa71f43bb0] , a frozen funds investigation or a cryptoasset investigation[^c-9144208631c4a0c8d1ad26ea38f3a6db];
- (b) the High Court, in relation to an order for the purposes of a civil recovery investigation or an exploitation proceeds investigation[^c-58178a39e049647c2c60437f305f546c] [^c-41199d89c63c8d2922a68c3b31120393]...
