# Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, section 382

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (210).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 382. Order to grant entry[^key-1d3fa50927dcb1047cfdc43b08f82dc6]

*Extent: S*

(1) This section applies if a sheriff makes a production order requiring a person to give a proper person access to material on any premises.

(2) The sheriff may, on an application made to him by the appropriate person and specifying the premises, make an order to grant entry in relation to the premises.

(3)[^key-df7f2f37de71c43806453514c41249ea][^key-4ea02cade7e3f889e9a33a39cfaaf43e] An order to grant entry is an order requiring any person who appears to the appropriate person to be entitled to grant entry to the premises to allow a proper person to enter the premises to obtain access to the material.
