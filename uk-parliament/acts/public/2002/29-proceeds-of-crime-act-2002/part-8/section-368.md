## 368. Disclosure of information[^key-ddb338940b33eaa6292a2a8138c14a20][^key-cc6425b13c1f148cb0c9617533faf902][^key-e5df15ff912c6f2c60d806ff071f3e65][^key-affe24dda7cc93af4c7326b489aee7fe][^key-224d39d6acdb0e77e1e1ddccb8d1cc59]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

A customer information order has effect in spite of any restriction on the disclosure of information (however imposed).
