# Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, section 343

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (210).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/29

## 343. Judges[^key-d241098ea0b82fe421aaa3228f253dd6]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) In this Chapter references to a judge in relation to an application must be construed in accordance with this section.

(2) In relation to an application for the purposes of a confiscation investigation , a money laundering investigation[^key-a34fbf6face7de89ba94bf72c94417c7] , a detained cash investigation, a detained property investigation[^key-981573b5214111549be134917368f7e8] , a frozen funds investigation or a cryptoasset investigation[^key-daffb73103c47213e4364f8abad0b146] a judge is—

- (a) in England and Wales, a judge entitled to exercise the jurisdiction of the Crown Court;
- (b) in Northern Ireland, a Crown Court judge.

(3) In relation to an application for the purposes of a civil recovery investigation or an exploitation proceeds investigation[^key-de7af009289c12dad8e87560f8440a0e] [^key-33c7b519e5655cc10f15321e0444472a]... a judge is a judge of the High Court.
