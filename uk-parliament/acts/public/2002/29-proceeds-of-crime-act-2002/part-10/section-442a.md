## 442A. Data protection legislation[^c-769ac48ff476b765dac2237d33bf18ef]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part, “**the data protection legislation**” has the same meaning as in the Data Protection Act 2018 (see section 3 of that Act).
