# Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, section 2C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (210).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2C. Prosecuting authorities[^key-41de613dbf3e71ac890381ad679a56f8]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Anything which the Director of Public Prosecutions is authorised or required to do under, or in relation to, Part 5 or 8 of this Act may be done by a member of his staff if the member of staff is authorised by the Director (generally or specifically) for that purpose.

(2) Anything which [^key-3203ebf4337bc6dfb1821589eb8807b9]... the Director of the Serious Fraud Office is authorised or required to do under, or in relation to, Part 2, 4,[^key-aedeffffc2e5455579e7e20a6957eddd] 5, 7[^key-e02eeaa371900efb1a53311632c9f3d9] or 8 of this Act may be done by a member of his staff if the member of staff is authorised by the Director [^key-49a5f222dd9938a511fefe66cdf5dc53]... (generally or specifically) for that purpose.

(3) Anything which a relevant Director or a member of his staff is authorised or required to do under, or in relation to, Part 2, 4,[^key-879049d7a71ad912e6d6ecda493f9b1f] 5 or 8 of this Act may be done by a person providing services under arrangements made by the relevant Director if the person is authorised by the relevant Director (whether generally or specifically) for that purpose.

(3A)[^key-5b1a4d13cd0c8003866f1c47fbd36aa4] Subsection (3) does not apply to the functions of the Director of Public Prosecutions for Northern Ireland [^key-53b10cde78f3cefa118e47f36e75c00f]... under section 302A , 303X[^key-17c238d8615c33759ac9838273e56573] , 303Z19, 303Z53 or 303Z65[^key-d0095ee65bfae7caf09762fbbb5e9bb0].

(4) In this section “**relevant Director**” means—

- (a) the Director of Public Prosecutions,
- (b) the Director of Public Prosecutions for Northern Ireland,
- (c)[^key-4b5564a77d2673b451cc44d44c744956] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (d) the Director of the Serious Fraud Office.
