## SCHEDULE 7 — Powers of trustee for civil recovery

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Section 267

### 1. Sale[^key-4e6ef575ce38c73985af048cf6478a8c]

Power to sell the property or any part of it or interest in it.

### 2. Expenditure[^key-03c07c66503da8072a206323670b1831]

Power to incur expenditure for the purpose of—

- (a) acquiring any part of the property, or any interest in it, which is not vested in him,
- (b) discharging any liabilities, or extinguishing any rights, to which the property is subject.

### 3. Management[^key-b80d102d3a6687cdffcb291ae5547306]

(1) Power to manage property.

(2) Managing property includes doing anything mentioned in paragraph 5(2) of Schedule 6.

### 4. Legal proceedings[^key-d975670a106d177192ed3002280836ca]

Power to start, carry on or defend any legal proceedings in respect of the property.

### 5. Compromise[^key-1ff89832ef1f229986e757884093dad0]

Power to make any compromise or other arrangement in connection with any claim relating to the property.

### 6. Supplementary[^key-3300019fbe03732005dfc887b9075ede]

(1) For the purposes of, or in connection with, the exercise of any of his powers—

- (a) power by his official name to do any of the things mentioned in sub-paragraph (2),
- (b) power to do any other act which is necessary or expedient.

(2) Those things are—

- (a) holding property,
- (b) entering into contracts,
- (c) suing and being sued,
- (d) employing agents,
- (e) executing a power of attorney, deed or other instrument.
