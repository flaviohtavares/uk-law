## 214. Appeal to Supreme Court[^c-e15640892bf6e92830c12f776c7d920e][^c-e3634034bab4be7cd4cb838ceea63874][^c-e3634034bab4be7cd4cb838ceea63874][^c-71c4e92234a9fe72e9a83cb0ced9f631]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An appeal lies to the Supreme Court[^c-80c6a364fdc4a643effe91d381ace7a7] from a decision of the Court of Appeal on an appeal under section 213.

(2) An appeal under this section lies at the instance of any person who was a party to the proceedings before the Court of Appeal.

(3) On an appeal under this section the Supreme Court[^c-3e87889516a2ac6caee037d330a43831] may—

- (a) confirm the decision of the Court of Appeal, or
- (b) make such order as it believes is appropriate.
