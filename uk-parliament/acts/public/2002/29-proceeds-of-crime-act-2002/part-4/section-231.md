# Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, section 231

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (210).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/29

## 231. Realisable property[^key-e97d7ebcaada2ae026947c467df4eab3][^key-e3634034bab4be7cd4cb838ceea63874][^key-e3634034bab4be7cd4cb838ceea63874]

*Extent: N.I.*

Realisable property is—

- (a) any free property held by the defendant;
- (b) any free property held by the recipient of a tainted gift.
