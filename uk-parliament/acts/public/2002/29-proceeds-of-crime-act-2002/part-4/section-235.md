## 235. Confiscation orders[^c-9a2e5d4a596ec7e15e213c23375b7c20][^c-e3634034bab4be7cd4cb838ceea63874][^c-e3634034bab4be7cd4cb838ceea63874]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) A confiscation order is satisfied when no amount is due under it.

(1A)[^c-397b8a4a6ef5729231210e71b497870a] The “amount payable” under a confiscation order, where part of that amount has been paid, means the amount that remains to be paid.

(2) A confiscation order is subject to appeal until there is no further possibility of an appeal on which the order could be varied or quashed[^c-0e873e3d40a2faaf07870a179e392c07]....
