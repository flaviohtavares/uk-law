# Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, section 176

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (210).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 176. Information[^key-cdd45b230f958e98534fb8ed237533e0][^key-e3634034bab4be7cd4cb838ceea63874][^key-e3634034bab4be7cd4cb838ceea63874]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) the court proceeds under section 156 in pursuance of section 169 or 170, or
- (b) the prosecutor [^key-27e5c87fdcaeeae204f0b49cb7f802f7]... applies under section 171.

(2) In such a case—

- (a) the prosecutor [^key-60902f816bd5d568e71befaf7b0fd1e7]... must give the court a statement of information within the period the court orders;
- (b) section 166 applies accordingly (with appropriate modifications where the prosecutor [^key-f17b929289ecda7c6242552dfb8a8e0b]... applies under section 171);
- (c) section 167 applies accordingly;
- (d) section 168 applies as it applies in the circumstances mentioned in section 168(1).
