## 193. Appeal to Supreme Court[^key-4edde4bff9dcc8541cf57fab2b897878][^key-e3634034bab4be7cd4cb838ceea63874][^key-e6a2365e6b28fd124a5831264291e81f][^key-e3634034bab4be7cd4cb838ceea63874][^key-f58cae3b41b4899c14252c3937e8331e]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An appeal lies to the Supreme Court[^key-c24c072818929148777e6cccd00cd2a0] from a decision of the Court of Appeal on an appeal under section 192.

(2) An appeal under this section lies at the instance of any person who was a party to the proceedings before the Court of Appeal.

(3) On an appeal under this section the Supreme Court[^key-2c43ae8a6552e66d0a70c0771ff9f08f] may—

- (a) confirm the decision of the Court of Appeal, or
- (b) make such order as it believes is appropriate.
