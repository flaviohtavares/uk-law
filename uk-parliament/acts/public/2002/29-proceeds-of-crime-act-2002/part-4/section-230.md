# Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, section 230

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (210).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/29

## 230. Free property[^c-e5a2254167f99089879d3e639d989e3f][^c-e3634034bab4be7cd4cb838ceea63874][^c-e3634034bab4be7cd4cb838ceea63874]

*Extent: N.I.*

(1)[^c-9c5ba17d0e72e21c45cb6bacc83338d7] Property is free unless it falls within subsection (2) or (3).

(2)[^c-ef1bb895485cc95a6d0c2cbb1ab1733f] Property falls within this subsection if[^c-a2c26b9a1a8327858a5bb951c732f2ac] an order is in force in respect of it under any of these provisions—

- (a) section 27 of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 (c. 38) (forfeiture orders);
- (b) Article 11 of the Criminal Justice (Northern Ireland) Order 1994 (S.I. 1994/2795 (N.I. 15)) (deprivation orders);
- (c) Part 2 of the Proceeds of Crime (Scotland) Act 1995 (c. 43) (forfeiture of property used in crime);
- (d) section 143 of the Powers of Criminal Courts (Sentencing) Act 2000 (c. 6) or Chapter 4 of Part 7 of the Sentencing Code[^c-93342682320150a2e5001259faefb059] (deprivation orders);
- (e) section 23, 23A[^c-bd811d825c8c43ac4c86864ebaf8af9e] or 111 of the Terrorism Act 2000 (c. 11) (forfeiture orders);
- (ea)[^c-4a293f1ae292e06b9424dfa81a682130] paragraph 3(2), 6(2), 10D(1), 10G(2), 10J(3), 10S(2) , 10Z2(3), 10Z7AG(1), 10Z7BB(2), 10Z7CA(3), 10Z7CE(3) or 10Z7DG(3)[^c-e435aa87d1e1e9b6c5d5a1fc645f74a4] of Schedule 1 to the Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001;
- (f) section 245A,[^c-019f9911f2f1cf9caf9e3cfc8d3c3796] 246, 255A, 256,[^c-dbe6eff19374a542f958dee70f876feb] 266, 295(2) , 298(2), 303L(1), 303O(3), 303R(3), 303Z3[^c-412013496039f2b8fc4b94a0d2b382d7] , 303Z14(4), 303Z32(1), 303Z37(2), 303Z41(4), 303Z45(3) or 303Z60(4)[^c-4e15be27605db51ef0720226d8b64dbb] of this Act.

(3)[^c-2ad5eb884498f1556e39245acdd4023a] Property falls within this subsection if—

- (a) it has been forfeited in pursuance of a forfeiture notice under section 297A or an account forfeiture notice under section 303Z9[^c-f18b545db1bcf11fab34e18d21a24575];
- (b) it is detained under section 297C , 297D[^c-415df83ee3d414e5f6dea107e64ea378] , 298(4) or 303O(9)[^c-c6f79cfaa48a86aec68f013ff6822d21].
- (ba)[^c-be586ebb1891f6f502b745693b5c7a38] it is detained under section 303Z30, 303Z31 or 303Z32 in a case where section 303Z42(2) applies;
- (bb) it is detained under section 303Z57 or 303Z58 in a case where section 303Z60(6) applies;
- (c)[^c-da8694d4e54c04eef986bd3891c154e1] it is the forfeitable property in relation to an order under section 303Q(1) or 303Z44(1)[^c-ff0315b18f23a0f4df818525d7f0ce27].
- (d)[^c-cd2a8d088d54c96223b51cb12d1d987a] it has been forfeited in pursuance of a cash forfeiture notice under paragraph 5A of Schedule 1 to the Anti-terrorism, Crime and Security Act 2001 or an account forfeiture notice under paragraph 10W of that Schedule;
- (e) it is detained under paragraph 5B, 5C, 9A or 10G(7) of that Schedule;
- (ea)[^c-d31735cb86354bbb8b8a901988f9a0f8] it is detained under paragraph 10Z7AE, 10Z7AF or 10Z7AG of that Schedule in a case where paragraph 10Z7CB(2) of that Schedule applies;
- (eb) it is detained under paragraph 10Z7DD or 10Z7DE of that Schedule in a case where paragraph 10Z7DG(5) of that Schedule applies;
- (f) it is the forfeitable property in relation to an order under paragraph 10I(1) or 10Z7CD(1)[^c-142ee7823c19e98db07677ae203bd23d] of that Schedule.
