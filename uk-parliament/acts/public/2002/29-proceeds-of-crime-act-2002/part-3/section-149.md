## 149. Realisable property[^c-ea40bf60a4546ee5ec8d8f0927a430ff][^c-d59f6c2a9c42161ca1815c0c61d9e1f6][^c-d59f6c2a9c42161ca1815c0c61d9e1f6]

*Extent: S*

Realisable property is—

- (a) any free property held by the accused;
- (b) any free property held by the recipient of a tainted gift.
