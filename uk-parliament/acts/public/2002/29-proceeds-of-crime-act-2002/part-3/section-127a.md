## 127A. Sections 127B to 127R: meaning of “appropriate officer”[^key-5f055d61f195772ac525359146e8e002]

*Extent: S*

In sections 127B to 127R “**appropriate officer**” means—

- (a) an officer of Revenue and Customs, or
- (aa)[^key-0c2d6f5f4249e272f726ef074fcd66c1] an immigration officer, or
- (b) a constable.
