## 445. External investigations[^c-6e8cc4f2ff75bfd5bc7a5e8915b46965]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Her Majesty may by Order in Council make—

- (a) provision to enable orders equivalent to those under Part 8 to be made, and warrants equivalent to those under Part 8 to be issued, for the purposes of an external investigation;
- (b) provision creating offences in relation to external investigations which are equivalent to offences created by Part 8 and section 453B[^c-5b75ad946e25ca9f2d9d0248e7a2cae1].

(2) An Order under this section may include—

- (a) provision corresponding to any provision of Part 8 (subject to any specified modifications);
- (b) provision about the functions of the Secretary of State, the Lord Advocate, the Scottish Ministers, the Department of Justice in Northern Ireland,[^c-601caa419a857b8d159392a799c91e1a] the National Crime Agency or its officers[^c-671dd796b4dd6107f913b5c61778eb0c][^c-9436d3b50fbde5091f6c6f13c49ec795], the Director of Public Prosecutions, the Director of Public Prosecutions for Northern Ireland, [^c-f36ecad299295d7fff037807e23cd73d]... the Director of the Serious Fraud Office, constables and customs officers;
- (c) provision about evidence (including evidence required to establish whether an investigation is being carried out in a country or territory outside the United Kingdom).
