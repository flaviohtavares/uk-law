# Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, section 303Z6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (210).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/29

## 303Z6. Restriction on proceedings and remedies[^key-8e2605df0368107104fe07adfca57959][^key-45f2d136e27084069f284536f0f7d825]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a court in which proceedings are pending in respect of an account maintained with a relevant financial institution[^key-ff2e75e4533722637d01064d3cc57512] is satisfied that an account freezing order has been applied for or made in respect of the account, it may either stay the proceedings or allow them to continue on any terms it thinks fit.

(2) Before exercising the power conferred by subsection (1), the court must (as well as giving the parties to any of the proceedings concerned an opportunity to be heard) give such an opportunity to any person who may be affected by the court's decision.

(3) In relation to Scotland, the reference in subsection (1) to staying the proceedings is to be read as a reference to sisting the proceedings.
