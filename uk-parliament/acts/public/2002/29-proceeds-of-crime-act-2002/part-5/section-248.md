## 248. Registration[^c-0e5d040d38daf6182a824c84df08d030]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The registration Acts—

- (a) apply in relation to property freezing orders, and in relation to interim receiving orders,[^c-d3c43bd7fe61ff56f16ecdcbae26de2b] as they apply in relation to orders which affect land and are made by the court for the purpose of enforcing judgements or recognisances,
- (b) apply in relation to applications for property freezing orders, and in relation to applications for interim receiving orders,[^c-86d0f5cd479ada417ce85891c21111d0] as they apply in relation to other pending land actions.

(2) The registration Acts are—

- (a) *(not yet in force: the Land Registration Act 1925 (c. 21),)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c4079441]
- (b) the Land Charges Act 1972 (c. 61), and
- (c) the Land Registration Act 2002 (c. 9).

(3) But no notice may be entered in the register of title under the Land Registration Act 2002 in respect of a property freezing order or[^c-c3ee0f2e002b8fa05473c8b1c7599aea] an interim receiving order.

(4) *(not yet in force: A person applying for an interim receiving order must be treated for the purposes of section 57 of the Land Registration Act 1925 (inhibitions) as a person interested in relation to any registered land to which—)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c4079461]

- ((not yet in force: a) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c4079461] *(not yet in force: the application relates, or)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c4079461]
- ((not yet in force: b) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c4079461] *(not yet in force: an interim receiving order made in pursuance of the application relates.)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c4079461]
