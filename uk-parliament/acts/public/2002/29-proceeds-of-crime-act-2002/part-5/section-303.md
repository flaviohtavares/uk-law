# Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, section 303

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (210).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 303. “The minimum amount”[^c-0cb0c3fea107fb3291974bcb7f3a0d0e][^c-a04f6cc12aec5c0ed823eb227184f5cd]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Chapter, the minimum amount is the amount in sterling specified in an order made by the Secretary of State after consultation with the Scottish Ministers and the Department of Justice[^c-c3816a5416ae07c0d9e1b95b82eb5275] .

(2) For that purpose the amount of any cash held in a currency other than sterling must be taken to be its sterling equivalent, calculated in accordance with the prevailing rate of exchange.
