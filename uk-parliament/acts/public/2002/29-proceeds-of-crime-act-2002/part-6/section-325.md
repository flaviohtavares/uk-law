(2)[^c-fd941471178953ae0afdc68fcc28c5cb] Every National Crime Agency officer[^c-07459dffdc0194fa49ac3280cd074cca] who is assigned to carry out any function of the National Crime Agency[^c-bc8e5b6fb5e9f4647b0251ade6f7681e] under this Part must, as soon as practicable after being so assigned, make a declaration in the form set out in Schedule 8 before a person nominated by the Director General of the National Crime Agency[^c-5a30e3274fc3b961fd5993004ba9c167] for the purpose.
