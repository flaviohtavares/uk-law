## 90. Procedure on appeal to the Supreme Court[^c4026071][^key-b3661b05d1dc275d6eab94c925a24976][^key-a3889e161e736d1b5e0be99edde6119b][^key-bc59da76f74a5e9debfbda22fd326c18][^key-a3889e161e736d1b5e0be99edde6119b][^key-bc59da76f74a5e9debfbda22fd326c18][^key-0caf86402feb792485a94727bf8fd1a9]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Section 33(3) of the Criminal Appeal Act 1968 (limitation on appeal from criminal division of the Court of Appeal) does not prevent an appeal to the Supreme Court[^key-4a93ae1d9437ac18a2a8df17544fd3ff] under this Part.

(2) In relation to appeals to the Supreme Court under this Part, the Secretary of State may make an order containing provision corresponding to any provision in the Criminal Appeal Act 1968 (subject to any specified modifications).
