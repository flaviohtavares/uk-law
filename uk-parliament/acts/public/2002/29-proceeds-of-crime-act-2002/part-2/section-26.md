# Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (210).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 26. Information[^c-af8b7c91c906872ffd63b9614320e803][^c-a3889e161e736d1b5e0be99edde6119b][^c-bc59da76f74a5e9debfbda22fd326c18][^c-a3889e161e736d1b5e0be99edde6119b][^c-bc59da76f74a5e9debfbda22fd326c18]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies if—

- (a) the court proceeds under section 6 in pursuance of section 19 or 20, or
- (b) the prosecutor [^c-a179b760af5d13cc165ec8ac728dc69d]... applies under section 21.

(2) In such a case—

- (a) the prosecutor [^c-fa81104780a47768acc968ffdde2f4fe]... must give the court a statement of information within the period the court orders;
- (b) section 16 applies accordingly (with appropriate modifications where the prosecutor [^c-24efb4b16052e3c39c8e7046d779fd16]... applies under section 21);
- (c) section 17 applies accordingly;
- (d) section 18 applies as it applies in the circumstances mentioned in section 18(1).
