# Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, section 44A

## 44A. Detention of property pending appeal[^key-49d305b764d03bf083b20e241cb3d5a3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) This section applies where—

- (a) a restraint order includes provision under section 41A authorising the detention of property, and
- (b) the restraint order is discharged under section 42(5) or 67J(3)(b)[^key-12ec20dffcc15b7573ee87c1e75cae5d].

(2) This section also applies where—

- (a) a restraint order includes provision under section 41A authorising the detention of property, and
- (b) the restraint order is varied under section 42(5) or 67J(3)(b)[^key-98576c45ed578af1b88b9ac3f84ffd0a] so as to omit any such provision.

(3) The property may be detained until there is no further possibility of an appeal against—

- (a) the decision to discharge or vary the restraint order, or
- (b) any decision made on an appeal against that decision.
