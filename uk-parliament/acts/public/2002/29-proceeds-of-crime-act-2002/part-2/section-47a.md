## 47A. Sections 47B to 47S: meaning of “appropriate officer”[^c-aba2a29eed054d2a12dfdb2a9ad47eaa]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) In sections 47B to 47S “**appropriate officer**” means—

- (a) an officer of Revenue and Customs,
- (aa)[^c-454c1874e4961a10af655c20a8d3741e] an immigration officer;
- (b) a constable, [^M_F_752e3fb5-e526-4766-f1e1-ff358ae81a6f]...
- (ba)[^M_F_ceaf3301-7343-44cf-8ad8-14c71a7c9294] an SFO officer, or
- (c)[^c-18e48475d7e7aaefa403f6bbba9e6475] an accredited financial investigator.

(2) In subsection (1)(c) the reference to an accredited financial investigator is a reference to an accredited financial investigator who falls within a description specified in an order made for the purposes of that provision by the Secretary of State or the Welsh Ministers[^c-9b374d1fb2a4dbb5b495643ea9d2f2a2] under section 453.
