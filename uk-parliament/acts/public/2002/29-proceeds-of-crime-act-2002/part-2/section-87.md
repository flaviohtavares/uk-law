## 87. Confiscation orders[^c-7963b7a42bc699987608b91f8d5d3999][^c-a3889e161e736d1b5e0be99edde6119b][^c-bc59da76f74a5e9debfbda22fd326c18][^c-a3889e161e736d1b5e0be99edde6119b][^c-bc59da76f74a5e9debfbda22fd326c18][^c-8b3589310fa0371c2adc8c407d26c7d3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A confiscation order is satisfied when no amount is due under it.

(1A)[^c-d6ad989ae49b3a302c7dcb4346477202] The “amount payable” under a confiscation order, where part of that amount has been paid, means the amount that remains to be paid.

(2) A confiscation order is subject to appeal until there is no further possibility of an appeal on which the order could be varied or quashed[^c-928a6959088f9821608ad8552ea01720]....
