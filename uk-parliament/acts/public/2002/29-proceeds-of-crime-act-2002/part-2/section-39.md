## 39. Reconsideration etc: variation of prison term[^key-53e3e78f15547315eefa11504b744ea4][^key-a3889e161e736d1b5e0be99edde6119b][^key-bc59da76f74a5e9debfbda22fd326c18][^key-3d7ba393ae21e97ddae20aa0045f32a9][^key-dca43db4b548dd0ba5e4d68a9d8ddda8][^key-cdc38fc7a7f8860afeb2cd1c2587b49c][^key-a3889e161e736d1b5e0be99edde6119b][^key-bc59da76f74a5e9debfbda22fd326c18][^key-3d7ba393ae21e97ddae20aa0045f32a9][^key-cdc38fc7a7f8860afeb2cd1c2587b49c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Subsection (2) applies if—

- (a) a court varies a confiscation order under section 21, 21A,[^key-433f481710589294f9e18f1d2f764045] 22, 23, 29, 32 or 33,
- (b) the effect of the variation is to vary the maximum period applicable in relation to the order under section 35(2A)[^key-b4e066c675f8a9d9b27c64072268b86b], and
- (c) the result is that that maximum period is less than the term of imprisonment or detention fixed in respect of the order under section 129(3) of the Sentencing Code[^key-9947c401b981cf8aaf7c145561ebd523].

(2) In such a case the court must fix a reduced term of imprisonment or detention in respect of the confiscation order under section 129(3) of the Sentencing Code[^key-ded722c04d904120b3048dc6aad433c0] in place of the term previously fixed.

(3) Subsection (4) applies if paragraphs (a) and (b) of subsection (1) apply but paragraph (c) does not.

(4) In such a case the court may amend the term of imprisonment or detention fixed in respect of the confiscation order under section 129(3) of the Sentencing Code[^key-e1bf3421d00155a6122bc0cde28c3eed].

(5) If the effect of section 12 is to increase the maximum period applicable in relation to a confiscation order under section 35(2A)[^key-4b0863a86907de78358277230c5c6c16], on the application of the prosecutor[^key-c57b3826d9d379c6bcf5a14872adc5a9] the Crown Court may amend the term of imprisonment or detention fixed in respect of the order under section 129(3) of that Code[^key-04d9d4293f8eef382d627f89938644ae].
