# Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, section 91

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (210).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 91. Crown Court Rules[^c-79f5b5fec1689b7fd1ee5637e4a0eb33][^c-a3889e161e736d1b5e0be99edde6119b][^c-bc59da76f74a5e9debfbda22fd326c18][^c-a3889e161e736d1b5e0be99edde6119b][^c-bc59da76f74a5e9debfbda22fd326c18]

*Extent: E+W*

In relation to—

- (a) proceedings under this Part, or
- (b) receivers appointed under this Part,

Criminal Procedure Rules[^c-f8800ebf6ea640e1a2a46657ee6c0d53] may make provision corresponding to provision in Civil Procedure Rules.
