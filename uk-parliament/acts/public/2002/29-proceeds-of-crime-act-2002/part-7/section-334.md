# Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, section 334

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (210).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 334. Penalties[^c-ec7917786f6d06614df3b73a3e49b997][^c-ab8d591eb66fe915fb3e58427c0f1106][^c-ab8d591eb66fe915fb3e58427c0f1106]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person guilty of an offence under section 327, 328 or 329 is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to both, or
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 14 years or to a fine or to both.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under section 330, 331 or 332[^c-2e1ab56673c9cb7604af2366ca533e44] is liable—

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum or to both, or
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years or to a fine or to both.

(3)[^c-b04cff7c0cb842e7fe306872b2033758] A person guilty of an offence under section 339(1A) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale.
