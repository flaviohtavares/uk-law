# Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, section 339ZA

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (210).** Text as at 2026-06-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 339ZA. Disclosures to the NCA[^key-ffe0a99d4f2135c420a619264cd343a3][^M_F_571f8d09-50d4-4d13-ba5c-258e979bf803]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a disclosure is made under this Part to a constable or an officer of Revenue and Customs, the constable or officer of Revenue and Customs must disclose it in full to a person authorised for the purposes of this Part by the Director General of the National Crime Agency[^M_F_1f26efbe-08ea-4a13-faa6-8250f8538c54] as soon as practicable after it has been made.
