> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/30

## 10B. Efficiency etc in exercise of functions[^key-a3766445b343fede2037ab38fa79138c][^key-3f9f833322cb9779a260109b1f2b5180]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Director General and the Office must carry out their functions efficiently and effectively.
