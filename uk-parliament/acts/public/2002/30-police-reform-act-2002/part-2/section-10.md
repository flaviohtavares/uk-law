## 10. General functions of the Director General[^c20012591][^c-5c0b94dcbde88c0cd695dd61fa219c45][^c-3f9f833322cb9779a260109b1f2b5180][^c-74d3d239053b41265f11a373f2e8975f][^c-9c61c39d9a4b9099ed4348b90e864724][^c-206aa61bd2128d47e3606f31adc531cc][^c-4b03a062a205195f97b11eb247ede23c][^c-0d7809a5d1b87706d92d3c4895677eb7][^c-d4f5700932cf4b85bf6dd56c7aab7966]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The functions of the Director General shall be—

- (a) to secure the maintenance by the Director General [^c-883a6881c81c777f35353d1f7587940d]..., and by local policing bodies[^c-fbcb3dd3752502560aa8327fa8b0bf69] and chief officers, of suitable arrangements with respect to the matters mentioned in subsection (2);
- (b) to keep under review all arrangements maintained with respect to those matters;
- (c) to secure that arrangements maintained with respect to those matters comply with the requirements of the following provisions of this Part, are efficient and effective and contain and manifest an appropriate degree of independence;
- (d) to secure that public confidence is established and maintained in the existence of suitable arrangements with respect to those matters and with the operation of the arrangements that are in fact maintained with respect to those matters;
- (e) to make such recommendations, and to give such advice, for the modification of the arrangements maintained with respect to those matters, and also of police practice in relation to other matters, as appear, from the carrying out by the Director General of the Director General's[^c-d446da07aa57815caa95328614c9d3c8] other functions, to be necessary or desirable; [^c20028611]...
- (f) to such extent as the Director General[^c-e23d987cde6c5e8201f1395cdcea6725] may be required to do so by regulations made by the Secretary of State, to carry out functions in relation to [^c20028621]... bodies of constables maintained otherwise than by local policing bodies[^c-a1e31f5451acb259a5375465fb1c0ff1] which broadly correspond to those conferred on the Director General in relation to police forces by the preceding paragraphs of this subsection;[^c20028541] [^c20057751]...
- (g) to carry out functions in relation to the National Crime Agency[^c-b4293eaafdf5eb5d2281b74cc970342a] which correspond to those conferred on the Director General in relation to police forces by paragraph (e) of this subsection ; and[^c20031531]
- (ga)[^c-75fc4651852804f8880ca4a0991f9a6b] to carry out such corresponding functions in relation to enforcement officers (within the meaning of Part 5 of the Employment Rights Act 2025) acting in the exercise of functions conferred on them by virtue of section 114B of the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984;
- (gb) to carry out such corresponding functions in relation to officers of the Food Standards Agency acting in the exercise of functions conferred on them by virtue of—
  - (i) section 114C of the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (PACE powers for food crime officers), or
  - (ii) section 39A of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994 (powers for food crime officers: inferences from silence) , and[^c-acbbb51522ca06a545be9d38208ab5a7]
- (gc) to carry out such corresponding functions in relation to public sector fraud investigators acting in the exercise of functions conferred on them by section 7 of the Public Authorities (Fraud, Error and Recovery) Act 2025 (Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 powers for public sector fraud investigators) ; and[^c-f4e8e6cb9e6b2dfcb17f4eb5c88a9625]
- (gd) to carry out such corresponding functions in relation to DWP investigators acting in the exercise of functions conferred on them by section 109D of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 (Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 powers for DWP investigators).
- (h)[^c-f99a318f9e341f05ffa69b3458f0d4e3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Those matters are—

- (a) the handling of complaints (within the meaning given by section 12)[^c-ec989e7047ed0631233f61ef3bf2ea6e] or other concerns raised by virtue of Part 2B (whistle-blowing)[^c-d36c290cab3de6ff6b653be9a958ca9f];
- (b) the recording of matters from which it appears that there may have been conduct by persons serving with the police[^c-8bc944e1329b661600b94c149285194a] which constitutes or involves the commission of a criminal offence or behaviour justifying disciplinary proceedings;
- (ba)[^c19975981] the recording of matters from which it appears that a person has died or suffered serious injury during, or following, contact with a person serving with the police;
- (c) the manner in which any such complaints or other concerns[^c-dcd453da081c096478fb48a8be6c3c59] or any such matters as are mentioned in paragraph (b) or (ba)[^c19976021] are investigated or otherwise handled and dealt with.

(3) The Director General shall also have the functions which are conferred on the Director General[^c-3a0330ebfe5b1668e89c097e82b17f3c] by—

- (a)[^c20028631] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) any agreement or order under section 26 of this Act (other bodies of constables);
- (bc)[^c-ca3de345744682166a4faa36727d2ba9] any regulations under section 26C of this Act (the National Crime Agency);
- (bca)[^c-8fa1fb670cccd587a412ac76f282a105] any regulations under section 26CA of this Act (enforcement officers appointed under Employment Rights Act 2025);
- (bd)[^c-9f8d94adce9ea1428041b867f2cc1fdb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (be)[^c-ffa21bef123d03796dbfea25111515f5] any regulations under section 26E of this Act (food crime officers);
- (bf)[^c-6d0e8ee99c871954244484852fe28022] any regulations under section 26G of this Act (public sector fraud investigators);
- (bg)[^c-30ff9fd6b04bc7efe2dab21c171aad4d] any regulations under section 26H of this Act (DWP investigators);
- (c) any regulations under section 39 of this Act (police powers for contracted-out staff); or
- (d) any regulations or arrangements relating to disciplinary or similar proceedings against persons serving with the police, or against members of [^c20028641]... any body of constables maintained otherwise than by a local policing body[^c-da1d9864cd3b3e3949f3d70447bc2748].

(3A)[^c-21c1e333178c32a4d8ff7bf32418a465] The Director General also has any functions conferred on the Director General[^c-9bcc33d14a2371d98eb175add1599581] by regulations under section 29C of this Act (regulations about super-complaints).

(3B)[^c-4a4f4f756082fe1e095d090c62859048] The Director General also has the functions which are conferred on the Director General[^c-ff4fe32baabea97746269a8a5751e49f] by Part 2B (whistle-blowing).

(4) It shall be the duty of the Director General—

- (a) to exercise the powers and perform the duties conferred on the Director General[^c-a42003d66ed2917a1af4d0ce110efa7a] by the following provisions of this Part in the manner that the Director General considers best calculated for the purpose of securing the proper carrying out of the Director General's[^c-6a8cb2a275a34800bc8baedbdf6d15bb] functions under subsections (1) and (3); and
- (b) to secure that arrangements exist which are conducive to, and facilitate, the reporting of misconduct by persons in relation to whose conduct the Director General has functions.

(5) It shall also be the duty of the Director General—

- (a) to enter into arrangements with the chief inspector of constabulary for the purpose of securing co-operation, in the carrying out of their respective functions, between the Director General and the inspectors of constabulary; and
- (b) to provide those inspectors with all such assistance and co-operation as may be required by those arrangements, or as otherwise appears to the Director General to be appropriate, for facilitating the carrying out by those inspectors of their functions.

(5A)[^c-2538884a24b0158711054baf05c0555c] In carrying out functions the Director General must have regard to any advice provided to the Director General by the Office (see section 10A(1)(c)).

(6) Subject to the other provisions of this Part, the Director General may do anything which appears to the Director General[^c-6e0ccb9009c9caa8f84c65a179a61bfe] to be calculated to facilitate, or is incidental or conducive to, the carrying out of the Director General's[^c-c846bc5b58ddbe6e236176716913054c] functions.

(7)[^c-9e3affe2fce2124fdbd7882b545644a0] The Office[^c-029a297e467af1212d2d958a55b16b1f] may, in connection with the making of any recommendation or the giving of any advice to any person for the purpose of carrying out—

- (a) the Director General's[^c-55004c216443e9b4e3e0720864c89ab0] function under subsection (1)(e), [^c20028651]...
- (b) any corresponding function conferred on the Director General[^c-743de878a2ab3df5fd3bf7e8a2018e9b] by virtue of subsection (1)(f), or[^c20028581]
- (c) the Director General's function under subsection (1)(g) or (h)[^c20031601],

impose any such charge on that person for anything done by the Director General for the purposes of, or in connection with, the carrying out of that function as the Director General thinks fit.

(7A)[^c-66144991030f637f8a1423b5db7113e2] In this section, “**public sector fraud investigators**” means authorised investigators within the meaning given by section 7 of the Public Authorities (Fraud, Error and Recovery) Act 2025.

(7B)[^c-6cb931d1e6d82b21250a1e64e92ce1b0] In this section, “**DWP investigators**” means authorised investigators within the meaning given by section 109D of the Social Security Administration Act 1992.
