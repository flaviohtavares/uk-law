## 24. Consultation on regulations[^c2342771][^c20012731][^c21090721][^c21093141][^key-41645fe2c3bc1d5ab7350400656325af][^key-245217763d6641ca39b6a5c3605aad2e][^key-dfc7b118994de4f4c98717f0f69aa528][^key-c06e700a1a4a158452d42df75404c859][^key-3f9f833322cb9779a260109b1f2b5180]

*Extent: E+W*

Before making any regulations under this Part, the Secretary of State shall consult with—

- (a)[^key-60c5bab50d1938ab67c0f175d3c8e206] the Office;
- (aa) the Director General;
- (b)[^key-8ae83ebc83ad91e710e4992fba985d98][^c20032051] such persons as appear to the Secretary of State to represent the views of police and crime commissioners;
- (ba) the Mayor's Office for Policing and Crime;
- (bb) the Common Council;
- (c) the National Police Chiefs' Council[^key-80d4a658bb82352abac20acc39af127c]; and
- (d) such other persons as he thinks fit.
