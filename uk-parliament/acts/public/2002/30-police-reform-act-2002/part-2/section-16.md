## 16. Payment for assistance with investigations[^c20012651][^key-538d3c9c69a323365abec23daea3fe19][^key-6509241667c96cee7db799f0c028e3af][^key-74d3d239053b41265f11a373f2e8975f][^key-9c61c39d9a4b9099ed4348b90e864724][^key-206aa61bd2128d47e3606f31adc531cc][^key-4b03a062a205195f97b11eb247ede23c][^key-0d7809a5d1b87706d92d3c4895677eb7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where—

- (a) one police force is required to provide assistance to another in connection with an investigation under this Part; or
- (b) a police force is required to provide assistance to the[^key-a030c8e6bbb1c2c6d476be0915ff4812] Director General[^key-f1e42f82e35eab5132920867fa277e3c] in connection with an investigation under this Part or a review under paragraph 25 of Schedule 3.

(2) For the purposes of this section—

- (a) assistance is required to be provided by one police force to another in connection with an investigation under this Part if the chief officer of the first force (“**the assisting force**”) complies with a requirement under section 15 (3) or (5) that is made in connection with
  - (ai)[^key-e8292ecfa8c2c24c4b8806c353c01623] an investigation of a complaint where the complainant expressed dissatisfaction with the other force,
  - (i)[^c19976131] an investigation of a recordable conduct matter[^key-493e2c3cc7dfebac04a999319a0c441c] relating to the conduct of a person who, at the time of the conduct, was a member of the other force, or
  - (ii) an investigation of a DSI matter in relation to which the relevant officer was, at the time of the death or serious injury, a member of the other force; and
- (b) assistance is required to be provided by a police force (“the assisting force”) to the[^key-4424ec9c19ae1ddd1d0214c7c155ec02] Director General in connection with an investigation under this Part or a review under paragraph 25 of Schedule 3 if the chief officer of that force complies with a requirement under section 15(4) that is made in connection with
  - (ai)[^key-71341ff9e87b1bdd6bfef3b39e678990] an investigation of a complaint where the complainant expressed dissatisfaction with a force other than that force,
  - (i)[^c19976161] an investigation of a recordable conduct matter[^key-8277091d2c2dd22f4acce84b7cb7e956] relating to the conduct of a person who, at the time of the conduct, was not a member of that force, [^key-aaea78fe0a31bea5e734d80956aa2157]...
  - (ii) an investigation of a DSI matter in relation to which the relevant officer was, at the time of the death or serious injury, not a member of that force, or[^key-01069eb23248cd6bf1ee79390894aaa7]
  - (iii) a review under paragraph 25 of Schedule 3 of the outcome of a complaint where the complainant expressed dissatisfaction with a force other than that force.

(3) Where the assistance is required to be provided by one police force to another, the local policing body[^key-79b8d583a9a9a6fefbd7aa2ee81f1093] maintaining that other police force shall pay to the local policing body maintaining the assisting force such contribution (if any) towards the costs of the assistance—

- (a) as may be agreed between them; or
- (b) in the absence of an agreement, as may be determined in accordance with any arrangements which—
  - (i) have been agreed to by local policing bodies[^key-cd08038255c480ff866515dc895d0fd9] generally; and
  - (ii) are for the time being in force with respect to the making of contributions towards the costs of assistance provided, in connection with investigations under this Part, by one police force to another; or
- (c) in the absence of any such arrangements, as may be determined by the Secretary of State.

(4) Where the assistance is required to be provided by a police force to the Director General, Office[^key-bd59dee7ecb1184d047d27c866f94d07] shall pay to the local policing body[^key-c89aece7957faad96dd4602a1a8ae327] maintaining the assisting force such contribution (if any) towards the costs of the assistance—

- (a) as may be agreed between the Director General and that body[^key-1e0e06a5c66daa97bd3ee458790df110]; or
- (b) in the absence of an agreement, as may be determined in accordance with any arrangements which—
  - (i) have been agreed to by local policing bodies[^key-8ca9e8f1300f9bdb4f3f2d46ee9d777a] generally and by the Director General; and
  - (ii) are for the time being in force with respect to the making of contributions towards the costs of assistance provided, in connection with investigations under this Part, to the Director General; or
- (c) in the absence of any such arrangements, as may be determined by the Secretary of State.

(5)[^c20029411] In this section (subject to subsection (6))—

- (a) references to a police force and to a local policing body[^key-95defad7c3805ddfccb70ca9cded6a7a] maintaining a police force include references to the National Crime Agency[^key-7af176d98a29bb67c886eed7f62d81ab]; and
- (b) in relation to that Agency, references to the chief officer are references to the Director General of that Agency[^key-ef51d40e009cbacd261d36464858e99d].

(6) This section shall have effect in relation to cases in which assistance is required to be provided by the National Crime Agency[^key-3bad031c2fc19d81dca915b027e040ac] as if—

- (a) the reference in subsection (3)(b) to local policing bodies[^key-bfa34611e57ce47d73ae542c69da2b43] generally included a reference to the Agency; and
- (b) the reference in subsection (4)(b) to local policing bodies generally were a reference to the Agency.

(7) This section is without prejudice to the application of section 24 of the 1996 Act (assistance given voluntarily by one force to another) in a case in which assistance is provided, otherwise than in pursuance of any duty imposed by section 15 of this Act, in connection with an investigation under this Part.
