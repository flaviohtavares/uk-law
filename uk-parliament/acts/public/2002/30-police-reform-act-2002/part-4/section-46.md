# Police Reform Act 2002, section 46

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-11-09. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/30
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 46. Offences against designated and accredited persons etc.[^c19956811]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Any person who assaults—

- (a) a designated person in the execution of his duty,
- (b) an accredited person in the execution of his duty,
- (ba)[^c20053561] an accredited inspector in the execution of his duty, or
- (c) a person assisting a designated or accredited person or an accredited inspector[^c20053851] in the execution of his duty,

is guilty of an offence and shall be liable, on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, or to both.

(2) Any person who resists or wilfully obstructs—

- (a) a designated person in the execution of his duty,
- (b) an accredited person in the execution of his duty,
- (ba)[^c20053611] an accredited inspector in the execution of his duty, or
- (c) a person assisting a designated or accredited person or an accredited inspector in the execution of his duty,

is guilty of an offence and shall be liable, on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one month or to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale, or to both.

(3) Any person who, with intent to deceive—

- (a) impersonates a designated person , an accredited person or an accredited inspector[^c20053871] ,
- (b) makes any statement or does any act calculated falsely to suggest that he is a designated person , that he is an accredited person or that he is an accredited inspector[^c20053911] , or
- (c) makes any statement or does any act calculated falsely to suggest that he has powers as a designated or accredited person or as an accredited inspector[^c20053961] that exceed the powers he actually has,

is guilty of an offence and shall be liable, on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, or to both.

(4) In this section references to the execution by a designated person , accredited person or accredited inspector[^c20053981] of his duty are references to his exercising any power or performing any duty which is his by virtue of his designation or accreditation.

(5)[^c-1fbeaed1415622dfb9ee3c5bc86d5c95] References in this section to a designated person are to—

- (a) a designated person within the meaning given by section 47(1), and
- (b) a person in relation to whom a designation under section 38B is for the time being in force.
