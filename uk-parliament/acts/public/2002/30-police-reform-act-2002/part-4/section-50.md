# Police Reform Act 2002, section 50

## 50. Persons engaging in anti-social behaviour[^key-f5727accfcd9af9912173c1cb75f3dbe]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) If a constable in uniform has reason to believe that a person has engaged, or is engaging, in anti-social behaviour[^key-e4621c8b14ab752c14d8a8bd13cb5ac0] [^key-77dcc2c045f412d3bc64da194ba7811e]..., he may require that person to give his name and address to the constable.

(1A)[^key-580c580658b065adab5cfb110bc8e523] In subsection (1) “**anti-social behaviour**” means—

- (a) conduct that has caused, or is likely to cause, harassment, alarm or distress to any person, or
- (b) housing-related anti-social conduct, as defined by section 2 of the Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014 (ignoring subsection (2) of that section).

(2) Any person who—

- (a) fails to give his name and address when required to do so under subsection (1), or
- (b) gives a false or inaccurate name or address in response to a requirement under that subsection,

is guilty of an offence and shall be liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.
