# Police Reform Act 2002, section 81

## 81. Exemptions from firearms legislation

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) After section 16A of the Firearms (Amendment) Act 1988 (c. 45) there shall be inserted—

> **16B. Possession of firearms on Ministry of Defence Police premises**
>
> (1) A person who is being trained or assessed in the use of firearms under the supervision of a member of the Ministry of Defence Police may, without holding a certificate or obtaining the authority of the Secretary of State under section 5 of the principal Act, have in his possession a firearm and ammunition on relevant premises for the purposes of the training or assessment.
>
> (2) In this section “**relevant premises**” means premises used for any purpose of the Ministry of Defence Police.

(2) After Article 12A of the Firearms (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 (S.I. 1981/155 (N.I. 2)) there is inserted— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19951131]

> **12B. Possession of firearms on Ministry of Defence Police premises (affected by repeal, see note)[^c19951131]**
>
> (1) A person who is being trained or assessed in the use of firearms under the supervision of a member of the Ministry of Defence Police may, without holding a firearm certificate or obtaining the authority of the Secretary of State under Article 6, have in his possession a firearm and ammunition on relevant premises for the purposes of the training or assessment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19951131]
>
> (2) In this Article “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19951131]**relevant premises *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19951131]**” means premises used for any purpose of the Ministry of Defence Police. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19951131]
