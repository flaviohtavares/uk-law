# Police Reform Act 2002, section 106

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 106. General interpretation[^c19956581]

*Extent: E+W*

In this Act—

- “**the 1984 Act**” means the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 (c. 60);
- “**the 1996 Act**” means the Police Act 1996 (c. 16);
- “**the 1997 Act**” means the Police Act 1997 (c. 50);
- [^c-35795308e3c720af6e7f275c5dd0e0ff]...
- “[^c19956591]**the British Transport Police Force *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19956591]**” means the force of constables appointed under section 53 of the British Transport Commission Act 1949 (c. xxix); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19956591]
- “**modifications**” includes omissions, alterations and additions, and cognate expressions shall be construed accordingly.
