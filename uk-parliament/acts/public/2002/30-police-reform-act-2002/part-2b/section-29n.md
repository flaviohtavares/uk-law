# Police Reform Act 2002, section 29N

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (17).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/30
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29N. Interpretation[^key-2c39f0af6dd8bee3a3f609c11ed14793]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Part—

- [^key-bd0599d895f7059de83c25e8d8450f15]...
- “**appropriate authority**” has the meaning given by section 29E(3);
- “**chief officer**” means the chief officer of police of any police force; and “**acting chief officer**” means (as appropriate)—
  - (a) a person exercising or performing functions of a chief constable in accordance with section 41 of the Police Reform and Social Responsibility Act 2011,
  - (b) a person exercising powers or duties of the Commissioner of Police of the Metropolis in accordance with section 44 or 45(4) of that Act, or
  - (c) a person exercising duties of the Commissioner of Police for the City of London in accordance with section 25 of the City of London Police Act 1839;
- “**conduct**” has the same meaning as in Part 2 (see section 29(1));
- “[^key-d936cf1fea441d93ac21f689f998a9e4]**the Director General**” means the Director General of the Independent Office for Police Conduct;
- “**whistle-blower**” has the meaning given by section 29D(3).

(2) References in this Part to a person serving with the police have the same meaning as in Part 2 (see section 12(7)).
