### 1. Determination of penalties by Board (affected by repeal, see note)[^c18666101][^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]

(1) The Board may make a determination— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]

- (a) imposing a penalty under section 31, 32(2)(b) or (3) or 33, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]
- (b) setting it at such amount as, in their opinion, is appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]

(2) The Board must give notice of a determination of a penalty under this paragraph to the person on whom the penalty is imposed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]

(3) The notice must state the date on which it is given and give details of the right to appeal against the determination under section 38. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]

(4) After the notice of a determination under this paragraph has been given the determination must not be altered except on appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]

(5) A penalty determined under this paragraph becomes payable at the end of the period of thirty days beginning with the date on which the notice of determination is given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]

**2.**[^c18666131] (1) On an appeal *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b] [^key-4986663221b9090605f8ba514d70fd76]... under section 38 against the determination of a penalty under *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b] paragraph 1 that is notified to the First-tier tribunal, the tribunal[^key-43d6ea8b337ce8c6a68e4907ff5408f5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b] may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]

- (a) if it appears that no penalty has been incurred, set the determination aside, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]
- (b) if the amount determined appears to be appropriate, confirm the determination, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]
- (c) if the amount determined appears to be excessive, reduce it to such other amount (including nil) as *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b] the First-tier Tribunal considers[^key-742adb3158d575ea2a1b559df33ba731] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b] appropriate, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]
- (d) if the amount determined appears to be insufficient, increase it to such amount not exceeding the permitted maximum as *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b] the First-tier Tribunal considers *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b] appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]

(2)[^key-39fa13559c2aafa3829ba06846d975e4] In addition to any right of appeal on a point of law under section 11(2) of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007, the person liable to the penalty may appeal to the Upper Tribunal against the amount of the penalty which has been determined under sub-paragraph (1), but not against any decision which falls under section 11(5)(d) or (e) of that Act and was made in connection with the determination of the amount of the penalty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]

(2A) Section 11(3) and (4) of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 applies to the right of appeal under sub-paragraph (2) as it applies to the right of appeal under section 11(2) of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]

(2B) On an appeal under this paragraph the Upper Tribunal has the same powers as are conferred on the First-tier Tribunal by virtue of this paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]

### 5. Mitigation of penalties (affected by repeal, see note)[^c18666161][^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]

The Board may in their discretion mitigate any penalty under this Part or stay or compound any proceedings for any such penalty and may also, after judgment, further mitigate or entirely remit any such penalty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]

### 6. Time limits for penalties[^c18666171][^M_C_cda8cc7e-bb7f-4101-ded7-d7d1058df3fc]

(1) In the case of a penalty under section 31 relating to a tax credit for a person or persons for the whole or part of a tax year (other than a penalty to which sub-paragraph (3) applies), the Board may determine the penalty at any time before the latest of—

- (a) the end of the period of one year beginning with the expiry of the period for initiating an enquiry under section 19 into the entitlement of the person, or the joint entitlement of the persons, for the tax year,
- (b) if such an enquiry is made, the end of the period of one year beginning with the day on which the enquiry is completed, and
- (c) if a decision relating to the entitlement of the person, or the joint entitlement of the persons, for the tax year is made under section 20(1) or (4), the end of the period of one year beginning with the day on which the decision is made.

(2) In the case of a penalty under section 32 relating to a tax credit for a person or persons for the whole or part of a tax year (other than a penalty to which sub-paragraph (3) applies), the Board may determine the penalty, or commence proceedings for it, at any time before—

- (a) if an enquiry into the entitlement of the person, or the joint entitlement of the persons, for the tax year is made under section 19, the end of the period of one year beginning with the day on which the enquiry is completed, and
- (b) otherwise, the end of the period of one year beginning with the expiry of the period for initiating such an enquiry.

(3) In the case of—

- (a) a penalty under section 31 or 32 relating to a requirement imposed by virtue of regulations under section 25, or
- (b) a penalty under section 33,

the Board may determine the penalty, or commence proceedings for it, at any time before the end of the period of six years after the date on which the penalty was incurred or began to be incurred.

### 7. Recovery of penalties (affected by repeal, see note)[^c18666181][^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]

(1) A penalty payable under this Part is to be treated for the purposes of Part 6 of the Taxes Management Act 1970 (c. 9) (collection and recovery) as if it were tax charged in an assessment and due and payable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]

(2) Regulations under section 203(2)(a) of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 (c. 1) ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]PAYE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]) apply to a penalty payable under this Part as if it were an underpayment of tax for a previous year of assessment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e302e1fb0cb13923fc674ae45b40549b]
