# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 67

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Interpretation[^key-df0ec32a2cbd13c10ef762d215d94a43]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Act—

- “**the Board**” means the Commissioners of Inland Revenue,
- “[^key-286417c5dc6bf4c51f14445da1ce43f8]**cautioned**”, in relation to any person and any offence, means cautioned after the person concerned has admitted the offence; and “caution” is to be interpreted accordingly;
- “**modifications**” includes alterations, additions and omissions, and “**modifies**” is to be construed accordingly,
- “**the Northern Ireland Department**” means the Department for Social Development in Northern Ireland,
- “**prescribed**” means prescribed by regulations, and
- “**tax credit**” and “**tax credits**” have the meanings given by section 1(2).
