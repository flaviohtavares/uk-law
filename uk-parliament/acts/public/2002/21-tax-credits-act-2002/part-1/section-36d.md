# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 36D

## 36D. Section 36C: supplementary (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-20e7dcee7cc06c0bd204a294bdc5d26d][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) the conviction of any person of any offence is taken into account for the purposes of the application of section 36C in relation to that person, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) that conviction is subsequently quashed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

all such payments and other adjustments shall be made as would be necessary if no restriction had been imposed by or under section 36C that could not have been imposed if the conviction had not taken place. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) Subsection (3) applies where, after the agreement of any person (“P”) to pay a penalty under the appropriate penalty provision is taken into account for the purposes of the application of section 36C in relation to that person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) P's agreement to pay the penalty is withdrawn under subsection (5) of the appropriate penalty provision, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) it is decided on an appeal or in accordance with regulations under the Social Security Act 1998 or the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]S.I. 1998/1506 (N.I. 10)) that any overpayment made to which the agreement relates is not recoverable or due, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) the amount of any over payment to which the penalty relates is revised on an appeal or in accordance with regulations under the Social Security Act 1998 or the Social Security (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 and there is no new agreement by P to pay a penalty under the appropriate penalty provision in relation to the revised overpayment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) In those circumstances, all such payments and other adjustments shall be made as would be necessary if no restriction had been imposed by or under section 36C that could not have been imposed if P had not agreed to pay the penalty. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) For the purposes of section 36C— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) the date of a person's conviction in any proceedings of a benefit offence shall be taken to be the date on which the person was found guilty of that offence in those proceedings (whenever the person was sentenced) or in the case mentioned in paragraph (b)(ii) the date of the order for absolute discharge, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) references to a conviction include references to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (i) a conviction in relation to which the court makes an order for absolute or conditional discharge, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] an order for absolute discharge made by a court of summary jurisdiction in Scotland under section 246(3) of the Criminal Procedure (Scotland) Act 1995 without proceeding to a conviction, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] a conviction in Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) In section 36C references to any previous application of that section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) include references to any previous application of a provision having an effect in Northern Ireland corresponding to provision made by that section, but *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) do not include references to any previous application of that section the effect of which was to impose a restriction for a period comprised in the same disqualification period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
