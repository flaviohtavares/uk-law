# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 10

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 10. Entitlement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17822011][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The entitlement of the person or persons by whom a claim for working tax credit has been made is dependent on him, or either or both of them, being engaged in qualifying remunerative work. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) Regulations may for the purposes of this Part make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) as to what is, or is not, qualifying remunerative work, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) as to the circumstances in which a person is, or is not, engaged in it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) The circumstances prescribed under subsection (2)(b) may differ by reference to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) the age of the person or either of the persons, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) whether the person, or either of the persons, is disabled, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) whether the person, or either of the persons, is responsible for one or more children or qualifying young persons, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (d) any other factors. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) Regulations may make provision for the purposes of working tax credit as to the circumstances in which a person is or is not responsible for a child or qualifying young person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
