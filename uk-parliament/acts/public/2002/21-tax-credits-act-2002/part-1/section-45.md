# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 45

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 45. Inalienability (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Every assignment of or charge on a tax credit, and every agreement to assign or charge a tax credit, is void; and, on the bankruptcy of a person entitled to a tax credit, the entitlement to the tax credit does not pass to any trustee or other person acting on behalf of his creditors. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) In the application of subsection (1) to Scotland— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) the reference to assignment is to assignation (“ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**assign *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” being construed accordingly), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) the reference to the bankruptcy of a person is to the sequestration of his estate or the appointment on his estate of a judicial factor under section 41 of the Solicitors (Scotland) Act 1980 (c. 46). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
