# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 39A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 39A. Late appeals (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-6d2c87a647ef7b6376821835cf0c9358][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs may treat a late appeal under section 38 as made in time where the conditions specified in subsections (2) to (6) are satisfied, except that the Commissioners may not do so in the case of an appeal made more than one year after the expiration of the time (original or extended) for appealing. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) An appeal may be treated as made in time if the Commissioners are satisfied that it is in the interests of justice to do so. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) For the purposes of subsection (2) it is not in the interests of justice to treat an appeal as made in time unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) the special circumstances specified in subsection (4) are relevant; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) some other special circumstances exist which are wholly exceptional and relevant, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

and as a result of those special circumstances it was not practicable for the appeal to be made in time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) The special circumstances mentioned in subsection (3)(a) are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) the appellant or a partner or dependant of the appellant has died or suffered serious illness; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) the appellant is not resident in the United Kingdom; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) normal postal services were disrupted. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) In determining whether it is in the interests of justice to treat an appeal as made in time, regard shall be had to the principle that the greater the amount of time that has elapsed between the expiration of the time for appealing and the submission of the notice of appeal, the more compelling should be the special circumstances. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(6) In determining whether it is in the interests of justice to treat an appeal as made in time, no account shall be taken of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) that the appellant or any other person acting for the appellant was unaware of or misunderstood the law applicable to the appellant’s case (including ignorance or misunderstanding of any time limit); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) that the Upper Tribunal or a court has taken a different view of the law from that previously understood and applied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(7) If in accordance with the preceding provisions of this section the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs treat a late appeal under section 38 as made in time, it is to be treated as having been brought within any applicable time limit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
