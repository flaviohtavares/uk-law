## 40. Annual reports (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Board must make to the Treasury an annual report about— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a)[^c-4717affa7d4dd49ea5f1dfd576cc2fc5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) the number of awards of child tax credit and of working tax credit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) the number of enquiries conducted under section 19, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (d) the number of penalties imposed under this Part, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (e) the number of prosecutions and convictions for offences connected with tax credits. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) The Treasury must publish each annual report made to it under subsection (1) and lay a copy before each House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
