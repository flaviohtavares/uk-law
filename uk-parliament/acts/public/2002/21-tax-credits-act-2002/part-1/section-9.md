# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 9

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 9. Maximum rate (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17821791][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The maximum rate at which a person or persons may be entitled to child tax credit is to be determined in the prescribed manner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) The prescribed manner of determination must involve the inclusion of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) an element which is to be included in the case of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] every person or persons entitled to child tax credit who is, or either or both of whom is or are, responsible for a child or qualifying young person who was born before 6 April 2017,[^c-a180d62b0ae6f68dc0c7b00c0bba5bf6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) an element in respect of each child or qualifying young person for whom the person is, or either or both of them is or are, responsible. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] , and[^c-9badb361fe1f5c8236ad618735d852d9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) an element which is to be included in the case of a child or qualifying young person who is disabled or severely disabled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) The element specified in paragraph (a) of subsection (2) is to be known as the family element of child tax credit and that specified in paragraph (b) of that subsection is to be known as the individual element of child tax credit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] and that specified in paragraph (c) of that subsection is to be known as the disability element of child tax credit[^c-16f1f269b4bd51c3c1539647ec61a80c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] .

(3A)[^c-a356fe5bb78021c781f04e7eb0704c36] Subsection *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] (3B) applies in the case of a person or persons entitled to child tax credit where the person is, or either or both of them is or are, responsible for a child or qualifying young person born on or after 6 April 2017. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3B) The prescribed manner of determination in relation to the person or persons must not include an individual element of child tax credit in respect of the child or qualifying young person unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) he is (or they are) claiming the individual element of child tax credit for no more than one other child or qualifying young person, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) a prescribed exception applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) The prescribed manner of determination may involve the inclusion of such other elements as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) The prescribed manner of determination— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) may include provision for the amount of the family element of child tax credit to vary according to the age of any of the children or qualifying young persons or according to any such other factors as may be prescribed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) may include provision for the amount of the individual element of child tax credit to vary according to the age of the child or qualifying young person or according to any such other factors as may be prescribed, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c)[^c-f6f18877ea6f362dd6b2543d1119569d] may include provision for the amount of the disability element of child tax credit to vary according to whether the child or qualifying young person is disabled or severely disabled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(6) A child or qualifying young person is disabled, or severely disabled, for the purposes of this section only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) he satisfies prescribed conditions, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) prescribed conditions exist in relation to him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(7) If, in accordance with regulations under section 8(2), more than one claimant may be entitled to child tax credit in respect of the same child or qualifying young person, the prescribed manner of determination may include provision for the amount of any element of child tax credit included in the case of any one or more of them to be less than it would be if only one claimant were so entitled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(8) “**Claimant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) in the case of a single claim, the person who makes the claim, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) in the case of a joint claim, the persons who make the claim. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
