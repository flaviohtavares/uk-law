## 29. Recovery of overpayments (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where an amount is liable to be repaid *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] or paid[^key-53308d6e8b71e3dab457a79b935852e0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] by a person or persons under section 28, the Board must give him, or each of them, a notice specifying the amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) The notice must state which of subsections (3) to (5) is to apply in relation to the amount or any specified part of the amount; and a notice may at any time be replaced by another notice containing a different statement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) Where a notice states that this subsection applies in relation to an amount (or part of an amount), it is to be treated for the purposes of Part 6 of the Taxes Management Act 1970 (c. 9) (collection and recovery) as if it were tax charged in an assessment and due and payable by the person or persons to whom the notice was given at the end of the period of thirty days beginning with the day on which the notice is given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4)[^key-3df4d4b5537df3b77dfe392e5f379ee5] Where a notice states that this subsection applies in relation to an amount (or part of an amount), it may be recovered— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) subject to provision made by regulations, by deduction from payments of any tax credit under an award made for any period to the person, or either or both of the persons, to whom the notice was given; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) by the Secretary of State— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (i) by deductions under section 71ZC of the Administration Act (deduction from benefit); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] by deductions under section 71ZD of that Act (deduction from earnings); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] as set out in section 71ZE of that Act (court action *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] etc *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) by the relevant Northern Ireland Department— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (i) by deductions under section 69ZC of the Administration (Northern Ireland) Act (deduction from benefit); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] by deductions under section 69ZD of that Act (deduction from earnings); or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (iii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] as set out in section 69ZE of that Act (court action *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] etc *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]).

(5) Where a notice states that this subsection applies in relation to an amount (or part of an amount), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] PAYE[^c22566521] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] regulations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] apply to it as if it were an underpayment of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] income tax[^key-bd45556104b4f36fc3f08b53998e1493] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] for a previous year of assessment by the person or persons to whom the notice was given *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] that is not a relevant debt (within the meaning of section 684 of the Income Tax (Earnings and Pensions) Act 2003)[^key-c93de5397d4d09dad9ac8f5a401eda53] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e].
