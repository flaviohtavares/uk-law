# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 21B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21B. Late application for a review (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-d7cfd72731c66ff0dd5a5251663b12ad][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs may in a particular case extend the time limit specified in section 21A(1)(a) for making an application for a review if all of the following conditions are met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) The first condition is that the person seeking a review has applied to the Commissioners for an extension of time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) The second condition is that the application for the extension— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) explains why the extension is sought, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) is made within 13 months of the notification of the original decision or of the date the original decision was made if not notified because of section 23(3) . *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) The third condition is that the Commissioners are satisfied that due to special circumstances it was not practicable for the application for a review to have been made within the time limit specified in section 21A(1)(a). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) The fourth condition is that the Commissioners are satisfied that it is reasonable in all the circumstances to grant the extension. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(6) In determining whether it is reasonable to grant an extension, the Commissioners must have regard to the principle that the greater the amount of time that has elapsed between the end of the time limit specified in section 21A(1)(a) and the date of the application, the more compelling should be the special circumstances on which the application is based. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(7) An application to extend the time limit specified in section 21A(1)(a) which has been refused may not be renewed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
