# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 12

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Child care element (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17822271][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The prescribed manner of determination of the maximum rate at which a person or persons may be entitled to working tax credit may involve the inclusion, in prescribed circumstances, of a child care element. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) A child care element is an element in respect of a prescribed proportion of so much of any relevant child care charges as does not exceed a prescribed amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) “**Child care charges *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” are charges of a prescribed description incurred in respect of child care by the person, or either or both of the persons, by whom a claim for working tax credit is made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) “**Child care *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**”, in relation to a person or persons, means care provided— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) for a child of a prescribed description for whom the person is responsible, or for whom either or both of the persons is or are responsible, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) by a person of a prescribed description. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) The descriptions of persons prescribed under subsection (4)(b) may include descriptions of persons approved in accordance with a scheme made by the appropriate national authority under this subsection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(6) “**The appropriate national authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) in relation to care provided in England, the Secretary of State, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) in relation to care provided in Scotland, the Scottish Ministers, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) in relation to care provided in Wales, the National Assembly for Wales, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (d) in relation to care provided in Northern Ireland, the Department of Health, Social Services and Public Safety. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(7) The provision made by a scheme under subsection (5) must involve the giving of approvals, in accordance with criteria determined by or under the scheme, by such of the following as the scheme specifies— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) the appropriate national authority making the scheme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) one or more specified persons or bodies or persons or bodies of a specified description, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) persons or bodies accredited under the scheme in accordance with criteria determined by or under it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(8) A scheme under subsection (5) may authorise— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) the making of grants or loans to, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) the charging of reasonable fees by, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

persons and bodies giving approvals. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
