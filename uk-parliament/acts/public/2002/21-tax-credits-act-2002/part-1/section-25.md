## 25. Payments of working tax credit by employers (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17823231][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulations may require employers, when making *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] payments of, or on account of,[^c22566461] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] PAYE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] income *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] and in any such other circumstances as may be prescribed, to pay working tax credit, or prescribed elements of working tax credit, to employees. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) The regulations may, in particular, include provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) requiring employers to make payments of working tax credit, or prescribed elements of working tax credit, in accordance with notices given to them by the Board, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) for the payment by the Board of working tax credit in cases where an employer does not make payments of working tax credit, or prescribed elements of working tax credit, in accordance with the regulations and with any notices given by the Board, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) prescribing circumstances in which employers are not required to make, or to continue making, payments of working tax credit, or prescribed elements of working tax credit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (d) for the provision of information or evidence for the purpose of enabling the Board to be satisfied whether employers are complying with notices given by the Board and with the regulations, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (e) requiring employers to provide information to employees (in their itemised pay statements or otherwise), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (f) for the funding by the Board of working tax credit paid or to be paid by employers (whether by way of set off against income tax, national insurance contributions or student loan deductions for which they are accountable to the Board or otherwise), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (g) for the recovery by the Board from an employer of funding under paragraph (f) to the extent that it exceeds the amount of working tax credit paid by the employer, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (h) for the payment of interest at the prescribed rate on sums due from or to the Board, and for determining the date from which interest is to be calculated, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (i) for appeals with respect to matters arising under the regulations which would otherwise not be the subject of an appeal. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3)[^c-9c1dba4b299d04346d09a332d41436bb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-ddcb36ebe3cc43c1d3bd6785c15128b3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) In this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- “**employee *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” means a person who receives any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] payment of, or on account of,[^c22566481] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] PAYE *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] income *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- “**employer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**”, in relation to an employee, means a person who makes any such payment to the employee. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(6) [^c22566501]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) “**Student loan deductions *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” means deductions in accordance with regulations under section 22(5) of the Teaching and Higher Education Act 1998 (c. 30), section 73B(3) of the Education (Scotland) Act 1980 (c. 44) or Article 3(5) of the Education (Student Support) (Northern Ireland) Order 1998 ( *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]S.I. 1998/1760 (N.I. 14)).
