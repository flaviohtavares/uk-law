# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 36A

## 36A. Loss of working tax credit in case of conviction (affected by repeal, see note) etc (affected by repeal, see note) for benefit offence (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-20e7dcee7cc06c0bd204a294bdc5d26d][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsection (4) applies where a person (“the offender”)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) is convicted of one or more benefit offences in any proceedings, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) after being given a notice under subsection (2) of the appropriate penalty provision by an appropriate authority, agrees in the manner specified by the appropriate authority to pay a penalty under the appropriate penalty provision to the appropriate authority, in a case where the offence to which the notice relates is a benefit offence, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) is cautioned in respect of one or more benefit offences. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) In subsection (1)(b)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) “**the appropriate penalty provision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” means section 115A of the Social Security Administration Act 1992 (penalty as alternative to prosecution) or section 109A of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 (the corresponding provision for Northern Ireland); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) “**appropriate authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (i) in relation to section 115A of the Social Security Administration Act 1992, the Secretary of State or an authority which administers housing benefit or council tax benefit, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] in relation to section 109A of the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992, the Department (within the meaning of that Act) or the Northern Ireland Housing Executive. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) Subsection (4) does not apply by virtue of subsection (1)(a) if, because the proceedings in which the offender was convicted constitute the current set of proceedings for the purposes of section 36C, the restriction in subsection (3) of that section applies in the offender's case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) If this subsection applies and the offender is a person who would, apart from this section, be entitled (whether pursuant to a single or joint claim) to working tax credit at any time within the disqualification period, then, despite that entitlement, working tax credit shall not be payable for any period comprised in the disqualification period— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) in the case of a single claim, to the offender, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) in the case of a joint claim, to the offender or the other member of the couple. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) Regulations may provide in relation to cases to which subsection (4)(b) would otherwise apply that working tax credit shall be payable, for any period comprised in the disqualification period, as if the amount payable were reduced in such manner as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(6) For the purposes of this section, the disqualification period, in relation to any disqualifying event, means the relevant period beginning with such date, falling after the date of the disqualifying event, as may be determined by or in accordance with regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(7) For the purposes of subsection (6) the relevant period is— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) in a case falling within subsection (1)(a) where the benefit offence, or one of them, is a relevant offence, the period of three years, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) in a case falling within subsection (1)(a) (but not within paragraph (a) above)), the period of 13 weeks, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) in a case falling within subsection (1)(b) or (c), the period of 4 weeks. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(8) The Treasury may by order amend subsection (7)(a), (b) or (c) to substitute a different period for that for the time being specified there. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(9) This section has effect subject to section 36B. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(10)[^key-e7f44b84673748cffc36e04400ef0e87] In this section and section 36B— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- “benefit offence“ means any of the following offences committed on or after the day specified by order made by the Treasury— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (a) an offence in connection with a claim for a disqualifying benefit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (b) an offence in connection with the receipt or payment of any amount by way of such a benefit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (c) an offence committed for the purpose of facilitating the commission (whether or not by the same person) of a benefit offence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (d) an offence consisting in an attempt or conspiracy to commit a benefit offence; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- “**disqualifying benefit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” has the meaning given in section 6A(1) of the Social Security Fraud Act 2001; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- “disqualifying event“ means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (a) the conviction falling within subsection (1)(a); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (b) the agreement falling within subsection (1)(b); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (c) the caution falling within subsection (1)(c); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- “**relevant offence *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” has the meaning given in section 6B of the Social Security Fraud Act 2001. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
