# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Overpayments (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_C_c9fa8a5f-994b-4790-9a66-c86b7f54d4f1][^c-16b60830c325ab1c5d6d8e71eeda8ef9][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the amount of a tax credit paid for a tax year to a person or persons exceeds the amount of the tax credit to which he is entitled, or they are jointly entitled, for the tax year (as determined in accordance with the provision made by and by virtue of sections 18 to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] 21C[^c-995587c1607afc459c6fc683b3648bf3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]), the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] Commissioners may[^c-7e005d0d2ae2e6a02488cd3d4ea191df] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] decide that the excess, or any part of it, is to be *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] —[^c-11e194efeff93a3dd09507e9c2e6745d] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) repaid to the Commissioners; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) treated as if it were an amount recoverable by the Secretary of State under section 71ZB of the Administration Act or (as the case may be) by the relevant Northern Ireland Department under section 69ZB of the Administration (Northern Ireland) Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e].

(2) In this Part such an excess is referred to as an overpayment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) For overpayments made under awards on single claims, the person to whom the tax credit was awarded is liable to repay *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] to the Commissioners, the Secretary of State or (as the case may be) the relevant Northern Ireland Department, the amount which the Commissioners decide is to be repaid or treated as recoverable under subsection (1)(b)[^c-674e4e0d610c4615a267be114bd8a141] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] .

(4) For overpayments made under awards on joint claims, the persons to whom the tax credit was awarded are jointly and severally liable to repay *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] to the Commissioners, the Secretary of State or (as the case may be) the relevant Northern Ireland Department, the amount mentioned in subsection (3) unless the Commissioners decide that each is liable for[^c-2f8d52093d2fbdf5233e54a6832ab214] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] a specified part of that amount. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) Where it appears to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] Commissioners[^c-605e630ed51e7ae16644386607356d1c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] that there is likely to be an overpayment of a tax credit for a tax year under an award made to a person or persons, the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] Commissioners *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] may, with a view to reducing or eliminating the overpayment, amend the award or any other award of any tax credit made to the person or persons; but this subsection does not apply once a decision is taken in relation to the person or persons for the tax year under section 18(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(6) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] Commissioners[^c-bd05fb632969d47a7ccdfddd900b99eb] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] decide under section 16 to terminate an award of a tax credit made to a person or persons on the ground that at no time during the period to which the award related did the person or persons satisfy— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) section 8(1) (if the award related to child tax credit), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) section 10(1) (if it related to working tax credit), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] Commissioners *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] may decide that the amount paid under the award, or any part of it, is to be treated for the purposes of this Part (apart from subsection (5)) as an overpayment. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(7)[^c-91630da10aacf87a9f6f11cf31ca0ce2] In this section and in section 29— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- “the Administration Act” means the Social Security Administration Act 1992; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- “the Administration (Northern Ireland) Act” means the Social Security Administration (Northern Ireland) Act 1992; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- “the relevant Northern Ireland Department” means the Department for Communities. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(8) In this section, “the Commissioners” means the Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
