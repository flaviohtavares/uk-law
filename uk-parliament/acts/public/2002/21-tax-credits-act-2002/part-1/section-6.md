# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 6. Notifications of changes of circumstances (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-68e9138f2b014106f41e9508f7389d48][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulations may provide that any change of circumstances of a prescribed description which may increase the maximum rate at which a person or persons may be entitled to a tax credit is to do so only if notification of it has been given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) Regulations under subsection (1) may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) provide for notification of a change of circumstances given in prescribed circumstances to be treated as having been given on a prescribed date earlier or later than that on which it is given, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) provide that, in prescribed circumstances, a notification of a change of circumstances may be given for a period wholly or partly after the date on which it is given, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) provide that, in prescribed circumstances, an amendment of an award of a tax credit in consequence of a notification of a change of circumstances may be made subject to the condition that the requirements for entitlement to the amended amount of the tax credit are satisfied at a prescribed time. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) Regulations may require that, where a person has or persons have claimed a tax credit, notification is to be given if there is a change of circumstances of a prescribed description which may decrease the rate at which he is or they are entitled to the tax credit or mean that he ceases or they cease to be entitled to the tax credit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) Regulations under this section may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) require a notification to be given in a prescribed manner and within a prescribed time, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) specify the person or persons by whom a notification may be, or is to be, given, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) provide that, in prescribed circumstances, one person may act for another in giving a notification. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
