# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 21C

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21C. Late review: award of relevant disability benefits (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-c7d4657aff14c4b99a7cb694df2e42fb][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs must review a relevant decision if they are notified (whether in writing or otherwise) within the period mentioned in subsection (3)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) of the identity of the person seeking a review; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) of the decision in question; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) that the condition in subsection (2) is met. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) The condition is that it has been determined that the person is entitled, whether in respect of themselves or a child for whom they are responsible, to a relevant disability benefit in respect of a tax year to which the relevant decision related. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) The period is the period of one month beginning with the day on which the person’s claim for the benefit is determined in that person’s favour. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) The sole purpose of a review under this section is to consider whether, as a result of the condition in subsection (2) being met, the relevant decision should— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) be upheld; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) be varied; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) be cancelled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) The Commissioners must carry out the review as soon as is reasonably practicable *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]*.*

(6) When the review has been carried out, the Commissioners must give the person notice of the conclusion containing sufficient information to enable that person to know— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) the conclusion on the review; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) if the conclusion is that the relevant decision is varied, details of the variation, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) the reasons for the conclusion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(7) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) the Commissioners notify the person of further information or evidence that they may need for carrying out the review, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) that information or evidence is not provided to them by the date specified in the notice, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

the review may proceed without that information or evidence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(8) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- “relevant decision” means a decision within section 38(1) or any variation of such a decision resulting from an appeal brought against it; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- “relevant disability benefit”, in relation to any part of the United Kingdom, means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (a) armed forces independence payment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (b) attendance allowance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (c) disability assistance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (d) disability living allowance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (e) employment and support allowance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (f) housing benefit, where a pensioner or a disability premium is included; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (g) incapacity benefit (whether short term or long term); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (h) income support where a pensioner or a disability premium is included; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (i) jobseeker’s allowance where a pensioner or a disability premium is included; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (j) limited capability for work credit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (k) a mobility supplement or a constant attendance allowance where the supplement or benefit is paid in conjunction with a war pension or industrial injuries disablement benefit; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (l) personal independence payment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (m) severe disablement allowance; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (n) statutory sick pay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
