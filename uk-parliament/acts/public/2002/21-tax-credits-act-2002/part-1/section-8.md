# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 8

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Entitlement (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17790791][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The entitlement of the person or persons by whom a claim for child tax credit has been made is dependent on him, or either or both of them, being responsible for one or more children or qualifying young persons. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) Regulations may make provision for the purposes of child tax credit as to the circumstances in which a person is or is not responsible for a child or qualifying young person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) For the purposes of this Part a person is a child if he has not attained the age of sixteen; but regulations may make provision for a person who has attained that age to remain a child for the purposes of this Part after attaining that age for a prescribed period or until a prescribed date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) In this Part “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**qualifying young person *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” means a person, other than a child, who— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) has not attained such age (greater than sixteen) as is prescribed, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) satisfies prescribed conditions. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) Circumstances may be prescribed in which a person is to be entitled to child tax credit for a prescribed period in respect of a child or qualifying young person who has died. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
