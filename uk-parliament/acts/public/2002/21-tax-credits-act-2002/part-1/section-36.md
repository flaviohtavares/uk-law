## 36. Powers in relation to documents (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Section 20BA of the Taxes Management Act 1970 (c. 9) (orders for delivery of documents) applies (with Schedule 1AA and section 20BB) in relation to offences involving fraud in connection with, or in relation to, tax credits as in relation to offences involving serious fraud in connection with, or in relation to, tax. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2)[^key-279d804e5e2c934cbd172d8e707d2789] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-279d804e5e2c934cbd172d8e707d2789] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Any regulations under Schedule 1AA to the Taxes Management Act 1970 which are in force immediately before the commencement of subsection (1) apply, subject to any necessary modifications, for the purposes of that Schedule as they apply by virtue of that subsection (until amended or revoked). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
