# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 7

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Income test (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17790771][^c-1545a44df01fa46524baddc6639fbe93][^M_C_215933d1-9b72-47ce-fabf-78a6ec271c23][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The entitlement of a person or persons of any description to a tax credit is dependent on the relevant income— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) not exceeding the amount determined in the manner prescribed for the purposes of this paragraph in relation to the tax credit and a person or persons of that description (referred to in this Part as the income threshold), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) exceeding the income threshold by only so much that a determination in accordance with regulations under section 13(2) provides a rate of the tax credit in his or their case. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to the entitlement of a person or persons to a tax credit for so long as the person, or either of the persons, is entitled to any social security benefit prescribed for the purposes of this subsection in relation to the tax credit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) In this Part “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**the relevant income *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) if an amount is prescribed for the purposes of this paragraph and the current year income exceeds the previous year income by not more than that amount, the previous year income, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) if an amount is prescribed for the purposes of this paragraph and the current year income exceeds the previous year income by more than that amount, the current year income reduced by that amount, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) if an amount is prescribed for the purposes of this paragraph and the previous year income exceeds the current year income by not more than that amount, the previous year income, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (d) if an amount is prescribed for the purposes of this paragraph and the previous year income exceeds the current year income by more than that amount, the current year income increased by that amount, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (e) otherwise, the current year income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) In this Part “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**the current year income *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) in relation to persons by whom a joint claim for a tax credit is made, the aggregate income of the persons for the tax year to which the claim relates, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) in relation to a person by whom a single claim for a tax credit is made, the income of the person for that tax year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) In this Part “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**the previous year income *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) in relation to persons by whom a joint claim for a tax credit is made, the aggregate income of the persons for the tax year preceding that to which the claim relates, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) in relation to a person by whom a single claim for a tax credit is made, the income of the person for that preceding tax year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(6) Regulations may provide that, for the purposes of this Part, income of a prescribed description is to be treated as being, or as not being, income for a particular tax year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(7) In particular, regulations may provide that income of a prescribed description of a person for the tax year immediately before the preceding tax year referred to in subsection (5) is to be treated as being income of that preceding tax year (instead of any actual income of that description of the person for that preceding tax year). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(8) Regulations may for the purposes of this Part make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) as to what is, or is not, income, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) as to the calculation of income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(9) Regulations may provide that, for the purposes of this Part, a person is to be treated— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) as having income which he does not in fact have, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) as not having income which he does in fact have. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(10) The Board may estimate the amount of the income of a person, or the aggregate income of persons, for any tax year for the purpose of making, amending or terminating an award of a tax credit; but such an estimate does not affect the rate at which he is, or they are, entitled to the tax credit for that or any other tax year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
