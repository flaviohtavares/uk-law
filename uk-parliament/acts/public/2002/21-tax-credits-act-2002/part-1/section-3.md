# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Claims (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17783691][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Entitlement to a tax credit for the whole or part of a tax year is dependent on the making of a claim for it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) Where the Board— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) decide under section 14 not to make an award of a tax credit on a claim, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) decide under section 16 to terminate an award of a tax credit made on a claim, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(subject to any appeal) any entitlement, or subsequent entitlement, to the tax credit for any part of the same tax year is dependent on the making of a new claim. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) A claim for a tax credit may be made— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) jointly by the members of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] couple[^key-38acd92b6fbd0eef3c50179b125b30d9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] both of whom are aged at least sixteen and are in the United Kingdom, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) by a person who is aged at least sixteen and is in the United Kingdom but is not entitled to make a claim under paragraph (a) (jointly with another). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) Entitlement to a tax credit pursuant to a claim ceases— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) in the case of a joint claim, if the persons by whom it was made could no longer jointly make a joint claim, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) in the case of a single claim, if the person by whom it was made could no longer make a single claim. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5A) In this Part “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] **couple *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]** ” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a)[^key-7f4a819e4f3fc11132585f8726fcae27] two people who are married to, or civil partners of, each other and are neither— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (i) separated under a court order, nor *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] separated in circumstances in which the separation is likely to be permanent, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) two people who are not married to, or civil partners of, each other but are living together as if they were a married couple or civil partners. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(7) Circumstances may be prescribed in which a person is to be treated for the purposes of this Part as being, or as not being, in the United Kingdom. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(8) In this Part— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- “**joint claim *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” means a claim under paragraph (a) of subsection (3), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- “**single claim *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” means a claim under paragraph (b) of that subsection. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
