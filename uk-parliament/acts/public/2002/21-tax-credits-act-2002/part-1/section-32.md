# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Failure to comply with requirements (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17823791][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a person fails— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) to provide any information or evidence which he is required to provide by virtue of section 14(2), 15(2), 16(3), 18(10) or 19(2) or regulations under section 25, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) to comply with a requirement imposed on him by a notice under section 17 by virtue of subsection (2)(a), (4)(a) or (6)(a) of that section, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

the penalties specified in subsection (2) may be imposed on him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) The penalties are— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) a penalty not exceeding £300, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) if the failure continues after a penalty is imposed under paragraph (a), a further penalty or penalties not exceeding £60 for each day on which the failure continues after the day on which the penalty under that paragraph was imposed (but excluding any day for which a penalty under this paragraph has already been imposed). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) Where a person fails to give a notification required by regulations under section 6(3), a penalty not exceeding £300 may be imposed on him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) No penalty under subsection (2) may be imposed on a person in respect of a failure after the failure has been remedied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) For the purposes of this section a person is to be taken not to have failed to provide information or evidence, comply with a requirement or give a notification which must be provided, complied with or given by a particular time— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) if he provided, complied with or gave it within such further time (if any) as the Board may have allowed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) if he had a reasonable excuse for not providing, complying with or giving it by that time, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) if, after having had such an excuse, he provided, complied with or gave it without unreasonable delay. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(6) Where the members of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] couple[^c-e3157cf9e2d774830475473a72888870] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] both fail as mentioned in subsection (1)(b), the aggregate amount of any penalties under subsection (2) imposed on them in relation to their failures must not exceed the amounts specified in that subsection; and where the members of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] couple *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] both fail as mentioned in subsection (3), the aggregate amount of any penalties imposed on them in relation to their failures must not exceed £300. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
