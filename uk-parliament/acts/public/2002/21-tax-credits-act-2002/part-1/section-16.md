# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 16

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 16. Other revised decisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17822471][^key-7bedb342d8b31fc77b7ca51e8b2c13db][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where, at any time during the period for which an award of a tax credit is made to a person or persons, the Board have reasonable grounds for believing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) that the rate at which the tax credit has been awarded to him or them for the period differs from the rate at which he is, or they are, entitled to the tax credit for the period, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) that he has, or they have, ceased to be, or never been, entitled to the tax credit for the period, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

the Board may decide to amend or terminate the award. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) Where, at any time during the period for which an award of a tax credit is made to a person or persons, the Board believe— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) that the rate at which a tax credit has been awarded to him or them for the period may differ from the rate at which he is, or they are, entitled to it for the period, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) that he or they may have ceased to be, or never been, entitled to the tax credit for the period, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

the Board may give a notice under subsection (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) A notice under this subsection may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) require the person, or either or both of the persons, to whom the tax credit was awarded to provide any information or evidence which the Board consider they may need for considering whether to amend or terminate the award under subsection (1), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) require any person of a prescribed description to provide any information or evidence of a prescribed description which the Board consider they may need for that purpose, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

by the date specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
