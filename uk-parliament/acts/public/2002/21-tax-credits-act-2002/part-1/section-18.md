# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 18

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Decisions after final notice (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_C_658c1658-1b3f-4b47-ef33-580204d75a17][^c-7bedb342d8b31fc77b7ca51e8b2c13db][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) After giving a notice under section 17 the Board must decide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) whether the person was entitled, or the persons were jointly entitled, to the tax credit, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) if so, the amount of the tax credit to which he was entitled, or they were jointly entitled, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

for the tax year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) But, subject to subsection (3), that decision must not be made before a declaration or statement has been made in response to the relevant provisions of the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) If a declaration or statement has not been made in response to the relevant provisions of the notice on or before the date specified for the purposes of section 17(4), that decision may be made after that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) In subsections (2) and (3) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**the relevant provisions of the notice *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) the provision included in the notice by virtue of subsection (2) of section 17, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) the provision included in the notice by virtue of subsection (4) of that section, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) any provision included in the notice by virtue of subsection (6) of that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) Where the Board make a decision under subsection (1) on or before the date referred to in subsection (3), they may revise it if a new declaration or statement is made on or before that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(6) If the person or persons to whom a notice under section 17 is given is or are within paragraph (a) or (b) of subsection (8) of that section, the Board must decide again— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) whether the person was entitled, or the persons were jointly entitled, to the tax credit, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) if so, the amount of the tax credit to which he was entitled, or they were jointly entitled, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

for the tax year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(7) But, subject to subsection (8), that decision must not be made before a statement has been made in response to the provision included in the notice by virtue of subsection (8) of section 17. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(8) If a statement has not been made in response to the provision included in the notice by virtue of that subsection on or before the date specified for the purposes of that subsection, that decision may be made after that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(9) Where the Board make a decision under subsection (6) on or before the date referred to in subsection (8), they may revise it if a new statement is made on or before that date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(10) Before exercising a function imposed or conferred on them by subsection (1), (5), (6) or (9), the Board may by notice require the person, or either or both of the persons, to whom the notice under section 17 was given to provide any further information or evidence which the Board consider they may need for exercising the function by the date specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(11) Subject to sections *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] 19, 20,[^M_F_10b312b3-a62e-453f-e4d8-055fbff30b8e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] 21A, 21B and 21C[^c-d7544041a6a37d0352e0b1e5911c977f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] and regulations under section 21 (and to any revision under subsection (5) or (9) and any appeal)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) in a case in which a decision is made under subsection (6) in relation to a person or persons and a tax credit for a tax year, that decision, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) in any other case, the decision under subsection (1) in relation to a person or persons and a tax credit for a tax year, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

is conclusive as to the entitlement of the person, or the joint entitlement of the persons, to the tax credit for the tax year and the amount of the tax credit to which he was entitled, or they were jointly entitled, for the tax year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
