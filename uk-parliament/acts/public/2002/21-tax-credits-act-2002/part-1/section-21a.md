# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 21A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 21A. Review of decisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-d7cfd72731c66ff0dd5a5251663b12ad][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Commissioners for Her Majesty’s Revenue and Customs must review any decision within section 38(1) if they receive a written application to do so that identifies the applicant and decision in question, and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) that application is received within 30 days of the date of the notification of the original decision or of the date the original decision was made if not notified because of section 23(3), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) it is received within such longer period as may be allowed under section 21B. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) The Commissioners must carry out the review as soon as is reasonably practicable. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) When the review has been carried out, the Commissioners must give the applicant notice of their conclusion containing sufficient information to enable the applicant to know— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) the conclusion on the review, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) if the conclusion is that the decision is varied, details of the variation, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) the reasons for the conclusion. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) The conclusion on the review must be one of the following— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) that the decision is upheld; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) that the decision is varied; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) that the decision is cancelled. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) Where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) the Commissioners notify the applicant of further information or evidence that they may need for carrying out the review, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) the information or evidence is not provided to them by the date specified in the notice, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

the review may proceed without that information or evidence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
