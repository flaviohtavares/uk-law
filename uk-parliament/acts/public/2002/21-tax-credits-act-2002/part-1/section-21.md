## 21. Decisions subject to official error (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_C_2363e082-60c8-4e83-9458-3b5460c68c16][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Regulations may make provision for a decision under section 14(1), 15(1), 16(1), 18(1), (5), (6) or (9), 19(3) or 20(1) or (4) to be revised in favour of the person or persons to whom it relates if it is incorrect by reason of official error (as defined by the regulations). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
