# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 5

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 5. Period of awards (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17790751][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a tax credit is claimed for a tax year by making a claim before the tax year begins, any award of the tax credit on the claim is for the whole of the tax year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2)[^key-d763a8eeb2487028fa09a8dc072d67a7][^key-97c39218a831e1272a8bfe94f4423045] An award on any other claim for a tax credit is for the period beginning with the date on which the claim is made and ending at the end of the tax year in which that date falls. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) Subsections (1) and (2) are subject to any decision by the Board under section 16 to terminate an award. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
