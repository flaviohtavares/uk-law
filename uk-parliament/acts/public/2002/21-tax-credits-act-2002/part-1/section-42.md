# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 42

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21

## 42. Persons subject to immigration control (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulations may make provision in relation to persons subject to immigration control or in relation to prescribed descriptions of such persons— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) for excluding entitlement to, or to a prescribed element of, child tax credit or working tax credit (or both), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) for this Part to apply subject to other prescribed modifications. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) “**Person subject to immigration control *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” has the same meaning as in section 115 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999 (c. 33). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
