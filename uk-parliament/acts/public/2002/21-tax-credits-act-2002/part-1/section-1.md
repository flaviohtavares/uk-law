# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Introductory (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17781611][^c-7a971339f7c5fb9bb045a8583367ad9d][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act makes provision for— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) a tax credit to be known as child tax credit, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) a tax credit to be known as working tax credit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) In this Act references to a tax credit are to either of those tax credits and references to tax credits are to both of them. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) The following (which are superseded by tax credits) are abolished— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) children’s tax credit under section 257AA of the Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1988 (c. 1), *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) working families’ tax credit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) disabled person’s tax credit, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (d) the amounts which, in relation to income support and income-based jobseeker’s allowance, are prescribed as part of the applicable amount in respect of a child or young person, the family premium, the enhanced disability premium in respect of a child or young person and the disabled child premium, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (e) increases in benefits in respect of children under sections 80 and 90 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 (c. 4) and sections 80 and 90 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits (Northern Ireland) Act 1992 (c. 7), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (f) the employment credit under the schemes under section 2(2) of the Employment and Training Act 1973 (c. 50) and section 1 of the Employment and Training Act (Northern Ireland) 1950 (c. 29 (N.I.)) known as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**New Deal 50plus *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**”.
