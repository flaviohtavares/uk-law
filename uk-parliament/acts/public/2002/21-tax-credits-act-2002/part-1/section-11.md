# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Maximum rate (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17822261][^key-3281f77e01e3b56e4d25402e5d6e950e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The maximum rate at which a person or persons may be entitled to working tax credit is to be determined in the prescribed manner. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) The prescribed manner of determination must involve the inclusion of an element which is to be included in the case of all persons entitled to working tax credit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) The prescribed manner of determination must also involve the inclusion of an element in respect of the person, or either or both of the persons, engaged in qualifying remunerative work— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) having a physical or mental disability which puts him at a disadvantage in getting a job, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) satisfying such other conditions as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) The element specified in subsection (2) is to be known as the basic element of working tax credit and the element specified in subsection (3) is to be known as the disability element of working tax credit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) The prescribed manner of determination may involve the inclusion of such other elements as may be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(6) The other elements may (in particular) include— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) an element in respect of the person, or either of the persons or the two of them taken together, being engaged in qualifying remunerative work to an extent prescribed for the purposes of this paragraph, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) an element in respect of the persons being the members of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] couple[^key-48154f955b45241ac67590a3f9ca0a9b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e],
- (c) an element in respect of the person not being a member of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] couple[^key-326f9a33a744b6137f29416a55c7c7a2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] but being responsible for a child or qualifying young person, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (d) an element in respect of the person, or either or both of the persons, being severely disabled, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (e) an element in respect of the person, or either or both of the persons, being over a prescribed age, satisfying prescribed conditions and having been engaged in qualifying remunerative work for not longer than a prescribed period. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(7) A person has a physical or mental disability which puts him at a disadvantage in getting a job, or is severely disabled, for the purposes of this section only if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) he satisfies prescribed conditions, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) prescribed conditions exist in relation to him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
