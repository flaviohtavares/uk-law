# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Notice of decisions (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17823121][^M_C_2beebc2a-2560-4dfd-b0a5-d872b44d556a][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) When a decision is made under section 14(1), 15(1), 16(1), 18(1), (5), (6) or (9), 19(3) or 20(1) or (4) or regulations under section 21, the Board must give notice of the decision to the person, or each of the persons, to whom it relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) Notice of a decision must state the date on which it is given and include details of any right *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]to a review under section 21A and of any subsequent right[^c-eaf3bde033860a9a31ca66d9675e26b0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] to appeal against the decision under section 38. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) Notice need not be given of a decision made under section 14(1) or 18(1) or (6) on the basis of declarations made or treated as made by the person or persons in response to the notice given to him or them under section 17 if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) that notice, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) in the case of a decision under subsection (6) of section 18, that notice or the notice of the decision under subsection (1) of that section, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

stated what the decision would be and the date on which it would be made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
