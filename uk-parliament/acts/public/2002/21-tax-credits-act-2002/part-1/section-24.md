# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 24

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Payments (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17823171][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the Board have made an award of a tax credit, the amount of the tax credit awarded must be paid to the person to whom the award is made, subject to subsections (2) and (3). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) Where an award of a tax credit is made to the members of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] couple[^key-b6ac72ce6b64c0ab0d4c94b734bf5a79] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e], payments of the tax credit, or of any element of the tax credit, are to be made to whichever of them is prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) Where an award of a tax credit is made on a claim which was made by one person on behalf of another, payments of the tax credit, or of any element of the tax credit, are to be made to whichever of those persons is prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) Where an award of a tax credit has been made to a person or persons for the whole or part of a tax year, payments may, in prescribed circumstances, continue to be made for any period, after the tax year, within which he is or they are entitled to make a claim for the tax credit for the next tax year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) Payments made under subsection (4) are to be treated for the purposes of this section and the following provisions of this Part as if they were payments of the tax credit for the next tax year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(6) Subject to section 25, payments of a tax credit must be made by the Board. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(7) Regulations may make provision about the time when and the manner in which a tax credit, or any element of a tax credit, is to be paid by the Board. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(8) If the regulations make provision for payments of a tax credit, or any element of a tax credit, to be made by the Board by way of a credit to a bank account or other account notified to the Board, the regulations may provide that entitlement to the tax credit or element is dependent on an account having been notified to the Board in accordance with the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
