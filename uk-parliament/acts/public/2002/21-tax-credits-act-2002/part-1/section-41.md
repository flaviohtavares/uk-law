# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Annual review (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-44728cfbf2389d022843097e049c7338][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Treasury must, in each tax year, review the amounts specified in subsection (2) in order to determine whether they have retained their value in relation to the general level of prices in the United Kingdom as estimated by the Treasury in such manner as it considers appropriate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) The amounts are monetary amounts prescribed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) under subsection (1)(a) of section 7, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) for the purposes of any of paragraphs (a) to (d) of subsection (3) of that section, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) under section 9, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (d) under section 11, otherwise than by virtue of section 12, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (e) under subsection (2) of section 13, otherwise than by virtue of subsection (3) of that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) The Treasury must prepare a report of each review. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) The report must include a statement of what each amount would be if it had fully retained its value. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) The Treasury must publish the report and lay a copy of it before each House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
