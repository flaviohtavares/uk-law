# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. Offence of fraud (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person commits an offence if he is knowingly concerned in any fraudulent activity undertaken with a view to obtaining payments of a tax credit by him or any other person. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) A person who commits an offence under subsection (1) is liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) on summary conviction, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or a fine not exceeding the statutory maximum, or both, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) on conviction on indictment, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding seven years, or a fine, or both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
