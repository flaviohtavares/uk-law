# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Power to enquire (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17823051][^M_C_5583c9f7-1f1c-4527-ca41-4af914adf5a7][^key-7bedb342d8b31fc77b7ca51e8b2c13db][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Board may enquire into— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) the entitlement of a person, or the joint entitlement of persons, to a tax credit for a tax year, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) the amount of the tax credit to which he was entitled, or they were jointly entitled, for the tax year, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

if they give notice to the person, or each of the persons, during the period allowed for the initiation of an enquiry. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) As part of the enquiry the Board may by notice— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) require the person, or either or both of the persons, to provide any information or evidence which the Board consider they may need for the purposes of the enquiry, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) require any person of a prescribed description to provide any information or evidence of a prescribed description which the Board consider they may need for those purposes, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

by the date specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) On an enquiry the Board must decide— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) whether the person was entitled, or the persons were jointly entitled, to the tax credit, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) if so, the amount of the tax credit to which he was entitled, or they were jointly entitled, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

for the tax year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) The period allowed for the initiation of an enquiry is the period beginning immediately after the relevant section 18 decision and ending— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) if the person, or either of the persons, to whom the enquiry relates is required by section 8 of the Taxes Management Act 1970 (c. 9) to make a return, with the day on which the return becomes final (or, if both of the persons are so required and their returns become final on different days, with the later of those days), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) in any other case, one year after the beginning of the relevant section 17 date. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) “ **The relevant section 18 decision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]** ” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) in a case in which a decision must be made under subsection (6) of section 18 in relation to the person or persons and the tax year to which the enquiry relates, that decision, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) in any other case, the decision under subsection (1) of that section in relation to the person or persons and that tax year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(6) “ **The relevant section 17 date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]** ” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) in a case in which a statement may be made by the person or persons in response to provision included by virtue of subsection (8) of section 17 in the notice given to him or them under that section in relation to the tax year, the date specified in the notice for the purposes of that subsection, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) in any other case, the date specified for the purposes of subsection (4) of that section in the notice given to him or them under that section in relation to the tax year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(7) A return becomes final— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) if it is enquired into under section 9A of the Taxes Management Act 1970 (c. 9), when the enquiries are completed (within the meaning of section 28A of that Act), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) otherwise, at the end of the period specified in subsection (2) of that section in relation to the return. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(8) An enquiry is completed at the time when the Board give notice to the person or persons of their decision under subsection (3); but if the Board give notice to the persons at different times the enquiry is completed at the later of those times. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(9) The person, or either of the persons, to whom the enquiry relates may at any time before such notice is given apply for a direction that the Board must give such a notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(10)[^key-72ddae14497ca8c89af9414193eca4a4] Any such application is to be subject to the relevant provisions of Part 5 of the Taxes Management Act 1970 (see, in particular, section 48(2)(b) of that Act), and the tribunal must give the direction applied for unless satisfied that the Board have reasonable grounds for not making the decision or giving the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(11) Where the entitlement of a person, or the joint entitlement of persons, to a tax credit for a tax year has been enquired into under this section, it is not to be the subject of a further notice under subsection (1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(12) Subject to *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] sections 20,[^M_F_8517dfb9-d577-49f0-d92c-c77e1f87200f] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] 21A, 21B and 21C[^key-6cb997a0e2d1f9c10da47de2d1fa9a0c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] and regulations under section 21 (and to any appeal), a decision under subsection (3) in relation to a person or persons and a tax credit for a tax year is conclusive as to the entitlement of the person, or the joint entitlement of the persons, to the tax credit for the ax year and the amount of the tax credit to which he was entitled, or they were jointly entitled, for the tax year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
