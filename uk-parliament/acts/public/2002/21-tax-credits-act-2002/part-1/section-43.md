# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 43

## 43. Polygamous marriages (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Regulations may make provision for this Part to apply in relation to persons who are parties to polygamous marriages subject to prescribed modifications. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) A person is a party to a polygamous marriage if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) he is a party to a marriage entered into under a law which permits polygamy, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) either party to the marriage has a spouse additional to the other party. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
