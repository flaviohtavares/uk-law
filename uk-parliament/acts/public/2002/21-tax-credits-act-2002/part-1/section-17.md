## 17. Final notice (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17823021][^M_C_2390da2a-473c-4c0f-bbfe-d3a61730ec5f][^key-7bedb342d8b31fc77b7ca51e8b2c13db][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a tax credit has been awarded for the whole or part of a tax year— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) for awards made on single claims, the Board must give a notice relating to the tax year to the person to whom the tax credit was awarded, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) for awards made on joint claims, the Board must give such a notice to the persons to whom the tax credit was awarded (with separate copies of the notice for each of them if the Board consider appropriate). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) The notice must either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) require that the person or persons must, by the date specified for the purposes of this subsection, declare that the relevant circumstances were as specified or state any respects in which they were not, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) inform the person or persons that he or they will be treated as having declared in response to the notice that the relevant circumstances were as specified unless, by that date, he states or they state any respects in which they were not. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) “**Relevant circumstances *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**” means circumstances (other than income) affecting— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) the entitlement of the person, or joint entitlement of the persons, to the tax credit, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) the amount of the tax credit to which he was entitled, or they were jointly entitled, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

for the tax year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) The notice must either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) require that the person or persons must, by the date specified for the purposes of this subsection, declare that the amount of the current year income or estimated current year income (depending on which is specified) was the amount, or fell within the range, specified or comply with subsection (5), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) inform the person or persons that he or they will be treated as having declared in response to the notice that the amount of the current year income or estimated current year income (depending on which is specified) was the amount, or fell within the range, specified unless, by that date, he complies or they comply with subsection (5). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) To comply with this subsection the person or persons must either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) state the current year income or his or their estimate of the current year income (making clear which), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) declare that, throughout the period to which the award related, subsection (1) of section 7 did not apply to him or them by virtue of subsection (2) of that section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(6) The notice may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) require that the person or persons must, by the date specified for the purposes of subsection (4), declare that the amount of the previous year income was the amount, or fell within the range, specified or comply with subsection (7), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) inform the person or persons that he or they will be treated as having declared in response to the notice that the amount of the previous year income was the amount, or fell within the range, specified unless, by that date, he complies or they comply with subsection (7). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(7) To comply with this subsection the person or persons must either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) state the previous year income, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) make the declaration specified in subsection (5)(b). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(8) The notice must inform the person or persons that if he or they— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) makes or make a declaration under paragraph (a) of subsection (4), or is or are treated as making a declaration under paragraph (b) of that subsection, in relation to estimated current year income (or the range within which estimated current year income fell), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) states or state under subsection (5)(a) his or their estimate of the current year income, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

he or they will be treated as having declared in response to the notice that the amount of the (actual) current year income was as estimated unless, by the date specified for the purposes of this subsection, he states or they state the current year income. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(9) “**Specified *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]**”, in relation to a notice, means specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(10) Regulations may— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) provide that, in prescribed circumstances, one person may act for another in response to a notice under this section, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) provide that, in prescribed circumstances, anything done by one member of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] couple[^key-521f7e04342433938e8918cfa563b023] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] in response to a notice given under this section is to be treated as also done by the other member of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] the couple[^key-15c04a9e0c7be01d10806e2498bb22d9] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e].
