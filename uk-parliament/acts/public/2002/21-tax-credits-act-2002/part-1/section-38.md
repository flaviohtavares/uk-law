## 38. Appeals (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17824451][^M_C_c366d9af-c9c9-47f3-f73b-fc5c4bfa93b7][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) An appeal may be brought against— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) a decision under section 14(1), 15(1), 16(1), 19(3) or 20(1) or (4) or regulations under section 21, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) the relevant section 18 decision in relation to a person or persons and a tax credit for a tax year and any revision of that decision under that section, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (c) a determination of a penalty under paragraph 1 of Schedule 2, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] [^key-edb47deffd3f90506680dd1bc98c19b8]...
- (ca (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-c513a36121d87cd4972b0a947e7aefe9][^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] a decision under section 36A or 36C that working tax credit is not payable (or is not payable for a particular period), and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (d) a decision under section 37(1). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(1B)[^key-7ead65dd444ec13c8b327b81f6f11f01] If in any case the conclusion of a review under section 21A *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] or 21C[^key-0b3e57870b9a865ecf638dcf87411ef7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] is to uphold the decision reviewed, an appeal by virtue of subsection (1) in that case may be brought only against the original decision. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(1C) If in any case the conclusion of a review under section 21A *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] or 21C[^key-eed30793ad87788a8235b2b7e0d52746] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] is to vary the decision reviewed, an appeal by virtue of subsection (1) in that case may be brought only against the decision as varied. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) “ **The relevant section 18 decision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]** ” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) in a case in which a decision must be made under subsection (6) of section 18 in relation to the person or persons and the tax credit for the tax year, that decision, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) in any other case, the decision under subsection (1) of that section in relation to the person or persons and the tax credit for the tax year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
