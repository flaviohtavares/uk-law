# Tax Credits Act 2002, section 31

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (18).** Text as at 2023-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 31. Incorrect statements (affected by repeal, see note) etc. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c17823781][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e][^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a person fraudulently or negligently— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) makes an incorrect statement or declaration in or in connection with a claim for a tax credit or a notification of a change of circumstances given in accordance with regulations under section 6 or in response to a notice under section 17, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) gives incorrect information or evidence in response to a requirement imposed on him by virtue of section 14(2), 15(2), 16(3), 18(10) or 19(2) or regulations under section 25 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e] or in response to a notification under section 21A(5)[^c-7953afdb92ee3d4e237e96d37f7dae95] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e],

a penalty not exceeding £3,000 may be imposed on him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(2) Where a person liable to a penalty under subsection (1) is a person making, or who has made, a claim for a tax credit for a period jointly with another and the penalty is imposed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) under paragraph (a) of that subsection in respect of the claim, a notification relating to the tax credit claimed or a notice relating to the tax credit awarded on the claim, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) under paragraph (b) of that subsection in respect of a requirement imposed on him with respect to the tax credit for the period, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

a penalty of an amount not exceeding £3,000 may be imposed on the other person unless subsection (3) applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(3) This subsection applies if the other person was not, and could not reasonably have been expected to have been, aware that the person liable to the penalty under subsection (1) had fraudulently or negligently made the incorrect statement or declaration or given the incorrect information or evidence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(4) Where penalties are imposed under subsections (1) and (2) in respect of the same statement, declaration, information or evidence, their aggregate amount must not exceed £3,000. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

(5) Where a person acts for another— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

- (a) in or in connection with a claim or notification referred to in subsection (1), or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
- (b) in response to a notice so referred to, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]

subsection (1) applies to him (as well as to any person to whom it applies apart from this subsection). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-a2531265b08f7ca2190513c24c66a27e]
