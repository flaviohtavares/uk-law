# Enterprise Act 2002, section 205

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 205. Super-complaints to regulators other than CMA[^c-2f83fa58bb40dedaf4c4bb84d752c99c]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Secretary of State may by order provide that section 11 is to apply to complaints made to a specified regulator in relation to a market of a specified description as it applies to complaints made to the CMA[^c-9898a70cbc160522da858066edf919f4], with such modifications as may be specified.

(2) An order under this section—

- (a) shall be made by statutory instrument, and
- (b) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(3) In this section—

- “**regulator**” has the meaning given in section 54(1) of the 1998 Act; and
- “**specified**” means specified in the order.
