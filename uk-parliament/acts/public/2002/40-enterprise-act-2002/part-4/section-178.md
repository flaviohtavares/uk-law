## 178. Minority reports of CMA: Part 4[^c-6b6194b1991fa57d69d635f176340d18]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsection (2) applies where, on a market investigation reference, a member of a group constituted in connection with the reference [^c-b2b1b8081a3bd49f47862197cedcaed1]..., disagrees with any decisions contained in the report of the CMA[^c-e276b99e85dfa4cb0d36b3efd54b20dc] under this Part as the decisions of the CMA.

(2) The report shall, if the member so wishes, include a statement of his disagreement and of his reasons for disagreeing.
