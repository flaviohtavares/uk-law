# Enterprise Act 2002, section 171

## 171. Advice and information: Part 4

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) [^c-95239d53427030001eeb7f3a6ab0dd71]... The CMA[^c-ae59bb3c069a3cc78f1af87ad1b29dfa] shall prepare and publish general advice and information about —[^c-ed6bbd0068c2825a6e2c5b1ea3d65c1f]

- (a) the making and consideration by it of market investigation references, and
- (b) the way in which relevant customer benefits may affect the taking of enforcement action in relation to such references.

(2) The CMA[^c-cbb94098e7e4119d683530542dcbad84] may at any time publish revised, or new, advice or information.

(3)[^c-339f9d6a2937894e456584ed964bcaae] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-339f9d6a2937894e456584ed964bcaae] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) Advice and information published under this section shall be prepared with a view to—

- (a) explaining relevant provisions of this Part to persons who are likely to be affected by them; and
- (b) indicating how the CMA[^c-50656b1fc3ffaae1494b1fe08d1dbe41] expects such provisions to operate.

(6)[^c-99195b271bc5b16885988f94d1c37ff5] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7) Advice (or information) published by virtue of subsection (1) [^c-1d226a5cf44f3fc31682a73edf9fed7d]... may include advice (or information) about the factors which the CMA[^c-53261f6f1665dfe6b8fd079d44fe841e] may take into account in considering whether, and if so how, to exercise a function conferred by this Part.

(8) Any advice or information published by the CMA[^c-4fbb6b2ea6c89c8a574f0c5cc2cf6125] under this section shall be published in such manner as the CMA[^c-f3061968955df8feb9b5979c4a05c1b5] considers appropriate.

(9) In preparing any advice or information under this section, the CMA shall consult such persons[^c-fde7732a3b93fa5b1e934b4416d6ae35] and such other persons as it considers appropriate.

(10)[^c-ddd75083d35ade45538042bdd98d8cdb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(11)[^c-56a26a62fbc08ca625fd36974d77a761] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
