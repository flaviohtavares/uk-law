## 161. Final orders: Part 4

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The CMA[^c-f12faf2df7ad9877c22fe817ca011939] may, in accordance with section 138, make an order under this section.

(2) The Secretary of State may, in accordance with section 147 or (as the case may be) 147A[^c-736e75ee42053ed42974854650a7ad47], make an order under this section.

(3) An order under this section may contain—

- (a)[^c16742821][^c16742911][^c16743691][^c16945851][^c16945911] anything permitted by Schedule 8; and
- (b) such supplementary, consequential or incidental provision as the person making it considers appropriate.

(4) An order under this section—

- (a) shall come into force at such time as is determined by or under the order; and
- (b) may be varied or revoked by another order.

(5)[^c-127298b967ea48f1dca6e3c00d450b6b] The Secretary of State shall not vary or revoke an order made by him under this section unless the CMA[^c-46d92d170dbc7da7d7e63bd226463fe6] advises , in accordance with section 162(3) or section 162A(8),[^c-4ae6417eed682b9530079ef9ef694881] that such a variation or revocation is appropriate [^c-91b86f1a2f0a30b8f8ca93f17ba5cda7]....
