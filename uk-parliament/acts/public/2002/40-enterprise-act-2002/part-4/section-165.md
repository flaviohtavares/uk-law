## 165. Procedural requirements for certain undertakings and orders: Part 4

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Schedule 10 (procedural requirements for certain undertakings and orders), other than paragraph 9 of that Schedule, shall apply in relation to undertakings under section 159 or 161C[^key-e29edca22ca1af8928bb0d81571d39ac] and orders under section 160 , 161 or 161C[^key-a6fe0df6c95a89f9ea7aa0b47854401f] as it applies in relation to undertakings under section 82 and orders under section 83 or 84.
