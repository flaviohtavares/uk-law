## 135. Variation of market investigation references[^key-09d06afbc2cbc93b9b881fd8f77500df][^key-fed6fdf2935d8405c3bbcae834e6b94e][^key-6c0879fc326fa811b7bc2f4671be5778][^key-0959ef6758f345563a2350268d6e2a4a][^key-f6c728f2e5d7a606127d48ec5368a69f][^key-90a8de1f94ac259959e6ab4148c7cff5][^key-4b49b26b1471a40198c989e3d7c8e333][^key-62783f861881363501a5f386e6d11a03][^key-a0230f567bb67f0457bb47d1f8ff5d19]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The CMA[^key-6b5d9c3b583124b8a93e22b93626fac6] or (as the case may be) the appropriate Minister may at any time vary a market investigation reference made by it under section 131 or (as the case may be) by the appropriate Minister under section 132[^key-584c8f45e975470da5521afe18cd09dd].

(2) The Office of Fair Trading[^key-873b9c7cc629e8e03c9ab592f4a51d76]... appropriate Minister shall consult the CMA[^key-c608a7590e0a2613720e1732b119ce7d] before varying any such reference made by him[^key-d71947fed74b0cac9d9dcb397b4b28d9].

(3) Subsection (2) shall not apply if the CMA[^key-d5ad7db5a022297f02bd22620fe4d55a] has requested the variation concerned.
