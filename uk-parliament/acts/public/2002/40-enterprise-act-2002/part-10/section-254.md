# Enterprise Act 2002, section 254

>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 254. Application of insolvency law to foreign company[^c19222041][^c-3d842c8db810ca7ab23dc24ffef88377][^c-e9664b061f0588d07a3d4f6e22218d28][^c-b283ec8603f3441defcaa00f1bbac579][^c-b3111c661c78d471143ce04dceae541b][^c-6c22cb85e0befedcc32dc1ed716687c6][^c-6f3d06e9566da3f507f2799611dc77e5]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The Secretary of State may by order provide for a provision of the Insolvency Act 1986 to apply (with or without modification) in relation to a company incorporated outside Great Britain.

(2) An order under this section—

- (a) may make provision generally or for a specified purpose only,
- (b) may make different provision for different purposes, and
- (c) may make transitional, consequential or incidental provision.

(3) An order under this section—

- (a) must be made by statutory instrument, and
- (b) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.
