# Enterprise Act 2002, section 253

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/40

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 253. Liquidator’s powers

*Extent: E+W+S*

The following shall be inserted in Part I of Schedule 4 to the Insolvency Act 1986 (c. 45) (liquidator’s powers in winding up: powers exercisable only with sanction) after paragraph 3—

> **3A.**
>
> Power to bring legal proceedings under section 213, 214, 238, 239, 242, 243 or 423.
