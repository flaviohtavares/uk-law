# Enterprise Act 2002, section 82

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/40

## 82. Final undertakings

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The CMA[^key-eb42a0aba6c781a1f4c172a3895244dd] may, in accordance with section 41, accept, from such persons as it considers appropriate, undertakings to take action specified or described in the undertakings.

(2) An undertaking under this section—

- (a) shall come into force when accepted;
- (b) may be varied or superseded by another undertaking; and
- (c) may be released by the CMA.

(3) An undertaking which is in force under this section in relation to a reference under section 22 , 33, 68B or 68C[^key-30a07c5033fb050f2dcc57485f7e06db] shall cease to be in force if an order under section 76(1)(b) or 83 comes into force in relation to the subject-matter of the undertaking.

(4) No undertaking shall be accepted under this section in relation to a reference under section 22 , 33, 68B or 68C[^key-7a48db23661ed5c04e08770201d14753] if an order has been made under—

- (a) section 76(1)(b) or 83 in relation to the subject-matter of the undertaking; or
- (b) section 84 in relation to that reference.

(5) The CMA[^key-96497c275cd5a3ff4e2332d1c0041e08] shall, as soon as reasonably practicable, consider any representations received by it in relation to varying or releasing an undertaking under this section.
