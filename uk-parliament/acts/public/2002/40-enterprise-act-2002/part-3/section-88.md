# Enterprise Act 2002, section 88

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/40

## 88. Contents of certain enforcement orders[^c16774731][^c19016441][^key-944ef22cbb5236166176fea379487a32]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies in relation to any order under section 70C,[^key-7198a5c7845ea21390d1e52a14ef0bb8] 75, 83 or 84 or under paragraph 5, 10 or 11 of Schedule 7.

(2) The order or any explanatory material accompanying the order shall state—

- (a) the actions that the persons or description of persons to whom the order is addressed must do or (as the case may be) refrain from doing;
- (b) the date on which the order comes into force;
- (c) the possible consequences of not complying with the order; and
- (d) the section of this Part under which a review can be sought in relation to the order.
