## 96. Merger notices[^key-a1ca3fb6bab80dc78ce3a2acc2f2da29]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person authorised to do so by regulations under section 101 may give notice to the CMA[^key-f46516e3e763d768760d5cbed8d2e8f5] of arrangements or proposed arrangements which might have resulted or might result in the creation of a relevant merger situation[^key-6738814ab137c3f0a2992158fee3b59f].

(2) Any such notice (in this Part a “**merger notice**”)—

- (a) shall be in the prescribed form;
- (aa)[^key-63e1f2ac6f4ce60d7fb17b0d92b681d5] shall contain the prescribed information; and
- (b) shall state that the existence of the proposal has been made public.

(2A)[^key-afd62c1b90f0cb0ec2c4265d2b137875] Where the CMA is satisfied that a merger notice meets the requirements of subsection (2), it shall give notice to that effect to the person who gave the merger notice.

(3)[^key-79cff346a8f331d41cec54b9043e3075] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^key-79cff346a8f331d41cec54b9043e3075] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) In this section and sections 99(5)(c) and 100(1)(c) “**prescribed**” means prescribed by the CMA[^key-b0d538b5bb1dd1caab156ffa6c041f41] by notice having effect for the time being and published online[^key-dec30aba10e5bef805d13331694549b0].

(6) In this Part “**notified arrangements**” means arrangements of which notice is given under subsection (1) above or arrangements not differing from them in any material respect.
