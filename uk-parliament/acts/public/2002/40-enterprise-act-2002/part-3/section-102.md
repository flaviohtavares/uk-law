# Enterprise Act 2002, section 102

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Power to modify sections 97 to 101[^key-a1ca3fb6bab80dc78ce3a2acc2f2da29]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The Secretary of State may, for the purposes of determining the effect of giving a merger notice and the action which may be or is to be taken by any person in connection with such a notice, by order modify sections 97 to 101.
