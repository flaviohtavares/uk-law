# Enterprise Act 2002, section 106

## 106. Advice and information about references under section 22, 33, 68B or 68C[^c-a9c7b263771af8cda6fa67a4ca055d60]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) [^c-f03bae78cc17d647ba3ee10c016a2f08]... The CMA[^c-4878885bc87ca14751d0a40e011dbad5] shall prepare and publish general advice and information about—[^c-06dec1d075efa18b61171e3384590195]

- (a) the making and consideration by it of references under section 22 , 33, 68B or 68C[^c-3bc1b2140ae065e230014a4677a087dc], and
- (b) the way in which relevant customer benefits may affect the taking of enforcement action in relation to such references.

(2) The CMA[^c-4d4bea570562ccd9be0e323f525ce49d] may at any time publish revised, or new, advice or information.

(3)[^c-bf055814ccb3eae0384b0fde61d7da56] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-bf055814ccb3eae0384b0fde61d7da56] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) Advice and information published under this section shall be prepared with a view to—

- (a) explaining relevant provisions of this Part to persons who are likely to be affected by them; and
- (b) indicating how the CMA[^c-6654f4590df0dbfe83823dc2cb3c25c2] or (as the case may be) the Commission expects such provisions to operate.

(6) Advice (or information) published by virtue of subsection (1) [^c-e61cc5321433a3d7e4e0f385a4297135]... may include advice (or information) about the factors which the CMA[^c-40a48dfd7ff63071c04713aba0386695] may take into account in considering whether, and if so how, to exercise a function conferred by this Part.

(7) Any advice or information published by the CMA[^c-9370f7a9ba810e1b750dda26135384f3] under this section shall be published in such manner as the CMA[^c-0ff5bbb6ce8f374b6ef864dc564cdc9a] considers appropriate.

(8) In preparing any advice or information under this section, the CMA shall consult such persons[^c-da462df7da320ec5a402dcbb66e32ba0] as it considers appropriate.

(9)[^c-1454073408fe24c6d566009174730df7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
