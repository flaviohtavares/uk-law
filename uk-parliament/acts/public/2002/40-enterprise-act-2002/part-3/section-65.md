# Enterprise Act 2002, section 65

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/40

## 65. Investigations and reports on references under section 62

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The CMA[^M_F_9820b119-3fea-4a4c-f0ab-938e712ccc6d] shall prepare a report on a reference under section 62 and give it to the Secretary of State within the period permitted by virtue of this section.

(2) The report shall, in particular, contain—

- (a) the decisions of the CMA on the questions which it is required to answer by virtue of section 63;
- (b) its reasons for its decisions; and
- (c) such information as the CMA considers appropriate for facilitating a proper understanding of those questions and of its reasons for its decisions.

(2A)[^c19082861] Where the report relates to a reference under section 62 which has been made after a report of OFCOM under section 61A, the CMA shall give a copy of its report (whether or not published) to OFCOM.

(3) Sections 51 and 52 (but not section 53) shall apply for the purposes of a report under this section as they apply for the purposes of a report under section 50.

(4) The CMA[^c-ff0ec50f7b993e5fe77e6b9cc5409903] shall carry out such investigations as it considers appropriate for the purpose of producing a report under this section.
