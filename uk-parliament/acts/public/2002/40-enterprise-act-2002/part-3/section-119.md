# Enterprise Act 2002, section 119

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 119. Minority reports of CMA[^c19020681][^c-a1ca3fb6bab80dc78ce3a2acc2f2da29][^c-4bcb29df7f3a754a4ee0773ae6b02be0]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsection (2) applies where, on a reference to the CMA[^c-761d4d46b0be4b134c9e37a1af3eb652] under this Part, a member of a group constituted in connection with the reference [^c-ae7875c35e69f5e46f64b2965f38991f]..., disagrees with any decisions contained in the report of the CMA under this Part as the decisions of the CMA.

(2) The report shall, if the member so wishes, include a statement of his disagreement and of his reasons for disagreeing.
