## 115. Recovery of penalties[^c16944011][^c16943941][^c16943751][^c16943711][^c16943691][^c16943671][^c16943631][^c16943611][^c16943571][^c16943551][^c16943531][^c16941341][^c16941321][^c16940761][^c16940711][^c16940121][^c16939881][^c16939441][^c16937941][^c16937741][^c16786211][^c16785661][^c16751651][^c19020581][^key-a9655cdf8bbc175a9dbcb7f043c6c30f][^key-1c1d344a34204236743291403ecac051][^key-515563403340ba04eff2c2727330186f][^key-0fd7bbcabc209272c99a66e46423e7fd][^key-a1ca3fb6bab80dc78ce3a2acc2f2da29][^key-b6c29fe02e80e6f6e5ca6be3968c5d3b][^key-e0be43131ddeaae1733d28ecf890890d][^key-d931f15ed6ec80def169610f8e062638][^key-bc50115c508d828f86fee1b1ae25be80][^key-79d414bd30478efbc22db163b2ae5a16][^key-d071c64c2d9237e1771ae1b37f941137][^key-7755cb347bc1777cc10a04ee5604320f][^key-a1ca3fb6bab80dc78ce3a2acc2f2da29]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a penalty imposed under section 110(1) or (1A)[^key-9c37ddc6fdbe25f76b6faaaab9b882e0], or any portion of such a penalty, has not been paid by the date on which it is required to be paid and—

- (a) no application relating to the penalty has been made under section 114 during the period within which such an application may be made, or
- (b) any such application which has been made has been determined, withdrawn or otherwise dealt with,

the appropriate authority[^key-92de9f213a2421ae395c97e13dba6645] may recover from the person on whom the penalty was imposed any of the penalty and any interest which has not been paid; and in England and Wales and Northern Ireland such penalty and interest may be recovered as a civil debt due to the appropriate authority.
