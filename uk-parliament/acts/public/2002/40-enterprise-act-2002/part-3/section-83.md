## 83. Order-making power where final undertakings not fulfilled

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subsection (2) applies where the CMA[^key-801d893a8af3b86d987e4186ccb3ee48] considers that—

- (a) an undertaking accepted by it under section 82 has not been, is not being or will not be fulfilled; or
- (b) in relation to an undertaking accepted by it under that section, information which was false or misleading in a material respect was given to the CMA[^key-9c221c6fce9a8d5cb05ad0e592201892] by the person giving the undertaking before the CMA decided[^key-115c6e312b0587c5a21ed5c12c2d52d0] to accept the undertaking.

(2) The CMA[^key-d60e303681d9fccd42c0a3e3fd5987a3] may, for any of the purposes mentioned in section 41(2), make an order under this section.

(3) Subsections (3) to (5) of section 41 shall apply for the purposes of subsection (2) above as they apply for the purposes of subsection (2) of that section.

(4) An order under this section may contain—

- (a)[^c16742781][^c16742871][^c16743651] anything permitted by Schedule 8; and
- (b) such supplementary, consequential or incidental provision as the CMA[^key-ec55d663bcc9d4cc8dd6319df62dbc20] considers appropriate.

(5) An order under this section—

- (a) shall come into force at such time as is determined by or under the order;
- (b) may contain provision which is different from the provision contained in the undertaking concerned; and
- (c) may be varied or revoked by another order.
