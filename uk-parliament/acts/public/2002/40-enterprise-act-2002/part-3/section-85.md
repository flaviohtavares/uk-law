# Enterprise Act 2002, section 85

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/40

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 85. Enforcement regime for public interest and special public interest cases[^c-944ef22cbb5236166176fea379487a32]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Schedule 7 (which provides for the enforcement regime in public interest and special public interest cases) shall have effect.

(2) The CMA[^c-417b5f0fc05061896b2bba078d174a5f] may advise the Secretary of State or the Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster[^c-fdc7f1c006d64ffec35bfa654d6f5b0f] in relation to the taking by either of them[^c-5890dd904a72f6904a9e20d9a38158a1] of enforcement action under Schedule 7.
