# Enterprise Act 2002, section 89

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/40
>

## 89. Acceptance and subject-matter of undertakings[^c19016461][^key-944ef22cbb5236166176fea379487a32][^key-5d44259b5db44ff851080b41569408aa]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(A1)[^key-7ec671aba1f9d68e7df7bc9488f55868] An appropriate authority may not accept an enforcement undertaking from a person unless it has provided the person with information about the possible consequences of failing to comply with the undertaking.

(1)[^c16742801][^c16742891][^c16743671] The provision which may be contained in an enforcement undertaking is not limited to the provision which is permitted by Schedule 8.

(2) In this Part “**enforcement undertaking**” means an undertaking under section [^key-ad5bcd962470ed2761f622956ad2b0dd]... 73, 80 or 82 or under paragraph [^key-c893fc6f37cef97f6b7bfb358aadfa96]... 3 or 9 of Schedule 7.

(3)[^key-65dd294476d600f3ed1e582cfafdd0ac] In this section, “**appropriate authority**” has the same meaning as in section 94AA.
