# Enterprise Act 2002, section 91

## 91. Register of undertakings and orders[^c19016481][^c19083341][^c-944ef22cbb5236166176fea379487a32]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The CMA[^c-2294ebeb7772a1c3471a07db44f37f2d] shall compile and maintain a register for the purposes of this Part.

(2) The register shall be kept in such form as the CMA considers appropriate.

(3)[^c16774771] The CMA[^c-b91e12a6487a5a7591b24bf2262080ad] shall ensure that the following matters are entered in the register—

- (a) the provisions of any enforcement undertaking accepted under this Part;
- (b) the provisions of any enforcement order made under this Part;
- (c) the details of any variation, release or revocation of such an undertaking or order; and
- (d) the details of any consent given by the CMA[^c-7e31a2c72ceb5596a55e68e9610b8760] under section 77(2) or (3) or 78(2) or by the Secretary of State under paragraph 7(2) or (3) or 8(2) of Schedule 7.

(4) The duty in subsection (3) does not extend to anything of which the CMA[^c-9d57a186379ef87dfda7741e23a86532] is unaware.

(5) The Secretary of State[^c-acf14640a1117f841fb574cc21b42e0c] shall inform the CMA[^c-169ac10a71857ab521cbf591507f1cce] of any matters which are to be included in the register by virtue of subsection (3) and which relate to enforcement undertakings accepted by the Secretary of State[^c-f442836f4466c6429ef819800505d665], enforcement orders made by them or consents given by the Secretary of State.

(6) The CMA[^c-589d25fd2be32acff7c34b09fc304582] shall ensure that the contents of the register are available to the public—

- (a) during (as a minimum) such hours as may be specified in an order made by the Secretary of State; and
- (b) subject to such reasonable fees (if any) as the CMA may determine.

(7) If requested by any person to do so and subject to such reasonable fees (if any) as the CMA may determine, the CMA shall supply the person concerned with a copy (certified to be true) of the register or of an extract from it.

(8)[^c-863e710fdde1e204c11138eb7edee1ad] In subsection (5), “**Secretary of State**” includes the Chancellor of the Duchy of Lancaster.
