# Enterprise Act 2002, section 57

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (3).** Text as at 2026-10-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 57. Duties of CMA and OFCOM to inform Secretary of State[^key-7eeba85da5a67f13cec8a7b61b4a5e41]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The CMA[^key-491347a4d5ee52714134636437bf146e] shall, in considering whether to make a reference under section 22, 33, 68B or 68C[^key-fb5c49334301d6536fe27e4f750ac8a4], bring to the attention of the Secretary of State any case which it believes raises any consideration specified in section 58 unless it believes that the Secretary of State would consider any such consideration immaterial in the context of the particular case.

(2) The CMA and[^key-71cf7a964010cb6c6f95666d09e26fda] OFCOM shall bring to the attention of the Secretary of State any representations about exercising his powers under section 58(3) which have been made to the CMA or (as the case may be)[^key-aeaf5492c3283e68cfc90426e407e0d9] OFCOM.
