# National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002, section 25B

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2024-12-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 25B. Power of the Authority to advise regulatory bodies etc.[^c-89e8e60133956ff281aab76c94556376]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Authority may, for the purpose of assisting the Authority in its performance of its functions under this group of sections, provide advice or provide auditing services to—

- (a) a regulatory body;
- (b) a body which has functions (whether or not relating to health or social care) corresponding to those of a regulatory body.

(2) A body to which the Authority provides advice or auditing services under this section must pay such fee as the Authority may determine.

(3) In this section, “**this group of sections**” has the meaning given by section 25(5) but does not include section 26A.
