# National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002, section 28

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2024-12-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/17

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Complaints about regulatory bodies[^c836821]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Privy Council[^key-b79480b6450024dfdefa9b01f4cb51f3] may make provision in regulations about the investigation by the Authority[^key-671c89243789697267434a0b1ee6bbf8] of complaints made to it about the way in which a regulatory body other than Social Work England[^key-e754370d01418320077c0984bfef5eff] has exercised any of its functions.

(2) The regulations may, in particular, make provision as to—

- (a) who (or what description of person) is entitled to complain,
- (b) the nature of complaints which the Authority[^key-d276f4e0e29a58218dd4608485326404] must (or need not) investigate,
- (c) matters which are excluded from investigation,
- (d) requirements to be complied with by a person who makes a complaint,
- (e) the procedure to be followed by the Authority[^key-22c525cd4e560e1086b5284851e97dbc] in investigating complaints,
- (f) the making of recommendations or reports by the Authority following investigations,
- (g) the confidentiality, or disclosure, of any information supplied to the Authority or acquired by it in connection with an investigation,
- (h) the use which the Authority may make of any such information,
- (i) the making of payments to any persons in connection with investigations,
- (j) privilege in relation to any matter published by the Authority[^key-007f91361f4c0c6795e0df3d8b9452b8] in the exercise of its functions under the regulations.

(3) The regulations may also make provision—

- (a) empowering the Authority[^key-7cf10490563678a14ad95f8bf816b764] to require persons to attend before it,
- (b) empowering the Authority to require persons to give evidence or produce documents to it,
- (c) about the admissibility of evidence,
- (d) enabling the Authority[^key-07f8b8b80c6f5de13ab6838e2862d6fa] to administer oaths.

(4) No person shall be required by or by virtue of regulations under this section to give any evidence or produce any document or other material to the Authority[^key-70155c1628b2fb90d5eccc912e660256] which he could not be compelled to give or produce in civil proceedings before the High Court or, in Scotland, the Court of Session.
