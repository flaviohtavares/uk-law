# National Health Service Reform and Health Care Professions Act 2002, section 41

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2024-12-13. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2002/17
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 41. Financial provisions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any expenditure incurred by the Secretary of State in consequence of this Act, and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable out of money so provided by virtue of any other Act.
