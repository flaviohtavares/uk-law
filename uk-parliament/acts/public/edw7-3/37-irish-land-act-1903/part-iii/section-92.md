# Irish Land Act 1903, section 92

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 92. Amendment of 44 & 45 Vict. c.49 s.50(3) as to time within which address may be presented

The period within which an address may be presented in respect of rules under section fifty of the Act of 1881, or under that section as amended by any enactment, shall be forty days instead of one hundred days.
