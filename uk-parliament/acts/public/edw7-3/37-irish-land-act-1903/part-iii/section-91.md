# Irish Land Act 1903, section 91

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 91. Subdivided holdings

In the construction of subsection three of section five of the Act of 1896, the words " tenants in common " shall be deemed to include two or more persons between whom the holding is divided.
