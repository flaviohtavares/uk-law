# Irish Land Act 1903, section 68

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 68. Extension of 59 & 60 Vict. c.47 s.29(2) to guarantee deposits under any of Purchase Act

(1) Subsection two of section twenty-nine of the Act of, 1896 (which enables the Land Commission to nay the person entitled thereto the whole or any part of a guarantee deposit) shall apply to any such deposit made or retained in respect of advances under any of the Land Purchase Acts.

(2) Subsection three of the said section twenty-nine is hereby repealed.
