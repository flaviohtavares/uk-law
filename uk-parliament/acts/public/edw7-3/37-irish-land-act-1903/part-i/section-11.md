# Irish Land Act 1903, section 11

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 11. As to guarantee deposit

No guarantee deposit shall be made or retained in respect of an advance made in pursuance of the foregoing provisions of this Act.
