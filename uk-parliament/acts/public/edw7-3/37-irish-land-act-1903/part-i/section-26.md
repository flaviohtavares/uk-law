# Irish Land Act 1903, section 26

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 26. Sale of lunatic's estate

Where a person who would otherwise be entitled to sell land under the Land Purchase Acts is a lunatic, the Lord Chancellor may order the land to be sold as if the sale was required for one of the purposes mentioned in section sixty-three of the [^m00011]Lunacy Regulation (Ireland) Act, 1871, and that section shall apply accordingly.
