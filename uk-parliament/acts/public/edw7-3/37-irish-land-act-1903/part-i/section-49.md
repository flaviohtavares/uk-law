# Irish Land Act 1903, section 49

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 49. Registration fees

No fee shall be payable in the Local Registration of Title Office on the registration of the ownership of any land purchased by the Land Commission or the Congested Districts Board, or for any land certificate issued to the Commission or Board, or any purchaser from them.
