# Irish Land Act 1903, section 31

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 31. Transfer of stock

The stock shall be transferable in the books of the Bank of England and the Bank of Ireland in like manner as other stock transferable under the [^m00012]National Debt Act, 1870, and shall be subject to the provisions of that Act, and any enactment amending that Act, so far as is consistent with the tenor of this Act.
