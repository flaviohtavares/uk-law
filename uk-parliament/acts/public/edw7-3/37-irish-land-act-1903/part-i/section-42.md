# Irish Land Act 1903, section 42

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 42. Power to invest in stock

All persons, including the National Debt Commissioners, shall have the like power of investing in the stock as they have in consolidated stock.
