# Irish Land Act 1903, section 10

> This is the text as originally enacted or made. It may have been changed since; check legislation.gov.uk for the current version.

## 10. Exclusion of certain estates

No estate shall be purchased by the Land Commission which is not in the main agricultural or pastoral.
