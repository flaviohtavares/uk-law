# Children Act 1989, section 23A

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 23A. The responsible authority and relevant children.[^c15162121]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The responsible local authority shall have the functions set out in section 23B in respect of a relevant child.

(2)[^c15162131] In subsection (1) “ **relevant child** ” means (subject to subsection (3)) a child who—

- (a) is not being looked after by any local authority in England or by any local authority in Wales[^c-8526b27d2cfc8f59f310cea33fae0325];
- (b) was, before last ceasing to be looked after, an eligible child for the purposes of paragraph 19B of Schedule 2; and
- (c) is aged sixteen or seventeen.

(3) The Secretary of State[^c-542505a4493caf829bb3b50f79712746] may prescribe—

- (a) additional categories of relevant children; and
- (b) categories of children who are not to be relevant children despite falling within subsection (2).

(4) In subsection (1) the “ **responsible local authority** ” is the one which last looked after the child.

(5) If under subsection (3)(a) the Secretary of State[^c-50106601a70b2a2a643590306e593aa4] prescribes a category of relevant children which includes children who do not fall within subsection (2)(b) (for example, because they were being looked after by a local authority in Scotland), the[^c19936731] Secretary of State may in the regulations also provide for which local authority is to be the responsible local authority for those children.
