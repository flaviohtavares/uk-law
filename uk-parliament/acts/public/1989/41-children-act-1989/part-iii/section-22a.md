# Children Act 1989, section 22A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (96).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 22A. Provision of accommodation for children in care[^c-0b0a55e612f5d009c7db02bc0dd1eacf][^c19936481]

*Extent: E+W*

When a child is in the care of a local authority, it is their duty to provide the child with accommodation.
