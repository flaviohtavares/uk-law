Children Act 1989

1989 c. 41

[^c15158811][^c15158821][^c15158831][^c15158841][^c15158851][^c15158861][^c15158871][^c15158881][^c20891311][^c20891291]An Act to reform the law relating to children; to provide for local authority services for children in need and others; to amend the law with respect to children’s homes, community homes, voluntary homes and voluntary organisations; to make provision with respect to fostering, child minding and day care for young children and adoption; and for connected purposes.

\[16th November 1989\]
