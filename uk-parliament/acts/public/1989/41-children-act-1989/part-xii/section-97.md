## 97. Privacy for children involved in certain proceedings.[^c15170991][^M_C_e6bf7c28-05fe-4ff0-fd38-7f9c08c1b55c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-c05a4f7c325fd40bc19e12b654e7e7f6] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c15171021] No person shall publish to the public at large or any section of the public[^c18915381] any material which is intended, or likely, to identify—

- (a) any child as being involved in any proceedings before the High Court[^c15171031] or the family court[^key-37c59f61b2fad77bf2c1a899bd816916] in which any power under this Act or the Adoption and Children Act 2002[^c18915401] may be exercised by the court with respect to that or any other child; or
- (b) an address or school as being that of a child involved in any such proceedings.

(3) In any proceedings for an offence under this section it shall be a defence for the accused to prove that he did not know, and had no reason to suspect, that the published material was intended, or likely, to identify the child.

(4)[^c15171051] The court or the Lord Chancellor[^c15171061] may, if satisfied that the welfare of the child requires it and, in the case of the Lord Chancellor, if the Lord Chief Justice agrees[^c18915441] , by order dispense with the requirements of subsection (2) to such extent as may be specified in the order.

(5) For the purposes of this section—

- “**publish**” includes—
  - (a) include in a programme service (within the meaning of the Broadcasting Act 1990);[^c15171071]
  - (b) cause to be published; and
- “**material**” includes any picture or representation.

(6) Any person who contravenes this section shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale.

(6A)[^key-0650a7badfa99acb4f6b4f84213b15cd] It is not a contravention of this section to—

- (a) enter material in the Adoption and Children Act Register (established under section 125 of the Adoption and Children Act 2002), or
- (b) permit persons to search and inspect that register pursuant to regulations made under section 128A of that Act.

(7)[^key-a8fc0d7f6ba39430f431d68257888079] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8)[^key-a8fc0d7f6ba39430f431d68257888079] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9)[^c18915451] The Lord Chief Justice may nominate a judicial office holder (as defined in section 109(4) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005) to exercise his functions under subsection (4).
