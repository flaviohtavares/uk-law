# Children Act 1989, section 104A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (96).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/41

## 104A. Regulations made by the Welsh Ministers under section 31A or Part 7[^key-5554d40683bdeb4682dddd45dcfa1975]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Any power of the Welsh Ministers under section 31A or Part 7 to make regulations shall be exercisable by statutory instrument.

(2) Any such statutory instrument shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of the National Assembly for Wales.
