# Children Act 1989, section 65A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (96).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 65A. Appeal against refusal of authority to give consent under section 65.[^c15167321]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An appeal against a decision of an appropriate authority under section 65 shall lie to the First-tier Tribunal[^c19946081] .

(2) On an appeal the Tribunal may confirm the authority’s decision or direct it to give the consent in question.
