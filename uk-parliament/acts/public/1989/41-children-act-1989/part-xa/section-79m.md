# Children Act 1989, section 79M

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 79M. Appeals.[^c15168111]

*Extent: E+W*

**No longer in force (ended 2011-04-01).**

(1) An appeal against—

- (a) the taking of any step mentioned in section 79L(1); [^c15168121]. . .
- (b) an order under section 79K, or[^c15168131]
- (c) a determination made by the Assembly[^c18889481] under this Part (other than one falling within paragraph (a) or (b)) which is of a prescribed description,

shall lie to the First-tier[^c19947711] Tribunal.

(2) On an appeal, the First-tier Tribunal may—

- (a) confirm the taking of the step or the making of the order or determination[^c15168141] or direct that it shall not have, or shall cease to have, effect; and
- (b) impose, vary or cancel any condition.
