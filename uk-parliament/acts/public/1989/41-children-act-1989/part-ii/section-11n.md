# Children Act 1989, section 11N

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (96).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11N. Enforcement orders: warning notices[^c18897731]

*Extent: E+W*

Where the court makes an enforcement order, it is to attach to the order a notice warning of the consequences of failing to comply with the order.
