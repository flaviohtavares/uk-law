# Children Act 1989, section 13

>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 13. Change of child’s name or removal from jurisdiction.[^c15159981]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Where a child arrangements order to which subsection (4) applies[^c-aa6a164045bfcc3b691826349d4cf516] is in force with respect to a child, no person may—

- (a) cause the child to be known by a new surname; or
- (b) remove him from the United Kingdom;

without either the written consent of every person who has parental responsibility for the child or the leave of the court.

(2) Subsection (1)(b) does not prevent the removal of a child, for a period of less than one month, by a person named in the child arrangements order as a person with whom the child is to live[^c-8acddf352b2b6d47961ee903c88cef44] .

(3) In making a child arrangements order to which subsection (4) applies,[^c-36471b8b8e25c8162b08474f2a283026] the court may grant the leave required by subsection (1)(b), either generally or for specified purposes.

(4)[^c-0b6cb59880f8735693a83d647509e6cb] This subsection applies to a child arrangements order if the arrangements regulated by the order consist of, or include, arrangements which relate to either or both of the following—

- (a) with whom the child concerned is to live, and
- (b) when the child is to live with any person.
