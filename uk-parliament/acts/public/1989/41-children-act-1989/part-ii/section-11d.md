# Children Act 1989, section 11D

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (96).** Text as at 2026-09-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11D. ... Activity conditions: further provision[^c18897301][^key-b43991a40ff6035038f155e16855326a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A child arrangements order[^key-d75436c4bab8ca913a0168beff0edb14] may not impose an activity[^key-f59dd79d8b339afdc31abeffdd742564] condition on an individual who is a child unless the individual is a parent of the child concerned.

(2) If a child arrangements order[^key-47347a442a7ba72555b7c89da6b39fdb] is an excepted order (within the meaning given by section 11B(4)), it may not impose (and it may not be varied so as to impose) a an activity[^key-82ae3de5bec3d23b21079113d85097ee] condition.

(3) A child arrangements order[^key-fbba82ff386e06b767bb1a1e5a0a5ecc] may not impose an activity[^key-c74cfbd1cbae20991f0b81f2023e75be] condition on an individual unless the individual is habitually resident in England and Wales; and a condition ceases to have effect if the individual subject to the condition ceases to be habitually resident in England and Wales.
