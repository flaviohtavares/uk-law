# Children Act 1989, section 11P

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11P. Orders under section 11O(2): further provision[^c18897761]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A court may not make an order under section 11O(2) requiring an individual to pay compensation in respect of a failure by him to comply with a provision of a child arrangements order[^key-a1bba9bbf446b130d1f4d726cc706b7e] unless it is satisfied that before the failure occurred the individual had been given (in accordance with rules of court) a copy of, or otherwise informed of the terms of—

- (a) in the case of a failure to comply with a provision of a child arrangements order where the order[^key-c9a86d95b53a57275a631ec7533a85c1] was varied before the failure occurred, a notice under section 11I relating to the order varying the child arrangements[^key-d6cf93a7a0dbcbec65c4114dc3703f56] order or, where more than one such order has been made, the last order preceding the failure in question;
- (b) in any other case, a notice under section 11I relating to the child arrangements[^key-e17787ef529d2eecc6a4c526b0c41874] order.

(2) A court may not make an order under section 11O(2) requiring an individual to pay compensation in respect of a failure by him to comply with a provision of a child arrangements[^key-10855e58d4cb3dc248eed881d22b01f0] order where the failure occurred before the individual attained the age of 18.

(3) A court may not make an order under section 11O(2) requiring an individual to pay compensation in respect of a failure by him to comply with a provision of a child arrangements order where the child arrangements order[^key-f559a45a42ea9dd19cc089e7171d5723] is an excepted order (within the meaning given by section 11B(4)).
