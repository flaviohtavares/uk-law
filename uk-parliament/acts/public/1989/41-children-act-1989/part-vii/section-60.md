## 60. Voluntary homes.[^c15166701][^c15166691]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c15166711] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c15166711] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c15166731] In this Act “**voluntary home**” means a children’s home which is carried on by a voluntary organisation but does not include a community home.

(4) Schedule 5 shall have effect for the purpose of supplementing the provisions of this Part.
