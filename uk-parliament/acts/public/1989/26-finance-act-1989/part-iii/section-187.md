# Finance Act 1989, section 187

> Text as at 2026-03-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 187. Repeals.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The enactments specified in Schedule 17 to this Act (which includeunnecessary enactments) are hereby repealed to the extent specified in thethird column of that Schedule, but subject to any provision at the end of anyPart of that Schedule.

(2)[^c13122111] The repeal of the enactments specified in Part XIV of Schedule 17 shallcome into force on such day as the Treasury may appoint by order made bystatutory instrument; and different days may be appointed for differentenactments.
