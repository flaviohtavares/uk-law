# Finance Act 1989, section 186

> Text as at 2026-03-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 186. Interpretation etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In this Act “**the Taxes Act 1970**” means the [^c13122071]Income and Corporation Taxes Act 1970 and “**the Taxes Act1988**” means the [^c13122081]Income and Corporation Taxes Act1988.

(2) Chapter II of Part I of this Act shall be construed as one with the [^c13122091]Value Added Tax Act 1983.

(3) Part II of this Act, so far as it relates to capital gains tax, shall beconstrued as one with the [^c13122101]Capital Gains Tax Act 1979.
