# Finance Act 1989, section 17

> Text as at 2026-03-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Disbursements in Port of London.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 17 of the [^c13110501]Customs and Excise Management Act1979 (general rule that customs and excise receipts, after deduction of disbursements, are to be paid into the Commissioners’ General Account at the Bank of England) paragraph (a) of subsection (5) (special rule that disbursements in Port of London are to be paid out of that Account) shall cease to have effect.
