# Finance Act 1989, section 170

> Text as at 2026-03-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 170. Up-rating of certain penalties.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c13116531] In section 23(8) of the Taxes Act 1988 (maximum penalty for agents failing to make certain payments on behalf of principals), for “£50” there shall be substituted “£300”.

(2)[^c13116541] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^key-58398fa413a5558ba282ec7f41f88845] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) In—

- (a)[^key-96bab0781e1492afe60490c3ffaa3592] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b)[^key-96bab0781e1492afe60490c3ffaa3592] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) section 658(5) of that Act (maximum penalty for false statements or representations relating to purchased life annuities),

for “£500” there shall be substituted “£3,000”.

(5) In paragraph 2(4) of Schedule 19A to that Act and Schedule 16A to the [^c13116551]Finance Act 1973 (maximum penalty for incorrect return byLloyd’s agent), for the words “£500 in the case of fraud and £250 in the case of negligence” there shall be substituted “£3,000”.

(6) This section shall apply in relation to things done or omitted on or after the day on which this Act is passed.
