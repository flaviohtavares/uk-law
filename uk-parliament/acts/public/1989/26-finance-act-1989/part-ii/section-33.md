# Finance Act 1989, section 33

> Text as at 2026-03-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 33. Married couples.[^c13110791]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Sections 257 to 257F and 265 of the [^c13110801]Taxes Act 1988,as inserted for the year 1990-91 and subsequent years by the Finance Act 1988,shall be amended as follows.

(2) In section 257(1) for “£2,605” there shall be substituted “£2,785”.

(3) In section 257(2) for “£3,180” there shall be substituted “£3,400”.

(4) In section 257(3)—

- (a)[^key-558eba2ca03fa7bfcac8438314eed32c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) for “£3,310” there shall be substituted “£3,540”.

(5) In section 257(5)—

- (a) for “£10,600” there shall be substituted “£11,400”,and
- (b)[^key-db700751a297b2920a3dbe51616f1faf] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c13110811] In section 257A(1) for “£1,490” there shall be substituted “£1,590”.

(7) In section 257A(2) for “£1,855” there shall be substituted “£1,985”.

(8) In section 257A(3)—

- (a)[^key-a194ae1653a8a17c3f99cc00f8ed0832] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (b) for “£1,895” there shall be substituted “£2,025”.

(9) In section 257A(5)—

- (a) for “£10,600” there shall be substituted “£11,400”,and
- (b)[^key-6ce472944f3c5cf1d49a00d96e3b4d31] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(10) In sections [^c13110821]. . . 257D(8) and[^c13110831] 265(3) after paragraph (b) there shall be inserted

> or
>
> - (c) on account of any payments to which section 593(2) or 639(3) applies,

.

(11)[^c13110841] In section 257E(1)(b) for “80” there shall be substituted “75”.

(12)[^c13110841] In section 257E(2)(a) for “£3,180” there shall be substituted “£3,400”.

(13)[^c13110841] In section 257E(2)(b) for “£3,310” there shall be substituted “£3,540”.
