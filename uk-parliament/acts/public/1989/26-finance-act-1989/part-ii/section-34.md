# Finance Act 1989, section 34

> Text as at 2026-03-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34. Charge and rate of corporation tax for financial year 1989.[^c13110871]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Corporation tax shall be charged for the financial year 1989 at the rate of 35 per cent.
