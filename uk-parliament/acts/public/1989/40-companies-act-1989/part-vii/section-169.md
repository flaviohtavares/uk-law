# Companies Act 1989, section 169

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2024-01-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 169. Supplementary provisions.[^c13694951]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c13694961] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Sections 296 and 297 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 apply[^c13694971] in relation to a failure by a recognised investment exchange or recognised clearing house to comply with an obligation under this Part as to a failure to comply with an obligation under that Act.

(2A)[^c-3235de8f3f0ac961f9f670babbeb7d93] Section 296 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 applies in relation to a failure by a recognised CSD to comply with an obligation under this Part as to a failure to comply with an obligation under that Act.

(3)[^c13694981] Where the recognition of an investment exchange, clearing house or central securities depository[^c-d36ed9514bf7c79f20c76c3087e3b404] is revoked under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000, the appropriate authority[^c13694991] may, before or after the revocation order, give such directions as it[^c13695001] thinks fit with respect to the continued application of the provisions of this Part, with such exceptions, additions and adaptations as may be specified in the direction, in relation to cases where a relevant event of any description specified in the directions occurred before the revocation order takes effect.

(3A)[^c13695011] “The appropriate authority” means—

- (a) in the case of an overseas investment exchange or clearing house, the Treasury;
- (b)[^c-88787b676b251c95a9dbcf1177be5ac8] in the case of a UK investment exchange, the FCA, [^c-5bceb187491364cf113a3b2c046ed77b]...
- (c) in the case of a UK clearing house, the Bank of England, and[^c-b4a27917c133440207e71303c02fe9c4]
- (d) in the case of a central securities depository, the Bank of England.

(4)[^c13694961] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5) Regulations under section 414 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (service of notices) may make provision[^c13695031] in relation to a notice, direction or other document required or authorised by or under this Part to be given to or served on any person other than the Treasury[^c13695041], the FCA or the Bank of England[^c-c508544e5b4a3ca26a12c04fe555a2b3].
