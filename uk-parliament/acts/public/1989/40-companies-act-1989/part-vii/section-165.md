# Companies Act 1989, section 165

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2024-01-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/40

## 165. Adjustment of prior transactions.[^c13694441]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) No order shall be made in relation to a transaction to which this section applies under—

- (a) section 238 or 339 of the Insolvency Act 1986 (transactions at an under-value),
- (b) section 239 or 340 of that Act (preferences), or
- (c) section 423 of that Act (transactions defrauding creditors).

(2) As respects Scotland, no decree shall be granted in relation to any such transaction—

- (a) under section 98 or 99 of the Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 2016[^c-6d11dbf64b58bba89c05b1f03d315f10] or section 242 or 243 of the Insolvency Act 1986 (gratuitous alienations and unfair preferences), or
- (b) at common law on grounds of gratuitous alienations or fraudulent preferences.

(3) This section applies to—

- (a) a market contract to which a recognised body[^c-1cd9129e8584ab459e0ed44c6d3df00c] is a party or which is entered into under its default rules, [^c-4f0ce2ab8548488ad064f569158d8862]...
- (ab)[^c-78fa396083928985e9ba16ca929210f8] a market contract to which this Part applies by virtue of section 155(2B), and
- (b) a disposition of property in pursuance of a market contract referred to in paragraph (a) or (ab).

(4) Where margin is provided in relation to a market contract and (by virtue of subsection (3)(a), (3)(ab)[^c-2114d342ed9ae6b88546d688b442c786] or otherwise) no such order or decree as is mentioned in subsection (1) or (2) has been, or could be, made in relation to that contract, this section applies to—

- (a) the provision of the margin,
- (ab)[^c-7b53689ea058b2fcec2b0a46d9566d24] a qualifying collateral arrangement,
- (b) any contract effected by the recognised body[^c-a56816b4b3e0776a60b300f68ffd0dd8] in question for the purpose of realising the property provided as margin, and
- (c) any disposition of property in accordance with the rules of the recognised body[^c-3a97c422d0954e366ed6d9daf813d16a] in question[^c-a5d2baa8c0f6e37fb6154aa8099a6c71] as to the application of property provided as margin.

(5)[^c-fa2e349a6217b5f2f4baffc5a79d1156] This section also applies to—

- (a) the provision of default fund contribution to a recognised body[^c-41670748abbbf904aa5b3b22f37cb0aa],
- (b) any contract effected by a recognised body[^c-4b155cbe108ab3bfa86446fbcc147d16] for the purpose of realising the property provided as default fund contribution, [^c-fa3bf498bdd0444986a749611fd6fb84]...
- (c) any disposition of property in accordance with the rules of the recognised body[^c-4a9171cc10675c791753ac023aacc98c] as to the application of property provided as default fund contribution,[^c-5991cfbed06fd21dbd72d59ba2f8bffa]
- (d)[^c-51b9f59610aecd11eda84200d33d7b43] a transfer of a clearing member client contract, a client trade or a qualifying collateral arrangement as mentioned in paragraphs (c) to (e) of section 158(1), and
- (e) a qualifying property transfer.
