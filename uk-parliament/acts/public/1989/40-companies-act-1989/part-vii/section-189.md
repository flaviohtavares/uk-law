# Companies Act 1989, section 189

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2024-01-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 189. Meaning of “relevant office-holder”.[^c13696721]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The following are relevant office-holders for the purposes of this Part—

- (a) the official receiver,
- (b) any person acting in relation to a company as its liquidator, provisional liquidator, administrator or administrative receiver,
- (c) any person acting in relation to an individual (or, in Scotland, any debtor within the meaning of the Bankruptcy (Scotland) Act 2016[^c-c9c229906404d805e3414b3bb42426e7]) as his trustee in bankruptcy or interim receiver of his property or as trustee[^c-e248ffdc392e9352e2221a9f634a85fd] or interim trustee in the sequestration of his estate,
- (d) any person acting as administrator of an insolvent estate of a deceased person.

(2) In subsection (1)(b) “**company**” means any company, society, association, partnership or other body which may be wound up under the [^c13696741]Insolvency Act 1986.
