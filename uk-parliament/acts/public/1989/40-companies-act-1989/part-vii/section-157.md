# Companies Act 1989, section 157

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2024-01-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 157.[^c13693781]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A recognised body[^key-acf762c1eaccb1c2348b60fb91296e0e] shall give the appropriate regulator[^key-02920cac2a6734c79bd3d4c247342036] at least three months[^key-44f7c2ecf0db5e6e7f293014621b8377] notice of any proposal to amend, revoke or add to its default rules; and the regulator[^key-d92a1486454f35230617599a47475db7] may within three months from receipt of the notice direct the recognised body[^key-f593f17cc540517be6cdf26d52ebdc8f] not to proceed with the proposal, in whole or in part.

(1A)[^key-496d11c31e623733a2f795ac9cd2a04d] The appropriate regulator may, if it considers it appropriate to do so, agree a shorter period of notice and, in a case where it does so, any direction under this section must be given by it within that shorter period.

(2) A direction under this section may be varied or revoked.

(3) Any amendment or revocation of, or addition to, the default rules of a recognised body[^key-c55357bc0c088004094381c5f7bd761c] in breach of a direction under this section is ineffective.

(4)[^key-66bdffea69a88ce9fd26097c2a3383c0] The appropriate regulator”—

- (a) in relation to a recognised UK investment exchange, means the FCA, and
- (b) in relation to a recognised clearing house[^key-bb4a5889b48023b2d5337622a221ac15] or a recognised CSD[^key-a74aed37fbda94fbcc9cf29b1807b86e], means the Bank of England.
