## 125. Delivery of documents to the registrar.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) For section 707 of the Companies Act 1985 (power of registrar to accept information on microfilm, &c.) substitute— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]

> **707. Delivery to the registrar of documents otherwise than in legible form. (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]**
>
> (1) This section applies to the delivery to the registrar under any provision of the Companies Acts of documents otherwise than in legible form. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]
>
> (2) Any requirement to deliver a document to the registrar, or to deliver a document in the prescribed form, is satisfied by the communication to the registrar of the requisite information in any non-legible form prescribed for the purposes of this section by regulations or approved by the registrar. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]
>
> (3) Where the document is required to be signed or sealed, it shall instead be authenticated in such manner as may be prescribed by regulations or approved by the registrar. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]
>
> (4) The document must— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]
>
> - (a) contain in a prominent position the registered number of the company to which it relates, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]
> - (b) satisfy any requirements prescribed by regulations for the purposes of this section, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]
> - (c) be furnished in such manner, and conform to such requirements, as the registrar may specify for the purpose of enabling him to read and copy the document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]
>
> (5) If a document is delivered to the registrar which does not comply with the requirements of this section, he may serve on the person by whom the document was delivered (or, if there are two or more such persons, on any of them) a notice indicating the respect in which the document does not comply. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]
>
> (6) Where the registrar serves such a notice, then, unless a replacement document— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]
>
> - (a) is delivered to him within 14 days after the service of the notice, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]
> - (b) complies with the requirements of this section (or section 706) or is not rejected by him for failure to comply with those requirements, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]
>
> the original document shall be deemed not to have been delivered to him. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]
>
> But for the purposes of any enactment imposing a penalty for failure to deliver, so far as it imposes a penalty for continued contravention, no account shall be taken of the period between the delivery of the original document and the end of the period of 14 days after service of the registrar’s notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]
>
> (7) The Secretary of State may by regulations make further provision with respect to the application of this section in relation to instantaneous forms of communication. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]
>
> (8) Regulations made for the purposes of this section may make different provision with respect to different descriptions of document and different forms of communication, and as respects delivery to the registrar for England and Wales and delivery to the registrar for Scotland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-e912d1fabbccfe9ecea95c2dfa85279a]
