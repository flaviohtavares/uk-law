## 208. Summary proceedings in Scotland for offences in connection with disqualification of directors.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 21 of the [^c13697511]Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986 (application of provisions of the [^c13697521]Insolvency Act 1986), after subsection (3) add—

> (4) For the purposes of summary proceedings in Scotland, section 431 of that Act applies to summary proceedings for an offence under section 11 or 13 of this Act as it applies to summary proceedings for an offence under Parts I to VII of that Act.

.
