# Companies Act 1989, section 55

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (11).** Text as at 2024-01-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/40
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Investigations by inspectors not leading to published report.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In section 432 of the [^c13687771]Companies Act 1985 (appointment of inspectors by Secretary of State), after subsection (2) (investigation of circumstances suggesting misconduct) insert—

> (2A) Inspectors may be appointed under subsection (2) on terms that any report they may make is not for publication; and in such a case, the provisions of section 437(3) (availability and publication of inspectors’ reports) do not apply.
