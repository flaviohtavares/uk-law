## 71. Investigation of unregistered companies.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In Schedule 22 to the Companies Act 1985 (provisions applying to unregistered companies), for the entry relating to Part XIV substitute—

> |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | Part XIV (except section 446) | Investigation of companies and their affairs; requisition of documents. | — |
