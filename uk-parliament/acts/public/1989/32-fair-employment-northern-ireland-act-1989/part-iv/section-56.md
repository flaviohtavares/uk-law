# Fair Employment (Northern Ireland) Act 1989, section 56

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 56. Restriction on proceedings for contravention of legislation.

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1999-03-01).**

(1) Except as provided by the Fair Employment (Northern Ireland) Acts or regulations made under those Acts, no proceedings whether civil or criminal shall be brought against any person in respect of a contravention of, or failure to comply with, any provision of those Acts or of such regulations.

(2) Nothing in subsection (1) above prevents any application for judicial review.
