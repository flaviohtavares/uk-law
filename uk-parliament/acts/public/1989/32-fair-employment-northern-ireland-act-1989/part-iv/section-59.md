# Fair Employment (Northern Ireland) Act 1989, section 59

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Minor and consequential amendments and repeals.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1999-03-01).**

(1) The minor and consequential amendments in Schedule 2 to this Act have effect.

(2) The enactments specified in Schedule 3 to this Act are repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule.
