# Fair Employment (Northern Ireland) Act 1989, section 29

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Monitoring applicants.

*Extent: N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1999-03-01).**

(1) The Department may, by regulations made after consultation with the Commission, provide that the employer shall, for the purpose of enabling the composition of those applying to fill vacancies for employment in any registered concern in Northern Ireland to be ascertained (whether or not section 27(3) or (4) of this Act has effect in respect of their applications), seek to obtain such information about them as may be prescribed.

(2) The regulations may—

- (a) require information of a prescribed description which is obtained in pursuance of the regulations[^c1051872] to be retained by the employer for such period as may be prescribed,
- (b) provide that a person who—
  - (i) without reasonable excuse fails to comply with such a requirement, or
  - (ii) knowingly gives any false information to another who is seeking to obtain information in pursuance of the regulations, or
  - (iii) knowingly includes any false information in any records kept in pursuance of the regulations,is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a finenot exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, [^c1051873]. . .
- (bb)[^c1051874] provide that, subject to such exceptions as may be prescribed, a person who discloses any information of a prescribed description which is obtained in pursuance of the regulations is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale, and
- (c) make different provision for different cases.
