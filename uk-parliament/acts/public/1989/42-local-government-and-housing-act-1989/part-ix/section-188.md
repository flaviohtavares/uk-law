# Local Government and Housing Act 1989, section 188

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (36).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 188. Repeal of s. 2 of the Education (Grants and Awards) Act 1984.

*Extent: E+W*

Section 2 of the [^c15280011]Education (Grants and Awards) Act 1984 (limit on expenditure approved for grant purposes) shall not apply in relation to any expenditure approved for the financial year beginning with 1st April 1990 or any subsequent financial year.
