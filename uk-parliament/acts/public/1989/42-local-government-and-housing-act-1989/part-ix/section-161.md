# Local Government and Housing Act 1989, section 161

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (36).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 161. Housing authorities not required to keep a housing stock.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) At the end of section 9 of the [^c15278911]Housing Act 1985 (provision of housing accommodation) there shall be added the following subsection—

> (5) Nothing in this Act shall be taken to require (or to have at any time required) a local housing authority itself to acquire or hold any houses or other land for the purposes of this Part.

(2) At the end of section 2 of the [^c15278921]Housing (Scotland) Act 1987 (powers of local authorities to provide housing accommodation) there shall be added the following subsection—

> (6) Nothing in this Act shall be taken to require (or to have at any time required) a local authority itself to acquire or hold any houses or other land for the purposes of this Part.
