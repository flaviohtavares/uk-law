*Extent: E+W*

**1.** The repeal in the City of London Sewers Act 1897 shall have effect on the commencement day, within the meaning of section 6 of this Act.

*Extent: E+W*

**2.** The repeals in Parts XIII and XIV of the Housing Act 1985, section 30 of the Social Security Act 1986 and section 129 of the Housing Act 1988 shall have effect for years beginning on or after 1st April 1990.

*Extent: E+W*

**3.** The repeals in Schedule 3 to the Local Government Act 1988 shall have effect in relation to reports made after the coming into force of, in the case of paragraph 7(2) and (3), section 26 and, in the case of paragraph 15(a),section 27 of this Act.

*Extent: E+W*

**4.** The repeals in the Local Government Finance Act 1988 shall have effect in accordance with Schedule 5 to this Act.
