## 18. Schemes for basic, attendance and special responsibility allowances for local authority members.[^c15266491]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c16988641][^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] Subject to subsection (1A),[^c15266501] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] the Secretary of State may by regulations authorise or require any such relevant authority as may be specified or described in the regulations to make a scheme providing for the payment of— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] a basic allowance for every member of the authority who is a councillor; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c15266511][^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] an attendance allowance in relation to the carrying out by any such member of such duties as may be specified in or determined under the regulations; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] a special responsibility allowance for any such member who has such special responsibilities in relation to the authority as may be so specified or determined. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d]

(1A (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c15266521][^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] In relation to a district council, county council, county borough council or London borough council, subsection (1) above shall have effect with the omission of paragraph (b). *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] Regulations under this section may also authorise or require a scheme made by a relevant authority under the regulations to include provision for the payment to appointed members of allowances in respect of such losses of earnings and expenses as— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] are necessarily sustained or incurred in the carrying out, in connection with their membership of the authority or any committee or sub-committee of the authority, of duties specified in or determined under the regulations; and *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fa37389a656a9b59c57367a44c4dd5f6][^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] are not of a description in respect of which provision is made for an allowance under any of sections 174 to 176 of the Local Government Act 1972 or sections 46 to 48 of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d]

(2A (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c15266531][^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] Regulations under this section may authorise or require a scheme made by a district council, county council, county borough council or London borough council to include provision for the payment to members of the council of allowances in respect of such expenses of arranging for the care of children or dependants as are necessarily incurred in the carrying out of their duties as members. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d]

(3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c16988681][^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] Without prejudice to the generality of the powers conferred by subsections (1) *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] to (2A)[^c15266541] *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] above, regulations under this section may contain such provision as the Secretary of State considers appropriate for requiring a scheme made by a relevant authority under the regulations— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] to make it a condition of any payment by way of allowance that, in the financial year to which the payment would relate, the aggregate amount which the authority has paid out or is already liable to pay out under the scheme does not exceed such maximum amount as may be specified in or determined under the regulations; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] to make provision for different maximum amounts to be applicable, for the purposes of any such condition, in relation to different allowances or in relation to different members or members of different groups; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d]
- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] to make provision in relation to claims which cannot be paid by virtue of any such condition and provision for the payment to members of the authority who are councillors of an amount by way of supplement to the basic allowance where, in any financial year, the aggregate paid out or owing under the scheme is less than an amount specified in or determined under the regulations; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d]
- (d (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] to provide that the amount authorised by virtue of subsection (2) above to be paid by way of allowance in any case shall not exceed such amount as may be so specified or determined; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d]
- (e (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] to contain such provision as may be so specified or determined with respect to the general administration of the scheme, with respect to the manner in which, time within which and forms on which claims for any allowance are to be made and with respect to the information to be provided in support of any such claim; *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d]
- (f (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d] to contain such provision as may be so specified or determined for avoiding the duplication of payments or of allowances, for determining the bodies by which payments of allowances are to be made and for the apportionment of payments between different bodies. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-687a5722aa79b6d514a536a8a4c8df9d]

(3A (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-327986dd03cf5b8655ed83e4a53d152b][^c15266551] Regulations under this section may make provision for or in connection with— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-327986dd03cf5b8655ed83e4a53d152b]

- (a (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-327986dd03cf5b8655ed83e4a53d152b] enabling district councils, county councils, county borough councils or London borough councils to determine which members of the council are to be entitled to pensions, allowances or gratuities, *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-327986dd03cf5b8655ed83e4a53d152b]
- (b (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-327986dd03cf5b8655ed83e4a53d152b] treating the basic allowance or the special responsibility allowance as amounts in respect of which such pensions, allowances or gratuities are payable. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-327986dd03cf5b8655ed83e4a53d152b]

(3A (Extent: W)) Regulations may be made by the Welsh Ministers to make provision for or in connection with— *(Extent: W)*

- (a (Extent: W)) enabling county councils or county borough councils to determine which members of the council are to be entitled to gratuities, *(Extent: W)*

(3D (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-91fc3c9dbacae96b0196026c0fe88f3f] Regulations under this section may make provision for or in connection with the establishment by the National Assembly for Wales on a permanent or temporary basis of a panel which is to have such functions as may be specified in the regulations in relation to allowances, or pensions, allowances or gratuities, payable to members of county councils and county borough councils in Wales. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-91fc3c9dbacae96b0196026c0fe88f3f]

(3E (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-83ccd1421945366ddf4e5319ee5a2fe0] Regulations under subsection (3B) above may include provision— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-83ccd1421945366ddf4e5319ee5a2fe0]

- (c (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-83ccd1421945366ddf4e5319ee5a2fe0] for or in connection with the appointment by councils of joint panels. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-83ccd1421945366ddf4e5319ee5a2fe0]

- (b) with respect to the persons who may or must be appointed to such a panel.

(3G (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-9e5f0ab54a1aacb623c3183b43f7e2bd] Regulations under subsection (3B), (3C) or (3D) may include provision— *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-9e5f0ab54a1aacb623c3183b43f7e2bd]
