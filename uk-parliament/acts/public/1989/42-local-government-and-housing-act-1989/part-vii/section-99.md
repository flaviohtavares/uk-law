# Local Government and Housing Act 1989, section 99

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (36).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/42
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 99. Directions and guidance.

*Extent: E+W*

Any power under this Part to give [^c17104661]. . . guidance may be so exercised as to make different provision for different cases, different descriptions of cases and different areas and, in particular, with respect to different local housing authorities or descriptions of authority (including a description framed by reference to authorities in a particular area).
