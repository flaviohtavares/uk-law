### Generation licence holders

**2.** Article 50(6)[^c-4612004fabc51895cb862876ef70bfae] of the Electricity Regulation (duty to keep specified information at the disposal of the Authority) is a relevant provision in relation to the holder of a generation licence.

### Transmission licence holders

**3.** The following are relevant provisions in relation to all holders of a transmission licence—

- (a) section 9;
- (b) sections 26 to 29 of the Energy Act 2010 (adjustment of charges to help disadvantaged groups of customers).

### Electricity System Operator[^M_F_b8afa763-ca15-41ab-b31f-b35db534685b]
