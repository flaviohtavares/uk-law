# Electricity Act 1989, section 102

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (62).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Production and supply of heat or electricity etc. by Scottish local authorities.

*Extent: E+W+S*

The provisions of Schedule 13 (which inserts into the [^c4119141]Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 provisions analogous to sections 11 (production and supply of heat or electricity or both by local authorities in England and Wales) and 12 (provisions supplementary to the said section 11) of the [^c4119151]Local Government (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1976) shall have effect.
