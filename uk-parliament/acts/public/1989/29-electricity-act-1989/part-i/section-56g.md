## 56G. Service by Authority of certain notices on Citizens Advice and Consumer Scotland.[^c4116081][^c-d9de6b50b4f2c332184dc45cd3c32125][^c-b826479db8b1d887d195ef6e6dc73a78]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Where the Authority is required by any provision of this Part to publish a notice or any other document, the Authority shall send a copy of the document to Citizens Advice[^c-dce6a3c07d92e3ca5853b863afc52060] and Consumer Scotland[^c-49eba797abba46a5c65bad5e5b5bfdad].
