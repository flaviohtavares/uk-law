# Electricity Act 1989, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (62).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/29
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Publication of information and advice.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c4115761] If it appears to the Authority that the publication of any advice and information would promote the interests of consumers in relation to electricity conveyed by distribution systems or transmission systems[^key-887672038133e9e0f0840b2ed8438e3b], the Authority may publish that advice or information in such manner as it thinks fit.

(2) In publishing advice or information under this section the Authority shall have regard to the need for excluding, so far as that is practicable, any matter which relates to the affairs of a particular individual or body of persons (corporate or unincorporate), where publication of that matter would or might, in the opinion of the Authority, seriously and prejudicially affect the interests of that individual or body.

(2A) Before deciding to publish under this section any advice or information relating to a particular individual or body of persons the Authority shall consult that individual or body.

(3) The[^key-c2a8e9b2aa3a580bec5e239896e87fbe] CMA shall consult the Director before publishing under section 6 of the Enterprise Act 2002[^key-722417ff0fda8ea56fd94ba74fb4aa7b] any information or advice which may be published by the Director under this section.

(4)[^c4115771] In this section “consumers” includes both existing and future consumers.
