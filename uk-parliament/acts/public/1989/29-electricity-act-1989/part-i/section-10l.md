# Electricity Act 1989, section 10L

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (62).** Text as at 2026-07-24. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/29

## 10L. Continuation or withdrawal of certification[^c-0ad53e4f3cafa17a3266363ef95540bb]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where the Authority reviews under section 10I(5)[^c-7c1b0206844378cc3dc8b63f4dbc6144] whether the certification basis in respect of a certified person continues to apply, it may, within the 4 months mentioned in section 10I(6)[^c-cc78e54d27d9e1628973b5e99768b842] or where relevant the 4 months mentioned in section 10I(10)[^c-f487d924436413da7e07082502d5ef5e], either—

- (a) make a [^c-13c08cbc15dc749f006fe8e8a73a2ef1]... decision to continue the certification[^c-bbd8fb3640ccea2ee945b0a92b985bd0] on the certification ground mentioned in section 10I(1); or
- (b) make a [^c-80ed1c0c4ceb5df245679f9b2b365cc8]... decision to withdraw the certification[^c-e611a82dd2526a815dac7556fc8e748c].

(2) If the Authority does not make a decision under subsection (1) within those 4 months, it is to be taken as having made a [^c-1e1b4c9dab5f518c0ae8404b13f7a4d5]... decision at the end of the 4 months to continue the certification[^c-21bc52524fe309598c5052a762ea4dcc] on the certification ground mentioned in section 10I(1).

(3) Where the Authority reviews under section 10I(8) whether the fourth or fifth test under section 10F is now passed, it may, within the 4 months mentioned in section 10I(9) or where relevant the 4 months mentioned in section 10I(10)[^c-bd38aca0092007aaa253355c9eaeec4c], either—

- (a) make a [^c-b4ed06b501a7e9ac08058fbce9edc040]... decision to continue the certification[^c-9742437f5370638bbe24bcc7d6eadb6e] on the basis that the test is now passed; or
- (b) make a [^c-ed097673aa8f71409fec93f994806c2a]... decision to withdraw the certification[^c-571f7fd0548e4cbe7ce27c32c810c2a1].

(4) If the Authority does not make a decision under subsection (3) within those 4 months, it is to be taken as having made a [^c-4d54d9e1e0a8a2bc78fa69d9a318c53a]... decision at the end of the 4 months to continue the certification[^c-34a110275c8b741e9c30b62eaa27b432] on the basis that the test is now passed.

(5) As soon as is reasonably practicable after a [^c-e3bc26778370c020bdae8262865d8734]... decision is made (or taken to be made) under this section, the Authority must—

- (a) notify the decision to the certified person and the Secretary of State; [^c-44e96c04c9250db49af853833ac3eadb]...
- (b)[^c-44e96c04c9250db49af853833ac3eadb] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c-fc3713f7f56b966061474ac929d46cce] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6A)[^c-fc3713f7f56b966061474ac929d46cce] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^c-fc3713f7f56b966061474ac929d46cce] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8)[^c-fc3713f7f56b966061474ac929d46cce] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9)[^c-fc3713f7f56b966061474ac929d46cce] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(9A)[^c-fe67d25b296bbd014fd32bc38bb0d1f3] The Authority must publish its decision to continue a certification, or to withdraw a certification, together with the reasons for it, in such manner as it considers appropriate.

(10) Section 10E(8)(b)[^c-65937c557c5530a7535b578403d66d8f] applies in relation to a decision mentioned in this section as it applies in relation to a decision mentioned in section 10E(1), but as if—

- (a) the references in section 10E(8)(b)[^c-b340a85ba1201e8adf6d8e02c74cc13c] to the certification of the applicant were references to the continued certification of the person certified; and
- (b) the reference in section 10E(8)(b) to a report prepared under section 10C were a reference to a report prepared under section 10K.
