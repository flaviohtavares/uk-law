# Employment Act 1989, section 27

> Text as at 2018-01-01.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 27. Power to make corresponding provision for Northern Ireland.

*Extent: N.I.*

(1) An Order in Council under paragraph 1(1)(b) of Schedule 1 to the [^c1205537]Northern Ireland Act 1974 (legislation for Northern Ireland in the interim period) which states that it is made only for purposes corresponding to those of any of the following provisions of this Act, namely, sections 1 to 6, 7(1), 8 to 11 [^c1205538]. . .—

- (a) shall not be subject to paragraph 1(4) and (5) of that Schedule (affirmative resolution of both Houses of Parliament), but
- (b) shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House.

(2) It is hereby declared that for the purposes of the [^c1205539]Northern Ireland Constitution Act 1973 a provision contained in—

- (a) an Order in Council under paragraph 1(1)(b) of Schedule 1 to the said Act of 1974 which states that it is made only for purposes corresponding to section 1, 2, 6 or 10(3) to (6) of this Act, or
- (b) any statutory rule for the purposes of the [^c1205540]Statutory Rules (Northern Ireland) Order 1979 made under such an Order as is mentioned in paragraph (a),

is not a provision dealing with an excepted matter.
