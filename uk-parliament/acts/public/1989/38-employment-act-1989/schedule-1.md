# Employment Act 1989, SCHEDULE 1

> Text as at 2018-01-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## SCHEDULE 1 — Provisions Concerned with Protection of Women at Work

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 4.

### Enactments

Section 61 of the [^c1205555]Factory and Workshop Act 1901, as set out in Schedule 5 to the [^c1205556]Factories Act 1961.

Section 205 of the [^c1205557]Public Health Act 1936.

Sections 74, 128 and 131 of the [^c1205558]Factories Act 1961.

### Statutory instruments

Regulation 3 of the [^c1205566]Regulations dated 21st January 1907 (Manufacture of paints and colours).

Regulation 10 of the [^c1205567]Regulations dated 12th August 1911 (Smelting of materials containing lead, the manufacture of red or orange lead, and the manufacture of flaked litharge).

Regulation 1 of the [^c1205568]Indiarubber Regulations 1922.

Regulation 1(ii) of the [^c1205569]Electric Accumulator Regulations 1925.

Regulation 6(1)(i) to (vi) of the [^c1205570]Pottery (Health and Welfare) Special Regulations 1950.

[^key-3cadc7b0f1cc14df81eb00f4f700cdde]...

Article 20(8) of the [^c1205572]Air Navigation Order 1985 so far as relating to pregnancy.

### Other instruments

Paragraph 118 of the Approved Code of Practice relating to the [^c1205573]Control of Lead at Work Regulations 1980 (approved under section 16 of the [^c1205574]Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974).

The following provisions of the medical standards contained in Merchant Shipping Notice No. M 1331 (issued for the purposes of Regulation 7 of the [^c1205575]Merchant Shipping (Medical Examination) Regulations 1983), namely—

- (a) Part X so far as relating to gynaecological conditions, and
- (b) Part XI.
