# Prevention of Terrorism (Temporary Provisions) Act 1989, section 19

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Prosecutions and evidence.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2001-02-19).**

(1) Proceedings shall not be instituted—

- (a) in England and Wales for an offence under section 2, 3, 8, 9, 10, 11, 17 ,18 or 18A[^c1215229] \] above or Schedule 7 to this Act except by or with the consent of the Attorney General; or
- (aa)[^c1215230] in England and Wales for an offence under section 13A, 13B,[^c1215231]16A 16B or 16D or under Schedule 6A[^c1215232] except by or with the consent of the Director of Public Prosecutions;
- (b) in Northern Ireland for an offence under section 8, 9, 10, 11, 17 ,18 or 18A above or Schedule 7 to this Act except by or with the consent of the Attorney General for Northern Ireland.

(2) Any document purporting to be an order, notice or direction made or given by the Secretary of State for the purposes of any provision of this Act and to be signed by him or on his behalf shall be received in evidence, and shall, until the contrary is proved, be deemed to be made or given by him.

(3) A document bearing a certificate purporting to be signed by or on behalf of the Secretary of State and stating that the document is a true copy of such an order, notice or direction shall, in any legal proceedings, be evidence, and in Scotland sufficient evidence, of the order, notice or direction.
