# Opticians Act 1989, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (100).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/44
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Provision as to death or bankruptcy of registered optician.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Where a registered optometrist or registered dispensing optician[^key-414a1b0d96c21925616278886f4d890a] dies at a time when he is carrying on business or is in practice as an optometrist or dispensing optician[^key-ddb4871948946a672995391d8e566303] , then during the three years beginning with his death or such longer period as the Council may in any particular case allow, section 28 above shall not operate to prevent—

- (a) his executors or administrators;
- (b) his surviving spouse or his surviving civil partner[^key-ce58175bdd9d5e742bc84ea37238f034] ;
- (c) any of his children; or
- (d) trustees on behalf of his surviving spouse or his surviving civil partner[^key-62b126dddc37f6c90ca9a173a2aaf2ed] or any of his children,

from taking or using in relation to that business or practice, but in conjunction with the name in which he carried it on, any title which he was entitled to take or use immediately before his death.

(2) Where a registered optometrist or registered dispensing optician becomes bankrupt at a time when he is carrying on business or is in practice as an optometrist or dispensing optician, then, during the three years beginning with the bankruptcy, section 28 above shall not operate to prevent his trustee in bankruptcy from taking or using in relation to that business or practice, but in conjunction with the name in which he carried it on, any title which he was entitled to take or use immediately before the bankruptcy.

(2A)[^key-35a3e8adc17bc9c2e3027ea1a0a7144c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Where—

- (a) a person by virtue of subsection (1) or (2) above takes or uses any title in relation to the business or practice—
  - (i) of a deceased optometrist or dispensing optician[^key-368c2df11f9f1e670a5e4d36911f5d27]; or
  - (ii) of an optometrist or dispensing optician who has become bankrupt; and
- (b) an offence under section 24, 25 or 27 above is committed in the course of that business or practice,

the Fitness to Practise Committee[^key-c79319b42a16c859ec43738985325f98] may, if they think fit, direct that subsection (1) or (2) above shall cease to apply in relation to that business or practice.

(4) This Act shall have effect in relation to any case in which it is alleged that there has been a conviction of any such offence and to any direction under subsection (3) above as it has effect in relation to a case in which it is alleged that a registrant’s fitness to practise or as the case may be a business registrant’s fitness to carry on business as an optometrist or a dispensing optician or both, is impaired and the making of an order under Part 2A above[^key-f56b19ff77f1cbede610af290ca36b55].

(5) In its application to Scotland subsection (2) above shall have effect as if—

- (a) for the reference to a registered optometrist or registered dispensing optician[^key-be576abbe257644b049acd65477904fc] becoming bankrupt there were substituted a reference to the estate of a registered optometrist or registered dispensing optician being sequestrated (cognate expressions being construed accordingly); and
- (b) for the reference to a registered optometrist’s or registered dispensing optician's[^key-b269b127ce25fcbdf19c713db84b8770] trustee in bankruptcy there were substituted a reference to the permanent trustee on his sequestrated estate.

(6) In its application to Northern Ireland subsection (2) above shall have effect as if for a registered optometrist’s or a registered dispensing optician's[^key-d75fbc6214f07cb02f8d646f7bbce518] trustee in bankruptcy there were substituted a reference to the assignee in bankruptcy.
