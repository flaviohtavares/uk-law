# Opticians Act 1989, section 2

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. The Education Committee.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There shall continue to be a committee of the Council known as the Education Committee, for the purpose of giving advice and assistance to the Council (whether or not in response to a reference from them) on matters relating to optical training, education and assessment[^key-6538db2a42f798a331913bffb21e7201].

(2) The Education Committee shall continue to be constituted in accordance with rules made by the Council [^key-f0d9ecec57335b802d5655dcbedd2b00]...
