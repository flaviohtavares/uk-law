## 37. Consequential amendments and repeals.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the definition of “**ophthalmic optician**” in section 128(1) of the [^c8063921]National Health Service Act 1977 and section 108(1) of the [^c8063931]National Health Service (Scotland) Act 1978—

- (a) for “section 2 of the Opticians Act 1958” there shall be substituted “section 7 of the Opticians Act 1989”; and
- (b) for “section 4” there shall be substituted “section 9”.
