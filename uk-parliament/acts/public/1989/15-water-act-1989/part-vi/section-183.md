# Water Act 1989, section 183

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (6).** Text as at 2026-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1989/15

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 183. General financial provisions.

*Extent: E+W*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) any administrative expenses or charges incurred by any Minister of the Crown or Government department in consequence of the provisions of this Act; and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable out of money so provided under any other Act.
