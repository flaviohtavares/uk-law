# Children and Young Persons Act 1933, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo5/23-24/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 11. †Exposing children under seven to risk of burning.[^c6079431][^c6079421]

*Extent: E+W*

If any person who has attained the age of sixteen years, having responsibility for[^c6079441] any child under the age of twelve[^c6079451] years, allows the child to be in any room containing an open fire grate or any heating appliance liable to cause injury to a person by contact therewith[^c6079461] not sufficiently protected to guard against the risk of his being burnt or scalded without taking reasonable precautions against that risk, and by reason thereof the child is killed or suffers serious injury, he shall on summary conviction be liable to a fine not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale[^c6079471]:

Provided that neither this section, not any proceedings taken thereunder, shall affect any liability of any such person to be proceeded against by indictment for any indictable offence.
