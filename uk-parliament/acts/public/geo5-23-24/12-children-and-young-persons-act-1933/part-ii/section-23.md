# Children and Young Persons Act 1933, section 23

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo5/23-24/12

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 23. Prohibition against persons under sixteen taking part in performances endangering life or limb.

*Extent: E+W*

No person under the age of sixteen years, and no child aged sixteen years,[^c6081591]shall take part in any performance to which[^c6081601] section 37(2)[^c6081611] of the [^c6081621]Children and Young Persons Act 1963 applies and in which his life or limbs are endangered and every person who causes or procures such a person or child[^c6081631], or being his parent or guardian allows him, to take part in such a performance, shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale[^M_F_099b5a88-6074-46b3-845e-2b4b8a69a137]:

Provided that no proceedings shall be taken under this subsection except by or with the authority of a chief officer of police.
