## 81. Classification, administration, and management.[^c6088681]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c6088691] The Secretary of State may classify approved schools according to the age of the persons for whom they are intended, the religious persuasion of such persons, the character of the education and training given therein, their geographical position, and otherwise as he thinks best calculated to secure that a person sent to an approved school is sent to a school appropriate to his case, or as may be necessary or the purposes of this Act.

(2) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c6088701]

(3)[^c6088691] The provisions set out in the Fourth Schedule to this Act shall have effect in relation to the administration of approved schools and the treatment of persons sent thereto.
