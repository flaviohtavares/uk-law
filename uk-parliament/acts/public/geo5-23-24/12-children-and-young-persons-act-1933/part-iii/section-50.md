# Children and Young Persons Act 1933, section 50

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (49).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Geo5/23-24/12

## 50. Age of criminal responsibility.

*Extent: E+W*

It shall be conclusively presumed that no child under the age of ten[^c6086411] years can be guilty of any offence.
