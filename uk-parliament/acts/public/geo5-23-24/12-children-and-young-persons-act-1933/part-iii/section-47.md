## 47. Procedure in youth courts.[^c6084751]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Youth courts shall sit as often as may be necessary for the purpose of exercising any jurisdiction conferred on them by or under this or any other Act.

(2)[^c6084771][^c-1261fb160ffbb59e2a4fd098fd5339d3] [^c6084781]. . . No person shall be present at any sitting of a youth court except—

- (a) members and officers of the court;
- (b) parties to the case before the court, their legal representatives[^c20380901], and witnesses and other persons directly concerned in that case;
- (c) bonâ fide representatives of newspapers or news agencies;
- (d) such other persons as the court may specially authorise to be present:
