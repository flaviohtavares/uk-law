## 48. Miscellaneous provisions as to powers of youth courts.[^c6085231]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A youth court sitting for the purpose of hearing a charge against, . . . [^c6085251], a person who is believed to be a child or young person may, if it thinks fit to do so, proceed with the hearing and determination of the charge . . . [^c6085251] notwithstanding that it is discovered that the person in question is not a child or young person.

[(2)[^c6085271] The attainment of the age of eighteen[^c6085281] years by . . . [^c6085291] a person in whose case an order for conditional discharge has been made, shall not deprive a youth court of jurisdiction to enforce his attendance and deal with him in respect of . . . [^c6085291] the commission of a further offence . . . [^c6085291].

(3) When a youth court has remanded a child or young person for information to be obtained with respect to him, any youth court acting in the same local justice area[^c20367451]—

- (a) may in his absence extend the period for which he is remanded, so, however, that he appears before a court or a justice of the peace at least once in every twenty-one days;
- (b) when the required information has been obtained, may deal with him finally;
