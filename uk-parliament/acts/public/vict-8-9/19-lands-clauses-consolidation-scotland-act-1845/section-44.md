# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 44

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 44. Witnesses to be summoned.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If either party so request in writing, the sheriff shall summon before him any person considered necessary to be examined as a witness touching the matters in question.
