# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 88

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 88. Decision of sheriff not conclusive, &c.[^c5626791][^key-5eed9e81f4db805142b7eb489e4ca839][^key-ce40394bd44b6205fccc6d5e28fd1ad8][^c5626781]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

On the trial of any action for any such penalty as aforesaid the decision of the sheriff under the provision herein-before contained shall not be held conclusive as to the right of entry on any such lands by the promoters of the undertaking.
