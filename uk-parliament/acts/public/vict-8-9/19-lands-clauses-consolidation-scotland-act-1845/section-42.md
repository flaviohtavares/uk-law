# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 42

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 42. Sheriff to preside; jury may view

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The sheriff shall preside on the said inquiry; and the party claiming compensation shall be deemed the pursuer, and the proceedings at such trials shall be conducted in like manner as in criminal trials; and, if either party so request, the sheriff shall order the jury, or any seven or more of them, to view the place or matter in controversy.
