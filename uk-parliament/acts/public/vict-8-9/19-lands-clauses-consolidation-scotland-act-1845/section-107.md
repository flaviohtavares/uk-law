# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 107

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 107. Company to continue the payment of feu duties, &c.[^c5626921]

*Extent: S*

It shall be lawful for the promoters of the undertaking to enter upon and continue in possession of such lands without redeeming the charges thereon, provided they pay the amount of such annual or recurring payment when due, and otherwise fulfil all obligations accordingly, and provided they shall not be called upon by the party entitled to the charge to redeem the same.
