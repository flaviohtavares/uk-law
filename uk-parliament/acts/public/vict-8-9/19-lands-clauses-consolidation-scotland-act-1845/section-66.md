# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 66

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: Scotland.

## 66. Expences of the arbitration.[^c5624771][^c5624761]

*Extent: S*

If the arbiters shall determine that the sum so deposited was sufficient, the expences of and incident to such arbitration, to be determined by the arbiters, shall be in the discretion of the arbiters; but if the arbiters shall determine that a further sum ought to be paid or deposited by the promoters of the undertaking, all the expences of and incident to the arbitration shall be borne by the promoters of the undertaking.
