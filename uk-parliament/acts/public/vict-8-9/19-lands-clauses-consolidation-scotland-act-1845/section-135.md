# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 135

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 135. Application of penalties.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The sheriff or justices by whom any such penalty or forfeiture shall be imposed, where the application thereof is not otherwise provided for, may award not more than one half thereof to the informer, and the remainder shall be paid into the Exchequer.[^c5627991]
