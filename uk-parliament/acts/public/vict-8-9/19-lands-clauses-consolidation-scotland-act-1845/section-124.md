# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 124

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 124. Lands to be conveyed to the purchasers.[^c5627691]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Upon payment or tender to the promoters of the undertaking of the purchase money so agreed upon or determined as aforesaid they shall convey such lands to the purchasers thereof[^key-c77b2bc1c70bf14ecb95e913a4b60c9e]....
