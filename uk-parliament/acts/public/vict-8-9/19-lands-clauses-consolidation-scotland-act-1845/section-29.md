# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 29

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. If either arbiter refuse to act, the other to proceed ex parte.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If when more than one arbiter shall have been appointed either of the arbiters refuse or for seven days neglect to act the other arbiter may proceed ex parte, and the decision of such arbiter shall be as effectual as if he had been the single arbiter appointed by both parties.
