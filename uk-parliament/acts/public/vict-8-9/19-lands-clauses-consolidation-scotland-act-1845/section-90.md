# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 90

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Parties not to be required to sell part of a house.[^c5626831][^key-1134220231c995bc50f448aa1c08f4a5][^key-fbc22e4902209bb3ab09d855b5cdadbe][^key-5f80302fcfc7bdd99478f8ba0924a06d][^key-1ba4efdd161389059e7475e8fccc0c26][^key-6606f68fa8df9cb25e118098344edbaa][^key-da9c40ef62ce9bb19b29b1d51c7c08ad][^key-712ca632f4ba25cf7c27cd47bd13d24b][^key-4b1fdaa98178f93d7ebdddd052f3dcc3][^key-644cc6edc7b837b12fbe9d775cc12272][^key-c57e20e2b3f472d8c7eada0ed936a9b2][^key-b8cbbf32d713ec1ec555e3d4b4d0a6f7][^key-f0d08329b565c14a6035aaceb0cecc66][^key-ca63c9d063ceb913f9a29024ea15ac13][^key-6150c09568b567143010692c2fafa787][^key-b5c4f84afd7d90e3293abf580ce1bd28][^c5626821]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

And be it enacted, that no party shall at any time be required to sell or convey to the promoters of the undertaking a part only of any house or other building or manufactory, if such party be willing and able to sell and convey the whole thereof.
