# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 70

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Sums not exceeding 20l. to be paid to parties.[^c5625451][^c5625441]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If such money shall not exceed the sum of twenty pounds, the same shall be paid to the parties entitled to the rents and profits of the lands in respect whereof the same shall be payable, for their own use and benefit; or in case of the [^key-b1bc3b3d899611d6b99dac8018f60913]... legal disability by reason of nonage[^c5625461] incapacity by reason of mental disorder within the meaning of the Mental Health (Scotland) Act, 1960[^c5625471], or other incapacity of any such parties, then such money shall be paid, for their use, to the respective [^key-b1bc3b3d899611d6b99dac8018f60913]... legal representatives of a child (within the meaning of Part I of the Children (Scotland) Act 1995),[^c5625481] guardians, tutors, curators, judicial factors, or trustees of such persons.
