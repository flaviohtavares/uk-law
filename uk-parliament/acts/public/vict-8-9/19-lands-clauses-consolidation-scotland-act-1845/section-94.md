# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 94

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 94. Meeting to appoint a committee.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The meeting so called may appoint a committee not exceeding five in number, of the parties entitled to any such rights; and at such meeting the decision of the majority of the persons entitled to such rights present shall bind the minority and all absent parties; but such meeting shall not be effectual for the purpose unless five at the least of the parties entitled attend the same, if there be so many as five in all of the parties entitled to such rights.
