# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 80

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Form of conveyances.[^c5626371]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

[^key-34404acd043041647a0f217e1eec461d]... conveyances of lands so to be purchased as aforesaid may be according to the form of Schedule (A.)[^key-989d1f2bba164ffcd32428e0ea71a199] to this Act annexed, or as near thereto as the circumstances of the case will admit; which [^key-34404acd043041647a0f217e1eec461d]... conveyances, being duly executed, and being registered in [^key-34404acd043041647a0f217e1eec461d]... the general register of sasines [^key-34404acd043041647a0f217e1eec461d]... shall give and constitute a good and undoubted right and complete and valid [^key-34404acd043041647a0f217e1eec461d]... title in all time coming to the promoters of the undertaking, and their successors and assigns, to the premises therein described, any law or custom to the contrary notwithstanding[^key-34404acd043041647a0f217e1eec461d]....
