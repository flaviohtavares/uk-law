# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 97

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. If no committee be appointed, the amount to be determined by a valuator.[^c5626851]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If, upon being duly convened by the promoters of the undertaking, no effectual meeting of the parties entitled to such rights shall take place, or if, taking place, such meeting fail to appoint such committee, the amount of such compensation shall be determined by a valuator, to be appointed by the sheriff, as herein-before provided in the case of parties who cannot be found
