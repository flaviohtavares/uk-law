# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 46

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. If the party make default the inquiry not to proceed.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the party claiming compensation shall not appear at the time appointed for the inquiry such inquiry shall not be further proceeded in, but the compensation to be paid shall be such as shall be ascertained by a valuator appointed by the sheriff in manner herein-after provided.
