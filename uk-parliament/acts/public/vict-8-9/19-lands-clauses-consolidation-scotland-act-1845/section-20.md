# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 20

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 20. Dispute, as to compensation may be referred to arbitration.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If no agreement be come to between the promoters of the undertaking and the owners of or parties by this or the special Act enabled to sell and convey any lands taken or required for or injuriously affected by the execution of the undertaking, or any interest in such lands, as to the value of such lands or of any interest therein, or as to the compensation to be made in respect thereof, it shall be lawful for the parties to refer the same to arbitration.
