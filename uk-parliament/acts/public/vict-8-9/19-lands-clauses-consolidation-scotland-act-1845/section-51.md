# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 51

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 51. Particulars of the expences.[^c5624131]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The expences of any such inquiry shall, in case of difference, be settled by the sheriff on the application of either party; and such expences shall include all reasonable charges and expences incurred in summoning, impannelling, and returning the jury, taking the inquiry, the attendance of witnesses, the employment of counsel and agents, recording the verdict and judgment thereon, and otherwise incident to such inquiry, including . . . [^c5624141] to the sheriff . . . [^c5624141] his reasonable travelling expences . . . [^c5624141] Provided always, that . . . [^c5624141] in all cases of inquiry as aforesaid before the sheriff, with or without a jury, the . . . [^c5624141] expences of the sheriff shall be borne by the promoters of the undertaking.
