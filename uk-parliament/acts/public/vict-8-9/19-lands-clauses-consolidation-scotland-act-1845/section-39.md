# Lands Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 39

> Text as at 2017-04-11.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Jurymen to be summoned.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Upon the receipt of such petition as aforesaid the sheriff shall summon a jury of twenty-five indifferent persons, duly qualified to act as common jurymen, for the trial of civil causes in the Court of Session, to meet at a time and place to be named by the sheriff in the warrant for that purpose.
