# Railways Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 154

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 154. Penalty on company failing to keep or deposit Act.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the company shall fail to keep or deposit, as hereinbefore mentioned, any of the said copies of the special Act, they shall forfeit level 2 on the standard scale[^c5590611] for every such offence, and also five pounds for every day afterwards during which such copy shall be not so kept or deposited.
