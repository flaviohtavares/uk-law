# Railways Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 81

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Contract not to affect persons not parties to it.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Provided always, that no such contract as aforesaid shall in any manner alter, affect, increase, or diminish any of the tolls which the respective companies, parties to such contracts, shall for the time being be respectively authorized and entitled to demand or receive from any person or any other company, but that all other persons and companies shall, notwithstanding any such contract, be entitled to the use and benefit of any of the said railways, upon the same terms and conditions, and on payment of the same tolls, as they would have been in case no such contract had been entered into.
