# Railways Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 50

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Penalty for failing to restore road.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If any such road be not so restored, or the substituted road so completed as aforesaid, within the periods herein or in the special Act fixed for that purpose, the company shall forfeit to the roads authority for[^c5589291] the road interfered with by the company, if a public road, or if a private road to the owner thereof, twenty pounds for every day after the expiration of such periods respectively during which such road shall not be so restored or the substituted road completed; and it shall be lawful for the sheriff or justices by whom any such penalty is imposed to order the whole or any part thereof to be laid out in executing the work in respect whereof such penalty was incurred.
