# Railways Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 87

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 87. Milestones.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The company shall cause the length of the railway to be measured, and posts or other conspicuous objects to be set up and maintained along the whole line thereof, at the distance of one quarter of a mile from each other, with numbers or marks inscribed thereon denoting such distances.
