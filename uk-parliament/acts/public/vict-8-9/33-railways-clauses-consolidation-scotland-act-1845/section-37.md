# Railways Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 37

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 37. Compensation to be ascertained under the Lands Clauses Acts.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The amount and application of the purchase money and other compensation payable by the company in any of the cases aforesaid shall be determined in the manner provided by the said Land Clauses Consolidation Act for determining the amount and application of the compensation to be paid for lands taken under the provisions thereof.
