# Railways Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 96

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 96. Penalty on passengers practising frauds on the company.[^c5590131]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If any person travel or attempt to travel in any carriage of the company, or of any other company or party using the railway, without having previously paid his fare, and with intent to avoid payment thereof, or if any person, having paid his fare for a certain distance, knowingly and wilfully proceed in any such carriage beyond such distance without previously paying the additional fare for the additional distance, and with intent to avoid payment thereof, or if any person knowingly and wilfully refuse or neglect, on arriving at the point to which he has paid his fare, to quit such carriage, every such person shall for every such offence forfeit to the company a sum not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale[^c5590141].
