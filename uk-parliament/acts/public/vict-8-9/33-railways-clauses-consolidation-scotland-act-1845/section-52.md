# Railways Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 52

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Company to make sufficient approaches and fences to bridleways and footways crossing on the level.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If the railway shall cross any road[^c5589321] other than the carriageway of a public road[^c5589331] on the level, the company shall at their own expence make and at all times maintain convenient ascents and descents and other convenient approaches, with handrails and other fences, and shall, if such road be a cycle track or[^c5589341] bridleway, erect and at all times maintain good and sufficient gates, and if the same shall be a footway or footpath[^c5589351], good and sufficient gates or stiles on each side of the railway, where the road shall communicate therewith.
