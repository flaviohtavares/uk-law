# Railways Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 65

> Text as at 2017-12-28.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 65. Accommodation works not to be required after five years.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The company shall not be compelled to make any further or additional accommodation works for the use of owners and occupiers of land adjoining the railway after the expiration of the prescribed period, or, if no period be prescribed, after five years from the opening of the railway for public use.
