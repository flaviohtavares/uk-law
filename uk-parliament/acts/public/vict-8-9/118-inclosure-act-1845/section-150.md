# Inclosure Act 1845, section 150

> Text as at 2014-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 150. Notices of intended exchanges and divisions to be given.

*Extent: E+W*

Provided always, that no such order of exchange or order of division and allotment as aforesaid shall be confirmed by the commissioners until notice shall have been given by advertisement in two[^c562282] successive weeks of such proposed exchange or division and allotment and one month shall have elapsed from the publication of the last of such advertisements; and in case before the expiration of such one month any person entitled to any estate in or to any charge upon any land included in such proposed exchange or division and allotment shall give notice in writing to the commissioners of his dissent from such proposed exchange or division and allotment, as the case may be, the commissioners shall not confirm an order for such exchange or such division or allotment unless such dissent shall be withdrawn, or it shall be shown to the commissioners that the estate or charge of the party so dissenting shall have ceased.
