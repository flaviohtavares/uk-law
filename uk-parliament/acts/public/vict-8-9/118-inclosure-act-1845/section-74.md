# Inclosure Act 1845, section 74

> Text as at 2014-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 74. †Provision for awarding allotments for exercise to individuals, subject to the obligation of permitting it to be used.[^c562187][^c562188]

*Extent: E+W*

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c562189] the person to whom the land so to be appropriated shall be allotted, and all future owners thereof, shall, unless it shall be otherwise directed by the award, be subject to the obligation of maintaining the fences of such land, and of preserving the surface thereof in good condition, and of permitting such land to be at all times used for exercise and recreation by the inhabitants of the parish and neighbourhood, and, subject to such obligations, the herbage of such land shall belong to the person to whom such land shall be so allotted.
