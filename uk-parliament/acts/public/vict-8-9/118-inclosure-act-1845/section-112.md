# Inclosure Act 1845, section 112

> Text as at 2014-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 112. Rents of allotment how to be applied.[^c562235]

*Extent: E+W*

All rents payable in respect of the allotment under the management of the allotment wardens shall be payable to such wardens, who shall have the same remedies for recovery thereof under section 72(1) of the Tribunals, Courts and Enforcement Act 2007 (commercial rent arrears recovery)[^c-d889a1e15e489f3c6cd1524608e8e696] and otherwise as if the legal estate of and in such allotment were vested in them under this Act; and such rents shall be applicable, in the first place, to the payment of all rates, taxes, tithes, tithe rent-charge, . . . [^c562236], and of all expences incurred by the allotment wardens in the execution of their trusts and powers under this Act; . . . [^c562237]
