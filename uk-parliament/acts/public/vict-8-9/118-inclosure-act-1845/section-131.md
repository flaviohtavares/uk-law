# Inclosure Act 1845, section 131

> Text as at 2014-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 131. Persons attending meetings to pay their own expences.

*Extent: E+W*

Provided always, that the several persons interested in the inclosure or other proceeding under this Act, and their respective agents, shall pay their own expences whenever they shall attend any of the meetings to be held in the matter of any inclosure.
