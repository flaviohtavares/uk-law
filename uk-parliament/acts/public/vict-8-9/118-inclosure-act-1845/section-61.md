# Inclosure Act 1845, section 61

> Text as at 2014-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 61. Power to valuer to make watercourses, &c.

*Extent: E+W*

[^c562151]. . .All such ponds, ditches, watercourses, embankments, tunnels, and bridges shall at all times afterwards be repaired, cleansed, and maintained by such persons and in such manner as the valuer shall direct. [^c562151]. . .
