# Inclosure Act 1845, section 107

> Text as at 2014-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 107. Allotments may be sub-divided by supplemental order.

*Extent: E+W*

[^c562229]. . .Every such separate instrument shall have the same power and effect as if it were contained in the said award; and such instrument shall be engrossed, and deposited with the award, and shall thenceforth be deemed to be part thereof to all intents and purposes. [^c562229]. . .
