# Inclosure Act 1845, section 164

> Text as at 2014-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 164. Persons giving false evidence, &c. to be guilty of a misdemeanor.

*Extent: E+W*

If any person under the provisions of this Act . . . [^c562307] shall wilfully refuse to attend in obedience to any lawful summons of the commissioners or an assistant commissioner or valuer, or to give evidence or shall wilfully alter, withhold, destroy, or refuse to produce any book, court roll, or writing, map, plan, or survey or any copy of the same, which may be lawfully required to be produced before the commissioners or assistant commissioner or valuer, he shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor.
