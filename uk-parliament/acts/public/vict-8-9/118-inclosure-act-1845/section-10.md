# Inclosure Act 1845, section 10

> Text as at 2014-04-06.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 10. Commissioners may delegate powers to assistant commissioners.

*Extent: E+W*

The commissioners may delegate to the assistant commissioners, or to any one or more of them, such of the powers hereby given to the commissioners as the commissioners shall think fit, (except the power to confirm awards, or to do any act herein required to be done under the seal of the commissioners,) and the power so delegated shall be exercised under such regulations as the commissioners shall direct; and the commissioners may at any time recall or alter all or any of the powers delegated as aforesaid, and, notwithstanding the delegation thereof, may act as if no such delegation had been made; and all acts done by any such assistant commissioner in pursuance of such delegated power shall be obeyed by all persons as if they had proceeded from the commissioners, and the non-observance thereof shall be punishable in like manner.
