# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 26

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 26. Enforcement of calls by action.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If at the time appointed by the company for the payment of any call any shareholder fail to pay the amount of such call, it shall be lawful for the company to sue such shareholder for the amount thereof in any court of law or equity having competent jurisdiction, and to recover the same, with lawful interest from the day on which such call was payable.
