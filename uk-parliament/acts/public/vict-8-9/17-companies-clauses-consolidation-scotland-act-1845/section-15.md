# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 15

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Regulating the form of transfers of shares.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c558254] All transfers of shares of the company shall be valid and effectual if executed according to the usual mode of executing such instruments either in England or Scotland, or partly according to the one and partly according to the other.
