# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 60

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 60. New shares to be considered same as original shares.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The capital so to be raised by the creation of new shares shall be considered as part of the general capital, and shall be subject to the same provisions in all respects, whether with reference to the payment of calls, or the forfeiture of shares on nonpayment of calls, or otherwise, as if it had been part of the original capital, except as to the times of making calls for such additional capital, and the amount of such calls, which respectively it shall be lawful for the company from time to time to fix as they shall think fit.
