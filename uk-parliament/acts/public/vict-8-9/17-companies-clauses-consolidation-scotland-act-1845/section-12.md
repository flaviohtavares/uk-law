# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 12

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 12. Certificate to be evidence.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The said certificate shall be admitted in all courts as a prima facie evidence of the title of such shareholder, his executors, administrators, successors, or assigns, to the share therein specified; nevertheless the want of such certificate shall not prevent the holder of any share from disposing thereof.
