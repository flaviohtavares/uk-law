# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 136

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 136. Costs to be in the discretion of the arbitrators.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Except where by this or the special Act, or any Act incorporated therewith, it shall be otherwise provided, the costs of and attending every such arbitration to be determined by the arbitrators shall be in the discretion of the arbitrators or the umpire, as the case may be.
