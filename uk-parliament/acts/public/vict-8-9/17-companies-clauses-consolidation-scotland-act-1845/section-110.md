# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 110

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 110. Duty of auditors.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

It shall be the duty of such auditors to receive from the directors the half-yearly or other periodical accounts and balance sheet required to be presented to the shareholders, and to examine the same.
