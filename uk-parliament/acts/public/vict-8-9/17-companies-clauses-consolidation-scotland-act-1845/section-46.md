# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 46

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Mortgages to be personal estate.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

All mortgages and money lent on mortgage to the company shall be personal estate, and transmissible as such, and shall not be of the nature of real estate.
