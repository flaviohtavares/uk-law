# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 80

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 80. Regulations as to proxies.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No person shall be entitled to vote as a proxy unless the instrument appointing such proxy have been transmitted to the secretary of the company within the prescribed period, or, if no period be prescribed, not less than forty-eight hours before the time appointed for holding the meeting at which such proxy is to be used.
