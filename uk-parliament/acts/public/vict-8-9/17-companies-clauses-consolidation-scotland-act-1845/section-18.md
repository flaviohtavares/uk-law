# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 18

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 18. Closing of transfer books.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

It shall be lawful for the directors to close the register of transfers for the prescribed period, or if no period be prescribed then for a period not exceeding fourteen days previous to each ordinary meeting, and they may fix a day for the closing of the same, of which seven days notice shall be given by advertisement in some newspaper as after mentioned; and any transfer made during the time when the transfer books are so closed shall, as between company and the party claiming under the same, but not otherwise, be considered as made subsequently to such ordinary meeting.
