# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 52

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Transfers of interest to be stamped.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The interest on any such mortgage or bond shall not be transferable except by deed duly stamped.
