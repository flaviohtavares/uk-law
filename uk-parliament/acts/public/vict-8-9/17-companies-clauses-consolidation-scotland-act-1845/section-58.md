# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 58

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 58. Access to account books by mortgagees.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

At all reasonable times the books of account of the company shall be open to the inspection of the respective mortgagees and bond creditors thereof, with liberty to take extracts therefrom, without fee or reward.
