# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 72

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72. Business at extraordinary meetings.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No extraordinary meetings shall enter upon any business not set forth in the notice upon which it shall have been convened.
