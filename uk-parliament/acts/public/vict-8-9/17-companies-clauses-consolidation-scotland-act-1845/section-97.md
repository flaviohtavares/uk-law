# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 97

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 97. Occasional chairman of directors.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If at any meeting of the directors neither the chairman nor deputy chairman be present, the directors present shall choose some one of their number to be chairman of such meeting.
