# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 64

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 64. Power to consolidate shares into stock.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

It shall be lawful for the company from time to time, with the consent of three fifths of the votes of the shareholders present in person or by proxy at any general meeting of the company, when due notice for that purpose shall have been given, to convert or consolidate all or any part of the shares then existing in the capital of the company, and in respect whereof the whole money subscribed shall have been paid up, into a general capital stock, to be divided amongst the shareholders according to their respective interests therein.
