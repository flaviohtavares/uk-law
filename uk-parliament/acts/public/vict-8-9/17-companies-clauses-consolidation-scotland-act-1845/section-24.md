# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 24

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 24. Interest to be paid on calls unpaid.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If, before or on the day appointed for payment, any shareholder do not pay the amount of any call to which he is liable, then such shareholder shall be liable to pay interest for the same, at the rate allowed by law, from the day appointed for the payment thereof to the time of the actual payment.
