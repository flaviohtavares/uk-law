# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 4

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Short title of the Act.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In citing this Act in other Acts of Parliament and in legal instruments it shall be sufficient to use the expression “The Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845.”
