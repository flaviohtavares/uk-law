# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 11

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Certificates of shares to be issued to the shareholders.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

On demand of the holder of any share the company shall cause a certificate of the proprietorship of such share to be delivered to such shareholder; and such certificate shall have the common seal of the company affixed thereto; and such certificate shall specify the share in the undertaking to which such shareholder is entitled; and the same may be according to the form in the schedule (A.) to this Act annexed, or to the like effect; and for such certificate the company may demand any sum not exceeding the prescribed amount, or if no amount be prescribed then a sum not exceeding 12½p[^c558252].
