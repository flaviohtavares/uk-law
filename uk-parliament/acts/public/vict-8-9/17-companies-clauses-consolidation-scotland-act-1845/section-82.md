# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 82

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 82. Votes of lunatics and minors, &c.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If any shareholder is suffering from mental disorder within the meaning of the[^c558262] [^c558263]Mental Health (Scotland) Act 1960, and is incapable by reason of his mental disorder of managing and administering his property and affairs, he may vote by his tutor, curator, or other person appointed to manage his estate; and if any shareholder be a minor he may vote by his tutors or curators or any one of them; and every such vote may be given either in person or by proxy.
