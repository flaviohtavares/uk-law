# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 29

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Proof of proprietorship.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The production of the register of shareholders shall be prima facie evidence of such defender being a shareholder, and of the number and amount of his shares.
