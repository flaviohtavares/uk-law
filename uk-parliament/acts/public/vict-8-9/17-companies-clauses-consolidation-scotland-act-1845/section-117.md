# Companies Clauses Consolidation (Scotland) Act 1845, section 117

> Text as at 2009-11-23.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 117. Sureties not to be discharged.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

No such proceeding against or dealing with any such officer as aforesaid shall deprive the company of any remedy which they might otherwise have against such officer, or any surety of such officer.
