# Land Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 106

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/8-9/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 106. If no committee be appointed, the amount to be determined by a surveyor.[^c5584771][^key-9cad8a85b306201137b74a70c4918d0f]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

If, upon being duly convened by the promoters of the undertaking, no effectual meeting of the parties entitled to such commonable or other rights shall take place or if, taking place, such meeting fail to appoint such committee, the amount of such compensation shall be determined by a surveyor, . . . [^c5584781].
