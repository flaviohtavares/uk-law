## 11. Payment of rents to be charged on tolls.[^c5583481]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

The yearly rents reserved by any such conveyance shall be charged on the tolls or rates, if any, payable under the special Act, and shall be otherwise secured in such manner as shall be agreed between the parties, and shall be paid by the promoters of the undertaking as such rents become payable; and if at any time any such rents be not paid within thirty days after they so become payable, and after demand thereof in writing, the person to whom any such rent shall be payable may either recover the same from the promoters of the undertaking, . . . [^c5583491] in any of the superior courts, [^c-e202c70567518729ba470e7b8f9dd847]....
