# Land Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 92

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/8-9/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Parties not to be required to sell part of a house.[^c5584601][^c5584611][^c5584621]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

And be it enacted, that no party shall at any time be required to sell or convey to the promoters of the undertaking a part only of any house or other building or manufactory, if such party be willing and able to sell and convey the whole thereof.
