# Land Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 151

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/8-9/18

> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 151. Penalty on company failing to keep, &c.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

If the company shall fail to keep or deposit, as herein-before mentioned, any of the said copies of the special Act, they shall forfeit twenty pounds[^c5585451] level 2 on the standard scale for every such offence, and also five pounds for every day afterwards during which such copy shall be not so kept or deposited.
