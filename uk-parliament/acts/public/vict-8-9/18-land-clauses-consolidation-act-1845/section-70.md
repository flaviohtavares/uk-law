# Land Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/8-9/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Order for application, and investment meanwhile.[^c5583811][^c5583821]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Such money may be so applied as aforesaid upon an order of the Court of Chancery . . . [^c5583831] made on the petition of the party who would have been entitled to the rents and profits of the lands in respect of which such money shall have been deposited, and if, before it is so applied, it is[^c5583841] invested under section 38 of the Administration of Justice Act 1982[^c5583851] the annual proceeds thereof shall be paid to the party who would for the time being have been entitled to the rents and profits of the lands.
