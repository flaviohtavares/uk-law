# Land Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 78

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/8-9/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 78. Application of monies so deposited.[^c5584181][^c5584191]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Upon the application . . . [^c5584201] of any party making claim to the money so paid or[^c5584211] deposited as last aforesaid, or any part thereof, or to the lands in respect whereof the same shall have been so paid or deposited, or any Part of such lands, or any interest in the same, the said Court of Chancery . . . [^c5584231] may . . . [^c5584201]order distribution of the money according to the respective estates, titles or interests of the parties making claim to such money or lands, or any part thereof, and if, before the money is distributed, it is[^c5584251] invested under section 38 of the Administration of Justice Act 1982[^c5584261] payment likewise of the dividends thereof and may make such other order in the premises as to such court shall seem fit.
