# Land Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 76

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/8-9/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 76. Where parties refuse to convey, or do not show title, or cannot be found, the purchase money to be deposited.[^c5584081][^c5584091]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

If the owner of any such lands purchased or taken by the promoters of the undertaking, or of any interest therein, on tender of the purchase money or compensation either agreed or awarded to be paid in respect thereof refuse to accept the same, or neglect or fail to make out a title to such lands, or to the interest therein claimed by him, to the satisfaction of the promoters of the undertaking, or if he refuse to convey or release such lands as directed by the promoters of the undertaking, or if any such owner be absent from the kingdom, or cannot after diligent inquiry be found, . . . [^c5584101] it shall be lawful for the promoters of the undertaking to pay into the[^c5584111] Senior Courts[^c-e307ba444cf2b2f68b0c5bef1f904ba7] the purchase money or compensation payable in respect of such lands.
