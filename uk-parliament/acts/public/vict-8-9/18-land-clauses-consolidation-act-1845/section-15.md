# Land Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 15

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/8-9/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 15. Municipal corporations not to sell without the approbation of the Treasury.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Nothing in this or the special Act contained shall enable any municipal corporation to sell for the purposes of the special Act, without the approbation of the Treasury, any lands which they could not have sold without such approbation before the passing of the special Act, other than such lands as the company are by the powers of this or the special Act empowered to purchase or take compulsorily.
