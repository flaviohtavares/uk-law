# Land Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 68

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (1).** Text as at 2023-12-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/Vict/8-9/18
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 68. †To be settled by arbitration or jury, at the option of the party claiming compensation.[^c5583681][^c5583691][^c5583701][^c5583711][^c5583721][^key-1decd1a163b58ed0f1861e29160b840e][^c5583671]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

If any party shall be entitled to any compensation in respect of any lands, or of any interest therein, which shall have been taken for or injuriously affected by the execution of the works, and for which the promoters of the undertaking shall not have made satisfaction under the provisions of this or the special Act, or any Act incorporated therewith, . . . [^c5583731] such party may have the same settled . . . [^c5583731]
