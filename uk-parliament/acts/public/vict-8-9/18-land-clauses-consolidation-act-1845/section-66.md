# Land Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 66

## 66. If further sum awarded, promoters to pay or deposit same within 14 days.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

If the arbitrators shall award that a further sum ought to be paid or deposited by the promoters of the undertaking, they shall pay or deposit as the case may require, such further sum within fourteen days after the making of such award, or in default thereof the same may . . . [^c5583651] recovered, . . . [^c5583651] in any of the superior courts.
