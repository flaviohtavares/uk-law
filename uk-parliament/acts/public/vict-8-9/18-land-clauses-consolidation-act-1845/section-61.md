## 61. Valuation, &c. to be produced to the owner of the lands on demand.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

. . . [^c5583601]the valuation to be made by such surveyor . . . [^c5583601]shall be preserved . . . [^c5583601]by the promoters of the undertaking, and they shall at all times produce the said valuation . . . [^c5583601]on demand, to the owner of the lands comprised in such valuation, and to all other parties interested therein.
