# Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 68

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 68. Extraordinary meetings.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Every general meeting of the shareholders, other than an ordinary meeting, shall be called an “Extraordinary Meeting”; and such meetings may be convened by the directors at such times as they think fit.
