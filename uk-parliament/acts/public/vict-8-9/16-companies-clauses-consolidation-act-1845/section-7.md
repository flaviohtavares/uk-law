# Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 7

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 7. Shares to be personal estate.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

All shares in the undertaking shall be personal estate, and transmissible as such, and shall not be of the nature of real estate.
