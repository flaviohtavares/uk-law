# Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 102

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 102. Qualification of auditors.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Where no other qualification shall be prescribed by the special Act, every auditor shall have at least one share in the undertaking; and he shall not hold any office in the company, nor be in any other manner interested in its concerns, except as a shareholder.
