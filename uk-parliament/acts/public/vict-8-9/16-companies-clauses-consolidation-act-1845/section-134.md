# Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 134

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 134. Submission to arbitration to be made rule of court.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

The submission to any such arbitration may be made a rule of any of the superior courts, on the application of either of the parties.
