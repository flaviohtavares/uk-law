# Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 52

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 52. Interest to cease on expiration of notice to pay off mortgage or bond.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

If the company shall have given notice of their intention to pay off any such mortgage or bond at a time when the same may lawfully be paid off by them, then at the expiration of such notice all further interest shall cease to be payable on such mortgage or bond, unless, on demand of payment made pursuant to such notice, or at any time thereafter, the company shall fail to pay the principal and interest due at the expiration of such notice on such mortgage or bond.
