# Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 46

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Transfers of mortgages and bonds to be stamped.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Any party entitled to any such mortgage or bond may from time to time transfer his right and interest therein to any other person; and every such transfer shall be by deed duly stamped, wherein the consideration shall be truly stated; and every such transfer may be according to the form in the schedule (E.) to this Act annexed, or to the like effect.
