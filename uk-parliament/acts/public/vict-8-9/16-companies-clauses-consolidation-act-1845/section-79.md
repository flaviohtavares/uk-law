# Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 79

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 79. Votes of lunatics and minors, &c.[^c5582011]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

If any shareholder be a person of unsound mind[^c5582021] or idiot, such person of unsound mind or idiot may vote by his committee; and if any shareholder be a minor he may vote by his guardian or any one of his guardians; and every such vote may be given either in person or by proxy.
