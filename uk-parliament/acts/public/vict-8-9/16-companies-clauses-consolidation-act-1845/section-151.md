# Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 151

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: Northern Ireland.

## 151. Distress not unlawful for want of form.[^c5582201]

*Extent: N.I.*

No distress levied by virtue of this or the special Act, or any Act incorporated therewith, shall be deemed unlawful, not shall any party making the same be deemed a trespasser, on account of any defect or want of form in the summons, conviction, warrant of distress, or other proceeding relating thereto, nor shall such party be deemed a trespasser ab initio on account of any irregularity afterwards committed by him, but all persons aggrieved by such defect or irregularity may recover full satisfaction for the special damage in an action upon the case.
