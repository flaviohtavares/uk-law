# Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 69

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Business at extraordinary meetings.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

No extraordinary meeting shall enter upon any business not set forth in the notice upon which it shall have been convened.
