# Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 137

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 137. Notices to joint proprietors of shares.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

All notices directed to be given to the shareholders shall, with respect to any share to which persons are jointly entitled, be given to whichever of the said persons shall be named first in the register of shareholders; and notice so given shall be sufficient notice to all the proprietors of such share.
