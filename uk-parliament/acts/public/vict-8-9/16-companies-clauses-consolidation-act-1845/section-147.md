# Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 147

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 147. Penalties to be summarily recovered before two justices.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Every penalty or forfeiture imposed by this or the special Act, or any Act incorporated therewith, or by any bye-law made in pursuance thereof, the recovery of which is not otherwise provided for, may be recovered by summary proceeding before two justices . . . [^c5582161]
