# Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 4

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Short title of the Act.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

In citing this Act in other Acts of Parliament and in legal instruments it shall be sufficient to use the expression “The Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845.”
