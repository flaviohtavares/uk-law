# Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 49

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Transfers of interest to be stamped.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

The interest on any such mortgage or bond shall not be transferable, except by deed duly stamped.
