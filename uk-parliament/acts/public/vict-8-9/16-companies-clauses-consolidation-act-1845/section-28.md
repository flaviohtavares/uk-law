# Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 28

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 28. Proof of proprietorship.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

The production of the register of shareholders shall be prima facie evidence of such defendant being a shareholder, and of the number and amount of his shares.
