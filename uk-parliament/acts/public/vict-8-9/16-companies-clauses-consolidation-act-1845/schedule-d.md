## SCHEDULE (D) — Form of Bond.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Sect. 41.

“The Company.”

Bond, number £

By virtue of \[here name the special Act\], we, “The Company,” in consideration of the sum of pounds to us in hand paid by A.B. of , do bind ourselves and our successors unto the said A.B., his executors, administrators, and assigns, in the penal sum of pounds.

The condition of the above obligation is such, that if the said company shall pay to the said A.B., his executors, administrators, or assigns, \[at (in case any other place of payment than the principal office of the company be intended),\] on the day of which will be in the year one thousand eight hundred and , the principal sum of pounds, together with interest for the same at the rate of pounds per centum per annum, payable half-yearly on the day of and day of , then the above-written obligation is to become void, otherwise to remain in full force. Given under our common seal, this day of one thousand eight hundred and .
