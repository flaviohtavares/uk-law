# Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 118

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 118. Balance sheet to be produced at the meeting.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

The directors shall produce to the shareholders assembled at such ordinary meeting the said balance sheet, applicable to the period immediately preceding such meeting, together with the report of the auditors thereon, as herein-before provided.
