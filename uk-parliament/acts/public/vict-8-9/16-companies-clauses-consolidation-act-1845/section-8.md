# Companies Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 8

> Text as at 2022-03-25.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 8. Shareholders.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Every person who shall have subscribed the prescribed sum or upwards to the capital of the company, or shall otherwise have become entitled to a share in the company, and whose name shall have been entered on the register of shareholders herein-after mentioned, shall be deemed a shareholder of the company.
