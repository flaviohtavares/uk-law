# Railways Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 86

> Text as at 2024-12-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 86. Company to employ locomotive power, carriages, &c.[^c6405231][^c-f29b8311510eb20586621373207c4050][^c-11d4f5aaf3a1dd12777b5d0417abe40d][^c-7c597854dc43ac5a6185b543a02d116d]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

It shall be lawful for the company to use and employ locomotive engines or other moving power, and carriages and waggons to be drawn or propelled thereby, and to carry and convey upon the railway all such passengers and goods as shall be offered to them for that purpose . . . [^c6405241]
