# Railways Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 163

> Text as at 2024-12-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 163. Penalty on company failing to keep or deposit such copies.[^c-4959ee2e45a3b0befe8bd8be9146a021][^c-caa2af93821aed92f3201ec8b45be2dd]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

If the company shall fail to keep or deposit as hereinbefore mentioned, any of the said copies of the special Act, they shall forfeit level 2 on the standard scale[^c6406101] for every such offence, and also five pounds for every day afterwards during which such copy shall be not so kept or deposited.
