# Railways Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 95

> Text as at 2024-12-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 95. †Tolls to be taken only whilst boards exhibited and milestones set up. Penalty for defacing, etc. milestones.[^c6405301]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

. . . [^c6405311] if any person wilfully pull down . . . [^c6405321] any such . . . [^c6405311] milestone, he shall forfeit a sum not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale[^c6405341] for every such offence.
