## 103. Penalty on passengers practising frauds on the company.[^c6405411][^c-2074e0c933ec2d85ae802549d17f4ce5][^c-05fa7aa6dee9abde786a7440f84b1fda][^c-ea31029871defa9848434dce3f3f6c6d][^c-48e84b747d92f4e7c49062c844e6f393][^c-020015e01a45d3f2dfffeb887fcad53a][^c-1224e7cef7a9ffad6ef8aca5b0020e8d][^c-3fe748982a28039e7835c8f570de4677][^c-abf37e72080fbf6a0b52a790f1197b1f][^c-396314d46e4779b5fabd879aab19975f][^c-cb8ea32533f9b7b3a87c870d0da68eed][^c-3eb9d089476e292b1cf265458939c3df][^c-47e8715ba1018d0e33f584f627c28514][^c-f2c9215aa4dab2b1fbcdb3207a0b7f1c][^c-965649a704f382b7d03592cfffd04744][^c-f1fdb305b550cfc34405b5dee7fdaf06][^c-10167809b1c3e89522790e7b6b450524][^c-066c7889beea6b815e081353bad5ef41][^c-987f4cb275b0ef246046c117895dd364][^c-69e85ff2f6e1e373e636ce3805cf20ad][^c-1990fd1f9d1efe32fee8500eb96c1b11][^c-e8b68ef4b9cbf3ec66aa6714f1ce2a08][^c-08719c4c08a65af31c52043dc92385b5][^c-9d3968c3f77878d76733225268d564b4][^c-9c0ebbe5b2cd49e4a58506c323965e20][^c-5980e764ffcb2a16ded7b31311b72aa0][^c6405401]

. . . [^c6405421] if any person knowingly and wilfully refuse or neglect, on arriving at the point to which he has paid his fare, to quit such carriage, every such person shall for every such offence forfeit . . . [^c6405431] a sum not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale[^c6405441].
