# Railways Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 4

> Text as at 2024-12-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 4. Short title of the Act.[^c-9e787866f46b3fbc55b278d09ac7f646][^c-bc2334cf48c12f08248dfdafb49d143c]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

In citing this Act in other Acts of Parliament and in legal instruments it shall be sufficient to use the expression “The Railways Clauses Consolidation Act 1845”.
