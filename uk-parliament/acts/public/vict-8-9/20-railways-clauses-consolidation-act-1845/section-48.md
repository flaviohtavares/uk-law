# Railways Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 48

> Text as at 2024-12-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 48. As to crossing of turnpike roads adjoining stations.

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

Where the railway crosses any turnpike road on a level adjoining to a station, . . . [^c6404321] the company shall be subject to all such rules and regulations with regard to such crossings as may from time to time be made by the Board of Trade.

## Annotations
