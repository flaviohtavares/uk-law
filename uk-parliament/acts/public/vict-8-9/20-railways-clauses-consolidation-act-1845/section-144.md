# Railways Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 144

> Text as at 2024-12-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 144. Penalty for defacing boards used for such publication.[^c-3f5d3ad496e40afeb4a6128d6d0960c0][^c-2db33180bd2ec2f5e0209ca9a9390974][^c-3609f992d9051a1d190346c5e5eec2d5][^c-05eab67a2fd902ce0762b05eb424694d][^c-d567f48eff5aa2eee4a2eba7ae409685][^c-d8e67a4e4e421506374e65cd9bba8535][^c-2e09a0e7b3353a5c832397807ef4a22e][^c-a43b689ec01121015bf65689e36ea4eb][^c6405851]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

If any person pull down or injure any board put up or affixed for the purpose of publishing any byelaw of the company or any penalty imposed by this or the special Act[^c6405861] , or shall obliterate any of the letters or figures thereon, he shall forfeit for every such offence a sum not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale[^c6405871] , and shall defray the expences attending the restoration of such board.
