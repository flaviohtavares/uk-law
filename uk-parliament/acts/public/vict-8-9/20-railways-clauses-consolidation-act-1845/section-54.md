# Railways Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 54

> Text as at 2024-12-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 54. Penalty for not substituting a road.[^c6404411]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

If the company do not cause another sufficient road to be so made before they interfere with any such existing road as aforesaid, they shall forfeit twenty pounds for every day during which such substituted road shall not be made after the existing road shall have been interrupted; and such penalty shall be paid to the trustees, commissioners, surveyor, or other person having the management of such road, if a public road, and shall be applied for the purposes thereof, or in case of a private road the same shall be paid to the owner thereof; and every such penalty shall be recoverable with costs by action in any of the superior courts.
