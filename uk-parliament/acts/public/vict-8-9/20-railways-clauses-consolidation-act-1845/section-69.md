# Railways Clauses Consolidation Act 1845, section 69

> Text as at 2024-12-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Northern Ireland.

## 69. Differences as to accommodation works to be settled by justices.[^c6404601][^c-cb00a8ce9ed263d9ff5d39b846668f6b][^c-4df62204bcd81443f010c36d73c1c07a][^c-8bfcedee7325cc104f17eee6789cd46e][^c-ae4e51ada65da58fce9d9b7e36aa2f75][^c-5add8b8a866dd82226cfc719eb588db5][^c-2858756bcea7f1a847b9f4733034d649][^c-37c4ceb9a8118867c73057dd0bfba92c]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

If any difference arise respecting the kind or number of any such accommodation works, or the dimensions or sufficiency thereof, or respecting the maintaining thereof, the same shall be determined by two justices; and such justice shall also appoint the time within which such works shall be commenced and executed by the company.
