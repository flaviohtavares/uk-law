# Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014, section L1

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## L1. Special measures for witnesses[^c-46ea62b2babd461d652a29242d0ad146]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) [Chapter 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/part/2/chapter/1) of [Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/part/2) of the [Youth Justice and Criminal Evidence Act 1999](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/contents) (special measures directions in the case of vulnerable and intimidated witnesses) applies to proceedings under this Part as it applies to criminal proceedings, but with—

- (a) the omission of [sections 17](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/section/17)[(4) to (7)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/section/17/subsection/4), [21](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/section/21)[(4C)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/section/21)[(e)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/section/21), [22A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/section/22A), [27](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/section/27)[(10)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/section/27) and [32](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/section/32) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/contents) (which make provision appropriate only in the context of criminal proceedings), and
- (b) any other necessary modifications.

(2) Rules of court made under or for the purposes of [Chapter 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/part/2/chapter/1) of [Part 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/part/2) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/contents) apply to proceedings under this Part—

- (a) to the extent provided by rules of court, and
- (b) subject to any modifications provided by rules of court.

(3) [Section 47](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/section/47) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/contents) (restrictions on reporting special measures directions etc) applies with any necessary modifications—

- (a) to a direction under [section 19](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/section/19) of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/contents) as applied by this section;
- (b) to a direction discharging or varying such a direction.

Sections 49 and 51 of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/contents) (offences) apply accordingly.

(4) This section does not affect the application of any provision of [that Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1999/23/contents) to criminal proceedings relating to an offence under section I1 of this Act.
