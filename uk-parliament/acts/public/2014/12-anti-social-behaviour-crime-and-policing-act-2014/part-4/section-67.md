# Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014, section 67

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 67. Offence of failing to comply with order[^c-bd7de3098c765703f1e4bd5adacedaec]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) It is an offence for a person without reasonable excuse—

- (a) to do anything that the person is prohibited from doing by a public spaces protection order or an expedited order[^c-9944aafe98e41cf40d4291e524a186f9], or
- (b) to fail to comply with a requirement to which the person is subject under a public spaces protection order or an expedited order.

(2) A person guilty of an offence under this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 3 on the standard scale.

(3) A person does not commit an offence under this section by failing to comply with a prohibition or requirement that the local authority did not have power to include in the public spaces protection order or expedited order[^c-7a5c94470d5ccefe07964b50e8781176].

(4) Consuming alcohol in breach of a public spaces protection order or an expedited order[^c-c1009ea361454b4d1596117f8614b9c7] is not an offence under this section (but see section 63).
