# Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/12

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Byelaws[^c-499f72dda35e526d31356fdef926079e]

*Extent: E+W*

A byelaw that prohibits, by the creation of an offence, an activity regulated by a public spaces protection order or an expedited order[^c-9d3fba26be702e8223f354b473d7dd60] is of no effect in relation to the restricted area during the currency of the order.
