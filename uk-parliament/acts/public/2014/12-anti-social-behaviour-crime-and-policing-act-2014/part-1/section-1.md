# Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014, section 1

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Power to grant youth injunctions[^key-d8f2302ef2c49976c7eaa85684e653b2][^key-7415db9afa1d3f0a221c77a42ee3a6bb]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A court may grant an injunction under this section (a “youth injunction”)[^key-00e07f1adc0e0c8f0e6afe1c8840bc49] against a person aged 10 or over but under 18[^key-d1a5f583e752f51de3f64cffc7f27b7a] (“**the respondent**”) if two conditions are met.

(2) The first condition is that the court is satisfied, on the balance of probabilities, that the respondent has engaged or threatens to engage in anti-social behaviour.

(3) The second condition is that the court considers it just and convenient to grant the injunction for the purpose of preventing the respondent from engaging in anti-social behaviour.

(4) A youth injunction[^key-246615fd862ef068b379fc776b339165] may for the purpose of preventing the respondent from engaging in anti-social behaviour—

- (a) prohibit the respondent from doing anything described in the injunction;
- (b) require the respondent to do anything described in the injunction.

(4A)[^key-30d621d3c6212f449396095fb91cb69c] But a youth injunction may not have the effect of excluding the respondent from the place where the respondent normally lives.

(5) Prohibitions and requirements in a youth injunction[^key-113f924cb60b6603e8885a60007134ae] must, so far as practicable, be such as to avoid—

- (a) any interference with the times, if any, at which the respondent normally attends school or any other educational establishment, or at which the respondent normally works[^key-9f3d4418365330820e4f541cc86861e9];
- (b) any conflict with the requirements of any other court order or injunction to which the respondent is[^key-e67020fefdc366e082dd328af891312f] subject.

(6)[^key-4317a8c5e58b81a4ae5900cb0ccc34c5] A youth injunction must specify the period for which it has effect, which must be no more than 12 months.

(7) A youth injunction[^key-cf7969c1eb704bff5f6227d9a8570394] may specify periods for which particular prohibitions or requirements have effect.

(8)[^key-05d507fef1f0ff694e34f570ac86ff39] An application for a youth injunction must be made to a youth court.

(9)[^key-b6a3f0de97519cf983b3951de8282297] In this Part, anti-social behaviour means—

- (a) conduct that has caused, or is likely to cause, harassment, alarm or distress to any person, or
- (b) housing-related anti-social conduct (see section 2).
