# Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014, section 6

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/12

## 6. Applications without notice[^c-e3a8d17317b1cde700a859161c9aedec][^c-0599284d15b225c76ceba197f0da02e5]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) An application for an injunction under this Part[^c-de7e3435cf00a54f048afa91b3e26f79] may be made without notice being given to the respondent.

(2) If an application is made without notice the court must either—

- (a) adjourn the proceedings and grant an interim injunction (see section 7), or
- (b) adjourn the proceedings without granting an interim injunction, or
- (c) dismiss the application.
