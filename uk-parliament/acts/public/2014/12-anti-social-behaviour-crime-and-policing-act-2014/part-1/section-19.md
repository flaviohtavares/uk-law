# Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014, section 19

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19. Guidance[^c-980f868ac9bdb271be8b503df3bf1bf3]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may issue guidance to persons entitled to apply for injunctions under this Part[^c-d1ca4688b08dbdab0e88d29ce10b376c] (see section 5) about the exercise of their functions under this Part.

(2) The Secretary of State may revise any guidance issued under this section.

(3) The Secretary of State must arrange for any guidance issued or revised under this section to be published.
