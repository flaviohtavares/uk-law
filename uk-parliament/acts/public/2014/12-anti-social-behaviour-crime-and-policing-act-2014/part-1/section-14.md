# Anti-social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014, section 14

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (26).** Text as at 2026-10-26. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/12
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 14. Requirements to consult etc[^c-238e8cd78bee9f5624311479499c7a4a]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) A person applying for an injunction under this Part[^c-fe6f948d64053e41b63142c62dd5c5c7] must before doing so—

- (a) in the case of a youth injunction,[^c-e389ab178af2b170a8f579749ad15dd3] consult the local youth offending team about the application [^c-f6bb3d9058b3056f6579c88fbfbca235]...;
- (b) in any case,[^c-98d5ff7760092322b5ba15dfc08f32c3] inform any (or, where paragraph (a) applies, any other).[^c-891a6b5f542c2f523c04f76ae6de45da] body or individual the applicant thinks appropriate of the application.

This subsection does not apply to a without-notice application.

(2) Where the court adjourns a without-notice application, before the date of the first on-notice hearing the applicant must—

- (a) in the case of a youth injunction,[^c-4bbfb09eab9e13ad0c07c71c50505c36] consult the local youth offending team about the application, if the respondent will be aged under 18 on that date;
- (b) in any case,[^c-6ff2ba59090384aa52111f36799aabb0] inform any (or, where paragraph (a) applies, any other)[^c-a0b3edee97e047adda6d7e631f9b55ae] body or individual the applicant thinks appropriate of the application.

(3) A person applying for variation or discharge of an injunction under this Part[^c-b4a7cbe4a97baba4ac1b6bfaacb0c585] granted on that person's application must before doing so—

- (a) in the case of a youth injunction,[^c-cc1ba803a82a7e99a595d6efef24f548] consult the local youth offending team about the application for variation or discharge, if the respondent will be aged under 18 when that application is made;
- (b) in any case,[^c-bbeb5eb2c3db86ac79e1ab1f0c96ad13] inform any (or, where paragraph (a) applies, any other)[^c-447d0e5f310160b68f4d1c223664bcfd] body or individual the applicant thinks appropriate of that application.

(4) In this section—

- “**local youth offending team**” means—
  - (a) the youth offending team in whose area it appears to the applicant that the respondent lives, or
  - (b) if it appears to the applicant that the respondent lives in more than one such area, whichever one or more of the relevant youth offending teams the applicant thinks it appropriate to consult;
- “**on-notice hearing**” means a hearing of which notice has been given to the applicant and the respondent in accordance with rules of court;
- “**without-notice application**” means an application made without notice under section 6.
