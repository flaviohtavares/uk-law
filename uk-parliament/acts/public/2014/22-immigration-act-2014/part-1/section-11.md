# Immigration Act 2014, section 11

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-02-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 11. Biometric immigration documents[^key-6f2c899647b5b6fba95f17b03750b468][^key-27323c4d7c376ffebe1ca4abc45ef3c3]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

After section 7(2) of the UK Borders Act 2007 (effect of failure to comply with regulations about biometric immigration documents) insert—

> (2A) If the regulations require a biometric immigration document to be used in connection with an application or claim, they may require or permit the application or claim to be disregarded or refused if that requirement is not complied with.
