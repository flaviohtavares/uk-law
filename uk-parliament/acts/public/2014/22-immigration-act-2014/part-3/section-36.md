## 36. Crown application[^key-94877ee8f8a6417e775f31806d9ff047]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

This Chapter binds the Crown, except where the Crown is the responsible landlord for the purposes of section 23 or the landlord for the purposes of section 33A.[^key-cc74e4c5f65f086cf7dcb1b316f8e507]
