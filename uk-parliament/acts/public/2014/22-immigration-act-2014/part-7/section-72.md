# Immigration Act 2014, section 72

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2026-02-04. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 72. Financial provision

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The following are to be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) expenditure incurred under or by virtue of this Act by the Secretary of State, and
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable under any other Act out of money so provided.
