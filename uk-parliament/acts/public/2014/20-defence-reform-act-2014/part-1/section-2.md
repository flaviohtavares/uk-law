# Defence Reform Act 2014, section 2

> This provision is not yet in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Financial assistance

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**Not yet in force.**

(1) The Secretary of State may give financial assistance to a contractor.

(2) The financial assistance may be given on terms and conditions that the Secretary of State thinks appropriate.

(3) “**Financial assistance**” means loans, guarantees or indemnities, or any other kind of financial assistance (actual or contingent).
