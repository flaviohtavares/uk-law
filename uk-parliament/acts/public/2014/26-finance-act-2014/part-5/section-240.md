## 240. Amendment , withdrawal or reissue of conduct notice[^c-c059304f5fcbc9ceb824f8bc1fb1364f]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where a conduct notice has been given to a person.

(2) An authorised officer may at any time amend the notice.

(3) An authorised officer—

- (a) may withdraw the notice if the officer thinks it is not necessary for it to continue to have effect, [^c-ed6221669f6f9986174d37a0cbec8ee4]...
- (b)[^c-ed6221669f6f9986174d37a0cbec8ee4] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c-4a1f314020a0cf51888f30d145cd3cef] An authorised officer may (instead of amending the notice) withdraw the notice and give a new conduct notice.
