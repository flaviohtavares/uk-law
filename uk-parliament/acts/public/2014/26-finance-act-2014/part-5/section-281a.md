# Finance Act 2014, section 281A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-06-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 281A. VAT and other indirect taxes[^c-a24f9bddd12b2bb2977b0da8fde1b184][^c-df953ab9622203582dd4935c06091011]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In the provisions mentioned in subsection (2)—

- (a) “**tax**” includes value added tax and other indirect taxes[^c-19b460f1c29eaa68c6dac50821f37317], and
- (b) “**tax advantage**” has the meaning given by section 234(3) and also includes a tax advantage as defined for[^c-7e49f82adc1f2b473bbc99ec0918e083] VAT in paragraph 6, and for other indirect taxes in paragraph 7, of Schedule 17 to FA 2017 (disclosure of tax avoidance schemes: VAT and other indirect taxes).

(2) Those provisions are—

- (a) section 237D;
- (b) section 241B;
- (c) Schedule 34A.

(3) Other references in this Part to “tax” are to be read as including value added tax or other indirect taxes[^c-65434b0a8f33478aca885bcc1de53491] so far as that is necessary for the purposes of sections 237A to 237D, 241A and 241B and Schedule 34A; but “**tax**” does not include value added tax or other indirect taxes in section 237A(10) or 237B(3).

(4)[^c-34e58d265a3250c2811790aafb750ca6] In this section “**indirect tax**” has the same meaning as in Schedule 17 to FA 2017.
