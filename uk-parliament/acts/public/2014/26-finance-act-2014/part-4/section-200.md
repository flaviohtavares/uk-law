# Finance Act 2014, section 200

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-06-25. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/26
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 200. “Relevant tax”

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In this Part, “**relevant tax**” means—

- (a) income tax,
- (b) capital gains tax,
- (c) corporation tax, including any amount chargeable as if it were corporation tax or treated as if it were corporation tax,
- (ca)[^key-f95657520613d234a730e6581ff239e3] apprenticeship levy,
- (d) inheritance tax,
- (e) stamp duty land tax, and
- (f) annual tax on enveloped dwellings.
