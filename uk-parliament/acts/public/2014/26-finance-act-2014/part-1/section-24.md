*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(3) Section 139 (cars with a CO<sub>2</sub> figure: the appropriate percentage) is amended in accordance with subsections (4) to (6).

- (a) in paragraph (a) for “5%” substitute “ 7% ”,

(9) In subsection (3)(a), for “5%” substitute “ 7% ”.
