## 31. Bereavement support payment: contribution condition and amendments

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^key-2df0132dbce19243e083f32000e864df][^key-d3d44f12e6879e354cbea361d518e8a6] For the purposes of section 30(1)(d) the contribution condition is that, for at least one tax year during the deceased's working life—

- (a) he or she actually paid Class 1 or Class 2 national insurance contributions, and
- (b) those contributions give rise to an earnings factor (or total earnings factors) equal to or greater than 25 times the lower earnings limit for the tax year.

(2)[^key-2df0132dbce19243e083f32000e864df][^key-d3d44f12e6879e354cbea361d518e8a6] For earnings factors, see sections 22 and 23 of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992.

(3)[^key-2df0132dbce19243e083f32000e864df][^key-d3d44f12e6879e354cbea361d518e8a6] For the purposes of section 30(1)(d) the contribution condition is to be treated as met if the deceased was an employed earner and died as a result of—

- (a) a personal injury of the kind mentioned in section 94(1) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992, or
- (b) a disease or personal injury of the kind mentioned in section 108(1) of that Act.

(4)[^key-2df0132dbce19243e083f32000e864df][^key-d3d44f12e6879e354cbea361d518e8a6] In this section the following expressions have the meaning given by section 122(1) of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992—

- “employed earner”,
- “lower earnings limit”,
- “tax year”, and
- “working life”.

(5)[^key-10fec75d56a2de1cc6bf219cbd84ecf7][^key-da784637894657404ece469603291d39] Schedule 16 contains amendments to do with bereavement support payment.
