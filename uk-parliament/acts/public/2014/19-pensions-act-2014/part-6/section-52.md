# Pensions Act 2014, section 52

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2026-04-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/19
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 52. Public service pension schemes: transitional arrangements[^c-c8761180b64d4d4fbb37476e0d1f0f01]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Section 18 of the Public Service Pensions Act 2013 (restriction of existing pension schemes) is amended as follows.

(2) After subsection (5) insert—

> (5A) Scheme regulations may also provide for exceptions to subsection (1) in the case of—
>
> - (a) persons who were members of a public body pension scheme specified in the regulations, or who were eligible to be members of such a scheme, immediately before 1 April 2012, and
> - (b) such other persons as the regulations may specify, being persons who before that date had ceased to be members of a scheme referred to in paragraph (a) or to be eligible for membership of such a scheme.

(3) In each of subsections (6) and (8), after “(5)” insert “ or (5A) ”.
