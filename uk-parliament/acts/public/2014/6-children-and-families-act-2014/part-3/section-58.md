# Children and Families Act 2014, section 58

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2026-10-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/6
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 58. Appeals and claims by children: pilot schemes (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-f2a8d39a671a4a00a97652b64280828b][^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c][^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may by order make pilot schemes enabling children in England to— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]

- (a) appeal to the First-tier Tribunal under section 51; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]
- (b) make a claim to the First-tier Tribunal under Schedule 17 to the Equality Act 2010 (disabled pupils: enforcement) that a responsible body in England has contravened Chapter 1 of Part 6 of that Act because of the child's disability. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]

(2) An order under subsection (1) may, in particular, make provision— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]

- (a) about the age from which children may appeal or make a claim; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]
- (b) in respect of appeals under section 51, about mediation and the application of section 55; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]
- (c) about the bringing of appeals or making of claims by a child and by his or her parent concurrently; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]
- (d) about determining whether a child is capable of bringing an appeal or making a claim, and the assistance and support a child may require to be able to do so; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]
- (e) enabling a person to exercise a child's rights under an order under subsection (1) on behalf of the child; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]
- (f) enabling children to have access to advice and information which is available to a parent or young person in respect of an appeal or claim of a kind mentioned in subsection (1); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]
- (g) about the provision of advocacy and other support services to children; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]
- (h) requiring notices to be given to a child (as well as to his or her parent); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]
- (i) requiring documents to be served on a child (as well as on his or her parent). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]

(3) An order under subsection (1) may apply a statutory provision, with or without modifications. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]

(4) In subsection (3), “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c] **statutory provision *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]** ” means a provision made by or under this or any other Act, whenever passed or made. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]

(5) This section is repealed at the end of five years beginning with the day on which this Act is passed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-6417af1590538bda58842d1642edec1c]
