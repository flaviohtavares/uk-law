# Local Audit and Accountability Act 2014, section 1A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (173).** Text as at 2026-10-05. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/2

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1A. Establishment and constitution[^c-d689777b59ea230db37999d939f7cf9b]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A body corporate called the Local Audit Office is established.

(2) Schedule 1A makes provision about the Office, including provision about its constitution and proceedings.
