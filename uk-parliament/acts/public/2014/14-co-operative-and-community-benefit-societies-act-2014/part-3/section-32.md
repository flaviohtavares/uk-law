# Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-08-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/14

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Bodies corporate may be members

*Extent: E+W+S*

Shares in a registered society may be held by any other body corporate (if that body's regulations so permit) by its corporate name.
