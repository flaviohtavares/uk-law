# Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014, section 104

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-08-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/14
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 104. Power of society to make rules authorising inspection of books

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A registered society may by its rules authorise the inspection of such of its books upon such conditions as may be specified in the rules.

(2) The rules may not authorise a person to inspect another person's loan or deposit account without that other person's written consent.

(3) Subsection (2) does not apply to inspection by an officer of the society or a person specially authorised by a resolution of the society.
