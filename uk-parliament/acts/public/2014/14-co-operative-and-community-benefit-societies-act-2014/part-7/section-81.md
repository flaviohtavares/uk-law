# Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014, section 81

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-08-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/14

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 81. Duty to display latest balance sheet at registered office[^key-aab491db179cbe7546e2d907b8deb175]

*Extent: E+W+S*

A registered society must, at all times, display a copy of its latest balance sheet in a conspicuous position at its registered office.
