# Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014, section 141

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2023-08-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/14

## 141. Recovery of costs and expenses[^key-e72c5caa8378ac5fce232723657b2a93]

*Extent: E+W+S*

Any costs or expenses ordered or directed by the FCA or PRA to be paid by any person under this Act are recoverable summarily (or, in Scotland, are recoverable) as a civil debt.
