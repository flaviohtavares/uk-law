# Transparency of Lobbying, Non-Party Campaigning and Trade Union Administration Act 2014, section 1

> Text as at 2026-02-18.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Prohibition on consultant lobbying unless registered[^key-a853e55741549ea4b0afea37fadf6c93]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) A person must not carry on the business of consultant lobbying unless the person is entered in the register of consultant lobbyists.

(2) Sections 3 to 7 make provision about the keeping and publication of the register.
