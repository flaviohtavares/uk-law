# Water Act 2014, section 53

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 53. Procedure

*Extent: E+W*

(1) Before making exit regulations, the Secretary of State is to consult—

- (a)[^c-933b7e3c1942fb3e5aa5b775d278089c] the Welsh Ministers;
- (b)[^c-933b7e3c1942fb3e5aa5b775d278089c] the WSRA;
- (c)[^c-933b7e3c1942fb3e5aa5b775d278089c] the CMA;
- (d)[^c-933b7e3c1942fb3e5aa5b775d278089c] the Consumer Council for Water;
- (e)[^c-933b7e3c1942fb3e5aa5b775d278089c] relevant undertakers whose areas are wholly or mainly in England;
- (f)[^c-a0f2eb0da2e358ba98144402c8f2f056] water supply licensees and sewerage licensees;
- (g)[^c-933b7e3c1942fb3e5aa5b775d278089c] persons whom the Secretary of State considers to represent the interests of investors in the water industry.

(2)[^c-933b7e3c1942fb3e5aa5b775d278089c] The power to make exit regulations is to be exercised by statutory instrument.

(3)[^c-933b7e3c1942fb3e5aa5b775d278089c] A statutory instrument containing exit regulations made by the Secretary of State may not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.
