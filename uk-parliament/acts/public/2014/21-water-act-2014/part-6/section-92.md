# Water Act 2014, section 92

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (4).** Text as at 2024-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/21
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 92. Financial provisions

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

There shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) expenditure incurred under or by virtue of this Act by the Secretary of State;
- (b) any increase attributable to this Act in the sums payable under any other Act out of money so provided.
