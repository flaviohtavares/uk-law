An Act to amend the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993 in relation to the permitted signatories of notices; and for connected purposes. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdfe99189cde1e9e35351f904fc88c63]

## 1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Amendment of the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-fdfe99189cde1e9e35351f904fc88c63][^key-fdfe99189cde1e9e35351f904fc88c63]

In section 99(5)(a) of the Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993 (requirement for notice under section 13 or 42 of that Act to be signed by tenants or tenant personally) after “42” insert *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdfe99189cde1e9e35351f904fc88c63] “ in respect of premises in Wales ” *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdfe99189cde1e9e35351f904fc88c63]. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdfe99189cde1e9e35351f904fc88c63]

## 2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note). Short title, commencement and extent (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note)[^key-fdfe99189cde1e9e35351f904fc88c63][^key-fdfe99189cde1e9e35351f904fc88c63]

(1 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fdfe99189cde1e9e35351f904fc88c63] This Act may be cited as the Leasehold Reform (Amendment) Act 2014. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdfe99189cde1e9e35351f904fc88c63]

(2 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fdfe99189cde1e9e35351f904fc88c63] This Act comes into force at the end of the period of two months beginning with the day on which it is passed. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdfe99189cde1e9e35351f904fc88c63]

(3 (Extent: W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-fdfe99189cde1e9e35351f904fc88c63] This Act extends to England and Wales only. *(Extent: W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-fdfe99189cde1e9e35351f904fc88c63]
