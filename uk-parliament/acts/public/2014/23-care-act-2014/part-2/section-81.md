# Care Act 2014, section 81

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 81. Duty of candour[^c-d88d064595aef301911258afae3f35fe][^c-fb3bcab7fc72fff7b02e21553218046c]

*Extent: E+W*

In section 20 of the Health and Social Care Act 2008 (regulation of regulated activities), after subsection (5) insert—

> (5A) Regulations under this section must make provision as to the provision of information in a case where an incident of a specified description affecting a person's safety occurs in the course of the person being provided with a service.
