# Care Act 2014, section 62

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 62. Power to meet child's carer's needs for support[^key-b5233cd91c08d092b8a21506059579ea][^key-4a62f516d0fe4d3070d9d19880c2ff17]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^key-848391b5b6b8dc8a9b3e92f2369f9985] Where a local authority, having carried out a child's carer's assessment, is satisfied that the carer has needs for support, it may meet such of those needs as it considers appropriate.

(2)[^key-3b57bbee4fb47ed341dd499d0b701d0d][^key-0c37f6e245815d2c395a3839ef70aed8] Regulations may make provision in connection with the exercise of the power under subsection (1); the regulations may, in particular, provide for provisions of this Part to apply with such modifications as may be specified.

(3)[^key-848391b5b6b8dc8a9b3e92f2369f9985] In deciding whether or how to exercise the power under subsection (1), a local authority must have regard to any services being provided to the carer under—[^key-0d81bdb4a71e535e3bfd6620b71809d2]

- (a) section 17 of the Children Act 1989, or
- (b) sections 37 to 39 of the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014.

(4)[^key-848391b5b6b8dc8a9b3e92f2369f9985] “**Carer**” has the same meaning as in section 60.
