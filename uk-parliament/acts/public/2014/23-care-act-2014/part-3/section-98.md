# Care Act 2014, section 98

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (15).** Text as at 2026-07-15. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/2014/23
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 98. Ensuring sufficient skilled health care workers for the health service[^c-2a36907c0cffb013c94728f68c6377a4]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) NHS England[^c-48c5a5f7acbd6e3339c6e35ad943c8df] must exercise its functions under this Chapter[^c-f93809eaae96422f9eeffa9a2e01a371] with a view to ensuring that a sufficient number of persons with the skills and training to work as health care workers for the purposes of the health service is available to do so throughout England.

(2) Regulations may provide that the duty under subsection (1) may only be performed, or may not be performed, in relation to persons of a specified description.
