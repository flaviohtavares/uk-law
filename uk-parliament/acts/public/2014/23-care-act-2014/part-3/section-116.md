## 116. Membership of the United Kingdom Ethics Committee Authority[^c-05c9d03f18a5cdf23f8f117d31eb4b87]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In regulation 5 of the Medicines for Human Use (Clinical Trials) Regulations 2004 (S.I. 2004/1031) (United Kingdom Ethics Committee Authority)—

- (a) in paragraphs (1), (2) and (3), for “the Secretary of State for Health”, in each place it appears, substitute “ the Health Research Authority ”, and
- (b) in paragraph (2), for “the Secretary of State” substitute “ the Health Research Authority ”.
