## 99. Quality improvement in education and training, etc.[^c-115d10ff2e1e6cbc005abe9b8ddcb26b]

*Extent: E+W*

(1) NHS England[^c-fc62df37882c6ddf96f1af0f968a1034] must exercise its functions under this Chapter[^c-1e017954c19b44ae874c6e4092c423f2] with a view to securing continuous improvement—

- (a) in the quality of education and training provided for health care workers;
- (b)[^c-f6565fd9e172a3918120f9d0b71ae377] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2)[^c-de345388356b6df75fc51188d21ed997] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3)[^c-7d688f58b2bb70e7b22fd475d6a03dd1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) NHS England[^c-4965d719ce5a789ec061f0f76633c66d] must exercise its functions under this Chapter[^c-2d6c3a8cd06dfb33c70901edd009946c] with a view to securing that education and training for health care workers is provided in a way which promotes the NHS Constitution.

(5)[^c-0c15f48d8a2f95cde8b4193e7cc50ff7] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) “**NHS Constitution**” has the meaning given by section 1(1) of the Health Act 2009.
