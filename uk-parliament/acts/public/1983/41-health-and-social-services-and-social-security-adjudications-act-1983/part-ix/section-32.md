# Health And Social Services And Social Security Adjudications Act 1983, section 32

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2016-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Commencement.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) This section and sections 33 and 34 below shall come into force on the day this Act is passed.

(2)[^c12739431] Subject to subsection (1) above, the provisions of this Act shall come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order made by statutory instrument appoint and different days may be appointed in pursuance of this subsection for different provisions or different purposes of the same provision.
