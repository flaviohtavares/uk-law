# Health And Social Services And Social Security Adjudications Act 1983, section 29

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2016-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/41
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29. Minor and consequential amendments.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The enactments specified in Part I of Schedule 9 to this Act shall have effect subject to the amendments there specified (being minor amendments and amendments consequential on the foregoing provisions of this Act).

(2) Part II of that Schedule shall have effect in place of the provisions of the [^c12739351]Residential Homes Act 1980 relating to meals and recreation for old people.
