## 30. Repeals and revocation.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The enactments specified in Part I of Schedule 10 to this Act (which include enactments already obsolete or unnecessary) are repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Part of that Schedule.

(2) The instrument specified in Part II of that Schedule is revoked to the extent specified in the third column of that Part.
