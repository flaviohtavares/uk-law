# Health And Social Services And Social Security Adjudications Act 1983, section 24

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (14).** Text as at 2016-04-06. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/41

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 24. Interest on sums charged on or secured over interest in land.[^c12739261]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Any sum charged on or secured over an interest in land under this Part of this Act shall bear interest from the day after that on which the person for whom the local authority provided the accommodation dies.

(2)[^c12739271] The rate of interest shall be such reasonable rate as the Secretary of State may direct or, if no such direction is given, as the local authority may determine.
