# National Heritage Act 1983, section 32

> Text as at 2024-11-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 32. Establishment of Commission.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There shall be a body known as the Historic Buildings and Monuments Commission for England.

(2) Schedule 3 shall have effect with respect to the Commission.
