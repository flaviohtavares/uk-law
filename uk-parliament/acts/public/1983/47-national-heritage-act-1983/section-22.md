# National Heritage Act 1983, section 22

> Text as at 2024-11-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 22. Finance.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may out of money provided by Parliament pay to the Board such sums towards their expenditure as the Treasury may approve.

(2) The payment may be made on such conditions as the Secretary of State imposes with the Treasury’s approval.
