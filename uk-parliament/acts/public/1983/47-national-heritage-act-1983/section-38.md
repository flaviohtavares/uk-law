# National Heritage Act 1983, section 38

> Text as at 2024-11-04.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

## 38. Finance.

*Extent: E+W*

(1) The Secretary of State may out of money provided by Parliament—

- (a) pay to the Commission such sums towards their expenditure as the Treasury may approve;
- (b) defray such expenditure of the Commission as the Treasury may approve.

(2) The payment may be made on such conditions as the Secretary of State imposes with the Treasury’s approval.
