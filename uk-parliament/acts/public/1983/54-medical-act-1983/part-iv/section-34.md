# Medical Act 1983, section 34

## 34. Publication of the registers[^key-c2b6e83cb87d3e7a1cc592c21c2cd20b][^key-04733e571039ef5b79b5e8b37a74d6c5][^key-9f837fdbed69fc73380220a9cf1023d7]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Registrar shall cause to be published from time to time (electronically or otherwise) a list of all persons who, on a date specified by him at the time of publication, appear in[^key-21820ec9f5f2d174f2e9034d30f3e179] each of the registers[^key-f3e72860229d67035f9f5fdf16227cc1].

(2) The list published in accordance with subsection (1) above shall include in respect of each practitioner—

- (a) information about his registered qualifications;
- (b) a statement about whether or not he holds a licence to practise; [^key-764549c3f48dbf918da3b83f5c7fa896]...
- (ba)[^key-76edb8ed91e97a1572a24ea5301fe58e] in relation to the list of persons on the Specialist Register—
  - (i) the specialty in respect of which a person's name is included in that register; and
  - (ii) the name, or a description of, any field within that specialty which is indicated in respect of that person in that register; and
- (c) such other particulars (if any) as the General Council may direct in relation to that list.
