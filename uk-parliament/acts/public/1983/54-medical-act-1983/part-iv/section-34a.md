# Medical Act 1983, section 34A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (189).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 34A. Proof of registration[^key-4c4dd77e0763c1ce00906785b3f3d0c9]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The Registrar may issue a certificate that a person—

- (a) is registered in any of the registers[^key-ded1a08c5e74df556c5675831ee529bc];
- (b) is not registered in any of the registers;
- (c) was registered in any of the registers at a specified date or during a specified period;
- (d) was not registered in any of the registers at a specified date or during a specified period;
- (e) has never been registered in any of the registers;
- (f) holds a licence to practise;
- (g) does not hold a licence to practise;
- (h) held a licence to practise at a specified date or during a specified period;
- (i) did not hold a licence to practise at a specified date or during a specified period; or
- (j) has never held a licence to practise.

(1A)[^key-3fd69701c375503ffe0f7c451f5103ed] In relation to a person who is registered in the Specialist Register, the Registrar may issue a certificate that that person is, or is not, registered on that register with a particular specialty and in a particular field within that specialty.

(2) A certificate issued under subsection (1) or (1A)[^key-23f6acc14bf979277a088a4f7f7d20f4] above shall be evidence (and in Scotland sufficient evidence) of the matters certified.
