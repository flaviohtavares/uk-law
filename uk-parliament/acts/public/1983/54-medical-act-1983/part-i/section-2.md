# Medical Act 1983, section 2

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (189).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 2. Registration of medical practitioners.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) There shall continue to be kept by the registrar of the General Council (in this Act referred to as “**the Registrar**”) a register[^c-4e73e7f36c85e5d852c3ba4be72dd4ca] of medical practitioners registered under this Act containing the names of those registered and the qualifications they are entitled to have registered under this Act.

(2) The register referred to is[^c-9ce425f10f19d44b966699810fe4472d] “the register of medical practitioners” consisting of the following lists[^c-1ddc553e1db767de827e759c2e019617]—

- (a) the principal list,
- (aa)[^c-7fba642b2df9d6f44fb39f6347eec4ad] if anyone is registered under section 18A, the emergency powers doctors list, and[^c-26119f7c46ecd0f61dceb2e7f9f2377f]
- (b)[^c-8e778d807c6d63c3d3d88a936d33ff9e] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c) the visiting overseas doctors list, [^c-37617b9d198f17e9cbc56159c344f1d9]...
- (d)[^c-37617b9d198f17e9cbc56159c344f1d9] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

[^c-391dfa5d28e869a323f758f908194260]. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(3) Medical practitioners shall be registered as fully registered medical practitioners or provisionally [^c-10e5f4fc6cc0edbf5e21d8f7ad6f44d9]... as provided in Parts II and III of this Act and in the appropriate list of the register of medical practitioners [^c-5f9e7c96fd5cb30db979e62fbf69c05e]... as provided in Part IV of this Act.

(4)[^c-963979982d78453276a1b9e1e4bb1af5] Section 35C(2)(da) (the necessary knowledge of English) shall not apply in determining whether a person’s fitness to practise is impaired for the purposes of registration under this Act.
