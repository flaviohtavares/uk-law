# Medical Act 1983, section 29F

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (189).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 29F. Appeals

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If a licensing authority decides under this Part—

- (a) to refuse to grant a licence to practise to a medical practitioner;
- (b) to withdraw a licence to practise from a medical practitioner; or
- (c) to refuse to restore a licence to practise to a medical practitioner,

the practitioner may appeal to a Registration Appeals Panel.

(1A)[^key-164a4d4395d2b7ff6c740a7e6b3bd2bb] If a licensing authority decides under—

- (a) section 44C(8)[^key-e5e827048ffb392fffcc86c2824bcdba] to refuse to grant a licence to practise to a medical practitioner; or
- (b) section 44C(9)(a)[^key-40237e31431d072f7c9a33b43d2f69f0] to withdraw a licence to practise from a medical practitioner,

the practitioner may appeal to a Registration Appeals Panel.

(2) Schedule 3B (which provides for the procedures to be followed before a Registration Appeals Panel) shall apply in relation to any appeal under subsection (1) or (1A)[^key-5a2d4b9e37b1eb029b01ed7947ee6310] above.

(3) A decision under this Part to withdraw a licence to practise from a medical practitioner shall not be carried into effect—

- (a) until the time for bringing any appeal against the decision has expired without an appeal being brought; or
- (b) where an appeal is brought, until the date on which the appeal is finally disposed of or abandoned or fails by reason of its non-prosecution.
