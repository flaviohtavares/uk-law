# Medical Act 1983, section 19A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (189).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 19A. Full registration of EEA nationals etc by virtue of overseas qualifications accepted by a relevant European State ...[^key-e32e270405277eaf2d4f6e137d332737][^key-6e165ae349ae3c24e070a1ecc6085a52]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

An exempt person—

- (za)[^key-8c80be7dcc4d6ce208bcc29690dcdb9f] who has made an application for registration under this section before IP completion day,
- (a) whose case falls within regulation 3(8)(e)[^key-b3e2eb3f0634f4fefc9ce02df3d89645] of the General Systems Regulations,
- (b) to whom regulations 27 to 34[^key-4483f25f5a47d5785a94aa3ef08e835a] of those Regulations apply by reason of the operation of regulation 3(5)[^key-eeabdca2361521d4a57b58dac40cdde2] of those Regulations,
- (c) who is permitted to pursue the profession of medical practitioner in the United Kingdom by virtue of Part 3 of those Regulations (having, in particular, successfully completed any adaptation period, or passed any aptitude test, that he may be required to undertake pursuant to that Part of those Regulations), and
- (d) whose fitness to practise is not impaired,

is entitled to be registered under this section as a fully registered medical practitioner.
