# Medical Act 1983, section 3

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (189).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/54

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 3. Registration by virtue of primary United Kingdom or primary European qualifications.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act any person whose fitness to practise is not impaired and[^c-0c7624d995f1f662b09bc75bd6393d9d] who—

- (a)[^c-4fc934df5f460422ab924b3378c22eae] holds one or more primary United Kingdom qualifications and has satisfactorily completed an acceptable programme for provisionally registered doctors; or
- (b) being a national of any relevant European State[^c-09713b61a162f645e630288426929a2e], holds one or more primary European qualifications and has made an application, before IP completion day, for registration under this paragraph or is provisionally registered under section 15A[^c-909b3fb0965d38b394f68c1b8d7e1996],

is entitled to be registered under this section as a fully registered medical practitioner.

(2) Any person who—

- (a) is not a national of a relevant European State[^c-08b2929fca7e433aa26721f8763ebdbc]; but
- (b)[^c-0338643678c47e8ba215d00541154688] is, by virtue of an enforceable EU[^c-df444f9beb71fb342af82be2f7f6d884] right, entitled to be treated, for the purposes of access to and pursuit of the medical profession, no less favourably than a national of a relevant European State,

shall be treated for the purposes of subsection (1)(b) above as if he were such a national.
