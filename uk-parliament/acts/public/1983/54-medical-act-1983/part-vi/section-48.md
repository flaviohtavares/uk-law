# Medical Act 1983, section 48

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (189).** Text as at 2025-01-01. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/54
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 48. Certificates invalid if not signed by fully registered medical practitioners who hold licences to practise.[^c-9f57da49b7fb5e88f39f884b620e0813]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A certificate required by any enactment, whether passed before or after the commencement of this Act, from any physician, surgeon, licentiate in medicine and surgery or other medical practitioner shall not be valid unless the person signing it is fully registered and holds a licence to practise[^c-82d25477a03a47a02591733004b8ec31].
