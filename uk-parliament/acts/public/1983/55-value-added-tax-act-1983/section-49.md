# Value Added Tax Act 1983, section 49

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 49. Refund of tax to Government of Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-09-01).**

The Commissioners shall refund to the Government of Northern Ireland the amount of the tax charged on the supply of goods or services to, that Government, on the acquisition of any goods by that Government from another member State or on the importation of any goods by that Government from a place outside the member States[^c14324511], after deducting therefrom so much of that amount as may be agreed between them and the Department of Finance and Personnel for Northern Ireland as attributable to supplies acquisitions[^c14324521] and importations for the purpose of a business carried on by the Government of Northern Ireland.
