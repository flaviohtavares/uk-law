# Value Added Tax Act 1983, section 17

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 17. Exemptions.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-09-01).**

(1) A supply of goods or services is an exempt supply if it is of a description for the time being specified in Schedule 6 to this Act and an acquisition of goods from another member State is an exempt acquisition if the goods are acquired in pursuance of an exempt supply.[^c14319911]

(2) The Treasury may by order vary that Schedule by adding to or deleting from it any description of supply or by varying any description of supply for the time being specified in it and the Schedule may be varied so as to describe a supply of goods by reference to the use which has been made of them or to other matters unrelated to the characteristics of the goods themselves.
