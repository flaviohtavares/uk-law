# Value Added Tax Act 1983, section 46

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 46. Service of notices.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-09-01).**

Any notice, notification, requirement or demand to be served on, given to or made of any person for the purposes of this Act may be served, given or made by sending it by post in a letter addressed to that person or his tax representative at the last or usual residence or place of business of that person or representative.[^c14323861]
