# Value Added Tax Act 1983, section 38

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 38. Administration, collection and enforcement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-09-01).**

Schedule 7 to this Act shall have effect subject to section 46A(6) below,[^c14321831] with respect to the administration, collection and enforcement of the tax.
