# Value Added Tax Act 1983, section 50

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 50. Consequential, transitional and saving provisions and repeals.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-09-01).**

(1) Schedule 9 (consequential amendments) and Schedule 10 (savings and transitional provisions) to this Act shall have effect but without prejudice to the operation of sections 15 to 17 of the [^c14324531]Interpretation Act 1978 (which relate to the effect of repeals).

(2) The enactments specified in Schedule 11 to this Act are hereby repealed to the extent mentioned in the third column of that Schedule.
