# Value Added Tax Act 1983, section 1

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 1. Value added tax.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 1994-09-01).**

A tax, to be known as value added tax, shall be charged in accordance with the provisions of this Act on the supply of goods and services in the United Kingdom (including anything treated as such a supply) on the acquisition in the United Kingdom from other member States of any goods and on the importation of goods from places outside the member States.[^c14317671]
