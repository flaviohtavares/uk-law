## 98. Premises not affected for rates.[^c-3d5e191be25e7f7d9d6df73ed45a44ea][^c-36adb485424538f05ca319a283993c33][^c14496761][^c14496771][^c14496781][^c14496791][^c-131aa7bf7f02a251d6ad54328720d910][^c-db39ce682292f2a905fe5e21511f1247][^c14496751][^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

The use of any premises for the holding of public meetings in furtherance of any person’s candidature at a parliamentary or local government election does not render any person liable to be rated or to pay any rate for the premises.
