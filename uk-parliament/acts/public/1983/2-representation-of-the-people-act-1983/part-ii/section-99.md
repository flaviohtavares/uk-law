# Representation of the People Act 1983, section 99

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 99. Officials not to act for candidates.[^c14496801][^c14496811][^c14496831][^c14496821][^M_C_ee41b815-48ab-49ed-fc16-701e277edc78][^c-9222b310a23dc318c5ab440d2d4b4325][^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If

- (a) any returning officer at a parliamentary or local government election, or
- (b) any officer or clerk appointed under the parliamentary elections rules, or the rules under section 36 , section 36A[^c-e9b672e600a6e72670b3338a41dc37fe] or section 42 above, as the case may be, or
- (c) any partner or clerk of any such person,

acts as a candidate’s agent in the conduct or management of the election, he shall be guilty of an offence, but nothing in this subsection prevents a candidate from acting as his own election agent.

(2)[^c14496841] A person guilty of an offence under this section shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 4 on the standard scale.
