# Representation of the People Act 1983, section 107

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 107. Corrupt withdrawal from candidature.[^c14497321][^c14497331][^c14497351][^c14497341][^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Any person who corruptly induces or procures any other person to withdraw from being a candidate at an election, in consideration of any payment or promise of payment, and any person withdrawing in pursuance of the inducement or procurement, shall be guilty of an illegal payment.
