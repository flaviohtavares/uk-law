## 76. Limitation of election expenses.[^c14492981][^c14492961][^c14492941][^c14492951][^c14493001][^c14492991][^c14492972][^c-0522e2fa742d2bc9bad6fc925dfa4d1c][^M_C_93b24a33-fcc1-465b-a54c-49416bc0aac8][^c-a74e98e474fb049a86157667748ff177][^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c14493011] The election expenses incurred by or on behalf of a candidate at an election must not in the aggregate exceed the maximum amount specified in subsection (2) below or, in the case of any of the Authority elections mentioned in subsection (2A)(a) to (c) below, the maximum amount prescribed by order under that subsection.

(1A)[^c14493021] Where a registered political party submits a list of candidates to be London members of the London Assembly at an ordinary election, any election expenses incurred by or on behalf of any of those candidates must not in the aggregate exceed the maximum amount prescribed by order under subsection (2A)(d).[^c14493031]

(1B)[^c14493041] Where any election expenses are incurred in excess of a maximum amount specified in subsection (2) below[^c19541261] or prescribed by order under subsection (2A) below, any candidate or election agent who—

- (a) incurred, or authorised the incurring of, the election expenses, and
- (b) knew or ought reasonably to have known that the expenses would be incurred in excess of that maximum amount,

shall be guilty of an illegal practice.

(2) That maximum amount is—

- (a) for a candidate at a parliamentary general election, being an election[^c14493051]—
  - (i) in a county constituency, £11,390[^c-d21e6b56ba096f67c3ec9f9198264790] together with an additional 12p[^c-81364d487e12fed6aa407eb5a488d96f] for every entry in the register of electors [^c15284811]. . . and
  - (ii) in a borough constituency, £11,390[^c-c72e77804ee88f60c541385bac265bf2] together with an additional 8p[^c-cb5e4a9dea439ca67cdcfa6856c40d4f] for every entry in the register of electors [^c14493111]. . .
- (aa)[^c14493121] for a candidate at a parliamentary by-election, £180,050[^c-350b87d024ab8379144a9d2110466dc2];
- (b) for a candidate at a local government election other than an Authority election[^c14493131]—
  - (i)[^c14493141] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ia)[^c14493161] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ib)[^c-fec9a389b9adf87bdb2e4337f7bcde40] at a local government election in England, £960[^M_F_6b27f00f-b975-4439-eec0-e359a9849432] together with an additional 8p[^M_F_5ee091e0-050f-4db6-d7bb-a5b44f0bf6d6] for every entry in the register of electors;
  - (ic)[^c-480b465f09d6b12f37f5728c9198d6ff] at a local government election in Wales, £806 together with an additional 7p for every entry in the register of electors;
  - (ii) at any other local government election, £740[^c-66e1608827e04511819f0407af678685] together with an additional 6p[^c-a1e9e9bad307f8e3279004ccfeb9fe86] for every entry in the register of electors [^c14493111]. . ..

(2A)[^c14493201][^c14493211] As respects Authority elections, each of the following, that is to say—

- (a) the maximum amount for a candidate in an election of the Mayor of London,
- (b) the maximum amount for a candidate in an election of a constituency member of the London Assembly,
- (c) the maximum amount for an individual candidate in an election of the London members of the London Assembly at an ordinary election,
- (d) the maximum amount for the purposes of subsection (1A) above,

shall be such as the Secretary of State may prescribe in an order made by statutory instrument.

(2B) An order under subsection (2A) above shall not be made unless a draft of the order has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

(3)[^c14493221] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4)[^c14493231] In subsection (2) above “**the register of electors**” means the register of parliamentary electors, or (as the case may be) local government electors, for the constituency or electoral area in question as it has effect on the last day for publication of notice of the election.

(5) The maximum amount mentioned above for a candidate at a parliamentary election or an Authority election (including the maximum amount for the purposes of subsection (1A) above)[^c14493241] or a local government election in England or Wales[^c-6638f52d707411bb5f9e95de4ce622fa] is not required to cover the candidate’s personal expenses.

(6) Where at an election a poll is countermanded or abandoned by reason of a candidate’s death, the maximum amount of election expenses shall, for any of the other candidates who then remain validly nominated, be twice or, if there has been a previous increase under this subsection, three times what it would have been but for any increase under this subsection; but the maximum amount shall not be affected for any candidate by the change in the timing of the election or of any step in the proceedings at the election.

## 76. Limitation of election expenses.[^c19540251][^c14492961][^c14492941][^c14492951][^c14493001][^c14492991][^c14492972][^c-0522e2fa742d2bc9bad6fc925dfa4d1c][^c-a74e98e474fb049a86157667748ff177]

*Extent: S*

(1) The election expenses incurred by or on behalf of a candidate at an election must not in the aggregate exceed the maximum amount specified in subsection (2) below or, in the case of any of the Authority elections mentioned in subsection (2A)(a) to (c) below, the maximum amount prescribed by order under that subsection.

(1A) Where a registered political party submits a list of candidates to be London members of the London Assembly at an ordinary election, any election expenses incurred by or on behalf of any of those candidates must not in the aggregate exceed the maximum amount prescribed by order under subsection (2A)(d).

(1B) Where any election expenses are incurred in excess of a maximum amount specified in subsection (2) below or prescribed by order under subsection (2A) below , any candidate or election agent who—

- (a) incurred, or authorised the incurring of, the election expenses, and
- (b) knew or ought reasonably to have known that the expenses would be incurred in excess of that maximum amount,

shall be guilty of an illegal practice.

(2) That maximum amount is—

- (a) for a candidate at a parliamentary general election, being an election—
  - (i) in a county constituency, £11,390 together with an additional 12p for every entry in the register of electors [^c15284811]. . . and
  - (ii) in a borough constituency, £11,390 together with an additional 8p for every entry in the register of electors [^c14493111]. . .
- (aa) for a candidate at a parliamentary by-election, £180,050;
- (b) for a candidate at a local government election other than an Authority election—
  - (i)[^c14493141] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ia)[^c14493161] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
  - (ii) at any other local government election, £940[^c-025278d61e381f5c56ba6c72b3314749] together with an additional 8p[^c-d4c7de25cf703f3a6bbef7a7f14ad1a2] for every entry in the register of electors [^c14493111]... .

(2A)[^c14493201] As respects Authority elections, each of the following, that is to say—

- (a) the maximum amount for a candidate in an election of the Mayor of London,
- (b) the maximum amount for a candidate in an election of a constituency member of the London Assembly,
- (c) the maximum amount for an individual candidate in an election of the London members of the London Assembly at an ordinary election,
- (d) the maximum amount for the purposes of subsection (1A) above,

shall be such as the Secretary of State may prescribe in an order made by statutory instrument.

(2B) An order under subsection (2A) above shall not be made unless a draft of the order has been laid before, and approved by a resolution of, each House of Parliament.

(3)[^c14493221] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) In subsection (2) above “**the register of electors**” means the register of parliamentary electors, or (as the case may be) local government electors, for the constituency or electoral area in question as it has effect on the last day for publication of notice of the election.

(5) The maximum amount mentioned above for a candidate at a parliamentary election or an Authority election (including the maximum amount for the purposes of subsection (1A) above) or a local government election in England or Wales is not required to cover the candidate’s personal expenses.

(5A)[^c-d2b57c9ac243085480757e49b8e3642a] The maximum amount mentioned above for a candidate at a local government election in Scotland is not required to cover—

- (a) the candidate’s personal expenses;
- (b) expenses that are reasonably attributable to the candidate having a physical or mental impairment that has a substantial and long-term adverse effect on the candidate’s ability to carry out normal day-to-day activities.
- (c)[^c-8cb3a789b6ff3cf6c02b8c06075d7230] reasonable expenses incurred that are reasonably attributable to an individual’s disability,
- (d) reasonable expenses incurred that are reasonably attributable to the protection of persons or property[^c-16903d39ee69e46abe7be7e64b7630a3],
- (e) reasonable expenses incurred that are reasonably attributable to the translation of anything into another language.

(6) Where at an election a poll , other than a poll for a local government election in Scotland,[^c-d11735c44ee8d68550a2c6af858da897] is countermanded or abandoned by reason of a candidate’s death, the maximum amount of election expenses shall, for any of the other candidates who then remain validly nominated, be twice or, if there has been a previous increase under this subsection, three times what it would have been but for any increase under this subsection; but the maximum amount shall not be affected for any candidate by the change in the timing of the election or of any step in the proceedings at the election.

(6A)[^c-f35788ed35baf1e401d25b4aa76f6bd5] Where a poll for a local government election in Scotland is countermanded or abandoned by reason of a candidate’s death, the maximum amount will, for any of the other candidates who then remain validly nominated, be increased by an amount equal to the amount specified by subsection (2)(b)(ii) for each countermanding or abandonment which occurs.

(7) Where the date of the poll at an ordinary local election has been postponed under section 43A(1) or 43B(1) of this Act, the maximum amount will have effect in relation to any candidate at that election as if the amount specified in subsection (2)(b)(ii) were increased by one half.

(8) Where the date of the poll at an election to fill a casual vacancy in the office of councillor has been fixed under section 37A(2) or 37B(2) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973, the maximum amount will have effect in relation to any candidate at that election as if the amount specified in subsection (2)(b)(ii) were increased by one half.

(9) Where the date of the poll at an election to fill a casual vacancy in the office of councillor has been fixed under section 37A(4) of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973, the maximum amount will have effect in relation to any candidate at that election as if the maximum amount specified in subsection (2)(b)(ii) were increased by one half.

(10) Where more than one of the circumstances described by subsections (6A), (7), (8) or (9) are applicable to a poll, the increases in the maximum amount provided for by the applicable subsections are to apply cumulatively.

(11) Other than in the circumstances described by subsections (6A) to (9), the maximum amount specified in subsection (2)(b)(ii) for a candidate shall not be affected by a change in the timing of a local government election in Scotland or of any step in the proceedings at such an election.

(12) For the purposes of this section—

- “**casual vacancy in the office of councillor**” means a casual vacancy in the office of councillor to which section 36 of the Local Government (Scotland) Act 1973 applies,
- “**ordinary local election**” has the meaning given in section 43(1C) of this Act.
