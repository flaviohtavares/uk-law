## 111. Prohibition of paid canvassers.[^c14497651][^c14497661][^c14497671][^c14497691][^c14497701][^c19430281][^c20059281][^c14497681][^key-5a9c59230099a119345e1686590e5a4d][^key-4276bded3d4f3a8d28bb996f10e7ff8c][^key-6d2e597ca732ba3b1fdef9b05e18e272][^key-6f1ef86ecc52b68fe3bed83ce58f7bee][^key-288d166f21c7627692124b939cdefdd4][^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

If a person is, either before, during or after an election, engaged or employed for payment or promise of payment as a canvasser for the purpose of promoting or procuring a candidate’s election—

- (a) the person so engaging or employing him, and
- (b) the person so engaged or employed,

shall be guilty of illegal employment.
