## 110. Details to appear on election publications.[^c-d2ae0bcd2eef6ddeb3e478f20613fd8d][^c-d78b722454e58526096dd0bb1ea7dd2c][^c-1b83e8de7930cc39bfbdb94b36ec0f12][^c-81fc9b7a01ca36117a85013b42a91425][^c14497621][^c14497641][^c19430261][^c14497632][^c20059231][^c14497611][^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c19430241] This section applies to any material which can reasonably be regarded as intended to promote or procure the election of a candidate at an election (whether or not it can be so regarded as intended to achieve any other purpose as well). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

(2) No material to which this section applies shall be published unless— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

- (a) in the case of material which is, or is contained in, such a document as is mentioned in subsection (4), (5) or (6) below, the requirements of that subsection are complied with; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]
- (b) in the case of any other material, any requirements falling to be complied with in relation to the material by virtue of regulations under subsection (7) below are complied with. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

(3) For the purposes of subsections (4) to (6) below the following details are “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]**the relevant details *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]**” in the case of any material falling within subsection (2)(a) above, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

- (a) the name and address of the printer of the document; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]
- (b) the name and address of the promoter of the material; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]
- (c) the name and address of any person on behalf of whom the material is being published (and who is not the promoter). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

(4) Where the material is a document consisting (or consisting principally) of a single side of printed matter, the relevant details must appear on the face of the document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

(5) Where the material is a printed document other than one to which subsection (4) above applies, the relevant details must appear either on the first or the last page of the document. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

(6) Where the material is an advertisement contained in a newspaper or periodical— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

- (a) the name and address of the printer of the newspaper or periodical must appear either on its first or last page; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]
- (b) the relevant details specified in subsection (3)(b) and (c) above must be included in the advertisement. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

(7) The Secretary of State may, after consulting the Electoral Commission, by regulations make provision for and in connection with the imposition of requirements as to the inclusion in material falling within subsection (2)(b) above of the following details, namely— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

- (a) the name and address of the promoter of the material; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]
- (b) the name and address of any person on behalf of whom the material is being published (and who is not the promoter). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

(8) Regulations under subsection (7) above may in particular specify— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

- (a) the manner and form in which such details must be included in any such material for the purpose of complying with any such requirement; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]
- (b) circumstances in which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]
  - (i) any such requirement does not have to be complied with by a person of any description specified in the regulations, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c19430241] a breach of any such requirement by a person of any description so specified is not to result in the commission of an offence under this section by that person or by a person of any other such description; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]
- (c) circumstances in which material is, or is not, to be taken for the purposes of the regulations to be published or (as the case may be) published by a person of any description so specified. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

(9) Where any material falling within subsection (2)(a) above is published in contravention of subsection (2), then (subject to subsections (11) and (12) below)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

- (a) the promoter of the material, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]
- (b) any other person by whom the material is so published, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]
- (c) the printer of the document, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

(10) Where any material falling within subsection (2)(b) above is published in contravention of subsection (2), then (subject to regulations made by virtue of subsection (8)(b) above and to subsections (11) and (12) below)— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

- (a) the promoter of the material, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]
- (b) any other person by whom the material is so published, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding level 5 on the standard scale. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

(11) It shall be a defence for a person charged with an offence under this section to prove— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

- (a) that the contravention of subsection (2) above arose from circumstances beyond his control; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]
- (b) that he took all reasonable steps, and exercised all due diligence, to ensure that that contravention would not arise. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

(12) Where a candidate or his election agent would (apart from this subsection) be guilty of an offence under subsection (9) or (10) above, he shall instead be guilty of an illegal practice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

(13) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

- “**print *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]**” means print by whatever means, and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]**printer *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]**” shall be construed accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]
- “**the promoter *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]**”, in relation to any material to which this section applies, means the person causing the material to be published; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]
- “**publish *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]**” means make available to the public at large, or any section of the public, in whatever form and by whatever means. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]

(14) For the purpose of determining whether any material is material such as is mentioned in subsection (1) above, it is immaterial that it does not expressly mention the name of any candidate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19430241]
