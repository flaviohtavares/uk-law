# Representation of the People Act 1983, section 74A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (63).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/2
>

## 74A. Expenses incurred otherwise than for election purposes.[^c14489562][^c-a74e98e474fb049a86157667748ff177][^c14489551][^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Neither section 73 above nor sections 78 and 79 below shall apply to election expenses—

- (a) which are incurred by or on behalf of a candidate otherwise than for the purposes of the candidate’s election, but
- (b)[^c19428311] which by virtue of section 90ZA(1)[^c19428151] below fall to be regarded as election expenses by reason of the property, services or facilities in respect of which they were incurred being used for the purposes of the candidate’s election.

(2) The candidate’s election agent shall make a declaration of the amount (determined in accordance with section 90B below) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19428261] of any election expenses falling within subsection (1) above.

(3)[^c19428351] In this section “**for the purposes of the candidate’s election**” has the same meaning as in sections 90ZA and[^c19428171] 90C below.
