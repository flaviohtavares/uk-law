# Representation of the People Act 1983, section 79

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/2

## 79. Disputed claims.[^c14493561][^c14493571][^c14493591][^c14493581][^c-a74e98e474fb049a86157667748ff177][^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) If the election agent disputes any claim sent in to him within the period of 21 days[^c14493601] mentioned in section 78 above or refuses or fails to pay the claim within the period of 28 days so mentioned, the claim shall be deemed to be a disputed claim.

(2) The claimant may, if he thinks fit, bring an action for a disputed claim in any competent court, and any sum paid by the candidate or his agent in pursuance of the judgment or order of the court shall nor be deemed to be in contravention of section 73(1) above or of section 78(2).

(3)[^c14493611] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) Subsections (4) to (7) of section 78 apply in relation to a disputed claim as they apply in relation to a claim for election expenses sent in after that period of 21 days.
