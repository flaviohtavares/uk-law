# Representation of the People Act 1983, section 181

## 181. Director of Public Prosecutions.[^c14502651][^c14502671][^c-e404becdf702092867e63861c5decdae][^c-d07661a0af3e40927c583cd0fa1e93c8][^c-8523dd7c3b809a9b3e741f68e3226b92][^c-f3f0fc188b837f9069253a12bb3de891][^c-b23f2f4f77dc8103d2facada5f99bcf5][^c-3943894d0f2be52a3b57e080622e78b9][^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c14502691][^c19500691][^c20067821][^c-24bbf65556674d7f1e79ecf7c7fa9bbd][^c-72e98d4e99a1137a7ed78b673857e921][^c-ec3add72b1e5bcdecc53ae470056691f] Where information is given to the Director of Public Prosecutions that any offence under this Act has been committed[^c14502701], it is his duty to make such inquiries and institute such prosecutions as the circumstances of the case appear to him to require.

(2)[^c-ec3add72b1e5bcdecc53ae470056691f] The Director by himself or by his assistant or by his representative appointed under subsection (3) below may and, if the election court so requests him, shall[^c14502711] attend the trial of every election petition.

(3)[^c-ec3add72b1e5bcdecc53ae470056691f] The Director may nominate, [^c14502721]. . . , a barrister, solicitor or authorised person[^c19481241][^c14502721]. . . to be his representative for the purposes of this Part of this Act [^c14502721]. . . .

(3A)[^c-ec3add72b1e5bcdecc53ae470056691f][^c19481261] In subsection (3) “**authorised person**” means a person (other than a barrister or solicitor) who, for the purposes of the Legal Services Act 2007, is an authorised person in relation to an activity which constitutes the exercise of a right of audience (within the meaning of that Act).

(4)[^c14502751] The Director in performing any duty under this Act shall act in accordance with regulations under the [^c14502761]Prosecution of Offences Act 1979, and subject to them in accordance with the directions (if any) given to him by the Attorney General; and any assistant or representative of the Director in performing any duty under this Part shall act in accordance with those regulations and directions (if any) and with the directions given to him by the Director.

(5)[^c-ec3add72b1e5bcdecc53ae470056691f] There shall be allowed to the Director and his assistant or representative for the purposes of this Part (other than his general duties under subsection (1) above) such allowances for expenses as the Treasury may approve.

(6)[^c-ec3add72b1e5bcdecc53ae470056691f] The costs incurred in defraying the expenses of the Director incurred for those purposes (including the remuneration of his representative) shall, in the first instance, be paid by the Treasury, and [^c14502771]. . . shall be deemed to be expenses of the election court; but if for any reasonable cause it seems just to the court so to do, the court shall order all or part of those costs to be repaid to the Treasury by the parties to the petition, or such of them as the court may direct.

(7) In the application of this section to Scotland, subsections (2) to (6) shall be omitted.

(8)[^c14502781] In the application of this section to Northern Ireland, the reference to the [^c14502791]Prosecution of Offences Act 1979 does not apply.
