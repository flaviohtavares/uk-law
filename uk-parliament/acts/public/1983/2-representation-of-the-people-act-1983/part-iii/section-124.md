## 124. Judges’ expenses and reception: England and Wales and Northern Ireland.[^c14498571][^c14498581][^c14498592][^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In relation to the trial of a parliamentary election petition—

- (a) in England and Wales and Northern Ireland, the travelling and other expenses of the judges and all expenses properly incurred in [^c14498601]. . . providing them with necessary accommodation and with a proper court shall be defrayed by the Treasury out of moneys provided by Parliament;
