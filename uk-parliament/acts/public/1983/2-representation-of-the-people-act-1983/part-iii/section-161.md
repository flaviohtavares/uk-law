# Representation of the People Act 1983, section 161

>

## 161. Justice of the Peace.[^c14501081][^c14501102][^c19538101][^c20068321][^c20210251][^c-69fec57b5a4fc93af8d73e45ba2ac193][^c-2a580dea19f98114dacb8a5de05bc749][^c-69d0b903b7e45eca2f8c14dff9e228a2][^c-725ce1ac2f3749d71a24a7d0b84cf000][^c-1f23b75b6952f03c4ef5da76922aeea0][^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a justice of the peace is reported by an election court to have been guilty of any corrupt practice in reference to an election, [^c14501111]. . . the court shall[^c14501121] report the case to the Lord Chancellor and the Lord Chief Justice[^c19538051] or, in the case of a justice of the peace for any area in Scotland,[^c14501131] to[^c19538071] the Secretary of State with such evidence as may have been given of the corrupt practice.
