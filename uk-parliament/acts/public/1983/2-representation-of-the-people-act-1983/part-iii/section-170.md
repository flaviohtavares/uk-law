## 170. Conviction of illegal practice on charge of corrupt practice etc.[^c14501841][^c14501861][^c20066951][^c20210411][^c-692e9499b08ea74edae081fb2138fbb1][^c-1d1334621b70be5ac107f9c7dde5e5a1][^c-495e4944765a5da1205f077cae3cfb50][^c-98fd681873c0cbcdbd07dd45aac1cc76][^c-41ea91bf91d40794561814744c3ef899][^c-7fdcdc5c1855143b67819520277e31a7][^c-3b5b590a722b2db4f39092389a2c4a9d][^c-1bfdc2f7987f2fc34a9cd772b4047292][^c-e475aa6589738696d0d397d301f16158][^c-d7d77bbb05f347a7b8351d8ffe825b45][^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A person charged with a corrupt practice may, if the circumstances warrant such finding, be found guilty of an illegal practice (which offence shall for that purpose be an indictable offence), and a person charged with an illegal practice may be found guilty of that offence notwithstanding that the act constituting the offence amounted to a corrupt practice.
