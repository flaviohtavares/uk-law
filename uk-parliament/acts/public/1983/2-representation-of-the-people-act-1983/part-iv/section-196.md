## 196. Costs or expenses.[^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

In relation to municipal elections in the City, any costs or expenses directed to be paid under section 132 [^c14503441]. . . above by the authority for which the election was held shall—

- (a) if incurred in respect of a ward election, be paid out of the general rate; and
- (b) in any other case, be paid by the chamberlain of the City out of the City’s cash.
