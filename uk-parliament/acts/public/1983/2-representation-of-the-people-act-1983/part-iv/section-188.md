## 188. Parts II and III as to local elections in Scotland.[^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Except in the phrase “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19427941]**election to any public office *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19427941]**” wherever it is used, references in Parts II and III and section 189 of this Act to elections or to elections under the local government Act do not include references to elections under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19427941] Local Government etc. (Scotland) Act 1994[^c14503331] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19427941] other than elections of councillors. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19427941]
