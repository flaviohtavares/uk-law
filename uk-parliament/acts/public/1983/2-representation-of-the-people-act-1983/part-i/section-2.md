## 2. Local government electors.[^c14478151]

*Extent: E+W+N.I.*

(1) A person is entitled to vote as an elector at a local government election in any electoral area if on the date of the poll he—

- (a) is registered in the register of local government electors for that area;
- (b) is not subject to any legal incapacity to vote (age apart);
- (c) is a Commonwealth citizen, a citizen of the Republic of Ireland *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-90790fdd04a498ef5318d0706f08e258] , a relevant citizen of the Union or (in Wales) a qualifying foreign citizen *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-d383b1d431ed591d725631442626a527] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-90790fdd04a498ef5318d0706f08e258] Ireland or— *(Extent: E+W)*
  - (i (Extent: E+W)) in relation to a local government election in England, a qualifying EU citizen or an EU citizen with retained rights, or *(Extent: E+W)*
  - (ii (Extent: E+W)) in relation to a local government election in Wales, a relevant citizen of the Union or a qualifying foreign citizen; and *(Extent: E+W)*
- (d) is of voting age (that is, 18 years or over except in Wales (see subsection (1A))[^c-2506bbe2223c01a5ea5cfd60a7c946bc]).

*Extent: W*

(1A)[^c-cb9eaf94d3ace420e273c044009eb939] In Wales, voting age is 16 years or over.

(2) A person is not entitled to vote as an elector—

- (a) more than once in the same electoral area at any local government election; or
- (b) in more than one electoral area at an ordinary election for a local government area which is not a single electoral area.

## 2. Local government electors.[^V_S_b5c1f4f7-6219-bc94-3e1c-9269ab02cbc3][^c14479931]

*Extent: S*

(1) A person is entitled to vote as an elector at a local government election in any electoral area if on the date of the poll he—

- (a) is registered in the register of local government electors for that area;
- (b) is not subject to any legal incapacity to vote (age apart);
- (c) is a Commonwealth citizen, a citizen of the Republic of Ireland or —[^V_S_8a9c3a91-b6c6-b616-65d1-397eb56acd80]
  - (i) except in Scotland, a relevant citizen of the Union;
  - (ii)[^V_S_1ea7daff-a720-e9d6-81a3-0c59ee77f40e] in Scotland, a qualifying foreign national, and
- (d) is of voting age (that is, 18 years or over , except in Scotland (see subsection (1A))[^V_S_615c3e76-9512-a263-deee-c46010926cd6]).

(1A)[^V_S_ecd4e0e4-8b20-c259-3c29-337d97bb4eb8] In Scotland, voting age is 16 years or over.

(2) A person is not entitled to vote as an elector—

- (a) more than once in the same electoral area at any local government election; [^V_S_31b31ab8-6728-356a-e9e4-5e157cf3c6be]...
- (b) in more than one electoral area at an ordinary election for a local government area which is not a single electoral area , or[^V_S_82429b41-165f-bdac-af1b-95dcf163d012]
- (c) in more than one electoral area at an election of councillors for any local government area in Scotland when the polls for more than one local government election are held on the same day.
