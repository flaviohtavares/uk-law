# Representation of the People Act 1983, section 26

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/2

## 26. Returning officer: Northern Ireland.[^c14481291][^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In Northern Ireland, the Chief Electoral Officer for Northern Ireland is the returning officer for each constituency.

(2)[^c14481311][^c14481301] Sections 14(5) and 14A(2) and (3) of the [^c14481321]Electoral Law Act (Northern Ireland) 1962 (appointment of temporary deputy and delegation to assistants) shall have effect in relation to the Chief Electoral Officer in his capacity as returning officer.
