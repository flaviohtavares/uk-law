## 25. Returning officers: Scotland.[^c14481201][^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) In Scotland, the returning officer for a parliamentary election is—

- (a) in the case of a constituency wholly situated in one local government[^c14481211] area, the person under section 41 below who is, or who may discharge the functions of, the returning officer at elections of councillors for the local authority for that area[^c14481221];
- (b)[^c14481231] in the case of a constituency situated in more than one local government area, such person mentioned above as the Secretary of State may by order direct.

(2) Every local authority[^c14481251] shall place at the disposal of the returning officer for a constituency wholly or partly situated in their[^c14481261] area, for the purpose of assisting the returning officer in the discharge of any functions conferred on him in relation to a parliamentary election in that constituency, the services of officers employed by the authority[^c14481271].
