> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/2

## 207. Citation and commencement.[^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Representation of the People Act 1983, and is included among the Acts which may be cited as the Representation of the People Acts.

(2)[^c14506961] This Act shall come into force on such day as the Secretary of State may by order made by statutory instrument appoint.
