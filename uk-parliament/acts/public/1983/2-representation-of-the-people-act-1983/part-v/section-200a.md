# Representation of the People Act 1983, section 200A

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/2
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 200A. Remuneration for free postal services provided under Act.[^c14503981][^c14503991][^key-86dcec07357b9ed351ca685f8c80479a][^c14503971][^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This section applies where any postal services are provided without charge by a universal postal service provider[^key-b390bb99a36ed2db7533f2b5db445003] in pursuance of this Act.

(2)[^c14504001] The universal postal service provider shall be entitled to be remunerated for having provided the services at the rate fixed in relation to them by virtue of a scheme under section 89 of the Postal Services Act 2000.

(3) A sum which a universal postal service provider is entitled to receive by virtue of this section shall be charged on, and issued out of, the Consolidated Fund.

(4)[^key-4db1771e052864998998c961791ccbd6] In this section “**postal services**” has the meaning given by section 27 of the Postal Services Act 2011.
