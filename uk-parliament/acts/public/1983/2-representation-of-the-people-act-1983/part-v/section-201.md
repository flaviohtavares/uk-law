# Representation of the People Act 1983, section 201

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (71).** Text as at 2026-09-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/2

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 201. Regulations.[^c14504391][^c14504401][^c14479931]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c14504411] Any power conferred by this Act to make regulations shall, except where this Act otherwise provides, be a power exercisable by the Secretary of State and except in the case of regulations under section 29(8)[^c14504421]by statutory instrument.

(2)[^c14504431] No regulations shall be made under this Act by the Secretary of State otherwise than under section[^c14504451][^c14504441] 10(4) or[^key-64670856d8c63104eb4b18f8b63ed2ef] 110(7) above or section 203(4) or 203A(2) *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)*[^key-36c2b6a45f2ede932ff0c66578bb4f00] below unless a draft of the regulations has been laid before and approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament.

(2A)[^c14504461] Any regulations under section 10(4) or[^key-dd55e499631007d9358123acfc464562] 110(7) above or section 203A(2) *(Extent: E+W+N.I.)*[^key-9e5134598210018b1cc9352dff5c76ac] shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament.

(2C)[^key-18748c7bb10d27e090d3c06c9b3da81e] Regulations made for the purposes only of omitting a particular kind of evidence from the kinds of evidence that a person is or may be required to provide by virtue of regulations under paragraph 1(2A) or 3ZA(3) of Schedule 2 shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament (and subsection (2) does not apply to regulations made for those purposes only).

(3)[^c14504471] Any regulations under this Act may make different provision for different cases, circumstances or areas and may contain such incidental, supplemental, saving or transitional provisions as the Secretary of State , or the Electoral Commission (in the case of any regulations made by them),[^c14504481] thinks fit.
