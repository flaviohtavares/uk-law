Pet Animals Act 1951 (Amendment) Act 1983

1983 c. 26

An Act to amend the Pet Animals Act 1951 regulating the sale of pet animals.

\[9th May 1983\]

Be it enacted by the Queen’s most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Lords Spiritual and Temporal, and Commons, in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e]

## 1. Amendment of 1951 Act.

The *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e] [^c7561311]Pet Animals Act 1951 shall be amended as follows:— *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e]

- (a (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e] in section 2 (Pets not to be sold in streets, etc.), there shall be substituted for the word “except” the word “or”; *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e]
- (b (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e] in subsection (3) of section 7 (Interpretation) there shall be omitted the words *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e]

  > “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e]**premises *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e]**” includes any stall or barrow in a market, but save as aforesaid does not include any stall or barrow or any part of a street or public place *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e]

  . *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e]

## 2. Short title, commencement and extent.

(1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e] This Act may be cited as the Pet Animals Act 1951 (Amendment) Act 1982. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e] This Act shall come into operation at the end of a period of six months beginning with the day on which it is passed. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e] This Act does not apply to Northern Ireland. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-141818b8c166fd709f68225a324fdd8e]
