# Mental Health Act 1983, section 140

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (398).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/20

## 140. Notification of hospitals having arrangements for special cases.[^c10392851][^c19861481]

*Extent: E+W*

It shall be the duty of every[^key-10dfb8c979b72e0d9fde45ec1bdae5ed] integrated care board[^key-76de8bfd1ce333275cf6cc3d4a4bb149] and of [^key-2a305d5f5cf3fb5da30926a972d10b23]... every Local Health Board[^c19859581] to give notice to every local social services authority for an area wholly or partly comprised within the area of the[^c10392881] integrated care board[^key-23185fd0162c5ccf8e627eec4639881c] or [^key-2484371a0bed963564492bf23a3e4e2b]... Local Health Board specifying the hospital or hospitals administered by or otherwise available[^c10392891] to the[^c10392911] integrated care board[^key-337ffa397c65c882e812b2dcf8b000fd] or [^key-9f24b3eb834e571d5baf5b6121e5350e]... Local Health Board in which arrangements are from time to time in force

- (a)[^c19861501] for the reception of patients in cases of special urgency;
- (b) for the provision of accommodation or facilities designed so as to be specially suitable for patients who have not attained the age of 18 years.
