# Mental Health Act 1983, section 76

> Extent: England, Wales.

## 76. Visiting and examination of patients.[^c10388621]

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c20394991] For the purpose of advising whether an application to the appropriate tribunal[^c20394971] should be made by or in respect of a patient who is liable to be detained or subject to guardianship [^c19852791]. . . under Part II of this Act or a community patient,[^c19852751] or of furnishing information as to the condition of a patient for the purposes of such an application, any registered medical practitioner or approved clinician[^c19852741] authorised by or on behalf of the patient or other person who is entitled to make or has made the application—

- (a) may at any reasonable time visit the patient and examine him in private, and
- (b) may require the production of and inspect any records relating to the detention or treatment of the patient in any hospital or to any after-care services provided for the patient under section 117 below.[^c10388641].

(2) Section 32 above shall apply for the purposes of this section as it applies for the purposes of Part II of this Act.
