## 88. Patients absent from hospitals in England and Wales.[^c19854091]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) Subject to the provisions of this section, any person who, under section 18 above or section 138 below or under the said section 18 as applied by section 22 above, may be taken into custody in England and Wales may be taken into custody in, and returned to England and Wales from, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19854091] Northern Ireland[^c19854041] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19854091].

(2)[^c19854061] For the purposes of the enactments referred to in subsection (1) above in their application by virtue of this section, the expression “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19854091]**constable *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19854091]**” includes an officer or constable of the Police Service of Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19854091]

(3) For the purposes of the said enactments in their application by virtue of this section *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19854091] to Scotland or Northern Ireland *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20622991] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19854091], any reference to an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19854091] approved mental health professional[^c19854081] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19854091]shall be construed as including a reference— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19854091]

- (a) in Scotland, to any mental health officer within the meaning of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20623061] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19854091] Mental Health (Care and Treatment)(Scotland) Act 2003[^c18665551] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20623061] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19854091];
- (b) in Northern Ireland, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c20623241] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19854091] to any *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19854091] approved social worker within the meaning of the Mental Health (Northern Ireland) Order 1986[^c10389271] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19854091].

(4) This section does not apply to any person who is subject to guardianship. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c19854091]
