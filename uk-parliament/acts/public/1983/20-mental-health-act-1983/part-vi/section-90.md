# Mental Health Act 1983, section 90

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (398).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/20

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 90. Regulations for purposes of Part VI.

*Extent: E+W*

Section 32 above shall have effect as if references in that section to Part II of this Act included references to this Part of this Act , so far as this Part of this Act applies to patients removed to England and Wales or for whom responsibility is transferred to England and Wales.[^c19868831]
