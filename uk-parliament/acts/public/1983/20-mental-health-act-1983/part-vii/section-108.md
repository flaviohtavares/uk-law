# Mental Health Act 1983, section 108

> This provision is no longer in force.
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 108. General provisions as to rules under Part VII.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

**No longer in force (ended 2007-10-01).**

(1)[^c18638131] Rules under section 106(5) are to be made by the Lord Chancellor after consulting the Lord Chief Justice.

(1A)[^c18638911] Rules under any other provision of this Part of this Act are to be made in accordance with Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Constitutional Reform Act 2005.

(2) Rules under this Part of this Act may contain such incidental and supplemental provisions as appear requisite for the purposes of the rules.

(3)[^c18638931] The Lord Chief Justice may nominate a judicial office holder (as defined in section 109(4) of the Constitutional Reform Act 2005) to exercise his functions under this section.
