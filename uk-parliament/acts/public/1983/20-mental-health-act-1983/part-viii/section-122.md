# Mental Health Act 1983, section 122

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (398).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 122. Provision of pocket money for in-patients in hospital.[^M_E_99e58876-0237-4e76-84bd-a93ed4931984]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) Welsh Ministers may (in relation to Wales)[^key-db88ef1a3c4989c1aa66e1362ee58f2d] pay to persons who are receiving treatment as in-patients (whether liable to be detained or not) in special hospitals or other hospitals, being hospitals[^c10390411] wholly or mainly used for the treatment of persons suffering from mental disorder, such amounts as the Welsh Ministers think fit[^key-b231a58750da3990bb1b95b1ef624067] in respect of those persons'[^key-3660d75aca5c168ed205c3dbcba83af5] occasional personal expenses where it appears to the Welsh Ministers[^key-a3d7e1fe31e70e19c26ce5acf0213e9a] that those persons[^key-b5d73c58aee2b3ac2b0b18cedb391ccd] would otherwise be without resources to meet those expenses.

(2) For the purposes of [^key-bbec2d9f6ed9516ede6c7534b393bed8]... the National Health Service (Wales) Act 2006[^c18673901] , the making of payments under this section to persons for whom hospital services are provided under that Act[^key-18ce82c2d2b34e4fa41a9dc5d27fb565] shall be treated as included among those services.
