# Mental Health Act 1983, section 63

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (398).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 63. Treatment not requiring consent.

*Extent: E+W*

The consent of a patient shall not be required for any medical treatment given to him for the mental disorder from which he is suffering , not being a form of treatment to which section 57, 58 or 58A above applies,[^c19849781] if the treatment is given by or under the direction of the approved clinician in charge of the treatment[^c19849761] .
