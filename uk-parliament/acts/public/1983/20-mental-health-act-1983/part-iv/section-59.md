# Mental Health Act 1983, section 59

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (398).** Text as at 2026-06-29. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1983/20
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 59. Plans of treatment.

*Extent: E+W*

Any consent or certificate under section 57 , 58 or 58A[^c19842431] above may relate to a plan of treatment under which the patient is to be given (whether within a specified period or otherwise) one or more of the forms of treatment to which that section applies.
