# Mental Health Act 1983, section 61

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 61. Review of treatment.

*Extent: E+W*

(1)[^c20380411] Where a patient is given treatment in accordance with section 57(2) , 58(3)(b) or 58A(4) or (5)[^c19789361] above , or by virtue of section 62A below in accordance with a Part 4A certificate (within the meaning of that section)[^c19789561] that falls within section 64C(4) below[^key-0de01fd2b3bdf198f9d40e2c93e75aae], a report on the treatment and the patient’s condition shall be given by the approved clinician in charge of the treatment[^c19789281] to the regulatory authority[^c20380271]—

- (a) on the next occasion on which the responsible clinician[^c19789301] furnishes a report under section 20(3)[^c10388231] , 20A(4) or 21B(2) above in respect[^c19789581] of the patient; and
- (b) at any other time if so required by the regulatory authority.

(2) In relation to a patient who is subject to a restriction order , limitation direction[^c10388241] or restriction direction subsection (1) above shall have effect as if paragraph (a) required the report to be made—

- (a) in the case of treatment in the period of six months beginning with the date of the order or direction, at the end of that period;
- (b) in the case of treatment at any subsequent time, on the next occasion on which the responsible clinician[^c19789341] makes a report in respect of the patient under section 41(6) , 45B(3)[^c10388251] or 49(3) above.

(3)[^c20380431] The regulatory authority may at any time give notice [^c19849391]. . . directing that, subject to section 62 below, a certificate given in respect of a patient under subsection 57(2) , 58(3)(b) or 58A(4) or (5)[^c19849401] above shall not apply to treatment given to him (whether in England or Wales)[^c20380351] after a date specified in the notice and sections 57 , 58 and 58A[^c19849461] above shall then apply to any such treatment as if that certificate has not been given.

(3A)[^c19849441] The notice under subsection (3) above shall be given to the approved clinician in charge of the treatment.
