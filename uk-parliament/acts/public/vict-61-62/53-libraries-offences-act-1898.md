# Libraries Offences Act 1898

> Text as at 2014-08-01.
>
> Extent: England, Wales.

*Extent: E+W*

Libraries Offences Act 1898

1898 c.53

An Act to provide for the Punishment of Offences in Libraries.

\[12th August 1898\]

## 1. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Libraries Offences Act 1898.

## 2. Penalty for offences.

Any person who, in any library or reading-room to which this Act applies, to the annoyance or disturbance of any person using the same,—

(1) behaves in a disorderly manner;

(2) uses violent, abusive, or obscene language;

(3)[^c-56d1b7ef86c32402e7f3e9bd81a4ac46] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) or who, after proper warning, persists in remaining therein beyond the hours fixed for the closing of such library or reading-room,

shall be liable on summary conviction to a penalty not exceeding level 1 on the standard scale[^c577524].

## 3. Application of Act.

This Act shall apply—

- (a) . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . [^c577525]
- (b) to any library or reading-room maintained by a Society that is a registered society within the meaning of the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014, or is registered[^c-3f380c5133bd6d3caca84a06d2c79925] under the [^c577529]Friendly Societies Act 1974,[^c577528] or by any registered Trade Union.

## 4. Extent of Act.

This Act shall not apply to Scotland or Ireland.
