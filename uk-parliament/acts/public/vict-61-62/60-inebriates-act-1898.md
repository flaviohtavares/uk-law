[^M_F_4ce3be0f-3ee9-4e87-ed22-5da7c3efd685]An Act to provide for the treatment of Habitual Inebriates.

\[12th August 1898\][^c577530]

## 1—20. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c577531]

## Supplemental

### 21—29. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .[^c577532]

### 30. Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Inebriates Act 1898, and the First Schedule to this Act shall have effect for the purposes of the following enactments, namely—[^c577533]

- the [^c577534]Licensing Act 1902, section 6,
- the [^c577535]Licensing (Scotland) Act 1903, section 71, and . . . [^c577536]

(being enactments which operate by reference to the list of offences set out in the Schedule).
