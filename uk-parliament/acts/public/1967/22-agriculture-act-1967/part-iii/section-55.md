# Agriculture Act 1967, section 55

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 55. Powers of entry and of obtaining information.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-f66e93ba6375e0114bb3fe8aa3bcc9c7] A person duly authorised in writing by a Rural Development Board may, on producing if so required a duly authenticated document showing his authority, enter on any land in the Board’s area for the purpose of determining whether, and in what way, any of the functions of the Board should be exercised in relation to the land. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f66e93ba6375e0114bb3fe8aa3bcc9c7]

The right of entry under this subsection may be exercised at any reasonable time, but a person shall not demand admission as of right to any land which is occupied unless at least forty-eight hours’ notice, or in the case of land occupied for residential purposes at least seven days’ notice, of the intended entry has been given to the occupier. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f66e93ba6375e0114bb3fe8aa3bcc9c7]

A person who wilfully obstructs any person acting in the exercise of his powers under this subsection shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f66e93ba6375e0114bb3fe8aa3bcc9c7] level 1 on the standard scale[^c14214561] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f66e93ba6375e0114bb3fe8aa3bcc9c7].

(2) A person leaving any land which he has entered by virtue of the foregoing subsection shall, if the land is unoccupied or the occupier is temporarily absent, leave it as effectively secured against trespassers as he found it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f66e93ba6375e0114bb3fe8aa3bcc9c7]

(3) A Board may by notice served on the owner or occupier of any land in their area, require him to furnish them with such information as may be specified in the notice with regard to the land as the Board may reasonably require for the discharge of such of their functions in relation to the land as may be specified in the notice. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f66e93ba6375e0114bb3fe8aa3bcc9c7]

A person who fails without reasonable cause, or neglects, to furnish to the Board within three months after service of the notice the information specified in the notice shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f66e93ba6375e0114bb3fe8aa3bcc9c7] level 3 on the standard scale *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f66e93ba6375e0114bb3fe8aa3bcc9c7].

(4) Information obtained under this section shall not be disclosed except— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f66e93ba6375e0114bb3fe8aa3bcc9c7]

- (a) with the consent of the person by whom the information was furnished, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f66e93ba6375e0114bb3fe8aa3bcc9c7]
- (b) to a member, officer or servant of the Board or to any person exercising functions on behalf of the Board, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f66e93ba6375e0114bb3fe8aa3bcc9c7]
- (c) to any Minister or to an officer or servant or other person appointed by or exercising functions on behalf of any Minister, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f66e93ba6375e0114bb3fe8aa3bcc9c7]
- (d) for the purpose of any proceedings pursuant to this Part of this Act, or of any criminal proceedings which may be taken whether pursuant to this Act or otherwise, or for the purpose of a report of any such proceedings, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f66e93ba6375e0114bb3fe8aa3bcc9c7]

and a person who discloses information in contravention of this subsection shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding one hundred pounds or imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or both, or on conviction on indictment to a fine or imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-f66e93ba6375e0114bb3fe8aa3bcc9c7]
