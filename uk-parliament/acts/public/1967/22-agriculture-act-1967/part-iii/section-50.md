## 50. Control of sale of certain land: exceptions and supplemental provisions.[^c14214041][^c14214051][^c14214031]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03] The last foregoing section shall only apply to land which at the time when it is or is to be transferred is agricultural land or woodland or unenclosed mountain, hill or heath land, or common land or waste land. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]

(2) Where one of the grounds on which an appeal is brought under subsection (5) of the last foregoing section is that the proposed transfer is part of a proposed transaction involving land in the Board’s area which is not within subsection (1) of this section, or involving land which is not within the Board’s area, the appropriate Minister may, if he decides not to allow the appeal but is of opinion that the appellant would be substantially prejudiced if he is unable to dispose in one transaction of all the land comprised in the proposed transaction, with the consent of the appellant direct that any purchase notice served under the last foregoing section by the appellant shall relate both to the land to which the application relates and also to the other land, or such part of it as the appropriate Minister may direct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]

(3) The last foregoing section shall not apply to a transfer to or from— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]

- (a) a local authority, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]
- (aa (affected by repeal, see note))[^c14214061][^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03] a National Park authority; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]
- (b) statutory undertakers as defined in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03] section 336(1) of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990[^c14214071] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03] or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03] section 214 of the Town and Country Planning (Scotland) Act 1997[^c14214081] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03],
- (c) any body corporate which is established by or under any enactment for the purpose of carrying on under national ownership any industry or part of an industry or undertaking and whose affairs are managed by the members thereof, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]
- (d) the National Trust, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]
- (e)[^c14214091] . . .
- (f)[^c-3c77397d8b1a7125f54d67b51ddc42f2] United Kingdom Research and Innovation, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]
- (g)[^c-54465f81b5d2ba8acbb1c037b30d418f] the Environment Agency *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03][^c-7d850afe1e97498a85688de9edb2bba3]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03] or any water undertaker or sewerage undertaker; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]
- (h)[^c14214111] an internal drainage board *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03] [^c14214121] . . .

In this subsection the “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03] **National Trust *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]** ” means in relation to Scotland the National Trust for Scotland for Places of Historic Interest or Natural Beauty incorporated by the Order confirmed by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03] [^c14214131]National Trust for Scotland Order Confirmation Act 1935; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03] [^c14214141]. . .

(4) The last foregoing section shall not apply to a transfer by an individual to a member of his family or to the trustees of a settlement exclusively for the benefit of members of his family, and in this subsection “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03] **family *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]** ” means the individual’s husband or wife, any lineal descendant or ancestor of his, and his brother and sister and any child of a brother or sister, and in deducing any such relationship an adopted child shall be treated as a child and any relationship of the half blood shall be treated as a relationship of the whole blood. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]

(5) The last foregoing section shall not apply to a transfer of land effected in pursuance of a contract of sale concluded before the land came to be in the area of the Board or, if the order establishing the Board so provides, before the expiry of such period not exceeding three months from the date on which it is established as may be specified in the order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]

(6) The last foregoing section shall not apply— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]

- (a) to a transfer to give effect to the devolution of land on death or bankruptcy or sequestration or under the terms of a settlement, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]
- (b) to a transfer in exercise of the rights conferred on a mortgagee heritable creditor or chargee by a mortgage heritable security or charge created before the relevant land came to be in the area of the Board. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]

(7) If land is transferred in contravention of subsection (1) of the last foregoing section the Board shall be deemed to be authorised to acquire all the estate and interest of the transferor in the land transferred (whether in his hands, or in the hands of persons deriving title under him) by a compulsory purchase order, but any notice to treat given in pursuance of this subsection shall be served within six years from the date of transfer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]

“**Compulsory purchase order *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]**” has the same meaning as in section 49(7) of this Act, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03] Part IV of the Acquisition of Land Act 1981[^c14214151] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03] and Part IV of Schedule 1 to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03] [^c14214161] Acquisition of Land (Authorisation Procedure) (Scotland) Act 1947 (validity of compulsory purchase orders) shall apply in relation to any notice to treat served in pursuance of this subsection as they apply in relation to a compulsory purchase order. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]

(8) In assessing compensation in accordance with section 5 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03] [^c14214171] Land Compensation Act 1961 or section 12 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03] [^c14214181] Land Compensation (Scotland) Act 1963 (which relate to compulsory purchases of land) no account shall be taken of any depreciation of the value of the relevant interest which is attributable to the provisions of the last foregoing section and this section. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]

(9) Failure to obtain consent under subsection (1) of the last foregoing section to any transaction shall not invalidate that transaction. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-840eb475fb7c3eb19d92dc44930a3e03]
