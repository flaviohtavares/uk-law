# Agriculture Act 1967, section 57

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/22

## 57. Interpretation of Part III.

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1)[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9] In this Part of this Act, except where the context otherwise requires— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9]

- “**amalgamation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9]**”, “**boundary adjustment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9]**”, “**commercial unit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9]**”, “**intermediate unit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9]**” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9]**uncommercial unit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9]**” have the same meanings as in Part II of this Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9]
- “the appropriate forestry authority” means the Forestry Commission in relation to England[^c-3064af0ebf1434da4b249673e77f0ff5] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9] , the Scottish Ministers in relation to[^c-92d87b9e470cb71a77240b028083376e] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9] Scotland *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9] [^c-a4d8335c2a29afa8cb9956c588d0382a]... *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9];
- “**prescribed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9]**”, in relation to the contents of a scheme, means prescribed by the scheme; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9]
- “**woodland *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9]**” includes all land used primarily for the growing of trees. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9]

(2) For the purposes of this Part of this Act land in the area of a Rural Development Board shall be regarded as having been within that area from the date when the Board is established except that, if included by an order varying the Board’s area, it shall be regarded as being within that area from the date when that order takes effect. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9]

(3) Section 107 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9] [^c14214591]Agriculture Act 1947 or, as the case may be, section 83 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9] [^c14214601]Agriculture (Scotland) Act 1948 (manner of service of notice) shall apply to notices required or authorised to be served by a Minister or a Rural Development Board under this Part of this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-913cf347299588be58d1b89b314deec9]
