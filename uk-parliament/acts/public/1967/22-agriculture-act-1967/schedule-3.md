### Condition restricting transfers without Minister’s consent (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

**3.** (1) It shall be a condition that, except with the written consent of the appropriate Minister, no person shall transfer to any other a part only, or any estate or interest in a part only, of the land comprised in the unit. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

(2) References in this paragraph to the transfer of land, or of an estate or interest in land, include references to devolution on death or under the terms of a settlement and the High Court may, on the application of a person affected by this sub-paragraph, vary the way in which property is to devolve on a death or under a settlement so that there is no breach of the conditions specified in this paragraph but, subject to that, so that the persons interested in the unit of land, including those to whom property would devolve on the death or under the settlement, so far as required to surrender any interest in the unit of land, are compensated by receiving part of the proceeds of sale of the land or in any other way. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

(3) References in this paragraph to the transfer of land, or an estate or interest in land— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

- (a) include, subject to paragraph (b) below, references to transfer by way of the creation or assignment of a lease, agreement for a lease or tenancy, including in each case a sublease or sub-tenancy, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]
- (b) do not include references to the granting or assignment of any right of occupation the grant of which is made (whether or not expressly to that effect) in contemplation of the use of the land only for grazing or mowing during some specified period of the year, and, without prejudice to the foregoing words, in Northern Ireland do not include references to the transfer or assignment of any rights arising by virtue of a conacre agreement, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]
- (c) do not include references to any reconveyance or other transaction by way of discharge of a mortgage, heritable security or charge, or of the release or disburdening of any land from a mortgage, heritable security or charge. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

(4) The application of this paragraph to any transaction shall not invalidate that transaction, but this sub-paragraph shall not be taken as affecting the terms written into a lease, agreement or tenancy by paragraph 1 of this Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

### Condition restricting non-agricultural use without Minister’s consent (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

**4.** It shall be a condition that, except with the written consent of the appropriate Minister, the whole of the unit of land shall at all times be used for agricultural purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

### Condition requiring giving of information (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

**5.** It shall be a condition that the owner of the unit of land *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb] or any such other person having an estate or interest in the unit of land as may be agreed between the appropriate Minister, the owner and that other person[^c14215591] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb] shall, on being required so to do by the appropriate Minister, certify that the conditions in paragraphs 3 and 4 above are being observed with respect to the unit of land, and any tenant of the unit of land shall, on being so required in writing by the owner, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb] or, if the tenant has been informed of such an agreement as aforesaid, by the other person in question, furnish to the owner or, as the case may be, to that other person[^c14215601] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb] such information as he may reasonably require for the purpose of enabling him to comply with this condition. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

### Breach of condition (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

**7.** (1) A person by whom the condition specified in paragraph 3 or paragraph 4 above is breached as respects any unit of land shall be liable to pay to the appropriate Minister— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

- (a) an amount equal to the relevant Exchequer payments, together (except in the case of relevant Exchequer payments representing a loan or interest on a loan) with interest until payment from such date, not being earlier than the relevant date, and at such rate, as may be determined by the appropriate Minister with the approval of the Treasury, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]
- (b) subject to sub-paragraph (2) below, an additional amount equal to so much of the value of the unit, when subject to the conditions imposed by this Schedule, as is attributable to the land which is the subject of the breach, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

and the amount which that person is liable to pay under this sub-paragraph shall be a charge on all the estate and interest in the land comprised in the unit which he had at the time of the breach, binding him and his successors in title. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

(2) The additional amount payable by virtue of sub-paragraph (1)(b) above shall *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb] not exceed £1500[^c14215661] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

(3) Before commencing proceedings against any person to enforce the liability imposed on him by, or the charge arising under, sub-paragraph (1) above, the appropriate Minister shall serve on him a notice specifying the condition alleged to have been breached and, if it is the condition relating to use, the nature of the use constituting the breach; and, unless within two months of the service of the notice that person has— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

- (a) admitted in writing the breach and his liability in respect thereof or, as the case may be, the breach and the existence of the charge, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]
- (b) agreed in writing the amount recoverable by virtue of paragraph (b) of that sub-paragraph, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

the matter or matters still in issue shall be determined by arbitration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

(4) Section 84 of the Agricultural Holdings Act 1986[^c14215671] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb] (procedure for arbitrations) shall have effect as if any such matter were one required by that Act to be determined by arbitration under that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

(5) sections 61 and 64 of the Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 1991[^c14215681] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb] (provisions regarding arbitrations) shall have effect as if any question in sub-paragraph (3) above were a matter required by that Act to be determined thereunder; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb] sections 60(2) and 80(2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb] of that Act shall have effect as if the parties to the dispute were the landlord and tenant of an agricultural holding. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

(6) For the purposes of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb] the[^c14215701] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb] [^c14215711]Limitation Act 1980 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb], no cause of action or right to receive money shall be deemed to have accrued to the appropriate Minister by virtue of sub-paragraph (1) above until the date on which he served the notice referred to in sub-paragraph (3) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

(7) If a breach of either of the said conditions constitutes a breach of the terms of any lease, agreement for a lease or tenancy (whether written in by paragraph 1 of this Schedule or not) any person who is or was at any time entitled under the lease, agreement or tenancy to enforce those terms, or to exercise by forfeiture or otherwise any sanction for their breach, shall be liable to pay to the appropriate Minister the amounts specified in sub-paragraph (1) above so far as not recovered from any other person unless he shows to the satisfaction of the court in which proceedings for recovery are taken against him that he has, in exercise of those rights, taken all reasonable steps to prevent the breach, and to make good the results of the breach. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

(8) On the receipt by the appropriate Minister of all sums due to him under this paragraph in consequence of a breach or, if he accepts a lesser amount in satisfaction of those sums, on the giving by him of a written discharge therefor, the conditions specified in this Schedule, so far as applied by reference to the relevant Exchequer payments taken into account in arriving at those sums or by reason of the making of any grant under section 27(1)(a) of this Act, shall cease to be applicable to the unit of land. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]

(9) A person shall not be liable to pay any part of the relevant Exchequer payments both under this Schedule and under some other provision of this Act, or where he has already become so liable by reason of a previous breach of condition. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-0e3b7552d4a7972f47b45976d71de8cb]
