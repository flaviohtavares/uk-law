## 74. Expenses and receipts of Ministers.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-70a1a5f57dce26e30b94fd31d3b0b1fb] Any expenses incurred by any Minister under this Act shall be defrayed out of money provided by Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70a1a5f57dce26e30b94fd31d3b0b1fb]

(2) Any sums received or recovered by any Minister in pursuance of this Act or any order made thereunder shall, except as otherwise expressly provided, be paid into the Exchequer. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-70a1a5f57dce26e30b94fd31d3b0b1fb]
