# Agriculture Act 1967, section 71

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/22

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 71. Offences by bodies corporate.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

Where a body corporate is guilty of an offence under this Act, and that offence is proved to have been committed with the consent or connivance of, or to be attributable to any neglect on the part of, any director, manager, secretary or other similar officer of the body corporate, or any person who was purporting to act in any such capacity, he, as well as the body corporate, shall be guilty of that offence and shall be liable to be proceeded against and punished accordingly. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3f07b33b47175c2c4d5dc224bbb95bb7]

In this section “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3f07b33b47175c2c4d5dc224bbb95bb7]**director *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3f07b33b47175c2c4d5dc224bbb95bb7]**”, in relation to a body corporate established by or under any enactment for the purpose of carrying on under national ownership any industry or part of an industry or undertaking, being a body corporate whose affairs are managed by its members, means a member of that body corporate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-3f07b33b47175c2c4d5dc224bbb95bb7]
