# Agriculture Act 1967, section 75

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/22

## 75. Short title, interpretation, repeals and commencement.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) This Act may be cited as the Agriculture Act 1967.

(2) In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

- “[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891]**the appropriate Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891]**” means, in relation to England and Wales or Northern Ireland, the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food and, in relation to Scotland, the Secretary of State; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891]
- “agriculture", “agricultural land", “agricultural unit" and cognate expressions and references to farming shall be construed except in relation to Scotland in accordance with section 109 of the [^c14215321]Agriculture Act 1947, and in relation to Scotland, in accordance with section 86 of the [^c14215331]Agriculture (Scotland) Act 1948;
- “**assignment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891]**”, in relation to Scotland, means assignation; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891]
- “**heritable security *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891]**” has the same meaning as in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891] [^c14215341]Conveyancing (Scotland) Act 1924 except that it includes a security constituted by ex facie absolute disposition or assignation, and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891]**heritable creditor *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891]**” shall be construed accordingly; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891]
- “**land *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891]**” includes any estate or interest in land; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891]
- “**local authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891]**” means, in England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891] [^c14215351]..., the council of a county, of a *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891] [^c14215361]... London borough, the Common Council of the City of London and an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891] [^c14215361]... district council *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891][^key-959e2704a409e48978334242bd649a38]... and, in Scotland, a county council, joint county council of a combined county, town council or district council; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891]
- “**pension *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891]**”, in relation to any person, means a pension of any kind whatsoever, whether contributory or not, payable to or in respect of him, and includes a gratuity so payable on his retirement or death, and a return of contributions to a pension fund, with or without interest thereon or any other addition thereto. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-755e29d76e58f180e14414aad5e3d891]

(3) In this Act references to England and Wales shall be construed as if Wales included Monmouthshire. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c949d680d3ccba1123407df3402f2f88]

(4) References in this Act to the duration of a scheme are references to the period within which applications for approval of proposals qualifying for grant under the scheme must be made *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c949d680d3ccba1123407df3402f2f88] [^key-43b33b47e674b97e96971c8410234335]...; and the provisions of this Act limiting the duration of a scheme shall not prevent a scheme providing different periods for different purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c949d680d3ccba1123407df3402f2f88]

(5) Any reference in this Act to any enactment shall be construed as a reference to that enactment as amended or extended by any other enactment, including this Act, and in this Act “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c949d680d3ccba1123407df3402f2f88]**enactment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c949d680d3ccba1123407df3402f2f88]**” includes an enactment of the Parliament of Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c949d680d3ccba1123407df3402f2f88]

(6) Any power of giving directions conferred by this Act shall include a power, subject to the like conditions, to vary or revoke a direction so given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c949d680d3ccba1123407df3402f2f88]

(7) The Acts mentioned in Schedule 7 to this Act shall be repealed to the extent specified in the third column of that Schedule, but subject to the provisions at the end of that Schedule. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-c949d680d3ccba1123407df3402f2f88]
