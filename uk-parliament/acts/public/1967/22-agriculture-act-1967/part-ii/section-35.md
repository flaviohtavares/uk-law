# Agriculture Act 1967, section 35

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 35. General provisions for schemes under Part II.[^c-7a6cbace02690b61553411beb7be334b][^c14212671]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

A scheme under section 26 or section 27 of this Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a6cbace02690b61553411beb7be334b]

- (a) shall be made by the appropriate Minister with the approval of the Treasury, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a6cbace02690b61553411beb7be334b]
- (b) without prejudice to the provisions of subsection (4) of the said section 26 as to schemes under that section, may, in the case of a scheme under the said section 27[^c14212681] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a6cbace02690b61553411beb7be334b] authorise the making of different grant in different circumstances, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a6cbace02690b61553411beb7be334b]
- (c) may authorise the reduction or withholding of grant where assistance in respect of expenditure for which the grant is made is or may be given otherwise than under the scheme, but so that where expenditure, or part of expenditure, is eligible for grant at different rates, grant at the higher rate shall be allowed, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a6cbace02690b61553411beb7be334b]
- (d) may authorise the reduction or withholding of grant in respect of land for the benefit of which any other prescribed grant or contribution has been made out of money provided by Parliament or has been so made within a prescribed period , *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a6cbace02690b61553411beb7be334b]
- (e) may be made for England and Wales, for Scotland, for Northern Ireland, or for any two or all of those parts of the United Kingdom jointly, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a6cbace02690b61553411beb7be334b]
- (f) may contain such incidental and supplemental provisions as appear to the appropriate Minister expedient *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a6cbace02690b61553411beb7be334b] including transitional provisions treating as having been done under or in pursuance of the scheme anything done under or in pursuance of a previous scheme *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a6cbace02690b61553411beb7be334b]
- (g) may be varied or revoked by a subsequent scheme, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a6cbace02690b61553411beb7be334b]
- (h) shall be made by statutory instrument of which a draft has been laid before and approved by resolution of each House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-7a6cbace02690b61553411beb7be334b]
