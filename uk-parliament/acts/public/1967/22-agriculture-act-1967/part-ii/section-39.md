# Agriculture Act 1967, section 39

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 39. Provisions relating to Northern Ireland.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] Subject to this section, this Part of this Act shall extend to Northern Ireland with the exception of section 28 and section 38 and, subject to the following provisions of this section, of section 29. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]

(2) The Department of Agriculture for Northern Ireland[^c14212771] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] may acquire by agreement any estate or interest in— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]

- (a) any land used for agriculture, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]
- (b) any other agricultural land, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]
- (c) where any such land as is mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b) above is offered to the said *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] for acquisition by it on the condition that it also acquires other land not falling within either of those paragraphs, that other land, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]

for the purpose of effecting amalgamations of agricultural land and reshaping agricultural units. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]

(3) The said *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] may manage, farm or dispose of land acquired by it under subsection (2) above— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]

- (a) in such manner as appears to the said *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] expedient for the purpose for which the land was acquired, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]
- (b) if the said *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] is satisfied that the land ought to be devoted to some other purpose, in such manner as appears to the said *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] expedient therefor, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]

and in section 27(1)(b)of this Act as it applies in Northern Ireland the reference to the appropriate Minister shall include a reference to the said[^c14212831] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f].

Section 45(3) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] [^c14212851]Interpretation Act (Northern Ireland) 1954 shall have effect for the purposes of this subsection as it has effect for the purposes of an enactment of the Parliament of Northern Ireland passed after the commencement of that Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]

(4) Sections 4 and 5 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] [^c14212861]Land Law (Ireland) Act 1881 shall not have effect in relation to any tenancy granted by the said *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] under subsection (3) above. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]

(5) Subsections (2) and (3) above shall in particular confer power to enter into transactions involving loss, including transactions corresponding to those into which the appropriate Minister may enter under section 29(2) of this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]

(5A)[^c14212891][^c14212881] Sections 26(1)(a) and (bb) and 27(5B)[^c14212901] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] of this Act shall, in their application to Northern Ireland, have effect as if references to a tenant as defined in the Acts there mentioned were references to a tenant who holds under a contract of tenancy for a life or lives or for a term of years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]

(6) Section 29(3) of this Act, and Schedule 3 to this Act when read with the said section 29(3), shall apply in Northern Ireland as if— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]

- (a) the said *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] were substituted for the appropriate Minister, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]
- (b) the reference to legal costs in the said section 29(3)(a) included a reference to the costs of obtaining any requisite consent of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] the Department of Finance for Northern Ireland *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f],
- (c) the said section 29(3)(a) included a reference to compensation for disturbance under section 3 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] [^c14212931]Landlord and Tenant (Ireland) Act 1870. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]

(7)[^c14212941] The functions conferred by this section on the said *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] shall be exercised in accordance with arrangements made between the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] and the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food with the approval of the Treasury, and the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food shall pay out of money provided by Parliament any expenses incurred by the said *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] in exercising those functions, and any sums received by that *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] Department *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f] in exercising those functions (including any sums so received or recovered under Schedule 3 to this Act) shall be paid over to the said Minister. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-eb97dfe52a2c9778e4adbcc8eb0b835f]
