## 40. Interpretation of Part II.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f] In this Part of this Act, except where the context otherwise requires— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]

- “**amalgamation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]**” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]**boundary adjustment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]**” have the meanings given by section 26 of this Act; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]
- “**exchange *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]**” in relation to land in Scotland means excambion; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]
- “**prescribed *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]**”, in relation to the contents of a scheme, means prescribed by the scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]

(2) In this Part of this Act— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]

- (a) “**commercial unit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]**” means an agricultural unit which in the opinion of the appropriate Minister is capable, when farmed under reasonably skilled management, of providing full-time employment for an individual occupying it and for at least one other man (or full-time employment for an individual occupying it and employment for members of his family or other persons equivalent to full-time employment for one man); *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]
- (b) “**intermediate unit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]**” means an agricultural unit which, in the opinion of the appropriate Minister, is capable, when farmed under reasonably skilled management, of providing full-time employment for an individual occupying it. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]

In this subsection “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]**full-time employment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]**” shall be construed in accordance with any provisions defining that expression in any scheme made under section 26 of this Act. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]

(3) In this Part of this Act “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]**uncommercial unit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]**” shall be construed by reference to the definition of commercial unit, except that, where the appropriate Minister so directs, it may be treated as referring to the relevant agricultural unit exclusive of any one dwelling house of the unit *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f] or of an area sufficient to provide a site for one dwelling house[^c14212991] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f] and, if the Minister so directs, exclusive of any small portion of land to be occupied with that dwelling house. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]

(4) Where any agricultural land in Scotland consists of or includes a croft or holding, for the purposes— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]

- (a) of determining under the preceding provisions of this section whether an agricultural unit formed by that land is commercial, intermediate or uncommercial, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]
- (b) of calculating under section 27(2)(c) of this Act the income derived from the land, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]

the land shall be taken to include any right in pasture or grazing land held by the tenant or landholder whether alone or in common with others and deemed to form part of the croft or holding. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]

In this subsection “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]**croft *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]**” and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]**holding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]**” have the meanings ascribed to them by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f] [^c14213001]Crofters (Scotland) Act 1955 and the Small Landholders (Scotland) Acts 1886 to 1931 respectively. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-c2730add1a070c83e7f5b2c0e4d59a8f]
