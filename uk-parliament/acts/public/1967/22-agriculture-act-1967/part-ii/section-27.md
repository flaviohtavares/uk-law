# Agriculture Act 1967, section 27

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 27. Grants for individuals relinquishing occupation of uncommercial units. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a][^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1) The appropriate Minister may in accordance with a scheme make a grant by way of a lump sum payment or an annuity to or for the benefit of an individual who in prescribed circumstances *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a] relinquishes his occupation of, or of a part of, an uncommercial unit[^c14212091] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a] of agricultural land— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]

- (a) where the relinquishment of all or any part of that uncommercial unit is effected as part of an *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a] amalgamation approved in pursuance of a scheme under section 26 of this Act, or[^c14212101] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]
- (b) in accordance with arrangements which the appropriate Minister or, subject to the provisions of Part III of this Act, a Rural Development Board makes with him (whether or not his landlord or any other person is a third party) to enable the appropriate Minister or the Board to dispose of the unit so that it is owned or occupied with other land, or so as to improve the shape of any agricultural units, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]
- (c) subject to the provisions of Part III of this Act, in accordance with arrangements approved by a Rural Development Board as being for the purpose of facilitating the afforestation of land in the area of the Board, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a] or[^c14212111] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]
- (d) where the grant is made in pursuance of provisions of a scheme implementing, or arising out of or related to, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a] an EU[^c-01d783866d7153868eb6c0e55afe2f8b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a] obligation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]

(2) A scheme under this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]

- (a) may, subject to any prescribed exceptions, require the individual to have been in occupation of the whole of the uncommercial unit for a prescribed period, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]
- (b) may exclude an individual who occupied the uncommercial unit under a short-term letting as defined in the scheme. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]
- (c) may apply to the individual a test by reference to the income (calculated in a prescribed way) derived from *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a] the trade or business of, or employment in, agriculture[^c14212121] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a] including, if the scheme so provides, income so derived by the individual’s wife or husband or partner or by any other person jointly occupying the land. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]

(3) A scheme under this section may authorise the making of grant subject to such conditions as the appropriate Minister may specify. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]

(4) Grants shall not be payable under this section to any one individual in respect of more than one uncommercial unit, and a scheme under this section shall contain provisions for securing that the amount of the grant payable in respect of an uncommercial unit occupied by more than one individual is an amount which is not more than approximately equivalent to the amount which would be payable if it had been occupied by a single individual. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]

(5) A scheme under this section may provide that, after the death of a person in receipt of a grant by way of annuity, grant under this section of an amount specified in the scheme may be payable to a surviving widow or widower. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]

(5A)[^c14212131] A scheme under this section may make provision, in a case where a person who has submitted an application for a grant under this section, and who has in prescribed circumstances either relinquished or become under an obligation to relinquish occupation of the uncommercial unit of agricultural land in question, subsequently dies before the application has been dealt with, for the application to be proceeded with after the death and for grants under this section of such respective amounts as may be determined by or under the scheme to be payable either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]

- (a) by way of annuity— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]
  - (i) in respect of any period after the relinquishment and before the death, for the benefit of the deceased’s estate; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a] in respect of any period falling after both the relinquishment and the death, to any person who was both at the date of the death and at the time when the application was made the spouse of the deceased; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]
- (b) by way of a lump sum payment for the benefit of the deceased’s estate. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]

(5B)[^c14212141] A scheme made *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a] by virtue of subsection (1)(a), (b) or (d) of this section[^c14212151] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a] may make provision whereby a person is treated for the purposes of the scheme, to such extent and in such cases as may be prescribed— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]

- (a) as the occupier of any land in respect of which he has granted, or agreed to grant, to another person a licence or tenancy of a kind not making that other person a tenant as defined in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a] Agricultural Holdings Act 1986[^c14212161] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a] (or, in Scotland, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a] the Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 1991[^c14212171] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]);
- (b) as relinquishing his occupation of that land if he relinquishes his remaining estate or interest in the land; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]
- (c) as relinquishing his occupation of, or of part of, an uncommercial unit of agricultural land if the land was, or was part of, such a unit immediately before he first granted or agreed to grant such a licence or tenancy as aforesaid; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]

and, in relation to any provision made by virtue of this subsection, subsections (2), (4) and (5A) of this section shall not apply but provision may be made in the scheme for corresponding purposes. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]

(6) The duration of a scheme under this section shall be a period not exceeding seven years, but that period may be extended from time to time by subsequent schemes under this section for periods not exceeding seven years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]

(7) Any grant under this section shall be paid out of money provided by Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-fece0b169da757acca7ab6f48a9b3d8a]
