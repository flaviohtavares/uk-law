## 26. Grants for amalgamations and boundary adjustments. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785][^c14211621][^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785][^c14211631]

(1) The appropriate Minister may in accordance with a scheme and subject to section 50 of the[^c14211641] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] [^c14211651]Agriculture Act 1970 make, out of money provided by Parliament, grants of amounts determined in such manner as may be provided by or under the scheme in connection with the carrying out of any of the following transactions approved by him in pursuance of the scheme, that is to say— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]

- (a) [^c14211661]. . . transactions for securing that agricultural land which is *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] or forms part of[^c14211671] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] an uncommercial unit, but which together with some other agricultural land could form an intermediate unit or commercial unit, shall be owned and occupied with that other land, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] or, if not so owned, occupied with that other land by a person who shall occupy any part of the resulting unit not owned by him as a tenant (as defined in the[^c14211681] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] Agricultural Holdings Act 1986[^c14211691] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] or, in Scotland, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] the Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 1991[^c14211701] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785], and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]
- (b) [^c14211661]. . . transactions for securing that, where an intermediate unit or a commercial unit is not all in the same ownership, any part of it comes to be in the same ownership as the rest of that unit, or in the same ownership as some other part of that unit, but excluding transactions which bring into the same ownership and occupation two or more parts of the unit each of which could by itself form a commercial unit, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]
- (bb (affected by repeal, see note))[^c14211721][^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] transactions for securing that, where the person occupying an intermediate unit or a commercial unit occupies any part or parts of it otherwise than as owner or as a tenant as defined in the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] Agricultural Holdings Act 1968 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] (or, in Scotland, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] the Agricultural Holdings (Scotland) Act 1991 *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]), he comes to occupy that part or all those parts as owner or as such a tenant, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]
- (c) [^c14211661]. . . transfers or exchanges of agricultural land (or estates or interests in agricultural land) for the purpose of giving more satisfactory boundaries to one or more agricultural units; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]

and for the purposes of paragraph (a) above, such assumptions as the appropriate Minister may consider reasonable may be made as to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] works and facilities which will be carried out or provided[^c14211761] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] for the benefit of the unit to be formed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]

Transactions within paragraphs (a), (b) and (bb) above[^c14211771] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] are in this Part of this Act referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]**amalgamations *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]**”, and transactions within paragraph (c) are in this Part of this Act referred to as “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]**boundary adjustments *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]**”.

(2) A scheme under this section may restrict the amalgamations and boundary adjustments to which it applies in any way, and may in particular exclude amalgamations of land which has reverted from being in single ownership or occupation *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] or, as respects transactions within subsection (1)(bb) of this section, cases where after the transaction has been effected the unit is not in single ownership[^c14211781] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785].

- (b) may given subject to such conditions as the appropriate Minister may specify, and in particular subject to any condition as to the time within which the amalgamation or boundary adjustment is to be carried out or as to the carrying out or provision within a specified period of specified works or facilities appearing to the appropriate Minister to be necessary as a consequence of the amalgamation or boundary adjustment; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]

  - (i)[^c14211901] any such grant as aforesaid in respect of such expenditure as the appropriate Minister may certify as being expenditure related to the relevant unit, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]
  - (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] such sum as the appropriate Minister may certify as the sum representing his administrative expenses (including an appropriate proportion of overhead expenses and other fixed or general expenses) incurred in connection with making the grant, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]

  and “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]**the relevant date *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]**” shall, for any grant and the related administrative expenses, be the date when the grant was paid. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]

(8)[^c14211861] In the case of the payment of any grant under this section *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] or any such grant under section 29 of the Agriculture Act 1970 as is referred to in the last foregoing subsection[^c14211921] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] in connection with a boundary adjustment the appropriate Minister may, if he thinks fit, designate in the document giving his approval to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] boundary adjustment *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785] any land appearing to him to benefit from the boundary adjustment as land which, after the payment of that grant, is to be a relevant unit subject to the provisions of Schedule 3 to this Act, and paragraphs (a) and (c) of the last foregoing subsection shall apply in relation to the boundary adjustment as they apply in relation to an amalgamation. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]

(9) The duration of a scheme under this section shall be a period not exceeding seven years, but that period may from time to time be extended by further schemes under this section for periods not exceeding seven years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-9edca197be39c8f48810ad131b9ce785]
