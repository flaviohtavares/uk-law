## 68. Application of capital money in payment of improvement rentcharges.

*Extent: E+W+S*

Section 73(1)(xiii) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8292d5a0bbc9adc51ac2d687284809ab] [^c14215131]Settled Land Act 1925 (under which capital money arising under that Act may be applied in the redemption of improvement rentcharges) shall, in its application to any charge created under the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8292d5a0bbc9adc51ac2d687284809ab] [^c14215141]Improvement of Land Act 1864 in respect of an improvement benefiting agricultural land, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8292d5a0bbc9adc51ac2d687284809ab] [^c14215151] . . . have effect as if the reference to redemption included a reference to discharging as it falls due so much of any periodical payment as represents repayment of capital. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-8292d5a0bbc9adc51ac2d687284809ab]
