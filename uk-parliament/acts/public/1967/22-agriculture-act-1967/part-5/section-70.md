# Agriculture Act 1967, section 70

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (7).** Text as at 2025-09-10. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/22
>
> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

## 70. Guaranteed prices: minor amendments as respects powers of entry and offences.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-f763ea01fe6e3dda659bf32e0b1938de] In the application in Northern Ireland of section 5(1)(d) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f763ea01fe6e3dda659bf32e0b1938de] [^c14215211]Agriculture Act 1957 (powers of entry in connection with guaranteed prices) and any order made thereunder before the coming into force of this section, references to authorised officers of the Minister shall include references to authorised officers of *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f763ea01fe6e3dda659bf32e0b1938de] the Department of Agriculture for Northern Ireland[^c14215221] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f763ea01fe6e3dda659bf32e0b1938de].

(2)[^c14215231] In section 7(3)(b) of that Act (penalty for altering, cincealing or defacing a mark applied to produce) the word “removes" shall be added before the words “alters, conceals or defaces". *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f763ea01fe6e3dda659bf32e0b1938de]

(3) This section extends to Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-f763ea01fe6e3dda659bf32e0b1938de]
