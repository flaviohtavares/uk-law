## 64. Grants towards fulfilling guarantees of bank loans to agriculture or horticulture businesses.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996] Section 9 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996] [^c14214861]Agriculture and Horticulture Act 1964 (grants towards fulfilling guarantees of bank loans to horticulture businesses) shall not apply in relation to any guarantee given after 31st March 1966 (but the provisions of this section shall apply in relation to such guarantees). *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]

(2) The Minister may, in such manner and subject to such conditions as he may with the approval of the Treasury determine, make a grant to any person in respect of expenditure incurred by that person in fulfilling a guarantee given by him as security for a loan made in the course of a banking business to a person requiring the loan for the purposes of an agriculture or horticulture business carried on by him, where— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]

- (a)[^c14214871][^c14214881] the guarantee was given during the period of three years beginning on 1st April 1966, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]
- (b) the guarantee was given in the year ending on 31st March 1966 and the grant could not be given under the said section 9 of that Act of 1964 and is given after the end of the said year. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]

(3)[^key-3c34bfa93cc788a2eda9d26012656c0c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(4) The aggregate of sums paid by way of grant under this section *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996] [^key-a6380b5b2e4d792e53ec6479d53eb881]... in the year ending on 31st March 1968 or in any subsequent year shall be subject to a limit of three hundred thousand pounds increased, where the aggregate of sums so paid in the last preceding year falls short of the limit for that year (whether it be the limit under this or the last foregoing subsection), by the amount of the difference or six hundred thousand pounds, whichever is the less. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]

(5)[^key-2c6a2b7e61e795091d7c713b0a423f19] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6)[^c14214891] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(7)[^key-40bed256ab84e816026994454f9c9660] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(8) In this section— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]

- “**agriculture or horticulture business *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]**” means either— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]
- (a) a business which consists, or so much of a larger business as consists, of the producing in the United Kingdom of agricultural or horticultural produce for sale or of the producing in the United Kingdom of agricultural or horticultural produce for sale and its storage, preparation for market or transport, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]
- (b) the activities of any co-operative association so far as they consist of assisting members in the production in the United Kingdom of agricultural or horticultural produce for sale by the provision of buildings, equipment, facilities or services required in connection therewith, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]
- (c) a co-operative marketing business; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]

- “**agricultural or horticultural produce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]**” means anything (whether live or dead) produced in the course of agriculture and without prejudice to the generality of that definition the expression “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]**agricultural or horticultural produce *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]**” shall include all horticultural produce as defined in section 8(1) of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996] [^c14214901]Horticulture Act 1960; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]
- “**co-operative marketing business *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]**” means a business carried on by a co-operative association and consisting of, or so much of a larger business so carried on as consists of, the storage, preparation for market or marketing, for the sole or primary purpose of assisting members engaged in the production in the United Kingdom of agricultural or horticultural produce for sale, of agricultural or horticultural produce produced by members of the association; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]
- “**co-operative association *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]**” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]
- (a) a registered society as defined in section 74 of the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996] [^c14214911]Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1965 or a society registered under the Industrial and Provident Societies Acts (Northern Ireland) 1893 to 1963, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]
- (b) any body which (whether incorporated or not) has a written constitution from which the Minister is satisfied, having regard to any provision as to the manner in which profits of the body are to be applied for the benefit of its members and all other relevant provisions, that the body is in substance a co-operative association; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]

- “**the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]**” means the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]
- “**year *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]**” means a period of twelve calendar months. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]

(8A)[^c14214921] For the purposes of the definitions in subsection (8) above of “co-operative marketing business" and “co-operative association", the members of a co-operative association shall be deemed to include the members of any other co-operative association which— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]

- (a) is a member of the first mentioned association, or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]
- (b) is deemed to be such a member by virtue of paragraph (a) above or this paragraph. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]

(9) This section extends to Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-29d9782eadeb57f85eada6a89565b996]
