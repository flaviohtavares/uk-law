## 67. Sick pay for agricultural workers.[^c14215031]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] The minimum rates of wages for workers employed in agriculture which the Agricultural Wages Board has power to fix under section 3(1)(a) of the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] [^c14215041]Agricultural Wages Act 1948 may include minimum rates of wages for periods when a worker so employed is absent in consequence of sickness or injury, and may include them notwithstanding that under the contract of employment no remuneration is payable in respect of any such period. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]

(2 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] In section 3(7) of the said Act, and paragraph 5 of Schedule 4 to that Act (which authorise— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] the fixing of a minimum rate of wages so that remuneration received is calculated by reference to periods during the currency of employment, and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] alternative provisions applying according to different circumstances arising during the currency of employment) *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]

references to currency of employment shall, in accordance with subsection (1) of this section, include periods of sickness or injury. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]

(3 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] An order of the Agricultural Wages Board which fixes minimum rates of wages for periods when a worker is absent in consequence of sickness or injury— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] may treat a period of sickness or injury as being during the currency of employment notwithstanding that the contract of employment has terminated, but not where the contract of employment is terminated by notice given before the commencement of the sickness or the occurrence of the injury, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] may limit the period or periods for which a minimum rate of wages is so fixed in any way, and in particular may relate the period or periods to the duration of the period for which the worker has worked for the employer, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] may make the right to the minimum rate of wages depend on compliance by the worker with any conditions, including in particular conditions as to the production of a medical certificate or other evidence of incapacity for work due to sickness or injury, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]
- (d (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] may provide for the times at which, and conditions subject to which, the right to receive wages at the minimum rate is to accrue, and the wages are to become payable, *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]
- (e (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] may provide for account to be taken, in arriving at the minimum rate of wages, of any benefits *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] payable under Parts II to V of the Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992[^c14215051] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]or Part II of the[^c14215061] *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] [^c14215071]Social Security Pensions Act 1975 *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]

(4 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] Subsection (1) of this section shall not alter the construction of any order made under the said Act before the passing of this Act. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]

(5 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] Section 17 of the said Act shall apply for the interpretation of this section. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]

(6 (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] In the application of this section to Scotland— *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]

- (a (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] any reference to the Agricultural Wages Board shall be construed as a reference to the Scottish Agricultural Wages Board; *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]
- (b (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] any reference to the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] [^c14215081]Agricultural Wages Act 1948 shall be construed as a reference to the *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] [^c14215091]Agricultural Wages (Scotland) Act 1949; and *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]
- (c (Extent: E+W) (affected by repeal, see note))[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c] any reference to paragraph 5 of Schedule 4 to the said Act of 1948 shall be construed as a reference to paragraph 5 of Schedule 3 to the said Act of 1949. *(Extent: E+W)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^c-d9fba0ce04d9a82d425798a0a7b1ad4c]
