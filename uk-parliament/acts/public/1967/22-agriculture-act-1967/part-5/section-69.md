## 69. False statements to obtain grants, etc.

*Extent: E+W+S+N.I.*

(1)[^key-7de72b452ffba2f9a440a41cbe14da2c] If any person, for the purpose of obtaining for himself or any other person— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7de72b452ffba2f9a440a41cbe14da2c]

- (a) any grant under this Act, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7de72b452ffba2f9a440a41cbe14da2c] [^c14215161]. . .
- (b)[^c14215171] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
- (c)-(e)[^c14215181] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

or for the purpose of inducing the appropriate Minister or a Rural Development Board to make a loan or guarantee under section 28 or section 47 of this Act, knowingly or recklessly makes a false statement he shall be liable— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7de72b452ffba2f9a440a41cbe14da2c]

- (i) on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7de72b452ffba2f9a440a41cbe14da2c] level 3 on the standard scale[^c14215191] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7de72b452ffba2f9a440a41cbe14da2c] or imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or both. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7de72b452ffba2f9a440a41cbe14da2c]
- (ii (affected by repeal, see note))[^c14215201][^key-7de72b452ffba2f9a440a41cbe14da2c] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) This section extends to Northern Ireland. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^key-7de72b452ffba2f9a440a41cbe14da2c]
