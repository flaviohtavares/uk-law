Greenwich Hospital Act 1967

1967 c.74

An Act to amend the enactments relating to Greenwich Hospital in respect of the investment of capital money and the annual estimates of income and expenditure.

\[27th July 1967\][^c669525]

## 1. Investment of the hospital capital account.

(1) Money standing to the credit of the Greenwich Hospital capital account may be invested by the Secretary of State, subject to such conditions as the Treasury may from time to time determine with respect to the advice to be obtained for the purpose, on the mortgage or in the purchase of land or in other investments of any description whatsoever.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Greenwich Hospital Act 1967.
