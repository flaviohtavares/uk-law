Section 50. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

**1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] (1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] In so far as any appointment, order, regulation, byelaw, application, claim for compensation or reference made, or notice or directions given, or other thing done under an enactment repealed by this Act could have been made, given or done under a corresponding provision of this Act, it shall not be invalidated by the repeal but shall have effect as if made, given or done under that corresponding provisions. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] Anything begun under an enactment repealed by this Act may be continued under this Act as if begun thereunder. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] Any reference in this Act to things done, suffered or occurring in the past shall, so far as the context requires for the purpose of continuity of operation between an enactment repealed by this Act and the corresponding enactment in this Act, be construed as including a reference to things done, suffered or occurring before the commencement of this Act. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] So much of any document as refers expressly or by implication to any enactment repealed by this Act shall, if and so far as the context permits, be construed as referring to this Act or the corresponding enactment therein. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] The generality of sub-paragraphs (1) to (4) above shall not be prejudiced by anything in the subsequent provisions of this Schedule. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

**2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] For the purposes of section 16(4) of this Act, any such anniversary of an application as is there referred to shall, in the case of an application made before the commencement of this Act and having effect as if made under this Act, be taken as falling on the date on which it would have fallen if this Act had not been passed. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

**3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] A notice given by the Commissioners under section 11(1) of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c377651]Forestry Act 1951 and having effect as if given under section 24 of this Act shall, in a case where the notice was by virtue of section 11(4) of that Act inoperative immediately before the commencement of this Act, remain inoperative under section 25(2) of this Act for as long as, and no longer than, it would have so remained but for the passing of this Act. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

**4 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] (1 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] References in this Act to land acquired by the Minister thereunder shall include references to land which, immediately before the commencement of this Act, was vested in that Minister having been acquired by him or any predecessor of his under section 4 of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c377652]Forestry Act 1945, or vested by that section in the appropriate Minister for the purposes of that Act, or transferred to the Minister by the operation of Article 9(1) of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c377653]Secretary of State for Wales and Minister of Land and Natural Resources Order 1965 (which Order transferred functions of the Minister of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food under the Forestry Acts 1919 to 1951 partly to the Minister of Land and Natural Resources and partly to the Secretary of State for Wales) or of Article 2 of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c377654]Ministry of Land and Natural Resources (Dissolution) Order 1967 (which dissolved the Ministry of Land and Natural Resources and transferred its principal functions under the said Acts to the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries and Food). *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(2 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] For the purposes of this Act, *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] but subject to sub-paragraph (3),[^key-d8782010f6ee6e61e6278f2219f0c2e1] *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]any land which was immediately before the commencement of this Act for the time being placed or deemed to have been placed at the disposal of the Commissioners under the Forestry Act 1945 shall be treated as continuing after that commencement to be so placed by virtue of section 39(1) of this Act, without prejudice to the power of the Minister to make any other disposition with regard to that land. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(3 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note))[^key-247827fd2fd77a91144ede16066f69cb][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] Any land in Wales which, immediately prior to 1 April 2013, was treated as being placed at the disposal of the Commissioners pursuant to sub-paragraph (2) shall thereafter be treated as being placed at the disposal of the Natural Resources Body for Wales by virtue of section 39(1) of this Act, without prejudice to the power of the Welsh Ministers to make any other disposition with regard to that land. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

**5 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] The references to the Forestry Commissioners in section 48 of the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c377655]Settled Land Act 1925 (which contains regulations respecting forestry leases) and in the definition of “ *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]**forestry lease *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]**” in section 117 of that Act shall be construed as references to the Minister, and the reference in the said definition to the *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c377656]Forestry Act 1919 shall be construed as a reference to this Act. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

**6 (Extent: S) (affected by repeal, see note).**[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] Any power under any enactment to amend or repeal an enactment repealed by this Act includes power to amend or repeal the corresponding provision of this Act. *(Extent: S)* *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
