## 25. Appeal against notice under s. 24. (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) If a person to whom a notice under section 24 is given claims— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) that the works in question have been carried out in accordance with the conditions of the felling licence or, in the case of felling directions, that they have been complied with; or *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) that the steps required by the notice to be taken are not required by the conditions or directions, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

he may by a notice served on the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] where the notice is given in respect of land or trees in[^c377352] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] England or Wales[^c-f890e99829f2f485b50ecd26f455b098] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7], [^c-0583ecb4a7e3d86ebc486031b7d7bc34]... in the prescribed manner and within the prescribed period after the receipt of the notice under section 24, request the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c-1382ce560a1b8c16f11a004f72810954]... to refer the matter to a committee appointed in accordance with section 27 below. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(2) A notice under section 24 shall be inoperative until the expiration of the prescribed period for the purposes of subsection (1) above and, where a request to the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c-00e62ed1621fae6bcab78ad1549df0e2]... under that subsection is made, until the conclusion of any proceedings under this section in pursuance of the request. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(3) Where such a request is made by a person receiving a notice under section 24, the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c-7515df4c0cd1ecf8dc6d718a5d343308]... shall, unless he is of opinion that the grounds for the request are frivolous, refer the matter accordingly to a committee so appointed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(4) The committee to whom a matter is referred under this section, after complying with section 27(3), shall make a report on the reference to the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] and who[^c-e7cf307c0a146aa3bf1b3e507024bb7b] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] shall, after considering the report, confirm or cancel the notice to which the reference relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
