## 17B. Appeal against restocking notice. (affected by repeal, see note)[^c377225][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) A person on whom a restocking notice has been served who objects to the notice or to any condition contained therein may by notice served within the prescribed time and in the prescribed manner request the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] where the restocking notice relates to land in[^c377320] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] England or Wales[^key-ed689e57cba945392b7b9939f695aad1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^key-7708dea3739c10d688df71f7a0f6645c]... to refer the matter to a committee appointed in accordance with section 27 of this Act; and— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-d64aa58f089dfdb4049be8f2d74426ed]... shall, unless he is *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-0038e771be1aa75c69f55304455c110c]... of the opinion that the grounds of the request are frivolous, refer the matter accordingly; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) the committee, after compliance with subsection (3) of that section, shall thereupon make a report to the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-2158b7f333293cd7b7790220c6e03a51]....

(2) The Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-ea7741f17cabbb5024080aec9dddc498]... may, after considering the committee’s report, direct the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority[^key-c8364f8a4fd111d6b713b29cac3a03c1] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] to withdraw the notice or to notify the objector that it shall have effect subject to such modification as the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-ea7741f17cabbb5024080aec9dddc498]... shall direct. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
