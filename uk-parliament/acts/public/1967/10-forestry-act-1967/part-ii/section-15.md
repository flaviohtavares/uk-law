## 15. Trees subject to preservation order under Planning Acts. (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) If an application is made to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority[^c-028697bc363ad6cae5474e6dc91019d7] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] for a felling licence in respect of trees to which a tree preservation order relates, and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] relevant consent[^c-18799c1c7a8920832342fa38934c9fce] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] is required for the felling of those trees, then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7], if they propose to grant the licence, shall give notice in writing to the authority by whom the order was made; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] may in any case refer the application to the said authority. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(1A)[^c-5d08d7b3f283a53b30816aa3ce46f496] In subsection (1) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] **relevant consent *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]** ” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) in the case of trees in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] England or Wales[^c-fd11abb75109c9363534a37fee0085a0] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7], consent under tree preservation regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b)[^c-7783546833ed5907c9e4774dcf139858] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(2) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] give the notice required by subsection (1)(a) above and the authority within the prescribed period after receipt of the notice object to the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority's[^c-f548fb7b02e215359fff5f01edaf1c04] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] proposal to grant a felling licence and do not withdraw their objection, then— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] shall not deal with the application, but shall refer it to the Minister, and the application shall then be dealt with under the Town and Country Planning Acts; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) if in pursuance of the application the Minister consents to the felling, section 9(1) of this Act shall not apply so as to require a felling licence for the felling of any trees in accordance with the consent. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(3) Where the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] refer an application under subsection (1)(b) above,— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) the application shall be dealt with under the Town and Country Planning Acts; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) so long as the tree preservation order applying to the trees remains in force, section 9(1) shall not apply so as to require a felling licence for the felling of any trees to which the application relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(4) Where in the case of any trees— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] under this section refer an application for a felling licence to the Minister or an authority who have made a tree preservation order relating to the trees; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) a felling licence in respect of the trees has been previously refused by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7],

no account shall be taken, in calculating any compensation payable under section 11 of this Act in consequence of the previous refusal, of deterioration occurring after the date of the reference. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

This subsection shall be without prejudice to section 11(5) of this Act, in a case to which that subsection applies. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(5) Except as provided by the foregoing provisions of this section, no *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] relevant application shall be entertained[^c-4dbd92b7693895d8d80d74dfc07ceb7c] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] in respect of the felling of trees in the case of which section 9(1) of this Act applies so as to require a felling licence. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(5A)[^c-2bfdecc4c5a150ab4c97ca7159caa9c7] In subsection (5) “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] **relevant application *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]** ” means— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) in the case of trees in *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] England or Wales[^c-a0f0b2cf7d77157b59047bac918378ff] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7], an application under tree preservation regulations for consent under the regulations. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b)[^c-5d73a736f28459016f86e15b984bb3e3] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(6) Where, in the case of trees to which a tree preservation order relates, a felling licence is granted by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] after the date on which the order comes into force, the licence shall, notwithstanding anything in that order, be sufficient authority for the felling of any trees to which the order relates. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(7) Schedule 3 to this Act shall have effect for explaining the procedure applicable where this section requires an application to be dealt with under the Town and Country Planning Acts. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
