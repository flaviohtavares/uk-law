## 27. Committees of reference for purposes of ss. 16, 20, 21 (affected by repeal, see note) , 25, 26A, 26B and 26C (affected by repeal, see note).[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^c377359][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^c-0825a6f91e972f23a900bf127e0d1283][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

*Extent: W*

(1) References in sections 16, *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] 17B,[^c377360] 20, 21 , 25, 26A, 26B and 26C[^c-e12c22851fcde3c8808dbb97bf98f0b2] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] of this Act to a committee appointed in accordance with this section are to a committee consisting of— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) a chairman appointed by the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] in relation to cases where the trees are, or the land is, in[^c377361] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] England or Wales[^c-767cfd7da0cca55f5ddb78e3033e1128] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c-2856611a8339596c1f9a1c8a794e2194]... ; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) two other members selected by the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c-3bd5cbbceb3c7f3d4f2649812855ff95]... from a panel of persons appointed by him, after such consultation as is provided for below, for the conservancy in which the trees are growing: *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

  But this is subject to subsections (1A) and (1B)[^c-7d140aeff8bd44e73ee0751faa083b94] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7].

(1A)[^c-158f5cea1f5e8dc49cc0ed3b94225e67] The members of a committee appointed in relation to a case concerning trees or land in England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c-677ac8d74f1a2e0618275c8e02184131]... shall not include any Forestry Commissioner or employee of the Commissioners. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(1B) The members of a committee appointed in relation to a case concerning trees or land in Wales shall not include any member or employee of the Natural Resources Body for Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(2) The consultation required by subsection (1)(b) above is to be with— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) the regional advisory committee for the said conservancy; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) organisations appearing to the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c-f856a88ac29c7c169599e6fa1c9895e0]... to represent the interests of owners of woodlands and timber merchants respectively; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (c) organisations concerned with the study and promotion of forestry. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(3) On any reference being made to them under this Part of this Act a committee appointed in accordance with this section shall— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) afford to the person concerned with the subject-matter of the reference an opportunity of appearing before them and of making representations to them on the matter in question; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) if they think fit, or are so required by the said person, inspect the trees or land to which the reference relates; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (c) take into consideration any information furnished to them by the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority[^c-3006ada705111f8c3d1cc41e57b49bc6] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] as to the performance within the conservancy in which the trees are growing of their duty of promoting the establishment and maintenance *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c377364]... of adequate reserves of growing trees. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

For purposes of this subsection “ *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]**the person concerned with the subject-matter of the reference *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]**” is the person at whose request the reference was made, except that in the case of a reference by the Minister *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c-10346062ddc194f0560ba8aa529cada5]... of a notice under section 21 it is the person by whom the notice was given. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(4) The Minister may pay to the members of a committee appointed by him under this section such remuneration as he may, with the consent of the Treasury *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] as regards England[^c377366] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c-5bf69d131539af15e743971a3bfefed6]... , determine. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(4A)[^c-a86bcac944ecbac1afda09aebc35bc90] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
