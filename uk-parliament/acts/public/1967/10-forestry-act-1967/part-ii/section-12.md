## 12. Conditional licences. (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The conditions which may under section 10(2) *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] (a) or (b) *(Extent: E+W)*[^c-f2eeacafb99b1ca1d1b243899c493825] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] above be attached to a felling licence are such as the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority[^c-1c7ebe34c3c19b502af00eb912019742] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7], after consultation with the applicant for the licence, determine to be expedient for securing— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) the restocking or stocking with trees of the land on which the felling is to take place, or of such other land as may be agreed between the *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] and the applicant; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) the maintenance of those trees in accordance with the rules and practice of good forestry for a period not exceeding ten years. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(2) No conditions shall be imposed on the grant of a felling licence where it is for trees on land subject to a forestry dedication covenant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c-becb8cf97ac4c30726d12eb35c5c3de0]... and the felling is in accordance with a plan of operations or other working plan approved by the[^c377307] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] appropriate forestry authority *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] and in force under the covenant *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^c-becb8cf97ac4c30726d12eb35c5c3de0]... .
