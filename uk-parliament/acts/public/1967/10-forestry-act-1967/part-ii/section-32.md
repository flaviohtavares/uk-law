## 32. Regulations. (affected by repeal, see note)[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7][^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

*Extent: E+W+S*

(1) The appropriate legislative authority may[^key-815911005e0fdd40d44894032a367e08] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^M_F_78f2c99f-7b7e-4b77-c380-de335e467ea3]... by statutory instrument make regulations for prescribing anything which by this Part of this Act is authorised to be prescribed. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(2) A power conferred by this Part of this Act to prescribe the manner in which a claim or notice may be made or given thereunder shall include power to require that any particulars specified in the claim or notice shall be verified by statutory declaration. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(3)[^c377374] Regulations made under this Part of this Act *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] by the Commissioners[^key-f39141542307dff2c80f2a9a8d39eea3] *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] may make provision as regards England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-8259577b66f04f42bdd9e581e297d8a3]... [^key-db79c67206bb84075828e4ed7a8014e2]... .

(4) A statutory instrument containing regulations made under this Part of this Act making provision only as regards England *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7] [^key-c5a73c130be58fb8fb84c09a9a699da1]...—

- (a) if the regulations are made under section 9(5)(b) or (c), shall be of no effect unless approved by a resolution of each House of Parliament; and *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) in a case not falling within the foregoing paragraph, shall be subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of either House of Parliament. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(5)[^key-09690ee0de2d091e9e13a3a180928ba1] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

(5A)[^key-813a5ac80106982cd1f5e86e4901e680] A statutory instrument containing regulations under this Part making provision only as regards Wales— *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

- (a) in the case of regulations under section 9(5)(b) or (c), must not be made unless a draft of the instrument has been laid before, and approved by resolution of, the National Assembly for Wales; *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]
- (b) in a case not falling within paragraph (a), is subject to annulment in pursuance of a resolution of the National Assembly for Wales. *(affected by repeal, see note)*[^M_F_19958652-57e8-4bc4-ed4b-225cd3e274e7]

(6)[^key-22095408baf7799a97341c2cbdfd1097] . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
