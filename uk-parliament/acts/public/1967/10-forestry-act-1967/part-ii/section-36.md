# Forestry Act 1967, section 36

> **Warning: changes not yet applied to this text (30).** Text as at 2026-10-30. See https://www.legislation.gov.uk/changes/affected/ukpga/1967/10

> Extent: England, Wales, Scotland.

## 36. Application of Part II to London.

*Extent: E+W+S*

This Part of this Act shall not apply to trees standing or growing on land within the area of Greater London other than the outer London Boroughs within the meaning of the [^c377394]London Government Act 1963.
